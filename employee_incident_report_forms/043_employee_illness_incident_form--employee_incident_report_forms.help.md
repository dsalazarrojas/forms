# Employee Illness Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information about employee illness incidents and track and manage them in a standardized way, facilitating effective communication and decision-making within HR or management teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions to access the employee's data.
* Select the correct employee and reportee (who reported the incident).
* Fill in the employee's name and contact information.
* Choose the correct incident type (Illness, Injury, Other, or Unknown).
* Enter start and end dates for the incident.
* Describe the incident, its cause, and any treatment administered.
* Provide information on location, witness, manager contact information, and follow-up details.
* Set the incident status and resolution date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Employee Illness Incident Form Data** (`id: 1`): Enter a brief description or any additional comments about the incident.
* **2. Reported By** (`id: 2`): Select who reported the incident.
*   *   Choose from: John, Jane, Bob
* **3. Employee Name** (`id: 3`): Enter the name of the employee involved.
* **4. Employee Email** (`id: 4`): Enter the employee's email address.
* **5. Incident Type** (`id: 5`): Choose the type of incident (Illness, Injury, Other, or Unknown).
*   *   Choose from: Illness, Injury, Other, Unknown
* **6. Start Date** (`id: 6`): Enter the date the incident started.
* **7. End Date** (`id: 7`): Enter the date the incident ended.
* **8. Description** (`id: 8`): Describe the incident, including details about its cause.
* **9. Cause** (`id: 9`): Specify the cause of the incident.
* **10. Treatment** (`id: 10`): Note any treatment administered to the employee.
* **11. Location** (`id: 11`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **12. Witness** (`id: 12`): If applicable, enter any witness information.
* **13. Manager Name** (`id: 13`): Enter the manager's name involved.
* **14. Manager Email** (`id: 14`): Enter the manager's email address.
* **15. Incident Reported By** (`id: 15`): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **16. Incident Reported Date** (`id: 16`): Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **17. Form Submitted By** (`id: 17`): Enter the name of who submitted the form.
* **18. Form Submitted Date** (`id: 18`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **19. Follow Up Date** (`id: 19`): Enter the date of any follow-up action or review.
* **20. Follow Up Note** (`id: 20`): Add any follow-up comments or notes.
* **21. Incident Status** (`id: 21`): Choose the status of the incident (Active, Resolved).
*   *   Choose from: Active, Resolved
* **22. Resolution Date** (`id: 22`): Enter the date the incident was resolved.
* **23. Resolution Note** (`id: 23`): Add any resolution comments or notes.
* **24. Employee Return To Work Status** (`id: 24`): Check if the employee has returned to work.
*   *   Choose from: True, False, Unknown
* **25. Form Notes** (`id: 25`): Add any additional comments or notes about the form submission.

## Tips
* Ensure you complete all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Save the form after completion to keep a record of the incident.
