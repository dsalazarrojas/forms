# Corrective Action Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document corrective actions taken in response to incidents or near-misses. It ensures that actions are well-documented and reviewed, allowing lessons learned to be captured and applied to prevent similar incidents in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the incident date and time to document the date and time of the incident.
2. Provide a brief description of the issue that occurred.
3. Select the nonconformances that were involved in the incident.
4. Describe the corrective action taken to address the issue.
5. Select the outcome of the action taken.
6. Rate the effectiveness of the action taken.
7. Provide evidence supporting the action taken.
8. Enter comments about the action taken.
9. Select the employees who were issued the corrective action to.
10. Select the employee who issued the corrective action.
11. Select the employee who reviewed the corrective action.
12. Enter the date and time the corrective action was reviewed.
13. Select the status of whether the corrective action was closed.
14. Enter comments about the review of the corrective action.
15. Enter comments about the employee who reviewed the corrective action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **corrective_action_form_1_label**: Enter a brief description of the form.
* **incident_date**: Enter the date of the incident.
	+ Format: YYYY-MM-DD.
* **incident_time**: Enter the time of the incident.
	+ Format: HH:MM:SS.
* **issue_description**: Provide a brief description of the issue that occurred.
* **nonconformances**: Select the nonconformances that were involved in the incident.
* **corrective_action_taken**: Describe the corrective action taken to address the issue.
* **corrective_action_outcome**: Select the outcome of the action taken.
* **corrective_action_effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of the action taken.
* **corrective_action_evidence**: Provide evidence supporting the action taken.
* **corrective_action_comments**: Enter comments about the action taken.
* **corrective_action_issued_to**: Select the employees who were issued the corrective action to.
* **corrective_action_issued_by**: Select the employee who issued the corrective action.
* **corrective_action_reviewed_by**: Select the employee who reviewed the corrective action.
* **corrective_action_review_date**: Enter the date the corrective action was reviewed.
* **corrective_action_review_time**: Enter the time the corrective action was reviewed.
* **corrective_action_closed**: Select the status of whether the corrective action was closed.
* **corrective_action_review_comments**: Enter comments about the review of the corrective action.
* **corrective_action_reviewed_by_comments**: Enter comments about the employee who reviewed the corrective action.
* **corrective_action_reviewed_by_name**: Enter the name of the employee who reviewed the corrective action.
* **corrective_action_reviewed_by_title**: Enter the title of the employee who reviewed the corrective action.
* **corrective_action_reviewed_by_department**: Enter the department of the employee who reviewed the corrective action.
* **corrective_action_reviewed_by_location**: Enter the location of the employee who reviewed the corrective action.
* **corrective_action_reviewed_by_phone**: Enter the phone number of the employee who reviewed the corrective action.
* **corrective_action_reviewed_by_email**: Enter the email of the employee who reviewed the corrective action.

Note: Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure that the corrective action is well-documented.
