# Damaged Equipment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Damaged Equipment Form is designed to record incidents of damaged equipment in the workplace. Please use this form when reporting any equipment damage that has occurred.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" field on the last page to submit your report.
2. Fill out the "Equipment Form" page with details about the damaged equipment.
3. On the "Damage Description" page, type a detailed description of what happened to the equipment.
4. Once you have filled out all the fields, click on "Submit" to submit your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Form** (`Equipment Form`, `Text`, Optional): This is the first page of the form where you will provide details about the damaged equipment. Please fill out this field with the name, model number, and any other relevant information about the equipment.
* **Damage Description** (`Damage Description`, `Text`, Optional): On this page, provide a detailed description of what happened to the equipment. Describe the damage, the circumstances surrounding the incident, and any other relevant details.
