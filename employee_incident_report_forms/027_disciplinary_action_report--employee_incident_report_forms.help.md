# disciplinary_action_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "disciplinary_action_report" form is used to document and track disciplinary actions taken against an employee. It helps managers and HR personnel to gather and store information about incidents that led to disciplinary actions, employee demographics, and details about the action itself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the incident that led to the disciplinary action in the `incident_description` field.
2. Select the type of disciplinary action taken in the `discipline` field. This could be Verbal warning, Written warning, or Suspension.
3. Enter the date the disciplinary action was taken in the `report_date` field.
4. Enter the date the action is effective from in the `effective_date` field.
5. Enter the employee's name in the `employee_name` field.
6. Enter the employee's employee number in the `employee_number` field.
7. Select or enter the incident date in the `incident_date` field.
8. Enter the reason for the disciplinary action in the `reason_for_discipline` field.
9. Enter your name as the manager in charge in the `manager_name` field (optional but recommended for tracking and record-keeping).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **report_form** (`report_form`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the incident that led to the disciplinary action. Be as detailed as possible without including any unnecessary information.
* **report_date** (`report_date`, date, not required): Enter the date when the disciplinary action was reported.
* **incident_description** (`incident_description`, text, not required): Enter a detailed description of what happened, including circumstances and context.
* **discipline** (`discipline`, select_one, not required): Select the type of disciplinary action taken - Verbal warning, Written warning, or Suspension.
* **effective_date** (`effective_date`, date, not required): Enter the date when the disciplinary action takes effect.
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as the manager in charge.
* **employee_number** (`employee_number`, text, not required): Enter the employee's employee number.
* **incident_date** (`incident_date`, date, not required): Select or enter the date of the incident.
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, not required): Enter the employee's name.
* **reason_for_discipline** (`reason_for_discipline`, text, not required): Enter a detailed description of why the disciplinary action was taken.
