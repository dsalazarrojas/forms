# Fitness For Duty Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness For Duty Form is used to report incidents or medical conditions that may affect an employee's ability to work safely and effectively. This form helps ensure that necessary medical clearance is obtained and that employees receive prompt guidance and support to prevent injuries and ensure the well-being of both the employee and their colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all necessary information before completing it. This will save you time and effort during the review process.
2. Complete each section carefully and honestly, as the information provided will be used to inform medical clearance and support decisions.
3. If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Incident** (Page 1): This section is used to describe the incident or medical condition that has occurred. Please provide a clear and concise description of what happened.
* **Return To Work** (Page 2): If the employee is currently able to return to work, select the date they will return. If the employee is unable to return to work, select the date they will return or indicate that they are currently unable to return.
* **Job Status** (Page 3): Indicate the current status of the employee's job, which can be:
	+ Normal: The employee is able to perform their duties without restrictions.
	+ Limited: The employee can perform some, but not all, of their duties due to the incident or medical condition.
	+ Unable To Work: The employee is currently unable to perform any of their duties.
* **Medical Clearance** (Page 4): If the employee has medical clearance, select "Medical Clearance". If they do not have medical clearance, select "Other" and provide an explanation.
* **Supervisor Signature** (Page 5): This section is for the supervisor's electronic signature. Please enter your name and title to confirm that you have reviewed and approved the form.
