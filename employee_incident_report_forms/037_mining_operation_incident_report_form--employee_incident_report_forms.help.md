# Mining Operation Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mining Operation Incident Report Form is used to report incidents that occurred during mining operations. It collects information about incidents, including general details, dates and times, incident types, and incident statuses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the general information about the incident on page 1.
2.  Enter the date and time the incident occurred on page 2.
3.  Provide details about the incident on page 3.
4.  Attach any supporting documents that are relevant to the incident on page 4.
5.  Specify the location where the incident occurred on page 5.
6.  Select the type of incident on page 6.
7.  Choose the current status of the incident on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **General Info (1)**: Enter a brief summary of the incident.
*   **Date of Incident (2)**: Enter the date and time of the incident using the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Incident Details (3)**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
*   **Supporting Documents (4)**: Attach any relevant documents that support your report, such as photos or videos.
*   **Location (5)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Type (6)**: Select 'Yes' if the incident was an actual event, or 'No' if it was not.
*   **Incident Status (7)**: Choose whether the incident is currently Active or Inactive.
 
# Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Double-check the accuracy of your report before submitting.
*   Ensure that you are not reporting duplicate incidents.
*   Attach relevant supporting documents to provide context to your report.
*   Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form.
