# Worker's Compensation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker's Compensation Intake Form is used to gather information about a workers' compensation claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the **Employee Info** section.
2. Describe your injury in the **Injury Details** section.
3. Enter your employer's information in the **Employer Info** section.
4. Specify your medical provider in the **Medical Provider** section.
5. Select the claim status and type from the corresponding dropdown menus.
6. Enter the date of the claim in the **Claim Date** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info (1)**: Enter your name, ID number, or other identifying information as required.
* **Injury Details (3)**: Describe your injury, including the date and circumstances of the incident.
* **Employer Info (4)**: Enter your supervisor's or HR representative's name and contact information.
* **Medical Provider (5)**: Enter the name and contact information of your medical provider.
* **Claim Status (7)**: Select from:
	+ Worker's compensation claim
	+ Other claim
	+ No claim
* **Claim Type (8)**: Select from:
	+ Work-related injury
	+ Work-related illness
	+ Non-work-related injury
	+ Non-work-related illness
* **Claim Date (9)**: Enter the date of the claim in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Employee Injury Info (2)**: Enter any additional information about your injury, including the type of injury and how it occurred.
* **Injury Details (3)**: Enter any further details about your injury, including the severity and impact on your daily activities.
* **Medical Provider (6)**: Enter any relevant medical information about your treatment or diagnosis.
* **Claim Details (7)**: Enter any additional information about your claim, such as claimant name, address, and phone number.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have reviewed and understand the claim process before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, please contact your HR representative for clarification.
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure efficient processing of your claim.
