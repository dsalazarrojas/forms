# Incident Resolution Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document incidents that occur within the organization, including details about the incident, the employees involved, and the resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Incident Information" page.
2. Enter the date of the incident.
3. Provide the location where the incident occurred.
4. Choose the type of incident (e.g. Incident, Injury, Illness, Other).
5. Describe the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
6. Enter the employee's name who was involved in the incident.
7. Enter the employee's email address.
8. Enter the employee's phone number.
9. Enter the manager's name who was involved in the resolution process.
10. Enter the manager's email address.
11. Choose the resolution status (e.g. Resolved, In Progress, Not Resolved).
12. Enter the resolution date.
13. Provide any additional resolution details in the "Resolution Note" field.
14. Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
15. Enter the email address of the incident reporter.
16. Enter the phone number of the incident reporter.
17. Check the box to indicate if the incident report form has been submitted.
18. Enter the date the form was submitted.
19. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
20. Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
21. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident (e.g. Incident, Injury, Illness, Other).
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Employee E-mail**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager involved in the resolution process.
* **Manager E-mail**: Enter the manager's email address.
* **Resolution Status**: Choose the status of the resolution process (e.g. Resolved, In Progress, Not Resolved).
* **Resolution Date**: Enter the date the resolution was completed.
* **Resolution Note**: Provide any additional details about the resolution process.
* **Incident Reporter Name**: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reporter E-mail**: Enter the email address of the incident reporter.
* **Incident Reporter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the incident reporter.
* **Incident Report Form Submitted**: Check the box to indicate if the incident report form has been submitted.
* **Incident Report Form Submitted Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Incident Report Form Submitted By Name**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Incident Report Form Submitted By E-mail**: Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
* **Incident Report Form Submitted By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
