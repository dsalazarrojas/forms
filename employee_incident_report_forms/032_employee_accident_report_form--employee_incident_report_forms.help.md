# Employee Accident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Accident Report Form is a tool to report accidents that occur in the workplace. It is essential to provide accurate and detailed information about the incident to help management understand what happened and take necessary actions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Review the form to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask your supervisor or HR department for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Accident Date
* **Accident Date**: Enter the date the accident occurred in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 2. Accident Time
* **Accident Time**: Enter the time the accident occurred in the format `HH:MM:SS`.

### 3. Accident Location
* **Accident Location**: Enter a brief description of the location where the accident occurred.

### 4. Description
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the accident, including the circumstances leading up to and following the incident.

### 5. Witnesses
* **Witnesses**: Check the box if there were witnesses to the accident. You can also select multiple witnesses if they were present.

### 6. Employee
* **Employee**: Enter your employee ID number.

### 7. Employee ID
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.

## Tips

* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask your supervisor or HR department for assistance.
* Please fill out the form as soon as possible after the accident occurs to ensure accurate information is recorded.
