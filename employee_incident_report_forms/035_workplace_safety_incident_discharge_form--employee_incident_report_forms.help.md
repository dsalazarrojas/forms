# Workplace Safety Incident Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Incident Discharge Form is used to document and track workplace safety incidents. This form is essential for maintaining a safe and healthy work environment, ensuring that incidents are reported and investigated thoroughly, and that corrective actions are taken to prevent similar incidents from happening again.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and complete each section accurately.
2. Fill in the following information for each incident:
	* **Incident Information**: Briefly describe the incident, including the date, time, location, and any witnesses involved.
	* **Incident Details**: Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any injuries sustained and their severity.
	* **Investigation Status**: Select the current status of the investigation (Not Started, In Progress, or Complete).
	* **Incident Reported**: If the incident was reported by an employee, select 'True', otherwise select 'False'.
	* **Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
	* **Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
	* **Workplace Safety Incident**: Briefly describe the incident, focusing on its impact on the workplace.
	* **Investigation Outcome**: Determine the outcome of the investigation (Not Applicable, Minor, or Serious).
	* **Root Cause**: Identify the root cause of the incident.
	* **Corrective Action**: Describe any corrective actions taken or planned to prevent similar incidents.
	* **Preventive Action**: Describe any preventive measures taken or planned to prevent similar incidents.
	* **Employee Involvement**: Indicate the number of employees involved in the incident.
	* **Supervisor Involvement**: Indicate the number of supervisors involved in the incident.
	* **Witness List**: Select all witnesses to the incident (True or False).
	* **Report Status**: Select the current status of the report (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).
	* **Incident Category**: Identify the category of the incident (Near Miss, Actual Harm, or Injury).
	* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident (Slip, Trip, and Fall, Equipment Related, or Other).
	* **Incident Location**: Select the location where the incident occurred (Office, Warehouse, or Other).
	* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
	* **Injury Description**: If an injury was sustained, describe it in detail.
	* **Injured Part**: Identify the part of the body injured.
	* **Medical Attention**: Indicate whether medical attention was sought (False or True).
	* **Medical Provider**: Enter the name of the medical provider (if applicable).
	* **Medical Report**: Enter any relevant medical reports (if applicable).
	* **Submit**: Review and submit the form once complete.
	* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the incident.
	* **Incident ID**: Enter a unique ID for the incident (if applicable).
	* **Date Modified**: Enter the date the incident was last modified (if applicable).
3. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are complete and accurate.

* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are complete.

* Use the incident ID to track and reference each incident.
* Update the date modified field regularly to keep the incident record up-to-date.
