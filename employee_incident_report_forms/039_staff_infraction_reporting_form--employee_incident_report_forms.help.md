# Staff Infraction Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Infraction Reporting Form is used to document and report incidents or infractions involving employees. This form is intended to capture witness details and incident information to facilitate investigations and ensure accurate documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the witness details: Provide the witness's name, email, and phone number to contact them if needed.
2. Describe the incident: Enter a brief description of the incident, including the date and time it occurred.
3. Classify the incident: Choose the category that best describes the incident.
4. Add any further comments or actions taken: Provide any additional information about the incident, including any actions taken to address it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Incident Report Form Fields** (`employee_incident_report_form_fields`):
	+ This is the form's title.
* **Witness Details** (`witness_details`):
	+ Enter a brief description of the witness's role or position in the organization.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`):
	+ Enter the witness's name.
* **Witness Email** (`witness_email`):
	+ Enter the witness's email address. This field is used to contact the witness if needed.
* **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`):
	+ Enter the witness's phone number. This field is optional.
* **Witness Location** (`witness_location`):
	+ Enter the location where the witness was present during the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`):
	+ Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`):
	+ Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Evidence Type** (`evidence_type`):
	+ Enter a brief description of any evidence collected during the investigation.
* **Incident Evidence Description** (`evidence_description`):
	+ Provide a detailed description of the evidence collected during the investigation.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`):
	+ Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`):
	+ Enter a brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Category** (`incident_category`):
	+ Choose the category that best describes the incident (e.g., "Category 1," "Category 2," "Category 3").
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`):
	+ Enter any actions taken as a result of the incident.
* **Investigation Status** (`investigation_status`):
	+ Choose the status of the investigation (e.g., "Status 1," "Status 2," "Status 3").
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`):
	+ Enter the person who submitted the report.
* **Witness Position** (`witness_position`):
	+ Enter the witness's job title or role in the organization.
* **Witness Department** (`witness_department`):
	+ Enter the department where the witness is employed.
* **Incident Date In** (`incident_date_in`):
	+ Enter the date the incident started.
* **Incident Time In** (`incident_time_in`):
	+ Enter the time the incident started.
* **Out Incident Date** (`incident_date_out`):
	+ Enter the date the incident ended.
* **Out Incident Time** (`incident_time_out`):
	+ Enter the time the incident ended.
* **Incident Start Time** (`incident_start_time`):
	+ Enter the start time of the incident.
* **Incident End Time** (`incident_end_time`):
	+ Enter the end time of the incident.
