# Manufacturing Workplace Violence Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and report workplace violence incidents that occur in a manufacturing setting, ensuring a timely and appropriate response.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the date the incident occurred.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident location.
3. Describe the incident in detail, including any injuries or damage sustained.
4. List the names and contact information of any witnesses.
5. Provide the name and contact information of the incident reporting officer.
6. Select the escalation level for the incident (if applicable).
7. Select the status of the incident (if applicable).
8. Provide the name and contact information of the incident reporting officer.
9. Enter the cost of any damage or injury sustained (if applicable).
10. Describe the witness statements (if any).
