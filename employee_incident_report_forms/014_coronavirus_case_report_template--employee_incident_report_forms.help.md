# Coronavirus Case Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coronavirus Case Report Template is a tool for reporting suspected or confirmed cases of COVID-19 among employees. This form helps in gathering essential information for prompt response and action.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "Employee" question with one or more of the provided options: "Yes" or "No".
2. If you answered "Yes", enter your name in the "employee_name" field.
3. If applicable, enter your workplace location in the "workplace_location" field.
4. If applicable, enter the community location where you were exposed to COVID-19 in the "community_location" field.
5. If you can, try to recall the date you suspected you contracted or were exposed to COVID-19 in the "suspected_date" field.
6. Enter any symptoms you have experienced related to COVID-19 in the "symptoms" field.
7. If you reported this case, enter your name and contact information in the "reported_by" and "contact_information" fields.
8. Enter the date you reported this case in the "reported_date" field.
9. Finally, fill in the "coronavirus_case_report_template_[1-16]" fields with any additional information about your case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee**: Select "Yes" if you are an employee and have reason to believe you have contracted or been exposed to COVID-19.
* **employee_name**: Enter your name if you answered "Yes" to the "Employee" question.
* **workplace_location**: Enter the name of your workplace, if applicable.
* **community_location**: Enter the community where you were exposed to COVID-19, if applicable.
* **suspected_date**: Try to recall the date you suspected you contracted or were exposed to COVID-19, if known.
* **symptoms**: Enter any symptoms you have experienced related to COVID-19.
* **reported_by**: Enter your name for reporting this case, if you reported it.
* **contact_information**: Enter your contact information, if you reported the case.
* **reported_date**: Enter the date you reported this case.
* **coronavirus_case_report_template_1-16**: These fields are for any additional information about your case. You can use them to provide further details about your situation.
