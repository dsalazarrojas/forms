# Injury Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Injury Incident Form is designed to gather information about incidents or injuries that occur within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date of the incident from the calendar.
2. Enter the time of the incident.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident in the space provided.
4. Describe the injury that occurred in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Incident Report Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can report the incident.
* **Incident Date**: The date of the incident. Please select the date from the calendar provided.
* **Incident Time**: The time of the incident. Please enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
* **Incident Details**: A brief description of what happened during the incident. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Injury Description**: A description of the injury that occurred. Please include any relevant details, such as the location of the injury and the severity of the injury.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any information, please ask for help or clarification.
- This form is confidential and will be reviewed and handled by authorized personnel only.
