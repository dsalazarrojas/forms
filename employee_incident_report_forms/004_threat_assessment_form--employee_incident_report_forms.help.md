# Threat Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Threat Assessment Form is used to collect information about a potential or ongoing threat to an individual or organization's safety and security. This form helps assess the severity and type of threat, allowing for informed decision-making and proactive measures to mitigate risks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form sections below to understand what information is required for each section.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info**: This section is used to collect general information about the individual or organization affected by the threat. Provide the employee's name, title, and any other relevant details.
* **Risk Factor**: Enter a numerical value to indicate the level of risk associated with this threat (e.g., 1-10).
* **Threat Level**: Select the level of threat from the list:
	+ Low: A minor threat with no immediate danger.
	+ Medium: A moderate threat that may cause some harm or disruption.
	+ High: A significant threat that may cause substantial harm or disruption.
	+ Critical: A severe threat that may cause extreme harm or disruption.
* **Threat Type**: Select all applicable types of threat from the list:
	+ Cyber Threat: A threat related to computer systems or networks.
	+ Physical Threat: A threat related to physical harm or violence.
	+ Human Threat: A threat from an individual or group.
* **Threat Area**: Select the area affected by the threat from the list:
	+ Network: A threat related to computer networks.
	+ System: A threat related to computer systems.
	+ Physical: A threat related to physical assets or people.
* **Risk Area**: Select all applicable areas affected by the threat from the list:
	+ Network: A threat related to computer networks.
	+ System: A threat related to computer systems.
	+ Physical: A threat related to physical assets or people.
* **Risk Factor Area**: Select the area where the risk factor is most relevant from the list:
	+ Network: A threat related to computer networks.
	+ System: A threat related to computer systems.
	+ Physical: A threat related to physical assets or people.
* **Employee Info**: This section is used to collect any additional information about the employee affected by the threat.
* **Threat Info**: This section is used to collect any additional information about the threat itself.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or explanations about the threat.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the individual or organization affected by the threat.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the individual or organization affected by the threat.
