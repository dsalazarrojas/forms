# Virtual Training Program Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through how to complete the Virtual Training Program Effectiveness Survey form, which is designed to gather feedback on the virtual training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Rate your overall satisfaction with the virtual training program.
2. Evaluate the quality of the virtual session.
3. Indicate the relevance of the virtual training program to your goals.
4. List any challenges or obstacles that you encountered during the virtual session.
5. Provide suggestions for improving the program.
6. Rate the performance of the virtual trainer.
7. Rate your satisfaction with the virtual session.
8. Offer any additional feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction (1)**: This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the virtual training program. Select one of the following options:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Session Quality (2)**: This question asks you to evaluate the quality of the virtual session. Select one of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **Relevance (3)**: This question asks you to indicate how relevant the virtual training program is to your goals. Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Challenges (4)**: This question asks you to list any challenges or obstacles that you encountered during the virtual session.
* **Suggestions (5)**: This question asks you to provide suggestions for improving the virtual training program.
* **Trainer Performance (7)**: This question asks you to rate the performance of the virtual trainer. Select one of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Session Satisfaction (8)**: This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the virtual session. Select one of the following options:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Email Address (10)**: This question asks you to provide your email address.
* **Feedback (11)**: This question asks you to provide any additional feedback or suggestions.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Provide specific examples or details to support your answers whenever possible.
* If you have any concerns or complaints, please make sure to include them in your feedback.
