# Leadership Seminar Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on the leadership seminar, helping us gauge the trainers' performance, content quality, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question with a rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
2. Rate each section based on your experience and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Trainer's Performance** (`trainer_rating`, `number`, required/true): Rate the trainer's performance, including their ability to engage, communicate effectively, and answer questions. 
- **Content Relevance** (`content_rating`, `number`, required/true): Rate how relevant and useful the training content was, considering your expectations and learning outcomes. 
- **Materials Provided** (`materials_provided`, `number`, required/true): Rate the quality and completeness of materials provided during the seminar, such as handouts or presentations. 
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required/true): Rate your overall satisfaction with the seminar, including all aspects of the training experience.
