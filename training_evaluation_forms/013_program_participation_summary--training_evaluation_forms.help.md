# program_participation_summary - Help Guide
## Purpose
The program_participation_summary form is designed to collect data on participants' program experience, including their participation status, satisfaction level, feedback, and demographic information. This form is meant to be filled out by participants after completing a program or training session, and it's intended to gather valuable insights for program evaluation and improvement purposes.

This form is not meant to be used for any other purpose than program evaluation and participant feedback, and it's essential to ensure that the participant fills out the form only once per program or session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have participated in the program or training session you're being asked to summarize.
2. Fill out the required fields, including participant name, program outcome, feedback, and completion status.
3. Provide your satisfaction level and select the relevant options for program participation summary form comments and form completion status.
4. Enter the date and time of your program participation start and end, as well as your participant start and end times.
5. Enter your total hours spent in the program.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments about your program participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name, as this will be used to identify you as a participant. (Text, Optional)
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes. (Email, Required)
* **Program Participation Summary**: Describe your experience in the program or training session. (Text, Required)
* **Program Outcome**: Summarize the outcome or result of your participation in the program. (Text, Required)
* **Feedback**: Provide any feedback or suggestions you have about the program or training session. (Text, Optional)
* **Program Participation Summary Form Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the program participation summary form itself. (Text, Optional)
* **Participant Satisfaction**: Select your level of satisfaction with the program or training session. (Select One, Required)
* **Program Participation Summary Form Comments**: Select any additional comments or feedback about the program participation summary form. (Select Multiple, Optional)
* **Form Completion Status**: Indicate whether you have completed the form or not. (Select One, Required)
* **Program Participation Summary Form Date**: Enter the date of your program participation. (Date, Required)
* **Program Participation Summary Form Time**: Enter the time of your program participation. (Time, Required)
* **Participant Program Participation Date Start**: Enter the start date of your program participation. (Date, Required)
* **Participant Program Participation Time Start**: Enter the start time of your program participation. (Time, Required)
* **Participant Program Participation Date End**: Enter the end date of your program participation. (Date, Required)
* **Participant Program Participation Time End**: Enter the end time of your program participation. (Time, Required)
* **Participant Start Time**: Enter your start time in the program. (Time, Required)
* **Participant End Time**: Enter your end time in the program. (Time, Required)
* **Participant Duration**: Enter the duration of your program participation. (Number, Required)
* **Participant Total Hours**: Enter the total hours you spent in the program. (Number, Required)
* **Program Participation Summary Form Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the program participation. (Text, Optional)
* **Participant Program Participation Form Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your program participation. (Text, Optional)
* **Program Participation Summary Form Program Name**: Enter the name of the program or training session you participated in. (Text, Optional)
* **Participant Program Participation Form Program Name**: Enter the name of the program or training session you participated in. (Text, Optional)
* **Form Participation Summary Form ID**: Enter the ID of the program participation summary form. (Text, Optional)

This guide is designed to help you complete the program_participation_summary form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
