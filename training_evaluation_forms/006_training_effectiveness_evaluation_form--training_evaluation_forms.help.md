# Training Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of a training session. It is used to gather feedback from participants and assess the overall quality of the training. The feedback provided will help us improve our training programs and make them more effective for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "participant name" field.
2. Provide your feedback on the training session in the "feedback" field.
3. Rate the effectiveness of the training using the scale provided in the "rating scale" field.
4. Rate the overall effectiveness of the training by entering a number between 1 and 5 in the "overall effectiveness rating" field.
5. List any areas where you feel the training could be improved in the "areas of improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant name`, text, required): This is where you should enter your name so we know who is providing the feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please provide any comments or suggestions you have about the training session.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Use this field to rate the effectiveness of the training, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating scale`, text, optional): This field allows you to select the scale to use for your rating.
* **Overall Effectiveness Rating** (`overall effectiveness rating`, number, optional): Enter a number between 1 and 5 to rate the overall effectiveness of the training.
* **Areas of Improvement** (`areas of improvement`, text, optional): List any specific areas where you feel the training could be improved.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so we can understand your concerns.
* Use the rating scale provided to ensure consistency in your ratings.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the "areas of improvement" field.
