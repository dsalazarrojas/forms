# Advanced Endobariatric Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of the Advanced Endobariatric Course, helping course organizers and instructors evaluate and improve the course content, instruction, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your information as an attendee
2. Select your professional role in the medical field
3. Indicate your years of experience in your field
4. Enter the title of the course you attended
5. Specify the date and location of the course
6. Evaluate the relevance and technical accuracy of the course content
7. Assess the quality of the instructor's presentation skills and knowledge
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the course

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Professional Role**: Select your role in the medical field, such as Surgeon, Gastroenterologist, Nurse, or others.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been working in your field.
* **Course Title**: Enter the name of the course you attended, as given.
* **Course Date**: Enter the date the course took place, including the month and year.
* **Course Location**: Enter the location where the course was held.
* **Relevance to Practice**: Select how relevant the course content was to your daily practice, from Extremely relevant to Not relevant.
* **Technical Accuracy**: Evaluate the instructor's technical knowledge, from Highly accurate to Inaccurate.
* **Depth of Content**: Assess the level of detail in the course content, from Too detailed to Not detailed enough.
* **Instructor Knowledge**: Select how knowledgeable the instructor was about the subject matter.
* **Presentation Skills**: Rate the instructor's presentation skills, from Exceptional to Poor.
* **Engagement Level**: Assess how engaging the instructor's presentation was.
* **Knowledge Gained**: Rate how much new knowledge you gained from the course, from Extensive amount to No new knowledge.
* **Confidence Increase**: Select if the course increased your confidence in endobariatric procedures.
* **Overall Recommendation**: Indicate if you would recommend this course to your colleagues.

**Tips**:
- Be honest and detailed in your feedback, as it will help course organizers and instructors improve the course content and instruction.
- If you're unsure about a particular field, select the option that best reflects your opinion.
- Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time to complete this form.
