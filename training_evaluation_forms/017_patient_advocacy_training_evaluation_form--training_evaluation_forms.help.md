# Patient Advocacy Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on their experience with the Patient Advocacy Training program. Your input will help us understand what you found useful and what areas we should focus on improving.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer each question honestly and based on your actual experience with the program.
* You can choose one or multiple options for each question, depending on your response.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Comments" field.
* You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your experience with the program.
