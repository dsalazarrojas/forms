# Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from trainees who have attended the Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course. The feedback will help us improve the course content, instructor performance, and overall training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer all required fields, marked as "Required".
2. Provide your honest feedback on the course content, instructor performance, and overall experience.
3. Rate the course using the rating fields, if applicable.
4. Share your thoughts on how the course can be improved.
5. Check the "Agree to Share" options to decide if you want to share your feedback with the instructor, training center, employer, or others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor Performance Rating**: Rate the performance of the instructor, using the numerical rating system from 1 to 10.
* **Course Rated (1-10)**: Rate the overall quality of the course, using the numerical rating system from 1 to 10.
* **Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Feedback**: Provide your thoughts on the course content and overall experience.
* **Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Suggestions**: Suggest ways to improve the course content.
* **Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Rating**: Rate the course as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you are with the course, using the multiple-choice options "Satisfied", "Very Satisfied", "Neutral", "Dissatisfied", or "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Improvement**: Share your thoughts on how the course can be improved.
* **Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Comments**: Provide any additional comments on the course.
* **Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments on the course.
* **Agree to Improve Course**: Check "Yes" if you agree to help improve the course.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments on the course.
* **Agree to Share with Instructor**: Check "Yes" if you agree to share your feedback with the instructor.
* **Agree to Share with Training Center**: Check "Yes" if you agree to share your feedback with the training center.
* **Agree to Share with Employer**: Check "Yes" if you agree to share your feedback with your employer.
* **Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Employer Comments**: Provide any comments on your employer's participation in the course.
* **Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Training Center Comments**: Provide any comments on the training center's participation in the course.
* **Agree to Recommend Course**: Check "Yes" if you agree to recommend the course to others.
* **Agree to Recommend to Colleagues**: Check "Yes" if you agree to recommend the course to your colleagues.
* **Agree to Recommend on Social Media**: Check "Yes" if you agree to recommend the course on social media.
* **Agree to Recommend to Friends and Family**: Check "Yes" if you agree to recommend the course to your friends and family.
* **Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Employer Participation**: Check "Yes" if your employer participated in the course.
* **Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Training Center Participation**: Check "Yes" if the training center participated in the course.
* **Agree to Share with Others**: Check "Yes" if you agree to share your feedback with others.

Note: The fields marked as "Required" are essential to complete the form and provide feedback. Please make sure to answer all required fields to help us improve the course.
