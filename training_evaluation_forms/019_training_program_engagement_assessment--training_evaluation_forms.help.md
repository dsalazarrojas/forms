# Training Program Engagement Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and suggestions from attendees to improve the training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have a clear understanding of each question.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Fill in the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label>Introduction</label> (1, Introduction, Note, Optional)
* This is a general introduction to the form. Please feel free to provide any comments or feedback about the training program.

### <label>Rating Scale</label> (2, Rating Scale, Select One, Optional)
* Please rate your overall satisfaction with the training program using the scale:
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Satisfied
	+ 5: Very Satisfied

### <label>Engagement Level</label> (3, Engagement Level, Select One, Optional)
* Please rate your engagement level with the training program:
	+ Low: You did not feel engaged or interested in the training program.
	+ Medium: You were somewhat engaged, but there was room for improvement.
	+ High: You were highly engaged and interested in the training program.

### <label>Learning Objectives</label> (4, Learning Objectives, Note, Optional)
* Please share what you learned from the training program. This could include new skills, knowledge, or insights gained.

### <label>Feedback</label> (5, Feedback, Note, Optional)
* Please provide any additional feedback about the training program.

### <label>Suggestions</label> (6, Suggestions, Note, Optional)
* Please share any suggestions for improvement for future training programs.

### <label>Overall Satisfaction</label> (7, Overall Satisfaction, Select One, Optional)
* Please rate your overall satisfaction with the training program:
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Satisfied
	+ 5: Very Satisfied

### <label>Will Attend Next</label> (8, Will Attend Next, Select One, Optional)
* Would you like to attend the next training session?
	+ Yes
	+ No

### <label>Will Recommend</label> (9, Will Recommend, Select One, Optional)
* Would you recommend this training program to others?
	+ Yes
	+ No

### <label>Will Attend Next Session</label> (10, Will Attend Next Session, Select One, Optional)
* Will you attend the next session of this training program?
	+ Yes
	+ No

### <label>Attended Previous Sessions</label> (11, Attended Previous Sessions, Select One, Optional)
* Have you attended any previous sessions of this training program?
	+ Yes
	+ No

### <label>Session Date</label> (12, Session Date, Date, Optional)
* Please enter the date you attended the training session.

### <label>Session Start Time</label> (13, Session Start Time, Time, Optional)
* Please enter the start time of the training session.

### <label>Session Duration</label> (14, Session Duration, Number, Optional)
* Please enter the duration of the training session.

### <label>Session Length</label> (15, Session Length, Number, Optional)
* Please enter the length of the training session.

### <label>Training Programs Attended</label> (16, Training Programs Attended, Number, Optional)
* Please enter the number of training programs you have attended.

## Tips
* Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is for your feedback and suggestions, so please be honest and constructive in your responses.
