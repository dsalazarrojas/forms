# DAT Trainee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DAT Trainee Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from trainees who have participated in the Diagnostic and Assessment Training (DAT) program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each question on "Trainee Survey Page 4" as required.
2. Enter your Trainee ID, Name, and Email (if available).
3. Provide details about your training experience on "Trainee Survey Page 1".
4. Select the start and end dates of your training on "Trainee Survey Page 2".
5. Enter the location of your training on "Trainee Survey Page 2".
6. Provide any additional notes about your experience on "Trainee Survey Page 3".
7. Finally, select the evaluation options for "Trainee Survey Page 3" and "Trainee Survey Page 4".


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainee ID** (Trainee ID, text, required/optional): Enter your unique identifier assigned by the training program.
* **Name** (Name, text, required/optional): Enter your name, if available.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, if available.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your experience.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date you started your training.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date you ended your training.
* **Location** (Location, text, required/optional): Enter the location where you received your training.
* **Evaluation** (Evaluation, select_one, required): Select the primary reason for your evaluation.
* **Evaluation** (Evaluation, select_multiple, required): Select all the applicable reasons for your evaluation.
* **Evaluation** (Evaluation, select_multiple, required): Select all the applicable reasons for your evaluation.

Please note that the evaluation fields are required for multiple pages, ensuring that trainees provide comprehensive feedback.
