# Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from trainees about their experience with a trainer or training session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing detailed answers to all questions.
2. Select the most accurate options for select-one and select-multiple fields.
3. Be as descriptive as possible in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainer Professionism** (`Trainer Professionism`, text, required): 
  Please describe the trainer's professionalism during the session.
* **Communication** (`Communication`, text, optional): 
  Describe the trainer's communication style and effectiveness.
* **Motivation** (`Motivation`, text, required): 
  Explain what motivated you to participate in the training session.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, text, required): 
  Rate your overall satisfaction with the training session.
* **Training Environment** (`Training Environment`, text, optional): 
  Describe the physical environment where the training took place.
* **Instructor Professionism** (`Instructor Professionism`, select-one, required): 
  Select the level of the trainer's professionism (None, Other).
* **Instructor Professionalsm** (`Instructor Professionalsm`, select-multiple, optional):

* **Communication Importance** (`Communication Importance`, text, optional):
