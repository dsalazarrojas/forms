# Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from trainees about their experience with a trainer or training session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing detailed answers to all questions.
2. Select the most accurate options for select-one and select-multiple fields.
3. Be as descriptive as possible in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainer Professionism** (`Trainer Professionism`, text, required): 
  Please describe the trainer's professionalism during the session.
* **Communication** (`Communication`, text, optional): 
  Describe the trainer's communication style and effectiveness.
* **Motivation** (`Motivation`, text, required): 
  Explain what motivated you to participate in the training session.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, text, required): 
  Rate your overall satisfaction with the training session.
* **Training Environment** (`Training Environment`, text, optional): 
  Describe the physical environment where the training took place.
* **Instructor Professionism** (`Instructor Professionism`, select-one, required): 
  Select the level of the trainer's professionism (None, Other).
* **Instructor Professionalsm** (`Instructor Professionalsm`, select-multiple, optional): 
  Select all applicable skills or qualities that the trainer demonstrated (None, Other).
* **Communication Importance** (`Communication Importance`, text, optional): 
  Describe how important communication was to your experience.
* **Trainer Professionalsm** (`Trainer Professionalsm`, select-multiple, required): 
  Select all applicable skills or qualities that the trainer demonstrated (None, Other).
* **Communication Style** (`Communication Style`, select-one, optional): 
  Select the trainer's communication style (None, Other).
* **Training Facility** (`Training Facility`, text, optional): 
  Describe the physical facility where the training took place.
* **Training Equipment** (`Training Equipment`, text, optional): 
  Describe the equipment used during the training session.
* **Training Schedule** (`Training Schedule`, text, optional): 
  Describe the training schedule and its fit for you.
* **Instructor Support** (`Instructor Support`, select-multiple, optional): 
  Select all applicable types of support that the trainer provided (None, Other).
* **Feedback Frequency** (`Feedback Frequency`, text, optional): 
  Describe how frequently the trainer provided feedback.
* **Communication Skills** (`Communication Skills`, text, optional):

* **Personal Attention** (`Personal Attention`, text, optional):

* **Feedback Quality** (`Feedback Quality`, text, optional): 
  Describe the quality of the feedback provided by the trainer.
* **Training Environment Climate** (`Training Environment Climate`, text, optional): 
  Describe the training environment climate.
* **Overall Experience** (`Overall Experience`, text, optional): 
  Describe your overall experience with the training session.
* **Trainer Satisfaction** (`Trainer Satisfaction`, text, optional): 
  Rate your satisfaction with the trainer.
* **Instructor Supports** (`Instructor Supports`, select-multiple, optional):

  Describe how important communication was to your experience.
* **Trainer Attitude** (`Trainer Attitude`, text, optional): 
  Describe the trainer's attitude.
* **Overall Quality** (`Overall Quality`, text, optional): 
  Rate the overall quality of the training session.
