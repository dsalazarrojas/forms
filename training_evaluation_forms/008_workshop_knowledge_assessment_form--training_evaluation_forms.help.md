# Workshop Knowledge Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Knowledge Assessment Form is used to evaluate participants' knowledge and skills acquired during a workshop. This form is completed by trainers to provide feedback on the participant's performance, helping to identify areas of improvement and areas of strength.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details section: Enter the participant's name, email, and phone number (if available). This section is meant to gather information about the participant.
2. Select the session attended: Choose the session that the participant attended from the dropdown menu. This will help trainers identify the specific session that the participant was assessed during.
3. Evaluate the participant's workshop knowledge: Provide an evaluation of the participant's workshop knowledge, skills, and attitude in the text field.
4. Evaluate the participant's session consistency: Provide an evaluation of the participant's session consistency in the text field.
5. Evaluate the trainer: Provide feedback on the trainer's performance, including their skills and attitude.
6. Add any additional comments: If there's anything additional the trainer wants to comment on, use this text field.
7. Rate the overall performance: Select Excellent, Good, or Fair from the dropdown menu to rate the participant's overall performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**
	+ **Name**: Enter the participant's name.
	+ **Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
	+ **Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number (if available).
* **Session Attended**
	+ Select the session that the participant attended.
* **Workshop Knowledge**
	+ Evaluate the participant's workshop knowledge, skills, and attitude.
* **Session Consistency**
	+ Evaluate the participant's session consistency.
* **Trainer Evaluation**
	+ Evaluate the trainer's performance, including their skills and attitude.
* **Feedback**
	+ Provide any feedback or comments on the trainer's performance.
* **Overall**
	+ Rate the participant's overall performance as Excellent, Good, or Fair.
* **Comments**
	+ Provide any additional comments on the participant's performance.
