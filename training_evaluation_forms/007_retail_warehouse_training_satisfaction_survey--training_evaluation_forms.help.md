# Retail Warehouse Training Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Warehouse Training Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from trainees on their experience with the retail warehouse training program. The purpose of this form is to collect information on the quality of the training provided, including the trainer's effectiveness, the training materials, and the overall safety coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Select a rating from 1 to 10 for each question on pages 1-3 (Training Quality, Safety Coverage, and Trainer Effectiveness).
* Type in your response to each question on pages 4-9 (Training Materials, Safety Training, Trainer Communication, Trainer Support, Training Environment, and Overall Satisfaction).
* Use the "Additional Comments" field on page 10 to provide any additional feedback or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Quality** (`page_1`): Rate the quality of the training you received on a scale from 1 to 10. Please provide a rating that reflects your overall experience.
	+ Page 1: This question assesses how well the training met your expectations.
* **Safety Coverage** (`page_2`): Rate the coverage of safety information provided during the training on a scale from 1 to 10. Please provide a rating that reflects your overall experience.
	+ Page 2: This question evaluates how comprehensive the safety information was.
* **Trainer Effectiveness** (`page_3`): Rate the effectiveness of the trainer on a scale from 1 to 10. Please provide a rating that reflects your overall experience.
	+ Page 3: This question assesses how well the trainer communicated and facilitated the training.
* **Training Materials** (`page_4`): Describe any materials or resources that were provided during the training. Be as specific as possible.
	+ Page 4: This question is an open-ended field for you to provide details about the materials or resources used.
* **Safety Training** (`page_5`): Describe your experience with safety training during the retail warehouse training. Be as specific as possible.
	+ Page 5: This question is an open-ended field for you to provide details about your experience with safety training.
* **Trainer Communication** (`page_6`): Describe how well the trainer communicated with you during the training. Be as specific as possible.
	+ Page 6: This question is an open-ended field for you to provide details about the trainer's communication style.
* **Trainer Support** (`page_7`): Describe how well the trainer supported you during the training. Be as specific as possible.
	+ Page 7: This question is an open-ended field for you to provide details about the trainer's support.
* **Training Environment** (`page_8`): Describe your experience with the training environment. Be as specific as possible.
	+ Page 8: This question is an open-ended field for you to provide details about the training environment.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`page_9`): Describe your overall satisfaction with the training. Be as specific as possible.
	+ Page 9: This question is an open-ended field for you to provide details about your overall satisfaction.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_10`): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the training. Be as specific as possible.
	+ Page 10: This is an open-ended field for you to provide any additional comments or feedback.
