# Personal Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Training Evaluation Form is used to track and evaluate the progress of personal training programs. This form is completed by trainers after each training session to provide an assessment of the client's progress and provide feedback for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the client, such as their first name, last name, date of birth, email, and client name.
2. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Trainer Name" field.
4. Enter the training start date and end date in the "Training Start Date" and "Training End Date" fields, respectively.
5. Select the training duration using the "Training Duration" field.
6. Enter the date of evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" field.
7. Select the evaluation time using the "Evaluation Time" field.
8. Assess the client's progress as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" in the "Progress" field.
9. Enter any feedback or comments in the "Feedback" field.
10. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" and "Evaluation Comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter the client's first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the client's last name (if available).
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter the client's date of birth.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Name** (Text, Required): Enter the client's full name.
* **Trainer Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name.
* **Training Start Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date when the training started.
* **Training End Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date when the training ended.
* **Training Duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the training (in hours or days).
* **Evaluation Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (Time, Required): Select the time of the evaluation.
* **Progress** (Select One, Required): Assess the client's progress as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Feedback** (Text, Optional): Enter any feedback or comments about the client's progress.
* **Comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the client's progress.
* **Evaluation Comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any evaluation comments or notes about the client's progress.
