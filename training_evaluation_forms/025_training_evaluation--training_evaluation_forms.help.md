# Training Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and evaluations from trainees after completing a training session. It helps trainers and training administrators understand the effectiveness of the training and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Evaluation Form**: This field is used to enter your comments about the training evaluation.
* **Trainer Name**: Please enter the name of the trainer who provided the training.
* **Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the training on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Feedback**: Please enter any comments or concerns you had during the training session.
* **Course Name**: Please enter the name of the course you attended.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date you completed the training session.
* **Comments**: Please enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the training session.
* **Additional Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional information or suggestions you have about the training session.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Use specific examples or incidents to support your comments.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide concrete and actionable advice.
* Do not repeat any feedback or comments already provided by other trainees.
