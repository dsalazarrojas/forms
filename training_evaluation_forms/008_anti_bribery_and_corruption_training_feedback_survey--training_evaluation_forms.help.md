# Anti Bribery And Corruption Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their experience with the Anti Bribery And Corruption Training. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure that the training is effective in achieving its objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly, making sure to answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Use the provided options for each question to select your responses.
3. If a question does not apply to you, select the relevant option (e.g. "Not very relevant", "Not at all", etc.).
4. For the "Suggestions for Improvement" and "Additional Comments" sections, provide any additional feedback or ideas you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`1`): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Department** (`2`): Select your department or division from the list provided.
* **Job Level** (`3`): Choose your position level from the list provided.
* **Training Date** (`4`): Enter the date you attended the training.
* **Training Format** (`5`): Select how the training was delivered (e.g. in-person workshop, virtual live session, self-paced online, etc.).
* **Training Duration** (`6`): Select the length of the training session.
* **Content Quality Rating** (`7`): Rate the quality of the training content.
* **Content Relevance** (`8`): Rate how relevant the content was to your work.
* **Trainer Effectiveness** (`9`): Rate the effectiveness of the trainer or instructor.
* **Learning Objectives Clarity** (`10`): Rate how clear the learning objectives were.
* **Knowledge Gained** (`11`): Rate how much you learned from the training.
* **Topics Covered** (`12`): This section is not applicable for your input. You can move on.
* **Understanding of Bribery Definitions** (`13`): Indicate your level of understanding of what constitutes bribery.
* **Ability to Recognize Corruption** (`14`): Rate your ability to identify corrupt practices.
* **Understanding of Reporting Procedures** (`15`): Rate your understanding of how to report incidents.
* **Usefulness of Case Studies** (`16`): Rate the helpfulness of the case studies provided.
* **Quality of Training Materials** (`17`): Rate the quality of the handouts and resources provided.
* **Training Pace** (`18`): Rate the pace of the training session.
* **Confidence in Applying Knowledge** (`19`): Rate how confident you are in applying what you learned.
* **Would Recommend** (`20`): Indicate whether you would recommend this training to colleagues.
* **Areas Needing Additional Training** (`21`): Select areas that require additional training.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`22`): Provide any suggestions for improving the training.
* **Additional Comments** (`23`): Any additional feedback you may have.
* **Willing to Participate in Follow-up** (`24`): Indicate your willingness to participate in a follow-up session.

Note: Please be as thorough as possible when filling out this form, and feel free to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
