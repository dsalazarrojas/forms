# Youth Work Methods Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from participants after a training session. The goal is to measure the overall satisfaction with the session, gather session feedback, collect information about the session topics, and get suggestions from the participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for "Overall Satisfaction" that best describes your experience with the session.
2. Choose the session topics that were covered during the session.
3. Select your demographic information.
4. Leave any additional suggestions or comments in the "Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction, select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to rate your overall satisfaction with the session. Choose one of the options that best describes your experience: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
- **Session Topics** (Session Topics, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to select the session topics that were covered during the session. Choose all the topics that apply to you.
- **Participant Demographics** (Participant Demographics, select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to select your demographic information. Choose one of the options that best describes you: Age Range, Role Participating, Training Level, or Years Training.
- **Session Feedback** (Session Feedback, note, required/optional): This field is a text area where you can leave any feedback about the session.
- **Suggestions** (Suggestions, note, required/optional): This field is a text area where you can leave any suggestions about the training session.

## Tips
- Please select all applicable session topics.
- Please answer all demographic questions truthfully.
- Be specific with your feedback and suggestions, so that the trainers can understand and improve the session.
- If you have any additional comments, please leave them in the "Suggestions" field.
