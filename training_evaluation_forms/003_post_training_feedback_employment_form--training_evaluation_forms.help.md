# Post Training Feedback Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from trainees on their experiences, skills gained, and employment outcomes after completing a training session. Your input will help trainers and instructors evaluate the effectiveness of their programs and make improvements for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the sections below, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Please fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form** (1, text, required): This section is for providing feedback on your overall experience with the training session. Please describe your thoughts, comments, or suggestions about the training.
* **Employment Outcomes** (2, text, optional): If applicable, please share any positive or negative employment outcomes you experienced as a result of the training, such as job opportunities or new career paths.
* **Employment Form** (3, text, optional): This is a duplicate field that seems to be a leftover from the form design. Please ignore it and do not fill it out.

**Tips**

* Be honest and thorough in your feedback to help us improve our training programs.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, you can email them to [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for further evaluation.
* Your input is greatly appreciated, and we value your time and expertise in helping us improve our training programs.
