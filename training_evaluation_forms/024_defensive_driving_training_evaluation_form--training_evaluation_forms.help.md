# Defensive Driving Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Defensive Driving Training Evaluation Form is a tool to gather feedback from students on their experience with a defensive driving training course. This form helps assess the quality and effectiveness of the course, instructor performance, and overall satisfaction with the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the course name where you attended the training.
* Enter the name of the instructor who led the training.
* Choose how satisfied you were with the course content.
* Provide feedback on the instructor's performance and course organization.
* Indicate what materials were used during the training.
* Enter any additional feedback you may have about the course.
* If applicable, provide the date and time of the training.
* Enter the location where the training took place.
* Enter the number of attendees and students who attended the training.
* Provide the number of instructors who led the training.
* Enter the length of the course.
* Enter the evaluation date and time.
* Enter the location where the evaluation took place.
* If you have an email, please enter it for further contact.
* Enter a phone number if you have one.
* If the course you are evaluating is not in our records, enter the course ID or name.
* If you have feedback about another course, enter the course name and instructor name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course you attended.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor who led the training.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you were with the course content (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Course Content** (`course_content`, text, required): Provide feedback on the course content.
* **Instructor Performance** (`instructor_performance`, text, required): Provide feedback on the instructor's performance.
* **Course Organization** (`course_organization`, text, required): Provide feedback on the course organization.
* **Instructor Clarity** (`instructor_clarity`, text, required): Provide feedback on the instructor's clarity.
* **Materials** (`materials`, select_multiple, required): Indicate what materials were used during the training (Whiteboard, Handouts, Online Materials, Other).
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide any additional feedback you may have about the course.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you attended the training.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you attended the training.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where the training took place.
* **Number of Attendees** (`number_of_attendees`, number, required): Enter the number of attendees who attended the training.
* **Number of Students** (`number_of_students`, number, required): Enter the number of students who attended the training.
* **Number of Instructors** (`number_of_instructors`, number, required): Enter the number of instructors who led the training.
* **Course Length** (`course_length`, number, required): Enter the length of the course.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required): Enter the time of this evaluation.
* **Evaluation Location** (`evaluation_location`, text, required): Enter the location where this evaluation took place.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for further contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number for further contact.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, text, required): Enter the course ID or name if not in our records.
* **Other Course Name** (`course_name_other`, text, required): Enter the name of another course if you have feedback.
* **Other Instructor Name** (`instructor_name_other`, text, required): Enter the name of the other instructor if you have feedback.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
