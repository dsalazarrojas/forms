# Patient Safety Leadership Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the Patient Safety Leadership Training program and gather feedback from participants. The evaluation will help us understand how effective the training was and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Please fill in your **Participant Name** in the first field.
2. Enter the **Session ID**, which is the unique identifier for this training session.
3. Select the date you attended the **Training Date**.
4. Rate your **Overall Rated Satisfaction** and **Overall Rated Quality** by selecting a number between 1 and 5.
5. Provide your **Feedback Comments** about the training, if any.
6. Select your **Participant Rating** and **Participant Rating Scale** by choosing from the available options.
7. Rate the **Training Leadership** and select your rating from the **Training Leadership Scale**.
8. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
9. Optionally, provide any **Feedback to Trainers**, **Participant Suggestions**, and **Additional Comments** regarding the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant in the training session.
* **Session ID**: A unique identifier assigned to this training session.
* **Training Date**: The date you attended the training session.
* **Overall Rated Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the training on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Rated Quality**: Rate the quality of the training on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the training.
* **Participant Rating**: Rate your overall rating of the training on a scale of 1-5.
* **Participant Rating Scale**: Choose a rating from the available options (Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, Poor).
* **Training Leadership Rating**: Rate the leadership of the training on a scale of 1-5.
* **Training Leadership Scale**: Choose a rating from the available options (Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, Poor).
* **Feedback to Trainers**: Provide any feedback to the trainers about the training.
* **Participant Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions or ideas for future improvements.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the training.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields, marked as **Required**.
- If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us at [your email address].
- Thank you for taking the time to evaluate the Patient Safety Leadership Training program. Your feedback is invaluable to us.
