# Healthcare Team Collaboration Workshop Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and evaluation from participants on the quality and effectiveness of the Healthcare Team Collaboration Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best fit your experience with the workshop on each question.
2. Provide detailed feedback in the text fields.
3. Review your answers and ensure they accurately reflect your thoughts and experiences.
4. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop quality** (`workshop_quality`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all that apply to describe the quality of the workshop you attended. Options are Good and Poor.
* **Collaboration effectiveness** (`collaboration_effectiveness`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply to describe the effectiveness of collaboration among team members. Options are Yes and No.
* **Improvement ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, `text`, required: false): Please share any improvement ideas you have regarding the workshop.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any feedback you have about the workshop.

## Tips

* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them in the Improvement ideas field.
* Take a moment to review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
