# Skincare Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experience in the workshop and gather constructive feedback for future improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To get the most out of this form, please complete all the fields as accurately and honestly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the workshop name? (Workshop Name)**: Enter the name of the workshop you attended.
* **Overall Rating (Overall Rating)**: Rate your overall experience with the workshop on a scale from 1-5.
* **How can we improve this workshop? (How can we improve this workshop?)**: Share any thoughts you have on how we could make the workshop better for future participants.
* **What was your instructor's name? (Trainer Name)**: Enter the name of the instructor who led the workshop.
* **How would you rate your instructor? (Instructor Rating)**: Rate your instructor's performance, with 1 being the lowest and 5 the highest.
* **How would you rate the workshop content? (Content Rating)**: Rate the content of the workshop, with 1 being the lowest and 5 the highest.
* **How would you rate the time? (Time Rating)**: Rate how well-organized and efficient the workshop was, with 1 being the lowest and 5 the highest.
* **How would you rate the facilities? (Facilities Rating)**: Rate the facilities and equipment provided for the workshop, with 1 being the lowest and 5 the highest.
* **How would you rate the value for money? (Value for Money)**: Rate the perceived value of the workshop in relation to the cost, with 1 being the lowest and 5 the highest.
* **Overall experience? (Overall Experience)**: Share your overall experience with the workshop, with a brief summary.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement? (Suggestions for Improvement)**: If you have any suggestions on how to make the workshop better, please share them here.

Note: Please answer all questions as accurately and honestly as you can. Your feedback is invaluable for us to improve future workshops!
