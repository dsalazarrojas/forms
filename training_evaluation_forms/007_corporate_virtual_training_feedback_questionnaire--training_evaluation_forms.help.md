# Corporate Virtual Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from trainees after a corporate virtual training session. It helps our training team understand the effectiveness of the session and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the session name in the "Session Name" field. This will help us identify the specific session you are providing feedback on.
2. Select your department(s) affiliation from the list in the "Department" field. This will help us understand the different groups that participated in the session.
3. Type your feedback in the "Feedback" field. This is your opportunity to tell us what you liked or disliked about the session.
4. Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field. This is an optional field where you can elaborate on your feedback.
5. Rate your overall experience with the session in the "Rating" field. Please select "Good" if you were satisfied with the session, "Fair" if it was average, and use the provided options.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will help us contact you if we need more information from you.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is optional but will allow us to contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Name (text, optional)**: This field is for you to enter the name of the training session you are providing feedback on.
* **Department (select_multiple, required)**: Select all the departments that you are affiliated with.
* **Feedback (text, required)**: Provide your honest feedback about the session. What did you like or dislike?
* **Comments (note, optional)**: If you have any additional comments, please provide them here.
* **Rating (select_one, required)**: Rate your overall experience with the session. Please select "Good" if you were satisfied, "Fair" if it was average.
* **Email (email, optional)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
