# Home Infection Control Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about home-based infection control training programs, including details about training sessions, participants, and instructors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's name, if required.
2. Provide the training date and time.
3. Select the training topic from the available options.
4. Select the status of the training session (Completed, Completed Partially, or No Show).
5. If necessary, enter notes about the session.
6. If necessary, provide the participant's email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Topic** (`training_topic`, required/optional): Select from options to indicate the topic of the training session (e.g., Infection Control, Infection Prevention and Control, Infection Prevention and Control 2, Infection Prevention and Control 3).
* **Status** (`training_status`, required/optional): Select the status of the training session (e.g., Completed, Completed Partially, No Show).
* **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the session.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, optional): Enter the name of the participant, if applicable.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, optional): Enter any feedback from the participant.
* **Educator Name** (`educator_name`, optional): Enter the name of the instructor or educator.
* **Educator Role** (`educator_role`, optional): Enter the role of the instructor or educator.
* **Education Type** (`education_type`, optional): Enter the type of education or training provided.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, optional): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, optional): Enter the time of the training session.
* **Location** (`location`, optional): Enter the location of the training session.

Please note that some fields are required, as indicated by the 'required' property in the form YAML.
