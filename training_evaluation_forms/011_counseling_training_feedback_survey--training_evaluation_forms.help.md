# Counseling Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counseling Training Feedback Survey is a form designed to gather your thoughts and experiences about the training session. Your input will help us improve the quality of our training program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all required questions (those marked as "required" in the form).
2. Select all applicable options for multiple-choice questions.
3. Write as much or as little as you need to answer open-ended questions.
4. Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session engagement** (2, number, required): Rate your level of engagement during the session, using the numbers 1-5.
* **What were the primary goals for this session?** (3, select multiple, required): Select all the goals that applied to your experience.
* **How would you rate the trainer?** (4, select one, required): Choose how well the trainer met your expectations.
* **What was your favorite part of the session?** (5, text, optional): Describe the most enjoyable or useful part of the session.
* **What areas for improvement?** (6, text, optional): Suggest areas where the training session could be improved.
* **Do you have any recommendations?** (7, text, optional): Share any suggestions you may have for future sessions.
* **Additional comments** (8, text, optional): Use this space to provide any additional information or thoughts about the session.
