# Post-Training Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to identify and assess potential risks that may arise after training, to ensure a safe and efficient operation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Read each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or our risk management team for clarification.
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
4. If you have any additional information or comments, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This section is an introduction to the form and its purpose.

### Risk Area of Application
* **Risk Area of Application** (`risk_area_of_application`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks whether the risk is related to a specific area of application. Please select "Yes" if the risk is applicable to a particular area, and "No" otherwise.

### Type of Incident
* **Type of Incident** (`risk_type_of_incident`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field asks what type of incident could potentially occur. Please select all applicable types from the list.

### Risk Level
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `number`, optional): This field asks for a numerical value to represent the risk level. Please enter a value between 1 and 5, with 1 being low risk and 5 being high risk.

### Risk Frequency
* **Risk Frequency** (`risk_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks how frequently the risk occurs. Please select "Yes" if the risk is frequent, and "No" otherwise.

### Risk Likelyhood
* **Risk Likelyhood** (`risk_likelyhood`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field asks how likely the risk is to occur. Please select all applicable likelihoods from the list.

### Risk Precedent
* **Risk Precedent** (`risk_precedent`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field asks whether the risk has occurred before. Please select "Yes" if the risk has occurred before, and "No" otherwise.

### Risk Control Measurements
* **Risk Control Measurements** (`risk_control_measurements`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field asks about controls currently in place to mitigate the risk. Please select all applicable controls from the list.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`risk_control_measurements_2` is not displayed as it has the same label as "Risk Control Measurements"): You can include any additional information or comments about the risk you have identified.

Tips:

* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or our risk management team for clarification.
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section.
