# Medical Billing Course Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation of a Medical Billing course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and provide specific comments or feedback where possible.
3. You can submit the form as many times as you want, but only the first submission will be saved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Evaluation (Page 1)** (`medical_billing_course_review_form_page_1`, text, required: false): This is the first page where you can evaluate the session you attended.
* **Session Evaluation (Page 2)** (`medical_billing_course_review_form_page_2`, text, required: false): Similar to the first page, provide your thoughts on the session.
* **Instructors Feedback (Page 3)** (`instructor_performance`, select_one, required: false): Rate the instructor's performance as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Session Comments (Page 4)** (`course_review_form_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the session.
* **Session Rating (Page 5)** (`session_rating`, select_one, required: false): Rate the session as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Course Materials Feedback (Page 6)** (`medical_billing_course_review_form_page_6`, text, required: false): Provide feedback on the course materials.
* **Materials Quality (Page 7)** (`course_materials_quality`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the materials quality as High, Very High, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Instructor Performance (Page 8)** (`instructor_performance`, select_one, required: false): Rate the instructor's performance as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Additional Comments (Page 9)** (`medical_billing_course_review_form_page_9`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the course.
* **Course Satisfaction (Page 10)** (`course_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the course as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Would You Recommend This Course (Page 11)** (`would_recommended_course`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you would recommend the course to others.
* **Page 12-19** (`medical_billing_course_review_form_page_12` to `medical_billing_course_review_form_page_19`, text, required: false): These pages are free-form text fields for you to provide any additional feedback or comments.
