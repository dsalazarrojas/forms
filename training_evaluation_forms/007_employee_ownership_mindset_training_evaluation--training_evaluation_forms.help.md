# prophets_form_title - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of our training program on employee mindset and behavior. It will help us gather feedback and data to improve future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Answer each question on its own page.
3. Fill out the form in its entirety before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Content Quality** (`employee_ownership_mindset_training_evaluation_form_1`): Rate the quality of the content presented in the training session. Please use the numbers 1-5 to indicate the level of quality you observed, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
- **Trainer Effectiveness** (`employee_ownership_mindset_training_evaluation_form_2`): Rate the effectiveness of the trainer in delivering the content. Please use the numbers 1-5 to indicate the level of effectiveness you observed, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
- **Confidence in Applying Ownership Concepts** (`employee_ownership_mindset_training_evaluation_form_3`): Rate the confidence level of the employees in applying the ownership concepts learned during the training session. Please use the numbers 1-5 to indicate the level of confidence you observed, where 1 is low and 5 is high.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and provide detailed feedback to help us improve the training program.
- If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section at the end of the form.
