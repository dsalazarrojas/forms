# Supervised Practice Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supervised Practice Evaluation form is used to assess the progress and effectiveness of a training session by a supervisor. It gathers feedback on a trainee's performance, including strengths and areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the trainee's name and date of the session.
2. Record the session start and end times.
3. Enter the session duration.
4. Provide a brief feedback on the session.
5. Rate the trainee's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
6. List the trainee's strengths observed during the session.
7. List areas that need improvement.
8. Enter an overall comment on the trainee's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trainee Name** (`trainee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the trainee being evaluated.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required/optional): Record the date of the training session.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of the session.
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, time, required/optional): Record the end time of the session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the session.
* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief feedback on the session.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the trainee's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Strengths** (`strengths`, select_multiple, required/optional): List the trainee's strengths observed during the session.
* **Improvements** (`improvements`, select_multiple, required/optional): List areas that need improvement.
* **Overall Comment** (`overall_comment`, text, required/optional): Enter an overall comment on the trainee's performance.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the supervisor evaluating the trainee.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in providing feedback.
* Use the rating system to provide an honest assessment of the trainee's performance.
* Make sure to list specific strengths and areas that need improvement.
* Enter your name as the supervisor for accountability and transparency.
