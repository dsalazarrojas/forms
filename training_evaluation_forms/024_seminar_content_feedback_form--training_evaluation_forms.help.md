# Seminar Content Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees on the quality and relevance of a seminar. It helps us understand their experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Rate the seminar's quality using the provided options.
3. Add any additional feedback you may have.
4. Provide your email address for follow-up purposes.
5. Enter your name so we can address any personal feedback or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How was the session quality?** (`session_quality`, number, required: false): 
Please rate the overall quality of the session from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
- **How relevant was the material?** (`relevance`, number, required: false): 
Rate the relevance of the material presented from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
- **What improvement ideas do you have?** (`improvement_ideas`, text, required: false): 
Provide any suggestions or ideas you have to improve the seminar content or format.
- **Rate this seminar** (`rating`, select_one, options: [Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor], required: false): 
Select one of the provided options to rate the overall quality of the seminar.
- **Add any additional feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): 
Type any other feedback you may have about the seminar.
- **Your Email** (`email`, email, required: false): 
Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
- **Your Name** (`name`, text, required: false): 
Enter your name for personal feedback or concerns.
- **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): 
Enter the date of the seminar you attended.
- **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required: false): 
Enter the time of the seminar you attended.
