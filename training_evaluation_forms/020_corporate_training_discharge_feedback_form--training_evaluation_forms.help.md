# Corporate Training Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Training Discharge Feedback Form is a questionnaire designed to collect feedback from participants who have completed a training session. It is intended to help identify areas for improvement and provide suggestions for future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Make sure to review each question carefully before proceeding.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What was the training session you attended?** (`training_session`, text, optional): This field is where you can briefly describe the training session you attended. Please provide a concise and accurate account of the session.
- **Overall rating of the training session** (`rating`, number, optional): This field is used to rate your overall satisfaction with the training session. Use a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
- **How effective was the training session?** (`training_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): This field has three options: High, Medium, and Low. Please select the option that best describes your experience with the training session.
- **What recommendations would you give to improve future training sessions?** (`recommendations`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to suggest improvements for future training sessions. Select all the options that apply to you.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is where you can provide your email address if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide your phone number if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
- **Additional Comments** (`page_2`, note, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the training program.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the training team for assistance.
- Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
