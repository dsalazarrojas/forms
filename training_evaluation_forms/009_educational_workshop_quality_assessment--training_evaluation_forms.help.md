# Educational Workshop Quality Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the quality of educational workshops, gathering feedback from participants about the workshop's content, materials, presentation, trainer's knowledge, and overall experience. The goal is to provide valuable insights for workshop organizers to improve the quality of future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* `Workshop Name`: Enter the name of the workshop you attended.
	* `Participant Name`: Enter your name.
	* `Participant Email`: Enter your email address.
	* `Participant Phone`: Enter your phone number.
2. Rate the workshop's quality using the provided scales:
	* `Overall Satisfaction`, `Content Delivery`, `Materials Quality`, `Materials Organization`, `Presentation Delivery`, `Trainer Knowledge`, `Trainer Support`, `Overall Experience`: Choose one of the options from the dropdown menus for each scale.
3. Select the options that apply to you regarding:
	* `Trainer Support`: Choose any options that describe how you were supported during the workshop.
4. Enter any additional comments you have about the workshop:
	* `Additional Comments`: Type in the text area any comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, `text`, required): The name of the workshop you attended. This will help us associate your feedback with the specific workshop you're evaluating.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required): The date of the workshop you attended. This will help us track any changes in workshop quality over time.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, required): The time of the day when the workshop was held. This will help us understand how well the workshop's timing was managed.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): A scale to rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop. Choose one of the options to provide a brief but meaningful assessment.
* **Content Delivery** (`content_delivery`, `select_multiple`, required): A scale to rate the delivery of the workshop's content. Choose any options that apply to you.
* **Materials Quality** (`materials_quality`, `select_multiple`, required): A scale to rate the quality of the workshop's materials. Choose any options that apply to you.
* **Materials Organization** (`materials_organization`, `select_one`, required): A scale to rate the organization of the workshop's materials. Choose one of the options to provide a brief but meaningful assessment.
* **Presentation Delivery** (`presentation_delivery`, `select_one`, required): A scale to rate the delivery of the workshop's presentation. Choose one of the options to provide a brief but meaningful assessment.
* **Trainer Knowledge** (`trainer_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): A scale to rate the trainer's knowledge about the subject matter. Choose one of the options to provide a brief but meaningful assessment.
* **Trainer Support** (`trainer_support`, `select_multiple`, required): A scale to rate how well the trainer supported you during the workshop. Choose any options that apply to you.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required): A scale to rate your overall experience at the workshop. Choose one of the options to provide a brief but meaningful assessment.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): A text area for you to enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about the workshop.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name so we can associate your feedback with you.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
