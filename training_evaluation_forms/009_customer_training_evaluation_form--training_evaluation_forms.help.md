# Customer Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Training Evaluation Form is a survey designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience in a training session. Its purpose is to help us understand how well the session met the customer's needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly. This will help us understand your experience.
2. Please fill in all required fields. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Rating**: This field is used to rate how satisfied you were with the training session. Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **Session Date**: This field is used to record the date you attended the training session.
* **Session Time**: This field is used to record the time you attended the training session.
* **Instructor Rating**: This field is used to rate how well the instructor performed during the training session.
* **Course Content**: This field is used to rate how well the course content met your needs and expectations. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Instructor Performance**: This field is used to rate the instructor's performance during the session.
* **Session Length**: This field is used to rate how long the session felt to you.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: This field is used to rate your overall satisfaction with the training session.
* **Session Notes**: This field is used to record any notes or comments you have about the session. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Instructor Name**: This field is used to record the name of the instructor who led the training session.
* **Customer Comments**: This field is used to record any additional comments or suggestions you have about the training session.
* **Training Goals**: This field is used to record any specific goals or objectives you had for the training session.
* **Areas for Improvement**: This field is used to record any areas that could be improved in the training session or course.
* **Email**: This field is used to record your email address.
* **Phone**: This field is used to record your phone number.
* **Customer Name**: This field is used to record your name.

# Tips

* Please be as honest and thorough in your responses as possible.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Customer Comments" field.
* If you have any specific goals or objectives for the training session, please include them in the "Training Goals" field.
