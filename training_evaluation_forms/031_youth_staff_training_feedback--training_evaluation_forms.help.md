# Youth Staff Training Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from youth staff about their training experience. The feedback will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve on to provide better training sessions in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as follows:

1. Select the session ID to associate your feedback with the corresponding training session.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the session using the provided rating scale.
3. Provide detailed comments about your rating.
4. Enter your name and email (if you want to receive a response).
5. Add any attachments or supporting documents related to your feedback.
6. Provide specific suggestions for improvement.
7. Enter any additional comments you have about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **session_id (1)**: Enter the ID of the session you are providing feedback for.
* **Rating Scale (2)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the session using the provided scale (Excellent, Good, Fair, Very Unsatisfied, Somewhat Unsatisfied).

* **Feedback (4)**: Provide specific feedback about your experience with the session.
