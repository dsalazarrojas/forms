# Speaking Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaking Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback on a speaker's performance during a session. It will help the speaker improve their skills and provide valuable insights for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

*   Complete all the fields with the required information for the speaker you are evaluating.
*   Choose the appropriate options for each field based on your feedback.
*   Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Speaker Information** (`speaker_info`, `note`, required: false): This field is a note area where you can provide any additional information about the speaker, such as their name, role, or any other relevant details.
*   **Report 1** (`report_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how the speaker performed during Report 1, either "Yes" or "No".
*   **Report 2** (`report_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how the speaker performed during Report 2, either "Yes" or "No".
*   **Report 3** (`report_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how the speaker performed during Report 3, either "Yes" or "No".
*   **Report 4** (`report_4`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how the speaker performed during Report 4, either "Yes" or "No".
*   **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, `number`, required: false): Rate the speaker's overall performance on a scale, using the provided options.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the speaker.
*   **Speaker Feedback** (`speaker_feedback`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you would rate the speaker's performance, either "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", or "Needs Improvement".
*   **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the speaker's name.
*   **Speaker ID** (`speaker_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the speaker's ID.
*   **Session ID** (`session_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the session's ID.
*   **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the session's date.
*   **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the session's time.
*   **Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how you would rate the overall evaluation of the session, using the provided options.
*   **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, `number`, required: false): Rate the session's overall evaluation on a scale, using the provided options.
*   **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the evaluation.
*   **Speaker Attendance** (`speaker_attendance`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of attendees who were present at the session.

Note: This help guide is intended to be a general guide and may need to be adapted based on specific requirements and field changes.
