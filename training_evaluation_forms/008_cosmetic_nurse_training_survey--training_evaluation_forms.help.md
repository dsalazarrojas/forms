# Cosmetic Nurse Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for cosmetic nurses to provide feedback on their training experience. It will help us understand their perception of the training they received, which will inform our future training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form if you are a cosmetic nurse who has completed a training course.
2. The form is divided into several pages, each with its own set of questions. Please answer the questions on each page to the best of your ability.
3. You may skip questions that do not apply to you.
4. If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the "Additional Feedback" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, text, optional): Please provide the name of the training provider that delivered the course.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Please provide the name of the course you completed.
* **Rating Of Quality** (`rating_quality`, number, optional): Please rate the overall quality of the course on a scale of 1-5.
* **Outcome Rating** (`outcome_rating`, number, optional): Please rate how well the course met your training outcomes on a scale of 1-5.
* **What are the main training outcomes** (`training_outcomes`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Areas For Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply from the following:
	+ Communication
	+ Materials
	+ Organization
	+ Content
	+ Equipment
* **Time To Complete** (`time_to_complete`, time, optional): If you have any feedback on the time it took to complete the course, please provide it in this format.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the course.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like us to contact you for follow-up, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
