# Privacy And Information Security Training Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your experience with the privacy and information security training session. Your input will help us improve the format and content of future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide us with your feedback, please answer the questions as accurately as possible. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the training format** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the type of training format you attended:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What did you learn about privacy** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the areas of privacy you learned about during the training:
	+ Data Minimization
	+ Information Security
	+ Compliance and Data Protection
	+ Legal and Regulatory
* **What did you learn about security** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the areas of security you learned about during the training:
	+ Network Security
	+ Application Security
	+ Operating System Security
* **How can we improve the format and content of the training** (`text`, required: false): Share any suggestions you may have on how we can improve the training format and content.
* **How satisfied are you with the training session** (`select_one`, required: false): Select your level of satisfaction with the training:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Additional Comments** (`text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the training session.
* **What is your contact email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address (we'll keep it confidential).
* **What is your name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions that apply to you.
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
