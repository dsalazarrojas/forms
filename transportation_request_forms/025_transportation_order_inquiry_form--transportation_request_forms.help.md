# Transportation Order Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Transportation Order Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from end-users who need to request transportation services. It is used to capture essential details such as transportation company, start and end dates, email address, phone number, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the transportation company name in the "transportation_company" field.
2. Select the correct "contract_status" from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the relevant "corporate_agreement" options that apply to your inquiry.
4. Enter the start date in the "start_date" field.
5. Enter the end date in the "end_date" field.
6. Provide any additional notes in the "notes" field (optional).
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
8. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
9. Select who the inquiry should be assigned to from the "assigned_to" dropdown menu.
10. Fill in the required numerical information in "form_11", "form_13", "form_22", "form_23", and "form_24".
11. Select the correct options for "form_14", "form_15", "form_20", "form_21", and "form_25".
12. Select any other options that are relevant to your inquiry in "form_15".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **transportation_company** (required): This is the name of the transportation company that the user wants to use for their transportation service. For example, Uber, Lyft, or Taxi Co.
* **contract_status** (required): Choose the current status of the contract between the user and the transportation company. This will help the service provider to understand the user's relationship with the company.
* **corporate_agreement** (required): Select all the corporate agreements that apply to the user's transportation request. This will help the service provider to understand the user's business needs.
* **start_date** (required): Enter the date when the user wants to start their transportation service.
* **end_date** (required): Enter the date when the user wants to end their transportation service.
* **notes** (optional): Provide any additional information or comments about the user's transportation request.
* **email** (required): Enter your email address to enable the service provider to contact you for further information.
* **phone** (required): Enter your phone number so that the service provider can contact you directly.
* **assigned_to** (required): Select who the inquiry should be assigned to from the dropdown menu.
