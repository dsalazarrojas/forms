# online_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for travel bookings. Fill it out to provide us with the necessary details for your trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information in the **General Info** field.
2. Select your pick-up time using the **Pick up time** field.
3. Choose your **Drop off location** from the available options.
4. Select your vehicle type(s) from the available options in the **Vehicle type** field.
5. Enter your contact details in the **Contact details** field. This is a required field.
6. Add any special requests in the **Special requests** field.
7. Enter travel documents in the **Travel documents** field.
8. Select your **Travel agency** from the available options.
9. Choose your **Payment method** from the available options.
10. Add any notes in the **Notes** field.
11. Confirm that the details provided are correct by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **Confirm** field.
11. Enter the confirmation details in the **Confirm details** field. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: Enter your general information in this field.
* **Pick up time**: Select the date and time you'd like to be picked up.
* **Drop off location**: Choose a location from the available options where you'd like to be dropped off.
* **Vehicle type**: Select one or more vehicle types that you'd like to use for your trip.
* **Contact details**: Enter your contact information (e.g., phone number, email) so we can get in touch with you.
* **Special requests**: Enter any special requests you have for your trip.
* **Travel documents**: Enter any travel documents you need to provide (e.g., passport, ID).
* **Travel agency**: Select the travel agency responsible for your trip.
* **Payment method**: Select the payment method you'd like to use for your trip.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or information about your trip.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that the details provided are correct by selecting **Yes** or **No**.
* **Confirm details**: Enter any additional details to confirm your booking.
