# Towing Service Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Towing Service Notification form is designed for customers to submit a notification for towing services. This form is used to provide essential information about the towing service request, ensuring prompt and accurate handling of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your customer information in the "Customer Information" field.
2. In the "Request Details" field, describe the towing service you require.
3. Confirm the service type by selecting "Towing Service" in the "Service Confirmation" field.
4. Enter your pickup and drop-off information in the "Pickup Information" and "Drop-Off Information" fields.
5. Add any additional notes about the request in the "Additional Notes" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Select the towing service provider in the "Towing Service" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Enter your name, address, and other identifying details.
* **Request Details**: Describe the specific towing service you require, including any special requests.
* **Service Confirmation**: Select "Towing Service" to confirm the type of service.
* **Pickup Information**: Enter the address or location for pickup, including any relevant details.
* **Drop-Off Information**: Enter the address or location for drop-off, including any relevant details.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information about the request that is not covered in other fields.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Towing Service**: Select the towing service provider (if applicable).
