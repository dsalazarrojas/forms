# B2B Drop Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The B2B Drop Off Form is designed to collect information for the transportation of items between businesses. It helps ensure accurate and efficient logistics by gathering essential details from both the sender and recipient companies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the sender's company name and contact information (fields 1-4).
2. Enter the recipient company name and contact person (fields 5-7).
3. Select the preferred drop-off date and time (fields 8-9).
4. Enter the drop-off address (field 10).
5. Choose the type of items being transported (field 11).
6. Provide a detailed description of the items (field 12).
7. Enter the total quantity and weight of the items (fields 13-14).
8. Indicate any special handling requirements (field 15).
9. Confirm if tracking information is required (field 16).
10. Specify if a signature is required on delivery (field 17).
11. Add any additional instructions or notes (field 18).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sender Company Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Sender Contact Person** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person arranging the drop-off.
* **Sender Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Sender Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your company contact number for questions.
* **Recipient Company Name** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company receiving the items.
* **Recipient Contact Person** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person receiving the items.
* **Recipient Phone Number** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the direct line of the recipient.
* **Preferred Drop-Off Date** (`8`, `date`, required): Select the date when the items will be delivered.
* **Preferred Drop-Off Time** (`9`, `time`, required): Select the time window for the delivery.
* **Drop-Off Address** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter the full delivery address.
* **Type of Items** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary category of items being transported.
* **Detailed Item Description** (`12`, `text`, required): Describe the items being delivered.
* **Total Quantity** (`13`, `number`, required): Enter the number of items or packages.
* **Total Weight** (`14`, `number`, required): Enter the weight of the items in pounds or kilograms.
* **Special Handling Requirements** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required): Check any special handling needs (e.g., fragile, temperature-controlled).
* **Tracking Required** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if tracking information is needed.
* **Signature Required** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if a signature is required on delivery.
* **Additional Instructions** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter any extra notes or instructions for the delivery.
