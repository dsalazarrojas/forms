# Vehicle Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Booking Form is used to book a vehicle for transportation purposes. Please use this form to submit your vehicle booking request and provide relevant details for a smooth booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the required fields and provide the necessary information for each field.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
3. Submit the form after completing all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Booking Form 1** (`vehicle_booking_form_1`, text, required/optional): This field is a brief description of the vehicle booking. Please enter a short description of the vehicle you would like to book.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter the customer's details such as name and contact information.
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, optional): Provide details about the vehicle being booked, such as make, model, and plate number.
* **Date of Booking** (`date_of_booking`, date, required): Select the date you would like to book the vehicle on.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the booking, which can be Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the customer's ID for identification purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check all the details you entered before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or issues, please contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
