# Winter Break Transportation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your transportation needs during the winter break. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey, and provide as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Transportation Request" page.
2. Fill out the Campus field by selecting your current or intended campus location from the dropdown list.
3. If you have travel dates, please enter them in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 12/25/2022).
4. In the Travel Arrangements field, select all the options that apply to your transportation plans.
5. For travel method, select the method you plan to use (e.g., drive, fly, train, or bus).
6. In the Accessibility Needs field, provide any special travel accommodations you require (optional).
7. Enter your contact details, including your name and email address.
8. If you are traveling with others, list their names and affiliations (staff, faculty, or students) in the Travel Participants field.
9. Enter the travel distance and travel time if applicable.
10. Provide any additional comments or information about your travel plans in the Comments field.
11. Finally, select all the schools or campuses you are associated with.
12. If you are traveling with a group, select the group name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transportation Request**: This is the main page for collecting your winter break transportation information.
* **Campus**: Select your current or intended campus location from the dropdown list.
  + University of Winter
  + Winter Campus
  + Other Campus
* **Start Date**: Enter your travel dates in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 12/25/2022).
* **Travel Arrangements**: Select all the options that apply to your travel plans.
  + Private Vehicle
  + Public Transportation
  + Carpool
  + Other Arrangement
* **Travel Method**: Select the method you plan to use for your travel.
  + Drive
  + Fly
  + Train
  + Bus
* **Accessibility Needs**: Provide any special travel accommodations you require (optional).
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name and email address for contact purposes.
* **Travel Participants**: List the names and affiliations (staff, faculty, or students) of those traveling with you.
* **Travel Distance**: Enter the distance of your travel (if applicable).
* **Travel Time**: Enter the travel time (if applicable).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your travel plans.
* **School Participants**: Select all the schools or campuses you are associated with.
* **Campus**: Select all the campuses you are traveling to or visiting.
* **Travel Cost**: Enter the estimated cost of your travel (if applicable).
* **Travel Frequency**: Select how often you plan to travel during the winter break.
  + One-time
  + Repeatedly
  + Multiple times
* **Travel Group**: Select the name of the travel group you are part of (if applicable).
