# fleet-management-checklist-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help fleet managers track and assess the status of vehicles in their fleet. It is used to monitor and maintain the overall condition and safety of vehicles, ensuring they are in a good state to operate. The form collects information on various aspects of vehicle management, including vehicle status, maintenance dates, fuel levels, and safety equipment availability.
