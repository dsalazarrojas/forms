# Intermodal Transport Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback about your intermodal transport experience. Please use this form to report your satisfaction with the transfer process and provide any comments or suggestions you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of transfer you experienced (Yes/No).
2. Choose the route you traveled on.
3. Enter the name of the station where your journey started.
4. Enter the name of the station where your journey ended.
5. Enter the time you arrived at the start station.
6. Enter the time you arrived at the end station.
7. Report on the duration of your journey.
8. Select your level of satisfaction with the transfer process (Satisfied/Dissatisfied).
9. Provide any comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transfer Type** (select_one): This field asks you to confirm if you experienced a transfer during your journey.
* **Route** (select_multiple): Choose the route you traveled on from the list of available options.
* **Origin Station** (text): Enter the name of the station where your journey started.
* **Destination Station** (text): Enter the name of the station where your journey ended.
* **Start Time** (time): Enter the time you arrived at the start station.
* **End Time** (time): Enter the time you arrived at the end station.
* **Travel Time** (number): Report on the duration of your journey.
* **Transfer Satisfaction** (select_one): Select your level of satisfaction with the transfer process (Satisfied/Dissatisfied).
* **Comments** (note): Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about your transport experience.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us improve our service.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any concerns or complaints, please contact our customer support team.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time in helping us improve our service.
