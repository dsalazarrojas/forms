# Flight Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Flight Booking Form is used to collect customer information and flight details for booking flights. This form helps to gather essential data required for flight booking processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Details** information on the first page.
2. Provide your **Flight Details** on the second page.
3. Enter your **Contact Information** on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (optional): This field is where you can enter your personal details such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Flight Details** (optional): This field is where you can provide information about your flight, including flight number, departure and arrival time, and travel dates.
* **Contact Information** (optional): This field is where you can enter your preferred contact method, such as email or phone number, in case we need to contact you.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any) for a successful submission.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
