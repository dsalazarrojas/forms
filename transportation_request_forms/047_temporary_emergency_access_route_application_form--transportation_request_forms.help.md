# Temporary Emergency Access Route Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Temporary Emergency Access Route Application Form is designed to collect information for temporary emergency access routes. It helps in providing necessary information in emergency situations such as road closures or natural disasters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the User Information section.
2. Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact in the Emergency Contact section.
3. Select the start and end dates of your requested access period in the Start Date and End Date sections.
4. Describe the route you are requesting access to in the Route Description section.
5. Enter the contact number and email of the person requesting access in the Contact Number and Contact Email sections.
6. Choose the duration of your requested access in the Route Duration section.
7. Select the start and end times of your requested access in the Start Time and End Time sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (user_input_1): Enter your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you in case we need more information or clarification on your request.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact_input_2): Provide the name and contact information of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact** (contact_input_3): Enter the name and contact information of the person requesting access.
* **Start Date** (start_date_input_4): Select the start date of your requested access period.
* **End Date** (end_date_input_5): Select the end date of your requested access period.
* **Route Description** (route_description_input_6): Describe the route you are requesting access to in as much detail as possible.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number_input_7): Enter the contact number of the person requesting access.
* **Route Name** (route_name_input_8): Enter the name of the route you are requesting access to.
* **Start Time** (route_start_time_input_9): Select the start time of your requested access period.
* **End Time** (route_end_time_input_10): Select the end time of your requested access period.
* **Route Duration** (route_duration_input_11): Choose the duration of your requested access in the options provided.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email_input_12): Enter the email address of the person requesting access.
