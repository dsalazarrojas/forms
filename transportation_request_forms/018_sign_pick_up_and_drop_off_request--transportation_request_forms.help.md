# Sign Pick Up And Drop Off Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a transportation service for a pick up and drop off operation. This form is used to schedule a pick up and drop off for a customer, and to provide necessary information about the pick up and drop off times, vehicle type, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the date and time for the pick up and drop off operation by selecting the appropriate options from the "Pickup Date" and "Drop Off Date" fields.
2. Select the type of vehicle required for the operation by choosing from the options in the "Vehicle Type" field.
3. Enter the name and email of the person to be picked up and dropped off in the "Name" and "Email" fields.
4. Enter the phone number of the person to be contacted in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose the person responsible for the operation by selecting the "Assigned To" option.
6. Choose the tool assigned to the operation by selecting the "Assigned Tool" option.
7. Enter any additional comments or instructions in the "Comments" field.
8. Finally, review and submit the form to complete the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup Date** (`schedule_pick_up_and_drop_off_request`): Choose the date for the pick up operation.
* **Pickup Time** (`schedule_pick_up_and_drop_off_request`): Choose the time for the pick up operation.
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`): Enter the address or location where the pick up will take place.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter the name of the person to be picked up and dropped off.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email of the person to be picked up and dropped off.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the person to be contacted.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`): Choose the type of vehicle required for the operation (e.g. Car, Van, Truck, SUV).
* **Schedule Pick Up Time** (`schedule_pick_up_time`): Choose the time for the pick up operation.
* **Schedule Drop Off Time** (`schedule_drop_off_time`): Choose the time for the drop off operation.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Choose the person responsible for the operation.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Choose the tool assigned to the operation.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or instructions.
* **Form Completion Time** (`form_completion_time`): Enter the time when the form was completed.
* **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`): Enter the date when the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Please make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth transportation operation.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the transportation team for assistance.
