# International Transport Agreement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about international transportation agreements, including agreement types, shipping origins, destinations, cargo types, shipment dates, and contact details. It is intended for use by companies or individuals involved in international shipping to provide accurate and comprehensive data for their transportation agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **agreement type** from the provided options.
2. Choose the **shipping origin** from the given options.
3. Select the **shipping destination** from the provided options.
4. Choose the **cargo type** from the options.
5. Enter the **shipment date** in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
6. Enter the **shipment time** in the format of `HH:MM:SS`.
7. Provide any **comments** regarding the shipment.
8. Select the **shipping service** from the options, if applicable.
9. Enter the **contact person**, **email**, and **phone** details.
10. Choose the **contact method** from the options.
11. Enter the **contact details** if necessary.
12. Add **company** and **department** details, if applicable.
13. Enter the **job title**, if necessary.
14. Enter the **company phone**, **company email**, and **address** details.
15. Enter the **city**, **country**, and **postal code** details.
16. Enter the **fax** number, if necessary.
17. Enter the **ship date** in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agreement Type**: Choose the type of transportation agreement you wish to make, from the options provided.
  + Type 1, Type 2, Type 3
* **Shipping Origin**: Select the origin of the shipment, from the options provided.
  + Origin 1, Origin 2, Origin 3
* **Shipping Destination**: Select the destination of the shipment, from the options provided.
  + Destination 1, Destination 2, Destination 3
* **Cargo Type**: Choose the type of cargo being transported, from the options provided.
  + Type 1, Type 2, Type 3
* **Shipment Date**: Enter the date of the shipment in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Shipment Time**: Enter the time of the shipment in the format of `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the shipment.
* **Shipping Service**: Select the shipping service used, if applicable.
  + Service 1, Service 2, Service 3
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Method**: Choose the contact method used, from the options provided.
  + Method 1, Method 2, Method 3
* **Contact Details**: Enter any additional contact details, if necessary.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company.
* **Department**: Enter the department name, if applicable.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title, if necessary.
* **Company Phone**: Enter the company phone number.
* **Company Email**: Enter the company email address.
* **Address**: Enter the shipping address.
* **City**: Enter the city name.
* **Country**: Enter the country name.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code.
* **Fax**: Enter the fax number, if necessary.
* **Ship Date**: Enter the ship date in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
