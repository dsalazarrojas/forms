# Rideshare User Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from new users to onboard them into our rideshare services. Please complete this form to register for our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Rider Type" option from the dropdown menu to specify whether you are a driver or a rider.
2. Fill in your "Name" in the text field.
3. Enter your "Email" in the format of "username@domain.com".
4. Enter your "Phone" number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or "XXX-XXX-XXXXX".
5. Select your "Rider Type" again to confirm your selection (this is to prevent misselection).
6. Fill in your "Vehicle" information in the text field.
7. Enter your "License" information in the text field.
8. Enter your "Address" in the text field.
9. Enter your "City" in the text field.
10. Enter your "State" in the text field.
11. Enter your "Zip" in the text field.
12. Enter your "Driver License" information in the text field.
13. Enter your "Vehicle License" information in the text field.
14. Enter your "Registration" information in the text field.
15. Enter your "Insurance" information in the text field.
16. Enter your "Insurance Number" in the text field.
17. Enter your "Insurance Provider" in the text field.
18. Select the "Insurance Date" in the date format "YYYY-MM-DD".
19. Enter your "Driver Experience" in the number field.
20. Select your "Rating" in the number field.
21. Fill in your "Notes" in the text field.
22. Select your "Agreement" options by checking the corresponding boxes.
23. Select your "Agreement 2" options by checking the corresponding boxes.
24. Select your "Agreement 3" options by checking the corresponding boxes.
25. Select your "Agreement 4" options by checking the corresponding boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Fill in your name as you would like to be identified in our system.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in the format "username@domain.com".
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or "XXX-XXX-XXXXX".
* **Rider Type** (`Rider Type`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are a "Driver" or a "Rider".
* **Vehicle** (`Vehicle`, `text`, required): Fill in your vehicle information.
* **License** (`License`, `text`, required): Enter your license information.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): Enter your address in the text format.
* **City** (`City`, `text`, required): Enter your city in the text format.
* **State** (`State`, `text`, required): Enter your state in the text format.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, `text`, required): Enter your zip in the text format.
* **Driver License** (`Driver License`, `text`, required): Enter your driver license information.
* **Vehicle License** (`Vehicle License`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle license information.
* **Registration** (`Registration`, `text`, required): Enter your registration information.
* **Insurance** (`Insurance`, `text`, required): Enter your insurance information.
* **Insurance Number** (`Insurance Number`, `text`, required): Enter your insurance number.
* **Insurance Provider** (`Insurance Provider`, `text`, required): Enter your insurance provider information.
* **Insurance Date** (`Insurance Date`, `date`, required): Select the insurance date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Driver Experience** (`Driver Experience`, `number`, required): Enter your driver experience.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, `number`, required): Select your rating.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required): Fill in any notes you may have.
* **Agreement** (`Agreement`, `text`, required): Select your agreement options by checking the corresponding boxes.
* **Agreement 2** (`Agreement 2`, `text`, required): Select your agreement 2 options by checking the corresponding boxes.
* **Agreement 3** (`Agreement 3`, `text`, required): Select your agreement 3 options by checking the corresponding boxes.
* **Agreement 4** (`Agreement 4`, `text`, required): Select your agreement 4 options by checking the corresponding boxes.
