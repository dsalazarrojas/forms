# Stranded Citizen Transportation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stranded Citizen Transportation Request Form is designed for citizens who are in emergency situations and require transportation assistance. The form helps us gather necessary information to provide timely and effective support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Enter your name in the "Contact Name" field, which is required to identify you as the stranded citizen.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, which is also required to contact you.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which is optional but recommended for our crew to reach you.
5. Type in your current location in the "Location" field, which is required to know where to send help.
6. Give a detailed description of your situation in the "Description of crisis" field, which helps us understand your needs.
7. Leave any additional message or request in the "Message" field, which is optional but helps us know more about your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the stranded citizen.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so our crew can contact you.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted via email.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Type in your current location, including city, state, or any other relevant details.
* **Description of crisis** (`description_of_crisis`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your emergency situation.
* **Message** (`contact_message`, text, optional): Leave any additional message or request for our crew to know more about your needs.
* **Emergency Request** (`stranded_citizen_transportation_request_form`, note, required): Note: This field is not a field to fill out, but rather a note to acknowledge that you are submitting an emergency request.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to help us provide effective assistance.
* If you are unable to access the form online, please contact us directly for alternative solutions.
* We will review your form submission promptly and will contact you as soon as possible to discuss further assistance.
