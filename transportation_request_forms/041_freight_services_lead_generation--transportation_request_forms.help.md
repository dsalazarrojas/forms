# Freight Services Lead Generation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freight Services Lead Generation form is designed to collect information from end-users about their shipping needs, which will be used to create leads for freight services sales and operations teams.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to send the collected information for review and follow-up by our sales and operations teams.
2. Enter your contact information in the following fields:
	* Contact Name
	* Contact Email
	* Contact Phone
3. Provide the shipping details:
	* Shipping From
	* Shipping To
4. Select the type of service you are interested in:
	* Air
	* Ocean
	* Rail
	* Road
5. (Optional) Enter any additional notes about your shipping requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (1): Enter your name as the contact person for this shipment.
* **Contact Email** (2): Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up communications.
* **Contact Phone** (3): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for follow-up communications.
* **Ship From** (4): Enter the origin location of the shipment (e.g., city, state, or country).
* **Ship To** (5): Enter the destination location of the shipment (e.g., city, state, or country).
* **Service Type** (6): Select the type of service you are interested in (e.g., Air, Ocean, Rail, or Road).
* **Ship Date** (7): Enter the date you would like the shipment to be completed.
* **Ship Time** (8): Enter the time of day you would like the shipment to be completed (if applicable).
* **Notes** (9): Provide any additional notes or instructions about your shipment.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that our sales and operations teams can properly assist you with your shipping needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your shipment, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Our sales and operations teams will review your submitted information and follow up with you to discuss further details about your shipment.
