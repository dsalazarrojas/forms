# Cartage And Hire Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experience with the company's cartage and hire services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details:
	* Enter your name.
	* Enter the service or job number (as provided by the company).
2. Provide service details:
	* Specify the date of service.
	* Choose the primary service category (cartage or freight, equipment hire, vehicle hire, or logistics support).
3. Evaluate the service experience:
	* Answer whether the pickup and delivery were on time.
	* Rate the condition of items upon arrival (1-5, with 5 being perfect).
	* Rate the efficiency of the loading and unloading process (1-5, with 5 being fast).
4. Evaluate equipment and staff:
	* If applicable, rate the condition of hire equipment or vehicle (1-5, with 5 being good).
	* Rate the performance and reliability of the equipment (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
	* Rate the clarity of usage instructions provided (1-5, with 5 being clear).
5. Rate staff support:
	* Rate the professionalism of our team (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
	* Rate the communication quality during the hire or transport period (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
6. Overall assessment:
	* Rate the value for money of the service (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
	* Choose whether you would recommend our services.
	* Choose whether you would use our services again.
7. Additional comments and suggestions:
	* Enter any other comments or concerns.
	* Suggest any improvements to the company's operations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (customer_name)**: Please enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Service or Job Number (service_id)**: Enter the number provided by the company for accurate tracking.
* **Date of Service (date_of_service)**: Enter the date of the service completion.
* **Type of Service (service_type)**: Select the primary service category (cartage or freight, equipment hire, vehicle hire, or logistics support).
* **Was the pickup on time? (on_time_pickup)**: Choose whether the pickup was completed on time.
* **Was the delivery on time? (on_time_delivery)**: Choose whether the delivery was completed on time.
* **Condition of items upon arrival (cargo_condition)**: Rate the condition of items (1-5, with 5 being perfect).
* **Efficiency of the loading and unloading process (loading_efficiency)**: Rate the speed of the loading and unloading process (1-5, with 5 being fast).
* **Condition of hire equipment or vehicle (equipment_condition)**: Rate the condition of hire equipment or vehicle (if applicable) (1-5, with 5 being good).
* **Performance and reliability of equipment (equipment_performance)**: Rate the performance and reliability of equipment (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
* **Clarity of usage instructions provided (instruction_clarity)**: Rate the clarity of usage instructions provided (1-5, with 5 being clear).
* **Professionalism of our team (staff_professionalism)**: Rate the professionalism of our team (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
* **Communication quality during the hire or transport period (communication_quality)**: Rate the communication quality during the service (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
* **Helpfulness of customer support (support_helpfulness)**: Rate the helpfulness of customer support (if applicable) (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
* **Value for money (overall_value)**: Rate the value for money of the service (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
* **Would you recommend our services? (recommendation_interest)**: Choose whether you would recommend our services.
* **Will you use our services again? (use_again_interest)**: Choose whether you would use our services again.
* **What could we improve in our operations (improvement_suggestions)**: Enter any other comments or concerns.
* **Any other comments or concerns (additional_feedback)**: Enter any other comments or suggestions.
