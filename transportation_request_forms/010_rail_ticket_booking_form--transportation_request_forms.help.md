# Rail Ticket Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers for rail ticket bookings. Please fill out the following details to submit your ticket request.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary details.
- Ensure you have selected the correct options for your ticket request.
- Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address for communication regarding your ticket request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter a phone number where you can be reached for further clarification or updates.
* **Departure Station** (`departure_station`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the station from which you would like to depart.
* **Arrival Station** (`arrival_station`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the station at which you would like to arrive.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to travel from your chosen departure station.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you would like to depart from your chosen departure station.
* **Seat Class** (`seat_class`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the class of service you prefer (First Class, Second Class, Third Class).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more payment methods you would like to use for this transaction (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total cost of your ticket request, or leave blank if you would like us to provide a quote.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information or requests you may have regarding your ticket request.

Note: The required fields are those marked with `required: true`. The form will not be submitted without these fields being filled out.
