# trip_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trip_survey form is used to record details about trips taken by employees or team members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant fields that apply to your trip. The form is designed to be flexible, so you can choose the information that is relevant to your trip.
2. Fill out the fields with the necessary information. Make sure to provide accurate and complete details about your trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **trip_survey_form** (Trip survey form, Select Multiple): This is the primary section of the form. Select all the fields that apply to your trip to provide a comprehensive overview.
* **trip_type** (Trip type, Text): Describe the type of trip you took (e.g. business, pleasure, conference).
* **departure_date** (Departure date, Date): Enter the date you left for your trip.
* **return_date** (Return date, Date): Enter the date you returned from your trip.
* **destination** (Destination, Text): Describe the location(s) you visited during your trip.
* **travel_details** (Travel details, Text): Provide any additional information about your trip, such as flight numbers, hotel names, etc.
* **mode_of_transportation** (Mode of transportation, Select One): If applicable, select the mode of transportation you used for your trip (e.g. car, plane, train).
* **preferred_language** (Preferred language, Select One): If necessary, select the language(s) you preferred or were required to use during your trip.
* **comments** (Comments, Text): Provide any comments or remarks about your trip.
* **assigned_tool** (Assigned tool, Note): If you had an assigned tool or equipment for the trip, list it here.
* **trip_survey_attachments** (Trip survey attachments, Text): Attach any files or documents related to your trip.
* **travel_documents** (Travel documents, Text): Attach any documents or receipts related to your trip.
* **trip_survey_comments** (Trip survey comments, Text): Provide any additional comments about your trip.
* **personnel** (Personnel, Text): If you had any colleagues or team members with you during the trip, list them here.
* **personnel_comments** (Personnel comments, Text): Provide any comments or remarks about your colleagues or team members.
* **travel_agency** (Travel agency, Select One): If you used a travel agency for your trip, select 'Yes'.
* **travel_agency_comments** (Travel agency comments, Text): Provide any comments or remarks about the travel agency.
* **trip_survey_attachment_1** to **trip_survey_attachment_8** (Trip survey attachments 1-8, Text): Attach any additional files or documents related to your trip.
