# Student Transportation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students for transportation requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student information section, including student name, student ID, and student age.
2. Select the destination and pickup time for the student's transportation request.
3. Choose the type of vehicle that best suits the student's needs.
4. Provide the parent's contact information, including a phone number and name.
5. Enter the emergency contact's name and contact information.
6. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_transportation_booking_form_1`, text, optional): Enter the student's name, as you want to be listed on the transportation records.
* **Destination** (`student_transportation_booking_form_2`, text, optional): Enter the student's destination, such as "School" or "Home".
* **Pickup Time** (`student_transportation_booking_form_3`, time, optional): Choose the time when you want to be picked up from your current location.
* **Dropoff Time** (`student_transportation_booking_form_4`, time, optional): Enter the time when you want to be dropped off at your destination.
* **Vehicle Type** (`student_transportation_booking_form_5`, select multiple, optional): Choose the type of vehicle you prefer for your transportation, such as "Sedan", "Bus", or "Train".
* **Student Name** (`student_transportation_booking_form_6`, text, optional): Enter your student's name.
* **Student ID** (`student_transportation_booking_form_7`, number, optional): Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Age** (`student_transportation_booking_form_8`, number, optional): Enter your student's age.
* **Parent Contact Info** (`student_transportation_booking_form_9`, text, optional): Enter your parent's contact information, including phone number and name.
* **Parent Contact** (`student_transportation_booking_form_10`, text, optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact** (`student_transportation_booking_form_11`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`student_transportation_booking_form_12`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to help us serve you better.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
* We will do our best to accommodate your transportation requests based on the information provided.
