# Self Driven Bus Hire Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to outline the terms and conditions of a self-driven bus hire agreement between the bus owner and the hirer. It captures essential information required for a secure and smooth transaction process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out your information as the bus owner on page 1 (Bus Owner Info).
2. On the following page (Hirer Info), fill out your information as the person hiring the bus.
3. Provide detailed information about the agreement in the Agreement Details page.
4. Review and agree on the payment terms on the Payment Terms page.
5. Finally, enter the vehicle details on the Vehicle Details page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bus Owner Info (Page 1)**:
  * **Bus Owner Name** (`bus_owner_info`, text, required): This field is for the bus owner to enter their name as it appears on their identification documents.
  * **Contact Number** (`bus_owner_info`, text, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
  * **Email** (`bus_owner_info`, text, required): Enter your email address to facilitate communication.
* **Hirer Info (Page 2)**:
  * **Hirer Name** (`hirer_info`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
  * **Contact Number** (`hirer_info`, text, required): Enter the phone number where you can be reached for any updates or concerns.
  * **Email** (`hirer_info`, text, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Agreement Details (Page 3)**:
  * **Agreement Type** (`agreement_details`, text): Specify the type of agreement (e.g., rental, lease, or charter).
  * **Agreement Start Date** (`agreement_details`, text): Enter the start date of the agreement.
  * **Agreement End Date** (`agreement_details`, text): Enter the end date of the agreement.
  * **Agreement Duration** (`agreement_details`, text): Specify the duration of the agreement.
  * **Agreement Amount** (`agreement_details`, text): Enter the total amount agreed upon.
  * **Agreement Notes** (`agreement_details`, text): Include any additional details about the agreement.
* **Payment Terms (Page 4)**:
  * **Payment Method** (`payment_terms`, text): Specify the payment method (e.g., cash, credit card, or bank transfer).
  * **Payment Schedule** (`payment_terms`, text): Outline the payment schedule (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly).
  * **Payment Amount** (`payment_terms`, text): Enter the payment amount.
  * **Late Payment Fees** (`payment_terms`, text): Outline any late payment fees.
* **Vehicle Details (Page 5)**:
  * **Vehicle Number** (`vehicle_details`, text): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) or other unique identifier for the vehicle.
  * **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_details`, text): Specify the type of vehicle (e.g., bus, van, or sedan).
  * **Vehicle Features** (`vehicle_details`, text): Describe any unique features of the vehicle (e.g., color, model year, or special equipment).

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
* Ensure that the payment terms and vehicle details are consistent with the actual agreement.
* Review the form with the bus owner and hirer to confirm understanding and agreement.
