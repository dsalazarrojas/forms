# Transportation Safety Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Safety Feedback Form is designed to gather information about your transportation experiences, including incidents, near misses, or safety concerns. This form helps us understand what happened, how to prevent similar incidents in the future, and how to improve transportation safety overall.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. You can leave any fields blank if you're unsure or do not have the information available. When you've filled out all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: Please provide a brief description of your transportation experience, including any relevant details about your trip.
* **What are your safety concerns?**: Describe any safety concerns you experienced during your trip. This could include near misses, close calls, or actual incidents.
* **Describe the incident**: Provide a detailed account of what happened, including any circumstances leading up to the incident.
* **Time of day**: If you have the time of day, please enter it in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 14:30).
* **What type of transportation were you using?**: Choose the type of transportation you were using when the incident occurred (e.g., Taxi, Uber, Lyft, Public transit).
* **Transportation number**: If you have a vehicle registration number, license plate number, or other identifying information, please enter it here.
* **Witness name**: If someone witnessed the incident, please provide their name.
* **Witness contact**: If you have their contact information (phone number, email, etc.), please enter it here.
* **What was the relation of the witness?**: If the witness was a family member, passenger, driver, or something else, please choose the correct option.
* **Witness statement**: If the witness has a statement to make, please have them write it down.
* **What is your statement?**: Please provide your own account of what happened.
* **Email**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your email address.
* **Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your phone number.
* **Address**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your address.
* **Date**: If you have the date of the incident, please enter it in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-07-25).
* **Safety Rating**: If you feel it's relevant, please rate your overall safety experience, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
