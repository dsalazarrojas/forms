# Public Transit Service Quality Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public Transit Service Quality Audit Form is designed to assess the quality of public transit services provided by your organization. This form helps to gather feedback from users on their experiences with the service, including route-based evaluations, service levels, and any comments they may have. The audit form is used to identify areas of improvement and help enhance the overall service quality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your honest evaluation of the service quality by selecting one of the options provided in the "Service Level" field.
2. Enter the Route ID, if applicable, in the "Route ID" field. This helps us to track the performance of specific routes.
3. Enter your name and title in the "Auditor Name" field.
4. Provide any comments or observations you may have in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter the date and time of your visit in the "Date of Visit" and "Time of Visit" fields.
6. Review your answers and submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Level** (Service Level, Select One, required): Evaluate the overall quality of the service. Please select one of the three options: Excellent, Good, or Fair.
* **Route ID** (Route ID, Number, optional): Enter the ID of the specific route you used during your visit, if applicable.
* **Auditor Name** (Auditor Name, Text, optional): Enter your name and title (e.g., John Doe, Transit Auditor).
* **Comments** (Comments, Text, optional): Share any comments or observations you may have about your experience.
* **Date of Visit** (Date of Visit, Date, optional): Enter the date of your visit in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time of Visit** (Time of Visit, Time, optional): Enter the time of your visit in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Route-Based Evaluation** (Route-Based Evaluation, Text, optional): Please provide a text-based evaluation of your experience on the specific route, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when providing your comments and evaluations.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please feel free to share them in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any suggestions for improvements, please include them in the "Comments" field as well.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the support team for assistance.
