# Barging Service Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Barging Service Quote Request form is designed to collect information from clients to provide quotes for barging services. This form is used by clients to provide details about their transportation needs, including contact and billing information, route and logistics, cargo and equipment specifications, and other relevant details. The form helps to ensure that all necessary information is provided to accurately estimate the costs and services required for the transportation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact and billing information, including your company name, primary contact person, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the pickup and delivery locations, including full addresses or GPS coordinates for remote sites.
3. Specify the type of cargo/freight and its estimated weight in tons.
4. Enter the dimensions of the cargo in feet (Length x Width x Height).
5. Indicate whether the cargo contains hazardous materials.
6. Select any additional equipment or services required, such as crane services or insurance coverage.
7. Enter any project notes or special handling instructions.
8. Finally, enter the date of the quote request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company / Organization Name** (`company_name`): Enter your company or organization's name.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the primary contact person for the transportation request.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Pickup / Loading Location** (`pickup_location`): Enter the full address or GPS coordinates of the pickup location.
* **Delivery / Unloading Location** (`delivery_location`): Enter the full address or GPS coordinates of the delivery location.
* **Preferred Pickup Date** (`preferred_pickup_date`): Select the preferred date for pickup.
* **Service Urgency** (`urgency_level`): Select the level of urgency for the service, with options:
	+ Routine - Within 2 weeks
	+ Important - Within 1 week
	+ Critical / Emergency - Within 48 hours
* **Type of Cargo / Freight** (`cargo_type`): Select the type of cargo/freight:
	+ Heavy Machinery (Construction/Mining)
	+ Building Materials (Steel, Lumber)
	+ Bulk Commodities (Grain, Coal, Gravel)
	+ Vehicles / Boats
	+ Modular Buildings / Containers
	+ Oversized / Project Cargo
* **Estimated Total Weight (Tons)** (`cargo_weight`): Enter the estimated weight of the cargo in tons.
* **Does the cargo contain hazardous materials?** (`hazardous_materials`): Select whether the cargo contains hazardous materials.
* **Additional Equipment or Services Required** (`equipment_needed`): Select any additional equipment or services required, such as:
	+ Crane Service (Loading)
	+ Crane Service (Unloading)
	+ Tugs / Escort
	+ Specialized Lashing / Securing
	+ Insurance Coverage
* **Project Notes or Special Handling Instructions** (`project_notes`): Enter any additional project notes or special handling instructions.
* **Date of Quote Request** (`submission_date`): Enter the date of the quote request.
