# Charter Bus Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charter Bus Quote Request form is used to gather information for a quote for a charter bus service. This form is divided into sections that correspond to different aspects of your trip.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Requestor Information section, which includes your first name, last name, email, phone number, and organization or company name (if applicable).
2. In the Trip Details section, select the purpose of your trip and enter the departure and return dates.
3. In the Passenger Information section, enter the number of passengers and indicate if a wheelchair-accessible bus is needed.
4. You can also specify any special needs or requirements for the trip.
5. In the Additional Services section, choose if WiFi, power outlets, and restroom stops are needed.
6. Finally, estimate your budget and indicate if it's flexible.
7. Review your answers to ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you can be contacted for quote communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further questions or quote updates.
* **Organization or Company**: If you're representing a company, enter the name of your organization.
* **Purpose of Trip**: Select the purpose of your trip from the options provided (e.g. Tour or Sightseeing, Corporate Event, etc.)
* **Departure Date**: Enter the date of departure in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date of return in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Departure Location**: Enter the location from where the bus will pick up passengers.
* **Destination**: Enter the location where the trip is headed.
* **Number of Passengers**: Enter the total number of people traveling with you.
* **Wheelchair Accessible Bus Needed**: Select 'Yes' if a bus with wheelchair accessibility is required.
* **Any Special Needs or Requirements**: Describe any special needs or requirements for the trip (e.g. wheelchair lifts, extra comfort, etc.)
* **WiFi on Bus**: Select 'Yes' if internet access is needed on the bus.
* **Power Outlets Needed**: Select 'Yes' if charging stations are required on the bus.
* **Restroom Stops Included**: Select 'Yes' if restroom breaks are needed.
* **Estimated Budget**: Enter an approximate budget for the charter bus service.
* **Budget is Flexible**: Select 'Yes' if you're willing to accommodate higher costs.
