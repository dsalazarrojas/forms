# Car Rental Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a car rental service for a specific period. Fill out the form to provide us with your details, rental preferences, and other necessary information to facilitate a successful car rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Customer Information (1)**: Fill out your contact details, including First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and any other relevant contact information.
2. **Rental Timing (10)**: Provide the date and time you wish to pick up the car.
3. **Pickup Location (21)**: Choose where you would like to pick up your car. You can select from various options, including our office, airport, or home delivery.
4. **Rental Duration (15)**: Select the duration of your rental from the options provided.
5. **Car Type (16)**: Choose the type of vehicle you prefer.
6. **Delivery Details (24)**: Check if you need any delivery preferences, such as delivery at a specific time or address.
7. **Delivery Preferences (27)**: Select your preferred delivery time.
8. **GPS Needed (30)**: Indicate if you need GPS for your trip.
9. **Child Seat/Babysit Needed (31)**: Check if you need a child seat or booster seat.
10. **Driver Information (37)**: Enter your driver's license number and the state it was issued in.
11. **Date of Birth (39)**: Enter your date of birth to confirm you're 21 or older.
12. **Payment Information (43)**: Choose your preferred payment method.
13. **License State (38)**: Enter the state that issued your driver's license.
14. **Additional Driver(s) (40)**: Check if you have any additional drivers.
15. **Additional Comments (46)**: Enter any other relevant information or comments.
