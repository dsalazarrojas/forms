# Shipping Waybill Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Waybill Form is used to capture and store shipping information for efficient tracking and management of shipments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Shipment Details section with the relevant information about the shipment.
2. Select the Pickup Date from the provided calendar or enter it manually.
3. Choose the Shipment Type from the available options.
4. Select the Status of the shipment from the options provided.
5. Enter the Shipper Details in the text fields.
6. Enter the Shipper Contact information, including Email and Phone number.
7. Enter the Shipper Address in the text field.
8. Select the Shipper State from the options provided.
9. Enter the Shipper Postal Code.
10. Select the Shipper Country.
11. Enter the Shipper Shipping Service Provider information, including their Address, Phone number, and Country.
12. Review and complete all fields accurately before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipment Details**: Enter a brief description of the shipment, including any relevant details.
* **Pickup Date**: The date the shipment will be picked up.
* **Shipment Type**: Select whether the shipment is Yes or No.
* **Status**: Select the status of the shipment as Active or Inactive.
* **Shipper Details**: Enter any additional details about the shipper.
* **Shipper Contact**: Enter the shipper's contact information, including Email and Phone number.
* **Shipper Address**: Enter the shipper's address.
* **Shipper State**: Select the shipper's state.
* **Shipper Postal Code**: Enter the shipper's postal code.
* **Shipper Country**: Select the shipper's country.
* **Shipper Shipping Service Provider**: Enter the name of the shipping service provider.
* **Shipper Shipping Service Provider Contact**: Enter the contact information for the shipping service provider.
* **Shipper Shipping Service Provider Phone**: Enter the phone number of the shipping service provider.
* **Shipper Shipping Service Provider Address**: Enter the address of the shipping service provider.
* **Shipper Shipping Service Provider Country**: Select the country of the shipping service provider.
* **Shipper Name**: Enter the shipper's name.
* **Shipper E-Mail**: Enter the shipper's Email address.
* **Shipper Phone**: Enter the shipper's phone number.
* **Shipper Address**: Enter the shipper's address.
* **Shipper City**: Enter the shipper's city.
* **Shipper State**: Select the shipper's state.
* **Shipper Postal Code**: Enter the shipper's postal code.
* **Shipper Country**: Select the shipper's country.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* For fields with options, select the most relevant option for your shipment details.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your shipping department for assistance.
