# Auto Transport Carrier Contract Termination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Transport Carrier Contract Termination Form is used by carriers to notify Auto Transport of their intention to terminate their contract. This form gathers essential information about the contract and the reasons for termination, ensuring a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your carrier company name.
2. Enter the unique reference number for your contract.
3. Choose the correct contract effective date.
4. Select the reason for termination from the list provided.
5. Enter a brief description of the reason for termination.
6. Answer whether there are any outstanding loads in transit.
7. If applicable, provide a list of load IDs still pending delivery.
8. Choose the current status of your final payment.
9. Confirm whether the 30-day notice period has been met.
10. Enter the name of the authorized representative for your carrier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Carrier Company Name** (`carrier_company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your transportation company.
* **Contract Reference Number** (`contract_ref`, text, required): Enter the unique reference number for this contract.
* **Contract Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, required): Select the date the contract originally began.
* **Reason for Termination** (`termination_reason`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options (Service Quality, Price Increase, Contract Expiration, Business Closure, Change in Strategy).
* **Description of Termination Cause** (`termination_cause_desc`, text, required): Briefly explain the decision to terminate the contract.
* **Outstanding Loads in Transit?** (`loads_in_transit`, select_one, required): Yes or No.
* **Load IDs Pending Delivery** (`pending_load_ids`, text, optional): List any remaining loads still pending delivery (optional).
* **Final Payment Status** (`final_payment_status`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options (Paid, Pending, Dispute).
* **Confirm 30-Day Notice Period** (`confirm_notice`, select_one, required): Yes or No.
* **Authorized Representative Name** (`authorized_rep`, text, required): Enter the name of the person authorized to terminate the contract.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled in for the form to be considered complete.
