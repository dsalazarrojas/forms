# Street Parking Pass Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users who require a parking pass for a specific street or location. It's intended for administrative or regulatory purposes, such as processing parking permits or requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide details about the vehicle you are requesting a parking pass for, including make, model, and license plate number.
3. Add any additional notes or comments that might be helpful in processing your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Vehicle Details** (Vehicle Details, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect details about the vehicle you are requesting a parking pass for. Please provide the make, model, and license plate number of the vehicle.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required/optional): This field is a text area where you can add any additional comments or notes that might be helpful in processing your request. You can use this space to explain any special circumstances or requirements for your parking pass.
