# Ride Share Appointment Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a ride share appointment for a passenger or group of passengers to be picked up at a specific date and time. This information will be reviewed by our team to ensure that we have all necessary details for a successful pickup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Choose the desired Pickup Date for your ride share appointment. Please select a date for which you would like to be picked up.
2. Select the Pickup Time of your desired pick-up.
3. Enter your Pickup Address, including any relevant details such as city, state, and zip code.
4. Enter the number of Passengers who will be traveling with you.
5. Specify your Pickup Location, providing as much detail as possible for our drivers to know exactly where to pick you up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup Date**: Choose the date for which you would like to be picked up.
* **Pickup Time**: Select the time of your desired pick-up.
* **Pickup Address**: Enter your address, including city, state, and zip code, where you would like to be picked up.
* **Passenger Count**: Enter the number of people traveling with you.
* **Pickup Location**: Provide as much detail as possible about the location where you would like to be picked up.
