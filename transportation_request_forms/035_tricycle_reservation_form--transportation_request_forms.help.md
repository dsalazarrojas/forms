# Tricycle Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tricycle Reservation Form is used to book or request tricycles for transportation or other reasons. This form gathers information about the user and their reservation request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the First Name field with your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field for contact purposes.
3. Add your Phone number in the Phone field for additional contact information.
4. Select a Date for the reservation.
5. Choose a Time for the reservation.
6. Enter the expected Ride Duration and Ride Distance for the reservation.
7. Select the type of Tricycle you need for the reservation.
8. Provide any additional Notes about your reservation.
9. If applicable, select an Assigned Tool or Tricycle from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email): Your contact email address.
* **Phone** (text): Your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Date** (date): The date you need the tricycle for.
* **Time** (time): The time you need the tricycle for.
* **Ride Duration** (number): The expected duration of the ride.
* **Ride Distance** (number): The expected distance of the ride.
* **Tricycle Type** (select_one): The type of tricycle you need (e.g. Tricycle 1, Tricycle 2, Tricycle 3).
* **Notes** (note): Any additional information about your reservation.
* **Assigned Tool** (text): If a tricycle is assigned to you for this reservation, enter its ID here.
 
Note: This form is designed to be filled out once per reservation. If you already have a valid reservation, you should not need to fill out this form again.
