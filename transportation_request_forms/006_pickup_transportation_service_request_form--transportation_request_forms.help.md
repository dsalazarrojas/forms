# prophets_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prophets Form is a transportation request form. It's used to request or arrange transportation services for personal or commercial purposes. If you're unsure which type of transport you need, please select all applicable options in the "What is your preferred mode of transportation?" field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Prophets Form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Select your preferred mode of transportation in the "What is your preferred mode of transportation?" field. You can choose more than one option if needed.
3. Enter your starting location in the "Where is your starting location?" field.
4. Enter your destination location in the "Where is your destination?" field.
5. Select a date for your travel in the "What is your preferred date of travel?" field.
6. Select a time for your travel in the "What time do you want to travel?" field.
7. If you have a phone number, provide it in the "Do you have a phone number you'd like to provide?" field. This will help us contact you with any updates.
8. If you have any special requests or comments, type them in the "Do you have any special requests or comments?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your name?** (text, required): This field is where you can input your name for identification purposes.
- **How do you want to travel?** (select one, optional): Select your preferred mode of transportation, such as bus, car, or bike.
- **Where is your starting location?** (text, required): Enter the location from which you'll be traveling.
- **Where is your destination?** (text, required): Enter the location to which you'll be traveling.
- **What is your preferred date of travel?** (date, optional): Select a date for your travel.
- **What time do you want to travel?** (time, optional): Select a time for your travel.
- **Can we call or text you with updates?** (text, required): If you want to receive updates, provide your phone number.
- **Do you have any special requests or comments?** (note, optional): Provide any additional information for the transport provider.
- **What is your preferred mode of transportation?** (select multiple, optional): This field is similar to the "How do you want to travel?" field but allows selecting multiple options if needed.
- **Do you have a phone number you'd like to provide?** (select one, optional): This field is optional but helps the transport provider contact you.
- **Do you have any special requests or comments?** (note, optional): This is a text field for any additional comments or requests.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Select all applicable options if you have multiple preferences.
- If you have a phone number, please ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any special requests, be as clear and concise as possible.
