# HOV Lane Toll Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HOV Lane Toll Payment Form is designed to collect information about your trip, including payment method, trip details, and vehicle information. This form is used to facilitate accurate and efficient toll payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your payment details, such as the amount you want to pay and your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or Debit Card).
2. Provide information about your trip, including the trip start time, trip end time, trip length, trip distance, and trip route.
3. Enter details about your vehicle, including the vehicle type (Car, Bus, Truck, or Motorcycle), vehicle license number, and vehicle plate number.
4. Enter any additional comments or comments about your trip.
5. Review and submit the form to complete your toll payment.
