# Cold Chain Route Audit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for requesting a cold chain route audit order. The purpose of this form is to collect relevant information to enable the efficient execution of the audit process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, please follow these steps:

* Ensure all required fields are filled in as per the instructions.
* Fill out the `requester` field with your full name.
* In the `company` field, enter the name of your organization.
* Provide an accurate description of the route in the `route_description` field.
* Enter the scheduled audit date according to the format YYYY-MM-DD in the `scheduled_audit_date` field.
* Indicate whether historical temperature logs are available or not in the `temperature_logs_available` field.
* If required, choose a priority level for the audit in the `compliance_priority` field.
* Enter your contact phone number in the `contact_phone` field, if necessary.
* In the `additional_requirements` field, mention any special audit focus areas if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Requester name
* **Requester name**: `requester` (text): Enter your full name to enable proper contact and follow-up.
### Company name
* **Company name**: `company` (text): Enter the name of your organization requesting the audit.
### Route description
* **Route description**: `route_description` (text): Provide the origin and destination details of the route.
### Vehicle details
* **Vehicle details**: `vehicle_details` (text): Enter truck ID and refrigeration type, if applicable.
### Scheduled audit date
* **Scheduled audit date**: `scheduled_audit_date` (date): Enter the date of the audit in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
### Temperature logs available
* **Temperature logs available**: `temperature_logs_available` (select): Indicate whether historical temperature logs are available or not. Choose between `True` and `False`.
### Compliance priority
* **Compliance priority**: `compliance_priority` (select): Choose a priority level for the audit, if required. Select between `High`, `Medium`, and `Low`.
### Contact phone
* **Contact phone**: `contact_phone` (text): Enter your phone number for audit coordination, if necessary.
### Additional requirements
* **Additional requirements**: `additional_requirements` (text): Specify any special audit focus areas if required.
