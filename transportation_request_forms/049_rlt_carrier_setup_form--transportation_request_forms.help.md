# RLT Carrier Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for RLT (Returnable, Less-Than-Load) carrier setup. RLT carriers are used for shipping small, lightweight packages or packages that do not require a full truckload shipment. This form will help you set up your RLT carrier account, providing necessary contact information and transportation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the required fields (Carrier, Contact Name, Phone, and Email).
3. Choose your transport mode (Carrier, Air, or Ocean) and transport type (Road, Rail, or Air) from the provided options.
4. Enter your carrier information (optional).
5. Fill out your contact information (Contact Name, Phone, and Email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transport Mode** (`transport_mode`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the transport mode that best suits your shipment needs (Carrier, Air, or Ocean).
* **Transport Type** (`transport_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the transport type that best suits your shipment needs (Road, Rail, or Air).
* **Carrier** (`carrier`, `text`, required: false): Enter your carrier name or ID (optional).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person responsible for the shipment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the shipment.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the shipment.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the shipping location (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use the correct transport mode and type to ensure your shipment is handled properly.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact the help desk for assistance.
