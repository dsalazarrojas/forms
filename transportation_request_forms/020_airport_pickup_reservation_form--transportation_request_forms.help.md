# Airport Pickup Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Airport Pickup Reservation Form is a crucial tool for our airport pickup services. It collects vital information from clients to ensure that their pickup experience is tailored to their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information, including their primary name and contact email address.
2. Enter the client's mobile number, including the country code.
3. Select the airport where the client will be arriving.
4. Choose the airline and flight number for easy tracking.
5. Provide the arrival date and scheduled arrival time.
6. Specify the drop-off address where the client would like to be picked up.
7. Indicate the number of passengers and large suitcases they will be bringing.
8. Select the preferred vehicle class for the pickup.
9. Choose the meeting point, whether it's inside the arrival hall, curbside, or at the taxi stand.
10. If applicable, mention any special requests, such as child seats or pet carriers.
11. Finally, select the preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Passenger Name**: This field is required and asks for the client's name.
* **Email Address**: Enter the client's email address for easy contact and communication.
* **Mobile Number (with country code)**: Enter the client's mobile number, including the country code for accurate contact.
* **Airport Name**: Select the airport where the client will be arriving.
* **Airline**: Select the airline the client will be traveling with.
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number for easy tracking and identification.
* **Arrival Date**: Provide the date of the client's arrival.
* **Scheduled Arrival Time**: Specify the scheduled arrival time of the client.
* **Drop-off Address**: Enter the address where the client would like to be picked up.
* **Number of Passengers**: Indicate the number of people in the client's party.
* **Number of Large Suitcases**: Specify the number of large suitcases the client will be bringing.
* **Preferred Vehicle Class**: Select the type of vehicle the client prefers for the pickup.
* **Meeting Point**: Choose where the driver should meet the client, whether it's inside the arrival hall, curbside, or at the taxi stand.
* **Special Requests**: If the client has any special requests, such as child seats or pet carriers, mention them here.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select the preferred payment method for the client, whether it's cash, credit card, or corporate account.

By following this guide, clients can ensure that they provide accurate and complete information, allowing us to tailor our services to their needs and preferences. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
