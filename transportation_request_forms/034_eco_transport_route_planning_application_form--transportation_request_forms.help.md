# eco_transport_route_planning_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The eco-transport route planning application form is used to collect and document information about eco-transport routes, ensuring that all necessary details are captured and the transportation process is streamlined.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your company's details:
	* Enter the company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide contact information:
	* Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact Person" field (optional, but recommended for follow-up purposes).
	* Enter the company email address in the "Email" field.
	* Enter the company phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Enter the address of the transport:
	* Enter the address in the "Address" field.
4. Plan the route:
	* Select the date of the planned route in the "Route Planning Date" field.
	* Enter the time of the planned route in the "Route Planning Time" field.
5. Add any relevant notes:
	* Enter any additional notes or comments about the route in the "Route Planning Notes" field (optional).
6. Assess the environmental impact:
	* Select the level of environmental impact for the route from "Low", "Medium", "High" options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (Company Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person (Contact Person, text, optional)**: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide your contact person's name for follow-up purposes.
* **Email (Email, email, required)**: Enter the email address of your company.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required)**: Enter your company phone number.
* **Address (Address, text, required)**: Enter the address where the transport will take place.
* **Route Planning Date (Route Planning Date, date, required)**: Select the date of the planned route.
* **Route Planning Time (Route Planning Time, time, required)**: Enter the time of the planned route.
* **Route Planning Notes (Route Planning Notes, note, optional)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the route.
* **Environmental Impact (Environmental Impact, select_one, required)**: Select the level of environmental impact for the route from "Low", "Medium", "High" options.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the transportation request is processed efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant department for clarification.
* This form will be reviewed and processed by the relevant department after submission.
