# International Motorcycle Shipping Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers interested in shipping their motorcycles internationally. It gathers details about the motorcycle, contact information, shipping instructions, payment details, additional services, terms and conditions, and other required documents to facilitate the shipping process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "General Information" section with your motorcycle's details.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Enter the shipping details in the "Shipping Details" section.
4. Add shipping instructions and special requests in the "Shipping Instructions" section.
5. Enter payment details in the "Payment Details" section.
6. Specify any additional services you require in the "Additional Services" section.
7. Review and accept the terms and conditions in the "Terms and Conditions" section.
8. Upload any required documents in the "Upload Documents" section.
9. Upload your signature in the "Upload Signature" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Page 1)** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter your motorcycle's make, model, and year of manufacture. For example, "Honda CB500X 2020".
* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can reach you regarding your shipment.
* **Shipping Details (Page 1)** (`shipment_details`, text, optional): Provide the destination country and any relevant shipping details, such as shipping method and tracking numbers.
* **Shipping Instructions (Page 1)** (`shipping_instructions`, text, optional): Add any specific instructions for shipping, such as preferred shipping speed or special handling requirements.
* **Payment Details (Page 2)** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter the payment method and amount for your shipment.
* **Additional Services (Page 2)** (`additional_services`, text, optional): If you require additional services, such as insurance or special packaging, select them here.
* **Terms and Conditions (Page 2)** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Review and accept our shipping terms and conditions.
* **Upload Documents (Page 3)** (`upload_documents`, text, optional): Upload any required documents, such as proof of ownership or customs forms.
* **Upload Signature (Page 3)** (`upload_signature`, text, optional): Upload a scanned copy of your signature for verification purposes.
