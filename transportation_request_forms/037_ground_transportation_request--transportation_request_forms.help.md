# Ground Transportation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ground Transportation Request form is a tool used to request ground transportation for official purposes or personal use. It helps users to schedule transportation in advance, specifying the type of vehicle, start and end locations, and other necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Ground Transportation Request** text box and enter a brief description of your request.
2. Choose the **Request Date** by selecting the desired date for your transportation request.
3. Select the type of **Vehicle** required by choosing from the available options.
4. Select the **Start Location** and **End Location** by choosing from the given options.
5. Enter the **Start Time** and **End Time** for your transportation request.
6. If needed, enter the number of **Vehicle Count** required.
7. Add any additional **Comments** about your request.
8. Select the **Assigned User** who will be responsible for your transportation request.
9. Choose the **Vehicle Status** to indicate whether the vehicle is available or not.
10. Select the **Assigned Tool** and **Assigned Group** if required for your specific request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Ground Transportation Request**: Enter a brief description of your transportation request.
- **Request Date**: The date when you would like to have your transportation arranged.
- **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle you need for your transportation (e.g., Car, Van, Bus).
- **Start Location**: The location where your transportation will start (e.g., Location 1, Location 2, Location 3).
- **End Location**: The location where your transportation will end (e.g., Location 1, Location 2, Location 3).
- **Start Time**: The time when you would like to start your transportation.
- **End Time**: The time when you would like to end your transportation.
- **Vehicle Count**: The number of vehicles required for your transportation request.
- **Comments**: Add any additional comments or details about your request.
- **User Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your request.
- **Assigned User**: The person responsible for arranging your transportation.
- **Vehicle Status**: The status of the vehicle required (e.g., Available, Not Available, Unknown).
- **Assigned Tool**: The tool or equipment required for your transportation (e.g., Tool 1, Tool 2, Tool 3).
- **Assigned Group**: The group related to your transportation request (e.g., Group 1, Group 2, Group 3).
