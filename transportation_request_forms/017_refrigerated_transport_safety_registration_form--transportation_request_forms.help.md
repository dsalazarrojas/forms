# Refrigerated Transport Safety Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register the safety status of refrigerated vehicles and drivers for transportation purposes. It is designed to collect relevant information about the vehicle, driver, and transport details to ensure compliance with safety regulations and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Review the form for any errors or discrepancies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Registration**: Enter the unique registration number of the vehicle.
* **Driver Details**: Enter the name, contact person, and contact number of the driver responsible for transporting the refrigerated goods.
* **Safety Inspection**: Describe the safety inspection conducted on the vehicle, including any defects or issues found.
* **Vehicle Safety Rating**: Enter a numerical rating of the vehicle's safety status, with 1 being the highest and 5 being the lowest.
* **Vehicle Equipment Condition**: Describe the condition of the vehicle's equipment, including any issues or defects.
* **Driver Experience**: Enter the years of experience of the driver in operating a refrigerated vehicle.
* **Safety Inspection Result**: Select "Pass" if the vehicle has passed the safety inspection, or "Fail" if it has not.
* **Vehicle License Number**: Enter the license number of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle being used for transport (Truck, Van, Train, Boat, Air, or Other).
* **Transport Date**: Enter the date on which the transport is taking place.
* **Transport Time**: Enter the estimated time of transport.
* **Vehicle Registration Number**: Enter the unique registration number of the vehicle.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company responsible for the transport.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the company responsible for the transport.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the company responsible for the transport.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the company responsible for the transport.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the transport.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **Vehicle Status**: Describe the current status of the vehicle (e.g. operational, maintenance, out of service).
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the transport.
* **Transport Vehicle**: Enter the name of the vehicle being used for transport.
* **Transport Driver**: Enter the name of the driver responsible for the transport.
* **Transport Details**: Enter any additional details about the transport (e.g. route, cargo, etc.).
* **Transport Date**: Enter the date on which the transport is taking place.
* **Transport Time**: Enter the estimated time of transport.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are not mandatory fields and can be left blank unless required by specific regulations or policies.
