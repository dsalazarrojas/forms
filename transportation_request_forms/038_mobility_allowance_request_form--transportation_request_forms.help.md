# Mobility Allowance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users regarding their mobility allowance requests. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary details to process and fulfill these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill in the user's personal details.
2.  Select the type of mobility assistance required (Wheelchair, Scooter, or Other).
3.  Specify the frequency of use (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
4.  Enter the start and end dates of the required use.
5.  Select the duration of the required use (Less than 30 days, 1-3 months, 6-12 months, or More than 1 year).
6.  Upload any relevant medical evidence.
7.  Attach any supporting documents.
8.  Add any additional notes if necessary.
9.  Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **user-info (User Info)**: This field is for personal details such as name, phone number, and address.
    *   Please fill in the name of the person requesting the mobility allowance.
*   **mobility-type (Mobility Type)**: This field is to select the required type of mobility assistance (Wheelchair, Scooter, or Other).
    *   Choose the type of assistance that best suits your needs.
*   **frequency-of-use (Frequency of Use)**: This field is to specify how often the mobility assistance is required.
    *   Choose the frequency that best suits your needs (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
*   **start-date (Start Date)**: This field is to enter the start date of the required use.
    *   Enter the date when you first started using the mobility assistance.
*   **end-date (End Date)**: This field is to enter the end date of the required use.
    *   Enter the date when you expect to no longer need the mobility assistance.
*   **mobility-duration (Mobility Duration)**: This field is to select the duration of the required use.
    *   Choose the duration that best suits your needs (Less than 30 days, 1-3 months, 6-12 months, or More than 1 year).
*   **medical-evidence (Medical Evidence)**: This field is to upload any relevant medical evidence.
    *   Attach any medical documentation that supports your mobility allowance request.
*   **supporting-documents (Supporting Documents)**: This field is to attach any additional supporting documents.
    *   Attach any additional documents that support your request.
*   **notes (Notes)**: This field is for any additional comments or information.
    *   Add any further details that might be helpful for processing your request.
*   **submit-button (Submit Button)**: This field is to submit your request.
    *   Click this button to submit your completed form.
*   **email (Email)**: This field is to enter your email address.
    *   Enter a valid email address for follow-up purposes.
*   **phone (Phone)**: This field is to enter your phone number.
    *   Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
*   **address (Address)**: This field is to enter your address.
    *   Enter your address for follow-up purposes.
*   **name (Name)**: This field is to enter your name.
    *   Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
*   **organization (Organization)**: This field is for organizational details.
    *   If you are affiliated with an organization, please enter its name and contact information.
*   **department (Department)**: This field is for departmental details.
    *   If you are affiliated with a department, please enter its name.
*   **contact-name (Contact Name)**: This field is to enter the contact person's name.
    *   Enter the name of the person who can be contacted for further information.
*   **contact-email (Contact Email)**: This field is to enter the contact person's email address.
    *   Enter the contact person's email address for follow-up purposes.
*   **contact-phone (Contact Phone)**: This field is to enter the contact person's phone number.
    *   Enter the contact person's phone number for follow-up purposes.
*   **contact-relationship (Contact Relationship)**: This field is to describe the relationship between you and the contact person.
    *   Choose the relationship that best suits your circumstance.
*   **contact-title (Contact Title)**: This field is to enter the contact person's title.
    *   Enter the contact person's title for reference.
*   **contact-address (Contact Address)**: This field is to enter the contact person's address.
    *   Enter the contact person's address for follow-up purposes.
*   **submit-message (Submit Message)**: This field is to enter any additional comments.
    *   Add any further comments or details that might be helpful for processing your request.
*   **submit-name (Submit Name)**: This field is to enter your name.
    *   Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
*   **submit-email (Submit Email)**: This field is to enter your email address.
    *   Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
