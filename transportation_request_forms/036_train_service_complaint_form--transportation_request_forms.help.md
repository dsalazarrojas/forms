# Train Service Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and complaints from passengers about their experiences with train services. By submitting this form, you can help train operators identify areas for improvement and provide better service to passengers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
2. You can provide as much detail as possible in the "Complaint Details" field, including any relevant information about your experience.
3. If you have any feedback, such as comments or suggestions, please use the "Train Service Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Details**: This field is where you can provide a detailed description of your complaint or issue with the train service. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Train Service**: Indicate whether or not you experienced any issues with the train service. If you answered "Yes", please describe the issue.
* **Date of Complaint**: Enter the date when your complaint occurred.
* **Time of Complaint**: Enter the time when your complaint occurred.
* **Passenger Information**: Please provide any relevant information about yourself, such as your name, contact information, or any other identifying details.
* **Train Service Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the train service.
* **Expected Resolution**: Indicate whether or not you expected a resolution to your complaint. If you answered "Yes", please describe what resolution you expected.
* **Actual Resolution**: Indicate whether or not you received a resolution to your complaint. If you answered "Yes", please describe what resolution you received.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing complaint details and feedback.
* If you have any additional documentation or evidence related to your complaint, such as photos or videos, you can attach them to your submission.
* Our goal is to use your feedback to improve the train service, so please be as constructive as possible in your feedback.
