# Carrier Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to onboard carriers with a new transportation company, ensuring that they meet the necessary requirements and standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Complete the form in sections, starting with the carrier's basic information.
3. Submit all necessary documents and confirm their availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **DOT Number** (`DOT Number`, text, required): Enter your carrier's USDOT number.
* **MC Number** (`MC Number`, text, required): Enter your carrier's MC number.
* **Primary Admin Contact** (`Primary Admin Contact`, text, required): Enter the name of your primary administrator contact.
* **Admin Email** (`Admin Email`, email, required): Enter your administrator's email address for official onboarding communication.
* **Admin Phone** (`Admin Phone`, text, required): Enter your administrator's phone number.
* **Primary Fleet Type** (`Primary Fleet Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of fleet you operate (Asset-Based, Brokerage, Both, Owner-Operator).
* **Are you a Hazmat certified carrier?** (`Are you a Hazmat certified carrier?`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you are certified, 'False' otherwise.
* **Regions Served** (`Regions Served`, select_multiple, required): Select the regions you serve (Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Southwest, West, Canada, Mexico).
* **Preferred Commodity Types** (`Preferred Commodity Types`, select_multiple, required): Select the commodity types you primarily transport (Food and Beverage, Industrial, Electronics, Retail, Automotive, Chemical, Pharma).
* **SCAC Certificate available?** (`SCAC Certificate available?`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you have the certificate, 'False' otherwise.
* **FMCSA Safety Rating screenshot available?** (`FMCSA Safety Rating screenshot available?`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you have the screenshot, 'False' otherwise.
* **W-9 Form available?** (`W-9 Form available?`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you have the form, 'False' otherwise.
* **I certify that my company is in full compliance with FMCSA regulations** (`I certify that my company is in full compliance with FMCSA regulations`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you are in compliance, 'False' otherwise.
* **Do you have a drug and alcohol testing policy?** (`Do you have a drug and alcohol testing policy?`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you have a policy, 'False' otherwise.
* **Are all your vehicles ELD compliant?** (`Are all your vehicles ELD compliant?`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if all vehicles are compliant, 'False' otherwise.
* **I acknowledge the onboarding requirements and company standards** (`I acknowledge the onboarding requirements and company standards`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you acknowledge the requirements.
* **Today's Date** (`Today's Date`, date, required): Enter the current date.
* **Authorized Representative Signature** (`Authorized Representative Signature`, text, required): Enter your name as the authorized representative, typing it as a digital signature.

Note that some fields are marked as optional, but please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays in the onboarding process. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
