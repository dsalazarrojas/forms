# passenger_arrival_shift_pickup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from passengers for shift pickup purposes, ensuring a smooth and efficient transportation experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the passenger's information in the "passenger_info" field.
2. Provide details about the passenger's arrival in the "arrival_details" field.
3. Select the preferred shift for pickup from the "preferred_shift" field, which has the following options:
	* 06:00 - 08:00
	* 08:00 - 10:00
	* 10:00 - 12:00
	* 12:00 - 14:00
	* 14:00 - 16:00
	* 16:00 - 18:00
	* 18:00 - 20:00
	* 20:00 - 22:00
4. Optionally, provide any additional contact information in the "contact_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Passenger Information (`passenger_info`):
	* **Passenger Information**: Please fill in the passenger's name, contact information (if available), and any other relevant details.

### Arrival Details (`arrival_details`):
	* **Arrival Details**: Describe the passenger's expected arrival time, mode of transportation (if applicable), and any other relevant information about their arrival.

### Preferred Shift (`preferred_shift`):
	* **Preferred Shift**: Select the preferred shift for pickup from the available options. This will help us schedule the pickup more efficiently.

### Contact Information (`contact_info`):
	* **Contact Information**: Provide any additional contact details for the passenger, such as their phone number or email. This will help us get in touch with them if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields for accurate and efficient processing.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the transportation team for assistance.
