# Event Logistics Vehicle Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users for event logistics vehicle booking requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Scroll through the pages and fields to understand what information each field collects.
2.  Fill out the necessary fields with relevant information about the vehicle you want to book and your contact details.
3.  Review your submissions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Vehicle Request
**Vehicle Request**: 
`<type>` text
(required/optional): This field is for users to describe the type of vehicle they need for the event.

*   Please provide a brief but clear description of the type of vehicle you require.
*   This information helps us determine the type of vehicle that can be booked for you.

### Contact Details
**Contact Details**: 
`<type>` text
(required/optional): This field is for users to provide their contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.

*   Ensure to provide accurate contact information so we can respond to your request efficiently.
*   This information also helps us verify your identity and ensure the vehicle is delivered to the correct person.

### Vehicle Details
**Vehicle Details**: 
`<type>` text
(required/optional): In this field, you can specify the vehicle's features you require, such as color, capacity, and other relevant attributes.

*   Please be as specific as possible when describing the vehicle features you need.
*   This information helps us find the right vehicle for your event logistics.

### Vehicle Status
**Vehicle Status**: 
`<type>` text
(required/optional): This field allows users to update the status of the vehicle request they have submitted.

*   If you need to update any information in your vehicle request, please use this field to inform us.
*   This helps us track and manage your vehicle request efficiently.

### Event Details
**Event Details**: 
`<type>` text
(required/optional): Users can provide additional context about the event, such as the date, time, location, and purpose of the event.

*   Please provide any additional context about the event that may help us better understand your vehicle requirements.
*   This helps us tailor the vehicle to your specific event needs.
