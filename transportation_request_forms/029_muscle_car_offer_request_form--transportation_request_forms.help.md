# muscle_car_offer_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The muscle_car_offer_request_form is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about a specific type of muscle car. The form is intended for users who want to request a vehicle that meets their specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the muscle car make**: Type the name of the muscle car make in the text field provided.
2. **Select the muscle car model**: Choose the specific model of the muscle car from the dropdown menu.
3. **Enter the year**: Type the year of the muscle car you are requesting.
4. **Enter the price**: Type the price of the muscle car you are requesting.
5. **Select the transmission**: Choose the transmission type of the muscle car, either Automatic or Manual.
6. **Select the color(s)**: Choose the color(s) of the muscle car you are requesting.
7. **Select the interior**: Choose the interior type of the muscle car, either Leather or Cloth.
8. **Select the exterior**: Choose the exterior type of the muscle car, either Chrome Wheels or Alloys.
9. **Select the drive type**: Choose the drive type of the muscle car, either 2WD or 4WD.
10. **Select the engine size**: Choose the engine size of the muscle car, either 4.0L or 4.5L.
11. **Select the drive train type**: Choose the drive train type of the muscle car, either RWD or AWD.
12. **Select the drive train config**: Choose the drive train configuration of the muscle car, either Front or Rear.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **muscle car make**: This field is used to enter the name of the muscle car make. For example, if you are requesting a Chevrolet, type "Chevrolet" in this field.
* **muscle car model**: This field is used to select the specific model of the muscle car. Choose from the dropdown menu to select the correct model.
* **year**: This field is used to enter the year of the muscle car you are requesting.
* **price**: This field is used to enter the price of the muscle car you are requesting.
* **transmission**: This field is used to select the transmission type of the muscle car, either Automatic or Manual.
* **color**: This field is used to select the color(s) of the muscle car you are requesting. You can choose one or multiple colors.
* **interior**: This field is used to select the interior type of the muscle car, either Leather or Cloth.
* **exterior**: This field is used to select the exterior type of the muscle car, either Chrome Wheels or Alloys.
* **drive type**: This field is used to select the drive type of the muscle car, either 2WD or 4WD.
* **engine size**: This field is used to select the engine size of the muscle car, either 4.0L or 4.5L.
* **drive train type**: This field is used to select the drive train type of the muscle car, either RWD or AWD.
* **drive train config**: This field is used to select the drive train configuration of the muscle car, either Front or Rear.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully to ensure that your request is processed accurately.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the transportation team for assistance.
* This form is for requesting a specific type of muscle car. If you have a different type of vehicle request, please use the corresponding form.
