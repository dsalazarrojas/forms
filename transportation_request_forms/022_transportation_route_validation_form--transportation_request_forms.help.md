# Transportation Route Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form gathers information about routes, including their properties, vehicle details, and notable notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the **Route Details** page, provide a brief description of the route.
2. On the **Vehicle Information** page, enter any relevant details about the vehicle, such as its make, model, and type.
3. On the **Driver Assignment** page, specify which driver or team is associated with this route.
4. On the **Route Notes** page, record any additional notes or comments about the route.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Route Details
• **Route Details** (Route Details, Text, Not Required): Enter a brief description of the route, including its name, location, or any other identifying information.

### Vehicle Information
• **Vehicle Information** (Vehicle Information, Text, Not Required): Enter details about the vehicle, such as its make, model, year, and type. This could be a truck, bus, or any other type of vehicle used for transportation.

### Driver Assignment
• **Driver Assignment** (Driver Assignment, Text, Not Required): Specify which driver or team is assigned to this route. You can include their name, ID number, or any other identifier.

### Route Notes
• **Route Notes** (Route Notes, Note, Not Required): Add any additional comments, notes, or remarks about the route. This is a great place to capture any notable issues, weather conditions, or other factors affecting the route.
