# Transportation Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about transportation-related incidents. This form is designed to gather details such as incident date, type, vehicle type, location, and description to help with incident management and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the date of the incident from the calendar picker.
2.  Choose the type of incident (Yes/No).
3.  Indicate whether a vehicle was involved (Yes/No).
4.  Report the incident to the relevant person or team.
5.  Provide a brief description of the incident.
6.  Enter the vehicle's location.
7.  Add any relevant photos or videos of the incident.
8.  Enter the incident status (Active/Inactive).
9.  Report any associated transportation costs.
10.  Attach any additional photos or videos that can help with incident investigation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of incident (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether a vehicle was involved (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, required/optional): Report the incident to the relevant person or team.
* **Vehicle Location** (`vehicle_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required/optional): Enter the person or team assigned to handle the incident.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of the incident (e.g., Active or Inactive).
* **Transportation Cost** (`transportation_cost`, number, required/optional): Report any associated transportation costs.
* **Incident Photo** (`incident_photo`, text, required/optional): Attach any relevant photos of the incident to help with investigation.
* **Incident Video** (`incident_video`, text, required/optional): Attach any relevant videos of the incident to help with investigation.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use the calendar picker for the incident date to select the correct date.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident and its location.
* If the incident involves a vehicle, ensure you indicate the type and location accurately.
* Use relevant photos or videos to help with incident investigation.
