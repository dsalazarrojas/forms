# Ride Tracking Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ride Tracking Log Form is used for tracking vehicle information for a ride, including vehicle type, start and end locations, start and end times, distance, odometer reading, comments, assigned user, vehicle status, and vehicle condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of vehicle being used for the ride.
2. Indicate whether the Prophets ID is assigned to the vehicle.
3. Enter the vehicle registration number, if applicable.
4. Enter the start and end dates for the ride.
5. Enter the start and end times for the ride.
6. Enter the distance traveled for the ride.
7. Record the odometer reading at the start and end of the ride.
8. Add any comments about the ride.
9. Select the user to whom the ride is assigned.
10. Indicate whether the ride starts and ends at a specific location.
11. Select the status of the vehicle at the time of the ride (Active or Inactive).
12. Indicate the condition of the vehicle at the time of the ride (Yes or No).
13. Add any additional notes about the ride.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle used for the ride. This field is required.
* **Prophets ID**: Indicate whether the Prophets ID is assigned to the vehicle used for the ride.
* **Vehicle Registration Number**: Enter the registration number of the vehicle used for the ride, if applicable.
* **Date Start**: Enter the date the ride started.
* **Date End**: Enter the date the ride ended.
* **Time Start**: Enter the time the ride started.
* **Time End**: Enter the time the ride ended.
* **Distance**: Enter the distance traveled during the ride.
* **Odometer Reading**: Record the odometer reading at the start and end of the ride.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes about the ride.
* **Assigned User**: Select the user to whom the ride is assigned.
* **Ride Start Location**: Indicate whether the ride starts at a specific location.
* **Ride End Location**: Indicate whether the ride ends at a specific location.
* **Vehicle Status**: Select the status of the vehicle at the time of the ride (Active or Inactive).
* **Vehicle Condition**: Indicate the condition of the vehicle at the time of the ride (Yes or No).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the ride.
