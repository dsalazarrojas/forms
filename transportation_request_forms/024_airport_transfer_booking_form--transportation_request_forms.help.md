# Airport Transfer Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book airport transfers for passengers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal and transfer details.
2. Choose the transfer type that best suits your needs.
3. Provide accurate information for the payment method and confirm your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Passenger Information
* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted by us for any updates or issues.
* **Mobile Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for any last-minute updates.
### Transfer Details
* **Transfer Type**: Choose from options:
	+ Airport to Destination: From the airport to your destination.
	+ Destination to Airport: From your destination to the airport.
	+ Round Trip: A round-trip transfer.
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number for tracking delays.
* **Date of Transfer**: Select the date you need the transfer.
* **Preferred Pickup/Arrival Time**: Choose a preferred time for your pickup or arrival.
* **Pickup Location**: Enter the airport name or hotel name where you would like to be picked up.
* **Drop-off Location**: Enter the destination address where you would like to be dropped off.
* **Number of Passengers**: Enter the number of passengers that need the transfer.
* **Total Luggage Count**: Enter the total number of luggage you have.
* **Preferred Vehicle Class**: Choose from options:
	+ Standard Sedan
	+ Business Sedan
	+ Minivan (7 Seats)
	+ Large Van (15 Seats)
### Payment and Confirmation
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests (e.g. child seat), please enter them here.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose from options:
	+ Pre-pay by Credit Card
	+ Pay Cash to Driver
	+ Pay by Link (Email)
* **Booking Confirmation**: Confirm that you agree to the transfer terms and cancellation policy.

### Tips

- Please fill in all required fields for a successful booking.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- For any last-minute changes, contact us through the provided contact information.
