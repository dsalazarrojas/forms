# Cold Transport Compliance Permit Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to apply for a cold transport compliance permit, which is required for companies or individuals that transport refrigerated or frozen goods. The permit ensures that the applicant's transportation operations comply with relevant regulations and safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure they are complete and consistent.
3. If you need assistance or clarification, do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your company or individual name as it should appear on the permit.
* **Vehicle registration number** (`vehicle_registration`, `text`, required): Enter the registration number of the truck or trailer you will be using for cold transport.
* **Route description** (`route_description`, `text`, required): Describe the typical routes and stops your vehicle will take during transportation.
* **Temperature controls description** (`temperature_controls`, `text`, required): Explain the equipment you will use for temperature control during transport.
* **Compliance documentation available** (`compliance_documentation`, `select_one`, required): If you have any compliance documentation (e.g. certificates or inspection reports), select "True" and attach them to your application.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address for permit correspondence and any other necessary communication.
* **Requested permit start date** (`requested_permit_start`, `date`, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the permit to start, enter it in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Requested permit end date** (`requested_permit_end`, `date`, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the permit to end, enter it in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Special conditions** (`special_conditions`, `text`, optional): If there are any specific conditions or restrictions related to your transportation operations, describe them here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any doubts or uncertainties about a field, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* Ensure that the vehicle registration number you enter is correct and up-to-date.
* Attach any relevant compliance documentation (e.g. certificates or inspection reports) to your application.
* If you have a specific start and end date for the permit, ensure that they are consistent and do not overlap.
* If you have any special conditions or restrictions, describe them clearly and accurately to avoid delays in the permit application process.
