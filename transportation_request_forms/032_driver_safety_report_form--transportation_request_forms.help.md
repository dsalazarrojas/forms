# Driver Safety Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Safety Report Form is used to gather information related to a safety incident involving a company vehicle or equipment. This form is used by drivers to report incidents that have occurred while they are working.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and company name in their respective fields.
2. Enter the vehicle's registration number as it appears on the vehicle's registration card.
3. Choose the type of vehicle you were operating at the time of the incident from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident.
5. Enter a detailed description of the incident, including the location where it occurred.
6. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID card.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company you are working with.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's registration number as it appears on the registration card.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of vehicle you were operating at the time of the incident from the available options (car, truck, bus).
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred in the format 12:00 PM.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Field 9** (`field_9`, text, optional): This field is not required, but you may enter any additional information or comments about the incident.
