# vehicle_transfer_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of a vehicle transfer, including the transfer summary, seller and buyer information, vehicle details, and transfer agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the `vehicle_transfer` field with a brief description of the transfer.
2. Enter the seller's details in the `seller_details` field, including their contact information.
3. Enter the buyer's details in the `buyer_details` field, including their contact information.
4. Provide additional details about the vehicle in the `vehicle_info` field.
5. Document the transfer agreement in the `vehicle_transfer_agreement` field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the `notes` field.
7. Date and time of the transfer should be recorded in the `date_time_of_transfer` field.
8. Obtain the signatures of the buyer and seller in the `signature_buyer` and `signature_seller` fields.
9. Document the vehicle's details, including make, model, year, and other relevant information in the corresponding fields.
10. Record the contact numbers of the buyer and seller in the `buyer_phone` and `seller_phone` fields.
11. If applicable, enter the amount paid or agreed upon for the transfer in the `transfer_amount` field.
12. Record the addresses of the seller and buyer in the `seller_address` and `buyer_address` fields.
13. Document the last inspection and last registration dates in the `date_of_last_inspection` and `date_of_last_registration` fields.
14. Finally, indicate whether the buyer has accepted the vehicle transfer agreement in the `vehicle_transfer_agreement_accepted` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vehicle_transfer**: A brief description of the vehicle transfer.
* **seller_details**: Seller's contact information.
* **vehicle_info**: Additional details about the vehicle being transferred.
* **buyer_details**: Buyer's contact information.
* **transfer_details**: Specific details about the transfer, if any.
* **vehicle_transfer_agreement**: The transfer agreement or contract.
* **notes**: Additional comments or notes about the transfer.
* **date_time_of_transfer**: Date and time of the transfer.
* **signature_buyer**: Buyer's signature, indicating agreement to the transfer.
* **signature_seller**: Seller's signature, indicating agreement to the transfer.
* **vehicle_transfer_agreement_accepted**: Whether the buyer has accepted the vehicle transfer agreement.
* **vehicle_make**, **vehicle_model**, **vehicle_year**, **vehicle_year_model**, **vehicle_color**, **vehicle_plate**, and **vehicle_vin**: Details about the vehicle being transferred.
* **buyer_phone**, **seller_phone**: Contact numbers of the buyer and seller.
* **transfer_amount**: Amount paid or agreed upon for the transfer.
* **seller_address**, **buyer_address**: Addresses of the seller and buyer.
* **date_of_last_inspection**, **date_of_last_registration**: Last inspection and last registration dates of the vehicle.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check the vehicle's details for correctness.
- Verify the buyer's and seller's contact information for accuracy.
- Ensure the buyer has accepted the vehicle transfer agreement.
- Review and sign the form carefully before submitting.
