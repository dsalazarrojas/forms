# Passenger Service Feedback Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Passenger Service Feedback Request Form is used to collect information about your experience with transportation services. Your feedback will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with your feedback on the service you used.
2. Make sure to provide specific details about the service, such as the route number and date of the journey.
3. Select the service type you used (e.g. Bus, Train, Bike).
4. Rate your service experience using the service rating field.
5. Provide any additional comments you may have about your experience.
6. Click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Service Feedback** (Required: No): This is the main field where you can type in your feedback about the service you received. 
* **Service Type** (Optional): Select the type of service you used, e.g. Bus, Train, Bike. This helps us understand which service type you are providing feedback on.
* **Route Number** (Optional): Enter the route number of the service you used. If you're not sure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Date of Journey** (Optional): Enter the date you traveled using the service. This helps us track when the service was provided.
* **Time of Journey** (Optional): Enter the time you traveled using the service. This helps us understand your experience during your journey.
* **Service Rating** (Optional): Rate your experience with the service using the number from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your experience. This is your chance to tell us anything else you'd like to share.
