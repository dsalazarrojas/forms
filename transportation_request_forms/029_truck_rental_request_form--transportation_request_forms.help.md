# truck_rental_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The truck rental request form is used to collect information for a truck rental request. This form helps us understand your transportation needs and ensure that we can provide the correct vehicle for your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the type of truck you require from the "Truck Type" dropdown menu.
5. Choose the start and end dates for the rental period from the calendar.
6. Select the duration of the rental in the "Rental Duration" field.
7. Choose the reason for the rental from the "Reason for Rental" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the name of the agency or organization you are representing.
9. Enter the site or location where the truck will be used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your first name as it is spelled on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your last name as it is spelled on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Truck Type ID** (`truck_type_id`, `number`, **Required**): Enter a unique ID number for the truck type you require.
* **Truck Type** (`truck_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the type of truck you need, such as small, large, or extra-large.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, **Required**): Choose the start date of the rental period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, **Required**): Choose the end date of the rental period.
* **Rental Duration** (`rental_duration`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the number of days or hours you require the truck for.
* **Reason for Rental** (`reason_for_rental`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Choose the reason for the rental, such as business or personal use.
* **Agency** (`agency`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the agency or organization you are representing.
* **Site** (`site`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the site or location where the truck will be used.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We'll do our best to accommodate your truck rental request.
