# Transportation Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The transportation log form is used to record details of trips made by vehicles. This form is intended to help users track vehicle movements, identify potential issues, and optimize routes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2.  Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
3.  Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Transportation Log Form** (`transportation_log_form_page`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the form.
*   **Transportation Route** (`transportation_route`, text, not required): Enter the route taken during the trip.
*   **Transportation Start Time** (`transportation_start_time`, date, not required): Enter the time the vehicle started moving.
*   **Transportation End Time** (`transportation_end_time`, time, not required): Enter the time the vehicle stopped moving.
*   **Transportation Distance** (`transportation_distance`, number, not required): Enter the total distance traveled.
*   **Transportation Fuel Used** (`transportation_fuel_used`, number, not required): Enter the amount of fuel used during the trip.
*   **Transportation Notes** (`transportation_notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the trip.
*   **Delivery Name** (`delivery_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person or entity being delivered to.
*   **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, not required): Enter the address of the person or entity being delivered to.
*   **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, select_one, not required): Choose one of the following options:
    *   On Time
    *   Late
    *   Missed
*   **Vehicle Name** (`vehicle_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the vehicle used for the trip.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all fields as accurately and completely as possible.
*   Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact the relevant team for assistance.
