# Transportation Fare Change Feedback Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and comments from users regarding fare changes in the transportation system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of fare change you experienced or are concerned about.
2. Enter the amount of the fare change.
3. Choose the effective date of the fare change.
4. Provide the reason for the fare change.
5. Share your feedback and comments about the fare change.
6. If you have any further comments or suggestions, please provide them in the comments section.
7. Finally, provide your contact information so that we can follow up with you and address any concerns you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Fare Changes Feedback**: Enter a brief description of the fare change you experienced or are concerned about.
- **Type of Fare Change**: Select all the types of fare change that apply to your experience.
  • Option 1: Increase
  • Option 2: Decrease
  • Option 3: No Change
- **Amount of Fare Change**: Enter the actual amount of the fare change (e.g., 10% increase).
- **Effective Date**: Enter the date when the fare change became effective.
- **Reason for Fare Change**: Provide a brief explanation for the fare change (e.g., to fund a new project).
- **Rider Feedback**: Share your feedback about the fare change (e.g., how did it affect you?).
- **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the fare change.
- **Frequency of Fare Changes**: Select how frequently you experience fare changes (e.g., once a year).
  • Option 1: Frequently
  • Option 2: Occasionally
  • Option 3: Rarely
- **Contact Name**: Enter your name so that we can follow up with you.
- **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates or responses.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with updates or responses.
- **Contact Address**: Enter your address so that we can contact you with updates or responses.
- **Transit Agency**: If you are not associated with a transportation agency, enter your affiliation with a transportation agency if any.
- **Fare Suggestion**: If you have a suggestion for a future fare change, please provide it here.

Note to the reader: This guide is based on the assumption that the reader is a user of the transportation system and is experiencing or concerned about fare changes. The fields are self-explanatory, but please don't hesitate to contact the transportation agency's customer service if you need further clarification.
