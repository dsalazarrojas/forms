# International Pet Travel Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about pets traveling internationally, including details about the pet, travel plans, and necessary documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following sections:

1. **Pet Information**: Enter your pet's details, including any relevant information about their vaccination, veterinary care, and medical history.
2. Select the **Travel Mode**: Choose how your pet will be traveling (Air, Land, or Sea).
3. Enter **Flight Details**: If your pet is traveling by air, provide additional flight information in this field.
4. Select **USDA Requirements**: Choose which USDA requirements you are aware of (USDA-requirements, USDA-guidelines, or USDA-accreditation).
5. Select **Travel Arrangements**: Choose how your pet will be traveling (Air, Land, or Sea).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Information** (`pet-information`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your pet's name, species, age, weight, and any other relevant information.
* **Trip Details** (`trip-details`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide details about your trip, including destination, travel dates, and any other relevant information.
* **Veterinarian Info** (`veterinarian-info`, `text`, required/optional): If your pet requires a health certificate for travel, please provide the contact information of your pet's veterinarian.
* **Pet Vaccine Info** (`pet-vaccine-info`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide information about your pet's vaccine history, including type, date, and location.
* **Veterinary Doc Info** (`veterinary-doc-info`, `text`, required/optional): If your pet requires a veterinary certificate for travel, please provide the contact information of your pet's veterinarian.
* **International Travel Rules** (`international-travel-rules`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select the USDA requirements you are aware of (USDA-requirements, USDA-guidelines, or USDA-accreditation).
* **Travel Arrangements** (`travel-arrangements`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose how your pet will be traveling (Air, Land, or Sea).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of your pet's information before submitting the form.
* Ensure that your pet meets all necessary USDA requirements for international travel.
* If you are unsure about any of the information required, please consult with a relevant authority or expert.
