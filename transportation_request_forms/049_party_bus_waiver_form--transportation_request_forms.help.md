# Party Bus Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Party Bus Waiver Form is a document that requires party bus operators to agree to certain terms and conditions before allowing passengers to ride on their vehicle. This form is used to gather information about the party bus, its operator, and the passengers to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Party Bus Waiver Form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the waiver information, which includes the vehicle type, party size, and pickup/dropoff location.
2. Enter the driver's name, driver's license number, driver's contact number, and driver's contact information for a second driver (if applicable).
3. Select the vehicle type for each bus operating on the trip.
4. Enter the emergency contact's name, contact number, and relationship to you.
5. List any medical conditions or medical treatments that may affect your ability to travel.
6. Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
7. Enter the pickup and dropoff time for the party bus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Waiver Information** (`waiver_information`, text, optional)
Enter any additional information you feel is necessary to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.

### 2. **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the driver who will be operating the party bus.

### 3. **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, required)
Enter the driver's driver's license number.

### 4. **Driver Contact Number** (`driver_contact_number`, text, required)
Enter the driver's contact number.

### 5. **Driver Name** (`driver_name_2`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the second driver who will be operating the party bus.

### 6. **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number_2`, text, required)
Enter the driver's second driver's license number.

### 7. **Driver Contact Number** (`driver_contact_number_2`, text, required)
Enter the contact number of the second driver.

### 8. **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_multiple, required)
Select the type of vehicle that will be operating on the trip. Options are: Party Prophets, Limousine, Bus.

### 9. **Party Size** (`party_size`, number, required)
Enter the number of people in your party.

### 10. **Pickup/Dropoff Location** (`pickup_dropoff_location`, text, required)
Enter the pickup and dropoff location for the party bus.

### 11. **Waiver Agreement** (`waiver_agreement`, text, required)
Agree to the waiver terms and conditions.

### 12. **Waiver Acknowledgement** (`waiver_acknowledgement`, text, required)
Acknowledge that you have read and understood the waiver terms and conditions.

### 13. **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

### 14. **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required)
Enter the contact number of the emergency contact person.

### 15. **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required)
Enter your relationship to the emergency contact person (e.g., spouse, parent, etc.).

### 16. **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required)
List any medical conditions or medical treatments that may affect your ability to travel.

### 17. **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, text, required)
List any medical treatments or medications you are currently taking.

### 18. **Signature** (`signature`, text, required)
Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the waiver terms and conditions.

### 19. **Date** (`date`, date, required)
Enter the date you are signing the form.

### 20. **Time** (`time`, time, required)
Enter the time you are signing the form.

### 21. **Driver License Number 2** (`driver_license_number_2`, text, required)
Enter the driver's license number of the second driver.

### 22. **Driver Contact Number 2** (`driver_contact_number_2`, text, required)
Enter the contact number of the second driver.

### 23. **Vehicle Type 2** (`vehicle_type_2`, text, required)
Select the type of vehicle that will be operating on the trip.

### 24. **Vehicle Type 3** (`vehicle_type_3`, text, required)
Enter the type of vehicle that will be operating on the trip.

### 25. **Vehicle Type 4** (`vehicle_type_4`, text, required)
Enter the type of vehicle that will be operating on the trip.

### 26. **Vehicle Type 5** (`vehicle_type_5`, text, required)
Enter the type of vehicle that will be operating on the trip.

### 27. **Vehicle Type 6** (`vehicle_type_6`, text, required)
Enter the type of vehicle that will be operating on the trip.

### 28. **Vehicle Type 7** (`vehicle_type_7`, text, required)
Enter the type of vehicle that will be operating on the trip.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please list them in the Medical Conditions field.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please ask your driver for guidance.
* Remember to sign and date the form after completing it.
