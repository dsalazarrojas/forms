# taxi_help_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The taxi_help_form is designed to gather information from passengers for taxi service reservations. It is essential to provide accurate and clear information to ensure efficient processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the service request you are making.
2. Choose your preferred taxi driver from the provided options.
3. Enter the date of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request** (`service_request`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your service request.
* **Taxi Driver** (`taxi_driver`, select_one, required): Select your preferred taxi driver from the options provided.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of your request in the format MM/DD/YY.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the service request is clear and concise.
- Selecting the correct taxi driver helps in ensuring that the service is delivered by the requested driver.
- Enter the correct date to ensure that the service is delivered on the requested day.
