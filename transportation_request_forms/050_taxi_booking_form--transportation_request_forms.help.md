# Taxi Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you book a taxi with ease. Please fill out the form with your trip details, and our team will be in touch to confirm your booking.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your trip details.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pickup Address 
* **Pickup Address** (`pickup_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where you would like to be picked up.

### Dropoff Address 
* **Dropoff Address** (`drop_off_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where you would like to be dropped off.

### Pickup Time 
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to be picked up. Please use the format "HH:MM".

### Dropoff Time 
* **Dropoff Time** (`dropoff_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to be dropped off. Please use the format "HH:MM".

### Date of Booking 
* **Date of Booking** (`date_of_booking`, date, optional): Enter the date of your trip. Please use the format "YYYY-MM-DD".

### Name of Passenger 
* **Name of Passenger** (`name_of_passenger`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person booking the taxi.

### Contact Number 
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number where we can reach out to you for updates.

### Email 
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where we can reach out to you for updates.

### Number of Passengers 
* **Number of Passengers** (`number_of_passengers`, number, optional): Enter the number of people you'll be traveling with.

### Preferred Vehicle 
* **Preferred Vehicle** (`preferred_vehicle`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have a preferred vehicle type (e.g., Sedan, SUV, etc.), or "No" if no preference.

### Additional Details 
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for our drivers to know (e.g., special requirements, etc.).
