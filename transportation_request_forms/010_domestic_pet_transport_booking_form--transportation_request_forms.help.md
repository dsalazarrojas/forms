# Domestic Pet Transport Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about domestic pet transport bookings, including pickup and destination details, contact information, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pet being transported (Domestic Pet, Other Animal, or None).
2. Enter the pickup location details in the text field.
3. Provide additional information about the pickup location in the `pickup_location_hint` field.
4. Enter the destination details, including what, where, and when (date).
5. Provide your contact details, including name, phone number, and any other relevant information.
6. Select the transport method (Air, Sea, or Other).
7. Enter any special instructions or notes for the transport process.
8. Sign and date your signature (not required for payment).
9. Finally, choose the payment method (Bank Transfer, Cash, or Online Payment) and provide any additional payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pickup_details** (`pickup_details`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of pet being transported (Domestic Pet, Other Animal, or None).
* **pickup_location** (`pickup_location`, text, required): Enter the address or location where the pet will be picked up from.
* **pickup_location_hint** (`pickup_location_hint`, text, optional): Provide additional information about the pickup location.
* **destination_details** (`destination_details`, text, required): Enter the details about the destination, including what, where, and when (date).
* **what** (`what`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of what is being transported.
* **where** (`where`, text, optional): Enter the destination address or location.
* **when** (`when`, date, optional): Enter the date when the transport is required.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact name and any other relevant information.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the destination address or location.
* **transport_method** (`transport_method`, select_one, required): Select the transport method (Air, Sea, or Other).
* **special_instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or notes for the transport process.
* **signature** (`signature`, date, optional): Sign and date your signature (not required for payment).
* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the payment method (Bank Transfer, Cash, or Online Payment).
* **payment_details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional payment details.
* **signature_date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Sign and date your signature (not required for payment).
* **signature_time** (`signature_time`, time, optional): Enter the signature time (not required for payment).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any special instructions or requests, do not hesitate to include them in the `special_instructions` field.
* For payment, please select the most suitable method and provide any additional details as required.
