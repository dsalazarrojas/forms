# Distribution Route Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering a new distribution route. It will help us optimize the route for efficient delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section with the required information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information.
3. Review your answers before submission to ensure they are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (1)**: This section is for entering your basic contact information.
	+ **Name**: Enter your name.
	+ **Contact Info (2)**: Enter your contact details for us to reach out to you.
* **Route Details (3)**: Provide a brief description of the route you'd like to register.
* **Delivery Info (4)**: Enter any additional delivery information such as any special instructions.
* **Shipping Method (5)**: Choose whether the delivery will be yes or no.
* **Delivery Date (6)**: Enter the date for the delivery.
* **Delivery Time (7)**: Enter the time of the delivery.
* **Delivery Notes (8)**: Enter any additional notes about the delivery.
* **Route Comments (9)**: Add any comments about the route.
* **Delivery Location (10)**: Enter the location for the delivery.
* **Shipping Method Details (11)**: Enter any additional information about the shipping method.
* **Additional Comments (12)**: Enter any additional comments about the delivery.
* **Delivery Person (13)**: Enter the person delivering.
* **Delivery Company (14)**: Enter the company delivering.
* **Delivery Phone (15)**: Enter the phone number for the delivery person.
* **Delivery Email (16)**: Enter the email address for the delivery person.
* **Route Status (17)**: Choose the status of the route (Active or Inactive).
* **Other Route Status (18)**: If the route status is other, enter a description.
* **Route Status Details (19)**: Enter any additional details about the route status.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* Make sure to provide all required information.
