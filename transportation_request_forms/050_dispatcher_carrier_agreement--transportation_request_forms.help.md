## Dispatcher Carrier Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from dispatchers and carriers to agree on the terms of a transport or delivery request. It helps in ensuring that both parties have a clear understanding of the details of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the dispatch date and time of the transport or delivery.
2. Enter the name of the carrier or the name of the company you are representing.
3. Provide the contact information of the carrier or company.
4. Choose whether or not you agree to the carrier agreement.
5. If you agree, enter your carrier license number.
6. Add any additional notes about the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Dispatch Date
* **Dispatch Date** (`dispatch_date`, date, required: false): This field is used to record the date of the transport or delivery. Select the date when the delivery or transport will take place.

### 2. Dispatch Time
* **Dispatch Time** (`dispatch_time`, time, required: false): This field is used to record the time of the transport or delivery. Choose the time when the delivery or transport will take place.

### 3. Carrier Name
* **Carrier Name** (`carrier_name`, text, required: false): This field is for you to enter the name of the carrier or the company you are representing.

### 4. Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide the contact information of the carrier or company, such as phone number, email, or address.

### 5. Carrier Agreement
* **Carrier Agreement** (`carrier_agreement`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether or not you agree to the carrier agreement. Select 'Yes' to agree, or 'No' to disagree.

### 6. Carrier License
* **Carrier License** (`carrier_license`, text, required: false): If you agreed to the carrier agreement, you will need to enter your carrier license number.

### 7. Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): You can add any additional notes about the agreement in this field.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the guidelines below or contact our support team for assistance.
* If you are unable to complete the form, please contact our support team for help.
