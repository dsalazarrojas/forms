# Motorcoach Travel Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about a motorcoach travel request, including trip details, contact information, and special requests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the trip and its purpose.
2. Select the departure date and time for the trip.
3. Select the arrival date and time for the trip.
4. Enter the origin of the trip.
5. Enter the destination of the trip.
6. Enter the number of passengers.
7. Add any special requests or requirements for the trip.
8. Enter your name and contact information, including email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the trip and its purpose.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to depart for the trip.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, time, required): Select the time you plan to depart for the trip.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to arrive at your destination.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required): Select the time you plan to arrive at your destination.
* **Trip Origin** (`trip_origin`, text, required): Enter the location or address where the trip will start.
* **Trip Destination** (`trip_destination`, text, required): Enter the location or address where the trip will end.
* **Passenger Count** (`passenger_count`, number, required): Enter the number of passengers who will be traveling.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or requirements for the trip.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the travel service provider or a team member for guidance.
* This form is intended for internal use, and the information collected will be used to facilitate communication and coordination with the motorcoach travel service provider.
