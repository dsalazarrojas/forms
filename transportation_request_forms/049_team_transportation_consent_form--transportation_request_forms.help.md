# Team Transportation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents or guardians about their child's transportation arrangements, ensuring a clear record of consent and details about the travel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide parent/guardian information, including their name and email address.
2. Enter the student's name.
3. Specify the travel dates.
4. Choose the travel mode (e.g., car, bus, train, or flight).
5. Provide driver details, including their name, license number, and vehicle information.
6. Enter the phone number and school contact information.
7. Specify the emergency contact person(s).
8. Obtain school permission and student permission for the travel.
9. Provide additional travel reason and notes (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (`parent_information`): Please enter your name and email address as a parent or guardian of the student.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the student's full name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`): Enter your email address as a parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Information** (`guardian_info`): Please provide any necessary information about your child's guardian(s).
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`): Specify the dates of the travel.
* **Travel Mode** (`travel_mode`): Choose the mode of transportation (e.g., car, bus, train, or flight).
* **Driver Details** (`driver_details`): Enter the driver's name, license number, and vehicle information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Specify the emergency contact person(s).
* **School Email** (`school_email`): Enter the school email address.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Provide any additional notes or information about the travel.
* **School Permission** (`school_permission`): Obtain permission from the school for the travel.
* **Student Permission** (`student_permission`): Obtain permission from the student for the travel.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`): Provide the reason for the travel.
* **Driver's License** (`driver_license`): Enter the driver's license number.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Enter the vehicle make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Enter the vehicle model.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the vehicle year.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`): Enter the vehicle license plate number.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`): Choose the type of vehicle (e.g., car, bus, truck, van).
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the school name.
* **School Address** (`school_address`): Enter the school address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`): Enter the school phone number.
* **School Email** (`school_email`): Enter the school email address.
* **Notes 2** (`notes2`): Provide any additional notes or information about the travel.
