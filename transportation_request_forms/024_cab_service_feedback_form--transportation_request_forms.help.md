# Cab Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cab Service Feedback Form is designed to gather information from passengers about their experience with our cab services. This form will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure that our drivers and vehicles meet our high standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you've recently taken our cab service before filling out this form.
2. Fill out the form completely, leaving out any fields that don't apply to your experience.
3. Be honest and detailed in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Passenger name** (`passenger_name`, text, Optional): This is the passenger's name. It will be used to address feedback to the passenger. You can leave this field blank if you wish.
* **Trip date** (`trip_date`, date, Required): Please provide the date of your trip to help us pinpoint which ride this feedback is associated with.
* **Pickup location** (`pickup_location`, text, Optional): Enter the address or landmark where your trip started.
* **Drop off location** (`dropoff_location`, text, Optional): Enter the address or landmark where your trip ended.
* **Driver professionalism rating** (`driver_professionalism`, number, Required): Rate the driver's professionalism on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Vehicle cleanliness rating** (`vehicle_cleanliness`, number, Required): Rate the cleanliness of the vehicle on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Overall experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, Required): Choose your overall satisfaction with the cab service.
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, text, Optional): If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please use this field to provide them.

## Tips
* Please be honest and detailed in your responses.
* If you have any issues or concerns with your cab service, please contact our customer support team directly.
* We appreciate your feedback and look forward to serving you better in the future.
