# Animal Transport Company Liability Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Transport Company Liability Consent Form is a critical document that ensures the safe and healthy transport of animals. It gathers information about the animal, transport process, and owner's liability. This guide helps you understand each section of the form and its purpose.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide accurate and truthful information about yourself and your animal.
2. Fill in all required fields.
3. If unsure about any section, ask for clarification before proceeding.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`owner_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Last Name** (`owner_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Email Address** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`owner_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Street Address** (`owner_address`, text, optional): Enter your residential address if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of an alternate contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the alternate contact person.
* **Animal Name** (`animal_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your pet.
* **Species** (`animal_species`, select_one, required): Choose the species of your pet from the provided options.
* **Age** (`animal_age`, text, required): Enter the age of your pet.
* **Weight** (`animal_weight`, number, required): Enter the weight of your pet in pounds.
* **Gender** (`animal_gender`, select_one, required): Choose the gender of your pet from the provided options.
* **Microchip Number** (`animal_microchip`, text, optional): Enter the microchip number if available.
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text, required): Enter the location where your pet will be picked up.
* **Dropoff Location** (`dropoff_location`, text, required): Enter the location where your pet will be dropped off.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the pickup.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the pickup.
* **Transport Type** (`transport_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of transport from the provided options.
* **Species** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any special transport requirements.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any health issues or medical conditions of your pet.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): Enter any medications your pet is currently taking.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities** (`allergies_or_sensitivities`, text, optional): Enter any known allergies or sensitivities of your pet.
* **Special Feeding Instructions** (`special_feeding_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any diet requirements or special feeding instructions.
* **Veterinary Clearance** (`veterinary_clearance`, select_one, required): Choose whether your pet is healthy for transport.
* **Acknowledge Transport Risks** (`acknowledge_risks`, select_one, required): Choose whether you acknowledge the transport risks involved.
* **Accept Liability Waiver** (`accept_liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Choose whether you accept the liability waiver.
* **Understand Care Standards** (`understand_care_standards`, select_one, required): Choose whether you understand the care standards.
* **Have Pet Insurance or Alternative Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have pet insurance or alternative coverage.
* **Consent to Transport** (`consent_transport`, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to the transport of your pet.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (`agree_to_terms`, select_one, required): Choose whether you agree to all terms and conditions.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of consent.
* **Additional Notes or Instructions** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or instructions for the transporter.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If you are unsure about any section, ask for clarification.
- Ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
