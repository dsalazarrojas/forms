## Student Transportation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for students to request transportation to and from school or work/school. You can use it to arrange for your school to provide transportation to and from your school, home, or work locations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Provide your details in the required fields and select your transportation type from the dropdown options.
*   Enter the locations for pickup and dropoff.
*   Include any additional notes if necessary.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Transport** (`student_transportation_form`, text): Enter a brief description of your transportation request.
*   **Student First Name** (`student_first_name`, text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date): This field is a duplicate of the previous field. Please ensure you have filled out the correct date of birth.
*   **Student Email** (`student_email`, email): Enter your student's email address.
*   **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text): Enter your student's phone number.
*   **School Year** (`school_year`, text): Enter the year you're currently attending school.
*   **Transportation Type** (`transportation_type`, select_one): Select the type of transportation you require:
    *   Home: To travel from home to school.
    *   Work: To travel from work to school.
    *   School: To travel from school to another school or work location.
    *   Other: Specify other transportation options.
*   **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text): Enter the address or location where you would like to be picked up.
*   **Dropoff Location** (`dropoff_location`, text): Enter the address or location where you would like to be dropped off.
*   **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, note): Include any additional information or notes to help with your transportation request.
*   **Staff Notes** (`staff_notes`, note): Include any additional information or notes for the staff handling your transportation request.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields correctly.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your school's transportation department.
