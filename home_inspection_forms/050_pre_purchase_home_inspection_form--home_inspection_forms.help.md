# Pre Purchase Home Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a pre-purchase home inspection. It will help real estate agents and inspectors provide a report on the condition of a home before the buyer makes an offer.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information for your pre-purchase home inspection.
2. Provide your contact information and details about the home being inspected.
3. Describe the inspection findings and any issues with the property.
4. Add any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm the inspection was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre Purchase Home Inspection Form Page 1** (`title`, text, **not required**):
	+ This page is a general overview of the form. It serves as a title for the document.
* **Home Inspector Info** (`home_inspector_info`, text, **not required**):
	+ This field is for the home inspector to provide their contact information and details about the inspection.
* **Property Info** (`property_info`, text, **not required**):
	+ This field is for the home inspector to describe the property being inspected, including its address and any relevant details.
* **Inspection Results** (`inspection_results`, text, **not required**):
	+ This field is for the home inspector to describe the findings of the inspection, including any issues or concerns.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, **not required**):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or observations the home inspector has about the inspection.
* **Home Inspector Signature** (`home_inspector_signature`, text, **not required**):
	+ This field is for the home inspector to sign and date the inspection report.

## Tips

* Be thorough and detail-oriented when completing this form to ensure the buyer and seller have a clear understanding of the property's condition.
* Use the form for each inspection, even if the property is not being purchased.
* This form is intended for use in a real estate context, so be sure to follow local and professional guidelines for home inspections.
