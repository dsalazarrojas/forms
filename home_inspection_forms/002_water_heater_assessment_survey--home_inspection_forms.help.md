# Water Heater Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Heater Assessment Survey is a form used to collect information about a water heater's status and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of water heater you are assessing:
	* Gas
	* Electric
	* Tankless
	* Other (please specify)
2. Indicate the current status of the water heater:
	* Working
	* Not Working
	* Unknown
3. Optional: If the water heater is not working, enter a rating from 0-10 to describe its condition.
4. Add any comments about the water heater.
5. Enter the year the water heater was installed.
6. Enter the serial number of the water heater.
7. Enter the model number of the water heater.
8. (Optional) Enter the manufacturer of the water heater.
9. Describe the condition of the water heater:
	* Good
	* Needs Maintenance
	* Bad
	* Unknown
10. Enter the location where the water heater is installed.
11. (Optional) Enter the date the water heater was removed.
12. (Optional) Enter the location where the water heater was installed.
13. Add any additional notes about the installation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Water Heater Type**: Choose the type of water heater you are assessing.
* **Current Status**: Indicate if the water heater is working or not. If it's not working, please describe its current status.
* **Temperature**: (Optional) Enter a rating from 0-10 to describe the water heater's temperature performance.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the water heater.
* **Year Installed**: Enter the year the water heater was installed.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the water heater.
* **Model Number**: Enter the model number of the water heater.
* **Manufacturer**: (Optional) Enter the manufacturer of the water heater.
* **Condition**: Describe the condition of the water heater. Choose one of the options or provide a detailed description.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the water heater is installed.
* **Date Installed**: Enter the date the water heater was installed.
* **Date Removed**: (Optional) Enter the date the water heater was removed.
* **Installation Location**: Enter the location where the water heater was installed.
* **Installation Notes**: (Optional) Add any additional notes about the installation.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about a question, try to make an educated guess or leave it blank.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
