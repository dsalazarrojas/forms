# Home Visit Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Visit Safety Checklist is designed for inspectors to document their findings during a home visit inspection. It ensures that the inspector systematically checks and reports on various aspects of the home's safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the checklist carefully to understand the items being inspected.
2. For each item, select the relevant option that reflects your observations:
   - "True": The item is present and is a concern.
   - "False": The item is not present or is not a concern.
   - "Unknown": The item is uncertain or needs further investigation.
3. Fill in the date of the visit and, if applicable, the time.
4. Add any contact numbers or notes that you may find during the inspection.
