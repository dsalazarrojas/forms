# Exterior Door Replacement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exterior Door Replacement Survey is a form used to gather information about exterior door replacement projects. It helps in collecting data on various aspects of the project, including replacement requests, project details, client information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Provide detailed information in the "Project Details" and "Project Notes" sections.
3. Select the correct options for each field based on your knowledge or research.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Home Inspection (Page 1)

* **Home Inspection** (`home_inspection_forms_page_1`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is used to describe the home's current condition and any relevant details about the inspection.

### Replacement Requests (Page 1)

* **Replacement Requests** (`replacement_requests_page_1`, `select_one`, required: false):
