# Drone Roof Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drone Roof Inspection Checklist is a form designed to collect information about the condition of a roof and any necessary repairs or maintenance. This form will help inspectors and other stakeholders in creating a comprehensive report.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the roof's condition and select the appropriate condition from the drop-down menu next to "Roof Condition".
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
3. Enter the name of the inspector in the "Inspector Name" field.
4. Enter the name of the contractor in the "Contractor Name" field (if applicable).
5. Enter the model of the drone used for the inspection in the "Drone Model" field.
6. Enter the location of the inspection in the "Inspection Location" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the inspection in the "Inspection Notes" field.
8. If images or videos were taken during the inspection, select "Yes" next to "Images" to upload them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Roof Condition 
• **Roof Condition** (`Roof Condition`, select_one, required: false): Select the current condition of the roof, which can be Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### Inspection Date 
• **Inspection Date** (`Inspection Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the inspection was performed.

### Inspector Name 
• **Inspector Name** (`Inspector Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection.

### Contractor Name 
• **Contractor Name** (`Contractor Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contractor who performed the work (if applicable).

### Drone Model 
• **Drone Model** (`Drone Model`, text, required: false): Enter the model of the drone used for the inspection.

### Inspection Location 
• **Inspection Location** (`Inspection Location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the inspection was performed.

### Inspection Notes 
• **Inspection Notes** (`Inspection Notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.

### Images 
• **Images** (`Images`, select_multiple, required: false): If images or videos were taken during the inspection, select "Yes" to upload them.

**Tips**

* Make sure to select "Yes" next to "Images" if you have images or videos to upload.
* Double-check the information entered for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the support team.
