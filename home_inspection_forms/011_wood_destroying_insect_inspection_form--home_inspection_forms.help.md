# Wood Destroying Insect Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record information about wood-destroying insect infestations in residential or commercial properties.

## How to Complete This Form
1.  Enter your inspector name and title.
2.  Provide the property location.
3.  Record the date of the inspection.
4.  Select the type of pest infestation found.
5.  Enter any notes or observations.
6.  Sign with your company name and license number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name as the inspector conducting the survey.
* **Property Location**: Enter the address or location of the property being inspected.
* **Date of Inspection**: Record the date of the inspection using the calendar selector.
* **Pest Type**: Select the type of pest infestation found from the available options.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or observations made during the inspection.
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign the form with your company name and license number (if applicable).
* **Inspector Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Inspector License Number**: Enter your license number if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are familiar with the types of infestations listed in the form.
* Please enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
* This form is not intended for official certification or licensing purposes.
