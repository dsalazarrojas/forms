# Window Treatment Cost Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Window Treatment Cost Calculator is a form designed to provide an estimate of the total cost of window treatments, including fabric and labor costs, based on the measurements of windows.

## How To Complete This Form
To use the Window Treatment Cost Calculator, simply fill out the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Window Treatment Cost Calculator** (`window_treatment_cost_calculator`): Enter the total cost of the window treatment.
* **Window Width** (`window_width`): Enter the width of the window in the measurement unit you selected (e.g., inches, feet, etc.).
* **Window Height** (`window_height`): Enter the height of the window in the measurement unit you selected (e.g., inches, feet, etc.).
* **Window Length** (`window_length`): Enter the length of the window in the measurement unit you selected (e.g., inches, feet, etc.).
* **Use Measurement Unit** (`measurement_unit`): Select whether the measurements are in feet or inches.
* **Fabric Cost per Sqft** (`fabric_cost_per_sqft`): Enter the cost of fabric per square foot of window treatment.
* **Labour Cost per Sqft** (`labour_cost_per_sqft`): Enter the cost of labor per square foot of window treatment.
* **Total Measurement Sqft** (`total_measurement_sqft`): Enter the total measurement of the window in square feet.
* **Fabric Measurement Sqft** (`fabric_measurement_sqft`): Enter the fabric measurement of the window in square feet.
* **Labour Measurement Sqft** (`labour_measurement_sqft`): Enter the labor measurement of the window in square feet.
* **Fabric Cost Sqft** (`fabric_cost_sqft`): This field should automatically calculate the fabric cost based on the fabric cost per square foot and fabric measurement.
* **Labour Cost Sqft** (`labour_cost_sqft`): This field should automatically calculate the labor cost based on the labor cost per square foot and labor measurement.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`): This field should automatically calculate the total cost by adding fabric and labor costs.

## Tips
Remember to select the correct measurement unit for the window width, height, and length to ensure accurate calculations.
