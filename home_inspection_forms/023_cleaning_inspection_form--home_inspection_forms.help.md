# Cleaning Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the cleanliness and overall condition of a residential or commercial property during a cleaning inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section and ensure you are in the correct property location.
2. Enter the inspection date and time when asked.
3. Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection (inspector).
4. Enter the name of the property owner or manager.
5. Enter the property address.
6. Answer the questions in each section based on your observations. Use the options provided to rate the cleanliness and condition of each area.
7. Enter any additional comments or observations in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date the inspection was conducted.
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time the inspection was conducted.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the property owner or manager.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property inspected.
* **Countertops - Clean and Sanitized**: Answer as follows:
	+ Pass: Countertops are clean and sanitized.
	+ Fail: Countertops are not clean or sanitized.
	+ Partial: Countertops are partially clean or sanitized.
	+ Not Applicable: No countertops were inspected.
* **Sink and Faucet - Clean**: Answer as follows:
	+ Pass: Sinks and faucets are clean.
	+ Fail: Sinks and faucets are not clean.
	+ Partial: Sinks and faucets are partially clean.
	+ Not Applicable: No sinks or faucets were inspected.
* **Appliances - Cleaned**: Answer as follows:
	+ Pass: Appliances are clean.

* **Floor - Swept and Mopped**: Answer as follows:

	+ Partial: Floors are partially clean or well-maintained.
	+ Not Applicable: No floors were inspected.
* **Kitchen Notes**: Enter any issues or observations.

	+ Not Applicable: No shower or tub were inspected.
* **Sink and Faucet - Clean**: Answer as follows:
	+ Pass: Sinks and faucets are clean.
	+ Fail: Sinks and faucets are not clean.
	+ Partial: Sinks and faucets are partially clean.
	+ Not Applicable: No sinks or faucets were inspected.
* **Mirrors - Cleaned and Streak-free**: Answer as follows:
	+ Pass: Mirrors are clean and streak-free.
	+ Fail: Mirrors are not clean or streak-free.
	+ Partial: Mirrors are partially clean or streak-free.
	+ Not Applicable: No mirrors were inspected.
* **Floor - Cleaned**: Answer as follows:
	+ Pass: Floors are clean.
	+ Fail: Floors are not clean.

	+ Pass: Floors are clean and polished.

* **Living Areas Notes**: Enter any issues or observations.
* **Trash removed and replaced**: Answer as follows:
	+ Pass: Trash has been removed and replaced.
	+ Fail: Trash has not been removed and replaced.
	+ Partial: Trash has been partially removed and replaced.
	+ Not Applicable: No trash was inspected.
* **Windows - Clean**: Answer as follows:
	+ Pass: Windows are clean.
	+ Fail: Windows are not clean.

* **Any Unpleasant Odors**: Answer as follows:
	+ False: No unpleasant odors present.
	+ Yes - Minor: Minor unpleasant odors present.
	+ Yes - Moderate: Moderate unpleasant odors present.
	+ Yes - Severe: Severe unpleasant odors present.
* **Overall Cleanliness Rating**: Answer as follows:
	+ Excellent: Property is clean and well-maintained.
	+ Good: Property is mostly clean, but some areas need attention.
	+ Acceptable: Property is somewhat clean, but there are noticeable issues.
	+ Poor: Property is not clean.
	+ Unacceptable: Property is severely dirty and needs immediate attention.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any general observations or recommendations.
* **Inspection Result**: Answer as follows:
	+ Pass: Property meets the required cleanliness standards.
	+ Pass with Corrections Needed: Property meets most standards, but some corrections are needed.
	+ Fail: Property does not meet the required cleanliness standards.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before filling it out to ensure accuracy.
* Use the options provided to rate the cleanliness and condition of each area.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the inspection, enter "Not Applicable" as the answer.
* Ensure the property owner or manager is aware of the results and any necessary corrections.
