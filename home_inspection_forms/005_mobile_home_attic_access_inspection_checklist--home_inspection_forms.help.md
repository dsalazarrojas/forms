# Mobile Home Attic Access Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Home Attic Access Inspection Checklist form is a tool used to record the details of an attic access inspection in a mobile home. This includes the inspector's name, the property address, date of the inspection, hours worked, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate page and section to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter the inspector's name in the "Inspector Name" field, which is a required field.
3. Select the property address from the "Property Address" dropdown list, which is also a required field.
4. Enter the inspection date in the "Inspection Date" field.
5. Note down the total hours worked by the inspector in the "Hours Worked" field.
6. Add any relevant notes about the inspection in the "Inspector Notes" field.
7. Enter the inspection cost in the "Inspection Cost" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspector Name** (required): This field is for the name of the inspector who performed the inspection. Enter the inspector's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Property Address** (required): Select the property address from the available options to identify the property being inspected.
* **Inspection Date** (optional): Enter the date of the inspection, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Hours Worked** (optional): Record the total hours worked by the inspector to complete the inspection.
* **Inspector Notes** (optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Inspection Cost** (optional): Enter the cost of the inspection, if applicable.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct property address to ensure the inspection is associated with the correct property.
- Enter accurate and complete information in all fields to ensure accurate records.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or support personnel for assistance.
