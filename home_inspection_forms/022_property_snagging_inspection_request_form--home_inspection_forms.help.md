# Property Snagging Inspection Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to request a property inspection for a specific property. It allows users to provide details about the property, requested inspection date, and any additional comments for the inspector.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **property details** in the first field. This is where you should provide information about the property, such as its location, address, or any specific features you want inspected.
2. Select a **requested inspection date** in the calendar format (MM/DD/YYYY) in the second field. This is the date when the inspection is required.
3. In the third field, **Inspector Comments**, you can provide any additional information or specific areas of concern you want the inspector to focus on during the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property details**: Enter a brief description of the property you want inspected, including its location, address, or any specific features you want examined.
* **Requested inspection date**: Select a date for the inspection. This will help schedule the inspection accordingly.
* **Inspector Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes you want the inspector to consider while performing the inspection.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing property details to ensure the inspector knows exactly what to look for.
* Choose a date that works best for your schedule and the inspector's availability.
* Use this field to provide any additional context or information that may be relevant to the inspection.
