# kitchen_planning_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive kitchen planning checklist designed to gather essential information for a project. It will help you collect project requirements, renovation preferences, timelines, kitchen features, design preferences, material preferences, installation requirements, testing needs, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
2. Enter your project requirements, including any specific tasks or objectives you want to achieve.
3. Specify your renovation preferences, such as desired changes or updates.
4. Provide timelines for your project, including deadlines and milestones.
5. Describe the kitchen features you want to include or modify.
6. Share your design preferences, including style, color scheme, and overall aesthetic.
7. List your material preferences, including materials, colors, and textures.
8. Specify your installation requirements, including labor, equipment, and timing.
9. Indicate any testing or inspection needs for your project.
10. Confirm your submission details, including project name, date, and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Requirements** (`project_requirements`, text, required: false): A brief description of your project's objectives and goals.
* **Renovation Preferences** (`renovation_preferences`, text, required: false): Any specific changes or updates you want to make during the renovation.
* **Timelines** (`timelines`, text, required: false): Deadlines, milestones, and expected completion dates for your project.
* **Kitchen Features** (`kitchen_features`, text, required: false): A list of features you want to include or modify in your kitchen.
* **Design Preferences** (`design_preferences`, text, required: false): Your preferred style, color scheme, or overall aesthetic for the kitchen design.
* **Material Preferences** (`material_preferences`, text, required: false): A list of materials, colors, or textures you prefer for the kitchen design.
* **Installation** (`installation`, text, required: false): Labor, equipment, and timing requirements for the installation process.
* **Testing** (`testing`, text, required: false): Any testing or inspection needs for your project, such as electrical or plumbing checks.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, required: false): Confirmation of your project submission details, including project name, date, and contact information.
