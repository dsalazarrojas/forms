# Framing Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users inspect and document the condition of a building's framing, foundation, walls, roof, electrical, plumbing, heating, ventilation, and exterior systems. It provides a comprehensive checklist for assessing the structural integrity and safety of a building.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read through each field carefully and answer accordingly.
* Provide a clear and concise description for each system or component.
* Focus on the condition of each system or component, and describe any observed issues or defects.
* Make sure to answer each field as thoroughly as possible to ensure a comprehensive assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Framing Inspection** (`1`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the building's framing system, including any signs of damage, wear, or defects.
* **Foundation Inspection** (`2`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the building's foundation, including any signs of damage, settlement, or cracks.
* **Wall Inspection** (`3`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the building's walls, including any signs of damage, water leaks, or structural issues.
* **Roof Inspection** (`4`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the building's roof, including any signs of damage, leaks, or missing or damaged roofing materials.
* **Electrical Inspection** (`5`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the building's electrical system, including any signs of damage, worn or loose connections, or flickering lights.
* **Plumbing Inspection** (`6`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the building's plumbing system, including any signs of leaks, clogged drains, or other issues.
* **Heating Inspection** (`7`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the building's heating system, including any signs of damage, leaks, or inefficiency.
* **Ventilation Inspection** (`8`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the building's ventilation system, including any signs of dust accumulation, dirty air filters, or other issues.
* **Exterior Inspection** (`9`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the building's exterior, including any signs of damage, wear, or needed repairs.
* **Foundation Inspection** (`10`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the building's foundation, including any signs of damage, settlement, or cracks.

## Tips
* Make sure to be thorough and accurate in your descriptions.
* Use your best judgment to determine the condition of each system or component.
* Take photos or notes to supplement your written descriptions.
* Review and revise your answers as needed to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
