# Brand Standard Room Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the condition and cleanliness of hotel rooms, ensuring they meet brand standards and customer expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the inspector's name and ID
2. Fill out the date and time of the inspection
3. Complete all the fields as instructed below
4. Fill out any issues found and corrective actions needed
5. Sign with your name and submit

## Field-by-Field Explanation
1. **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`): Enter your full name as the inspector.
   * Type: text
   * Required: Yes

2. **Inspector ID** (`inspector_id`): Enter your employee or contractor ID.
   * Type: text
   * Required: Yes

3. **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`): Enter the date of the inspection.
   * Type: date
   * Required: Yes

4. **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`): Enter the time of the inspection.
   * Type: time
   * Required: Yes

5. **Property Name** (`property_name`): Enter the name of the hotel or property.
   * Type: text
   * Required: Yes

6. **Property Address** (`property_address`): Enter the full address of the hotel or property.
   * Type: text
   * Required: Yes

7. **Room Number** (`room_number`): Enter the room being inspected.
   * Type: text
   * Required: Yes

8. **Room Type** (`room_type`): Choose the type of room being inspected.
   * Type: select_one
   * Required: Yes
   * Options: Standard Room, Deluxe Room, Suite, Executive Room, Accessible Room, Other

9. **Overall Cleanliness** (`overall_cleanliness`): Rate the overall cleanliness of the room on a scale of Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor.
   * Type: select_one
   * Required: Yes

10. **Floor Condition** (`floor_condition`): Rate the condition of the flooring on a scale of Excellent, Good, Needs Attention, Poor.
   * Type: select_one
   * Required: Yes

11. **Wall Condition** (`wall_condition`): Rate the condition of the walls and paint on a scale of Excellent, Good, Needs Attention, Poor.
   * Type: select_one
   * Required: Yes

12. **Ceiling Condition** (`ceiling_condition`): Rate the condition of the ceiling on a scale of Excellent, Good, Needs Attention, Poor.
   * Type: select_one
   * Required: Yes

13. **Furniture Condition** (`furniture_condition`): Rate the condition of all furniture on a scale of Excellent, Good, Needs Attention, Poor.
   * Type: select_one
   * Required: Yes

14. **Bedding Condition** (`bedding_condition`): Rate the condition of the bed and bedding on a scale of Excellent, Good, Needs Attention, Poor.
   * Type: select_one
   * Required: Yes

15. **Bathroom Cleanliness** (`bathroom_cleanliness`): Rate the cleanliness of the bathroom on a scale of Excellent, Good, Needs Attention, Poor.
   * Type: select_one
   * Required: Yes

16. **Amenities Working Order** (`amenities_working`): Choose whether all amenities are functional, if there are minor issues, or major issues.
   * Type: select_one
   * Required: Yes

17. **Brand Standards Met** (`brand_standards_met`): Choose whether the room meets brand standards, with minor or major deviations, or is non-compliant.
   * Type: select_one
   * Required: Yes

18. **Issues Found** (`issues_found`): Describe any issues found during the inspection.
   * Type: text
   * Required: No

19. **Corrective Actions Needed** (`corrective_actions`): Describe any corrective actions needed.
   * Type: text
   * Required: No

20. **Follow-Up Required** (`follow_up_required`): Choose whether a follow-up inspection is needed or not.
   * Type: select_one
   * Required: Yes

21. **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`): Sign with your name as the inspector.
   * Type: text
   * Required: Yes

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Rate all conditions with the utmost care and accuracy.
- Only submit once per inspection.
- This form is for authorized personnel only.
