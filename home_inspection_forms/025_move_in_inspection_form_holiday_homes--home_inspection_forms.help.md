# move-in-inspection-form-holiday-homes - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for recording the details of a holiday home move-in inspection. It is intended to capture the essential information about the move-in process, including the date of move-in, contact information of the renter, and any comments or notes from the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the move-in inspection from the calendar input field.
2. Enter the name of the move-in inspector who performed the inspection.
3. Enter the name of the renter associated with the move-in.
4. Enter the contact information of the renter.
5. Add any comments or notes about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Move In Date <img src="calendar-icon.png">
* **Move In Date**: Enter the date when the renter moved into the holiday home.

### Move In Inspector
* **Move In Inspector**: Enter the name of the person who performed the move-in inspection.

### Move In Renter Name
* **Move In Renter Name**: Enter the name of the person who rented the holiday home.

### Move In Contact Info
* **Move In Contact Info**: Enter the contact information of the person who rented the holiday home, such as a phone number or email address.

### Comments
* **Comments**: Add any comments or observations from the inspection.

### Notes
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or observations from the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct date of move-in to ensure accurate tracking of move-in inspections.
* Be thorough when entering the contact information of the renter to ensure that they can be reached in case of any issues.
* Use the comments and notes fields to capture any important observations or comments from the inspection.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
