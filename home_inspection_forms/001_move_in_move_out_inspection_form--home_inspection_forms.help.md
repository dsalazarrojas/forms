# Move In Move Out Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document the inspection process when a property is being moved in or out. It captures information about the inspection date, status, and findings, as well as contact information for the inspector and property address.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the property's condition at the time of inspection in the "Move In Move Out Inspection" field.
2. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
3. Record the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
4. Enter the name or ID of the inspector in the "Inspector" field.
5. Record the property's address in the "Property Address" field.
6. Enter the contact phone number of the inspector in the "Inspector Phone" field.
7. Enter the contact email of the inspector in the "Inspector Email" field.
8. Select the status of the inspection (Not Conducted, Conducted, or Pending) in the "Inspection Status" field.
9. Add any comments or notes about the inspection in the "Inspection Comments" field.
10. Select any recommendations or suggestions made by the inspector in the "Recommendations" field.
11. Select any issues found during the inspection in the "Issues" field.
12. Select any deficiencies found during the inspection in the "Deficiencies" field.
13. Record any additional comments or recommendations in the "Recommendations and Comments" field.
14. Record the date the property was moved out in the "Move Out Date" field.
15. Select the status of the move-out inspection (Not Conducted, Conducted, or Pending) in the "Move Out Status" field.
16. Record any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.
17. Select any additional issues found during the inspection in the "Additional Issues" field.
