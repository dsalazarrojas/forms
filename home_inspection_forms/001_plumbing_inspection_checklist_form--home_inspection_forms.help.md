# plumbing_inspection_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **plumbing_inspection_checklist_form** is a form designed to collect information during a home inspection. It helps to record and track details about the inspection, including the date and time of the visit, the name and ID of the inspector, the type of job, and other relevant details. This form is meant to be used by plumbers or inspectors to document their visits and provide a professional and organized record of their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form fields with the relevant information for the inspection.
2. Select the correct **Job Type** from the dropdown menu to indicate the nature of the visit.
3. Enter the **Address** and other contact information (including **City**, **State**, **Zip**, and **Country**) for the inspected location.
4. Enter your own **Inspector Name** and **Inspector ID** so that you can be identified as the person conducting the inspection.
5. Enter any relevant **Comments** or notes about the visit.
6. Once all fields are filled in, click submit to save and complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Inspection Forms**:
    * (`1`, `text`, required: false)
        This field is not actually used in this form.
* **Date Visited**:
    * (`2`, `date`, required: false)
        Enter the date on which you conducted the inspection.
* **Time Visited**:
    * (`3`, `time`, required: false)
        Enter the time at which you conducted the inspection.
* **Inspector Name**:
    * (`4`, `text`, required: false)
        Enter your name as the person conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector ID**:
    * (`5`, `text`, required: false)
        Enter your ID or number as the person conducting the inspection.
* **Client Name**:
    * (`6`, `text`, required: false)
        Enter the name of the client or customer whose home you inspected.
* **Job Type**:
    * (`7`, `select_one`, required: true)
        Choose the correct option from the list that best describes the nature of the visit:
        Service Call
        Installation
        Maintenance
        Inspection
* **Address**:
    * (`8`, `text`, required: false)
        Enter the address of the location where you conducted the inspection.
* **City**:
    * (`9`, `text`, required: false)
        Enter the city where the inspected location is located.
* **State**:
    * (`10`, `text`, required: false)
        Enter the state where the inspected location is located.
* **Zip**:
    * (`11`, `text`, required: false)
        Enter the zip code of the inspected location.
* **Country**:
    * (`12`, `text`, required: false)
        Enter the country where the inspected location is located.
* **Phone**:
    * (`13`, `text`, required: false)
        Enter the contact phone number for the inspected location or client.
* **Comments**:
    * (`14`, `note`, required: false)
        Enter any notes or comments about the visit.
* **Job Status**:
    * (`15`, `text`, required: false)
        Enter any status information or notes about the job.

## Tips
* Be sure to complete all fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If a field is marked as optional, it is still important to fill it in if possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the administrator or support team for assistance.
