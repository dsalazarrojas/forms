# Mobile Home Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Home Inspection Form is used to gather information about a mobile home's condition, sale price, and other relevant details. This form should not be used to collect sensitive or confidential information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your contact information, including your name and phone number.
2. If applicable, provide information about the seller's contact details.
3. Enter the address of the mobile home.
4. Enter the date of birth of the property's owner.
5. Enter the date of sale of the property.
6. Enter the sale price of the property.
7. Select the type of property (e.g., Mobile Home, House, etc.).
8. Enter the size of the property in square feet.
9. Enter the age of the property.
10. Describe the current condition of the property.
11. Describe the condition of the roof.
12. Describe the condition of the foundation.
13. For the electrical and other utilities (e.g., plumbing, heating, safety equipment, exterior and interior paint, electrical outlets, fixtures, and appliances), select all that apply.
14. Finally, add any additional comments or concerns you have about the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): Please enter your name and contact information to be reached for further questions or inquiries.
* **Seller Contact Information** (`seller_contact_information`, `text`, optional): If you are not the property owner, please provide the contact information of the person representing the seller.
* **Mobile Home Address** (`mobile_home_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the mobile home being inspected.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of birth of the property's owner. This information helps to establish the property's age and any applicable historical context.
* **Date of Sale** (`date_of_sale`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the property was sold.
* **Sale Price** (`sale_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price at which the property was sold.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of property being inspected (e.g., Mobile Home, House, etc.).
* **Property Size** (`property_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the size of the property in square feet.
* **Property Age** (`property_age`, `number`, optional): Enter the age of the property.
* **Condition** (`condition`, `text`, optional): Describe the current condition of the property, including any notable features or issues.
* **Roof Condition** (`roof_condition`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the roof, including any needed repairs or replacements.
* **Foundation Condition** (`foundation_condition`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the foundation, including any needed repairs or replacements.
* **Electrical** (`electrical`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the status of the electrical systems (e.g., Working, Not Working, Needs Repair, etc.).
* **Plumbing** (`plumbing`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the status of the plumbing systems (e.g., Working, Not Working, Needs Repair, etc.).
* **Heating** (`heating`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the status of the heating systems (e.g., Working, Not Working, Needs Repair, etc.).
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the status of the safety equipment (e.g., Working, Not Working, Needs Repair, etc.).
* **Exterior Paint** (`exterior_paint`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the status of the exterior paint (e.g., True, False, Needs Repair, etc.).
* **Interior Paint** (`interior_paint`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the status of the interior paint (e.g., True, False, Needs Repair, etc.).
* **Electrical Outlets** (`electrical_outlets`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the status of the electrical outlets (e.g., Working, Not Working, Needs Repair, etc.).
* **Electrical Fixtures** (`electrical_fixtures`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the status of the electrical fixtures (e.g., Working, Not Working, Needs Repair, etc.).
* **Plumbing Fixtures** (`plumbing_fixtures`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the status of the plumbing fixtures (e.g., Working, Not Working, Needs Repair, etc.).
* **Heating Fixtures** (`heating_fixtures`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the status of the heating fixtures (e.g., Working, Not Working, Needs Repair, etc.).
* **Appliances** (`appliances`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the status of the appliances (e.g., True, False, Needs Repair, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns you may have about the property.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy.
- Ensure that all fields are complete and legible.
- If you are unsure about a particular field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
- This form should not be used to collect sensitive or confidential information.
- The information you provide is for the purpose of the inspection and evaluation of the property.
