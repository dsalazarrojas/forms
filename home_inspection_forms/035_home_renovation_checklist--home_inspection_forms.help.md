# Home Renovation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Renovation Checklist is a comprehensive guide to help homeowners and project managers track and document the progress of a home renovation project. This form is designed to cover various aspects of the renovation process, from general information to specific tasks and materials required for different areas of the project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form's layout and understanding the different sections and fields.
2. Go through each field and provide the necessary information for the specific task or area of the project.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions and fields, as indicated by the "required" label.
4. Use the "hint" provided for each field to guide you through the process.
5. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Info (page 1)
* **General Info**: This field is for capturing general information about the project, such as project name, address, and contact details. Please provide a brief description of the project.

### Exterior Work (page 2)
* **Exterior Work**: This field is for recording any exterior work required for the project, including repairs, painting, or replacement of doors, windows, or roofs. Please list all exterior work to be done.

### Interior Work (page 3)
* **Interior Work**: This field is for recording interior work needed for the project, including carpentry, tiling, or installing electrical or plumbing fixtures. Please list all interior work to be done.

### Electrical (page 4)
* **Electrical**: This field is for capturing any electrical work needed for the project, such as wiring, circuit breaker, or installing new lighting fixtures. Please list all electrical work to be done.

### Plumbing (page 5)

### Structural (page 6)

### Foundation (page 7)

### Roofing (page 8)

### Flooring (page 9)

### Trim (page 13)

### Cabinets (page 14)

## Tips

* Double-check all fields to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If unsure about any field, please refer to the project plan or consult with the project manager.
* This form is designed to be a general guide and may require adaptation for specific projects.
* Please use the space provided for each field to list all work needed.
