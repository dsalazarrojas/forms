# Athlete Performance Video Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Performance Video Upload Form is a tool for athletes to upload video of their performance, which will be reviewed by coaches or scouts for recruitment, tryouts, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your athlete full name in the "Athlete Full Name" field.
2. Fill in your athlete ID number in the "Athlete ID Number" field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Provide your contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your primary sport in the "Sport" field.
6. Enter your main position played in the "Primary Position" field.
7. Enter the name of your current or former team in the "Team Name" field.
8. Choose the purpose of your video submission from the "Purpose of Video Submission" field.
9. Choose the type of footage in the "Type of Video" field.
10. Enter the date the video was recorded in the "Video Recording Date" field.
11. Specify the location where the video was recorded in the "Video Location" field.
12. Provide information about your opponent or context in the "Opponent or Context" field (if applicable).
13. Enter the length of the video in minutes in the "Video Duration" field.
14. Upload your video file in the "Video File Upload" field or provide a link to your video in the "Video Link Alternative" field.
15. Describe any additional files you'd like to upload in the "Additional Files" field.
16. List any key highlights for coaches to note in the "Key Highlights to Note" field.
17. List any relevant statistics or achievements in the "Relevant Stats or Achievements" field (if applicable).
18. Provide any coach references who can vouch for your footage in the "Coach References" field (if applicable).
19. Specify whether you grant permission to share your video with other coaches or scouts in the "Permission to Share Video" field.
20. Choose your preferred follow-up contact method in the "Follow-Up Preference" field.
21. Add any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Athlete ID Number**: If you have a student or team identification number, enter it here.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Sport**: Select your primary sport from the list provided.
* **Primary Position**: Enter your main position played.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your current or former team.
* **Purpose of Video Submission**: Choose the reason for submitting your video, such as college recruitment, professional tryout, or team selection.
* **Type of Video**: Choose the type of footage you're submitting, such as game footage, practice, highlights, or skills demonstration.
* **Video Recording Date**: Enter the date the video was recorded.
* **Video Location**: Specify where the video was recorded.
* **Opponent or Context**: If applicable, provide information about your opponent or context.
* **Video Duration**: Enter the length of the video in minutes.
* **Video File Upload**: Upload your video file or provide a link to it.
* **Additional Files**: Upload any additional videos or documents you'd like to share.
* **Key Highlights to Note**: Describe any key highlights for coaches to notice.
* **Relevant Stats or Achievements**: List any relevant statistics or achievements.
* **Coach References**: If applicable, list coaches who can vouch for your footage.
* **Permission to Share Video**: Specify whether you grant permission to share your video with other coaches or scouts.
* **Follow-Up Preference**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional information or comments.
