# Athlete Biometric Data Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Biometric Data Consent Form is a crucial document that ensures athletes understand their biometric data rights and responsibilities. It helps to establish a clear and transparent process for data collection, storage, and management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the `Athlete Full Name` field.
2. Provide your ID number in the `Athlete ID Number` field.
3. Select your date of birth in the `Date of Birth` field in the MM-DD-YYYY format.
4. Select your primary sport from the list of options in the `Sport` field.
5. Choose the types of biometric data that will be collected from the list in the `Types of Biometric Data Collected` field.
6. Select the purpose of data collection from the list in the `Purpose of Data Collection` field.
7. Choose the data collection frequency from the list in the `Data Collection Frequency` field.
8. Select the location where data will be stored from the list in the `Data Storage Location` field.
9. Choose who will have access to your data from the list in the `Who Has Access to Your Data` field.
10. Select how long your data will be retained from the list in the `Data Retention Period` field.
11. Choose whether data can be shared with external partners from the list in the `Third-Party Data Sharing` field.
12. Select whether your data can be anonymized for research purposes from the list in the `Data Anonymization for Research` field.
13. Confirm that you understand your right to withdraw consent at any time by selecting `Yes - I Understand` in the `Understanding of Withdrawal Rights` field.
14. If you are under 18, provide your parent or guardian's name and email address in the `Parent or Guardian Name` and `Parent or Guardian Email` fields.
15. Sign your name as an athlete and the name of your parent or guardian in the `Athlete Signature` and `Parent or Guardian Signature` fields, respectively.
16. Date the form by entering the date in the `Date of Consent` field.
17. Provide the name of the witness who witnessed your consent in the `Witness Name` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Athlete ID Number** (`athlete_id`, text, required): Enter your ID number assigned by your school or team.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Select your date of birth in the MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Sport** (`sport`, select_one, required): Select the primary sport you participate in.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your team or organization.
* **Types of Biometric Data Collected** (`data_types_collected`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of biometric data you are willing to have collected.
* **Purpose of Data Collection** (`data_collection_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the primary purpose for data collection.
* **Data Collection Frequency** (`collection_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often data will be collected.
* **Data Storage Location** (`data_storage_location`, select_one, required): Select where data will be stored.
* **Who Has Access to Your Data** (`data_access`, select_multiple, required): Select who will have access to your data.
* **Data Retention Period** (`data_retention_period`, select_one, required): Select how long data will be kept.
* **Third-Party Data Sharing** (`third_party_sharing`, select_one, required): Select whether data can be shared with external partners.
* **Data Anonymization for Research** (`data_anonymization`, select_one, required): Select whether your data can be anonymized for research purposes.
* **Understanding of Withdrawal Rights** (`right_to_withdraw`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand you can withdraw consent at any time.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, optional): Enter your parent or guardian's name if you are under 18.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, email, optional): Enter your parent or guardian's email address if you are under 18.
* **Athlete Signature** (`athlete_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the athlete.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name as the parent or guardian if you are under 18.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, date, required): Date the form by entering the date when you signed it.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness who witnessed your consent.

## Tips

- Please take your time to read and understand each field carefully before answering.
- Make sure to answer truthfully and accurately.
- If you are under 18, please have your parent or guardian review and sign the form with you.
- Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
- Remember that you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.
