# Basketball Tryout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for basketball players to register for a tryout with our organization. It collects personal and contact information, as well as details about their basketball experience and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Personal Information section, which includes your name, date of birth, and grade level.
2. Next, move on to the Contact Information section, where you will provide your phone number and email address.
3. Then, provide your parent/guardian's name and phone number.
4. If you have previous basketball experience, please provide details about it in the Basketball Experience section.
5. If you have any medical conditions or previous injuries, please list them in the Medical Information section.
6. Finally, add any additional comments or information you would like us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. First Name
* **First Name** (text, required): This is your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): This is your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): This is your birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Grade Level** (select_one, required): Please select your current grade level (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior).
* **Phone Number** (text, required): This is your phone number.
* **Email Address** (email, required): This is your email address.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (text, required): This is your parent/guardian's name.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (text, required): This is your parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Height (inches)** (number, required): This is your approximate height in inches.
* **Weight (pounds)** (number, required): This is your approximate weight in pounds.
* **Preferred Position** (select_one, required): Please select your primary basketball position (Point Guard, Shooting Guard, Small Forward, Power Forward, Center).
* **Years Playing Basketball** (number, required): This is the number of years you have been playing basketball.
* **Previous Teams/Leagues** (text, optional): If you have previous basketball experience, list it here.
* **Any Medical Conditions** (text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Previous Injuries** (text, optional): If you have any previous injuries, list them here.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): This is an opportunity to add any additional information you think we should know.
