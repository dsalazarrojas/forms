# Horseracing Rider Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in becoming horseracing riders. We will use this information to assess their eligibility and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that you have not already submitted this form in the past to avoid duplication of effort.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employment Status (ID: 3)**: 
    - Label: Employment Status
    - Type: select_one
    - Required: true
    You have two options for this field: Employed or Unemployed. Please select the option that best describes your current employment status.

* **Employment Type (ID: 4)**: 
    - Label: Employment Type
    - Type: text
    - Required: true
    Please provide a brief description of your current employment, including your job title, industry, and any other relevant details.

* **Horse Breed (ID: 5)**: 
    - Label: Horse Breed
    - Type: text
    - Required: false
    If you have experience working with horses, please mention the breed(s) you have experience with.

* **Horse Breed 2 (ID: 6)**: 
    - Label: Horse Breed 2
    - Type: text
    - Required: false
    If you have experience working with multiple horse breeds, please mention the second breed here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your interest in becoming a horseracing rider, and we look forward to reviewing your application.
