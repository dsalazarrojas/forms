# Adult Sports Roster Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Sports Roster Form is a collection of information about athletes participating in team activities. It is used to gather essential details such as name, date of birth, position, emergency contact, and waiver and consent information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields: athlete full name, date of birth, emergency contact name and phone, and liability waiver. The liability waiver is crucial for the athlete's safety and well-being.
2. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please provide a description in the "Medical conditions or allergies" field. This information is vital for the team's medical staff.
3. If you have a preferred jersey number, please enter it in the "Jersey number" field. This is optional.
4. If you have any insurance provider and policy number, please enter it in the "Insurance details" field. This information is optional.
5. Finally, indicate your consent to use your photos for promotional materials by selecting "True" in the "Consent to use photos" field. Please note that this field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete full name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Please enter your complete name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Position or role** (`position`, select_one, optional): Select your primary playing position, such as Goalkeeper, Defender, Midfielder, Forward, or Substitute.
* **Jersey number** (`jersey_number`, number, optional): If you have a preferred jersey number, please enter it here.
* **Medical conditions or allergies** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Provide a description of any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Insurance provider and policy number** (`insurance_details`, text, optional): If you have an insurance provider and policy number, please enter it here.
* **Liability waiver signed** (`waiver_signed`, select_one, required): Please indicate that you have read and agreed to the liability waiver by selecting "True".
* **Consent to use photos** (`consent_photo_release`, select_one, optional): Indicate your consent to use your photos for promotional materials by selecting "True".
