## Purpose
The Ultimate Frisbee Skills Assessment Form is designed to help athletes evaluate their skills and abilities in the sport of Ultimate Frisbee. This form will collect information about your experience, skill levels, and preferences to provide personalized coaching or training recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To get the most out of this form, please complete each page carefully and accurately.
2. Each question has a specific purpose, and please answer accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Athlete Info
* **athlete_info**: This page is where you can provide your contact information, including your name, email, and other relevant details. Please fill in your information accurately, as it will help us provide personalized feedback and support.

### Assessment
* **assessment**: This page is where you can evaluate your skills and abilities in Ultimate Frisbee. Please answer each question honestly, and provide as much detail as possible. This will help us identify your strengths and weaknesses, and provide targeted recommendations.

### Submit
* **Submit**: Once you have completed all pages, click the "Submit" button to send your assessment. Please review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
