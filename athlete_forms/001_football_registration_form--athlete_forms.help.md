# Football Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Football Registration Form is used to collect information from new or existing players registering for a football team. It helps the team's administrators and coaches manage player information in the team's database.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Football Registration Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed. Note that the form may not have any required fields, but it's essential to fill in any marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Player
* **New Player** (`new_player`, text, optional): This field is for entering your player name. If you're new to the team, this is where you'll enter your name. If you already have an account, you might not need to fill this in.

### Player Position
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple positions you play. For example, you can select both Fullback and Defensiveback if you play both.

### Player Name
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, optional): If you already have an account with the team, this is likely not needed. This is where you'll enter your name if you are new to the team.

### Player Email
* **Player Email** (`player_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow the team to contact you. This will also allow you to log in to the team's platform.

### Player Phone
* **Player Phone** (`player_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so the team can contact you. This is not required but will help the team reach out to you.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask a team administrator for help.
