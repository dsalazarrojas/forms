# Gymnastics Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the necessary information and consent from participants before allowing them to engage in gymnastics activities at the facility. The form ensures that the participant is aware of the rules, risks, and medical clearance required for the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's information, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Confirm that you are aware of the waiver terms and conditions.
3. Ensure that you have provided medical clearance for the participant, if required.
4. Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the waiver terms.
