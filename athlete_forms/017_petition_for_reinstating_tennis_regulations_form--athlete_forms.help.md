# Petition For Reinstating Tennis Regulations Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to provide a structured way for stakeholders to submit petitions to reinstate tennis regulations that have been altered or have caused an incident. The form will help identify the nature of the petition, the affected regulations, and any additional information that may be relevant to the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the petition and the reason for it in the "Petition Details" field.
2. Select the type of petition you are submitting (Petition, Appeal, or Reconsideration) in the "Petition Type" field.
3. Describe the reason for the petition in the "Reason For Petition" field.
4. Specify the affected regulations in the "Affected Regulations" field.
5. Indicate the type of affected regulations (Rule, Regulation, or Standard) in the "Affected Regulations Type" field.
6. Describe the impact of the change or incident in the "Impact of Change" field.
7. Provide the date and time of the incident in the "Date Of Incident" and "Time Of Incident" fields.
8. Describe the sport regulations affected in the "Sport Regulations" field.
9. Provide a detailed description of the affected regulations and their effect in the "Affected Regulations Description" field.
10. If applicable, attach relevant documents or evidence in the "Attachment" field.
11. Upload any relevant attachments in the "Upload Attachment" field.
12. Sign the form with your name and title/position in the "Sign Officer" and "Sign Date" fields.
13. Specify the date for review in the "Review Date" field.
14. Select the review status (Pending, Reviewing, Approved, or Denied) in the "Review Status" field.
15. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
16. Add any comments from the user in the "User Comments" field.
17. Sign the form with your name and title/position in the "Officer Comments" and "Officer Signature" fields.
18. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Petition Details** (1): A brief description of the petition and the reason for it.
* **Petition Type** (2): Select the type of petition you are submitting (Petition, Appeal, or Reconsideration).
* **Reason For Petition** (3): Describe the reason for the petition.
* **Affected Regulations** (4): Specify the regulations that are affected.
* **Affected Regulations Type** (5): Indicate the type of affected regulations (Rule, Regulation, or Standard).
* **Impact of Change** (6): Describe the impact of the change or incident.
* **Date Of Incident** (7): Provide the date of the incident.
* **Time Of Incident** (8): Provide the time of the incident.
* **Sport Regulations** (9): Describe the sport regulations affected.
* **Affected Regulations Description** (10): Provide a detailed description of the affected regulations and their effect.
* **Attachment** (11): Attach relevant documents or evidence.
* **Upload Attachment** (12): Upload any relevant attachments.
* **Sign Officer** (13): Sign the form with your name and title/position.
* **Sign Date** (14): Specify the date of signing.
* **Review Date** (15): Specify the date for review.
* **Review Status** (16): Select the review status (Pending, Reviewing, Approved, or Denied).
* **Notes** (17): Provide any additional comments or notes.
* **User Comments** (18): Add any comments from the user.
* **User Signature** (19): Sign the form with your name.
* **Officer Comments** (20): Add any comments from the officer.
* **Officer Signature** (21): Sign the form with your name and title/position.
* **Submit** (22): Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.
