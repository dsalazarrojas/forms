> **Soccer Player Evaluation Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The **Soccer Player Evaluation Form** is used to assess a soccer player's skills and abilities. The form is designed to help coaches, scouts, and trainers evaluate a player's technical, tactical, and psychological skills, as well as their overall performance. This form is essential for making informed decisions about player development and team selections.

## How To Complete This Form

### **To complete this form, follow these steps:**

1. Fill in the **Player Name** field with the name of the player being evaluated.
2. Choose the **Player Position** from the select menu, which reflects the player's primary role in the team.
3. Select the **Technical Skills** that the player possesses, such as Dribbling, Passing, Shooting, Ball Control, and Ball Possession.
4. Select the **Tactical Skills** that the player demonstrates, such as Ball Control, Field Reading, Decision Making, Strategic Awareness, and Vision.
5. Choose the **Physical Skills** that the player exhibits, including Endurance, Speed, Agility, Power, and Strength.
6. Select the **Psychological Skills** that the player possesses, such as Focus, Motivation, Teamwork, Confidence, and Emotional Control.
7. In the **Evaluation Comments** field, provide any additional comments about the player's skills and abilities.
8. Rate the player's overall performance with a number from 1 to 5 in the **Overall Evaluation** field.
9. If the player has potential for professional development, enter the level of recommendation from 1 to 5 in the **Recommendation** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (Text): Enter the player's name.
* **Player Position** (Select One): Choose the player's primary role in the team (Soccer Player, Midfielder, Forward, Defender, Goalkeeper).
* **Technical Skills** (Select Multiple): Select the skills you've observed in the player's technical abilities, such as Dribbling, Passing, Shooting, Ball Control, and Ball Possession.
* **Tactical Skills** (Select Multiple): Select the skills you've observed in the player's tactical abilities, such as Ball Control, Field Reading, Decision Making, Strategic Awareness, and Vision.
* **Physical Skills** (Select Multiple): Select the skills you've observed in the player's physical abilities, including Endurance, Speed, Agility, Power, and Strength.
* **Psychological Skills** (Select Multiple): Select the skills you've observed in the player's psychological abilities, such as Focus, Motivation, Teamwork, Confidence, and Emotional Control.
* **Evaluation Comments** (Text): Provide any additional comments or observations about the player's skills and abilities.
* **Overall Evaluation** (Number): Rate the player's overall performance from 1 to 5.
* **Recommendation** (Number): If you believe the player has potential for professional development, rate the level of recommendation from 1 to 5.

### Tips
* Please fill in the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Make sure to choose the correct options for each field.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer back to the player's performance or behavior during the evaluation.
