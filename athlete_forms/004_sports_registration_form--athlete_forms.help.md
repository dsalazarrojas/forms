# Sports Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for athletes to register for sports activities, programs, or events. Please ensure that you are registering for the correct event or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address accurately and completely.
2. Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and emergency contact purposes.
3. Provide your date of birth for verification purposes.
4. Enter your emergency contact's name and relationship to you (e.g., parent, guardian, or spouse).
5. Enter your emergency contact's phone number for easy communication.
