# Volleyball Tryout Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and evaluate a volleyball player's performance during a tryout session. The form gathers information about the player's name, position, team, physical attributes, skill level, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the player's name, position, team, height, weight, and player ID.
2. Evaluate the player's skill level and select the corresponding option.
3. Enter the tryout consistency score, overall score, and evaluation date and time.
4. Add any comments or notes about the player's performance.
5. Select the tryout status (attended, declined, or pending).
6. Enter the evaluator's comments and the team's comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter the player's name.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required): Enter the player's position on the team (e.g. outside hitter, setter, libero).
* **Team** (`team`, text, required): Enter the player's team name.
* **Height** (`height`, number, required): Enter the player's height in inches.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, required): Enter the player's weight in pounds.
* **Skill Level** (`skill_level`, select_one, required): Select the player's skill level (novice, intermediate, advanced, beginner).
* **Tryout Consistency Score** (`tryistency_score`, number, required): Enter the player's tryout consistency score (1-100).
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, number, required): Enter the player's overall score (1-100).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about the player's performance.
* **Player ID** (`player_id`, text, required): Enter the player's ID number.
* **Coach Comments** (`coach_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments from the coach.
* **Player Notes** (`player_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the player.
* **Team Comments** (`team_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments from the team.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluator** (`evaluator`, text, required): Enter the name of the evaluator.
* **Evaluator Comments** (`evaluator_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments from the evaluator.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the team name.
* **Team Location** (`team_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the team.
* **Athlete Form Type** (`athlete_form_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of athlete form (volleyball, soccer, other).
* **Tryout Date** (`tryout_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the tryout.
* **Tryout Time** (`tryout_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the tryout.
* **Tryout Location** (`tryout_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the tryout.
* **Tryout Notes** (`tryout_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the tryout.
* **Tryout Status** (`tryout_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the tryout status (attended, declined, pending).

Note: If a field is marked as required, it is essential to fill in that field. If a field is optional, it is not required but can be filled in for additional information.
