# Football League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to register football players and teams in a league. It collects information such as player name, contact details, team information, and game schedule.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your player details, including name, email, phone number, and age.
2. Select your team from the dropdown list of teams.
3. Choose your player type (Forward, Midfielder, or Defender).
4. Select your position (Goalkeeper, Forward, or Defender).
5. Enter your jersey number.
6. Choose your shirt color.
7. Select your game schedule and time.
8. Choose the game field (Grass, Turf, or Indoor).
9. Rate your and your team's abilities (Good or Excellent).
10. Enter your team's logo.
11. Enter your team's description.
12. Enter any game details and comments.
13. Select the game status (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
14. Review your information and click "Submit" to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Age** (number, required): Enter your age.
* **Height** (number, required): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (number, required): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Team** (select_one, required): Select your team from the dropdown list.
* **Coach Name** (text, optional): Enter your coach's name (if you have one).
* **Coach Email** (email, optional): Enter your coach's email (if you have one).
* **Team Name** (text, required): Enter your team name.
* **Player Type** (select_one, optional): Choose your position (Forward, Midfielder, or Defender).
* **Position** (select_multiple, required): Choose your position (Goalkeeper, Forward, or Defender).
* **Jersey Number** (number, required): Enter your jersey number.
* **Shirt Color** (select_one, required): Choose your shirt color (Red, Blue, or White).
* **Game Schedule** (date, required): Enter the game schedule in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Game Time** (time, required): Enter the game time in the format HH:MM.
* **Game Field** (select_one, required): Choose the game field (Grass, Turf, or Indoor).
* **Player Abilities** (select_multiple, optional): Rate your abilities (Good or Excellent).
* **Team Logo** (text, required): Enter your team logo.
* **Team Description** (text, optional): Enter your team description.
* **Game Details** (text, optional): Enter any game details.
* **Game Comments** (text, optional): Enter any game comments.
* **Game Status** (select_one, required): Choose the game status (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Submit** (select_one, required): Click "Submit" to complete the registration process.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have entered accurate and complete information.
* Double-check your details before clicking "Submit".
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the league administrators.
