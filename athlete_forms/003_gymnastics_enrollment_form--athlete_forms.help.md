# Gymnastics Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gymnastics Enrollment Form is a tool for students to register for gymnastics classes and provide necessary information for the administration team to assign suitable class schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your valid Email address.
3. Provide your birth date to help us understand your age and eligibility for certain classes.
4. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
5. Write a brief description of your gymnastics experience, including years of experience.
6. Select the class schedules that you are interested in (you can select multiple schedules).
7. Choose your preferred Class Time from the options provided.
8. Click the "Submit" button to send your enrollment request.
9. If required, provide any additional Notes about your enrollment request.
10. Finally, confirm your enrollment by selecting the desired options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth to help us understand your age and eligibility for certain classes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number for any further questions or updates.
* **Experience** (`experience`, Text, Optional): Write a brief description of your gymnastics experience, including years of experience.
* **Class Schedules** (`class_schedules`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the days you are interested in enrolling in classes.
* **Class Times** (`class_times`, Select One, Optional): Choose your preferred Class Time from the provided options.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Text, Optional): Click to send your enrollment request.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Provide any additional information about your enrollment request.
* **Confirm Enrollment** (`confirm_enrollment`, Select Multiple, Optional): Confirm your enrollment by selecting the desired options.
* **Enrollments** (`enrollments`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your preferred enrollment options.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any errors or rejections.
* If you have any questions or issues, please contact the administration team.
* Please do not submit duplicate enrollment requests.
