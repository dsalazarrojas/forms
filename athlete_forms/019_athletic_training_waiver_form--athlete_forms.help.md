# Athletic Training Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive waiver and consent document for athletes to provide medical and safety information before participating in athletic activities. It ensures the athlete's and organization's liability and responsibilities are clearly understood and managed.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your personal details: Fill in your full name, date of birth, age, email address, phone number, and sport or activity you will participate in.
* Provide medical information: Answer questions about any health conditions, allergies, medications, previous injuries, and physical limitations you may have.
* Confirm your medical clearance: Check that you have been cleared to participate by a physician.
* Acknowledge risks: Confirm that you understand the risks involved in athletic participation and agree to assume personal risk.
* Release of liability: Agree to release the organization from liability for any injuries or damages during participation.
* Indemnify organization: Agree to indemnify the organization against claims or suits arising from your participation.
* Emergency authorization: Authorize emergency medical treatment in case you are unable to provide consent.
* Acknowledge the waiver: Confirm that you have read and understand this waiver.
* Optional: If under 18 years old, provide parent or guardian consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (2)**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Date of Birth (3)**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Age (4)**: Enter your current age in years.
* **Email Address (5)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number (6)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Sport/Activity (7)**: Select the sport or activity you will participate in.
* **Organization/Team (8)**: Enter the name of the team or organization you are representing or affiliated with.
* **Health Conditions (10)**: List any medical conditions that may impact your athletic performance or safety.
* **Allergies (11)**: List any known allergies to medications or materials.
* **Current Medications (12)**: List all medications you regularly take.
* **Previous Injuries (13)**: List any prior athletic injuries or surgeries you have had.
* **Physical Limitations (14)**: Describe any physical limitations that may affect your performance or ability to participate.
* **Physician Cleared (15)**: Check that you have been cleared to participate by a physician.
* **I understand risks (16)**: Confirm that you understand the risks of athletic participation.
* **I assume personal risk (17)**: Check that you agree to assume personal risk for injuries during participation.
* **I Release Organization (18)**: Check that you agree to release the organization from liability.
* **I indemnify organization (19)**: Check that you agree to indemnify the organization against claims or suits.
* **Emergency Authorization (22)**: Check that you authorize emergency medical treatment if needed.
* **I have read and understand this waiver (23)**: Confirm that you have read and understand this waiver.
* **Parent/Guardian Consent (25)**: If under 18 years old, provide parent or guardian consent.
