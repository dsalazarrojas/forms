# Baseball Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baseball Enrollment Form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians of children enrolling in baseball leagues or programs. This form helps us understand the child's demographic details, medical history, and other relevant information to ensure their safe participation in the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the player's first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide the player's date of birth in the "MM/DD/YYYY" format.
3. Select the player's gender from the provided options.
4. Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
5. Provide their email address for communication purposes.
6. Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
7. Enter the player's home address, including the complete street address.
8. Enter the city and state where the player resides.
9. Enter the player's zip code.
10. Select the division or age group the player will be participating in.
11. Enter the current school name (if applicable).
12. Select the player's grade level.
13. Choose the years of previous baseball experience.
14. Select the positions the player has played before.
15. Indicate which hand the player bats and throws with.
16. Enter any medical conditions or allergies.
17. List any medications the player is currently taking.
18. Enter the name of the medical insurance provider.
19. Provide the emergency contact's full name.
20. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
21. Specify the relationship with the emergency contact.
22. Indicate if transportation assistance is needed.
23. Select whether any siblings are also enrolling.
24. Choose how you heard about the baseball league or program.
25. Indicate your interest in volunteering as a parent or coach.
26. Select the uniform T-shirt size.
27. Confirm that you understand and accept the liability waiver agreement.
28. Provide your full name as the parent or guardian.
29. Enter the date you sign this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player First Name**: Enter the player's given first name.
* **Player Last Name**: Enter the player's given last name.
* **Player Date of Birth**: Enter the player's date of birth in the "MM/DD/YYYY" format.
* **Player Gender**: Select the player's gender.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name**: Enter the parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the parent or guardian's email address for communication purposes.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Home Address**: Enter the complete street address where the player resides.
* **City**: Enter the city where the player resides.
* **State**: Enter the state where the player resides.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the player's zip code.
* **Division or Age Group**: Select the division or age group the player will be participating in.
* **Current School Name**: Enter the current school name (if applicable).
* **Grade Level**: Select the player's current grade level.
* **Previous Baseball Experience**: Select the years of previous baseball experience.
* **Positions Played**: Select all the positions the player has played before.
* **Bats**: Indicate which hand the player bats with.
* **Throws**: Indicate which hand the player throws with.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: Enter any medical conditions or allergies the player has.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications the player is currently taking.
* **Medical Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of the medical insurance provider.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify the relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Transportation Assistance Needed**: Indicate if transportation assistance is needed.
* **Sibling Also Enrolling**: Select whether any siblings are also enrolling.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Choose how you heard about the baseball league or program.
* **Parent Volunteer Interest**: Indicate your interest in volunteering as a parent or coach.
* **Uniform T-Shirt Size**: Select the uniform T-shirt size.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement**: Confirm that you understand and accept the liability waiver agreement.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign this form.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill in all the required fields correctly to avoid any issues with registration.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact us for clarification.
* Please review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
