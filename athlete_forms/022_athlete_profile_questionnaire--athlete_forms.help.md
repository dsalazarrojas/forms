# Athlete Profile Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Profile Questionnaire is designed to collect essential information about you as an athlete. This form is used to assess your eligibility, athletic background, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2.  Fill out each section carefully, and if you're unsure about a field, refer to the field explanations below.
3.  Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Athlete Full Name** (Required)
    *   This field collects your full name, including first and last name.
*   **Preferred Name** (Optional)
    *   If you prefer to be addressed by a nickname or a shorter name, please enter it here.
*   **Date of Birth** (Required)
    *   Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format (e.g., 02-12-1999).
*   **Gender** (Required)
    *   Select your gender from the options provided.
*   **Athenite ID Number** (Optional)
    *   If you have a student or team identification number, please enter it here.
*   **Email Address** (Required)
    *   Enter your primary contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (Required)
    *   Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Street Address** (Required)
    *   Enter your home address.
*   **City** (Required)
    *   Enter the city of your residence.
*   **State or Province** (Required)
    *   Enter the state or province where you reside.
*   **ZIP or Postal Code** (Required)
    *   Enter the ZIP or postal code where you reside.
*   **Primary Sport** (Required)
    *   Select the primary sport you play.
*   **Secondary Sports** (Optional)
    *   If you participate in other sports, select them from the options provided.
*   **Primary Position** (Required)
    *   Enter the main position you play in your sport.
*   **Years of Experience** (Required)
    *   Enter the number of years you've been playing this sport.
*   **Current Team Level** (Required)
    *   Select your current competition level.
*   **School Name** (Required)
    *   Enter the name of your current school.
*   **Grade Level** (Required)
    *   Select your current grade level.
*   **Grade Point Average** (Optional)
    *   If applicable, enter your current GPA.
*   **Height** (Required)
    *   Enter your height in feet and inches or cm.
*   **Weight** (Required)
    *   Enter your weight in pounds or kg.
*   **Parent or Guardian Name** (Required)
    *   Enter the full name of your parent or guardian.
*   **Parent or Guardian Email** (Required)
    *   Enter the contact email of your parent or guardian.
*   **Parent or Guardian Phone** (Required)
    *   Enter the contact phone number of your parent or guardian.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (Required)
    *   Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (Required)
    *   Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (Required)
    *   List any medical conditions or allergies you have.
*   **Current Medications** (Optional)
    *   If you take any medications, list them.
*   **Athletic Goals** (Required)
    *   Enter your athletic goals.
*   **Previous Teams or Clubs** (Optional)
    *   If you have played on previous teams or in clubs, list them.
*   **Athletic Achievements** (Optional)
    *   List any awards, honors, or accomplishments you've achieved.
*   **Why Do You Want to Join** (Required)
    *   Explain what motivates you to join.
*   **Availability** (Optional)
    *   If you have any schedule restrictions, enter them.
*   **How Did You Hear About Us** (Required)
    *   Select where you heard about us from.
*   **Additional Comments** (Optional)
    *   Enter any other relevant information you feel is important for us to know.

Please fill out this form with care and attention to detail. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out.
