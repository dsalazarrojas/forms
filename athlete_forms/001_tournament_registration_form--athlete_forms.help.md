# tournament_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tournament registration form is designed to collect information from teams participating in a tournament. This form helps the tournament organizers to efficiently manage team registrations and get the necessary details to proceed with the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the team details in the "Team Details" field.
2. Enter the team name in the "Team Name" field.
3. Enter the team manager's name in the "Team Manager Name" field.
4. Enter the team manager's phone number in the "Team Manager Phone" field.
5. Enter the team manager's email address in the "Team Manager Email" field.
6. List all player names in the "Player Names" field.
7. Enter the player count in the "Player Count" field.
8. Enter player details, such as name and any other relevant information, in the "Player Details" field.
9. Select the division for the team in the "Team Manager Division" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Details** (`team_details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your team.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required): Enter your team name.
* **Team Manager Name** (`team_manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the team manager.
* **Team Manager Phone** (`team_manager_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the team manager's phone number.
* **Team Manager Email** (`team_manager_email`, `text`, email, required): Enter the team manager's email address.
* **Player Names** (`player_names`, `note`, required): List all player names.
* **Player Count** (`player_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of players on your team.
* **Player Details** (`player_details`, `text`, required): Enter player details, such as name and any other relevant information.
* **Player Name 1** (`player_name`, `text`, optional): Enter a player name.
* **Team Manager Division** (`team_manager_division`, `select_one`, required): Select the division for your team.
  The options for this field are: Division 1, Division 2, and Division 3.
  Select the division that best represents your team.
