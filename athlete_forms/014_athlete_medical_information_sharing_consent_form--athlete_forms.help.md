# Athlete Medical Information Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical information sharing consent from athletes, which includes the types of information to share, with whom to share it, and the purpose and method of sharing. It also covers emergency information sharing, consent duration, and the athlete's understanding of revocation rights, HIPAA rights, and electronic communication consent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Provide your student or team identification number, if applicable.
3. Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
4. Enter the name of your team or organization.
5. Select your primary sport.
6. Select all types of medical information you are willing to share.
7. Choose who can receive your medical information.
8. Specify the purpose of sharing your medical information.
9. Select the method of sharing your medical information.
10. Decide on emergency information sharing.
11. Choose the duration of your consent.
12. Confirm your understanding of revocation rights.
13. Acknowledge your HIPAA rights.
14. Consent to receiving medical information electronically.
15. If applicable, provide a parent or guardian's name and email.
16. Sign the form with your full name.
17. If the athlete is under 18, sign the form with your parent or guardian's full name.
18. Date the form with the current date.
19. If applicable, enter the witness name.
