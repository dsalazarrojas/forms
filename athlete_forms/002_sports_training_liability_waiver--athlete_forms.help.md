# Sports Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Training Liability Waiver form is designed to collect athlete-specific information for safety and emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information ready, such as your personal details and emergency contact information.
2. Enter your first name in the first_name field.
3. Enter your last name in the last_name field.
4. Select the date of your training session or the date you're enrolling in the training program.
5. Enter your date of birth in the date_of_birth field.
6. Provide your address in the address field.
7. Enter your contact phone number in the contact_phone field.
8. Enter your contact email in the contact_email field.
9. Enter your emergency contact's name in the emergency_contact_name field.
10. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the emergency_contact_phone field.
11. Enter your emergency contact's email in the emergency_contact_email field.
12. Provide a description of your emergency contact relationship in the emergency_contact_relationship field.
13. Finally, sign in the emergency_signature field, which serves as a verification of the athlete's agreement to the waiver terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sports_training_liability_waiver** (note): This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about your sports training.
* **first_name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **date** (date): Select the date of your training session or the date you're enrolling in the training program.
* **date_of_birth** (date): Enter your date of birth.
* **address** (text): Provide your address.
* **contact_phone** (text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **contact_email** (email): Enter your contact email address.
* **emergency_contact_name** (text): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **emergency_contact_phone** (text): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **emergency_contact_email** (email): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **emergency_contact_relationship** (text): Provide a description of your emergency contact's relationship to you.
* **emergency_signature** (text): Sign this field to verify your agreement to the waiver terms.
