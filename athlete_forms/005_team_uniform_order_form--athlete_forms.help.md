# team_uniform_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The team_uniform_order_form is used to collect information from customers to place a team uniform order. This form is designed to be completed by customers who want to order uniforms for their team. The form is intended to gather all necessary information to process the order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for "Order Information" on the first page.
2. Provide personal details in the "Customer Info" section.
3. Enter shipping information.
4. Add any additional customer notes.
5. Enter a valid email address.
6. Enter your team's phone number.
7. Add any additional information about your team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Information** (Order Information, text, required): Enter the team uniform order details, including order type, quantity, and any other relevant information.
* **Customer Info** (Customer Info, text, optional): Enter your personal details, such as name and contact information.
* **Shipping Info** (Shipping Info, text, optional): Enter your shipping information, including shipping address and any other relevant details.
* **Customer Notes** (Customer Notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your team's phone number where you can be reached.
* **Team Info** (Team Info, text, optional): Add any additional information about your team, such as team name, jersey number, etc.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure accurate processing of your order.
- Double-check your email address for correctness before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
