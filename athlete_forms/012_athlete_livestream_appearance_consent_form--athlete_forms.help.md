# Athlete Livestream Appearance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and obtain consent from athletes participating in livestream events. It ensures that they understand and agree to the terms of their appearance in the livestream, including recording and content usage.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the personal details section with your full name, athlete ID number, and date of birth.
2. Enter the name of your team or organization.
3. Select your primary sport.
4. Provide the name and date of the livestream event.
5. Choose where the livestream will be hosted (e.g., YouTube, Twitch, etc.).
6. Select the type of appearance you'll have in the livestream (e.g., live interview, gameplay commentary, etc.).
7. Choose the expected duration of the livestream appearance.
8. Agree to the terms of recording and content usage.
9. Specify how your image and likeness can be used.
10. Choose the duration of your consent.
11. Select whether you'd like to review content before publication.
12. If you're under 18, fill out the parent/guardian section with their name and email.
13. Sign the form to validate your consent.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the livestream.
* **Athlete ID Number**: If applicable, enter your student or team identification number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team or organization.
* **Sport**: Select your primary sport.
* **Livestream Event Name**: Enter the name of the livestream event.
* **Livestream Date**: Enter the date of the livestream event.
* **Livestream Platform**: Choose where the livestream will be hosted (e.g., YouTube, Twitch, etc.).
* **Type of Appearance**: Select the type of appearance you'll have in the livestream (e.g., live interview, gameplay commentary, etc.).
* **Expected Duration**: Choose the expected duration of the livestream appearance.
* **Content Recording Consent**: Agree to the terms of recording and content usage.
* **Image and Likeness Consent**: Specify how your image and likeness can be used.
* **Commercial Compensation**: Choose whether you'll receive compensation for commercial use of the livestream content.
* **Consent Duration**: Choose the duration of your consent.
* **Content Review Rights**: Select whether you'd like to review content before publication.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (if applicable): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (if applicable): Enter the contact email of your parent or guardian.
* **Athlete Signature**: Sign the form to validate your consent.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (if applicable): Sign the form if you're under 18.
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the date this form is signed.
## Tips
* Be sure to review the form carefully before signing.
* If you're under 18, ensure your parent or guardian has reviewed and signed the form as well.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers.
* This form is a one-time consent form, and you're free to withdraw your consent at any time.
* Please note that this form may be subject to variations depending on the specific requirements and regulations of the organization or institution hosting the event.
