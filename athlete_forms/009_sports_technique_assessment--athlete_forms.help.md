# Sports Technique Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Sports Technique Assessment" form is designed to evaluate an athlete's sports technique, including their skill level, technique name, and other relevant details. This form helps trainers and coaches to assess the athlete's progress and make informed decisions for their development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in their labels.
2. Ensure that you enter the athlete's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Select the correct sport and position for the athlete.
4. Enter the athlete's technique name.
5. Choose the athlete's skill level.
6. Enter any additional technique notes.
7. Enter the date of the last evaluation.
8. Enter the score, if applicable.
9. Enter any comments or feedback for the athlete.
10. If the athlete needs retesting, enter the retest date.
11. Enter the trainer's signature.
12. Enter the trainer's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Name (1)**: Enter the athlete's name, e.g., John Doe.
* **Date of Birth (2)**: Enter the athlete's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD, e.g., 1990-02-12.
* **Sport (3)**: Select the sport the athlete competes in, e.g., Soccer.
* **Position (4)**: Enter the athlete's position on the field, e.g., Goalkeeper.
* **Technique Name (5)**: Enter the name of the athlete's technique, e.g., Free Throw.
* **Skill Level (6)**: Select the athlete's skill level, e.g., High, Medium, or Low.
* **Technique Notes (7)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the athlete's technique.
* **Evaluation Date (8)**: Enter the date of the last evaluation, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Score (9)**: Enter the score, if applicable.
* **Comments (10)**: Enter any comments or feedback for the athlete.
* **Retest Date (11)**: Enter the date the athlete needs to be retested, if necessary.
* **Trainer Signature (12)**: Sign your name as the trainer.
* **Trainer Name (13)**: Enter your name as the trainer, this is a required field.
* **Date (14)**: Enter the current date.
