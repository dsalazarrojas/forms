# Athlete Annual Performance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Athlete Annual Performance Report is a tool used by coaches and evaluators to assess an athlete's performance over a specific season or year. It is a crucial tool for evaluating an athlete's strengths and areas for improvement to help them grow and develop as a player.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct reporting period (season or year).
2. Enter the athlete's name and identification number.
3. Choose the athlete's primary sport and position.
4. Enter the total number of games played and games started.
5. Evaluate the athlete's overall performance, technical skills, physical conditioning, teamwork, and attendance.
6. List the athlete's key strengths and areas for improvement.
7. Set goals for the next season if applicable.
8. Sign and date the form.
9. Add any additional comments or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (1): Enter the athlete's first and last name.
* **Athlete ID Number** (2): Enter the athlete's student or team identification number.
* **Reporting Period** (3): Choose the season or year this report is for (e.g., Fall Season, Winter Season, etc.).
* **Year** (4): Enter the calendar year for this report.
* **Sport** (5): Choose the athlete's primary sport (e.g., Football, Basketball, etc.).
* **Primary Position** (6): Enter the athlete's main position played.
* **Total Games Played** (7): Enter the number of games the athlete participated in.
* **Total Games Started** (8): Enter the number of games the athlete started.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (9): Evaluate the athlete's overall performance (Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, Needs Improvement).
* **Technical Skills Rating** (10): Evaluate the athlete's sport-specific technical abilities (Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, Needs Improvement).
* **Physical Conditioning Rating** (11): Evaluate the athlete's fitness and conditioning (Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, Needs Improvement).
* **Teamwork Rating** (12): Evaluate the athlete's collaboration and team play (Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, Needs Improvement).
* **Attendance Rating** (13): Evaluate the athlete's practice and game attendance (Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, Needs Improvement).
* **Key Strengths** (14): List the athlete's primary strengths.
* **Areas for Improvement** (15): List areas that need development.
* **Goals for Next Season** (16): Recommended goals for the athlete.
* **Coach Name** (17): Enter the name of the evaluating coach.
* **Coach Signature** (18): Type full name as signature.
* **Evaluation Date** (19): Enter the date of this evaluation.
* **Additional Comments** (20): Add any other observations or notes.

## Tips

* Complete the form thoroughly and accurately to provide a fair and comprehensive evaluation of the athlete.
* Use the form for the correct season and year to ensure relevance and consistency.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.
* Consult with your supervisor or the athlete's manager if you have questions or concerns.
