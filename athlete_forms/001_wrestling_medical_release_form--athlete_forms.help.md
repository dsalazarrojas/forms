# wrestling_medical_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical clearance information from athletes participating in wrestling events, ensuring a safe and secure environment for all participants. It is essential to complete this form carefully and accurately to guarantee a smooth experience during the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your name as it appears on your official documents, such as your driver's license or passport.
2.  Provide your date of birth to ensure correct documentation and verification purposes.
3.  If you have a medical insurance provider, please enter their name. This is optional but recommended for insurance coverage and emergency purposes.
4.  Enter your medical insurance provider's address, if applicable.
5.  If your medical insurance provider has a phone number, please enter it.
6.  The physician signature is required for medical clearance purposes. Please have a licensed physician sign this field.
7.  List the name of your emergency contact, a person responsible for your safety and well-being.
8.  Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
9.  Specify your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse, etc.).
10. Enter an alternate phone number for your emergency contact, if available.
11. List any medical procedures you are currently undergoing or have undergone in the past.
12. Disclose any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced.
13. List any allergies you have, including food, medication, or environmental allergies.
14. List any medications you are currently taking.
15. Describe your medical history, including past injuries, surgeries, and health conditions.
16. Select one of the consent options, indicating your permission for medical treatment as needed during the event.
17. Enter the name of the event you are participating in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth to ensure correct documentation and verification.
*   **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`, text, optional): If you have a medical insurance provider, enter their name.
*   **Medical Insurance Provider Address** (`medical_insurance_provider_address`, text, optional): If you have a medical insurance provider, enter their address.
*   **Medical Insurance Provider Phone** (`medical_insurance_provider_phone`, text, optional): If you have a medical insurance provider, enter their phone number.
*   **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, note, required): Have a licensed physician sign this field for medical clearance purposes.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): List the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact.
*   **Alternate Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone_alternate`, text, optional): Enter an alternate phone number for your emergency contact.
*   **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, text, optional): List any medical procedures you are currently undergoing or have undergone in the past.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Disclose any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have, including food, medication, or environmental allergies.
*   **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe your medical history, including past injuries, surgeries, and health conditions.
*   **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate your permission for medical treatment as needed during the event.
*   **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are participating in.

## Tips
*   Ensure all required fields are filled correctly to guarantee a smooth experience during the event.
*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to ensure your safety and well-being.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, please discuss them with the event staff or medical personnel.
*   Remember to sign the consent section to grant permission for medical treatment as needed during the event.
