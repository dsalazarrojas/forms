# Player Assessment And Certification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Assessment And Certification Form is used to collect and document information about a player's performance, skills, and other attributes. This form helps coaches, trainers, and other personnel to assess a player's abilities and make informed decisions about their training and development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information about the player's assessment.
3. Choose the correct options for each field based on the player's performance and attributes.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes about the player's assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Assessment** (text): Enter your observations and comments about the player's performance.
* **Certification** (select_one): Select "Certified" if the player has met the required standards, "Not Certified" otherwise.
* **Skills** (select_multiple): Choose the skills that the player has demonstrated, such as "Yes" for skills they possess, "No" for skills they do not possess.
* **Performance** (date): Enter the date of the player's performance assessment.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of the player's performance assessment.
* **Feedback** (note): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the player's performance.
* **Email** (email): Enter the player's email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter the player's phone number.
* **Player Name** (text): Enter the player's name.
* **Player Position** (select_one): Select "Yes" if the player is in a position of authority, "No" otherwise.
* **Coach Name** (text): Enter the name of the coach or trainer who assessed the player.
* **Date of Assessment** (date): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessment Type** (select_one): Select "Yes" if the assessment was a regular assessment, "No" otherwise.
* **Assessment Date** (date): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessment Time** (time): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Certification Date** (date): Enter the date of certification.
* **Certification Time** (time): Enter the time of certification.
* **Certification Type** (select_one): Select "Yes" if the certification was successful, "No" otherwise.
* **Assessment Score** (number): Enter the score of the assessment.
* **Player Age** (number): Enter the player's age.
* **Certification Score** (number): Enter the certification score.
* **Assessment Comments** (note): Enter any additional comments about the assessment.
* **Notes** (note): Enter any additional notes about the player.
* **Comments** (note): Enter any additional comments about the player.
* **Review Comments** (note): Enter any additional comments about the review process.
