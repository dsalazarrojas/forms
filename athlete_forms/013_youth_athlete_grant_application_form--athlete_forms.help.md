# Youth Athlete Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Athlete Grant Application Form is designed to collect information from youth athletes who require financial assistance to support their athletic endeavors. This form helps us to evaluate their eligibility and assess their need for financial support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name, birth date, email address, phone number, and address as required.
2. If you have any additional contact information for your parent or guardian, please provide their name, email address, and phone number.
3. Indicate your financial need by describing your current financial situation.
4. Choose the documents that you would like to submit as supporting documents.
5. Select the current status of your grant application.
6. Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful in assessing your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Birth Date** (date): Enter your birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Athlete Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Athlete Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Athlete Address** (text): Enter your address.
* **Parent Info** (text): If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's name, email address, and phone number.
* **Grant Amount** (number): Indicate the amount of money you are requesting for financial support.
* **Financial Need** (text): Describe your current financial situation, including any income or expenses that may affect your ability to support your athletic endeavors.
* **Supporting Documents** (select_multiple): Choose the documents that you would like to submit as supporting documents, such as proof of income or financial records.
* **Grant Status** (select_one): Select the current status of your grant application, such as pending, approved, or denied.
* **Supporting Info** (text): Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful in assessing your application.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out this form accurately and completely. Incomplete or inaccurate forms may result in delays or denial of your application. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
