# Soccer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about soccer players and teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your player position from the options provided.
2. Indicate whether you are a team member or not.
3. Enter your playing time (in minutes) in the "Playing Time" field.
4. Enter your age.
5. Enter your height (in centimeters).
6. Enter your weight (in kilograms).
7. Enter your player number.
8. Enter your shirt number.
9. Use the "Player Position Note", "Player Position Note 1", "Player Position Note 2", and "Player Position Note 3" fields to provide any additional comments or information about your position.
10. Use the "Team Members Note" field to provide any additional comments or information about your team members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Player Position** (`player_position`, select_one): Select your position on the team (e.g., goalkeeper, defender, midfielder, forward).
  This field is used to indicate your position on the team.
  Please select the correct option that best represents your role on the team.
- **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple): Indicate whether you are a team member or not (Yes/No).
  This field is used to determine your affiliation with the team.
  Select "Yes" if you are a member of the team, and "No" otherwise.
- **Playing Time** (`playing_time`, number): Enter the amount of playing time you have per week (in minutes).
  This field is used to calculate your playing time.
  Please enter a number between 0 and the maximum playing time allowed for the team.
- **Player Age** (`player_age`, number): Enter your age.
  This field is used to determine your age group.
  Please enter your age accurately.
- **Team Name** (`team_name`, text): Enter your team name.
  This field is used to indicate the team you are a part of.
  Please enter the correct team name.
- **Position** (`position`, text): Enter any additional comments or information about your position.
  This field is used to provide more context about your position.
  Please enter any relevant details.
- **Height** (`height`, number): Enter your height (in centimeters).
  This field is used to determine your height.
  Please enter your height accurately.
- **Weight** (`weight`, number): Enter your weight (in kilograms).
  This field is used to determine your weight.
  Please enter your weight accurately.
- **Player Number** (`player_number`, number): Enter your player number.
  This field is used to identify you as a player.
  Please enter the correct player number.
- **Shirt Number** (`shirt_number`, number): Enter your shirt number.
  This field is used to determine your shirt number.
  Please enter the correct shirt number.
- **Player Position Note** (`player_position_note`, note): Provide any additional comments or information about your position.
  This field is used to provide more context about your position.
  Please enter any relevant details.
- **Player Position Note 1** (`player_position_note_1`, note): Provide any additional comments or information about your position.
  This field is used to provide more context about your position.
  Please enter any relevant details.
- **Player Position Note 2** (`player_position_note_2`, note): Provide any additional comments or information about your position.
  This field is used to provide more context about your position.
  Please enter any relevant details.
- **Player Position Note 3** (`player_position_note_3`, note): Provide any additional comments or information about your position.
  This field is used to provide more context about your position.
  Please enter any relevant details.
- **Team Members Note** (`team_members_note`, note): Provide any additional comments or information about your team members.
  This field is used to provide more context about your team members.
  Please enter any relevant details.
