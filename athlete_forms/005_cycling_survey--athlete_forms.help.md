# Cycling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your cycling experience, skills, and habits to help us better understand your needs and provide you with more accurate and relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Go through each page and answer each question carefully.
2. Make sure to fill in all required information before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or advice from your team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This is the first page of the form where you will be asked to provide your general information such as name, email, and other contact details.
* **Cycling Experience**: This page asks about your experience with cycling, whether you have participated in any cycling events, and whether you have any cycling-related skills or facts.
* **Cycling Facts**: This page is about your knowledge of cycling facts, such as whether you know the rules of cycling or have any special cycling-related knowledge.
* **Skills**: This page asks about your skills as a cyclist, such as your fitness level, time management, and physical condition.
* **Time Management**: This page is about your time management skills and how you manage your training and racing schedule.
* **Physical Condition**: This page is about your physical condition, such as your strength, endurance, and flexibility.
* **Training Habits**: This page asks about your training habits, including the number of hours you train per week, frequency, and intensity.
* **Racing Habits**: This page is about your racing habits, including the distance you race, frequency, and location.
* **Bike Type**: This page asks about the type of bike you use for racing and training.
* **Race Frequency 2**: This page is about your racing frequency, including how often you participate in races.
* **Training Location**: This page is about where you train, including your preferred location and frequency.
* **Finisher**: This page asks about whether you have finished any races.
* **Dropped Out**: This page is about whether you have ever dropped out of a race.
* **Race Finisher**: This page asks about whether you have ever finished a race.
* **Training Location 2**: This page is about where you train, including your preferred location and frequency.
* **Race Finisher 2**: This page asks about whether you have ever finished a race.
