# Athlete Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Performance Evaluation Form is designed to assess the performance of athletes in various aspects, including athletic ability, sport-specific skills, speed, strength, endurance, agility, game intelligence, decision-making, competitiveness, practice habits, attendance, teamwork, leadership, coachability, and overall performance. This form will help coaches, evaluators, and other stakeholders evaluate athletes' progress and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Enter the Athlete's Information:
  * **Athlete Full Name:** Enter the athlete's first and last name.
  * **Athlete ID Number (Optional):** If available, enter the athlete's ID number or student/ team identification number.
  * **Evaluation Date:** Enter the date of the evaluation.

### 2. Evaluator's Information:
  * **Evaluator Name:** Enter your name and title (e.g., Head Coach, Assistant Coach, etc.).
  * **Evaluator Role:** Select your role in the athlete's evaluation (e.g., Head Coach, Assistant Coach, Position Coach, etc.).

### 3. Sport and Position:
  * **Sport:** Select the primary sport the athlete participates in (e.g., Football, Basketball, etc.).
  * **Primary Position:** Enter the athlete's main position played in the sport.

### 4. Evaluation Period:
  * **Evaluation Period:** Select the period being evaluated (e.g., Pre-Season, Early Season, Mid-Season, etc.).

### 5. Athletic and Sport-Specific Skills:
  * **Athletic Ability Rating:** Rate the athlete's overall athletic ability (e.g., Excellent, Above Average, etc.).
  * **Sport-Specific Skills:** Rate the athlete's technical skills for the sport (e.g., Excellent, Above Average, etc.).
  * **Speed Rating:** Rate the athlete's speed and quickness (e.g., Excellent, Above Average, etc.).
  * **Strength Rating:** Rate the athlete's physical strength (e.g., Excellent, Above Average, etc.).
  * **Endurance Rating:** Rate the athlete's stamina and conditioning (e.g., Excellent, Above Average, etc.).
  * **Agility Rating:** Rate the athlete's agility and coordination (e.g., Excellent, Above Average, etc.).

### 6. Game Intelligence and Teamwork:
  * **Game Intelligence:** Rate the athlete's understanding of game strategy (e.g., Excellent, Above Average, etc.).
  * **Teamwork:** Rate the athlete's ability to work with teammates (e.g., Excellent, Above Average, etc.).

### 7. Leadership and Coachability:
  * **Leadership:** Rate the athlete's leadership qualities (e.g., Excellent, Above Average, etc.).
  * **Coachability:** Rate the athlete's responsiveness to coaching (e.g., Excellent, Above Average, etc.).

### 8. Additional Information:
  * **Performance Highlights:** List key accomplishments and highlights during the evaluation period.
  * **Areas Needing Improvement:** Identify areas where the athlete needs to improve.
  * **Goals for Next Period:** Set goals for the upcoming period.
  * **Overall Performance Rating:** Rate the athlete's overall performance (e.g., Excellent, Above Average, etc.).
  * **Evaluator Comments:** Provide any additional comments or observations.
  * **Evaluator Signature:** Sign your name as the evaluator, typing it out in full.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when evaluating the athlete's performance.
* Consider multiple factors when rating the athlete's skills and abilities.
* Use clear and concise language when providing feedback.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your ratings.
* Take into account any contextual factors that may affect the athlete's performance (e.g., injuries, illness, etc.).
