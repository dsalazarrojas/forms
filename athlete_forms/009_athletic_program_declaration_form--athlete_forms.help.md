# Athletic Program Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from student athletes who are interested in participating in various sports. The form helps the athletic program to understand the student's background, interests, and medical information, which is essential for ensuring their safety and well-being during participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Student Athlete Information section with your basic details.
2.  Choose the sport you are interested in participating in from the Sport Declaration section.
3.  Provide any additional information that you feel is necessary to declare your interest in the sport.
4.  If applicable, select your preferred position or role in the sport.
5.  List any prior experience you may have in the sport.
6.  Indicate your preference for participating in Junior Varsity or Varsity.
7.  Provide the necessary contact information for your parent or guardian.
8.  Include any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your participation.
9.  List any current medications that you are taking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter your student ID number issued by the school.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Grade Level**: Select your current grade level from the available options.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school you are currently attending.
* **Home Address**: Enter your home address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your student's phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your student's email address.
* **Sport to Participate In**: Choose the sport you are interested in participating in from the dropdown list.
* **Preferred Position**: Select your preferred position or role in the sport (optional).
* **Prior Athletic Experience**: List any prior experience you may have in the sport.
* **Parent/Guardian Name**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact (if different from your parent/guardian).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact (if different from your parent/guardian).
* **Medical Conditions**: List any chronic or relevant health conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you may have, including medication and environmental allergies.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.

Please take your time to fill out the form accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your athletic program representative.
