# Return To Training Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by athletes or participants to provide their consent for returning to training after a medical condition, injury, or other circumstances that may impact their participation. It aims to ensure that the participant understands and agrees to the facility rules, participant rules, and other relevant agreements before returning to training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name** in the first field.
2. If you have a medical condition, please select the relevant options for **Medical Condition Agreement**.
3. Choose your **Return Time Agreement** to indicate when you plan to return to training.
4. Select your **Emergency Contact Agreement** to indicate your emergency contact information.
5. Read and agree to the **Facility Rules**, **Participant Rules**, and other **Acknowledgement** fields carefully. Select the relevant options for each field.
6. Once you have completed all the fields, review your selections to ensure accuracy and completeness.
7. Submit the form to complete the consent process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (required): This is the name of the participant, usually the athlete or individual returning to training.
* **Date Of Return** (optional): This field is not used in this form, as it is not necessary for the consent process.
* **Facility Rules** (required): This field requires you to acknowledge and agree to the rules of the facility, such as safety protocols, emergency procedures, and other relevant guidelines.
* **Participant Rules** (required): This field requires you to acknowledge and agree to the rules of participation, such as code of conduct, respect for others, and other relevant guidelines.
* **Emergency Contact** (optional): This field allows you to provide emergency contact information, in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Condition** (optional): This field allows you to indicate if you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation. Select the relevant options to indicate your condition.
* **Return Time** (optional): This field is not used in this form, as it is not necessary for the consent process.
* **Facility Rules Acknowledgement** (required): This field requires you to acknowledge and agree to the facility rules.
* **Participant Rules Acknowledgement** (required): This field requires you to acknowledge and agree to the participant rules.
* **Emergency Contact Agreement** (required): This field requires you to agree to the emergency contact information you provided.
* **Medical Condition Agreement** (required): This field requires you to agree to the medical condition disclosure.
* **Return Time Agreement** (required): This field requires you to agree to your return time.
* **Acknowledgement 1**, **Acknowledgement 2**, ..., **Acknowledgement 13** (required): These fields require you to acknowledge and agree to various agreements and guidelines.
* **Acknowledgement 1**, **Acknowledgement 2**, ..., **Acknowledgement 13** (optional): These fields are not used in this form, as they are not necessary for the consent process.

## Tips

* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure you understand and agree to all the fields before completing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrator.
* Once you have completed the form, you will receive confirmation of your consent.
