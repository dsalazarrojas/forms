# Player Tryout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Tryout Registration Form is a form used to collect information from players interested in participating in a tryout process. This form is used to schedule and coordinate tryouts, as well as to verify player availability and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your player information on the first page.
2. Provide your name in the "Player Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field and will be used for communication purposes.
4. Select your team from the list provided in the "Team" field. This is a required field and will help the tryout organizers schedule and coordinate the tryouts accordingly.
5. Provide your available date and time in the "Available Date" and "Available Time" fields, respectively. This information will help the tryout organizers schedule the tryouts around your availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Information** (`player_info`, text, not required): This field is for players to provide additional information about themselves.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, not required): This field is for players to provide their name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is for players to enter their email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): This field is for players to enter their phone number.
* **Team** (`team`, select_one, required): This field is for players to select their team. This will help the tryout organizers schedule and coordinate the tryouts accordingly.
* **Tryout Date** (`tryout_date`, date, not required): This field is for players to enter the date they are available for the tryout.
* **Tryout Time** (`tryout_time`, time, not required): This field is for players to enter the time they are available for the tryout.
* **Available Date** (`available_date`, date, not required): This field is for players to enter the date they are available for scheduling purposes.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, time, not required): This field is for players to enter the time they are available for scheduling purposes.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the necessary information ready before completing the form.
* Double-check your email address to ensure it's correct and accessible.
* Select the correct team to ensure that your tryout is scheduled accordingly.
* Enter your available date and time carefully, as this will help the tryout organizers schedule the tryouts around your availability.
