# Basketball Roster Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage basketball team roster information, including team name, coach and player details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the team information page (page 1) and enter the team's name.
2. Proceed to the coach's contact information page (page 2) and enter the head coach's name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter the current season year.
4. For each player, enter their full name, jersey number, position, and grade level.
5. Review and submit the form when all required fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Team Name
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required): Enter the official team name.

### Head Coach Information
* **Head Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the head coach.
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, `email`, required): Enter the head coach's email address.
* **Coach Phone Number** (`coach_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the head coach's phone number.

### Player 1 Information
* **Player 1 Full Name** (`player_1_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the first player.
* **Player 1 Jersey Number** (`player_1_jersey`, `number`, required): Enter the player's jersey number.
* **Player 1 Position** (`player_1_position`, `select_one`, required): Select the player's primary position.
* **Player 1 Grade Level** (`player_1_grade`, `select_one`, optional): Enter the player's grade level (if applicable).

### Player 2 Information
* **Player 2 Full Name** (`player_2_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the second player.
* **Player 2 Jersey Number** (`player_2_jersey`, `number`, required): Enter the player's jersey number.
* **Player 2 Position** (`player_2_position`, `select_one`, required): Select the player's primary position.
* **Player 2 Grade Level** (`player_2_grade`, `select_one`, optional): Enter the player's grade level (if applicable).

### Player 3 Information
* **Player 3 Full Name** (`player_3_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the third player.
* **Player 3 Jersey Number** (`player_3_jersey`, `number`, required): Enter the player's jersey number.
* **Player 3 Position** (`player_3_position`, `select_one`, required): Select the player's primary position.
* **Player 3 Grade Level** (`player_3_grade`, `select_one`, optional): Enter the player's grade level (if applicable).

### Additional Information
* **Assistant Coach Name** (`assistant_coach_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the full name of the assistant coach (if applicable).
* **Team Manager Name** (`team_manager_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the full name of the team manager (if applicable).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional roster information (if applicable).

## Tips
