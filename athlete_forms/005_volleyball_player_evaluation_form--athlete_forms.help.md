# Volleyball Player Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect relevant information about a volleyball player's performance and characteristics, enabling evaluators to make informed decisions about their development and placement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the player's details, including name, team affiliation, and performance evaluation.
2. Evaluate the player's performance based on the provided options.
3. Provide a written justification for your evaluation using the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Sign and date the form to ensure accountability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name**: Enter the player's full name.
* **Evaluation Date**: The date when the evaluation was performed.
* **Team Name**: The name of the team the player is associated with.
* **Coach's Name**: Optional. If applicable, enter the name of the player's coach.
* **Evaluation Result**: Evaluate the player's performance as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Evaluation Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for the evaluation result.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the player's performance.
* **Email**: Enter the player's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Optional. Enter the player's contact phone number.
* **Player Rating**: Rate the player's performance on a scale (not defined in the provided YAML).
* **Overall Rating**: Evaluate the player's overall performance as a number between 1 and 5 (not defined in the provided YAML).
* **Player Position**: Select the player's position on the volleyball team (Setter, Opposite, Middle Blocker, Libero, or Outside Hitter).
* **Player Height**: Enter the player's height (units not defined in the provided YAML).
* **Player Weight**: Enter the player's weight (units not defined in the provided YAML).
* **Player Age**: Enter the player's age (units not defined in the provided YAML).
* **Player Experience**: Enter the player's experience (units not defined in the provided YAML).
* **Colleges Attended**: Optional. Enter the colleges or institutions the player has attended.
* **High School Graduated**: Evaluate if the player has graduated from high school.
* **College Graduated**: Evaluate if the player has graduated from college.
* **Signed with**: Select where the player has signed with (Pro, College, High School, or None).
* **Evaluation Completed**: Confirm that the evaluation has been completed.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the player.
* **Coach's Signature**: The coach's signature confirms their approval of the evaluation.
* **Assistant Coach's Signature**: Optional. The assistant coach's signature can confirm their agreement with the evaluation.
* **Evaluator's Signature**: The evaluator's signature confirms their completion of the form.

Note: Some fields are not clearly defined in the provided YAML (e.g., units for player height and weight, scale for overall rating). Please clarify or omit these fields in the final output to maintain consistency and accuracy.
