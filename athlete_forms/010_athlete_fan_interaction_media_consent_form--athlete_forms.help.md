# Athlete Fan Interaction Media Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from athletes to be photographed or recorded during fan interaction events. It ensures that athletes are aware of how their media will be used and gives them control over the type of interaction and where it will be used.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Review the fields carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of each field.
3. Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`1`): Your full name as an athlete.
	+ This is the name you will be known by, and will be displayed in any media used.
	+ Please enter your name in the format "First Name Last Name".
* **Athlete ID Number** (`2`): Your student or team identification number (if applicable).
	+ If you have an ID number, enter it here to help us verify your identity.
	+ This is optional, but it can help us keep your information accurate.
* **Team Name** (`3`): The name of the team or organization you represent.
	+ This is the team or organization you are representing when interacting with fans.
	+ Please enter the full name of the team or organization.
* **Sport** (`4`): The primary sport you compete in.
	+ Select one of the options from the list to indicate your primary sport.
	+ This helps us understand the context of your event and media interaction.
* **Event Name** (`5`): The name of the event with fan interaction.
	+ This is the specific event where you will be interacting with fans.
	+ Enter the full name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`6`): The date of the event with fan interaction.
	+ Enter the date on which the event will take place.
* **Types of Fan Interactions** (`8`): Check all that apply to the types of fan interactions you are comfortable with.
	+ Select all the interactions you are willing to participate in, such as Autograph Signing, Photo Opportunities, etc.
* **Media Recording Consent** (`9`): Choose your consent level for being photographed or recorded.
	+ Select "Yes - Full Consent" if you have no restrictions, "Yes - With Restrictions" if you have some, or "No - No Consent" if you are not comfortable with any media.
* **Photo Consent** (`10`): Choose your consent level for being photographed.
	+ Select "Yes" if you are comfortable with photos, or "No" if you are not.
* **Video Consent** (`11`): Choose your consent level for being video recorded.
	+ Select "Yes" if you are comfortable with video, or "No" if you are not.
* **Audio Consent** (`12`): Choose your consent level for being audio recorded.
	+ Select "Yes" if you are comfortable with audio, or "No" if you are not.
* **Approved Media Usage** (`13`): Choose where you are comfortable with your media being used.
	+ Select all the places where you are willing to see your media used, such as Team Website, Team Social Media, etc.
* **Social Media Tagging Consent** (`14`): Choose whether you are comfortable with being tagged in social media posts.
	+ Select "Yes" if you are comfortable with tagging, "No" if you are not, or "Only on Official Team Accounts" if you only want to be tagged by your official team.
* **Commercial Use Consent** (`15`): Choose your consent level for commercial use of your media.
	+ Select "Yes" if you are comfortable with commercial use, "No" if you are not, or "Only with Additional Compensation" if you want additional compensation.
* **Consent Duration** (`16`): Choose how long your consent is valid.
	+ Select "This Event Only" if your consent is only valid for this event, "Current Season" if it's valid for the current season, "One Year" if it's valid for a year, or "Indefinitely" if it's valid until you revoke it.
* **Any Restrictions or Limitations** (`17`): Enter any additional restrictions or limitations you have.
	+ If you have any additional restrictions or limitations, enter them here.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`18`): If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's name.
	+ This field is required if you are under 18 years old.
* **Athlete Signature** (`19`): Sign your name as the athlete.
	+ This is your signature as the athlete, in your full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`20`): If you are under 18, sign your parent or guardian's name.
	+ This is your parent or guardian's signature, in their full name.
* **Date of Consent** (`21`): Sign the date of the consent.
	+ Enter the date on which you sign this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your team representative or event coordinator.
* Your consent is voluntary, and you can revoke it at any time.
* If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must sign for you.
* This form is subject to change, and we may update it as needed.
