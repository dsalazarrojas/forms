# Athletic Team Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect athlete information, including personal details, contact information, and medical history, for the purpose of team management and athlete development. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that your athlete information is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer all required questions.
2. Enter your name, jersey number, team name, date of birth, age, position, height, and weight in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your email address, phone number, home address, city, state, and zip code in the correct fields.
4. Enter the name, phone number, and email address of your parent/guardian.
5. If necessary, provide medical history, allergies, and medical conditions.
6. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Jersey Number**: Enter your assigned player number.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Age**: Enter your current age.
* **Position**: Enter your primary playing position.
* **Height**: Enter your height in feet and inches.
* **Weight**: Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Athlete Email**: Enter your primary email address.
* **Athlete Phone Number**: Enter your preferred contact number.
* **Home Address**: Enter your street address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state or province.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Parent/Guardian Name**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If different from parent/guardian, enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical History**: If necessary, provide any relevant medical history.
* **Allergies**: If necessary, list any known allergies.
* **Medical Conditions**: If necessary, list any chronic or relevant medical conditions.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled out.
