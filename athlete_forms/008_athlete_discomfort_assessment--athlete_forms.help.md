# Athlete Discomfort Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an athlete's discomfort, allowing athletic trainers and medical professionals to understand the nature and severity of the issue. It is used to identify potential injuries or issues that may impact an athlete's performance or well-being.

## How to Complete This Form
1. Provide your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter your ID number if provided by your team or organization.
3. Select the date of the discomfort assessment.
4. Choose your primary sport.
5. Select the location of the discomfort on your body.
6. Choose the type of discomfort you are experiencing.
7. Rate your pain level from 1 to 10.
8. Indicate when you first noticed the discomfort.
9. Describe the activity you were doing when the discomfort started.
10. List factors that make the discomfort worse or better.
11. Indicate if you have experienced a previous injury to the affected area.
12. If yes, describe the previous injury and when it occurred.
13. Indicate if the discomfort impacts your performance.
14. Indicate if you have sought medical attention or treatment.
15. If yes, describe the current treatment you are undergoing.
16. Indicate if you have missed any practices or games due to the discomfort.
17. Indicate if the athletic trainer should be notified.
18. Provide any additional comments about your discomfort.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Athlete ID Number**: Enter your ID number if provided by your team or organization. Leave this field blank if you don't have an ID number.
* **Assessment Date**: Select the date of the discomfort report.
* **Sport**: Choose the primary sport you participate in.
* **Location of Discomfort**: Select the area of your body where you are experiencing discomfort.
* **Type of Discomfort**: Choose the type of discomfort you are experiencing (e.g., sharp pain, dull ache, etc.).
* **Pain Scale Rating**: Rate your pain level from 1 to 10.
* **Onset Time**: Indicate when you first noticed the discomfort.
* **Activity at Onset**: Describe the activity you were doing when the discomfort started.
* **Factors That Make It Worse**: Select factors that make your discomfort worse (e.g., running, jumping, etc.).
* **Factors That Make It Better**: Select factors that make your discomfort better (e.g., rest, ice, etc.).
* **Previous Injury**: Indicate if you have experienced a previous injury to the affected area.
* **Previous Injury Details**: Describe the previous injury and when it occurred.
* **Impacts Performance**: Indicate if the discomfort is affecting your performance.
* **Sought Medical Attention**: Indicate if you have sought medical attention or treatment.
* **Current Treatment**: Describe any current treatment you are undergoing.
* **Missed Activities**: Indicate if you have missed any practices or games due to the discomfort.
* **Notify Athletic Trainer**: Indicate if the athletic trainer should be notified.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information about your discomfort.
