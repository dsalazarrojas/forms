# Athletic League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletic League Registration Form is a crucial document that helps the league manage and communicate with its registered athletes. It ensures that the league has the necessary information to contact, place, and manage athletes during the season.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents (page 1, athlete_name).
2. Provide your date of birth (page 1, date_of_birth).
3. Select your age group (page 3, age_group).
4. Choose your gender (page 4, gender).
5. Enter your email address for league communications (page 5, email).
6. Provide your primary contact phone number (page 6, phone).
7. Select the sport you are registering for (page 7, sport).
8. (Optional) If you have a preferred team, enter the name of that team (page 8, team_preference).
9. (Optional) If you have a preferred playing position, enter the name of that position (page 9, position).
10. Indicate your experience level in the sport (page 10, experience_level).
11. Let us know if you have participated in this league before (page 11, previous_league).
12. If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian (page 12, parent_guardian_name). 
13. If you are under 18, provide the contact email for your parent or guardian (page 13, parent_email).
14. If you are under 18, provide the contact phone number for your parent or guardian (page 14, parent_phone).
15. Provide the name of a person to contact in case of an emergency (page 15, emergency_contact_name).
16. Provide the phone number for the emergency contact (page 16, emergency_contact_phone).
17. If applicable, list any medical conditions or allergies you have (page 17, medical_conditions).
18. Indicate the registration fee amount you paid in USD (page 18, registration_fee).
19. Confirm the status of your registration fee payment (page 19, payment_status).
20. Sign the waiver and liability release form (page 20, waiver_signed).
21. If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must sign the consent form (page 21, parent_consent).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Name** (page 1, athlete_name): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (page 1, date_of_birth): Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
* **Age Group** (page 3, age_group): Select the age group that you belong to, based on your date of birth.
* **Gender** (page 4, gender): Choose your gender from the available options.
* **Email Address** (page 5, email): Enter your primary email for league communications.
* **Phone Number** (page 6, phone): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Sport** (page 7, sport): Select the sport you are registering for.
* **Team Preference** (page 8, team_preference): If you have a preferred team, enter the name of that team.
* **Preferred Position** (page 9, position): If you have a preferred playing position, enter the name of that position.
* **Experience Level** (page 10, experience_level): Indicate your experience level in the selected sport.
* **Previous League Participation** (page 11, previous_league): Let us know if you have participated in this league before.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (page 12, parent_guardian_name): If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (page 13, parent_email): If you are under 18, enter the contact email for your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (page 14, parent_phone): If you are under 18, enter the contact phone number for your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (page 15, emergency_contact_name): Provide the name of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (page 16, emergency_contact_phone): Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (page 17, medical_conditions): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, list them here.
* **Registration Fee** (page 18, registration_fee): Indicate the registration fee amount paid in USD.
* **Payment Status** (page 19, payment_status): Confirm the status of your registration fee payment.
* **Waiver and Liability Release Signed** (page 20, waiver_signed): Sign the waiver and liability release form to participate in the league.
* **Parent/Guardian Consent** (page 21, parent_consent): If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must sign the consent form.

**Tips**

- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the league for clarification.
