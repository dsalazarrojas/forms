# speed_training_progress_tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "speed_training_progress_tracker" form is used to track and record information about each speed training session, including speed, distance, pace, comments, and session notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the session ready, including the speed, distance, pace, and any comments or notes.
2. Enter the required information for each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Speed Session** (`speed_session`, `number`, required: false)
 Enter the speed of the athlete during this session. If you are unsure how to calculate or input the speed, please consult your training program guidelines.

### 2. **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date of the speed session. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 3. **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the name of the athlete who participated in this session.

### 4. **Speed Session** (`speed_session`, `number`, required: false)
 Enter the speed session number, as assigned by the system.

### 5. **Distance (km)** (`distance_km`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the distance covered during this session in kilometers.

### 6. **Pace (min/km)** (`pace_minutes_per_km`, `number`, required: false)
 Enter the pace at which the athlete completed this session in minutes per kilometer.

### 7. **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false)
 Enter any comments or notes made during or about this session.

### 8. **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, `note`, required: false)
Enter any additional notes or comments about this session.

### 9. **Speed Session ID** (`speed_session_id`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the speed session ID, as assigned by the system.
