# Soccer Referee Training Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Referee Training Participation Form is designed to collect information from soccer referees about their training preferences and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below, one at a time. Make sure to fill out all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *). If you have any questions or concerns about a particular field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referee First Name** (`first_name`, text, false): Enter your first name as a referee.
* **Referee Last Name** (`last_name`, text, false): Enter your last name as a referee.
* **Referee Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Referee Phone** (`phone`, text, false): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Training Course** (`training_course`, select_one, false): Choose whether you have attended the training course by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Referee ID** (`referee_id`, number, false): Enter your unique referee ID number (if applicable).
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, false): Select the date you plan to attend the training session.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, false): Select the time you plan to attend the training session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, false): Enter any additional comments or notes you have about your training participation.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your participation is recorded correctly.
- If you have any special requests or needs, please include them in the 'Notes' field.
- If you have already completed this form for a previous training session, you do not need to fill it out again.
