# football_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from football game attendees about their overall experience at the event. It will help us understand what they thought of the game, the food and drink quality, the staff's attentiveness, and their overall opinion of the stadium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Review the questions on each page of the form.
2.  Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3.  Use the provided options for select fields or type your answers directly into the text fields.
4.  Make sure to enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates about future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction (football_survey_page_1)
* **Introduction** (note, no input required): This page provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.

### 2. What was your experience at the football game?
* **What was your experience at the football game?** (text, optional): Share your honest thoughts about the overall experience you had at the game.

### 3. How did the home team perform?
* **How did the home team perform?** (select one, optional): Select "Yes" if you thought the home team did well, or "No" if they didn't. 

### 4. How did the away team perform?
* **How did the away team perform?** (select one, optional): Select "Yes" if you thought the away team did well, or "No" if they didn't. 

### 5. What was the quality of the food and drinks?
* **What was the quality of the food and drinks?** (select multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your experience. Options include "Good" and "Poor".

### 6. How well was the staff attending to your needs?
* **How well was the staff attending to your needs?** (select multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your experience. Options include "Yes" and "No".

### 7. What was your experience of the stadium?
* **What was your experience of the stadium?** (text, optional): Share your thoughts about the stadium's facilities, amenities, or overall atmosphere.

### 8. What do you think of the game?
* **What do you think of the game?** (number, optional): Rate the game on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### 9. Email
* **Email** (email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates about future events, enter your email address here.

### 10. Phone
* **Phone** (text, optional): This field is currently not used in this form, but if you'd like to provide your phone number for any reason, you can do so here.
