# national_sports_combine_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register athletes for a national sports combine event. It collects contact information, emergency contact details, and demographic information from athletes to facilitate their participation in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact details, including your name and contact email.
2. Provide emergency contact information for a parent or guardian if you are a minor.
3. Select your sport of participation from the list of available options.
4. Enter your name and date of birth.
5. Select your gender.
6. Enter your address.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.
8. Select the sports you are participating in for the combine event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact name and contact email address. (Text, required)
* **Emergency Contact**: If you are a minor, enter the contact information for a parent or guardian. (Text, optional)
* **Emergency Contact Details**: Enter any additional emergency contact information. (Text, optional)
* **Sport**: Select the sport you will be participating in. (Select One, optional)
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on event materials. (Text, optional)
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address for communication from the event organizers. (Email, optional)
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number. (Text, optional)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth. (Date, optional)
* **Gender**: Select your gender. (Select One, optional)
* **Address**: Enter your address for event communication and delivery. (Text, optional)
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your participation. (Note, optional)
* **Sport Participated In**: Select the sports you will be participating in for the combine event. (Select Multiple, optional)
* **Participate Acknowledge**: Acknowledge that you will participate in the event. (Note, optional)

Note: All optional fields are not required to be completed to proceed with registration.
