# Athlete Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Evaluation Form is a standardized tool used by coaching staff to assess an athlete's performance and development potential. It is designed to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the athlete's technical skills, physical attributes, mental toughness, teamwork, coachability, work ethic, leadership qualities, and overall assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, you will need to provide information for each of the listed fields. Please make sure to answer all required questions and select the relevant options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, text, **required**): Enter the full name of the athlete being evaluated.
* **Athlete ID Number** (`athlete_id`, text, **optional**): Enter the athlete's student or team identification number.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of this evaluation.
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, **required**): Enter your name as the evaluator.
* **Evaluator Role** (`evaluator_role`, select_one, **required**): Select your role with the team: Head Coach, Assistant Coach, Position Coach, Athletic Director, Scout, or Other.
* **Sport** (`sport`, select_one, **required**): Select the sport being evaluated: Football, Basketball, Soccer, Track and Field, Swimming, Volleyball, Baseball, Softball, Tennis, or Other.
* **Primary Position** (`position`, text, **required**): Enter the athlete's primary position.
* **Type of Evaluation** (`evaluation_type`, select_one, **required**): Select the type of evaluation: Pre-Season, Mid-Season, Post-Season, Tryout Evaluation, Recruitment Evaluation, or Periodic Check-in.
* **Technical Skill Rating** (`skill_rating`, select_one, **required**): Rate the athlete's technical skills: Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, or Needs Improvement.
* **Physical Attributes Rating** (`physical_rating`, select_one, **required**): Rate the athlete's physical attributes: Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, or Needs Improvement.
* **Mental Toughness Rating** (`mental_rating`, select_one, **required**): Rate the athlete's mental toughness: Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, or Needs Improvement.
* **Teamwork Rating** (`teamwork_rating`, select_one, **required**): Rate the athlete's ability to work with the team: Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, or Needs Improvement.
* **Coachability Rating** (`coachability_rating`, select_one, **required**): Rate the athlete's responsiveness to coaching: Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, or Needs Improvement.
* **Work Ethic Rating** (`work_ethic_rating`, select_one, **required**): Rate the athlete's dedication and effort: Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, or Needs Improvement.
* **Leadership Qualities** (`leadership_qualities`, select_one, **required**): Rate the athlete's leadership qualities: Strong Leader, Emerging Leader, Follows Well, or Needs Development.
* **Key Strengths** (`strengths`, text, **required**): List the athlete's top strengths.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`weaknesses`, text, **required**): List areas the athlete needs to develop.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, **required**): Suggest next steps for the athlete's development.
* **Overall Assessment** (`overall_assessment`, text, **required**): Provide a summary evaluation of the athlete.
* **Overall Recommendation** (`recommendation_level`, select_one, **required**): Rate your overall recommendation for the athlete: Highly Recommend, Recommend, Recommend with Reservations, or Do Not Recommend.
* **Evaluator Signature** (`evaluator_signature`, text, **required**): Type your full name as the signature.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Provide thoughtful and specific feedback for each field.
* Use the evaluation form as a tool to help the athlete grow and develop.
* If unsure about any field, please feel free to consult with the team or ask for clarification.
