# Athlete Bio Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from athletes for the publication of their bio and photo. It collects information about the athlete's name, sport, team level, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the athlete's full name as it should appear.
2. Enter the athlete's ID number if applicable.
3. Enter the athlete's date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
4. Select the athlete's primary sport.
5. Select the athlete's team level.
6. Enter the athlete's primary position or event.
7. Select the current academic year.
8. Enter the athlete's hometown.
9. Enter the name of the high school attended.
10. List major achievements, records, or awards.
11. List academic achievements such as GPA or honors.
12. List extracurricular activities.
13. Enter the athlete's future goals.
14. Select whether to consent to photo publication.
15. Select approved publication channels for the bio.
16. Select the consent duration.
17. If the athlete is under 18, enter the parent or guardian's name.
18. If the athlete is under 18, sign with the parent or guardian's signature.
19. Sign with the athlete's signature.
20. Enter the date this form is signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Athlete Full Name**: Enter the athlete's full name as it should appear.
- **Athlete ID Number**: Enter the athlete's ID number if applicable.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter the athlete's date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
- **Sport**: Select the athlete's primary sport.
- **Team Level**: Select the athlete's team level.
- **Primary Position**: Enter the athlete's primary position or event.
- **Year in School**: Select the current academic year.
- **Hometown**: Enter the athlete's hometown.
- **High School Attended**: Enter the name of the high school attended.
- **Career Highlights**: List major achievements, records, or awards.
- **Academic Achievements**: List academic achievements such as GPA or honors.
- **Extracurricular Activities**: List extracurricular activities.
- **Future Goals**: Enter the athlete's future goals.
- **Photo Publication Consent**: Select whether to consent to photo publication.
- **Approved Publication Channels**: Select approved publication channels for the bio.
- **Consent Duration**: Select the consent duration.
- **Parent or Guardian Name**: If the athlete is under 18, enter the parent or guardian's name.
- **Parent or Guardian Signature**: If the athlete is under 18, sign with the parent or guardian's signature.
- **Athlete Signature**: Sign with the athlete's signature.
- **Date of Consent**: Enter the date this form is signed.
