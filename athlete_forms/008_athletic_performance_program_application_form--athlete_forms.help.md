# Athletic Performance Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants to the Athletic Performance Program. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to get the most out of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each section carefully and answer all the required fields.
- Make sure to provide as much detail as possible for each question.
- If you have any doubts or questions, contact us for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (`2`, text, required):* Enter your name as it appears on your government ID or passport.
* **Email Address (`3`, email, required):* Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number (`4`, text, required):* Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth (`5`, date, required):* Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Age (`6`, number, not required):* Enter your current age.
* **Primary Sport (`8`, text, required):* Select your main sport or discipline.
* **Current Team/Organization (`9`, text, required):* Enter the name of your current team or organization.
* **Years of Competition Experience (`10`, number, required):* Enter the number of years you've been competing in your sport.
* **Current Skill Level (`11`, select_one, required):* Assess your current performance level:
	+ Beginner: Just starting out.
	+ Intermediate: Some experience.
	+ Advanced: Solid experience.
	+ Elite: High-level experience.
	+ Professional: Professional athlete.
* **Primary Performance Goals (`14`, text, required):* Describe what you aim to achieve in the program.
* **Specific Areas for Improvement (`15`, select_multiple, required):* Select the areas you'd like to improve:
	+ Speed.
	+ Strength.
	+ Endurance.
	+ Technique.
	+ Mental Game.
	+ Injury Prevention.
	+ Other (please specify).
* **Available Time for Training (`16`, select_one, required):* Select the number of hours you can commit to training per week:
	+ 5-10 hours.
	+ 10-15 hours.
	+ 15-20 hours.
	+ 20+ hours.
* **Available Training Days (`17`, select_multiple, required):* Select the days you can train:
	+ Monday.
	+ Tuesday.
	+ Wednesday.
	+ Thursday.
	+ Friday.
	+ Saturday.
	+ Sunday.
* **Preferred Training Time (`18`, select_one, required):* Select your preferred training time:
	+ Early Morning.
	+ Mid-Morning.
	+ Afternoon.
	+ Evening.
	+ Flexible.
* **Currently Work with a Coach? (`19`, select_one, required):* Are you currently under coaching supervision?
	+ Yes.
	+ No.
* **Current Coach Name (`20`, text, not required):* If yes, enter the name of your coach.
* **Medical Conditions (`22`, text, not required):* List any relevant health conditions.
* **Past Injuries (`23`, text, not required):* Describe any previous injuries.
* **Current Fitness Level (`24`, select_one, required):* Assess your current overall fitness level:
	+ Poor.
	+ Below Average.
	+ Average.
	+ Above Average.
	+ Excellent.
* **Motivation Statement (`25`, text, required):* Share your motivation and goals for joining the program.
