# Tennis Player Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Tennis Player Evaluation Form is designed to assess the performance of a tennis player. This form is used to evaluate the player's overall skills, manners, and behavior during the game.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting the evaluation, review the form carefully to ensure you understand the criteria. The form is divided into two sections:

1.  Numerical evaluations of the player's performance (pages 1-9).
2.  Subjective feedback and comments (pages 10-15).

Complete each page thoroughly, taking note of the following:

*   For numerical evaluations (pages 1-9), rate the player's performance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   For subjective feedback and comments (pages 10-15), provide as much detail as possible to give a comprehensive assessment of the player's skills and behavior.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Overall Performance (required)

*   **Overall Performance** (`overall_performance`, `number`, required): Evaluate the player's overall performance during the match. Consider factors such as consistency, accuracy, and strategy. Rate on a scale of 1-5.

### Page 2: Game Manners (required)

*   **Game Manners** (`game_manners`, `number`, required): Assess the player's behavior and sportsmanship during the game. Consider factors such as respect for opponents, fair play, and respect for the umpire. Rate on a scale of 1-5.

### Page 3: Serve and Volley (required)

*   **Serve and Volley** (`serve_and_volley`, `number`, required): Evaluate the player's serve and volley skills. Consider factors such as power, accuracy, and consistency. Rate on a scale of 1-5.

### Page 4: Net Game (required)

*   **Net Game** (`net_game`, `number`, required): Assess the player's net play skills. Consider factors such as aggression, positioning, and shot placement. Rate on a scale of 1-5.

### Page 5: Forehand (required)

*   **Forehand** (`forehand`, `number`, required): Evaluate the player's forehand skills. Consider factors such as power, consistency, and accuracy. Rate on a scale of 1-5.

### Page 6: Backhand (required)

*   **Backhand** (`backhand`, `number`, required): Assess the player's backhand skills. Consider factors such as power, consistency, and accuracy. Rate on a scale of 1-5.

### Page 7: Volleys (required)

*   **Volleys** (`volleys`, `number`, required): Evaluate the player's volley skills. Consider factors such as power, consistency, and accuracy. Rate on a scale of 1-5.

### Page 8: Drop Shots (required)

*   **Drop Shots** (`drop_shots`, `number`, required): Assess the player's drop shot skills. Consider factors such as placement, timing, and consistency. Rate on a scale of 1-5.

### Page 9: Smash (required)

*   **Smash** (`smash`, `number`, required): Evaluate the player's smash skills. Consider factors such as power, control, and consistency. Rate on a scale of 1-5.

### Page 10: Volleys Returned (required)

*   **Volleys Returned** (`volleys_returned`, `number`, required): Assess the player's ability to return volleys. Consider factors such as footwork, positioning, and reaction time. Rate on a scale of 1-5.

### Page 11: Overall Score (required)

*   **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, `number`, required): Evaluate the player's overall performance. Consider factors such as total points won, opponent's performance, and game strategy. Rate on a scale of 1-5.

### Page 12: Player Manner (required)

*   **Player Manner** (`player_manner`, `select_multiple`, required): Evaluate the player's behavior and sportsmanship during the game. Select one or more of the following:
    *   Good: The player demonstrated good sportsmanship and behavior during the game.
    *   Fair: The player demonstrated fair sportsmanship and behavior during the game, but with some minor issues.
    *   Poor: The player demonstrated poor sportsmanship and behavior during the game.

### Page 13: Player Manner Hints (optional)

*   **Player Manner Hints** (`player_manner_hints`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions for improving the player's behavior and sportsmanship.

### Page 14: Player Manner Comments (optional)

*   **Player Manner Comments** (`player_manner_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback for the player's behavior and sportsmanship.

### Page 15: Additional Comments (optional)

*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback for the player's performance.
