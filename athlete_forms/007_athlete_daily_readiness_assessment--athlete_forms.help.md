# Athlete Daily Readiness Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Athlete Daily Readiness Assessment is a tool used to evaluate an athlete's readiness to train on a given day. This assessment helps athletes rate their overall readiness and identify areas where they may need to modify their training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the athlete's full name in the "Athlete Full Name" field.
2. Enter the athlete's ID number in the "Athlete ID Number" field, if applicable.
3. Select the current date and time of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" and "Assessment Time" fields.
4. Choose the primary sport or activity the athlete is currently participating in from the "Sport" dropdown menu.
5. Rate the athlete's sleep quality, muscle soreness, energy level, motivation level, and stress level using the corresponding dropdown menus.
6. Select any areas of pain or discomfort from the "Areas of Pain or Discomfort" dropdown menu.
7. Choose the athlete's hydration status and nutrition status using the corresponding dropdown menus.
8. Check if the athlete is experiencing any illness symptoms.
9. Rate the athlete's overall readiness to train using the "Overall Readiness Score" field.
10. Determine if the athlete needs modified training using the "Training Modification Needed" dropdown menu.
11. If necessary, provide any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field.
12. Check if the coach needs to review the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter the athlete's full name in this field.
* **Athlete ID Number** (2, text, optional): Enter the athlete's ID number, if applicable.
* **Assessment Date** (3, date, required): Select the current date of the assessment.
* **Assessment Time** (4, time, required): Select the current time of the assessment.
* **Sport** (5, select_one, required): Choose the primary sport or activity the athlete is currently participating in.
* **Sleep Quality Last Night** (6, select_one, required): Rate your sleep quality as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Hours of Sleep** (7, number, required): Enter the number of hours you slept last night.
* **Muscle Soreness Level** (8, select_one, required): Rate your current muscle soreness as None, Mild, Moderate, Severe, or Very Severe.
* **Energy Level** (9, select_one, required): Rate your energy level as Very High, High, Moderate, Low, or Very Low.
* **Motivation Level** (10, select_one, required): Rate your motivation level as Very Motivated, Motivated, Neutral, Unmotivated, or Very Unmotivated.
* **Stress Level** (11, select_one, required): Rate your stress level as None, Low, Moderate, High, or Very High.
* **Areas of Pain or Discomfort** (12, select_multiple, required): Select any areas of pain or discomfort you are experiencing.
* **Hydration Status** (13, select_one, required): Choose your hydration status as Well Hydrated, Adequately Hydrated, Slightly Dehydrated, Dehydrated, or Very Dehydrated.
* **Nutrition Status** (14, select_one, required): Rate your nutrition status as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Illness Symptoms** (15, select_one, required): Check if you are experiencing any illness symptoms, such as None, Mild Cold Symptoms, Fever, Respiratory Issues, or Gastrointestinal Issues.
* **Overall Readiness Score** (16, number, required): Rate your overall readiness to train using a score from 1 to 10.
* **Training Modification Needed** (17, select_one, required): Determine if you need modified training, as No - Full Training, Yes - Light Intensity, Yes - Reduced Volume, Yes - Skip Certain Exercises, or Yes - Complete Rest.
* **Additional Notes** (18, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or factors affecting your readiness.
* **Coach Review Required** (19, select_one, required): Check if this assessment requires coach review.
