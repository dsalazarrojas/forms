# Baseball League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from players for a baseball league. It is essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure proper team management and player safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the personal information section, which includes:
	* First name: Enter your full first name.
	* Last name: Enter your full last name.
	* Date of birth: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	* Email address: Enter your email address.
	* Phone number: Enter your phone number.
2. If you are under 18, provide your parent or guardian's name and phone number.
3. In the contact information section:
	* Emergency contact name: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
	* Emergency contact phone: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
4. The baseball experience section is where you'll choose your:
	* Age division or experience level: Select the level that best fits your age and experience.
	* Primary position: Choose the primary position you play.
	* Secondary position (optional): Choose a secondary position you play.
	* Years of baseball experience: Enter the number of years you have played baseball.
5. In the team preference and availability section:
	* Preferred team (if applicable): Enter the team you prefer.
	* Availability: Select whether you are available for all league games and practices.
6. Finally, in the health and safety section:
	* Any medical conditions: If you have any medical conditions, enter them here.
	* Sports injury insurance: Select whether you have sports injury insurance.
	* Waiver agreement: Agree to the league waiver and terms of play.
	* Confirmation: Confirm that all information is accurate.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you are under 18, ensure your parent or guardian completes their part of the form.
* Review the waiver agreement carefully before agreeing to it.
* If you have any medical conditions, be sure to disclose them to ensure proper safety measures can be taken.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
