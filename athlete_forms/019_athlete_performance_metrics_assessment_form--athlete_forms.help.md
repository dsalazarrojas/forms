# Athlete Performance Metrics Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Performance Metrics Assessment Form is a tool for coaches, trainers, and athletic directors to collect detailed information about an athlete's performance, including physical attributes and sports-specific metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the athlete's full name, identification number, and sport.
2. Enter the assessment date.
3. Select the primary sport from the options.
4. Enter the athlete's height, weight, and body fat percentage (if measured).
5. Fill in the sports-specific metrics for the selected sport.
6. Enter the training recommendations and assessor's name, signature, and any other required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name (1)**: Enter the athlete's full name.
* **Athlete ID Number (2)**: Enter the athlete's identification number.
* **Assessment Date (3)**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Sport (4)**: Select the primary sport from the options.
* **Height (6)**: Enter the athlete's height in feet and inches or cm.
* **Weight (7)**: Enter the athlete's weight in pounds or kg.
* **Body Fat Percentage (8)**: If measured, enter the athlete's body fat percentage.
* **40-Yard Dash Time (9)**: Enter the athlete's 40-yard dash time in seconds.
* **Vertical Jump (10)**: Enter the athlete's vertical jump height in inches.
* **Broad Jump (11)**: Enter the athlete's broad jump distance in inches or cm.
* **Shuttle Run Time (12)**: Enter the athlete's shuttle run time in seconds.
* **Bench Press Max (13)**: Enter the athlete's bench press max weight in pounds or kg.
* **Squat Max (14)**: Enter the athlete's squat max weight in pounds or kg.
* **Deadlift Max (15)**: Enter the athlete's deadlift max weight in pounds or kg.
* **VO2 Max (16)**: If measured, enter the athlete's VO2 max value.
* **Resting Heart Rate (17)**: Enter the athlete's resting heart rate in beats per minute.
* **Sport-Specific Metric 1 (18)**: Enter the name of the sport-specific metric for the athlete.
* **Sport-Specific Value 1 (19)**: Enter the value of the sport-specific metric for the athlete.
* **Sport-Specific Metric 2 (20)**: Enter the name of the second sport-specific metric for the athlete.
* **Sport-Specific Value 2 (21)**: Enter the value of the second sport-specific metric for the athlete.
* **Previous Assessment Date (22)**: If applicable, enter the date of the previous assessment.
* **Improvements Noted (23)**: List any areas of improvement for the athlete.
* **Areas of Decline (24)**: List any metrics that declined for the athlete.
* **Training Recommendations (25)**: Enter suggested focus areas for the athlete's training.
* **Assessor Name (26)**: Enter the name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessor Signature (27)**: Type the full name as a signature.
