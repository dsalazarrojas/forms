# League Player Profile Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect player information, emergency contacts, and consent for participation and data sharing for athletes in the league.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the player information section with the required details.
2. List emergency contacts with their relationship and contact information.
3. Check the consent boxes to indicate whether you consent to participate, share your data, and allow emergency contacts to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false):
  This section collects basic information about the player. Please fill in their name and any other details that you feel are relevant.

* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, `text`, required: false):
  List all emergency contacts with their relationship (e.g., mother, father, guardian, coach) and contact information (name, phone number, email, address).

* **Participation Consent** (`participation_consent`, `text`, required: false):
  Check the box to indicate whether you consent to participate in the league activities.

* **Player Profile** (`player_info`, `select_one`, required: false):
  Select the category that best describes the player's status (Player, Athlete, or Participant).

* **Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false):
  Fill in the name of the emergency contact person.

* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.

* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false):
  Enter the email of the emergency contact person.

* **Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  Select the relationship of the emergency contact person (Mother, Father, Guardian, Coach, or Other).

* **Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter the address of the emergency contact person.

* **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`, `text`, required: false):
  Fill in the name of the emergency contact person.

* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, `email`, required: false):
  Enter the email of the emergency contact person.

* **Emergency Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  Select the relationship of the emergency contact person (Mother, Father, Guardian, Coach, or Other).

* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter the address of the emergency contact person.

* **Next Of Kin Name** (`emergency_next_of_kin_name`, `text`, required: false):
  Fill in the name of the next of kin contact person.

* **Emergency Next Of Kin Phone** (`emergency_next_of_kin_phone`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter the phone number of the next of kin contact person.

* **Emergency Next Of Kin Email** (`emergency_next_of_kin_email`, `email`, required: false):
  Enter the email of the next of kin contact person.

* **Emergency Next Of Kin Address** (`emergency_next_of_kin_address`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter the address of the next of kin contact person.

* **Emergency Next Of Kin Relationship** (`emergency_next_of_kin_relationship`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  Select the relationship of the next of kin contact person (Mother, Father, Guardian, Coach, or Other).

* **Consent to Participate** (`consent_to_participate`, `select_one`, required: false):
  Check the box to indicate whether you consent to participate in the league activities.

* **Consent to Share** (`consent_to_share`, `select_one`, required: false):
  Check the box to indicate whether you consent to share your data with the league.

* **Emergency Contact Consent** (`emergency_contact_consent`, `select_one`, required: false):
  Check the box to indicate whether you consent to be contacted by emergency contacts.

* **Athlete Info 2** (`athlete_info_2`, `text`, required: false):
  This field is for any additional athlete information that you would like to provide.

* **Player Name** (`player_name`, `text`, required: false):
  Fill in your name.

* **Date of Birth** (`player_dob`, `date`, required: false):
  Enter your date of birth.
