# Player Medical Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Medical Report Form is used to report and document the medical history, condition, test results, and contact information of athletes. This form helps in gathering essential medical information to ensure athlete safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your player's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or previous injuries.
2. Provide details of any medical tests or results, such as X-rays, MRI, or test results.
3. Report any surgeries or operations that the player has undergone.
4. Enter contact information for the player or emergency contact.
5. If the player has an emergency contact, choose the option that best describes their relationship to the athlete (Primary Care Physician or Other).
6. If the emergency contact is not a primary care physician, provide additional information about the contact in the 'Emergency Contact Other' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Medical Report Form (Player Medical Report Form, 1)**: This field is the title of the form that summarizes its purpose.
* **Medical History (2)**: Describe any previous medical conditions or injuries that the player may have, including dates, symptoms, and treatments received.
* **Medical Condition (3)**: Specify any current medical conditions that the player may have.
* **Medical Test Result (4)**: Report any recent medical test results, such as X-rays, MRIs, or blood work.
* **Injury (5)**: Document any current or past injuries that the player has experienced.
* **Surgery (6)**: List any surgeries or operations that the player has undergone.
* **Contact Information (7)**: Enter the player's contact information.
* **Emergency Contact (8)**: Choose how the player would like to be contacted in case of an emergency. Options are Primary Care Physician or Other.
* **Other (9)**: If the emergency contact is not a primary care physician, enter the relationship and any additional contact information.
