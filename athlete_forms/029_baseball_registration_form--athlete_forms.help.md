# Baseball Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register players for a baseball league, tournament, or program. It collects information about the player's personal details, playing experience, and medical information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the player's first name, last name, and date of birth in the respective text fields.
2. Select the player's gender, division or league, and grade level from the select_one fields.
3. Choose the positions the player has played or wants to play from the select_multiple field.
4. Select the player's bats and throws from the select_one fields.
5. Provide information about any medical conditions or allergies and list any medications the player is taking.
6. Enter the player's school name and grade level (if not already provided).
7. Fill in the emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and relationship.
8. Select the equipment package needed and the type of uniform T-shirt size.
9. Choose whether to consent to photo and video use and accept the liability waiver agreement.
10. Sign the waiver agreement by typing your name, and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player First Name**: Enter the player's first name.
* **Player Last Name**: Enter the player's last name.
* **Player Date of Birth**: Enter the player's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Player Age**: Enter the player's current age.
* **Player Gender**: Select the player's gender.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name**: Enter the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Home Address**: Enter the player's home address.
* **City**: Enter the player's city.
* **State**: Enter the player's state.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the player's ZIP code.
* **Division or League**: Select the division or league the player is registering for.
* **School Name**: Enter the player's current school name (if not already provided).
* **Grade Level**: Select the player's current grade level.
* **Years of Baseball Experience**: Select the number of years the player has played baseball.
* **Positions Played**: Select all the positions the player has played or wants to play.
* **Bats**: Select the player's batting hand.
* **Throws**: Select the player's throwing hand.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: If the player has any medical conditions or allergies, list them here.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications the player is taking.
* **Medical Insurance Provider**: Enter the player's medical insurance provider.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Explain the relationship between the player and the emergency contact.
* **Team or Coach Request**: If there's anything specific the team or coach should know about, write it here.
* **Uniform T-Shirt Size**: Select the player's T-shirt size.
* **Equipment Package Needed**: Select the equipment package required.
* **Photo and Video Consent**: Choose whether to consent to photo and video use for team materials.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement**: Agree or disagree to the liability waiver agreement.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Sign the waiver agreement by typing your full name.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date signed.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian (already filled out above).
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian (already filled out above).
