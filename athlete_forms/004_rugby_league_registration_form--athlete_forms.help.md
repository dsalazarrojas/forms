# rugby_league_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rugby League Registration Form is used to collect information from athletes registering for a rugby league. This form is designed to gather essential details about each athlete, including contact information, medical history, rugby experience, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the User Details section.
2. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, in the Contact Info section.
3. Share your medical information in the Medical Info section.
4. Enter information about your emergency contact in the Emergency Contact section.
5. Provide your medical history in the Medical History section.
6. Share your rugby experience in the Rugby Experience section.
7. Record your registration details, including the date and time of registration, in the Registration Date and Registration Time sections.
8. Upload any relevant medical certificates in the Medical Certificate section.
9. Provide any additional referee report in the Referee Report section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details**: Enter your personal details, including your name and other relevant information.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Info**: 
* **Medical Info**: Share any relevant medical information that is not included in your medical history.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the contact information of a person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history that may affect your participation in the rugby league.
* **Rugby Experience**: Share your experience in playing rugby, including any relevant experience or certifications.
* **Registration Date**: Record the date you are registering for the rugby league.
* **Registration Time**: Record the time of your registration.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Medical Certificate**: If applicable, upload any medical certificate that may be required.
* **Referee Report**: Provide any additional report from a referee that may be relevant.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities or organizers for guidance.
* The registration form is designed to be completed by the athlete themselves, so ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
