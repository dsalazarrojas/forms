# Hockey Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your hockey training habits and preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the options that best fit your situation.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Feedback" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hockey Survey Form**: A text field where you can provide any additional information or comments.
* **Experience**: Select your current experience level in hockey. This will help us understand your current skill level.
	+ Beginner: You are new to hockey or have limited experience.
	+ Intermediate: You have some experience in hockey, but are not yet advanced.
	+ Advanced: You have a high level of experience in hockey.
* **Training Frequency**: Select how often you train in hockey. This will help us understand your training schedule.
	+ Daily: You train every day.
	+ Weekly: You train most days of the week.
	+ Monthly: You train less frequently.
* **Training Frequency**: Select the time of day when you train.
	+ Morning: You train in the morning hours.
	+ Afternoon: You train in the afternoon hours.
	+ Evening: You train in the evening hours.
* **Equipment**: Select any equipment you use for training. This will help us understand your training setup.
	+ Stick: You use a hockey stick.
	+ Puck: You use a puck or puck bag.
	+ Skates: You use hockey skates.
* **Training Duration**: Select the duration of your training sessions.
	+ Less than 1 hour: Your training sessions are less than an hour.
	+ 1-2 hours: Your training sessions are between 1-2 hours.
	+ 2-4 hours: Your training sessions are between 2-4 hours.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your training habits or preferences.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your training habits or preferences.
