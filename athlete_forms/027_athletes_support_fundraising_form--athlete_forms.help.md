# Athletes Support Fundraising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to donate to support athletes and their teams.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information:
   * **Donor Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
   * **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can send you a receipt.
   * **Phone Number**: (Optional) Enter your phone number for contact.
   * **Street Address**: Enter your mailing address.
   * **City**: Enter your city.
   * **State**: Enter your state or province.
   * **ZIP Code**: Enter your postal code.
2. Select the athlete and team you want to support:
   * **Athlete Name to Support**: Select the athlete you want to support.
   * **Athlete Team**: Select the team the athlete is part of.
3. Choose your donation details:
   * **Sport**: Select the sport the athlete plays.
   * **Fundraising Campaign**: Choose which campaign this donation is for.
   * **Donation Amount in USD**: Enter the amount you want to donate.
   * **Donation Frequency**: Choose if this is a one-time or recurring donation.
4. Payment information:
   * **Payment Method**: Choose how you want to pay.
   * **Card Type**: (Optional) If you choose to pay by credit card, select the type of card.
   * **Card Number**: (Optional) Enter your credit card number.
   * **Security Code**: (Optional) Enter the security code on the back of your card.
   * **Cardholder Name**: (Optional) Enter the name on your card.
   * **Billing Address**: (Optional) Enter the address associated with your card.
5. Optional information:
   * **Employer Matching Gift**: If your employer matches donations, choose Yes.
   * **Tribute Donation**: If this is a donation in honor or memory of someone, select In Honor Of or In Memory Of.
   * **Tribute Name**: Enter the name of the person being honored.
   * **Tax Receipt**: Choose if you want a tax-deductible receipt.
   * **Newsletter Signup**: Choose if you want to receive updates.
   * **Additional Comments**: Leave any message for the athlete or team.
   * **Referral Source**: Where did you hear about this fundraiser?
