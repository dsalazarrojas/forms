# Student Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Form is used to collect student information for registration or verification purposes. This form should only be completed by students to provide their personal details, including name, email, phone number, birthday, and address.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all required and optional fields pre-filled if necessary.
2. Complete all fields, as indicated by the "Required" label on each field.
3. Enter your details accurately and consistently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (student_name, text, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student Email** (student_email, email, Required): Enter your official email address associated with your student ID or account.
* **Student Phone** (student_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Birthday** (student_birthday, date, optional): Select your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (student_address, text, optional): Enter your current home address.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and consistently.
* Use the format specified for the "Birthday" field.
* If you have any issues while filling this form, contact your school administrator.
