# College Entrance Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Entrance Exam Form is a tool used by colleges and universities to gather information from students who are applying for entrance exams. This form helps the institution to understand the candidate's qualifications, preferences, and requirements for the exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your Date of Birth.
6. Choose the type of exam you are applying for in the "Entrance Exam Type" field.
7. Select your preferred exam date in the "Preferred Exam Date" field.
8. Specify your current high school or institution in the "Current High School or Institution" field.
9. Enter your expected graduation year in the "Expected Graduation Year" field.
10. (Optional) Enter your cumulative GPA on a 4.0 scale.
11. (Optional) Enter the number of times you have taken the exam previously.
12. (Optional) Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have submitted prior test scores.
13. (Optional) Enter your prior test score.
14. Enter the name of your intended major or program in the "Intended Major or Program" field.
15. Enter the primary college or university you are applying to in the "Primary College or University Choice" field.
16. (Optional) List other colleges or universities you are considering.
17. Select "Not Started", "Self-Studying", "Taking Prep Course", "With Private Tutor", or "Ready to Test" to indicate your test preparation status.
18. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you need testing accommodations.
19. (Optional) Specify any additional accommodations you need.
20. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you need financial aid.
21. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are an English Language Learner.
22. (Optional) If you are an English Language Learner, specify the English proficiency test you have taken (e.g., TOEFL, IELTS, etc.).
23. (Optional) List the institutions to which you would like to send your test scores.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Select your date of birth.
* **Entrance Exam Type** (Select One, Required): Select the type of exam you are applying for (e.g., SAT, ACT, GRE, GMAT, LSAT, etc.).
* **Preferred Exam Date** (Date, Required): Select your preferred exam date.
* **Current High School or Institution** (Text, Required): Specify your current high school or institution.
* **Expected Graduation Year** (Number, Required): Enter your expected graduation year.
* **Cumulative GPA** (Number, Optional): (Optional) Enter your cumulative GPA on a 4.0 scale.
* **Prior Test Attempts** (Number, Optional): (Optional) Enter the number of times you have taken this exam previously.
* **Prior Test Score** (Number, Optional): (Optional) Enter your prior test score.
* **Intended Major or Program** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your intended major or program.
* **Primary College or University Choice** (Text, Required): Enter the primary college or university you are applying to.
* **Secondary College Choices** (Text, Optional): (Optional) List other colleges or universities you are considering.
* **Test Preparation Status** (Select One, Required): Select your test preparation status (e.g., Not Started, Self-Studying, Taking Prep Course, With Private Tutor, Ready to Test).
* **Accommodations Needed** (Select One, Required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you need testing accommodations.
* **Accommodation Details** (Text, Optional): (Optional) Specify any additional accommodations you need.
* **Financial Aid Needed** (Select One, Required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you need financial aid.
* **English Language Learner** (Select One, Required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are an English Language Learner.
* **English Proficiency Test Score** (Text, Optional): (Optional) If you are an English Language Learner, specify the English proficiency test you have taken (e.g., TOEFL, IELTS, etc.).
* **Schools to Send Scores To** (Text, Optional): (Optional) List the institutions to which you would like to send your test scores.
* **I Consent to Exam and Score Reporting** (Select One, Required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you consent to taking the exam and score reporting.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need help with any of the fields, please contact us.
* You can save your progress and return to the form later.
* Please review your form carefully before submission.
