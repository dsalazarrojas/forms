# Second Term Childcare Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Second Term Childcare Program Registration Form is a required document that needs to be completed for the enrollment of a child in the second term of our program. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we have the necessary information to provide the best possible care for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, please ensure that you have all the necessary documents and information ready. This form is split into several sections, each with its own set of questions. Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your abilities. If you are unsure about any question, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Family Information (id: 2)
* **Family Information** (`family_info`, text, required): Please provide your name, address, phone number, and email address of your family members who will be accompanying the child in the program. This information will help us to keep in touch with you and ensure that we can reach you in case of an emergency.

### Address Information (id: 3)
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Please provide the address where the child will be staying during the program. This is crucial for us to send important information and updates to you.

### Emergency Contact Information (id: 4)
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required): Please provide the name, phone number, and relationship of the emergency contact person. This is essential for us to know who to contact in case of an emergency.

### Medical History (id: 5)
* **Medical History** (`medical_info`, text, optional): If the child has any medical conditions or allergies, please provide the necessary information. This will help us to provide the best possible care for the child.

### Payment Terms (id: 6)
