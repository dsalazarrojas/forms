# Generic Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Generic Scholarship Application Form is designed to collect information from students who are applying for a scholarship. The form includes fields that require students to provide their details about themselves, their academic background, financial information, and other relevant data. This information will be reviewed and evaluated as part of the scholarship selection process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) and the optional fields that provide additional context.
2. Ensure that you provide the most up-to-date information and that it is free of errors.
3. Review your information before submitting to avoid mistakes and ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
4. Click the submit button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student Information (Page 1)

* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, required): Enter your full name and any other personal details that you want to include.
* **Submitter** (`student_info`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who is submitting the application on your behalf.

### Academic Programs (Page 2)

* **Academic Programs** (`academic_programs`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have completed or plan to pursue any academic programs (e.g., courses, certifications, or degrees).
* **Programs Completed** (`academic_programs`, select_multiple, optional): List any academic programs you have completed or are currently pursuing.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (`academic_programs`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, provide your expected graduation date.

### School Information (Page 3)

* **School Information** (`school_info`, text, optional): Mention your current or previous institutions you've attended.
* **School Name** (`school_info`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current or previous school.
* **Year** (`school_info`, text, optional): Enter the year you graduated or attended the school.

### Contact Information (Page 4)

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your current contact information (e.g., email address, phone number).
* **Email** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Academic Background (Page 5)

* **Academic Background** (`academic_background`, text, optional): Include any relevant academic background information that you think is relevant to your application.
* **GPA** (`academic_background`, text, optional): Enter your current GPA or highest GPA achieved.

### Scholarship Information (Page 6)

* **Scholarship Information** (`scholarship_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant details about the scholarship you are applying for.
* **Scholarship Amount** (`scholarship_info`, text, optional): Enter the amount you are applying for.

### Submission Information (Page 7)

* **Submission Information** (`submission_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that you believe will support your application.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_info`, text, optional): Enter the date you submitted your application.

### Financial Information (Page 8)

* **Financial Information** (`financial_info`, text, optional): Provide any financial information that is relevant to your application (e.g., financial aid, scholarships received).
* **Financial Assistance** (`financial_info`, text, optional): Describe any financial assistance you receive (e.g., scholarships, grants, loans).

### Additional Information (Page 9)

* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or context that you feel is important to your application.
* **References** (`additional_info`, text, optional): List any relevant references or recommendations.

### Submitted By (Page 10)

* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): The name of the person submitting the application on your behalf.
* **Submission Date** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): The date the application was submitted.

### Submitted On (Page 11)

* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, text, optional): Date and time of submission.
