# Language School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language School Application Form is designed to collect information from users who want to learn a new language at our school. The form is used to gather details about the user's language preferences and study schedule to facilitate the process of signing up for language lessons.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill in all the fields with your information.
2.  If you're unsure about a field, you can ask for help or guidance.
3.  Ensure that you provide a valid email address and phone number as required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`language_school_form_first_name`, text, required/optional): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`language_school_form_last_name`, text, required/optional): Your last name.
* **Email** (`language_school_form_email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This is how we will contact you to schedule lessons.
* **Language** (`language_school_form_language`, select_one, required/optional): Select the language you want to learn. Options include English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Italian, and Portuguese. You can also select "Other" and provide the language in the notes field.
* **English Level** (`language_school_form_english_level`, select_one, required/optional): If you're applying to learn a language other than English, please select your level of English proficiency. Options include Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced.
* **Start Date** (`language_school_form_start_date`, date, required/optional): The start date for your lessons. Please select a date in the future that works for you.
* **Duration** (`language_school_form_duration`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the duration for which you want to study the language. Options include 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, and 2 years. You can also select "Other" and provide the duration in the notes field.
* **Schedule** (`language_school_form_schedule`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select the time of day that is best for you to study. Options include Morning, Afternoon, and Evening.
* **Notes** (`language_school_form_notes`, note, required/optional): Any additional information or notes you'd like to provide about your application.
* **Phone** (`language_school_form_phone`, text, required): This is your phone number. Please enter a valid number that we can use to contact you.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate smooth communication and scheduling.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will contact you shortly after receiving your application to schedule your lessons.
