# Senior High School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who are applying to senior high school. It is used by school administrators to gather essential details about the student's background, educational history, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked with an asterisk *) as they are essential for the school's processing.
3. If you are unsure about any information, contact the school for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Name (*)
**Student Name** (`name`, `text`, required/required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email (*)
**Email** (`email`, `email`, required/required): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you with important updates and information about the school.

### Phone (*)
**Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.

### School Name (*)
**School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required/required): Enter the name of the school you are currently or were enrolled in previously.

### School Start Date (*)
**School Start Date** (`school_start_date`, `date`, required/required): Enter the date you started at your current or previous school. If you are currently enrolled, enter the current date.

### School Address (*)
**School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, required/required): Enter the address of your current or previous school.

### Parent Name (*)
**Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required/required): Enter your parent's or guardian's name.

### Parent Email
**Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your parent's or guardian's email address. This is optional but recommended for communication purposes.

### Parent Phone (*)
**Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required/required): Enter your parent's or guardian's phone number.

### Student Statement
**Student Statement** (`student_statement`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief statement about yourself, your interests, or your goals.

### Student References
**Student References** (`student_references`, `select_multiple`, required/required): Select the relevant option(s) that best describe you, such as "Athletic Achievements" or "Academic Performance".

### Submit
**Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required/required): This is the final step to complete the form. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.
- Make sure to proofread your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions, don't hesitate to contact the school for assistance.
