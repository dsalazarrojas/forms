# Undergraduate Application CV Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Undergraduate Application CV Submission" form is designed to collect information about your personal details, academic background, and other relevant information for the undergraduate application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have all the necessary information required for the undergraduate application process.
3. Please check the "submit_form_4" field to confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name**: This field is for your first name. Please enter your name as it is written on your identification documents.
  - **date_of_birth**: This field is for your date of birth. Please enter your birthdate in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
  - **address**: This field is for your current address. Please enter the address where you currently reside.
  - **university_name**: This field is for your current or intended university name. Please enter the name of the university you are applying to or currently attend.
  - **academic_degree**: This field is to confirm whether you have completed a higher degree (e.g., high school diploma). Please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
  - **academic_level**: This field is to confirm your academic level (e.g., High, Low). Please select "High" or "Low" accordingly.
  - **email**: This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
  - **phone**: This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number with the country code.
  - **cv_attachment**: This field is for uploading your CV. Please upload your CV in a supported file format.
  - **additional_comments**: This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
  - **undergraduate_program**: This field is to confirm whether you are applying to an undergraduate program. Please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
  - **academic_field_of_interest**: This field is to confirm your academic field of interest. Please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
  - **upload_cv**: This field is for uploading your CV. Please upload your CV in a supported file format.
  - **academic_references**: This field is for any academic references you would like to provide.
  - **submit_form_2**: This field is to select the submission time. Please select the time when you would like to submit your application.
  - **submit_form_3**: This field is to select the submission time. Please select the time when you would like to submit your application.
  - **submit_form_4**: This field is to confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the submission. Please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
  - **submit_form_5**: This field is to select the submission options. Please select the options that apply to your submission.
  - **submit_form_6**: This field is to select the submission options. Please select the options that apply to your submission.
  - **submit_form_7**: This field is to confirm whether you are ready to submit your application. Please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
  - **submit_form_8**: This field is to confirm whether you have submitted your application. Please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
  - **submit_form_9**: This field is to select the submission options. Please select the options that apply to your submission.
  - **submit_form_10**: This field is to confirm the submission of your application. Please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
  - **submit_form_11**: This field is to confirm whether you have submitted your application. Please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the relevant authority for clarification.
