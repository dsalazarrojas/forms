# Principal Resume Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Principal Resume Submission Form is a document that allows school principals to submit their personal and professional information to support their application process. This form is designed to gather relevant details about the principal's background, experience, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your personal application information in the **Principal Application Information** field (Field 1).
2.  Provide your contact information in the **Contact Information** field (Field 2).
3.  Describe your work experience in the **Work Experience** field (Field 3).
4.  List your education background in the **Education Background** field (Field 4).
5.  List your references in the **References** field (Field 5).
6.  If applicable, upload your principal resume in the **Upload Principal Resume** field (Field 6).
7.  If applicable, upload supporting documents in the **Upload Supporting Documents** field (Field 7).
8.  Finally, add your signature in the **Principal Signature** field (Field 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Principal Application Information (Field 1)**: Enter your personal details such as your name, title, and school affiliation. This will help the reviewers understand who you are and what role you hold.
*   **Contact Information (Field 2)**: Provide your contact information including your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details. This will enable us to get in touch with you for further information.
*   **Work Experience (Field 3)**: Describe your relevant work experience, including your role, duration of employment, and achievements. This will help reviewers assess your expertise and qualifications.
*   **Education Background (Field 4)**: List your academic qualifications, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and any relevant courses taken. This will demonstrate your academic standing and prepare you for further education or career growth.
*   **References (Field 5)**: Provide the names and contact information of individuals who can vouch for your skills and character. This will help reviewers gauge your interpersonal skills and professional reputation.
*   **Upload Principal Resume (Field 6)**: Upload a copy of your resume, which should reflect your education, work experience, and achievements. This will give the reviewers a comprehensive overview of your qualifications.
*   **Upload Supporting Documents (Field 7)**: Upload any additional documents that are relevant to your application, such as certificates, awards, or publications. This will provide further proof of your credentials.
*   **Principal Signature (Field 8)**: Sign the form with your name, date, and any other identifying information. This is a formal declaration that the information provided is accurate and truthful.
