# Leave Application Form For School - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leave Application Form For School is used by students to request leave from school. It is a simple, multi-page form that collects necessary details about the student's leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the School Leave Details page (page 1).
	* Enter your leave details in the text field.
2. Fill out the Student Details page (page 2).
	* Enter your student details in the text field.
3. Select the Leave Type (page 3).
	* Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Leave, Absence, Vacation, or Holiday).
4. Enter the Start Date and End Date of your leave (page 4).
	* Enter the start and end dates of your leave in the date fields.
5. Enter the Reason for your leave (page 5).
	* Enter a brief description of why you are requesting leave.
6. Enter the number of Days Off (page 6).
	* Enter the number of days you are requesting leave.
7. Enter the Contact Person (page 7).
	* Enter the name and contact information of the person you wish to be contacted in case of an emergency.
8. Enter the Phone and Email of the Contact Person (page 8).
	* Enter the phone number and email of the contact person.
9. Select the Leave Status (page 9).
	* Choose the current status of your leave request (Applied, Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
10. Enter any Comments (page 10).
	* Enter any additional comments about your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Leave Details** (`school_leave_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of your leave details.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of your student details.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Leave, Absence, Vacation, or Holiday).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required):
	+ Enter a brief description of why you are requesting leave.
* **Days Off** (`days_off`, number, required):
	+ Enter the number of days you are requesting leave.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the person you wish to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Leave Status** (`leave_status`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the current status of your leave request (Applied, Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments about your leave request.

Note: Fields marked as required must be filled out. Optional fields are not mandatory but are recommended if applicable.
