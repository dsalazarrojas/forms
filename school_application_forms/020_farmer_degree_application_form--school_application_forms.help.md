# Farmer Degree Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farmer Degree Application Form is a document intended for farmers or individuals interested in pursuing a degree in agriculture or farming-related fields. This form collects basic contact information and specifics about the applicant's farm and background. Completers of this form will be evaluated for eligibility for our degree programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name, email, farm name, farm location, and farm size.
2. Select the type(s) of farm you are associated with (if applicable).
3. Input your date of birth (if you are a student under 25, this field is required).
4. Write a brief description of your farming experience.
5. Review and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your farm or farm-related business.
* **Farm Location** (`farm_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your farm or farm-related business.
* **Farm Size** (`farm_size`, number, required/optional): Select the size of your farm using the following numbers:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
* **Farm Type** (`farm_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all farm types that apply to you:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Farm Experience** (`farm_experience`, note, required/optional): Write a brief description of your farm experience and what you have accomplished as a farmer. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid delays in processing your application.
* Use a valid email address for contact purposes.
* Select the correct farm size and type to ensure accurate assessment of your application.
* Write a clear and concise description of your farm experience.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
