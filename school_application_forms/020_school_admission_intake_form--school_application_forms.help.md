# School Admission Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Admission Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from prospective students and their families, ensuring a smooth and efficient admission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the **prospective_student** section.
2. Provide accurate contact information in the **contact_person** field.
3. Enter the **parent_details**, which includes the parent's name, contact information, and other relevant details.
4. Select the student's **student_abilities** by choosing from the provided options.
5. Enter the **date_of_birth** and **date_of_completion** of the student.
6. Fill in the **student_address**, which includes the student's address and other relevant details.
7. Provide the **emergency_contact** information, including the name and contact details of the emergency contact person.
8. Enter the **school_details**, which includes the school's name, address, and other relevant information.
9. Provide the **guardian_details**, which includes the guardian's name, contact information, and other relevant details.
10. Enter the **medical_details**, which includes any medical conditions or requirements.
11. Enter the **guardian_permission**, which includes the guardian's permission for emergency contact.
12. Enter the **school_administration** information, which includes the school administration's name, contact information, and other relevant details.
13. Enter the **school_administration_2** information, which includes the school administration's name, contact information, and other relevant details.
14. If applicable, add **additional_comments** in the designated field.
15. Review and complete the **notes** field for any additional information.
16. Enter the **student_achievement**, which includes any relevant academic achievements or records.
