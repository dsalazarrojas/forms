# Online Summer School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect student information and contact details to create an application for the online summer school program. It's intended for school administrators to gather data for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student count.
2. Fill in the school name.
3. Select the grade level of the student.
4. Choose the start date of the summer class.
5. Enter the student's name.
6. Enter a contact email.
7. Enter a contact phone number.
8. Enter the student's address.
9. Enter the emergency contact's name.
10. Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
11. Enter the emergency contact's email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Count (student_count, number, optional)**: Enter the number of students applying for the online summer school program.
* **School Name (school_name, text, optional)**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Grade Level (grade_level, select_one, optional)**: Select the grade level of the student (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
* **Summer Class Start (summer_class_start, date, optional)**: Enter the start date of the summer class.
* **Student Name (student_name, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the student.
* **Contact Email (contact_email, email, optional)**: Enter the contact email of the person filling out the form.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone, text, optional)**: Enter the contact phone number of the person filling out the form.
* **Student Address (student_address, text, optional)**: Enter the address of the student.
* **Emergency Contact Name (emergency_contact_name, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (emergency_contact_phone, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email (emergency_contact_email, email, optional)**: Enter the email of the emergency contact.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
- Double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact the school administration.
