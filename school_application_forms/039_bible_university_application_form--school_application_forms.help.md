# Bible University Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bible University Application Form is a comprehensive application form designed for prospective students to apply to the Bible University. It collects essential information about personal details, contact information, educational background, spiritual background, and program selection. Each section is designed to help the admissions team get to know you better and assess your eligibility for the programs offered at the Bible University.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the personal details section, which includes fields for First Name, Last Name, Middle Name, Date of Birth, and Citizenship.
2. In the Contact Information section, provide your Email Address, Phone Number, Mailing Address, City, State or Province, Postal Code, and Country.
3. The Educational Background section includes questions about your high school or secondary school, graduation year, and college experience. If you have attended college, choose "Yes, completed degree" or "Yes, some college" from the options.
4. In the Spiritual Background section, describe your faith journey, current church or faith community affiliation, and brief testimony or conversion story.
5. The Program Selection section lets you choose the program or degree you are interested in, select a field of study, and choose your desired start semester.
6. Finally, write a statement of purpose in the essay section to explain why you want to attend the Bible University and provide additional information if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (ID: 2): Please enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (ID: 3): Please enter your full last name.
* **Middle Name** (ID: 4): This field is optional, but please enter your middle name if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (ID: 5): Enter your date of birth in the format "Month - Day - Year".
* **Citizenship** (ID: 6): Enter your country of citizenship.
* **Email Address** (ID: 8): Please provide your primary email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (ID: 9): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (ID: 10): Enter your full street address for mailing purposes.
* **City** (ID: 11): Enter the city of your mailing address.
* **State or Province** (ID: 12): Enter the state or province of your mailing address.
* **Postal Code** (ID: 13): Enter the postal code of your mailing address.
* **Country** (ID: 14): Enter the country of your mailing address.
* **High School or Secondary School** (ID: 16): Enter the name of the institution where you completed your high school or secondary school education.
* **Graduation Year** (ID: 17): Enter the year you graduated from high school or secondary school.
* **High School GPA** (ID: 18): If applicable, enter your high school grade point average.
* **Prior College or University Experience** (ID: 19): Select one of the options to indicate your college experience.
* **Previous Institution Name** (ID: 20): If applicable, enter the name of the previous institution you attended.
* **Previous GPA** (ID: 21): If applicable, enter your college GPA.
* **Religious or Faith Background** (ID: 23): Describe your faith journey in this field.
* **Church or Faith Community Affiliation** (ID: 24): Enter your current church or faith community affiliation.
* **Brief Testimony or Conversion Story** (ID: 25): Write a brief testimony or conversion story about your faith experience.
* **Interest in Ministry or Religious Vocation** (ID: 26): Select the option that best describes your interest in ministry or religious vocation.
* **Program or Degree Interested In** (ID: 28): Select the program or degree you are interested in.
* **Preferred Field of Study** (ID: 29): Select the field of study you prefer.
* **Desired Start Semester** (ID: 30): Select the desired start semester for your program.
* **Statement of Purpose** (ID: 32): Write a statement explaining why you want to attend the Bible University.
* **Additional Information** (ID: 33): Provide any additional information that you believe is relevant to your application.

By following this guide, you will be able to fill out the form accurately and provide the necessary information to the Bible University admissions team.
