# Cosmetology School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cosmetology School Application Form is a collection of questions designed to gather necessary information from applicants seeking admission to a cosmetology school. The form is divided into sections that cover various aspects of an applicant's background, education, and personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, contact information, and other required fields.
2. Answer the questions about your cosmetology background and education history.
3. Provide details about your high school graduation and any prior cosmetology experience.
4. Enter your payment method and application fee.
5. Confirm your current cosmetology status and provide your state license number and type.
6. Add any other relevant information.
7. Sign the form to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required/false): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/false): Enter your address and phone number.
* **Application Fee** (`application_fee`, number, required): Enter the application fee amount.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay the application fee.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, number, required/false): Enter the payment details (not required if you chose not to pay by credit card).
* **Cosmetology Background** (`cosmetology_background`, select_multiple, required): Answer yes or no if you have prior experience in cosmetology.
* **Academic History** (`academic_history`, select_multiple, required): Answer yes or no if you have completed high school and a GED program.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, select_multiple, required): Answer yes or no if you have completed any prior education related to cosmetology.
* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, required/false): Enter any other personal information you feel is relevant.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/false): Enter your address.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, number, required): Enter your Social Security number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **High School Graduation** (`high_school_graduation`, date, required/false): Enter your high school graduation date.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, required): Enter the year you graduated from high school.
* **High School Information** (`high_school_info`, text, required/false): Enter any information about your high school experience.
* **Cosmetology School Experience** (`cosmetology_school_experience`, select_multiple, required/false): Answer yes or no if you have prior experience in cosmetology school.
* **Current Cosmetology Status** (`current_cosmetology_status`, select_one, required/false): Answer Active or Inactive if you currently have a cosmetology license.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, select_multiple, required): Answer yes or no if you have completed any prior education related to cosmetology.
* **License Info** (`license_info`, text, required/false): Enter your license information.
* **State License Number** (`state_license_number`, number, required): Enter your state license number.
* **State License Type** (`state_license_type`, select_one, required): Answer yes or no if you have a state license.
* **Certification Info** (`certification_info`, text, required/false): Enter your certification information.
* **Other Info** (`other_info`, text, required/false): Enter any other information you feel is relevant.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign the form to confirm your submission.
