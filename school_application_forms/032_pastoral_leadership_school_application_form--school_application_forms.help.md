# Pastoral Leadership School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help applicants for the Pastoral Leadership School provide necessary information for the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as name, email, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Select your application status and choose the relevant attachment(s) if applicable.
3. Provide information about your church and denomination.
4. Add a contact person and their contact information.
5. Provide references for the church elder and pastor, if applicable.
6. Confirm your pastor and church presence.
7. Attach any relevant documents and add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to verify your identity.
* **Application Status**: Choose the current status of your application.
* **Church Name**: Enter the name of your church.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of a person who can be contacted for further information.
* **Reference Person**: Enter the name of a person who can provide a reference for you.
* **Reference Name**: Enter the name of the reference person.
* **Reference Phone**: Enter the phone number of the reference person.
* **Reference Email**: Enter the email address of the reference person.
* **Application Form Attachments**: Attach any relevant documents for your application.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any comments or information not captured in the other fields.
* **School Application Form Notes**: Add any additional comments or information not captured in the other fields.
* **Pastoral Leadership School Reference**: Select the type of reference you're seeking (Pastor's Reference, Church Elder's Reference, or Church Pastor's Reference).
* **Pastor**: Confirm if you are a pastor.
* **Church**: Confirm if your church has the necessary qualifications for the pastoral leadership school.
* **Denomination**: Confirm if your church is part of a denomination.
* **Application Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Application Submission Time**: Enter the time you submitted your application.
* **School Application Form Comments**: Add any additional comments or information not captured in the other fields.
* **School Application Form Attachments**: Attach any additional documents.
* **Application Form Comments**: Add any additional comments or information not captured in the other fields.

Note: This help guide is a general guide. Specific requirements may vary depending on the school's policies.
