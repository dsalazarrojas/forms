# informatics_institute_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants who are interested in applying to the informatics institute. It covers essential details such as contact information, education background, and upload requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by providing your basic contact information, including your name, address, contact number, and email.
2. Enter your application deadline, if applicable.
3. Fill in your date of birth.
4. Describe your education background, highlighting your relevant experience and skills.
5. Share your work experience, including any relevant projects or accomplishments.
6. Indicate whether you need to upload any supporting documents, such as certificates or transcripts.
7. Confirm whether you have uploaded the required documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Information** (`Applicant Information`, text, optional): Please enter your name, as the applicant.
- **Application Deadline** (`Application Deadline`, date, optional): If you have a specific application deadline, please enter it here. If not, you can leave it blank.
- **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): Provide your current address for contact purposes.
- **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for communication.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
- **Education Background** (`Education Background`, text, optional): Describe your education background, including relevant degrees, courses, or training programs.
- **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, text, optional): Share your work experience, including any relevant projects or accomplishments.
- **Upload Documents** (`Upload Documents`, note, optional): This field is for uploading supporting documents. Please follow the instructions on the next page for more information on what to upload and how.
- **Uploaded Documents** (`Uploaded Documents`, select multiple, optional): Indicate whether you have uploaded the required documents. Please choose 'Yes' if you have uploaded the documents and 'No' if you haven't.
- **Upload Certificate** (`Upload Certificate`, select multiple, optional): Indicate whether you need to upload any certificates. Please choose 'Yes' if you have certificates to upload and 'No' if you don't.
- **Upload Transcript** (`Upload Transcript`, select multiple, optional): Indicate whether you need to upload any transcripts. Please choose 'Yes' if you have transcripts to upload and 'No' if you don't.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required information accurately and completely.
- Upload only the required documents to avoid unnecessary clutter.
- Double-check your selections in the uploaded documents fields to ensure accuracy.
