# Referee Course Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The referee course application form is used to collect information from students who are applying to participate in a referee course. This form helps the course organizers to understand the student's preferences and details about the course they are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* Enter your first name in the "Student First Name" field.
	* Enter your last name in the "Student Last Name" field.
2. Provide your email address and phone number:
	* Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Select your school:
	* Choose your school from the drop-down list in the "Student School" field.
4. Choose a referee course:
	* Select one of the available referee courses from the drop-down list in the "Referee Course Name" field.
5. Specify course details:
	* Select the number of sessions you want to take in the "Referee Sessions" field.
	* Choose a time slot for the course in the "Referee Time Slot" field.
	* Enter the duration of the course in the "Referee Course Duration" field.
6. Provide referee course notes (optional):
	* Enter any additional comments or feedback about the referee course in the "Referee Course Notes" field.
7. Confirm your referee course selection:
	* Confirm your referee course choice by re-entering the course name in the "Referee Course 2" field.
8. Additional information (optional):
	* Enter any additional information about the referee course in the "Referee Course 2 2" field.
