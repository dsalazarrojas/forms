# Admission Reason Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the applicant's background, motivations, and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields marked as <Required> in red on the form.
2. Provide accurate and complete answers to each question, using your own words.
3. Double-check your answers for spelling and grammar mistakes before submitting.
4. Make sure to attach supporting documents or materials as required.
5. Submit the completed form by the deadline specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (<Required>): Enter your legal name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Email Address** (<Required>): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (<Required>): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your current residential address.
* **City**: Enter the city of your residence.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your residence.
* **Country**: Enter the country of your residence.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Program Applying For**: Enter the name of the program you are applying for.
* **Degree Level**: Select the level of degree you are pursuing (Certificate, Diploma, Associate, Bachelor's, Master's, Doctorate, or Other).
* **Start Term**: Select the term you wish to start (Fall, Spring, Summer, Winter, or Other).
* **Start Year**: Enter the year you wish to start.
* **Reason for Admission**: Explain why you are applying for this program.
* **Career Goals**: Describe your career goals after completing this program.
* **Academic Interests**: Mention your academic areas of interest.
* **Why This Institution**: Explain why you chose this particular institution.
* **Special Qualities**: Describe any special qualities you bring to the program.
* **Challenges Overcome**: (Optional) Describe any challenges you have overcome.
* **Contribution to Community**: (Optional) Explain how you will contribute to the school community.
* **Education Background**: Describe your educational background.
* **Work Experience**: (Optional) Describe any relevant work experience.
* **Extracurricular Activities**: (Optional) Describe any extracurricular activities you have participated in.
* **References**: (Optional) Provide contact information for references.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date of submitting this application.
* **Applicant Signature**: Sign electronically to confirm your application.

Note that some fields have specific formatting requirements (e.g. Date of Birth, Start Year). Please make sure to follow these requirements when filling out the form. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the admissions office.
