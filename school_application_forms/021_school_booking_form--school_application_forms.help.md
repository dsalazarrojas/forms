# school_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit a booking request to the school. It captures essential details such as the request type, date, time, and facilities required, as well as contact information for the person making the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of booking you would like to request.
2. Enter the date you would like to book.
3. Choose the time you would like to book.
4. Provide the name of the school or institution you are booking for.
5. Select the facilities you require for your booking.
6. Enter the name and phone number of the contact person.
7. Enter the email address of the contact person.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about your booking request (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Booking Request** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your booking request.
- **Date Request** (`2`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to book.
- **Time Request** (`3`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time you would like to book.
- **School Name** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the school or institution you are booking for.
- **Booking Type** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of booking you would like to make.
  - Option 1: Type 1
  - Option 2: Type 2
  - Option 3: Type 3
- **Facilities Requested** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the facilities you require for your booking.
  - Option 1: Option 1
  - Option 2: Option 2
  - Option 3: Option 3
- **Contact Person** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (`9`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Additional Comments** (`10`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your booking request.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- Double-check your contact information to ensure that it is correct.
- Use clear and concise language when filling in the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the school administrators.
