# Out Of District Transfer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Out Of District Transfer Application Form is designed to help students and parents submit an application for out-of-district transfer requests. This form collects relevant information to facilitate the evaluation and processing of transfer requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Student Details** page.
2. Provide **Parent Details**.
3. Enter **School Details**.
4. Specify the **Reason For Transfer** (this field is required).
5. Enter **Transfer School Details** (this field is required).
6. Sign and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Details
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, optional): Please enter the student's name and any other relevant information about them.
### Parent Details
* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, text, optional): Enter the parent's name and contact information.
### School Details
* **School Details** (`school_details`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the student's current school.
### Reason For Transfer
* **Reason For Transfer** (`reason_for_transfer`, text, required): Clearly state the reason why the student wants to be transferred out of the district. This field is mandatory.
### Transfer School Details
* **Transfer School Details** (`transfer_school_details`, text, required): Enter the details about the school the student is requesting to be transferred to.
### Signature
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign the form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
