# University Engineering Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who are applying to the University's Engineering program. The data collected will be used by the admissions committee to evaluate a student's eligibility for admission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide all required information for each field.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your current email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your current phone number where you can be reached.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Major Interest**: Select the Engineering field of study that interests you the most (Engineerings, Computer Science, Architecture).
* **Application Fee**: Enter the application fee (optional). This will be calculated based on your selected major. If not required, leave blank.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you plan to apply or have applied to the program. (Note: this field is not required for initial submission, but it will be used for follow-up purposes.)

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and consistently with your official documents.
* Use correct format and syntax for date fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the admissions committee at [contact email/phone number].
