# evening_school_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for students who want to enroll in the evening school program at [Institution Name]. It collects essential details to assess your eligibility and tailor the program to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the instructions carefully and ensure you haven't submitted this form before.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.
3. Double-check your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`User Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant personal details as per the provided space.
* **Education Level** (`Education Level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your current education level from the provided options (Primary, Secondary, Higher, or Other).
* **Academic Background** (`Academic Background`, `text`, required/optional): Share your academic history, including any notable achievements or credentials that support your application.
