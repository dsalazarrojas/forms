# new_international_student_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new international student application form is designed to gather information from students applying to your school as international students. The form includes fields to input personal details, contact information, and upload relevant files.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Student Name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Enter your Email in the `Email` field.
3. Enter your Contact Number in the `Contact Number` field.
4. Enter your Home Address in the `Home Address` field.
5. Enter your Office Address in the `Office Address` field.
6. Upload any relevant files in the `Upload File 1` to `Upload File 10` fields.
7. Read and agree to the terms of the form in the `Acknowledgement` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as per your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address that you regularly check.
* **Contact Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Home Address** (text, required): Enter your permanent residence address.
* **Office Address** (text, required): Enter your current office or workplace address.
* **Acknowledgement** (note, required): Read and agree to the terms of the form. This includes our data protection and handling policies.
* **Upload File 1** (text, required): Upload any relevant documents such as your passport, transcripts, or certificates.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
