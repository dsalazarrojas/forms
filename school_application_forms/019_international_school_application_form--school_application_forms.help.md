# International School Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from international students to facilitate their application process. Please take a few minutes to review the required fields and supporting materials needed for a successful application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your child's name and date of birth in the respective fields.
2. Enter your name and email address for verification purposes.
3. Select your child's grade level from the provided options.
4. Choose their native language from the options provided.
5. Fill in the contact details of your child's guardians (if applicable).
6. Enter your child's medical history and any allergies they may have.
7. Review your application carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the school your child is currently attending.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter your name, as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, required: true): Enter your child's ID number (if any).
* **Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, `date`, required: true): Enter your child's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your child's current grade level from the provided options (1-7).
* **Student Language** (`student_language`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the language your child speaks or understands from the options provided.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **School Type** (`school_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of school your child is currently attending (Public, Private, or Charter).
* **Application Reason** (`application_reason`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reason for your child's application to our school.
* **Address** (`student_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your child's address if different from yours.
* **City** (`student_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where your child resides.
* **State** (`student_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state where your child resides.
* **Zip** (`student_zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter your child's zip code.
* **Guardian 1 Name** (`guardian1_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your child's first guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian 1 Phone** (`guardian1_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of your child's first guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian 1 Email** (`guardian1_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of your child's first guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian 2 Name** (`guardian2_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your child’s second guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian 2 Phone** (`guardian2_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of your child’s second guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian 2 Email** (`guardian2_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of your child's second guardian (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your emergency contact (if different from your child's guardians).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact (if different from your child's guardians).
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of your emergency contact (if different from your child's guardians).
* **Medical History** (`student_medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Enter any medical conditions or allergies your child may have.
* **Allergies** (`student_allergies`, `text`, required: false): Enter any allergies your child may have.
