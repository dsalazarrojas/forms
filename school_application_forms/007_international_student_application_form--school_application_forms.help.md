# International Student Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather essential information from international students who are applying to a program or institution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth application process.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bio Data** (Page 1, Text): Enter your personal background information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (Page 1, Text): Provide your contact details, including your email address, phone number, and any other ways we can get in touch with you.
* **Passport Details** (Page 1, Text): Enter your passport details, including your passport number, expiration date, and issuing country.
* **Medical Data** (Page 1, Text): Share any medical information that may be relevant to your application, such as any medical conditions or allergies.
* **Qualifications** (Page 1, Text): List your relevant qualifications, skills, or experiences that make you a strong candidate for the program.
* **Motivation For Appliance** (Page 1, Text): Explain why you are applying to the program or institution, including your goals and expectations.
* **Supporting Documents** (Page 1, Text): Upload any supporting documents, such as certificates, diplomas, or transcripts, that demonstrate your qualifications.
* **Select Course** (Page 2, Multi-Select): Choose the courses you are interested in applying for. Please select all applicable options.
* **Further Comments** (Page 2, Text): Enter any additional comments or information that you would like to share with us.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your application carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Upload any supporting documents as a PDF or JPEG file.
* Please note that this form is for international students, so ensure you provide information relevant to your application.
