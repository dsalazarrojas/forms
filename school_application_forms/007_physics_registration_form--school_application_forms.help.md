# Physics Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Physics Registration Form is a form used by students to register for the physics course. This form collects personal and course-related information to help the physics registrar with the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you have all the necessary information and guidance from your physics teacher or the university administration before filling out this form.
2.  Fill in your Student ID number in the **Student ID** field.
3.  Answer the **Select Course** question by checking the box next to "Yes" if you are interested in registering for the physics course, or "No" otherwise.
4.  Enter your **Student Name** in the **Student Name** field.
5.  Provide your parent/guardian's name in the **Parent Name** field if you are a dependent student.
6.  Enter your **Date of Birth** in the **Date of Birth** field in the format MM/DD/YY.
7.  Enter your **Phone Number** in the **Phone Number** field.
8.  Enter your **Email** address in the **Email** field if you have one.
9.  Check the box next to "Yes" or "No" in the **Physics Registrar** field, depending on whether you have met with the physics registrar.
10.  Enter the date you met with the physics registrar in the **Registration Date** field, if you answered "Yes" in the previous step.
11.  Finally, enter the name of the physics course you wish to register for in the **Physics Course** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (student_id, number, required: false): Enter your student ID number.
* **Select Course** (course_id, select_one, required: false): Check "Yes" to express your interest in registering for the physics course, or "No" otherwise.
* **Student Name** (student_name, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID card.
* **Parent Name** (parent_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of your parent or guardian, if you are a dependent student.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YY format.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Physics Registrar** (physics_registrar, select_one, required: false): Check "Yes" if you have met with the physics registrar, or "No" otherwise.
* **Registration Date** (reg_date, date, required: false): Enter the date you met with the physics registrar if you answered "Yes" in the previous step.
* **Physics Course** (physics_course, text, required: false): Enter the name of the physics course you wish to register for.
