# University Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Application Form is a tool designed to gather information from students applying to the university. It will be used by the admissions office to review and process applications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form page by page.
2. For each page, read the field labels and descriptions carefully.
3. Enter your relevant information in the designated fields.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a required label).
5. Once you have completed all the pages, review your form for accuracy and completeness.
6. Submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Information** (`Personal Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Education Background** (`Education Background`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your educational background, including schools attended and degrees earned.
* **SAT Information** (`SAT Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your SAT scores, if applicable.
* **Activity Information** (`Activity Information`, `text`, required/optional): List your academic activities, hobbies, or other relevant experiences.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form on your behalf, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to use clear and concise language when filling out each field.
* Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact form@gic.mx.
