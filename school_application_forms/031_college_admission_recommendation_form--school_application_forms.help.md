## College Admission Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The College Admission Recommendation Form is a document that provides an in-depth evaluation of a student's strengths, weaknesses, and potential. It is typically submitted by recommenders (e.g., teachers, counselors, coaches) to support a student's college application. The form helps colleges and universities assess a student's fit for their institution and programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information: Provide your name, email, and phone number.
2. Rate the student's qualities and skills: Answer the rating questions (e.g., critical thinking, leadership, teamwork) using the provided options.
3. Describe the student's strengths and areas for growth: Enter your observations and thoughts on the student's top strengths and areas for improvement.
4. Share a memorable moment: Describe a specific instance where the student demonstrated exceptional skills or abilities.
5. Compare the student to their peers: Share your impression of the student's standing relative to their peers.
6. Assess college readiness: Evaluate whether the student is prepared for college-level work.
7. Provide an overall recommendation: Choose the level of enthusiasm you recommend the student for college.
8. Add any additional comments: You can include any other relevant information that supports or contextualizes your recommendation.
9. Sign and date the form: Confirm that your recommendation is accurate and genuine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recommender Full Name** (Required): Your name.
	+ Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (Required): Your email address.
	+ Enter your work email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): Your contact phone number.
	+ If you have a work phone number, please enter it for easier communication.
* **Job Title** (Required): Your position.
	+ Enter your job title or position at your institution.
* **Department** (Required): Your department or school unit.
	+ Enter your department or school unit for reference.
* **School Name** (Required): Where you work.
	+ Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **School Address** (Required): School address.
	+ Enter the physical address of your school or institution.
* **How Long Have You Known the Student** (Required): Select an option from the provided list.
	+ Choose how long you have known the student, from Less than 1 year to Over 4 years.
* **Relationship to Student** (Required): Select an option from the provided list.
	+ Choose how you relate to the student, e.g., teacher, counselor, coach, or other.
* **Student Full Name** (Required): The student's full name.
	+ Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Student Email** (Required): The student's email address.
	+ Enter the student's email address for contact purposes.
* **Student Graduation Year** (Required): The year the student graduated.
	+ Enter the year the student graduated or will graduate.
* **Courses Taught** (Optional): List any courses you have taught the student.
	+ If applicable, list any courses you have taught the student.
* **Academic Performance Rating** (Required): Rate the student's academic performance relative to peers.
	+ Choose how the student's performance compares, e.g., Top 5%, Top 10%, Below Average.
* **Intellectual Curiosity Rating** (Required): Rate the student's love for learning.
	+ Choose how the student approaches new ideas and concepts.
* **Work Ethic Rating** (Required): Rate the student's dedication to their work.
	+ Choose how strongly the student applies themselves to their tasks.
* **Class Participation Rating** (Required): Rate the student's engagement in class.
	+ Choose how well the student participates in class discussions and activities.
* **Critical Thinking Skills Rating** (Required): Rate the student's analysis abilities.
	+ Choose how effectively the student analyzes information and problems.
* **Writing Ability Rating** (Required): Rate the student's written communication.
	+ Choose how well the student writes and conveys their thoughts.
* **Leadership Qualities Rating** (Required): Rate the student's leadership potential.
	+ Choose how effectively the student leads and motivates others.
* **Teamwork Rating** (Required): Rate the student's collaboration and cooperation.
	+ Choose how well the student works with others.
* **Character Rating** (Required): Rate the student's integrity and values.
	+ Choose how well the student aligns with the institution's values and principles.
* **Resilience Rating** (Required): Rate the student's ability to handle challenges.
	+ Choose how well the student handles setbacks and difficult situations.
* **Student Top Strengths** (Required): Describe the student's top strengths.
	+ Enter a list of the student's exceptional abilities or qualities.
* **Areas for Growth** (Optional): Describe areas where the student can improve.
	+ Enter any areas where the student may struggle or has room for improvement.
* **Describe a Memorable Moment** (Required): Share a specific instance of the student's excellence.
	+ Describe a moment where the student demonstrated exceptional skills or abilities.
* **How Does Student Compare to Peers** (Required): Compare the student to their peers.
	+ Share your impression of the student's standing relative to their peers.
* **College Readiness Assessment** (Required): Evaluate the student's preparedness for college.
	+ Choose how well the student is prepared for college-level work.
* **Overall Recommendation Level** (Required): Choose the level of enthusiasm you recommend the student for college.
	+ Choose how strongly you recommend the student for college.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): Any other information.
	+ Enter any other relevant information that supports or contextualizes your recommendation.
* **Recommender Certification** (Required): Confirm your recommendation.
	+ Choose to certify that your recommendation is accurate and genuine.
