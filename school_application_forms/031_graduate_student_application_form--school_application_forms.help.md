# Graduate Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who are applying to a graduate program. Please provide the requested information accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1. Fill in your personal details.
2. Select your student status and program type (if applicable).
3. Provide your contact and academic information.
4. Specify your program start date and expected graduation date.
5. Enter your address and other required contact details.
6. Select your graduate level and field of study (if applicable).
7. Provide your research interests and personal statement.
8. Upload your resume, transcripts, and other required documents.
9. Provide any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (2)** (`text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (3)** (`date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Email (4)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address that you use for communication.
* **Contact Phone (5)** (`text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number if you have one.
* **Student Status (6)** (`select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Active
	+ Active (Non-Degree)
	+ Active (Non-Degree, International)
	+ Active (International)
	+ Deferred
	+ Inactive
	+ Other
* **Program Type (7)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the following programs:
	+ MA
	+ PhD
	+ Other
* **Program Start Date (8)** (`date`, required): Enter the date you plan to start your program.
* **Expected Graduation Date (9)** (`date`, required): Enter the date you expect to graduate from your program.
* **Address (10)** (`text`, required): Enter your current address.
* **City (11)** (`text`, required): Enter the city where you live.
* **State (12)** (`text`, required): Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip Code (13)** (`text`, required): Enter your zip code.
* **University (14)** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the university you are affiliated with or attended.
* **Graduate Level (15)** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the following levels:
	+ Bachelor
	+ Master
	+ PhD
* **Field of Study (16)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the following fields:
	+ Accounting
	+ Biology
	+ Business
	+ Chemistry
	+ Computer Science
	+ Engineering
	+ Geography
	+ History
	+ Mathematics
	+ Other
	+ Physics
	+ Psychology
	+ Statistics
* **Research Interests (17)** (`text`, required): Enter a brief description of your research interests.
* **Personal Statement (18)** (`text`, required): Write a brief statement outlining your background and motivation for applying.
* **References (19)** (`text`, required): Upload your references or enter their names and contact information.
* **Resume (20)** (`text`, required): Upload your resume or attach a link to it.
* **Transcripts (21)** (`text`, required): Upload your transcripts or attach a link to them.
* **Proof of Enrollment (22)** (`text`, required): Upload proof of your enrollment (e.g., letter from the university).
* **Proof of Degree (23)** (`text`, required): Upload proof of your degree (e.g., diploma or transcript).
* **Language Proficiency (24)** (`text`, required): Enter your language proficiency level.
* **Additional Comments (25)** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
