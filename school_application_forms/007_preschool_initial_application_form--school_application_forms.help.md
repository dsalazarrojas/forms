# Preschool Initial Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is the Preschool Initial Application Form, designed for parents or guardians to provide information about their child's application for preschool enrollment. The form helps our school administration collect relevant data about the child and their family for admission processing.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Begin by providing the required contact information in the "Parent Information" section (Field 1).
2.  In the "Student Information" section (Field 2), provide details about your child, including their student ID number if applicable.
3.  The "Contact Information" section (Field 3) requires details about the contact person to reach out in case of emergencies.
4.  The "Emergency Contact" section (Field 4) is for providing a secondary contact in case of emergencies.
5.  The "Student Documentation" section (Field 5) might be used for uploading or describing any relevant documents about your child.
6.  For your child's signature and your own signature, ensure they are filled out accurately as they confirm you understand and agree with the application details.
7.  Fill out "School Information" (Field 7) with details about your child's previous school and relevant information about their academic background.
8.  The "Notes" section (Field 9) can be used for any additional comments or information about your child you'd like to share with us.
9.  In "School Admin Signature" (Field 10), the school administration will confirm they've received your application.
10. Ensure the "Date of Submission" (Field 12) and "Time of Submission" (Field 13) are filled out accurately.
11. Fill in "Phone Number" (Field 14), "Email" (Field 15), and "Student ID Number" (Field 16) with your contact and student details.
12. Select the correct "Grade Level" (Field 17) of your child.
13. Enter your child's "Birthdate" (Field 18).
14. Select your child's "Gender" (Field 19), "Race" (Field 20), "Disability Status" (Field 21), "Language" (Field 22), and "Student Language" (Field 23) carefully.
15. Finally, fill out "Family Language" (Field 24) with your family's primary language.
