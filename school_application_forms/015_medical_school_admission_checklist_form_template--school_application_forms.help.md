# Medical School Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help medical school applicants provide their information for the admission process. It includes various fields to gather details about their educational background, medical experiences, personal info, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the Applicant Info section, which includes fields such as name, contact information, and other personal details.
2. Proceed to the Educational Background section, where you will provide information about your educational history, including the name of your medical school, program name, degree name, major, year of graduation, and date of graduation.
3. In the Medical Experiences section, you will describe your medical experiences, including any relevant training, certifications, or work experience in the medical field.
4. The Personal Info section is where you will provide your contact information, such as phone number and email address.
5. In the Medical School Application section, you will provide your medical school name and address.
6. The Medical Degree Preferences section is where you will indicate your preferences for your medical degree.
7. In the Submission Info section, you will provide any additional information about your submission.
8. Upload any relevant attachments or documents in the Upload Attachments section.
9. Confirm your information in the Confirm Info section.
10. Sign the form electronically by selecting "Yes" in the Signature Block.
11. Finally, select "Finish" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Info (1)**: Fill in your name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Educational Background (2)**: Provide information about your educational history, including your medical school, program name, degree name, major, year of graduation, and date of graduation.
* **Medical Experiences (3)**: Describe your medical experiences, including any relevant training, certifications, or work experience in the medical field.
* **Personal Info (4)**: Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Contact Info (5)**: Provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Medical School Application (6)**: Enter your medical school name and address.
* **Medical Degree Preferences (7)**: Indicate your preferences for your medical degree.
* **Submission Info (8)**: Provide any additional information about your submission.
* **Upload Attachments (9)**: Upload any relevant attachments or documents required for your application.
* **Confirm Info (10)**: Double-check your information to ensure accuracy.
* **Signature Block (11)**: Sign the form electronically by selecting "Yes".
* **Sign (12)**: Indicate your agreement to submit your application by selecting "Yes".
* **Submit (13)**: Review your application and submit it for review.
* **Medical School Name (14)**: Enter your medical school name.
* **Program Name (15)**: Enter the name of your program.
* **Degree Name (16)**: Enter the name of your degree.
* **Major (17)**: Enter your major.
* **Year of Graduation (18)**: Enter the year you graduated.
* **Date of Graduation (19)**: Enter the date of your graduation.
* **Medical School Address (20)**: Enter your medical school address.
* **Contact Phone (21)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email (22)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Medical License Number (23)**: Enter your medical license number.
* **Medical License State (24)**: Enter the state where your medical license is issued.
