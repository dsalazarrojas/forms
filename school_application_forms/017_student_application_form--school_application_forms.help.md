# Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Application Form is designed to collect demographic information about students for administrative and academic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Student ID number.
2. Provide your Full Name.
3. Enter your Date of birth.
4. Enter your Student Email address.
5. Enter your Parent Email address.
6. Enter the name of your School.
7. Select your Grade Level.
8. Enter the title of your Course (if applicable).
9. Enter the year you are in at school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID (1)**: Enter your unique Student ID number to identify yourself in the school's system.
* **Full Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth (3)**: Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Student Email (4)**: Enter your student email address for communication purposes.
* **Parent Email (5)**: Enter your parent's email address for communication purposes.
* **School Name (6)**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Grade Level (7)**: Select your current grade level in school (e.g. 9, 10, 11, etc.).
* **Course Title (8)**: Enter the title of your current course (if applicable).
* **School Year (9)**: Enter the year you are currently in at school (e.g. 9, 10, 11, etc.).
