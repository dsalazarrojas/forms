# Junior On Site Elementary School Teacher Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for the Junior On Site Elementary School Teacher position. The school administration will use the information provided in this form to evaluate your suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Application Form (page 1) with your contact information and a brief description of your teaching experience and qualifications.
2. On page 2, provide your education background, if any.
3. On page 3, describe your teaching experience, highlighting your relevant skills and accomplishments.
4. On page 4, write a personal statement about your teaching philosophy, approach, and goals.
5. On page 5, upload your resume, which should include your work experience, education, and relevant skills.
6. On page 6, enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
7. On page 7, indicate whether you have any professional references. If yes, provide their contact information.
8. On page 8, enter your job title (if applicable) and school address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form (page 1)** (`application_form`, text, required): Fill in your contact information and a brief description of your teaching experience and qualifications.
* **Education Background (page 2)** (`education_background`, note, optional): If you have any relevant education background, please describe it briefly.
* **Taching Experience (page 3)** (`teaching_experience`, text, required): Describe your teaching experience, highlighting your relevant skills and accomplishments.
* **Personal Statement (page 4)** (`personal_statement`, text, optional): Write a personal statement about your teaching philosophy, approach, and goals.
* **Resume (page 5)** (`resume`, text, required): Upload your resume, which should include your work experience, education, and relevant skills.
* **Contact Information (page 6)** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **References (page 7)** (`references`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you have any professional references. If yes, provide their contact information.
* **Email Address (page 8)** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (page 8)** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title (page 8)** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **School Address (page 8)** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter your school address.
