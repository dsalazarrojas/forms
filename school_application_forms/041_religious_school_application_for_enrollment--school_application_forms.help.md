# Religious School Application for Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect enrollment information from parents or guardians of students who wish to enroll in a religious school. The purpose of the form is to gather essential details about the student and their family, including contact information, academic background, and demographic data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Provide the student's name.
2. Enter the parent's email address.
3. Input the phone number of the parent or guardian.
4. Enter the school address where the student is currently enrolled or plans to enroll.
5. Select the student's current grade level.
6. Enter the student's date of birth.
7. Select the school year the student wishes to enroll in.
8. (Optional) If there's any other relevant information, enter it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification documents.
- **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the parent or guardian's email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number for easy contact.
- **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the school address where the student is currently enrolled or plans to enroll.
- **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, number, required/optional): Select the current grade level of the student.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the student's date of birth in the format "Month, Day, Year" (e.g., January 01, 2000).
- **School Year** (`school_year`, number, required/optional): Select the school year the student wishes to enroll in.
- **Other Field** (`other_field`, text, required/optional): If there's any other relevant information, enter it here. 

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough in filling out the form to avoid delays in the application process.
- Ensure that the information provided is up-to-date and correct.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
