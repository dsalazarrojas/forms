# SMV4A Field Trip Permission Form (Stripe) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SMV4A Field Trip Permission Form (Stripe) is a standardized form used to grant field trip permission to students. It collects necessary contact information, trip details, and any relevant notes or instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select a date for the field trip in the "date" field.
5. Choose a time for the field trip in the "time" field.
6. For the "note" field, provide any additional information or instructions regarding the field trip.
7. Choose your response for "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields by selecting one or more of the provided options.
8. If applicable, enter a decimal value for the "decimal" field and/or an integer value for the "integer" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your name here.
* **email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address here.
* **phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **date** (date, date, required): Select a date for the field trip.
* **time** (time, time, required): Choose a time for the field trip.
* **note** (note, note, optional): Provide any additional information or instructions regarding the field trip.
* **Select One** (Select One, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options by selecting "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the provided options by selecting "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **decimal** (decimal, number, optional): If required, enter a decimal value here. 
* **integer** (integer, number, optional): If required, enter an integer value here.

## Tips

* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication and organization of the field trip.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about the form or the field trip, do not hesitate to contact authorized personnel.
* This form is intended to be filled out by authorized personnel, so please do not share it with unauthorized individuals.
* The form will be reviewed and verified by school administrators before approval is granted for the field trip.
