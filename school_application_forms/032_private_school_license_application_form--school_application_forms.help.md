# private_school_license_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from a private school applying for a license. It is meant to gather necessary details about the school, including its name, type, and license status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name as they appear on your official documents.
2. Provide your street address, zip code, and phone number for communication purposes.
3. Enter your email address for verification and communication.
4. Select the type of school you are applying for (Elementary, High, Middle, or Private).
5. Choose the type of license you are applying for (Charter, Public, Private, or Religious).
6. Indicate the current license status (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
7. If applicable, enter the start and end dates of your license.
8. You can add any additional notes or comments if necessary.
9. Fill in the category and description of your school (if applicable).
10. If you submitted the form, enter the name of the person who submitted it and the date and time of submission.
11. If the form has been updated, indicate the name of the person who updated it and the date and time of update.
12. If the form has been deleted, indicate the name of the person who deleted it and the date and time of deletion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`First name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (`Last name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Street address** (`Street address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of your school.
* **Zip code** (`Zip code`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of your school's location.
* **Phone number** (`Phone number`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number of your school.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of your school.
* **School name** (`School name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **School type** (`School type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of school you are applying for (Elementary, High, Middle, or Private).
* **License type** (`License type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of license you are applying for (Charter, Public, Private, or Religious).
* **License status** (`License status`, select_one, required): Indicate the current license status (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Start date** (`Start date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your license (if applicable).
* **End date** (`End date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your license (if applicable).
* **Note** (`Note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your school.
* **Category** (`Category`, text, required/optional): Enter the category of your school (if applicable).
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your school (if applicable).
* **Submitted by** (`Submitted by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted date** (`Submitted date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted time** (`Submitted time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Updated by** (`Updated by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who updated the form.
* **Updated date** (`Updated date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was updated.
* **Updated time** (`Updated time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the form was updated.
* **Deleted by** (`Deleted by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who deleted the form.
* **Deleted date** (`Deleted date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was deleted.
* **Deleted time** (`Deleted time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the form was deleted.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can consult with the relevant authorities or school administration.
- Remember to submit the form accurately and honestly.
