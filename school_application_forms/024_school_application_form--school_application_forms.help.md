# School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Application Form is designed to gather information from students applying to a school or program. This form is typically used for students to provide their personal and academic information to support their application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information as a applicant (Page 1).
2. Enter your student details, including any relevant academic background (Page 2).
3. Enter your guardian's contact information (Page 3).
4. Describe your academic history, including any relevant experiences or achievements (Page 4).
5. Upload any required documents, such as transcripts or letters of recommendation (Page 5).
6. Write a statement explaining why you are a good fit for the school or program (Page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`Applicant Information`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your personal details, including your name, address, and contact information.
* **Student Details** (`Student Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant academic background information, such as previous schools attended, grades, or academic achievements.
* **Guardian Contact Information** (`Guardian Contact Information`, `text`, required: false): Provide your guardian's contact information, including their name, address, and relationship to you.
* **Academic History** (`Academic History`, `text`, required: false): Describe your academic history, including any relevant experiences or achievements.
* **Upload Documents** (`Upload Documents`, `text`, required: false): Upload any required documents, such as transcripts or letters of recommendation.
* **Student Statement** (`Student Statement`, `text`, required: false): Write a statement explaining why you are a good fit for the school or program.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the school or program administrators.
* Double-check that all required documents are uploaded.
* Proofread your student statement for spelling and grammar errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the school or program administrators.
