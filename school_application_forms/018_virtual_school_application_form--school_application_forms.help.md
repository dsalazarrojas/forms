# Virtual School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual School Application Form is a tool used to gather information from students and their parents/guardians for school-related purposes. It helps the school to collect and process application data efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Fill in all the required fields with valid information.
3. Make sure to upload any required documents.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Info** (id: 1, type: text, required: false): Provide a brief overview of your application.
* **Student Info** (id: 2, type: text, required: false): Enter your student's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Parent Info** (id: 3, type: text, required: false): Enter your name, relationship with the student, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (id: 4, type: text, required: false): Enter your contact information so the school can reach out to you.
* **Student Details** (id: 5, type: text, required: false): Provide any additional details about your student, such as their interests or goals.
* **Educational Background** (id: 6, type: text, required: false): Enter your student's educational background, including their previous schools and qualifications.
* **Upload Documents** (id: 7, type: text, required: false): Upload any required documents, such as birth certificates or transcripts.
* **Upload Additional Documents** (id: 8, type: text, required: false): Upload any additional documents that support your application.
* **Email** (id: 10, type: email, required: false): Enter your email address so the school can contact you.
* **Phone** (id: 11, type: text, required: false): Enter your phone number so the school can contact you.
* **Date** (id: 12, type: date, required: false): If applicable, enter the date you're applying for or the date you'd like to start school.
* **Time** (id: 13, type: time, required: false): If applicable, enter the time you'd like to start school.
* **Note** (id: 14, type: note, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about your application.
* **Address** (id: 15, type: text, required: false): Enter your address so the school can contact you.
* **School Name** (id: 16, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the school you're applying to.
* **School Email** (id: 17, type: text, required: false): Enter the email address of the school.
* **School Phone** (id: 18, type: text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the school.
* **Student School Name** (id: 19, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the school your student is currently in or will be attending.
* **Student School Grade** (id: 20, type: text, required: false): Enter your student's current grade level.
* **Student School Class** (id: 21, type: text, required: false): Enter your student's current class or division.
* **Student School Start Date** (id: 22, type: date, required: false): If applicable, enter the date your student started school.
* **Student School End Date** (id: 23, type: date, required: false): If applicable, enter the date your student will end school.
* **Parent School Name** (id: 24, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the school your parent/guardian is associated with.
* **Parent School Email** (id: 25, type: text, required: false): Enter the email address of the school your parent/guardian is associated with.

Note: Make sure to fill out all the required fields and provide accurate information to ensure a smooth application process.
