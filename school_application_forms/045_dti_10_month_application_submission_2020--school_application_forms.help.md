# dti-10-month-application-submission-2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting applications for a 10-month program. Please fill it out accurately to ensure your application is considered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out all the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Attach any required documents as specified in the form.
3. Review your application carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (<required>)
* **First Name**: Enter your first name here.

### Last Name (<required>)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.

### Middle Name (<optional>)
* **Middle Name**: Enter your middle name here, if applicable.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).

### Email (<required>)
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address here.

### Address (<required>)
* **Address**: Enter your current address.

### City (<required>)
* **City**: Enter the city where you currently reside.

### Postal Code (<required>)
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code here.

### Phone Number (<required>)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number here.

### High School Diploma Earned
* **High School Diploma Earned**: Select 'Yes' if you have earned a high school diploma, 'No' if you have not, or 'Other' if it's not a high school diploma.

### Post Secondary Education Attended
* **Post Secondary Education Attended**: Select all relevant post-secondary education institutions you have attended.

### Highest Degree Earned
* **Highest Degree Earned**: Select the highest degree you have earned.

### Years of Post Secondary Education
* **Years of Post Secondary Education**: Enter the number of years you have attended post-secondary education.

### Years of Post Secondary Education Relevant to Field
* **Years of Post Secondary Education Relevant to Field**: Enter the number of years your post-secondary education was relevant to your field of work.

### Job Title
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.

### Employer Name
* **Employer Name**: Enter the name of your current or past employer.

### Job Duration
* **Job Duration**: Enter the number of years you have worked in your current or past job.

### Job Skill Used
* **Job Skill Used**: Enter the skills you have used in your current or past job.

### Job Skill Level
* **Job Skill Level**: Enter the level of your job skills.

### First Reference
* **First Reference**: Enter the name and contact information of your first reference.

### Job Title of First Reference
* **Job Title of First Reference**: Enter the job title of your first reference.

### Employer of First Reference
* **Employer of First Reference**: Enter the name of your first reference's employer.

### Upload Document
* **Upload Document**: Attach any relevant documents required for your application.

Note: This form has been designed to be user-friendly and clear in its instructions. If you have any questions or issues, please contact our support team at [support email].
