## English Medium Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to register their details for English medium programs or courses. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to facilitate a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **Student Name** in the text field provided.
2. Provide your **Parent's Name**, as required by the institution.
3. Enter your **Date Of Birth** in the format of day/month/year (DD/MM/YYYY).
4. If you have a valid email address, please enter it in the **Email** field. This is not required, but it will help us contact you if needed.
5. You can add your **Phone** number for us to contact you, if needed.
6. Enter the **School Name** where you are currently studying or wish to register with.
7. Select your preferred **Medium** from the options provided (Option 1 or Option 2).
8. Choose one or more **Program Name(s)** for which you wish to register.
9. Enter your **Address** where you currently reside.
10. Select your **Level** of registration (Option 1 or Option 2).
11. Enter the **Medium Name**, which is related to your registration preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian who is accompanying you in this registration process.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of day/month/year (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address if you have one. This will help us contact you regarding your registration.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for us to contact you, if needed.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school you are currently studying or wish to register with.
* **Medium**: Select the medium you prefer to learn through (Option 1 or Option 2).
* **Program Name**: Choose one or more programs you wish to register for (Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Address**: Enter your current address where you reside.
* **Level**: Select the level of your registration (Option 1 or Option 2).
* **Medium Name**: Enter the name related to your preferred registration (Medium Name).
