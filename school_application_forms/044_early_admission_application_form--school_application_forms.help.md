# Early Admission Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Early Admission Application Form is a simplified version of the general application form designed for students who want to get early admission into the school. It's a document used to gather information from students before they apply for regular admission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details such as name, email, and contact information in the **Application Form** section.
2. Select the program(s) you are interested in from the **Select One Program** and **Select Multiple Programs** sections.
3. Provide any relevant notes or comments about your application in the **Notes** section.
4. Upload any required documents such as transcripts or other additional information in the **Upload Documents** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Application Form
* **Application Form**: Enter your name, which will be used as a reference for your application.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Notes**: Enter any relevant notes or comments about your application.

### Select One Program
* **Select One Program**: Select the program you are interested in early admission from the drop-down menu.

### Select Multiple Programs
* **Select Multiple Programs**: Select multiple programs you are interested in early admission from the drop-down menu.

### Upload Documents
* **Upload Documents**: Upload any required documents such as transcripts or additional information.

### Other Fields
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Academic Level**: Enter your academic level.
* **Student Status**: Select your status as an Active or Inactive student.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your current school.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of your school.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of your school.
* **School Website**: Enter the website of your school.
* **Upload Additional Documents**: Upload any additional documents.
* **Upload Other Documents**: Upload any other documents.
* **Upload Transcripts**: Upload your transcripts.
* **Upload Additional Info**: Upload any additional information.
