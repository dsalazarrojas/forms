# Academy Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students or their guardians to submit an application to the academy. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Read and carefully follow the instructions provided at the top of the form.
- Complete all the required fields as indicated. 
- Use the hints for guidance on what to input in each field.
- Submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name. This is a required field.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name. This is a required field.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email address**: Enter your valid email address. This is a required field.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number. This is a required field.
* **Mailing address**: Enter your full mailing address in the format Street, City, State, Zip Code. This is a required field.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the given options. This field is not required.
* **Parent or Guardian Information (if under 18)**: If you're under 18, please fill in your parent or guardian's details.
* **Parent or guardian full name**: Enter your parent or guardian's full name.
* **Parent or guardian phone number**: Enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Parent or guardian email**: Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Academic Information**: 
  - **Name of previous or current school**: Enter the name of the school you're currently attending or previously attended.
  - **Grade level applying for**: Select the grade level you're applying for from the given options. This is a required field.
  - **Areas of academic interest**: Select all the areas of academic interest that apply to you.
  - **Does the applicant have any special educational needs or accommodations?**: If yes, please describe in this field.
* **How did you hear about our academy**: Select how you heard about the academy from the given options. This field is not required.
* **Why are you interested in attending this academy?**: Enter your reason for applying. This is a required field.
* **I certify that the information provided is accurate and complete**: Select 'Yes - I certify' to confirm you've filled in the form accurately. This is a required field.
