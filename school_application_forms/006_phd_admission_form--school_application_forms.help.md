# Phd Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phd Admission Form is a document designed to collect essential information from potential PhD applicants for the admission process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Applicant Information section (Page 1) with your contact details and any other information requested.
2. Proceed to the Contact Details section (Page 2) and provide your contact details.
3. In the Academic Details section (Page 3), describe your academic background and any relevant experiences.
4. If you have any research interests, provide them in the Research Interests section (Page 4).
5. If you have any academic certificates, upload them in the Upload Certificates section (Page 5).
6. Describe your research experience in the Research Experience section (Page 6).
7. In the Publication History section (Page 7), list any publications you have.
8. If you have any referees or academic references, provide their information in the Referee Info and Referee Name sections (Pages 8 and 9).
9. Finally, confirm your referee's position (Page 10) and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicat_info`, text, required/optional): This is the initial section to introduce yourself, providing any necessary contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Here, you will provide your contact information to be reached.
* **Academic Details** (`academic_details`, text, required/optional): In this section, you should describe your academic background, highlighting any relevant experiences.
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`, text, required/optional): This is the place to list your research interests and any relevant areas of study.
* **Upload Certificates** (`upload_certificates`, note, required/optional): If you have any certificates, such as diplomas or transcripts, you can upload them here.
* **Research Experience** (`research_experience`, text, required/optional): Describe your research experience, including any relevant projects or studies.
* **Publication History** (`publication_history`, text, required/optional): List any publications you have authored or co-authored.
* **Referee Info** (`referee_info`, text, required/optional): Provide any information about your referees or academic references.
* **Referee Name** (`referee_name`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm if you have a referee and select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Referee Position** (`referee_position`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your referee's position and select 'Yes' or 'No'.
