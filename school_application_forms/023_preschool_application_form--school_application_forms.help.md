# Preschool Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Application Form is designed to collect essential information from parents and guardians to support their child's admission to our preschool program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:
1. Select "Parent Info" to indicate if you're the parent/guardian or a representative.
2. Fill out "Child Info" with your child's details (name, age, etc.).
3. Provide your "Contact Info" in the format specified.
4. Answer "Preschool Info" to indicate if you're seeking admission to our preschool program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Info** (Select Multiple):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you're a parent or guardian.
	+ Choose "No" if you're a representative or other.
* **Child Info** (Note):
	+ Write your child's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (Select One):
	+ Choose "Yes" to indicate you want us to contact you.
	+ Choose "No" if you prefer not to be contacted.
* **Preschool Info** (Date):
	+ Enter the date you're interested in starting our preschool program.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate and detailed as possible in your responses.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
* We'll use the information provided to create an application form with necessary details for the admission process.
