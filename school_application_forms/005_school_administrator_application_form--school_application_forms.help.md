# School Administrator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Administrator Application Form is a tool used to collect information from school administrators who are interested in applying for a position within the school.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the Application Information page**, which includes fields for your name and any other relevant details.
2. **Provide your name** in the `applicant_name` field.
3. **Enter your contact phone number** in the `applicant_phone` field.
4. **Enter the name of the school** you are associated with in the `school_name` field.
5. **Describe your experience as an administrator in schools** in the `administrator_experience` field.
6. **Provide details about your education background** in the `education_background` field.
7. **Enter the name of the institution where you received your education** in the `education_institution` field.
8. **Select your current position as a school administrator** from the `school_administrator_position` field (e.g. Principal, Teacher, Dean, Director).
9. **Select at least two areas of specialization** from the `job_specialization` field (e.g. Curriculum and Instruction, Class Management, Budgeting and Financial Management, School Event Planning, Facilities Management).
10. **Enter the number of years you have worked in the school** in the `years_of_service` field.
11. **Select your current job status** from the `job_status` field (e.g. Full Time, Part-time, Volunteer, Retired).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Information**: This page is the first step in the form and requires you to provide basic information about yourself and your application.
* **Applicant Name**: This field is for your full name as an applicant.
* **Applicant Phone**: This field is for your contact phone number.
* **School Name**: This field is for the name of the school you are associated with.
* **Administrator Experience**: This field is for you to describe your experience as an administrator in schools.
* **Education Background**: This field is for you to provide details about your education background.
* **Education Institution**: This field is for you to enter the name of the institution where you received your education.
* **School Administrator Position**: This field is for you to select your current position as a school administrator.
* **Job Specialization**: This field is for you to select at least two areas of specialization.
* **Years of Service**: This field is for you to enter the number of years you have worked in the school.
* **Job Status**: This field is for you to select your current job status.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Select all applicable options for your job specialization and job status.
- Be as detailed as possible when describing your experience and education background.
