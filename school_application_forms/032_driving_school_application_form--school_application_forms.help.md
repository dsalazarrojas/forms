# driving_school_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to apply to a driving school or program. It helps the driving school to gather necessary details about the applicant and facilitate the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your details in the **Applicant Details** section.
2.  Choose your preferred driving school from the **driving_school_name** dropdown menu.
3.  Select a date for your application.
4.  Enter your contact number.
5.  Provide your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Applicant Details**: This section is where you can fill in your name, address, and other relevant details about yourself. This information will be used to contact you and keep you updated about your application status.
*   **driving_school_name**: Please choose the driving school you are interested in applying to. This will help us to direct your application to the correct school.
*   **application_date**: Select a date for your application. This will help us to schedule your application review and assessment.
*   **contact_number**: Enter a phone number where we can contact you for further information or updates about your application.
*   **email**: Provide your email address so we can send you information and updates about your application status.
*   **driving_school_address**: This field is not currently used. If you need to provide additional information about your driving school, please use this field to enter your address.
