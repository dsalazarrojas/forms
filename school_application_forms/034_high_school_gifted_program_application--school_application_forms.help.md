# High School Gifted Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Gifted Program Application form is designed to collect information from students interested in joining the High School Gifted Program.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Use the space provided below each field to enter your answer.
3. For fields with specific instructions, follow the guidance provided for each field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **High School Gifted Program Application** (`high_school_gifted_program_application`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the application. This field is required.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name. This field is optional.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school you currently attend. This field is optional.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you. This field is optional.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This field is optional.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter your home address. This field is optional.
* **Student Transcript** (`student_transcript`, text, optional): Attach or enter the URL of your transcript, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **Student Resume** (`student_resume`, text, optional): Attach or enter the URL of your resume, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): Enter your guardian's name. This field is optional.
* **Guardian Contact Email** (`guardian_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your guardian's email address. This field is optional.
* **Guardian Contact Phone** (`guardian_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your guardian's phone number. This field is optional.
* **Guardian Address** (`guardian_address`, text, optional): Enter your guardian's home address. This field is optional.
