# Student Exchange Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Exchange Application form is designed to collect information from students applying for exchange programs. This form is used to gather basic contact information, school affiliations, and program preferences of the students.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in your student name.
- Enter your valid student email address.
- Provide your student phone number.
- Select your birthdate in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
- Check if you have attended school in the past or if you are currently attending school.
- Choose the country you are from.
- Select the programs you are interested in (you can choose multiple programs).
- Select your native language.
- Choose if you are interested in participating in a student exchange program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (text)**: Enter your full name.
* **Student Email (text)**: Enter a valid email address associated with your student account.
* **Student Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Birthdate (date)**: Select your birthdate in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Student School (select_one)**: 
  - If you have attended school in the past, choose 'Yes'.
  - If you are currently attending school, choose 'No'.
* **Student Country (select_one)**: Choose the country you are from.
* **Student Program (select_multiple)**: Choose multiple programs you are interested in.
* **Student Language (select_one)**: Choose your native language.
* **Student Exchange Program (select_one)**: Choose if you are interested in participating in a student exchange program.
  - If you are interested, choose 'Yes'.
  - If you are not interested, choose 'No'.
