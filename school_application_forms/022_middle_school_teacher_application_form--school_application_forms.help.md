# Middle School Teacher Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect application information from middle school teachers. It is not intended for other types of applications or forms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Applicant Information** (page 1) by providing your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your **Contact Information** (page 2) to ensure we can reach you for any follow-up questions or communications.
3. Share your **Work Experience** (page 3) by describing your previous teaching experiences and relevant work history.
4. Provide your **Education Background** (page 4) by listing your educational qualifications and relevant coursework.
5. Upload your **Teaching Certification** (page 5) or other relevant documents.
6. Write a brief **Personal Statement** (page 6) explaining why you are a strong fit for the position or program.
7. Upload your **Resume** (page 7) for a detailed review of your qualifications.
8. Finally, click **Submit** (page 8) to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Applicant Information
- **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required): Please provide your name and any other relevant details.

### Page 2: Contact Information
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your email and phone number to ensure we can reach you for any follow-up questions or communications.

### Page 3: Work Experience
- **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required): Describe your previous teaching experiences and relevant work history.

### Page 4: Education Background
- **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required): List your educational qualifications and relevant coursework.

### Page 5: Teaching Certification
- **Teaching Certification** (`teaching_certification`, text, required): Upload your teaching certification or other relevant documents.

### Page 6: Personal Statement
- **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, text, required): Explain why you are a strong fit for the position or program.

### Page 7: Resume Upload
- **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload`, text, optional): Upload your resume for a detailed review of your qualifications.

### Page 8: Submit
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to complete your application. 

### Page 9: Cancel
- **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, required): Click to cancel your application.

Tips:

* Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting your application.
* Use clear and concise language in your responses.
* Upload your resume in a format that can be easily read by our review team.
* Double-check for spelling and grammar mistakes before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact info].
