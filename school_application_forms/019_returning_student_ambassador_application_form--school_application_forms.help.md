# Returning Student Ambassador Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from students who are re-applying to be ambassadors for the school. Completing this form ensures that you are eligible to re-apply and provides the required details for the selection process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, including all required fields.
2. Double-check that the form is relevant to your re-application and that you are not already an ambassador.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form Information** (`application_form_information`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about your application.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your full name as it appears on your transcript or ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (`expected_graduation_date`, date, required: false): Enter your expected graduation date.
* **Major and Minor** (`major_and_minor`, note, required: false): Indicate your major and minor (if applicable).
* **Returning Ambassador Role** (`returning_ambassador_role`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you are re-applying as a 'returning ambassador' or 'ambassador'.
* **Previous Ambassador Experience** (`previous_ambassador_experience`, select_one, required: false): Indicate if you have previous ambassador experience.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required: false): Enter your student ID number.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, time, required: false): Enter the year you are re-applying.
* **Returning Ambassador Statement 1** (`returning_ambassador_statement`, note, required: false): Please provide a statement explaining why you are re-applying as a returning ambassador.
* **Returning Ambassador Statement 2** (`returning_ambassador_statement_2`, note, required: false): Provide another statement supporting your application.
* **Returning Ambassador Statement 3** (`returning_ambassador_statement_3`, note, required: false): Enter a further statement detailing your qualifications or strengths.
* **Returning Ambassador Statement 4** (`returning_ambassador_statement_4`, note, required: false): Please provide a statement describing your previous experience as an ambassador.
* **Returning Ambassador Statement 5** (`returning_ambassador_statement_5`, note, required: false): Enter a statement outlining your goals for the role.
* **Returning Ambassador Statement 6** (`returning_ambassador_statement_6`, note, required: false): Provide any additional information you believe is relevant to your application.

## Tips

* Double-check that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
* If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to the contact person listed on the form.
