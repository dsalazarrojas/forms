# School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a school enrollment form used to collect required information for new students enrolling in a school program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents required for this form.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "School Programs" section, depending on whether you are applying for a program.
3. Enter your grade level (if applicable).
4. Fill out the student, parent, and payment information sections.
5. If you are enrolling in multiple programs, select the programs from the "Other Programs" list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enrollment Form** (`enrollment_form`, `note`, required): This is the main form section where you can enter your information.
* **School Programs** (`school_programs`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you are applying for a school program, and "No" if not.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter your current grade level.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, `text`, required): Enter your student's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's name and contact information.
* **Program Information** (`program_info`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about the school program you are enrolling in.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required): Enter the payment method and information.
* **Other Programs** (`other_programs`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you are enrolling in multiple programs, select the programs from the list.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact the school administration.
