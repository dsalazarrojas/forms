# School Clothing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Clothing Application Form is used to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's clothing needs for school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your child's name in the `student_name` field.
2. Enter your child's ID number in the `student_id` field.
3. Provide your name and contact information in the `parent_name`, `parent_address`, `parent_email`, and `parent_phone` fields.
4. Select the student's grade level from the options provided in the `student_grade_level` field.
5. Choose the clothing items your child needs from the options in the `student_clothing_needs` field.
6. Enter your child's school name and address in the `school_name` and `school_address` fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your child's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Student ID**: Enter your child's student ID number, if applicable.
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **Parent Address**: Enter your address to contact you for further information.
* **Parent Email**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Student Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Student Grade Level**: Choose the student's grade level as of the current school year.
* **Student Clothing Needs**: Select all the clothing items your child requires. Choose from options like Shoes, Socks, Pants, Shirt, Jacket, or Other.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your child's school.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of your child's school.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled accurately and completely.
- You can select multiple clothing items in the `student_clothing_needs` field.
- If your child requires clothing for a specific reason (e.g., uniform, dress code), provide a brief explanation in the `Other` option under `Student Clothing Needs`.
