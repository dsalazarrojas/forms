# Application_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and audition details from applicants for a specific audition or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the date of your desired audition in the "Audition Date" field.
5. Select the time of your desired audition in the "Audition Time" field.
6. Enter the address to which you can be contacted in the "Contact Address" field.
7. Select the audition room where you would like to be considered for in the "Audition Location" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Audition Date** (`audition_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you would like to be considered for the audition.
* **Audition Time** (`audition_time`, time, required: false): Select the time you would like to be considered for the audition.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address where you can be contacted.
* **Audition Location** (`audition_location`, select_one, required: false): Select the audition room where you would like to be considered.
* **Notes** (`contact_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about your application.

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check your contact information and audition details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the application process, do not hesitate to reach out to the contact person.
* Ensure that you have not already submitted an application for this audition before filling out this form.
