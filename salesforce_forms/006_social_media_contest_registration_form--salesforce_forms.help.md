# social_media_contest_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in a social media contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the second field. This must be a valid email address.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the third field.
4. Enter your **Social Media Handle** in the fourth field. This is where we can find your social media account.
5. Select your **Social Media Type** from the dropdown menu in the fifth field.
6. Enter your **Social Media URL** in the sixth field if you want to link your social media account.
7. Select your **Social Media Platform** from the dropdown menu in the seventh field.
8. Enter your **Social Media Content Link** if you want to link your content in the eighth field.
9. Select the type of **Social Media Contest Type** you are participating in, if applicable.
10. Enter your **Prize Category** if you are participating in a contest for a prize.
11. Select your **Prize Type** from the dropdown menu in the eleventh field.
12. Enter a description of your **Other Prize Description** if you are participating in a contest for an other type of prize.
13. Answer **Yes** or **No** to **Participant Agreement** questions in the thirteenth and fourteenth fields.
14. Enter your **Participant Age** in the sixteenth field.
15. Enter your **Participant Country**, **Participant City**, **Participant State**, and **Participant Zip** in the respective fields.
16. Enter your **Participant Country of Origin** if it is different from your current country.
17. Select your **Participant Language** from the dropdown menu.
18. Select your **Participant Gender** from the dropdown menu.
19. Enter your **Participant Occupation** if you want to share this information.
20. Enter any **Participant Comments** you have about your participation in the contest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like to be identified in the contest.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Please enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Social Media Handle** (`social_media_handle`, text, required): Please enter your social media handle where we can find your account.
* **Social Media Type** (`social_media_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of social media platform you are using (e.g. Instagram, Facebook, Twitter).
* **Social Media URL** (`social_media_url`, text, required): Please enter the URL of your social media account if you want to link it.
* **Social Media Platform** (`social_media_platform`, select_one, required): Please select the platform where your social media account is located (e.g. Facebook, Twitter, Snapchat).
* **Social Media Content Link** (`social_media_content_link`, text, required): Please enter the URL of the content you are submitting for the contest.
* **Social Media Contest Type** (`social_media_contest_type`, text, required): Please select the type of contest you are participating in.
* **Prize Category** (`prize_category`, text, required): Please enter the category of the prize you are participating for.
* **Prize Type** (`prize_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of prize you are participating for (e.g. gift card, other).
* **Other Prize Description** (`other_prize_description`, text, required): Please enter a description of your other prize if you are participating in a contest for an other type of prize.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_multiple, required): Please answer yes or no to indicate your agreement with the terms and conditions of the contest.
* **Participant Agreement Holds** (`participant_agreement_holds`, select_multiple, required): Please answer yes or no to indicate if you agree to hold the participant agreement.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required): Please enter your age.
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, text, required): Please enter your country.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, text, required): Please enter your city.
* **Participant State** (`participant_state`, text, required): Please enter your state.
* **Participant Zip** (`participant_zip`, text, required): Please enter your zip code.
* **Participant Country of Origin** (`participant_country_of_origin`, text, required): Please enter your country of origin.
* **Participant Language** (`participant_language`, select_one, required): Please select your language.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, required): Please select your gender.
* **Participant Occupation** (`participant_occupation`, text, required): Please enter your occupation.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, required): Please enter any comments you have about your participation in the contest.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Please enter valid information where applicable.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to this help guide.
* If you have any issues with filling out the form, please contact us.
