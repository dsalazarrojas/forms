# Virtual Event Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from attendees for virtual events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the type of event you wish to attend from the "Event Type" field.
4. If you have a purchase order, specify it in the "Purchase Order" field.
5. If you have attendees, add their names and email addresses in the "Attendee List", "Attendee Name", and "Attendee Email" fields.
6. If you have purchase order items, describe them in the "Purchase Order Items Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **Required**: no): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**: no): Enter your email address.
* **Company** (`company`, text, **Required**: no): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, **Required**: no): Select the type of event you wish to attend from the list.
* **Purchase Order** (`purchase_order`, time, **Required**: no): If you have a purchase order, specify the date and time of the event.
* **Attendee List** (`attendee_list`, text, **Required**: no): List the attendees attending the event, one name per line.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, **Required**: no): Enter the name of each attendee.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, **Required**: no): Enter the email address of each attendee.
* **Purchase Order Items** (`purchase_order_items`, text, **Required**: no): Describe the items included in your purchase order.
* **Purchase Order Items Description** (`purchase_order_items_description`, text, **Required**: no): Describe each item in your purchase order.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, **Required**: no): Enter the email address of each attendee.
