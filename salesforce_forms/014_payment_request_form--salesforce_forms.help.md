# Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Request Form is used to collect information necessary for processing payments. This form is typically used for requesting payments for invoices, expenses, or other financial transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
* For fields without a required indicator, you may choose not to fill them in or leave them as is.
* Click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Request Form**: Enter a brief description of the payment request if necessary.
* **Amount Due**: Enter the amount of money owed.
* **Payment Method**: Select "Yes" if you are paying through a bank transfer, or "No" otherwise.
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any specific payment terms or instructions for the payment, such as payment method or due date.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date you are expecting to receive the payment.
* **Account Holder**: Enter the name of the account holder or individual making the payment.
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number associated with the payment.
* **Address**: Enter the address associated with the account holder or individual making the payment.
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of the bank or financial institution associated with the payment.
* **Bank Account**: Enter the account number or identifier at the bank.
* **Routing Number**: Enter the routing number of the bank account.
