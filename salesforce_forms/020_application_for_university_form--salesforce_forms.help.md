# Application For University Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Application For University Form is used to collect personal and academic information from applicants to determine eligibility for admission to our university.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
- Provide honest and truthful information.
- Make sure to check the required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, **text**, required): This field is used to capture the applicant's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, **text**, required): This field is used to capture the applicant's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, **date**, required): This field is used to capture the applicant's date of birth.
* **Email Address** (`email`, **email**, required): This field is used to capture the applicant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, **text**, required): This field is used to capture the applicant's phone number.
* **Citizenship** (`citizenship`, **select_one**, required): This field is used to determine if the applicant is a domestic, international, or permanent resident.
* **High School Name** (`high_school_name`, **text**, required): This field is used to capture the name of the high school the applicant attended.
* **High School Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, **number**, required): This field is used to capture the year the applicant graduated from high school.
* **High School GPA** (`gpa`, **number**, required): This field is used to capture the applicant's high school GPA.
* **Standardized Test Score (SAT/ACT)** (`standardized_test_score`, **text**, optional): This field is used to capture any standardized test scores the applicant has.
* **Degree Level** (`degree_level`, **select_one**, required): This field is used to determine if the applicant is applying for an undergraduate, graduate, or other degree program.
* **Intended Major/Field of Study** (`intended_major`, **text**, required): This field is used to capture the major or field of study the applicant is interested in.
* **Secondary interests or minors** (`secondary_interests`, **text**, optional): This field is used to capture any secondary interests or minors the applicant has.
* **Describe your extracurricular activities** (`extracurricular_activities`, **text**, optional): This field is used to capture any extracurricular activities the applicant has participated in.
* **Leadership experience** (`leadership_experience`, **text**, optional): This field is used to capture any leadership experience the applicant has.
* **Community service or volunteer work** (`community_service`, **text**, optional): This field is used to capture any community service or volunteer work the applicant has.
* **Why do you want to attend our university?** (`essay_prompt_1`, **text**, required): This field is used to capture the applicant's reasons for wanting to attend our university.
* **Describe a challenge you have overcome** (`essay_prompt_2`, **text**, optional): This field is used to capture a challenge the applicant has overcome.

This guide will help you complete the form accurately and efficiently. Please make sure to review each field carefully and submit truthful and honest information.
