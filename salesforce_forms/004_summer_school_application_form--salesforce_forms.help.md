# Summer School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students applying to attend summer school programs. It gathers essential details about the student, their contact and parent details, and other relevant information to ensure a smooth and efficient application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling in the `Application Form` section with your name, school year, and other relevant details.
2. Provide your `Contact Information`, including your address, phone number, and email.
3. If applicable, enter your `Tuition Fees` and select the preferred payment method.
4. Upload any required `Application Documents` in a format that is easily accessible.
5. In the `Additional Comments` section, provide any additional information you'd like to share about your application.
6. Enter your `Student Details`, including any relevant medical information or special requirements.
7. Provide information about your parents' details in the `Parent Details` section.
8. Specify your emergency contact information in the `Emergency Contact` section.
9. Finally, provide a brief `Student History` about your past educational background.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form**:
	+ This is the main section for you to input your application details.
* **Contact Information**:
	+ Enter your full name, address, phone number, and email address for us to reach you efficiently.
* **Tuition Fees**:
	+ If applicable, enter the fees you are willing to pay for the summer school program.
* **Payment Method**:
	+ Choose how you plan to pay for your application (e.g., online, bank transfer, or other methods).
* **Application Documents**:
	+ Upload relevant documents required for your application, such as birth certificate or transcripts.
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ This is an optional field for you to add any additional information about your application.
* **Student Details**:
	+ Enter any medical information or special requirements you have as a student.
* **Parent Details**:
	+ Provide information about your parents, such as their name, phone number, and contact address.
* **Emergency Contact**:
	+ Specify an emergency contact person, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
* **Student History**:
	+ Give a brief overview of your past educational background, including schools attended and relevant achievements.
