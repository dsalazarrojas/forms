# Field Trip Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record payment information for field trips, allowing the organization to track and manage payments made for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form with all required information**, including:
	* Trip Details (page 1)
	* Payment Method (page 3)
	* Payment Status (page 9)
2. **Enter any additional information if needed**, such as:
	* Contact Info (page 2)
	* Payment Amount (page 3)
	* Trip Date and Start/End Time (page 6)
	* Student Count (page 5)
3. **Select the correct payment method and status** from the options provided in the Payment Method and Payment Status fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the trip.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name and any relevant contact information.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount for the trip.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required):
	* Select the method used to make the payment (Bank Transfer, Cash, or Credit Card).
* **Student Count** (`student_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of students attending the trip.
* **Trip Date** (`trip_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the trip.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the trip.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the trip.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required):
	* Select the payment status (Pending, Paid, or Unpaid).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the payment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure correct processing of the payment.
* Select the correct payment method and status to reflect the actual payment details.
* Enter any additional comments or notes to help with payment processing and communication.
* Contact the organization if you have any questions or need help completing the form.
