# Client Trip Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients about their trip experiences, allowing us to identify areas of improvement and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section according to your honest experience with the trip. Please answer each question with the corresponding scale or option provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, `required`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address as associated with your booking.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, `optional`): If you'd like to provide your phone number for further contact.
* **Destination(s) visited** (`destination`, `text`, `required`): Enter the destinations you visited during your trip.
* **Trip start date** (`trip_start_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the start date of your trip in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Trip end date** (`trip_end_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the end date of your trip in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Your Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your travel agent or tour operator.
* **Satisfaction with trip planning process** (`planning_satisfaction`, `select_one`, `required`):
	+ **Very Dissatisfied**, **Dissatisfied**, **Neutral**, **Satisfied**, or **Very Satisfied**: How satisfied were you with our trip planning process?
* **Accuracy of bookings and reservations** (`booking_accuracy`, `select_one`, `required`):
	+ **Poor**, **Fair**, **Good**, **Very Good**, or **Excellent**: How would you rate the accuracy of our bookings and reservations?
* **Fairness of pricing and value** (`price_fairness`, `select_one`, `required`):
	+ **Very Unfair**, **Unfair**, **Fair**, **Very Fair**, or **Excellent Value**: How would you rate the fairness of our pricing and value?
* **Communication from travel agent** (`communication_quality`, `select_one`, `required`):
	+ **Poor**, **Fair**, **Good**, **Very Good**, or **Excellent**: How would you rate the communication from our travel agents?
* **Quality of accommodations** (`accommodations`, `select_one`, `required`):
	+ **Poor**, **Fair**, **Good**, **Very Good**, or **Excellent**: How would you rate the quality of accommodations?
* **Transportation arrangements** (`transportation`, `select_one`, `required`):
	+ **Poor**, **Fair**, **Good**, **Very Good**, or **Excellent**: How would you rate the quality of transportation arrangements?
* **Quality of activities and tours** (`activities`, `select_one`, `optional`):
	+ **Poor**, **Fair**, **Good**, **Very Good**, or **Excellent**: How would you rate the quality of activities and tours, if applicable.
* **Overall trip experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, `required`):
	+ **Very Dissatisfied**, **Dissatisfied**, **Neutral**, **Satisfied**, or **Very Satisfied**: How would you rate your overall trip experience?
* **What were the highlights of your trip?** (`highlights`, `text`, `optional`): Share any standout moments or experiences from your trip.
* **What could be improved?** (`improvements`, `text`, `optional`): Suggest any areas that could be improved for future trips.
* **Would you recommend this travel agent?** (`recommendation`, `select_one`, `required`):
	+ **Definitely Not**, **Probably Not**, **Unsure**, **Probably Yes**, or **Definitely Yes**: Would you recommend our travel agency to others?
