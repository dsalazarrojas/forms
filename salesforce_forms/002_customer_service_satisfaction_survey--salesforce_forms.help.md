# Customer Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Satisfaction Survey is a tool to measure customer satisfaction with our customer service. It's essential to complete this form to provide us with valuable feedback on how we can improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, considering your recent experience with our customer service.
2. Select the most suitable options for each question, based on your feelings and perceptions.
3. You can add any additional comments or thoughts in the "What did you like or dislike about your experience?" field.
4. Submit the form to help us improve our service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Service Satisfaction Survey** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of the form, which is not a field to fill but rather a heading to guide you through the form.
* **How would you rate your overall experience with our customer service?** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with our customer service using the options: very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, or not satisfied.
* **How would you rate your overall experience with our customer service?** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with our customer service using the options: very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, or not satisfied. This question is similar to question 2, but allows for more nuanced feedback.
* **How did you contact our customer service?** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the methods you used to contact our customer service, such as phone call, email, chat, in person, or in store.
* **How did you find our service?** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your experience with our service, using the options: helpful, somewhat helpful, not helpful, very helpful, or not helpful.
* **What did you like or dislike about your experience?** (`6`, `note`, required: false): Share any specific thoughts, likes, or dislikes about your experience with our customer service. Please be as detailed as possible.

## Tips
- Be honest and genuine in your responses.
- Answer the questions based on your actual experience, rather than speculation.
- If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide them in the "What did you like or dislike about your experience?" field.
