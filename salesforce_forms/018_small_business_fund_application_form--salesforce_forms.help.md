# small_business_fund_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Small Business Fund Application Form is designed for small business owners or representatives to apply for funding. This form gathers essential information about the business and project details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your business name as it appears on your business cards or licenses.
2. Provide your business phone number.
3. Enter your business email address.
4. Answer yes or no to indicate if you are applying for a specific category of funding.
5. Enter the amount of grant money you are requesting.
6. Briefly describe the terms of the grant.
7. Provide a detailed description of your project.
8. Enter the budget for your project.
9. Select the start and end dates for your project.
10. Enter your bank's name.
11. Enter your business bank account number.
12. Enter your bank routing number.
13. Enter the name of the account holder.
14. Select the type of account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Small Business Fund Application** (`id: 1`): This is the title of the form.
* **Business Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your business name as it appears on your business cards or licenses.
* **Address** (`id: 3`): Enter your business address.
* **Business Phone** (`id: 4`): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Email** (`id: 5`): Enter your business email address.
* **Category** (`id: 6`): Check "Yes" if you are applying for a specific category of funding.
* **Grant Amount** (`id: 7`): Enter the amount of grant money you are requesting.
* **Grant Terms** (`id: 8`): Briefly describe the terms of the grant.
* **Project Description** (`id: 9`): Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Project Budget** (`id: 10`): Enter the budget for your project.
* **Project Start Date** (`id: 11`): Select the start date for your project.
* **Project End Date** (`id: 12`): Select the end date for your project.
* **Bank Name** (`id: 13`): Enter your bank's name.
* **Bank Account Number** (`id: 14`): Enter your business bank account number.
* **Bank Routing Number** (`id: 15`): Enter your bank routing number.
* **Account Holder** (`id: 16`): Enter the name of the account holder.
* **Account Type** (`id: 17`): Select the type of account.
