# Promotion Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Promotion Entry Form is used to collect information about a promotion from customers, including their contact details, preferences, and agreement on specific terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name**, which is required to identify you as the person entering information about the promotion.
2. Enter your **Email** address, which is used to contact you regarding the promotion.
3. Provide your **Phone** number, which is optional but helps us stay in touch.
4. Select your preferred **Preferred Channel**, as it will help us tailor our communication and services to your needs.
5. Briefly describe the **Service Value** of the promotion, which is optional.
6. Choose your **Agreement** with the promotion terms, as it will help us understand your commitment.
7. Enter any additional **Customer Message** regarding the promotion.
8. If applicable, provide your **Company Name**, **Company Phone**, and **Company Email** for business-to-business promotions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents or marketing materials.
* **Email**: Type in your active email address where you can be reached regarding the promotion.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached, this field is optional but recommended.
* **Preferred Channel**: Select one or more channels that you prefer to receive communication about the promotion. Options are:
	+ Channel A
	+ Channel B
	+ Channel C
* **Service Value**: Briefly describe the value you expect to receive from the promotion, if any.
* **Agreement**: Select which option you agree with regarding the promotion terms. Options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Customer Message**: Enter any additional message regarding the promotion.
* **Company Name**: If you are representing a company, enter your company's name.
* **Company Phone**: Enter your company's phone number, if applicable.
* **Company Email**: Enter your company's email address, if applicable.
