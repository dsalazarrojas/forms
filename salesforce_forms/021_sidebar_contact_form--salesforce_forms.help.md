# Sidebar Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect contact information from users and allow them to send a message to the company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Name" field with your name.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Type a message in the "Message" field.
4. If you're unsure about the category of your message, select one of the options in the "Category" field (e.g., "Yes", "No") or choose "Select Category" to enter a custom category.
5. If you'd like to select multiple categories, use the "Select Category" field.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text): Enter your name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Message** (text): Type a message to the company or organization.
* **Category** (select_one): Select one of the options (e.g., "Yes", "No") or choose "Select Category" to enter a custom category.
* **Select Category** (select_multiple): Select multiple categories for your message.
* **Submit** (text): Click this button to send the form.
* **Required Message** (note): This field is not actually used in the form, it's just a placeholder for other fields that might be required in the future.
* **Required Name** (note): This field is not actually used in the form, it's just a placeholder for other fields that might be required in the future.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your message is sent successfully.
* If you're unsure about a field, feel free to ask the form owner for clarification.
* If you'd like to add more fields or customize the form for your specific needs, please contact the form owner for assistance.
