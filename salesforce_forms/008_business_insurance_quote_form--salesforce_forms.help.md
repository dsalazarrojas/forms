# Business Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from businesses to provide accurate insurance quotes. Please fill out the form completely and carefully to ensure you receive the best possible coverage for your business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your business's legal name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide the primary contact person's name in the "Contact Name" field.
3. Enter the email address for quote delivery in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter the direct phone number of the primary contact in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Fill out the "Business Address" field with your business's full address.
6. Choose your primary industry type from the "Industry Type" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the number of years your business has been in operation in the "Years in Business" field.
8. Enter the approximate total number of employees in the "Number of Employees" field.
9. Enter your business's approximate annual revenue in the "Annual Revenue" field.
10. Select all necessary coverage types from the "Desired Coverage Types" dropdown menu.
11. Choose the preferred coverage limit amount from the "Desired Coverage Limits" dropdown menu.
12. Indicate your current insurance status and whether you're seeking better rates or have a policy expiring soon in the "Current Insurance Status" field.
13. If you have current coverage, enter the name of your current insurance carrier in the "Current Carrier" field.
14. If you have current coverage, enter the policy expiration date in the "Policy Expiration Date" field.
15. Enter the number of years without claims in the "Claims Free Years" field.
16. Indicate whether you need additional insured endorsements in the "Additional Insureds Required" field.
17. Choose whether you need certificates for clients in the "Certificate of Insurance Requirements" field.
18. Select when you need coverage in the "Quote Timeline" field.
19. Choose your preferred payment method in the "Payment Preference" field.
20. If desired, select other services of interest from the "Additional Services Interested In" dropdown menu.

* **Company Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your business's legal name.
* **Contact Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Provide the primary contact person's name.

* **Years in Business** (`number`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Number of Employees** (`number`, `number`, required): Enter the approximate total number of employees.
* **Annual Revenue** (`number`, `number`, required): Enter your business's approximate annual revenue.
* **Desired Coverage Types** (`select_multiple`, `text`, required): Select all necessary coverage types.
* **Desired Coverage Limits** (`select_one`, `text`, required): Choose the preferred coverage limit amount.
* **Current Insurance Status** (`select_one`, `text`, required): Indicate your current insurance status.
* **Current Carrier** (`text`, `text`, optional): If you have current coverage, enter the name of your current insurance carrier.
* **Policy Expiration Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): If you have current coverage, enter the policy expiration date.
