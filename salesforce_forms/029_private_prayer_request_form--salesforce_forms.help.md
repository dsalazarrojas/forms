# Private Prayer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Private Prayer Request Form is designed to capture personal and private prayer requests from individuals. It's meant to help provide guidance and support to those who have submitted these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and honestly.
2. Ensure that you're filling out the form for a genuine prayer request and not for any other purpose.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prayer Request Form** (`prayer_request_form`, text, required): This is the main section where you can type out your prayer request. Please be as specific and detailed as possible.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): This field is required for us to know who is making the prayer request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): We need your email address to contact you if needed for further information.
* **Prayer Request** (`prayer_request`, text, required): Describe your prayer request in this field. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Request Date** (`prayer_request_date`, date, required): Please provide the date when you would like us to pray for you.
* **Request Details** (`prayer_request_details`, text, required): Add any additional details that you feel would be helpful for us to know about your prayer request.
* **Blessing Requested** (`prayer_request_blessing`, select_multiple, required): We will use this to know what kind of blessing you are requesting.
* **Requester Comments** (`prayer_requester_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information to share with us, please use this field.
* **Requester Blessings** (`prayer_requester_blessing`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to track any blessings or support you have received.
* **Requester Email** (`prayer_requester_email`, email, optional): This field is not required but can be used for internal tracking purposes.
* **Requester Phone** (`prayer_requester_phone`, text, optional): This field is not required but can be used for internal tracking purposes.
* **Requester Name** (`prayer_requester_name`, text, optional): This field is not required but can be used for internal tracking purposes.

## Tips
* Please be honest and genuine in filling out this form.
* Make sure to proofread your form before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
