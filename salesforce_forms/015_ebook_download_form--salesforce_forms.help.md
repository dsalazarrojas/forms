# Ebook Prophets Download Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ebook Prophets Download Form is designed to facilitate ebook downloads from the company's website. The form collects customer information for verification and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the type of ebook you would like to download from the "Ebooks" field.
5. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
6. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm Phone" field.
7. Confirm the type of ebook you selected in the "Confirm Ebooks" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ebook Prophets** (`ebook_prophets_download_form_1`): Enter the title of the ebook you are interested in downloading.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for verification purposes.
* **Ebooks** (`ebook_type`): Select the type of ebook you would like to download.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`): Confirm the email address you entered earlier.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`): Confirm the phone number you entered earlier.
* **Confirm Ebooks** (`confirm_ebook_type`): Confirm the type of ebook you selected earlier. 

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly. Some fields are not required but are still necessary for the form to be submitted properly. Make sure to confirm your email and phone number to complete the form.
