# Social Media Content Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Content Request Form is designed to capture the details of social media content requests from various content requesters. The form is used to gather information about the requested content, including type, location, and publishing schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Request Details** field with a brief description of the content you are requesting.
2. Select the **Content Type** you are requesting (Video, Image, or Post).
3. Choose the **Content Location** where you want the content to be published (Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram).
4. Select the **Publishing Schedule** (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
5. If necessary, provide **Additional Details** about your request.
6. Fill in your **Content Requester Information**:
	* Enter your email address in the **Content Requester Email** field.
	* Enter your phone number in the **Content Requester Phone** field.
	* Enter a brief note about yourself in the **Content Requester Notes** field.
7. Specify the **Request Deadline** and **Review Deadline** if applicable.
8. If required, select the **Approval Status** (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
9. Fill in the **Reviewer Information**:
	* Enter the name of the reviewer in the **Review Notes** field.
	* Enter notes about the review process in the **Review Notes** field.
10. Finally, select the **Request Status** (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Details**: A brief description of the content you are requesting.
* **Content Requester**: A unique identifier for the content requester.
* **Content Type**: The type of content you are requesting (Video, Image, or Post).
* **Content Description**: A brief description of the content you are requesting.
* **Content Location**: The location where you want the content to be published (Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram).
* **Content Publishing Schedule**: The frequency at which you want the content to be published (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Additional Details**: Optional field for providing more details about your request.
* **Content Request Date**: The date when you requested the content.
* **Content Request Time**: The time when you requested the content.
* **Content Requester Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Content Requester Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Content Requester Notes**: A brief note about yourself.
* **Request Deadline**: The deadline for the request.
* **Review Deadline**: The deadline for the review.
* **Approval Required**: Whether approval is required or not.
* **Review Notes**: Notes about the review process.
* **Approval Result**: The result of the approval process.
* **Request Status**: The status of the request (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Review Name**: The name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Notes**: Notes about the review process.
* **Content Requester Job Title**: The job title of the content requester.
* **Content Requester Location**: The location of the content requester.
* **Reviewer Name**: The name of the reviewer.
