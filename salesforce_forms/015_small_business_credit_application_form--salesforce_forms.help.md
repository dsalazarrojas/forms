# small_business_credit_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from small business owners applying for a loan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about your small business.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by a star *).
3. If a field has options, select the correct option that applies to your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Small Business Details** (text): Enter a brief description of your small business, including its name and type.
* **Business Credit History** (text): Describe your business's credit history, including any past loans or credit accounts.
* **Business Finances** (text): Share your business's financial information, such as revenue and expenses.
* **Owner Details** (text): Enter your name, title, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Application Terms** (text): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the loan application.
* **Business Location** (text): Enter your business's physical address.
* **Contact Details** (text): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Business Products/Services** (select multiple): Indicate what type of products or services your business offers (Yes or No).
* **Loan Amount** (number): Enter the requested loan amount.
* **Loan Term** (time): Enter the length of time you wish to repay the loan.
* **Loan Status** (select one): Indicate whether your loan is active (in use) or inactive (not in use).
* **Date of Last Loan** (date): Enter the date of your last loan.
* **Last Loan Term** (number): Enter the length of your last loan.
* **Loan Investments** (note): Describe any investments or collateral you are offering for the loan.
* **Loan Security** (select multiple): Indicate whether your loan is secured or unsecured (Yes or No).
* **Application Date** (date): Enter the date you submitted this loan application.
* **Owner Email** (email): Enter your business's email address.
* **Owner Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Owner Address** (text): Enter your business's address.
* **Business License** (select one): Indicate whether your business has a license (Yes or No).
* **Tax Identification** (select one): Indicate whether you have a tax identification number (Yes or No).
* **Social Security Number** (select one): Indicate whether you have a social security number (Yes or No).

Note: The fields that are marked as required are essential to complete the loan application. Make sure to fill those out accurately.
