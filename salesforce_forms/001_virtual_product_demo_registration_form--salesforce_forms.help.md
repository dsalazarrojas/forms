# Virtual Product Demo Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers or prospects register for a demo of our virtual product. Please complete the form accurately to ensure a smooth demo experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Email address to help us contact you regarding demo details.
3. Enter your Phone Number for further communication.
4. Select one preferred time slot for the demo from the options provided.
5. If you wish to attend multiple demos, select all applicable time slots.
6. If you're registering for a demo on a specific Date and Time, please ensure you've selected the correct time slot in the previous step.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, which will help us address you during the demo.

### Last Name
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, which will help us address you during the demo.

### Email
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive demo details and other important updates.

### Phone Number
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication regarding demo details.

### Select One Appoinmt
*   **Select One Appoinmt** (`select_one_appointment`, select_one, required): Choose one time slot for the demo from the options 8:00 am - 9:00 am, 9:00 am - 10:00 am, or 10:00 am - 11:00 am. This will be the time slot you're registered for.

### Select Multiple Appoinmnts
*   **Select Multiple Appoinmnts** (`select_multiple_appointments`, select_multiple, required): If you wish to attend multiple demos, select all applicable time slots. Note that duplicate selections may be removed.

### Date
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Not applicable for this form as the demo schedule is time-based.

### Time
*   **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Not applicable for this form as the demo schedule is time-based.
