# Church Event Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees on their experience with church events. It will help the organizers understand what they did well and what areas need improvement for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the options that best describe your experience with the event you attended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (text): Enter your name if you're willing to share it. This will help us follow up with you if needed.
- **Email Address** (email): Enter your email address for follow-up contact if needed.
- **Which Event Did You Attend** (select one): Choose the specific event you attended from the options provided.
- **Event Date** (date): Enter the date of the event you attended.
- **Overall Event Rating** (select one): Rate the event overall on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
- **Registration Experience Rating** (select one): Rate the registration process on a scale from Excellent to Poor, or select "Did not register" if you didn't sign up.
- **Venue Rating** (select one): Rate the event location on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
- **Content Quality Rating** (select one): Rate the program content on a scale from Excellent to Poor, or select "Not applicable" if it didn't apply to the event.
- **Speaker Performance Rating** (select one): Rate the speakers or leaders on a scale from Excellent to Poor, or select "Not applicable" if it didn't apply to the event.
- **Food and Beverage Rating** (select one): Rate the food and beverages provided on a scale from Excellent to Not provided.
- **Value for Time Rating** (select one): Rate whether your time was well spent at the event on a scale from Excellent value to Waste of time.
- **What Did You Like Best** (text): Share the highlights or best parts of the event.
- **What Could Be Improved** (text): Provide suggestions on what could be improved for future events.
- **Would You Attend Future Events** (select one): Let us know if you would definitely, probably, or not attend future events.
- **Would You Recommend This Event** (select one): Share whether you would recommend this event to friends or family.
- **Would You Like to Volunteer at Future Events** (select one): Indicate if you would be interested in volunteering for future events.
- **Any Additional Comments** (text): Share anything else you'd like to say about the event.

Please answer these questions honestly and thoroughly to help us better understand your experience and improve future church events. Thank you!
