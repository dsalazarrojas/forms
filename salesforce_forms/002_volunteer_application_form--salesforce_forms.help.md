## Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

The Volunteer Application Form is designed to gather essential information from individuals who wish to volunteer for a particular role within the organization. This form helps us understand your background, experience, and availability to match you with suitable volunteer opportunities.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form carefully, making sure to provide accurate and truthful information.
2.  Enter your details in the required fields (marked as required).
3.  You can select multiple options for fields that allow multiple selections.
4.  If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

• **First Name** (`volunteer_application_form_first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
• **Last Name** (`volunteer_application_form_last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
• **Email** (`volunteer_application_form_email`, email, optional): If you have a valid email address, please enter it. This will help us contact you with any updates or queries.
• **Phone** (`volunteer_application_form_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you, if needed.
• **Date of Birth** (`volunteer_application_form_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth to help us understand your age and experience.
• **Employment Status** (`volunteer_application_form_employment_status`, select_multiple, required): Select your current employment status from the options provided. This helps us understand your work schedule and commitments.
• **Employment Start Date** (`volunteer_application_form_employment_start_date`, date, optional): If you have been employed, enter your start date to help us understand your work history.
• **Employment End Date** (`volunteer_application_form_employment_end_date`, date, optional): If you have been employed, enter your end date to help us understand your work history.
• **Employment Duration** (`volunteer_application_form_employment_duration`, number, optional): If you have been employed, enter the duration of your employment to help us understand your work history.
• **Education** (`volunteer_application_form_education`, select_one, optional): Select your highest level of education completed.
• **Work Experience** (`volunteer_application_form_work_experience`, text, optional): Please describe your work experience, including any previous roles or positions held.
• **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_application_form_volunteer_experience`, text, optional): Describe any volunteer experience you may have, including any relevant roles or positions held.
• **Available Days** (`volunteer_application_form_available_days`, select_multiple, required): Select the days you are available to volunteer.
• **Available Time** (`volunteer_application_form_available_time`, time, required): Enter the time you are available to volunteer.
• **Skills** (`volunteer_application_form_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select any skills or areas of expertise you possess that would be beneficial for the volunteer role.
• **Reference** (`volunteer_application_form_reference`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of a professional or personal reference.
• **Reference Relationship** (`volunteer_application_form_reference_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select how you know the reference person.
• **Reference Contact** (`volunteer_application_form_reference_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the reference person.
• **Signature** (`volunteer_application_form_signature`, note, optional): If you wish to sign a physical copy of this form, please print your name and signature in the space provided.
• **Terms and Conditions** (`volunteer_application_form_terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Please select the option that indicates you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
• **Acknowledgement** (`volunteer_application_form_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Please select the option that indicates you understand the role you are applying for is subject to a satisfactory police check.
• **Additional Comments** (`volunteer_application_form_additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information you would like to share, please enter them here.
• **English Proficiency** (`volunteer_application_form_english_proficiency`, select_one, required): Select your level of English proficiency.
• **English Proficiency Reason** (`volunteer_application_form_english_proficiency_reason`, text, optional): If you selected 'Satisfactory', please describe your reason for selecting this option.
 
This guide will help you complete the form with confidence and accuracy. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
