# Visitor Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Log Form is a mandatory form for visitors to provide their personal and contact information, as well as details about their visit, such as purpose, arrival and departure times, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field, if you have one.
3. Provide your phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select the Purpose of Visit from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter your Arrival Time and Departure Time in the corresponding fields. Please note that these fields are required.
6. If you have any comments or feedback, please enter them in the Visitor Comments field.
7. Select the Staff Present and Visitor Type from the dropdown menus.
8. If you are visiting as part of a company or department, select the corresponding option from the Department dropdown menu.
9. Enter your Date of Visit in the Date field.
10. Enter your Company and Job Title in the corresponding fields.
11. Enter the name of the Contact Person, if applicable.
12. If you want to sign in as a visitor, you can enter your name and signature in the Visitor Sign In field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you have one.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, select_one, optional): Choose the reason for your visit from the dropdown menu.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required): Enter the time you arrived at the office.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to leave the office.
* **Visitor Comments** (`visitor_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have about your visit.
* **Staff Present** (`staff_present`, select_multiple, optional): Select if any staff members are present with you.
* **Visitor Type** (`visitor_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of visitor you are.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): If you are visiting as part of a company or department, select the corresponding option.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Visitor Sign In** (`visitor_sign_in`, text, optional): If you want to sign in as a visitor, enter your name and signature.
