# testing_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The testing_appointment_form is a form used to collect information from patients for testing appointments. This form is essential for scheduling and preparing for these appointments, ensuring that all necessary details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
4. Select your disease from the Disease dropdown menu.
5. Enter your schedule date in the Schedule field in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
6. Provide your Doctor Contact information in the Doctor Contact field.
7. Add any additional information about the patient in the Patient Info Note field (optional).
8. Add any additional information about the doctor contact in the Doctor Contact Note field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
- **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
- **Disease** (select_one, required): Select the disease you are seeking testing for from the dropdown menu.
- **Schedule** (date, required): Enter the date you are available to visit the doctor in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
- **Doctor Contact** (text, required): Enter your doctor's name and contact information.
- **Patient Info Note** (note, optional): Add any additional information about the patient you'd like to share with the doctor.
- **Doctor Contact Note** (note, optional): Add any additional information about the doctor contact you'd like to share with the healthcare team.
