# Event RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event RSVP Form is designed to gather information from event attendees, allowing them to confirm their participation, provide their contact details, and optionally add a message or notes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the contact details section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the Event section, enter the event name, date, time, and any additional details you'd like to share.
3. Choose whether you are attending the event by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the Attending dropdown menu.
4. If applicable, select a date and time for your RSVP to ensure you are accounted for.
5. Add any additional notes or messages in the Message field.
6. Finally, confirm your RSVP by checking the "Confirm" checkbox.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: Enter your name and any other contact information as requested.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Event**: Enter the name of the event, and any additional details that will help us plan.
* **Attending**: Choose whether you are attending the event by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **RSVP Date**: If you are attending, please select the date of the event.
* **RSVP Time**: If you are attending, please select the time of the event.
* **Message**: Add any additional comments or notes about the event.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that you have filled out the form correctly.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any other relevant information about your participation.
* **Event Details 2**: Enter any additional information about the event.
* **Event Time 2**: Select the time of the event (this may not be necessary if you already selected the original Event Time field).
* **Event Date 2**: Select the date of the event (this may not be necessary if you already selected the original Event Date field).
