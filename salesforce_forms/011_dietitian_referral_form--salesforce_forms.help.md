# Dietitian Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dietitian Referral Form is designed to provide a structured approach to making referrals to dietitians. This form will guide you through a series of questions to help ensure that you provide the necessary information for a referral.
## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, simply follow the steps below:
- Fill in the required fields (indicated by an *) with the relevant information.
- Select the correct options from the dropdown menus as needed.
- Enter any additional comments or notes in the Referral Reason field.
- Finally, submit the form to send the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information (1)** (`text`, *optional*): Enter the client's basic information, such as their name and ID number (if applicable).
- **Contact Information (2)** (`text`, *optional*): Enter the contact person's name and phone number (or email) for follow-up purposes.
- **Referral Source (3)** (`select_one`, *optional*): Select the source of the referral, such as a doctor or another healthcare professional.
- **Referral Recommender (4)** (`select_multiple`, *optional*): Select up to three dietitian recommendations.
- **Contact Phone (5)** (`text`, *optional*): Enter the contact phone number for the person providing the referral.
- **Contact E-mail (6)** (`email`, *optional*): Enter a valid email address for the person providing the referral.
- **Referral Reason (7)** (`note`, *optional*): Enter any additional comments or notes for the referral.
- **Referral Message (8)** (`text`, *optional*): Enter any additional information you'd like to include with the referral.
- **Referral Date (9)** (`date`, *optional*): Enter the date the referral is being made.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct referral source and recommender(s) for accurate referral processing.
- Ensure that the contact email address is valid and up-to-date.
- Use the referral reason field to provide any additional context or comments about the referral.
- Submit the form with all required fields completed to ensure successful referral processing.
