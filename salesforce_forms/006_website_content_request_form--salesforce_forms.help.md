# website_content_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting website content updates. It's meant for internal stakeholders to submit their requests for website content changes. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of the requested update.
2. Enter your name.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
5. Enter your preferred content.
6. Select the category of the requested update.
7. Choose the status of the requested update.
8. Select a date for the requested update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **website_content_request** (Title, text, required: false)
Enter a brief description of the requested update.

### 2. **name** (Name, text, required: false)
Enter your name to help us contact you regarding your request.

### 3. **email** (Email, email, required: false)
Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.

### 4. **phone** (Phone, text, required: false)
If you would like us to contact you via phone, enter your phone number here.

### 5. **content** (Content, text, required: false)
Enter the specific content you would like to update or add to the website.

### 6. **category** (Category, select_one, required: false)
Select the category for your requested update.

### 7. **status** (Status, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the status of your requested update.

### 8. **date** (Date, date, required: false)
Select a date for your requested update.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when providing your requested content update.
* Ensure you select the correct category for your update.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* Select the correct status for your update.
* Choose a realistic date for your update.
