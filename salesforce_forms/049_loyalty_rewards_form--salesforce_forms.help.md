# Loyalty Rewards Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Loyalty Rewards Form" is used to collect information from customers who participate in a loyalty rewards program. This form helps track customer data and determines reward eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name** (`form_1`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
2. **Last Name** (`form_2`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
3. **Email** (`form_3`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
4. **Phone** (`form_4`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
5. **Loyalty Points** (`form_5`, number, optional): Enter your current loyalty points.
6. **Reward Type** (`reward_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of reward you would like to receive. Options are:
	* Loyalty Voucher
	* Free Product
	* Discount
7. **Reward Amount** (`reward_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the reward you are eligible for.
8. **Store Visited Count** (`store_visited_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of times you have visited our store.
9. **Store Visited Count Hint** (`store_visited_count_hint`, note, optional): This field only counts visits within a certain period of time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name to help us identify you in our loyalty program.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name to help us identify you in our loyalty program.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your rewards.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you with any updates or special offers.
* **Loyalty Points**: If you have loyalty points, enter the current amount to help us track your progress.
* **Reward Type**: Select the type of reward you would like to receive. If you are not sure, just select one of the options.
* **Reward Amount**: Enter the amount of the reward you are eligible for. This will be determined based on your loyalty points.
* **Store Visited Count**: Enter the number of times you have visited our store. This helps us track your progress in our loyalty program.
* **Store Visited Count Hint**: This is a note to remind you that this field only counts visits within a certain period of time.
