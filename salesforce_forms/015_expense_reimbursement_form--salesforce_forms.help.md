# Expense Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Reimbursement Form is used to track and manage employee expenses within an organization. It helps collect and verify essential details related to employee expenses, including date, description, amount, category, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company address, employee name, date of expense, and expense description.
2. Choose the correct expense category from the provided options.
3. Enter the expense amount, if applicable.
4. Select the expense status, which can be either "Option 1" or "pending".
5. Enter other optional fields as required or desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Address** (Company Address, text, required): Enter the address of the company where the expense was incurred.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter the name of the employee who incurred the expense.
* **Date of Expense** (Date of Expense, date, required): Enter the date when the expense was incurred.
* **Expense Description** (Expense Description, text, required): Provide a brief description of the expense.
* **Expense Amount** (Expense Amount, number, required): Enter the total expense amount.
* **Expense Category** (Expense Category, select_one, required): Choose the category of the expense from the provided options.
* **Submitted by** (Submitted by, text, required): Enter the name or email of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted on** (Submitted on, date, required): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Approved by** (Approved by, text, required): Enter the name or email of the person approving the form.
* **Approved on** (Approved on, date, required): Enter the date when the form was approved.
* **Denied by** (Denied by, text, required): Enter the name or email of the person denying the form.
* **Denied on** (Denied on, date, required): Enter the date when the form was denied.
* **Expense Status** (Expense Status, select_one, required): Choose the status of the expense from the provided options.
* **Expense ID** (Expense ID, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the expense.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, text, required): Enter the unique ID of the employee who incurred the expense.
* **Submitted By Email** (Submitted By Email, email, required): Enter the email of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted On Date** (Submitted On Date, date, required): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Approved By Email** (Approved By Email, email, required): Enter the email of the person approving the form.
* **Approved On Date** (Approved On Date, date, required): Enter the date when the form was approved.
* **Denied By Email** (Denied By Email, email, required): Enter the email of the person denying the form.
* **Denied On Date** (Denied On Date, date, required): Enter the date when the form was denied.
* **Expense Type** (Expense Type, select_one, required): Choose the type of expense from the provided options.
* **Expense Reimbursement Amount** (Expense Reimbursement Amount, number, required): Enter the total expense reimbursement amount.
* **Employee Salary** (Employee Salary, number, required): Enter the employee's salary.
* **Employee Salary 2** (Employee Salary 2, number, required): Enter the employee's second salary.

Please note that some fields may have specific requirements or additional instructions depending on the organization's policies and procedures. If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
