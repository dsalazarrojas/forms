# medical_volunteer_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Volunteer Application form is designed to collect information about medical volunteers. It is used to gather contact and demographic information, medical license status, training, availability, and other relevant details from interested volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "Required" in red).
2. Enter your contact information (Emergency Contact, Name, Email, Medical License, Medical Volunteer ID, and Medical Volunteer Date).
3. Check your availability and select your available shifts.
4. Confirm your medical fields training and emergency medical certification.
5. Choose your volunteer roles and groups.
6. Select your availability and volunteer status.
7. Enter your available hours and shifts information (Shift Start, Shift End, Shift Length, and End Date).
8. Confirm your volunteer termination status and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for emergency contacts.
* **Medical License**: Check if you hold a medical license.
	+ 'Yes' means you hold a medical license.
	+ 'No' means you do not hold a medical license.
* **Medical Fields Training**: Check if you have received medical fields training.
	+ 'Yes' means you have received medical fields training.
	+ 'No' means you have not received medical fields training.
* **Available Shifts**: Select the shifts you are available to work.
* **Emergency Medical Certification**: Check if you hold an emergency medical certification.
	+ 'Yes' means you hold an emergency medical certification.
	+ 'No' means you do not hold an emergency medical certification.
* **Available Shifts**: Select the shifts you are available to work.
* **Shift Start**: Enter the start time of your shift.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you will end your shift.
* **Availability Ending**: Enter the time you will end your availability.
* **Available Hours**: Enter the number of hours you are available to work per shift.
* **Volunteer Role**: Check the roles you are interested in volunteering for.
* **Volunteer Group**: Check the groups you are interested in volunteering with.
* **Volunteer Status**: Check your current volunteer status.
	+ 'Active' means you are currently active as a volunteer.
	+ 'Inactive' means you are not currently active as a volunteer.
* **Medical Volunteer ID**: Enter your Medical Volunteer ID number.
* **Medical Volunteer Date**: Enter the date you started volunteering.
* **Shifts Ending**: Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Available Ending**: Enter the time you will end your availability.
* **Shift Length**: Enter the length of your shift.
* **Volunteer Termination**: Check if you have been terminated as a volunteer.
	+ 'Yes' means you have been terminated as a volunteer.
	+ 'No' means you have not been terminated as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Termination Date**: Enter the date of your volunteer termination.
* **Shift Start**: Enter the start time of your shift.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you will end your availability.
* **Availability Ending**: Enter the time you will end your availability.
* **Available Ending**: Enter the time you will end your availability.
