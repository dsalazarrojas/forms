# Easter Giveaway Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Giveaway Form is designed to collect information from users about themselves for the purpose of participating in an Easter giveaway. This form helps us understand who is eligible for the giveaway and provides necessary details for further communication and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Input your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Provide your date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
5. Select the **Select One** option that best describes you.
6. Choose multiple options from the **Select Multiple** field.
7. Add any additional comments or information in the **Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (text, optional)**: Enter your name for us to associate with your entry.
* **Email (email, optional)**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: Input your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date of Birth (date, optional)**: Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Time (time, optional)**: Select your favorite time of day for the Easter giveaway.
* **Select One (select_one, optional)**: Select one option that best describes you.
* **Select Multiple (select_multiple, optional)**: Choose all options that apply to you.
* **Note (note, optional)**: Add any additional comments or information about yourself.
* **Confirm Email (email, optional)**: Re-enter your email address for verification purposes.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy before confirming.
* Be honest and accurate when selecting options.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
