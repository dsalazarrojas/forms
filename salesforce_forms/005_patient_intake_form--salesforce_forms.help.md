# Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient intake form is a comprehensive form designed to collect relevant information from patients, including contact details, medical history, appointment information, and other necessary data for patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and completely for each patient.
2. Ensure that you are providing the most up-to-date information for the patient.
3. Review each section carefully to avoid missing any important details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for entering the patient's contact details, such as name, address, and phone number.
* **Patient Information** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for entering the patient's basic information, such as name and date of birth.
* **Medical History** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for entering the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses or conditions.
* **Appointment Information** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for entering the appointment details, including date, time, and location.
* **Medical History Details** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for entering a detailed medical history for the patient.
* **Allergies** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for listing any allergies the patient has.
* **Medications** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for listing any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Patient Consent** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for the patient to review and sign off on the consent form.
* **HIPAA** (`id: 9`, `type: select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the HIPAA option for patient confidentiality.
* **Contact Method** (`id: 10`, `type: select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the contact method for the patient.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for entering the patient's phone number.
* **Email** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for entering the patient's email address.
* **Appointment Scheduling** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for scheduling an appointment with the patient.
* **Insurance** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for entering the patient's insurance information.
* **Payment Method** (`id: 15`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): This field is for selecting the payment method.
* **Payment Amount** (`id: 16`, `type: number`, required: false): This field is for entering the payment amount.
* **Payment Terms** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for entering payment terms.
* **Payment Plan** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for entering payment plan details.
* **Medical Group** (`id: 19`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): This field is for selecting the medical group.
* **Medical Board** (`id: 20`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): This field is for selecting the medical board.
* **Patient Notes** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for entering any notes about the patient.
* **Staff Notes** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for entering any staff notes about the patient.
* **Follow-up** (`id: 23`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for scheduling a follow-up appointment with the patient.
* **Signature** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is for the patient to sign the form.
* **Date** (`id: 25`, `type: date`, required: false): This field is for entering the date of the appointment.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are providing the most up-to-date and accurate information for the patient.
* Review each section carefully to avoid missing any important details.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the relevant medical staff for clarification.
