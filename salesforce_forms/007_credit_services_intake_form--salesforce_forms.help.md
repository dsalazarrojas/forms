## Field-by-Field Explanation
## Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, optional): Enter your name, and a valid contact email to ensure we get in touch with you for any further information.

## Contact Information
* **Contact Person** (contact_person, text, optional): Enter the name of the person you are authorizing this credit service request.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for any updates.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Contact Address** (contact_address, text, optional): Enter your full address for us to reach you.

## Credit Details
* **What type of credit are you applying for** (credit_type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of credit you're interested in applying for (loan, credit card, or investment).
* **Social Security Number** (social_security_number, text, optional): Enter your social security number for verification purposes.
* **Annual Income** (annual_income, number, optional): Enter your annual income for credit assessment.
* **Employment Status** (employment_status, select_one, optional): Select your employment status (employed, self-employed, or unemployed).
* **Credit Score** (credit_score, number, optional): Enter your credit score for better assessment.
* **Loan Amount** (loan_amount, number, optional): Enter the loan amount you're interested in.

## Payment Details
* **Loan Term** (loan_term, number, optional): Enter the loan term for the credit service.
* **Interest Rate** (interest_rate, number, optional): Enter the interest rate for the credit service.
* **Annual Interest** (annual_interest, number, optional): Enter the annual interest for the credit service.
* **Fees** (fees, number, optional): Enter any applicable fees for the credit service.
* **Payment Schedule** (payment_schedule, select_multiple, optional): Choose the payment schedule (monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually).
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, optional): Select the payment method (check, online banking, phone, or in-person).

## Employment Details
* **Employment Length** (employment_length, number, optional): Enter the length of your employment.
* **Employment Status** (employment_status, select_one, optional): Select your employment status (employed, self-employed, or unemployed).
* **Employment Type** (employment_type, select_one, optional): Select your employment type (full-time, part-time, or contract).
* **Income Frequency** (income_frequency, select_one, optional): Select your income frequency (monthly, annually, or quarterly).
* **Income Verification** (income_verification, select_one, optional): Select whether your income is verifiable or not.
* **Tax ID Number** (tax_id_number, text, optional): Enter your tax ID number.
* **Other Credit Inquiries** (other_credit_inquiries, text, optional): Enter any other credit inquiries.

## Additional Details
