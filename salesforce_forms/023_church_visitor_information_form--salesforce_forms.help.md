# Church Visitor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Visitor Information Form is designed to collect information from visitors about their personal details, visit experience, interests, and communication preferences. This form will help us understand how to serve and engage with our community more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields (marked as required).
2. For optional fields, provide as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to answer each question truthfully and accurately.
4. If you are visiting with family or group, fill out the form for each person separately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`visitor_name`, text, required): This is your full name, including any middle names or titles (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.).
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your active email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you are comfortable sharing your date of birth, please enter it here.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter your street address where you can be contacted or visited.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you can be contacted or visited.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter the state or province where you can be contacted or visited.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code or ZIP where you can be contacted or visited.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): If you are visiting from outside the country, please enter your country of origin.
* **Date of Visit** (`visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Service or Event Attended** (`service_attended`, text, optional): If you attended a specific service or event, please describe it here.
* **Is This Your First Time Visiting** (`first_time_visitor`, select_one, required): Indicate whether this is your first time visiting our church.
* **How Did You Hear About Our Church** (`visitor_source`, select_one, required): Choose how you heard about our church.
* **Number of Family Members Visiting** (`family_members_visiting`, number, optional): If you are visiting with family, please specify the number of family members with you.
* **Number of Children with You** (`children_count`, number, optional): If you are visiting with children, please specify the number of children with you.
* **Ages of Children** (`children_ages`, text, optional): If you are visiting with children, please describe their ages here.
* **Prior Church Experience** (`church_background`, select_one, optional): If you have prior church experience, choose how you would describe it.
* **Spiritual Interests** (`spiritual_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the spiritual interests that apply to you.
* **Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are interested in volunteering.
* **Skills or Talents to Offer** (`volunteer_skills`, text, optional): If you are interested in volunteering, describe your skills or talents here.
* **May We Contact You with Information About Our Church** (`contact_permission`, select_one, required): Indicate whether we can contact you with information about our church.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred contact method (email, phone, text message, or mail).
* **Sign Up for Our Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you would like to sign up for our newsletter.
* **What Was Your First Impression of Our Church** (`first_impression`, text, optional): If you would like to share your first impression of our church, describe it here.
