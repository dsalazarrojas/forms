# New Patient Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for patients to register themselves with the medical practice, providing essential details such as their name, contact information, and medical practice affiliation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone.
2. Select your Date of Birth.
3. Choose your Medical Practice from the dropdown list.
4. Provide any additional Patient Details in the space provided.
5. Check the box to indicate your consent to terms and conditions.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Practice** (Medical Practice, select_one, required): Select the medical practice you are associated with.
* **Patient Details** (Patient Details, note, required): Enter any additional details about yourself as a patient.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Terms and Conditions, select_multiple, required): Check the box to indicate you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Consent** (Consent, select_multiple, required): Check the box to indicate your consent to the terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Click this button to submit your registration.
* **Cancel** (Cancel, text, required): Click this button to cancel your registration and start again.
