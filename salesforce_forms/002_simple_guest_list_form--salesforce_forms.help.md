# Simple Guest List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and record guest attendance and details for an event. It includes fields to input event name, guest names, guest contact information, and event date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter Event Name**: Type in the name of the event, such as a meeting, party, or presentation.
2. **Add Guest Names**: Choose from the list of guests attending the event. This field is used to select guests who are attending. 
3. **Enter Guest E-mails**: Input a guest's email address. This field is for contact information only.
4. **Add Guest Phones**: Input a guest's phone number. This field is for contact information only.
5. **Record Guest Visits**: Choose a date and time that the guest plans to visit. This field is used to track guest visit plans.
6. **Add Guest Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the guest. This field is optional.
7. **Record Event Details**: Enter the date and time of the event. This field is used to keep track of the event schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name (Text, Required/Optional: Optional)**: Enter the name of the event, such as a meeting, party, or presentation.
* **Guest Names (Select Multiple, Required/Optional: Required)**: Choose from the list of guests attending the event. This field is used to select guests who are attending.
* **Guest E-mails (Email, Required/Optional: Required)**: Input a guest's email address. This field is for contact information only.
* **Guest Phones (Text, Required/Optional: Required)**: Input a guest's phone number. This field is for contact information only.
* **Guest Dates (Date, Required/Optional: Required)**: Choose a date that the guest plans to visit. This field is used to track guest visit plans.
* **Guest Phones (Text, Required/Optional: Optional)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the guest. This field is optional.
* **Event Date (Date, Required/Optional: Optional)**: Enter the date of the event. This field is used to keep track of the event schedule.
* **Event Time (Time, Required/Optional: Optional)**: Enter the time of the event. This field is optional.
* **Guest Notes (Note, Required/Optional: Optional)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the guest. This field is optional.
