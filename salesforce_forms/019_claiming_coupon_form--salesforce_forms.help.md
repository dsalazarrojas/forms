# Claiming Coupon Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Claiming Coupon Form is used to claim a coupon and receive a discount on a purchase. To fill out this form, follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a valid coupon code to claim.
2. Fill out the customer information fields:
	* **Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to be contacted in case of any issues.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
3. Enter the coupon details:
	* **Coupon Code**: Enter the unique code on your coupon.
	* **Coupon Type**: Choose the type of coupon you have (e.g. Percentage discount, Fixed amount discount, etc.).
	* **Discount Amount or Percentage**: Enter the amount or percentage of the discount.
	* **Coupon Expiration Date**: Enter the date when the coupon will expire.
4. Fill out the purchase details:
	* **Date of Claim**: Enter the date you're claiming the coupon.
	* **Original Purchase Amount**: Enter the amount you paid before the discount.
	* **Product Category**: Choose the category of products the coupon applies to (e.g. Electronics, Clothing and Fashion, etc.).
	* **Items Purchased**: List the items you purchased or a description of the items.
5. Provide promotional information:
	* **Where did you find this coupon**: Choose where you found the coupon (e.g. Email newsletter, Website, Social media, etc.).
6. Confirm your payment method:
	* **Payment Method Used**: Choose the method you used to pay for the purchase (e.g. Credit card, Debit card, etc.).
7. Confirm you have read the coupon terms and conditions:
	* **I agree to the coupon terms and conditions**: Choose "Yes, I agree" to confirm you have read and understood the terms.
