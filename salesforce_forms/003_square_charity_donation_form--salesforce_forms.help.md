# Square Charity Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Charity Donation Form is designed to collect donations from customers for a charity or non-profit organization. It gathers essential information from donors to process their contributions efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Complete the "Address 1" and "Address 2" fields with your full address.
6. Enter the city and state of your address in the "City" and "State" fields.
7. Enter your Zip code in the "Zip" field.
8. Specify the amount you wish to donate in the "Amount" field.
9. Select the purpose of your donation from the list of options provided in the "Purpose" field.
10. Choose the type of donation you are making from the list of options in the "Donation Type" field.
11. Add any additional details about your donation in the "Donor Details" field.
12. Enter the name of the organization or recipient of the donation in the "Organization" field, if applicable.
13. Enter the date and time of the donation in the "Date of Donation" and "Time of Donation" fields, if required.
14. Leave any additional information or comments for the organization in the "Note" field, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Address 1**: Enter your address line 1 for delivery purposes.
* **Address 2**: Enter your address line 2 for delivery purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside or are based.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside or are based.
* **Zip**: Enter your postal zip code for delivery purposes.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Purpose**: Choose the purpose of your donation from the provided options.
* **Donation Type**: Choose the type of donation you are making from the provided options.
* **Donor Details**: Add any additional details about your donation.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization or recipient of the donation, if applicable.
* **Date of Donation**: Enter the date of the donation, if required.
* **Time of Donation**: Enter the time of the donation, if required.
* **Note**: Leave any additional information or comments for the organization, if desired.
