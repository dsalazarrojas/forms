# Youth Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather information from potential youth volunteers about their application to volunteer for a specific youth organization. This form is used to collect personal details, volunteer preferences, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Enter your first name in the **First name** field (Field 1).
3.  Enter your last name in the **Last name** field (Field 2).
4.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field (Field 3).
5.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field (Field 4).
6.  Select your Date of Birth from the calendar in the **Date of Birth** field (Field 5).
7.  Enter your zip code in the **Zip** field (Field 6).
8.  Enter your address in the **Address** field (Field 7).
9.  Enter your city name in the **City** field (Field 8).
10.  Select your state of residence from the options in the **State** field (Field 9).
11.  Select the volunteer role you're interested in from the options in the **What role do you wish to volunteer as?** field (Field 10).
12.  Enter the number of hours you can volunteer each week in the **How many hours can you volunteer each week?** field (Field 11).
13.  Select the start date you can begin volunteering from the calendar in the **When can you start volunteering?** field (Field 12).
14.  Select the end date you plan to volunteer until from the calendar in the **When do you plan to end volunteering?** field (Field 13).
15.  Enter a reference name, if applicable, in the **Please provide a reference name** field (Field 14).
16.  Enter a brief description of your volunteer experience in the **Volunteer experience** field (Field 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (Field 1): Enter your first name.
*   **Last name** (Field 2): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (Field 3): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (Field 4): Enter your phone number.
*   **Date of Birth** (Field 5): Select your Date of Birth from the calendar.
*   **Zip** (Field 6): Enter your zip code.
*   **Address** (Field 7): Enter your address.
*   **City** (Field 8): Enter your city name.
*   **State** (Field 9): Select your state of residence from the options.
*   **What role do you wish to volunteer as?** (Field 10): Select the volunteer role you're interested in from the options.
*   **How many hours can you volunteer each week?** (Field 11): Enter the number of hours you can volunteer each week.
*   **When can you start volunteering?** (Field 12): Select the start date you can begin volunteering from the calendar.
*   **When do you plan to end volunteering?** (Field 13): Select the end date you plan to volunteer until from the calendar.
*   **Please provide a reference name** (Field 14): Enter a reference name if applicable.
*   **Volunteer experience** (Field 15): Enter a brief description of your volunteer experience.

## Tips

- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely.
- Be honest when selecting your volunteer role and availability.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organization for assistance.
