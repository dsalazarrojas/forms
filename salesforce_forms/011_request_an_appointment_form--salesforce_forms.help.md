# Request An Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request An Appointment Form is used to collect information for scheduling appointments with healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **First Name** field with your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide your **Email** address for contact and appointment reminder purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone** number, if you have one (optional).
5. Select the type of appointment you are requesting from the **Appointment Type** dropdown (e.g., Consultation, Check-up, Follow-up, or Other).
6. Choose the desired **Date** for your appointment from the calendar.
7. Select the **Time** for your appointment.
8. Choose the healthcare provider you would like to see from the **Doctor** dropdown (e.g., Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, Dr. Wilson).
9. Type any additional **Message** you have for the healthcare provider in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact and appointment reminder purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of appointment you are requesting. Options include Consultation, Check-up, Follow-up, or Other.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select a date for your appointment from the calendar.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select a time for your appointment.
* **Doctor** (`doctor`, `select_one`, required): Choose the healthcare provider you would like to see.
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, required): Enter any additional information you have for the healthcare provider in this text area.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the scheduling team for clarification.
