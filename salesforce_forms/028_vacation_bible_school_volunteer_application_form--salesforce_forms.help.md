# Vacation Bible School Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Vacation Bible School Volunteer Application Form is used to collect information from potential volunteers for the Vacation Bible School program. This form is meant for individuals who wish to participate as volunteers in the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Volunteer Information" section.
2. Provide your emergency contact person's name and their contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
3. Briefly describe yourself in the "Volunteer Details" section.
4. Indicate your availability to volunteer in the "Availability" section by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. If you have volunteered before, please describe your experience with Vacation Bible School in the "Experience With Vacation Bible School" section.
6. If you have any medical conditions that may affect your ability to volunteer, please select "Yes" in the "Medical Conditions" section.
7. Finally, read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Volunteer Agreement" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information** (Text, Optional)
	+ This is where you can provide a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Contact Information** (Text, Optional)
	+ Please fill in your contact information so we can reach out to you with any further questions or details.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text, Optional)
	+ Provide the name and contact information of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Volunteer Details** (Text, Optional)
	+ Briefly describe yourself and why you're interested in volunteering.
* **Availability** (Select One, Optional)
	+ Indicate if you are available to volunteer by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Volunteer Hours** (Number, Required)
	+ Enter the number of hours you are able to volunteer per week.
* **First Time Volunteer** (Select One, Optional)
	+ If this is your first time volunteering, select "Yes".
* **Experience With Vacation Bible School** (Select One, Optional)
	+ If you have experience with Vacation Bible School, select "Yes".
* **Medical Conditions** (Select One, Optional)
	+ If you have any medical conditions that may affect your ability to volunteer, select "Yes".
* **Volunteer Agreement** (Checkbox, Required)
	+ Read and agree to the terms and conditions of volunteering.
* **I Agree** (Text, Optional)
	+ This is a placeholder field and should not be filled.
* **Agreement Text** (Text, Optional)
	+ This is where you can provide any additional comments regarding your agreement.
