# Customer Interest Profile - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Interest Profile form is used to collect essential information about customers to enhance their experience and tailor our services to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
- Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Interest Profile** (`form_title`, text, required: false): This is a brief title for the form. It's not required, but helps identify the purpose of the form.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's name in this field.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Type in the customer's email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date for the customer's visit or interaction.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Choose the time for the customer's visit or interaction.
- **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the customer.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Choose the tool assigned to the customer from the available options (chatjimmy, other, None).
- **Customer Experience** (`customer_experience`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the customer's experience (better, worse, None).
- **More Info** (`more_info`, note, required: false): Add any additional information about the customer.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If a field is not required, it's optional, but providing information can help enhance the customer experience.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field label for clarification.
