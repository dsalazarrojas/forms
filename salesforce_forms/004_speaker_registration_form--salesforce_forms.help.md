# Speaker Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Registration Form is a tool for registering speakers for events, conferences, or workshops. This form helps event organizers collect essential information about each speaker, including their contact details, presentation topic, and social media presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
4. Describe your presentation topic in the **topic** field.
5. Briefly describe your affiliation or organization in the **affiliation** field.
6. Write a brief biography in the **biography** field.
7. Upload a profile picture or image in the **image** field.
8. Provide a link to a video presentation or recording in the **video** field.
9. Add any additional audio files or recordings in the **audio** field.
10. Enter your social media profiles (e.g., Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook) in the **Socials** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Details**: Enter your basic contact information, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
* **name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed for the event.
* **email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for event-related inquiries.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for event organizers to contact you.
* **topic**: Describe the topic of your presentation or workshop.
* **affiliation**: Mention your current affiliation or organization, if any.
* **biography**: Write a brief description of your background or experience related to your presentation topic.
* **image**: Upload a profile picture or image to be displayed during the event or on marketing materials.
* **video**: Provide a link to a video recording or presentation relevant to your topic.
* **audio**: Add any additional audio files or recordings related to your presentation.
* **Socials**: Enter your social media profiles (e.g., Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook) for attendees to connect with you.
* **Facebook**: Enter your Facebook profile link, if applicable.
