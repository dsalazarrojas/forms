# gift_card_giveaway_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to enter their details for a gift card giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your personal details.
2. Select your favorite food.
3. Choose your favorite color.
4. Enter your birthday.
5. Enter your time preference.
6. Enter the gift card value (if applicable).
7. Enter a description of your favorite food.
8. Enter the name of the gift card winner.
9. Enter the recipient's name for the gift card.
10. Enter a message for the gift card recipient (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **email_address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **gift_card_details** (`gift_card_details`, text, required): Enter any gift card details (if applicable).
* **favorite_food** (`favorite_food`, select_one, required): Select your favorite food (e.g., Sushi, Pizza, Salad).
* **favorite_color** (`favorite_color`, select_multiple, required): Select your favorite color (e.g., Red, Blue, Green).
* **birthday** (`birthday`, date, required): Enter your birthday (in the format YYYY-MM-DD).
* **time** (`time`, time, required): Enter your time preference.
* **gift_card_value** (`gift_card_value`, number, required): Enter the gift card value (if applicable).
* **favorite_food_description** (`favorite_food_description`, text, required): Enter a description of your favorite food.
* **gift_card_winner** (`gift_card_winner`, text, required): Enter the name of the gift card winner.
* **gift_card_recipient** (`gift_card_recipient`, text, required): Enter the name of the gift card recipient.
* **gift_card_message** (`gift_card_message`, text, required): Enter a message for the gift card recipient.
* **gift_card_message_2** (`gift_card_message_2`, text, required): Enter an additional message for the gift card recipient.
