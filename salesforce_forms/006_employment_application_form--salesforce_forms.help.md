# Employment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Application Form is designed to collect information from job applicants about their contact details, work experience, education background, job skills, availability, and personal information. This form will help hiring managers and recruiters evaluate potential candidates for a job opening.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide clear and accurate information about yourself. Answer each section thoroughly, and make sure to fill in all fields, especially the required ones.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Contact Information (page 1)
* **Contact Information**: This is where you provide your basic contact details such as your name, email address, and phone number.

### 2. Work Experience (page 2)
* **Work Experience**: Here, please describe your previous work experience, highlighting your job roles, job titles, and the companies you've worked with.

### 3. Education History (page 2)
* **Education History**: In this section, please provide information about your educational background, including the institutions you've attended, courses you've completed, and degrees earned.

### 4. Job Skills (page 2)
* **Job Skills**: This section is for listing the skills you think are relevant to the job you are applying for, such as languages, software proficiency, or other areas of expertise.

### 5. Availability (page 2)
* **Availability**: Please indicate your availability for an interview or work schedule.

### 6. Contact Information (page 3)
* **Contact Information**: This is the second part of your contact details, providing an opportunity to add any additional information.

### 7. Work History (page 3)
* **Work History**: Describe your work history, including job roles, job titles, and companies you've worked with.

### 8. Personal Info (page 3)
* **Personal Info**: This section is for any additional personal information you want to share with the hiring manager.

### 9. Education (page 3)
* **Education**: Provide more details about your education background, such as courses taken, degrees earned, or relevant certificates.

### 10. Job History (page 3)
* **Job History**: Similar to Work History, this section is for describing your job roles, job titles, and companies worked with.

Note to the user: This form has several sections where you can provide information about yourself as a job applicant. Please answer all fields accurately and thoroughly, especially the required ones. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
