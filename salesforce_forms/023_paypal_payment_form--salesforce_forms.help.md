# paypal_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The paypal_payment_form is used to collect payment information from customers, ensuring accurate and secure payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the payment details section, providing your payment method and amount.
2. Enter your customer name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
4. Select your country of residence from the provided options.
5. Confirm your payment details by checking the 'True' option.
6. Enter a valid email address to confirm your payment details.
7. Fill in any other required fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **(1) payment_details**: This field is used to collect information about your payment method and amount.
* **(2) customer_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **(3) email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **(4) phone_number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **(5) country**: Select the country where you reside from the options provided.
* **(6) amount**: Enter the amount you wish to pay for our services or products.
* **(7) payment_method**: Select the payment method you wish to use (PayPal, Visa, or Mastercard).
* **(8) confirm_payment**: Check 'True' to confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the payment terms.
* **(9) confirm_email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached to confirm your payment details.
* **(10) confirm_payment**: This field is intended to confirm the payment details, however, it's unclear how it's related to (9), so it's best-effort explained as an option to confirm that the payment details are correct.
 

## Tips

* Double-check your payment details before submitting the form.
