# Awards Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Awards Nomination Form is used to nominate an employee, manager, or team for an award within your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, including the nominee's contact details and justification for the nomination.
2. Select the correct award category for the nomination.
3. Provide a detailed explanation of the nominee's achievements and their impact.
4. Estimate the nominee's length of contribution to the organization, if applicable.
5. Submit the form by selecting the nomination date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter the full name of the person you are nominating.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person being nominated.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the nominee's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Nominee's Full Name**: Enter the full name of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee's Current Position**: Enter the job title or role of the person being nominated.
* **Department or Area**: Enter the department or area where the person is working, if applicable.
* **Award Category**: Select the appropriate award category for the nomination from the list provided.
* **Why Should This Person Receive This Award?**: Provide a detailed explanation of why the person deserves to receive this award, including specific examples and justification.
* **Describe Measurable Impact or Results**: If applicable, provide measurable metrics or specific outcomes that demonstrate the impact of the nominee's work.
* **How Long Has This Person Contributed?**: Estimate the number of years the person has contributed to the organization.
* **Nomination Submission Date**: Select the date you are submitting the nomination, which will be recorded as the nomination date.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide detailed and specific information about the nominee's achievements and impact.
* Ensure you are not nominating someone for an award they have already received in the past year.
* Review the award categories carefully to select the most suitable one for the nomination.
* Be as specific as possible when providing justification and examples for the nomination.
