# Salesforces Account Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salesforces Account Form is used to create or update salesforce account information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button after filling out all required fields.
2. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the salesforce account owner or contact person.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the salesforce account owner or contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the salesforce account owner or contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the salesforce account owner or contact person.
* **Account Name** (`account_name`, text, optional): Enter a unique and descriptive name for the salesforce account.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the salesforce account, including any relevant details or context.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for correct formatting when entering it as an email.
* If you're not sure which field to fill out, you can leave them blank or remove them if not required.
* This form is not intended for duplicate submissions; if you need to edit existing information, please contact the salesforce account management team directly.
