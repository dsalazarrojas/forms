# Art's Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to capture contact information and details for follow-up with potential customers or interested parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you submitted your inquiry.
2. Fill out your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
3. If applicable, enter your Company and Job Title.
4. Choose the reason for your contact by selecting from the provided options.
5. Enter the Subject of your message.
6. Enter your message or inquiry.
7. If you have a preferred contact method, select it from the options provided.
8. Choose the best time to contact you.
9. Indicate the urgency of your inquiry.
10. Select how you heard about Art's work.
11. If you have any additional information, enter it in the provided text box.
12. Finally, select whether you want to receive Art's newsletter and consent to the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Date**: The date you submitted your inquiry.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for Art to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone. (Optional)
* **Company**: If you are contacting Art in a professional capacity, enter your company name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title if applicable.
* **Contact Reason**: Choose the reason for your contact from the provided options.
* **Subject**: Enter the subject of your message.
* **Message**: Enter your message or inquiry.
* **Product Interest**: Select the products or services you are interested in. (Optional)
* **Budget Range**: If applicable, select your budget range. (Optional)
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select the preferred contact method for Art to contact you.
* **Best Time to Contact**: Choose the best time for Art to contact you.
* **Urgency**: Indicate the urgency of your inquiry.
* **How Heard**: Select how you heard about Art's work.
* **Referral Source**: If you were referred by someone, enter their name or contact information. (Optional)
* **Newsletter Signup**: Select whether you want to receive Art's newsletter.
* **Privacy Consent**: Consent to Art's terms of service and privacy policy.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any other relevant information about your inquiry. (Optional)

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly to avoid delays in responding to your inquiry.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field label for clarification.
* Some fields are optional and can be filled out at your discretion.
* Please respect Art's time and respond to follow-up requests in a timely manner.
