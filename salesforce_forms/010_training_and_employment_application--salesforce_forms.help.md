# Training And Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates for training and employment purposes. It's meant to provide potential employers with a comprehensive view of the candidate's background, skills, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required sections, marked as "required".
2. Enter accurate and honest information in each section.
3. Review and confirm your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Contact Information**: 
  * Enter your name, last name, and any other contact details you'd like to share.
* **Employment History**: 
  * Briefly describe your previous work experience, including job titles, dates of employment, and a brief description of your role.
* **Skills**: 
  * Select all relevant skills you possess, such as "Yes" for "Yes" and "No" for "No".
* **Contact Address**: 
  * Enter your home or work address, as preferred.
* **Contact Email**: 
  * Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Contact Phone**: 
  * Enter a phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Resume**: 
  * Upload your resume, if available.
* **Employment Status**: 
  * Briefly describe your current employment status, such as "Employed", "Unemployed", etc.
* **Employment Type**: 
  * Select whether you're a full-time or part-time employee.
* **Job Title**: 
  * Enter the job title you're applying for, if known.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* Double-check your submission before clicking "Submit".
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for clarification.
