# Property Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information about a property from a potential buyer or seller. This form is used to collect relevant data such as property name, location, and contact information to help our team respond to inquiries in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Ensure that the required fields (marked with \*asterisk\*) are filled in before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Name (property_inquiry_form_property_name)** (`text`, *): Enter the name of the property in question.
* **Street (property_inquiry_form_street)** (`text`, *): Enter the street address of the property.
* **ZIP (property_inquiry_form_zip)** (`text`, *): Enter the ZIP code of the property.
* **State (property_inquiry_form_state)** (`select_one`, *): Select the state where the property is located from the provided options (CA, NY, TX).
* **Property Type (property_inquiry_form_property_type)** (`select_multiple`, *): Select the type of property (Single Family, Multi Family, Duplex).
* **Bedrooms (property_inquiry_form_bedrooms)** (`number`, *): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Bathrooms (property_inquiry_form_bathrooms)** (`number`, *): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **SQFT (property_inquiry_form_sqft)** (`number`, *): Enter the square footage of the property.
* **Price (property_inquiry_form_price)** (`number`, *): Enter the price of the property.
* **Contact Name (property_inquiry_form_contact_name)** (`text`, *): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (property_inquiry_form_contact_email)** (`email`, *): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (property_inquiry_form_contact_phone)** (`text`, *): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Additional Comments (property_inquiry_form_additional_comments)** (`text`, *): Enter any additional comments or notes related to the property inquiry.
* **Property Inquiry Date (property_inquiry_form_property_inquiry_date)** (`date`, *): Enter the date of the property inquiry.
* **Property Inquiry Time (property_inquiry_form_property_inquiry_time)** (`time`, *): Enter the time of the property inquiry.
* **Lead Source (property_inquiry_form_lead_source)** (`select_one`, *): Select the source of the lead (Internet, Social Media, Other).
* **Property Inquiry Agent (property_inquiry_form_property_inquiry_agent)** (`select_multiple`, *): Select the agent responsible for the property inquiry (Jim, John).

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a timely response from our team.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us directly.
