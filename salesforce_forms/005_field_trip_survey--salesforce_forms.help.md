# Field Trip Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Trip Survey is a form used to collect data on field trips for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Field Trip Survey, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have relevant information to provide.
2. Make sure you haven't completed this form for the same field trip before.
3. If applicable, answer the "event_organizer" field with either 'Yes' or 'No', indicating whether the event was organized by the school.
4. In the "student_feedback" field, provide any feedback or comments you have about the field trip.
5. In the "additional_comments" field, provide any additional information or comments about the field trip.
6. Fill out the "field_trip_cost" field with a numerical value representing the cost of the field trip.
7. In the "student_participated" field, select all the students who participated in the field trip (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **date (Field Trip Date)** (`field_trip_date`, date): Enter the date of the field trip.
* **start_time (Field Trip Start Time)** (`field_trip_start_time`, time): Enter the start time of the field trip.
* **end_time (Field Trip End Time)** (`field_trip_end_time`, time): Enter the end time of the field trip.
* **event_organizer (Event Organizer)** (`event_organizer`, select_one): Select 'Yes' if the event was organized by the school, 'No' otherwise.
* **teacher (Teacher Involved)** (`teacher`, select_one): Select 'Yes' if you were a teacher involved in the field trip, 'No' otherwise.
* **student_feedback (Student Feedback)** (`student_feedback`, text): Provide any feedback or comments about the field trip.
* **additional_comments (Additional Comments)** (`additional_comments`, text): Provide any additional information or comments about the field trip.
* **field_trip_cost (Field Trip Cost)** (`field_trip_cost`, number): Enter the numerical cost of the field trip.
* **student_participated (Student Participated)** (`student_participated`, select_multiple): Select all students who participated in the field trip.
* **student_participated_other (Student Participated Other)** (`student_participated_other`, text): If students participated but are not listed in the previous field, provide their names here.
