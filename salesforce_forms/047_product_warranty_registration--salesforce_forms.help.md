# product_warranty_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a product warranty for a customer. The form gathers information about the product, including the customer's contact details and warranty dates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the required fields as follows:
	* "Client Name": enter the customer's name.
	* "Product Number": enter the product number.
	* "Warranty Start Date": select the start date of the warranty period.
	* "Warranty End Date": select the end date of the warranty period.
	* "Contact Phone": enter the customer's phone number.
3. Optionally, you can also fill in the following fields:
	* "Product Description": provide a brief description of the product being registered.
	* "Email": enter the customer's email address.
	* "Address": enter the customer's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (Required)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Product Number (Required)**: Enter the product number to ensure accuracy in registration.
* **Product Description (Optional)**: Provide a brief description of the product being registered.
* **Warranty Start Date (Required)**: Select the start date of the warranty period from the calendar picker.
* **Warranty End Date (Required)**: Select the end date of the warranty period from the calendar picker.
* **Contact Phone (Required)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Email (Optional)**: Enter the customer's email address for contact purposes.
* **Address (Optional)**: Enter the customer's address for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check the product number and product description to ensure accuracy in registration.
* Verify that the contact phone and email fields match the customer's information.
* If you need help with the form, please contact the support team.
