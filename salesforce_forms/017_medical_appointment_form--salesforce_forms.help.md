# Medical Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Appointment Form is a digital representation of the process for scheduling medical appointments. It captures essential patient and appointment information to facilitate communication between patients and medical staff.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Patient Name** in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select a date in the "Date" field using the calendar interface.
3. Enter the **Time** of the appointment in the "Time" field.
4. Enter the **Medical Record Number** of the patient in the "Medical Record Number" field.
5. Select the **Status** of the appointment in the "Status" field, choosing from:
	* Scheduled
	* Rescheduled
	* Canceled
6. Enter the **Doctor** name in the "Doctor" field.
7. Enter the **Patient Phone** in the "Patient Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name (patient_name)**: Enter the name of the patient as it appears on their identification.
- **Date (date)**: Select a date for the appointment using the calendar interface.
- **Time (time)**: Enter the scheduled time for the appointment.
- **Doctor (doctor_name)**: Enter the name of the doctor or medical professional responsible for the appointment.
- **Medical Record Number (medical_record_number)**: Enter the patient's medical record number, which may be used for billing or record-keeping purposes.
- **Status (appointment_status)**: Choose the status of the appointment from the options provided:
	* **Scheduled**: The appointment is confirmed.
	* **Rescheduled**: The appointment has been rescheduled.
	* **Canceled**: The appointment has been canceled.
- **Doctor (doctor_name_2)**: Enter the name of the doctor or medical professional responsible for the appointment (this field may be used for redundant or backup purposes).
- **Patient Phone (patient_phone)**: Enter the patient's phone number for follow-up or communication purposes.
## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to medical staff for guidance.
* For any other questions or concerns, you may contact the medical office directly.
