# Trial Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the details of a trial request for a product or service. It is intended for users who want to explore the product or service before committing to a purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Provide accurate and truthful details about the lead.
3. Select the relevant options for the trial request.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trial Request Form (1)**: A text field for entering the description of the trial request.
* **Lead Details (2)**: A text field for entering the details of the lead.
* **Name (3)**: A text field for entering the name of the contact person.
* **Email (4)**: A text field for entering the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone (5)**: A text field for entering the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company (6)**: A text field for entering the name of the company.
* **Role (7)**: A dropdown select field with options 'Yes' and 'No' for indicating the role of the contact person.
* **Location (8)**: A multi-select field with options 'Yes' and 'No' for indicating the location of the contact person.
* **Trial Start (9)**: A date field for entering the start date of the trial.
* **Trial Duration (10)**: A numeric field for entering the duration of the trial.
* **Trial End (11)**: A date field for entering the end date of the trial.
* **Notes (12)**: A note field for adding additional notes about the trial request.
* **Trial Request (13)**: A dropdown select field with options 'Option 1', 'Option 2', and 'Option 3' for selecting the type of trial request.
* **Lead Status (14)**: A dropdown select field with options 'Option 1', 'Option 2', and 'Option 3' for selecting the status of the lead.
* **Assigned To (15)**: A text field for entering the name of the assigned person.
* **Assigned By (16)**: A text field for entering the name of the person assigning the trial.
* **Lead Owner (17)**: A multi-select field with options 'Yes' and 'No' for indicating the lead owner.
* **Trial Agreement (18)**: A note field for adding any additional trial agreements.
* **Trial Request Status (19)**: A dropdown select field with options 'Option 1', 'Option 2', and 'Option 3' for selecting the status of the trial request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Use the dropdown select fields carefully, as they have specific options.
* Use the note fields to add any additional comments or agreements.
