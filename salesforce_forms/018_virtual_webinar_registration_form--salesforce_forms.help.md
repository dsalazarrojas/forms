# Virtual Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees who want to register for a virtual webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the required information in the fields below.
2. Double-check the accuracy of your email and phone number to ensure you receive updates and reminders.
3. Select the correct options for confirm email and confirm phone.
4. Read the terms and conditions carefully and select "Agree to the terms and conditions" if you understand and agree.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Webinar Information
* **Webinar Information** (`webinar_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter a brief description of the webinar you are interested in attending.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication and updates.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication and updates.

### Name
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name for identification purposes.

### Question
* **Question** (`question`, `text`, required: false): If you have any question, please feel free to ask.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Please provide your preferred time for the webinar (if any).

### Location
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): If you want to attend the webinar in person, please provide the location.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to provide.

### Confirm Email
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, `select_one`, required: false): 
	+ Please confirm your email address for accuracy. If your email is not listed above, please enter it here.

### Confirm Phone
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
	+ If your phone number is listed above, please select to confirm. If not, please enter it here.

### Agree to Terms
* **Agree to Terms** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, required: false): Please carefully review the terms and conditions and select "Agree to the terms and conditions" if you understand and agree.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" to submit your registration.

Note: All fields are optional, but it's recommended to complete as much information as possible to get the most out of the webinar experience.
