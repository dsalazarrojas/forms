# Courier Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Courier Reimbursement Form is used to track and reimburse employees for work-related expenses, specifically for mileage and travel costs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read and understand each field before filling in the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
- Only complete this form for actual work-related activities.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid duplication of efforts and ensure your request is processed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the delivery or travel.
* **Start Mileage** (`mileage_start`, optional): Enter your start mileage for the work-related activity.
* **End Mileage** (`mileage_end`, optional): Enter your end mileage for the work-related activity.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, optional): Enter the start time of the work-related activity.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, optional): Enter the end time of the work-related activity.
* **Start Address** (`start_address`, optional): Enter the start address of the work-related activity.
* **End Address** (`end_address`, optional): Enter the end address of the work-related activity.
* **Reimbursed Amount** (`reimbursed_amount`, optional): Enter the total amount you are seeking reimbursement for.
* **Reimbursement Reason** (`reimbursed_reason`, optional): Briefly describe the reason for your reimbursement request.
* **Receipts** (`receipts`, optional): Select all relevant receipts related to your work-related activity.
	+ Gas Receipt
	+ Tolls Receipt
	+ Food Receipt
	+ Other Receipt
* **Other Reimbursement** (`other_reimbursement`, optional): Enter any other reimbursement details not covered by the receipt options.
* **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your reimbursement request.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
