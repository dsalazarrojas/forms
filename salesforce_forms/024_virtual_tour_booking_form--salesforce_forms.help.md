# Virtual Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Tour Booking Form is designed for guests to book a virtual tour. It is not intended for repeat submissions from the same guest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Virtual Tour Details page and provide a brief description of the tour you are interested in.
2. On the Guest Information page, provide your name and any additional information about yourself.
3. On the Payment Info page, enter any necessary payment details.
4. On the Booking Confirmation page, review and submit your booking information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Virtual Tour Details (tour_details)** (`tour_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the virtual tour you are interested in.
* **Guest Information (guest_info)** (`guest_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name and any additional information about yourself.
* **Payment Info (payment_info)** (`payment_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any necessary payment details.
* **Booking Confirmation (booking_confirmation)** (`booking_confirmation`, `text`, required: false): Review and submit your booking information.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We recommend booking your virtual tour as soon as possible to ensure availability.
