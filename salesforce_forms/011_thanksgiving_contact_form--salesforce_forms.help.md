# Thanksgiving Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and messages from customers on Thanksgiving. It is meant to help the organization's internal teams respond to customer inquiries efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the required fields (First Name, Email, and Phone) with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. If you have a message or note for the organization, please enter it in the "Message" field.
3. If you have a specific time and date in mind for the contact, please enter them in the "Time" and "Date" fields, respectively.
4. If you have any additional category-specific information, please select the relevant category from the "Category" field and enter the corresponding options in the "Category Options" field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
6. If you have an assigned tool or resource, please enter it in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): If you have a message or note for us, please enter it here.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you have a specific time in mind for the contact, please enter it here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the contact, please enter it here.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your request.
	+ Options: "Yes" or "No"
* **Category Options** (`category_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional categories that apply to your request.
	+ Options: "Yes" or "No"
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you have an assigned tool or resource, please enter it here.
