## warranty_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The warranty registration form is used to capture essential information for warranty registration, ensuring customers receive timely service and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warranty Details** (`warranty_details`): Enter a brief description of the warranty, including any relevant details or notes.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter your name and preferred contact method (phone, email, or webchat).
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`): Enter your shipping address and any other relevant shipping details.
* **Warranty Terms** (`warranty_terms`): Review and agree to the warranty terms to ensure you are registered for the correct warranty.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`): Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or webchat).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name as it appears on the warranty document.
* **Warranty Start Date** (`warranty_start_date`): Enter the start date of the warranty period.
* **Warranty End Date** (`warranty_end_date`): Enter the end date of the warranty period.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`): Enter the product ID associated with the warranty.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are complete and accurate before submitting the form.
* Review the warranty terms carefully to understand your responsibilities and coverage.
* Keep a copy of your warranty registration for future reference.
