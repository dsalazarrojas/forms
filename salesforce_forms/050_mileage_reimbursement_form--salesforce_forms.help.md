# Mileage Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mileage Reimbursement Form is designed for employees to submit their mileage reimbursement requests, providing details about their trip, including mileage, dates, and reimbursement requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your Employee ID (ID number assigned to you by the company).
2. Select the date of the trip for which you are requesting reimbursement.
3. Enter the total miles traveled during the trip.
4. Choose the date you are requesting reimbursement for.
5. Enter the amount you are requesting for reimbursement, including the currency.
6. Select the reimbursement rate (e.g., 10%, 15%, 20%).
7. Select the approver who will review your request.
8. Provide any additional comments for your request.
9. Add any comments from the approver.
10. If approved, select the date of approval.
11. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number).
12. Select the department and job title of the person requesting reimbursement.
13. Select the department and job title of the approver.
14. Enter your country and city of residence.
15. Enter the approver's country and city (if applicable).
16. Select the status of the reimbursement request (pending, approved, or rejected).
