# Special Event Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a special event, such as a film shoot, photo shoot, or location scout, with event details and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **Title**, **Event Type**, **Event Date**, **Event Time**, **Venue**, **Contact Info**, and **Message**.
2. Select the required **Event Type** from the list of options: Film Set, Location Scout, or Photo Shoot.
3. Choose the payment method(s) you are willing to pay.
4. Specify the number of **Event Space** you need.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: A brief description of your event.
* **Event Details**: A brief description of your event.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event you are requesting (Film Set, Location Scout, or Photo Shoot).
* **Event Date**: The date of your event.
* **Event Time**: The time of your event.
* **Venue**: The location of your event.
* **Contact Info**: Your name, phone number and email.
* **Contact Number**: Your contact number (phone or other).
* **Contact Email**: Your email address.
* **Message**: A detailed message for the event organizer.
* **Event Space**: The number of spaces or resources you need.
* **Fee**: The amount you are willing to pay.
* **User Agreement**: A brief statement of agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Agreement Terms**: A detailed statement of the terms and conditions.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method(s) you are willing to use (Cash, Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Terms**: A note field for additional terms and conditions.
* **Submit**: Submit the form after filling out all the required fields.
* **Cancel**: Cancel the form submission if you change your mind or need to correct any errors.
