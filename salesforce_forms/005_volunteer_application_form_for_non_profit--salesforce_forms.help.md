# Volunteer Application Form For Non Profit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from potential volunteers to help the non-profit organization determine their suitability for volunteer positions.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Make sure to provide your non-profit name, contact email, and contact phone number (if available).
3.  Indicate the number of volunteer hours you are able to commit to per week.
4.  Provide the start and end dates for your volunteer position, if applicable.
5.  Select the frequency at which you are able to volunteer (daily, weekly, biweekly, or monthly).
6.  Choose the status you are currently at with the non-profit (active, inactive, or pending).
7.  Select the shifts you are available to work (morning, afternoon, evening, or night).
8.  List any skills you possess that would be beneficial for the non-profit (communication, leadership, problem solving, time management).
9.  Provide references for at least two people who know you (e.g., previous work supervisors, professors, or friends).
10.  Indicate whether you have access to a vehicle (True or False).
11.  If you have any additional information or notes, please include them in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **non_profit** (`non_profit`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the non-profit organization you are interested in volunteering with.
*   **contact_email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address that can be used for communication with the non-profit.
*   **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if available) that can be used for communication with the non-profit.
*   **volunteer_hours** (`volunteer_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the number of volunteer hours you are able to commit to per week.
*   **volunteer_start_date** (`volunteer_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your volunteer position, if applicable.
*   **volunteer_end_date** (`volunteer_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of your volunteer position, if applicable.
*   **volunteer_frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select the frequency at which you are able to volunteer (daily, weekly, biweekly, or monthly).
*   **volunteer_status** (`volunteer_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the status you are currently at with the non-profit (active, inactive, or pending).
*   **volunteer_shifts** (`volunteer_shifts`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the shifts you are available to work (morning, afternoon, evening, or night).
*   **volunteer_skills** (`volunteer_skills`, `select_multiple`, required): List any skills you possess that would be beneficial for the non-profit (communication, leadership, problem solving, time management).
*   **volunteer_references** (`volunteer_references`, `text`, required): Provide references for at least two people who know you (e.g., previous work supervisors, professors, or friends).
*   **volunteer_vehicles_available** (`volunteer_vehicles_available`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether you have access to a vehicle (True or False).
*   **volunteer_notes** (`volunteer_notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional information or notes, please include them in the space provided.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all required fields. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the non-profit organization.
