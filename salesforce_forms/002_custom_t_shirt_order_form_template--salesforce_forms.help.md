# Custom T Shirt Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom T Shirt Order Form Template is designed to gather essential information from customers for a t-shirt order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired T shirt size.
2. Choose the T shirt color.
3. If required, upload a T shirt design image or provide a description.
4. Enter the customer's name.
5. Enter the shipping address.
6. Enter the billing address.
7. If required, provide any additional order notes.
8. Enter the order date and time (if applicable).
9. Enter the order phone number (if applicable).
10. Enter the order email (if applicable).
11. Enter any additional order notes (if applicable).

14. Enter the order amount (if applicable).
15. Enter the order tax (if applicable).
16. Enter the order total (if applicable).
17. Enter the customer's name (required).
18. Enter the customer's name (required).
