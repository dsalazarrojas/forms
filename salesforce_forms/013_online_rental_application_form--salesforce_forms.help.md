# Online Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Rental Application Form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in renting a property. This form helps us to understand their contact details, property preferences, and other relevant information to facilitate the rental application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Contact Info" section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide details about the property you are interested in, including any relevant images.
3. Specify your contact preferences (e.g., Tenant Contact, Landlord Contact, or Both).
4. If applicable, select the move-in date for the rental property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, **required**): Fill out this section with your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you about your rental application.
* **Property Info** (`property_info`, text, **optional**): Provide any relevant details about the property you are interested in, such as its address, square footage, or amenities.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_multiple, **optional**): Specify how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Tenant Contact, Landlord Contact, or Both).
* **Property Images** (`property_images`, number, **optional**): If you have any images of the property, upload them here to help us get a better understanding of your interest in the property.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, **optional**): If you have a preferred move-in date, select it here to help us plan for your rental application.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have a valid email address and phone number to facilitate communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact us.
* Once you have completed the form, a member of our team will review your application and contact you to discuss your rental options.
