# Shipping Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides a detailed explanation of how to complete the Shipping Quote Form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address (if applicable).
2. Select the shipping service you wish to use (UPS, FedEx, or DHL).
3. Enter your delivery date and time, as well as any estimated delivery time.
4. Provide package details, including any relevant notes or information about the shipment.
5. Enter the estimated cost of the shipment.
6. Select your preferred payment method (bank transfer, PayPal, or credit card).
7. Sign the form with your customer signature and salesperson signature.
8. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address**: This field is required to provide the delivery address.
*  **Contact Number**: This field is optional and provides an alternative contact method for the customer.
* **Email**: This field is optional and allows the customer to provide an email address for communication.
*  **Delivery Date**: This field is required and establishes the date of delivery.
*  **Delivery Time**: This field is optional and provides an estimated delivery time.
*  **Shipping Service**: This field is required and selects the shipping service to be used (UPS, FedEx, or DHL).
*  **Package Details**: This field is required and allows you to enter any relevant notes or information about the package.
*  **Estimated Cost**: This field is required and estimates the cost of the shipment.
*  **Payment Method**: This field is optional and allows you to select a payment method (bank transfer, PayPal, or credit card).
*  **Customer Signature**: This field is required and must be signed by the customer.
*  **Salesperson Signature**: This field is required and must be signed by the salesperson.
*  **Additional Info**: This field is optional and provides space for any additional information.
*  **Shipping Quote Terms**: This field is optional and allows for any relevant terms or notes about the shipment.
*  **Terms and Conditions**: This field is required and must be confirmed by the customer.
*  **Confirm Terms**: This field is required and confirms that the customer has read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
*  **Shipping Quote Number**: This field is optional and provides a unique identifier for the shipment.
*  **Quote Expires**: This field is optional and establishes when the quote expires.
*  **Estimated Delivery Time**: This field is optional and provides an estimated delivery time.
*  **Delivery Address**: This field is optional and provides the delivery address.
*  **Delivery City**: This field is optional and provides the delivery city.
*  **Delivery State**: This field is optional and provides the delivery state.
*  **Delivery Zip**: This field is optional and provides the delivery zip code.
*  **Customer Name**: This field is optional and provides the customer's name.
*  **Customer Email**: This field is optional and provides the customer's email address.
*  **Customer Phone**: This field is optional and provides the customer's phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you need to make changes to a quote, please refer to the original quote number.
* If you're unsure about any information, please ask your salesperson for assistance.
