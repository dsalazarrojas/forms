# Vacation Bible School Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents and guardians regarding their child's participation in the Vacation Bible School program. Please fill out this form to register your child for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out all required fields.
2.  Answer the questions accurately and completely.
3.  Review and submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information regarding yourself as a parent or guardian.
    *   Please enter any relevant details about yourself.
*   **Child Info** (`child_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information regarding your child.
    *   Please enter any relevant details about your child.
*   **Group Info** (`group_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information regarding your child's group or class.
    *   Please enter any relevant details about your child's group or class.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for you to enter your email address.
    *   Please enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your phone number.
    *   Please enter your phone number.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is for you to select the date you would like for your child's Vacation Bible School to start.
    *   Please select a date for the Vacation Bible School.
*   **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, true): This field is for you to select one item from the provided list.
    *   Please select one option from the provided list.
*   **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, true): This field is for you to select multiple items from the provided list.
    *   Please select multiple options from the provided list.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, false): This field is for you to enter any additional notes or comments.
    *   Please enter any additional notes or comments.

# Tips
*   Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
