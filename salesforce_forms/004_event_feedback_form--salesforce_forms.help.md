# event_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "event_feedback_form" is a tool designed to gather feedback from attendees about a specific event. This feedback will help event organizers to understand the attendees' experiences and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Event Feedback** section with your overall impression of the event.
2. Enter the **Presenter Name** of the person who presented at the event.
3. Briefly describe the **Event Description** to help the organizers understand what they presented.
4. Select the date and time of the event from the calendar in the **Event Date** and **Event Time** sections.
5. Answer the following questions:
   - **Presenter**: Was the presenter engaging and knowledgeable? (Select 'Yes' or 'No')
   - **Attendees**: Were there enough attendees for the event? (Select 'Yes' or 'No')
6. Provide additional information about the **Venue** and **Services** used for the event.
7. If any additional **Services** were used, describe them in the **Additional Services** section.
8. Lastly, share any **Suggestions** for improvement and enter any **Comments** about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Feedback**: This field is for you to share your general impression of the event. It's a short paragraph where you can describe your overall experience.
* **Presenter Name**: Enter the name of the presenter who spoke at the event.
* **Event Description**: Briefly describe the event, including what you learned or what you thought was interesting.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event from the calendar.
* **Presenter**: Answer 'Yes' if the presenter was engaging and knowledgeable, 'No' otherwise.
* **Attendees**: Answer 'Yes' if there were enough attendees for the event, 'No' otherwise.
* **Venue**: Enter the name of the venue where the event took place.
* **Services**: Describe any services used for the event, such as catering or equipment rental.
* **Additional Services**: If you used any additional services, describe them here.
* **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions on how the event could be improved.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments about the event.

This guide is designed to help users understand the purpose of the "event_feedback_form" and how to complete it successfully.
