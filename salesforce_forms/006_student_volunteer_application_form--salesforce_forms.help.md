# Student Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information from students who want to participate in volunteer activities. This form helps the volunteer team manage the volunteer program effectively by gathering details about the student's availability, volunteer preferences, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to the required and optional fields.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): This field is intended to collect the student's name and contact information. Please enter your name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Volunteer Info** (`volunteer_info`, text, required: false): This field is meant to capture any additional information about your volunteer preferences or interests. You can type in any details you'd like to help us better understand your volunteer needs.
* **Application Info** (`application_info`, text, required: false): This field is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information about your application. Please use this space to explain your motivations or any specific requirements you have for volunteering.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required: false): This field is used to record the date when you're available to volunteer. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, required: false): This field is intended to capture the time when you're available to volunteer. Please enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Volunteer Duration** (`volunteer_duration`, text, required: false): This field is for you to specify the duration of your volunteer period.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, text, required: false): This field is meant for the student's supervisor or mentor to provide their signature and approval.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required: false): This field is for the student's manager or department head to provide their signature and approval.
* **Volunteer Duration Hrs** (`volunteer_duration_hrs`, number, required: false): This field is for you to specify the number of hours you're committed to volunteering.
* **Volunteer Duration Exceptions** (`volunteer_duration_exceptions`, text, required: false): This field is for you to specify any exceptional circumstances that may affect your volunteer duration.
* **Student Acceptance** (`student_acceptance`, date, required: false): This field is used to record the date when the student accepts the volunteer assignment.
* **Student Rejection** (`student_rejection`, date, required: false): This field is used to record the date when the student rejects the volunteer assignment.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
