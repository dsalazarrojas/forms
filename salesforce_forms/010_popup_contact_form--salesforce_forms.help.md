# Popup Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Popup Contact Form is a brief contact form that allows visitors to input their first name, email, phone number, and a message. This form is intended to be used for sales and general contact purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please note that this field requires a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Type your message in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (form_1_name)**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Email (form_1_email)**: Enter your email address in this format: `example@email.com`. This field is required.
* **Phone (form_1_phone)**: Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Message (form_1_message)**: Type your message in this field. This field is required for us to respond to your inquiry.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate.
* The message field should be a brief description of your inquiry.
* If you need further assistance, please contact us directly at [insert contact information].
