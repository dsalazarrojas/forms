# Warranty Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a warranty claim to the salesforce team. To create a successful claim, please ensure you have the necessary information and attachments ready.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the claim process.
2. Fill out the form by entering the required information for each field.
3. Attach any necessary documents or images to support your claim (e.g., photos, repair records).
4. Provide a detailed note with any additional context or explanations for your claim.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, address, and contact information to ensure we can reach you regarding the claim.
* **Product info** (`product_info`, text, required/optional): Describe the product with the issue, including its serial number, model number, or any other relevant details.
* **Claim details** (`claim_details`, text, required/optional): Clearly state the issue with your product, including the problem you've encountered and any relevant dates or circumstances.
* **Claim attachment** (`claim_attachment`, text, required/optional): Attach any supporting documents or images that demonstrate the issue, such as photos or repair records.
* **Claim note** (`claim_note`, note, required/optional): Write a detailed note providing additional context or explanations about your claim, such as any previous interactions with the salesforce team.
* **Customer signature** (`customer_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign and date the form to confirm your submission and agreement to the terms and conditions of the warranty.
* **Salesforce signature** (`salesforce_signature`, text, required/optional): This field is intended for the salesforce team member to sign and date the form upon receipt and acknowledgement of the claim. Please do not fill this in.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have provided all required information to avoid delays in processing your claim.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the salesforce team for guidance.
* Attach any necessary documentation to support your claim to expedite the process.
