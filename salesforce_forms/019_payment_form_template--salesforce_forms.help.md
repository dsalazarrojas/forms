# Payment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Form Template is a form used to collect payment information from customers for various sales-related activities within the Salesforce platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field.
2. Make sure to select a payment method from the "Select Payment Method" field.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field if needed.
4. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Payment Method** (id: 8): Choose the payment method you wish to use for the transaction. Select from Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal.
* **Card Name** (id: 4): This field is for the name of the card being used for the payment.
* **Card Number** (id: 5): Enter the credit card number used for the payment.
* **Card Expire** (id: 6): This field is for the expiration date of the card being used for the payment. Please enter the month (01-12) and the year as a numeric value.
* **Amount** (id: 7): Enter the total amount of the transaction.
* **Customer Name** (id: 2): Enter the name of the customer making the payment.
* **Billing Address** (id: 3): Enter the billing address of the customer making the payment.
* **Phone** (id: 11): Enter the phone number of the customer making the payment.
* **Email** (id: 10): Enter the email address of the customer making the payment.
* **Card Security CCV** (id: 12): Enter the Card Security CCV (Card Verification Value) found on the back of the credit card used for the payment.
* **Card Expire Month** (id: 13): This field is related to the Card Expire field. Please match the month of the card's expiration date.
* **Note** (id: 9): Enter any additional notes or comments about the transaction in this field.
* **Card Expire** (id: 6): This field is likely meant to be related to the Card Expire field, but it seems to be a duplicate field. Please ignore this field as it's not necessary.
* **Card Number** (id: 5): This field is meant to collect the credit card number for the payment transaction.
* **Amount** (id: 7): Enter the total amount of the transaction.
* **Phone** (id: 11): Enter the phone number of the customer making the payment.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid any payment processing issues.
* Double-check the required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the "Select Payment Method" field to determine which payment method to use.
* Enter any additional comments or notes if needed.
