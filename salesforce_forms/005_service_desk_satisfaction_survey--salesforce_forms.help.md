# service_desk_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The service desk satisfaction survey is a tool used to gather feedback from customers or users about their experiences with the service desk. This form helps identify areas of improvement and ensures that the service desk team is meeting customer expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of issue you encountered (Technical or Non-Technical) on the first page.
2. Provide a description of the issue (page 1, question 1).
3. Enter the date you first connected to us (page 1, question 2).
4. Enter the date you last connected to us (page 1, question 3).
5. Select your preferred method of communication (page 1, question 4).
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience (page 1, question 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of issue did you encounter** (Select One, page 1):
	+ Choose the type of issue you experienced (Technical or Non-Technical).
* **Description of issue** (Text, page 1):
	+ Briefly describe the issue you encountered.
* **What was you first connected to us** (Date, page 1):
	+ Enter the date you first connected to our service.
* **What was you last connected to us** (Date, page 1):
	+ Enter the date you last connected to our service.
* **How did you prefer communication** (Select One, page 1):
	+ Select your preferred method of communication (Email, Phone, or Chat).
* **Description of preferred communication method** (Text, page 1):
	+ Provide a brief description of your preferred communication method.
* **Service Desk Satisfaction - Summary** (Note, page 2):
	+ Summarize your overall experience with the service desk.
