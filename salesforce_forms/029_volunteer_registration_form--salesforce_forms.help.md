# volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The volunteer registration form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers for events, projects, or other initiatives. This form helps organizers manage volunteer shifts, projects, and volunteer availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the next field.
3. Provide your **Email** address, which will be used to contact you about your volunteer status.
4. Enter your **Phone Number**, which is useful for organizers to reach you.
5. Select your **Event Selection**:
	* "Yes" if you're interested in participating.
	* "No" if you're not interested.
6. Choose the **Volunteer Selection** options:
	* "Yes" if you're available to volunteer.
	* "No" if you're not available.
7. Choose your desired **Volunteer Shift**:
	* Select a specific date and time for your volunteer shift.
8. Provide a **Volunteer Date**, which is your preferred date for volunteering.
9. Enter any additional comments in the **Volunteer Note** field.
10. Select your **Project Selection**:
	* "Yes" if you're interested in participating in the project.
	* "No" if you're not interested.
11. Choose your **Volunteer Role** options:
	* Select multiple roles you're interested in or available for.
12. Enter the number of **Volunteer Hours** you're willing to commit to.
13. Provide your **Volunteer Availability**:
	* Select a date and time for when you're available to volunteer.
14. Enter any **Volunteer Conflicts**:
	* A date and time when you're not available to volunteer.
15. Enter any additional comments in the **Volunteer Comments** field.
16. Select the **Project Start Date** and **Project End Date** for the project you're interested in.
17. Choose your **Volunteer Status**:
	* "Active" if you're an active volunteer.
	* "Inactive" if you're not currently volunteering.
18. Enter the **Volunteer Termination Date**, which is the date you'd like to stop volunteering.
19. If applicable, enter the **Assigned Tool** and **Assigned Tool ID** for the project you're working on.
20. Enter your name in the **Volunteer Form Submitted By** field.
21. Enter your email address in the **Volunteer Form Submitted By Email** field.
22. Enter your phone number in the **Volunteer Form Submitted By Phone** field.
23. Finally, select the date and time you submitted this form in the **Volunteer Form Submitted Date** field.
