# Sales Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Lead Generation Form is used to collect information about potential clients or customers for sales teams to follow up with.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Company name.
6. Enter your Department.
7. Enter your Job Title (or Job Title 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, depending on the number of titles you have).
8. Enter your Job Function.
9. Enter your Location.
10. Enter your Company Website.
11. Select your Company Type (Private, Public, or Non-profit).
12. Enter your Company Address 1.
13. Enter your Company Address 2.
14. Enter your Company Address 3.
15. Enter your Company Address 4.
16. (Optional) Enter your Revenue.
17. (Optional) Enter the number of Employees.
18. (Optional) Enter your industry.
19. (Optional) Enter your Company Address 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your personal name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your family name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your business or personal email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your work or personal phone number.
* **Company** (text, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Department** (text, optional): Enter your department in the company.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter your job title in the company.
* **Job Title 1** (text, optional): Enter your first job title.
* **Job Title 2** (text, optional): Enter your second job title.
* **Job Title 3** (text, optional): Enter your third job title.
* **Job Title 4** (text, optional): Enter your fourth job title.
* **Job Title 5** (text, optional): Enter your fifth job title.
* **Job Title 6** (text, optional): Enter your sixth job title.
* **Job Title 7** (text, optional): Enter your seventh job title.
* **Job Function** (text, optional): Enter your job function.
* **Location** (text, optional): Enter your location.
* **Company Website** (text, optional): Enter your company's website.
* **Industry** (text, optional): Enter your industry.
* **Revenue** (number, optional): Enter your company's revenue.
* **Employees** (number, optional): Enter the number of employees in your company.
* **Company Type** (select_one, optional): Select your company type (Private, Public, or Non-profit).
* **Address 1** (text, optional): Enter your company's address 1.
* **Address 2** (text, optional): Enter your company's address 2.
* **Address 3** (text, optional): Enter your company's address 3.
* **Address 4** (text, optional): Enter your company's address 4.
