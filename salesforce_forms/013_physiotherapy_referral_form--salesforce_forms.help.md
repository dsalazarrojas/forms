## physiotherapy_referral_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The physiotherapy referral form is designed to gather essential information for referring patients to physiotherapy services. This form helps healthcare professionals collect and document relevant patient data, ensuring that patients receive the best possible care. The form is used to create a detailed record of a patient's medical history, current medical condition, and treatment plan, facilitating effective communication between healthcare providers and improving patient outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill in the patient's name in the **patient_name** field. This field is a required field and is used to identify the patient uniquely.
2.  Next, provide the patient's phone number in the **patient_phone** field. This field is optional but helps healthcare professionals contact the patient for further information or to schedule appointments.
3.  Enter the name of the physiotherapist who will be treating the patient in the **physiotherapist_name** field. This field is optional and is used to keep records of the treatment team.
4.  Enter the phone number of the physiotherapist in the **physiotherapist_phone** field. This field is optional and is used for communication purposes.
5.  Describe the patient's current **medical_condition** in the provided text field. This field is optional but helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's current health status.
6.  Provide a detailed account of the patient's **medical_history** in the provided text field. This field is optional but helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's past health events.
7.  Write a detailed **physiotherapy_treatment_plan** in the provided note field. This field is optional but helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's treatment goals and objectives.
8.  Finally, select the start date of the treatment in the **treatment_start_date** field. This field is optional and is used to schedule appointments and track treatment progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **patient_name**: Enter the patient's name.
*   **patient_phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
*   **physiotherapist_name**: Enter the physiotherapist's name.
*   **physiotherapist_phone**: Enter the physiotherapist's phone number.
*   **medical_condition**: Describe the patient's current medical condition.
*   **medical_history**: Provide a detailed account of the patient's medical history.
*   **physiotherapy_treatment_plan**: Write a detailed treatment plan for the patient.
*   **treatment_start_date**: Select the start date of the treatment.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly to ensure effective communication and treatment.
*   If a field is not required, it is still an important part of the patient's record and should be completed if possible.
*   If you have any doubts or questions about this form, please contact your healthcare team for guidance.
