# Fancy Envelope Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fancy Envelope Contact Form is a form designed to collect contact information and comments from users. It is intended to be used in sales or business settings to gather details from customers or clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Type in your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If you represent a company, enter your company name in the "Company" field.
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter your Zip code in the "Zip" field.
8. If you have any comments or feedback, enter them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, string, required): The form title, currently set to "Fancy Envelope Contact Form". This field is not directly editable by users.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you represent a company, enter your company name here.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address here.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback here.
* **File 1** (`file`, text, optional): If required, upload a file here.
* **File 2** (`file_2`, text, optional): If required, upload another file here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (First Name, Last Name, and Email).
* Use a valid email address and phone number (if provided).
* Keep your comments brief and to the point.
* If you're attaching files, make sure they're relevant and not exceeding the file size limit.
* Review your submission before submitting to ensure you've provided all necessary information.
