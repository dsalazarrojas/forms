# Prayer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prayer Request Form is used to collect information from users about their prayer requests. This form allows users to provide their general information, prayer type, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your General Information, including your Name and Email.
2. Select your preferred Prayer Type from the options provided.
3. Write a Message describing your prayer request.
4. If necessary, provide a specific Date of Birth and Prayer Request Date.
5. You may also add Additional Comments if you have any further information about your request.
6. Finally, assign your request to a specific person by selecting from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (General Information, text, not required)
	+ This section allows you to provide your name and any other general information about yourself.
* **Name** (Name, text, not required)
	+ Please enter your name as you would like it to be recorded for prayer requests.
* **Email** (Email, email, not required)
	+ Enter your email address so that we can contact you if necessary.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, not required)
	+ If you would like to specify a specific date of birth for your prayer request, enter it here.
* **Prayer Type** (Prayer Type, select_one, not required)
	+ Select the type of prayer request you are submitting, such as General, Birthday, or Get Well.
* **Message** (Message, note, not required)
	+ Use this space to write a brief description of your prayer request.
* **Prayer Request Date** (Prayer Request Date, time, not required)
	+ If you have a specific time or date in mind for the prayer request, enter it here.
* **Assigned to** (Assigned to, select_multiple, not required)
	+ Select who you would like to assign your prayer request to.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, not required)
	+ Add any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, not required)
	+ If you have any further information about your request, add it here.

## Tips
* Be specific and clear in your prayer request to ensure that your request is accurately understood.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the assigned individual.
* This form is for internal use only, so please do not share it with others.
* Make sure to review your submission before submitting it to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
