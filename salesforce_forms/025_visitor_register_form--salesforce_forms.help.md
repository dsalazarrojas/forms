# Visitor Register Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Register Form is designed to gather essential information about each visitor to ensure a smooth experience during their visit. This includes details about their name, contact information, visit schedule, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. If you have an email address, enter it in the "Email" field. This is an optional field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field. This is an optional field.
4. Select your "Visitor Type" from the available options: Student, Staff, or Guest.
5. Enter your department in the "Department" field. This is an optional field.
6. If you have any specific purpose for your visit, enter it in the "Purpose" field. This is an optional field.
7. Enter your arrival and departure times in the "Arrival Time" and "Departure Time" fields, respectively. These fields are optional.
8. Take a photo of yourself with your visitor image in the "Visitor Image" field. This is an optional field.
9. Provide any additional contact form notes in the "Contact Form Notes" field. This is an optional field.
10. Select the type of signature you would like to use from the list: Signature 1, Signature 2, or Signature 3. This is an optional field.
11. If you have any further visit details, such as the date of your visit, enter it in the "Visit Date" field. This is an optional field.
12. Enter the details of your staff sign in and sign out in the "Staff Sign In" and "Staff Sign Out" fields, respectively. These fields are optional.
13. Finally, enter your visitor ID in the "Visitor ID" field. This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Text)**: Enter your name to help our staff recognize you during your visit.
* **Email (Email)**: If you have an email address, enter it so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone (Number)**: If you have a phone number, enter it so we can reach you if required.
* **Visitor Type (Select One)**: Select your visitor type: Student, Staff, or Guest.
* **Department (Text)**: Enter your department if you are visiting from one.
* **Purpose (Text)**: If you have any specific purpose for your visit, enter it here.
* **Arrival Time (Time)**: Enter your expected arrival time.
* **Departure Time (Time)**: Enter your expected departure time.
* **Visitor Image (Note)**: Take a photo of yourself with your visitor image.
* **Contact Form Notes (Text)**: Provide any additional contact form notes.
* **Visitor Signature (Select Multiple)**: Select the type of signature you would like to use.
* **Visit Date (Date)**: If you have further visit details, enter the date of your visit.
* **Staff Sign In (Text)**: Enter the details of your staff sign in.
* **Staff Sign Out (Text)**: Enter the details of your staff sign out.
* **Visitor ID (Text)**: Enter your visitor ID. This is a required field.
* **Visitor ID 2 (Text)**: This field is not used, as "Visitor ID" is a required field.
* **Visitor ID 3 (Text)**: This field is not used, as "Visitor ID" is a required field.
