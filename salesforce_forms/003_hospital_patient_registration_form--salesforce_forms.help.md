### Hospital Patient Registration Form - Help Guide

#### Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a hospital patient, including personal details, medical history, and medical insurance information. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete data.

#### How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as **Required**) with accurate information.
2. Enter your phone number and email address (if applicable) for contact purposes.
3. Select relevant medical specialties, allergies, conditions, and blood type (if applicable).
4. Enter your patient ID and age.
5. Select your doctor's specialty (if applicable).
6. Select the medical facility where you are registered (if applicable).

#### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your mobile phone number or landline number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Patient Address**: Enter your current address for billing and correspondence purposes.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history, such as previous treatments or surgeries.
* **Medical Specialist**: Select any relevant medical specialties, such as doctor, nurse, or surgeon.
* **Medication Allergies**: Select any medications you are allergic to.
* **Medical Condition**: Select any known medical conditions, such as hypertension, diabetes, or asthma.
* **Patient ID**: Enter a unique patient ID number assigned to you by the hospital.
* **Medical Test Results**: Enter any relevant test results or lab reports.
* **Medical Insurance**: Enter your medical insurance information, such as policy number or provider name.
* **Medical Group**: Enter the name of your medical group or clinic.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter your doctor's name and specialty (if applicable).
* **Patient Gender**: Select your gender (male/female).
* **Patient Age**: Enter your current age.
* **Blood Type**: Select your blood type (A, B, AB, O).
* **Doctor Specialist**: Select your doctor's specialty (if applicable).
* **Medical Facility**: Select the medical facility where you are registered (if applicable).

#### Tips
* Ensure accuracy and completeness of information to avoid any potential errors or complications.
* If you need assistance or have questions, please contact the hospital staff for help.
* Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly, and take a few moments to review it before submission.
