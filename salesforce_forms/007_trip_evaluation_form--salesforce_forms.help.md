## Trip Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trip Evaluation Form is a tool used to evaluate trips and provide feedback. It helps to assess whether a trip was successful or not, and to gather information on trip dates, location, type, status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Trip Evaluation Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the evaluation outcome of your trip by choosing from the available options.
2. Enter the dates of your trip using the date input field.
3. Provide the location of your trip in the text input field.
4. Choose the type of your trip from the available options.
5. Select the status of your trip from the available options.
6. Enter the client ID for the trip.
7. Enter the project ID for the trip.
8. Enter the project name and other relevant details about the trip.
9. Enter the trip name and other relevant details about the trip.
10. Enter any notes about the trip.
