# Veterinarian Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinarian Appointment Form is designed to collect information about a pet's visit to a veterinarian.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide the necessary information about your pet, including its type, name, and details.
2. Enter the owner's contact information, including their phone number, address, and email.
3. Schedule an appointment by choosing a date and time for the visit.
4. Provide any additional details or requests for the veterinarian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet details**: Enter a brief description of your pet's characteristics or any relevant health information.
* **owner details**: Enter your name, phone number, address, and email for the owner's contact information.
* **visit date**: Choose a date for the scheduled appointment.
* **visit time**: Select a time slot for the visit.
* **pet type**: Select the type of pet, such as Cat, Dog, or Other.
* **pet name**: Enter your pet's name for easy identification.
* **phone**: Enter the owner's phone number for easy contact.
* **address**: Enter the owner's address for easy location.
* **email**: Enter the owner's email for easy contact.
* **appointment time**: Schedule the visit time. 
* **veterninarian**: Enter the veterinarian's name. 

Note: Only the fields marked as required will be checked for completion during the submission process. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your information for correctness and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your veterinarian directly.
* The veterinarian will review and confirm your appointment details before the visit. 
* Please arrive on time for your scheduled appointment to avoid delays.
