# Product Waiting List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Waiting List Form is designed to be used by sales and customer service teams to track products that are waiting on a list. It helps the team to keep accurate and up-to-date information about product status and customer details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Customer Id** field and enter the customer's ID. This is the unique identifier for the customer.
2. Choose the **Product Id** field and enter the product's ID. This is the unique identifier for the product.
3. Select the **Status** field and choose the current status of the product from the options provided (Active, Inactive, In Progress, Waiting to ship, Available to ship, Not available, Cancelled).
4. If necessary, select additional status options from the **Status options** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Id** (Product Waiting List Form Customer Id, Number, Required): This field is used to identify the customer who is waiting on the product. Enter the customer's unique ID as provided by your system.
* **Product Id** (Product Waiting List Form Product Id, Number, Required): This field is used to identify the product that is waiting on the list. Enter the product's unique ID as provided by your system.
* **Status** (Product Waiting List Form Status, Select One, Required): This field is used to track the current status of the product. Choose the status from the options provided (Active, Inactive, In Progress, Waiting to ship, Available to ship, Not available, Cancelled).
* **Status options** (Product Waiting List Form Status options, Select Multiple, Not Required): This field is used to select additional status options for the product. Choose the status options that apply to the product from the provided list (In Progress, Waiting to ship, Available to ship, Not available, Cancelled).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and up-to-date information about product status and customer details.
- If you're unsure about any of the field's meaning, please contact your supervisor or team leader for clarification.
- If you're using this form for a customer, please make sure to follow the company's internal policies and procedures for data handling and confidentiality.
