# Visitor Sign In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Sign In Form is used to collect information from visitors during their visit to a host's office or location. This information is used to maintain accurate visitor records and ensure a smooth visit experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Guest Name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Enter the Host Name in the "Host Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter the date of your visit in the "Date Reported" field using the date format (DD/MM/YY).
5. Enter the time of your visit in the "Time Reported" field using the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
6. Enter your department in the "Host Dept." field and your building number in the "Host Building" field.
7. Enter your floor number in the "Host Floor" field and your office number in the "Host Office" field.
8. Enter the visitor's department in the "Guest Dept." field and building number in the "Guest Building" field.
9. Enter the visitor's floor number in the "Guest Floor" field and office number in the "Guest Office" field.
10. Select the purpose of your visit from the "Reason for Visit" dropdown menu.
11. Sign your name in the "Host Signature" field.
12. Sign your name in the "Guest Signature" field.
13. Ensure the date and time stamped fields are updated automatically upon form submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`visitor_sign_in_form`, text, required): Enter your name as a visitor.
* **Host Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your host or the person you are visiting.
* **Phone Number** (`visitor_phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date Reported** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit in the format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Time Reported** (`time_of_visit`, time, required): Enter the time of your visit in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Host Dept.** (`host_department`, text, required): Enter the department of your host or the area they are located in.
* **Host Building** (`host_building`, text, required): Enter the building number of your host's office.
* **Host Floor** (`host_floor`, text, required): Enter the floor number of your host's office.
* **Host Office** (`host_office`, text, required): Enter the office number of your host's office.
* **Guest Dept.** (`guest_department`, text, required): Enter the department of the visitor, e.g., their office or area they are visiting.
* **Guest Building** (`guest_building`, text, required): Enter the building number of the visitor's office or area.
* **Guest Floor** (`guest_floor`, text, required): Enter the floor number of the visitor's office or area.
* **Guest Office** (`guest_office`, text, required): Enter the office number of the visitor's office or area.
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of your visit from the dropdown menu.
* **Host Signature** (`host_signature`, note, required): Sign your name as the host or the person being visited.
* **Guest Signature** (`guest_signature`, note, required): Sign your name as the visitor.
* **Date Stamped** (`date_stamped`, date, required): This field is updated automatically upon form submission.
* **Time Stamped** (`time_stamped`, time, required): This field is updated automatically upon form submission.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the date and time fields for correct format and content.
* If you are unable to complete any field, please contact the host or facility management for assistance.
