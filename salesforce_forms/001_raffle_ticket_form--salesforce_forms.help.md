# Raffle Ticket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Raffle Ticket Form is used to collect and manage raffle ticket sales and related information. It is intended for users to enter their name, email address, select a raffle ticket, and provide payment and order details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Enter Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Enter Email** field.
3. Select the raffle ticket you would like to enter from the **Select a Ticket** dropdown list.
4. Choose a date for your raffle entry in the **Select a Date** field.
5. Select a payment option from the **Payment Options** dropdown list.
6. Enter the total amount for your raffle entry in the **Total Amount** field.
7. Enter the quantity of tickets you would like to purchase in the **Ticket Quantity** field.
8. Provide a brief description of your order in the **Order Description** field.
9. Enter the date of your order in the **Order Date** field.
10. Enter the total price of your order in the **Total Price** field.
11. Select the payment status of your entry from the **Payment Status** dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Name**: Enter your full name as it should be printed on the raffle ticket.
* **Enter Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted if you win.
* **Select a Ticket**: Choose the raffle ticket you would like to enter.
* **Select a Date**: Enter the date you would like to have your raffle entry.
* **Ticket Price**: Enter the price of the ticket you are purchasing.
* **Payment Options**: Select one or more payment options from the list.
* **Total Amount**: Enter the total amount you are paying.
* **Ticket Quantity**: Enter the number of tickets you are purchasing.
* **Order Description**: Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of your order.
* **Total Price**: Enter the total price of your order.
* **Payment Status**: Select the payment status of your entry, either "Paid" or "Not Paid".
