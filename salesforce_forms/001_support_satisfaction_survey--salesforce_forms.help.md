# Support Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers or users about their experience with the support team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
2. For question 1, select one of the options that best describes your level of satisfaction with the support team.
3. For questions 2-4, provide a brief explanation of your thoughts and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Support Satisfaction** (`support_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the support team. Select one of the options that best describes your feelings:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Question Two** (`question_two`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief explanation of your recent experience with the support team. What went well, what didn't go well, or anything in between.
* **Question Three** (`question_three`, `text`, required: false): If you had a particularly positive or negative experience, please share more details about it.
* **Question Four** (`question_four`, `text`, required: false): Is there anything else you'd like to share with us about your experience?
