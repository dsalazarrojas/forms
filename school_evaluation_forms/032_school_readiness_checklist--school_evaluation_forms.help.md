# School Readiness Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Readiness Checklist is a form designed to help schools assess and evaluate students' readiness to learn. It covers various aspects of a student's ability, including Language, Math, Reading, Science, Writing, Social-Emotional, Communication, Critical Thinking, and Problem Solving. The goal is to identify areas where a student may need additional support or resources to prepare them for the academic challenges they will face.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the provided fields with the information requested. The form is divided into sections, and each section corresponds to a different area of assessment. Follow the prompts for each field to provide accurate and detailed information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Parent Info (<parent_info, text, optional>
Enter the parent's name, contact information, or any relevant details about the parent that will help the school understand their involvement in the student's education.

### 2. Student Info (<student_info, text, optional>
Enter the student's name, contact information, or any other relevant details about the student that will help the school get in touch with them.

### 3. Language Assessment (<language_assessment, number, optional>
Rate the student's language skills, with the following scale:
* 1: Limited language skills
* 2: Developing language skills
* 3: Proficient language skills

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest assessments of the student's skills.
* If a student requires additional support, be sure to indicate this in the comments or recommendations section.
* If a student has demonstrated exceptional skills, highlight this as well.
* This form is meant to be a tool for school administrators to assess student readiness, not for evaluation purposes.
