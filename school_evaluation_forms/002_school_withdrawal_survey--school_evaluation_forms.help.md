# school_withdrawal_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The school_withdrawal_survey form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from students regarding their school experience. This form helps school administrators understand student satisfaction levels, reasons for leaving, and any additional comments or suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the `name` field.
2. Enter the student's email address in the `email` field.
3. Select the student's grade level from the dropdown menu in the `grade` field.
4. In the `who_filled_out` field, indicate who filled out the form (e.g., student, parent, or teacher).
5. Provide reasons for leaving in the `reasons_for_leaving` field.
6. Share your thoughts about the school in the `thoughts` field.
7. List any disappointments in the `disappointments` field.
8. Offer recommendations for improvement in the `recommendations` field.
9. Enter the name and email of the person who filled out the form in the `name` and `email` fields, respectively.
10. Select the date and time of completion from the calendars or clock in the `completed_on` and `completed_at` fields, respectively.
11. Choose your satisfaction level from the dropdown menu in the `satisfaction_level` field.
12. Provide additional information in the `additional_info` field.
13. Select whether you agree or disagree with all the statements in the `agree` field.
14. Provide comments and feedback in the `comments` and `comments_2`, `comments_3`, `comments_4`, `comments_5`, `comments_6`, `comments_7`, `comments_8`, `comments_9`, `comments_10`, `comments_11` fields, respectively.
15. Indicate whether you agree or disagree with the statement in the `agree_disagree` field.
16. Repeat the process until you have filled out all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: Enter the student's name.
* **email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **grade**: Select the student's grade level.
* **who_filled_out**: Indicate who filled out the form (e.g., student, parent, or teacher).
* **reasons_for_leaving**: Provide reasons for leaving the school.
* **thoughts**: Share your thoughts about the school.
* **disappointments**: List any disappointments.
* **recommendations**: Offer recommendations for improvement.
* **name**: Enter the name of the person who filled out the form.
* **email**: Enter the email of the person who filled out the form.
* **completed_on**: Select the date of completion from the calendar.
* **completed_at**: Select the time of completion from the clock.
* **satisfaction_level**: Choose your satisfaction level from the dropdown menu.
* **additional_info**: Provide additional information.
* **agree**: Select whether you agree or disagree with all the statements.
* **comments**: Provide comments and feedback.
* **comments_2**, **comments_3**, **comments_4**, **comments_5**, **comments_6**, **comments_7**, **comments_8**, **comments_9**, **comments_10**, **comments_11**: Provide additional comments and feedback.
* **agree_disagree**: Indicate whether you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **comments_12**, **comments_13**, **comments_14**: Provide more comments and feedback.

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your feedback and comments.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the `reasons_for_leaving` and `disappointments` fields.
* Keep the `additional_info` field concise but informative.
* Ensure that your name and email are accurate and up-to-date.
* Select the correct date and time of completion from the calendars or clock.
* Choose the correct satisfaction level from the dropdown menu.
* Use the `agree` field to express your overall opinion.
* Use the `comments` fields to elaborate on your thoughts and opinions.
