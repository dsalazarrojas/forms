# what_if_101_idea_journal_2021_2022 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The what_if_101_idea_journal_2021_2022 form is designed to help you keep track of your ideas and projects in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your idea or project in the "Idea Journal Title" field.
2. Select the date and time when you conceived or started working on your idea.
3. Enter a description of your idea or project in the "Idea Journal Idea" field.
4. Choose the type of project you are working on (e.g., School Project, Class Project, Personal Project, or Other).
5. Select all the statuses that apply to your idea or project from the list (e.g., New, Active, On Hold, Archived).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Idea Journal Title**: <title> (Text, Required): Enter a short and descriptive title for your idea or project.
* **Idea Journal Date**: <date> (Date, Required): Select the date when you started working on or conceived your idea.
* **Idea Journal Time**: <time> (Time, Optional): If applicable, enter the time of day when you started working on or conceived your idea.
* **Idea Journal Idea**: <idea> (Text, Required): Describe your idea or project in detail, including any relevant notes or details.
* **Idea Journal Type**: <type> (Select One, Required): Choose the type of project you are working on (e.g., School Project, Class Project, Personal Project, Other).
* **Idea Journal Status**: <status> (Select Multiple, Required): Select all the statuses that apply to your idea or project (e.g., New, Active, On Hold, Archived).
