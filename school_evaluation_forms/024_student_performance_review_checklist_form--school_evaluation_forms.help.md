# Student Performance Review Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a structured way to evaluate student performance during the school term. It aims to capture relevant information about the student, their school term, and their performance in various areas. The goal is to help teachers and evaluators conduct a thorough and fair assessment of the student's progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the school term the student was in during the evaluation period.
3. Choose the date of the evaluation from the calendar.
4. Select the correct evaluation date.
5. Choose the student's level of performance in each relevant area (High, Middle, or College).
6. Select the specific student levels that apply to the areas where you have marked "High" performance.
7. Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the evaluation was conducted by a counselor.
8. Select the name of the counselor who conducted the evaluation.
9. Describe the student's performance in the "Student Performance" field.
10. List the student's strengths and areas of improvement in the "Strengths Areas" and "Areas For Improvement" fields.
11. Add any comments or recommendations in the "Comments" and "Recommendations" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name as it appears on their school record.
* **School Term** (`school_term`, text, required/optional): Select the school term the student was in during the evaluation period.
* **Date Of Evaluation** (`date_of_evaluation`, date, required/optional): Choose the date of the evaluation from the calendar.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the evaluation.
* **Student Level** (`student_level`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the level of performance in each relevant area (High, Middle, or College).
* **Select All That Apply** (`student_level_options`, select_one, required/optional): Select the specific student levels that apply to the areas where you have marked "High" performance.
* **Evaluation Counselor** (`evaluation_counselor`, select_one, required/optional): Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the evaluation was conducted by a counselor.
* **Evaluation Counselor** (`evaluation_counselor`, select_one, required/optional): Select the name of the counselor who conducted the evaluation.
* **Student Performance** (`student_performance`, text, required/optional): Describe the student's performance in this area.
* **Strengths Areas** (`strengths_areas`, text, required/optional): List the student's strengths and areas of improvement.
* **Areas For Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required/optional): List the areas where the student needs improvement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Add any comments or feedback about the student's performance.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required/optional): Add any recommendations for the student.
