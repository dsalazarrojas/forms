# Educational Sector Indicator Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and evaluate indicators for educational institutions. It gathers information about schools, including their type, address, and other relevant details. This form can be used by evaluators or administrators to monitor and analyze performance metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the text boxes provided with relevant information about the school.
2. Select the type of school from the drop-down menu.
3. Submit the form to send in your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Information 1 (school_info)**: This field is used to enter the name or description of the school.
* **School Type (school_type)**: Select the type of school from the provided options: Government, Private, or Public.
* **School Head (school_head)**: Enter the name or title of the school head or administrator.
* **School Address (school_address)**: Enter the address of the school.
* **School Contact (school_contact)**: Enter the contact information of the school.
* **School Information 2-20 (school_info_2-school_info_20)**: These fields are used to enter additional information about the school.
* **Submit**: Once all fields are filled, click this button to send in your evaluation.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all fields truthfully and accurately.
- If you need more space to enter information, consider breaking up long answers into multiple fields.
- This form is designed to collect data, so be clear and concise in your input.
