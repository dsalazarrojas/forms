# School Safety Plan Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess and document the school's safety plan, which is a critical component of the school's overall emergency response and crisis management strategy. This form helps school administrators and staff to identify potential risks and vulnerabilities, and to develop and refine their safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the school's current safety protocols and procedures, and consider potential risks and vulnerabilities.
2. Choose one or more of the safety protocols to select from the options listed in the "safety protocol" field.
3. Provide information on the school's emergency contact person or team in the "emergency contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Safety Plan** (`school_safety_plan`, `text`, not required): This field is for you to provide a written description of the school's safety plan, including any relevant details and protocols in place to ensure student and staff safety.
* **Safety Protocol** (`safety_protocol`, `select_one`, not required): This field is to select one or more of the safety protocols that the school has in place, such as Emergency Response Plan, Lockdown Procedure, Fire Evacuation Plan, or First Aid Kit Availability. You can choose one or more of the options that best describe the school's safety protocols.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, not required): This field is for you to provide information on the school's emergency contact person or team, including their name(s), title(s), phone number(s), and any other relevant contact information.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and update the form regularly to ensure that the school's safety plan remains current and effective.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of the school's safety plan, please consult with the school administration or relevant authorities for guidance.
- This form should be completed by authorized school personnel only.
