# Student Daily Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Daily Feedback Form is a tool used by educators to track student progress and receive feedback from students on a daily basis. This form helps identify areas of improvement and provides valuable insights for educators to make informed decisions about student development.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Student ID

* **Student Id** (`student_id`, `number`, required): Enter your student ID as assigned by your school or institution. This is a unique identifier for your student profile.

### Step 2: Date of Feedback

* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of the feedback you are providing. If you are unsure, select the current date.

### Step 3: Subject of Feedback

* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required): Describe the subject of your feedback, such as a specific lesson or activity.

### Step 4: Answer Question 1

* **Question 1** (`question1`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or multiple answers to the question: "Did you understand the material?"
* **Question 1 Answer** (`question1_answer`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your answer to Question 1.

### Step 5: Answer Question 2

* **Question 2** (`question2`, `select_one`, optional): Select one answer to the question: "Was the activity engaging?"
* **Question 2 Answer** (`question2_answer`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your answer to Question 2.

### Step 6: Answer Question 3

* **Question 3** (`question3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or multiple answers to the question: "Will you apply the concepts learned in this lesson?"
* **Question 3 Answer** (`question3_answer`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your answer to Question 3.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your feedback to help educators provide the best support for your learning journey.
* Try to answer each question as thoroughly as possible, and provide examples or explanations where applicable.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your educator for clarification.

Note: The fields that are marked as "optional" can be skipped if you are unsure or do not have an answer. However, providing answers will provide more valuable feedback to your educators.
