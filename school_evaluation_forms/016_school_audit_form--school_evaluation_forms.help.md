# School Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Audit Form is designed to help schools evaluate their internal operations and identify areas for improvement. It collects information on various aspects of school functioning, from operational effectiveness to continuous improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form's pages one by one.
2. For each page, enter your thoughts and comments in the text fields provided.
3. You can fill out the form as many times as you need, and it is recommended to save your progress and come back to it later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School**: This field is used to enter the name or identifier of the school or educational institution being audited.
* **Operational Effectiveness**: This field is used to assess the school's operational efficiency and performance. Please provide comments and feedback on how well the school is running its day-to-day activities.
* **Gather Feedback**: This field is used to collect thoughts and suggestions on how the school can gather more efficient and effective feedback from students, teachers, and the community.
* **Continuous Improvement**: This field is used to identify areas where the school can improve and grow. Please provide suggestions and ideas for ongoing enhancement and development.
* **School Evaluation Forms**: This field is used to evaluate the school's evaluation forms and processes. Please provide feedback on how the forms can be improved and made more efficient.
