# Homework Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record and track student homework information, including titles, due dates, completion status, grades, and contact details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
- Provide the homework title in the "Homework Title" field.
- Enter the due date of the homework in the "Due Date" field.
- Indicate the completion status of the homework by selecting either "Yes" or "No" in the "Completed" field.
- Enter the student's score for the homework in the "Score" field.
- If applicable, enter the grade received for the homework in the "Grade" field.
- Add any additional notes about the homework in the "Notes" field.
- Enter the student's email address in the "Email Address" field.
- Enter the student's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
- Identify the tool assigned to the student for the homework in the "Assigned Tool" field.
- If a specific tool is assigned, select it from the options. Otherwise, enter a note to indicate the assigned tool.
- Enter any additional details about the tool assigned in the "User ID" field. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (student_name): The name of the student who completed the homework.
* **Homework Title** (homework_title): The title or description of the homework assignment.
* **Due Date** (due_date): The date by which the homework was due.
* **Completed** (completed): Indicates if the homework was completed ("Yes") or not completed ("No").
* **Score** (score): The score or points earned by the student for the homework.
* **Grade** (grade): The grade received by the student for the homework.
* **Notes** (notes): Additional comments or notes about the homework.
* **Email Address** (email_address): The email address of the student or contact person.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number): The phone number of the student or contact person.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool): The tool or resource assigned to the student for the homework, such as a specific software or platform.
* **User ID** (user_id): A unique identifier for the user or contact person.
* **User Name** (user_name): The name of the user or contact person.
* **User Email** (user_email): The email address of the user or contact person.
* **User Phone** (user_phone): The phone number of the user or contact person.
* **School Name** (school_name): The name of the school or institution.
* **School ID** (school_id): A unique identifier for the school or institution.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool_id): The tool or resource assigned to the student for the homework.
