# English Level Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The English Level Assessment Form is used to assess a student's English language skills, evaluate their progress, and monitor their improvement over time. This form is typically completed by teachers to evaluate a student's English level, providing valuable feedback and guidance for further development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2.  Choose the correct English level for the student from the provided options.
3.  Record the date of the assessment.
4.  Score the student's English language skills.
5.  Provide any necessary remarks or comments regarding the assessment.
6.  Select the assigned teacher for the student.
7.  Enter the school name and department name (if applicable).
8.  Choose the assessment tool and method used.
9.  Specify the assessment frequency (if applicable).
10.  Record the submitted date and status of the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as per the school records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address for further communication.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Record the student's date of birth for demographic details.
* **English Level** (`english_level`, select_one, required): Select the student's current English language level from the provided options.
*   *Option 1*: Choose this for the beginner level students.
*   *Option 2*: Choose this for students with moderate proficiency.
*   *Option 3*: Choose this for students with advanced proficiency.
*   *Option 4*: Choose this for students with near-native proficiency.
*   *Option 5*: Choose this for students with native proficiency.
*   *Option 6*: Choose this for students with superior proficiency.
*   *Option 7*: Choose this for students with exceptional proficiency.
*   *Option 8*: Choose this for students who require extra support.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Record the date of the assessment.
* **Score** (`score`, number, required): Enter the student's score based on their English language skills.
* **Score** is a numerical value indicating the student's proficiency level.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, required): Provide any necessary remarks or comments regarding the assessment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments for the teacher or student.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required): Select the teacher who has assigned the assessment.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the person who submitted the assessment (if different from the assigned teacher).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Record the date the assessment was submitted.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher who conducted the assessment.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Record the name of the student's school.
*   *Note: This is the name of the school where the student is studying.*
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the school.
*   *Note: This is the location of the school where the student is studying.*
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, optional): Enter the department name (if applicable).
*   *Note: This is the department where the student studies.*
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the teacher who conducted the assessment.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Record the department name where the student is studying.
* **Student Level** (`student_level`, number, required): Enter the student's level (e.g., 1-10).
*   *Note: This field is used to indicate the student's level.*
* **Assessment Tool** (`assessment_tool`, text, required): Choose the tool used for assessment.
*   *Note: This could be a software or a manual assessment method.*
* **Assessment Method** (`assessment_method`, text, required): Choose the method of assessment.
*   *Note: This could be an oral or written test.*
* **Assessment Frequency** (`assessment_frequency`, text, optional): Record the frequency of the assessment.
*   *Note: This is the number of times the student is assessed each week or month.*
* **Remarks Frequency** (`remarks_frequency`, text, optional): Record the frequency of the remarks.
*   *Note: This is the number of times the teacher provides remarks each week or month.*
* **Submitted Frequency** (`submitted_frequency`, text, optional): Record the frequency of submission.
*   *Note: This is the number of times the student submits their work each week or month.*
* **Assessment Status** (`assessment_status`, text, required): Record the status of the assessment.
*   *Note: This could be "Completed," "Ongoing," or "Incomplete."*
* **Assessment Result** (`assessment_result`, text, required): Record the result of the assessment.
*   *Note: This could be "Pass," "Fail," or "Remedial."*
