# First Day Of School Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The First Day of School Questionnaire is a form designed to collect demographic and educational information about students and their teachers on the first day of school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your previous school information in the "Previous School" field.
3. Select your grade level from the "Grade Level" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your family background information in the "Family Background" field.
5. List your interests and hobbies in the "Interests and Hobbies" field.
6. Enter your previous year in school in the "Previous Year in School" field.
7. List your family members in the "Family Members" field.
8. Select your previous year grade level from the "Previous Year Grade Level" dropdown menu.
9. Select your first language from the "First Language" dropdown menu.
10. Select your school language from the "School Language" dropdown menu.
11. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
12. Select your school time in the "Time of School" field.
13. Enter your email (optional).
14. Enter your phone number (optional).
15. Enter your teacher name.
16. List your student interests in the "Student Interests" field.
17. List your teacher interests in the "Teacher Interests" field.
18. Select your student language(s) from the "Student Language" dropdown menu.
19. Select your teacher language(s) from the "Teacher Language" dropdown menu.
20. Select your contact preference (home, school, or other) for both you and your teacher.
21. Enter any additional notes in the "Student Notes", "Teacher Notes", and "School Notes" fields.
22. If needed, enter any other notes in the "Other Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Previous School** (`previous_school`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your previous school.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current grade level.
* **Family Background** (`family_background`, `text`, required): Enter your family background information.
* **Interests and Hobbies** (`interests_and_hobbies`, `text`, required): List your interests and hobbies.
* **Previous Year in School** (`previous_year_in_school`, `number`, required): Enter the year you started school.
* **Family Members** (`family_members`, `text`, required): List your family members.
* **Previous Year Grade Level** (`previous_year_grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your previous year's grade level.
* **First Language** (`first_language`, `select_one`, required): Select your first language.
* **School Language** (`school_language`, `select_one`, required): Select your school language.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of School** (`time_of_school`, `time`, required): Select your school time.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, `text`, required): List your interests.
* **Teacher Interests** (`teacher_interests`, `text`, required): List your teacher's interests.
* **Student Language** (`student_language`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your language(s).
* **Teacher Language** (`teacher_language`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your language(s).
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your contact preference.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your teacher's contact preference.
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any notes about you.
* **Teacher Notes** (`teacher_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any notes about your teacher.
* **School Notes** (`school_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any notes about your school.
* **Other Notes** (`other_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any other notes.

## Tips
* Be accurate and detailed when filling in the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your teacher or school administration for guidance.
* If you need help with completing the form, contact your teacher or school administration.
