# parent_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for parents to give their feedback on the school or institution they are involved with. It is a way for the school to receive feedback and suggestions from parents to improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the "Parent Name" field with your name.
2. In the "Parent Feedback" field, type any comments or suggestions you have about the school or institution.
3. For the "Rating" field, select a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your satisfaction level with the school or institution.
4. In the "Select One" field, choose either "Yes" or "No" to answer a question.
5. In the "Select Multiple" field, choose one or more of the options (Yes, No) to answer a question.
6. If you have a specific date you would like to reference, enter it in the "Date" field.
7. For the "Time" field, enter the time of day you would like to reference.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field if you would like to be contacted back.
9. If you would like to add a note to your feedback, enter it in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Feedback** (`parent_feedback`, text, optional): Type any comments or suggestions you have about the school or institution.
  - This field is a text field where you can enter any comments or suggestions you have about the school or institution.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a parent.
  - This field is for you to identify yourself as a parent who is giving feedback.
- **Feedback Message** (`feedback_message`, text, optional): Type any additional comments or information about your feedback.
  - This field is for you to provide more information or details about your feedback.
- **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Select a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your satisfaction level with the school or institution.
  - This field is for you to rate your satisfaction level with the school or institution on a scale of 1-5.
- **Select One** (`select_one_1`, select_one, optional): Choose either "Yes" or "No" to answer a question.
  - This field is for you to answer a question and choose one of the options.
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple_1`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options (Yes, No).
  - This field is for you to answer a question and choose multiple options.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter a specific date you would like to reference.
  - This field is for you to enter a specific date you would like to reference in your feedback.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of day you would like to reference.
  - This field is for you to enter the time of day you would like to reference in your feedback.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted back.
  - This field is for you to enter your email address so the school or institution can contact you back.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted back.
  - This field is for you to enter your phone number so the school or institution can contact you back.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Type any additional notes or comments you have.
  - This field is for you to add any additional notes or comments to your feedback.
