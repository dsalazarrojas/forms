## Purpose
The Preschool Teacher Performance Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the performance of preschool teachers, providing a framework for school administrators to evaluate the teacher's skills and areas of improvement. This form helps to ensure that teachers are meeting the expected standards of their role and provides a basis for professional development and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
2.  Select the evaluation date in the "Evaluation Date" field.
3.  Enter the duration of the evaluation in the "Evaluation Time" field.
4.  Enter the teacher's ID number in the "Teacher ID" field.
5.  Select the school administrator who will be evaluating the teacher in the "School Administrator" field.
6.  Provide feedback from who the evaluation will be based on in the "Feedback From" field.
7.  Choose the areas of evaluation for "Evaluation Area 1".
8.  Select multiple evaluation areas for "Evaluation Area 2" as applicable.
9.  Provide comments about the teacher's performance in the "Comments" field.
10.  Select the overall performance of the teacher for the evaluation period in the "Overall Performance" field.
11.  Enter the date of the evaluation (if it's a re-evaluation or follow-up evaluation) in the "Evaluation Date 2" field.
12.  Enter the duration of the evaluation (if it's a re-evaluation or follow-up evaluation) in the "Evaluation Time 2" field.
13.  Enter the name of the teacher (if it's a re-evaluation or follow-up evaluation) in the "Teacher Name 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the teacher being evaluated.
*   **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation (if it's a one-time evaluation).
*   **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the duration of the evaluation.
*   **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, number, optional): Enter the teacher's ID number.
*   **School Administrator** (`school_administrator`, text, optional): Select the school administrator who will be evaluating the teacher.
*   **Feedback From** (`feedback_from`, text, optional): Provide feedback from who the evaluation will be based on.
*   **Evaluation Area 1** (`evaluation_area_1`, select_one, optional): Choose one area from the list for evaluation: Lesson Planning and Preparation, Classroom Environment, Lesson Delivery, Assessment and Feedback, Childcare, Communication, Teamwork and Team Collaboration, Parent-Teacher Relationship.
*   **Evaluation Area 2** (`evaluation_area_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple areas from the list for evaluation: Classroom Management, Classroom Organisation, Safety and Supervision, Supervising Children, Classroom Decor.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about the teacher's performance.
*   **Overall Performance** (`overall_performance`, select_one, optional): Select the overall performance of the teacher: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
*   **Evaluation Date 2** (`evaluation_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation (if it's a re-evaluation or follow-up evaluation).
*   **Evaluation Time 2** (`evaluation_time_2`, time, optional): Enter the duration of the evaluation (if it's a re-evaluation or follow-up evaluation).
*   **Teacher Name 2** (`teacher_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher (if it's a re-evaluation or follow-up evaluation).
