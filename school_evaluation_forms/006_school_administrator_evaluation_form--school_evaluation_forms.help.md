# School Administrator Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate the performance of a school administrator, assessing their overall performance, communication skills, leadership skills, teamwork, professionalism, decision-making skills, problem-solving skills, time management, adaptability, accountability, parent-teacher relationship, student-administrator relationship, and teacher-administrator relationship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and instructions before proceeding.
2. Answer each question based on your assessment of the administrator's performance over a specific period.
3. Select the most appropriate option for each question, taking into account the administrator's strengths and weaknesses.
4. Provide a clear and concise justification for your answer, if requested.
