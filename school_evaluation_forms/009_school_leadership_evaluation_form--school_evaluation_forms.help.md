# School Leadership Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance and competencies of school leaders and provide feedback for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and data required to complete the form.
2.  Follow the instructions and guidelines provided in this help guide.
3.  Answer each question accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **School Leader Title** (`14`, `text`, `required`): Enter the title of the school leader being evaluated.
*   **School Leader Role** (`15`, `text`, `required`): Enter the role or position of the school leader being evaluated.
*   **Evaluation Period** (`13`, `number`, `required`): Enter the time frame for the evaluation period.
*   **Evaluation Date** (`16`, `select_one`, `required`: Enter the date of the evaluation.
*   **Evaluation Time** (`17`, `time`, `required`): Enter the time of the evaluation.
*   **School Leader Email** (`18`, `email`, `required`): Enter the email address of the school leader being evaluated.
*   **School Leader Phone** (`19`, `text`, `required`): Enter the contact phone number of the school leader.
*   **Evaluation Report Sender** (`24`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the sender of the evaluation report.
*   **Form Submitted By** (`25`, `text`, `required`): Enter your name and position.
*   **Leader Performance** (`2`, `select_one`, `not required`): Select the performance aspect of the school leader being evaluated (Leader Development, Effective Communication, Problem Solving, Decision Making, Vision and Goals).
*   **Leader Competencies** (`4`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the competencies of the school leader being evaluated (Leadership, Communication, Problem Solving, Vision and Goals, Interpersonal Skills).
*   **Evaluation Method** (`5`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the evaluation methodology (360 Degree Feedback, Feedback Surveys, Interviews, Focus Groups).
*   **Evaluation Ratings** (`6`, `number`, `required`): Enter the evaluation ratings.
*   **Evaluation Comments** (`7`, `note`, `not required`): Enter any additional comments for the evaluation.
*   **Follow-up Plan** (`8`, `time`, `not required`): Enter any follow-up plans or timelines.
*   **Action Plan** (`9`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the action plan to be implemented (Implement, Review, Refine).
*   **Evaluation Report** (`10`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Select the recipients of the evaluation report (Report to school leader, Report to school leader and HR, Report to school leader, HR and other stakeholders).
*   **Leader Performance Period** (`12`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the performance period of the school leader (6 months, 1 year, 2 years, 3 years, 5 years).
*   **Next Evaluation** (`23`, `date`, `not required`): Enter the date for the next evaluation.
