# School Nurse Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Nurse Performance Evaluation Form is a tool for assessing the performance of school nurses, with the ultimate goal of providing them with feedback and recommendations for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate fields with the correct information and ratings.
2. Provide detailed comments and suggestions for areas of improvement.
3. Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Evaluation Form (Page 1)
* **School Name** (`school_name`, required): Enter the name of the school where the school nurse works.
* **School Nurse Name** (`school_nurse_name`, required): Enter the name of the school nurse being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, required): Rate the school nurse's performance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Comments** (`comments`, required): Provide any comments or feedback on the school nurse's performance.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, required): Select areas where the school nurse needs improvement. Options include:
	+ Academic Program
	+ Communication
	+ Community Involvement
	+ Curriculum
	+ Data Analysis
	+ Data Interpretation
	+ Data Use
	+ Instructional Planning
	+ Interpersonal and Communication Skills
	+ Leadership
	+ Learning Environment
	+ Management
	+ Parent Communication
	+ Program Evaluation
	+ Program Planning
	+ Student Support

### School Nurse Evaluation
* **School Nurse Rating** (`school_nurse_rating`, required): Rate the school nurse's performance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **School Nurse Comments** (`school_nurse_comments`, required): Provide any comments or feedback on the school nurse's performance.
* **School Nurse Areas for Improvement** (`school_nurse_areas_for_improvement`, required): Select areas where the school nurse needs improvement. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **School Nurse Recommended Actions** (`school_nurse_recommended_actions`, required): Select recommended actions for the school nurse's improvement. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **School Nurse Submission Date** (`school_nurse_submission_date`, required): Enter the date of submission for the school nurse's evaluation.
* **School Nurse Submitter** (`school_nurse_submitter`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

* **School Principal Comments** (`school_principal_comments`, required): Provide any comments or feedback on the school principal's performance.
* **School Principal Areas for Improvement** (`school_principal_areas_for_improvement`, required): Select areas where the school principal needs improvement. Options include:

	+ Yes

* **School Principal Submission Date** (`school_principal_submission_date`, required): Enter the date of submission for the school principal's evaluation.
* **School Principal Submitter** (`school_principal_submitter`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

### Teacher Evaluation
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, required): Rate the teacher's performance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, required): Provide any comments or feedback on the teacher's performance.
* **Teacher Areas for Improvement** (`teacher_areas_for_improvement`, required): Select areas where the teacher needs improvement. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

### Additional Tips
* Please be thorough and accurate when filling out this form.
* Provide specific examples or instances where the school nurse, school principal, or teacher demonstrated good or bad performance.
* Use the areas for improvement and recommended actions to provide constructive feedback and support for growth and development.
* Submit the form by the required date to ensure that it is reviewed and acted upon by the school administration.
