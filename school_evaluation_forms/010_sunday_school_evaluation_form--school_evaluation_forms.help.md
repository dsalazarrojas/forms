# Sunday School Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sunday School Evaluation Form is a tool to gather data on the student's experience and performance in their Sunday school class.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. If you are a student, please provide your feedback and thoughts on your experience in the class.
3. If you are a parent, please provide feedback on your child's experience and performance in the class.
4. Be as detailed as possible in your responses, but keep your answers concise and relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required: false): Please enter your ID number as provided by your school.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your teacher's name.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of your class.
* **Lesson Plan** (`lesson_plan`, note, required: false): This is a text area for you to describe any lesson plans or topics covered in class.
* **Feedback from Student** (`feedback_from_student`, text, required: false): Please provide any feedback or thoughts you have on your experience in the class.
* **Feedback from Parent** (`feedback_from_parent`, text, required: false): Please provide any feedback or thoughts you have on your child's experience and performance in the class.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the class, using a number from 1 to 10.
* **Suggestion for Improvement** (`suggestion_for_improvement`, text, required: false): Please provide any suggestions you have for improving the class.
* **Teacher Availability** (`teacher_availability`, time, required: false): Please enter the teacher's availability in the format HH:MM.
* **Class Time** (`class_time`, number, required: false): Please enter the duration of the class, in minutes.
* **Student Absent Days** (`student_absent_days`, number, required: false): Please enter the number of days you have been absent from class.
