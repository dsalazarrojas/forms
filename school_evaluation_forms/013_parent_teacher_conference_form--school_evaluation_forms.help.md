# Parent Teacher Conference Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parent Teacher Conference Form is a tool designed to facilitate open communication between parents and teachers during parent-teacher conferences. It helps to gather information from parents about a student's progress, behavior, and attendance, while also providing teachers with a space to share their own observations and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling out the form**: Begin by entering the student's name, parent's name, and other required information.
2. **Choose the student's attendance status**: Select the student's attendance status for the conference.
3. **Share your comments and observations**: Fill out the text fields for parent comments and teacher comments, providing any relevant information or feedback you'd like to share.
4. **Record any notes**: Take notes on the student's progress, behavior, and attendance.
5. **Review and update**: Review the form and update any necessary fields before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the parent's name.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the conference.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the conference.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your name as the teacher.
* **Student Attendance**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ **Student**: The student attended school on the specified date.
	+ **Absent**: The student was not present in school on the specified date.
	+ **Late**: The student was late to school on the specified date.
* **Parent Comments**: Share any comments or concerns you'd like to discuss with the parent.
* **Teacher Comments**: Share any comments or feedback you'd like to provide to the parent.
* **Student Notes**: Record any additional notes or observations about the student's progress, behavior, or attendance.
* **Teacher Notes**: Record any additional notes or observations about the student's progress, behavior, or attendance.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is included.
* Be clear and concise in your comments and observations.
* Use the "Student Notes" and "Teacher Notes" fields to record any additional information that may not fit in the other fields.
* Consider attaching any relevant documents or materials to support your comments and observations.
