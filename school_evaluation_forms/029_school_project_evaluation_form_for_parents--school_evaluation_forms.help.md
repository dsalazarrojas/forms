# School Project Evaluation Form For Parents - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Project Evaluation Form for Parents is a tool used to collect feedback and ratings from parents about a specific project undertaken by the school. This form helps the school administration to assess the project's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the project's details and evaluate it based on your understanding and experiences.
2.  Fill in the form with your honest feedback and ratings.
3.  Provide specific comments on the project in the "Comments" section.
4.  Answer the question about whether the project will be implemented, and if not, choose the reason(s) why.
5.  Enter your contact information, including email and phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Overall Rating** (Required): This field requires you to rate the project based on your overall satisfaction and experience. Please select a number that best represents your rating.
  - Please note that the Overall Rating is a subjective measure and reflects your personal experience with the project.

  - **Parent Feedback** (Optional): This field is where you can provide detailed feedback about the project, its strengths, and weaknesses.
  - Please keep your feedback concise and specific, focusing on the project's aspects that impressed or disappointed you.

  - **Comments** (Optional): This field allows you to provide additional comments or insights about the project that may not have been captured in the "Parent Feedback" section.
  - Please use this space to offer more information or clarification about your feedback, if needed.

  - **Will the project be implemented?** (Required): Please indicate if the project will be implemented in its entirety or partially.
  - If the project will not be implemented, choose the reason(s) why from the available options.

  - **Why was the project not implemented?** (Optional): If the project was not implemented, please select all the reasons that apply from the given options.
  - Please be as specific as possible when providing reasons, using the available options as a guide.

  - **Email** (Optional): Enter your email address so that the school administration can contact you for further communication.
  - Please ensure that your email is entered correctly to facilitate communication.

  - **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number for easier contact with the school administration.
  - Please be aware that entering your phone number is optional and will only be used for communication purposes.

## Tips

- Please be honest and thorough in your feedback and ratings.
- Keep your comments and feedback specific, concise, and clear.
- If you have any questions or need clarification about the form, contact the school administration.
