# Event Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture essential information for event photography services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Details** section with the event's title, date, time, and location.
2. Enter the client's **Name** and contact information in the respective fields.
3. Select the services offered by the photographer from the provided options in the **Services** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
* **Event Date**: Enter the event's date.
* **Event Time**: Enter the event's time.
* **Location**: Enter the event's location.
* **Photographer Details**: Enter the photographer's contact information, if applicable.
* **Photographer Name**: Enter the photographer's name, if applicable.
* **Photographer Email**: Enter the photographer's email address, if applicable.
* **Photographer Phone**: Enter the photographer's phone number, if applicable.
* **Services**: Select the services offered by the photographer, such as:
	+ Option 1: Service 1
	+ Option 2: Service 2
	+ Option 3: Service 3
	+ Option 4: Service 4

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate event information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event coordinator or photographer for assistance.
- Review the form carefully to ensure all services are accurately selected.
