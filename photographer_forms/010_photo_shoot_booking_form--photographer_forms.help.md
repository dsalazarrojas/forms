# Photo Shoot Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help photographers manage bookings for photo shoots. It captures essential information from clients, including their contact details, booking preferences, and payment options. The form is used to streamline the booking process, ensuring that all necessary information is collected in one place.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form ID** (field 1) with a unique identifier for each booking.
2. Enter the **Photographer**'s name (field 2) who will be shooting the photos.
3. Provide the **Client Name** (field 3) and their **Contact E-mail** (field 4) to ensure communication is established.
4. Enter the **Contact Phone** (field 5) number for easy communication.
5. Select a **Date** (field 6) for the photo shoot from the calendar.
6. Choose a **Start Time** (field 7) for the shoot.
7. Determine the **Duration** (field 8) of the shoot in hours.
8. Choose the **Photo Shoot Type** (field 9) as **outdoor**, **indoor**, or **studio**.
9. Enter the **Photo Shoot Date** (field 10) for the actual photo shoot.
10. Set the **Photo Shoot Duration** (field 11) in hours.
11. Provide the **Photo Shoot Location** (field 12) where the shoot will take place.
12. Add any **Photo Shoot Notes** (field 13) for additional information.
13. Input the **Photo Shoot Price** (field 14) for the session.
14. Select a **Payment Method** (field 15) such as **card**, **bank transfer**, **paypals**, or **stripe**.
15. Enter the **Payment Amount** (field 16) for the booking.
16. Optional fields (fields 17-21) are for internal management purposes and are not required for client-facing submissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID** (field 1): A unique identifier for each booking.
* **Photographer** (field 2): The name of the photographer assigned to the shoot.
* **Client Name** (field 3): The name of the client booking the shoot.
* **Contact E-mail** (field 4): The email address of the client for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (field 5): The phone number of the client for easy communication.
* **Date** (field 6): Select a date for the photo shoot from the calendar.
* **Start Time** (field 7): Choose the start time for the photo shoot.
* **Duration** (field 8): The length of the photo shoot in hours.
* **Photo Shoot Type** (field 9): The type of photo shoot (outdoor, indoor, or studio).
* **Photo Shoot Date** (field 10): The actual date of the photo shoot.
* **Photo Shoot Duration** (field 11): The length of the photo shoot in hours.
* **Photo Shoot Location** (field 12): The location where the photo shoot will take place.
* **Photo Shoot Notes** (field 13): Additional information for the photo shoot.
* **Photo Shoot Price** (field 14): The price of the photo shoot session.
* **Payment Method** (field 15): Choose the payment method for the booking (card, bank transfer, paypals, or stripe).
* **Payment Amount** (field 16): The amount to be paid for the booking.
* **Form Submitted** (field 17): Timestamp when the form was submitted (internal use).
* **Submitted By** (field 18): The user who submitted the form (internal use).
* **Submitted At** (field 19): Timestamp when the form was submitted (internal use).
* **Updated By** (field 20): The user who last updated the form (internal use).
* **Updated At** (field 21): Timestamp when the form was last updated (internal use).
* **Message to Photographer** (field 22): A message for the photographer (internal use).
* **Message to Client** (field 23): A message for the client (internal use).
* **Booking Time** (field 24): The time the booking was made (internal use).
* **Photo Shoot Booking Time** (field 25): The time for the photo shoot (internal use).

Please note that some fields marked as "required: false" are not visible to the end user as they are for internal management purposes.
