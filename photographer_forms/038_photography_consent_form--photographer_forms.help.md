# photography_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography_consent_form is used to gather necessary information from photographers for events, shoots, or exhibitions. Please read each section carefully and fill out the form accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and fill out each section according to the instructions.
2. Fill out the "Photographer Consent" section with a brief description or statement of your agreement with the event organizer or exhibition director.
3. Select a valid "Release Date" for your photography work.
4. If you have any details regarding your photography work, such as your portfolio, social media links, or contact information, fill out the "Photographer Details" section.
5. Indicate your availability for contact by filling out the "Photographer Contact" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer Consent** (`photographer_consent`, text, optional): A brief description or statement of your agreement with the event organizer or exhibition director.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): A valid date for your photography work to be released or published.
* **Photographer Details** (`photographer_details`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have any details regarding your photography work, such as your portfolio, social media links, or contact information.
* **Photographer Contact** (`photographer_contact`, text, optional): A way for the event organizer or exhibition director to contact you for any further questions or inquiries.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask the event organizer or exhibition director.
* This form is a one-time submission, please do not fill it out multiple times.
* The event organizer or exhibition director will review and verify your submission before proceeding with further collaboration.
