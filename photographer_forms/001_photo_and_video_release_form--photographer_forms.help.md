# Photo And Video Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo and Video Release Form is used to collect information about a photo or video shoot for administrative or organizational purposes. This form helps in ensuring that the required information is captured accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. **Enter the Client's Name**: In the "Client's Name" field, type the name of the person or entity that commissioned the photo or video shoot.
2. **Enter the Client's Email and Contact Phone Number (Optional)**: If you'd like to ensure we can contact the client, provide their email address and contact phone number in the corresponding fields. This is optional but helps us keep the client informed.
3. **Specify the Photo Location**: In the "Photo Location" field, provide the location where the photo was taken.
4. **Specify the Date and Time of the Photo**: In the "Date of the Photo" and "Time of the Photo" fields, provide the exact date and time the photo was taken.
5. **Describe the Photo or Video**: In the "Description of the Photo or Video" field, provide a brief description of the shoot's content.
6. **Specify the Intended Use**: In the "Intended Use of the Material" field, specify how the photo or video will be used. This helps us ensure that we're capturing the image or video for the right purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Client's Name**: Enter the name of the person or entity that commissioned the photo or video shoot.
  • Type: Text
  • Optional: No (required)

- **Email**: Provide the client's email address for easier contact.
  • Type: Email
  • Optional: No (required)

- **Contact Phone Number**: If available, provide the client's contact phone number.
  • Type: Text
  • Optional: Yes

- **Photo Location**: Specify the location where the photo was taken.
  • Type: Text
  • Optional: No (required)

- **Date of the Photo**: Enter the date of the photo.
  • Type: Date
  • Optional: No (required)

- **Time of the Photo**: Enter the time of the photo.
  • Type: Time
  • Optional: No (required)

- **Description of the Photo or Video**: Describe the content of the shoot.
  • Type: Text
  • Optional: No (required)

- **Intended Use**: Specify how the photo or video will be used.
  • Type: Text
  • Optional: No (required)
