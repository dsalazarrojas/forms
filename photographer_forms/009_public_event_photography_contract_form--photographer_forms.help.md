# public_event_photography_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for public event photography contracts, ensuring both event organizers and photographers are aware of their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (Event Details, Photographer Details).
2. Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to the event in the event_name, event_date, event_location, and event_contact fields.
3. Include your payment terms and requirements in the payment_terms field.
4. Make sure to proofread your responses for clarity and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (Event Details, text, required):
	+ A brief description of the event, including its purpose and any specific details that are relevant to the photographers.
* **event_name** (event_name, text, optional):
	+ The name of the event, if applicable.
* **event_date** (event_date, date, optional):
	+ The date of the event. If you're unsure, leave this field blank for now, and you can update it later if necessary.
* **Photographer Details** (Photographer Details, text, required):
	+ A description of your role as a photographer, your experience, and any relevant credentials.
* **Contract Terms** (Contract Terms, text, optional):
	+ Any specific terms you have for the event, such as restrictions on usage of captured images.
* **Event Location** (event_location, text, optional):
	+ The location of the event.
* **Photographer Contact** (Photographer Contact, text, optional):
	+ Your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Event Contact** (Event Contact, text, optional):
	+ The contact information for the event organizers.
* **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms, text, optional):
	+ Your payment requirements, including any applicable rates or conditions.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
- Keep the text concise and clear for easier reference.
- Proofread for grammar and spelling errors.
- Update any fields that require changes after the contract is signed.
