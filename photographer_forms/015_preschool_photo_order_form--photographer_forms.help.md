# Preschool Photo Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Preschool Photo Order Form is used to gather information from parents about their child's photo session, including whether they will be attending a photo session, their child's name, contact details, and preferences for the photo package and event time. This information helps us prepare for the photo shoot and ensures we capture the best moments for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Preschool Photo Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether your child will be attending a photo session.
2. Provide your child's name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Choose your preferred photo package.
6. Select the event date and time for the photo session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Session** (photo_session, select_one, optional):
    Select 'Yes' if your child will be attending a photo session, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Child Name** (child_name, text, required):
    Enter your child's full name.
* **Parent Name** (parent_name, text, optional):
    Enter your name, as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Email** (email, email, required):
    Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional):
    Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Photo Package** (photo_package, select_multiple, optional):
    Choose the photo package you prefer for your child's photo session.
* **Event Date** (event_date, date, optional):
    Select the date for the photo session.
* **Event Time** (event_time, time, optional):
    Select the time for the photo session.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- We will contact you with further instructions once we receive your completed form.
