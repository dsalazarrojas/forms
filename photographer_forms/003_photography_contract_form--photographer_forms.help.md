# Photography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and establish clear understandings between the photographer and client. It captures key details such as the client's and photographer's information, session terms, and payment expectations. This form serves as a foundation for both parties to agree on and sign off on the session's scope and terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  In the **Photography Session Details** field, enter the session's title, date, time, and location.
2.  In the **Client Details** field, fill in your name and contact information.
3.  In the **Coverage Terms** field, select the desired level of coverage for the session: **Standard**, **Deluxe**, or **Ultimate**. This will determine the scope and services included in the session.
4.  In the **Payment Terms** field, enter the agreed payment terms, including the amount and method of payment.
5.  In the **Contract Terms** field, write any additional terms or conditions specific to the session.
6.  In the **Signature** field, have both the photographer and client sign to confirm agreement on the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Photography Session Details**: Enter the title of the photography session, including the date and time of the session. This field is used to help identify the session and its scope.
- **Client Details**: Fill in your name and contact information, including any preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, or address). This field helps the photographer and client maintain communication and ensures timely updates.
- **Coverage Terms**: This field determines the level of coverage for the session:
  *   **Standard**: Standard coverage includes services such as event or product photography.
  *   **Deluxe**: Deluxe coverage includes additional services such as extended hours, additional images, or travel fees.
  *   **Ultimate**: Ultimate coverage includes comprehensive services, including all previous services and premium services.
- **Payment Terms**: Enter the agreed payment terms, including the amount and method of payment (e.g., check, credit card, or online transfer). This field secures the payment expectations for the session.
- **Contract Terms**: Write any specific terms or conditions unique to the session, such as location restrictions, model releases, or equipment requirements. This field is a chance to add any special considerations or agreements not already covered by the form.
- **Signature**: Have both the photographer and client sign to confirm their agreement on the session's terms and conditions. This is a legally binding agreement that ensures both parties understand their roles and responsibilities.
