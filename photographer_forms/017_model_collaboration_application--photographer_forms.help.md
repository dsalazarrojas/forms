# model_collaboration_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The model collaboration application form is used to collect necessary information for model collaboration. This form is not meant for general contact or inquiry purposes, but rather for specific collaboration requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the Photographer Details form with your name, contact number, and any other relevant information.
2. Next, list out the items in your portfolio that you'd like to feature in our collaboration.
3. Specify your collaboration preferences, including any specific requests or requirements you have.
4. Provide your contact information, including a valid email address and a phone number.
5. Finally, submit the form to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer Details**:
	+ A text field to enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Portfolio Items**:
	+ A text field to list out the items in your portfolio that you'd like to feature in our collaboration.
* **Collaboration Preferences**:
	+ A text field to specify any specific requests or requirements you have.
* **Contact Info**:
	+ A field to enter your email address. Please note that a valid email address is required.
* **Agency Details**:
	+ A text field to provide any relevant information about the agency you represent.
* **Brand Details**:
	+ A text field to provide any relevant information about the brand you collaborate with.
* **Submission**:
	+ A text field for any additional information or submission comments.
* **Phone**:
	+ A text field to enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email**:
	+ A field to enter a valid email address. Please note that a valid email address is required.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out each field, as this will help us get a clear understanding of your collaboration needs.
* If you have any attachments or links to your portfolio, please include them as a separate email or message after submitting this form.
