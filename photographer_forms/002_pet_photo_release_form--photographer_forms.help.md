# Pet Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pet Photo Release Form" is used to obtain consent from pet owners for procedures or activities involving their pet's photos. This form ensures that the pet owner is aware of and agrees to the use of their pet's photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Ensure all required fields (marked with an asterisk \* in the form) are filled out.
3. Review the "Agreement" field to understand the terms and conditions of using your pet's photos.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_name** (Text, Required): Enter your pet's name.
* **owner_name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as the pet owner.
* **date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you are providing this form (if applicable).
* **time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you are providing this form (if applicable).
* **address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **city** (Text, Optional): Enter your city for contact purposes.
* **state** (Text, Optional): Enter your state for contact purposes.
* **zip** (Text, Optional): Enter your zip code for contact purposes.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **email** (Text, Optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **agreement** (Note, Optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of using your pet's photos.
* **signature** (Text, Optional): Sign your name to indicate agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **sign_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you signed the form (if applicable).
* **sign_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you signed the form (if applicable).
* **signed_at** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the form was signed (if applicable).
* **owner_signature** (Text, Optional): Enter your signature as the pet owner.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure the consent is valid.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities or contact us for clarification.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.
* The form is only valid if all required fields are filled out.
