# Seasonal Portrait Special Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Portrait Special Lead Form is used to capture essential information from customers interested in booking a photo shoot or event photography session with your photography business during the seasonal periods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required details and submit the form. You will be contacted to schedule and confirm your photo shoot or event session after reviewing your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer Form Data** (photographer_form_data, text, required/optional: no): This field is not actually used for end users. The field is likely used by the system or internal users for storing or retrieving photographer data. 
* **Name (name, text, required: no)**: Please enter your name as it should be displayed on your portrait.
* **Email (email, email, required: no)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your photo shoot or event session.
* **Phone (phone, text, required: no)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about any last-minute details or to schedule your session.
* **Location (location, text, required: no)**: Tell us about the location where you would like to have the photo shoot or event session.
* **Event (event, text, required: no)**: Briefly describe the event or occasion you want to capture with your photo shoot.
* **Date (date, date, required: no)**: Select a date for your photo shoot or event session.
* **Time (time, time, required: no)**: Select a time for your photo shoot or event session.
* **Photo Session (photo_session, text, required: no)**: Describe any specific details about your photo shoot or event session that we should know about.
* **Follow Up (follow_up, text, required: no)**: If you have any other questions or concerns, or would like to schedule a follow-up call, let us know here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required information accurately.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, don't hesitate to mention them in the "Photo Session" field.
- We will contact you within 24 hours to confirm your submission and schedule your photo shoot or event session.

Please fill out the form carefully, and we will be in touch soon!
