# Photo Waiver Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form captures information related to a photo shoot or event, including client and photographer details, release dates and times, and agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the form's purpose and the information it captures.
2. Complete all required fields accurately and truthfully.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Waiver Release Form** (`1`, text, not required): This field captures the primary waiver release text.
* **Client Name** (`2`, text, not required): Enter the client's name.
* **Release Date** (`3`, date, not required): Record the date when the waiver will be released.
* **Photographer Name** (`4`, text, required): Enter the photographer's name.
* **Location** (`5`, text, not required): Enter the location of the photo shoot.
* **Event Date** (`6`, date, not required): Record the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`7`, time, not required): Record the time of the event.
* **Phone Number** (`8`, text, not required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Email** (`9`, email, not required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Notes** (`10`, note, not required): Enter any additional information related to the photo shoot or event.
* **Photographer Address** (`11`, text, not required): Enter the photographer's address.
* **Event Address** (`12`, text, not required): Enter the address of the event location.
* **Client Agreement** (`13`, select_one, not required): Select "Client Agreed" to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Photographer Agreement** (`14`, select_one, not required): Select "Photographer Agreed" to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Waiver Signed** (`15`, select_multiple, not required): Select "Yes" if you have signed the waiver.
* **Waiver Date** (`16`, date, not required): Record the date the waiver was signed.
* **Waiver Time** (`17`, time, not required): Record the time the waiver was signed.
* **Waiver Note** (`18`, note, not required): Enter any notes related to the waiver.
* **Photographer Fee** (`19`, text, not required): Enter the photographer's fee.
* **Event Fee** (`20`, text, not required): Enter the event fee.
* **Photographer Expense** (`21`, text, not required): Enter the photographer's expenses.
* **Event Expense** (`22`, text, not required): Enter the event expenses.
* **Waiver Total** (`23`, number, not required): Enter the total amount of the waiver.
* **Waiver Paid** (`24`, select_one, not required): Select "Yes" if the waiver has been paid.
* **Photographer Signature** (`25`, note, not required): Sign the form to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
