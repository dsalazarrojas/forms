# Photography Retainer Contract Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Photography Retainer Contract Form is a document used to establish a contract between the client and the photographer for a photography retainer agreement. This form outlines the terms and conditions of the agreement, including the contract price, duration, and services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the client's information, including their name and contact details.
*   Select the contract date and price.
*   Specify the length of the contract.
*   Choose the services to be provided (e.g. Portrait, Event, or Wedding photography).
*   Enter the payment terms and any additional information relevant to the contract.
*   Provide the photographer's information and studio details.
*   Outline the contract terms and any specific conditions or expectations.
*   Add any necessary notes or comments about the contract.
*   Finally, sign and date the contract for both the client and the photographer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information (1)**: This field is for entering the client's name and contact details.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Text
*   **Contract Date (2)**: Enter the date when the contract will start and end.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Date
*   **Contract Price (3)**: Enter the price for the contracted services.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Number
*   **Contract Length (4)**: Enter the length of the contract.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Number
*   **Services (5)**: Select the services to be provided, such as Portrait, Event, or Wedding photography.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Multiple selection
*   **Payment Terms (6)**: Enter any specific payment terms or conditions.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Text
*   **Photographer Information (7)**: Enter the photographer's name, contact details, and studio information.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Text
*   **Studio Information (8)**: Enter any additional information about the studio or photographer's services.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Text
*   **Contract Terms (9)**: Enter any specific terms or conditions for the contract.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Text
*   **Notes (10)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the contract.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Text
*   **Contract Expires (11)**: Enter the date when the contract will expire.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Date
*   **Contract Start Date (12)**: Enter the start date of the contract.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Date
*   **Contract End Date (13)**: Enter the end date of the contract.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Date
*   **Photographer Agreement (14)**: Enter the photographer's agreement or terms.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Text
*   **Client Agreement (15)**: Enter the client's agreement or terms.
    *   Required field: No
    *   Type: Text
*   **Client Signature (16)**: Sign and date the contract for the client.
    *   Required field: Yes
    *   Type: Note
*   **Photographer Signature (17)**: Sign and date the contract for the photographer.
    *   Required field: Yes
    *   Type: Note
