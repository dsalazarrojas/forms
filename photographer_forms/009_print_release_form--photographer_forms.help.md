# Print Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Print Release Form is a document that collects information from the client to ensure their consent and provide necessary details for printing and sharing their photos.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Print Release Form** text field with a brief description of the print request.
2. Enter the **Subject to Print** name in the text field.
3. Provide information about the **Photographer**, including their name.
4. Enter the **Date Taken** of the photo in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/12/2022).
5. Optionally, enter the **Location Taken** where the photo was captured.
6. Check the **Photographer Contact Info** to select whether to share contact information with the client.
7. Fill out the **Client Name** text field with your name or organization's name.
8. Enter the **Client Email** address in the format of name@example.com.
9. Enter the **Client Phone** number in the format of (123) 456-7890.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Print Release Form** (text): A brief description of the print request.
* **Subject to Print** (text): Enter the name of the subject or object being printed.
* **Photographer Info** (text): Information about the photographer, including their name.
* **Subject Release Info** (text): Information about the subject release, including any specific release terms or conditions.
* **Date Taken** (date): Enter the date the photo was taken, in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/12/2022).
* **Location Taken** (text): Optionally, enter the location where the photo was captured.
* **Photographer** (text): Enter the name of the photographer or studio.
* **Contact Info** (select multiple): Select whether to share contact information with the client.
* **Client Name** (text): Enter your name or organization's name.
* **Client Email** (email): Enter the client's email address in the format of name@example.com.
* **Client Phone** (text): Enter the client's phone number in the format of (123) 456-7890.
