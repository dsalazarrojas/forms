# League Photo Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the League Photo Order Form, which is designed for clients to book appointments and order photography services. The form requires clients to provide personal details, appointment information, and preferences for services and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide Personal Details**: Fill in the First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone fields with your correct information.
2. **Schedule an Appointment**: Select the Appointment Date and Time that works best for you.
3. **Choose Photography Packages**: Select the photography packages you are interested in (Yes/No).
4. **Select Services**: Choose the services you would like to have (Yes/No).
5. **Payment Method**: Indicate how you would like to pay (Yes/No).
6. **Specify Location**: Choose the location for the appointment (Yes/No).
7. **Photo Details**: Select the photo size, type, orientation, and quality (Yes/No or Good/Poor).
8. **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (1, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name** (2, text, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email** (3, email, **required**): Enter a valid email address for communication and notifications.
- **Phone** (4, text, **required**): Enter your phone number for contact and appointment reminders.
- **Appointment Time** (5, date, **required**): Choose a date and time for your appointment.
- **Photography Packages** (6, select_one, **required**): Choose the photography packages you are interested in (Yes/No).
- **Appointment Date** (7, date, **required**): Choose the date for your appointment (same as Appointment Time).
- **Photography Services** (8, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the services you would like to have (Yes/No).
- **Note** (9, note, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or notes about your appointment.
- **Email Opt-in** (10, text, **optional**): If you would like to receive promotional emails from us, enter your email address.
- **Appointment Location** (11, select_one, **required**): Choose the location for your appointment (Yes/No).
- **Payment Method** (12, select_multiple, **required**): Indicate how you would like to pay (Yes/No).
- **Note 2** (13, note, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or notes about your appointment.
- **Email Opt In 2** (14, text, **optional**): If you would like to receive promotional emails from us, enter your email address.
- **Appointment Location 2** (15, select_one, **required**): Choose the location for your appointment (Yes/No).
- **Payment Method 2** (16, select_multiple, **required**): Indicate how you would like to pay (Yes/No).
- **Note 3** (17, note, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or notes about your appointment.
- **Email Opt In 3** (18, text, **optional**): If you would like to receive promotional emails from us, enter your email address.
- **Appointment Date 2** (19, date, **required**): Choose a date for your appointment.
- **Photo Size** (20, select_one, **required**): Choose the photo size you are interested in (Yes/No).
- **Photo Type** (21, select_one, **required**): Choose the photo type you are interested in (Yes/No).
- **Photo Location** (22, select_one, **required**): Choose the location of the photo (Yes/No).

- **Photo Quality** (24, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the photo quality (Good/Poor).
- **Photo Notes** (25, note, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or notes about your photo order.
