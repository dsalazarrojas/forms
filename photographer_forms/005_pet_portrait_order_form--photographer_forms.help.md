# Pet Portrait Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Portrait Order Form is used to collect information from pet owners to create a custom portrait of their pet. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary details about your pet and the desired portrait.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. You will need to provide the following information:
- A brief description of your pet.
- Your contact email address.
- Your contact phone number.
- The breed of your pet.
- Whether or not your pet is a type of pet that can be photographed. (e.g. "Yes" for a pet that can be photographed and "No" for a pet that cannot.)
- Whether or not you want a photo of your pet's face. (This is a separate option, as some pets may not have a distinct face.)
- The date and time of the photo shoot. (Please note that the photo shoot will be scheduled based on these details.)
- The size of the portrait you would like. (Choose "Yes" for a larger portrait and "No" for a smaller one.)
- Any additional notes or comments about the portrait order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name** (pet_name): This is the name of your pet. Please provide a brief description of your pet.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email): This is the email address we will use to contact you about the photo shoot and the status of your portrait order.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone): This is the phone number we will use to contact you about the photo shoot and the status of your portrait order.
* **Pet Breed** (pet_breed): This is the breed of your pet. Please select the correct breed from the options provided.
* **Pet Type** (pet_type): This is a check box to indicate whether or not your pet is a type of pet that can be photographed.
* **Pet Photo Type** (pet_photo_type): This is a check box to indicate whether or not you want a photo of your pet's face.
* **Photo Date** (photo_date): This is the date of the photo shoot. We will schedule the shoot based on this date.
* **Photo Time** (photo_time): This is the time of the photo shoot. We will schedule the shoot based on this time.
* **Portrait Size** (pet_photo_size): This is a check box to indicate the size of the portrait you desire. Please select "Yes" for a larger portrait and "No" for a smaller one.
* **Notes** (notes): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about the portrait order.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the email address and phone number you provide are accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any specific requests or requirements for the photo shoot, please include them in the "Notes" field.
- We will contact you with the schedule and details of the photo shoot once we receive this form.
