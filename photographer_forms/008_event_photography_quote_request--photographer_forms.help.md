# event_photography_quote_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a quote request form for event photography services. It will help our team gather all the necessary information to provide an accurate quote for your event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Event Details" field with a brief description of your event.
2. Select the type of event you are planning (Wedding, Party, Corporate Event).
3. Enter the date of the event.
4. Choose the time zone of the event (UTC, EST, CST, MST, PST).
5. Enter the expected number of guests.
6. Provide the location of the event.
7. Enter the name of the photographer(s) you would like to consider for your event.
8. Enter the email address of the photographer(s).
9. Enter the phone number of the photographer(s).
10. Add any additional inquiry details.
11. In the "Quote Request" field, enter the budget you have in mind for the event.
12. Select the services you are interested in (Wedding, Party, Corporate Services).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: A brief description of your event.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are planning (Wedding, Party, Corporate Event).
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time Zone**: Choose the time zone of the event (UTC, EST, CST, MST, PST).
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the expected number of guests.
* **Event Location**: Provide the location of the event.
* **Photographer Name**: Enter the name of the photographer(s) you would like to consider for your event.
* **Photographer Email**: Enter the email address of the photographer(s).
* **Photographer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the photographer(s).
* **Inquiry Details**: Add any additional information about your event.
* **Photographer Message**: Add any comments or messages for the photographer.
* **Follow Up**: Add any follow-up information or questions.
* **Quote Request**: Enter the budget you have in mind for the event.
* **Services Offered**: Select the services you are interested in (Wedding, Party, Corporate Services).
