# Request A Photoshoot Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a photoshoot from the photographer. It is designed to collect essential information from the client, such as their name, email, phone number, website, and services required. This information will be used to schedule and prepare for the photoshoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This is the address we will use to contact you.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone number" field.
4. Enter your website in the "web site" field.
5. Select one or more services required from the "services required" dropdown menu.
6. If you have any specific requests or quotes, enter them in the "quote request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Web site** (`website`, text, required/optional): Enter your website URL.
* **Services required** (`services_required`, select_one, required/optional): Select one or more services required for the photoshoot.
* **Quote Request** (`quote_request`, text, required/optional): If you have any specific requests or quotes for the photoshoot, enter them here.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your email address.
* We will contact you at your email address to discuss your photoshoot request and schedule a photoshoot at a later time.
