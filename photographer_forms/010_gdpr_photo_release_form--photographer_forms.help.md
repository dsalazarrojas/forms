# gdpr_photo_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals to release their photos. The form collects personal information such as name, email, photo location, and photo date/time. It also provides an opportunity for the individual to confirm their understanding of the photo release agreement and agree to other photo agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
* Fill in your first and last name.
* Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* Specify the location where the photo was taken.
* Enter the date and time the photo was captured.
* Provide a brief description of the photo.
* Check the box confirming you understand the photo release agreement and any other photo agreements that apply to you.
* If you have any other agreements, please specify them in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Photo Location** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the photo was taken.
* **Photo Date** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the photo was captured.
* **Photo Time** (`6`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time the photo was captured.
* **Photo Description** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the photo.
* **I Understand** (`8`, `select_one`, required/true): Confirm that you understand the photo release agreement.
* **Photo Agreement** (`9`, `note`, required/false): This field is currently not being used.
* **Email Address** (`10`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Other Photo Agreements** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required/true): If you have any other photo agreements, select them from the options provided.
* **Other Agreements** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any other agreements that apply to you.
* **Other Agreements Details** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Provide details about any other agreements you have.
* **Signature** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): This field is intended for a digital signature. 

Please remember to sign the form before submitting it. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your point of contact.
