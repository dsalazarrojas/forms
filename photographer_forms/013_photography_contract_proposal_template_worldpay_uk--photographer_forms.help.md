# Photography Contract & Proposal Template WorldPay UK - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for creating a photography contract and proposal in the UK. It is designed to gather essential information from clients and photographers working with WorldPay UK, making it easier to agree on and document photography projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information on page 1, "Client Information."
2. Provide session details on the next page.
3. Select the desired photo package from the options on page 3.
4. Enter payment details on page 4.
5. Sign the contract on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, required): Enter the client's name, address, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required): Describe the session, including the date, time, location, and any specific requirements or expectations.
* **Photo Package** (`photo_package`, select_multiple, required): Choose the photo package that best suits the client's needs from the available options.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Enter the payment method, amount, and any other relevant financial information.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, note, required): Both the client and photographer must sign and date the contract to make it legally binding.

* Double-check the form for completeness before submitting.
