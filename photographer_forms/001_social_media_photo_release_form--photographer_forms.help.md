# Social Media Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for obtaining consent from subjects or their representatives to use their photos on social media. It is designed for photographers to ensure they have the necessary permissions to share photos of their subjects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Photographer Name", "Address", "Phone", and "Email" fields with your contact information.
2. Provide the subject's name in the "Subject's Name" field.
3. If necessary, indicate how you got permission from the subject in the "Have you gotten permission from" field.
4. Enter the date and time you signed the release in the "Signed" field.
5. Sign your name in the "Signed By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Your name as the photographer.
- **Address** (`photographer_address`, text, required): Your address as the photographer.
- **Phone** (`photographer_phone`, text, required): Your phone number as the photographer.
- **Email** (`photographer_email`, email, required): Your email address as the photographer.
- **Subject's Name** (`subject_name`, text, required): The name of the subject in the photo.
- **Subject's Name on Photo** (`subject_name_on_photo`, text, required): A description of how the subject's name appears on the photo (e.g. "on the front of the picture").
- **Have you gotten permission from** (`photo_release_authorized`, select_multiple, required): Specify the permission you got from the subject or their representative (options: "Permission from subject", "Permission from subject's parent or guardian", "Permission from subject's next of kin", "Permission from someone else").
- **Signed** (`photo_release_signed_date`, date, required): The date and time you signed this release.
- **Signed By** (`photo_release_signed_by`, text, required): Sign your name as the photographer.
- **Signed** (`photo_release_signed`, note, required): This field is for you to sign your name as the photographer, acknowledging that the subject has given their consent to be photographed. 

## Tips
- Always ensure you have the necessary permissions before taking or publishing photos of subjects.
- Keep a record of the signed releases for future reference.
