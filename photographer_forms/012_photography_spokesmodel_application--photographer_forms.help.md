# Photography Spokesmodel Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Photography Spokesmodel Application form to help you complete it accurately. The form is intended for photographers who want to apply as models for various photography projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked with *) with your accurate information.
2. Select all applicable areas of interest from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your preferred hair color from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Photographer Name
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Enter your name, as it will be used to identify you as the photographer.

### 2. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Type in your email address that you use for work purposes.

### 3. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that you use for work purposes.

### 4. Portfolio Link
* **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): Provide a link to your online portfolio or website, if available.

### 5. Model Name
* **Model Name** (`model_name`, text, required): Enter the name you use for your modeling portfolio, if different from your real name.

### 6. Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information you'd like to share with clients.

### 7. Areas of Interest
* **Areas of Interest** (`model_interest_areas_of_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas of interest that apply to you:
	+ Fashion
	+ Lifestyle
	+ Fitness
	+ Beauty

### 8. Hair Color
* **Hair Color** (`model_interest_hair_color`, select_one, required): Choose the color of your hair from:
	+ Blonde
	+ Brown
	+ Black
	+ Gray
	+ Red
	+ Burgundy
	+ White
	+ Other

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Select multiple areas of interest relevant to your work.
- Ensure your email and phone number are up-to-date and accessible.
- If you have a portfolio link, provide it for potential clients to review your work.
- Be honest and accurate when selecting your hair color.
