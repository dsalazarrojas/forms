# Photography Model Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Model Release Form is a document that grants permission to a photographer to use a model's likeness in a photography session. It's a crucial step in ensuring that the model is aware of and consents to the use of their image.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the model's information, including their full name and address, in the "Model Information" section.
2. Enter the photographer's name and address in the "Photographer Information" section.
3. Date and time of the photography session should be filled in the "Date" and "Time" fields respectively.
4. The model and photographer should both sign the form to confirm their understanding and agreement.
5. The contact number and email of the model are optional but recommended for future contact.
6. You can add any comments or statements from both the model and photographer in the "Model Statement" and "Photographer Statement" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Model Information** (`model_full_name`, `text`, required): Fill in the model's full name and address.
* **Photographer Information** (`photographer_full_name`, `text`, required): Fill in the photographer's name and address.
* **Model Signature** (`model_signature`, `text`, required): The model should sign this section to confirm their understanding and agreement.
* **Photographer Signature** (`photographer_signature`, `text`, required): The photographer should sign this section to confirm their understanding and agreement.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Fill in the date of the photography session.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Fill in the time of the photography session.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the model's contact number for future reference.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the model's email for future reference.
* **Model Statement** (`model_statement`, `text`, optional): The model can add any comments or statements related to their appearance in the photos.
* **Photographer Statement** (`photographer_statement`, `text`, optional): The photographer can add any comments or statements related to the photography session.
* **Model Signature** (`model_signature`, `text`, optional): The model should review and sign this section to confirm their agreement to the model statement.
* **Photographer Signature** (`photographer_signature`, `text`, optional): The photographer should review and sign this section to confirm their agreement to the photographer statement.
