# Photo Booth Rental Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Booth Rental Form is designed to collect necessary information for renting a photo booth. It is used to record and update the status of photo booths, ensuring that they are accurately tracked and managed.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing all requested information for each field.
2. Ensure that the "Customer Name", "Email", "Phone Number", "Address", and "City" fields are completed for each new booking.
3. Select the correct photo booth type from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the desired rental date and time.
5. Choose the number of hours the photo booth is rented for.
6. Set the rental price.
7. Add any relevant notes about the rental.
8. Select the assigned tool and photo booth rental status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the customer renting the photo booth.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address where the photo booth will be used.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where the photo booth will be used.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select whether the photo booth is available, rented, or not available.
* **Photo Booth Type** (`photo_booth_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose from "Single" or "Multi" photo booth type.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date for the photo booth rental.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time for the photo booth rental.
* **Photo Booth Rental Hours** (`photo_booth_rental_hours`, number, required): Choose the number of hours the photo booth is rented for.
* **Photo Booth Rental Price** (`photo_booth_rental_price`, number, required): Enter the rental price of the photo booth.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the rental.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Select the assigned tool for the photo booth rental.
* **Photo Booth Rental Status** (`photo_booth_rental_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the photo booth rental, which can be "Available", "Rented", or "Not Available".

Note: Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and that the photo booth is properly updated to reflect the changes in its status.
