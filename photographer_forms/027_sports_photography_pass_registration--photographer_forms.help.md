# Sports Photography Pass Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Photography Pass Registration form is designed for event organizers and photographers to register for access to sports events. The form collects essential information about the event and the photographer's details, including the event title, start and end times, date, and event location. Additionally, it captures the photographer's information, such as their experience and equipment. The form also allows for the registration of media and photo passes, which are used to grant access to the event for photography purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields as indicated in the form. Required fields are marked with a asterisk * in the field label.
2.  Enter the event details, such as event title, start and end times, date, and event location.
3.  Provide your photographer's information, including your experience and equipment.
4.  Indicate the number of media and photo passes you require.
5.  Choose a payment method and date for the photo passes.
6.  Fill out any other relevant information, such as event status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer**: *The person or organization responsible for the event.*
* **Event Title**: *Name of the event.*
* **Photographer Info**: *Enter your name and contact information.*
* **Photographer Media**: *Enter your media ID number or website URL.*
* **Pass Type**: *Select from: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.*
* **Start Time**: *Enter the start time of the event in 24-hour format.*
* **End Time**: *Enter the end time of the event in 24-hour format.*
* **Date**: *Enter the date of the event in YYYY-MM-DD format.*
* **Event Description**: *Provide a brief description of the event.*
* **Photographer Experience**: *Enter your level of experience (e.g., 1-5 years)*
* **Photographer Equipment**: *List your photography equipment (e.g., cameras, lenses, etc.).*
* **Media Passes**: *Enter the number of media passes you require.*
* **Photo Passes**: *Enter the number of photo passes you require.*
* **Photo Pass Price**: *Enter the price of a single photo pass.*
* **Photo Passes Total**: *This field is calculated based on the number of photo passes and pass price.*
* **Total Cost**: *This field is calculated based on the number of photo passes and pass price.*
* **Payment Method**: *Select from: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.*
* **Payment Date**: *Enter the payment date in YYYY-MM-DD format.*
* **Event Location**: *Enter the location of the event.*
* **Event Date**: *Enter the date of the event in YYYY-MM-DD format.*
* **Event Time**: *Enter the event start time in 24-hour format.*
* **Event Status**: *Select from: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.*
