# live_music_session_photography_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for live music session photographers to obtain consent from their clients for the purpose of photographing them during the event. By filling out this form, both the photographer and client will have a clear understanding of the photography session terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand all the questions and their implications.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information for each field.
3. Sign and date the form to acknowledge your acceptance of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): This field is for describing the event where the photography session will take place. Please provide a brief description of the event, including its name, date, and location.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): This field is for your name as the photographer. Please enter your full name as it should be spelled.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is for your email address. Please ensure it is accurate and active so we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for your phone number. If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your number.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, text, optional): This field is for sharing social media links to your photography work or contact information. If you'd like to be contacted through social media, please enter your links.

## Tips
- Always provide honest and accurate information in each field.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you understand all the terms and conditions.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before completing the form.
- This form is intended to be a guide and a contract for the photography session. Please sign and date the form after completing it to acknowledge your acceptance of the terms and conditions.
