# School Photo Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit order requests for student photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
3. Enter the school's email address in the "School E-mail" field.
4. Enter the school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
5. Enter the date you would like to place the order on in the "Order Date" field.
6. Select the time you would like to place the order on in the "Order Time" field.
7. Select the type of photo you would like to order (Full Size, Half Size, or Quarter Size) in the "Photo Type" field.
8. Enter the number of students included in the order in the "Student Count" field.
9. Enter any additional notes or instructions in the "Notes" field.
10. Select the tools and equipment that will be assigned to the students for the photo shoot in the "Assigned Tool" field.
11. Enter the name of the file where the photos will be output in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Photo Order** (1): This field is used to enter the order request.
* **Student Name** (2): Enter the name of the student who will be photographed.
* **Parent Name** (3): Enter the name of the parent or guardian of the student.
* **School E-mail** (4): Enter the email address of the school or school district.
* **School Phone** (5): Enter the phone number of the school or school district.
* **Order Date** (6): Enter the date you would like to place the order on.
* **Order Time** (7): Select the time you would like to place the order on.
* **Photo Type** (8): Select the size of the photo you would like to order (Full Size, Half Size, or Quarter Size).
* **Student Count** (9): Enter the number of students included in the order.
* **Notes** (10): Enter any additional notes or instructions for the order.
* **Assigned Tool** (11): Select the tools and equipment that will be assigned to the students for the photo shoot.
* **Output File** (12): Enter the name of the file where the photos will be output.
