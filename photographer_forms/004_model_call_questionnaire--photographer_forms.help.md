# Model Call Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Call Questionnaire is a form designed for photographers to provide information about their availability and experience for model casting or modeling calls. This form helps model casting agencies or clients gather essential details about photographers for potential collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form with your accurate and detailed information.
* Enter your Photographer Name, which will be used to identify you in the model casting process.
* Enter your Contact Number, so the model casting agency or client can easily get in touch with you.
* Enter your Email Address, which will be used as a contact point for further communication.
* If you have a Portfolio URL, please enter it to showcase your work and experience.
* Enter your Location, so the model casting agency or client can know where you're based.
* If you have model call experience, please enter the number of times you've been in a model call.
* Lastly, indicate your Availability, which will help the model casting agency or client know when you're available for work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, optional): Please enter your name as you want to be referred to in the model casting process.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, optional): Enter your mobile or phone number where you can be easily reached for communication.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, optional): Enter your primary email address for further communication and model casting process updates.
* **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`, optional): If you have a professional portfolio URL, enter it to showcase your work and experience.
* **Location** (`location`, optional): Enter your location, city, or state to help model casting agency or client know where you're based.
* **Model Call Experience** (`model_call_experience`, optional): If you have experience in model calls, enter the number of times you've been in a model call.
* **Availability** (`availability`, optional): Briefly describe your availability for work, e.g., days of the week, hours, or specific dates you're available.
* **Availability Days** (`availability_days`, optional): If you want to specify days you're available, enter them here, e.g., Monday to Friday.

## Tips

* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Make sure your portfolio URL links to a professional website or a platform showcasing your work.
* If you have availability constraints, please specify them clearly in the "Availability" field.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask the model casting agency or client for clarification.
