# Aerial Photography Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients for aerial photography projects, allowing us to provide an accurate quote for the services required. Please fill out this form to ensure we have a clear understanding of your project requirements and can create a tailored quote for your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the following steps:

1. Enter your client information, including your name and contact details.
2. Specify the type of aerial photography project you need.
3. Provide details about the project location, date, and any specific shots or requirements.
4. Choose the desired deliverable format and specify any special requirements or restrictions.
5. Indicate your estimated budget range and preferred payment method.
6. Select the timeline for the final deliverable delivery.
7. Add any additional comments or information about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Information

* **Client Name (Client Name)** (Required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address (Email Address)** (Required): Enter your email address for quote delivery and communication.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (Required): Enter your phone number for quick communication.

### Project Details

* **Type of Aerial Photography Project (Type of Aerial Photography Project)** (Required): Select the type of aerial photography project you need (e.g., Real Estate, Construction, etc.).
* **Project Location (Project Location)** (Required): Enter the full address or coordinates of the shooting location.
* **Desired Project Date (Desired Project Date)**: Specify when you would like the aerial photography to take place.
* **Preferred Time of Day (Preferred Time of Day)**: Select the time of day that works best for the shoot (e.g., Morning, Mid-day, Afternoon, etc.).
* **Estimated Flight Duration (Hours) (Estimated Flight Duration (Hours))**: Enter how long you anticipate the drone flights will take.
* **Specific Shots Needed (Specific Shots Needed)** (Required): Describe the specific shots or angles you require.
* **Desired Deliverable Format (Desired Deliverable Format)** (Required): Choose the format you would like the final deliverables in (e.g., High-resolution photos, 4K video, etc.).

### Special Requirements

* **Special Requirements or Restrictions (Special Requirements or Restrictions)**: Add any special requirements or restrictions for the project.
* **Property Permission Obtained (Property Permission Obtained)** (Required): Indicate if you have obtained permission to fly over the property.
* **Restricted Air Space (Restricted Air Space)** (Required): Indicate if the location is near airports or in restricted air space.
* **Weather Contingency Plan (Weather Contingency Plan)**: Specify how you would like us to handle rescheduling due to weather.
* **Estimated Budget Range (Estimated Budget Range)**: Enter your estimated budget range for the project.
* **Preferred Payment Method (Preferred Payment Method)**: Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit card, Bank transfer, etc.).
* **Timeline for Delivery (Timeline for Delivery)** (Required): Specify when you need the final deliverables.

### Additional Information

* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)**: Add any other information about your project.

Note: This form is designed to guide you through the process of providing essential details for your project. Please fill out the form as accurately as possible to ensure we can create a tailored quote for your needs.
