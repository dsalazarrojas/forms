# Newborn Photography Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Newborn Photography Booking Form is a booking form for newborn photography sessions. It is designed to collect essential information from customers, including their name, email, session date, and consent. Optional information such as phone number, address, session time, and notes may also be provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "customer name" field with your full name.
2. Enter your email address in the "customer email" field.
3. Choose a session date in the "session date" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "consent" field to indicate your agreement to the terms of service.
5. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the "customer phone" field for easy communication.
6. (Optional) Provide your address in the "customer address" field, which will be used for delivery and logistics purposes.
7. (Optional) Select your preferred session time in the "session time" field.
8. (Optional) Include any additional comments or notes in the "notes" field, which will be used to personalize your photography session experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### * Customer Name (`customer_name`, text, required):
Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the photos and communication materials.
* Customer Email (`customer_email`, email, required):
Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you with updates and reminders.
* Session Date (`session_date`, date, required):
Choose the date you would like to schedule your newborn photography session.
* Consent (`consent`, select_one, required):
Select "Yes" to agree to our terms of service, including our policies on image usage and ownership.
* Session Time (`session_time`, time, optional):
(If selected) Choose your preferred session time to accommodate your schedule.
* Customer Phone (`customer_phone`, text, optional):
Provide your phone number for easy communication and scheduling purposes.
* Customer Address (`customer_address`, text, optional):
Enter your address for delivery and logistics purposes.
