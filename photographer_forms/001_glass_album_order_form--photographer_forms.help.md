# Glass Album Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for photographers to create and manage album orders. It is designed to collect essential information about each album, making it easier for photographers to organize and track their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On **page 1**, enter the album's name in the **Album Order** field.
2. On **page 2**, provide more details about the album, such as its title, in the **Album Details** field.
3. On **page 3**, enter the title of the album in the **Album Title** field.
4. On **page 4**, enter your name and contact information as the **Photographer**.
5. On **page 5**, select the date of the order in the **Date of Order** field.
6. On **page 6**, select the time of the order in the **Time of Order** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Album Order** (Page 1): Enter the name of the album you are creating.
* **Album Details** (Page 2): Provide more information about the album, such as its title.
* **Album Title** (Page 3): Enter the title of the album.
* **Photographer** (Page 4): Enter your name and contact information as the photographer responsible for the album.
* **Date of Order** (Page 5): Select the date you received the order for the album.
* **Time of Order** (Page 6): Select the time you received the order for the album.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information as the photographer to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure that you enter the correct date and time of the order to avoid confusion.
* Use this form for internal purposes only, and do not share it with external parties without permission.
