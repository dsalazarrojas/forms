# Photo Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Upload Form is for uploading photos with metadata for further processing or review. This form captures essential information about the photo, including title, description, upload date and time, status, and user who uploaded it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) or select from options where available.
2. Review all fields carefully to ensure accurate and relevant information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Upload Photo** (id: 1, text, required: false): Upload a photo from your computer or select from the provided options.
* **Photo Title** (id: 2, text, required: false): Enter a short and descriptive title for the uploaded photo.
* **Photo Description** (id: 3, text, required: false): Enter a short description for the uploaded photo.
* **Upload Date** (id: 4, date, required: false): Select the date the photo was uploaded.
* **Upload Time** (id: 5, time, required: false): Select the time the photo was uploaded.
* **Upload Status** (id: 6, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the photo upload (e.g., Pending, In Progress, Done, Cancelled).
* **Upload User** (id: 7, text, required: false): Enter the name of the user who uploaded the photo.
* **Photo Tags** (id: 8, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more tags for the photo from the provided options.
* **Upload User Hint** (id: 9, note, required: false): This field is currently unused.
* **Photo Tags Hint** (id: 10, note, required: false): This field is currently unused.

## Tips
* Review and validate all fields before submitting the form to ensure accurate and relevant information is provided.
* If you encounter any issues while uploading a photo, contact the support team for assistance.
