# Drone Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from drone operators about their drone and its usage. This information will help property owners or managers to better understand and coordinate drone-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about yourself and your drone.
2. Make sure to provide complete and detailed information about your drone and its usage.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drone Operator Info** (Drone Operator Info, text, optional): Please enter your name and contact information so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, optional): Please enter your name and contact information so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Drone Release Info** (Drone Release Info, text, optional): Please describe your drone and its usage, including any relevant details such as drone type, purpose of flight, and estimated duration of flight.
* **Property Info** (Property Info, text, optional): Please provide information about the property where you plan to fly your drone, including its address and any other relevant details.
