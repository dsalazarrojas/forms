# School Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and payment details from school photography clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the Client Information in the "Client Information" field.
2.  Select the Payment Gateway and Payment Method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.) in the "Payment Gateway" and "Payment Method" fields, respectively.
3.  Enter the Due Date and Amount Due in the "Due Date" and "Amount Due" fields, respectively.
4.  Enter any additional Payment Terms in the "Payment Terms" field.
5.  Indicate whether the client has agreed to the Payment Terms by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Payment Terms Agreed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information (id: 1)
* **Client Information** (text, required: false):
Enter the client's name and contact information.

### Payment Details (id: 2)
* **Payment Details** (text, required: false):
Enter any additional notes or details about the payment.

### Terms and Conditions (id: 3)
* **Terms and Conditions** (text, required: false):
Enter any terms and conditions of the payment agreement.

### Contract Signed (id: 4)
* **Contract Signed** (text, required: false):
Enter the date and time the contract was signed.

### Payment Gateway (id: 5)
* **Payment Gateway** (select_one, required: false):
Select the payment gateway to be used for the payment (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Square).

### Payment Method (id: 6)
* **Payment Method** (select_multiple, required: false):
Select one or more payment methods to be used for the payment (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, other).

### Payment Status (id: 7)
* **Payment Status** (select_one, required: false):
Select the payment status (e.g., paid, pending, unpaid).

### Due Date (id: 8)
* **Due Date** (date, required: false):
Enter the date by which the payment is due.

### Amount Due (id: 9)
* **Amount Due** (number, required: false):
Enter the amount due for the payment.

### Payment Terms (id: 10)
* **Payment Terms** (text, required: false):
Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.

### Payment Terms Agreed (id: 11)
* **Payment Terms Agreed** (select_one, required: false):
Select whether the client has agreed to the payment terms ("True" or "False").

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the system administrator for assistance.
