# Talent Profile Shooting Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to talent profiles and shooting bookings. It's intended for talent representatives or photographers to capture and store relevant details about a talent's profile and a specific shooting project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is a brief description of the form's main topic.
* **Contact Info** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the contact information of the person submitting the form.
* **Shoot Details** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide a description of the shoot, including any relevant details.
* **Talent Info** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter information about the talent, such as their name, contact information, or relevant details.
* **Additional Info** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the shoot or talent profile.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Review the form for accuracy before submitting.
