# Wedding Photography Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about the wedding details and preferences of the couple to provide a personalized and stress-free experience. It's recommended to carefully review this guide to ensure you complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
- Review each field carefully before submitting your answers.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to contact us for clarification.
- You can save your progress and return to this form later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wedding Date** (wedding_date): Please enter the date of the wedding ceremony.
  - Format: MM/DD/YYYY
  - Example: 05/25/2023
- **Photographer Information** (photographer_info): Please select all that apply:
  - Yes: You have hired a photographer for your wedding.
  - No: You have not hired a photographer for your wedding.
- **Couple Information** (couple_info): Please select one:
  - Yes: The couple is involved in the selection process.
  - No: The couple is not involved in the selection process.
- **Wedding Details** (wedding_details): Please select all that apply:
  - Yes: The wedding has a specific theme or style.
  - No: The wedding does not have a specific theme or style.
- **Preferences** (preferences): Please select one:
  - Yes: You have specific preferences for the photographer's work.
  - No: You do not have specific preferences for the photographer's work.
- **Notes** (notes): If you have any additional comments or information, please use this field to provide them.
  - Enter any relevant details or comments about your wedding or photography needs.
- **Couple Expectations** (couple_expectations): If you have any specific expectations or requirements for the couple's photo shoot, please use this field to provide them.
  - Enter any specific expectations or requirements for the couple's photo shoot.
- **Photographer Expectations** (photographer_expectations): If you have any specific expectations or requirements for the photographer's work, please use this field to provide them.
  - Enter any specific expectations or requirements for the photographer's work.
- **Special Requests** (special_requests): If you have any special requests or needs, please use this field to provide them.
  - Enter any special requests or needs you may have.
- **Photo Deliverables** (photo_deliverables): Please select one:
  - Yes: You want the photographer to provide a specific number of photos or albums.
  - No: You do not have a specific requirement for photos or albums.
- **Contact Information** (contact_information): Please select all that apply:
  - Yes: You want to be contacted for updates or information.
  - No: You do not want to be contacted for updates or information.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your answers.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for clarification.
- Save your progress and return to this form later if needed.
