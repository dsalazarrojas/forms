# Photography Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a photography session between a photographer and a client. It includes fields for collecting contact information, description of the work, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Enter your information as a photographer**:
	* Enter your name in the "Photographer Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Photographer Email" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Photographer Phone" field.
2. **Enter your client's information**:
	* Enter your client's name in the "Client Name" field.
	* Enter your client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
	* Enter your client's phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
3. **Provide a description of the work**:
	* Enter a brief description of the photography session in the "Description of Work" field.
4. **Schedule the booking session**:
	* Select a date for the booking session in the "Booking Session" field.
5. **Choose a contract type**:
	* Select the type of contract you want to sign, such as "Standard Contract", "Custom Contract", or "Non-Disclosure Agreement".
6. **Confirm payment details**:
	* Enter any relevant payment information in the "Payment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Name**: Enter your name as the photographer.
* **Photographer Email**: Enter your email address as the photographer.
* **Photographer Phone**: Enter your phone number as the photographer.
* **Client Name**: Enter your client's name.
* **Client Email**: Enter your client's email address.
* **Client Phone**: Enter your client's phone number.
* **Description of Work**: Provide a brief description of the photography session.
* **Booking Session**: Select a date for the booking session.
* **Payment**: Enter any relevant payment information.
* **Contract Type**: Choose the type of contract you want to sign, such as "Standard Contract", "Custom Contract", or "Non-Disclosure Agreement".

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your photographer contact for assistance.
