# legal_photography_contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for creating a legal photography contract between a photographer and a client. It outlines essential information for a session, including date, time, location, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the relevant information for your photography session.
2. Ensure you have permission from the client to collect their details, such as their address and phone number.
3. Be mindful of payment terms, as they are a critical part of the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **legal_photography_contract**: Enter a brief description of the session or contract details.
* **photographer**: Provide your name or your organization's name.
* **session_date**: Enter the date of the photography session.
* **start_time**: Enter the start time of the photography session.
* **end_time**: Enter the end time of the photography session.
* **location**: Enter the address or location of where the session will take place.
* **client_address**: Enter the client's address (if applicable).
* **client_phone**: Enter the client's phone number (if applicable).
* **payment_terms**: Specify the payment terms for the session, including payment methods and amounts.
* **cancellation_terms**: Outline the cancellation policies, including any fees associated with cancellations.
* **cancellation_fees**: Enter any cancellation fees if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and sign the contract with your client before the session.
* Keep a copy of the contract for your records.
* Communicate any questions or concerns to your client about this form.
