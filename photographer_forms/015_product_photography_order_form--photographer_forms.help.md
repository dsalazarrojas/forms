# Product Photography Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for photographers to capture information about the products they wish to photograph, their quantity, and contact details of the client. Fill out this form to provide the necessary information for your photography services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the type of product you wish to photograph ("Yes" or "No").
2. Enter the name of the product(s) you wish to photograph.
3. Specify the quantity of product(s) you wish to photograph.
4. Upload or enter the image URL of the product(s) you wish to photograph.
5. Enter the name of the contact person for this order.
6. Enter the email address of the contact person.
7. Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose "Yes" if you wish to order photography services for this product, "No" otherwise.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the product you wish to photograph.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of items you wish to have photographed.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, required/optional): Upload a photo of the product or enter a URL where the photo can be accessed. Please note that uploading a large image may be subject to our server limitations.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person to contact for this order.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
