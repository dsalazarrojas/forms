# photo_print_release_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo_print_release_form_template is a form used to obtain permission from clients to print and release their photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide the client's contact information, including their email address, in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Photo Release" field, indicating whether or not the client grants permission to print their photo.
4. Describe the materials you plan to use or provide in the "Materials" field, if necessary.
5. In the "Agreement" field, you can add any additional information or terms that need to be agreed upon between you and the client.
6. Select either "Print Permission" or "Do Not Print" in the "Print Permission" field, indicating how the client wants their photo to be printed.
7. Enter your signature in the "Signature" field, confirming that you have obtained the client's permission.
8. Date and time the permission was given in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Name (photographer_name)
* This is where you will enter the client's name.
* Make sure to spell it correctly to avoid any miscommunication issues.

### Contact Information (contact_information)
* Enter the client's email address or any other contact information.
* This will allow you to reach out to them in case of any questions or further clarification.

### Email (email)
* Enter the client's email address.
* Make sure it is correct to ensure smooth communication.

### Photo Release (photo_release)
* Select "Yes" if you have the client's permission to print their photo.
* Select "No" if they refuse to print their photo.

### Materials (materials)
* Describe the materials you plan to use or provide.
* Be specific to avoid any confusion.

### Agreement (agreement)
* Add any additional terms or conditions that need to be agreed upon between you and the client.
* Keep it clear and concise.

### Print Permission (photo_print_permission)
* Select "Print Permission" if you have the client's permission to print their photo.
* Select "Do Not Print" if they refuse to print their photo.

### Signature (signature)
* Sign your name to confirm that you have obtained the client's permission.
* Make sure your signature is legible and clear.

### Date (date)
* Date and time when the client gave you their permission.
* Make sure to include the correct date and time.

### Photographer Information (photographer_info)
* This field is optional and not required for the permission process.
* You can use it to add any additional information about you and your work.
