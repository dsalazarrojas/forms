# Family Photography Contract Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Family Photography Contract Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a family photography session. It is used to gather essential information from clients and ensure that both the client and the photographer are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to review and understand each field's purpose before providing information.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact your photographer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, required): This section requires you to provide your name and contact information. Please enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Photographer Details** (`photographer_details`, `text`, required): This section provides information about your photographer. Please enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, `text`, required): This section outlines the details of your session. Please provide information about the date, time, location, and type of session you are booking.
* **Services** (`services`, `select_multiple`, required): This section asks you to choose the services you would like to include in your session. Please select the options that apply to your session.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required): This section outlines the payment terms for your session. Please review and agree to the payment terms outlined.
* **Billing** (`billing`, `text`, required): This section provides information about how you would like to be billed. Please select the billing option that applies to your session.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, `text`, required): This section outlines the cancellation policy for your session. Please review and agree to the cancellation policy outlined.
* **Cancellation Refund** (`cancellation_refund`, `select_multiple`, required): This section asks you to confirm whether you would like to receive a refund in the event of cancellation. Please select the option that applies to your session.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before signing.
* Make sure to ask your photographer if you have any questions or concerns.
* This form is a contract, so please make sure to understand the terms and conditions before signing.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
