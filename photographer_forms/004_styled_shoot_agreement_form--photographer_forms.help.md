# Styled Shoot Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create or confirm an agreement between the photographer and other parties involved in a styled shoot. It collects information about the photographer, planner, venue, creative vendor, shoot details, roles and responsibilities, image usage permissions, and an optional agreement text.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the text fields:
	* **Photographer**: The name and contact information of the photographer.
	* **Planner**: The name and contact information of the planner involved in the shoot.
	* **Venue**: The location where the shoot will take place.
	* **Creative Vendor**: The name and contact information of the creative vendor or other parties involved in the shoot.
	* **Shoot Details**: A description of the shoot, including date, time, and any other relevant details.
2. Select your responses for the select multiple fields:
	* **Roles**: Select all the roles involved in the shoot (e.g., "Yes" for the photographer, "Yes" for the planner, etc.).
	* **Image Usage Permissions**: Select all the permissions for image usage (e.g., "Yes" for commercial use, "Yes" for editorial use, etc.).
3. Add any additional terms or agreements in the **Agreement Text** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer**: Enter the name and contact information of the photographer responsible for the shoot.
* **Planner**: Enter the name and contact information of the planner involved in the shoot.
* **Venue**: Enter the location where the shoot will take place.
* **Creative Vendor**: Enter the name and contact information of the creative vendor or other parties involved in the shoot.
* **Shoot Details**: Provide a description of the shoot, including date, time, and any other relevant details.
* **Roles**: Select all the roles involved in the shoot (e.g., "Yes" for the photographer, "Yes" for the planner, etc.).
* **Image Usage Permissions**: Select all the permissions for image usage (e.g., "Yes" for commercial use, "Yes" for editorial use, etc.).
* **Agreement Text**: Enter any additional terms or agreements that need to be included in the contract.
