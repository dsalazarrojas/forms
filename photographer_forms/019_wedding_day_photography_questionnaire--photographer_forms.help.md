# Wedding Day Photography Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to gather details about your wedding day photography service to ensure a smooth and successful experience for both you and your photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category of your photography service from the options provided.
2. Provide a brief description of your photography service.
3. Choose the venues where the photography services will be performed.
4. Enter your name, email, and phone number so that the photographer can contact you.
5. Select the type of photography service you require (e.g., full day, half day, elopement, or mini session).
6. Enter the date and time of the event.
7. Choose the locations for the photography services.
8. Select the style of photography you prefer (e.g., classic, modern, vintage).
9. Choose any additional services you require (e.g., hair and makeup, wedding planner, DJ, flowers).
10. Enter the estimated number of guests.
11. Select the photography equipment you will be using.
12. Enter any notes or comments you have for the photographer.
13. Add any client comments or feedback.
14. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
