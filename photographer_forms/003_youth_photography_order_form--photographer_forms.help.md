# Youth Photography Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Photography Order Form is used to collect information from clients for photography services. The form is intended to be used by photographers or those acting on their behalf to gather necessary details from clients.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the photographer(s) who will be handling your photography session.
2. Provide your email address so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
3. Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
4. Select a date and time for your photography session.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments about your session.
6. Indicate if you have selected a photographer for the session.
7. Choose the service(s) you would like to book for your session.
8. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **YouthPhotographyOrderForm**: Enter the name of this form to help us identify it in your records.
* **PhotographerName**: Please enter the name of the photographer who will be handling your session.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Date**: Select a date for your photography session. You can enter a specific date or a date range.
* **Time**: If you have selected a date, enter the time for your session. Note that if you select a date range, this field will not be required.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or details about your session that you would like to share with the photographer.
* **SelectPhotographer**: Check the box if you have selected a photographer for your session.
* **SelectService**: Choose the services you would like to book for your session. Select one or multiple options as needed.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your form once you have completed all required fields.
* **Price**: This field is not used in the current implementation, but is reserved for future use.
* **Yes/No** options for **SelectPhotographer** and **SelectService** allow you to indicate whether you have selected a photographer or service.
