# Maternity Session Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maternity Session Questionnaire is designed to gather essential information from clients for a smooth and enjoyable maternity photo session experience. This guide will walk you through each field to help you complete the form accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Session Details page (id: 1). This page is where you'll provide general information about your session.
2. Move on to the Client Info page (id: 2). Here, you'll share your basic contact information.
3. Next, proceed to the Birth Details page (id: 3) to enter any relevant birth details.
4. On the Contact Preferences page (id: 4), select your preferred contact method (First name, Last name, Phone number, Email, Text, or Other (Specify)).
5. Provide your email address on the Email field (id: 5).
6. Enter your phone number on the Phone number field (id: 6).
7. If applicable, provide any medical history information on the Medical History field (id: 7).
8. Select the session date and time on the Session Date and Time page (id: 8-9).
9. On the Photographer Info page (id: 10), you can provide any additional information about yourself or your session preferences.
10. Finally, you can add any additional notes or comments on the Notes field (id: 11). If you have any other information that's not included in this form, feel free to add it in the Other field (id: 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details** (id: 1): This is where you'll provide general information about your session, including any specific requests or requests.
* **Client Info** (id: 2): This field collects your basic contact information, such as your name.
* **Birth Details** (id: 3): If you're expecting or have recently given birth, please provide any relevant birth details, including the expected/born date and time.
* **Contact Preferences** (id: 4): Select your preferred contact method for communication before, during, and after the session.
* **Email** (id: 5): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (id: 6): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Medical History** (id: 7): If you have any medical history or conditions that may impact the session, please let us know.
* **Session Date** (id: 8): Select the date for your session.
* **Session Time** (id: 9): Choose the time for your session.
* **Photographer Info** (id: 10): Provide any additional information about yourself or your session preferences.
* **Notes** (id: 11): Add any additional notes or comments about your session.
* **Other** (id: 12): If there's anything else you'd like to share or add that's not included in this form, please feel free to do so.
