# Photo Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Release Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from subjects or models for photos taken by photographers. It ensures that the subjects or models are aware of how their photos will be used and that they agree to such use.

## How To Complete This Form
- Select the correct answer for "photo_releasing_party" to indicate whether you are the owner of the photos or the acquiring party.
- Choose the correct answer(s) for "photo_use" to indicate how the photos will be used, such as Commercial, Personal, or Other (please specify).
- If applicable, select the correct date for "photo_release_date" when the photos were taken.
- Select the correct answer for "photo_receiving_party" to indicate whether you are the owner of the photos or the acquiring party.
- If applicable, provide a signature in the "photo_signature" field.
- If applicable, enter the date of release for "date".
- If applicable, enter the time of release for "time".
- If applicable, enter a description of "other_use" for any other use not specified above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **photo_releasing_party**: This field asks you to select whether you are the owner of the photos or the acquiring party. This will help ensure that the correct party is responsible for obtaining consent. Choose "Owner of the photos" if you are the photographer taking the photo, and "Acquiring party" if you are the one who will be using the photos.
* **photo_use**: This field allows you to choose how the photos will be used. You can select "Any purpose", "Commercial use", "Personal use", or "Other (please specify)" to indicate how the photos will be used.
* **photo_release_date**: This field allows you to specify the date the photos were taken. If this field is not applicable, leave it blank.
* **photo_receiving_party**: This field asks you to select whether you are the owner of the photos or the acquiring party. This will help ensure that the correct party is responsible for obtaining consent. Choose "Owner of the photos" if you are the photographer taking the photo, and "Acquiring party" if you are the one who will be using the photos.
* **photo_signature**: This field is for a signature from the subject or model. If this field is not required, leave it blank.
* **date**: If the photos were taken on a specific date, this field allows you to specify it.
* **time**: If the photos were taken at a specific time, this field allows you to specify it.
* **other_use**: This field is for any other use not specified above, and allows you to provide a brief description of it.
* **photo_signature**: This field is not applicable if the "photo_releasing_party" field is selected as "Acquiring party". If applicable, provide a signature from the subject or model. 

Note: The fields marked as "required" should not be left blank. The fields marked as "not required" can be left blank if not applicable.
