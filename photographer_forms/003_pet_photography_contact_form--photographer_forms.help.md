# Pet Photography Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Photography Contact Form is designed to collect information from pet owners who are interested in photography services for their pets. This form is meant to gather details about the pet, the owner's preferences, and contact information to schedule a photography session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Pet Information" section with the required details about your pet.
2. Provide the "Pet Details" section with any relevant information about your pet that would be beneficial for the photography session.
3. Choose your "Session Preferences" in the "Session Preferences" section.
4. Enter your "Client Details" in the "Client Details" section.
5. Provide information about the "Session Details" in the "Session Details" section.
6. Enter your "Contact" information in the "Contact" section.
7. If you have any existing "Sessions", you can list them in the "Sessions" section.
8. Choose your preferences for the "Session Preferences" section.
9. Enter any "Messages" in the "Messages" section.
10. If you'd like to share photos, you can add them to the "Gallery" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Information** (`pet_photography_contact_form_data`, text, required): This field is used to enter a brief description of your pet.
* **Pet Details** (`pet_details`, text, required): In this field, you can provide any specific details about your pet that would be helpful for the photography session.
* **Session Preferences** (`pet_photography_session_preferences`, text, required): Here, you can specify your preferences for the photography session.
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Session Details** (`photography_session_details`, text, required): Provide information about the session, such as the date, time, and location.
* **Contact** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your name and phone number.
* **Sessions** (`pet_photo_sessions`, text, required): List any existing sessions you have had with us.
* **Session Preferences** (`pet_photo_session_preferences`, text, required): Choose your preferences for the photography session.
* **Messages** (`client_messages`, text, required): Enter any messages or comments you'd like to include.
* **Gallery** (`photo_gallery`, text, required): If you'd like to share photos, you can add them to this section.
