# Photography Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help photographers create quotes or proposals for their services. It collects information about the client, event details, services required, and pricing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the company or organization associated with the event.
2. Provide a brief description of the event, including any relevant details.
3. Enter the name of the client or individual associated with the event.
4. Enter a valid email address of the client or individual.
5. Enter a valid phone number of the client or individual.
6. Select the services you require from the list of options.
7. Select the price for the services from the list of options.
8. Enter the date of the event.
9. Enter the time of the event.
10. Enter a brief summary or quote for the services provided.
