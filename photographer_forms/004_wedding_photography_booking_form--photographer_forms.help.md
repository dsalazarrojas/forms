# Wedding Photography Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the Wedding Photography Booking Form, so you can provide us with the necessary details to create a custom and memorable experience for your special day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section as accurately as possible, providing all required information (indicated by *).
2. Select options from the provided choices for fields with multiple options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Date** (Page 1, id: 1): Enter the date of your wedding. Select the month and year of your wedding.
* **Photographer** (Page 1, id: 2): This field is not required, but if you have a specific photographer you'd like to book, enter their name.
* **Contact Number** (Page 1, id: 3): Enter your or your contact person's phone number. This will allow us to get in touch with you about the booking.
* **Contact Email** (Page 1, id: 4): Enter your or your contact person's email address. This will help us communicate with you regarding the booking.
* **Wedding Time** (Page 1, id: 5): Enter the time of your wedding (AM/PM).
* **Wedding Location** (Page 1, id: 6): Enter the location of your wedding (e.g., venue, church, etc.).
* **Number of Guests** (Page 1, id: 7): Enter the number of guests attending your wedding.
* **Wedding Details** (Page 1, id: 8): Select all that apply from the provided options (e.g., Outdoor, Indoor, Beach).
* **Photographer Details** (Page 1, id: 9): Select the photographer you'd like to book. Please note that this field is not required.
* **Notes** (Page 1, id: 10): Enter any additional notes or comments about your booking.

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Selecting a specific photographer will help us tailor your experience according to your preferences.
- If you have any special requests, please include them in the "Notes" field.
- If you have any questions, please contact us at your provided contact information.
