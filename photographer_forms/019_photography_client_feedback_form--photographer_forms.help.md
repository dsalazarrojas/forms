# Photography Client Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Client Feedback Form is a tool used by photographers to gather valuable insights from their clients about their services. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your feedback and rate your experience with the photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the services you purchased from the photographer.
3. Enter the date and time of your photography session.
4. Provide a brief description of the session, including the location.
5. Rate your experience with the photographer.
6. Enter any additional comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photography Services** (`what_services_purchased`, `select_one`): Please select the services you purchased from the photographer. This can be Wedding, Event, Portrait, Product, or Family Photography.
* **Photo Session Date** (`photo_session_date`, `date`): Enter the date of your photography session. This is the date when the photographer took the photos.
* **Photo Session Time** (`photo_session_time`, `time`): Enter the time of your photography session. This is the time when the photographer took the photos.
* **Photo Session Location** (`photo_session_location`, `text`): Enter the location where the photographer took your photos. This can be any public or private location where the session took place.
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, `text`): This is your opportunity to provide a brief description of your experience with the photographer. Be honest and detailed in your feedback, as it will help the photographer improve their services.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`): Rate your experience with the photographer. Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to indicate your satisfaction with the service.
* **Additional Comments** (`photo_session_comments`, `text`): If you have any additional comments or suggestions for the photographer, this is the place to enter them.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields to ensure the photographer has a complete understanding of your experience.
* Be as detailed as possible in your feedback and comments.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please include them in the comments section.
