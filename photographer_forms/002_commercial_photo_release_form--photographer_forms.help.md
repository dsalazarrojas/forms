The purpose of this form is to obtain commercial photo release agreements from clients, ensuring that photographers understand their rights and responsibilities when using photographs taken during a shoot. This form helps establish clear guidelines for photo usage, compensation, and model releases, which is essential for photographers to avoid potential legal issues and disputes.

The form is designed to collect information about the photographer, client, and shoot details, including usage rights, duration, territory, compensation, and model release requirements. This helps to ensure that both parties are on the same page and can avoid misunderstandings or misuses of the photographs.

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

## Purpose
The Commercial Photo Release Form is a crucial document that ensures clear communication between the photographer and the client about the usage, rights, and responsibilities of the photographs taken during a shoot. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly is essential for a smooth and successful collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Gather Required Information
- Complete all required fields marked as necessary. Missing any required information may delay or even lead to the cancellation of the shoot.
- Be honest and accurate when providing information about the photographer, client, and shoot details.

### Step 2: Review and Select Options
- Select the correct shoot purpose and usage rights that apply to the shoot.
- Choose the territory where the photographs will be used.
- Determine the duration of the photo usage.
- Decide on the type and amount of compensation.
- Decide if exclusive rights are being granted.

### Step 3: Sign and Date the Form
- Ensure that the client signs and dates the form to confirm their agreement.
- Review and verify that all required fields are complete before finalizing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Photographer Information (1)
* **Photographer Information** (`note`, required: false)
	+ Provides space for the photographer to add any additional details about themselves or their company.

### Photographer Name (2)
* **Photographer Name** (`text`, required: true)
	+ Full legal name of the photographer.

### Photography Business Name (3)
* **Photography Business Name** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Name of the photography company if applicable.

### Photographer Email (4)
* **Photographer Email** (`email`, required: true)
	+ Contact email address of the photographer.

### Photographer Phone (5)
* **Photographer Phone** (`text`, required: true)
	+ Contact phone number of the photographer.

### Client Information (6)
* **Client Information** (`note`, required: false)
	+ Provides space for the client to add any additional details about themselves or their company.

### Client Name (7)
* **Client Name** (`text`, required: true)
	+ Full legal name of the client.

### Client Email (8)
* **Client Email** (`email`, required: true)
	+ Client contact email.

### Shoot Date (9)
* **Shoot Date** (`date`, required: true)
	+ Date when the photoshoot took place.

### Shoot Location (10)
* **Shoot Location** (`text`, required: true)
	+ Location where the photos were taken.

### Shoot Purpose (11)
* **Shoot Purpose** (`select_one`, required: true)
	+ Primary purpose of the photoshoot (Commercial Advertising, Product Photography, Corporate Headshots, Event Coverage, Real Estate, Fashion/Portfolio, Social Media Content).

### Usage Rights (12)
* **Usage Rights** (`select_multiple`, required: true)
	+ Select all applicable usage rights (Print Advertising, Digital Marketing, Social Media, Website, Brochures/Catalogs, Billboards, Television, Internal Use Only).

### Usage Duration (13)
* **Usage Duration** (`select_one`, required: true)
	+ Choose the duration of photo usage (1 year, 2 years, 3 years, 5 years, Perpetual/Unlimited).

### Usage Territory (14)
* **Usage Territory** (`select_one`, required: true)
	+ Choose the geographic area where the photographs will be used (Local/Regional, National, North America, Worldwide).

### Compensation Type (15)
* **Compensation Type** (`select_one`, required: true)
	+ Choose the type of compensation (One-Time Fee, Royalty Based, Trade/Exchange, No Compensation).

### Compensation Amount (16)
* **Compensation Amount** (`number`, required: false)
	+ Total compensation amount in USD (if applicable).

### Exclusive Rights (17)
* **Exclusive Rights** (`select_one`, required: true)
	+ Decide if exclusive rights are being granted (Yes - Exclusive, No - Non-Exclusive).

### Model Release Required (18)
* **Model Release Required** (`select_one`, required: true)
	+ Decide if this shoot includes recognizable people (Yes, No).

### Additional Terms (19)
* **Additional Terms** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Any additional terms or conditions.

### Client Signature Date (20)
* **Client Signature Date** (`date`, required: true)
	+ Date of client agreement.
