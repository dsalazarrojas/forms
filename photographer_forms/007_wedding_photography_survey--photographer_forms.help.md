# Wedding Photography Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Photography Survey form is a tool used to collect and record essential details about a wedding event, allowing planners and coordinators to better understand the scope and requirements of the event. The form is designed to be completed by individuals involved in the wedding planning process or anyone responsible for coordinating the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below:

1. Ensure you have all the required information about the wedding event.
2. Start filling out the form section by section, corresponding to the wedding details.
3. Provide accurate and complete information for each field, as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Details** (`wedding_photography_survey_details`): This is the main section of the form where you will provide general information about the wedding event.
* **Venue** (`wedding_photography_survey_details_venue`): In this field, enter the location or venue where the wedding will take place.
* **Date** (`wedding_photography_survey_details_date`): Enter the date of the wedding event. Use the date format as provided in the input field.
* **Time** (`wedding_photography_survey_details_time`): Enter the time of the wedding event. Use the time format as provided in the input field.
* **Guests** (`wedding_photography_survey_details_guests`): Enter the expected number of guests attending the wedding event.
* **Budget** (`wedding_photography_survey_details_budget`): Enter the estimated budget for the wedding event.
* **Location** (`wedding_photography_survey_details_location`): Enter the specific location or address of the venue where the wedding will take place.
* **Photographer Details** (`wedding_photography_survey_details_photographer_details`): This section provides information about the photographer handling the event. 
*   **Name** (`wedding_photography_survey_details_photographer_details_name`): Enter the name of the photographer.
*   **Email** (`wedding_photography_survey_details_photographer_details_email`): Enter the email address of the photographer.
*   **Phone** (`wedding_photography_survey_details_photographer_details_phone`): Enter the contact phone number of the photographer.
*   **Address** (`wedding_photography_survey_details_photographer_details_address`): Enter the address of the photographer.

Note: If any of the fields are not applicable or not known, please indicate so in the field to ensure accurate and complete information is recorded.
