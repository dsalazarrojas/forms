# Baby Photography Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians to book a photography session for their baby. The form will be used to schedule and prepare for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out each field with the required information. Some fields are marked as "required", which means they must be filled out to proceed with the booking.
*   Select the correct options for each field based on your preferences and circumstances.
*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the primary contact person.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for booking confirmation.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number for session coordination.
*   **Baby Name** (`baby_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your baby for session purposes.
*   **Baby Age** (`baby_age`, select_one, required): Select the age range of your baby from the provided options.
*   **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of photography session you prefer from the options.
*   **Preferred Session Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select your first choice date for the session.
*   **Alternative Session Date** (`alternative_date`, date, optional): Choose a backup date option, if you have one.
*   **Preferred Time of Day** (`preferred_time`, select_one, required): Select the time of day when your baby is most cooperative.
*   **Session Location Preference** (`session_location`, select_one, required): Choose where you would like the photos taken, from the provided options.
*   **Photography Package** (`package_selection`, select_one, required): Select the desired package for your session.
*   **Props and Outfits** (`props_provided`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like props and outfits to be handled during the session.
*   **Special Requests or Themes** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any specific themes or ideas you have in mind.
*   **Will Siblings Be Included** (`siblings_included`, select_one, required): Indicate if other children will be participating in the session.
*   **Number of Siblings** (`number_of_siblings`, number, optional): Enter the number of siblings that will be participating.
*   **Will Parents Be in Photos** (`parents_in_photos`, select_one, required): Choose if you would like family portraits included in the session.
*   **Baby Health or Special Needs Notes** (`baby_health_notes`, text, optional): Enter any allergies, sensitivities, or special considerations for your baby.
*   **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Select the source of your referral.
*   **Deposit Payment** (`deposit_payment`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are ready to pay the deposit to secure the booking.
*   **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date, required): Select the date of this reservation.
*   **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or questions you may have.
