# Photo Waiver Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals who wish to participate in photo shoots or have their photographs used in various media. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that you are aware of how your likeness will be used and to provide necessary information for identification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and fields involved.
2.  Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
3.  Enter your email address for contact purposes.
4.  If applicable, provide your address for identification purposes.
5.  Enter your birth date.
6.  Select the date of the photo shoot or when the photo was taken.
7.  Specify the time of the photo shoot or when the photo was taken.
8.  Enter the location of the photo shoot or where the photo was taken.
9.  Select the type of photo license you possess (e.g., Driver's License, State ID, or Passport).
10. Enter your photo license number (if applicable).
11. Select the date of your photo license expiration (if applicable).
12. Enter the date your photo license was issued.
13. Enter the place where your photo license was issued (if applicable).
14. Enter the authority that issued your photo license (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific address, please enter it for identification purposes.
*   **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your birth date.
*   **Photo Date** (`photo_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of the photo shoot or when the photo was taken.
*   **Photo Time** (`photo_time`, `time`, optional): Specify the time of the photo shoot or when the photo was taken.
*   **Photo Location** (`photo_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the photo shoot or where the photo was taken.
*   **Photo License** (`photo_license`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of photo license you possess (e.g., Driver's License, State ID, or Passport).
*   **Photo License Number** (`photo_license_number`, `text`, optional): If you have a photo license number, please enter it.
*   **Photo License Expires** (`photo_license_expires`, `date`, optional): If your photo license expires, select the date of expiration.
*   **Photo License Issue Date** (`photo_license_issue_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date your photo license was issued.
*   **Photo License Issue Place** (`photo_license_issue_place`, `text`, optional): Enter the place where your photo license was issued.
*   **Photo License Issue Authority** (`photo_license_issue_authority`, `text`, optional): Enter the authority that issued your photo license.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your photographer or contact the relevant authority for clarification.
*   This form is for your protection and identification purposes; the information collected will only be used for this specific purpose.
