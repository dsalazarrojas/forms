# Family Photo Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for creating a custom family photo order. It requires selecting photo packages, photo types, and other essential details to process the order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out all required fields as they appear on the form.
- For non-required fields, you can select any option that best suits your needs.
- Ensure to review your selections before submitting the form to avoid any errors or misinterpretations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Photo Packages** (`photo_packages`, `text`, required): Choose the photo package that suits your needs from the available options.
- **Photo Types** (`photo_types`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more photo types for your family photo order. Options include Family Photo, Senior Photo, and Wedding Photo.
- **Number Photos** (`number_photos`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the number of photos you want to order, from 1 to 3.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the start date of your photo shoot or order.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the end date of your photo shoot or order.
- **Package Price** (`package_price`, `select_one`, not required): Select the package price for your order from $50 to $100.
- **Total Price** (`total_price`, `number`, not required): This field will be calculated automatically based on your selections.
- **Photo Type** (`photo_type_id`, `select_one`, not required): Select the specific photo type for your order from Family Photo, Senior Photo, or Wedding Photo.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the payment method for your order, either PayPal, Stripe, or Bank Transfer.

## Tips
- Double-check your selections for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you need assistance or clarification on any field, feel free to contact our support team.
