# School Photography Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Photography Order Form Template is a tool used by schools to collect order details from students or parents. It helps to streamline the photography order process and ensures that all necessary information is gathered accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **User Input** section with your name and contact information.
2. Provide details about your order in the **Order Details** section.
3. Enter your **Contact Info** for communication purposes.
4. If applicable, add your **Payment Info** to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): Describe the order details, including any specific requests or requirements.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, such as email, phone number, or address.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): If you are paying for the order, provide payment information, such as method and amount.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any delays or issues with your order.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all information is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the school's administration or support team.
