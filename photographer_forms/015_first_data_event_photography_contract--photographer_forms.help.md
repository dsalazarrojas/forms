# First Data Event Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create contracts for event photography services. It captures essential information from both the photographer and the client, enabling efficient communication and payment tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and contact information (photographer name, email, and phone) in the relevant fields.
2.  Provide event details, including the service type (Yes/No), date of service, and payment details.
3.  Confirm your payment status and the contract status.
4.  Add any additional service notes or client information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Photography Contract Form** (1): This is the form title.
*   **Photographer Name** (2): Enter your name as the photographer.
*   **Photographer Email** (3): Enter your email address.
*   **Photographer Phone** (4): Enter your phone number.
*   **Payment Details** (5): Enter any additional payment information, such as payment terms or payment method.
*   **Amount** (6): Enter the total amount to be paid.
*   **Date of Service** (7): Enter the date of the service.
*   **Service Type** (8): Choose 'Yes' if the service is confirmed, 'No' otherwise.
*   **Service Details** (9): Enter any additional service details, such as equipment or services included.
*   **Service Notes** (10): Enter any notes or comments about the service.
*   **Client Information** (11): Enter any client information, such as contact name or contact address.
*   **Payment Status** (12): Choose 'Active' if the payment is active, 'Inactive' otherwise.
*   **Contract Status** (13): Choose 'Active' if the contract is active, 'Inactive' otherwise.
*   **Payment Confirmation** (14): Enter any payment confirmation or additional payment information.

## Tips
*   Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information to avoid any confusion or delays.
*   Review and confirm the details before submitting the form to ensure that everything is accurate and complete.
