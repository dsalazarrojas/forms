# Photography Equipment Rental Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients who wish to rent photography equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. If you wish to rent equipment, select "Yes" for "Equipment Type".
5. Assess the condition of the equipment you wish to rent (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
6. Enter the start date and end date for the rental period.
7. Indicate the duration of the rental period.
8. If necessary, provide any special instructions or additional information.
9. If you are a photographer, select "Yes" for "Photographer" (this field is not mandatory but recommended for internal purposes).
10. Select the final status of the rental (Active or Inactive, which will likely be set by the system administrator).
11. Enter a list of the equipment you wish to rent.
12. Enter the total price for the rental.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed in our records.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you regarding the rental.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can easily contact you if needed.
* **Equipment Type**: Select "Yes" if you wish to rent photography equipment.
* **Equipment Condition**: Assess the condition of the equipment you wish to rent (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Rental Start Date**: Enter the date you wish to start using the rented equipment.
* **Rental End Date**: Enter the date you expect to return the rented equipment.
* **Rental Duration**: Enter the length of time for which you wish to rent the equipment.
* **Special Instructions**: If there are any special requirements or conditions for the rented equipment, enter them here.
* **Photographer**: Select "Yes" if you are a photographer and would like us to have your contact information for internal purposes.
* **Rental Status**: Select "Active" if the rental is active (i.e., the equipment is not available for others to rent) or "Inactive" if it is not.
* **Equipment List**: Enter a list of the equipment you wish to rent.
* **Total Price**: Enter the total price for the rental.
