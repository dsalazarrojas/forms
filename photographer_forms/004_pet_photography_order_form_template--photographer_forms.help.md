# Pet Photography Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a pet photography order form template for pet owners to provide information for their pet's photography session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your pet's information and your own contact details.
2. Select the type of photography session you would like to book (Standard, Premium, or Deluxe).
3. Choose any additional services you would like to include in the session (Photo Session, Video Session, or Album Creation).
4. Add any additional notes or comments for the photographer.
5. Review your information and submit the form to request your photography session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`): Enter your pet's name, e.g., "Bella the Dog".
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`): Enter your name, e.g., "John Doe".
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address, e.g., "johndoe@example.com".
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number, e.g., "123-456-7890".
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your address, e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown, USA".
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you would like to schedule the photography session.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`): Select the type of photography session you would like to book (Standard, Premium, or Deluxe).
	+ Standard session includes a standard photo shoot with a few photos.
	+ Premium session includes a premium photo shoot with more photos and extra services.
	+ Deluxe session includes a deluxe photo shoot with premium services and additional perks.
* **Services** (`services`): Select any additional services you would like to include in the session (Photo Session, Video Session, or Album Creation).
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes or comments for the photographer.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Choose to submit your order form or select "Other" to provide more information.
