# Newborn Photography Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from parents or guardians about their newborn photography experience. It will help us understand their preferences and needs when it comes to capturing memories of this special time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *).
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. If you have any questions or need help, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Parent Information
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, text, required: false): This field is for the parent or guardian to provide their name and contact information.

### Page 2: Photo Details
* **Photo Details** (`photo_details`, text, required: false): Describe the photo shoot, including any specific details or preferences you have for the session.

### Page 3: Photo Preferences
* **Photo Preferences** (`photo_preferences`, text, required: false): Share any specific photo preferences, such as posing, lighting, or location.

### Page 4: Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.

### Page 5: Photo Location
* **Photo Location** (`photo_location`, text, required: false): Indicate where the photo shoot will take place (e.g., home, studio, park).

### Page 6: Photo Date
* **Photo Date** (`photo_date`, text, required: false): Choose the date for the photo shoot.

### Page 7: Photo Time
* **Photo Time** (`photo_time`, text, required: false): Select the time for the photo shoot.

### Page 8: Photo Notes
* **Photo Notes** (`photo_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional notes or instructions for the photographer.
