# Picture Day Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a photographer's event or photo shoot on a specific day. It is used to collect details such as name, contact information, event details (date and time), and photo specifications (type, size, format, and resolution). This form is intended to be used by photographers to register their events and photos for official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or mail).
5. Choose the date and time of your event.
6. Enter the location of your event.
7. Provide a brief description of your event.
8. Select the type of photo you will be capturing (landscape, portrait, or both).
9. Choose the size of the photo you will be capturing (small, medium, or large).
10. Select the format of the photo you will be capturing (JPEG, PNG, or TIFF).
11. Choose the resolution of the photo you will be capturing (low, medium, or high).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1): Enter your full name, as it should be spelled out.
* **Email** (2): Enter your email address to be contacted about the event or photo shoot.
* **Phone** (3): Enter your phone number so we can reach you for any last-minute details or updates.
* **Contact Method** (4): Choose how you would like to be contacted about the event or photo shoot: Phone, Email, or Mail.
* **Event Date** (5): Enter the date of your event or photo shoot.
* **Event Time** (6): Enter the time of your event or photo shoot.
* **Location** (7): Enter the location of your event or photo shoot. This can be an address, a specific landmark, or even a public place.
* **Event Description** (8): Provide a brief description of your event or photo shoot. This can include any important details about the event, such as what will be captured or any specific requirements.
* **Photo Type** (9): Select the type of photo you will be capturing: Landscape, Portrait, or Both.
* **Photo Size** (10): Choose the size of the photo you will be capturing: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Photo Format** (11): Select the format of the photo you will be capturing: JPEG, PNG, or TIFF.
* **Photo Resolution** (12): Choose the resolution of the photo you will be capturing: Low, Medium, or High.

Note: Each field is marked with a number to help you quickly identify which field is which.
