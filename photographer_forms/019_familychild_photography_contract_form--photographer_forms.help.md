# FamilyChild Photography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the information and agreements necessary for a family child photography session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Go through each section of the form and fill out the required information.
*   Ensure you read and understand the terms of the contract before signing.
*   Review each field explanation for further guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
•   **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, not required): Enter your or your child's name and contact details.

### Photographer Information
•   **Photographer Information** (`photographer_info`, text, not required): Enter your name, contact details, and other relevant information.

### Session Information
•   **Session Information** (`session_info`, text, not required): Provide details about the session, including date, time, and location.

### Contract Terms
•   **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, select_multiple, not required): Select all applicable terms from the list:
    - Session Details
    - Usage Rights
    - Revisions
    - Cancellation
    - Refund
    - Termination

### Client Agreement
•   **Client Agreement** (`client_agreement`, select_one, required): Choose one of the two options:
    - I agree to the contract terms
    - I do not agree to the contract terms

### Photographer Agreement
•   **Photographer Agreement** (`photographer_agreement`, select_one, required): Choose one of the two options:
    - I agree to the contract terms
    - I do not agree to the contract terms
