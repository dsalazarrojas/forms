# Wedding DVD Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for potential clients to provide information about their preferences and interests for their wedding services. It helps our team understand how to assist you better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information:
	* Fill in the "User Input" field with any relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information:
	* Enter your "Contact Number" for us to reach out to you.
	* Enter your "Email Address" for us to contact you.
3. Choose your preferred services:
	* Select how you "Hear About Us" (e.g., Online Search, Social Media, etc.).
	* Choose the services you are interested in from the "Which Services Do You Want?" section (e.g., Videography, Photography, etc.).
4. Leave any additional notes or comments:
	* Use the "Notes" section for any special requests or information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): A field to enter any relevant details about yourself or your event.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, text, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your inquiry.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific wedding date in mind, enter it here.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about our services (e.g., Online Search, Social Media, etc.).
	+ Online Search: You found us through our website or social media.
	+ Social Media: You saw our posts or ads on social media.
	+ Word of Mouth: A friend or family member recommended us.
	+ Print Ad: You saw our advertisement in a magazine or newspaper.
	+ Other: Please specify other.
* **Which Services Do You Want?** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the services you are interested in:
	+ Videography: Services for capturing and recording your special moments.
	+ Photography: Services for capturing your special moments through photos.
	+ Wedding Planning: Services for planning and coordinating your wedding day.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Leave any additional notes or comments you'd like to share.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, text, optional): Confirm that you have provided accurate and complete information.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information to help us assist you better.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will contact you to discuss your preferences and options.
