# Request A Photographer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a photography session. The goal is to gather information about the session's specifics and the client's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Request A Photographer" option to indicate whether you are requesting a photographer or not.
2. Enter the name of the photographer you are requesting.
3. Provide the location of the photography session.
4. Choose a start and end time for the session.
5. Add any special instructions or notes about the session in the "Session Details" field.
6. Select any specific category or categories relevant to the session (e.g., "Yes" for a specific category).
7. Select the required equipment for the session (e.g., "Yes" for a specific piece of equipment).
8. Select the equipment the photographer will be bringing (e.g., "Yes" for a specific piece of equipment).
9. Enter any client requirements or preferences in the "Client Requirements" field.
10. Enter the studio's phone number and email address, and the photographer's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request A Photographer** (`request_a_photographer`, Select One, Required): This field indicates whether you are requesting a photographer or not. Choose "Yes" if you are looking for a photographer's services, and "No" otherwise.
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the photographer you are requesting or are.
* **Location** (`location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location where the photography session will take place.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Date, Optional): Choose a start time for the photography session.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, Date, Optional): Choose an end time for the photography session.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, Text, Optional): Provide any special instructions or notes about the session.
* **Category** (`category`, Select Multiple, Required): Select any categories relevant to the session.
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the equipment required for the session.
* **Photographer Equipment** (`photographer_equipment`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the equipment the photographer will bring to the session.
* **Client Requirements** (`client_requirements`, Text, Optional): Enter any client requirements or preferences.
* **Studio Phone** (`studio_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the studio's phone number.
* **Studio Email** (`studio_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the studio's email address.
* **Photographer Phone** (`photographer_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the photographer's phone number.
