# Wedding Videography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Videography Contract is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of the videography services for your special day. It's a crucial part of the booking process and helps ensure that both you and your videographer are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information and contact details in the "Personal Info" section.
2. Provide your wedding details, such as the date and location of the event.
3. Choose your preferred video package from the "Video Package" section.
4. Upload your e-signature in the "E-Signatures" section.
5. Review and agree to the terms of the contract in the "Customer Terms" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Info (Required: No)
* **Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed in the contract.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address and phone number where you can be reached.

### Contact Info (Optional)
* **No explanation provided**

### Wedding Details (Optional)
* **Wedding Details**: Provide a brief description of your wedding event, including the date, location, and any notable details.

### Video Package (Optional)
* **Select the video package that best suits your needs**:
	+ Option 1: Basic Package
	+ Option 2: Deluxe Package
	+ Option 3: Premium Package

### E-Signatures (Optional)
* **Upload your digital signature**: This is where you'll upload your digital signature that will be used for your contract.

### Customer Terms (Optional)
* **Review and agree to the terms**: This section outlines the terms of service and payment details. Please read carefully and check the box to confirm your agreement.
