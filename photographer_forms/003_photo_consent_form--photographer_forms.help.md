# Photo Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from clients for photography sessions. It confirms that both the photographer and the client understand the terms and conditions of the session, including the type of photography (e.g., Portrait, Landscape, Action), the event date, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is required from you and the client.
2. Check the "Photo Consent" field and select "True" if you have obtained the client's consent for the session.
3. Select the type of photography service you will be providing (e.g., Portrait, Landscape, Action) from the "Photographer Forms" field.
4. Enter the date of the event or photography session in the "Event Date" field.
5. Provide your and the client's information in the "Photographer Information" and "Client Information" fields.
6. Enter any contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
7. Review your answers to ensure everything is correct and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Consent** (`photo_consent_form`, select_one, required): This field is used to confirm that the client has given their consent for the photography session. Select "True" to confirm you have obtained consent.
* **Photographer Forms** (`photographer_forms`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the type of photography service you will be providing (e.g., Portrait, Landscape, Action).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the event or photography session.
* **Photographer Information** (`photographer_info`, text, not required): Provide your contact information, such as your name, email, and phone number.
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, not required): Enter the client's contact information, such as their name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional contact information, such as the session location or your website.

## Tips
* Always review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have completed it correctly.
* Be sure to obtain consent from the client before marking the "Photo Consent" field as "True".
* Use the "Photographer Forms" field to select the specific type of photography service you will be providing.
* If you need to make any changes to the form, please contact your supervisor or support team.
