# Square Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Cake Order Form is used to process cake orders from customers, collecting necessary details for order delivery and payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **Order Number** in the first field.
2. Fill out your **Customer Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your desired **Delivery Date** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
4. In the **Order Type** field, select the type of cake you'd like to order (Cake, Frosting, Topping, or any combination of these options).
5. Enter the **Total Price** of your order.
6. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** (Visa, MasterCard, or American Express).
7. Enter the **Payment Date** for your order.
8. Fill out your **Phone Number** to ensure we can contact you.
9. Enter your **Email** to receive order updates and confirmations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Number** (text, required): Enter your unique order ID.
* **Customer Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as the customer.
* **Delivery Date** (date, required): Enter the date you'd like to receive your cake order.
* **Order Type** (select multiple, optional): Select the type of cake order you'd like to place. Options: Cake, Frosting, Topping.
* **Total Price** (number, required): Enter the total price of your order.
* **Payment Method** (select one, required): Choose your preferred payment method: Visa, MasterCard, or American Express.
* **Payment Date** (date, required): Enter the date of payment for your order.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number for order updates and confirmations.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address to receive order updates and confirmations.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
- If you have any special requests or instructions, you can include them in the "Order Type" field.
- If you'd like to cancel or modify your order, contact us at the phone number or email provided.
