# Square Email Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Email Subscription Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in subscribing to Square's newsletters, promotions, or updates. This form is intended for customers who want to stay informed about Square's latest news, offers, and product information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is the name by which we will address you.
2. Enter your **valid email address** in the "Email" field. This is the email address we will use to contact you.
3. Select the types of emails you would like to subscribe to in the "Subscription Type" field. You can choose from **Newsletter**, **Promotions**, or **Updates**.
4. Confirm your email subscription by selecting **Accept** in the "Confirm" field.
5. If you have any additional information you'd like to include, you can add it in the "Note" field.
6. If you have a **phone number**, you can enter it in the "Phone" field. This is optional.
7. If you have any **required information** you'd like to note, you can add it in the "Required Note" field. This field is optional.
8. If you have any specific **email-related information**, you can add it in the "Email" field. This field is optional.
9. If you want to set a **specific date for email confirmation**, you can select it in the "Email Confirmation" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Subscription Type**: Select the types of emails you'd like to subscribe to.
* **Confirm**: Confirm your email subscription by selecting **Accept**.
* **Note**: Add any additional information you'd like to include.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Required Note**: Add any required information you'd like to note (optional).
* **Email**: Add any specific email-related information (optional).
* **Email Confirmation**: Set a specific date for email confirmation (optional). 

Note: This form is not intended for internal purposes, and users are not required to fill out every field. The fields marked as optional should be filled out only if the user has specific requirements or information to include.
