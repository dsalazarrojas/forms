# Square Donate For Clean River Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations for cleaning a river and gather information from donors about their donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your donor information, including your contact details.
2. Select the purpose of your donation (e.g., Help Clean the River, Support Conservation Efforts, or Donate to a Specific Project).
3. Enter the amount you are donating.
4. Choose a date for your donation.
5. Enter the time of your donation.
6. Add any additional information you'd like to share about your donation.
7. Confirm that you are donating to support the river cleaning efforts.
8. If you'd like to be contacted for a follow-up, select the corresponding option.
9. Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
10. Choose a contact method (e.g., Email, Phone, or Mail).
11. Enter your contact information (e.g., name, address, phone number).
12. Enter a thank-you message for the donors or volunteers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information**: Please fill in your name and contact information, which will help us keep you updated about your donation.
* **What is the purpose of your donation?**: This is where you select the reason for your donation (e.g., Help Clean the River, Support Conservation Efforts, or Donate to a Specific Project).
* **Amount Donated**: Enter the amount you are donating to support the river cleaning efforts.
* **Date of Donation**: Choose the date when you made your donation.
* **Time of Donation**: Enter the time when you made your donation.
* **Additional Information**: Add any extra information you'd like to share about your donation.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm that you are donating to support the river cleaning efforts.
* **Donations Details**: Enter any details about your donation.
* **River Cleaning Status**: Let us know if you've already cleaned the river, haven't yet, or it's not applicable.
* **Would you like to be contacted for a follow-up?**: If you'd like to receive a follow-up, select the corresponding option.
* **Any additional comments?**: Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
* **Contact Method**: Choose a contact method (e.g., Email, Phone, or Mail).
* **Contact Info 2**: Enter any additional contact information (e.g., company, department, or team).
* **Thank You Message**: Enter a message to express your gratitude to the donors or volunteers.
* **Confirmation Message**: Enter a message to confirm your donation.
