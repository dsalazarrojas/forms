# Square Orphanage Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations and relevant information from individuals or organizations for the Square Orphanage program. It is intended to facilitate the proper allocation and management of donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Donation Amount" field with the amount you would like to donate.
2. Choose the type of donation you would like to make using the "Donation Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. If you are representing an organization, enter the name of the organization in the "Organization" field.
5. If you would like to be contacted, enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted in the "Contact Phone" field.
7. If you would like to provide additional comments or information about your donation, please use the "Donation Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you would like to donate. Please ensure this value is a valid number.
* **Donation Type** (`donation_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of donation you would like to make from the available options: Cash, In-Kind, or Other.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter the name of the organization.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted, enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted, enter your phone number.
* **Donation Comment** (`donation_comment`, note, optional): If you would like to provide additional comments or information about your donation, use this field.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the donation amount is a valid number.
- If you are donating on behalf of an organization, ensure that the organization name is correctly spelled and spelled correctly.
- If you would like to be contacted, ensure that the email and phone number fields are correctly formatted.
