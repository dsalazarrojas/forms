# Square Newsletter Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Newsletter Subscription Form is used to collect subscription information from users who wish to receive the Square newsletter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the third field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the fourth field. This will help us contact you for any further communication.
5. Select your preferred **Subscription Format** from the fifth field. You can choose between **Digital** and **Print**.
6. Choose your **Subscription Duration** from the sixth field. You can choose between **1 month**, **3 months**, **6 months**, and **12 months**.
7. Select your preferred **Print Format** from the seventh field. You can choose between **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, and **Bi-Annualy**.
8. Select your preferred **Digital Format** from the eighth field. You can choose between **PDF**, **Email**, and **Mobile App**.
9. Enter your **Preferred Email** in the ninth field.
10. Confirm your **Email** in the tenth field.
11. Confirm your **Phone** number in the eleventh field.
12. Confirm your **Phone Number** in the twelfth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in the Square newsletter.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in the Square newsletter.
* **Email** (Email, text, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you use to receive communications.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Subscription Format** (Subscription Format, select_one, required/optional): Choose between **Digital** and **Print** formats for your subscription.
* **Subscription Duration** (Subscription Duration, select_one, required/optional): Choose the duration of your subscription from **1 month**, **3 months**, **6 months**, and **12 months**.
* **Print Format** (Print Format, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose between **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, and **Bi-Annualy** formats for your print subscription.
* **Digital Format** (Digital Format, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose between **PDF**, **Email**, and **Mobile App** formats for your digital subscription.
* **Preferred Email** (Preferred Email, text, required/optional): Enter the email address you would like to use for subscription communications.
* **Confirm Email** (Confirm Email, text, required/optional): Re-enter your email address to confirm it is accurate.
* **Confirm Phone** (Confirm Phone, text, required/optional): Re-enter your phone number to confirm it is accurate.
* **Confirm Phone Number** (Confirm Phone Number, text, required/optional): Re-enter your phone number to confirm it is accurate.
