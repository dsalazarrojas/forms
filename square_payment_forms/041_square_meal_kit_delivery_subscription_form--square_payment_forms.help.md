# square_meal_kit_delivery_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "square_meal_kit_delivery_subscription_form" is a form used to manage delivery subscriptions for the Square Meal Kit. This form is used to collect information from customers to create or update their delivery subscriptions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information in each field, or click "Skip" if the field is not required.
2. Review your submission before confirming to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter your delivery address where you would like to receive your Square Meal Kit subscription.
* **Delivery Frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you would like to receive your Square Meal Kit subscription. Options: Once, Twice, Weekly, Biweekly.
* **Start Date** (`subscription_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to start your subscription. This will determine when your delivery schedule begins.
* **End Date** (`subscription_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to end your subscription. This will determine when your delivery schedule ends.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address associated with your subscription. This is used to send you updates and confirmations about your subscription.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number associated with your subscription. This is used to contact you about your subscription if needed.
