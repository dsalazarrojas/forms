# Square Membership Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Membership Payment Form is used to collect payment information from Square members to help us better understand your preferences and tailor our services to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the requested information.
2. Choose your preferred payment method and frequency.
3. Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Date of Birth Month** (`date_of_birth_month`, select_one, optional): Select your date of birth month.
- **Date of Birth Day** (`date_of_birth_day`, select_multiple, optional): Select your date of birth day.
- **Date of Birth Year** (`date_of_birth_year`, select_one, optional): Select your date of birth year.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you'd like to pay.
- **Payment Method Option** (`payment_method_option`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment method option.
- **Payment Card Number** (`payment_card_number`, text, optional): Enter your payment card number.
- **Payment Card Expiration** (`payment_card_expiration`, date, optional): Enter your payment card expiration date in the format MM/YYYY.
- **Payment Card CVV** (`payment_card_cvv`, text, optional): Enter your payment card CVV.
- **Payment Card Holder** (`payment_card_holder`, text, optional): Enter the name on your payment card.
- **Street** (`street`, text, optional): Enter your street address.
- **Address 1** (`address1`, text, optional): Enter your address line 1.
- **Address 2** (`address2`, text, optional): Enter your address line 2.
- **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your location.
- **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): Select the state of your location.
- **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your location.
- **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country of your location.
- **Payment Method Option** (`payment_method_option_option`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred payment method option.
- **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
- **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, text, optional): Select your preferred payment frequency.
- **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Confirm your submission.
