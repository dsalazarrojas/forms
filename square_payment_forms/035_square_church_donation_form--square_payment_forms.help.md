# square_church_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Church Donation Form is designed to collect user information and donation details for church donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Review each field to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form once you have finished filling it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (text)**: This field is for entering your personal details such as name and contact information.
* **Donation Details (text)**: Provide information about the donation you are making, including the amount and any specific details or comments.
* **Payment Info (text)**: Enter payment information such as your payment method and any additional payment details.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field to avoid any issues with processing.
* Review each field before submitting the form to ensure you have provided all required information.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the form administrators for assistance.
