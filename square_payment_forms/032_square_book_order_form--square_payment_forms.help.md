# Square Book Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Book Order Form is a user-friendly form designed to help customers provide their order information for books. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details: Enter your First Name, Email, Phone, Address, City, and State in the corresponding fields.
2.  Enter your order details: Provide information about the items you want to order (Order Items), the quantity you need, and any applicable price, discount, tax, and total.
3.  Review and submit: Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
*  **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
*  **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*  **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your full address.
*  **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside or wish to receive your order.
*  **State** (`State`, text, required): Enter the state or region where you reside.
*  **Zip** (`Zip`, number, required): Enter your postal zip code.
*  **Order Items** (`Order Items`, text, required): Enter the name of the items you wish to order.
*  **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of the items you wish to order.
*  **Price** (`Price`, number, required): Enter the price of the items you wish to order.
*  **Discount** (`Discount`, number, optional): If applicable, enter any discount on the items.
*  **Tax** (`Tax`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the tax on the items.
*  **Total** (`Total`, number, optional): This field is automatically generated and should not be manually edited.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have provided all necessary information to process your order correctly.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact our support team.
