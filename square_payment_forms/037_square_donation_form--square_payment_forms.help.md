# Square Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations from users, aiding in the organization's fundraising efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Donation Name** in the text field labeled "Donation Name". This is the name you would like to be associated with your donation.
2. If desired, provide any additional notes about your donation in the text field labeled "Donation Notes".
3. Enter the **Amount** of your donation in the numeric field labeled "Amount".
4. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu. You can choose between **Credit Card** or **Bank Transfer**.
5. Enter your **Email Address** in the field labeled "Email". This will help us contact you with updates about your donation.
6. If you have a **Phone Number**, enter it in the field labeled "Phone". This will help us contact you for any further instructions.
7. Finally, enter your **Address** in the text field labeled "Address". This will help us deliver any goods or services associated with your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Name** (Required: False, Type: Text): Enter your name as you would like it to be associated with your donation.
* **Donation Notes** (Required: False, Type: Text): Provide any additional notes about your donation, such as the purpose or specific goods/services you wish to support.
* **Amount** (Required: False, Type: Number): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Payment Method** (Required: False, Type: Select One): Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu:
	+ **Credit Card**: Use a credit card to make a secure and convenient donation.
	+ **Bank Transfer**: Use a bank transfer to make a donation directly from your bank account.
* **Email** (Required: False, Type: Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates about your donation.
* **Phone** (Required: False, Type: Text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further instructions.
* **Address** (Required: False, Type: Text): Enter your address so we can deliver any goods or services associated with your donation.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter your email address and phone number correctly so that we can contact you with updates and further instructions.
