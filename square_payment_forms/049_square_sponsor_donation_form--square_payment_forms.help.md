# Square Sponsor Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Sponsor Donation Form is intended to collect donation information from sponsors. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is provided accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Form Data" section by providing the following information:
	* Amount: The amount of the donation.
	* Duration: The duration of the donation.
	* Notes: Any additional comments or information about the donation.
	* Email: The email address of the sponsor.
	* Time: The time of the donation.
	* Submission Date: The date of the submission.
	* Donation: The total donation amount.
* Select "Sponsor Info" options to indicate "Yes" or "No" for sponsorship.
2. Click submit to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, required): The main form section where you can enter all relevant information.
* **Sponsor Info** (`sponsor_info`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate sponsorship status.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): The amount of the donation.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, optional): The frequency of the donation (e.g., one-time, recurring).
* **Duration** (`duration`, text, required): The duration of the donation.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): The payment method used for the donation (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Any additional comments or information about the donation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): The email address of the sponsor.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): The time of the donation.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): The date of the submission.
* **Sponsor** (`sponsor`, text, required): The name of the sponsor.
* **Donation** (`donation`, number, required): The total donation amount.
* **Total Donation** (`total_donation`, number, required): The total donation amount.
