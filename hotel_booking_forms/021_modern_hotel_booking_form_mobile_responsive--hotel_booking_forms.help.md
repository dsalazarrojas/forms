# modern_hotel_booking_form_mobile_responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for hotel booking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before filling out the form.
* Scroll through the form pages to gather information for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (Customer Information, text, optional): This section is for providing basic customer information. Please fill in your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Booking Details** (Booking Details, text, optional): Enter the details of your booking, including dates and room preferences.
* **Special Requests** (Special Requests, text, optional): If you have any special requests (e.g., extra pillows, extra towels), list them here.
* **Payment Method** (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, required): Select one or more payment methods for your booking. Please note that credit card information will be collected securely and only for the purpose of booking processing.
* **Arrival Date** (Arrival Date, date, required): Enter your arrival date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Departure Date** (Departure Date, date, required): Enter your departure date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in the "Customer Information" and "Booking Details" sections to ensure smooth booking processing.
* Use the "Special Requests" section to specify any special needs or preferences.
* Make sure to select at least one payment method, as this is a required field.
