# Guest Acknowledgement Form For Bookings And Short Term Rentals - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather important information from guests who have made bookings or rentals in a hotel. It is used to ensure that the guest acknowledges their agreement with the hotel, provides accurate contact and payment information, and records any disputes that may have arisen during their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately, using the following fields to provide the required information:
	+ **Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	+ **Contact Info**: Please enter your contact information, including any phone numbers and email addresses that you would like us to use to contact you.
	+ **Address**: Please enter your current address, or the address of the hotel or rental location.
	+ **Payment Date**: Please enter the date of your payment.
	+ **Amount Paid**: Please enter the amount you paid for your stay.
	+ **Reason For Dispute**: Please enter any reason you may have had for disputing any charges or services provided during your stay.
	+ **Additional Notes**: Please enter any additional information that you feel is relevant to your stay.
	+ **CC Number**: Please enter your credit card number, without any dashes or spaces.
	+ **CC Month**: Please enter the month of your credit card expiration date.
	+ **CC Year**: Please enter the year of your credit card expiration date.
	+ **CVV**: Please enter your credit card verification value (the three or four digit code on the back of your card).
	+ **Exp. Month**: Please enter the month of your credit card expiration date.
	+ **Exp. Year**: Please enter the year of your credit card expiration date.
	+ **Bank Name**: Please enter the name of your bank or financial institution.
	+ **Bank Acct**: Please enter your bank account number, without any dashes or spaces.
	+ **Bank Routing**: Please enter your bank's routing number.
	+ **Bank ABA**: Please enter your bank's ABA number.
	+ **Check Number**: Please enter the number of your check (if applicable).
	+ **Dispute Date**: Please enter the date of any disputes that may have arisen during your stay.
	+ **Dispute Description**: Please enter a brief description of any disputes that may have arisen during your stay.
	+ **Dispute Amount**: Please enter the amount of any disputes that may have arisen during your stay.
	+ **CC Type**: Please select the type of credit card you used to make payment.
	+ **CC Provider**: Please select your credit card provider.
	+ **Dispute Status**: Please select the status of any disputes that may have arisen during your stay.
