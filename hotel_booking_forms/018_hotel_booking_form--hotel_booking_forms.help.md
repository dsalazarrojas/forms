# Hotel Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate hotel booking requests. It captures essential details from guests to ensure a smooth and efficient booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First name** in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the format "example@example.com" in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone number** without any special characters in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your **Checkin** date (arrival date) from the calendar icon.
6. Select your **Checkout** date (departure date) from the calendar icon.
7. For **Room type**, choose from the available options to select multiple types of rooms required.
8. Enter the **Room quantity** if you need more than one room.
9. In the **Special requests** field, type any additional requests or instructions you have for the hotel staff.
10. Select **Payment method** to confirm your preferred payment method:
    - "Yes" for credit card.
    - "No" for other payment methods.
11. If you selected "No" for **Payment method**, you can type your preferred **Other** in the "Other" field.
12. In **Card type**, choose from the available options (e.g., "Yes", "No") for credit card types.
13. Enter your **Card number** without any special characters.
14. Enter your **CVV** (card verification value) without any special characters.
15. Enter your **Expiration date** (card expiration date) in the format "MM/YY" without any special characters.
16. In **Security code** field, enter your security code.
17. Enter your **Address** in the format "Street, City, State ZIP".
18. Enter your **ZIP** code without any special characters.
19. Enter your **City** in the format "City".
20. Enter your **State** in the format "State".
21. Enter your **Country** in the format "Country".
22. For **Accept terms**, confirm that you have read and accepted the hotel's terms and conditions:
    - "Yes" for acceptance.
    - "No" for rejection.
23. If you selected "No" for **Accept terms**, provide your **Card Type Other** in the "Card Type Other" field.
24. If you selected "No" for **Accept terms**, enter your **Card Number Other** in the "Card Number Other" field.
25. If you selected "No" for **Accept terms**, enter your **Expiration Date Other** in the "Expiration Date Other" field.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter your email and phone number correctly to ensure smooth communication with the hotel.
* Be specific with your special requests to ensure the hotel staff can attend to them promptly.
* Confirm your payment method to avoid any misunderstandings.
* Enter your address and other contact details correctly to ensure efficient delivery of your booking documents.
* Review your form before submission to ensure all fields are filled correctly.
