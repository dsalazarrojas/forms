# Hotel Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Billing Form is used to collect information from guests or customers about their hotel billings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Start by entering the guest charges, selecting the applicable room rates, and choosing any additional services that were utilized.
* Select the due date for payment and enter the payment amount.
* Choose the payment method you would like to use (e.g. Credit Card, Bank Draft).
* Enter the customer's name, phone number, and email address for communication.
* Enter the customer's address for billing purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Charges** (`guest_charges`, number, optional): Enter the total amount charged to the guest.
* **Room Rates** (`room_rates`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more of the options provided (Good, Fair).
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, select one, optional): Choose one of the services provided (Service A, Service B).
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): Enter the date by which payment is due.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount to be paid.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select one, optional): Choose one of the payment methods (Credit Card, Bank Draft).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's full name.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address for billing purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the hotel staff for assistance.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly and efficient, so please take a few moments to review the form carefully before submission.
