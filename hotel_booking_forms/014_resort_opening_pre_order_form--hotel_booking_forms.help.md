# Resort Opening Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect information from potential guests who are interested in booking a room at a resort. This information will be used to finalize the booking process and ensure that the guest's needs are met during their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
3. If you have any special requests, please make sure to include them in the "Special Request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details**: This field is used to collect your name and address.
* **Preferred Stay Date**: Select the date you would like to stay at the resort. Please note that the date format is dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Preferred Stay Duration**: Choose the length of your stay, from 1 day to 3 days.
* **Package Choice**: Select the type of package you would like to book, from Deluxe, Standard, or Suite.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Preferred Stay End Date**: If you have a preferred end date for your stay, enter it here.
* **Special Request**: If you have any special requests or requirements, such as a specific room type or amenities, please enter them here.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Address (continued)**: If you have a lengthy address, you can continue entering it here.

**Tips**
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If you have any special requests, please make sure to include them in the "Special Request" field.
* Please enter a valid email address and phone number.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
