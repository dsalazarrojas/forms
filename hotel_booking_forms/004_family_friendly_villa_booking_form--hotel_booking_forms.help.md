# Family Friendly Villa Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Family Friendly Villa Booking Form" is used to gather essential information from users for booking a family-friendly villa. This form helps us understand your preferences and requirements for a comfortable and enjoyable stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as you will not be able to proceed without them.
2.  Ensure you enter accurate information for each field, especially the dates and room type.
3.  If you have any special requests or comments, please provide them in the "comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name** (`user_name`, text, not required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Email** (`user_email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further communication.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required\*): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
*   **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, date, required\*): Enter the date you plan to check-in at the villa.
*   **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, date, required\*): Enter the date you plan to check-out from the villa.
*   **Room Number** (`room_number`, number, required\*): Enter the room number you have selected for your stay.
*   **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_one, required\*): Choose the type of room that suits your needs: Apartment, Suite, or House.
*   **Adult Guests** (`adult_guests`, number, required\*): Enter the number of adults staying with you.
*   **Child Guests** (`child_guests`, number, required\*): Enter the number of children staying with you.
*   **Comment** (`comment`, note, not required): If you have any special requests or comments, please enter them here.

## Tips
Please double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors in processing your booking. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
