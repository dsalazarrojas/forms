# Hotel Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide hotel staff with necessary information to process a guest's hotel reservation request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the hotel you would like to book from the "Hotel" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your check-in date in the "Checkin" field.
3. Enter your check-out date in the "Checkout" field.
4. Select the type of room you would like to book from the "Room" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your first name in the "First" field.
6. Enter your last name in the "Last" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hotel**: Select the hotel you would like to book from the dropdown menu.
* **Checkin**: Enter the date you would like to check in (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Checkout**: Enter the date you would like to check out (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Room**: Select the type of room you would like to book.
* **First**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Last**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that you would like to use for correspondence.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any communication purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your check-in and check-out dates accurately to avoid any confusion.
* Selecting the correct room type will help the hotel staff to prepare for your stay.
* Double-check your personal details for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests, please include them in the form's comments section (not shown in this guide).
* Hotel staff will use this information to process your reservation request, so please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
