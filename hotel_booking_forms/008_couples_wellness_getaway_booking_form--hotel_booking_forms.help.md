# Couples Wellness Getaway Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help couples book a customized wellness getaway package at our resort, ensuring that they have a relaxing and rejuvenating stay.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To fill out the form, simply go through each page, and provide the required information. The form has 25 pages, but some pages might be redundant, so feel free to skip them if you're unsure.
2.  Be sure to review each page carefully, as some fields may have specific requirements or restrictions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Page 2: Guest Information** (`Guest Information`, `text`, required): This page is for providing your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourselves as a couple.
*   **Page 4: (page_4)** (`page_4`, `select_one`, required): This page is for selecting a specific wellness package or activity that interests you both.
*   **Page 8: Additional Information** (`page_8`, `text`, required): This page is for providing any additional information that might be helpful for our staff to know when preparing your stay.
*   **Page 16: (page_16)** (`page_16`, `number`, required): This page is for choosing the number of nights you plan to stay with us.
*   **Page 18: Additional Details** (`page_18`, `select_one`, required): This page is for selecting any additional details or amenities you might need for your stay.
*   **Page 20: Dates** (`page_20`, `date`, required): This page is for selecting your desired arrival and departure dates.
*   **Page 24: (page_24)** (`page_24`, `select_one`, required): This page is for choosing any specific services or activities you'd like to have during your stay.

## Tips
*   Be sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
