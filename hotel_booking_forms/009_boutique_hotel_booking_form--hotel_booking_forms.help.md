# Boutique Hotel Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boutique Hotel Booking Form is used to collect information from customers for booking a room at the boutique hotel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as the lead guest.
2. Provide your email address for booking confirmation.
3. Enter your contact phone number for check-in.
4. Select the date you plan to arrive at the hotel.
5. Select the date you plan to depart from the hotel.
6. Choose your preferred room type from the available options (Standard, Deluxe, or Suite).
7. Enter the total number of guests staying in your room.
8. If you have any special requests or needs, please provide them in this section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest name**: Please enter your full name as the lead guest.
* **Contact email**: This email will be used for booking confirmation and any further communication with the hotel. Please ensure that this is a valid and active email address.
* **Phone number**: Enter your contact phone number for the hotel staff to reach you for check-in and any other queries.
* **Check in date**: Select the date you plan to arrive at the hotel. Please note that this date will be used to confirm your booking.
* **Check out date**: Select the date you plan to depart from the hotel. Please ensure that this date is consistent with your check-in date.
* **Room type**: Choose your preferred room type from the available options (Standard, Deluxe, or Suite). Please note that the room type will affect the rate and availability.
* **Number of guests**: Enter the total number of guests staying in your room. This will help the hotel staff to prepare for your stay.
* **Special requests**: If you have any special requests or needs, please provide them in this section. This could include dietary requirements, mobility needs, or any other special requests. Please be as detailed as possible to ensure that the hotel staff can cater to your needs.
