# Hotel Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from hotel guests to provide a convenient and efficient way to register guests and their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your check-in date in the "Check-in Date" field.
5. Select your check-out date in the "Check-out Date" field.
6. Select the type of guest you are (Individual, Family, or Group) in the "Guest Type" field.
7. Select the type of room you are booking in the "Room Type" field.
8. If you have any specific room size preferences, select the size of the room you prefer in the "Room Size" field.
9. If you have any additional information about your reservation, type it in the "Reservation Details" field.
10. If you have any notes about your stay, type them in the "Guest Note" field.
11. Click "Submit" to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Name**: Enter your name in this field.
- **Email**: Enter your email address in this field.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number in this field.
- **Check-in Date**: Select your check-in date in this field.
- **Check-out Date**: Select your check-out date in this field.
- **Guest Type**: Select if you are an individual, family, or group.
- **Room Type**: Select the type of room you are booking.
- **Room Size**: Select the size of the room you prefer.
- **Reservation Details**: Provide any additional information about your reservation.
- **Guest Note**: Add any notes about your stay.
- **Staff Response**: This field is for hotel staff to enter their response to your request.
