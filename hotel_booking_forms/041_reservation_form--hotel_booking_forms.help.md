# Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reservation Form is used to collect information from guests for hotel reservations. This form is designed to gather essential details for a smooth and successful booking experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Choose your checkin and checkout dates.
6. Select the room type (Yes/No).
7. Enter the room number (if applicable).
8. If you have a specific room rate, enter it.
9. If you have a special rate that's not listed, you can specify it under "Room Rate Other".
10. If you have any special requests, type them in the "Special Requests" field.
11. Enter any additional comments or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Checkin** (Date): Select the date you plan to arrive at the hotel.
* **Checkout** (Date): Select the date you plan to leave the hotel.
* **Room Type** (Select Multiple): Choose from "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you require a specific room type.
* **Room Number** (Number): If you have a specific room number, enter it here. 
* **Room Rate** (Text): Enter a rate if you have one. If not, leave this field blank. 
* **Room Rate Other** (Text): If you have a room rate that's not listed, enter it here. 
* **Special Requests** (Note): Type in any special requests or instructions for your stay.
* **Comments** (Text): Enter any additional comments or instructions for your stay.

Note: This form is for hotel reservations, and all fields are not required, but providing accurate information will help ensure a smooth and successful booking experience. If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance. 

Tips: If you have any specific requests, please type them in the "Special Requests" field. If you have any additional comments, you can enter them in the "Comments" field.
