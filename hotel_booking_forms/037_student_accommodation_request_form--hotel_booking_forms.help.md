**Student Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

The Student Accommodation Request Form is used to collect information from students who are requesting accommodation at a university or hotel. This form helps us to understand your accommodation needs and preferences, so we can provide you with the best possible options.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your request type (yes or no)
2. Choose the type of room you require (single, double, or triple)
3. Enter your student name
4. Enter your accommodation dates (start and end dates)
5. Enter your preferred room type (single, double, or triple)
6. Enter your student email address
7. Enter your student phone number
8. Write a message to us with any additional information about your accommodation needs
9. Write a message to the accommodation team with any additional information about the room you are looking for

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Type**: Choose 'yes' if you are requesting accommodation, and 'no' if you are not.
* **Room Type**: Select the type of room you require, either single, double, or triple.
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your university or student ID card.
* **Dates**: Enter the dates when you require accommodation, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Accommodation Type**: Select the type of accommodation you require.
* **Preferred Room Type**: Select your preferred type of room.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when you want your accommodation to start.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when you want your accommodation to end.
* **Student Email**: Enter your university or student email address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your student phone number.
* **Student Message**: Write a message to us with any additional information about your accommodation needs.
* **Room Message**: Write a message to the accommodation team with any additional information about the room you are looking for.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Double-check your accommodation dates to ensure they are correct.
* If you have any additional requirements or requests, please include them in the student or room message fields.
* You can also upload any supporting documents or attachments with your form submission.
* If you have any questions or need help completing this form, please contact the accommodation team.
