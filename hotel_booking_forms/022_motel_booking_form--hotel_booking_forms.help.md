# Motel Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Motel Booking Form is designed to gather information from customers for their motel stay. This form will help us process their requests efficiently and ensure a smooth check-in process. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form correctly:

1.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4.  Choose the type of booking you require from the "Type of Booking" dropdown menu.
5.  Enter the number of rooms you need in the "Number of Rooms" field.
6.  Enter the number of adults in your group in the "Number of Adults" field.
7.  Enter the number of children in your group in the "Number of Children" field.
8.  Select your preferred accommodation options from the "Accommodation Option" dropdown menu.
9.  Enter the accommodation number you've selected in the "Accommodation Number" field.
10. Choose your check-in date in the "Check-In Date" field.
11. Choose your check-in time in the "Check-In Time" field.
12. Choose your check-out date in the "Check-Out Date" field.
13. Choose your check-out time in the "Check-Out Time" field.
14. If you have any comments or special requests, you can add them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Text, required: false)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (Email, required: false)**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (Text, required: false)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Type of Booking (Select One, required: false)**: Choose from Single Room, Double Room, Triple Room, or Suite.
* **Number of Rooms (Number, required: false)**: Enter the number of rooms you require for your stay.
* **Number of Adults (Number, required: false)**: Enter the number of adults in your group.
* **Number of Children (Number, required: false)**: Enter the number of children in your group.
* **Accommodation Option (Select Multiple, required: false)**: Choose from Single Room, Double Room, or Triple Room.
* **Accommodation Number (Number, required: false)**: Enter the number you've selected in the previous field.
* **Check-In Date (Date, required: false)**: Choose your check-in date for your stay.
* **Check-In Time (Time, required: false)**: Choose your check-in time for your stay.
* **Check-Out Date (Date, required: false)**: Choose your check-out date for your stay.
* **Check-Out Time (Time, required: false)**: Choose your check-out time for your stay.
* **Comments (Text, required: false)**: Add any additional comments or special requests.
