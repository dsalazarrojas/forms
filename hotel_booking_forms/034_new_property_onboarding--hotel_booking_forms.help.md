# New-Property-Onboarding - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for property owners to provide essential information about their properties, allowing them to effectively manage guest interactions and communications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property name in the "property name" field.
2. Enter the host name (the person responsible for the property).
3. Enter the guest name.
4. Choose the property type (Apartment, House, Resort, or Other).
5. Select the check-in date.
6. Select the check-out date.
7. Enter the arrival time.
8. Enter the room number.
9. Enter the room name.
10. Choose the room type (King, Queen, Twin, or Other).
11. (Optional) Select the bed types (Double, Single, Queen, or King).
12. (Optional) Choose whether to have extra beds (One, Two, or Three extra beds).
13. Select the property features (Balcony, TV, Air Conditioning, or other relevant options).
14. (Optional) Select any special features (Free WiFi, Gym, Swimming pool, or other relevant options).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **property name** (`property_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the property (e.g., "My Cozy Hotel").

### 2. **host name** (`host_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the person responsible for the property (e.g., "John Doe").

### 3. **guest name** (`guest_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the guest who will be staying at the property.

### 4. **property type** (`property_type`, select_one, required)
Choose the type of property (Apartment, House, Resort, or Other).

### 5. **checkin date** (`checkin_date`, date, required)
Select the date the guest will check-in to the property.

### 6. **checkout date** (`checkout_date`, date, required)
Select the date the guest will check-out of the property.

### 7. **arrival time** (`arrival_time`, time, required)
Enter the time the guest will arrive at the property.

### 8. **room number** (`room_number`, text, required)
Enter the room number where the guest will be staying.

### 9. **room name** (`room_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the room (e.g., "Deluxe Suite").

### 10. **room type** (`room_type`, select_one, required)
Choose the type of room (King, Queen, Twin, or Other).

### 11. **bed type** (`bed_type`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose the type of beds (Double, Single, Queen, or King). This field is optional.

### 12. **extra beds** (`extra_beds`, select_one, required)
Select whether to have extra beds (One, Two, or Three extra beds).

### 13. **property features** (`property_features`, select_multiple, required)
Choose the features of the property (Balcony, TV, Air Conditioning, or other options).

### 14. **special features** (`special_features`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose any special features (Free WiFi, Gym, Swimming pool, or other options). This field is optional.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out.
- Review the form carefully to avoid mistakes.
