# Airbnb Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Airbnb Check In Form is used to collect information from guests checking into an Airbnb unit, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable stay.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as it appears on your ID or reservation.
2. Provide your estimated arrival time.
3. Enter your ID number (e.g., passport number or ID card number).
4. If applicable, enter your room/unit number.
5. If you have a preferred contact method (phone number or email), enter it for easy communication with your host.
6. In case of an emergency, provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
7. Add any important notes for the host, such as key collection instructions.
8. Sign and confirm that you understand and agree to the rules and regulations outlined by the host.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest name**: <guest_name> (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or reservation.
* **Arrival time**: <arrival_time> (time, optional): Estimated time of arrival.
* **ID document number**: <id_document> (text, required): Passport or ID number for recordkeeping.
* **Room or unit number**: <room_number> (text, optional): If assigned, enter the unit number.
* **Contact phone**: <contact_phone> (text, optional): Preferred contact method (mobile phone number).
* **Emergency contact name and phone**: <emergency_contact> (text, optional): Name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Check in notes**: <check_in_notes> (text, optional): Any specific instructions or information for the host.
* **Guest acknowledgement**: <guest_signature> (note, required): Acknowledge and agree to the host's rules and regulations.

## Tips

* Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
* Ensure your arrival time is estimated correctly to avoid any misunderstandings.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your host directly.
