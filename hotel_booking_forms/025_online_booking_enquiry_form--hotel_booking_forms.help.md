# Online Booking Enquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers who are interested in making a booking but have not yet done so. It helps us to gather necessary information to process their inquiry and provide them with the best available options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information on the "Contact Information" page.
2. Select your accommodation type and dates of stay on the "Accommodation Details" page.
3. Enter your arrival and departure dates on the "Arrival Date" and "Departure Date" page.
4. Indicate the number of guests in your party on the "Number of Guests" page.
5. Add any additional feedback or comments about your stay on the "Feedback" page.
6. Share your experience with us on the "Experience" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and contact details so we can get in touch with you to process your inquiry.
* **Accommodation Details** (`Accommodation Details`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of accommodation you are interested in (single, double, suite) and we will let you know about availability.
* **Arrival Date** (`Arrival Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the hotel.
* **Departure Date** (`Departure Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to depart from the hotel.
* **Number of Guests** (`Number of Guests`, `number`, required/optional): Indicate the number of people in your party.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Add any comments or feedback you have about your stay.
* **Experience** (`Experience`, `text`, required/optional): Share any experience you have had with us before.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your dates and numbers for accuracy.
* If you have any special requests or questions, you can add them in the "Feedback" and "Experience" fields.
* We will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible after receiving your completed form.
