**day_out_of_days - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a day out of days. This form is designed to gather data on two dates and the number of days between them.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the "date1" page.
	* Enter the start date in the format of `mm/dd/yyyy`.
2. Go to the "date2" page.
	* Enter the end date in the format of `mm/dd/yyyy`.
3. On the "result" page.
	* Enter the number of days between the two dates in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **start_date**: <label> (start_date, date, required/optional: false)
	+ Enter the start date of the period being measured.
* **end_date**: <label> (end_date, date, required/optional: false)
	+ Enter the end date of the period being measured.
* **result**: <label> (result, text, required/optional: false)
	+ Enter the total number of days between the start and end dates.
