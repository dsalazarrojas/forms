# City Hostel Dorm Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Hostel Dorm Booking Form is designed to gather necessary information for a smooth and secure booking experience.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Enter Guest Full Name
*   Enter your full name in the format "First Name Last Name"
### Step 2: Enter Check In and Check Out Dates
*   Enter your arrival and departure dates in the correct format (e.g., 2024-02-20)
### Step 3: Select Bed Preference
*   Choose from the available bed options: Upper bunk, Lower bunk, or Single bed
### Step 4: Enter Number of Guests
*   Enter the total number of people in your booking
### Step 5: Enter Contact Email
*   Enter a valid email address for booking confirmation
### Step 6: Enter Contact Phone (Optional)
*   Enter your emergency contact phone number (optional)
### Step 7: Enter Special Requests (Optional)
*   Enter any accessibility or dietary notes
### Step 8: Select Payment Method
*   Choose your preferred payment option: Credit card, Bank transfer, or Pay on arrival
### Step 9: Review Booking Terms and Conditions
*   Read the cancellation policy and acknowledge that you have read it
