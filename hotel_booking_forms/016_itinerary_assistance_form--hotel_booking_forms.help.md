# Itinerary Assistance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers about their travel plans and requirements for assistance with hotel booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply read through each field and provide the necessary information. Please note that all fields are not required, but providing accurate and complete information will ensure that the hotel staff can better assist you with your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Travel Dates
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, `date`, Not Required): Enter your planned travel dates in the correct format (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY).

### 2. Participant Names
* **Participant Names** (`participant_names`, `text`, Not Required): List the names of all participants traveling with you.

### 3. Accommodations
* **Accommodations** (`accommodations`, `select_multiple`, Not Required): Check all that apply to indicate your accommodation preferences:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 4. Expected Length
* **Expected Length** (`expected_length`, `number`, Not Required): Enter the expected length of your stay.

### 5. Actual Length
* **Actual Length** (`actual_length`, `number`, Not Required): This field is for you to enter the actual length of your stay, which may differ from your expected length.

### 6. Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, Not Required): If you have any additional information about the participants, such as ages or special requests, please enter it here.

### 7. Accommodations Details
* **Accommodations Details** (`accommodations_details`, `text`, Not Required): If you have any specific requests or requirements for your accommodations, please enter it here.

### 8. Travel Documents
* **Travel Documents** (`travel_documents`, `select_multiple`, Not Required): Check all that apply to indicate your travel document requirements:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 9. Participant Status
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, `select_one`, Not Required): Select the status of the participants:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive

### 10. Participant Status Details
* **Participant Status Details** (`participant_status_details`, `text`, Not Required): If you have any additional information about the participant status, please enter it here.
