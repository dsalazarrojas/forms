# room_accommodation_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The room_accommodation_application form is designed to collect information for student housing requests, allowing students to book a room in a hotel or dormitory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by selecting the correct page (student_housing_request) that corresponds with your request.
2. Enter your preferred move in date in the "Move In Date" field. This is the date you would like to move into the accommodation.
3. If applicable, provide any special requirements you may have in the "Special Requirements" field.
4. Enter your preferred move-in time in the "Move In Time" field. This is the time you would like to arrive at the accommodation.
5. Specify the duration of your stay in the "Move In Duration" field. This will help us determine the length of your stay.
6. Enter your preferred move-out date in the "Move Out Date" field. This is the date you would like to move out of the accommodation.
7. If applicable, provide any additional special requirements in the "Special Requirements 2" field.
8. Enter your move-out time in the "Move Out Time" field. This is the time you would like to depart from the accommodation.
9. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field. This will help us contact you with any important information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Housing Request**: This is the page where you can start filling out the form. Please select this option if you're looking to book a room.
* **Move In Date**: Enter the date you would like to move into the accommodation.
* **Special Requirements**: If you have any special requirements or requests for your stay, please specify them here.
* **Move In Time**: Enter the time you would like to arrive at the accommodation.
* **Move In Duration**: Specify the duration of your stay in hours or days.
* **Move Out Date**: Enter the date you would like to move out of the accommodation.
* **Special Requirements 2**: If you have any additional special requirements, please specify them here.
* **Move Out Time**: Enter the time you would like to depart from the accommodation.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you with any important information.
