# Hotel Meeting And Booking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for hotel meetings and bookings. It helps us understand the requirements of the event, ensuring that the meeting or booking can be arranged effectively and efficiently.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form with all the required information.
2. Ensure that you have filled out all the fields accurately.
3. Review the information provided before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): This is the title of the event.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, optional): This is the date when you want to have the meeting.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, optional): This is the start time of the event.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, optional): This is a list of attendees that will be attending the event. Please choose 'Yes' if attendees will be present.
* **Meeting End Time** (`meeting_end_time`, time, optional): This is the end time of the event.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Please enter the total number of guests that will be attending the event.
* **Hotel Preferences** (`hotel_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): This is a list of the amenities you would like to have available during the event. Please choose 'Yes' if the amenity is desired.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, select_multiple, optional): This is a list of any special requests you may have for the event. Please choose 'Yes' if you have any specific requirements.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Please enter your name, email, and phone number for us to get in touch with you.
* **Hotel ID** (`hotel_id`, select_one, optional): This is not currently used and can be ignored.
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, text, optional): Please submit your request and we will get back to you with the status of your meeting or booking.

Note: All fields are optional, but it is recommended to fill out as much information as possible to ensure that we can effectively arrange your meeting or booking. 

## Tips
* Ensure that you have filled out all the required information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information before submitting to avoid any mistakes.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
