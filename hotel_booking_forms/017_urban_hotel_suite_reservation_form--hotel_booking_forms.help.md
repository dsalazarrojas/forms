# Urban Hotel Suite Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for guests to book a room at the Urban Hotel Suite. It collects necessary information to process the booking request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Guest Information** on page 1.
2. Select the type of **Room and Services** you require.
3. Choose your **Payment Information**.
4. Enter the details of your payment method on **Payment Details**.
5. Confirm your **Booking** on the last page.
6. Enter your **Contact Us** and **Phone Number**.
7. Provide your **Email Address**.
8. Select the **Date and Time** of your stay.
9. Add any **Special Requests**.
10. Confirm your **Phone Number**, **Email**, **Date**, and **Time**.
11. Select your **Room Type**, **Bed Type**, and **Meal Plan**.
12. Confirm your **Payment Method** and **Card Number**.
13. Enter the **Expiration Date** of your card.
14. Add any **Special Requests**.
15. Confirm your booking by reviewing and confirming all previous fields.
