# Holiday Package Hotel Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Package Hotel Pre Order Form is used to gather information for holiday package hotel bookings. Fill out this form to provide your details for a smooth and efficient booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the hotel details that best suit your needs on **Page 2**.
2. Enter your contact information on **Page 3**, **Page 4**, and **Page 5**.
   - **Page 3** is for your name.
   - **Page 4** is for your email address.
   - **Page 5** is for your phone number.
3. Optionally, provide any additional information on **Page 6**.
4. Select a package option on **Page 10** that best suits your requirements.
5. If needed, enter any other information on the subsequent pages (Pages 7-9, 11-25).
