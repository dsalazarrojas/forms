# Hotel Room Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for hotel room booking purposes, including personal details, room preferences, and booking dates.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4.  Indicate if you prefer a room with **Yes** or **No** **Room Type** in the "Room Type" field. This field allows you to specify if you have any specific room preferences (e.g., smoking or non-smoking).
5.  Select the **Booking Date** for your stay using the date format (year, month, day). This is the date you plan to arrive at the hotel.
6.  Enter your preferred **Room Number** in the "Room Number" field. This field is optional, but filling it out can help the hotel prepare your room.
7.  Choose whether you want a room with **Yes** or **No** **Room Size** in the "Room Size" field. This field likely refers to whether you need a larger or smaller room.
8.  Provide your preferred **Arrival Time** using the time format (hour, minute, AM/PM) in the "Arrival Time" field. This helps the hotel staff anticipate your arrival.
9.  Enter your preferred **Departure Date** using the date format (year, month, day) in the "Departure Date" field.
10.  In the **Room Amenities** field, select **Yes** or **No** for any amenities you require in your room, such as a bathtub or a microwave.
11.  Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field, which is optional but can help the hotel staff contact you.
