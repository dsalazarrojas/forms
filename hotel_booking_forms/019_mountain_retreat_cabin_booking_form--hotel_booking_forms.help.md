The purpose of this form is to facilitate the booking of a Mountain Retreat Cabin. It is designed to gather essential information from the user, including check-in and check-out dates, cabin type, guest details, and agreement to terms and conditions.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the dates you wish to arrive and depart from the "checkin" and "checkout" fields.
2.  Choose the type of cabin you desire from the "cabin_type" field.
3.  Provide the guest's name and phone number in the "guest_name" and "guest_phone" fields, respectively.
4.  Enter the guest's email address in the "guest_email" field.
5.  In the "terms_and_agreement" field, select the checkbox for "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions" to confirm your understanding and agreement.
6.  Review the terms and conditions carefully in the "terms_and_conditions" field.
7.  Finally, select the "submit" option that reflects your status regarding the terms and conditions.

# Mountain Retreat Cabin Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the booking of a Mountain Retreat Cabin.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Checkin** (`checkin`, date, required/optional): Select the date you wish to arrive.
* **Checkout** (`checkout`, date, required/optional): Select the date you wish to depart.
* **Cabin Type** (`cabin_type`, text, required/optional): Choose the type of cabin you desire.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required/optional): Provide the guest's name.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the guest's phone number.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Terms and Agreement** (`terms_and_agreement`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions" to confirm your understanding and agreement.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required/optional): Review the terms and conditions carefully.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select the option that reflects your status regarding the terms and conditions.
