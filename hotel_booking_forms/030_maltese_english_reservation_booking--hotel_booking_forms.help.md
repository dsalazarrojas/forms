# Maltese English Reservation & Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from guests for hotel reservation and booking purposes. It captures details about the guests and their preferences to ensure a smooth and efficient booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the First name field.
2. Enter your Last name in the Last name field.
3. Enter your Checkin date in the Checkin date field (date format: dd/mm/yyyy).
4. Enter your Checkout date in the Checkout date field (date format: dd/mm/yyyy).
5. Enter your E-mail address in the E-mail field (format: user@example.com).
6. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
7. Select your preferred Room type from the Room type dropdown list.
8. Enter the Number of guests in the Number of guests field.
9. Enter your Arrival time in the Arrival time field (24-hour clock: HH:MM).
10. Select the Event type that you are booking for from the Event type dropdown list.
11. Enter the Event date in the Event date field (date format: dd/mm/yyyy).
12. Enter any additional Notes about your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Checkin date**: Enter the date you will be arriving at the hotel.
* **Checkout date**: Enter the date you will be departing from the hotel.
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address as per your registered contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number as per your contact information.
* **Room type**: Select the type of room that suits your needs:
	+ Single
	+ Double
	+ Suite
* **Number of guests**: Enter the total number of people staying with you.
* **Arrival time**: Enter your estimated arrival time at the hotel (24-hour clock: HH:MM).
* **Event type**: Select the type of event you are booking for:
	+ Party
	+ Event
	+ Meeting
* **Event date**: Enter the date of your event.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your stay.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your stay.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests, please mention them in the Notes field.
