# hotel_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "hotel_check_in_form" is used to collect client information for hotel check-in purposes. This form is typically completed by clients during their stay at the hotel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information and any optional fields as needed.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid any submission errors.
3. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the client plans to check in to the hotel.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the client plans to check out of the hotel.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure successful submission.
* If you need assistance with filling out the form, please contact hotel staff for help.
* The form is for client information purposes only; sensitive information should not be included.
