# Researcher Collaboration Lodge Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Researcher Collaboration Lodge Booking Form is designed to help researchers book a room in the collaboration lodge. This form gathers information about the researcher's stay, including arrival and departure dates, room preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, which is indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
3. Choose your preferred room type from the dropdown menu.
4. Add any additional requirements or comments in the "Additional Requirements" field.
5. Submit the form to complete your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **researcher collaboration lodge booking form**: This is the title of the form, which is not a field to fill out.
* **start date**: Enter the start date of your stay.
* **end date**: Enter the end date of your stay.
* **arrival date**: Enter the date you will arrive at the lodge.
* **departure date**: Enter the date you will depart from the lodge.
* **number of guests**: Enter the number of people in your group.
* **room type** (Single Room, Double Room, Triple Room, Other): Choose the type of room you prefer.
* **additional requirements**: If you have any special requirements or requests, please list them here.
* **name**: Enter your name.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **organization**: Enter the name of your organization or institution.
* **department**: Enter the name of your department or team.
* **project title**: Enter the title of your project.
* **project description**: Enter a brief description of your project.
* **arrival time**: Enter the time you will arrive at the lodge.
* **note**: If you have any additional comments or requests, please write them here.
* **arrival time**: Enter the time you will arrive at the lodge.
* **departure time**: Enter the time you will depart from the lodge.
* **number of days**: Enter the number of days you plan to stay.
* **start time**: Enter the start time of your stay.
* **end time**: Enter the end time of your stay.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
