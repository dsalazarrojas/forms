# Hostel Booking Form Light Coral And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for booking a room at a hostel or hotel. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To book a room, please fill out the form with your personal and accommodation preferences. You can fill out the form at your own pace, and you can skip questions that are not relevant to your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Check In Date** (date): Select your check-in date.
* **Check Out Date** (date): Select your check-out date.
* **Room Type** (select_one): Choose the type of room you require (Single, Double, or Triple).
* **Number of Adults** (number): Enter the number of adults staying with you.
* **Number of Children** (number): Enter the number of children staying with you.
* **Extra Service** (select_multiple): Select any extra services you require, such as room cleaning or food service.
* **Payment Method** (select_one): Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
* **Note** (note): Enter any additional information or requests you have for your stay.
* **Category** (select_multiple): Select the category of accommodation you require (Hotel or Hostel).
