# Hostel Booking Form Light Olive And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a room in the hostel. It will guide you through the necessary information required to complete your booking.

## How To Complete This Form
Please ensure you fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk. If a field is optional but still needs to be completed, fill out the necessary information for it. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email in this format: `your-email@example.com`.
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you wish to check in on, in the format `DD/MM/YYYY`.
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you wish to check out on, in the format `DD/MM/YYYY`.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, `text`, required): Choose the room type from the options provided.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, `text`, required): Enter the room number you wish to book.
* **Bed Type** (`bed_type`, `text`, required): Choose the bed type from the options provided.
* **Guests** (`guests`, `number`, required): Enter the number of guests in your group.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your address in the format `street-name, city, state, zip`.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Enter any additional notes or requests you may have for your stay.
* **Room Type** (`select_room_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the room type(s) you wish to book from the options provided.
* **Bed Type** (`select_bed_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the bed type from the options provided.
* **Genders** (`select_genders`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the gender(s) of the guests.
* **Meals Plan** (`select_meal`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the meal plan(s) you would like to have.
* **Language** (`select_language`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred language.
* **Smoking** (`select_smoking`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether or not you smoke.
* **Parking** (`select_parking`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether or not you require parking.
* **Cancellation** (`select_cancellation`, `select_one`, required): Choose the cancellation policy you prefer.
* **Checkin Time** (`select_checkin`, `select_one`, required): Choose the checkin time you prefer.
* **Checkout Time** (`select_checkout`, `select_one`, required): Choose the checkout time you prefer.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you wish to stay on, in the format `DD/MM/YYYY`.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you wish to arrive on, in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Notes** (`note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes or requests you may have for your stay.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the user assigned to your booking.
* **Select Room Type** (`select_room_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the room type(s) you wish to book from the options provided.
* **Select Bed Type** (`select_bed_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the bed type from the options provided.
