# Hotel RFP Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from the user for a hotel request for proposal (RFP). The purpose of this form is to collect contact details, event details, room requirements, and other relevant information to help the hotel assess the user's needs and provide an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your contact details, including your email address, phone number, and a brief message about your request.
2. Next, provide event details such as the date you plan to arrive and depart, and the time you would like to arrive and depart.
3. Select the type of room you are interested in.
4. Provide a brief description of the event, including its type, size, and frequency.
5. Finally, review your answers and ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Please enter your email address, phone number, and a brief message about your request.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Please enter the date you plan to arrive and depart, and provide a brief description of the event.
* **Room Details** (`room_details`): Please enter the details of the room you are interested in, including the room rate.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`): Please select the type of room you are interested in.
* **Date In** (`date_in`): Please enter the date you plan to arrive.
* **Date Out** (`date_out`): Please enter the date you plan to depart.
* **Time In** (`time_in`): Please enter the time you plan to arrive.
* **Time Out** (`time_out`): Please enter the time you plan to depart.
* **Note** (`note`): Please enter any additional comments or instructions.
* **Email** (`email`): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Please enter your phone number.
* **Message** (`message`): Please enter a brief message about your request.
* **Name** (`name`): Please enter your name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Please enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`): Please enter your department.
* **Company** (`company`): Please enter the name of your company.
* **Address** (`address`): Please enter your company's address.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`): Please enter the room number you are interested in.
* **Room Rate** (`room_rate`): Please enter the room rate you are willing to pay.
* **Room Amenity** (`room_amenity`): Please enter any additional room amenities you would like to include.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`): Please enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Please select the type of event you are hosting.
* **Event Size** (`event_size`): Please select the size of the event.
* **Event Frequency** (`event_frequency`): Please select the frequency of the event.
