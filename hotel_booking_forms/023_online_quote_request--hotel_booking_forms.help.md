# online_quote_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_quote_request form is used to request quotes or estimates for travel plans, such as tour, hotel, flight, or cruise bookings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your travel details in the "Travel Details" field. This is the starting point for our team to gather all the necessary information to provide an accurate quote for your trip.
2.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This will ensure that our team can contact you with any questions or updates about your quote.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This is an optional field, but it will help our team contact you if we need more information to complete your quote.
5.  Select your travel date in the "Travel Date" field. Please make sure to select the correct date for your trip.
6.  Choose your travel time in the "Travel Time" field. Please select the correct time of your trip.
7.  Select the location you plan to travel to in the "Travel Location" field. Choose from the options provided: Paris, Rome, or Tokyo.
8.  Choose the type of travel you are interested in by selecting the option from the "Travel Type" field. Choose from Tour, Hotel, Flight, or Cruise.
9.  Enter your budget in the "Budget" field. Please be as accurate as possible with your budget constraints.
10.  If you have any special requests or additional information, please provide it in the "Special Requests" field.
11.  If there is a specific tool assigned to you for this project, please enter it in the "Assigned Tool" field.
12.  Enter any additional category information in the "Category" field.
13.  If you want to attach an output file, please provide it in the "Output File" field.
14.  Enter any form IDs in the "Form Ids" field.
15.  Enter a brief description of your request in the "Description" field.
16.  Enter a title for your request in the "Form Title" field.
17.  Enter a category for your request in the "Form Category" field.
18.  Enter a second category for your request in the "Form Category2" field.
19.  Enter a brief description of your request in the "Description2" field.
20.  Enter a third category for your request in the "Category3" field.
21.  Enter a fourth category for your request in the "Form Category4" field.
22.  Enter a brief description of your request in the "Description4" field.
23.  Enter a fifth category for your request in the "Category5" field.
24.  Enter a brief description of your request in the "Description5" field.
