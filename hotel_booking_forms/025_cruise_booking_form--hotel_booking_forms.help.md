# Cruise_Booking_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cruise_Booking_Form is used to collect customer information for booking cruises. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the travel agent who will be handling your booking.
3. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you with updates and confirmation.
4. Write any special requests or notes that you'd like to include with your booking.
5. Choose your preferred payment method.
6. Add any additional comments or messages to the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information that we should know.
- **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, select_one, optional): Select the travel agent who will be handling your booking.
- **Ship Info** (`ship_info`, text, optional): Enter details about the ship you're booking, such as its name or type.
- **Cabin Info** (`cabin_info`, text, optional): Provide any specific cabin preferences or requirements you have.
- **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, optional): Enter the dates of your cruise or trip.
- **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, optional): Provide the start and end times of your trip.
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Mention any special requests or needs you have, such as dietary restrictions.
- **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter any payment details or references you'd like to include with your booking.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred payment method, such as credit card or bank transfer.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your booking.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and confirmation.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can call you with important updates.
- **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, optional): Write a message to the customer, such as a welcome or introduction.
