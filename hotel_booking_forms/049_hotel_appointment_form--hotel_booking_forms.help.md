# Hotel Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Appointment Form is designed to collect information for hotel appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the correct page for each section.
2. Fill in the required information in each page.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hotel Reservation Form (page 1)**: This is the first page of the form. It's a text field where you can enter any information you want to include in your reservation.
* **Guest Details (page 2)**: Here, you can enter the guest's details such as name, address, etc.
* **Room Details (page 3)**: Enter the room details you require, including room rate and room type.
* **Room Rate (page 4)**: Choose the room rate you require, which can be either Good or Fair.
* **Checkin Check Out (page 5)**: Enter the check-in and check-out dates and times.
* **Room Type (page 6)**: Select the room type you require, such as Single, Double, or Suite room.
* **Room Services (page 7)**: Enter any additional room services you need.
* **Payment Terms (page 8)**: Enter the payment terms you require, such as payment methods or terms.
* **Special Requirements (page 9)**: Enter any special requirements you have for the room or stay.
* **Hotel Name (page 10)**: Enter the name of the hotel where you are staying.
* **Guest Name (page 11)**: Enter your name or the name of the guest you are booking for.
* **Address (page 12)**: Enter the address of the guest you are booking for.
* **Date (page 13)**: Enter the date of the booking.
* **Time (page 14)**: Enter the time of the booking.
* **Phone (page 15)**: Enter the phone number of the guest or contact person.
* **Email (page 16)**: Enter the email of the guest or contact person.
* **Comments (page 17)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the booking.
* **Form Submission (page 18)**: This is the final page where you can review your submission before submitting it.
