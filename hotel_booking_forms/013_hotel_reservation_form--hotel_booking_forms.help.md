# Hotel Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hotel reservation form is designed to collect information from guests to create a reservation at the hotel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Reservation Date" field.
2. Select the date of your desired reservation from the date picker.
3. Enter your "Guest Name" in the text field.
4. Enter the "Room Number" you are interested in.
5. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you'd like to make your reservation. Please note that the date format is in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person making the reservation.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, number, optional): Enter the specific room you are interested in.

## Tips
- Please double-check the reservation date before submitting the form.
- Make sure to enter your name correctly to ensure that you can be contacted for any updates or changes to your reservation.
- If you're unsure about which room to choose, you can ask the hotel staff for assistance.
