# corporate_housing_lease_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a corporate housing lease booking request.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please ensure you have all the necessary information with you, such as arrival and departure dates, cities and airports, number of guests, and accommodation preferences.
- Start by filling out the form with your arrival date, check-in date and check-out date.
- Enter the arrival city and arrival airport for your trip.
- Enter the departure city and departure airport for your trip.
- Enter the number of guests that will be staying in the accommodation.
- Select the accommodation preferences from the drop-down menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Arrival Date** (`checkin_date`): Enter the date you arrive at the destination.
* **Arrival Date 2** (`checkin_date_2`): Enter the date you arrive at the destination (Note: This field is likely a duplicate of the first arrival date field and may be removed or corrected).
* **Departure Date** (`checkout_date`): Enter the date you depart from the destination.
* **Arrival City** (`arrival_city`): Enter the city where you will be arriving.
* **Arrival Airport** (`arrival_airport`): Enter the airport code (e.g. JFK, LAX) where you will be arriving.
* **Departure City** (`departure_city`): Enter the city where you will be departing.
* **Departure Airport** (`departure_airport`): Enter the airport code (e.g. JFK, LAX) where you will be departing.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`): Enter the number of guests that will be staying in the accommodation.
* **Accommodation Preferences** (`corporate_housing_lease_booking_form_8`): Select your accommodation preferences from the drop-down menu.
* **Additional Accommodation Information** (`corporate_housing_lease_booking_form_9`): Select any additional accommodation preferences or options (Note: This field is a select multiple field with multiple options, but it's not entirely clear what it represents).
* **Notes** (`corporate_housing_lease_booking_form_10`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your booking.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the corporate travel department.
- This form is designed for corporate housing lease bookings, not personal or leisure travel bookings.
