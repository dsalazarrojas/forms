# Lodge Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to reserve a room at a hotel or resort. Please fill it out to provide us with the necessary information about your stay.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your reason for visiting (Business or Leisure).
2. Choose your check-in and check-out dates.
3. Select the number of guests who will be staying with you.
4. Indicate the type of room you would like to reserve.
5. If you have any special requests, please let us know.
6. Review and submit your form to complete the reservation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reserve for lodging** (text, required): Enter the reason for your visit (e.g., leisure, business, etc.).
* **On business or leisure travelling** (select_one, required): Choose between 'Leisure' or 'Business' to indicate the purpose of your visit.
* **Check in date** (date, required): Enter the date you would like to check-in to the hotel.
* **Check out date** (date, required): Enter the date you would like to check-out of the hotel.
* **Number of guests** (select_multiple, required): Select the number of guests who will be staying with you (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Room type** (select_multiple, required): Choose the type of room you would like to reserve (e.g., single, double, suite, suite with beds, family room).
* **Special requests** (text, optional): If you have any special requests or preferences, please let us know here.
* **Output fields** (note, not applicable): This field is not used for user input and is only for internal use.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" or "not applicable" can be ignored and are not required to be filled out.
