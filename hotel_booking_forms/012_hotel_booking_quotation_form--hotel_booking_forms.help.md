# Hotel Booking Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Booking Quotation Form is designed to collect essential information from clients for the purpose of providing an accurate quote for their hotel booking needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Review the form carefully and fill out each field accurately to provide the best possible quote for your client.
2.  Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3.  Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Below is a detailed explanation of each field:

*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_1** (`text`, not required)
    *   Please enter the client's name or the name of the person responsible for the booking.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_2** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter the client's email address for easy contact.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_3** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter a short description of the client's requirements.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_4** (`text`, not required)
    *   Describe the type of accommodation the client is looking for.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_5** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter the number of people traveling.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_6** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter the travel dates (arrival and departure dates).
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_7** (`text`, not required)
    *   Describe any special requirements or requests the client may have.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_8** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter the preferred method of communication (email, phone, etc.).
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_9** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter the client's preferred mode of transportation.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_10** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter any additional services required (food, activities, etc.).
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_11** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter any other special requests or requirements.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_12** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter the client's budget for the booking.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_13** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter any special amenities the client requires.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_14** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter the client's preferred accommodation type.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_15** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter the number of rooms required.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_16** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter the number of bedrooms required.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_17** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter the number of beds required.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_18** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter any special requests for the bedrooms.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_19** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter any special requests for the beds.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_20** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter the client's preferred payment method.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_21** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter any special requirements for payment.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_22** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter any additional requirements.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_23** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter any special notes or comments.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_24** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter any other requirements.
*   **hotel\_booking\_form\_25** (`text`, not required)
    *   Enter any other comments or notes.

## Tips
*   Be sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Ensure all required fields are completed.
*   Keep the form concise and clear to ensure the best possible quote for the client.
