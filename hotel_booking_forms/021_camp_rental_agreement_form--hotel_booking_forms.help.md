# Camp Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide camp administrators with essential information to confirm a rental request. It helps ensure that all necessary details are collected accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Please enter your first name and last name in the Renter First Name and Renter Last Name fields.
2. If you are renting on behalf of an organization, please enter the Organization Name.
3. Enter your contact email and phone number in the Email Address and Phone Number fields.
4. Provide your street address in the Address field.
5. Enter the city and state in the City and State fields.
6. Enter your zip code in the ZIP Code field.
7. Select the purpose of your rental in the Purpose of Rental field.
8. Choose the facilities or areas you need for your event in the Facility or Area Requested field.
9. Enter the start and end dates for your rental in the Rental Start Date and Rental End Date fields.
10. Enter the start and end times for your rental in the Rental Start Time and Rental End Time fields.
11. Enter the estimated number of guests in the Estimated Number of Guests field.
12. If you are renting for an overnight event, enter the number of overnight guests in the Number of Overnight Guests field.
13. Select the age range of guests in the Age Range of Guests field.
14. If you need catering services, select the type of meal service in the Catering Services Needed field.
15. If you need equipment rental, select the type of equipment in the Equipment Rental Needed field.
16. If you have any special requirements or accommodations, enter them in the Special Requirements or Accommodations field.
17. Enter the total rental fee in the Total Rental Fee field.
18. Enter the security deposit in the Security Deposit field.
19. Select the payment schedule in the Payment Schedule field.
20. Confirm that you have read and understand the cancellation policy in the Cancellation Policy Acknowledged field.
21. Confirm that you accept responsibility for any damages in the Damage Responsibility Acknowledgement field.
22. Confirm that you have read and agree to follow the camp rules and regulations in the Camp Rules and Regulations Acknowledged field.
23. If you have event insurance, select Yes - will provide certificate or No - need information in the Do You Have Event Insurance field.
24. Enter your signature in the Renter Signature field, typing your full name as it appears on your signature.
25. Enter the date of your signature in the Date Signed field.
26. If you would like to contact the camp representative, enter their name in the Camp Representative Name field.
27. If you would like the camp representative to sign on your behalf, enter their signature in the Camp Representative Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Renter First Name** (`renter_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Renter Last Name** (`renter_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, optional): If renting on behalf of an organization, enter the organization's name.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your contact email.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Provide your street address.
*   **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city or town.
*   **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province.
*   **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter the postal code.
*   **Purpose of Rental** (`rental_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Select the purpose of your rental (e.g., Corporate retreat, Church group, etc.).
*   **Facility or Area Requested** (`facility_requested`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all facilities or areas you need for your event.
*   **Rental Start Date** (`rental_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the check-in date.
*   **Rental Start Time** (`rental_start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the check-in time.
*   **Rental End Date** (`rental_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the check-out date.
*   **Rental End Time** (`rental_end_time`, `time`, required): Enter the check-out time.
*   **Estimated Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of guests.
*   **Number of Overnight Guests** (`number_of_overnight_guests`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of guests staying overnight.
*   **Age Range of Guests** (`age_range_of_guests`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the age range of guests (e.g., Under 12, 13-17, etc.).
*   **Catering Services Needed** (`catering_needed`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of meal service you need.
*   **Equipment Rental Needed** (`equipment_rental`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of equipment you need.
*   **Special Requirements or Accommodations** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requirements or accommodations.
*   **Total Rental Fee** (`rental_fee`, `number`, required): Enter the total rental fee.
*   **Security Deposit** (`security_deposit`, `number`, required): Enter the security deposit.
*   **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment schedule.
*   **Cancellation Policy Acknowledged** (`cancellation_policy_acknowledged`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the cancellation policy.
*   **Damage Responsibility Acknowledgement** (`damage_responsibility`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you accept responsibility for any damages.
*   **Camp Rules and Regulations Acknowledged** (`rules_and_regulations`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to follow the camp rules and regulations.
*   **Do You Have Event Insurance** (`insurance_required`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have event insurance.
*   **Renter Signature** (`renter_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your signature, typing your full name as it appears on your signature.
*   **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your signature.
*   **Camp Representative Name** (`camp_representative`, `text`, optional): If you would like to contact the camp representative, enter their name.
*   **Camp Representative Signature** (`camp_representative_signature`, `text`, optional): If you would like the camp representative to sign on your behalf, enter their signature.
