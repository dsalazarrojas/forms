# Disney’s Aulani Resort Vacation Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a vacation quote from Disney’s Aulani Resort. It gathers information about travel agents and their customers to facilitate the quote process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes as needed in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Arrival Date (Page 1)
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the resort.

### Check In Check Out Dates (Page 2)
* **Check In Check Out Dates** (`checkin_check_out_dates`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select yes or no to indicate whether you have specific check in and check out dates for your trip.

### Guest Type (Page 3)
* **Guest Type** (`guest_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of guest (yes or no) indicating if you are booking a group.

### Guests (Page 4)
* **Guests** (`guests`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of people in your group.

### Travel Agent (Page 5)
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your travel agent's name or agency name.

### Travel Agency (Page 6)
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your travel agency's name or address.

### Travel Agency Address (Page 7)
* **Travel Agency Address** (`travel_agency_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your travel agency's address.

### Contact Name (Page 8)
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.

### Contact E-mail (Page 9)
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the contact person.

### Contact Phone (Page 10)
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

### Travel Agent Notes (Page 11)
* **Travel Agent Notes** (`travel_agent_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments from the travel agent.

### Travel Agent Authorized (Page 12)
* **Travel Agent Authorized** (`travel_agent_authorized`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select whether the travel agent is authorized to proceed with the quote.

### Travel Agency Notes (Page 13)
* **Travel Agency Notes** (`travel_agency_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from the travel agency.

### Comments (Page 14)
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your trip.

### Quote Request (Page 15)
* **Quote Request** (`quote_request`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select yes or no to indicate if you are requesting a quote.

Note: This help guide assumes that the form is not filled out completely. If a field is not required, it can be left blank, but make sure to fill in all required fields.
