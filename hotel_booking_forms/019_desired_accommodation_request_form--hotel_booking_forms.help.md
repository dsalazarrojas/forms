# desired_accommodation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "desired_accommodation_request_form" is a form designed to collect information about your desired accommodation for booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant details about your desired accommodation.
2. Fill in your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Accommodation details** (`desired_accommodation_request_form_page_1`, text, optional): Describe your desired accommodation in your own words.
* **Accommodation type** (`accommodation_type`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or more of the following options: Hotel, Hostel, Motel, Other
* **Check-in date** (`check_in_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to check-in.
* **Check-out date** (`check_out_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to check-out.
* **Room type** (`room_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one of the following options: Single, Double, Suite, Other
* **Room capacity** (`room_capacity`, number, optional): Enter the number of people that will be staying in the room.
* **Number of guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Enter the total number of guests.
* **Contact name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
