# Guest Transport Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect important information about your guest's transport needs to ensure you get the best experience at our hotel.
## How To Complete This Form
- To start, select the "Form Submitted" field and indicate whether the form has been submitted or not.
- If the form has been submitted, select the correct date and time of submission.
- Then, please provide the following details:
  - Your arrival and departure times using the "Pick Up Time" and "Drop Off Time" fields.
  - Your preferred transport method, such as taxi, bus, or train.
  - The type of travel you are doing (e.g., Day, Evening, Late Evening, Early Morning).
  - Whether you would like to travel alone or with a driver.
  - If you have any special instructions or requirements, feel free to type them in the "Special Instructions" field.
- Finally, provide any additional information you think is helpful for us to know.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submitted** (Yes/No): Check this field to indicate whether the form has been filled out or not.
* **Guest Arrival Details**: Please enter your arrival details, including arrival time and any other relevant information.
* **Guest Transport Method** (Taxi, Bus, Train, Other): Select the transport method you would like to use.
* **Pick Up Time**: Enter the time you would like to be picked up.
* **Drop Off Time**: Enter the time you would like to be dropped off.
* **Travel Type** (Day, Evening, Late Evening, Early Morning): Select the type of travel you are doing.
* **Transport Type** (Self Drive, Driver, Airport Pick Up): Select how you would like to be transported.
* **Special Instructions**: If you have any special requests or requirements, type them here.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information you think is helpful for us to know.
* **Guest Confirmation** (Yes/No): Confirm that the form has been filled out by you.
* **Form Submitted Date**: If the form has been submitted, enter the date of the submission.
* **Form Submitted Time**: If the form has been submitted, enter the time of the submission.
