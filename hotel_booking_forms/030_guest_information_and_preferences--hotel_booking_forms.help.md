# Guest Information And Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide personal and preference information for guests visiting the hotel.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the date of your arrival in the "Arrival Date" field.
4. Select the time of your arrival in the "Arrival Time" field.
5. Choose the length of your stay in the "Stay Length" field.
6. Select your preferred language from the "Preferred Language" dropdown list.
7. If applicable, select your stay preferences from the "Stay Preferences" multi-select list.
8. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" text field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): Select the date of your arrival at the hotel.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required): Select the time of your arrival at the hotel.
* **Stay Length** (`stay_length`, number, required): Choose the number of days you plan to stay at the hotel.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_one, required): Select your preferred language from the list.
* **Stay Preferences** (`stay_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional preferences for your stay, such as extra towels or a non-smoking room.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or requests you may have for your stay.
