# mental_health_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health referral form is designed to collect information related to mental health referrals. This form helps healthcare professionals or administrators gather relevant details for referring clients to mental health services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields marked with a red \*.
3.  Provide as much detail as possible in the remaining fields.
4.  Review the Terms and Conditions section and ensure you agree to them.
5.  Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields and reviewed the Terms and Conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information** (1, text, **required: false**):
    *   Please provide the client's name, address, and any other relevant information.
*   **Referral Reason** (2, text, **required: false**):
    *   Describe the reason for the referral.
*   **Mental Health Professional** (3, select_one, **required: false**):
    *   Select the type of mental health professional you are referring the client to, such as Psychologist or Social Worker.
*   **Appointment Date** (4, date, **required: false**):
    *   Enter the date of the scheduled appointment.
*   **Appointment Time** (5, time, **required: false**):
    *   Enter the time of the scheduled appointment.
*   **Phone** (6, text, **required: false**):
    *   Provide the client's phone number.
*   **Email** (7, email, **required: false**):
    *   Enter the client's email address.
*   **Note** (8, note, **required: false**):
    *   Add any additional comments or notes about the client.
*   **Required Fields Are Marked With A Red \*** (9, note, **required: false**):
    *   Please review the form to ensure you have completed all required fields before submitting.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (10, select_multiple, **required: false**):
    *   Review the terms and conditions of the referral and select 'Yes' if you agree to them.
*   **Submission Date** (11, date, **required: false**):
    *   This field is automatically filled in upon form submission and indicates when the form was submitted.

Note: The form is designed to be flexible and allows for optional fields to be filled in as needed. However, please ensure you have completed all the required fields before submitting the form.
