# Mental Health Safety Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Mental Health Safety Plan is a tool to help individuals identify potential safety concerns and prepare for mental health crises. This form is designed to be completed by individuals who want to take a proactive approach to their mental health.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the General Information section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Choose the mental health issues that apply to you from the list in the Mental Health Status section.
3. Identify any risk factors that may affect your mental health, such as family history, social isolation, or lack of support.
4. Select a coping mechanism that works for you, such as mindfulness or exercise.
5. Enter at least one emergency contact person and their relationship to you.
6. Enter their contact information, including phone number and email.
7. Choose how often you need follow-up, and how you would like to be contacted.
8. Provide the contact information for your mental health provider.
9. List the crisis hotline and emergency services you need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Mental Health Status** (`mental_health_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the mental health issues that apply to you, such as depression, anxiety, or bipolar disorder.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, select_multiple, required): Identify any risk factors that may affect your mental health, such as family history, social isolation, or lack of support.
* **Coping Mechanisms** (`coping_mech`, select_one, required): Choose a coping mechanism that works for you, such as mindfulness or exercise.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter at least one contact person and their relationship to you.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, text, required): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter the contact person's relationship to you.
* **Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the contact person's email.
* **Mental Health Provider** (`mental_health_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of your mental health provider.
* **Follow Up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, number, required): Choose how often you need follow-up, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Follow Up Method** (`follow_up_method`, text, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as phone or email.
* **Follow Up Location** (`follow_up_location`, text, required): Choose where you would like to be contacted, such as home or hospital.
* **Crisis Hotline** (`crisis_hotline`, text, required): Enter the crisis hotline number.
* **Crisis Hotline Phone** (`crisis_hotline_phone`, text, required): Enter the crisis hotline phone number.
* **Crisis Hotline Email** (`crisis_hotline_email`, email, required): Enter the crisis hotline email.
* **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, select_multiple, required): List the emergency services you need, such as 24/7 crisis line or support groups.
* **Crisis Planning** (`crisis_planning`, date, required): Enter the date of your last crisis planning meeting or review.
* **Crisis Response Plan** (`crisis_response_plan`, text, required): Describe your crisis response plan.
* **Mental Health Support** (`mental_health_support`, text, required): Enter the name of your mental health support person.
* **Mental Health Support Person** (`mental_health_support_person`, text, required): Enter the name of your mental health support person.
* **Relationship to Mental Health Support** (`mental_health_support_relationship`, text, required): Enter the relationship of your mental health support person.
* **Mental Health Support Phone** (`mental_health_support_phone`, text, required): Enter the mental health support person's phone number.
* **Mental Health Support Email** (`mental_health_support_email`, email, required): Enter the mental health support person's email.
* **Mental Health Status Change** (`mental_health_status_change`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if your mental health status has improved, stayed the same, or worsened.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and update your mental health safety plan regularly.
* Keep a copy of your mental health safety plan and share it with your loved ones.
* Be proactive in identifying and addressing potential mental health crises.
* Don't hesitate to reach out for help if you need it.
* Review and update this form after any changes in your mental health status.

Please note that this form is not a substitute for professional mental health advice. If you are in a mental health crisis, please reach out to a professional mental health provider or crisis hotline for help.
