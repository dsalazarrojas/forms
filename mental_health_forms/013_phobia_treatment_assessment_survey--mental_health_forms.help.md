# Phobia Treatment Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phobia Treatment Assessment Survey is a tool designed to help mental health professionals evaluate the severity of phobias and create a personalized treatment plan for patients. This form is not a substitute for a professional evaluation or diagnosis, and it is recommended that it be completed by a licensed mental health professional only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the survey questions carefully and answer them based on the patient's condition.
* Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Use the options provided for each question to select the most accurate response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Phobia Treatment Assessment Survey (Page 1)

* **Phobia Treatment Assessment Survey** (`phobia_treatment_assessment_survey`): This is the title of the form, which should be left blank.
* **Anxiety Level** (`anxiety_level`): Please rate the patient's anxiety level on a scale of 0-100, where 0 represents no anxiety and 100 represents extreme anxiety.
* **Fear Trigger** (`fear_trigger`): Please select "Yes" if the patient experiences fear in response to a specific trigger, and "No" otherwise.
* **Avoidance Behavior** (`avoidance_behavior`): Please select all the ways in which the patient avoids the feared object or situation.
* **Phobias Screened** (`phobias_screened`): Please enter the date when the patient's phobias were first noticed.
* **Symptoms Dose** (`symptoms_dose`): Please rate the severity of the patient's symptoms on a scale of 0-100, where 0 represents no symptoms and 100 represents extreme symptoms.
* **Fear Level** (`fear_level`): Please rate the patient's fear level on a scale of 0-100, where 0 represents no fear and 100 represents extreme fear.
* **Fear Memory** (`fear_memory`): Please select "Yes" if the patient recalls the fear trigger, and "No" otherwise.
* **Avoidance Frequency** (`avoidance_frequency`): Please rate how often the patient avoids the feared object or situation.
* **Treatment Goals** (`treatment_goals`): Please select all the goals for the treatment plan.
* **Fear Trigger Location** (`fear_trigger_location`): Please describe the location where the patient experiences fear.
* **Fear Trigger People** (`fear_trigger_people`): Please describe the people associated with the patient's feared object or situation.
* **Fear Trigger Situations** (`fear_trigger_situations`): Please describe the situations that trigger the patient's fear.
* **Phobias Diagnosis** (`phobias_diagnosis`): Please select "Yes" if the patient has been diagnosed with phobias.
* **Anxiety Level Assessed** (`anxiety_level_assessed`): Please rate the patient's anxiety level on a scale of 0-100, as assessed by a mental health professional.
* **Fear Memory Length** (`fear_memory_length`): Please rate how long the patient recalls the fear trigger.
* **Symptoms Duration** (`symptoms_duration`): Please rate how long the patient's symptoms have been present.
* **Symptoms Frequency** (`symptoms_frequency`): Please rate how often the patient experiences symptoms.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`): Please describe the treatment plan for the patient.
* **Treatment Goals Status** (`treatment_goals_status`): Please select the status of the treatment goals.

### Tips

* Please answer all questions based on the patient's condition.
* If unsure about any question, please consult with a mental health professional.
* This form is intended for use by mental health professionals only, and it is not a substitute for a professional evaluation or diagnosis.
* Please save the completed form for future reference and updates to the treatment plan.
