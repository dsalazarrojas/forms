# GAD 7 Anxiety Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The GAD 7 Anxiety Assessment Form is a tool to help assess the level of anxiety in individuals. This form is designed to measure various aspects of anxiety, including generalized anxiety, fear of misfortune, worry about performance, worry about stability, and worry about mortality. The form is based on the Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD) scale, which is a well-established assessment tool used to evaluate the severity of anxiety symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions based on your personal experiences and feelings.
2. For each question, select the response that best describes your anxiety level or experience.
3. Make sure to answer all questions accurately and honestly.
4. If you are unsure about any question, try to answer based on your best judgment or consult with a healthcare professional.
5. Complete the form in a quiet and comfortable environment, without distractions.
6. Take your time to answer each question carefully, and do not rush through the form.
7. If you need help or have questions, consult with a healthcare professional.
