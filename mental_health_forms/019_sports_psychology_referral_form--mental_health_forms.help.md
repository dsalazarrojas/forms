# sports_psychology_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports psychology referral form is used to refer an athlete to the sports psychology services for mental health support and guidance. This form helps collect necessary information from the athlete's school or organization to facilitate the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the athlete's school name in the "School Name" field.
2. Enter the athlete's name in the "Athlete Name" field.
3. Select the sport the athlete participates in from the "Sport" field.
4. Provide a brief explanation for the referral in the "Referral Reason" field.
5. (Optional) Enter the coach's name and contact person's name in their respective fields if you are referring an athlete who has a coach or contact person.
6. Enter the email address for the referral in the "Email" field.
7. Enter the phone number for the referral in the "Phone" field.
8. Enter the date of the referral in the "Date" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
9. Enter the time of the referral in the "Time" field in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
10. (Optional) Provide any additional notes about the referral in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the school or organization the athlete is affiliated with.
* **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the athlete's name, e.g., John Doe.
* **Sport** (`sport`, text, **Required**): Select the sport the athlete participates in, e.g., football, basketball, etc.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, **Required**): Provide a brief explanation for the referral, e.g., "Concerns about anxiety symptoms" or "Difficulty with performance pressure."
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, **Optional**): Enter the name of the athlete's coach, if applicable.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, **Optional**): Enter the name of the contact person at the school or organization, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter the email address for the referral, e.g., [john.doe@example.com](mailto:john.doe@example.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Required**): Enter the phone number for the referral, e.g., 123-456-7890.
* **Date** (`date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date of the referral in the format of MM/DD/YYYY, e.g., 05/15/2024.
* **Time** (`time`, time, **Required**): Enter the time of the referral in the format of HH:MM AM/PM, e.g., 02:30 PM.
* **Note** (`note`, note, **Optional**): Provide any additional notes or comments about the referral, if applicable.
