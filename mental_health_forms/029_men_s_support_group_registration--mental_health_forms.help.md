# Men's Support Group Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for men to register and join a support group, providing a platform for them to connect with others who share similar experiences and emotions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and understand the purpose and the importance of the information you are providing.
2. Fill in your contact information (Participant Details) and indicate your availability (Availability).
3. Provide your contact information (Contact Info) and emergency contact information (Emergency Contacts).
4. Describe your relationship with your emergency contacts (Emergency Contact Relationship).
5. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details, text, required/optional): This field is where you provide your basic contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Availability** (Availability, text, required/optional): Please indicate your availability to participate in the group, including your preferred schedule and any constraints or limitations.
* **Consent** (Consent, text, required/optional): This is a field for you to provide your consent to participate in the group and for us to use your information for group purposes.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required/optional): Please provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Group Info** (Group Info, text, required/optional): Describe how you heard about the support group and what you expect from it.
* **Emergency Contacts** (Emergency Contacts, text, required/optional): List the names and contact information of your emergency contacts.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Emergency Contact Relationship, text, required/optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contacts, such as family members or friends.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when providing your information.
* Respect the confidentiality and anonymity of other group members.
* Use the support group as a safe space to share your experiences and emotions without fear of judgment or repercussions.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
