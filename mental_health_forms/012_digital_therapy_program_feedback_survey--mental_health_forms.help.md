# Digital Therapy Program Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from participants in the digital therapy program. Your feedback will help us improve the program for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. You can provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Satisfaction** (1/1): Please rate how satisfied you are with the overall participant experience in the digital therapy program.
* **Session Satisfaction** (1/1): Please rate your satisfaction with each session, from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 6 (very satisfied).
* **Rating 1** (1/1): Please rate how well the program met your expectations.
* **Rating 2** (1/1): Please rate how well the program addressed your goals and needs.
* **Rating 3** (1/1): Please rate how well the program improved your mental health.
* **Comments** (open-ended): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the program.
* **Email** (text): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (text): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Rating 4** (1/1): Please rate how well the program met your expectations.
* **Rating 5** (1/1): Please rate how well the program addressed your goals and needs.
* **Rating 6** (1/1): Please rate how well the program improved your mental health.
* **Session Satisfaction 2** (1/1): Please rate your satisfaction with this session.
* **Session Satisfaction 3** (1/1): Please rate your satisfaction with this session.
* **Session Satisfaction 4** (1/1): Please rate your satisfaction with this session.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
