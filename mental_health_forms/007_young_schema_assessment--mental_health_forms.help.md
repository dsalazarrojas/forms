# Young Schema Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Young Schema Assessment is a mental health assessment tool designed to help identify potential issues and areas for improvement in a person's mental well-being. It evaluates several aspects of mental health, including anxiety, depression, relationships, self-awareness, and emotional regulation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the best answer for each question, based on how you feel at the moment.
3. Please provide clear and specific answers, without being too brief or too lengthy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Anxiety Level** (`anxiety_level`, number, not required): This question asks you to rate your current level of anxiety, with higher numbers indicating a greater level of anxiety.
* **Depression Level** (`depression_level`, number, not required): This question asks you to rate your current level of depression, with higher numbers indicating a greater level of depression.
* **Anger Level** (`anger_level`, number, not required): This question asks you to rate your current level of anger, with higher numbers indicating a greater level of anger.
* **Self-Worth Level** (`self_worth_level`, number, not required): This question asks you to rate your current level of self-worth, with higher numbers indicating a greater sense of self-worth.
* **Relationships with Others** (`relationships_with_others`, text, not required): Describe your current relationships with others, including family, friends, and romantic partners.
* **Social Support** (`social_support`, text, not required): Think about the social support you receive from family, friends, or others, and describe how it affects your well-being.
* **Self-Reflection Skills** (`self_reflection_skills`, text, not required): Describe your ability to reflect on your thoughts, feelings, and behaviors, and how it helps you in managing your emotions and relationships.
* **Problem Focusing** (`problem_focusing`, text, not required): Describe how you focus on problems and challenges, and how it affects your mental well-being.
* **Goal Setting** (`goal_setting`, text, not required): Describe your ability to set and achieve goals, and how it affects your motivation and motivation levels.
* **Decision Making** (`decision_making`, text, not required): Describe how you make decisions, and how it affects your emotions and well-being.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication`, text, not required): Describe your ability to communicate effectively with others, and how it affects your relationships and emotions.
* **Problem Solving** (`problem_solving`, text, not required): Describe your ability to solve problems, and how it affects your mental well-being.
* **Emotional Regulation** (`emotional_regulation`, text, not required): Describe how you regulate your emotions, and how it affects your mental well-being.
* **Stress Management** (`stress_management`, text, not required): Describe how you manage stress, and how it affects your mental well-being.
* **Mindfulness** (`mindfulness`, text, not required): Describe your ability to be mindful and present in the moment, and how it affects your mental well-being.
* **Self-Compassion** (`self_compassion`, text, not required): Describe how you treat yourself with kindness and understanding, and how it affects your mental well-being.
* **Self-Acceptance** (`self_acceptance`, text, not required): Describe how you accept and accept yourself, and how it affects your mental well-being.
* **Self-Expression** (`self_expression`, text, not required): Describe how you express yourself, and how it affects your mental well-being.
* **Self-Alinement** (`self_alignment`, text, not required): Describe how you align your thoughts, feelings, and actions, and how it affects your mental well-being.
* **Self-Realisation** (`self_realisation`, text, not required): Describe how you realize your thoughts, feelings, and behaviors, and how it affects your mental well-being.
* **Self-Transcendence** (`self_transcendence`, text, not required): Describe how you transcend your limitations and challenges, and how it affects your mental well-being.
* **Emotional State** (`emotional_state`, select_one, not required): Choose how you are feeling right now, with options: Very Sad, Neutral, or Happy.
* **Emotional State 2** (`emotional_state_2`, select_one, not required): Choose how you are feeling right now, with options: Very Sad, Neutral, or Happy.
* **Emotional State 3** (`emotional_state_3`, select_one, not required): Choose how you are feeling right now, with options: Very Sad, Neutral, or Happy.
