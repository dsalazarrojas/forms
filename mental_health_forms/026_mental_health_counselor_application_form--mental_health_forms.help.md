# mental_health_counselor_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in applying to become mental health counselors. It is not intended for individuals seeking mental health services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in the **user_data** section.
2. Provide your contact information in the **contact_info** section.
3. Describe your work history in the **work_history** section.
4. Share your educational background in the **education** section.
5. Outline your relevant experience in the **experience** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_data**: A text field where you can enter any relevant personal details you'd like to share. This field is optional.
* **contact_info**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. This field is optional.
* **work_history**: Describe your previous work experience, including job titles, dates of employment, and a brief description of your roles and responsibilities. This field is optional.
* **education**: Share your educational background, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and relevant coursework or specializations. This field is optional.
* **experience**: Outline your relevant experience in mental health counseling, including training or certification, number of years of experience, and specific skills or areas of expertise. This field is optional.
