# Stress Attack Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Stress Attack Tracking Form is a personal record designed to help individuals track and manage their stress attacks. By accurately documenting the details of each attack, you can better understand your triggers, patterns, and responses, ultimately gaining control over your mental well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form for each stress attack as it occurs.
2. Enter the Date and Time of the attack, ensuring accuracy to the best of your ability.
3. Choose the Attack type from the options provided (Physical, Emotional, Mental, Financial, Social, or Other).
4. Describe the Description of the attack, including any relevant details.
5. Select the Severity level of the attack (Low, Medium, High, or Critical).
6. Enter the Duration of the attack in minutes.
7. Specify the Frequency of the attacks, if any.
8. Add any additional Notes about the attack, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Form Title** (`stress_attack_tracking_form`, text, required: false)
Enter a brief title for this form to help you identify it in your records.

### 2. **Date** (`date`, date, required: true)
Enter the date of the stress attack, ensuring accuracy.

### 3. **Time** (`time`, time, required: true)
Enter the time of the stress attack, ensuring accuracy.

### 4. **Attack Type** (`attack_type`, select_one, required: true)
Choose the type of attack from the provided options:
* Physical: Stress attacks related to physical sensations or experiences.
* Emotional: Stress attacks related to emotions or emotional responses.
* Mental: Stress attacks related to mental health or cognitive functions.
* Financial: Stress attacks related to financial concerns or pressures.
* Social: Stress attacks related to social interactions or relationships.
* Other: Stress attacks of any other type (please specify in the Description field).

### 5. **Description** (`description`, text, required: false)
Enter a detailed description of the stress attack, including any context or contributing factors.

### 6. **Severity** (`severity`, select_one, required: true)
Choose the level of severity for the attack from the provided options:
* Low: A mild stress attack with minimal impact.
* Medium: A moderate stress attack with some impact on daily life.
* High: A severe stress attack with significant impact on daily life.
* Critical: An extreme stress attack with extreme impact on daily life.
* None: No stress attack occurred.

### 7. **Duration** (`duration`, number, required: true)
Enter the duration of the stress attack in minutes.

### 8. **Frequency** (`frequency`, text, required: true)
Specify how frequently you experience stress attacks, e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, or as needed.

### 9. **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false)
Add any additional notes or context about the stress attack, if needed.

### Tips

* Be as accurate and thorough as possible when completing this form.
* Use this form as a personal record, and do not share it with others without your consent.
* Review and update your records regularly to track your progress and identify patterns.
* If you experience frequent or severe stress attacks, consider consulting a mental health professional for guidance and support.
