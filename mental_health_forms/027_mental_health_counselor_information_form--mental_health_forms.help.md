# Mental Health Counselor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and maintain accurate and up-to-date information about mental health counselors who wish to get listed on our platform. It is essential to ensure that the information provided is accurate, comprehensive, and meets the required standards for listing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **Client Id** field with your unique client identification number (if applicable).
2. Enter your **Client Name** in the provided text field.
3. Select your **Preferred Language** from the dropdown menu:
	* English
	* Spanish
	* French
	* Other (please specify in the notes section)
4. Choose your preferred **Contact Method**:
	* Phone
	* Email
	* Mail
5. Enter your **Mental Health Counselor Name** in the text field.
6. Enter your **Mental Health Counselor Title** in the text field.
7. Provide your **Years of Experience** in the numeric field.
8. Enter your **Education Background** in the text field.
9. Choose your **Work Experience**:
	* Clinical
	* Non-Clinical
10. Select your **Mental Health Focus Areas**:
	* Anxiety
	* Depression
	* Trauma
11. Enter your **Availability** in the time field (format: 08:00-17:00).
12. Choose your **Availability Days**:
	* Monday
	* Tuesday
	* Wednesday
	* Thursday
	* Friday
13. Enter any additional **Note** regarding your practice or qualifications.
14. Enter your **Email** for communication purposes.
15. Enter your **Phone Number** for communication purposes.
16. Enter your **Mental Health Counselor E-Mail** for professional purposes.
17. Enter your **Mental Health Counselor Phone** for professional purposes.
18. Enter your **Physical Address** for in-person meetings or consultations.
19. Enter your **Website** for online presence.
20. Select your **Specialty**:
	* Anxiety
	* Depression
	* Trauma
	* Other (please specify in the notes section)
21. Enter your **Mental Health Counselor License** number (if applicable).
22. Enter your **Mental Health Counselor License State** (if applicable).
23. Enter your **Certification** (if applicable).
24. Choose your **Insurance**:
	* Blue Cross
	* Aetna
	* Cigna
	* Other (please specify in the notes section)
25. Choose your **Payment Method**:
	* Cash
	* Insurance
	* Free
26. Enter your **Mental Health Counselor Education** (if applicable).
27. Enter your **Mental Health Counselor Supervising Experience** (if applicable).
28. Enter your **Mental Health Counselor Supervising License** (if applicable).
29. Enter your **Mental Health Counselor Supervising Certification** (if applicable).
30. Enter your **Mental Health Counselor Supervising Insurance** (if applicable).
31. Enter your **Mental Health Counselor Supervising Education** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Id**: Unique client identification number (if applicable).
* **Client Name**: The name of the mental health counselor.
* **Preferred Language**: The preferred language for communication with the client.
* **Contact Method**: The preferred method of contact (phone, email, or mail).
* **Mental Health Counselor Name**: The name of the mental health counselor.
* **Mental Health Counselor Title**: The title or position of the mental health counselor.
* **Years of Experience**: The number of years the mental health counselor has been practicing.
* **Education Background**: The educational background of the mental health counselor.
* **Work Experience**: The type of experience the mental health counselor has (clinical or non-clinical).
* **Mental Health Focus Areas**: The areas of focus for the mental health counselor.
* **Availability**: The counselor's availability in a 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00-17:00).
* **Availability Days**: The days of the week the mental health counselor is available.
* **Note**: Any additional comments or qualifications the mental health counselor wishes to mention.
* **Email**: The email address of the mental health counselor for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: The phone number of the mental health counselor for communication purposes.
* **Mental Health Counselor E-Mail**: The email address of the mental health counselor for professional purposes.
* **Mental Health Counselor Phone**: The phone number of the mental health counselor for professional purposes.
* **Physical Address**: The physical address of the mental health counselor for in-person meetings or consultations.
* **Website**: The website of the mental health counselor for online presence.
* **Specialty**: The specialty area of focus for the mental health counselor.
* **Mental Health Counselor License**: The license number of the mental health counselor (if applicable).
* **Mental Health Counselor License State**: The state in which the mental health counselor is licensed (if applicable).
* **Certification**: The certification of the mental health counselor (if applicable).
* **Insurance**: The insurance provider or coverage of the mental health counselor.
* **Payment Method**: The payment method of the mental health counselor.
* **Mental Health Counselor Education**: The educational background of the mental health counselor.
* **Mental Health Counselor Supervising Experience**: The supervising experience of the mental health counselor.
* **Mental Health Counselor Supervising License**: The supervising license of the mental health counselor.
* **Mental Health Counselor Supervising Certification**: The supervising certification of the mental health counselor.
* **Mental Health Counselor Supervising Insurance**: The supervising insurance of the mental health counselor.
* **Mental Health Counselor Supervising Education**: The supervising education of the mental health counselor.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and comprehensively. Double-check for spelling and grammar errors before submitting. If you are unsure about any field, please review the provided explanations and ask for clarification before proceeding.
