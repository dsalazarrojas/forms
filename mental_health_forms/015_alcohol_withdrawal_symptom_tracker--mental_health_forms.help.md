# Alcohol Withdrawal Symptom Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help individuals track and manage their symptoms during alcohol withdrawal. It is intended for use by individuals experiencing or having experienced alcohol withdrawal.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your **Participant ID**. This is a unique identifier for your records.
2.  Enter your **Participant Name**. This is your full name.
3.  Enter the **Check-in Date** and **Check-in Time**. These fields help us understand when you last checked in for support.
4.  Enter the number of **Days Since Last Drink**. This helps us track your progress over time.
5.  Enter the number of **Hours Since Last Drink** (if less than a day). This helps us understand how long you've been sober.
6.  Rate your **Overall Withdrawal Severity** from None to Very Severe.
7.  Rate your **Anxiety Level** from None to Very Severe.
8.  Rate your **Tremor/Shakiness Severity** from None to Very Severe.
9.  Rate your **Sweating Level** from None to Very Severe.
10.  Rate your **Nausea Level** from None to Very Severe.
11.  Rate your **Headache Severity** from None to Very Severe.
12.  Rate your **Insomnia Severity** from None to Very Severe.
13.  Rate your **Irritability Level** from None to Very Severe.
14.  Rate your **Depression Level** from None to Very Severe.
15.  Rate your **Confusion Level** from None to Very Severe.
16.  Check if you're experiencing **Heart Palpitations** (rapid heartbeat), **Hallucinations**, **Seizure Activity**, or **Other** additional symptoms.
17.  Rate your **Appetite Level** from Normal to Nauseated at thought of food.
18.  Rate your **Hydration Status** from Well Hydrated to Severely Dehydrated.
19.  List any **Medications Taken** today.
20.  Select whether you're taking **Medication as Prescribed** or not.
21.  Rate your **Alcohol Cravings Intensity** from None to Overwhelming.
22.  Select all **Coping Strategies Used**, such as Deep Breathing, Meditation, Exercise, etc.
23.  Check if you've **Contacted Support** today.
24.  Rate your **Hours of Sleep Last Night**.
25.  Rate your **Sleep Quality** from Excellent to Very Poor.
26.  Rate your **Overall Mood Rating** from Very Good to Very Bad.
27.  Self-Assess your **Relapse Risk** from No risk to Very High Risk.
28.  Check if you need an **Emergency Contact**.
29.  Enter any **Additional Symptoms** not listed.
30.  Enter **Notes for Counselor**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant ID** (1): Enter your unique identifier.
*   **Participant Name** (2): Enter your full name.
*   **Check-in Date** (3): Enter the date of this check-in.
*   **Check-in Time** (4): Enter the time of this check-in.
*   **Days Since Last Drink** (5): Enter the number of days since your last drink.
*   **Hours Since Last Drink** (6): Enter the number of hours since your last drink (if less than a day).
*   **Overall Withdrawal Severity** (7): Rate your overall symptoms from None to Very Severe.
*   **Anxiety Level** (8): Rate your anxiety from None to Very Severe.
*   **Tremor/Shakiness Severity** (9): Rate your tremors from None to Very Severe.
*   **Sweating Level** (10): Rate your excessive sweating from None to Very Severe.
*   **Nausea Level** (11): Rate your nausea from None to Very Severe.
*   **Headache Severity** (12): Rate your headache pain from None to Very Severe.
*   **Insomnia Severity** (13): Rate your sleep difficulties from None to Very Severe.
*   **Irritability Level** (14): Rate your irritability from None to Very Severe.
*   **Depression Level** (15): Rate your feelings of depression from None to Very Severe.
*   **Confusion Level** (16): Rate your mental confusion from None to Very Severe.
*   **Heart Palpitations** (17): Check if you're experiencing a rapid heartbeat.
*   **Hallucinations** (18): Check if you're experiencing hallucinations.
*   **Seizure Activity** (19): Check if you're experiencing seizure activity.
*   **Appetite Level** (20): Rate your appetite from Normal to Nauseated at thought of food.
*   **Hydration Status** (21): Rate your hydration from Well Hydrated to Severely Dehydrated.
*   **Medications Taken** (22): List any medications you've taken today.
*   **Medication Compliance** (23): Select whether you're taking your medications as prescribed.
*   **Alcohol Cravings Intensity** (24): Rate your cravings from None to Overwhelming.
*   **Coping Strategies Used** (25): Select all coping strategies you've used, such as Deep Breathing, Meditation, etc.
*   **Contacted Support** (26): Check if you've contacted support today.
*   **Hours of Sleep Last Night** (27): Enter the total hours you slept last night.
*   **Sleep Quality** (28): Rate your sleep quality from Excellent to Very Poor.
*   **Overall Mood Rating** (29): Rate your mood from Very Good to Very Bad.
*   **Relapse Risk Self-Assessment** (30): Self-Assess your risk of relapse from No risk to Very High Risk.
*   **Need Emergency Contact** (31): Check if you need emergency contact.
*   **Additional Symptoms** (32): Enter any additional symptoms not listed.
*   **Notes for Counselor** (33): Enter any notes for your counselor.

## Tips

*   This form is designed to be used during alcohol withdrawal. Use it regularly to track your symptoms and progress.
*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form. This will help your healthcare professionals provide better support.
*   Rate your symptoms as they truly are, not how you feel they should be.
*   If you're unsure about any symptom or question, please consult with your healthcare professional.
*   This form is intended for tracking your progress and symptoms during alcohol withdrawal. If you have concerns or questions, please reach out to your healthcare professional for guidance.
