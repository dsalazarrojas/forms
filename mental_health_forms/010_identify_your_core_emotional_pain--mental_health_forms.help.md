# Identify Your Core Emotional Pain - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you understand your current emotional state and identify areas where you may need to focus your self-care efforts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please answer each question based on your current emotional state. You can refer to the explanations for each field below for more information on what each question is asking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's been causing you distress** (`core_pain`, `text`, required: false): This question is asking you to describe the underlying cause of your current emotional pain or distress. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Areas of distress** (`distress_areas`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to identify the areas where you feel pain or distress. Please select all that apply.
* **How would you rate your current level of distress** (`distress_level`, `number`, required: false): This question is asking you to rate your current level of distress on a scale, with higher numbers indicating greater distress.
* **How do you feel right now?** (`emotional_expression`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is asking you to describe your current emotional state.
* **Emotional intensity** (`emotional_intensity`, `number`, required: false): This question is asking you to rate how intense your current emotions feel.
* **Describe your core pain** (`core_pain_description`, `text`, required: false): This question is asking you to describe the pain or distress you're currently experiencing.
* **Where do you feel this pain?** (`pain_location`, `text`, required: false): This question is asking you to describe where you feel the pain or distress.
* **Relationships** (`relationships`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to identify relationships that may be contributing to your distress.
* **Self-care activities** (`self_care`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to identify self-care activities that you currently engage in.
* **Support system** (`support_system`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to identify people or resources that support you.
* **Mental health resources** (`mental_health`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to identify resources you've used for mental health.
* **Wellness practices** (`wellness_practices`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to identify wellness practices you currently engage in.
* **Self-reflection** (`self_reflection`, `number`, required: false): This question is asking you to rate how often you make time for self-reflection.
* **Self-forgiveness** (`self_forgiveness`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is asking you to describe how often you practice self-forgiveness.
* **Self-care planning** (`self_care_planning`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to identify how often you plan for self-care.
* **Self-care goals** (`self_care_goals`, `text`, required: false): This question is asking you to describe your self-care goals.
* **What motivates you to practice self-care?** (`self_care_motivation`, `text`, required: false): This question is asking you to describe what motivates you to practice self-care.
* **Self-care barriers** (`self_care_barrier`, `text`, required: false): This question is asking you to describe any barriers you face in practicing self-care.
* **Self-care support** (`self_care_support`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to identify people or resources that support you in practicing self-care.
* **Self-care practices** (`self_care_practices`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to identify self-care activities you currently engage in.
* **Relationship status** (`relationships_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to describe your current relationship status.
* **Mental health status** (`mental_health_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to describe your current mental health status.
* **Wellness status** (`wellness_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to describe your current wellness status.
* **How would you rate your core pain?** (`core_pain_score`, `number`, required: false): This question is asking you to rate your core pain on a scale.
* **What do you hope to achieve through this assessment?** (`mental_health_goal`, `text`, required: false): This question is asking you to describe your goal for completing this assessment.
