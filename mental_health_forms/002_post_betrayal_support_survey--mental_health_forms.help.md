# Post Betrayal Support Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Betrayal Support Survey is a tool designed to help individuals who have experienced betrayal to access support and resources tailored to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please answer each question honestly, and remember that your responses will be kept confidential.
2. For open-ended questions (e.g., text fields), feel free to write as much or as little as you need to express your thoughts and feelings.
3. If you're unsure about any question, you can always ask for clarification or skip it if you don't feel comfortable answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance.
* **How have you been feeling lately?** (`feeling_scale`, text, required): Please describe your current emotional state, including any feelings of sadness, anger, fear, or numbness.
* **What support needs have you experienced?** (`well_being_support_needs`, text, required): Describe the types of support you feel you need, such as emotional, practical, or financial support.
* **Recovery patterns** (`recovery_patterns`, text, required): Share any patterns or strategies you've used or would like to use to support your recovery, such as mindfulness, self-care, or seeking help from others.
* **What would make you feel supported?** (`counselor_or_coaching_support_needs`, text, required): Describe what would make you feel supported in a counseling or coaching relationship, such as having a safe space to share your emotions or receiving practical advice.
* **Peer Support** (`peer_support_needs`, text, required): Share what would make you feel supported in a peer relationship, such as having a safe and non-judgmental space to share your feelings or receiving emotional support.
* **What are your recovery goals?** (`recovery_goals`, text, required): Set specific, achievable goals for your recovery, such as seeking closure, rebuilding trust, or developing emotional regulation.
* **What would make you feel supported in a counseling or coaching relationship?** (`counselor_or_coaching_support_needs_description`, text, required): Describe what would make you feel supported in a counseling or coaching relationship, such as having a non-judgmental space or receiving guidance on coping mechanisms.
* **What would make you feel supported in a peer relationship?** (`peer_support_needs_description`, text, required): Describe what would make you feel supported in a peer relationship, such as having a safe and understanding space or receiving emotional validation.

## Tips
* Remember to be as honest and open as possible in your responses.
* You can always ask for clarification or skip any question that makes you uncomfortable.
* This form is for your personal use, so feel free to modify or add to it as needed.
* Your responses will be kept confidential, and your support needs will be used to inform the development of tailored support strategies.
