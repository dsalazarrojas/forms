# Psychology Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychology Checklist is a form designed to help individuals assess their mental health status, identify potential concerns, and guide them on the next steps to take.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing each page carefully and answering the questions accordingly.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible to help the form's algorithms understand your situation.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this will ensure accurate results.
