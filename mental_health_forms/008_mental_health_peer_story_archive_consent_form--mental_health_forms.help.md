# Mental Health Peer Story Archive Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Peer Story Archive Consent Form is a collection of fields that will help you share your personal story related to mental health, which will be archived for future reference. Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date, as it will be used to help others understand your experiences and potentially support others in similar situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Share your story in the "Story" field. This is a text area where you can type as much or as little as you'd like.
5. Check the box to confirm your Consent.
6. If you're affiliated with an organization, enter the name of your organization in the "Organization" field.
7. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
8. Enter the name of your City in the "City" field.
9. Enter your Country of residence in the "Country" field.
10. Enter your Postal Code in the "Postal Code" field.
11. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name in this field.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your email address in this field.
* **Story** (`story`, `text`, required: false): Please share your personal story related to mental health in this text area. You can type as much or as little as you'd like.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required: false): Check this box to confirm that you have read and understand the terms of this form.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): If you're affiliated with an organization, please enter the name of your organization in this field.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your physical address in this field.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of your city in this field.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of your country of residence in this field.
* **Postal Code** (`postal`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your postal code in this field.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number in this field.
