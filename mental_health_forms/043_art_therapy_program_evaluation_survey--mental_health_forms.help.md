# Art Therapy Program Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of the Art Therapy Program. It is intended to gather information from participants about their experiences, goals, and improvements in mental health, emotional regulation, self-expression, and social skills after participating in the program.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To get started, please select the date you participated in the survey.
2. Fill in your name (optional) and identification number (if you have one).
3. Select the name of the art therapy program you participated in.
4. Choose how long you were in the program and how often you had sessions.
5. Select your age group.
6. Choose how you were referred to the program.
7. Select your primary concerns (e.g., anxiety, depression, etc.).
8. Describe your goals for participating in the program.
9. Rate how much your goals were met.
10. Rate your mental health improvement, emotional regulation, self-expression, and social skills improvement.
11. Choose how effective you think art therapy was for you.
12. Rate your therapist's competence and empathy.
13. Rate the program structure, materials, and environment safety.
14. If you would participate again or recommend the program, select your preference.
15. If you want to, provide additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, Date, Required): The date you participated in the survey, which is also the date you started the art therapy program.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, Text, Optional): Your name, which is optional but helps us know who you are. If you don't want to provide your name, it's okay!
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, Text, Optional): Your identification number, which is also optional but helps us know you better.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, Text, Required): The name of the art therapy program you participated in.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, Select One, Required): The length of time you were in the program. Select from Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, or More than 1 year.
* **Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`, Select One, Required): How often you had sessions. Select from Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, or As Needed.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, Select One, Optional): Your age group. Select from Under 18, 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, or 65 or Older.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, Select One, Required): How you were referred to the program. Select from Self-Referral, Healthcare Provider, School, Family or Friend, Community Organization, or Other.
* **Presenting Concerns** (`presenting_concerns`, Select Multiple, Required): Your primary concerns. Select all that apply from Anxiety, Depression, Trauma or PTSD, Grief or Loss, Stress Management, Relationship Issues, Self-Esteem, Life Transitions, or Other.
* **Program Goals** (`program_goals`, Text, Required): Describe your goals for participating in the program.
* **Goals Met** (`goals_met`, Select One, Required): Rate how much your goals were met. Select from Completely Met, Mostly Met, Partially Met, Minimally Met, or Not Met.
* **Mental Health Improvement** (`mental_health_improvement`, Select One, Required): Rate your mental health improvement. Select from Significant Improvement, Moderate Improvement, Some Improvement, Little Improvement, No Change, or Worsened.
* **Emotional Regulation** (`emotional_regulation`, Select One, Required): Rate your emotional regulation improvement. Select from Significant Improvement, Moderate Improvement, Some Improvement, Little Improvement, No Change, or Worsened.
* **Self-Expression** (`self_expression`, Select One, Required): Rate your self-expression improvement. Select from Significant Improvement, Moderate Improvement, Some Improvement, Little Improvement, No Change, or Worsened.
* **Social Skills** (`social_skills`, Select One, Required): Rate your social skills improvement. Select from Significant Improvement, Moderate Improvement, Some Improvement, Little Improvement, No Change, or Worsened.
* **Coping Strategies** (`coping_strategies`, Select One, Required): Rate your coping strategies improvement. Select from Significant Improvement, Moderate Improvement, Some Improvement, Little Improvement, No Change, or Worsened.
* **Art Therapy Effectiveness** (`art_therapy_effectiveness`, Select One, Required): How effective you think art therapy was for you. Select from Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, or Very Ineffective.
* **Therapist Competence** (`therapist_competence`, Select One, Required): Rate your therapist's competence. Select from Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Therapist Empathy** (`therapist_empathy`, Select One, Required): Rate your therapist's empathy. Select from Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Program Structure** (`program_structure`, Select One, Required): Rate the program structure. Select from Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Materials Provided** (`materials_provided`, Select One, Required): Rate the materials provided in the program. Select from Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Environment Safety** (`environment_safety`, Select One, Required): Rate the safety of the environment. Select from Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Most Helpful Component** (`most_helpful_component`, Text, Optional): Describe what was most helpful in the program. If you don't have any, just write "N/A".
* **Least Helpful Component** (`least_helpful_component`, Text, Optional): Describe what was least helpful in the program. If you don't have any, just write "N/A".
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, Text, Optional): If you have any suggestions, write them down here.
* **Would Participate Again** (`would_participate_again`, Select One, Required): Would you participate again? Select from Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Unsur, Probably No, or Definitely No.
* **Would Recommend to Others** (`would_recommend_to_others`, Select One, Required): Would you recommend this program to others? Select from Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Unsur, Probably No, or Definitely No.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): If you have any other comments or thoughts about the program, write them down here.
## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
* If a question does not apply to you, select "N/A" and move on.
* It is not necessary to complete every field, but the more information you provide, the more we can learn from your experience.
