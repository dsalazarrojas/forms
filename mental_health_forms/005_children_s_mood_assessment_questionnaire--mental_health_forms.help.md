# Children's Mood Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Children's Mood Assessment Questionnaire is designed to collect information about a child's mood, behavior, and emotional well-being from their caregiver or professional. This assessment helps in understanding the child's current state and identifying potential areas of concern that may require attention or support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter basic details about the child, including their name, date of birth, and current age.
2. Provide information about the caregiver's relationship to the child and their contact details (name, email, and phone number).
3. Indicate the period for which this assessment is being conducted (e.g., today, past week, or month).
4. Describe the child's overall mood during this period and any significant mood changes.
5. Identify emotional indicators and behavioral observations from the caregiver or staff.
6. Select any additional services that might be helpful for the child.
7. Enter any additional comments or concerns from the caregiver or professional.
8. Finally, confirm whether follow-up is recommended and at what frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child's Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter the child's first and last name.
* **Child's Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Child's Age** (`child_age`, number, required): Enter the child’s current age.
* **Child's Gender** (`child_gender`, select_one, optional): Select the child’s gender (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, Other).
* **Current Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, optional): Select the child’s current grade level (Pre-K, Kindergarten, 1st Grade, 2nd Grade, 3rd Grade, 4th Grade, 5th Grade, 6th Grade, 7th Grade, 8th Grade, 9th Grade, 10th Grade, 11th Grade, 12th Grade).
* **Caregiver Information** (`caregiver_information`, note, optional): Enter brief details about the caregiver or parent.
* **Caregiver Name** (`caregiver_name`, text, required): Enter the caregiver's name.
* **Relationship to Child** (`caregiver_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the caregiver’s relationship to the child (Mother, Father, Grandparent, Foster parent, Legal guardian, Other relative, Other).
* **Caregiver Email** (`caregiver_email`, email, required): Enter the caregiver’s email address.
* **Caregiver Phone Number** (`caregiver_phone`, text, required): Enter the caregiver’s phone number.
* **Assessment Period** (`assessment_period`, note, optional): Briefly describe the time frame for this assessment.
* **Date of Assessment** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date this assessment is being conducted.
* **What time period does this assessment cover** (`assessment_period_range`, select_one, required): Select the period this assessment covers (Today only, Past 3 days, Past week, Past 2 weeks, Past month).
* **Mood Observations** (`mood_observations`, note, optional): Enter any observations about the child’s mood.
* **Overall Mood** (`overall_mood`, select_one, required): Select the best description of the child’s overall mood (Very happy and cheerful, Generally happy, Neutral or mixed, Generally sad or down, Very sad or depressed).
* **Have you noticed any significant mood changes** (`mood_changes`, select_one, required): Select if you’ve noticed any significant mood changes (Yes, significant improvement, Yes, slight improvement, No noticeable change, Yes, slight decline, Yes, significant decline).
* **Emotional Indicators** (`emotional_indicators`, select_multiple, required): Select all the indicators that have been observed (Smiles frequently, Appears anxious or worried, Shows interest in activities, Withdrown or isolated, Irritable or easily frustrated, Calm and relaxed, Tearful or crying often, Energetic and enthusiastic, Fatigued or low energy, Aggressive or defiant).
* **Behavioral Observations** (`behavioral_observations`, note, optional): Enter any behavioral observations about the child.
* **Sleep Patterns** (`sleep_patterns`, select_one, required): Select the best description of the child’s sleep patterns (Sleeping well, no issues, Minor sleep difficulties, Moderate sleep problems, Significant sleep disturbances, Not sure).
* **Appetite and Eating** (`appetite_changes`, select_one, required): Select the best description of the child’s appetite and eating (Normal appetite, Slightly increased, Slightly decreased, Significantly increased, Significantly decreased, Not sure).
* **Social Interactions with Others** (`social_interactions`, select_one, required): Select the best description of the child’s social interactions (Very social and engaged, Moderately social, Normal for this child, Less social than usual, Very withdrawn).
* **Academic Performance or Concentration** (`academic_performance`, select_one, optional): Select the best description of the child’s academic performance or concentration (Performing well, Slight decline, Noticeable decline, Significant decline, Not applicable, Not sure).
* **Specific Concerns or Behaviors Noticed** (`specific_concerns`, text, optional): Enter any specific concerns or behaviors that have been noticed.
* **Identified Triggers for Mood Changes** (`triggers_identified`, text, optional): Enter any identified triggers for the child’s mood changes.
* **Coping Strategies** (`coping_strategies`, text, optional): Enter any coping strategies that seem to help the child.
* **Support and Intervention Needed** (`support_needed`, note, optional): Enter any support or intervention that might be necessary for the child.
* **Currently Receiving Mental Health Support** (`currently_receiving_support`, select_one, required): Select if the child is currently receiving mental health support (Yes, regular therapy, Yes, occasional counseling, Yes, medication management, No, but being considered, No support at this time).
* **Additional Services Needed** (`additional_services_needed`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the additional services that might be helpful for the child (Individual counseling, Family therapy, Group therapy, Psychiatric evaluation, School-based support, Peer support groups, Parenting support, None at this time).
* **Additional Comments from Caregiver** (`caregiver_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns from the caregiver.
* **Comments from Staff or Professionals** (`staff_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or concerns from staff or professionals.
* **Follow-up Recommended** (`follow_up_needed`, select_one, required): Select if follow-up is recommended (Yes, urgent, Yes, within 1-2 weeks, Yes, within 1 month, Routine follow-up, No follow-up needed).

Please answer the questions honestly and comprehensively to provide a complete picture of the child’s situation. If you need help or clarification on any question, please do not hesitate to ask.
