# Ayahuasca Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about participants' experiences with Ayahuasca, a plant medicine commonly used in shamanic rituals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your answers to the questions asked. You can type in your responses in the text fields, select from the provided options for select fields, and provide dates in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` for the `Date of Ayahuasca Experience` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (1)**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Age (2)**: Enter your current age in years.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for follow-up communication.
* **Date of Ayahuasca Experience (4)**: Enter the date when you participated in the Ayahuasca ceremony in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Location of Experience (5)**: Enter the location where the Ayahuasca ceremony took place. If you do not remember or are unsure, leave this field blank.
* **Had you participated in ayahuasca ceremonies before? (6)**: Select the option that best describes your previous experience.
	+ No, this was my first time
	+ Yes, 1-2 times
	+ Yes, 3-5 times
	+ Yes, more than 5 times
* **What was your primary intention or reason for participating? (7)**: Describe the reason or goal you had in mind when you participated in the Ayahuasca ceremony.
* **Describe physical sensations you experienced (8)**: Provide a detailed account of any physical sensations you felt during the ceremony, such as nausea, visions, or body sensations.
* **Describe your emotional experience (9)**: Share your emotional experience during the ceremony, including any feelings or emotions you felt.
* **What insights or realizations did you gain? (10)**: Describe any personal breakthroughs, understandings, or realizations you gained from the experience.
* **What was the most challenging part of the experience? (11)**: Share the most difficult aspect of the experience, if any.
* **What was the most positive aspect? (12)**: Share the most enjoyable or beneficial part of the experience, if any.
* **Have there been changes in your life since the experience? (13)**: Describe any shifts in your perspective, behavior, or well-being since the experience.
* **Current mental health status (14)**: Select the option that best describes your current mental health status.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Struggling
* **Any additional observations or notes (15)**: Share any additional thoughts, comments, or insights you have about your experience.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly, but feel free to skip any question that makes you uncomfortable.
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers to provide valuable insights.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for support.
