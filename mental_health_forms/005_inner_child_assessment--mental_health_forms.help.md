# Inner Child Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inner Child Assessment form is designed to gather information about your inner child, including your emotional status, mental health services, and other relevant details. This form is intended to be used in a mental health setting, possibly for assessment and treatment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read each section carefully and understand the questions being asked.
*   Provide accurate and honest answers to each question.
*   Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your mental health services, medical history, and other relevant details.
*   If you're unsure about any question, ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This is where you'll enter your first name. Please type it in as it should appear on official documents.
*   **Age** (`age`, number, optional): This is where you'll enter your age. Please enter your age in the format "X years old" or simply "X".
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This is where you'll enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
*   **Emotional Status** (`emotional_status`, select_one, optional): This question asks about your current emotional status. Please select one of the options provided, such as "I am feeling okay", "I am feeling sad", etc.
*   **Childhood Memory** (`childhood_memory`, text, optional): This is where you'll share a memory from your childhood. Please be as detailed as possible.
*   **Memory Details** (`memory_details`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select the types of memories you have from your childhood. Please choose all that apply from the options provided, such as "Play", "School", etc.
*   **Emotional State** (`emotional_state`, select_one, required): This question asks about your current emotional state. Please select one of the options provided, such as "I am feeling calm", "I am feeling anxious", etc.
*   **Mental Health Provider** (`mental_health_provider`, select_one, required): This question asks if you have a mental health provider. Please select "Yes" or "No".
*   **Mental Health Services** (`mental_health_services`, select_one, required): This question asks about the types of mental health services you've used in the past. Please select "Yes" or "No".
*   **Other Mental Health Services** (`other_mental_health_services`, text, optional): If you've used other mental health services not listed, please specify them here.
*   **Mental Health Caregiver** (`mental_health_caregiver`, text, optional): If you have a mental health caregiver, please provide their name and contact information.
*   **Mental Health Caregiver Phone** (`mental_health_caregiver_phone`, text, optional): If you have a mental health caregiver, please provide their phone number.
*   **Mental Health Caregiver Email** (`mental_health_caregiver_email`, email, optional): If you have a mental health caregiver, please provide their email address.
*   **Mental Health Caregiver Relationship** (`mental_health_caregiver_relationship`, text, optional): If you have a mental health caregiver, please describe your relationship with them.
*   **Mental Health Caregiver Years** (`mental_health_caregiver_years`, number, optional): If you have a mental health caregiver, please enter the number of years you've been seeing them.
*   **Mental Health Caregiver Notes** (`mental_health_caregiver_notes`, note, optional): If you have additional notes about your mental health caregiver, please provide them here.
*   **Family History** (`family_history`, text, optional): This is where you'll share any relevant family history that may impact your mental health.
*   **Family History Details** (`family_history_details`, text, optional): If you have additional information about your family history, please provide it here.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This is where you'll share any relevant medical history that may impact your mental health.
*   **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, text, optional): If you have additional information about your medical history, please provide it here.
*   **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): This is where you'll list any current medications you're taking.
*   **Current Medications Details** (`current_medications_details`, text, optional): If you have additional information about your current medications, please provide it here.
*   **Current Medical Conditions** (`current_medical_conditions`, text, optional): This is where you'll list any current medical conditions you have.
*   **Current Medical Conditions Details** (`current_medical_conditions_details`, text, optional): If you have additional information about your current medical conditions, please provide it here.
*   **Other** (`other`, text, optional): If you have any other relevant information that doesn't fit into the above categories, please provide it here.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
*   If you're unsure about any question, ask for help or clarification.
*   Provide as much detail as possible when answering each question.
*   Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
