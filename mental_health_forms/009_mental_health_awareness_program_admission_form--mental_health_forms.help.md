# mental-health-awareness-program-admission-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health awareness program admission form is designed to collect essential information from participants to determine their eligibility for the program. This information includes program preferences, participant details, and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing program information, which helps us understand your interests and preferences for the program.
2. Proceed to fill in your participant information, which includes demographic details such as your contact phone number and email (if applicable).
3. Provide your consent to participate in the program.
4. Select your interest in mental health programs.
5. Add any additional comments or information that you would like to share with us.
6. If you have a contact phone number, please provide it.
7. Enter your participant date of birth.
8. If applicable, enter your participant time of day.
9. Finally, add any additional information that you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Information** (`Program Information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your interest in the mental health awareness program. This field helps us understand your preferences for the program.
* **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, `text`, required: true): Provide your demographic details, such as your contact phone number and email (if applicable).
* **Consent** (`Consent`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select 'Yes' to confirm your consent to participate in the program.
* **Mental Health Programs** (`Mental Health Programs`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the mental health programs that interest you.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or information that you would like to share with us.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`Contact Phone Number`, `text`, required: false): If you have a contact phone number, please provide it for us to reach you.
* **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`, `email`, required: false): If you have an email, please provide it so we can contact you (if applicable).
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`Participant Date of Birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your participant date of birth for our records.
* **Participant Time of Day** (`Participant Time of Day`, `time`, required: false): If applicable, enter your participant time of day.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information that you would like to provide.
