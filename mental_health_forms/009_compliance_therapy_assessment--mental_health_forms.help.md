# Compliance Therapy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Compliance Therapy Assessment" form is a crucial tool for therapists to gather essential information about clients' mental health concerns, progress, and safety risks. This guide will walk you through each field to help you complete the form accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's **Client name**.
2. Select the **Date of session** from the calendar.
3. Describe the **Presenting concerns** that brought the client to therapy.
4. Indicate the client's **Engagement level** (High, Moderate, or Low).
5. Note any **Barriers to progress** that may hinder the client's treatment.
6. Identify any **Safety concerns** (None, Low, Moderate, or High).
7. Provide **Recommended actions** for the client's treatment plan.
8. Confirm if a **Follow up appointment** is required (True or False).
9. Enter your **Clinician name**, which will be used to track the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name**: Enter the client's full name. This is a required field.
* **Date of session**: Select the date of the session from the calendar. This is a required field.
* **Presenting concerns**: Describe the main issues that led the client to seek therapy. This is a required field.
* **Engagement level**: Choose the level of engagement the client demonstrated during the session. Options: High, Moderate, or Low.
* **Barriers to progress**: Note any obstacles or challenges that may hinder the client's progress. This is an optional field.
* **Safety concerns**: Identify any immediate safety risks noted during the session. Options: None, Low, Moderate, or High.
* **Recommended actions**: Provide suggestions for the client's treatment plan. This is a required field.
* **Follow up needed**: Indicate if a follow-up appointment is necessary. Options: True or False.
* **Clinician name**: Enter your name, which will be used to track the assessment. This is a required field.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields.
- Be specific and concise in your answers.
- If unsure, use your best judgment and provide a clear explanation.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- Ensure the form is submitted with accurate and up-to-date information.
