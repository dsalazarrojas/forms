# Counseling Method Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Counseling Method Preferences Survey" form is designed to collect data on your preferences for counseling sessions. This information will help counselors provide you with more tailored services to meet your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the counseling methods you prefer from the list.
2. Choose your preferred communication style for sessions.
3. Select how often you would like to have sessions.
4. Choose your preferred language for sessions.
5. Select your preferred time of day for sessions.
6. Choose how you want to be contacted for sessions.
7. Provide any additional comments you'd like to share.
8. If you want to be contacted for follow-up, select how you'd like to be contacted.
9. Choose how often you'd like to have follow-up sessions.
10. You are free to provide your emergency contact information if you feel it's necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What kind of counseling methods do you enjoy?** (`counseling_methods`, select_multiple): This field allows you to select the types of counseling methods you prefer. Choose from the list of available options to best describe your preferences.
* **Preferred communication style** (`communication_styles`, select_one): Select the communication style you prefer for sessions. This will help the counselor understand how to best communicate with you.
* **How often do you prefer sessions?** (`frequency_of_sessions`, select_one): This field is used to determine how often you'd like to have sessions. Choose from the available options to fit your needs.
* **Preferred language** (`preferred_language`, select_one): This field is used to determine your preferred language for sessions. Choose the language you feel most comfortable with.
* **Preferred time of day** (`preferred_time`, select_one): Select your preferred time of day for sessions. Choose from the list of available options to fit your schedule.
* **How do you want to be contacted?** (`contact_method`, select_multiple): This field is used to determine how you'd like to be contacted for sessions. Choose from the available options to best fit your needs.
* **Additional comments** (`notes`, text): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional comments or information you'd like to share with the counselor.
* **Emergency contact information** (`emergency_contact`, text): This field is optional and is used to provide your emergency contact information. You are not required to fill this in.
* **What are your next steps after leaving the session?** (`next_steps`, text): This field is optional and allows you to provide any information about your next steps after leaving the session.
* **Follow up contact information** (`follow_up_contact`, text): This field is optional and is used to provide follow-up contact information.
* **Follow up method** (`follow_up_method`, select_one): Select how you'd like to be contacted for follow-up sessions. Choose from the available options to best fit your needs.
* **Follow up frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_one): This field is used to determine how often you'd like to have follow-up sessions. Choose from the available options to fit your needs.
