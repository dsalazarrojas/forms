# Caregiver Burnout Case Interview Consent - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure the informed consent of participants in a caregiver burnout case interview study. The purpose of this form is to gather essential information about the research study, the participant's understanding and agreement to participate, and their informed consent to proceed with the interview.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Read each field's label and hint carefully to understand what information is being requested.
3. Make sure to review and agree to each section carefully before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study or Research Title** (`study_title`, text, required): This is the title of the research project that you will be participating in. Please enter the name of the study or research project.
* **Study Date and Interview Date** (`study_date`, date, required): This field requests the study date and the date of the interview. Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Researcher or Clinician Name** (`researcher_name`, text, required): This is the name and title of the principal investigator or interviewer conducting the research. Please enter their name and title.
* **Researcher Title and Affiliation** (`researcher_title`, text, required): This field requests the title and affiliation of the researcher or clinician. Please enter their title and organization.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This is your full name. Please enter your name as it is listed on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): This field requests your date of birth. Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): This is your email address. Please enter the email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): This field requests your phone number, including the area code. Please enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, not required): This field requests the name of an emergency contact person. Please enter the name of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, not required): This field requests the phone number of the emergency contact person, including the area code. Please enter the phone number.
* **Purpose of Study** (`study_purpose`, text, not required): This field requests a brief description of the purpose of the study. Please enter a short description of why you are participating in this study.
* **I Understand the Purpose of This Research** (`participation_understanding`, select_one, required): This field requests your understanding of the study's purpose. Please select the option that best describes your understanding.
* **Voluntary Participation Acknowledgment** (`participation_voluntary`, select_one, required): This field requests your acknowledgment that participation is voluntary. Please select the option that indicates you understand you can decline or withdraw at any time.
* **Confidentiality and Privacy Protection** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): This field requests your agreement to maintain confidentiality and privacy. Please select the option that indicates you understand how your information will be protected.
* **Understanding of Data Storage and Use** (`data_storage`, select_one, required): This field requests your understanding of how your data will be stored and used. Please select the option that indicates you understand the data storage and use procedures.
* **Permission to Record Interview** (`interview_recording`, select_one, required): This field requests your permission to record the interview. Please select the option that indicates whether you agree to recording.
* **Recording Storage and Destruction** (`recording_storage`, select_one, not required): This field requests your agreement to the storage and eventual destruction of recorded data. Please select the option that indicates you agree to the terms.
* **Right to Review Transcript** (`transcript_review`, select_one, not required): This field requests your right to review and correct the transcript of the interview. Please select the option that indicates you want to review the transcript.
* **Expected Interview Duration** (`interview_duration`, select_one, required): This field requests your agreement to the expected interview duration. Please select the option that indicates you accept or have time constraints.
* **Preferred Interview Location** (`interview_location`, select_one, required): This field requests your preferred interview location. Please select the option that indicates your preferred location.
* **Acknowledgment of Sensitive Topics** (`sensitive_topics`, select_one, required): This field requests your acknowledgment that sensitive topics will be discussed. Please select the option that indicates you understand sensitive topics will be discussed.
* **Understanding of Support Services** (`distress_support`, select_one, required): This field requests your understanding of support services available in case of distress. Please select the option that indicates you are aware of support services.
* **Understanding of Potential Risks and Benefits** (`risks_understanding`, select_one, required): This field requests your understanding of the potential risks and benefits of participating. Please select the option that indicates you understand the risks and benefits.
* **Compensation or Incentive Information** (`compensation`, text, not required): This field requests information about compensation or incentives for participating in the study. Please enter any relevant information.
* **Access to Research Results** (`results_access`, select_one, not required): This field requests your agreement to access and view research results. Please select the option that indicates whether you want to receive a summary of findings.
* **Contact Information Storage** (`contact_info_storage`, select_one, required): This field requests your agreement to store your contact information. Please select the option that indicates you want to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Permission for Data Sharing** (`data_sharing`, select_one, not required): This field requests your permission to share your data with other researchers. Please select the option that indicates you allow or restrict data sharing.
* **All My Questions Have Been Answered** (`questions_answered`, select_one, required): This field requests your confirmation that all your questions have been addressed. Please select the option that indicates you are satisfied with the answers.
* **I Voluntarily Consent to Participate in This Interview** (`consent_final`, select_one, required): This field requests your final consent to participate. Please select the option that indicates you voluntarily consent.
* **Date of Consent** (`signature_date`, date, required): This field requests the date of your consent. Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Tips
* Read each field carefully and make sure to understand what is being requested.
* Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
* Review and agree to each section carefully before proceeding.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please ask the researcher or clinician conducting the study.
