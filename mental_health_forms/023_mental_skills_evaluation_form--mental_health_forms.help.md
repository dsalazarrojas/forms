# Mental Skills Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help assess an individual's mental skills in various areas, allowing for a comprehensive understanding of their mental fitness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the corresponding page and follow the instructions below.

### Page 1: Self Motivation
* On the "Self Motivation" page, please describe in a few sentences how motivated you feel when working on tasks or projects.
* Please provide a clear and concise answer to help us understand your self-motivation levels.

### Page 2: Building Confidence
* On the "Building Confidence" page, describe a situation where you felt confident and achieved a desired outcome.
* Please share your thoughts on what contributed to your confidence and how it helped you.

### Page 3: Individual Visualization
* On the "Individual Visualization" page, think of a time when you used visualization techniques to overcome a challenge or obstacle.
* Describe the situation and how visualization helped you in your answer.

### Page 4: Faith
* On the "Faith" page, share a personal experience where you drew upon your faith to overcome a difficult situation.
* Please provide details on how your faith helped you during this time.

### Page 5: Dealing Difficult Situations
* On the "Dealing Difficult Situations" page, describe a challenging situation where you had to think critically and come up with a solution.
* Please share your thoughts on how you approached the situation and what skills you used to resolve it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Self Motivation** (`self_motivation`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can express your level of motivation when working on tasks or projects.
* **Building Confidence** (`building_confidence`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for describing a situation where you felt confident and achieved a desired outcome.
* **Individual Visualization** (`individual_visualization`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for thinking of a time when visualization techniques helped you overcome a challenge or obstacle.
* **Faith** (`faith`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can share a personal experience where you drew upon your faith to overcome a difficult situation.
* **Dealing Difficult Situations** (`dealing_difficult_situations`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for describing a challenging situation where you had to think critically and come up with a solution.

## Tips
* Please answer each field honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can use bullet points or paragraphs to structure your answers.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
