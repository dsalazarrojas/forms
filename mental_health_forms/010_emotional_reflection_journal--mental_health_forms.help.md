# Emotional Reflection Journal - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you, our client, reflect on your emotional state and share your thoughts and feelings with your mental health professional. It's a private and confidential space where you can express yourself honestly without fear of repercussions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **First Reflection** field with your initial thoughts and feelings.
2.  Select an option from the **Track Mood** dropdown menu to describe how you're feeling.
3.  If you'd like, provide an **Intensity Score** on a scale of 1-10 to give your mental health professional a better understanding of your emotional state.
4.  Add any additional **Note** you'd like to record about your thoughts and feelings.
5.  If you'd like to record a future reflection, select a date for **Next Reflection**.
6.  If you'd like to include any thoughts or feedback from your mental health professional, you can type in **Therapist Notes**.
7.  Similarly, if you'd like to add any feedback or suggestions from your mental health educator, you can type in **Educator Notes**.
8.  If you're finished with this reflection, please mark it as **Completed**.
9.  Record the date and time when you finished this reflection in **Completed On** and **Completed At**.
10.  If you'd like, record who completed this reflection in **Completed By**.
11.  Finally, provide your **Email** address so that you can be contacted by your mental health professional if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Reflection** (Text): A space for you to write about your initial thoughts and feelings.
* **Track Mood** (Select One): Choose an option to describe how you're feeling: Happy, Sad, Angry, Fearful, Calm, Anxious.
* **Intensity Score** (Number): A scale of 1-10 to gauge your emotional state.
* **Note** (Text): An extra space to record any additional thoughts and feelings.
* **Next Reflection** (Date): A date to schedule a future reflection.
* **Therapist Notes** (Text): A field for your mental health professional to add any thoughts or feedback.
* **Educator Notes** (Text): A field for your mental health educator to add any feedback or suggestions.
* **Completed** (Select One): Mark this reflection as finished or incomplete.
* **Completed On** (Date): The date when you completed this reflection.
* **Completed At** (Time): The time when you completed this reflection.
* **Completed By** (Text): Who completed this reflection.
* **Email** (Email): Your email address for future contact.
