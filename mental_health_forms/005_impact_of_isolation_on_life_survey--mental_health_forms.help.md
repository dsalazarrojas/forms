# Impact Of Isolation On Life Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Impact of Isolation On Life Survey is a collection of questions designed to help us understand how isolation is affecting your life. The survey will help us better understand your relationships, emotional states, coping mechanisms, and overall well-being during this time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select one option per question and proceed to the next page. If you have any concerns or doubts about the questions, please feel free to share them with us in the "Other comments" section at the end of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your relationship with the person you live with** (`1`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand who you live with during the lockdown. Please select one of the options:
	+ Isolated: You live with others, but you feel isolated.
	+ Alone: You live alone.
	+ With others: You live with others, and you feel supported.
* **What type of relationship you have with the person you live with** (`2`, `select_multiple`): This question is related to the previous one and helps us understand your relationship dynamics. Please select all that apply:
	+ Partner: Your partner or spouse.
	+ Spouse: Your spouse or partner.
	+ Boyfriend: Your boyfriend.
	+ Girlfriend: Your girlfriend.
	+ Ex-partner: Your ex-partner.
	+ Family member: A family member (e.g., parent, sibling, or child).
	+ Friend: A close friend.
	+ Other: Something else (please specify in the "Other comments" section).
* **How often do you feel lonely** (`3`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand how often you experience feelings of loneliness. Please select one of the options:
	+ 0: Never.
	+ 1-2 days: 1-2 days a week.
	+ 3-4 days: 3-4 days a week.
	+ 5-7 days: 5-7 days a week.
	+ Almost every day: Almost every day.
* **What types of social support do you have** (`4`, `select_multiple`): This question helps us understand your social support network. Please select all that apply:
	+ Family: Family members or family friends.
	+ Friends: Close friends.
	+ Community: Your community or neighbors.
	+ Online community: Online friends or online communities.
	+ Other: Something else (please specify in the "Other comments" section).
* **What is your main coping mechanism for loneliness** (`5`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand how you deal with feelings of loneliness. Please select one of the options:
	+ Talk to friends or family: Talking to loved ones.
	+ Exercise: Engaging in physical activity.
	+ Hobbies: Engaging in hobbies or creative activities.
	+ Watching movies or TV shows: Watching movies or TV shows.
	+ Sleeping: Sleeping or resting.
	+ Other: Something else (please specify in the "Other comments" section).
* **Has your life changed significantly since the start of the lockdown** (`6`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand if you feel like your life has changed since the lockdown started. Please select one of the options:
	+ True: Yes, your life has changed significantly.
	+ False: No, your life has not changed significantly.
* **How have you felt emotionally since the start of the lockdown** (`7`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand how your emotional state has changed since the lockdown started. Please select one of the options:
	+ Better: Your emotional state has improved.
	+ Worse: Your emotional state has worsened.
	+ About the same: Your emotional state has not changed.
* **What is the quality of your sleep** (`8`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand the quality of your sleep during the lockdown. Please select one of the options:
	+ Better: You sleep better.
	+ Worse: You sleep worse.
	+ About the same: Your sleep is unchanged.
* **How would you rate your physical health** (`9`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand your physical well-being during the lockdown. Please select one of the options:
	+ Much better: You feel much better physically.
	+ A bit better: You feel a bit better physically.
	+ About the same: You feel unchanged physically.
	+ A bit worse: You feel a bit worse physically.
	+ Much worse: You feel much worse physically.
* **How would you rate your mental health** (`10`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand your mental well-being during the lockdown. Please select one of the options:
	+ Much better: You feel much better mentally.
	+ A bit better: You feel a bit better mentally.
	+ About the same: You feel unchanged mentally.
	+ A bit worse: You feel a bit worse mentally.
	+ Much worse: You feel much worse mentally.
* **What is your level of anxiety** (`11`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand your level of anxiety during the lockdown. Please select one of the options:
	+ 0: None.
	+ 1-2: A low level of anxiety.
	+ 3-4: A moderate level of anxiety.
	+ 5-7: A high level of anxiety.
	+ 8-10: Very high anxiety.
* **Do you have any other comments or concerns that you would like to share with us** (`12`, `note`): This is an optional field where you can share any comments or concerns you have about the survey or your situation.
