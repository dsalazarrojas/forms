# Mental Health Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Billing Form is designed to collect and track essential information for mental health billing purposes. This includes client contact details, billing dates and amounts, payment information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the client's name and billing date.
2. Provide payment information, including billing address and notes.
3. Select the billing provider and payment status.
4. Add any additional billing notes or payment terms.
5. Enter the client's phone number and email for contact purposes.
6. Select the payment type and term.
7. Enter any additional payment terms or descriptions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client.
* **Billing Date** (`billing_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for which the billing is being made.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional payment information.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter the billing address of the client.
* **Payment Notes** (`payment_notes`, text, optional): Enter any payment notes or comments.
* **Billing Provider** (`billing_provider`, text, optional): Enter the billing provider's name.
* **Billing Status** (`billing_status`, select, optional): Select the current billing status (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Client Phone Number** (`client_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Billing Notes** (`billing_notes`, text, optional): Enter any billing notes or comments.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Terms Description** (`payment_term_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the payment terms.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount due for billing.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select, optional): Select the current payment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Client E-mail** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Billing Terms** (`billing_terms`, text, optional): Enter any billing terms or conditions.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of payment being made.
* **Client Name** (`client_name_alias`, text, optional): Enter the client's name (alias).
* **Billing Provider** (`billing_provider_alias`, text, optional): Enter the billing provider's name (alias).
* **Payment Term** (`payment_term`, text, optional): Enter any payment term or conditions.
* **Payment Term Description** (`payment_term_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the payment term.
