# Mental Health First Aid Training Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details from individuals interested in participating in mental health first aid training. It is used to register participants in the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in your basic contact information (participant name, organization name, participant email, participant phone number, and organization contact person).
- Enter the date and time of your preferred training session in the "Training Session Date" and "Training Session Time" fields.
- Provide a valid email and phone number in the "Participant Email" and "Participant Phone" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This is where you enter your name to be associated with your participation in the training session.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization you represent or are associated with.
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to attend the training session.
* **Training Session Time** (`training_session_time`, time, required): Choose the time slot for the training session you prefer.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number to ensure you can be contacted in case of any last-minute updates or changes.
* **Organization Contact Person** (`organization_contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person associated with your organization who will be the primary contact for the training session.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the date and time you select are correct and feasible for you.
- Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy to avoid any issues with communication.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the organization contact person for assistance.
