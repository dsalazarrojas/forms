# Mental Health Awareness Initiative Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Awareness Initiative Claim Form is used to report mental health claims and concerns to the organization. This form is intended to gather information about claimants and their experiences with mental health issues. The information collected will be used to better understand mental health trends and provide support and resources to claimants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information in the "Claimant" section.
2. Provide a brief description of your mental health concerns or claims in the "Claim description" field.
3. Indicate if you have any supporting documents or evidence related to your claim.
4. Check the "Support" box if you need or have support from the organization.
5. If applicable, provide your organization's contact information, including their name, email, phone number, and website.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Claimant Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Claimant Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Claim Date**: Date of the mental health claim or concern.
* **Claim Time**: Time of the mental health claim or concern.
* **Claim Description**: Provide a brief description of your mental health claim or concern.
* **Claim Details**: Provide any additional details about your mental health claim or concern.
* **Support**: Check this box if you need or have support from the organization.
* **Supporting Documents**: Check this box if you have supporting documents or evidence related to your claim.
* **Claimant Agreement**: This is a note field for any additional comments or agreements from the claimant.
* **Organization Name**: Enter your organization's name where the claim is being submitted to.
* **Organization Email**: Enter your organization's email address where you can be reached.
* **Organization Phone**: Enter your organization's phone number where you can be reached.
* **Organization Address**: Enter your organization's address.
* **Organization Website**: Enter your organization's website.
* **Organization Contact**: Enter the name of the person or department to contact.
* **Organization Fax**: Enter your organization's fax number.
* **Organization Notes**: This is a note field for any additional comments from the organization.
* **Claimant Notes**: This is a note field for any additional comments from the claimant.
* **Claimant Signature**: Please sign your name as the claimant.
* **Organization Signature**: Please sign your name as the organization representative.
* **Signature Date**: Date the form is signed.
* **Signature Time**: Time the form is signed.
* **Submission Date**: Date the form is submitted.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
- If you need help or have questions, contact the organization's contact person.
- This form is for mental health claims and concerns only. If you have general inquiries or feedback, please contact the organization through other means.
