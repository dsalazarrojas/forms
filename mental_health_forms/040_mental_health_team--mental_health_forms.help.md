# mental_health_team - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information regarding mental health referrals from patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Begin by filling out the referral form, which includes details about the referral type, date, and time.
2.  Move on to the patient information section, where you will input the patient's name, age, and other relevant details.
3.  Complete the referral status and comments section, where you can provide a brief description of the referral and its current status.
4.  Finally, review your completed form to ensure all required fields are filled and proceed with submitting.
