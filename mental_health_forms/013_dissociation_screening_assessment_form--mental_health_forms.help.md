# Dissociation Screening Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dissociation Screening Assessment Form is designed to assess an individual's experience with dissociation, a complex psychological phenomenon that can be triggered by various types of trauma. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the individual's mental health situation better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please carefully read each question and answer accordingly.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please do not hesitate to seek guidance from a healthcare professional.
3. Complete each question on one page at a time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Screening Assessment Form (Page 1)
**Screening Assessment Form** (`screening_assessment_form`, text, required): 
Please provide a brief description of your current mental health status, including any symptoms you are experiencing, such as anxiety, depression, or suicidal thoughts.

### 2. Trauma Experiences (Page 2)
**Trauma Experiences** (`trauma_experiences`, select_multiple, optional): 
Please select all the trauma experiences that you have undergone, such as abuse, neglect, or combat experiences. You can choose multiple options if applicable.

* Yes: Select if you have experienced any of the listed trauma.
* No: Select if you have not experienced any of the listed trauma.

### 3. Dissociation Questions (Page 3)
**Dissociation Questions** (`dissociation_questions`, text, optional): 
Please describe any experiences you have had with dissociation, such as feeling disconnected from your body or surroundings. Be as specific as possible.

### 4. Dissociative Features (Page 4)
**Dissociative Features** (`dissociative_features`, text, optional): 
Please describe any dissociative features you are experiencing, such as depersonalization or derealization. Be as specific as possible.

### 5. Emotional Dysfunctionality (Page 5)
**Emotional Dysfunctionality** (`emotional_dysfunctionality`, text, optional): 
Please describe any emotional symptoms you are experiencing, such as mood swings or emotional numbness. Be as specific as possible.

### 6. Emotional Regulation (Page 6)
**Emotional Regulation** (`emotional_regulation`, text, optional): 
Please describe your current emotional regulation strategies, such as deep breathing or exercise. Be as specific as possible.

### 7. Emotional Responses (Page 7)
**Emotional Responses** (`emotional_responses`, text, optional): 
Please describe your typical emotional responses to stressful situations, such as anger or anxiety. Be as specific as possible.

### 8. Self-Care (Page 8)
**Self-Care** (`self_care`, text, optional): 
Please describe your self-care strategies, such as meditation or journaling. Be as specific as possible.

### 9. Coping Strategies (Page 9)
**Coping Strategies** (`coping_strategies`, text, optional): 
Please describe your coping strategies, such as talking to a friend or engaging in a hobby. Be as specific as possible.

### 10. Mental Health Impact (Page 10)
**Mental Health Impact** (`mental_health_impact`, text, optional): 
Please describe the impact of your mental health on your daily life, relationships, and work or school performance. Be as specific as possible.
