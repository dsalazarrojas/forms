# Mental Health First Aid Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a Mental Health First Aid Course. If you have already registered, please do not fill out this form again as it is not necessary. Please ensure that you have already read and understood the course details before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your **User Details** on page 1. Enter your name, and any other relevant details you would like to provide.
* On page 2, select the **Course Selection** that you would like to register for. You can choose between "Mental Health First Aid Course" and "Mental Health First Aid Refresher Course".
* On page 3, select a date for the course. Please choose a date that you are available to attend.
* On page 4, select a time for the course. Please choose a time that suits you best.
* On page 5, enter your **Email** address. This is where you will receive communication about your course registration and any updates.
* On page 6, enter your **Phone** number. This is optional but recommended in case we need to contact you.
* On page 7, you can add any additional **Additional Information** about your registration.
* On page 8, enter your **Submission** notes or comments.
* On page 9, enter a date for submission of your registration.
* On page 10, select a time for submission of your registration.
* On page 11, enter a **Submission Comment** about your registration.
* On page 12, select a course again from the **Select a course** dropdown.
* On page 13, enter another **Submission Date**.
* On page 14, select a time for submission 2.
* On page 15, enter another **Submission Comment**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (Page 1): Your full name and any other relevant details you would like to provide.
* **Course Selection** (Page 2): Choose the course you would like to register for: "Mental Health First Aid Course" or "Mental Health First Aid Refresher Course".
* **Dates** (Page 3): Select a date that you are available to attend the course. This will be the date you will attend the actual course.
* **Time** (Page 4): Select a time that suits you best for the course. This will be the time you will start the course on the selected date.
* **Email** (Page 5): Enter your email address where you would like to receive communication about your course registration and any updates.
* **Phone** (Page 6): Your phone number, this is optional but recommended in case we need to contact you.
* **Additional Information** (Page 7): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Submission** (Page 8): Enter any notes or comments about your registration.
* **Submission Date** (Page 9): Enter the date of your registration submission.
* **Submission Time** (Page 10): Select the time of your registration submission.
* **Submission Comment** (Page 11): Enter a comment about your registration submission.
* **Select a course** (Page 12): Select a course again from the dropdown menu, this is for confirmation purposes.
* **Second Submission Date** (Page 13): Enter the date of the second submission of your registration.
* **Second Submission Time** (Page 14): Select the time of the second submission of your registration.
* **Second Submission Comment** (Page 15): Enter a comment about the second submission of your registration.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have already read and understood the course details before proceeding.
* Please fill out the form carefully as this will ensure that your registration is correct and up-to-date.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to us for assistance.
