# Student Mental Health Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Mental Health Check Form is a tool used to assess a student's mental health and well-being. It is designed to gather information from school psychologists, teachers, and parents to understand a student's current mental health status, identify potential concerns, and provide targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
* Select the relevant options for each field based on your knowledge of the student's mental health status.
* For fields with multiple options (e.g., select_multiple), choose all that apply.
* For fields with single-choice options (e.g., yes/no), select only one option per field.
