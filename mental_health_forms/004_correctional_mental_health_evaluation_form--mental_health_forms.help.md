# Correctional Mental Health Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the mental health of individuals in correctional facilities, gathering information on their mental health concerns, care team, goals, objectives, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by ensuring that the form is completed for the correct individual and that it is not a duplicate entry.
2.  Fill out the fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (2): Please enter the first name of the individual being evaluated.
*   **Last Name** (3): Enter the last name of the individual being evaluated.
*   **Date of Birth** (4): Enter the date of birth of the individual being evaluated.
*   **Date of Last Assessment** (5): Enter the date of the last mental health assessment for this individual.
*   **Mental Health Concerns** (6): List any mental health concerns that the individual has, such as anxiety, depression, or trauma.
*   **Mental Health Care Team** (7): List any mental health care professionals working with the individual, including their names and roles.
*   **Mental Health Goals** (8): Enter short-term and long-term goals for the individual's mental health, such as reducing anxiety or improving sleep.
*   **Mental Health Objectives** (9): Enter any specific objectives or targets for the individual's mental health, such as attending counseling sessions.
*   **Treatment Plans** (10): List any treatment plans or interventions being implemented to address the individual's mental health concerns.
*   **Mental Health Services** (11): Enter any mental health services that the individual is receiving, such as medication or therapy.
*   **Mental Health Outcomes** (12): Enter any positive outcomes or improvements that the individual has made in their mental health.
*   **Clinical Review Notes** (13): Enter any notes or observations from clinical reviews or assessments.
*   **Internal Coordination** (14): Enter any internal coordination notes, such as referrals or collaborations with other teams.
*   **Facility** (15): Indicate if the individual is in a facility, such as a hospital or correctional institution.
*   **Location** (16): Enter the location of the facility where the individual is currently residing.
*   **Date of Evaluation** (17): Enter the date of the current mental health evaluation.
*   **Date of Next Evaluation** (18): Enter the date of the next planned mental health evaluation.
*   **Evaluator Name** (19): Enter the name of the mental health evaluator.
*   **Evaluator Title** (20): Enter the title of the mental health evaluator.
*   **Evaluator Organization** (21): Enter the organization of the mental health evaluator.
*   **Evaluator Contact Info** (22): Enter contact information for the mental health evaluator.
*   **Evaluation Form Version** (23): Enter the version of the evaluation form used.
*   **Date of Revision** (24): Enter the date of the last revision of the form.
*   **Revision Notes** (25): Enter any explanation for any revisions made to the form.

Note: Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to provide a comprehensive assessment of the individual's mental health.
