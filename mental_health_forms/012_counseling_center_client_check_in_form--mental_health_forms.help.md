# Counseling Center Client Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Counseling Center Client Check In Form is designed to capture essential details about a client's session, allowing for efficient communication and record-keeping at the Counseling Center. This form serves as a tool for clients to check in for their sessions and for staff to track client progress and provide necessary care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the client's check-in details such as date and check-in time.
2. Enter the client's name and contact information for future reference.
3. Record the session topic and length for accurate session documentation.
4. Indicate the session status and location.
5. Provide any necessary notes for the client's next steps.
6. Review and submit the form to ensure accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Check In** (`client_check_in`, text, required): Enter the client's check-in details such as date and check-in time.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Record the date of the client's session.
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, text, required): Enter the check-in time of the client.
* **Counselor Name** (`counselor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the counselor handling the session.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number for contact.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address for communication.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, required): Record any necessary notes about the client's session.
* **Next Appointment** (`next_appointment`, date, required): Record the date of the client's next appointment.
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, text, required): Enter the topic of the client's session.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, select multiple, required): Select the length of the client's session.
* **Check In Location** (`check_in_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the client checked in.
* **Check In Status** (`check_in_status`, select multiple, required): Indicate the status of the client's check-in.
* **Session Status** (`session_status`, select multiple, required): Indicate the status of the client's session.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the client's session.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the client.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, text, required): Enter the type of the client's session.
* **Next Steps** (`next_step`, select multiple, required): Indicate the next steps for the client.
