# Mental Wellness Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Wellness Declaration Form is a tool for individuals to assess and declare their current mental wellness status. It is used to gather information about a person's mental health, emotional wellness level, and support systems in place. This form is not a clinical assessment, but rather a self-reporting tool to help individuals identify areas of concern and potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. For each question, select the answer that best describes your current situation.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, as indicated by the "required" label next to each field.
4. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **mental wellness declaration** (1, mental_wellness_declaration, note, not required) - Please provide a brief statement about your current mental wellness status. This is an open-ended field where you can describe your feelings, thoughts, and experiences.
* **mental health status** (2, mental_health_status, select_one, not required) - Select the option that best describes your current mental health status:
	+ Active: I am actively managing my mental health.
	+ Inactive: I am not actively managing my mental health.
* **emotional wellness level** (3, emotional_wellness_level, number, not required) - Rate your current emotional wellness level from 0 to 10, with 0 being extremely low and 10 being extremely high.
* **mental health days** (4, mental_health_days, number, not required) - If you have experienced any mental health days in the past week, enter the number of days.
* **support system** (5, support_system, select_multiple, not required) - Check the boxes for any support systems you have in place:
	+ Yes: I have a support system in place.
	+ No: I do not have a support system in place.
* **self-care plans** (6, self_care_plans, text, not required) - Describe your self-care plans, such as exercise, meditation, or hobbies.
* **mental health services** (7, mental_health_services, select_one, not required) - Select the option that best describes your access to mental health services:
	+ Yes: I have access to mental health services.
	+ No: I do not have access to mental health services.
* **mental health services frequencies** (8, mental_health_services_frequencies, select_multiple, not required) - Check the boxes for how often you access mental health services:
	+ Yes: I access mental health services regularly.
	+ No: I do not access mental health services regularly.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is not a substitute for professional medical advice, but rather a tool to help you assess your mental wellness status.
