# Mental Capacity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Capacity Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the mental capacity of a patient, including their cognitive state, ability to concentrate, and overall mental status. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's mental capacity and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Be sure to fill in all required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (\*).
3. If a field is not required, feel free to provide information if you think it is relevant to the patient's mental capacity.
4. If you are unsure about any question, consult with the patient or their healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (\*):**
	+ This field is used to capture the patient's basic contact information.
	+ Please fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Capacity Assessment (\*):**
	+ This field assesses the patient's mental capacity, which refers to their ability to understand and respond to their environment.
	+ Please select "competent" if the patient is able to understand and respond to their surroundings.
* **Cognitive Status:**
	+ This field evaluates the patient's cognitive state, including their ability to think, reason, and understand information.
	+ Please select all applicable options (e.g., normal, impaired) that describe the patient's cognitive status.
* **Capacity to Concentrate:**
	+ This field measures the patient's ability to focus and maintain attention.
	+ Please rate the patient's ability to concentrate on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being unable to concentrate and 10 being able to concentrate fully.
* **Mental State:**
	+ This field is used to describe the patient's mental state, including their mood, emotional state, and overall well-being.
	+ Please provide a brief description of the patient's mental state.
* **Physical Health:**
	+ This field assesses the patient's physical health, which may impact their mental capacity.
	+ Please provide any relevant information about the patient's physical health.
* **Medical History:**
	+ This field captures the patient's medical history, which may also impact their mental capacity.
	+ Please provide any relevant information about the patient's medical history.
* **Risk Factors:**
	+ This field identifies potential risk factors that may affect the patient's mental capacity.
	+ Please list any known risk factors that may impact the patient's mental capacity.
* **Medications:**
	+ This field is used to capture the patient's current medications and their potential effects on their mental capacity.
	+ Please list any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Mental Capacitor:**
	+ This field is used to assess the patient's mental state and cognitive function.
	+ Please select "True" if the patient is mentally capacitated, and "False" otherwise.
* **Capacity Level:**
	+ This field measures the patient's mental capacity at different levels.
	+ Please select the patient's capacity level for each field, using the provided options (e.g., high, low, moderate, unknown).

Note: Please fill in all fields consistently and accurately to the best of your ability. If you are unsure about any question, consult with the patient or their healthcare provider for guidance.
