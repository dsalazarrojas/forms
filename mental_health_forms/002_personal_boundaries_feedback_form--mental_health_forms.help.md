# Personal Boundaries Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Boundaries Feedback Form is a tool designed to assess an individual's perceptions of their workplace environment, relationships, and overall well-being. It helps identify areas where individuals feel safe or threatened, respected or disrespected, and where they may need support or resources to maintain healthy boundaries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and accurately, selecting the best response for each question based on your personal experiences and feelings. The form has two main sections: "Assessment" and "Feedback." 

### Assessment Section
The "Assessment" section is a series of numerical and select-one questions that gauge your perceptions of your workplace environment. Please select the number or option that best aligns with your experience.

*   **Workplace Safety** (`workplace_safety_score`): Rate your overall safety and security in the workplace, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very much."
*   **Boundary Respect** (`boundary_respect_score`): Rate how much you feel respected in the workplace, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very much."
*   **Workgroup Safety** (`workgroup_safety_score`): Rate your safety and security within your work group, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very much."
*   **Physical Safety** (`physical_safety_score`): Rate your physical safety and security in the workplace, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very much."
*   **Emotional Safety** (`emotional_safety_score`): Rate your emotional safety and security in the workplace, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very much."
*   **Communication** (`communication_score`): Rate the quality of communication in your work environment, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very much."
*   **Respect** (`respect_score`): Rate how much you feel respected in your work environment, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very much."
*   **Physical Environment** (`physical_environment_score`): Rate the physical safety and comfort of your work environment, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very much."
*   **Mental Space** (`mental_space_score`): Rate your mental safety and comfort in the workplace, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very much."
*   **Emotional Environment** (`emotional_environment_score`): Rate the emotional safety and comfort of your work environment, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very much."
*   **Physical Harms** (`physical_harms`): Select all the types of physical harms you have experienced or witnessed in the workplace.
*   **Physical Harms Concerns** (`physical_harms_concerns`): Describe any physical concerns you have about your work environment.
*   **Physical Interventions** (`physical_interventions`): Select if you have experienced any physical interventions in the workplace.
*   **Emotional Interventions** (`emotional_interventions`): Select if you have experienced any emotional interventions in the workplace.
*   **Mental Space Interventions** (`mental_space_interventions`): Select if you have experienced any mental space interventions in the workplace.
*   **Emotional Environment Interventions** (`emotional_environment_interventions`): Select if you have experienced any emotional environment interventions in the workplace.
*   **Physical Environment Interventions** (`physical_environment_interventions`): Select if you have experienced any physical environment interventions in the workplace.
*   **Boundary Conflict** (`boundary_conflict`): Select if you have experienced any boundary conflicts in the workplace.
*   **Boundary Conflict Frequency** (`boundary_conflict_frequency`): Select the frequency of boundary conflicts you have experienced in the workplace.
*   **Mental Health Impact** (`mental_health_impact`): Select the impact of your work environment on your mental health.
*   **Physical Safety Concerns** (`physical_safety_concerns`): Describe any physical safety concerns you have about your work environment.

### Feedback Section
The "Feedback" section is an open-ended text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for improving your work environment.

*   **Feedback** (`feedback`): Share any additional feedback or suggestions you have for creating a more positive and safe work environment.
*   **Suggestions** (`suggestions`): Share any suggestions you have for improving your work environment.

## Tips
When filling out this form, please be as honest and specific as possible. Your feedback is essential in helping us create a more supportive and respectful work environment, where everyone can feel safe and comfortable. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form.
