# mental_health_progress_notes_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health progress notes form is used to track and document the mental health progress and sessions of a patient. This form helps providers to maintain accurate and up-to-date records of patient information, including session dates, patient status, and medications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "mental health progress notes form" title.
2. Select the relevant date of the patient's session.
3. Enter the patient's ID number.
4. Enter the duration of the session.
5. Choose the patient's mental status and mood.
6. List any medications the patient is currently taking.
7. List any allergies the patient has.
8. Record any relevant medical history.
9. Record any relevant social history.
10. Document any physical exam results or notes.
11. Document the patient's current plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Date (Required)
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the patient's session, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
### patient_id (Required)
* **patient_id**: Enter the patient's ID number, as assigned by your organization.
### Session Duration (Required)
* **Session Duration**: Enter the length of the patient's session, in minutes.
### Mental Status (Optional)
* **Mental Status**: Choose one or more of the following:
	+ Anxious
	+ Depressed
	+ Euthymic
	+ Manic
	+ Normal
### Mood (Optional)
* **Mood**: Choose one of the following:
	+ Anxious
	+ Depressed
	+ Euthymic
	+ Manic
	+ Normal
### Medications (Optional)
* **Medications**: Select one or more of the following:
	+ Antidepressant
	+ Antipsychotic
	+ Benzodiazepine
	+ Lithium
### Allergies (Optional)
* **Allergies**: Select one or more of the following:
	+ Pain
	+ Sedative
	+ Sleep Aid
### Medical History (Optional)
* **Medical History**: Record any relevant medical history for the patient.
### Social History (Optional)
* **Social History**: Record any relevant social history for the patient.
### Physical Exam (Optional)
* **Physical Exam**: Record any relevant physical exam results or notes.
### Plan (Optional)
* **Plan**: Document the patient's current plan, as discussed during the session.

## Tips
* Ensure you have reviewed the patient's previous notes before completing this form.
* Double-check for accuracy and completeness before saving the form.
* Use the options provided for each field to ensure consistency and standardization.
