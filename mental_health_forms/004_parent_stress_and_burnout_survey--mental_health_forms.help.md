# Parent Stress And Burnout Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the stress levels and coping strategies of parents or caregivers who are providing care for a loved one. The survey consists of questions that will help us understand how often you provide care, how you manage stress, and how you interact with others for support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and select the relevant answers for each question.
2. Answer each question honestly, and to the best of your ability.
3. If you need to provide additional information or have any comments, use the "Additional Comments" field at the end of the form.
4. Please note that all answers are confidential and will be used to help us better understand the needs of parents and caregivers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page is an introduction to the survey and explains its purpose.
* **Demographic Questions**: This page has questions about your demographic information (e.g., whether you are a parent or a caregiver, how often you provide care, etc.).
	+ **Caregiver Info**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are a caregiver.
	+ **Frequency of Care**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate how often you provide care.
* **Caregiver Info**: This page has questions about your information as a caregiver.
	+ **Caregiver Coping Strategies**: Please select 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Other' to indicate how you manage stress as a caregiver.
	+ **Caregiver Stress Level**: Please select 'High', 'Low' or 'Other' to indicate your current level of stress.
* **Caregiver Stress Factors**: This page has questions about how you react to stress.
	+ **Emotional Reactions**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate how you typically react emotionally to stress.
	+ **Social Support**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you receive social support.
	+ **Caregiver Help**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you receive help from others.
* **Parent Info**: This page has questions about your information as a parent.
	+ **Parent Coping Strategies**: Please select 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Other' to indicate how you manage stress as a parent.
	+ **Parent Stress Level**: Please select 'High', 'Low' or 'Other' to indicate your current level of stress.
* **Additional Coping Strategies**: This page has questions about any additional coping strategies you use.
	+ **Other Coping Strategies Used by Caregiver**: Please select 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Other' to indicate any additional strategies you use.
	+ **Other Coping Strategies Used by Parent**: Please select 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Other' to indicate any additional strategies you use.
* **Survey Completion**: This page is for you to provide the date and time you completed the survey.
* **Comments**: This page is for you to provide any additional comments or information about your experience as a parent or caregiver.
* **Additional Comments**: This page is for you to provide any additional comments or information about your experience as a parent or caregiver.
