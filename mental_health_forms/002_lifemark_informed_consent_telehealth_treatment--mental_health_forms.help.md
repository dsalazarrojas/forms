This form is used for obtaining informed consent from patients prior to telehealth treatment. The form collects basic patient information, their consent to treatment, and relevant details about the service being provided. It also captures emergency contact information, date and time of service, and signatures from both the provider and patient.

Form purpose: Obtain informed consent from patients for telehealth treatment.

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your patient's basic information, including their name and contact details.
2.  Provide your consent to treatment by entering a statement agreeing to the terms.
3.  Enter your contact information for emergency purposes.
4.  Select the services you will be receiving.
5.  Enter the date and time of your service appointment.
6.  Enter your signature to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms.
7.  Enter your healthcare provider's signature to confirm they have read and will provide the agreed-upon service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required): Enter your patient's name and contact information.
    \[Example: John Doe, email: johndoe@example.com\]
*   **Consent Statement** (`consent_statement`, text, required): Provide a statement agreeing to the treatment terms and conditions.
    \[Example: "I consent to receive telehealth treatment from Lifemark."\
    \]
*   **Provider Information** (`provider_info`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information as the provider.
    \[Example: Dr. Smith, email: drsmith@lifecyclehealthcare.com\]
*   **Telehealth Services** (`telehealth_services`, select multiple, required): Select the services you will be receiving.
    \[Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5\]
*   **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, text, required): Enter your contact information for emergency purposes.
    \[Example: John Doe, phone: 123-456-7890\]
*   **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
    \[Example: johndoe@example.com\]
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the person to reach out in case of an emergency.
    \[Example: Jane Doe, phone: 987-654-3210\]
*   **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required): Enter the date of your service appointment.
    \[Format: MM/DD/YYYY\]
*   **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, required): Enter the time of your service appointment.
    \[Format: HH:MM\]
*   **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, text, required): Enter your signature as the provider.
    \[Example: Dr. Smith\]
*   **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Enter your signature as the patient.
    \[Example: John Doe\]
*   **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your service.
    \[Example: "Note: This is a test note."\]

Please ensure you have completed all required fields before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
