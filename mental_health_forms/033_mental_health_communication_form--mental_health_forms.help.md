# mental_health_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid mental health professionals in collecting and recording vital information about a patient's mental health status, medical history, and medication. It serves as a communication tool between the practitioner and the patient, ensuring accurate and thorough documentation for personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **communication_form** section, providing any relevant information about the patient's communication preferences and requirements.
2. In the **mentalhealth** field, select one of the provided options to indicate the patient's current mental health status.
3. The **medical_history** field is a note field where you can document any relevant medical history or conditions related to the patient's mental health.
4. Select any prescribed medications for the patient in the **medication_list** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **communication_form** (`prophets`, text, optional): This field is for noting any relevant details about the patient's communication preferences and requirements. Enter any relevant information here.
* **mentalhealth** (`mentalhealth`, select_one, required): This field is for selecting the patient's current mental health status from the provided options.
	+ Options: choice1, choice2, choice3
* **medical_history** (`mentalhealth`, note, optional): This field is for documenting any relevant medical history or conditions related to the patient's mental health.
* **medication_list** (`medication_list`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting any prescribed medications for the patient from the provided options.
	+ Options: choice1, choice2, choice3

## Tips
- Ensure you have a clear understanding of the patient's mental health status and medical history before completing this form.
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out the **medical_history** field.
- Select all relevant medications for the patient in the **medication_list** field.
- Use this form as a communication tool to ensure accurate and personalized care for the patient.
