## Therapy Feedback Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Therapy Feedback Consent Form is designed to gather feedback from patients about their therapy experiences, allowing the therapy center or organization to identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to better serve their patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Go to the first page and fill out the Session Info field with details about your recent therapy session, such as date, time, and any other relevant information.
2.  On the second page, rate your therapist's performance using the provided scale (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
3.  You can also provide any feedback about your therapist in the Feedback field.
4.  If you wish to share your session information, select "True" in the corresponding field.
5.  Finally, on the last page, provide your email and phone number for further communication.
6.  Check the consent box to confirm that you have provided your feedback willingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Info** (1): This field is for providing session-specific details such as date, time, and any other relevant information.
  - Type: Text field
  - Required: Not required
- **Therapist** (2): Rate your therapist's performance on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
  - Type: Select multiple options
  - Required: Not required
- **Feedback** (3): Provide any feedback or comments about your therapist.
  - Type: Text field
  - Required: Not required
- **Would you like to share your session info** (4): Check the box if you wish to share your session information.
  - Type: Select one option
  - Required: Not required
- **Date and Time** (5): Enter the date and time of your session.
  - Type: Date field
  - Required: Not required
- **E-mail** (6): Provide your email address for further communication.
  - Type: Email field
  - Required: Not required
- **Phone** (7): Enter your phone number.
  - Type: Text field
  - Required: Not required
- **Consent** (8): Check the box to confirm that you have provided your feedback willingly.
  - Type: Select one option
  - Required: Not required

## Tips
Please note that this form is designed to collect feedback and ratings for improvement purposes only. All information provided will be kept confidential and used solely for this purpose. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
