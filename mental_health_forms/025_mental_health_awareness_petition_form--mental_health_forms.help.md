# Mental Health Awareness Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Awareness Petition Form is used to collect information and signatures from individuals or organizations in support of mental health awareness. This form is designed to gather essential details, including names, contact information, and a personal message, to help raise awareness and promote mental health initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, please follow these steps:
	* Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) symbol.
	* Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
	* Provide your Email address, if you have one.
	* Fill in your Phone number, if you have one.
	* Write a brief Message describing your support for mental health awareness.
	* Select the type of Signature you would like to provide (Digital, Handwritten, or X-Signature).
	* If applicable, provide a Signature Image URL, Signature OTP (One-Time Password), and Signature Note.
	* Enter your Organization and Role, if you are representing an organization.
	* Provide the Contact Person, Contact Email, Contact Phone, and Contact Note details, if you are representing an organization.
	* Enter your Address, City, State, Zip, and Country details, if you have a physical location.
	* Add any Comments, if you have additional information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (Email, Optional): If you have a valid email address, please enter it. This will help us contact you.
- **Phone** (Text, Optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it.
- **Message** (Text, Required): Write a brief message describing your support for mental health awareness.
- **Signature** (Text, Required): This field is for your digital signature.
- **Can we share your info?** (Select One, Required): Select 'True' to allow us to share your information with our organization and 'False' to keep it confidential.
- **Date** (Date, Optional): If you have a specific date for your signature.
- **Time** (Time, Optional): If you have a specific time for your signature.
- **Signature Type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the type of signature you want to provide: Digital, Handwritten, or X-Signature.
- **Signature Image URL** (Text, Optional): If you have a digital signature image, please provide the URL.
- **Signature OTP** (Text, Optional): If you have a one-time password for your signature, please enter it.
- **Signature Note** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional note for your signature, please enter it.
- **Organization** (Text, Optional): If you are representing an organization, enter its name.
- **Role** (Text, Optional): If you are representing an organization, enter your role within it.
- **Contact Person** (Text, Optional): If you are representing an organization, enter the contact person's name.
- **Contact Email** (Text, Optional): If you are representing an organization, enter the contact email.
- **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): If you are representing an organization, enter the contact phone number.
- **Contact Note** (Text, Optional): If you are representing an organization, enter any additional contact note.
- **Address** (Text, Optional): If you have a physical location, enter your address.
- **City** (Text, Optional): If you have a physical location, enter the city.
- **State** (Text, Optional): If you have a physical location, enter the state or region.
- **Zip** (Text, Optional): If you have a physical location, enter the zip code.
- **Country** (Text, Optional): If you have a physical location, enter the country.
- **Comments** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional comments, enter them here.
