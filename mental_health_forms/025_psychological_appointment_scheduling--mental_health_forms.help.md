# Psychological Appointment Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a psychological appointment. It collects information about the patient's contact preferences, available days and times, and appointment details. Please fill out this form to request a new appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. In the "Appointment Request" section, describe your reason for requesting a psychological appointment.
3. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or video call) in the "Preferred Contact Method" section.
4. Choose the days of the week when you are available for the appointment in the "Available Days" section.
5. Select the start and end times for the appointment in the "Start Time" and "End Time" sections.
6. In the "Preferred Days" section, choose the days of the week when you would like to receive calls.
7. Select the time for the call in the "Preferred Time" section.
8. If you have any special requests (e.g. accommodations or specific location), enter them in the "Special Request" section.
9. In the "Therapist Info" section, enter any relevant information about the therapist you prefer to work with.
10. Choose the type of appointment (individual, group, or couples) and session frequency (daily, weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) in the "Appointment Type" and "Session Frequency" sections.
11. Enter any additional notes or comments about the appointment in the "Note" section.
12. Enter your email address in the "Email" section (if applicable).
13. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section (if applicable).
14. If you have any other requests or comments, enter them in the "Video Call" section (if applicable).

* **Therapist Info** (Therapist Info, text, required): Enter any relevant information about the therapist you prefer to work with.
* **Appointment Type** (Appointment Type, select_one, required): Select the type of appointment (individual, group, or couples).
