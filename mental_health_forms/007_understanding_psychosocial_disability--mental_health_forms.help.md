## understanding-psychosocial-disability - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is designed to help researchers understand and gather information about psychosocial disability. It aims to gather user identification, select one psychosocial disability type, disability duration, and other relevant details. This information will help researchers to better understand the psychosocial disability and its impact on individuals.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **user identification** in the first field. This should include your name, email address, and any other identifying information.
2. Choose the correct **select-one-psychosocial-disability-type** from the dropdown menu. This will help the researchers to understand the specific type of psychosocial disability you are experiencing.
3. Indicate if you **disability_duration** has a certain duration or if it's not applicable.
4. Share your experiences with **experiences-of-psychosocial-disability** to provide more context about your psychosocial disability.
5. Describe the **challenges-of-psychosocial-disability** you have faced due to your psychosocial disability.
6. Share your **ideas-for-advocacy** if you have any suggestions or ideas for advocacy.
7. If applicable, fill in the **research_participation** field with the number of research participation you are willing to take part in.
8. Provide the details about the **awareness_project**, **mental_health_team**, **researcher_organization**, **advocate_organization**, and **other_organization**.
9. You may also provide **contact_info** if you prefer to be contacted for further questions or discussions.
10. Finally, fill in any additional **additional_notes** you may have.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user-identification** (text, required): Please fill in your name, email address, and any other identifying information.
* **select-one-psychosocial-disability-type** (select_one, required): Choose the specific type of psychosocial disability you are experiencing.
* **disability-duration** (select_one, required): Indicate if your psychosocial disability has a certain duration or if it's not applicable.
* **experiences-of-psychosocial-disability** (text, required): Share your experiences with psychosocial disability to provide more context.
* **challenges-of-psychosocial-disability** (text, required): Describe the challenges you have faced due to your psychosocial disability.
* **ideas-for-advocacy** (text, required): Share your ideas or suggestions for advocacy.
* **research_participation** (number, optional): If applicable, fill in the number of research participation you are willing to take part in.
* **awareness_project** (text, required): Provide details about the awareness project you are involved in.
* **mental_health_team** (text, required): Describe the mental health team you are working with.
* **researcher_organization** (text, required): Fill in the details about the researcher organization you are affiliated with.
* **advocate_organization** (text, required): Provide details about the advocate organization you are part of.
* **other_organization** (text, optional): If you are affiliated with any other organization, fill in the details.
* **other_organization_details** (text, optional): Provide more details about the other organization you are affiliated with.
* **contact_info** (text, optional): Fill in your contact information if you prefer to be contacted for further questions or discussions.
* **additional_notes** (text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **select_one_mental_health_profession** (select_one, optional): If you are a mental health professional, choose 'Yes' to indicate your profession.
* **researcher_interests** (text, optional): Share your interests as a researcher.
* **awareness_project_description** (text, optional): Describe the awareness project you are involved in.
* **awareness_project_duration** (text, optional): Provide the duration of the awareness project you are involved in.
* **researcher_organization_url** (text, optional): Fill in the URL of the researcher organization you are affiliated with.
* **research_participation_agreement** (select_one, optional): If you are willing to participate in research, choose 'Yes' to indicate your agreement.
* **researcher_organization_contact_info** (text, optional): Fill in the contact information of the researcher organization you are affiliated with.
* **awareness_project_location** (text, optional): Provide the location of the awareness project you are involved in.
* **research_questions** (text, optional): Answer any research questions or discussions.
* **mental_health_organization** (text, optional): Describe the mental health organization you are part of.
* **researcher_interests** (text, optional): Share your interests as a researcher.
* **researcher_organization_contact_info** (text, optional): Fill in the contact information of the researcher organization you are affiliated with.

### Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure that your information is accurate and complete.
* You can provide any additional notes or comments in the **additional_notes** field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to complete. If you encounter any issues, please let us know.
* This form is subject to change and updates may be made in the future.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form.
