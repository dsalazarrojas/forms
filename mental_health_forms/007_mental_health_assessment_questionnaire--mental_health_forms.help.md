# mental_health_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health assessment questionnaire is a tool designed to gather information about an individual's mental health concerns, symptoms, and history. It helps healthcare providers better understand the individual's mental health needs and provide appropriate care and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the mental health assessment questionnaire, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing a brief introduction to your mental health concerns and concerns.
2. Answer each question in the corresponding section, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the relevant options that apply to your situation.
4. Provide any additional information that may be helpful for your healthcare provider to understand your mental health needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, optional): This is a brief introduction to your mental health concerns and concerns.
* **Mental Health Concerns** (`2`, `text`, optional): Describe any mental health concerns you have, such as anxiety, depression, or trauma.
* **Anxiety** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable anxiety-related symptoms from the list, such as:
	+ feeling overwhelmed or anxious
	+ difficulty concentrating or making decisions
	+ excessive worry or fear
	+ physical symptoms like headaches or stomach problems
* **Depression** (`4`, `text`, optional): Describe any depressive symptoms you experience, such as feelings of sadness, loss of interest, or changes in appetite.
* **Trauma** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of trauma you have experienced:
	+ Emotional trauma (e.g., abuse, neglect)
	+ Physical trauma (e.g., injury, surgery)
	+ Complex trauma (e.g., attachment issues, multiple traumas)
* **Stress** (`6`, `number`, optional): Rate your stress levels from 1-10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **Emotional Symptoms** (`7`, `text`, optional): Describe any emotional symptoms you experience, such as irritability, mood swings, or emotional numbing.
* **Cognitive Symptoms** (`8`, `text`, optional): Describe any cognitive symptoms you experience, such as difficulty concentrating, memory problems, or difficulty making decisions.
* **Sleep** (`9`, `select_one`, optional): Select the quality of your sleep:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Relationships** (`10`, `text`, optional): Describe your relationships with family, friends, or colleagues, including any conflicts or difficulties.
* **Self-Care** (`11`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable self-care activities you engage in:
	+ Exercise or physical activity
	+ Meditation or mindfulness
	+ Spending time with loved ones
	+ Creative activities
* **Mental Health History** (`12`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief history of your mental health, including any previous diagnoses, treatment, or hospitalizations.
* **Medical History** (`13`, `text`, optional): Describe any medical conditions or concerns that may impact your mental health.
* **Medication** (`14`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any medications you take for mental health conditions:
	+ Antidepressants
	+ Anti-anxiety medication
	+ Mood stabilizers
* **Treatment** (`15`, `text`, optional): Describe any previous treatment or therapy you've received for mental health concerns.
* **Emergency Contact** (`16`, `text`, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Next Steps** (`17`, `text`, optional): Describe any next steps or appointments you have scheduled.
* **Mental Health Services** (`18`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of mental health services you may need:
	+ Counseling or therapy
	+ Medication management
	+ Support groups
* **Follow-Up Appointments** (`19`, `text`, optional): Schedule follow-up appointments with your healthcare provider.
* **Follow-Up Contacts** (`20`, `text`, optional): Provide any follow-up contact information.
* **Mental Health Provider** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Select your mental health provider:
	+ Psychiatrist
	+ Therapist
	+ Primary care physician
* **Mental Health Specialty** (`22`, `text`, optional): Describe any mental health specialties or interests (e.g., trauma, anxiety, addiction).
* **Mental Health Location** (`23`, `text`, optional): Describe any mental health location or clinic where you receive services.
* **Follow-Up Notes** (`24`, `text`, optional): Provide any follow-up notes for your healthcare provider.
* **Next Steps Notes** (`25`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional next steps or instructions for your healthcare provider.

## Tips

* Be as specific and detailed as possible when answering each question.
* If you have difficulty understanding a question or section, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is confidential and meant to help your healthcare provider provide personalized care and support.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
