# mental_health_startup_accelerator_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health startup accelerator application form is designed to help startups seeking guidance on mental health and wellness initiatives for their business growth. By completing this form, you will provide the required information to our team, who will review your submission and offer tailored advice and resources to support your startup's growth.

## How To Complete This Form

Before you begin, make sure you have all the necessary information and answers ready. The form is divided into several pages, each with its own fields and explanations. Read each page carefully and answer all the required questions thoroughly. If you encounter any issues or have questions, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name and contact information. This is a general information page where you can introduce yourself and your startup.
* **Startup Stage** (`startup_stage`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the current stage of your startup (e.g., Early-Stage, Late-Stage, Scalable-Startups, Series-A, Series-B, Series-C, or Other).
* **Mission** (`mission`, text, required):
	+ Describe the mission and purpose of your startup.
* **Startup Name** (`startup_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter the name of your startup.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required):
	+ List the names of your team members who will be representing your startup.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required):
	+ Enter the email address of the person responsible for contact and communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required):
	+ Provide the phone number of the person responsible for contact and communication.
* **Startup Description** (`startup_description`, note, optional):
	+ Provide a detailed description of your startup, including its goals, challenges, and achievements.
* **Additional Comments** (`additionalchartinstance`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your startup's mental health and wellness initiatives.

## Tips

* Be as thorough and accurate as possible when filling out the form to ensure that our team can provide you with the best possible guidance and support.
* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
