# mental_health_workshop_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health workshop reservation form is a tool used by workshop organizers to collect information from participants about their preferences and availability for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### workshop_details
* **workshop_details** (`workshop_details`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the workshop you are interested in reserving.
* **participant_info** (`participant_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing information about yourself as a participant, such as your name and contact details.
* **workshop_preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, text, required/optional): This field is for specifying your preferences for the workshop, such as the date and time you can attend.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing your contact details, including your email and phone number.
* **workshop_availability** (`workshop_availability`, text, required/optional): This field is for specifying your availability to attend the workshop, such as your availability for a set period of time.

## Tips
* Be sure to answer all the required fields.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact the workshop organizers.
