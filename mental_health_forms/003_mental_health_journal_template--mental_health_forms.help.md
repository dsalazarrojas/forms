Mental Health Journal Template - Help Guide
=====================================

### Purpose
The Mental Health Journal Template is a form designed to help individuals track their mental health progress over time. This template is intended for personal use, providing a structured format for recording daily mood, mood ratings, and other relevant mental health metrics.

### How To Complete This Form
To use this template, follow these steps:

1. Select the corresponding answers for each field, based on your current state.
2. For select_multiple fields, such as Daily Mood and Medications Reminders, choose the options that best describe your experience.
3. In the Notes and Thoughts field, provide any additional information or comments you'd like to track.
4. When scheduling a consult or session, enter the desired date and time.
5. Track your progress over time by revisiting the form regularly and updating your answers accordingly.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Mood** (daily_mood): Select how you felt today, choosing from 'Yes' (e.g., good, great, okay) or 'No' (e.g., bad, terrible).
* **Mood Rating** (mood_rating): Rate your current mood on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Energy Level** (energy_level): Rate your current energy level on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Anxiety Level** (anxiety_level): Rate your current anxiety level on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Sleep Quality** (sleep_quality): Rate your sleep quality on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Stress Level** (stress_level): Rate your current stress level on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
