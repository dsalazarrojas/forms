# mental_health_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for mental health professionals to track and record mental health information, providing a comprehensive overview of patients' mental states, symptoms, and treatment plans. It's designed to help professionals make informed decisions and provide targeted care.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To get started, please review the form fields below.
*   Fill in the required information, such as mental state, symptoms, stress level, and appointment details.
*   Select the relevant answers for each question from the provided options.
*   Be thorough when filling out the form, as it will help professionals better understand and support you.
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Header**: This field is not required and serves as a header for the form.
*   **Mental State**: Please select all the mental states that apply to your current condition. (Required)
    *   Anxiety
    *   Depression
    *   Mood
    *   Mood Disorder
    *   Other
*   **Symptoms**: Please select the symptoms that you are currently experiencing. (Required)
    *   Anxiety Symptoms
    *   Depression Symptoms
    *   Other Symptoms
    *   No Symptoms
*   **Stress Level**: Please select your current stress level. (Required)
    *   High
    *   Low
    *   Medium
*   **Medication**: Please indicate whether you are currently taking any medication. (Optional)
    *   'Yes'
    *   'No'
*   **Appointment**: Please provide the date and time of your next appointment. (Required)
*   **Next Visit**: Please provide the date of your next visit. (Required)
*   **Mental Health Monitoring**: This field is optional and provides a text area for you to note any specific concerns or observations.
*   **Emergency Contact**: Please select all emergency contacts that you would like to be reached in case of an emergency. (Required)
    *   Mother
    *   Father
    *   Spouse
    *   Child
    *   Other
*   **Contact Name**: Please provide the name of your emergency contact. (Required)
*   **Contact Number**: Please provide the contact number of your emergency contact. (Required)
*   **Provider Information**: Please provide any relevant information about your mental health professional. (Required)
*   **Medical History**: Please provide a brief history of your medical background. (Required)
*   **Medication Side Effects**: This field is optional and provides a text area for you to note any side effects from your medication.
*   **Mental Health Professional**: Please select your current mental health professional. (Optional)
    *   Psychologist
    *   Therapist
    *   Doctor
*   **Professional Name**: Please provide the name of your mental health professional. (Optional)
*   **Professional Relationship**: Please indicate whether you have a professional relationship with your mental health professional. (Optional)
    *   'Yes'
    *   'No'
*   **Professional Notes**: This field is optional and provides a text area for your mental health professional to note any additional information.
*   **User Notes**: This field is optional and provides a text area for you to note any personal thoughts or observations.
*   **Follow Up Plan**: Please provide a plan for your follow-up appointment. (Required)
*   **Follow Up Date**: Please provide the date of your follow-up appointment. (Required)
*   **Follow Up Time**: Please provide the time of your follow-up appointment. (Required)
*   **Follow Up Method**: Please select the follow-up method. (Required)
    *   Phone Call
    *   Video Call
    *   Meeting
    *   Other
*   **Follow Up Frequency**: Please select the frequency of your follow-up appointments. (Required)
    *   Once
    *   Twice
    *   Weekly
    *   Monthly
    *   Quarterly
    *   Yearly

# Tips

*   Be thorough when filling out the form, as it will help mental health professionals provide you with accurate and effective care.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to consult with your mental health professional.
*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before proceeding.
