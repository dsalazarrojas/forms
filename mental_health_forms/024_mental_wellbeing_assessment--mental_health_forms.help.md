# mental_wellbeing_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental_wellbeing_assessment form is designed to help individuals evaluate their mental health status, set goals for improvement, and identify areas of concern.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your User Name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter a valid email address you want to use for further communication or follow-up.
3. If you have a phone number, provide it for contact purposes. This is optional.
4. Briefly describe your medical history, including any mental health conditions you have.
5. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Rate your current mental health status from Excellent to Very Bad.
7. Check all mental health services you are currently using or would like to use.
8. Identify who you get support from (Family, Friend, Mental Health Professional, Self).
9. If you wish, describe your short-term and long-term goals for improving your mental health.
10. You will receive a mental well-being score based on your answers.
11. Add any comments regarding your current mental well-being or the results of your assessment.
12. Identify areas where you are concerned (Anxiety, Depression, Mood, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name** : This field requires your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** : This is your email address which will be used for further communication or follow-up.
- **Phone** : If you have a phone number, please enter it for contact purposes.
- **Medical History** : Describe any mental health conditions you have or have had.
- **Date of Birth** : Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Mental Health Status** : Rate your current mental health status from Excellent to Very Bad.
- **Mental Health Services** : Select all services you are using or would like to use.
- **Mental Health Support System** : Identify who provides you support (Family, Friend, Mental Health Professional, Self).
- **Mental Health Goals** : Describe short-term and long-term goals for improving mental health.
- **Mental Wellbeing Score** : This is a numerical score based on your answers.
- **Mental Wellbeing Comment** : Add comments on your current mental well-being or the assessment results.
- **Mental Health Concerns** : Identify areas of concern (Anxiety, Depression, Mood, Other).
