# mental_health_review_appointment_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health review appointment form template is designed to standardize the process of reviewing and documenting mental health appointments. This form is intended to be filled out by the patient or individual being reviewed, as well as the provider and reviewer, to ensure that all necessary information is collected and documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the mental health form fields with your personal information and details about your mental health issues.
2. Enter your date of birth in the date of birth field.
3. If you have any mental health issues, enter them in the mental health issue field.
4. List any relevant medical history in the medical history field.
5. If you have any medication allergies, list them in the medication allergies field.
6. If you have any medical conditions, list them in the medical conditions field.
7. Enter the date and time of your appointment in the appointment date and appointment time fields.
8. Enter the location of your appointment in the appointment location field.
9. Write any additional notes about your appointment in the appointment notes field.
10. Enter the name and license number of your provider in the provider name and provider license number fields.
11. Enter the name and license number of the reviewer in the reviewer name and reviewer license number fields.
12. Choose the type of review you are undergoing in the review type field.
13. Choose the frequency of your reviews in the review frequency field.
14. Enter any additional notes about your review in the notes field.
15. Finally, enter the date of your review in the review date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mental Health Form** (`mental health form`, text, required): Fill out this field with any mental health issues or concerns you are experiencing.
* **Date of Birth** (`date of birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Mental Health Issue** (`mental health issue`, text, optional): If you have any mental health issues, list them here.
* **Medical History** (`medical history`, text, optional): If you have any relevant medical history, list it here.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication allergies`, text, optional): If you have any medication allergies, list them here.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Medication Conditions** (`medication conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medication conditions, list them here.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Appointment Location** (`appointment location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your appointment.
* **Appointment Notes** (`appointment notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your appointment.
* **Provider Name** (`provider name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your provider.
* **Provider License Number** (`provider license number`, text, optional): Enter the license number of your provider.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your reviewer.
* **Reviewer License Number** (`reviewer license number`, text, optional): Enter the license number of your reviewer.
* **Review Type** (`review type`, text, optional): Choose the type of review you are undergoing.
* **Review Frequency** (`review frequency`, text, optional): Choose the frequency of your reviews.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your review.
* **Review Date** (`review date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your review.
