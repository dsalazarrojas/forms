# mental_health_support_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for volunteers to register themselves for mental health support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your organization name.
- Select your role as a volunteer.
- Enter your contact email.
- Provide your availability, including weekends and holidays.
- Add any personal information you'd like to share.
- Indicate your volunteer hours.
- Specify your volunteer start date.
- Provide availability details.
- Enter your volunteer end date.
- Enter your contact phone number.
- Write a description of your volunteer role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are registering with (not required).
* **Role**: Select your role as a volunteer, either "Volunteer", "Staff", or "Other" (not required).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted (not required).
* **Availability**: Enter your availability, specifying if you are available on weekends and holidays (required).
* **Personal Information**: Provide any personal details you'd like to share about yourself (not required).
* **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the number of hours you are able to volunteer (not required).
* **Volunteer Start Date**: Enter the date you started volunteering (not required).
* **Availability Details**: Add any additional details about your availability (not required).
* **Volunteer End Date**: Enter the date you ended your volunteering (not required).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted (not required).
* **Volunteer Description**: Write a description of your volunteer role and experience (not required).
