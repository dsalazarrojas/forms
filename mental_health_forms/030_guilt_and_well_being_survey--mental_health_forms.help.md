# Guilt And Well Being Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help you assess your mental well-being and guilt feelings. It is a self-reporting tool used to gather information about your feelings and well-being scores.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
* Answer each question to the best of your ability.
* Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Text)**: This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and instructions.
* **Guilt Feelings** (Required, Text): Describe your current levels of guilt and feelings of remorse or regret.
	+ This is a required field as it helps understand your emotional state.
* **Well Being Scores 1-5** (Required, Select One): Choose the score that best describes your current well-being.
	+ This is a required field as it helps measure your overall well-being.
* **Emotions (Time)**: Report the amount of time you have spent feeling emotions such as anxiety, anger, or sadness.
	+ This field is required as it helps track time spent feeling negative emotions.
* **Feelings (Text)**: Describe your current feelings and emotions.
	+ This is an optional field as it provides additional information about your emotional state.
* **Well Being Scores 2-10** (Optional, Select One/Select Multiple/Number): Choose the score that best describes your current well-being for each question.
	+ These fields are optional as they provide additional context and information about your well-being.
* **Well Being Scores 11-17** (Optional, Select One/Select Multiple/Number): Choose the score that best describes your current well-being for each question.
	+ These fields are optional as they provide additional context and information about your well-being.
* **Feelings 2-3** (Optional, Text): Describe your current feelings and emotions.
	+ These fields are optional as they provide additional information about your emotional state.
* **Well Being Scores 18-25** (Optional, Select One/Select Multiple/Number): Choose the score that best describes your current well-being for each question.
	+ These fields are optional as they provide additional context and information about your well-being.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to the relevant authorities or professionals.
