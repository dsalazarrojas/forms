# Mental Health Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Workshop Registration Form is a tool to collect participant information and preferences for a mental health workshop. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure a smooth and personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready, including participant details, contact information, and workshop preferences.
2. Complete each field according to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Name, text, required/optional): Please enter your full name as you would like to be referred to during the workshop.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive important workshop updates and information.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number to enable communication and follow-up on workshop details.
* **Address** (Address, text, required/optional): Enter your address for workshop venue and follow-up purposes.
* **Registration Date** (Registration Date, date, required/optional): Select the date you plan to register for the workshop.
* **Registration Time** (Registration Time, time, required/optional): Choose your preferred registration time.
* **Workshop Preferences** (Workshop Preferences, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred workshop time: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Workshop Date** (Workshop Date, date, required/optional): Select the date you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (Workshop Time, time, required/optional): Choose your preferred workshop time.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or requirements for your participation in the workshop.

## Tips
1. Review your answers carefully before submission.
2. If you are unsure about any information, please contact us.
3. Please note that workshop capacity is limited, and registrations will be reviewed on a first-come, first-served basis. 

Note: This help guide provides a general explanation for the form fields. If your specific use case has additional requirements or constraints, please refer to your workshop administrator or organization's guidelines.
