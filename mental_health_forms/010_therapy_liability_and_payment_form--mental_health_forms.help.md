# Therapy Liability And Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track information related to therapy session liabilities, payment terms, and refund policies for clients.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form for each new client or session interaction.
*   Ensure that each field is only filled out once per client or session.
*   Review and update the form as necessary to reflect changes in client or payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information** (`client_info`): Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
*   **Consent** (`consent`): Confirm that the client understands the payment terms and policies.
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Note any specific payment terms or conditions agreed upon by the client.
*   **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): Enter any additional payment information, such as the payment method or amount.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select whether the client has agreed to pay for the session or not.
*   **Client Payment** (`client_payment`): Enter the client's payment amount, if applicable.
*   **Session Payment** (`session_payment`): Select whether the session is paid or not.
*   **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Choose whether the payment is active or inactive.
*   **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the payment amount, if applicable.
*   **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes related to the payment or session.
*   **Client Notes** (`client_notes`): Add any client-specific notes.
*   **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`): Select whether a payment gateway is used or not.
*   **Payment Gateway Error** (`payment_gateway_error`): Enter any errors related to the payment gateway.
*   **Session Details** (`session_details`): Enter any session-specific details.
*   **Payment Refund** (`payment_refund`): Select whether a refund is available or not.
*   **Refund Reason** (`payment_refund_reason`): Enter the reason for the refund.
*   **Refund Amount** (`payment_refund_amount`): Enter the refund amount, if applicable.
*   **Refund Status** (`payment_refund_status`): Choose whether the refund is active or inactive.
*   **Refund Notes** (`payment_refund_notes`): Add any refund-specific notes.
*   **Refund Date** (`payment_refund_date`): Enter the date of the refund.
*   **Refund Time** (`payment_refund_time`): Enter the time of the refund.
*   **Refund Method** (`payment_refund_method`): Select whether a refund method is used or not.
*   **Refund Status** (`payment_refund_status`): Choose whether the refund is active or inactive.
*   **Refund Amount** (`payment_refund_amount`): Enter the refund amount, if applicable.

## Tips

*   Review and update the form regularly to ensure accuracy and consistency.
*   Use the form to track and manage client and payment information effectively.
*   Consider adding any additional fields or sections as needed to suit your specific requirements.
