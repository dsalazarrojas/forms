# Trauma Exposure And Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trauma Exposure And Impact Survey is a tool designed to help you understand and document your experiences of being exposed to trauma. It's a self-reporting tool, so please complete it on your own and don't fill it out on behalf of someone else.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Use the space provided for each question to write your thoughts, feelings, and experiences. If a question doesn't apply to you, write "Not Applicable" and proceed.
3. For select_one type questions, choose one answer from the options provided.
4. For text type questions, provide as much detail as you need to answer the question.
5. Take your time to complete the form, and don't rush through it.
6. If you need to, you can save your progress and return to it later. Your answers will be saved, so you can pick up where you left off.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Trauma Exposure
* **Trauma Exposure** (`trauma_exposure`, `text`, required: false): This is the opening question of the survey. Please describe your experiences of being exposed to trauma. This could include a single event or ongoing exposure.

### Trauma Exposure Frequency
* **Trauma Exposure Frequency** (`trauma_exposure_frequency`, `number`, required: false): How often have you been exposed to trauma? Please provide a number that best describes your experience.

### Trauma Exposure Duration
* **Trauma Exposure Duration** (`trauma_exposure_duration`, `text`, required: false): How long have you been exposed to trauma? Please describe in as much detail as needed.

### Trauma Exposure Type
* **Trauma Exposure Type** (`trauma_exposure_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Have you been exposed to a single traumatic event, or ongoing trauma? Please choose one from the options provided.

### Trauma Exposure Severity
* **Trauma Exposure Severity** (`trauma_exposure_severity`, `select_one`, required: false): How severe was your experience of trauma? Please choose one from the options provided.

### Trauma Impact
* **Trauma Impact** (`trauma_exposure_impact`, `text`, required: false): How has trauma impacted you? This could include physical, emotional, or psychological effects.

### Trauma Impact Frequency
* **Trauma Impact Frequency** (`trauma_impact_frequency`, `text`, required: false): How often has trauma impacted you? Please describe in as much detail as needed.

### Trauma Impact Duration
* **Trauma Impact Duration** (`trauma_impact_duration`, `text`, required: false): How long has trauma impacted you? Please describe in as much detail as needed.

### Trauma Impact Type
* **Trauma Impact Type** (`trauma_impact_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Has trauma had a physical, emotional, or psychological impact on you? Please choose one from the options provided.

### Trauma Impact Severity
* **Trauma Impact Severity** (`trauma_impact_severity`, `select_one`, required: false): How severe has trauma impacted you? Please choose one from the options provided.

### Trauma Exposure Caregiver
* **Trauma Exposure Caregiver** (`trauma_exposure_caregiver`, `select_one`, required: false): Have you been exposed to trauma through a caregiver? Please choose one from the options provided.

### Trauma Exposure Caregiver Type
* **Trauma Exposure Caregiver Type** (`trauma_exposure_caregiver_type`, `text`, required: false): If you've been exposed to trauma through a caregiver, describe the type of caregiving.

### Trauma Exposure Refusal
* **Trauma Exposure Refusal** (`trauma_exposure_refusal`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If you have refused to be exposed to trauma, please choose the reasons from the options provided.

### Trauma Refusal Exposure Caregiver
* **Trauma Refusal Exposure Caregiver** (`trauma_refusal_exposure_caregiver`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If you have refused to be exposed to trauma through a caregiver, please choose the reasons from the options provided.
