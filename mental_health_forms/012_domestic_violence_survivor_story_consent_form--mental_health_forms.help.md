# Domestic Violence Survivor Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help survivors of domestic violence share their story and receive support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name, date of birth, phone number, address, and email address.
2. Share your domestic violence survivor story in the space provided.
3. Sign the form to confirm your consent to share your story.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survivor Name** (`Survivor Name`, text, required): Please enter your first and last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Please enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Please enter your current address or a safe location where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Please enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Domestic Violence Survivor Story** (`Domestic Violence Survivor Story`, text, required): Please share your story in this space, including any details that you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, text, required): Please sign below to confirm that you have provided your story and consent to share it with us.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as you are comfortable sharing.
* If you are in immediate danger, please call emergency services or a crisis hotline for support.
* If you are not comfortable sharing certain details, you may leave them out or share them separately with a trusted authority figure.
* This form is a secure way to share your story, but we encourage you to take steps to protect your identity if you wish.
