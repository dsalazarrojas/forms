# emotional_distance_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This emotional distance assessment form is designed to collect information on relationships between individuals, focusing on emotional connections and intensity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Respondents will typically start by selecting a partner and describing their relationship with them.
2. They will be asked about their emotional state and how they feel about this partner.
3. The form will guide the user to describe their relationship in detail and answer questions about their frequency and duration of contact with this partner.
4. Repeat the process for each partner, including any other partners listed.
5. At the end, answer additional questions about the last contact with each partner.
6. Finally, review all information provided and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How do you feel about (partner 1/2/3/4/5)
* **How do you feel about (partner 1)**: This field asks how you feel about your partner. Select one or more emotions from the options provided.
* **Partner 1/2/3/4/5**: These fields ask for the name of each partner.
* **Relationship with partner 1/2/3/4/5**: This field is used to select the nature of the relationship with each partner from a predefined list.
* **Describe your partner 1/2/3/4/5**: This field is used to provide a detailed description of your relationship with each partner.
* **Emotional state**: Select one or more emotions that describe your emotional state with this partner.
* **Description of emotional state other**: If you feel an emotion not listed, describe it here.
* **Date last contact**: Provide the date of the last contact with this partner.
* **Time last contact**: Provide the time of the last contact with this partner.
* **Frequency of last contact**: Select how often you last contacted this partner.
* **Length of total relationship**: Enter the length of time you have been in contact with this partner.
* **Partner 1/2/3/4/5 name**: If you have more than two partners, these fields ask for the name of each additional partner.
* **Partner 1/2/3/4/5 relationship**: These fields are used to select the nature of the relationship with each additional partner.
* **Describe your partner 1/2/3/4/5**: These fields are used to provide a detailed description of your relationship with each additional partner.
* **Submit**: Once you've completed all the required fields, click this button to submit your form.

Note: Some fields may appear multiple times depending on the number of partners you've selected, but the process remains the same. Make sure to review all sections thoroughly and answer all questions honestly.
