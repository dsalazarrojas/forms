# Mental Health Treatment Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Treatment Time Off Form is a required form for employees to request mental health treatment time off. This form helps the HR department track and process your time off requests, ensuring that your needs are met and that you can focus on your mental well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your request is processed quickly and efficiently.
2. Enter your employee name and ID in the respective fields.
3. Choose the type of treatment you are undergoing (inpatient, outpatient, or virtual).
4. Select the start and end dates for your time off.
5. Choose the start and end times for your time off (if applicable).
6. Clearly state your reason for absence (e.g., scheduled medical appointment, therapy session).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name (<code>employee_name</code>)
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employment record.
### Employee ID (<code>employee_id</code>)
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID number.
### Treatment Type (<code>treatment_type</code>)
* **Treatment Type**: Select the type of treatment you are undergoing, such as inpatient, outpatient, or virtual.
### Start Date (<code>start_date</code>)
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your treatment, if applicable.
### Start Time (<code>start_time</code>)
* **Start Time**: Enter the time you plan to start your treatment, if applicable.
### End Date (<code>end_date</code>)
* **End Date**: Enter the date you plan to end your treatment.
### End Time (<code>end_time</code>)
* **End Time**: Enter the time you plan to end your treatment, if applicable.
### Reason for Absence (<code>reason_for_absence</code>)
* **Reason for Absence**: Clearly state your reason for requesting time off, such as a scheduled medical appointment or therapy session.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
* This form is confidential and will be kept internal use only.
