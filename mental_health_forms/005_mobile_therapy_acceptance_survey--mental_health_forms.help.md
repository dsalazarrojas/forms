# Mobile Therapy Acceptance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mobile Therapy Acceptance Survey is designed to gather information about your opinions and experiences with mobile therapy apps. Your input will help us understand how people interact with these apps and inform the development of better services. Please answer the questions honestly, and if you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as honestly as possible, and remember that all answers are anonymous.
2. Select your preferred answer option for each question from the available choices.
3. If a question doesn't apply to you, feel free to mark the "None of the above" or "Not Applicable" option.
4. Take your time to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you use mobile therapy apps?**
	+ This question is about your typical usage frequency of mobile therapy apps.
	+ Please select the option that best describes how often you use these apps.
* **How likely are you to recommend mobile therapy apps?**
	+ This question is about your willingness to recommend mobile therapy apps to others.
	+ Please choose the option that best represents your confidence in recommending these apps.
* **How do you usually access therapy services? (Select all that apply)**
	+ This question is about your preferred method of accessing therapy services.
	+ Select all the options that apply to you, such as mobile apps, in-person therapy, or both.
* **How likely are you to recommend mobile therapy apps?**
	+ This question is identical to the previous one, but with a slightly different phrasing.
	+ Please choose the option that best represents your willingness to recommend these apps.
