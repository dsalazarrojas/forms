# mental_health_clinic_patient_intake_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by mental health clinics to gather essential patient information for diagnosis and treatment planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure accuracy, please fill out the form completely and submit it to the designated authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (patient_info)** (`intake_info`, Text, Optional)
	+ This is the first section of the form where you'll be asked to provide your basic information.
* **Mental Health Status (mental_health_status)** (`mental_health_status`, Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Please select any mental health status that applies to you, using the options provided.
* **Primary Diagnosis (primary_diagnosis)** (`primary_diagnosis`, Text, Optional)
	+ Describe your primary mental health diagnosis in this field.
* **Medical History (medical_history)** (`medical_history`, Text, Optional)
	+ Share any relevant medical history or concerns you have.
* **Social Support (social_support)** (`social_support`, Text, Optional)
	+ If you have any social support systems or concerns, please list them here.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)** (`contact_info`, Email, Optional)
	+ Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Ensure you complete the form according to your actual mental health status.
- If you have any concerns or questions, consult with a licensed mental health professional before submitting the form.
- If you need help, contact: [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx)
