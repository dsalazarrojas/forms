# therapy_walk_in_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The therapy walk-in registration form is designed to collect essential information from clients who are registering for therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information, including their name, contact details, and any relevant medical conditions.
2. Provide your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.
3. If applicable, provide the emergency contact person's information.
4. Check the medical conditions that apply to you.
5. Sign the consent and therapy agreement forms.
6. Fill in your billing information, including your insurance details or payment method.
7. Finally, sign and date the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (Page 1)** (`client_info`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name as you wish to be addressed.
* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your address, phone number, and email so we can contact you effectively.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 1)** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
* **Medical Conditions (Page 2)** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions that may be relevant to your therapy sessions.
* **Consent (Page 2)** (`consent`, `note`, optional): Please indicate your consent for the therapy sessions by checking 'True'.
* **Medical History (Page 2)** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant medical history that may impact your therapy sessions.
* **Therapy Agreement (Page 3)** (`therapy_agreement`, `select_one`, optional): Please agree or disagree to the therapy terms by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Emergency Agreements (Page 3)** (`emergency_agreement`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please specify if you have any emergency agreements or not by selecting 'True', 'False', or 'Not Disclosed'.
* **Billing Information (Page 3)** (`billing_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your insurance details or payment method for billing purposes.
* **Signature (Page 4)** (`signature`, `text`, optional): Sign and date the form to complete the registration process.
* **Date (Page 4)** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of completion.

Note: Some fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information is essential for effective therapy care. If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to the therapist or support staff for assistance.
