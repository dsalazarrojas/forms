# Chronic Illness Peer Support Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals register for a Chronic Illness Peer Support program. It collects contact information, health context, and preferences for peer matching and support. The data collected will be used to create a safe and supportive environment for participants to share experiences, emotions, and coping strategies related to their chronic illness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant contact information, including your full name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select your preferred method of contact (email, phone call, or text message).
3. Provide information about your primary chronic condition(s) and years since diagnosis (if applicable).
4. Indicate your support goals and preferred meeting format (in-person group, virtual video call, one-on-one peer match, or text-based online chat).
5. Select your typical availability for meetings (weekday mornings, afternoon, evenings, weekend mornings, or afternoons).
6. If you have prior experience in a support group, indicate whether it was facilitated or informal.
7. Express your interest in becoming a peer mentor, usually requiring 2+ years of living with the condition.
8. Provide any specific accessibility needs you require for the support group meetings.
9. Agree to maintain the confidentiality of all group participants.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Full Name
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
*   Type: Text input field
*   Required: Yes

### 2. Primary Phone Number
* **Primary Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number.
*   Type: Text input field
*   Required: Yes

### 3. Email Address
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
*   Type: Email input field
*   Required: Yes

### 4. Preferred Method of Contact
* **Preferred Method of Contact**: Select your preferred way to receive contact from the peer support team (Email, Phone Call, Text Message).
*   Type: Select one from a dropdown list
*   Required: Yes

### 5. Health Context (Optional)
*   **Health Context**: Provide a brief description of your chronic condition(s) and any relevant health context (if applicable).
*   Type: Text input field
*   Required: No

### 6. Years Since Diagnosis
*   **Years Since Diagnosis**: If applicable, enter the number of years since your diagnosis.
*   Type: Number input field
*   Required: No

### 7. Support Goals
*   **What do you hope to gain from peer support?**: Select all that apply (Emotional Support, Practical Coping Strategies, Social Connection, Information Sharing, Reduced Isolation).
*   Type: Multiple selection from a dropdown list
*   Required: Yes

### 8. Preferred Meeting Format
*   **Preferred Meeting Format**: Select your preferred meeting format (In-Person Group, Virtual Video Call, One-on-One Peer Match, Text-Based Online Chat).
*   Type: Select one from a dropdown list
*   Required: Yes

### 9. Generally Available for Meetings
*   **Generally Available for Meetings**: Select your typical availability for meetings (Weekday Mornings, Weekday Afternoons, Weekday Evenings, Weekend Mornings, Weekend Afternoons).
*   Type: Multiple selection from a dropdown list
*   Required: Yes

### 10. Have you participated in a Support Group Before?
*   **Have you participated in a support group before?**: If you have prior experience, select Yes - Facilitated or Yes - Informal.
*   Type: Select one from a dropdown list
*   Required: Yes

### 11. Interested in Being a Peer Mentor?
*   **Are you interested in becoming a Peer Mentor?**: Select Yes (usually requires 2+ years of living with the condition) if you're interested.
*   Type: Select one from a dropdown list
*   Required: Yes

### 12. Specific Accessibility Needs
*   **Specific Accessibility Requirements**: Enter any necessary accommodations or needs you require for the support group meetings (e.g., closed captioning, wheelchair access, low-light environment).
*   Type: Text input field
*   Required: No

### 13. Confidentiality Agreement
*   **Confidentiality Agreement**: Select I Agree to maintain the confidentiality of all group participants.
*   Type: Select one from a dropdown list
*   Required: Yes
