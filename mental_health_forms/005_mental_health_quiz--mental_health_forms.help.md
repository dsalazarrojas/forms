# Mental Health Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Quiz is designed to help users understand their mental health concerns and provide a platform for them to express their feelings and thoughts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Answer the questions honestly**: Be truthful and honest in your responses to the questions.
2. **Select your concerns**: Choose the options that best describe your concerns from the select_one field.
3. **Choose your answers**: Select all the options that apply to you from the select_multiple field.
4. **Enter your thoughts**: Write down your thoughts and feelings in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Mental Health Quiz Questions**:
	* Label: Mental Health Quiz Questions (Select One)
	* Description: This section allows you to choose one option that best describes your mental health concerns. Please select the option that applies to you.
- **Mental Health Concerns**:
	* Label: Mental Health Concerns
	* Description: This is a text field where you can describe your mental health concerns in as much detail as possible. Please be honest and clear in your description.
- **Mental Health Form**:
	* Label: Mental Health Form
	* Description: This is a text field where you can enter any additional thoughts or feelings you have about your mental health. Please write down anything that comes to mind and be as detailed as possible.
- **Mental Health Quiz**:
	* Label: Mental Health Quiz
	* Description: This is a text field where you can enter your name or any other identifying information if you want to. It's optional and for your reference only.
- **Mental Health Concerns 2**:
	* Label: Mental Health Concerns 2
	* Description: This is another select_one field where you can choose one option from the list that best describes another aspect of your mental health concerns. Please select only one option that applies to you.
- **No fields**:
	* Label: (None)
	* Description: This is the beginning of the form, just to confirm that you are starting the quiz. Please answer the questions and proceed from there.

Note: The form has multiple fields with the same label, which might cause confusion. To resolve this, we'll focus on the first occurrence of each label in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
