# mental_health_leave_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report a mental health leave. A mental health leave is a type of leave that allows employees to take time off from work due to mental health reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information.
2. Make sure to include the correct dates for the start and end of your leave.
3. Provide a brief reason for your leave in the "reason" field.
4. Enter your employee information, including your name and email address.
5. Enter your manager's information, including their name and email address.
6. If you have a doctor's contact information, enter it in the "doctor_name", "doctor_license", and "doctor_phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **request_reason** (`request_reason`, text, optional): This field is for entering a brief reason for your mental health leave.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, date, optional): This is the date you would like to start your mental health leave. If you are unsure, you can enter the current date.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, date, optional): This is the date you would like to end your mental health leave.
* **reason** (`reason`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief reason for your mental health leave. Be as specific as possible so that your manager can understand your needs.
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
* **employee_email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager, as it appears on their business card.
* **manager_email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **doctor_name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your doctor or mental health professional.
* **doctor_license** (`doctor_license`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's professional license number or other relevant identification.
* **doctor_phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form correctly and accurately.
* Be as specific as possible in the "reason" field to help your manager understand your needs.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your HR department for assistance.
