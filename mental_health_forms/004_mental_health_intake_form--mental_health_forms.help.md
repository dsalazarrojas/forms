# Mental Health Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Intake Form is a collection of information used by healthcare professionals to assess and understand a patient's mental health history, current status, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Medical History**: This section is where you should provide a detailed description of your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, and previous treatments. Please be as comprehensive as possible and include any relevant information that may be helpful for the healthcare professional to understand your mental health situation.

2. **Supporting Documents**: This section allows you to upload any relevant supporting documents such as ID cards, disability IDs, or proof of insurance. Please ensure that these documents are accurate and up-to-date.

3. **Fee Payments**: In this section, you can provide information about any previous payments or fees associated with your mental health care, including dates and amounts.

4. **Date**: Please select the date of your most recent mental health appointment or assessment. Use the format "yyyy-mm-dd" (e.g., 2022-02-15) for the date.

5. **Time**: Select the time of your most recent mental health appointment or assessment in the format "hh:mm AM/PM" (e.g., 10:00 AM).

6. **Patient Data**: In this section, please provide any additional notes or information that you feel may be relevant to your mental health care. Please be as comprehensive as possible.
