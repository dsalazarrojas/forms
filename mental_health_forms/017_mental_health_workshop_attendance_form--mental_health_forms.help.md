# Mental Health Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mental Health Workshop Attendance Form is designed to collect information from participants about their attendance at a mental health workshop. This form will be used to track attendance, gather feedback, and facilitate communication with participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the workshop being attended.
2. Provide your name as a participant.
3. Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
4. Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
5. Select the date of the workshop you attended.
6. Choose the time of the workshop you attended.
7. Add any additional notes about your experience with the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the workshop being attended. This will help us identify the specific workshop you participated in.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Your name as a participant. Please ensure that it accurately reflects your real name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about the workshop and any related matters.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication purposes. We may use this to send you reminders or updates.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you attended the workshop. This helps us track the attendance dates.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required: false): Select the time you attended the workshop. This aids in scheduling and planning.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or observations about your experience with the workshop. This helps us understand your feedback and improve future workshops.
