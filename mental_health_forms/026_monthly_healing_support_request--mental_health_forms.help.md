# Monthly Healing Support Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your monthly healing support requests. It will help the support team provide personalized support to you, and ensure efficient support delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. This will help the support team understand your needs and provide better support.
2. Review each field carefully and make sure you've entered the correct information.
3. If you're unsure about any field, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Please enter your name and email or phone number so we can contact you about your support request.
* **Support Preferences** (`support_preferences`, text, optional): Describe your healing support needs and preferences. This will help us understand your requirements better.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): Check the consent box to allow us to contact you about your healing support request.
* **How do you want to be contacted?** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Other, None).
* **If Other, Please Specify** (`contact_method_other`, text, optional): If you chose "Other", please specify how you want to be contacted (e.g. messaging app, phone call, etc.).
