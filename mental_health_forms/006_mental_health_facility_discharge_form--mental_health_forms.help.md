# mental health facility discharge form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health facility discharge form is used to document the discharge of a patient from a mental health facility, ensuring accurate and comprehensive information is recorded about the patient's discharge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Facility Name** in the first field.
2. Enter the **Patient Name** in the second field.
3. Enter the **Date of Discharge** in the third field (in date format, e.g., 2022-07-25).
4. Enter the **Time of Discharge** in the fourth field (in 24-hour format, e.g., 14:30).
5. Enter the **Medical Record Number** in the fifth field.
6. Select the **Discharge Status** from the dropdown menu (Discharged, Transferred, or Deceased).
7. Enter the **Discharge Diagnosis** in the seventh field.
8. Enter any **Follow-up Care** instructions for the patient in the eighth field.
9. Select the **Next Care Setting** from the dropdown menu (Home, Outpatient, or Inpatient).
10. Enter the name of the **Next Provider** responsible for the patient's care in the tenth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the facility where the patient was treated.
* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the patient being discharged.
* **Date of Discharge** (date, required): Enter the date of the patient's discharge (e.g., 2022-07-25).
* **Time of Discharge** (time, required): Enter the time of the patient's discharge (e.g., 14:30).
* **Medical Record Number** (text, required): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Discharge Status** (select_one, optional): Select the status of the patient's discharge (Discharged, Transferred, or Deceased).
* **Discharge Diagnosis** (text, optional): Enter any relevant medical diagnosis related to the patient's discharge.
* **Follow-up Care** (text, optional): Enter any follow-up care instructions for the patient.
* **Next Care Setting** (select_multiple, optional): Select the next care setting for the patient (Home, Outpatient, or Inpatient).
* **Next Provider** (text, optional): Enter the name of the next provider responsible for the patient's care.

## Tips

* Be accurate and clear when filling out the form to ensure accurate records.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the facility staff or supervisor.
