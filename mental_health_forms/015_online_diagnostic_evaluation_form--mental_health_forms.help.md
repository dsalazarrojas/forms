# online_diagnostic_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information for an online diagnostic evaluation, covering various aspects of a person's health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Provide clear and concise responses to each field.
3. Be as detailed as needed when describing your medical history, mental health, and other sensitive topics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`title`, `text`, **optional**): Please provide any general information about yourself, such as name, date of birth, or contact details.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`title`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your contact information, including address, phone number, and email. This will help us reach you for follow-up questions or further evaluations.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`title`, `text`, **optional**): Describe any significant medical events, allergies, or current medical conditions that may affect your health.

### Mental Health History
* **Mental Health History** (`title`, `text`, **optional**): Share any mental health concerns, therapy experiences, or relevant emotional events that may be important for your evaluation.

### Social History
* **Social History** (`title`, `text`, **optional**): Describe your social situation, including family dynamics, work environment, or any social challenges you're facing.

### Risk Factors
* **Risk Factors** (`title`, `text`, **optional**): List any factors that may affect your health, such as smoking, excessive exercise, or substance use.

### Family History
* **Family History** (`title`, `text`, **optional**): Describe any significant family medical or mental health concerns.

### Substance Use
* **Substance Use** (`title`, `text`, **optional**): Answer truthfully about any substance use, including nicotine, alcohol, or other substances.

### Medications
* **Medications** (`title`, `text`, **optional**): List any prescribed or non-prescribed medications you're currently taking or have taken in the past.

## Tips
- Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness.
- Be thorough but concise in your answers.
- If you need clarification on any question, do not hesitate to reach out for help.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
