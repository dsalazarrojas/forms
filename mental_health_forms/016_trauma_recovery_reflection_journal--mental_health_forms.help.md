# Trauma Recovery Reflection Journal - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trauma Recovery Reflection Journal is a form designed to help individuals reflect on their trauma recovery journey. By filling out this form, users can monitor their mental state, identify coping skills they use, and write down their reflections on their progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on each field to enter your information.
2. Fill out the form with your thoughts and feelings related to your trauma recovery.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and honest information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trauma Recovery Date** (`trauma_recovery_date`, date, required/optional): This field is for selecting a specific date that marks a significant event in your trauma recovery journey. You can choose any date that is relevant to your recovery.
* **Mental State** (`mental_state`, text, required/optional): Describe your current mental state. This is a private space for you to share your thoughts, feelings, and emotions related to your mental health.
* **Coping Skills Used** (`coping_skills_used`, text, required/optional): In this field, list the coping skills you have used to manage your trauma symptoms. These can be techniques, activities, or strategies that help you cope with stress or difficult emotions.
* **Reflections** (`reflections`, text, required/optional): Write down your thoughts, feelings, and insights about your trauma recovery journey. This is a space for you to reflect on your progress, successes, and challenges.

## Tips
* Be honest and authentic in your responses, as this will help you process your trauma recovery better.
* Take your time to fill out the form, and don't hesitate to come back to it later if needed.
* Remember that this form is a personal and private space for you to reflect on your journey; don't worry about grammar or formatting errors, just focus on expressing yourself.
