# mental_health_access_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "mental_health_access_records_release_form" is a form used to collect and process requests for access to mental health records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name and date of birth.
2. Provide the patient's contact information.
3. Enter the name and contact information of the person authorized to access the records.
4. Enter the reason for the request.
5. Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms of the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Contact Information** (`patient_contact_info`, text, required): Provide the patient's contact information.
* **Authorized Person** (`authorized_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person authorized to access the patient's records.
* **Authorized Person Contact Information** (`authorized_person_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the authorized person.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider or organization.
* **Reason For Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required): Enter the reason for the request.
* **Sign Here** (`signature`, note, required): Sign here to acknowledge that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms of the request.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date the request was signed.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and that all required fields are marked as such.
- The "Sign Here" field is where the authorized person will physically sign the request.
- Ensure that the patient has given their consent for the information to be shared before filling out this form.
- Double-check that all details are correct before submitting the form.
