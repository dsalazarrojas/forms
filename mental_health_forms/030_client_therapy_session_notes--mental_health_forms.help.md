# Client Therapy Session Notes - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and document information about client therapy sessions. It helps mental health professionals track client progress, maintain accurate records, and ensure continuity of care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the client's personal information (Client Name, Client ID Number, Session Date, Session Time, Session Duration).
2. Choose the session format (In-Person, Video Therapy, Phone Call, or Other).
3. Describe the client's mood and emotional state (Mood and Affect).
4. List the presenting issues or symptoms (Presenting Issues or Symptoms).
5. Identify recent stressors or events (Recent Events).
6. Record the topics discussed during the session (Topics Discussed).
7. Specify the therapeutic interventions or techniques used (Therapeutic Interventions or Techniques Used).
8. Provide details about how these techniques were applied (Intervention Details).
9. Note the client's response to the interventions (Client Response to Interventions).
10. Document any insights or breakthroughs during the session (Insights or Breakthroughs During Session).
11. Assess the client's progress toward treatment goals (Progress Toward Treatment Goals).
12. Evaluate changes in symptoms or concerns (Changes in Symptoms or Concerns).
13. Conduct a safety assessment (Safety Assessment).
14. Record the client's current coping skills (Client's Current Coping Skills).
15. Assign homework or tasks for the client to complete between sessions (Homework or Assignments Given).
16. Review previous homework completion (Previous Homework Completion).
17. Assess the client's capacity to complete homework (Client's Capacity to Complete Homework).
18. Plan for the next session (Plan for Next Session).
19. Set the next session date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Session Information -- (session_info)**: A space to note any additional information about the session.
* **Client Name (client_name)**: Enter the client's full name.
* **Client ID Number (client_id)**: Enter the client's internal reference number.
* **Therapist/Counselor Name (therapist_name)**: Enter the name of the mental health professional who conducted the session.
* **Session Date (session_date)**: Enter the date of the session.
* **Session Time (session_time)**: Enter the start time of the session.
* **Session Duration (session_duration)**: Enter the duration of the session in minutes.
* **Session Format (session_format)**: Choose the format of the session (In-Person, Video Therapy, Phone Call, or Other).
* **-- Presenting Concerns -- (presenting_concerns)**: A space to note any additional information about the client's concerns.
* **Chief Complaint or Reason for Visit (chief_complaint)**: Describe what brought the client in for the session.
* **Mood and Affect (mood_and_affect)**: Describe the client's observable emotional state.
* **Presenting Issues or Symptoms (presenting_issues)**: List the client's current problems or concerns.
* **Recent Stressors or Events (recent_events)**: Describe what has been happening in the client's life.
* **-- Session Content and Interventions -- (session_content)**: A space to document session content.
* **Topics Discussed (topics_discussed)**: List the topics discussed during the session.
* **Therapeutic Interventions or Techniques Used (interventions_used)**: Specify the techniques used during the session.
* **Intervention Details (intervention_details)**: Describe how the techniques were applied.
* **Client Response to Interventions (client_response)**: Describe the client's response to the interventions.
* **Insights or Breakthroughs During Session (insights_breakthroughs)**: Note any important realizations or shifts during the session.
* **Progress Toward Treatment Goals (goals_progress)**: Assess the client's progress toward treatment goals.
* **Changes in Symptoms or Concerns (symptom_changes)**: Evaluate changes in symptoms or concerns.
* **Safety Assessment (safety_assessment)**: Conduct a safety assessment.
* **Client's Current Coping Skills (coping_skills)**: Describe the client's current coping skills.
* **-- Homework and Follow-up -- (homework_section)**: A space to document homework or tasks assigned.
* **Homework or Assignments Given (homework_assigned)**: List the assigned homework or tasks.
* **Previous Homework Completion (prev_homework_completion)**: Review previous homework completion.
* **Client's Capacity to Complete Homework (client_capacity)**: Assess the client's capacity to complete homework.
* **Plan for Next Session (next_session_plan)**: Plan for the next session.
* **Next Session Date (next_session_date)**: Set the next session date.
