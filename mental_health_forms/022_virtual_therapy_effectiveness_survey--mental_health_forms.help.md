# Virtual Therapy Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Therapy Effectiveness Survey is designed to gather your thoughts, feelings, and experiences with the virtual therapy program. Your feedback will help us understand what works well and areas for improvement, enabling us to provide better support and services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read each field carefully and answer honestly based on your experiences.
2.  Select your responses from the provided options for fields that offer them.
3.  For text fields, provide detailed and specific comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Virtual Therapy Effectiveness Survey** (`survey_start`): This is a note field to introduce you to the survey and its purpose. Please read it carefully to understand what's being asked.
* **How did you feel during your therapy sessions?** (`introduction`): Select the option that best describes your emotional state during your therapy sessions. This helps us understand the emotional support you received.
* **How satisfied are you with your therapy?** (`satisfaction`): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the therapy sessions, using the provided number options. This feedback will help us assess the effectiveness of our sessions.
* **How engaged did you feel in therapy?** (`engagement`): Choose how engaged you felt during the sessions, using the number options provided. This helps us understand the level of participation and interest you had in the therapy process.
* **How supportive did you feel your therapist?** (`supportiveness`): Select how supportive you felt during your therapy sessions, using the provided options. This feedback will help us gauge the supportiveness of our therapists.
* **How clear were the explanations about your therapy?** (`communication`): Choose how clear you found the explanations about your therapy, using the multiple options provided. This helps us assess the communication quality of our sessions.
* **How much time did you spend on therapy?** (`time_commitment`): Select the time commitment you had for therapy, using the multiple options provided. This feedback will help us understand the time demands of our sessions.
* **How much progress did you feel you made in therapy?** (`progress`): Indicate your perceived progress in therapy, using the provided options. This helps us understand the effectiveness of our sessions.
* **What are your recommendations for this Virtual Therapy Program?** (`recommendations`): Provide detailed and specific comments or suggestions for improving the program.
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions for this Virtual Therapy Program?** (`comments`): Share any final thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have for future improvements.

By following this guide, you'll be able to complete the Virtual Therapy Effectiveness Survey accurately and provide valuable feedback to help us improve the program. Thank you for taking the time to participate!
