# Trauma And Oppression Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trauma And Oppression Survey is designed to gather information about an individual's experiences with trauma and oppression. This information is used to help identify individuals who may be in need of support or services related to trauma and oppression.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select all that apply for multiple choice questions.
3. Enter your answer in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`intro`, `note`): This is a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Trauma History** (`trauma_history`, `select_multiple`): Select all the types of trauma you have experienced.
	+ Physical trauma
	+ Emotional trauma
	+ Sexual trauma
	+ Psychological trauma
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Trauma Description** (`trauma_description`, `text`): Describe your trauma experiences.
* **Oppression History** (`oppression_history`, `select_one`): Select the type of oppression you have experienced.
	+ Racial
	+ Socioeconomic
	+ Sexual
	+ Physical
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Oppression Description** (`oppression_description`, `text`): Describe your oppression experiences.
* **Support Systems** (`support_systems`, `select_multiple`): Select all the support systems you have in place.
	+ Family
	+ Friends
	+ Mental health professionals
	+ Self-help groups
	+ Online support groups
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Feelings/Emotions** (`feelings_emotions`, `select_one`): Select the emotions you are experiencing.
	+ Sadness
	+ Anger
	+ Fear
	+ Anxiety
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Coping Mechanisms** (`coping_mechanisms`, `select_multiple`): Select all the coping mechanisms you use.
	+ Self-care
	+ Exercise
	+ Talking to someone
	+ Mindfulness
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Help Services** (`help_services`, `select_one`): Select the services you need help with.
	+ Mental health professionals
	+ Self-help groups
	+ Online support groups
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Trauma/Oppression Connection** (`trauma_oppression_connection`, `select_one`): Select your understanding of the relationship between trauma and oppression.
	+ Trauma and oppression are connected
	+ Trauma and oppression are separate
* **Help Needed** (`help_needed`, `select_multiple`): Select the services you need help with.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Demographics** (`demographics`, `select_one`): Select your demographics.
	+ Male
	+ Female
* **Age** (`age`, `number`): Enter your age.
* **Country of Origin** (`country_of_origin`, `text`): Enter your country of origin.
* **Language Spoken** (`language_spoken`, `text`): Enter the language you speak.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, `text`): Enter your ethnicity.
* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, `select_one`): Select your relationship status.
	+ Single
	+ In a relationship
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`): Select your education level.
	+ High school
	+ Some college
	+ College degree
	+ Post-graduate degree
* **Household Income** (`household_income`, `number`): Enter your household income.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`): Select your employment status.
	+ Employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Pregnant/Parenting
	+ Student
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, `select_one`): Select whether you have health insurance.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Mental Health Services** (`mental_health_services`, `select_one`): Select whether you have mental health services.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Trauma Services** (`trauma_services`, `select_one`): Select whether you have trauma services.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Oppression Services** (`oppression_services`, `select_one`): Select whether you have oppression services.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Other Trauma** (`other_trauma`, `text`): Describe any other traumatic experiences.
