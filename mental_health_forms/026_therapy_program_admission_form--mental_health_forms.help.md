# Therapy Program Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Therapy Program Admission Form is a crucial document that enables patients and therapists to collaborate and agree on the terms of the therapy program. It ensures that both parties understand and consent to the terms of the program, which includes the practice name, patient information, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill in your personal information in the first section, including your name, email, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Enter the practice information in the second section, including the practice name, address, and phone number.
3. Select the program start and end dates, as well as the date for the form submission.
4. Choose the program name and select the schedule for the therapy sessions.
5. Review and sign the patient consent and agreement sections, ensuring you understand the terms and conditions of the program.
6. Enter your email address and choose whether you consent to receive emails from the practice.
7. Finally, submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, text): Enter the name of the practice or clinic.
* **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text): Enter your first name.
* **Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date): Select your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date): Select the start date of the program.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date): Select the end date of the program.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text): Select the name of the program you are applying for.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text): Enter the name of the therapist you will be working with.
* **Therapist Phone** (`therapist_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the therapist.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text): Enter your address.
* **Practice Address** (`practice_address`, text): Enter the address of the practice.
* **Practice Phone** (`practice_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the practice.
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, select_one): Select one of the options to confirm your agreement to the terms of the program.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple): Select one of the options to confirm your understanding of the program terms.
* **Note** (`note`, note): Enter any additional comments or notes about your experience with the practice.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one): Select "Submit" to finalize the form.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Select the date you are submitting the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time): Select the time you are submitting the form.
* **Therapist Agreement** (`therapist_agreement`, select_multiple): Select one of the options to confirm your understanding of the program terms.
* **Practice Agreement** (`practice_agreement`, select_multiple): Select one of the options to confirm your understanding of the program terms.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, select_multiple): Select one of the options to confirm your availability for the therapy sessions.
* **Email Consent** (`email_consent`, select_multiple): Select one of the options to confirm your consent to receive emails from the practice.
* **Email Agreement** (`email_agreement`, text): Enter your email address to confirm your agreement to receive emails from the practice.

## Tips

* Ensure you carefully review and understand each field before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field based on your preferences and needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the practice for assistance.
