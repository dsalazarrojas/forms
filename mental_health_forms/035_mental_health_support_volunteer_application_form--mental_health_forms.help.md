# mental_health_support_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in volunteering their time and skills to support mental health services. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the volunteer's background, availability, and interests to determine the best fit for their role within our organization. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic information on the "Volunteer Info" page.
2. Enter your contact details on the "Contact Details" page.
3. Share your medical history with us on the "Medical History" page.
4. If applicable, provide emergency contact information on the "Emergency Contact Info" page.
5. Describe your commitment to volunteering on the "Volunteer Commitment" page.
6. Let us know about your availability on the "Volunteer Availability" page.
7. Select the mental health services you are interested in or have experience with on the "Mental Health Services" page.
8. Choose your preferred language on the "Preferred Language" page.
9. If you have a reference, enter their email address on the "Volunteer Reference" page.
10. Enter your phone number on the "Phone Number" page.
11. Finally, enter your email address on the "Email" page.
12. Share your volunteer interest on the "Volunteer Interest" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Info (1)**: Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Details (2)**: Provide your contact details, including your name, address, and any other contact information you'd like to share.
* **Medical History (3)**: Share any medical history or health conditions that may impact your volunteering activities.
* **Emergency Contact Info (4)**: If applicable, provide the name and contact information of a family member or friend who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Volunteer Commitment (5)**: Describe your commitment to volunteering with our organization, including your availability and any relevant experience.
* **Volunteer Availability (6)**: Let us know about your availability to volunteer, including days and hours you can commit to.
* **Mental Health Services (7)**: Select the mental health services you are interested in or have experience with (e.g., therapy, support groups, counseling, etc.).
* **Preferred Language (8)**: Choose the language you prefer to communicate with us (e.g., English, Spanish, French, Other).
* **Volunteer Reference (9)**: If you have a reference who can vouch for your volunteering experience, enter their email address.
* **Phone Number (10)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email (11)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Volunteer Interest (12)**: Share your interests and motivations for volunteering with our organization.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will review your application and get back to you soon.
