# share_your_recovery_journey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "share_your_recovery_journey" form is designed to be a personal and reflective exercise to help individuals share their recovery experiences, insights, and feelings related to overcoming mental health challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Form Identifier (page 1)
2. Share a short recovery story (page 2)
3. Reflect on what helped you recover (page 3)
4. Specify if you have permission to share your recovery story (page 4)
5. Provide the date of your recovery (page 5)
6. Share the location of your recovery (page 6)
7. Specify the support systems you had during recovery (page 7)
8. Enter your email address (page 8)
9. Enter your phone number (page 9)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Identifier** (page 1):
	+ A unique identifier for your recovery story.
	+ Enter a number that you can use to reference your story in the future.
* **Recovery Story** (page 2):
	+ Share a short story about your recovery journey.
	+ Be as detailed as you can, but keep it concise.
* **Recovery Insights** (page 3):
	+ Reflect on what helped you recover.
	+ Think about the people, places, or things that contributed to your recovery.
* **Permission to Share** (page 4):
	+ Specify if you have permission to share your recovery story.
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have given permission to share.
* **Date of Recovery** (page 5):
	+ Enter the date when you started recovering.
	+ Use the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Location of Recovery** (page 6):
	+ Share the location where you were recovering.
	+ This can be a physical location, a community, or a support group.
* **Support Systems** (page 7):
	+ Specify the support systems you had during recovery.
	+ Choose from options like Social Media, Support Groups, or Therapist.
* **Email** (page 8):
	+ Enter your email address.
	+ We will use this to contact you about your recovery story.
* **Phone** (page 9):
	+ Enter your phone number.
	+ We will use this to contact you about your recovery story.

## Tips
- Be honest and authentic in your sharing.
- Keep your answers concise and clear.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
