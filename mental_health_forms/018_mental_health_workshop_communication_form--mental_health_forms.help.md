# mental_health_workshop_communication_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from workshop participants to facilitate communication and ensure everyone's needs and preferences are met during the workshop. It is crucial to fill this form accurately to ensure a productive and comfortable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Choose relevant options from the dropdown menus that best represent your preferences and goals.
3. Enter your participant details, such as email and phone number, if applicable.
4. Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the workshop organizers.
