# Introduction To Recovery Programs - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in recovery programs related to mental health, addiction, and substance use. This information will help our team assess your readiness and needs for recovery programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Select only the options that apply to you.
3. If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Interested In Recovery** (`Interested In Recovery`, select multiple, optional): Select all the recovery programs that interest you (e.g., Alcohol Recovery, Drug Recovery, Mental Health Recovery, Addiction Recovery).
* **Support System** (`Support System`, select multiple, optional): Select all the support systems you have (e.g., Family, Friends, Recovery Centers, Counselors, Online Communities).
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your recovery history or needs.
* **Date Of Last Use** (`Date Of Last Use`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of your last use of a recovery program or resource.
* **Time Of Last Use** (`Time Of Last Use`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time of your last use of a recovery program or resource.
* **Location Of Last Use** (`Location Of Last Use`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the location of your last use of a recovery program or resource.
* **Referral Source** (`Referral Source`, select one, optional): Select how you found out about our recovery programs (e.g., Online Search, Social Media, Word of Mouth, Healthcare Provider, Recovery Center).
* **Referral Source Other** (`Referral Source Other`, text, optional): If the referral source is not listed, please specify.
* **Has Dependable Support** (`Has Dependable Support`, select one, optional): Indicate if you have dependable support for recovery (e.g., No, Yes).
* **How Often Do You Use Recovery Resources** (`How Often Do You Use Recovery Resources`, select one, optional): Select how often you use recovery resources (e.g., Never, Rarely, Occasionally, Regularly, Almost Daily).
* **What Motivate To Change** (`What Motivate To Change`, text, optional): Enter what motivates you to change or seek recovery.
* **What Motivate To Remain Unchanged** (`What Motivate To Remain Unchanged`, text, optional): Enter what motivates you to remain unchanged.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about your recovery history or needs.
* **Interested In Other Programs** (`Interested In Other Programs`, select multiple, optional): Select if you are interested in other recovery programs not listed (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Interested In Other Programs Other** (`Interested In Other Programs Other`, text, optional): If you are interested in other programs not listed, please specify.
* **Has Other Family Member In Recovery** (`Has Other Family Member In Recovery`, select one, optional): Indicate if you have other family members in recovery (e.g., No, Yes).
* **Has Other Family Member In Recovery Other** (`Has Other Family Member In Recovery Other`, text, optional): If you have other family members in recovery, please specify.
* **Has Other Friend Or Family Member In Recovery** (`Has Other Friend Or Family Member In Recovery`, select one, optional): Indicate if you have other friends or family members in recovery (e.g., No, Yes).
* **Has Other Friend Or Family Member In Recovery Other** (`Has Other Friend Or Family Member In Recovery Other`, text, optional): If you have other friends or family members in recovery, please specify.
* **Has Other Colleague In Recovery** (`Has Other Colleague In Recovery`, select one, optional): Indicate if you have other colleagues in recovery (e.g., No, Yes).
* **Has Other Colleague In Recovery Other** (`Has Other Colleague In Recovery Other`, text, optional): If you have other colleagues in recovery, please specify.
* **Has Other Acquaintance In Recovery** (`Has Other Acquaintance In Recovery`, select one, optional): Indicate if you have other acquaintances in recovery (e.g., No, Yes).
* **Has Other Acquaintance In Recovery Other** (`Has Other Acquaintance In Recovery Other`, text, optional): If you have other acquaintances in recovery, please specify.

## Tips
* Be honest and complete when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask a member of our support team for guidance.
* This form will be reviewed confidentially and used to assess your readiness and needs for recovery programs.
