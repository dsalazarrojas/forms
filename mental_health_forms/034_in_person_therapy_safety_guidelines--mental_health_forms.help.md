# In Person Therapy Safety Guidelines - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In Person Therapy Safety Guidelines form is used to collect information about the client's symptoms, exposure, and safety plan to ensure their safety and well-being during in-person therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Screen Symptoms** (`screen_symptoms`, select multiple, required): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing at the moment. This will help the therapist understand your current mental state and provide appropriate care.
* **Confirm Safety Protocol** (`confirm_safety_protocol`, select one, required): Select the safety protocol that best describes your current situation. This will help the therapist understand your level of risk and provide personalized guidance.
* **Organize Data Collection** (`organize_data_collection`, text, required): Enter any relevant information that may help the therapist understand your history and current situation.
* **Recent Symptoms** (`recent_symptoms`, text, required): Describe any recent symptoms or changes in your mental health that you are experiencing.
* **Recent Exposure** (`recent_exposure`, text, required): Describe any recent exposure to situations or individuals that may impact your mental health.
* **Safety Plan** (`safety_plan`, text, required): Outline your safety plan, including any steps you have taken or plan to take to protect yourself from harm.
* **Pre-Visit Data** (`pre_visit_data`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about your mental health history or current situation that you would like to share with your therapist.
* **Screen Exposure** (`screen_exposure`, select multiple, required): Select all the exposures you are experiencing at the moment. This will help the therapist understand your current situation and provide appropriate care.
* **Screen Symptoms 2** (`screen_symptoms_2`, select multiple, required): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing at the moment. This is similar to the Screen Symptoms field, but may include additional options specific to your situation.
