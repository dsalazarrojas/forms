**Hoarding Support Assessment - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help assess and address hoarding behavior, a complex issue linked to mental health conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately, based on your personal experience or knowledge.
2. Be as detailed as possible, but feel free to skip questions that do not apply to you.
3. If you're unsure about any answer, please provide as much information as you can.
4. Please note that all answers will be kept confidential and used for support and guidance only.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Briefly describe the individual's hoarding behavior.
	+ This is an open-ended question to gather an understanding of the individual's hoarding habits and circumstances.
* **Hoarding Problem Starting**: Describe the starting point or trigger of the individual's hoarding behavior.
	+ This question aims to help identify the initial cause or catalyst for the hoarding behavior.
* **Hoarding Frequency**: Indicate how often the individual engages in hoarding behavior.
	+ Provide a frequency scale, e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, rarely.
* **Hoarding Severity**: Describe the severity of the individual's hoarding behavior.
	+ This could be a scale, e.g., mild, moderate, severe.
* **Hoarding Type**: Identify the type of hoarding behavior (e.g., material possessions, food, paper).
	+ Be as specific as possible to help tailor support.
* **Hoarding Location**: Indicate the location where the individual's hoarding occurs.
	+ This could be their home, workplace, public spaces.
* **Hoarding Support Services**: Describe any services currently being used to support the individual's hoarding behavior.
	+ This could include counseling, therapy, or support groups.
* **Hoarding Support Funding**: Indicate any funding sources currently providing support for the individual's hoarding behavior.
	+ This could include government programs, non-profit organizations, or personal financial resources.
* **Hoarding Intervention**: Describe any interventions or treatments currently in place to address the individual's hoarding behavior.
	+ This could include counseling, therapy, or specialized programs.
* **Hoarding Support Group**: Describe any support groups the individual is currently participating in.
	+ This could be in-person or online groups.
* **Mental Health Services**: Describe any mental health services the individual is currently receiving.
	+ This could include therapy, counseling, or medication management.
* **Mental Health Provider**: Identify the mental health provider working with the individual.
	+ This could be a therapist, counselor, or psychiatrist.
* **Mental Health Condition**: Indicate any mental health conditions the individual may be experiencing.
	+ This could include depression, anxiety, PTSD, or substance abuse.
* **Housing Services**: Describe any housing services the individual is currently using.
	+ This could include emergency shelters, long-term housing, or supportive housing.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide information for emergency contact in case of a crisis.
	+ This could include a name, phone number, or address.
* **Medical Services**: Describe any medical services the individual is currently receiving.
	+ This could include doctor visits, medication management, or hospitalizations.
* **Financial Support**: Indicate any financial support the individual is currently receiving.
	+ This could include government benefits, non-profit organizations, or personal financial resources.
* **Other Support**: Describe any other support the individual is currently receiving.
	+ This could include family support, friends, or community resources.
* **Hoarding Support Services 2**: Describe any additional support services for hoarding.
	+ This could include specialized programs or online resources.
* **Hoarding Support Funding 2**: Indicate any additional funding sources for hoarding support.
	+ This could include government programs, non-profit organizations, or personal financial resources.
* **Hoarding Intervention 2**: Describe any additional interventions or treatments for hoarding.
	+ This could include specialized programs or online resources.
* **Hoarding Support Group 2**: Describe any additional support groups the individual is currently participating in.

* **Mental Health Provider 2**: Identify any additional mental health provider working with the individual.
	+ This could be a therapist, counselor, or psychiatrist.
