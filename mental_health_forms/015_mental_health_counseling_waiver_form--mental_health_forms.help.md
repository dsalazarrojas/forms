# mental_health_counseling_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain informed consent and waiver of liability from clients seeking mental health services. It's a necessary step in our counseling process to ensure you understand the terms and conditions of our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure you answer all questions honestly and thoughtfully.
3. If you need help or clarification, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mental Health Counseling Waiver Form** (`mental_health_counseling_waiver_form`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Client Informed Consent** (`client_informed_consent`, text, required: false): Please indicate that you've been informed of and understand the terms and conditions of our services, including the limits of confidentiality and potential risks and benefits.
* **Mental Health Services Acknowledged** (`mental_health_services_acknowledged`, number, required: false): Select `1` to acknowledge you've received and understand these services.
* **Counseling Waiver Agreement** (`counseling_waiver_agreement`, text, required: false): Please explain your understanding of our services, including any concerns or questions you may have.
* **Mental Health Counseling Waiver Concerns** (`mental_health_counseling_waiver_concerns`, text, required: false): If you have any concerns or questions about our services, please list them here.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide contact information for a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: false): Explain your relationship with the emergency contact person listed above.
* **Mental Health Services Agree** (`mental_health_services_agree`, number, required: false): Select `1` to indicate you agree to our services.
* **Mental Health Services Disagreement Reason** (`mental_health_services_disagreement_reason`, text, required: false): If you disagree with our services, please explain the reason here.

## Tips

* Be honest and forthcoming with your answers to ensure we can provide you the best possible care.
* If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* This form is a required part of our intake process. Please complete it carefully and accurately to ensure we can provide you the support you need.
