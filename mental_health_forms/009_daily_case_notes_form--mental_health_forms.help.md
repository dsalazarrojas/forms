# Daily Case Notes Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Case Notes Form is a document used for recording important details from each session with a client, including client session notes, session date and time, session duration, and next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter **Client Session Notes** to record brief notes about the client's session.
2. Select the **Session Date** for the session you are documenting.
3. Enter the **Session Time** in the format `HH:MM`.
4. Enter the **Session Duration** in minutes.
5. Enter the **Session Topic** to describe the topic of the session.
6. Record any **Client Progress** made during the session.
7. Enter any **Counselor Notes** regarding the session.
8. List the **Next Steps** to be taken, including any actions or follow-up tasks.
9. If applicable, schedule a **Follow Up Date** and **Follow Up Time** for future contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Session Notes**: Brief notes about the client's session. This field should be used to record any important details or issues discussed during the session.
* **Session Date**: The date of the session being documented. Ensure this date is accurate and corresponds to the correct session.
* **Session Time**: The time of the session in the format `HH:MM`. Enter this in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
* **Session Duration**: The length of the session in minutes.
* **Session Topic**: A brief description of the topic discussed during the session.
* **Client Progress**: Notes on any progress made by the client during the session.
* **Counselor Notes**: Additional notes from the counselor regarding the session.
* **Next Steps**: A list of actions or tasks to be taken, including follow-up activities.
* **Follow Up Date**: The date scheduled for a future follow-up contact.
* **Follow Up Time**: The time scheduled for the future follow-up contact in the format `HH:MM`.
