# Veterans Mental Health Program Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterans Mental Health Program Donation Form is used to collect donations for the mental health initiatives of veteran communities. This form helps us to better serve these individuals and families.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the on-screen instructions and fill out the required fields. Each field is described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as it is recorded in official records.
* **Email** (2): Provide a valid email address to receive donation confirmations or updates from us.
* **Donation Amount** (3): Specify the amount you wish to donate, in currency of your choice (e.g., USD).
* **Dedication** (4): Add any personal message or dedication to the donation, e.g., to honor a veteran.
* **Anonymity** (5): Choose whether you wish to donate anonymously or not.
* **Comments** (6): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the program.
* **Email Notification** (7): Choose whether you want to receive email updates or not.
* **Phone Number** (8): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you for further information.
* **Address** (9): Enter your mailing address to receive any physical mail from us.
* **City** (10): Enter your city of residence for proper addressing.
* **State** (11): Enter your state of residence for proper addressing.
* **Zip** (12): Enter your zip code for proper addressing.
* **Confirm Phone** (13): Confirm your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Confirm Email** (14): Confirm your email address for contact purposes.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required, but providing accurate information helps us better serve the veteran community.
