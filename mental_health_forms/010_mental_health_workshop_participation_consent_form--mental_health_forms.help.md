# mental_health_workshop_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health workshop participation consent form is designed to capture essential information from workshop participants, ensuring a smooth and safe experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please go through each section carefully and fill out the required and optional fields as instructed. Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to ensure all necessary information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `text`, required): This section is for providing your name, contact information, and any other identifying details. Please fill out your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you efficiently.
* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, `text`, required): Describe the workshop you're participating in, including the topic, date, time, and duration. This information helps us understand your schedule and expectations.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you consent to participate in the workshop. This is a critical field to ensure your participation is acknowledged and respected.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and contact information. This helps us reach out to them in case of an emergency.
* **Workshop Facilitator** (`workshop_facilitator`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific requests or preferences for the workshop facilitator, please list them here.
* **Workshop Coordinator** (`workshop_coordinator`, `text`, optional): If you have any questions or concerns regarding the workshop coordinator, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, optional): If you know the workshop date, please provide it here. This helps us schedule and prepare for your participation.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, optional): If you know the workshop time, please provide it here. This helps us plan and schedule the workshop effectively.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, `number`, optional): If you know the expected duration of the workshop, please provide it here.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions about the workshop, please share them here.
* **Workshop Facilitator Contact** (`workshop_facilitator_contact`, `text`, optional): If you need to contact the workshop facilitator, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Workshop Coordinator Contact** (`workshop_coordinator_contact`, `text`, optional): If you need to contact the workshop coordinator, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check 'Yes' if you agree to the participant agreement. This includes terms like respecting other participants, adhering to confidentiality, and following workshop guidelines.
* **Emergency Contact Agreement** (`emergency_contact_agreement`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check 'Yes' if you agree to the emergency contact agreement. This includes giving permission for us to contact your emergency contact in case of an emergency.
