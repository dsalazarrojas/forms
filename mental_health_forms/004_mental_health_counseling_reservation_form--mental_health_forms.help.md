## mental_health_counseling_reservation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients to facilitate communication with their chosen therapist. It includes fields for contact information, scheduling preferences, and consent.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Page 1: First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Phone Number**
    *   Enter your first and last name to identify yourself as a client.
    *   Provide your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your session.
    *   Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you in case of emergencies.
2.  **Page 2: Appointment Time**, **Appointment Date**, **Preferred Day**, **Preferred Time**, **Notes**
    *   Choose your preferred time for the session.
    *   Select the date you would like to schedule the session.
    *   Select your preferred day of the week for the session.
    *   Enter any additional information you'd like to share with your therapist in the notes field.
3.  **Page 3: Therapist Information**, **Session Type**, **Session Duration**, **Session Frequency**, **Payment Method**, **Insurance Provider**, **Insurance Card**
    *   Enter the name and email of your chosen therapist.
    *   Select the type of session you would like to have (Individual, Group, or Family).
    *   Choose the session duration from 30, 60, 90, or 120 minutes.
    *   Select the frequency of your sessions (Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly).
    *   Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Cash, or Insurance).
    *   If paying through insurance, enter the insurance provider's name.
    *   If paying through insurance, enter your insurance card number.
4.  **Page 4: Emergency Contact Information**
    *   Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
    *   Select your relationship with the emergency contact person (Parent, Spouse, Child, Friend, or Other).
    *   Enter the contact person's phone number.
    *   Enter the contact person's email address.
    *   Enter the contact person's address.
5.  **Page 5: Client and Therapist Consent**
    *   Indicate whether you, as a client, consent to begin sessions.
    *   Indicate whether your chosen therapist has consented to work with you.
6.  **Submit**
    *   Once you have filled out all the required fields, click submit to send your reservation request to us.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   Be specific with your preferred days and times for sessions.
*   Enter accurate information for emergency contacts.
*   Review your information carefully before submitting.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.

By following these steps and tips, you will be able to complete the mental health counseling reservation form effectively and accurately.
