# Mental Health Therapy Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information required for reimbursement claims related to mental health therapy services. It should be filled out by clients who have received mental health therapy sessions and are seeking reimbursement. Therapists and healthcare providers can also use this form to collect and document client information for reimbursement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the **Client Information** section with your personal details. This includes your name, address, and any other requested information.
2.  Then, move on to the **Therapist Information** section and enter your therapist's details. This includes their name, credentials, and contact information.
3.  Finally, complete the **Claim Information** section with the dates and details of your therapy sessions, including session type and duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): This section is used to collect your personal details as a client, such as your name and address. Please enter the information accurately and completely.
*   **Therapist Information** (`therapist_info`, text, optional): This section is used to collect your therapist's details, such as their name and contact information. Please enter the information accurately and completely.
*   **Claim Information** (`claim_info`, text, optional): This section is used to collect details about your therapy sessions, including session dates and type. Please enter the information accurately and completely.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Double-check the form before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant parties for clarification.
