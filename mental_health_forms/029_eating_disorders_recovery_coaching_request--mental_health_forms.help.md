# Eating Disorders Recovery Coaching Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals seeking recovery coaching for eating disorders, such as bulimia, anorexia, and binge eating, to request coaching services from a licensed recovery coach. The form will help us understand your needs and preferences to provide personalized support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your phone number and recovery goals in the respective fields.
3. Select the type of coaching approach you are interested in (individual coaching, group coaching, or other).
4. Choose your eating disorder diagnosis from the provided options (bulimia, anorexia, or binge eating, or other).
5. Optionally, you can provide the frequency and ideal session length you prefer.
6. Enter the coaching location (optional).
7. Provide the emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship with the individual.
8. You can optionally select a recovery coach from our team.
9. Confirm your understanding of the client agreement.
10. Choose a date and time for your first session (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.

### Recovery Goals
* **Recovery Goals** (`recovery_goals`, `text`, required): Please describe your recovery goals and what you hope to achieve through coaching.

### Eating Disorder Diagnosis
* **Eating Disorder Diagnosis** (`eating_disorder_diagnosis`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of eating disorder you are seeking recovery for: bulimia, anorexia, binge eating, or other.

### Coaching Approach
* **Coaching Approach** (`coaching_approach`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of coaching approach you prefer: individual coaching, group coaching, or other.

### Frequency of Sessions
* **Frequency of Sessions** (`frequency_of_session`, `number`, optional): If you have a preferred frequency of sessions, please enter it.

### Ideal Session Length
* **Ideal Session Length** (`ideal_session_length`, `number`, optional): If you have a preferred session length, please enter it.

### Coaching Location
* **Coaching Location** (`coaching_location`, `text`, optional): If you have a preferred coaching location, please enter it.

### Emergency Contact Name
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact Phone
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Relationship
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.

### Recovery Coach
* **Recovery Coach** (`recovery_coach`, `text`, optional): If you prefer to work with a specific recovery coach, please enter their name.

### Client Agreement
* **Client Agreement** (`client_agreement`, `date`, required): Confirm your understanding of the client agreement.

### Session Date
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a preferred date for your first session, please enter it.

### Session Time
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, optional): If you have a preferred time for your first session, please enter it.

### Session Duration
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `number`, optional): If you have a preferred session duration, please enter it.

### Total Sessions
* **Total Sessions** (`total_sessions`, `number`, optional): If you have a preferred number of sessions, please enter it.
