# mental_health_program_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients about their mental health programs. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a thorough understanding of the client's needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Carefully read through each field explanation below to ensure you understand what is required and what is optional.
2. Fill out all required fields (indicated by *) with accurate information.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (*): Program name is the title of the mental health program being requested. It is a required field and should accurately reflect the type of program the client is inquiring about.
* **Client Name** (*): Client Name is the name of the person filling out the form. It is a required field and should be the client's full name.
* **Client Phone** (*): Client Phone is the phone number of the person filling out the form. It is a required field and should be a valid phone number.
* **Client Email** (*): Client Email is the email address of the person filling out the form. It is a required field and should be a valid email address.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact us for assistance.
* If you are unsure about what information to provide, please refer to the form's purpose and the field explanations above.
