# Minor Interaction Suitability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Minor Interaction Suitability Assessment form is designed to evaluate and assess the suitability of interactions between adults and minors. This form is used to gather information about planned interactions, participant details, and safety considerations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Planned Interaction field with a brief description of the planned interaction.
2. Enter the Date of Interaction, if known.
3. Provide details about the location of the interaction in the Location of Interaction field.
4. Add Adult Participant Details and Minor Participant Details, including any relevant information about their ages, roles, or involvement.
5. Enter a detailed description of the interaction in the Interaction Description field.
6. If necessary, enter Key Safety Details to document any safety concerns.
7. Identify the person who reviewed the risk assessment in the Risk Reviewed By field.
8. Enter the date the risk assessment was reviewed in the Risk Reviewed On field.
9. Describe any planned action in the Action Planned field.
10. Select the Follow-Up field to indicate the next steps or follow-up required.
11. Indicate the Review Status, which can be Completed, Pending, or Not Started.
12. Add any Reviewer Notes for the assessor's comments or recommendations.
13. Finally, confirm whether the form has been completed in the Form Completed field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Planned Interaction**: A brief description of the planned interaction.
* **Date of Interaction**: The date of the interaction, if known.
* **Location of Interaction**: The location where the interaction will take place.
* **Adult Participant Details**: Information about the adult participant involved, including their age, role, or other relevant details.
* **Minor Participant Details**: Information about the minor participant involved, including their age, role, or other relevant details.
* **Interaction Description**: A detailed description of the interaction.
* **Key Safety Details**: Any safety concerns or considerations relevant to the interaction.
* **Risk Reviewed By**: The person who reviewed the risk assessment.
* **Risk Reviewed On**: The date the risk assessment was reviewed.
* **Action Planned**: A description of any planned action or follow-up required.
* **Follow-Up**: The next steps or follow-up required.
* **Review Status**: The status of the review, which can be Completed, Pending, or Not Started.
* **Reviewer Notes**: Comments or recommendations from the reviewer.
* **Form Completed**: Confirmation of whether the form has been completed, which can be True, False, or Partially.
