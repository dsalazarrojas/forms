# shift_patterns_and_mental_fatigue_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees track their work patterns and identify potential factors that may contribute to mental fatigue. By providing detailed information on their work schedule and duration, employees can help their organization better understand their work habits and provide resources to support their well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. For each field, provide information about your work patterns and habits.
3. Make sure to include all relevant information, even if you're unsure about some questions.
4. Review and submit your form once you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Work Patterns** (`work_patterns`, text, optional)
This field is for describing your typical work patterns, such as your daily routine, shift hours, and any recurring work schedules.

### **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional)
This field is for reporting the number of hours you typically work in a day.

### **Work Duration** (`work_duration`, date, optional)
This field is for selecting the date of your longest work period in the past week. Please use the calendar date format (YYYY-MM-DD) to enter this information.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
- If you're unsure about any information, it's better to provide an estimate rather than guessing.
- This form is for anonymous and confidential reporting, so feel free to answer honestly without worrying about repercussions.
