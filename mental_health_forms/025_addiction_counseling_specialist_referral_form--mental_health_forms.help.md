# Addiction Counseling Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help addiction counseling specialists receive essential information about clients being referred for treatment, ensuring they are well-prepared for the client's appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Referrer Name**: Enter the name of the person making the referral.
2. **Client Name**: Enter the name of the individual being referred.
3. **Contact Information**: Provide the client's phone number and email (if available) for smooth communication.
4. **Reason for Referral**: Describe the symptoms, concerns, or observations that led to the referral.
5. **Risk Assessment**: Select the level of risk present (Low, Moderate, or High) to help the specialist understand the client's urgency.
6. **Preferred Treatment**: Choose the recommended treatment approach (Individual therapy, Group therapy, IOP, or Residential).
7. **Urgency of Referral**: Select the urgency of the referral (Routine, Priority, or Immediate).
8. **Supporting Documents**: If applicable, attach any relevant medical notes, consent forms, or assessments.
9. **Additional Notes**: Provide any extra context or information about the client that the specialist should be aware of.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer Name**: Enter the name of the person who is making the referral.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the individual being referred for addiction counseling services.
* **Contact Information for Client**: Provide the client's phone number and email (if available) for smooth communication with the specialist.
* **Reason for Referral**: Describe the symptoms, concerns, or observations that led to the referral. This will help the specialist understand the client's situation.
* **Risk Assessment**: Select the level of risk present (Low, Moderate, or High) to help the specialist understand the urgency of the referral.
	+ Low: The client is not in immediate danger, but may benefit from counseling services.
	+ Moderate: The client is at risk of self-harm or harm to others, but is not in immediate danger.
	+ High: The client is in immediate danger and requires urgent attention.
* **Preferred Treatment**: Choose the recommended treatment approach (Individual therapy, Group therapy, IOP, or Residential) that suits the client's needs.
* **Urgency of Referral**: Select the urgency of the referral (Routine, Priority, or Immediate) to help the specialist prioritize their schedule.
	+ Routine: The referral is not time-sensitive.
	+ Priority: The specialist should see the client within the next few days.
	+ Immediate: The client requires urgent attention, ideally within the next 24 hours.
* **Supporting Documents**: If applicable, attach any relevant medical notes, consent forms, or assessments to provide context.
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any extra information about the client that the specialist should be aware of to ensure a smooth and effective consultation.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure the specialist has all necessary information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or a mental health professional for guidance.
* Attach any relevant supporting documents to provide context.
* Ensure the specialist is the right fit for the client's needs.
* Verify the client's consent for treatment before referring them to the specialist.
