# Mental Health Metrics Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on an individual's mental health metrics, such as their current mental state, well-being, mental health history, current treatment, support systems, and progress monitoring. This data can be used to track and evaluate the effectiveness of treatment plans and support systems, helping mental health professionals to better understand the client's mental health status and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the space provided, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses, as the information collected will be used to inform treatment decisions.
3. If you are unsure about any question, you can skip it or ask for clarification from your mental health professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (1, text, optional)
	+ This section is for general contact information of the client, which will help us identify who they are and their context.
* **Mental State** (2, text, optional)
	+ Please describe your current mental state, e.g., feeling anxious, depressed, or stable.
* **Well-being** (3, text, optional)
	+ Please describe your current well-being, e.g., physical comfort, emotional balance, or overall quality of life.
* **Mental Health History** (4, text, optional)
	+ Please provide a brief history of your mental health concerns, e.g., previous diagnoses, treatments, or significant events that have affected your mental health.
* **Current Treatment** (5, text, optional)
	+ Please list any current treatments or therapies you are receiving, e.g., medication, therapy sessions, or support groups.
* **Current Support Systems** (6, text, optional)
	+ Please list any support systems you have in place, e.g., family, friends, or mental health support groups.
* **Mental Health Metrics** (7, text, optional)
	+ Please provide any relevant metrics on your mental health status, e.g., symptoms, severity, or specific concerns.
* **Treatment Planning** (8, text, optional)
	+ Please describe any specific goals or objectives you have for your treatment plan, e.g., reducing anxiety or improving mood.
* **Progress Monitoring** (9, text, optional)
	+ Please provide any information on how you are progressing towards your goals, e.g., successes, setbacks, or changes.
* **Client Goals** (10, text, optional)
	+ Please provide any additional goals or concerns you have for your mental health treatment, e.g., personal growth, self-improvement, or specific objectives.
