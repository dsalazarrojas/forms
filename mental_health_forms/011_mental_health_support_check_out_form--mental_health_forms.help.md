# mental_health_support_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health support check out form is a way for clients to provide feedback on their experience with a mental health professional. This includes sharing any concerns, questions, or suggestions they may have, which can help the professional to improve their services and better support clients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Select the date of your session in the "Session Date" field.
3. Share any feedback or comments in the "Feedback" field.
4. If you have a primary contact email, please enter it in the "Email" field.
5. If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field.
6. Select any mental health professionals you have worked with from the list in the "Mental Health Professional" field. You can choose multiple professionals if applicable.
7. Rate your experience on a scale from 0 to 10 in the "Rating" field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about your session in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a client.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Select the date of your mental health session.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback or comments you have about your session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your primary contact email if you have one.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your primary phone number if you have one.
* **Mental Health Professional** (`mental_health_professional`, select_multiple, optional): Select the mental health professional(s) you worked with. Multiple selections are allowed.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your session.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments you have about your session.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to the mental health professional directly.
* Your feedback is valuable and helps us to improve our services.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
