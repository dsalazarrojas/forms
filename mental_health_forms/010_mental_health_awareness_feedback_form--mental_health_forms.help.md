# Mental Health Awareness Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Awareness Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their mental health experience. This form helps us understand how we can improve our services and provide better care for our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. You can submit this form anonymously, but please note that your feedback is valuable to us and helps us improve our services. If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact us directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mental Health Form** (1): Please provide a brief description of your experience.
* **Patient Name** (2): Provide your name, which will be kept confidential.
* **Medical Professional** (3): If applicable, please provide the name of the medical professional you consulted.
* **Date of Visit** (4): Enter the date of your visit or appointment.
* **Time of Visit** (5): Enter the time of your visit or appointment.
* **Feedback Rating** (6): Rate your experience on a scale of 0-5, where 0 is "poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Rating Comment** (7): Provide any comments or feedback related to your rating.
* **Overall Experience** (8): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions about your overall experience.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible to help us improve our services.
- If you have any concerns or complaints, please contact us directly so we can address them promptly.
- Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input.
