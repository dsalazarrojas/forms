# Therapy Services Eligibility Screening - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about you to determine your eligibility for therapy services. Please answer the questions accurately and completely to help our team assess your suitability for these services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). If a field is not marked as required, you can leave it blank.
2. Ensure that you enter your information accurately and consistently, especially for date fields.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask for help.

* **First Page Section 1 Field 2** (Date of Birth): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
