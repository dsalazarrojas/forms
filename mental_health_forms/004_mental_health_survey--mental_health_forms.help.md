# mental_health_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health survey is designed to gather information about your mental well-being and help identify potential areas of concern. It is intended for self-assessment and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Please answer the questions honestly, and if you have any concerns, consider sharing this information with a mental health professional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant answers for each page by choosing one or multiple options as prompted.
2. For text fields, provide as much detail as possible in the answer.
3. If you're unsure about any question, answer based on your best understanding.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **mental_health_survey_page** (Select Multiple): This page asks how you're managing your mental health. Please choose one or more of the options that apply to your situation.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **how_am_i_doing** (Text Field): This question is asking about your current mental health status. Try to provide as much detail as possible about how you're feeling or coping with mental health challenges.
* **get_thoughts_flowin** (Text Field): Describe any thoughts or feelings that are currently flowing through your mind. This will help us better understand your mental state.
* **mental_health_survey_page_2** (Select One): Please choose one of the options to indicate your mental health status.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **how_am_i_feeling** (Select Multiple): Choose one or more of the options that best describe your current emotional state.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **what_can_help** (Text Field): Think about any resources, support, or strategies that could help improve your mental health. Be as specific as possible.
* **next_steps** (Text Field): Describe any steps you're planning to take or need help with to improve your mental health.
* **follow_up** (Time Field): If you'd like to schedule a follow-up appointment or discussion, please provide a specific time frame (e.g., "next week" or "within two weeks").

## Tips
* If you have any concerns or questions about your answers, consider sharing them with a mental health professional.
* Take care of yourself and prioritize your mental health and well-being.
* Remember that this survey is for informational purposes only and should not replace professional medical advice or treatment.
