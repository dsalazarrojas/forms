# mental_health_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The mental health release form is a crucial document that helps us understand a patient's mental health history, medical background, and emergency contact information. This form is designed to gather essential information to provide personalized care and support.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Please review the form carefully before starting.
2. Fill in all the required fields (indicated by asterisk *) carefully and accurately.
3. For each field, make sure to select the correct option from the provided dropdown menus.
4. If you need to provide additional information not listed in the form, please use the "Other" field provided for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information
* **General Information** (`id: 1`): This section captures basic identifying information about you, such as your name, date of birth, and address.
* **Insurance Number** (`id: 12`): Please provide your insurance number to help us understand your coverage and benefits.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 14`): Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you easily.
* **Email** (`id: 15`): Provide your email address for secure communication.

### Medical Information
* **Medical Information** (`id: 21`): This field is for your medical insurance details, including your insurance provider and any relevant medical history.
* **Mental Health Insurance** (`id: 22`): This field is for your mental health insurance details, including your insurance provider and any relevant coverage.
* **Medication List** (`id: 4`): Please indicate if you are taking any medication or have any medical conditions that may affect your mental health.
* **Date of Birth** (`id: 13`): Enter your date of birth to help us understand your age and medical history.

### Mental Health History
* **Mental Health History** (`id: 3`): This section is for you to provide details about your mental health history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or hospitalizations.
* **Mental Health Providers** (`id: 9`): Please list any mental health providers you have seen in the past, including their names and contact information.
* **Mental Health Crisis Services** (`id: 10`): Indicate if you have accessed mental health crisis services in the past.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`id: 7`): Identify at least one emergency contact person, including their name and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`id: 8`): Indicate the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Other** (`id: 20`): If you have an emergency contact not listed above, please provide their details here.

### Additional Information
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`id: 19`): Enter your emergency contact's email address for secure communication.
* **Mental Health Providers Other** (`id: 23`): If you have mental health providers not listed above, please provide their details here.
* **Crisis Services Other** (`id: 24`): If you have accessed mental health crisis services not listed above, please provide details here.

Please note that all fields marked as "Other" are optional and should only be used for information not captured in other fields. If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please reach out to us for support.
