# Psychology Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a patient's mental health, medical history, and personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, as required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Provide your address, date of birth, and medical record number if required.
5. Sign with your name and date to confirm that you have completed the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, \*):
	* This is where you provide your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
- **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, \*):
	* Check all the symptoms that you are currently experiencing. 
	* For example, if you are experiencing anxiety, depression, or sleep problems.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_one, \*):
	* Choose a medical condition that best describes your medical background.
	* For example, if you have a history of diabetes, heart disease, or chronic pain.
- **Family Medical History** (`family_medical_history`, text, \*):
	* Report any significant medical conditions that affect your family members.
	* For example, if your parents or siblings have diabetes, heart disease, or cancer.
- **Mental State** (`mental_state`, select_multiple, false):
	* Choose any mental state that describes your current feelings or behaviors.
	* For example, anxiety, depression, or stress.
- **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, time, false):
	* Provide a time estimate of how long you have been experiencing your mental state.
	* Use the format "X days", "Y weeks", "Z months", or "W years".
	* For example, "I have been feeling depressed for 2 weeks".
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, false):
	* Add any additional information that is relevant to your mental health.
	* For example, your personal goals or expectations from treatment.
- **Email** (`email`, email, \*):
	* Enter your email address for contact purposes.
	* This will help the mental health professional contact you with any questions or updates.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, false):
	* Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
	* This will help the mental health professional contact you with any questions or updates.
- **Address** (`address`, text, false):
	* Enter your address for contact purposes.
	* This will help the mental health professional contact you with any questions or updates.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, false):
	* Enter your date of birth for demographic purposes.
- **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, false):
	* Enter your medical record number for identification purposes.
- **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, \*):
	* Sign with your name and date to confirm that you have completed the form accurately.

## Tips
- Ensure that you answer all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, ask your mental health professional for clarification.
