# mental_wellness_program_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The mental wellness program registration form is designed to gather information about participants in a mental wellness program. This form collects personal details, health-related information, and program-specific data to ensure that participants are properly registered and monitored throughout the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name and other required information.
2. Fill in your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide your date of birth.
4. Indicate if you have a mental health provider.
5. Select if you have any mental health issues or services.
6. Specify the start and end dates of the program.
7. Write down your mental health goals and outcomes.
8. Select whether you have mental health assessment or support.
9. Choose your mental health service provider.
10. Choose the mental health services you need.
11. Write down any notes about the program.
12. Confirm your participant agreement.
13. Select the program administrator.
14. Select your participant status.
15. Select the program status.
16. Choose the type of program.
17. Indicate if this is a mental health referral.
18. Select your mental health goals.
19. Select your mental health outcomes.
20. Choose the mental health services you need.
