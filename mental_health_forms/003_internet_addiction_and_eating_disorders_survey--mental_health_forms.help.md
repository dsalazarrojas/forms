# Internet Addiction And Eating Disorders Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on internet addiction and eating disorders. The form is intended for individuals who are interested in participating in a mental health study or who have been referred by a mental health professional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully read each question and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out the demographic data section with accurate and complete information.
3. Answer the questions in the behavioral insights section honestly and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **intro_page**: This is the introduction page of the form. Please read through the entire page before proceeding.
* **Introduction**: This is a text field where you can type in any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to the survey.
* **demographic_data**: This section asks for demographic data, including whether you answered "Yes" or "No" to the following questions:
	+ Have you ever experienced internet addiction?
	+ Have you ever experienced eating disorders?
* **questions**: This is a text field where you can type in any questions or concerns you have about the survey.
* **behavioral_insights**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about your behaviors or habits that may be relevant to the survey.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
- Please note that this form is intended for individuals who are interested in participating in a mental health study or who have been referred by a mental health professional.
