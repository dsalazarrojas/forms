# mental_health_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The mental_health_information_form is a collection of questions and fields used to gather information about an individual's mental health. This form is used to collect relevant details that can aid in providing effective mental health support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Name** (Text)
   - **Contact Email** (Email)
   - **Contact Phone** (Text)
   - **Mental Health Issue(s)** (Select Multiple)
   - **Mental Health Support Services** (Select Multiple)
   - **Mental Health Additional Info 1** (Yes/No)
   - **Mental Health Additional Info 2** (Yes/No)

2. Provide optional fields as needed:
   - **Date of Birth** (Date)
   - **Emergency Contact** (Select One)
   - **Mental Health Goal(s)** (Select One)
   - **Mental Health Support Group** (Text)
   - **Mental Health Facilitator** (Text)
   - **Mental Health Counselor** (Text)
   - **Mental Health Provider** (Text)
   - **Mental Health Location** (Text)
   - **Mental Health Time** (Time)
   - **Mental Health Frequency** (Text)
   - **Mental Health Duration** (Select One)
   - **Mental Health Severity** (Text)
   - **Mental Health Additional Info 3** (Text)
   - **Mental Health Additional Info 4** (Yes/No)
   - **Mental Health Additional Info 5** (Text)
   - **Mental Health Additional Info 6** (Text)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Name** (Text):
   Enter your name as it appears on your official documentation.

- **Contact Email** (Email):
   Enter your email address so we can contact you.

- **Contact Phone** (Text):
   Enter your phone number so we can contact you.

- **Date of Birth** (Date):
   If you wish to provide your date of birth, please enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

- **Emergency Contact** (Select One):
   Choose the emergency contact option that best describes your situation.

- **Mental Health Issue(s)** (Select Multiple):
   Select all the mental health issues that apply to you.

- **Mental Health Goal(s)** (Select One):
   Choose the mental health goal that best describes your objective.

- **Mental Health Support Group** (Text):
   If you are part of a mental health support group, please enter the name of the group.

- **Mental Health Facilitator** (Text):
   If you have a mental health facilitator, please enter their name.

- **Mental Health Counselor** (Text):
   If you have a mental health counselor, please enter their name.

- **Mental Health Provider** (Text):
   If you have a mental health provider, please enter their name.

- **Mental Health Location** (Text):
   If you want to provide the location of your mental health sessions, please enter it here.

- **Mental Health Time** (Time):
   If you want to provide the time of your mental health sessions, please enter it here.

- **Mental Health Frequency** (Text):
   If you want to provide the frequency of your mental health sessions, please enter it here.

- **Mental Health Duration** (Select One):
   Choose the duration of your mental health sessions.

- **Mental Health Severity** (Text):
   Describe the severity of your mental health issues.

- **Mental Health Support Services** (Select Multiple):
   Select all the mental health support services that apply to you.

- **Mental Health Additional Info 1** (Yes/No):
   Answer if you have any additional information to provide.

- **Mental Health Additional Info 2** (Yes/No):
   Answer if you have any additional information to provide.

- **Mental Health Additional Info 3** (Text):
   Enter any additional information you would like to provide.

- **Mental Health Additional Info 4** (Yes/No):
   Answer if you have any additional information to provide.

- **Mental Health Additional Info 5** (Text):
   Enter any additional information you would like to provide.

- **Mental Health Additional Info 6** (Text):
   Enter any additional information you would like to provide.

## Tips
Please fill out the form as completely and accurately as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out.
