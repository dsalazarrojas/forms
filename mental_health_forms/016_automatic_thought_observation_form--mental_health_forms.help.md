# Automatic Thought Observation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Automatic Thought Observation Form is a tool used to collect information about a person's thoughts, emotions, and physical sensations during a specific event or situation. This form helps identify patterns and triggers that contribute to automatic thoughts, providing valuable insights for therapists, coaches, or individuals working on mental health. 

This form is designed to be used in the context of cognitive-behavioral therapy or coaching sessions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections to understand the context and purpose of each section.
2. Enter your responses accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Be honest and objective when describing your thoughts, emotions, and physical sensations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: <label> (Required) - Enter the client's name, as this will help identify the individual associated with this form submission.
* **Date of Observation**: <label> (Required, date format) - Enter the date when the observation took place.
* **Time of Observation**: <label> (Optional, time format) - Enter the time when the observation took place.
* **Therapist or Coach Name**: <label> (Optional) - If you're working with a therapist or coach, enter their name to associate it with the observation.
* **Triggering Event or Situation**: <label> (Required) - Describe the specific event or situation that triggered the thought.
* **Emotional Context**: <label> (Required) - Specify the emotions present during the event or situation.
* **Where Did This Occur**: <label> (Optional) - Describe the location where the event or situation took place.
* **Automatic Thought**: <label> (Required) - Record the exact thought as it occurred.
* **Thought Clarity**: <label> (Required, select_one) - Rate the clarity of the thought using the options Crystal Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Vague, Fleeting.
* **Initial Belief in Thought**: <label> (Required, number, 0-100%) - Rate the initial intensity of your belief in the thought.
* **Primary Emotion**: <label> (Required, select_one) - Identify the primary emotion experienced.
* **Emotion Intensity**: <label> (Required, number, 0-10) - Rate the intensity of the emotion.
* **Physical Sensations**: <label> (Optional) - Describe any physical reactions experienced.
* **Evidence Supporting the Thought**: <label> (Required) - Describe evidence that supports the thought.
* **Evidence Against the Thought**: <label> (Required) - Describe evidence that contradicts the thought.
* **Alternative Thought 1**: <label> (Required) - Suggest an alternative perspective on the thought.
* **Alternative Thought 2**: <label> (Optional) - Suggest another alternative perspective on the thought.
* **Emotion Intensity After Analysis**: <label> (Required, number, 0-10) - Rate the intensity of the emotion after analysis.
* **Key Insights and Learning Points**: <label> (Optional) - Describe any key insights or learning points from this exercise.

Remember to answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible, providing as much detail as you can. The more complete your responses are, the more valuable the insights will be for you and your therapist or coach. 

By following these steps and using the provided fields, you'll be able to gather a comprehensive understanding of automatic thoughts and their emotional context. This form is designed to help you and your mental health professional work together to identify and address these automatic thoughts effectively.
