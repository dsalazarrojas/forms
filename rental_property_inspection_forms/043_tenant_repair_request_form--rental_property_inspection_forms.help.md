# Tenant Repair Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Repair Request Form is designed for tenants or authorized personnel to submit repair requests to the property management company. Please use this form to notify us of any issues or repairs that need to be addressed in the rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the date you reported the issue in the date_issue_reported field.
2. In the location_issue_reported field, describe the exact location where the issue is occurring.
3. In the description_issue_reported field, provide a detailed description of the issue.
4. Select the category of the issue from the issue_category field.
5. Choose the priority of the issue from the issue_priority field.
6. Assign the issue to a specific staff member from the assign_to field.
7. Update the status of the issue from the status field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Repair Request Form** (`tenant_repair_request_form`, required): This field is the title of the form and should not be changed.
* **Date Issue Reported** (`date_issue_reported`, optional): Enter the date you reported the issue. This helps us track the issue's history.
* **Location Issue Reported** (`location_issue_reported`, optional): Describe the exact location of the issue, including the apartment/unit number, room, or area where the issue is occurring.
* **Description Issue Reported** (`description_issue_reported`, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue, including any relevant details or photos.
* **Issue Category** (`issue_category`, optional): Select the category of the issue from the dropdown list. Please choose the most relevant category for your issue.
* **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`, optional): Choose the priority of the issue from the dropdown list. This helps us schedule repairs efficiently.
* **Assign To** (`assign_to`, optional): Assign the issue to a specific staff member. Please ensure they are aware of the issue.
* **Status** (`status`, optional): Update the status of the issue from the dropdown list. Please keep this up-to-date as the issue is resolved.
