# Brand Standard Room Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to assess the condition of a rental property, ensuring it meets the brand's standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out each section carefully and thoroughly.
2.  Use the provided scales or options when rating the condition of each area.
3.  Document any issues or concerns you notice during the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Inspection Date** (id: 1):
    *   Date when the inspection was conducted
*   **Room Number** (id: 2):
    *   Enter the room number or identifier
*   **Guest Name** (id: 3):
    *   Name of the current or departing guest (optional)
*   **Check-in Date** (id: 4):
    *   When the guest checked in
*   **Check-out Date** (id: 5):
    *   When the guest is checking out
*   **Bed and Bedding Condition** (id: 6):
    *   Rate the condition of beds and linens
    *   *   Excellent
        *   Good
        *   Acceptable
        *   Needs cleaning
        *   Damaged
*   **Furniture Condition** (id: 7):
    *   Assess all furniture pieces
    *   *   Excellent
        *   Good
        *   Acceptable
        *   Needs repair
        *   Damaged
*   **Walls and Ceiling Condition** (id: 8):
    *   Check for stains, marks, or damage
    *   *   Excellent
        *   Good
        *   Minor marks
        *   Needs cleaning
        *   Damaged or stained
*   **Flooring Condition** (id: 9):
    *   Assess carpet or hard flooring
    *   *   Excellent
        *   Good
        *   Acceptable
        *   Needs cleaning
        *   Damaged
*   **Bathroom Condition** (id: 10):
    *   Rate bathroom cleanliness and fixtures
    *   *   Excellent
        *   Good
        *   Acceptable
        *   Needs cleaning
        *   Damaged
*   **Bathroom Fixtures and Plumbing** (id: 11):
    *   Check sinks, toilets, showers
    *   *   Excellent
        *   Good
        *   Minor issues
        *   Needs repair
        *   Major issues
*   **HVAC and Temperature Control** (id: 12):
    *   Test heating and cooling system
    *   *   Working properly
        *   Good
        *   Minor issues
        *   Needs adjustment
        *   Not working
*   **Lighting Fixtures and Bulbs** (id: 13):
    *   Check all lights and bulbs
    *   *   All working
        *   Most working
        *   Some not working
        *   Several out
        *   Most out
*   **Television and Electronics** (id: 14):
    *   Test TV, remote, and other electronics
    *   *   All working
        *   Good

        *   Needs replacement
        *   Not working
*   **Doors and Locks** (id: 15):
    *   Check all door locks and hinges

*   **Windows and Window Coverings** (id: 16):
    *   Check windows, screens, blinds

        *   Good

*   **Overall Cleanliness** (id: 17):
    *   General assessment of room cleanliness
    *   *   Spotless
        *   Very clean
        *   Clean
        *   Needs cleaning
        *   Dirty
*   **Odors or Unusual Issues** (id: 18):
    *   Note any smells or unusual conditions
*   **Maintenance and Repairs Needed** (id: 19):
    *   List all items needing repair or maintenance
*   **Does Room Need Deep Cleaning** (id: 20):
    *   Select if professional deep clean required
    *   *   False
        *   Yes - light cleaning
        *   Yes - thorough cleaning
        *   Yes - complete deep clean
*   **Inspector Name** (id: 21):
    *   Name of person conducting inspection

## Tips

*   Be thorough and honest in your assessment.
*   Use the provided scales or options to ensure consistency.
*   Document any issues or concerns clearly.
*   Provide clear and concise notes, if applicable.
