# Security Deposit Itemized Deduction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and itemize deductions from a tenant's security deposit. A security deposit is a refundable sum of money given to a landlord by a tenant at the start of a rental agreement. It is typically returned to the tenant when they vacate the premises, minus any deductions for damages or repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the tenant's information, including their name and contact details.
2. Enter the inspection date when the property was inspected.
3. Fill in the damage amount and repair cost, if applicable.
4. Select the payment status, which can be Pending, Received, or Paid.
5. Enter the security deposit amount and any related deduction status.
6. Fill in the deduction date.
7. Enter the landlord's name.
8. Sign and date the form for both the tenant and landlord.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Info**: Enter the name and contact details of the tenant who paid the security deposit.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date when the property was inspected for damages or repairs.
* **Damage Amount**: Enter the total amount of damages or repairs needed.
* **Repair Cost**: Enter the cost of the repairs or damages.
* **Payment Status**: Select one of the following options:
	+ **Pending**: The payment is still pending.
	+ **Received**: The payment has been received.
	+ **Paid**: The payment has been paid.
* **Security Deposit**: Enter the original security deposit amount.
* **Deduction Status**: Select one of the following options:
	+ **Pending**: The deduction is still pending.
	+ **Received**: The deduction has been received.
	+ **Paid**: The deduction has been paid.
* **Deduction Date**: Enter the date when the deduction was made.
* **Landlord Name**: Enter the name of the landlord who inspected the property.
* **Tenant Signature**: A space for the tenant to sign and date the form, indicating they have reviewed and agree with the deduction.
* **Landlord Signature**: A space for the landlord to sign and date the form, indicating they have reviewed and agree with the deduction.
