# Rental Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Inspection Report Form is used to document issues found during a rental property inspection, ensuring accurate and timely reporting and resolution of these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the tenant's name in the "Tenant Name" field.
2. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
3. Select the property status (Inspection Required, Not Required, or Required but not done) in the "Property Status" field.
4. Describe the issue in the "Issue Description" field.
5. Select the issue date and time in the "Issue Date" and "Issue Time" fields, respectively.
6. Choose the issue status (Open, Closed, or Waiting for repair) in the "Issue Status" field.
7. Select the repair request status (Accepted, Declined, or Pending) in the "Repair Request" field.
8. Determine the issue priority (High, Medium, or Low) in the "Issue Priority" field.
9. Identify the issue category (Electrical, Plumbing, or HVAC) in the "Issue Category" field.
10. Upload any issue images, audio, or video attachments in the "Issue Image", "Issue Audio", and "Issue Video" fields, respectively.
11. Add any issue notes or comments in the "Issue Note" field.
