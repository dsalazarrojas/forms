# Property Management Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the condition and status of a rental property. It will help property managers or inspectors to document their findings and report them to relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the property manager's name in the "property_manager_name" field.
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the "date_of_inspection" field in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
3. Enter the property's address in the "property_address" field.
4. Provide any additional notes about the property's condition, systems, and maintenance in the "notes" field (optional).
5. Indicate the status of the property's electrical systems, plumbing, heating, cooling, exterior damage, fire hazards, and safety issues by selecting either "Yes" or "No" in the corresponding fields.
6. Describe the overall condition of the property in the "property_condition" field.
7. Indicate whether the property's systems require repair and maintenance in the "repair_and_maintenance" field.
8. Select the maintenance status of the property in the "maintenance_status" field.
9. Enter any comments about the property in the "comments" field (optional).
10. Fill in the inspector's name, signature, phone number, and email in the corresponding fields.
11. Enter the property manager's name, phone number, and email in the corresponding fields.
12. Add any additional comments from the property manager in the "property_manager_note" field (optional).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Be sure to review and sign the form to confirm that the information is accurate.
* This form is not a substitute for a thorough and regular property maintenance schedule.
* Use this form to document your findings and report them to relevant parties.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, consult the property manager or a professional.
* Use this form for its intended purpose and do not duplicate or alter any information on the form.
