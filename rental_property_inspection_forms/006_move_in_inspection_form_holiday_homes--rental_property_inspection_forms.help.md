# Move In Inspection Form Holiday Homes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from tenants when they move into a holiday home. This form is used to document the condition of the property at the start of the tenancy, allowing the landlord to track and monitor the condition of the property over time.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and address in the corresponding fields.
2. If you have a contact number, please provide it for the landlord or property manager.
3. Add any comments or remarks about the property, such as its cleanliness, maintenance, or any issues you've encountered.
4. Attach any photos of the property that you'd like to document.
5. Select the date you moved in.
6. Identify yourself as the move-in inspector.
7. Sign the form, indicating that the information provided is accurate.
8. Sign as the move-in inspector, confirming that the inspection was conducted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name (`tenant_name`)**: Enter your name as the tenant of the holiday home.
* **Address (`address`)**: Enter the address of the holiday home where you're moving in.
* **Contact Number (`contact_number`)**: If you have a contact number, please provide it for the landlord or property manager.
* **Comments (`comments`)**: Add any comments or remarks about the property's condition, such as its cleanliness, maintenance, or any issues you've encountered.
* **Photos (`photos`)**: Attach photos of the property that you'd like to document. This can be helpful for the landlord or property manager to reference later.
* **Move In Date (`date_moved_in`)**: Select the date you moved into the holiday home.
* **Move In Inspector (`move_in_inspector`)**: Identify yourself as the move-in inspector.
* **Tenant Signature (`tenant_signature`)**: Sign the form, indicating that the information provided is accurate.
* **Move In Inspector Signature (`move_in_inspector_signature`)**: Sign as the move-in inspector, confirming that the inspection was conducted.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest information about the property's condition.
* Take clear and well-lit photos of the property's interior and exterior.
* Sign the form as soon as possible after moving in.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records, in case of any future disputes.
