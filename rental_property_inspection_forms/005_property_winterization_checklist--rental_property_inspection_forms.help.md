## property_winterization_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on the winterization status of a rental property. The purpose of this form is to ensure that property owners and managers can efficiently document and track the winterization status of their rental properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields:

* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the rental property.
* **Winterization Status**: Choose one of the three options: Not winterized, Winterized, or Partially winterized.
* **Property Winterization Status Date**: Enter the date when the property was winterized or when the status changed.
* **Property Winterization Status Time**: Enter the time when the property was winterized or when the status changed.
* **Property Winterization Status Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for the winterization status.
* **Property Winterization Status Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the winterization status.
* **Property Winterization Status Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the winterization status.
* **Property Winterization Status Attachments**: Upload any supporting documents or photos related to the winterization status.
* **Property Winterization Status Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the winterization status.
