# Tenant Welfare Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Welfare Inspection Form is used to gather information about the well-being and conditions of the rental property. The form is completed by the tenant or their representative to provide a comprehensive overview of their living environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct **Rental Property ID** for the inspected property.
2.  Enter the **Unit Number** of the inspected property.
3.  Fill in the **Date Of Visit**, the date the inspection was performed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Rental Property ID** (number, required): This field is used to identify the property being inspected. Select the correct ID to ensure that the form data is linked to the correct property.
*   **Unit Number** (text, optional): Enter the unit number of the inspected property for more detailed tracking.
*   **Date Of Visit** (date, optional): Fill in the date the inspection was performed. This will help track and record the visit accurately.
