# Landlord Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help landlords gather information about a rental property's condition, including its general information, building details, exterior features, and various systems such as plumbing, electrical, and heating. The form is intended to be completed by a landlord or their representative during a property inspection, and the gathered data can be used to identify areas that need attention or maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Landlord Information" field.
3. Provide general information about the property, including its address, location, and any notable features.
4. Describe the building's overall condition, including its age, size, and any notable repairs or renovations.
5. Answer questions about the property's exterior, including its appearance, condition, and any notable features such as landscaping or outdoor spaces.
6. Provide information about the property's roof, including its condition, age, and any notable features such as skylights or chimneys.
7. Answer questions about the property's plumbing, including its condition, age, and any notable features such as water heaters or fixtures.
8. Provide information about the property's electrical system, including its condition, age, and any notable features such as outlets or lighting.
9. Answer questions about the property's heating system, including its condition, age, and any notable features such as thermostats or radiators.
10. Provide information about the property's cooling system, including its condition, age, and any notable features such as air conditioning units or vents.
11. Describe the property's flooring, including its type, condition, and any notable features such as carpeting or tile.
12. Answer questions about the property's pest control, including its condition, age, and any notable features such as termites or rodents.
13. Provide information about the property's safety features, including smoke detectors, carbon monoxide detectors, and emergency exit routes.
14. Answer questions about any other miscellaneous information not covered in the previous fields.
15. Enter any comments or observations about the property.
16. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information (general_info)**: Enter the property's address, location, and any notable features such as parking or amenities.
* **Building Information (building_info)**: Describe the building's overall condition, including its age, size, and any notable repairs or renovations.
* **Exterior Information (exterior_info)**: Describe the property's exterior appearance, condition, and any notable features such as landscaping or outdoor spaces.
* **Roof Information (roof_info)**: Provide information about the roof's condition, age, and any notable features such as skylights or chimneys.
* **Plumbing Information (plumbing_info)**: Answer questions about the plumbing system's condition, age, and any notable features such as water heaters or fixtures.
* **Electrical Information (electrical_info)**: Describe the electrical system's condition, age, and any notable features such as outlets or lighting.
* **Heating Information (heating_info)**: Answer questions about the heating system's condition, age, and any notable features such as thermostats or radiators.
* **Cooling Information (cooling_info)**: Provide information about the cooling system's condition, age, and any notable features such as air conditioning units or vents.
* **Flooring Information (flooring_info)**: Describe the flooring's type, condition, and any notable features such as carpeting or tile.
* **Pest Information (pest_info)**: Answer questions about the pest control system's condition, age, and any notable features such as termites or rodents.
* **Safety Information (safety_info)**: Provide information about the property's safety features, including smoke detectors, carbon monoxide detectors, and emergency exit routes.
* **Miscellaneous Information (miscellaneous_info)**: Answer questions about any other miscellaneous information not covered in the previous fields.
* **Tenant Information (tenant_info)**: Enter information about the current tenants, including their names, phone numbers, and lease terms.
* **Landlord Information (landlord_info)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Comments (comments)**: Enter any comments or observations about the property.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask a professional for assistance.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
* Update the form as needed based on any changes or issues that arise.
