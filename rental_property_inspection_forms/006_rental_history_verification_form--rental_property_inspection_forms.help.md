# Rental History Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Rental History Verification Form is used to gather information about a property's rental history. This includes details about the property, the tenant, and the verification process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form in a new browser tab or window.
2.  Enter the required information in each field.
3.  Ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
4.  Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info**: Enter the name and contact information of the applicant.
* **Rental History**: Enter the date of the rental agreement.
* **Property Info**: Enter the details about the property, such as its address and size.
* **Rental Application**: Enter the details about the rental application, such as the application date and status.
* **Verification Details**: Select the type of person or organization verifying the rental history (Landlord, Property Manager, Building Manager, or Property Management Company).
* **Verification Status**: Select the status of the verification (Verified, Not Verified).
* **Reason for Not Verified**: If the verification status is not verified, provide a reason for why it is not verified.
* **Verification Result**: Select the result of the verification (Approved, Denied, Conditional Approved).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the verification process.
* **Verification Date**: Enter the date of the verification.
* **Verified By**: Enter the name of the person or organization verifying the rental history.
* **Verified Name**: Enter the name of the person verifying the rental history.
* **Verified Title**: Enter the title of the person verifying the rental history.
* **Verified Agency**: Enter the name of the organization verifying the rental history.
* **Tenant Contact**: Enter the contact information of the tenant.
* **Property Status**: Select the status of the property (Active, Inactive, Unknown).
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Multi-Unit).
* **Property Size**: Enter the size of the property.
* **Property Year Built**: Enter the year the property was built.
* **Property Street**: Enter the street address of the property.
* **Property City**: Enter the city of the property.
* **Property State**: Enter the state of the property.
* **Property Zip**: Enter the zip code of the property.
* **Property Country**: Enter the country of the property.
* **Tenant Name**: Enter the name of the tenant.
