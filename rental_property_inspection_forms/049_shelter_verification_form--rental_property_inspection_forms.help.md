## Shelter Verification Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is used to verify the details of a shelter being considered for rental. This form will help assess the suitability of the shelter for rental purposes.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Shelter Verification Form" page with the required information.
2. Enter the contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Provide the shelter owner's information in the "Owner Info" field.
4. Enter the shelter's location in the "Shelter Location" field.
5. Describe the shelter in the "Shelter Description" field (optional).
6. Indicate whether the shelter has any inspection dates in the "Inspection Dates" field.
7. Specify the type of inspection that has been conducted in the "Inspection Type" field (optional).
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field (optional).
9. Enter the date the shelter was last checked in the "Date Checked" field (optional).

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shelter Verification Form** (<code>):** This is the title of the form.
* **Contact Info** (<code>contact_info_1</code>): Please enter the contact information of the shelter, including name, phone number, and email address.
* **Owner Info** (<code>shelter_owner_info</code>): Please enter the owner's information, including name and contact details.
* **Shelter Location** (<code>shelter_location</code>): Enter the location of the shelter, including address and any relevant geographical details.
* **Shelter Description** (<code>shelter_description</code>): Please provide a brief description of the shelter, including its amenities and any notable features.
* **Inspection Dates** (<code>inspection_dates</code>): Indicate whether the shelter has had any inspections or check-ups. 
* **Inspection Type** (<code>inspection_type</code>): Specify the type of inspection that has been conducted (optional).
* **Notes** (<code>notes</code>): Add any additional comments or information about the shelter (optional).
* **Date Checked** (<code>date_checked</code>): Enter the date the shelter was last checked (optional).
