# Property Investor Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by property investors to collect and manage contact information from individuals who want to rent a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property address that the individual is interested in renting.
2. Provide the contact information of the individual, including their name, phone number, and email address.
3. Enter the name and phone number of the contact person responsible for the inquiry.
4. Enter the email address of the contact person (if applicable).
5. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property that the individual is interested in renting. This is the location that you are looking to rent out.
* **Contact Information**: This is where you enter the details of the individual who is interested in renting the property.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and phone number of the person who is contacting you about the property.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person (if applicable). This will help you communicate with them more efficiently.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that you can contact the individual effectively.
* If you are already storing this information in another system, please do not duplicate it here.
* This form is not intended for collecting sensitive or confidential information.
