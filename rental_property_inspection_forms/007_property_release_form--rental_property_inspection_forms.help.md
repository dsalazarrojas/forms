# Property Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Release Form is designed to collect information about a rental property inspection, allowing tenants to provide details about themselves and the property in question.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the tenant.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email address.
3. Enter the name of the landlord or property manager.
4. Provide details about the agent or representative (if applicable).
5. Enter the address of the property.
6. Select the date and time of the release.
7. Confirm the release date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as the tenant.
* **Contact Info** (`tenant_contact_info`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can reach you.
* **Landlord** (`landlord_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the person in charge of the property.
* **Agent Info** (`agent_info`, text, optional): If there's an agent handling the property, please enter their information.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the property.
* **Date of Release** (`date_of_release`, date, optional): Select the date of the release.
* **Release Date & Time** (`release_date_time`, time, optional): Confirm the release date and time.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed to provide accurate data.
- Double-check the information you enter to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask the property manager or contact person for guidance.
