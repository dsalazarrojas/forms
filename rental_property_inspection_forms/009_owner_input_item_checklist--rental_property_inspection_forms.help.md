# Owner Input Item Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Owner Input Item Checklist" form is used by owners or property managers to input and verify the condition of a rental property. This form helps to track and document the items checked and verified during the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Input the property condition in the "property_condition" field.
3. Check the items that are present or verified during the inspection using the "items_checked" field.
4. Add any relevant comments or notes about the property in the "notes" field.
5. Confirm your verification status as an owner by selecting "Active" or "Inactive" in the "owner_verification_status" field.
6. Update the property status to "Active" or "Inactive" accordingly.
7. Provide next steps or further instructions in the "next_steps" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **property_condition**: Describe the overall condition of the property, including any notable issues or concerns.
* **items_checked**: List the items that are checked and verified during the inspection. Select "Yes" for present items and "No" for absent items.
* **notes**: Add any relevant comments or notes about the property, such as maintenance requests or concerns.
* **owner_confirmation**: Confirm your ownership status by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **owner_confirmation_date**: Date of ownership confirmation.
* **owner_confirmation_time**: Time of ownership confirmation.
* **items_verified**: Select "Yes" if all items are verified, "No" otherwise.
* **condition_comments**: Add any comments or notes about the property condition.
* **property_status**: Update the property status to "Active" or "Inactive" based on your verification.
* **owner_verification_status**: Confirm your verification status as an owner by selecting "Active" or "Inactive".
* **next_steps**: Provide next steps or further instructions.
* **items_not_verified**: List the items that are not verified during the inspection. Select "Yes" for absent items and "No" otherwise.
