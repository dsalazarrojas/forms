# Tenant Move In/Out Photo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Move In/Out Photo Submission form is designed to collect photos of a rental property at the time of a tenant's move-in or move-out. These photos will help property managers document the property's condition, identify potential issues, and ensure a smooth transition for both tenants and property owners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Property ID number to identify the specific property being inspected.
2. Select the Move In Date, the date of the tenant's move-in.
3. Select the Move Out Date, the date of the tenant's move-out.
4. Add photos of the property's current condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Move In Out Photo Submission**: A text field where you can optionally provide a brief description of the photo submission.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Property ID**: A text field to enter the property's unique identifier.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for tracking purposes.
* **Move In Date**: A date field to select the date of the tenant's move-in.
	+ This field is not required, but please use the correct date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Move Out Date**: A date field to select the date of the tenant's move-out.
	+ This field is not required, but please use the correct date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Photos**: A text field where you can upload photos of the property.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for visual documentation.

## Tips
* Be sure to provide clear and accurate photos of the property's current condition.
* If you're unsure about what information to provide, you can contact the property manager for guidance.
* The submission of this form is not a formal inspection, but rather a photo documentation to supplement official inspections.
