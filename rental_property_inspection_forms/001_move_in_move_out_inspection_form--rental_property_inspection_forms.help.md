# Move In Move Out Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Move In Move Out Inspection Form is a form used to document the details of a tenant's move-in and move-out experience. This form helps to collect essential information about the condition of a rental property at the time of move-in and move-out, including any damages or repairs that were observed. This form is typically used by property managers, landlords, or maintenance staff to facilitate the inspection and reporting process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the tenant's identification and contact details on the "Tenant Information" page.
2. Answer the "Property Details" questions to describe the condition of the property at move-in and move-out.
3. Document any damages or issues observed on the "Damage Information" page.
4. Provide details of any repairs or maintenance performed on the "Repair Information" page.
5. Upload photo evidence of the property's condition on the "Photo Evidence" page.
6. Provide additional information about move-in and move-out details on the "Move In Move Out Details" pages.
7. Sign the form with your signature on the "Tenant Signature" and "Inspector Signature" pages.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about the tenant's move-in and move-out experience.
* Use the "Photo Evidence" page to upload photos of the property's condition at move-in and move-out.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please contact your supervisor or manager for guidance.
* The Move In Move Out Inspection Form is a mandatory document for all tenants and property staff.
