## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users on job jingles. It may appear to be a template for multiple surveys, so please ensure you are filling out the correct one.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand what each page and field is asking for.
2. Fill out all required fields, denoted by a red asterisk (\*).
3. Select all applicable options for each select_multiple and select_one field.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
6. Enter a date in the Date field.
7. Enter a time in the Time field.
8. Select one option for the Rating field.
9. Enter any additional feedback in the text fields.
