# Short Answer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information from end-users about a product or service they have interacted with.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form and ensuring you understand what each section is asking for.
2. Answer each question or section with as much detail as you can provide.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses, but keep in mind that sensitive or personal information is not required.
4. If you're unsure about any question, contact your support team for clarification.
5. Once you've completed the form, review your responses for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction (1)**: This section provides a space for you to write a brief introduction to your feedback. This can include your thoughts, experiences, or any relevant context about the product or service you've interacted with.
* **Product Name (2)**: Please provide the name of the product or service you're giving feedback on. Try to be as specific as possible and avoid generic terms.
* **Product Category (3)**: Select the category that best describes the type of product or service you've interacted with. You can choose from the options provided or type in a custom category if it's not listed.
* **Contact Details (4)**: Please provide your contact information so we can follow up with you if needed. This can include your email, phone number, or any other relevant contact method.
* **Customer Feedback (5)**: Share your thoughts, concerns, or suggestions about the product or service. This is a space for you to provide any feedback, positive or negative, that will help us improve our service.
* **Improvement Areas (6)**: Identify areas where you think we could improve our product or service. Be as specific as possible with your suggestions.
* **Service Team (7)**: Indicate whether you would like to be contacted by our service team for follow-up or support. Select 'Yes' if you're interested, or 'No' if you're not.
* **Follow Up (8)**: If you would like to be contacted for follow-up or support, provide a date for us to schedule a follow-up.
* **Time (9)**: If you would like to provide a specific time for a follow-up, use this field to enter a time.
