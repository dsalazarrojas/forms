# Lifestyle Task Completion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lifestyle Task Completion Survey is a form designed to gather information about an individual's habits and completion of tasks. This survey is used to help understand an individual's habits and identify areas for improvement. It is a crucial tool for professionals, researchers, and individuals looking to understand their own habits and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Email (optional).
3. Answer the questions regarding your Task Completion Score, Habit Frequency Score, and Date of Last Completion (if applicable).
4. Select the frequency at which you complete habits (Habit Frequency).
5. Choose how often you follow through on tasks (Follow-Through).
6. Select any barriers you face while completing tasks (Barriers) (if applicable).
7. Select the level of support you have for completing tasks (Support).
8. Provide any comments you have about your habits and completion (Comments) (if applicable).
9. If you are planning to follow up on any progress or results (Follow-up Planned) (if applicable).
10. Select any next steps you would like to take (Next Steps) (if applicable).
11. Finally, select a follow-up date for any progress or results (Follow-up Date) (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to help us identify you in our database.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): This is an optional field to collect your email address if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Task Completion Score** (`task_completion_score`, `number`, required): Rate the score from 1-10 for the completion of tasks you were assigned to.
* **Habit Frequency Score** (`habit_frequency_score`, `number`, required): Rate the score from 1-10 for the frequency of habits you have.
* **Date of Last Completion** (`date_of_last_completion`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last task completion, if applicable.
* **Habit Frequency** (`habit_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate how often you complete habits. Select all that apply from: Rarely, Occasionally, Often, Very Often.
* **Follow-Through** (`follow_through`, `select_one`, required): Rate how often you follow through on tasks. Select one from: Rarely, Occasionally, Often, Very Often.
* **Barriers** (`barriers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply from: Motivation, Time, Habits, Motivation/Time, Motivation/Habits, Time/Habits, Motivation/Time/Habits.
* **Support** (`support`, `select_one`, required): Rate the level of support you receive. Select one from: None, Minimal, Moderate, Significant, High.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any comments about your habits and completion.
* **Follow-up Planned** (`follow_up_planned`, `select_one`, optional): Select one from: Yes, No.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply from: More Information, Additional Support, Referral, Next Steps.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for any planned follow-up.

Please note that all fields with `required: true` are mandatory and must be completed.
