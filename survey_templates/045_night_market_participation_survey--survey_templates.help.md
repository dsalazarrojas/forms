# Night Market Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Night Market Participation Survey is designed to gather information about users' experiences and feedback on a specific event held at night markets. This data will help event organizers better understand their audience and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Event Name" field with the name of the event you attended.
2. Select the correct date of the event from the calendar.
3. Fill in the "Event Time" field with the time you attended the event.
4. Choose how you attended the event by selecting one of the options: "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
5. If you have any feedback or rating for the event, fill in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Fill in the time you attended the event.
* **Attended** (`attended`, select_one, required): Choose how you attended the event:
	+ Option 1: I attended the event
	+ Option 2: I did not attend the event
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience from 1 to 10.
* **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`, text, optional): Provide a brief comment on your rating.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or improvements for future events.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates on future events.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Fill in your phone number for us to get in touch with you.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the event.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and honest as possible when filling in the form.
* If you have any suggestions or feedback, we appreciate your input.
* If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact us.
