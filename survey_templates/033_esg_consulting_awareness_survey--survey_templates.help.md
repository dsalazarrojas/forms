# ESG Consulting Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gauge your level of awareness about the Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) consulting services provided by the organization. Your responses will help us identify areas of improvement and tailor our training and support to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all the options that apply to each question.
3. Enter any additional comments or details where applicable.
4. Review and submit your form at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **ESG Familiarity** (Required: False): This question asks about your level of familiarity with the ESG consulting services. Please provide a brief description of your experience or understanding with the services.
* **ESG Topics** (Multiple Options, Required: False): Select all the topics related to ESG that you are familiar with. Please choose the options that apply to your work or experience.
* **ESG Challenges** (Required: False): If you have faced any challenges or obstacles while implementing ESG practices, please describe them in the space provided.
* **ESG Services** (Required: False): Select the ESG services that you are aware of or have used. This will help us understand the services that you need more training on.
* **ESG Awareness Level** (Required: False): Choose the level of awareness you have about ESG consulting services. This will help us gauge your understanding and identify areas for improvement.
* **ESG Implementation Level** (Required: False): Describe your current level of implementation of ESG practices within your department or team.
* **ESG Benchmarking** (Required: False): If you are involved in benchmarking ESG practices, please provide any relevant details or results.
* **ESG Sustainability** (Required: False): If you have any thoughts or comments about ESG sustainability, please share them with us.

Tips:
* Be honest and provide accurate information to help us better understand your needs and challenges.
* If you are not sure about any of the questions, please select the option "Not sure" or provide a brief explanation.
* Please note that your responses will be kept confidential and used only for internal training and support purposes.
