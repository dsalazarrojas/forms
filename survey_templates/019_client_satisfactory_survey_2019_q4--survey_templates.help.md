# Client Satisfactory Survey 2019 Q4 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from clients about their experience with your service during the 4th quarter of 2019. Your input will help us understand what we did well and what we can improve on, allowing us to provide better service in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your name and ID (if you have one) in the respective fields.
2. Choose the service you used from the dropdown menu.
3. Select the date you received the service.
4. Answer the satisfaction questions by selecting your level of satisfaction with our service.
5. Provide feedback on specific aspects of our service, such as quality, response time, communication, and problem resolution.
6. Select any additional services you would like us to provide in the future.
7. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Please provide your name for reference purposes.
* **Client ID** (`client_id`, text, optional): If you have a client ID, please provide it.
* **Which Service Did You Use** (`service_used`, select_one, required): Choose the service you used from the dropdown menu.
* **When Did You Receive Service** (`service_date`, date, required): Select the date you received the service.
* **Overall How Satisfied Are You with Our Service** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with our service.
* **How Would You Rate the Quality of Service** (`service_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of our service.
* **How Would You Rate Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate the value for money aspect of our service.
* **How Would You Rate Staff Knowledge and Expertise** (`staff_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the staff's knowledge and expertise.
* **How Would You Rate Staff Courtesy** (`staff_courtesy`, select_one, required): Rate the staff's courtesy.
* **How Would You Rate Response Time** (`response_time`, select_one, required): Rate the response time of our service.
* **How Well Was Your Issue Resolved** (`problem_resolution`, select_one, required): Rate the resolution of your issue.
* **How Would You Rate Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the communication quality of our service.
* **How Easy Was It to Work with Us** (`ease_of_working`, select_one, required): Rate how easy it was to work with us.
* **Did We Meet Your Expectations** (`met_expectations`, select_one, required): Rate whether we met your expectations.
* **How Well Did We Adhere to Timelines** (`timeline_adherence`, select_one, optional): Rate our adherence to timelines.
* **How Likely Are You to Use Our Services Again** (`likelihood_to_return`, select_one, required): Rate how likely you are to use our services again.
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend Us to Others** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, select_one, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend us to others.
* **How Do We Compare to Competitors** (`compared_to_competitors`, select_one, optional): Rate how we compare to competitors.
* **What Were You Most Satisfied With** (`most_satisfied_with`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to what you were most satisfied with.
* **What Were You Least Satisfied With** (`least_satisfied_with`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to what you were least satisfied with.
* **What Could We Improve** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Are There Additional Services You Would Like** (`additional_services_needed`, text, optional): Let us know if there are any additional services you would like us to provide.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions.
