# Medical Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect medical information from users, which can be used for various healthcare-related tasks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. If you're unsure about any question, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
3. Fill out the form completely, and do not leave any fields blank unless specified as optional.
4. If you have any medical issues, select all relevant options for the "medical_issues" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Diabetes** (`diabetes_survey`, text, required):
	+ This field is for entering a diagnosis of diabetes, if applicable.
	+ Please type in the details of your diabetes diagnosis, if applicable.
* **Medical Issues** (`health_issues`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field is for selecting any medical issues you may have.
	+ Choose all that apply from the following options:
		- option 1
		- option 2
		- option 3
* **Doctor Visits** (`doctor_visits`, number, optional):
	+ This field is for recording the number of doctor visits you've had in the past year.
	+ Please enter a whole number, as this field is not for decimal entries.
* **Medication** (`medication`, note, optional):
	+ This field is for taking notes about your current medication.
	+ Please include any relevant details about your current medication regimen.

## Tips

* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask a healthcare professional.
* Make sure to submit the form completely, as missing information can delay or affect the outcome of your healthcare process.
