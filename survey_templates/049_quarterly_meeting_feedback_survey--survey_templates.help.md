# Quarterly Meeting Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from attendees on a quarterly meeting. It's intended to gather feedback on the meeting's quality, identify action items, and document any notable events or discussions. By completing this form, attendees can provide valuable insights to improve future meetings and facilitate better communication within the team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Provide Feedback**: In the "Feedback" field, write a brief comment about your overall experience with the meeting. This section is optional, so feel free to skip it if you don't have any feedback to share.
2.  **Rate the Meeting**: Select a rating for the meeting, from 1 to 5, to indicate how productive and valuable you found it. This will help us understand your perception of the meeting's quality.
3.  **List Action Items**: In the "Action Items" field, select one or more options from the dropdown menu to indicate what action items you took away from the meeting. This will help us identify key takeaways and follow-up tasks.
4.  **Identify Attendees**: In the "Attendees" field, list any notable attendees who participated in the meeting. This is helpful for future reference and to ensure everyone's voices are included in the meeting's outcomes.
5.  **Date and Time**: Provide the date and time the meeting took place in the respective fields. This information helps us keep track of meeting schedules and ensures timely follow-up.
6.  **Location**: Specify where the meeting was held, so we can keep records of meeting locations for future reference.
7.  **Notes**: In the "Notes" field, add any additional comments or insights that didn't fit into the other fields. This is a great place to jot down any important details or questions that came up during the meeting.
8.  **Email and Phone**: If you have any contact information for the meeting, such as an email or phone number, please provide it in the respective fields. This will help us follow up with attendees and keep them informed about future meetings.
9.  **Follow-up Questions**: If you have any questions that require further discussion or clarification, please provide them in the "Follow-up questions" field. This helps us stay on top of any issues or concerns that arose during the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `text`, optional): Write a brief comment about your overall experience with the meeting.
*   **Rate the Meeting** (`Rate the meeting`, `number`, required): Select a rating for the meeting, from 1 to 5, to indicate how productive and valuable you found it.
*   **Action Items** (`Action Items`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more options from the dropdown menu to indicate what action items you took away from the meeting.
*   **Attendees** (`Attendees`, `text`, required): List any notable attendees who participated in the meeting.
*   **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Provide the date the meeting took place.
*   **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Specify the time the meeting occurred.
*   **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required): Indicate where the meeting was held.
*   **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or insights that didn't fit into the other fields.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): If you have an email address associated with the meeting, please provide it.
*   **Phone** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): If you have a phone number for the meeting, please provide it.
*   **Follow-up Questions** (`Follow-up questions`, `text`, optional): If you have any questions that require further discussion or clarification, please provide them.

## Tips

*   Be sure to complete the form with accurate and honest feedback.
*   Provide as much detail as possible for each field, so we can better understand the meeting's outcomes.
*   If you're unsure about any field, just ask, and we'll clarify it for you!
