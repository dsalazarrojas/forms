# Parent And Infant Activity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from parents or caregivers about their infant or toddler's activities. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions to help us better understand their experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, follow the instructions below for each page.
2.  Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What Is Your Age** (`parent_and_infant_survey`, `number`, required: false):
    This field is for you to enter your age.
*   **What Is The Age Of The Infant** (`infant_age`, `number`, required: false):
    This field is for you to enter the age of the infant or toddler.
*   **Have You Participated In Any Of The Activities Listed Above** (`have_you_participated_in_any_of_the_activities_listed_above`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Select all the activities that you have participated in with your infant or toddler, such as:
    *   Infant and Toddler Story Time
    *   Library Story Time
    *   Music and Movement Time
    *   Other (please describe)

## Tips

*   Take your time and answer each question carefully to ensure you provide accurate information.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
*   Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and help us better understand your experiences with your infant or toddler!
