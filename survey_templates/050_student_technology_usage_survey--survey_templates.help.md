# Student Technology Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about students' technology usage habits, including their online behavior, safety practices, and help-seeking habits.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the options that apply to your situation.
3. If a question has multiple options, select all that apply.
4. Fill in any required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
5. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your primary source of internet access?** (`tech_access`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all sources of internet access that apply to you, such as "Home", "School", "Library", or "Mobile network".
* **How familiar are you with technology?** (`tech_familiarity`, number)
	+ Answer with a numerical value indicating your level of familiarity with technology, on a scale of 1-10.
* **What technology devices do you own?** (`technology_devices_owned`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all devices you own, such as "Smartphone", "Laptop", "Tablet", or "Desktop".
* **Your email address** (`email`, email)
	+ Enter your email address as it appears on your records.
* **Your phone number** (`phone`, text)
	+ Enter your phone number as you wish to share it.
* **Additional comments** (`note`, note)
	+ Type any additional comments or remarks you'd like to share.
* **On average, how much time do you spend on a typical weekday using technology for school work?** (`tech_usage`, time)
	+ Enter the average time you spend on technology for school work on a typical weekday.
* **Do you use technology to help with your school security?** (`tech_security`, select_one)
	+ Select "Yes" if you use technology to help with school security, "No" otherwise.
* **What skills have you learned from technology?** (`tech_skills_used`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all skills you've learned from technology, such as "Coding", "Communication", or "Organization".
* **What skills do you use to stay safe online?** (`tech_skills_used`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all skills you use to stay safe online, such as "Password management", "Firewall settings", or "Online etiquette".
* **What technology tools do you use for online safety?** (`tech_security_protection`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all tools you use for online safety, such as "Anti-virus software", "Firewall", or "Parental controls".
* **What do you report about suspicious activity?** (`tech_security_protection2`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all actions you take when you report suspicious online activity, such as "Report to teacher", "Parent", or "School admin".
* **What do you do when you see a suspicious online post?** (`tech_security_protection3`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all actions you take when you see a suspicious online post, such as "Report it", "Block it", or "Delete it".
* **How do you deal with online harassment?** (`tech_security_protection4`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all ways you deal with online harassment, such as "Report it", "Talk to someone", or "Avoid it".
* **What skills have you learned from technology?** (`tech_skills_used`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all skills you've learned from technology, such as "Problem-solving", "Creativity", or "Time management".
* **On average, how many hours do you spend on a device?** (`tech_usage_hours`, number)
	+ Enter the average time you spend on a device on a typical weekday.
* **Do you get help from your parents or guardians when you use technology?** (`tech_support_2`, select_one)
	+ Select "Yes" if you get help from your parents or guardians when you use technology, "No" otherwise.
* **How many times do you use technology in a day?** (`tech_usage_frequency`, select_one)
	+ Select how many times you use technology in a day, such as "Multiple times", "Once", or "Rarely".
* **How do you usually get help when you need it with technology?** (`tech_support_3`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all ways you get help when you need it with technology, such as "Parent", "Teacher", "Online resource", or "Self-help".
* **What do you do when you have a tech problem?** (`tech_support_4`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all actions you take when you have a tech problem, such as "Ask a teacher", "Ask a peer", or "Online search".
* **How do you usually use technology?** (`tech_use`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all ways you usually use technology, such as "For school work", "For fun", or "For communication".
* **What do you think technology can improve about your school work?** (`techistency`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all things you think technology can improve about your school work, such as "Organization", "Creativity", or "Time management".
* **How does technology make your learning more useful?** (`tech_usefulness`, select_multiple)
	+ Select all ways technology makes your learning more useful, such as "Access to information", "Collaboration", or "Feedback".

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly to help us understand your technology usage habits.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification or seek help.
* Please note that this survey is for your school and will not be shared without your consent.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to type them in the "Additional comments" field.
