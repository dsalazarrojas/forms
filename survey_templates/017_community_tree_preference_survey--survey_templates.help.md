# Community Tree Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Tree Preference Survey is a tool to gather information from community members about their preferences and opinions on tree planting and maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select all that apply for questions that allow multiple selections.
3. Use the provided hints for guidance on each question.
4. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (ID: 1): Your name is optional but will help us address any follow-up questions.
* **Email Address** (ID: 2): Enter your email address for follow-up questions or to be contacted about survey results.
* **Your Neighborhood** (ID: 3): Select your neighborhood where you live.
* **Street or Area for Suggestion** (ID: 4): Provide a specific location where you'd like to see tree planting.
* **Current Tree Coverage** (ID: 5): Rate the current tree coverage in your area.
* **Are you interested in having more trees planted in your area?** (ID: 6): Express your level of interest in tree planting.
* **What types of trees would you prefer?** (ID: 7): Select all tree types you'd like to see planted.
* **What size trees would you prefer?** (ID: 8): Choose the size of trees you'd like to see planted (optional).
* **Where should trees be planted?** (ID: 9): Select all preferred locations for tree planting.
* **What is the most important benefit of trees to you?** (ID: 10): Identify the primary reason why trees are important.
* **Other important benefits** (ID: 11): Select all benefits of trees that are important to you.
* **Do you have any concerns about tree maintenance?** (ID: 12): Express your concerns about tree maintenance.
* **Please describe your maintenance concerns** (ID: 13): Provide specific concerns about tree maintenance.
* **Would you be interested in volunteering for tree planting?** (ID: 14): Show your interest in community involvement.
* **If yes, how could you help?** (ID: 15): Select all ways you'd be willing to contribute to tree planting.
* **How important is tree planting compared to other community needs?** (ID: 16): Rate the priority level of tree planting in your community.
* **Would you be willing to contribute financially to tree planting?** (ID: 17): Express your willingness to donate or sponsor trees.
* **If yes, what amount would you consider?** (ID: 18): Choose your financial contribution level (optional).
* **How knowledgeable are you about tree care?** (ID: 19): Assess your knowledge level about tree care.
* **Would you be interested in tree care workshops?** (ID: 20): Show your interest in educational opportunities.
* **Any additional comments or suggestions?** (ID: 21): Share your thoughts on tree planting and maintenance.
* **Survey Completion Date** (ID: 22): This will be automatically recorded as the date you completed the survey.

Note: All questions are required unless specified as optional, and some may have multiple answers or selections. Review each question carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and thoughtful responses.
