# AI Chatbot Name Suggestions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect suggestions for an AI chatbot's name from various contributors. The goal is to gather diverse ideas and opinions to identify a suitable name for the AI chatbot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form instructions and fields carefully.
2. For each proposed name, provide a brief reasoning statement explaining why you chose that name.
3. Select the tone of your proposed name from the provided options.
4. Evaluate the memorability, pronunciation, and potential keywords to avoid for each name.
5. Compare your suggested names with the existing internal shortlist and choose the one you like most.
6. Provide any additional visual or logo ideas you associate with the name.
7. If you want to follow up on your suggestion, provide your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contributor Name** (`contributor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name or identity.
* **Proposed Chatbot Name (Suggestion 1)** (`name_suggestion_1`, `text`, required/true): Provide a name for the chatbot.
* **Why did you choose this name?** (`suggestion_1_reasoning`, `text`, required/true): Explain the reasoning behind your name choice.
* **Tone of Suggestion 1** (`suggestion_1_tone`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the tone you associate with the name.
* **Proposed Chatbot Name (Suggestion 2)** (`name_suggestion_2`, `text`, optional/true): Provide an alternative name.
* **Reasoning for Suggestion 2** (`suggestion_2_reasoning`, `text`, optional/true): Explain the reasoning behind your alternative name choice (if provided).
* **Who is the target audience for this name?** (`target_audience_perception`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select the target audience for the chatbot.
* **Memorability Rating** (`name_memorability_rating`, `number`, required/true): Rate the name's memorability from 1 (hard) to 5 (very easy).
* **Is the name easy to pronounce?** (`pronunciation_difficulty`, `select_one`, required/true): Evaluate the name's pronunciation difficulty.
* **Keywords or themes to AVOID** (`avoid_keywords`, `text`, optional/true): Enter any keywords or themes that you would like to avoid in the name.
* **Suggested Tagline / Slogan** (`potential_tagline`, `text`, optional/true): Provide a tagline or slogan associated with the name.
* **Feedback on Current Internal Shortlist** (`existing_options_feedback_header`, `note`, optional/true): Evaluate the existing internal suggestions.
* **Which of these existing names do you like most?** (`favorite_from_list`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose the most liked name from the existing shortlist.
* **Visual/Logo Ideas** (`logo_visualization`, `text`, optional/true): Describe any visual or logo concepts associated with the name.
* **Contact Email (If we want to follow up on your suggestion)** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional/true): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your suggestion.
