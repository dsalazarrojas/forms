# Institutional Sustainability Goals Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to evaluate the level of awareness and engagement of institutional employees with sustainability goals, identifying areas where more support is needed to promote a culture of sustainability within the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them honestly to provide accurate information.
2. For questions with options, select all that apply.
3. Fill in the required fields with your actual information (e.g., email address for question 7).
4. Ensure you answer all required questions, marked as "Required: true".
5. Click submit to complete and submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the survey** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This section introduces the survey and its purpose.
* **What do you think are the main reasons why we should have a sustainability awareness campaign?** (`awareness`, `text`, not required): Please provide a brief answer explaining your thoughts on the importance of a sustainability awareness campaign.
* **How engaged are you in sustainability activities?** (`staff_engagement`, `select_multiple`, not required): Indicate how much you are engaged in sustainability activities, choosing all that apply:
	+ Option 1: Not at all
	+ Option 2: Somewhat
	+ Option 3: Very much
* **Which of the following channels would you prefer to receive sustainability information?** (`channels`, `select_one`, not required): Select one way to receive sustainability information:
	+ Option 1: Email
	+ Option 2: Social media
	+ Option 3: Meetings and talks
* **How would you like to receive follow-up information on sustainability?** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, not required): Choose one way to receive follow-up information:
	+ Option 1: Email
	+ Option 2: Quarterly reports
	+ Option 3: Newsletters
* **How do you prefer to communicate about sustainability?** (`communication`, `text`, not required): Please provide a brief answer explaining how you prefer to communicate about sustainability.
* **Do you prefer emails for sustainability information?** (`email`, `email`, not required): If you prefer to receive sustainability information via email, please provide your actual email address (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, not required): If you prefer to receive information on a specific date, please provide it in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2024-02-15).
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, not required): If you prefer to receive information at a specific time, please provide it in the format HH:MM (e.g., 14:30).
* **Date**: This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Time**: This field is not applicable for this form.
