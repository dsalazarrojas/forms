# Retail Holiday Customer Experience Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience at a retail store. This feedback will help the store management to identify areas of improvement and provide better services to their customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the "Overall Rating" field to rate your overall experience at the store.
2. Fill in the "Customer Name" field with your name.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Answer each question on the subsequent pages, rating your experience according to the options provided.
5. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Location**: Enter the location of the store you visited.
* **Visit Time**: Enter the time you visited the store.
* **Store Experience**: Rate your overall experience at the store using the number field.
* **Staff Interaction**: Rate the interaction with the staff at the store.
	+ Choose a rating between "Excellent", "Good", "Neutral", and "Poor".
* **Product Quality**: Rate the quality of the products available at the store.
	+ Select all applicable options.
* **Product Availability**: Rate the availability of products at the store.
	+ Choose a rating between "In stock", "Out of stock", and "Not applicable".
* **Store Atmosphere**: Rate the atmosphere of the store.
* **Staff Knowledge**: Rate the knowledge of the staff at the store.
	+ Choose a rating between "Excellent", "Good", "Neutral", and "Poor".
* **Return Policy**: Enter the store's return policy.
* **Customer Service**: Rate the customer service at the store.
	+ Choose a rating between "Excellent", "Good", "Neutral", and "Poor".
* **Store Location**: Enter the store's location.
* **Product Quality Rating**: Rate the quality of the products.
* **Staff Interaction 2**: Rate the interaction with the staff at the store (this is a duplicate of the "Staff Interaction" field, please skip this field).
* **Staff Knowledge 2**: Rate the knowledge of the staff at the store (this is a duplicate of the "Staff Knowledge" field, please skip this field).
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience at the store.
* **Staff Appearance 2**: Rate the appearance of the staff at the store (this is a duplicate of the "Staff Appearance" field, please skip this field).
* **Product Availability 2**: Rate the availability of products at the store (this is a duplicate of the "Product Availability" field, please skip this field).
* **Store Atmosphere 2**: Enter the store's atmosphere (this is a duplicate of the "Store Atmosphere" field, please skip this field).
* **Staff Knowledge 3**: Rate the knowledge of the staff at the store (this is a duplicate of the "Staff Knowledge" field, please skip this field).
* **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall experience at the store.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name.

Note: The fields labeled with "2" are duplicates and can be skipped. 

Tips: Please make sure to answer all the questions honestly and provide detailed feedback to help the store management improve their services.
