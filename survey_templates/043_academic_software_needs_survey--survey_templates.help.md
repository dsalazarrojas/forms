# Academic Software Needs Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their needs and preferences regarding academic software. It's a survey to help us better understand the current state of software usage in academic settings and identify areas where new software development can be beneficial.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the questions according to your role and institution's needs. If you're not sure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification or provide additional comments where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Your Role in the Institution

* **Your Role in the Institution** (`respondent_role`, `select_one`, required): Please select the primary role you hold in the institution. This will help us understand the different needs and preferences of various roles.

### Department or College

* **Department or College** (`department_college`, `text`, required): Please enter your primary department or college affiliation. This will help us understand the specific needs and requirements of different departments and colleges.

### Primary Software Needs

* **Primary Software Needs** (`primary_software_needs`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the types of software you need most. This could include statistical analysis, data visualization, programming environments, reference management, content management, video conferencing, document collaboration, or other.

### Current Software Satisfaction

* **Current Software Satisfaction** (`current_software_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Please rate your satisfaction with the current software available on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.

### Software Access Frequency

* **Software Access Frequency** (`software_access_frequency`, `number`, required): Please rate how often you need access to specialized software on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is never and 5 is very often.

### Software Training Needs

* **Software Training Needs** (`software_training_needs`, `number`, required): Please rate how much training you need for new software on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is none and 5 is extensive.

### Collaboration Software Importance

* **Collaboration Software Importance** (`collaboration_software_importance`, `number`, required): Please rate how important collaborative software is to your work on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not at all and 5 is very important.

### Mobile Access Importance

* **Mobile Access Importance** (`mobile_access_importance`, `number`, required): Please rate how important mobile access is to your work on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not at all and 5 is very important.

### Cloud-Based Preference

* **Cloud-Based Preference** (`cloud_based_preference`, `number`, required): Please rate your preference for cloud-based or locally installed software on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is locally installed and 5 is cloud-based.

### Current Software Limitations

* **Current Software Limitations** (`current_software_limitations`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the limitations of your current software. This could include cost, functionality, usability, integration, performance, support, and updates.

### Budget Constraint Impact

* **Budget Constraint Impact** (`budget_constraint_impact`, `number`, required): Please rate how much budget constraints affect your software access on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not at all and 5 is very much.

### Software Recommendation Priority

* **Software Recommendation Priority** (`software_recommendation_priority`, `text`, required): Please enter your top priority for new software acquisition.

### Integration Requirements

* **Integration Requirements** (`integration_requirements`, `text`, required): Please enter any existing systems that new software must integrate with.

### Security and Compliance Needs

* **Security and Compliance Needs** (`security_compliance_needs`, `text`, required): Please enter any security or compliance requirements that software must meet.

### Software Evaluation Criteria

* **Software Evaluation Criteria** (`software_evaluation_criteria`, `text`, required): Please enter the most important criteria when evaluating new software.

### Additional Comments

* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required): Please enter any other thoughts about software needs at your institution.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be completed before submitting the form.
