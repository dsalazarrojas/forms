# Townhouse Development Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from residents about their experiences and expectations in the community. Your input will help us improve our services and amenities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your resident name in the "Resident Name" field.
2. Provide the name of your community in the "Community Name" field.
3. Share your feedback in the "Feedback" field.
4. Rate your satisfaction with our community by selecting a number from 1 to 5 in the "Satisfaction Rating" field.
5. If you have any suggestions or comments, enter them in the "Suggestions" field.
6. If you'd like to be contacted for further discussions, provide your contact information in the "Contact Information", "Email", "Phone", and "Address" fields.
7. If you'd like to add a photo of yourself, you can do so in the "Resident Photo (optional)" field.
8. Indicate your relationship with the community board by selecting "Community Board Member", "Non-Board Member", or "Other" in the "Board Member" field.
9. If you're a member of a committee, select the relevant committee(s) you're a part of in the "Committee Member" fields.
10. Let us know if you have a pet by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Has Pet" field.
11. If you have children, indicate their presence by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Has Child" field.
12. If you own or lease a vehicle, select "True" or "False" in the "Has Vehicle" field.
13. If you have a vehicle, specify its type in the "Type of Vehicle" field.
14. Indicate your residential status by selecting "Renter" or "Owner" in the "Residential Status" field.
15. If you have a lease, indicate its truth by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Lease" field.
16. If you have a lease, specify its type in the "Type of Lease" field.
17. If you have a lease term, select the relevant term in the "Lease Term" field.
18. If you have a lease rent, enter the rent amount in the "Lease Rent" field.
19. If you have a lease rent per square foot, enter the rent amount per square foot in the "Rent per Sqft" field.
20. If you'd like to suggest or recommend any community features, select the relevant feature(s) in the "Community Features" field.
21. If you'd like to suggest or recommend any board meeting frequency, select the relevant frequency in the "Board Meetings" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your lease or rental agreement.
* **Community Name (Text, Required)**: Type the name of your community.
* **Feedback (Text, Required)**: Share your thoughts, comments, or suggestions about your experience in the community.
* **Satisfaction Rating (Number, Required)**: Rate your satisfaction with our community on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is dissatisfied and 5 is satisfied.
* **Suggestions (Text, Optional)**: If you have any suggestions or comments, enter them here.
* **Contact Information (Email, Optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted for further discussions, provide your email address.
* **Email (Email, Optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted for further discussions, provide your email address.
* **Phone (Text, Optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
* **Address (Text, Optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted at your home address, enter it here.
* **Resident Photo (Text, Optional)**: If you'd like to add a photo of yourself, upload it here.
* **Board Member (Select One, Optional)**: Indicate your relationship with the community board by selecting "Community Board Member", "Non-Board Member", or "Other".
* **Committee Member (Select Multiple, Optional)**: If you're a member of a committee, select the relevant committee(s) you're a part of.
* **Has Pet (Select One, Optional)**: Let us know if you have a pet.
* **Has Child (Select One, Optional)**: Indicate if you have children.
* **Has Vehicle (Select One, Optional)**: Let us know if you own or lease a vehicle.
* **Type of Vehicle (Select Multiple, Optional)**: If you own or lease a vehicle, specify its type.
* **Residential Status (Select One, Optional)**: Indicate your residential status by selecting "Renter" or "Owner".
* **Lease (Select One, Optional)**: Indicate if you have a lease.
* **Type of Lease (Select Multiple, Optional)**: If you have a lease, specify its type.
* **Lease Term (Select Multiple, Optional)**: If you have a lease term, select the relevant term.
* **Lease Rent (Number, Optional)**: If you have a lease rent, enter the rent amount.
* **Rent per Sqft (Number, Optional)**: If you have a lease rent per square foot, enter the rent amount per square foot.
* **Community Features (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Suggest or recommend any community features you think we should consider.
* **Board Meetings (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Suggest or recommend any board meeting frequency you think would be beneficial.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback, we value your input!
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* We appreciate your time and input, thank you for helping us improve our community!
