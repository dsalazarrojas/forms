# Supplier Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from suppliers about their business and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the page that corresponds to the section of the survey you want to fill out (Supplier Survey, Contact Info, Shipping Info, Payment Terms, etc.).
2. Fill out the required and optional fields on each page.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field.
4. Once you've filled out all the pages, click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Survey** (Supplier Survey, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your business and services offered.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required/optional): Enter your company name, contact person, email, phone number, and website.
* **Shipping Info** (Shipping Info, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address, including your business name, address line 1, address line 2, city, state, zip, country, and contact person.
* **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms, text, required/optional): Describe your payment terms, including payment methods, payment deadlines, and any late payment fees.
* **Order Details** (Order Details, text, required/optional): Enter the product or service details you offer, including product code, product name, and order quantity.
* **Delivery Info** (Delivery Info, text, required/optional): Enter the delivery information for your products, including delivery time, delivery method, and delivery contact person.
* **Payment Options** (Payment Options, text, required/optional): Describe your payment options, including credit card, wire transfer, and other payment methods.
* **Supplier Terms** (Supplier Terms, text, required/optional): Enter any terms and conditions that apply to your business, including contract terms and cancellation policies.
* **Order Terms** (Order Terms, text, required/optional): Enter any terms and conditions that apply to your business, including product liability and return policies.
* **Delivery Terms** (Delivery Terms, text, required/optional): Enter any terms and conditions that apply to your business, including delivery schedules and deadlines.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to double check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* This form is for legitimate business purposes only.
