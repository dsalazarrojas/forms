# Digital Product Prototype Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and assessments on digital product prototypes. It helps stakeholders to gauge the overall satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by filling out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*). For non-required fields, you can choose to fill out as much or as little information as you like.
2. Begin by providing a brief title for the product prototype, and then enter the URL where it can be accessed.
3. If applicable, enter the version number of the product prototype.
4. You can upload or provide a link to an image of the product prototype.
5. For the rating section, select one or more of the provided options (Good, Fair, or Poor) to indicate your overall assessment.
6. In the comments section, provide any additional feedback or comments you have about the product prototype.
7. If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them in the suggested improvements field.
8. For the concerns field, enter any concerns or issues you have with the product prototype.
9. In the additional comments field, provide any further comments or observations you have about the product prototype.
10. Enter your overall satisfaction with the product prototype in the satisfaction section, using one of the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
11. If applicable, enter the team size for the product prototype.
12. Indicate whether you have any feedback on the design or functionality of the product prototype.
13. Select whether you have any feedback on the functionality of the product prototype.
14. Finally, provide any contact information or next steps you'd like to see taken on the product prototype.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **product prototype title** (text): Enter a brief title for the product prototype.
* **product prototype url** (text): Enter the URL where the product prototype can be accessed.
* **product prototype version** (number): If applicable, enter the version number of the product prototype.
* **product prototype image** (text): You can upload or provide a link to an image of the product prototype.
* **rating** (select multiple): Select one or more of the provided options (Good, Fair, or Poor) to indicate your overall assessment.
* **comments** (text): Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about the product prototype.
* **suggestions** (text): Enter any suggestions for improvement.
* **concerns** (text): Enter any concerns or issues you have with the product prototype.
* **overall satisfaction** (select one): Enter one of the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor) to indicate your overall satisfaction.
* **team name** (text): Enter the name of the team who created the product prototype.
* **team size** (number): If applicable, enter the size of the team.
* **email** (email): Enter the email address where you can be contacted.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **date** (date): Enter the date you reviewed the product prototype.
* **time** (time): Enter the time you reviewed the product prototype.
* **time zone** (select one): Select the time zone where you reviewed the product prototype.
* **feedback on design** (select multiple): Indicate whether you have any feedback on the design of the product prototype.
* **feedback on functionality** (select multiple): Select whether you have any feedback on the functionality of the product prototype.

* **next steps** (text): Enter any next steps you'd like to see taken on the product prototype.
