# Beauty Event Format Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information about your preferences for beauty events, including in-person and virtual formats. We want to understand your preferences to better tailor our future events to meet your needs and interests. Your participation will help us create more engaging and relevant experiences for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select your preferred format type for events (in-person, virtual, hybrid) and the benefits that come with it.
3. Choose your preferred activities, duration, and time of day for events.
4. Let us know how far you are willing to travel for in-person events and what accommodation needs you may have.
5. Indicate your budget tolerance for event attendance.
6. Share your comfort level with virtual event technology.
7. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional)
Your name will be kept confidential and only used for communication purposes.

*   Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.

### **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional)
We'll use this to contact you about future events and updates.

*   Enter a valid email address.

### **Type of Attendee** (`attendee_type`, `select_one`, required)
What kind of attendee are you?

*   Choose one: Professional, Student, Beauty Enthusiast, Media, Vendor, Other

### **Beauty Event Attendance Frequency** (`event_attendance_frequency`, `select_one`, required)
How often do you attend beauty events?

*   Choose one: Never, Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently, Very Frequently

### **Preferred Event Format** (`format_preferences_header`, `select_one`, required)
Which event format do you prefer?

*   Choose one: In-Person Only, Virtual Only, Hybrid, No Preference

### **Preferred Event Activities** (`preferred_activities`, `select_multiple`, required)
What activities do you prefer at events?

*   Choose multiple: Product Demonstrations, Workshops, Networking, Speed Dating, Panel Discussions, Hands-on Experiences, Keynote Speeches

### **Preferred Event Duration** (`event_duration_preference`, `select_one`, required)
How long would you like events to last?

*   Choose one: Half Day, Full Day, Multi-day, Flexible

### **Preferred Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, `select_one`, required)
What time of day do you prefer events to take place?

*   Choose one: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekend, Flexible

### **Willingness to Travel Distance** (`transportation_distance`, `select_one`, required)
How far are you willing to travel for in-person events?

*   Choose one: Prefer Local, Under 50 miles, Under 100 miles, National, International

### **Accommodation Needs** (`accommodation_needs`, `text`, optional)
What accommodation needs do you have for in-person events?

*   Provide any necessary information for us to consider.

### **Budget Tolerance** (`budget_tolerance`, `select_one`, required)
What is your budget tolerance for event attendance?

*   Choose one: Free Only, Under 50 dollars, 50-100 dollars, 100-200 dollars, 200+ dollars

### **Comfort with Virtual Event Technology** (`technology_comfort`, `select_one`, required)
How comfortable are you with virtual event technology?

*   Choose one: Not Comfortable, Somewhat Comfortable, Comfortable, Very Comfortable

### **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional)
Do you have any accessibility needs for events?

*   Provide any necessary information for us to consider.

### **Topics or Products of Most Interest** (`content_topics`, `text`, required)
What topics or products are you most interested in at events?

*   Provide a list of your preferred topics or products.

### **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional)
Any additional comments or suggestions you may have?

*   Share your thoughts with us.

By providing accurate and honest answers, you'll help us create more engaging and relevant events for you. Thank you for your participation!
