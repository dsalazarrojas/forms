# Plant Preference Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Plant Preference Quiz is a survey designed to gather information about your preferences for plant sales. Your input will help us understand what type of plants you are interested in, how you prefer to purchase them, and what features are important to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, as it applies to you.
2. Select the options that best match your preferences.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a star *).
4. Submit your answers to complete the form.
