# Website User Experience Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from website visitors about their experience on our website. The information gathered will help us understand how often users visit, find, and interact with our website, and what areas need improvement. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions to help us better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best fit your experiences and provide any additional information requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you visit our website?** (`user_experience`, text, optional)
  This field asks about your frequency of visiting our website. Answer with a brief description, e.g., "Regularly", "Occasionally", "Rarely", or "Never".
* **Rate your experience** (`website_satisfaction`, number, optional)
  Please rate your overall satisfaction with our website on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".
* **How easy was it to use?** (`website_usability`, select_one, optional)
  Choose how easy or difficult it was to use our website from the following options: Very easy, Somewhat easy, Neutral, Somewhat difficult, Very difficult.
* **How easy was it to find what you were looking for?** (`website_usability_2`, select_one, optional)
  Choose how easy or difficult it was to find the information you needed on our website from the following options: Very easy, Somewhat easy, Neutral, Somewhat difficult, Very difficult.
* **How would you rate the appearance of our website?** (`website_appearance`, select_multiple, optional)
  Choose all that apply to rate our website's appearance from the following options: Simple and clean, Well-designed, Unattractive, Cluttered.
* **How would you rate the content on our website?** (`website_content`, select_multiple, optional)
  Choose all that apply to rate our website's content from the following options: Well-organized, Interesting, Boring, Unhelpful.
* **How easy was it to navigate?** (`website_navigation`, select_one, optional)
  Choose how easy or difficult it was to navigate our website from the following options: Very easy, Somewhat easy, Neutral, Somewhat difficult, Very difficult.
* **Would you like to provide an email address?** (`email`, email, optional)
  If you'd like to stay updated on our latest news and updates, please provide your email address. You can leave this blank if you'd rather not.
* **Would you like to provide a phone number?** (`phone`, text, optional)
  If you'd like to provide feedback via a phone call, please enter your phone number. You can leave this blank if you'd rather not.
* **What date did you visit our website?** (`date`, date, optional)
  If you'd like to provide the date of your last visit, please enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
