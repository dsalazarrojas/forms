# Public Opinion On Law Enforcement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather public opinion on law enforcement, aiming to collect information on people's trust levels, safety perception, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer accurately.
- Use numbers for rating questions.
- Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
- Enter valid contact information (e.g., name, title, role) in the required fields.
- Fill out the form completely and correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. How would you rate your trust level with law enforcement?** (`how_would_you_rate_your_trust_level_with_law_enforcement`, number, required): Please rate your trust level with law enforcement using numbers (e.g., 1-10).
* **2. Do you feel safe in your neighborhood?** (`do_you_feel_safe_in_your_neighborhood`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to express your safety perception.
* **3. What do you think is the main reason for lack of trust?** (`what_do_you_think_is_the_main_reason_for_lack_of_trust`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the options.
* **4. Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (`do_you_have_any_suggestions_for_improvement`, text, required): Provide text-based answers for suggestions.
* **5. (Note: Contact Information)** (`contact_info`, note, required): Enter your contact information (e.g., name, title, role).
* **6. E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address (if applicable).
* **7. Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your valid phone number (if applicable).
* **8. Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter a date in the format 'mm/dd/yyyy' (if applicable).
* **9. Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter a time in the format 'hh:mm AM/PM' (if applicable).
* **10. How would you rate your experience with law enforcement?** (`how_do_you_rate_your_experience_with_law_enforcement`, number, required): Please rate your experience with law enforcement using numbers (e.g., 1-10).
* **11. Do you agree or disagree with the statements below?** (`do_you_agree_or_disagree_with_the_statements_below`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each statement.
* **12. (No specific label)** (`statements`, text, optional): Enter your thoughts or comments regarding the statements.
* **13. Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter your name (e.g., John Doe).
* **14. Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter your title (e.g., Police Officer).
* **15. Role** (`role`, text, required): Enter your role (e.g., Law Enforcement Officer).
* **16. E-mail 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address (if applicable).

* **20. Contact Information 2** (`contact_info_2`, note, optional): Enter your contact information (if applicable).
* **21. Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **22. Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter your thoughts or comments.
