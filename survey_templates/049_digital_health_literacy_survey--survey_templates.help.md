# digital_health_literacy_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on an individual's health literacy. It is essential to complete this form accurately to provide reliable data for research or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill in the required fields. Each page is designed to collect specific data, so please answer the questions accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Page**: Please select your answer to the first question, which is to indicate if you have any experience with online health care services. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Participant Name**: Please enter your name as it should appear in the survey results. This is an optional field, but it's recommended for your own identification purposes.
* **Age**: Please enter your age in years. This field is optional, but it's essential to have a correct age for accurate representation of your data.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address. This field is optional, but it's recommended for your own identification purposes.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number. This field is optional, but it's recommended for your own identification purposes.
* **Date Of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This field is optional, but it's essential to have a correct date of birth for accurate representation of your data.
* **Medical Condition**: Please indicate if you have any medical conditions. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Health Care Provider**: Please select all the healthcare providers that have treated you. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No' for each provider.
* **Medication**: Please indicate if you take any medication. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Doctor Specialty**: Please indicate the specialty of your doctor. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Hospital**: Please indicate if you have been to a hospital. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Insurance**: Please indicate if you have insurance. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medical History**: Please select all the medical history that applies to you. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No' for each history.
* **Family History**: Please select all the family medical history that applies to you. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No' for each history.
* **Doctor Visits**: Please indicate if you have visited a doctor recently. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medical Test**: Please indicate if you have undergone any medical test. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medical Result**: Please indicate the result of your medical test. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medical Record**: Please indicate if you have a medical record. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Doctor Visit History**: Please select all the doctor visit history that applies to you. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No' for each history.
* **Doctor Recommendation**: Please indicate if you have a doctor's recommendation. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Doctor Consultation**: Please indicate if you have had a doctor consultation. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Doctor Consultation Type**: Please indicate the type of doctor consultation you had. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Doctor Consultation Time**: Please enter the time spent during your doctor consultation. This field is optional.
* **Doctor Consultation Date**: Please enter the date of your doctor consultation. This field is optional.
* **Doctor Consultation Frequency**: Please indicate the frequency of your doctor consultations. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.

Please ensure to complete each field accurately and honestly, as this data will be used for research or other purposes. If you're unsure about any question, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
