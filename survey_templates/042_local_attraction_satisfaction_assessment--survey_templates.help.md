# Local Attraction Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors of a local attraction, allowing us to understand their experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "what-is-your-name" field.
2. Share your experience of the tour in the "what-was-the-tour-experience-like" field.
3. Rate the guides on a scale from 1 to 5 in the "how-would-you-rate-the-guides" field.
4. Describe the facilities in the "what-were-the-facilities-like" field.
5. Choose an overall experience rating from the options provided in the "overall-experience" field.
6. Indicate if you have any recommendations for the attraction in the "have-you-got-any-recommendations" field.
7. Optionally, provide your email address in the "email" field.
8. Optionally, provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
9. Add any additional comments or notes in the "note" field.
10. If you'd like to contact us, choose from the available options in the "contact-us" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what-is-your-name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **what-was-the-tour-experience-like** (text, optional): Describe your experience of the tour, including any notable events or highlights.
* **how-would-you-rate-the-guides** (select multiple, optional): Rate the guides on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **what-were-the-facilities-like** (text, optional): Describe the facilities, including their cleanliness and availability.
* **overall-experience** (select one, optional): Choose one of the provided options to rate your overall experience.
	+ very-good: Very satisfying
	+ good: Satisfying
	+ fair: Average
	+ poor: Dissatisfying
	+ very-poor: Unsatisfying
* **have-you-got-any-recommendations** (select multiple, optional): Indicate if you have any recommendations for the attraction.
* **email** (email, optional): Optionally, provide your email address for future contact.
* **phone** (text, optional): Optionally, provide your phone number for future contact.
* **note** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **contact-us** (select one, optional): Choose how you would like to contact us, including by email, phone, or none.
	+ email: Contact us via email.
	+ phone: Contact us via phone.
	+ none: No contact preference.
