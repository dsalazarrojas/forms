# Corporate Sustainability Maturity Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Sustainability Maturity Survey is designed to evaluate a company's commitment to corporate sustainability, covering various aspects such as social, environmental, and governance (ESG) performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page and read the hints provided for each field to understand what information is required.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions and hints.
3. Ensure you answer all required fields (indicated by the "required: true" label).
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1, Not Required)
	+ A brief overview of the survey and its purpose.
* **Domain** (Page 2, Not Required)
	+ Describe your company's domain or industry.
* **Sustainability Management** (Page 3, Required)
	+ Describe your approach to sustainability management, including any policies, procedures, and goals.
* **Human Rights** (Page 4, Required)
	+ Select the level of importance you assign to human rights in your company's operations:
		+ High
		+ Medium
		+ Low
		+ Not Applicable
* **Environmental Impact** (Page 5, Required)
	+ Select all applicable ways the company measures its environmental impact:
		+ Greenhouse Gas Emissions
		+ Water Usage
		+ Energy Consumption
		+ Waste Management
		+ Recycling
* **Stakeholder Engagement** (Page 6, Required)
	+ Describe how your company engages with stakeholders to promote transparency and accountability.
* **Social and Ethical** (Page 7, Required)
	+ Select all applicable social and ethical priorities for your company:
		+ Diversity and Inclusion
		+ Human Rights
		+ Fair Labor Practices
		+ Supply Chain Responsibility
* **Community** (Page 8, Required)
	+ Describe how your company contributes to the well-being of local communities.
* **Challenges** (Page 9, Required)
	+ Select all applicable challenges your company faces in achieving its sustainability goals:
		+ Lack of clear objectives
		+ Limited resources
		+ Lack of clear policies
		+ Lack of clear communication
		+ Lack of clear metrics
* **Submission** (Page 10, Required)
	+ Select the submission option:
		+ Submit
		+ Save Draft
		+ Cancel

## Tips

* Ensure you answer all required fields carefully and accurately.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they reflect your company's current state of sustainability maturity.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
