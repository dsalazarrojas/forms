# Swim Wear Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Swim Wear Survey is a form designed to collect information from users about their preferences and opinions on swimwear items. This form is intended to gather data on individual items, material, size range, color scheme, and satisfaction level. Please answer the questions honestly to help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your information. You can select one or multiple options for each field as needed. If you have any comments or questions, please use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name**: A field for you to enter your name.
* **What type of swimwear item do you like?**: A field to select one or multiple swimwear items you prefer.
* **What size range do you wear?**: A field to select your preferred size range.
* **What type of material do you prefer?**: A field to select one or multiple materials for swimwear.
* **What color scheme do you like?**: A field to select one or multiple color schemes.
* **On a scale of 1-5, how do you rate your satisfaction with the swimwear?**: A field to rate your satisfaction level.
* **What areas would you like to see improved in swimwear?**: A field for you to provide any comments or suggestions.
* **Email**: A field for you to enter your email address.
* **Phone number**: A field for you to enter your phone number.
* **Comments**: A field for you to enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Do you like swimwear item 1, 2, 3, ...?**: A field to select one or multiple items from the list.
 

These fields are all related to swimwear items.
