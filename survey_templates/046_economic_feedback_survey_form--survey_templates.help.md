# Economic Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Economic Feedback Survey Form is designed to gather information about your current economic situation and priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the page you want to fill out (main_page or main_page_2).
2. Answer each question according to your current economic situation and priorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **main_page** (1, text, required/optional): This is the main page of the form, where you can provide any additional information about your economic situation.
* **economic_conditions** (2, text, required/optional): Describe your current economic situation.
* **priorities** (3, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate what you consider to be your top priorities for the coming period. Choose from the available options.
* **outlook** (4, select_one, required/optional): Indicate your overall outlook on your economic situation. Choose from the available options.
* **comments** (5, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your economic situation.
* **email** (6, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further information.
* **phone** (7, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **submitted_by** (8, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you submitted this form (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **submitted_at** (9, date, required/optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **submitted_time** (10, time, required/optional): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **main_page_2** (11, text, required/optional): This is the second page of the form, where you can provide additional information about your priorities.
* **question_1** (12, text, required/optional): Answer the first question on the second page.
* **question_2** (13, text, required/optional): Answer the second question on the second page.
