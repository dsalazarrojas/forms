# Government Scheme Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Scheme Awareness Survey is a form designed to collect information on the awareness and participation levels of government schemes among the target population.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Read the introduction to understand the purpose and context of the form.
2.  Select one of the options for each question that best describes your response.
3.  Provide any additional comments or information that you think would be helpful in understanding your response.
4.  Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `Note`, required: `false`): This is a brief overview of the survey and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
*   **Awareness of Scheme** (`awareness_of_scheme`, `Select One`, required: `false`): This question asks you to select one of the options that best describes your level of awareness about the government scheme. Choose the option that you think best fits your situation.
*   **Participation in Scheme** (`participation_in_scheme`, `Text`, required: `false`): This question is a text-based question where you can describe your experience or interaction with the scheme.
*   **Satisfaction with Scheme** (`satisfaction_with_scheme`, `Number`, required: `false`): This question asks for a numerical value between 1 and 5 that best describes your satisfaction level with the scheme.
*   **Barriers to Access Scheme** (`barriers_to_access_scheme`, `Select Multiple`, required: `true`): This question allows you to select multiple barriers that you face in accessing the scheme.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `Text`, required: `false`): This is a text-based question where you can provide any additional information or comments that you think would be helpful in understanding your response.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `Email`, required: `false`): This question asks for your contact information, which will be kept confidential and used for follow-up purposes only.

## Tips
*   Please answer each question as honestly and accurately as possible.
*   If you are unsure of any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.
*   Your responses will be kept confidential and used for the purpose of understanding the effectiveness and accessibility of the government schemes.
