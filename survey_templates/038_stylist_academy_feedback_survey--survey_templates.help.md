# Stylist Academy Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stylist Academy Feedback Survey is designed to gather information from users to improve the services and offerings provided by the academy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your honest and constructive feedback.
2. Select the options for the satisfaction level and recommended fields according to your experience with the academy.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the comments field.
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
5. Fill in your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
6. Provide the date and time you visited the academy, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Question** (form_1): This field is open for you to provide any comments or suggestions about your experience with the academy.
* **Satisfaction Level** (form_2): Please select one of the following options to indicate your level of satisfaction with the academy's services:
	+ 5: Very satisfied
	+ 4: Satisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 2: Dissatisfied
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
* **Recommended** (form_3): Please select all that apply to indicate if you:
	+ Yes: Would recommend the academy to friends and family
	+ Maybe: Not sure yet, but might recommend in the future
	+ False: Would not recommend the academy
* **Comments** (form_4): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please type them in this field.
* **Email** (form_5): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Phone** (form_6): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Date** (form_7): If you visited the academy, please enter the date you visited.
* **Time** (form_8): If you visited the academy, please enter the time you visited.
* **Date and Time** (form_9): If you visited the academy, please enter the date and time you visited.
