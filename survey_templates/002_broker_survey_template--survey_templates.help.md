# Broker Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Broker Survey Template is designed to collect feedback from brokers and industry professionals to help improve services and features that cater to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully to ensure you understand the questions and expectations.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Broker Name** (`broker_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as a broker.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your brokerage firm.
* **Years of Experience in Real Estate** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Specify your years of experience in the real estate industry.
* **How Satisfied Are You with Current Services?** (`service_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction level with current services (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **What Services Could be Improved?** (`service_improvements`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback on services that could be improved.
* **How Would You Rate Current Market Conditions?** (`market_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Rate the current market conditions (Excellent, Good, Fair, Slow, Very Slow).
* **Main Challenges Facing Your Business** (`business_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the main challenges your business is facing (Rising interest rates, Inventory shortage, Competition, Regulatory changes, Technology gaps, Client acquisition).
* **Primary Technology Tools Used** (`technology_usage`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the primary technology tools you use (CRM Software, MLS Platform, Virtual Tours, Social Media, Email Marketing, Mobile Apps).
* **Primary Source of Client Leads** (`client_acquisition`, `select_one`, required): Specify the primary source of your client leads (Referrals, Online Advertising, Social Media, Open Houses, Cold Calling, Other).
* **Average Commission Rate Charged** (`average_commission`, `number`, optional): Enter your average commission rate charged as a percentage.
* **Interest in New Industry Features** (`new_features_interest`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select areas of interest for new industry features (AI-powered tools, Blockchain transactions, Virtual reality tours, Analytics dashboards, Automation tools).
* **Training or Education Needs** (`training_needs`, `text`, optional): Specify any training or education needs you have.
* **Email for Survey Follow-up** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for any follow-up questions or discussions.

By following these guidelines, you'll be able to complete the Broker Survey Template accurately and effectively. If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
