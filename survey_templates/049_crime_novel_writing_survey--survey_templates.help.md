# Crime Novel Writing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences, habits, and preferences as a crime novel writer. The goal of this survey is to help you improve your writing skills and connect with other writers in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your answers. You can select multiple options for select multiple fields, and type in text fields. Make sure to answer each question honestly and thoroughly, as your responses will help us better understand your needs and provide more effective guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's Your Writing Experience** (`What's Your Writing Experience`, text, required: false): This field is where you can briefly describe your writing experience. This will help us understand your background and help us provide more tailored guidance.
* **Writer Identity** (`writer_identity`, select_one, required: true): Please select "New" if you are new to writing crime novels, or "Experienced" if you have written crime novels before.
* **How Often Do You Write** (`writing_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how often you write. Choose "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", or "Rarely" to reflect your writing frequency.
* **How Do You Write** (`writing_style`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the styles you use when writing, such as "Plot", "Character", "Pacing", and "Theme".
* **Habits** (`writing_habits`, text, required: false): This is where you can describe your writing habits, such as your daily routine or any specific writing practices you follow.
* **Where Do You Find Inspiration** (`inspiration`, text, required: false): Describe where you find inspiration for your writing, such as books, movies, or conversations.
* **What's Your Biggest Challenge** (`challenges`, text, required: false): Share your biggest challenge as a crime novel writer.
* **Favorite Genre** (`favorite_genre`, select_one, required: true): Select your favorite genre of crime novel.
* **What Editors Look For** (`editors_choice`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose what editors look for in a crime novel, such as "Strong Conflict" and "Well-Developed Characters".
* **What Publishers Look For** (`publishers_choice`, select_multiple, required: false): Select what publishers look for in a crime novel, such as "Commercial Appeal" and "Originality".
* **What Writing Groups Do You Participate In** (`writing_groups`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the writing groups you participate in, such as local writing groups or online writing communities.
* **What Instructors Do You Participate In** (`instructor_choice`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the instructors or courses you participate in, such as online courses or writing workshops.
* **Do You Use Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the social media platforms you use, such as Facebook or Twitter.
* **Favorite Author** (`favorite_author`, text, required: false): Share your favorite author of crime novels.
* **Favorite Book** (`favorite_book`, text, required: false): Describe your favorite crime novel.
* **Writing Goals** (`writing_goals`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your writing goals, such as "To Become a Published Author" or "To Learn About Writing".
* **What Writing Resources Do You Use** (`writing_resources`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the writing resources you use, such as books, conferences, or online communities.
* **How Often Do You Write** (`writing_frequency2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how often you write, similar to the first "How Often Do You Write" field.
* **Writing Groups** (`writing_groups2`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the types of writing groups you participate in, such as local or online.
* **Favorite Website** (`favorite_website`, text, required: false): Share your favorite website related to crime novel writing.
* **Favorite Forum** (`favorite_forum`, text, required: false): Describe your favorite forum for crime novel writers.
* **Favorite Blogger** (`favorite_blogger`, text, required: false): Share your favorite blogger on the subject of crime novel writing.
* **Favorite YouTube Channel** (`favorite_youtube_channel`, text, required: false): Describe your favorite YouTube channel related to crime novel writing.
* **Favorite Podcast** (`favorite_podcast`, text, required: false): Select your favorite podcast on the subject of crime novel writing.
* **Additional Comments** (`notes`, note, required: false): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about your writing experience.
