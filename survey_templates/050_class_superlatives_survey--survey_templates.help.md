**Class Superlatives Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Class Superlatives Survey is a form for students to nominate their classmates for various superlative categories, such as Most Likely to Succeed and Best Smile. The survey allows students to recognize and celebrate their peers' achievements and qualities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name and student ID (if applicable).
2. Nominate classmates for the superlative categories listed, such as Most Likely to Succeed, Best Smile, and Best Sense of Humor.
3. You can add custom superlatives if you want to recognize additional qualities or characteristics of your classmates.
4. Write a special message to the class if you'd like.
5. Finally, confirm that your nominations are genuine and in good fun by selecting the "Yes, I certify" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (2): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or as you would like it to appear on the survey results.
* **Student ID** (3): If applicable, enter your student ID number to help the survey administrators identify you.
* **Class Year or Grade** (4): Select the year or grade in which you are currently enrolled.
* **Most Likely to Succeed** (6): Nominate a classmate who has the potential to achieve great things in life.
* **Best Smile** (7): Nominate a classmate with the best smile in the class.
* **Best Sense of Humor** (8): Nominate a classmate who always knows how to make you laugh.
* **Most Friendly** (9): Nominate a classmate who is always willing to lend a helping hand.
* **Most Athletic** (10): Nominate a classmate who excels in athletics or has a remarkable talent for sports. (Note: This field may be optional and subject to change depending on school requirements.)
* **Best Dressed** (11): Nominate a classmate with a great sense of style.
* **Most Likely to Be Famous** (12): Nominate a classmate with a flair for show business or a passion for being in the spotlight.
* **Biggest Flirt** (13): Nominate a classmate who is known for their charming personality. (Note: This field may be optional and subject to change depending on school requirements.)
* **Most School Spirit** (14): Nominate a classmate who shows exceptional school pride and spirit.
* **Class Clown** (15): Nominate a classmate who is always making you laugh.
* **Most Hardworking** (16): Nominate a classmate who consistently demonstrates a strong work ethic.
* **Best Music Taste** (17): Nominate a classmate with exceptional musical talents or a unique sense of style.
* **Most Likely to Travel the World** (18): Nominate a classmate with a love for adventure and travel.
* **Best Leadership Skills** (19): Nominate a classmate who has demonstrated exceptional leadership skills or abilities.
* **Most Artistic or Creative** (20): Nominate a classmate with exceptional artistic or creative talents.
* **Custom Superlative 1 Category** (22): Create your own superlative category (e.g., Best Team Player, Most Likely to be a Millionaire).
* **Custom Superlative 1 Nomination** (23): Nominate a classmate for your custom superlative category.
* **Custom Superlative 2 Category** (24): Create another custom superlative category.
* **Custom Superlative 2 Nomination** (25): Nominate a classmate for your custom superlative category 2.
* **Additional Comments** (26): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the nominees.
* **Special Message to the Class** (27): Write a special message to the class, if desired.
* **I certify that my nominations are genuine and in good fun** (28): Confirm that your nominations are made in good fun and are not intended to be taken seriously.

**Tips**

* Be sincere and honest in your nominations.
* Consider nominating classmates who are deserving of recognition and appreciation.
* Keep the nominations light-hearted and fun, and avoid nominations that could be hurtful or embarrassing.
* Review your nominations carefully before submitting the form.
* Remember that the survey is a fun way to celebrate your classmates and their qualities, so keep it positive and respectful!
