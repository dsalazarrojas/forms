# Social Media Brand Insights - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Brand Insights form is a tool used to collect data about your social media presence and engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Select the social media platforms you use.
5. Provide a Presence Score between 1-5.
6. Provide an Engagement Score between 1-5.
7. Describe areas where you'd like to improve.
8. Add any Additional Comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address in the format "name@example.com".
* **Which social media platforms do you use?** (`social_media_platforms`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the social media platforms you use (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, Snapchat, YouTube).
* **Presence Score** (`presence_score`, `number`, `required`): Rate your presence score between 1 and 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Engagement Score** (`engagement_score`, `number`, `required`): Rate your engagement score between 1 and 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **What are areas for improvement?** (`improvement_areas`, `note`, `required`): Describe the areas you'd like to improve in your social media presence.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `optional`): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your social media presence.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Use real email addresses.
* Select all social media platforms used from the options provided.
* Presence and engagement scores should be between 1 and 5.
* Be as specific as possible when describing areas for improvement.
* Additional comments are optional, but can provide valuable feedback to help improve your social media presence.
