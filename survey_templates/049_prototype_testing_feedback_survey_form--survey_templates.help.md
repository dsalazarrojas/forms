# Prototype Testing Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users who have participated in a prototype testing session. Your feedback will help us improve the quality and usability of the prototype, making it more user-friendly and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
3. For numerical fields, please provide scores between 1 and 10.
4. For text fields, please provide clear and concise answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`feedback_score`, number, required: false): This section is where you can rate your overall experience with the prototype. Rate it from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Usability** (`usability_score`, number, required: false): This section allows you to rate how easy it was to use the prototype. Again, rate it from 1 to 10.
* **Overall** (`overall_score`, number, required: false): This section is where you can rate your overall satisfaction with the prototype. Rate it from 1 to 10.
* **Questions** (`questions`, text, required: false): Here, you can provide any questions you had during the prototype testing session.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to send you a summary of your feedback.
* **Feedback Email** (`feedback_email`, email, required: false): This is a secondary email address for us to contact you for more feedback or to send you updates on the prototype.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is optional and should be used to provide your phone number if you have any concerns or questions that require a call-back. 

Note that all fields are optional, but it's recommended that you fill out all fields to provide a comprehensive feedback.
