# mobile_app_subscription_pricing_inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your mobile app subscription pricing preferences and feedback. Your input will help us understand your needs and make informed decisions on our pricing and billing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and ensure you have answered all required fields (indicated by a red star next to the field label).
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately, based on your real preferences and experiences.
3. Take care to select the correct options for each question.
4. Once you have completed the form, click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Form Questions** (`Form Questions`, 1, required: false): This is the section where you'll find the individual questions that make up this form. Please answer each question to provide as much detail as you can.
- **Pricing Feedback** (`Pricing Feedback`, 2, required: false): Select all the options that apply to you, as this will help us understand your thoughts on our pricing model.
- **Billing Preferences** (`Billing Preferences`, 3, required: false): Choose how you would prefer your billing to be handled. This will help us understand your needs and preferences.
- **Value Perceptions** (`Value Perceptions`, 4, required: false): Select all the options that apply to your current or potential experiences with our mobile app subscription.
- **Billing Frequency** (`Billing Frequency`, 5, required: false): Choose how often you would like to be billed for your mobile app subscription.
- **Price Range** (`Price Range`, 6, required: false): Please select a price range that you feel comfortable with for your mobile app subscription.
- **Monthly Cost** (`Monthly Cost`, 7, required: false): Enter the amount you would be willing to pay per month for your mobile app subscription.
- **Yearly Cost** (`Yearly Cost`, 8, required: false): Enter the amount you would be willing to pay per year for your mobile app subscription.
- **Payment Plan** (`Payment Plan`, 9, required: false): Choose a payment plan that suits your needs.
- **Discount** (`Discount`, 10, required: false): If you think our current discount is not sufficient, please enter a percentage that you feel is more reasonable.
- **Subscription Status** (`Subscription Status`, 11, required: false): Select your current subscription status with our mobile app.
