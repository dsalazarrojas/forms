# Hardware Maintenance Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with hardware maintenance services. It helps us understand how our maintenance services are performing and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Introduction** (Page 1): This page is a brief overview of the form. Please review and acknowledge that you understand the purpose of this form.
2. Fill out **Customer** (Page 2): Enter your company name and department/organization as the customer.
3. Enter **Contact** (Page 2): Provide your email address so we can follow up on your feedback.
4. Enter **Maintenance** (Page 2): Describe the maintenance experience you'd like to provide feedback on.
5. **Feedback** (Page 3): Share any comments or suggestions you have about the maintenance service. This is an optional field, but your feedback is valuable to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is this feedback for**: This section is a brief introduction to the form, explaining what we are collecting feedback on.
* **Customer**: Please enter the name of your company or department/organization as the customer. This will help us understand which maintenance request was made.
* **Contact**: This field requires your email address, so we can follow up on your feedback and ensure we get in touch with you.
* **Maintenance**: In this field, please describe the maintenance service or issue you'd like to provide feedback on (e.g., "equipment cleaning", "repairs", etc.).
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts on your maintenance experience. This is an opportunity for you to suggest improvements or express any concerns you may have.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time!
