# Engagement Party RSVP Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engagement Party RSVP Survey is a form designed to collect information from guests about their attendance and preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you with important updates and reminders.
3. Choose whether you will be attending the event by selecting "True" or "False" from the dropdown menu.
4. Select the date and time you plan to arrive at the event.
5. If you have guests attending with you, choose the number of attendees from the dropdown menu.
6. If you have any special requests or comments, type them in the "Special Requests" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address here. This will be used to send you updates and reminders.
* **Will you be attending?**: Choose "True" if you will be attending the party, and "False" if you won't.
* **RSVP Date**: Select the date you plan to attend the party.
* **Start Time**: Select the time you plan to start attending the party.
* **End Time**: Select the time you plan to end attending the party. Note that you can enter the time you plan to leave the party.
* **How many guests will be attending?**: Choose the number of guests that will be attending with you.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests or comments, type them in here.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your attendance or preferences.
* **End Time**: Select the time you plan to end attending the party.
