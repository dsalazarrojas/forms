# Process Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions for improvement from employees on various aspects of the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question in the space provided for the corresponding field.
2. You can type in your response in the text field below each question.
3. If you have no suggestions or feedback, you can simply type "No comments" or "None" in the field.
4. Please make sure to fill out all fields, as they are optional but help us get a comprehensive view of your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, optional): We want to know about your overall experience working with our team. Please share your thoughts about what's working well and what could be improved.
- **Workplace Environment** (`workplace_environment`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the current workplace environment. What are some things that make you feel supported or challenged?
- **Management Performance** (`management_performance`, text, optional): How would you rate your supervisor's performance? What are some things they do well, and what areas for improvement do you see?
- **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share your ideas on how we can improve our processes, policies, or tools. Be as specific as possible.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you have any thoughts on the tools we use, please let us know. Do you feel like they are meeting your needs, or are there any issues you've experienced?
- **Category** (`category`, text, optional): If you have any other comments or suggestions that don't fit into the above categories, please provide them here.

## Tips
- Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
- You can provide as much or as little information as you feel comfortable with.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
