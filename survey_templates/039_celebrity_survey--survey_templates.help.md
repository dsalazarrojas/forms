# Celebrity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from respondents about a celebrity's suitability for a particular project, covering various aspects such as producer or casting information, project details, and evaluation criteria.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the "Producer or Casting Information" section, which includes your name and email address.
2. Move on to the "Project Information" section, where you will fill out the project name, type, and genre or style.
3. In the "Celebrity Evaluation" section, provide the name and profession of the celebrity being evaluated, along with their current popularity level and box office appeal.
4. Continue to the "Project Fit Assessment" section, where you will rate the celebrity's role fit, appeal to the target audience, and brand alignment.
5. In the "Career Trajectory" section, select the celebrity's career momentum and quality of recent projects.
6. Finally, in the "Risk Assessment" section, you may provide any potential risk factors and make an overall recommendation for the celebrity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (Respondent Name): Enter your name to identify yourself as the respondent.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for any further clarification.
* **Production Company or Organization**: Enter the production company or organization you are working with.
* **Your Role**: Describe your role in the project, such as producer, casting director, or writer.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project, e.g., "Movie A" or "TV Show B".
* **Project Type**: Select the type of project, e.g., Feature Film, Television Series, Advertisement, Music Video, etc.
* **Genre or Style**: Choose the genre or style of the project, e.g., Drama, Comedy, Action, etc.
* **Target Demographic**: Describe the main audience for the project, e.g., young adults, families, etc.
* **Celebrity Being Evaluated**: Enter the name and profession of the celebrity being evaluated.
* **Current Popularity Level**: Select the celebrity's current popularity level, e.g., A-List, B-List, Rising Star, etc.
* **Box Office Appeal**: Rate the celebrity's box office appeal, e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Unknown.
* **Role Fit**: Evaluate how well the celebrity fits the role, e.g., Perfect Fit, Good Fit, Acceptable Fit, etc.
* **Appeal to Target Audience**: Rate the celebrity's appeal to the target audience, e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor.
* **Brand Alignment**: Evaluate how well the celebrity aligns with the project's brand or values, e.g., Excellent Alignment, Good Alignment, Misaligned, etc.
* **Career Momentum**: Select the celebrity's career momentum, e.g., Rising, Stable, Declining, Resurgence, Unknown.
* **Quality of Recent Projects**: Evaluate the quality of the celebrity's recent projects, e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor.
* **Potential Risk Factors**: Describe any potential risk factors, e.g., controversies, personal issues, etc.
* **Overall Recommendation**: Make an overall recommendation for the celebrity, e.g., Highly Recommended, Recommended, Neutral, Not Recommended, Strongly Not Recommended.
* **Additional Comments or Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the celebrity.
