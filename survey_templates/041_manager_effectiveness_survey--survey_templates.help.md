# Manager Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to help organizations assess and improve their management practices by capturing various aspects of manager performance, communication style, and team dynamics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your first name and last name.
2. Select your title from the options provided.
3. Enter your department and job title.
4. Provide the name of your reporting manager.
5. Select your reporting manager's title from the options provided.
6. Enter your job function.
7. Enter your work unit.
8. Specify your work hours.
9. Specify your work hours end.
10. Describe your work hours frequency.
11. Describe your communication style.
12. Describe your communication frequency.
13. Describe your feedback frequency.
14. Describe your manager's effectiveness.
15. Describe your team's effectiveness.
16. Describe your team's dynamics.
17. Describe your work-life balance.
18. Describe your job satisfaction.
19. Describe your career growth.
20. Describe your job security.
21. Describe your work stress.
22. Describe your team stress.
23. Describe your employee loyalty.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This is where you enter your first name. 
* **Last Name**: This is where you enter your last name.
* **Title**: Please select your title from the provided options, which may include CEO, Manager, Director, Supervisor, or Team Leader.
* **Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Reporting Manager Name**: Enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager**: Please select your reporting manager's title from the options provided.
* **Job Function**: Describe your job function.
* **Work Unit**: Describe your work unit.
* **Work Hours**: Specify your work hours.
* **Work Hours End**: Specify your work hours end.
* **Work Hours Frequency**: Describe your work hours frequency.
* **Communication Style**: Describe your communication style.
* **Communication Frequency**: Describe your communication frequency.
* **Feedback Frequency**: Describe your feedback frequency.
* **Manager Effectiveness**: Describe your manager's effectiveness.
* **Team Effectiveness**: Describe your team's effectiveness.
* **Team Dynamics**: Describe your team's dynamics.
* **Work-Life Balance**: Describe your work-life balance.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Describe your job satisfaction.
* **Career Growth**: Describe your career growth.
* **Job Security**: Describe your job security.
* **Work Stress**: Describe your work stress.
* **Team Stress**: Describe your team stress.
* **Employee Loyalty**: Describe your employee loyalty.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* This survey is designed to be completed by managers and is meant to help organizations improve their management practices.
* Your responses will be kept confidential.
* Your participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw your responses at any time.
