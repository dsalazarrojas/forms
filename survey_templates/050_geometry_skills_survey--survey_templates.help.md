# Geometry Skills Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geometry Skills Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and opinions from users on their geometry learning experiences. This form aims to identify challenging topics and areas that need improvement in geometry-related training or education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them according to your true feelings and experiences.
2. Fill out all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Leave optional fields blank if you don't have an opinion or don't feel they apply to you.
4. Select all relevant options for multi-select fields (e.g., "select_multiple").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. geometry_learning_feedback (Note, optional)
* **geometry_learning_feedback**: This is a note field where you can share your thoughts, feelings, or any additional information about your geometry learning experiences.

### 2. what_topics_are_difficult (Select One, optional)
* **what_topics_are_difficult**: This question asks about topics that are difficult for you in geometry. Please select only one topic that you find challenging.

### 3. how_confident_do_you_feel (Number, optional)
* **how_confident_do_you_feel**: This question asks about your level of confidence in geometry. Enter a number from 0 to 10, where 0 is "not confident at all" and 10 is "very confident".

### 4. topic_challenges (Select Multiple, optional)
* **topic_challenges**: This question asks about topics that challenge you in geometry. Please select all the relevant options.

### 5. what_topics_are_needed_for_training (Select Multiple, optional)
* **what_topics_are_needed_for_training**: This question asks about topics that need improvement or additional training in geometry. Please select all the relevant options.

### 6. geometry_survey_form_6 (Time, optional)
* **geometry_survey_form_6**: This field is currently empty, but it could be for timing or duration information. Please leave it blank and only fill out if it has any relevance to you.

Tips:

* Be honest and thorough in your answers to provide valuable feedback.
