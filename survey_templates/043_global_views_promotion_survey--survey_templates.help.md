# Global Views Promotion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness and improvement areas of global views promotion. It aims to collect information from users on their views, measurement methods, and ideas for improvement, helping to refine and improve the global views promotion strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Global Views Promotion Survey Form**: This is the main form that you will be filling out.
2. Select the answers to the questions according to your views and thoughts.
3. Be sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure your feedback is complete.
4. You can add any additional comments or observations in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Global Views Promotion Survey Form
* **What are your views on the global promotion?**: Describe your thoughts and opinions on the global promotion.
* **How do you measure promotion effectiveness?**: Select the method you use to measure the promotion effectiveness.
* **What are ideas to improve future promotions?**: Suggest any ideas or areas for improvement.
* **Global Views Promotion Survey Form Description**: Add any additional comments or description about the global promotion.
* **Additional comments**: Add any additional comments or observations.
* **What region are you from?**: Select the region you are from.
* **What language do you use for this survey?**: Select the language you use to complete this survey.
* **Phone Number**: This field is optional and should be filled out if you want to be contacted for any further questions or discussions.
* **Email**: This field is optional and should be filled out if you want to be contacted for any further questions or discussions.
* **Date**: This field is optional and should be filled out if you want to provide a specific date for the survey completion.
* **Time**: This field is optional and should be filled out if you want to provide a specific time for the survey completion.
* **Decimal**: This field is optional and should be filled out if you want to provide a decimal value for the survey.
* **Integer**: This field is optional and should be filled out if you want to provide an integer value for the survey.
* **Date Range**: This field is optional and should be filled out if you want to provide a date range for the survey completion.
* **Time Range**: This field is optional and should be filled out if you want to provide a time range for the survey completion.

Note: The fields marked as optional should only be filled out if you have a relevant value to provide.
