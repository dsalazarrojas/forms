# Video Testimonial Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user testimonials in various formats (video, audio, and text) to gather feedback and opinions from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Name** in the text field below.
2. Enter your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you about your submission.
3. You can either upload a **Testimonial Video** file or provide a link to the video you'd like to share.
4. You can either upload an **Testimonial Audio** file or provide a link to the audio you'd like to share.
5. Write your **Testimonial Text** in the text field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name**: Enter your name as you'd like to be addressed.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about your submission.
  - This will be used to verify your identity and ensure we can respond to your submission.
- **Testimonial Video**: You can either upload a video file (.mp4, .mkv, .mov, etc.) or provide a link to the video you'd like to share.
  - Please make sure the video is not too large to upload or share.
- **Testimonial Audio**: You can either upload an audio file (wav, mp3, etc.) or provide a link to the audio you'd like to share.
  - Please make sure the audio is not too large to upload or share.
- **Testimonial Text**: Write your testimonial in this text field.
