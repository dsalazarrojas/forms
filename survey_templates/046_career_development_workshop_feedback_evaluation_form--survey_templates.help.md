# Career Development Workshop Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Workshop Feedback --** (`feedback_header`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field with a label. You can enter any information you feel is relevant or helpful.
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification card.
* **Workshop date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date on which you attended the workshop. This field is required to ensure that the feedback is tied to the specific workshop you attended.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop on a scale of Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
* **Usefulness of content** (`usefulness_of_content`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the usefulness of the material presented on a scale of Very useful, Useful, Somewhat useful, or Not useful.
* **Instructor effectiveness** (`instructor_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the effectiveness of the instructor on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, required: false): Provide any feedback on how the workshop could be improved.
* **Topics for future workshops** (`topics_for_future_workshops`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose any topics you would like to see included in future workshops.
* **Would recommend** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you would recommend this workshop to your colleagues.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or thoughts you have about the workshop.
