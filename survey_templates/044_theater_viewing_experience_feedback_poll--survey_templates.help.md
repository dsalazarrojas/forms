# theater_viewing_experience_feedback_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "theater_viewing_experience_feedback_poll" form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience at a theater, allowing us to understand their overall satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have an account and are logged in to access the form.
* Select the "theater_viewing_experience" field and provide a brief answer to "How was the overall experience?"
* Select the corresponding option(s) for "seat_comfort" that best reflects your experience.
* For "sound_quality", select the option that best describes your experience.
* Select the option(s) that best reflect your experience with "staff_friendliness".
* Answer "overall_experience" by selecting whether or not you would recommend the theater to friends.
* If you have additional comments or feedback, enter them in the "feedback" field.
* Fill in your "email", "phone", and "date" fields if applicable.
* Enter the name of the "theater", "show", "seat_number", "staff_name", and "performance_date" if applicable.
* Provide the "ticket_number" and "parking_fees" if applicable.
* Enter the price of "concession_price" and "merchandise_price" if applicable.
* Finally, select "merchandise_availability" if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How was the overall experience? (theater_viewing_experience)**: A text field where you can describe your overall experience at the theater.
* **How would you rate the seat comfort? (seat_comfort)**: Select one or multiple options to rate the comfort of your seat.
* **How would you rate the sound quality? (sound_quality)**: Select the option that best describes your experience with the sound quality.
* **How would you rate the staff friendliness? (staff_friendliness)**: Select one or multiple options to rate the friendliness of the staff.
* **Would you recommend this theater to friends? (overall_experience)**: Select whether or not you would recommend the theater to friends.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback about your experience? (feedback)**: A text field for you to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Date (performance_date)**: Enter the date you attended the show.
* **Time (performance_time)**: Enter the time you attended the show.
* **Theater Name (theater_name)**: Enter the name of the theater you visited.
* **Show Name (show_name)**: Enter the name of the show you attended.
* **Seat Number (seat_number)**: Enter the number of the seat you were seated in.
* **Staff Name (staff_name)**: Enter the name of the staff member who served you.
* **Staff Count (staff_count)**: Enter the number of staff members you interacted with.
* **Staff Dress (staff_dress)**: Enter a description of the staff's dress code.
* **Concession Price (concession_price)**: Enter the price of concessions you purchased.
* **Concession Price (USD) (concession_price_usd)**: Enter the concession price in USD.
* **Parking Fees (parking_fees)**: Enter the parking fees you paid.
* **Food and Drink Price (food_and_drink_price)**: Enter the price of food and drink you purchased.
* **Merchandise Availability (merchandise_availability)**: Select whether or not merchandise was available.
* **Merchandise Price (merchandise_price)**: Enter the price of merchandise you purchased.
