# Market Researcher Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Researcher Information Form is used to collect information about market researchers for internal purposes, such as research or resource allocation. Please complete this form to provide your details for our records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Form ID**, a unique number that identifies you in our system.
2. Select your **Category**, which should be one of the following options: Marketing, Research, Analysis, or Strategy.
3. Provide a **Description** of yourself, a brief overview of your background and expertise.
4. Enter your **Years of Experience** as a market researcher.
5. Enter your **Email** address.
6. Enter your **Phone Number**.
7. Enter your **Date of Birth** (mm/dd/yyyy).
8. Enter your **Years of Service** (optional).
9. Specify your **Availability**, e.g., 8am-5pm.
10. Enter your **Hourly Rate**.
11. Upload your **File**, if required.
12. Add any additional **Notes** you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID**: Enter a unique number that identifies you in our system. This is a required field.
* **Category**: Please select one of the following options: Marketing, Research, Analysis, or Strategy.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your background and expertise as a market researcher.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been working as a market researcher.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for internal communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for internal communication.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Years of Service**: Enter the number of years you have been working as a market researcher (optional).
* **Availability**: Specify your working hours, e.g., 8am-5pm.
* **Hourly Rate**: Enter your hourly rate for market research services.
* **File**: Upload a file if required, e.g., a resume or portfolio.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only, and your information will be kept confidential.
