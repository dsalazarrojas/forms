# Debate Platform Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the debate platform. It will help us understand your satisfaction, identify areas where we can improve, and gather suggestions for future refinements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question as thoroughly as possible.
2. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Additional comments?" field.
3. If you have any contact information (email or phone number), please enter it in the "Email Address" and "Phone" fields.
4. You can select multiple options for some questions, such as "What were your favorite topics during the debate?".
5. Make sure to save your progress and submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How did you find the debate platform?** (`survey_start`, text, optional): A place to share your overall experience with the platform, e.g., "It was great!", "Helpful but needs improvement".
* **What were your favorite topics during the debate?** (`debate_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Select all topics that you enjoyed during the debate.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Which topics are you interested in holding?** (`debate_topics_held`, select_one, optional): Choose a topic you'd like to see more of during the debate.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Other topics?** (`debate_topics_other`, text, optional): If there's a topic not listed that you're interested in, enter it here.
* **How would you rate your overall experience with the debate platform?** (`overall_experience`, text, optional): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not satisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".
* **What could we improve with the platform's user experience?** (`platform_ux`, text, optional): Share any ideas on how we can improve the user experience.
* **What topics would you like to see refined or expanded?** (`topic_refinements`, select_one, optional): Choose a topic you think we should focus on improving.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you may have.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Please be specific with your answers to help us better understand your needs and expectations.
* We appreciate any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
