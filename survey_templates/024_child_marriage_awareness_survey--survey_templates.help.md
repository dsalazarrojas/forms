# Child Marriage Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Child Marriage Awareness Survey is designed to gather insights from individuals on their awareness and attitudes towards child marriage. This survey aims to understand the legal marriage age, awareness of the term child marriage, and community attitudes towards this practice.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your gender from the options provided.
2. Choose your age group from the options.
3. Answer the questions regarding awareness and attitudes towards child marriage.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving public awareness on this issue.
5. Enter the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gender of Respondent (id: 2)**: Select your gender from the provided options: Female, Male, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say.
* **Age Group (id: 3)**: Choose your current age bracket: Under 18, 18 to 24, 25 to 34, 35 to 44, or 45 and Older.
* **Are you aware of the legal marriage age in your country? (id: 5)**: Select whether you are aware or not aware of the legal marriage age in your country.
* **Have you heard of the term - child marriage? (id: 6)**: Indicate whether you have knowledge of the term child marriage.
* **In your opinion, what is the ideal age for marriage? (id: 8)**: Select your ideal age for marriage: 18 to 21, 22 to 25, 26 to 30, or Over 30.
* **Do you think child marriage impacts education? (id: 9)**: Select whether you strongly agree, agree, are neutral, or disagree that child marriage affects education.
* **Do you think poverty is a primary driver of child marriage? (id: 10)**: Indicate whether you think poverty is a primary driver of child marriage or not.
* **Are there awareness programs in your community? (id: 12)**: Select whether there are active programs or none in your community.
* **How can we better educate the public on this issue? (id: 13)**: Provide any additional suggestions or ideas on how to improve public awareness.
* **Today Date (id: 14)**: Enter the current date of response.

Note: All questions are optional unless otherwise stated as required.
