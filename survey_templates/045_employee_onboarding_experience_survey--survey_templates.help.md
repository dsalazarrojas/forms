# Employee Onboarding Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from new hires about their onboarding experience, helping managers and HR to identify areas of improvement and refine the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **New Hire Experience** page, which asks for a brief description of your new hire experience.
2. On the next page, provide the **Manager Name** of your supervisor.
3. Move on to the **Department** page, where you will select your department from the list.
4. The **Job Title** page is where you will enter your job title.
5. On the **Start Date** and **End Date** pages, select the dates relevant to your employment.
6. The **Onboarding Process** page asks you to rate your experience with the onboarding process.
7. On the **Communication** page, rate the level of communication you received from your manager.
8. The **Feedback** page is where you can provide any additional feedback about your experience.
9. On the **Recommendations** page, share any suggestions for improvement for your manager or HR.
10. Finally, rate your overall **Onboarding Experience Rating** and **Departmental Support**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Hire Experience
* **New Hire Experience** (`new_hire_experience`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of your new hire experience.

### Manager Name
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required: false): The name of your supervisor.

### Department
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the department you belong to from the list:
	+ IT
	+ Marketing
	+ Sales
	+ Support

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.

### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): The date you started your employment.

### End Date
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): The date you ended your employment.

### Onboarding Process
* **Onboarding Process** (`onboarding_process`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your experience with the onboarding process:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Poor

### Communication
* **Communication** (`communication`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the level of communication you received from your manager:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Poor

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback about your experience.

### Recommendations
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required: false): Share any suggestions for improvement for your manager or HR.

### Onboarding Experience Rating
* **Onboarding Experience Rating** (`onboarding_experience_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall onboarding experience:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Poor

### Departmental Support
* **Departmental Support** (`departmental_support`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the support from your department:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Poor

### Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers to help us improve the onboarding process.
* You can provide feedback and recommendations anonymously.
* This form is not a formal evaluation, but rather a tool for improvement.
