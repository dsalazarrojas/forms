# Marina Scheduling Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on marina scheduling, customer preferences, and feedback from customers or clients of the marina. It is intended to help the marina understand their customers' needs and preferences, and improve their services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the different pages and fill out the relevant fields. Please note that some fields are marked as "required" which means you must complete those fields to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marina Scheduling Feedback** (`Scheduling Feedback`, `select_one`, required): This field is a multiple-choice question asking you to provide feedback on your experience with the marina's scheduling. Select one of the options to indicate your feedback.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks you to select multiple options regarding your availability for marina activities. Please select all applicable options.
* **Marina Name** (`Marina Name`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering the name of the marina you are interacting with.
* **Boater Preferences** (`Boater Preferences`, `select_one`, required): This field is a multiple-choice question asking you to select your boater preferences. Select one of the options to indicate your preference.
* **Scheduling Question** (`Scheduling Question`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks you to select multiple options regarding your scheduling question. Please select all applicable options.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): This field is for entering your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering your phone number.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): This field is for entering a specific date.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): This field is for entering a specific time.
* **Marinas List** (`Marinas List`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is a multiple-choice question asking you to select multiple marinas. Please select all applicable options.
* **Marina Comment** (`Marina Comment`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering any comments about the marina.
* **Marina Email** (`Marina Email`, `email`, optional): This field is for entering the email address of the marina.
* **Marina Phone** (`Marina Phone`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering the phone number of the marina.
* **Marina Address** (`Marina Address`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering the address of the marina.
* **Marina Contact** (`Marina Contact`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering the contact information of the marina.
* **Marina Notes** (`Marina Notes`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering any notes about the marina.
* **Marina Comments** (`Marina Comments`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering any comments about the marina.
* **Marina Attachment** (`Marina Attachment`, `text`, optional): This field is for attaching any relevant information about the marina.
* **Marina Website** (`Marina Website`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering the website of the marina.
* **Marina Scheduling Question** (`Marina Scheduling Question`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering any scheduling question you have for the marina.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all applicable options for multiple-choice questions.
* Be accurate when entering text fields.
* Ensure you have the correct contact information for the marina.
* Review your answers for completeness before submitting the form.
