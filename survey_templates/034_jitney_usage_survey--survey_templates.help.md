# jitney_usage_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jitney Usage Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users about their experiences with Jitney. This survey aims to gauge overall satisfaction, identify areas of improvement, and provide valuable insights to help us better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. If you're not satisfied with the current state of Jitney, provide suggestions for improvement in the 'suggestions' section.
3. If you didn't use Jitney, please explain why in the 'why_didnt_use' section.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the 'additional_comments' section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **how_satisfied** (`how_satisfied`, select_one, required: false): How satisfied are you with Jitney overall? Please select one of the options:
	+ very satisfied
	+ somewhat satisfied
	+ not satisfied
* **why_didnt_use** (`why_didnt_use_jitney`, text, required: false): Please explain why you didn't use Jitney if you chose 'not satisfied' in the 'how_satisfied' question.
* **suggestions** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, required: false): Share your ideas for improvement with us!
* **demographics** (`demographics`, note, required: false): A dedicated space for you to add any demographic information (e.g., name, contact info) if needed.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address (if you're comfortable sharing it).
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (if you're comfortable sharing it).
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or thoughts you have about your Jitney experience.
