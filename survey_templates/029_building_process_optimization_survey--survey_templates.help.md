# Building Process Optimization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help project teams and stakeholders assess the effectiveness of their building process, identify areas of improvement, and provide suggestions for optimization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer all questions that are relevant to your work on the building project.
3. Make sure to review and revise your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are completing this survey.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Department or Team** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department or team you work with (if applicable).
* **Project Name or Building** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project being evaluated.
* **Current Project Phase** (`project_phase`, select_one, required): Select the current phase of the project (e.g. Planning, Construction, etc.).
* **Effectiveness of Planning Process** (`planning_process`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the planning process for the project (Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, Very Ineffective).
* **Effectiveness of Team Communication** (`communication_workflow`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of team communication for the project (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Issues With Task Coordination** (`coordination_issues`, text, optional): Describe any issues with task coordination that have occurred on the project.
* **Major Process Bottlenecks** (`workflow_bottlenecks`, select_multiple, optional): Select any major process bottlenecks that have occurred on the project (e.g. Planning Phase, Approval Process, etc.).
* **Most Critical Bottleneck** (`critical_bottleneck`, text, optional): Describe the most critical bottleneck on the project.
* **Adherence to Project Schedule** (`schedule_adherence`, select_one, required): Rate how well the project is adhering to the schedule (Ahead of Schedule, On Schedule, Slightly Behind, Significantly Behind, Very Behind).
* **Adherence to Budget** (`budget_adherence`, select_one, required): Rate how well the project is adhering to the budget (Under Budget, On Budget, Slightly Over, Significantly Over, Very Over).
* **Meeting Quality Standards** (`quality_standards`, select_one, required): Rate whether the project is meeting quality standards (Exceeding Standards, Meeting Standards, Below Standards, Far Below Standards).
* **Clarity of Process and Procedures** (`process_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of the project process and procedures (Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear, Very Unclear).
* **Effectiveness of Project Management Tools** (`tool_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Rate the effectiveness of project management tools (Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, Not Using Tools).
* **Risk Management Process Effectiveness** (`risk_management`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the risk management process (Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, Very Ineffective).
* **Ideas for Process Optimization** (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional): Provide any ideas you have for improving the project process.
* **Top Priority Improvements** (`priority_improvements`, text, optional): Identify the top priority improvements needed on the project.

## Tips

* Answer honestly and thoroughly, and do not skip any questions that might be relevant to your work on the building project.
* Review and revise your answers before submitting the form.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
* Select the most accurate option for each question.
