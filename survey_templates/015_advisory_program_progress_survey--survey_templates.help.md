# Advisory Program Progress Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Advisory Program Progress Survey is a tool used to measure the effectiveness of the advisory program in supporting students' academic and personal goals. This survey aims to gather feedback from students on their experiences with their advisors, their meeting frequency, and their progress towards achieving their goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name (if not anonymous) in the **Participant Name** field.
2. Select the **Grade / Year Level** from the provided options.
3. Enter the **Advisor Name** provided by your advisor.
4. Select the frequency at which you meet with your advisor from the **Meeting Frequency** options.
5. Indicate how helpful the advisory meetings are to you from the **Usefulness of Sessions** options.
6. Choose all the topics that are covered in your advisory meetings from the **Topics Covered** options (if applicable).
7. Answer whether you are meeting the goals set at the start of the year from the **Progress on Personal Goals** options.
8. Indicate how supported you feel by your advisor from the **Quality of Support** options.
9. Optionally, provide suggestions for improvement in the **Suggestions for Improvement** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Participant Name): Enter your name if you wish to provide feedback. If you prefer to remain anonymous, leave this field blank.
* **Grade / Year Level** (Grade / Year Level): Select your current grade level (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, or Graduate Student).
* **Advisor Name** (Advisor Name): Enter the name of your advisor.
* **Meeting Frequency** (Meeting Frequency): Select the frequency at which you meet with your advisor (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Once per semester, or Never).
* **Usefulness of Sessions** (Usefulness of Sessions): Indicate how helpful the advisory meetings are to you (Extremely Helpful, Very Helpful, Somewhat Helpful, Not Very Helpful, or Not Helpful at All).
* **Topics Covered** (Topics Covered): Choose all the topics that are covered in your advisory meetings (Academic Performance, Career Planning, Personal Issues, Course Selection, College Applications, or Extracurriculars).
* **Progress on Personal Goals** (Progress on Personal Goals): Answer whether you are meeting the goals set at the start of the year (On Track, Behind Schedule, Ahead of Schedule, or No Goals Set).
* **Quality of Support** (Quality of Support): Indicate how supported you feel by your advisor (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree).
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (Suggestions for Improvement): Optionally, provide suggestions on how to improve the advisory program.
* **Topics Covered** is not required, you can skip it if no meeting happened.
