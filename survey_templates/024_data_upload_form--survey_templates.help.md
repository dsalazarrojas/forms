# data_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to upload data and files within the "survey_templates" category. It helps users provide a description, choose assigned tools and category, and upload additional files to support their data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief description of your data and files in the "Purpose" field.
2. Select the assigned tool for your data from the dropdown menu in the "Assigned Tool" field.
3. Select the category for your data from the dropdown menu in the "Category" field.
4. If needed, upload additional files to support your data in the "Additional Files" field.
5. Optionally, provide a description of your additional files in the "Description" field.
6. Submit the form to complete the data upload process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Purpose** (text, required/optional): A brief description of your data and files. This field is used to provide context and information about what you are uploading.
* **Upload Files** (text, required/optional): Enter the paths or locations of the files you are uploading. If you are uploading multiple files, separate them with a comma.
* **Assigned Tool** (select one, required/optional): Select the tool that is most closely related to your data and files. This will help us provide you with relevant information and services.
* **Category** (select one, required/optional): Choose a category that best describes your data and files. This will help us organize and make your data more discoverable.
* **Additional Files** (checkbox, required/optional): If you need to upload more files, check this box and upload your additional files. Please be mindful of file size and format.
* **Description** (text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your additional files. This will help us understand their content and purpose.
