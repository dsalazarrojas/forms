# Organic Pre Qualification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about a company's organic products and customer demographics. It will help us understand who the company targets and what kind of customers they are likely to be interested in their products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Organic Pre Qualification Survey** page with a brief description of the company's organic products.
2. Select the correct answer for **organic_products_sold** by choosing whether the company sells organic products (True) or not (False).
3. Choose the customer segmentation that best describes the company's target audience from the options **demographics**, **behavior**, or **interests**.
4. Fill out the **email** field with the company's email address (if required).
5. Fill out the **phone** field with the company's phone number (if required).
6. Fill out the **date_of_birth** field with the company's date of birth (if required) in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
7. Fill out the **time_of_birth** field with the company's time of birth (if required) in the HH:MM:SS format.
8. Review and submit the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organic Pre Qualification Survey** (`organic_pre_qualification_survey`, text, not required): This is the main page title and description of the company's organic products.
* **company_name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company.
* **organic_products_sold** (`organic_products_sold`, select_one, required): Choose whether the company sells organic products (True) or not (False).
* **customer_segmentation** (`customer_segmentation`, select_multiple, required): Choose the customer segmentation that best describes the company's target audience from the options **demographics**, **behavior**, or **interests**.
* **email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter the company's email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter the company's phone number.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter the company's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **time_of_birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, not required): Enter the company's time of birth in the HH:MM:SS format.

## Tips
* Be accurate and concise when filling out the form.
* If you have any doubts, refer back to this guide or consult with the company representative.
* Make sure to submit the form to complete the survey process.
