# Tax Document Submission Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with submitting tax documents. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the ease of use, efficiency, and overall satisfaction of the tax document submission process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Please select the correct options for each question, and provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
3. Make sure to save your progress and review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Submission Process
* **Submission Process** (`submission_process`, text, optional):
	+ Please describe your experience with the submission process. How easy or difficult was it to submit your tax documents?

### Tax Firm Experience
* **Tax Firm Experience** (`tax_firm_experience`, select_one, optional):
	+ How would you rate your experience with the tax firm you submitted to? Choose one of the following:
		+ Yes
		+ No

### Upload Process
* **Upload Process** (`upload_process`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ How was your experience with uploading your tax documents?
		+ Yes (all documents uploaded correctly)
		+ No (some documents not uploaded correctly)
		+ No (no documents uploaded)

### Document Submission
* **Document Submission** (`document_submission`, date, required):
	+ Please enter the date you submitted your tax documents.

### Support Experience
* **Support Experience** (`support_experience`, time, optional):
	+ If you needed help with the submission process, how long did you have to wait for support?
		+ 0-5 minutes
		+ 5-10 minutes
		+ 10-30 minutes
		+ More than 30 minutes

### Overall Experience
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, note, optional):
	+ Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with submitting tax documents.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any other feedback or suggestions, please provide them here.
Please note that the form is divided into two pages: "Submission Process", "Tax Firm Experience", "Upload Process", "Document Submission", "Support Experience", and "Overall Experience".
