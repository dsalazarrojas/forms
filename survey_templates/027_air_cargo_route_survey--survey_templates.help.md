# Air Cargo Route Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Air Cargo Route Survey" form is designed to gather information from air cargo companies about their current operations and desired new routes. This form will help us better understand your cargo transportation needs and preferences, enabling us to provide valuable insights and support for optimizing your route planning and operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your company information**: Start by filling out the "Company Name" and "Contact Person" fields with the relevant details.
2. **Select your primary cargo type**: Choose the type of cargo you primarily transport from the options provided (e.g., General Cargo, Perishables, Pharmaceuticals, etc.).
3. **Provide your current routes**: List the major routes you currently use for transportation in the "Major Routes Used" field.
4. **Specify your shipment frequency**: Select the frequency of your shipments (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Ad-hoc).
5. **Share your annual volume**: Enter the annual volume of cargo you transport in tons or TEUs (e.g., 10, 50, 100, etc.).
6. **Rate your satisfaction**: Evaluate your overall satisfaction with your current routes by selecting one of the options (e.g., Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very Satisfied).
7. **Identify new destinations**: Specify the desired new destinations you'd like to visit.
8. **List current challenges**: Select up to three current challenges you face in your operations (e.g., High Cost, Flight Delays, Capacity Shortages, Customs Clearance, Last Mile Delivery).
9. **Set priority for new routes**: Indicate your priority level for new routes, with options ranging from High to Low.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name as it appears on any relevant documents or records.
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name and title of the person responsible for this survey response.
* **Primary Cargo Type**: Choose the type of cargo you primarily transport to help us understand your specific needs.
* **Major Routes Used**: List the major routes you currently use for transportation, in the format "Origin - Destination" (e.g., New York - Los Angeles).
* **Frequency of Shipments**: Select how often you ship cargo (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Annual Volume**: Enter the annual volume of cargo you transport in tons or TEUs.
* **Satisfaction with Current Routes**: Evaluate your overall satisfaction with your current routes by selecting one of the options.
* **Desired New Destinations**: Specify the new destinations you'd like to visit, separated by commas (e.g., Tokyo, Paris, Sydney).
* **Current Challenges**: Select up to three challenges you face in your operations, using the options provided (e.g., High Cost, Flight Delays, etc.).
* **Priority for New Routes**: Indicate your priority level for new routes, with options ranging from High to Low.
