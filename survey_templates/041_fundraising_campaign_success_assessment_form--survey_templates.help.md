# Fundraising Campaign Success Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the success of a fundraising campaign by collecting feedback and data from participants and donors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each page's "Next" button to navigate through the form.
2. Fill out all the required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
3. Select from the provided options for each field as applicable.
4. Enter any additional comments or details in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Feedback
**Participant Feedback** (1.1) (text, optional)
This field is for gathering feedback from participants about their experience with the campaign.

### Donor Feedback
**Donor Feedback** (2.1) (text, optional)
