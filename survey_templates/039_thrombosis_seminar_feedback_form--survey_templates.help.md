# Thrombosis Seminar Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from attendees of the Thrombosis Seminar. Your responses will help us improve future seminars and provide better content for future attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields. 
2. Select the type of event you attended (Thrombosis Seminar or Other).
3. Answer each question in the text fields provided, sharing your thoughts and experiences about the seminar.
4. Submit your completed form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event you attended (Thrombosis Seminar or Other).
* **Question 1** (`questions_1`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about the seminar's content, organization, or presentation.
* **Question 2** (`questions_2`, text, optional): Describe any challenges you faced while attending the seminar.
* **Question 3** (`questions_3`, text, optional): Suggest improvements or additions for future seminars.
* **Question 4** (`questions_4`, text, optional): Do you have any questions or topics you'd like to have explored further during the seminar?
* **Question 5** (`questions_5`, text, optional): How likely are you to recommend this seminar to others?
* **Question 6** (`questions_6`, text, optional): Any other comments or suggestions about the seminar?

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as they will help us improve the seminar.
* If you have any difficulty filling out this form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
