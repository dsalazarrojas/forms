# Donor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Survey form is designed to collect essential information from donors, including their contact details, donation history, and frequency of donations. This data helps us understand the donor's behavior and preferences, enabling us to provide personalized services and optimize our resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please ensure you have the necessary information ready before starting.
- Fill out each field accurately and completely, as this information is used to create your donor profile.
- If a field is marked as "required," make sure to fill it out before proceeding.
- For the "frequency_of_donation" field, select the option that best describes your donation frequency. If none of the provided options match your situation, you can select "Other" and provide a description in the "frequency_of_donation_other_description" field.
- Use the "first_donation" and "last_donation" fields to enter the dates of your first and last donations, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Survey** (1, text, required/optional): This field is a placeholder for the form title and should not be filled out by the user.
* **Name** (2, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents, such as your driver's license or passport.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Contact Phone** (4, text, optional): If you have a preferred phone number where we can contact you, please enter it.
* **Donation Amount** (5, number, required): Enter the total amount you have donated to our organization.
* **Frequency of Donation** (6, select_one, required): Select the frequency of your donations from the provided options. If none of the options match your situation, select "Other" and provide a description in the "Frequency of Donation Other Description" field.
* **Frequency of Donation Other** (7, text, optional): If you selected "Other" for the "Frequency of Donation" field, please provide a brief description of your donation frequency.
* **First Donation** (8, date, required): Enter the date of your first donation.
* **Last Donation** (9, date, required): Enter the date of your last donation.
* **Frequency of Donation Other Description** (10, text, optional): If you selected "Other" for the "Frequency of Donation" field, please provide a brief description of your donation frequency, including the frequency and any other relevant details.
