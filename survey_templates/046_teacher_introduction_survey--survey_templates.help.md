# teacher-introduction-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect introductory information about a teacher, including their title, username, email, phone number, and a brief description.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the teacher in the **Form Title** field.
2. Enter the teacher's **Username** in the **Username** field.
3. Enter the teacher's **Email** address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter the teacher's **Phone** number in the **Phone** field.
5. Enter a brief description of the teacher in the **Description** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a title that identifies the teacher, such as "Mr. John Smith" or "Ms. Jane Doe".
* **Username** (`user_username`, text, required: false): Enter the teacher's username, which is often a unique identifier used to log in to the system.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter the teacher's email address, which will be used for communication and notifications.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the teacher's phone number, which can be used for contact purposes.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the teacher, such as their teaching subject or area of expertise.
* **Category** (`form_categories`, text, required: false): Enter a category or tag that identifies the teacher, such as their department or role.
