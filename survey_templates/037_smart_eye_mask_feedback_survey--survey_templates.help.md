# Smart Eye Mask Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Eye Mask Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with the Smart Eye Mask. The form aims to gather information on various aspects of the mask, including overall satisfaction, feasibility, comfort, design, materials, size, style, color, face shape, fit, breathing difficulty, face shape difficulty, overall quality, recommendation, and improvement suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you're answering the questions that apply to your experience with the Smart Eye Mask.
2. Answer each question based on your honest opinion and feedback.
3. Provide detailed comments and suggestions where possible.
4. Make sure to submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `text`, **optional**): Answer how satisfied you are with the Smart Eye Mask, on a scale from 0 to 10, where 10 is extremely satisfied and 0 is extremely dissatisfied.
* **How would you rate your experience?** (`rating`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose a rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How feasible is the Smart Eye Mask?** (`mask_feasibility`, `number`, **optional**): Rate how easy or difficult it is to use the mask, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **How comfortable is the mask?** (`comfort_level`, `number`, **optional**): Rate how comfortable the mask is to wear, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **What do you think about the mask's design?** (`mask_design`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose multiple options that describe your opinion about the mask's design (Very good, Good, Neutral, Bad, Very bad).
* **How would you rate the materials used in the mask?** (`mask_materials`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose a rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Overall quality** (`overall_quality`, `number`, **optional**): Rate the overall quality of the mask, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **What size is your mask?** (`mask_size`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose a size that describes your mask (S, M, L, XL).
* **What style would you like for your mask?** (`mask_style`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose multiple options that describe the style you prefer (Sport, Fashion, Everyday, Travel, Custom).
* **What color would you like for your mask?** (`mask_color`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose multiple options that describe the color you prefer (Black, White, Red, Blue, Green).
* **What face shape would you like for your mask?** (`face_shape`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose a face shape that describes your preference (Round, Oval, Heart, Diamond).
* **How does the mask fit?** (`mask_size_fits`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose multiple options that describe the fit (Too small, Just right, Too large, Too tight).
* **How difficult is the breathing?** (`breathing_difficulty`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose an option that describes your difficulty with breathing (Very difficult, Somewhat difficult, Neutral, Easy, Very easy).
* **How does the mask fit your face shape?** (`face_shape_difficulty`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose an option that describes how well the mask fits your face shape (Very difficult, Somewhat difficult, Neutral, Easy, Very easy).
* **Would you recommend this mask?** (`overall_recommendation`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose an option that describes your recommendation (True, False, Undecided).
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `note`, **optional**): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the mask.
* **Do you have any comments?** (`comments`, `note`, **optional**): Provide any comments or feedback you have about the mask.
* **How did you hear about this mask?** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose multiple options that describe how you heard about the mask (Social Media, Ad, Online search, Friend/family, Other).
* **How did you purchase the mask?** (`how_did_you_purchase`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose multiple options that describe how you purchased the mask (Online, In-store, Friend/family, Other).
* **How satisfied are you with the service?** (`satisfaction_with_service`, `number`, **optional**): Rate your satisfaction with the service, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **How satisfied are you with the product?** (`satisfaction_with_product`, `number`, **optional**): Rate your satisfaction with the product, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **How much overall value do you get from this mask?** (`overall_value`, `number`, **optional**): Rate the overall value you get from the mask, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Would you recommend this mask?** (`would_you_recommend`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose an option that describes your recommendation (True, False, Undecided).
* **Why didn't you recommend this mask?** (`why_didnt_recommend`, `note`, **optional**): Provide any reasons why you didn't recommend the mask.
* **Do you have any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, `note`, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the mask.
