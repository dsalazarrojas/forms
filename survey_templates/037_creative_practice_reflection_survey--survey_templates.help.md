# creative_practice_reflection_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback on a creative practice or project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Start by providing a brief answer to the "what_is_this_survey" field on page 1.
2. Move on to page 2 and fill in the "artist_name" field with your name or nickname.
3. On page 3, enter "project_details" about the creative project you're reflecting on.
4. Answer the "feedback_questions" field on page 4 with any comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
5. Finally, on the last page, write your "practice_reflection" in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: What is this Survey
* **what_is_this_survey** (Text, Required: False): Please provide a brief description of the survey, e.g., "A survey on my current art project."
### Page 2: Artist Information
* **artist_name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your name or nickname, e.g., "John Doe".
### Page 3: Project Details
* **project_details** (Text, Required: False): Describe your creative project, including relevant details such as title, date, and any notable features.
### Page 4: Feedback Questions
* **feedback_questions** (Text, Required: False): Share any thoughts, comments, or feedback on your creative practice or project.
### Page 5: Reflection
* **practice_reflection** (Text, Required: False): Reflect on your experience with the creative practice or project, including any lessons learned or takeaways.
