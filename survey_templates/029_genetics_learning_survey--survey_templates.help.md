# Genetics Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect information from users about their learning experience in genetics. It's meant to be a quick and easy way to gather feedback that can be used to improve the genetics program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the first field to indicate whether or not you've taken a course in genetics.
2. Enter any text you'd like in the second field for comments or feedback.
3. Enter your email address in the third field for us to get in touch with you.
4. Enter your phone number in the fourth field if you'd like us to reach out to you by phone.
5. Select your date of birth from the date picker in the fifth field.
6. Select the time of day you'd like to be contacted in the sixth field.
7. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the seventh field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_page** (`first_page_label`, select_one, optional): Check this box if you've taken a course in genetics, or not.
* **second_page** (`second_page_label`, text, optional): Enter any text you'd like to add about your learning experience.
* **email_address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to reach out to you by phone.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Select your date of birth from the calendar picker.
* **time_of_day** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): Select the time of day you'd like to be contacted.
* **note_or_comment** (`note_or_comment`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have.

Tips:

* Please answer all questions honestly, and if you're unsure about any of the questions, just let us know.
* Don't worry if you've never taken a course in genetics - we welcome feedback from all users.
* If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter a valid phone number.
* If you'd like to provide additional comments or feedback, please use the "Note or Comment" field.
