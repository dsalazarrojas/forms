This form is intended for assessing the knowledge and skills of bakers and individuals interested in sourdough bread-making, with the aim of providing feedback to help them improve their skills. The form is part of a process used to help bakers understand their current knowledge level, identify areas where they need improvement, and develop an action plan to enhance their baking skills.

**Sourdough Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide**

# Purpose
This form is designed to help you evaluate your knowledge and skills in sourdough bread-making, including your experience, familiarity, and specific abilities in different areas.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions and fields as accurately as possible, providing your best estimate of your experience, skills, and familiarity with sourdough bread-making.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your situation.
3. Provide any additional comments or explanations in the "Notes" field, if needed.
4. Ensure that you have completed all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User info**: This field is for your basic contact information and should be filled in accurately to ensure that we can contact you for feedback or further assistance.
* **Sourdough info**: Please provide any relevant information about your sourdough bread-making experience, such as the types of breads you have made or the techniques you have used.
* **Baking experience**: This field is for you to rate your experience in baking, with a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Familiarity**: Rate your familiarity with sourdough bread-making techniques, from 1 to 10.
* **Needs**: Indicate if you have any specific needs or challenges you are facing in your sourdough bread-making journey.
* **Evaluation 1 to 12**: Please answer "Yes" or "No" to the specific questions, providing your current level of proficiency in each area.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or thoughts you have that are not covered in the other fields.
* **Email**: Provide your email address, which we will use to contact you for feedback or further assistance.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, which we will use to contact you in case of any issues.
* **Date and Time 1 to 4**: Please select a date and time for each field, if applicable.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are most active in baking, if applicable.

Please note that some fields are not required, but it is recommended to fill them in for a more comprehensive assessment.
