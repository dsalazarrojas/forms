# Lighting Design Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on lighting design projects. It's meant to be filled out by lighting designers and clients after a project has been completed or is currently in progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Make sure you're filling out the correct form for your project.
2. Fill out all required fields and provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields.
3. Answer all questions truthfully and concisely.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the project you're providing feedback on.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the project, if applicable.
* **Lighting Designer** (`lighting_designer`, text, not required): Enter the name of the lighting designer who worked on the project.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the project on a scale of 1-5.
* **Lighting Quality Score** (`lighting_quality_score`, number, not required): Rate the quality of the lighting design on a scale of 1-5.
* **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, text, not required): Enter the duration of the project.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, not required): Provide any additional feedback you have about the project.
* **Lighting Designer Email** (`lighting_designer_email`, email, not required): Enter the email of the lighting designer who worked on the project.
* **Lighting Designer Phone** (`lighting_designer_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the lighting designer who worked on the project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, not required): Select the current status of the project (Completed, In Progress, Not Started).
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, not required): Enter the email of the client.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the client.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, not required): Enter the start date of the project.

Note: Please keep in mind that this form is meant to be filled out by both lighting designers and clients. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the project manager for clarification.
