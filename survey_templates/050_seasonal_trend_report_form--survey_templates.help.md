# Seasonal Trend Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on seasonal trends in the market, including product categories, sales, and marketing strategies. Completing this form will help you provide valuable insights for business decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the product category that your report is about.
2. Choose the season for which the report is applicable.
3. Fill in the relevant details for each field, making sure to complete any required fields.
4. Submit the form to get started with your seasonal trend analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is the product category? 
* **Electronics, Fashion, Home Goods, Beauty, Sports, Other** (`seasonal_trend_report_form`, select_one, required: false): Select the product category that your report is about.

### 2. Which season is this report for? 
* **Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter** (`seasonal_trend_report`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the season for which the report is applicable.

### 3. Product Name 
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the product.

### 4. Product Description 
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the product.

### 5. Product Image 
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, required: false): Provide the image of the product.

### 6. Seasonal Trend Data 
* **Seasonal Trend Data** (`seasonal_trend_data`, text, required: false): Enter any additional seasonal trend data related to the product.

### 7. Sales Trend Data 
* **Sales Trend Data** (`sales_trend_data`, text, required: false): Enter any sales trend data related to the product.

### 8. Marketing Strategy 
* **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, text, required: false): Describe the marketing strategy for this season.

### 9. Customer Perspectives 
* **Customer Perspectives** (`customer_perspectives`, text, required: false): Enter any customer perspectives related to the product.

### 10. Competitor Perspectives 
* **Competitor Perspectives** (`competitor_perspectives`, text, required: false): Enter any competitor's product strategies for this season.

### 11. Sales Forecast 
* **Sales Forecast** (`sales_forecast`, number, required: false): Enter the sales forecast for the product.

### 12. Holiday Sales Forecast 
* **Holiday Sales Forecast** (`sales_forecast_holiday`, number, required: false): Enter the holiday sales forecast for the product.

### 13. Non-holiday Sales Forecast 
* **Non-holiday Sales Forecast** (`sales_forecast_non_holiday`, number, required: false): Enter the non-holiday sales forecast for the product.

### 14. Product Price 
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required: false): Enter the product price.

### 15. Sales Channel 
* **Sales Channel** (`sales_channel`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the sales channel for the product.

### 16. Seasonal Trend Data 2 
* **Seasonal Trend Data 2** (`seasonal_trend_data2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional seasonal trend data related to the product.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
