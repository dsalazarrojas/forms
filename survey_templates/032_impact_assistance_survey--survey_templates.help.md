**Impact Assistance Survey - Help Guide**

This guide will explain the purpose and process of completing the Impact Assistance Survey. Please carefully read through each section to ensure you understand the survey's purpose, how to complete it, and what each field is intended to represent.

Before starting the survey, please note that it's designed to collect information about the impact of various outcomes on a specific set of participants. This survey is used to gather data for research purposes.

**Purpose**
The Impact Assistance Survey is a tool designed to collect data on the outcomes of a specific intervention or program. The survey aims to measure the effects of these outcomes on participants, providing valuable insights for researchers, administrators, and policymakers.

**How To Complete This Form**

To complete the Impact Assistance Survey, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant sections and questions that apply to your experience or situation.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Review your responses for completeness and consistency before submitting the survey.
