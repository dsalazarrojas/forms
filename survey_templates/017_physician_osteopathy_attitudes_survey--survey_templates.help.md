# Physician Osteopathy Attitudes Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the attitudes and opinions of physicians regarding osteopathy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each page of the form, which are displayed separately.
2. Read each field label carefully and answer the question or statement presented.
3. Enter your response in the text field provided.
4. Use the navigation buttons to move to the next or previous page.
5. When completed, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physician Osteopathy Attitudes Survey Page 1**: This is the first page of the survey, where you will be asked about your general attitudes towards osteopathy.
* **Physician Osteopathy Attitudes Survey Page 2**: This page is designed to gather more specific information about your experiences and opinions on osteopathy.
* **Physician Osteopathy Attitudes Survey Page 3**: This page will ask about your knowledge and understanding of osteopathy principles and practices.
* **Physician Osteopathy Attitudes Survey Page 4**: This page is focused on your attitudes towards the benefits and challenges of osteopathy.
* **Physician Osteopathy Attitudes Survey Page 5**: This page will gather more information about your experiences and opinions on using osteopathy in your practice.
* **Physician Osteopathy Attitudes Survey Page 6**: This page is designed to understand your attitudes towards the future development and integration of osteopathy in your profession.
* **Physician Osteopathy Attitudes Survey Page 7**: This page will ask about your suggestions and recommendations for improving osteopathy education and training.
* **Physician Osteopathy Attitudes Survey Page 8**: This is the last page of the survey, where you can provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability and avoid duplicating responses.
* Take your time to review each page and answer each question carefully.
* If you have any difficulties or concerns, feel free to contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
