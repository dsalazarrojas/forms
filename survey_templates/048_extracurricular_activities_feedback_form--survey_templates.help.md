# extracurricular_activities_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on extracurricular activities. It is intended to be completed by individuals who have participated in these activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all required fields.
2. For the rating fields, select all options that apply.
3. For the date fields, enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. For the time fields, enter the time in the correct format (HH:MM).
5. Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., example@example.com).
6. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
