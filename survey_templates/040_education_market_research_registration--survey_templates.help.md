# Education Market Research Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register education market research projects and collect information from participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about your project.
2. Make sure to provide details about your research focus, participant information, and project status.
3. Enter the contact email and phone number of the researcher or project lead.
4. Provide the participant count and follow-up information (email and phone) if applicable.
5. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Name
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school or institution associated with this project.

### Researcher Name
* **Researcher Name** (`researcher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the researcher or project lead.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the researcher or project lead.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the researcher or project lead (optional).

### School Location
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the school or institution.

### Research Focus
* **Research Focus** (`research_focus`, text, required): Describe the main focus of the research project.

### Project Title
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title of the research project.

### Participant Role
* **Participant Role** (`participant_role`, text, required): Describe the role of the participants in the research project.

### Project Description
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the research project.

### Project Start Date
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the project.

### Project End Date
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the project.

### Participant Start Date
* **Participant Start Date** (`participant_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of participant involvement (optional).

### Follow-up Email
* **Follow-up Email** (`follow_up_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address to follow up with participants (optional).

### Follow-up Phone
* **Follow-up Phone** (`follow_up_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number to follow up with participants (optional).

### Project Lead
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, text, required): Enter the name of the project lead.

### Research Area
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, text, required): Describe the research area or field.

### Research Interest
* **Research Interest** (`research_interest`, text, required): Describe the research interest or focus.

### Research Focus
* **Research Focus** (`research_focus`, text, required): Describe the research focus or area of study.

### Education Level
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, text, required): Describe the education level of the participants (e.g. high school, undergraduate, graduate).

### Participant Status
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, text, required): Describe the status of the participants (e.g. active, inactive, pending).

### Project Status
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, text, required): Describe the current status of the project.

### Follow-up Method
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, text, optional): Describe the method of follow-up (optional).

### Follow-up Frequency
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, text, optional): Describe the frequency of follow-up (optional).

### Participant Count
* **Participant Count** (`participant_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of participants.

### Follow-up Date
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last follow-up (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about your project and participants.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact the research team.
