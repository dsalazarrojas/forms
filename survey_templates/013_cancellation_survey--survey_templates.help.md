# Cancellation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your cancelled service or product. It will help us understand the reasons behind your cancellation and provide us with valuable feedback to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your full name and account email address associated with the cancelled service.
2. Indicate the service or product you cancelled.
3. Select the main reason for cancellation from the options provided.
4. Provide additional details about your choice, if needed.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service and satisfaction with the onboarding process.
6. Choose an option for how likely you are to use our service again.
7. If you selected "Yes" to using us again, please provide suggestions on how we can improve our retention.
8. Indicate whether you consent to be contacted for a follow-up interview.
9. Finally, please provide a submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Account Email Address** (`account_email`, email, required): Enter the email associated with your cancelled service account.
* **Service or Product Cancelled** (`cancelled_item`, text, required): Specify the service or product you cancelled.
* **Cancellation Date** (`cancellation_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the cancellation took effect.
* **Main Reason for Cancellation** (`cancellation_reason_main`, select_one, required): Select the most significant factor for your cancellation from the provided options.
* **Elaborate on Reason** (`cancellation_reason_elaboration`, text, optional): Provide more details about your choice, if needed.
* **Competitor Switched to** (`competitor_switched_to`, text, optional): If you chose to go with a competitor, specify who it was.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
* **Satisfaction with Onboarding** (`onboarding_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the onboarding process.
* **Feature Gap** (`feature_gap`, note, optional): This is a note field for you to provide feedback on any feature gaps you experienced.
* **Missing Feature Description** (`missing_feature_description`, text, optional): Describe the specific feature you were looking for.
* **Customer Support Rating** (`customer_support_rating`, number, required): Rate our customer support from 1 to 5 stars.
* **Re-engagement** (`re_engagement`, select_one, required): Choose how likely you are to use our service again.
* **Return Likelihood** (`return_likelihood`, select_one, required): If you selected "Yes" to using us again, this field will be visible, prompting you to provide suggestions on how we can improve our retention.
* **Improvement Suggestion** (`improvement_suggestion`, text, optional): Share ideas on how we can improve our retention.
* **Consent to Follow-up Interview** (`follow_up_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you consent to be contacted for a follow-up interview.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the current date for record-keeping purposes.

Please fill out the form accurately and completely, providing as much detail as possible. Your feedback is valuable to us, and it will help us make necessary improvements to provide better services in the future.
