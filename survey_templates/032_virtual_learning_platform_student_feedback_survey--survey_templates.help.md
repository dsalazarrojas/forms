# Virtual Learning Platform Student Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience with the virtual learning platform. The purpose of this survey is to gather insights on the effectiveness of the platform, identify areas of improvement, and understand what students think about the courses offered by the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name in the provided text field.
2. Enter the name of the course you were taking in the Course Name field, if applicable.
3. If you have an Email address, please provide it for us to contact you if needed.
4. Rate your experience with the platform using the Rating field (1-5 stars).
5. Share your thoughts and suggestions with us in the Feedback field.
6. Add any additional Comments about your experience with the platform.
7. If you have any Suggestions for improvements, please provide them in the Suggest Improvements field.
8. Let us know if you would Recommend our courses to others by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
9. Choose how you agree or disagree with some of the following statements:
	* Do you think our platform is easy to use? (Agree/Disagree)
	* Do you think our platform is useful? (Agree/Disagree)
10. Select the time it took you to complete the course from the provided options in the Completion Time field.
11. Let us know if you received Feedback for learners and if it was useful or not.
12. If you have any Feedback for instructors, please provide it in the Feedback for instructors field.
13. Select the level of Support you received from us in the Support field (Yes/No).
14. Briefly describe your Overall Experience with the platform in the Overall Experience field.
15. Provide any additional Suggestions for improvement in the Suggestions for improvement field.
16. Let us know if you would Will take our courses again by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
17. Finally, if you have any other comments or concerns, please share them with us in the Contact Us field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required: false): If you took a course, please enter the name of the course.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you have an Email address, please provide it for us to contact you if needed.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): Rate your experience with the platform using 1-5 stars.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts and suggestions with us.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments about your experience with the platform.
* **Suggest Improvements** (`suggest_improvements`, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them here.
* **Recommend** (`recommend`, select_one, required: false): Let us know if you would recommend our courses to others.
* **Agree/Disagree** (`agree_disagree`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how you agree or disagree with some of the following statements.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, time, required: false): Select the time it took you to complete the course.
* **Completion Time** (`completion_time`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time you completed the course.
* **Feedback for learners** (`feedback_for_learners`, select_one, required: false): Let us know if you received feedback for learners and if it was useful or not.
* **Feedback for instructors** (`feedback_for_instructors`, text, required: false): If you have any feedback for instructors, please provide it here.
* **Support** (`support`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the level of Support you received from us.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your Overall Experience with the platform.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, required: false): Provide any additional Suggestions for improvement.
* **Will take our courses** (`will_take_our_courses`, select_multiple, required: false): Let us know if you would Will take our courses again.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, text, required: false): If you have any other comments or concerns, please share them with us.
