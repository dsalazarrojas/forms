# Learning Readiness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to evaluate a user's readiness to take a learning course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions honestly, and make sure to select all required fields. You may select multiple options for questions that allow for multiple choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Please introduce yourself** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first question on the form where you will introduce yourself. Please provide a brief introduction about who you are and why you are interested in the course.
* **Are you interested in the course?** (`question1_section1`, `select_one`, true): Please select whether you are interested in the course or not.
* **How motivated are you to take the course?** (`question2_section1`, `select_one`, required): Please select your level of motivation to take the course.
* **Do you have the required skills for the course?** (`question3_section1`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the skills you have for the course.
* **How much time do you have available for the course?** (`question4_section1`, `select_one`, required): Please select how much time you have available for the course.
* **What is your current level of knowledge about this topic?** (`question5_section1`, `select_one`, required): Please select your current level of knowledge about this topic.
* **How much experience do you have with this topic?** (`question6_section1`, `select_one`, required): Please select your level of experience with this topic.
* **Are you comfortable with learning through online tutorials?** (`question7_section1`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select whether you are comfortable with learning through online tutorials.
* **Do you have any physical or learning disabilities that may affect your ability to learn?** (`question8_section1`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you have any physical or learning disabilities that may affect your ability to learn.
* **Thank you for taking the quiz!** (`final_page`, `text`, required/optional): This is the final question, which is not required but a thank you message for taking the quiz.
