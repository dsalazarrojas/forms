# Passenger Inflight Service Preference Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects passenger feedback on their in-flight experiences, specifically their preferences regarding flight and service quality.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred flight number from the flight number dropdown list.
2. Choose your preferred airline type from the cabin preferences list.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the flight service.
4. Indicate whether you would like to increase, decrease or have no change in flight services.
5. Choose your preferred routes.
6. Enter your email address and phone number to provide personal contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Flight Preferences** (`flight_preferences`): Select your preferred flights from the list.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Bad
	+ Terrible
* **Cabin Preferences** (`cabin_preferences`): Choose your preferred airline type.
	+ A320
	+ B737
	+ C747
	+ D777
* **Service Satisfaction** (`service_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with the flight service.
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`): Indicate whether you would like to increase, decrease or have no change in flight services.
	+ Increase
	+ Decrease
	+ No change
* **Route Preferences** (`route_preferences`): Choose your preferred routes.
	+ A
	+ B
	+ C
* **Cabin Preferences 2** (`cabin_preferences_2`): Choose your preferred airline type 2.
	+ A
	+ B
	+ C
* **Comments** (`comments`, `comments_2`, `comments_3`, `comments_4`, `comments_5`, `comments_6`, `comments_7`, `comments_8`, `comments_9`, `comments_10`, `comments_11`, `comments_12`): Enter any comments you may have about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
