# Laptop Research Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect data on laptop usage habits and preferences of users. It helps businesses and organizations understand their audience's behavior and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction page.
2. Select your laptop usage type (work, personal, or both).
3. Choose the type of laptop you currently use (Mac, PC, Linux, or other).
4. Select multiple options for your laptop brand, model, and screen resolution.
5. Enter the year your laptop was purchased.
6. Select how often you use your laptop (daily, weekly, monthly, or rarely).
7. Choose how long you typically use your laptop per day.
8. Select multiple options for your laptop storage capacity.
9. Choose your laptop connectivity options (Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or both).
10. Select multiple options for your laptop operating system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Laptop Research Survey** (`intro`, `note`, `required: false`):
  This is an introduction page that explains the purpose of the form. Please read it carefully to understand the survey questions.
* **What is your laptop usage?** (`laptop_usage`, `select_one`, `required: true`):
  Select one of the options: Laptop for work, Laptop for personal use, or Both.
* **What type of laptop do you currently use?** (`laptop_type`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`):
  Choose multiple types of laptops you currently use (Mac, PC, Linux, or Other).
* **Laptop brand** (`laptop_brand`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`):
  Select multiple brands of laptops you currently use (Apple, Dell, HP, Lenovo, or Other).
* **Laptop model** (`laptop_model`, `text`, `required: false`):
  Enter the model of your current laptop.
* **Year laptop was purchased** (`laptop_purchase_year`, `date`, `required: false`):
  Enter the year you purchased your laptop.
* **How often do you use your laptop?** (`laptop_use_frequency`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`):
  Select multiple options for how often you use your laptop (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely).
* **How long do you typically use your laptop per day?** (`laptop_usage_duration`, `time`, `required: false`):
  Enter the time you typically use your laptop per day.
* **Laptop screen resolution** (`laptop_screen_resolution`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`):
  Select multiple options for your laptop screen resolution (720p, 1080p, 1440p, 2160p, or 4K).
* **Laptop storage capacity** (`laptop_storage`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`):
  Select multiple options for your laptop storage capacity (128 GB, 256 GB, 512 GB, 1 TB, 2 TB, or More than 2 TB).
* **Laptop connectivity options** (`laptop_connectivity`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`):
  Select multiple options for your laptop connectivity (Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or Both).
* **Laptop operating system** (`laptop_operating_system`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`):
  Select multiple options for your laptop operating system (Windows, macOS, Linux, Chrome OS, or Other).
