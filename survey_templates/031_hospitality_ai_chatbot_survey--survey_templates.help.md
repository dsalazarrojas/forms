# Hospitality AI Chatbot Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather user feedback on their interaction with the Hospitality AI Chatbot. It is designed to be a quick and easy way for users to provide their input and help us improve the chatbot's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and make sure you have interacted with the chatbot before proceeding.
2. Answer each field with as much detail as possible, but feel free to skip any field that you don't want to fill out.
3. Use the options provided for select_one fields (e.g., Very Good, Good, Fair, Bad).
4. Enter your name and email address in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief description of the form, explaining what it's about and why we're asking you to fill it out.
- **How was the chatbot interaction?** (`how_was_the_chatbot_interaction`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the chatbot interaction on a scale of Very Good, Good, Fair, Bad.
- **Do you have further feedback?** (`do_you_have_further_feedback`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions for the chatbot, feel free to enter them here.
- **What is your name?** (`what_is_your_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name so we can address you personally in our response.
- **What is your email?** (`what_is_your_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if necessary.
- **What is your phone number?** (`what_is_your_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
- Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form and help us improve the chatbot!
