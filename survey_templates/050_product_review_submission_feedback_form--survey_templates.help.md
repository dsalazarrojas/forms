# Product Review Submission Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on products to help improve our product offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, but only provide information about the specific product you're reviewing.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Enter your submission date and time accurately.
4. Choose the correct submission channel (e.g., Website, Phone, Email).
5. Select whether or not you would recommend the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional information about the product, such as its name, description, or features.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please share any thoughts, comments, or concerns you have about the product.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, number, optional): Rate the product on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Product Review** (`review`, text, optional): Write a detailed review of the product, including its pros and cons.
* **Would you recommend this product?** (`recommend`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you would recommend the product, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this feedback form. This will be automatically populated if you don't fill it out.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted this feedback form. This will be automatically populated if you don't fill it out.
* **Submission Channel** (`submission_channel`, select_one, required): Choose how you submitted this feedback form (e.g., Website, Phone, Email).
* **Submission IP** (`submission_ip`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you accessed the submission form from a Public Network or Private Network.

Note: The fields with an asterisk (\*) are required fields and must be filled out to submit the form.
