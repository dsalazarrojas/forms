# Livestock Worker Health Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about the health and safety awareness of workers on livestock farms. Your participation is crucial in helping us better understand the needs and challenges faced by farm workers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. You may select multiple options for each question.
3. If you encounter any difficulty or have questions, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm location** (`farm_location`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of your farm location.
* **Worker type** (`worker_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Farm worker
	+ Livestock handler
	+ Farm equipment operator
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Health issues experienced** (`health_issues`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check all that apply:
	+ Musculoskeletel
	+ Cardiovascular
	+ Respiratory
	+ Mental health
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Safety practices followed on the farm** (`safety_practices`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Always follow safety rules
	+ Sometimes follow safety rules
	+ Rarely follow safety rules
* **Have access to care** (`access_care`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Undecided
* **Have access to training programs** (`training_programs`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Undecided
* **Have an emergency plan in place** (`emergency_plan`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Undecided
* **Farm size** (`farm_size`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of acres your farm covers.
* **Farm income** (`farm_income`, number, required/optional): Enter the total income of your farm.
* **Worker experience** (`worker_experience`, number, required/optional): Enter the average number of years of experience of your workers.
* **Worker hours per day** (`worker_hours`, number, required/optional): Enter the average number of hours your workers spend per day.
* **Worker ages** (`worker_ages`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ <18, 65
	+ 18 - 29
	+ 30 - 45
	+ 46 - 65
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Worker languages** (`worker_languages`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ Other
* **Farm equipment used** (`farm_equipment`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Tractors
	+ Tractors and other farm equipment
	+ Other
* **Farm species** (`farm_species`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Cattle
	+ Pigs
	+ Horses
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Farm products** (`farm_products`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Livestock
	+ Grains and seeds
	+ Fruits and vegetables
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Health services used** (`health_services`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Medical services
	+ Mental health services
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Farm products sold** (`farm_products_sold`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Undecided
* **Farm equipment used on the farm** (`farm_equipment_used`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Tractors
	+ Trucks
	+ Other
* **Farm species sold** (`farm_species_sold`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Cattle
	+ Pigs
	+ Horses
	+ Other
* **Average worker hours per day** (`worker_hours_per_day`, number, required/optional): Enter the average number of hours your workers spend per day.
* **Average worker income** (`worker_income`, number, required/optional): Enter the average income of your workers.
* **Average farm products sold per week** (`farm_products_sold`, number, required/optional): Enter the average number of farm products sold per week.
* **Total farm income** (`total_farm_income`, number, required/optional): Enter the total income of your farm.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, select "Other (please specify)" for your answer. 
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact us.
