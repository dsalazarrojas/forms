# Wedding Vendor Post Event Satisfaction Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help wedding vendors assess and report their satisfaction with the overall performance of our team after an event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the vendor category (Wedding Venue, Caterer, Florist, etc.) from the "vendor" field dropdown list.
2. Rate your satisfaction with our overall performance on a scale of 1-5 stars, selecting all relevant stars for each category (rating_1, vendor_rating_2, vendor_rating_3).
3. Provide any comments or notes about your experience in the "comments" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the event in the "event_date" and "event_time" fields.
5. Enter your contact information (name, email, phone number) in the "contact" field.
6. Enter any additional information or notes about the event in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Vendor Post Event Satisfaction Check In Form** (`wedding_vendor_post_event_satisfaction_check_in_form`): This is the main form title.
* **Vendor** (`vendor`): Select the category of vendor you represented (e.g., Wedding Venue, Caterer, Florist, etc.).
* **Rating** (`rating`): This field is not used, instead use the separate "rating_1", "vendor_rating_2", and "rating_3" fields for rating the overall performance.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Contact** (`contact`): Enter your name, email, and phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional information or notes about the event.
* **Vendor Rating 1** (`rating_1`): Rate our overall performance as 1, 2, or 3 stars for the first category.
* **Vendor Rating 2** (`vendor_rating_2`): Rate our overall performance as 1, 2, or 3 stars for the second category.
* **Vendor Rating 3** (`rating_3`): Rate our overall performance as 1, 2, or 3 stars for the third category.
