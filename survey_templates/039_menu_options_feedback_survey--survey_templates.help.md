# menu_options_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on users' preferences for menu options, including their top choice, rating, desired menu items, and frequency and price range.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your top choice from the menu list.
2. Rate your top choice on a scale of 1-5.
3. Choose which menu items you would like to see more of.
4. Select the frequency with which you order your preferred menu items.
5. Optionally, specify other menu items you would like to see more of or the frequency with which you order them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your top choice from the menu** (`menu_item`): Select the menu item that you prefer from the list.
* **Rate your top choice** (`menu_item_rating`): Rate your top choice on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **On a scale of 1-5** (`rating_scale`): This field is not directly used in the form, but it's a redundant label from the original form schema. Please ignore this field.
* **Rating** (`rating_scale_label`): Select the rating you chose for your top choice from the options 1-5.
* **Which menu items would you like to see more of** (`menu_items`): Select all the menu items you would like to see more often.
* **Specify other menu items** (`menu_items_other`): If there's a menu item not listed, specify it here.
* **Which menu items would you like to see more of - Specify other menu items** (`menu_items_other`): This field is redundant and should be ignored.
* **How often do you order your preferred menu items** (`menu_item_frequency`): Select how often you order your preferred menu item (Daily, Weekly, Rarely).
* **Specify frequency** (`menu_item_frequency_other`): This field is redundant and should be ignored.
* **What is the price range of your preferred menu items?** (`menu_item_price`): Select the price range ($, $$, $$$) of your preferred menu item.
