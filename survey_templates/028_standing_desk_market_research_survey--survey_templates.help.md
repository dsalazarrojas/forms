# standing_desk_market_research_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather market research on standing desk adoption and user experience. It aims to collect data on user preferences, behavior, and company characteristics to inform product development and marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability. You may skip questions that do not apply to you. Please provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have you ever used a standing desk?** (`standing_desk_adoption`, text, required): This question is meant to gauge your experience with standing desks. If you have used a standing desk, please describe your experience. If not, please let us know why you have not used one.
* **Why do you use or want to use a standing desk?** (`standing_desk_purpose`, text, required): This question is meant to understand your motivations for using or wanting to use a standing desk. Please provide a brief explanation.
* **What type of standing desk do you have or would you want to have?** (`desk_type`, select_multiple, required): This question is meant to understand your preferences for standing desk types. You may select multiple options.
* **How many employees does your company have?** (`company_size`, number, required): This question is meant to understand the size of your company. Please provide a number.
* **How likely are you to buy a standing desk in the next 6 months?** (`buying_intent`, number, required): This question is meant to gauge your purchase intent. Please rate your likelihood on a scale of 0-10.
* **What is your budget for a standing desk?** (`budget`, number, required): This question is meant to understand your budget for a standing desk. Please provide a number.
* **What ergonomic features do you consider important?** (`ergonomic_features`, select_multiple, required): This question is meant to understand your preferences for ergonomic features. You may select multiple options.
* **Does your standing desk have height adjustment?** (`height_adjustment`, select_multiple, required): This question is meant to understand your standing desk's height adjustment capabilities. You may select multiple options.
* **On average, how much time do you spend sitting on your standing desk?** (`sit_stand_height`, time, required): This question is meant to understand your usage habits on your standing desk. Please provide a time range.
* **On average, how much time do you spend standing on your standing desk?** (`stand_time`, time, required): This question is meant to understand your usage habits on your standing desk. Please provide a time range.
* **What is your preferred height for standing while working?** (`preferred_height`, number, required): This question is meant to understand your preferred height for standing while working. Please provide a number.
* **What type of surface do you prefer on your standing desk?** (`work_surface`, select_multiple, required): This question is meant to understand your preferences for the surface type on your standing desk. You may select multiple options.
* **How often do you use your standing desk?** (`sit_stand_ratio`, select_multiple, required): This question is meant to understand your usage frequency on your standing desk. You may select multiple options.
* **What motivated you to get a standing desk?** (`purchase_reason`, text, required): This question is meant to understand your motivations for purchasing a standing desk. Please provide a brief explanation.
* **Were you recommended a standing desk by a colleague, friend, or family member?** (`recommended_by`, select_multiple, required): This question is meant to understand if you were influenced by others to purchase a standing desk. You may select multiple options.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to share about your standing desk experience?** (`other_info`, note, required): This question is meant to gather any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Would you be open to follow-up questions or discussions about your standing desk experience?** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, required): This question is meant to understand your willingness to participate in further discussions.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, required): This question is meant to gather your contact information.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text, required): This question is meant to gather your phone number.
* **Any additional comments?** (`comments`, text, required): This question is meant to gather any further comments or feedback.
* **Any other information?** (`other`, text, required): This question is meant to gather any additional information.
* **Any other comments or feedback?** (`survey_comments`, text, required): This question is meant to gather any other comments or feedback.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving this survey?** (`survey_suggestions`, text, required): This question is meant to gather any suggestions for improving the survey.
* **What is your company website?** (`company_website`, text, required): This question is meant to gather your company website.
* **What is your company social media presence?** (`company_social_media`, text, required): This question is meant to gather your company social media presence.
