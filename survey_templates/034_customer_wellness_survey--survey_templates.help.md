# Customer Wellness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assess a customer's wellness and symptoms, helping us understand their current state and provide targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and provide clear and accurate answers.
2. If a customer has had multiple contacts with your organization, make sure to fill out the form for each individual contact separately.
3. Be thorough and provide as much information as possible, but do not worry if some questions are left blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Customer Wellness** (`patient_wellness`, text, optional)

* This field is meant to capture the customer's overall wellness state. Please describe their current wellness using your own words.

### 2. **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, optional)

* Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 3. **Date of Last Contact** (`date_of_last_contact`, date, optional)

* Enter the date of the customer's last interaction with your organization.

### 4. **Time of Last Contact** (`time_of_last_contact`, time, optional)

* Enter the time of the customer's last interaction with your organization.

### 5. **Patient Well-Being Scale** (`patient_wellness_scale`, select_one, required)

* Choose a rating from 1 to 3:
	+ 1 (Very Poor)
	+ 2 (Fair)
	+ 3 (Good)

### 6. **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, select_multiple, optional)

* Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 7. **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional)

* Describe any next steps or follow-up actions that need to be taken for the customer.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about a question, leave it blank or mark it as "Unknown".
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or colleague.
