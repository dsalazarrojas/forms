# Crystal Form Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Crystal Form Submission" form is designed to collect information from users about their crystal submissions, which includes the date of submission, user contact details, event information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Submit the form**: Simply click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. **Fill out the form**: Complete all the required fields (none are required, but fill out as many as possible for a complete submission).
3. **Click "Submit" again**: Once you have filled out all the fields, click "Submit" to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. form_submission_date** (Date)
*   A field to enter the date of your crystal form submission. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### **2. user_email** (Email)
*   Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### **3. event_name** (Text)
*   Enter the name of the event related to your crystal form submission.

### **4. message** (Note)
*   Enter any additional message or notes related to your crystal form submission.

### **5. application_type** (Select One)
*   Choose the type of application: Feedback, Application, or Event.

### **6. user_name** (Text)
*   Enter your name for reference purposes.

### **7. form_submission_time** (Time)
*   Enter the time of your crystal form submission.

### **8. application_id** (Number)
*   Enter a unique identifier for the application, if applicable.

Tips:
*   Make sure to fill out all the fields as accurately and completely as possible.
*   Use the correct format for dates and times.
*   If you are unsure about any field, you can always ask for help or clarification.
*   Once submitted, you will not be able to make changes to your submission.
