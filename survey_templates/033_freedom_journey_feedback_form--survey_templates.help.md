# Freedom Journey Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants after a session, event, or experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Session Satisfaction` field with your honest opinion about the session.
2. Share your recommendations for improvement in the `Recommendations` field.
3. Express your sentiment about the session in the `Participant Sentiment` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Satisfaction**: This field is where you can rate your overall satisfaction with the session. Please type in a brief answer, such as "good", "bad", or provide a specific reason why you felt that way.
* **Recommendations**: If you have any suggestions or ideas for how to improve the session, please type them in this field. This is a great opportunity to help us learn and grow.
* **Participant Sentiment**: This field is where you can express your emotions or feelings about the session. Please type in a brief answer, such as "positive", "negative", or provide a specific reason why you felt that way.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve future sessions.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas, please make sure to type them clearly and concisely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
