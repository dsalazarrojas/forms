# Preventive Health Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand individuals' habits, attitudes, and experiences related to preventive health. Please answer the questions honestly to help us improve our services and programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill out all the required fields (indicated by asterisks).
2. Select the options that best describe your habits and attitudes related to preventive health.
3. Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a text field to gather your thoughts about your current health engagement. You can write anything you like in this field.
* **Age**: Please enter your age in years.
* **Income**: Select one of the following income ranges:
	+ Less than 20,000
	+ 20,000-40,000
	+ More than 40,000
* **Occupation**: This is a text field to provide any additional comments or information about your occupation or job.
* **Habits**: Select all the habits that best describe your daily routine:
	+ I exercise regularly
	+ I eat a balanced diet
	+ I get enough sleep
	+ I take care of my mental health
	+ I get regular check-ups
	+ I practice stress management
	+ I use relaxation techniques
	+ I take my medications as prescribed
	+ I have a healthy relationship with food
	+ I engage in physical activity
	+ I get vaccinated
	+ I have a healthy relationship with substances
* **Attitudes**: Select all the attitudes that best describe your motivation and confidence:
	+ I am motivated to make healthy changes
	+ I am confident in my abilities
	+ I am willing to make changes to improve my health
	+ I have the knowledge to make healthy choices
	+ I have the resources to make healthy changes
* **Barriers**: This is a text field to provide any additional comments or information about any barriers you face in maintaining healthy habits or attitudes.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to help us understand your needs and experiences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us.
* This survey is completely anonymous and your responses will be kept confidential.
