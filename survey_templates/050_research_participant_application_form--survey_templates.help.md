# Research Participant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in research projects or studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide the participant's contact information in the "Participant Contact Info" field.
3. Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant Email" field.
4. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
5. If applicable, provide any additional information about the participant in the "Participant Additional Info" field.
6. Indicate whether the participant was conducted by the research team or not in the "Participant Conducted By" field.
7. Select the participant's informed consent status in the "Participant Informed Consent" field.
8. Select whether the participant agrees to participate in the research in the "Participant Agreement" field.
9. Select whether the participant has references or not in the "Participant References" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (research_participant_application_form_data): This field is for the participant's name.
* **Participant Contact Info** (research_participant_contact_info): This field is for any contact information you may have for the participant.
* **Participant Email** (research_participant_contact_email): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (participant_phone): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Additional Info** (research_participant_additional_info): This field is for any additional information you may have about the participant.
* **Participant Conducted By** (research_participant_conducted_by): Indicate whether you conducted the participant or not.
* **Participant Informed Consent** (research_participant_informed_consent): Select the participant's informed consent status.
* **Participant Agreement** (research_participant_agreement): Indicate whether the participant agrees to participate in the research.
* **Participant References** (research_participant_references): Select whether the participant has references or not.
