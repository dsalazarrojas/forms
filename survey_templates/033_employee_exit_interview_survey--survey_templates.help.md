# Employee Exit Interview Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Exit Interview Survey is a tool used to gather information from departing employees about their reasons for leaving and their overall experience. This will help us understand their perspectives and make informed decisions for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. For select_multiple and select_one fields, choose one or more of the provided options.
4. If you have any additional comments, use the `comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reason for Leaving**: 
  * This field allows you to select one or more of the provided options to describe why you are leaving. 
  * Please provide specific reasons to help us understand your decision.

  * Why are you leaving?
  * What opportunities are you leaving behind?
  * What are your plans after leaving?

* **New Employer Details**: 
  * This field is for you to describe your new employer and any details about your new role.
  * Please provide as much information as possible to help us understand your new situation.

* **Communication**: 
  * This field allows you to rate the communication style of your previous manager.
  * You can choose from three options: Effective, Ineffective, or Neutral.
  * This will help us understand how we can improve our communication within the organization.

* **Feedback**: 
  * This field is for you to provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
  * Please be as specific and honest as possible.

* **Suggestions**: 
  * This field is for you to suggest any improvements or changes you think we can make within the organization.
  * Please be as specific and detailed as possible.

* **Comments**: 
  * This field is for any additional comments or observations you may have.
  * Please use this field to provide any information not covered in the other fields.

* **Overall Experience**:
