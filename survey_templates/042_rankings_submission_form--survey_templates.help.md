# Rankings Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rankings Submission Form is used to collect user input and ranking data for analysis and decision-making processes. The form is designed to be completed by individuals, providing an opportunity to provide their rankings for various topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the form link to access the form.
* Review the form questions carefully.
* Select the relevant options for each question, as indicated by the label and options provided.
* Enter your answers or rankings in the provided fields.
* Review your selections before submitting the form.
* Submit the form once you have completed all the questions and reviewed your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (text): Provide a brief statement or description to accompany your ranking selections.
* **Ranking** (select_one): Choose one option from the list provided. Options are:
	+ One
	+ Two
	+ Three
* **Score** (number): Enter a numerical value to represent your ranking or score.
* **Ordered List** (select_multiple): Select one or more options from the list provided. Multiple selections are allowed.
	+ One
	+ Two
	+ Three
* **Date** (date): Enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (time): Enter a time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Note 1-17** (note): Provide additional notes or comments for each note field. These fields are not required and can be used to provide supplementary information.

Note: This form is designed for users to provide their rankings and input, and some fields may be optional. Please ensure that you have reviewed and understood each question before submitting the form.
