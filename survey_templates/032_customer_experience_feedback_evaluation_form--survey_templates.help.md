# Customer Experience Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers to improve our services and products. We use this information to identify areas of improvement and implement changes to enhance your experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Survey Form**: This section is an open-ended text area where you can express your thoughts and opinions about your experience with us.
2. Fill out the **Feedback Title** field to provide a brief title for your feedback.
3. **Feedback Description**: Please provide a detailed description of your experience, including any positive or negative aspects.
4. **Rating**: Evaluate your experience by selecting a rating from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
5. **Comment**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them in this field.
6. **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you to follow up on your feedback.
7. **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you for further clarification, if necessary.
8. **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Title (Feedback Title)**: A brief title for your feedback, which will be used to identify your entry in our database. (*Required*)
* **Feedback Description (Feedback description)**: A detailed description of your experience, including any positive or negative aspects. (*Required*)
* **Rating (Rating)**: Evaluate your experience by selecting a rating from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest. (*Required*)
* **Comment (Comment)**: Any additional comments or suggestions you may have. (*Optional*)
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you to follow up on your feedback. (*Required*)
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you for further clarification, if necessary. (*Required*)
* **Submit (Submit)**: Click this button to send your feedback to us. (*Required*)
