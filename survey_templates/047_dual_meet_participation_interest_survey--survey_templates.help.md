# Dual Meet Participation Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is used to collect information from parents and coaches about their interest in participating in dual meet events. The survey helps us understand what events and dates work best for them, ensuring a smooth and successful dual meet experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your preferred dates and times for participation in dual meet events.
2. Select the correct options for your interest and availability.
3. Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, and school details.
4. If applicable, provide emergency contact information.
5. Confirm your participation and consent for your child/coach to attend dual meet events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dual Meet Participation Interest Survey Form** (`dual_meet_participation_interest_survey_form`): This is the main form header, please fill out the information below.
* **Participation Interest** (`participation_interest`): Select your interest in participating in dual meet events. Please choose one of the options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Availability** (`availability`): If you have a specific date you cannot participate, please enter it here (optional).
* **Consent** (`consent`): Confirm that you consent to your child/coach attending dual meet events. Please choose one of the options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): If applicable, please enter the name of your school.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name and email of the contact person for your school.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person for your school.
* **School Address** (`school_address`): If applicable, please enter the address of your school.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`): If applicable, please enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`): If applicable, enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`): If applicable, enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Comments** (`comments`): If you have any additional comments or information, please enter them here (optional).
* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`): Confirm that your child's parent consents to participating in dual meet events. Please choose one of the options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **School Email** (`school_email`): If applicable, enter the email of your school.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`): If applicable, enter the phone number of your school.
* **Coach Consent** (`coach_consent`): Confirm that your coach consents to participating in dual meet events. Please choose one of the options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Meet Day** (`meet_day`): If applicable, enter the day of the meet.
* **Meet Time** (`meet_time`): If applicable, enter the time of the meet.
* **Meet Date** (`meet_date`): If applicable, enter the date of the meet.
* **Meet Start Time** (`meet_start_time`): If applicable, enter the start time of the meet.
* **Meet End Time** (`meet_end_time`): If applicable, enter the end time of the meet.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`): If applicable, enter the name of the coach.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`): If applicable, enter the name of the team.
* **Team Email** (`team_email`): If applicable, enter the email of the team.
* **Team Phone** (`team_phone`): If applicable, enter the phone number of the team.
