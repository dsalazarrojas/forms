NGO Technology Capacity Survey - Help Guide

### Purpose
The NGO Technology Capacity Survey is a form used to evaluate the technology capabilities of an NGO (Non-Governmental Organization) to provide support services and assess its overall capacity to effectively utilize technology.

To ensure the survey is relevant and accurate, we should note that this form is designed for NGOS and is intended to assess their technological capacity in providing support services and other relevant areas. It is not designed for individual assessment, but rather for organizations to report on their collective capacity. Therefore, the questions are framed accordingly.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the correct responses based on the options provided in the form.
2. Be honest and accurate when providing information about your organization's technology capabilities.
3. If an option does not apply to your organization, select "None" or leave the field blank (if not required).

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Id** (`organization_id`, number, required: false): Enter your organization's unique identifier.
* **Tech Leads Id** (`tech_leads_id`, number, required: false): Enter your organization's technology lead's identifier.
* **Has Technology Leads** (`has_technology_leads`, select_one, required: false): Check if your organization has technology leads.
* **Tech Leads Count** (`tech_leads_count`, number, required: false): Enter the number of technology leads you have.
* **Has Digital Skills** (`has_digital_skills`, select_one, required: false): Check if your organization has digital skills.
* **Has ICT Equipment** (`has_ict_equipment`, select_multiple, required: false): Check what ICT equipment your organization has. Options include Computers, Laptops, Tablets, and Mobile Phones.
* **Has Internet Access** (`has_internet_access`, select_multiple, required: true): Check if your organization has access to the internet. Options include Fast Internet, Medium Internet, and No Internet.
* **Has Support Services** (`has_support_services`, select_multiple, required: true): Check what support services your organization offers. Options include Email Support, Phone Support, and Online Chat Support.
* **Has Technology Support** (`has_technology_support`, select_multiple, required: false): Check what technology support your organization offers. Options include Email Support, Phone Support, and Online Chat Support.
* **Has Technology Training** (`has_technology_training`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if your organization offers technology training. Options include Basic Computer Training, Advanced Computer Training.
* **Has Technology Resources** (`has_technology_resources`, number, required: false): Enter the number of technology resources your organization has.
* **Has Digital Security** (`has_digital_security`, select_multiple, required: false): Check what digital security measures your organization uses. Options include Firewall, Anti-virus, Encryption, and Backup.
* **Has Digital Storage** (`has_digital_storage`, select_multiple, required: false): Check what digital storage options your organization uses. Options include Local Server, Cloud Storage.
* **Has Network Admin** (`has_network_admin`, select_multiple, required: true): Check if your organization has a network admin. Options include Network Admin, System Admin, and IT Manager.
* **Has IT Training** (`has_it_training`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if your organization offers IT training. Options include Network Training, System Training, IT Training.
* **IT Skills Level** (`it_skills_level`, select_multiple, required: true): Check your organization's IT skills level. Options include Basic, Advanced, Expert.
* **Has Other Technology** (`has_other_technology`, select_multiple, required: true): Check what other technologies your organization uses. Options include Software, Hardware.

Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields accurately and honestly.
