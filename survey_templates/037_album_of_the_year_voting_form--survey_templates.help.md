# Album Of The Year Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Album Of The Year Voting Form" is an annual music award voting system that allows users to choose their favorite albums in different categories. This guide will walk you through the form fields and help you complete it successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Register as a voter by providing your email address and selecting your primary music genre of interest.
2. Choose your favorite album for each category, such as "Album of the Year," "Best Debut Album," "Best Produced Album," and "Breakthrough Artist of the Year."
3. Provide a reason for your main category choice and optionally provide a hidden gem or honorable mention.
4. Confirm that this is your only vote for this year.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter Registration** (`Voter Email Address`, `Email`, required): This section is where you register as a voter. Please provide a valid email address to ensure we can contact you and keep you updated on the results.
* **Voter Email Address** (`Voter Email Address`, `Email`, true): Enter your email address to receive updates on the voting results and other music award related information.
* **Region/Country** (`Voter Location`, `Text`, false): This field is optional and not required for voting. You can choose your region or country of origin.
* **Primary Music Genre of Interest** (`Primary Genre of Interest`, `Select One`, true): Select the primary music genre that you are most interested in. This will help us better understand your musical preferences.
* **The Nominations** (`Nominations`, `Note`, false): This is a note section for you to review the nominees before making your selections.
* **Album of the Year** (`Album of the Year`, `Select One`, true): Choose the album that defined your year in the music world.
* **Best Debut Album** (`Best Debut Album`, `Select One`, true): Select the best debut album that caught your attention this year.
* **Best Produced Album** (`Best Produced Album`, `Select One`, true): Choose the album with the best sound design and engineering.
* **Breakthrough Artist of the Year** (`Breakthrough Artist of the Year`, `Select One`, true): Select the artist who made a significant impact this year.
* **Hidden Gem or Under-the-radar Favorite** (`Hidden Gem or Under-the-radar Favorite`, `Text`, false): Share a great album that people might have missed.
* **Why did you choose your Album of the Year?** (`Reason for Main Vote`, `Text`, true): Explain why you chose your main category choice.
* **Are you a member of any of the nominees fan clubs?** (`Fan Loyalty`, `Select One`, false): This field is optional and not required for voting. Please select if you're a member of any of the nominees' fan clubs.
* **Opt-in to our music awards newsletter for results** (`Mailing List Optin`, `Select One`, true): Choose to receive updates on the voting results and other music award related information.
* **I confirm this is my only vote for this year** (`Validation Check`, `Select One`, true): Confirm that this is your only vote for this year to ensure fair voting practices.
