# Classic Literature Reading Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to track participants' reading habits, preferences, and experiences with classic literature. The goal is to gather data to help book clubs, libraries, or reading groups optimize their reading programs and provide better experiences for their members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in the participant's details**: Enter your full name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. **Provide book-specific information**: For each classic literature title listed, select the option that best describes your progress with that book: "Completed", "In Progress", "Started", or "Not Started". If you've read the book, you can provide a rating and reflection in the corresponding "Rating and Reflection" field.
3. **List additional reads**: If you've read other classic literature not listed, describe them in the "Other Classic Books Read This Period" field.
4. **Choose your preferences**: Select your favorite classic literature genre and rate the difficulty of the books you've read.
5. **Share your reading habits**: Indicate how often you read classic literature and whether you're interested in participating in group discussions about these books.
6. **Add any comments or suggestions**: Share any feedback or suggestions you have about the reading program.

* **Book Club or Organization**: The name of the book club or library you're affiliated with, if applicable.
* **Reporting Period**: The month and year for this reading report.

	+ **Not Started**: You haven't begun reading the book.
