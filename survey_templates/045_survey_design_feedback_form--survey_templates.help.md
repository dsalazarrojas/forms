# Survey Design Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survey Design Feedback Form is used to gather feedback from participants on the effectiveness of surveys. This form collects information on the participant's experience, survey rating, and comments, which can be used to improve the survey design process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions on the first page of the form.
2. Provide your participant information, including your ID, name, email, phone number, and survey title and description.
3. Rate the survey using the provided scale.
4. Enter any comments or feedback you may have about the survey.
5. Submit your feedback by selecting "Submit" and complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Page** (`first_page`, text, optional): This is the beginning of the form where you'll start providing your feedback.
- **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, select_one, optional): This field asks if you are a participant in the survey. Please select 'Yes' if you are, and 'No' if you are not.
- **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, number, optional): This field is for your participant ID.
- **Survey Information** (`survey_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional information about the survey.
- **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): This is the title of the survey you took.
- **Survey Description** (`survey_description`, text, optional): This field is for entering a description of the survey.
- **Survey Rating** (`survey_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the survey using the provided scale.
    - **Very Good**
    - **Somewhat Good**
    - **Neutral**
    - **Somewhat Poor**
    - **Poor**
- **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): This field is for providing any comments or feedback about the survey.
- **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Select "Submit" to complete and submit your feedback.
- **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): This field will be automatically populated with the date you submit your feedback.
- **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): This field will be automatically populated with the time you submit your feedback.
- **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): This field will be automatically populated with a unique ID for the form.
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): This field is for your name as a participant.
- **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): This field is for your email address as a participant.
- **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): This field is for your phone number as a participant.
