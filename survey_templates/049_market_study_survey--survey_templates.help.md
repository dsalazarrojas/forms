# market_study_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The market_study_survey form is designed to collect data on customer opinions, buying habits, and contact information for market analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages below, which include:
	* **Customer Opinions**
	* **Buying Habits**
	* **Contact Info**
2. Select the appropriate answer for each field based on your knowledge.
3. Fill out the form completely, but note that fields with "required: false" are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Opinions
* **Customer Opinions** (`customer_opinions`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This field asks about customers' general opinions on various aspects of the market.
	+ Select all options that apply.

### Buying Habits
* **Buying Habits** (`buying_habits`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This field asks about your buying habits.
	+ Choose the answer that best describes your behavior.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for collecting your contact information.
	+ Enter your email or phone number to be contacted for further analysis.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask a colleague or supervisor for guidance.
* This form is for market analysis purposes, so please be respectful of business confidentiality.
