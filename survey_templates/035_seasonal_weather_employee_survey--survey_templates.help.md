# Seasonal Weather Employee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Weather Employee Survey is a form designed to collect information about seasonal weather conditions and employee feedback on how they handled it. By filling out this form, you will be providing valuable insights that can help us improve our responses to extreme weather events and better support our employees during these situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Weather Conditions field with a brief description of the weather conditions you experienced during the event.
2. In the Employee Feedback field, provide your feedback on how you handled the weather conditions, including any challenges you faced and any suggestions for improvement.
3. If you received extreme weather support, select the level of support you received from our team.
4. Describe the safety practices you followed during the event in the Safety Practices field.
5. Provide any additional information or comments in the Planning field.
6. Enter your company email address in the Email field.
7. Enter your company phone number in the Phone field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about the event in the Note field.
9. Fill in the Date and Time fields with the date and time of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Weather Conditions** (`seasonal_weather_employee_survey`, text, optional): Describe the weather conditions you experienced during the event.
- **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, optional): Provide your feedback on how you handled the weather conditions, including any challenges you faced and any suggestions for improvement.
- **Extreme Weather Support** (`extreme_weather_support`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of support you received from our team during the event.
  - Extreme Support 1: Received some support but could have done better.
  - Extreme Support 2: Received moderate support and was able to handle the situation effectively.
  - Extreme Support 3: Received excellent support and was able to handle the situation with ease.
- **Safety Practices** (`safety_practices`, text, optional): Describe the safety practices you followed during the event.
- **Planning** (`planning`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about the planning and preparations made for the event.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the event.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Fill in the date of the event.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Fill in the time of the event.
