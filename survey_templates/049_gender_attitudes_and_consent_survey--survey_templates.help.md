# Gender Attitudes And Consent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gender Attitudes And Consent Survey is designed to collect information about users' attitudes and feelings towards different aspects of gender roles, identities, and relationships. This form is meant to gather insights on how users perceive and interact with others based on their gender.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly, and select the options that best match your thoughts and feelings. You may select one or multiple options depending on the question type. Make sure to complete all the required fields (marked as "Required" in the form). If a question has multiple options, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Consent** (`3`, select_one, required: false): This question asks you to select how you perceive consent. Please choose one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Gender Identity** (`4`, text, required: false): Please describe your gender identity. Type in a brief explanation.
* **Role In Public Life** (`5`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks you to select which of the following best describe your role in public life. Please choose one or more of the options:
	+ Very Low
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
	+ Very High

	+ Very Low
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
	+ Very High
* **Attitudes Towards Gender** (`7`, select_one, required: false): This question asks you to select which of the following best describe your attitudes towards traditional gender roles. Please choose one of the following:
	+ Traditional
	+ Non-traditional
	+ Neither
* **Attitudes Towards Consent** (`8`, select_one, required: false): This question asks you to select how you perceive consent. Please choose one of the following:
	+ Forced

* **Feelings Towards Self and Other Gender** (`9`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks you to select which of the following best describe your feelings towards yourself and other gender. Please choose one or more of the options:

* **Beliefs In Society** (`11`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks you to select which of the following best describe your beliefs in society regarding gender roles. Please choose one or more of the options:
	+ Very Traditional

	+ Non-traditional
	+ Very Non-traditional
* **Beliefs In School** (`12`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks you to select which of the following best describe your beliefs in school regarding gender roles. Please choose one or more of the options:
	+ Very Low
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
	+ Very High

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and honestly.
* Select all applicable options for multiple-choice questions.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for help.
* You may stop and save your progress and come back later to complete the form.
* If you need assistance with technical issues, contact our support team.
* Please note that your responses will be treated confidentially and used for research purposes only.
