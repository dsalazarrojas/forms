The form "Language Camp Experience Survey" is designed to collect feedback from participants who have attended a language camp program. The purpose of this survey is to gather information about the overall experience of the participants, including their feedback on the camp staff, facilities, and activities offered. This feedback will help the language camp organizers to identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to provide better services to future participants.

# Language Camp Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your experience at the language camp, including your feedback on the staff, facilities, and activities offered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please answer all the questions honestly, and try to provide as much detail as possible.
2. For the "Overall Experience" question, rate your experience on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
3. For the "Facilities" question, select all the options that apply to your experience.
4. For the "Activities" question, please specify which activities were offered and which ones you enjoyed the most.
5. For the "Suggestions" question, please provide any suggestions you have for future language camps.
6. If you have a contact phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field.
7. Finally, use the "Comments" field to provide any other comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience** (`Overall Experience`, number, required/optional): Rate your experience at the language camp on a scale.
* **How would you rate the camp staff?** (`camp_staff`, select_one, required/optional): Please rate the camp staff as Good or Fair.
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the facilities that were available and useful to you during the camp.
* **Activities** (`activities`, text, optional): Please specify which activities were offered and which ones you enjoyed the most.
* **Do you have any suggestions for future camps?** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions you have for future language camps.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Please provide any other comments or feedback you may have about your experience at the language camp.
