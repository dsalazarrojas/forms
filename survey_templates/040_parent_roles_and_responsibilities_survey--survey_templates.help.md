# Parent Roles And Responsibilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a student's skills and abilities, demographic information, and parent/ guardian involvement in their education. Please answer the questions honestly to help us better understand the student's strengths and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly about your student's skills and abilities.
2. For the select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the best option that describes your student's level of proficiency.
3. For the number fields, rate your student's skills on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
4. Use the "Additional comments" field for any additional information you'd like to share about your student.
