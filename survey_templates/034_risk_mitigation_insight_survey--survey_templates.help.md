# Risk Mitigation Insight Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Risk Mitigation Insight Survey" form is a tool designed to gather information on risk mitigation strategies and their effectiveness. This form will help users understand what measures are being taken to prevent and mitigate risks, and how effective these measures are. It is crucial to fill out this form accurately to gain valuable insights that can inform decision-making and risk management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. For the "Risk Mitigation Priorities" page, select the priority level of your current risk mitigation strategy.
4. For the "Risk Mitigation Effectiveness" page, select all the effective strategies that apply to your current risk mitigation approach.
5. On the "Risk Mitigation Comments" page, provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your risk mitigation strategy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Risk Mitigation Priorities (Page 2)
* **Risk Mitigation Priorities (Page 2, Risk Mitigation Priorities, required/optional)**: This page asks you to select the priority level of your current risk mitigation strategy. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Risk Mitigation High
	+ Risk Mitigation Medium
	+ Risk Mitigation Low

### Risk Mitigation Effectiveness (Page 3)
* **Risk Mitigation Effectiveness (Page 3, Risk Mitigation Effectiveness, required/optional)**: This page allows you to select all the effective strategies that apply to your current risk mitigation approach. Please choose the strategies that are currently being used:
	+ Risk Mitigation Effective
	+ Risk Mitigation Effective 1
	+ Risk Mitigation Effective 2

### Risk Mitigation Comments (Page 4)
* **Risk Mitigation Comments (Page 4, Risk Mitigation Comments, required/optional)**: This page provides a space for you to add any additional comments or feedback about your risk mitigation strategy. Please provide any relevant information that may be helpful for us to understand your approach.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form honestly and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please consult with your team or supervisor for guidance.
* The information collected will be used to inform decision-making and risk management.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
