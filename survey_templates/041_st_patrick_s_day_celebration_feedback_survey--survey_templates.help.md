# St. Patricks Day Celebration Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from attendees at a St. Patricks Day event. It will help us understand your experience and interest in the event, which will inform us on how to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer the questions based on your actual experience with our St. Patricks Day event.
3. Fill in any comments you have about your experience.
4. Submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Attended** (`event_attended`, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate if you actually attended the St. Patricks Day event.
	+ Select "Yes" if you attended the event.
	+ Select "No" if you did not attend the event.
* **How would you rate our St. Patricks Day event?** (`attendance_rate`, number, Optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale from 1 to 100.
	+ Think about how satisfied you were with the event's organization, food, drinks, and activities.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, Optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale from 1 to 100.
	+ This is similar to the previous question, but more specific to your overall experience with the event.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, Optional): Share any thoughts or suggestions you might have about the event.
	+ This is your chance to add any details or comments that didn't fit in the previous questions.
	+ Be honest and specific with your feedback.
