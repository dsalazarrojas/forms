# E Grocery Shopping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your shopping habits and demographics to help improve the grocery shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the frequency of your grocery shopping by choosing one or multiple options from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose your preferred shopping channels, including online and in-store options, by selecting the relevant options.
3. Rate your store experience for both online and in-store shopping.
4. Select your age group from the provided options.
5. Choose your household income range.
6. Select the number of household members.
7. Choose your education level.
8. Select your employment status and that of the household.
9. Indicate if you are employed or not.
