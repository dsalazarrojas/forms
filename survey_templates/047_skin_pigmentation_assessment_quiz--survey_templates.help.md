# skin_pigmentation_assessment_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The skin_pigmentation_assessment_quiz form is used to collect user information about their skin condition, specifically their skin pigmentation levels and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on "Submit" to start the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  Enter your information and select your options for each field as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Info
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about yourself, such as your name, date of birth, or medical history.

### Skin Pigmentation Levels
* **Skin Pigmentation Levels** (`skin_pigmentation_levels`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following skin pigmentation levels:
	+ None
	+ Mild
	+ Moderate
	+ Severe
	+ Very Severe

### Skin Concerns
* **Skin Concerns** (`skin_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the following skin concerns:
	+ Hyper-Pigmentation
	+ Hypopigmentation
	+ Skin Redness
	+ Skin Discoloration

### Skin Pigmentation Treatment
* **Skin Pigmentation Treatment** (`skin_pigmentation_treatment`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about your current skin treatment, such as medications or therapies.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your skin condition that you would like to share with us.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
- Select all relevant skin concerns to provide a comprehensive picture of your skin condition.
- Enter any relevant information about your skin treatment to help us better understand your condition.
