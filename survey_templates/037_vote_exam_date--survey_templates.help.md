# Vote Exam Date - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from exam participants about their experience. It is designed to gather data on exam dates, exam results, and participant responses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by selecting the date of the exam you are responding to.
2. Choose your response for the exam result.
3. If you have a teacher or contact person associated with the exam, enter their name.
4. If you have a contact email or phone number, provide it for follow-up.
5. Review your selections to ensure accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exam Date**: The date of the exam you are responding to. Please enter a valid date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Exam Date (yes, this field is labeled twice - ignore one of them)**: Please ignore this field as it has the same label and name, but different id. You are selecting the date of the exam above.
* **Vote**: Choose your response to the exam result.
* **Teacher Name**: If you have a specific teacher associated with this exam, enter their name.
* **Email**: If you have a contact email associated with this exam, enter it here. Please follow the standard email format (e.g. `jane.doe@example.com`).
* **Phone**: If you have a contact phone number associated with this exam, enter it here.
* **Vote Result**: Select all applicable results that describe your response. 

## Tips
* Please ensure you are selecting the correct exam date.
* Double-check your selections for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field or process, please ask your administrator for clarification.
