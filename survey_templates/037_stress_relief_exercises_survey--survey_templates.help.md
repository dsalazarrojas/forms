# Stress Relief Exercises Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your current stress levels, habits, and needs. Your input will help us better understand how to support you and improve our workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions for each field below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief note for you to provide any additional context or information before proceeding with the survey.
* **Stress Levels**: Choose one of the options that best describes your current stress levels:
	+ Option 1: I feel overwhelmed and anxious.
	+ Option 2: I am moderately stressed.
	+ Option 3: I am relaxed and calm.
* **Stress Habits**: Check all the habits that apply to you:
	+ Yes: I frequently engage in activities that reduce my stress levels (e.g., meditation, exercise, hobbies).
	+ No: I do not regularly practice stress-reducing activities.
* **Program Needs**: Check all the programs or resources you feel are necessary for you:
	+ Yes: More training or workshops on stress management.
	+ Yes: One-on-one coaching or counseling sessions.
	+ Yes: Access to wellness programs or resources (e.g., yoga, mindfulness, mental health professionals).
	+ Yes: Other (please specify in the Additional Information section).
* **Additional Information (optional)**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please provide them here. This is an open text field for any further information you'd like to share.
