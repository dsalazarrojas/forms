# phrase_elimination_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "phrase_elimination_survey" template is designed to collect information about phrases or words that are intended to be retired or replaced in a specific context. This survey aims to gather data on the phrases to be eliminated, the reason for retirement, and any suggested alternatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the survey, providing a clear and concise description of the purpose and context of the survey.
2. Provide a detailed description of the survey, explaining its objectives and goals.
3. Indicate which phrases are to be retired by selecting 'Yes' for each phrase listed in the 'Phrases to Retire' section.
4. Specify the reason for retiring each phrase by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the 'Reason for Retirement' section.
5. Optionally, provide a suggested alternative for each phrase, if applicable.
6. Enter your email address to receive a confirmation of completion.
7. Verify your phone number to confirm the completion of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Phrase Elimination Survey** (`survey_title`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise title of the survey that describes its purpose and context.
* **Phrases to Retire** (`phrases_to_retire`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' for each phrase that is intended to be retired.
* **Reason for Retirement** (`reason_for_retirement`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the reason for retiring each phrase.
* **Suggested Alternative** (`suggested_alternative`, text, optional): Provide a suggested alternative for each phrase, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email_input`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive a confirmation of completion.
* **Phone** (`phone_input`, text, required): Verify your phone number to confirm the completion of the survey.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question carefully and thoroughly to ensure accurate data collection.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Ensure that your email address is correct to receive the confirmation of completion.
