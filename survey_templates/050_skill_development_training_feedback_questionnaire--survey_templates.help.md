# Skill Development Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on their experience with the skill development training they received. Your input will help instructors improve their teaching methods and provide a better learning experience for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by providing your participant information, including any relevant details about yourself.
- Answer the questions about your training experience, including whether you acquired new skills and your overall performance during the training.
- Indicate your assessment of the trainer's performance and your acquisition of new skills.
- Provide any suggestions for improvement and specify whether you found the training valuable.
- Rate your overall satisfaction with the training.
- Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
- Finally, type any further feedback or comments you have about the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Information**: This is the first page of the form, where you can provide any relevant details about yourself as a participant. Please include your name, role, and any other information that would help the instructor understand your context.
- **Training Experience**: In this field, please describe your past experience with similar trainings or relevant skills you brought to the training.
- **Skills Acquired**: Indicate whether you acquired new skills during the training by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the options provided.
- **Trainer Performance**: Assess the trainer's performance by selecting 'High', 'Medium', or 'Low' from the options provided.
- **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving the training or instructor's performance.
- **Training Value**: Indicate whether you found the training valuable by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the options provided.
- **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the training by selecting one of the options provided.
- **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
- **Feedback**: Provide any final feedback or comments about the training.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will greatly help the instructors improve future trainings.
- Focus on specific details about your experience and suggestions for improvement.
- If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the 'Comments' field.
