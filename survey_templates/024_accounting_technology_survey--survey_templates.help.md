# Accounting Technology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to provide you with a detailed understanding of the Accounting Technology Survey form and its usage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, select the relevant information for your organization.
2. Fill in your **Organization Name**, which is the name of your company.
3. Select all applicable technologies in the **Current Tech Stack** section, as it relates to your current firm's tech infrastructure.
4. In the **Technology Adoption Level** section, choose one option that describes your firm's rate of adopting new technology.
5. For **Cloud vs On-Premise Preference**, select one option that best represents your firm's tech strategy preference.
6. In **Technology Budget Adequacy**, select one option that describes your firm's current tech budget situation.
7. In the **Planned Tech Investments** section, choose all applicable future focus areas for your tech investments.
8. In the **Impact of Automation on Efficiency** section, select one option that describes the impact of automation on your firm's efficiency.
9. In the **Most Valuable Software Currently Used**, enter the name of the most valuable software currently used by your firm.
10. In the **Biggest Tech Frustration**, enter a brief description of the biggest tech frustration your firm faces.
11. In the **Interest in Beta Testing New Tools**, select one option that describes your firm's interest in early-release software.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Current Tech Stack** (select_multiple, required): Select all applicable technologies used in your firm.
* **Technology Adoption Level** (select_one, required): Describe your firm's rate of adopting new technology.
* **Cloud vs On-Premise Preference** (select_one, required): Choose one option that best represents your firm's tech strategy preference.
* **Technology Budget Adequacy** (select_one, required): Describe your firm's current tech budget situation.
* **Planned Tech Investments** (select_multiple, optional): Choose all applicable future focus areas for your tech investments.
* **Impact of Automation on Efficiency** (select_one, required): Describe the impact of automation on your firm's efficiency.
* **Most Valuable Software Currently Used** (text, optional): Enter the name of the most valuable software currently used by your firm.
* **Biggest Tech Frustration** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the biggest tech frustration your firm faces.
* **Interest in Beta Testing New Tools** (select_one, required): Choose one option that describes your firm's interest in early-release software.
