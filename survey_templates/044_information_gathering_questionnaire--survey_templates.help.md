# Information Gathering Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users on their experiences, thoughts, and feelings about a product or service. It is intended to gather feedback on audience engagement, satisfaction, and retention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages to understand the questions and topics covered.
2. Select the relevant pages for your response, as some pages are optional.
3. Complete each page according to the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro**: (Intro, note, required: no) - This page is a brief introduction to provide context for the questions that follow. Please take a moment to read through the form and understand what is being asked.
* **What do you think**: (audience_insights_page, select_multiple, required: no) - On this page, select all the options that apply to your thoughts and feelings about the product or service.
* **How do you feel**: (satisfaction_feedback_page, select_one, required: no) - Choose the option that best reflects your sentiment on the following questions. Please select only one option.
* **Contact Details**: (contact_details_page, text, required: no) - Please enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your feedback.
* **Contact Information**: (contact_details_page, email, required: no) - Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you for further information.
* **Contact Information**: (contact_details_page, text, required: no) - Please enter any other contact information you feel is necessary for us to reach you.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Please note that some fields are optional, but your feedback is valuable to us.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
