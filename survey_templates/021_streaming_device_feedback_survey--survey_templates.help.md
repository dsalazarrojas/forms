# Streaming Device Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback on streaming devices, including any issues experienced and suggestions for improvement. Your input will help us better understand your needs and improve the streaming devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your general information about the streaming device in the 'Streaming Device Info' page.
* Rate your device and provide any comments in the 'Rate Your Device' page.
* If you experienced any technical issues, please report them in the 'Technical Issues' page, including a description of the issue, its category, severity, and any additional information.
* If you have any suggestions or comments on release notes, please provide them in the 'Release Notes' page.
* Finally, provide any further contact information or additional comments in the 'Contact Info and Comments' page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Streaming Device Info** (page 1, text): Enter a brief description of the streaming device you are providing feedback on.
* **Rate Your Device** (page 2, select_one): Rate your device from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Technical Issues** (page 3, text): Describe any technical issues you experienced with the device.
* **Suggestions** (page 4, text): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Comments** (page 5, text): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Product Name** (page 6, text): Enter the name of the product or service you are providing feedback on.
* **Contact Info** (page 7, email): Enter your contact information, including your email address.
* **Phone Number** (page 8, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Release Notes** (page 9, note): Enter any release notes or updates related to the device.
* **Release Notes Date** (page 10, date): Enter the date you received the release notes.
* **Release Notes Time** (page 11, time): Enter the time you received the release notes.
* **Release Notes Additional Info** (page 12, text): Enter any additional information related to the release notes.
* **Technical Issues List** (page 13, select_multiple): Check any technical issues you experienced from the list provided.
* **Technical Issues Description** (page 14, text): Describe the technical issues you experienced.
* **Technical Issues Category** (page 15, text): Enter the category of the technical issues you experienced.
* **Technical Issues Severity** (page 16, select_one): Rate the severity of the technical issues from Minor, Major, Critical, or Crash.
* **Technical Issues Frequency** (page 17, text): Describe the frequency of the technical issues you experienced.
* **Technical Issues Impact** (page 18, text): Describe the impact of the technical issues you experienced.
* **Technical Issues Reproducibility** (page 19, text): Describe the reproducibility of the technical issues you experienced.
* **Technical Issues Steps to Reproduce** (page 20, text): Describe the steps you took to reproduce the technical issues.
* **Technical Issues Additional Info** (page 21, text): Enter any additional information related to the technical issues.
* **Technical Issues Status** (page 22, select_one): Rate the status of the technical issues as New, Open, Fixed, or Closed.

This guide will help you complete the streaming device feedback survey effectively.
