# Workout Feature Usability Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workout Feature Usability Inquiry Form is a tool to help us better understand how users interact with our product features, allowing us to identify areas of improvement and implement changes to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply go through each field and provide your honest feedback about the specified feature. You can submit your responses at your own pace.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. <feature_name>
* **Enter feature name**: Enter the name of the feature you are reporting about. This could be a specific workout or exercise, a particular module, or any other identifiable element within our product.

### 2. <feature_description>
* **Briefly describe the feature**: Describe your experience with this feature in a few sentences. What did you like or dislike about it? 

### 3. <satisfaction>
* **Overall satisfaction with this feature**: Choose how satisfied you are with this feature. You can select from the options provided:
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Very satisfied

### 4. <suggestions>
* **Do you have any suggestions for this feature?**: If you have any ideas or suggestions for improving this feature, please share them here. This could be a new feature or a modification to an existing one.

### 5. <category>
* **How would you categorize this feature?**: Choose a category for this feature:
	+ Core Feature
	+ Non-Core Feature
	+ Other

### 6. <frequency>
* **How often do you use this feature?**: Select how often you use this feature:
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Frequently
	+ Almost always

### 7. <improvement_plan>
* **Are there any areas for improvement?**: If you have any ideas or suggestions for improving this feature, please share them here. This could be a new feature or a modification to an existing one.

### 8. <contact_information>
* **Is there anything else you'd like to share with us?**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them here. 

### 9. <product_owner>
* **Are you the product owner or end user?**: Please select whether you are the owner of the product or an end-user:
	+ Owner
	+ End user

### 10. <email>
* **Email**: This is a required field if you are the owner or wish to be contacted for follow-up questions. 

### 11. <phone>
* **Phone number**: This is an optional field for you to provide your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.

### 12. <date>
* **When did you try this feature?**: Enter the date you tried this feature in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

Tips:
* Please be as specific as possible with your answers.
* If you have any questions, please contact us for further assistance.
* Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our product and meet your needs.
