# Simulation Test Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simulation Test Survey is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the simulation test. The survey is used to assess the user's overall experience, gather ratings and comments, and provide suggestions for improvement. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey to help us better understand your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand each question.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to provide a brief explanation or ask for clarification.
4. Submit your completed survey to provide your valuable feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **1. What was your overall experience with the simulation test?**
* **Label:** What was your overall experience with the simulation test?
* **Type:** Text
* **Description:** Please provide a brief description of your overall experience with the simulation test. This will help us understand your general impression of the test.

### **2. How would you rate your experience with the simulation test?**
* **Label:** How would you rate your experience with the simulation test?
* **Type:** Number
* **Description:** Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Not Satisfied" and 5 being "Very Satisfied".

### **3. What did you think of the test's difficulty level?**
* **Label:** What did you think of the test's difficulty level?
* **Type:** Text
* **Description:** Provide a brief description of your thoughts about the test's difficulty level.

### **4. How likely are you to recommend this simulation test to others?**
* **Label:** How likely are you to recommend this simulation test to others?
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Description:** Select one of the options: "True", "False", or "Unsure".

### **5. What was the most challenging part of the simulation test?**
* **Label:** What was the most challenging part of the simulation test?
