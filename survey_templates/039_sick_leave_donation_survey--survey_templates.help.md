# Sick Leave Donation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback about the sick leave donation program, allowing employees to share their opinions, concerns, and suggestions about the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the options that best fit your thoughts and feelings about the sick leave donation program.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
4. Click submit to send your feedback.
