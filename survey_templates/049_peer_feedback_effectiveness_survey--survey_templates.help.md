# Peer Feedback Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help teams assess the effectiveness of peer feedback. It will help you understand how well your team is communicating and providing feedback, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the answer that best describes your experience with peer feedback for each question.
2. For open-ended questions, provide specific examples or examples that illustrate your point.
3. Be honest in your responses, and try to provide constructive feedback that will help your team improve.
