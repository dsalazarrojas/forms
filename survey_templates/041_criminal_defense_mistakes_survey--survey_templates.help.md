# Criminal Defense Mistakes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Criminal Defense Mistakes Survey" is a self-reporting instrument designed to collect feedback from criminal defense professionals about mistakes that have occurred during their cases. The information gathered through this survey will help to identify areas of improvement in the defense process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read through each page carefully and answer the questions according to your experience with criminal defense cases.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible to help with the analysis.
3. If you are unsure of any question, feel free to elaborate on your answer in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Error Type** (`error_type`, Select One, Optional): Please select the type of error that occurred during your case. This will help us understand the nature of the mistake.
* **Error Occurred** (`error_occurred`, Select Multiple, Optional): Check the boxes that apply to the type of error that occurred. This will help us understand the scope of the mistake.
* **Impact on Case** (`impact_on_case`, Text, Optional): Describe how the error affected the outcome of your case. Be as specific as possible.
* **Cause of Error** (`cause_of_error`, Text, Optional): Provide an explanation for why the error occurred. Try to identify any contributing factors.
* **Description of Error** (`error_description`, Text, Optional): Give a detailed account of what happened during the error.
* **Prevention Strategies** (`prevention_strategies`, Text, Optional): Suggest any steps that can be taken to prevent similar errors in the future.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, Text, Optional): Share any ideas you have for improving the defense process based on your experience with the mistakes you've encountered.
* **Survey Introduction** (`survey_intro`, Note, Not Required): This section is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about the survey itself.
