# Transgender Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transgender Survey is a tool designed to gather information about the gender identity and experiences of transgender individuals. This survey aims to collect data to better understand the needs and concerns of this community, and to provide a platform for individuals to share their stories and insights.

This survey is intended for individuals who identify as transgender, including those who are transmasculine, transfeminine, non-binary, genderqueer, or any other gender identity within the transgender spectrum. It is not a clinical or medical survey, but rather a self-identified survey for research and educational purposes.

Before starting the survey, please ensure that you have not already completed it. If you have previously completed this survey, please do not submit another response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions and answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Answer each question carefully, as the information collected will help us better understand the experiences of transgender individuals.
3. If you have any questions or concerns about any of the questions, you can contact us at [insert contact information].

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief text field where you can write a short introduction about yourself, including any relevant context or information that you feel is important for us to know.
* **Age**: This is a numeric field where you can enter your age.
* **Sex Assignment at Birth**: Please select "Yes" if you were assigned male at birth and "No" if you were assigned female at birth.
* **Gender Identity**: Please select all the options that apply to how you identify, including "Yes" for transmasculine, transfeminine, non-binary, genderqueer, or any other gender identity within the transgender spectrum.
* **How Do You Identify?**: Please select "Yes" if you identify as transgender, non-binary, genderqueer, or any other gender identity within the transgender spectrum.
* **Sex at Last Contact**: Please select "Yes" if your sex at your last medical or social interaction was different from your current sex identity.
* **Sex at Last Medical Contact**: Please select "Yes" if your sex at your last medical or social interaction was different from your current sex identity.
* **Medical History**: This is a text field where you can write a brief summary of your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or treatments.
* **Sex at Birth Medical History**: This is a text field where you can write a brief summary of your medical history from birth, including any relevant medical conditions or treatments.

## Tips
* Please answer each question accurately and honestly, as the information collected will help us better understand the experiences of transgender individuals.
* If you have any questions or concerns about any of the questions, you can contact us at [insert contact information].
* We will keep your responses confidential and anonymous, unless you choose to share your story or insights with us explicitly.
