# Inhibition Kinetics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inhibition Kinetics Survey form is designed to collect information from researchers about inhibition kinetics experiments. This form helps to gather data from experiments conducted by researchers in the field of inhibition kinetics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Enzyme Name.
2. Enter your Enzyme Concentration.
3. Enter your Inhibitor Concentration.
4. Select your Reaction Time.
5. Choose your Inhibitor Type.
6. Select your Experimental Method.
7. Choose your Experimental Setup.
8. Enter your Kinetic Data.
9. Add any additional Notes.
10. Enter your Email.
11. Enter your Phone Number.
12. Enter your Researcher Name.
13. Enter your Institution.
14. Enter your Department.
15. Enter your Research Group.
16. Enter your Research Topic.
17. Select your Survey Date.
18. Enter who Submitted the Form, if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enzyme Name** (`enzyme_name`, text, required): This field asks for the name of the enzyme used in the experiment. It's the name of the enzyme, e.g. 'Acetylcholinesterase' (required field).
* **Enzyme Concentration** (`enzyme_concentration`, number, required): This field requires the concentration of the enzyme used in the experiment. Enter the value in the desired format. (required)
* **Inhibitor Concentration** (`inhibitor_concentration`, number, required): This field asks for the concentration of the inhibitor used in the experiment. Enter the value in the desired format. (required)
* **Reaction Time** (`reaction_time`, time, required): This field is for the time of reaction used in the experiment. Select the appropriate time frame from the options. (required)
* **Inhibitor Type** (`inhibitor_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of inhibitor used in the experiment. Select from the options provided.
* **Experimental Method** (`experimental_method`, select_one, required): Select the method used in the experiment. Choose from the options provided.
* **Experimental Setup** (`experimental_setup`, select_one, required): This field is for the experimental setup used in the experiment. Select from the options provided.
* **Kinetic Data** (`kinetic_data`, text, required): This field is for the kinetic data collected during the experiment. Enter any additional data you'd like to include.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes about the experiment. Enter any relevant information that you'd like to include.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Researcher Name** (`researcher_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a researcher.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, required): Enter the institution where the experiment was conducted.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department where the experiment was conducted.
* **Research Group** (`research_group`, text, required): Enter the research group where the experiment was conducted.
* **Research Topic** (`research_topic`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the research topic.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Select the date the survey was filled out.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): If available, enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

Tips:
* Please provide accurate and detailed information about your experiment.
* Be sure to select the correct options for each field to ensure accurate data collection.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
