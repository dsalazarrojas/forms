# Shower Doors Specifications Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey collects detailed specifications about shower doors. It aims to gather data on the type of shower door material, size, handle type, frame type, frame color, panel type, and installation method. The survey also collects contact and address information for the installer, as well as customer details. This data can be used to analyze and improve the quality of shower door installations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your shower doors specifications in the provided fields.
2. Select the relevant options for each field from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments about the installation in the "Notes" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shower Doors Specifications**: Enter a brief description of the shower doors being installed.
* **Material Type**: Choose the material type of the shower door from the options: Acrylic, Glass, or Wood.
* **Door Size**: Enter the size of the shower door.
* **Handle Type**: Select the type of handle on the shower door from the options: T-Handle, L-Handle, or No Handle.
* **Frame Type**: Choose the type of frame for the shower door from the options: Wood Frame, Aluminum Frame, or Steel Frame.
* **Frame Color**: Select the color of the frame from the options: White, Gray, or Black.
* **Panel Type**: Choose the type of panel for the shower door from the options: Glass, Acrylic, or Wood.
* **Panel Size**: Enter the size of the panel.
* **Installation Method**: Select the method of installation from the options: Frame, Sliding, or Hinged.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the installation.
* **Installer Name**: Enter the name of the installer.
* **Installer Contact**: Enter the contact information of the installer.
* **Installer Address**: Enter the address of the installer.
* **Installer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the installer.
* **Installer Email**: Enter the email address of the installer.
* **Installer License**: Enter the license number or ID of the installer.
* **Installer Insurance**: Enter any relevant insurance information.
* **Installer Equipment**: Enter any equipment used by the installer.
* **Installer Vehicle**: Enter the vehicle used by the installer.
* **Installer Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the installer.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the address of the customer.
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the customer.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have permission to share this information with the survey creator.
* Please enter accurate and detailed information to ensure the quality of the survey results.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the survey creator for clarification.
