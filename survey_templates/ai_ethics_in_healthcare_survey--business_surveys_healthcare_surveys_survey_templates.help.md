# AI Ethics In Healthcare Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather opinions and experiences from healthcare stakeholders on AI ethics in healthcare, including benefits, risks, and challenges associated with AI adoption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the healthcare industry from the dropdown menu.
2. Answer questions about your experience with AI in healthcare.
3. Select all that apply for benefits and risks of AI in healthcare.
4. Rate your trust in AI systems in healthcare.
5. Answer questions about AI ethics, including algorithm transparency, patient consent, bias detection, and data privacy.
6. Share your opinions on the future of AI in healthcare, including adoption rate and regulatory priorities.
7. Provide any additional concerns or recommendations you have on AI ethics in healthcare.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your role in healthcare**: Please select your role in the healthcare industry, such as Clinician, Researcher, Patient, Administrator, Policy maker, or Technology developer.
* **Years of experience in healthcare**: This question aims to understand your level of experience in the healthcare industry. Please answer with the number of years you've been working in healthcare.
* **Have you used AI tools in healthcare settings?**: Select how often you use AI tools in healthcare settings: Yes, regularly, Yes, occasionally, No, but interested, or No, and not interested.
* **Benefits of AI in healthcare (select all that apply)**: Please select all the benefits you think AI brings to healthcare, such as Improved diagnostics, Faster treatment, Reduced costs, Better patient outcomes, Administrative efficiency, and Enhanced research capabilities.
* **Risks of AI in healthcare (select all that apply)**: Please select all the risks you associate with AI in healthcare, such as Privacy concerns, Algorithm bias, Job displacement, Reduced human oversight, Over-reliance on technology, and Misdiagnosis risk.
* **How much do you trust AI systems in healthcare?**: Rate your trust in AI systems in healthcare on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **How important is algorithm transparency in healthcare AI?**: Rate the importance of transparency in healthcare AI on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Should patients always consent to AI-assisted diagnosis?**: Select whether patients should always, sometimes, only for certain procedures, or not be consented to AI-assisted diagnosis.
* **Who should be responsible for detecting bias in healthcare AI?**: Select who you think should be responsible for detecting bias in healthcare AI, such as Developers, Healthcare institutions, Government regulators, Medical professionals, or Shared responsibility.
* **Rate importance of patient data privacy in AI systems**: Rate the importance of patient data privacy in AI systems on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **How quickly should AI be adopted in healthcare?**: Select how quickly you think AI should be adopted in healthcare: Rapidly, Gradually, Slowly, Not at all, or Uncertain.
* **Top priority for AI regulation in healthcare**: Select the top priority for AI regulation in healthcare, such as Safety standards, Bias prevention, Data protection, Clinical validation, Professional oversight, or Other.
* **Is a global ethical framework for healthcare AI needed?**: Select whether you strongly agree, agree, are neutral, disagree, or strongly disagree that a global ethical framework for healthcare AI is needed.
* **Additional concerns about AI ethics in healthcare**: Please provide any specific concerns you have about AI ethics in healthcare.
* **Recommendations for improving AI ethics in healthcare**: Share your suggestions for improving AI ethics in healthcare.
* **Contact information (optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your contact information.
