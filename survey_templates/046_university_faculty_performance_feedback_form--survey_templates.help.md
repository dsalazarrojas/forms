# University Faculty Performance Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students on the performance of their instructors, covering various aspects of teaching quality, communication, time management, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the evaluation date and rate your instructor's performance on each of the following dimensions:
	* Teaching Quality
	* Communication Skills
	* Time Management
	* Course Materials
	* Student Counseling
	* Student Facilitation
	* Course Organization
	* Student Engagement
	* Instructor Support
	* Course Relevance
	* Overall Experience
2. Provide a rating on a scale from 1-5, where:
	* 1: Very Dissatisfied
	* 2: Somewhat Dissatisfied
	* 3: Neutral
	* 4: Somewhat Satisfied
	* 5: Very Satisfied
3. In the "Additional Comments" field, provide any other thoughts or concerns you may have about your experience with this course.
4. Select your level of satisfaction with the submit option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor Evaluation Form**: Provide a brief description of your experience with this course, including any specific incidents or events that have been impactful.
* **Evaluation Date**: Select the date you took this course or the date you are providing this feedback.
* **Teaching Quality**: Rate your instructor's teaching quality on a scale from 1-5.
* **Communication Skills**: Rate your instructor's communication skills on a scale from 1-5.
* **Time Management**: Rate your instructor's time management on a scale from 1-5.
* **Course Materials**: Rate the quality of course materials provided on a scale from 1-5.
* **Student Counseling**: Rate your instructor's willingness to counsel students on a scale from 1-5.
* **Student Facilitation**: Rate your instructor's ability to facilitate student learning on a scale from 1-5.
* **Course Organization**: Rate the overall organization of the course on a scale from 1-5.
* **Student Engagement**: Rate student engagement with the course material on a scale from 1-5.
* **Instructor Support**: Rate your instructor's support for students on a scale from 1-5.
* **Course Relevance**: Rate the relevance of course content on a scale from 1-5.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience with this course on a scale from 1-5.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any other thoughts or concerns you may have about your experience with this course.
* **Submit**: Select your level of satisfaction with the submit option.

Note: This form is meant to be completed by students, so please provide feedback based on your personal experience with this course.
