# Multiplier Event Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form aims to collect feedback and satisfaction information from attendees of the "Multiplier Event" to help us improve future event experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form if you attended the "Multiplier Event".
- Answer each question to the best of your ability.
- You can skip questions that don't apply to you.
- If you need help with any question, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your experience with the online event?** (event_satisfaction)
  This question allows you to describe your overall experience with the event.
* **How would you rate the event organizers?** (event_organizers)
  Please rate the event organizers based on how well you think they did their job.
  * Choose one of the two options: Good or Fair.
* **Additional feedback** (feedback)
  If you have any additional comments, suggestions, or concerns about the event, please use this space to share them.
* **How can we best contact you for follow-up?** (contact_info)
  If you have a preferred method of contact (e.g., email, phone, or other), please specify it here.
* **Phone number** (phone_number)
  If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Date of Event** (date_of_event)
  Please enter the date on which the event took place.
* **Time of event** (time_of_event)
  Please enter the time of the event.
* **Multiplier Event Name** (multiplier_event_name)
  Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Location of the event** (multiplier_event_location)
  Describe the location of the event.
* **Type of event participation** (event_participation_type)
  If you participated in the event, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
* **Level of event participation** (event_participation_level)
  If you participated in the event, select your level of participation (High or Low).
* **Attendance at event** (event_attendance)
  Please describe how many people attended the event.
* **Satisfaction with event** (event_satisfaction)
  Indicate your level of satisfaction with the event (Satisfied or Dissatisfied).
* **Suggestions for improvement** (event_suggestions)
  Share any ideas on how we can improve future events.

## Tips
- Be honest with your responses, as your feedback is valuable to us.
- If you're unsure about any question, just make your best effort.
- Thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts and experiences!
