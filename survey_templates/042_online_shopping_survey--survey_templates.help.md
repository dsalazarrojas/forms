# Online Shopping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their online shopping experience. Your responses will help us identify areas of improvement for the online store.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions based on your actual experience with the online store.
2. For questions with multiple options, choose the one that best describes your experience.
3. If a question does not apply to your experience, answer "neutral" or provide a brief explanation in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Take your time to answer each question carefully, as your feedback is valuable to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Online Store**: Select the type of online store you are shopping at (e.g., book, magazine, clothing, furniture).
* **Customer Service**: Rate the customer service experience you had with the store (very good, somewhat good, neutral, very bad).
* **Product Quality**: Rate the quality of the products you purchased (very good, somewhat good, neutral, very bad).
* **Price Range**: If applicable, select the price range of the products you purchased (e.g., very affordable, somewhat affordable, neutral, very expensive).
* **Delivery Speed**: Rate the speed of delivery from the store (very fast, somewhat fast, neutral, very slow).
* **Return Policy**: Rate the return policy of the store (very strict, somewhat strict, neutral, very relaxed).
* **Customer Service Quality**: Rate the quality of customer service you received (very good, somewhat good, neutral, very bad).
* **Product Relevance**: Rate the relevance of the products to what you were looking for (very relevant, somewhat relevant, neutral, very irrelevant).
* **Ease of Purchasing**: Rate the ease of purchasing process (very easy, somewhat easy, neutral, very difficult).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the purchasing experience (very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, very dissatisfied).
* **Product Selection**: Rate the friendliness of the product selection process (very friendly, somewhat friendly, neutral, very unfriendly).
* **Product Information**: Rate the quality of product information provided by the store (very good, somewhat good, neutral, very bad).
* **Store Layout**: Rate the store's layout (very intuitive, somewhat intuitive, neutral, very non-intuitive).
* **Search Functionality**: Rate the store's search functionality (very good, somewhat good, neutral, very bad).
* **Checkout Process**: Rate the ease of the checkout process (very easy, somewhat easy, neutral, very difficult).
* **Product Price**: Rate the price of the products (very affordable, somewhat affordable, neutral, very expensive).
* **Product Availability**: Rate the availability of products in the store (always available, sometimes available, never available).
* **Store Atmosphere**: Rate the atmosphere of the store (very friendly, somewhat friendly, neutral, very unfriendly).
* **Product Return**: Rate the return policy of the products (always return, sometimes return, never return).
* **Customer Experience**: Rate your overall customer experience with the store (very good, somewhat good, neutral, very bad).
* **Product Warranty**: Rate the warranty offered by the products (always warranty, sometimes warranty, never warranty).
* **Order Speed**: Rate the speed of order processing (very fast, somewhat fast, neutral, very slow).
* **Product Return Policy**: Rate the return policy of the products (very strict, somewhat strict, neutral, very relaxed).
* **Customer Service Contact**: If you need to contact customer service, enter their contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, enter them here.
