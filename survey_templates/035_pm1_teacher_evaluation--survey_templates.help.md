# PM1 Teacher Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PM1 Teacher Evaluation form is designed to collect feedback from teachers regarding their experiences with their students, administrators, and colleagues. This feedback will be used to identify areas for improvement and opportunities to enhance teacher satisfaction and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic contact information:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
2. Provide your job title and department
3. Specify your years of teaching experience
4. Indicate your teaching level
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Job Title** (`text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`text`, required: false): Enter your department.
* **Years Teaching** (`text`, required: false): Enter the number of years you have been teaching.
* **Teaching Level** (`text`, required: false): Indicate your level of teaching experience (e.g., elementary, high school, university, etc.).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields.
* If you need help or have questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
