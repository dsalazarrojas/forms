# Speech Recording Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on speech recordings. Please provide constructive and actionable comments to help us improve our recordings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form once per recording.
2. Rate the recording using the scale provided in the "How would you rate the recording?" field.
3. Share your suggestions for improvement in the "What are some suggestions for improvement?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate the recording? (rating)**: Rate the recording on a scale. This field is used to gauge your overall satisfaction with the recording.
* **What are some suggestions for improvement? (feedback)**: Provide actionable and constructive comments on how we can improve the recording. Be specific with your suggestions.

## Tips
* Be kind and respectful in your feedback.
* Please provide one feedback per recording.
* Keep your feedback concise and clear.
* Avoid duplicating previous feedback. If you have similar feedback, please let us know.
