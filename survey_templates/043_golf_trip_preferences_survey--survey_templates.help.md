# Golf Trip Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your golf trip preferences. It will help us understand your interests, preferences, and needs for a golf trip, which will enable us to provide a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select your preferred trip location from the available options.
3. Choose your preferred trip length and type.
4. Select your interests and preferences.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Golfer Name** (`golf_trip_preferences_survey_golfer_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your golf handicap card or driver's license.
* **Trip Location** (`golf_trip_preferences_survey_trip_location`): Select the location where you would like to go for your golf trip. This could be a city, state, or country.
* **Trip Date** (`golf_trip_preferences_survey_trip_date`): Enter the date you would like to go on your trip. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Trip Length** (`golf_trip_preferences_survey_trip_length`): Choose how long you would like your trip to be. Options include:
	+ Option 1: One day
	+ Option 2: Two days
	+ Option 3: More than two days
* **Golfer Interests** (`golf_trip_preferences_survey_golfer_interests`): Select the activities you are interested in doing during your trip. You can select multiple options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Trip Type** (`golf_trip_preferences_survey_trip_type`): Choose the type of trip you would like to take. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Golfer Notes** (`golf_trip_preferences_survey_golfer_notes`): Add any additional notes or comments about your preferences or interests.
* **Trip Notes** (`golf_trip_preferences_survey_trip_notes`): Add any additional notes or comments about the trip itself.
* **Submitter** (`golf_trip_preferences_survey_submitter`): This field is not required and is for internal use only.
* **Submit Date** (`golf_trip_preferences_survey_submit_date`): This field is not required and is for internal use only.
* **Submit Time** (`golf_trip_preferences_survey_submit_time`): This field is not required and is for internal use only.
