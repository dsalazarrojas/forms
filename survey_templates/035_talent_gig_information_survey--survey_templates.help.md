# Talent Gig Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Gig Information Survey form is designed to collect data about one-time or occasional events where artists or performers showcase their skills. It's used to gather details about the event, including the event date, duration, location, type, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event details such as event date, event time, duration, and location.
2. Choose the type of event from the options provided.
3. Provide contact information for the event, including contact name and email.
4. Optional: Enter the contact phone number if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gig Details** (`talent_gig_information_survey`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the event, e.g., "Concert by John Doe at 7:00 PM on 2024-02-16"
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Performance Duration** (`performance_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the event in minutes.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the event, e.g., "New York City, NY".
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of event from the options provided:
	+ Type 1
	+ Type 2
	+ Type 3
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips
* Double-check the event date and time to ensure they are correct.
* Use the correct format for the event duration (minutes).
* Choose the correct event type from the options provided.
* Make sure to enter the correct contact information for the event.
