# Average Calculation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Average Calculation Survey is a form designed to collect and average numeric values for specific purposes, such as business or scientific research.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Start Survey" button to begin.
2.  Fill out the required fields with your name and email address.
3.  Provide up to five numeric values to be averaged.
4.  Rate the data quality and provide comments on data sources.
5.  Select the frequency of data collection.
6.  Confirm if the data has been verified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (Full Name, text, required): Please enter your full name to ensure accountability and data integrity.
*   **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address to facilitate data tracking and accountability.
*   **Survey Topic** (Survey Topic, text, required): Describe the topic being measured to ensure accurate data organization and analysis.
*   **Value 1** (Value 1, number, required): Enter the first numeric value to be averaged.
*   **Value 2** (Value 2, number, required): Enter the second numeric value to be averaged.
*   **Value 3** (Value 3, number, required): Enter the third numeric value to be averaged.
*   **Value 4** (Value 4, number, optional): Enter the fourth numeric value to be averaged.
*   **Value 5** (Value 5, number, optional): Enter the fifth numeric value to be averaged.
*   **Data Quality Rating** (Data Quality Rating, select_one, required): Rate the reliability of your data using the provided scale.
*   **Comments on Data Sources** (Comments on Data Sources, text, optional): Provide comments on the origin of your data to ensure transparency and trustworthiness.
*   **Frequency of Data Collection** (Frequency of Data Collection, select_one, required): Select the frequency at which your data is collected.
*   **Has this data been verified?** (Has this data been verified?, select_one, required): Confirm if your data has been verified.

Tips:

*   Make sure to provide accurate and reliable data to ensure the accuracy of the averaged results.
*   Ensure that your data is collected and submitted at the correct frequency to avoid duplication of effort.
*   Review and revise your submission if you notice any errors or inconsistencies in your data.
