# Arachnid Species Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about arachnid species for the purpose of documenting and registering new species discoveries. The data collected will help scientists and researchers to better understand and manage arachnid diversity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information.
2. Enter the species' common name.
3. Enter the species' scientific name.
4. Provide the date and location of discovery.
5. Select the primary habitat of the species.
6. If applicable, upload a photo or link to the species.
7. Indicate whether a specimen was collected.
8. If a specimen was collected, provide the storage location.
9. Measure the body length of the species.
10. Describe the color and distinctive features of the species.
11. Record any behavioral observations.
12. List any associated species found in the same location.
13. Evaluate the venom or medical significance of the species.
14. Indicate your level of confidence in the identification.
15. List the reference materials or experts consulted.
16. Provide information on the conservation status of the species.
17. Enter your email address for follow-up questions.
18. Select whether to share the data publicly or keep it private.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contributor Name** (`contributor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the person registering the species.
* **Institution or Organization** (`institution_affiliation`, text, optional): Enter the name of the institution or organization associated with the discovery.
* **Common Name of Species** (`species_common_name`, text, required): Enter the common name used to identify this arachnid.
* **Scientific Name - Latin Binomial** (`species_scientific_name`, text, required): Enter the scientific name of the species in the format 'genus species'.
* **Date of Discovery** (`discovery_date`, date, required): Enter the date the species was observed or collected.
* **Geographic Location** (`discovery_location`, text, required): Enter the country, state, city, and coordinates if available.
* **Habitat Type** (`habitat_type`, select_one, required): Select the primary habitat where the species was found.
* **Specimen Photo or Image URL** (`specimen_photo_url`, text, optional): If available, provide a link to a photograph or image of the species.
* **Was a Specimen Collected?** (`specimen_collected`, select_one, required): Indicate whether a specimen was collected.
* **Storage Location of Specimen** (`specimen_storage_location`, text, optional): If a specimen was collected, enter the location where it is stored.
* **Body Length in Millimeters** (`size_body_length_mm`, number, optional): Measure and record the length of the species.
* **Color Description** (`color_description`, text, optional): Record the primary and secondary colors observed.
* **Distinctive Features** (`distinctive_features`, text, optional): Record any markings, patterns, or unique characteristics.
* **Behavioral Observations** (`behavioral_notes`, text, optional): Record any activity patterns, web construction, or hunting behavior.
* **Associated Plant or Animal Species** (`associated_species`, text, optional): If applicable, list other species found in the same location.
* **Venom or Medical Significance** (`venom_toxicity_level`, select_one, optional): Evaluate the venom or medical significance of the species.
* **Identification Confidence Level** (`identification_confidence`, select_one, required): Indicate your confidence level in the identification.
* **Reference Materials or Experts Consulted** (`reference_materials_used`, text, optional): List books, papers, or specialist names consulted.
* **Known Conservation Status** (`conservation_status`, select_one, optional): Enter the conservation status of the species.
* **Contributor Email Address** (`contributor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up questions.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contributor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Permission to Share Data Publicly** (`data_sharing_permission`, select_one, required): Select whether to share this data publicly or keep it private.
