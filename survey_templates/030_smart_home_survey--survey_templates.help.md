# Smart Home Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Home Survey is designed to gather information about your smart home devices, their locations, and your perceived savings. This will help us understand how you interact with smart home devices and inform the development of new features and improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully, and don't hesitate to ask if you need help.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. For questions that ask for a specific number, enter a numerical value.
4. For questions that ask for a short text response, type your answer in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Smart Home Setup

* **What is your smart home setup?** (`smart_home_survey_1`, text, optional): Briefly describe your smart home setup, including any devices you currently have.
* **What kind of smart home devices do you have?** (`smart_home_2`, select multiple, optional): Select all the smart home devices you have, such as Amazon Echo, Google Home, or Apple HomePod.

### Page 2: Smart Home Devices

* **Where do you have smart home devices located?** (`smart_home_3`, select one, optional): Tell us where you keep your smart home devices, such as in your home or office.
* **How many smart home devices do you have?** (`smart_home_4`, number, optional): Enter the number of smart home devices you have.
* **What smart home technologies do you use?** (`smart_home_5`, text, optional): Describe any smart home technologies you're using, such as smart lighting or security systems.

### Page 3: Smart Home Experiences

* **Smart home ideas:** (`smart_home_6`, select multiple, optional): Share any smart home ideas you have, such as integrating with other smart devices or services.
* **Do you think you save money with smart home devices?** (`smart_home_7`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value indicating how much you think you save with smart home devices.
* **What currency do you use for smart home savings?** (`smart_home_8`, select one, optional): Select the currency you use for smart home savings, such as USD or EUR.

### Page 4: Smart Home Ideas

* **Additional smart home ideas:** (`smart_home_10`, text, optional): Share any additional smart home ideas or experiences you'd like to share with us.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
