# Church Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather feedback from church members and guests on their comfort levels and concerns regarding the reopening of the church for in-person services. Your input will help inform the church's decision-making process to ensure a safe and effective reopening.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Enter your email address if you wish to receive updates on the reopening process.
4. Submit your survey responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are you a regular member or a guest?** (`member_status`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand your status within the church. Please select "Regular Member" if you are a regular attendee, "Regular Attendee (non-member)" if you attend regularly but are not a member, "Occasional Visitor" if you attend occasionally, or "New to the church" if you are new to the church.
* **How comfortable do you feel returning to in-person services?** (`comfort_level_returning`, number, required): This question gauges your comfort level on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is Very Uncomfortable and 5 is Very Comfortable.
* **When do you plan to return to in-person services?** (`attendance_timeline`, select_one, required): Choose when you plan to return to in-person services, whether immediately upon reopening, within a few weeks, after several months, or not until a vaccine/treatment is widely available.
* **What safety measures are most important to you?** (`safety_priorities`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from socially distanced seating, mandatory masks, hand sanitizing stations, limited capacity, professional cleaning between services, no congregational singing.
* **Will you continue to watch online even if you attend in-person?** (`online_service_preference`, select_one, optional): Indicate how often you will watch online services, whether frequently, occasionally, or not at all.
* **Would you feel comfortable sending your children to kids' ministry?** (`children_ministry_concern`, select_one, optional): Select whether you feel comfortable sending your children to kids' ministry, not applicable, or not.
* **Preferred service time (if multiple are offered)** (`preferred_service_time`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred service time from 8-30 AM, 10-00 AM, 11-30 AM, or evening service.
* **Are you willing to volunteer to help with cleaning or ushering?** (`volunteer_willingness`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are willing to volunteer to help with cleaning or ushering.
* **Do you support health screenings (e.g., temperature checks) at the door?** (`health_screening_opinion`, select_one, optional): Select whether you support, oppose, or are undecided on health screenings.
* **Do you have any specific health concerns we should be aware of?** (`specific_concerns`, text, optional): Share any specific health concerns or needs you have that we should be aware of.
* **Other suggestions for a safe reopening** (`additional_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any other suggestions you may have for a safe reopening of the church.
* **Email address for reopening updates** (`email_for_updates`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to receive updates on the reopening process.
* **Date** (`survey_completion_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed this survey.
