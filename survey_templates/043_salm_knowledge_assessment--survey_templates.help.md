# Salm Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, Salm Knowledge Assessment, is designed to collect user input for assessment purposes. It's a versatile tool for various uses within the industry and category specified in its metadata. Please note that the actual use and purpose of the form may vary depending on the specific context.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Salm Knowledge Assessment Form** page with your responses.
2. On the **Salm Questions** page, select one of the provided options (1, 2, or 3) that best answer the question.
3. In the **Salm Knowledge Areas** page, select all applicable knowledge areas (1, 2, or 3) that apply to your assessment.
4. For the **Salm Assessment Date**, enter the date of your assessment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. In the **Salm Assessment Time** field, enter the time of your assessment in the format HH:MM AM/PM or HH:MM 24-hour.
6. On the **Salm Assessment Outcome** page, select the outcome that best describes your assessment results (1, 2, or 3).
7. In the **Salm Additional Comments** field, provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Salm Knowledge Assessment Form**: This is the main form section where you provide your responses.
* **Salm Questions**: This page asks you to select one of the three provided questions that best answer the question.
*   *Salm Questions* is a dropdown menu where you select one of the three options.
* **Salm Knowledge Areas**: This page asks you to select all applicable knowledge areas that apply to your assessment.
*   *Salm Knowledge Areas* is a checkbox menu where you select one or more of the three options.
* **Salm Assessment Date**: Enter the date of your assessment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Salm Assessment Time**: Enter the time of your assessment in the format HH:MM AM/PM or HH:MM 24-hour.
*   If you're unsure of the format, please refer to your device's date and time settings.
* **Salm Assessment Outcome**: This page asks you to select the outcome that best describes your assessment results.
*   *Salm Assessment Outcome* is a dropdown menu where you select one of the three options.
* **Salm Additional Comments**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
*   Please keep your comments concise and relevant to the assessment.
