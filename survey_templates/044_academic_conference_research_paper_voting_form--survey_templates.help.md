# Academic Conference Research Paper Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect peer-review votes for academic conference research papers, providing a comprehensive evaluation of each paper across various aspects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review Form Instructions**: Please read the form instructions and complete the form carefully.
2. **Enter Reviewer Information**: Fill out the form with your reviewer information, including name, email, and institutional affiliation.
3. **Enter Paper Details**: Enter the paper's unique submission ID and title.
4. **Rate the Paper**: Evaluate the paper's originality, methodology, clarity, significance, and references using the provided rating scale.
5. **Overall Recommendation**: Choose your overall recommendation for the paper (Strong Accept, Accept, Weak Accept, Borderline, Weak Reject, or Strong Reject).
6. **Strengths and Weaknesses**: Summarize the main strengths and weaknesses of the paper.
7. **Detailed Comments**: Provide constructive feedback for the authors.
8. **Confidential Comments**: Leave any confidential comments for the program committee (not for the authors).
9. **Conflict of Interest**: Declare any conflicts of interest you may have with the paper.
10. **Save and Submit**: Once you have completed all the sections, save and submit your review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reviewer Full Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as a reviewer.
- **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Institutional Affiliation** (`reviewer_affiliation`, text, required): Enter your institutional affiliation.
- **Review Panel or Track** (`review_panel`, text, required): Enter the review panel or track you are assigned to.
- **Paper Submission ID** (`paper_id`, text, required): Enter the unique paper identifier provided by the conference organizers.
- **Paper Title** (`paper_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the paper.
- **Originality and Novelty of the Research** (`originality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the paper's originality and novelty on a scale of 1-5.
- **Soundness of Methodology** (`methodology_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the paper's methodology on a scale of 1-5.
- **Clarity and Quality of Writing** (`clarity_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the paper's writing quality on a scale of 1-5.
- **Significance and Impact of Findings** (`significance_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the paper's significance and impact on a scale of 1-5.
- **Adequacy of References and Literature Review** (`references_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the paper's references and literature review on a scale of 1-5.
- **Overall Recommendation** (`overall_recommendation`, select_one, required): Choose your overall recommendation for the paper (Strong Accept, Accept, Weak Accept, Borderline, Weak Reject, or Strong Reject).
- **Key Strengths** (`strengths`, text, required): Summarize the main strengths of the paper.
- **Key Weaknesses** (`weaknesses`, text, required): Summarize the main weaknesses of the paper.
- **Detailed Comments** (`detailed_comments`, text, optional): Provide constructive feedback for the authors.
- **Confidential Comments** (`confidential_comments`, text, optional): Leave any confidential comments for the program committee.
- **Conflict of Interest** (`conflict_of_interest`, select_one, required): Declare any conflicts of interest you may have with the paper.
- **Conflict of Interest Description** (`conflict_description`, text, optional): If you have a conflict of interest, please describe it.

## Tips
* Please complete the form carefully, as it will be used to inform the program committee's decisions.
* If you have already submitted a vote for a paper, you will not be able to access the form again to submit a new vote.
* You can leave the confidential comments section empty if you do not have any confidential comments.
