# Back To School Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Back To School Survey is designed to gather perspectives from the entire school community on various aspects of the school's readiness for the new academic year. This includes information on the level of preparedness, safety concerns, communication effectiveness, and other relevant topics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your role in the school community from the options provided in the "respondent_role" field.
2. Enter the name of your school in the "school_name_input" field.
3. Answer the "preparedness_rating" question on your level of preparedness for the new academic year, using the scale provided.
4. Rate your level of concern regarding "safety_concerns_rating" on the same scale as the "preparedness_rating" question.
5. Identify your biggest safety concern in the "primary_safety_concern" field, if applicable.
6. Select whether you have all the necessary technology and internet access for the year in the "technology_readiness_check" field.
7. Rate the need for additional social-emotional support services in the "social_emotional_support_needs" field.
8. Choose your preferred method for receiving school news and updates in the "preferred_engagement_method" field.
9. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback in the "additional_comments_readiness" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Please select your role (Parent/Guardian, Student, Teacher/Staff Member, Community Member):** This field determines which group you belong to and allows the school administration to categorize your responses accordingly.
* **School Name:** Enter the name of your school to help the administration understand which school you are referring to and to ensure accurate tracking of your responses.
* **How prepared do you feel for the start of the new academic year? (1=Not at all prepared, 5=Fully prepared):** Answer this question using the provided scale to indicate your level of preparedness for the new academic year.
* **Rate the clarity of communication from the school administration (1=Not at all clear, 5=Very clear):** This question helps the school administration understand how effectively they are communicating with you and the school community.
* **Level of concern regarding school facility safety and hygiene (1=No concern, 5=Very high concern):** Rate your level of concern using the provided scale to help the school administration address potential safety issues.
* **What is your biggest safety concern?:** If you have a concern, provide a specific issue in this field to help the school administration understand the root cause.
* **Do you have all the necessary technology and internet access for the year? (Yes, fully equipped, Partial access, No, need assistance):** Select the answer that best describes your situation to help the school administration plan accordingly.
* **Rate the need for additional social-emotional support services (1=No need, 5=Urgent need):** Answer this question using the provided scale to help the school administration understand the level of need for additional support services.
* **Preferred method for receiving school news and updates (Email Newsletter, Text Alerts, School Website, Social Media, Printed Letters):** Choose the method that you prefer to receive school news and updates.
* **Any other feedback you'd like to provide regarding the school reopening?:** Provide any additional comments or concerns you may have about the school reopening.
