# Market Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Market Survey** is a form designed to collect data about your market preferences, target markets, and customer characteristics. This form helps you gather essential information for informed business decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability. If a question doesn't apply to you, feel free to note it in the "introduction" field.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the most accurate option that reflects your answer.
3. You can provide additional comments or explanations in the "introduction" field if needed.
4. Ensure all required fields (indicated by *) have an answer.
