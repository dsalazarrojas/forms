# Medication Adherence Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication Adherence Motivation Survey is used to collect information on factors influencing medication adherence, motivations for adherence, and potential reasons for non-adherence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page, which provides background information on the survey.
2. Enter the patient's identifier (e.g., email or patient ID).
3. Provide information on the patient's medical condition.
4. Describe the patient's medication regimen.
5. Specify the patient's medication dosage.
6. Rate the patient's motivation for adherence.
7. List reasons for non-adherence.
8. Rate the patient's relationship with the healthcare provider.
9. Rate the patient's satisfaction with care.
10. Rate the healthcare provider's satisfaction with the patient.
11. Rate the patient's adherence level.
12. Provide follow-up suggestions.
13. Enter any comments from the healthcare provider.
14. Enter any comments from the patient.
15. Enter contact information for the patient and healthcare provider.
16. Set the survey completion date and time.
17. Indicate who submitted the survey (patient, healthcare provider, or other).
18. Set the submitted date and time.
19. Enter a unique survey ID and Jotform ID.
20. Enter the full name of the person who submitted the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page provides background information on the survey and its purpose.
* **Patient Identifier**: Enter the patient's unique identifier, such as their email or patient ID.
* **Medical Condition**: Describe the patient's medical condition.
* **Medication Regimen**: Describe the patient's medication regimen, including the type and dosage of medication.
* **Medication Dosage**: Enter the patient's current medication dosage.
* **Motivation for Adherence**: Rate the patient's motivation for adhering to their medication regimen, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **Reasons for Non-Adherence**: List potential reasons why the patient may not be adhering to their medication regimen. Select all applicable options.
* **Healthcare Provider Identifier**: Enter the healthcare provider's identifier, such as their email or provider ID.
* **Patient Relationship with Provider**: Rate the patient's relationship with the healthcare provider, with options Strong, Weak, or None.
* **Patient Satisfaction**: Rate the patient's satisfaction with their care.
* **Provider Satisfaction**: Rate the healthcare provider's satisfaction with the patient.
* **Adherence Level**: Rate the patient's adherence level, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **Followup Suggestions**: Enter any suggestions for follow-up care or interventions.
* **Provider Comments**: Enter any comments from the healthcare provider.
* **Patient Comments**: Enter any comments from the patient.
* **Patient Contact**: Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number, email, or address.
* **Healthcare Provider Contact**: Enter the healthcare provider's contact information, including phone number, email, or address.
* **Survey Completion Date**: Set the date the survey was completed.
* **Survey Completion Time**: Set the time the survey was completed.
* **Submitted By**: Indicate who submitted the survey, with options Patient, Healthcare Provider, or Other.
* **Submitted On**: Set the date the survey was submitted.
* **Submitted Time**: Set the time the survey was submitted.
* **Survey ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the survey.
* **Jotform ID**: Enter the Jotform ID associated with the survey.
* **Submitted By Full Name**: Enter the full name of the person who submitted the survey.

**Tips**

* Ensure that this form is not already complete for the same patient to avoid duplicate data.
* Be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the healthcare provider or patient for clarification.
