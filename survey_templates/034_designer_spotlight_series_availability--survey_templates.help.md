# Designer Spotlight Series Availability - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Designer Spotlight Series Availability" form is designed to collect information from designers about their availability for participation in the Designer Spotlight Series. This includes their interview schedule, coordinator details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Designer Details" section with your name and any other relevant information.
2.  Provide your interview schedule in the "Interview Schedule" field.
3.  Add the "Coordinator Details" section if you are coordinating or have coordinated with a specific person or team.
4.  Select your availability for the Designer Spotlight Series in the "Availability" field.
5.  If necessary, add any follow-up information in the "Follow-up" field.
6.  Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
7.  Choose the next step for your submission in the "Next Step" field.
8.  Finally, confirm your submission by selecting the correct option in the "Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Designer Details (Designer Details)**: This field is for your name and any other relevant information about yourself as a designer.
* **Interview Schedule (Interview Schedule)**: Please provide your available dates and times for interviews.
* **Coordinator Details (Coordinator Details)**: If you are coordinating or have coordinated with a specific person or team, add their details here.
* **Availability (Availability)**: Select all the options that apply to your availability for the Designer Spotlight Series.
* **Follow-up (Follow-up)**: If you have any additional follow-up information, add it here.
* **Notes (Notes)**: Add any additional notes that you think are relevant to your submission.
* **Next Step (Next Step)**: Choose the next step for your submission.
* **Submission Date (Submission Date)**: Enter the date you are submitting your information.
* **Submission Time (Submission Time)**: Enter the time you are submitting your information.
* **Confirmation (Confirmation)**: Confirm your submission by selecting the correct option.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your submission is complete and accurate.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
