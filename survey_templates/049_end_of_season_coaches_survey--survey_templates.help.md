# end_of_season_coaches_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The end_of_season_coaches_survey is a form designed for coaches to provide feedback on the performance and behavior of teams and players at the end of each season. This feedback is essential for team management to make informed decisions for the next season.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each field and ensure you have a clear understanding of what is being asked.
2. Select the most accurate option for each field, taking into account your honest opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_performance** (`team_performance`, select_one, required): Rate the overall performance of your team this season. Choose one of the following options:
	+ excellent
	+ good
	+ fair
	+ poor
* **coaching_performance** (`coaching_performance`, select_multiple, required): Select all the coaching performances that best describe the coaching staff's attitude and behavior this season:
	+ excellent
	+ good
	+ fair
	+ poor
* **player_attendance** (`player_attendance`, number, required): Enter the average number of players who attended all games this season.
* **coaching_attitude** (`coaching_attitude`, select_one, required): Rate the coaching staff's attitude this season. Choose one of the following options:
	+ positive
	+ neutral
	+ negative
* **sportsmanship** (`sportsmanship`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the sportsmanship behaviors that best describe the team's sportsmanship this season:
	+ excellent
	+ good
	+ fair
	+ poor
* **communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Rate the level of communication between the coaching staff and players this season. Choose one of the following options:
	+ excellent
	+ good
	+ fair
	+ poor
* **team_environment** (`team_environment`, select_one, required): Rate the overall team environment this season. Choose one of the following options:
	+ excellent
	+ good
	+ fair
	+ poor
* **overall_experience** (`overall_experience`, number, optional): Enter the overall experience you had this season.
* **coaches_satisfaction** (`coaches_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the coaching satisfactions that best describe the coaching staff's satisfaction this season:
	+ very satisfied
	+ satisfied
	+ neutral
	+ dissatisfied
	+ very dissatisfied
