# Non Profit Volunteer Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect feedback from non-profit volunteers on their satisfaction with the organization. The collected data will help us better understand their needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the page you want to complete, either "Volunteer Feedback", "How engaged do you feel with our nonprofit", etc.
2. For each page, answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. If a field is optional, please provide information if it is relevant to your experience.
4. For multi-select fields, choose all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Volunteer Feedback** (Volunteer Satisfaction Poll 1):
  • This is a note field where you can provide any general feedback about your experience as a volunteer.
  • Please be as detailed and specific as possible.
- **How engaged do you feel with our nonprofit?** (Volunteer Engagement Poll 2):
  • This field is a text field where you can describe your level of engagement with the organization.
  • Please answer with a few words or a short paragraph.
- **Education or Training** (Volunteer Education Poll 3):
  • This field is a multi-select field where you can choose all the types of education or training you have received from the organization.
  • Please select all that apply.
  • Options: Yes, No
- **Any suggestions** (Volunteer Suggestions Poll 4):
  • This field is a text field where you can provide any suggestions or ideas for improvement.
  • Please be as specific as possible.
- **Education or Training Value** (Volunteer Education Value Poll 5):
  • This field is a multi-select field where you can choose all the types of education or training you have received from the organization that have value to you.
  • Please select all that apply.
  • Options: Yes, No
- **What matters most for your satisfaction** (Volunteer Satisfaction Important Poll 6):
  • This field is a select-one field where you can choose the most important factor for your satisfaction with the organization.
  • Please select one option.
  • Options: Satisfied, Dissatisfied
- **How satisfied are you with our program?** (Volunteer Satisfaction Scale Poll 7):
  • This field is a number field where you can rate your level of satisfaction with the organization's program on a scale of 1-10.
  • Please answer with a number between 1 and 10.
