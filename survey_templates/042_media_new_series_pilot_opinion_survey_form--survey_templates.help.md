# Media New Series Pilot Opinion Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions on a media series pilot episode. The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from individuals on their experience watching the pilot episode and their expectations for future episodes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the space provided. Make sure to provide detailed and honest feedback.
2. If a question does not apply to your experience, simply type "N/A" (Not Applicable) in the field.
3. You can skip questions that are not relevant to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pilot Episode Experience** (`pilot_episode_experience`, `text`, required: `false`): This field asks about your experience watching the pilot episode. Please describe your overall impression of the episode, including what you liked or disliked about it.
* **Overall Reception** (`overall_reception`, `text`, required: `false`): This field asks about your overall impression of the pilot episode. Please provide a brief description of your reception, e.g., "engaging", "confusing", "boring", etc.
* **Interest in Future Episodes** (`interest_in_future_episodes`, `text`, required: `false`): This field asks about your interest in watching future episodes of the series. Please describe your level of interest and why you might or might not be interested in watching more episodes.
