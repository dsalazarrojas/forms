# Stock Valuation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Stock Valuation Assessment form is designed to provide a comprehensive evaluation of a stock's current and potential future value. It helps investors and analysts assess the stock's risk level, expected growth rate, and other essential factors that influence its value.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Stock Valuation Assessment form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with the company's name and current stock price.
2. Select the stock's valuation range and risk level.
3. Enter the expected growth rate and other valuation inputs, such as revenue, earnings growth, dividends, and debt.
4. Choose the investment strategy and target date.
5. Provide reasons for the chosen risk level and expected stock price change.
6. Evaluate the stock's value and potential growth or decline.
7. Select the most suitable valuation metrics for further analysis.
