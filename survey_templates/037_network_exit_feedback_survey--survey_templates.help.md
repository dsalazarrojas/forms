# Network Exit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand why you are leaving our network and where we can improve to better support you and our members in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select all that apply for multiple choice questions.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Other (please specify)" option for the corresponding question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Why are you leaving?** (`offboarding_reason`, text, optional): This field is for you to briefly explain why you are leaving the network. Please provide a short description of your reasons.
* **What is your current membership status?** (`current_membership_status`, select_one, optional): This field helps us understand your current relationship with the network. Please select one of the provided options to indicate your current status.
* **Why are you leaving?** (`reasons_for_leaving`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for you to select the reasons why you are leaving the network. Please choose all that apply from the provided options.
* **What could we improve on the network experience?** (`feedback_on_network_experience`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any general feedback on how we can improve the network experience.
* **How could we improve to prevent you from leaving?** (`how_did_we_miss_it`, select_one, optional): This field is for you to suggest how we can improve to prevent similar situations in the future. Please select one of the provided options.
