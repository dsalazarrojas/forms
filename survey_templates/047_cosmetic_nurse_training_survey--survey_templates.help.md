# Cosmetic Nurse Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the Cosmetic Nurse Training Course. Your input will help us evaluate the course's quality, outcomes, and overall effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the answer that best describes your experience with the course for each question.
2. If a question does not apply to you, please select the "N/A" or "Not Applicable" option.
3. For the "Additional Comments" field, you can provide any other feedback or comments you think would be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Course Quality** (`course_quality`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
* This question asks you to rate the quality of the course. Select all that apply:
	+ Good
	+ Poor
### 2. **Outcomes** (`outcomes`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
* This question is about the outcomes or results of the course. Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
### 3. **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false)
* This is a space for you to provide any feedback or comments about your experience with the course.
### 4. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false)
* Here, you can add any additional comments or feedback you think would be helpful.
### 5. **Course Outcomes** (`course_outcomes`, `select_one`, required: false)
* This question is about the outcomes of the course. Select one of the options:
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
### 6. **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false)
* This is a rating field where you can enter a number.
### 7. **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false)
* Please provide your email address.
### 8. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false)
* This is a field for you to enter your phone number.
### 9. **Trainer Rating** (`trainer_rating`, `number`, required: false)
* This is a rating field where you can enter a number.
### 10. **Training Provider Rating** (`training_provider_rating`, `number`, required: false)
* This is a rating field where you can enter a number.
