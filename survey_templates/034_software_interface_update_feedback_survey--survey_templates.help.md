# software_interface_update_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the software interface. It is intended to be completed by software users, developers, and other stakeholders involved in the development process. The collected feedback will help identify areas of improvement and suggestions from users to enhance their experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Type in your company name in the "Company" field, if applicable.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Add your phone number in the "Phone number" field, if necessary.
6. Select your department from the available options in the "Department" field, if applicable.
7. Choose your Job title from the options in the "Job title" field, if necessary.
8. Select your level of satisfaction in the "Satisfaction level" field.
9. Share any usability issues you encountered in the "Usability issues encountered" field.
10. Provide any improvement suggestions in the "Improvement suggestions" field.
11. Add any additional comments in the "Additional comments" field, if necessary.
12. Provide a brief explanation for your satisfaction level in the "Why were you satisfied or dissatisfied?" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`form_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`form_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (`form_company`, text, optional): Type in your company name.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Add your phone number.
* **Department** (`form_department`, select_multiple, optional): Select your department from Team A, Team B, or Team C.
* **Job title** (`form_job_title`, select_one, optional): Choose your job title from Software Engineer, Software Manager, or Software Developer.
* **Satisfaction level** (`form_satisfaction_level`, select_one, optional): Select your level of satisfaction from Satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Usability issues encountered** (`form_usability_issues`, text, optional): Share any issues you faced while using the software.
* **Improvement suggestions** (`form_improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional comments** (`form_additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments, if necessary.
* **Why were you satisfied or dissatisfied?** (`form_satisfaction_why`, text, optional): Explain your reason for your satisfaction or dissatisfaction level.
