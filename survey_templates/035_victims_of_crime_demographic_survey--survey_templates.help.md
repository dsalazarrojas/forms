# Victims Of Crime Demographic Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information from victims of crime to provide support and understanding of the circumstances surrounding the crime.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Victim Information" section, which includes the victim's sex.
2. Move on to the "Crime Type" section, where you can select one or multiple types of crime that occurred.
3. Fill out the "Age" field with the victim's age.
4. In the "Contact Information" section, choose how you would like to be contacted.
5. Provide the location information in the "Location Information" section.
6. Enter the time of day the crime occurred in the "Time Of Day" field.
7. Select the weapon used in the crime from the "Weapon Used" section.
8. Enter the victim's date of birth.
9. In the "Race/Ethnicity" section, select the victim's racial identity.
10. Fill out the "Marital Status" field with the victim's marital status.
11. Enter the victim's occupation.
12. In the "Education Level" section, select the victim's educational level.
13. Fill out the "Income" field with the victim's annual income.
14. In the "Family Information" section, provide any relevant family details.
15. Select the victim's employment status.
16. In the "Health Insurance" section, indicate whether the victim has health insurance.
17. Choose the victim's housing status.
18. In the "Victim Support" section, provide any information about support received or needed.
19. In the "Witness" section, indicate whether there was a witness present.
20. In the "Suspect Information" section, provide any information about the suspect.
21. In the "Suspect Relationship" section, describe the relationship between the victim and the suspect.
22. In the "Suspect Location" section, provide the location where the suspect was last seen.
23. In the "Suspect Weapon" section, describe the weapon allegedly used by the suspect.
24. In the "Additional Information" section, provide any other information that is not covered in the other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Victim Information** (Sex, Required/Optional): The victim's sex, which will help us better understand the demographics of victims.
* **Crime Type** (Crime Type, Required/Optional): Select one or multiple types of crime that occurred to the victim.
* **Age** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the victim's age.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Method, Required/Optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Location Information** (Location Types, Required/Optional): Select the location where the crime occurred.
* **Time Of Day** (Time, Required/Optional): Enter the time of day the crime occurred.
* **Weapon Used** (Weapon Types, Required/Optional): Select the weapon allegedly used in the crime.
* **Date Of Birth** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the victim's date of birth.
* **Race/Ethnicity** (Racial Identity, Required/Optional): Select the victim's racial identity.
* **Marital Status** (Marital Status, Required/Optional): Fill out the victim's marital status.
* **Occupation** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the victim's occupation.
* **Education Level** (Education Level, Required/Optional): Select the victim's educational level.
* **Income** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the victim's annual income.
* **Family Information** (Text, Required/Optional): Provide any relevant family details.
* **Employment** (Employment Status, Required/Optional): Select the victim's employment status.
* **Health Insurance** (Insurance Status, Required/Optional): Indicate whether the victim has health insurance.
* **Housing Information** (Housing Status, Required/Optional): Choose the victim's housing status.
* **Victim Support** (Text, Required/Optional): Provide any information about support received or needed.
* **Witness** (Witness Present, Required/Optional): Indicate whether there was a witness present.
* **Suspect Information** (Text, Required/Optional): Provide any information about the suspect.
* **Suspect Relationship** (Text, Required/Optional): Describe the relationship between the victim and the suspect.
* **Suspect Location** (Text, Required/Optional): Provide the location where the suspect was last seen.
* **Suspect Weapon** (Text, Required/Optional): Describe the weapon allegedly used by the suspect.
* **Additional Information** (Text, Required/Optional): Provide any other information that is not covered in the other fields.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can ask for help.
* You can always add any additional information in the "Additional Information" field.
