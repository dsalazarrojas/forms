The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the cooperation experience with vendors. This form is intended to gather opinions and ratings from users on their satisfaction and experience with suppliers, including their satisfaction with the supplier's performance, the level of collaboration, and the effectiveness of communication.

To complete this form, follow the guidelines below:

## Purpose
The Vendor Cooperation Experience Survey is designed to gather feedback on your experience with suppliers. It consists of multiple pages, each with its own set of questions and fields.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. For each page, complete the questions and fields as instructed.
3. Use the available options to select your answers, or enter a free-form response if allowed.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Satisfaction** (`supplier_satisfaction`, number, required: false): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the supplier. Select a number from 1 to 5 to rate your satisfaction, where 1 is "Very Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Communication Level** (`communication_level`, select_multiple, required: false): This question measures your satisfaction with the level of communication from the supplier. Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your rating of the level of collaboration between your team and the supplier. Select one of the following options:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Primary Contact** (`vendor_contact_info`, email, required: false): This question asks for the email address of your primary contact at the supplier.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about the method(s) you used to communicate with the supplier. Select one of the following options:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Video Calls
	+ In-Person Meetings
* **Communication Frequency** (`frequency_of_communication`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about the frequency of communication with the supplier. Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Never
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your rating of the effectiveness of the communication from the supplier. Select one of the following options:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not Very Effective
	+ Not at All Effective

**Tips**

* Be honest and sincere in your responses.
* Select the most suitable options for each question.
* Complete all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please include them in the free-form response field (if available).
