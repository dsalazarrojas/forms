# Vote For Your Preferred Candidate - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from voters about their preferred candidate in an election or other voting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please select your preferred candidate from the list of available options.
* Confirm that you are voting from your own source, such as your personal computer or phone.
* Enter your email address and any additional information about the candidate(s) you are voting for (if applicable).
* Provide the date and time you are casting your vote.
* Confirm that you are voting from your own source (if applicable).
* If you have any additional information about the candidates, feel free to enter it in the provided field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Your Preferred Candidate** (`candidate_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the candidate you wish to vote for from the list of available options.
* **Vote Source** (`vote_source`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are voting from your own source (e.g. your personal computer or phone).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Enter any additional information about the candidate(s) you are voting for.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure that you can be contacted if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted about your vote.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are casting your vote.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you are casting your vote.
* **Additional Information** (`note_2`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the candidates (if applicable).
* **Additional Information About Candidates** (`candidate_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the candidates (if applicable).
* **Confirm Vote Source** (`vote_source_confirm`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are voting from your own source.
* **Select Another Candidate** (`candidate_selection_2`, select_one, optional): If you wish to vote for another candidate, select that option.
* **Note 3** (`note_3`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the candidates (if applicable).
