# Medical Laboratory Technician Program Graduate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from graduates of the Medical Laboratory Technician program to understand their experience, strengths, and weaknesses of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your Program Name.
2. Provide the date you graduated (MM/DD/YYYY format).
3. Enter the name of your instructor.
4. Rate your program length (in months or years).
5. Describe the strengths of the program's curriculum.
6. Describe the weaknesses of the program's curriculum.
7. Rate your instructor's quality (on a scale of 1-5).
8. Evaluate the quality of the course materials (Good, Fair, or Poor).
9. Rate the level of support you received (1-5).
10. Enter the date you graduated (MM/DD/YYYY format).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name**: 
    * Enter the name of the program you graduated from.
* **When did you graduate**: 
    * Enter the date you completed the program in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Instructor Name**: 
    * Enter the name of your instructor or the person who taught you.
* **Program Length**: 
    * Rate the length of the program in months or years.
* **Curriculum Strengths**: 
    * Describe the strong points of the program's curriculum. What aspects did you enjoy or find helpful?
* **Curriculum Weaknesses**: 
    * Describe the areas where you struggled or felt the program's curriculum was lacking. What could be improved?
* **Instructor Quality**: 
    * Rate your instructor's quality as a teacher on a scale of 1-5.
* **Course Materials**: 
    * Evaluate the quality of the course materials provided (Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Course Support**: 
    * Rate the level of support you received from the course (1-5).
* **Graduation Date**: 
    * Enter the date you graduated in MM/DD/YYYY format.
