# Photography Tour Client Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from clients who have participated in a photography tour. It will help us understand their experience, satisfaction, and provide valuable insights to enhance the tour experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer all questions that apply to your experience.
- Select one or multiple options for each question where applicable.
- You can provide as much detail as you need in the "Comments" section.
- Make sure to fill in your "Email" and "Phone" fields with correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief welcome message from us.
* **Rate Your Tour**: Please rate your experience with us from 1 to 5 stars.
* **Satisfaction**: Select one of the satisfaction levels: Not Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Satisfied, Very Satisfied, Extremely Satisfied.
* **Routes**: Choose the route(s) that best describe your experience. Please select one or multiple options.
* **Your Guide**: Select how satisfied you are with your guide. Please choose one of the options.
* **What Would You Improve**: If you have any suggestions, please type them here.
* **Comments**: Share your thoughts and experiences with us.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can reach out to you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Submit**: This date is not required for submission, it's a timestamp for internal tracking.
 
 Note: This form will help us identify areas of improvement and make the tour experience better for everyone. Your feedback is valuable, and we appreciate your input.
