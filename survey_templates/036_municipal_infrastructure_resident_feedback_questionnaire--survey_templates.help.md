# Municipal Infrastructure Resident Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this questionnaire is to gather feedback from residents about their neighborhood services, local conditions, and overall community satisfaction. This feedback will be used by city officials to understand the needs and concerns of their constituents and to inform decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose your answers carefully, as they will help city officials understand your perspective on neighborhood services, local conditions, and community satisfaction.
2. For each question, select all that apply or enter your response in the text field provided.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, even if they are not necessarily relevant to you.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the note field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **neighborhood_services** (`neighborhood_services`, select multiple, required/optional): 
  Choose all that apply about the services you use or would like to use in your neighborhood. Options include "Yes" and "No".
- **local_conditions** (`local_conditions`, text, required/optional): 
  Describe your current local conditions. Please be as detailed as possible about any issues or concerns you have.
- **community_satisfaction** (`community_satisfaction`, select one, required/optional): 
  Choose how satisfied you are with your community, with options for "Satisfied" and "Dissatisfied".
- **services_improvement** (`services_improvement`, note, required/optional): 
  If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving services in your neighborhood, enter them here.
- **contact_info** (`contact_info`, email, required/optional): 
  Please enter your email address so that city officials can contact you for follow-up questions or to discuss your feedback.
- **resident_suggestions** (`resident_suggestions`, text, required/optional): 
  If you have any additional comments or suggestions for improving your neighborhood, enter them here.
- **follow_up** (`follow_up`, select multiple, required/optional): 
  Choose all that apply about how you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions, with options for "Yes" and "No". 

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate with your responses, as they will help city officials make informed decisions.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact city officials using the email address provided.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this questionnaire!
