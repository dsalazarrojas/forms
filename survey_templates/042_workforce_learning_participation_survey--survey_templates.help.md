# Workforce Learning Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from users regarding their participation in workforce learning programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections, each addressing different aspects of the training. Please answer each question truthfully and provide specific examples or reasons for your ratings.
2. Select the relevant options for each question that best describe your experience with the training.
3. If you have suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the "Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Program** (Select Multiple): Choose all the training programs you participated in.
	+ This question helps us understand the scope of your experience with the training programs.
* **Rate This Training** (Select One): Rate your overall experience with the training as "Good" or "Fair".
	+ This question helps us evaluate the effectiveness of the training.
* **What Did You Like** (Text): Provide specific reasons why you liked the training.
	+ This question helps us understand what worked well in the training.
* **What Did You Dislike** (Text): Provide specific reasons why you disliked the training.
	+ This question helps us identify areas for improvement in the training.
* **Suggestions** (Text): Provide suggestions for improving the training program.
	+ This question helps us gather ideas for future development.
* **How Useful Was This Training** (Select One): Rate the usefulness of the training on a scale of 1 to 10.
	+ This question helps us evaluate the practical application of the training.
* **Training Type** (Select One): Choose the type of training that best describes your experience.
	+ This question helps us understand the nature of the training.
* **Training Status** (Select One): Choose the status of your training participation.
	+ This question helps us track your participation status.
* **Training Date** (Date): Enter the date of the training session.
	+ This question helps us record the training session date.
* **Training Time** (Time): Enter the time of the training session.
	+ This question helps us record the training session time.
* **Assigned** (Select One): Choose the person assigned to the training session.
	+ This question helps us track the assigned person.
* **Submitted** (Select One): Choose the status of your submission.
	+ This question helps us track your submission status.
* **Training Location** (Select Multiple): Choose the locations where the training was conducted.
	+ This question helps us understand the training location.
* **Session Length** (Number): Enter the length of the session.
	+ This question helps us record the session length.
* **Session Time** (Time): Enter the time of the session.
	+ This question helps us record the session time.
* **Session Date** (Date): Enter the date of the session.
	+ This question helps us record the session date.
