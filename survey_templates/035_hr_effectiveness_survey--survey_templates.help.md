# HR Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help HR professionals assess the effectiveness of HR services in your organization and provide feedback on areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the overall impact of HR services in your organization.
2. Choose the functional areas where HR services are currently being utilized.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with HR services.
4. Rate the impact of HR services on business outcomes.
5. Rate the impact of HR services on financial outcomes.
6. Rate the impact of HR services on operational outcomes.
7. Rate the impact of HR services on cultural outcomes.
8. Select the overall impact of HR services in your organization.
9. Provide an overall recommendation for HR services.
10. Provide additional comments on your experience with HR services.
11. Enter the tool assigned to you by your HR team.
12. Select the date and time you conducted this survey.
13. Enter the time of day you conducted this survey.
14. Enter any additional comments about your experience with HR services.
15. Enter your email address for contact information.
16. Enter your phone number for contact information.
17. Enter your job title or position.
18. Enter your company name (optional).
19. Select your job function in your organization.
20. If your job function is not listed, enter your job function in the text field.
21. Enter your department name (optional).
22. Enter your location (optional).
23. Enter who assigned you to conduct this survey (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Impact** (id: 1): Select the overall impact of HR services in your organization from the provided options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Functional Areas** (id: 2): Choose the functional areas where HR services are currently being utilized. Select all that apply from the options: Recruitment, Training, Benefits, Compensation, Employee Relations, Performance Management, Organizational Design, Communication.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (id: 3): Rate your overall satisfaction with HR services using a numerical rating.
* **Effectiveness Score** (id: 4): Rate the effectiveness of HR services on a numerical scale.
* **Impact on Business Outcomes** (id: 5): Rate the impact of HR services on business outcomes using a numerical scale.
* **Impact on Financial Outcomes** (id: 6): Rate the impact of HR services on financial outcomes using a numerical scale.
* **Impact on Operational Outcomes** (id: 7): Rate the impact of HR services on operational outcomes using a numerical scale.
* **Impact on Cultural Outcomes** (id: 8): Rate the impact of HR services on cultural outcomes using a numerical scale.
* **Overall Impact** (id: 9): Select the overall impact of HR services in your organization from the provided options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Overall Recommendation** (id: 10): Provide an overall recommendation for HR services.
* **Additional Comments** (id: 11): Provide any additional comments about your experience with HR services.
* **Assigned Tool** (id: 12): Enter the tool assigned to you by your HR team.
* **Survey Date** (id: 13): Select the date you conducted this survey.
* **Survey Time** (id: 14): Select the time of day you conducted this survey.
* **Survey Time AM/PM** (id: 15): Select AM or PM for the time of day you conducted this survey.
* **Comments** (id: 16): Provide any additional comments about your experience with HR services.
* **Email** (id: 17): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone Number** (id: 18): Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Job Title** (id: 19): Enter your job title or position.
* **Company** (id: 20): Enter your company name (optional).
* **Job Function** (id: 21): Select your job function in your organization from the provided options: Manager, Director, Supervisor, Coordinator, Employee.
* **Other Job Function** (id: 22): If your job function is not listed, enter your job function in the text field.
* **Department** (id: 23): Enter your department name (optional).
* **Location** (id: 24): Enter your location (optional).
* **Assigned By** (id: 25): Enter who assigned you to conduct this survey (optional).
