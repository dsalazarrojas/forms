# Resource Collaboration Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resource Collaboration Feedback Survey is designed to gather end-user feedback on various aspects of a resource. This feedback helps inform decision-making and drive product or service development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction on the first page.
2. Answer the questions on subsequent pages, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the ratings and options that best describe your experience with the resource.
4. Add any additional comments about your experience.
5. Enter your contact information to allow us to follow up with you, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This page is an overview of the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Resource Name**: This is the name of the resource you are providing feedback about. Please enter the actual name of the resource.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the resource, selecting from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Resource Outcome**: Select all options that describe your experience with the resource, including Highly, Moderately, Not, or Very.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your experience with the resource. This is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email address and a contact number (if available) so that we can follow up with you, if needed.
