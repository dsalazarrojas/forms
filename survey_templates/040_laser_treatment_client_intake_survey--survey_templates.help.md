# Laser Treatment Client Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about your laser treatment experience. It helps us understand your background, contact details, and treatment specifics. Please take a few minutes to carefully fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your Client Name
2. Enter your Contact Phone and/or Contact Email
3. Provide your Client Address (City and State)
4. Enter your Client Postal Code (zip or postal code)
5. Describe the treatment details you have undergone or will undergo
6. Check the box to give your consent for the treatment
7. Add any additional Client Notes you want to share with us

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the person receiving the laser treatment.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the client's phone number so we can contact them about their treatment.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the client's email address for communication purposes.
* **Client Address**: Enter the client's address to ensure we can send them information and packages.
* **Client City**: Enter the city where the client resides.
* **Client State**: Enter the state where the client resides.
* **Client Postal**: Enter the client's postal code (zip or postal code).
* **Treatment Details**: Describe the laser treatment you have undergone or will undergo.
* **Consent**: Check this box to give your consent for the treatment. This is an essential step to ensure we can proceed with your care.
* **Client Note**: Add any additional information you want to share with us about your client experience. This can be useful for us to provide personalized care.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can provide the best possible care.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* Please check the "Consent" box to confirm your consent for the treatment.
