# emerging_technology_integration_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emerging Technology Integration Voting Form is a tool for evaluating and prioritizing technologies for integration into an organization. It helps in making informed decisions by providing a structured and transparent process for assessing and selecting technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and fill in the information as required.
2. Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Technology Priorities
• **Technology Priorities** (`Technology Priorities`, `text`, required: false): This field allows you to list the technologies that you are considering for integration. Please provide a brief description of each technology, including its name and a brief overview of its features and benefits.

### Current State
• **Current State** (`Current State`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to select the current state of the technology, such as "In Development" or "In Progress". This will help in tracking the progress of the technology.

### Integration Plan
• **Integration Plan** (`Integration Plan`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the plans and strategies for integrating the technology into your organization.

### Technical Risks
• **Technical Risks** (`Technical Risks`, `text`, required: false): Identify any technical risks or challenges that may arise during the integration process.

### Business Risks
• **Business Risks** (`Business Risks`, `text`, required: false): Identify any business risks or challenges that may arise during the integration process.

### Technical Skills
• **Technical Skills** (`Technical Skills`, `text`, required: false): Identify the technical skills required for the integration process, including any special skills or training needed.

### Integration Timeline
• **Integration Timeline** (`Integration Timeline`, `date`, required: false): Provide a rough estimate of the timeline for integrating the technology, including any milestones or deadlines.

### Expected Benefits
• **Expected Benefits** (`Expected Benefits`, `text`, required: false): Describe the expected benefits of integrating the technology, including any potential cost savings, increased efficiency, or improved customer satisfaction.

### Actual Benefits
• **Actual Benefits** (`Actual Benefits`, `text`, required: false): Describe any actual benefits that have been achieved since integrating the technology.

### Integration Status
• **Integration Status** (`Integration Status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the current status of the integration process, such as "In Progress" or "Completed".

### Technical Lead
• **Technical Lead** (`Technical Lead`, `text`, required: false): Identify the technical lead or point person responsible for the integration process.

### Business Owner
• **Business Owner** (`Business Owner`, `text`, required: false): Identify the business owner or stakeholder responsible for the integration process.

### Business Stakeholders
• **Business Stakeholders** (`Business Stakeholders`, `text`, required: false): Identify any business stakeholders who are involved in the integration process.

### Technical Stakeholders
• **Technical Stakeholders** (`Technical Stakeholders`, `text`, required: false): Identify any technical stakeholders who are involved in the integration process.

### Integration Team
• **Integration Team** (`Integration Team`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the members of the integration team, including any external partners or vendors.

### Review Cycle
• **Review Cycle** (`Review Cycle`, `date`, required: false): Provide a rough estimate of the review cycle for the integration process, including any scheduled review dates.

### Next Steps
• **Next Steps** (`Next Steps`, `text`, required: false): Describe any next steps or actions that need to be taken for the integration process.

### Comments
• **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about the integration process.

### Form Completed
• **Form Completed** (`Form Completed`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the reason why the form is being completed, such as "New Technology" or "Review and Update".

### Form Completion Date
• **Form Completion Date** (`Form Completion Date`, `date`, required: false): Provide the date when the form was completed.

### Technology Used
• **Technology Used** (`Technology Used`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the technologies used during the integration process, including any external tools or platforms.

### Technology Description
• **Technology Description** (`Technology Description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the technology, including its purpose and features.

### Technology URL
• **Technology URL** (`Technology URL`, `text`, required: false): Provide the URL of the technology, if applicable.

### Comments Comments
• **Comments Comments** (`Comments Comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about the technology.

### Additional Comments
• **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about the integration process.
