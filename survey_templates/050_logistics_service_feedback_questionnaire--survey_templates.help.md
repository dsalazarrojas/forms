# Logistics Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their logistics service experience. It is used to collect information about their satisfaction and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the relevant category that best describes your experience with our logistics service.
2. Provide your feedback on your experience with our logistics service.
3. Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or additional information.
4. Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
5. Select the date of your experience with our logistics service.
6. Select the time of your experience with our logistics service.
7. Choose any submission options that apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Service** (Select One): Choose the option that best describes your experience with our customer service: 
  * Option 1: Very Satisfied
  * Option 2: Satisfied
  * Option 3: Neutral
  * Option 4: Dissatisfied
* **Logistics Experience** (Number): Please enter a numerical value to rate your experience with our logistics service.
* **Feedback** (Text): Provide your feedback on your experience with our logistics service. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Suggestions** (Text): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them here.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or additional information.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date** (Date): Select the date of your experience with our logistics service.
* **Time** (Time): Select the time of your experience with our logistics service.
* **Submission** (Select Multiple): Choose any submission options that apply to your experience: 
  * Option 1: Option 1
  * Option 2
  * Option 3
