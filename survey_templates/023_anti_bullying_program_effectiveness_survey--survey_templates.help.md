# Anti Bullying Program Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is to help you complete the Anti Bullying Program Effectiveness Survey. This survey is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of the school's Anti-Bullying Program by gathering feedback from various stakeholders, including staff, students, and parents. Your input will help program coordinators understand areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the category that best describes you from the Respondent Category dropdown menu. If you are unsure, please choose the option that best fits your role.
2.  Answer the questions truthfully, considering the provided hints.
3.  For numerical fields (e.g., Quality of Program Materials and Resources), use the scale provided in the hint (e.g., 1 to 5).
4.  If you have any additional comments or suggestions for program coordinators, please type them in the "Other Comments for Program Coordinators" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Respondent Category** (`respondent_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Please select the category that best describes you from the options provided: Staff, Student, or Parent.

- **Do you recognize the name of the current Anti-Bullying Program?** (`program_recognition`, `select_one`, **required**): Select 'True' if you are aware of the program's name. If you are not aware, please select 'False'.

- **Quality of Program Materials and Resources** (`material_rating`, `number`, **required**): Rate the quality of the materials and resources provided by the Anti-Bullying Program on a scale from 1 to 5. A higher number indicates better quality.

- **Impact on Overall School Culture** (`culture_impact`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the option that best describes the impact of the Anti-Bullying Program on the overall school culture.

- **Accessibility of Reporting Tools** (`reporting_accessibility`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose how easy it is to report an incident using the provided tools. A more difficult experience may indicate the need for improvement.

- **Administrative Response Time to Reported Issues** (`response_time_rating`, `select_one`, **required**): Select how quickly the program's administrators respond to reported incidents. Faster responses can indicate effective program management.

- **Which workshops or sessions were most helpful?** (`helpful_sessions`, `text`, **optional**): Provide any relevant information on workshops or sessions that were particularly useful or impactful.

- **Areas of the school where you feel least safe** (`ongoing_safety_concerns`, `text`, **optional**): Share specific areas where you feel least safe or uncomfortable, which could be contributing to bullying concerns.

- **Do you feel staff are adequately trained to handle bullying?** (`staff_support_adequacy`, `select_one`, **required**): Select 'True' if you believe staff is well-trained, or choose 'False' or 'Unsure' otherwise.

- **Other Comments for Program Coordinators** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, **optional**): If you have any further comments or suggestions, please type them here.

Note: All fields are marked as required or optional, which means you must complete the fields marked as required or can choose not to fill out optional fields as you see fit.
