# Parent Reading Activity Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Reading Activity Guide is a survey form used to gather information from parents about their reading habits with their child. This form will help the program or school understand how often and in what context parents read with their child, what topics they read about, and what feedback they give to their child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure that you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter your child's name in the "Parent Name" field.
3. Enter your child's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
4. Answer the questions about your reading habits with your child, including how often you read, the time of day, location, and topics you read about.
5. Select the feedback you usually give to your child about their reading.
6. Add any additional notes if necessary.
7. Enter the name, address, phone number, and email of the program or school your child is associated with (if applicable).
8. Select who submitted the form (School or Parent).
9. Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
10. Add any additional submission notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your child's name in this field.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your child's email address in this field.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your child's name in this field.
* **Child Age** (`child_age`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your child's age in this field.
* **How often do you read with your child?** (`reading_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how often you read with your child.
	+ Once a day
	+ 2-3 times a week
	+ 1 time a week
	+ Less than once a week
* **What time of day do you read with your child?** (`reading_time`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the time of day you read with your child.
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
	+ Other
* **Where do you read with your child?** (`reading_location`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the location where you read with your child.
	+ At home
	+ At a library or bookstore
	+ At a park or playground
* **What topics do you usually read with your child?** (`reading_content`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the topics you usually read with your child.
	+ Books
	+ Magazines
	+ Comics
	+ News
* **What language do you read with your child?** (`reading_language`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the language you read with your child.
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ Other
* **What is your child's reading level?** (`reading_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your child's reading level.
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **What difficulty level of content do you read with your child?** (`reading_difficulty`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the difficulty level of content you read with your child.
	+ Easy
	+ Medium
	+ Hard
* **What feedback do you give to your child about their reading?** (`caregiver_feedback`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the feedback you usually give to your child about their reading.
	+ Encouragement
	+ Correction
	+ Praise
	+ Other
* **Additional notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes if necessary.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Your relationship with the child** (`parent_relationship`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your relationship with the child.
	+ Mother
	+ Father
	+ Grandparent
	+ Other
* **Name of the program or school** (`program_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the program or school your child is associated with.
* **Address of the program or school** (`program_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the program or school your child is associated with.
* **Phone number of the program or school** (`program_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the program or school your child is associated with.
* **Email of the program or school** (`program_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the program or school your child is associated with.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select who submitted the form.
	+ School
	+ Parent
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional submission notes if necessary.
* **Name of the program or school 2** (`program_name_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the second program or school your child is associated with.
* **Address of the program or school 2** (`program_address_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the second program or school your child is associated with.
* **Phone number of the program or school 2** (`program_phone_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the second program or school your child is associated with.
* **Email of the program or school 2** (`program_email_2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the second program or school your child is associated with.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all the necessary information before completing the form.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you answer is accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the program or school for assistance.
