# Library Reading Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Reading Program Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from participants and librarians about library reading programs. This form helps library management or administrators assess the effectiveness and impact of these programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter the name of the participant who attended the program in the **Participant Name** field.
4. Enter the name of the program being evaluated in the **Program Name** field.
5. Select the date you attended the program in the **Evaluation Date** field.
6. Enter the time you attended the program in the **Evaluation Time** field.
7. Enter the location where the program took place in the **Program Location** field.
8. Enter a score for your evaluation of the program (e.g. 1-5) in the **Evaluation Score** field.
9. Provide any additional comments about your experience in the **Feedback Comment** field.
10. Enter the number of program participants in the **Number of Program Participants** field.
11. Enter the duration of the program (e.g. in hours) in the **Program Duration** field.
12. Enter a brief description of the program in the **Program Description** field.
13. Enter any additional evaluation notes in the **Evaluation Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name so that we can address you properly and associate your feedback with your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for any follow-up questions or to provide you with more information.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant who attended the program, if different from yours.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Select the date you attended the program.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you attended the program.
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the program took place.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, number, optional): Enter a score from 1 to 5 to evaluate the program.
* **Feedback Comment** (`feedback_comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Number of Program Participants** (`program_participants`, number, optional): Enter the number of participants in the program.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of the program (e.g. in hours).
* **Program Description** (`program_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the program.
* **Evaluation Notes** (`evaluation_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional evaluation notes.
