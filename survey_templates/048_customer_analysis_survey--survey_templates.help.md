# Customer Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Analysis Survey is a tool used to collect information about customers' preferences and behaviors. This information is used to better understand their needs, preferences, and purchasing habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please contact the support team for clarification.

* **Customer Preferences** (`customer_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all that apply:
	+ Yes

* **Customer Behaviors** (`customer_behaviors`, note, required: false): This is a text area where you can provide any relevant information about the customer's behavior.

* **Customer Purchases** (`customer_purchases_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the customer's purchases.
