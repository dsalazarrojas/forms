# Coaching Staff Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from coaching staff members on their role satisfaction, responsibilities, and overall experience within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Respondent Information**
	* This section is meant to collect basic contact information from the respondent, such as their name and email address.
	* Please provide your full name as requested in the "Staff Member Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
2. **Role and Responsibilities**
	* In this section, you will be asked about your satisfaction with your role and responsibilities.
	* Rate your satisfaction with your role using the scale "Very satisfied" to "Very dissatisfied".
	* Rate the clarity of your role and responsibilities using the scale "Very clear" to "Very unclear".
	* Rate the manageability of your workload using the scale "Very manageable" to "Very unmanageable".
3. **Coaching Environment**
	* In this section, you will be asked about your experience with team collaboration, management support, and available resources.
	* Rate your experience with team collaboration using the scale "Excellent" to "Very poor".
	* Rate the support you receive from management using the scale "Excellent support" to "No support".
	* Rate the availability of resources and equipment using the scale "Excellent" to "Inadequate".
4. **Professional Development**
	* In this section, you will be asked about your opportunities for professional growth and development.
	* Rate the opportunities for growth and development using the scale "Excellent opportunities" to "No opportunities".
	* Rate the opportunities for professional growth using the scale "Many opportunities" to "No opportunities".
5. **Compensation and Benefits**
	* In this section, you will be asked about your satisfaction with compensation and benefits.
	* Rate your satisfaction with compensation using the scale "Very satisfied" to "Very dissatisfied".
	* Rate your satisfaction with benefits using the scale "Very satisfied" to "Very dissatisfied".
6. **Leadership**
	* In this section, you will be asked about your experience with leadership, including the head coach's leadership and communication with leadership.
	* Rate the leadership of the head coach using the scale "Excellent" to "Very poor".
	* Rate your experience with communication with leadership using the scale "Excellent" to "Very poor".
7. **Overall Feedback**
	* In this section, you will be asked about your overall feedback on the organization and whether you would recommend it as a place to work.
	* Rate your experience with overall feedback using the scale "Definitely yes" to "Definitely no".
	* Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide them in the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and provide feedback. Your input is valuable and will help us improve our organization.
