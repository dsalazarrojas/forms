# public_composting_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from the public about their interest in composting and their role in the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields according to your information and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Zip Code** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Enter your zip code (optional, but helps to identify your location).
* **Address** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your address.
* **Organization** (`6`, `text`, required: false): If you are affiliated with an organization, enter its name here.
* **Message** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments or messages about your interest in composting.
* **What is your role in the community?** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following options:
	+ Student
	+ Teacher
	+ Parent
	+ Community Leader
	+ Other
* **Which of the following are you interested in?** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply from the following options:
	+ Community Composting
	+ Food Waste Reduction
	+ Urban Agriculture
	+ Education and Outreach
	+ Community Garden
	+ Community Policy
* **Date** (`10`, `date`, required: false): Date (optional, but helps to track when you filled this form).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the fields that apply to you.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the relevant authorities or project team.

Note: This help guide is a general explanation, and you should check the form's description and any specific instructions from the project team before completing it.
