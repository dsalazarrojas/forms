# System Design Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The System Design Experience Survey aims to gather information about your experience and skills in system design, including challenges faced, required training, and areas where you would like to improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
4. For free-text fields, provide as much detail as possible.
