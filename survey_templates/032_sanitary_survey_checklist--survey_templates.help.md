# Sanitary Survey Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to conduct a comprehensive sanitary survey of a facility, ensuring it meets the required standards for a safe and healthy environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. For each field, provide detailed descriptions of the facility's condition, including any observations or issues that have been noted.
3. If a field is marked as optional, you may skip it if not applicable or relevant to your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Facility Capability (`facility_capability`, text, optional)**: Describe the facility's current capability to maintain a safe and healthy environment, including any issues or challenges that have been encountered.
- **Construction Condition (`construction_condition`, text, optional)**: Report on the overall condition of the facility's construction and infrastructure, including any damage, wear, or deterioration.
- **Operation Status (`operation_status`, text, optional)**: Provide an overview of the facility's operation, including any changes or concerns related to water supply, waste disposal, or other critical functions.
- **Physical Condition (`physical_condition`, text, optional)**: Describe the facility's physical state, including any signs of wear, tear, or damage.
- **Capability Capacity (`capability_capacity`, text, optional)**: Discuss the facility's capacity to handle potential scenarios, such as emergencies or large events.
- **Water System Condition (`water_system_condition`, text, optional)**: Evaluate the condition and performance of the facility's water system, including any issues or concerns.
- **Facility Design (`facility_design`, text, optional)**: Assess the overall design and layout of the facility, including any areas that may be improved or updated.

## Tips
* Be thorough and honest in your responses to ensure an accurate assessment.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, consult with facility management or relevant experts.
* This form is not intended to be used as a substitute for regular maintenance or inspections, but rather as a supplemental tool for facilities management.
