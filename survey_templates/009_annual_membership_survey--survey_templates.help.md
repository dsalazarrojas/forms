# Annual Membership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your experiences and opinions on being a member of our organization. Your responses will help us understand what we do well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer all questions that apply to you.
3. Choose the best option that fits your experience.
4. If a question does not apply to you, please select "Not applicable" or "Have not used".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name (page 1)** (`member_name`, `text`, `optional`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your membership documents.
* **Email Address (page 1)** (`member_email`, `email`, `required`): Please enter your email address where we can contact you if needed.
* **Member ID Number (page 1)** (`member_id`, `text`, `optional`): If you have a member ID number, please enter it here. If not, please leave it blank.
* **Membership Level (page 1)** (`membership_level`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the level of membership you hold: Student, Individual, Professional, Corporate, Lifetime, or Other.
* **How long have you been a member (page 1)** (`years_member`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the length of time you have been a member: Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, or More than 10 years.
* **Primary reasons for membership (page 1)** (`primary_reasons`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Choose all the reasons why you are a member, from Networking opportunities, Access to resources, Professional development, Community support, Publications and materials, Discounts and benefits, Industry involvement, or Other.
* **Membership provides good value for money (page 1)** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how strongly you agree that membership provides good value for money: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly disagree.
* **I actively use the member benefits (page 1)** (`benefits_utilization`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose how strongly you agree that you use the member benefits: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly disagree.
* **Most valued membership benefit (page 1)** (`most_valued_benefit`, `text`, `optional`): If you could choose one benefit, what would it be?
* **Least valued or unused benefits (page 1)** (`least_valued_benefit`, `text`, `optional`): If you could choose one benefit, what would it be?
* **Quality of member communications (page 2)** (`communication_quality`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the quality of our communications, such as newsletters, updates, and announcements: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, or Very poor.
* **Participation in member events (page 2)** (`event_participation`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how often you participate in events: Regularly, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **Quality of events offered (page 2)** (`event_quality`, `select_one`, `optional`): Rate the quality of our events: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, or Not applicable.
* **Usefulness of member website or portal (page 2)** (`website_usefulness`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the usefulness of our website or portal: Very useful, Useful, Somewhat useful, Not useful, or Have not used.
* **I feel part of this community (page 2)** (`community_feeling`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how strongly you feel like you are part of our community: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly disagree.
* **Quality of member support and service (page 2)** (`support_quality`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the quality of our support and service: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, or Have not needed support.
* **Overall satisfaction with membership (page 2)** (`satisfaction_overall`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate your overall satisfaction with membership: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Likelihood to renew membership (page 2)** (`renewal_likelihood`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how likely you are to renew your membership: Very likely, Likely, Undecided, Unlikely, or Very unlikely.
* **Improvements you would suggest (page 2)** (`improvements_needed`, `text`, `optional`): If you have any suggestions for improvements, please list them here.
* **New benefits you would like to see (page 2)** (`new_benefits_suggestions`, `text`, `optional`): If you have any suggestions for new benefits, please list them here.
* **Would you recommend membership to others (page 2)** (`recommend_others`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how likely you are to recommend membership to others: Definitely, Probably, Unsure, Probably not, or Definitely not.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
- If you are unsure about any question, please select the answer that best fits your experience.
- If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the "Improvements you would suggest" and "New benefits you would like to see" fields.
