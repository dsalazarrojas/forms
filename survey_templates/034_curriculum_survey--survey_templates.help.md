# Curriculum Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your curriculum development and progress. Please answer the questions honestly to provide valuable insights to educators and administrators.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each section as instructed, and make sure to fill out all required fields.
2.  Review each question carefully to ensure you are answering the correct information.
3.  If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Curriculum Survey** (`curriculum_survey`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your curriculum.
*   **Section 1** (`section_1`, text, optional): This section is for you to describe your curriculum's main focus or objective.
*   **question_1** (`question_1`, select_one, optional): Choose the best option that describes the primary goal of your curriculum.
    \- option 1: To provide basic knowledge and skills
    \- option 2: To foster critical thinking and creativity
    \- option 3: To promote collaboration and communication
*   **Section 2** (`section_2`, text, optional): Describe the curriculum's structure and organization.
*   **question_2** (`question_2`, select_one, optional): Choose the best option that describes how you structured your curriculum.
    \- option 3: Linear and sequential
    \- option 4: Modular and flexible
*   **Section 3** (`section_3`, text, optional): Explain the curriculum's teaching methods and materials.
*   **question_3** (`question_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the best options that describe the teaching methods used in your curriculum.
    \- option 1: Lectures
    \- option 2: Discussions
    \- option 3: Group projects
*   **Section 4** (`section_4`, text, optional): Describe the curriculum's assessment and evaluation methods.
*   **question_4** (`question_4`, select_one, optional): Choose the best option that describes the assessment method used in your curriculum.
    \- option 2: Quizzes and exams
    \- option 3: Projects and presentations
*   **Section 5** (`section_5`, text, optional): Explain the curriculum's progress and development.
*   **question_5** (`question_5`, time, optional): Estimate the total duration of your curriculum.
*   **Section 6** (`section_6`, text, optional): Describe any relevant dates or timelines associated with your curriculum.
*   **question_6** (`question_6`, date, optional): Choose a date associated with your curriculum.
*   **Section 7** (`section_7`, text, optional): Explain any other relevant information about your curriculum.
*   **question_7** (`question_7`, number, optional): Provide the number of sections or modules in your curriculum.
*   **Section 8** (`section_8`, text, optional): You may add any additional information that is not covered above.

## Tips

*   Be honest and complete when filling out this form.
*   Review your answers carefully before submitting.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
