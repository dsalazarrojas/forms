# City Development Priority Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Development Priority Poll is a tool designed to gather feedback and priorities from residents, business owners, and frequent visitors to the city. This information will help city officials understand what improvements are needed and how to prioritize them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Your Name" and "Email Address" fields respectively.
2. Rate the importance of each development priority (e.g., downtown revitalization, housing, transportation) by selecting a number from 1 (least important) to 5 (most important).
3. Choose your preferred pace and type of development by selecting from the available options.
4. Select all that apply from the list of concerns about development.
5. Select all that apply from the list of public investment priorities.
6. Rate the importance of environmental standards, historic preservation, community involvement, and additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Please enter your name to help us know who you are and what your opinions are.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can keep you informed about developments and progress.
* **Resident Type**: Select your type of residency status:
	+ Year-round resident: You reside in the city all year round.
	+ Seasonal resident: You reside in the city only during specific seasons.
	+ Business owner: You own a business in the city.
	+ Property owner: You own a property in the city.
	+ Frequent visitor: You visit the city regularly, but do not reside there.
* **Downtown Revitalization and Commerce**: Rate the importance of revitalizing the downtown area and promoting commerce.
* **Housing Development**: Rate the importance of developing housing in the city.
* **Transportation Infrastructure**: Rate the importance of improving transportation systems, such as roads, bridges, and public transit.
* **Parks and Recreation Areas**: Rate the importance of creating or improving park and recreation areas.
* **Waterfront or Riverfront Development**: Rate the importance of developing the waterfront or riverfront areas.
* **Commercial Districts and Retail**: Rate the importance of promoting commercial districts and retail businesses.
* **Cultural Venues and Arts Districts**: Rate the importance of creating or improving cultural and arts districts.
* **Technology and Innovation Centers**: Rate the importance of developing technology and innovation centers.
* **Green Spaces and Sustainability**: Rate the importance of creating or improving green spaces and promoting sustainability.
* **Preferred Pace of Development**: Select your preferred pace of development:
	+ Accelerated growth: Fast-paced development.
	+ Moderate growth: Moderate-paced development.
	+ Slow, careful growth: Careful and slow development.
	+ No new development: No new development.
	+ Mixed approach: Combination of the above.
* **Preferred Type of Development**: Select your preferred type of development:
	+ Residential: Focused on housing development.
	+ Commercial: Focused on commercial development.
	+ Mixed-use: Combination of residential and commercial development.
	+ Industrial: Focused on industrial development.
	+ Balanced growth: Combination of all the above.
* **Development Concerns**: Select all that apply:
	+ Traffic and congestion.
	+ Environmental impact.
	+ Loss of community character.
	+ Affordability concerns.
	+ Loss of green space.
	+ Parking issues.
	+ Impact on schools.
	+ Cultural displacement.
	+ Gentrification.
* **Public Investment Priorities**: Select all that apply:
	+ Education infrastructure.
	+ Healthcare facilities.
	+ Transportation systems.
	+ Utilities modernization.
	+ Public buildings and facilities.
	+ Parks and green space.
	+ Public safety.
	+ Storm water management.
* **Environmental Standards**: Rate the importance of environmental standards:
	+ Very important.
	+ Important.
	+ Somewhat important.
	+ Not important.
* **Historic Preservation**: Rate the importance of historic preservation:
	+ Very important.
	+ Important.
	+ Somewhat important.
	+ Not important.
* **Community Involvement**: Rate the importance of community involvement in planning:
	+ Essential.
	+ Very important.
	+ Important.
	+ Somewhat important.
	+ Not important.
* **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Your Single Highest Development Priority**: If you have a single priority, please describe it in the space provided.
* **Additional Suggestions or Comments**: If you have any other suggestions or comments, please provide them in the space provided.
