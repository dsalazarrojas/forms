# Patient Preparation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about patient preparation for medical appointments.

## How To Complete This Form

This form consists of 8 sections, each designed to gather specific details about the patient. Please complete all the sections to ensure that the patient is properly prepared for their appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name to identify them correctly.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe the patient's current medical condition, including any relevant details or symptoms.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the patient's scheduled appointment.
* **Patient Contact Method** (`patient_contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how the patient would like to be contacted, such as phone, email, or SMS.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief overview of the patient's medical history, including any previous conditions or treatments.
* **Allergy Status** (`allergy_status`, select_one, optional): Select any allergies the patient may have, such as Label 1, Label 2, or Label 3.
* **Medication Status** (`medication_status`, text, optional): Describe the patient's current medication status, including any prescribed medications or supplements.

## Tips

* Ensure that you complete all sections to provide a thorough understanding of the patient's preparation.
* If a patient has no allergies, select "None" or "No allergies".
* If a patient has multiple allergies, select all relevant options.
