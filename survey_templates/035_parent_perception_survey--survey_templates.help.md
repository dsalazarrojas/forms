# parent_perception_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **Parent Perception Survey** is a tool designed to gather feedback from parents about their experiences and perceptions regarding a program. This form helps to collect data on how parents view their interaction with the program, their overall satisfaction, and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Parent Perception Survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your parent-school information, including the name of the school and any other relevant details.
2. Answer the questions about your experience and satisfaction with the program.
3. Select the areas where you'd like to see improvement, and provide any additional comments.
4. If you have specific suggestions for improvement, please share them in the box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Parent School Information** (`parent_perception_survey_school_info`): Enter your parent-school information.
  (Text field)

- **Parent Experience** (`parent_perception_survey_parent_experience`): Describe your experience with the program.
  (Text field)

- **Parent Satisfaction** (`parent_perception_survey_satisfaction`):
  Select your level of satisfaction with the program.
  - I am very satisfied
  - I am somewhat satisfied
  - I am neither satisfied nor dissatisfied
  - I am somewhat dissatisfied
  - I am very dissatisfied

- **Parent Program Satisfaction** (`parent_perception_survey_program_satisfaction`):
  Select your level of satisfaction with the program.
  - Very Good
  - Somewhat Good
  - Neither Good Nor Bad
  - Somewhat Bad
  - Very Bad

- **Communication** (`parent_perception_survey_communication`): Describe your experience with the communication.
  (Text field)

- **Parent Program Improvement** (`parent_perception_survey_program_improvement`):
  Select an option from the list to indicate how you'd like to improve the program.
  - Improve the current program
  - Keep the current program
  - Do not improve or discontinue the program

- **Additional Comments** (`parent_perception_survey_additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments about the program.
  (Text field)

- **Parent Program Improvement Details** (`parent_perception_survey_program_improvement_details`): Enter any details about your improvement suggestions.
  (Text field)

- **Parent Program Improvement Comments** (`parent_perception_survey_program_improvement_comments`): Enter any comments about your improvement suggestions.
  (Text field)

- **Parent Program Improvement Priority** (`parent_perception_survey_program_improvement_priority`):
  Select the priority of your improvement suggestion.
  - High Priority
  - Medium Priority
  - Low Priority
  - Not Applicable

- **Parent Program Improvement Impact** (`parent_perception_survey_program_improvement_impact`):
  Select the impact of your improvement suggestion.
  - High
  - Medium
  - Low
  - Not Applicable

- **Parent Program Improvement Frequency** (`parent_perception_survey_program_improvement_frequency`):
  Select how often you'd like to see your improvement suggestions implemented.
  - Regularly
  - Occasionally
  - Rarely
  - Not Applicable

- **Parent Program Improvement Suggestion** (`parent_perception_survey_program_improvement_suggest`): Enter your improvement suggestion.
  (Text field)

- **Parent Program Improvement Suggestion Details** (`parent_perception_survey_program_improvement_suggest_details`): Enter any details about your improvement suggestion.
  (Text field)

- **Parent Perception** (`parent_perception_survey_parent_perception`): Enter your overall perception of the program.
  (Text field)

## Tips

- Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
