# Mental Health Support Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track client sessions, providing mental health professionals with a quick and easy way to gather and document session details and client feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in all the required fields (if any).
*   Select the relevant options for each field as needed.
*   Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Mental Health Professional Comments" field.
*   Verify the client's contact information is correct.
*   Enter the session details, such as date and time.
*   Complete the "Session Length" field with the actual session duration.
*   Enter the "Session ID" and "Client ID" (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Session Notes**: A free-text field for recording any notes about the session. 
*   **Session Outcome**: Select 'Yes' if the client had a positive outcome, 'No' otherwise. 
*   **Client Satisfaction**: Select all options that apply to the client's level of satisfaction.
*   **Recommendations**: A free-text field for the mental health professional to record any recommendations for future sessions or follow-up actions. 
*   **Client Feedback**: A free-text field for the client to provide feedback about the session. 
*   **Mental Health Professional Comments**: A free-text field for the mental health professional to record any comments or observations about the client. 
*   **Client Contact Info**: Enter the client's email address or other contact information. 
*   **Mental Health Professional Contact Info**: Enter the mental health professional's contact information. 
*   **Date**: Enter the date of the session. 
*   **Time**: Enter the start and end time of the session. 
*   **Session Length**: Enter the actual duration of the session. 
*   **Client ID**: Enter the client's ID number. 
*   **Mental Health Professional ID**: Enter the mental health professional's ID number. 
*   **Session ID**: Enter the unique ID for the session. 
*   **Session Number**: Enter the session number or order of the session.
