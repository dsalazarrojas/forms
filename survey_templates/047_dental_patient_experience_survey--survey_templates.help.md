# Dental Patient Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from dental patients about their experience at the clinic.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the following information:
	* Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents (page 1: patient_name)
	* Choose the date of your visit to the clinic (page 2: date)
	* Rate your experience with the quality of care you received (page 3: experience_rating)
	* Describe your interaction with the staff, including how they treated you and made you feel (page 4: staff_interaction)
	* Share your thoughts on the appointment scheduling process (page 5: appointment_scheduling)
	* Share your thoughts on the communication with the dental team during your visit (page 6: communication)
	* Rate your experience with pain management during your visit (page 7: pain_management)
	* Share any comments on the billing process (page 8: billing)
	* Describe your experience with the dental team, including their professionalism and expertise (page 9: dental_team)
	* Write a brief summary of your overall experience at the clinic (page 10: overall_experience)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Patient Name** (page 1): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Visit** (page 2): Choose the date of your visit to the clinic.
* **Quality of Care** (page 3): Rate your experience with the quality of care you received on a scale of 1-5.
* **Staff Interaction** (page 4): Describe your interaction with the staff, including how they treated you and made you feel.
* **Appointment Scheduling** (page 5): Share your thoughts on the appointment scheduling process.
* **Communication** (page 6): Share your thoughts on the communication with the dental team during your visit.
* **Pain Management** (page 7): Rate your experience with pain management during your visit.
* **Billing** (page 8): Share any comments on the billing process.
* **Dental Team** (page 9): Describe your experience with the dental team, including their professionalism and expertise.
* **Overall Experience** (page 10): Write a brief summary of your overall experience at the clinic.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when providing your feedback.
* Please rate your experience on a scale of 1-5 for the Quality of Care and Pain Management fields.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them in the comments sections of the relevant fields.
