# EBook Download And Offer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "EBook Download And Offer Form" is a simple text-based form designed to collect information from potential customers who want to download an eBook. It is intended to be a quick and easy way to gather contact details and preferences from interested individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "download_and_offer_form" page with the desired information.
2. On the next page, provide your contact information, including your email address, in the "contact_information" field.
3. Fill out the "name", "company", and "job_title" fields with your relevant details.
4. Choose the "download_ebook" option that best describes your request.
5. If you would like to download the eBook again, fill out the "download_ebook_again" field.
6. Finally, choose whether you would like to receive an offer for the eBook from our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Download and Offer Form (`download_and_offer_form`, text, optional)**: Enter the title of the eBook you would like to download.
* **Contact Information (`contact_information`, text, optional)**: Enter your email address to receive further information about the eBook.
* **Email (`email`, email, optional)**: Enter your email address associated with your company or personal account.
* **Name (`name`, text, optional)**: Enter your name to be associated with the download request.
* **Company (`company`, text, optional)**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Job Title (`job_title`, text, optional)**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Download Ebook (`download_ebook`, text, optional)**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ No Offer
	+ Offer 1
	+ Offer 2
* **Download Ebook Again (`download_ebook_again`, text, optional)**: If you would like to download the eBook again, fill out this field with the reason.
* **Offer Ebook (`offer_ebook`, select_one, optional)**: Choose whether you would like to receive an offer for the eBook from our team.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure efficient communication with our team.
* If you have already downloaded the eBook in the past, you may not need to fill out this form again. However, if you are interested in downloading it again, this form can still be used to request access.
