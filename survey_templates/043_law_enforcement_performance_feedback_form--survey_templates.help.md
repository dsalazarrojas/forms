# Law Enforcement Performance Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Law Enforcement Performance Feedback Form is designed to help law enforcement officers provide feedback on their performance in specific incidents or situations. This form is meant to aid in the professional development and improve community relations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are using the form for its intended purpose.
2. Provide accurate and honest feedback about your performance in the specified incident or situation.
3. Answer each question carefully, taking into account your experiences and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your full last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Police Force** (`police_force`, select_one, required): Select the police force or department you work with.
* **Police Officer Name** (`police_officer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the officer you are providing feedback about.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident or situation.
* **Officer Rating** (`officer_rating`, number, required): Rate your performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_multiple, required): Select the rating scale that best matches your performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about your performance or the incident.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter a contact number where you can be reached for follow-up questions.
* **Police Station** (`police_station`, select_one, required): Select the police station you work at.
* **Officer Credential** (`officer_credential`, text, optional): Enter any relevant credentials or certifications you hold.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of incident or situation you were involved in.
* **Officer Response** (`officer_response`, text, optional): Provide any response or actions you took in the incident.
* **Interaction Type** (`interaction_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of interaction you had in the incident.
* **Police Equipment** (`police_equipment`, text, optional): Enter any police equipment you used during the incident.
* **Interaction Details** (`interaction_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the interaction.
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, select_one, required): Select the outcome of the incident.
* **Officer Notes** (`officer_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or reflections you may have about your performance.
* **Community Suggestions** (`community_suggestions`, text, optional): Enter any suggestions you may have from the community or the public.
* **Officer Agreement** (`officer_agreement`, select_multiple, required): Select how you agree or disagree with the ratings or comments provided.
* **Officer Concerns** (`officer_concerns`, text, optional): Enter any concerns or issues you may have about your performance or the incident.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of a contact person.
* **Department Comments** (`department_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the department or superiors.
* **Community Comments** (`community_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the community or public.
