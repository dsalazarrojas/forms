# International Buyer Return Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect feedback from international customers about their return experiences and satisfaction with a company's return policies. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better service to international buyers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Return Policy**: Select the return policy that best represents your experience with our company.
	* Choose the option that best describes your experience.
2. **Return Challenges**: Select all challenges you faced during the return process.
	* Choose all challenges that apply.
3. **Return Clarity**: Enter the level of clarity about our return policy when you first received it.
	* Enter a number between 1 (not clear at all) and 10 (very clear).
4. **Return Satisfaction**: (Optional) Enter the level of satisfaction with our return process.
	* Enter a number between 0 (very dissatisfied) and 10 (very satisfied).
5. **Return Process**: Describe any challenges you faced during the return process.
	* Provide a brief description.
6. **Return Fees**: (Optional) Enter the amount you paid in fees during the return process.
	* Enter a number.
7. **International Experience**: Select whether you have international experience with our company.
	* Choose 'Yes' if you have experience, 'No' otherwise.
8. **Return Cancellations**: Describe any reasons for return cancellations.
	* Provide a brief description.
9. **Global Return Satisfaction**: (Optional) Enter the level of satisfaction with our return policy on a global scale.
	* Enter a number between 0 (very dissatisfied) and 10 (very satisfied).

## Tips
* Please select only one option for "Return Policy" that best represents your experience.
* For "Return Challenges" and "Return Cancellations", choose all challenges or reasons that apply.
* For "Return Clarity", "Return Satisfaction", and "Global Return Satisfaction", enter numbers between 1 and 10.
* Be as specific as possible when answering "Return Process" and "Return Cancellations".
