# Constructive Feedback And Self Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate constructive feedback and self-assessment for employees. It is meant to be completed by employees to reflect on their performance, identify areas for improvement, and set goals for the next review period. The form gathers information on employee performance, skills, and goals, allowing managers and supervisors to provide targeted feedback and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details: Provide your full name, employee identification number, department, and current job title.
2. Select your overall performance rating for the review period.
3. Describe your major accomplishments and strengths during this period.
4. Identify areas where you can improve and provide suggestions for growth.
5. Select your progress on previous goals and explain your reasoning.
6. Describe your goals for the upcoming review period.
7. List any new skills or competencies gained during this period.
8. Indicate whether you require any training or development support.
9. Rate your teamwork, communication, and productivity levels.
10. Summarize feedback from your peers and supervisor (if applicable).
11. Indicate any additional support needed from management.
12. Describe your career goals and aspirations.
13. Add any other comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee identification number if applicable.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Specify the department or division you belong to.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Review Period Start Date** (`review_period_start`, date, required): Enter the start date of the review period.
* **Review Period End Date** (`review_period_end`, date, required): Enter the end date of the review period.
* **Overall Self Rating** (`self_rating_overall`, select_one, required): Rate your overall performance (Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, Needs Improvement).
* **Key Accomplishments This Period** (`key_accomplishments`, text, required): Describe your major achievements during this period.
* **Personal Strengths** (`strengths`, text, required): Identify your key strengths in this role.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required): Identify areas where you can improve and provide suggestions for growth.
* **Were Previous Goals Met** (`goals_met`, select_one, required): Select your progress on previous goals (All Goals Met, Most Goals Met, Some Goals Met, Few Goals Met, No Goals Met).
* **Goals Progress Description** (`goals_description`, text, optional): Explain your progress on goals.
* **New Goals for Next Period** (`new_goals`, text, required): Describe goals for the upcoming review period.
* **Skills Developed** (`skills_developed`, text, optional): List any new skills or competencies gained.
* **Training or Development Needed** (`training_needed`, text, optional): Indicate whether you require any training or development support.
* **Teamwork and Collaboration Rating** (`teamwork_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your teamwork and collaboration performance (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Communication Skills Rating** (`communication_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your communication abilities (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Productivity Rating** (`productivity_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your productivity level (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Feedback Received from Peers** (`feedback_from_peers`, text, optional): Summarize feedback from your colleagues.
* **Feedback from Supervisor** (`feedback_from_supervisor`, text, optional): Summarize feedback from your supervisor.
* **Additional Support Needed** (`additional_support_needed`, text, optional): Indicate any additional support needed from management.
* **Career Aspirations** (`career_aspirations`, text, optional): Describe your career goals and aspirations.
* **Additional Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, optional): Add any other thoughts or feedback.
