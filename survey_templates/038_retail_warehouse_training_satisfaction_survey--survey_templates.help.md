# Retail Warehouse Training Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from retail warehouse trainers on their training experiences and the effectiveness of the training.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing your name, email, and any other required information.
2. Rate your experience with the trainers using the provided scale.
3. Provide feedback on your satisfaction and suggestions for improvement.
4. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your name, as it appears on your badge or ID.
* **Last Name**: Your last name, as it appears on your badge or ID.
* **Email**: The email address associated with your company ID or badge.
* **Store ID**: Your store ID, which may be printed on your badge or ID.
* **Trainer Name**: The name of the trainer who conducted your training.
* **Training Date**: The date of your training session.
* **Trainer Rating**: Rate the effectiveness of the trainer, using the options provided (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Training Quality**: Rate the quality of the training, using the options provided (Good, Average, Poor).
* **Safety Coverage**: If you have any safety concerns or issues, please describe them in this field.
* **Trainer Effectiveness**: Describe how effective you feel the trainer was in providing the necessary training and knowledge.
* **Training Safety Concerns**: If you have any safety concerns, please describe them in this field.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the training, using the options provided (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor).
* **Comments**: Any comments or suggestions you have about the training or trainers.
* **Suggestions**: Any suggestions you have for improving the training or trainers.
* **Store ID**: Your store ID, which may be printed on your badge or ID.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Use the provided options to rate your experience.
* Be honest and provide any feedback or suggestions for improvement.
* Submit the form once completed.
