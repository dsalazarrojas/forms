# Time And Attendance Vendor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate a time and attendance vendor's performance. It assesses the vendor's services, including vendor information, rating, timekeeping, time sheets, payroll integration, reporting, support, billing, implementation, and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the vendor's information and provide a rating for their overall performance.
2. Answer questions about their timekeeping and time sheets features.
3. Determine if their payroll integration and reporting features meet your needs.
4. Evaluate their support and communication.
5. Provide feedback on their implementation and billing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Vendor Information** (`Vendor Information`, `text`, required: false)
* This is the section where you can provide any additional information about the vendor.

### 2. **Rate Vendor** (`Rate Vendor`, `number`, required: false)
* Rate the vendor's overall performance on a scale from 1 to 10.

### 3. **Timekeeping** (`Timekeeping`, `text`, required: false)
* Describe the vendor's timekeeping features and their effectiveness.

### 4. **Time Sheets** (`Time Sheets`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Choose how the vendor's time sheets meet your needs:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 5. **Payroll Integration** (`Payroll Integration`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
* Determine if the vendor's payroll integration features meet your needs:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 6. **Reporting** (`Reporting`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Choose how the vendor's reporting features meet your needs:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 7. **Support** (`Support`, `text`, required: false)
* Provide feedback on the vendor's support.

### 8. **Billing** (`Billing`, `number`, required: false)
* Rate the vendor's billing practices.

### 9. **Implementation** (`Implementation`, `time`, required: false)
* Provide the date and time of the vendor's implementation.

### 10. **Communication** (`Communication`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
* Determine if the vendor's communication features meet your needs:
	+ Yes
	+ No
