# Customer Survey Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our business or service. It will help us gather valuable insights to improve our services and enhance customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form title to briefly describe the purpose of your inquiry.
2. Provide a short description of your inquiry, so we can better understand the context.
3. Select the category for your survey from our pre-defined options or choose "Other" if your survey doesn't fit into any category.
4. If you have selected "Other" in the previous step, please provide a description of what your survey is about.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
6. If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you directly.
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Survey Inquiry Form** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the purpose of your inquiry.
* **The Customer Survey Inquiry Form helps gather feedback from clients** (`form_description`, text, required: false): Provide a short description of your inquiry, so we can better understand the context.
* **Survey Templates** (`form_category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category for your survey from our pre-defined options or choose "Other" if your survey doesn't fit into any category.
* **Other** (`form_category_other`, text, required: false): If you have selected "Other" in the previous step, please provide a description of what your survey is about.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, required: false): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you directly.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, text, required: false): You can also use this field to provide additional comments or feedback about your survey.
