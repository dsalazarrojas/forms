# Graduation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Survey is designed to collect feedback and information from graduates of our institution. This data will help us understand your experience and plans after graduation, and how we can improve our services and programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions (indicated by **).
4. If you have any additional comments, feel free to add them in the "Any Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your experience at our school like?** (`graduation_experience`, text): This question is asking about your overall experience at our institution. Please provide a brief description of your experience, including any positive or negative aspects.
* **Post-graduation plans** (`graduation_plans`, select_one): This question is asking about your immediate plans after graduation. Please select one of the options provided, or type in "Other" and provide a brief description of your plans.
* **Satisfaction with your academic program** (`satisfaction_level`, number): This question is asking about your level of satisfaction with your academic program. Please select a number from 1-5 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Email address** (`email`, email): This question is asking for your email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text): This question is asking for your phone number. Please enter your phone number, including any necessary country code.
* **Name of your academic program** (`name_of_program`, text): This question is asking about the name of the program you graduated from. If unsure, please don't worry and just enter "Unknown" or ask for help.
* **How many years did you attend the program?** (`years_attended`, number): This question is asking about how many years you attended our institution. Please select the correct number.
* **Graduation date** (`graduation_date`, date): This question is asking for your graduation date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of day** (`time_of_day`, time): This question is asking about the time of day you graduated. Please select the correct time.
* **Any additional comments** (`comments`, note): This question is asking for any additional comments or feedback you may have. Please use this field to share any further information that may be helpful to us.
