# Healthcare Patient Visit Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their visit to the hospital. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the overall rating of the patient's visit, the ratings of the doctor and staff, and to collect any recommendations or suggestions the patient may have.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the relevant answers from the provided options.
3. Enter any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Enter the required fields accurately and completely.
5. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Overall Patient Rating** (`patient_rating`, `number`, required)
Enter your overall rating of the visit as a number from 1-5.

### 2. **Doctor** (`doctor_rating`, `number`, required)
Rate your experience with the doctor as a number from 1-5.

### 3. **Staff** (`staff_rating`, `number`, required)
Rate your experience with the staff as a number from 1-5.

### 4. **Visit Experience** (`visit_experience`, `text`, required)
Enter a brief description of your visit experience.

### 5. **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, `number`, optional)
Enter the duration of your appointment.

### 6. **Communication** (`communication`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all that apply from the provided options regarding your communication experience.

### 7. **Do you have any recommendations or suggestions** (`recommendations`, `note`, required)
Enter any recommendations or suggestions you have for future improvements.

### 8. **Will you require followup treatment** (`followup`, `select_one`, required)
Select "True" if you will require followup treatment, "False" if not, and "Maybe" if unsure.

### 9. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional)
Enter any additional comments or suggestions.

### 10. **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required)
Enter the date of your visit.

### 11. **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, required)
Enter the time of your visit.

### 12. **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required)
Enter the name of your doctor.

### 13. **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required)
Enter your medical condition.

### 14. **Patient's Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required)
Enter your name.

### 15. **Patient's Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, required)
Enter your email.

### 16. **Patient's Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required)
Enter your phone number.

### 17. **Hospital's Name** (`hospital_name`, `text`, required)
Enter the name of the hospital.

### 18. **Department** (`department`, `text`, required)
Enter the department you visited.

### 19. **Date** (`date`, `date`, required)
Enter the date of the form, please note this field is used for form submission time stamp.

### 20. **Time** (`time`, `time`, required)
Enter the time of the form submission.

### 21. **Type of Visit** (`visit_type`, `select_one`, required)
Select the type of visit.

### 22. **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `text`, required)
Enter your medical record number.

### 23. **Doctor's Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, `text`, required)
Enter your doctor's specialty.

### 24. **Staff Rating** (`staff_rating`, `number`, optional)
Rate your experience with the staff as a number from 1-5.

### 25. **Staff's Name** (`staff_name`, `text`, required)
Enter the name of the staff.

Please note that this form is intended to be filled out by patients, and the purpose is to collect feedback about their visit to the hospital. Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
