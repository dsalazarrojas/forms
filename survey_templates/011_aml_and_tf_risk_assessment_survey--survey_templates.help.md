# AML And TF Risk Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an organization's Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Terrorist Financing (TF) risks. Please complete this form to provide a comprehensive overview of your organization's risk exposure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Organization Name**, the official name of your organization.
2. Enter the **Assessment Date**, the date when the risk assessment is conducted.
3. Provide your **Assessor Name**, the name of the person conducting the assessment.
4. Select your organization's **Business Sector**, which is the industry or sector your organization operates in.
5. Choose the type of **Customer Base Profile** your organization serves, which can be individuals, businesses, high net worth individuals, international customers, or politically exposed persons.
6. Select the **Geographic Exposure**, which represents the countries or regions where your organization operates.
7. Enter your organization's **Annual Transaction Volume**, which is the estimated annual volume of transactions processed.
8. Choose the **Percentage of Cash Transactions**, which represents the percentage of transactions involving cash.
9. Answer whether your organization has **Correspondent Banking Relationships** with foreign banks.
10. Confirm if your organization offers **Private Banking Services**.
11. Indicate if your organization is involved in **Trade Finance Activities**.
12. Answer whether your organization has customers who are **Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)**.
13. Confirm if your organization has in place **Enhanced Due Diligence Procedures** for high-risk clients.
14. Answer whether your organization has a formal **Suspicious Activity Reporting Process**.
15. Confirm if your organization has a regular **Staff Training Program** on AML/TF risks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): The official name of your organization. Please fill in the exact name as it is registered.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): The date when the risk assessment is conducted. Enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): The name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Business Sector** (`business_sector`, select_one, required): The industry or sector your organization operates in. Choose from the following options:
	+ Banking
	+ Insurance
	+ Securities
	+ Real Estate
	+ Money Services
	+ Other
* **Customer Base Profile** (`customer_base_profile`, select_one, required): What type of customers does your organization serve? Choose from the following options:
	+ Individuals
	+ Businesses
	+ High Net Worth
	+ International
	+ Politically Exposed Persons
	+ Cash Intensive Businesses
* **Geographic Exposure** (`geographic_exposure`, select_multiple, required): Countries or regions where your organization operates. You can choose multiple options.
	+ Domestic Only
	+ Developed Markets
	+ Emerging Markets
	+ High Risk Jurisdictions
	+ Sanctioned Countries
* **Annual Transaction Volume** (`transaction_volume`, select_one, required): Estimated annual volume of transactions processed. Choose from the following options:
	+ Low (under $1M)
	+ Medium ($1M-$10M)
	+ High ($10M-$100M)
	+ Very High (over $100M)
* **Percentage of Cash Transactions** (`cash_transaction_percentage`, number, required): What percentage of transactions involve cash? Enter a numerical value.
* **Correspondent Banking Relationships** (`correspondent_banking`, select_one, required): Does your organization have relationships with foreign banks? Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Private Banking Services** (`private_banking_services`, select_one, required): Does your organization offer private banking services? Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Trade Finance Activities** (`trade_finance_activities`, select_one, required): Is your organization involved in trade finance? Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)** (`politically_exposed_persons`, select_one, required): Does your organization have clients who are PEPs? Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Enhanced Due Diligence Procedures** (`enhanced_due_diligence`, select_one, required): Are enhanced due diligence procedures in place for high-risk clients? Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Partial
* **Suspicious Activity Reporting Process** (`suspicious_activity_reporting`, select_one, required): Is there a formal process for reporting suspicious activities? Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ In Development
* **Staff Training Program** (`staff_training_program`, select_one, required): Is there a regular training program on AML/TF risks? Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Annual Only

## Tips

- Please answer all required questions to ensure a complete and accurate assessment.
- Review your answers carefully to ensure they are accurate and reflect your organization's current risk exposure.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact your risk management team or refer to your organization's risk management policies.
