# Data Process Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users on their experience with data process training programs. It aims to understand what types of training programs are effective and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have participated in data process training programs.
2. Select the training programs you have participated in on "What Training Programs Have You Participated In".
3. Rate your experience with the program on "How Would You Rate This Training Program".
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback on "Additional Comments".
5. Finally, indicate whether you would recommend the program on "Would You Recommend This Program".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Survey** (`title`, `note`, required: false): This section is a note that provides a brief overview of the form.
* **What Training Programs Have You Participated In** (`training_programs`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the training programs that you have participated in. You can choose multiple programs by holding down the Ctrl key while selecting.
* **How Would You Rate This Training Program** (`training_survey_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience with the training program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Comments** (`training_survey_comment`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the training program.
* **Would You Recommend This Program** (`training_survey_recommendation`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you would strongly agree, somewhat agree, neither, somewhat disagree, or strongly disagree with the program.
