# pilot_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from pilots on their flight experiences, safety perception, communication quality, and other related aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. Submit your feedback to help us improve.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **flight experience** (`flight_experience`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options that best describes your flight experience.
- **safety_perception** (`safety_perception`, text, required/optional): Describe your perception of safety during the flight.
- **communication_quality** (`communication_quality`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the options that best describe the communication quality you experienced.

- **improvement_opportunities** (`improvement_opportunities`, text, required/optional): Describe any areas for improvement you've identified during the flight.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
- Use clear and concise language.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask your team or supervisor for guidance.
