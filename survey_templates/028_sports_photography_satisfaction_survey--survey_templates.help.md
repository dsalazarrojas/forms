# sports_photography_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help customers complete the sports photography satisfaction survey.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the overall quality of the service by choosing from the options below.
2. Rate the professionalism of the photographer by selecting from the options provided.
3. Evaluate the communication of the service by selecting from the options below.
4. Select the delivery time of the service, whether it was fast, average, slow, or very slow.
5. Choose whether you felt the service was overpriced, priced right, or underpriced.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service by selecting from the options provided.
7. If you have any comments about your experience, please provide them in the text area below.
8. If you have any additional comments, please add them here.
9. Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
10. Enter the date and time of your photo shoot.
11. Select the duration of the photo shoot.
12. Enter the name of the photographer who shot your photos.
13. Select the type of photo shoot you had (e.g. wedding, event, commercial).
14. Enter the number of photos taken during the shoot.
15. Enter the price range of the photo shoot.
16. Rate your satisfaction with the service on a scale from 1 to 10.
17. Enter any review text about your experience.
18. Rate your satisfaction with the service on a scale from 1 to 10 for different time ranges.
19. Rate your satisfaction with the service on a scale from 1 to 10 for different price ranges.
20. Select whether you have submitted your review.
21. Select the options below to confirm your completion of this form.

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service, whether it was satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or dissatisfied.
* **Comments** (`7`, `note`, required: false): If you have any comments about your experience, please add them here.
* **Additional Comments** (`8`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments, add them here.
