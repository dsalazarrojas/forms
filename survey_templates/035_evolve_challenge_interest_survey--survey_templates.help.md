# Evolve Challenge Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evolve Challenge Interest Survey is a form designed to gauge the interest and preferences of potential participants in a challenge program. The survey aims to gather information on the participant's demographics, communication preferences, and challenge-related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.
4. Indicate your **Availability** for participating in challenges.
5. Select your preferred **Challenge Frequency** (e.g., how often you'd like to participate in challenges).
6. Choose your preferred **Challenge Duration** (e.g., how long you'd like each challenge to last).
7. For **Communication Preferences**, select all the channels you would prefer to be contacted through for updates and information about challenges (e.g., In-person, Phone, Email, Social Media).
8. Finally, please confirm your **Participant Status** (e.g., are you interested in participating or already a participant in the challenge program).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like it to be known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: true): Enter your last name as you would like it to be known.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required: false): Indicate your availability for participating in challenges (e.g., full-time, part-time, flexible).
* **Challenge Frequency** (`challenge_frequency`, number, required: false): Select how often you'd like to participate in challenges (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
* **Challenge Duration** (`challenge_duration`, number, required: false): Select how long you'd like each challenge to last (e.g., hours, days, weeks).
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the communication channels you prefer (e.g., In-person, Phone, Email, Social Media).
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, text, required: true): Confirm your participant status (e.g., interested, already participating, other).
