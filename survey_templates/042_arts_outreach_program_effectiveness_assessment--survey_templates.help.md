# Arts Outreach Program Effectiveness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience with arts outreach programs. The information collected will help program organizers and facilitators evaluate the effectiveness of their programs and make improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Select the correct program date and location from your experience.
3. Answer the questions about your experience with the program, rating the effectiveness of the program, the quality of the instructors, and more.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the program.
5. Finally, indicate your likelihood of recommending the program to others and your interest in participating in future programs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you participated in.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Program Date**: Select the date you attended the program.
* **Program Location**: Enter the location where the program took place.
* **First Time Attending This Program**: Indicate whether this was your first time attending the program.
* **Overall Program Effectiveness - Scale 1-10**: Rate the overall effectiveness of the program on a scale of 1-10.
* **Quality of Instructors or Facilitators**: Rate the quality of the instructors or facilitators of the program.
* **Content Relevance and Usefulness**: Rate the content of the program, how relevant and useful it was for you.
* **Learning Environment Quality**: Rate the quality of the learning environment, including space and materials.
* **Participant Engagement Level**: Indicate your level of engagement with the program, on a scale of very engaged to not engaged.
* **Skills or Knowledge Acquired**: If applicable, describe any skills or knowledge you acquired from the program.
* **Impact on Personal or Creative Growth**: Describe any impact the program had on your personal or creative growth.
* **Increase in Arts Appreciation**: Indicate if you experienced an increase, decrease, or no change in your appreciation for arts.
* **Sense of Community or Belonging**: Indicate whether you felt a strong, moderate, weak, or no sense of community or belonging.
* **Suggested Improvements**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving the program.
* **Likelihood to Recommend to Others**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend the program to others.
* **Interest in Future Program Participation**: Indicate your interest in participating in future programs.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the program.
