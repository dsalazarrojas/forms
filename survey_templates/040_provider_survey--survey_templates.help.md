# Provider Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The provider survey form is a tool used to collect and record information about providers for various purposes. This form helps to streamline the collection of provider data, ensuring consistency and reducing errors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the provider's name in the "provider_name" field.
2. Enter the provider's address in the "address" field.
3. Enter the provider's email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter the provider's phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter the provider's license number in the "license" field.
6. Select the provider's specialties from the "specialties" field. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown list.
7. Select the languages the provider speaks from the "languages" field. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown list.
8. If the provider speaks languages not listed, enter them in the "other_languages" field.
9. Add any additional notes about the provider in the "notes" field.
10. Enter the provider's experience (e.g., years of experience) in the "experience" field.
11. Click "Submit" to complete the form and submit the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **provider_name** (required: false): Enter the name of the provider, e.g., "John Doe".
* **address** (required: false): Enter the address of the provider.
* **email** (required: false): Enter the email address of the provider.
* **phone** (required: false): Enter the phone number of the provider.
* **license** (required: false): Enter the provider's license number.
* **specialties** (required: false): Select the specialties of the provider. Options: Yes, No.
* **languages** (required: false): Select the languages the provider speaks. Options: Yes, No.
* **other_languages** (required: false): If the provider speaks languages not listed, enter them here.
* **notes** (required: false): Add any additional notes about the provider.
* **experience** (required: false): Enter the provider's experience (e.g., years of experience).
* **provider_notes** (required: false): This field is for additional notes, but its functionality is not explicitly defined in the form schema. It may be intended for free-form comments or additional information.
