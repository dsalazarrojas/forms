# Mathematics Education Philosophy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from math educators about their educational philosophy, teaching approaches, and practices to better understand the diversity of math education and inform the development of better educational resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Read each label carefully and make sure you understand what it means before providing your input.
3. If you have any doubts or questions, contact us for clarification.
4. The form is not a comprehensive or exhaustive assessment, but rather a brief snapshot of your current perspective.
