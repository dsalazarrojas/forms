# Coaching Progress Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Progress Check In form is designed to help coaches and clients track progress, identify areas for improvement, and set goals for the next meeting. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form only if you have had a meeting or session with your coach.
2. Enter your name and email address to identify yourself as a client.
3. Provide the date of your last meeting or session.
4. Answer the questions regarding your progress, goals, obstacles, and focus for the next period.
5. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible to help your coach understand your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as a client.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address that your coach can use to contact you.
- **Check-In Date** (`check_in_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of this check-in.
- **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required): Enter your coach's name.
- **Weeks Since Last Session** (`weeks_since_last_session`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of weeks since your last session with the coach.
- **Overall Progress Since Last Session** (`overall_progress`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall progress since the last session.
- **Primary Goal Status** (`goal_1_status`, `select_one`, required): Rate the progress on your main goal.
- **Primary Goal Details** (`goal_1_details`, `text`, required): Describe your progress or challenges on your main goal.
- **Secondary Goal Status** (`goal_2_status`, `select_one`, required): Rate the progress on your second goal (if applicable).
- **Secondary Goal Details** (`goal_2_details`, `text`, optional): Describe your progress or challenges on your second goal (if applicable).
- **Actions Completed Since Last Session** (`actions_completed`, `text`, required): List the actions you have taken since the last session.
- **Obstacles Faced** (`obstacles_faced`, `text`, optional): List any obstacles or challenges you have faced.
- **How Did You Overcome** (`how_did_you_overcome`, `text`, optional): Describe how you overcame obstacles.
- **Current Motivation Level** (`current_motivation_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate your current motivation level.
- **Current Confidence Level** (`current_confidence_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate your current confidence level.
- **Current Stress Level** (`current_stress_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate your current stress level.
- **Wins and Celebrations** (`wins_and_celebrations`, `text`, required): List your successes and achievements.
- **Lessons Learned** (`lessons_learned`, `text`, optional): Describe any lessons learned from the session.
- **Support Needed** (`support_needed`, `text`, optional): Specify any support you need from your coach or others.
- **Focus for Next Period** (`focus_for_next_period`, `text`, required): Describe your focus and goals for the next meeting or period.
- **New Goals** (`new_goals`, `text`, optional): Add any new objectives you want to set.
- **Goals to Remove or Modify** (`goals_to_remove`, `text`, optional): Specify any goals you want to remove or modify.
- **Accountability Request** (`accountability_request`, `text`, optional): Specify areas where you need accountability from your coach.
- **Preference for Next Session** (`session_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you want to approach the next session with your coach.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or information.
 
Note: Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible when filling out the form. Your input will help your coach understand your progress and provide better guidance for the next meeting. 

Tips:
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask your coach for clarification.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your coach directly.
- Remember, this form is a tool to help you and your coach work together towards your goals.
