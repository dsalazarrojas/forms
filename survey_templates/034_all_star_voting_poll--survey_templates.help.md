# All Star Voting Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from fans who want to vote for their favorite all-star player in a specific sport. It's a fun and engaging way for fans to participate in the voting process and share their opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. Select your top three choices in the voting section.
3. Answer the fan information section questions to the best of your ability.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name for verification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`2`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for verification and to receive updates about the poll.
* **Fan ID** (`3`, `text`, optional): If you have a fan ID, enter it here.
* **Sport** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Select the sport you are voting in.
* **Voting Category** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category you are voting for.
* **First Choice** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your top pick.
* **First Choice Team** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your top pick's team.
* **Second Choice** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your runner-up pick.
* **Second Choice Team** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your runner-up pick's team.
* **Third Choice** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your third pick.
* **Third Choice Team** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your third pick's team.
* **Why Did You Choose This Player?** (`12`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation of why you chose your top pick.
* **Games Attended This Season** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): Select the number of games you have attended this season.
* **Fan Since** (`14`, `select_one`, optional): Select how long you have been a fan.
* **Favorite Team** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your favorite team.
* **Follow Other Sports?** (`16`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the sports you follow besides your favorite one.
* **Social Media Handle** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter your Twitter/Instagram handle for features and promotions.
* **Age Group** (`18`, `select_one`, optional): Select your age range.
* **Location** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter your city/state.
* **Championship Prediction** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter who you think will win the championship.
* **Planning to Attend All-Star Game?** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Select if you plan to attend the all-star game.
* **Additional Comments** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter any other thoughts or comments.

Tips:
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting your vote.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact us for assistance.
* Have fun and enjoy the voting process!
