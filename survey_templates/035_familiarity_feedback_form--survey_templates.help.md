# Familiarity Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Familiarity Feedback Form is designed to collect information from users about their familiarity with a service or product. This form is intended to be completed by users who have had some level of exposure to the service or product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "user_familiarity" to indicate your level of familiarity with the service or product.
2. Rate your familiarity with "rating".
3. Share any suggestions for improvement with "suggestion".
4. Provide feedback on your experience with "feedback".
5. Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
6. Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" for "service_familiarity" to indicate if you have used the service or not.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for "service_familiarity_2" to indicate if you would like to receive additional information about the service.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" for "product_familiarity" to indicate if you have used the product or not.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" for "product_familiarity_2" to indicate if you would like to receive additional information about the product.
11. Enter the date and time you last used the service or product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Familiarity** (user_familiarity): A measure of how well you know the service or product. Please select from a scale of completely unfamiliar to very familiar.
* **Rating** (rating): Rate your familiarity with the service or product.
* **Suggestion** (suggestion): Provide any comments or suggestions on how to improve the service or product.
* **Feedback** (feedback): Share your experience and feedback on the service or product.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Service Familiarity** (service_familiarity): Select "Yes" if you have used the service or not.
* **Service Familiarity 2** (service_familiarity_2): Select "Yes" if you would like to receive additional information about the service.
* **Product Familiarity** (product_familiarity): Select "Yes" if you have used the product or not.
* **Product Familiarity 2** (product_familiarity_2): Select "Yes" if you would like to receive additional information about the product.
* **Date Familiarity** (date_familiarity): Enter the date and time you last used the service or product.
* **Time Familiarity** (time_familiarity): Enter the time you last used the service or product.

## Tips
* Please provide honest and accurate feedback to help us improve the service or product.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
* Duplicate responses will not be accepted.
