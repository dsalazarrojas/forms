# Customer Satisfaction Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Satisfaction Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess how well-versed our customers are with our knowledge base. This form will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better services to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the principles that you think are essential for customer satisfaction.
2. Choose the correct answer to the question.
3. Enter your score out of 10 for the knowledge base.
4. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Notes" field.
5. Enter your email address and phone number.
6. Enter your first and last name.
7. If you wish, enter additional feedback on customer satisfaction principles, knowledge, and scores.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_satisfaction_principles** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all the principles that you think are essential for customer satisfaction.
* **correct_answer** (`select_one`, required): Choose the correct answer to the question.
* **score** (`number`, required): Enter your score out of 10 for the knowledge base.
* **notes** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Notes" field.
* **email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **first_name** (`text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **customer_satisfaction_score** (`number`, optional): Enter additional feedback on customer satisfaction score.
* **score_out_of_ten** (`number`, optional): Enter additional feedback on score out of 10.
* **customer_satisfaction_principles_list** (`text`, optional): Enter additional feedback on customer satisfaction principles.
* **customer_satisfaction_knowledge** (`text`, optional): Enter additional feedback on customer satisfaction knowledge.
* **customer_satisfaction_knowledge_score** (`number`, optional): Enter additional feedback on customer satisfaction knowledge score.
* **customer_satisfaction_knowledge_score_out_of_ten** (`number`, optional): Enter additional feedback on customer satisfaction knowledge score out of 10.
* **assigned_tool** (`text`, required): Enter the tool assigned to you for the quiz.

Please note that the optional fields are for additional feedback, and you can skip them if you don't have anything to add.
