# Typing Lesson Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with typing lessons. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and provide better learning experiences for you and others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the answer that best reflects your experience.
2. Fill out each section as instructed.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro Page**: This is an introductory page that provides background information about the form.
* **Lesson Feedback**: Choose how satisfied you were with the lessons. 
	+ Very Satisfied: You were extremely pleased with the lessons.
	+ Satisfied: You were fairly pleased with the lessons.
	+ Not Satisfied: You were not pleased with the lessons.
	+ Very Not Satisfied: You were not pleased at all with the lessons.
* **Lesson Effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of the lessons.
	+ Very Effective: The lessons were highly effective.
	+ Somewhat Effective: The lessons were somewhat effective.
	+ Neither Effective Nor Ineffective: The lessons had mixed effectiveness.
	+ Somewhat Ineffective: The lessons were somewhat ineffective.
	+ Very Ineffective: The lessons were highly ineffective.
* **Difficulty Level**: Rate the difficulty level of the lessons.
	+ Very Easy: The lessons were too easy.
	+ Somewhat Easy: The lessons were fairly easy.
	+ Neither Easy Nor Hard: The lessons had a mixed difficulty level.
	+ Somewhat Hard: The lessons were somewhat challenging.
	+ Very Hard: The lessons were very challenging.
* **Instructor Quality**: Rate the quality of the instructors.
	+ Very Good: The instructors were highly skilled.
	+ Good: The instructors were fairly skilled.
	+ Neither Good Nor Bad: The instructors had a mixed level of skill.
	+ Bad: The instructors were not skilled.
	+ Very Bad: The instructors were very unskilled.
* **Lesson Materials**: Choose whether you had all the materials you needed.
	+ Yes: You had all the materials you needed.
	+ No: You did not have all the materials you needed.
* **Lesson Length**: Choose the ideal length of the lessons.
	+ Just Right: The lessons were the right length.
	+ Too Long: The lessons were too long.
	+ Too Short: The lessons were too short.
* **Any Suggestions or Comments**: Share any suggestions or comments you have about the lessons.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for feedback).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted for feedback).
* **Name**: Enter your name (if you want to be acknowledged for your feedback).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments you have about the lessons.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your feedback.

Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and your feedback will be greatly appreciated!
