# Sleep Medicine Follow Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be filled out by healthcare professionals to gather information on a patient's sleep medicine needs. It is used to assess and document the patient's sleep difficulty, symptoms, diagnosis, and treatment progress. The information collected through this form will be used to guide further medical interventions and ensure the best possible care for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form accurately and honestly, providing the most up-to-date information on the patient's condition.
2. Use the provided fields to input relevant information about the patient's sleep medicine needs.
3. Make sure to answer all questions marked as "required", as they are essential for the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Patient Information

* **Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Sleep Difficulty** (`sleep_difficulty`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Mild
	+ Moderate
	+ Severe
* **Sleep Symptoms** (`sleep_symptoms`, text, required): Describe the patient's sleep symptoms in detail.
* **Sleep Diagnosis** (`sleep_diagnosis`, text, required): Provide the patient's sleep diagnosis or condition.
* **Treatment Progress** (`treatment_progress`, text, required): Document the patient's current treatment progress.

### Page 2: Treatment Evaluation

* **Treatment Effectiveness** (`treatment_effectiveness`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Effective
	+ Ineffective
	+ Neutral
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the patient's treatment or condition.

Note: If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please consult with the healthcare professional managing your care.
