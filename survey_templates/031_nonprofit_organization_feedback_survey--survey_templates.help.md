# nonprofit_organization_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from supporters of nonprofit organizations. It aims to collect information about the supporter's experience, feedback, and demographics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. You can choose only one answer for questions marked as "select_one", and select multiple answers for questions marked as "select_multiple".
3. For any required fields, please provide a valid answer.
4. You can leave any field blank if you're unsure or do not have an answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Survey** (`text`, optional): A short description about your feedback.
* **Program Areas** (`select_one`, optional): Choose one area of the nonprofit organization's programs that you've interacted with: Community Programs, Community Resources, Community Services, or Other.
* **Supporter Feedback** (`text`, optional): Share any specific feedback about your experience with the nonprofit organization.
* **Supporter Demographics** (`note`, optional): Share your contact information or any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Supporter Engagement** (`select_one`, optional): Rate your level of engagement with the nonprofit organization: High, Low, or Medium.
* **Communication Methods** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose the methods through which you'd like to receive communication from the nonprofit organization: Email, Phone, Text, or Mail.
* **Feedback Comments** (`text`, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide.
