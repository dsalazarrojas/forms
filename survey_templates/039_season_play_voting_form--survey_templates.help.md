# Season Play Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Season Play Voting Form aims to collect feedback from users on players, teams, and coaches within a season. This form is designed to gather information that can be used for evaluation, analysis, and improvement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the Introduction section.
2. Fill in the Player Name field with the name of the player you are voting for.
3. Enter the Team Name field with the team you are voting for.
4. Select the category/categories for the player, team, or coach you want to vote for in the Vote For section.
5. Enter the Season Start Date and Season End Date fields with the actual dates of the season.
6. Optionally, add any comments or feedback in the Comments field.
7. Finally, click the Submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the season and its purpose.
* **Player Name** (required): Enter the name of the player you are voting for. This field is required as it helps us understand your opinion on a specific individual.
* **Team Name** (optional): Enter the name of the team you are voting for. While not required, this field provides additional context to your feedback.
* **Vote For** (required): Select one or more categories from Player, Team, or Coach to cast your vote for. This field allows you to express your opinion on multiple aspects of the season.
* **Season Start Date** (required): Enter the start date of the season. This field ensures we have a clear understanding of the season's timeline.
* **Season End Date** (required): Enter the end date of the season. Similar to the Season Start Date, this field is vital for context.
* **Comments** (optional): Add any additional feedback or comments about the season or your experience. While not required, this field helps us understand your perspective in more detail.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your feedback. Please ensure you've filled out all required fields before submitting.
