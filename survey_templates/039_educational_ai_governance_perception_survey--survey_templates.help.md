# Educational AI Governance Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights from educational institutions on their current and future perceptions, approaches, and concerns regarding AI governance. The purpose of this survey is to help institutions understand their strengths, weaknesses, and areas of improvement in AI governance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible for each field.
2. Select all relevant options that apply to your institution.
3. Fill in all required fields, even if it may require some extra effort.
4. Review your answers and make sure they accurately reflect your institution's current and future plans regarding AI governance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educational AI Governance Perception Survey**: This is a note field that introduces the purpose and scope of the survey.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your institution's primary focus area (AI Governance, Data Management, or Digital Transformation).
* **What is your perception of AI governance in your institution?**: Please describe your current understanding or perception of AI governance in your institution.
* **What is your institution's approach to AI governance?**: Describe your institution's current approach or strategy for AI governance.
* **Which department is primarily responsible for AI governance within your institution?**: Select all departments that are involved in AI governance (IT, Research and Development, Academic Affairs, or Other).
* **How do you currently use AI in your institution?**: Select all methods you currently use AI for (Decision Making, Data Analysis, Automating Tasks, or Other).
* **How do you envision AI being used in your institution in the future?**: Select all methods you foresee AI being used for (Decision Making, Data Analysis, Automating Tasks, or Other).
* **What is your biggest concern about AI governance in your institution?**: Select all concerns you have regarding AI governance (Bias, Transparency, Regulatory Compliance, or Other).
* **How do you rate your institution's AI governance?**: Rate your institution's current AI governance performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Bad).
* **How do you rate your institution's AI governance in terms of transparency?**: Rate your institution's transparency in AI governance (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Bad).
* **What role do you think AI will play in your institution's future?**: Select all roles you see AI playing in your institution's future (Strategic Decision Making, Data Analysis, Automating Tasks, or Other).

## Tips
- Answer all fields accurately and truthfully to help your institution gain a better understanding of their AI governance.
- Be as detailed as possible for each field, but avoid unnecessary information.
- If you're unsure about any question, please select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
- This survey is for internal use only, and your institution's responses will help them improve their AI governance.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
