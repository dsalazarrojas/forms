# faculty_hiring_decision_voting_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the collective evaluation and decision-making process for faculty hiring decisions within an organization. The form collects feedback and ratings from committee members, enabling them to weigh in on the qualifications and suitability of candidates for faculty positions. 

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form by providing your name and position title.
2. Evaluate each candidate by selecting from the available options or providing a rating.
3. Indicate for whom you are voting by selecting from the available options.
4. Provide additional comments or ratings as needed.
5. Select your committee membership and position.
6. Choose the status of the candidate.
7. Finally, provide a rating of your committee members and indicate their relevant experience.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the candidate being evaluated.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, optional): Enter the position title for which the candidate is being evaluated.
* **Candidate Evaluation** (`candidate_evaluation`, text, optional): Provide a text-based evaluation or comments on the candidate's qualifications.
* **Vote For** (`vote_for`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable candidates you are voting for.
* **Candidate Rating** (`candidate_rating`, number, optional): Rate the candidate's qualifications on a scale of 1-5.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Record the time of the evaluation.
* **Committee Member** (`committee_member`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable committee members.
* **Committee Position** (`committee_position`, select_one, optional): Select the position on the committee you are voting as.
* **Candidate Status** (`candidate_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the candidate.
* **Committee Rating** (`committee_rating`, number, optional): Rate the committee member's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Candidate Relevant Experience** (`candidate_relevant_experience`, select_one, optional): Select the relevant experience of the candidate.
