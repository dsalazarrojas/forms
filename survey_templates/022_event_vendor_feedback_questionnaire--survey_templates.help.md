# event_vendor_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended for event vendors to provide feedback about their experience with events they have participated in. The feedback will help the event organizers to improve their services and create a better experience for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the event information:
   a. event_date: Select the date of the event you are providing feedback about.
   b. event_time: Select the time of the event you are providing feedback about.
   c. event_venue: Enter the venue name or location of the event.
2. Enter vendor details:
   a. vendor_name: Enter the name of the vendor you are providing feedback about.
   b. vendor_email: Enter the email address of the vendor.
   c. vendor_phone: Enter the phone number of the vendor.
3. Provide feedback and ratings:
   a. rating: Select your rating of the event (1-5).
   b. feedback: Enter your comments about the event.
   c. comments: Enter any additional comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### event_date (Required)
* **Event Date (event_date)**: Select the date of the event you are providing feedback about.

### event_time (Required)
* **Event Time (event_time)**: Select the time of the event you are providing feedback about.

### event_venue (Required)
* **Event Venue (event_venue)**: Enter the name or location of the venue where the event took place.

### vendor_name (Required)
* **Vendor Name (vendor_name)**: Enter the name of the vendor you are providing feedback about.

### vendor_email (Required)
* **Vendor Email (vendor_email)**: Enter the email address of the vendor.

### vendor_phone (Required)
* **Vendor Phone Number (vendor_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the vendor.

### feedback (Required)
* **Feedback (feedback)**: Enter your comments and feedback about the event.

### rating (Required)
* **Rating (rating)**: Select your rating of the event (1-5).

### comments (Required)
* **Additional Comments (comments)**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information to help the event organizers improve their services.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your event management team.
