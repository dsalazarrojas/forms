# Mobile App Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with our mobile app. It will help us understand how you think the app performed and what we can do to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
2. For the rating section, select the option that best represents your experience.
3. In the feedback section, describe your experience with the app in as much detail as possible.
4. If you have any suggestions for improvements, please provide them in the suggestion field.
5. Enter your name and email address (or phone number) so we can contact you if we need more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **app_name (Text)**: Please enter the name of the mobile app you are providing feedback about.
* **feedback (Text)**: Describe your experience with the app, including what you liked and disliked about it.
* **rating (Select One)**: Select the rating that best represents your experience with the app.
	+ Option 1: I was satisfied with the app.
	+ Option 2: I was neutral about the app.
	+ Option 3: I was dissatisfied with the app.
	+ Option 4: I would not recommend the app.
	+ Option 5: I have not used the app.
* **email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number or any other contact information you would like to provide.
* **suggestion (Text)**: If you have any suggestions for improvements, please provide them in this field.
* **submission_page (Date)**: You can leave this field blank if not applicable.
* **time (Time)**: Enter the time of submission.
* **name (Text)**: Enter your name so we can address our response to you.
* **email_2 (Email)**: You can leave this field blank if you don't want to provide a secondary email address.
* **phone_2 (Text)**: You can leave this field blank if you don't want to provide a secondary phone number.
* **suggestion_2 (Text)**: You can leave this field blank if you don't have any additional suggestions.
* **submission_page_2 (Date)**: You can leave this field blank if not applicable.
* **time_2 (Time)**: You can leave this field blank if not applicable.
