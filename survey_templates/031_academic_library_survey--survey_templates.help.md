# Academic Library Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Library Survey is a tool used by the library to understand your opinions and experiences with our services and resources. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve and tailor our services to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, select the correct answer for each question based on your experience and preferences.
- Make sure to answer all required questions (marked with an asterisk *).
- For optional questions, provide as much information as you think is relevant.
- Once you've completed all questions, review your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Profile** (`section_user_profile`, `note`): This section is a brief introduction to your user profile. Please provide a short description of yourself (Student, Faculty, Staff, or Visitor) and your relationship with the library (e.g., regular user, occasional user, etc.).
* **User Role** (`user_role`, * `select_one`): Please select the category that best describes your role at the academic institution (Student, Faculty, Staff, or Visitor).
* **Frequency of Library Use** (`frequency_of_use`, * `select_one`): Indicate how often you use the library's services and resources (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_overall`, * `select_one`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the library's services and resources (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied).
* **Quality of Resources** (`resources_quality`, `select_one`): If you'd like, rate the quality of the library's collection and availability (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Services Rating** (`services_rating`, `select_one`): Rate the library's services, such as reference and interlibrary loan (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Facilities and Study Space Rating** (`facilities_rating`, `select_one`): Rate the library's study spaces and facilities (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Digital Resources Usage** (`digital_resources_usage`, `select_multiple`): Select all the digital resources you use from our collection (E-books, Databases, Journals, Streaming media, Institutional repository).
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`): Share any ideas you have to improve the library's services or resources.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`): If you have any further feedback or suggestions, please include them here.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you're unsure about a question, try your best to provide an answer that reflects your experience.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to the library.

Note: This guide is intended to be a concrete, concise, and practical resource to help users understand the form and its purpose. The guide is based on the provided YAML fields and explanations are provided in the form of a help guide that is easy to follow.
