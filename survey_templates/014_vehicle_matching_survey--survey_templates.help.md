# Vehicle Matching Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Matching Survey is designed to help the sales team better understand the customer's vehicle preferences and needs, and match them with suitable vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out the form in a clear and legible handwriting or type in the designated fields.
3. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure that you've included all relevant information.
4. Submit the completed form to the sales team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Needs** (`buyer_needs`, text, optional):
	+ Briefly describe what you need in a vehicle, such as "reliable", "low maintenance", "fuel efficient", etc.
* **Buyer Budget** (`buyer_budget`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the maximum amount you are willing to spend on a vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type** (`what_vehicle_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the type of vehicle you are interested in (e.g. Honda, Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Desired Vehicle Age** (`desired_vehicle_age`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the age of the vehicle you are interested in (e.g. New, Used, Both).
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the status of your lead (e.g. Qualified, Disqualified, Not Started).
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the color of the vehicle you are interested in (e.g. Red, Blue, Green, etc.).
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the range of budget you are willing to spend on a vehicle.
* **Target Price** (`target_price`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the maximum price you are willing to pay for a vehicle.
* **Time to Purchase** (`time_to_purchase`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the timeframe in which you would like to purchase a vehicle (e.g. 1 week, 2 weeks, etc.).
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the make of the vehicle you are interested in (e.g. Honda, Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Desired Vehicle Mileage** (`desired_vehicle_mileage`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the mileage range you are interested in (e.g. 0-100,000, 100,000-200,000, etc.).
* **Preferred Dealership** (`preferred_dealership`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the dealership you would prefer to purchase from (e.g. Dealership A, Dealership B, etc.).
* **Preferred Dealership Address** (`preferred_dealership_address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the address of the dealership you are interested in.
* **Preferred Dealership Phone** (`preferred_dealership_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the dealership you are interested in.
