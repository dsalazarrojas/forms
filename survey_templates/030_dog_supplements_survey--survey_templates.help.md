# Dog Supplements Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from dog owners about their pets' supplement usage, dietary habits, and health information. The data collected will help us better understand pet owners' habits and preferences regarding dog supplements and overall pet care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Fill in any additional information or comments in the "Note" field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form where you will be asked to provide any general information about your dog.
* **Pet Info** (`pet_info`, number, optional): Please enter your dog's age.
* **Pet Supplements** (`pet_supplements`, select_one, optional): Select the type of supplements your dog is currently using:
	+ Supplement A
	+ Supplement B
	+ Supplement C
* **Pet Food** (`pet_food`, text, optional): Describe your dog's current food type or brand.
* **Health Issues** (`health_issues`, select_multiple, optional): Check the health issues your dog may be experiencing:
	+ Health Issue 1
	+ Health Issue 2
	+ Health Issue 3
* **Veterinary Recommendation** (`vet_recommendation`, select_one, optional): Select your veterinarian's recommended supplement:
	+ Recommended 1
	+ Recommended 2
	+ Recommended 3
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, optional): Please provide any additional feedback or comments about your dog's supplements or health.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you have any).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have any).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you last updated your dog's supplement information.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you last updated your dog's supplement information.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your dog's supplements or health.
