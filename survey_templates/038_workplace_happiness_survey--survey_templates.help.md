# workplace_happiness_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about employee satisfaction and happiness in the workplace. It's essential to answer this form honestly to help management identify areas for improvement and optimize employee well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For text fields, type your response in the given field.
3. For select fields, choose one of the options provided.
4. For number fields, enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 (except for select fields).
5. Leave blank fields as is, without entering any text.
6. Review your responses before submitting the form.
