# P.E. Class Structure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback and information about the structure of Physical Education (P.E.) classes. Your input will help us understand what students feel about their P.E. class experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each question. You may skip any question that does not apply to you. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What does P.E. class look like to you?** (`class_organization_page`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the options that best describe your P.E. class experience. Choose from:
	+ PE Class
	+ Gym Class
	+ Dance Class
	+ Other
* **How engaged do you feel in P.E. class?** (`class_engagement_page`, select_one, required: false): Select how you feel about your level of engagement in P.E. class.
	+ Very Engaged
	+ Somewhat Engaged
	+ Not Very Engaged
	+ Not at All Engaged
* **Any improvement ideas?** (`improvement_ideas_page`, text, required: false): Share any thoughts or suggestions you have for making P.E. class better.
* **Comments** (`comments_page`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your P.E. class experience.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions that apply to you.
- Provide clear and concise answers in the text fields.
- If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field. 

Please note that some fields are optional, so you can skip them if they do not apply to you. Your feedback and input are valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation in this survey.
