# love_notes_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect love notes, short messages or notes that users can share with others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name.
2. Choose your organization (e.g., school, event, or nonprofit).
3. Provide details about the event or context (e.g., event name, date, location).
4. Write a message for the love notes.
5. Select the date and time of the event.
6. Decide who to share the love notes with (public, private, or both).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the love notes.
* **Organization** (`organization`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of organization that best describes the event or project (e.g., school, event, nonprofit).
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the event or context, such as its name, date, and location.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Write a short and sweet message for the love notes.
* **Share With** (`share_with`, select_multiple, optional): Decide who to share the love notes with (public, private, or both).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time of the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Name, Event Details, Message, Date, Time).
* If you are sharing with both public and private individuals, you can select both options.
