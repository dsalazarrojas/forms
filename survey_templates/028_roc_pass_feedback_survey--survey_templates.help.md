# ROC Pass Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ROC Pass Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our service. This form is intended to help us understand what our customers think about our service and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, to the best of your abilities.
2. Select the relevant options that best fit your experience.
3. Be thorough in your answers, but concise and clear.
4. If you're unsure about any question, please provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. How was your experience with our service?
* **Introduction** (`<text>`, required: false): Please describe your experience with our service in a few words.

### 2. How satisfied are you with our service?
* **Satisfaction** (`<select_one>`, required: false): Answer on a scale of 1-5, with:
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied

### 3. How often do you use our service?
* **Usage** (`<select_multiple>`, required: false): Select all that apply:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely

### 4. What are some areas that need improvement?
* **Improvement** (`<text>`, required: false): Please provide specific feedback on areas that we can improve.

### 5. How would you like to be contacted for feedback?
* **Contact** (`<select_one>`, required: false): Please select how you'd like to be contacted:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ In-person

### 6. What is your email address?
* **Email** (`<email>`, required: false): Please enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted).

### 7. What is your phone number?
* **Phone** (`<text>`, required: false): Please enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).

Note: This form is optional, and you are not required to complete all fields. However, we appreciate your feedback and would be glad to hear your thoughts.
