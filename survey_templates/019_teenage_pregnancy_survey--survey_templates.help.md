# Teenage Pregnancy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents of teenage pregnancy.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has two pages: "main_page" and "section_what_happened", "section_what_causes", "question_1", "question_2", "question_3", "question_4", and "question_5". Please complete each page according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **main_page** (main_page, text, required: false): This is the first page of the form. Please enter a brief description of the case.
* **What_Happened** (section_what_happened, text, required: false): This section is for describing what happened in the case of the teenage pregnancy.
* **What_Causes** (section_what_causes, select_one, required: false): Please select one of the options to describe what may have caused the pregnancy. Options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **question_1** (question_1, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **question_2** (question_2, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event.
* **question_3** (question_3, number, required: false): Enter a number related to the event.
* **question_4** (question_4, number, required: false): Enter another number related to the event.
* **question_5** (question_5, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the event.

## Tips

- Please ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact us for assistance.
- This form is for collecting data only and should not be used for official reporting or investigation purposes.
