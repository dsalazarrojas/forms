# Ethical Use Of AI In Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather opinions and insights from educators on the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in education. The survey will help us understand your understanding, concerns, and recommendations regarding the responsible use of AI in educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please go through each page carefully and fill in the required information. You can select multiple options or enter text responses as per the question type. Do not skip any page, and make sure to save your progress by clicking the "Next" button until you reach the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A note section to acknowledge that you are ready to participate in the survey.
* **Educators**: A multiple-choice question to determine your level of expertise in AI.
	+ Please select one of the options: AI-Expert, AI-Novice, or Other.
* **Benefits**: A multiple-choice question to identify the benefits you see from AI adoption in education.
	+ Select all that apply. Options are: Improved student outcomes, Increased teacher efficiency, Other.
* **Concerns**: A multiple-choice question to identify your concerns about AI adoption in education.
	+ Select all that apply. Options are: Privacy, Security, Cost, Other.
* **Responsible Use**: A multiple-choice question to determine how you believe AI should be used in education.
	+ Check all that apply. Options are: Transparency, Accountability, Other.
* **Student Outcomes**: A text-entry field to provide detailed information on how AI might impact student outcomes in your institution.
* **Educator Outcomes**: A text-entry field to provide detailed information on how AI might impact educator outcomes in your institution.
* **Administrator Outcomes**: A text-entry field to provide detailed information on how AI might impact administrator outcomes in your institution.
* **Policies**: A text-entry field to provide recommendations on what policies you would suggest for the responsible use of AI in education.
* **Practices**: A text-entry field to provide suggestions on what practices you would recommend for the responsible use of AI in education.
* **Additional Comments**: A text-entry field for any further comments you may have.
* **Expertise**: A multiple-choice question to determine your level of AI expertise.
	+ Select one of the options: AI-Expert, AI-Novice, Other.
* **Institution Size**: A multiple-choice question to determine the size of your institution.
	+ Select one of the options: Small, Medium, Large, Other.
* **Age Range**: A multiple-choice question to determine the age range of students your institution primarily serves.
	+ Select all that apply. Options are: Elementary School, High School, College, Other.
* **Years in Education**: A multiple-choice question to determine your years of experience in education.
	+ Select one of the options: 0-5, 6-15, 16-25, 26+.
* **Department**: A multiple-choice question to determine the department you work in.
	+ Select all that apply. Options are: IT, Curriculum Development, Other.
* **Additional Information**: A text-entry field to provide any additional information you would like to share.
* **Contact Information**: A set of text-entry fields to provide your contact email, phone number, name, job title, and institution.
* **Comments**: A text-entry field for any further comments you may have.
* **Confirm**: A multiple-choice question to confirm your submission.
	+ Select one of the options: True or False.

Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form. If you need any clarification or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us. Thank you for participating in this survey!
