# AI Content Recommendation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "AI Content Recommendation Consent Form" is designed to collect user information and preferences for AI-driven content recommendations. This form ensures users understand how their data will be used and what they consent to.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your legal name, email address, and age group.
2. Select your preferred categories of content for recommendations (if applicable).
3. Choose how often you'd like to receive recommendations.
4. Review and acknowledge our privacy policy.
5. Consent to sharing anonymized usage data with trusted partners.
6. Choose your preferred notification method.
7. Sign and acknowledge the consent form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, required): Enter the email address we'll use to send personalized content recommendations.
* **Age group** (`age_group`, required): Select your age group for more accurate content recommendations. Choose from: Under 18, 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65 or older.
* **Consent to receive personalized content recommendations** (`consent_agreement`, required): Choose "Yes, I consent" to receive AI-driven recommendations based on your preferences and usage data.
* **Data usage consent** (`data_usage_consent`, required): Choose "Agree" to allow data analysis for recommendation accuracy.
* **Content categories** (`content_categories`, optional): Select all applicable content categories you're interested in (e.g., News & Current Events, Technology, Entertainment, etc.).
* **Recommendation frequency** (`recommendation_frequency`, required): Choose how often you'd like to receive recommendations: Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Privacy policy acknowledgment** (`privacy_policy_acknowledged`, required): Verify you've read and understood our privacy policy.
* **Data sharing consent** (`data_sharing_consent`, required): Choose "Consent" to share anonymized usage data with trusted partners.
* **Preferred notification method** (`notification_preference`, required): Select how you'd like to receive notifications: Email, Mobile push notifications, Both, or None.
* **Consent timestamp** (`consent_timestamp`, not required): This field is automatically populated and not editable.
* **Marketing communication consent** (`marketing_communication_consent`, optional): Choose to receive marketing communications related to recommended content.
* **Data retention period** (`data_retention_period`, required): Choose how long we should retain your preference data: 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, Indefinitely, or Until you withdraw consent.
* **Withdrawal method** (`withdrawal_method`, required): Select how you'd prefer to withdraw consent in the future: Via email, Through app settings, Via website portal, or Any of the above.
* **Additional comments or concerns** (`additional_comments`, optional): Share any specific preferences or concerns about data usage.
* **Terms acceptance** (`terms_acceptance`, required): Choose "Accept" to agree to the terms and conditions governing AI content recommendations.
* **Electronic signature** (`signature`, required): Type your name as your electronic signature.
* **Consent form version** (`consent_version`, not required): This field is not editable and tracks updates to our policies.
