# Leadership Skills Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Skills Survey is designed to help individuals evaluate their leadership skills and provide valuable information for personal growth and development. This form is used to collect detailed information about an individual's leadership abilities, style, and development needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the survey carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Fill out each section as instructed, providing as much detail as possible for each question.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **leadership skills survey** (`leadership skills survey`, 1): This is the introduction to the form. Please read through it to understand the purpose and scope of the survey.
* **Leadership skills assessment** (`self_assessment`, 2): Assess your current leadership skills and abilities.
* **Interpersonal skills** (`interpersonal_skills`, 3): Describe your interpersonal skills, including your ability to communicate effectively, build relationships, and work with others.
* **Social interaction** (`social_interaction`, 4): Describe your social interaction skills, including your ability to navigate complex social situations and build strong relationships with colleagues and customers.
* **Decision making** (`decision_making`, 5): Choose one of the options to describe your decision-making style:
	+ Option 1: Autocratic (make decisions without input or feedback)
	+ Option 2: Collaborative (involve others in the decision-making process)
	+ Option 3: Analytical (make decisions based on data and analysis)
* **Communication skills** (`communication_skills`, 6): Describe your communication skills, including your ability to effectively communicate ideas, thoughts, and feelings.
* **Conflict resolution** (`conflict_resolution`, 7): Choose all applicable options to describe your conflict resolution skills:
	+ Option 1: Aggressive (confrontation)
	+ Option 2: Avoidant (avoidance)
* **Adaptability** (`adaptability`, 8): Describe your ability to adapt to changing situations and environments.
* **Problem solving** (`problem_solving`, 9): Choose one of the options to describe your problem-solving style:
	+ Option 1: Creative
	+ Option 2: Analytical
	+ Option 3: Systematic
	+ Option 4: Intuitive
* **Leadership ability** (`leadership_ability`, 10): Describe your leadership abilities, including your vision, mission, and values.
* **Leadership style** (`leadership_style`, 11): Describe your leadership style, including your approach to leadership and management.
* **Leadership values** (`leadership_values`, 12): Describe your leadership values, including your principles and ethics.
* **Team management** (`team_management`, 13): Describe your team management skills, including your ability to lead and motivate a team.
* **Feedback skills** (`feedback_skills`, 14): Describe your feedback skills, including your ability to receive and give feedback.
* **Leadership development** (`leadership_development`, 15): Describe your leadership development, including your willingness to learn and grow as a leader.
* **Leadership survey** (`leadership_skills_survey`, 16): This is a note field, please use it to provide any comments or observations about your leadership skills.
* **Leadership roadmap** (`leadership_roadmap`, 17): Describe your leadership roadmap, including your short-term and long-term goals.
* **Roadmap comments** (`leadership_roadmap_comments`, 18): Provide any comments or observations about your leadership roadmap.
* **Final comments** (`final_comments`, 19): Provide any final comments or observations about your leadership skills and development.

Note: This form is for personal development purposes only. Please answer honestly and accurately to provide valuable information for your growth and improvement.
