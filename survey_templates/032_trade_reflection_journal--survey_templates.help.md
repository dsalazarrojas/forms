# Trade Reflection Journal - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and analyze information about completed trades. It is essential for users to accurately document their trading activities to gain valuable insights and improve future trades.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have filled out any required fields, marked as (required: true) in the form.
2. Fill in your Email address in the Email field. This is used for identification and record-keeping purposes.
3. Input the date of the trade in the Trade Date field. This is a required field to track the time of the trade.
4. Select the type of trade (Buy or Sell) in the Trade Type field.
5. Enter your notes about the trade in the Entry Notes field.
6. Reflect on your experience in the Entry Reflect field.
7. Describe any actions you took or plan to take in the Entry Actions field.
8. Summarize your conclusions about the trade in the Entry Conclusions field.
9. Outline the next steps you plan to take in the Entry Next Steps field.
10. Select how much of the trade was reflected upon (None, Some, or All) in the Entry Reflect Total field.
11. Add any additional comments about the trade in the Entry Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to be identified and tracked in the system.
- **Trade Date** (`trade_date`, date, required: true): Record the date of the trade for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
- **Trade Type** (`trade_type`, select_one, required: true): Choose between Buy or Sell to categorize the trade type.
- **Entry Notes** (`entry_notes`, text, required: false): Add notes about the trade, including any relevant details.
- **Entry Reflect** (`entry_reflect`, text, required: false): Reflect on your experience and feelings about the trade.
- **Entry Actions** (`entry_actions`, text, required: false): Describe any actions you took or plan to take as a result of the trade.
- **Entry Conclusions** (`entry_conclusions`, text, required: false): Summarize your conclusions and takeaways from the trade.
- **Entry Next Steps** (`entry_next_steps`, text, required: false): Outline the next steps you plan to take related to the trade.
- **Entry Reflect Total** (`entry_reflect_total`, select_multiple, required: true): Select how much of the trade was reflected upon (None, Some, or All).
- **Entry Comments** (`entry_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or insights about the trade.

Note: This form is only accessible to authorized personnel, and repeated submissions should not be necessary.
