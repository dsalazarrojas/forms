# Therapist Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapist Evaluation Survey is a tool for clients to share their feedback on their experience with a therapist. It helps the therapist understand their strengths and weaknesses to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer the questions based on your experience with the therapist.
2. Select the answer that best reflects your feelings and perceptions.
3. Fill in the required fields (marked as required) with your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, false): Please share your thoughts on your experience with the therapist. Be as detailed or brief as you like.
* **How would you rate the therapist’s communication style?** (`feedback_question_1`, select_one, false): How would you rate the therapist's communication style from Very poor to Excellent?
* **How would you rate the therapist's professionalism?** (`feedback_question_2`, select_one, false): How would you rate the therapist's professionalism from Very poor to Excellent?
* **How responsive is the therapist to your needs?** (`feedback_question_3`, select_one, false): How would you rate the therapist's responsiveness from Very poor to Excellent?
* **How would you rate the overall satisfaction with the therapy sessions?** (`feedback_question_4`, select_one, false): How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the therapy sessions from Very poor to Excellent?
* **How likely are you to recommend this therapist to others?** (`feedback_question_5`, select_one, false): How likely are you to recommend this therapist to others, from Very unlikely to Very likely?
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, false): Please enter the name of the tool assigned to the therapist.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, false): Please enter the name of the user assigned to the therapist.

Note: The fields marked as required are not currently required, but please fill them out to provide as much feedback as you can.
