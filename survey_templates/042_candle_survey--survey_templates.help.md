# Candle Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your experiences and opinions on various aspects of our candle products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Make sure to answer all required questions (marked as "required" in bold).
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional Comments" field at the end of the form.
4. Review and confirm your answers before submitting the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Group** (`customer_age_group`, select_one, required): This field asks you to select your age group.
	+ Under 18
	+ 18-24
	+ 25-34
	+ 35-44
	+ 45-54
	+ 55+
* **Gender** (`customer_gender_pref`, select_one, required): This field is for you to select your gender identity.
	+ Female
	+ Male
	+ Non-binary
	+ Prefer not to say
* **Frequency of purchasing candles** (`purchase_frequency_candle`, select_one, required): This field asks how often you buy new candles.
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Product Feedback** (`product_feedback_section`, note, optional): This field is for any thoughts or comments about our candles.
* **Favorite Candle Scents** (`favorite_scent_categories`, select_multiple, required): Select all the scents you enjoy from the list.
	+ Floral
	+ Fruity
	+ Woody or Spicy
	+ Fresh or Clean
	+ Sweet or Gourmand
	+ Unscented
* **Rate the scent throw** (`scent_throw_rating`, select_one, required): This field asks you to rate the strength of the aroma while burning.
	+ Very Weak
	+ Weak
	+ Neutral
	+ Strong
	+ Very Strong
* **Rate the burn time of our candles** (`burn_time_rating`, select_one, required): This field asks you to rate how long our candles last.
	+ Very Poor
	+ Poor
	+ Average
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Importance of eco-friendly materials** (`eco_friendly_importance`, select_one, required): This field asks you to rate the importance of eco-friendly materials in our products.
	+ Not Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Important
	+ Very Important
* **Preferred candle type** (`preferred_wax_type`, select_one, required): This field is for you to select your favorite base material of our candles.
	+ Soy Wax
	+ Beeswax
	+ Paraffin
	+ Coconut Wax
	+ Other
* **Shopping Experience** (`shopping_exp_section`, note, optional): This field is for any comments or feedback on your shopping experience.
* **Overall satisfaction with the shop** (`overall_shop_satisfaction`, number, required): This field asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with our shop on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Satisfaction with price point** (`price_satisfaction_rating`, select_one, required): This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with our prices.
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **How likely are you to recommend us to a friend?** (`nps_recommendation_score`, number, required): This field asks you to rate how likely you are to recommend us to a friend on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Any improvements for our packaging?** (`packaging_improvement_feedback`, text, optional): This field is for any suggestions or feedback on our packaging.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_candle`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you would like to share.
* **I consent to having my feedback used for testimonials** (`testimonial_consent_candle`, select_one, required): This field asks you to confirm if you consent to having your feedback used for testimonials.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Date of Survey** (`survey_completion_date`, date, required): This field is automatically generated and indicates the date you completed the survey.
