# College Size Seal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Size Seal Survey is designed to gather feedback and opinions on the college's seal size and design. This information will be used to inform future decisions about the use of the college seal in official contexts.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
- Respondents who are familiar with the college seal are encouraged to participate.
- For each question, select the best answer based on your experience and opinion.
- Leave additional feedback or suggestions in the "Additional Feedback" section if you have any other thoughts or ideas.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Provide your name, which will help us understand who responded to this survey. You can remain anonymous if you prefer. (Optional)
* **Your Role at the College**: Select the role that best describes you in relation to the college (Student, Faculty, Staff, Alumni, Administrator, or Other).
* **Department or Area**: If you are a student or staff member, please specify your department or area of study/affiliation. This will help us understand your perspective based on your specific context.
* **-- College Seal and Logo Feedback --**: This is a note section where you can provide any general feedback or thoughts about the college seal. 
* **How familiar are you with the college seal?**: Select how familiar you are with the college seal, based on your experience with it. (Very Familiar, Familiar, Somewhat Familiar, or Not Familiar)
* **Current seal size on official documents**: Select the size of the college seal as it appears on official documents, such as diplomas, letterheads, and certificates. (Too Small, Appropriate Size, Too Large, or No Opinion)
* **Seal size on official correspondence**: Select the size of the college seal as it appears on official letters, forms, and envelopes. (Too Small, Appropriate Size, Too Large, or No Opinion)
* **Seal size on website and digital materials**: Select the size of the college seal as it appears on the website and other digital materials. (Too Small, Appropriate Size, Too Large, or No Opinion)
* **Overall visibility of the college seal**: Select how easy it is to see and recognize the college seal. (Excellent, Good, Adequate, Poor, or Very Poor)
* **Distinctiveness of college seal design**: Select how distinctive the college seal design is compared to other institutions. (Very Distinctive, Distinctive, Average, Not Very Distinctive, or Generic)
* **Would you recommend a larger seal?**: If you were in charge of designing the seal, select whether you would recommend making it larger. (Strongly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, Do Not Recommend, or Strongly Oppose)
* **Would you recommend larger seal on official correspondence?**: If you were in charge of designing official correspondence, select whether you would recommend making the seal larger. (Strongly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, Do Not Recommend, or Strongly Oppose)
* **Preferred seal location on official documents**: Select where you would prefer to see the college seal on official documents. (Top center, Top left, Top right, Bottom center, Bottom left, Bottom right, or No preference)
* **Seal color appearance satisfaction**: Select your level of satisfaction with the color appearance of the college seal. (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied)
* **Any desired changes to seal design or appearance**: If you have any specific thoughts or suggestions about the seal design or appearance, type them below. (Text input field)
* **Consistency of seal use across college materials**: Select how well the college seal is used consistently across different materials such as letterheads, diplomas, etc. (Very Consistent, Consistent, Somewhat Consistent, Inconsistent, or Very Inconsistent)
* **The college seal makes me proud**: Select how strongly you agree that the college seal makes you proud. (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree)
* **The college seal is important to the institution**: Select how strongly you agree that the college seal is important to the institution. (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree)
* **Overall satisfaction with college seal usage**: Select your level of satisfaction with the college seal usage in general. (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied)
* **Additional Feedback or Suggestions**: If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions about the college seal, type them below. 

## Tips
- Please answer each question based on your honest experience and opinion.
- If you are unsure about an answer, select the option that best represents your opinion.
- Thank you for participating in this survey! Your feedback is valuable to us.
