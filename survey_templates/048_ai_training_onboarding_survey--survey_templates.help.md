# AI Training Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from learners about their background, goals, preferences, and expectations from the training to provide an effective and tailored learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and answers.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, as this information will help the training team tailor the content and schedule to your needs.
3. Use the provided hints and options as guidance for each question.
4. If you need assistance or clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to the training team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Learner Profile** (`--learner_profile--`, `note`, required: false): This section is a note field that is not displayed to the user. It is likely a placeholder for an internal note that the training team will use to guide the form completion.
* **Learner name** (`learner_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the training.
* **Prior experience with AI** (`prior_experience`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one of the options to indicate your level of experience with AI:
	+ None: You have no experience with AI.
	+ Some practical experience: You have some experience with AI, but not extensive.
	+ Completed courses: You have completed courses or training programs related to AI.
	+ Professional experience: You have experience with AI in a professional setting.
* **Learning goals for this training** (`learning_goals`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select up to three goals you want to achieve from this training:
	+ Understand fundamentals: You want to learn the basics of AI.
	+ Apply to job tasks: You want to learn how to apply AI in your job tasks.
	+ Certification preparation: You want to prepare for AI-related certifications.
	+ Project development: You want to develop AI projects.
* **Preferred learning format** (`preferred_learning_format`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose your preferred learning format:
	+ Live instructor led: You prefer live, instructor-led training sessions.
	+ Self-paced online: You prefer self-paced online learning.
	+ Hybrid: You prefer a mix of live and online learning.
	+ Cohort based: You prefer learning with a group of people in a cohort setting.
* **Preferred availability windows** (`availability`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your preferred availability for the training, including days and times you are available.
* **Accessibility requirements** (`accessibility_requirements`, `text`, required: false): If you have any accessibility requirements, such as accommodations for disabilities, please enter them here.
* **Technology readiness** (`technology_ready`, `select_one`, required: true): Check if you have access to required tools:
	+ 'True': You have access to the required tools.
	+ 'False': You don't have access to the required tools.
	+ Need assistance: You need assistance with getting access to the required tools.
* **Expectations from the training** (`expectations`, `text`, required: false): If you have any specific expectations from the training, please enter them here.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or information, please enter them here.

Note: The fields with `required: true` are mandatory, while the fields with `required: false` are optional.
