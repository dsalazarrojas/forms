# Return To Work Anxiety Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your feelings and concerns before and after returning to the office after the COVID-19 pandemic. It will help you gauge your anxiety levels and provide valuable feedback for your organization's return to work strategy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select all the options that apply to your feelings and concerns before and after returning to the office.
2. Choose only one option for each select-one field.
3. Provide detailed answers for the text fields (Feelings and Thoughts, Concerns and Worries, Suggestions and Comments, and Additional Comments).
4. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Return to Office** (`first_page`, select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to your feelings when returning to the office. Choose only the options that best fit your personal feelings and concerns.
	+ Option 1: Feeling excited and ready to return to the office.
	+ Option 2: Feeling anxious or worried about returning to the office.
	+ Option 3: Feeling neutral, neither excited nor worried about returning to the office.
* **Feelings and Thoughts** (`feelings_and_thoughts`, text): Describe your feelings and thoughts about returning to the office.
* **Return to Work Support** (`return_to_work_support`, select_one): Choose the level of support you feel you need when returning to the office.
	+ Option 1: I need a lot of support.
	+ Option 2: I need some support.
	+ Option 3: I don't need support.
* **Concerns and Worries** (`concerns_and_worries`, text): Describe any concerns and worries you have about returning to the office.
* **Suggestions and Comments** (`suggestions_and_comments`, text): Provide any suggestions or comments you have about returning to the office.
* **Feelings Before Covid** (`feelings_before_covid`, select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to your feelings before the COVID-19 pandemic.
	+ Option 1: Feeling excited and engaged in work.
	+ Option 2: Feeling stressed or overwhelmed.
	+ Option 3: Feeling neutral, neither stressed nor engaged.
* **Feelings After Covid** (`feelings_after_covid`, select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to your feelings after the COVID-19 pandemic.
	+ Option 1: Feeling more confident and motivated.
	+ Option 2: Feeling anxious or worried.
	+ Option 3: Feeling neutral, neither anxious nor motivated.
* **Work From Home** (`work_from_home`, select_one): Choose how often you worked from home during the pandemic.
	+ Option 1: Frequently.

	+ Option 3: Never.

	+ Option 1: Frequently.
	+ Option 2: Occasionally.
	+ Option 3: Never.

	+ Option 1: Flexible schedule.
	+ Option 2: Traditional schedule.
	+ Option 3: No preference.
* **Return to Office Anxiety Score** (`return_to_office_anxiety_score`, number): Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to gauge your anxiety level when returning to the office.
* **Feelings After Return to Work** (`feelings_after_return_to_work`, select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to your feelings after returning to work.
	+ Option 1: Feeling positive and engaged.
	+ Option 2: Feeling anxious or worried.
	+ Option 3: Feeling neutral, neither positive nor anxious.

	+ Option 1: Very supportive.
	+ Option 2: Somewhat supportive.
	+ Option 3: Not supportive.
* **Return to Office Support Needed** (`return_to_office_support_needed`, select_one): Choose the level of support you feel you need when returning to the office.
	+ Option 1: A lot of support.
	+ Option 2: Some support.
	+ Option 3: No support.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to suggestions for improvement.

	+ Option 2: Better communication tools.

* **Return to Office Concerns** (`return_to_office_concerns`, select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to your concerns about returning to the office.

	+ Option 2: Fear of being asked to work long hours.
	+ Option 3: Other concerns.
* **Return to Office Support Needed Details** (`return_to_office_support_needed_details`, select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to the support you feel you need when returning to the office.
	+ Option 1: Training or orientation on new work procedures.

* **Return to Office Concerns Details** (`return_to_office_concerns_details`, select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to your concerns about returning to the office.

* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have about improving the return-to-work experience.

* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the return-to-work experience.
