# Workshop Participant Expectation Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from workshop participants on their expectations, preferences, and commitments. It's designed to help the facilitator better understand the participant's needs and tailor the workshop to their requirements. This information is not only useful for the facilitator but also for the participant themselves, as it serves as a reference point for their own preparation and engagement throughout the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly to the best of your ability.
2.  Fill in all the fields as required, as indicated by the "Required" label in each field.
3.  If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask the facilitator or contact support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How are you feeling today** (`1`, `text`, `false`): This field is a brief introduction to help us understand your state of mind before the workshop begins.
* **What do you hope to get out of this workshop** (`2`, `text`, `false`): Please describe your expectations and goals for this workshop. This will help us tailor the content and activities to meet your needs.
* **How ready are you to participate** (`3`, `number`, `false`): On a scale of 1-5, how prepared and committed are you to fully engage with the workshop?
* **Are there any topics you are specifically interested in** (`4`, `select_multiple`, `false`): If there are any specific topics or subjects that interest you, please select them from the provided options.
* **Do you have any technical skills you would like to share** (`5`, `text`, `false`): If you have any technical skills or expertise that you'd like to share with the group, please describe them in this field.
* **How do you prefer to communicate** (`6`, `select_one`, `false`): How would you like to be communicated with during the workshop? Please select the option that best describes your preference.
* **What are your preferred learning methods** (`7`, `text`, `false`): Describe your preferred learning style or methods, this will help us tailor the content and activities to your learning needs.
* **How will you follow up on the workshop** (`8`, `text`, `false`): Please describe how you plan to follow up on the workshop, whether it's through email, phone, or other means.
* **Would you like to share any technical skills** (`9`, `select_multiple`, `false`): If you have any technical skills or expertise that you'd like to share with the group, please select the options that apply.
* **How would you like the facilitator to facilitate** (`10`, `text`, `false`): Describe how you'd like the facilitator to facilitate the workshop, this will help us ensure a positive experience for you.
* **Can you commit to the full workshop schedule** (`11`, `select_one`, `false`): Confirm your commitment to attend the entire workshop as scheduled.
* **What is your email** (`12`, `email`, `false`): Please provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **What is your phone number** (`13`, `text`, `false`): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Any note** (`14`, `note`, `false`): If you have any additional comments or notes you'd like to share, please do so in this field.
* **Do you have any feedback** (`15`, `text`, `false`): If you have any feedback or suggestions on the workshop, please share them with us.
* **Is there anything you are expecting to hear from us** (`16`, `text`, `false`): Please describe anything you are expecting to hear from us after the workshop, this will help us prioritize follow-up communication.
