# Beverage Launch Marketing Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Launch Marketing Poll Form is designed to collect data on consumer opinions and preferences regarding a new beverage product. This form helps the research team understand how consumers perceive the product, what features appeal to them, and what they are willing to pay for it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is for follow-up surveys if needed.
3. Select your age group from the "Age Range" dropdown menu.
4. Select your gender from the "Gender" dropdown menu.
5. Indicate your level of interest in trying new beverages in the "Interest in New Beverages" dropdown menu.
6. Rate the appeal of the new beverage concept in the "Concept Appeal Rating" dropdown menu.
7. Choose how interested you are in the flavor profile in the "Flavor Interest" dropdown menu.
8. Rate the attractiveness of the packaging in the "Packaging Appeal" dropdown menu.
9. Answer questions about the brand name, purchase intent, expected price range, purchase frequency, preferred purchase location, and most appealing feature.
10. Indicate your willingness to recommend the product to others in the "Would You Recommend to Others" dropdown menu.
11. If you have any concerns or hesitations about trying the product, type them in the "Concerns or Hesitations" field.
12. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.
13. If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for follow-up surveys if needed.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, `select_one`, required): Select your age group from the options provided.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Interest in New Beverages** (`beverage_interest`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your level of interest in trying new beverages.
* **Concept Appeal Rating** (`concept_appeal`, `select_one`, required): Rate the appeal of the new beverage concept.
* **Flavor Interest** (`flavor_interest`, `select_one`, required): Choose how interested you are in the flavor profile.
* **Packaging Appeal** (`packaging_appeal`, `select_one`, required): Rate the attractiveness of the packaging.
* **Brand Name Recall** (`name_recall`, `select_one`, required): Answer about the memorability of the brand name.
* **Purchase Intent** (`purchase_intent`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your purchase intent for the product.
* **Expected Price Range** (`price_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Answer about the expected price range for the product.
* **Expected Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Answer about how often you would buy the product.
* **Preferred Purchase Location** (`purchase_location`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose where you would expect to buy the product.
* **Effective Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate which marketing channels would influence you most.
* **Most Appealing Feature** (`unique_selling_point`, `select_one`, required): Choose the feature that stands out to you the most.
* **Would You Recommend to Others** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your willingness to recommend the product to others.
* **Any Concerns or Hesitations** (`concerns`, `text`, false): If you have any concerns or hesitations, type them here.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, false): If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, false): If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them here.
