# Camera Demo Request Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your needs and requirements for a camera demo. Please fill out the form below to help us provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form carefully, one page at a time.
- Make sure to answer each question accurately, as this will help us tailor the demo to your needs.
- If you have any issues or need assistance, contact us and we'll be happy to help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Contact Full Name
**Contact Full Name** (`contact_person_name`, `text`, required): 
This field is for your full contact name. Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.

### 2. Company or Organization Name
**Company or Organization Name** (`company_org_name`, `text`, required): 
Enter the name of your company or organization.

### 3. Job Title - Role
**Job Title - Role** (`job_title_demo`, `text`, required): 
Enter your job title or role within your company or organization.

### 4. Professional Email Address
**Professional Email Address** (`contact_email_demo`, `email`, required): 
Enter your professional email address where we can contact you.

### 5. Phone Number
**Phone Number** (`contact_phone_demo`, `text`, required): 
Enter your phone number where we can contact you.

### 6. -- Demo Requirements --
**-- Demo Requirements --** (`demo_needs_header`, `note`, required): 
This section is a heading for the next page, please proceed to the next page for more information.

### 7. Primary Intended Use Case
**Primary Intended Use Case** (`camera_use_case`, `select_one`, required): 
Select the primary intended use case for the camera demo. This will help us provide you with relevant information and equipment.

### 8. Specific Camera Models Interest
**Specific Camera Models Interest** (`specific_model_interest`, `text`, required): 
List specific camera models you are interested in seeing during the demo.

### 9. Projected Number of Units Needed
**Projected Number of Units Needed** (`projected_unit_count`, `number`, required): 
Estimate the number of camera units you will need.

### 10. Preferred Demo Format
**Preferred Demo Format** (`demo_format_pref`, `select_one`, required): 
Select the preferred demo format for you. We will accommodate your request subject to availability.

### 11. Requested Demo Date
**Requested Demo Date** (`preferred_demo_date`, `date`, required): 
Enter the date you would like to have the camera demo, subject to availability.

### 12. Specific Technical Requirements
**Specific Technical Requirements** (`technical_requirements_notes`, `text`, required): 
Provide any specific technical requirements you have for the camera demo, e.g., low-light performance, 8K resolution, weatherproofing.

### 13. Current Budget Status
**Current Budget Status** (`budget_status`, `select_one`, required): 
Let us know your current budget status for the camera demo.

### 14. How did you hear about our camera solutions?
**How did you hear about our camera solutions?** (`referral_vendor`, `select_one`, required): 
Tell us how you heard about our camera solutions, this will help us understand our outreach effectiveness.
