# youth_handball_selection_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting information about youth handball players.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form with the player's personal details.
2. Provide the player's playing experience, including position, years of experience, and skill level.
3. Enter the player's physical characteristics, including height and weight.
4. Enter contact information for emergency contacts and the player's team.
