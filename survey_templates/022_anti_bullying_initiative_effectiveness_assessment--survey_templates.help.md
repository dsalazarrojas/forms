# Anti-Bullying Initiative Effectiveness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the effectiveness of anti-bullying initiatives within the school. It's essential to be honest and accurate when answering the questions, as the data collected will help the school administration and staff make informed decisions about future initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the school from the options provided.
2. Answer the questions based on your experience and perception.
3. Provide your honest feedback and suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required):
	+ This question helps us understand who is responding to this form (Student, Parent/Guardian, Faculty Member, or Administrative Staff).
* **Grade Level (if applicable)** (`grade_level`, text, optional):
	+ This question is only applicable if you are a student. Please provide your grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, etc.).
* **Awareness of School Anti-Bullying Policies** (`policy_awareness`, select_one, required):
	+ This question assesses your level of awareness about the school's anti-bullying policies.
* **How safe do you feel at school?** (`safety_feeling`, number, required):
	+ This question asks about your perception of safety at school, with 1 being very unsafe and 5 being very safe.
* **Frequency of witnessing bullying behaviors this semester** (`bullying_witness_freq`, select_one, required):
	+ This question asks how often you've witnessed bullying behaviors in this semester.
* **Confidence in School's Response to Bullying Reports** (`reporting_confidence`, select_one, required):
	+ This question measures your confidence in the school's response to bullying reports.
* **Effectiveness of Recent Anti-Bullying Initiatives** (`initiative_impact`, select_one, required):
	+ This question assesses the effectiveness of recent anti-bullying initiatives.
* **Positive Changes Noticed Since Initiatives Started** (`perceived_changes`, text, optional):
	+ This question is an opportunity to provide feedback on any positive changes you've noticed since the initiatives started.
* **Gaps in Current Prevention Programs** (`gap_analysis`, text, optional):
	+ This question is an opportunity to suggest areas for improvement in the current prevention programs.
* **Level of Concern Regarding Cyberbullying** (`cyberbullying_concern`, select_one, required):
	+ This question measures your level of concern about cyberbullying.
* **Suggested Actions for Improvement** (`suggested_actions`, text, optional):
	+ This question is an opportunity to provide suggestions for improving the anti-bullying initiatives.
