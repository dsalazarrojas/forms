# Women Golf Shorts Shopping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Women Golf Shorts Shopping Survey is a form used to gather feedback from women golfers on their shopping preferences and experience. This survey aims to collect information on their shopping habits, satisfaction with their current shopping experience, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferences from the options provided under "shopper_preferences".
2. Choose your satisfaction rating with your current shopping experience from the options under "rating".
3. Select your buying habits from the options provided under "buying_habits".
4. If you have any additional information about your shopping experience, type it in the "other_info" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
7. Enter the date and time of your last golf shopping experience in the "date_time" field.
8. Select the channels you use for shopping from the options provided under "shopping_channels".
9. Select the audience you are most likely to shop with from the options provided under "audience".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### shopper_preferences
* **shopper_preferences** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select all that apply. Please choose the shopping preferences that best suit your needs.

### rating
* **rating** (`select_one`, `required: false`): Rate your satisfaction with your current shopping experience.

### buying_habits
* **buying_habits** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select all that apply. Please choose the buying habits that best suit your needs.

### other_info
* **other_info** (`note`, `required: false`): If you have any additional information about your shopping experience, please type it in here.

### email_address
* **Email address** (`email`, `required: false`): Enter your email address where you can be reached.

### phone_number
* **Phone number** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted.

### date_time
* **Date and time** (`date`, `required: false`): Enter the date and time of your last golf shopping experience.

### shopping_channels
* **shopping_channels** (`select_one`, `required: false`): Select the channel you use most for shopping.

### audience
* **audience** (`select_one`, `required: false`): Select the audience you are most likely to shop with.
