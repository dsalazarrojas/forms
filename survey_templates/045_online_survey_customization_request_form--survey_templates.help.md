**Online Survey Customization Request Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The Online Survey Customization Request Form is used to request customizations for online surveys. This form is intended for users to provide details about the specific customizations they need for their surveys. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a title for your survey in the "form title" field.
2. Select the category of your survey in the "category" field.
3. Describe your survey in the "description" field.
4. Choose the features you want to include in your survey in the "features" field.
5. Select the formats you want to use for your survey in the "formats" field.
6. Provide details about data collection in the "data collection" field.
7. Specify the tool assigned to your survey in the "assigned tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form title (text, required/optional: optional)**: Enter a short and descriptive title for your survey.
* **category (select_one, required/optional: false)**: Choose the category that best matches your survey's topic or subject.
* **description (text, required/optional: false)**: Provide a detailed description of your survey, including its purpose and goals.
* **features (select_multiple, required/optional: false)**: Select from the options to specify which features you would like to include in your survey, such as multiple choice or open-ended questions.
* **formats (select_multiple, required/optional: false)**: Choose the formats you want to use for your survey, such as text, image, or video.
* **data collection (text, required/optional: false)**: Describe how data will be collected and managed for your survey.
* **assigned tool (text, required/optional: false)**: Specify the tool that will be used to create and conduct your survey.

## Tips
* Please be specific and detailed in your responses to ensure that your request is accurately understood.
* Review your form title to ensure it accurately reflects the content of your survey.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to the instructions or contact your support team for assistance.
