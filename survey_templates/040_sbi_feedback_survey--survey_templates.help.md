# SBI Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience at our branches. The survey will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer each question truthfully.
2. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
3. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (`id: 1`): This page is not applicable as it's just a page label.

* **How was your interaction with our staff?** (`id: 3`): Rate your experience with our staff's interaction.
* **Did our staff show knowledge of our products?** (`id: 4`): Rate if our staff had knowledge of our products.
* **Time Spent Waiting in Queue** (`id: 5`): Rate if you spent an excessive amount of time waiting in queue.

* **Staff Friendliness** (`id: 12`): Rate if our staff were friendly.
