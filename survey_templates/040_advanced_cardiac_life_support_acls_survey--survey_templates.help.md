# Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the knowledge, skills, and confidence of clinicians in providing Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) care. It is intended for use by healthcare professionals to evaluate their own knowledge and performance in managing cardiac emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your clinician's name in the "Clinician Name" field.
2. Select your professional role from the "Professional Role" dropdown list.
3. Enter your current ACLS certification expiration date in the "ACLS Certification Expiration Date" field.
4. For the "First Line Medication for VF and pVT" field, select the medication you would choose to administer in a ventricular fibrillation emergency.
5. Choose the recommended compression depth for adult patients in the "Recommended Compression Depth Adult" field.
6. For "Treatment for Unstable Bradycardia," select the treatment you would administer for a heart rate that is too slow with symptoms.
7. Rate your confidence in leading a code (Mega Code) and interpreting EKG changes in the "Confidence Leading a Mega Code" and "Confidence in EKG Interpretation" fields, respectively.
8. Evaluate the quality of simulation training and areas within ACLS that require more review in the "Quality of Simulation Training" and "Areas within ACLS Requiring More Review" fields.
9. Choose your preferred renewal format for ACLS certification.
10. Finally, provide any additional comments on how to improve the training process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinician Name** (`clinician_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name in this field as it will appear on the certification badge.
* **Professional Role** (`professional_role`, `select_one`, required): Select your profession from the list (e.g., MD and DO, RN, Paramedic, Respiratory Therapist).
* **ACLS Certification Expiration Date** (`acls_certification_expiration_date`, `date`, required): Enter your current expiration date for your ACLS certification.
* **First Line Medication for VF and pVT** (`first_line_medication_for_vf_pvt`, `select_one`, required): Select the medication you would administer in a ventricular fibrillation emergency.
* **Recommended Compression Depth Adult** (`recommended_compression_depth_adult`, `select_one`, required): Select the recommended compression depth for adult patients.
* **Treatment for Unstable Bradycardia** (`treatment_for_unstable_bradycardia`, `select_one`, required): Select the treatment you would administer for a heart rate that is too slow with symptoms.
* **Confidence Leading a Mega Code** (`confidence_leading_a_mega_code`, `select_one`, required): Rate your confidence in leading a code with the provided options.
* **Confidence in EKG Interpretation** (`confidence_in_egk_interpretation`, `select_one`, required): Rate your confidence in EKG interpretation with the provided options.
* **Quality of Simulation Training** (`quality_of_simulation_training`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the quality of simulation training with the provided options.
* **Areas within ACLS Requiring More Review** (`areas_within_acls_requiring_more_review`, `text`, required): Provide text describing areas within ACLS that require more review.
* **Preferred Renewal Format** (`preferred_renewal_format`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred renewal format for ACLS certification.
* **Additional Comments on Training Improvement** (`additional_comments_on_training_improvement`, `text`, required): Provide comments on how to improve the training process.
