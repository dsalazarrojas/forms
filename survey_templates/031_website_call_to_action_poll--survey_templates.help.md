# Website Call To Action Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your experience with our website's call-to-action features. We value your input and want to ensure that we are meeting your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your honest feedback.
2. Select "Submit" to submit your responses.
3. Be as detailed as possible, especially on the "Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (Intro Page)**: This is a placeholder title for the form. Please ignore and skip it.
* **What do you like most about our website? (question_page)**: Select all that apply from the given options. You can choose multiple options.
	+ 'Yes' if you agree with the option
* **Rate the website? (rating_page)**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **What can we do better? (feedback_page)**: Provide your thoughts and suggestions on how we can improve the website.
* **Your website URL (website_url)**: Enter the URL of your website for our records.
* **Your Contact Info (contact_info)**: Enter your contact information for our reference.
* **Email (email_page)**: Enter your email address to be contacted for follow-up questions or feedback.
* **Name (name_page)**: Enter your name for our records.
* **Comment (comment_page)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
* **Confirm Email (email_confirmation)**: Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
