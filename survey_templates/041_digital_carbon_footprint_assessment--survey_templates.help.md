# Digital Carbon Footprint Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to assess an individual's digital carbon footprint, helping them understand their impact on the environment and providing insights for improving their sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your organization name, school name, and team name (if applicable).
2. Select your team type (School, Organization, Personal).
3. Provide your email address, phone number, and start and end dates of digital activities.
4. Enter your average daily time spent online and the average monthly data usage.
5. Estimate your monthly carbon footprint in kg CO2e.
6. Choose strategies you use to reduce carbon footprint (Reduce energy consumption, Use renewable energy, Invest in carbon offsetting, or Other).
7. Select your current sustainability status and future goals.
8. List the measures you take to reduce carbon footprint.
9. Provide your estimated reduction of carbon footprint.
10. Answer questions about carbon offsetting, such as whether you invest in it and how often you buy offsetting credits.
11. Enter the amount you allocate for carbon offsetting.
12. Provide the amount invested in carbon offsetting.
13. If applicable, describe other offsetting information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the name of your Organization?** (`organization_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **What is the name of your School?** (`school_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **What is the name of your Team?** (`team_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your team.
* **What is your Team Type (School/Organization/Personal)?** (`team_type`, select_one, Optional): Select your team type.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Start Date of Digital Activities** (`start_date`, date, Optional): Enter the start date of your digital activities (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **End Date of Digital Activities** (`end_date`, date, Optional): Enter the end date of your digital activities (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Average Daily Time Spent Online** (`average_daily_time_spent_online`, number, Optional): Enter the average daily time spent online.
* **What activities do you perform online?** (`online_activities`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the online activities you perform (Gaming, Social Media, Online Shopping, Streaming, Browsing, or Other).
* **Which social media platforms do you use?** (`social_media_platforms`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the social media platforms you use (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, YouTube, or Other).
* **Average Monthly Data Usage (GB)** (`average_monthly_data_usage`, number, Optional): Enter the average monthly data usage.
* **What is your estimated monthly carbon footprint (in kg CO2e)?** (`carbon_emissions`, number, Optional): Estimate your monthly carbon footprint.
* **What strategies are you using to reduce carbon footprint?** (`reduction_strategies`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the strategies you use to reduce carbon footprint (Reduce energy consumption, Use renewable energy, Invest in carbon offsetting, or Other).
* **What is your current sustainability status?** (`current_sustainability_status`, select_one, Optional): Select your current sustainability status (Not started, Started but not yet fully engaged, Fully engaged, or Other).
* **What are your future goals regarding sustainability?** (`future_goals`, select_multiple, Optional): Select your future goals regarding sustainability (Reduce energy consumption, Use renewable energy, Invest in carbon offsetting, or Other).
* **What measures do you take to reduce carbon footprint?** (`sustainability_measures`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the measures you take to reduce carbon footprint (Reduce energy consumption, Use renewable energy, Invest in carbon offsetting, or Other).
* **What is your estimated reduction of carbon footprint?** (`carbon_footprint_reduction_percentage`, number, Optional): Enter the estimated reduction of carbon footprint.
* **Do you invest in carbon offsetting?** (`carbon_offsetting`, select_one, Optional): Select whether you invest in carbon offsetting (True or False).
* **What measures do you take to improve your sustainability?** (`sustainability_measures`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the measures you take to improve your sustainability (Reduce energy consumption, Use renewable energy, Invest in carbon offsetting, or Other).
* **Do you buy carbon offsetting credits?** (`carbon_credit_offsetting`, select_one, Optional): Select whether you buy carbon offsetting credits (True or False).
* **How often do you buy carbon offsetting credits?** (`offsetting_credits`, select_multiple, Optional): Select how often you buy carbon offsetting credits (Frequently, Periodically, Rarely, or Never).
* **How much money do you allocate for carbon offsetting?** (`budget_for_offsetting`, number, Optional): Enter the amount allocated for carbon offsetting.
* **What is the amount invested in carbon offsetting?** (`carbon_offsetting_investment`, number, Optional): Enter the amount invested in carbon offsetting.
* **Other offsetting information** (`other_offsetting`, text, Optional): Describe other offsetting information.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
* Take the form as a starting point to identify areas for improvement.
* Use the insights gained from the form to implement sustainable practices.
