# Daily Gratitude Journal - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Gratitude Journal is designed to help you reflect on the things you are grateful for each day. It's a simple way to cultivate a positive mindset by acknowledging and appreciating the good things that happen in your life. By filling out this form, you can help yourself develop a greater sense of gratitude and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Daily Gratitude Journal, follow these steps:
1. Enter the date of the day that you want to journal.
2. Select the entries that you are grateful for by checking the boxes next to them.
3. For each selected entry, write down a brief description of why you are grateful for it.
4. Repeat the process for each selected entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **date** (required): Enter the date of the day that you want to journal.
* **list_of_gratitude_entries** (required): Select the entries that you are grateful for by checking the boxes next to them. You can select multiple entries.
	+ **Yes**: I am grateful for this entry.
	+ **No**: I am not grateful for this entry.
* **entry_1** (optional): Write down a brief description of why you are grateful for the selected **Yes** entry 1.
* **entry_2** (optional): Write down a brief description of why you are grateful for the selected **Yes** entry 2.
* **daily_gratitude_journal_form** (optional): This is a required field, but it's not necessary to fill in, as it's just the title of the journal.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the entries that you are truly grateful for, and be specific about why you are grateful for them.
* Don't overthink it, just be honest and genuine in your reflections.
* Remember that it's okay if you don't have anything to be grateful for, and it's all about progress, not perfection.
