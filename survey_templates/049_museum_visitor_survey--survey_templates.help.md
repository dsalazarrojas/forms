# Museum Visitor Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Museum Visitor Survey is a form designed to gather information from visitors to the museum about their experiences and preferences. This form is used to collect feedback, suggestions, and contact information from visitors to help the museum improve its services and offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and questions carefully before starting.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. For the "Feedback" section, select all that apply.
4. Provide as much detail as possible in the "Contact Info" section.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome Page** (Welcome Page, text, optional):
	+ This is the introduction page where you will be asked to provide some basic information about your visit.
* **Visitor Info** (Visitor Info, text, optional):
	+ Please provide your name, email address, and any other contact information you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, select multiple, required):
	+ Choose all that apply from the following options:
		- Option 1: Yes, I enjoyed my visit.
		- Option 2: No, I did not enjoy my visit.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, optional):
	+ If you'd like to be contacted about any future exhibitions or events, please provide your email address or other contact information here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all required questions.
* If you need help or have any questions, do not hesitate to ask the museum staff.
* Thank you for taking the time to participate in this survey! Your feedback is greatly appreciated.
