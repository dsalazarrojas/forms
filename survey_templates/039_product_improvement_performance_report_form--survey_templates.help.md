# product_improvement_performance_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The product improvement performance report form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from customers to enhance the performance and features of a product. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide detailed feedback to help us improve.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your company and contact information in the designated fields.
2. Rate the product's quality, ease of use, and overall satisfaction.
3. Provide feedback and suggestions for improvement.
4. Select any additional features you would like to see in future products.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Product Improvement Performance Report Form**: This section is just an introduction to the form.
* **Company Name**: Please enter your company name where you are using our product.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the person filling out the form.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for any follow-up questions or feedback.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you are using.
* **Quality (1-5)**: Rate the quality of the product. The higher the number, the better the product quality.
* **Ease of Use (1-5)**: Rate how easy or difficult it was to use the product. The higher the number, the easier it was to use.
* **Overall Satisfaction (1-5)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the product. The higher the number, the more satisfied you are.
* **Feedback**: Provide detailed feedback about your experience with the product. This is your chance to tell us about any issues or suggestions.
* **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions for future products or improvements.
* **What features would you like to see in our products**: Select any additional features you would like to see in future products. You can choose multiple features. 

Note: The numbers 1-5 for quality, ease of use, and overall satisfaction are subjective and based on your personal experience. Please rate them according to your perception.
