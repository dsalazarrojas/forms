# Hospital Community Outreach Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Hospital Community Outreach Perception Survey" form aims to gather feedback from visitors about their experience with the services offered by the hospital. This form helps us understand what services are most appreciated, what areas need improvement, and how we can better serve our visitors in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form**: Answer each question honestly, and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
2. **Select the correct answer options**: For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best reflects your opinion or experience.
3. **Enter your contact information**: Provide your email address and phone number so we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What services do we offer?**: Choose all the services that you believe the hospital offers. 
	+ Option 1: Emergency Services
	+ Option 2: Outpatient Services
	+ Option 3: Inpatient Services
* **What do you think about these services?**: Share your thoughts about the services you selected above.
* **How often do you visit the hospital?**: Choose how often you visit the hospital.
* **How do you rate the cleanliness of the hospital?**: Rate the cleanliness of the hospital on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **How do you rate the quality of care?**: Rate the quality of care on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **How often do you visit the hospital?**: Choose how often you visit the hospital.
	+ Option 1: Daily
	+ Option 2: Weekly
	+ Option 3: Monthly
* **What time of day do you usually visit the hospital?**: Choose the time of day you usually visit the hospital.
* **What is your email address?**: Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
* **What is your phone number?**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Any additional comments?**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the hospital.
