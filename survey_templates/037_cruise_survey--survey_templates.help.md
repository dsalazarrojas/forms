**Purpose:** This form is designed to collect information about passengers and their travel details for a cruise survey. The survey is intended to gather data on passenger demographics, travel plans, and other relevant information.

**How To Complete This Form:**

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your passenger's name in the "Passenger Name" field.
2. Enter the departure port in the "Departure Port" field.
3. Enter the arrival port in the "Arrival Port" field.
4. Enter the cruise line name in the "Cruise Line" field.
5. Select a departure date in the "Departure Date" field.
6. Select the departure time in the "Departure Time" field.
7. Select the arrival date in the "Arrival Date" field.
8. Select the arrival time in the "Arrival Time" field.
9. Choose whether the passenger is a "Yes" or "No" answer for "Passenger Type".
10. Select one of the options from the list for "Passenger Type Options" (e.g., Senior, Student, Infant).
11. Enter any additional cabin information in the "Cabin Info" field.
12. Select the travel type (Single, Couple, Group) for "Travel Type".
13. Enter the travel agent's name in the "Travel Agent" field.
14. Enter the passenger's email address in the "Email" field.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

* **Passenger Name**: Enter the name of the passenger you are submitting the survey for.
* **Departure Port**: Enter the name of the port from which the passenger will be departing.
* **Arrival Port**: Enter the name of the port at which the passenger will be arriving.
* **Cruise Line**: Enter the name of the cruise line the passenger will be traveling with.
* **Departure Date**: Select the date on which the passenger will depart.
* **Departure Time**: Select the time of departure.
* **Arrival Date**: Select the date on which the passenger will arrive.
* **Arrival Time**: Select the time of arrival.
* **Passenger Type**: Choose "Yes" if the passenger is a certain type or "No" otherwise.
* **Passenger Type Options**: Select one of the available options for passenger type.
* **Cabin Info**: Enter any additional information about the passenger's cabin.
* **Travel Type**: Select the type of travel (Single, Couple, Group).
* **Travel Agent**: Enter the name of the travel agent.
* **Email**: Enter the passenger's email address.

**Tips:**
