# Favorite Color Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your favorite colors. Your participation will help the team understand community preferences and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the form completely and honestly.
- Select your favorite color from the options provided.
- For the remaining questions, answer with a simple 'True' or 'False' for yes or no questions, and select one or more colors for multiple choice questions.
- Make sure to select all options that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your favorite color?** (text)
  This is a text field where you can type in your favorite color.
- **Select your favorite color** (select_one)
  Choose one color from the list that you like the most.
- **Do you like bright colors?** (checkbox)
  Answer with 'True' if you like bright colors, 'False' if not.
- **What is your favorite shade of your favorite color?** (select_one)
  Choose one shade that you like in your favorite color.
- **Do you prefer warm or cool colors?** (select_one)
  Answer with 'Warm' if you like warm colors, 'Cool' if not.
- **Select one or more colors** (select_multiple)
  Choose one or more colors that you like.
- **Do you like colors with high saturation?** (checkbox)
  Answer with 'True' if you like bright and saturated colors, 'False' if not.
- **Select one or more colors from the palette** (select_multiple)
  Choose one or more colors from the palette provided.
- **Do you like colors with low saturation?** (checkbox)
  Answer with 'True' if you like dull and less saturated colors, 'False' if not.
- **Select one or more colors from the range** (select_multiple)
  Choose one or more colors from the range provided.
