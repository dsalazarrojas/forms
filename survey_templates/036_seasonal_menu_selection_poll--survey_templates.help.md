# Seasonal Menu Selection Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help businesses understand customer preferences for seasonal menu items. The goal is to gather information on users' favorite seasonal menu items, their preferred cooking methods, dietary restrictions, and other preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferences for each question. Make sure to choose only one option for each question.
2. Fill out each page according to the instructions below.
