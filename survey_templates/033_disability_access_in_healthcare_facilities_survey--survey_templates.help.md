# disability_access_in_healthcare_facilities_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "disability_access_in_healthcare_facilities_survey" form is designed to collect information on the accessibility of healthcare facilities. This form is used to identify areas for improvement and ensure equal access to care for individuals with disabilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the facility type from the list provided.
2. Fill in the facility name and building address as accurately as possible.
3. Check all the accessibility features that apply to the facility from the list provided.
4. If there are accessibility problems found, check all the problems that apply and provide a brief description of each problem.
5. Provide suggestions for improvement, if any.
6. Fill in the contact information of the person completing the survey, including name, email, phone number, contact organization, title, department, and comments.
7. Finally, provide the facility location and contact position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Type** (`id: 2`, `label: Facility Type`): Select the type of facility you are completing the survey for (Hospital, Clinic, or Other).
* **Facility Name** (`id: 3`, `label: Facility Name`): Enter the name of the facility as accurately as possible.
* **Building Address** (`id: 4`, `label: Building Address`): Enter the address of the facility building.
* **Building Accessibility Features** (`id: 5`, `label: Building Accessibility Features`): Check all the features that apply to the facility, such as wheelchair accessible, automatic doors, wide doorways, clear signage, assistive technology, accessible parking, accessible restrooms, and accessible entrance.
* **Accessibility Problems Found** (`id: 6`, `label: Accessibility Problems Found`): Check all the problems that apply, and provide a brief description of each problem.
* **Accessibility Problems Description** (`id: 7`, `label: Accessibility Problems Description`): Provide a brief description of each problem you checked in the previous field.
* **Suggestions** (`id: 8`, `label: Suggestions`): Provide any suggestions for improvement for the facility.
* **Survey Completed By** (`id: 9`, `label: Survey Completed By`): Enter the name of the person completing the survey.
* **Date Survey Completed** (`id: 10`, `label: Date Survey Completed`): Enter the date the survey was completed.
* **Time Survey Completed** (`id: 11`, `label: Time Survey Completed`): Enter the time the survey was completed.
* **Contact Name** (`id: 12`, `label: Contact Name`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 13`, `label: Contact Email`): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 14`, `label: Contact Phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Organization** (`id: 15`, `label: Contact Organization`): Enter the organization of the contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`id: 16`, `label: Contact Title`): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Department** (`id: 17`, `label: Contact Department`): Enter the department of the contact person.
* **Contact Comments** (`id: 18`, `label: Contact Comments`): Enter any additional comments from the contact person.
* **Contact Signature** (`id: 19`, `label: Contact Signature`): Enter the signature of the contact person.
* **Other Organization** (`id: 20`, `label: Other Organization`): If the contact person is not affiliated with a listed organization, enter the name of their organization.
* **Other** (`id: 21`, `label: Other`): Enter any additional information.
* **Facility Location** (`id: 22`, `label: Facility Location`): Enter the location of the facility.
* **Contact Position** (`id: 23`, `label: Contact Position`): Enter the position of the contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`id: 24`, `label: Contact Title`): Enter the title of the contact person.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unable to complete a field, please leave it blank.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or the contact person for guidance.
* This form is intended for healthcare facilities only. If you are not affiliated with a healthcare facility, please do not complete this form.
