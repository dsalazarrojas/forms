# Log Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experience with log management tools and practices. It's meant to be a general assessment of your needs and concerns regarding log management.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on "Submit" when you're finished with the survey.
2.  Answer each question to the best of your ability, and be as specific as you'd like.
3.  If a question is not applicable, just skip it or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in IT?** (`role`, `select_one`, required/false): This question helps us understand who might be using log management tools and in what capacity. Please select the option that best describes your role.
* **How many team members do you have?** (`team_size`, `select_multiple`, required/false): We want to know more about the scale of your team to better understand log management needs. Choose all that apply from the provided options.
* **Ease of data collection in your log management tool** (`log_data`, `number`, required/false): This question aims to evaluate your experience with data collection from your log management tool. Rate the ease of data collection from 0 to 10, where 0 means very difficult and 10 means very easy.
* **Are you concerned about security and compliance in log management?** (`security_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required/false): We're interested in hearing about your concerns related to security and compliance in log management. Select all that apply from the provided options.
* **How often do you provide feedback on log management tools?** (`feedback_frequency`, `select_one`, required/false): This question is about how frequently you offer feedback on log management tools. Please select the option that best fits your practice.
* **Satisfaction with your log management tool** (`log_management_satisfaction`, `number`, required/false): This question is to evaluate your level of satisfaction with your current log management tool. Rate it from 0 to 10, where 0 means very unsatisfied and 10 means very satisfied.
* **Urgency for log management improvements** (`log_management_urgency`, `select_one`, required/false): We're interested in knowing how urgently you need improvements in log management. Choose the option that best describes your situation.
* **Top 3 log management improvements** (`log_management_priorities`, `select_multiple`, required/false): This question is to gather your top priorities for improvements in log management. Select all that apply from the provided options.
* **Additional comments or feedback?** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required/false): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them here. This is an optional field for you to express your thoughts.

## Tips
* Please be honest and specific in your answers.
* If you're unsure about any question, you can leave it blank.
* We appreciate your time and feedback!
