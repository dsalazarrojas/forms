# Zoo Member Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Zoo Member Experience Survey" is a form designed to gather feedback from visitors about their experience at the zoo. The survey helps us understand what you enjoyed, what you didn't enjoy, and what we can improve to provide better services in the future. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and share your thoughts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Rate your overall experience on the scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
3. Choose how satisfied you are with your visit from the options provided.
4. Enter your email address (if you want to receive feedback responses) or leave it blank.
5. Provide any additional feedback you'd like to share.
6. Select the time of your visit.
7. Choose a category related to your visit.
8. Leave comments about your experience.
9. Select options for two statements (you can select multiple options for each statement).
10. Optionally, select options for two more statements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`zoo_member_experience_survey_1_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Rate your overall experience** (`zoo_member_experience_survey_2_rate`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **How satisfied are you with your visit?** (`zoo_member_experience_survey_3_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Choose how satisfied you are with your visit.
* **Email (optional)** (`zoo_member_experience_survey_4_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive feedback responses.
* **Additional feedback** (`zoo_member_experience_survey_5_feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any additional thoughts about your experience.
* **Visit Time** (`zoo_member_experience_survey_6_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your visit in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Category** (`zoo_member_experience_survey_7_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose a category related to your visit.
* **Comments** (`zoo_member_experience_survey_8_comments`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts about your experience.
* **Do you agree with the following statement?** (`zoo_member_experience_survey_9_agree`, `select_multiple`, required): Select options for two statements.
* **Do you disagree with the following statement?** (`zoo_member_experience_survey_10_disagree`, `select_multiple`, optional): Optionally, select options for two more statements.
* **Zoo Member Experience Survey 11 Agree 2** (`zoo_member_experience_survey_11_agree_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Optionally, select options for another statement.
* **Zoo Member Experience Survey 12 Disagree 2** (`zoo_member_experience_survey_12_disagree_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Optionally, select options for another statement.

## Tips
* Please be honest and sincere in your answers, as your feedback is crucial to helping us improve.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your time and feedback.
