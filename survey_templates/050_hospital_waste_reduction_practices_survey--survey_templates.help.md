# Hospital Waste Reduction Practices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about hospital waste reduction practices, allowing us to better understand how healthcare facilities are managing waste generation, types, and reduction efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Introduction**: Please provide a brief introduction to your hospital's waste reduction practices, if applicable.
2. **Hospital Information**: Enter a short text description of your hospital's waste management practices and policies.
3. **Waste Stream**: Select all relevant waste streams that are present in your hospital (e.g., Healthcare, Non-healthcare, Other).
4. **Waste Types**: Choose the type(s) of waste your hospital is managing (e.g., Non-hazardous, Hazardous, Unknown).
5. **Waste Quantities**: Enter the estimated quantities of waste generated in your hospital, if applicable.
6. **Reduction Efforts**: Describe the waste reduction efforts your hospital is implementing or has implemented.
7. **Barriers**: Identify any barriers or challenges that hinder your hospital's waste reduction efforts.
8. **Staff Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions or ideas staff members have for improving waste reduction practices.
9. **Data-Driven Planning**: Share any data or metrics that inform your hospital's waste reduction planning and decision-making.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hospital Information**: This field is for you to describe your hospital's waste management practices and policies in a brief text.
* **Waste Stream**: This field allows you to select all relevant waste streams present in your hospital, such as Healthcare and Non-healthcare. Please choose all that apply.
* **Waste Types**: In this field, select the type(s) of waste your hospital is managing, like Non-hazardous or Hazardous. You can select one or multiple options.
* **Waste Quantities**: If you can, please enter the estimated quantities of waste generated in your hospital. This information will help us better understand your hospital's waste generation.
* **Reduction Efforts**: This field is for you to describe the waste reduction efforts your hospital is implementing or has implemented, such as recycling or composting.
* **Barriers**: Identify any challenges or barriers that hinder your hospital's waste reduction efforts, such as lack of resources or education.
* **Staff Suggestions**: This field is for you to provide any suggestions or ideas staff members have for improving waste reduction practices.
* **Data-Driven Planning**: Share any data or metrics that inform your hospital's waste reduction planning and decision-making, such as waste audits or surveys.

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing accurate and detailed information will help us better understand your hospital's waste reduction practices.
