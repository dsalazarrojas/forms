# Mobile Health App Usability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the usability of a mobile health app from end-users. The goal is to understand their overall satisfaction with the app, identify any issues they have encountered, and gather suggestions for new features.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant options that best describe your experience with the app.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Ensure that you enter your email address and phone number (if applicable) in the correct format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### App Navigation (1)
* **App Navigation** (`text`, optional): Describe any issues you have with navigating the app, such as difficulties finding certain features or menus.

### Satisfaction Rating (2)
* **How satisfied are you with this app?** (`select_one`, optional): Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the app:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Not at All Satisfied

### Issues (3)
* **Issues** (`text`, optional): If you have encountered any issues while using the app, describe them here.

### Feature Requests (4)
* **What features would you like to see added?** (`text`, optional): Suggest any features you would like to see included in the app.

### Additional Comments (5)
* **Additional Comments** (`text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions about the app, enter them here.

### Contact Information (6)
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion or to follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable) so we can contact you for further discussion or to follow up on your feedback.

### Last Use (8, 9)
* **When was the last time you used this app?** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of your last use of the app.
* **Time of last use** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of your last use of the app.

### Recommendation and Usability (10, 11)
* **How likely are you to recommend this app?** (`select_one`, optional): Select the option that best describes your likelihood to recommend the app:
	+ Very Likely
	+ Somewhat Likely
	+ Not Very Likely
	+ Not at All Likely
* **How would you rate the overall usability of this app?** (`select_one`, optional): Select the option that best describes your rating of the app's overall usability:
	+ Very Usable
	+ Somewhat Usable
	+ Not Very Usable
	+ Not at All Usable
