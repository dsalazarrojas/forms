# Healthcare Facility Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare facility feedback poll is a tool to gather information about your experience with our healthcare facility. Your input will help us understand our strengths and weaknesses and make informed decisions to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review our introduction to understand the purpose and scope of this feedback poll.
2. Provide your facility name in the field below.
3. Rate your overall experience with the facility (1-5).
4. Suggest any improvements or suggestions for the facility.
5. Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates on our progress).
6. Provide your phone number (optional).
7. Share any comments or additional feedback you may have.
8. Click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1): A brief introduction to the purpose and scope of this feedback poll.
* **Facility Name** (2): Enter your healthcare facility's name to help us understand your feedback.
* **Rate** (3): Rate your overall experience with the facility from 1 to 5.
* **Suggest** (4): Provide any suggestions or improvements you think we can implement.
* **Email** (5): Enter your email address to receive updates on our progress or for us to contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (6): Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted directly.
* **Comment** (7): Share any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Submit** (8): Click to send your feedback to us.
* **Date** (9): The date of your visit or interaction with the facility.
* **Time** (10): The time of your visit or interaction with the facility.

Note: All fields are optional, but the more information you provide, the more valuable your feedback will be to us.
