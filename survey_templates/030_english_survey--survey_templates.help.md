# English Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The English Survey form is a tool used to collect information about your experience with the English language. This data is used to gather insights and statistics to better understand your needs and preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Review the form to ensure you understand each field.
2. Enter your name in the "english_survey_name" field.
3. Enter your age in the "english_survey_age" field.
4. Select your gender from the "english_survey_gender" dropdown list.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
7. Review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **English Survey Name (english_survey_name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Age (english_survey_age)**: Enter your age in years.
* **Gender (english_survey_gender)**: Select your gender from the list: Male, Female, Other.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
 
## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure accurate data collection.
* Do not submit duplicate or incomplete forms, as this may lead to inaccurate results.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions on improving this form, please let us know.
