# E Commerce Shopping Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their shopping experience on our e-commerce platform. By providing your feedback, you are helping us improve the quality of our service and better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the order or customer you are providing feedback for.
2. Fill in the order details, product details, and purchase date.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the product or service.
4. Share your comments or suggestions.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for us to contact you (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (1)**: Briefly describe the order you are providing feedback for, including order number, date, and any relevant details.
* **Product Details (2)**: Provide information about the product you purchased, including its name, description, and any issues you encountered.
* **Purchase Date (3)**: Enter the date you made the purchase.
* **Rating (4)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the product or service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Satisfaction (5)**: Share your level of satisfaction with the product or service.
* **Comment (6)**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about your shopping experience.
* **Prophets (7)**: We made a typo in the field label; please ignore this field and feel free to enter anything you like (it will not be used for any analysis).
* **Email (8)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback (optional).
* **Phone (9)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your feedback (optional).

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Use clear and concise language to help us understand your concerns.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the contact information provided.
