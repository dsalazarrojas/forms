# Convention Evaluation Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback on the convention you attended. Your input will help us improve future conventions and provide a better experience for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. For each question, select the answer that best reflects your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Optional - your name (if you'd like to share it with us).
* **Email Address**: For follow-up if needed (we won't use it for marketing purposes).
* **Organization**: Company or institution you represent or affiliated with.
* **Convention Name**: Which convention did you attend (e.g., "Spring Fling" or "Summer Bash").
* **Convention Date**: When was the convention (e.g., "March 12-14, 2023").
* **Type of Attendance**: How did you attend the convention (e.g., "In Person - Full Event", "In Person - Partial", "Virtual", or "Hybrid").
* **Days Attended**: Number of days you attended (optional, but helpful for planning).
* **Quality of Sessions**: How would you rate the quality of the sessions (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Quality of Speakers**: How would you rate the quality of the speakers (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Relevance of Content**: How relevant was the content to your needs (Very Relevant, Relevant, Neutral, Not Very Relevant, or Not Relevant).
* **Quality of Provided Materials**: How would you rate the quality of the materials (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Registration Process**: How would you rate the registration process (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Venue Quality**: How would you rate the venue quality (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Logistics and Organization**: How would you rate the logistics and organization (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Staff Helpfulness**: How helpful were the staff (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Networking Opportunities**: How would you rate the networking opportunities (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Exhibition or Vendor Area Quality**: How would you rate the quality of the exhibition or vendor area (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor) (optional, only if applicable).
* **Value for Money**: How would you rate the value for the money you spent (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Expectations Met**: Were your expectations met (Exceeded, Met, Partially Met, Not Met).
* **Knowledge Gained**: How much knowledge did you gain (A Great Deal, A Lot, Moderate, Little, or None).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with the convention (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied).
* **Would Recommend**: Would you recommend this convention (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, or Definitely No).
* **Attend Again Next Year**: Would you attend again next year (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, or Definitely No).
* **Best Aspect**: What was the best aspect of the convention (text field).
* **Areas for Improvement**: What could be improved (text field).
* **Topics for Future Conventions**: What topics you would like to see in future conventions (text field).
* **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback or suggestions (text field).

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Select the answer that best reflects your experience.
* Use the "Additional Comments" field to provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
