# Challenge Completion Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Challenge Completion Report form is used to collect information from participants about their completion of a challenge, including the date of completion, participant details, and their experience with the challenge.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct challenge for which you are reporting completion.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the form.
3. Ensure that the participant details are accurate and up-to-date.
4. Provide clear and concise answers to the questions.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date (*)**: The date on which you are submitting this challenge completion report.
* **Participant Name (*)**: The full name of the participant who completed the challenge.
* **Email Address (*)**: The email address of the participant for confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Optional. If provided, it will be used for follow-up contact.
* **Challenge Name (*)**: The name of the challenge completed.
* **Challenge Type (*)**: The category of the challenge (e.g., Fitness, Weight loss, etc.).
* **Challenge Start Date (*)**: The date on which the participant started the challenge.
* **Challenge Completion Date (*)**: The date on which the participant completed the challenge.
* **Challenge Duration (*)**: The duration of the challenge (e.g., 7 days, 14 days, etc.).
* **Completion Status (*)**: The status of the participant's completion (e.g., Fully completed, Partially completed, etc.).
* **Goals Achieved**: The level of achievement of the participant's goals during the challenge.
* **Evidence Submitted**: Whether the participant has submitted evidence of completion.
* **Evidence Type**: The type of evidence submitted (e.g., Photos, Videos, etc.).
* **Final Results**: A description of the participant's final results.
* **Obstacles Faced**: Any challenges or obstacles faced by the participant during the challenge.
* **Lessons Learned**: Any lessons or takeaways from the challenge.
* **Overall Experience**: The participant's overall experience with the challenge (e.g., Excellent, Very good, etc.).
* **Would Participate Again**: Whether the participant would join another challenge.
* **Recommend to Others**: Whether the participant would recommend the challenge.
* **Favorite Aspect**: The participant's favorite part or aspect of the challenge.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: Any suggestions for improvement for the challenge.
* **Testimonial**: A testimonial or feedback from the participant.
* **Participant Signature (*)**: The signature of the participant, confirming their completion of the challenge.

Please note that fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required fields.
