# Synthetic Food Ingredients Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants about their knowledge and experience with synthetic food ingredients. The goal is to assess their understanding of various synthetic additives, food colorants, and preservatives commonly found in food products. By completing this form, you will help us improve our knowledge base and develop more effective education and outreach materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the participant information page, where you will be required to provide basic details about yourself.
2. Proceed to the synthetic additive knowledge page, where you will choose all that apply from the list of options regarding artificial sweeteners, flavor enhancers, food colorants, and preservatives.
3. On the synthetic food items page, select all the items that you are familiar with or have used in the past.
4. Optionally, you can add additional comments or feedback on the comments page.
5. Finally, submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info (Participant Info)**: This field collects participant information, such as name, email, or other identifying details that will help us identify and contact you for follow-up or to verify your responses.
* **Synthetic Additive Knowledge**: This field allows you to demonstrate your knowledge of various synthetic food additives. Select "I don’t know" if you are unsure of any of the options.
* **Synthetic Food Items**: This field is used to identify synthetic food items you are familiar with or have used. Select all that apply from the list of options.
* **Comments**: This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback you may have about the quiz or the form. You can write as much or as little as you like.
* **Submit**: This is the final step to submit your responses. Make sure to review your answers carefully before clicking submit.
