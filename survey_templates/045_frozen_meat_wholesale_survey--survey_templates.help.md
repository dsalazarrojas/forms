# Frozen Meat Wholesale Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from buyers about their experience with the frozen meat wholesale business.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To start, select the "Buyer Feedback" field and write a brief description of your experience with the frozen meat wholesale business.
*   For the "Supplier Satisfaction" field, select all the suppliers that met your needs and expectations.
*   In the "Purchasing Priorities" field, select the most important factor for your purchasing decision.
*   If you have any other comments or information about your purchasing experience, you can type it in the "Comments" field.
*   Finally, provide your contact information (email and phone number) so we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Buyer Feedback (buyer_feedback)**: A text field where you can write a brief description of your experience with the frozen meat wholesale business.
*   **Supplier Satisfaction (supplier_satisfaction)**: A field where you can select all the suppliers that met your needs and expectations.
    *   **Beef**
    *   **Pork**
    *   **Lamb**
    *   **Poultry**
    *   **Seafood**
    *   **Other**: Select "Other" if none of the above options apply.
*   **Purchasing Priorities (purchasing_priorities)**: A field where you can select the most important factor for your purchasing decision.
    *   **Price**
    *   **Quality**
    *   **Quantity**
    *   **Delivery Time**
    *   **Other**: Select "Other" if none of the above options apply.
*   **Food Service Purchasing (food_service_purchasing)**: A numerical field where you can enter the average amount of money you spend on frozen meat products per week.
*   **Retail Purchasing (retail_purchasing)**: A field where you can select the most important factor for your retail purchasing decision.
    *   **Price**
    *   **Quality**
    *   **Quantity**
    *   **Other**: Select "Other" if none of the above options apply.
*   **Frozen Product (frozen_product)**: A note field where you can provide any additional information about the frozen meat products.
*   **Comments (comments)**: A text field where you can type any additional comments or information about your purchasing experience.
*   **Email (email)**: A field where you can enter your email address so we can get back to you.
*   **Phone (phone)**: A field where you can enter your phone number so we can get back to you.
*   **Buyer Information (buyer_info)**: A field where you can enter any other information about yourself, such as your name and business name.

**Tips**

*   Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
*   Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our service.
