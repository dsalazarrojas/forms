# palliative_care_quality_perception_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather data about your experience with your care team and the care you receive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible. You can refer to your medical records or discuss with your care team if you are unsure about any information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`1`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing basic information about yourself, such as your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Personalized Demographics** (`2`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing more detailed information about yourself, such as your medical history, allergies, or any other relevant details.
* **Care Experience** (`3`, `text`, optional): This section is for describing your experiences with your care team, including any positive or negative interactions.
* **Care Team** (`4`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing feedback about your care team's performance, including their communication and responsiveness.
* **Pain Management** (`5`, `text`, optional): This section is for discussing your experiences with pain management, including any successes or challenges.
* **Medications** (`6`, `text`, optional): This section is for listing any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequency.
* **Family Involvement** (`7`, `text`, optional): This section is for describing your family's role in your care, including any support systems.
* **Medical Team** (`8`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing feedback about your medical team's performance, including their communication and care coordination.
* **Overall Experience** (`9`, `number`, optional): This section is for rating your overall experience with your care team on a scale of 1-10.
* **Care Environment** (`10`, `text`, optional): This section is for describing your care environment, including any comforts or challenges you face.
* **Communication** (`11`, `text`, optional): This section is for discussing your experiences with communication from your care team, including any successes or challenges.
* **Emotional Support** (`12`, `text`, optional): This section is for describing any emotional support you receive from your care team or family.
* **Care Continuity** (`13`, `text`, optional): This section is for rating your experiences with care continuity on a scale of 1-10.
* **Care Plan** (`14`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing feedback about your care plan, including any successes or challenges.
* **Patient Safety** (`15`, `text`, optional): This section is for discussing any safety concerns or incidents you have experienced.
* **Family Support** (`16`, `text`, optional): This section is for describing any support you receive from your family.
* **Staff Support** (`17`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing feedback about your care team's responsiveness and support.
* **Patient Frustration** (`18`, `text`, optional): This section is for discussing any frustrations you experience with your care.
* **Staff Frustration** (`19`, `text`, optional): This section is for discussing any frustrations you experience with staff.
* **Family Frustration** (`20`, `text`, optional): This section is for discussing any frustrations you experience with family members.
* **Staff Caregivers** (`21`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing feedback about your care team's responsiveness and support.
* **Patient Caregivers** (`22`, `text`, optional): This section is for discussing any caregivers you rely on for support.
* **Communication Quality** (`23`, `text`, optional): This section is for rating the quality of communication from your care team on a scale of 1-10.
* **Care Continuity Score** (`24`, `number`, optional): This section is for rating your experiences with care continuity on a scale of 1-10.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`25`, `number`, optional): This section is for rating your overall satisfaction with your care team on a scale of 1-10.
