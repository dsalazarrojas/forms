# Subscription Cancellation Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your reasons for cancelling your subscription, provide feedback, and schedule follow-up communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the introduction page carefully to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Select the reasons for cancellation that apply to you.
3. Enter the date and time of your cancellation.
4. Provide feedback about your experience with our service.
5. Suggest any improvements or changes you would like to see in our service.
6. If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, enter your email address and phone number.
7. Schedule follow-up communication by selecting the method and frequency of contact.
8. Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
9. Review and submit the form.

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This page provides a brief explanation of the form and its purpose.
* **Reasons Cancellation** (`reasons_cancellation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the reasons that apply to you for cancelling your subscription. You can choose one or multiple reasons.

	+ Reason 2: [Insert explanation for Reason 2]
	+ Reason 3: [Insert explanation for Reason 3]
* **Cancellation Date** (`cancellation_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your cancellation.
* **Cancellation Time** (`cancellation_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your cancellation.
* **Service Feedback** (`service_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide feedback about your experience with our service.
* **Cancellation Feedback** (`cancellation_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your cancellation experience.
* **Recommended Service** (`recommended_service`, `select_one`, required: false): Suggest any other service that you would recommend to us.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted by email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Follow up Message** (`follow_up_message`, `text`, required: false): Enter a message that we can use to contact you.
* **Follow up Time** (`follow_up_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your desired follow-up communication.
* **Follow up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, `number`, required: false): Select how frequently you would like to be contacted.
* **Follow up Method** (`follow_up_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the method of follow-up communication you would like.
* **Follow up Status** (`follow_up_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of your follow-up communication.
* **Follow up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments or questions about your follow-up communication.
* **Follow up Next Action** (`follow_up_next_action`, `select_one`, required: false): Suggest any next actions or steps for us to take.
* **Cancellation Status** (`cancellation_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of your cancellation.
* **Cancellation Reminders** (`cancellation_remarks`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any cancellation reminders you would like to receive.
* **Cancellation Next Steps** (`cancellation_next_steps`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Suggest any next steps or actions for us to take.

Note: The explanations for each reason for cancellation can be customized based on your company's policies and procedures.
