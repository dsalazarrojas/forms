# Organizational Clarity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help you provide feedback on the overall clarity of your role and responsibilities within your team. It consists of questions related to communication, understanding, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Read each question carefully and answer the options that best fit your perception.
2. Select the response that best answers each question.
3. Provide feedback and suggestions where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section is an introduction to the survey and explains its purpose.
* **How do you feel about the overall quality of communication within your team?** (`communication`, `select_one`): This question assesses your opinion on the effectiveness of team communication. Select "Very Good", "Neutral", or "Poor" based on your experience.
* **How well do you understand your role and responsibilities within the team?** (`understanding`, `select_one`): Choose the level of understanding you have about your duties within the team, from "Extremely well" to "Not at all well".
* **Do you feel that your role and responsibilities are clear?** (`clarity`, `select_one`): Answer if your role and responsibilities are clear ("Yes" or "No").
* **Are there any areas that you would like to provide feedback on?** (`feedback`, `text`): Provide any suggestions or areas where you'd like to see improvements.
* **How would you like to be updated about organizational improvements?** (`follow_up`, `select_one`): Select how frequently you would like to be updated on changes, from "Regularly" to "Never".
* **Which of the following are the most effective communication channels?** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`): Choose the channels you find most suitable for communication, such as "Email", "Phone", "Team meetings", and so on.
* **What is your preferred communication frequency for team meetings?** (`communication_frequencies`, `select_one`): Select the frequency you prefer for team meetings, from "Daily" to "Never".
