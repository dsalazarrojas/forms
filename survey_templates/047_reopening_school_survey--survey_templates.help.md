# Reopening School Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information from parents and guardians about their concerns and preferences regarding sending their children back to school. Your input will help school administrators make informed decisions about reopening and will provide valuable insight into what parents are thinking.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a * in the label).
3. Select the options that best represent your opinions and preferences.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Get parent input about whether to send their children to school** (Text field, Optional):
	+ This field is for you to briefly state your opinion on whether you would like to send your child to school.
* **Free survey for school administrators** (Select one option, Optional):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you would like school administrators to take into account your opinion on sending your child back to school.
	+ Choose 'No' if you don't want school administrators to consider your opinion.
* **Works on any device** (Select multiple options, Optional):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you would like to know how the school's systems will work on your device (phone, tablet, computer, etc.).
	+ Choose 'No' if you don't mind not knowing how the school's systems will work on your device.
* **Easy to customize and embed** (Note field, Optional):
	+ You can type any comments, questions, or concerns about how the school's systems are easy or difficult to customize and embed.
	+ This field is for your personal feedback. 

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification on any of the questions, you can contact your child's school or administrator.
* Your input will help inform school decisions, so please take a few minutes to complete this form.
