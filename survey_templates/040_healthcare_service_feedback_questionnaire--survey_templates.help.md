# Healthcare Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their healthcare services. It is used to gather information about the quality of care provided, the satisfaction of patients, and any areas for improvement. This form is intended for patients who have recently received healthcare services and can provide valuable insights for healthcare providers to enhance their services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Comments" field.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the patient who received the healthcare service.
- **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the healthcare service was provided.
- **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time on which the healthcare service was provided.
- **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the doctor who provided the healthcare service.
- **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the healthcare service. Choose from Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the healthcare service.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address, if available.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter a contact phone number, if available.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about the healthcare service.
- **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the category of the healthcare service provided. Choose from Cardiology, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, Internal Medicine, Nephrology, Neurology, or Pulmonology.
- **Doctor Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the specialty of the doctor who provided the healthcare service. Choose from Cardiology, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, Internal Medicine, Nephrology, Neurology, or Pulmonology.
