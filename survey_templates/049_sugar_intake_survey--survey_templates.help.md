# Sugar Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather information about your sugar intake habits, allowing us to better understand your sugar consumption patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. Fill in the required fields as indicated by the required label in each question.
4. Select one or multiple answers from the options provided for each question.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

	* Label: How often do you consume sugar?
	* Type: number
	* Required: false
	* Explanation: This question helps us understand how frequently you consume sugar in your daily or weekly routine.
