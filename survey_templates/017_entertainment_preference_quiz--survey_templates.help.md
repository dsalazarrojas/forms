# Entertainment Preference Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your preferences for entertainment. It is intended for entertainment industry professionals to learn about audience interests. The goal is to understand how often you enjoy different types of entertainment, such as movies, music, live events, and social media.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read the introduction page for a brief overview of the form.
2. Select your preferences for each type of entertainment.
3. Indicate how often you usually enjoy each type of entertainment.
4. Choose your favorite genres for movies, music, and live events.
5. Select your favorite social media platform.
