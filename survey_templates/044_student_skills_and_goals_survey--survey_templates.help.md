# Student Skills And Goals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Skills And Goals Survey is a self-assessment tool designed to help students identify their skills and goals. It is meant to be a personal and honest reflection of your strengths, areas of improvement, and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by reviewing the form carefully and reading each section title and label.
2.  Answer each question to the best of your ability, being honest and accurate.
3.  Use the options provided for select fields or type in your answer for text fields.
4.  If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction**: A brief introduction to the survey, explaining the purpose and instructions for completion.
*   **Student Info**: Please provide your name, grade level, and any other relevant student information. This information will not be shared publicly.
*   **Skills Survey**: This section allows you to rate your skill level in different areas:
    *   Communication
    *   Teamwork
    *   Problem Solving
    *   Time Management
    *   Adaptability
    *   Leadership
    *   Creativity
    *   Critical Thinking
    *   Innovation
*   **Learning Preferences**: Please select all the ways in which you prefer to learn:
    *   Online Learning
    *   In Person
    *   Hybrid
    *   Self-Paced
    *   Project-Based
*   **School Year Goals**: Choose one of the following areas of focus for this school year:
    *   Academic Achievement
    *   Career Development
    *   Character Education
    *   Social Responsibility
*   **Comments**: This section is an opportunity for you to provide any additional information or feedback about your skills and goals.
