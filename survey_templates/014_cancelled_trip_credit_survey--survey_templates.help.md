# Cancelled Trip Credit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from customers who have had their trips cancelled. Your responses will help us improve our cancellation process and better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your Full Name in the Full Name field.
2. Enter the email address associated with your booking in the Booking Email Address field.
3. Enter the Booking Reference Number found in your reservation confirmation.
4. Enter the destination of the trip that was cancelled in the Cancelled Destination field.
5. Enter the Original Travel Date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Provide a brief description of why your trip was cancelled in the Cancellation Details section.
7. Select who initiated the cancellation (e.g. Traveller, Travel Agency, Airline, Other).
8. Choose the main reason for the cancellation (e.g. Health Reasons, Natural Disaster, Flight Cancellation, Personal Reasons, Entry Restrictions, Other).
9. Rate your satisfaction with the credit process in the Credit and Refund Satisfaction section.
10. Rate how easy it was to receive your credit in the Ease of Credit Rating section.
11. Select whether you received the full amount of credit expected in the Full Amount Received section.
12. Provide information on your future travel plans, including when you plan to use your travel credit, in the Future Travel Plans section.
13. Select whether you would use this agency or service again in the Would You Use This Agency or Service Again section.
14. Provide feedback on the communication during the cancellation process in the Communication Feedback section.
15. Rate your overall experience with the cancellation process in the Overall Experience Rating section.
16. Enter the date you are submitting this form in the Submission Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Booking Email Address** (`booking_email`, email, required): Enter the email address you used to make your booking.
* **Booking Reference Number** (`booking_reference`, text, required): Enter the reference number found on your booking confirmation.
* **Cancelled Destination** (`cancelled_destination`, text, required): Enter the destination where you were planning to travel.
* **Original Travel Date** (`original_travel_date`, date, required): Enter the date you were originally planning to travel in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Cancellation Details** (`cancellation_details_section`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of why your trip was cancelled.
* **Who Initiated the Cancellation** (`initiator_of_cancellation`, select_one, required): Select who initiated the cancellation (e.g. Traveller, Travel Agency, Airline, Other).
* **Main Reason for Cancellation** (`primary_cancellation_reason`, select_one, required): Select the main reason for the cancellation (e.g. Health Reasons, Natural Disaster, Flight Cancellation, Personal Reasons, Entry Restrictions, Other).
* **Credit and Refund Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_section`, note, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the credit process (1-5 stars).
* **Satisfaction with the Credit Process** (`credit_process_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the credit process (e.g. Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very Satisfied).
* **How Easy Was it to Receive Credit** (`ease_of_credit_rating`, number, required): Rate how easy it was to receive credit (1-5 stars).
* **Did You Receive the Full Amount** (`full_amount_received`, select_one, required): Select whether you received the full amount of credit expected (e.g. 'True', Partial amount, 'False', Still waiting).
* **Future Travel Plans** (`future_plans_section`, note, optional): Provide information on your future travel plans, including when you plan to use your travel credit.
* **Plan to Use Travel Credit** (`credit_usage_plan`, select_one, required): Select when you plan to use your travel credit (e.g. Within 6 months, 7-12 months, Over a year, Unsure).
* **Would You Use This Agency or Service Again** (`use_service_again`, select_one, required): Select whether you would use this agency or service again (e.g. 'True', Maybe, 'False').
* **Feedback on Communication** (`communication_feedback`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the communication during the cancellation process.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (`overall_experience_rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience with the cancellation process (1-10 scale).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
