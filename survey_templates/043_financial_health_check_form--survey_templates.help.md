# Financial Health Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Health Check Form is designed to help you assess your financial health by providing an overview of your income, expenses, savings, investments, and other financial indicators. This form is meant to be a tool for personal reflection and is not intended for use in professional financial planning. Please note that this form does not provide any personal financial advice. If you are seeking professional financial advice, please consult a licensed financial advisor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in each field with the requested information. You can use numbers to input your financial data.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your financial situation or any relevant comments.
* **Income** (`income`, number, optional): Enter your total monthly income.
* **Expenses** (`expenses`, number, optional): Enter your total monthly expenses.
* **Savings** (`savings`, number, optional): Enter your total monthly savings.
* **Investments** (`investments`, number, optional): Enter the value of your investments.
* **Financial Status** (`financial_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options to describe your current financial status:
	+ Option 1: I'm financially stable
	+ Option 2: I'm struggling to make ends meet
	+ Option 3: I'm saving for a specific goal
* **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency`, text, optional): Enter how often you receive income (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly, etc.).
* **Financial Influence** (`financial_influence`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Option 1: I have a steady income
	+ Option 2: I have a variable income
	+ Option 3: I have no income
* **Savings Goal** (`savings_goal`, number, optional): Enter your target savings amount.
* **Investment Goal** (`investment_goal`, number, optional): Enter your target investment amount.
* **Investment Strategy** (`investment_strategy`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Conservative
	+ Option 2: Moderate
	+ Option 3: Aggressive
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Option 1: Low risk
	+ Option 2: Moderate risk
	+ Option 3: High risk
* **Risk Free** (`risk_free`, number, optional): Enter how much of your portfolio is risk-free (e.g., cash, bonds, etc.).

## Tips
This form is for personal reflection and is not intended for use in professional financial planning. Please consult a licensed financial advisor for personalized advice. Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form. Review each field carefully before submitting.
