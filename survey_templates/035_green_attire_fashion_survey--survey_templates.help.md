# Green Attire Fashion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand your thoughts and behaviors related to sustainable fashion. Your responses will help us better understand what sustainable fashion means to you, your daily fashion practices, and your preferences when buying clothing. Your feedback will contribute to the development of more sustainable and fashionable clothing choices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question in the space provided, and make sure to answer all questions that are marked as required.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. You may leave the space for non-required questions blank if you don't have an answer or prefer not to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What does sustainable fashion mean to you** (`green_attire_fashion_survey`): Please describe in your own words what sustainable fashion means to you. This question is not required but will help us understand your perception of sustainable fashion.

* **How do you currently practice sustainable fashion in your daily life** (`fashion_practices`): Share your current practices or habits related to sustainable fashion. This could include recycling, buying second-hand, or choosing eco-friendly clothing. 

* **What are your preferences when buying clothing** (`fashion_preferences`): Choose all that apply from the following options:
	+ Sustainable
	+ Fashionable
	+ Affordable
	+ Trendy
* This question will help us understand what matters most to you when buying clothing.

* **What is your perception of the fashion industry's impact on the environment** (`fashion_industry_impact`): Share your thoughts on how the fashion industry affects the environment.

* **What are your preferences for eco-friendly clothing options** (`consumer_preferences`): Share your preferences for clothing that is environmentally friendly.

* **Which of the following clothing types do you prefer** (`clothing_types`): Choose all that apply:
	+ T-shirt
	+ Pants
	+ Skirt
	+ Dress
	+ Jacket
* This question will help us understand your favorite clothing items.

* **How frequently do you purchase new clothing** (`purchase_frequency`): Please select one of the following:
	+ Frequently
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Never

* **Where do you usually purchase clothing** (`purchase_location`): Choose all that apply:
	+ Online
	+ In-store
	+ Through a friend

* **What is your budget for clothing** (`clothing_price`): Please select one of the following:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High

* **What size do you usually wear** (`clothing_size`): Choose all that apply:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
	+ Extra large

* **How do you usually find out about new fashion trends** (`fashion_influence`): Choose all that apply:
	+ Social media
	+ Influencers
	+ Fashion bloggers
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Friends and family

* **Do you prefer a certain brand over others** (`brand_affiliation`): Please select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not sure
