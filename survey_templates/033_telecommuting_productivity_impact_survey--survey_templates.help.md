# Telecommuting Productivity Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to understand your experience with telecommuting and its impact on your productivity, satisfaction, and work arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly, as your input will help to improve our work environment.
2. Select the option that best describes your answer for each question.
3. For questions with "select multiple" or "select one" options, you can select more than one option or select only one option respectively.
4. For open-ended questions, please provide as much detail as possible to help us better understand your thoughts and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a required field where you can write a brief introduction to this survey.
* **How much time do you work from home?** (`how_much_time`, `text`, required): This question is used to gauge your level of remote work. Answer this question by typing the number of hours you spend working from home on a typical day.
* **How much time you work from home** (`how_much_time_worked_from_home`, `number`, required): This question is to be answered with a specific numerical value, indicating the total number of hours you spend working from home on a daily basis.
* **What is your self-reported productivity level?** (`productivity`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the options that apply to your experience with telecommuting.
	+ Increased Productivity: You report being more productive when working from home.
	+ Slightly Decreased Productivity: You report being slightly less productive when working from home.
	+ No Change: Your productivity has not changed significantly when working from home.
	+ Much Decreased Productivity: You report being much less productive when working from home.
* **How satisfied are you with your telecommuting experience?** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your level of satisfaction with telecommuting.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are very satisfied with your telecommuting experience.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with your telecommuting experience.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about your telecommuting experience.
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied: You are somewhat dissatisfied with your telecommuting experience.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You are very dissatisfied with your telecommuting experience.
* **How frequent do you work from home?** (`frequency_worked_from_home`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that apply to your frequency of working from home.
	+ Always: You work from home every day.
	+ Frequently: You work from home most days, but not every day.
	+ Occasionally: You work from home some days, but not most days.
	+ Rarely: You work from home very rarely.
	+ Never: You never work from home.
* **What kind of work arrangement are you in?** (`work_arrangements`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that apply to your current work arrangement.
	+ Full Time Remote: Your job is fully remote.
	+ Part Time Remote: Your job involves part-time remote work.
	+ Hybrid Remote: Your job involves both on-site and remote work arrangements.
	+ In Office: Your job is primarily on-site.
	+ Other: Other (Please specify in the feedback field)
* **How often do you communicate with colleagues remotely?** (`communication`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that apply to your frequency of communication with colleagues while working from home.
	+ Always: You communicate with colleagues remotely every day.
	+ Frequently: You communicate with colleagues remotely most days.
	+ Occasionally: You communicate with colleagues remotely some days.
	+ Rarely: You communicate with colleagues remotely very rarely.
	+ Never: You never communicate with colleagues remotely.
* **How often do you collaborate with colleagues remotely?** (`collaboration`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that apply to your frequency of collaboration with colleagues while working from home.
	+ Always: You collaborate with colleagues remotely every day.
	+ Frequently: You collaborate with colleagues remotely most days.
	+ Occasionally: You collaborate with colleagues remotely some days.
	+ Rarely: You collaborate with colleagues remotely very rarely.
	+ Never: You never collaborate with colleagues remotely.
* **What is your feedback on Telecommuting?** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you have on telecommuting.
* **Do you have suggestions to improve Telecommuting?** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions to improve telecommuting, please provide them here.
