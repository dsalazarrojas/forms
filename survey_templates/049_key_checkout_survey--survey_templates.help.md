# Key Checkout Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Key Checkout Survey form is designed to collect feedback and information from customers after their checkout process. This form is used to gather their experiences, concerns, and suggestions, which will help us improve our services and make their future experiences more pleasant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Welcome to Key Checkout Survey**: This is an introductory page to let you know that you've reached the end of the checkout process.
2. Fill in the **Customer Info** field with a brief description of yourself.
3. Choose your preferred **Contact Method** by selecting from the options: phone, email, or address.
4. Type in your **Contact Info** if you've selected to contact you through email or address.
5. If you've selected to contact you through email, enter your email address in the **Email Info** field.
6. Leave any additional **Feedback Comments** about your experience with Key Checkout.
7. Choose how you would rate your experience by selecting one or more of the options: 5, 4, 3, 2, or 1.
8. Finally, provide any **Additional Comments** you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info** (text, required: false): A short description of yourself.
  - This field is for you to provide some context about who you are, such as your name, company, or title.
- **Contact Method** (select one, required: false): How you would like to be contacted.
  - Choose from phone, email, or address to indicate your preferred contact method.
- **Contact Info** (text, required: false): Your contact information (if selected).
  - If you've chosen to be contacted through email or address, enter your relevant information here.
- **Email Info** (email, required: false): Your email address (if selected).
  - Enter your email address if you've chosen to be contacted through email.
- **Feedback Comments** (text, required: false): Additional comments about your experience.
  - Share any thoughts or suggestions you have about our services.
- **Rating** (select multiple, required: false): Rate your experience.
  - Choose 5, 4, 3, 2, or 1 to reflect your satisfaction level with our services.
- **Additional Comments** (text, required: false): Any final thoughts or comments.
  - Share any other thoughts or comments about your experience with Key Checkout.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in as much information as you can, especially if you've chosen to be contacted through email or address.
* If you've selected to rate your experience, please choose at least one option to let us know your thoughts.
* Remember to provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve our services.

I hope this guide helps you complete the Key Checkout Survey form accurately and effectively!
