# Advocate Pre Training Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us tailor the training to your needs and interests as an advocate. It will help us understand your current experience, confidence in advocacy skills, primary goals, learning style, specific topics of interest, and any challenges you face in your role. This information will enable us to create a customized training program that addresses your needs and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the correct answers for each field, making sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Advocacy Experience** (Required) (`experience_level`, select_one): This field helps us understand your level of experience in advocacy. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ None
	+ Beginner (< 1 year)
	+ Intermediate (1-3 years)
	+ Advanced (3-5 years)
	+ Expert (5+ years)
* **Confidence in Advocacy Skills** (Required) (`confidence_level`, select_one): This field assesses your confidence in your current advocacy skills. Please rate your confidence on a scale of 1-5 (1=Low, 5=High).
	+ 1 (Low)
	+ 2
	+ 3 (Moderate)
	+ 4
	+ 5 (High)
* **Primary Goals** (Required) (`primary_goals`, select_multiple): Please select all that apply to your primary goals for this training. You can select up to three goals.
	+ Learn new strategies
	+ Network with other advocates
	+ Improve public speaking
	+ Understand policy processes
	+ Digital organizing tools
* **Preferred Learning Style** (Optional) (`learning_style`, select_one): This field helps us understand how you learn best.
	+ Visual (Charts, slides)
	+ Auditory (Listening, discussion)
	+ Kinesthetic (Hands-on activities)
	+ Reading/Writing
* **Specific Topics of Interest** (Required) (`topics_of_interest`, select_multiple): Please select up to three specific topics that interest you the most.
	+ Legislative Process
	+ Community Mapping
	+ Social Media Advocacy
	+ Fundraising
	+ Coalition Building
	+ Public Relations
* **Biggest Challenges Faced** (Optional) (`challenges`, text): If you'd like to share any challenges you face as an advocate, please describe them in this field.
* **Full Name** (Required) (`full_name`, text): Please enter your full name for proper recognition and communication.
* **Email Address** (Required) (`email`, email): Please enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your training needs and schedule.
* **Organization / Affiliation** (Optional) (`organization`, text): This field is optional, but if you're affiliated with an organization, please enter it so we can recognize your connection.

## Tips

* If you need help with any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask for clarification.
* We appreciate your input, which will help us create a more effective training program tailored to your needs!
