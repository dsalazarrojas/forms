# Breakup Recovery Reflection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you reflect on your recent breakup and identify areas where you may need support. It's a personal assessment to help you understand your emotional state, identify patterns and lessons learned, and consider your readiness to move forward.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form honestly, using your current feelings and experiences.
2. For each question, select the option that best describes your situation.
3. Use the text fields to share any additional thoughts or reflections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, Optional): Your name, for your records.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, Optional): Your email address, for follow-up resources.
* **How Long Was the Relationship** (`relationship_duration`, select_one, Required): The duration of your relationship, from Less than 3 months to 5+ years.
* **Time Since Breakup** (`time_since_breakup`, select_one, Required): How long ago your relationship ended, from Less than 1 week to 6+ months.
* **Current Emotional State** (`current_emotional_state`, select_multiple, Required): Your current emotional state, including Sad, Angry, Confused, Relieved, Anxious, Hopeful, Numb, Accepting, Lonely, Empowered.
* **Emotional Intensity** (`emotional_intensity`, select_one, Required): The intensity of your emotions, from Very Intense to Minimal.
* **Support System** (`support_system`, select_one, Required): Your current level of support, from Strong Support System to No Support.
* **Coping Mechanisms** (`coping_mechanisms`, select_multiple, Required): The ways you cope with your emotions, including Talking to Friends, Exercise, Journaling, Therapy or Counseling, Meditation, Creative Activities, Spending Time Alone, Watching Movies or TV, Reading, Other.
* **Daily Functioning** (`daily_functioning`, select_one, Required): Your daily functioning level, from Very Well to Very Poorly.
* **Sleep Patterns** (`sleep_patterns`, select_one, Required): Your current sleep patterns, from Sleeping Well to Irregular.
* **Appetite Changes** (`appetite_changes`, select_one, Required): Any changes in your eating habits, from No Change to Eating Less.
* **Lessons Learned** (`lessons_learned`, text, Required): A text field for you to reflect on what you learned from the relationship.
* **Areas for Personal Growth** (`personal_growth`, text, Required): A text field for you to reflect on areas where you'd like to work on.
* **Relationship Patterns Noticed** (`relationship_patterns`, text, Optional): A text field for you to reflect on any patterns you observed in the relationship.
* **Readiness to Date Again** (`readiness_to_date`, select_one, Required): Your current readiness to start dating, from Not Ready at All to Ready to Start.
* **Goals for Healing** (`goals_for_healing`, text, Required): A text field for you to reflect on your healing goals.
* **Interest in Professional Help** (`professional_help_interest`, select_one, Required): Your interest in seeking professional help, from Already in Therapy to Not Interested.
* **Reflection Frequency** (`reflection_frequency`, select_one, Required): How often you reflect on the relationship, from Constantly to Rarely.
* **Hope for Future Relationships** (`hope_for_future`, select_one, Required): Your current hope for future relationships, from Very Hopeful to Not Hopeful at All.
* **Additional Thoughts** (`additional_thoughts`, text, Optional): A text field for you to share any additional thoughts or reflections.

Note: Please fill out the form honestly, and remember that your responses are for your personal reflection and growth. This form is not a substitute for professional help, but rather a tool to support your personal healing and growth.
