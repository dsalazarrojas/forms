# Global Health Equity Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Global Health Equity Awareness Survey is a comprehensive form designed to collect data on global health concerns and inequities. It helps organizations and researchers to identify trends, gaps, and areas of improvement in the field of global health equity.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Follow the page navigation to complete the form.
2.  Answer each question carefully, as the data collected will be used to inform global health initiatives and research.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Global Health Topics** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_1`): This text field is used to select a specific global health topic. Enter your answer carefully, as it will help identify the focus of your survey response.
*   **Country/Region of Focus** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_2`): This dropdown menu allows you to select the country or region that you are focusing on. This information will help researchers understand the geographical context of your concerns.
*   **Age Group** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_3`): This multiple-choice field allows you to select one or more age groups that you are interested in. Select all that apply.
*   **Date of Birth** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_4`): This date field requires you to enter your date of birth. This information is required to ensure that you are eligible to participate in the survey.
*   **Time of Day** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_5`): This time field allows you to select your current time of day. This information will help researchers understand your availability and response patterns.
*   **Note** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_6`): This text field provides an opportunity for you to share any additional comments or information that you feel is relevant to your survey response.
*   **Email Address** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_7`): This email field is used to collect your contact information. If you wish to be contacted for further questions or to receive updates, please enter your email address here.
*   **Phone Number** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_8`): This text field allows you to enter your phone number. This information will be used for contact purposes only, if desired.
*   **Global Health Equity Awareness Survey** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_9`): This multiple-choice field allows you to select one or more areas of global health that you are aware of. Please choose all that apply.
*   **Survey Submission** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_10`): This text field is used to submit your survey response. Enter your response carefully, as it will be used to inform global health initiatives and research.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate in your responses, as they will be used to inform global health research and initiatives.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the asterisk (\*).
