# Personal Knowledge Generation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Personal Knowledge Generation Survey" is a form designed to collect information about your habits and preferences related to personal knowledge generation. This survey aims to gather data to help us understand how you create and maintain your knowledge, and what tools and habits are most effective for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question according to your personal experiences and preferences.
* Select only one option for questions marked as "select_one", unless otherwise specified.
* Select multiple options for questions marked as "select_multiple".
* Enter your actual age if the question asks for a numerical value.
* Use the provided date format for questions marked as "date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Survey Start
* **What is your age?** (number, optional): This question asks for your current age.
* **How do you usually start your day?** (select_one, optional):
	+ Early riser: If you wake up early every day.
	+ Late riser: If you wake up late every day.
	+ As soon as possible: If you don't have a fixed schedule.
* **How often do you work on your personal projects?** (select_multiple, optional):
	+ Rarely: If you rarely work on personal projects.
	+ Occasionally: If you sometimes work on personal projects.
	+ Often: If you frequently work on personal projects.
	+ Daily: If you work on personal projects every day.
