# Wellness Product Subscription Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help the organization understand user interests in wellness products, providing essential information to tailor subscription offerings effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select all the types of wellness products you are interested in (e.g., protein powder, collagen supplements, vitamins, etc.).
2.  Choose a price range you are willing to pay for a subscription (e.g., 0-10, 11-20, 21-50, 51-100, or Other - specify).
3.  Specify how often you would like to receive your subscription (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-monthly).
4.  Choose how often you would like to receive updates on new products (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Rarely).
5.  If you have any notes or questions about the subscription offerings, type them in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What type of wellness products are you interested in?
* **What type of wellness products are you interested in?** (`product_type`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select one or more of the following options: Protein powder, Collagen supplements, Vitamins, Essential oils, Herbal supplements.
* **What price range are you willing to pay for a subscription?** (`pricing`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose a price range from 0-10, 11-20, 21-50, 51-100, or Other (please specify).
* **How often would you like to receive your subscription?** (`frequency`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select one or more of the following options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-monthly.
* **How often would you like to receive updates on new products?** (`updates`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select one or more of the following options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Rarely.
* **Do you have any notes or questions about our subscription offerings?** (`notes`, `note`, `required`): Type any notes or questions you have about the subscription offerings in this field.
