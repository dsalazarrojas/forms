# Global Education Data Ethics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Education Data Ethics Survey is designed to collect data on the attitudes and practices of stakeholders in the education sector with regards to data management, collection, and use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the stakeholder type that best describes you (Student, Teacher, Parent, Researcher, or Policymaker).
2. Select the role(s) you play in education (Academic, Researcher, or Admin).
3. Choose your level of education (Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, or Non-School).
4. Select your level of data ethics understanding (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
5. Determine who data is collected from (Personal, Aggregate, or Both).
6. Decide how you use or intend to use data (Anonymize, De-Identify, or Identify).
7. Provide your location (if necessary).
8. Fill out your contact information (optional).
9. Provide any additional comments or observations you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stakeholder Type**: Select the type of stakeholder you are (e.g., Student, Teacher, Parent, Researcher, Policymaker).
* **Role**: Choose the roles that apply to you (e.g., Academic, Researcher, Admin).

* **Data Collected**: Determine who data is collected from (e.g., Personal, Aggregate, Both).
