# Submit Your Question For Q+A - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect questions and answers from users. When you submit a question, you will be able to select one or multiple answers that best respond to your question. The collected questions and answers will help us improve our services and better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your question in the "First Question" field.
2. If you have any email address associated with your question, please enter it in the "Email" field.
3. Choose one or multiple options that best answer your question from the "Select One" field.
4. Select multiple options that best answer your question from the "Select Multiple" field.
5. Enter the date and time you would like to be associated with your question in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. Add any additional information or comments about your question in the "Note" field.
7. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field (this will help us better address any follow-up questions or concerns).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Question** (`First Question`, `text`, required/optional): This is the main question you would like to submit.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): If you have an email address associated with your question, please enter it here. This will help us contact you for follow-up questions or clarifications.
* **Select One** (`Select One`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the option that best answers your question from the provided options.
* **Select Multiple** (`Select Multiple`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose multiple options that best answer your question from the provided options.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to be associated with your question.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to be associated with your question.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional information or comments about your question here. This is a good place to provide context or details that might be helpful to us.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required/optional): This is a required field to help us better address any follow-up questions or concerns related to your submission.
