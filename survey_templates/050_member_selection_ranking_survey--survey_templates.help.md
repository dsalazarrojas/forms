# Member Selection Ranking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form, Member Selection Ranking Survey, is designed to help assess the ranking of members of an organization. The results will be used to identify the most suitable candidates for leadership roles within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, enter the title of the survey in the `Member Selection Ranking Survey` text field.
2.  In the `Survey Description` text field, provide a brief description of the survey to help users understand the purpose and context.
3.  Choose the `Ranking Type` by selecting either `Yes` or `No` from the dropdown menu. This indicates whether the user is ranking members for leadership positions or not.
4.  If `Yes` is selected, provide an explanation for the `Other` text field if the ranking is not a standard leadership position.
5.  List the `Ranking Criteria` in the text field, providing the criteria that will be used to evaluate the members.
6.  Enter the `Ranking Weightage` as a numerical value, indicating the weightage or importance of each criterion.
7.  Enter your email address in the `Email` text field.
8.  Enter your phone number in the `Phone` text field.
9.  Lastly, enter your name in the `Name` text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Member Selection Ranking Survey**: Enter the title of the survey in this text field.
*   **Survey Description**: Provide a brief description of the survey to help users understand the purpose and context.
*   **Ranking Type**: Select either `Yes` or `No` to indicate whether the user is ranking members for leadership positions.
*   **Other**: If `Yes` is selected, enter a reason for the `Other` ranking.
*   **Ranking Criteria**: List the criteria that will be used to evaluate the members.
*   **Ranking Weightage**: Enter the weightage or importance of each criterion as a numerical value.
*   **Submit**: This is the final step to submit the survey.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
*   **Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form honestly and accurately to ensure the results are valid and reliable.
*   Ensure that you have authority to fill out the form before submitting your rankings.
