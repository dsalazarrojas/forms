# intimacy_expectations_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your intimacy expectations and boundaries in a relationship. It will help you evaluate how often you expect physical and emotional intimacy from your partner and how important it is for you. The form is divided into several sections, each addressing a different aspect of intimacy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question based on your personal experiences and expectations. Select all that apply for each section, and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief overview of your relationship and expectations.
	+ Type: text
	+ Description: Introduce yourself and provide a brief background about your current relationship status.
* **Relationship Status**: 
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Description: Choose how you currently see your relationship.
* **Intimacy Expectations**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select all the types of intimacy you expect from your partner (e.g., Physical touch, Emotional intimacy, Financial support, etc.).
* **Intimacy Boundaries**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select all the boundaries you have regarding intimacy (e.g., No boundaries, Some boundaries, Physical boundaries (e.g., no kissing, no touching), etc.).
* **Intimacy Boundary Reasons**: 
	+ Type: text
	+ Description: Please describe the reasons behind your boundaries and why they are important for you.
* **Relationship Expectations**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select all the types of expectations you have from your partner (e.g., Emotional support, Financial support, Physical support, etc.).
* **Communication Style**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select how you prefer to communicate with your partner (e.g., Open and direct, Indirect and sensitive, Avoiding conflict, etc.).
* **Communication Channels**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select all the methods of communication you prefer with your partner (e.g., Phone, Email, Text message, In-person conversation, etc.).
* **Emotional Expression**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select how you like to express your emotions with your partner (e.g., Openly, Indirectly, Rarely, etc.).
* **Conflict Resolution**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select how you like to resolve conflicts with your partner (e.g., Directly, Indirectly, Avoiding, etc.).
* **Emotional Support**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select how you expect your partner to support you emotionally (e.g., Constantly, Sometimes, Rarely, etc.).
* **Intimacy Expectations Important**: 
	+ Type: number
	+ Description: Rate the importance of your intimacy expectations on a scale of 1-10.
* **Intimacy Expectations Frequency**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select how often you expect these expectations from your partner (e.g., Never, Rarely, Sometimes, etc.).
* **Intimacy Boundaries Important**: 
	+ Type: number
	+ Description: Rate the importance of your intimacy boundaries on a scale of 1-10.
* **Intimacy Boundaries Frequency**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select how often you enforce your intimacy boundaries (e.g., Never, Rarely, Sometimes, etc.).
* **Review Frequency**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select how often you review your intimacy expectations and boundaries with your partner (e.g., Never, Rarely, Sometimes, etc.).
* **Review Style**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select how you like to review your intimacy expectations and boundaries with your partner (e.g., Directly, Indirectly, Avoiding, etc.).
* **Relationship Status Update**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select how often you update your relationship status with your partner (e.g., Never, Rarely, Sometimes, etc.).
* **Intimacy Expectations Update**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select how often you update your intimacy expectations with your partner (e.g., Never, Rarely, Sometimes, etc.).
* **Boundaries Update**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select how often you update your intimacy boundaries with your partner (e.g., Never, Rarely, Sometimes, etc.).
* **Relationship Expectations Update**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Select how often you update your relationship expectations with your partner (e.g., Never, Rarely, Sometimes, etc.).
* **Notes**: 
	+ Type: text
	+ Description: Add any additional comments or notes about your expectations and boundaries.
* **Relationship Expectations Important**: 
	+ Type: number
	+ Description: Rate the importance of your relationship expectations on a scale of 1-10.
* **Boundaries Important**: 
	+ Type: number
	+ Description: Rate the importance of your boundaries on a scale of 1-10.
* **Score**: 
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Description: Rate your satisfaction with your relationship on a scale of 1-10.

Note: This form is for personal use and not for official purposes. Please answer honestly and as accurately as possible.
