# Community Photo Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Photo Submission Form is designed to collect metadata and information for community use, ensuring that your photos are properly credited and utilized.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Submission Information** (`submission_info`, `note`, required: false): This section is a note field where you can provide any additional information about your photo submission.
2. **First Name** (`submitter_first_name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your first name.
3. **Last Name** (`submitter_last_name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your last name.
4. **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address for confirmation and updates.
5. **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you want to be contacted for any updates or questions, please provide your phone number.
6. **Photo Category** (`submission_category`, `select_one`, required: true): Select a category for your photo from the list provided.
7. **Photo Title** (`photo_title`, `text`, required: true): Give your photo a title that best describes it.
8. **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, `text`, required: true): Briefly describe the story behind your photo.
9. **Location Where Photo Was Taken** (`location_taken`, `text`, required: true): Provide the city and state where the photo was taken.
10. **Date Photo Was Taken** (`date_taken`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date the photo was taken.
11. **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, `text`, required: false): If different from the submitter, enter the photographer's name.
12. **Usage Rights** (`usage_rights`, `note`, required: false): This section is a note field where you can provide information about usage rights.
13. **Do You Own the Rights to This Photo** (`ownership`, `select_one`, required: true): Select 'Yes - I Took This Photo' if you took the photo, 'Yes - I Have Permission' if you have permission to use the photo, or 'False' if not.
14. **Grant Usage Permission** (`usage_permission`, `select_one`, required: true): Select 'Yes - I Grant Permission' if you grant usage permission, or 'No - For Review Only' if not.
15. **Approved Usage Scope** (`usage_scope`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the approved usage scopes for your photo.
16. **How Should You Be Credited** (`attribution_preference`, `text`, required: true): Enter your name for proper photo attribution.
17. **Photo Specifications** (`photo_specs`, `note`, required: false): This section is a note field for technical details about your photo.
18. **File Format** (`file_format`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the format of your photo file (e.g., JPEG, PNG, TIFF, etc.).
19. **Approximate File Size** (`file_size`, `text`, required: false): Enter the approximate size of your photo file in MB.
20. **Photo Resolution** (`resolution`, `text`, required: false): Enter the photo resolution in width x height in pixels.
21. **Has This Photo Been Published Before** (`previously_published`, `select_one`, required: true): Select 'True' if the photo has been previously published, or 'False' if not.
22. **Publication Details** (`publication_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the publication details if the photo has been previously published.
23. **Submit for Photo Contest** (`contest_entry`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'True' if you are submitting the photo for a contest, or 'False' if not.
24. **Do You Have Additional Photos to Submit** (`additional_photos`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'True' if you have additional photos to submit, or 'False' if not.
25. **Additional Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information about your photo.
