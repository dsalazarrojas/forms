# theater_performance_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
Theater Performance Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from attendees about their overall experience with a theater performance. This form asks questions about various aspects of the show, such as quality of performance, sound, lighting, and overall impact. It also collects contact information for follow-up purposes. The form is intended to help theater staff understand what worked well and what can be improved upon for future performances.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for overall experience.
3. Provide a brief description of the performance quality, sound quality, lighting quality, and set design in the text fields.
4. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the respective fields if you wish to be contacted for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience** (select_one):
	+ Select "Yes" if you enjoyed the overall experience of the performance.
	+ Select "No" if you did not enjoy the overall experience.
* **Performance Quality** (text):
	+ Describe the quality of the performance, such as the skill level of the actors, the pacing of the show, etc.
* **Sound Quality** (text):
	+ Describe the quality of the sound system and acoustics during the performance.
* **Lighting Quality** (text):
	+ Describe the quality of the lighting and its impact on the performance.
* **Set Design** (text):
	+ Describe the quality of the set design and its relevance to the performance.
* **Overall Impact** (text):
	+ Describe the overall emotional impact of the performance on you.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (text):
	+ Provide any suggestions you have for improving future performances.
* **Attendee Name** (text):
	+ Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Attendee Email** (email):
	+ Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Attendee Phone** (text):
	+ Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide thoughtful responses to help us improve our performances.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact our support team for assistance.
