# Player Sentiment And Demographics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect player sentiment and demographic information to help game developers and publishers tailor their content, marketing strategies, and user experience to better suit their target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages below and answer each question accordingly. You can select multiple options for most questions.
