# Menstrual Distress Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's menstual health status, including symptoms, experiences, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your personal experiences and feelings.
2. Be as detailed as possible when describing symptoms or experiences.
3. If a field is not relevant to your situation, you can leave it blank or indicate "Not applicable."

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall health status**:
	+ A brief description of your current overall health status.
* **Physical symptoms**:
	+ Describe any physical symptoms you've experienced related to menstual health.
* **Pain Level 3 days**:
	+ Rate your pain level, on a scale of 1-10, over the past 3 days.
* **Pain Level 4-7 days**:
	+ Rate your pain level, on a scale of 1-10, over the past 4-7 days.
* **Emotional Distress**:
	+ Describe any emotional symptoms or distress you've experienced related to menstual health.
* **Interference with daily life**:
	+ Describe how menstual symptoms have affected your daily activities.
* **Sexual activity**:
	+ Describe any changes or difficulties you've experienced with sexual activity due to menstual symptoms.
* **Work life**:
	+ Describe how menstual symptoms have affected your work or daily responsibilities.
* **Relationship status**:
	+ Describe your current relationship status and any effects of menstual symptoms on it.
* **Education status**:
	+ Describe your educational background and any effects of menstual symptoms on it.
* **Menopause status**:
	+ Describe your menopause status and any effects of menstual symptoms on it.
* **Previous menstuel symptoms**:
	+ Describe any previous menstuel symptoms or experiences.
* **Medical condition**:
	+ Describe any existing medical conditions that may be related to menstul health.
* **Medication**:
	+ Describe any medications you're currently taking or have taken for menstuel symptoms.
* **Treatment status**:
	+ Describe your treatment plans or history related to menstuel symptoms.
* **Other symptoms**:
	+ Describe any other menstuel symptoms or experiences not listed above.
* **Comments**:
	+ Add any additional comments or information about your menstuel health experiences.
* **Menstruel distress**:
	+ Describe any menstuel distress or discomfort you're experiencing.
* **Menstruel Distress Scale Score**:
	+ Rate your menstuel distress on a scale of 1-10, based on your experiences over the past month.
* **Date of last menstruation**:
	+ Enter the date of your last menstruation.
* **Date of last period**:
	+ Enter the date of your last period.
* **Date of next menstruation**:
	+ Enter the date of your next expected menstruation (if known).
* **Menopause start date**:
	+ Enter the date of your menopause start, if known.
* **Menopause end date**:
	+ Enter the date of your menopause end, if known.
* **Date of last menstruation period**:
	+ Enter the date of your last menstruation period.
* **Date of menstuel period**:
	+ Enter the date of your last menstuel period (if different from above).
