# Employee Onboarding Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Onboarding Survey aims to gather feedback from new employees to improve our onboarding process. Your input will help us create a more efficient and supportive onboarding experience for future colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: This section is a general welcome and introduction to the survey. Read it thoroughly to understand what to expect from the following sections.
2. Complete each section in order. Start with **Job Details** and provide information about your new role, job title, and any relevant job descriptions.
3. **Team Information**: Choose the type of team member you are, selecting from Team Lead, Team Manager, or Team Member.
4. **Job Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with your current job on a scale from 1 to 5.
5. **Feedback**: Share any positive or negative experiences you've had so far, and what you've enjoyed or struggled with.
6. **Suggestions**: Think about ways we can improve onboarding for you and your team, and type in any suggestions or ideas you may have.
7. **Next Steps**: If you have any upcoming events or deadlines, such as training sessions or meetings, be sure to mention them in this section.
8. **Review**: Select the status of your onboarding process: Review, Approve, or Not Review.
9. **Submit**: Choose how you would like to submit your survey response: Submit, Save as Draft, or Not Submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief welcome and introduction to the survey.
* **Job Details**: A text input field to provide job-specific information. (Job Title, Description, etc.)
* **Team Information**: A multiple-choice select field to categorize your team role. (Team Lead, Team Manager, Team Member)
* **Job Satisfaction**: A numeric input field to rate your satisfaction with your current job. (Scale 1-5)
* **Feedback**: A text input field to share your experiences and thoughts.
* **Suggestions**: A text input field to offer ideas for improvement.
* **Next Steps**: A date input field to record upcoming events or deadlines.
* **Review**: A single-choice select field to update your onboarding status. (Review, Approve, Not Review)
* **Submit**: A single-choice select field to finalize your survey submission. (Submit, Save as Draft, Not Submit)
