# Corporate Virtual Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees after a virtual training session. Your input is crucial in helping us improve our training programs, and we appreciate your time and effort in filling this out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the department and session you are providing feedback for.
2. Answer each question honestly, giving your opinion on the session's quality, facilitator's performance, and content.
3. Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes (optional).
4. Add any additional notes you'd like to share with us.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Virtual Training Feedback (id: 1)**: Please provide a brief feedback on your overall experience with the session.
* **Department (id: 2)**: Please select the department that organized this training session.
* **Session (id: 3)**: Please select the session you are providing feedback for.
* **Was the training session helpful? (id: 4)**: Answer this question based on your experience with the session. You can choose one of the options: Very Helpful, Somewhat Helpful, Not Helpful, or Other (please provide a brief explanation).
* **Were the session materials clear and relevant? (id: 5)**: Answer this question based on your experience with the materials provided. You can choose one of the options: True or False.
* **How would you rate the facilitator's performance? (id: 6)**: Answer this question based on your experience with the facilitator. You can choose one of the options: Excellent, Good, Average, or Poor.
* **Were the session materials well-structured and organized? (id: 7)**: Answer this question based on your experience with the materials provided. You can choose one of the options: True or False.
* **Session length was just right (id: 8)**: Answer this question based on your experience with the session duration. You can choose one of the options: True or False.
* **Length of the session in minutes (id: 9)**: Please enter the actual length of the session in minutes.
* **Email (id: 10)**: If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification, please provide your email address.
* **Phone (id: 11)**: If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification, please provide your phone number.
* **Note (id: 12)**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please add them here.
* **Submit (id: 13)**: Click this button to send your feedback.
