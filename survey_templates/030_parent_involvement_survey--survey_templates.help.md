# Parent Involvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to understand the level of involvement and relationship between parents and their children. Please answer these questions honestly, and remember that the information collected will be used to inform support and resources for the family.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question with the information that best reflects your relationship with your child.
- You can choose from the provided options for questions with multiple choice options.
- For questions without options, please provide a brief answer in the text field.
- If you are unsure about any question, you can skip it or ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **parent_survey_1** (Text): This question is a general inquiry about your relationship with your child.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your relationship.
- **parent_survey_2** (Select One: Yes/No): Have you had regular conversations with your child about their daily activities?
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you have had regular conversations, 'No' otherwise.
- **parent_survey_3** (Text): What activities have you been involved in with your child recently?
	+ Please describe any activities you have participated in with your child, such as school events or family projects.
- **parent_survey_4** (Select Multiple: Yes/No): Which of the following activities do you engage in with your child?
	+ Choose from the list 'Yes' for activities you participate in, 'No' for activities you do not.
- **parent_survey_5** (Date): What is the most recent date you spent quality time with your child?
	+ Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **parent_survey_6** (Time): What time of day do you usually spend with your child?
	+ Enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00, 16:30).
- **parent_survey_7** (Note): Do you have any notes about your relationship with your child?
	+ Please provide any additional information or comments about your relationship.
- **parent_survey_8** (Email): What is your email address (if any) that your child uses for school or communication with you?
	+ Please enter your email address.
- **parent_survey_9** (Text): Do you have any concerns or worries about your child's well-being?
	+ Please describe any concerns you may have.
- **parent_survey_10** (Text): Is there anything else you would like to share about your relationship with your child?
	+ Please provide any additional information or thoughts.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- You can skip any question that makes you uncomfortable or unsure.
- If you need help or clarification, please ask.
