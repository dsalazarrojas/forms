# Visual Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visual Preference Survey form is used to collect data on building characteristics and their distances to various amenities. This data is used to prioritize and identify building locations that fit specific criteria and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each question carefully, providing the most accurate information possible.
2. Questions will be asked in a series of pages, each with a set of questions.
3. Use the dropdown menus or text fields to answer each question.
4. For questions with dropdown menus, select the most relevant answer.
5. If a question does not apply to your building, select "N/A" (Not Applicable) and continue with the next question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Building Location**: Type your building's location in the text field.
	+ Please provide the full address of the building, including street number and street name.
* **Building Design Style**: Select one of the design styles that best fits your building.
	+ Modern, Contemporary, Art Deco, Artisanal, or Post-Modern.
* **Building Size**: Enter the size of your building.
	+ This is usually measured in square feet.
* **Building Height**: Enter the height of your building.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Architectural Style**: Select one or more of the architectural styles that best fit your building.
	+ Modern, Contemporary, Art Deco, Artisanal, or Post-Modern.
* **Building Color**: Select the color scheme that best represents your building.
	+ Light Gray, Dark Gray, White, Red, or Blue.
* **Building Materials**: Select the materials used in the construction of your building.
	+ Glass, Steel, Metal, Wood, or Other.
* **Building Orientation**: Select the direction your building faces.
	+ North-South, East-West, or North-South-East-West.
* **Age of Building**: Select the age of your building.
	+ New, Old, or Very Old.
* **Building Height Above Ground**: Enter the height of your building above ground level.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Building Height Above Street**: Enter the height of your building above street level.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Intersection**: Enter the distance to the nearest intersection.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Park**: Enter the distance to the nearest park.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Public Transportation**: Enter the distance to the nearest public transportation.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Public Space**: Enter the distance to the nearest public space.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Water**: Enter the distance to the nearest water source.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Green Space**: Enter the distance to the nearest green space.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest School**: Enter the distance to the nearest school.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Hospital**: Enter the distance to the nearest hospital.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Fire Station**: Enter the distance to the nearest fire station.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Police Station**: Enter the distance to the nearest police station.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Fire Department**: Enter the distance to the nearest fire department.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Community Center**: Enter the distance to the nearest community center.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Library**: Enter the distance to the nearest library.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.
* **Distance To Nearest Museum**: Enter the distance to the nearest museum.
	+ This is usually measured in feet.

## Tips
- Take your time and answer each question carefully.
- If you're unsure of an answer, select "N/A" (Not Applicable) and continue with the next question.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, contact the administrator or supervisor.
- Review your answers before submitting the form for accuracy and completeness.
- The data collected will be used to prioritize building locations that fit specific criteria and requirements.
