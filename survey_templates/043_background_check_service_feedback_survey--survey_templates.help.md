# Background Check Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the background check service. This feedback will help us understand our strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance our screening services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer every question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the rating options for each field (Turnaround time, Accuracy, Ease of use, etc.) based on your actual experience with the service.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields to share your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Feedback - Background Screening Experience
* **Company Name** (`client_company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company you have used our screening service for.
* **Your Name and Title** (`respondent_name_feedback`, text, optional): If you want to share your name and title, please enter it here. Otherwise, you can skip this field.
* **Satisfaction with Turnaround Time (TAT)** (`turnaround_time_rating`, number, required): Rate how fast you received your background check report, with 1 being very slow and 5 being very fast.
* **Accuracy and Quality of the Final Reports** (`report_accuracy_rating`, number, required): Rate the accuracy and quality of the final reports you received, with 1 being very poor and 5 being very good.
* **Ease of Use of the Online Submission Portal** (`portal_ease_of_use`, number, required): Rate how easy or difficult it was to use our online portal, with 1 being very difficult and 5 being very easy.
* **Professionalism and Responsiveness of Our Support Staff** (`customer_service_professionalism`, number, required): Rate the professionalism and responsiveness of our support staff, with 1 being very poor and 5 being very good.
* **Overall Value for the Price** (`value_for_price_rating`, number, required): Rate how much value you received for the price you paid, with 1 being very poor and 5 being very good.
* **Likelihood to Recommend Our Service** (`likelihood_to_recommend_bg`, number, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend our service to others, with 0 being not likely and 10 being very likely.
* **What Did We Do Particularly Well?** (`areas_of_excellence`, text, optional): If you have any comments on what we did particularly well, please enter it here.
* **How Can We Improve Our Service?** (`areas_for_improvement_bg`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter it here.
* **Any Other Comments or Suggestions?** (`additional_comments_bg_service`, text, optional): If you have any other comments or suggestions, please enter it here.
* **Permission to Use as a Testimonial?** (`permission_to_quote`, select_one, required): Please select whether you allow us to use your feedback as a testimonial, with options for anonymous or with your company name.
