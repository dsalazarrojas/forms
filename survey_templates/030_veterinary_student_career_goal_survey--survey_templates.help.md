**Veterinary Student Career Goal Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Veterinary Student Career Goal Survey is designed to gather information about your career goals, interests, and preferences as a veterinary student. This form will help us understand your aspirations and expectations to provide guidance and support throughout your academic journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully read each field explanation to understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question accurately and truthfully to the best of your ability.
3. Use the options provided for select fields, or enter your own text responses for open-ended questions.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your interests?** (`student_interests`, text, optional): This question allows you to share your hobbies, passions, or areas of interest outside of your academic work. Please enter your interests in the text box provided.
* **What type of work do you want to do after graduation?** (`work_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options provided that best represent your desired career path after graduation.
	+ Option 1: Clinical work (e.g., veterinarian)
	+ Option 2: Research and development (e.g., scientist)
	+ Option 3: Education and teaching (e.g., professor)
* **What are your post-graduation plans?** (`graduation_plans`, text, optional): Describe your plans after graduation, including any specific goals or aspirations you have for your career.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided job titles that best represents your desired career path after graduation.
	+ Veterinarian
	+ Animal Care Technician
	+ Veterinary Researcher
	+ Veterinary Professor
* **Work environment** (`work_environment`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options provided that best represent your desired work environment.
	+ Clinic
	+ Laboratory
	+ Research Institution
	+ University
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the options provided that best represent your desired work location.
	+ Urban
	+ Rural
	+ Suburban
	+ Coastal
* **What salary are you expecting?** (`salary`, number, optional): Enter your expected salary range in numbers.
* **What benefits are you expecting?** (`benefits`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options provided that best represent your desired benefits.
	+ Medical
	+ Dental
	+ Vision
	+ 401(k)
* **Are there any other benefits?** (`benefits_other`, text, optional): If you have any other benefits not listed, please describe them.
* **Benefits details** (`benefits_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the benefits you are expecting.
* **Work life balance** (`work_life_balance`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided that best represents your desired work-life balance.
	+ Flexible schedule
	+ Flexible work hours
	+ Job security
	+ Job flexibility
* **Job security** (`job_security`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options provided that best represent your desired job security.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Work flexibility** (`work_flexibility`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the options provided that best represent your desired work flexibility.
	+ True
	+ False
* **What certifications do you need?** (`professional_certifications`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options provided that best represent the certifications you need for your desired career path.
	+ Certified
	+ Registered
	+ Licensed
	+ Associate
* **Education background** (`education_background`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided that best represents your educational background.
	+ Bachelor
	+ Master
	+ Doctoral
	+ Postdoctoral
* **What work experience do you have?** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe any relevant work experience you have, if applicable.

Note that some fields are optional, and you should only complete them if you have a relevant answer.
