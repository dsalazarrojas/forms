# personal_narrative_writing_guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The personal narrative writing guide is a template for writing a story or essay by providing a structured approach to inputting thoughts, experiences, and reflections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the **Introduction** field, providing a brief overview of your story or essay.
2. Move on to the **Background** field, where you can share more details about the context and circumstances that led to your story or essay.
3. In the **Writing Plan** field, outline the structure and organization of your writing.
4. Provide an **Evaluation** of your story or essay, discussing what worked well and areas for improvement.
5. Summarize your conclusion in the **Conclusion** field.
6. Enter your email address in the **Email** field for contact information.
7. If you have a phone number you'd like to include, add it in the **Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (text): A brief overview of your story or essay. Please try to keep it short and concise.
* **Background** (text, optional): More details about the context and circumstances that led to your story or essay.
* **Writing Plan** (text, optional): Outline the structure and organization of your writing.
* **Evaluation** (text, optional): A critical look at what worked well and areas for improvement in your story or essay.
* **Conclusion** (text, optional): A summary of your story or essay, wrapping up any loose ends.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact information.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter a phone number for additional contact information.
