# Letter Guess Game Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Letter Guess Game Submission form collects information from players who want to participate in the Letter Guess Game. This form is used by the game administrator to review and manage submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your submission in the **Letter Guess Game Submission** field. This is the primary field where you can provide your answer.
2. Fill in your **Contestant Details** field with your name and other relevant information about yourself.
3. Enter your participant's name in the **Participant Name** field. This is the name of the person who will be playing the game.
4. Add your email address in the **Email** field. This will help the game administrator contact you if they need to.
5. Finally, provide your contact number in the **Contact Number** field. This is optional, but recommended in case the game administrator needs to reach you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Letter Guess Game Submission** (`letter_guess_game_submission`, text, optional): Enter your submission in this field. This is the primary field where you can provide your answer.
* **Contestant Details** (`contestant_details`, text, optional): Fill in your name and other relevant information about yourself. This will help the game administrator know who you are.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be playing the game.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Add your email address so the game administrator can contact you if needed.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): Enter your contact number, which is optional but recommended in case the game administrator needs to reach you.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields correctly to ensure that your submission is considered for the game.
* If you have any doubts about what to enter in a particular field, contact the game administrator for clarification.
* Please note that some fields are optional, but providing your contact number can help the game administrator contact you if needed.
