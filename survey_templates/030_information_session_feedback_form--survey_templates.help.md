# Information Session Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and information from participants after an information session, providing valuable insights for future planning and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
Follow the steps mentioned above to complete this form accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Questionnaire**: A text field where you can provide your thoughts and opinions about the session.
* **Rating**: A scale to rate the session, with options from 1 to 5.
* **Comments**: A text field for providing any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Contact Details**: A field to enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Participant Survey**: A field to select which options apply to you.
* **Feedback Question**: A field to select the most relevant option from the given choices.
* **Follow Up**: A date field to schedule a follow-up meeting or event.
* **Session Details**: A time field to record the session details.
* **Feedback Form Submitted**: A text field to confirm that the form has been submitted.
* **Name**: A text field to enter your name.
* **Email**: An email field to enter your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Participant Details**: A field to select whether you are a participant.
* **Feedback Details**: A text field for providing any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Participant Details**: A field to select which options apply to you. 

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
