# Sponsorship Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and comments from our sponsors. We want to hear about their experience, any issues they encountered, and their overall satisfaction with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer every question honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Please fill out each page completely, and don't skip any questions.
3. Use the "submit" button to complete the form and send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Sponsor Satisfaction (Page 1)
* **Sponsor Satisfaction (1)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our organization. Please select a number from 1 to 5.

### Sponsor Details (Page 2)
* **Sponsor Details (2)**: Share with us any comments or feedback about our organization. Please write as much or as little as you'd like.

### Comments And Requests (Page 3)
* **Comments And Requests (3)**: Share with us any comments or requests you may have.

### Feedback (Page 4)
* **Feedback (4)**: Share with us any feedback you may have. 

### Contact Info (Page 5)
* **Contact Info (5)**: Please provide us with your contact information so we can follow up on your feedback. 

### Email (Page 6)
* **Email (6)**: Please provide your email address so we can contact you about your feedback. 

### Phone (Page 7)
* **Phone (7)**: Please provide your phone number so we can contact you about your feedback. 

### Date (Page 8)
* **Date (8)**: The date you completed this form. 

### Time (Page 9)
* **Time (9)**: The time you completed this form. 

Note: All fields are optional, and you are not required to fill out this form if you don't have any feedback. However, your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate any comments or suggestions you may have.
