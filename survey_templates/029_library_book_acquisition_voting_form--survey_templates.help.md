# Library Book Acquisition Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback on book acquisition and help the library make informed decisions about their collections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your submission in the "Library Book Acquisition Voting Form" field. This title will be used to identify your submission in the output file.
2. Select a category that best describes the type of book you are interested in acquiring. You can choose from the options:
	* Survey Templates
	* Librarian Forms
3. Enter a brief description of your submission in the "Description" field.
4. In the "What would you like to see more of?" field, select as many book categories that you are interested in. You can select from:
	* Romance
	* Mystery
	* Science
	* Non-Fiction
	* Biography
	* Fantasy
	* Science Fiction
	* Graphic Novel
	* History
	* Poetry
	* Humor
	* Horror
5. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can include them in the "Output File" field.
6. If you want to ensure that your submission is saved with a specific filename, you can specify it in the "Output File" field.
7. Check the "Required?" field to indicate if the submission is required.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to ensure that you are not duplicating a submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Library Book Acquisition Voting Form** (`title`, text, not required):
	+ A short title to identify your submission.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, not required):
	+ Choose a category that best describes the type of book you are interested in.
* **Description** (`description`, text, not required):
	+ Enter a brief description of your submission.
* **What would you like to see more of?** (`form_description`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select as many book categories that you are interested in.
* **Output File** (`output_filename`, text, not required):
	+ This is where your submission will be saved, enter a filename if you want to.
* **Required?** (`required_checkbox`, number, not required):
	+ Check this box if the submission is required.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required):
	+ Enter your email address to ensure that you are not duplicating a submission.
