# Environmental Survey Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users regarding their environmental initiatives and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the environmental initiatives that best describes your current practices.
2. Provide your feedback and comments about your experiences.
3. Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further questions.
4. Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
5. Select the time you typically spend on environmental initiatives.
6. Select the date you first started your environmental practices.
7. Please fill out any additional comments or information you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Environmental Initiatives**: Select one of the options that best describes your current environmental practices.
	+ Select one: This is the first option, choose this if it describes your current practices.
	+ Select two: This is the second option, choose this if it describes your current practices.
	+ Select three: This is the third option, choose this if it describes your current practices.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and experiences about the environmental initiatives you selected.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further questions.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Time**: Select the amount of time you typically spend on environmental initiatives.
* **Date**: Select the date you first started your environmental practices.
* **Additional Comments**: Fill out any additional comments or information you would like to share.

Please note that this form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in learning more about the environmental practices and habits of the company or organization they are interacting with. If you are a legitimate user, please ensure that you do not submit the form if you have already done so in the past.
