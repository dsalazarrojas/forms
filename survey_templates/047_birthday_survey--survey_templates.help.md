# Birthday Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your birthday preferences, traditions, and experiences. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions honestly, and we'll use the information to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully and answer the questions in order.
2.  Fill out the required fields first (marked as *required*).
3.  Answer the optional questions as much as you'd like, but feel free to skip them if you don't have an opinion or preference.
4.  Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name** (id: 4, `name: respondent_name`, `type: text`): This is your full name. Please enter it as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email Address** (id: 5, `name: respondent_email`, `type: email`): Enter your email address where we can contact you or send you the results.
*   **How Are You Celebrating Your Birthday** (id: 9, `name: how_celebrating`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Describe your birthday celebration plans. This can be a simple dinner with family or a big party with friends.
*   **Your Celebration Style** (id: 10, `name: celebration_style`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: true`): Choose the type(s) of celebration you prefer, such as Family Gathering, Friend Party, or Solo Celebration.
*   **Who Do You Want to Celebrate With** (id: 20, `name: who_to_celebrate_with`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the names of the people you'd like to celebrate with, such as family, friends, or colleagues.
*   **Preferred Group Size** (id: 21, `name: group_size_preference`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Choose the preferred group size for your celebration, from Solo to Very Large.
*   **Your Birthday Wish for This Year** (id: 30, `name: birthday_wish`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Share a birthday wish or hope for this year.
*   **Birthday Message to Others** (id: 31, `name: message_to_others`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Share a message with the world to celebrate your birthday.
*   **Share Survey Results With Someone** (id: 33, `name: share_with_birthday_person`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Choose who you'd like to share the results with, such as a family member, friend, or colleague.
*   **Email to Share With** (id: 34, `name: share_with_email`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter the email address of the person you'd like to share the results with.
*   **Additional Comments or Thoughts** (id: 36, `name: additional_comments`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your birthday.
*   **Special Birthday Traditions** (id: 23, `name: birthday_traditions`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Describe any special birthday traditions you follow or would like to start.
*   **New Traditions You Want to Start** (id: 24, `name: new_traditions`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Share new ideas for birthday traditions you'd like to start.
*   **Birthday Must-Haves** (id: 25, `name: must_haves`, `type: text`, `required: false`): List any birthday must-haves, such as a specific gift or activity.
*   **Best Birthday Memory** (id: 27, `name: best_birthday_memory`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Describe a memorable birthday experience.
*   **Most Meaningful Gift Ever Received** (id: 28, `name: most_meaningful_gift`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Share the most meaningful gift you've ever received.
*   **Favorite Birthday Foods or Treats** (id: 17, `name: favorite_food`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Share your favorite foods or treats for birthdays.
*   **Favorite Cake Flavor** (id: 18, `name: favorite_cake`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Share your favorite cake flavor.
*   **Special Interests** (id: 15, `name: special_interests`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Choose the special interests you enjoy on your birthday, such as dining out, entertainment, or shopping.
*   **Favorite Gift Types** (id: 16, `name: favorite_gift_type`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Choose the types of gifts you prefer, such as experiences, practical items, or luxury items.
*   **When is Your Birthday** (id: 8, `name: celebration_date`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Enter your birthday date.
*   **Where Are You Celebrating** (id: 11, `name: celebration_location`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Share the location of your birthday celebration.
*   **Date of Celebration** (id: 12, `name: celebration_date_actual`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Enter the actual date of your birthday celebration.
*   **Ideal Birthday** (id: 14, `name: ideal_birthday`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Describe your perfect birthday celebration.
*   **Additional Birthday Interests** (id: 35, `name: additional_interests`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Share any additional birthday interests or activities you enjoy.
*   **Your Age** (id: 6, `name: respondent_age`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter your age.

Note: Some fields have specific instructions or examples to help you complete them accurately.
