# Shipping Options Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the purpose of the Shipping Options Feedback Questionnaire, a form designed to collect your preferences and needs regarding shipping options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question with your honest response to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to provide specific answers whenever possible.
3. If you're unsure about any option, feel free to provide your best guess or estimate.
4. Take about 5-10 minutes to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **What is your preferred shipping speed?** (`shipping_speed`, `select_one`, required: false)
Your preferred shipping speed is the time it takes for the shipping service to deliver your packages. Choose from the options:

*   Same Day: Next business day or sooner.
*   1-3 business days: 1-3 business days after the order is placed.
*   2-5 business days: 2-5 business days after the order is placed.

### **How often do you like to receive shipments?** (`shipping_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false)
This question is about how frequently you like to receive shipments. Choose from the options:

*   Never: You never receive shipments.
*   Monthly: Once a month.
*   Weekly: Once a week.
*   Bi-Weekly: Every two weeks.

### **What is your preferred shipping method?** (`shipping_method`, `select_one`, required: false)
This question is about your preferred shipping method. Choose from the options:

*   Standard Shipping: This is our standard shipping method.
*   Express Shipping: Faster shipping option available at an extra fee.

### **Which shipping service provider do you usually use?** (`shipping_service_provider`, `select_one`, required: false)
Choose the shipping service provider you usually use or would recommend.

*   UPS
*   USPS
*   FedEx

### **What is your average shipping cost expectation?** (`shipping_cost`, `number`, required: false)
Please provide the amount of money you expect to pay for shipping each time you receive a shipment.

### **How satisfied are you overall with the shipping service?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the shipping service.

*   Very Satisfied: Very satisfied with the shipping service.
*   Somewhat Satisfied: Somewhat satisfied with the shipping service.
*   Neutral: Neutral or average satisfaction.
*   Somewhat Dissatisfied: Somewhat dissatisfied with the shipping service.
*   Very Dissatisfied: Very dissatisfied with the shipping service.

### **Shipping Frequency 2** (`shipping_frequency_2`, `select_one`, required: false)
This question is similar to the previous question, but with more options. Choose from:

*   Daily: Daily shipments.
*   Weekly: Weekly shipments.
*   Bi-Weekly: Every two weeks.
*   Monthly: Monthly shipments.
*   Quarterly: Quarterly shipments.

### **What are your shipping preferences?** (`shipping_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Choose all that apply:

*   Fastest: You want the shipping to be as fast as possible.
*   Easiest: You want the shipping to be as easy as possible.
*   Cheapest: You want the shipping to be as cheap as possible.

### **Do you have any comments on the shipping options?** (`shipping_comments`, `text`, required: false)
If you have any additional comments or suggestions about our shipping options, please provide them here.
