# nashville_virtual_training_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and evaluation data from participants who have attended virtual training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your actual experience with the virtual training session.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: This is the name of the person completing the form.
* **Session Date**: The date of the virtual training session you attended.
* **Session Time**: The time of the virtual training session you attended.
* **Trainer Name**: The name of the trainer who led the virtual training session.
* **Feedback Score**: A score out of 10 to rate your overall feedback experience.
* **Feedback Comments**: Any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Training Location**: The location where the virtual training session was held.
* **Training Topic**: The topic of the virtual training session you attended.
* **Participant Feedback**: Please select "Yes" if you received feedback from the trainer.
* **Training Evaluations**: Please select all that apply if you felt the training was evaluated well.
* **Participant Email**: Your email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Participant Notes**: Any additional notes or comments.
* **Survey Completed**: Please select "Yes" if you have completed the survey.
* **Feedback Received**: Please select all that apply if you received feedback from the trainer.
* **Trainer Notes**: Any additional notes or comments from the trainer.
* **Session Duration**: The length of the virtual training session in minutes.
* **Training Hours**: The total number of hours of the virtual training session.
* **Feedback Frequency**: The frequency of feedback you received from the trainer.
* **Training Type**: Please select "Yes" if the virtual training session was relevant to your needs.
* **Participant Rating**: A rating out of 10 to rate your overall satisfaction with the training.
* **Participant Recommend**: Please select "Yes" if you would recommend the training to others.
* **Participant Recommend Reasons**: Please select all that apply if you would recommend the training.
* **Participant Satisfaction**: Please select "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied" to rate your overall satisfaction with the training.
* **Participant Comments**: Any additional comments or notes.

Note: All fields with `select_one` and `select_multiple` type are meant to be selected from the provided options, while fields with `text` type are meant to be filled with free-form text.
