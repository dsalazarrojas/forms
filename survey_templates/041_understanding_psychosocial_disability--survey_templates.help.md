## understanding-psychosocial-disability - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is created to gather information on psychosocial disability, mental health, and research projects. Your input will help shape the understanding and improve the lives of individuals and communities affected by psychosocial disability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in as much detail as possible.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
