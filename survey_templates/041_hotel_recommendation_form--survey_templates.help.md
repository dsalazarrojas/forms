# Hotel Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hotel Recommendation Form is a form designed to collect information from users about their hotel preferences. This form is used to gather information on travel dates, budgets, locations, and other relevant details to provide users with tailored hotel recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **What is your name** field.
2. Provide the date of your next trip in the **Travel Date** field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Enter your budget for the trip in the **Budget** field.
4. Select your preferred travel locations from the list in the **Location** field.
5. Choose your level of satisfaction with hotels from the **Hotel Rating** field.
6. Select the duration of your trip from the options in the **Travel Length** field.
7. Indicate if you are a travel agent or not in the **Travel Agent** field.
8. Choose your preferred type of hotel from the list in the **Preferred Hotel** field.
9. Select how you plan to travel from the options in the **Travel Mode** field.
10. Select the duration of your prophets from the **Prophets Length** field.
11. If you're traveling for prophets, select your prophets location from the list in the **Prophets Location** field.
12. Enter your budget for the prophets in the **Prophets Budget** field.
13. If you have a second travel date, fill in the **Travel Date 2** field.
14. Finally, select your preferred type of hotel for the second travel date from the list in the **Preferred Hotel 2** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name**: This field is required and should be filled out with your name.
* **Travel Date**: This field is required and should be filled out with the date of your next trip in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Budget**: This field is required and should be filled out with your budget for the trip.
* **Location**: This field is required and should be selected with all locations you are interested in visiting.
* **Hotel Rating**: This field is required and should be selected with your level of satisfaction with hotels.
* **Travel Length**: This field is required and should be selected with the duration of your trip.
* **Travel Agent**: This field is required and should be selected if you are a travel agent or not.
* **Preferred Hotel**: This field is required and should be selected with your preferred type of hotel.
* **Travel Mode**: This field is required and should be selected with how you plan to travel.
* **Prophets Length**: This field is required for prophets and should be selected with the duration of your prophets.
* **Prophets Location**: This field is not required and should be selected with your prophets location if applicable.
* **Prophets Budget**: This field is required for prophets and should be filled out with your budget for the prophets.
* **Travel Date 2**: This field is required and should be filled out with the date of your second travel in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Preferred Hotel 2**: This field is required and should be selected with your preferred type of hotel for the second travel date.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the form administrators.
