# Healthcare Workshop Topics Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and input from participants on healthcare workshop topics, including their preferences, rankings, and facilitator interests. This information will help organizers to better understand the audience's needs and preferences for future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Title**: Please enter your name as you would like it to appear on the form.
2. **Topic Preferences**: Select your preferred topics for the healthcare workshop. You can choose one or more topics from the list.
3. **Ranking**: Please provide a score between 1-5 for each topic, with 1 being the highest priority and 5 being the lowest priority.
4. **Facilitator Interest**: Indicate whether you are interested in facilitating the healthcare workshop on each topic.
5. **Additional Notes**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the text box below.
6. **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification or follow-up.
7. **Phone**: Please enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
8. **Facilitator Interest 1-4**: Select whether you are interested or not interested in facilitating the workshop on each topic 1-4.
9. **Ranking 1-10**: Provide your ranking scores for each topic 1-10.
