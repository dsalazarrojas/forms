# Pet Care Quality Owner Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from pet owners on the quality of services provided by pet care services. The feedback will help us understand the pet owners' experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Please select the date of the pet care service that you would like to provide feedback on.
2. Choose the type of pet care service that you received (e.g., Pet Grooming, Boarding, Walking, or Clinic Visit).
3. Evaluate the quality of the service you received (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average).
4. Provide your overall feedback on the service (e.g., Very Good, Good, Fair).
5. If you wish to provide additional contact information, you can enter your phone number or email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Care Service Date <type:date>** (`pet_care_service_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the pet care service you would like to provide feedback on.
* **Pet Care Service Type <type:select_one>** (`pet_care_service_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of pet care service you received (e.g., Pet Grooming, Boarding, Walking, or Clinic Visit).
* **Pet Care Service Quality <type:select_one>** (`pet_care_service_quality`, select_one, required): Evaluate the quality of the service you received (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average).
* **Pet Care Owner Feedback <type:select_one>** (`pet_care_owner_feedback`, select_one, required): Provide your overall feedback on the service (e.g., Very Good, Good, Fair).
* **Contact Phone <type:text>** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to provide additional contact information, you can enter your phone number.
* **Contact E Mail <type:email>** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address if you would like to provide additional contact information.
