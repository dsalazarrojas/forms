# home_network_safety_inspection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The home_network_safety_inspection_form is designed to gather information about the safety and security of a network in a home environment. This form is used to help identify potential risks and vulnerabilities associated with a home network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page to understand the purpose and guidelines of the form.
2. On the "Network Info" page, enter a brief description of your home network, including its purpose and the number of devices connected.
3. On the "Network Devices" page, select the types of devices connected to your network from the list of options (Router, Modem, Switch, Access Point, Other, or None).
4. On the "Firewall" page, indicate whether your network has a firewall enabled or not. If not, describe the reason for not having a firewall.
5. On the "Network Connections" page, select the type of network connections used (Wired, Wireless, Both, or None).
6. On the "Network Security" page, enter a brief description of your network security measures.
7. On the "Network Threats" page, enter the number of threats or issues you are aware of on your network.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Network Info (Network Info)** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your home network, including its purpose and the number of devices connected.
* **Network Devices (Network Devices)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the types of devices connected to your network from the available options (Router, Modem, Switch, Access Point, Other, or None).
* **Firewall (Firewall)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether your network has a firewall enabled or not. Select one or both "True" and "Other (please describe)" options if you have a custom firewall setup.
* **Network Connections (Network Connections)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the type of network connections used (Wired, Wireless, Both, or None).
* **Network Security (Network Security)** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your network security measures.
* **Network Threats (Network Threats)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the number of threats or issues you are aware of on your network.
