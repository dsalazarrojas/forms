# Hatch Application Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information from potential applicants for the Hatch application process. It gathers essential details about the applicant's background, skills, and preferences to facilitate a thorough evaluation and matching with potential job opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Read the form carefully**: Before starting, make sure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. **Fill out the form**: Answer each field accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. **Submit your application**: Once you have completed all the required and optional fields, submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information (1)**: Please provide your basic contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Personal Details (2)**: Share your personal background, including your age, nationality, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Information (3)**: Enter your contact details, such as your phone number, LinkedIn profile, or any other way to get in touch.
* **Resume Information (4)**: Attach or paste your resume, ensuring it's up-to-date and relevant to the position you're applying for.
* **Education (5)**: List your educational background, including degrees, institutions, and relevant courses.
* **Work Experience (6)**: Provide a detailed description of your work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
* **Skills (7)**: Specify your skills and certifications relevant to the job you're applying for.
* **Availability (8)**: Indicate your availability to start work, including any relevant dates or timeframes.
* **Job Interest (9)**: Clearly state your job interest, including specific positions or industries you're targeting.
* **Other Experience (10)**: Share any other relevant experience, such as internships, volunteer work, or projects, that may be beneficial to your application.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Attach any relevant documents or links to your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
