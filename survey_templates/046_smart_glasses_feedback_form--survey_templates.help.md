# Smart Glasses Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Smart Glasses Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback and gather information about the user's experience with Smart Glasses. The form is divided into multiple sections, each with its own set of fields that ask for various types of information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Navigate through the pages by clicking on the "Submit" button at the bottom of each page.
2. Fill in the fields as you see fit, providing as much or as little information as you like.
3. Use the fields to provide your feedback, suggestions, and ratings about Smart Glasses.
4. Once you have filled out all the fields, click on the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Smart Glasses Feedback**: A text field where you can provide your overall feedback about the product. (_required: false_)
* **User Experience**: A text field where you can share your experience using the product. (_required: false_)
* **Product Satisfaction**: A number field where you can rate your satisfaction with the product. (_required: false_)
* **Feedback**: A text field where you can provide feedback or suggestions about the product. (_required: false_)
* **Suggest Improvement**: A text field where you can suggest improvements for the product. (_required: false_)
* **Rate Product**: A number field where you can rate your satisfaction with the product. (_required: false_)
* **Email**: An email field where you can provide your email address. (_required: false_)
* **Phone**: A text field where you can provide your phone number. (_required: false_)
* **Date**: A date field where you can select a date. (_required: false_)
* **Time**: A time field where you can select a time. (_required: false_)
* **Which option do you have**: A select one field where you can select one of the provided options. (_required: false_)
* **Select multiple options**: A select multiple field where you can select multiple options. (_required: false_)
* **Select Date**: A date field where you can select a date. (_required: false_)
* **Select Time**: A time field where you can select a time. (_required: false_)
* **Submit**: A text field where you can provide any final comments or suggestions. (_required: false_)
* **Name**: A text field where you can provide your name. (_required: false_)
* **Age**: A number field where you can provide your age. (_required: false_)
* **Country**: A select one field where you can select your country. (_required: false_)
* **Gender**: A select multiple field where you can select your gender. (_required: false_)
* **Language**: A select multiple field where you can select your language. (_required: false_)
* **Rating**: A select multiple field where you can select a rating. (_required: false_)
* **Comments**: A text field where you can provide additional comments. (_required: false_)
* **Rating Comments**: A text field where you can provide comments about your rating. (_required: false_)
* **Suggest**: A text field where you can provide suggestions. (_required: false_)

## Tips

* Please fill in the fields as much as possible, providing as much information as you like.
* You can skip any field that you do not feel is relevant to your feedback.
* Please be honest and provide clear and concise answers.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
