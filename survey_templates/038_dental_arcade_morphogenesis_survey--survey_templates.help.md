# Dental Arcade Morphogenesis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Dental Arcade Morphogenesis Survey form. The form is designed to collect information about participants and their experiences with the Dental Arcade Morphogenesis.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Answer the questions on each page, taking note of any specific instructions or hints provided.
*   If a field has options, select the most relevant one from the available choices.
*   For free-text fields, provide as much detail as needed.
*   Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

Here is a detailed explanation of each field in the form:

*   **Introduction**: This is a static page that serves as an introduction to the survey.
*   **Participant Demographics**: This section is where you can select your demographic information (e.g., "Yes", "No", "Other").
*   **Questions**: This section is similar to "Participant Demographics" but asks different questions (e.g., "Yes", "No", "Other").
*   **Screening**: This field is likely related to screening procedures or processes (e.g., selecting a date).
*   **Assessment**: Select an option from the list (e.g., "Yes", "No", "Other") to assess something.
*   **Questionnaire**: You are not allowed to enter a date or time.
*   **Participant Information**: Please provide your participant information here (e.g., free-text response).
*   **Conclusion**: This is a static page with additional text or notes.
*   **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information you think is relevant (e.g., free-text response).
*   **Screening Formulas**: Enter the screening formulas related information here (e.g., free-text response).
*   **Participant Comments**: Enter your participant comments here (e.g., free-text response).
*   **Dental Professionals Information**: Enter your professional information here (e.g., free-text response).
*   **Survey End**: This page is used to confirm that the survey is complete.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address here (e.g., email input).
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number here (e.g., phone input).
*   **Email Page 2**: Enter your email address here (e.g., email input).
*   **Phone Page 2**: Enter your phone number here (e.g., phone input).
*   **Email Page 3**: Enter your email address here (e.g., email input).

Please review your responses carefully before submitting the form. If you have any questions, feel free to ask.
