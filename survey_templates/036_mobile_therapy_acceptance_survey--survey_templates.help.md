# mobile_therapy_acceptance_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on your mobile therapy preferences and habits, helping us understand your experiences and attitudes towards mobile devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through each question carefully.
2. Select the options that best match your thoughts and feelings.
3. Answer all required questions, indicated by a red star .
4. Use the provided space for any additional comments or explanations.
