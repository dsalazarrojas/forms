# Attractiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information on participants' perception of attractiveness and various factors that influence it. Your responses will contribute to research, and your consent is required for data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a clear and honest manner.
2. Answer all required questions.
3. Select your preferred response options for each question.
4. You may choose to answer questions as anonymous or non-anonymous, but your name and email will help us contact you for follow-up if needed.
5. Review and submit your responses when finished.
