# Nurse Concierge Service Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and data from patients about their experiences with our nurse concierge service. The information collected will help us identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the required information. You can find the fields explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Patient Feedback** 
(patient_feedback)
* This is the main section for providing your feedback about our service. Please let us know about your experience, what we did well, and what we could improve on.

### 2. **Care Quality** 
(care_quality)
* This field allows you to rate the quality of our care. Choose one or more of the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor) to describe your experience.

### 3. **Communication with Nurse** 
(communication_with_nurse)
* Please provide your feedback about how well you were communicated with by our nurses. This can include anything from how clear their instructions were to how responsive they were to your needs.

### 4. **Nurse Availability** 
(nurse_availability)
* If you have any feedback about our nurse's availability, please share it here. For example, how easy was it to get in touch with a nurse when you needed them?

### 5. **Overall Satisfaction** 
(overall_satisfaction)
* Rate your overall satisfaction with our service. This will help us understand how we're meeting your needs.

### 6. **Comments** 
(comments)
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions for us, please let us know in this field. This will help us make improvements and provide even better service.

### 7. **Patient ID** 
(patient_id)
* This is where you can enter your patient ID, if you have one.

### 8. **Facility Name** 
(facility_name)
* Please let us know which facility you visited our nurse at, if you can.

### 9. **Facility Type** 
(facility_type)
* Choose the type of facility where you received our service (Clinic, Hospital, Homecare).

### 10. **Date of Birth** 
(patient_date_of_birth)
* If you're comfortable sharing, please enter your date of birth. This helps us track demographics.
