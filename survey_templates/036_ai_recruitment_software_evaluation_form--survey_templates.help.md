# AI Recruitment Software Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Recruitment Software Evaluation Form is designed to assess the performance and capabilities of AI-driven recruitment software. It consists of several sections that cover various aspects of the software, including its core features, usability, and technical aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your actual experience with the software.
2. Fill out all required fields to ensure a comprehensive evaluation.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
4. If you're unsure about any field, please leave a note in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **1. Software & Vendor Identification**
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`): Please enter your name as the person evaluating the software.
* **Your Primary Role** (`evaluator_role`): Select your primary role related to the software evaluation (e.g., HR / Talent Acquisition Manager, Recruiter / Sourcer, etc.).
* **Software / Vendor Name** (`vendor_software_name`): Enter the name of the software or vendor being evaluated.

### **2. Core AI Capabilities**
* **Resume Parsing & Data Extraction** (`resume_parsing_rating`): Rate the accuracy of converting CVs into structured profiles.
* **Automated Candidate Screening / Ranking** (`automated_screening_rating`): Rate the quality of matches between candidates and job descriptions.
* **Interview Scheduling / Conversational AI** (`interview_automation_rating`): Rate the effectiveness of chatbots and scheduling logic.

### **3. User & Candidate Experience**
* **Ease of use for Recruiting Teams** (`ux_recruiter_rating`): Rate the intuitiveness of the dashboard and workflows.
* **Candidate Experience** (`candidate_experience_rating`): Rate the friction level for applicants during the process.

### **4. Technical, Ethics & Compliance**
* **Integration with existing ATS (Applicant Tracking System)** (`ats_integration_capability`): Indicate whether the software has native integration or requires API / Custom integration.
* **Does the software include explicit bias mitigation or diversity tools?** (`bias_mitigation_tools`): Rate the availability of tools for mitigating bias and promoting diversity.
* **Data Privacy & Compliance** (`compliance_certification`): Select relevant compliance certifications (e.g., GDPR, EEO, etc.).
* **Pricing Model** (`vendor_pricing_model`): Select the pricing model used by the software (e.g., Per Seat / User, etc.).

### **5. Final Recommendation**
* **Final Recommendation** (`final_recommendation`): Indicate your overall recommendation for the software (e.g., Shortlist for Pilot, Approved for Purchase, etc.).
* **Key Reasons for Recommendation** (`recommendation_justification`): Provide your justification for the recommendation, including strengths and weaknesses.

### **6. Next Steps / Action Items**
* **Next Steps / Action Items** (`action_items`): Suggest any next steps or action items for the software development team or vendor.
