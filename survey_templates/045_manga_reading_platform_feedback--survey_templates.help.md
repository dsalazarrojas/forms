# Manga Reading Platform Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for users to provide feedback on their experience with the Manga Reading Platform. This will help us improve the platform and make it more useful for you and other users.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Title**: Enter a brief title for your feedback.
2. Enter your feedback in the **Platform** field, telling us about your favorite manga platform.
3. Select the **Issues** that you experienced while using the platform (e.g., issue 1, issue 2, issue 3, issue 4).
4. **Rating**: Rate your favorite platform from 1-5.
5. **Comments**: Tell us about your experience in the **Comments** field. You can add any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
6. If you'd like, you can also add any **Additional Comments** or **Phone Number**.
7. Finally, enter your **Email** address so we can contact you if we need more information.
8. Review all fields and submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: Enter a brief title for your feedback (e.g., "Feedback on the platform's user interface").
* **Platform**: This is where you'll tell us about your favorite manga platform (e.g., Crunchyroll, Funimation, etc.).
* **Issues**: Select one or more issues that you experienced while using the platform. For example:
	+ Issue 1: "Difficulty navigating the site"
	+ Issue 2: "Slow loading times"
	+ Issue 3: "Inconsistent chapter numbering"
	+ Issue 4: "No favorite feature"
* **Rating**: Rate your favorite platform from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments**: Share your thoughts and experience about the platform.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone Number**: This field is optional and not required. If you'd like to be contacted, please enter your phone number.
* **Additional Comments**: If you'd like to add any additional comments or thoughts, you can do so here.

## Tips

* Please be honest and clear in your feedback.
* Try to be as specific as possible when describing your experience and issues.
* If you have any additional questions or need help, feel free to ask.
