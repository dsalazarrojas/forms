# Household Beverage Container Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about your household's use and habits regarding beverage containers. Your responses will help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions based on the beverage containers you have at home.
2. Make sure to answer all questions honestly, and provide the best information you can.
3. If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Beverage Container** (`household_beverage_container_survey`, `select_one`, **required: false**): Choose one of the options to indicate whether you have a household beverage container or not.
	+ Yes: You have a household beverage container.
	+ No: You do not have a household beverage container.
* **Household Beverage** (`household_beverage`, `text`, **required: false**): Describe the type of beverage you typically store or use in your household beverage container.
* **Size of Container** (`household_beverage_size`, `number`, **required: false**): If you have a household beverage container, please enter its size in milliliters (mL) or liters (L).
* **Type of Beverage** (`beverage_type`, `select_multiple`, **required: false**): Check all that apply to describe the type of beverages you store or use in your household:
	+ Yes: Juice or nectar
	+ Yes: Soda or cola
	+ Yes: Water or seltzer
* **Household Beverage Container Odor** (`household_beverage_container_odor`, `note`, **required: false**): If you have a household beverage container with a distinctive smell, please describe it in this field.
* **Material of Container** (`household_beverage_container_material`, `text`, **required: false**): Describe the material your household beverage container is made of (e.g., plastic, glass, metal, etc.).
* **Reusability** (`household_beverage_container_reusability`, `text`, **required: false**): If you reuse your household beverage containers, describe how you clean and maintain them (e.g., wash, dry, store, etc.).

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
