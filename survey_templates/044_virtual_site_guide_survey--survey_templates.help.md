This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the Virtual Site Guide. It will help us identify areas of improvement and provide a better user experience for future users.

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback about the Virtual Site Guide, including the virtual tour experience, usability, and any suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2.  Select the options that best describe your experience with the Virtual Site Guide.
3.  Fill in any necessary information such as your email and phone number.
4.  Provide any comments or suggestions you have for improving the Virtual Site Guide.
5.  Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Virtual Site Guide Survey** (`1`, `text`, optional): This is the title of the form and is here for your information only.
*   **Virtual tour experience** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Please select how you would rate your experience with the virtual tour, using the options provided.
    *   Virtual Site Guide
    *   Not very good
    *   Not sure
*   **Usability rating** (`3`, `number`, optional): If you have any feedback about the usability of the Virtual Site Guide, please provide a number between 0-5 to rate it.
*   **Improvement suggestion** (`4`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving the Virtual Site Guide, please type them in here.
*   **Email** (`5`, `email`, optional): If you want to be contacted for follow up, please enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`6`, `text`, optional): If you would like to be contacted over the phone for follow-up, please enter your phone number.
*   **Rating and comment** (`7`, `note`, optional): This is a free-text field for any comments or suggestions you have about your experience with the Virtual Site Guide. If you want to rate it, you can enter a number between 0-5.
*   **Submit** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Once you have completed the form, select all the options that apply to submit it.

## Tips

*   Make sure to submit the form only once you have filled out all the fields.
*   Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
