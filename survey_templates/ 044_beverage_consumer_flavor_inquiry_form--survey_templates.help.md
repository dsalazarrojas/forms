# Beverage Consumer Flavor Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from beverage consumers about their experiences and preferences related to a specific product. It helps us to understand their satisfaction, preferences, and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and email address.
2. Enter a valid phone number (optional).
3. Select a date that is relevant to your inquiry.
4. Choose the option that best describes your primary selection for the product.
5. Enter a whole number or rating for the product.
6. Provide any additional details you think are relevant.
7. Confirm any agreement or conditions that apply.
8. Enter a time if scheduling is required (optional).
9. Add any final comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number**: Enter a phone number with country code if you are outside the local region. (Optional)
* **Date**: Select the date that is relevant to your inquiry.
* **Primary selection**: Choose the option that best describes your selection for the product.
	+ **Excellent**: The product met or exceeded your expectations.
	+ **Good**: The product was satisfactory, but not exceptional.
	+ **Fair**: The product was average or met some of your expectations.
	+ **Poor**: The product did not meet your expectations.
* **Quantity or rating**: Enter a whole number or rating for the product.
* **Additional details**: Provide any additional information that is relevant to your inquiry.
* **Agreement**: Select any conditions that apply to your response.
	+ **I agree**: You agree with the terms and conditions.
	+ **I need follow-up**: You need further information or clarification.
	+ **Not applicable**: This condition does not apply to you.
* **Estimated value**: Enter a value in decimal format, if applicable.
* **Preferred time**: Enter a time if scheduling is required. (Optional)
* **Final comments or notes**: Provide any final comments or notes.
