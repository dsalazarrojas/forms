# Organizational Alignment Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Alignment Impact Survey is a tool to measure the strategic focus and capacity of an organization. It helps identify potential gaps between what the organization aims to achieve and what it can realistically deliver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct answer for each page. You will be asked to make selections on several pages.
2.  Ensure you are logged in with your authorized email address.
3.  Fill out all required fields.
4.  Review and submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Alignment (strategy_alignment)
•   **Alignment** (`1`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of alignment between your current strategic focus and your organization's capacity. Select 1 if you are aligned with your current capacity, 2 if you need to make some adjustments, and 3 if there is a significant gap.

### Strategic Focus (strategic_focus)
•   **Strategic Focus** (`A`, `B`, `C`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the relevant strategic focus areas for your organization. These will be areas that you are currently focusing on and expect to make an impact.

### Organisational Capacity (organizational_capacity)
•   **Organisational Capacity** (number, required): Enter the numerical value that represents your organization's current capacity. Please note that this field requires a numerical answer, so enter only numbers.

### Impact On The Organisation (impact_on_organisation)
•   **Impact On The Organisation** (`A`, `B`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the areas where you expect the impact of your strategy on your organization to be significant. These will be the effects your strategic changes will have.

### What will be the impact of this strategy on the organization (strategy_impact)
•   **What will be the impact of this strategy on the organization** (select one: `A`, `B`, `C`, required): Choose how you expect your strategic changes to impact your organization. Select A for beneficial, B for neutral, and C for negative.

### Submission Date (submission_date) and Submission Time (submission_time)
•   **Submission Date** (date, required): Enter the date you submit this form.
•   **Submission Time** (time, required): Enter the exact time you submit this form.

### Email (email_address)
•   **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.

### Phone (phone_number)
•   **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.

### Comments (comments_2)
•   **Comments 2** (text, optional): Enter additional comments if you need to provide more context or insights.

Note: Please ensure that you have permission to access and fill out this form, and that your answers reflect your genuine opinions and experiences.
