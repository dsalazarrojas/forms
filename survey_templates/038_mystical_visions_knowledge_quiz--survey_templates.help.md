# Mystical Visions Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mystical Visions Knowledge Quiz form is designed to collect user feedback on the Mystical Visions Knowledge Quiz. This form is intended to gather information on user's experience, knowledge, and preferences related to the quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "main_page" tab to start.
2. Read and understand the content on the "intro_page".
3. Answer the questions on the "questions_page" by selecting one or multiple options.
4. Provide a time estimate for the questions on the "answers_page".
5. Finally, submit your feedback on the "submit" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **main_page** (text, required: false): This is the first page of the form where you will start your feedback journey. Read the instructions and introduction provided.
* **intro** (text, required: false): This page explains the background and context of the Mystical Visions Knowledge Quiz. Take a moment to read and understand.
* **questions** (select multiple, required: false): On this page, select all the options that apply to your experience with the Mystical Visions Knowledge Quiz.
	+ 'Yes': You have had a positive experience with the quiz.
	+ 'No': You have not had an experience with the quiz.
* **answers** (time, required: false): Estimate the time you spent on the quiz.
* **submit** (select one, required: false): Confirm your decision to submit your feedback.
