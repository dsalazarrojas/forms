# Reading Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on your reading habits and attitudes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the frequency with which you engage in various forms of reading. For each question, choose one of the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teachers Name**: This is a required field for identification purposes.
* **Student ID**: This is a required field for identification purposes.
* **How often do you read for leisure?**: Select how often you read for pleasure, such as novels, stories, or other types of fiction.
* **How often do you read for education or personal development?**: Select how often you read non-fiction, such as textbooks, articles, or online courses.
* **How often do you read for work or professional purposes?**: Select how often you read materials related to your job or profession.
* **How often do you read books or articles that challenge your opinions or worldviews?**: Select how often you read content that challenges your views on politics, social issues, or other topics.
* **How often do you engage in other forms of media?**: Select how often you listen to podcasts, watch documentaries, or watch videos.
* **How often do you share or discuss what you have read with others?**: Select how often you share or discuss what you have read with friends, family, or colleagues.
* **How often do you read fiction for pleasure?**: Select how often you read stories, novels, or other types of fiction for enjoyment.
* **How often do you read non-fiction for pleasure?**: Select how often you read articles, online courses, or other types of non-fiction for pleasure.
* **How often do you read self-help or motivational books?**: Select how often you read books on self-improvement, motivation, or inspiration.
* **How often do you read books or articles on politics or social issues?**: Select how often you read content on politics, social issues, or current events.
* **How often do you read online reviews or ratings of products or services?**: Select how often you read reviews on products, services, or companies.
* **How often do you read or listen to audiobooks or podcasts?**: Select how often you listen to or read audiobooks, podcasts, or other types of audio content.
* **Other (please specify)**: This is an optional field for any other form of reading not listed above.
* **How often do you read on a typical day?**: Select how often you read on an average day.
* **How long do you typically read in a single sitting?**: Enter the number of hours you typically read in a single session.
* **What do you usually read for?**: Select the main purpose of your reading, such as leisure, education, or work.
* **Other (please specify)**: This is an optional field for any other purpose not listed above.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can select multiple options for the "What do you usually read for?" question.
* You are free to answer any or all of the questions in this survey.
