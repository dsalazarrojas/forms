# tech_product_user_experience_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback on technical products, covering various aspects such as user experience, product usability, and support channels.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant information for each field to provide an honest representation of your experience with the technical product.
2. Be as specific as possible when answering questions, especially when it comes to usability issues and support channels.
3. If a question doesn't apply to your experience, select "No" or "N/A" (Not Applicable).
