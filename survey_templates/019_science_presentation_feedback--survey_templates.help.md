# Science Presentation Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Science Presentation Feedback form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from users about science presentations. This form helps to evaluate the quality and content of the presentations, providing valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Rate the presentation**: Rate the presentation on the scale provided under "Science Talk Evaluation".
2. Fill out the "Rating" field with your feedback about the presentation. 
3. Provide any comments or suggestions for improvement in the "Comments" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback for the "Improve Areas" section, please fill out the fields accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Science Talk Id (1)**: Enter the ID number of the presentation you are providing feedback for.
* **Rating (2)**: Choose one of the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) to rate the presentation.
* **Feedback (3)**: Provide a brief comment about the presentation. 
* **Comments (4)**: Add any additional comments about the presentation.
* **Speaker (8)**: Mention the name of the speaker of the presentation.
* **Title (9)**: Enter the title of the presentation.
* **Likers (10)**: Count the number of people who liked the presentation.
* **Dislikers (11)**: Count the number of people who disliked the presentation.
* **Comments (12)**: Add any additional comments for the "Comments" field.
* **Suggestions (13)**: Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Improve Areas (14)**: Specify areas where the presentation needs improvement.
* **Improve Areas Comments (15)**: Add any comments for the "Improve Areas" section.
* **Improve Areas Suggestions (16)**: Add any additional suggestions for improvement.
