# post_graduation_career_outcome_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a post-graduation career outcome survey designed to collect information about your current job and career status after graduating. It will help us understand your career path, job satisfaction, industry, and other related aspects of your post-graduation career outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your **Current Job** from the provided options.
2. Enter the **Years of Experience** if you've worked in your current job for more than 1 year.
3. Rate your **Job Satisfaction** on a scale of 0 to 5.
4. Select your **Job Satisfaction Scale** from the provided options.
5. Choose the **Industry** you're working in from the provided options (if applicable).
6. Select the **Job Function** that best describes your role.
7. Enter the **Job Location** where you work.
8. Choose the **Highest Education Level** achieved.
9. Select the **Job Type** that best describes your job (if applicable).
10. Choose the **Job Sector** that best describes your job (if applicable).
11. Enter the **Salary Range** of your current job.
12. Select the company size of your current employer.
13. Select the **Job Classification** that best describes your job.
14. Enter the **Job Title** of your current job.
15. Enter a brief description of your **Job Duties**.
16. Enter a brief description of the **Job Requirements**.
17. Enter a brief description of your **Job Description**.
18. Enter a brief description of your **Job Responsibilities**.
19. Enter a brief description of your **Job Abilities**.
20. Enter a brief description of the **Job Skills** required for your job.
21. Enter a brief description of your **Job Experience**.
22. Enter a brief description of your **Education Background**.
23. Enter a brief description of your **Career Outcome**.
24. Enter any additional **Survey Comments** you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Current Job**: This is the job you're currently working in. Select the option that best describes your current job.
* **Years of Experience**: If you've worked in your current job for more than 1 year, enter the number of years you've been working.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 0 to 5, where 0 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Job Satisfaction Scale**: Select the option that best describes your current level of job satisfaction.
* **Industry**: Select the industry you're working in from the provided options.
* **Job Function**: Select the job function that best describes your role.
* **Job Location**: Enter the location where you work.
* **Highest Education**: Choose the highest level of education you've achieved.
* **Job Type**: Select the job type that best describes your job (if applicable).
* **Job Sector**: Select the job sector that best describes your job (if applicable).
* **Salary Range**: Enter the salary range of your current job.
* **Company Size**: Select the company size of your current employer.
* **Job Classification**: Select the job classification that best describes your job.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of your current job.
* **Job Duties**: Enter a brief description of your job duties.
* **Job Requirements**: Enter a brief description of the job requirements.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job description.
* **Job Responsibilities**: Enter a brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **Job Abilities**: Enter a brief description of your job abilities.
* **Job Skills**: Enter a brief description of the job skills required for your job.
* **Job Experience**: Enter a brief description of your job experience.
* **Education Background**: Enter a brief description of your education background.
* **Career Outcome**: Enter a brief description of your career outcome.
* **Survey Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your experience with this survey.

Note: The survey questions are designed to be completed by post-graduates of our program, and the fields are labeled to provide context and guidance for your answers. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
