# Milk Donation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Milk Donation Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from milk donors to help the organization improve its milk donation program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Donator ID" field and choose the correct ID for your donation. This will help us track your feedback to your specific donation.
2. Rate your experience by selecting a number between 1-9 in the "Rating" field.
3. Provide any comments or additional feedback in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter the date of your donation in the "Donation Date" field, using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
5. Select the contact method you would like to be used to get in touch with you, such as "Mobile", "Email", or "Mail".
6. If you would like to receive any further communication, please enter a satisfaction level (1-9) in the "Satisfaction" field.
7. Enter any suggestions you have for how we can improve in the "Suggestions" field.
8. Finally, select the name of your Donor Organization from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donator Information**: Please provide your name and any other information about yourself as the milk donor.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with our milk donation program. A higher number indicates a more positive experience.
* **Comments**: Share any thoughts or comments you have about your experience with our milk donation program.
* **Donation Date**: Enter the date of your milk donation in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Contact Method**: Select the method you would like us to use to get in touch with you, such as mobile, email, or mail.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your level of satisfaction with your experience (1-9).
* **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions you have on how we can improve our milk donation program.
* **Donator ID**: Please select the correct ID for your donation from the list.
* **Donor Organization**: Select the name of the organization that you donated to.
* **Mobile**: Select if you would like to be contacted by mobile phone.
* **Email**: Select if you would like to be contacted by email.
* **Mail**: Select if you would like to be contacted by mail.
* **Other**: Select if you would like to be contacted by other means.
* **Suggestion**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience.
* **Donator A, B, C, D, E, and F**: Select the correct Donator ID from the list.
* **Donator Org A, B, C, D, E**: Select the correct Donor Organization from the list.
