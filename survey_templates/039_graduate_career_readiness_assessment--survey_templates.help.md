# Graduate Career Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Graduate Career Readiness Assessment" is a tool designed to assess your readiness for career advancement. It helps us understand your current career goals, job search status, and any support needs you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please go through each page step-by-step:

* Page 1: Graduates introduction - A brief introduction to your current situation.
* Page 2: Graduates career goals - Describe your short-term and long-term career goals.
* Page 3: Job search - Answer whether you are currently job searching.
* Page 4: Graduates education background - Provide a brief description of your educational background.
* Page 5: Graduates work experience - Describe your work experience, if any.
* Page 6: Graduates career support needs - Identify any support needs you have for career advancement.
* Page 7: Job search status - Select your current job search status.
* Page 8: Job search outcome - Describe the outcome of your last job search.
* Page 9: Job offer - If you have a job offer, enter the number of offers you have received.
* Page 10: Job acceptance - Answer whether you have accepted a job offer.
* Page 11: Job title - Describe your job title if you have accepted a job offer.
* Page 12: Job description - Briefly describe your job role.
* Page 13: Job location - Specify your job location.
* Page 14: Job salary range - Answer if you expect a certain salary range for your new job.
* Page 15: Job type - Select if you are expecting a specific job type.
* Page 16: Job status - Select the status of your current or new job.
* Page 17: Job outcome - Answer whether you expect a certain job outcome.
* Page 18: Graduates outcome - Answer whether you expect a certain outcome for your graduates.
* Page 19: Graduates career readiness score - Enter your self-assessment of your career readiness.
* Page 20: Graduates career counseling needs - Identify any career counseling needs you have.
* Page 21: Graduates career services - Describe any career services you are interested in.
* Page 22: Graduates follow up - Answer whether you would like to be contacted for follow up.
* Page 23: Graduates career pathway - Describe your career pathway.
* Page 24: Job placement services - Describe any job placement services you are interested in.
* Page 25: Job assistance programs - Describe any job assistance programs you are interested in.
