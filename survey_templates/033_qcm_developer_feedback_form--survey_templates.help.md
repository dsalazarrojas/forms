# QCM Developer Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The QCM Developer Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback and insights from developers on their experience with a particular submission. This form is used to gather information on the developer's satisfaction, areas of improvement, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions relevant to your experience with the submission.
2. Be honest and specific with your feedback, focusing on areas that need improvement.
3. Use the available space to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
4. Ensure that your email address is correct for follow-up communication.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`note`, optional): This field is a text box for you to briefly introduce yourself and provide any context relevant to your feedback.
* **Developer Insights** (`text`, optional): Please provide any specific insights or observations about the submission, including any notable strengths and weaknesses.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`number`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the submission on a scale of 1-5.
* **Improvement Areas** (`select_one`, optional): Select one of the options provided to indicate areas that need improvement:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Feedback** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions on how we can improve our processes or services.
* **Developer Comments** (`text`, optional): Add any further comments or insights from your perspective as a developer.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you for follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Submission Review** (`time`, optional): Please estimate the time spent reviewing the submission.
* **Review** (`text`, optional): Add any final comments or observations about the submission.

Note: All fields are optional, but your feedback is crucial for us to improve our services.
