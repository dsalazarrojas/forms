# Event Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information about an event, allowing event organizers to assess the success and satisfaction of the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please answer each question carefully and honestly. You may need to scroll down to see all the questions.
2. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the respective fields.
3. Enter the Date of the event.
4. Fill in the Start and End Time of the event.
5. Enter the Location of the event.
6. Write a brief description of the event.
7. Answer the survey questions by selecting the corresponding options.
8. Provide feedback comments about your experience.
9. Write any comments or feedback about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`event_survey_first_name`, `text`, Required: false): Fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`event_survey_last_name`, `text`, Required: false): Fill in your last name.
* **Email** (`event_survey_email`, `email`, Required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Event Date** (`event_survey_event_date`, `date`, Required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Start Time** (`event_survey_start_time`, `time`, Required: false): Select the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`event_survey_end_time`, `time`, Required: false): Select the end time of the event.
* **Location** (`event_survey_location`, `text`, Required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_survey_event_description`, `text`, Required: false): Write a brief description of the event.
* **Survey Questions** (`event_survey_survey_questions`, `text`, Required: false): Answer the survey questions as instructed.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`event_survey_participant_satisfaction`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select your level of satisfaction with the event.
* **Participant Engagement** (`event_survey_participant_engagement`, `select_multiple`, Required: false): Select all that apply regarding your engagement with the event.
* **Feedback Comments** (`event_survey_feedback_comments`, `text`, Required: false): Provide feedback comments about your experience.
* **Participant Comments** (`event_survey_participant_comments`, `text`, Required: false): Write any comments or feedback about the event.
* **Survey Status** (`event_survey_survey_status`, `select_one`, Required: false): Select the survey status.
* **Survey Completed** (`event_survey_survey_completed`, `select_one`, Required: false): Select if you have completed the survey.

## Tips

- Make sure to answer all the questions honestly and carefully.
- If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them in the Feedback Comments or Participant Comments fields.
- If you have any questions or issues, contact the event organizers.
