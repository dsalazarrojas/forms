# producer_profile_2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information about existing producers for internal purposes such as categorization and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Enter your producer name.
3. Enter your producer title.
4. Enter your level of experience.
5. Enter your contact email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. (Optional) Enter any notes about yourself.
8. Select your producer profile from the list of options.
9. Select your producer interests from the list of options.
10. Select your producer goals from the list of options.
11. Select your producer value from the list of options.
12. Select your producer quality from the list of options.
13. (Optional) Enter any additional notes about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Producer Name** (`producer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it is to be displayed.
* **Producer Title** (`producer_title`, text, required): Enter your title or position.
* **Producer Experience** (`producer_experience`, number, required): Enter your level of experience, e.g., 1-10 years.
* **Producer Location** (`producer_location`, text, optional): Enter your location, e.g., city, state, or country.
* **Producer Contact** (`producer_contact`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Producer Phone** (`producer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Producer Notes** (`producer_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about yourself.
* **Producer Profile** (`producer_profile`, select_multiple, required): Select a profile type from the list of options, e.g., Producer A, Producer B, etc.
* **Producer Interests** (`producer_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select your producer interests from the list of options, e.g., Marketing, Sales, etc.
* **Producer Goals** (`producer_goals`, select_one, required): Select your producer goals from the list of options, e.g., Increase Sales, Improve Efficiency, etc.
* **Producer Value** (`producer_value`, select_one, required): Select your producer value from the list of options, e.g., Cost-effective, High-quality, etc.
* **Producer Quality** (`producer_quality`, select_multiple, required): Select your producer quality from the list of options, e.g., Fast, Reliable, etc.
* **Producer Notes** (`producer_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about yourself.
