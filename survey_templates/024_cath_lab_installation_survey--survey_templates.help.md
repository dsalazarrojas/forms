# Cath Lab Installation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about the installation of a cath lab in a healthcare facility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the required information about the facility, including the hospital or clinic name, contact name, contact email, and contact phone number.
2. Enter the address of the facility and the location of the cath lab within the facility.
3. List any existing cardiac equipment in the facility.
4. Provide the dimensions of the cath lab room and the ceiling height.
5. Select the type and condition of the existing flooring.
6. Describe the electrical service available, including amperage.
7. Confirm the availability of a backup power system.
8. Choose the status of the HVAC system.
9. Confirm the availability of plumbing access.
10. Confirm the presence of radiation shielding in place.
11. Confirm if a radiation safety officer is on staff.
12. Confirm the preferred installation start date.
13. Enter any additional requirements or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hospital or Clinic Name** (`hospital_name`, required): Enter the name of the hospital or clinic where the cath lab will be installed.
* **Contact Name** (`contact`, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this installation.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, required): Enter the full address of the facility, including suite number.
* **Cath Lab Location** (`cath_lab_location`, required): Enter the location of the cath lab within the facility.
* **Existing Cardiac Equipment** (`existing_equipment`, optional): List any existing cardiac equipment in the facility.
* **Cath Lab Room Dimensions** (`room_dimensions`, required): Enter the dimensions of the cath lab room in feet (length x width x height).
* **Ceiling Height** (`ceiling_height`, required): Enter the height of the ceiling in feet.
* **Flooring Type** (`flooring_type`, required): Select the type of flooring (Concrete, Tile, Linoleum, Wood, or Other).
* **Flooring Condition** (`flooring_condition`, required): Select the condition of the flooring (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Needs Replacement).
* **Electrical Service** (`electrical_service`, required): Enter the electrical service available, including amperage.
* **Backup Power System** (`backup_power`, required): Confirm if a backup power system is available.
* **HVAC System Status** (`hvac_system`, required): Select the status of the HVAC system (Adequate, Needs Upgrade, Planned Upgrade, or Inadequate).
* **Plumbing Access** (`plumbing_access`, required): Confirm if plumbing access is available.
* **Radiation Shielding** (`shielding_in_place`, required): Confirm if radiation shielding is in place.
* **Radiation Safety Officer** (`radiation_safety_officer`, required): Confirm if a radiation safety officer is on staff.
* **Preferred Installation Start Date** (`preferred_installation_date`, required): Enter the preferred start date for the installation.
* **Additional Requirements** (`additional_requirements`, optional): Enter any additional requirements or notes.
