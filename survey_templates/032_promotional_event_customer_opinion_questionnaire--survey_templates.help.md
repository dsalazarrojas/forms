# promotional_event_customer_opinion_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect customer feedback and opinions about promotional events. It helps us understand customer satisfaction, gather feedback, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required and optional fields based on your experience with the promotional event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the promotional event you attended.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, optional): If you provided any information about yourself as an attendee, please describe it here.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`, text, optional): Please provide your thoughts and suggestions for the event.
* **Event Satisfaction** (`event_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select the level of satisfaction with the event:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Recommend Event** (`recommend_event`, select_multiple, optional): If you would recommend this event, please select all that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive more information about future events, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to provide your phone number for follow-up questions or to receive more information, please enter it here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please indicate the date you attended the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please indicate the time you attended the event.
