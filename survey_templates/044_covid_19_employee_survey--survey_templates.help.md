# COVID 19 Employee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees about their experiences and opinions on COVID-19 policies and protocols within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your abilities.
- You can select one or multiple options for each question, depending on the type of question.
- You can also provide additional comments and suggestions in the text fields.
- If you've already completed a similar survey, please don't repeat your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): This is the date you're completing this survey. It's essential to keep a record of when you answered the questions.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as an employee of the company.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, optional): If you're an active employee, enter your employee ID. If you're a former employee, you can leave this blank.
- **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your department or team.
- **How Long Have You Worked Here?** (`tenure`, `text`, optional): If you've been with the company for a certain amount of time, enter the number of months or years.
- **Primary Work Location** (`work_location`, `select_one`, required): Select the location where you primarily work, which can be Office, Remote, Hybrid, or Field.
- **How has COVID-19 Impacted Your Work?** (`covid_impact`, `text`, optional): Describe how the pandemic has affected your work, if at all.
- **Do you have Workplace Safety Concerns?** (`workplace_safety_concerns`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have minor, moderate, or significant concerns about workplace safety.
- **Please Describe Your Safety Concerns** (`safety_concern_details`, `text`, optional): If you have concerns, describe them in this text field.
- **Do You Think Masks Should Be Required?** (`masks_required`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you think masks should be mandatory or not.
- **How Do You Feel About Vaccination Requirements?** (`vaccination_policy`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you feel about vaccination requirements, from strongly supporting to strongly opposing.
- **Should Regular COVID-19 Testing Be Required?** (`testing_opinion`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you think regular testing should be mandatory, or if you're unsure.
- **Are You Comfortable Returning to the Office?** (`return_to_office`, `select_one`, required): Select how comfortable you are with returning to the office, from very uncomfortable to very comfortable.
- **Would You Like to Continue Remote Work Options?** (`remote_work_continuation`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you'd like to continue working remotely, hybrid, or return to the office.
- **Have You Needed Mental Health Support Due to COVID?** (`mental_health_support`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you've needed, would like to access, or prefer not to access mental health support.
- **How Clear Is Company Communication About COVID Policies?** (`company_communication`, `select_one`, optional): Rate how clear the company's communication about COVID policies is, from very unclear to very clear.
- **Do You Think COVID Policies Are Fair?** (`policy_fairness`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you think the policies are very fair, somewhat fair, neutral, unfair, or very unfair.
- **Do You Feel Supported by Management During the Pandemic?** (`management_support`, `select_one`, optional): Select how supported you feel by management, from very supported to very unsupported.
- **What Are Your Main Concerns or Issues?** (`employee_concerns`, `text`, optional): Describe your main concerns or issues related to COVID-19 and the workplace.
- **How Can the Company Improve Its COVID Response?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Share your ideas on how the company can improve its response to the pandemic.
- **How Comfortable Are You Sharing Feedback?** (`feedback_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how comfortable you are sharing feedback, from fully transparent to preferring anonymity.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any other thoughts or comments about COVID-19 at work.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Keep your answers brief and clear.
- If you need to provide additional context, use the text fields.
- If you're unsure about any question, choose the option that best fits your feelings or experiences.
