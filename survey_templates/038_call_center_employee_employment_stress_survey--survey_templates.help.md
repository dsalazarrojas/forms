# Call Center Employee Employment Stress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Call Center Employee Employment Stress Survey is designed to measure the well-being and job satisfaction of call center employees. It collects information on various aspects of their work experience, including workload, management support, and main stressors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your experiences and feelings.
3. If a question has multiple options, select all that apply.
4. Fill out all the sections and answer all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (Employee ID, Department): This section is for collecting personal information about the respondent, including their name and department.
	+ This is an optional field.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID): This is another optional field for collecting employee ID information.
* **Department** (Department): This section is mandatory and collects the department the respondent is working in.
* **How Long Have You Worked Here?** (Tenure): This question asks for the length of time the respondent has been working in their current department.
	+ Select one of the options: Less than 3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or More than 5 years.
* **How Would You Rate Your Current Workload?** (Workload Level): This question gauges the respondent's perception of their current workload.
	+ Select one of the options: Far too light, Light, Moderate, Heavy, Far too heavy.
* **Does Call Volume Cause You Stress?** (Call Volume Stress): This question asks whether the high call volume causes stress to the respondent.
	+ Select one of the options: Not at all, Slightly, Moderately, Significantly, Extremely.
* **Do You Feel Time Pressure During Your Shift?** (Time Pressure): This question inquires about the respondent's feeling of time pressure during their shift.
	+ Select one of the options: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Always.
* **Overall Job Satisfaction** (Job Satisfaction): This question gauges the respondent's overall job satisfaction.
	+ Select one of the options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **I Feel Fulfilled in My Role** (Role Satisfaction): This question asks whether the respondent feels fulfilled in their role.
	+ Select one of the options: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly disagree.
* **What Is Your Current Stress Level?** (Stress Level): This question asks about the respondent's current stress level.
	+ Select one of the options: Very low, Low, Moderate, High, Very high.
* **Do You Experience Physical Fatigue?** (Physical Fatigue): This question inquires whether the respondent experiences physical fatigue.
	+ Select one of the options: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Always.
* **Do You Experience Emotional Exhaustion?** (Emotional Exhaustion): This question asks whether the respondent experiences emotional exhaustion.
	+ Select one of the options: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Always.
* **Do You Feel Supported by Your Manager?** (Manager Support): This question inquires about the respondent's feeling of being supported by their manager.
	+ Select one of the options: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly disagree.
* **Do Your Teammates Support You?** (Team Support): This question asks whether the respondent feels supported by their teammates.
	+ Select one of the options: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly disagree.
* **Do You Have Adequate Training and Resources?** (Training Resources): This question inquires about the respondent's perception of having adequate training and resources.
	+ Select one of the options: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly disagree.
* **What Are Your Main Stressors?** (Main Stressors): This question asks the respondent to select all the main stressors that they face in their work.
	+ Select all that apply: High call volume, Difficult customers, Unrealistic targets, Lack of support, Technical issues, Work-life balance, Career advancement concerns, Compensation, Other.
* **Suggestions for Reducing Stress** (Suggestions): This question asks the respondent to provide any suggestions they have for reducing stress.
	+ Fill in the open-ended text field.
