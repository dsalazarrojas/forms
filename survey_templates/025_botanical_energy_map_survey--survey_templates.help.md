# Botanical Energy Map Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Botanical Energy Map Survey is designed to gather insights into the impact of green spaces on individuals' physical and emotional well-being. By providing accurate and honest responses, you will contribute to the development of more livable and sustainable urban environments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring you answer all required questions.
2. For each question, provide honest and specific responses based on your actual experience.
3. Take your time to ensure you complete the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information

* **Participant ID / Name** (`part_id`): This is a unique identifier for you as a participant. Please provide your name as it appears on your ID or as you would like to be referenced in the survey results.
* **Date of Visit** (`visit_dt`): This is the date you visited the green space or garden.
* **Time of Visit** (`visit_tm`): This is the time you spent in the green space or garden.

### Perception of Vitality

* **How would you rate the overall energy / vitality of this space?** (`energy_rating`): Choose one of the four options:
	+ High - Vibrant
	+ Moderate - Balanced
	+ Low - Calming
	+ Depleted
* **What elements contribute to this feeling?** (`contrib_elements`): Select all that apply:
	+ Birdsong
	+ Lush Foliage
	+ Moving Water
	+ Sunlight
	+ Fragrance
	+ Colorful Flowers

### Map Mapping

* **Specific location within the space** (`spot_loc`): Describe the exact location within the garden or green space that you found most energizing or peaceful.

### Emotional Response

* **What is your primary emotional response to this spot?** (`emotional_resp`): Choose one of the five options:
	+ Peaceful
	+ Energized
	+ Inspired
	+ Melancholy
	+ Safe

### Environmental Factors

* **How well-maintained does this space feel?** (`maint_rating`): Choose one of the three options:
	+ Pristine
	+ Well Kept
	+ Natural/Wild
	+ Neglected

### Environmental Factors

* **How crowded is the space today?** (`crowd_lvl`): Choose one of the four options:
	+ Empty
	+ Few People
	+ Moderately Busy
	+ Very Crowded

### Botanical Notes

* **Describe any specific botanical features that caught your attention** (`botanical_notes`): Describe any notable plants, flowers, or other features of the garden or green space that stood out to you.

### Submission

* **Submission Date** (`sub_date`): This will be automatically filled in with the date and time you submit the form.

By following this guide, you will ensure that your responses are accurate and helpful in creating a more livable and sustainable urban environment. Thank you for participating in this survey!
