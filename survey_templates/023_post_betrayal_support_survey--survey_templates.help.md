# Post Betrayal Support Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals who have experienced betrayal in their relationships assess their current well-being, identify their support needs, and explore recovery patterns and resources. It's essential to complete this form honestly, as the data will help us provide more accurate support and guidance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all questions honestly, and select all applicable options for each question.
2. For select_multiple questions, choose all applicable options that apply to you.
3. For select_one questions, choose only one option that best describes your situation.
4. For number questions, enter a whole number that represents your desired frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the current level of your overall well-being?**
  - This question is to gauge your current emotional state. Select the option that best describes your well-being level at this moment.
  - Options: Very Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor
  - Required: Yes

* **What kind of support do you need in your life right now?**
  - This question helps us understand your support needs. Select all options that apply to you.
  - Options: Emotional Support, Practical Support, Spiritual Support, Physical Support

* **What recovery patterns you are familiar with or interested in?**
  - This question is to explore your interests and knowledge in recovery patterns. Select all options that apply to you.
  - Options: Counseling, Coaching, Peer Support, Online Support

* **What kind of current support do you have in place?**
  - This question helps us understand your current support systems. Select all options that apply to you.
  - Options: Spouse, Family, Friend, Peer Support Group, Online Support

* **Currently seeing a therapist or counselor?**
  - This question is to gather information about your current involvement with counseling. Select one option that best describes your situation.
  - Options: True, False, Undecided
  - Required: No

* **How often would you like to meet with a counselor?**
  - This question is to learn about your desired meeting frequency with a counselor. Enter a whole number that represents your desired frequency.
  - Required: No

* **Currently seeing a coach or mentor?**
  - This question is to understand your current involvement with coaching. Select one option that best describes your situation.
  - Options: True, False, Undecided
  - Required: No

* **How often would you like to meet with a coach or mentor?**
  - This question is to learn about your desired meeting frequency with a coach or mentor. Enter a whole number that represents your desired frequency.
  - Required: No

* **Currently part of a peer support group?**
  - This question is to gather information about your involvement with a peer support group. Select one option that best describes your situation.
  - Options: True, False
  - Required: No

* **How often do you plan to meet with a peer support group?**
  - This question is to learn about your desired meeting frequency with a peer support group. Enter a whole number that represents your desired frequency.
  - Required: No
