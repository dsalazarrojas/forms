# matrix evaluation survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **matrix evaluation survey** form is designed to assess performance across various aspects and periods. It helps gather data and insights to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **survey name** to identify the purpose of the survey.
2. Select the **performance area** that you want to evaluate (e.g., area1, area2, or area3).
3. Choose the **evaluation period** when the performance was being evaluated (e.g., 2022-01-01 to 2022-01-31).
4. Enter the **evaluation start date** when the evaluation started.
5. Enter the **evaluation end date** when the evaluation ended.
6. Optionally, provide a **evaluation score** out of a maximum of 10.
7. Add any relevant **evaluation comments**.
8. Select the **performance area 2** to evaluate (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
9. Select the **evaluation period 2** when the performance was being evaluated (e.g., Option 1 or Option 2).
10. Optionally, select the **evaluation score 2** out of a maximum of 10.
11. Add any relevant **evaluation comments 2**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Name** (`survey_name`, `text`, required): Enter a name to identify the purpose of the survey.
* **Performance Area** (`performance_area`, `select_one`, required): Select the area you want to evaluate (e.g., area1, area2, or area3).
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, `date`, required): Choose the period when the performance was evaluated (e.g., 2022-01-01 to 2022-01-31).
* **Evaluation Start** (`evaluation_start`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation End** (`evaluation_end`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, `number`, optional): Enter a score out of 10 to evaluate performance.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any relevant comments about the evaluation.
* **Performance Area 2** (`performance_area_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select additional areas to evaluate (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Evaluation Period 2** (`evaluation_period_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the period when the performance was evaluated (e.g., Option 1 or Option 2).
* **Evaluation Score 2** (`evaluation_score_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select a score out of 10 to evaluate performance.
* **Evaluation Comments 2** (`evaluation_comments_2`, `text`, optional): Add any relevant comments about the evaluation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use the **Evaluation Score** field to provide a score out of 10.
* Use the **Evaluation Period** field to select a specific period.
* Use the **Evaluation Period 2** field to select a specific period for additional evaluations.
* You can add multiple scores and comments for each evaluation period.
