# COVID 19 Holiday Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about how individuals plan to celebrate holidays during the COVID-19 pandemic. This will help in understanding the current situation and preparing for future holiday celebrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields. You will be asked for your name, email address, and a few questions about your holiday plans and COVID-19 precautions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1): Your name is required to identify you as a respondent.
* **Email Address** (2): Your email address will be used to contact you for follow-up, if necessary.
* **Survey Date** (3): This field is not relevant to the form's purpose and can be ignored.
* **Planning to Celebrate Holiday** (5): Select "Yes" if you plan to celebrate a holiday.
* **Which Holiday** (6): Choose the holiday you plan to celebrate.
* **Where Will You Celebrate** (7): Select where you plan to celebrate the holiday.
* **Number of People in Gathering** (8): If you are planning a gathering, provide the estimated number of people.
* **Planning to Travel** (10): Select "Yes" if you plan to travel for the holiday.
* **Travel Destination** (11): If you plan to travel, provide the destination.
* **Duration of Trip** (12): If you plan to travel, provide the length of your trip.
* **Mode of Transportation** (13): Select the mode of transportation you plan to use for travel.
* **Taking COVID-19 Precautions** (15): Select "Yes" if you plan to take precautions to prevent the spread of COVID-19.
* **Types of Precautions** (16): Select all the precautions you are taking or intend to take.
* **Vaccination Status** (18): Provide your vaccination status.
* **Household Members Vaccinated** (19): If you are vaccinated, select whether your household members are also vaccinated.
* **Vaccination Status** (22): This question seems to be a duplicate of 18, but it is not relevant to the form's purpose.
* **Confidence in Safety Measures** (22): Rate your confidence in the safety measures you are taking or intend to take.
* **COVID-19 Concerns** (21): If you have any COVID-19-related concerns, provide them in this text field.
* **Behavior Change** (23): Select "Yes" if your plans have changed due to COVID-19 concerns.
* **May We Contact You for Follow-up** (25): Select "Yes" if you are open to being contacted for follow-up surveys or questions.
