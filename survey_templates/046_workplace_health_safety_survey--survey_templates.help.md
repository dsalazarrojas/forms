# Workplace Health Safety Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather your experiences, concerns, and perceptions regarding workplace health and safety. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and provide a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the most appropriate option that best describes your experience or perception.
3. Do not skip any pages or questions.
4. If you have any issues with the form or need help, contact form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: What is your work environment like?
* **What is your work environment like?** (Text field): Describe your typical work environment, including your workspace, colleagues, and supervisor.
### Page 2: Have you experienced any workplace hazards?
* **Have you experienced any workplace hazards?** (Select one):
	+ Agreed and reported the incident: If you have reported a hazard and taken action.
	+ Not reported the incident: If you have not reported a hazard.
	+ Did not experience: If you have not encountered any hazards.
### Page 3: Do you know how to access the company's safety and health policy documents?
* **Do you know how to access the company's safety and health policy documents?** (Text field): Describe how you access policy documents, such as procedures and guidelines.
### Page 4: Have you ever reported a safety or health issue?
* **Have you ever reported a safety or health issue?** (Select one):
	+ Always: If you have consistently reported issues.
	+ Frequently: If you have reported issues several times.
	+ Sometimes: If you have reported issues occasionally.
	+ Never: If you have never reported any issues.
### Page 5: Do you have access to personal protective equipment (PPE) when needed?
* **Do you have access to personal protective equipment (PPE) when needed?** (Checkbox):
	+ Option A: Always.
	+ Option B: Not always.
### Page 6: Do you feel supported by your supervisor or manager regarding workplace safety?
* **Do you feel supported by your supervisor or manager regarding workplace safety?** (Select multiple):
	+ Strongly agree: If you feel strongly supported.
	+ Agree: If you feel somewhat supported.
	+ Neutral: If you feel indifferent.
	+ Disagree: If you feel somewhat unsupported.
	+ Strongly disagree: If you feel strongly unsupported.
### Page 7: How often do you feel that your work environment is safe?
* **How often do you feel that your work environment is safe?** (Select one):
	+ Always safe: If you always feel safe.
	+ Mostly safe: If you feel safe most of the time.
	+ Sometimes safe: If you feel safe occasionally.
	+ Not safe: If you feel unsafe occasionally.
	+ Not at all safe: If you never feel safe.
### Page 8: Would you like to report incidents or concerns regarding work safety or health?
* **Would you like to report incidents or concerns regarding work safety or health?** (Checkbox):
	+ Option A: Yes, you want to report.
	+ Option B: No, not now.
### Page 9: Would you report the incident if you experienced or observed it?
* **Would you report the incident if you experienced or observed it?** (Select one):
	+ I would report it: If you intend to report the incident.
	+ Not sure: If you're unsure.
	+ Not report it: If you don't plan to report the incident.
### Page 10: How confident are you in the company's ability to handle reports of incidents or concerns?
* **How confident are you in the company's ability to handle reports of incidents or concerns?** (Select one):
	+ Very confident: If you feel highly confident.
	+ Somewhat confident: If you feel somewhat confident.
	+ Neutral: If you feel indifferent.
	+ Somewhat not confident: If you feel somewhat not confident.
	+ Not confident at all: If you feel no confidence.
### Page 11: How often do you experience stress or anxiety related to work?
* **How often do you experience stress or anxiety related to work?** (Select one):
	+ Often: If you frequently experience stress or anxiety.
	+ Sometimes: If you occasionally experience stress or anxiety.
	+ Rarely: If you seldom experience stress or anxiety.
	+ Never: If you never experience stress or anxiety.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you need help or have questions, contact form@gic.mx.
* This form is intended to improve the workplace environment and worker well-being.
