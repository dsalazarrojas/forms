# Cognitive Enhancement Product Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights from users who have used a cognitive enhancement product. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly based on your personal experience with the product.
2. For select_one fields, choose the option that best matches your experience.
3. For text fields, provide a detailed answer in your own words.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required/optional): This is the name you want to be associated with your response. It's optional but helps us understand who is providing the feedback.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required/true): This is the email address where you can be reached if we need to contact you for further information.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, required/true): Select the range that best describes your age.
* **Product Name or Type** (`product_name`, text, required/true): This is the name or type of the cognitive enhancement product you used.
* **How long have you used this product** (`usage_duration`, select_one, required/true): Choose how long you've used the product based on the provided options.
* **Frequency of Use** (`usage_frequency`, select_one, required/true): Select how often you use the product.
* **Improvement in Mental Clarity** (`mental_clarity`, select_one, required/true): Rate how much improvement you experienced in mental clarity, from 1 (no improvement) to 5 (significant improvement).
* **Improvement in Focus and Concentration** (`focus_concentration`, select_one, required/true): Rate how much improvement you experienced in focus and concentration, from 1 (no improvement) to 5 (significant improvement).
* **Improvement in Memory Function** (`memory_improvement`, select_one, required/true): Rate how much improvement you experienced in memory function, from 1 (no improvement) to 5 (significant improvement).
* **Improvement in Cognitive Speed** (`cognitive_speed`, select_one, required/true): Rate how much improvement you experienced in cognitive speed, from 1 (no improvement) to 5 (significant improvement).
* **Overall Product Effectiveness** (`overall_effectiveness`, select_one, required/true): Choose how effective you found the product to be, from 1 (not effective) to 5 (very effective).
* **Ease of Use** (`ease_of_use`, select_one, required/true): Rate how easy or difficult it was to use the product, from 1 (difficult) to 5 (very easy).
* **Consistency of Results** (`consistency`, select_one, required/true): Select how consistent the results are.
* **Side Effects** (`side_effects`, text, optional/true): If you experienced any negative side effects, please describe them.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required/true): Rate how good of value you got from the product in relation to its cost, from 1 (poor value) to 5 (excellent value).
* **Would you recommend this product?** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required/true): Choose how likely you are to recommend the product.
* **Will you continue using this product?** (`continue_use`, select_one, required/true): Select how likely you are to continue using the product.
* **Product Strengths** (`product_strengths`, text, optional/true): If there are any aspects of the product that you particularly enjoy or find useful, please elaborate.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional/true): If there are any areas where the product could be improved, please suggest them.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional/true): If you have any other feedback or suggestions, please share them.

## Tips

* Be honest and detailed in your answers.
* If you're unsure about any question, answer according to your best understanding.
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting.
