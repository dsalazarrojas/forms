# Pre Webinar Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pre Webinar Survey" is a form used to gather essential information from attendees before the webinar. This information will be used to tailor the content and provide personalized updates and resources to ensure that the attendees get the most value out of the webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter your name, email, and other required fields.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. name
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.

### 2. email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your business email address.

### 3. phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number.

### 4. company
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your current or previous company name.

### 5. position
* **Position** (`position`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position within the company.

### 6. department
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to within the company.

### 7. location
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the city or region where you are located.

Note: All fields are optional but providing accurate information will help us tailor the content and provide the most relevant resources to you.
