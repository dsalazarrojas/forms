The purpose of this form is to collect information and feedback from members of an art collective about their collaboration theme voting preferences. The form is designed to help the collective decide on the theme of a project by allowing members to vote on and provide feedback about different theme options.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Member Information**: This section is used to gather basic information about the member, including their name and email address. Please fill in your name and email address accurately.
2. **Project Information**: In this section, you will need to provide the name of the project and the type of collaboration you are interested in (e.g. Group exhibition, Themed series, etc.). The project name is required, as it will help us understand the context of your theme preferences.
3. **Theme Selection Voting**: In this section, you will be presented with multiple theme options, each with a description. You will be asked to vote on whether or not you support each theme option. You can choose "True", "False", or "Abstain" for each option.
4. **Theme Preference**: If you have a preferred theme, please describe it in the text field provided.
5. **Execution Ideas**: If you have ideas on how to execute the chosen theme, please provide them in the text field.
6. **Role Interest**: If you are interested in taking on a specific role in the project, please describe it in this field.
7. **Commitment Level**: Please select the level of commitment you are willing to make to the project. This will help us understand your availability and participation level.
8. **Additional Comments**: Finally, you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the project.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Name**: Your name as a member of the art collective.
* **Member ID**: A unique ID assigned to you as a member.
* **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be reached.
* **Project Name**: The name of the project you are collaborating on.
* **Type of Collaboration**: The type of collaboration you are interested in (e.g. Group exhibition, Themed series, etc.).
* **Voting Date**: The date by which you will cast your vote.
* **Theme Option 1 - Description**: A description of the first theme option.
* **Do you vote for Theme Option 1**: Select "True" if you support the theme, "False" if you do not, or "Abstain" if you are unsure.
* **Theme Option 2 - Description**: A description of the second theme option.
* **Do you vote for Theme Option 2**: Select "True" if you support the theme, "False" if you do not, or "Abstain" if you are unsure.
* **Theme Option 3 - Description**: A description of the third theme option.
* **Do you vote for Theme Option 3**: Select "True" if you support the theme, "False" if you do not, or "Abstain" if you are unsure.
* **Theme Preference**: Describe the theme you are most excited about.
* **Execution Ideas**: Describe how you would execute the chosen theme.
* **Role or contribution you are interested in**: Describe the role you are interested in taking on in the project.
* **Commitment Level**: Select the level of commitment you are willing to make to the project.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the project.

### Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback and input are valuable to the collective and will help us make informed decisions about the project theme.
