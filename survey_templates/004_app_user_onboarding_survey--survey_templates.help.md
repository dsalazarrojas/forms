# App User Onboarding Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects feedback on your onboarding experience with our app. Please answer the questions honestly to help us improve the app for you and our other users. This survey is completely anonymous and only takes a few minutes to complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Fill out each section at your own pace.
* Make sure to select one option from each dropdown menu where applicable.
* If a field requires an answer, be as specific as possible in your response.
