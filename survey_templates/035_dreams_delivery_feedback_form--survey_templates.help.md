# Dreams Delivery Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dreams Delivery Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the service. This form helps us understand how well we are meeting our delivery commitments, such as timeliness and product condition. It also provides an opportunity for customers to rate our communication and delivery instructions. This information will be used to improve our services and better serve our customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for your feedback in the "Dreams Delivery Feedback Form" field.
2. Rate our timeliness from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very late" and 5 is "Very on time".
3. Select the product condition from the options: "New", "Gently used", "Used", or "Damaged".
4. Rate our communication from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
5. Indicate if our delivery instructions were clear or not.
6. Add any additional comments or suggestions you have.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dreams Delivery Feedback Form** (`survey_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your feedback. This will help us understand the context of your comments.
* **Timeliness** (`timeliness_rating`, number, optional): Rate our timeliness from 1 to 5. Please select the number that best reflects your experience.
* **Product Condition** (`product_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select the product condition that best describes your experience. Choose one or more of the options provided.
* **Communication** (`communication_rating`, number, optional): Rate our communication from 1 to 5. Please select the number that best reflects your experience.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, select_one, optional): Indicate if our delivery instructions were clear or not.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions or concerns.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing your feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to add them in the "Comments" field.
* If you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or concerns, please enter your email address and phone number.
