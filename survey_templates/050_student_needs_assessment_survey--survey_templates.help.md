# Student Needs Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Needs Assessment Survey is designed to gather feedback from students on their needs and experiences within their program of study. This form helps us understand what challenges they face and what resources they need to succeed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and detailed in your responses.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Note" field to add them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the most challenging part of your current program of study?** (`student_needs_assessment_survey`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can describe the most challenging part of your program of study.
* **How do you really feel about our tutors?** (`student_needs_assessment_survey`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can express your honest opinion about your experiences with our tutors.
* **What did you find helpful in your teaching experience?** (`student_needs_assessment_survey`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can describe any resources or tools that have been helpful in your learning experience.
* **Do you need help with anything?** (`student_needs_assessment_survey`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ This field offers a list of options (Yes/No) for you to indicate whether you need help with anything.
* **What kind of help do you need?** (`student_needs_assessment_survey`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ This field allows you to select multiple areas where you need help (e.g., academic support, personal issues, etc.).
* **Date of completion** (`student_needs_assessment_survey`, `date`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can select the date when you completed this form.
* **Time of completion** (`student_needs_assessment_survey`, `time`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can select the time when you completed this form.
* **Additional comments** (`student_needs_assessment_survey`, `note`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can add any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Email** (`student_needs_assessment_survey`, `email`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`student_needs_assessment_survey`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter your phone number.
* **What would you like to learn more about?** (`student_needs_assessment_survey`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can suggest topics or resources you would like to learn more about.
* **Do you want to receive regular updates on new resources and tools?** (`student_needs_assessment_survey`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ This field allows you to select whether you want to receive updates on new resources and tools.
