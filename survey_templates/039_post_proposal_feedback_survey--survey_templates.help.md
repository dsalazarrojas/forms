# Post Proposal Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on a proposal to help improve its quality and overall presentation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each field with the relevant information.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the `Review Comments` and `Suggestion` fields.
3. Select a rating from the `Rating` field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Post Proposal Feedback** (`proposal_title`, `note`, optional): This field is where you can provide a brief title or heading for the proposal.
* **Rating** (`rating_scale`, `select_one`, optional): Choose a rating based on your overall assessment of the proposal:
	+ Unsatisfactory: The proposal failed to meet my expectations.
	+ Satisfactory: The proposal met my expectations, but could use some improvement.
	+ Good: The proposal met my expectations and was satisfactory.
	+ Excellent: The proposal exceeded my expectations.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide detailed comments on the proposal, including strengths and weaknesses.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them here.
## Tips
* Please fill out the form only once for each proposal.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the `Review Comments` and `Suggestion` fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
