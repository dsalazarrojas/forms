# Lot Survey Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lot Survey Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from surveyors and developers on land or property surveys. It helps in monitoring and tracking progress or performance on construction projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out your personal information in the "User Input" section with any relevant comments.
2.  Enter the **Lot Number** as it appears on the surveyor's report or contract.
3.  Select the **Lot Status** from the provided options (Status 1, Status 2, Status 3).
4.  Choose the **Lot Type** you surveyed from the provided options (Type 1, Type 2, Type 3).
5.  Enter your **Surveyor Name** as it appears on the surveyor's report or contract.
6.  Enter your **Surveyor Email**, as used for contacting you.
7.  Enter the **Developer Name** as it appears on the surveyor's report or contract.
8.  Enter the **Developer Email**, as used for contacting them.
9.  Enter the **Date Visited**, as it appears on the surveyor's report or contract.
10. Enter the **Time Visited**, as it appears on the surveyor's report or contract.
11. Enter any additional **Comments** about the survey or visit.
12. Sign your **Signature** as verification of your submission.
13. Finally, enter the **Date Submitted**, as the date you're submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lot Number**: Enter the Lot Number as it appears on the surveyor's report or contract.
* **Lot Status**: Select the current status of the surveyed lot from the provided options.
* **Lot Type**: Choose the type of lot you surveyed from the provided options.
* **Surveyor Name**: Enter the name of the surveyor who conducted the survey.
* **Surveyor Email**: Enter the email of the surveyor for further contact.
* **Developer Name**: Enter the name of the developer or company involved in the project.
* **Developer Email**: Enter the email of the developer or company for further contact.
* **Date Visited**: Enter the date you visited the surveyed lot.
* **Time Visited**: Enter the time you visited the surveyed lot.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments or feedback about the survey or visit.
* **Signature**: Sign your submission to verify your identity.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date you're submitting this form.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the Lot Number, as it's used for tracking and reference.
* Ensure you have the correct contact information for the surveyor and developer.
* Use the correct date and time of visit to ensure accurate records.
* Leave comments if you have any issues or concerns about the survey.
* Sign your submission to verify your identity.
* Submit the form carefully and accurately to prevent duplication or errors.
