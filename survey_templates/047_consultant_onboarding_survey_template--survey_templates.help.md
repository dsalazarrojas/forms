# Consultant Onboarding Survey Template - Help Guide

## Purpose

This onboarding survey template is a crucial tool for consultants to provide their feedback on their onboarding experience, helping the organization improve the process and provide better support for future new hires. The survey is composed of several sections, each focused on a specific aspect of the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Provide detailed explanations for each question to help us understand your thoughts and feelings.
3. Be as specific as possible when answering questions, providing examples or anecdotes when relevant.
4. Take your time to complete the survey, as the more thoughtful and detailed your responses, the more valuable they will be to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Onboarding Identification

*   **Consultant Full Name** (`consultant_full_name_ident`, Text, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Onboarding Start Date** (`onboarding_start_date_ident`, Date, Required): Enter the date you began your onboarding process.
*   **Assigned Mentor or Sponsor (Name)** (`assigned_mentor_sponsor`, Text, Required): Provide the name of your assigned mentor or sponsor.
*   **Lead Onboarding Manager (Name)** (`onboarding_manager_ident`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your lead onboarding manager.

### Onboarding Experience Evaluation

*   **Rate your overall onboarding experience (1-10)** (`overall_onboarding_rating`, Number, Required): Rate your experience from 1 (Poor) to 10 (Exceptional).
*   **Clarity of Initial Orientation and Objectives** (`orientation_clarity_rating`, Number, Required): Rate the clarity of the initial orientation and objectives from 1 (Vague) to 5 (Very Clear).
*   **How well were the tools and access prepared for your arrival?** (`tool_access_preparedness`, Number, Required): Rate the preparedness of the tools and access from 1 (Not Ready) to 5 (Fully Ready).

### Team Integration and Culture

*   **I feel welcomed and supported by my new team** (`team_welcome_perception`, Number, Required): Rate how strongly you feel welcomed and supported by your new team from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree).
*   **Perception of Cultural Alignment with the Firm** (`culture_alignment_rating`, Number, Required): Rate your perception of cultural alignment with the firm from 1 (Poor Fit) to 5 (Excellent Fit).
*   **Effectiveness of Mentorship/Sponsorship Support** (`mentorship_effectiveness_rating`, Number, Required): Rate the effectiveness of mentorship/sponsorship support from 1 (Ineffective) to 5 (Highly Effective).

### Future Productivity and Readiness

*   **How prepared do you feel to start your primary assignments?** (`role_readiness_perception`, Number, Required): Rate how prepared you feel to start your primary assignments from 1 (Not Prepared) to 5 (Fully Prepared).
*   **What was the biggest challenge you faced during onboarding?** (`primary_onboarding_challenge`, Text, Optional): Describe the biggest challenge you faced during onboarding.
*   **What additional support or training do you feel you need?** (`additional_support_needed`, Text, Optional): Suggest any additional support or training needed to improve the onboarding process.
*   **One suggestion to improve our onboarding process** (`onboarding_improvement_suggestion`, Text, Optional): Share one idea to enhance the onboarding process.
*   **Date of Survey Completion** (`submission_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date this survey was completed.

### Additional Information

*   **Authorized Representative (Name)** (`authorized_representative_signature_label`, Text, Optional): If you wish, provide the name of an authorized representative to contact for further information or feedback.

## Tips

*   Be as specific and detailed as possible when answering questions.
*   Use examples or anecdotes to illustrate your points.
*   Provide honest and constructive feedback.
*   Take your time to complete the survey, as the more thoughtful your responses, the more valuable they will be to us.

Please remember, this feedback is crucial for us to better understand your onboarding experience and identify areas of improvement. Your input will help shape the onboarding process for future new consultants, making it more efficient and effective.
