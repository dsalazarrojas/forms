# homemade_soap_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on user preferences for homemade soap products, including purchase location, soap type, price range, purchase frequency, satisfaction level, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Select the options that apply to your experiences with homemade soap products.
2. Provide any additional feedback in the "Additional Feedback" field.
3. Enter your contact email address and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Purchase Location** (`purchase_location`, number, **required: false**):
	+ This field allows you to select the place where you usually purchase homemade soap products.
* **Soap Type** (`soap_type`, select_multiple, **required: false**):
	+ Select all the types of homemade soap products you are familiar with (e.g., Soap, Body Wash, Hand Soap, Body Oil, Lotion).
* **Price Range** (`price_range`, select_one, **required: false**):
	+ Choose the price range for the homemade soap products you typically buy (e.g., $0-$10, $11-$20, $21-$30, etc.).
* **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, select_multiple, **required: false**):
	+ Select how often you purchase homemade soap products (e.g., Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently, Always).
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, **required: false**):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction level with the homemade soap products you've used (e.g., Not Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Satisfied, Very Satisfied, Extremely Satisfied).
* **Will Recommend** (`will_recommend`, select_one, **required: false**):
	+ Indicate whether you would recommend the homemade soap products you've used (e.g., False, True).
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, note, **required: false**):
	+ Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the homemade soap products.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, email, **required: false**):
	+ Enter your email address where you can be contacted if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, **required: false**):
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be contacted if needed.
* **Contact Request** (`contact_request`, select_one, **required: false**):
	+ Indicate how you would like to be contacted (e.g., No Contact, Yes, Call Me, Yes, Email Me).
