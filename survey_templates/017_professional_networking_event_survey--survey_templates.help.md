# Professional Networking Event Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback about a professional networking event from attendees. It is meant to gather information about the event's effectiveness in facilitating meaningful connections and relationships among participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Review the form**: Take a moment to understand the questions and fields on the form.
2. **Provide event details**: In the first section, "Event Details", provide a brief description of the event, including its name, date, and any other relevant information.
3. **Provide attendee details**: In the second section, "Attendee Details", provide your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
4. **Submit your feedback**: In the third section, "Feedback", select your level of satisfaction with the event, which will help us understand your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details**: This field is optional and allows you to provide a brief description of the event. This could include the event's name, date, location, and any other relevant details.
  - **Type**: Text field where you can enter any text.
- **Attendee Details** (Name): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
  - **Type**: Text field to enter your name.
- **Attendee Details** (Email): Enter your email address.
  - **Type**: Text field to enter your email address.
- **Feedback**: Select one or multiple levels of satisfaction with the event:
  - **Excellent**: You were extremely satisfied with the event.
  - **Average**: You were moderately satisfied with the event.
  - **Poor**: You were not satisfied with the event.
  - **Type**: Multiple select field with three options.

## Tips
* Please be honest and concise when answering the questions.
* Your feedback will help us improve future events and enhance your experience.
