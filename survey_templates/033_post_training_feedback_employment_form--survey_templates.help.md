# Post Training Feedback Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Post Training Feedback Employment Form is a tool to gather information on the effectiveness of a training program in leading to employment. It is designed to collect data on participants' background, the course attended, and their employment outcomes after completion of the training.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have completed the training program before filling out this form.
3. Answer the questions to the best of your ability, and do not leave any required fields blank.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the training provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, `optional`): Please provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details that would be helpful for the training provider to know.

### Course Information
* **Course Information** (`course_info`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the course you attended, including the title, date, and any other relevant details.

### How did you feel about the course?
* **How did you feel about the course?** (`feedback`, `text`, `optional`): Share your thoughts and feelings about the course, including what you liked and disliked about it.

### Employment Outcome
* **Employment Outcome** (`employment_outcome`, `text`, `optional`): Describe your employment outcome, including whether you are currently employed, part-time employed, or unemployed.

### Training Provider
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, `text`, `optional`): If you received any training or support from a specific training provider, please mention their name here.

### Workforce Program
* **Workforce Program** (`workforce_program`, `text`, `optional`): If you are enrolled in a workforce program, please mention the name of the program here.

### Date Completed
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, `date`, `optional`): If you have completed the training program, please provide the date when you completed it.

### Time Completed
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, `text`, `optional`): If you have completed the training program, please provide the time when you completed it.

### Hours Completed
* **Hours Completed** (`hours_completed`, `number`, `optional`): If you have completed the training program, please provide the number of hours you spent on it.

### Training Hours
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, `number`, `optional`): If you have completed the training program, please provide the number of hours you spent on it.

### Employment Status
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, `optional`): Choose the best option that describes your current employment status:
	+ Unemployed
	+ Employed
	+ Part-Time
	+ Full-Time

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, `optional`): If you are employed, please provide your job title.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the training provider for clarification.
* This form is designed to gather anonymous and aggregate data, so your responses will not be identifiable.
