# industrial_design_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture product information for an industrial design checklist.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the product name and description in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the product category from the options provided.
3. For optional fields, provide the requested information if applicable.
4. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the product.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, number, required/optional): Enter a unique identifier for the product.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the product category from the options provided.
* **Product Subcategory** (`product_subcategory`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the product subcategory from the options provided.
* **Production Date** (`production_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the production date of the product.
* **Production Time** (`production_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the production time of the product.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the product.
* **Manufacturer Email** (`manufacturer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the contact email of the manufacturer.
* **Manufacturer Phone** (`manufacturer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number of the manufacturer.
* **Product Image URL** (`product_image_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL of a product image.
* **Product File URL** (`product_file_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL of a product file.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price of the product.
* **Production Costs** (`production_costs`, number, required/optional): Enter the production costs of the product.
* **Manufacturing Process** (`manufacturing_process`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the manufacturing process from the options provided.
* **Product Weight** (`product_weight`, number, required/optional): Enter the weight of the product.
* **Product Length** (`product_length`, number, required/optional): Enter the length of the product.
* **Product Width** (`product_width`, number, required/optional): Enter the width of the product.
* **Product Height** (`product_height`, number, required/optional): Enter the height of the product.
* **Product Depth** (`product_depth`, number, required/optional): Enter the depth of the product.
* **Product Material** (`product_material`, select_one, required/optional): Select the product material from the options provided.
* **Product Color** (`product_color`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the product color from the options provided.
* **Production Date Range** (`production_date_range`, select_one, required/optional): Select the production date range from the options provided.
* **Production Period** (`production_period`, select_one, required/optional): Select the production period from the options provided.
* **Production Frequency** (`production_frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the production frequency from the options provided.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Check the fields marked "required" are filled before proceeding.
* If any fields are marked as "required" but left empty, the form may not submit successfully.
* You may need to adjust this template according to specific product requirements.
