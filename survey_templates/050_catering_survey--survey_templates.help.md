# Catering Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Catering Survey form. Please follow along to ensure you provide accurate and thorough feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as prompted. You can type in your answers in the text boxes, and select from the options for questions that require a specific answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Please select the date of your event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event you held with us.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Number of Guests** (`guest_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests you had at the event.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our services.
* **Food Quality Rating** (`food_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the food we provided.
* **Food Taste** (`food_taste`, select_one, required): Rate the taste of the food we provided.
* **Food Presentation** (`food_presentation`, select_one, optional): Rate the presentation of the food we provided.
* **Portion Size** (`portion_size`, select_one, required): Rate the portion size of the food we provided.
* **Menu Variety** (`menu_variety`, select_one, optional): Rate the variety of the menu options we provided.
* **Service Rating** (`service_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of service we provided.
* **Staff Professionalism** (`staff_professionalism`, select_one, required): Rate the professionalism of our staff.
* **Staff Responsiveness** (`staff_responsiveness`, select_one, optional): Rate the responsiveness of our staff.
* **Timeliness** (`timeliness`, select_one, required): Rate whether our service was on time.
* **Setup Quality** (`setup_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the setup we provided.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, select_one, required): Rate the cleanliness of our staff's attire and equipment.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate whether our services were worth the cost.
* **Dietary Needs Met** (`dietary_needs_met`, select_one, optional): Rate whether we met your dietary needs.
* **Communication Rating** (`communication_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of communication with our staff.
* **Would You Recommend Us** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend us to others.
* **Would You Use Again** (`would_use_again`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would use our services again.
* **What Did You Like Best** (`best_aspect`, text, optional): Tell us about a highlight of your experience with us.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or areas where we can improve.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other feedback or comments you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Take as much time as you need to complete the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback. It's invaluable to us!
