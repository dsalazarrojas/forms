# Installation Specialist Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience with an installation specialist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Customer Name**: This is the name of the person who received the service.
2. Enter your **Email**: This is the email address where you would like to be contacted for any follow-up questions or discussions.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**: This is the phone number where you can be reached for any further questions or clarifications.
4. Choose a **Service Date**: This is the date when the service was provided.
5. Choose a **Service Time**: This is the time when the service was provided.
6. Select a **Rating**: This is a way to rate your experience with the installation specialist, with options ranging from Excellent to Poor.
7. Enter your **Comments**: This is an opportunity to provide more details about your experience, any issues you faced, or anything the installation specialist could have improved on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (id: 1, name: customer_name, text, required)**: Please enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email (id: 2, name: email, email, required)**: Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number (id: 3, name: phone_number, text, required)**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Service Date (id: 4, name: service_date, date, required)**: Choose the date when the service was provided.
* **Service Time (id: 5, name: service_time, time, required)**: Choose the time when the service was provided.
* **Rating (id: 6, name: rating, select_one, required)**: Rate your experience with the installation specialist.
* **Comments (id: 7, name: comments, note, required)**: Share any additional details or comments about your experience.
