# easter_week_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from users about their experiences during Easter week. This form is meant to be a flexible and user-friendly way to gather responses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form title.
2. Provide a category for the form.
3. Add a description about your experience during Easter week.
4. Identify the assigned tool or resource used in relation to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Easter Week Survey** (`form_title`, `text`, optional): This is the title of the form, which helps in identifying it for easy recognition.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, optional): This field is used to categorize the form under a specific group.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): This is a space for you to provide more information about your experience during Easter week.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): This field is related to the tool or resource used in relation to the form.
