# Cafe Coffee Blend Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our cafe's coffee blends. We value your opinion and want to understand how we can improve our coffee quality to meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the branch location where you visited from the options provided.
2. Choose the type of coffee drink you ordered.
3. Answer the following questions about your experience with the blend:
	* Aroma intensity and quality (scale 1-5)
	* Acidity level (choose one: Bright/High, Balanced, or Low/Mellow)
	* Body and mouthfeel (choose one: Heavy/Creamy, Medium/Silky, or Light/Tea-like)
	* Bitterness balance (choose one: Too Bitter, Well Balanced, or Very Smooth)
	* Overall flavor satisfaction (rate on scale 1-5)
4. Was the coffee served at the correct temperature?
5. Choose how well the presentation and visual appeal of the coffee was (choose one: Beautifully Presented, Standard, or Needs Improvement)
6. Rate how good value for money you think the blend is (choose one: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor)
7. Indicate if you would order this specific blend again (choose one: Definitely, Probably, or Not for me)
8. If you have any specific flavor notes or suggestions, feel free to add them in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Visit** (`date`, required): Enter the date you visited our cafe.
* **Cafe Branch Location** (`select_one`, required): Select the branch location where you visited from the options provided.
* **Coffee Blend Sampled** (`text`, optional): If you know the name of the blend, please enter it here.
* **Type of Coffee Drink** (`select_one`, required): Choose the type of coffee drink you ordered from the options provided.
* **Aroma Intensity and Quality** (`number`, required): Rate the aroma intensity and quality of the coffee on scale 1-5.
* **Perceived Acidity Level** (`select_one`, required): Choose one: Bright/High, Balanced, or Low/Mellow.
* **Body and Mouthfeel** (`select_one`, required): Choose one: Heavy/Creamy, Medium/Silky, or Light/Tea-like.
* **Bitterness Balance** (`select_one`, required): Choose one: Too Bitter, Well Balanced, or Very Smooth.
* **Overall Flavor Satisfaction** (`number`, required): Rate your overall flavor satisfaction on scale 1-5.
* **Serving Temperature** (`select_one`, required): Choose one: Perfect, Too Hot, or Too Cold.
* **Presentation and Visual Appeal** (`select_one`, required): Choose one: Beautifully Presented, Standard, or Needs Improvement.
* **Value for Money** (`select_one`, required): Choose one: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Would you Order this Blend Again** (`select_one`, required): Choose one: Definitely, Probably, or Not for me.
* **Specific Flavor Notes or Suggestions** (`text`, optional): Add any specific flavor notes or suggestions about the coffee here.
* **Are you a member of our Rewards Program** (`select_one`, optional): Check if you are a member of our rewards program.
