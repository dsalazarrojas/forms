# Community Volunteer Program Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from community volunteers on their experiences with our organization's volunteer program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address if you'd like to receive a follow-up email.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, using the provided options for guidance.
3. For the "new skills" question, list specific skills you've developed through your volunteer work.
4. Use the provided space for "additional comments" to elaborate on any points you'd like to make.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`volunteer_name`, `text`, Required): Your name as a volunteer.
* **Email Address** (`volunteer_email`, `email`, Required): Your email address for follow-up communications.
* **Which volunteer program did you participate in?** (`program_name`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the program you participated in from the dropdown list.
* **How long have you been volunteering?** (`participation_duration`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the duration of your service from the list.
* **Approximately how many hours do you volunteer per month?** (`hours_per_month`, `select_one`, Required): Estimate your monthly volunteer hours.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with the volunteer program?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
* **How would you rate the volunteer orientation process?** (`orientation_rating`, `select_one`, Required): Evaluate the initial training and onboarding process.
* **How would you rate the training provided?** (`training_rating`, `select_one`, Required): Rate the quality of the training you received.
* **How supported do you feel by your supervisor?** (`supervisor_support`, `select_one`, Required): Rate your sense of support from your supervisor.
* **Do you have a clear understanding of your role?** (`role_clarity`, `select_one`, Required): Evaluate your understanding of your volunteer role.
* **Do you have the resources needed to be effective?** (`resources_adequate`, `select_one`, Required): Rate the availability of necessary tools and materials.
* **Do you feel your volunteer work makes a difference?** (`impact_feeling`, `select_one`, Required): Rate your sense of impact from your volunteer work.
* **Are your skills being well utilized?** (`skills_utilized`, `select_one`, Required): Evaluate whether your skills are being used effectively.
* **Have you developed new skills through volunteering?** (`skills_developed`, `select_one`, Required): Rate your skill development through volunteering.
* **If yes, what new skills have you developed?** (`new_skills_list`, `text`, Optional): List specific skills you've developed through volunteering.
* **How satisfied are you with schedule flexibility?** (`schedule_flexibility`, `select_one`, Required): Rate your satisfaction with work-life balance.
* **How would you rate communication from the organization?** (`communication_rating`, `select_one`, Required): Evaluate the quality of updates and information you receive.
* **Do you feel appreciated and recognized for your contributions?** (`recognition_feeling`, `select_one`, Required): Rate your sense of appreciation and recognition.
* **Would you recommend this program to others?** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, Required): Rate your likelihood of recommending the program.
* **Do you plan to continue volunteering with us?** (`continue_volunteering`, `select_one`, Required): Rate your future commitment to the program.
* **What is the best part of volunteering here?** (`best_part`, `text`, Optional): Share your positive experiences.
* **What challenges have you faced?** (`challenges_faced`, `text`, Optional): Share any difficulties you've encountered.
* **What could we improve?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, Optional): Suggest areas for improvement.
* **What additional support would help you?** (`additional_support_needed`, `text`, Optional): Share any additional resources or assistance you need.
* **Any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, `text`, Optional): Share any further thoughts or suggestions.
* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, Required): This field is automatically filled in with today's date.

## Tips
- Answer honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.
- Use specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
