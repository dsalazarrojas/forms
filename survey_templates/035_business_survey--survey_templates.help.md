# Business Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Survey form is designed to gather information about your company's profile, industry, size, and practices. This form will help us understand your business context and provide valuable insights to help improve our services and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible. Make sure to provide detailed and specific information about your company, its industry, size, and practices. For any field with a dropdown menu, select the option that best describes your company's characteristics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the official name of your company.
* **Contact Person Name** (`company_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your company.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the business email address of your company.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the business phone number of your company.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your company's location.
* **Industry Type** (`industry_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of industry your company operates in.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select the number of employees in your company.
* **Years in Business** (`years_established`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of years your company has been in operation.
* **Primary Products or Services** (`primary_products_services`, text, required): Describe the main offerings of your company.
* **Approximate Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, optional): Select the approximate revenue range of your company.
* **Growth Trajectory** (`growth_trajectory`, select_one, required): Select the growth trajectory of your company over the past year.
* **Geographic Market Coverage** (`market_coverage`, text, required): Describe the areas served by your business.
* **Competitive Advantages** (`competitive_advantages`, text, optional): List what sets your company apart from others.
* **Main Business Challenges** (`main_challenges`, text, required): Identify the key challenges faced by your company.
* **Future Plans and Goals** (`future_plans`, text, optional): Describe your next steps and goals for your business.
* **Technology Adoption Level** (`technology_adoption`, select_one, optional): Select the level of technology adoption in your company.
* **Sustainability Practices** (`sustainability_practices`, select_one, optional): Select the level of sustainability practices in your company.
* **Recommendations for Improvement** (`business_recommendations`, text, optional): Provide suggestions to help improve your business.
* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_completion_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this survey completion.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled in order to complete the form.
