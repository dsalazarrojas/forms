# telecom_service_plan_satisfaction_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The goal of this form is to collect end-user feedback and experiences related to their telecom service plan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question based on your genuine experience with the telecom service plan.
2. Select the rating for "Plan Rating" that best reflects your overall experience with the plan.
3. For "Service Quality," select all the qualities that you experienced during your service usage.
4. If you have experienced any issues, select "Yes" and describe them in the "Issue Description" field.
5. If issues were resolved, select "Resolved" for "Issue Resolution." If issues were not resolved, select "Unresolved."
6. In "Recommendations," provide any suggestions you have for improving the service.
7. Fill out "Contact Information" with accurate and up-to-date contact details.
8. Ensure that "Email Address" and "Phone Number" are accurate.
9. Answer "Service Improvement" and "Follow Up" questions truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Comments** (`general_comments`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience with the telecom service plan.
* **Plan Rating** (`plan_rating`, select_one, required): Select a rating for your overall experience with the telecom service plan, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the qualities that best describe your experience with the service, such as "Very Poor," "Poor," "Fair," "Good," or "Excellent."
* **Communication Experience** (`communication_experience`, select_one, required): Select how you experienced the communication with the telecom service provider, with "Very Poor," "Poor," "Fair," "Good," or "Excellent" being the options.
* **Issues Experienced** (`issues_experienced`, select_multiple, optional): If you have experienced any issues with the service, select "Yes" and describe the issues in the "Issue Description" field.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" for "Issues Experienced," describe the issues you encountered with the service.
* **Issue Resolution** (`issue_resolution`, select_one, required): Select how the issues were resolved, with "Unresolved," "Resolved," or "Partially Resolved" being the options.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving the service, type them in this field.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide accurate and up-to-date contact information for follow-up or further discussion.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you back.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you back.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any further comments or feedback, please type them here.
* **Service Improvement** (`service_improvement`, select_one, required): Select whether you believe the service could be improved.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required): Select whether you would like to be contacted back for follow-up.
