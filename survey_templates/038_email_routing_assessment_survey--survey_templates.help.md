# Email Routing Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Email Routing Assessment Survey" form is designed to gather feedback and insights from users about the current email routing process. This form aims to understand how often users use email routing, their satisfaction with the current process, and areas where improvements can be made.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you have any suggestions for improving the email routing process, feel free to add them in the "What are some areas you would like to see improved in our email routing process?" field.
3. If you have a primary contact for questions or issues, please select the correct answer in the "Do you have a primary contact for questions or issues regarding the email routing process?" field.
4. Make sure to indicate how we can contact you by phone or email in the "Can we reach you by phone or email for questions or issues?" field. This will help us reach out to you directly and discuss any questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email Routing Form** (`email_routing_form`): This is the main form where you can provide feedback about your email routing experience. Please provide as much detail as possible in this field.
* **How often do you use email routing?** (`email_routing_process`): Select the option that best describes how often you use email routing, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.
* **How satisfied are you with our current email routing process?** (`email_routing_process2`): Please rate your satisfaction level with the current email routing process, using the number scale provided.
* **What are some areas you would like to see improved in our email routing process?** (`email_routing_process3`): Provide a detailed description of areas where you think we can improve the email routing process.
* **What is your primary role?** (`email_routing_process4`): Select your primary role, such as IT, Operations, or Other.
* **Are there any obstacles that are hindering your ability to use the current email routing process?** (`email_routing_process6`): Select all obstacles that apply to you, such as Difficulty in accessing information, Lack of clear instructions, or Other.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving the email routing process?** (`email_routing_process7`): This is an open field where you can provide any additional suggestions or feedback you have about improving the email routing process.
* **Do you have a primary contact for questions or issues regarding the email routing process?** (`email_routing_process8`): Select the option that best describes your primary contact, such as True or False.
* **Can we reach you by phone or email for questions or issues?** (`email_routing_process9`): Indicate how we can contact you, such as Phone, Email, or Neither.
* **Can you give us feedback about the current email routing process?** (`email_routing_process10`): This is an open field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the email routing process.
