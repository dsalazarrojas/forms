> # New Hire Orientation Survey Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from new hires about their onboarding experience to help the company improve the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Review all the pages and questions carefully.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Use the provided options for select questions, or type your answer for text and note questions.
4. Submit the completed form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Onboarding Process**:
	+ This question asks about the overall process of being welcomed into the company.
	+ Please answer with one of the provided options, if applicable.
* **Onboarding Time**:
	+ This question inquires about the amount of time it took for you to feel ready to work independently.
	+ Please enter a numerical value for this question.
* **Overall Impression**:
	+ This question seeks to understand your overall impression of the onboarding process.
	+ Please respond with a note to express your thoughts and feelings about the process.
* **Onboarding Experience**:
	+ This question asks about your overall experience with the onboarding process.
	+ Please select one of the provided options that best describes your experience.
* **Onboarding Feedback**:
	+ This question allows you to provide feedback on the onboarding process.
	+ Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Onboarding Department**:
	+ This question asks about which department you were welcomed into during the onboarding process.
	+ Please select the department that is most relevant to your experience.
* **Onboarding Device**:
	+ This question inquires about the devices that were provided or used during the onboarding process.
	+ Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Onboarding Comments**:
	+ This question gives you space to provide any additional comments or feedback about the onboarding process.
	+ Please be as detailed and honest as possible.

## Tips

* Answer each question carefully and honestly.
* If a question does not apply to your experience, please respond with "Not Applicable" or "Not Relevant".
* If you have any suggestions or comments about the onboarding process, please include them in the "Onboarding Comments" field.
