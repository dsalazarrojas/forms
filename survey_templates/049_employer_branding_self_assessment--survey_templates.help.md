# employer-branding-self-assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help employers assess their company's branding and self-assessment strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and answer all required fields.
- Select the options that best fit your company's situation.
- You can choose one or multiple options for each section, depending on your preferences.
- Make sure to save your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Engagement** (Page 1):
	+ **Employee Engagement** (`employee_engagement`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks how employees feel about their engagement with the company. Choose 'Yes' if employees feel engaged, or 'No' if they don't.
* **Job Satisfaction** (Page 1):
	+ **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): This field asks about how satisfied employees are with their job. Choose 'Satisfied' if employees are satisfied, or 'Dissatisfied' if they are not.
* **Diversity and Inclusion** (Page 1):
	+ **Diversity and Inclusion** (`diversity_and_inclusion`, text, required: false): This field asks about your company's diversity and inclusion practices. Please provide a brief description of your company's policies and procedures.
* **Benefits and Compensation** (Page 1):
	+ **Benefits and Compensation** (`benefits_and_compensation`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks about your company's benefits and compensation policies. Choose 'Yes' if your company offers these benefits, or 'No' if not.
* **Career Development** (Page 2):
	+ **Career Development** (`career_development`, text, required: false): This field asks about your company's career development programs and policies. Please provide a brief description of your company's programs.
* **Diversity and Inclusion Practices** (Page 2):
	+ **Diversity and Inclusion Practices** (`diversity_and_inclusion_practices`, text, required: false): This field asks about your company's diversity and inclusion practices. Please provide a brief description of your company's policies and procedures.
* **Work-Life Balance** (Page 2):
	+ **Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, select_one, required: false): This field asks about your company's work-life balance policies. Choose 'Yes' if your company offers these benefits, or 'No' if not.
* **Job Satisfaction Practices** (Page 2):
	+ **Job Satisfaction Practices** (`job_satisfaction_practices`, text, required: false): This field asks about your company's job satisfaction practices. Please provide a brief description of your company's policies.
* **Leadership Effectiveness** (Page 3):
	+ **Leadership Effectiveness** (`leadership_effectiveness`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks about your company's leadership's effectiveness. Choose 'Yes' if your company's leaders are effective, or 'No' if not.
* **Diversity and Inclusion Leadership** (Page 3):
	+ **Diversity and Inclusion Leadership** (`diversity_and_inclusion_leadership`, select_one, required: false): This field asks about your company's leadership's effectiveness in promoting diversity and inclusion. Choose 'Yes' if your company's leaders are effective, or 'No' if not.

## Tips
- Answer questions honestly and accurately.
- Make sure to review and edit your answers before submitting the form.
- This form is intended for internal use only and will not be shared with anyone outside the organization.
- Please save your answers before submitting the form, in case you need to review them later.
