# potato_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information and assess the user's knowledge on a specific subject matter related to potatoes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your phone number if you wish to be contacted for further information.
3. Answer the quiz questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is for collecting any additional information the user might want to provide in relation to their name and email address.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you have a phone number you would like to provide, enter it here.
* **Quiz Questions** (`quiz_questions`, `text`, required: true): Answer the quiz questions to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as you can.

### Tips
* Please answer the quiz questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions, feel free to ask the administrator for further guidance.
* This form is for educational purposes, and your participation is greatly appreciated!
