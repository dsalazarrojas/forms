# Training Needs Assessment Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Training Needs Assessment Evaluation Form" is designed to gather information about an employee's training needs. It helps to identify areas where an individual requires support or development to improve their performance and productivity. This form is meant to be completed by the employee themselves, providing an honest assessment of their training needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by carefully reading the form and understanding the purpose and instructions.
2. Answer each question honestly, selecting the most accurate response for each field.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. Enter numbers in the relevant fields for questions marked as numbers.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they accurately reflect your training needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### training_needs_assessment (Required: False)
A text field to describe your training needs.

* **training_needs_assessment**: A brief description of your training needs. (Required: False)

### employee_training_needs (Required: False)
A dropdown list with two options: "Yes" or "No".

* **employee_training_needs**: Check if you have any training needs. (Required: False)

### communication (Required: False)
A multiple-select option list with two options: "Yes" or "No".

* **communication**: Check all communication skills you need to improve, such as public speaking or presentation skills. (Required: False)

### training_needs_level_1 to training_needs_level_7 (Required: False)
A number field to rate your training needs on a scale from 1 to 7.

* **training_needs_level_1 to training_needs_level_7**: Rate the level of your training needs for each skill area using a scale from 1 to 7, with 1 being "no need" and 7 being "very high need". (Required: False)

### Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering, as this will help in creating a development plan tailored to your needs.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they accurately reflect your training needs.
- If you're unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for guidance or clarification.
