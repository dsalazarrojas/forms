# Skin Tone Matching Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their skin tone matching experience with your product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the fields as prompted, providing as much or as little information as you want.
2.  Be honest and thorough in your answers.
3.  Don't hesitate to provide additional comments or feedback if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, `number`, not required): This field is not required and is likely for identification purposes.
*   **Matching Method** (`matching_method`, `select_one`, not required): This field asks the customer to select the method used for skin tone matching.
*   **Skin Tone Matching Accuracy** (`skin_tone_matching_accuracy`, `select_multiple`, not required): This field asks the customer to rate the accuracy of their skin tone matching.
*   **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, not required): This field asks the customer to rate their satisfaction with the skin tone matching experience.
*   **Accuracy Accuracy** (`accuracy_accuracy`, `select_multiple`, not required): This field is similar to the previous one, asking the customer to rate their confidence in the accuracy of their skin tone matching.
*   **Shade Recommendation** (`shade_recommendation`, `select_multiple`, not required): This field asks the customer to select the shade they think they would most likely match with.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, not required): This field is for customers to provide any additional feedback or comments.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): This field is not required and is likely for customers to provide their contact information if they're interested in being contacted for follow-up questions or more information about the product or service.
