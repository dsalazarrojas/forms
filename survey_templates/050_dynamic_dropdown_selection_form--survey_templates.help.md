# Dynamic Dropdown Selection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dynamic Dropdown Selection Form is designed to gather user preferences for enhancing user experience and data collection. This form is intended to be completed by users who wish to provide their input on specific topics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Survey Templates" page to begin the form.
2. Review the available options for the "Survey Templates" page and select the options that apply to you.
3. Proceed to the next page, "Dynamic Dropdown Selection Form", to provide your input in the text field.
4. Review the available options for the "Enhance User Experience", "Data Collection Boosted", "Streamlining Data Collection", "Accuracy Boosted", and "Previous Answers" pages, and select the options that apply to you.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Templates** (`survey_templates`, dropdown, required/optional): Select the options that apply to you. This field allows you to choose from a list of survey templates. 
* **Dynamic Dropdown Selection Form** (`dynamic_dropdown_selection_form`, text, required/optional): Provide your answer to this field. This field allows you to give a brief description of your thoughts or answers related to the topic "Dynamic Dropdown Selection Form".
* **Enhance User Experience** (`enhance_user_experience`, text, required/optional): Provide your answer to this field. This field allows you to share your thoughts or opinions on how to enhance the user experience.
* **Data Collection Boosted** (`data_collection_boosted`, text, required/optional): Enter your answer for this field. This field is designed to gather your thoughts on data collection.
* **Streamlining Data Collection** (`streamlining_data_collection`, text, required/optional): Give your response to this field. This field is intended to help you provide suggestions on streamlining data collection.
* **Accuracy Boosted** (`accuracy_boosted`, text, required/optional): Share your answer with this field. This field is meant to gather your thoughts on accuracy.
* **Previous Answers** (`previous_answers`, text, required/optional): Enter your previous answers to this field. This field is designed to gather your previous responses or answers.
