# toy survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a simple survey template used to gather feedback or opinions from users. It is not required to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, "main page", you will be prompted to begin the survey.
2. On the second page, "contact-information", provide your contact information such as name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
3. On the third page, "favorite-colors", select your favorite colors from the available options (red, blue, green).
4. On the fourth page, "age", enter your age.
5. On the fifth page, "comments", provide any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main Page
* **Main Page** (`main-page`, text, not required): This is the first page of the survey where you will be prompted to begin.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact-information`, text, not required): Provide your contact information such as name, email, and phone number (if applicable).

### Favorite Colors
* **Favorite Colors** (`favorite-colors`, select_multiple, not required): Select your favorite colors from the available options (red, blue, green).

### Age
* **Age** (`age`, number, not required): Enter your age.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have.
