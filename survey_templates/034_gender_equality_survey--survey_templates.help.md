# Gender Equality Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gender Equality Survey aims to gather information about your experiences and feelings regarding gender equality in the workplace. This survey will help your management team or HR department understand your perspectives and improve the work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the corresponding options for each question from the provided list.
3. Enter your comments and contact information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`Intro`, note, required: false): This is a welcome note to introduce the survey and its purpose.
* **What is your gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): This question is asking about your biological gender.
	+ Please select your gender from the provided list (Male, Female, Other).
* **Have you experienced sex-based discrimination at the workplace** (`sex_at_workplace`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is asking if you have experienced any discriminatory behavior or treatment based on your sex or gender in the workplace.
	+ Please select all the experiences that apply from the provided list (True, False).
* **What is your age** (`age`, number, required: false): This question is asking about your age.
	+ Enter your age in the provided text field.
* **How many years have you been working at this organization** (`years_of_service`, number, required: false): This question is asking about your length of service at the current organization.
	+ Enter the number of years you have been working at this organization.
* **In which department do you work** (`department`, select_one, required: false): This question is asking about your current department.
	+ Please select the department you belong to from the provided list (Human Resources, Marketing, Finance, Operations, Other).
* **What is your shift time** (`shift_time`, select_one, required: false): This question is asking about your work shift schedule.
	+ Please select your typical work shift from the provided list (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, Variable).
* **What is your job title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): This question is asking about your job role or position.
	+ Enter your job title.
* **How would you rate your job performance** (`job_performance`, select_one, required: false): This question is asking about your evaluation of your job performance.
	+ Please select your job performance from the provided list (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **What benefits do you receive** (`benefits`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is asking about the benefits you receive from your employer.
	+ Please select all the benefits that apply from the provided list (Health Insurance, Life Insurance, Disability Insurance, Pension, Other).
* **What is your salary range** (`salary`, number, required: false): This question is asking about your current salary range.
	+ Enter your salary range.
* **What is your highest educational qualification** (`education`, select_one, required: false): This question is asking about your highest level of education completed.
	+ Please select the highest educational qualification from the provided list (Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, Ph.D., Other).
* **What is your native language** (`language`, select_one, required: false): This question is asking about your native language.
	+ Please select your native language from the provided list (English, Spanish, Chinese, Arabic, Other).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): This question is asking for any additional comments you may have.
	+ Enter your comments.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This question is asking for your contact email address.
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This question is asking for your contact phone number.
	+ Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any difficulties or concerns, please contact the HR department or management team.
* The information gathered from this survey will be used to improve the work environment and promote gender equality.
* Your responses will be kept confidential and anonymous.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
