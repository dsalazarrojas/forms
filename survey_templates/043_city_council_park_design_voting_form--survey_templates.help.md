# City Council Park Design Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather community feedback on the design of a new park in the city. Your input will help inform the city council's decision on how to create a park that meets the community's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Confirm that you are a member of the community by typing your address in the "Your Address" field.
4. Choose how often you would use the park in the "How Often Would You Use the Park" field.
5. Select your primary use for the park in the "Primary Use of the Park" field.
6. Rank the importance of various park amenities and features using the number scale provided.
7. Select any eco-friendly features you would like to see in the park.
8. Finally, provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have in the "Additional Suggestions or Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Your Address** (`address`, text, required): Confirm that you are a member of the community by typing your address.
* **Distance from Park** (`park_proximity`, select_one, required): Choose the distance you are from the proposed park location.
	+ Within 5 blocks: You are within 5 blocks from the proposed location.
	+ 5-10 blocks: You are between 5-10 blocks from the proposed location.
	+ 10-20 blocks: You are between 10-20 blocks from the proposed location.
	+ More than 20 blocks: You are more than 20 blocks from the proposed location.
	+ Outside neighborhood: You are not in the neighborhood.
* **How Often Would You Use the Park** (`expected_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would use the park.
	+ Daily: You would use the park every day.
	+ Several times a week: You would use the park several times a week.
	+ Weekly: You would use the park once a week.
	+ Monthly: You would use the park once a month.
	+ Rarely: You would use the park very rarely.
	+ Never: You would not use the park.
* **Primary Use of the Park** (`primary_use`, select_one, required): Choose your primary use for the park.
	+ Recreation and play: You would use the park for recreational activities.
	+ Exercise and sports: You would use the park for exercise and sports.
	+ Relaxation and peace: You would use the park for relaxation and peace.
	+ Social gatherings: You would use the park for social gatherings.
	+ Walking with pets: You would use the park for walking with pets.
	+ Other: Please choose "Other" and describe your primary use.
* **Playground Equipment and Areas** (`playground_importance`, number, required): Rank the importance of playground equipment and areas from 1-5.
* **Sports Courts (basketball, tennis)** (`sports_courts_importance`, number, required): Rank the importance of sports courts from 1-5.
* **Picnic Areas and Seating** (`picnic_areas_importance`, number, required): Rank the importance of picnic areas and seating from 1-5.
* **Walking and Biking Paths** (`walking_paths_importance`, number, required): Rank the importance of walking and biking paths from 1-5.
* **Water Features (fountain, splash pad)** (`water_features_importance`, number, required): Rank the importance of water features from 1-5.
* **Trees and Shaded Areas** (`trees_shade_importance`, number, required): Rank the importance of trees and shaded areas from 1-5.
* **Dog Park or Pet Area** (`dog_park_importance`, number, required): Rank the importance of a dog park or pet area from 1-5.
* **Community Center or Meeting Space** (`community_center_importance`, number, required): Rank the importance of a community center or meeting space from 1-5.
* **Outdoor Fitness Equipment** (`fitness_equipment_importance`, number, required): Rank the importance of outdoor fitness equipment from 1-5.
* **Adequate Lighting** (`lighting_priority`, number, required): Rank the importance of adequate lighting from 1-5.
* **Security and Supervision** (`security_priority`, number, required): Rank the importance of security and supervision from 1-5.
* **Regular Maintenance and Cleanliness** (`maintenance_commitment`, number, required): Rank the importance of regular maintenance and cleanliness from 1-5.
* **Park Size Preference** (`park_size_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred size for the park.
	+ Small neighborhood park: You would prefer a small park.
	+ Medium park: You would prefer a medium-sized park.
	+ Large regional park: You would prefer a large park.
	+ No preference: You have no preference for park size.
* **Design Aesthetic Preference** (`design_aesthetic`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred design aesthetic for the park.
	+ Modern and contemporary: You would prefer a modern and contemporary design.
	+ Natural and native plants: You would prefer a natural and native plant design.
	+ Multi-cultural celebration: You would prefer a multi-cultural celebration design.
	+ Sports and recreation focus: You would prefer a sports and recreation-focused design.
	+ Mixed styles: You would prefer a mixed design style.
* **Eco-Friendly Features Desired** (`eco_friendly_features`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the eco-friendly features you would like to see in the park.
	+ Rain gardens: You would like to see rain gardens in the park.
	+ Native plantings: You would like to see native plantings in the park.
	+ Composting stations: You would like to see composting stations in the park.
	+ Recycling areas: You would like to see recycling areas in the park.
	+ Solar lighting: You would like to see solar lighting in the park.
	+ Water conservation: You would like to see water conservation measures in the park.
* **One Feature You Would Not Want the Park Without** (`must_have_feature`, text, optional): Please list one feature that you would not want the park without.
* **Additional Suggestions or Comments** (`additional_suggestions`, text, optional): Please provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
