# Property Damage Recovery Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Damage Recovery Feedback Survey is a form used to collect feedback from customers or users who have experienced property damage. It's designed to gather information about the property name, location, type of damage, date, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, please select the appropriate "Property Name" from the list below.
  	* Select the correct property name: 
  	* `abc123`
  	* `def456`
  	* `ghi789`
  	* ...

2. Next, select all relevant locations where the damage occurred (multiple locations can be selected).
  	* Select all applicable locations: 
  	* `1`
  	* `2`
  	* `3`

3. Choose the type of damage that occurred.
  	* Select the correct damage type: 
  	* `fire`
  	* `water`
  	* `wind`

4. Enter a description of the damage that occurred.
  	* Enter a detailed description of the damage.

5. Enter the date the damage occurred.
  	* Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY

6. Enter your email address so we can contact you for further assistance.
  	* Enter your email address

7. Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further assistance.
  	* Enter your phone number

8. Enter your name so we can address you by name.
  	* Enter your name

9. Enter your location so we can locate you for further assistance.
  	* Enter your location

10. Enter the name of the person who submitted the report.
  	* Enter the name of the person

11. Enter the email address of the person who submitted the report.
  	* Enter the email address

12. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the report.
  	* Enter the phone number

13. Enter your location so we can locate you for further assistance.
  	* Enter your location

14. Enter any additional comments about the damage.
  	* Enter any additional comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Name** (`property_name`, `select_one`, **Required:** No)
	+ Select the name of the property damaged.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_multiple`, **Required:** No)
	+ Select all locations where the damage occurred.
* **Damage Type** (`damage_type`, `select_one`, **Required:** No)
	+ Choose the type of damage.
* **Damage Description** (`damage_description`, `text`, **Required:** No)
	+ Enter a detailed description of the damage.
* **Damage Date** (`damage_date`, `date`, **Required:** No)
	+ Enter the date the damage occurred in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, **Required:** No)
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you for further assistance.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, **Required:** No)
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further assistance.
* **Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, **Required:** No)
	+ Enter your name so we can address you by name.
* **Location** (`contact_location`, `text`, **Required:** No)
	+ Enter your location so we can locate you for further assistance.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by_name`, `text`, **Required:** No)
	+ Enter the name of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, `email`, **Required:** No)
	+ Enter the email address of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, `text`, **Required:** No)
	+ Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By Location** (`submitter_location`, `text`, **Required:** No)
	+ Enter your location so we can locate you for further assistance.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **Required:** No)
	+ Enter any additional comments about the damage.

## Tips
Please provide accurate and detailed information about the damage to help us assist you better.
