# job_role_clarity_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "job_role_clarity_survey" form is designed to gather information about job roles within an organization. It helps employees provide clarity on their responsibilities, expectations, and other relevant details, which will be used to evaluate and refine job roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go through each page and read the labels carefully.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### intro_page (Intro Label, Note, Not Required)
*   **intro_page** (Intro Label, Note, Not Required): This is an introductory page that welcomes you to the survey and explains its purpose.

### role_expectations (Expectations, Select Multiple, Required)
*   **expectations** (Expectations, Select Multiple, Required): Select all expectations that you have for your job role. This might include specific responsibilities, goals, or tasks you're required to accomplish.

### responsibilities (Responsibilities, Text, Required)
*   **responsibilities** (Responsibilities, Text, Required): Describe the key responsibilities for your job role in as much detail as possible.

### role_accountabilities (Accountabilities, Select One, Not Required)
*   **accountabilities** (Accountabilities, Select One, Not Required): You don't need to select anything for this field unless you feel it's necessary for your job role clarity.

### job_cleary (Clarity, Select Multiple, Not Required)
*   **clarity** (Clarity, Select Multiple, Not Required): You can select all that apply for clarity on your job role. This could be related to understanding your role's purpose or expectations.

### job_alignment (Alignment, Select One, Not Required)
*   **alignment** (Alignment, Select One, Not Required): This field is for selecting the best description of your job role's alignment within the company.

### job_communication (Communication, Select One, Not Required)
*   **communication** (Communication, Select One, Not Required): You can select the best description of your job role's communication style or expectations.

### day_to_day_coordination (Coordination, Select One, Not Required)
*   **coordination** (Coordination, Select One, Not Required): This is about selecting the best description of how you coordinate daily activities for your role.

### job_title (Job Title, Text, Not Required)
*   **job_title** (Job Title, Text, Not Required): If you feel it's necessary, provide a job title for your role. This will be used for reference purposes.

### manager (Manager, Select One, Not Required)
*   **manager** (Manager, Select One, Not Required): Select the name of your direct manager for the record.

### onboarding_process (Onboarding Process, Select One, Not Required)
*   **onboarding_process** (Onboarding Process, Select One, Not Required): You don't need to select anything for this field unless it's essential for your job role clarity.

### team_member (Team Member, Select One, Not Required)
*   **team_member** (Team Member, Select One, Not Required): This is about identifying your team affiliation.

### job_role_name (Role Name, Text, Not Required)
*   **role_name** (Role Name, Text, Not Required): If needed, provide a name for your role.

### role_description (Role Description, Text, Not Required)
*   **role_description** (Role Description, Text, Not Required): This is an optional field for describing your role in more detail.

### role_status (Role Status, Select One, Not Required)
*   **role_status** (Role Status, Select One, Not Required): You can choose the best status description for your role.

### expectations_score (Expectations Score, Number, Required)
*   **expectations_score** (Expectations Score, Number, Required): Provide a number from 1-5 to rate your expectations on a scale. 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### responsibilities_score (Responsibilities Score, Number, Required)
*   **responsibilities_score** (Responsibilities Score, Number, Required): Provide a number from 1-5 to rate your responsibilities on a scale of 1 to 5.

### job_cleary_score (Clarity Score, Number, Not Required)
*   **clarity_score** (Clarity Score, Number, Not Required): Select a score from 1-5 for how clear your job role is.

### job_role_alignment_score (Alignment Score, Number, Not Required)
*   **alignment_score** (Alignment Score, Number, Not Required): Rate your alignment with the company on a scale of 1-5.

### job_communication_score (Communication Score, Number, Not Required)
*   **communication_score** (Communication Score, Number, Not Required): Choose a score for your communication style on a 1-5 scale.

### day_to_day_coordination_score (Coordination Score, Number, Not Required)
*   **coordination_score** (Coordination Score, Number, Not Required): Rate your coordination on a daily basis with other team members or departments on a 1-5 scale.

### job_title_score (Job Title Score, Number, Not Required)
*   **job_title_score** (Job Title Score, Number, Not Required): If you want, provide a score for your job title on a 1-5 scale.

### manager_score (Manager Score, Number, Not Required)
*   **manager_score** (Manager Score, Number, Not Required): You can select a score for your manager on a 1-5 scale.

### onboarding_process_score (Onboarding Process Score, Number, Not Required)
*   **onboarding_process_score** (Onboarding Process Score, Number, Not Required): Choose a score for your onboarding process on a 1-5 scale.

### team_member_score (Team Member Score, Number, Not Required)
*   **team_member_score** (Team Member Score, Number, Not Required): You don't need to select a score for this field unless it's essential for clarity on your role within the team.
