# Building Condition Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Condition Assessment Survey is a comprehensive form used to evaluate the overall condition of a building. It helps in identifying potential issues and prioritizing necessary repairs or maintenance. This form is used to gather information about the building's physical state, systems, and safety features.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Begin by filling out the basic contact information: Assessor Name, Company, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2.  Enter the date of the assessment and the name and address of the building being assessed.
3.  Classify the building type (e.g., Office, Residential, etc.).
4.  Provide the year the building was constructed and its total square footage.
5.  Assess the condition of various building components such as foundation, structural elements, roof, exterior walls, windows and doors, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and interior spaces.
6.  Describe any major deficiencies found during the assessment.
7.  List recommended repairs and estimated repair costs (if any).
8.  Finally, provide an overall rating of the building's condition and its priority level for repairs or maintenance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the assessor.
*   **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Type in the name of the assessment firm or organization conducting the survey.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email for further communication.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Provide the contact number for any follow-up questions or concerns.
*   **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required): Record the date of the assessment.
*   **Building Name** (`building_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the building being assessed.
*   **Building Address** (`building_address`, `text`, required): Provide the full address of the building.
*   **Building Type** (`building_type`, `select_one`, required): Classify the building type (e.g., Office, Residential, etc.).
*   **Year Built** (`year_built`, `number`, optional): If known, enter the year the building was constructed.
*   **Total Square Footage** (`total_square_footage`, `number`, required): Enter the total square footage of the building.
*   **Number of Floors** (`number_of_floors`, `number`, required): Indicate the total number of floors in the building.
*   **Foundation Condition** (`foundation_condition`, `select_one`, required): Rate the condition of the foundation (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
*   **Structural Condition** (`structural_condition`, `select_one`, required): Assess the condition of the building's structural elements (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
*   **Roof Condition** (`roof_condition`, `select_one`, required): Rate the condition of the roof (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
*   **Exterior Walls Condition** (`exterior_walls`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the exterior walls (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
*   **Windows and Doors** (`windows_doors`, `select_one`, required): Rate the condition of windows and doors (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
*   **Plumbing System** (`plumbing_system`, `select_one`, required): Assess the condition of the plumbing system (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical, Not Assessed).
*   **Electrical System** (`electrical_system`, `select_one`, required): Rate the condition of the electrical system (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical, Not Assessed).
*   **HVAC System** (`hvac_system`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of the HVAC system (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical, Not Assessed).
*   **Interior Condition** (`interior_condition`, `select_one`, required): Assess the condition of interior spaces (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
*   **Flooring Condition** (`flooring_condition`, `select_one`, required): Rate the condition of floors (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
*   **Fire Safety Systems** (`fire_safety`, `select_one`, required): Rate the condition of fire safety systems (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical, Not Assessed).
*   **Accessibility Compliance** (`accessibility_compliance`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the accessibility compliance (Fully Compliant, Mostly Compliant, Partially Compliant, Non-Compliant, Not Assessed).
*   **Major Deficiencies** (`major_deficiencies`, `text`, required): Describe any major issues found during the assessment.
*   **Recommended Repairs** (`recommended_repairs`, `text`, required): List any recommended repairs.
*   **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the urgency of needed repairs (Immediate Action Required, Action Within 6 Months, Action Within 1 Year, Routine Maintenance, No Action Needed).
*   **Estimated Repair Cost** (`estimated_repair_cost`, `select_one`, optional): Provide an approximate cost for recommended repairs (Under $10,000, $10,000 - $50,000, $50,000 - $100,000, $100,000 - $500,000, Over $500,000).
*   **Overall Building Condition** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`, required): Finalize the assessment with an overall rating of the building's condition (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).

## Tips
*   Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to get a comprehensive assessment of the building's condition.
*   Make sure to save the completed survey for future reference or updates.
*   If any field marked as "required" is left empty, it will prevent you from submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field, seek guidance from the relevant authorities or consult a subject matter expert.
