# sports_nutrition_education_awareness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and insights from users on their experience with sports nutrition education awareness programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read through each question carefully and answer them to the best of your abilities.
2.  Ensure you have participated in the relevant program before filling out this form.
3.  Make sure to fill out only one entry per user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, not required): This is a text area where you can provide a brief introduction or explanation about your experience. You can write as much or as little as you want here.
* **Demographic Details** (`2`, `text`, not required): Please provide your demographic details such as your age, occupation, or any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Survey Background** (`3`, `text`, not required): Describe your background or experience with sports nutrition education, if any.
* **Nutrition Knowledge** (`4`, `text`, not required): Share your level of understanding about sports nutrition and what you've learned from the program.
* **Education Level** (`5`, `select_one`, not required): Choose one of the following options for your level of education about sports nutrition: High or Low.
    *   High
    *   Low

## Tips
Please note that this form is for feedback and insight gathering purposes only. Answer honestly and to the best of your abilities.
