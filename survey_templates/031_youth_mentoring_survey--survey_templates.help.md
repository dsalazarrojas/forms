# Youth Mentoring Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and evaluation data from youth participants in a mentoring program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Complete each field with accurate and honest information.
3. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. For fields with text input, provide detailed and descriptive answers.
5. Submit the survey when you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`text`, `General Info`, required: false): This field is for general information about your program experience. Please provide a brief description of your participation in the program.
* **Program Info** (`text`, `Program Info`, required: false): This field is for information about the program you participated in. Please provide details about the program's activities, duration, and any other relevant information.
* **Mentorship Info** (`text`, `Mentorship Info`, required: false): This field is for feedback about your mentorship experience. Please provide information about your mentor's guidance, support, and communication.
* **Participant Info** (`text`, `Participant Info`, required: false): This field is for information about your own participation in the program. Please provide details about your goals, expectations, and any challenges you faced.
* **Program Outcome** (`text`, `Program Outcome`, required: false): This field is for information about the outcome of the program. Please describe what you achieved or accomplished through the program.
* **Feedback** (`text`, `Feedback`, required: false): This field is for any additional feedback or comments you have about the program.
* **Evaluation** (`select_multiple`, `Evaluation`, required: false):
	+ **Mentoring relationship**: How would you describe your mentorship relationship?
	+ **Program support**: How would you describe the program's support for you?
	+ **Communication**: How would you describe the communication between you and your mentor?
	+ **Feedback/evaluation**: How would you describe the feedback and evaluation process?
	+ **Other**: Please specify any other aspects of the program that you would like to evaluate.
* **Program Eval** (`text`, `Program Eval`, required: false): This field is for any additional information about the program's evaluation.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`select_one`, `Overall Satisfaction`, required: false):
	+ **Very satisfied**
	+ **Somewhat satisfied**
	+ **Neutral**
	+ **Somewhat dissatisfied**
	+ **Very dissatisfied**
* **Recommendation** (`select_multiple`, `Recommendation`, required: false):
	+ **Strongly recommend**
	+ **Somewhat recommend**
	+ **Neither recommend nor not recommend**
	+ **Do not recommend**
* **Comments** (`text`, `Comments`, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have about the program.

## Tips
Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly. Your feedback is valuable for improving the program. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact email or phone number].
