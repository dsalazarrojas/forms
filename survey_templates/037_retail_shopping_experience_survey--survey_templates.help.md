# Retail Shopping Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their shopping experience in our store. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and provide your honest opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and select one answer per question.
2. Answer all questions on each page.
3. Submit your completed form to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **survey_questions** (`survey_questions`, select_multiple, required=False): How was your visit to our store?
	* This question is about your overall experience during your visit to our store.
- **shopping_experience** (`shopping_experience`, note, required=False): What is your experience like in our store?
	* This question is about your personal experience in our store, and any comments or feedback you have about it.
- **staff_friendliness** (`staff_friendliness`, select_one, required=False): How would you rate the friendliness of our staff?
	* This question is about how friendly and welcoming our staff are during your visit.
	* You can rate them as either Good or Fair.
- **product_range** (`product_range`, number, required=False): How would you rate the quality of our products?
	* This question is about your opinion on the quality of our products.
	* Please rate them on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is Poor and 5 is Excellent.
- **store_atmosphere** (`store_atmosphere`, select_one, required=False): How would you rate the store atmosphere?
	* This question is about your opinion on the store atmosphere, including decor and comfort.
	* You can rate them as either Good or Fair.
- **product_quality** (`product_quality`, select_one, required=False): How would you rate the product quality?
	* This question is about your opinion on the quality of our products.
	* You can rate them as either Good or Poor.
- **staff_knowledge** (`staff_knowledge`, select_one, required=False): How would you rate the knowledge of our staff?
	* This question is about how knowledgeable our staff are about our products.
	* You can rate them as either Good or Fair.
- **store_location** (`store_location`, select_one, required=False): How would you rate the location of our store?
	* This question is about your opinion on the location of our store.
	* You can rate them as either Good or Fair.
- **communication** (`communication`, select_one, required=False): How was the communication during your visit?
	* This question is about how our staff communicated with you during your visit.
	* You can rate them as either Yes or No.
- **product_return** (`product_return`, select_one, required=False): How likely are you to return to our store?
	* This question is about your likelihood to return to our store in the future.
	* You can rate them as either Yes or No.
- **overall_experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required=False): Overall, how would you rate your experience?
	* This question is about your overall experience in our store.
	* You can rate them as either Good or Fair.
- **product_recommendation** (`product_recommendation`, select_one, required=False): Would you recommend our store to a friend?
	* This question is about whether you would recommend our store to a friend or family member.
	* You can rate them as either Yes or No.
- **product_purchase** (`product_purchase`, select_one, required=False): How likely are you to make a purchase in our store?
	* This question is about your likelihood to make a purchase in our store.
	* You can rate them as either Yes or No.
- **staff_helpfulness** (`staff_helpfulness`, select_one, required=False): How helpful were our staff during your visit?
	* This question is about how helpful our staff were during your visit.
	* You can rate them as either Yes or No.
- **product_quality_investment** (`product_quality_investment`, select_one, required=False): How much do you think we invest in product quality?
	* This question is about our investment in product quality.
	* You can rate them as either Good or Poor.
