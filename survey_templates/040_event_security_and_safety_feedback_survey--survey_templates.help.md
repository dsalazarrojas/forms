# Event Security And Safety Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on the event's security and safety measures. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to help us understand what went well and what can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information on the first page, which includes the survey start time, event organizer, event start and end dates, venue, number of attendees, and overall survey end time.
2. On the second page, indicate whether the event was attended, and provide details on safety measures taken, such as the presence of a first aid kit, emergency exit routes, a safety briefing, and emergency contact numbers.
3. On the third page, rate the event's safety risk and procedure effectiveness, and indicate which security procedures were in place, such as security cameras, emergency exit routes, and security guards.
4. Finally, provide an overall rating of the event's security performance and add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey start** (`1`, `time`, required): Record the start time of the survey. This is to help us understand when you started filling out the survey.
* **Event attended** (`2`, `number`, optional): Indicate whether you attended the event. This helps us understand the demographics of our respondents.
* **Survey organizer** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person or organization that organized the event. This is crucial for us to know who to contact for more information.
* **Event start date** (`4`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the event started. This helps us to keep track of the time frame of the event.
* **Event end date** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the event ended. This helps us to keep track of the event duration.
* **Venue** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether the event venue had a security presence, such as police or security guards.
* **Number of attendees** (`7`, `number`, required): Provide an accurate count of the number of attendees at the event. This helps us to understand the scale of the event.
* **Safety measures taken** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all the safety measures that were taken, such as first aid kits, emergency exit routes, safety briefings, and emergency contact numbers. This helps us to understand what was done to ensure the attendees' safety.
* **Safety procedure** (`9`, `text`, required): Describe the safety procedures in place during the event. This is an opportunity to provide more details on what was done.
* **Event safety risk rating** (`10`, `number`, required): Rate the level of safety risk present during the event, with higher numbers indicating a higher risk.
* **Safety procedure effectiveness** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Rate the effectiveness of the safety procedures, choosing from highly effective, somewhat effective, or not effective at all.
* **Security procedures** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all the security procedures that were in place, such as security cameras, emergency exit routes, and security guards.
* **Overall security performance** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Provide an overall rating of the event's security performance, choosing from excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Additional comments** (`14`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them here.
* **Survey end** (`15`, `time`, required): Record the end time of the survey. This is to help us understand when you completed the survey.
