# Parenting Style Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parenting Style Survey is designed to help parents understand their own parenting style and how it may be impacting their child's development. This survey aims to gather information on various aspects of parenting, such as response frequency, physical contact, emotional support, and conflict resolution. By completing this survey, parents can gain insight into their own strengths and weaknesses, and identify areas for improvement to create a more harmonious and supportive relationship with their child.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select one or multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections (indicated by an "select_multiple" type).
3. Enter numerical values for questions that require a numerical answer (indicated by a "number" type).
4. For questions with no options, provide a brief written response.
