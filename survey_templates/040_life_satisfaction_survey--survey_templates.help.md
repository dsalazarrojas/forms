# Life Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback on different aspects of overall life satisfaction. This includes overall satisfaction, happiness, and well-being, as well as specific areas such as life activities, relationships, and personal perceptions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page and field carefully.
2. For fields with numeric values, please enter a number between 0 and 100.
3. For fields with select-one and select-multiple options, choose the option that best reflects your current state or feeling.
4. Make sure to answer all fields, as they are all relevant to the survey's purpose.
5. If a field is not required, you can still answer it if you feel it's relevant to your situation.
