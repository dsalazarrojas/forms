# Property Sentiment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Sentiment Survey is a form designed to gather information about a property's sentiment. It is meant to be completed by users who have a property for sale or rent, and it will help them to provide an accurate and detailed description of their property.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Review the questions carefully and answer them based on the information about your property.
2.  Please fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  If you are unsure about a question or its relevance to your property, please reach out to the support team for clarification.
4.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **property_price**: This field is used to capture the price of the property. Please enter the price in the format of a number.
*   **property_location**: This field is a dropdown menu that asks you to select the location of the property. Please choose the option that best describes the location of your property.
*   **property_condition**: This field is a dropdown menu that asks you to select the condition of the property. Please choose the option that best describes the condition of your property.
*   **property_size**: This field is used to capture the size of the property. Please enter the size in the format of a number.
*   **property_type**: This field is a dropdown menu that asks you to select the type of property. Please choose the option that best describes the type of your property.
*   **property_features**: This field is a dropdown menu that asks you to select the features of the property. Please choose the options that best describe the features of your property.
*   **property_sentiment**: This field is used to capture any additional information about the property. Please enter a brief description in the format of plain text.
*   **property_price_range**: This field is a dropdown menu that asks you to select the price range of the property. Please choose the option that best describes the price range of your property.
*   **property_value**: This field is used to capture the value of the property. Please enter the value in the format of a date.
*   **property_size_range**: This field is a dropdown menu that asks you to select the size range of the property. Please choose the option that best describes the size range of your property.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about a question or its relevance to your property, please reach out to the support team for clarification.
