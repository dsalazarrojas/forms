# Biscuit Awards Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to nominate individuals or achievements for Biscuit Awards. This form gathers information about the nominee, their achievements, and the justification for the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by reading the introductory note and understanding the purpose of the form.
2.  Fill out the nominator's section, providing your name, email address, phone number, and organization information.
3.  Select the award category for which you are nominating.
4.  Enter the information about the nominee, including their name, organization, title or position, and email address if available.
5.  Provide a brief description of why the nominee deserves the award and list specific achievements.
6.  If applicable, describe the nominee's impact on the biscuit industry.
7.  Select the scale of their achievement and describe any previous recognitions or awards received.
8.  Provide a link to any supporting documentation.
9.  If necessary, provide a contact name and email or phone number for verification.
10.  Confirm that the nomination is accurate and that you have permission to contact the nominee.
11.  Check the conflict of interest statement and provide any relevant information.
12.  Select whether you have permission to contact the nominee for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name** (`nominator_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
*   **Email Address** (`nominator_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`nominator_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Organization** (`nominator_organization`, `text`, required): Enter your company or organization name.
*   **Your Title or Position** (`nominator_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your role.
*   **Award Category** (`award_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the award category for which you are nominating.
*   **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the nominee.
*   **Nominee Organization** (`nominee_organization`, `text`, required): Enter the company or organization of the nominee.
*   **Nominee Title or Position** (`nominee_title`, `text`, required): Enter the nominee's role.
*   **Why They Deserve This Award** (`why_deserving`, `text`, required): Briefly describe why the nominee deserves the award.
*   **Key Achievements** (`achievements_list`, `text`, required): List specific accomplishments of the nominee.
*   **Impact on the Biscuit Industry** (`impact_on_industry`, `text`, required): Describe the nominee's influence on the industry.
*   **What Makes Them Unique** (`uniqueness`, `text`, optional): Describe what makes the nominee special.
*   **Years in the Biscuit Industry** (`years_in_industry`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years the nominee has been involved in the industry.
*   **Previous Recognition or Awards** (`previous_recognition_received`, `text`, optional): List any previous awards or honors received by the nominee.
*   **Scale of Achievement** (`awards_scale`, `select_one`, required): Rate the scope of the nominee's impact.
*   **Business or Program Growth** (`market_growth`, `text`, optional): Describe the growth achieved by the nominee.
*   **Reference Contact 1** (`reference_contact1`, `text`, optional): Enter a contact name and email or phone number.
*   **Reference 1 Contact Information** (`reference_contact1_info`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact's email or phone number.
*   **Reference Contact 2** (`reference_contact2`, `text`, optional): Enter another contact name and email or phone number.
*   **Supporting Documentation Link** (`supporting_documentation`, `text`, optional): Enter a link to any supporting documentation.
*   **I Confirm Accuracy** (`accuracy_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the nomination is accurate.
*   **Conflict of Interest Statement** (`conflict_of_interest`, `text`, optional): Check if there is a conflict of interest.
*   **We May Contact Nominee** (`consent_to_contact`, `select_one`, required): Confirm permission to contact the nominee.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, and provide as much information as possible.
- Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- Ensure that the nomination is fair and in line with the Biscuit Awards criteria.
