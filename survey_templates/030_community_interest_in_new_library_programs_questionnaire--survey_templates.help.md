# Community Interest In New Library Programs Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to help us understand what types of programs and services our community is interested in. Your input will help us tailor our offerings to best meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer each question carefully, and remember to select all that apply for the options that suit your preferences. If you have any suggestions for new programs or services that we haven't mentioned, feel free to provide them in the text field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Age Group of the Primary Participant
* **Age Group of the Primary Participant** (`respondent_age_group`, `select_one`, required): Please select the age group of the primary person filling out this form.

### 2. Which new program topics would you be most interested in?
* **Which new program topics would you be most interested in?** (`program_topic_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the program topics that you think would be engaging for you.

### 3. Preferred Days of the Week for Programs
* **Preferred Days of the Week for Programs** (`preferred_days`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the days of the week that you are available to attend programs.

### 4. Preferred Time of Day for Programs
* **Preferred Time of Day for Programs** (`preferred_times`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the time slots that you prefer for program attendance.

### 5. Preferred Frequency of Program Attendance
* **Preferred Frequency of Program Attendance** (`program_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose the frequency at which you would like to attend programs.

### 6. Preferred Program Format
* **Preferred Program Format** (`program_format`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred format for programs.

### 7. Would you be interested in volunteering to lead or assist a program?
* **Would you be interested in volunteering to lead or assist a program?** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you would be willing to volunteer to lead or assist a program.

### 8. Do you currently have a valid Library Card?
* **Do you currently have a valid Library Card?** (`library_card_holder`, `select_one`, required): Please confirm if you have a valid library card.

### 9. How often do you visit the library currently?
* **How often do you visit the library currently?** (`visit_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select the frequency at which you visit the library.

### 10. Program suggestions
* **Program suggestions** (`program_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions for new programs or services that we haven't mentioned.

### 11. Can we contact you about upcoming library programs?
* **Can we contact you about upcoming library programs?** (`contact_consent`, `select_one`, required): Let us know how you would prefer to be contacted about upcoming library programs.

### 12. Your Contact Email (if applicable)
* **Your Contact Email** (`respondent_email`, `email`, optional): Please provide your email address if you would like to be contacted about library programs.
