# Places And Sounds Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback about their experiences in different environments. It will help us understand how users perceive and respond to various environments in terms of sound conditions, moods, and productivity levels.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question as honestly as possible.
- Select the options that best apply to your experiences.
- You can skip any sections that do not apply to you.
- Make sure to enter a contact number and email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **intro_section** (Page 1, Text, Optional): This is a brief description of your current environment.
- **environment** (Page 2, Select One, Optional): How would you describe your current environment?
  • Yes: You are in a busy or noisy environment.
  • No: You are in a quiet or peaceful environment.
- **sound_condition** (Page 2, Select One, Optional): How would you describe the sound conditions in your current environment?
  • Yes: There are loud sounds or background noises.
  • No: The environment is quiet.
- **mood** (Page 2, Select Multiple, Optional): How would you describe your current mood?
  • Yes: You are feeling relaxed.
  • No: You are feeling stressed or anxious.
- **productivity** (Page 2, Select Multiple, Optional): How would you describe your productivity level?
  • Yes: You are highly productive.
  • No: You are not highly productive.
- **section_2** (Page 3, Text, Optional): Please describe any other comments or observations you have about your environment.
- **location** (Page 3, Select One, Optional): Where are you currently located? 
  • Yes: In a specific location (e.g., at home, in a store, etc.)
  • No: Not in a specific location (e.g., outdoors, in a vehicle, etc.)
- **section_3** (Page 3, Text, Optional): Would you like to provide any other information about yourself.
- **comment** (Note, Optional): Any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide.
- **contact** (Email, Optional): Please enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
  • [Insert email address]
- **phone** (Text, Optional): Please enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
  • [Insert phone number]
- **date** (Date, Optional): Please enter the date of your completion of this form.
  • [Insert date]
