# Alcohol Consumption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect anonymized data on alcohol consumption habits and behaviors. Please answer honestly to help us better understand patterns and trends in alcohol use.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all required questions (indicated by an asterisk *) on each page. You can select only one option from each select_one field.
2. For select_multiple fields, select all that apply.
3. Enter a whole number for the units_per_typical_day field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Range (Page 1, Question 1)** (`age_range`, `select_one`, *): Choose the age range that best describes you.
* **Gender Identity (Page 1, Question 2)** (`gender_identity`, `select_one`, *): Select your gender identity.
* **How often do you have a drink containing alcohol? (Page 2, Question 5)** (`drinking_frequency`, `select_one`, *): Answer how often you typically have a drink containing alcohol. 
* **Number of units of alcohol on a typical day (Page 2, Question 6)** (`units_per_typical_day`, `number`, *): If you do drink, enter the number of units on a typical day when you do drink. 
* **How often do you have six or more units on one occasion? (Page 2, Question 7)** (`binge_frequency`, `select_one`, *): Answer how often you have had six or more units of alcohol on one occasion.
* **Preferred Type of Alcohol (Page 3, Question 8)** (`primary_beverage_type`, `select_multiple`, *): Select all the types of alcohol you enjoy.
* **Where do you typically consume alcohol? (Page 3, Question 14)** (`drinking_locations`, `select_multiple`, *): Select all the places where you typically drink.
* **Primary Reason for Drinking (Page 3, Question 15)** (`reason_for_drinking`, `select_one`, *): Choose your primary reason for drinking.
* **Have you or someone else been injured as a result of your drinking? (Page 4, Question 11)** (`injury_related`, `select_one`, *): Answer whether you or someone else has been injured as a result of your drinking.
* **Have a relative, friend, or doctor been concerned about your drinking? (Page 4, Question 12)** (`concern_from_others`, `select_one`, *): Answer whether a relative, friend, or doctor has expressed concern about your drinking.
* **Have you felt guilty or remorseful after drinking in the last year? (Page 5, Question 10)** (`guilt_feelings`, `select_one`, *): Answer how often you feel guilty or remorseful after drinking.
* **I consent to my anonymized data being used for research purposes (Page 6, Question 16)** (`survey_consent`, `select_one`, *): Confirm your consent to participate in the survey.

## Tips

* Please be honest in your answers to help us better understand patterns and trends in alcohol use.
* You can select only one option from each select_one field.
* For select_multiple fields, select all that apply.
