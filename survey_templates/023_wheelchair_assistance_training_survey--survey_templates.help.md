# Wheelchair Assistance Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience with the wheelchair assistance training session. Your responses will help us improve the training program and better serve users in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the answer that best describes your response to each question.
2. Provide your email address and phone number if you would like to receive follow-up communication.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all questions.
