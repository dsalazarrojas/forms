# Pdy Staff Technology Checkout - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help staff members check out technology equipment, allowing for accurate inventory management and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you are only checking out equipment that you are authorized to use.
2. Review the form carefully to avoid duplicate entries.
3. Fill out the form with the necessary information as follows:
	* **technology_checkout_form_1** (text field): This field is required to provide a title for the checkout.
	* **equipment_checked_1** (select_one): Select the equipment you are checking out from the available options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
	* **equipment_checked_2** (select_one): Select the equipment you are checking out from the available options (Option 1, Option 2).
	* **technology_staff_1** (select_one): Select the staff member's name from the available options (Option 1, Option 2).
	* **technology_staff_2** (select_one): Select the staff member's name from the available options (Option 1, Option 2).
	* **technology_equipment_1** (select_multiple): Select the equipment items you are checking out from the available options (Option 1, Option 2).
	* **technology_equipment_2** (select_multiple): Select the equipment items you are checking out from the available options (Option 1, Option 2).
	* **teacher_report_1** (date field): Enter the date you plan to return the equipment.
	* **teacher_report_2** (date field): Enter the date you plan to return the equipment.
	* **teacher_report_3** (date field): Enter the date you plan to return the equipment.
