# Flea Market Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your experience at the flea market. We want to hear about your overall satisfaction and any feedback or comments you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For "Market Day", simply type in the number of the day you visited (if you're not sure, you can leave it blank).
3. Select one of the options for "What was your overall experience at the vendor table?".
4. For "Food and drinks", select all the options that apply to you.
5. For "Satisfaction", select one of the options that best reflects your feelings.
6. In the "Comments" field, type any additional feedback or comments you have about your visit.
7. If you have a contact email, please type it in the "Email" field.
8. If you have a contact phone number, type it in the "Phone" field.
9. Finally, type any other feedback or comments in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Market Day**: Please type in the number of the day you visited the flea market.
* **What was your overall experience at the vendor table?**: This question asks how you felt about your overall experience at the vendor table. Please select one of the options that best reflects your feelings. 
* **Food and drinks**: This section asks about the types of food and drinks you experienced during your visit. Please select all that apply.
* **Satisfaction**: This question asks how you felt about your overall satisfaction with your visit. Please select one of the options that best reflects your feelings. 
* **Comments**: This field is where you can type any additional feedback or comments you have about your visit.
* **Email**: If you have a contact email, please type it in here so we can reach out to you with any follow-up questions or offers.
* **Phone**: If you have a contact phone number, type it in here so we can reach out to you with any follow-up questions or offers.
* **Feedback**: This field is where you can type any other feedback or comments you have about your visit.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate with your answers.
* Take your time to complete the form thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
