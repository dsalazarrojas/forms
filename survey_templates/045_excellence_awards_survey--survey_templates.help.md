# Excellence Awards Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool used to nominate individuals or teams for excellence awards. The survey is designed to gather relevant information from the nominator and nominee. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your nominee receives the consideration they deserve.

## How To Complete This Form

### To begin, fill out your company's information

*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Please enter your company's name as it appears on its official documents.
*   **Company Rep Name** (`company_rep_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the representative from your company who nominated this individual or team.
*   **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, optional): If you're nominating a team, enter the name of the team leader or primary contact.

### Nomination Information

*   **Award Nomination** (`award_nomination`, select\_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you are nominating this individual or team for an award.
*   **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the individual or team being nominated.
*   **Nominee Job Title** (`nominee_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the nominee.
*   **Nominee Department** (`nominee_department`, text, optional): Enter the department where the nominee works.
*   **Nominee Location** (`nominee_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the nominee works.
*   **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the nominee.
*   **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the nominee.

### Nomination Details

*   **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the company.
*   **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the nomination.
*   **Nomination Time** (`nomination_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the nomination.
*   **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for nominating this individual or team.
*   **Nominee Brief Info** (`nominee_brief_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the nominee's work or achievements.
*   **Nominee Info** (`nominee_info`, text, optional): Enter more detailed information about the nominee.
*   **Nominee Image** (`nominee_image`, text, optional): Enter an image or URL of the nominee's work.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the nominee receives proper consideration.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the above explanations.
*   This form should not be used for duplicate nominations or to nominate individuals who have already been previously nominated.
