# Outdoor Activity Safety Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help you assess your outdoor activity safety awareness. Please answer the questions honestly to help us better understand your safety needs and provide you with relevant information and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. For the "Outdoor Habits" page, select all the outdoor activities you regularly participate in.
3. On the "Safety Equipment" page, check all the safety equipment you use or have access to.
4. On the "Training Needs" page, select all the safety training you need or have received.
5. In the "Safety Programs" page, describe any safety programs or initiatives you participate in or are interested in.
6. On the "Education Needs" page, select the area of safety training you are most interested in.
7. Provide your emergency contact information, including your email address and phone number.
8. If you have any other safety needs or concerns, please describe them in the "Other Needs" field.
9. Finally, provide any additional comments or notes in the "Submission Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Outdoor Habits** (`outdoor_habits`, `select_multiple`, required): This page asks you to select all the outdoor activities you regularly participate in, such as hiking, cycling, swimming, or rock climbing.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, `select_multiple`, required): This page asks you to check all the safety equipment you use or have access to, including helmets, first aid kits, emergency whistles, and maps.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, `select_multiple`, required): This page asks you to select all the safety training you need or have received, such as basic wilderness first aid, wilderness survival, or map and compass use.
* **Safety Programs** (`safety_programs`, `text`, required): This page asks you to describe any safety programs or initiatives you participate in or are interested in.
* **Education Needs** (`education_needs`, `select_one`, required): This page asks you to select the area of safety training you are most interested in, such as basic outdoor safety or safety and emergency response.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `email`, required): This page asks you to provide your emergency contact information, including your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): This page asks you to provide your phone number.
* **Other Needs** (`other_needs`, `text`, required): This page asks you to describe any other safety needs or concerns you have.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): This page is for you to provide any additional comments or notes about your safety awareness.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, `note`, required): This page is for you to provide any additional information about your submission.
