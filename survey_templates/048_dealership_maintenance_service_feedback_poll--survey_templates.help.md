# Dealership Maintenance Service Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their recent service visit to the dealership. The goal is to understand the customer's experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for "Service Visit ID" to match your recent service visit.
2. Choose the type of service you received (e.g., Oil Change, Tire Rotation, etc.).
3. Select the overall quality of the service you received (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Rate your satisfaction with the service (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
5. Indicate whether you would use this dealership again.
6. Indicate whether you would recommend this dealership to others.
7. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Visit ID (Required)**: Choose the ID of your recent service visit.
* **Service Type (Required)**: Select the type of service you received (e.g., Oil Change, Tire Rotation, etc.).
* **Technician Name (Optional)**: If you remember the name of the technician who served you, enter it here.
* **Service Quality (Required)**: Rate the quality of the service you received (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Satisfaction (Required)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Would Use Again (Required)**: Indicate whether you would use this dealership again.
* **Would Recommend (Required)**: Indicate whether you would recommend this dealership to others.
* **Improvement Areas (Optional)**: If you have any comments or suggestions for improvement, enter them here.
* **Additional Comments (Optional)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct "Service Visit ID" to ensure your feedback is accurately linked to your service visit.
* Be honest and accurate when rating the "Service Quality" and "Satisfaction" fields.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Improvement Areas" and "Additional Comments" fields to provide them.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
