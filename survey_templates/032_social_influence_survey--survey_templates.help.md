# Social Influence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on social influence, allowing users to provide input on various aspects of their social interactions and experiences. The survey is meant to be a comprehensive tool to gather insights on how people perceive and interact with their social environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read through each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Select the relevant options for each question. If a question does not apply to you, simply select "Prefer not to say" or leave it blank.
3. Provide any additional information in the "Additional info" field, if necessary.
4. Complete all the questions in the "Comments" field if you have any further comments or thoughts.
5. Finally, rate your level of agreement with a number from 1 to 5 in the "Rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **intro** (`intro`, note, optional): This is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **demographics** (`demographics`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options that describe your demographic characteristics, such as your sex and other relevant attributes.
* **behavior** (`behavior`, text, optional): Please describe your social behavior, providing more details if necessary.
* **impact** (`impact`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best describes how you perceive the impact of social influence on your life.
* **social** (`social`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which social groups you interact with, such as family, friends, or community.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you typically spend on social activities.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter a date if you want to provide a specific date for your answer.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have any contact information, please provide it here.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address, if you wish to be contacted.
* **additional info** (`additional info`, text, optional): If you have any further information to add, please type it here.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any thoughts or observations you have regarding the survey.
* **rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with social influence, using a scale from 1 to 5.
