# Automation Challenges in Manufacturing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about the challenges and experiences of manufacturing companies related to automation. Your participation will help us understand the current state of automation in the industry and provide insights to help companies overcome associated challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. Select the most accurate options for each question.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. If you have any additional comments or insights, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
