# Claims Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on the claims process from claimants, to help us understand their experiences and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Use the provided options to select your responses for questions with a select_one type.
3. For questions with a text type, provide as much detail as you would like.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by *) to submit a complete survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Claims Satisfaction Survey --**: This page introduces the purpose of the survey.
* **Claim Number**: Please enter your claim number, if known.
* **Name of Claims Representative**: If you know the representative who handled your claim, please enter their name.
* **Overall, how satisfied were you with the claims process?**: Select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Very Satisfied: You were extremely satisfied with the process.
	+ Satisfied: You were somewhat satisfied with the process.
	+ Neutral: The process was average.
	+ Dissatisfied: You were not satisfied with the process.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You were extremely dissatisfied with the process.
* **How easy was it to file your claim?**: Select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Very Easy: The process was straightforward and simple.
	+ Easy: The process was manageable and easy to follow.
	+ Moderate: The process was somewhat challenging, but you managed.
	+ Difficult: The process was difficult and time-consuming.
	+ Very Difficult: The process was extremely challenging and frustrating.
* **How would you rate the communication during your claim?**: Select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Excellent: The communication was prompt and informative.
	+ Good: The communication was satisfactory, but not consistently excellent.
	+ Fair: The communication was occasionally lacking or unclear.
	+ Poor: The communication was generally poor or unresponsive.
* **Was your claim handled in a timely manner?**: Select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Much faster than expected: The process was completed ahead of schedule.
	+ As expected: The process was completed on time.
	+ Slower than expected: The process took longer than expected.
	+ Unacceptably slow: The process took much longer than expected.
* **Were the steps of the process clearly explained to you?**: Select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Yes, completely: The steps were explained clearly and thoroughly.
	+ Partially: The steps were explained somewhat, but not completely.
	+ No, not at all: The steps were not explained clearly.
* **Do you feel the claim settlement was fair?**: Select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ True: The settlement was fair and reasonable.
	+ False: The settlement was not fair or reasonable.
	+ N/A (No settlement yet): The claim is still pending settlement.
* **Did the staff act professionally?**: Select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ True: The staff was professional and courteous.
	+ False: The staff was not professional or courteous.
* **What could we have done better?**: Provide any suggestions or comments on how we could improve the process.
* **How likely are you to recommend us to others?**: Rate your likelihood on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 is not at all likely and 10 is extremely likely.
* **Any additional feedback?**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience with our claims process.
