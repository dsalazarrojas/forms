# Hepatic Health Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge on the topic of Hepatic Health.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the 'Startpage' button on Page 1.
2. Select your answer to question 1 by clicking on 'Yes' or 'No' from the multiple choice options.
3. Answer question 2 by typing your response in the text box.
4. Answer question 3 by clicking on 'Yes' or 'No' from the multiple choice options.
5. Answer question 4 by typing your response in the text box.
6. Click on the 'Endpage' button on Page 4 to complete the form.
