1. Select the category that best describes the school system.
2. Provide a detailed description of the school system.
3. Choose the tool assigned to the school system.
4. Enter a unique identifier for the form (if required).

* **Category** (`category`, select_one): Choose the category that best describes the school system.

* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): Select the tool assigned to the school system.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number): Enter a unique identifier for the form (if required).
