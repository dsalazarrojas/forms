# Agent Exposure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **Agent Exposure Survey** is a standardized reporting form designed to collect information about workplace exposure incidents, helping to identify potential risks and improve safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you are reporting the correct information.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and completely, including all required fields.
3.  Select the correct options for each field based on your experience and knowledge of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name of Affected Individual** (`individual_name`): Enter the name of the person who was exposed to the substance or agent.
*   **Date of Exposure** (`exposure_date`): Enter the date on which the exposure incident occurred.
*   **Time of Exposure** (`exposure_time`): Enter the time when the exposure incident occurred.
*   **Location of Incident** (`incident_location`): Enter the specific location where the exposure incident occurred, such as a laboratory room or warehouse floor.
*   **Type of Exposure - Chemical, Biological, Radiation, Physical** (`exposure_type`): Select the primary hazard category of the exposure incident.
*   **Description of the substance or agent** (`agent_description`): Describe the exact substance or agent that was involved in the exposure incident.
*   **Immediate Symptoms Experienced - Nausea, Rash, Dizziness, Cough** (`immediate_symptoms`): Select all symptoms you experienced immediately after the exposure.
*   **Initial actions taken - e.g., washed area, evacuated** (`initial_actions`): Describe the immediate steps you took after the exposure.
*   **Was medical attention sought?** (`medical_attention`): Select 'Yes - Emergency Room' if you visited an emergency room or 'Yes - Occupational Health' if you visited your employer's health service, or select 'False' if no medical attention was sought.
*   **Name of supervisor notified** (`supervisor_notified`): Enter the name of the supervisor you notified about the incident.
*   **Personal Protective Equipment - PPE - worn at time of incident** (`ppe_worn`): Select all PPE you were wearing at the time of the incident.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or safety officer for assistance.
*   Keep a copy of the form for your records.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about workplace safety, please contact your supervisor or safety officer for guidance.
