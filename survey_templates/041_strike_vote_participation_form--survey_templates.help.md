# Strike Vote Participation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and data on proposed strike actions, affiliations, and other relevant information. It's essential to provide accurate and detailed responses to help us better understand your concerns and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select the proposed strike action that best describes your situation.
2.  Indicate your affiliation (e.g., union or non-union).
3.  Select your vote choice from the provided options.
4.  Share any additional feedback or comments you may have.
5.  Provide your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Proposed Strike Action (proposed_strike_action)**: A text field to describe your proposed strike action. 
*   **Affiliation (affiliation)**: A multiple select field that allows you to indicate if you're associated with a union or not.
*   **Vote (vote)**: A select one field that asks you to choose whether you're in favor of a strike or not. Please select one of the provided options.
*   **Feedback (feedback)**: A text field for you to share any additional comments or thoughts about the proposed strike action.
*   **Additional Feedback (additional_feedback)**: A text field for any extra information you'd like to share.
*   **Name (contact_name)**: A text field for your name, which will be used as a contact point.
*   **Email (contact_email)**: An email field for your email address, which will be used as a contact point.
*   **Phone (contact_phone)**: A text field for your phone number, which will be used as a contact point.

**Tips**

*   Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us.
*   Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation. 

Note: The form fields are not explicitly labeled with "required" or "optional", but the "Vote" field is marked as "required" in the YAML form definition, so it will be treated as a required field in the explanation.
