# Elder Care Technology Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about technology adoption in the elder care industry. Your participation will help us understand the current state of technology adoption and provide valuable insights for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability, providing honest and accurate information.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure you've provided the most relevant information.
3. Take your time to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask for help if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Survey Title (Page 1)
* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): Please provide a brief title for your organization or service.

### Introduction (Page 2)
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, optional): This section is for any additional context or information you'd like to provide about your organization or service.

### Demographic Information (Page 3)
* **Age** (`demographic_age`, number, optional): Please enter your age or the age range of the individuals you serve.

### Gender (Page 4)
* **Gender** (`demographic_gender`, select one, optional): Please select the gender(s) you serve (Yes/No).

### Occupation (Page 5)
* **Occupation** (`demographic_occupation`, select multiple, optional): Please select the occupation(s) related to your organization (Yes/No).

### Technology Use (Page 6)
* **Technology Use** (`technology_use`, text, optional): Describe your current use of technology in your service.

### Technology Type (Page 7)
* **Technology Type** (`technology_type`, select multiple, optional): Please select the type(s) of technology used in your service (Yes/No).

### Benefits (Page 8)
* **Benefits** (`benefit`, text, optional): Describe the benefits of technology adoption in your service.

### Barriers (Page 9)
* **Barriers** (`barrier`, text, optional): Identify any challenges or obstacles to technology adoption in your service.

### Recommendation (Page 10)
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, select one, optional): Do you recommend technology adoption to other elder care services (Yes/No).

### Suggestion (Page 11)
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional): Provide any additional suggestions or comments for future development.

### Comments (Page 12)
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Any additional comments or information about your service.

### Survey Completion (Page 13)
* **Survey Completion** (`survey_completion`, select multiple, optional): Have you already completed a similar survey (Yes/No).

### Contact Information (Page 14)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information for follow-up or further discussion.

### Email (Page 15)
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for future correspondence.

### Email Confirmation (Page 16)
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, email, optional): Confirm your email address.

## Tips
* Please take your time to complete the form carefully.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.
* Your participation is greatly appreciated, and your input will help shape the future of elder care technology.
