# salon_client_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their overall satisfaction and experiences at your salon. It is a crucial tool for understanding client needs and expectations, helping you refine and improve your services.

## How To Complete This Form
Please take a few minutes to answer this form honestly and thoroughly. You will be asked questions on several aspects of your visit, including overall satisfaction and specific services. You can provide any additional comments you would like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, required/optional): This question asks about your overall impression of your visit. Please rate it on a scale of 1 (Least Satisfied) to 10 (Most Satisfied).
* **Services** (`Services`, required/optional): Select all the services you experienced during your visit (e.g., haircut, coloring, etc.). Please choose one or more of the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Comments** (`Comments`, required/optional): Please feel free to share any comments or suggestions you have about your visit. This is your chance to let us know how we can improve.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted for any follow-up discussions or promotions, please provide your phone number. You can leave this field blank if you do not want to be contacted.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, required/optional): If you'd like to receive updates or special offers, please provide your email address. You can leave this field blank if you do not want to be contacted via email.
