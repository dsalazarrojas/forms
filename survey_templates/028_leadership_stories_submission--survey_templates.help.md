# leadership_stories_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect leadership stories from team members to share knowledge, experiences, and successes within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your leadership story by providing a title and description of your story.
2. Select the role of individuals involved in the story.
3. Choose the organization, team, or department that was involved in the story.
4. Describe the outcome or result of the story.
5. Specify the date when the story happened.
6. If necessary, provide additional details about the story, such as where it took place and how long it took.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Story Title** (`story_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title of your leadership story.
* **What is the story about?** (`story_description`, text, optional): Describe the main event, challenge, or situation in your story.
* **What is the role?** (`story_description_2`, select_one, optional): Choose the role of individuals involved in the story (Leader, Manager, Team Member).
* **Who was involved?** (`story_description_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more teams, departments, or organizations that were involved in the story.
* **What was the outcome?** (`story_description_4`, text, optional): Describe the result or conclusion of the story.
* **When did it happen?** (`story_description_5`, date, optional): Specify the date when the story happened.
* **Where did it happen?** (`story_description_7`, text, optional): Provide additional details about the location or context of the story.
* **How long did it take?** (`story_description_9`, number, optional): If applicable, specify the time it took to achieve the outcome.
* **Story Description 10** (`story_description_10`, date, optional): This field is currently not used and seems to be a duplicate. Please do not fill it out.
* **Story Description 11** (`story_description_11`, time, optional): This field is also not used and seems to be a duplicate. Please do not fill it out.
