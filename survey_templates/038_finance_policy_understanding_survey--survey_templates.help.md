# finance_policy_understanding_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The finance policy understanding survey is a tool to collect feedback from employees about their understanding of the company's finance policy. The survey is designed to help identify areas where the policy is unclear or difficult to understand, and to gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and read the instructions carefully.
2. Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select all applicable options for each question.
4. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the text fields.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, `note`, required: false): This page provides a brief introduction to the survey and its purpose.
* **Policy Awareness** (`policy_awareness`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all options that apply to your understanding of the finance policy:
	+ Option 1: I have read and understood the policy.
	+ Option 2: I have not read the policy but am familiar with its general principles.
	+ Option 3: I have not read the policy and am not familiar with its principles.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_page`, `text`, required: false): Share any thoughts or comments about the finance policy. Please provide specific examples or suggestions for improvement.
* **Compliance Teams** (`compliance_teams`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the compliance team that has the most involvement in finance policy discussions:
	+ Option 1: Team A
	+ Option 2: Team B
	+ Option 3: Team C
* **Finance Teams** (`finance_teams`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the finance team that has the most involvement in finance policy discussions:
	+ Option 1: Team A
	+ Option 2: Team B
	+ Option 3: Team C
* **Guideline Improvement** (`guideline_improvement`, `text`, required: false): Share any suggestions you have for improving the finance policy or guidelines.
* **Guideline Clarity** (`guideline_clarity`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all options that apply to the clarity of the finance policy:
	+ Option 1: The policy is clear and easy to understand.
	+ Option 2: The policy is somewhat clear but could be improved.
	+ Option 3: The policy is unclear or difficult to understand.
* **Guideline Clarity Rating** (`guideline_clarity_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the clarity of the finance policy on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1: Very unclear
	+ 2: Somewhat unclear
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat clear
	+ 5: Very clear
