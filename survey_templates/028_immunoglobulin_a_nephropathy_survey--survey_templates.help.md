# Immunoglobulin A Nephropathy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experiences with Immunoglobulin A Nephropathy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the options that best describe your experiences and circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Field 1): This field is used to gather general information about your condition.
* **Have you experienced symptoms such as fatigue or weakness?** (Field 2): Please select the option that best describes your experience with symptoms.
	+ Always: You have experienced symptoms frequently.
	+ Frequently: You have experienced symptoms often.
	+ Rarely: You have experienced symptoms occasionally.
	+ Never: You have not experienced symptoms.
* **Have you experienced any other symptoms?** (Field 3): Please enter any additional symptoms you have experienced.
* **How has your quality of life been affected by your condition?** (Field 4): Please select how your quality of life has been affected.
	+ Not at all: Your quality of life has not been affected.
	+ A little: Your quality of life has been slightly affected.
	+ Moderately: Your quality of life has been moderately affected.
	+ Severely: Your quality of life has been severely affected.
* **How long have you been experiencing symptoms?** (Field 5): Please select the duration of your symptoms.
	+ Less than 1 month: You have been experiencing symptoms for less than a month.
	+ 1-3 months: You have been experiencing symptoms for 1-3 months.
	+ 4-6 months: You have been experiencing symptoms for 4-6 months.
	+ More than 6 months: You have been experiencing symptoms for more than 6 months.
* **What support needs do you have?** (Field 6): Please select the types of support you need.
	+ Medical care: You need medical care.
	+ Emotional support: You need emotional support.
	+ Other: You need other types of support.
* **Do you currently receive any medical care?** (Field 7): Please select if you currently receive medical care.
	+ True: You currently receive medical care.
	+ False: You do not currently receive medical care.
* **Where do you currently receive medical care?** (Field 8): Please select where you currently receive medical care.
	+ Clinic: You currently receive medical care at a clinic.
	+ Specialist: You currently receive medical care from a specialist.
	+ Hospital: You currently receive medical care at a hospital.
* **What kind of support do you currently receive?** (Field 9): Please select the types of support you currently receive.
	+ Family: You receive support from family.
	+ Friend: You receive support from a friend.
	+ Other: You receive support from other sources.
* **How often do you feel supported?** (Field 10): Please select how often you feel supported.
	+ Frequently: You feel supported frequently.
	+ Rarely: You feel supported occasionally.
	+ Never: You do not feel supported.
* **Do you feel you are receiving adequate care?** (Field 11): Please select if you feel you are receiving adequate care.
	+ True: You feel you are receiving adequate care.
	+ False: You do not feel you are receiving adequate care.
* **Why do you feel you are receiving inadequate care?** (Field 12): Please enter any reasons why you feel you are receiving inadequate care.
* **Additional comments** (Field 13): Please enter any additional comments about your experiences.
