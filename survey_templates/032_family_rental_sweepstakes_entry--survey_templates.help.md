# family_rental_sweepstakes_entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in entering a rental sweepstakes or similar promotional contests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, click on the "Submit Entry" button.
2. Fill out the required fields with the requested information.
   - **Entry Type** (`entry_type`): Select the type of entry you are submitting (e.g. Entry, Win, Gift).
   - **Family Members** (`family_members`): Enter the number of family members participating in the sweepstakes.
   - **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
   - **Email** (`email`): Enter your valid email address.
   - **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
   - **Entry Date** (`entry_date`): Enter the date you are submitting your entry.
   - **Time** (`time`): Enter the time you are submitting your entry.
   - **Note** (`note`): Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your entry (optional).
   - **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`): Enter the name of the user assigned to your entry (optional).
   - **Created By** (`created_by`): This field is automatically populated and indicates who created the entry (read-only).
   - **Updated By** (`updated_by`): This field is automatically populated and indicates who last updated the entry (read-only).
   - **Updated At** (`updated_at`): This field is automatically populated with the date the entry was last updated (read-only).
   - **Updated Time** (`updated_time`): This field is automatically populated with the time the entry was last updated (read-only).
3. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to complete your entry.
