# Fundamentals Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess knowledge and comprehension in a educational setting, gathering information on various aspects of knowledge, including knowledge retention, comprehension, and effective assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the text fields with your thoughts and answers to the questions.
2. Each field is a text input, so type your answer or description in the corresponding field.
3. There is no time limit or requirement to answer all fields, so feel free to answer as many or as few as you like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **survey-template** (`survey_template`, text): A placeholder title for your quiz or survey.
* **knowledge-quiz** (`knowledge_quiz`, text): Describe the content of your quiz.
* **participants-knowledge** (`participants_knowledge`, text): Share your knowledge or thoughts on what participants should know.
* **knowledge-retention** (`knowledge_retention`, text): Discuss how participants can retain knowledge.
* **educators-trainers** (`educators_trainers`, text): Share your thoughts on the role of educators or trainers.
* **assess-effectively** (`assess_effectively`, text): Explain how to assess knowledge effectively.
* **knowledge-comprehension** (`knowledge_comprehension`, text): Share your knowledge or thoughts on knowledge comprehension.
* **participants-knowledge-retention** (`participants_knowledge_retention`, text): Discuss participants' knowledge retention.
* **retention-and-comprehension** (`retention_and_comprehension`, text): Share your thoughts on both retention and comprehension.
