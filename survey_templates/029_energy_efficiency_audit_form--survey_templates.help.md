# Energy Efficiency Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about energy efficiency audits. It is intended for facility managers, building owners, or energy auditors to gather data on energy usage and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the energy efficiency audit form's title: "energy_efficiency_audit_form".
2. Enter the date when you submitted the form under "date_submitted".
3. In the "energy_source" field, describe the type of energy being used (e.g. electricity, gas, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Energy Efficiency Audit Form (`energy_efficiency_audit_form`)
* **Energy Efficiency Audit Form** (`energy_efficiency_audit_form`, text, optional): A descriptive label for the form, but not necessary to fill out.

### Date Submitted
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): The date when this form was submitted. Use the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy) to ensure easy sorting and filtering.

### Energy Source
* **Energy Source** (`energy_source`, text, optional): Describe the type of energy being used (e.g. electricity, gas, etc.).

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the "Energy Source" field, as this information will help with data analysis.
* If you need to submit the form for multiple energy sources, consider using separate instances of the form.
* Make sure to review and correct any mistakes before submitting the form.
