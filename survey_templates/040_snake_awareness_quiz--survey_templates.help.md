# Snake Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snake Awareness Quiz is a fun and informative form designed to assess your knowledge and awareness about snakes. It is not a test, but rather an educational tool to help you learn about snakes and stay safe in the wild.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the best answer based on your knowledge and experience.
2. For questions that ask you to select multiple options, choose as many as you think are correct.
3. For questions that ask you to select one option, choose the one that you think is most accurate.
4. Take your time and answer each question honestly and carefully.
5. When you have finished, submit your form and review your answers.
