# Individual Greek Awards 2017 Rubric - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the Greek Awards 2017 rubric. It helps in evaluating and improving the award process by gathering information from award winners and nominees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in the text boxes and select the relevant options for each question. Each question is designed to gather specific feedback on the Greek Awards 2017 rubric.

* **greek_awards_2017_rubric** (`text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide a general description of the rubric.
