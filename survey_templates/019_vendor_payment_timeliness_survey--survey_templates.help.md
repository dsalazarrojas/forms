# Vendor Payment Timeliness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand how timely your payments are and gather insights to improve our payment processes. We value your feedback and appreciate your input.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields below with your answers. You can select from the provided options or provide more details in the comments section if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Payment Timeliness Survey** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your experience with payment timeliness.
* **Payment Speed** (`payment_speed`, `text`, required: false): Choose from: Fast, Medium, Slow, or describe your experience with payment speed.
* **Payment Communication** (`payment_communication`, `text`, required: false): Choose from: Fast, Medium, Slow, or describe your experience with payment communication.
* **Accounts Payable Process Satisfaction** (`accounts_payable_process_satisfaction`, `text`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the accounts payable process.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `text`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with our payment process.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): If you've been assigned a specific tool for payment, list the tool here.
* **Other (if not listed)** (`assigned_tool_other`, `text`, required: false): If the assigned tool is not listed, describe the tool you were assigned.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose from: Supplier, Buyer, Other.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose from: Finance, Procurement, Other.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from: Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from: Check, Wire Transfer, Electronic Funds Transfer, Other.
* **Payment Speed** (`payment_speed`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from: Fast, Medium, Slow.
* **Communication Speed** (`communication_speed`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from: Fast, Medium, Slow.
* **Payment Communication Speed** (`payment_communication_speed`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from: Fast, Medium, Slow.

Please fill out the form according to your experience and provide any additional feedback in the comments section. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve our payment processes.
