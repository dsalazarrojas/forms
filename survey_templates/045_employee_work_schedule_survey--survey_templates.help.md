# employee_work_schedule_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee work schedule survey is a tool designed to collect feedback from employees on their work schedule, allowing management to understand their opinions and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose your answer to the question: Do you currently have a shift schedule? (Page 1)
   - Select "Yes" if you currently have a shift schedule.
   - Select "No" if you do not currently have a shift schedule.
2. Select your shift preferences (Page 2, required). Choose all that apply:
   - Option 1
   - Option 2
3. Rate your satisfaction with the current schedule (Page 3, required). Choose one of the two options:
   - Option 1
   - Option 2
4. Enter your date of birth (Page 4, required). This is the date on which you were born.
5. Enter your email address (Page 5, required). This is your work email address.
6. Enter your phone number (Page 6, required). This is your work phone number.
7. (Optional) Add any additional comments or feedback (Page 7).
8. Finally, select the action to submit your feedback (Page 8, required). Choose one of the two options:
   - Option 1
   - Option 2

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_work_schedule_survey_form** (`select_one`, required/optional): This field asks whether you currently have a shift schedule.
* **shift_preferences** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose all shift preferences that apply to you.
* **schedule_satisfaction** (`select_one`, required): Rate your level of satisfaction with the current schedule.
* **employee_input** (`date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **employee_email** (`email`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **employee_phone** (`text`, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **note** (`note`, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about your schedule.
* **submit** (`select_one`, required): Choose the action to submit your feedback.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your feedback is accurate and complete.
* If you have any suggestions or concerns, please include them in the "note" field.
* If you have any issues with your phone number, please use the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
