# Vehicle Idling Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Idling Assessment Form is designed to gather information about vehicle idling habits. This form is used to collect data on how often and for how long vehicles are left idling. This information is crucial for optimizing fuel consumption and reducing emissions. Please ensure that the form is filled out correctly to provide accurate data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the frequency with which you typically leave your vehicle idling.
2. Choose the duration for which you usually leave your vehicle idling.
3. Optionally, provide more information by selecting from the available idling habits options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Idling Habits
• **vehicle idling habits** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to choose which idling habits you have. Select all that apply:
	+ idling habits

### idling duration
• **idling duration** (`time`, required: false): Enter the duration for which you usually leave your vehicle idling. This field is a time-based input field. You can enter a time in 24-hour format (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).

### idling frequency
• **idling frequency** (`select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to choose how often you typically leave your vehicle idling. Select one of the following options:
	+ daily
	+ weekly
	+ monthly

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Select all idling habits that apply to you.
* Choose the correct idling frequency based on how often you leave your vehicle idling.
