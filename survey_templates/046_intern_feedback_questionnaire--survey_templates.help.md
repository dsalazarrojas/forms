# Intern Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Intern Feedback Questionnaire is designed to collect insights from interns about their experiences with their organization's internship program. It aims to help organizations improve their programs and enhance intern satisfaction. This form is intended to gather honest and constructive feedback from interns, providing valuable information to organizations to make data-driven decisions and create a better work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read through the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2.  Select your feedback responses from the available options.
3.  Fill out all required fields, if applicable.
4.  Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Templates** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): This field allows you to input any additional relevant information about the internship program you are providing feedback about.
* **Intern Feedback Questionnaire** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Please briefly describe the purpose of the feedback questionnaire. This will help ensure that your feedback is accurate and relevant.
* **The Intern Feedback Questionnaire** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): This is a description of what the form is for, so you can ignore it. You can skip this field.
* **Intern Satisfaction** (`4`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Please select the level of satisfaction you experience from this internship program:
	+ Internally satisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Very satisfied
* **Intern Satisfaction** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all the reasons that apply for your level of satisfaction:
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Very satisfied
* **Intern Program Improvement** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Please select all areas that you think could be improved in the internship program:
	+ Provide more opportunities for professional development
	+ Improve communication between interns and supervisors
	+ Increase intern satisfaction with compensation and benefits
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Intern Feedback Questionnaire** (`7`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Please select the level of satisfaction you experience from this feedback questionnaire:
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Very satisfied

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your feedback to help us improve our internship program.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask your supervisor or someone from the HR department for clarification.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your input.</thinking>
