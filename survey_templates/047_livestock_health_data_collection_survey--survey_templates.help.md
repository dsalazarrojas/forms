# Livestock Health Data Collection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect livestock health data from farms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Start** (`id: 1`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether the farm is at the start of the survey.
* **farm name** (`id: 2`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your farm.
* **farm location** (`id: 3`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your farm.
* **farm size** (`id: 4`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the size of your farm.
* **herd type** (`id: 5`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of animal on your farm.
* **health status** (`id: 6`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the overall health status of your farm.
* **disease management** (`id: 7`, text, required: false): Enter the farm's disease management practices.
* **treatment administered** (`id: 8`, text, required: false): Enter any treatment administered to the animals.
* **vaccination history** (`id: 9`, text, required: false): Enter the vaccination history of the animals.
* **veterinary care** (`id: 10`, text, required: false): Enter the veterinary care practices on your farm.
* **biosecurity** (`id: 11`, text, required: false): Select the farm's biosecurity practices.
* **animal species** (`id: 12`, select_one, required: false): Choose the species of animal on your farm.
* **animal breed** (`id: 13`, select_one, required: false): Choose the breed of animal on your farm.
* **age range** (`id: 14`, number, required: false): Enter the age range of the animals.
* **health status animal** (`id: 15`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the health status of the animals.
* **mortality rate** (`id: 16`, number, required: false): Enter the mortality rate of the farm.
* **farm operated by** (`id: 17`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of farm operator.
* **farm operated since** (`id: 18`, number, required: false): Enter how long your farm has been in operation.
* **farm operated by type** (`id: 19`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of farm operator.
* **contact person** (`id: 20`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person for your farm.
* **contact phone** (`id: 21`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether a contact phone number is available.
* **contact email** (`id: 22`, text, required: false): Enter the contact email of your farm.
* **farm location coordinates** (`id: 23`, text, required: false): Enter the location coordinates of your farm.
* **farm location description** (`id: 24`, text, required: false): Enter a description of your farm's location.
