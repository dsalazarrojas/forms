# Evaluation Distribution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to provide their ratings and feedback on various aspects of an event or program. The goal is to collect data on participant satisfaction and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and contact number in the `form_name` and `contact_number` fields respectively.
2. For each rating field, select the relevant option from the provided choices.
   - For `event_effectiveness` and participant rating fields, select the number that best describes your rating.
   - For `participant_satisfaction_rating1` to `participant_satisfaction_rating20`, select "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied" accordingly.
3. Make sure to fill all the fields, as all are optional except for `form_name` and `contact_number`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Form Name** (`form_name`, text, required): Enter your name in this field. This will be used to identify your responses.
  - Example: John Doe

- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number. This will help us reach you in case of any questions.
  - Example: 123-456-7890

- **Event Effectiveness** (`event_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the event, with options 1-4.
  - Options: 1 (Very Effective), 2, 3, 4 (Not Effective)

- **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your participation.
  - This is an open field for your comments.

- **Satisfaction Ratings** (`participant_satisfaction_rating1` to `participant_satisfaction_rating20`, select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction with different aspects of the event, with options Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
  - Example: Select Satisfied or Dissatisfied for each rating.
