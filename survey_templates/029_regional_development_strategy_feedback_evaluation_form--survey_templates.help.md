# Regional Development Strategy Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the Regional Development Strategy, which is a plan to improve the region's infrastructure, resources, and services to support economic growth and development. This feedback will be used to evaluate the effectiveness of the strategy and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form by selecting the most relevant options for each field. You can select all that apply.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Evaluation Outcome" field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Regional Development Strategy Feedback
* **Regional Development Strategy Feedback** (`text`, required, false): This field is for providing general feedback on the regional development strategy. Please describe your thoughts and suggestions for improvement.

### Page 2: Development Initiatives
* **Development Team** (`select_multiple`, required, false): Choose all the development teams involved in the regional development strategy.
* **External Partners** (`select_multiple`, required, false): Choose all the external partners involved in the regional development strategy.
* **Local Government** (`select_multiple`, required, false): Choose all the local governments involved in the regional development strategy.
* **Other** (`select_multiple`, required, false): Choose any other stakeholders involved in the regional development strategy.

### Page 3: Evaluation Methodology
* **Evaluation Methodology** (`select_one`, required, false): Choose the evaluation methodology used in the regional development strategy.
	+ Quantitative: This approach focuses on numerical data and analysis.
	+ Qualitative: This approach focuses on non-numerical data and analysis.
	+ Mixed: This approach combines both quantitative and qualitative methods.

### Page 4: Stakeholders Involved
* **Development Team** (`select_multiple`, required, false): Choose all the development teams involved in the regional development strategy.
* **External Partners** (`select_multiple`, required, false): Choose all the external partners involved in the regional development strategy.
* **Local Government** (`select_multiple`, required, false): Choose all the local governments involved in the regional development strategy.
* **Other** (`select_multiple`, required, false): Choose any other stakeholders involved in the regional development strategy.

### Page 5: Evaluation Process
* **Evaluation Process** (`select_multiple`, required, false): Choose all the evaluation processes used in the regional development strategy.
* **Sequential** (`select_multiple`, required, false): This approach is a linear, step-by-step process.
* **Iterative** (`select_multiple`, required, false): This approach involves repeating the evaluation process.
* **Continuous Improvement** (`select_multiple`, required, false): This approach focuses on ongoing evaluation and improvement.

### Page 6: Evaluation Scope
* **Evaluation Scope** (`date`, required, false): If applicable, enter the date when the evaluation scope was last reviewed or updated.

### Page 7: Evaluation Frequency
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`time`, required, false): If applicable, enter the frequency at which the evaluation is conducted (e.g., annually, bi-annually).

### Page 8: Evaluation Outcome
* **Evaluation Outcome** (`note`, required, false): If applicable, enter any additional comments, observations, or outcomes from the regional development strategy feedback.
