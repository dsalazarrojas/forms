# market-research-checklist-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about market research initiatives, providing a comprehensive checklist for researchers to gather and document relevant details about their projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as you see fit. Each field is labeled with its corresponding name and description. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief introduction to your market research project.
* **Market Research Objectives** (`2`, `text`, optional): Define the specific goals and objectives of your market research project.
* **Target Audience** (`3`, `text`, optional): Identify the specific audience or group your market research project is intended to serve.
* **Research Questions** (`4`, `text`, optional): Determine the specific research questions or topics that will be explored in your project.
* **Market Research Methods** (`5`, `text`, optional): Outline the specific methods and approaches you'll use to collect and analyze data.
* **Data Sources** (`6`, `text`, optional): Identify the specific data sources or materials used in your project.
* **Results Analysis** (`7`, `text`, optional): Explain how you'll analyze and interpret the results of your project.
* **Recommendations** (`8`, `text`, optional): Outline any recommendations or conclusions drawn from your project.
* **Budget and Duration** (`9`, `text`, optional): Provide an overview of your project's budget and duration.
* **Project Status** (`10`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more of the following: Active, Inactive. This field indicates the project's status.
* **Research Type** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following: Qualitative, Quantitative, Mixed. This field indicates the type of research approach used in your project.
* **Research Question Type** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following: Open-Ended, Closed-Ended. This field indicates the type of research question type.
* **Data Collection Method** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following: Surveys, Focus Groups, Interviews, Observations, Online Communities, Other. This field indicates the method used to collect data.
* **Data Analysis Method** (`14`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following: Descriptive, Inferential. This field indicates the method used to analyze data.
* **Sampling Method** (`15`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following: Simple Random Sample, Stratified Random Sample, Systematic Sample, Cluster Sample, Multistage Sample. This field indicates the sampling method used.
* **Sampling Proportion** (`16`, `number`, optional): Enter the proportion of the sample size.
* **Sampling Coverage** (`17`, `number`, optional): Enter the coverage of the sample size.
* **Target Sample Size** (`18`, `number`, optional): Enter the desired sample size.
* **Sample Size Basis** (`19`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following: Population, Sample, List, Other. This field indicates the basis of the sample size.
* **Sample Duration** (`20`, `date`, optional): Enter the duration of the sample collection.
* **Sample Frequency** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following: Fixed Interval, Random Interval, Occasional, Continuous. This field indicates the frequency of the sample collection.
* **Data Quality Control** (`22`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following: True, False. This field indicates whether data quality control is applied.
* **Data Storage** (`23`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following: Cloud Storage, Local Storage, Hybrid. This field indicates the data storage method.
* **Data Security** (`24`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following: High, Medium, Low. This field indicates the data security level.
* **Data Retention** (`25`, `number`, optional): Enter the data retention period.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields and provide accurate information.
* Use the form as a checklist to ensure you haven't missed any important details.
* Feel free to adapt and modify the form to suit your specific project needs.
* Review and revise your answers as necessary to ensure they accurately reflect your project's goals and objectives.
