# Evening Of Elegance Guest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and information from guests who attended the "Evening Of Elegance" event. It will help us understand your experience and opinions about the event, which will be used to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. You can skip questions that are not relevant to you or your experience with the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gala Name** (`gala_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the gala you attended.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on the invitation or registration.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you attended the event.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Please rate your overall experience with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Please share any comments or feedback about the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): Please rate your satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Please select any preferences that were provided for the event.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please share any feedback about the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of event you attended (Gala, Fundraiser, or Charity Event).
* **Venue** (`venue`, text, optional): Please enter the venue where the event took place.
* **Event Manager** (`event_manager`, select_one, optional): Please select the event manager responsible for the event.
* **Team Leads** (`team_leads`, select_multiple, optional): Please select any team leads who were present at the event.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Please select any team members who were present at the event.
* **Comments 2** (`comments`, text, optional): Please share any additional comments about the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please enter the time you attended the event.
* **Rating 2** (`rating2`, number, optional): Please rate your experience with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments 2** (`comments2`, text, optional): Please share any additional comments about the event.
* **Email 2** (`email2`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Rating 3** (`rating3`, number, optional): Please rate your experience with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments 3** (`comments3`, text, optional): Please share any additional comments about the event.
* **Email 3** (`email3`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone 3** (`phone3`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Comments 4** (`comments4`, text, optional): Please share any final comments about the event.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your answers, as this will help us improve the event and provide better experiences for future attendees.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback and insights about the "Evening Of Elegance" event!
