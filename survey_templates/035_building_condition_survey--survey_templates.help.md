# Building Condition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Condition Survey form is designed to gather detailed information about a building's condition, providing an accurate and comprehensive assessment of its state.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the Surveyor Name field.
2. Enter the name of the organization or company conducting the survey in the Organization field.
3. Provide your contact email address in the Email Address field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the Phone Number field.
5. Select the date of the survey in the Survey Date field.
6. Enter the name of the building being surveyed in the Building Name field.
7. Enter the full address of the building in the Building Address field.
8. Classify the property type in the Property Type field.
9. Enter the year the building was constructed in the Year Constructed field (if applicable).
10. Enter the building's size in square footage in the Building Size field.
11. Enter the size of the property in the Lot Size field (if applicable).
12. Select the number of stories in the Number of Stories field.
13. Choose the primary exterior material of the building in the Exterior Material field.
14. Select the type of roof the building has in the Roof Type field.
15. Choose the type of foundation the building has in the Foundation Type field.
16. Rate the site condition in the Site Condition field.
17. Rate the drainage in the Drainage field.
18. Rate the parking facilities in the Parking Facilities field.
19. Rate the safety features of the building in the Safety Features field.
20. Rate the building's code compliance in the Code Compliance field.
21. Select the occupancy status of the building in the Occupancy Status field.
22. Describe any visible defects in the Visible Defects field.
23. Enter any maintenance needs in the Maintenance Needs field.
24. Select any environmental concerns in the Environmental Concerns field.
25. Rate the overall condition of the building in the Overall Condition field.
26. Provide a value assessment of the property in the Value Assessment field (if applicable).
27. Enter any key recommendations for the building's maintenance or improvement in the Recommendations field (if applicable).
28. Certify that the survey is accurate in the Surveyor Certification field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Surveyor Name**: Enter your name as the surveyor conducting the assessment.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization or company conducting the survey.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Survey Date**: Select the date you conducted the survey.
* **Building Name**: Enter the name of the building being surveyed.
* **Building Address**: Enter the full address of the building.
* **Property Type**: Choose the classification of the property (e.g., Single Family Residential, Commercial Office, etc.).
* **Year Constructed**: Enter the year the building was constructed (if known).
* **Building Size**: Enter the building's size in square footage.
* **Lot Size**: Enter the size of the property (if applicable).
* **Number of Stories**: Select the total number of stories in the building.
* **Exterior Material**: Choose the primary material used in the building's exterior.
* **Roof Type**: Select the type of roof the building has.
* **Foundation Type**: Choose the type of foundation the building has.
* **Site Condition**: Rate the condition of the building site (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
* **Drainage**: Rate the drainage in the building (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
* **Parking Facilities**: Rate the condition of parking facilities in the building (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Not Available).
* **Safety Features**: Rate the safety features of the building (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Inadequate).
* **Code Compliance**: Rate the building's code compliance (e.g., Fully Compliant, Mostly Compliant, Partially Compliant, Non-Compliant, Unknown).
* **Occupancy Status**: Select the current status of the building (e.g., Fully Occupied, Partially Occupied, Vacant, Under Renovation, Condemned).
* **Visible Defects**: Describe any visible defects or issues with the building.
* **Maintenance Needs**: Enter any maintenance needs or recommendations for the building.
* **Environmental Concerns**: Select any environmental concerns or issues with the building (e.g., None Observed, Asbestos, Lead Paint, Mold, Hazardous Materials, Soil Contamination, Water Damage, Pest Infestation).
* **Overall Condition**: Rate the overall condition of the building (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
* **Value Assessment**: Provide a value assessment of the property (e.g., Above Market, At Market, Below Market, Significantly Below Market).
* **Recommendations**: Enter any key recommendations for the building's maintenance or improvement.
* **Surveyor Certification**: Certify that the survey is accurate.
