# User Satisfaction Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The User Satisfaction Analysis Report Form is designed to collect user feedback on their satisfaction analysis, gathering information on contact details, preferences, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `user_satisfaction_analysis_form` field.
2. Provide your contact information in the `contact_info` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
4. Enter your phone number in the `phone_number` field.
5. Select a date in the `date` field.
6. Select a time in the `time` field.
7. Enter a note in the `note` field.
8. Choose one option from the `select_one` field.
9. Select multiple options from the `select_multiple` field.
10. Enter a decimal value in the `decimal` field.
11. Enter an integer value in the `integer` field.
12. Enter a date and time in the `date_time` field.
13. Enter text in the `text_area` field.
14. Choose multiple options from the `checkbox` field.
15. Choose multiple options from the `checkbox_2` field.
16. Enter text in the `submit` field.
17. Enter text in the `cancel` field.
18. Choose one option from the `select_one_2` field.
19. Select multiple options from the `select_multiple_2` field.
20. Enter a decimal value in the `decimal_2` field.
21. Enter an integer value in the `integer_2` field.
22. Enter text in the `date_time_2` field.
23. Enter text in the `text_area_2` field.
24. Choose multiple options from the `checkbox_2_2` field.
25. Enter text in the `submit_2` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Satisfaction Analysis** (`user_satisfaction_analysis_form`, text, required): Enter your thoughts on your satisfaction analysis.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your contact information.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select a date.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select a time.
- **Note** (`note`, note, required): Enter a note.
- **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Choose one option from the list.
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options from the list.
- **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, required): Enter a decimal value.
- **Integer** (`integer`, number, required): Enter an integer value.
- **Date and Time** (`date_time`, text, required): Enter a date and time.
- **Text Area** (`text_area`, text, required): Enter text in this field.
- **Checkbox** (`checkbox`, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple options from the list.
- **Another Checkbox** (`checkbox_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple options from the list.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Enter text in this field.
- **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, required): Enter text in this field.
- **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, select_one, required): Choose one option from the list.
- **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options from the list.
- **Decimal 2** (`decimal_2`, number, required): Enter a decimal value.
- **Integer 2** (`integer_2`, number, required): Enter an integer value.
- **Date Time 2** (`date_time_2`, text, required): Enter a date and time.
- **Text Area 2** (`text_area_2`, text, required): Enter text in this field.
- **Checkbox 2 2** (`checkbox_2_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple options from the list.
- **Submit 2** (`submit_2`, text, required): Enter text in this field.
