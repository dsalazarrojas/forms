# Student Engagement Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their engagement and experience in a course. It is meant to be completed after the course has concluded and is used to gather insights for future course development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the purpose and scope of the form.
2. Answer each question in as much detail as you like, using the available space.
3. Select your preferred option for each multi-choice question.
4. Submit your responses when you have completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Header** (form_header, note, optional): This section provides a space for you to write any general comments or thoughts about the course.
- **Introduction** (introduction, text, optional): This section is a space for you to briefly introduce your feedback. Please keep it concise and focused on the course.
- **What do you think about this course?** (question_1, select_one, optional): Select one of the available options that best describes your thoughts about the course.
- **What do you think about this course?** (question_2, select_one, optional): Another question to gather more specific feedback about the course. Choose the option that best represents your thoughts.
- **Question 3** (question_3, text, optional): This section is for you to provide any additional feedback or comments about the course. Be as specific as possible.
- **Question 4** (question_4, number, optional): This is a numerical question, please answer with a whole number.
- **Question 5** (question_5, number, optional): Another numerical question to gather more specific feedback.
- **Question 6** (question_6, date, optional): Please enter the date you first started attending the course.
