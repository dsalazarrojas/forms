# Movies Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Movies Survey form is designed to collect information about movies. It helps us understand your preferences and opinions about movies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. For select_multiple fields (Starring, Favorite Actor), select as many options as you like.
3. For date fields (Movie Release Date), select a valid date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Movie Title (`movie_title`, `text`, **optional**):** Enter the title of the movie you're thinking about.
* **Genre (`genre`, `select_one`, **optional**):** Choose the genre of the movie.
* **Starring (`starring`, `select_multiple`, **optional**):** Select the main actors you like.
* **Director (`director`, `text`, **optional**):** Enter the director's name.
* **Movie Release Date (`movie_release_date`, `date`, **optional**):** Select a date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` for the movie's release date.
* **Movie Rating (`movie_rating`, `select_one`, **optional**):** Choose the rating of the movie (Good or Fair).
* **Rating Scale (`rating_scale`, `number`, **optional**):** Enter a number to rate the movie's quality.
* **Rating Comment (`rating_comment`, `text`, **optional**):** Write your comment about the movie's quality.
* **Favorite (`favorite`, `select_one`, **optional**):** Choose if you like the movie.
* **Favorite Actor (`favorite_actor`, `select_multiple`, **optional**):** Select the actors you like in the movie.
* **Favorite Movie (`favorite_movie`, `text`, **optional**):** Enter the title of your favorite movie.
