# Leather Sofa Care Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leather Sofa Care Survey is a form designed to gather information about users' habits and experiences with their leather sofas. This form will help researchers understand how users take care of their leather sofas, including the frequency of maintenance, type of conditioner used, and color of the sofa.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the color of your leather sofa from the options provided.
2. Choose how often you maintain your leather sofa from the options provided.
3. Select the type of sofa you own from the options provided.
4. Indicate how often you clean your leather sofa from the options provided.
5. Check the boxes to indicate whether you use conditioner on your leather sofa, how often, and in what circumstances (daily, weekly, or sometimes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leather Sofa** (`leather_sofa_info`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the color of your leather sofa from the options Black, Brown, or White.
* **Maintenance** (`maintenance_info`, select_one, required/optional): Select how often you maintain your leather sofa from the options Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Sofa Type** (`sofa_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of sofa you own from the options Chesterfield, L-shaped, or Recliner.
* **Cleaning Frequency** (`cleaning_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Select how often you clean your leather sofa from the options Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Conditioner Use** (`conditioner_use`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether you use conditioner on your leather sofa, and if so, how often and in what circumstances.
