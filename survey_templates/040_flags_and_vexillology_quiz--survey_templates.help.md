# Flags And Vexillology Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flags And Vexillology Quiz form is designed to collect information about flags and vexillology. The form will ask you to select some options, provide answers, and submit your comments, email, phone number, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Select "Class Flags" from the dropdown menu. You can choose one or multiple options.
- Choose a single "Vexillology Flag" from the dropdown menu.
- Enter a single "Flag ID" from the dropdown menu.
- For "Vexillology Quiz Answers", enter a numerical value. This field is not required.
- Click "Submit" to submit your answers.
- Optionally, you can add comments or notes in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Class Flags** (`class_flags`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or multiple class flags that interest you. Examples: Flags, Clubs, Trivia Night.
* **Vexillology Flags** (`vexillology_flags`, select_one, required/optional): Choose a single vexillology flag that you know or like.
* **Flag ID** (`flag_id`, select_one, required/optional): Select a flag ID that you would like to use for your flags and vexillology quiz.
* **Vexillology Quiz Answers** (`vexillology_quiz_answers`, number, required/optional): Provide a numerical answer.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click to submit your answers.
* **Comments** (`flags_and_vexillology_quiz_form_comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any comments you want to add.
* **Email** (`flags_and_vexillology_quiz_form_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`flags_and_vexillology_quiz_form_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`flags_and_vexillology_quiz_form_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes.
