# Human Resources Accounting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employee data from the Human Resources department for accounting and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your Employee Name.
2. Enter your Job Title.
3. Select your Department (Yes or No).
4. Enter your Email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Optional): Please enter your full name as you wish to be listed in the HR database.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Optional): Enter your current job title or position in the organization.
* **Department** (`department`, Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate whether you are part of the department or not (Yes or No).
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your official email address used for communication within the organization.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the HR department for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only, and all data collected will be kept confidential within the organization.
