# Housing Accessibility Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Housing Accessibility Satisfaction Survey is a tool to measure the satisfaction of users regarding the accessibility of their living space.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your response to the first question: "first_page".
2. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Select your responses to the second page questions, "barriers" and "overall_satisfaction".
5. Select your response to the third page question, "first_page_3".
6. Select your response to the second question on the third page, "first_page_3".
7. Select your response to the last question, "last_page".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_page**: A multiple-choice question asking if your living space is accessible. 
* **first_name**: A field to input your first name. Please enter your full name or your preferred name to be used in this survey.
* **email**: A field to input your email address. This will help us contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates on our survey results. Please ensure your email address is accurate.
* **barriers**: A multiple-choice question asking if there are any barriers to accessibility in your living space. Select all that apply.
* **overall_satisfaction**: A scale from 0 to 10 to rate your overall satisfaction with the accessibility of your living space.
* **first_page_3**: A multiple-choice question asking if you have any other concerns or issues. 
* **first_page_3**: A multiple-choice question asking if you have any other concerns or issues. 
* **last_page**: A multiple-choice question asking if you have any other concerns or issues. 

Note: Please ensure all questions on the same page are answered before proceeding to the next page.
