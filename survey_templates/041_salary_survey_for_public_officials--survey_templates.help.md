# Salary Survey For Public Officials - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a collection of questions designed to gather your feedback and opinions as a public official. Your responses will help inform policy and resource allocation decisions to better support your role and the public service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question as honestly and accurately as possible. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you. Please note that this is a survey, and your responses will be kept confidential.
