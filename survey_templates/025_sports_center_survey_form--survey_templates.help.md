# Sports Center Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about visitors to the Sports Center, gathering data on their age groups, frequency of visits, and favorite sports activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your age group from the options provided:
	* Choose the correct age range that best describes you.
2. Indicate your frequency of visit to the Sports Center:
	* Select the option that best reflects how often you visit the Sports Center.
3. Select all the sports activities you enjoy the most:
	* Choose as many sports activities as you like from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Group (18-24)** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required)
	+ This field asks you to select your current age group. Please choose the age range that you belong to.
* **Frequency of Visit (Once a week)** (`frequency_of_visit`, `select_one`, required)
	+ This field asks you to indicate how often you visit the Sports Center. Select the option that best reflects your regularity of visits.
* **Favorite Sports Activity (Basketball, Football, Tennis, Volleyball, Golf, Hockey)** (`sports_activity`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ This field allows you to select all the sports activities you enjoy the most. Choose as many options as you like, but please be sure to select at least one.

## Tips
- Be sure to complete all required fields.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.
- This form is for your convenience and will help us understand your preferences and interests.
