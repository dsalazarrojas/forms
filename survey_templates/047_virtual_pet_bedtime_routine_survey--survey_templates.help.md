# Virtual Pet Bedtime Routine Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Pet Bedtime Routine Survey is designed to collect data on a virtual pet's bedtime routine. It consists of various fields that allow users to input different aspects of the routine, such as activities, challenges, and satisfaction levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Survey Templates" field and select a relevant category for your virtual pet's routine.
2.  Enter a brief description of your virtual pet's routine in the "Description" field.
3.  Choose the tools assigned to your virtual pet's routine from the "Assigned Tool" field.
4.  Input the file name for the output file in the "Output File" field.
5.  Input any additional form IDs related to your virtual pet's routine in the "Form IDs" field.
6.  Answer the "Routines" field with your virtual pet's routine activities.
7.  Select the challenges your virtual pet faces in the "Challenges" field.
8.  Choose the level of satisfaction with your virtual pet's routine in the "Satisfaction" field.
9.  Provide any additional information in the "While" field.
10.  Select the platform where your virtual pet's data is hosted in the "In Jotform" field.
11.  Input any data collection organization information in the "Organizing Data Collection" field.
12.  Enter any relevant information related to communities, educators, and creators in the "Communities, Educators, and Creators" field.
13.  Provide any additional information on understanding routines in the "Understand Routines" field.
14.  Select any additional challenges your virtual pet may face in the "Challenges 2" field.
15.  Provide any additional information on helping your virtual pet in the "Helping" field.
16.  Enter any information on creators in the "Creators" field.
17.  Input any additional information on routines in the "Routines 2" and "Routines 3" fields.
18.  Choose the level of satisfaction with your virtual pet's routine in the "Satisfaction 2" and "Satisfaction 3" fields.
