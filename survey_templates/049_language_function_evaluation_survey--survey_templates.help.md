# Language Function Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language Function Evaluation Survey is designed to assess language functions, including language proficiency, communication skills, and other related aspects. This survey is used to gather information about a language user's abilities, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. For questions with multiple options, select the option that best describes your language abilities or experiences.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully and then proceed to the next section.

### 2. Demographics
* **Demographics** (`demographics`, `select_multiple`, not required): Please select all that apply to describe your demographic information. This will help us better understand your background and experiences.

### 3. Language Proficiency
* **Language Proficiency** (`language_proficiency`, `number`, not required): This question assesses your current language proficiency level. Please select the number that best reflects your language abilities.

### 4. Language Ability
* **Language Ability** (`language_ability`, `number`, not required): This question evaluates your language abilities. Please select the number that best represents your language abilities.

### 5. Communication Skills
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, `select_one`, not required): This question assesses your communication skills. Please select the option that best describes your communication skills.

### 6. Reading Comprehension
* **Reading Comprehension** (`reading_comprehension`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question evaluates your reading comprehension skills. Please select all that apply.

### 7. Listening Comprehension
* **Listening Comprehension** (`listening_comprehension`, `select_one`, not required): This question assesses your listening comprehension skills. Please select the option that best describes your listening abilities.

### 8. Speaking Comprehension
* **Speaking Comprehension** (`speaking_comprehension`, `select_one`, not required): This question evaluates your speaking comprehension skills. Please select the option that best describes your speaking abilities.

### 9. Writing Comprehension
* **Writing Comprehension** (`writing_comprehension`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question assesses your writing comprehension skills. Please select all that apply.

### 10. Total Score
* **Total Score** (`total_score`, `number`, not required): This question will display a calculated score based on your previous answers. Please review your total score for accuracy.

### 11. Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your language functions. This will help us understand your strengths and areas for improvement.
