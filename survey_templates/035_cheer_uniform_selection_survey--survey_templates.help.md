# Cheer Uniform Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cheer Uniform Selection Survey is designed to gather feedback from members on their preferences for the team uniform. This survey will help the team create a uniform that is both functional and appealing to the members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Member Full Name in the "Member Full Name" field.
2. Select your team level from the "Team Level" dropdown menu.
3. Choose your preferred uniform design option from the "Primary Design Choice" dropdown menu.
4. Select your preferred color palette from the "Preferred Accent Color Palette" dropdown menu.
5. Provide your thoughts on the proposed fabric and material in the "Thoughts on proposed fabric and material" field.
6. Rate your overall style preference from the "Overall Style Preference" dropdown menu.
7. Indicate which factor is more important to you between comfort and style in the "Which is more important to you" dropdown menu.
8. If you have any specific fit or comfort concerns, provide them in the "Any specific fit or comfort concerns" field.
9. Add any additional uniform suggestions in the "Any other suggestions for the final design" field.
10. Finally, confirm that you have reviewed all options and cast your vote in the "I have reviewed all options and cast my vote" dropdown menu, and select the date you are casting your vote in the "Date of Vote" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Member Full Name** (required): Enter your full name as a member of the Cheer team.
*   **Team Level** (required): Choose your team level from Junior Varsity, Varsity, Competition Elite, or All-Stars.
*   **Primary Design Choice** (required): Select Option A - Traditional Classic, Option B - Modern Sleek, or Option C - Bold and Metallic as your preferred uniform design.
*   **Preferred Accent Color Palette** (required): Choose from Royal Blue and Silver, Navy Blue and Gold, or Electric Blue and White as your preferred color palette.
*   **Thoughts on proposed fabric and material** (optional): Provide your thoughts on the proposed fabric and material, focusing on breathability, comfort, and durability.
*   **Overall Style Preference** (required): Rate your overall style preference from Traditional / Heritage, Modern / High-Tech, or Flashy / Sparkly.
*   **Which is more important to you** (required): Indicate whether comfort for stunting, visual impact on stage, or an equal balance between comfort and style is more important to you.
*   **Any specific fit or comfort concerns** (optional): Provide any specific fit or comfort concerns, for example, length of skirt or sleeve tightness.
*   **Any other suggestions for the final design** (optional): Add any additional uniform suggestions you may have.
*   **I have reviewed all options and cast my vote** (required): Confirm that you have reviewed all options and cast your vote.
*   **Date of Vote** (required): Select the date you are casting your vote.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be completed to ensure the survey is valid.
