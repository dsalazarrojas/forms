# Wellness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help patients share their thoughts and feelings about their well-being, including mental health, physical health, substance abuse, and social support. The information collected will help healthcare providers understand patients' experiences and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
3. Be honest with yourself and do not worry about judgment or criticism.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (page 1)**: This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you prefer.
	+ **Label:** patient_info
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Demographics (page 2)**: This section is for sharing some basic information about yourself, such as your age, occupation, or living situation.
	+ **Label:** demographics
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Mental Health (page 3)**: This section is for sharing any thoughts or feelings about your mental health, such as stress, anxiety, or mood.
	+ **Label:** mental_health
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Substance Abuse (page 4)**: This section is for sharing any concerns or experiences about substance abuse, such as substance use or addiction.
	+ **Label:** substance_abuse
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Physical Health (page 5)**: This section is for sharing any concerns or experiences about your physical health, such as diet, exercise, or chronic conditions.
	+ **Label:** physical_health
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Wellness (page 6)**: This section is for sharing any activities or routines that you find helpful for your well-being, such as yoga, meditation, or hobbies.
	+ **Label:** wellness
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Lifestyle (page 7)**: This section is for sharing any aspects of your lifestyle that may impact your well-being, such as work-life balance, relationships, or finances.
	+ **Label:** lifestyle
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Social Support (page 8)**: This section is for sharing any social support systems or networks that you rely on, such as family, friends, or support groups.
	+ **Label:** social_support
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
* You can share as much or as little as you are comfortable with.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please feel free to contact the form owner.
