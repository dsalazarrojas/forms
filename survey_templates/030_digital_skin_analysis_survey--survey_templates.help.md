# Digital Skin Analysis Survey Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to collect data about a client's digital skin condition, goals, and current state. The form is intended to provide a comprehensive analysis of the skin's condition and help identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Survey Name**, a descriptive title for the analysis session.
2.  Indicate whether the client has experienced **Skin Type**, either "Yes" or "No".
3.  Select the areas of the skin that are affected, choosing from the available **Skin Areas** options.
4.  Rate your **Pain Level**, using a number from 1 to 10.
5.  Identify the **Pain Locations** where you are experiencing pain, selecting "Yes" or "No" for each area.
6.  Indicate your **Skincare Goals**, choosing from the available options.
7.  Specify which **Skin Products** you are currently using, selecting "Yes" or "No" for each product.
8.  Enter any **Follow-ups**, any additional comments or information about your skin condition.
9.  Enter your **Client Name** and other basic contact information.
10.  Enter your **Salon Address** and other business details.
11.  Enter your **Email** and **Phone** number.
12.  Specify the **Date** and **Time** of your analysis session.
13.  Add any additional **Notes** about your skin condition and the analysis process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Survey Name**: Enter a unique name for this analysis session. This will help in identifying the client and the session's purpose.
*   **Skin Type**: Indicate whether you have sensitive skin, allergies, or other skin types.
*   **Skin Area**: Select all areas where you are experiencing skin issues.
*   **Pain Level**: Rate your pain level from 1 to 10, where 1 is the least painful and 10 is the most painful.
*   **Pain Locations**: Identify the specific areas experiencing pain, selecting "Yes" or "No" for each location.
*   **Skincare Goals**: Specify your skincare goals, choosing from available options.
*   **Skin Products**: Indicate which skincare products you are currently using, selecting "Yes" or "No" for each product.
*   **Follow-ups**: Enter any additional comments or information about your skin condition.
*   **Client Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
*   **Salon Address**: Enter your salon's business address.
*   **Salon Contact**: Enter a contact person's name and phone number.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Date**: Specify the date of your analysis session.
*   **Time**: Specify the time of your analysis session.
*   **Notes**: Add any additional comments or observations about your skin condition and the analysis process.
