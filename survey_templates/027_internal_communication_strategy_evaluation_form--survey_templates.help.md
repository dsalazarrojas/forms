# Internal Communication Strategy Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees evaluate the internal communication strategy of the organization. It assesses the effectiveness and satisfaction of updates, update frequency, and other relevant aspects of the internal communication. The goal is to gather feedback to identify areas of improvement for the communication strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred update frequency from the list provided in the "Update Frequency" section.
2. Choose the most effective channel(s) for receiving updates from the list provided in the "Update Channels" section.
3. Rate the effectiveness and satisfaction of the updates and the internal communication strategy on a scale of 0 to 10.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" section.
5. Provide your email address and phone number, which will help us contact you for further discussions.
6. Share your thoughts on the internal communication strategy by providing any additional feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Update Frequency** (<label>Update Frequency, <type>select_multiple, required>false): Choose how often you receive updates from the organization, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Never.
* **Update Channels** (<label>Update Channels, <type>select_one, required>false): Select the most effective channel(s) for receiving updates, such as Email, Intranet, Mobile App, or Meetings.
* **Update Effectiveness** (<label>Update Effectiveness, <type>select_one, required>false): Rate the effectiveness of updates on a scale of Very Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Very Effective, or Not at All Effective.
* **Satisfaction with Updates** (<label>Satisfaction with Updates, <type>number, required>false): Rate your satisfaction with updates on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Satisfaction with the Internal Communication Strategy** (<label>Satisfaction with the Internal Communication Strategy, <type>number, required>false): Rate your satisfaction with the internal communication strategy on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Additional Comments** (<label>Additional Comments, <type>note, required>false): Share any additional thoughts or comments about the internal communication strategy.
* **Email** (<label>Email, <type>email, required>false): Provide your email address to help us contact you for further discussions.
* **Phone Number** (<label>Phone Number, <type>text, required>false): Provide your phone number to help us contact you for further discussions.
* **Feedback** (<label>Feedback, <type>text, required>false): Share any additional feedback or thoughts about the internal communication strategy.
* **Employee ID** (<label>Employee ID, <type>text, required>false): Provide your employee ID to help us identify you in our records.
