# Domestic Violence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect information about domestic violence experiences and provide resources for support. Please answer the questions honestly to help us better understand the situation and provide guidance on available support systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review the questions and select the options that apply to you.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Is there anything you would like to add?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Domestic Violence Survey** (`intro_page`, text, required: false): This is the introduction to the form.
* **Has your family experienced any domestic violence?** (`family_violence`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes" if you or your family has experienced domestic violence, and "No" otherwise.
* **Have you ever experienced any violence in your home?** (`student_violence`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes" if you have ever experienced violence in your home, and "No" otherwise.
* **Who would you like to talk to if you needed help or support?** (`support`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the options that apply to you, such as a parent/teacher, school counselor, another family member, friend, someone at work, or someone outside of school.
* **When would you like to start the conversation?** (`date`, date, required: false): If you have a specific date in mind to start the conversation, please enter it here.
* **What time would you prefer to have the conversation?** (`time`, time, required: false): If you have a specific time in mind to start the conversation, please enter it here.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address if you would like us to follow up with you.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you directly.
* **Is there anything you would like to add?** (`note`, note, required: false): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information or comments you would like to share with us.
