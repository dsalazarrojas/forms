# Donor Transparency Expectation Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donor Transparency Expectation Poll is designed to gather information on what donors expect from their donations. This information will help organizations tailor their communication and reporting to meet those expectations, building stronger relationships with their donors.

## How To Complete This Form

* To complete this form, navigate to the first page titled "Intro". This page is a note-type field that serves as an introduction to the form. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and objectives of this form.
* After reading the intro page, click on the "Next" button to move on to the next page.
* On the second page, "What do donors expect?", select one of the options from the dropdown menu to indicate what type of expectations donors typically have (Donor Expectations, Financial Expectations, Impact Expectations, Relationship Expectations, or Other).
* Continue to the next page, "Financial Expectations", and select all the options that apply to what donors expect from their financial information (Transparency of Finances, Financial Reporting, Budgeting and Spending, Audits and Accountability, or Other).
* On the next page, "Impact Expectations", select one option from the dropdown menu to indicate what type of impact donors expect from their donations (Measurable Impact, Tangible Outputs, Intangible Outputs, Results Reporting, or Other).
* Proceed to the next page, "Relationship Expectations", and select all the options that apply to what donors expect from their relationship with the organization (Regular Communication, Timely Updates, Responsive Feedback, Personalized Engagement, or Other).
* On the next page, "Other Expectations", select all the options that apply to other expectations donors may have (Other (please specify), or Specify other expectations).
* On the final page, "Comments", enter any additional comments or notes you have about your donors' expectations.
* Finally, on the "Follow-up" page, select whether you plan to follow up with your donors on their expectations (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro (Note)**: This is an introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and objectives.
* **What do donors expect? (Select One)**: Please select one of the options from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand the general type of expectation donors have.
* **Financial Expectations (Select Multiple)**: Select all the options that apply. Donors may expect transparency in finances, regular reporting, budgeting, and audits and accountability.
* **Impact Expectations (Select One)**: Please select one option from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand the type of impact donors expect.
* **Relationship Expectations (Select Multiple)**: Select all the options that apply. Donors may expect regular communication, timely updates, responsive feedback, personalized engagement, or other.
* **Other Expectations (Select Multiple)**: Select all the options that apply. This allows you to specify other expectations that are not listed in the previous options.
* **Comments (Note)**: Please enter any additional comments or notes you have about your donors' expectations.
* **Follow-up (Select One)**: Indicate whether you plan to follow up with your donors on their expectations.
