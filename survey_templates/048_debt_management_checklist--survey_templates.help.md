# Debt Management Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about debt assessment, management, and repayment. It's meant to help users understand their debt situation and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select all that apply from the options on each page to answer the questions accordingly.
2. Fill in the text field under "Debt management next steps" with any further actions or suggestions for managing your debt.
3. Complete all the questions on each page, as they will be asked in order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Assess debt** (`debt_assessment`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have assessed your debt.
  - **Assess how often** (`debt_frequency`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate how often you assess your debt.
  - **Amount** (`debt_amount`): Enter the total amount of your debt.
  - **Debt type** (`debt_type`): Select the type of debt you have (Credit card, Personal loan, Student loan, etc.).
  - **Priority** (`debt_priority`): Choose "High", "Medium", or "Low" to indicate the urgency of your debt.
  - **How often pay urgent debts** (`debt_frequency_urgent`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate how often you pay urgent debts.
  - **How often pay non-urgent debts** (`debt_frequency_non_urgent`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate how often you pay non-urgent debts.
  - **Debt payment method** (`debt_payment_method`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you've used debt payment methods.
  - **Payoff period** (`debt_payment_frequency`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you've made a payoff period.
  - **Payment status** (`debt_payment_status`): Select "Paid", "Not paid", or "Partially paid" to indicate your payment status.
  - **Repayment plan** (`debt_repayment_plan`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you've made a repayment plan.
  - **Debt counseling** (`debt_counseling`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you've sought debt counseling.
  - **Debt management tools** (`debt_management_tools`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate debt management tools you've used.
  - **Debt reduction methods** (`debt_reduction_methods`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate debt reduction methods you've used.
  - **Debt reduction effectiveness** (`debt_reduction_effectiveness`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate the effectiveness of debt reduction methods.
  - **Debt reduction satisfaction** (`debt_reduction_satisfaction`): Choose "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied" to indicate your satisfaction with debt reduction methods.
  - **Debt management goals** (`debt_management_goals`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your debt management goals.
  - **Debt management obstacles** (`debt_management_obstacles`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate debt management obstacles you've faced.
  - **Debt management strategies** (`debt_management_strategies`): Choose "Good", "Fair", or "Bad" to indicate the effectiveness of your debt management strategies.
  - **Debt management tools used** (`debt_management_tools_used`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate debt management tools you've used.
  - **Repayment status** (`debt_repayment_status`): Choose "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your repayment status.
  - **Debt reduction methods used** (`debt_reduction_methods_used`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate debt reduction methods you've used.
  - **Debt reduction methods satisfied** (`debt_reduction_methods_satisfied`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your satisfaction with debt reduction methods.
  - **Debt management outcomes** (`debt_management_outcomes`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate debt management outcomes.
  - **Debt management next steps** (`debt_management_next_steps`): Enter any further actions or suggestions for managing your debt.

## Tips
- Please answer all the questions as accurately as possible.
- If unsure about a question, try to answer based on your best judgment.
- Take your time to complete the form to ensure accurate information.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
