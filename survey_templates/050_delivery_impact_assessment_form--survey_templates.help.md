# Delivery Impact Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the impact of a delivery, providing valuable insights into its effectiveness and areas for improvement. It helps authorized personnel evaluate the quality and environmental, community, and operational impacts of a service or product delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and concisely, based on your actual experience and understanding of the delivery.
2. Select all applicable options for each field, if multiple choices are available.
3. Provide detailed and specific information in text fields, such as `service_area` and `time_of_day`.
4. Use the date field to record the date of the delivery.
5. Use the phone field to record any relevant phone numbers associated with the delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **delivery_impact_assessment** (Select Multiple): Choose all applicable options to describe the overall impact of the delivery. This field is used to capture a brief summary of the delivery's impact.
	+ 'Yes': The delivery had a positive impact on the client or customer.
	+ 'No': The delivery had no significant impact or negative impact on the client or customer.
* **service_quality** (Number): Enter a number value to rate the service quality of the delivery. Please use a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **environmental_impact** (Select One): Choose the most accurate answer to describe the environmental impact of the delivery.
	+ 'Yes': The delivery had an environmental impact (e.g., energy consumption, resource usage).
	+ 'No': The delivery did not have an environmental impact.
* **community_impact** (Text): Describe the community impact of the delivery. Please provide specific examples of how the delivery affected the community.
* **incident_reporting** (Select One): Choose the most accurate answer to describe incident reporting during the delivery.
	+ 'Yes': Incidents were reported during the delivery.
	+ 'No': No incidents were reported during the delivery.
* **service_area** (Text): Describe the service area where the delivery was made.
* **delivery_date** (Date): Enter the date of the delivery.
* **time_of_day** (Time): Enter the time of the delivery.
* **email** (Email): Enter the email associated with the delivery (if applicable).
* **phone** (Text): Enter any relevant phone numbers associated with the delivery (if applicable).
