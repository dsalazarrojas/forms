# Romantic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Romantic Survey is designed to gather information about romantic relationships, likely for research, marketing, or other purposes. Please answer the questions based on your personal experiences and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully to understand each question.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Select the most relevant answer for each select questions.
4. For the date question, choose the date of your last romantic date or the start date of your current relationship.
5. In the comments section, you can add any additional information you'd like to share about your romantic relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_keeps_romance_alive** (text): This is a question about what keeps romance alive in your relationship. Be honest, and answer based on your personal experiences.
* **communication** (Select One):
	+ choice1: Active listening
	+ choice2: Regular surprises
	+ choice3: Deep conversations
* **love_language** (Select Multiple): Choose all the ways love is expressed in your relationship.
	+ choice1: Words of affection
	+ choice2: Quality time
	+ choice3: Gifts
* **shared_interests** (Time): Please select a time when your shared interests started, such as when you met, started dating, or began sharing common hobbies.
* **date_night** (Date): Choose the date of your last romantic date or the start date of your current relationship.
* **favorite_activity** (Select One):
	+ choice1: Cooking together
	+ choice2: Traveling
	+ choice3: Watching movies
* **relationship_status** (Select One):
	+ choice1: Single
	+ choice2: In a relationship
	+ choice3: Married
* **comments** (Text): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your romantic relationship.
