# Newspaper Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help you complete the Newspaper Survey form in a way that provides valuable and useful feedback for our newspaper.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Enter your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Answer each question in the corresponding text fields (e.g. "What do you think of our newspaper?", "Do you have any feedback for our newspaper?", etc.).
3. If you want to select options from a dropdown menu, choose one or multiple answers that apply to you.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
5. Finally, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **What do you think of our newspaper?**: Answer this question with a brief text that describes your opinion about our newspaper.
* **Do you have any feedback for our newspaper?**: Share any comments, suggestions, or ideas you have about how we can improve.
* **What do you like about our newspaper?**: List the things you enjoy or appreciate about our newspaper.
* **What do you dislike about our newspaper?**: List the things you don't like or dislike about our newspaper.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Time**: Select your time of day from the dropdown menu.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Which of the above?**: Choose one of the options from the dropdown menu to express your opinion.
* **Which of the following?**: Choose multiple options from the dropdown menu to express your opinion.
* **Date**: Select the date of your feedback.
* **Select an option**: Choose one of the options from the dropdown menu.
* **Select multiple options**: Choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
* **Time 2**: Select your time of day from the dropdown menu.
* **Select5**: Choose one of the options from the dropdown menu.
* **Select6**: Choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
* **Date 2**: Select the date of your feedback.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your answers.
* Keep your answers concise and to the point.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas, feel free to share them.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact us at [your contact information].
