# post_conference_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The post-conference survey is designed to gather feedback from conference attendees on their experience at the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply work through each section, providing the requested information for each field. If a field is not relevant or applicable, you can leave it blank or mark it as "N/A" if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### conference_details (text, required: false)
* This field is for you to provide any details about the conference you attended, such as its title, location, or the date it took place.

### session_attendance (select_multiple, required: false)
* This field allows you to indicate whether you attended any sessions or not. Select all that apply from the options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### speaker_rating (number, required: false)
* This field is for you to rate the speakers' performance on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### feedback (text, required: false)
* This field is for providing any additional feedback you might have about the conference.

### suggestions (text, required: false)
* This field is for suggesting any ideas or improvements you have for future conferences.

### email (email, required: false)
* This is your email address, which will help us contact you if we need more information.

### phone (text, required: false)
* This is your phone number, which will help us contact you if we need more information.

### date (date, required: false)
* This field is for selecting the date of the conference you attended.

### time (time, required: false)
* This field is for selecting the time of the conference you attended.

## Tips
* If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* You can provide your email address or phone number to help us contact you for any follow-up questions.
* Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve the conference; be as descriptive as possible.
