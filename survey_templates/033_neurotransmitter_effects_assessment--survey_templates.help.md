# Neurotransmitter Effects Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Neurotransmitter Effects Assessment form used to collect data on the level of impact of a specific neurotransmitter on an individual's mood, stress, symptoms, anxiety, fatigue, and sleep quality.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select all that apply from the options below.
- For numerical fields (stress score), please enter a number.
- You can skip any fields that are not applicable to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mood Scale**: Select all the days where you felt better or worse about your mood.
  + Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Stress Score**: Enter a number from 0 to 10 to rate your stress level.
  + Type: number
* **Symptom Intensity**: Select whether you've experienced the following symptoms.
  + Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Anxiety Level**: Select the level of anxiety you've experienced.
  + Options: High, Low
* **Fatigue Level**: Select the level of fatigue you've experienced.
  + Options: High, Low
* **Sleep Quality**: Select your sleep quality.
  + Options: Good, Poor

## Tips
- Please answer honestly based on your current situation.
- If you're unsure about any option, choose the one that best applies to you.
- You can always seek help if you're struggling with any of these areas.
