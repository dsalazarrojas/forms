# Academic Creativity In Teaching Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to understand your experiences and perspectives on creative teaching practices, aiming to gather insights on how teachers incorporate creativity in their lessons, the methods they use, and their challenges and successes in this area.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to begin.
2. The form is divided into three main sections: "Personal Information", "Teaching Experience", and "Creative Teaching Practices".
3. Fill in the required fields for each section, providing accurate and detailed information where possible.
4. Select the relevant options for each field, making sure to consider the available choices carefully.
5. In some fields, you can describe your experiences and thoughts more extensively using text boxes (e.g., "Success Story" and "Additional Comments").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full name (id: 2)
* Label: Full name (optional)
* Type: Text
* Explanation: If you wish, please provide your name for the purpose of this survey. However, this is optional and will not affect your responses to the other questions.
### 2. Email address (id: 3)
* Label: Email address
* Type: Email
* Explanation: We need your email address to get in touch with you regarding this survey and for potential future communication. This will not be shared or used for any other purpose.
### 3. Name of your institution (id: 4)
* Label: Name of your institution
* Type: Text
* Explanation: Please provide the name of your institution where you teach.
### 4. Your current teaching role (id: 5)
* Label: Your current teaching role
* Type: Select One
* Explanation: Choose the teaching role you currently hold, such as K-12 Teacher, Adjunct Instructor, or Full Professor.
### 5. Primary subject area (id: 6)
* Label: Primary subject area
* Type: Select One
* Explanation: Please select your primary subject area, which can be Science, Technology, Engineering, Math (STEM), Humanities, Social Sciences, Arts and Design, Business and Economics, Health Sciences, or Other.
### 6. Years of teaching experience (id: 7)
* Label: Years of teaching experience
* Type: Select One

### 7. -- Creative Teaching Practices -- (id: 8)
* Label: -- Creative Teaching Practices --

* Explanation: This section is optional and allows you to share any initial thoughts or reflections on creative teaching practices.
### 8. Which creative teaching methods do you currently use? (id: 9)
* Label: Which creative teaching methods do you currently use
* Type: Select Multiple
* Explanation: Select all the creative teaching methods you currently use in your lessons, such as Project-based learning, Flipped classroom, Gamification, or others.

* Type: Select Multiple
* Explanation: Select all the barriers that prevent you from using more creative teaching approaches, such as Lack of time, Limited institutional support, or Assessment constraints.

### 16. Any additional comments or suggestions (id: 17)
* Label: Any additional comments or suggestions

# Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your experiences and thoughts.
* Remember that this survey is designed to help improve teaching practices, so your input is valuable and appreciated.
