# Social Work Report Writing Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to help social workers gauge their knowledge and skills in writing reports, which is a crucial task in the social work profession.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this quiz, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your understanding of report writing principles.
2. Select the options that best describe your knowledge and skills in each area.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** 
*   *Introduction*  (`title`, `note`, required: false): This section is for you to briefly introduce yourself and the context of the report you are writing. Please write a short statement that explains the purpose and scope of the report.

### 2. **Scenario Assessment** 
*   *Scenario Assessment*  (`scenario_assessment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the options that you think are relevant to the scenario described. This section tests your ability to assess scenarios and identify key points.

### 3. **Reporting Skills** 
*   *Reporting Skills*  (`reporting_skills`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option that best describes your reporting skills. This section tests your knowledge of reporting techniques and styles.

### 4. **Reporting Confidence** 
*   *Reporting Confidence*  (`reporting_confidence`, `number`, required: false): Rate your confidence level on a scale from 0 to 10, with 0 indicating no confidence and 10 indicating absolute confidence. This section tests your self-assessment and confidence in your reporting skills.

### 5. **Scenario Skills** 
*   *Scenario Skills*  (`scenario_skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the options that you think are relevant to your scenario skills. This section tests your ability to apply scenarios in your reporting.

### 6. **Reporting Knowledge** 
*   *Reporting Knowledge*  (`reporting_knowledge`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that you think are relevant to your reporting knowledge. This section tests your knowledge of reporting principles and methods.

### 7. **Conclusion** 
*   *Conclusion*  (`conclusion`, `note`, required: false): Write a brief conclusion that summarizes your report. Please keep it short and concise.

### 8. **Comments** 
*   *Comments*  (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about the quiz.

### 9. **Quiz Completed** 
*   *Quiz Completed*  (`quiz_completed`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option that best describes your completion status. This section is for you to indicate whether you have completed the quiz or not.

### 10. **Quiz Not Completed** 
*   *Quiz Not Completed*  (`quiz_not_completed`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option that best describes your status if you have not completed the quiz. This section is not to be taken lightly and should only be selected if you are unable to complete the quiz due to valid reasons.

### 11. **Score** 
*   *Score*  (`score`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option that best describes your expected score. This section tests your confidence in your reporting skills and self-assessment.

### 12. **Submit** 
*   *Submit*  (`submit_button`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option that best describes your intent to submit your work. This section is the final step in the quiz process.
