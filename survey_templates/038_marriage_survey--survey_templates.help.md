# Marriage Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a person's relationship, including their age, marriage details, and personal experiences. The data collected from this form will be used to analyze marriage trends and provide insights for further research.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill in your age in the "How Old Are You" field.
3. Enter your partner's age in the "Partner Age" field.
4. Select the date of your marriage in the "Marriage Date" field using the calendar picker.
5. Answer the question "How Long Married" to share the duration of your marriage.
6. Check the options under "Children" to indicate whether you have any children.
7. Choose the reason for your marriage from the "Reason For Marriage" options.
8. Add any additional comments or thoughts in the "Happy Marriage" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How Old Are You** (Age, Number, Required): Enter your age to provide context for your marriage experiences.
* **Partner Age** (Age, Number, Required): Enter your partner's age to provide context for your marriage experiences.
* **Marriage Date** (Date, Datepicker, Required): Select the date you got married, using the calendar picker.
* **How Long Married** (Years, Number, Required): Share the duration of your marriage to provide insight into your experiences.
* **Children** (Children, Select Multiple, Required): Check the options to indicate whether you have any children.
* **Reason For Marriage** (Select One, Required): Choose the reason for your marriage from the options.
* **Happy Marriage** (Note, Optional): Add any comments or thoughts about your marriage experiences.
