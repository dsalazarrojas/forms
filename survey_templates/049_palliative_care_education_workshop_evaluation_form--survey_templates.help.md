# palliative_care_education_workshop_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from participants of a palliative care education workshop. It aims to evaluate the effectiveness of the workshop, assess the presenter's knowledge, and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Use the available options to select your response, or type a free-form answer in the "Suggestions" field.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a red star *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. workshop_experience
* **How Was The Workshop** *: This question assesses your overall experience with the workshop. Please provide a brief description of your experience, including any highlights or challenges you faced.

### 2. topic_knowledge
* **What Did You Learn** *: This question asks about your knowledge gain from the workshop. Select all the topics that you felt you learned about during the workshop.

### 3. presentation_quality
* **How Well Was The Presentation** *: This question rates the quality of the workshop presentation. Use the available numbers to rate your satisfaction with the presentation.

### 4. content_relevance
* **Relevance To Work** *: This question asks about the relevance of the workshop content to your work. Select the option that best describes the level of relevance you experienced.

### 5. presenter_knowledge
* **Presenter's Knowledge** *: This question assesses the presenter's expertise on the topic. Select all the options that describe your perception of the presenter's knowledge.

### 6. workshop_satisfaction
* **How Satisfied Are You** *: This question rates your overall satisfaction with the workshop. Use the available numbers to rate your satisfaction.

### 7. future_workshops
* **Would You Attend Again** *: This question asks if you would attend another workshop with the same presenter. Select the option that best describes your intention.

### 8. suggestions
* **Suggestions** *: This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the workshop.

### 9. email
* **Email** *: This field is for your email address. Please provide a valid email address so that we can contact you if necessary.
