# DriveThru Service Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction ratings from customers about their drive-thru experience at a restaurant. The form is intended to help management understand customer perceptions of speed, accuracy, staff friendliness, and overall satisfaction, as well as other aspects of the service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, follow the on-screen instructions to navigate through each page.
2. For each page, select the response that best matches your experience.
3. If a page requires you to select from a set of options, choose the one that reflects your answer.
4. If you have comments or observations about your visit, you can add them in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Once you have completed all pages, submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How long did you wait in the drive-thru** (`id: 1`): This question asks about the duration of your wait in the drive-thru queue. Select the number of minutes you waited from 1 to 30 or more.
* **How accurate were the orders** (`id: 2`): This question assesses the accuracy of the orders you received. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Very accurate: You felt the orders were correct and accurate.
	+ Somewhat accurate: The orders were mostly correct, but there were a few minor mistakes.
	+ Not very accurate: There were noticeable mistakes in the orders.
	+ Not at all accurate: The orders were significantly incorrect.
* **How would you rate your experience with staff** (`id: 3`): This question measures your overall satisfaction with the staff's behavior and attitude. Select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Excellent: The staff were exceptional, friendly, and helpful.
	+ Good: The staff were friendly and helpful, but not exceptional.
	+ Fair: The staff were somewhat helpful, but not very friendly.
	+ Poor: The staff were not very helpful or friendly.
* **How clean was the restaurant area** (`id: 4`): This question evaluates the cleanliness of the restaurant area where you picked up your order. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Very clean: The area was clean and well-maintained.
	+ Somewhat clean: The area was somewhat clean, but could be improved.
	+ Neutral: The area was neither clean nor dirty.
	+ Somewhat dirty: The area was somewhat dirty, but not severely so.
	+ Very dirty: The area was extremely dirty and unhygienic.
* **How friendly were the staff** (`id: 5`): This question gauges the friendliness and hospitality of the staff. Select the option that best describes your experience.
