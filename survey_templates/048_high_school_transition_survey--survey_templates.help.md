# High School Transition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from students, parents, and school administration on the transition process of high school students. Your input is valuable for improvement and will help us understand various aspects of the high school transition process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review each page carefully to ensure you have answered all required questions.
3. Use the provided options for select questions, and type your answers in the text boxes for free-text questions.
4. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Background**: This page is a note field where you can provide any additional context or background information about your school experience.
* **Student Info**: Please enter your email address as requested for this field.
* **Support Systems**: This question asks about the support systems you have experienced or received during your high school transition process. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Resources**: This question asks about the resources you have used during your high school transition process. Select the resource type that best fits your experience.
* **Student Experience**: Please rate your experience during the high school transition process. Select from the options: Good, Average, or Fair.
* **Parent Experience**: This question is the same as Student Experience. Rate your parent's experience during the high school transition process.
* **School Administration**: This question also asks about the school administration's experience during your high school transition process. Rate from the options: Good, Average, or Fair.
* **Student Recommendations**: Please provide any recommendations or suggestions you have for improvement for the high school transition process.
* **Parent Recommendations**: This question is similar to Student Recommendations. Please provide any recommendations or suggestions your parent has for improvement for the high school transition process.
* **Comments**: This is an open-text field where you can add any additional comments or feedback you have about the high school transition process.
