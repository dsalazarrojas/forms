# Derby Event Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Derby Event Interest Survey is designed to collect information about your interests and preferences for a derby event. This survey will help us understand what topics you are interested in and what time you would prefer to attend.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Event Interest Survey Form**: Please fill out this field with any additional comments or questions about the event.
2. **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you are interested in.
3. **Preferred Time**: Select the preferred time you would like to attend the event.
4. **Event Topics**: Choose the topics you are interested in by selecting multiple options from the dropdown menu. This will help us tailor the event to your interests.
5. **Event Topics 1-8**: These fields are for additional, specific comments about each topic you selected. Please use each field to provide more details about the topic that corresponds to the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Interest Survey Form** (`event_interest_survey_form`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for any additional comments or questions about the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the event you are interested in.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the preferred time you would like to attend the event.
* **Event Topics** (`event_topics`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the topics you are interested in by selecting multiple options from the dropdown menu.
* **Event Topics 1-8** (`event_topics_1`, `text`, required/optional): Text fields for additional, specific comments about each topic you selected, corresponding to the labeled topic.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when selecting event topics and providing additional comments.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please use the event interest survey form field to communicate with us.
* We appreciate your input and will use it to tailor the event to your interests.
