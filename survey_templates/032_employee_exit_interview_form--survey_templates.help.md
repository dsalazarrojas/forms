# Employee Exit Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your experiences and perceptions during your time with the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form by providing your honest feedback about your job, team, and company.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your answers, providing specific examples and examples of events or incidents that illustrate your points.
3. You can use the "Comments" field to add any additional information that you think is relevant to your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Your name as an employee.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): The title of your job role within the company.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, required): A brief explanation of why you are leaving the company.
* **How Much Do You Like Our Company?** (`how_much_do_you_like_our_company`, select_one, required): Rate your level of satisfaction with the company culture.
* **How Much Support Did You Receive?** (`how_much_support`, number, optional): A rating of the support you received from your colleagues, manager, or team.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, optional): Name of your immediate supervisor.
* **Team** (`team`, text, optional): Briefly describe your team dynamics, including your relationships and interactions with team members.
* **Date of Last Day** (`date_of_last_workday`, date, required): The date of your last day of work with the company.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Any additional thoughts, suggestions, or concerns you have about your experience with the company.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Specify how you would like to be contacted after you have left the company. Select all that apply.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, text, optional): Name of your supervisor.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Name of the department you belonged to.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, text, optional): Name of your reporting manager.
* **Job Performance** (`job_performance`, text, optional): Briefly describe your job performance and accomplishments during your time with the company.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional): Rate the level of effective communication you experienced during your time with the company.
* **Company Culture** (`company_culture`, text, optional): Describe the company culture and how it impacted your experience.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Name of your manager.
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, text, optional): Your employee number with the company.

## Tips

* Please be honest and open in your feedback, as it will help us understand your experience and improve employee experience and retention in the future.
* If you need help or guidance, do not hesitate to contact your HR representative.
* This form is confidential and will be used only for internal purposes.
