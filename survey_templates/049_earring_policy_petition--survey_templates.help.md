# earring_policy_petition - Help Guide
## Purpose
The earring policy petition form is designed to gather feedback and support for an earring policy. This form is meant to be a general-purpose tool for expressing opinions and thoughts on the policy, and it is not intended for any specific organization or entity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User email** in the first field. This is the email address you can be reached at if your submission is successful.
2. Enter your **Name** in the second field. This is a basic identification field.
3. In the **Message** field, type a brief description of your support or opinion on the policy.
4. Select all that apply from the **Are you supporting this policy?** field to indicate your level of support.
5. If you are representing an **Organization**, enter the name of the organization in the corresponding field.
6. Click the **Submit** button to send your feedback and support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User email**: This is the email address that you can be reached at if your submission is successful.
* **Name**: This is a basic identification field to help us understand who is submitting feedback.
* **Message**: This field is where you can type a brief description of your support or opinion on the policy.
* **Are you supporting this policy?**: Select all that apply to indicate your level of support. Options include:
	+ Option 1: I fully support this policy.
	+ Option 2: I partially support this policy.
	+ Option 3: I do not support this policy.
* **Organization**: If you are representing an organization, enter the name of the organization in this field.
* **Submit**: This button will send your feedback and support to the designated recipient.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter a valid email address in the **User email** field.
* Keep your **Message** brief and to the point.
* Select only one or more options from the **Are you supporting this policy?** field that accurately reflect your support level.
* If you are representing an organization, make sure to enter the correct name in the **Organization** field.
