# Consumer Spending Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This Consumer Spending Survey is designed to gather information about your personal spending habits, financial situation, and attitudes towards money. The information collected will help businesses and organizations understand consumer behavior and preferences.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Review the form and make sure you understand each question.
2. Select the correct answers for each question based on your personal experiences and attitudes.
3. Complete all required fields (marked as `required: true`).
4. You can leave optional fields (marked as `required: false`) blank if you prefer not to provide that information.
5. Once you've completed the form, review it again for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Age Group** (`respondent_age`): Select your age range to help us understand your demographic profile.
* **Annual Household Income** (`household_income`): Choose your annual household income bracket to help us understand your financial situation.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`): Select your current employment situation to help us understand your work-life balance.
* **Average Monthly Spending** (`monthly_spending`): Choose your average monthly spending to help us understand your financial management.
* **Top Spending Categories** (`spending_categories`): Select all categories where you spend most of your money.
* **Weekly Grocery Spending** (`grocery_spending`): Enter the average amount you spend on groceries per week.
* **Dining Out Frequency** (`dining_out_frequency`): Select how often you eat at restaurants.
* **Monthly Online Shopping Spending** (`online_shopping`): Choose your average monthly online purchases.
* **Monthly Entertainment Budget** (`entertainment_budget`): Select your spending on entertainment activities like movies, streaming, etc.
* **Savings Rate** (`savings_rate`): Choose the percentage of your income you save monthly.
* **Credit Card Usage** (`credit_card_usage`): Select how you use credit cards.
* **Current Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`): Select your primary financial goals, if any.
* **Economic Outlook** (`economic_outlook`): Select how confident you are about your financial future.
* **Recent Spending Changes** (`spending_changes`): Select how your spending has changed in the past year.
* **Brand Loyalty** (`brand_loyalty`): Select how important brand name is in your purchases.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address for prize drawing notification, if you want to participate.
