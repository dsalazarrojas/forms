# self_reflection_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self-reflection survey is designed to gather information about an individual's self-reflection habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the self-reflection survey, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the self-improvement goals that are relevant to you.
3. Choose how often you practice self-reflection.
4. Enter the time you typically spend on self-reflection.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes about your self-reflection habits.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Optionally, provide any additional comments about your self-reflection habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Self-Reflection Survey** (`intro_page`, text, optional): This is the introduction page where you can provide a brief description of the survey.
* **Self-Improvement Goals** (`self_improvement_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more self-improvement goals that you're interested in, such as Career Development, Financial Management, Health and Wellness, or Personal Growth.
* **How Often** (`self_reflection_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you practice self-reflection, such as Never, Occasionally, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly.
* **Self-Reflection Time** (`self_reflection_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you typically spend on self-reflection.
* **Self-Reflection Notes** (`self_reflection_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your self-reflection habits.
* **Email** (`self_reflection_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`self_reflection_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`self_reflection_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about your self-reflection habits.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If a question is not applicable, feel free to skip it.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us.
