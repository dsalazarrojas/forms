# Cheesecake Preferences Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your demographics, cheesecake preferences, and opinions to help us better understand your needs and tailor our products accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct answers for each question based on your personal preferences and experiences.
2. Answer each question carefully, as the data collected will be used to improve our products and services.
3. If you're unsure about a particular question, please select the option that best represents your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Section 1: About You

* **What is your age group** (`consumer_age_range`): This question helps us understand your age group, which may influence your cheesecake preferences.
* **How often do you eat cheesecake** (`consumption_frequency_choice`): This question helps us understand how often you enjoy cheesecake, which informs our product offerings and inventory.
* **Would you try a savory cheesecake** (`willingness_try_savory_cake`): This question explores your openness to trying new and unique cheesecake flavors.
* **Fair price for a premium slice of cheesecake** (`premium_slice_price_opinion`): This question helps us understand your perceived value for our premium products.
* **Do you buy frozen or fresh cheesecake more often** (`buy_frozen_vs_fresh_pref`): This question assists us in understanding your purchasing habits and preferences.
* **Survey Date** (`preference_survey_date`): This is an optional question for tracking the completion date and time of the survey.
* **Receive new flavor alerts via email** (`newsletter_opt_in_cake`): This question gives you the option to receive exclusive updates on new flavor releases and promotions.

### Section 2: Preferences

* **What texture do you prefer** (`preferred_cake_texture`): This question helps us understand your preferred texture, which informs our product development.
* **Favorite type of crust** (`favorite_crust_material`): This question explores your preferred crust material, which influences our product offerings.
* **Most important topping/sauce** (`favorite_topping_category`): This question helps us understand your desired toppings and sauces.
* **Preferred flavor profiles** (`preferred_flavor_profiles`): This question allows you to select multiple preferred flavor profiles, which guides our product development.
* **Dream cheesecake flavor description** (`dream_flavor_description`): This is an optional question for you to describe your ideal cheesecake flavor.
* **What is your age group** (`consumer_age_range`): This question is a duplicate of the first question in Section 1.

Note: Please ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and honestly. The information collected will help us create better products and experiences for you and others like you.
