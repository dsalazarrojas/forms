# Monthly MVP Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly MVP Voting Form is used to collect votes for the team members who have made significant contributions to the team's performance during the month. The form is used to identify the team members who deserve recognition and rewards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your team name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "Your Name" and "Email" fields respectively.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
4. Select the team you want to vote for in the "Vote Team" field.
5. Optionally, add a note about your vote in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Team Name (Team Name)
*   **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the team you are a member of.

### Your Name (Your Name)
*   **Your Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your company's records.

### Email (Email)
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your company email address.

### Phone (Phone)
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

### Vote Team (Vote Team)
*   **Vote Team** (`vote_team_name`, `select_one`, required): Select the team you think is doing an excellent job and deserves recognition.

### Note (Note)
*   **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Optionally, add a note about your vote if you feel it's necessary to explain your selection.
