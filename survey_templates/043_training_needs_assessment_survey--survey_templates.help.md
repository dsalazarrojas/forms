# Training Needs Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Needs Assessment Survey is designed to gather information about each user's demographics and training requirements to provide better training services that meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question to the best of your knowledge.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by *).
3. If a question is not applicable to you, please mark "No" or choose "None of the above".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **demographic info** (text, required: false): 
This field is for entering your demographic information.
* **date of birth** (date, required: false): 
This field is for entering your date of birth.
* **education level** (select_one, required: false): 
Select your education level.
* **gender** (select_one, required: false): 
Select your gender.
* **job title** (text, required: false): 
This field is for entering your job title.
* **training needs** (select_multiple, required: false): 
Select all training needs that apply to you.
* **category 1** (select_one, required: false): 
Select category 1.
* **category 2** (select_one, required: false): 
Select category 2.
* **preferred language** (select_one, required: false): 
Select your preferred language.
* **preferred location** (select_one, required: false): 
Select your preferred location.
* **preferred format** (select_multiple, required: false): 
Select all preferred formats that apply.
* **what type 1** (select_one, required: false): 
Select what type 1.
* **what type 2** (select_one, required: false): 
Select what type 2.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure that your responses are counted correctly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact your supervisor or training coordinator for guidance.
* This form should take you about 5-10 minutes to complete.
