# Public Transportation Plan Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your public transportation needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and fill out the required fields. You will be asked about your travel frequency, preferred mode of transportation, services used, and submission confirmation. For some questions, you will have the option to select multiple answers, while others require a single selection or free-text response. Make sure to review each section carefully and answer all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Public Transportation Plan Inquiry Form Page 1 Label**: This is the first page of the form, where you will be introduced to the survey.
* **Travel Frequency Label**: This question asks about your travel frequency, and you can select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Travel Mode Label**: This question asks about your preferred mode of transportation, which is a required field.
* **Travel Mode Comment Label**: If you have a comment about your travel mode, you can provide it here.
* **Transportation Services Label**: This question asks about the services you use when traveling, and you can select all that apply. This field is also required.
* **Transportation Services Comment Label**: If you have a comment about the services you use, you can provide it here.
* **Public Transportation Inquiry Form Comments Label**: This is a free-text field for any additional comments or concerns.
* **Email Label**: This field is for providing your email address.
* **Phone Label**: This field is for providing your phone number.
* **Email Confirmation Label**: This question confirms if you want to receive email updates.
* **Phone Confirmation Label**: This question confirms if you want to receive phone updates.
* **Public Transportation Inquiry Form Comments Label**: This is another free-text field for any additional comments or concerns.
* **Additional Comments Label**: This is a free-text field for any additional comments or concerns.
* **Public Transportation Inquiry Form Submit Label**: This question asks you to confirm your submission.
* **Submit Confirmation Label**: This question confirms if you are sure about your submission.
* **Public Transportation Inquiry Form Submit Confirmation Label**: This question confirms if you are sure about your submission confirmation.
* **Submit Time Label**: This field is for providing the time of submission.
* **Submit Date Label**: This field is for providing the date of submission.
* **Submit Date Time Label**: This field is for providing the date and time of submission.
* **Email Confirmation Time Label**: This field is for providing the time of email confirmation.
* **Submit Date Time Confirmation Label**: This field is for confirming the date and time of submission.
* **Submit Date Time Confirmation Time Label**: This field is for providing the time confirmation of the date and time of submission.
* **Submitted Date Time Label**: This field is for providing the date and time of submitted data.
* **Submitted Date Label**: This field is for providing the date of submitted data.
* **Public Transportation Inquiry Form Submit Confirmation Label**: This is another free-text field for any additional comments or concerns.

Tips:
* Please make sure to answer all required questions.
* If you have any comments or concerns, please use the free-text fields to provide them.
* Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
