# Editorial Service Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess the satisfaction of editors who have worked on projects. It collects feedback on their overall experience, rating, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project name where the editorial service was provided.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the service on a scale from 1 to 5.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
4. If you would recommend our service to others, select 'True' or 'False'.
5. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
6. Enter your project ID (this will help us identify the specific project).
7. Enter the date when the service was assigned to you.
8. Enter the date when the service was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project where the editorial service was provided. 
* **Rating - 1-5**: Rate your satisfaction with the service on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
* **Will you recommend our service?**: Indicate whether you would recommend our service to others. Select 'True' if you would recommend, 'False' if you would not.
* **Additional comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience. This can be any information you think is relevant.
* **Project ID**: Enter the project ID associated with this service. This will help us identify the specific project.
* **Assigned Date**: Enter the date when the service was assigned to you.
* **Completed Date**: Enter the date when the service was completed.

## Tips

* If you have any questions or need help with this form, contact us.
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to help us understand your experience.
* If you have any issues with the form submission, let us know so we can assist you with the problem.
