# Hairbrush Sanitization Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the sanitization practices and awareness of hairbrush handling among salon staff and customers. It will help us understand the current state of sanitization awareness and best practices in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and to the best of your knowledge.
2. For select multiple or select one fields, choose all that apply or select the most relevant option.
3. Provide accurate and detailed information in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Information** (`salon_info`, text, required: false): Please provide your salon's name, address, or any other relevant information.
* **Salons Participating** (`salons_participating`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all salons that you are familiar with or work for.
* **Hairbrushes Used** (`hairbrushes_used`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of hairbrushes you use.
* **Cleaning Habits** (`cleaning_habits`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all cleaning habits you follow for hairbrushes.
* **Sanitization Practices** (`sanitization_practices`, select_one, required: false): Select the method you use for sanitization.
* **Hygiene Awareness** (`hygiene_awareness`, number, required: false): Rate your level of awareness about hygiene practices.
* **Awareness Level** (`awareness_level`, select_one, required: false): Select your level of awareness about hairbrush sanitization.
* **Salon Staff** (`salon_staff`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all salon staff that you are familiar with.
* **Hairbrush Handling** (`hairbrush_handling`, select_one, required: false): Select how you handle hairbrushes.
* **Product Usage** (`product_usage`, select_one, required: false): Select the product usage for hairbrushes.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required: false): Select follow up for hairbrush sanitization.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Frequency Used** (`frequency_used`, number, required: false): Rate the frequency you use hairbrushes.
* **Time Used** (`time_used`, time, required: false): Rate the time you use hairbrushes for.
* **Duration Used** (`duration_used`, number, required: false): Rate the duration you use hairbrushes.
* **Frequency Clean** (`frequency_clean`, number, required: false): Rate the frequency you clean hairbrushes.

This form is designed to be completed by salon staff and customers to provide information about their sanitization practices and awareness of hairbrush handling.
