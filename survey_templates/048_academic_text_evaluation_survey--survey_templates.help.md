# Academic Text Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Text Evaluation Survey is a form designed to gather feedback on academic texts from evaluators. This form helps identify the strengths and weaknesses of a text, providing valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Evaluator Name, Email, and Institution affiliation.
2. Provide the title and authors of the text being evaluated.
3. Select the purpose of the evaluation (e.g., peer review for publication, course textbook evaluation).
4. Rate the text on the evaluation criteria (content relevance, argument quality, evidence support, writing clarity, originality, methodology, and conclusion validity).
5. Describe the major strengths and areas for improvement of the text.
6. Select your overall recommendation for the text (accept without revision, accept with minor revisions, revise and resubmit, reject, or not sure).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Evaluator Name (**Evaluator Name**, text, required)
Enter your full name as the person evaluating the text.

### Evaluator Email (**Evaluator Email**, email, required)
Enter your professional email address for correspondence.

### Evaluator Institution (**Evaluator Institution**, text, required)
Enter your university or organization affiliation.

### Text Title (**Text Title**, text, required)
Enter the title of the text being evaluated.

### Text Authors (**Text Authors**, text, required)
Enter the names of the authors of the text.

### Evaluation Purpose (**Evaluation Purpose**, select_one, required)
Select the purpose of this evaluation (e.g., peer review for publication, course textbook evaluation).

### Content Relevance Rating (**Content Relevance Rating**, number, required)
Rate the content relevance of the text on a 1-5 scale.

### Argument Quality Rating (**Argument Quality Rating**, number, required)
Rate the argument quality of the text on a 1-5 scale.

### Evidence Support Rating (**Evidence Support Rating**, number, required)
Rate the evidence support of the text on a 1-5 scale.

### Writing Clarity Rating (**Writing Clarity Rating**, number, required)
Rate the writing clarity of the text on a 1-5 scale.

### Originality Rating (**Originality Rating**, number, required)
Rate the originality of the text on a 1-5 scale.

### Methodology Rating (**Methodology Rating**, number, required)
Rate the methodology of the text on a 1-5 scale.

### Conclusion Validity Rating (**Conclusion Validity Rating**, number, required)
Rate the conclusion validity of the text on a 1-5 scale.

### Major Strengths (**Major Strengths**, text, optional)
Describe the major strengths of the text.

### Areas for Improvement (**Areas for Improvement**, text, optional)
Describe areas for improvement in the text.

### Recommendation Decision (**Recommendation Decision**, select_one, required)
Select your overall recommendation for the text (accept without revision, accept with minor revisions, revise and resubmit, reject, or not sure).
