# Home Language Survey For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the language usage habits of a child at home and with their parents. The survey aims to help identify potential areas of support or enrichment for children who may be struggling with language development, or to understand the language diversity of a child's environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the language(s) that apply to you from the drop-down menus.
3. Enter any relevant notes or comments about your child's performance.
4. If you have any recommendations or concerns, select the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Response** (`parent_response`, text, optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide a brief response about your child's language usage habits at home.
* **Language Used At Home** (`language_used_at_home`, select_one, optional): Select the language(s) you use at home, e.g., English, Spanish, or Other.
* **Language Used With Parents** (`language_used_with_parents`, select_one, optional): Select the language(s) you use with your child's parents, e.g., English, Spanish, or Other.
* **Parent Native Language** (`parent_native_language`, select_multiple, optional): If you are a native speaker of multiple languages, select all the languages you are fluent in.
* **Language Used By Child** (`language_used_by_child`, select_multiple, optional): If your child uses multiple languages, select all the languages they use.
* **Child Performance** (`child_performance`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments about your child's performance in school or at home.
* **Parent Response To Performance** (`parent_response_to_performance`, text, optional): Enter your response to your child's performance, e.g., concerns or recommendations.
* **Parent Recommendations** (`parent_recommendations`, select_multiple, optional): Select any recommendations or concerns you have about your child's language development.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions about your child's language usage habits.
* **Email Parent** (`email_parent`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further guidance or support.
