# Orthopaedic Surgery Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the quality of care provided by the orthopaedic surgery team. It consists of questions that ask about the experience of the patient after surgery, including the surgical team's performance, post-operative care, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible. For each question, select the answer that best describes your experience. If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Post-Operative Survey Question One**: Enter your thoughts about the first question asked after surgery. 
* **Surgical Team**: This question asks how the surgical team performed. Select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Excellent: The team performed exceptionally well.
	+ Good: The team performed well, but there were some minor issues.
	+ Fair: The team performed adequately, but with some noticeable issues.
	+ Poor: The team performed poorly.
* **Post-Operative Survey Question Two**: Enter your thoughts about the second question asked after surgery.
* **Pain Management**: This question asks about the effectiveness of pain management during and after surgery. Select all options that apply.
	+ Excellent: The pain management was very effective.
	+ Good: The pain management was somewhat effective.
	+ Fair: The pain management was somewhat ineffective.
	+ Poor: The pain management was not effective at all.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the care provided by the orthopaedic surgery team. Enter your thoughts about your experience.
* **Complications during Surgery**: This question asks whether you experienced any complications during surgery. Select 'True' if you experienced any complications, 'False' otherwise.
* **Post-operative Surgery Follow-up**: This question asks for the date of your last follow-up appointment after surgery. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Post-operative Surgery Follow-up Time**: This question asks for the time of your last follow-up appointment after surgery. Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
