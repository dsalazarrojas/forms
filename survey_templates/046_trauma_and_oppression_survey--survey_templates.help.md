# Trauma And Oppression Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect information about traumatic experiences, feelings, and emotions related to oppression and adversity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form consists of 8 sections, which are listed below.
2. Please answer all the sections that apply to you.
3. You can skip any sections that do not pertain to you or your experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traumatic Events** (id: 2, name: traumatic_events, type: select_multiple): This section asks you to choose from a list of traumatic events that you may have experienced, such as Sexual Assault, Racism, Homophobia, and others. Please select all events that you have experienced.
* **Oppression Experiences** (id: 3, name: oppression_experiences, type: select_one): This question allows you to choose one type of oppression experience from a list, such as Racism, Sexism, Classism, and others. Select the one that best applies to you.
* **Trauma Experiences** (id: 4, name: trauma_experiences, type: text): Please provide any information about your trauma experiences in this text field.
* **Feelings and Emotions** (id: 5, name: feelings_emotions, type: text): This is a text field for you to share your feelings and emotions related to the traumatic events or experiences you have undergone.
* **Help Sources** (id: 6, name: help_sources, type: select_multiple): Please select all sources of help that you have used or are using, such as Mental Health Professional, Support Groups, Hotlines, and others.
* **Help Sources (Others)** (id: 7, name: help_sources_others, type: select_multiple): This section is similar to the previous one, but asks you to select help sources for others that you know or have helped.
* **Trauma Services** (id: 8, name: trauma_services, type: text): This text field is for you to provide any additional information about trauma services that you may have accessed.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can skip any question that you do not feel comfortable answering.
* Take your time and answer each question carefully.
* This form is designed to collect information for research and improvement of services and support for you and others. 

Please remember that all answers provided will be kept confidential.
