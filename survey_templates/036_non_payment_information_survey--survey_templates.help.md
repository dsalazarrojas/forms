# Non Payment Information Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their non-payment experiences, helping us improve our services. Please ensure that you have not submitted this survey before, as duplicate submissions may lead to data duplication and skew results.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select one or more options for "What type of service do you use?".
2. Describe your experience with the service in the "What was your experience like?" field.
3. Rate your likelihood of recommending the service by selecting one option for "How likely are you to recommend?".
4. Provide your thoughts about the service in the "What do you think about the service?" field.
5. Suggest ways we can improve by filling in "How can we improve?".
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service by selecting one option for "How did you find the service overall?".
7. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions, provide your email address in the "Would you like to share your email for follow-up questions?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of service do you use?** (`non_payment_information_survey_1`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more options to indicate the type of service you used. This will help us understand your context and tailor our non-payment information collection.
* **What was your experience like?** (`non_payment_information_survey_2`, text, required/optional): Describe your experience with the service in a few sentences. This will help us understand your satisfaction and pain points.
* **How likely are you to recommend?** (`non_payment_information_survey_3`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your likelihood of recommending the service to others. This will help us gauge the service's overall quality.
* **What do you think about the service?** (`non_payment_information_survey_4`, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the service. This will help us identify areas for improvement.
* **How can we improve?** (`non_payment_information_survey_5`, text, required/optional): Suggest ways we can improve the service. This will help us prioritize our development efforts.
* **How did you find the service overall?** (`non_payment_information_survey_6`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service. This will help us evaluate the service's quality and effectiveness.
* **Would you like to share your email for follow-up questions?** (`non_payment_information_survey_7`, text, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions, provide your email address here. This will help us reach out to you directly and gather more detailed information.
