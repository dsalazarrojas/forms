# Public Services Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Services Awareness Survey is designed to gauge your overall awareness and usage of public services in your region. This survey aims to collect information to help improve public services and provide better support to citizens.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Read the Introduction section to understand the purpose and scope of the survey.
2. Answer the questions regarding your awareness and usage of public services, including your rating and frequency of usage.
3. Select the regions you currently reside in.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback.
5. If applicable, specify public services that you have difficulty accessing.
6. Optionally, provide your contact information for further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: A brief overview of the survey purpose and instructions.
- **What is your rating for overall awareness of public services?** (`awareness_rating`, number): Rate your overall awareness of public services on a scale from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
- **How often do you use public services?** (`usage_frequency`, select_one): Select from the options: Never, Less than once a month, About once a month, Several times a month, Almost every week, Every week.
- **Which region do you live in?** (`regions`, select_multiple): Select all applicable regions from Region 1, Region 2, Region 3, Region 4.
- **Demographics** (`demographics`, text): Provide information about your demographics (optional).
- **Comments** (`comments`, text): Write any additional comments or feedback you have about public services.
- **What are some public services that you have difficulty accessing?** (`public_service_access`, select_multiple): If you face difficulties accessing certain services, select all that apply from Service 1, Service 2, Service 3.
- **Would you like to be contacted for further discussion?** (`contact_info`, select_one): Select 'Yes' if you are open to being contacted by the survey team for further discussion, or 'No' if you prefer not to be contacted.
- **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address if you are interested in being contacted or want to provide more information.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
- **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Write any additional comments or questions you have.
