# university_support_services_student_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their satisfaction with university support services. The data collected will help the institution understand what services are performing well and where there may be opportunities for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing a brief description of the purpose of this survey.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. For the question "What is your primary use of campus services?", select all the services you used from the list.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the services using the scale provided.
6. If you have any suggestions or comments, provide them in the text box.
7. For the question "Which campus support services did you use?", select all the services you used from the list.
8. Rate the effectiveness of communication from campus support services on a scale of 1-5.
9. Rate the time efficiency of campus support services on a scale of 1-5.
10. Rate the quality of campus support services on a scale of 1-5.
11. Select whether you are likely to use campus support services again.
12. Select all the services you used from the list "Frequency of use of campus services".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the purpose of this survey**: A brief description of the survey's purpose is required. This is the initial question to help the institution understand the context of your responses.
* **Contact name**: Your name is required.
* **Email**: Your email address is required.
* **What is your primary use of campus services?**: This question helps the institution understand what services you used the most. Select all services that apply to you.
* **Overall satisfaction rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Do you have any suggestions or comments?**: If you have any suggestions or comments about the services, provide them here.
* **Which campus support services did you use?**: Select all services you used from the list.
* **Communication effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of communication from campus support services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Time efficiency**: Rate the time efficiency of campus support services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Quality of campus support services**: Rate the quality of campus support services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Are you likely to use campus support services again?**: Select whether you are likely to use campus support services again.
* **Frequency of use of campus services**: Select all services you used from the list.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide them in the text box.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the institution.
