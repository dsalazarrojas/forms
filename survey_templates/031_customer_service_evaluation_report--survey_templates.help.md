# Customer Service Evaluation Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Evaluation Report is a form designed to collect feedback and evaluation on a customer's experience with the company's service. This report helps to monitor customer satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions on the form as accurately as possible, providing as much detail as you can.
2. Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5 stars.
3. Describe what the agent did that made you feel positive, negative, or neutral about the interaction.
4. Indicate if you will use the company's services again.
5. If you answered "No", please provide a reason for not using the service again.
6. Enter your email address and/or phone number for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Survey Form** (`customer_survey_form`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **1-5 Star Rating** (`rating1`, number, optional): Rate your experience with the company's service, on a scale of 1-5 stars.
* **What did the agent do that made you feel positive about the interaction?** (`customer_feedback`, text, optional): Describe the actions or behaviors of the agent that made you feel positive about the interaction. This could be anything from being friendly and helpful, to providing clear and concise information, to resolving your issue quickly.
* **What did the agent do that made you feel negative about the interaction?** (`customer_feedback_negative`, text, optional): Describe the actions or behaviors of the agent that made you feel negative about the interaction.
* **What did the agent do that made you feel neutral about the interaction?** (`customer_feedback_neutral`, text, optional): Describe the actions or behaviors of the agent that made you feel neutral about the interaction.
* **Will you use our services again?** (`will_use_again`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you will use the company's services again, and provide a reason if you answered "No".
* **If no, please provide reason** (`why_not_use_again`, text, optional): If you answered "No" to the previous question, explain why you will not use the company's services again.
* **Email** (`email_input`, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for further contact.
* **Phone** (`phone_input`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used for further contact.
 
## Tips
- Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
- Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
- If you are unsure of any question, please leave it blank.
- Your feedback is important to us and helps us improve our services. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form.
