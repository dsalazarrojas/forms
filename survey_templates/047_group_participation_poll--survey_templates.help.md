# Group Participation Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Participation Poll form is designed to collect information from group members about their interest and availability for a specific group and activity. This form helps the organizers to gauge the interest and availability of group members and make informed decisions about upcoming events or activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the group name in the **group_name** field.
2. Enter the name of the activity in the **activity_name** field.
3. Select a date when you will be available to participate in the activity in the **available_date** field.
4. Choose a specific time when you will be available to participate in the activity in the **available_time** field.
5. Indicate if you are interested in participating in the activity in the **interested** field by selecting one or more options.
6. Choose an available option from the list in the **available** field.
7. Enter your email address in the **email** field (if you want to be contacted).
8. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field (if you want to be contacted).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **group_name** (`group_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the group you belong to.
### **activity_name** (`activity_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the activity you are interested in.
### **available_date** (`available_date`, date, required/optional): Select a date when you will be available to participate in the activity.
### **available_time** (`available_time`, time, required/optional): Choose a specific time when you will be available to participate in the activity.
### **interested** (`interested`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if you are interested in participating in the activity by selecting one or more options.
### **available** (`available`, select_one, required/optional): Choose an available option from the list.
### **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted).
### **phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your response is considered.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always contact the form owner for clarification.
* You can always save your progress and come back later to complete the form if needed.
