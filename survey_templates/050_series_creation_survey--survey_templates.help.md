# series_creation_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Series Creation Survey" form is designed to collect information from users regarding their series, including title, description, tags, and other details. This form is intended for series creators to provide essential information about their series, which will be displayed on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your series in the "Series Creation Survey" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your series in the "show description" field.
3. Add any relevant tags for your series in the "show tags" field.
4. Specify the type of series you are creating.
5. Add any idea tags related to your series in the "show idea tags" field.
6. Enter a detailed description of the idea behind your series in the "show idea description" field.
7. Specify the type of idea behind your series in the "show idea type" field.
8. Select the current status of your idea in the "show idea status" field.
9. Set the priority level of your idea in the "show idea priority" field.
10. Select the submission status of your series in the "show idea submission status" field.
11. Enter the date and time you submitted your series for consideration in the "show idea submission date" and "show idea submission time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Series Creation Survey** (`show_title`, text, optional): Enter a short title for your series.
* **show description** (`show_description`, text, optional): A brief description of your series.
* **show tags** (`show_tags`, text, optional): Add relevant tags to your series.
* **show type** (`show_type`, text, optional): Specify the type of series you are creating.
* **show idea tags** (`show_idea_tags`, text, optional): Add any idea tags related to your series.
* **show idea description** (`show_idea_description`, text, optional): A detailed description of the idea behind your series.
* **show idea type** (`show_idea_type`, text, optional): Specify the type of idea behind your series.
* **show idea status** (`show_idea_status`, text, optional): Select the current status of your idea.
* **show idea priority** (`show_idea_priority`, number, optional): Set the priority level of your idea.
* **show idea submission status** (`show_idea_submission_status`, text, optional): Select the submission status of your series.
* **show idea submission date** (`show_idea_submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted your series.
* **show idea submission time** (`show_idea_submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted your series.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information about your series.
* Use standard time and date format for the submission date and time fields.
* You can add or remove fields as necessary, depending on your series' specific needs.
