# Beauty Retail Checkout Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the checkout experience of a beauty retail store. By completing this form, you will help the store understand what works well and what can be improved to provide better service to customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the "Store Location Visited" dropdown menu to choose the location of the store you visited.
2. Enter the date you visited the store in the "Visit Date" field.
3. Choose the time of your visit from the "Time of Visit" dropdown menu.
4. Select your customer type from the "Are You A" dropdown menu.
5. Choose how long you had to wait in the checkout line from the "Wait Time in Checkout Line" dropdown menu.
6. Rate your experience with the checkout speed and efficiency from the "Checkout Speed and Efficiency" dropdown menu.
7. Select the payment options available during checkout from the "Availability of Payment Options" dropdown menu.
8. Rate your experience with the checkout technology, such as the POS system and card reader, from the "Checkout Technology Experience" dropdown menu.
9. Choose how available staff were during your visit from the "Staff Availability at Checkout" dropdown menu.
10. Rate your experience with the staff's friendliness and courtesy from the "Staff Friendliness and Courtesy" dropdown menu.
11. Rate the staff's product knowledge from the "Staff Product Knowledge" dropdown menu.
12. Rate your experience with the staff's helpfulness during your visit from the "Staff Helpfulness with Questions" dropdown menu.
13. Rate the store's cleanliness and organization from the "Store Cleanliness and Organization" dropdown menu.
14. Rate the comfort and safety of the checkout area from the "Checkout Area Comfort and Safety" dropdown menu.
15. Rate the lighting and product visibility from the "Lighting and Product Visibility" dropdown menu.
16. If you experienced any issues or problems during checkout, describe them in the "Any Issues or Problems During Checkout" text field.
17. If you experienced any technical issues, describe them in the "Technical Issues Experienced" text field.
18. If you experienced any pricing or promotional issues, describe them in the "Pricing or Promotional Issues" text field.
19. Rate your overall experience with the checkout process from the "Overall Checkout Experience" dropdown menu.
20. Rate whether you would recommend this store to others from the "Would You Recommend This Store" dropdown menu.
21. Rate whether you would shop here again from the "Will You Shop Here Again" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Location Visited**: Select the location where you visited the store.
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date you visited the store.
* **Time of Visit**: Choose the time of your visit from the options provided.
* **Are You A**: Select your type of customer from the options provided.
* **Wait Time in Checkout Line**: Rate how long you had to wait in the checkout line.
* **Checkout Speed and Efficiency**: Rate your experience with the checkout speed and efficiency.
* **Availability of Payment Options**: Select the payment options that were available during checkout.
* **Checkout Technology Experience**: Rate your experience with the checkout technology.
* **Staff Availability at Checkout**: Rate how available staff were during your visit.
* **Staff Friendliness and Courtesy**: Rate your experience with the staff's friendliness and courtesy.
* **Staff Product Knowledge**: Rate the staff's product knowledge.
* **Staff Helpfulness with Questions**: Rate your experience with the staff's helpfulness.
* **Store Cleanliness and Organization**: Rate the store's cleanliness and organization.
* **Checkout Area Comfort and Safety**: Rate the comfort and safety of the checkout area.
* **Lighting and Product Visibility**: Rate the lighting and product visibility.
* **Any Issues or Problems During Checkout**: Describe any issues or problems you experienced during checkout.
* **Technical Issues Experienced**: Describe any technical issues you experienced.
* **Pricing or Promotional Issues**: Describe any pricing or promotional issues you experienced.
* **Overall Checkout Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the checkout process.
* **Would You Recommend This Store**: Rate whether you would recommend this store to others.
* **Will You Shop Here Again**: Rate whether you would shop here again.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure of an answer, select the option that best describes your experience.
* If you have any issues or problems that are not listed, please describe them in the "Any Issues or Problems During Checkout" text field.
