# storm_impact_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on the impact of a storm on a specific location or area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form instructions and questions carefully.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to save your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Storm Impact**: (`storm_impact`, note, required) This field is for you to describe the impact of the storm. Please provide a brief, detailed description of what happened during the storm.
  - **Infrastructure Damage**: (`infrastructure_damage`, select_multiple, required) Check all the infrastructure that was damaged during the storm, such as roads, bridges, and buildings.
  - **Home Damage**: (`home_damage`, select_multiple, required) Check all the homes that were damaged or affected by the storm.
  - **Storm Description**: (`storm_description`, text, required) Describe the storm and its effects in as much detail as possible.
  - **Environment Damage**: (`environment_damage`, select_multiple, required) Check all the environmental features that were damaged or affected by the storm, such as parks, beaches, or wildlife habitats.
  - **Storm Impact**: (`storm_impact`, note, required) This field is for you to provide additional information about the storm's impact.
  - **Infrastructures Damage**: (`infrastructure_damage`, select_one, required) Check the infrastructure that was damaged during the storm.
  - **Home Damage**: (`home_damage`, select_multiple, required) Check all the homes that were damaged or affected by the storm.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question honestly and accurately.
- Use the provided options for select and select_multiple fields.
- Save your answers before submitting the form.
