# Pedagogical Research Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from users about their familiarity with and involvement in pedagogical research. It aims to assess the awareness and engagement of educators or researchers with pedagogical research activities or initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and choose the options that apply to you.
2. Select all the options that you identify with for each field.
3. Fill out each field as instructed.
4. Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school**: This field allows you to select all the schools or institutions you are familiar with or have experience in. Choose all the options that apply to you.
* **research_areas**: Choose all the research areas you have experience in or are familiar with. These might include topics such as curriculum development, educational technology, educational theory, or instructional design.
* **familiarity_levels**: Please select the level of familiarity you have with pedagogical research. If you have experience in conducting research, you might choose "High".
* **impact**: Indicate if you think that pedagogical research has had an impact on your teaching or education practices. Choose "Yes" if you believe research has had an impact.
* **gathered_feedback**: Indicate if you have received feedback from others about your pedagogical research work. Choose "Yes" if you have received feedback.
* **teaching_practice**: Select all the teaching practices you have used based on pedagogical research. Choose all the options that apply to you.
* **training_needs**: Please select all the areas where you need further training or support for pedagogical research. Choose all the options that apply to you.
* **teaching_initiatives**: Indicate if you have implemented any teaching initiatives based on pedagogical research. Choose "Yes" if you have.

## Tips

* Take your time and carefully read each field before selecting your options.
* If you are not sure about the meaning of a field, try to make an informed guess or ask for clarification.
* You can choose multiple options for each field, so do not be afraid to select as many as apply to you.
