# service_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers on their overall experience at the service location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Provide your contact information if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions.
3. Select the options that best describe your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your overall experience at this location?** (`first_question`, text, required): This is the main question that asks for your overall evaluation of your experience at the location.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience out of some number (e.g. 5 stars).
* **How satisfied are you with your experience?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select from Option 1, 2, or 3 to describe your level of satisfaction.
* **Additional comments** (`comment`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Provide your name so we can contact you if needed.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Provide your company name if relevant.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): Provide your job function or title if relevant.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Provide the name of the contact person at your company if relevant.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Provide the department of your company if relevant.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Provide the date of your experience.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Provide the time of your experience.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Provide the location of your experience.
* **Staff Service Quality** (`staff_service_quality`, select_one, optional): Select from Option 1, 2, or 3 to describe the quality of service you received.
* **Staff Attitude** (`staff_attitude`, select_one, optional): Select from Option 1, 2, or 3 to describe the attitude of the staff.
* **Staff Knowledge** (`staff_knowledge`, select_one, optional): Select from Option 1, 2, or 3 to describe the knowledge of the staff.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, optional): Select from Option 1, 2, or 3 to describe the level of communication you received.
* **Resolution** (`resolution`, select_multiple, optional): Select from Option 1: reported improved, 2, or 3 to describe the resolution of your issue.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Service Quality Improved** (`service_quality_improved`, select_one, optional): Select from Option 1, 2, or 3 to describe how service quality improved.
* **Service Quality Dropped** (`service_quality_dropped`, select_one, optional): Select from Option 1, 2, or 3 to describe how service quality dropped.
* **Service Quality Frequent Problem** (`service_quality_frequent_problem`, select_one, optional): Select from Option 1, 2, or 3 to describe how service quality frequent problems are affecting your experience.
* **Service Quality Uncommon Problem** (`service_quality_uncommon_problem`, select_one, optional): Select from Option 1, 2, or 3 to describe how service quality uncommon problems are affecting your experience.
* **Service Quality Rare Problem** (`service_quality_rare_problem`, select_one, optional): Select from Option 1, 2, or 3 to describe how service quality rare problems are affecting your experience.
