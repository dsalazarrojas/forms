# Interdisciplinary Teaching Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback about your experience with our interdisciplinary teaching approach. Your anonymous responses will help us improve our teaching methods and provide better support to our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Select all that apply for the "Benefits" question.
3. Provide a brief description for the "Challenges" question.
4. Choose one option for the "Effectiveness" question.
5. Type in your email address and phone number (if applicable).
6. Enter the date for the "Date" question.

* **Intro** (`intro`, note, optional): This is a brief introduction to get you started.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the benefits you have experienced with our interdisciplinary teaching approach.

	+ Option 3: [Insert Option 3 description here]
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, optional): Describe any challenges you have faced while using our interdisciplinary teaching approach.
* **Effectiveness** (`effectiveness`, select_one, optional): How effective do you think our interdisciplinary teaching approach has been?
	+ Option 1: [Insert Option 1 description here]
	+ Option 2: [Insert Option 2 description here]
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Do you have any additional comments or suggestions about our interdisciplinary teaching approach?
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you completed this form.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you have any difficulties in answering a question, please contact us for assistance.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback about our interdisciplinary teaching approach!
