# Client Self Care Routine Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to help you assess your current self-care practices and identify areas for improvement. It will help you develop strategies to prioritize self-care and improve your overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question to the best of your ability. You can select one of the provided options for each question, and provide additional information if necessary. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Self-Care and Wellness Assessment --** (`survey_intro`, note, required: false): This is an introductory section to provide context for the survey.
* **Your Name** (`client_name`, text, required: true): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required: true): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Current Overall Wellness Level** (`current_wellness_level`, select_one, required: true): Please select one of the provided options to describe your current overall wellness level.
* **How Important is Self-Care to You?** (`self_care_priority`, select_one, required: true): Please select one of the provided options to indicate how important self-care is to you.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_one, required: true): Please select one of the provided options to describe how often you exercise.
* **Types of Exercise You Do** (`exercise_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the types of exercise you regularly do.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, select_one, required: true): Please select one of the provided options to rate your sleep quality.
* **Average Hours of Sleep Per Night** (`sleep_hours`, number, required: false): Please enter the number of hours you sleep per night.
* **Nutrition and Eating Habits** (`nutrition_habits`, select_one, required: true): Please select one of the provided options to rate your nutrition and eating habits.
* **Daily Water Intake** (`water_intake`, select_one, required: false): Please select one of the provided options to describe your daily water intake.
* **Stress Management Practices** (`stress_management`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the stress management practices you regularly do.
* **Mental Health and Well-being** (`mental_health`, select_one, required: true): Please select one of the provided options to rate your mental health and well-being.
* **Skincare Routine** (`skincare_routine`, select_one, required: false): Please select one of the provided options to describe your skincare routine.
* **Challenges to Self-Care** (`self_care_challenges`, text, required: false): Please provide information on what prevents you from practicing self-care.
* **Self-Care Goals** (`self_care_goals`, text, required: false): Please provide information on your wellness goals.
* **What Support Would Help You?** (`support_needed`, text, required: false): Please provide information on what would help you improve your self-care.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly to provide accurate information.
- Select one of the provided options for each question.
- You can provide additional information if necessary.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
