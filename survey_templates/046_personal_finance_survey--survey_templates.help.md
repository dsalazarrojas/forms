# Personal Finance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your personal financial situation, including your income, employment status, and savings habits. The purpose of this form is to help us provide you with personalized financial guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and what information is required.
2. Please complete each section with accurate and honest information about your personal finances.
3. If you are unsure about any section, you can contact our support team for clarification.
4. Once you have completed all sections, review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy.
5. Submit the form to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Annual income in thousands** (`annual_income`, `number`, required: false): Please enter your annual income in thousands of dollars. For example, if your annual income is $50,000, please enter "5".
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select your current employment status from the following options:
	+ Employed
	+ Self-employed
	+ Student
	+ Retired
* **Income source** (`income_source`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the sources of your income. You can choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Wages
	+ Investments
	+ Business
	+ Other
* **Annual savings** (`annual_savings`, `number`, required: false): Please enter your annual savings amount. If you do not have any savings, please enter "0".
* **Is this information about an individual or a family?** (`family_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select whether this information is about an individual or a family.
* **Annual expense budget** (`annual_expense_budget`, `number`, required: false): Please enter your annual expense budget. If you do not have any expenses, please enter "0".
* **Do you have any outstanding debt?** (`outstanding_debt`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select whether you have any outstanding debt.
	+ No
	+ Yes
* **Annual savings as a percentage of income** (`annual_savings_percentage`, `number`, required: false): Please enter your annual savings as a percentage of your income. If you do not have any savings, please enter "0".
* **Employment type** (`employment_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your employment type from the following options:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Contractor
	+ Freelancer
* **Do you have any dependents?** (`dependents`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select whether you have any dependents.
	+ No
	+ Yes
* **Annual Savings 2** (`annual_savings_2`, `number`, required: false): Please enter your annual savings amount 2. If you do not have any savings, please enter "0".
* **Income Source 2** (`income_source_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the sources of your income 2. You can choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Wages
	+ Investments
	+ Business
	+ Other

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields carefully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any section, you can contact our support team for clarification.
* Once you have completed all sections, review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy.
* Submit the form to proceed with confidence.
