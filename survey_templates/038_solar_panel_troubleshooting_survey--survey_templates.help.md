# Solar Panel Troubleshooting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help our team troubleshoot and resolve issues with your solar panel system. By providing detailed information about your issue, you will be able to get assistance from our team and schedule services if necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please fill out each field carefully and accurately.
2.  Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of your issue in the "Issue Description" field.
3.  Upload any relevant photos of your solar panel system in the "Issue Photos" field.
4.  If you have already scheduled a service visit, provide the date and time in the "Schedule Service" fields.
5.  Select the type of service you require (Installation, Maintenance, or Repair) in the "Service Type" field.
6.  Choose the priority level of your issue (High, Medium, or Low) in the "Issue Priority" field.
7.  Select the category of your issue (Electrical, Mechanical, Panel Related, or Other) in the "Issue Category" field.
8.  Provide a brief summary of your issue in the "Issue Summary" field.
9.  Add any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field.
10.  Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, in the "Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): A field to enter any additional information that is not covered in the other fields.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): A field to describe the issue you are experiencing with your solar panel system.
* **Issue Photos** (`issue_photos`, text, optional): A field to upload photos of your solar panel system to help us better understand the issue.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): A field to enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Schedule Service** (`service_schedule`, text, optional): A field to schedule a service visit, including date and time.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, optional): A field to select the type of service you require (Installation, Maintenance, or Repair).
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, optional): A field to select the date for the scheduled service.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, optional): A field to select the time for the scheduled service.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, text, optional): A field to enter the service provider's name and contact information.
* **Issue Details** (`issue_details`, text, optional): A field to provide more information about your issue.
* **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`, select_one, optional): A field to select the priority level of your issue (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Issue Category** (`issue_category`, select_one, optional): A field to select the category of your issue (Electrical, Mechanical, Panel Related, or Other).
* **Issue Summary** (`issue_summary`, text, optional): A field to briefly describe your issue.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): A field to add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): A field to enter your email address.
