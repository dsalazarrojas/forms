# Retail Store Layout Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from customers, employees, or other stakeholders on the layout of a retail store. The goal is to understand how the store's layout affects customer experience and employee productivity. Your input will help us improve the store's layout and overall shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this questionnaire, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the correct answers to each question based on your experience in the retail store.
2. Fill in the required information in the fields that have a "required" status next to them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_layout** (`store_layout`, text, required: false): This field is for you to describe the store layout in your own words.
* **customer_experience** (`customer_experience`, number, required: true): This field asks you to rate your experience in the store, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **layout_improvement** (`layout_improvement`, text, required: false): In this field, you can suggest improvements to the store's layout.
* **employee_experience** (`employee_experience`, number, required: true): This field asks you to rate the store's layout in terms of how it affects employee productivity, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **customer_flow** (`customer_flow`, text, required: false): Describe the flow of customers in the store in this field.
* **store_layout_improvement** (`store_layout_improvement`, text, required: false): Suggest improvements to the store's layout in this field.
* **product_display** (`product_display`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if the product display in the store meets your expectations, and "No" otherwise.
* **section** (`section`, note, required: false): This is an optional field for you to add any additional comments or observations about your experience in the store.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have in this field.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or updates.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: true): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or updates.
* **name** (`name`, text, required: true): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **company** (`company`, text, required: true): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **job_title** (`job_title`, text, required: true): Enter your job title or position.
* **department** (`department`, text, required: true): Enter the department you belong to.
* **team** (`team`, text, required: true): Enter the team you belong to.
* **position** (`position`, text, required: true): Enter your position or role in the company.
* **store** (`store`, text, required: true): Enter the name of the store you are providing feedback on.
* **date** (`date`, date, required: true): Enter the date of your visit or interaction with the store.
* **time** (`time`, time, required: true): Enter the time of your visit or interaction with the store.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
