# Civic Space Freedom Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey aiming to collect data on citizens' perceptions of civic freedom. It inquires about personal feelings and experiences regarding assembly and protest, expression, and the overall civic environment in your region. By providing your input, you help researchers understand changes and trends in civic freedom worldwide.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all required fields filled. The form has a mix of optional and required questions. The required fields are marked as such. 
2. Answer each question honestly and thoughtfully.
3. For questions with multiple options, choose the one that best describes your feelings or experiences.
4. For questions with open-ended fields (such as "Specific Incident Mention"), you can provide any specific trend or event that you consider noteworthy.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Region or Country of Residence (id: 2)
 *Region or Country of Residence* (text, required): Enter your region or country of residence.

### Age Range (id: 3)
*Age Range* (select_one, required): Select the range that best describes your age.

### How Free to Assemble and Protest (id: 4)
*How free do people feel to assemble and protest in your area?* (number, required): This is a 5-point scale. Select a number to indicate how free you feel to assemble and protest in your area. A score of 1 means extremely restricted, while 5 means completely free.

### How Safe to Express Opinions (id: 5)
*How safe do you feel expressing opinions that disagree with the government?* (number, required): This is a 5-point scale. Select a number to indicate how safe you feel expressing opinions that disagree with the government. A score of 1 means very unsafe, while 5 means very safe.

### Civic Environment and Trends (id: 6)
*Civic Environment and Trends* (note, optional): This is an optional field for any additional comments or thoughts regarding your civic environment and trends.

### Trust in the Judicial System (id: 7)
*Do you believe the legal system is independent and fair?* (select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your belief about the independence and fairness of the legal system.

### Media Freedom (id: 8)
*In your opinion, how free is the local media from government influence?* (select_one, required): This question is about your feeling of how free the media is from government influence. Select the option that best describes your feeling.

### Concerns about Digital Surveillance (id: 9)
*How concerned are you about digital surveillance of civic activists?* (select_one, required): This question is about your level of concern regarding digital surveillance. Select the option that best describes your feeling.

### Trend in Civic Freedom (id: 10)
*Over the past 3 years, has civic freedom in your area...* (select_one, required): Select the option that best describes the trend of civic freedom in your area over the past 3 years.

### Barriers to Advocacy (id: 11)
*What is the main barrier to civic advocacy in your region?* (select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to describe the main barrier to advocacy in your region.

### Ease of NGO Registration (id: 12)
*How easy is it for new NGOs/Nonprofits to register legally?* (select_one, optional): This question is about how easy or difficult it is for new organizations to register.

### Specific Incident Mention (id: 13)
*(Optional) Mention any specific trend or event of concern* (text, optional): This field is for any specific trends or events that you feel are noteworthy.

### Your Role in the Survey (id: 14)
*I am completing this survey as a...* (select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your role in society, and why you're completing this survey.

### Date of Completion (id: 15)
*Date of Completion* (date, required): Provide the date you are completing this survey.
