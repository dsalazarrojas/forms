# Product Survey 13 18 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their interactions with a product, allowing us to gather feedback and data on customer experiences, preferences, and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to answer all required questions.
2. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please reach out to the support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your role or position.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, select_multiple, required): Select all the products you are interacting with.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you placed the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you placed the order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the address where your order was shipped to.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Enter any details about your order that are relevant to your experience.
* **Survey Category** (`survey_category`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the categories that best describe your experience.
* **Survey Subcategory** (`survey_subcategory`, select_one, required): Select the subcategory that best describes your experience.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you took the survey.
* **Survey Time** (`survey_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you took the survey.
* **Survey Comments** (`survey_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Customer Company** (`customer_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Customer Job Title** (`customer_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Customer Department** (`customer_department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Customer Job Function** (`customer_job_function`, text, optional): Enter your job function.
* **Customer Manager** (`customer_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Customer Designation** (`customer_designation`, text, optional): Enter your designation.
* **Customer Designation Level** (`customer_designation_level`, text, optional): Enter your designation level.
* **Customer Designation Date** (`customer_designation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your designation.
* **Customer Designation Time** (`customer_designation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your designation.
