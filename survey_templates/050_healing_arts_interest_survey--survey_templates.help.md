# Healing Arts Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge your interest in various healing arts workshops, allowing us to better understand your preferences and availability. By completing this survey, you'll help us tailor future workshop offerings to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of this form to send your responses.
2. Fill out the form in the required fields (indicated by a asterisk*) with the requested information.
3. Select the relevant options from the drop-down lists (e.g., "what-kind-of-workshop-are-you-most-interested-in") to express your preferences.
4. If you need to provide additional comments or suggestions, use the text field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (full-name)** (`text`, *): This field is for providing your full name, which will help us address you personally.
* **Your Email (your-email)** (`email`, *): Enter your email address so we can contact you about future workshop opportunities.
* **What Kind of Workshop Are You Most Interested In (workshop_type)** (`select_one`, *): Choose from the options provided (yoga, meditation, tai-chi) to express your interest in one of these healing arts.
* **Availability (availability)** (`date`, *): Indicate when you are available to attend a workshop. Please use the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Comments or Suggestions (comments)** (`text`, *): Share any additional thoughts or ideas you may have about our workshops or this survey.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your responses, as they will help us tailor our workshop offerings to better meet your needs.
* Select all applicable options for "How Many Days Per Week Do You Have Availability" if you have multiple available days.
* You can enter any additional comments or suggestions in the text field at the end of the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on a particular field, feel free to ask and we'll be happy to help!
