# personal_trainer_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "personal_trainer_feedback_form" is a tool for clients to provide feedback on their personal training sessions. This feedback will help us to improve our service and provide better training experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Rate Your Training Session**: Select one or more of the available ratings to describe your experience.
2. **Rate Your Trainer's Professionalism**: Select your rating for the trainer's professionalism.
3. **Rate Your Client Satisfaction**: Select your rating for your overall satisfaction with the service.
4. **Overall Rating**: Select your overall rating for the training session.
5. **Trainer Support**: Describe any suggestions or ideas for how we can improve our service.
6. **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
7. **Email**: Enter your email address to help us contact you if we need to.
8. **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
9. **Training Session Date**: Select the date of your training session.
10. **Training Session Time**: Select the time of your training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Session Rating**: A rating to describe your overall experience with your training session.
	+ **Excellent**
	+ **Very Good**
	+ **Good**
	+ **Average**
	+ **Poor**
* **Trainer Professionalism**: A rating to describe your perception of your trainer's professionalism.
	+ **High**
	+ **Medium**
	+ **Low**
* **Client Satisfaction**: A rating to describe your overall satisfaction with the service.
	+ **High**
	+ **Medium**
	+ **Low**
* **Overall Rating**: A rating to describe your overall satisfaction with the training session.
	+ **High**
	+ **Medium**
	+ **Low**
* **Trainer Support**: Describe any suggestions or ideas for how we can improve our service.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Training Session Date**: Select the date of your training session.
* **Training Session Time**: Select the time of your training session.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Email, Phone, Training Session Date, and Training Session Time).
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback or suggestions.
* Use the available ratings to provide clear and concise feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
