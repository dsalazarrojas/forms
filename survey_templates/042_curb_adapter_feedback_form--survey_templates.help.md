# Curb Adapter Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curb Adapter Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from users about the performance of our adapters in specific projects. This feedback is essential to help us identify areas for improvement and enhance our adapters' efficiency and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project you are providing feedback on.
2. **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of installation in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/15/2023).
3. **Adapter Performance** (`adapter_performance`, select_one, required): Rate the adapter's performance using one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. **Rating** (`rating`, number, not required): Enter a rating between 1 and 5.
5. **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments or remarks about the adapter's performance.
6. **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you with further questions or to follow up on your feedback.
7. **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for clarification or to follow up on your feedback.
8. **Additional Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the adapter's performance.
9. **Project Details** (`project_details`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant options from the list to provide more context about the adapter and project.
10. **Installation Location** (`installation_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the adapter was installed.
11. **Adapter Model** (`adapter_model`, select_one, required): Select the model of the adapter used in the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are providing feedback on.
- **Installation Date**: Enter the date of installation in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Adapter Performance**: Rate the adapter's performance using one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
- **Rating**: Enter a rating between 1 and 5.
- **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or remarks about the adapter's performance.
- **Email**: Enter your email address so we can reach out to you with further questions or to follow up on your feedback.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for clarification or to follow up on your feedback.
- **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the adapter's performance.
- **Project Details**: Select all relevant options from the list to provide more context about the adapter and project.
- **Installation Location**: Enter the location where the adapter was installed.
- **Adapter Model**: Select the model of the adapter used in the project.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact the support team.
