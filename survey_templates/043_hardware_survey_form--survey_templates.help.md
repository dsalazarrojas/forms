# Hardware Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The hardware survey form is a collection of questions that helps keep track of your company's hardware inventory. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field below. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Hardware Type** (`hardware_type`, select_one): Select the type of hardware you are describing. Choose from: CPU, Motherboard, Monitor, Keyboard, or Mouse.
- **Hardware Model** (`hardware_model`, text): Enter the model number or name of the hardware you are describing.
- **Processor Speed** (`processor_speed`, number): Enter the processor speed of the hardware. 
- **Ram Capacity** (`ram_capacity`, number): Enter the ram capacity of the hardware.
- **Description** (`description`, text): Briefly describe the hardware, including any notable features.
- **Installed Software** (`installed_software`, note): List any software installed on the hardware. 
- **Assigned Tools** (`assigned_tools`, text): List any tools assigned to the hardware.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text): Enter the assigned tool for this hardware. 
- **Hardware Purchase Date** (`hardware_purchase_date`, date): Enter the date you purchased the hardware.
- **Hardware Purchase Cost** (`hardware_purchase_cost`, number): Enter the purchase price of the hardware.
- **Hardware Purchase Currency** (`hardware_purchase_currency`, select_one): Select the currency of the purchase. Choose from: USD, EUR, JPY, or CNY.
- **Hardware Purchase Location** (`hardware_purchase_location`, select_one): Select the location of the purchase. Choose from: USA, Japan, China, or Canada.
- **Hardware Survey Form Comments** (`hardware_survey_form_comments`, text): Add any additional comments or notes about the hardware.
- **Hardware Form Comments** (`hardware_form_comments`, text): Add any comments or notes about this form.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text): Add any additional comments or notes about the hardware.
- **Additional Hardware Types** (`hardware_type_2`, select_multiple): If you have more hardware types, select from: GPU, Power Supply, Storage Device, or Network Card.
- **Additional Hardware Models** (`hardware_model_2`, select_multiple): If you have more hardware models, select from: Dell, HP, Lenovo, or Apple.
- **Additional Hardware Purchase Date** (`hardware_purchase_date_2`, date): If you have more purchase dates, enter the date for this hardware.
- **Additional Hardware Survey Form Comments** (`hardware_survey_form_comments_2`, text): Add any additional comments or notes about this hardware.
- **Additional Hardware Form Comments** (`hardware_form_comments_2`, text): Add any additional comments or notes about this form.
- **Additional Hardware Purchase Cost** (`hardware_purchase_cost_2`, number): If you have more purchase costs, enter the cost for this hardware.
- **Additional Hardware Purchase Currency** (`hardware_purchase_currency_2`, select_one): If you have more currencies, select from: USD, EUR, JPY, or CNY.
- **Additional Hardware Purchase Location** (`hardware_purchase_location_2`, select_one): If you have more locations, select from: USA, Japan, China, or Canada.
- **Additional Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool_2`, text): Enter the assigned tool for this additional hardware. 

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information about your hardware. If you need help with a particular field or have questions, feel free to ask.
