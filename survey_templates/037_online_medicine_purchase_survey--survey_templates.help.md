# Online Medicine Purchase Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights on online shoppers' habits, motivations, and concerns regarding medicine purchases. It's a tool to help businesses and researchers understand the online medicine purchasing behavior of their customers or target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions.
2. Start by reading each question carefully and answering them according to your actual experiences and perceptions.
3. Fill out the form honestly and accurately, taking care not to leave any questions blank.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure you've answered all questions correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Habits** (`Habits`, `text`, `optional`):
    This section is where you can describe your general habits of buying medicine. Please type in your answer in a few words or short sentence.
* **Motivations** (`Motivations`, `select_one`, `optional`):
    Choose one of the options that best describes why you buy medicine online.
* **Concerns** (`Concerns`, `select_multiple`, `optional`):
    Select all the concerns that apply to you when buying medicine online.
* **Satisfaction** (`Satisfaction`, `number`, `optional`):
    Rate your satisfaction with your last online medicine purchase experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **How often do you buy medicine?** (`medicine_purchase_habits`, `date`, `optional`):
    If you have a specific date in mind when you usually buy medicine, please type it in. This helps us understand your purchasing habits.
* **Age** (`age_range`, `number`, `optional`):
    Please enter your age range for a more accurate representation of our audience.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, `optional`):
    Choose your gender from the given options.
* **Income** (`income_range`, `number`, `optional`):
    Please enter your income range for a more accurate representation of our audience.
    Note: This is a sensitive question, and your answer will be kept confidential.
Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, and do not hesitate to ask if you have any questions or concerns. Thank you for your participation!
