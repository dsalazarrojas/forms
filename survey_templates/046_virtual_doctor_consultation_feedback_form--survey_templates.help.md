# virtual_doctor_consultation_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for patients to provide feedback on their virtual doctor consultation. It helps to understand the patient's satisfaction and experience with the doctor's services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Answer questions 1, 2, and 9 for the "question_1", "question_2", and "patient_satisfaction" sections, which are multiple-choice questions.
3. Select the best options for questions 3, 5, 7, 10, and 11, which are multiple-choice questions.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Answer question 11 for the "quality_of_care" section, which is a multiple-choice question.
7. Select the best options for question 12, "overall_experience", which is a multiple-choice question.
8. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "note" field.
9. Click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (text): This is the field for you to enter your name.
* **question_1** (select_one): This is a multiple-choice question asking about your experience with the doctor. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **question_2** (select_multiple): This is a multiple-choice question asking about the quality of the doctor's service. Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **date** (date): This field is for you to enter the date of your virtual doctor consultation.
* **time** (time): This field is for you to enter the time of your virtual doctor consultation.
* **note** (note): This is a free text field for you to add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **email** (email): This field is for you to enter your email address, which will be used to contact you if needed.
* **phone** (text): This field is for you to enter your phone number, which will be used to contact you if needed.
* **patient_satisfaction** (select_one): This is a multiple-choice question asking about your overall satisfaction with the doctor's services. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **provider_satisfaction** (select_multiple): This is a multiple-choice question asking about your overall satisfaction with the doctor's services. Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **quality_of_care** (select_one): This is a multiple-choice question asking about the quality of care provided by the doctor. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **overall_experience** (select_multiple): This is a multiple-choice question asking about your overall experience with the doctor's services. Select all the options that apply to your experience.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions honestly and accurately.
* Make sure to enter the correct date and time of your virtual doctor consultation.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "note" field to add it.
* Click "Submit" to submit your feedback.
