# Educational Program Accreditation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Program Accreditation Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about an educational program's accreditation status. It helps in evaluating the program's adherence to accreditation standards and making informed decisions about its continuation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are authorized to fill out the form.
2. Provide the required information for the program title, institution, and evaluation criteria.
3. Optional fields can be left blank if not applicable.
4. Enter the date and time of submission and review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### program_title (Text)
Enter the title of the educational program.

*   **program_title** (`program_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the educational program.

### institution_id (Number)
Enter the institution's ID number.

*   **institution_id** (`institution_id`, number, optional): Enter the institution's ID number.

### program_length (Number)
Enter the length of the educational program.

*   **program_length** (`program_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the educational program in years or any other suitable unit.

### program_type (Select One)
Select the type of the educational program.

*   **program_type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Master' or 'Doctoral' depending on the type of the program.

### evaluation_criterion (Select Multiple)
Select the evaluation criteria applied to the program.

*   **evaluation_criterion** (`evaluation_criterion`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable criteria from the list, such as 'Criterion 1', 'Criterion 2', and 'Criterion 3'.

### evaluation_scale (Select One)
Select the evaluation scale used for the program.

*   **evaluation_scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_one, optional): Select 'Scale 1', 'Scale 2', or 'Scale 3' depending on the evaluation scale used.

### evaluation_scale_2 (Select Multiple)
Select the evaluation scale applied for the program.

*   **evaluation_scale_2** (`evaluation_scale_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' depending on the evaluation scale applied.

### evaluation_scale_3 (Number)
Enter the evaluation scale value for the program.

*   **evaluation_scale_3** (`evaluation_scale_3`, number, optional): Enter a value for the evaluation scale.

### evaluation_scale_4 (Number)
Enter the evaluation scale value for the program.

*   **evaluation_scale_4** (`evaluation_scale_4`, number, optional): Enter a value for the evaluation scale.

### date_of_completion (Date)
Enter the date of program completion.

*   **date_of_completion** (`date_of_completion`, date, optional): Enter the date when the program was completed.

### time_of_completion (Time)
Enter the time of program completion.

*   **time_of_completion** (`time_of_completion`, time, optional): Enter the time when the program was completed.

### note (Note)
Enter any additional comments or notes.

*   **note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the program.

### email (Email)
Enter the institution's email address.

*   **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the institution's email address.

### phone (Text)
Enter the institution's phone number.

*   **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the institution's phone number.

### submit (Select One)
Select whether to submit the evaluation.

*   **submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to submit the evaluation.

### submit_date (Date)
Enter the date of submission.

*   **submit_date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.

### submit_time (Time)
Enter the time of submission.

*   **submit_time** (`submit_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.

### submit_note (Note)
Enter any additional comments or notes about the submission.

*   **submit_note** (`submit_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the submission.

### submit_email (Email)
Enter the email address of the submitter.

*   **submit_email** (`submit_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the submitter.

### submit_phone (Text)
Enter the phone number of the submitter.

*   **submit_phone** (`submit_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the submitter.

### submit_review (Select One)
Select whether to review the evaluation.

*   **submit_review** (`submit_review`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to review the evaluation.

### submit_review_date (Date)
Enter the date of the review.

*   **submit_review_date** (`submit_review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the review.

### submit_review_time (Time)
Enter the time of the review.

*   **submit_review_time** (`submit_review_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the review.

### submit_review_note (Note)
Enter any additional comments or notes about the review.

*   **submit_review_note** (`submit_review_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the review.

### submit_review_email (Email)
Enter the email address of the reviewer.

*   **submit_review_email** (`submit_review_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the reviewer.

### submit_review_phone (Text)
Enter the phone number of the reviewer.

*   **submit_review_phone** (`submit_review_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
