# Home Delivery Service Quality Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on the quality of our home delivery service. Your responses will help us understand how we can improve our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with options, select the one that best matches your experience.
3. For text fields, provide as much detail as possible.
4. Please complete all questions on both pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **how_was_your_order_fulfilled** (select_one):
	+ This question asks how satisfied you are with the fulfillment of your order. Please select one of the options:
		- Very Satisfied
		- Somewhat Satisfied
		- Not Satisfied
		- Very Dissatisfied
		- Neutral
* **how_satisfied_with_delivery_time** (select_multiple):
	+ This question asks how satisfied you are with the delivery time. You can select multiple options:
		- Very Satisfied
		- Somewhat Satisfied
		- Not Satisfied
		- Very Dissatisfied
		- Neutral
* **what_do_you_like** (text):
	+ This question asks for any positive experiences you had with our service. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **what_do_you_dislike** (text):
	+ This question asks for any negative experiences you had with our service. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **were_your_items_packed_correctly** (select_multiple):
	+ This question asks if your items were packed correctly. You can select multiple options:
		- True
		- False
		- Unsure
* **were_your_items_in_good_condition** (select_multiple):
	+ This question asks if your items were in good condition. You can select multiple options:
		- True
		- False
		- Unsure
* **was_your_order_delivered_on_time** (select_one):
	+ This question asks if your order was delivered on time. Please select one of the options:
		- True
		- False
		- Unsure
* **any_other_comments** (text):
	+ This question is an open-ended question for you to provide any other comments or feedback about your experience. Please provide as much detail as possible.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any other comments or concerns, please provide them in the text field.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback! Your input is valuable to us.
