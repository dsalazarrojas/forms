# Are You Feeling Well Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Are You Feeling Well Survey" is designed to assess participants' overall well-being, physical and mental health, and lifestyle factors. This survey aims to collect information about how participants are feeling, their physical and emotional state, and their daily habits. The data collected will help us better understand participants' needs and provide support when necessary.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your participant information: This includes your name and ID or student number (if applicable).
2. Select your overall feeling on the scale:
	* "How are you feeling overall today?"
	* "Energy Level": Choose how much energy you have today.
	* "Emotional State": Select how you would describe your emotional state.
3. Report any physical symptoms:
	* "Are you experiencing any physical symptoms?"
	* "Did you have a fever or elevated temperature?"
4. Choose your daily habits:
	* "Did you drink adequate water today?"
	* "Did you engage in physical activity?"
	* "How would you rate your nutrition intake today?"
5. If you need medical attention or rest:
	* "Do you need medical attention or rest?"
6. Additional comments or concerns:
	* "Any additional comments or concerns"

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **ID or Student Number**: Optional identifier for students, used for tracking purposes.
* **Overall Well-Being**: Select how you are feeling today on the scale provided.
* **Physical Symptoms**: Check if you are experiencing any physical symptoms.
* **Fever Check**: Select if you have a fever or elevated temperature.
* **Energy Level**: Choose how much energy you have today.
* **Emotional State**: Select how you would describe your emotional state.
* **Stress Level**: Choose your current stress level.
* **Sleep Quality**: Select how well you slept last night.
* **Water Intake**: Select if you drank adequate water today.
* **Physical Activity**: Choose if you engaged in physical activity.
* **Nutrition Intake**: Select how well you would rate your nutrition intake today.
* **Medical Attention**: Select if you need medical attention or rest.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or concerns.
