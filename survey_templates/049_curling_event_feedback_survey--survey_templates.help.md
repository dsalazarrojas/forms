# Curling Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience at a curling event. The feedback collected will help us understand what worked well and what can be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your answer for each question by choosing from the available options or entering your response in the text fields.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details (Select Multiple)
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the ways you felt about your experience at the event.

### name (text, optional)
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name or alias if you'd like to be recognized for your feedback.

### Rating (number, optional)
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Provide your rating of the event, on a scale of 1 to 5.

### Comment (text, optional)
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Share any thoughts or observations about your experience at the event.

### Email (email, optional)
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive future event updates.

**Tips**

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Keep your answers concise and clear.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact us at [support email].
* If you have suggestions for improving our events, we'd love to hear them!
