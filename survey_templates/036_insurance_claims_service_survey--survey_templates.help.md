# Insurance Claims Service Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the insurance claims service. Your responses will help us understand how we can improve the service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the on-screen instructions and fill in the required information. The form is divided into several sections, and each section is described below.

* **Introduction** (`intro`, `note`, optional): This section is a general introduction to the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

* **Satisfaction with Outcome** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the outcome of your claim. The options are:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Not satisfied
	+ Not at all satisfied

	+ True
	+ False
* **Reason for Delay** (`delay_reason`, `select_one`, optional): If you answered "True" to the previous question, choose one of the following reasons for the delay.
	+ Communication
	+ Unclear about process
	+ Unclear about outcome
	+ Other

	+ Very effective
	+ Somewhat effective
	+ Not effective
	+ Not at all effective

	+ Very satisfied

	+ Not satisfied

	+ True

	+ True
	+ False
