# Dining Out Decision Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps users make informed decisions when dining out by asking questions about their preferences and restrictions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on the Main Page to provide detailed information about your dining preferences and requirements.
2. Choose your preferred options from the drop-down menus or text fields as instructed.
3. Ensure you select an option from each field with an *required* label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page Group**: This field asks you to choose between Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Vegan, and Other options. Please select all that apply.
* **Veggies**: Select all the vegetables you would like to have or are willing to eat. You can choose multiple options.
* **Quantity**: Please enter the quantity of each selected veggie you would like to have.
* **Veggies Allergies**: Select all the food allergies you have related to vegetables, such as Gluten, Lactose, or Other.
* **Veggies Dietary Options**: If you have any special dietary requirements for vegetables, please enter them here (e.g., low-sodium, organic).
* **Food**: Please enter any specific foods you would like to have or are willing to eat.
* **Beverage**: Enter any specific beverages you would like to have or are willing to drink.
* **Time**: If you have a specific time range for dining, please enter it here.
* **Date**: If you have a specific date in mind for dining, please enter it here.
* **Notify**: Please indicate if you would like to be notified when your meal is ready or available.
* **Notify Time**: If you would like to be notified at a specific time, please enter it here.
* **Notify Date**: If you would like to be notified on a specific date, please enter it here.

## Tips
