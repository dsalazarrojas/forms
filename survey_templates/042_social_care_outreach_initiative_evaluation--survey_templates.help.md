# Social Care Outreach Initiative Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the success of the Social Care Outreach Initiative by collecting information on the participant's experience with the services provided. It is designed to gather insights on the effectiveness of the initiative and identify areas of improvement. Please provide honest and detailed feedback to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are:
	* First Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Visit Outcome
	* Relationship
	* Follow-up Action
	* Date
	* Time
	* Location
	* Provider Name
	* Provider Relationship
	* Follow-up Contact
2. For the select_multiple fields, choose all applicable options that apply to your experience.
3. Use the provided text boxes to enter any additional comments or notes relevant to your experience.
4. Make sure to select an option for all required fields to complete the form successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name* (1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or records.
* **Email* (2)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up or communication.
* **Phone* (3)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for follow-up or communication.
* **Feedback (4)**: Select all the ways in which you feel about the services provided:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neither Agree Nor Disagree
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Service Improvement (5)**: Select how you feel about the improvement of the services provided:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neither Agree Nor Disagree
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Visit Outcome (6)**: Select the outcome of your visit:
	+ Met Expectations
	+ Exceeded Expectations
	+ Not Met Expectations
* **Visit Outcome Notes (7)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your visit.
* **Relationship (8)**: Select how you know the provider:
	+ Friend
	+ Family Member
	+ Colleague
	+ Neighbor
	+ Other
* **Participant Relationship Notes (9)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your relationship with the provider.
* **Follow-up Action (10)**: Select the action that will be taken after the visit:
	+ Schedule Follow-up Visit
	+ Schedule Follow-up Call
	+ No Follow-up Needed
	+ Other
* **Follow-up Action Notes (11)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the follow-up action.
* **Service Request (12)**: Select the types of services you requested:
	+ Home Visits
	+ Phone Calls
	+ Email
	+ Text Messages
	+ In-Person Visits
* **Service Request Notes (13)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the services requested.
* **Follow-up Request (14)**: Select if you need any follow-up:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Follow-up Request Notes (15)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the follow-up request.
* **Date (17)**: Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time (18)**: Enter the time of your visit.
* **Location (19)**: Enter the location of your visit.
* **Provider Name (20)**: Enter the name of the provider who visited you.
* **Provider Relationship (22)**: Select how you know the provider:
	+ Self
	+ Spouse
	+ Partner
	+ Colleague
	+ Other
* **Provider Relationship Notes (23)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your relationship with the provider.
* **Follow-up Contact (24)**: Select the preferred contact method for follow-up:
	+ Phone Call
	+ Email
	+ Text Message
	+ Visit
	+ Other
* **Follow-up Contact Notes (25)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the follow-up contact.

Note: Please ensure all fields are filled out as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
