# Member Satisfaction Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Member Satisfaction Survey Template is a tool to help the gym staff understand member satisfaction and preferences, allowing us to make informed decisions to improve the member experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully to provide an accurate representation of your experience.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the text fields.
