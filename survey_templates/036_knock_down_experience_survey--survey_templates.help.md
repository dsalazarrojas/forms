# Knock Down Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the quality of a service provided. It is meant to be filled out by customers or employees who have received the service and want to share their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Knock Down Experience Survey** (`survey_header`, text, optional): This is the title of the survey. You can enter a brief description if you like, but it is not required.
2. **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required): Enter the date you received the service.
3. **Site** (`site`, text, optional): Enter the address of the site where you received the service.
4. **Team** (`team`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team that provided the service.
5. **Service Type** (`service_type`, text, optional): Select or enter the type of service you received.
6. **Experience Rating** (`experience_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your experience on a scale from Very Poor to Excellent.
	* Very Poor: You were very disappointed with the service.
	* Poor: You were somewhat disappointed, but it was not a total failure.
	* Average: The service was okay, but not outstanding.
	* Good: The service was satisfactory.
	* Excellent: The service was top-notch.
7. **Additional Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the service, enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Date**: Enter the date you received the service. This will help us track when you received the service and compare it with other feedback.
* **Site**: Enter the address of the site where you received the service. This will help us understand which location provided the service.
* **Team**: Enter the name of the team that provided the service. This will help us identify the specific team that provided the service.
* **Service Type**: Select or enter the type of service you received. This will help us understand what type of service you received.
* **Experience Rating**: Rate your experience on a scale from Very Poor to Excellent. This will help us understand how well the service met your expectations.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback about the service, enter them here. This is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback that may not fit in the other fields.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback. Your input will help us improve our services.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please describe them as clearly as possible in the Additional Comments field.
* If you need to contact someone about your feedback, please provide your contact information (name, email, phone number, etc.) along with your feedback.
