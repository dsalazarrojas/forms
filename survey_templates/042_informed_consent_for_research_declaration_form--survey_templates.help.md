# Informed Consent For Research Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Informed Consent For Research Declaration Form is designed to collect informed consent from research participants. It's essential to fill it out carefully to ensure accurate and valid consent.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out Page 1: `research_declaration_form`.
2. On this page, you'll see a text field where you can enter a declaration about your research.
3. Fill out Page 2: `participant_consent` next, which is also a text field for you to provide your consent.
4. Finally, on Page 3: `research_practices`, enter any additional details about the research practices you're using.
## Tips
- Be thorough and honest when filling out each section.
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **research_declaration_form** (`Page 1`, text, required): Enter a clear declaration about your research, stating the purpose, scope, and any other relevant details.
* **participant_consent** (`Page 2`, text, required): Provide a thorough and informed consent from yourself as a participant. Be specific about what you're consenting to and what you're aware of.
* **research_practices** (`Page 3`, text, required): Share any additional information about the research practices, methodology, or tools used in your study.
