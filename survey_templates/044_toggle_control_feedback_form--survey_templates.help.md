## Toggle Control Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Toggle Control Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback on the toggle control, a user interface element used to toggle between two states. Your feedback will help us improve the control to make it more user-friendly and efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, follow these steps:

1. Rate your experience with the toggle control.
2. Report any bugs or issues you encountered.
3. Describe the device and platform you were using when experiencing the issue.
4. Walk through the steps to reproduce the issue.
5. Describe what you expected to see when interacting with the toggle control.
6. Describe what you actually saw when interacting with the toggle control.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate this toggle control?** (`toggle_control_rating`, Number, Optional): Please rate your experience with the toggle control on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "not at all" and 5 is "very much".
* **Have you found any bugs?** (`bugs_found`, Select Multiple, Optional): If you found any bugs or issues, select the type of bug you encountered. You can choose from "Bug in the toggle control" or "Bug in the surrounding UI". If you didn't find any bugs, select "No bugs".
* **What would you like to improve in this toggle control?** (`feedback`, Note, Optional): Share your thoughts on what you would like to see improved in the toggle control.
* **Which device were you using?** (`device`, Select Multiple, Optional): Please select the device you were using when experiencing the issue. You can choose from "Desktop", "Mobile", "Tablet", or "Other".
* **What platform were you using?** (`platform`, Select Multiple, Optional): Please select the platform you were using when experiencing the issue. You can choose from "Windows", "macOS", "iOS", "Android", or "Other".
* **What steps did you take to reproduce the issue?** (`steps`, Note, Optional): Describe the steps you took to reproduce the issue.
* **What did you expect to see?** (`expected_result`, Note, Optional): Describe what you expected to see when interacting with the toggle control.
* **What did you actually see?** (`actual_result`, Note, Optional): Describe what you actually saw when interacting with the toggle control.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible with your answers to provide valuable feedback.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve the toggle control.
