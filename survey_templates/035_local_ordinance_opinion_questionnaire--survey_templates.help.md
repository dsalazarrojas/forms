# local-ordinance-opinion-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and suggestions on local ordnances from the public. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form with your thoughts and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your contact information: Please fill out the contact information section with your First Name, Last Name (if applicable), Email, and Phone Number.
2.  Answer the question: In the question field, type in your opinion or question regarding the local ordnance.
3.  Add any additional comments: If you'd like to provide further details or context, please use the comment field.
4.  Confirm your zip code: Enter your zip code to ensure we can direct your feedback to the correct location.
5.  Review and submit: Double-check your information and submit the form to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, **required**): This field is used to collect your basic contact information. You will be required to fill out this field as it's a crucial part of our feedback system.
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): This field is used to collect your first name for contact purposes.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **optional**): If you'd like to provide your last name for contact purposes, please use this field. However, it's not required.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address here. This will be used to contact you if we need further clarification on your feedback.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, **required**): Please enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or "XXX XXX XXXX". This will be used for further communication if needed.
*   **Question** (`question`, text, **required**): Type in your opinion or question about the local ordnance. Be as specific or general as you'd like, but try to keep it concise.
*   **Comment** (`comment`, text, **optional**): If you have more to say or need to provide additional context, use this field for further details.
*   **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, **required**): Enter your zip code to ensure that your feedback is directed to the correct location.
*   **Address** (`address_2`, text, **required**): This field is used for additional address details, if you'd like to provide them.
