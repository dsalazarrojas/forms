# Online Course Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Course Feedback Questionnaire is a form designed to collect feedback from students on their experience with an online course. It helps course creators understand what aspects of the course worked well and what needs improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional questions to the best of your ability.
- Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* This is just a title for the form. You don't need to enter any information here.
### Student Name
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the feedback.
### Your feedback
* **Your feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Write your general thoughts on the course. This is a good place to mention anything that you think is important for the course creators to know.
### Rating
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your experience with the course on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
### More feedback
* **More feedback** (`page_2`, `text`, optional): If you have more thoughts or comments on the course, enter them here.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted by the course creators for further discussion.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by the course creators for further discussion.
### Additional feedback
* **Additional feedback** (`page_3`, `note`, optional): This is a note field where you can write any additional comments or suggestions you have.
### What did you like best about the course?
* **What did you like best about the course?** (`question_1`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the options below that best describes what you liked most about the course.
  - Instructor
  - Materials
  - Support team
  - Learning pace
  - Other
### What did you like least about the course?
* **What did you like least about the course?** (`question_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all that apply from the options below that best describes what you liked least about the course.
  - Instructor
  - Materials
  - Support team
  - Learning pace
  - Other
### Would you recommend this course?
* **Would you recommend this course?** (`question_3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the options below that best describes your answer.
  - Yes
  - No
