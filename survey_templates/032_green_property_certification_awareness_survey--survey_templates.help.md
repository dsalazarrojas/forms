# Green Property Certification Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gauge the awareness of green certification categories, perceived importance and benefits of green certification, and familiarity with the certification process. It is designed to gather information about the current state of green property certification and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply navigate through the pages of questions, and for each question, select your answers from the provided options. If a question allows for a free-text response, provide a brief description of your answer. Ensure to fill in all required fields and submit the form once you have finished.
