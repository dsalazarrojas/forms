# Renewable Energy Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renewable Energy Policy Survey is a form designed to collect opinions and perceptions from individuals on renewable energy policies. It is used to gather data in a research or academic context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the field labeled "first_name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "last_name".
3. Provide your organization or affiliation in the field labeled "organization".
4. Enter your email address in the field labeled "email".
5. If you have a phone number, enter it in the field labeled "phone_number". 
6. Select one option from the list provided for "select_one". 
7. Select one or more options from the list provided for "select_multiple". 
8. If applicable, select a date for "date" or enter a time for "time". 
9. Leave a note or comment in the field "note" if you have any additional information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **organization** (text, required): Enter the name of your organization or affiliation.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it.
* **select_one** (select_one, required): Choose one option from the list to express your opinion on renewable energy policies.
* **select_multiple** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options from the list to express your opinion on renewable energy policies.
* **date** (date, optional): If applicable, select a date that is relevant to the context of the survey.
* **time** (time, optional): If applicable, select a time that is relevant to the context of the survey.
* **note** (note, optional): Leave a note or comment if you have any additional information to share.
