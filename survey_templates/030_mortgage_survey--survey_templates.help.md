# Mortgage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their property and loan details for financial or real estate purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Fill in the relevant information for each field, and make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Info** (Property Info, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about the property being mortgaged. Please enter the address and any other relevant details about the property.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect contact information for the user, including their name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Loan Info** (Loan Info, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about the loan, including the loan amount and term. Please enter the relevant details about the loan.
* **Credit Score** (Credit Score, number, required/optional): This field is used to collect the user's credit score. Please enter a valid credit score.
* **Income** (Income, number, required/optional): This field is used to collect the user's income. Please enter a valid income amount.
* **Assets** (Assets, number, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about the user's assets. Please enter a valid asset amount.
* **Property Location** (Property Location, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about the property's location. Please enter the address or location of the property.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect the user's contact phone number. Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): This field is used to collect the user's email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Loan Amount** (Loan Amount, number, required/optional): This field is used to collect the loan amount. Please enter a valid loan amount.
* **Loan Term** (Loan Term, number, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about the loan term. Please enter a valid loan term.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select, required/optional): This field is used to select the payment method for the loan. Please select a valid payment method (Cash, Check, or Wire Transfer).
* **Payment Date** (Payment Date, date, required/optional): This field is used to collect the payment date for the loan. Please enter a valid payment date.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all entered information for accuracy.
* If a field is marked as "required", please make sure to fill it in.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the form administrator for clarification.
