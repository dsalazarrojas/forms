# DAT Trainee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is meant to help you complete the DAT Trainee Survey with ease. The survey is designed to gather your opinions and experiences regarding the program, allowing us to understand what works well and what can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your response.
3. For open-ended questions, provide a brief but detailed answer.
4. Make sure to complete all required questions (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to the DAT Trainee Survey** (Page 1, Text): This is the introduction page of the survey.
* **What was your overall impression of the program?** (Page 1, Text): Share your general feeling about the program. Be as specific as possible.
* **How likely are you to recommend this program to a friend or colleague?** (Page 1, Select One): Choose the option that best describes your likelihood of recommending the program.
	+ Strongly Agree: You would definitely recommend the program.
	+ Somewhat Agree: You would probably recommend the program.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about recommending the program.
	+ Somewhat Disagree: You would not recommend the program.
	+ Strongly Disagree: You would strongly advise against recommending the program.
* **What aspects of the program did you find most valuable or useful?** (Page 1, Text): Mention the specific aspects that you found helpful or useful.
* **What aspects of the program did you find least valuable or confusing?** (Page 1, Text): Share the aspects that you found less helpful or confusing.
* **How would you rate the quality of the instructors and facilitators?** (Page 1, Number): Evaluate the quality of the instructors and facilitators on a scale.
* **How would you rate the quality of the materials and resources provided?** (Page 1, Select Multiple): Choose the options that best describe the quality of the materials and resources.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **How would you rate the overall organization and logistics of the program?** (Page 1, Time): Evaluate the organization and logistics of the program on a scale.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving the program?** (Page 1, Text): Provide any ideas or suggestions you have for improving the program.
* **How likely are you to apply the knowledge and skills gained from this program in your work or personal life?** (Page 1, Select Multiple): Choose the options that best describe your likelihood of applying the knowledge.
	+ Very Likely
	+ Likely
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Unlikely
	+ Not at All Likely
* **Have you attended any other programs or training events in the past year? If so, which ones?** (Page 1, Text): Share any other programs or training events you attended in the past year.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback about the program?** (Page 1, Text): Add any additional comments or feedback about the program.
* **Dat Trainee Survey Page 2** (Page 2, Text): This is the second page of the survey. Please proceed to the next question.
* **Dat Trainee Survey Page 3** (Page 2, Text): This is the third page of the survey. Please provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Quality of Program Evaluation** (Page 3, Date): This question is likely not applicable to this form.

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML fields and is intended to provide a general understanding of the form's purpose and completion process.
