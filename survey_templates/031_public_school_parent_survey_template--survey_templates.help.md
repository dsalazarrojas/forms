# Public School Parent Survey Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from parents about their experiences and perceptions of their child's school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Please provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers where possible.
3. If you're unsure about a question or need clarification, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Experience** (`school_experience`, text, **required**): Please describe your overall experience at your child's school. What do you like or dislike about the school?
* **Teacher Interaction** (`teacher_interaction`, text, optional): How often do you interact with your child's teachers? Do you feel like you can get in touch with them easily?
* **Parent Involvement** (`parent_involvement`, text, optional): Are you actively involved in your child's education? If so, what activities do you participate in?
* **Teacher Parent Communication** (`teacher_parent_communication`, text, optional): How would you rate the communication between you and your child's teachers?
* **Student Performance** (`student_performance`, text, optional): What are your thoughts on your child's academic performance at school?
* **School Fees** (`school_fees`, number, **required**): What is your perception of the fees charged by the school? Are they reasonable or exorbitant?
* **School Environment** (`school_environment`, text, optional): What do you think about the school's physical environment, such as the cleanliness and condition of the facilities?
* **Transportation** (`transportation`, text, optional): How do you feel about the school's transportation services?
* **School Security** (`school_security`, text, optional): Do you feel safe and secure while on the school premises?
* **School Facilities** (`school_facilities`, text, optional): Are the school's facilities adequate for your child's needs?
* **Parent Expectations** (`parent_expectations`, text, optional): What do you expect from the school in terms of academic and personal development?
* **School Events** (`school_events`, text, optional): How often do you feel informed about school events and activities?
* **School Climate** (`school_climate`, text, optional): What's your perception of the school's atmosphere and culture?
* **School Governance** (`school_governance`, text, optional): Do you feel engaged in the school's governance and decision-making processes?
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, text, optional): Do you feel supported by your child's teachers?
* **Student Support** (`student_support`, text, optional): Are you aware of the support services available to students at the school?
* **Student Services** (`student_services`, text, optional): How do you feel about the extracurricular activities and services offered by the school?
* **School Culture** (`school_culture`, text, optional): What's your perception of the school's culture and values?
* **School Image** (`school_image`, text, optional): Do you feel proud to be associated with the school?
* **School Identity** (`school_identity`, text, optional): Do you feel connected to the school's values and mission?
* **School Mission** (`school_mission`, text, optional): Do you feel that the school's mission aligns with your expectations?
* **School Vision** (`school_vision`, text, optional): Do you feel that the school's vision for the future is clear and achievable?
* **School Values** (`school_values`, text, optional): Do you feel that the school's values align with your own?
* **School Objectives** (`school_objectives`, text, optional): Do you feel that the school's objectives are realistic and achievable?

Please note that some fields are not required, but providing information is encouraged to help the school better understand your concerns and needs.
