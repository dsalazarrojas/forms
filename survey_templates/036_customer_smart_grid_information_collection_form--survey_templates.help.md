# Customer Smart Grid Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers regarding their smart grid systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions being asked.
2. Answer each question as accurately and completely as possible.
3. Enter your information in the text fields and select your responses from the drop-down menus.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
5. Review your responses before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Smart Grid Information Collection** (`customer_smart_grid_information_collection`, Text, Optional): This field is a text input for any additional information the customer would like to provide about their smart grid system.
* **Contract Type** (`customer_smart_grid_contract_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field allows you to select all the types of contracts you have with your smart grid system. Please select all that apply.
  - Contract Type 1
  - Contract Type 2
  - Contract Type 3
* **Contract Start Date** (`customer_smart_grid_contract_start_date`, Date, Optional): This field is used to enter the start date of your contract with your smart grid system.
* **Contract End Date** (`customer_smart_grid_contract_end_date`, Date, Optional): This field is used to enter the end date of your contract with your smart grid system.
* **Contract Price** (`customer_smart_grid_contract_price`, Number, Optional): This field is used to enter the price of your contract with your smart grid system.
* **Contract Length** (`customer_smart_grid_contract_length`, Number, Optional): This field is used to enter the length of your contract with your smart grid system.
* **Contract Length Unit** (`customer_smart_grid_contract_length_unit`, Select One, Optional): This field is used to select the unit of your contract length. Please select from:
  - Months
  - Years
  - Days
* **Customer Service Request** (`customer_smart_grid_customer_service_request`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field allows you to select all the customer service requests you have made with your smart grid system. Please select all that apply.
  - Customer Service Request 1
  - Customer Service Request 2
  - Customer Service Request 3
* **Customer Service Request Other** (`customer_smart_grid_customer_service_request_other`, Text, Optional): This field is a text input for any additional customer service requests not listed.

Note: Please ensure to fill in all the relevant fields and provide accurate and complete information to help us better serve you.
