# Group Meeting Availability - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a group meeting. It helps to gather details such as meeting location, time zone, start and end times, meeting date, time zone offset, meeting format, and attendees. Providing accurate information will enable efficient meeting planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the meeting location where the meeting will take place.
2. Select the time zone for the meeting from the given options.
3. Enter the start time of the meeting.
4. Enter the end time of the meeting.
5. Select the meeting date.
6. Choose the time zone offset for the meeting (if applicable).
7. Select the meeting format from the available options.
8. List the attendees for the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Location** (text): Please enter the physical location of the meeting. This is a text field where you can type in the address or description of the meeting location.
* **Time Zone** (select_one): Select the time zone for the meeting from the available options. This will help ensure that your meeting time is accurate and consistent across all regions.
* **Start Time** (time): Enter the start time of the meeting in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **End Time** (time): Enter the end time of the meeting in 24-hour format (e.g., 16:00 for 4:00 PM).
* **Meeting Date** (date): Select the date of the meeting in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time Zone Offset** (number): If applicable, enter the time zone offset in minutes (e.g., -5 for EST or -6 for CST).
* **Meeting Format** (select_multiple): Choose the meeting format from the available options to indicate the type of meeting, such as in-person, virtual, or phone-based.
* **Attendees** (text): List the names or email addresses of the attendees for the meeting, separated by commas.
