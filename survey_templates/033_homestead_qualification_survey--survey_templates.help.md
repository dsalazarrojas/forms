# homestead_qualification_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "homestead_qualification_survey" form is designed to collect contact and applicant information for a homestead qualification process. It is intended to be filled out by applicants who wish to be qualified for a homestead.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Contact Information" page with your contact details:
	* Enter your name, phone number, and email address in the required fields.
2. Proceed to the "Contact Information" page and fill out your contact details again, as the "Contact Information" page is duplicated.
3. In the "Applicant Info", "Applicant Phone", "Applicant Phone 2", and "Applicant Phone 3" fields, enter the applicant's personal details.
4. In the "Applicant Email", "Applicant Email 2", and "Applicant Email 3" fields, enter the applicant's email addresses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for communication.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the contact person (this is a duplicate of the previous "Contact Information" page).

### Applicant Info
* **Applicant Name**: Enter the applicant's name.

### Applicant Phone
* **Applicant Phone**: Enter the applicant's phone number.

### Applicant Phone 2
* **Applicant Phone 2**: Enter the applicant's phone number (this is a duplicate field of "Applicant Phone").

### Applicant Email
* **Applicant Email**: Enter the applicant's email address.

### Applicant Email 2
* **Applicant Email 2**: Enter the applicant's email address (this is a duplicate field of "Applicant Email").

### Applicant Phone 3
* **Applicant Phone 3**: Enter the applicant's phone number (this is a duplicate field of "Applicant Phone").

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your application is complete and accurate.
* Double-check your contact and applicant information for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or support team.

Note: The duplicated "Contact Information" page and "Applicant Phone" fields are currently a feature of this form. However, it's recommended to fill out the "Applicant Phone" fields only once.
