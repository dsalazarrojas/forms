# Software Implementation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Implementation Evaluation Form is a crucial tool for internal teams to evaluate their software implementation experience. It helps us understand the successes and challenges faced by teams during the implementation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form honestly and thoroughly.
2.  Make sure you have the required information and permissions to access the form.
3.  Answer each question carefully, and provide as much detail as possible.
4.  Use the provided options for select fields, or type in your answer if it's not listed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Software Implementation** (1, note): This field is just a heading for the form, and is not editable.
*   **Deployment Team** (2, text): Provide the names of the individuals on your team.
*   **Evaluation Criteria** (3, select_multiple): Choose all the criteria that apply to your experience:
    *   Option 1
    *   Option 2
    *   Option 3
*   **Implementation Status** (4, select_one): Choose one of the following:
    *   Complete
    *   Partial
    *   Not Started
*   **Effectiveness** (5, number): Rate the effectiveness of the implementation process on a scale of 1-10.
*   **Challenges Faced** (6, text): Describe any challenges you faced during the implementation process.
*   **Lessons Learned** (7, text): Share any valuable lessons you learned during the implementation process.
*   **Recommendations** (8, text): Provide any recommendations for future implementations.
*   **Next Steps** (9, text): Outline the next steps you plan to take after the implementation process.
*   **Additional Comments** (10, text): Add any additional comments or feedback.
*   **Evaluation Date** (11, date): Enter the date of the evaluation.
*   **Evaluation Time** (12, time): Enter the time of the evaluation.
*   **Email** (13, email): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (14, text): Enter your phone number.
*   **Name** (15, text): Enter your name.
*   **Department** (16, text): Enter your department name.
*   **Job Title** (17, text): Enter your job title.
*   **Team Members** (18, select_multiple): Choose all team members involved:
    *   Member 1
    *   Member 2
    *   Member 3
    *   Member 4
    *   Member 5
*   **Team Member Comments** (19, text): Add any comments about team members.
*   **Team Lead** (20, select_one): Choose your team lead:
    *   Team Lead 1
    *   Team Lead 2
*   **Team Lead Comments** (21, text): Add any comments about the team lead.
*   **Project Manager** (22, select_one): Choose your project manager:
    *   Project Manager 1
    *   Project Manager 2
*   **Project Manager Comments** (23, text): Add any comments about the project manager.
*   **IT Manager** (24, select_one): Choose your IT manager:
    *   IT Manager 1
    *   IT Manager 2
*   **IT Manager Comments** (25, text): Add any comments about the IT manager.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out the form honestly and thoroughly.
*   Use the provided options for select fields, or type in your answer if it's not listed.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the team for assistance.
