# Presentation Tools Comparison Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Presentation Tools Comparison Survey is a tool for users to share their experiences with presentation tools. We use this information to improve our knowledge on presentation tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the slide types that you've used in your presentation.
2. Enter the tools you've used in the 'Tools Used' field.
3. Rate the tool in the 'Rating Tool' field, using 'Good' or 'Fair' for your response.
4. Provide any additional comments in the 'Rating Comment' field.
5. Rate your experience with the tool in the 'Ease of Use' field, using a number from 1-10.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with the tool in the 'Overall Satisfaction' field, using a number from 1-10.
7. Enter the name of the tool you used in the 'Tool Name' field.
8. Enter the time you spent using the tool in the 'Rating Time' field.
9. Enter any comments or feedback in the 'Comments' field.
10. Enter your email address in the 'Email' field for contact purposes.
11. Enter your phone number in the 'Phone' field for contact purposes.
12. Enter the category of the tool in the 'Category' field.
13. Enter the name of the output file in the 'Output File' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Slide Type (Select Multiple)**: Select all the slide types that you've used in your presentation.
* **Tools Used (Text)**: Enter the tools you've used in your presentation. You can enter as many tools as you like.
* **Rating Tool (Select One)**: Rate the tool you used as 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **Rating Comment (Text)**: Provide any additional comments about your experience with the tool.
* **Ease of Use (Number)**: Rate your experience with the tool using a number from 1-10, where 1 is very hard to use and 10 is very easy.
* **Overall Satisfaction (Number)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the tool using a number from 1-10, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.
* **Tool Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the tool you used in your presentation.
* **Rating Time (Time)**: Enter the time you spent using the tool in your presentation.
* **Comments (Note)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the tool.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool (Text)**: Enter the tool that was assigned to you for your presentation.
* **Category (Text)**: Enter the category of the tool you used.
* **Output File (Text)**: Enter the name of the output file you created with the tool.
