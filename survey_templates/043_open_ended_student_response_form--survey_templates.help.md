# Open Ended Student Response Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Ended Student Response Form is designed to collect student responses to an open-ended question. This form is meant to be used by students to provide their thoughts, opinions, or answers to a specific question.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email (if you want to be contacted or if it's required by your institution).
4. Read the question carefully and provide your response in the "Answer" field.
5. Click "Submit" to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name:
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
### Last Name:
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
### Email:
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if required or if you want to be contacted).
* Note: This field is optional, and it's up to you to decide if you want to provide your email address.
### Question:
* **Question**: Read the question carefully and provide your response in the "Answer" field.
### Answer:
* **Answer**: Enter your response to the question.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise response to the question.
* Use your best judgment when answering the question.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, contact the form owner or your institution's support team.
