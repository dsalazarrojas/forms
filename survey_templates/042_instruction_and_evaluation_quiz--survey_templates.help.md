# Instruction And Evaluation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation on the quiz's content, relevance, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the quiz's content and questions.
2. Select all the answers that apply to the "What is the participant doing?" question on page 1.
3. Evaluate the quiz as a whole and select one of the options for the "How would you rate this quiz as a whole?" question on page 2.
4. Enter your final score out of a total score (not provided, as it depends on the quiz's configuration).
5. Provide any additional feedback or comments about the quiz in the text field on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the participant doing?** (`What is the participant doing?`, select_multiple, Optional): This question helps us understand what the participant is doing while taking the quiz. Select all the options that apply to the participant's activities.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **How would you rate this quiz as a whole?** (`How would you rate this quiz as a whole?`, select_one, Optional): This question measures your overall satisfaction with the quiz's content and questions.
	+ Options: Good, Fair
* **What is your final score?** (`What is your final score?`, number, Optional): This question asks for your evaluation of the participant's performance on the quiz.
	+ Note: Enter a numerical value as your final score.
* **Do you have any feedback about this quiz?** (`Do you have any feedback about this quiz?`, text, Optional): This question provides a space for you to share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions about the quiz.
