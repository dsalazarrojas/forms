# e-commerce_chatbot_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather customer feedback on our e-commerce chatbot's performance. We value your opinion and use your feedback to improve the chatbot's functionality and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Enter your email address and/or phone number if you'd like to receive more information or support.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Section** (`form_section`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have about your interaction with the chatbot.
* **Rate our chatbot** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate the chatbot's performance on a scale from 1 to 5. Please be honest!
* **What did you like or dislike about the chatbot?** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about what worked well or didn't work well for you.
* **Your email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive more information or support from us.
* **Your phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you directly.
* **Do you have any feature suggestions?** (`suggest_feature`, text, optional): Share any ideas you may have to improve the chatbot's functionality.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to send your feedback to us.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* We value your opinion and use it to improve the chatbot's performance.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to contact us directly.
