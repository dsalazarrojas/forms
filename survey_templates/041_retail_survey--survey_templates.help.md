# Retail Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Survey is designed to collect data from customers about retail stores. This data will be used to analyze store performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the store you are filling out the survey for from the drop-down menu for "Store Manager".
2. Provide the store's customer information on the "Customer Information" page.
3. Answer the questions on the "Customer Survey" page.
4. On the "Store Details" page, provide the store's contact information and hours of operation.
5. Answer the questions on the "Manager Feedback" page.
6. Enter the store's performance metrics on the "Store Performance" page.
7. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_details`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's customer information, including any relevant details about the customers.
* **Customer Survey** (`customer_survey`, `text`, not required):
	+ Answer a set of questions about the store's customer service.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's phone number.
* **Store Manager** (`store_manager`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	+ Select the store manager(s) responsible for the store.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, `time`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's hours of operation.
* **Store Days** (`store_days`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ Select the days the store operates.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's name.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's phone number.
* **Store Email** (`store_email`, `email`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's email address.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's location.
* **Store Type** (`store_type`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ Select the type of store (e.g. clothing, electronics, etc.).
* **Store Size** (`store_size`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's size.
* **Store Customers** (`store_customers`, `number`, not required):
	+ Enter the number of customers per day.
* **Manager Feedback** (`store_manager_survey`, `time`, not required):
	+ Enter the store manager's feedback on the store's performance.
* **Customers per day** (`store_customers_per_day`, `number`, not required):
	+ Enter the number of customers per day.
* **Store Sales** (`store_sales`, `number`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's sales metrics.
* **Store Profit** (`store_profit`, `number`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's profit metrics.
* **Store Inventory** (`store_inventory`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's inventory status.
* **Store Discounts** (`store_discounts`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's discount offers.
* **Store Coupons** (`store_coupons`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's coupon offers.
* **Store Giftcards** (`store_giftcards`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's giftcard offers.
* **Store Referrals** (`store_referrals`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's referral policies.
* **Store Website** (`store_website`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter the store's website.
* **Store Comments** (`store_comments`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter any additional comments about the store.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can submit the form with any relevant information you have. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your supervisor for guidance.
