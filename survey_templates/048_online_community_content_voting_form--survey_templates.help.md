# Online Community Content Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Community Content Voting Form is designed to gather feedback from community members on content submitted to the online community. This form helps moderators and administrators to understand user preferences and opinions on various content items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information:
	* Enter the title of the content item you are voting on in the "Title" field.
	* Enter a description or any other relevant information about the content in the "Content" field.
2. If desired, add a rating for the content item.
3. Choose your vote by selecting the corresponding option(s) in the "Vote" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email" field for identification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter**: A field to enter the name of the person submitting the content.
* **Content**: A field to enter a description or any other relevant information about the content.
* **Rating**: A field to rate the content item, with the options being a number that indicates your level of agreement or preference.
* **Vote**: A field to express your opinion on the content, with options being "Yes" and "No".
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address for identification purposes, which will be kept confidential.

## Tips
* Please be honest and sincere when providing feedback through this form.
* Make sure to provide as much information as possible to help moderators and administrators make informed decisions.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the online community moderators.

Note: The form fields are not necessarily in order of importance, but rather, they are in the order they appear in the original YAML.
