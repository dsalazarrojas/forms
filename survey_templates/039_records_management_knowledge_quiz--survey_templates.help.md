# Records Management Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a quiz designed to assess your understanding of records management concepts and principles.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this quiz, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Enter your answers in the text box provided for each question.
3. Make sure to complete all questions, as some may be required (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Knowledge**: A short paragraph describing your understanding of records management concepts and principles. This is an open-ended question, so feel free to provide as much or as little information as you like. (*)
* **Feedback**: A text box where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about the quiz or records management in general. This is an optional question, so feel free to skip it if you'd like. (Optional)
* **Quiz Results**: This is where you'll find your quiz results once you've completed all questions. You will not need to fill in any information for this field. (Not required, displayed for informational purposes only)

## Tips

* If you have any questions or need clarification on a question, feel free to ask.
* Please provide specific examples or examples when answering questions where possible.
* You can take as much time as needed to complete the quiz, but keep in mind that it's designed to be a quick assessment.
