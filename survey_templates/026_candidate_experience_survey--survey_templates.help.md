# Candidate Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Candidate Experience Survey is a crucial step in our hiring process, allowing us to gather valuable feedback from candidates and make data-driven decisions to improve the overall experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. For select_one and select_one questions, choose the option that best describes your experience.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, use the text fields to share them.
4. Submission Date is automatically filled in by the system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Position Applied For (id: 2)**:
	+ Please provide the title of the position you applied for.
	+ This information will help us understand which role you were interested in.
* **Recruiter Name (id: 3)**:
	+ Share the name of the primary contact person you interacted with during the hiring process.
	+ This helps us know who was involved in communicating with you.
* **Overall experience with our hiring process (id: 4)**:
	+ Rate your overall experience with our hiring process using the scale below:
		- Poor
		- Average
		- Good
		- Very Good
		- Excellent
* **Ease of applying for the position (id: 5)**:
	+ Indicate how easy or difficult you found the initial application form:
		- Very Difficult
		- Difficult
		- Neutral
		- Easy
		- Very Easy
* **Where did you find the job posting (id: 6)**:
	+ Select the source where you saw the job posting:
		- LinkedIn
		- Indeed
		- Company Website
		- Employee Referral
		- Recruitment Agency
		- Other
* **Clarity of information provided about the role (id: 8)**:
	+ Rate how well we explained the job requirements and expectations:
		- Very Poor
		- Poor
		- Average
		- Good
		- Excellent
* **Timeliness of communication from the recruiter (id: 9)**:
	+ Rate how responsive the recruiter was to your queries and communication:
		- Very Poor
		- Poor
		- Average
		- Good
		- Excellent
* **Did you feel well-informed throughout the process (id: 10)**:
	+ Indicate whether you felt informed about the hiring process and expectations:
		- True
		- False
		- Neutral
* **Professionalism of the interviewers (id: 12)**:
	+ Rate the professionalism of the interviewers you interacted with:
		- Very Poor
		- Poor
		- Average
		- Good
		- Excellent
* **Relevance of the interview questions (id: 13)**:
	+ Rate how well the interview questions matched the job requirements:
		- Very Poor
		- Poor
		- Average
		- Good
		- Excellent
* **Comfort during the interview process (id: 14)**:
	+ Rate how comfortable you felt during the interview:
		- Very Uncomfortable
		- Uncomfortable
		- Neutral
		- Comfortable
		- Very Comfortable
* **One thing we could improve in our hiring process (id: 16)**:
	+ Share any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve:
		- Be as specific and concrete as possible.
* **One thing you liked most about your experience (id: 17)**:
	+ Share something positive about your experience:
		- Be as specific and concrete as possible.
* **Likelihood of recommending our company to others (id: 18)**:
	+ Indicate how likely you are to recommend our company:
		- 1 to 10 scale
* **Would you consider applying to us again in the future (id: 19)**:
	+ Indicate if you would consider applying to our company again:
		- True
		- False
		- Undecided
* **Submission Date (id: 20)**:
	+ This field is automatically filled in by the system and shouldn't be edited.

By following this guide, you'll be able to provide valuable feedback and help us improve the candidate experience. Thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts!
