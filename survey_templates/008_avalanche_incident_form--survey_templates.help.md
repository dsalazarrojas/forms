# Avalanche Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Avalanche Incident Form is a crucial tool for recording and reporting avalanche incidents. This form helps gather essential information that aids in understanding the circumstances surrounding the incident, allowing for better decision-making and analysis processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (marked as "required") to ensure that your report is complete and accurate.
2. Enter your full name and email address to be contacted by the response team if necessary.
3. Provide details about the incident, including the date, time, location, and any relevant information about the avalanche.
4. Select the correct options for the avalanche size, type, trigger mechanism, and type of injuries or fatalities.
5. List any rescue equipment used during the response.
6. Write a detailed description of the incident, including any notable circumstances or events.
7. Report the current weather conditions at the time of the incident, if known.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Observer Full Name** (`observer_name`): Your full name.
* **Observer Email Address** (`observer_email`): Your email address. Please ensure it's accurate so that the response team can contact you if necessary.
* **Observer Phone Number** (`observer_phone`): Your phone number. Although optional, it's recommended to provide this information for prompt response.
* **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`): The date of the avalanche incident. Use the date format as per your local region's standard date format.
* **Time of Incident** (`incident_time`): The approximate time of the avalanche incident. Select a time format that suits your needs (HH:MM AM/PM or 24-hour format).
* **Location of Incident** (`incident_location`): The location where the avalanche occurred. Provide the name of the mountain, peak, or basin if available.
* **Avalanche Size Rating** (`avalanche_size`): Select the rating that best describes the scale of the avalanche. This helps responders estimate the severity of the incident.
* **Type of Avalanche** (`avalanche_type`): Identify the type of avalanche that occurred. Choose from the options provided (Dry Slab, Wet Slab, Loose Snow (Dry), Loose Snow (Wet), Glide Avalanche).
* **Trigger Mechanism** (`trigger_mechanism`): Describe the event or activity that triggered the avalanche. Choose from the options (Natural, Human, Remote, Explosive, Cornice Fall).
* **Number of People Involved** (`people_involved`): Report the number of people caught or buried during the incident.
* **Injuries or Fatalities** (`injury_status`): Indicate the status of injuries or fatalities reported. Choose from the options (No injuries reported, Injuries sustained (Minor), Injuries sustained (Severe), Fatalities reported).
* **Rescue Equipment Used** (`rescue_equipment`): List all the rescue equipment used during the response. Select all that apply from the options (Beacon (Transceiver), Shovel, Probe, Recco Reflector, Avalanche Airbag, Professional Rescue Team).
* **Incident Summary and Description** (`incident_summary`): Provide a detailed account of the incident, including notable circumstances or events.
* **Current Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`): Report the current weather conditions at the time of the incident, if known. Choose from the options (Clear and Sunny, Overcast, Light Snowing, Heavy Snowing, High Winds, Raining).
