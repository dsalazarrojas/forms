# Pet Sitter Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experience with a pet sitter. The feedback is used to help the business understand how well the pet sitter meets the expectations of its clients and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field. The form is divided into several questions that ask for ratings and feedback about the pet sitter's service. You can answer each question by selecting one of the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How likely are you to recommend the pet sitter** (1)
	+ This question asks you to rate how likely you are to recommend the pet sitter to others.
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
		- Unacceptable
* **Overall rating of the pet sitter** (2)
	+ This question asks for your overall rating of the pet sitter.
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
		- Unacceptable
* **Feedback** (3)
	+ This question asks for any additional feedback you may have about your experience with the pet sitter.
	+ Please provide a brief but clear description of your experience.
* **How satisfied are you with the service quality** (4)
	+ This question asks for your satisfaction with the service quality of the pet sitter.
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Very satisfied
		- Satisfied
		- Neutral
		- Dissatisfied
		- Very dissatisfied
* **How would you rate the communication** (5)
	+ This question asks for your rating of the pet sitter's communication.
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
		- Unacceptable
* **How would you rate the availability** (6)
	+ This question asks for your rating of the pet sitter's availability.
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Available
		- Almost always available
		- Sometimes available

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and provide clear and concise feedback.
* You can provide any additional feedback or comments in the "Feedback" section.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us.
* This form is for clients who have used the pet sitter's services and wish to provide feedback.
