# Conflict Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences and perceptions of conflict in the workplace. Your responses will help management and HR identify areas where conflict management could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. You may remain anonymous if you wish, but please be aware that some questions are required.
3. Use the provided examples or answers as references if you need help.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Suggestions" or "Additional Comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, Optional): Your name will help us track your responses and provide feedback.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, Required): Please enter your department or division name.
* **Length of Employment** (`tenure`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand the level of experience you have within the company.
* **How Often Do You Experience Workplace Conflict** (`conflict_frequency`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand the frequency of conflicts in your work environment.
* **Most Common Source of Conflict** (`conflict_source`, `select_one`, Required): Please select the primary source of conflict you observe in your workplace.
* **Where Do Conflicts Occur** (`conflict_level`, `select_multiple`, Required): This question helps us understand the level of conflicts in your workplace.
* **How Does Conflict Affect Your Work** (`conflict_impact`, `select_multiple`, Required): This question helps us understand the impact of conflicts on your work.
* **Are You Aware of Conflict Resolution Procedures** (`resolution_awareness`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand your awareness of conflict resolution procedures.
* **How Effective Are Current Resolution Methods** (`resolution_effectiveness`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand your perception of the effectiveness of current resolution methods.
* **How Does Management Handle Conflict** (`management_response`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand how management handles conflicts.
* **HR Support Rating** (`hr_support_rating`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand your perception of HR support for conflict resolution.
* **Are You Comfortable Reporting Conflict** (`comfortable_reporting`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand whether you feel safe raising conflict concerns.
* **Do You Fear Retaliation for Reporting** (`fear_retaliation`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand your concern about retaliation for reporting conflicts.
* **Have You Received Conflict Resolution Training** (`training_received`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand whether you have received formal training on conflict resolution.
* **How Helpful Was the Training** (`training_helpfulness`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand the effectiveness of the training you received.
* **Preferred Method of Conflict Resolution** (`preferred_resolution_method`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand your preferred method of conflict resolution.
* **Would Better Communication Reduce Conflict** (`communication_improvement`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand whether better communication would reduce conflict.
* **Are Conflict Policies Clear** (`policy_clarity`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand the clarity of conflict policies.
* **Additional Resources Needed** (`additional_resources_needed`, `select_multiple`, Required): This question helps us understand what additional resources would be beneficial for conflict management.
* **Would You Participate in Conflict Resolution Training** (`willingness_participate_training`, `select_one`, Required): This question helps us understand your interest in conflict resolution training.
* **Suggestions for Improving Conflict Management** (`suggestions`, `text`, Optional): This question provides an opportunity for you to suggest any additional ideas or improvements for conflict management.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, Optional): This question provides an opportunity for you to share any other feedback or comments you may have.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective in your answers.
- Please use the provided examples as references if you need help.
- If you have any questions, feel free to ask.
- Your input is valuable in helping us improve the workplace environment.
