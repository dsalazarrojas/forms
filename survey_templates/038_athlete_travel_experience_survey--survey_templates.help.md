# Athlete Travel Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from athletes on their experiences while traveling for events or competitions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Athlete Full Name, Destination, Travel Dates, and Primary Purpose of Travel.
2. Assess your experience with transportation, including Punctuality of Departure and Arrival, Comfort Level during travel, and Efficiency of Check-in and Boarding Process.
3. Evaluate your accommodation, including Quality and Cleanliness of Room, Proximity of Accommodation to the Venue, and Quietness and Quality of Environment for Rest.
4. Rate your experience with nutrition and amenities, including Quality and Variety of Food Provided, Availability of Performance-Specific Nutrition, and Access to Recovery or Training Facilities at Hotel.
5. Finally, provide feedback on your Overall Satisfaction with Travel Logistics, and suggest improvements for future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it is written on your athlete identification.
* **Destination**: Enter the city and country where the event or competition took place.
* **Travel Dates**: Enter the dates of the event or competition, e.g., February 1-5, 2026.
* **Primary Purpose of Travel**: Select the category that best describes why you traveled, such as "Competition or Tournament" or "Training Camp".
* **Transport Mode**: Select the primary mode of transportation you used, such as "Flight (Commercial)", "Train", or "Team Bus or Coach".
* **Punctuality of Departure and Arrival**: Rate your experience with the punctuality of departures and arrivals, on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Comfort Level during Travel**: Rate your experience with the comfort level during travel, on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Efficiency of Check-in and Boarding Process**: Rate your experience with the efficiency of check-in and boarding process, on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Hotel or Accommodation Name**: Enter the name of the hotel or accommodation where you stayed.
* **Quality and Cleanliness of Room**: Rate your experience with the quality and cleanliness of your room, on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Proximity of Accommodation to the Venue**: Rate your experience with the proximity of the accommodation to the venue, on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Quietness and Quality of Environment for Rest**: Rate your experience with the quietness and quality of the environment for rest, on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Quality and Variety of Food Provided**: Rate your experience with the quality and variety of food provided, on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Availability of Performance-Specific Nutrition**: Rate your experience with the availability of performance-specific nutrition, on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Access to Recovery or Training Facilities at Hotel**: Rate your experience with access to recovery or training facilities at the hotel, on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Overall Satisfaction with Travel Logistics**: Rate your overall satisfaction with travel logistics, on a scale from 1 (Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
* **What went well on this trip?**: Provide specific feedback on what went well during your trip.
* **What could be improved for next time?**: Provide specific feedback on what could be improved for future events.
* **Describe any issues with luggage or equipment transport**: If you experienced any issues with luggage or equipment transport, describe them in detail.
* **Date of Survey Completion**: Enter the date you are completing this survey.
