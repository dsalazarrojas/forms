# Website Audit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Audit Survey is a tool designed to gather information about your website's functionality and structure, helping identify areas of improvement for a better user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your experience with the website.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible.
3. Submit your survey once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **page_one**: This is the introductory page of the survey, where you can provide any general comments or feedback about your experience with the website.
* **section_one** (Section 1): This section is a free-text area for you to provide any notes or comments about your experience with the website.
* **section_two** (Section 2): In this section, you will be asked to provide the date and time you last visited the website.
* **section_three** (Section 3): This section is for you to report any time spent on the website during your last visit. Please enter the total time in hours and minutes.

Note: The provided YAML metadata does not include any validation or constraints for the date and time fields, so be sure to enter the correct format for these fields.
