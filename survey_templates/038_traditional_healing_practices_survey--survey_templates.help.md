# Traditional Healing Practices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experiences and perceptions of traditional healing practices, such as acupuncture, herbalism, and mindfulness. Your responses will help us better understand the effectiveness and prevalence of these practices and inform the development of programs and resources that support them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the traditional healing methods you have used in the past (page 1).
2. Indicate how often you use each selected method (page 2).
3. Enter the number of years you have been using each selected method (page 3).
4. Choose how often you use each selected method (page 4).
5. Select all the benefits you are aware of for each method (page 5).
6. Choose your level of knowledge about each selected method (page 6).
7. Indicate your perception of the effectiveness of each selected method (page 7).
8. Select your level of trust in each selected method (page 8).
9. Enter your confidence level for each selected method (page 9).
10. Indicate if you are involved in any programs that promote these methods (page 10).
11. Select how much support you receive from your community for each selected method (page 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traditional Healing Methods Used** (`traditional_healing_methods_used`, select_multiple): Choose all the traditional healing methods you have used in the past, such as acupuncture, herbalism, and massage.
* **Frequency of Use** (`frequency_of_use`, number): Enter the number of times you use each selected method in a typical week.
* **Years of Use** (`years_of_use`, number): Enter the number of years you have been using each selected method.
* **How Often Use** (`how_often_use`, select_one): Choose how often you use each selected method, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Awareness of Benefits** (`awareness_of_benefits`, select_multiple): Select all the benefits you are aware of for each method, such as pain relief and stress relief.
* **Knowledge Level** (`knowledge_level`, select_one): Choose your level of knowledge about each selected method, such as high, medium, or low.
* **Perceptions of Effectiveness** (`perceptions_of_effectiveness`, select_one): Indicate your perception of the effectiveness of each selected method, such as very effective, somewhat effective, or not effective.
* **Trust in Methods** (`trust_in_methods`, select_one): Select your level of trust in each selected method, such as a lot, some, or none.
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, number): Enter your confidence level for each selected method.
* **Program Participation** (`program_participation`, select_multiple): Indicate if you are involved in any programs that promote these methods, such as true, false, or not sure.
* **Community Support** (`community_support`, select_one): Select how much support you receive from your community for each selected method, such as a lot, some, or none.
