# Language School Monthly Progress Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language School Monthly Progress Check In form is a tool for language school administrators to track student progress, assess growth, and provide constructive feedback in a structured and standardized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's User Input, if any additional information is required.
2. Enter the student's Student ID to identify them uniquely.
3. Enter the student's name to provide personalization in feedback and assessment.
4. Select the language being studied by the student.
5. Select the student's learning level (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
6. Enter the name of the teacher or instructor who filled out the form.
7. Write a brief note about the student's progress.
8. Enter the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input (<id: 1>)
• **User Input**: A brief description of any additional information about the student's situation.

### Student ID (<id: 2>)
• **Student ID**: A unique identifier for the student to ensure accurate tracking of their progress and feedback.

### Student Name (<id: 3>)
• **Student Name**: Enter the student's name to provide personalization in feedback and assessment.

### Language (<id: 4>)
• **Language**: Select the language being studied by the student (e.g., English, Spanish, French).

### Level (<id: 5>)
• **Level**: Select the student's learning level (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).

### Teacher Name (<id: 6>)
• **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher or instructor who filled out the form.

### Student Progress (<id: 7>)
• **Student Progress**: Write a brief note about the student's progress, including any areas of improvement.

### Teacher Comments (<id: 8>)
• **Teacher Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions for the student.

### Date Submitted (<id: 9>)
• **Date Submitted**: Enter the date on which the form was submitted.

### Time Submitted (<id: 10>)
• **Time Submitted**: Enter the time at which the form was submitted.

## Tips
- Use this form for all students, regardless of their language learning goals and levels.
- Use unique student IDs to avoid data duplication.
- Keep feedback and comments concise and constructive.
- Review and adjust this form as needed to suit your language school's specific needs.
