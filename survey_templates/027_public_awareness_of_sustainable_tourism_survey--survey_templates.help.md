# Public Awareness Of Sustainable Tourism Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights from users on their experiences and perceptions of sustainable tourism practices. It's a tool to help us better understand public awareness and opinions on this topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "Public Awareness Of Sustainable Tourism Survey" title.
2. Select the category that best describes your experience.
3. Provide a brief description of your visit.
4. Enter your contact email address.
5. Choose the locations you visited.
6. Enter the date of your visit.
7. Select the time of your visit.
8. Choose the reason(s) you traveled.
9. Select the sustainable practices you've experienced during your visit.
10. Rate your satisfaction with our sustainable practices.
11. Finally, submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Public Awareness Of Sustainable Tourism Survey** (`survey_title`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which you'll need to fill in.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Please select the category that best describes your experience. You can choose from: Category A, Category B, Category C.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your visit.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the location(s) you visited. You can select multiple locations.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select the time of your visit.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, text, required: false): Choose the reason(s) you traveled.
* **Sustainable Practices** (`sustainable_practices`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the sustainable practices you've experienced during your visit. You can choose multiple options.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with our sustainable practices. You can choose from: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied.

## Tips
- Please be honest and sincere in your feedback.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and share your insights!
