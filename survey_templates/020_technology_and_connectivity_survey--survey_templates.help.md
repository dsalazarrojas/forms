# Technology And Connectivity Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a technology and connectivity survey designed to collect data on individuals' habits and usage patterns related to their devices, internet access, and time spent online.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions based on your personal experiences and habits.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
4. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to complete this survey on behalf of others.
