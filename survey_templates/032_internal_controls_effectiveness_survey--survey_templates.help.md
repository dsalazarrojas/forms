# Internal Controls Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather your feedback on the effectiveness of internal controls within our organization. Your responses will help us identify areas of strength and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions based on your honest experience and perceptions.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best describes your experience or perception.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
