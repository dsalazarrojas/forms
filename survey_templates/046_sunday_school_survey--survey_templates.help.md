# sunday_school_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience at Sunday school. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Complete each section of the survey as honestly as possible.

### Step 2: Answer each question to the best of your ability, even if you're unsure or neutral.

### Step 3: Submit the completed form to provide us with your valuable feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What would you like to talk about**: This is a text field where you can share any thoughts or topics you would like to discuss.
* **How did you feel about last Sunday school**: This question asks about your feelings and emotions related to the last Sunday school event. Choose 'Yes' if you felt positive emotions, 'No' if you felt negative emotions, or leave this field blank if you're unsure.
* **Favorite part of last Sunday school**: This question asks about the aspects of Sunday school that you enjoyed the most. Choose 'Yes' for each part that you liked.
* **Sunday School is**: This question asks about your general opinion about Sunday school. Choose 'Yes' if you think Sunday school is beneficial, 'No' if you don't think it's beneficial, or leave this field blank if you're unsure.
* **Name of favorite teacher**: This is a text field where you can share the name of your favorite Sunday school teacher.
* **Have your parents attended last Sunday school**: This question asks about the presence of your parents at the last Sunday school event. Choose 'Yes' if they attended, 'No' if they didn't.
* **How would you rate last Sunday school**: This question asks about your rating of the last Sunday school event. Choose 'Good', 'Fair', or leave this field blank if you're unsure.
* **What was missing from last Sunday school**: This is a text field where you can share anything that you feel was missing from the last Sunday school event.
* **Do you feel safe attending last Sunday school**: This question asks about your feelings of safety while attending Sunday school. Choose 'Yes' if you feel safe, 'No' if you don't feel safe.
* **Would you recommend last Sunday school to a friend**: This question asks about your willingness to recommend Sunday school to a friend. Choose 'Yes' if you would recommend, 'No' if you wouldn't.
* **Comments**: This is an optional text field where you can share any additional thoughts or comments about Sunday school.

Note: This guide is for reference only, and your feedback is greatly appreciated. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
