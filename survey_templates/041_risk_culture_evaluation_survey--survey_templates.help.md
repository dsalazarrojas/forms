# Risk Culture Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Culture Evaluation Survey is designed to assess the risk culture within your organization. It's essential to understand how risk awareness, reporting, and improvement processes are perceived by employees. This survey will help identify areas of strength and areas where improvement is needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Choose the most suitable response for each question based on your honest opinion.
3. If you're unsure or have questions about a particular question, please ask your supervisor or manager for guidance.
4. Do not repeat your answers if you've already completed this form in the past.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Awareness (Risk Informed)** (`risk_awareness`, `select_one`, required: false): This question assesses how well-informed you are about the risks within the organization. Please select the option that best describes your level of understanding.
* **Risk Reporting (Risk Reporting)** (`risk_reporting`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question evaluates how comfortable you are with reporting risks within the organization. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Improvement Ideas (Improvement Ideas)** (`improvement_ideas`, `note`, required: false): This field is for any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the risk culture within the organization.
* **Risk Culture Score** (`risk_culture_score`, `number`, required: false): Please enter a number between 1 and 100 that represents your perception of the overall risk culture within the organization.
* **Risk Compliance (Risk Compliance)** (`risk_compliance`, `select_one`, required: false): This question evaluates how well-informed you are about the organization's risk policies and procedures. Please select the option that best describes your level of understanding.
* **Reporting Comfort (Reporting Comfort)** (`reporting_comfort`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question assesses how comfortable you are with reporting risks within the organization. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Improvement Action (Improvement Action)** (`improvement_action`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is for any plans or actions you have for improving the risk culture within the organization.
* **Risk Informed Percentage (Risk Informed Percentage)** (`risk_informed_percentage`, `number`, required: false): Please enter a number between 0 and 100 that represents the percentage of risks you're aware of within the organization.
* **Reporting Confidence (Reporting Confidence)** (`risk_reporting_confidence`, `number`, required: false): Please enter a number between 0 and 100 that represents your confidence in reporting risks within the organization.
