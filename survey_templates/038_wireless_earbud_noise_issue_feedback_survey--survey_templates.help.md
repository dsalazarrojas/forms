# Wireless Earbud Noise Issue Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from customers experiencing noise-related issues with their wireless earbuds. It will help us understand the type of earbuds used, the frequency and location of the issue, and any troubleshooting steps taken or recommended.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide detailed and accurate feedback, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address if you'd like to be contacted for further clarification or follow-up questions.
2. Enter the date when you first noticed the issue with your earbuds.
3. Briefly describe the issue you're experiencing.
4. Select the type of earbuds you are using (Noise Cancelling or Earbuds).
5. Choose the frequency of the issue (e.g., 1-5, 6-10, etc.).
6. Indicate the type of issue you are experiencing (Noisy, Intermitent Noise, or Constant Noise).
7. Specify the location of the issue (Left Earbud, Right Earbud, or Both Earbuds).
8. Share any troubleshooting steps you have taken or would recommend (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): This is your name, which we use to acknowledge your feedback and follow up with you if needed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further clarification or follow-up questions.
* **Product Issue Date** (`product_issue_date`, date, optional): This is the date when you first noticed the issue with your earbuds.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): Describe the problem you're experiencing with your earbuds in a few words.
* **Wireless Bud Type** (`wireless_bud_type`, select_multiple, required): Please select the type of earbuds you are using (Noise Cancelling or Earbuds).
* **Issue Frequency** (`issue_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you experience the issue (e.g., 1-5, 6-10, etc.).
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of issue you are experiencing (Noisy, Intermitent Noise, or Constant Noise).
* **Issue Location** (`issue_location`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the location of the issue (Left Earbud, Right Earbud, or Both Earbuds).
* **Troubleshooting Steps** (`troubleshooting_steps`, text, optional): Share any steps you've taken or recommend to resolve the issue.
