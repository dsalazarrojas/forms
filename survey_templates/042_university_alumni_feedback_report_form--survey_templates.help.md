# University Alumni Feedback Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from university alumni about their experiences and current status after graduation. It's a tool for the university to gather insights and improve its services for future graduates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in their respective fields.
2. Provide a valid **Email** address that is up-to-date.
3. Fill in your **Graduation Year**.
4. Select the month you graduated in the **Graduation Month** field.
5. Enter your graduation time in the **Graduation Day** field in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2 PM).
6. Describe your **Current Occupation**.
7. Provide the name of your **Current Company**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address that is up-to-date and can be used to contact you.
* **Graduation Year**: The year you graduated from the university.
* **Graduation Month**: Select the month you graduated from the university.
* **Graduation Day**: Enter the time you graduated in 24-hour format.
* **Current Occupation**: A brief description of your current job or occupation.
* **Current Company**: Enter the name of your current company or organization.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the university's support team.
