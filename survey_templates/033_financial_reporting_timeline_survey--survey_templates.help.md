# Financial Reporting Timeline Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about financial reporting frequencies, deadlines, and other relevant aspects of your financial close process. The data collected will help us better understand your financial close process and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand each question before starting.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the relevant options for each question, using the dropdown menus or typing in the text fields as needed.
4. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

* **Other (if other, please specify)**: If you have a different reporting frequency not listed in the options, please specify it in this text field.
