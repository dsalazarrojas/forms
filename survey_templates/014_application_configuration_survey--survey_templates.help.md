# Application Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback on the application's configuration and identify areas for improvement. Your input will help us refine the application and provide a better user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question based on your actual experience with the application.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
4. Do not provide speculative or hypothetical answers.
5. If you have a recurring issue, submit a new response for each occurrence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Your name or alias to help us understand your responses.
* **Application name** (`app_name`, `text`, required: true): The name of the application you're configuring. This will help us associate your feedback with the correct application.
* **Current configuration summary** (`current_config`, `text`, required: false): Describe the current settings and features you are using in the application.
* **Desired changes** (`desired_changes`, `text`, required: true): List the changes you think would improve the application's usability and functionality.
* **Difficulty level** (`difficulty_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how difficult you find the application's configuration process.
	+ Very easy
	+ Easy
	+ Moderate
	+ Difficult
	+ Very difficult
* **Most important features** (`important_features`, `text`, required: false): List the top 3 features that are crucial for the application's success.
* **Integration requirements** (`integration_requirements`, `text`, required: false): If there are any external systems that need to integrate with the application, list them here.
* **Contact for follow-up** (`contact_for_follow_up`, `email`, required: false): Provide your email address if you'd like us to follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your overall satisfaction with the application.
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Unsatisfied
	+ Very unsatisfied
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Any other feedback or thoughts about the application.

## Tips
* Be detailed and honest in your answers.
* Provide specific examples or scenarios to support your responses.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to us directly.
* Your input is invaluable for improving the application, and we appreciate your time and effort.
