# School Survey For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Survey For Parents is designed to gather essential information about how parents are involved in their child's education. It helps the school understand how parents communicate with teachers, their meeting frequency, and volunteering habits.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully, and make sure you answer all the questions accurately.
2. Please answer the questions based on your personal experience as a parent.
3. Select the options that best describe your relationship with the school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Involvement** (`Parent Involvement`, required, select_one): This question asks about your level of involvement with your child's school activities. Please choose one of the options: Parent is very involved, Parent is somewhat involved, or Parent is not involved.
* **How Do Parents Communicate With Your School** (`how_do_parents_communicate_with_your_school`, required, select_multiple): This question asks how you usually communicate with your child's school. Select all the applicable options: Email, Phone, Text, or In-person.
* **How Frequently Do Parents Meet with Your Teacher** (`how_frequently_do_parents_meet_with_your_teacher`, required, number): Please indicate how often you meet with your child's teacher, using the numbers provided as a guide.
* **Do Parents Volunteer In Your Child's Classroom?** (`do_parents_volunteer_in_your_child_s_classroom`, required, select_one): This question asks if you volunteer in your child's classroom. Please choose Yes or No.
* **If Yes, How Frequently Do Parents Volunteer?** (`if_yes_how_frequently`, required, number): If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, please indicate how often you volunteer in your child's classroom.
* **How Often Do Parents Attend Parent-Teacher Meetings?** (`how_often_do_parents_attend_parent_teacher_meetings`, required, select_multiple): This question asks about your attendance frequency at parent-teacher meetings. Select all applicable options: Regularly, Occasionally, or Rarely.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional, note): Feel free to add any additional comments or thoughts you have about your relationship with the school.
* **Email** (`Email`, optional, email): Please provide your email address if you would like to stay in touch with the school.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, optional, text): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by the school.
* **School Website** (`School Website`, optional, text): Please provide the school's website address if you would like to access additional information.
* **What Do You Think?** (`what_do_you_think`, optional, note): This field is for your personal feedback about the school.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be completed before submission. Optional fields can be left blank if not applicable.
