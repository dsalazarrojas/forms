# Editing Tools Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Editing Tools Assessment form is designed to help departments evaluate their usage and needs of editing tools. It gathers information about the tools and features used by each department, which helps in making informed decisions about resource allocation and optimization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections that correspond to your department.
2. Answer each question according to your department's usage and needs.
3. Make sure to answer each question as accurately as possible.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tools usage across departments**: This section allows you to describe the tools currently being used across various departments. Please provide a brief overview of the tools and their usage.
* **Satisfaction level** (`editing_satisfaction_level`): This question is about your department's satisfaction level with the current editing tools. Rate your satisfaction level on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Features needed** (`features_needed`): Select all the features that your department currently needs or would like to have in the editing tools. You can choose multiple features from the list provided.
* **Training and support needed** (`training_needs`): Indicate the level of training and support your department requires. Choose from "None", "Basic", "Advanced", or "Custom" based on your department's needs.
* **Likert scale** (`tool_likert_scale`): If you need to rate your satisfaction level with the current editing tools, use this scale. Rate your satisfaction level on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Employees in department 1**, **2**, ..., **10** (`department_1_employees`, `department_2_employees`, etc.): These questions are about the number of employees in each department. Please provide the exact number of employees in your department.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when answering each question.
* If you're unsure about a particular feature or tool, feel free to ask for clarification or additional support.
* Make sure to submit your completed form to ensure that your department's needs are taken into account.
