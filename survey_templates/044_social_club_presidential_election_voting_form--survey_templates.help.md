# Social Club Presidential Election Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects demographic information and preferences from social club members for the presidential election process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select the "Submit" button at the bottom of the page.
2.  Enter your first name and last name in the respective text fields.
3.  Enter your email address in the email field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5.  Enter your age in the age field.
6.  Enter your date of birth in the date field.
7.  Enter your time of day in the time field.
8.  Enter any additional notes or comments in the notes field.
9.  Select your favorite colors from the options provided in the "Favorite Colors" field.
10.  Select your position within the club from the options provided in the "Position Held" field.
11.  Finally, click "Submit" again to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
*   **Age** (`age`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter your time of day.
*   **Notes** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
*   **Favorite Color** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your favorite color from the options provided. Options: blue, red, green, yellow.
*   **Favorite Colors** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select one or multiple favorite colors from the options provided. Options: blue, red, green, yellow.
*   **Position Held** (`select_one_required`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your position within the club from the options provided. Options: Member, President, Vice President, Treasurer.
