# Post Session Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback on the effectiveness of sessions, which are conducted to facilitate learning, collaboration, or other objectives. The goal is to identify what went well, what did not go as planned, and what can be improved for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the name of the session that took place.
2. Enter the name of the person who facilitated the session.
3. Choose the date of the session.
4. Enter the time the session took place.
5. Describe the session goals.
6. Describe the session outcomes.
7. Write about what went well during the session.
8. Write about what did not go as planned.
9. Describe next steps for future sessions.
10. Rate your overall satisfaction with the session.
11. Indicate the duration of the session.
12. Provide any additional comments or feedback on the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Name** (1): Enter the name of the session that took place.
* **Facilitator Name** (2): Enter the name of the person who facilitated the session.
* **Date** (3): Choose the date the session occurred.
* **Time** (4): Enter the time the session took place.
* **Session Goals** (5): Describe the objectives or goals for the session.
* **Session Outcomes** (6): Describe the outcomes or results of the session.
* **What Went Well** (7): Write about what went well or effectively during the session.
* **What Did Not Go As Planned** (8): Write about what did not go as planned or was challenging.
* **Next Steps** (9): Describe what steps will be taken for future sessions based on this feedback.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (10): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session using the provided scale.
* **Session Duration** (11): Enter the length of time the session took place.
* **Facilitator Comments** (12): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the session.
