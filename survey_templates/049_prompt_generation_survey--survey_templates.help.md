# prompt_generation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "prompt_generation_survey" form is used to collect information about a prompt, which is a sequence of words used to trigger a response from a system, model, or user. The form helps in gathering details about the prompt, making it easier to understand and process the prompt.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the topic of your prompt.
2. Provide the main question you are trying to answer.
3. Give a brief description of your prompt.
4. Select the category and subcategory of your topic, if applicable.
5. Identify your target audience for the prompt.
6. Specify the target age group of your audience.
7. Choose the type of prompt you are dealing with.
8. Select how often you use this prompt.
9. Add any additional information about your prompt.
10. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the topic of your prompt**: This field allows you to specify the main subject matter of your prompt.
* **What is the main question you are trying to answer**: This field should contain the central query or question that your prompt is trying to address.
* **Describe your prompt in a few words**: Give a brief description of your prompt to provide context.
* **What category does your topic fall under**: Select the category that best describes your topic.
*   *Example*: Select "Option 1" or "Option 2" based on the provided options.
* **What is your subcategory**: If applicable, provide more detailed information about your topic's subcategory.
* **Who is your target audience**: Choose all applicable audience groups for your prompt.
*   *Example*: Select "Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3".
* **What is the target age group of your audience**: Select the age group of your audience.
*   *Example*: Select "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3", or "Option 4".
* **What kind of prompt is this**: Choose the type of prompt you are dealing with.
*   *Example*: Select "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **How often do you use this prompt**: Select how frequently you use this prompt.
*   *Example*: Select "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3", or "Option 4".
* **Do you have any additional information about your prompt**: If you have any further details about your prompt, provide them here.
* **Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form**: This field is not editable.

Note: The options for "What category does your topic fall under", "What kind of prompt is this", "How often do you use this prompt", and "Who is your target audience" may vary and should be selected based on the provided choices. Similarly, the options for "Who is your target audience" and "What is the target age group of your audience" may also vary based on the provided choices.
