# Medical Ethics Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess your understanding and application of medical ethics principles in clinical practice. The questions are meant to be thought-provoking and will help healthcare providers reflect on their own practices and decision-making processes. Please take your time to answer these questions honestly and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and consider a real-life situation that illustrates your answer.
2. Provide a detailed and concise response to each question, explaining your thought process and decision-making process.
3. Be specific about your actions, decisions, and outcomes in your response.
4. Use clear and proper grammar and spelling in your answers.
5. Do not provide generic or hypothetical answers; instead, focus on actual experiences or situations you have faced.
