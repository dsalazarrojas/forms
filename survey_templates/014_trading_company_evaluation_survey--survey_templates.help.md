# Trading Company Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us evaluate our trading company's performance and customer experience. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer each question based on your experience with the company.
3. Use the space provided for additional comments and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: The name of the company you are providing feedback about.
* **What prophets are you looking for in a trading company?**: A brief description of what you are looking for in a trading company.
* **Overall Performance**: Rate the company's overall performance from Very Good, Average, to Poor.
* **Recommendation**: Rate how likely you are to recommend the company to others.
* **Reason for Recommendation**: If you answered "Highly Likely" to recommend, please provide the reason for your recommendation.
* **Customer Service**: If applicable, describe any experiences you have had with the company's customer service team.
* **Product/Service Quality**: If you have used the company's products or services, rate their quality.
* **Reason for Dissatisfaction**: If you are not satisfied with your experience, please provide a brief description of the reason.
* **Contact for Updates**: Would you like to be contacted for updates or follow-up information about the company's services?
* **Additional Feedback**: Any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the company.
* **Back Contact**: Are you available for back contact in case we need more information?
* **Other Comments**: Any other comments or feedback about your experience with the company.
* **Back Contacted**: Has the company contacted you back about your experience?
* **Relationship**: If you have a relationship with the company (e.g., customer, partner, etc.), please describe it.
* **Communication**: If you have had any communication with the company, please describe it.
* **Timeliness**: If you have had any experiences with the company's timeliness (e.g., delivery times, responsiveness), please describe it.
* **Main Reason**: If you were not satisfied, please describe the main reason.
* **Current Status**: If you are currently using or have used the company's services, please describe your current status.
* **Used Company**: Have you used the company's services?
* **Rate Company**: Have you rated the company's services?
* **Main Reason Not Satisfied**: If you are not satisfied, please describe the main reason.
* **Contact for Future**: Are you interested in being contacted for future opportunities?
* **Rate Company Again**: Would you rate the company's services again?
* **Contact for Opportunities**: Are you interested in being contacted for future business opportunities?

Please note that answers to some questions might be optional, but are highly appreciated for our records. Thank you for taking the time to provide us with your feedback!
