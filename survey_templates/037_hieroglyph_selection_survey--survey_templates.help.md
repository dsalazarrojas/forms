# Hieroglyph Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect your preferences and demographics regarding hieroglyph symbols. Your responses will help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the hieroglyph symbol you like best from the list provided.
2. Choose your age group from the list.
3. Select your gender from the list.
4. Choose your highest level of education completed from the list.
5. Enter your profession in the text field.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Select the date of your visit (if applicable).
9. Select the time of your visit (if applicable).
10. Add any comments or notes you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which hieroglyph symbol do you like best? (`hieroglyph_symbol`, select_one, Not Required): Choose the hieroglyph symbol that you find most appealing. This will help us understand your personal preferences.
* **What is your age group? (`age`, select_multiple, Not Required): Select all the age groups that apply to you. This will help us understand your demographics.
* **What is your gender? (`gender`, select_one, Not Required): Select your gender from the list. This will help us understand your demographics.
* **What is your highest level of education completed? (`education_level`, select_one, Not Required): Choose your highest level of education completed from the list. This will help us understand your educational background.
* **What is your profession? (`profession`, text, Not Required): Enter a brief description of your profession or job title. This will help us understand your occupation.
* **What is your email address? (`email`, email, Not Required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you. This will help us reach you with any follow-up questions or surveys.
* **What is your phone number? (`phone`, text, Not Required): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you. This will help us reach you with any urgent matters.
* **Date of visit? (`date_of_visit`, date, Not Required): If applicable, select the date of your visit. This will help us track your visit history.
* **Time of visit? (`time_of_visit`, time, Not Required): If applicable, select the time of your visit. This will help us track your visit history.
* **Any comments or notes? (`note`, note, Not Required): Add any comments or notes you may have. This will help us understand your thoughts and suggestions.
