# Website Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from website visitors to improve the user experience and overall satisfaction with the website. The survey will ask questions about the website's design, functionality, and user experience to help us identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions carefully.
2. Answer each question in the text fields provided.
3. Make sure to complete all pages of the survey, which are indicated by the page numbers at the top of each section.
4. If you have any questions or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page One**: Please answer the questions on this page to get started with your feedback.
* **Page Two**: On this page, please continue providing your feedback about your experience with our website.
* **Page Three**: Thank you for taking the time to complete the survey. This is the last page.
* **Title** (optional): If you'd like to provide a title for your feedback, please enter a short name here.
* **No other fields** are required, but feel free to provide as much feedback as you'd like.
