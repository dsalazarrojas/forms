# Food Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Food Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from customers about their experience with a food establishment. This form collects information about the customer's name, order number, food quality, service level, staff welcoming, food price, environment, food quality score, customer satisfaction, recommendation, date, time, and any additional notes they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your **Customer Name** (1) with your name as it appears on your identification.
* Enter the **Order Number** (2) if you have it. This can be found on your receipt or order confirmation.
* Evaluate the quality of the **Food** you received (3). Please select a rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* Describe the **Service Level** (4) of your experience. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* Select the level of **Staff Welcoming** (5) you experienced. Choose from the options: Very Welcoming, Somewhat Welcoming, or Not Welcoming.
* Enter the price of the **Food** (6) you received.
* Describe the **Environment** (7) of your experience. Choose from the options: Very Clean, Somewhat Clean, or Not Clean.
* Enter a **Food Quality Score** (8) from 1 to 5, indicating how good you thought the food was.
* Rate your **Customer Satisfaction** (9) with the service. Choose from the options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
* Let us know if you would **Recommend** (10) this establishment to others. Choose from the options: True or False.
* Enter the **Date** (11) of your visit, if you know it.
* Enter the **Time** (12) of your visit, if you know it.
* Add any additional **Note** (13) you have about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Order Number (2)**: Enter the number of your order if you have it.
* **Food Quality (3)**: Rate the quality of the food you received on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Service Level (4)**: Choose how good or bad the service was.
* **Staff Welcoming (5)**: Rate how welcoming the staff were to you.
* **Food Price (6)**: Enter the price of the food you received.
* **Environment (7)**: Choose how clean the environment was.
* **Food Quality Score (8)**: Enter a score from 1 to 5, indicating how good you thought the food was.
* **Customer Satisfaction (9)**: Rate how satisfied you were with the service.
* **Would You Recommend (10)**: Let us know if you would recommend this establishment to others.
* **Date (11)**: Enter the date of your visit, if you know it.
* **Time (12)**: Enter the time of your visit, if you know it.
* **Note (13)**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
