# Coronavirus Business Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your business and its reopening status during the COVID-19 pandemic. Your input will help us understand your business's needs and provide targeted support to help you navigate this challenging time.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out this form only if your business is currently open or planning to reopen.
* Please answer each question accurately and in a way that best describes your current situation.
* You can use the options provided for each question as a guide, but feel free to add any additional comments or information if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Business Status:** 
  * **Fully Reopened** (business is fully operational and open to customers)
  * **Partially Reopened** (business is operational, but with reduced hours or services)
  * **Closed** (business is currently closed due to the pandemic)
  * **Not Applicable** (business has closed permanently or is not affected by the pandemic)

  Select the option that best describes your business's current status.

* **2. Reopen Reason:**
  * **Need help from government** (you are seeking financial or other forms of aid to support your business)
  * **Not enough customers** (you are not getting enough customers to sustain your business)
  * **Not enough staff** (you are short-staffed and need help finding or training workers)
  * **Other** (please select this option if you have any other reasons for not reopening)

  Select all options that apply to your business's situation.

* **3. Reopen Help Needed:**
  * **Financial support** (you are looking for grants, loans, or other forms of financial assistance)
  * **Supply chain support** (you need help sourcing or distributing essential goods or services)
  * **Marketing support** (you need help promoting your business or reaching new customers)
  * **Other** (please select this option if you have any other specific needs)

  Select all options that apply to your business's situation.

* **4. Reopening Plans:**
  * **Immediate re-open** (you plan to reopen immediately, within the next few days or weeks)
  * **Short-term re-open** (you plan to reopen within the next few months)
  * **Long-term re-open** (you plan to reopen at a later date, but not within the next few months)
  * **Not planning to re-open** (you have decided not to reopen your business)

  Select the option that best describes your business's reopening plans.

* **5. Reopen Date:** 
  * Enter the date you plan to reopen your business (if known)

* **6. Reopen Time:**
  * Enter the time you plan to reopen your business (if known)

* **7. Comments:**
  * Add any additional comments or information about your business or reopening plans.

* **8. Contact Number:**
  * Enter the phone number of the person responsible for your business

* **9. Email:**
  * Enter the email address of the person responsible for your business

* **10. Business Address:**
  * Enter the physical address of your business

* **11. Owner Name:**
  * Enter the name of the business owner or representative

* **12. Owner Contact:**
  * Enter any additional contact information for the owner or representative

* **13. Business Type:**
  * **Restaurant** (business is a restaurant)
  * **Bar** (business is a bar)
  * **Store** (business is a store)
  * **Hotel** (business is a hotel)
  * **Other** (please select this option if your business does not fit any of the above categories)

  Select the type of business you operate.

* **14. Business License:**
  * Enter any business license numbers or relevant information

* **15. Business Location:**
  * Enter the location of your business

* **16. Business Zip:**
  * Enter the zip code of your business

* **17. Owner Phone:**
  * Enter the phone number of the business owner or representative

* **18. Owner Email:**
  * Enter the email address of the business owner or representative

* **19. Owner Notes:**
  * Add any additional comments or information about the business owner or representative.
