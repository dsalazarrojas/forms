# Strategy Workshop Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information and feedback from stakeholders during a strategy workshop. It is divided into several sections, each corresponding to a specific aspect of the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

*   Fill in the `Stakeholder Input` field with the details of the stakeholders involved in the workshop.
*   Provide the `Session Details` in the corresponding field.
*   Enter the `Date and Time` of the workshop.
*   Describe the `Objective and Metrics` of the workshop, including what needs to be achieved and how success will be measured.
*   Provide a brief description of the `Current State` of the project, including its current status and progress.
*   Describe the next `Next Steps` that will be taken following the workshop.
*   If applicable, provide `Stakeholder Feedback` and describe the reasons behind it.
*   Choose the participation level of `Stakeholder Participation` from the available options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
*   If no resources are needed, leave the `Project Resources` field blank.
*   Enter the `Workshop Conclusion` which will summarize the key points discussed during the workshop.
*   Finally, enter any notes from the `Project Manager Notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stakeholder Input**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Enter the details of the stakeholders involved in the workshop, such as their names, titles, and roles.</span>
* **Session Details**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Provide the date, location, and any other relevant details about the workshop session.</span>
* **Date and Time**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Enter the date and time of the workshop.</span>
* **Objective and Metrics**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Describe the objective of the workshop, including what needs to be achieved and how success will be measured.</span>
* **Current State**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Describe the current state of the project, including its current status and progress.</span>
* **Next Steps**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Describe the next steps that will be taken following the workshop, including any actions or decisions that need to be made.</span>
* **Stakeholder Feedback**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">If applicable, provide feedback from the stakeholders, describing the reasons behind it.</span>
* **Stakeholder Participation**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Choose the participation level of the stakeholders, selecting one of the available options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).</span>
* **Project Resources**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">If no resources are needed, leave this field blank.</span>
* **Workshop Conclusion**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Summarize the key points discussed during the workshop, including any key takeaways or outcomes.</span>
* **Project Manager Notes**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Enter any notes from the project manager, including any important information or actions that need to be taken.</span>
