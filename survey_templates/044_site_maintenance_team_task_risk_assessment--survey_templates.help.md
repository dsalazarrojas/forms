# site_maintenance_team_task_risk_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the potential risks associated with maintenance tasks performed by the site maintenance team. This form helps identify and document risks to prioritize tasks and ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the maintenance task and identify potential risks involved.
2. Select the risk factors (High, Medium, Low) that apply to the task.
3. Choose the impact level (Critical, Minor) of the risk.
4. Choose the severity level (Critical, Minor) of the risk.
5. Select the likelihood level (Low, Medium, High) of the risk.
6. Describe each risk factor, impact level, severity level, and likelihood level in the comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **potential_risk_factors**: Identify the potential risk factors involved in the task (High, Medium, Low).
	+ This field is where you select the risk factors that apply to the task.
* **risk_impact_level**: Choose the impact level of the risk (Critical, Minor).
	+ This field is used to select the impact level of the risk, indicating how severe the consequence of the risk would be.
* **risk_severity_level**: Choose the severity level of the risk (Critical, Minor).
	+ This field is used to select the severity level of the risk, indicating the level of harm or consequence.
* **risk_likelihood_level**: Select the likelihood level of the risk (Low, Medium, High).
	+ This field is used to select the likelihood level of the risk, indicating how likely the risk is to occur.
* **describe_potential_risk_factors**: Describe the potential risk factors in detail.
	+ This field is used to provide more information about the risk factors selected in the previous field.
* **describe_risk_impact_level**: Describe the risk impact level in detail.
	+ This field is used to provide more information about the risk impact level selected.
* **describe_risk_severity_level**: Describe the risk severity level in detail.
	+ This field is used to provide more information about the risk severity level selected.
* **describe_risk_likelihood_level**: Describe the risk likelihood level in detail.
	+ This field is used to provide more information about the risk likelihood level selected.
* **site_maintenance_team_comments**: Add any additional comments about the task and risk assessment.
	+ This field is used to add any additional information or comments about the task and risk assessment.

## Tips
* Be thorough when assessing risks to ensure accurate risk levels and descriptions.
* Use the comments section to provide additional context or information that may be relevant to the risk assessment.
* Review and revise the risk assessment as needed based on new information or changing circumstances.
