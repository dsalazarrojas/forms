# Speaker And Topic Suggestions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect suggestions for speakers and topics to be featured in events or presentations. It is designed to be filled out by event organizers or administrators to provide a list of potential speakers and topics for consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are logged in and have the necessary permissions to access this form.
2. Review the questions in each page carefully and fill out the form completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **speaker_and_topic_suggestions** (note): This is the main section of the form where you can provide a brief description or summary of the speaker and topic suggestions.
* **speaker_name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of a suggested speaker. This field is optional and is not required to be filled out.
* **topic_suggestions** (select_one, required: false): Select either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the speaker has suggested a topic.
* **other** (text, required: false): If the speaker has suggested a topic other than the ones listed, enter it here. This field is optional and is not required to be filled out.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
- You can add or remove pages from the form as needed to accommodate your specific requirements.
