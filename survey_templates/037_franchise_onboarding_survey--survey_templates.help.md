# Franchise Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Onboarding Survey is a tool to collect feedback and information from franchisees about their onboarding experience. This survey aims to understand the effectiveness of the onboarding process and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the **Franchise Name** of the franchise you joined.
5. Describe your **Experience** with the franchise onboarding process in the "Experience" field.
6. Select all the options that apply to your experience with the franchise from the "Questions List" dropdown menu.
7. Add any additional **Notes** about your experience in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter the **Date of Join** (MM/DD/YYYY) of when you joined the franchise.
9. Enter the time you joined the franchise in the "Time of Join" field.
10. Enter the **Franchisor Name** of the franchise.
11. Enter the **Franchisor Email** address of the franchise.
12. Enter the **Franchisor Phone** number of the franchise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it is known in your personal documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname or family name.
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with your account or the one you used to join the franchise.
* **Franchise Name**: Enter the name of the franchise you joined, e.g., "Joe's Pizza".
* **Experience**: Share your thoughts and feelings about your onboarding experience, e.g., "The training process was thorough and well-organized."
* **Questions List**: Select all the options that apply to your experience from the list of questions provided.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience that you didn't mention in the "Experience" field.
* **Date of Join**: Enter the date (MM/DD/YYYY) when you joined the franchise.
* **Time of Join**: Enter the time when you joined the franchise.
* **Franchisor Name**: Enter the name of the franchisor or the person who manages the franchise.
* **Franchisor Email**: Enter the email address of the franchisor or the person who manages the franchise.
* **Franchisor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the franchisor or the person who manages the franchise.

**Tips**

* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* Make sure to enter the correct date and time of join.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, add them to the "Notes" field.
