# Assessment Concepts Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to understand your current professional role, familiarity with assessment concepts, preferred assessment methods, and views on assessment-related topics. Your responses will help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the answer that best reflects your current situation and opinion.
3. If a question is not applicable to your work or experience, please select "N/A" (Not Applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Current Professional Role** (Classroom Teacher, School Administrator, Corporate Trainer, Curriculum Developer, Research Academic)
	+ This question aims to determine your current job title and responsibilities.
* **Familiarity with Formative Assessment Concepts** (1 - unaware, 5 - expert)
	+ This question measures your level of understanding and experience with formative assessment concepts, which help inform instruction and improve student learning during the learning process.
* **Familiarity with Summative Assessment Concepts** (1 - unaware, 5 - expert)
	+ This question assesses your awareness and application of summative assessment concepts, which involve evaluating student learning at the end of a lesson or unit to inform further instruction.
* **Which assessment method do you find most effective?** (Objective Tests - MCQ, True/False, Essay and Written Response, Performance-based Tasks, Portfolio Assessment, Peer and Self-assessment)
	+ This question seeks to understand your preference for assessment methods, which may influence your instructional practices.
* **Effectiveness of using rubrics for grading consistency** (1 - not effective, 5 - highly effective)
	+ This question explores your experience with rubrics, which help standardize grading and feedback processes.
* **How often should students receive formal assessment feedback?** (After every lesson, Weekly, Mid-term and Final only, End of unit/module)
	+ This question aims to understand your views on the frequency of providing feedback to students.
* **Perceived impact of standardized testing on student learning** (Optional text field)
	+ This question seeks to gather your thoughts on the influence of standardized testing on student learning outcomes.
* **Suggestions for innovative assessment tools or platforms** (Optional text field)
	+ This question invites you to share any innovative assessment tools or platforms you think could improve assessment processes.
* **How do you use assessment data to modify your instruction?** (Optional text field)
	+ This question explores how you incorporate assessment data into your teaching practices.
* **Survey completion date** (Date field)
	+ This is for internal tracking purposes only. Please ensure you provide an accurate date of completion.

**Tips**

* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you're unsure about a question, please select "N/A" (Not Applicable).
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
