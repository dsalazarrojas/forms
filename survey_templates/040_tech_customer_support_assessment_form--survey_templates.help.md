# Tech Customer Support Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Customer Support Assessment Form is a tool used to gather feedback from customers about their experience with the tech support team. The form helps in assessing the performance of the support team, identifying areas for improvement, and making data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the "Tech Customer Support Form Data" field with a brief description of your experience with the tech support team.
2. In the "Customer Feedback" field, share any additional comments or suggestions you might have about your experience.
3. Select the relevant options for "Which channel did you contact us through?" to indicate the contact method used.
4. Choose how you would rate your overall experience in the "How would you rate your overall experience?" field.
5. If applicable, provide the time taken in the "Time Taken" field.
6. Enter the date and time of the contact in the "Date of Contact" and "Time of Contact" fields, respectively.
7. Select the "Contact Method" option to indicate the method used to contact us.
8. Choose the "Channel of Contact Method" option to specify the channel used for contact.
9. Finally, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tech Customer Support Form Data** (`title`, text, optional): A field for you to provide a brief description of your experience with the tech support team.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, optional): A field for any additional comments or suggestions you might have about your experience.
* **Which channel did you contact us through?** (`channel`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the channels through which you contacted us.
	+ Option 1: Phone
	+ Option 2: Email
	+ Option 3: Chat
* **How would you rate your overall experience?** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5.
* **Time Taken** (`time_taken`, number, optional): If applicable, provide the time taken for the contact.
* **Date of Contact** (`date_of_contact`, date, optional): Enter the date of the contact.
* **Time of Contact** (`time_of_contact`, time, optional): Enter the time of the contact.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method used to contact us.
	+ Option 1: In-person
	+ Option 2: Online
	+ Option 3: Other
* **Channel of Contact Method** (`channel_of_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select the channel used for contact.
	+ Option 1: Social Media
	+ Option 2: Email
	+ Option 3: Phone

## Tips
* Please fill out all the relevant fields according to your experience.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide.
* Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our support services.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
