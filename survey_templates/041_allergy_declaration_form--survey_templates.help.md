# Allergy Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Allergy Declaration Form is a tool to help us ensure your safety and well-being while you're with us. Please complete this form to inform us about any allergies or medical conditions that may require special attention.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Carefully read through each section and answer the questions accordingly.
*   If you're unsure about any question, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
*   Please be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your contact email address in case we need to reach out to you.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number in case we need to contact you.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event or visit.
*   **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event or occasion.
*   **Party Size** (`party_size`, number, required): Enter the number of people in your group.
*   **Number with Allergies** (`number_with_allergies`, number, required): Enter the number of people with allergies in your group.
*   **Names of Allergic Individuals** (`allergic_person_names`, text, required): List the names of individuals with allergies.
*   **Do You Have Food Allergies?** (`food_allergies_present`, select_one, required): If you have food allergies, please select 'True.'
*   **Specific Allergens** (`specific_allergens`, text, optional): List the specific allergens you are allergic to.
*   **Allergen Categories** (`allergen_categories`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the allergen categories that apply to you.
*   **Allergy Severity** (`allergy_severity`, select_one, required): Rate the severity of your allergy.
*   **Typical Reaction Symptoms** (`reaction_symptoms`, text, optional): Describe any typical symptoms that occur when you experience an allergic reaction.
*   **Cross-Contamination Concern** (`cross_contamination_concern`, select_one, required): If you are concerned about cross-contamination, please select 'Yes, very concerned.'
*   **Do You Carry Epinephrine?** (`epinephrine_carried`, select_one, required): If you carry an EpiPen or auto-injector, please select 'Yes, always.'
*   **Other Medical Conditions** (`other_medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any other medical conditions, please describe them here.
*   **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have dietary restrictions, please list them here.
*   **Safe Food Options** (`safe_food_options`, text, optional): If you have any safe food options, please list them here.
*   **Ingredient Verification Needed** (`ingredient_verification`, select_one, required): If you need to verify the ingredients, please select 'Yes, for all items.'
*   **Chef Consultation Requested** (`chef_consultation`, select_one, optional): If you would like to speak with a chef, please select 'Yes, please.'
*   **Separate Preparation Requested** (`separate_preparation`, select_one, optional): If you need a separate preparation area, please select 'Yes, essential.'
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to call in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person to call in case of an emergency.
*   **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance`, text, optional): Enter your medical insurance provider's name.
*   **Liability Acknowledgment** (`liability_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Please select 'Yes, I understand the risks.'
*   **Information Accuracy** (`information_accuracy`, select_one, required): Please select 'Yes, all information is accurate.'
*   **Staff Notification Consent** (`staff_notification`, select_one, required): If you would like us to inform staff of your allergies, please select 'Yes, please inform all relevant staff.'
*   **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): If you have any other important information, please add it here.
