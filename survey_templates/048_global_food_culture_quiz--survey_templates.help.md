# Global Food Culture Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Food Culture Quiz is a self-assessment tool designed to explore the diverse aspects of food culture, allowing users to share their thoughts, experiences, and perspectives on various topics related to food culture.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Global Food Culture Quiz, follow these simple steps:

* Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
* Respond to all the questions that apply to your interests or experiences.
* You can skip any questions that do not pertain to your knowledge or experiences.
* Provide as much detail as possible in each answer.
* Use the available space to explain your answers if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Global Food Culture Quiz** (1): This field is the title of this self-assessment tool.
* **Food Culture Quiz** (2): This is the main section of the form where you can share your thoughts and experiences on various aspects of food culture.
* **Quiz Questions** (3): This field is not used in this form.
* **Cultural Background** (4): Share your cultural background related to food culture, such as your upbringing, family traditions, or personal experiences.
* **Background Info** (5): Additional information about your cultural background.
* **Cultural Context** (6): Describe the cultural context in which you have experiences related to food culture.
* **Context Info** (7): Additional information about the cultural context.
* **Cultural Impact** (8): Share your thoughts on the impact of food culture on society, environment, or individuals.
* **Impact Info** (9): Additional information about the cultural impact.
* **Food System** (10): Describe the food system in your country or region.
* **Food System Info** (11): Additional information about the food system.
* **Global Cuisine** (12): Share your thoughts on global cuisine and its diversity.
* **Global Cuisine Info** (13): Additional information about global cuisine.
* **Culinary Schools** (14): Describe the schools or institutions that have influenced your food culture.
* **Schools Info** (15): Additional information about the culinary schools.
* **Food Bloggers** (16): Share your favorite food bloggers or influencers.
* **Blogger Info** (17): Additional information about the food bloggers.
* **Food Event Organizers** (18): Describe the events or festivals related to food culture that you have attended.
* **Event Organizers Info** (19): Additional information about the food event organizers.
* **Food Industry** (20): Share your thoughts on the food industry and its practices.
* **Industry Info** (21): Additional information about the food industry.
* **Food Sustainability** (22): Describe the sustainable practices in the food system.
* **Sustainability Info** (23): Additional information about food sustainability.
* **Food Environment** (24): Share your thoughts on the impact of food on the environment.
* **Environment Info** (25): Additional information about the food environment.

Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and all answers will be kept confidential. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
