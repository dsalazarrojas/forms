# site_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer feedback and satisfaction data from customers who have interacted with a service provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your honest and detailed feedback.
2. Choose the category and service type that best describe the service you received.
3. Select all the service types that apply to the experience.
4. Provide a detailed description of the service you received (up to 2,000 characters).
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
6. Indicate whether or not you would recommend the service to others.
7. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
8. Enter your name and contact information so that we can follow up with you.
9. Enter your contact email address.
10. Enter your phone number (if available).
11. Date and time of the review (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
12. Time and date of creation (this will be automatically populated, do not fill this out).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Survey** (`id: 1`): A text box where you can provide a detailed description of your experience with the service provider.
* **Category** (`id: 2`): Choose the category that best describes the service you received. For example, if you received interior design services, select "interior designer".
* **Service Type** (`id: 3`): Select all the service types that apply to your experience. You can choose multiple options.
* **Service Name** (`id: 4`): Provide the name of the service you received (e.g., "Interior Design Consultation").
* **Service Details** (`id: 5`): Provide a detailed description of the service you received (up to 2,000 characters).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`id: 6`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service on a scale of 0-100%.
* **Recommendation** (`id: 7`): Indicate whether or not you would recommend the service to others. Select "True" if you would recommend, "False" if you would not, or "neutral" if you are undecided.
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 8`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.
* **Customer Name** (`id: 9`): Enter your name so that we can address you personally in our response.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 10`): Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you.
* **Contact Number** (`id: 11`): Enter your phone number (if available) so that we can contact you.
* **Review Date and Time** (`id: 12`): Enter the date and time of the review (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
* **Review Time and Date Created** (`id: 13`): This field will be automatically populated and should not be filled out by the user.
* **Created at** (`id: 14`): This field will be automatically populated and should not be filled out by the user.

Note: The review date and time fields (`id: 12` and `id: 13`) are likely used for tracking purposes and should be filled out accurately. The created at field (`id: 14`) is an automatically generated timestamp and should not be filled out by the user.
