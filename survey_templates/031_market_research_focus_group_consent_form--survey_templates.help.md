# Market Research Focus Group Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from market research study participants, ensuring they are aware of and consent to their involvement in the study.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Participant Information**: Provide your name, age, and any other demographic details required.
2. **Research Study**: Enter a brief description of the research study you are participating in.
3. **Focus Group Details**: Enter the focus group details, as required.
4. **Research Study Type**: Select the type of research study you are participating in (Focus Group, Interview, or Online Survey).
5. **Participant Awareness**: Indicate your level of awareness about the study.
6. **Research Study Start Date**: Enter the start date of the study.
7. **Research Study End Date**: Enter the end date of the study.
8. **Research Study Start Time**: (Optional) Enter the start time of the study.
9. **Research Study End Time**: (Optional) Enter the end time of the study.
10. **Participant Contact Information**: Provide any additional contact information you are willing to share.
11. **Participant Contact Method**: Select your preferred method of contact.
12. **Participant Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (if you are willing to share).
13. **Participant Email**: Enter your email (if you are willing to share).
14. **Participant Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.
15. **Research Study Description**: Enter a brief description of the study.
16. **Informed Consent**: Confirm that you have received informed consent regarding the study.
17. **Participant Consent**: Indicate your consent to participate in the study.
18. **Research Study Leader**: Enter the name of the study leader or lead researcher.
19. **Research Study Coordinator**: Enter the name of the study coordinator.
20. **Research Study Sponsor**: Enter the name of the study sponsor.
21. **Research Study Funding**: Enter the source of study funding (if applicable).
22. **Research Studies Inclusion**: Indicate the criteria for inclusion in the study.
23. **Research Studies Exclusion**: Indicate the criteria for exclusion in the study.
24. **Research Study Inclusion Criteria**: (Optional) Enter any additional inclusion criteria.
25. **Research Study Exclusion Criteria**: (Optional) Enter any additional exclusion criteria.
