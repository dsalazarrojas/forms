# Social Connection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Connection Survey is designed to gather information about the social connections between teachers and students in a school setting. This form is intended to measure various aspects of teacher-student relationships, including student-teacher ratio, teacher qualifications, experience, and interaction. By completing this survey, you will help us better understand the social connections within schools.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your first name and email address on the first page.
2. Proceed to the second page and select the type of school (Elementary, High School, University) that applies to your school.
3. On the next pages, select the grade level of your students (Grade 6-8, Grade 9-10, etc.).
4. Then, choose the class size (Small, Medium, Large) that best describes your school's class size.
5. After that, select your teacher qualifications (High School Diploma, Master's or Doctoral Degree, etc.).
6. Next, select your teaching experience (Less than 2 years, 2-5 years, etc.).
7. Choose how well you support your students (Strong, Somewhat, Not Very Well, etc.).
8. Select how well your students support you (Strong, Somewhat, Not Very Well, etc.).
9. Indicate the quality of your interaction with students (Very Good, Good, Neutral, etc.).
10. Assess the teacher climate at your school (Very Good, Good, Neutral, etc.).
11. Finally, select the student climate at your school (Very Good, Good, Neutral, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name, which will be used for identification purposes only.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's name to help us identify it.
* **School Type** (`school_type`, select_one, required/true): Select the type of school that best describes your school (Elementary, High School, University).
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_multiple, required/true): Choose the grade levels that your school offers (Grade 6-8, Grade 9-10, etc.).
* **Class Size** (`class_size`, select_one, required/true): Select the class size at your school (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Student-Teacher Ratio** (`student_teacher_ratio`, number, required/optional): Enter the teacher-student ratio at your school.
* **Teacher-Student Ratio** (`teacher_student_ratio`, number, required/optional): Enter the student-teacher ratio at your school.
* **Teacher Qualifications** (`teacher_qualifications`, select_multiple, required/true): Select the qualifications held by your teachers (High School Diploma, Master's or Doctoral Degree, etc.).
* **Teacher Experience** (`teacher_experience`, select_one, required/true): Select your teaching experience (Less than 2 years, 2-5 years, etc.).
* **Student Engagement** (`student_engagement`, select_one, required/true): Choose how well your students are engaged (High, Medium, Low).
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support_prophets`, select_one, required/true): Select how well you support your students (Strong, Somewhat, Not Very Well).
* **Student Support** (`student_support_providing`, select_one, required/true): Select how well your students support you (Strong, Somewhat, Not Very Well).
* **Teacher-Student Interaction** (`teacher_student_interaction`, select_one, required/true): Indicate the quality of your interaction with students (Very Good, Good, etc.).
* **Teacher Climate** (`teacher_climate`, select_one, required/true): Assess the teacher climate at your school (Very Good, Good, Neutral, etc.).
* **Student Climate** (`student_climate`, select_one, required/true): Select the student climate at your school (Very Good, Good, Neutral, etc.).

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Social Connection Survey with ease and accuracy.
