# reading_habits_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on reading habits, specifically to gauge an individual's reading frequency, interests, and preferred reading locations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely, ensuring you answer all required questions.
- For the "reading_frequency" field, select all applicable options that describe your reading habits.
- For the "reading_interest" field, select one option that best describes your primary interest.
- For the "reading_place" field, enter a specific location where you usually read.
- For all frequency fields, select all applicable options that describe your habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: This section is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
- **reading_purpose**: Select "Yes" if you read for pleasure, "No" otherwise.
- **reading_frequency**: This field allows you to specify how often you read. Select all applicable options to describe your habits.
	+ Yes, daily
	+ 2-3 times a week
	+ 1-2 times a week
	+ Rarely
- **reading_time**: Enter the amount of time you typically spend reading each day.
- **reading_interest**: Select one option that best describes your primary interest in reading (e.g., fiction, non-fiction, etc.).
	+ Fiction
	+ Non-fiction
	+ Both
- **reading_place**: Enter a specific location where you usually read (e.g., home, library, etc.).
- **reading_frequency_2** to **reading_frequency_16**: These fields allow you to specify your reading habits across different categories. Select all applicable options to describe your habits.
- **Submission**: This field is for any additional comments or information you'd like to share.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Select all applicable options for frequency fields to ensure accurate representation of your reading habits.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
