# Rotary Representative Election Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rotary Representative Election Survey is a tool to collect nominations for a rotary representative election. It is designed to gather information about individuals who wish to be nominated as representatives for the rotary organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are providing accurate information.
2. Fill in the "Nomination Form" fields with the required information.
3. Provide your contact details in the corresponding "Contact Info" fields.
4. Repeat the process for each section of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form 1**: Enter the name of the individual you wish to nominate. This is the main form where you can provide a brief description of the nominee.
* **Contact Info 1**: Enter your contact information, which will be used to reach out to you for more information about the nomination.
* **Nomination Form 2**: Enter the name of another individual you wish to nominate. This field is similar to Nomination Form 1.
* **Contact Info 2**: Enter your contact information again, as the same as before.
* 
This pattern continues for each section, with the next Nomination Form field followed by a Contact Info field. Please make sure to review each field carefully and provide accurate information. 

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you need to make changes, please don't hesitate to contact the organizers.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification.
