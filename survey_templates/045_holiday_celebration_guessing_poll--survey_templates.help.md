# Holiday Celebration Guessing Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users on their experiences and opinions related to snowfall and winter activities. It will help inform decisions on winter-related marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information, including your name and any other identifying information.
2. Answer the questions about snowfall amounts in various cities, such as New York City and Tokyo.
3. Share your favorite winter destination and how many times you've visited it.
4. Choose your favorite winter activity.
5. Select the month with the most snowfall in your hometown.
6. Report the snowfall amount in your hometown in December.
7. Share your favorite holiday tradition related to snowfall.
8. Enter the number of snowshoes you own.
9. Select the regions with most and least snowfall.
10. Report the snowfall amount in New York City (if applicable).
11. Choose a time of day to go outside when it's snowing.

* **Participant Information (Participant Information)**: This is where you can enter your name and any other identifying information.

* **Snowfall in Paris, France (Snowfall in Paris, France)**: Enter the amount of snowfall you think occurs in Paris.
* **Favorite Winter Destination (Favorite Winter Destination)**: Enter your favorite winter destination.
* **Times visited favorite snow destination (Times visited favorite snow destination)**: Enter the number of times you've visited your favorite winter destination.
* **Favorite Winter Activity (Favorite Winter Activity)**: Enter your favorite winter activity.
* **Month with most snowfall in hometown (Month with most snowfall in hometown)**: Select the month with the most snowfall in your hometown.
* **Snowfall in hometown in December (Snowfall in hometown in December)**: Enter the amount of snowfall you think occurs in your hometown in December.
* **Favorite Holiday Tradition related to snowfall (Favorite Holiday Tradition related to snowfall)**: Enter your favorite holiday tradition related to snowfall.
* **Number of snowshoes owned (Number of snowshoes owned)**: Enter the number of snowshoes you own.
