# Wildlife Observation Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Observation Checklist Form is designed to collect information about observed species in the wild. This data can be used for research, conservation efforts, or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all necessary details about the species you observed, such as the species name, its ID, and any unique characteristics.
2.  Enter the species name in the field labeled "Species Observed."
3.  For the "location_observed" field, type the exact location where you observed the species.
4.  If you know the species' ID, please enter it in the "Species ID" field.
5.  Enter the date of the observation in the "date_observed" field using the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Species Observed** (`species_observed`, text, required): Enter the name of the species you observed. This should include its scientific name for accurate classification.
*   **location_observed** (`location_observed`, text, required): Type the exact location where you observed the species, including any notable features or landmarks.
*   **Species ID** (`species_id`, number, optional): If you know the species' ID, enter it here. This helps with further classification and research.
*   **date_observed** (`date_observed`, date, required): Enter the date of the observation in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD). This helps track the timeline of observations.

## Tips

*   Always fill out the form as accurately as possible to ensure valuable data.
*   Be precise with the species name and ID to avoid confusion.
*   If you're unsure about any field, try to recall as much detail as you can.
*   If you have further observations or notes about the species, consider appending additional information in a separate note or document.
