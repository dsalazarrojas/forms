# Marketing Campaign Reaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on marketing campaigns, allowing us to evaluate their effectiveness and make data-driven decisions. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several fields, each with its own specific purpose.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Marketing Campaign Reaction Poll
* **Marketing Campaign Reaction Poll** (`text`, required): Enter a brief description of the marketing campaign you're reacting to.

### 2. Campaign Title
* **Campaign Title** (`text`, required): Enter the title of the marketing campaign you're reacting to.

### 3. Reaction Scale
* **Reaction Scale** (`select_one`, required): Select your overall reaction to the campaign:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Bad
	+ Very Bad

### 4. Reaction Scale Comments
* **Reaction Scale Comments** (`text`, optional): If you selected a reaction, provide additional comments about your reaction.

### 5. Channel Performance
* **Channel Performance** (`select_one`, required): Select your evaluation of the campaign's performance:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### 6. Intent to Act
* **Intent to Act** (`select_multiple`, required): Indicate your likelihood of taking action based on the campaign:
	+ True (very likely)
	+ False (not likely)
	+ Maybe

### 7. Comments
* **Comments** (`text`, optional): Share any additional thoughts or feedback about the campaign.

### 8. Email
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be notified about future campaigns or updates.

### 9. Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted directly.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out the form completely and honestly.
- Use the "Reaction Scale" and "Channel Performance" fields to provide your overall reaction and evaluation.
- In the "Intent to Act" field, select all that apply if you're unsure or likely to take action. 
- Use the "Comments" field to provide detailed feedback on the campaign. 

This guide is intended to be helpful, not exhaustive. If you're still unsure about a field or have questions, contact [support email].
