# weekly_student_well_being_check_in - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track the well-being of students, specifically their stress levels, emotional states, and overall mood. This form is designed to be completed by students themselves or by a trusted individual who has regular contact with the student. It helps to identify potential areas of support that the student may need, providing valuable insights for educators, counselors, or support staff to provide targeted assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: Provide a brief introduction to the student's current situation, including any recent events or changes that may be affecting their well-being.
2. Complete each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_id** (number, Required): Enter the student's unique identifier number.
* **student_name** (text, Required): Enter the student's name.
* **stress_level** (select_one, Required):
	+ Select the level of stress the student is experiencing:
		- Very High
		- High
		- Medium
		- Low
		- Very Low
* **emotional_state** (select_one, Required):
	+ Select the student's current emotional state:
		- Sad
		- Anxious
		- Stressed
		- Overwhelmed
		- Happy
		- Calm
		- Focused
		- Distracted
* **support_needs** (select_multiple, Required):
	+ Select the types of support the student may need:
		- Academic Support
		- Emotional Support
		- Counseling Support
		- Family Support
		- Friend Support
		- Peer Support
* **overall_mood** (select_one, Required):
	+ Select the student's overall mood:
		- Very Poor
		- Poor
		- Neutral
		- Good
		- Excellent
* **suggestions_for_support** (text, Optional): Provide any additional suggestions or comments for support that may not be included in the above fields.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the student's consent to complete this form.
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when providing information about the student's well-being.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, consult with the student or a trusted individual who knows them well.
