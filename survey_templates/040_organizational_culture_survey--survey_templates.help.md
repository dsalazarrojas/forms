# Organizational Culture Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help your company assess its organizational culture and identify areas for improvement. Your honest and accurate feedback is crucial in providing a comprehensive picture of your work experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the answers that best describe your current experiences and perceptions.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
3. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.
4. Please fill out all required fields.
