# Participant Networking Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants in events or activities on their networking experiences, opportunities, and suggestions for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the designated field.
2. Enter your **Event ID**, which is unique to each event.
3. Answer the questions in the **Networking Experiences** field by selecting all that apply from the available options.
4. Answer the questions in the **Networking Opportunities** field by selecting all that apply from the available options.
5. If applicable, answer the **Improvements** and **Suggestions** questions by selecting all that apply from the available options.
6. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (Text): Enter your name as a participant in the event.
* **Event ID** (Number): Enter the unique ID assigned to this event.
* **Feedback** (Text): Optional feedback on the event.
* **Email** (Email): If you wish to be contacted for follow-up, enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text): If you wish to be contacted for follow-up, enter your phone number.
* **Date/Time** (Date): Enter the date and time you participated in the event.
* **Networking Experiences** (Select Multiple): Answer the following questions by selecting all that apply:
	+ Positive
	+ Somewhat positive
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat negative
	+ Negative
* **Networking Opportunities** (Select Multiple): Answer the following questions by selecting all that apply:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Improvements** (Select Multiple): Answer the following questions by selecting all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Suggestions** (Select Multiple): Answer the following questions by selecting all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No

Note: Some fields may not be applicable depending on your response to the **Networking Experiences** field.
