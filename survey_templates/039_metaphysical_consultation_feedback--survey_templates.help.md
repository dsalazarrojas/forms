# Metaphysical Consultation Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metaphysical Consultation Feedback form is used to collect feedback from users about their experiences with the metaphysical consultation service. This form helps us understand the effectiveness and quality of the service, allowing us to make improvements for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title describing your experience with the metaphysical consultation service.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the service on a scale of 0-10.
3. Rate the practitioner who provided the consultation on a scale of 0-10.
4. Provide any recommendations for the practitioner, if applicable.
5. Add any notes about areas for improvement for the service.
6. If a follow-up appointment is needed, select a date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Metaphysical Consultation Feedback** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title describing your experience with the metaphysical consultation service. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the service on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.
* **Practitioner** (`practitioner_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the practitioner who provided the consultation on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is not satisfactory and 10 is excellent.
* **Recommendation** (`practitioner_recommendation`, text, required: false): If you have any recommendations or suggestions for the practitioner, please enter them here. This will help us improve our service.
* **Improvement** (`improvement_notes`, text, required: false): If you have any thoughts on how we can improve the service, please enter them here. Your feedback is invaluable to us!
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date, required: false): If a follow-up appointment is needed, select a date and time for the next consultation.
