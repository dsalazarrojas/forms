# Horse Riding Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your experience with a horse riding service. Your feedback is valuable for improving our service and providing the best possible experience for you and other customers.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1.  Review the survey questions carefully and provide honest answers.
2.  Answer each question with a numerical rating (e.g., 1-5) unless instructed otherwise.
3.  Provide clear and concise text answers for open-ended questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide any relevant background information about your experience.
*   **Rating Lesson**: Rate your experience with the lesson you received, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   **Rating Tour**: Rate your experience with the tour or activity you participated in, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   **Comments**: Please provide any additional comments about your experience. Be as detailed as possible.
*   **Additional Comments**: This field is for any other thoughts or suggestions you have about the service.
*   **Rating Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the service, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   **Rating Quality**: Rate the quality of the service you received, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   **Rating Safety**: Rate the safety provided during the service, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   **Rating Facilities**: Rate the facilities provided, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   **Suggestions**: This field is for any suggestions you have about how we can improve the service.
*   **Additional Comments**: This field is a duplicate of the previous "Comments" field and serves as a second opportunity to provide any additional thoughts or suggestions.

Note: The "Additional Comments" field is not intended for redundant information and should not be used to repeat what you have already mentioned in the "Comments" field. Use it to add new ideas or thoughts instead.
