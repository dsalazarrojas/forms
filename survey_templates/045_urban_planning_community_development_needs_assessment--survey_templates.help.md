# Urban Planning Community Development Needs Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from community members about the needs and priorities for community development in urban planning areas. It will help us understand the current state of community infrastructure, resources, and public services, as well as identify areas that require improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each field and answer the questions accordingly.
2.  For the community infrastructure field, please rate the overall condition of community infrastructure on a scale from 1 to 10.
3.  In the community resources field, describe the available community resources such as parks, libraries, and public buildings.
4.  Rate the quality of public services by selecting a number that best represents the current state (e.g., 1 for poor, 5 for excellent).
5.  For the priority improvements fields, select up to three improvements that you believe are the most critical to address in the area.
6.  In the additional feedback field, provide any other comments or suggestions you may have about the area.
7.  Select how you came across this survey from the options provided.
8.  In the contact info field, provide your contact information if you'd like to be contacted for further discussions.
9.  Enter your email address in the email field.
10.  Finally, enter your phone number in the phone field.
11.  Review your answers carefully and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Condition of Community Infrastructure**: Rate the overall condition of community infrastructure such as roads, sidewalks, and public buildings. 
* **Community Resources**: Describe the available community resources such as parks, libraries, and public buildings. 
* **Quality of Public Services**: Rate the quality of public services such as healthcare, education, and public safety. 
* **First Priority Improvement**: Select up to three improvements that you believe are the most critical to address in the area. 
* **Second Priority Improvement**: Select up to three improvements that you believe are the second most critical to address in the area. 
* **Third Priority Improvement**: Select up to three improvements that you believe are the third most critical to address in the area. 
* **Additional Feedback**: Provide any other comments or suggestions you may have about the area. 
* **How Did You Come Across This Survey?**: Select how you came across this survey from the options provided. 
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information if you'd like to be contacted for further discussions. 
* **Email**: Enter your email address. 
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. 

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
- We value your input and appreciate your time in completing this survey.
