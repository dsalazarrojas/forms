# policy_implementation_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The policy implementation feedback form is a tool used to collect feedback on the status of policies, their implementation plans, evaluation methods, and frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all required fields.
- For non-required fields, feel free to provide any additional relevant information.
- If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that corresponds to your employee ID.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required): Provide a brief description of your observations and insights on the policy implementation.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department you belong to for which you are providing feedback.
* **Team** (`team`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select the team(s) you belong to for which you are providing feedback.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this feedback.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you are submitting this feedback.
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the policy (Active or Inactive).
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the policy implementation.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date and time you are submitting this feedback.
* **Policy Id** (`policy_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the policy being reviewed.
* **Policy Name** (`policy_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the policy being reviewed.
* **Policy Description** (`policy_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the policy being reviewed.
* **Implementation Plan** (`implementation_plan`, text, required): Describe the implementation plan and steps taken to execute the policy.
* **Evaluation Method** (`evaluation_method`, text, required): Describe the evaluation method used to assess policy effectiveness.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criteria`, text, required): Describe the criteria used to evaluate policy effectiveness.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, text, required): Describe how frequently policy evaluations are conducted.
* **Policy Impact** (`policy_impact`, text, required): Describe the impact of the policy on the organization.
* **Policy Status Comments** (`policy_status_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments on the policy status.
