# Fire Resistant Jacket Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Resistant Jacket Survey is designed to collect feedback on fire-resistant jackets from users. It is intended to help manufacturers understand how their products perform in various aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Each field has a specific purpose, which is explained below.
3. You can skip any question that does not apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a short introduction to the form. Please feel free to add any comments or observations you have about the fire-resistant jacket you are evaluating.
* **Jacket Performance** (`jacket_performance`, `number`, required: false): Rate the performance of the fire-resistant jacket from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Safety Features** (`safety_features`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the safety features of the fire-resistant jacket:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Comfort** (`comfort`, `number`, required: false): Rate the comfort of the fire-resistant jacket from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Durability** (`durability`, `number`, required: false): Rate the durability of the fire-resistant jacket from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Ease of Movement** (`ease_of_movement`, `number`, required: false): Rate the ease of movement of the fire-resistant jacket from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the fire-resistant jacket from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Manufacturer Recommendation** (`manufacturer_recommendation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Do you recommend the fire-resistant jacket?
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Undecided
