# CGPA Conversion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CGPA Conversion Survey form is designed to convert CGPA scores from one grading system to another, helping students facilitate academic mobility and compatibility in their applications.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the provided fields with your academic details, including name, university name, country of education, academic stream, and original grading scale.
2.  Specify the target conversion system for your CGPA score, which may include US 4.0 Scale, UK Classification, ECTS (European), or other systems.
3.  Enter the maximum possible CGPA in your original system and the current CGPA value you need to convert.
4.  Select the purpose of conversion, which may be for graduate school applications, job applications, visa requirements, or internal records.
5.  Confirm that you have uploaded your official transcripts for verification and specify the year of graduation.
6.  Choose the desired processing speed for the conversion (standard or rush).
7.  Confirm the accuracy of the provided information and certify that it matches your official records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Full Name** (`Student Full Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **University or Awarding Institution** (`University or Awarding Institution`, text, required): Type your university's name as it appears on your transcript.
*   **Country of Education** (`Country of Education`, text, required): Enter your country of education.
*   **Academic Major/Stream** (`Academic Major/Stream`, text, required): Enter your academic major or stream as it appears on your transcript.
*   **Original Grading Scale** (`Original Grading Scale`, select_one, required): Select your original grading scale, which may be 4.0 Scale (US), 5.0 Scale, 7.0 Scale, 10.0 Scale (India/Europe), or Percentage (0-100).
*   **Current Cumulative CGPA/Percentage** (`Current Cumulative CGPA/Percentage`, number, required): Enter your current cumulative CGPA or percentage value.
*   **Maximum Possible CGPA in your system** (`Maximum Possible CGPA in your system`, number, required): Enter the maximum possible CGPA value in your system.
*   **Target Conversion System** (`Target Conversion System`, select_one, required): Select the target conversion system for your CGPA, which may be US 4.0 Scale (WES Equivalent), UK Classification (1st, 2:1, etc.), ECTS (European), or German Scale (1-5).
*   **Purpose of Conversion** (`Purpose of Conversion`, select_one, required): Specify the purpose of conversion, which may be for graduate school applications, job applications, visa requirements, or internal records.
*   **Have you uploaded your official transcripts for verification?** (`Have you uploaded your official transcripts for verification?`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have uploaded your official transcripts for verification.
*   **Year of Graduation** (`Year of Graduation`, number, required): Enter the year you graduated.
*   **Your Contact Email** (`Your Contact Email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you want to receive the results.
*   **Required Processing Speed** (`Required Processing Speed`, select_one, required): Choose between standard (5-7 days) or rush (48 hours) processing speed.
*   **I certify that the academic data provided matches my official records** (`I certify that the academic data provided matches my official records`, select_one, required): Confirm that the provided information matches your official records.
