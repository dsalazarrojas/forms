# phishing_risk_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The phishing_risk_assessment_form is used to assess the risk of a potential phishing attempt.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed:

*   Please select "High" or "Low" for the risk level.
*   Enter your email address if you have been targeted by a phishing email.
*   Enter your phone number if you have been targeted by a phishing call.
*   Indicate whether the department is aware of the phishing attempt.
*   Provide a description of the phishing attempt.
*   Enter the date of the phishing attempt.
*   Enter the time of the phishing attempt.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **phishing risk** (page 1, required: false):
    *   A number indicating the risk level of the phishing attempt.
*   **risk level** (page 1, required: false):
    *   Select "High" if the risk level is severe, "Low" if it is mild.
*   **email** (page 1, required: false):
    *   Enter your email address if you have been targeted by a phishing email.
*   **phone number** (page 1, required: false):
    *   Enter your phone number if you have been targeted by a phishing call.
*   **department** (page 2, required: false):
    *   Indicate whether the department is aware of the phishing attempt.
*   **description** (page 2, required: false):
    *   Provide a description of the phishing attempt.
*   **date** (page 2, required: false):
    *   Enter the date of the phishing attempt.
*   **time** (page 2, required: false):
    *   Enter the time of the phishing attempt.
