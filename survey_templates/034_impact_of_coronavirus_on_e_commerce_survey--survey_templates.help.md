# impact_of_coronavirus_on_e_commerce_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the impact of the coronavirus on e-commerce. It aims to gather information on various aspects of e-commerce, including its effects, industry sectors, regions, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for each field based on your experience and knowledge.
2. Answer each question as accurately and comprehensively as possible.
3. Ensure you provide clear and concise answers for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide a brief background or introduction to your answer.
* **Impact of coronavirus**: Select all the options that you feel best describe the impact of the coronavirus on your business or industry.
	+ Option 1: Positive impact
	+ Option 2: Negative impact
	+ Option 3: No impact
* **Effect on e-Commerce**: Choose one option that best describes the effect of the coronavirus on your e-commerce business.
	+ Option 4: Positive effect
	+ Option 5: Negative effect
	+ Option 6: No effect
* **Industry Sector**: Select one option that best describes your industry sector.
	+ Option 7: Online Retail
	+ Option 8: Online Services
	+ Option 9: Other (please specify)
* **Region**: Select all the regions that best describe your business or industry's location.
* **Country**: Choose one country that best describes your business or industry's location.
	+ Option 13: USA
	+ Option 14: Canada
	+ Option 15: Other (please specify)
* **Year**: Enter the year when the impact of the coronavirus occurred.
* **Revenue Loss**: Enter the estimated revenue loss due to the coronavirus.
* **Sales Increase**: Enter the estimated sales increase due to the coronavirus.
* **Employee Loss**: Enter the estimated number of employees lost due to the coronavirus.
* **Business Closure**: Select all the options that best describe the impact on your business or industry.
	+ Option 16: Full business closure
	+ Option 17: Partial business closure
	+ Option 18: No business closure
* **Government Support**: Select all the options that best describe the government support provided to your business or industry.
	+ Option 19: Financial support
	+ Option 20: Non-financial support
	+ Option 21: No government support
* **Financial Loss**: Choose one option that best describes the financial loss due to the coronavirus.
	+ Option 22: Financial loss
	+ Option 23: No financial loss
	+ Option 24: Other (please specify)
* **Survey Completed**: Choose one option that best describes when the survey was completed.
	+ Option 25: Immediately after the impact
	+ Option 26: After some time has passed
	+ Option 27: Never
* **Demographic**: Choose one option that best describes your demographic information.
	+ Option 28: Age group 18-24
	+ Option 29: Age group 25-54
	+ Option 30: Other (please specify)
* **Additional Comments**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your experience with the coronavirus and e-commerce.

Please note that the options provided are not exhaustive, and you can select all the options that best fit your experience.
