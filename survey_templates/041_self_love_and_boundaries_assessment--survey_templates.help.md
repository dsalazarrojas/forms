# Self Love And Boundaries Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Love And Boundaries Assessment is a form designed to help you evaluate your self-worth and boundary-setting habits. This form provides a framework for you to assess your self-worth on a scale from 1 to 10 and explore your boundary-setting habits through a series of questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. For each question, rate your self-worth on a scale from 1 to 10.
2. For the boundary-setting habits questions, describe your current habits in as much detail as possible.
3. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
4. Review your answers carefully to gain valuable insights into areas where you may need to focus on personal growth.
