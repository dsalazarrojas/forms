# Magnet Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a magnet's attributes and usage habits to improve product design and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **magnet_information_survey** field with a title that best describes the magnet product or service you are surveying.
2.  Select your primary category for the magnet product or service.
3.  Choose how often you use the magnet product or service.
4.  Rate your satisfaction with the magnet product or service.
5.  Choose how often you purchase the magnet product or service.
6.  Select the channels you typically use to purchase the magnet product or service.
7.  Choose the methods you use to purchase the magnet product or service.
8.  Indicate the factors that influence your purchases of the magnet product or service.
9.  Enter any additional comments about your experience with the magnet product or service.
10.  Provide your email address to receive any follow-up information.
11.  Enter your phone number to help us reach you if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **magnet_information_survey**: Enter the title of the magnet product or service you are surveying.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **what_is_your_primary_category**: Select your primary category for the magnet product or service.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Magnet, Accessories, Software, Other
	+ Required: Yes
* **how_often_do_you_use_magnet**: Choose how often you use the magnet product or service.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, Never
	+ Required: Yes
* **how_satisfied_are_you**: Rate your satisfaction with the magnet product or service on a scale from 1-5.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: Yes
* **how_often_do_you_purchase**: Choose how often you purchase the magnet product or service.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Never
	+ Required: Yes
* **which_channel_do_you_purchase_from**: Select the channels you typically use to purchase the magnet product or service.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: Online, In-store, Both
	+ Required: Yes
* **how_do_you_purchase**: Choose the methods you use to purchase the magnet product or service.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: Online, In-store, Other
	+ Required: Yes
* **what_influences_your_purchases**: Indicate the factors that influence your purchases of the magnet product or service.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: Social Media, Influencers, Friends and Family, Online Ads, Other
	+ Required: Yes
* **any_additional_comments**: Enter any additional comments about your experience with the magnet product or service.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **email_address**: Enter your email address to receive any follow-up information.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: Yes
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number to help us reach you if necessary.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **name**: Enter your name.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
