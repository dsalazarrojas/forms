# Morality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect survey responses on moral issues. Respondents will be asked to fill out three text input fields, one on each page.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate to the first page, labeled "first_page."
2. Enter your response in the text field provided.
3. Click on the "Next" or "Previous" button to move between pages.
4. Repeat the process until you have filled out all three pages.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (`page_1`, `text`, required): Respondents will be asked to fill out a text field on the first page.
* **Second Page** (`page_2`, `text`, required): Respondents will be asked to fill out a text field on the second page.
* **Third Page** (`page_3`, `text`, optional): Respondents will be asked to fill out a text field on the third page. This field is not required.

## Tips
- Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
- If you have any issues or difficulties while filling out the form, contact form@gic.mx.
- Thank you for taking the time to contribute to the Morality Survey.
