# Vendor Leadership Reception Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from vendors after they have received leadership receptions, allowing for the evaluation and improvement of leadership reception services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields. Each field with an asterisk \* is required.
2. Select the correct option for the "Category" field.
3. For the "Vendor Leadership Reception Satisfaction Rating" field, rate your satisfaction with the leadership reception on a scale from 0 to 10.
4. Answer the "Vendor Leadership Reception Would Recommend" question with either "Yes" or "No".
5. Add any comments or issues you may have.
6. Add any suggestions or improvements you'd like to see.
7. Enter the name and email of the person who collected the feedback.
8. If necessary, add any additional comments or follow up information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Leadership Reception Feedback** (\*): Enter a brief description of the leadership reception you received.
* **Category** (\*): Select the category that best describes the type of feedback you are providing. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Description**: Optional field for any additional comments or details about your feedback.
* **Assigned Tool**: Optional field to indicate which tool was used for the leadership reception.
* **Vendor Leadership Reception Feedback Form ID**: A unique identifier for this form submission.
* **Output File**: Optional field to attach a file to your feedback.
* **Collected At**: The date and time the feedback was collected.
* **Collected By**: The name of the person who collected the feedback.
* **Collected By Email**: The email address of the person who collected the feedback.
* **Collected By Phone**: Optional field to enter the phone number of the person who collected the feedback.
* **Collected By Title**: Optional field to enter the title of the person who collected the feedback.
* **Vendor Leadership Reception Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the leadership reception on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Vendor Leadership Reception Would Recommend**: Answer with either "Yes" or "No" whether you would recommend this leadership reception to others.
* **Comments**: Optional field to add any additional comments.
* **Issues**: Optional field to report any issues you experienced.
* **Suggestions**: Optional field to suggest improvements.
* **Improvements**: Optional field to suggest specific improvements.
* **Follow Up**: Optional field to add any follow-up information.
* **Follow Up At**: Optional date for follow-up.
* **Follow Up By**: Optional field to enter the name of the person who will follow up.
* **Follow Up By Name**: Optional field to enter the title of the person who will follow up.
* **Follow Up By Phone**: Optional field to enter the phone number of the person who will follow up.
* **Follow Up By Email**: Optional field to enter the email address of the person who will follow up.
