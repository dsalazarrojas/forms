# Construction Project Billing Software Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users evaluate construction project billing software based on various factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the evaluator's name and title.
2. Specify the software being evaluated.
3. Evaluate the software's core billing features.
4. Evaluate the software's technical and integration aspects.
5. Assess the software's usability, cost, and support.
6. Make a final recommendation based on your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator's Name and Title (2)** (`text`, `required`): Enter your name and title to identify yourself as the evaluator.
* **Name of Software Being Evaluated (3)** (`text`, `required`): Specify the software being evaluated.
* **Date of Evaluation (4)** (`date`, `required`): Enter the date when the evaluation was performed.
* **Evaluation Phase (5)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the current evaluation phase: Initial Research/Desk Review, Sales Presentation/Demo, Free Trial/Sandboxing, or Post-Implementation Review.
* **Core Billing Features (6)** (`note`, `required`): This is a section for noting the software's core billing features.
* **Supports Progress/AIA G702-G703 Billing (7)** (`number`, `required`): Evaluate how well the software supports progress and AIA G702-G703 billing, rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Change Order Management Integration (8)** (`number`, `required`): Evaluate the software's change order management integration, rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Automatic Retainage Tracking and Calculation (9)** (`number`, `required`): Evaluate the software's automatic retainage tracking and calculation, rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Subcontractor Billing Portal/Management (10)** (`number`, `required`): Evaluate the software's subcontractor billing portal, rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Technical and Integration Factors (11)** (`note`, `required`): This is a section for noting the software's technical and integration factors.
* **Sync Capabilities with Accounting Software (ERP) (12)** (`select_one`, `required`): Evaluate how well the software integrates with accounting software such as Sage, QuickBooks, Oracle, rating from Seamless Native Sync, Third-party Connector Required, Manual Export/Import Only, to No Integration Possible.
* **Mobile Accessibility for Site Personnel (13)** (`number`, `required`): Evaluate the software's mobile accessibility for site personnel, rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Data Security and Compliance Features (14)** (`number`, `required`): Evaluate the software's data security and compliance features, rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Usability and Cost Factors (15)** (`note`, `required`): This is a section for noting the software's usability and cost factors.
* **Ease of Use and Learning Curve for New Users (16)** (`number`, `required`): Evaluate the software's ease of use and learning curve, rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Vendor Training and Customer Support Quality (17)** (`number`, `required`): Evaluate the software's vendor training and customer support, rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Perceived Value for Money (18)** (`number`, `required`): Evaluate the software's perceived value for money, rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Preferred Pricing Model for this Tool (19)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the preferred pricing model for the tool: Monthly Subscription, Annual Subscription (Discounted), Per-Project Fee, or Enterprise/Custom Quote.
* **Final Recommendation (21)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the overall purchase recommendation: High Priority Purchase, Consider with Reservations, Do Not Purchase, Further Research Required.
* **Primary Reason for Recommendation (22)** (`text`, `required`): Specify the primary reason for your final recommendation.
* **Additional Evaluation Notes (23)** (`text`, `required`): Provide any additional notes or comments about the software.
* **Date Checklist Completed (24)** (`date`, `required`): Enter the date the checklist was completed.
