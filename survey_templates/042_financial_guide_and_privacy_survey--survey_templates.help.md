# Financial Guide And Privacy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Financial Guide And Privacy Survey" is designed to collect feedback and preferences from users regarding financial guides and privacy concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully, and answer according to your preferences.
2. Choose the correct option that best represents your opinion for each question.
3. Make sure to fill in all the required fields, as indicated by the asterisk symbol (*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **financial_guide_preferences** (select multiple): This question is to gather your preferences regarding the financial guide. Choose all the options that apply to you:
	+ Yes: I am interested in the financial guide.
	+ No: I am not interested in the financial guide.
* **privacy_comfort_levels** (text): This question is to understand your comfort levels with certain privacy concerns. Please type your answer in the text field.
* **financial_guide_and_privacy_survey_page_3** (number): This question is to gather more detailed information about your financial situation. Please enter a number that best represents your answer.
* **financial_guide_and_privacy_survey_page_5** (date): This question is to gather more information about a specific date that is relevant to your financial situation. Please enter a date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **financial_guide_and_privacy_survey_page_6** (time): This question is to gather more information about a specific time that is relevant to your financial situation. Please enter a time in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
* **financial_guide_and_privacy_survey_page_7** (email): This question is to gather contact information for follow-up communication. Please enter your valid email address.
* **financial_guide_and_privacy_survey_page_9** (select one): This question is to gather your opinion on a specific matter. Choose one of the options:
	+ Yes: I agree.
	+ No: I disagree.
* **financial_guide_and_privacy_survey_page_10** (select one): This question is to gather your opinion on another specific matter. Choose one of the options:
	+ Yes: I agree.
	+ No: I disagree.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly, as your input will help us improve our services.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- This survey is not mandatory, but your input is greatly appreciated.
