# Utility Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our utility service. This will help us understand customer satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your name and organization to help us identify your account and understand your experience.
2. Answer the satisfaction level questions honestly, selecting one of the provided options (High, Medium, or Low).
3. Choose the contact method you would like to use for follow-up (Phone, Email, or Web).
4. Provide your contact number if you choose to use Phone as your contact method.
5. Enter the date and time you received the service.
6. Answer the staff interaction question honestly, selecting Yes or No.
7. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
8. Choose whether you would like to be followed up on (Yes or No).
9. Enter any next steps or actions you would like to take.
10. Provide a response from our staff member.
11. Enter your response as a customer.
12. Rate your satisfaction with our service using a number from 1-5.
13. Enter any reasons for your rating.
14. Choose a rating scale (e.g., 1-5, 1-10, etc.).
15. Enter the basis for your rating.
16. Provide any suggestions for service improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, **text**, required): Enter your name as it appears on your account or identification documents.
* **Customer Organization** (`customer_organization`, **text**, optional): Enter your organization's name if you are completing this form on behalf of your company.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, **text**, required): Enter the location where you received the service.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, **select_one**, required): Choose one of the options (High, Medium, or Low) to represent your satisfaction with the service.
* **Service Reliability** (`service_reliability`, **select_multiple**, required): Select all options that apply to your experience (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Improvement Opportunities** (`improvement_opportunities`, **text**, optional): Enter any suggestions or comments about how we could improve the service.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, **select_one**, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Web).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, **text**, optional): Enter your phone number if you chose Phone as your contact method.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, **email**, optional): Enter your email address if you chose Email as your contact method.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, **date**, required): Enter the date you received the service.
* **Time Of Service** (`time_of_service`, **time**, required): Enter the time you received the service.
* **Staff Interaction** (`staff_interaction`, **select_one**, optional): Choose if you had positive interaction with our staff (Yes or No).
* **Comments** (`comments`, **text**, optional): Enter any additional comments about your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, **note**, optional): Enter any further comments or suggestions.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, **select_one**, required): Choose if you would like to be followed up on (Yes or No).
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, **text**, optional): Enter any next steps or actions you would like to take.
* **Staff Response** (`staff_response`, **text**, optional): Enter a response from our staff member.
* **Customer Response** (`customer_response`, **text**, optional): Enter your response as a customer.
* **Service Rating** (`service_rating`, **number**, required): Rate your satisfaction with our service using a number from 1-5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, **number**, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service using a number from 1-5.
* **Rating Reasons** (`rating_reasons`, **text**, optional): Enter any reasons for your rating.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, **text**, optional): Choose a rating scale (e.g., 1-5, 1-10, etc.).
* **Rating Basis** (`rating_basis`, **text**, optional): Enter the basis for your rating.
* **Service Improvement** (`service_improvement`, **text**, optional): Enter any suggestions for service improvement.
