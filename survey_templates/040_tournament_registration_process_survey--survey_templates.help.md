# Tournament Registration Process Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and insights from participants about their experience with the tournament registration process. Your responses will help us identify areas where we can improve and make informed decisions for future tournaments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each question based on your actual experience with the tournament registration process. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to skip it or provide additional comments for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Feedback
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Please share your overall feedback about your experience with the tournament registration process. What did you like or dislike about it?

### Registration Experience
* **Registration Experience** (`registration_experience`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your experience with registering for the tournament, including any challenges you faced or positive experiences you'd like to highlight.

### Registration Process
* **Registration Process** (`registration_process`, `text`, required/optional): Outline the steps you followed during the registration process. Were there any areas where you felt unclear or needed additional guidance?

### Registration Workflow
* **Registration Workflow** (`registration_workflow`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the sequence of events you encountered during the registration process, including any handovers or transitions that you felt were unclear or confusing.

### Communication
* **Communication** (`communication`, `text`, required/optional): Share your experience with the communication from the tournament organizers, including any messages or updates you received and how they helped or hindered your registration process.
