**Categorical Variable Relationship Survey - Help Guide**

### Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the relationship between two categories (Category A and Category B). It will help you understand how respondents perceive the connection between these categories, their interaction frequency, and the importance of Category B to their daily life or work.

### How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the required fields**: Ensure you answer all questions marked as "required".
2. **Select your responses**: Choose the most accurate response for each question from the provided options.
3. **Provide qualitative feedback**: Answer the "open-ended thoughts" question in a detailed but clear manner.
4. **Optional fields**: You may skip sections like "confounding factors" if you're not aware of any other variables influencing the relationship.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

#### Category A Information

* **Age Group**: Select your age group (Under 18, 18-24, ..., 65+).
* **Gender Identity**: Choose your gender identity (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say).
* **Education Level**: Select your highest level of education completed (High School, Associate Degree, ..., Doctorate or Professional).
* **Primary Employment Sector**: Choose your primary employment sector (Education, Technology, ..., Other).

#### Relationship Perception

* **In your view, how strongly does Category A influence Category B?**: Select how strongly you believe Category A influences Category B (Strong Positive Relationship, Weak Positive Relationship, No Relationship, Weak Negative Relationship, Strong Negative Relationship).
* **How often do you interact with elements from Category A?**: Choose how frequently you interact with elements from Category A (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, Never).
* **How important is Category B to your daily life or work?**: Select the importance of Category B to your daily life or work (5 - Critically Important, 4, 3 - Neutral, 2, 1 - Not Important).
* **Do you believe Category B can exist without Category A?**: Choose if you think Category B can exist without Category A (Yes, absolutely, Possibly, No, they are linked).

#### Qualitative Feedback and Additional Information

* **Describe the relationship between these two categories in your own words**: This is an open-ended question for you to elaborate on your perception of the relationship between Category A and Category B.
* **Are there any other variables that might influence this relationship?**: If you're aware of any other factors that might influence the relationship, list them here.
* **Would you participate in a follow-up interview?**: Select if you'd be interested in participating in a follow-up interview.
* **Follow-up Email (if yes)**: If you selected "Yes" for the previous question, provide your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Date**: Ensure you fill out the date you completed the form.

Please note that this form is designed to be completed by one respondent at a time. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for support.
