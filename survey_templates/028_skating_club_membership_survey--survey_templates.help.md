# Skating Club Membership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information from users about their interest in joining the skating club. It is intended to help us understand who is interested in joining the club and what kind of information we need from them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your club name in the field labeled "Club Name" on page 1 of the form.
2. Enter your name in the field labeled "Name" on page 1.
3. Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email" on page 2, which is marked as required.
4. If you want to be contacted by the club, you can optionally enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone" on page 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Club Name (1)**: Enter the name of the skating club you are interested in joining.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it should be used by the club.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address that the club can use to contact you.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number if you want the club to be able to contact you about membership.
