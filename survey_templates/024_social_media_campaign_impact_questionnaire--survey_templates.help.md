# social_media_campaign_impact_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to measure the impact of a social media campaign on various aspects of the company's social media presence. This form is to be filled out by users who have participated in or have information about a social media campaign, and is meant to gather data on the campaign's effects on platform awareness, brand awareness, engagement, conversion rates, and customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure you have the necessary information and access to fill out this form accurately.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please consult with your team or supervisor for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Impact (Text, required)** (`campaign_impact`, text, required)
	+ This field is used to describe the overall impact of the social media campaign on its intended goals.
* **Social Media Channels (Multiple Select, optional)** (`social_media_channels`, select_multiple, false)
	+ This field allows you to select which social media channels were used during the campaign (e.g. Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, etc.).
* **Platform Awareness (Number, required)** (`platform_awareness`, number, required)
	+ This field measures how much the campaign increased awareness of the company's platform or product among the target audience.
* **Brand Awareness (Number, required)** (`brand_awareness`, number, required)
	+ This field measures how much the campaign increased recognition and awareness of the company's brand among the target audience.
* **Engagement (Text, required)** (`engagement`, text, required)
	+ This field describes the level of engagement the campaign generated among the target audience (e.g. comments, likes, shares, etc.).
* **Conversion (Select One, optional)** (`conversion`, select_one, false)
	+ This field measures whether the campaign led to any conversions (e.g. sales, sign-ups, etc.).
* **Customer Satisfaction (Number, required)** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, required)
	+ This field measures how satisfied the target audience was with the campaign's content or message.
* **Message Implied (Time, required)** (`message_implied`, time, required)
	+ This field measures the time it took for the campaign's message to be understood by the target audience.
