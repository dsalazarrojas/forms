# Workforce Inclusion Strategy Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Inclusion Strategy Request Form is designed to help organizations collect and analyze data on their workforce inclusion efforts. This form will guide you through a series of questions to identify your current challenges, support needs, and proposed strategies for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form sections in order, starting from the first page.
2. For each question, provide a clear and concise answer.
3. Use the provided hints or options as guidance when needed.
4. Review each question before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organizational Context** (`organizational_context`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This section is meant to help us understand your organization's current work environment and context.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your organization and its current workforce composition.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, `text`, required: false):
	+ In this section, list the primary challenges your organization faces regarding workforce inclusion.
	+ Be specific about the areas where you're experiencing difficulties.
* **Support Needs** (`support_needs`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Describe the support systems or resources your organization needs to improve workforce inclusion.
	+ This could include training, diversity and inclusion programs, or other initiatives.
* **Current Strategies** (`current_strategies`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Share your organization's current approaches to address workforce inclusion challenges.
	+ This could include policies, practices, or procedures already in place.
* **Proposed Strategies** (`proposed_strategies`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Outline new ideas or strategies to improve workforce inclusion.
	+ Consider both short-term and long-term initiatives.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Share any additional comments, suggestions, or observations related to workforce inclusion.
	+ This is an opportunity to provide further context or insights.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Use specific examples when possible.
- Proofread your answers for clarity and accuracy.
- If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.
