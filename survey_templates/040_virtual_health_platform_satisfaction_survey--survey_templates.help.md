# Virtual Health Platform Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Virtual Health Platform Satisfaction Survey form. The form is designed to collect user feedback on their experience with the Virtual Health Platform Services (Vhps).

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each section, and answer the questions in the order they appear. Make sure to provide clear and concise answers, as this will help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction to Vhps** (`intro`, `text`, optional): This is a short description of your experience with the Virtual Health Platform Services (Vhps). Please provide a brief introduction to your experience with our services.
* **Satisfaction with Vhps** (`satisfaction_with_vhps`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with our services. Please select one of the options: Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
* **Satisfaction with Vhps Services** (`satisfaction_with_vhps_services`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your satisfaction with the specific services offered by Vhps. Please select one or more of the available options (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) to rate your satisfaction.
* **Rating Vhps** (`rating_vhps`, `number`, required): This question asks for a numerical rating of your satisfaction with Vhps. Please enter a number between 1 and 5.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any suggestions you have for us to improve our services.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, `email`, optional): This is the contact information of the user, which will be used to reach out to them for further clarification or discussion.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): This is an opportunity to provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with our services.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please make sure to provide them clearly and concisely.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
