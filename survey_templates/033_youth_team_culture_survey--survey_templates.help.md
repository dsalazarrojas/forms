# Youth Team Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Team Culture Survey is designed to measure the culture and effectiveness of a youth team, likely within an organization or institution that works with young people. This survey is intended to gather information on how team members feel about their team's morale, communication, and sense of belonging. This data can help the team lead and administrators make informed decisions to improve team dynamics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question based on your own experiences and feelings about the team.
2. Make sure to select the best answer that reflects your thoughts and opinions.
3. If you are unsure about any question, you can select "Not Specified" or "Other" as an option.
4. For the "communication_channels" question, you can select one or more options that apply to your team's communication methods.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required/optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Team Morale** (`team_morale`, `number`, required/optional): On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate your team's morale? Please consider factors like team spirit, motivation, and overall positive energy.
* **Team Communication** (`team_communication`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the team's communication style. Is it open, honest, and inclusive? Please give an example or two if possible.
* **Team Belonging** (`team_belonging`, `number`, required/optional): On a scale of 1-5, how strong do you feel about your connection to the team? Please consider factors like feeling valued, supported, and connected to the team.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, `select_one`, required/optional): What methods does the team use to communicate with each other? Please select one or more options that apply to your team's communication methods.
* **Team Belonging Value** (`team_belonging_value`, `number`, required/optional): On a scale of 1-5, how important is feeling part of the team to you? Please consider factors like feeling valued, supported, and connected to the team.
* **Communication Value** (`communication_value`, `number`, required/optional): On a scale of 1-5, how important is communication to you in the team? Please consider factors like clear and respectful communication, active listening, and conflict resolution.
* **Morale Value** (`morale_value`, `number`, required/optional): On a scale of 1-5, how important is team morale to you? Please consider factors like team spirit, motivation, and overall positive energy.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and truthfully based on your experiences and feelings.
* If you are unsure about any question, you can select "Not Specified" or "Other" as an option.
* If you have any suggestions or comments about the survey, please feel free to share them with the team lead or administrator.
