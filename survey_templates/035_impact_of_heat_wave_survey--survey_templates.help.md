# Impact of Heat Wave Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "impact_of_heat_wave_survey" form is designed to collect information on the impact of heat waves, coping strategies, and demographic information from individuals and communities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the general information section with your name, email, and other relevant details.
2. Provide information about the heat wave, such as its severity and duration.
3. Select the level of impact of the heat wave on you or your community.
4. List any coping strategies you employed during the heat wave, such as staying hydrated, staying inside, or taking breaks.
5. Mention any local needs that arose due to the heat wave.
6. Fill in the demographic information, including your location, time zone, and date.
7. Select your time of completion for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, required/optional): Fill in your name, email, and other relevant details to identify yourself.
* **Heat Wave Information** (`heat_wave_info`, text, required/optional): Describe the heat wave you experienced, including its severity and duration.
* **Heat Wave Impact** (`heat_wave_impact`, select one, required/optional): Select the level of impact of the heat wave on you or your community: Severe impact, Moderate impact, or No impact.
* **Coping Strategies** (`coping_strategies`, select multiple, required/optional): Choose all the coping strategies you used during the heat wave, such as staying hydrated, staying inside, or taking breaks.
* **Local Needs** (`local_needs`, text, required/optional): Mention any local needs that arose due to the heat wave.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, text, required/optional): Fill in your location, time zone, and date to provide context for your responses.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Specify your location.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, text, required/optional): Choose your time zone.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select your time of completion for this form.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling in the text fields.
* Select only one option for "Heat Wave Impact" and all relevant options for "Coping Strategies".
* Use a standard format for your location and time zone.
* Ensure to select your correct time of completion for the form.

Please follow these instructions to complete the "impact_of_heat_wave_survey" form accurately and thoroughly.
