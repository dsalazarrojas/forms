# Criminal Justice Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to criminal justice topics. It is intended for research and educational purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the title of your submission in the `form_title` field.
2. Provide a brief summary of your criminal case or event in the `brief_details` field.
3. Describe the police response to your case in the `police_response` field.
4. Share the impact on the victim in your case in the `impact_on_victim` field.
5. Enter the time of the incident or event in the `time` field, using the correct format for time (HH:MM:SS).
6. Enter the date of the incident or event in the `date` field, using the correct format for dates (YYYY-MM-DD).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (text, optional): Enter a brief title for your submission, e.g., "John Doe's Criminal Case".
* **brief_details** (text, optional): Provide a brief summary of your criminal case or event. Be as detailed as possible, including any relevant facts and circumstances.
* **police_response** (text, optional): Describe the response of the police to your case, including any actions taken or decisions made.
* **impact_on_victim** (text, optional): Share the impact on the victim in your case, including any emotional or physical effects.
* **time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the incident or event in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the incident or event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
