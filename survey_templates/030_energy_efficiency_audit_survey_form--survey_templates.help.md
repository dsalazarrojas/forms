# energy_efficiency_audit_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Efficiency Audit Survey Form is designed to collect data about a building's energy characteristics, usage patterns, and efficiency measures. This information will help in assessing a building's energy efficiency and providing recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each field carefully and answer according to your knowledge or access to building information.
- You can fill out this form on any page, as each page is separate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Energy Efficiency Audit Survey Form (Page 1)** (`text`, required: False): This page is a general description of the purpose and scope of the energy efficiency audit.
* **Company Information (Page 2)** (`text`, required: False): Enter information about the company or building owner.
* **Building Details (Page 3)** (`text`, required: False): Provide a brief description of the building's characteristics.
* **Building Characteristics (Page 4)** (`select_one`, required: False): Select the type of building (Commercial, Residential, Industrial).
* **Lighting Characteristics (Page 5)** (`number`, required: False): Enter the number of lighting fixtures or units installed in the building.
* **Energy Usage Patterns (Page 6)** (`select_multiple`, required: False): Choose which energy usage patterns apply to your building (Peak Hours, Non-Peak Hours, Base Case).
* **Equipment Characteristics (Page 7)** (`text`, required: False): Enter information about the building's equipment characteristics.
* **Energy Efficiency Measures (Page 8)** (`select_multiple`, required: False): Choose which energy efficiency measures apply to your building (Lighting, Mechanical, Electrical).
* **Energy Usage Data (Page 9)** (`date`, required: False): Enter the date of the most recent energy usage data available.
* **Survey Completion (Page 10)** (`select_one`, required: False): Confirm that you have filled out this form correctly.

## Tips
- If you need help, refer to the provided help guide for each field.
- Ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult the building's owner or manager for assistance.
