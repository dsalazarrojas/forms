# Senior Care Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Care Onboarding Survey is a form used to collect information about a resident's medical conditions, emergency contacts, and other relevant details during the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct senior care facility from the list of options.
2. Enter the resident's date of admission to the facility.
3. Provide a brief description of the resident's medical conditions.
4. Enter the name and contact information for the emergency contact.
5. List the resident's current medications.
6. Provide information about any known medical conditions.
7. List any allergies or sensitivities the resident has.
8. Indicate if a social worker is assigned to the resident.
9. Enter the name and contact information for the resident's doctor.
10. Indicate the nature of the resident's relationship with their doctor.
11. Provide a description of any emergency services the resident requires.
12. List any necessary medical supervision for the resident.
13. Confirm onboarding completion date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **senior_care_facility**: Enter the name of the senior care facility where the resident is currently living.
* **new_resident_date_of_admission**: Enter the date the resident was admitted to the facility.
* **resident_medical_conditions**: Describe the resident's current medical conditions, including any chronic illnesses or injuries.
* **emergency_contact_name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_phone**: Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **medications**: List any medications the resident is currently taking.
* **medical_conditions**: Provide a detailed description of any known medical conditions.
* **resident_allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities the resident has.
* **social_worker**: Check 'True' if a social worker is assigned to the resident, 'False' otherwise.
* **social_worker_name**: Enter the name of the assigned social worker (if applicable).
* **doctor_contact_name**: Enter the name of the resident's doctor.
* **doctor_contact_phone**: Enter the phone number for the doctor's contact information.
* **doctor_contact_relation**: Describe the nature of the resident's relationship with their doctor.
* **emergency_services**: Describe any emergency services the resident requires (e.g., emergency alert system, etc.).
* **medical_supervision**: Provide information about any necessary medical supervision (e.g., regular check-ins, etc.).
* **onboarding_completed**: Enter the date and time the form was completed.

## Tips

- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and clearance to access and complete this form.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information you enter for each field.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the facility's policies and procedures for guidance.
