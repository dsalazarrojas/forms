# Behavior Based Psychometric Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess an individual's behavior in a workplace setting, helping to provide insights into their preferences and tendencies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each section honestly, based on your typical behavior in a workplace setting.
2. Select the response that best describes your behavior for each question.
3. Be careful when filling out the assessment, as it is intended to provide accurate and meaningful results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment ID or Employee Number** (`participant_id`, `text`, required): Enter your unique identifier or employee number to help us track your results.
* **-- Social and Team Interaction --** (`section_social_interaction`, `note`, required): This section assesses your behavior in social situations. Select the response that best describes how you typically interact with your colleagues.
* **When faced with a conflict in a team, I usually** (`conflict_resolution_style`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you typically handle conflicts within teams:
	+ Seek a compromise immediately
	+ Stand my ground to ensure the right outcome
	+ Listen to all sides before forming an opinion
	+ Avoid the conflict if possible
* **I feel energized when meeting new people and networking** (`networking_comfort_level`, `number`, required): Rate how much you enjoy meeting new people and networking:
	+ 1 strongly disagree
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 strongly agree
* **I prefer to work on complex tasks** (`team_vs_individual_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you typically prefer to work:
	+ Entirely independently
	+ Primarily independently with occasional check-ins
	+ Collaboratively with a small team
	+ Within a large, structured group
* **-- Decision Making and Problem Solving --** (`section_decision_making`, `note`, required): This section examines your approach to decision-making and problem-solving. 
* **My decisions are primarily driven by** (`data_vs_intuition`, `select_one`, required): Choose what typically drives your decisions:
	+ Objective data and logic
	+ Past experience and patterns
	+ Intuition and gut feeling
	+ Consensus and group input
* **How would you rate your professional risk tolerance?** (`risk_tolerance_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your comfort with taking risks:
	+ 1 very cautious
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 very risk-taking
* **I am comfortable making decisions with incomplete information** (`response_to_ambiguity`, `number`, required): Rate your comfort with making decisions without complete information:
	+ 1 strongly disagree
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 strongly agree
* **-- Organization and Structure --** (`section_organization_structure`, `note`, required): This section evaluates your approach to organization and structure. 
* **How detailed are your project plans usually?** (`planning_detail_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose how detailed your project plans typically are:
	+ Extremely detailed (every step mapped)
	+ Moderately detailed (key milestones only)
	+ High-level (flexible approach)
	+ Minimal (I prefer to adapt as I go)
* **Meeting a deadline is more important than perfect quality** (`deadline_adherence_perception`, `number`, required): Rate the importance of meeting deadlines:
	+ 1 strongly disagree
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 strongly agree
* **-- Completion --** (`section_summary_attestation`, `note`, required): This section is for your confirmation of honest answers and timestamp of completion. 
* **I have answered these questions to the best of my knowledge** (`assessment_honesty_statement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have answered honestly to the best of your knowledge:
	+ I Confirm
* **Date of Completion** (`completion_timestamp`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time you completed the assessment.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and accurately to get meaningful results.
- If you are unsure about any question, choose the option that best describes your typical behavior.
- Do not fill out the assessment more than once per person.
