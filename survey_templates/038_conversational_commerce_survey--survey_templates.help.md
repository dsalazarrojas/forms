# Conversational Commerce Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences with conversational commerce, a type of commerce that involves interacting with chatbots or other digital agents to purchase products or services. The survey aims to understand your preferences, behaviors, and concerns related to conversational commerce, as well as your satisfaction with its ease of use, response quality, and problem resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and email address (if you'd like to receive follow-up information)
2.  Select your age range from the options provided
3.  Choose your gender (if you're comfortable sharing this information)
4.  Provide your location (country or region)
5.  Answer the questions related to your use of conversational commerce:
