# event_meal_contribution_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from event attendees about their experiences with meal contributions, specifically their ratings and satisfaction levels. The form will help event organizers understand what attendees think about the quality and quantity of food provided, and what suggestions they have for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the rating and satisfaction levels for each section.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" and "Suggestion Box" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Rating (1)
* **Event Rating** (`event_meal_rating`, number): Rate the overall quality of the food provided. A rating of 1-5 is displayed as "Very Satisfied" to "Very Dissatisfied".

### Future Contribution (2)
* **Future Contribution** (`future_contribution`, select_one): Choose whether or not you think the food contribution has potential for future events.

### Comments (3)
* **Comments** (`comments`, text): Provide any thoughts or concerns you have about the food contribution.

### Email (4)
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates on future events.

### Phone (5)
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates on future events.

### Additional Comments (6)
* **Additional Comments** (`comments_2`, text): Provide any additional thoughts or concerns you have about the food contribution.

### Event Feedback (7)
* **Event Feedback** (`event_feedback`, text): Provide any comments or suggestions about the event itself.

### Recommendation (8)
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, radio): Choose whether or not you would recommend this event to others.

### Attendance Frequency (9)
* **Attendance Frequency** (`attendance_frequency`, select_one): Rate how often you attend events like this one.

### Event Satisfaction (10)
* **Event Satisfaction** (`event_satisfaction`, select_one): Rate your level of satisfaction with the event.

### Venue Satisfaction (11)
* **Venue Satisfaction** (`venue_satisfaction`, select_one): Rate your level of satisfaction with the venue.

### Suggestion Box (12)
* **Suggestion Box** (`suggestion_box`, text): Provide any suggestions or thoughts about the event or venue.

## Tips
- Please answer each question as honestly as possible.
- If you have any specific concerns or suggestions, please include them in the corresponding fields.
- Your input will help event organizers understand what works and what needs improvement.
