# Investment Bonds Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your awareness, motivations, and experiences related to bond investments. It is used to help us better understand your needs and preferences when it comes to bond investments. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant answers to each question based on your personal experiences and thoughts. For text fields, provide a brief but detailed description. For select and date fields, choose the options that best describe your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Awareness of bonds** (`awareness`, text, optional): Please describe your level of awareness about bond investments. This will help us understand how much you know about bond investments.
- **Motivations for bond investment** (`motivations`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options that best describes your motivations for investing in bonds:
  - Bond investing for returns
  - Bond investing for income
  - Bond investing for growth
  - Other (please specify)
- **Satisfaction with bond investments** (`satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that best describe your satisfaction level with your bond investments:
  - Very satisfied
  - Somewhat satisfied
  - Neutral
  - Somewhat dissatisfied
  - Very dissatisfied
- **Education about bond investments** (`education`, date, optional): Please provide any relevant dates for your education or training related to bond investments.
- **Experience with bond investments** (`experience`, time, optional): Please describe the amount of time you've spent investing in bonds.
- **Demographic information** (`demographic_information`, email, optional): Please provide your contact information so we can get back to you if needed.
- **Demographic details** (`demographic_details`, text, optional): Please provide any additional demographic details you'd like to share.
- **Demographics** (`demographics`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to your demographics:
  - Male
  - Female
  - Other
  - Prefer not to say
- **Age** (`demographics_2`, number, optional): Please provide your age.
- **Demographic details 2** (`demographic_details_2`, text, optional): Please provide any additional demographic details you'd like to share.
- **Occupation** (`demographics_3`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to your occupation:
  - Working
  - Student
  - Other
  - Prefer not to say
- **Annual income** (`demographics_4`, number, optional): Please provide your annual income.
- **Education level** (`demographics_5`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to your education level:
  - High school
  - Bachelor
  - Master
  - Other
  - Prefer not to say
- **Employment status** (`demographics_6`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to your employment status:
  - Employed
  - Self-employed
  - Retired
  - Other
  - Prefer not to say
- **Location** (`demographics_7`, text, optional): Please provide your location.
- **Investment experience** (`demographics_8`, number, optional): Please describe the amount of experience you have with bond investments.
- **Investment goals** (`demographics_9`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to your investment goals:
  - Wealth management
  - Retirement planning
  - Diversification
  - Other
  - Prefer not to say
- **Investment horizon** (`demographics_10`, number, optional): Please provide the time frame for your investments.
- **Frequency of investment decisions** (`demographics_11`, text, optional): Please describe how often you make investment decisions.
- **Investment knowledge** (`demographics_12`, text, optional): Please describe your level of knowledge about investments.
- **Investment confidence** (`demographics_13`, text, optional): Please describe your level of confidence in your investment decisions.

- **Additional comments** (`demographics_16`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback.
