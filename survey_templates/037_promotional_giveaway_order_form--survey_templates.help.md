# promotional_giveaway_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to collect participant information and details for a promotional giveaway order. It is used to gather the necessary data to process and fulfill the order. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field with the required and optional information.
2. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, optional): Enter the participant's information, if applicable.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter the shipping details for the order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter the order details, if applicable.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more shipping methods for the order.
	+ Select all applicable options.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address for the order.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, optional): Enter the shipping cost, if applicable.
* **Order Cost** (`order_cost`, number, optional): Enter the order cost, if applicable.
* **Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the participant's name.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional participant notes, if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
