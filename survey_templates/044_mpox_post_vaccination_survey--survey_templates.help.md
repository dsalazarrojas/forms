# Mpox Post-Vaccination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about patients after receiving the Mpox vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Vaccination Date** of the patient in the date field.
2. Select the **Vaccination Site** where the patient received the vaccine.
3. Enter any **Symptoms** experienced by the patient.
4. Choose the **Vaccination Dose** administered to the patient.
5. Enter the **Patient ID** (if available).
6. If necessary, select any **Medical Conditions** associated with the patient.
7. If applicable, enter the **Vaccination Date 2** of the second dose.
8. Select the type of **Vaccine Type** administered (Mpox, Comiranta, or Paxlovid).
9. Enter the **Vaccine Batch** number for tracking purposes.
10. Enter any **Symptoms** experienced by the patient (if different from the previous question).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vaccination Date**: Enter the date the patient received the vaccine.
* **Vaccination Site**: Select the location where the patient received the vaccine (Left Arm, Right Arm, Left Leg, Right Leg, Left Foot, Right Foot).
* **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms experienced by the patient after vaccination.
* **Vaccination Dose**: Enter the dose number of the vaccine administered.
* **Patient ID**: Enter the ID number of the patient (if available).
* **Medical Condition**: Select any medical conditions associated with the patient (Fever, Rash, Swelling, Redness, or Other).
* **Vaccination Date 2**: Enter the date of the second dose of the vaccine (if applicable).
* **Vaccine Type**: Select the type of vaccine administered (Mpox, Comiranta, or Paxlovid).
* **Vaccine Batch**: Enter the batch number of the vaccine for tracking purposes.
* **Symptoms**: Repeat the question for any additional or different symptoms experienced by the patient.
