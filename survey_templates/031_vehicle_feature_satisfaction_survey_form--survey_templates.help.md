# Vehicle Feature Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers on their satisfaction with various features of vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the applicable options for each field as instructed.
3. Ensure that you answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification document.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

### Vehicle Model
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, select multiple, required): Select the models of the vehicle(s) you are providing feedback about. Use the dropdown options to choose one or more models that apply to your feedback.

### Feature Satisfaction
* **Feature Satisfaction** (`feature_satisfaction`, select one, required): Rate your satisfaction level with the features of the vehicle(s) you are providing feedback about, using the options provided:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied

### Vehicle Feature Satisfaction Rating
* **Vehicle Feature Satisfaction Rating** (`vehicle_feature_satisfaction_rating`, number, required): Provide a numerical rating from 1 to 10 to rate your overall satisfaction with the vehicle's features.

### Comment
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the vehicle's features.

### Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please let us know so we can address them accordingly.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
