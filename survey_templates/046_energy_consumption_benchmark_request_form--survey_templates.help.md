# Energy Consumption Benchmark Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting energy consumption benchmark data from facility managers or energy consultants. It helps us collect and analyze energy consumption information for our energy efficiency department.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **First Name**, **Email**, and **Phone Number** fields with your personal details.
2. Provide your **Building Location** and **Building Type** accurately.
3. Select the **Energy Source** and **Energy Type** that best suits your building's energy consumption.
4. Enter the **Benchmark Date** and **Benchmark Time** when you would like to collect the data.
5. Fill out the **Benchmark Duration** and **Comments** fields to provide more details about the data you are requesting.
6. Provide your **Facility Manager's Email**, **Company Name**, and **Building ID** (if applicable).
7. Enter the **Energy Consumption Data** and select the **Energy Units** and **Energy Consumption Rate** accordingly.
8. Select the **Energy Consultant** if you are not the facility manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please fill out your first name accurately.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): The date you are submitting this form.
* **Building Location** (`building_location`, text, required): Enter the exact location of your building.
* **Facility Manager Email** (`facility_manager_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your facility manager.
* **Energy Consumption Data** (`energy_consumption_data`, text, required): Describe the energy consumption data you would like to collect.
* **Energy Source** (`energy_source`, select_one, required): Select the energy source of your building (e.g., Gas, Electricity, Steam).
* **Energy Type** (`energy_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of energy your building consumes (e.g., Heating, Cooling, Lighting).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or details about the data you are requesting.
* **Building Manager** (`building_manager`, text, optional): If you are not the facility manager, enter their name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Building Type** (`building_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of building (e.g., Commercial, Residential, Industrial).
* **Building Size** (`building_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of your building (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
* **Benchmark Date** (`benchmark_date`, date, required): The date you would like to collect the data.
* **Benchmark Time** (`benchmark_time`, time, required): The time you would like to collect the data.
* **Benchmark Duration** (`benchmark_duration`, number, required): The duration of the data collection.
* **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`, text, optional): If you are not the facility manager, enter their name.
* **Energy Consumption** (`energy_consumption`, number, required): Enter your energy consumption data.
* **Energy Units** (`energy_units`, select_one, required): Select the units of your energy consumption data (e.g., kWh, MMBtu).
* **Energy Consumption Rate** (`energy_consumption_rate`, number, required): Enter the energy consumption rate.
* **Energy Consultant** (`energy_consultant`, text, optional): If you are not the facility manager, enter their name.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or details.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Building ID** (`building_id`, text, optional): Enter your building ID.

Before you submit this form, please make sure to fill it out accurately and carefully. If you are unsure about any information, please ask for help.
