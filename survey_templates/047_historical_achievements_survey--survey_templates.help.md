# Historical Achievements Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the public's familiarity with historical events, their perceived impact on daily life, and other related aspects. It is essential to provide honest and accurate responses to help us better understand the historical context.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the first page "Survey Start" for an introduction to the survey.
2. Read and understand each field's label and purpose before entering your response.
3. Select the correct options for each field according to your knowledge and understanding.
4. Fill in the "Historical Events" field by selecting up to 10 historical events from the given options.
5. Provide your honest assessment of each event's importance using the "How important do you think each event was?" field.
6. Choose the correct historical figure(s) from the "Historical Figure" field.
7. Select the correct historical period from the "Historical Period" field.
8. Choose the correct option for "Impact on Law", "Impact on Society", and "Impact on Economy" fields.
9. If you have any other historical figures or periods that you think are relevant, you can use the "Historical Figure (other)" and "Historical Period (other)" fields.
10. If you have any other impacts on law, society, or economy, you can use the "Impact on Law (other)", "Impact on Society (other)", and "Impact on Economy (other)" fields.
11. Fill in the "Other Event" field if you have any other historical event that you think is important.
12. Select the frequency with which you have heard about this event using the "How often have you heard of this event?" field.
13. If the event date is not known or not remembered, you can fill in the "Date" field with an approximate or exact date.
14. Select the time of the event using the "Time" field.
15. Fill in the "Event Importance" field with a number between 1-10 to rate the event's importance.
16. Fill in the "Perceived Importance" field with a number between 1-10 to rate the event's importance as perceived by others.
17. Fill in the "Learning Habits" field with any historical learning habits that you would like to mention.
18. Finally, click on "Survey End" to submit your responses.
