# Kitchen Renovation Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients regarding their recent kitchen renovation experience. It will help us evaluate client satisfaction and identify areas for improvement in our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your level of satisfaction with our services in the "Client Satisfaction" section.
2. Provide any comments or details about your project in the "Project Details" section.
3. Indicate if you participated in our referral program in the "Referral Program" section.
4. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
5. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Client Phone" field.
6. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Client E-mail" field.
7. Select the services you received in the "Renovation Services" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Satisfaction** (`Client Satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options to indicate your level of satisfaction with our services. You can choose between "Satisfied" and "Dissatisfied".
* **Project Details** (`Project Details`, `note`, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or details about your project. This is an optional field, but if you have any information you'd like to share with us, please do not hesitate to do so.
* **Referral Program** (`Referral Program`, `select_multiple`, required): Please indicate if you participated in our referral program. You can select one or more of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Client Name** (`Client Name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Project Manager** (`Project Manager`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the project manager who worked on your project.
* **Renovation Services** (`Renovation Services`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the services you received during your renovation. You can choose one or more of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Client Phone** (`Client Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the client.
* **Client E-mail** (`Client E-mail`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person. Please ensure that the email address is valid and in the correct format.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Project Details" field to provide them.
