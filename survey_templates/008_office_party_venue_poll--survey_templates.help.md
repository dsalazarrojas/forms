# office-party-venue-poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for an office party poll, aiming to gather feedback on venue preferences and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:

* Enter your favorite food in the "what's your favorite food" field.
* Select a date for the party from the calendar.
* Choose whether you prefer 'Yes' or 'No' for the "venue" field.
* Select multiple options for "venue type" and "guest list" fields.
* Choose additional "guest list" options.
* Enter the party time for the event.
* Enter an additional party time.
* Enter a note (optional).
* Enter your email address in the "contact email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "contact phone" field.
* Enter an additional email address.
* Enter an additional phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **what_is_your_favorite_food** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your favorite food to help us get a better idea of the party menu.
- **party_date** (`date`, required/optional): Select a date from the calendar for the party.
- **venue** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for the main venue options.
- **venue_type** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple options for the venue type.
- **guest_list** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose multiple options for the guest list.
- **guest_list_2** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select additional guest list options.
- **partytime** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the party time.
- **partytime_2** (`time`, required/optional): Enter an additional party time.
- **note** (`note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes for the party.
- **contact_email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **contact_phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **contact_email_2** (`email`, required/optional): Enter an additional email address.
- **contact_phone_2** (`text`, required/optional): Enter an additional phone number.
- **contact_phone_3** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your third contact phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your responses are included in the poll results.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us get a better understanding of your preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
