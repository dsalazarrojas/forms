# Mom Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from mothers to understand their personal details, habits, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, provide a brief answer.
4. Please do not duplicate your responses.
