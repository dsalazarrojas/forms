# Scientific Conference Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the first name field.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
4. Answer the question about sessions you liked, selecting one option that best suits your experience.
5. List the sessions you didn't like.
6. Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about the conference.
7. Rate the quality of presentations you attended.
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
9. Rate your satisfaction with the event venue, food, and other aspects.
10. Identify areas where the event could improve.
11. Confirm if you're willing to submit your feedback.
12. Select how you'd like to be followed up (phone, email, or mail).
13. Specify the frequency of follow-up (if applicable).
14. Set the date and time for the next follow-up (if applicable).
15. Enter the location for the next follow-up (if applicable).
16. Add any additional note for the next follow-up (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required): 
Please enter your first name.
### 2. **Email** (`attendee_email`, `email`, required): 
Please enter your email address.
### 3. **Phone** (`attendee_phone`, `text`, optional): 
Please enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
### 4. **Liked Sessions** (`session_liked`, `select_one`, optional): 
Please select one option that best describes the session you liked.
### 5. **Disliked Sessions** (`session_disliked`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
Please list the sessions you didn't like.
### 6. **Session Suggestions** (`session_suggestion`, `text`, optional): 
Please add any suggestions you have for the conference.
### 7. **Presentation Quality** (`presentation_quality`, `number`, optional): 
Please rate the quality of presentations you attended.
### 8. **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `text`, optional): 
Please rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
### 9. **Event Satisfaction** (`event_satisfaction`, `text`, optional): 
Please rate your satisfaction with the event venue, food, etc.
### 10. **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, optional): 
Please list areas where the event could improve.
### 11. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): 
Please add any additional comments or suggestions you have.
### 12. **Submission** (`feedback_submission`, `select_one`, optional): 
Please confirm if you're willing to submit your feedback.
### 13. **Follow up** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
Please select how you'd like to be followed up (phone, email, or mail).
### 14. **Follow Up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, `number`, optional): 
Please specify the frequency of follow-up (if applicable).
### 15. **Next Follow Up** (`follow_up_next`, `date`, optional): 
Please set the date and time for the next follow-up (if applicable).
### 16. **Next Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_next_time`, `time`, optional): 
Please set the time for the next follow-up (if applicable).
### 17. **Next Follow Up Place** (`follow_up_next_place`, `text`, optional): 
Please enter the location for the next follow-up (if applicable).
### 18. **Next Follow Up Note** (`follow_up_next_note`, `text`, optional): 
Please add any additional notes for the next follow-up (if applicable).

### Tips:
- Be honest in your feedback, and provide suggestions that can improve the event.
- Please fill out all fields that apply to your experience.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [Your Email/Contact].
