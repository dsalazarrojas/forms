# International Trade Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Trade Survey is designed to collect information on international trade activities, helping us understand the current state of trade in various markets and industries.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select all the markets that your business operates in from the list of options under "Markets Served".
2.  Enter the trade volume (in a numerical value) in the "Trade Volumes" field.
3.  If applicable, select all the trade bodies your business has served or is involved with from the list of options under "Trade Bodies Served".
4.  Enter the number of consultants involved in trade activities in the "Consultants Involved" field.
5.  If applicable, select all the research areas your business is involved with from the list of options under "Research Areas".
6.  If applicable, select all the market characteristics your business is involved with from the list of options under "Market Characteristics".
7.  Check the box to indicate whether trade partnerships are in place.
8.  Select the frequency of participation in trade events from the list of options under "Trade Events Participation".
9.  If applicable, select all the documentation types your business has prepared or is involved with from the list of options under "Trade Documentation".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Markets Served** (`Markets Served`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select all the markets that your business operates in to provide accurate information about your international trade activities.
* **Trade Volumes** (`Trade Volumes`, `number`, Required): Enter the trade volume (in a numerical value) to report the current state of your business's trade activities.
* **Trade Challenges** (`Trade Challenges`, `select_one`, Required): If there are any trade challenges your business is facing, choose the option that best describes the situation.
* **Trade Bodies Served** (`Trade Bodies Served`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select all the trade bodies your business has served or is involved with to provide a complete picture of your international trade activities.
* **Consultants Involved** (`Consultants Involved`, `number`, Optional): Enter the number of consultants involved in trade activities to report the level of engagement.
* **Researchers Involved** (`Researchers Involved`, `select_multiple`, Optional): If applicable, select all the researchers involved in trade activities to provide accurate information.
* **Research Areas** (`Research Areas`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select all the research areas your business is involved in to report the scope of your trade activities.
* **Market Characteristics** (`Market Characteristics`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select all the market characteristics your business is involved with to report the specific aspects of your trade activities.
* **Trade Partnerships** (`Trade Partnerships`, `checkbox`, Optional): Check this box to indicate whether trade partnerships are in place.
* **Trade Events Participation** (`Trade Events Participation`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the frequency of participation in trade events to report your business's level of engagement.
* **Trade Documentation** (`Trade Documentation`, `select_multiple`, Optional): If applicable, select all the documentation types your business has prepared or is involved with to provide a complete picture of your trade activities.
