# social_entrepreneurship_awareness_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "social_entrepreneurship_awareness_survey" form is designed to help us understand the level of awareness and engagement among individuals or organizations regarding social entrepreneurship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the introduction section, which provides context or instructions on how to proceed.
2. Select your awareness level of social entrepreneurship (high, medium, low).
3. Choose the type(s) of entrepreneurship you are interested in (social enterprise, non-profit, for-profit).
4. Indicate your current level of engagement (high, medium, low).
5. Choose whether you currently have any programs in place (yes/no).
6. Select the type(s) of support you need (financial, logistical, mentorship, other).
7. Indicate whether you have any community outreach (yes/no).
8. Choose your time commitment (less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, 2-5 hours, 5-10 hours, more than 10 hours).
9. Indicate whether you have completed the survey before (yes/no).
10. Enter your contact email and phone number (though these fields may be optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (1)**: A brief welcome message or instructions on how to complete the form.
* **Awareness Level (2)** (`awareness_level`, select_one, required: false): The level of awareness you have about social entrepreneurship (high, medium, low).
* **Entrepreneurship Type (3)** (`entrepreneurship_type`, select_multiple, required: false): The type(s) of entrepreneurship you are interested in (social enterprise, non-profit, for-profit).
* **Engagement Level (4)** (`engagement_level`, select_one, required: false): The level of engagement you have with social entrepreneurship (high, medium, low).
* **Current Programs (5)** (`current_programs`, select_multiple, required: false): Whether you currently have any social entrepreneurship-related programs (yes/no).
* **Support Needed (6)** (`support_needed`, select_multiple, required: false): The type(s) of support you need (financial, logistical, mentorship, other).
* **Community Outreach (7)** (`community_outreach`, select_one, required: false): Whether you have any community outreach (yes/no).
* **Time Commitment (8)** (`time_commitment`, select_one, required: false): The amount of time you are willing to commit to social entrepreneurship (less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, 2-5 hours, 5-10 hours, more than 10 hours).
* **Survey Completion (9)** (`survey_completion`, select_one, required: false): Whether you have completed the survey before (yes/no).
* **Contact Email (10)** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Your contact email (though this may be optional).
* **Contact Phone (11)** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Your contact phone number (though this may be optional).
