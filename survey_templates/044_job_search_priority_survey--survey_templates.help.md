# job_search_priority_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from candidates about their job search priorities, preferences, and requirements. This will help us better understand their needs and provide more effective services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Navigate through the pages using the navigation buttons (Next, Previous, etc.).
3. For each field, provide as much information as possible, but note that some fields are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information**: Please enter your basic contact information for our records.
* **Job Details**: Enter the job title, company name, or job description you are searching for.
* **Job Level**: Choose the level of job you are looking for (Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job you are looking for (Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Job Category**: Choose multiple categories that fit your job search (Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Salary**: Enter your expected salary range.
* **Bonus**: Enter any expected bonus range.
* **Job Location**: Enter the location where you are willing to work.
* **Job Employment Type**: Choose the type of employment you are looking for (Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Job Employment Status**: Select the employment status you are looking for (Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Job Priority**: Choose your priority level for this job search (Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Job Description**: Briefly describe the job you are looking for.
* **Job Required Skills**: List any necessary skills for the job.
* **Job Duties**: List any job-specific duties or responsibilities.
* **Job Qualifications**: List any relevant qualifications or credentials.
* **Job Education**: Enter your educational background.
* **Job Experience**: Briefly describe your relevant work experience.
* **Job References**: Enter any relevant job references.
* **Job Interview Questions**: Enter any interview questions you have been asked.
* **Salary Range**: Enter your expected salary range.
* **Other Benefits**: List any other benefits you are looking for.
* **Interviewer Comments**: Any comments you have for the interviewer.
* **Comments**: Additional comments about your job search.

**Tips**

* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Do not duplicate information that you have already submitted.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact us directly.
