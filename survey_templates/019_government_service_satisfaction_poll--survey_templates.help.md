# Government Service Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Government Service Satisfaction Poll is a form designed to gauge the satisfaction of citizens with the services provided by the government. This form is intended to help identify areas of improvement and measure the effectiveness of government services in meeting the needs of citizens.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the provided options for each question.
2. Answer each question based on your actual experience with the government service.
3. Fill out any additional comments or follow-up information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Interaction** (`service_interaction`, Select One, Required: No): Choose whether you had a satisfactory interaction with the government service. Please select "Yes" if you were satisfied with the service.
* **Follow-up Needs** (`follow_up_needs`, Select Multiple, Required: No): Check all the follow-up needs you have with the government service.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, Number, Required: False): If you have any suggestions or complaints, please rate the quality of the service you received.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, Number, Required: False): Rate your overall experience with the government service.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, Select One, Required: No): Check if you had follow-up contact with the government service.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, Select One, Required: No): Check if the follow-up method was satisfactory.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, Select One, Required: No): Check if the follow-up contact method was satisfactory.
* **Next Interaction** (`next_interaction`, Select One, Required: No): Choose whether you expect to have another interaction with the government service.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, Select One, Required: No): Check the status of your follow-up request.
* **Follow-up Followed** (`follow_up_followed`, Select One, Required: No): Check if you followed up on the issue.
* **Follow-up Followed Other** (`follow_up_followed_other`, Text, Required: False): If other, please specify.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, Text, Required: False): Enter any additional comments about your follow-up experience.
* **Follow-up Other** (`follow_up_other`, Text, Required: False): If other, please specify.
* **Service Rating** (`service_rating`, Number, Required: False): Rate the overall quality of the service provided.
* **Follow-up Needs Other** (`follow_up_needs_other`, Text, Required: False): If other, please specify.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, Text, Required: False): Enter any additional comments about your follow-up experience.
