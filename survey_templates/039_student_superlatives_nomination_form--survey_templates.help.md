# Student Superlatives Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Superlatives Nomination Form is designed to acknowledge and recognize students who exhibit extraordinary qualities and contributions on campus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category you would like to nominate a student for (e.g., Most Inspiring, Innovative, etc.).
2. Enter the name and contact information of the student you are nominating.
3. Enter the name and contact information of the nominator (if different from the student).
4. Optionally, provide additional information about the student's contributions and qualities that warrant recognition.
5. Click "Submit" to send your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Most Inspiring Lecturer** (`Most Inspiring`): Select the lecturer who has been the most inspiring to you during your academic journey.
* **Nominator** (`Lecturer's Name`): Enter the name of the lecturer you are nominating.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Info`): Enter the contact information of the lecturer you are nominating (e.g., office phone number, email, etc.).
* **Email** (`Email`): Enter the email address of the lecturer you are nominating.
* **Midnight Warrior** (`Midnight`): Select the lecturer who has demonstrated exceptional leadership and dedication.
* **Nominator's Name** (`Nominator's Name`): Enter your name if you are nominating someone else.
* **Nominator's Contact Info** (`Nominator's Contact Info`): Enter your contact information (e.g., office phone number, email, etc.).
* **Nominator's Email** (`Nominator's Email`): Enter your email address.
* **Nominator's Title** (`Nominator's Title`): Enter your title (e.g., academic advisor, professor, etc.).
* **Nominator's Department** (`Nominator's Department`): Enter your department or office (e.g., engineering, business, etc.).
* **Nominator's Position** (`Nominator's Position`): Enter your position or role (e.g., professor, advisor, etc.).
* **Nominator's Phone** (`Nominator's Phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Nominator's Email (2)** (`Nominator's Email 2`): Enter your email address.
* **Other** (`Other`): Select additional qualities or characteristics that you would like to mention about the student (e.g., leadership, mentorship, etc.).
