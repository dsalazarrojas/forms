# Teacher Autonomy And Creativity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure the level of autonomy and creativity that teachers feel in their working environments. It's a tool for understanding the factors that influence their work and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, based on your personal experience and perceptions.
2. Select the option that best describes your level of agreement or influence from the provided choices.
3. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome** (`Introduction`, note, optional): This is a welcome note that provides an introduction to the survey.
* **How much freedom** (`Autonomy_freedum`, number, optional): This question assesses how much freedom you feel you have in your work. Choose a number that best represents your level of autonomy.
* **Innovative teaching** (`Creativity_support`, select_one, optional): This question measures how much support you receive for innovative teaching practices. Select the option that best describes your level of influence.
* **Teacher influence** (`Teacher_influence`, select_multiple, optional): This question evaluates how much influence you feel from your colleagues, administrators, or other teachers. Select all that apply.
* **Decision making** (`Decision_making`, select_one, optional): This question assesses how much influence you have in decision-making processes. Select the option that best describes your level of agreement.
* **Communication** (`Communication`, select_one, optional): This question measures the level of influence you feel from communication with your colleagues, administrators, or students. Select the option that best describes your level of agreement.
* **Innovative teaching support** (`Innovation_support`, select_multiple, optional): This question evaluates how much support you receive for innovative teaching practices. Select all that apply.
* **Teacher ownership** (`Teacher_ownership`, select_one, optional): This question assesses how much ownership you feel for your work. Select the option that best describes your level of agreement.
* **School environment** (`School_environment`, number, optional): This question measures the overall environment and resources available to you. Choose a number that best represents your level of satisfaction.
* **Principal influence** (`Principal_influence`, select_one, optional): This question evaluates how much influence the principal or head of your school has on your work. Select the option that best describes your level of agreement.
* **Peer influence** (`Peer_influence`, select_multiple, optional): This question measures how much influence your colleagues have on your work. Select all that apply.
* **Student influence** (`Student_influence`, select_multiple, optional): This question assesses how much influence your students have on your work. Select all that apply.
* **Teacher influence 2-8** (`Teacher_influence_2` to `Teacher_influence_8`, select_multiple/one, optional): These questions evaluate the influence of various factors on your work, such as colleagues, administrators, or other teachers. Select all or the option that best describes your level of agreement.
* **Other influence 2-5** (`Other_influence_2` to `Other_influence_5`, select_one, optional): These questions measure the influence of various factors on your work, such as parents, or other external factors. Select the option that best describes your level of agreement.

## Tips

* Take your time to carefully review each question and select the option that best represents your feelings and experiences.
* If you're unsure about a particular question, try to choose the option that is most true for you.
* Remember that this survey is anonymous, and your responses will help us better understand your work environment and identify areas for improvement.
