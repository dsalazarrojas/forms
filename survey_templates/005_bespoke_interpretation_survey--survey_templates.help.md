# Bespoke Interpretation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with our interpretation service. It aims to assess the clarity, accuracy, and overall satisfaction of our interpreters and equipment to provide valuable insights for continuous improvement.

<thinking>

To ensure that you are providing the most accurate and helpful feedback, please only complete this form if you have actually used our interpretation service. If you haven't used our service before, your answers will not be taken into account and will only add noise to our data.

We are not asking you to rate or review our service lightly. Your feedback is crucial to our growth and improvement, and we appreciate your honesty and time.

<\thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in your event or meeting name** in the "Event or Meeting Name" field.
2. **Select all the languages you used** for the interpretation in the "Languages Interpreted" field.
3. **Rate the clarity and accuracy of the interpretation** in the "Clarity and Accuracy of Interpretation" field.
4. **Choose your level of subject matter knowledge** in the "Knowledge of Subject Matter" field.
5. **Rate the professionalism and conduct of the interpreter** in the "Professionalism and Conduct" field.
6. **Rate the audio clarity** in the "Audio Clarity Rating" field.
7. **Rate the reliability of the interpretation equipment** in the "Reliability of Interpretation Equipment" field.
8. **Rate your experience with our platform** in the "Ease of Use of Interpretation Platform" field.
9. **Rate your overall experience** on a scale of 1 to 10 in the "Overall Experience Rating" field.
10. **Let us know if you would use our service again** in the "Would you use this interpretation service again" field.
11. **Provide any additional recommendations for improvement** in the "Recommendations for Improvement" field.
12. **Optional: Your name and organization** fields are for your convenience, but not required.
13. **Optional: Your contact email address** field is for us to reach out to you with questions or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event or Meeting Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event or meeting where you used our interpretation service.
* **Languages Interpreted** (`languages_utilized`, select_multiple, required): Select all languages that were used for the interpretation.
* **Clarity and Accuracy of Interpretation** (`interpreter_clarity_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how clear the message was in the target language, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Knowledge of Subject Matter** (`subject_matter_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate how well the interpreter understood technical terms used.
* **Professionalism and Conduct** (`interpreter_professionalism`, select_one, required): Rate the interpreter's professionalism, where 1 is unprofessional and 5 is highly professional.
* **Audio Clarity Rating** (`audio_clarity_score`, select_one, required): Rate the audio clarity, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Reliability of Interpretation Equipment** (`equipment_reliability`, select_one, required): Rate the reliability of the interpretation equipment, where 1 is failed to work and 5 is completely reliable.
* **Ease of Use of Interpretation Platform** (`platform_ease_of_use`, select_one, required): Rate how easy it was to use our platform, where 1 is difficult and 5 is very easy.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (`overall_experience_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience, from 1 to 10.
* **Would you use this interpretation service again** (`future_service_intent`, select_one, required): Let us know if you would use our service again.
* **Recommendations for Improvement** (`improvement_recommendations`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or suggestions on how we can improve our service.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, if you'd like to be acknowledged in our feedback.
* **Organization Name** (`respondent_organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization's name, if you'd like to be acknowledged.
* **Contact Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address, if you'd like us to reach out to you.
* **Your Email** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, for us to contact you with questions or follow-up.
