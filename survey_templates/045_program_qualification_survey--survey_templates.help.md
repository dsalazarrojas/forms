# Program Qualification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant details about your background, training, and experience in relation to the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please answer the questions to the best of your knowledge and truthfully.
2.  Be concise in your responses, as this form is not a comprehensive survey.
3.  If you are unsure about any question, please do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
4.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form, as this will help us assess your qualifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Program Ready
•  **Program Ready**: This field is a simple text input where you can provide a brief statement about your current program status. You can type in your own answer, up to 100 characters.

### 2. Applicant Details
•  **Applicant Details**: This field is a text input for you to provide your personal details. Please enter your relevant contact information, such as your name, email address, and phone number, up to 100 characters.

### 3. Training Provider Info
•  **Training Provider Info**: This field is a text input for you to provide information about your training provider. Please enter the name of your training provider, up to 100 characters.

### 4. Program Readiness Insights
•  **Program Readiness Insights**: This field is a text input for you to provide any additional comments or insights about your program readiness. You can enter up to 200 characters.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and provide accurate information to ensure a smooth assessment process.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx. 
*   This form is a brief assessment tool, and additional documentation may be required for the formal application process.
*   Review your answers carefully before submission.
