# Storage Solutions Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with Storage Solutions. Your responses will help us improve our services and better serve you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. Click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`title`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
* **Storage Used** (`storage_used`, `text`, **optional**): If you have used Storage Solutions' services, please describe your experience with us.
* **How would you rate this storage product?** (`rating_product`, `select_one`, **optional**): Rate your experience with the storage product using the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **What is your satisfaction with this storage product?** (`satisfaction_with_product`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose one or more of the options that best describe your satisfaction with the product (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **What is your satisfaction with this storage service?** (`satisfaction_with_service`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose one or more of the options that best describe your satisfaction with the service (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Storage Service Used** (`storage_service_used`, `text`, **optional**): If you have used Storage Solutions' services, please describe your experience with them.
* **What is your storage product?** (`storage_product`, `text`, **optional**): If you have used a specific storage product, please describe it.
* **What is your preferred storage location?** (`storage_location`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose one of the options that best describe your preferred storage location (Home, Office, Other).
* **How long do you store your data?** (`storage_duration`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose one of the options that best describe how long you store your data (Short term, Long term, Other).
* **Would you like to share more contact details?** (`contact_details`, `select_one`, **required**): If you would like to provide more information about yourself, please select "Yes" and we will contact you.
* **Share your name** (`contact_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
* **Share your phone number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your phone number.
* **Share your email address** (`contact_email`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your email address.
* **Leave a message** (`contact_message`, `text`, **optional**): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Is this submission confirmation?** (`submission_confirmation`, `note`, **optional**): This is a confirmation that you have submitted your feedback.
* **What type of storage product would you like to provide more feedback on?** (`storage_product_category`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose one or more of the options that best describe the type of storage product you would like to provide feedback on (Yes, No).
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **optional**): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Would you like to contact us?** (`contact_us`, `select_one`, **required**): If you would like to contact us, please select "Yes" and we will contact you.
* **What is the reason for your feedback?** (`feedback_reason`, `text`, **optional**): If you would like to provide the reason for your feedback, please enter it here.
* **How did you hear about Storage Solutions?** (`storage_solutions`, `text`, **optional**): If you have any information about how you heard about Storage Solutions, please enter it here.
* **Have you used Storage Solutions' services before?** (`storage_solutions_service`, `select_one`, **optional**): If you have used Storage Solutions' services, please select "Yes".
* **How satisfied are you with Storage Solutions?** (`storage_solutions_satisfaction`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose one of the options that best describe your satisfaction with Storage Solutions (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **How would you rate Storage Solutions' products?** (`storage_solutions_product`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose one of the options that best describe your experience with Storage Solutions' products (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Would you recommend Storage Solutions?** (`storage_solutions_recommend`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose one of the options that best describe your willingness to recommend Storage Solutions (Yes, No).
