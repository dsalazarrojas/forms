# Visitor Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback on your experience visiting our website. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance your overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the questions carefully and answer them as honestly as possible.
2. Fill out the form completely, but only if you've recently visited the website.
3. If you've already completed this form, please don't fill it out again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to our website** (1):
	+ A brief introduction to our website, explaining the purpose of this survey.
* **How Satisfying was your Visit** (2):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with your visit to the website (Scale: 1-5, where 1 is not satisfying and 5 is very satisfying).
* **How Satisfying was your Visit 2** (3):
	+ Similar to the previous question, rate your overall satisfaction with your visit to the website (Scale: 1-5, where 1 is not satisfying and 5 is very satisfying).
* **Do you recommend this website?** (4):
	+ Answer "Yes", "No", or "Undecided" to indicate whether you would recommend our website to others.
* **What do you enjoy most about this website?** (5):
	+ Describe in a few sentences what you enjoyed about your visit to the website.
* **What could we improve?** (6):
	+ Describe in a few sentences what you think we could improve on our website.
* **Any comments or suggestions?** (7):
	+ Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have about your visit to the website.
* **Your Email** (8):
	+ If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your time and feedback in helping us improve our website!
