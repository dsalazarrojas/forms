# Onion Shed Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onion Shed Management Survey is a form designed for onion farmers to gather information about their onion sheds, including storage capacity, conditions, and challenges faced during storage. This form helps users optimize their practices and improve the onion storage process.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Go to the Onion Shed Management Survey form.
*   Start filling out the form from the first field.
*   Complete each field according to the guidelines below.
*   Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Onion Shed Name** (`onion_shed_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your onion shed. This will help identify your specific onion storage area and ensure accurate tracking.
*   **Shed Location** (`shed_location`, text, required: false): Provide the physical address or location of your onion shed, including street address, town, or country. This helps track the storage location and facilitates coordination with other onion farmers.
*   **Shed Capacity** (`shed_capacity`, number, required: false): Enter the storage capacity of your onion shed in units or tons. This information helps estimate the amount of onions that can be stored.
*   **Shed Operations** (`shed_operations`, select_multiple, required: false):
    *   Select all the operations that take place in your onion shed. These operations might include:
        + Operations
        + Maintenance
        + Harvesting
        + Packing
        + Cleaning
        + Monitoring
        + Repair
*   **Storage Type** (`storage_type`, select_one, required: true): Choose from the following storage types:
    *   Silo
    *   Bin
    *   Room
    *   Crate
    *   Rack
*   **Storage Capacity** (`storage_capacity`, number, required: false): Enter the capacity of your storage in units or tons.
*   **Storage Location** (`storage_location`, text, required: false): Provide the location where the stored onions are kept, including any specific details like floor numbers or aisle numbers.
*   **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, select_multiple, required: false):
    *   List all challenges you have faced while storing onions, such as:
        + Equipment failure
        + Labour shortages
        + Storage capacity
        + Weather conditions
        + Insect pests
        + Water damage
        + Fire
*   **Storage Outcome** (`storage_outcome`, select_one, required: false):
    *   Choose how you would rate the storage outcome:
        + Very Good
        + Good
        + Fair
        + Poor
*   **Storage Outcome Description** (`storage_outcome_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the storage outcome, explaining your choice.
*   **Onion Shed Manager** (`onion_shed_manager`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person in charge of the onion shed operations.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Provide the name of the contact person for the onion shed.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the onion shed.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email of the onion shed.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to make about your onion shed.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the support team.
*   This form is designed for onion farmers to gather valuable insights into their onion sheds, so be as detailed as possible.
