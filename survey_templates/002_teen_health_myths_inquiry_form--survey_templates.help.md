# Teen Health Myths Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Teen Health Myths Inquiry Form is designed to gather information on commonly held myths about health among teenagers.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and fill it out completely.
- For each field, enter accurate and honest information about the myth you are referring to.
- Make sure to answer all questions as thoroughly as possible.
- If a question does not apply to the myth, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Commonly Held Myths**: This is a free-text field where you can describe or list the commonly held myths about health among teenagers. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **What is the common myth?**: Describe the specific myth you are referring to. This field is for a brief explanation of the myth.
* **How many students believe this myth?**: Choose the percentage of students you believe hold this myth, based on your understanding or experience.
* **Where does this myth originate?**: Select the source of the myth, such as schools, parents, social media, or other.
* **How often do you believe this myth impacts student health?**: Choose how often you believe the myth affects student health, such as often, sometimes, or rarely.
* **What source of information do you rely on when you hear a myth?**: Select the source of information you rely on when you hear a myth, such as teachers, social media, parents, or other.
* **How reliable is the source of the myth?**: Evaluate the reliability of the source of information you rely on, such as very reliable, somewhat reliable, or not very reliable.
* **Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or observations about the myth.
* **How does this myth affect student health?**: Choose how much you believe the myth affects student health, such as very much, somewhat, or not very much.
* **How would you like to address this myth in health education?**: Select how you would like to address the myth in health education, such as include it, omit it, or address it in a different way.
* **Other comments**: This field is for any additional comments or observations about the myth.

Note: This form is for educational and research purposes, and the data collected will help to better understand the beliefs and attitudes of students. Please ensure that you have filled out this form accurately and honestly.
