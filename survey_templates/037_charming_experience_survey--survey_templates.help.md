# Charming Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their visit to a charming location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please make sure you have visited the location before taking this survey.
2. Review the questions carefully and answer honestly.
3. Select the rating that best describes your experience.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: This is an optional field where you can provide your name. If you prefer to remain anonymous, you can skip this field.
* **Email Address**: This field is for follow-up purposes only. We will use it to get in touch with you if needed, but your email address will not be shared with anyone else.
* **Date of Visit**: Please select the date of your visit to the location.
* **Is This Your First Visit**: Choose whether this was your first time visiting the location or if you've been here before.
* **Overall Experience Rating**: Select the rating that best describes your overall experience at the location.
* **Atmosphere and Ambiance**: Choose the rating that best describes the atmosphere and ambiance of the location.
* **Cleanliness and Maintenance**: Select the rating that best describes the cleanliness and maintenance of the location.
* **Staff Friendliness and Helpfulness**: Choose the rating that best describes how friendly and helpful the staff was.
* **Speed of Service**: Select the rating that best describes the speed of service you received.
* **Staff Knowledge and Expertise**: This field is optional, but if you have experience with the staff, please rate their knowledge and expertise.
* **Quality of Products or Services**: Select the rating that best describes the quality of what you purchased or the service you received.
* **Value for Money**: Choose the rating that best describes whether you thought the price was worth it.
* **Likelihood to Return**: Select the rating that best describes your likelihood to return to the location.
* **Would You Recommend Us to Others**: Choose whether you would recommend the location to others.
* **What Went Well**: Provide any positive comments about your experience.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions on how we can improve the location.
* **Likelihood of Recommending to Others**: Choose whether you would recommend us to others.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the text fields.
* Your feedback will help us improve the location, and we appreciate your time and consideration.
