# Employee English Language Training Needs Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to assess the English language training needs of employees to help identify areas of improvement and create a personalized training plan to enhance their language skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions in each section, providing as much detail as possible. The form has multiple sections, each with its own set of questions. Please review each section carefully and answer the questions according to your current English language training status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Language Training Needs** (`language_training_needs`): This field is where you can describe your current English language training needs. Please provide a brief description of what you need help with.
* **English Level** (`english_level`): This field is to help us understand your current level of English proficiency. Please select the level that best describes your current skills.
* **Prior Experience with English** (`prior_experience`): Have you had any prior experience with English language training? If yes, please select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
* **English Language Skills** (`english_language_skills`): Please select the English language skills you would like to improve. You can choose one or multiple skills from the list.
* **English Language Training Goals** (`english_language_training_goals`): What specific English language skills do you want to focus on for training? Please provide a brief description of your goals.
* **Frequency of English Training** (`training_frequency`): How often would you like to have English language training sessions? Please select the frequency that best suits your needs.
* **Proficient in English Language Training** (`english_proficient`): Do you feel proficient in English language training? If yes, please provide a brief explanation of your proficiency level.
* **English Training Facility** (`training_facility`): Where would you like to have English language training sessions? Please provide a location or describe a preferred location.
* **Supervisor Confirmation** (`supervisor_confirmation`): Have you confirmed with your supervisor that you need English language training? If yes, please provide a confirmation message.
* **Current English Language Proficiency Level** (`english_language_proficiency`): What is your current English language proficiency level? Please select the level that best describes your skills.
* **Prior English Language Courses** (`prior_english_courses`): Have you taken any English language courses in the past? If yes, please provide a brief description.
* **English Language Training Needs** (`english_language_training_needs`): Please describe any additional English language training needs you may have.
* **English Language Training Objectives** (`english_language_training_objectives`): What are your English language training objectives? Please provide a brief description.
* **English Language Training Status** (`training_status`): What is the status of your English language training? Please select the status that best describes your situation.
* **Location of English Language Training** (`training_location`): Where will the training sessions be held? Please provide a location or describe a preferred location.
* **Language of Training Sessions** (`training_language`): What language would you like to use for English language training sessions? Please provide a language or describe a preferred language.

**Note:** All fields are optional and are meant to help us understand your current English language training needs. Please answer as much as you can, and provide any additional details that may be helpful for us to create a personalized training plan for you.
