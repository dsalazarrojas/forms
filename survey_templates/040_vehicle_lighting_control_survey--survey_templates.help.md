## Vehicle Lighting Control Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on vehicle lighting controls. It is meant to gauge user opinions on the effectiveness and satisfaction with the current vehicle lighting system. This survey is part of our ongoing effort to improve vehicle safety and functionality. It is not a required form, and your input is voluntary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the answers that apply to you from each section.
2. Answer as honestly as possible, and be thorough in your input.
3. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_lighting_controls** (Multiple Select)
	+ This section asks about your vehicle's current lighting controls.
	+ You have two options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
	+ Answer 'Yes' if your vehicle's lighting controls are currently in use or functional.
	+ Answer 'No' if your vehicle's lighting controls are not currently in use or functional.
* **satisfaction** (Select One)
	+ This section asks about your level of satisfaction with your vehicle's current lighting controls.
	+ You have two options: Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
	+ Answer 'Satisfied' if you are pleased with your vehicle's lighting controls.
	+ Answer 'Dissatisfied' if you are not satisfied with your vehicle's lighting controls.
* **safety_features** (Multiple Select)
	+ This section asks about the current safety features available on your vehicle.
	+ You have two options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
	+ Answer 'Yes' if your vehicle is equipped with safety features like automatic emergency services or hazard lights.
	+ Answer 'No' if your vehicle is not equipped with safety features.
* **reported_issues** (Multiple Select)
	+ This section asks about any reported issues with your vehicle's current lighting controls.
	+ You have two options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
	+ Answer 'Yes' if you have reported an issue with your vehicle's lighting controls.
	+ Answer 'No' if you have not reported an issue with your vehicle's lighting controls.
* **priority_features** (Select One)
	+ This section asks about the priority of features you would like to see in a future vehicle lighting system.
	+ You have two options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
	+ Answer 'Yes' if you would like to see a specific feature added or improved in a future vehicle lighting system.
	+ Answer 'No' if you do not see a need for any specific feature improvements in a future vehicle lighting system.

## Tips
* Please be as thorough as possible when answering.
* Select 'Yes' or 'No' only for the options that apply to your specific vehicle.
* Take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, contact the help desk for guidance.
