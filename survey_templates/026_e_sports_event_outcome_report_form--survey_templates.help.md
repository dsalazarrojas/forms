# e_sports_event_outcome_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about e-sports events, such as tournament details, performance, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the tournament information (page 1).
2. Select the date of the event (page 2).
3. Choose the venue where the event took place (page 3).
4. Specify the type of tournament (page 4).
5. Select the game mode (page 5).
6. Choose the outcome of the event (page 6).
7. Provide any relevant information about player performance (page 7).
8. Describe the audience engagement (page 8).
9. Evaluate the overall success of the event (page 9).
10. Add any feedback or comments (page 10).
11. Enter the contact information of the event organizer and player (pages 11-12).
12. Add any additional comments or details about the event (page 13).
13. Specify the game version (page 14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tournament Info (Page 1)**: Enter a brief description of the tournament, including its name and details.
* **Date (Page 2)**: Select the date when the e-sports event took place.
* **Venue (Page 3)**: Choose the location where the event occurred.
* **Tournament Type (Page 4)**: Choose between Team Based, Solo, or Combination as the type of tournament.
	+ Team Based: A tournament with teams participating in it.
	+ Solo: A tournament where one player competes individually.
	+ Combination: A mix of team and solo competition.
* **Game Mode (Page 5)**: Select between Casual or Competitive as the type of game mode.
	+ Casual: A game mode with a relaxed atmosphere and non-competitive environment.
	+ Competitive: A game mode with a high level of competition and intensity.
* **Event Outcome (Page 6)**: Choose one or more of the following outcomes: Won, Lost, or Drawn.
	+ Won: The team or player emerged victorious.
	+ Lost: The team or player did not emerge victorious.
	+ Drawn: The outcome was a tie or a draw.
* **Player Performance (Page 7)**: Enter any comments or information about player performance, such as scores, strategies, or notable moments.
* **Audience Engagement (Page 8)**: Describe the engagement level of the audience, including their reactions, interactions, or feedback.
* **Overall Success (Page 9)**: Evaluate the success of the event, including its organization, execution, and impact.
* **Feedback (Page 10)**: Add any comments or suggestions for future events.
* **Additional Comments (Page 11)**: Add any additional information or comments about the event.
* **Organizer Contact (Page 12)**: Enter the contact information of the event organizer.
* **Player Contact (Page 13)**: Enter the contact information of the player or team.
* **Event Details (Page 14)**: Add any additional comments or details about the event.
* **Game Version (Page 15)**: Specify the version of the game played during the event.
