# remote_learning_survey_for_kindergarten_kids - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from kindergarten parents or caregivers on their experiences and opinions about remote learning initiatives for their children. The goal is to understand the effectiveness of these initiatives and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. You may skip any questions that do not apply to your child's situation.
3. Please provide clear and concise responses in the text fields.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields provided at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **parent_response** (`parent_response`, text, optional): This is the first question where you can share your thoughts, opinions, or experiences about the remote learning initiative.
* **parent_response_2** (`parent_response_2`, text, optional): This question is similar to the first one, where you can provide your feedback or opinions about the remote learning program.
* **parent_response_3** (`parent_response_3`, text, optional): This question is another opportunity to share your thoughts or experiences about the remote learning initiative.
* This pattern continues for **parent_response_4**, **parent_response_5**, **parent_response_6**, **parent_response_7**, **parent_response_8**, **parent_response_9**, **parent_response_10**, **parent_response_11**, and **parent_response_12**.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions that apply to your child's situation.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields provided at the end of the form.
* Remember to provide clear and concise responses in the text fields.
* If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact the form author at form@gic.mx.
