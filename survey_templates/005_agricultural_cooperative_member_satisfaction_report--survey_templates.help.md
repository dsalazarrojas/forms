# Agricultural Cooperative Member Satisfaction Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from agricultural cooperative members about their experience and satisfaction with the cooperative's services. This will help the cooperative to understand its strengths and weaknesses and make data-driven decisions to improve its services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your member name and ID.
2. Select your membership duration.
3. Choose your primary agricultural activity.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the cooperative services, supplies, pricing fairness, support staff helpfulness, and communication effectiveness.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
6. Rate your likelihood to recommend the cooperative to others (on a 1-10 scale).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Name** (`member_name`, Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your membership card.
* **Member ID** (`member_id`, Required): Enter your unique cooperative ID.
* **Membership Duration** (`membership_duration`, Required): Choose the duration of your membership.
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ 10+ years
* **Farm Type** (`farm_type`, Required): Choose your primary agricultural activity.
	+ Crop Farming
	+ Livestock
	+ Mixed Farming
	+ Dairy
	+ Horticulture
	+ Other
* **Satisfaction with Services** (`service_satisfaction`, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with cooperative services.
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Quality of Supplies** (`supplies_quality`, Required): Rate the quality of the supplies provided.
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
	+ N/A
* **Pricing Fairness** (`pricing_fairness`, Required): Rate the fairness of the cooperative's pricing.
	+ Unfair
	+ Fair
	+ Very Fair
* **Support Staff Helpfulness** (`support_staff`, Required): Rate the helpfulness of the cooperative's support staff.
	+ Not Helpful
	+ Somewhat Helpful
	+ Very Helpful
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication`, Required): Rate the cooperative's communication effectiveness.
	+ Ineffective
	+ Effective
	+ Very Effective
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvements`, Optional): Provide any suggestions or comments for improvement.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_recommend`, Required): Rate your likelihood to recommend the cooperative to others (on a 1-10 scale).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
