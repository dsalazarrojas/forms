# trade_union_agreement_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from union members regarding their agreement to a trade union agreement. It helps the union to gauge the collective opinion of its members on the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the **introduction** field with a brief statement about your understanding of the trade union agreement.
* In the **member_info** field, provide any additional information you deem relevant about yourself as a member.
* In the **vote** field, select **Yes** or **No** to indicate your approval or rejection of the trade union agreement.
* In the **signature** field, add your signature (not a typed signature, but a handwritten or digital one).
* In the **member_name** field, enter your name as per your membership ID card.
* In the **union_agreement** field, provide any further comments or remarks about the trade union agreement.
* In the **agreement_date** field, enter the date you are signing this form.
* In the **eligible** field, select **Yes** or **No** to indicate if you are eligible to vote on this trade union agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction (id: 1)**: This is a text field where you can write a brief statement about your understanding of the trade union agreement.
* **member_info (id: 2)**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional information you deem relevant about yourself as a member.
* **vote (id: 3)**: This field allows you to select **Yes** or **No** to indicate your approval or rejection of the trade union agreement.
* **signature (id: 4)**: This is a note field where you can add your signature, which should match the signature on your membership ID card.
* **member_name (id: 5)**: Please enter your name as per your membership ID card in this text field.
* **union_agreement (id: 6)**: This is a text field where you can provide any further comments or remarks about the trade union agreement.
* **agreement_date (id: 7)**: This is a date field where you can enter the date you are signing this form.
* **eligible (id: 8)**: This field allows you to select **Yes** or **No** to indicate if you are eligible to vote on this trade union agreement.
