# Training Course Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help us understand your preferences and interests regarding training courses. Your input will help us shape future course offerings to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select all that apply from the provided options.
2. Fill in your preferred email address and/or phone number if you would like to be contacted regarding the course.
3. Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Topic of Interest** (`select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select "Yes" if you're interested in taking a course on this topic.
* **Training Method** (`select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all methods that interest you (e.g., in-person, online, or blended).
* **Preferred Scheduling Method** (`select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select "Yes" if you have a preferred scheduling method (e.g., weekdays or weekends).
* **Other Preferred Scheduling Method** (`text`, required: false):
	+ If your preferred scheduling method isn't listed, please provide your own.
* **Schedule Time** (`time`, required: false):
	+ If you have a specific schedule in mind, please enter it in the format 12:00 PM.
* **Schedule Time Note** (`note`, required: false):
	+ Add any additional information about your schedule preferences.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address to be contacted about the course.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number to be contacted about the course.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
