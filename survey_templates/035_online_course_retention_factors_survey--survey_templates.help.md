# Online Course Retention Factors Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about the factors that influence student retention in an online course. The purpose of this survey is to collect feedback from students, instructors, and administrators to identify areas that need improvement and optimize the learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: Read the introduction page, which provides background information about the survey.
2. Select the factors that influence student retention from the list on **What Factors Influence Student Retention**.
3. Indicate how students feel about their course on **How Students Feel About Their Course**.
4. Identify what would improve the course on **What Would Improve the Course**.
5. Provide your contact information on **Contact Information** (optional).
6. Enter your name and institution (if applicable) on **Name**, **Institution**, and **Role** (optional).
7. Answer the question about the course details on **Course Name**, **Course Level**, **Course Section**, and **Year** (optional).
8. Complete the survey on **Survey Completed** (optional).
9. Provide any additional comments on **Additional Comments** (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What Factors Influence Student Retention** (2):
	+ Select all the factors that you think influence student retention. The options are:
		- Retention and Engagement
		- Quality of Education
		- Student Support
		- Other
* **How Students Feel About Their Course** (3):
	+ Select how students feel about their course, where:
		- Very Satisfied
		- Somewhat Satisfied
		- Somewhat Dissatisfied
		- Very Dissatisfied
* **What Would Improve the Course** (4):
	+ Select all the options that you think would improve the course. The options are:
		- Online Resources
		- Course Content
		- Instructor Support
		- Other
* **Comments** (5):
	+ Enter any comments or feedback about the survey or the course.
* **Retention Factors** (6):
	+ Select all the factors that you think influence student retention. The options are:
		- Financial Constraints
		- Family Obligations
		- Other
* **Contact Information** (7):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (8):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (9):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Institution** (10):
	+ Enter your institution's name.
* **Role** (11):
	+ Enter your role in the institution.
* **Department** (12):
	+ Enter the department where you are affiliated with.
* **Course Name** (13):
	+ Enter the name of the course.
* **Course Level** (14):
	+ Enter the level of the course (e.g., undergraduate, graduate, etc.).
* **Course Section** (15):
	+ Enter the section number or letter.
* **Year** (16):
	+ Enter the academic year of the course.
* **Term** (17):
	+ Enter the term of the course (e.g., Fall, Spring, Summer).
* **How Was the Survey for You** (18):
	+ Select how easy or difficult the survey was for you:
		- Very Easy
		- Somewhat Easy
		- Somewhat Difficult
		- Very Difficult
* **When Did You Complete the Survey** (19):
	+ Enter the date and time you completed the survey.
* **Time Spent on the Survey** (20):
	+ Enter the amount of time you spent on the survey.
* **Additional Comments** (21):
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback about the survey or the course.

Note: Please fill out all required fields, and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields. Thank you for your participation in this survey!
