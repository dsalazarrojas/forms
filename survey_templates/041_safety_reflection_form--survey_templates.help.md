# Safety Reflection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Safety Reflection Form is designed to collect information about incidents that have occurred, with the aim of identifying areas for improvement and enhancing workplace safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the incident you are reporting.
2. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
3. Indicate if your team was involved in the incident.
4. Choose the type of incident that occurred.
5. Indicate if a near miss was experienced.
6. Choose the actions taken to prevent similar incidents.
7. Provide a brief description of the incident.
8. Record any lesson learned from the incident.
9. Outline an action plan to address the incident.
10. Enter the date of the last review of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): Type in the location where the incident occurred.
* **Team Involved** (`team_involved`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if your team was involved in the incident.
	+ Select `Yes` if your team was involved.
	+ Select `No` if your team was not involved.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of incident that occurred.
	+ Select `Option 1`
	+ Select `Option 2`
	+ Select `Option 3`
* **Near Miss** (`near_miss`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if a near miss was experienced.
	+ Select `Yes` if a near miss was experienced.
	+ Select `No` if no near miss was experienced.
* **Prevention Actions** (`prevention_actions`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the actions taken to prevent similar incidents.
	+ Select `Yes` if you took prevention actions.
	+ Select `No` if no prevention actions were taken.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Lesson Learned** (`lesson_learned`, text, required: false): Record any lesson learned from the incident.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`, text, required: false): Outline an action plan to address the incident.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the last review of the incident in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form.
* Make sure to review and update this form regularly to ensure accuracy and completeness of the information collected.
