# Makeup Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your preferences, habits, and experiences with makeup. Your responses will help us improve our product offerings and customer support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the page with the "how_do_you_like_making_up" field first.
2. Fill in your answer to this page in the provided text field.
3. Continue to the next page, "age", and enter your age if required.
4. On the "favorite_brand" page, select your favorite brand from the available options.
5. On the "do_you_use_makeup" page, select all that apply regarding your use of makeup.
6. On the "when_do_you_start_wearing_makeup" page, select the best fit for when you start using makeup.
7. On the "how_much_do_you_like_it" page, rate your enjoyment of makeup on a scale.
8. On the "favorite_product_type" page, select your favorite type of product.
9. On the "what_is_your_favorite_color" page, select all that apply regarding your favorite color.
10. On the "how_long_have_you_been_wearing_makeup" page, enter the number of years you've been using makeup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How Do You Like Making Up** (`how_do_you_like_making_up`, text, required): Please describe in as few as 50 characters or more about how you like making up.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): If required, please enter your age.
* **Favorite Brand** (`favorite_brand`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following: Mac, Estee Lauder, L'Oreal.
* **Do You Use Makeup** (`do_you_use_makeup`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply: Morning routine, Night time, Both, Never.
* **When Do You Start Wearing Makeup** (`when_do_you_start_wearing_makeup`, time, optional): Please select the best fit for when you start using makeup.
* **How Much Do You Like It** (`how_much_do_you_like_it`, number, optional): Rate your enjoyment of makeup on a scale of 1-5.
* **Favorite Product Type** (`favorite_product_type`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following: Foundation, Mascara, Powder.
* **What Is Your Favorite Color** (`what_is_your_favorite_color`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply: Red, Pink, Blue, Green.
* **How Long Have You Been Wearing Makeup** (`how_long_have_you_been_wearing_makeup`, number, optional): Please enter the number of years you have been using makeup.
