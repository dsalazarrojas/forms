# Fitness Studio Customer Journey Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Studio Customer Journey Survey is designed to gather feedback on various aspects of your visit to help us improve our services and facilities.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2.  Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3.  Enter your responses in the provided text or select fields.
4.  Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction (1)
*   **Introduction** (`intro`, `note`, required: false): This field is a brief note about your visit. Please use this space to provide any necessary context or comments about your experience.

### Visit Date (2)
*   **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your visit to the Fitness Studio.

### Visit Time (3)
*   **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your visit to the Fitness Studio.

### Staff Member (4)
*   **Staff Member** (`staff_member`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if you had a staff member during your visit. Please select 'Yes' if you had a staff member assigned to you.

### Visit Service (5)
*   **Visit Service** (`visit_service`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the type of service you received during your visit. Please select 'Yes' if you received the service.

### Class Type (6)
*   **Class Type** (`class_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the type of class or session you attended during your visit. Please select 'Yes' if you attended a class or session.

### Satisfaction (7)
*   **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with your visit. Select 'Satisfied' if you were satisfied or 'Dissatisfied' if not.

### Visit Frequency (8)
*   **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate how often you visit the Fitness Studio. Please select 'Yes' if you visit regularly or 'No' otherwise.

### Visit Reason (9)
*   **Visit Reason** (`visit_reason`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate why you visited the Fitness Studio. Please select 'Yes' if this was your first visit or 'No' otherwise.

### Member Feedback (10)
*   **Member Feedback** (`member_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the Fitness Studio.

### Additional Comments (11)
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any further comments or suggestions, please use this field to provide them.

### Tips

*   Please answer each question truthfully and accurately.
*   If you have any additional feedback or comments that do not fit in the provided fields, please use the 'Additional Comments' field.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance or clarification.
