# Expectations_Assessment_Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Expectations_Assessment_Survey form is designed to assess individual expectations and priorities within a team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. Answer the questions honestly and take as much time as you need.
3. Complete each page according to the instructions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Section 1: introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This is an optional note field where you can take a moment to review the survey instructions and purpose before proceeding.
* **Section 2: section_a** (`section_a`, `select multiple`, optional): Select all the options that best describe your expectations.
* **Section 3: section_b** (`section_b`, `text`, optional): Type in your detailed answer in this free-text field.
* **Section 4: section_c** (`section_c`, `time`, optional): Enter a specific time or date in this field.
* **Section 5: introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This is another optional note field where you can take a moment to review the survey again.
* **Section 6: section_d** (`section_d`, `select one`, optional): Select one option that best describes your answer.
* **Section 7: section_e** (`section_e`, `number`, optional): Enter a whole number in this field.
* **Section 8: section_f** (`section_f`, `number`, optional): Enter a decimal number in this field.
* **Section 9: section_g** (`section_g`, `date`, optional): Select a specific date.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Take your time to complete each section carefully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your organization's support team.
