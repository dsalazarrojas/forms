# Student Performance Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Performance Assessment Questionnaire is a tool used to assess the performance of students in an educational setting. It is meant to be completed by teachers or instructors to provide feedback on a student's progress, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the student's profile and any previous assessments to ensure consistency and accuracy in your feedback.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing specific examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
3. Use the space provided for each question to provide detailed feedback.
4. Complete each question on the relevant page (e.g. "introduction", "student_info", "assessment_info", "feedback", "additional_feedback", "overall_performance").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (Page 1):
	* A brief overview of the student's current performance, including any notable achievements or challenges.
- **Student Info** (Page 2):
	* Please provide a brief description of the student, including their strengths and any notable characteristics.
- **Assessment Info** (Page 3):
	* Describe the student's performance in specific classes or subjects, including any notable successes or areas for improvement.
- **Feedback** (Page 4):
	* Provide feedback on the student's overall performance, including strengths, areas for improvement, and any recommendations for future growth.
- **Additional Feedback** (Page 5):
	* Provide any additional feedback or comments that are not captured in the previous questions.
- **Overall Performance** (Page 6):
	* Rate the student's overall performance, using the scale provided.
