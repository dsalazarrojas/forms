# Healthy Relationship Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Healthy Relationship Checklist is a self-assessment form designed to help you evaluate the quality and strength of your relationship. It's a tool to provide you with a snapshot of your relationship's current state, helping you identify areas of improvement and potential concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. For each question, select a number that best represents your current situation. For example, if you're very satisfied with your relationship, select 5. If you're not satisfied, select a lower number.
3. Take your time and don't rush through the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate the quality of your relationship** (`relationship_quality`, number, required): This question is about how satisfied you are with your relationship overall. Choose a number that best reflects your feelings.
* **How would you rate your communication** (`communication`, number, optional): This question is about the quality of communication in your relationship. Consider how open, honest, and effective you and your partner are when discussing important issues.
* **How would you rate conflict resolution** (`conflict_resolution`, number, optional): This question is about how you and your partner handle disagreements and conflicts. Think about whether you're able to resolve issues in a healthy and constructive way.
* **Intimacy** (`intimacy`, number, optional): This question is about the closeness and connection you feel with your partner. Consider how often you initiate physical affection, share personal thoughts and feelings, and prioritize quality time together.
* **Emotional support** (`emotional_support`, number, optional): This question is about how much you feel supported and cared for by your partner. Think about whether you feel heard, validated, and understood in your relationship.
* **How much conflict are you having** (`conflict`, number, optional): This question is about the amount of conflict or tension you're experiencing in your relationship. Consider whether you're experiencing frequent or intense disagreements.
* **How much do you trust your partner** (`trust`, number, optional): This question is about the level of trust you feel with your partner. Think about whether you feel secure, secure, and confident in your relationship.
* **How satisfied are you in your relationship** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): This question is about your overall happiness and contentment in your relationship. Consider how fulfilled and content you feel.
* **How committed are you to your relationship** (`commitment`, number, optional): This question is about your level of commitment and dedication to your partner and relationship. Think about whether you're willing to work through challenges and grow together.
* **Sex and Romance** (`sex_and_romance`, number, optional): This question is about the quality of your romantic and physical connection with your partner. Consider how often you initiate intimacy and how satisfying you feel your romantic life is.
* **Intimacy Level** (`intimacy_level`, number, optional): This question is about the level of intimacy and closeness you feel with your partner. Consider how often you have meaningful conversations, share personal thoughts and feelings, and prioritize quality time together.

Note: If you have any doubts or concerns about a particular question, consider discussing it with your partner or seeking support from a trusted friend or therapist.
