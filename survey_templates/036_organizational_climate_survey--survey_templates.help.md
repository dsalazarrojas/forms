# Organizational Climate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Climate Survey is designed to gauge the current state of our organization's climate, helping us understand areas of strength and weakness. Your honest and thorough responses will help us improve our work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

Please select the correct response for each question based on your current experience. You may choose multiple options for questions that allow multiple answers. Ensure you answer each question accurately and truthfully.

1. **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Indicate your level of satisfaction with your current role within the organization. You can select a single number from the provided options.
2. **Workplace Environment** (`workplace_environment`, text, required: false): Describe your current workplace environment. Please provide a clear and concise text response.
3. **Manager Satisfaction** (`manager_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your level of satisfaction with your manager's performance and leadership. Select one of the provided options:
	* Satisfied
	* Dissatisfied
4. **Department Satisfaction** (`department_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all departments within the organization that you are satisfied with:
	* Satisfied
	* Dissatisfied
5. **Work Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time you feel you have a balance between your work and personal life.
6. **Employee Engagement** (`employee_engagement`, time, required: false): Estimate the time you spend engaging in work-related activities outside of work hours.
7. **Communication** (`communication`, note, required: false): Write any additional comments or concerns regarding communication within the organization.
8. **Diversity and Inclusion** (`diversity_and_inclusion`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your level of agreement with promoting diversity and inclusion within the organization. Select one of the provided options:
	* Yes
	* No
9. **Employee Safety** (`employee_safety`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all areas within the organization where you feel safe:
	* Yes
	* No
10. **Team Satisfaction** (`team_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all departments within the organization that you are satisfied with:
	* Satisfied
	* Dissatisfied
11. **Leadership Satisfaction** (`leadership_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all departments within the organization that you are satisfied with:
	* Satisfied
	* Dissatisfied
12. **Company Satisfaction** (`company_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all departments within the organization that you are satisfied with:
	* Satisfied
	* Dissatisfied

## Tips

* Answer each question carefully and truthfully.
* If you have any comments or concerns, please include them in the "Communication" field.
* If you need help or clarification on any question, please contact the designated contact person.
