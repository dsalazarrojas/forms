# Youth Risk Behavior Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on youth risk behavior, which may be used for research or analysis purposes. The form is intended to be completed by students.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure that you have the necessary consents from your parent and guardian.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Review each field to understand what information is being requested.
