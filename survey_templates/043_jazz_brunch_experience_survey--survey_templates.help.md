# Jazz Brunch Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a jazz brunch event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the required and optional information.
2. Select the correct date of the event from the calendar.
3. Choose the music style of the event from the options provided.
4. Rate your experience for each of the following:
	* Food Quality (1-5)
	* Food Value (1-5)
	* Service Attitude (1-5)
	* Overall Experience (1-5)
5. Select all the food items you ordered from the list.
6. Rate the music performance from the options provided.
7. Select the service quality you experienced from the options provided.
8. Choose the goal of the event from the options provided.
9. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
10. Enter your email address, phone number, and name for contact information.
11. Fill in your job title, company, department, location, and any other relevant information about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date**: The date of the jazz brunch event you attended.
* **Event Time**: The time of the jazz brunch event you attended.
* **Music Style**: The style of music played during the event (Blue, Jazz/Blues, Swing, Classic, Rock, Latin).
* **Food Quality**: Rate the quality of the food on a scale of 1-5.
* **Food Value**: Rate the value for money of the food on a scale of 1-5.
* **Service Attitude**: Rate the attitude of the service staff on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience at the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Food Items**: Select all the food items you ordered from the list (Eggs Benedict, Pancakes, Waffles, Quiche, Omelette).
* **Music Performance**: Rate the music performance from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Service Quality**: Select the service quality you experienced from the options provided (Cleanliness, Friendliness, Knowledge, Efficiency).
* **Goals**: Choose the goal of the event from the options provided (Entertainment, Business, Cultural, Other, Social).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Name**: Enter your name for contact information.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title for contact information.
* **Company**: Enter your company name for contact information.
* **Department**: Enter the department you belong to for contact information.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you attended the event for contact information.
* **Minimalist**: Rate how minimalist the decor was on a scale of 1-5.
* **Modern**: Rate how modern the decor was on a scale of 1-5.
* **Art**: Rate the art displayed during the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Contemporary**: Rate how contemporary the decor was on a scale of 1-5.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Make sure to rate each question on a scale of 1-5.
* Select all the applicable options for each question.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the comments section.
* Enter your email address and phone number accurately.
* Fill in all the required information for contact details.
