# mistake_acknowledgment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a mistake or incident that occurred in an organizational context.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Provide detailed and concise answers for each field.
3. Review and edit your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, no): Briefly describe the mistake or incident.
* **Mistake Description** (`mistake_description`, text, no): Describe the details of the mistake or incident.
* **Responsible Party** (`responsible_party`, text, no): Identify the person or team responsible for the mistake.
* **Impact** (`impact`, text, no): Describe the effects or consequences of the mistake.
* **Lesson Learned** (`lesson_learned`, text, no): Document any valuable insights or knowledge gained from the experience.
* **Action Plans** (`action_plans`, text, no): Outline the steps to be taken to prevent similar mistakes in the future.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, no): Describe the next steps to be taken to address the issue.
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`, text, no): Identify the underlying cause of the mistake or incident.
* **Responsible Party Agreed** (`responsible_party_agreed`, text, no): Confirm that the responsible party has acknowledged and agreed to take responsibility for their actions.

## Tips
- Please answer each field carefully and accurately.
- Review and edit your answers before submitting the form.
- Use clear and concise language to ensure easy understanding of the information provided.
