# Supported Tasks For Learners - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information on learner support needs and satisfaction. It is intended for users who require support for their learning experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form's pages and fields below.
2. Complete each field according to the explanations provided.
3. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Learner Support Needs (<name>: <label>**, <type>: <type>, required/optional>: <required>)**: This field is for the learner to express their current support needs. Please provide a brief description of what you need to learn.
* **Help Methods (<name>: <label>, <type>: <type>, required/optional>: <required>)**: Select one or more methods that you would like to receive support through:
	+ Online support chat
	+ Phone call
	+ Email
* **Learner Satisfaction Level (<name>: <label>, <type>: <type>, required/optional>: <required>)**: Indicate your current level of satisfaction with the support you have received, if any. Please select one or more of the following options:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
