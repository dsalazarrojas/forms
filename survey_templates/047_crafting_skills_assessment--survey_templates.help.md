# Crafting Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crafting Skills Assessment form is a series of questions designed to evaluate a user's relevant skills and background when applying for or referring to a crafting position that involves teaching or sharing their expertise with others. This form will help us understand your qualifications and suitability for such roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Answer "How long have you been practicing your craft?" field with the number of years you have been practicing.
3. If applicable, upload a sample of your work in the "Can you please upload a sample of your work?" field.
4. Answer "Do you have teaching experience?" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. If "Yes", answer "What kind of teaching experience do you have?" field by selecting all that apply from the list.
6. Answer "Are you certified?" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
7. Answer "Do you have teaching certification?" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
8. Answer "Do you have a teaching assistant certificate?" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
9. If you are affiliated with a craft organization, enter the name of the organization in the "Are you affiliated with a craft organization?" field.
10. Provide a brief description of your background in the "Please provide a brief description of your background?" field.
11. If applicable, upload your resume or bio in the "Upload your resume or bio?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your name?** (`<label>`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **How long have you been practicing your craft?** (`<label>`, `number`, required/optional): Answer with the number of years you have been practicing your craft.
- **Can you please upload a sample of your work?** (`<label>`, `text`, required/optional): Upload a sample of your work to demonstrate your skills and expertise.
- **Do you have teaching experience?** (`<label>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you have taught or instructed others in a crafting-related field, "No" otherwise.
- **What kind of teaching experience do you have?** (`<label>`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): If you have teaching experience, select all that apply from the list: Instructing classes, Instructing workshops, Online teaching, Private instruction.
- **Are you certified?** (`<label>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you have a relevant certification, "No" otherwise.
- **Do you have teaching certification?** (`<label>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you have a teaching certification, "No" otherwise.
- **Do you have a teaching assistant certificate?** (`<label>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you have a teaching assistant certificate, "No" otherwise.
- **Are you affiliated with a craft organization?** (`<label>`, `text`, required/optional): If you are affiliated with a craft organization, enter the name of the organization.
- **Please provide a brief description of your background?** (`<label>`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your background and experience in the crafting field.
- **Upload your resume or bio?** (`<label>`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, upload your resume or bio to demonstrate your credentials.
