The Environmental Literacy Research Application Form is used to collect information from applicants for environmental literacy research projects. This form is intended for individuals interested in applying for research grants or collaborations with institutions focused on environmental literacy. By providing detailed information about the applicant's background, interest, and project specifics, the form helps the project administrators to assess the eligibility and potential success of the project. To complete the form correctly, please ensure that you provide accurate and comprehensive answers for each field, and that you submit your application before the application deadline.

# Environmental Literacy Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Literacy Research Application Form is used to collect information from applicants for environmental literacy research projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form correctly, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and comprehensive information.
2. Ensure that you provide true and up-to-date contact information.
3. Be honest and accurate in answering the fields related to your relationship with the submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (id: 1): Enter your first name as it is written in official documents.
- **Last Name** (id: 2): Enter your last name as it is written in official documents.
- **Address** (id: 3): Enter your current address.
- **Postal Address** (id: 4): Enter your postal address for mailing purposes.
- **Email** (id: 5): Enter your primary email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Verify Email** (id: 6): Re-enter your email address to verify its accuracy.
- **Phone** (id: 7): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
- **Email Type** (id: 8): Select the type of email address you provided (Residential, Academic, Work, or Personal).
- **Interest in Project** (id: 9): Indicate if you are interested in the project or not. Select True if you are interested, False if you are not, or Undetermined if you are unsure.
- **Project Duration** (id: 10): Select the duration of your planned project (1-3 months, 4-6 months, 7-12 months, or more than 1 year).
- **Research Topic** (id: 11): Enter a brief description of your research topic.
- **Comments** (id: 12): Enter any additional comments or information about your project.
- **Submitter Info** (id: 13): Enter a brief description of the submitter (if not you).
- **Submitter Signature** (id: 14): Sign your submission with your actual signature.
- **Relationship** (id: 15): Indicate your relationship with the submitter (Self, Researcher, Student, or Other).
- **Institution** (id: 16): Enter the name of your institution (if not you).
- **Department** (id: 17): Enter the name of your department (if not you).
- **Submitter Email** (id: 18): Enter the submitter's email address.
- **Submitter Phone** (id: 19): Enter the submitter's phone number.
- **Submitter Email Verification** (id: 20): Re-enter the submitter's email address to verify its accuracy.
- **Relationship Description** (id: 21): Describe your relationship with the submitter (if not you).
- **Relationship Date** (id: 22): Enter the date of your relationship with the submitter (if not you).
- **Relationship Reason** (id: 23): Enter the reason for your relationship with the submitter (if not you).
- **Submitter Signature Date** (id: 24): Enter the date of your signature.
- **Submitter Signature 2** (id: 25): Re-enter your signature for verification.
