# phobia_treatment_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phobia Treatment Assessment Survey is a form designed to gather information from patients who have undergone phobia treatment. It helps clinicians evaluate the effectiveness of treatment plans and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your email and phone number, in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
2. Indicate whether you experience any symptoms associated with phobias in the "Symptoms" field.
3. Select the frequency of your symptoms from the "Symptom Frequency" field.
4. Answer the question about treatment goals in the "Treatment Goals" field.
5. List any current treatment you are receiving in the "Current Treatment" field.
6. Indicate the effectiveness of your treatment in the "Treatment Planning" field.
7. Provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your treatment in the "Additional Info" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required): Please fill in your email address so that we can contact you about your progress and experiences with phobia treatment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number so that we can contact you about your treatment.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" if you currently experience any symptoms associated with phobias.
* **Symptom Frequency** (`symptom_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the frequency of your symptoms from the list.
* **Treatment Goals** (`treatment_goals`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" if you have set treatment goals.
* **Current Treatment** (`current_treatment`, `select_multiple`, required): List any current treatment you are receiving.
* **Treatment Planning** (`treatment_planning`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the effectiveness of your treatment.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your treatment.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click the "Submit" button to send your survey.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): You may add any additional comments or notes about your progress in this field.
