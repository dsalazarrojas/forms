# Butterfly Observation Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Butterfly Observation Log form is designed to collect information about butterfly sightings, including observer details, observation circumstances, and any additional notes or photographs. This form is intended for research purposes, such as monitoring butterfly populations, habitats, and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. Don't worry if you're unsure about some fields – you can always leave them blank or choose the options that best fit your observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Observer Name (1)**: Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your contact email address. This will help us reach out to you for follow-up information.
* **Observation Date (3)**: Enter the date you observed the butterfly.
* **Observation Time (4)**: If you remember, approximate the time of your sighting.
* **Location Name or Site (5)**: Enter the name of the location where you observed the butterfly.
* **Location Address or Coordinates (6)**: If you know the exact address or GPS coordinates, please enter them. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Habitat Type (7)**: Choose the type of environment where you observed the butterfly. Options include:
	+ Garden or yard
	+ Forest
	+ Meadow or grassland
	+ Mountain
	+ Wetland
	+ Coastal area
	+ Urban park
	+ Agricultural area
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Butterfly Species (8)**: Enter the common name of the butterfly species you observed.
* **Identification Confidence (9)**: Indicate how certain you are about the species identification:
	+ Certain - positive identification
	+ Very likely
	+ Likely
	+ Uncertain
	+ Just a guess
* **Life Stage Observed (10)**: Choose the stage of life you observed the butterfly in:
	+ Egg
	+ Caterpillar or larva
	+ Chrysalis or pupa
	+ Adult butterfly
	+ Unsure
* **Number of Butterflies Observed (11)**: Enter the number of butterflies you observed.
* **Butterfly Behavior (12)**: Choose the behaviors you observed:
	+ Feeding on flowers
	+ Resting on plants
	+ Flying or in flight
	+ Mating
	+ Laying eggs
	+ Puddling (drinking from ground)
	+ Basking in sun
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Host or Nectar Plants Observed (13)**: If you observed any plants nearby, enter their names.
* **Weather Conditions (14)**: Indicate the weather conditions during your observation:
	+ Sunny
	+ Cloudy
	+ Rainy
	+ Warm
	+ Cool
	+ Windy
	+ Humid
	+ Clear
* **Temperature Estimate (15)**: If you remember, enter the approximate temperature in degrees.
* **Threats or Disturbances Observed (16)**: If you observed any threats or disturbances, choose the options:
	+ Pesticide use nearby
	+ Habitat damage
	+ Predators present
	+ Human activity
	+ No threats observed
* **Photo or Image Taken (17)**: Indicate if you captured a photo or will provide it separately:
	+ Yes, photo attached or provided
	+ Yes, photo will be submitted separately
	+ No photo taken
* **Additional Observation Notes (18)**: Enter any extra details about your sighting.
* **I Consent to Data Use for Research (19)**: Choose how you'd like us to use your data:
	+ Yes, use my data
	+ No, keep my data private
	+ Unsure
* **Interested in Follow-Up Communication (20)**: Let us know if you'd like to receive updates about the program:
	+ Yes, contact me
	+ No, do not contact
	+ Maybe, unsure
