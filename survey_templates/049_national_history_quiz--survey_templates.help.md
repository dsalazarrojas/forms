# national_history_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The National History Quiz form is designed to assess users' knowledge of historical events or facts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below.

1.  Start by answering the introduction text response question.
2.  Select the correct answers for the multiple-choice questions (question 1, question 2, and question 6).
3.  Input dates for the date question (question 3).
4.  Input times for the time question (question 4).
5.  Use the note field for additional information, if necessary.
6.  Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
7.  Optionally, enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Text): Please provide a brief introduction or context for your answers. This field is optional.
* **Question 1** (Select One): Choose the correct answer for this multiple-choice question.
* **Question 2** (Select Multiple): Select all the correct options for this multiple-choice question.
* **Question 3** (Date): Input the date relevant to the question asked.
* **Question 4** (Time): Input the time relevant to the question asked.
* **Question 5** (Note): Use this field for any additional information you wish to provide.
* **Email** (Email): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Text): Optionally, enter your phone number for contact purposes.
