# on_call_availability_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The on_call_availability_survey form is used to collect information about an individual's on-call availability for a given week.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Select your Onsite preferences.
5. Select your availability for each day of the week (Monday to Friday).
6. For each day you are available, select the start and end times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`on_call_availability_survey_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`on_call_availability_survey_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`on_call_availability_survey_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`on_call_availability_survey_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Onsite** (`on_call_availability_survey_onsite`, select_one, required): Select whether you prefer to be onsite or offsite.
* **Monday** (`on_call_availability_survey_mon`, select_multiple, required): Select your availability for Monday.
	+ Select Morning, Afternoon, or Evening as available.
* **Tuesday** (`on_call_availability_survey_tue`, select_multiple, required): Select your availability for Tuesday.
	+ Select Morning, Afternoon, or Evening as available.

	+ Select Morning, Afternoon, or Evening as available.
* **Onsite 2** (`on_call_availability_survey_onsite_2`, select_multiple, required): Select your onsite preferences for your work schedule.
* **Monday Start** (`on_call_availability_survey_mon_start`, time, optional): Enter the start time for Monday.
