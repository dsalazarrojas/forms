# Fireplace And Furnace Cleaning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fireplace And Furnace Cleaning Survey is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction ratings from customers who have used the fireplace and furnace cleaning services. This form will help us understand how well we've met your expectations and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Enter your answers in the required fields.
3. You can skip questions that are not relevant to your experience.
4. Use the "Select One" and "Select Multiple" options to choose from the available answers.
5. Enter your email address in the "Contact" field if you'd like us to get in touch with you about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please take a moment to read it and then proceed to the questions that follow.
- **Satisfaction** (`Satisfaction`, `number`, optional): This question asks you to rate your level of satisfaction with the service. Please choose a rating from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).
- **Repeat Booking** (`Repeat Booking`, `select_one`, optional): Please indicate if you would like to book the service again in the future. You can choose between "True" and "False".
- **Rating** (`Rating`, `number`, optional): This question allows you to provide a numerical rating of your experience. Please keep it brief and to the point.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, `select_multiple`, optional): Share your thoughts and experiences with us. You can select multiple options from the list provided.
- **Schedule Next** (`Schedule Next`, `date`, optional): If you'd like to book another appointment with us, please enter a date and time that suits you best.
- **Contact** (`Contact`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address if you'd like us to get in touch with you about your feedback. We'll be grateful to hear from you directly.
