# Software Usability Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the software's usability, helping us to understand your experiences and improve the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly, providing as much detail as you feel is necessary.
2. Select the rating that best matches your experience.
3. Type in your thoughts on the feedback field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Software Usability Feedback Form** (`software_usability_feedback_form`, text, optional): This is a brief description of your overall experience with the software. Be as detailed as you like.
* **Software Experience** (`software_experience`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on your experience with the software, including any positive or negative aspects.
* **Usability Rating** (`usability_rating`, select_one, optional): Choose a rating that best matches your experience, using the numbers 1-5, where:
	+ 1 (Poor): The software was difficult to use or had a lot of bugs.
	+ 2 (Fair): The software had some usability issues, but I was able to use it.
	+ 3 (Good): The software was generally easy to use, but had some minor issues.
	+ 4 (Very Good): The software was easy to use and had only minor issues.
	+ 5 (Excellent): The software was very easy to use and had no major issues.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, text, optional): Share your overall satisfaction with the software, including any reasons for satisfaction or dissatisfaction.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any specific feedback or suggestions you have for improving the software.
