# Pilot Issue Reporting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from pilots regarding any issues that occurred during flight operations. Its purpose is to identify potential safety concerns and facilitate further investigation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, taking care to select the correct options for each field.
3. Ensure you have answered all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*).
4. Review your completed form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pre_flight_check** (select_one): Select the option that best describes the pre-flight check you performed.
* **flight_data** (text): Enter a brief description of the flight data that may be relevant to the issue.
* **issue_description** (text): Describe the issue that occurred during the flight.
* **crew_involved** (select_multiple): Check the box if crew members were involved in the issue.
* **location** (text): Enter the location where the issue occurred.
* **issue_severity** (select_one): Select the level of severity of the issue (Low, High).
* **safety_action** (text): Enter any safety action taken as a result of the issue.
* **follow_up_needed** (select_one): Check the box if follow-up is needed on this issue.
* **comments** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes you have about the issue.
* **submit** (text): Click the submit button to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
- If unsure about any of the fields, consult with a supervisor or colleague before submission.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
