# Sustainable Education Leadership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to understand the current state and challenges of education sector's emphasis on sustainability and leadership perspectives, providing valuable feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. For the multiple-choice questions, select only one answer.
3. For the scale rating questions, rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is Low and 5 is High.
4. For the "What are some of the biggest sustainability challenges that you face?" question, list as many challenges as you can think of.
5. For the "What are some of the leadership perspectives that you would like to see from your institution?" question, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the level of emphasis on sustainability in education?** (education_rating): 
  * A question that measures the degree of importance given to sustainability in educational settings.

* **What is your scale rating for the level of emphasis on sustainability in education?** (education_rating_scale): 
  * A question that asks you to rate the level of emphasis on sustainability on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is Low and 5 is High.

* **What are some of the biggest sustainability challenges that you face?** (challenges): 
  * A question that inquires about the difficulties encountered in incorporating sustainability into education. 

* **What are some of the leadership perspectives that you would like to see from your institution?** (leadership_perspectives): 
  * A question that asks for the desired leadership views in the institution in regards to sustainability.

* **What is your scale rating for the leadership perspectives that you would like to see from your institution?** (leadership_perspectives_scale): 
  * A question asking you to rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is Low and 5 is High, for the desired leadership views in the institution.

* **Follow-up Comments** (follow_up_comments): 
  * An optional section to provide any additional information or thoughts about your answers.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately to provide valuable feedback.
* You can use the space provided to elaborate on your answers.
* If you have any additional comments, use the follow-up comments section to provide more information.
