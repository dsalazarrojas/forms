# workplace_feedback_channel_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Feedback Channel Survey is designed to gather information about employee feedback channels, communication channels, and overall satisfaction with the workplace. This survey helps us understand how employees experience and perceive the workplace environment and provides valuable insights to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the employee feedback channels you have used or interacted with in the past month.
2. Choose the communication channels you frequently use or prefer.
3. Select your level of satisfaction with the frequency of use of the selected channels.
4. Choose your level of satisfaction with your overall experience in the workplace.
5. Indicate the level of satisfaction of your department manager and the department as a whole.
6. Type in any additional comments about your experience or suggestions for improvement.
7. Enter your email address for contact information, if you wish to be contacted for further discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee Feedback Channels
* **Yes, No** (`employee_feedback_channels`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all the channels you have used or interacted with, such as email, phone, or in-person meetings.

### 2. Communication Channels
* **Yes, No** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the channels you prefer or frequently use, like team chat, phone calls, or video meetings.

### 3. Frequency of Use
* **Yes, No** (`frequency_of_use`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate how often you use the selected employee feedback channels, such as daily, weekly, monthly, or never.

### 4. Employee Satisfaction
* **Satisfied, Dissatisfied** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the workplace.

### 5. Manager Satisfaction
* **Satisfied, Dissatisfied** (`manager_satisfaction`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with your department manager.

### 6. Department Satisfaction
* **Satisfied, Dissatisfied** (`department_satisfaction`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with your department as a whole.

### 7. Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions about your experience or the workplace.

### 8. Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to submit your feedback. 

### 9. Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further discussions or follow-up.

### 10. Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
