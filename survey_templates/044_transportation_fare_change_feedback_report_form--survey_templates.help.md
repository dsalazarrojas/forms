# Transportation Fare Change Feedback Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about changes in transportation fares. It helps us understand how effectively we communicated fare changes and the reasons behind them. Your input will help us improve our processes and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with the required information.
2. Select the correct fare change percentage from the Fare Change dropdown menu.
3. Rate the fare increase from 1 to 4, with 4 being the most significant increase.
4. Choose the reason for the fare increase from the Reason for fare increase dropdown menu.
5. Answer the question about the effectiveness of the communication of the fare change using a number from 1 to 10, with 10 being most effective.
6. Select the communication channel that was most effective for you.
7. Enter the date and time of when you submitted your feedback.
8. If you have any information about the fare change, enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about your experience with the fare change. Please use this space to describe your thoughts or observations.
* **Fare Change** (`fare_change`): This field is a dropdown menu with different percentage increments. Please select the range that best describes the fare change you experienced.
* **Fare Increase** (`fare_increase`): This field is a rating system from 1 to 4, where 4 is the highest increase. Please rate the fare increase as you feel it was communicated to you.
* **Reason for Fare Increase** (`reason_for_fare_increase`): This field is a dropdown menu with different reasons for fare increases. Please select all the reasons that apply to you.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`): This field asks you to rate the effectiveness of the communication of the fare change. Please rate it as a number from 1 to 10.
* **Communication Channel** (`communication_method`): This field is a dropdown menu with different communication channels. Please select the one that was most effective for you.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): This field asks you to enter the date when you submitted this feedback.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`): This field asks you to enter the time when you submitted this feedback.
* **Rider Name** (`rider_name`): This field is optional and allows you to enter your name. If you have any information about the fare change, you can include it here.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form as accurately as possible.
* Use the dropdown menus to select the most suitable options for your experience.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input.
