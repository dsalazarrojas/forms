# personality_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personality Assessment Form is a tool used to gather information about an individual's personality traits, birth date, contact details, and preferences. This data is collected to better understand individual differences and facilitate more effective communication and collaboration within our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name** in the text field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field.
3. Click on the calendar icon to select your **Birth Date**.
4. Enter your **Email** address in the format `username@email.com`.
5. Input your **Phone Number** in the format `123-456-7890` (e.g., area code + phone number).
6. Select all that apply for **Personality Traits**:
	* 'Yes' for traits that you identify with or feel are relevant to your personality.
7. For **Preferences**, select one option that best matches your preferences:
	* 'Yes' if you prefer that option.
8. In the **Tendencies** field, provide a brief description of your personality tendencies or characteristics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**:
	* A text field to input your first name.
* **Last Name**:
	* A text field to input your last name.
* **Birth Date**:
	* A field to input your birth date using a calendar icon to select the date.
* **Email**:
	* A field to input your email address in the format `username@email.com`.
* **Phone Number**:
	* A field to input your phone number in the format `123-456-7890` (e.g., area code + phone number).
* **Personality Traits**:
	* Select all that apply for traits that you identify with or feel are relevant to your personality (e.g., introvert, outgoing, creative, analytical).
* **Preferences**:
	* Select one option that best matches your preferences (e.g., team player, independent worker, morning person, night owl).
* **Tendencies**:
	* A field to describe your personality tendencies or characteristics (e.g., optimistic, pessimistic, detail-oriented, people-pleaser).
