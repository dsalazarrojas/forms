# Animal Shelter Website Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback about your experience with our website. Your input will help us understand what works well and what needs improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, based on your genuine experience with our website.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, and make sure to provide clear and concise answers.
3. You can skip questions that are not applicable to your experience.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the designated text fields.
