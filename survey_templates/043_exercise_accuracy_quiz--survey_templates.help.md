# Exercise Accuracy Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exercise Accuracy Quiz is a form designed to evaluate the accuracy of exercises performed in a fitness setting. It helps in assessing whether exercises are performed correctly, taking into account factors such as question type, exercise name, correct reps, weight, order, equipment used, body part targeted, muscle group, intensity, and duration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct question type.
2. Provide the exercise name.
3. Enter the correct number of reps.
4. Enter the correct weight.
5. Choose whether the exercise is performed in the correct order.
6. Select the correct equipment used.
7. Choose whether the exercise targets the correct body part.
8. Select the correct muscle group.
9. Choose whether the exercise is performed with correct intensity.
10. Choose whether the exercise is performed for the correct duration.
11. Confirm that you have finished the exercise.
12. (Optional) Enter any additional notes about the exercise.
13. (Optional) Enter your email address and phone number for any follow-up communications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start**: This field is for confirming that you have started the exercise.
* **Question Type**: Choose whether the exercise is a question about the exercise's type or not.
* **Exercise Name**: Provide the name of the exercise being performed.
* **Correct Reps**: Enter the number of correct reps performed during the exercise.
* **Correct Weight**: Enter the weight used during the exercise.
* **Correct Order**: Choose whether the exercise is performed in the correct order.
* **Correct Equipment**: Select the correct equipment used for the exercise.
* **Correct Body Part**: Choose whether the exercise targets the correct body part.
* **Correct Muscle Group**: Select the correct muscle group targeted by the exercise.
* **Correct Intensity**: Choose whether the exercise was performed with correct intensity.
* **Correct Duration**: Choose whether the exercise was performed for the correct duration.
* **End**: Confirm that you have finished the exercise.
* **Exercise Name 2-10**: These fields are for optional exercise names that may be relevant for additional exercises performed.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes about the exercise or any other relevant information.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for any follow-up communications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up communications.
* **Date**: Enter the date when the exercise was performed.
