# Presidential Approval Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their perception of presidential approval ratings. It is a multi-page form that inquires about the user's ratings, comments, and demographic information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill out each page of the form, moving from top to bottom.
* For the **Presidential Approval Rating** section, select one of the five options (Strongly Approve, Approve, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree).
* For the **Additional Comments** field, enter any additional thoughts or concerns you may have.
* For the **Contact Information** field, enter your email address.
* For the **Phone Number** field, enter your phone number (this field is optional).
* For the **Age** field, enter your age.
* For the **Zip Code** field, enter your zip code (this field is optional).
* For the **Presidential Approval Rating Scale** field, enter a number between 1 (Strongly Approve) and 5 (Strongly Disagree).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Presidential Approval Rating**: This field asks for your rating on how you approve or disapprove of the president's performance. You can select one of five options: Strongly Approve, Approve, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
* **Additional Comments**: This field allows you to provide any additional thoughts or concerns you may have.
* **Contact Information (Email)**: This field requires your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): This field is for your phone number, which is optional.
* **Age**: This field asks for your age.
* **Zip Code** (Optional): This field is for your zip code.
* **Presidential Approval Rating Scale**: This field asks you to rate your approval on a scale from 1 to 5, with 1 being Strongly Approve and 5 being Strongly Disagree.
