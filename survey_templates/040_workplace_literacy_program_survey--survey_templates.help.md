# Workplace Literacy Program Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees participating in the Workplace Literacy Program. Please answer the questions honestly to help us improve the program and better serve your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, marked as `*Required` fields.
2. Enter your company name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select your literacy level, education background, and company size from the provided options.
4. Choose relevant educational programs that have helped you.
5. Rate the effectiveness of the program.
6. Input your educational institution and field of study (if relevant).
7. Enter the program start and end dates.
8. Provide any additional comments or notes you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Company Name (1)
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name as you prefer to be known.
* **Company Name (required)**: This field is required, so please enter your company name accurately.

### Employee Name (2)
* **Employee Name**: Enter your employee name as you prefer to be known.

### Email (3)
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Phone (4)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).

### Literacy Level (5)
* **Literacy Level**: Choose your current level of literacy from the options:
	+ Elementary
	+ High school
	+ College or higher
	+ None

### Educational Needs (6)
* **Educational Needs**: Enter any educational needs or areas you'd like to improve.

### Educational Programs (7)
* **Educational Programs**: List any educational programs that have been helpful to you.

### Program Effectiveness (8)
* **Program Effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of the program as:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not Very Effective

### Date (9)
* **Date**: Input the date you started the program.

### Time (10)
* **Time**: Input the time you started the program (optional).

### Note (11)
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share.

### Company Size (12)
* **Company Size**: Choose your company size from the options:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large

### Job Title (13)
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.

### Location (14)
* **Location**: Enter your location.

### Department (15)
* **Department**: Enter your department.

### Job Function (16)
* **Job Function**: Enter your job function.

### Years of Service (17)
* **Years of Service**: Enter the number of years you've been with the company (optional).

### Years of Experience (18)
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience (optional).

### Education Level (19)
* **Education Level**: Choose your education level from the options:
	+ Less than High School
	+ High School
	+ Some College
	+ College or higher

### Field of Study (20)
* **Field of Study**: Enter your field of study.

### Educational Institution (21)
* **Educational Institution**: Enter the name of your educational institution.

### Program Start Date (22)
* **Program Start Date**: Input the date you started the program.

### Program End Date (23)
* **Program End Date**: Input the date you ended the program.

### Program Goals (24)
* **Program Goals**: Enter any program goals you achieved.

### Submission (25)
* **Submission**: Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, leave it blank or contact your supervisor for guidance.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your HR department.
