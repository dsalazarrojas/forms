# Disguise Skills Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disguise Skills Assessment form is designed to help evaluate a participant's skills in disguise. It is intended to be filled out by participants who are being evaluated on their skills in various areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name** in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the **Event** you are being evaluated for.
3. Indicate the **Session Length** you are being evaluated for.
4. Select your **Self-Assessed Skill Level** from the options provided.
5. Choose the **Skills** you believe you possess from the list provided.
6. Enter any **Additional Skills** you may have that are not listed.
7. Provide any **Comments** about your skills or evaluation.
8. Rate your **Overall Rating** (not required for this form, but optional).
9. Finally, select the **Evaluation Date** and **Evaluation Time** for the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Your name as a participant in the evaluation.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): The name of the event you are being evaluated for.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, required: false): The length of the session you are being evaluated for.
* **Self-Assessed Skill Level** (`skill_level`, select_one, required: false): Select your level of expertise in disguise, from Beginner to Expert.
* **Skills** (`self_assessment`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the skills you possess from the list provided.
* **Additional Skills** (`additional_skills`, text, required: false): Enter any additional skills you may have that are not listed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Provide any comments about your skills or evaluation.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): Your overall rating (optional).
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false): The date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required: false): The time of the evaluation.
