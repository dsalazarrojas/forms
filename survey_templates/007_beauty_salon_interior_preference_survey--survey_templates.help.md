# Beauty Salon Interior Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your preferences for the interior design of a beauty salon. Your input will aid us in creating a welcoming and comfortable space that meets your needs and enhances your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your preferred answers to each question and submit your responses. You can choose multiple options for some questions, so feel free to pick as many or as few as you like. If you have any additional comments or preferences, you can also add them in the last section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Aesthetic Style Preferences
**Section: Aesthetic Style Preferences** (`section_aesthetic_style`, `note`, `required: false`):
This section is an introduction to the survey. It's not a question that requires an answer, but it helps set the tone for the rest of the form.

### 2. Which interior style do you prefer for a salon?
**preferred_style** (`select_one`, `required: true`):
Choose one of the following interior styles:
*   Modern Minimalist
*   Industrial Chic
*   Vintage / Retro
*   Luxury / Glam
*   Natural / Organic
*   Bohemian

Select the style that best reflects your personal taste and preferences.

### 3. What color palette makes you feel most relaxed?
**color_palette_preference** (`select_multiple`, `required: true`):

*   Earth Tones (Green, Brown, Terracotta)

Select the color palettes that bring you calmness and relaxation.
