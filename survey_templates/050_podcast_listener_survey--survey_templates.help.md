# Podcast Listener Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from our podcast listeners to better understand their preferences, habits, and expectations. Your input will help us create a more engaging and informative podcast experience for you and our audience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your name, email address, and answer the questions as prompted.
2. Provide your thoughts on our podcast, and what you would like to see more of.
3. Select your preferences for our podcast format (e.g., video, audio, written).
4. Answer how often you listen to our podcast and which devices you use.
5. Finally, provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (1)** (`name`, `text`, required): Please provide your name for us to address you personally.
* **Email (2)** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone (3)** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field for you to share your phone number if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Category (4)** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): Select `Yes` if you're interested in more of our content.
* **What do you think about our podcast? (5)** (`question_one`, `note`, optional): Share your thoughts on our podcast.
* **Recommendation (6)** (`question_two`, `select_one`, required): Select how likely you are to recommend our podcast to a friend.
* **More of what? (7)** (`question_three`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose which type of content you'd like to see more of in our podcast.
* **Format (8)** (`question_four`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred format for our podcast (e.g., video, audio, written).
* **Additional Comments (9)** (`question_five`, `note`, optional): Leave any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to share.
* **Multi-Device Listening (10)** (`question_six`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you listen to our podcast on more than one device.
* **Device (12)** (`name_of_device`, `select_one`, required): Select the devices you use to listen to our podcast.
* **Listening Frequency (13)** (`frequency_of_use`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you listen to our podcast.
* **Age (14)** (`age`, `number`, optional): Share your age group, if you'd like.
* **Time of Day (15)** (`time_of_day`, `time`, optional): This is optional and we're curious about your listening habits.
* **Time Zone (16)** (`time_zone`, `time`, optional): Share your time zone, if you'd like.
