# Contestant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from contest participants, helping event organizers improve future events by understanding their strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields, such as the "Name of the Contest Event", "Ease of Registration", "Communication from Organizers", "Fairness of Judging", "Venue and Facilities Satisfaction", "Best Part of the Contest", "Biggest Disappointment (if any)", "How can we make this better next year", and "Do you plan to enter our future contests?".
2.  If applicable, fill in your name for identification purposes only.
3.  Rate the event's ease of registration, communication from organizers, fairness of judging, and venue and facilities satisfaction on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
4.  Provide your thoughts on the best part of the contest and any biggest disappointments you experienced.
5.  Share your suggestions on how to improve the event for next year.
6.  Indicate how likely you are to recommend our contest to others (0 = Not likely, 10 = Very likely).
7.  Choose your intention to participate in our future contests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contestant Name (Optional)**: If you'd like to be identified, please fill in your name for internal reference purposes only.
* **Name of the Contest Event**: The name of the event you participated in.
* **Ease of Registration**: Rate how easy or difficult it was to register for the event. (1 = Poor, 2 = Fair, 3 = Average, 4 = Good, 5 = Excellent)
* **Communication from Organizers**: Rate how clear and informative you found the communication from the event organizers. (1 = Poor, 2 = Fair, 3 = Average, 4 = Good, 5 = Excellent)
* **Fairness of Judging**: Rate how fair you felt the judging process was. (1 = Poor, 2 = Fair, 3 = Average, 4 = Good, 5 = Excellent)
* **Venue and Facilities Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the venue and facilities provided for the event. (If virtual, rate the platform used.) (1 = Poor, 2 = Fair, 3 = Average, 4 = Good, 5 = Excellent)
* **Best Part of the Contest**: Describe the best part of the contest, what made it enjoyable or memorable for you.
* **Biggest Disappointment (if any)**: If you experienced any disappointments, describe them here.
* **How can we make this better next year**: Share your suggestions on how to improve the event for next year.
* **How likely are you to recommend our contest to others** (0 = Not likely, 10 = Very likely): Indicate how likely you are to recommend our contest to others.
* **Do you plan to enter our future contests** (Yes, Maybe, No, I am done): Choose your intention to participate in our future contests.
* **Date of Submission**: Please fill in the date you are submitting this form.

Note: All fields marked with \* are required, while others are optional.
