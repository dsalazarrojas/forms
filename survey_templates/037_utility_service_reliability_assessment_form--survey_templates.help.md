# Utility Service Reliability Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form helps assess the reliability of utility services, providing valuable information for identifying areas of improvement in service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly, providing concrete answers to each section.
2. Select the corresponding ratings and options that best describe your experience with the utility service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reliability of Service** (Required, Select Multiple):
    * This question asks how reliable you find the utility service to be. Select all the options that apply:
        - '5': Very reliable
        - '4': Somewhat reliable
        - '3': Neutral
        - '2': Somewhat unreliable
        - '1': Very unreliable
        - '0': Not reliable at all
- **Frequency of Outage** (Required, Select One):
    * This question inquires about how often the utility service experiences outages. Select one of the options:
        - Daily
        - Weekly
        - Monthly
        - Never
- **Cause of Outage** (Required, Select Multiple):
    * This question investigates the reasons behind utility service outages. Select all the options that apply:
        - High winds
        - Lightning
        - Other (please specify)
- **Service Repairs** (Required, Select One):
    * This question determines if the utility service repairs are completed or not. Select one of the options:
        - Completed
        - Not completed
- **Frequency of Repairs** (Required, Select Multiple):
    * This question asks about the frequency of utility service repairs. Select all the options that apply:
        - 1-5
        - 6-10
        - More than 5
- **Overall Experience** (Optional, Text):
    * This question allows you to provide feedback on your overall experience with the utility service.
- **Comments** (Optional, Text):
    * This section is for providing additional comments or observations about your experience with the utility service.
- **Service Reliability** (Optional, Text):
    * This field is not used in the form, so you can ignore it.
