# IA Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The IA Survey is designed to collect user responses to a series of questions, helping us better understand your opinions, experiences, and thoughts on the given topics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your genuine opinions, experiences, or thoughts.
2. Select one or more options for each question.
3. Provide detailed answers for each text field.
