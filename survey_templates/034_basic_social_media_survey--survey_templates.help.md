# Basic Social Media Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to understand your social media habits and preferences regarding our organization's social media presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Respond to each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the most accurate answer for each question.
3. Fill in any required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your name (optional)** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Your name is not required to be filled in for the survey to be completed. However, it is encouraged for us to have a record of your responses.
* **Email address (optional)** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address is not required to be filled in for the survey to be completed. However, it is encouraged for us to have a contact point to reach you.
* **Which social media platforms do you use?** (`active_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the social media platforms you currently use.
* **Primary social media platform** (`primary_platform`, select_one, required): Please select the platform you use the most.
* **Hours spent on social media daily** (`daily_usage`, select_one, required): Please select how many hours you spend on social media per day.
* **Do you follow our organization on social media?** (`org_follow`, select_one, required): Please select whether you follow our organization on social media.
* **Rate the quality of our social media content** (`content_quality`, number, optional): Please rate the quality of our social media content on a scale of 1-5.
* **How likely are you to engage with our posts?** (`engagement_likelihood`, number, optional): Please rate how likely you are to engage with our posts on a scale of 1-5.
* **What type of content interests you most?** (`preferred_content`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the type of content that interests you the most.
* **How often should we post?** (`posting_frequency`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you would like to see our posts.
* **What could we improve on social media?** (`improvements`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas on how we could improve our social media presence, please let us know.
* **Any other suggestions or feedback?** (`suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any other suggestions or feedback, please let us know.
* **Overall satisfaction with our social media presence** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Please rate your overall satisfaction with our social media presence on a scale of 1-5.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
