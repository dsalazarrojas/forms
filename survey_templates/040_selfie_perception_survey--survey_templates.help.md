# Selfie Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Selfie Perception Survey is a form designed to gather information about your selfie-taking habits and preferences. Your input will help us understand how you interact with yourself and the world around you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select the options that apply to you from the dropdown menus.
4. If you select "Other" for any of the questions, provide a brief explanation in the text field below.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Survey Questions
* **Survey Questions**: This is a text box where you can provide any additional information about your selfie-taking habits and preferences. (text)

### 2. Participant Information
* **Participant Information**: Select your gender from the dropdown menu. (select_one, options: Male, Female, Other)

### 3. Selfie Frequency
* **Selfie Frequency**: Choose how often you take selfies from the options below. 
  * Daily
  * Weekly
  * Monthly
  * Rarely (select_multiple)

### 4. Selfie Reasons
* **Selfie Reasons**: Type in what motivates you to take selfies. This is an open-ended text field. (text)

### 5. Self-Image
* **Self-Image**: This field is not applicable for this form. (time)

### 6. Comments
* **Comments**: This is a text area where you can provide any further comments or thoughts about your selfie-taking habits. (note)

### 7. Selfie Habits
* **Selfie Habits**: This field is not applicable for this form. (number)

### 8. Participant Location
* **Participant Location**: Choose your location from the dropdown menu. 
  * Urban
  * Rural
  * Town
  * City (select_multiple)

### 9. Other Selfie Frequency
* **Other Selfie Frequency**: If you selected "Other" for the previous question, type in how often you take selfies. (text)

### Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
