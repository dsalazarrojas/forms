# Argumentative Essay Outline Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Argumentative Essay Outline Form is designed to help students create a comprehensive outline for their argumentative essays. This form will guide students through the essential sections and questions to consider when developing a clear and effective argument.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), with your information:
	* Student Name
	* Email Address
	* Class Name or Course Code
	* Date of Submission
	* Essay Title or Topic
	* Thesis Statement
	* What types of evidence you will use
	* Approximate number of sources
2. Answer the optional fields, marked as "note," with a brief description:
	* Student Information (section)
	* Essay Topic (section)
	* Thesis Statement (section)
	* Main Argument 1
	* Main Argument 2 (if applicable)
	* Main Argument 3 (if applicable)
	* Evidence and Support (section)
	* Counterargument (section)
	* Conclusion (section)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Required): Enter your name as it appears on your school records.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your school-issued email address.
* **Class Name or Course Code** (Required): Enter your class name or course code to help identify your assignment.
* **Date of Submission** (Required): Enter the date you plan to submit your essay.
* **Essay Title or Topic** (Required): Briefly describe your essay's main subject.
* **Thesis Statement** (Required): Clearly state the main argument or claim you will make in your essay.
* **What types of evidence will you use** (Required): Select all the types of evidence you plan to use to support your argument.
* **Approximate number of sources** (Optional): Estimate the number of sources you will use for research.
* **Main Argument 1** (Required): Present your first main argument or point.
* **Main Argument 2** (Required): Present your second main argument or point (if applicable).
* **Main Argument 3** (Optional): Present your third main argument or point (if applicable).
* **What is the opposing viewpoint** (Optional): Describe the opposing viewpoint or counterargument.
* **How will you refute this counterargument** (Optional): Explain how you will respond to the opposing viewpoint.
* **How will you conclude your essay** (Optional): Choose the approach you will use to conclude your essay.
* **Additional notes or questions** (Optional): Add any additional information or questions you have about the assignment.

Note: The optional fields can be left blank if you do not have information to provide.
