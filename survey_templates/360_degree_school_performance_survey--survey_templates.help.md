# 4 Point Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for inspectors to conduct a comprehensive 4-point inspection on a property. The goal is to evaluate the condition of various systems and components of the property, including the roof, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC systems. The form is intended to collect information about property conditions, observed defects, and recommended actions or repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and photos before starting the form.
2.  Begin by filling out the property and inspector information sections, including the property address, owner's name, inspection date, and your name and license (if applicable).
3.  Evaluate the condition of the roof, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC systems by selecting one of the provided options for each system.
4.  Check off any observed defects that you have noticed during the inspection, and describe any recommended actions or repairs.
5.  Finally, add any additional notes or observations about the property condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property address** (`Property address`, `text`, required): Enter the street address of the property, including city, state, and zip code.
* **Property owner name** (`Property owner name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the property owner as it appears on the deed or lease.
* **Inspection date** (`Inspection date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you completed the inspection.
* **Inspector name** (`Inspector name`, `text`, required): Enter your name and any relevant licenses or certifications.
* **Roof condition** (`Roof condition`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options to describe the overall condition of the roof:
	+ Good
	+ Fair (minor issues)
	+ Poor (major issues)
	+ Not accessible
* **Electrical system condition** (`Electrical system condition`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options to describe the condition of the electrical system:
	+ Good
	+ Requires repair
	+ Hazardous
	+ Not applicable
* **Plumbing system condition** (`Plumbing system condition`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options to describe the condition of the plumbing system:
	+ Good
	+ Requires repair
	+ Hazardous / active leak
	+ Not applicable
* **HVAC / heating condition** (`HVAC / heating condition`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options to describe the condition of the HVAC or heating system:
	+ Operational
	+ Partially operational
	+ Non-operational
	+ Not present
* **Observed defects** (`Observed defects`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all that apply:
	+ Roof leaks
	+ Faulty wiring
	+ Plumbing leaks
	+ HVAC failure
	+ Structural cracks
	+ Water intrusion / mold
* **Overall safety rating** (`Overall safety rating`, `integer`, required): Enter a rating from 1 to 10, with 10 being fully safe and 1 being an immediate hazard.
* **Photos taken** (`Photos taken and attached`, `select_one`, required): Select whether photos were taken and attached to the form.
* **Recommended actions or repairs** (`Recommended actions or repairs`, `text`, required): Enter a short list of recommended actions or repairs.
* **Inspector additional notes** (`Inspector additional notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or observations about the property condition.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form to ensure that the data is complete and reliable.
* Use specific and clear language when describing observed defects and recommended actions or repairs.
* Attach photos to support your observations and recommendations.
