# workshop_learning_outcome_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience in a workshop or training session. It helps facilitators and trainers to understand what worked well and what could be improved in the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Participant Name in the Participant Name field.
2. Enter the Session ID if you attended a session.
3. Select the correct Session Date and Session Time for the session you attended.
4. Enter the Session Length, if necessary.
5. Enter the name of the Trainer you interacted with.
6. Rate your Participant Rating and Reported Rating, using a scale (not specified, so assume it's a 1-5 scale).
7. Write your comments for Participant Comments, Facilitator Rating, and overall Session Objectives.
8. Describe the Session Activities.
9. Provide an overall evaluation of your Overall Experience.
10. Finally, add any feedback and recommendations you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as you want it to appear in the feedback report.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID of the session you attended, if applicable.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the session you attended.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of the session you attended.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the session in minutes or hours.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the trainer you interacted with.
* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with the session, using a 1-5 scale.
* **Reported Rating** (`reported_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate how well the session met its objectives, using a 1-5 scale.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, required/optional): Share any comments about your experience in the session.
* **Facilitator Rating** (`facilitator_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the effectiveness of the facilitator, using a 1-5 scale.
* **Facilitator Comments** (`facilitator_comments`, text, required/optional): Share any comments about the facilitator.
* **Session Goals** (`session_goals`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the session goals.
* **Session Objectives** (`session_objectives`, text, required/optional): Describe the session objectives.
* **Session Activities** (`session_activities`, text, required/optional): List the activities that took place during the session.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with the session.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Share any feedback you have about the session.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required/optional): Suggest any improvements or changes for future sessions.
