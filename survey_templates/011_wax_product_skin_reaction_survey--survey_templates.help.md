# Wax Product Skin Reaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experience with various products used at salon spas. Your input will help us understand your reactions and improve our products for better service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all that apply from the provided options.
2. Enter any comments or feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Salon Spas Organizations**: Enter the name of the salon spa where you had your experience.
* **Reactions To Products**: Select the reaction(s) that apply to each product from the list.
	+ "Yes" for a positive reaction.
	+ Leave blank for a neutral or negative reaction.
* **Product Used**: Enter the name of the product used.
* **Product Used 2**: Enter the name of the second product used.
* **Reactions To Products 2**: Enter your reaction to the second product.
* **Reactions To Products 3**: Enter your reaction to the third product.
* **Product Used 2**: Enter the name of the second product used.
* **Reactions To Products 4**: Enter your reaction to the fourth product.
* ...
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you don't have an experience with a product, leave it blank.
* You can enter as many or as few reactions as apply to each product.
