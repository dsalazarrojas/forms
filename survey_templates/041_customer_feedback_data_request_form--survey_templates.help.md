# Customer Feedback Data Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and data from customers to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the survey questions.
3. Select the recommended product or service from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter your email and phone number (if applicable).
5. Fill out the contact person information (if applicable).
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the text boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (Required): This section is used to gather information about the customer's experience with our service. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Customer Info** (Optional): This field is used to collect any additional information about the customer that would be helpful to know.
* **Rating** (Required): This is a scale of 1-5 to rate the customer's experience.
* **Comment** (Optional): This is a text box for the customer to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Recommended Product or Service** (Required): This dropdown menu lists our recommended product or service. Please select the one that best matches the customer's experience.
* **Email** (Optional): This is the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (Optional): This is the customer's phone number.
* **Contact Person** (Optional): This field is used to collect information about the contact person for the customer.
* **Contact Person Department** (Optional): This field is used to collect information about the department of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Job Title** (Optional): This field is used to collect information about the job title of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Email** (Optional): This is the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone** (Optional): This is the phone number of the contact person.
* **Customer Service Rep** (Optional): This field is used to collect information about the customer service representative.
* **Customer Service Rep Department** (Optional): This field is used to collect information about the department of the customer service representative.
* **Customer Service Rep Job Title** (Optional): This field is used to collect information about the job title of the customer service representative.
* **Customer Service Rep Email** (Optional): This is the email address of the customer service representative.
* **Customer Service Rep Phone** (Optional): This is the phone number of the customer service representative.
* **Customer Comments 1-7** (Optional): These text boxes are for the customer to provide any additional comments or feedback.

Note: All fields with "Required: true" are marked with an asterisk (*). Please fill out all the required fields. If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
