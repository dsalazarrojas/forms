# Business Travel Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences and perceptions of business travel. It will help us understand your thoughts on various aspects of business travel, such as travel frequency, policy clarity, and overall satisfaction. Your responses will be used to identify areas for improvement to enhance our business travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your Employee Name and Employee ID (if applicable) on Page 1.
2. Select your department and answer the questions regarding your travel experiences on Page 1.
3. For each question, choose the most accurate answer from the provided options.
4. You can also provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement on Page 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, optional): If you have an employee ID, please enter it here (if not, leave blank).
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Select your department to help us better understand your role.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): The date you are completing this survey.
* **Last Business Trip Date** (`last_trip_date`, `date`, optional): The date of your most recent business trip.
* **Travel Frequency** (`trip_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you travel for business, from Weekly to Rarely.
* **Typical Trip Destinations** (`trip_destinations`, `text`, optional): Indicate the typical places you visit for business.
* **Flight Booking Experience** (`flight_experience`, `select_one`, required): Describe how easy or difficult it is to book flights.
* **Travel Policy Clarity** (`travel_policy_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Rate the clarity of our travel policy.
* **Expense Reimbursement Process** (`expense_reimbursement`, `select_one`, required): Assess how smooth or problematic the expense reimbursement process is.
* **Travel Support Quality** (`travel_support`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the quality of support you receive from our company while traveling.
* **Hotel Accommodations Quality** (`accommodations_quality`, `select_one`, required): Assess the quality of hotel accommodations provided.
* **Travel Safety Measures** (`travel_safety`, `select_one`, required): Rate how safe you feel while traveling for business.
* **Impact on Work-life Balance** (`travel_time_impact`, `select_one`, required): Describe the effect of business travel on your work-life balance.
* **Travel Stress Level** (`travel_stress_level`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate how stressful business travel is for you.
* **Overall Travel Experience** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Assess your overall satisfaction with business travel.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions for improving our business travel experience.
* **Additional Support Needed** (`additional_support`, `text`, optional): Describe any additional support you would like us to provide for future business trips.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by employees who have experience with business travel. If you have not traveled for business recently, you can still provide general feedback on your past experiences. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
