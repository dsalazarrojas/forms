## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences, knowledge, and behaviors related to self-harm.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, select the response that best represents your current state or experience.
3. Do not worry about running out of space; you can type as much as you need to in the text fields.
4. If you have a question or need clarification on any of the fields, please do not hesitate to ask.
