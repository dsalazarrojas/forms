# program_download_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their program downloads. Please fill in the required fields to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide any additional information as needed. Make sure to double-check your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name for registration purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name for registration purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, email, not required): Confirm your email address for verification.
* **Company** (`company`, text, not required): Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter your job title for registration purposes.
* **Download Type** (`download_type`, select_one, not required): Select how you want to download the program (software, digital, service, other, or unknown).
* **How did you find us** (`how_did_you_find_us`, select_multiple, not required): Select the channel through which you found us (online search, social media, friend, other, or unknown).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date you are interested in downloading the program.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time of day you are interested in downloading the program.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, time, not required): Select your time zone for scheduling purposes.
* **Country** (`country`, text, not required): Enter your country of residence for registration purposes.
* **Job Title (Other)** (`job_title_other`, text, not required): Enter any other job title that is not listed above.
* **Download Name** (`download_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the download you are interested in downloading.

**Tips**: Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields to avoid any issues with your registration. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or contact our support team.
