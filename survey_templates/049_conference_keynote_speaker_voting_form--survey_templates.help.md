# Conference Keynote Speaker Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Keynote Speaker Voting Form is a survey that enables attendees to vote on their top speaker choices for the conference.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To submit your vote, simply fill out the form with your preferred speaker choices.
*   You can choose up to three speakers.
*   Make sure to provide your email address so that we can verify your identity if needed.
*   If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional comments" field.
*   Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to submit your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Voter name**: This field is optional and will help us know who submitted the form.
*   **Email address**: This field is optional but will be used for verification purposes. Please make sure to provide a valid email address.
*   **Select your top speaker choices**: Choose up to three speakers from the list provided. You can select speakers by clicking on the radio buttons next to their names.
*   **Reason for top choice**: This field is optional, but it will help us understand why you chose the speaker you did. Please keep your answer brief.
*   **Preferred session format**: Choose the format you prefer for the keynote session. This will help us tailor the program to your needs.
*   **Topic interest level**: Indicate how relevant the topic is to your interests. This will help us gauge the level of interest in each speaker's topic.
*   **Conflicts of interest**: If you have any conflicts of interest with the speakers, please let us know. This will help us avoid any potential conflicts.
*   **Additional comments**: If you have any other feedback or suggestions, please use this field to provide them.
*   **Consent to contact**: Choose whether you consent to be contacted about your vote.
*   **Submission reference**: A system-generated reference number for your submission.

Please remember that each attendee can only submit one vote. Please make sure to fill out the form carefully and honestly. We appreciate your feedback and participation in this process.
