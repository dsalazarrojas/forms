# customer_follow_through_call - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer follow through call form is used to record and track the progress of customer calls. This form helps ensure that customers are followed up on in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the first page with any relevant details about the customer call.
2. On the second page, select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you followed up on the customer after the initial call.
3. If you followed up on the customer, provide any additional comments in the 'Additional Comments' field.
4. On the third page, select 'Option 1', 'Option 2', or 'Option 3' to indicate the resolution status of the customer issue.
5. On the fourth page, enter any relevant customer contact information if applicable.
6. On the fifth page, enter the date when you followed up on the customer after the initial call.
7. On the sixth page, select the 'Resolution Status' from the available options.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page (customer_follow_through_call_page)**: Enter any relevant details about the customer call.
* **Follow Through (customer_follow_through_call_page2)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you followed up on the customer after the initial call.
* **Customer Contact Info (customer_follow_through_call_page3)**: Enter any relevant customer contact information if applicable.
* **Additional Comments (customer_follow_through_call_page4)**: Provide any additional comments about the customer call.
* **Resolution Status (customer_follow_through_call_page5)**: Select 'Option 1', 'Option 2', or 'Option 3' to indicate the resolution status of the customer issue.
* **Customer Follow Through Call (customer_follow_through_call_page6)**: Enter the date when you followed up on the customer after the initial call.
* **Follow-up Date (customer_follow_through_call_page7)**: Enter the date when you followed up on the customer after the initial call.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form after the initial customer call.
* Ensure that you fill out all relevant fields to provide accurate and timely information.
* Use the 'Additional Comments' field to provide any additional context or information about the customer call.
* The form is divided into multiple pages, each with its own set of fields, so make sure to review each page carefully before submitting the form.
