# Nonprofit Organization Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about nonprofit organizations, helping us understand their experiences and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Start by writing a brief description of the nonprofit organization you're providing feedback on.
2. Choose the category you're providing feedback on (e.g., Survey Templates, Feedback Forms).
3. Provide any additional information about the organization in the "Nonprofit organization name", "Nonprofit organization email", and "Nonprofit organization phone" fields.
4. Answer questions about the organization's programs and services in the "Nonprofit program name", "Nonprofit program email", and "Nonprofit program phone" fields.
5. Share your thoughts about what this form was used for in the "What is this form used for" field.
6. Answer the "What do you think could be improved" and "How do you think we could improve" questions to share your suggestions.
7. Rate your overall satisfaction in the "How would you rate your overall satisfaction" field.
8. Rate your satisfaction with services and programs in the "How would you rate your satisfaction with services" and "How would you rate your satisfaction with programs" fields.
9. Share any observations about the most improvement you observed and the most disappointment you observed.
10. Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nonprofit organization feedback questionnaire** (`form_title`): A brief description of the nonprofit organization.
* **Survey templates feedback forms** (`category`): The category under which the feedback is being provided.
* **What is this form used for** (`what_is_this_form_used_for`): A brief description of the purpose of the form.
* **Nonprofit organization name** (`nonprofit_organization_name`): The name of the nonprofit organization.
* **Nonprofit organization email** (`nonprofit_organization_email`): The email address of the nonprofit organization.
* **Nonprofit organization phone** (`nonprofit_organization_phone`): The phone number of the nonprofit organization.
* **Nonprofit program name** (`nonprofit_program_name`): The name of a specific program offered by the nonprofit organization.
* **Nonprofit program email** (`nonprofit_program_email`): The email address of the specific program offered by the nonprofit organization.
* **Nonprofit program phone** (`nonprofit_program_phone`): The phone number of the specific program offered by the nonprofit organization.
* **What do you think could be improved** (`what_do_you_think_could_be_improved`): Suggestions for improvement.
* **How do you think we could improve** (`how_do_you_think_we_could_improve`): Additional suggestions for improvement.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction** (`how_would_you_rate_your_overall_satisfaction`): A rating of your overall satisfaction.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with services** (`how_would_you_rate_your_satisfaction_with_services`): A rating of your satisfaction with services offered by the nonprofit organization.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with programs** (`how_would_you_rate_your_satisfaction_with_programs`): A rating of your satisfaction with programs offered by the nonprofit organization.
* **What was the most improvement you observed** (`what_was_the_most_improvement_you_observed`): An observation of an improvement made.
* **What was the most disappointment you observed** (`what_was_the_most_disappointment_you_observed`): An observation of a disappointment made.
* **What was the reason you did not see improvement** (`what_was_the_reason_you_did_not_see_improvement`): A reason why improvement was not seen.
* **What was the reason you did not see disappointment** (`what_was_the_reason_you_did_not_see_disappointment`): A reason why disappointment was not seen.
* **What was the improvement you observed** (`what_was_the_improvement_you_observed`): Another observation of an improvement made.
* **What was the disappointment you observed** (`what_was_the_disappointment_you_observed`): Another observation of a disappointment made.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Any additional comments or suggestions.

## Tips

* Please answer all the questions carefully to provide us with accurate and valuable feedback.
* You can provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
