# Salad Kit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our salad kits. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by a * in the label.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the Additional Comments field to provide more information.
4. Once you have filled out all the fields, submit the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* This section provides a brief overview of the survey purpose.

### Product Name
* **Product Name**: This field is used to record the name of the product you are rating. Please enter the name of the product you are currently rating.
* Type: text
* Required: no

### Product Rating
* **Product Rating**: This field allows you to rate the quality of the product. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### Price Value
* **Price Value**: This field allows you to choose the value of the price of the product. Please choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent

### Quality of Ingredients
* **Quality of Ingredients**: This field is used to rate the quality of the ingredients. Please enter a number between 1 (poor) and 5 (excellent).
* Type: number
* Required: no

### Satisfaction with Product
* **Satisfaction with Product**: This field allows you to rate your satisfaction with the product. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied

### Would Recommend
* **Would Recommend**: This field allows you to choose if you would recommend the product. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ False

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments**: This field is used for customers to provide any additional comments or feedback about their experience. Please use this field to provide more information about your experience.
* Type: text
* Required: no

### How Did You Find Us
* **How Did You Find Us**: This field is used to track how customers found the store or website. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Social Media
	+ Online Search
	+ Referral
	+ Store
	+ Other

### Email
* **Email**: This field is used to record your email address. Please enter your email address.
* Type: email
* Required: no

### Phone
* **Phone**: This field is used to record your phone number. Please enter your phone number.
* Type: text
* Required: no
