# Voter Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voter Satisfaction Questionnaire is designed to collect information about voters' experiences, levels of satisfaction, and feedback on election processes. This form will help the organization to better understand areas of improvement and make informed decisions for future elections.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select the correct election date and time of the event you're providing feedback on.
2. Enter your unique voter ID number, if provided.
3. Describe the voting location where you cast your ballot.
4. Rate your level of satisfaction with the overall experience using the provided scale.
5. Optionally, provide any comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
6. If you were assigned a tool or resource during the voting process, select all that apply from the list.
7. Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Election Date** (`1`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the election or voting event you're providing feedback on.
* **Election Time** (`2`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the election or voting event you're providing feedback on.
* **Voter ID** (`3`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your unique voter ID number, if provided.
* **Voting Location** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where you cast your ballot.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`5`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your level of satisfaction with the overall experience: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Comments** (`6`, `note`, required/optional): Share any comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Assigned Tool** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all tools or resources that were assigned to you during the voting process (e.g., Tool 1, Tool 2, Tool 3).

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide accurate information to help us improve future election processes.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, feel free to include them in the comments section.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask for clarification or contact your supervisor for assistance.
