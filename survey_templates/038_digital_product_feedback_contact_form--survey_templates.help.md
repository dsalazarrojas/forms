# Digital Product Feedback Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Product Feedback Contact Form is designed to gather user feedback on digital products, providing a platform for users to report bugs, submit feature requests, or share general feedback. This form enables our team to collect valuable insights and improve our digital products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the `Name` field.
2.  Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
3.  Enter a valid email address in the `Email` field (must be a correct format).
4.  Select the type of feedback you have:
    *   Bug Report
    *   Feature Request
    *   General Feedback
5.  Optionally, provide a detailed description of your feedback in the `Description` field.
6.  Enter your feedback in the `Submit` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Name (Required)**: Enter your name in this field.
*  **Email (Required)**: Enter your valid email address in this field. Please note that email is required to be in a correct format (e.g., example@example.com).
*  **Email (Required)**: This field is identical to the previous "Email" field and is also required. Please ensure that the email format is correct.
*  **Subject (Required)**: Enter the subject of your feedback, which should be a concise and descriptive title of your feedback.
*  **Description (Optional)**: Provide a detailed description of your feedback, which will help our team understand your concerns or requests.
*  **Feedback Type (Optional)**: Select the type of feedback you're providing:
    *   Bug Report: For reporting bugs or issues with our digital products.
    *   Feature Request: For suggesting new features or improvements.
    *   General Feedback: For sharing general comments or suggestions.
*  **Bug Report (Optional)**: Provide a detailed description of the bug or issue you've encountered.
*  **Feature Request (Optional)**: Describe the feature you'd like to request, including any details or requirements.
*  **Submit**: Once you've filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to our team.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you enter your email address correctly to receive any follow-up communication from our team.
*   Be as detailed as possible when describing your feedback, so our team can better understand your concerns.
*   If you're reporting a bug, provide as much information as possible about the issue.
*   If you're requesting a feature, ensure that you've thoroughly described it.
