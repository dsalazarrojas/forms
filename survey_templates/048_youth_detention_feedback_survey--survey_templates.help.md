# Youth Detention Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Detention Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from individuals who have experienced a youth detention or incarceration. The survey aims to understand their experiences, needs, and concerns during their stay, ultimately helping to improve the quality of care and support services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions based on your actual experience and observations.
2. Be honest in your feedback, as it will help us improve services.
3. Provide detailed answers for each field to give us a comprehensive understanding of your experience.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, please share them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`intro`, `note`, not required): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully to understand the context and purpose of the survey.

### What is your general sense of safety while being detained?
* **Safety** (`safety`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your general sense of safety during your stay. Please select all that apply:
	+ Safety is always a top priority for me.
	+ I feel somewhat safe.
	+ Safety is a concern for me.

### How would you rate the support from the staff?
* **Staff Support** (`staff_support`, `number`, required): Rate the level of support you received from the staff during your stay, on a scale of 1 to 10.

### What services do you need to feel safe and supported?
* **Services** (`services`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about the specific services you need to feel safe and supported during your stay. Please select one of the following:
	+ Emergency Services
	+ Basic Services
	+ Both
	+ Other (please specify)

### Please specify other services you would like to see available.
* **Services Specify** (`services_specify`, `text`, not required): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, please specify the services you would like to see available. Be as detailed as possible.

### Do you have any other comments or suggestions?
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience and the services provided.
