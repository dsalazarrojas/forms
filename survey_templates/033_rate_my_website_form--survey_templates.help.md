# Rate My Website Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help website owners understand how their customers perceive their website. By completing this form, you will be able to provide feedback on various aspects of the website, including your overall rating and any comments you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall experience on the website using the "Website Rating" dropdown menu.
2. Provide any additional comments about your experience in the "Customer Comments" text field.
3. If you have any further feedback, you can provide your score for the website in the "Rate My Website Score" field.
4. If you have a website account, please enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will help us contact you if we need more information.
5. Finally, enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rate My Website** (`rate_my_website_form`, number, optional): This field is used to rate your overall experience on the website. Select a number between 1 and 5 to rate how good you think the website is.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments about your experience on the website. Please keep your comments concise and clear.
* **Website Rating** (`website_rating`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select a rating for your overall experience on the website. Choose from the dropdown menu to rate how good you think the website is.
* **Rate My Website Score** (`rate_my_website__score`, number, optional): This field is used to enter a numerical score for your overall experience on the website. Please enter a number between 1 and 5.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have a website account, please enter your email address in this field. This will help us contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is not currently used. Please do not fill it out.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your name. Please enter your name as it appears on your website account.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to provide a complete feedback form.
* If you have any further feedback, please use the "Customer Comments" field to provide it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
