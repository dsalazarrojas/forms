## Tips
*   Always follow proper food safety and storage practices to prevent contamination and spoilage.
*   Clean and sanitize your hands regularly, especially before handling food.
*   Store food at the correct temperature, either hot or cold, to prevent bacterial growth.
*   Use clean equipment and containers for food storage.
*   Label food with date and storage information to ensure proper handling.
*   Store food away from light and moisture to prevent spoilage.
*   Use airtight containers to maintain food quality.
*   Follow proper food storage and handling practices to prevent foodborne illnesses.
*   Always store food in the original packaging to prevent contamination.
*   Follow local food safety regulations and guidelines for proper food storage and handling.
