## Purpose
This form is intended for users to inquire about personal training and nutrition. It is designed to collect information that will help the trainer provide a tailored plan for the user's fitness goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter your title and select your contact form preferences (page 1: "Personal Training And Nutrition Inquiry" and page 2: "Contact Forms").
2. On the second page, enter your description (page 3: "description").
3. On the third page, select your goals by checking the boxes next to "Yes" for the objectives you want to achieve through personal training and nutrition (page 4: "goals").
4. On the fourth page, enter your preferences (page 5: "preferences").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Training And Nutrition Inquiry** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a title for this inquiry, which will help the trainer understand your goals and objectives.
* **Contact Forms** (`contact_forms`, `text`, required/optional): Select the type of contact form you would like to use (e.g., email, phone, etc.). This will allow the trainer to communicate with you effectively.
* **description** (`description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your goals and objectives. This will help the trainer understand your needs and provide a personalized plan.
* **goals** (`goals`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the goals you want to achieve through personal training and nutrition by checking the boxes next to "Yes". This could be anything from losing weight to improving overall health.
* **preferences** (`preferences`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information about your preferences (e.g., availability, equipment, etc.) that will help the trainer tailor the plan to your needs.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when entering your title, description, and preferences.
* Select all the goals that apply to you.
* Double-check your contact form preferences to ensure you can be reached effectively.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to ask the trainer for clarification.
