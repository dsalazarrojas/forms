# Urban Air Quality Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about urban air quality awareness and experiences from community members. The goal is to understand how much time people spend outdoors, their primary source of air pollution, and their level of concern about air quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the most relevant answer option for each question.
3. Fill in all required fields.
4. Provide any additional comments or concerns in the "Do you have any additional comments or concerns?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is an optional note field where you can briefly explain your participation in the survey.
* **How many hours per day do you spend outdoors?**: Please select the number of hours you spend outside each day.
* **On average, how much time per day do you spend in the city center?**: Select the number of hours you spend in the city center each day.
* **How many days per week do you work outdoors?**: Select all the days you work outdoors each week.
* **What is the primary source of air pollution for you?**: Select the primary source of air pollution that affects you.
* **How concerned are you about air pollution?**: Choose how concerned you are about air pollution.
* **Do you think air pollution is a serious issue in your community?**: Select your level of agreement with this statement.
* **Would you like to report any issues related to air quality in your community?**: Please use this field to report any air quality issues you are aware of.
* **Contact Information**: Fill in your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **How did you hear about this survey?**: Select how you found out about this survey.
* **How did you find this survey?**: Select how you accessed this survey.
* **What is your role in the community?**: Select your role in the community.
* **How long have you lived in this city?**: Enter your length of residency in the city.
* **What is your age?**: Enter your age.
* **Do you have any additional comments or concerns?**: Use this field to provide any additional feedback or suggestions.

Please note that some questions are marked as "required" which means they are necessary to complete the survey. The other questions are optional and can be skipped if you don't want to provide that information.
