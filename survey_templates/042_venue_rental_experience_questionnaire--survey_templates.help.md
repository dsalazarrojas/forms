# Venue Rental Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights from customers about their experience renting a venue from us. Your input will help us understand how we can improve our services and provide a better experience for future clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide information about your event, including the date and time.
2. Describe your experience with our rental process.
3. Share your thoughts on your overall satisfaction with the venue.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the event you rented from us.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the event you rented from us.
* **Venue Details** (`venue_details`, text, optional): Tell us about the venue you rented, including any notable features or issues.
* **Rental Process** (`rental_process`, text, optional): Share your experience with our rental process, including any positive or negative aspects.
* **Client Experience** (`client_experience`, text, optional): Describe your overall experience with our venue, including any highlights or areas for improvement.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, text, optional): Rate your satisfaction with our venue, using 5 stars as a reference.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about your experience with our venue.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or feedback about your experience with our venue.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing your feedback.
* If you have any images or videos that can support your feedback, please attach them to this form or email them to us separately.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your experience with us!
