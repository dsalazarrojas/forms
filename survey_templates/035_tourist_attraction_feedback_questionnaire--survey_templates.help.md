# Tourist Attraction Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect visitor feedback and ratings for tourist attractions. It's essential to provide honest and constructive feedback to help attraction managers and staff improve the services and experiences offered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. If you have any contact information, please provide it so that we can follow up with you if needed.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Info** (`visitor_info`, `text`, optional): Please provide your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall experience at the tourist attraction out of 5. This will help us understand what we're doing well and what we need to improve.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about your visit. This will help us identify areas where we can make improvements.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any further feedback or suggestions on how we can improve the experience for future visitors.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `email`, optional): If you'd like us to follow up with you regarding your visit, please provide your email address.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Please provide your first name so we can address you personally if we need to contact you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Please provide your last name so we can address you personally if we need to contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like us to follow up with you, please provide your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, optional): If you'd like us to follow up with you, please provide your email address.
