# Academic Recognition Systems Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about academic recognition systems, including respondents' roles, awareness of these systems, and their effectiveness and equity. This information will help us understand the strengths and weaknesses of current systems and provide insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the option that best describes your role within the academic institution.
2. Rate your level of familiarity with recognition systems using the provided options.
3. Rate the effectiveness of recognition systems in achieving its goals.
4. (Optional) Rate how clear the selection criteria are if you have experience with recognition systems.
5. (Optional) Rate the perceived equity of the process if you have experience with recognition systems.
6. (Optional) Select any barriers you have encountered while using recognition systems.
7. Share any practical changes or policies you would propose to improve recognition systems.
8. (Optional) Describe successful approaches or examples of good practice you have seen or been a part of.
9. (Optional) Provide any additional comments or insights about recognition systems.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent role** (`role`, `select_one`, `required`: true): This question helps us understand your position within the academic institution. Please select the option that best describes your role.
* **Awareness of recognition systems** (`awareness_of_systems`, `select_one`, `required`: true): This question assesses your level of familiarity with recognition systems. Rate your level of familiarity using the provided options.
* **Effectiveness of recognition systems** (`effectiveness_rating`, `select_one`, `required`: true): This question gauges the effectiveness of recognition systems in achieving its goals. Rate the effectiveness using the provided options.
* **Transparency of selection criteria** (`transparency_rating`, `select_one`, `required`: false): This question evaluates how clear the selection criteria are. If you have experience with recognition systems, rate how clear they are. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Equity in recognition decisions** (`equity_rating`, `select_one`, `required`: false): This question assesses the perceived equity of the process. If you have experience with recognition systems, rate how equitable you perceive the process to be. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Barriers to fair recognition** (`barriers`, `select_multiple`, `required`: false): This question asks you to select any barriers that exist while using recognition systems.
* **Ideas to improve recognition systems** (`improvement_ideas`, `text`, `required`: false): This field is for you to share practical changes or policies you would propose to improve recognition systems.
* **Examples of good practice** (`examples_of_good_practice`, `text`, `required`: false): This field is for you to describe successful approaches or examples of good practice you have seen or been a part of.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `required`: false): This field is for you to provide any other insights or comments about recognition systems.
