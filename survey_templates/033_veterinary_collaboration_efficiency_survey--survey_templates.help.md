# Veterinary Collaboration Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Collaboration Efficiency Survey is a tool to gather insights from end-users on the collaboration methods, challenges, and time management practices within veterinary hospitals, clinics, or similar settings. The survey aims to identify areas where collaboration efficiency can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly to provide accurate information.
2. Select the relevant options for each question that best describe your current collaboration practices or challenges.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to text fields.
4. If you encounter any technical issues while completing the form, contact the support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Work (Team Work)**: Describe your team's current collaboration practices, such as how often you meet or communicate with your colleagues.
* **Communication Methods** (Email, Phone, Chat, Video Conference, In Person): Select the methods you currently use for communication within your team. You can select multiple options.
* **Collaboration Challenges** (Communication Breakdown, Lack of Clear Roles, Lack of Feedback, Poor Time Management, Other): Identify the challenges you face in the collaboration process within your team.
* **Teamwork** (number): Indicate the frequency of teamwork within your department, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Communication Time Management** (Underestimating Time Needed, Overestimating Time Needed, Poor Time Management, Other): Select the time management issue you face in the communication process.
* **Feedback** (Lack of Regular Feedback, Inadequate Feedback, No Feedback, Other): Identify the type of feedback you receive or do not receive from your team members or superiors.
* **Collaboration Methods** (Regular Meetings, Project Management Software, Collaboration Tools, Other): Select the methods you use for collaboration within your team.
* **Hospital Clinics Partnerships** (No Partnerships, Limited Partnerships, Regular Partnerships, Other): Indicate the level of partnership between your department and other hospitals or clinics.
* **Communication** (text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have on how communication can be improved within your department.
* **Time Management** (number): Estimate the time spent on team meetings, discussions, and other collaborative activities, on a scale of 1-10.

Please note that you can provide more details or suggestions in the text fields, and use the scale for numerical fields accordingly.
