# Personalized Activity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your personal preferences and availability for activities. The information you provide will be used to create a customized activity plan tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the first name and last name fields with your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your email address and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Choose your preferred physical activity level (High, Low, or Medium) based on your current level of physical activity.
4. Select your availability for each day of the week (Monday to Sunday) by answering "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
5. Choose your preferred time of day for activity by selecting a time range (e.g., morning, afternoon, etc.).
6. Select your preferred time of day for activities during the week by selecting a specific time range.
7. If you have any specific availability notes, please type them in the "Availability notes" field.
8. If you have any additional comments, please type them in the "Availability comments" field.
