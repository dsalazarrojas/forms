# payroll_policy_communication_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "payroll_policy_communication_survey" form is used to collect feedback from employees on the effectiveness of payroll policy communication within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the "policy_understanding" question by selecting "Yes" if you have a good understanding of the policy, and "No" otherwise.
2. In "communication_channels", select all channels that you use for policy communication.
3. Choose your preferred method of communication in "preferences".
4. Select your level of satisfaction with the policy in "satisfaction".
5. Provide any comments or suggestions in the "comments" field.
6. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, provide your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Understanding** (`policy_understanding`, select_one, required): This field asks about your current level of understanding of the payroll policy. Please select "Yes" if you have a good understanding, and "No" otherwise.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, required): This field asks about the communication channels you use for policy communication. Select all channels that apply to you.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, select_one, required): This field asks about your preferred method of communication. Choose the option that best reflects your preference.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required): This field asks about your level of satisfaction with the policy. Select "Satisfied" if you are satisfied, and "Dissatisfied" otherwise.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): This field is for providing any comments or suggestions about the policy.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for providing your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for providing your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
