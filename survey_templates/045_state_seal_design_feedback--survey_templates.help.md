# State Seal Design Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

The State Seal Design Feedback form is used to collect feedback on the design of the State Seal, a symbol of the state's authority and identity. This form helps to gather opinions and suggestions from users, which will be reviewed and used to improve the design.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill in your name in the "Name" field if you wish to be recognized for your contribution.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the current state seal design using the "Rating" field, selecting a number that best represents your opinion.
3. In the "Comments" field, provide any specific feedback or suggestions you have on the design.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name to be credited for your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the current design, using a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the least satisfied and 5 is the most satisfied.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any specific feedback or suggestions you have on the design, and help us understand your thoughts.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback to help us better understand your opinions.
* If you have any images or examples of designs you'd like to reference, please attach them or provide a link to them.
* If you'd like to be notified about the progress or changes made to the design based on your feedback, please let us know in the "Comments" field.
