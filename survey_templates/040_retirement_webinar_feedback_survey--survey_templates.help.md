**Retirement Webinar Feedback Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from participants in a retirement webinar, helping us understand their experience, opinions, and interests in future retirement events. By providing their feedback, participants will aid us in improving the quality and content of our events, ensuring they meet their needs better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your rating for the webinar, answering the question "How did you like the webinar?"
2. Choose one or multiple options for your opinion on the speaker's presentation.
3. Evaluate the relevance of the content to you.
4. Answer whether you are interested in future retirement events.
5. Provide any additional feedback you would like to share with us.
6. Enter your email address so we can keep in touch.
7. Fill out your contact details: name, phone number, company (if applicable), role, department (if applicable), and job title (if applicable).
8. Select the date and time you attended the webinar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How did you like the webinar?** (`retirement_webinar_feedback_survey`, select_one): This question helps us understand your overall impression of the webinar. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **What did you think of the speaker's presentation?** (`speaker_presentation`, select_multiple): Choose one or multiple options that best describe your opinion of the speaker's presentation. This will help us understand what worked and what didn't.
* **How did you feel about the content?** (`content_relevance`, select_one): This question measures how relevant you found the content to be. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Are you interested in future retirement events?** (`future_events`, select_one): Let us know if you are interested in attending future retirement events.
* **Additional feedback** (`feedback`, text): This is your chance to provide any comments, suggestions, or insights you have about the webinar. We value your input.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Please enter your email address so we can keep in touch and send you information about future events.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further information.
* **Name** (`name`, text): Fill out your full name.
* **Company** (`company`, text): If you work or represent a company, enter its name.
* **Role** (`role`, text): This is your job title or role at your company.
* **Department** (`department`, text): If you have a specific department, please enter its name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text): Enter your job title or position.
* **Location** (`location`, text): Enter the location where you attended the webinar, which may not necessarily be your usual place of work or residence.
* **Date** (`date`, date): This is the date you attended the webinar.
* **Time** (`time`, time): Enter the time you attended the webinar.
