# Digital Learning Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your digital learning experience. We value your input and use it to improve our courses and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question in a thorough and honest manner.
2. For multiple choice questions, select only one option that best matches your experience.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.
4. Take your time, and do not rush through the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Questions** (Text): Describe your overall experience with our digital learning content.
- **What is your favorite subject?** (Select One): Choose the subject that you enjoyed most or found most engaging.
  - Select one of the options provided.
  - If none of the provided options match your experience, you can select "Other" and provide your own answer.
- **question1_answer** (Text): Share your thoughts on what made your experience unique or enjoyable.
- **question2** (Select One): Choose one of the options provided, or select "Other" if none match your experience.
  - If you select "Other", provide a brief explanation.
- **question2_answer** (Text): Share your thoughts on what made your experience unique or enjoyable.
- **question3** (Number): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the least and 5 being the most enjoyable.
  - This question is not about the subject, but about the overall experience.
- **question3_answer** (Text): Share your thoughts on why you rated your experience as such.
- **question4** (Date): Share the date you started or ended your learning experience.
- **question4_answer** (Text): Describe your experience on or around that date.
- **question5** (Time): Rate how long you spent on your learning experience.
  - This question is not about the duration of the course, but about how much time you personally spent.
- **question5_answer** (Text): Share your thoughts on why you spent that amount of time.
- **question6** (Select Multiple): Choose all that apply from the options provided.
- **question6_answer** (Text): Share your thoughts on why you selected those options.
- **question7** (Number): Rate how much you enjoyed your experience.
- **question7_answer** (Text): Share your thoughts on why you rated your experience as such.
- **question8** (Note): Share any additional notes or thoughts about your experience.
- **question8_answer** (Text): Describe your thoughts on what made your experience unique or enjoyable.
- **question9** (Email): Enter your email if you wish to be contacted for further discussion or to receive updates.
- **question9_answer** (Text): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
- **question10** (Text): Describe your experience in as much detail as possible.
- **question10_answer** (Text): Share your thoughts on why you had that experience.
- **Survey Comments** (Text): Share any general comments or suggestions about your experience.
- **Instructor Comments** (Text): Share any comments or suggestions for the instructor who led your learning experience.
- **Student Comments** (Text): Share any comments or suggestions for future students.
- **Instructor Rating** (Select One): Rate your instructor on a scale of 1-3, with 1 being least effective and 3 being most effective.
  - Select one of the options provided.
  - If none of the provided options match your experience, you can select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
