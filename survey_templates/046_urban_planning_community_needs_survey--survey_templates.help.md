# Urban Planning Community Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Planning Community Needs Survey is designed to collect information about community members' opinions and experiences regarding their neighborhood's needs and priorities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read the instructions carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of the form.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately, based on your personal experiences and opinions.
3. Use the space provided for each field to elaborate on your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Neighborhood Satisfaction** (`Neighborhood Satisfaction`, text, optional): Describe your overall satisfaction with your neighborhood, including any positive or negative experiences, concerns, or suggestions you have about your neighborhood's quality of life.
* **Neighborhood Priorities** (`Neighborhood Priorities`, text, optional): List the top three priorities you believe are essential for your neighborhood, such as public transportation, community centers, or parks.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, select_one, required): Select one way you would like to be contacted for follow-up or discussion:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Mail
	+ None
* **Neighborhood Characteristics** (`Neighborhood Characteristics`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the characteristics that describe your neighborhood, such as:
	+ Parks
	+ Public Transportation
	+ Community Center
	+ None
* **Neighborhood Satisfaction Rating** (`Neighborhood Satisfaction Rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your neighborhood on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, note, optional): Share any further comments or suggestions you may have about your neighborhood that are not covered by the previous questions.
