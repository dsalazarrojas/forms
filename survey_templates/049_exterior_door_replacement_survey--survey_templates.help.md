# Exterior Door Replacement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their exterior door replacement experiences. It is intended for users who are planning or have already replaced their exterior doors. The purpose of this form is to gather data on the process, challenges, and outcomes of exterior door replacement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Info** section with your name, email, and other relevant contact information.
2. Provide details about your **Project Details** in the text field, including any relevant information about your exterior door replacement project.
3. Enter your answer in the **Exterior Door Replacement Survey Form Template** field, using the provided template as a guide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (user_info)**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Project Details (project_details)**: Describe your exterior door replacement project, including the reason for replacement (e.g., repair, upgrade, or replacement due to damage).
* **Exterior Door Replacement Survey Form Template (exterior_door_replacement_survey_form_template)**: This field is designed to help you provide a detailed response using a structured template. Please refer to the provided template for guidance on how to fill in this section.
