# Vaccination Plan Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Vaccination Plan Survey form is used to collect information about vaccination plans, including their category, description, vaccination status, notes, email, and phone. This form is likely part of a larger survey template collection.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the category of the vaccination plan from the "Category" field.
2. Enter a brief description of the vaccination plan in the "Description" field.
3. Indicate if the vaccination plan is "No" or "Yes" in the "Vaccination Status Required" field.
4. Add any additional notes about the vaccination plan in the "Notes" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Choose the category of the vaccination plan from the dropdown menu.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the vaccination plan in this text field.
* **Vaccination Status Required**: Indicate if the vaccination plan requires or does not require vaccination. Choose "No" to indicate that no vaccination is required, or "Yes" to indicate that vaccination is required.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the vaccination plan. This field is a text area, so you can enter as much information as needed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to choose a category that accurately reflects the vaccination plan.
* Enter a brief and clear description of the vaccination plan.
* Be accurate when indicating if the vaccination plan is required or not.
* Use the notes field to add any additional information that is not included in the description.
* Double-check your email and phone numbers for accuracy.
