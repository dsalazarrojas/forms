# Trust Building Behavior Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help individuals assess and improve their trust building behavior. It's a tool for personal growth and development, as well as for coaches and facilitators to use with their clients or team members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction (Page 1)** (`Introduction`, note, optional): This page serves as an introduction to the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully to understand the form's objectives and expectations.
2. **Self Reflection (Page 2)** (`Self Reflection`, text, optional): This page asks you to reflect on your current trust building behavior. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
3. **Relationship Goals (Page 3)** (`Relationship Goals`, select_multiple, optional): In this section, you will identify the goals you want to achieve in your relationships. Please select all that apply.
4. **Trust Building Behaviors (Page 4)** (`Trust Building Behaviors`, select_one, optional): This section asks you to choose the trust building behaviors you currently employ. Please select one that best describes your actions.
5. **Trust Factors (Page 5)** (`Trust Factors`, text, optional): In this section, you will identify the factors that contribute to trust in your relationships. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
6. **Relationship Strengths (Page 6)** (`Relationship Strengths`, select_multiple, optional): This section asks you to identify the strengths of your relationships. Please select all that apply.
7. **Relationship Challenges (Page 7)** (`Relationship Challenges`, text, optional): This section is for you to reflect on the challenges you face in your relationships. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
8. **Trust Building Strategies (Page 8)** (`Trust Building Strategies`, text, optional): In this section, you will develop strategies for building trust. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
9. **Coaching Goals (Page 9)** (`Coaching Goals`, select_multiple, optional): This section asks you to identify the goals you want to achieve through coaching. Please select all that apply.
10. **Coaching Approaches (Page 10)** (`Coaching Approaches`, select_one, optional): In this section, you will choose the coaching approach you prefer. Please select one that best describes your preference.
11. **Next Steps (Page 11)** (`Next Steps`, text, optional): This section is for you to outline the next steps you will take to build trust. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
12. **Trust Building Plan (Page 12)** (`Trust Building Plan`, text, optional): In this final section, you will create a plan to build trust. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
