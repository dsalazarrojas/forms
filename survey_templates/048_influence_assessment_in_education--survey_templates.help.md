# Influence Assessment In Education - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help educators assess the influence of a course or program on their learning outcomes. The data collected from this form will be used to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the quality of education.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant options for each field as accurately as possible.
2. Provide detailed and honest feedback for fields with text options.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page**: This is the introductory page of the form.
* **Participant Information**:
	+ *Male*, *Female*, *Other*: Please select the gender that best describes you.
* *What's Your Age Group?*:
	+ Select all age groups that apply to you.
* *Role of Participant*:
	+ Select your role in the educational context (e.g., student, teacher, parent, administrator).
* *First Name*: Please enter your first name.
* *Last Name*: Please enter your last name.
* *E-Mail*: Please enter a valid email address.
* *School*: Please enter the name of your school (optional).
* *Department*: Please enter the name of your department (optional).
* *Program Area*: Please enter the name of the program area (optional).
* *Course Name*: Please enter the name of the course (optional).
* *Course Level*: Please enter the level of the course (optional).
* *Course Influence*:
	+ Please enter a numerical value between 0 and 100 to rate the influence of the course on your learning outcomes.
* *Course Effectiveness*:
	+ Please enter a numerical value between 0 and 100 to rate the effectiveness of the course.
* *Course Satisfaction*:
	+ Select your level of satisfaction with the course (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Unsatisfied, Very Unsatisfied).
* *Influence of Course*:
	+ Please enter your thoughts on the influence of the course.
* *Factors Affecting Learning*:
	+ Please enter any factors that affect your learning outcomes.
* *Learning Outcomes*:
	+ Please enter your thoughts on the learning outcomes of the course.
* *Teacher Support*:
	+ Please enter any teacher support you received.
* *Peer Support*:
	+ Please enter any peer support you received.
* *Parent Support*:
	+ Please enter any parent support you received.
* *Self-Directedness*:
	+ Please enter your level of self-directedness.
* *Time on Task*:
	+ Please enter how much time you spent on the task.
* *Other Factors*:
	+ Please enter any other factors that affect your learning outcomes.
* *Comments*:
	+ Please enter any additional comments about your experience with the course.
