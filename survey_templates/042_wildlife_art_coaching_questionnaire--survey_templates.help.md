# Wildlife Art Coaching Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for the wildlife art coaching program. Your input will help the coaching team tailor their feedback and guidance to suit your specific needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Enter your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your birth date in the "Birth date" field (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
6. Choose the topic(s) you would like to focus on during your coaching sessions using "Session topic" field. You can select one or multiple options.
7. Set the length of your coaching sessions in the "Session length" field.
8. Enter the date of your coaching sessions in the "Session date" field (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
9. Enter the time of your coaching sessions in the "Session time" field (in the 12-hour format, e.g., 02:00 PM).
10. Add any important notes about your progress or goals in the "Student notes" field.
11. Enter comments or suggestions from your coach in the "Coach comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name to help us get started with your coaching journey.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address for us to contact you with updates and guidance.
* **Birth date** (`birth_date`, date, required: false): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to reach you.
* **Session topic** (`session_topic`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the topic(s) you'd like to focus on during your coaching sessions. Options include:
	+ Coaching
	+ Feedback
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Session length** (`session_length`, number, required: false): Set the desired length of your coaching sessions.
* **Session date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your coaching sessions (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Session time** (`session_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your coaching sessions (in the 12-hour format, e.g., 02:00 PM).
* **Student notes** (`student_notes`, note, required: false): Share any important notes or progress updates about your goals and objectives.
* **Coach comments** (`coach_comments`, note, required: false): This field is reserved for comments from your coach. Please do not fill this out.
