# Corporate Training Program Participation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on the corporate training program. It will help us to identify areas of improvement and ensure that the program meets the needs of all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant options for each question by using the drop-down menus.
2. Fill out the "Email" and "Phone" fields with your contact information, if you wish to receive follow-up communications.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
4. Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Program Participation** (`training_program_participation`): Select all options that apply to your experience with the training program.
	+ Yes: I participated in the training program.
	+ No: I did not participate in the training program.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`): Enter the date you evaluated the training program, in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`): Enter the time you evaluated the training program, in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Training Program Name** (`training_program_name`): Enter the name of the training program you participated in.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`): Rate your overall experience with the training program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`): Select your level of satisfaction with the training program.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`): Provide any suggestions or areas where you think the program can be improved.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or feedback about the training program.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address if you wish to receive follow-up communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number if you wish to receive follow-up communications.
* **Training Program Rating** (`training_program_rating`): Rate the training program as a whole.
	+ Good: The program was well-organized and effective.
	+ Fair: The program was somewhat effective but had some issues.
* **Training Program Rating Scale** (`training_program_rating_scale`): Rate the training program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Training Program Satisfaction** (`training_program_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with the training program.
	+ Highly Satisfied: The program met all my expectations.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: The program met some of my expectations.
* **Training Program Satisfaction Level** (`training_program_satisfaction_level`): Rate your satisfaction with the training program.
* **Training Program Value** (`training_program_value`): Rate the value you received from the training program.
	+ Very Valuable: The program provided significant value to me.
	+ Valuable: The program provided some value to me.
* **Training Program Usefulness** (`training_program_usefulness`): Rate the usefulness of the training program.
	+ Very Useful: The program was very useful.
	+ Useful: The program was somewhat useful.
