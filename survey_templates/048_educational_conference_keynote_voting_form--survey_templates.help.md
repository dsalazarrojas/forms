# Educational Conference Keynote Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings on keynote speakers, sessions, and overall conference experience. Your input will help conference organizers improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your opinions and preferences.
3. Provide detailed feedback in the "What did you think of the conference?" field.
4. Rate the keynote speakers, sessions, and overall conference experience using the rating scale.
5. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which keynote speakers do you want to hear from again?** (`keynote_speakers`, `select_multiple`): Select all the keynote speakers you enjoyed the most, using the "Yes" option. For example, if you enjoyed both "Speaker A" and "Speaker B", select both options.
* **Which sessions would you like to be included in future conferences?** (`sessions`, `select_one`): Choose the sessions that you found most engaging and informative using the "Yes" option.
* **What did you think of the conference?** (`feedback`, `note`): Provide detailed feedback on your overall experience with the conference. Share your thoughts on what worked well and what didn't.
* **Rate the keynote speakers** (`rating`, `number`): Rate the keynote speakers using the numerical rating scale. This will help us understand how well they performed.
* **Rate the sessions** (`session_rating`, `number`): Rate the sessions using the numerical rating scale. This will help us understand their quality and relevance.
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`): Rate the conference as a whole using the numerical rating scale.
* **Speaker rating** (`speaker_rating`, `number`): Rate the speaker's performance using the numerical rating scale.
* **Would you like to see more speakers from this session type?** (`session_suggestion`, `select_one`): Choose "Yes" if you'd like to see more speakers from this session type, or "No" if you're not interested.
* **What is your overall satisfaction with the conference?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`): Choose "Satisfied" if you were happy with the conference, or "Dissatisfied" if you had a negative experience.
* **Will you attend this conference again?** (`will_attend`, `select_one`): Choose "Yes" if you'd like to attend the conference again, or "No" if you're not interested.
