# Best Day Poll Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about a participant's availability and preferences for an event or meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the first section.
2. Provide the name of the event or meeting you are interested in.
3. Select all the days you are generally available to attend.
4. Choose your preferred start and end time for the event.
5. Select the day you prefer to attend.
6. Choose your preferred event format (in-person, online, or hybrid).
7. If applicable, provide your dietary requirements.
8. Indicate whether you will be bringing a guest and, if so, the number of guests.
9. Provide a reason for your preferred day, if desired.
10. Select any days that are absolutely impossible for you to attend.
11. Make any general comments or suggestions.
12. Finally, confirm your date of response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Event or Meeting Name** (`event_name_text`, text, required): Enter the name of the event or meeting you are interested in.
* **Available Days** (`available_days_selection`, select_multiple, required): Select all the days you are generally free to attend.
* **Preferred Start Time** (`preferred_start_time`, time, required): Choose your preferred start time for the event.
* **Preferred End Time** (`preferred_end_time`, time, required): Choose your preferred end time for the event.
* **Most Preferred Day** (`top_choice_day`, select_one, required): Select the day you prefer to attend.
* **Preferred Event Format** (`event_format_choice`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred event format (in-person, online, or hybrid).
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements_text`, text, required): If applicable, list any dietary requirements.
* **Will you be bringing a guest?** (`guest_status`, select_one, required): Indicate if you will be bringing a guest and, if so, the number of guests.
* **Reason for Your Preferred Day** (`preference_reasoning`, text, required): Provide a reason for your preferred day, if desired.
* **Days You Cannot Attend** (`blackout_days`, select_multiple, required): Select any days that are absolutely impossible for you to attend.
* **General Comments or Suggestions** (`general_comments_text`, text, required): Provide any general comments or suggestions.
* **Date of Response** (`submission_date_stamp`, date, required): Confirm your date of response.
