# Seasonal Collection Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather your opinion on our seasonal collection, helping us to improve and refine it for future seasons. Your input is invaluable to us!

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question briefly and honestly.
2. You can skip any questions that don't apply to you.
3. For question 2, select the most appealing item from the list.
4. For questions 3, 4, and 5, select one option that best represents your opinion.
5. For question 6, feel free to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How do you feel about our seasonal collection?** (`1`, `text`, optional): This question is an open-ended text field where you can share your overall feelings about our seasonal collection.
* **What do you think is the most appealing item in our collection?** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks you to choose the item that you think is the most appealing in our collection.
* **How likely are you to recommend our seasonal collection to a friend or family member?** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): This question assesses your willingness to recommend our collection to others.
* **How would you rate our seasonal collection overall?** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks you to rate our collection on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Would you consider buying from us again?** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): This question checks if you would consider making another purchase from us.
* **What do you think we could improve on in our seasonal collection?** (`6`, `text`, optional): This question is an open-ended text field where you can provide any suggestions or ideas for improvement.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for us?** (`7`, `text`, optional): This question is another open-ended text field where you can share any further comments or suggestions.
* **What type of product are you purchasing from us most frequently?** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks you to select all the types of products you regularly buy from us.

## Tips
* Please take a few minutes to answer each question carefully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
