# Anonymous Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Anonymous Consent Form is a crucial step in the research participation process. It helps ensure that participants understand and agree to the terms of the study, including their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Read the introductory note carefully to understand the purpose and context of the study.
2. Answer the questions and select the corresponding options for each field.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Research Participation Consent --** (`intro_note`, note, optional): This is a note providing general information about the study. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Study or Program Title** (`study_title`, text, optional): If you have been referred to this study through a specific study or program, please mention it here.
* **I understand that my participation is voluntary and I may stop at any time** (`participation_voluntary`, select_one, required): Select "I agree" if you understand that your participation is voluntary and you can stop at any time.
* **I understand that my responses will be kept strictly anonymous** (`anonymity_assurance`, select_one, required): Select "I understand" to confirm that you agree to keep your responses anonymous.
* **I consent to the use of my anonymized data for research purposes** (`data_usage`, select_one, required): Select "True" to confirm that you agree to the use of your anonymized data for research purposes.
* **Are you 18 years of age or older?** (`age_verification`, select_one, required): Select "True" to confirm that you are 18 years of age or older.
* **I have been informed of any potential risks associated with this study** (`risk_understanding`, select_one, required): Select "I agree" to confirm that you have been informed of any potential risks.
* **For questions regarding this study - please contact the program administrator** (`contact_info_note`, note, optional): This is a note providing contact information for further inquiries.
* **I confirm that I wish to proceed with the survey** (`final_consent`, select_multiple, required): Select "I give my consent" to confirm that you agree to proceed with the survey.
* **Today's Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): This field will be automatically filled in with the current date.

## Tips

* Please make sure to carefully read and understand each field before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the program administrator.
* Once you have confirmed your consent, you will be able to proceed with the survey.
