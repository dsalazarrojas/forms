# E Commerce Shipping Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on your e-commerce shipping experiences. Your input will help us improve our shipping process and provide a better experience for you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. For numerical fields, please enter a value.
3. For select_one fields, choose the option that best fits your opinion.
4. Use the text fields to provide any additional comments or details about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Rate** (Shipping Rate, number, required: false): This field asks about the shipping rate you experienced. Enter the actual shipping cost you paid.
* **Order Details** (Order Details, text, required: false): If you have any additional information about your order, such as the order date or order ID, please enter it here.
* **Shipping Service Provider** (Shipping Service Provider, select_one, required: false): Choose the shipping service provider you used for your order.
* **Delivery Speed** (Delivery Speed, time, required: false): Enter the actual delivery speed you experienced. Please use the format HH:MM or MM/DD/HH:MM.
* **Expected Delivery Speed** (Expected Delivery Speed, time, required: false): This field asks about your expected delivery speed. Enter the time you thought you would receive your order.
* **Communication** (Communication, text, required: false): If you have any feedback on communication from the shipping service, please enter it here.
* **Order Value** (Order Value, number, required: false): Enter the value of your order.
* **Order Value Range** (Order Value Range, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the range that best fits the value of your order.
* **Carrier Performance** (Carrier Performance, number, required: false): Enter the actual carrier performance you experienced.
* **Customer Communication** (Customer Communication, text, required: false): If you have any feedback on communication from the customer service, please enter it here.
* **Order Comments** (Order Comments, text, required: false): If you have any additional comments about your order, please enter them here.
* **How Important is Delivery Speed** (How Important is Delivery Speed, select_one, required: false): Rate the importance of delivery speed for you.
* **Shipping Cost** (Shipping Cost, number, required: false): Enter the actual shipping cost you paid.
* **Order Details 1** (Order Details 1, text, required: false): If you have any additional information about your order, such as the order date or order ID, please enter it here.
* **Communication 2** (Communication 2, text, required: false): If you have any feedback on communication from the shipping service, please enter it here.
* **Order Details 2** (Order Details 2, text, required: false): If you have any additional information about your order, please enter it here.
* **How Important is Shipping Speed** (How Important is Shipping Speed, select_one, required: false): Rate the importance of shipping speed for you.
* **How Important is Shipping Cost** (How Important is Shipping Cost, select_one, required: false): Rate the importance of shipping cost for you.
* **How Fast is Shipping Speed** (How Fast is Shipping Speed, select_one, required: false): Rate the speed of shipping for you.
