# Employee Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their overall experience and provide a platform for them to share their thoughts and suggestions. It is meant to be a confidential and anonymous way to gather feedback, allowing for open and honest responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand the questions and sections.
2. Answer each question or select an option according to your honest opinion.
3. Be as detailed and specific as possible in your answers.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the comments section (page 4).
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **title**: This is the title of the form, which is self-explanatory.
* **feedback_questions (Page 1)**: This section is a collection of questions that ask about your overall experience and thoughts on specific aspects of your work. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' for each question.
* **overall_experience (Page 2)**: This question asks about your overall experience with your job. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' from the available options.
* **questions_page (Page 2)**: This section contains additional questions that allow you to provide more specific feedback on your experience.
* **comments (Page 3)**: This is a free-text section where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **manager_name (Page 4)**: This field is for you to input your manager's name, if you feel comfortable providing it.
* **email (Page 4)**: This field is for you to input your email address, if you feel comfortable providing it.
* **feedback_questions (Page 5)**: This section contains additional questions that allow you to provide feedback on specific aspects of your experience.
* **feedback_score_1 (Page 6)**: This field is for you to input a numerical score for your overall experience.
* **feedback_score_2 (Page 7)**: This field is for you to input another numerical score for your experience.
* **manager_phone (Page 8)**: This field is for you to input your manager's phone number, if you feel comfortable providing it.
* **date (Page 9)**: This field is for you to input the date of your feedback.
* **time (Page 10)**: This field is for you to input the time of your feedback.
* **overall_experience (Page 11)**: This question is similar to the one on Page 2, asking about your overall experience.

## Tips
* Please be honest and open in your answers, as this form is designed to be confidential and anonymous.
* If you have any concerns or worries about providing feedback, you can reach out to the management team for guidance.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our working environment and practices.
