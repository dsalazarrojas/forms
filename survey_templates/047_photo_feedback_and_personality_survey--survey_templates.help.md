# Photo Feedback And Personality Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and personality data from users, along with their demographic information and image preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please read through each page carefully and fill in all required fields.
- Start on the introduction page, where you will find a brief explanation of the form's purpose.
- On the User Demographic Data page, please provide your basic information, such as age, gender, etc.
- On the Image Details page, describe the photo that is being shown to you.
- On the Personality Traits page, select all the traits that you think apply to you.
- On the Personality Scores page, rate yourself on a scale of 1-5 for each trait.
- On the Image Emotions page, select all the emotions you feel when looking at the photo.
- On the Feedback page, provide any comments or thoughts you have about the image.
- On the Photo Preferences page, select your preferences for photo-taking and photo-sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page serves as an introduction to the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully to understand what is expected from you.
* **User Demographic Data**: Please provide your demographic data such as age, gender, etc. This information helps us understand who you are and how you might respond to the survey.
* **Image Details**: Here, you will be shown an image, and we would like you to describe it in your own words. Try to be as detailed as possible.
* **Personality Traits**: Select all the personality traits that you identify with. This will help us understand your personality and how it relates to your responses.
* **Personality Scores**: Rate yourself on a scale of 1-5 for each trait. Please be as honest as possible.
* **Image Emotions**: Select all the emotions you feel when looking at the image. This will help us understand how you emotionally respond to images.
* **Feedback**: Provide any comments or thoughts you have about the image. This is an opportunity to share any opinions or observations you may have.
* **Photo Preferences**: Select your preferences for photo-taking and photo-sharing. This will help us understand your photo-taking habits and preferences.
