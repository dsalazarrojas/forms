# Education And Life Expectancy Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on users' perceptions and opinions about the relationship between education and life expectancy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing your honest opinions on the relationships between education and life expectancy.
2. For the select one and select multiple questions, choose the answer that best reflects your opinion.
3. For the number question, enter a numerical value that represents how much education impacts life expectancy.
4. For the text question, provide a brief description of your thoughts on the topic.
5. Once you have completed all the pages, click on the "Education and Life Expectancy Quiz End of Quiz" page to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the relation between education and life expectancy?** (`education_and_life_expectancy_quiz_page_1`, select_one, not required): This question is asking you to decide if you think there is a relationship between education and life expectancy. Please select "Yes" if you think education has an impact on life expectancy, and "No" if you don't think it has any impact.
* **How does one's education affect life expectancy?** (`education_and_life_expectancy_quiz_page_2`, select_multiple, not required): In this question, you will be asked to select all the ways in which you think education affects life expectancy. For example, you may select "Yes" for "Improves health", "Yes" for "Increases income", etc.
* **How much does education impact life expectancy?** (`education_and_life_expectancy_quiz_page_3`, number, not required): This question asks you to estimate the impact of education on life expectancy. Please enter a numerical value that represents how much you think education affects life expectancy. For example, if you think education has a small impact, you may enter a low number, and if you think it has a large impact, you may enter a high number.
* **Life Expectancy and Education Quiz Instructions** (`education_and_life_expectancy_quiz_page_4`, text, not required): This question is an open-ended text field where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments you have about the relationship between education and life expectancy.
* **How does one's educational degree influence their life expectancy?** (`education_and_life_expectancy_quiz_page_5`, select_multiple, not required): Similar to question 2, this question asks you to select all the ways in which you think one's educational degree influences life expectancy.
* **What is the most important factor for life expectancy?** (`education_and_life_expectancy_quiz_page_6`, select_one, not required): In this question, you will be asked to select the most important factor that influences life expectancy. Please choose the answer that best reflects your opinion.
* **How does education affect life expectancy over time?** (`education_and_life_expectancy_quiz_page_7`, text, not required): This question is an open-ended text field where you can provide your thoughts on how education affects life expectancy over time.
* **How does education impact life expectancy in developed vs underdeveloped countries?** (`education_and_life_expectancy_quiz_page_8`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks you to select all the ways in which you think education impacts life expectancy in developed vs underdeveloped countries.
* **Education and Life Expectancy Quiz End of Quiz** (`education_and_life_expectancy_quiz_page_9`, note, not required): This page is not a question, but rather a marker for the end of the quiz.
* **How many questions do you have about this quiz?** (`education_and_life_expectancy_quiz_page_10`, text, not required): This question is for your feedback. If you have any questions or concerns about this quiz, please use this field to share them.
