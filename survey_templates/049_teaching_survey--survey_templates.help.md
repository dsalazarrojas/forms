# Teaching Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your teaching experience and preferences. It will help us understand your teaching habits, expertise, and thoughts on the subject, which will be used to improve the quality of our teaching services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the correct option for each question. Note that you can leave a question unanswered if you do not have an opinion or do not want to answer it.
2. Enter your name in the text field labeled "What is your name".
3. Enter the course ID if you are a teacher or instructor.
4. Answer questions 1-7 as best you can. Remember that questions 2, 3, 6, and 7 have multiple options to choose from, so select all that apply.
5. Enter any additional thoughts or comments you have in questions 4 and 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name here. This will help us get a better understanding of who is taking the survey.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID of the course you teach, if applicable.
* **How often do you teach this course?** (`question_1`, number, required/optional): Answer how often you teach this course, or leave it unanswered if you do not want to answer it.
* **What is your level of expertise in this subject?** (`question_2`, select_one, required/optional): Select one option that best describes your level of expertise in the subject.
* **How would you rate your experience in this subject?** (`question_3`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all options that apply to your experience in the subject.
* **What do you like about this subject?** (`question_4`, text, required/optional): Enter any thoughts or comments you have about what you like about the subject.
* **What do you dislike about this subject?** (`question_5`, text, required/optional): Enter any thoughts or comments you have about what you dislike about the subject.
* **Do you think this subject is relevant to your work?** (`question_6`, select_one, required/optional): Select one option that best describes your opinion.
* **Do you think this subject requires more resources?** (`question_7`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all options that apply to your opinion.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions as accurately as possible.
* If you have any additional comments or thoughts that are not addressed by the questions, please feel free to enter them in questions 4 and 5.
* Remember that all answers are confidential and will be used to improve our teaching services.
