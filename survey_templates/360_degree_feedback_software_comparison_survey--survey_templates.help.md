# 3D Sculpture Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 3D Sculpture Agreement Form is designed to create a comprehensive agreement between a client and an artist or studio regarding a 3D sculpture project. It collects essential information to ensure a smooth collaboration and clear understanding of project scope, timeline, and financial terms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's details, including their name and contact email.
2. Enter the artist or studio's name, which will be used for contract purposes.
3. Provide a short description of the project title and scope.
4. Select the materials and mediums used for the project.
5. Enter the estimated dimensions of the sculpture (in cm or inches).
6. Agree on the total fee (in USD) and payment schedule.
7. Set the estimated completion date, taking into account material lead times.
8. Decide on the number of revisions included in the project.
9. Specify the delivery terms and responsibilities.
10. Outline the cancellation and refund terms.
11. Acknowledge and accept the terms by typing your full name.
12. Enter the date of the client's acknowledgement.
13. Add any additional project notes or reference images/sketches.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client full name** (`client_name`): Enter the primary contacting individual or organization's name.
* **Client contact email** (`client_contact_email`): Enter the email address used for project communication and invoices.
* **Artist or studio name** (`artist_name`): Enter the legal name for the contract.
* **Project title** (`project_title`): Enter a short descriptive title for the project.
* **Project description and scope** (`project_description`): Provide a detailed description of the project, including materials and finish.
* **Materials and mediums planned** (`materials_and_mediums`): Select all the materials and mediums used for the project (e.g., Bronze, Resin / Composite, Stone / Marble, Wood, Mixed media, Metal (steel / aluminum)).
* **Estimated dimensions (HxWxD or description)** (`estimated_dimensions`): Enter the estimated dimensions of the sculpture in cm or inches.
* **Agreed total fee (USD)** (`total_fee`): Enter the agreed-upon total fee for the project in USD.
* **Payment schedule** (`payment_schedule`): Enter the payment schedule, including any deposit amounts and payment milestones (e.g., 30% deposit, 40% mid-project, 30% on delivery).
* **Estimated completion date** (`estimated_completion_date`): Enter the estimated completion date, taking into account material lead times.
* **Number of revisions included** (`revisions_allowed`): Enter the number of revisions included in the project (e.g., 2).
* **Delivery terms and responsibilities** (`delivery_terms`): Specify the delivery terms, including shipping, installation, and insurance notes.
* **Cancellation and refund terms** (`cancellation_policy`): Summarize the cancellation conditions and refund terms.
* **Client acknowledges and accepts terms** (`client_acknowledgement`): Type your full name to acknowledge and accept the terms.
* **Client signature date** (`client_signature_date`): Enter the date of the client's acknowledgement.
* **Additional project notes or attachments** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional project notes or reference images/sketches.

## Tips

* Be sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before signing off on the project.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* This form is a general template and should be adapted to fit the specific needs of your project.
