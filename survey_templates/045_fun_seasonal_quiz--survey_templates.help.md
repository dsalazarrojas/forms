# Fun Seasonal Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fun Seasonal Quiz is a fun and interactive form that allows users to express their preferences and interests related to seasonal activities, holidays, and fun facts. The form is designed to collect data on users' favorite foods, seasons, holidays, and other fun facts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Fun Seasonal Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting your favorite season from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose your favorite food from the list provided.
3. Select all your favorite holidays (you can choose more than one).
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter any relevant details about your favorite holiday in the "Additional Comments" field (this field will not be collected for analysis, but it's for personal notes).
6. Select all your favorite fun facts from the list provided.
7. Finally, submit the form and review your answers!

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fun Question (`fun_question`, number, optional):** Enter your favorite number from 1 to 10.
* **Favorite Food (`favorite_food`, text, optional):** What is your favorite food? Please select one from the list or enter a different option if not listed.
* **Favorite Season (`favorite_season`, select_one, optional):** Select one of the following seasons: Spring, Summer, Autumn, or Winter.
* **Favorite Holiday (`favorite_holiday`, select_multiple, optional):** Select all your favorite holidays from the list. You can choose more than one.
* **Email (`email`, email, optional):** Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone (`phone`, text, optional):** Enter your phone number. This is optional and for personal use.
* **Fun Fact (`fun_fact`, note, optional):** Enter any additional comments or fun facts about your favorite holiday.
* **Select Option 1 (`select_option_1`, select_multiple, optional):** Select all your favorite options from the list. You can choose more than one.
* **Select Option 2 (`select_option_2`, select_one, optional):** Select one of the following options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

## Tips
* Please note that all fields are optional, so feel free to answer as many or as few questions as you like.
