# Youth Volunteer Engagement Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from youth volunteers on their experience with our organization. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us improve our programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section according to your experiences. You can answer as much or as little as you like, and please provide specific examples or details when possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your overall feedback on your experience with us. What did you enjoy most about the event, what did you find challenging, and any other thoughts you have.
* **What was your experience like** (`What was your experience like`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experience with us in as much detail as you can. What were some highlights and challenges you faced?
* **What did you enjoy most** (`What did you enjoy most`, `text`, required: false): What activities, people, or experiences made your time with us most enjoyable?
* **What did you enjoy least** (`What did you enjoy least`, `text`, required: false): What activities, people, or experiences made your time with us least enjoyable?
* **What suggestions do you have for future events** (`What suggestions do you have for future events`, `text`, required: false): Think about what we could do better or what you would like to see more of in future events.
* **Volunteer** (`Volunteer`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you are a volunteer or not.
* **How many volunteer hours have you completed with us** (`How many volunteer hours have you completed with us`, `number`, required: false): If you are a volunteer, please estimate the total hours you have completed with us.
* **How many months have you volunteered with us** (`How many months have you volunteered with us`, `number`, required: false): If you are a volunteer, please estimate the total months you have volunteered with us.
* **How often do you volunteer with us** (`How often do you volunteer with us`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate how frequently you participate in our volunteer opportunities.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate your experience with us on a scale of 1-4, with 1 being Poor, 2 being Fair, 3 being Good, and 4 being Excellent.
* **Overall experience** (`Overall experience`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with us on a scale of 1-10.
* **Engagement** (`Engagement`, `number`, required: false): Rate how engaged you feel with our organization on a scale of 1-10.
* **Quality of activities** (`Quality of activities`, `number`, required: false): Rate the quality of activities and programs offered by us on a scale of 1-10.
* **Feedback from staff** (`Feedback from staff`, `number`, required: false): Rate how responsive and helpful our staff has been on a scale of 1-10.
* **Communication** (`Communication`, `number`, required: false): Rate how well you felt you were communicated with on a scale of 1-10.
* **Additional information** (`Additional information`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them here.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific when answering each question, and provide examples when possible.
* If you are not a volunteer, please answer the questions as if you are an observer of our programs and services.
* We value your feedback and appreciate your time in completing this form.
