# Human Needs Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Needs Questionnaire is a tool designed to help individuals understand their basic psychological needs. It assesses the level of importance each of 14 basic needs categories has in a person's life. Each category represents a fundamental aspect of human experience, including social, emotional, physical, and personal growth needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each category of needs and select the options that best describe your current state.
2. Answer each question honestly, considering your needs and preferences.
3. Provide any additional comments or thoughts in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief text field to introduce yourself or provide any relevant context.
* **Human needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on human needs in your life.
* **Social needs**: A single-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on social needs in your life.
* **Emotional needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on emotional needs in your life.
* **Psychological needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on psychological needs in your life.
* **Physical needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on physical needs in your life.
* **Self-expression needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on self-expression needs in your life.
* **Spiritual needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on spiritual needs in your life.
* **Relationship needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on relationship needs in your life.
* **Security needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on security needs in your life.
* **Personal growth needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on personal growth needs in your life.
* **Freedom needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on freedom needs in your life.
* **Self-esteem needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on self-esteem needs in your life.
* **Self-actualization needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on self-actualization needs in your life.
* **Affiliation needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on affiliation needs in your life.
* **Status needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Active", "Inactive", to indicate the level of importance you place on status needs in your life.
* **Autonomy needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on autonomy needs in your life.
* **Mastery needs**: A multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No" to indicate the level of importance you place on mastery needs in your life.
* **Other needs**: A text field to list any other needs not covered in the previous categories.
* **Introduction 2**: A brief text field to provide any additional context or thoughts.
* **Conclusion**: A text field to summarize your thoughts and reflections.
* **Thank you**: A text field to express gratitude or appreciation.
* **Comments**: A text field to provide any comments or feedback.
* **Email**: A text field to enter your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number.

Note: The form is designed to help individuals understand and prioritize their basic psychological needs. Each field is designed to assess the level of importance you place on a specific need category.
