# Choir Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and opinions from choir members and non-members about their experiences with the choir. Your input will help the choir leadership to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to better serve its members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Please select only one option for each multiple-choice question.
3. If a question does not apply to your experience, feel free to skip it.
4. You can provide additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your membership record or as you would like it to appear on our records.
* **Are you a current choir member**: Choose "Yes, current member" if you are currently a member of the choir. If you are a former member, choose "Yes, former member." If you are not a member but are interested in joining, choose "No, interested in joining." If you are not a member and not interested in joining, choose "No, just curious."
* **Voice Part**: If applicable, select the type of voice part you are or were a member of (e.g., Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass).
* **How long have you been with the choir**: Select the number of years you have been a member of the choir (e.g., Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 3-5 years, etc.).
* **Overall Experience**: Write a brief description of your overall experience with the choir.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied or dissatisfied you are with your overall experience.
* **Would you recommend this choir to others**: Select if you would recommend the choir to others.
* **Conductor or Director Rating**: Select how well you think the conductor or director does their job.
* **Music Repertoire Rating**: Select how good you think the music repertoire is.
* **Musical Direction**: Write a brief description of the musical direction and leadership of the choir.
* **Community and Atmosphere**: Write a brief description of the choir's community and atmosphere.
* **Sense of Community**: Select how strong or weak you think the sense of community is within the choir.
* **Inclusivity and Welcoming Environment**: Select how inclusive and welcoming you think the choir's environment is.
* **Member Support and Camaraderie**: Select how well supported and connected you feel with other members.
* **Performance Quality**: Select how good you think the performances are.
* **Performance Venues**: Select the quality of the venues where the choir performs.
* **Performance Frequency**: Select how frequent or infrequent you think the performances are.
* **Communication from Leadership**: Select how good you think the communication is from leadership.
* **Preferred Communication Methods**: Select all the methods you prefer for communication (e.g., email, text messages, social media, etc.).
* **Administration**: Write a brief description of the choir's administration and organization.
* **Membership Value for Cost**: Select how good you think the value is for the cost of membership.
* **Registration Process**: Select how good you think the registration process is.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Write any suggestions you have for improving the choir.
* **What Works Well**: Write a brief description of what works well in the choir.
* **What Needs Improvement**: Write a brief description of areas that need improvement.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Future Involvement**: Write a brief description of your future plans and involvement with the choir.
* **Do you plan to continue with the choir**: Select if you plan to continue as a member of the choir.
* **Are you interested in leadership opportunities**: Select if you are interested in leadership opportunities within the choir.
