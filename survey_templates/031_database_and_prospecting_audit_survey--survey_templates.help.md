# Database And Prospecting Audit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand the current state of your database and prospecting processes. Your feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and provide recommendations to enhance your database and prospecting operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Complete the form section by section, one page at a time.
2. Fill in the relevant information for each section, following the guidelines provided.
3. Make sure to save your progress regularly, as the form will save your input.
4. Once you've completed the form, review it carefully to ensure you've provided accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_page** (`first_page`, text, optional):
	+ This is the first page of the form. It is not a required field.
* **lead_generation** (`lead_gen`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one of the options to indicate the effectiveness of your lead generation processes.
	+ A: Very Effective
	+ B: Somewhat Effective
	+ C: Not Very Effective
* **prospeting_effectiveness** (`prospeting_eff`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all that apply to describe the effectiveness of your prospecting processes.
	+ D: Highly Effective
	+ E: Somewhat Effective
	+ F: Not Very Effective
* **database_quality** (`database_quality`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the quality of your database in a few sentences.
* **data_collection** (`data_collection`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your current data collection methods and processes.
* **lead_quality** (`lead_quality`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the average quality rating of your leads, on a scale of 0-10.
* **prospeting_process** (`prospeting_process`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the average time spent on prospecting processes per day.
* **prospeting_effectiveness_lead_gen** (`prospeting_effectiveness_lead_gen`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the effectiveness of your lead generation processes, on a scale of 0-10.
* **prospeting_effectiveness_data_col** (`prospeting_effectiveness_data_col`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the effectiveness of your data collection processes, on a scale of 0-10.
* **prospeting_quality_database** (`prospeting_quality_database`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the quality of your database, on a scale of 0-10.
* **prospeting_eff_data_col** (`prospeting_eff_data_col`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all that apply to describe the effectiveness of your data collection processes.
	+ H: Highly Effective
	+ I: Somewhat Effective
	+ J: Not Very Effective
* **prospeting_eff_leads** (`prospeting_eff_leads`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one to describe the effectiveness of your lead generation processes.
	+ K: Very Effective
	+ L: Somewhat Effective
	+ M: Not Very Effective

## Tips
- Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you're unsure about a particular question, please describe your current processes and we'll help guide you through the improvement process.
