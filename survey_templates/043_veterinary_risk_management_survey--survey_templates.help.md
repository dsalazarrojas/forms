# Veterinary Risk Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Risk Management Survey is designed to help veterinary professionals assess their risk management practices, identify areas of concern, and prioritize improvements. This survey aims to gather information on various risk areas, staff awareness, current protocols, and priority settings for safety and quality improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions carefully and answer each field honestly.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, taking into account your current practices and priorities.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the survey).
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What are the risk areas you are concerned about?** (`risk_areas`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to identify the risk areas that you are concerned about in your veterinary practice. Please select all the relevant options from the list: Risk of Injury, Financial Risk, Risk to Others, and Cyber Risk.
  • Select "Risk of Injury" if you are concerned about accidents or injuries to people or animals.
  • Select "Financial Risk" if you are concerned about financial losses or costs associated with your practice.
  • Select "Risk to Others" if you are concerned about risks affecting people or other businesses in the industry.
  • Select "Cyber Risk" if you are concerned about security risks or data breaches.
- **Are your staff members aware of these risk areas?** (`staff_awareness`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate your staff's level of awareness about the risk areas you identified. Please select one of the options:
  • Fully: Your staff is fully aware of these risk areas.
  • Partially: Your staff is partially aware of these risk areas.
  • Not at all: Your staff is not aware of these risk areas.
- **Are your current protocols and procedures up-to-date?** (`current_protocols`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate the effectiveness of your current protocols and procedures in managing risks. Please select one of the options:
  • Fully: Your protocols and procedures are up-to-date and effective.
  • Partially: Your protocols and procedures are partially up-to-date.
  • Not at all: Your protocols and procedures are not up-to-date.
- **How do you prioritize safety and quality improvements?** (`safety_and_quality`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate your priority for safety and quality improvements. Please select one of the options:
  • High: Safety and quality improvements are a top priority.
  • Medium: Safety and quality improvements are a moderate priority.
  • Low: Safety and quality improvements are a low priority.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them in this field. This is an optional field, and you can use it to provide further information or clarify your answers.

## Tips
Please note that all responses will be anonymous, and your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve risk management practices in the veterinary industry. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
