# Indian Independence History Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create a quiz on Indian Independence History, allowing you to test your knowledge on the subject. It is intended to be used in educational settings such as schools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "General Knowledge" page and provide your answer to the question in the text field.
2. Move on to the "Political Parties" page and choose all the political parties that were involved in Indian Independence.
3. On the "Dates of Importance" page, select the dates that are crucial to Indian Independence History.
4. On the "Historical Figures" page, select the historical figures who played a key role in Indian Independence.
5. On the "Independence Declaration" page, enter the date of the Independence Declaration.
6. On the "Quiz Questions" page, note down any additional questions you would like to ask in the quiz.
7. On the "Correct Answers" page, enter the correct answers to the questions.
8. On the "Incorrect Answers" page, enter the incorrect answers to the questions.
9. On the "Total Points" page, enter the total number of points to be awarded.
10. On the "Final Results" page, select the outcome of the quiz, i.e. if it was a success or a failure.
11. On the "Teacher Comments" page, note down any comments from the teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Knowledge** (General Knowledge, text, optional): This is the first page of the form where you need to provide your general knowledge on Indian Independence History.
- **Political Parties** (Political Parties, select multiple, optional): Select all the political parties that were involved in Indian Independence.
- **Dates of Importance** (Dates of Importance, date, optional): Select the dates that are crucial to Indian Independence History.
- **Historical Figures** (Historical Figures, select one, optional): Select the historical figures who played a key role in Indian Independence.
- **Independence Declaration** (Independence Declaration, text, optional): Enter the date of the Independence Declaration.
- **Quiz Questions** (Quiz Questions, note, optional): Note down any additional questions you would like to ask in the quiz.
- **Correct Answers** (Correct Answers, number, optional): Enter the correct answers to the questions.
- **Incorrect Answers** (Incorrect Answers, text, optional): Enter the incorrect answers to the questions.
- **Total Points** (Total Points, number, optional): Enter the total number of points to be awarded.
- **Final Results** (Final Results, select multiple, optional): Select the outcome of the quiz, i.e. if it was a success or a failure.
- **Teacher Comments** (Teacher Comments, note, optional): Note down any comments from the teacher.
