# Fitness Program Satisfaction Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool used to collect feedback from users about the fitness programs offered by our organization. By filling out this form, you will be helping us understand what works and what doesn't in our programs, allowing us to make necessary improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select your current fitness program from the list provided.
2.  Choose your current fitness level (beginner, intermediate, advanced).
3.  Provide feedback on your trainers (yes/no).
4.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the program (on a scale of 0-5).
5.  Share your fitness goals (yes/no).
6.  Select how often you attend classes (yes/no).
7.  Choose how often you work out (yes/no).
8.  Answer how you heard about our gym (yes/no).
9.  Add any additional comments or feedback you have.
10.  Please provide your contact details for follow-up or further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which Fitness Program** (`fitness_program`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose the fitness program you are currently enrolled in or interested in.
* **Current Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select your current fitness level, whether beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
* **Feedback on Trainers** (`feedback_on_instructors`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Provide feedback on your trainers, whether you are satisfied or not.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, **Required**): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program, on a scale of 0-5.
* **What are your fitness goals** (`fitness_goals`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Share your fitness goals, whether you are currently working on them or not.
* **Frequency of Classes** (`frequency_of_classes`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select how often you attend classes (yes/no).
* **Frequency of Workouts** (`frequency_of_workouts`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Choose how often you work out (yes/no).
* **How did you hear about our gym** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Answer how you heard about our gym (yes/no).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, **Required**): Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the program.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `note`, **Optional**): Please provide your contact details for further communication or follow-up.

Note: Please fill out this form only once per program, to avoid duplication of responses.
