# University Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from research participants and the research team, ensuring they understand and agree to the terms of the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read through each field carefully and fill out the required information.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask the research team for clarification.
*   Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Research Participant Information
*   **Research Participant Information (text)**: This field is used to collect contact information from the research participant.
*   **Research Participant Information** (text): This field is used to collect any additional information that is relevant to the research participant.
*   **Informed Consent** (text): This field is used to record the participant's informed consent to participate in the study.
*   **Study Information** (text): This field is used to collect any additional information that is relevant to the study.
*   **Participant Identification** (text): This field is used to identify the participant in the study.
*   **Informed Consent Continuation** (text): This field is used to record the participant's continued informed consent to participate in the study.
*   **Participant Agreement** (text): This field is used to collect any additional agreements from the participant.
*   **Study Institution Authorization** (text): This field is used to collect information from the study institution regarding the participant's involvement in the research.
*   **Contact Information** (text): This field is used to collect contact information from the research team.
*   **Participant Feedback** (text): This field is used to collect feedback from participants regarding their experience in the study.
*   **Participant Comments** (text): This field is used to collect any additional comments from participants.
*   **Research Participant Consent Date** (date): This field is used to record the date of the participant's consent.
*   **Research Participant Consent Date/Time** (time): This field is used to record the time of the participant's consent.
*   **Research Participant Consent Expiration Date** (date): This field is used to record the date of the participant's consent expiration.
*   **Research Lead Institution Authorization** (text): This field is used to collect information from the research lead institution.
*   **Study Institution Authorization 1** (text): This field is used to collect information from the study institution.
*   **Study Institution Authorization 2** (text): This field is used to collect information from the study institution.
*   **Study Institution Authorization 3** (text): This field is used to collect information from the study institution.
*   **Research Participant Signoff Date** (date): This field is used to record the date of the participant's signoff.
*   **Research Participant Signoff Time** (time): This field is used to record the time of the participant's signoff.
*   **Research Participant Signoff Expiration Date** (date): This field is used to record the date of the participant's signoff expiration.
*   **Study Institution Signoff Authorization** (text): This field is used to collect information from the study institution.
*   **Contact Info** (text): This field is used to collect contact information from the research team.
*   **Participant Questions** (text): This field is used to collect any questions from participants.
*   **Research Questions** (text): This field is used to collect any questions from the research team.
*   **Comments** (text): This field is used to collect any additional comments.

## Tips
*   Please fill out this form carefully and accurately.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the research team.
*   This form is designed to be user-friendly and straightforward. If you are unsure about any field, please ask the research team for clarification.
