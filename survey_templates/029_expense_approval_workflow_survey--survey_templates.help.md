# Expense Approval Workflow Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for submitting expenses for approval within a company or organization. It's meant to collect detailed information about an expense, including the type of expense, approver, and date, to enable efficient approval processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct expense type from the list provided (e.g., Business Travel, Entertainment, Other, Personal).
2. Select the approver responsible for reviewing and approving your expense.
3. Enter the date of the expense in the Date field.
4. Fill in any additional information you'd like to provide about your expense in the Description field.
5. Attach any relevant supporting documents or files to the form using the Attachments field.
6. Enter your email address in the Email field.
7. Fill in your phone number in the Phone field.
8. If necessary, provide any additional information in the Additional Information field.
9. Review your form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expense Type** (`expense_approval_workflow_survey_page_2`, select_one): Choose the type of expense you're submitting. Examples include Business Travel, Entertainment, Other, and Personal.
* **Approver** (`expense_approval_workflow_survey_page_3`, select_one): Select the person responsible for reviewing and approving your expense.
* **Date** (`expense_approval_workflow_survey_page_4`, date): Enter the date of the expense.
* **Amount** (`expense_approval_workflow_survey_page_6`, number): Enter the total amount of the expense.
* **Attachments** (`expense_approval_workflow_survey_page_8`, note): Attach any relevant documents or files to support your expense claim.
* **Email** (`expense_approval_workflow_survey_page_9`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`expense_approval_workflow_survey_page_10`, text): Fill in your phone number.
* **Additional Information** (`expense_approval_workflow_survey_page_11`, text): Provide any additional context or details about your expense.
