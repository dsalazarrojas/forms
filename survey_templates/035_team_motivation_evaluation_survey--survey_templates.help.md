# Team Motivation Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Motivation Evaluation Survey is a tool used to assess the motivation and performance of your team. This form will help you gather data on various aspects of team morale, recognition, communication, and job satisfaction. The collected data will be used to identify areas of improvement and provide recommendations for team development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Team (Team Name)
	* Manager
2. Answer the select_one and select_multiple fields as instructed:
	* Recognition (Select all the ways you are recognized by your team)
	* Communication (How would you rate your team's communication?)
	* Employee Engagement (Is your team fully engaged?)
	* Manager Support (How would you rate your manager's support?)
	* Job Satisfaction (How would you rate your job satisfaction?)
	* Team Lead (Is there a team lead assigned to your team?)
	* Team Lead Name (If yes, fill in the team lead's name)
3. Answer the select_one fields as instructed:
	* Team Motivation (How motivated is your team?)
	* Employee Satisfaction (How satisfied are you with your job?)
	* Communication Effectiveness (How would you rate your team's communication effectiveness?)
	* Feedback Frequency (How often do you receive feedback from your manager?)
4. Answer the text fields:
	* Collaboration (Describe your team's collaboration)
	* Workload Balance (Describe your team's workload balance)
	* Feedback Frequency (Describe your team's feedback frequency)
	* Team Performance (Describe your team's performance)
5. Answer the number fields:
	* Team Member Count (Enter the number of team members)
	* Team Size (Enter the size of your team)
6. Answer the email field:
	* Email (Enter your email address)
7. Fill in any additional comments or observations in the Comments field.
