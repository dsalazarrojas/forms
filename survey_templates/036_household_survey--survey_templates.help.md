# Household Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Household Survey form is designed to collect data about your household, including its demographics, income, expenses, and living arrangements. This information will help us improve our services and tailor them to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out. If a field is marked as "required," please do not leave it blank.
3. If you are unsure about any question or field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Household Information** (`household_survey_form_1`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your household.
- **Household Members** (`household_survey_form_2`, select_one, required): Select the number of members living in your household.
	+ 1-2: 1-2 members
	+ 3-4: 3-4 members
	+ 5 or more: 5 or more members
- **Household Income** (`household_survey_form_3`, number, required): Enter your household's monthly income.
- **Household Expenses** (`household_survey_form_4`, number, required): Enter your household's monthly expenses.
- **Household Type** (`household_survey_form_5`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of accommodation your household owns or rents.
	+ Rent
	+ Buy
- **Address** (`household_survey_form_6`, text, required): Enter your household's address.
- **Phone** (`household_survey_form_7`, text, required): Enter your household's phone number.
- **Household Survey Form 8** (`household_survey_form_8`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of household you live in.
	+ Adults Only
	+ Children Only
	+ Family
- **Monthly Income** (`household_survey_form_9`, number, required): Enter the monthly income per person in your household.
- **Monthly Expenses** (`household_survey_form_10`, number, required): Enter the monthly expenses per person in your household.
- **Household Income Per Person** (`household_survey_form_11`, number, required): Enter the total household income per person.
- **Outdoor Features** (`household_survey_form_12`, checkbox, required): Indicate if your household has any outdoor features.
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
- If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- Ensure that you have filled out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- If you have already submitted this form, please review your previous answers to ensure consistency before resubmitting.
