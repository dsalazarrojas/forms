# Cleft Condition Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to educate participants about cleft conditions, a group of rare birth defects that affect the lip and palate. The quiz aims to gauge the participant's knowledge and awareness of cleft conditions, including their causes, symptoms, treatment options, and impact on individuals and their families.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the most accurate answer.
2. Answer all questions in the required fields (marked as true for all questions marked as `required: true`).
3. If you are unsure or do not know the answer, select "Not sure" or "Don't know" as appropriate.
4. You can submit your quiz answers and proceed to the feedback and additional comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Participant Information --** (`section_header_participant`): This section collects basic participant information, including your name and email address. This information will be used to store your quiz results.
* **Your Name** (`participant_name`): Please enter your first and last name as you would like to be identified in the results.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`): Enter a valid email address where you can receive your results and any additional information about cleft conditions.
* **Age Group** (`participant_age_group`): Select your age range from the provided options.
* **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`): Enter the current date as this will be used to timestamp your quiz results.
* **-- Cleft Condition Awareness Quiz --** (`section_header_intro`): This section is a heading for the quiz questions.
* **What is a cleft?** (`question_1`): A cleft is a type of birth defect that affects the lip or palate. Select the correct description from the options provided.
* **How common are cleft conditions?** (`question_2`): Cleft conditions are relatively rare, with an estimated occurrence of 1 in 1000 births.
* **What types of clefts exist?** (`question_3`): Cleft conditions can be categorized into different types, including cleft lip, cleft palate, and cleft lip and palate.
* **What causes cleft conditions?** (`question_4`): Cleft conditions can be caused by a combination of genetic and environmental factors.
* **Can cleft conditions be detected prenatally?** (`question_5`): Cleft conditions can be detected prenatally through ultrasound.
* **At what age is cleft repair typically performed?** (`question_6`): Cleft repair is typically performed between 3-6 months old.
* **What are the main impacts of cleft conditions?** (`question_7`): Cleft conditions can have significant impacts on feeding, speech, dental, and psychological effects.
* **How many surgeries might a cleft patient need?** (`question_8`): The number of surgeries needed can vary, but 1-5 surgeries are common.
* **What is the primary goal of cleft repair?** (`question_9`): The primary goal of cleft repair is to restore function and appearance.
* **Can people with cleft conditions have normal speech?** (`question_10`): With proper treatment, people with cleft conditions can have normal speech.
* **What is the role of speech therapy?** (`question_11`): Speech therapy can help with articulation, address velopharyngeal insufficiency, and improve voice quality.
* **Are there psychological effects of cleft conditions?** (`question_12`): Cleft conditions can have significant psychological impacts on individuals and their families.
* **What is orthodontic treatment for cleft?** (`question_13`): Enter your thoughts on orthodontic treatment for cleft.
* **Can adults with untreated cleft get help?** (`question_14`): Adults with untreated cleft can receive limited treatment options.
* **What organizations provide support for cleft conditions?** (`question_15`): Select from the organizations that provide support for cleft conditions.
* **How would you rate your knowledge of cleft conditions before this quiz?** (`knowledge_level`): Rate your knowledge level from the options provided.
* **Are you interested in learning more about cleft conditions?** (`interest_learning_more`): Select your level of interest in learning more about cleft conditions.
* **Do you have a personal connection to cleft conditions?** (`personal_connection`): Select whether you have a personal connection to cleft conditions.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have about the quiz or cleft conditions.
