# Smart TV App Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart TV App Feedback Survey is designed to collect your thoughts and feedback about your experience with the Smart TV App. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and help us improve the app.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form when you are finished.
- Fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- If a field is not required, you may leave it blank, but it will not affect the submission of your form.
- If you have any issues or problems with the app, please describe them in the "issues" field.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them in the "suggestions" field.
- If you'd like to provide your email address, check the "email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_id**: This field is your unique ID for this feedback submission.
* **tv_model**: Please enter your TV model or the type of TV you used with the app.
* **experience**: How was your experience with the Smart TV App? Be as descriptive as possible.
* **rating**: Rate your experience with the app on a scale of 0-5.
* **issues**: If you encountered any problems or issues during your experience, please describe them here.
* **suggestions**: Do you have any suggestions for improvement? This is your chance to tell us how we can better serve you.
* **email**: If you'd like to receive further communication from us, please enter your email address here.
* **page_1** to **page_11**: This is a series of yes or no questions. You will see these questions in multiple pages. Please answer each question with "True" or "False". These questions are required fields.
* **submit**: Click this button to submit your feedback.
* **error**: This field is not intended for you to fill out. It's a placeholder for error messages. 

Note: Please be aware that some pages have multiple fields, but only the **page_1** to **page_11** fields are required, but they can be filled out on the same page as the other fields.
