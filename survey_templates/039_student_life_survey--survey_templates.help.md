# Student Life Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Life Survey is designed to collect feedback and information about student experiences, needs, and perceptions about campus services, academic support, and overall campus life.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and read the instructions provided for each field.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and honest responses.
3. Be as detailed as possible when providing comments or feedback.
4. Don't hesitate to skip any optional fields if you don't have any information to provide.
5. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Campus Experience** (`student_campus_experience`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your overall experience on campus. Please provide a brief account of your experiences, both positive and negative.
* **Student Life** (`student_life`, text, optional): This field is for you to share any comments or feedback you have about student life on campus, including social activities, clubs, or events.
* **Campus Services** (`campus_services`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have used campus services in the past year, and 'No' if not.
* **Academic Support** (`academic_support`, select_multiple, optional): Check the services you have used in the past year, including 'Yes' for each service.
* **Academic Support Options** (`academic_support_options`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have access to academic support options.
* **Academic Support Level** (`academic_support_level`, number, optional): If applicable, provide a numerical value to indicate the level of support you have received.
* **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, date, optional): Provide the date you received course materials, in the format 'MM/DD/YYYY'.
* **Course Materials Options** (`course_materials_options`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if you received course materials in the format you needed.
* **Student Life Survey Comments** (`student_life_survey_comments`, note, optional): Share any comments or feedback you have about the survey.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up contact if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact if needed.
* **Campus Experience Comments** (`campus_experience_comments`, note, optional): Share any comments or feedback you have about your campus experience.
* **Academic Support Comments** (`academic_support_comments`, note, optional): Share any comments or feedback you have about academic support.
* **Course Materials Comments** (`course_materials_comments`, note, optional): Share any comments or feedback you have about course materials.
* **Student Life Survey Comments** (`student_life_survey_comments`, note, optional): Share any comments or feedback you have about the survey itself.
