# NICU Resuscitation Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The NICU Resuscitation Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from healthcare professionals on resuscitation procedures in the NICU. This form helps in gathering information on various aspects of resuscitation, including the type of event, staff involved, patient details, resuscitation type, complications, and outcome.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please select the correct option for each field based on your experience or knowledge of the resuscitation procedures in the NICU.
2.  Please provide detailed information as much as possible to ensure accurate reporting and feedback.
3.  If you are unsure about any information, please select "Other" and add a comment in the "Feedback" field to describe your concerns or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event type** (`Event type`, select_one): Select the type of event that occurred during resuscitation. Choose one of the provided options: Cardiac Arrest, Seizure, or Other.
* **Staff involved** (`Staff involved`, select_multiple): Select all the staff members who were involved in the resuscitation procedure. Choose one or more of the provided options: Doctor, Nurse, Respiratory, or Other.
* **Age of patient** (`Age of patient`, text): Enter the age of the patient, if known.
* **Weight of patient** (`Weight of patient`, number): If known, enter the weight of the patient.
* **Resuscitation type** (`Resuscitation type`, select_multiple): Select the type of resuscitation that was performed. Choose one or more of the provided options: CPAP, PPV, or Other.
* **Complications** (`Complications`, select_multiple): Select all complications that occurred during resuscitation. Choose one or more of the provided options: None, Hypotension, Hypertension, Hypoxia, Bradycardia, or Other.
* **Outcome** (`Outcome`, select_one): Select the outcome of the resuscitation procedure. Choose one of the provided options: Good, Poor, or Other.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, note): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the resuscitation procedures, please provide it in this field.
