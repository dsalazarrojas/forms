## Purpose
This form collects information about the ethical research practices used in various scenarios, including survey templates consent forms, participant information, research practice guidelines, and acknowledgements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Survey Templates Consent Form** on the first page.
2. Provide **Participant Information** on the second page.
3. Review and acknowledge the **Research Practice Guidelines** on the third page.
4. Review and acknowledge any additional **Acknowledgement** sections as needed.

* **Survey Templates Consent Form** (Page 1, Required):
