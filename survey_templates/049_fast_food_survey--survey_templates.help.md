# Fast Food Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about fast food establishments, including their type, size, location, and contact information. It is designed to gather data that can be used for various purposes, such as market research or customer service inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as needed. Note that some fields are optional and can be left blank if you don't have the necessary information.
2. Enter the name of the fast food establishment in the "Fast Food Establishment" field.
3. Enter the location of the fast food establishment in the "Location" field.
4. Select the type of fast food establishment from the list in the "Fast Food Type" field. You can choose from the following options: Burger King, McDonald's, Taco Bell, Wendy's, or Other.
5. If the fast food establishment has multiple locations, select all applicable options in the "Size" field. You can choose from the following options: Big, Medium, Small, or Large.
6. Enter the number of locations for the fast food establishment in the "Number of Locations" field.
7. Enter any contact information you have for the fast food establishment in the "Contact Information" field.
8. If applicable, enter social media links for the fast food establishment in the "Social Media Links" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fast Food Establishment** (`fast_food_establishment`, text, required): Enter the name of the fast food establishment.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the fast food establishment.
* **Fast Food Type** (`fast_food_establishment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of fast food establishment from the list.
* **Size** (`fast_food_establishment_size`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable size options for the fast food establishment.
* **Number of Locations** (`number_of_locations`, number, required): Enter the number of locations for the fast food establishment.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any contact information for the fast food establishment.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, text, optional): Enter any social media links for the fast food establishment.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can leave it blank and we will contact you to clarify.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
