### Battle Arena Selection Survey - Help Guide

# Purpose

The Battle Arena Selection Survey is used by our team to gather feedback and opinions from players about the new arenas and features in our game. This survey is designed to help us understand your preferences and pain points so we can create more enjoyable and balanced game environments. Please take 5-10 minutes to answer these questions and help us shape the future of our game.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer each question carefully, and try to be as specific as possible.
2.  For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your opinion.
3.  For open-ended questions, provide detailed and concise feedback.
4.  If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in the provided text fields.
5.  If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask our team for help.
