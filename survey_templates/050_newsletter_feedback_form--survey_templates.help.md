# newsletter_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "newsletter_feedback_form" form is designed to collect feedback from users about our newsletter content. This feedback will help us understand what users think about our newsletter and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "newsletter_feedback_form" link to access the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields (marked with an asterisk \*):
    * feedback: Provide your feedback about our newsletter content.
3.  Choose the type of feedback you want to provide:
    * positive
    * negative
    * neutral
4.  Enter your comments if you wish.
5.  Rate the content (optional, but recommended):
    * Use the rating scale (low, medium, high) to indicate how helpful you found the content.
6.  Choose whether to receive a confirmation (optional, but recommended):
    * If you want to receive an email with a summary of your feedback, select the corresponding option.
7.  Optionally, enter a form ID to track your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback (\)**: Please provide your overall impression of the newsletter content.
* **Feedback Type (\*)**:
	+ **Positive**: If you found the content helpful and informative.
	+ **Negative**: If you didn't find the content helpful or informative.
	+ **Neutral**: If you had a neutral reaction to the content.
* **Email (\)**: Enter your email address to receive a confirmation email with a summary of your feedback.
* **Contact Name (\)**: Enter your name or alias for us to address your feedback.
* **Feedback Type (\*)**:
	+ **Positive**: If you found the content helpful and informative.
	+ **Negative**: If you didn't find the content helpful or informative.
	+ **Neutral**: If you had a neutral reaction to the content.
* **Comment (\)**: Use this field to add more details about your feedback.
* **Rating (\)**: Rate the helpfulness of the newsletter content (low, medium, high).
* **Rating Scale (**)**:
	+ **Low**: If you didn’t find the content helpful.
	+ **Medium**: If you found the content somewhat helpful.
	+ **High**: If you found the content very helpful.
* **Send Confirmation (\)**:
	+ **Yes**: If you want to receive a confirmation email with a summary of your feedback.
	+ **No**: If you don’t want to receive a confirmation email.
* **Form ID (\)**: Optionally, enter a form ID to track your feedback.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* We value your input and will use it to improve our newsletter content and services.
