# Stage Presence Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help stage performers improve their stage presence skills by providing them with anonymous feedback on areas where they can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question honestly and as thoroughly as possible.
2.  Review each field explanation below for more information on what to expect.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stage Presence Improvement Survey** (`stage_presence_improvement_survey`, select_multiple, required: false): This is a selection of multiple-choice questions that ask about your overall stage presence improvement needs.
    * Choose 'Yes' if you believe that the performer has a stage presence improvement need in this area.
* **Performer Name** (`performer_name`, text, required: false): This is the name of the performer who is receiving feedback.
    * Enter the name of the performer receiving feedback.
* **Self-Assessment** (`self_assessment`, text, required: false): This is an opportunity for you to provide your initial thoughts about the performer's stage presence.
    * Please enter your honest assessment of the performer's current stage presence.
* **Coaching Feedback** (`coaching_feedback`, text, required: false): This field is intended to provide specific feedback on areas for improvement.
    * Please include specific actions or advice that can help the performer improve.
* **Performance Improvement Areas** (`performance_improvement_areas`, select_one, required: false): This is a selection of single choice questions that ask about specific areas where the performer can improve their stage presence.
    * Choose the area where you think the performer needs improvement.
* **Improvement Actions** (`improvement_actions`, select_one, required: false): This is also a selection of single choice questions that ask about specific actions the performer can take to improve their stage presence.
    * Choose the action that you think the performer should take.
* **Feedback From** (`feedback_from`, select_multiple, required: false): This is a selection of multiple-choice questions that ask about where the feedback should come from.
    * Choose who you think should provide feedback to the performer.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): This is an opportunity for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts about the performer's stage presence.
    * Please include any additional information that you think will be helpful.
