# Fleet Services Needs Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fleet Services Needs Assessment form is designed to gather information about the requirements and needs of your fleet. This form will help in making informed decisions regarding fleet services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible. All questions are optional, but please note that some fields may be required for the form to be considered complete.

* **Vehicle Leases** (`id: 1`, `select_multiple`, optional):
  This field is used to indicate if you have any vehicle leases. Please select all that apply.
* **Fleet Size** (`id: 2`, `select_one`, optional):

* **Vehicle Usage** (`id: 8`, `select_multiple`, optional):
