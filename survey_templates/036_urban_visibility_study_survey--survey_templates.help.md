# Urban Visibility Study Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide provides a step-by-step explanation of how to complete the Urban Visibility Study Survey form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully before answering all questions.
2. Answer every question to the best of your ability.
3. Enter your contact email address for response tracking.
4. Choose the options that best match your experiences and perceptions.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
