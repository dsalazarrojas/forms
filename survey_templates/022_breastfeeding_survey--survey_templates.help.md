# Breastfeeding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences with breastfeeding, focusing on your satisfaction with breastfeeding support services, challenges you faced, and recommendations for improvement. Your input will help us understand your needs and provide better support for breastfeeding mothers and healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form

### Page 1: Respondent Type

* **Are you a** (`respondent_type`): Select your role:
	+ Breastfeeding mother
	+ Healthcare provider
	+ Lactation consultant
	+ Family member
	+ Community member
	+ Other
* Enter your selection in the corresponding drop-down menu.

### Page 2: Survey Date and Breastfeeding Status

* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`): Enter today's date in the date field. Please ensure you enter the correct date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Current Breastfeeding Status** (`breastfeeding_status`): Choose how you are currently feeding your baby:
	+ Exclusively breastfeeding
	+ Breastfeeding with supplements
	+ Exclusively formula
	+ Not applicable

	+ Over 12 months

* **How satisfied are you with your breastfeeding experience** (`satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction:
	+ 1 - Very dissatisfied
