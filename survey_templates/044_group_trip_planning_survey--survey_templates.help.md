# Group Trip Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through completing the Group Trip Planning Survey form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Provide the trip name in the "Trip Name" field.
2.  Enter the start date of the trip.
3.  Enter the end date of the trip.
4.  Fill in the location where the trip will take place.
5.  Fill out the "Participant Name" field with the name of the person or group member.
6.  Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant Email" field.
7.  Add the participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
8.  Select whether the participant plans to attend or not using the "Participant Attendee" dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Trip Name**: The name of the trip you are planning. This is the title of the trip that will be used for organizational purposes.
*   **Start Date**: Enter the date when the trip will start. This helps in scheduling and planning purposes.
*   **End Date**: Enter the date when the trip will end. This helps in determining the duration of the trip.
*   **Location**: Fill in the location where the trip will take place. This helps in planning transportation and accommodations for participants.
*   **Participant Name**: This field is for the name of the person or group member. Please fill it out accurately to ensure accurate records.
*   **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email address. This is useful for sending out updates and information about the trip.
*   **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number. This can be useful for communication during the trip.
*   **Participant Attendee**: Check yes or no to indicate whether the participant plans to attend the trip. This helps in making necessary arrangements and preparations.

## Tips
*   Be accurate when filling out the form to ensure that the right information is collected.
*   Double-check the participant's contact information for correct spelling and accuracy.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to your trip planning guidelines or consult with your supervisor for clarification.
