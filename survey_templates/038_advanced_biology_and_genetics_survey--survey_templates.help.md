# Advanced Biology And Genetics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended for users who are interested in completing the "Advanced Biology And Genetics Survey" form. The form is designed to gather information about the respondent's academic background, research interests, and attitudes towards genetics and genomics. It is used to assess the respondent's level of knowledge and interest in these areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing each section of the form and answering the questions relevant to your background and interests.
2. Make sure to select the options that best fit your responses.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply if you have multiple answers.
4. For free-text questions, type your answers concisely.
5. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name.
* **Current Educational Level** (`current_educational_level`, select_one, required): Select your current level of education (Undergraduate, Graduate Student, PhD and Researcher, Educator, Industry Professional).
* **Primary Area of Research or Study** (`primary_area_of_research_or_study`, text, required): Describe your primary area of research or study.
* **Familiarity with CRISPR-Cas9** (`familiarity_with_crispr_cas9`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of familiarity with CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing technology (Expert, Proficient, Familiar, Unfamiliar).
* **Knowledge of Epigenetics** (`knowledge_of_epigenetics`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of knowledge in epigenetics (Expert, Proficient, Familiar, Unfamiliar).
* **Areas of Interest** (`areas_of_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Select all areas of interest that apply to you (Molecular Genetics, Population Genetics, Genomics, Synthetic Biology, Bioethics).
* **Attitude toward Genetic Modification in Agriculture** (`attitude_toward_genetic_modification_in_agriculture`, select_one, required): Indicate your attitude towards genetic modification in agriculture (Strongly Support, Support, Neutral, Oppose, Strongly Oppose).
* **Importance of Bioethics in Curriculum** (`importance_of_bioethics_in_curriculum`, select_one, required): Indicate the importance of bioethics in curriculum (Crucial, Important, Secondary, Not Important).
* **Thoughts on Future of Genomics** (`thoughts_on_future_of_genomics`, text, optional): Provide your thoughts on the future of genomics.
* **Interest in Advanced Lab Training** (`interest_in_advanced_lab_training`, select_one, optional): Indicate your interest in advanced lab training (True, False).
* **Specific Lab Techniques to Learn** (`specific_lab_techniques_to_learn`, text, optional): Describe the specific lab techniques you would like to learn.
* **Attitude toward Bioethics in Curriculum** (`attitude_toward_bioethics_in_curriculum`, select_one, optional): Indicate your attitude towards bioethics in curriculum (Support, Oppose, Neutral).
