## Seminar Content Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seminar Content Feedback Form is used to gather feedback from users about a seminar or session they have attended. The purpose of this form is to collect opinions on various aspects of the seminar, such as its quality, relevance, and overall satisfaction.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the options that best match your thoughts and experiences.
3. Make sure to provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Improvement Ideas" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Quality** (`session_quality`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the quality of the seminar on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Relevance** (`relevance`, `number`, required: false): Rate the relevance of the seminar content on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not relevant at all and 5 is very relevant.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, `note`, required: false): Add any comments or suggestions you have for improvement, no matter how small they may seem.
* **Session Organization** (`session_organization`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how well the seminar was organized, selecting from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Seminar Length** (`seminar_length`, `number`, required: false): Rate the length of the seminar on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is too short and 5 is just right.
* **Content** (`content`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the level of relevance of the seminar content, choosing from Very Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Relevant, or Not at All Relevant.
* **Session Materials** (`session_materials`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the usefulness of the session materials, selecting from Very Useful, Somewhat Useful, Not Useful, or Not at All Useful.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your overall satisfaction with the seminar, selecting from Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback is valuable to us.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, do not hesitate to add them in the "Improvement Ideas" field.
* Make sure to provide clear and concise answers, so we can easily understand your feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
