# Report Generation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the report generation process, focusing on the clarity, usefulness, and format of reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question as honestly as possible.
2.  Select the relevant options for each question based on your experience with reports.
3.  Make sure to provide specific feedback where possible.
4.  If a question does not apply to you, you can select "Not applicable."
5.  You can skip questions that are not relevant to your experience.
