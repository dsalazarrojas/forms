# Event Catering Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Catering Satisfaction Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback about the quality of the catering services you experienced at a recent event. Your input will help us understand your expectations and preferences, allowing us to refine our services and provide better experiences in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your comments or feedback about the event catering service.
2. Rate the quality of the food (food quality).
3. Indicate whether the presentation was satisfactory (presentation).
4. Select the level of service quality you experienced.
5. Rate the value for money (value for money).
6. Share any additional comments or feedback about the experience (comments).
7. Enter your email address (email) so we can contact you if needed.
8. Provide your contact number (contact number) so we can follow up on any issues.
9. Date of the event (date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Catering Satisfaction Questionnaire Form 1** (`event_catering_satisfaction_questionnaire_form_1`, text, optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Food Quality** (`food_quality`, number, optional): Rate the quality of the food on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Presentation** (`presentation`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the boxes that apply to describe the presentation. You can select 'Yes' if you think the presentation was satisfactory.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, optional): Select the level of service quality you experienced. Options are Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Food Service Quality** (`food_service_quality`, number, optional): This field appears to be redundant with food quality.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, number, optional): Rate the value for money on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide your contact number so we can follow up on any issues.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
