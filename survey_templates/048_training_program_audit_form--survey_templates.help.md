# Training Program Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from participants on various aspects of the training program, providing valuable insights to improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have filled out this form only once for your evaluation.
2. Go through the form page by page, reading each field description carefully.
3. Answer each question honestly, selecting the best option that represents your experience with the training program.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **program_name**: <br>
	+ (Text Field) Enter the name of the training program you participated in.
* **participant_satisfaction**: <br>
	+ (Select One) Select your satisfaction level with the training program. Options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Not very satisfied, Not at all satisfied.
* **participant_relevance**: <br>
	+ (Number Field) Enter the number of hours you attended the training.
* **trainer_effectiveness**: <br>
	+ (Select Multiple) Choose how effective you found the trainer to be. Options: Very effective, Somewhat effective, Not very effective.
* **evaluation_process**: <br>
	+ (Text Field) Describe the evaluation process you went through during the training.
* **participant_effectiveness**: <br>
	+ (Select One) Choose how effective you found the participant to be. Options: Very effective, Somewhat effective, Not very effective.
* **training_location**: <br>
	+ (Select Multiple) Choose where the training took place. Options: In-person, Online, Both.
* **program_effectiveness**: <br>
	+ (Select Multiple) Choose the overall effectiveness of the program. Options: Very effective, Somewhat effective, Not very effective.
* **trainer_satisfaction**: <br>
	+ (Select One) Choose how satisfied you think the trainer was with the training program. Options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Not very satisfied, Not at all satisfied.
* **participant_availability**: <br>
	+ (Date Field) Enter the date you attended the training.
* **training_schedule**: <br>
	+ (Time Field) Enter the time slot you attended the training.
* **trainer_availability**: <br>
	+ (Select One) Choose the time slot of the trainer's availability. Options: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, All.
* **evaluation_time**: <br>
	+ (Time Field) Enter the time it took to evaluate the program.
* **participant_schedule**: <br>
	+ (Time Field) Enter the time slot you attended the training.
* **training_hours**: <br>
	+ (Number Field) Enter the number of hours you attended the training.
* **participant_hours**: <br>
	+ (Number Field) Enter the number of hours you attended the training.
* **evaluation_comments**: <br>
	+ (Text Field) Provide any comments on the evaluation process.
* **trainer_comments**: <br>
	+ (Text Field) Provide any comments on the trainer's performance.
* **program_description**: <br>
	+ (Text Field) Enter a short description of the training program.
* **evaluation_method**: <br>
	+ (Select One) Choose how you evaluated the program. Options: Survey, In-person.
* **participant_notes**: <br>
	+ (Text Field) Enter any notes or feedback you have for the participant.
* **trainer_notes**: <br>
	+ (Text Field) Enter any notes or feedback you have for the trainer.
* **participant_email**: <br>
	+ (Email Field) Enter the participant's email address (if you have it).
* **participant_phone**: <br>
	+ (Text Field) Enter the participant's phone number (if you have it).
* **program_category**: <br>
	+ (Select One) Choose the category of the training program. Options: Mandatory, Optional.

Please note that some fields are not required and can be left blank if not applicable.
