# innovation_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and input from employees on their experience with innovation processes, challenges, and suggestions for improvement within their organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, including your name, email, and job title.
2. Provide details about your innovation team and the innovation process you're familiar with.
3. Describe the challenges you've faced with the innovation process.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the innovation process.
5. Provide feedback and suggestions for improvement.
6. Add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **employee_name** (employee_name): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **job_title**: Enter your job title or role in the organization.
* **innovation_team**: Enter the name of your innovation team or department.
* **innovation_process**: Describe the innovation process or methodology you're familiar with.
* **innovation_process_challenges**: Describe the challenges you've faced with the innovation process.
* **impact_on_business**: Explain how the innovation process has impacted your business or work.
* **feedback_and_improvement**: Provide feedback and suggestions for improvement to the innovation process.
* **overall_rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the innovation process (1-5).
* **completion_date**: Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Field 14**: This field is not clearly labeled, and its purpose is not immediately apparent. If you need to fill in this field, please provide a brief explanation of your answer.
* **comments**: Add any additional comments or notes to provide further context.

Note: The "Field 14" field and "comments" field can be considered optional and open-ended, but it's best to provide a brief explanation or instruction for this field, as it can be unclear.
