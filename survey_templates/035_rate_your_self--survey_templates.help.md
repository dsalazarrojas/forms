# Rate Your Self - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Rate Your Self" form is a self-evaluation survey designed to help you assess your attitudes, opinions, or perceptions about yourself or others. It's a set of questions and rating scales to gauge your self-awareness and understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your thoughts and feelings.
2. For rating questions, select the option that best reflects your opinion or attitude.
3. For text fields, enter your response in the space provided.
4. You may complete the form without logging in, but be aware that some fields may require a valid email address.
5. If you need assistance, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read and understand the purpose and questions before proceeding.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that best reflect your opinion or attitude. This question allows you to rate or rank multiple aspects of yourself or others.
* **Answer Scale** (`answer_scale`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option that best answers your perception or attitude. This question helps assess your self-awareness or understanding.
* **Answer Scale 2** (`answer_scale_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Similar to the previous question, but with a different set of options. Again, choose the option that best reflects your opinion or attitude.
* **Rating 0-10** (`rating_0_10`, `number`, required: false): Enter a number between 0 and 10 to rate your attitude or opinion.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field for any additional comments or thoughts you have about the topic.
* **Prophets 2** (`prophets_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option that best reflects your opinion or attitude about the second topic.
* **Prophets 2 Time** (`prophets_2_time`, `time`, required: false): This field is likely intended for a specific time range, but its purpose is unclear. If you have any thoughts or comments, please enter them.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to complete the form. This may be required for form submission or follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for any additional comments or questions.
* **Answer Time** (`answer_time`, `time`, required: false): Similar to the previous field, this is also likely intended for a specific time range. If you have any thoughts or comments, please enter them.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field for any additional comments or thoughts you may have.
