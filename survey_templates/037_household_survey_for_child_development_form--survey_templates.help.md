# Household Survey For Child Development Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about households and their children to support child development programs. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to provide valuable data for these initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, follow the on-screen instructions and fill out each field according to your household and child's information.
*   Make sure to select all relevant options for each field, and use exact dates and numbers whenever required.
*   If a field is not required, you can leave it blank, but be aware that missing information may affect the survey's accuracy.
*   Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Household Location**: Please enter the address or geographic location where you currently reside.
*   **Family Members**: Enter the number of family members living in your household. This is a required field.
*   **Child's Age**: Enter the age of the child(ren) you are reporting on. This field is optional.
*   **Safety Risks**: Check all applicable safety risks that apply to your household.
*   **Family Income**: Enter the total monthly income of your household. This field is optional.
*   **Survey Date**: Enter the date you completed this form. This field is optional.
*   **Survey Time**: Enter the time you completed this form. This field is optional.
*   **Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information you'd like to share with survey administrators. This field is optional.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for future communication with survey administrators. This field is optional.

Note that the form requires a valid email address, so please ensure you provide a real and active email address.
