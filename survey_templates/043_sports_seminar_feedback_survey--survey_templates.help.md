# Sports Seminar Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from sports seminar attendees about their experience and perception of the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and answer according to your honest opinion.
2. Fill out the form for each seminar you've attended, ensuring to provide feedback for each event separately.
3. Answer all questions, but remember that some fields are optional and only required if you have an answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sports Seminar Feedback** (`seminar_sports_feedback`): This is an open-ended field where you can provide a general comment about your experience at the seminar.
* **Have you attended the seminar?** (`seminar_attended_training`): Check if you've actually attended the seminar. If yes, continue to fill out the rest of the form. 
* **Emacs Seminar** (`feedback_seminar`): Choose 'Yes' if you're familiar with Emacs, the seminar name is a hint, not a requirement. 
* **Which topics were covered in the seminar?** (`seminar_topics`): Select all the topics you think were covered during the seminar. 
* **How clear were the seminar topics presented?** (`seminar_presentation`): Rate the presentation clarity from 1 to 5, where 1 is not clear at all and 5 is very clear. 
* **Seminar presentation venue** (`seminar_presentation_venue`): Provide a short description of the seminar's location. 
* **Who was the seminar speaker?** (`seminar_speaker`): Name the person who led the seminar. 
* **Seminar date** (`seminar_date`): Enter the date of the seminar. 
* **Seminar time** (`seminar_time`): Enter the time of the seminar. 
* **Seminar duration** (`seminar_duration`): Estimate the duration of the seminar. 
* **Seminar location** (`seminar_location`): Provide the location where the seminar took place. 
* **Additional notes** (`seminar_notes`): Write any other notes you think might be helpful about the seminar.

## Tips

- Take your time to think about your answers before submitting the form.
- Provide honest and constructive feedback to help the seminar organizers.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the seminar organizers.

I hope this guide helps you fill out the form effectively and provides the organizers with valuable insights to improve future events. If you need further assistance, don't hesitate to ask.
