# Healthcare Technology Adoption Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from clinicians on their adoption and satisfaction with healthcare technology. The data collected will help us to understand their experiences and challenges with various healthcare tools and technologies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review each question carefully and provide your honest feedback.
2. For text fields, please provide a brief and clear answer in the required format (e.g., bullet points for barriers or multiple answers for innovation).
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable answers.
4. For numerical questions, provide a specific number.
5. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you like.
