# Financial Services Client Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their satisfaction levels with the financial services provided by the organization. It includes questions about the services used, areas of improvement, and contact information for follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read through the introduction page, which provides some background information about the survey.
2. Select all the services you have used from the list of options provided.
3. Provide your feedback about your experiences with the services.
4. Offer any suggestions for improvement.
5. Enter your email address and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
6. Choose how you would like to be contacted (today, yesterday, or later).
7. Enter your name, company name, and address.
8. If you have used any additional services (such as loan, insurance, or accounting), select them from the list.
9. Finally, submit the form and we will get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro`, `text`, required): This is a brief introduction to the survey, providing some background information about the purpose and goals of the form.
* **Services Used** (`services`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the services you have used from the list of options. This will help us understand which services are most commonly used and where we can improve.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, required): Write a brief note about your experiences with the services. This will help us understand your satisfaction levels and areas of improvement.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required): Offer any suggestions you have for improving our services. We value your feedback and will consider it when making changes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your feedback and suggestions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Contact** (`contact`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (today, yesterday, or later) so we can get back to you efficiently.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company so we can understand your affiliation with us.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your company address so we can get in touch with you more easily.
* **More Services** (`services_optional`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have used any additional services (such as loan, insurance, or accounting), select them from the list.
* **More Services (2)** (`services_optional_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional services you have used. Please note that this question is similar to the previous one and is intended for clients who need more options.
