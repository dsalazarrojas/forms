# online_store_checkout_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online store checkout feedback form is a tool to gather customer opinions and ratings after a purchase. This information will help the store management to better understand customer needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you're not submitting a duplicate feedback.
2. Fill in the form with your genuine opinions and ratings.
3. Submit your feedback to share your experience with the store management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Feedback
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This is a text field where you can type in your thoughts about your shopping experience. Share your opinions, suggestions, or any other comments you'd like to express.
### Product Rating
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field allows you to rate the product you purchased. You can choose a number rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
### Price Range
* **Price Range** (`price_range`, `number`, required: false)
	+ In this field, you can type in the price range you think the product was sold at. This can help the store management to adjust pricing for future products.
