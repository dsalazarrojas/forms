# Property Maintenance Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Maintenance Feedback Form is a tool to gather user feedback on property maintenance issues, helping us provide efficient and effective maintenance services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "What would you like to report?" question from the options provided (Maintenance Request, Other (please specify)).
2. If "Other (please specify)", select all maintenance issues you experience (Bugs and Pests, Leak and Water Issues, Smell Odor Issues, Other (please specify)) that apply.
3. Choose your preferred communication method (Phone Call, Text Message, Email, In-app Messaging).
4. Schedule the maintenance request by selecting a date from the calendar.
5. If you'd like to schedule the maintenance request, choose a date for the maintenance appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What would you like to report?** (Maintenance Request, Other (please specify)): This is the first step in reporting any maintenance issue you've encountered.
* **What maintenance issues do you experience?** (Select all that apply): Choose all maintenance issues that apply to your problem.
* **What is your preferred communication method?** (Phone Call, Text Message, Email, In-app Messaging): Let us know how you'd like us to contact you about your maintenance request.
* **When would you like to schedule the maintenance?** (Date): Select a date for the maintenance appointment.
* **What date would you like to schedule the maintenance?** (Date): Confirm the maintenance appointment date.
 
**Tips:**
* Make sure to answer all required fields (if any).
* Be as specific as possible when selecting issues and scheduling the maintenance request.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, feel free to contact our customer support team for assistance.
