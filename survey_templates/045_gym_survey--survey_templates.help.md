# Gym Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gym Survey is a form designed to collect information about your interactions with gyms and home workouts. This form will help us better understand your preferences and habits related to gyms and exercise.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your personal experiences and preferences.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your answers to help us understand your habits and habits better.
3. Ensure you fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
