# Citation Style Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Citation Style Quiz is designed to test your knowledge on different citation styles such as APA, MLA, Chicago, and more. It will ask you a series of questions about citation styles and help you identify which ones you need more practice on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the form with the required information. You will be asked a series of questions about citation styles, and you can select the correct answer from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address you would like to receive the results of the quiz.
* **School or Institution** (`institution`, text, required): Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): If you are taking this quiz for a course, enter the name of the course.
* **Your Experience with Citations** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select your level of experience with citations: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Question 1 - Which style is commonly used in social sciences** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer: APA, MLA, Chicago, or IEEE.
* **Question 2 - In MLA style, how do you format a book title** (`question_2`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer: Italicize, Quotation marks, Underline, or Bold.
* **Question 3 - What does APA stand for** (`question_3`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer: American Psychological Association, American Publishing Association, Academic Publishing Alliance, or Applied Psychology Association.
* **Question 4 - In APA style, where does the date appear in a reference** (`question_4`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer: After the author, At the end, After the title, or Before the publisher.
* **Question 5 - Which style uses footnotes or endnotes** (`question_5`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer: Chicago, APA, MLA, or CSE.
* **Question 6 - How are multiple authors listed in APA style** (`question_6`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer: First author et al., All authors listed, First two authors only, or Use and so on.
* **Question 7 - What is a DOI** (`question_7`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer: Digital Object Identifier, Document Online Index, Data Organization ID, or Digital Output Indicator.
* **Question 8 - In MLA, how do you cite a website** (`question_8`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer: Include URL and access date, Only the title, Just the author, or No citation needed.
* **Question 9 - Which element comes first in an APA reference** (`question_9`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer: Author, Title, Date, or Publisher.
* **Question 10 - What is plagiarism** (`question_10`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer: Using others work without credit, Citing too many sources, Quoting directly, or Paraphrasing.
* **Question 11 - When should you use quotation marks** (`question_11`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer: For direct quotes, For paraphrases, For summaries, or Never.
* **Question 12 - What is a works cited page** (`question_12`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer: MLA reference list, APA reference list, Chicago bibliography, or IEEE references.
* **How Confident Are You in Your Answers** (`confidence_level`, select_one, required): Select your level of confidence: Very confident, Somewhat confident, Not very confident, or Just guessing.
* **Which Citation Topics Are Most Difficult** (`topics_difficult`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the citation topics that you find most difficult: APA format, MLA format, Chicago format, In-text citations, Reference lists, Electronic sources, or Book citations.
* **Would You Like More Citation Training** (`further_learning`, select_one, required): Select whether you would like more citation training: True or False.
* **Any Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback on the quiz.

Note: Fields marked with `required` are mandatory and must be filled out. Optional fields are not required and can be left blank if not applicable.
