# Nutrition Graduation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from graduates regarding their experiences in the nutrition program. It will help us evaluate the effectiveness of the curriculum and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and answer them as honestly as possible.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Use the options provided for select fields to choose the best answer that applies to your experience.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nutrition Graduation Survey** (`title`, `note`, required: false):
	+ This is the title of the form, which will be displayed at the top of the page.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, required: false):
	+ Select the date you graduated from the program.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required: false):
	+ Select the date you are completing this survey.
* **Curriculum Satisfaction** (`curriculum_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with the curriculum.
	+ Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Outcomes** (`outcomes`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Describe your overall experience with the program.
* **Suggest Improvements** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide any suggestions for improvement to the program.
* **Graduation Outcomes** (`graduation_outcomes_achievements`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose what you achieved through the program.
	+ Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Academic Support** (`academic_support`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Describe the academic support you received during the program.
* **Support Services** (`support_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose what support services you used.
	+ Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with the support services.
	+ Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Program Evaluation** (`program_evaluation`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Describe your overall experience with the program.
* **Program Improvement** (`program_improvement`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide any suggestions for improvement to the program.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide any recommendations for the program.
* **Follow up Plan** (`follow_up_plan`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose if you have a follow-up plan for the program.
	+ Options: True, False, Maybe, N/A.
* **Graduate Survey** (`graduate_survey`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Describe your experience with the program.
* **Contact Info** (`graduate_contact`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide your contact information.
* **Email** (`graduate_email`, `email`, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Photo** (`graduate_photo`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Upload a photo.
* **Additional Comments** (`graduate_additional_comments`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`graduate_additional_comments_2`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments.
* **Signature** (`graduate_signature`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Sign your name.
* **Graduate Additional Comments 3** (`graduate_additional_comments_3`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments.
* **Additional Info** (`graduate_additional_info`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional information.
