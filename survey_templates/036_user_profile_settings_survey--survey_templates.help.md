# user-profile-settings-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a user profile survey to collect information about you. The goal is to gather your personal details, settings, and preferences. Please fill out this form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the first name field. This is your given name at birth.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the second field. This is your surname or family name.
3. Enter your **Email** in the third field. This should be a valid email address you check regularly.
4. For your **Password**, simply type your desired password in the fourth field. We will use this to secure your account.
5. Enter your **Date of birth** in the fifth field. This is your birthday in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. Enter your **Time** in the sixth field. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
7. In the **Note** field, add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
8. Enter your **Phone number** in the eighth field. This is the number we'll use to contact you.
9. For **Select One**, choose one option from the drop-down menu. This field is not required.
10. In **Select Multiple**, choose multiple options from the drop-down menu. You can select as many or as few as you like.
