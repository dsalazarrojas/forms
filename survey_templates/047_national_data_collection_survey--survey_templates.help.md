# National Data Collection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The National Data Collection Survey is designed to collect demographic and contact information from participants. The gathered data will help your organization understand the audience better and improve communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the survey instructions on page 1.
2. On page 2, select the relevant options for "Contact Information" and "Participant Address" from the dropdown menu.
3. On page 3, enter your participant details in the text field.
4. On page 4, provide demographic details in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Instructions** (page 1, text):
	+ This page provides instructions on how to complete the form.
* **Contact Information** (page 2, select_multiple):
	+ Select "Contact Information" for participants who want to be contacted.
	+ Select "Participant Address" for participants who want to provide their address.
* **Participant Details** (page 3, text):
	+ Enter your participant details, such as name, email, or phone number.
* **Demographic Details** (page 4, text):
	+ Enter demographic details, such as age, occupation, or location.
