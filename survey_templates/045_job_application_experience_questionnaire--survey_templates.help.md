# Job Application Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Application Experience Questionnaire is designed to collect information about your previous work experiences. This form will help us gather details about your job titles, company names, job start and end dates, job types, job statuses, work environments, and job locations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your job title and the company you worked for.
2.  Provide the start and end dates of your job.
3.  Choose the type of job you held (e.g., full-time, part-time, internship, etc.).
4.  Select the status of your job (Active, Inactive, Pending, Rejected, Resigned, or Terminated).
5.  Describe your work environment.
6.  Indicate your job location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Title** (Job Title): Enter the title of your job position.
- **Company Name** (Company Name): Enter the name of the company where you worked.
- **Contact Name** (Contact Name): This field is not directly used in the form but is likely used for administrative purposes, such as keeping a record of who filled out the form.
- **Job Start Date** (Job Start Date): Enter the date you started your job.
- **Job End Date** (Job End Date): Enter the date you ended your job.
- **Job Type** (Job Type): Choose the type of job you held (e.g., full-time, part-time, internship, etc.).
- **Job Status** (Job Status): Select the status of your job (Active, Inactive, Pending, Rejected, Resigned, or Terminated).
- **Work Environment** (Work Environment): Describe the work environment you experienced during your job.
- **Job Location** (Job Location): Enter the location where you worked.
