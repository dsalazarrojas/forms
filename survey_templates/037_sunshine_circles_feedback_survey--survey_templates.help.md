# sunshine_circles_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants about a session or event. It will help us understand how well the session was received, what was effective, and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your honest feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session ID** (`session_id`): A unique identifier for the session you attended. Please enter the number assigned to your session.
- **Overall Rating** (`rating`): Rate your overall experience with the session. Scale from 0 to 10, with 0 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
- **What we got right** (`rating_details`): Share your positive experiences or things that went well during the session. Be as specific as possible.
- **What we got wrong** (`rating_details_2`): Share your negative experiences or things that didn't go well during the session. Be as specific as possible.
- **Suggestions for Improvement** (`rating_details_3`): Share any suggestions on how we can improve future sessions.
- **Additional Comments** (`comments`): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the session, please share them here.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further feedback or to follow up on your participation.
- **Session Date** (`session_date`): The date of the session you attended.
- **Session Time** (`session_time`): The time of the session you attended.
- **Session Duration** (`session_duration`): The length of the session.
- **Number of Participants** (`participants`): The number of participants in the session.
- **Facilitator Name** (`facilitator`): The name of the person who facilitated the session.
- **Session Type** (`session_type`): Choose the type of session you attended. Options are:
  - Facilitator
  - Program
  - Staff
  - Student
- **Other Session Type** (`session_type_other`): If the session type you attended doesn't match any of the above, please specify it here.
- **Session Location** (`session_location`): Choose the location of the session. Options are:
  - School
  - Home
  - Online
  - Other (please specify)
- **Other Session Location** (`session_location_other`): If the session location you attended doesn't match any of the above, please specify it here.
- **Session Goals** (`session_goals`): Choose the goals of the session. Options are:
  - Build community
  - Build partnerships
  - Share experiences
  - Explore topics
  - Other (please specify)
- **Other Session Goals** (`session_goals_other`): If the session goals you attended doesn't match any of the above, please specify it here.
- **Participant Goals** (`participant_goals`): Choose the goals for you as a participant. Options are:
  - Increase connections
  - Learn from each other
  - Learn from facilitator
  - Share own experiences
  - Explore topics
  - Other (please specify)
- **Other Participant Goals** (`participant_goals_other`): If the participant goals you attended doesn't match any of the above, please specify it here.
- **Topics Discussed** (`session_topics`): Choose the topics discussed during the session. Options are:
  - Self care
  - Self awareness
  - Self mastery
  - Self improvement
  - Self motivation
  - Other (please specify)
- **Other Topics** (`session_topics_other`): If the topics discussed doesn't match any of the above, please specify it here.
- **Feedback on Session** (`session_feedback`): Choose how you felt about the session. Options are:
  - Was helpful
  - Was fun
  - Was enlightening
  - Was challenging
  - Was confusing
  - Other (please specify)
- **Other Feedback on Session** (`session_feedback_other`): If the session feedback you felt doesn't match any of the above, please specify it here.
- **Submit** (`submit`): Select your response to this feedback form. Options are:
  - I agree
  - I disagree
  - Unsure

Please fill in the form with your honest feedback and help us improve future sessions!
