# mental_health_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health assessment questionnaire is a tool used to collect demographic data and assess mental health status for patients.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. Provide detailed and specific answers to each question.
3. Do not leave any sections blank unless explicitly instructed to do so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **demographic_data (Required)**: Enter your demographic information such as age, sex, etc.
* **anxiety_scale (Optional)**: Rate your level of anxiety on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **depression_scale (Optional)**: Rate your level of depression on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **mood_state (Optional)**: Describe your current mood state (e.g., happy, sad, anxious, etc.).
* **mental_health_providers (Required)**: Check all mental health providers you have consulted (Psychologist, Doctor, Nurse).
* **medical_history (Optional)**: Describe your medical history, including any relevant health conditions.
* **medication_list (Optional)**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **mental_health_assessment (Optional)**: Provide a brief description of your mental health assessment.
* **mental_health_status (Optional)**: Select all that apply regarding your current mental health status (stable, unstable, unsure).
* **additional_notes (Optional)**: Provide any additional comments or information about your mental health status.
* **mental_health_provider_info (Optional)**: Provide any additional information about your mental health providers.
* **mental_health_assessment_questionnaire (Optional)**: This field is not clearly labeled and may be redundant.
* **mental_health_plan (Optional)**: Describe your mental health plan, including any treatments or therapies you are undergoing.
* **other_health_concerns (Optional)**: Describe any other health concerns you are experiencing.
* **mental_health_providers_list (Optional)**: Select all mental health providers you have consulted (Psychologist, Doctor, Nurse).
* **additional_comments (Optional)**: Provide any additional comments or information about your mental health status.
