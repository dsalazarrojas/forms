# Special Interest Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Interest Evaluation form is designed to help you identify your areas of focus or interest within a specific domain. It is used to assess your special interests and provide insight into your strengths or areas of interest.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully and select the option that best describes your interest in the respective area.
2.  You may select only one option per question.
3.  Make sure to answer all questions to ensure a thorough evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### intro
* **intro** (`intro`, note): This field serves as an introduction or an instruction, providing context or guidance on how to complete the form.

### Interests
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple): Choose any one or multiple of your primary interests. 

### Engagement
* **Engagement** (`engagement`, select_one): Choose your level of engagement within a primary interest, which could be Low, Medium or High.

### Motivation
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, text): Provide a brief explanation or reason for your chosen interests or engagement.

### Primary Interest 1
* **Primary Interest 1** (`primary_interest_1`, select_one): Select one area that interests you the most. This could be Option 1 or Option 2. 

### Primary Interest 2
* **Primary Interest 2** (`primary_interest_2`, select_one): Select another area that interests you. This could be Option 1 or Option 2. 

### Primary Interest 3
* **Primary Interest 3** (`primary_interest_3`, select_one): Select a third primary interest. This could be Yes or No. 

### Primary Interest 4
* **Primary Interest 4** (`primary_interest_4`, select_one): Select another primary interest. This could be Yes or No. 

### Primary Interest 5
* **Primary Interest 5** (`primary_interest_5`, select_one): Select a fifth primary interest. This could be Yes or No. 

### Primary Interest 6
* **Primary Interest 6** (`primary_interest_6`, select_one): Select a sixth primary interest. This could be Yes or No. 

### Primary Interest 7
* **Primary Interest 7** (`primary_interest_7`, select_one): Select seventh primary interest. This could be Yes or No. 

### Primary Interest 8
* **Primary Interest 8** (`primary_interest_8`, select_one): Select eighth primary interest. This could be Yes or No. 

### Primary Interest 8 
* **Primary Interest 8** (`primary_interest_8`, select_one): This field seems to be a duplicate of the above field. Please ignore this field for the sake of user experience. 

### Tips
*   Please select only one option per field.
*   Make sure to answer all fields to get the most accurate evaluation. 
*   If you have trouble understanding a question, refer back to the introductory section for guidance. 
*   If you need further assistance, contact your administrator for help.
