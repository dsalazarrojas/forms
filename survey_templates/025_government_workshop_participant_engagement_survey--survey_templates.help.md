# Government Workshop Participant Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from government workshop participants on their overall experience, satisfaction, and areas for improvement. Your input will help us refine our workshop format and content to better serve you and other participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
2. Select the options that best match your experience.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Comments" field.
5. If you would like to be contacted for further feedback, please select your preferred contact method in the "Contact Details" field.
