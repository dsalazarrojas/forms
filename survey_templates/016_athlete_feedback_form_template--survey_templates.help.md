# Athlete Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from athletes on their experiences with our programs. It is essential to provide accurate and honest feedback to help us improve the quality of our services. Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in a quiet and private setting, free from distractions.
- Ensure that you have all necessary information and documents ready before proceeding.
- If you are unsure about any field or question, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (1) (Required): This field is for the athlete's full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (3) (Optional): If the athlete is under 18, please provide the parent or guardian's name for parental consent.
* **Program or Class Name** (4) (Required): Enter the name of the program or class the athlete is enrolled in.
* **How long have you been with our program?** (5) (Required): Choose the correct duration of the athlete's enrollment.
* **Quality of Coaching and Instruction** (7) (Required): Rate the quality of coaching and instruction from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Training Intensity Level** (8) (Required): Select the intensity level of the training from 1 (Too Low) to 5 (Too High).
* **Progress Towards Your Athletic Goals** (9) (Required): Rate the progress the athlete has made towards their athletic goals from 1 (No Progress) to 5 (Exceeded Goals).
* **Engagement and Fun Level** (10) (Required): Select the level of engagement and fun from 1 (Not Engaged) to 5 (Highly Motivated).
* **Availability and Quality of Equipment** (12) (Required): Rate the availability and quality of equipment from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Schedule Flexibility and Consistency** (13) (Required): Select the schedule flexibility and consistency from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Effectiveness of Staff Communication** (14) (Required): Rate the effectiveness of staff communication from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **How likely are you to recommend our program to others?** (16) (Required): Select a score from 0 (Not at all likely) to 10 (Extremely likely).
* **What do you enjoy most about our program?** (17) (Optional): Write a brief description of what the athlete enjoys most about the program.
* **What is one thing we could do better to improve your experience?** (18) (Required): Provide a constructive feedback on how we can improve.
* **Would you like to be contacted to discuss your feedback?** (19) (Required): Select "True" to receive a call-back.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (20) (Optional, if selected "True" above): Select the preferred contact method (Email, Phone Call, In-person Meeting).
* **Date of Submission** (21) (Required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
 

This guide will help you complete the form accurately and provide the best possible feedback for us to improve our services. If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
