# self-defense-product-feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self-defense-product-feedback form is designed to collect detailed information from customers about their product purchase experiences, including satisfaction, recommendations, and any additional comments or issues they might have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please only complete this form if you have purchased a product from the company.
2. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
3. If you have a genuine issue or concern with your purchase, please provide specific details to help us understand the problem.
4. If you wish to provide positive feedback, please let us know what you liked about the product or service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Product Feedback
* **Product Feedback** (`product_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your purchase experience, including any issues or concerns you might have.

### 2. Satisfaction Level
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the following options to indicate your level of satisfaction with the product:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### 3. Recommended
* **Recommended** (`recommended`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if you would recommend this product to others:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 4. Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions about your purchase experience, please provide them here.

### 5. Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address.

### 6. Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.

### 7. Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted, please provide your name.

### 8. Product Price
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, `number`, required: false): If you would like to share the price you paid for the product, please provide it here.

### 9. Purchase Date
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, `date`, required: false): If you would like to share the date you made your purchase, please select a date.

### 10. Purchase Time
* **Purchase Time** (`purchase_time`, `time`, required: false): If you would like to share the time you made your purchase, please select a time.

### 11. Product Brand
* **Product Brand** (`product_brand`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to share the brand of the product, please provide it here.

### 12. Product Model
* **Product Model** (`product_model`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to share the model of the product, please provide it here.

### 13. Purchase Quantity
* **Purchase Quantity** (`purchase_quantity`, `number`, required: false): If you would like to share the quantity of the product you purchased, please provide it here.

### 14. Purchase Value
* **Purchase Value** (`purchase_value`, `number`, required: false): If you would like to share the value of the product (e.g., dollars), please provide it here.

### 15. Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional information about your purchase experience, please share it here.

### 16. Product Image Url
* **Product Image Url** (`product_image_url`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to share the URL of the product image, please provide it here.

### 17. Product Description
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to share a description of the product, please provide it here.
