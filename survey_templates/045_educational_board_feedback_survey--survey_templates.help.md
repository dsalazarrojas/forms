# Educational Board Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from educational board members on their experiences and perceptions of the current level of service provided by the organization. The feedback will help us understand their strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and inform future development and service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure that we can reach out to you for any follow-up questions or discussions.
3. Select your Stakeholder Role from the list to indicate your position or involvement with the organization.
4. Choose which departments you are involved with by selecting multiple options from the list.
5. Provide your feedback on what you think is our greatest strength, what you think is our greatest weakness, and how likely you are to recommend us to a friend or colleague.
6. Enter any additional suggestions or feedback you may have for improvements.
7. Enter the current Date and Time you are providing the feedback.
8. Enter your long-term goals and objectives for the organization.
9. Fill in the School Board and School District fields as applicable.
