# Nail Shape Customization Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the organization's nail shape customization options. It aims to understand customer preferences and satisfaction with the current offerings, and to gather suggestions for future improvements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions as honestly as possible.
2. Please note that some questions are optional, but we encourage you to provide as much information as you can.
3. Once you have completed the form, please click submit to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nail Shape Customization Feedback**: This is the main section of the form where you can provide your overall feedback on the nail shape customization options.
	+ Please provide your thoughts on what we are doing well and what could be improved.
* **Preferred Nail Shape**: We offer five different nail shapes: Almond, Square, Round, Oval, and Bell Shaped. Please select all that apply to your preferences.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with current nail shape?**: This question is a rating system where you can select one of the five options: Very Dissatisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Satisfied, Very Satisfied.
* **What shape would you like to see offered next?**: If you have a specific shape in mind that we don't offer currently, please let us know by typing it in the text field.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any other thoughts or suggestions about our nail shape customization services, please add them here.
* **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact information so we can follow up with you regarding any questions or to clarify any points from your feedback.
