# Employee Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Engagement Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on their job satisfaction, engagement levels, and overall company culture. It's an essential tool for managers and HR teams to understand employee sentiments and make data-driven decisions to improve the work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your company name in the first field.
2. Provide your name in the second field.
3. Enter your email address in the third field (optional).
4. Choose the size of your company from the options provided.
5. Select your job title from the options.
6. Select your department from the options.
7. Choose how many years you've been with the company.
8. Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
9. Select your level of satisfaction with communication from the options provided.
10. Choose how you rate team collaboration, autonomy, feedback opportunities, recognition, growth opportunities, diversity and inclusion, job security, work-life balance, and open communication from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company to help us understand which team this feedback is related to.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name to help us identify the responses.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to provide additional information.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Choose the size of your company to help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Select your job title to help us understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Choose your department to help us understand the team you're working with.
* **Years with Company** (`years_with_company`, select_multiple, required): Select how many years you've been with the company to help us understand your experience and perspective.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, required): Select your level of satisfaction with communication from the options provided.
* **Team Collaboration** (`team_collaboration`, date, required): This field is not applicable as it seems there's an error in the form definition, please disregard.
* **Autonomy** (`autonomy`, time, required): This field is not applicable as it seems there's an error in the form definition, please disregard.
* **Feedback Opportunities** (`feedback_opportunities`, text, required): Provide any feedback you have on opportunities for growth and development.
* **Recognition** (`recognition`, text, required): Provide any feedback you have on recognition and appreciation for your work.
* **Growth Opportunities** (`growth_opportunities`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with growth opportunities from the options provided.
* **Diversity and Inclusion** (`diversity_inclusion`, select_multiple, required): Select how important diversity and inclusion are to you.
* **Job Security** (`job_security`, text, required): Provide any feedback you have on job security and stability.
* **Work Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with work-life balance from the options provided.
* **Open Communication** (`open_communication`, select_multiple, required): Select your level of satisfaction with open communication from the options provided.
