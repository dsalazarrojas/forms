## Knowledge Lab Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Knowledge Lab Participation Survey is designed to collect feedback and input from participants in our Knowledge Lab sessions. This survey will help us understand how well our sessions are meeting participant needs and expectations. Please take a few minutes to answer these questions to help us improve our Knowledge Labs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For numerical questions, please enter a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
3. For text questions, please provide as much detail as you can.
4. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the "Improvement suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey session attendance (1)** (`survey_session_attendance`, number, required: false): This question asks about the number of sessions you attended in the Knowledge Lab.
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating (2)** (`overall_satisfaction_rating`, number, required: false): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the Knowledge Lab sessions on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Improvement suggestions (3)** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, required: false): Here, you can provide any suggestions you have for improving the Knowledge Lab sessions.
* **Session Length (4)** (`session_length`, number, required: false): This question asks about the length of the sessions you attended.
* **Team Name (5)** (`team_name`, text, required: false): If you are part of a team, please let us know the name of your team.
* **Session Attendance (6)** (`session_attendance`, number, required: false): This question asks about the number of sessions you attended.
* **Session Start Time (7)** (`session_start_time`, time, required: false): This question asks about the start time of the sessions you attended.
* **Session End Time (8)** (`session_end_time`, time, required: false): This question asks about the end time of the sessions you attended.
* **Rating Satisfaction (9)** (`rating_satisfaction`, number, required: false): This question asks about your rating for the overall satisfaction with the Knowledge Lab on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Rating Quality (10)** (`rating_quality`, number, required: false): This question asks about your rating for the quality of the Knowledge Lab on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Improvement area (11)** (`improvement_area`, text, required: false): Here, you can provide any areas where you feel the Knowledge Lab can be improved.
* **Rating Attendance (12)** (`rating_attendance`, number, required: false): This question asks about your rating for the attendance of the Knowledge Lab on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Rating Materials (13)** (`rating_materials`, number, required: false): This question asks about your rating for the materials provided by the Knowledge Lab on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Rating Instructor (14)** (`rating_instructor`, number, required: false): This question asks about your rating for the instructor of the Knowledge Lab on a scale from 1 to 5.
