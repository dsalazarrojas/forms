# innovation_strategy_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback on innovation initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and respond to the questions accordingly.
2. For select questions, choose the option that best reflects your opinion.
3. Fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief overview of the innovation initiative. Please read through it and ensure you understand the context before proceeding.
* **How do you rate this innovation initiative?** (`section_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how strongly you agree or disagree with this innovation initiative.
	+ Strongly agree
	+ Somewhat agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat disagree
	+ Strongly disagree
* **section_2** (`section_2`, `text`, required: false): This field is currently not defined in the form. Please fill it out with a brief description.
* **section_3** (`section_3`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or feedback on the innovation initiative.
* **section_4** (`section_4`, `text`, required: false): This is not a multiple-choice question, but a free text field. You can enter any information you feel relevant.
* **section_5** (`section_5`, `date`, required: false): This field is not defined in the form. You can fill it out with any date if you feel it's relevant.
* **section_6** (`section_6`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or feedback on the innovation initiative.
* **section_7** (`section_7`, `number`, required: false): This field is for numerical values, e.g. number of people involved or amount of budget spent.
* **section_8** (`section_8`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any final comments or feedback on the innovation initiative.
