# creative_software_training_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a brief questionnaire designed to gather feedback and information from participants after the creative software training session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question with the relevant information.
3. Do not leave any field blank without a valid response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Note): This section provides a space for you to briefly describe your experience with the creative software training session.
* **Participants Name** (Text): Enter your name as it appears on your participant badge or ID.
* **Email** (Email): Please enter a valid email address where we can contact you for any further information or follow-up questions.
* **Training Session** (Select Multiple): Indicate whether you attended the training session:
	+ Yes, I attended the session.
	+ No, I did not attend the session.
* **Rating** (Number): Rate your experience with the training session on a scale from 1 to 5. 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments** (Text): Share any additional comments or feedback about the training session.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and clear answers.
* Please ensure that the email address is correct and active, as we may contact you for further information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
