# Hospitality Venue Audit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers on their experience with a hospitality venue, helping the venue identify areas for improvement and provide better service to its customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name as required fields.
2. Provide your email address to ensure that your feedback can be directed to the correct venue.
3. Enter your phone number for the venue's reference.
4. Select the type of venue you visited from the options provided.
5. Rate the cleanliness and safety of the venue, providing a number rating (1-5) for both.
6. Rate the quality of the staff's service, providing a number rating (1-5) for both cleanliness and safety.
7. Provide an overall rating of the venue, which can be based on any aspect of the experience.
8. If you have any comments, please provide them in the text field. This is an optional field, but it will help to provide more specific and detailed feedback.
9. You may also provide additional comments on the cleanliness, safety, or staff of the venue, if you feel that they are particularly noteworthy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address to ensure that your feedback is directed to the correct venue.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for the venue's reference.
* **Venue Type** (`venue_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of venue you visited, such as Hotel, Restaurant, or Event Space.
* **Cleanliness Rating** (`cleanliness_rating`, number, required): Rate the cleanliness of the venue on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Safety Rating** (`safety_rating`, number, required): Rate the safety of the venue on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Staff Rating** (`staff_rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of the staff's service on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Provide an overall rating of the venue, which can be based on any aspect of your experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or feedback on your experience, please provide them here.
* **Cleanliness Comment** (`cleanliness_comment`, text, optional): If you want to provide more specific feedback on the cleanliness of the venue, please do so here.
* **Safety Comment** (`safety_comment`, text, optional): If you want to provide more specific feedback on the safety of the venue, please do so here.
* **Staff Comment** (`staff_comment`, text, optional): If you want to provide more specific feedback on the staff's service, please do so here.
