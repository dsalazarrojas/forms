# Friends And Family Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Friends and Family Survey is a tool designed to collect opinions and sentiments from friends and family members about a particular individual or situation. This form is intended for general use and is not meant to collect sensitive or personal information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select all applicable answers from the "General Opinions" field by clicking on the checkbox next to each option.
*   Provide a clear and concise answer to the "General Opinions" field.
*   Enter the number of people that have a specific opinion in the "No Of People" field.
*   Enter a short answer to each question in the fields "How", "Do", "To", "What", "This", "Is", "Of" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Opinions**: This field allows you to select all the opinions that apply to the question. Please click on the checkbox next to each option to select it.
*   **General Opinions** (text field): This field is a free-text field where you can provide a clear and concise answer to the question.
*   **No Of People**: This field is used to enter the number of people that have a specific opinion.
*   **How**: This field is used to enter a short answer to the question.
*   **Do**: This field is used to enter a short answer to the question.
*   **To**: This field is used to enter a short answer to the question.
*   **What**: This field is used to enter a short answer to the question.
*   **This**: This field is used to enter a short answer to the question.
*   **Is**: This field is used to enter a short answer to the question.
*   **Of**: This field is used to enter a short answer to the question.

Please note that the fields with the "General Opinions" label have two possible ways to be completed.
