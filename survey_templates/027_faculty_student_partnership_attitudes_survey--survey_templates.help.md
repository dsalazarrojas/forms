# Faculty Student Partnership Attitudes Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Faculty Student Partnership Attitudes Survey is designed to gather information on faculty and student opinions and attitudes towards their partnership and collaboration. This information will be used to inform strategic planning and evaluation of faculty-student partnership and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to each question, either "Yes" or "No" depending on your response.
2. You may select multiple options for some questions.
3. Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Faculty** (`1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks if you believe that the faculty has a positive partnership with students. You can select "Yes" if you think there is a partnership, or "No" if you do not think there is a partnership.
- **Student** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks if you believe that students have a positive partnership with faculty. You can select "Yes" if you think there is a partnership, or "No" if you do not think there is a partnership.
- **Faculty Students Collaboration** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks if you believe that faculty and students collaborate with each other. You can select "Yes" if you think collaboration is frequent, or "No" if you do not think collaboration is frequent.
- **Student Students Collaboration** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks if you believe that students collaborate with each other. You can select "Yes" if you think collaboration is frequent, or "No" if you do not think collaboration is frequent.
- **Faculty Students Involvement** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks if you believe that faculty are involved in student activities. You can select "Yes" if you think faculty are involved, or "No" if you do not think faculty are involved.
- **Student Students Involvement** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks if you believe that students are involved in student activities. You can select "Yes" if you think students are involved, or "No" if you do not think students are involved.
- **Faculty-Student** (`7`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about the faculty-student partnership.
- **Student-Student** (`8`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about student-student partnership.
- **Faculty Students Collaboration Frequency** (`9`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about the frequency of faculty-student collaboration.
- **Student Students Collaboration Frequency** (`10`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about the frequency of student-student collaboration.

## Tips

- Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the administrator.
- If you have any additional comments or notes, please use the text fields provided.
- This form should only be completed by faculty and students who have had direct interaction or collaboration with their partners.
