# Academic Data Sharing Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Data Sharing Attitudes Survey is designed to collect information about respondents' attitudes towards data sharing in academic settings. Your participation will help researchers understand how academics perceive and practice data sharing, which will inform the development of data sharing best practices and improve research collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information:
	* Respondent name: Enter your full name.
	* Respondent email: Enter your institution or personal email address.
2. Select your role in the institution:
	* Institutional affiliation: Enter your department or research group.
	* Role in institution: Choose the option that best describes your role (Faculty, Graduate student, Postdoctoral researcher, Librarian, Staff, or Other).
3. Answer questions about your willingness and barriers to data sharing:
	* Willingness to share data: Select how willing you are to share your research data (Very willing, Somewhat willing, Neutral, Somewhat unwilling, or Not willing).
	* Barriers to data sharing: Select all applicable barriers that you face when sharing data (Privacy concerns, Lack of credit or citation, Technical barriers, Legal or ethical restrictions, Time and effort required, or Other).
4. Choose your preferred data sharing platforms:
	* Preferred data sharing platforms: Select all applicable platforms where you would consider sharing your data (Institutional repository, Disciplinary repository, General data archive, Personal or lab website, or Publisher supplementary materials).
5. Specify the types of data you would share:
	* Types of data you would share: Select all applicable types of data you would share (Raw data, Processed data, Code and scripts, Protocols and methods, or Metadata only).
6. Identify incentives that would increase data sharing:
	* Incentives for sharing: Select up to three incentives that would increase your willingness to share your data (Formal recognition or citation, Funding agency requirement, Institutional mandate, Publisher policy, or Technical support).
7. Add any additional comments:
	* Additional comments or suggestions: Enter any other thoughts or suggestions you have about data sharing practices.
