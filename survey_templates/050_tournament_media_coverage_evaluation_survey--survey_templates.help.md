# Tournament Media Coverage Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback on the media coverage of a tournament. It assesses the quality of the coverage, its reach, and overall impact. This form will help gather suggestions for improvement and gather other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Rate the quality of media coverage as "Good" or "Poor".
2. Evaluate if the media coverage reached the desired audience.
3. Provide any improvement ideas you may have.
4. Enter the date of the event (if known).
5. Add any additional comments.
6. Enter names of the media team involved.
7. Enter the name of the event.
8. Enter the dates of the event.
9. Enter contact information for media outlets.
10. Enter the type of event.
11. Enter the name of the event organizer.
12. Enter the name(s) of the media outlet(s).
13. Evaluate if social media coverage was effective.
14. Rate the event overall (Good, Fair).
15. Add any comments.
16. Rate the overall event rating (a number).
17. Enter contact information (phone and email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coverage Quality (2)**: Evaluate the quality of media coverage as either "Good" or "Poor".
* **Audience Reach (3)**: Assess if media coverage reached the desired audience.

* **Media Team (7)**: Enter names of the media team involved.
* **Event Name (8)**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Dates (9)**: Enter the dates of the event.
* **Media Contacts (10)**: Enter contact information for media outlets.
* **Event Type (11)**: Enter the type of event.
* **Event Organizer (12)**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Media Outlets (13)**: Enter the name(s) of the media outlet(s).
* **Social Media (14)**: Evaluate if social media coverage was effective.
* **Rating (15)**: Rate the event overall (Good, Fair).
* **Comments (16)**: Add any comments about the event.
* **Overall Rating (17)**: Rate the overall event rating (a number).
* **Phone (18)**: Enter contact information (phone number).
* **Email (19)**: Enter contact information (email address).
* **Improvement Ideas 1 (20)**: Suggest any ideas for improvement 1.
* **Improvement Ideas 2 (21)**: Suggest any ideas for improvement 2.
* **Improvement Ideas 3 (22)**: Suggest any ideas for improvement 3.
* **Improvement Ideas 4 (23)**: Suggest any ideas for improvement 4.
* **Improvement Ideas 5 (24)**: Suggest any ideas for improvement 5.

## Tips
Please be as specific as possible when providing improvement ideas. If you have any additional information, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
