# Property Optimization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Optimization Survey is designed to gather valuable insights from property managers on their experiences and skills gained through optimization efforts. Your feedback will contribute to enhancing property management performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question based on your actual experience as a property manager.
2. If you're unsure about any question, use your best judgment to provide an honest response.
3. Ensure that you answer all questions truthfully, as your feedback will help us improve property management.
4. If you've already submitted this survey, do not submit again to avoid duplicate entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is property optimization?** (`what_is_property_optimization`, text, required/optional): This question asks you to describe your understanding of property optimization, which is the process of identifying and implementing improvements to enhance property performance. Please provide your honest thoughts on what optimization means to you in your role as a property manager.
* **How long have you managed properties?** (`manager_experience`, number, required/optional): This question is about your professional experience as a property manager. If you're not sure about the exact amount of time, use your best estimation.
* **What skills have you developed as a property manager?** (`manager_performance`, text, required/optional): In this section, please describe the skills you've gained through your experience with property optimization. Examples might include communication, problem-solving, or leadership skills. Be as specific as possible with your answers.
