# Digital Vs Print Reader Preferences Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This questionnaire aims to gather information about your reading preferences, focusing on the differences between digital and print media. Your responses will help us better understand your habits and preferences when it comes to reading.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please carefully review each question and select the most accurate response based on your personal preferences. Make sure to fill in all the required fields. If a question is not applicable to you, select "Not Applicable" or "None of the Above" if provided as an option.
