# restaurant_reopening_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from restaurants that have reopened after the COVID-19 pandemic, to ensure public health measures are in place and customer service meets expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Enter the restaurant's location in the `restaurant_location` field.
3. Enter the restaurant's name in the `restaurant_name` field.
4. For the `reopening_date`, enter the date the restaurant reopened or will reopen.
5. Indicate the safety measures your restaurant is taking to prevent the spread of COVID-19 by selecting all that apply in the `safety_measures` field.
6. Indicate the types of employee training your restaurant has undergone by selecting all that apply in the `employee_training` field.
7. Provide feedback about your customers' experience in the `customer_feedback` field.
8. Indicate your restaurant's approach to customer empathy by selecting all that apply in the `customer_empathy` field.
9. Indicate your restaurant's capacity in the `restaurant_capacity` field.
10. Indicate the number of customers your restaurant can accommodate in the `customer_capacity` field.
11. Enter the contact person's name in the `contact_person` field.
12. Enter the contact person's phone number in the `contact_phone` field.
13. Enter the contact person's email in the `contact_email` field.
14. Enter your restaurant's website in the `website` field.
15. Enter any social media links for your restaurant in the `social_media_links` field.
16. Indicate whether your restaurant offers online ordering options by selecting all that apply in the `online_ordering` field.
17. Indicate any delivery or pickup options by selecting all that apply in the `delivery_info` field.
18. Indicate any payment methods your restaurant accepts by selecting all that apply in the `payment_method` field.
19. Indicate whether your restaurant takes reservations or not by selecting all that apply in the `reservation` field.
20. Indicate whether your restaurant offers gift cards or not by selecting all that apply in the `gift_card` field.
21. Add any additional information or comments about your restaurant's reopening in the `note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **restaurant_location** (`text`, required): Enter the physical location of your restaurant.
* **restaurant_name** (`text`, optional): Enter the full name of your restaurant.
* **reopening_date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date your restaurant reopened or will reopen.
* **safety_measures** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate the safety measures your restaurant is taking to prevent the spread of COVID-19. Examples include:
	+ Sanitizing equipment and surfaces
	+ Encouraging social distancing
	+ Providing personal protective equipment (PPE) to employees
	+ Limiting capacity or implementing social distancing measures
	+ Providing hand sanitizer and handwashing stations
	+ Monitoring employee and customer temperatures
* **employee_training** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate the types of employee training your restaurant has undergone to prepare for reopening. Examples include:
	+ Health and safety training
	+ Customer service training
	+ Food safety and handling training
	+ COVID-19 prevention and control training
* **customer_feedback** (`text`, optional): Provide feedback about your customers' experience during the reopening process.
* **customer_empathy** (`time`, optional): Indicate your restaurant's approach to customer empathy, such as providing extra time or accommodations for customers.
* **restaurant_capacity** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate your restaurant's capacity during the reopening period. Examples include:
	+ Limited capacity due to social distancing measures
	+ Reduced hours of operation
	+ Limited services or offerings
* **customer_capacity** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate the maximum number of customers your restaurant can accommodate during the reopening period.
* **contact_person** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person for your restaurant.
* **contact_phone** (`text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **contact_email** (`email`, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **website** (`text`, optional): Enter the URL of your restaurant's website.
* **social_media_links** (`text`, optional): Enter any social media links for your restaurant.
* **online_ordering** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether your restaurant offers online ordering options. Examples include:
	+ Online ordering through your website
	+ Third-party delivery apps or platforms
	+ Phone or email ordering
* **delivery_info** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate any delivery options your restaurant offers. Examples include:
	+ Third-party delivery services
	+ In-house delivery services
	+ Curbside pickup
* **pickup_info** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate any pickup options your restaurant offers. Examples include:
	+ Curbside pickup
	+ In-store pickup
	+ Self-service or grab-and-go options
* **payment_method** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate any payment methods your restaurant accepts. Examples include:
	+ Credit cards
	+ Cash
	+ Online payment platforms
* **reservation** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether your restaurant takes reservations. Examples include:
	+ Online reservations
	+ Phone reservations
	+ In-person reservations
* **gift_card** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether your restaurant offers gift cards. Examples include:
	+ Digital gift cards
	+ Physical gift cards
	+ Online gift cards
* **note** (`note`, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your restaurant's reopening.
