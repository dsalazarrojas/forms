# AI Technique Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Technique Assessment is a self-assessment form that evaluates your current knowledge and experience in various AI techniques. It consists of multiple sections that cover different aspects of AI, including participant details, knowledge assessment, practical application, and self-evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Select the correct options for each question, and be sure to select all applicable options for multiple-choice questions.
3. Answer any text questions as concisely as possible while providing relevant information.
4. Be honest with your self-assessment of your understanding of AI concepts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Full Name** (`Full Name`, text, required): This is the field where you'll enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
- **Current AI Proficiency Level** (`Current AI Proficiency Level`, select_one, required): Self-assess your current knowledge of AI techniques. Choose from the following options:
	* Expert (Research/Architect level)
	* Advanced (Active Developer)
	* Intermediate (Knowledgeable but limited practice)
	* Beginner (Learning the basics)
	* None (No prior exposure)
- **Which AI techniques are you familiar with?** (`Which AI techniques are you familiar with?`, select_multiple, required): Select all the AI techniques you are familiar with from the given options:
	* Supervised Learning (Regression, Classification)
	* Unsupervised Learning (Clustering, PCA)
	* Reinforcement Learning
	* Deep Learning (Neural Networks)
	* Natural Language Processing (NLP)
	* Computer Vision
	* Generative AI / Large Language Models
- **Most Challenging Technique** (`Most Challenging Technique`, select_one, required): Choose the AI technique you find most challenging to learn and implement.
	* Supervised Learning
	* Unsupervised Learning
	* Reinforcement Learning
	* Deep Learning
	* NLP
	* Computer Vision
	* Generative AI
- **Have you implemented an AI model in a production environment?** (`Have you implemented an AI model in a production environment?`, select_one, required): Answer whether you have implemented an AI model in a production environment.
	* Yes
	* No
- **Tools & Frameworks used** (`Tools & Frameworks used`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the tools and frameworks you have used for AI development:
	* PyTorch
	* TensorFlow / Keras
	* Scikit-learn
	* HuggingFace Transformers
	* OpenAI / Anthropic APIs
	* LangChain / LlamaIndex
	* Pandas / NumPy
- **Describe a specific AI project or experiment you have worked on** (`Describe a specific AI project or experiment you have worked on`, text, optional): If you have worked on an AI project, describe it briefly. If not, enter "N/A".
- **How relevant was this assessment to your learning goals?** (`How relevant was this assessment to your learning goals?`, select_one, required): Rate the relevance of the assessment to your learning goals.
	* 1 = Irrelevant
	* 2 = Somewhat Relevant
	* 3 = Neutral
	* 4 = Somewhat Relevant
	* 5 = Highly Relevant
- **Clarity of the technical topics/questions provided** (`Clarity of the technical topics/questions provided`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of the technical topics and questions provided.
	* Very Clear
	* Clear
	* Neutral
	* Confusing
	* Very Confusing
- **Topics you would like to cover in more depth** (`Topics you would like to cover in more depth`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to 3 topics you would like to cover in more depth:
	* Advanced LLM Architectures
	* Prompt Engineering & RAG
	* Model Deployment & MLOps
	* Data Preprocessing & Feature Engineering
	* AI Ethics, Bias & Governance
	* Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)
- **Self-rated score on understanding Neural Networks** (`Self-rated score on understanding Neural Networks`, number, required): Rate your understanding of Neural Networks on a scale of 0-100.
- **Self-rated score on understanding Transformer architecture** (`Self-rated score on understanding Transformer architecture`, number, required): Rate your understanding of Transformer architecture on a scale of 0-100.
- **Primary goal for learning AI techniques** (`Primary goal for learning AI techniques`, text, required): State your primary goal for learning AI techniques.
- **Would you like to receive advanced training materials?** (`Would you like to receive advanced training materials?`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would like to receive advanced training materials.
- **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, optional): Enter your email address for materials delivery and future updates.
