# Site And Equipment Management Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about site and equipment management practices, which will help the team make informed decisions about maintenance, safety, and other relevant aspects. Completing this form will provide valuable insights into your site's equipment condition, maintenance frequency, and safety measures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the correct page (there are 3 pages in total).
2. Read each field carefully and fill in the required fields (indicated by *).
3. Use the provided options to select relevant answers for fields with dropdown or select options.
4. Provide detailed answers for text fields.
5. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site and Equipment Management Survey** (Text): This is the title of the form. Please do not enter any information in this field.
* **Maintenance and Inspection** (Select Multiple):
	+ Select all practices that apply to your site (e.g., Maintenance and inspection, Repairs and replacement, Other).
* **Safety Practices** (Select One):
	+ Choose one of the provided options (e.g., Regularly scheduled inspections, Spontaneous inspections, No answer).
* **Equipment Condition** (Select One):
	+ Choose one of the provided options (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Maintenance Frequency** (Text): Describe how often you perform maintenance on your site's equipment.
* **Maintenance Last Done** (Date): Enter the date of the last maintenance done on your site's equipment.
* **Safety Last Done** (Date): Enter the date of the last safety check done on your site's equipment.
* **Maintenance Cost** (Number): Enter the cost of maintenance for your site's equipment.
* **Safety Cost** (Number): Enter the cost of safety measures for your site's equipment.
* **Site Location** (Select One):
	+ Choose one of the provided options (e.g., New York, London, Paris, Other).
* **Team Name** (Text): Enter the name of your team responsible for site and equipment management.
* **Team Size** (Text): Describe the size of your team.
* **Department** (Select One):
	+ Choose one of the provided options (e.g., Maintenance, Operations, Other).
* **Contact Person** (Text): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for site and equipment management.
* **Contact Number** (Text): Enter the contact number of the person responsible for site and equipment management.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the person responsible for site and equipment management.
* **Comments** (Note): Provide any additional comments or notes about your site and equipment management practices.
* **Page 3** (Note): This is an additional comments field for any other information you want to share.

## Tips

* Be accurate when entering dates and numbers.
* Use the provided options for dropdown and select fields.
* Provide detailed answers for text fields.
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
