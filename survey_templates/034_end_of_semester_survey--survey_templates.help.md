# End Of Semester Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The End Of Semester Survey form is used to collect feedback from students at the end of each semester. This form is designed to gather information about the student's experience, satisfaction, and any challenges they faced during the semester.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing detailed and honest feedback.
3. For fields with date formats (Semester Start Date and Semester End Date), use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, optional): This is the main feedback section where you can share your thoughts, suggestions, or complaints about your experience during the semester. Please be as detailed as possible to help us improve our services.
* **Institution** (`institution_id`, number, optional): This field is used to identify the institution you are affiliated with. If you are a student, please enter your institution's ID number. If you are not a student, you can leave this field blank.
* **Semester Start Date** (`semester_start_date`, date, optional): This field is used to record the start date of the semester. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Semester End Date** (`semester_end_date`, date, optional): This field is used to record the end date of the semester. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): This field is used to identify the name of the course you are enrolled in. If you are not a student, you can leave this field blank.
