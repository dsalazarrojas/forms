# Candy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Candy Survey is designed to collect information about participants' preferences and habits regarding candy. This form will help the candy industry understand their target audience and gather valuable insights to improve their products and marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your participant information, including age and gender.
2. Select the most accurate age group that you belong to.
3. Choose your preferred gender identity.
4. Enter your country of residence.
5. Select how often you eat candy, and specify the time of day you usually eat it.
6. Indicate the main reason you eat candy.
7. Select up to three favorite types of candy.
8. Choose your favorite candy brand.
9. Rate the importance of sugar-free options for you.
10. Indicate where you usually buy candy.
11. Specify your average monthly spend on candy.
12. Rate the influence of packaging on your candy choice.
13. Indicate whether you've tried any new candy brands recently.
14. Express your interest in exotic or international candies.
15. If you have a favorite candy flavor, share it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: This section is where you provide your basic information, including age and gender identity.
* **Age Group**: Select the most accurate age group that you belong to.
* **Gender**: Choose your preferred gender identity.
* **Nationality**: Enter your country of residence.
* **Age Group**: Select how often you eat candy, and specify the time of day you usually eat it.
* **How Often Do You Eat Candy**: Choose how often you eat candy, from Daily, Weekly, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **Time Of Day**: Select the time of day you usually eat candy, from Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, or Any Time.
* **Main Reason For Eating Candy**: Indicate the main reason you eat candy, from Snack, Reward, Stress, Social Event, or Other.
* **Top 3 Favorite Types Of Candy**: Select up to three favorite types of candy from the list provided.
* **Favorite Candy Brand**: Choose your favorite candy brand.
* **Importance Of Sugar-Free Options**: Rate the importance of sugar-free options for you, from Not Important, Somewhat Important, or Very Important.
* **Where Do You Usually Buy Candy**: Indicate where you usually buy candy, from Supermarket, Convenience Store, Online, Specialty Shop, or Vending Machine.
* **Average Monthly Spend On Candy**: Specify your average monthly spend on candy in local currency.
* **Influence Of Packaging**: Rate the influence of packaging on your candy choice, from Low, Medium, or High.
* **Have You Tried Any New Candy Brands Recently**: Indicate whether you've tried any new candy brands recently.
* **Interest In Exotic Or International Candies**: Express your interest in exotic or international candies, from True or False.
* **Favorite Candy Flavor**: Share your favorite candy flavor, if you have one.
