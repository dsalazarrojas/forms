# Digital Collaboration Tool Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with a digital collaboration tool. The information collected will help the tool's developers understand how users rate their experience, which features they use most frequently, and what areas of improvement are needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your answer to "What is the name of the digital collaboration tool?" in the corresponding field.
2. Rate your experience with the tool by choosing a number from 1 to 5 in the "How would you rate your experience with the tool?" field.
3. Select the features of the collaboration tool that you use most frequently from the dropdown list.
4. Rate the features of the tool by choosing a number from 1 to 5 in the "How would you rate the features of the tool?" field.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the tool by choosing a number from 1 to 5 in the "How satisfied are you with the tool?" field.
6. Enter any areas of improvement for the tool in the "What areas of improvement for the tool do you think are most important?" field.
7. Enter any additional comments about the tool in the "Do you have any additional comments about the collaboration tool?" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the name of the digital collaboration tool?**: This field is where you enter the name of the digital collaboration tool you are evaluating.
* **How would you rate your experience with the tool?**: This field asks you to rate your experience with the tool on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What features of the collaboration tool do you use most frequently?**: This field is a dropdown list of features offered by the tool. Select all the features that you use most frequently.
* **How would you rate the features of the tool?**: This field asks you to rate the features of the tool on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **How satisfied are you with the tool?**: This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with the tool on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What areas of improvement for the tool do you think are most important?**: This field is where you can enter any areas of improvement that you think are most important.
* **Do you have any additional comments about the collaboration tool?**: This field is where you can enter any additional comments or feedback about the tool.
* **Email**: This field is where you can enter your email address.
* **Phone**: This field is where you can enter your phone number.
