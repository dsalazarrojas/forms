# Legal AI Consultation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal AI Consultation Feedback Form is a tool used to gather feedback from clients after a legal AI consultation. This feedback is essential for us to refine and improve our legal AI services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the client's name from the provided options.
2. Enter the client's email address.
3. Optionally, provide a confirmation of the email address.
4. Enter the date and time of the consultation.
5. Provide more details about the consultation.
6. Click "Submit" to send the feedback form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name**: Select the name of the client from the provided options.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address. This is the email address we will use to contact the client.
* **Email (confirmation)**: This field is optional and should be used to confirm the email address you entered. This will help us ensure that we have the correct email address.
* **Phone**: This field is not required, but it's a good idea to provide your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the consultation. This is in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the consultation. This is in the format 24-hour time (e.g., 14:30).
* **Consultation details**: Provide more details about the consultation, such as what was discussed and any issues that came up.
* **Additional details**: This field is not required, but if you have any additional details or comments, please feel free to add them.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send the feedback form to us.
 
## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible in the "Consultation details" field.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact us directly.
* We value your feedback and appreciate your time.
