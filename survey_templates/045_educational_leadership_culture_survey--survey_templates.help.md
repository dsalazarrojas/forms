# Educational Leadership Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Leadership Culture Survey is designed to assess the leadership culture within a school or district.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the school in the `school_name` field.
2. Enter the address of the school in the `school_address` field.
3. Answer the questions regarding the leadership and support provided by the school or district in the relevant fields.
4. Select the appropriate options for each question from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter any additional comments or information in the `additional_comments` field if needed.
6. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) if you want to receive feedback on your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Information
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school or district being surveyed.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the school or district being surveyed.
### Leadership and Support
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Do you have a department within your school? (Yes/No)
* **Leader Name** (`leader_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school or district leader.
* **Leader Title** (`leader_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the school or district leader.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, required): Enter the number of years the school or district leader has been in service.
* **Feedback on Leadership** (`feedback_on_leadership`, text, required): Provide feedback on the leadership provided by the school or district.
* **Feedback on School Culture** (`feedback_on_school_culture`, text, required): Provide feedback on the school culture.
* **District** (`district`, select_one, required): Is the school within a district? (Yes/No)
* **School District** (`school_district`, select_one, required): Is the school part of a school district? (Yes/No)
* **School Region** (`school_region`, select_one, required): Is the school part of a region? (Yes/No)
* **Teacher Leadership** (`teacher_leadership`, select_one, required): Does the school have teacher leadership? (Yes/No)
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, select_one, required): Does the school provide teacher support? (Yes/No)
* **Parent Leadership** (`parent_leadership`, select_one, required): Does the school have parent leadership? (Yes/No)
* **Parent Support** (`parent_support`, select_one, required): Does the school provide parent support? (Yes/No)
* **Staff Leadership** (`staff_leadership`, select_one, required): Does the school have staff leadership? (Yes/No)
* **Staff Support** (`staff_support`, select_one, required): Does the school provide staff support? (Yes/No)
* **Leadership Climate** (`leadership_climate`, select_one, required): What is the leadership climate within the school? (Yes/No)
### Additional Information
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the school or district.
* **Survey Taker Information** (`survey_taker_info`, text, required): Provide information about yourself as a survey taker.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive feedback on your responses.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive feedback on your responses.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the survey.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Enter the time you completed the survey.
