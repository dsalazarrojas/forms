# Payroll System Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Payroll System Performance Survey" is used to evaluate the effectiveness of the Payroll System and gather feedback from employees to inform improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the employee information:
   - Enter your employee name under "employee_name".
   - Select your department under "department".
   - Enter your job title under "Job Title".
   - Enter your location under "Location".

2. Rate the performance of the Payroll System in the following areas:
   - Select a rating from 1 to 5 for each "Rating 1" to "Rating 5" under the corresponding fields.

3. Calculate your total score under "Total Rating" by summing up the ratings you provided for "Rating 1" to "Rating 5".

4. Optionally, provide any comments about your experience with the Payroll System under "comments".

5. Review the following pages:
   - Select "Yes" or "No" for each page under "Page 2" to "Page 10". These questions pertain to specific features of the Payroll System.

6. Provide your email under "Email" for contact purposes.

7. Click "Submit" to submit your survey.
