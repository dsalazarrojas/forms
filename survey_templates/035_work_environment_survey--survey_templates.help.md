# work_environment_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on your work environment, team dynamics, and personal satisfaction. It helps us understand your experiences and opinions to improve your work life.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select "Yes" or "No" for questions about team members, communication, collaboration, and job urgency.
2. Answer the following questions with a brief description:
	* Work environment (text)
	* Job urgency (text)
	* Team leadership (text)
	* Workload balance (select multiple from "Yes" or "No")
3. Select one of the following options for the following questions:
	* Satisfaction (select one from "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied")
	* Work-life balance (select one from "Yes" or "No")
4. Fill out the form accurately and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team members**: Select all the team members you work with. This helps us understand who you interact with on a daily basis.
* **Work environment**: Describe your current work environment. For example, what are your physical surroundings like? Do you have a comfortable workspace?
* **Satisfaction**: Select "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied" to express your overall satisfaction with your job. This helps us understand your emotional state.
* **Communication**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have good communication with your colleagues.
* **Collaboration**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you enjoy collaborating with your team members.
* **Job urgency**: Describe the level of urgency in your job. For example, is it fast-paced or relaxed?
* **Team leadership**: Describe your experience with your team leaders. For example, are they supportive or not?
* **Workload balance**: Select all the "Yes" or "No" options that apply to you. For example, are your tasks manageable or overwhelming?
* **Job satisfaction**: Select all the "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied" options that apply to you. For example, do you enjoy your job or not?
* **Work-life balance**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you feel your work and personal life are balanced or not.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* If you are unsure about a question, select "No" or a neutral option.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please include them in the text boxes.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
