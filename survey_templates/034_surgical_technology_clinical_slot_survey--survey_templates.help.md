# Surgical Technology Clinical Slot Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surgical Technology Clinical Slot Survey is a tool used to gather information about clinical slots and their corresponding preferences and requirements. It is designed to be filled out by students and other stakeholders involved in clinical rotations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of each field.
2. Fill out all required fields, as indicated by the "required" field designation.
3. Select options for select fields carefully, as they will be used to filter and display results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Student Preferences
**Student Preferences**: (`Student Preferences`, `text`, `optional`) This field is a free-form text box where you can describe your preferences for clinical slots.

### Page 2: Clinical Rotations
**Clinical Rotations**: (`Clinical Rotations`, `select_one`, `optional`) Select one of the following options to indicate your preference for a clinical rotation:
*   **Cardiothoracic Surgery: Cardiac & General Thoracic Surgery**
*   **ENT Surgery: Ear, Nose & Throat**
*   **General Surgery: General Surgery**
*   **Neuroscience: Neurosurgery**
*   **Orthopedic Surgery: Orthopedic Surgery**
*   **Plastics and Reconstructive Surgery: Plastics & Reconstructive Surgery**
*   **Surgical Oncology: Surgical Oncology**

### Page 3: Availability
**Availability**: (`Availability`, `date`, `optional`) Please select the dates of your availability in 24-hour time format (e.g., 08:00).

### Page 4: Clinical Slot Preferences
**Clinical Slot Preferences**: (`Clinical Slot Preferences`, `select_multiple`, `optional`) Select all that apply:
*   **Yes**
*   **No**

### Page 5: Rotation Length
**Rotation Length**: (`Rotation Length`, `number`, `optional`) Enter the length of your desired rotation in hours.

### Page 6: Clinical Slot Length
**Clinical Slot Length**: (`Clinical Slot Length`, `number`, `optional`) Enter the length of your desired clinical slot in hours.

### Page 7: Rotation Start Date
**Rotation Start Date**: (`Rotation Start Date`, `date`, `optional`) Select the start date of your desired rotation.

### Page 8: Rotation End Date
**Rotation End Date**: (`Rotation End Date`, `date`, `optional`) Select the end date of your desired rotation.

### Page 9: Clinical Slot Start Time
**Clinical Slot Start Time**: (`Clinical Slot Start Time`, `time`, `optional`) Enter the start time of your desired clinical slot in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).

### Page 10: Clinical Slot End Time
**Clinical Slot End Time**: (`Clinical Slot End Time`, `time`, `optional`) Enter the end time of your desired clinical slot in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00).

### Page 11: Student Notes
**Student Notes**: (`Student Notes`, `note`, `optional`) Enter any additional notes about your preferences and requirements.

### Page 12: Program Name
**Program Name**: (`Program Name`, `select_one`, `optional`) Select the name of the program you are participating in. 
*   **Name1**
*   **Name2**
*   **Name3**
