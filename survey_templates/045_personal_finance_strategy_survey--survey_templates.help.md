# Personal Finance Strategy Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your current financial situation, goals, and strategies to help you create a comprehensive financial plan tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly, as the information will be used to provide you with accurate and helpful financial advice.
2. You can choose only one option for select_one fields (e.g., "Income from employment", "Income from investments", etc.).
3. For select_multiple fields, select all applicable options that apply to you (e.g., "Bank", "Credit Union", etc.).
4. Enter your answers in the format specified for each field type (e.g., date for "Financial Obligations").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Strategy** (`survey_strategy`, text, optional): 
  This field is a text box where you can briefly describe your current financial strategy or approach.
* **Current Financial Status** (`current_financial_status`, date, required): 
  Please enter your current financial status (e.g., "Employed", "Unemployed", etc.).
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, number, optional): 
  This field measures your willingness to take risks while investing your money (e.g., "Low", "Medium", "High").
* **Financial Goal** (`financial_goal`, text, optional): 
  Describe your financial goals, such as saving for a specific purpose or achieving a particular financial milestone.
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, select_one, required): 
  Select the source of your income (e.g., "Income from employment", "Income from investments", etc.).
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, select_multiple, optional): 
  Select all financial institutions you are associated with (e.g., "Bank", "Credit Union", "Insurance Company", etc.).
* **Current Assets** (`current_assets`, number, optional): 
  Enter your current asset value.
* **Debt Belongings** (`debt_belongings`, text, optional): 
  Describe any debts you may have.
* **Financial Obligations** (`financial_obligations`, date, optional): 
  Enter any upcoming financial obligations (e.g., bills, taxes, etc.).
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, select_one, required): 
  Select the timeframe for your financial goals (e.g., "Short-term goals (less than 1 year)", "Medium-term goals (1-5 years)", etc.).
* **Financial Institute** (`financial_institute`, select_multiple, optional): 
  Select all financial institutions you are associated with (e.g., "Bank", "Credit Union", "Insurance Company", etc.).
* **Financial Planning** (`financial_planning`, select_one, required):

* **Financial Planning Method** (`financial_planning_method`, text, optional):

* **Financial Institutions 2** (`financial_institutions_2`, select_multiple, optional):

* **Financial Objectives** (`financial_objectives`, text, optional):

## Tips

* Please answer this form honestly and accurately to help you create a comprehensive financial plan tailored to your needs.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact your financial advisor or planner.
* This form is meant to be a general-purpose tool and may not be applicable to your specific situation. Please review the answers carefully and discuss with your financial advisor if needed.
