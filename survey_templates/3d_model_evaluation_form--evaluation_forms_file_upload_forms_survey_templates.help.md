# 3D Model Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 3D Model Evaluation Form is a structured and systematic guide that helps assess the quality and performance of 3D models. This form ensures that evaluators provide consistent and comprehensive feedback, which is essential for future model development and enhancement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as the evaluator in the "Full name" field (Field 2).
2. Provide your email address for contact information in the "Email address" field (Field 3).
3. Select the project name that this 3D model belongs to from the dropdown list in the "Project name" field (Field 5).
4. Enter the specific name or identifier of the 3D model being evaluated in the "3D Model name" field (Field 6).
5. For "Model version", you can provide the version number or iteration of the model, if available (Field 7).
6. Evaluate the quality of the 3D model using the provided scale for "Geometry accuracy" (Field 9), "Topology quality" (Field 10), "Polygon count efficiency" (Field 11), "Texture resolution appropriateness" (Field 12), and "UV mapping quality" (Field 13).
7. Provide the average render time in seconds for "Average render time" (Field 15), and the total number of triangles and texture size in megabytes for "Polygon count" (Field 16) and "Texture size" (Field 17), if available.
8. Select the primary platform where the model will be used from the dropdown list in the "Target platform" field (Field 18).
9. Rate the overall performance of the 3D model using "Overall rating" (Field 20), and recommend a course of action using "Recommended action" (Field 21).
10. Provide any detailed feedback or suggestions for improvement in the "Detailed feedback" field (Field 23), and any suggested improvements or recommendations in the "Suggested improvements" field (Field 24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name (Field 2)** (`Evaluator name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as the evaluator, which helps identify who is providing the feedback.
- **Email address (Field 3)** (`Email address`, `email`, required/optional): This field is for contact information, allowing for follow-up questions or discussions.
- **Project name (Field 5)** (`Project name`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name of the project this 3D model belongs to, helping to contextualize the evaluation.
- **3D Model name (Field 6)** (`Model name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the specific name or identifier of the 3D model being evaluated.
- **Model version (Field 7)** (`Model version`, `text`, required/optional): If available, enter the version number or iteration of the model.
- **Geometry accuracy (Field 9)** (`Geometry accuracy rating`, `select_one`, required/optional): Evaluate the model's geometric accuracy on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
- **Topology quality (Field 10)** (`Topology quality`, `select_one`, required/optional): Assess the quality of the model's mesh structure and edge flow on the same scale.
- **Polygon count efficiency (Field 11)** (`Polygon count efficiency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Determine if the polygon density is suitable for the intended use on the same scale.
- **Texture resolution appropriateness (Field 12)** (`Texture resolution appropriateness`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate the texture resolution's suitability for the model on the same scale.
- **UV mapping quality (Field 13)** (`UV mapping quality`, `select_one`, required/optional): Evaluate the model's UV mapping quality on the same scale.
- **Average render time (Field 15)** (`Average render time`, `decimal`, required/optional): Estimate the average time taken to render a typical frame in seconds.
- **Polygon count (Field 16)** (`Polygon count`, `integer`, required/optional): Enter the total number of triangles in the model.
- **Texture size (Field 17)** (`Texture size`, `decimal`, required/optional): Provide the combined size of all textures in megabytes.
- **Target platform (Field 18)** (`Target platform`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the platform where the model will be primarily used from the given options.
- **Overall rating (Field 20)** (`Overall rating`, `integer`, required/optional): Rate the 3D model's performance on a scale from 1 to 10, with 1 being the worst and 10 being the best.
- **Recommended action (Field 21)** (`Recommended action`, `select_one`, required/optional): Suggest a course of action for the 3D model, such as "Approve as-is" or "Revise and resubmit".
- **Detailed feedback (Field 23)** (`Detailed feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any specific comments or observations about the model.
- **Suggested improvements (Field 24)** (`Suggested improvements`, `text`, required/optional): Make recommendations for enhancing the model.
- **Additional comments (Field 25)** (`Additional comments`, `text`, required/optional): Leave any additional comments or remarks about the model.

## Tips

- This form is designed to be completed in a clear and concise manner, ensuring that evaluators provide consistent and comprehensive feedback.
- Please rate the fields according to your expertise and experience with 3D models.
- If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, please consult with your colleagues or experts in the field.
- Remember to provide detailed feedback and suggestions for improvement to help enhance the 3D model.
