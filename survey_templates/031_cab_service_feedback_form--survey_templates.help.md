# Cab Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from passengers about their recent cab service experience. Your feedback will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the date of the ride in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
2. If you remember the driver's name or ID, please enter it in the "Driver Name or ID" field.
3. Describe the pickup and drop-off locations for the ride.
4. Answer the driver evaluation questions, rating the driver's professionalism and courtesy, safety, and overall performance.
5. Evaluate the efficiency of the chosen route and the overall cleanliness of the vehicle.
6. Rate the comfort of the cabin and the temperature inside the vehicle.
7. Answer the overall experience questions, including ease of booking, value for money, and likelihood to recommend.
8. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
9. Finally, indicate whether you would like to be contacted regarding this feedback and provide your email address if you would like to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ride Details**
	+ **Date of Ride**: Enter the date of the ride in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Driver Name or ID**: If you remember the driver's name or ID, enter it here.
* **Pickup Location**: Describe the location where the ride started.
* **Drop-off Location**: Describe the location where the ride ended.
* **Driver Professionalism and Courtesy**: Rate the driver's professionalism and courtesy on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Did you feel safe during the ride?**: Select one of the options: Very Safe, Safe, Somewhat Unsafe, or Very Unsafe.
* **Efficiency of the chosen route**: Select one of the options: Efficient, Reasonable, or Long/Poor Choice.
* **Overall Vehicle Cleanliness**: Select one of the options: Spotless, Clean, Average, or Needs Improvement.
* **Cabin Comfort and Temperature**: Select one of the options: Comfortable, Neutral, or Uncomfortable.
* **Ease of Booking Process**: Select one of the options: Very Easy, Easy, Neutral, or Difficult.
* **Value for Money**: Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How likely are you to recommend our service to others?**: Select one of the options: Definitely, Probably, Not Sure, or False.
* **Any additional feedback or comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **May we contact you regarding this feedback?**: Select one of the options: True or False.
* **Email Address for follow-up**: If you would like to be contacted regarding this feedback, enter your email address.
