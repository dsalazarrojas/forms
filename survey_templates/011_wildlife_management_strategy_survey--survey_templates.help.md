# Wildlife Management Strategy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through how to complete the Wildlife Management Strategy Survey form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions in the order they appear on the form.
2. For questions that ask for a specific response (e.g., select one option), choose the option that best represents your opinion or experience.
3. For questions that ask for text responses, provide as much detail as possible in the space provided.
4. If you have trouble finding the correct answers, you can refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`1`, text, required): This is the first question on the form, and it asks for your name. Please provide your name as you would like to be contacted.
* **What is the most critical conservation issue in your area?** (`2`, select_one, required): This question asks for your opinion on the most critical conservation issue in your area. Please choose one of the provided options (habitat loss, pollution, overhunting, climate change, or other) that you think best reflects the current situation.
* **How do you think we can best address this issue?** (`3`, text, required): After choosing the most critical conservation issue, this question asks you to suggest a way to address it. Please provide your thoughts on how this issue can be best addressed.
* **What do you think is the primary goal of wildlife management?** (`4`, select_one, required): This question asks for your opinion on the primary goal of wildlife management. Please choose one of the provided options (protecting wildlife, managing wildlife populations, balancing human wildlife interactions, or other) that best reflects your view.
* **Do you think wildlife management policies are effective?** (`5`, select_multiple, required): This question asks for your opinion on the effectiveness of wildlife management policies. You can choose multiple options (True, False, or undecided) that best reflect your views.
* **How do you think stakeholders should be involved in wildlife management decision-making?** (`6`, select_multiple, required): This question asks for your opinion on the involvement of stakeholders in wildlife management decision-making. You can choose multiple options (local communities, conservationists, government agencies, researchers, or other) that best reflect your views.
* **What role do you think you play in wildlife management decision-making?** (`7`, select_one, required): This question asks for your self-identification in the decision-making process. Please choose the option (leader, advisor, participant, or other) that best reflects your role.
* **How can we reach you for follow-up questions?** (`8`, email, required): This question asks for your email address so we can reach out to you for follow-up questions.
* **What is your phone number (or leave blank if you don't want to be contacted)?** (`9`, number, required): This question asks for your phone number, but feel free to leave it blank if you don't want to be contacted.
* **What is your location (e.g., country, region, or city)?** (`10`, text, required): This question asks for your location so we can tailor our follow-up questions to your specific context.
* **Do you have any additional comments or concerns about wildlife management?** (`11`, text, optional): This question is an open-ended space for you to share any additional thoughts or concerns you have.
* **Is there anything else you would like to share with us?** (`12`, text, optional): This question is also an open-ended space for you to share any other thoughts or comments.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions as thoroughly as possible, as your input is valuable to our study.
* You can skip any questions that don't apply to you or your experience.
* Feel free to add any additional comments or concerns you have, even if they are not listed here.
