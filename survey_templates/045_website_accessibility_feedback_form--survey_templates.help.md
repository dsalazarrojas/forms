# Website Accessibility Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The website accessibility feedback form is a tool for users to share their experiences and help us improve the website's accessibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the first question about your experience with our website. This will help us understand your perspective.
2. Provide the URL or anchor of the specific page that caused the accessibility issue, if possible.
3. Select the type of accessibility issue you encountered.
4. Describe the issue you experienced.
5. Share your ideas on how we can solve the issue.
6. Provide your email address, name, and phone number (optional) so we can contact you for further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your experience with our website? (website_accessibility_feedback_form)**: Describe your experience with our website, such as what led you to this form.
* **What page do you have accessibility issues with? (page_title)**: If you can, provide the exact URL or the name of the page that caused the issue.
* **Specific page URL or anchor? (page_anchor)**: If you can share the exact URL or anchor, that's very helpful!
* **What kind of accessibility issue? (issue_type)**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Screen reader issues
	+ Text size too small
	+ Button or input not accessible
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Describe the issue? (issue_description)**: Please share as much detail as possible about the issue you encountered.
* **What could we do to solve the issue? (solutions)**: Share your ideas on how we can make the website more accessible for all users.
* **Your email address (contact_email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Your name (contact_name)**: Please enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Your phone number (contact_phone)**: Provide your phone number to enable us to contact you more easily, if needed.
* **Message (contact_message)**: If you have any additional comments or questions, use this field to share them.
