# Farm Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farm Survey is designed to gather information from farmers and agonomists about their experiences, knowledge, and preferences related to farming practices. This data can help inform decisions and research in the agricultural industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form to understand what information is being asked.
2. For each field, provide accurate and thoughtful responses.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk or a hint).
4. Use the options provided in the multiple-choice fields to select the most accurate response.
5. Double-check your answers for errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Farm Survey
* **Farmers** and **Agronomists** (`farmers_and_agronomists`, select_multiple, required): Select all the categories that apply to you from the provided options. You are a farmer or an agonomist, or both.

### Farm Survey 2
* **Farm Survey 2** (`farm_survey_page_2`, text, required): Please provide a detailed response to this question. You are free to write as much or as little as you like, but make sure to answer the question as accurately as possible.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer honestly and accurately.
