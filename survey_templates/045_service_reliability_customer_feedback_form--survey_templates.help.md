# Service Reliability Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on the reliability of our services. It helps us to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance our service offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form with your honest feedback.
2. Answer each question carefully, as your input is valuable to us.
3. Make sure to provide your email address if you would like us to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service on a scale of 1-10.
* **Service Disruption** (`service_disruption`, text, optional): If you experienced any issues with our service, describe them in detail.
* **Impact** (`impact`, number, required): Estimate the impact of our service disruption on your business or personal activities (1-10).
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have on how we can improve our service.
* **Support Team** (`support_team`, select_one, required): Select the support team that assisted you (IT, SaaS, or Support).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Provide the date you experienced the issue.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Specify the time you experienced the issue.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
