# Community Health Impact Research Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the impact of a community health program. It aims to gather information on the program's effectiveness and sustainability, as well as identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your name and contact information (Optional)**: If you wish to share your name and email address, please fill in the fields "Name" and "Email Address." This will help us contact you if we need to follow up with you.
   - **Name (Optional)**: This field is for your name if you want to be identified.
   - **Email Address (Optional)**: This is your email address for follow-up.
2. **Identify yourself as a stakeholder**: Please select your relationship to the program from the list provided.
   - **Stakeholder Type**: This question determines your connection to the program.
3. **Choose the program you are evaluating**: Select the program you are responding to from the list provided.
   - **Program Name**: This is the name of the program being assessed.
4. **Assess your involvement with the program**: Please select how long you have been involved with the program.
   - **Duration of Involvement**: This determines your level of engagement.
5. **Evaluate the program's effectiveness**: Select your evaluation of the program's overall effectiveness.
   - **Program Effectiveness**: This assesses the program's overall success.
6. **Identify health benefits**: Please select all health benefits you perceive as the result of the program.
   - **Health Impact**: This question identifies the program's health outcomes.
7. **Describe community changes**: Please select all community benefits as a result of the program.
   - **Community Impact**: This assesses the program's community outcomes.
8. **Assess program reach**: Select how well the program met its intended goals.
   - **Program Reach Assessment**: This evaluates the program's reach.
9. **Evaluate resource adequacy**: Select your evaluation of the program's resources.
   - **Resource Adequacy**: This question assesses the sufficiency of the program's resources.
10. **Identify program strengths and weaknesses**: Provide your thoughts on what worked well and what needed improvement.
   - **Program Strengths**: This field allows you to describe the program's positive aspects.
   - **Program Weaknesses**: This field allows you to identify areas for improvement.
11. **Report any unintended consequences**: If you noticed any unexpected outcomes, please describe them.
   - **Unintended Consequences**: This field is for reporting any unexpected outcomes.
12. **Assess program sustainability**: Select your evaluation of the program's sustainability.
   - **Program Sustainability**: This question assesses the program's long-term viability.
13. **Evaluate replicability**: Select how easily the program could be replicated.
   - **Replicability**: This evaluates the program's potential for replication.
14. **Make recommendations for improvement**: Provide any suggestions for the program.
   - **Recommendations for Improvement**: This is for your suggestions for improvement.
15. **Determine future funding**: Select your recommendation for the program's future funding.
   - **Should Program Continue Receiving Funding**: This question evaluates the program's funding future.
16. **Add any additional comments**: If you have any other feedback, please include it here.
   - **Additional Comments**: This field is for any other comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Stakeholder Type** (`stakeholder_type`, select_one, required): This question asks about your relationship to the program.
  Please select the category that best describes your connection to the program.
  - **Program Participant**
  - **Healthcare Provider**
  - **Program Staff**
  - **Community Leader**
  - **Funder**
  - **Researcher**
  - **Family Member**
  - **Other** 
- **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): This is the name of the program you are evaluating.
- **Duration of Involvement** (`involvement_duration`, select_one, required): This question determines how long you have been involved with the program.
  Please select the option that best describes your involvement length.
  - **Less than 6 months**
  - **6-12 months**
  - **1-2 years**
  - **2-5 years**
  - **More than 5 years**
- **Program Effectiveness** (`program_effectiveness`, select_one, required): This question assesses the program's overall effectiveness.
  Please select your evaluation of the program's success.
  - **Very Effective**
  - **Effective**
  - **Somewhat Effective**
  - **Not Very Effective**
  - **Not Effective**
- **Health Impact** (`health_impact`, select_multiple, required): This question identifies the program's health outcomes.
  Please select all health benefits you perceive as the result of the program.
  - **Improved Health Outcomes**
  - **Increased Health Knowledge**
  - **Better Health Behaviors**
  - **Increased Access to Care**
  - **Reduced Health Disparities**
  - **Improved Quality of Life**
  - **No Observable Impact**
- **Community Impact** (`community_impact`, select_multiple, required): This question identifies community outcomes.
  Please select all community changes as a result of the program.
  - **Increased Awareness**
  - **Better Resources**
  - **Stronger Partnerships**
  - **Policy Changes**
  - **Increased Engagement**
  - **Reduced Stigma**
  - **No Observable Impact**
- **Program Reach Assessment** (`reach_assessment`, select_one, required): This question evaluates the program's reach.
  Please select how well the program met its intended goals.
  - **Exceeded Expectations**
  - **Met Expectations**
  - **Partially Met Expectations**
  - **Did Not Meet Expectations**
- **Resource Adequacy** (`resource_adequacy`, select_one, required): This question assesses the program's resources.
  Please select your evaluation of the program's resource sufficiency.
  - **More Than Adequate**
  - **Adequate**
  - **Somewhat Adequate**
  - **Inadequate**
- **Program Strengths** (`program_strengths`, text, required): This field allows you to describe the program's positive aspects.
- **Program Weaknesses** (`program_weaknesses`, text, required): This field allows you to identify areas for improvement.
- **Unintended Consequences** (`unintended_consequences`, text, required): If you noticed any unexpected outcomes, please describe them.
- **Program Sustainability** (`sustainability`, select_one, required): This question assesses the program's long-term viability.
  Please select your evaluation of the program's sustainability.
  - **Very Sustainable**
  - **Sustainable**
  - **Somewhat Sustainable**
  - **Not Sustainable**
- **Replicability** (`replicability`, select_one, required): This question evaluates the program's potential for replication.
  Please select how easily the program could be replicated.
  - **Easily Replicable**
  - **Replicable with Modifications**
  - **Difficult to Replicate**
  - **Not Replicable**
- **Should Program Continue Receiving Funding** (`future_funding`, select_one, required): This question evaluates the program's funding future.
  Please select your recommendation for the program's funding future.
  - **Yes, Increase Funding**
  - **Yes, Maintain Funding**
  - **Yes, Reduce Funding**
  - **No, Discontinue Funding**
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): This field is for any other comments you have.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback is crucial for the program's development and future improvements.
- If you are unsure about any question, please choose the option that best describes your situation.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
