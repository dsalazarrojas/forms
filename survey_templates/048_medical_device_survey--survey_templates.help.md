## Medical Device Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and data about a medical device's usability, performance, and safety. The survey aims to gather valuable insights to improve the device's quality and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the medical device in the "Medical Device Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the device in the "Device Description" field.
3. Evaluate the device's performance by selecting from the "Device Performance" dropdown menu.
4. Choose the level of usability for the device by selecting from the "Device Usability" multiple-choice menu.
5. Optionally, provide any additional comments about the device's safety in the "Device Safety" field.
6. Enter the compliance date for the device (if applicable).
7. Once you have filled out all the fields, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Device Name** (`medical_device_survey_device_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the medical device being surveyed.
* **Device Description** (`medical_device_survey_device_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the device, including its purpose and features.
* **Device Performance** (`medical_device_survey_device_performance`, select_one, optional): Rate the performance of the device as Good, Average, or Bad.
* **Device Usability** (`medical_device_survey_device_usability`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of usability for the device as Easy to use, Average usability, or Hard to use.
* **Device Safety** (`medical_device_survey_device_safety`, text, optional): Enter any comments or concerns about the device's safety features or any issues you've encountered.
* **Device Compliance** (`medical_device_survey_device_compliance`, date, optional): Enter the date when the device was last inspected or verified for compliance. 

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out the required fields (Device Name and Device Description).
- If you're unsure about the performance or usability of the device, please rate it as Average.
- Use the comments field to provide any additional feedback or comments about the device's safety.
- If the device has been verified for compliance, make sure to enter the date.

Please review this help guide before starting the survey to ensure a smooth and accurate submission process.
