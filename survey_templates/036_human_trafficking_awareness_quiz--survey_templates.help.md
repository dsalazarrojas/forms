# Human Trafficking Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to assess users' knowledge about human trafficking, its forms, victims, and prevention methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. Select all applicable answers for each multiple-choice question.
3. Provide a brief response to the final question fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is human trafficking?** (`awareness_of_human_trafficking`, text, not required): This question is a brief introduction to the concept of human trafficking.
* **What are the most common forms of human trafficking?** (`common_forms_of_ht`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: Human Trafficking for Sex, Labor Trafficking, Sex Trafficking, or Child Trafficking.
* **Who are the victims of human trafficking?** (`victims_of_ht`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options: Women, Men, Children, or Migrants.
* **How can individuals and communities help prevent human trafficking?** (`help_prevention`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options: Raise awareness, Educate others, Support survivors, or Report suspicious activities.
* **What are the consequences of human trafficking on individuals and communities?** (`consequences_of_ht`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: Financial loss, Psychological trauma, Social stigma, or Economic instability.
* **How can governments and organizations help prevent human trafficking?** (`government_role`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options: Enact laws and policies, Provide support services, Raise awareness, or Collaborate with international organizations.
* **How can technology be used to combat human trafficking?** (`tech_role`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: Data analysis, Social media campaigns, Digital education, or Artificial intelligence.
* **What is the role of media and social media in raising awareness about human trafficking?** (`media_role`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options: News coverage, Social media campaigns, Documentaries, or Influencer partnerships.
* **How can education and awareness programs help prevent human trafficking?** (`edu_awareness`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options: Classroom education, Community workshops, Online courses, or Public awareness campaigns.
* **What are the key challenges in combating human trafficking?** (`challenges_combating_ht`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options: Lack of resources, Limited awareness, Corruption, or Stigma.
* **What are some potential solutions to address human trafficking?** (`solutions_to_ht`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options: Stronger laws and regulations, Increased support for survivors, Improved collaboration between governments and organizations, or Increased awareness and education.
* **Final thoughts?** (`final_question`, note, not required): Provide a brief reflection on your understanding of human trafficking.
* **Final thoughts 2** (`final_question_2`, note, not required): This is an optional field for additional comments or reflections.
