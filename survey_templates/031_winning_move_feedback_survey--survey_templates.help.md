# Winning Move Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on an initiative that has been communicated to the team. This feedback is meant to help identify areas of improvement and areas of success for future initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully to ensure you are providing feedback on the initiative you participated in.
2. Answer all required questions to provide a complete and accurate survey response.
3. Use the available fields to provide feedback and comments as needed.
4. When filling out fields with ratings (e.g. "How often was the communication received?", "How valuable was the initiative to the team?"), choose the response that best fits your experience with the initiative.
