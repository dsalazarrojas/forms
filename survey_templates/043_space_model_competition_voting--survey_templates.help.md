# Space Model Competition Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Space Model Competition Voting form is designed to collect feedback and comments from judges and representatives of the competition organizers about the competition. This form will help gather information about their experience and opinions on the competition, including their thoughts on the winners, the overall experience, and their willingness to provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select your role as either a Judge or a Representative of the competition organizers.
2. Answer the questions about your experience with the competition, including what you thought of the winners and the overall experience.
3. Provide feedback about the competition format.
4. If you're a Representative, please provide your affiliation with the competition.
5. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions, select the correct option.
6. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
