# neurodivergent_identification_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to identify individuals who may be neurodivergent, meaning they have a neurological and developmental disorder that affects their behavior, social skills, and learning style. It aims to collect information about their age, contact details, and self-identified status as neurodivergent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your age, if you feel comfortable sharing this information.
2. Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
3. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select your neurodivergent status from the options provided (None, Neurotypical, Probable Neurodivergent, Possible Neurodivergent, Confirmed Neurodivergent).
5. Provide a self-reflection (optional) to help us understand your experiences and challenges.
6. Add any additional comments or thoughts (optional) that you'd like to share.
7. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Neurodivergent Status** (`neurodivergent_status`, select_multiple, required): Select your neurodivergent status from the options provided. If you are unsure, please choose "Probable Neurodivergent" or "Possible Neurodivergent" and we will follow up with you to discuss further.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age, if you feel comfortable sharing this information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Self Reflection** (`self_reflection`, text, optional): Provide a self-reflection to help us understand your experiences and challenges.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please choose "Probable Neurodivergent" and we will follow up with you to discuss further.
- You can request confidentiality and anonymity if you are concerned about your identity or personal details being shared.
