# Finder Qualification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Finder Qualification Survey is used to collect information about applicants and their skills and abilities for internal use.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a new tab or window.
2. Each section should be filled out completely and accurately.
3. Review and submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, **Optional**): 
    Enter the applicant's first name, last name, and any other identifying information.
* **Sourcing Experience** (`sourcing_experience`, number, **Optional**): 
    Enter your sourcing experience (e.g., number of candidates sourced).
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_one, **Optional**): 
    Select the skills that the applicant has, one at a time.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, **Optional**): 
    Enter the date and time the applicant is available.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, **Optional**):
