# sisv_basketball_playing_confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm the participation of individual players in a basketball game or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand the purpose of the confirmation.
2. Answer each question regarding the participation status of individual players.
3. Submit the form once you've confirmed all players' participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **player_1** (select_one, required/optional): Confirm whether Player 1 participated in the game.
* **player_2** (select_multiple, required/optional): Confirm whether Player 2 participated in the game. Select all applicable options to indicate multiple participations.
* **player_3** (select_one, required/optional): Confirm whether Player 3 participated in the game.

## Tips
* Be accurate when answering each question to ensure accurate tracking of player participation.
* If a player is not listed, you may need to create a separate form or contact the administrator for assistance.
