# Chess Opening Practice Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us better understand your opening play and provide you with personalized resources to improve your skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. Mandatory questions are marked with an asterisk (*). If you need help or have questions, please feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`player_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name for our records and future follow-up.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive further resources and updates on our opening programs.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, *): Choose your age group to help us understand your level of experience.
* **Chess Rating** (`chess_rating`, select_one, *): Enter your current chess rating for accurate assessment.
* **Years Playing Chess** (`years_playing`, select_one, *): Select how long you've been playing chess.
* **How Often Do You Play** (`play_frequency`, select_one, *): Choose how often you play chess.
* **Time Spent Studying Openings** (`opening_study_time`, select_one, *): Select how much time you spend studying openings.
* **Openings You Play as White** (`white_openings`, text, *): List your main openings with white.
* **Openings vs e4 as Black** (`black_openings_vs_e4`, select_multiple, *): Select the openings you usually play against e4.
* **Openings vs d4 as Black** (`black_openings_vs_d4`, select_multiple, *): Select the openings you usually play against d4.
* **How Do You Prepare Openings** (`opening_preparation_method`, select_multiple, *): Select all methods you use to prepare openings.
* **Biggest Opening Challenges** (`opening_challenges`, select_multiple, *): Select what you struggle with most in openings.
* **Common Opening Mistakes** (`opening_mistakes`, text, optional): Describe any common mistakes you make.
* **Opening Theory Knowledge** (`theory_knowledge`, select_one, *): Rate your knowledge of opening theory.
* **Opening Repertoire Stability** (`repertoire_stability`, select_one, *): Choose how consistent your opening repertoire is.
* **Do You Have a Coach** (`coach_guidance`, select_one, *): Select if you have a coach who guides you on openings.
* **Opening Success Rate** (`opening_success_rate`, select_one, *): Select how often you get good positions with your openings.
* **Favorite Opening** (`favorite_opening`, text, optional): Name your favorite opening to play.
* **Least Favorite Opening** (`least_favorite_opening`, text, optional): Name the opening you dislike the most.
* **Opening Study Goals** (`opening_goals`, text, optional): Describe your goals for studying openings.
* **Resources Needed** (`resources_needed`, text, optional): List any resources that would help your opening study.
* **Would Participate in Opening Training** (`would_participate_training`, select_one, *): Select if you would join an opening training program.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional thoughts or comments about opening study.

Note: Asterisks (\*) denote mandatory questions.
