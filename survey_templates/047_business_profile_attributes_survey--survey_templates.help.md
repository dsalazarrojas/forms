# Business Profile Attributes Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture business profile attributes of your company. It will help us understand your business's characteristics, such as industry, size, and growth rate, which will inform our future interactions and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the survey date.
2. Provide your company's name.
3. Enter your contact email and phone number.
4. Choose your primary industry from the options provided.
5. Select the size of your company (small, medium, large, or enterprise).
6. Specify your business type (B2B, B2C, B2G, non-profit, or other).
7. Choose your primary products or services offered.
8. Identify your target market.
9. Enter your geographic coverage area.
10. (Optional) Enter your approximate revenue range and growth rate.
11. List your main competitors.
12. Describe what makes your business different (unique selling proposition).
13. Specify your primary distribution channels.
14. List your key business challenges.
15. Outline your strategic objectives.
16. (Optional) Rate your customer satisfaction level (scale 1-5).
17. Click submit to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date (1)** (Required): Fill in the date on which you're completing this form.
* **Client Name (2)** (Required): Enter your company's name.
* **Client Email (3)** (Required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (4)** (Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Industry Sector (6)** (Required): Choose your company's primary industry:
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Retail
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Services
	+ Other
* **Company Size (7)** (Required): Choose your company's size:
	+ Small (1-50)
	+ Medium (51-250)
	+ Large (251-1000)
	+ Enterprise (1000+)
* **Business Type (8)** (Required): Choose your business type:
	+ B2B
	+ B2C
	+ B2G
	+ Non-profit
	+ Other
* **Primary Products or Services (11)** (Required): Describe your main products or services offered.
* **Target Market (12)** (Required): Identify your target market (customer demographic).
* **Geographic Coverage (13)** (Optional): Enter your service areas.
* **Growth Rate (14)** (Optional): Enter your approximate growth rate (recent %).
* **Main Competitors (15)** (Optional): List your main competitors.
* **Unique Selling Proposition (16)** (Required): Describe what makes your business different.
* **Primary Distribution Channels (17)** (Optional): Describe how you reach customers.
* **Key Business Challenges (18)** (Required): List your main business challenges.
* **Strategic Objectives (19)** (Optional): Outline your strategic objectives.
* **Customer Satisfaction Level (20)** (Optional): Rate your customer satisfaction level (scale 1-5).

Note: Required fields are marked with (Required).
