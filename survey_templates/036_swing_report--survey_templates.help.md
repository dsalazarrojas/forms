# Swing Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information or feedback from users. It consists of multiple fields that cater to different types of input, such as select, date, time, note, email, phone, text, integer, and decimal fields.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field to ensure you understand what type of input is required.
2. For select fields, choose the correct option from the dropdown menu (e.g., Yes or No).
3. For date fields, enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. For time fields, enter the time in 12-hour format (e.g., 01:00 PM).
5. For email fields, enter a valid email address.
6. For phone fields, enter a valid phone number.
7. For text fields, enter a short description or comment.
8. For integer fields, enter a whole number.
9. For decimal fields, enter a decimal number.
