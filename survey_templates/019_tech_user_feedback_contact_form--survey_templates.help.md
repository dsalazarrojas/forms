# tech_user_feedback_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tech_user_feedback_contact_form is a contact form designed to gather user feedback, bug reports, and suggestions for the system. This form is intended for users who need to report an issue or have a comment about their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Contact Us" link on the website.
2. Fill out the required fields (Name, Email, and Contact Form).
3. Select the purpose of your contact by filling out the appropriate feedback field (Bug Report or Suggestion).
4. Provide a brief description of your feedback in the text field.
5. Enter your contact information (Name, Email, and Phone number, if applicable).
6. Click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Form (Page 1)
* **Feedback 1**: What was the purpose of your visit? (`feedback_1`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to help us understand the context of your visit.
* **Describe the purpose of your visit**: (`feedback_1_response`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to provide more details about your visit.
* **Bug Report**: (`bug_report`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to report any bugs or issues you encountered during your visit.
* **Describe the bug**: (`bug_description`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to provide more details about the bug.
* **Suggestion**: (`suggestion`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to suggest new features or improvements.
* **Describe your suggestion**: (`suggestion_description`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to provide more details about your suggestion.
* **Contact Us**: (`contact_us`, email, optional)
	+ This field is used to contact us for any other inquiry.
* **Name**: (`name`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to provide your name.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, optional)
	+ This field is used to provide your email address.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to provide your phone number.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" are not required to be filled out.
