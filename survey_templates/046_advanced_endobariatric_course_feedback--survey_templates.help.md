# Advanced Endobariatric Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from attendees on the Advanced Endobariatric Course. Your feedback is crucial in helping us evaluate the effectiveness of the course in meeting its learning objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant section for the course you attended.
2. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
3. Choose the best option that suits your evaluation of each question.
4. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the "Suggestions for Future Topics" and "Additional Feedback for Organizers" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (Attendee Name, Text, required/optional): Please provide your name and title/position for reference purposes.
* **Professional Role** (Professional Role, Select One, required): Select your primary professional role from the list.
* **Course Date** (Course Date, Date, required): Please enter the date you attended the course.
* **Quality of Didactic Lectures** (Quality of Didactic Lectures, Select One, required): Evaluate the clarity and depth of the presentation of the didactic lectures. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Effectiveness of Live Demonstrations** (Effectiveness of Live Demonstrations, Select One, required): Assess the observation of procedural techniques during the live demonstrations. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Value of Hands-on Lab Sessions** (Value of Hands-on Lab Sessions, Select One, required): Evaluate the practical application of skills learned during the hands-on lab sessions. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Were Learning Objectives Met** (Were Learning Objectives Met, Select One, required): Indicate whether the learning objectives were met during the course. Choose from Yes - Completely, Yes - Partially, or False.
* **Impact on Clinical Practice** (Impact on Clinical Practice, Select One, required): Assess how much the course will change your approach to clinical practice. Choose from Significant, Moderate, Slight, or None.
* **Most Valuable Technique Learned** (Most Valuable Technique Learned, Text, optional): If you learned anything valuable during the course, please describe it in this section.
* **Overall Course Organization** (Overall Course Organization, Select One, required): Evaluate the organization of the course, including venue, schedule, and support. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Suggestions for Future Topics** (Suggestions for Future Topics, Text, optional): If you have any suggestions for future topics, please provide them in this section.
* **Additional Feedback for Organizers** (Additional Feedback for Organizers, Text, optional): If you have any additional feedback or comments for the course organizers, please provide them here.
