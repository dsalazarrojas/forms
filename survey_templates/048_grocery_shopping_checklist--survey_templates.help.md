# Grocery Shopping Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your grocery shopping habits and preferences. It will help us understand how you shop for groceries and what you look for in a store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. For the "shopping items" section, select all the items that apply to your current shopping habits.
3. In the "store" section, choose either "Yes" or "No" depending on your shopping frequency.
4. If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, fill in the remaining store information (store name, address, phone, and email).
5. For the "store hours" section, choose either "Yes" or "No" depending on whether the store is usually open during your shopping hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **grocery shopping checklist** (note): This is a note field where you can add any additional information about your current grocery shopping habits.
* **shopping items** (select multiple): Check all the items that you commonly shop for or are likely to shop for in your next grocery trip.
	+ Example: milk, eggs, bread, vegetables, etc.
* **store** (select one): How often do you visit this store for grocery shopping?
	+ Yes: You visit this store frequently.
	+ No: You do not visit this store often.
* **store name** (text): If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, please enter the name of the store you usually visit for grocery shopping.
* **store address** (text): Enter the address of the store.
* **store phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the store.
* **store email** (email): Enter the email address of the store.
* **store hours** (select one): Are the store hours usually open during your shopping hours?
	+ Yes: The store is usually open during your shopping hours.
	+ No: The store is not usually open during your shopping hours.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to add them in the "grocery shopping checklist" note field.
