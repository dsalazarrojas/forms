# Information Technology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an individual's experiences and attitudes towards information technology.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through the pages below and fill out the relevant information for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (optional): This is a brief page that provides some background information about the survey. Please feel free to read it, but you don't need to fill out anything here.
* **Technology Use** (optional, select multiple): On this page, you will see two options: "Yes" and "No". If you use information technology regularly, please select the one that best describes your experience.
* **Satisfaction** (optional, select one): On this page, you will see two options: "Satisfied" and "Dissatisfied". Please select the one that best describes your level of satisfaction with information technology.
* **Needs and Expectations** (optional, text input): This is the final page where you can provide any comments or feedback about your needs and expectations related to information technology. Be as specific as possible and provide any relevant details.
