# App Feature Interest Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The App Feature Interest Poll is a form used to collect feedback and interest levels from users about various features of an app. By filling out this form, you will contribute to the development and improvement of the app.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Poll date" field and enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "Respondent name" and "Email address" fields, respectively.
3. Choose your experience level with the app from the "Your experience level with the app" dropdown menu.
4. For each feature question, select one of the options according to your actual experience and preferences.
5. Select the "Which feature is highest priority for you" option from the list of features.
6. Answer the "Other features you would like to see" question if you have any other feature requests.
7. Choose how important you think it is to roll out new features quickly from the "How important is quick rollout of new features" dropdown menu.
8. If you are willing to pay for a premium plan, select one of the options in the "Would you pay for a premium plan with advanced features" dropdown menu.
9. Enter your acceptable price range for a premium plan in the "Acceptable price range for premium features" field.
10. Provide any general improvement suggestions in the "General improvement suggestions" field.
11. Select if you are interested in beta testing new features from the "Interested in beta testing new features" dropdown menu.
12. Finally, choose whether you want to be contacted for follow-up feedback from the "May we contact you for follow-up feedback" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Poll date** (`poll_date`, date, required): Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **What app are you using?** (`app_name`, text, required): Choose the name of the app you are using.
* **How would you describe yourself?** (`user_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you would describe yourself from the dropdown menu.
* **Your experience level with the app** (`user_experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your experience level with the app from the dropdown menu.
* **Current satisfaction with the app** (`current_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with the app from the dropdown menu.
* **Interest in Feature 1 - Advanced Analytics Dashboard** (`feature_one_interest`, select_one, required): Choose your level of interest in the Advanced Analytics Dashboard from the dropdown menu.
* **Interest in Feature 2 - Mobile App Integration** (`feature_two_interest`, select_one, required): Choose your level of interest in the Mobile App Integration from the dropdown menu.
* **Interest in Feature 3 - Automated Workflows** (`feature_three_interest`, select_one, required): Choose your level of interest in the Automated Workflows from the dropdown menu.
* **Interest in Feature 4 - Enhanced Collaboration Tools** (`feature_four_interest`, select_one, required): Choose your level of interest in the Enhanced Collaboration Tools from the dropdown menu.
* **Interest in Feature 5 - API Access for Developers** (`feature_five_interest`, select_one, required): Choose your level of interest in the API Access for Developers from the dropdown menu.
* **Which feature is highest priority for you?** (`highest_priority_feature`, select_one, required): Choose the feature that is most important to you.
* **Other features you would like to see** (`other_requested_features`, text, optional): Provide any other feature requests.
* **How important is quick rollout of new features?** (`feature_timeline_importance`, select_one, required): Choose how important it is to roll out new features quickly from the dropdown menu.
* **Would you pay for a premium plan with advanced features?** (`willingness_pay_premium`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would be willing to pay for a premium plan with advanced features.
* **Acceptable price range for premium features** (`acceptable_price_range`, text, optional): Enter your acceptable price range for a premium plan.
* **General improvement suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any general improvement suggestions for the app.
* **Interested in beta testing new features?** (`beta_interest`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are interested in beta testing new features from the dropdown menu.
