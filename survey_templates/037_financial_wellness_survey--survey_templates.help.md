# financial_wellness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on an individual's financial wellness and behavior, allowing for the assessment of their overall financial health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review each field carefully and select the most accurate option or enter the most relevant information.
3. Complete all required fields to ensure accurate and complete results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Financial Wellness
* **General Financial Wellness** (`General Financial Wellness`, `text`, optional): Describe your current financial wellness in a few sentences, including any areas of concern or goals.

### Current Savings
* **Current Savings** (`Current Savings`, `number`, optional): Enter your current savings or cash balance.

### Financial Goal
* **Financial Goal** (`Financial Goal`, `text`, optional): Describe your short-term or long-term financial goals, such as saving for a specific purpose or paying off debt.

### Risk Tolerance
* **Risk Tolerance** (`Risk Tolerance`, `text`, optional): Indicate your comfort level with taking risks with your investments, such as conservative, moderate, or aggressive.

### Investment Experience
* **Investment Experience** (`Investment Experience`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all relevant options for your investment experience, such as beginner, intermediate, or advanced.

### Financial Institution Interaction
* **Financial Institution Interaction** (`Financial Institution Interaction`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the best description of your current relationship with a financial institution, such as bank, investment firm, or credit union.

### Financial Institution
* **Financial Institution** (`Financial Institution`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of financial institution you primarily use for your finances, such as bank, investment firm, or credit union.

### Account Balance
* **Account Balance** (`Account Balance`, `number`, optional): Enter your current account balance, if applicable.

### Loan Balance
* **Loan Balance** (`Loan Balance`, `text`, optional): Describe your current loan balance or outstanding debt.

### Financial Institution Experience
* **Financial Institution Experience** (`Financial Institution Experience`, `text`, optional): Describe your experience with financial institutions, including any successes or challenges.

### Financial Wellness Score
* **Financial Wellness Score** (`Financial Wellness Score`, `number`, optional): Enter your self-assessment of your overall financial wellness, on a scale of 0-100.

### Financial Institution Rating
* **Financial Institution Rating** (`Financial Institution Rating`, `select_one`, optional): Choose a rating for your satisfaction with your current financial institution, such as excellent, good, or poor.
