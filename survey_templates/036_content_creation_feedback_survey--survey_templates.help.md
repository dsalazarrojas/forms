# Content Creation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Creation Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback and ratings from respondents on content projects, allowing us to improve the quality and engagement of our content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have read and understood the survey's purpose and instructions.
2. Select the content project you want to provide feedback on.
3. Complete each section of the survey, providing feedback and ratings as instructed.
4. Review and confirm that the information provided is accurate.
5. Submit the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Content Project Title (`content_title`)
* **Title**: *Content Project Title*
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: true
* Explanation: Enter the title of the content project you want to provide feedback on.

### 2. Content Project Identification Number (`content_id`)
* **Title**: *Content Project Identification Number*
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: true
* Explanation: Enter the identification number of the content project.

### 3. Content Asset Type (`content_type`)
* **Title**: *Content Asset Type*
* **Type**: select_one
* **Required**: true
* Explanation: Select the type of content asset (e.g., Blog Post, Video, Infographic, etc.).

### 4. Content Quality Rating (`content_quality_rating`)
* **Title**: *Content Quality Rating*
* **Type**: select_one
* **Required**: true
* Explanation: Rate the quality of the content, with 1 being poor, 5 being excellent.

### 5. Engagement Level Rating (`engagement_level_rating`)
* **Title**: *Engagement Level Rating*
* **Type**: select_one
* **Required**: true
* Explanation: Rate the engagement level of the content, with 1 being low, 5 being high.

### 6. Relevance to Target Audience Rating (`relevance_to_target_audience_rating`)
* **Title**: *Relevance to Target Audience Rating*
* **Type**: select_one
* **Required**: true
* Explanation: Rate the relevance of the content to the target audience, with 1 being low, 5 being high.

### 7. What did you like most about the content? (`specific_feedback_question_1`)
* **Title**: *What did you like most about the content?*
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: true
* Explanation: Share what you liked most about the content.

### 8. What areas could be improved? (`specific_feedback_question_2`)
* **Title**: *What areas could be improved?*
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: true
* Explanation: Provide feedback on areas that could be improved in the content.

### 9. Respondent Full Name (`respondent_name`)
* **Title**: *Respondent Full Name*
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: false
* Explanation: Enter your full name, if applicable.

### 10. Respondent Identification Number (`respondent_id`)
* **Title**: *Respondent Identification Number*
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: false
* Explanation: Enter your identification number, if applicable.

### 11. Age Range (`respondent_age_range`)
* **Title**: *Age Range*
* **Type**: select_one
* **Required**: false
* Explanation: Select your age range (Under 18, 18-24, etc.).

### 12. Survey Completed (`survey_completed`)
* **Title**: *Survey Completed*
* **Type**: select_one
* **Required**: true
* Explanation: Confirm that the survey is completed.

### 13. Signature Confirmation (`signature_confirmation`)
* **Title**: *I confirm that the information provided is accurate*
* **Type**: select_one
* **Required**: true
* Explanation: Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
