# learning_chapter_worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "learning_chapter_worksheet" form is used to collect feedback and progress updates from learners on each chapter of a learning program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name and chapter number to confirm your identity and indicate which chapter you are submitting feedback on.
2. Provide a reflection on your experience with the chapter, sharing any insights or challenges you faced.
3. Choose the relevant comprehension checks that apply to your experience with the chapter.
4. Enter any additional feedback or comments you have about the chapter.
5. Select the next steps you plan to take in relation to the chapter.
6. Enter the completion date for the chapter.
7. Report the amount of time you spent on the chapter.
8. Identify your learning goals for the chapter.
9. List any resources used during the chapter.
10. Enter any comments or feedback you have about the chapter.
11. Provide a signature to confirm submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name to confirm your identity.
* **Chapter Number** (`chapter_number`, number, required): Select the chapter number you are submitting feedback on.
* **Reflection** (`reflection`, text, required): Share your thoughts and insights about the chapter.
* **Comprehension Check** (`comprehension_check`, select_multiple, required): Choose the options that apply to your experience with the chapter.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any additional feedback or comments about the chapter.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Select the next steps you plan to take in relation to the chapter.
* **Completion Date** (`completion_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the chapter.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, number, optional): Report the amount of time you spent on the chapter.
* **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, select_one, required): Identify your learning goals for the chapter.
* **Resources Used** (`resources_used`, text, optional): List any resources you used during the chapter.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the chapter.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Provide a signature to confirm submission.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, required): Select the submitter for this form.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be completed before submitting the form.
