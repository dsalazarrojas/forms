# Crop Storage Management Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users regarding their crop storage management practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
2. Use the options provided for select fields.
3. If you have any additional information to provide, use the "Additional Information" field.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your general situation or background information.
* **Storage Practices** (`storage_practices`, select_multiple, optional):
  + Choose all the storage practices you employ.
  + Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Storage Capacity** (`storage_capacity`, select_one, optional): Choose your current storage capacity.
  + Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, note, optional): Describe any challenges you face with crop storage.
* **Storage Facility** (`storage_facility`, select_one, optional): Choose your current storage facility.
  + Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional):
  + Choose all team members responsible for crop storage.
  + Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Provide your contact information.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Describe any additional information you would like to share about your crop storage management practices.
