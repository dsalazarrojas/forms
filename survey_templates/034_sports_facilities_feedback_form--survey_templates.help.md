# Sports Facilities Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with sports facilities. The feedback will help us understand areas of improvement and ensure that our sports facilities meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Submit" to start filling out the form.
2. On the first page, provide a brief general feedback about your experience.
3. On the second page, select all the facility types that apply to your visit.
4. On the third page, provide more detailed feedback on what can be improved.
5. On the fourth page, rate your overall experience as Very good, Neutral, or Bad.
6. On the fifth page, submit your final feedback and click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sports Facilities Feedback Form** (`sports_facilities_feedback_form_page_1`, text, optional): Please provide a brief general feedback about your experience with the sports facilities.
* **Select Facility Type** (`sports_facilities_feedback_form_page_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the facility types that apply to your visit (e.g., Gym, Swimming Pool, Track, etc.).
* **What can be improved** (`sports_facilities_feedback_form_page_3`, text, optional): Provide more detailed feedback on what can be improved about the sports facilities.
* **How did you experience your visit?** (`sports_facilities_feedback_form_page_4`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall experience as Very good, Neutral, or Bad.
* **Your Feedback** (`sports_facilities_feedback_form_page_5`, text, optional): Submit your final feedback and click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have visited the sports facilities and have a genuine experience to share.
- If you have already submitted a feedback, please do not duplicate it.
- Please provide accurate and honest feedback to help us improve our sports facilities.
