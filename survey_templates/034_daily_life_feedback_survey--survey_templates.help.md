# daily_life_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your daily life, allowing you to reflect on your experiences and identify areas for improvement or satisfaction.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`daily_life_feedback_survey_page_1`): A brief note explaining the purpose of the form and its relevance to your daily life.
* **How satisfied are you with your daily life?** (`daily_life_feedback_survey_page_2`): Choose one of the two options, "Yes" or "No", to indicate your level of satisfaction with your daily life.
* **What do you like about your daily life?** (`daily_life_feedback_survey_page_3`): Provide a text response to describe the things that bring you joy or satisfaction in your daily life.
* **What would you like to improve in your daily life?** (`daily_life_feedback_survey_page_4`): Share your thoughts on areas you'd like to see changed or improved in your daily life.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with your daily life on a scale of 1-10?** (`daily_life_feedback_survey_page_5`): Choose a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your overall level of satisfaction with your daily life.
* **What are the things you value most about your daily life?** (`daily_life_feedback_survey_page_6`): Select all that apply from the two options, "Yes" or "No", to identify the aspects of your daily life that are most important to you.
* **How satisfied are you with your sleep?** (`daily_life_feedback_survey_page_7`): Choose a number between 1 and 10 to rate your satisfaction with your sleep.
* **How satisfied are you with your work-life balance?** (`daily_life_feedback_survey_page_8`): Select a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your level of satisfaction with your work-life balance.
* **How satisfied are you with your social life?** (`daily_life_feedback_survey_page_9`): Choose a number between 1 and 10 to rate your satisfaction with your social life.
* **Do you feel connected to your community?** (`daily_life_feedback_survey_page_10`): Select one of the two options, "Yes" or "No", to indicate your level of connectedness with your community.
