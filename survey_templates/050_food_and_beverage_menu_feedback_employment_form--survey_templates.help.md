# Food And Beverage Menu Feedback Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food And Beverage Menu Feedback Employment Form is designed to collect feedback and information from employees about their experiences with food and beverage menus in the workplace. This form aims to provide insights into employee satisfaction, job satisfaction, and other aspects of their work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the menu options and select the ones that apply to your experience.
2. List the specific menu items you'd like to review.
3. Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
4. Fill in your name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
5. Select your employment status, availability to work, and job experience.
6. Rate your job satisfaction and select the reasons for your satisfaction or dissatisfaction.
7. Evaluate your work environment and benefits.
8. Provide information about your job title, company, job description, requirements, qualifications, and duties.
9. Select the type of job you're looking for and your availability to work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Menu Review** (`menu_review`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the menu options that apply to your experience.
* **Menu Items** (`menu_items`, select_multiple, required: false): List the specific menu items you'd like to review.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your current employment status.
* **Available to Work** (`available_to_work`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you are available to work.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, select_one, required: false): Select your level of work experience.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Rate your job satisfaction.
* **Job Satisfaction Reason** (`job_satisfaction_reason`, text, required: false): Provide the reason for your job satisfaction or dissatisfaction.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate your work environment.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, select_one, required: false): Select your level of satisfaction with benefits.
* **Compensation** (`compensation`, number, required: false): Enter your current salary or compensation.
* **Salary** (`salary`, text, required: false): Enter your salary or compensation (if not already entered in the compensation field).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Company** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company you're working for or interested in.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, required: false): Enter the requirements of your job.
* **Job Qualifications** (`job_qualifications`, text, required: false): Enter your qualifications for the job.
* **Job Duties** (`job_duties`, text, required: false): Enter your job duties.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, required: false): Enter the type of job you're looking for.
* **Job Shift** (`job_shift`, text, required: false): Enter your job shift or schedule.
* **Job Schedule** (`job_schedule`, text, required: false): Enter your job schedule.
* **Job Availability** (`job_availability`, text, required: false): Enter your availability to work.

Tips:

* Please answer all questions that apply to your experience.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
* Ensure accuracy and honesty in your answers.
