# Iconography And Church Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from church members about their experiences and views on iconography, a key element in church settings. Your input is invaluable in helping us understand the impact of iconography on worship and church activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Begin with the introduction, which provides a brief background on the purpose and structure of the form.
2. Answer the questions about religious icons, rating their impact on your worship experience.
3. Share your thoughts on the impact of iconography on worship and church activities.
4. Provide demographic information, if you feel comfortable doing so.
5. Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
6. Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted for further discussions.
7. Submit the form to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`note`, `required: false`): This is a brief overview of the form's purpose and structure. Read it carefully to understand what to expect.
- **religious-icons** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`): Choose all the religious icons that have had an impact on your worship experience.
- **impact_worship** (`number`, `required: false`): Rate the impact of iconography on your worship experience. 
- **impact_engagement** (`number`, `required: false`): Rate the impact of iconography on your engagement with church activities.
- **impact_spiritual_offerings** (`number`, `required: false`): Rate the impact of iconography on your spiritual experiences. 
- **demographic_info** (`date`, `required: false`): If you're comfortable sharing, provide your demographic information.
- **comments** (`text`, `required: false`): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with iconography and church settings.
- **email** (`email`, `required: false`): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussions. 
Note: All fields are optional, except for the introduction, but please answer as many fields as you're comfortable with to provide a comprehensive understanding of your views.
