# Snowmobile Equipment Exchange Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snowmobile Equipment Exchange Survey form is a tool for collecting and recording information on snowmobile equipment being exchanged or sold. This form is designed to help facilitate transactions between buyers and sellers by providing a structured way of gathering essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact details, including your email address and phone number, to enable us to reach you regarding your submission.
2. Select the type of equipment you are exchanging or selling. You can choose one or multiple options from the available list.
3. Describe the condition of the equipment you are exchanging or selling.
4. Attach any relevant photos of the equipment.
5. Provide any additional contact information or details about yourself or the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact_details**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can contact you regarding your submission.
* **equipment_type**: Select the type of snowmobile equipment you are exchanging or selling from the list.
* **equipment_condition**: Indicate the condition of the equipment you are exchanging or selling.
* **photos**: Attach photos of the equipment to provide a clear view of its condition.
* **contact_photo**: Provide a photo of yourself for verification purposes.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address to enable us to contact you.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you.
* **equipment_description**: Enter a detailed description of the snowmobile equipment, including its brand, model, year, and any unique features.
* **snowmobile_details**: Enter any additional details about the snowmobile, such as its mileage, storage capacity, or notable features.
Note: This form is not intended for commercial purposes, but for internal use within the organization to facilitate snowmobile equipment exchange. Ensure you provide accurate and honest information to help us in processing your submission efficiently.
