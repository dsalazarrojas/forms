# Product Prototype Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help product developers understand user opinions and gather feedback on product prototypes. Fill out this form to provide your thoughts on a product, and help us create a better product for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing the product name.
2. Select the product category.
3. Rate the product (if you've used it before).
4. Share any comments about the product.
5. Provide your email address to help us contact you.
6. Enter a contact number (if applicable).
7. Add any additional feedback or suggestions about the product.
8. Suggest a feature, bugfix, or improvement for the product.
9. Enter the product version.
10. Share your user experience with the product.
11. Indicate if you will recommend the product.
12. Enter a date for any additional feedback or suggestion details.
13. Enter any additional contact details (if applicable).
14. Provide any additional information about the product.
15. Add a product image URL or path (if applicable).
16. Enter a URL for the product (if applicable).
17. Specify the type of product URL (HTTP, HTTPS, or Local).
17. (Optional) Attach any files related to the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Product Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category that best describes the product.
* **Overall Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required/optional): Rate the product on a scale of 1-5. If you haven't used the product, you can leave this field blank.
* **Comments** (`comment`, `note`, required/optional): Share any thoughts or comments about the product.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter a contact number (if you have one).
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Share any additional feedback or comments about the product.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, `select_multiple`, optional): Suggest a feature, bugfix, or improvement for the product.
* **Product Version** (`product_version`, `number`, required): Enter the version of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **User Experience** (`user_experience`, `select_one`, required): Share your experience with the product (Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, Very Negative).
* **Will Recommend** (`will_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you will recommend the product (True or False).
* **Suggestion Details** (`suggestion_details`, `date`, required): Enter a date for any additional feedback or suggestion details.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `time`, optional): Enter any additional contact details (if applicable).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about the product.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, `text`, optional): Add a product image URL or path (if applicable).
* **Product URL** (`product_url`, `text`, optional): Enter a URL for the product (if applicable).
* **Product URL Type** (`product_url_type`, `select_one`, required): Specify the type of product URL (HTTP, HTTPS, or Local).
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, `note`, optional): Attach any files related to the product (if applicable).
