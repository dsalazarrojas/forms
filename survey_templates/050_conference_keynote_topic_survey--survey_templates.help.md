# Conference Keynote Topic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Conference Keynote Topic Survey is designed to gather information from attendees on their preferences for keynote topics, speakers, and session formats for our upcoming conference. Your input will help us curate engaging and relevant keynotes that cater to the interests of our attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as you are comfortable sharing.
2. You can select up to three keynote topics that interest you the most.
3. If you have any additional topics you would like to see, please mention them in the "Additional topic suggestions" field.
4. You can choose one preferred speaker profile type (Academic, Industry leader, or Practitioner).
5. You can select one preferred keynote format (Lecture, Interview, or Fireside chat).
6. You can provide any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name**: Your name as registered to the conference.
* **Contact email**: Your email address for follow-up communication about the topics and keynotes.
* **Select preferred keynote topics**: Please choose up to three topics from the list that you find the most interesting.
	+ Innovation and Technology
	+ Leadership and Culture
	+ Sustainability and Climate
	+ Diversity and Inclusion
	+ Future of Work
	+ Industry-specific trends
* **Preferred speaker profile**: Which type of speaker you would like to see as a keynote?
	+ Academic
	+ Industry leader
	+ Practitioner
	+ Influencer
* **Preferred keynote format**: Which format would you prefer for the keynotes?
	+ Lecture
	+ Interview
	+ Fireside chat
	+ Panel
* **Reason for topic choice**: If you have a specific reason why you chose the topics you selected, please explain.
* **Additional topic suggestions**: If you have other topics you would like to see, please mention them here.
* **Willingness to participate**: If you are willing to participate in a panel or as a moderator, please indicate your willingness.
* **Additional comments**: Any other input or comments you would like to share about the keynotes.
