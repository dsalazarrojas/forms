# pre_work_strategy_planning_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions and answers from users regarding pre-work strategy planning, including questions, readiness, communication preferences, risk identification, project kickoff planning, team participation, stakeholder engagement, communication channels, team dynamics, and team capacity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page (or section) and answer the questions accordingly. For select-one questions, choose one of the available options. For select-multiple questions, choose all applicable options. You can navigate through the form using the page navigation buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, `note`, required: false): Answer any questions that come to mind regarding the project.
* **Readiness Questions** (`readiness_questions`, `text`, required: false): Describe your readiness for the project.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your preferred communication channels (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
* **Risk Identification** (`risk_identification`, `select_one`, required: false): Identify any risks you think the project may face.
* **Project Kickoff Planning** (`project_kickoff_planning`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you have completed project kickoff planning.
* **Team Participation** (`team_participation`, `text`, required: false): Describe your involvement in the team.
* **Stakeholder Engagement** (`stakeholder_engagement`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the stakeholders you would like to engage with (e.g., project manager, team leader, or client).
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable communication channels (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
* **Team Dynamics** (`team_dynamics`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all applicable team dynamics (e.g., collaboration, leadership, or motivation).
* **Team Capacity** (multiple occurrences with `team_capacity`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all applicable team capacities (e.g., skills, experience, or expertise). 

Note: There are multiple fields with the same name `team_capacity`, but with different id's and labels. It is likely that these fields are intended to capture different aspects of team capacity.
