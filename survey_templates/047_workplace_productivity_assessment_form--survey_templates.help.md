# workplace_productivity_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "workplace_productivity_assessment_form" is a tool used by managers and HR personnel to assess employee productivity. It helps evaluate an employee's performance, providing valuable insights into their work habits, strengths, and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Employee ID to identify yourself as the subject of the assessment.
2.  Enter your employee name as it appears on your official company documents.
3.  Select your department and work hours per day, if applicable.
4.  Enter your job title, manager's name, and work schedule (if applicable).
5.  Provide an employee rating, manager rating, and overall rating, using numbers from 1 to 10.
6.  Enter any feedback, suggestions, comments, and manager's name, if applicable.
7.  Add your email address and phone number, if you would like to be contacted regarding your performance.
8.  Enter specific ratings for job performance, work environment, collaboration, communication, time management, problem solving, and provide a rating for the hours worked per day, week, or month, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (1, number, required): Enter your unique company-issued ID number.
* **Employee Name** (2, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official company documents.
* **Department** (3, text, optional): Select your department or team from the list.
* **Manager's Name** (4, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Job Title** (5, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Work Hours** (6, number, required): Enter the number of hours you work per day.
* **Work Schedule** (7, time, optional): Enter your work schedule, if you have one (e.g., 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM).
* **Employee Rating** (8, number, required): Rate your performance from 1 to 10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **Manager Rating** (9, number, required): Rate your manager's performance from 1 to 10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **Overall Rating** (10, number, required): Rate your overall performance from 1 to 10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **Feedback** (11, text, required): Provide any feedback or comments you'd like to share with your manager.
* **Suggestions** (12, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, enter them here.
* **Comments** (13, text, optional): Enter any comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **Email** (14, email, required): Enter your email address so you can be contacted regarding your performance.
* **Phone** (15, text, required): Enter your phone number so you can be contacted regarding your performance.
* **Job Performance** (16, text, required): Rate your job performance from 1 to 10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
*   **Work Environment** (17, text, required): Rate your work environment from 1 to 10, with 1 being poor and 10 being good.
*   **Collaboration** (18, text, required): Rate your collaboration skills from 1 to 10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
*   **Communication** (19, text, required): Rate your communication skills from 1 to 10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
*   **Time Management** (20, text, required): Rate your time management skills from 1 to 10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
*   **Problem Solving** (21, text, required): Rate your problem-solving skills from 1 to 10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
*   **Work Hours Per Day** (22, number, optional): Enter the number of hours worked per day, if applicable.
*   **Work Hours Per Week** (23, number, optional): Enter the number of hours worked per week, if applicable.
*   **Work Hours Per Month** (24, number, optional): Enter the number of hours worked per month, if applicable.
*   **Work Hours Per Year** (25, number, optional): Enter the number of hours worked per year, if applicable.
