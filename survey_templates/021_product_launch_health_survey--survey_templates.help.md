# product-launch-health-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about the health of a product launch. It will help us understand how well the product met your expectations and where we can improve it for future launches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question honestly and thoroughly. You do not need to complete all the fields, but be as concise as possible. You can submit the form at your own pace.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Survey Intro
* **Survey Intro** (`1`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your interaction with the product and your motivation for filling out this form.

### Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product on a scale of Very High, High, Medium, Low, or Very Low.

### Recommendation Score
* **Recommendation Score** (`3`, `number`, optional): On a scale of 1-5, rate how likely you are to recommend this product to others.

### Improvement Ideas
* **Improvement Ideas** (`4`, `text`, optional): Share any thoughts on how we can improve the product. Be as specific as possible about what's not working for you.

### Satisfaction with Product Features
* **Satisfaction with Product Features** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether or not you are satisfied with the product's features.

### Satisfaction with Product Performance
* **Satisfaction with Product Performance** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether or not you are satisfied with the product's performance.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback is valuable to us.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the product team for support.
