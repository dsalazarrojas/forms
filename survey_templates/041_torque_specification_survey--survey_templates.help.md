# Torque Specification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a survey intended to gather information from users about their experiences or perspectives on torque specification. Please take this survey to contribute to the development or research in the field of torque specification.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, click on the form and select the first page.
2.  Scroll through each page and fill out the corresponding fields.
3.  For fields with the option to select multiple choices, you can select one or more options.
4.  Ensure that you fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your name** (`torque_specification_survey_form_fields_1`, `text`, required: false): This is the field where you can enter your name as a reference point for your answers.
*   **What is your department** (`torque_specification_survey_form_fields_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your department from the options provided (Engineering, Manufacturing, Maintenance) to indicate your role or affiliation.
*   **What standard are you using for torque specification?** (`torque_specification_survey_form_fields_3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more standards from the options (SAE, ISO, DIN, JIS, Other) that you commonly use for torque specification in your work.
*   **What is the common challenge in torque specification?** (`torque_specification_survey_form_fields_4`, `text`, required: false): This is the field where you can describe the most common issue or challenge you face in torque specification.

## Tips

*   Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out each field.
*   If you are unsure about an option, select "Other" and provide a detailed description.
*   This form is for your input, and your responses will contribute to the development of the field of torque specification.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
