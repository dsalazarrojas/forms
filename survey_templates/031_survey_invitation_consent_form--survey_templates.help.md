# survey_invitation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain your consent and gather essential information about you as a participant in a study or research project. Please read each section carefully and provide accurate information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and provide accurate information about yourself.
2. Answer each question in the provided text fields.
3. Review all the sections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro_page`, text, optional): This is a brief introduction to the form. You can read it to understand the project's purpose and objectives.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Please provide some information about yourself, including your name, age, or any other relevant details.
* **Consent Statement** (`consent_statement`, text, optional): This is where you agree to participate in the survey. Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of the project.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): You can provide additional contact information, such as your email or phone number, for follow-up communication.
* **Survey Info** (`survey_info`, text, optional): This is a place to provide any other information about the survey, such as the duration or frequency of the study.
* **Participant Declaration** (`participant_declaration`, text, optional): This is a statement where you agree to keep the survey data confidential and not share it with anyone without permission.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is an electronic adaptation of a traditional paper-based form, and your responses will be used for the purpose of the study.
