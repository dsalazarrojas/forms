# Retail Customer Brand Loyalty Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on customer loyalty to a retail store or brand. It helps to assess whether customers visit the store, are satisfied with their experience, intend to refer others, and have a positive experience with customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select all that apply from the options provided for each question, or enter your thoughts in the text fields. If a question is not applicable, you can choose "No" or leave the text field blank. Required fields are not marked as such, but are used to get a general idea of customer behavior and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Retail Customer Loyalty
* **retail_customer_loyalty (Yes/No)**: Please answer "Yes" if you identify as a loyal customer to this brand. This means you regularly visit the store, buy products, or recommend it to others.
### Customer Satisfaction
* **customer_satisfaction (text field)**: Enter a brief description of your overall satisfaction with the brand, such as "Good", "Excellent", or "Neutral".
### Referral Intent
* **referral_intent (Yes/No)**: Please answer "Yes" if you intend to refer this brand to others. This means you would recommend it to friends, family, or online communities.
### Store Visit
* **store_visit (number)**: If you have visited the store, please enter the number of times you have visited in the last month/year.
### Customer Experience
* **customer_experience (Yes/No)**: Please answer "Yes" if you have had a positive experience with the brand, such as good customer service or high-quality products.
### Customer Service
* **customer_service (text field)**: Enter a brief description of your experience with customer service, such as "Friendly and helpful" or "Improvable".
