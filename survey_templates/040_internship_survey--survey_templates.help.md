# Internship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Survey is designed to collect feedback from interns about their experiences during the internship program. Your responses will help us understand how we can improve the program to better meet the needs and expectations of future interns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
4. If you have any other comments or concerns, please use the space provided at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What did you do at your internship?** (`internship_survey`, text, required: false)
	+ Please describe your role and responsibilities during the internship.
* **How did you hear about the internship opportunity?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Please select how you heard about the internship opportunity:
		- Social Media
		- Referrals
		- Company Website
		- Job Boards
		- Other (please specify in the space provided)
* **What was the main reason you chose this internship?** (`what_was_main_reason`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Please select the main reason you chose this internship:
		- Compensation
		- Learning Opportunity
		- Work-Life Balance
		- Company Culture
		- Career Advancement
		- Other (please specify in the space provided)
* **What was the most enjoyable part of your internship?** (`what_was_most_enjoyable`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Please select all parts that you enjoyed during your internship:
		- Learning
		- Work-Life Balance
		- Compensation
		- Company Culture
		- Career Advancement
* **What was the least enjoyable part of your internship?** (`what_was_least_enjoyable`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Please select all parts that you did not enjoy during your internship:
		- Boredom
		- Unhelpful Colleagues
		- Lack of Clear Expectations
		- Poor Management
		- Other (please specify in the space provided)
* **Did you feel that your internship prepared you for your future career?** (`what_was_least_enjoyable`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Please select how you perceive the impact of your internship on your future career:
		- 'Yes'
		- 'No'
