## Purpose

This form is intended to gather feedback from members of the Business School's Advisory Committee, assessing the quality and effectiveness of the program. It will help the committee members provide insights on the relevance and currency of the course material, the preparedness of graduates for the industry, and their own engagement and satisfaction with the committee's work.

This form ensures that the committee's feedback is consistent and well-informed, allowing for data-driven decision-making and improvements to the program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please read each section carefully and provide your honest feedback. 

1. Respondent Information: Please provide your name and contact information.
2. Role on Advisory Committee: Describe your position or title on the committee.
3. Company or Organization: Specify your business affiliation.
4. Years Serving on Committee: If applicable, provide the duration of your service on the committee.
5. Program Quality Assessment: Evaluate the relevance and currency of the course material. 
6. Critical Skills Assessment: Assess the emphasis on technical and soft skills.
7. Committee Effectiveness: Evaluate the adequacy of meeting frequency and the committee's overall impact on school direction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: A text field for you to enter your full name.
* **Role on Advisory Committee**: A text field for you to describe your position or title on the committee.
* **Company or Organization**: A text field for you to specify your business affiliation.
* **Years Serving on Committee**: A numeric field for you to input the duration of your service on the committee.
* **Relevance of Program Content to Industry**: A dropdown field with options for you to evaluate the relevance of the course material.
* **Currency of Course Material**: A dropdown field for you to evaluate if the course content is up-to-date with industry trends.
* **Graduate Preparedness for Industry**: A dropdown field for you to assess the preparedness of graduates for industry roles.
* **Emphasis on Technical Skills**: A dropdown field for you to evaluate the emphasis on technical skills in the program.
* **Emphasis on Soft Skills**: A dropdown field for you to evaluate the emphasis on soft skills in the program.
* **Critical Missing Skills**: A text field for you to list any skills that students lack.
* **Adequacy of Meeting Frequency**: A dropdown field for you to evaluate the adequacy of meeting frequency for the committee.
* **Committee Impact on School Direction**: A dropdown field for you to evaluate the committee's impact on school direction.
* **Your Engagement Satisfaction**: A dropdown field for you to rate your engagement satisfaction with the committee's work.
* **Recommended Program Improvements**: A text field for you to suggest any improvements to the program.
* **Opportunities for Industry Partnership**: A text field for you to suggest ways to deepen industry engagement.
* **Additional Feedback**: A text field for you to provide any other thoughts or suggestions.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide constructive feedback to help improve the program.
* Please answer each question carefully, considering your own experience and expertise.
* If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the "Additional Feedback" field.
