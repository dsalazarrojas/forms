# Face Mask Requirement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Face Mask Requirement Survey is a template designed to gather information on face mask requirements. It's meant for an unknown audience and is likely used by a company or organization to assess or establish policies related to face masks.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by answering each question on each page to the best of your knowledge.
- Make sure to select all applicable answers, if you have the option to do so.
- Fill out all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate submission.
- Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Face Mask Requirement** (`face_mask_requirement_survey_first_page`, text, required: false): This is the introduction page of the survey, and it does not ask for any specific information. It's just a placeholder for the first page of the form.
* **Do you require face masks on this site/property?** (`face_mask_requirement_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Here, you'll select all applicable answers. Select 'Yes' for locations where face masks are required, and 'No' for those where they are not.
* **Do you provide face masks on site/property?** (`face_mask_requirement_3`, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' if face masks are available for employees or visitors, or 'No' if they are not.
* **Number of face masks provided** (`face_mask_requirement_4`, number, required: false): Enter the number of face masks provided, if any.
* **Date face masks were last restocked/ordered** (`face_mask_requirement_5`, date, required: false): Record the date when face masks were last restocked or ordered.
