# Beverage Flavor Satisfaction Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback on the satisfaction of a specific beverage flavor. It is essential to provide honest and accurate responses to help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in your personal details in Section 1.
3. Select the relevant date from the calendar provided or type in a date in the format MM/DD/YY.
4. Choose your primary selection from the options provided.
5. Enter a whole number for quantity or rating, or leave it blank if not applicable.
6. Provide any additional details about your experience.
7. Select all that apply in the agreement section.
8. Enter an estimated value if required, or leave it blank if not applicable.
9. Choose your preferred time if scheduling is required, or leave it blank if not applicable.
10. Add any final comments or notes in the closing remarks section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your legal full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, including country code if outside local region.
* **Date**: Select the relevant date or type in a date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Primary Selection**: Choose the option that best applies (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Quantity or Rating**: Enter a whole number for quantity or rating.
* **Additional Details**: Provide any additional information about your experience.
* **Agreement**: Select all that apply:
	+ I agree
	+ I need follow up
	+ Not applicable
* **Estimated Value**: Enter an estimated value if required, or leave it blank if not applicable.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose your preferred time if scheduling is required, or leave it blank if not applicable.
* **Final Comments or Notes**: Add any final comments or notes about your experience.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Use the format MM/DD/YY for dates.
* Enter whole numbers for quantity or rating.
* Choose all that apply in the agreement section.
