# swot_analysis_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the creation of a SWOT analysis for an entity, such as an organization, project, or business. The SWOT analysis is a structured evaluation tool that identifies internal and external factors that may impact its success.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question thoroughly, providing detailed explanations for the internal and external factors.
2. Ensure that each answer is relevant to the specific entity being analyzed.
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions, refer to the SWOT analysis framework for guidance.
4. Once you've completed the form, review your responses to ensure that they accurately reflect the entity's current and potential state.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **swot_analysis** (Required): A brief description of the entity being analyzed, including its mission, vision, and goals.
* **internal_strengths** (Required): Identify the entity's internal strengths, such as its resources, skills, and capabilities.
* **internal_weaknesses** (Required): Identify the entity's internal weaknesses, such as its limitations, challenges, or areas for improvement.
* **external_opportunities** (Required): Describe the external opportunities that the entity may capitalize on, such as new markets, partnerships, or technologies.
* **external_threats** (Required): Identify the external threats that the entity may face, such as market trends, competitor actions, or regulatory requirements.
* **key_factors** (Optional): Additional information or considerations that may impact the entity's success.
* **comments** (Optional): Any further remarks or observations that may be relevant to the analysis.
