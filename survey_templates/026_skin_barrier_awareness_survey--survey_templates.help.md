# Skin Barrier Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess individual skin barrier awareness, importance, frequency, and concerns. It is intended to be a brief assessment tool for users to provide insights into their skin health.

## How To Complete This Form
This form has [7] pages, and you can complete it at your own pace. Each page has its own questions or sections. Please read each page carefully and answer all questions that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction 
* **Introduction** (Note): This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and instructions.

### Skin Barrier Importance 
* **Skin Barrier Importance** (Select Multiple): This question asks you to select all the skin barriers that you find important. You can choose from:
	+ Dryness
	+ Acne
	+ Sensitive skin
	+ Rosacea
	+ Other (if none of the above apply, type it in the "Other Skin Barriers" field)

### Other Skin Barriers 
* **Other Skin Barriers** (Text): If you selected "Other" from the previous question, provide a brief description of what other skin barriers you consider important.

### How Often Do You 
* **How Often Do You** (Number): This question asks you to report how often you experience skin problems. Please enter a number that best represents your answer.

### Other Skin Frequency 
* **Other Skin Frequency** (Text): If your answer to the previous question is not one of the options provided, please describe how often you experience skin problems.

### Concerns About Skin 
* **Concerns About Skin** (Select One): This question asks you to identify your main concerns about your skin. You can choose from:
	+ Skin problems
	+ Skin issues
	+ Other skin (if none of the above apply, type it in the "Other Skin Concerns" field)

### Other Skin Concerns 
* **Other Skin Concerns** (Text): If you selected "Other" from the previous question, provide a brief description of your concerns.
