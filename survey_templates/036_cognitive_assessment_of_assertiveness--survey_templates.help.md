# Cognitive Assessment Of Assertiveness - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cognitive Assessment Of Assertiveness form is designed to measure an individual's assertiveness, which is a quality that enables them to express their thoughts, feelings, and needs in a clear and respectful manner. Assertiveness is a key component of effective communication and relationships. This form assesses various aspects of assertiveness, including the ability to express opinions, say no, stand up for oneself, respect others' boundaries, and communicate needs clearly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Respondent Information**: Please enter your full name and email address.
2. Complete the assertiveness assessments by selecting the response that best matches your feelings and behaviors. Use the rating system:
	* 1 - Strongly disagree
	* 2 - Disagree
	* 3 - Neutral
	* 4 - Agree
	* 5 - Strongly agree
3. Answer questions about your assertiveness strengths and areas for improvement.

* **I respect others while maintaining my boundaries** (`q5_respect_others`, `select_one`, required): Rate how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **I can give compliments to others comfortably** (`q6_compliments`, `select_one`, required): Rate how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **I can ask for and accept constructive feedback** (`q7_feedback`, `select_one`, required): Rate how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **I voice my concerns clearly when needed** (`q8_concerns`, `select_one`, required): Rate how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **I can admit my mistakes without defensiveness** (`q9_mistakes`, `select_one`, required): Rate how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement.

* **My assertiveness strengths** (`strengths`, `text`, optional): If you wish, describe your assertiveness strengths.
* **Areas for assertiveness improvement** (`areas_improve`, `text`, optional): If you wish, describe areas where you would like to improve assertiveness.

## Tips

* Be honest and rate your feelings and behaviors accurately.
* Use the rating system consistently throughout the form.
* Take your time and answer each question carefully.
* If you're unsure about a question, try to rate based on your general tendencies.
