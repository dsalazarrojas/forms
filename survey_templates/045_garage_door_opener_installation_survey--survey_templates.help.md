# Garage Door Opener Installation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their garage door opener installation needs. This survey will help the service company to better understand the customer's preferences and requirements for the installation, ensuring a more efficient and personalized service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details, including your name and contact information.
2. Choose the type of garage door opener you are interested in (Openers, Accessories, or Other).
3. Select your preferred installation time from the available options (Monday to Sunday).
4. Specify which features you would like to have in your garage door opener (Automatic Open/Close, Key Remote, Keyless Access, Keyless Code, Wi-Fi, or Smart Home Integration).
5. If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them in the "Customer Comments" field.
6. Provide the name of the service company you are interested in working with (if applicable).
7. Enter your contact number and email address for follow-up communication (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information to help the service company get in touch with you.
* **Door Type** (`door_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of garage door opener you are interested in:
	+ Openers: This is a standard garage door opener that can be used for most garage doors.
	+ Accessories: This is a specialized garage door opener that can be used for specific types of garage doors.
	+ Other: If you have a different type of garage door opener in mind, please specify it here.
* **Preferred Install Time** (`preferred_install_time`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred day for the installation:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **Feature Preferences** (`feature_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the features you would like to have in your garage door opener:
	+ Automatic Open/Close: Automatically open and close your garage door with a remote or smartphone app.
	+ Key Remote: Use a key to control your garage door opener.
	+ Keyless Access: Use a code to control your garage door opener without a key.
	+ Keyless Code: Use a code to control your garage door opener without a key.
	+ Wi-Fi: Connect your garage door opener to your home network for remote access.
	+ Smart Home Integration: Integrate your garage door opener with your smart home system for seamless control.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them here.
* **Service Company** (`service_company`, text, optional): If you are interested in working with a specific service company, please provide their name here.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for follow-up communication with the service company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up communication with the service company.
