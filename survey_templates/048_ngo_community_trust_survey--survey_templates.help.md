# NGO Community Trust Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NGO Community Trust Survey is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the services offered by the NGO. The survey aims to understand the level of trust users have in the organization, the frequency of service use, and contact details for follow-up communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Fill out the form only once you have actually been contacted by the NGO and have had a service interaction.
3. For each field, select or type the information that best reflects your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What services do you offer? (service_provided)**: Select one of the two options: 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have been offered services by the NGO.
* **How often do you use our services? (frequency_of_service)**: Select all the options that apply to your usage frequency.
* **How much do you trust us? (trust_level)**: Select the level of trust you feel towards the NGO from 'Very High' to 'Low'.
* **What can we do to improve? (improvement_suggestions)**: Enter any suggestions or comments about how the NGO can improve its services.
* **What are your thoughts on our services? (feedback_on_services)**: Enter any feedback or comments about the NGO's services.
* **Do you have an email address to contact you? (email_response)**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted by email.
* **Do you have a phone number we can contact you with? (phone_number)**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **When were you contacted by our team? (date_time_response)**: Select the date and time of the last contact with the NGO.
* **What time did you last contact us? (time_response)**: Select the time of the last contact with the NGO.
* **What date did you last contact us? (date_of_last_contact)**: Select the date of the last contact with the NGO.
* **Time Of Last Contact (time_of_last_contact)**: Select the time of the last contact with the NGO.
* **How did you contact us? (contact_method)**: Select the method of contact you used (Phone, Email, In-person, Online).
* **Please provide more details about your last contact with us (last_contact_details)**: Enter any additional comments or details about the last contact with the NGO.
* **Email Response2 (email_response2)**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted by email.
* **Phone Number2 (phone_number2)**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **How did you contact us this time? (contact_method2)**: Select the method of contact you used (Phone, Email, In-person, Online).
* **Last Contact Details2 (last_contact_details2)**: Enter any additional comments or details about the last contact with the NGO.
* **Do you have any suggestions on how we can improve our service? (contact_us)**: Select all the options that apply to your suggestions.
* **Would you like to be contacted for follow-up questions? (follow_up)**: Select 'Yes' if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **How would you like to be contacted? (follow_up_contact_method)**: Select 'Yes' if you want to be contacted by phone or email.
* **Would you like to be contacted by phone or email? (contact_us_for_follow_up)**: Select the preferred method of contact.
* **What are your next steps with our organization? (next_steps)**: Select all the options that apply to your next steps.
* **What are your more details on next steps with us? (next_steps_details)**: Enter any additional comments or details about your next steps with the NGO.
* **Additional contact details for follow up (follow_up_details)**: Enter any additional comments or details for follow-up contacts.
* **Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey (survey_completion)**: Enter any closing comments or feedback.
