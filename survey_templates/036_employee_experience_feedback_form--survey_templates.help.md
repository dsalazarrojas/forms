# Employee Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee experience feedback form is a tool used to gather feedback from employees about their experience with the company, helping identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the **Company Name** field with the name of the company you are providing feedback on.
2. In the **Manager Name** field, please enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
3. Answer the questions about your feedback on various aspects of the work environment, such as **Company Culture**, **Communication with Management**, **Communication with Colleagues**, **Time Management**, **Sustainability**, and **Growth Opportunities**. These questions are optional and you can choose not to answer them if you do not have any feedback.
4. Select your level of **Overall Satisfaction** with the company, using the drop-down menu.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the **Open Comments** field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (Required): Enter the name of the company you are providing feedback on.
* **Manager Name** (Required): Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Feedback on Manager** (Optional): Share your feedback on your manager's performance and behavior.
* **Feedback on Colleagues** (Optional): Share your feedback on your colleagues' performance and behavior.
* **Work-Life Balance** (Required): Select the level of work-life balance you experience at the company, using the drop-down menu.
* **Company Culture** (Optional): Select the level of company culture, using the multi-select menu.
* **Feedback on Workload** (Optional): Share your feedback on your workload and task management.
* **Feedback on Equipment** (Optional): Share your feedback on the company's equipment and resources.
* **Feedback on Training** (Optional): Share your feedback on the training and development opportunities provided by the company.
* **Feedback on Supervision** (Optional): Share your feedback on your supervision and guidance from management.
* **Feedback on Communication** (Optional): Share your feedback on the communication between you and your manager or colleagues.
* **Feedback on Teamwork** (Optional): Share your feedback on your teamwork and collaboration with colleagues.
* **Feedback on Time Management** (Optional): Share your feedback on time management and scheduling.
* **Feedback on Safety** (Optional): Share your feedback on the company's safety policies and procedures.
* **Feedback on Wellness** (Optional): Share your feedback on the company's wellness programs and policies.
* **Feedback on Health** (Optional): Share your feedback on the company's health-related policies and procedures.
* **Feedback on Environment** (Optional): Share your feedback on the company's work environment and facilities.
* **Feedback on Job Security** (Optional): Share your feedback on job security and career growth opportunities.
* **Feedback on Growth Opportunities** (Optional): Share your feedback on career growth and development opportunities.
* **Feedback on Diversity and Inclusion** (Optional): Share your feedback on diversity and inclusion in the workplace.
* **Feedback on Sustainability** (Optional): Share your feedback on the company's sustainability initiatives and practices.
* **Feedback on Communication with Management** (Optional): Share your feedback on communication with management.
* **Feedback on Communication with Colleagues** (Optional): Share your feedback on communication with colleagues.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Required): Select the level of overall satisfaction with the company, using the drop-down menu.
* **Open Comments** (Optional): Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the company.

## Tips
* Please be honest and open in your feedback, as it will help us identify areas for improvement.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please share them in the **Open Comments** field.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, please contact the HR or management team.
