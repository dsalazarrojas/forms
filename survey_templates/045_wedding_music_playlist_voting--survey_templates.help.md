# Wedding Music Playlist Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your personal preferences for wedding music. Your input will help us create a tailored music playlist for the wedding celebration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your full name, email address and phone number. This information will help us get in touch with you in case we need to follow up on any questions or concerns.
2. Choose one or more genres of music that you enjoy listening to:
	+ Pop
	+ Rock
	+ Jazz
	+ Classical
	+ R&B
	+ Hiphop
3. Select a song title that you would like to vote for, if you have one in mind. You can also leave this field blank if you don't have a specific song in mind.
4. If you'd like to vote for a specific artist, please let us know their name.
5. Choose the decade of music that you prefer:
	+ 1980s
	+ 1990s
	+ 2000s
6. You can provide a vote for a song by selecting a time duration, e.g., 2 minutes or 4 minutes.
7. Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you.
8. If you'd like to provide any additional comments or suggestions, please use the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Music Genre**: Select one or more genres that you enjoy listening to.
* **Song Title**: If you have a specific song in mind that you'd like to vote for, enter its title.
* **Artist Name**: If you have a specific artist in mind, enter their name.
* **Music Year**: Select the decade of music that you prefer.
* **Song Vote**: If you'd like to vote for a song, select a duration to indicate how much you'd like it to be played.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number, if you'd like us to be able to reach you.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Tips
* Please note that you can only vote for songs that are already in the wedding planner's database.
* You can also leave any fields blank if you don't have specific information for that field.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to us at [insert contact email or phone number].
