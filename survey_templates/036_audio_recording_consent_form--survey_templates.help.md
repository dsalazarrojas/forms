# Audio Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for audio recordings of participants. The form is designed to ensure that participants understand the purpose and usage of their recorded data, and that they are aware of their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Full Name** in the "Participant Full Name" field. This is the name you would like to be identified by in the recording.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field. This will be used to contact you for any further information or updates.
3. Choose the **Primary Purpose of Recording** from the dropdown list. This will help the recorder understand the purpose of the recording and ensure that it aligns with your needs.
4. Select the **Recording Method** from the dropdown list. This will help the recorder know how to conduct the recording.
5. Choose the **Recording Date** for the session. This will help ensure that the recording is accurately dated.
6. Select all the **Usage Permissions** that you grant for the recording. These include Internal Organization Use, Educational Purposes, Public Website or Social Media, Commercial Sale or Distribution, and Future Projects/Archiving.
7. Decide how you would like to be identified in the **Disclosure of Identity**. You can choose to use your Full Name, First Name Only, or Remain Anonymous (Pseudonym).
8. Acknowledge that you understand you can **Revoke Your Consent** in writing at any time before publication.
9. Enter your **Participant Digital Signature (Type Name)** in the "Participant Digital Signature" field.
10. Finally, enter the **Date of Signature** for your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be identified in the recording.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for any further information or updates.
* **Primary Purpose of Recording** (`recording_purpose`, select_one, required): Choose the primary purpose of the recording from the dropdown list.
* **Date of Recording Session** (`recording_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the recording session.
* **Recording Method** (`recording_method`, select_one, required): Choose the recording method from the dropdown list.
* **Usage Permissions** (`usage_permissions`, select_multiple, required): Select all the usage permissions that you grant for the recording.
* **Disclosure of Identity** (`identity_disclosure`, select_one, required): Decide how you would like to be identified in the recording.
* **Revoke Your Consent** (`revocation_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand you can revoke your consent in writing at any time before publication.
* **Participant Digital Signature (Type Name)** (`digital_signature`, text, required): Enter your participant digital signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it. Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the recorder.
* Once you have submitted the form, you can revoke your consent in writing at any time before publication.
