# Retail Product Satisfaction Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Product Satisfaction Assessment Form is a survey designed to gather information about customers' experiences with retail products. It aims to collect data on product satisfaction, customer feedback, and improvement suggestions. This form will help us understand customer needs and preferences, enabling us to improve product development and customer service.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your honest feedback.
2. Select your answers for each section from the provided options.
3. Add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional comments" section.
4. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product id** (`product_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Product rating** (`product_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the product (1-5).
* **Reason for return** (`reason_for_return`, text, optional): If you have returned the product, describe the reason.
* **Improvement suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Suggest ways to improve the product.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product (1-5).
* **Would recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_multiple, optional): Would you recommend this product? (True/False).
* **Purchase date** (`purchase_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of purchase.
* **Purchase time** (`purchase_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of purchase.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Name of the person you would like to contact for feedback.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Phone number of the contact person.
* **Order id** (`order_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of your order.
* **Order date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your order.
* **Product id** (`product_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the product.
* **Customer id** (`customer_id`, number, optional): Enter your customer ID.
* **Purchase value** (`purchase_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of your purchase.
* **Purchase location** (`purchase_location`, text, optional): Describe where you purchased the product.
* **Product category** (`product_category`, text, optional): Enter the category of the product.
* **Product name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product.
* **Purchase channel** (`purchase_channel`, select_multiple, optional): How did you purchase the product? (Online/Offline/Store/Other).
* **Product type** (`product_type`, select_multiple, optional): What type of product is it? (Hardware/Software/Service/Other).
* **Purchase method** (`purchase_method`, select_multiple, optional): How did you pay for the product? (Payment/Non-Payment/Trade/Other).
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions.
