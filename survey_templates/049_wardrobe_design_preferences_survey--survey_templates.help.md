# Wardrobe Design Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wardrobe Design Preferences Survey is a tool used to gather information about clients' design preferences for a specific project. This survey aims to collect their opinions on the client style, space, and design details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the client style on Page 1.
2. Enter the client space (if applicable) on Page 1.
3. Choose the design details that apply to you on Page 3.
4. Select the budget for your project on Page 4 (if applicable).
5. Choose your preferred style on Page 5.
6. Select the location of the wardrobe on Page 6.
7. Enter your contact information on Page 7 (if applicable).
8. Choose the contact method you prefer on Page 7.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Style** (`client_style`, text, required): This field collects the client's style preference. Enter your personal style preference as it will influence the design of your wardrobe.
- **Client Space** (`client_space`, number, optional): If you have a specific space constraint, enter the square footage of the space where the wardrobe will be installed.
- **Design Details** (`design_details`, select_multiple, required): Select all the design details that you want to incorporate in your wardrobe design. These can include things like color scheme, material selection, and hardware options.
- **Budget** (`budget_details`, number, optional): If you have a specific budget in mind, enter it here. This will help us understand your financial constraints.
- **Preferred Style** (`preferred_style`, select_one, required): Choose the style of your wardrobe from the options provided. This will give us an idea of your personal taste.
- **Wardrobe Location** (`wardrobe_location`, select_one, required): Select the location where you will be placing the wardrobe. This will help us tailor the design according to your space constraints.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, optional): If you have any contact information, enter it here so we can get in touch with you if needed.
- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e-mail, phone, or in-person) for any further discussion.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form to ensure we can provide you with the best results.
- If you have any questions or clarifications, feel free to ask us for help.
- We will use the information collected from this survey to design a wardrobe that fits your needs and style.
