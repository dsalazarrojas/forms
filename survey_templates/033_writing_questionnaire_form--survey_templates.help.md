# Writing Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their writing habits, progress, goals, and challenges. The data collected will help us better understand the writing habits and needs of our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Navigate through the pages using the navigation buttons.
3. Fill out each page according to the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Writing Habits** (`writing_habits`, `note`, required: false): Answer this question by describing your current writing habits, such as how often you write, where you write, and any other relevant details.
* **Writing Progress** (`writing_progress`, `number`, required: false): Please rate your current writing progress on a scale of 1-10.
* **Goals And Aspirations** (`goals_and_aspirations`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose one or more options that best answer your question.
	+ Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to the questions about your writing goals and aspirations.
* **Challenges And Obstacles** (`challenges_and_obstacles`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the answer that best describes the challenges or obstacles you face when writing.
* **Support System** (`support_system`, `text`, required: false): Please describe the system or resources that provide you support when writing.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `date`, required: false): Provide the date of your last submission or the date by which you plan to submit your work.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false): Please specify the time of day you plan to submit your work.
* **Review** (`review`, `number`, required: false): Rate your current writing review status on a scale of 1-10.

Note: The optional fields are not required to be filled out.
