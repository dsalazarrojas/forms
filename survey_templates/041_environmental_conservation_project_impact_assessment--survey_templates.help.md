# environmentalConservationProjectImpactAssessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Environmental Conservation Project Impact Assessment form is designed to gather information about a conservation project's objectives, goals, and location. This form is used to assess the impact of conservation projects on the environment and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the conservation project in the first field.
2. Describe the objective of the project in the second field.
3. Provide the location of the conservation project in the third field.
4. Enter the location of the project in the conservation project location field.
5. If applicable, provide additional information about the project's methodology.
6. Enter a brief description of the conservation objective in the remaining fields, each with a unique label (e.g., conservation objective description, conservation objective description 2, etc.).
