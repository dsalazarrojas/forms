# Newborn Photography Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a "Newborn Photography Survey" used to collect information from parents or guardians about their newborn baby's photography session.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please complete this form carefully to ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
2.  Select the options that best match your preferences for the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`): Select the session preferences that you would like to have during the photography session. Choose from the available options to indicate your preferences.
*   **Photographers** (`photographer_preferences`): Choose your preferred photographers for the session.
*   **Parent Details** (`parent_details`): Enter the name of the parent or guardian who will be present during the session.
*   **Studio Policy** (`studio_policy`): Indicate your understanding of our studio policy regarding the usage of the photographs.
*   **Consent** (`consent`): Confirm your consent for us to take and use your photographs.
*   **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian who will be receiving our communications.
*   **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian who will be contacted regarding the session.
*   **Note** (`note`): Add any additional notes or comments that you would like to share with us.
*   **Category** (`category`): Indicate if the session is for a specific category (e.g., family, baby, etc.).
*   **Tags** (`tags`): Select the tags that best describe your session.
*   **Created At** (`created_at`): This field is automatically populated and should not be modified.
*   **Updated At** (`updated_at`): This field is automatically populated and should not be modified.
*   **Created By** (`created_by`): This field is automatically populated and should not be modified.
*   **Updated By** (`updated_by`): This field is automatically populated and should not be modified.
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
*   **Parent Email** (`parent_email`): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
*   **Parent 2 Name** (`parent_name_2`): Enter the name of the second parent or guardian (if applicable).
*   **Parent 2 Email** (`parent_email_2`): Enter the email address of the second parent or guardian (if applicable).

## Tips
*   Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Be aware that some fields are not required, but please fill them out for completeness.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to us for clarification.
*   We will use this information to provide a smooth and enjoyable experience for the baby's photography session.
