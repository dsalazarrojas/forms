# Member Facility Satisfaction Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Member Facility Satisfaction Report Form is designed to collect information about your experiences and perceptions during your visit to the facility. This form will ask you to provide honest and constructive feedback on several aspects of your visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Visit Date" field with the date of your recent visit to the facility.
2. Enter your "Member Name" in the provided field.
3. Fill in the "Facility Name" field with the name of the facility you visited.
4. Rate the cleanliness of the facility by selecting one of the three options: Very Good, Fair, or Poor.
5. Rate the overall quality of service at the facility by selecting one of the three options: Very Good, Fair, or Poor.
6. Indicate whether you were satisfied with your visit to the facility by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
7. Decide if you would like to receive a discount or promotion at the facility in the future by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
8. If you have any comments or suggestions for the facility, you can provide them in the text field "Do you have any comments about your visit?"

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit Date**: The date of your recent visit to the facility.
* **Member Name**: Your full name or identifier as a member.
* **Facility Name**: The name of the facility where you visited.
* **How would you rate the cleanliness of the facility?**: Rate the cleanliness of the facility as Very Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of service at the facility?**: Rate the overall quality of service as Very Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Were you satisfied with your visit to the facility?**: Select 'Yes' if you were satisfied, 'No' otherwise.
* **Would you like to receive a discount or promotion at the facility in the future?**: Select 'Yes' if you would like a discount or promotion, 'No' otherwise.
* **Do you have any comments about your visit?**: If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you have been assigned a tool or equipment during your visit, enter its name.
* **Created**: The date and time this form was created.
* **Updated**: The date and time this form was last updated.

## Tips

* Please answer all required questions honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help improve the quality of service at the facility.
