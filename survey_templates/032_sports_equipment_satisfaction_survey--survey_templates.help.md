# Sports Equipment Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey template is designed to gather information about user experience with sports equipment. It is intended for users who have used sports equipment and are willing to share their experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of equipment you used from the dropdown list.
2. Rate the quality, durability, and satisfaction with the equipment.
3. Answer whether you would recommend the equipment to others.
4. Enter any additional comments you have about your experience with the equipment.
5. Provide your contact information (phone number or email) so we can follow up with you for further questions.
6. Select the type of sports you are interested in (e.g., cycling, running, swimming, yoga).
7. Rate the condition of the equipment you used.
8. Indicate your age and gender.
9. Enter any information about where you purchased the equipment.
10. Enter the cost of the equipment.
11. Report how often you use the equipment.
12. Select where you purchased the equipment.
13. Indicate if the equipment had a warranty.
14. Report if you returned or repaired the equipment.
15. Select how often you use the equipment.
