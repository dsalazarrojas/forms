# incident_complaint_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Complaint Form Template is used to collect detailed information about incidents that occur within the organization. It helps to identify potential issues, prevent future occurrences, and ensure accurate reporting of incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information of the person reporting the incident.
2. Describe the incident in detail.
3. Specify the location where the incident occurred.
4. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Accident, Near Miss, Harassment, etc.).
5. Provide any additional incident details.
6. Identify the employee who reported the incident and their manager.
7. Record the date and time the incident was reported.
8. Upload any relevant images, videos, or audio recordings related to the incident.
9. Attach any relevant documents related to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info (Page 1)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to allow us to contact you regarding this incident.
* **Incident Description (Page 1)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any witnesses or injuries.
* **Location (Page 1)**: Specify the exact location where the incident occurred, including any relevant details about the scene.
* **Incident Type (Page 2)**: Choose the type of incident that occurred from the options provided (e.g., Accident, Near Miss, Harassment, etc.).
* **Incident Details (Page 2)**: Provide any additional information about the incident, including any supporting evidence or context.
* **Reporting Employee (Page 2)**: Enter your name and employee ID number to identify yourself as the reporting party.
* **Reporting Manager (Page 3)**: Enter the name and employee ID number of the manager who reported the incident.
* **Reporting Date and Time (Page 3)**: Enter the date and time you reported the incident.
* **Witness Info (Page 3)**: Enter any information about witnesses, including their names, titles, or departments.
* **Incident Image (Page 4)**: Upload any relevant images of the incident scene or damage.
* **Incident Video (Page 4)**: Upload any relevant videos related to the incident.
* **Incident Audio (Page 5)**: Upload any relevant audio recordings related to the incident.
