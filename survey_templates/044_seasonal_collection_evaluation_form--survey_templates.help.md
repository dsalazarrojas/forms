# Seasonal Collection Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Collection Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the performance of seasonal collections, providing an assessment of their overall quality, features, and suitability. This form is essential for identifying strengths and weaknesses, making informed decisions, and driving improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the provided options for each question and select the most accurate response based on your honest evaluation.
2. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields (marked as `required: true`) are completed.
3. Use the options provided for each field as a starting point; you can also add additional comments or details as needed.
4. If you're unsure about an option or have a different response, please use the `Comment` field to provide more context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seasonal Collection Evaluation** (`seasonal_collection_evaluation`, `select_multiple`, `required: true`):
	+ Select one or multiple reasons why the seasonal collection meets or does not meet the expected standards.
	+ Possible options: `Yes`, `No`.
* **Rating (1-5)** (`rating`, `number`, `required: false`):
	+ Evaluate the overall rating of the seasonal collection, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `text`, `required: false`):
	+ Provide additional comments or details about the seasonal collection, if needed.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, `text`, `required: false`):
	+ Suggest areas for improvement or changes to enhance the seasonal collection.
* **Improvement Notes** (`improvement_notes`, `text`, `required: true`):
	+ Record any improvements or changes recommended for the seasonal collection.
* **Product Line** (`product_line`, `select_one`, `required: false`):
	+ Select the product line associated with the seasonal collection.
* **Season** (`season`, `select_one`, `required: false`):
	+ Choose the season the collection belongs to (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter).
* **Year** (`year`, `select_one`, `required: false`):
	+ Select the collection's year of release.
* **Designer** (`designer`, `select_one`, `required: false`):
	+ Choose the designer responsible for the collection.
* **Collection Name** (`collection_name`, `text`, `required: false`):
	+ Enter the name of the collection.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, `required: false`):
	+ Select the category of the seasonal collection.
* **Price Point** (`price_point`, `select_one`, `required: false`):
	+ Choose the price range of the collection.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`):
	+ Indicate whether the collection includes products or not.
* **Product Color** (`product_color`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`):
	+ Check if the collection features products with a specific color.
* **Material** (`material`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`):
	+ Indicate if the collection includes products made of specific materials.
* **Fabric Content** (`fabric_content`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`):
	+ Check if the collection includes products made of specific fabrics or materials.
* **Design Description** (`design_description`, `text`, `required: false`):
	+ Provide a brief description of the collection's design.
* **Overall Feeling** (`overall_feeling`, `select_one`, `required: false`):
	+ Evaluate the overall feeling of the collection (Good, Bad, Average).
* **Rating 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7** (`rating_2`, `rating_3`, `rating_4`, `rating_5`, `rating_6`, `rating_7`, `number`, `required: false`):
	+ Evaluate the collection's performance across different criteria (Rating 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7).
