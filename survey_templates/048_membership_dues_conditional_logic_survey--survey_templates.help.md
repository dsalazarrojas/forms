# Membership Dues Conditional Logic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from members about their membership dues preferences and payment methods. It helps the membership organization to understand how members want to pay their dues and when they would like to pay them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select how often you would like to pay your membership dues. Choose one or more of the options provided.
	* If you choose "Monthly", "Bi-Weekly", or "Weekly", you will be asked to choose a payment method for these frequencies.
2. Choose your preferred payment method.
	* Select one of the three options: PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check.
3. Provide feedback about your membership experience. 
	* You can share any comments or suggestions you have about your membership experience.
4. If you are a member who pays their dues on a different frequency than the ones provided, please specify this frequency in the space provided. 
	* This will help the membership organization to better understand your dues payment preferences.
5. Provide any additional comments you have about your membership experience or dues payment process.
	* This will help the membership organization to understand your needs and preferences better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Dues Frequency** (`membership_dues_frequency`, `select_multiple`): This field allows you to select how often you would like to pay your membership dues. Please choose one or more of the options provided.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`): This field allows you to choose how you would like to pay your membership dues. Please select one of the three options provided.
* **Feedback** (`membership_dues_feedback`, `text`): This field is for you to share any comments or suggestions you have about your membership experience.
* **Dues Frequency** (`membership_dues_frequency_2`, `select_one`): This field asks you to confirm your preferred dues frequency. If you didn't select "Monthly", "Bi-Weekly", or "Weekly" in the first field, please select the actual frequency you prefer here.
* **Dues Comments** (`membership_dues_comments`, `text`): This field is for you to provide any additional comments about your membership experience or dues payment process.
* **Membership Preferences** (`membership_dues_preferences`, `text`): This field is for you to share any additional information about your membership preferences.
* **Additional Comments** (`membership_dues_comments_2`, `text`): This field is for you to provide any additional comments you have about your membership experience or dues payment process.

Please note that all fields are optional unless otherwise specified as required. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
