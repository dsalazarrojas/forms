# rural_healthcare_access_challenges_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on healthcare access challenges in rural areas to inform policy and program decisions that aim to improve access to healthcare in these areas.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the organization or institution that is participating in this survey.
2. Provide the name of the city where the healthcare access challenges are being reported.
3. Select the state where the location is based.
4. Enter the name of the country where the healthcare access challenges are being reported.
5. Describe the barriers that prevent people in the area from accessing healthcare services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (Organization Name, text, required): Enter the name of the organization or institution that is participating in this survey.
* **Location City** (Location City, text, required): Enter the name of the city where the healthcare access challenges are being reported.
* **Location State** (prophets_location_state, select_one, required): Select the state where the location is based.
* **Location Country** (Location Country, text, required): Enter the name of the country where the healthcare access challenges are being reported.
* **Healthcare Access Barriers** (Healthcare Access Barriers, text, required): Describe the barriers that prevent people in the area from accessing healthcare services. For example, lack of transportation, lack of insurance, or lack of availability of services.
