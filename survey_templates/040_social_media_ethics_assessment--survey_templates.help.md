# Social Media Ethics Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Ethics Assessment is a tool designed to help you gauge your understanding and behavior when interacting with social media platforms. It's a set of questions intended to evaluate your level of awareness and adherence to responsible social media usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, considering your personal experiences and practices when using social media.
2. For select multiple-choice questions, choose the best response that applies to your behavior.
3. For text-based questions, provide your thoughts or descriptions in detail.
4. Make sure to fill out all required questions (marked as "true" in the form).
