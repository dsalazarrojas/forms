# Go To Market Strategy Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Go To Market Strategy Assessment form is designed to gather information about your product's launch plan, including target market, sales strategy, sales channels, and budget. This form will help you evaluate your product's readiness for launch and provide insights into its potential success.
