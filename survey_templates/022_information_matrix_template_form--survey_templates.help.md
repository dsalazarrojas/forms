# Information Matrix Template Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about your preferences, interests, and demographics. Please answer the questions honestly to help us get a better understanding of your likes and dislikes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question with the option that best describes your preference or habit.
3. If a question is not applicable, please leave it blank or indicate that you don't have a preference.
