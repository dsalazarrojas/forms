Makeup Color Psychology Preference Survey - Help Guide
=============================================

## Purpose
The Makeup Color Psychology Preference Survey is designed to collect information about users' color preferences, specifically in the context of makeup. The primary goal of the form is to understand how users connect colors with emotions, moods, and preferences related to makeup.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the color you prefer from the options provided.
2. If you have a specific color in mind that is not listed, please provide its name in the text field.
3. Choose the mood or season that you associate with your preferred color.
4. Select the parts of the body you would prefer to use the color on.
5. Choose the shade or intensity of the color you prefer.
6. If you have a specific color in mind that does not fit the options provided, please provide its name in the text field.
7. Choose the mood or emotion that you associate with your preferred color.
8. Select the season that you prefer or associate with your preferred color.
9. Choose the color preferred for.
10. Choose the preferred color shade.
11. If you have a specific color in mind that does not fit the options provided, please provide its name in the text field.
12. Choose the mood or emotion that you associate with your preferred color.
13. Provide any additional comments or preferences related to the color you prefer for your face.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Your Preferred Color**:
	+ This question asks you to choose a color that you prefer or associate with a particular emotion or mood.
* **Color Name** (Optional):
	+ If you have a specific color in mind that is not listed, please provide its name in this text field. This will help us understand your unique preferences.
* **Other Color Name**:
	+ Please specify any other color that you prefer or associate with a particular emotion or mood.
* **Preferred Mood**:
	+ Choose the mood or emotion that you associate with your preferred color.
* **Favorite Season**:
	+ Select the season that you prefer or associate with your preferred color.
* **Color Preferred For**:
	+ Choose the parts of the body you would prefer to use the color on.
* **Preferred Color Shade**:
	+ Select the shade or intensity of the color you prefer.
* **Color Name** (Optional):
	+ If you have a specific color in mind that does not fit the options provided, please provide its name in this text field.
* **Preferred Mood** (Optional):
	+ Choose the mood or emotion that you associate with your preferred color.
* **Face**:
	+ Please provide any additional comments or preferences related to the color you prefer for your face.
