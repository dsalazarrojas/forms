# soundscape_experience_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from participants after a session, gathering information about their rating, suggestion, emotion, and other details related to the session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form on your own time and pace, as it is not mandatory.
2. Answer each question truthfully and concisely.
3. Provide detailed feedback in the note fields where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Participant Rating** (`1`, `number`, optional): Rate your experience with the session on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Emotion** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you felt during the session:
	+ Very Happy
	+ Happy
	+ Neutral
	+ Sad
	+ Unhappy
	+ Uninterested
* **Session Suggestion** (`3`, `note`, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, type them here.
* **Contact Name** (`4`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to contact us, provide your email address so we can get in touch.
* **Participant Rating** (`5`, `number`, optional): Rate how much you enjoyed the session:
* **Artist Name** (`6`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to recognize the artist who led the session, type their name here.
* **Session Facilitator** (`7`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to recognize the session facilitator, type their name here.
* **Participant Suggestion** (`8`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or ideas for improvement, type them here.
* **Organization Name** (`9`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to mention the organization hosting the session, type their name here.
* **Session Date** (`10`, `date`, optional): If you remember the session date, type it here.

## Tips
* Please be honest and detailed in your feedback, as it helps us improve our sessions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us at your convenience.
* We value your input and look forward to hearing about your experience.
