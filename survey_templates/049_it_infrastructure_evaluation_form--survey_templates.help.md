# it_infrastructure_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "it_infrastructure_evaluation_form" is designed to gather information about your company's IT infrastructure. This form will ask you to evaluate the quality and performance of different components of your infrastructure, such as hardware, software, network, and more. This information will help you identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about future infrastructure investments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and questions carefully.
2. Select the most accurate answers for each question based on your current infrastructure setup.
3. Fill out the form with your answers, one section at a time.
4. Make sure to leave comments and comments if you have any additional information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current infrastructure level?** (`infrastructure_question`):
	+ This question asks you to select the type of infrastructure you currently have (Serverless, Virtualized, Physical, or Hybrid).
* **Hardware quality and performance** (`hardware_question`):
	+ Select the level of quality and performance of your current hardware (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Software quality and performance** (`software_question`):
	+ Select the level of quality and performance of your current software (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Network quality and performance** (`network_question`):
	+ Select the level of quality and performance of your current network (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Storage quality and performance** (`storage_question`):
	+ Select the level of quality and performance of your current storage (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Security quality and performance** (`security_question`):
	+ Select the level of quality and performance of your current security (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Scalability and performance** (`scalability_question`):
	+ Select the level of scalability and performance of your current infrastructure (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Support and maintenance** (`support_question`):
	+ Select the level of support and maintenance you receive for your infrastructure (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Communication and collaboration** (`communication_question`):
	+ Select the level of communication and collaboration you experience with your team and stakeholders (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Training and documentation** (`training_question`):
	+ Select the level of training and documentation provided for your infrastructure (High, Medium, or Low).
* **What level of infrastructure do you have?** (`infrastructure_level`):
	+ Select the level of infrastructure you currently have (Serverless, Virtualized, Physical, or Hybrid).
* **Datacenter quality and performance** (`data_center_question`):
	+ Select the level of quality and performance of your current datacenter (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Backup and recovery** (`backup_question`):
	+ Select the level of backup and recovery for your infrastructure (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Monitoring and logging** (`monitoring_question`):
	+ Select the level of monitoring and logging for your infrastructure (High, Medium, or Low).
* **What is the cost of your current infrastructure?** (`cost_question`):
	+ Enter the cost of your current infrastructure (optional).
* **Do you have any comments?** (`comments`):
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your infrastructure.
* **Do you have any infrastructure comments?** (`infrastructure_comments`):
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your infrastructure.
