# APA 7 Direct Quotation Citation Guide - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information for citing direct quotations in APA 7 style. Please ensure that you have all required information before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *) with the correct information.
2. For the "Length of Quotation" field, select "Less than 40 words" or "40 words or more" to indicate the length of your quotation.
3. Type the exact direct quotation text in the "Direct Quotation Text" field.
4. If you need to introduce the quote with a signal phrase, select "Yes" in the "Signal Phrase Needed" field.
5. Indicate how you are integrating the quote into your text by selecting the corresponding option in the "Integration Type" field.
6. Specify the purpose of the citation by selecting the reason for citing the source from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (`student_name`, text, *):** Enter your full name for identification purposes only.
* **Student Email (`student_email`, email, *):** Enter your academic email address.
* **Course Name (`course_name`, text, *):** Enter the name of the course where you are using APA citation.
* **Instructor Name (`instructor_name`, text, *):** Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Author Last Name (`author_last_name`, text, *):** Enter the last name of the source author.
* **Author First Initial(s) (`author_first_initial`, text, *):** Enter the first initial(s) of the source author.
* **Publication Year (`publication_year`, number, *):** Enter the year the source was published.
* **Source Title (`source_title`, text, *):** Enter the title of the book, article, or document.
* **Page Number(s) (`page_number`, text, *):** Enter the page number(s) where the quote appears.
* **Length of Quotation (`quotation_length`, select_one, *):** Select whether your quotation is less than 40 words or 40 words or more.
* **Direct Quotation Text (`direct_quotation_text`, text, *):** Type the exact direct quotation text.
* **Signal Phrase Needed (`signal_phrase_needed`, select_one, *):** Select "Yes" if you need to introduce the quote with a signal phrase.
* **Integration Type (`integration_type`, select_one, *):** Select how you are integrating the quote into your text.
* **Purpose of Citation (`citation_purpose`, select_one, *):** Select the reason you are citing the source.

This document is a help guide for the APA 7 direct quotation citation guide form. Please ensure that you complete all required fields to obtain a properly formatted citation. If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to your instructor or a relevant authority for further clarification.
