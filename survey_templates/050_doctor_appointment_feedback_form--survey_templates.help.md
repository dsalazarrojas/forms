# Doctor Appointment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctor Appointment Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from patients about their experiences with their doctors. Your feedback will help us understand what we are doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the answer that best fits your experience with the doctor for each question.
* You can only select one option for each question.
* Make sure to complete all the required fields, but you can skip optional ones if you like.
* Once you have finished, click "Submit" to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was the quality of care provided by your doctor?** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form`): This question asks about the overall quality of care you received from the doctor. Select "Good" if you were satisfied with the care, and "Poor" if you were not.
* **How likely are you to recommend this doctor to others?** (`doctor_feedback`): This question asks if you would recommend the doctor to friends and family. Select "Yes" if you would recommend, and "No" if you would not.
* **How clean was the doctor's office?** (`doctor_office`): This question asks about the cleanliness of the doctor's office. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **How would you rate the friendliness of the staff?** (`staff_friendliness`): This question asks about the friendliness of the staff at the doctor's office. Select "Good" if they were friendly, and "Fair" if they were not.
* **How would you rate the communication style of your doctor?** (`doctor_communication`): This question asks about how well the doctor communicated with you. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **How would you rate the professionalism of your doctor?** (`doctor_professionalism`): This question asks about the level of professionalism shown by the doctor. Select "Good" if they were professional, and "Fair" if they were not.
* **How would you rate the waiting time you spent before your appointment?** (`waiting_time`): This question asks about the length of time you spent waiting before your appointment. Select "Good" if it was reasonable, and "Fair" if it was not.
* **How would you rate the availability of your doctor?** (`doctor_availability`): This question asks about how easily you were able to get an appointment with the doctor. Select "Good" if it was easy, and "Fair" if it was not.
* **How satisfied were you with your appointment?** (`overall_satisfaction`): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the appointment. Select "Yes" if you were satisfied, and "No" if you were not.
* **How long did you spend with the doctor?** (`appointment_length`): This question asks about the length of time you spent with the doctor. Enter the time in hours and minutes (e.g., 1 hour 30 minutes).
