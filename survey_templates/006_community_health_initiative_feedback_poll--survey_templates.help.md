# Community Health Initiative Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from individuals who have participated in a health initiative or program. The information collected will help us understand your experience, satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Program or Initiative Name** in the first field. This is the name of the health program you participated in.
2. Select the **Date of Participation**, which is the date you took part in the program.
3. Rate your **Overall Satisfaction Level** with the initiative by selecting one of the following options:
	* Very Satisfied
	* Satisfied
	* Neutral
	* Dissatisfied
	* Very Dissatisfied
4. Answer **Did the program meet your expectations?** by selecting one of the following options:
	* Yes, completely
	* Partially
	* No, not at all
5. If you'd like, provide a brief description of what you liked most about the program in **Most helpful aspect of the program**.
6. If you have any suggestions for areas where the program could improve, enter them in **Areas for improvement**.
7. Rate your likelihood of recommending the program by selecting one of the following options:
	* 1 (Not Likely)
	* 2
	* 3
	* 4
	* 5 (Very Likely)
8. If you'd like, suggest future health topics you'd like to learn about in **Suggestions for future health topics**.
9. If you'd like to be contacted for future programs, select **Yes** and enter your **Contact Email or Phone**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program or Initiative Name (Program_name)**: The name of the health program you participated in.
* **Date of Participation (participation_date)**: The date you took part in the program.
* **Overall Satisfaction Level (satisfaction_level)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
* **Did the program meet your expectations (expectations_met)**: Answer whether the program met your expectations.
* **Most helpful aspect of the program (helpful_aspect)**: Describe what you liked most about the program.
* **Areas for improvement (improvement_areas)**: Suggest areas where the program could improve.
* **How likely are you to recommend this program (recommendation_likelihood)**: Rate your likelihood of recommending the program.
* **Suggestions for future health topics (future_topics)**: Suggest future health topics you'd like to learn about.
* **Would you like to be contacted for future programs (follow_up_interest)**: Select Yes if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Contact Email or Phone (contact_info)**: Enter your contact email or phone number if you selected Yes.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you have suggestions for areas where the program could improve, provide specific examples.
* If you'd like to be contacted for future programs, make sure to enter your contact information.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact us.
