# social_network_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about a company's social media presence and usage habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field (optional).
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
4. Select the social media platforms you use (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, Snapchat) in the "Social Media" field.
5. Answer the frequency questions for each social media platform (How Often Facebook, How Often Instagram, etc.).
6. Select the primary use of Facebook and Instagram in the "What Do They Use Facebook For" and "What Do They Use Instagram For" fields.
7. Enter a brief explanation of how they feel about social media in the "How Do They Feel About Social Media" field.
8. Enter the company name in the "Company" field.
9. Select the job function in the "Job Function" field.
10. Select the department in the "Department" field.
11. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
12. Enter the location (optional).
13. Select the job level in the "Job Level" field (optional).
14. Select the gender in the "Gender" field (optional).
15. Enter your age (optional).
16. Select the company size in the "Company Size" field (optional).
17. Select the company type in the "Company Type" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address (if desired).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if desired).
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple, required): Select the social media platforms you use (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, Snapchat).
* **How Often Facebook** (`how_often_facebook`, number, optional): Answer the frequency of Facebook usage.
* **How Often Instagram** (`how_often_instagram`, number, optional): Answer the frequency of Instagram usage.
* **How Often Twitter** (`how_often_twitter`, number, optional): Answer the frequency of Twitter usage.
* **How Often TikTok** (`how_often_tiktok`, number, optional): Answer the frequency of TikTok usage.
* **How Often Snapchat** (`how_often_snapchat`, number, optional): Answer the frequency of Snapchat usage.
* **How Often YouTube** (`how_often_youtube`, number, optional): Answer the frequency of YouTube usage.
* **How Often Other** (`how_often_other`, text, optional): Enter the other social media platforms you use.
* **What Do They Use Facebook For** (`what_do_they_use_facebook_for`, select_one, required): Select the primary use of Facebook.
* **What Do They Use Instagram For** (`what_do_they_use_instagram_for`, select_multiple, required): Select the primary use of Instagram.
* **What Do They Use Twitter For** (`what_do_they_use_twitter_for`, select_multiple, optional): Select the primary use of Twitter.
* **How Do They Feel About Social Media** (`how_do_they_feel_about_social_media`, text, required): Enter a brief explanation of how they feel about social media.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the company name.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, select_one, required): Select the job function.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location (if desired).
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, optional): Select the job level.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select the gender.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age (if desired).
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Select the company size.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, optional): Select the company type.
