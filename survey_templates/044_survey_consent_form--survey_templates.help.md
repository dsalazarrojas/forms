# Survey Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survey Consent Form is a standardized template used to obtain consent from individuals or organizations before proceeding with a survey or research activity. This form helps ensure that all parties involved are aware of the purpose, scope, and implications of the survey, and provides a clear understanding of their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form completely and honestly.
*   Review each field carefully and make sure you understand its purpose and relevance.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Details (id: 2)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant contact details. This is a required field.
*   **Purpose of Consent (id: 3)**: Explain the purpose of the survey or research activity. This should be a clear and concise statement of the survey's objectives and goals.
*   **Agreement (id: 18)**: Select the option that best represents your position regarding the survey or research activity. This is a required field.
*   **Name (id: 6)**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on the form.
*   **Email (id: 7)**: Enter your email address.
*   **Phone (id: 8)**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Organization (id: 9)**: If you are representing an organization, enter its name.
*   **Title (id: 10)**: If you are representing an organization, enter your title.
*   **Position (id: 11)**: If you are representing an organization, enter your position.
*   **Address (id: 12)**: Enter your address.
*   **City (id: 13)**: Enter your city.
*   **State (id: 14)**: Enter your state or province.
*   **Zip (id: 15)**: Enter your zip or postal code.
*   **Country (id: 16)**: Enter your country.
*   **Notes (id: 17)**: Add any additional comments or information related to your contact details.
*   **Comments (id: 19)**: Add any additional comments or questions about the survey or research activity.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by individuals or organizations who will be participating in a survey or research activity. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
