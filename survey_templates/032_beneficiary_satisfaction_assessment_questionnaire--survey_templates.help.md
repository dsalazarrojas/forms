# Beneficiary Satisfaction Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from beneficiaries about their experiences with a program or service. The feedback will help us understand their satisfaction, expectations, and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly and based on your personal experiences with the program or service.
2. For select-one questions, choose the option that best describes your answer.
3. Use the text fields to provide additional comments or suggestions, if needed.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all questions.
