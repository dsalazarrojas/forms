# Hybrid Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Hybrid Learning Survey, which aims to gather information about users' learning styles, preferences, and habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (`text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address in a standard email format (e.g., "user@example.com").
* **phone** (`text`, optional): Provide your phone number. This field can be left blank if you do not wish to provide this information.
* **learning_style** (`select_one`, optional): Select one or more of the following learning styles:
	+ Choice 1
	+ Choice 2
	+ Choice 3
* **learning_level** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more of the following learning levels:
	+ Choice 1
	+ Choice 2
	+ Choice 3
* **date_taken** (`date`, optional): Enter your preferred date for taking this survey in the format MM/dd/yyyy.
* **time_spent** (`time`, optional): If you have spent some time on the learning content, enter the time spent.
* **user_comment** (`note`, optional): Leave any additional comments about your experience.
