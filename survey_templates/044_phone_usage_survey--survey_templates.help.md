# phone_usage_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The phone usage survey is designed to collect data on your phone usage habits, helping us understand how you use your phone and identify areas where we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the general questions section to answer questions that are relevant to your phone usage.
2. Choose your mobile operator from the list of options.
3. Indicate your screen time in hours.
4. Select the type of device you are using (iPhone, Android, or Other).
5. Select the type of phone model you are using (Galaxy, iPhone, or Nokia).
6. Choose the operating system on your phone (iOS, Android, or Other).
7. Select all categories of screen usage that apply to you (Home, Work, Social Media, Gaming, or Other).
8. Choose how frequently you use your phone (Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-6 hours, or More than 6 hours).
9. Select your most used app (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, WhatsApp, or Other).
10. Choose all apps that you frequently use (select multiple apps that apply).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Questions** (General Questions, Text): This section is where you can provide any other comments or information about your phone usage not covered by the other fields.
* **Mobile Operator** (mobile_operator, Select One): Select the mobile operator that you are using. This could be your mobile provider or network operator.
* **Screen Time** (Screen Time, Number): This question asks for the average amount of time you spend on your phone in a day.
* **Mobile Device** (mobile_device, Select One): Choose the type of device you are using (iPhone, Android, or Other).
* **Phone Model** (phone_model, Select One): Select the type of phone model you are using (Galaxy, iPhone, or Nokia).
* **Phone OS** (phone_os, Select One): Choose the operating system on your phone (iOS, Android, or Other).
* **Screen Usage** (screen_usage, Select Multiple): Select all categories of screen usage that apply to you (Home, Work, Social Media, Gaming, or Other).
* **Usage Frequency** (usage_frequency, Select One): Indicate how frequently you use your phone (Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-6 hours, or More than 6 hours).
* **Most Used App** (most_used_app, Select One): Select the app that you use most frequently (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, WhatsApp, or Other).
* **App Usage Frequency** (app_usage_frequency, Select Multiple): Choose all apps that you frequently use.
