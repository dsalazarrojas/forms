# Farmland Transition Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farmland Transition Feedback Form is a survey designed to collect your thoughts and experiences about the transition process. Your input will help us improve our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please select your response to each question based on your experience with the transfer process.
2. If you need to provide additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Comments" field.
3. If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farmland Transition Form**: This is the main field where you can type any feedback or thoughts about the transition process.
* **Feedback on Transfer Process** (`feedback_question_1`): Choose how you experienced the transfer process from the following options:
	+ Completely: The process met all my expectations.
	+ Mostly: The process was mostly as expected, but had some minor issues.
	+ Not At All: The process did not meet my expectations.
	+ Not Applicable: I'm not sure what the question is asking.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`feedback_question_2`): Select your level of satisfaction with the transfer process:
	+ Very Satisfied: I'm highly satisfied with the process.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: I'm somewhat satisfied, but had some minor issues.
	+ Not Satisfied: I'm dissatisfied with the process.
	+ Not At All Satisfied: I'm extremely dissatisfied with the process.
* **Support Needs** (`support_needs`): If you have any specific needs or requirements for support, please list them here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Any additional thoughts, suggestions, or comments you'd like to share with us.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Please provide your contact information so we can get back to you.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`): Confirm your email address to help us verify your identity.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): If applicable, enter the person you'd like to be contacted.
* **Contact Person's Email** (Implicit): Not explicitly required but if entered will be used as contact info.
