# Media Impact On Academic Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to collect data on the impact of media on academic performance from students. Your input will help us understand how media usage influences students' habits, performance, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, choosing the most accurate response for you.
2. Select the options that best fit your media usage habits and experiences.
3. Be as specific as possible when answering questions about your favorite media platforms and activities.
4. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is an introduction page that sets the context for the survey. Please read through the information provided and proceed when ready.
* **Demographic Information**: This section gathers general information about you, such as your grade expectations and performance.
* **Student Technology Use**: This section asks how often you are connected to technology.
* **Academic Performance**: This section asks about your academic performance and expectations.
* **How does social media influence**: This section asks how social media influences your grades.
* **Favorite Media Platforms**: This section asks about your favorite media platforms.
* **Time Spent on Media**: This section asks how much time you spend on media.
* **Most Often Used Media**: This section asks about the most used media platforms.
* **How Often Do You Use Social Media**: This section asks about your media usage frequency.
* **How Do You Use Social Media**: This section allows you to describe your social media usage habits.
* **What Grade Do You Expect To Get**: This section asks about your expectations for your grades.
* **What Grade Do You Achieve**: This section asks about your current grade performance.
* **Media Grades Influence**: This section asks about the influence of media on your grades.
* **Media Habits**: This section asks about the impact of media on your habits.
* **Favorite Moments**: This section asks about the most enjoyable moments you've experienced through media.
* **Favorite Activities**: This section asks about the activities you enjoy most on media.
* **Favorite Moments Activities**: This section asks about the activities you enjoy most on media.
* **Favorite Places**: This section asks about the favorite places you've been to through media.
* **Favorite People**: This section asks about the favorite people you've interacted with through media.
* **Favorite Things**: This section asks about the favorite things you've discovered through media.
* **Overall Impact**: This section asks about the overall influence of media on your life.
* **Media Activities**: This section asks about your media-related activities.
* **Favorite Social Media**: This section asks about your favorite social media platforms for personal use.
* **Overall Impact Again**: This section asks about the overall impact of media on your life (again).
* **Favorite Media Platforms for Fun**: This section asks about your favorite media platforms for entertainment purposes.

Remember, your responses will help us better understand the impact of media on academic performance. Please feel free to contact us if you need help or have any questions!
