# Soccer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Survey form is designed to collect information about soccer players, which will be used for analysis and planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your general player information in the "Player Info" section.
2. Select your position on the field in the "Player Position" section.
3. Enter your height in inches in the "Player Height" section.
4. Report your game experience in years in the "Game Experience" section.
5. Indicate how many games you play per week in the "Games Played per Week" section.
6. Enter the total number of training hours you have per week in the "Training Hours" section.
7. Enter your team name in the "Team Name" section.
8. Report how many games you play per week, excluding days off, in the "Games Played per Week Days" section.
9. Set your game day start time in the "Game Day Start Time" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Info** (`player_info`, text, required: false): This section is for general player information. Please enter your name, contact information, or any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, select, required: false): Select one of the following options: 
  • Goalkeeper
  • Defense
  • Midfielder
  • Forward
* **Player Height** (`player_height`, number, required: false): Enter your height in inches.
* **Game Experience** (`game_experience`, number, required: false): Report your game experience in years.
* **Games Played per Week** (`game_frequency`, number, required: false): Indicate how many games you play per week.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, required: false): Enter the total number of training hours you have per week.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter your team name.
* **Games Played per Week Days** (`game_frequency_days`, number, required: false): Report how many games you play per week, excluding days off.
* **Game Day Start Time** (`game_day_start_time`, time, required: false): Set your game day start time.

## Tips
* Please ensure the data you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your coach or team management for clarification.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
* This survey is designed to be completed by soccer players only, so please do not attempt to complete it if you are not a soccer player.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact your team management.
