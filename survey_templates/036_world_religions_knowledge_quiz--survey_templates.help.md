# World Religions Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a quiz designed to test your knowledge of world religions. It has four sections, each focusing on a different aspect of religious background, beliefs, and practices. The form is meant to be completed by users with a basic understanding of religious concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the form, selecting the correct options for each field.
2. Provide demographic information when prompted.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure you have answered all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **start**: This is the beginning of the form. It is an optional introductory note.
* **Section 1** (`section_1_label`): This is the title of the first section.
* **What is your religious background?** (`section_1_question_1`): This is a question asking about your religious background. Select one of the options: Buddhism, Hinduism, Islam, Christianity, or Other.
* **What is your religious denomination?** (`section_1_question_2`): This is a question asking about your religious denomination. Select multiple options: Christian, Protestant, Catholic, or Other.
* **Section 2** (`section_2_label`): This is the title of the second section.
* **How would you rate the importance of religious freedom?** (`section_2_question_1`): This is a question asking about your opinion on the importance of religious freedom. Rate your answer on a scale of 1-10.
* **Section 3** (`section_3_label`): This is the title of the third section.
* **Have you ever attended a religious service or event?** (`section_3_question_1`): This is a question asking about your personal experiences with religious services or events. Select one option: True or False.
* **Section 4** (`section_4_label`): This is the title of the fourth and final section.
* **How many languages do you speak?** (`section_4_question_1`): This is a question asking about your language skills. Enter a numerical value.

## Tips
* Answer each question to the best of your ability.
* Be honest when answering demographic questions.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
