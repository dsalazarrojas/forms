# population_density_assessment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The population density assessment form is used to collect information about the population density of a city, town, or region. It helps in assessing the population density, which is the number of people living in a given area.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start on the "Introduction" page, where you can briefly introduce the area being assessed.
2. Proceed to the "Population" page and enter the estimated population of the city, town, or region.
3. On the "Area" page, enter the total area of the city, town, or region.
4. On the "Density" page, enter the population density of the city, town, or region.
5. On the "Urban areas" page, select "Yes" if the area has urban areas, "No" otherwise.
6. On the "Rural areas" page, enter the percentage of rural areas within the city, town, or region.
7. On the "Population growth" page, enter the estimated annual population growth rate.
8. On the "Contact name", "Contact email", and "Contact phone" pages, enter the contact person's information.
9. On the "Comments" page, enter any additional comments or notes about the area.
10. Finally, on the "Population density assessment" and "Area type" pages, select the appropriate assessment and type of area, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page is a brief introduction to the area being assessed.
* **Population**: Enter the estimated population of the city, town, or region.
* **Area**: Enter the total area of the city, town, or region.
* **Density**: Enter the population density of the city, town, or region.
* **Urban areas**: Select "Yes" if the area has urban areas, "No" otherwise.
* **Rural areas**: Enter the percentage of rural areas within the city, town, or region.
* **Population growth**: Enter the estimated annual population growth rate.
* **Contact name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the area.
* **Population density assessment**: Select the level of population density assessment: Low, Medium, or High.
* **Area type**: Select the type of area: Urban, Rural, or Mixed.
* **Population density**: Select the level of population density: High, Low, or Medium.
* **Region**: Select "Yes" if the area is a region, "No" otherwise.
* **Region other**: Enter the name of the region (optional).
