# Athlete Wellness Program Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Wellness Program Evaluation Survey is designed to assess the effectiveness of the wellness program in improving various aspects of an athlete's life. By completing this survey, you will help us understand how well the program has met your needs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name as an athlete.
2. Select your primary sport.
3. Indicate the length of your participation in the program.
4. Rate the impact of the program on five different areas: 
   - Sleep Quality and Consistency
   - Daily Nutritional Habits
   - Mental Health and Stress Management
   - Injury Prevention and Recovery Awareness
5. Rate the quality of educational materials, relevance of workshops, ease of accessing online resources, effectiveness of communication from wellness staff, and overall improvement.
6. Select the most valuable component of the program.
7. Provide any improvement suggestions you have for the program.
8. Indicate if you felt adequately supported throughout the program.
9. Choose how likely you are to continue the wellness practices learned in the program.
10. Specify any wellness topics you would like to see covered in the future.
11. Share any general comments or feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: Please enter your full name as an athlete.
* **Primary Sport**: Select the sport in which you primarily compete or practice.
* **Length of Program Participation**: Indicate the length of time you participated in the program.
* **Impact on Sleep Quality and Consistency**: Rate the impact of the program on your sleep quality and consistency using the following scale:
    + 1 - No Impact
    + 2 - Minimal
    + 3 - Moderate
    + 4 - Significant
    + 5 - Transformative
* **Impact on Daily Nutritional Habits**: Rate the impact of the program on your daily nutritional habits using the same scale as above.
* **Impact on Mental Health and Stress Management**: Rate the impact of the program on your mental health and stress management using the same scale as above.
* **Impact on Injury Prevention and Recovery Awareness**: Rate the impact of the program on your injury prevention and recovery awareness using the same scale as above.
* **Quality of Educational Materials**: Rate the quality of educational materials provided using the following scale:
    + 1 - Poor
    + 2 - Fair
    + 3 - Good
    + 4 - Very Good
    + 5 - Excellent
* **Relevance of Workshops**: Rate the relevance of wellness workshops to your sport using the following scale:
    + 1 - Not Relevant
    + 2 - Slightly Relevant
    + 3 - Moderately Relevant
    + 4 - Highly Relevant
* **Ease of Accessing Online Resources**: Rate the ease of accessing online wellness resources using the following scale:
    + 1 - Difficult
    + 2 - Fair
    + 3 - Easy
    + 4 - Very Easy
* **Effectiveness of Communication from Wellness Staff**: Rate the effectiveness of communication from wellness staff using the following scale:
    + 1 - Poor
    + 2 - Fair
    + 3 - Good
    + 4 - Very Good
    + 5 - Excellent
* **Overall Wellness Improvement Rating**: Rate the overall improvement in your wellness using the following scale:
    + 1 - No Improvement
    + 2 - Slight
    + 3 - Moderate
    + 4 - Significant
    + 5 - Exceptional
* **Most Valuable Component**: Select the most valuable component of the program.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions for improvement you have for the program.
* **Staff Support Feeling**: Indicate if you felt adequately supported throughout the program.
* **Continuation of Wellness Practices**: Choose how likely you are to continue the wellness practices learned in the program.
* **Future Wellness Topics**: Specify any specific wellness topics you would like to see covered in the future.
* **General Comments**: Share any general comments or feedback you have about the program.
* **Date of Evaluation**: Provide the date of this evaluation.
* **Any Other Comments**: Share any other comments or feedback you have.
