# Best Subscription Service Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a user's subscription service nomination. Nominees will be reviewed for consideration for a subscription service award.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your information in the fields as described below.
2. Fill out all required fields.
3. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`nominator_full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Your Email Address** (`nominator_email_addr`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address associated with your account.
* **Subscription Service Name** (`service_name_text`, `text`, required): Enter the official name of the subscription service you're nominating.
* **Website or App URL** (`service_website_url`, `text`, required): Provide the URL for the product information or access to the service.
* **Category of Subscription** (`subscription_category_select`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category that best describes the service (e.g., Entertainment and Streaming, Food and Beverage, etc.).
* **Subscription Frequency** (`subscription_frequency_choice`, `select_one`, required): Select how often the service is billed or delivered (e.g., Weekly, Monthly, etc.).
* **Price Range per Cycle** (`price_range_select`, `select_one`, required): Choose the price range of the service (e.g., Under $10, $10 - $25, etc.).
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money_rating`, `select_one`, required): Assess whether the cost is justified by the service (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Quality of Products or Content** (`product_quality_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of the products or content provided by the service (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Ease of Cancellation** (`cancellation_ease_rating`, `select_one`, required): Assess how user-friendly the unsubscribe process is (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Customer Support Responsiveness** (`customer_support_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the responsiveness of the customer support (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Variety and Selection** (`variety_selection_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the breadth of options available (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **What is the best thing about this subscription service?** (`best_thing_desc`, `text`, required): Describe one key strength of the service.
* **Why are you nominating this service for an award?** (`nomination_reason_text`, `text`, required): Explain what makes this service stand out from competitors.
* **Describe your most recent experience with this service** (`recent_experience_desc`, `text`, required): Describe your most positive experience with the service.
* **Would you recommend this subscription to others?** (`recommendation_status`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you would recommend the service to others (e.g., Definitely Yes, Probably, Unsure, False).
* **Date of Nomination** (`nomination_date_stamp`, `date`, required): Enter the date you're submitting this nomination.
