# internal_communications_quality_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the quality and effectiveness of internal communications within your organization. It will help you evaluate the type of communication that best suits your team's or department's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. Each page has a brief description or instruction to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, optional): This is a brief introduction page with no specific questions to answer. Please read through this page to understand the purpose and context of the survey.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the most effective type of communication for your team or department, either `Formal` or `Informal`. This will help us understand the preferred style of communication within your group.
* **Communication Frequency** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more from the following frequencies for internal communications within your team:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Semi-Annually
	+ Annually
* **Preferred Communication Channels** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one preferred channel for internal communications:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Team meetings
	+ Text message
	+ Social media
	+ Company intranet
* **Communication Strategy** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the most suitable communication strategy for your team or department:
	+ Formal
	+ Informal
* **Communication Level** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the level of formality for internal communications:
	+ Formal
	+ Informal
* **Communication Time** (`7`, `time`, optional): Please enter the approximate time spent on internal communications.
* **Response Rate** (`8`, `number`, optional): Please enter the response rate (e.g., percentage) for internal communications.
* **Communication Content** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please choose the types of content that are typically included in internal communications:
	+ Company news
	+ Team news
	+ Manager news
	+ Employee newsletters
	+ Internal news feed
* **Response Time** (`10`, `time`, optional): Please enter the approximate response time for internal communications.
* **Follow-up** (`11`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select when follow-up is necessary for internal communications:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
	+ As needed
* **Communication Quality** (`12`, `number`, optional): Please rate the quality of internal communications (e.g., 1-5).
* **Communication Channels Used** (`13`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please choose the channels used for internal communications:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Team meetings
	+ Text message
	+ Social media
	+ Company intranet
* **Communication Frequency 2** (`14`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more from the following frequencies for internal communications:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Semi-Annually
	+ Annually
* **Communication Level 2** (`15`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the level of formality for internal communications:
	+ Formal
	+ Informal
* **Time Spend** (`16`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please choose the approximate time spent on internal communications:
	+ Less than 1 hour
	+ 1-2 hours
	+ 2-4 hours
	+ More than 4 hours

Please note that some fields may be redundant or have overlapping questions, and your answers will help us understand the complexities of internal communications within your organization. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
