# Marine Refit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from marine refit users to improve the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required questions marked as `required: true` as best as you can.
2. Select a suitable option from the dropdown menus for questions marked as `type: select_one`.
3. Fill in all text fields with any additional comments or information.
4. If you have any further comments or suggestions, use the `additional_comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marine Refit Feedback Survey Form** (`id: 1`): This is the title of the form and a heading for the survey questions that follow.
* **project_id** (`id: 2`): Select the marine refit project ID that you are providing feedback on.
	+ Example: 2800
* **satisfaction_level** (`id: 3`): Choose your level of satisfaction with the refit process.
	+ Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied
* **improvement_opportunity** (`id: 4`): If you have any suggestions or areas for improvement, describe them here.
* **suggestions** (`id: 5`): Provide any additional suggestions or comments here.
* **contact_info** (`id: 6`): Enter your email address or other contact information so we can get back to you.
* **additional_comments** (`id: 7`): If you have any further comments or suggestions, provide them here.
