# Environmental Initiative Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Initiative Voting Form is designed to collect information from users on various environmental initiatives and priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what each field represents.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the most relevant answer from the provided list.
3. Enter your email address to receive any follow-up information or updates.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Community Input
* **Community Input** (`community_input`, text, optional): Provide any relevant input or feedback from your community or organization related to environmental initiatives.
### 2. Sustainability Priorities
* **Sustainability Priorities** (`sustainability_priorities`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following sustainability priorities:
	+ Climate Action
	+ Water Conservation
	+ Waste Reduction
### 3. Organization Size
* **Organization Size** (`organization_size`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the following organization sizes:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
### 4. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive any follow-up information or updates.
### 5. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up contact.
### 6. Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the environmental initiatives.
### 7. Submitted On
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): This field is automatically populated with the date and time of submission.
### 8. Submitted At
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, time, optional): This field is automatically populated with the time of submission.
