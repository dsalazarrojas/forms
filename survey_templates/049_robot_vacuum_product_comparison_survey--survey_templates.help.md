# Robot Vacuum Product Comparison Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information from users about their experience with robot vacuum products. It aims to identify common features, preferences, and satisfaction levels among robot vacuum users.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form by providing your general information and satisfaction levels with robot vacuum products.
- Choose all that apply from the "Robot Vacuum Info" dropdown menu.
- Use the other text fields to provide any additional information about your experience with robot vacuum products.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Provide a brief description of your background and experience with robot vacuum products.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction levels with robot vacuum products (e.g., 1-5 scale).
* **Feature Preferences**: Choose all that apply from the list of features.
* **Purchase Information**: Share details about your purchase experience with robot vacuum products.
* **Other Information**: Provide any additional information about your experience with robot vacuum products.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Name**: Enter your name (optional).
* **Address**: Enter your address (optional).
* **Other Questions**: Answer any additional questions you may have (e.g., what features matter most to you).
* **Other Information 2**: Provide any additional information about your experience with robot vacuum products.
* **Purchase Driver**: Share details about your primary purchase driver (e.g., budget, features).
* **Purchase Driver 2**: Share details about your secondary purchase driver (e.g., brand loyalty).
* **Purchase Driver 3**: Share details about your tertiary purchase driver (e.g., reviews).
* **General Comments**: Provide any general comments about your experience with robot vacuum products.
* **Robot Vacuum Info**: Choose all that apply from the dropdown menu.
