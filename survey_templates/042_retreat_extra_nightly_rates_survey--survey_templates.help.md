# Retreat Extra Nightly Rates Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences and habits related to extra nightly rates during retreats. Your responses will help us plan and optimize our retreat events to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
2. Select all relevant options for each question.
3. Choose the Submit option to confirm your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **extra nights** (<extra_nights>, select_multiple, optional): Please indicate whether you would like to have extra nights available during the retreat.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you would like to have extra nights available.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not want to have extra nights available.
* **pricing** (<pricing, select_one, optional): Select the price range you would like to pay for extra nights.
	+ '10' is not a valid price range, please select a more realistic option.
* **desired amenities** (<amenity_preferences, select_multiple, optional): Please indicate which amenities you would like to have available during the retreat.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you want the amenity.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not want the amenity.
* **what_do_you_want_to_do_tomorrow** (<what_do_you_want_to_do_tomorrow, select_one, optional): Please select an activity you would like to do during the retreat tomorrow.
	+ 'work' is not a recommended activity during the retreat, as it is not a working day.
* **what_do_you_want_to_do_the_day_after** (<what_do_you_want_to_do_the_day_after, select_one, optional): Please select an activity you would like to do during the retreat the day after.
	+ 'work' is not a recommended activity during the retreat, as it is not a working day.
* **Submit** (<submit, select_one, required): Please select 'Submit' to confirm your responses.
* **email** (<email, email, optional): Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted with further information about the retreat.
* **phone** (<phone, text, optional): Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted with further information about the retreat.
* **phone is not a required field and will not be used by default.
