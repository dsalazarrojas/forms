# mah_jongg_participation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information and feedback about mahjong participations and events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the relevant fields with the requested information and select the options that apply to your experience. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on any official communication from the club.
* **What is your club?** (`club_name`, select_one, optional): Choose the name of your club, if applicable.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event you participated in, such as Mah Jongg or Other.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Write any comments or feedback you have about the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event if you know it.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event if you know it.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the event if you know it.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event if you know it.
* **Attended** (`attended`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you attended the event, with options Yes, No, or Not Sure.
* **Attended Other** (`attended_other`, select_one, optional): If you answered Not Sure, choose Other to specify why.
* **How many attended** (`attended_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of people who attended the event if you know it.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Write any comments or feedback about the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Player Feedback** (`player_feedback`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you felt about your experience, with options I had fun, I did not have fun, or I had some fun.
* **Event Preferences** (`event_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferences for future events, with options Easy going, Challenging, Easy, or Fun.
* **Club Preferences** (`club_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your club preferences, with options Easy going, Challenging, Easy, or Fun.
* **Host Preferences** (`host_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferences for hosts, with options Easy going, Challenging, Easy, or Fun.
* **Other Preferences** (`other_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your other preferences, with options Easy going, Challenging, Easy, or Fun.
* **How many attended** (`attended_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of people who attended the event if you know it.
* **Player Comments** (`player_comments`, text, optional): Write any comments or feedback about the event.
* **Event Host** (`event_host`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event host if you know it.
* **Event Coordinators** (`event_coordinators`, text, optional): Enter the names of the event coordinators if you know them.
* **Event Suggestions** (`event_suggestions`, text, optional): Write any event suggestions or ideas you have.
