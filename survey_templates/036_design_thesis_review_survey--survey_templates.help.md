# Design Thesis Review Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from various stakeholders, including faculty members, guest critics, and alumni, on a design thesis. The purpose of this survey is to collect opinions and insights on the design thesis, helping to inform and improve the design process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a title for the design thesis.
2. Enter the name of the page(s) you are reviewing.
3. Select your role in the design school (Faculty, Guest, or Alumni).
4. Indicate if you are an external critic (yes or no).
5. Provide feedback on the design thesis.
6. If you are both a faculty member and an external critic, select both options.
7. Enter your email address (if applicable).
8. Enter your phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Design Thesis Review Survey** (1): 
	+ A short title for this survey.
	+ This field is not required, but it's a good idea to provide a title for reference.
* **Page Name** (2): 
	+ Enter the name of the page(s) you are reviewing.
	+ This field helps us keep track of the specific page(s) you are providing feedback on.
* **What is your role in the design school?** (3):
	+ Choose one of the following options: 
		- Faculty
		- Guest
		- Alumni
* **Are you an external critic?** (4):
	+ Answer with one of the following options: 
		- 'True'
		- 'False'
* **Feedback** (5):
	+ Share your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions on the design thesis.
	+ You can write as much or as little as you like, but try to be specific and constructive.
* **Are you a faculty member or an external critic?** (6):
	+ If you are both a faculty member and an external critic, select both options.
* **Email** (7):
	+ If you are providing feedback, enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (8):
	+ If you are providing feedback, enter your phone number so we can contact you.
