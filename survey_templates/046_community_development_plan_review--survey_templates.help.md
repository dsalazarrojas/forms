# Community Development Plan Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and reviews from community members on the development plan of the community center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose the area that you think needs the most attention from the development plan options.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the proposed development plan on a scale of 1 to 5.
4. Indicate if you feel adequately engaged in the development planning process.
5. Provide your ideas for improving the development plan.
6. Rate your satisfaction with recent development initiatives.
7. Share your ideas for future development initiatives.
8. Express your willingness to support a development initiative.
9. Leave a digital signature to confirm that the information provided is correct.
10. Submit the form with the date of review submission.
11. Choose whether you'd like to receive the community center's newsletter for updates.
12. Let us know how you heard about this plan review form.
13. If applicable, provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name of Reviewer**: Your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for follow-up and review results delivery.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number**: Your primary contact phone number.
* **Residential Address**: Your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Development Plan Review and Feedback**: This section is where you can provide your thoughts and feedback on the development plan.
* **Which area in our community's development plan needs the most attention**: Choose the area that you think requires the most improvement.
* **Rate your overall satisfaction with the proposed development plan**: Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Do you feel adequately engaged in the development planning process**: Choose if you're engaged or not.
* **Idea for Improvement**: Share your ideas on how to improve the development plan.
* **Rate your satisfaction with recent development initiatives**: Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **What are your ideas for future development initiatives**: Share your ideas for future development initiatives.
* **Would you be willing to support a development initiative**: Express your willingness to support a development initiative.
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)**: Leave a digital signature to confirm that the information provided is correct.
* **Date of Review Submission**: Provide the date of submission.
* **I would like to receive the community center's newsletter**: Choose if you'd like to receive updates.
* **How did you hear about this plan review form**: Let us know how you heard about this form.
* **Emergency Contact Person**: Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person if applicable.

## Tips
* Please be honest and sincere in your feedback and ratings.
* If you're unsure of an answer, you can choose "Not Sure".
* If you'd like to provide more details, you can type them in the corresponding text boxes.
* If you'd like to be updated with the community center's newsletters, select "Yes, sign me up!"
