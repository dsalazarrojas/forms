# Onboarding Success Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onboarding Success Monitoring Form is a tool used to gather feedback and suggestions from new employees after their first day on the job. This feedback helps to identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. If you have suggestions or feedback, please provide it in the corresponding fields.
3. If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask your HR representative for clarification.
4. Once you've completed the form, submit it and we'll review your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think is the number one thing that we did right on your first day?** (`onboarding_success_monitoring_form`, `text`, required: false) - Please share with us what you think was a positive aspect of your onboarding experience.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, required: false) - Please select your job title from the list provided.
* **What program are you in?** (`onboarding_program`, `select_multiple`, required: false) - Please select all applicable programs you are participating in.
* **Onboarding status** (`onboarding_status`, `select_one`, required: false) - Please select your current onboarding status.
* **Onboarding success** (`onboarding_success`, `select_one`, required: false) - Please select how successful you think your onboarding experience was.
* **Onboarding effectiveness** (`onboarding_effectiveness`, `number`, required: false) - Please rate your onboarding experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Onboarding feedback** (`onboarding_feedback`, `text`, required: false) - Please provide any feedback about your onboarding experience.
* **Onboarding culture fit** (`onboarding_culture_fit`, `select_one`, required: false) - Please select how well your onboarding experience fit with our company culture.
* **Onboarding supportiveness** (`onboarding_supportiveness`, `select_one`, required: false) - Please select how much support you received during your onboarding.
* **Onboarding clarity** (`onboarding_clarity`, `select_one`, required: false) - Please select how clear the onboarding process was for you.
* **Onboarding effectiveness score** (`onboarding_effectiveness_score`, `number`, required: false) - Please rate your onboarding effectiveness on a scale of 1-10.
* **Onboarding improvement areas** (`onboarding_improvement_areas`, `text`, required: false) - Please provide any areas for improvement.
* **Onboarding improvement areas additional** (`onboarding_improvement_areas_additional`, `text`, required: false) - Please provide additional areas for improvement.
* **Onboarding recommendation** (`onboarding_recommendation`, `select_multiple`, required: false) - Would you recommend the onboarding program to new employees?
* **Onboarding recommendation reason** (`onboarding_recommendation_reason`, `text`, required: false) - Please provide the reason for your recommendation.
* **Onboarding notes** (`onboarding_notes`, `text`, required: false) - Please provide any additional notes about your onboarding experience.
* **Onboarding suggestions** (`onboarding_suggestions`, `text`, required: false) - Please provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Onboarding recommendation reason additional** (`onboarding_recommendation_reason_additional`, `text`, required: false) - Please provide additional reason for your recommendation.
* **Onboarding notes additional** (`onboarding_notes_additional`, `text`, required: false) - Please provide additional notes about your onboarding experience.
* **Onboarding suggestions additional** (`onboarding_suggestions_additional`, `text`, required: false) - Please provide additional suggestions for improvement.
* **Onboarding improvement areas additional additional** (`onboarding_improvement_areas_additional_additional`, `text`, required: false) - Please provide additional areas for improvement.
* **Onboarding program status** (`onboarding_program_status`, `text`, required: false) - Please provide the status of your onboarding program.
* **Onboarding program status additional** (`onboarding_program_status_additional`, `text`, required: false) - Please provide additional status for your onboarding program.
* **Onboarding recommendation status** (`onboarding_recommendation_status`, `select_one`, required: false) - Please select the status of your recommendation.
* **Onboarding improvement status** (`onboarding_improvement_status`, `select_one`, required: false) - Please select the status of your improvement.

Note: Fields marked as `required: false` are optional and can be skipped if the user does not have an answer or input.
