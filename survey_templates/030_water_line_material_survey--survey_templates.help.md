# Water Line Material Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about water line materials, including installation details and material types.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the survey if desired.
2. Answer the questions about the water line material, including:
	* Installation year
	* Service line condition (New, Existing, or Unknown)
	* Material type (PVC, Copper, Galvanized, or Ductile)
	* Diameter size (in inches and mm)
	* Service line materials (select multiple options)
3. If applicable, enter the installation date and details about the installation year and month.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Installation Year (`installation_year`)** (`number`, Optional): Enter the year the water line was installed.
* **Service Line Condition (`service_line_condition`)** (`select_one`, Optional): Choose how long the water line has been in service (New, Existing, or Unknown).
* **Material Type (`material_type`)** (`select_multiple`, Optional): Select one or more of the material types available:
	+ PVC
	+ Copper
	+ Galvanized
	+ Ductile
* **Diameter Size (inches) (`diameter_size_inches`)** (`number`, Optional): Enter the diameter of the water line in inches.
* **Diameter Size (mm) (`diameter_size_mm`)** (`number`, Optional): Enter the diameter of the water line in millimeters.
* **Service Line Materials (`service_line_materials`)** (`select_multiple`, Optional): Select one or more of the materials that make up the service line:
	+ PVC
	+ Copper
	+ Galvanized
	+ Ductile
* **Installation Date (`installation_date`)** (`date`, Optional): If known, enter the date of installation.
* **Installation Year (`installation_date_year`)** (`number`, Optional): Enter the year the water line was installed, if different from the installation year.
* **Installation Month (`installation_date_month`)** (`select_one`, Optional): Choose the month the water line was installed (if known).
* **Notes (`notes`)** (`note`, Optional): Add any additional notes about the water line material.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
- If unsure about a field, try your best to estimate or leave it blank.
- If a field is not applicable, do not enter information.
- Save the completed form for future reference or to pass on to others.
