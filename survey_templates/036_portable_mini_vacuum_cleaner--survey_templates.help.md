# portable_mini_vacuum_cleaner - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage projects related to a portable mini vacuum cleaner. It is intended for collecting and organizing project information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on "Start" to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the "Project Title" field with the title of your project.
3. Provide a brief description of your project in the "Project Description" field, if needed.
4. Select the category of your project from the "Category" field. You can choose from "Engineering" or "Other".
5. If your project has any tags, select them from the "Tags" field. You can select multiple tags.
6. Add any additional notes about your project in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter the ID of the tool assigned to your project in the "Assigned Tool" field, if applicable.
8. Fill out the "Form ID" field with a unique identifier for your project form, if needed.
9. Upload the output file for your project in the "Output File" field. This is a required field.
10. Select the category of your project from the "Category" field again, as a confirmation.
11. If you have assigned a tool to your project, enter its ID in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (1): Enter the title of your project in this field.
* **Project Description** (2): Enter a brief description of your project, if needed.
* **Category** (3): Select the category of your project. This field must be filled out, and you can choose from "Engineering" or "Other".
* **Tags** (4): Select any tags associated with your project.
* **Notes** (5): Enter any additional notes about your project.
* **Assigned Tool** (6): Enter the ID of the tool assigned to your project, if applicable.
* **Form ID** (7): Enter a unique identifier for your project form, if needed.
* **Output File** (8): Upload the output file for your project. This field is required.
* **Category** (9): Confirm the category of your project. This field must be filled out and is the same as field 3.
* **Assigned Tool** (10): Enter the ID of the tool assigned to your project, if you have assigned one.
