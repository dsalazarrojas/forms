# NSW Touch Football Affiliate Facility Audit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NSW Touch Football Affiliate Facility Audit form is designed to gather information from facilities affiliated with the National Squash of Wales to assess their readiness and compliance with certain standards and guidelines. This form helps facility administrators or managers provide necessary details about their facilities to ensure they meet the required criteria.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the correct facility type from the drop-down menu.
2.  Enter the name of the facility in the text box provided.
3.  Fill in the address and phone number in the corresponding text boxes.
4.  Enter the facility's email address.
5.  If applicable, enter the website URL.
6.  Provide a brief description of the facility's licence details, if required.
7.  Fill in the necessary information regarding insurance coverage for the facility.
8.  Provide details about equipment and facilities offered by the facility.
9.  Enter the required information regarding the staff and their details.
10. Enter the name of the insurance provider (if applicable).
11. Fill in whether or not the facility is certified.
12. Fill in any comments or remarks regarding the facility.
13. Enter any additional information related to the facility's insurance.
14. Fill in the date of the survey.
15. Enter the time of the survey.
16. Provide the name of the person who conducted the survey.
17. Fill in the status of the facility (if not certified).
18. Enter any other comments or remarks not covered in other fields.
19. If applicable, enter any other information from the surveyor.
20. Enter the other status of the facility (if not certified).
