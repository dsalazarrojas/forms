# Insurance Customer Satisfaction Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Customer Satisfaction Request Form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with our insurance services. This form helps us to identify areas of improvement, address customer concerns, and provide better services to you and your colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, simply follow these steps:

1. **Policyholder Feedback**: Please share your thoughts and opinions about your experience with our insurance services. Be as specific as possible about what you liked or disliked.
2. **Rating**: Choose your rating from the options provided (Good, Fair, Poor, Excellent).
3. **Best Method to Contact**: Select the best way you would like to be contacted by us (Email, Phone, In-Person, Online Form).
4. **Name**: Provide your name, if you'd like to be associated with your feedback.
5. **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, if you'd like us to get in touch with you.
6. **Policy Number**: If you're submitting feedback about a specific policy, please provide the policy number.
7. **Date of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth, if you'd like to be contacted based on your age.
8. **Policyholder Relationship Status**: Select the relationship status of the policyholder (Policyholder is our client, Policyholder is not our client, Policyholder is ex-client, or Policyholder is ex-client in progress).
9. **Contact Frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to be contacted (Regularly, Occasionally, Never).
10. **Comments**: If you have additional comments or suggestions, please share them here.
11. **Assigned to**: Select the person or team you'd like to be assigned to (True, False).
12. **Comments 2**: Add any additional comments or suggestions here.
