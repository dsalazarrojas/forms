# Digital Planning And Journaling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand how you use our app. It's meant to be a brief and informal way for you to share your experiences, thoughts, and suggestions about our digital planning and journaling features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form**: Carefully read through each page and enter your answers in the corresponding fields.
2. **Answer all questions**: Please answer all questions to the best of your ability. You can type as much or as little as you like to answer each question.
3. **Submit your feedback**: Once you've completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **digital_planning_and_journaling_survey** (Page 1, Text Field):
	+ This is a text field where you can briefly describe your experiences with our digital planning and journaling features.
* **question_1** (Page 1, Text Field):
	+ How you currently use our app for digital planning and journaling.
* **question_2** (Page 2, Select Multiple Field):
	+ Please select all that apply from the options: "How I use the app for planning" is not relevant. You are asked about your use of the app: 
	+ "Option 3 label"
	+ "Other" 
* **question_3** (Page 3, Text Field):
	+ A text field where you can provide additional information or thoughts about your app usage.

## Tips
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please don't hesitate to include them in the "question_3" field.
* If you've answered "Other" for question 2, please provide a brief description in the "question_3" field.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your participation!
