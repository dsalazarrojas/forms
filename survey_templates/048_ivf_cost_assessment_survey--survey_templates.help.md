# IVF Cost Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IVF Cost Assessment Survey is designed to collect information from patients about their insurance coverage, financial situation, medical history, and treatment interests related to In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) treatments. This information will help healthcare professionals determine the feasibility of IVF treatment for each patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information (Page 1)**: Enter your personal information, including your name, contact details, and date of birth.
2. **Insurance Details (Page 2)**: Provide your insurance provider and policy number, if applicable.
3. **Treatment Interests (Page 3)**: List any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or other health concerns that may impact your IVF treatment.
4. **Insurance Coverage (Page 4)**: Specify your current insurance coverage, including any out-of-pocket expenses you're responsible for.
5. **Financial Information (Page 5)**: Report your income, employment status, and any financial constraints that may affect your IVF treatment.
6. **Medical History (Page 6)**: Describe your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
7. **Treatment Costs (Page 7)**: Estimate the costs associated with your IVF treatment, including any expected expenses for medication, procedures, or travel.
8. **Patient Consent (Page 8)**: Confirm your consent to provide this information and acknowledge that it will be used to assess your eligibility for IVF treatment.
