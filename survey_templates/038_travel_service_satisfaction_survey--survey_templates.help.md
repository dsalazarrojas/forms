## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as they pertain to your recent travel experience with the travel agency. The form will guide you through the different sections, and you can provide as much detail as you want.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Service Satisfaction Survey** (1, text, optional): This is the introduction to the form, providing context for the survey questions that follow.
* **Travel Agency** (2, text, optional): Please enter the name of the travel agency you used for your recent trip.
* **Travel Destination** (3, text, optional): Enter the destination or location you visited.
* **Travel Arrival Date** (4, date, optional): Enter the date you arrived at your destination.
* **Travel Duration** (5, number, optional): Enter the number of days you spent traveling.
* **Travel Type** (6, select_one, optional): Choose the mode of transportation you used for your trip (Plane, Car, Train, Ship, Other).
* **Travel Purpose** (7, select_multiple, optional): Choose the reason(s) for your trip (Business, Personal, Other).
* **Satisfaction Level** (8, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the travel service (Satisfied, Neutral, Unsatisfied).
* **Travel Service Quality** (9, select_multiple, optional): Choose the quality of service you experienced (Good, Bad, Excellent).
* **Communication** (10, text, optional): Provide any comments about communication with the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Staff** (11, text, optional): Provide any comments about the staff of the travel agency.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (12, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the travel service (Good, Bad, Neutral).
* **Travel Service Satisfaction** (13, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your travel service satisfaction.
* **Travel Service Improvement** (14, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improving the travel service.
* **Additional Comments** (15, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your travel experience.
* **Travel Service Improvement Plan** (16, text, optional): Provide a plan for improving the travel service.
* **Communication with Travel Agency** (17, text, optional): Provide any comments about communication with the travel agency.
* **Travel Service Satisfaction Score** (18, number, optional): Enter a score from 1-5 to rate your travel service satisfaction.
* **Travel Service Satisfaction Score 2** (19, number, optional): Enter a score from 1-5 to rate your travel service satisfaction.
* **Travel Service Satisfaction Score 3** (20, number, optional): Enter a score from 1-5 to rate your travel service satisfaction.
* **Travel Service Satisfaction Score 4** (21, number, optional): Enter a score from 1-5 to rate your travel service satisfaction.
* **Travel Service Satisfaction Score 5** (22, number, optional): Enter a score from 1-5 to rate your travel service satisfaction.
* **Additional Comments 2** (23, text, optional): Provide any additional comments.
* **Travel Service Satisfaction Score Total** (24, number, optional): Enter a score from 1-5 to rate your travel service satisfaction.
* **Overall Satisfaction Score** (25, number, optional): Enter a score from 1-5 to rate your overall satisfaction with the travel service.

Note: All fields are optional, and you can provide as much detail as you want for each question.
