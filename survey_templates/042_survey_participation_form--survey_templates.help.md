# survey_participation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The survey participation form is used to collect information from event attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have attended the event.
2. Review the form to ensure you haven't already submitted it.
3. Fill out the form with your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded.
* **Event Attended** (`event_attended`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you attended the event. If you attended, select "Yes", otherwise select "No".
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address as you would like it to be recorded. Please format your email as `username@example.com`.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): Share any comments or feedback you have about the event. This is an optional field, but any input will be greatly appreciated.

## Tips
This form is for event attendees only. If you didn't attend the event, please do not submit this form. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
