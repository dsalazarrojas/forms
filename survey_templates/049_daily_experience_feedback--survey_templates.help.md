# Daily Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Experience Feedback form is designed to collect information about your daily experiences, including what you liked or disliked about your experience, the quality of the service or product, and any other relevant comments or suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

### Step 1: Experience
*   Please enter a brief description of your experience today. This can be a positive or negative comment about the service or product.

### Step 2: Rating
*   Please select a rating for your experience out of 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### Step 3: Comments
*   This is an optional field for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience.

### Step 4: Contact Information
*   Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

### Step 5: Date and Time
*   Please enter the date and time of your experience.

### Step 6: Comments (Optional)
*   This is an optional field for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What was your experience today** (`experience`, `text`, required): 
    Please provide a brief description of your experience today.
*   **What rating would you give today** (`rating`, `select_one`, required): 
    Please select a rating for your experience out of 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
*   **What did you like or dislike about today** (`comment`, `note`, optional): 
    This is an optional field for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience.
*   **Your email** (`email`, `email`, required): 
    Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact`, `text`, required): 
    Please provide any additional contact information you'd like to share with us.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): 
    Please enter the date of your experience.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): 
    Please enter the time of your experience.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): 
    This is an optional field for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when providing your comments and suggestions.
*   If you have any additional comments or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
*   Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve our services and products.
