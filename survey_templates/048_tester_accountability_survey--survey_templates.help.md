# Tester Accountability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tester Accountability Survey is a form designed to collect data on the status of test cycles, blockers, and other relevant information from testers to evaluate their performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for "Test Cycle Status" and "Blocker Status" fields based on your current situation.
2. Provide a description of any accountability issues or blockers in the "Self Assessed Accountability" field.
3. Enter details about blockers in the "Blockers" field.
4. Select the "Task Status" field based on the current status of the task.
5. Enter the "Blocker Count" field with the number of blockers you are experiencing.
6. Select the frequency and impact of blockers in the "Blocker Frequency", "Blocker Impact", and "Blocker Type" fields.
7. Enter the comments for the blockers in the "Blocker Resolution Comments" field.
8. Select the "Tester Feedback" field based on your performance.
9. Enter the "Test Cycle Duration" field with the current duration of the test cycle.
10. Enter the "Tester Name", "Tester Email", "Tester Department", and "Tester Phone" fields with your contact information.
11. Enter the "Blocker Frequency Count", "Blocker Impact Count", and "Blocker Type Count" fields with the relevant counts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Cycle Status**: Select the current status of the test cycle.
	+ Testing
	+ Done
	+ Blocked
	+ Pending
* **Blocker Status**: Select the status of the blockers.
	+ None
	+ Blocking
* **Self Assessed Accountability**: Describe any accountability issues or blockers.
* **Blockers**: Enter details about blockers.
* **Blockers Comments**: Provide detailed comments on blockers.
* **Task Status**: Select the status of the task.
	+ In Progress
	+ Complete
* **Blocker Count**: Enter the number of blockers.
* **Blocker Frequency**: Select the frequency of blockers.
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Blocker Impact**: Select the impact of blockers.
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Blocker Type**: Select the type of blocker.
	+ Technical
	+ Non Technical
* **Blocker Severity**: Select the severity of blockers.
	+ Critical
	+ Major
	+ Minor
* **Blocker Category**: Select the category of blockers.
	+ Performance
	+ Security
	+ Usability
* **Blocker Resolution**: Select the resolution status of blockers.
	+ Completed
	+ Not Completed
* **Blocker Resolution Comments**: Provide comments on blocker resolution.
* **Blocker Frequency Count**: Enter the frequency count of blockers.
* **Blocker Impact Count**: Enter the impact count of blockers.
* **Blocker Type Count**: Enter the type count of blockers.
* **Tester Feedback**: Select your feedback on your performance.
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Test Cycle Duration**: Enter the duration of the test cycle.
* **Tester Name**: Enter your name.
* **Tester Email**: Enter your email.
* **Tester Department**: Enter your department.
* **Tester Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Tester Comments**: Provide any additional comments.
* **Blocker Frequency Count**: Enter the frequency count of blockers.
* **Blocker Impact Count**: Enter the impact count of blockers.
* **Blocker Type Count**: Enter the type count of blockers.
