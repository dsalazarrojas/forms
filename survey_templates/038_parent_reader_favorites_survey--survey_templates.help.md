# Parent Reader Favorites Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your reading habits and preferences as a parent or caregiver. It will help our team understand what you enjoy reading and how you read.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Choose the correct options for each question based on your preferences.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Provide as much or as little information as you like.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments or suggestions" field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is your favorite book genre? 
**What is your favorite book genre?** (`reader_preferences`, select_multiple, optional)
Your favorite book genre is the type of books you enjoy reading the most. This could be Action, Adventure, Classics, Fantasy, Mystery, Romance, Science Fiction, Horror, Literature, Non-Fiction, Historical Fiction, Mystery/Thriller, or any other genre not listed.

### 2. How often do you read? 
**How often do you read?** (`reading_frequency`, select_one, optional)
How often do you read books? This could be Daily, Several times a week, About once a week, Rarely, or Never.

### 3. What time of day do you prefer reading? 
**What time of day do you prefer reading?** (`reading_time`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the times of day when you enjoy reading. Options include Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Late Night.

### 4. What do you do with your family when reading? 
**Which of the following best describe your family reading habits?** (`family_reading_habits`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose how you and your family read together or separately.

### 5. What are your favorite books of all time? 
**What are your favorite books of all time?** (`favorite_books`, text, optional)
Tell us about your favorite books. You can mention as many or as few as you like.

### 6. Who are your favorite authors? 
**Favorite authors** (`favorite_authors`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the authors you enjoy reading the most. You can choose multiple authors.

### 7. Which books do you recommend? 
**Which of the following books do you recommend?** (`recommended_books`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the books you would recommend to others.

### 8. Do you have other books that you prefer? 
**Are there other books that you prefer?** (`reading_preferences_other`, text, optional)
Tell us about any other books you prefer that aren't listed.

### 9. What device do you prefer reading on? 
**What device do you prefer reading on?** (`reading_device`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the devices you prefer reading on, such as Physical book, E-Reader, Tablet, Smartphone, or Computer.

### 10. Do you have other reading habits? 
**Are there any other reading habits that you have?** (`reading_habit_other`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the other reading habits you have, such as reading at the beach or during exercise.

### 11. Do you have any additional comments or suggestions? 
**Additional comments or suggestions** (`notes`, text, optional)
Use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

### 12. What is your email address? 
**Email address** (`email`, email, optional)
Please provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted or receive updates about our surveys.
