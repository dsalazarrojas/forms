# Operational Discipline Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operational Discipline Assessment Survey is a tool used to evaluate the operational discipline of an individual or team. It assesses adherence to established procedures and guidelines that ensure safe and efficient task execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form pages and understand the questions being asked.
2.  Answer each question based on your actual performance and experience.
3.  For the select multiple and select one fields, select the answer that best reflects your situation.
4.  For the text field, provide a clear and concise response.
5.  Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Operational Discipline Survey Form
*  **Operational Discipline Survey Form** (`operational_discipline_assessment_survey_form_page`, note, not required): This is the main form page where you will answer questions about your operational discipline.
*  **procedures** (`procedures`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the procedures that you follow during tasks and activities.
	+  This field is not required, but it's recommended to answer it to provide a more accurate assessment.
*  **safety_practices** (`safety_practices`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the safety practices that you follow during tasks and activities.
	+  This field is not required, but it's recommended to answer it to provide a more accurate assessment.
*  **punctuality** (`punctuality`, select_one, not required): Select whether you always (yes) or never (no) demonstrate punctuality during tasks and activities.
*  **communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the ways you communicate with others during tasks and activities.
	+  This field is not required, but it's recommended to answer it to provide a more accurate assessment.

### Page 2: Additional Comments
*  **comments** (`comments`, text, not required): You can add any additional comments or remarks about your performance or the assessment process.
	+  This field is optional and not required, but it's a good opportunity to provide more context or information that may be relevant to the assessment.
