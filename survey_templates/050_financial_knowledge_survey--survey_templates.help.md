# Financial Knowledge Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Knowledge Survey is a tool to gather information on financial topics and assess the level of awareness among respondents. Please answer the questions honestly to provide valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand that it is not mandatory.
2. Answer the questions based on your knowledge and experience.
3. Choose the correct options for each question.
4. Enter your contact information if you wish to be contacted back.
