# AI Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Adoption Survey is designed to collect data on AI adoption in organizations, including demographic information, company details, and AI experiences and plans. This form is part of a larger survey and is meant to be completed by respondents from various industries and company sizes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Full name**: Please enter your full name as the survey respondent.
2. **Email address**: Enter your professional email address.
3. **Company name**: Enter the name of your organization.
4. **Job title**: Enter your current position in the organization.
5. **Department**: Select the department you belong to.
6. **Company size**: Choose the number of employees in your organization.
7. **Industry sector**: Select the industry that best describes your organization.
8. **Current AI maturity level**: Rate your organization's current AI adoption level.
9. **Annual AI investment budget**: Enter the approximate amount allocated for AI initiatives this year (optional).
10. **Current AI implementations**: Select all the AI technologies your organization is currently using.
11. **Biggest AI challenge**: Choose the primary obstacle to AI implementation in your organization.
12. **Benefits experienced from AI**: Select all the positive outcomes your organization has seen from AI (optional).
13. **AI future priority**: Rate the importance of AI to your organization's strategic plan.
14. **Planned AI investments**: Select all the AI technologies your organization plans to invest in (optional).
15. **AI decision-maker**: Choose the primary decision-making body for AI initiatives.
16. **Percentage of employees trained in AI**: Enter the approximate percentage of staff with AI-related training.
17. **Satisfaction with AI tools**: Rate your satisfaction with existing AI implementations.
18. **Overall AI readiness rating**: Rate your organization's readiness for AI transformation (1-10).
19. **Additional comments**: Share any other thoughts about your AI journey.
20. **Date of survey completion**: This field is automatically completed upon form submission.
