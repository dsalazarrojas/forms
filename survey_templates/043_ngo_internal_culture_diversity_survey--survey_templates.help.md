# NGO Internal Culture Diversity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect anonymous feedback from employees on various aspects of your NGO's internal culture, diversity, and work environment. Your responses will be used to identify areas for improvement and help us become a more inclusive and positive place to work.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure you're filling this form out once per employee. 
- Select all that apply for each question, using the options provided.
- For the 'Action Plan' field, write down a specific action or plan you would like to see implemented.
- For the 'Email' field, enter your official email address.
- For the 'Phone' field, enter your official phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Start** (`survey_start`, text, required: false): This field is not required but please enter your name or any comments you'd like to provide about yourself.
* **Staff Demographics** (`demographic_questions`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all that apply from the options provided. This will help us understand your demographics and experiences.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_questions`, select_one, required: false): Please select the feedback option that best describes your experiences and perceptions.
* **Workplace Experience** (`experience_questions`, time, required: false): You will need to select the time of your experience working at our NGO.
* **Cultural Diversity** (`culture_questions`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all that apply from the options provided. This will help us understand your experiences and perspectives on cultural diversity.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`, note, required: false): Write down a specific action or plan you would like to see implemented. This is your opportunity to suggest positive change.
* **Email** (`email_input`, email, required: false): Please enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`phone_input`, text, required: false): Please enter your official phone number. 

## Tips
- Please be honest and open about your experiences and perceptions.
- Your responses will help us create a more inclusive and positive work environment for everyone. 
- Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input.
