# Incident Improvement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Improvement Report Form is designed to collect information about incidents that occurred within the organization. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and provide insights for future prevention.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing a brief description of what happened in the first field, "What happened."
2. In the "Incident Summary" field, provide a more detailed explanation of the incident.
3. Select the type of incident that best describes what occurred, choosing from "Non-conformance Incident", "Near Miss", or "Corrective Action" in the "Non-conformance Incident" field.
4. Choose the categories that apply to your incident by selecting one or more from "Environmental", "Quality", and "Safety" in the "Category" field.
5. If applicable, enter the location where the incident occurred in the "Location" field.
6. Record the date of the incident in the "Date" field, in MM/DD format.
7. Enter the time of the incident in the "Time" field, in 12-hour format.
8. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Description" field.
9. If you have identified a root cause, enter your explanation in the "Root Cause" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What happened** (Page 1)
	+ Enter a brief description of what happened in a concise and factual manner.
* **Incident Summary** (Page 1)
	+ Provide a more detailed explanation of the incident, including as much information as possible.
* **Non-conformance Incident** (Page 1)
	+ Choose the option that best describes the type of incident that occurred.
* **Category** (Page 1)
	+ Select one or more categories that apply to your incident (Environmental, Quality, Safety).
* **Location** (Page 2)
	+ If applicable, enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Date** (Page 2)
	+ Record the date of the incident in MM/DD format.
* **Time** (Page 2)
	+ Enter the time of the incident in 12-hour format.
* **Description** (Page 2)
	+ Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any contributing factors.
* **Cause** (Page 3)
	+ If you have identified a cause for the incident, enter your explanation here.
* **Root Cause** (Page 3)
	+ If you have identified a root cause, enter your explanation here.

## Tips
- Be as concise and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
- Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of this form, please don't hesitate to contact the relevant department or team for guidance and support.
