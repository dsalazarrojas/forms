# lead-magnet-checklist-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help users create and configure a lead magnet checklist for their marketing strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the pages you want to include in your lead magnet checklist by clicking on the "Add Page" button.
2. Complete each page by filling out the fields with relevant information.
3. Use the "Save" button to save your progress and continue editing or submit the form when you are finished.
