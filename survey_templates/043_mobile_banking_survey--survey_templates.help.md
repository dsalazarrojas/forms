# Mobile Banking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the Mobile Banking Survey form, helping you understand its purpose and how to complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each page of the form and ensure you understand the questions and fields provided.
2.  Answer each question truthfully, considering the best fit for your experience with the mobile banking service.
3.  Be thorough and provide as much detail as possible for any open-ended questions.
4.  If you encounter any issues or need clarification on any question, seek support from a representative or contact our team at support@email.com.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Mobile Banking Service** (`mobile_banking_service`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks about your current or preferred mobile banking service. Please select the option that best describes your mobile banking experience or preference.
  *   For this question, you can choose one of the provided options that best fits your experience or preference.
- **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required/optional): This question assesses your level of satisfaction with the mobile banking service. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is 'Very Dissatisfied' and 5 is 'Very Satisfied'.
  *   Choose a rating that best reflects your current satisfaction level.
- **Improvements** (`improvements`, `note`, required/optional): This question is an opportunity to provide suggestions or areas for improvement for the mobile banking service. You can type any relevant comments or suggestions you may have.
  *   If you have any specific suggestions or comments, please type them in this field, considering your current experience with the service.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional comments or feedback on your mobile banking experience.
  *   Please feel free to share as much detail as possible, including any positive or negative aspects of your experience.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks for your contact information so our team can follow up on your feedback.
