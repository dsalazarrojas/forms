# Credit Application Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Credit Application Feedback Form" is designed to gather information about your experience with our credit application service. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our service and provide better support for you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question based on your genuine experience.
2.  Be as detailed as possible in your responses.
3.  If you need help with any question or field, do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How likely do you think you would use this service in the future?** (`credit_application_feedback_form`, number, optional): Answer this question by selecting a number from 1 to 5, where 1 means "Not likely at all" and 5 means "Very likely".
* **How clear was the credit application process?** (`credit_application_process`, number, optional): Answer this question by selecting a number from 1 to 5, where 1 means "Not clear at all" and 5 means "Very clear".
* **How satisfied were you with the customer service?** (`customer_service`, select multiple, optional): Select all options that apply, from "Excellent", "Good", "Fair" to "Poor", to describe your experience with our customer service team.
* **What was your overall experience with the service?** (`overall_experience`, text, optional): Provide a written description of your experience, including both positive and negative aspects.
* **Any additional comments?** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional thoughts, suggestions, or feedback you may have about our service.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (`contact_information`, select one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted, from "Email", "Phone", "Mail", or "None", to help us tailor our communication with you.
