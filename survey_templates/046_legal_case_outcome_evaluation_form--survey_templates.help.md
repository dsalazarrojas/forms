# Legal Case Outcome Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate the outcome of legal cases, providing a structured way to collect feedback and ratings from various stakeholders, including case managers, lawyers, and clients. The form is essential for analyzing and improving the performance of case managers, lawyers, and the overall legal case process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Legal Case Outcome** option from the dropdown menu for the case outcome.
2. Choose the **Case Type** from the dropdown menu.
3. Select the **Judge Decision** from the dropdown menu.
4. Provide written feedback for the **Client Feedback**, **Lawyer Feedback**, and **Case Manager** sections.
5. Rate the case outcome using the **Case Outcome Rating**, **Overall Outcome Rating**, **Lawyer Rating**, **Judge Rating**, and **Case Type Rating** fields.
6. Provide any additional comments in the **Additional Comments** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Legal Case Outcome**: Select the outcome of the legal case (Outcome 1, Outcome 2, or Outcome 3).
- **Case Type**: Choose the type of case (Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3).
- **Judge Decision**: Select the decision made by the judge (Decision 1, Decision 2, or Decision 3).
- **Client Feedback**: Provide any feedback from the client regarding the case outcome.
- **Lawyer Feedback**: Provide any feedback from the lawyer regarding the case outcome.
- **Case Manager**: Provide any feedback or comments from the case manager.
- **Case Manager Comment**: Provide any additional comments from the case manager (optional).
- **Lawyer Manager**: Provide any feedback or comments from the lawyer manager (optional).
- **Lawyer Manager Comment**: Provide any additional comments from the lawyer manager (optional).
- **Case Outcome Rating**: Rate the case outcome on a scale.
- **Case Outcome Satisfaction**: Rate the client's satisfaction with the case outcome on a scale.
- **Overall Outcome Rating**: Rate the overall outcome of the case on a scale.
- **Overall Outcome Satisfaction**: Rate the overall satisfaction with the case outcome on a scale.
- **Lawyer Rating**: Rate the lawyer's performance on a scale.
- **Lawyer Satisfaction**: Rate the lawyer's satisfaction with the case outcome on a scale.
- **Case Type Rating**: Rate the case type on a scale.
- **Case Type Satisfaction**: Rate the case type satisfaction on a scale.
- **Judge Rating**: Rate the judge's performance on a scale.
- **Judge Satisfaction**: Rate the judge's satisfaction with the case outcome on a scale.
- **Date of Disposition**: Enter the date of the case disposition (if applicable).
- **Time of Disposition**: Enter the time of the case disposition (if applicable).
- **Case Status**: Select the current status of the case (Status 1, Status 2, or Status 3).
- **Case Outcome Comments**: Provide any additional comments regarding the case outcome.
- **Lawyer Comments**: Provide any comments from the lawyer (optional).
- **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments regarding the case outcome.
