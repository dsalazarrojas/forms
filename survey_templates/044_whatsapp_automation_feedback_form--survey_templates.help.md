# whatsapp_automation_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the WhatsApp automation service, helping us understand your experiences, likes, and dislikes about its features, benefits, and overall usability.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully read each field's label and instructions.
2. Choose the relevant options for each field that best describe your experience or thoughts.
3. Enter your answers accurately and thoroughly, providing clear and concise feedback.
4. Review your submission before submitting it, ensuring that you've covered all relevant aspects of the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **features_benefits** (`features_benefits`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all features that you've used or benefited from during your interaction with the WhatsApp automation service. You can choose from:
	+ Automated Messages
	+ Sent Messages
* **suggestions** (`suggestions`, select_one, required: false): If you have a suggestion for improvement or a feature request, please choose from:
	+ Feature Request
	+ Feature Suggestion
	+ Bug Report
	+ Other (please describe briefly)
* **ratings** (`ratings`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the WhatsApp automation service, from 1 to 5.
* **open_ended** (`open_ended`, text, required: false): If you have any additional comments, thoughts, or suggestions not covered by the previous questions, please share them here. This is your chance to provide more in-depth feedback.
* **ratings2** (`ratings2`, number, required: false): Similar to ratings, rate your experience with the service, focusing on a specific aspect, from 1 to 5. 
* **open_ended2** (`open_ended2`, text, required: false): Provide any further comments or thoughts that are not covered by the ratings question.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please provide your email address, if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or discussion.
