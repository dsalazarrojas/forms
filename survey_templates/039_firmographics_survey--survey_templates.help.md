# Firmographics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Firmographics Survey is designed to collect comprehensive firmographic information about a company. This information is essential for understanding the company's demographics, size, industry, location, and other attributes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill in the required fields. Each page is designed to collect specific firmographic information about the company. You can submit your answers to the current page before moving on to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **About The Company** (`about_the_company`, text, optional): A brief description of the company.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): The official name of the company.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, text, optional): The type of company (e.g., startup, corporation, etc.).
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, text, optional): The number of employees in the company.
* **About Location** (`about_location`, text, optional): A brief description of the company's location.
* **Number Of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, optional): The number of employees in the company.
* **Company Industry** (`company_industry`, text, optional): The industry in which the company operates.
* **Employee Demographics** (`employee_demographics`, text, optional): A brief description of the company's employee demographics.
* **Employee Diversity** (`employee_diversity`, text, optional): A brief description of the company's employee diversity initiatives.
* **About Ownership** (`about_ownership`, text, optional): A brief description of the company's ownership structure.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): The company's official website URL.
* **Company Facebook Link** (`company_facebook_link`, text, optional): The company's Facebook link.
* **Company Twitter Link** (`company_twitter_link`, text, optional): The company's Twitter link.
* **Company Instagram Link** (`company_instagram_link`, text, optional): The company's Instagram link.
* **Company LinkedIn Link** (`company_linkedin_link`, text, optional): The company's LinkedIn link.
* **Company Address 1** (`company_address_1`, text, optional): The company's address line 1.
* **Company Address 2** (`company_address_2`, text, optional): The company's address line 2.
* **Company City** (`company_city`, text, optional): The city where the company is located.
* **Company State** (`company_state`, text, optional): The state where the company is located.
* **Company Postal Code** (`company_postal_code`, text, optional): The company's postal code.
* **Company Country** (`company_country`, text, optional): The country where the company is located.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, optional): The company's phone number.
* **Company Fax** (`company_fax`, text, optional): The company's fax number.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, optional): The company's email address.
* **Tips**:
	+ Please be as accurate as possible when filling out this form. If you're unsure about any information, please consult with your company's administrator or representative.
	+ Please ensure that the company's information is not already available in our system to avoid duplication of effort.
	+ If you're unsure about whether the company's information is already available, please check with our support team.
