# Online Dating Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Dating Survey" form is designed to collect information about users' behavior and preferences when it comes to online dating. By completing this survey, you will help us better understand how people interact with online dating platforms and what they value when searching for a romantic partner or relationships online.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply select the most accurate answers for each question, following the instructions below. Make sure to answer all required fields marked with *required*.

1. **How often do you use dating apps?**: Select the frequency of your online dating app usage from the provided options.
2. **Why did you take this survey?**: Please provide a brief reason for taking the survey.
3. **What is the most important feature in a dating app?**: Select all features that you consider essential in a dating app. You can choose more than one feature if needed.
4. **Have you ever had an online relationship?**: Select Yes or No to indicate if you have ever been in an online relationship.
5. **Are you currently single?**: Select Yes or No to indicate your current relationship status.
6. **What is your age?**: Enter your age in years.
7. **What is your gender?**: Select the correct gender identity.
8. **What is your educational background?**: Select the highest level of education completed.
9. **What is your annual income?**: Enter your annual income, if applicable.
10. **What is your current city?**: Type the name of the city you currently reside in.
11. **Which city do you prefer for dating?**: Type the name of the city you prefer for dating.
12. **Which device do you use for dating apps?**: Select all devices you use for online dating.
13. **How do you access dating apps?**: Select all methods you use to access online dating platforms.
14. **Have you ever been in an online relationship?**: Select Yes or No to indicate if you have ever been in an online relationship (this field is identical to #4, but you can confirm your answer here).
15. **None**: Please do not select this option.
16. **None 2**: Please do not select this option.
17. **None 3**: Please do not select this option.
18. **None 4**: Please do not select this option.
