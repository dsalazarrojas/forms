# Community Bake Off Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for the Community Bake Off Voting event. It collects details about the voting process, including the date of voting, unique vote reference number, and the voter's information. The form also asks for the voter's opinion on various categories, such as best overall baked good, best bread, best dessert, and so on. Additionally, it requests feedback on the event and the bakers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Voting Information" page and read the instructions.
2. Fill in the "Vote Date" field with the date you are casting your vote.
3. Enter your unique "Vote ID" in the corresponding field.
4. Provide your "Voter Information" in the "Voter Information" field, if required.
5. Select your "Voter Type" (e.g., Community Member, Event Attendee, Judge, Organizer, or Guest).
6. Select the "Event Name" and "Event Location" for the bake off event.
7. Choose your favorite entries in each category (e.g., Best Overall Baked Good, Best Bread, Best Dessert, etc.).
8. Rate the overall quality of the bakers based on the "Judging Criteria" and select your rating for "Taste Rating", "Presentation Rating", and "Creativity Rating".
9. Select your favorite baker from the list of entries.
10. Provide any "Event Feedback" about the bake off event.
11. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Would Attend Again".
12. Add any "Additional Comments" about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voting Information**: This field provides instructions for the voting process. (Required: false)
* **Vote Date**: Enter the date on which you are casting your vote. (Required: true)
* **Vote ID**: Enter your unique vote reference number. (Required: true)
* **Voter Information**: Enter any additional information about yourself as a voter. (Optional)
* **Voter Name**: Enter your full name. (Required: true)
* **Voter Email**: Enter your email address. (Required: true)
* **Voter Type**: Select your relationship to the event (e.g., Community Member, Event Attendee, Judge, Organizer, or Guest). (Required: true)
* **Event Information**: Enter any additional details about the bake off event. (Optional)
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the bake off event. (Required: true)
* **Event Location**: Enter the location where the event is being held. (Optional)
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the bake off event. (Optional)
* **Category Votes**: Select your favorite entry in each category. (Optional)
* **Best Overall Baked Good**: Select your favorite entry from the overall category. (Required: true)
* **Best Bread**: Select your favorite bread entry. (Optional)
* **Best Dessert**: Select your favorite dessert entry. (Optional)
* **Best Cookies**: Select your favorite cookie entry. (Optional)
* **Best Cake**: Select your favorite cake entry. (Optional)
* **Best Pastry**: Select your favorite pastry entry. (Optional)
* **Peoples Choice**: Select your personal favorite entry. (Required: true)
* **Judging Criteria**: Rate the overall quality of the bakers based on the judging criteria. (Optional)
* **Taste Rating**: Rate the overall taste of the entries. (Required: true)
* **Presentation Rating**: Rate the overall presentation of the entries. (Required: true)
* **Creativity Rating**: Rate the overall creativity of the entries. (Required: true)
* **Favorite Baker**: Select your favorite baker. (Optional)
* **Event Feedback**: Enter any feedback about the event. (Optional)
* **Would Attend Again**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Would Attend Again". (Required: true)
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions. (Optional)

Note: The required fields are marked as "true" in the form YAML, and the optional fields are marked as "false".
