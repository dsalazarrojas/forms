# Section Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Section Selection Form is designed to collect information about which sections you would like to select.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply click on the text input field next to each section label.
2. Type your selection for each section, and click "Submit" when you're finished.
3. Note that only one section is required, indicated by a asterisk (\*) next to its label.
