# hoa_fee_reduction_support_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "hoa_fee_reduction_support_survey" form is designed to collect information about Homeowners Association (HOA) fee reduction requests and related service values. It helps HOA administrators or support staff to track and manage these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field and provide the required information.
2. Make sure to fill out all fields marked as "required" to ensure that your data is accurately recorded.
3. If a field is optional, you can skip it or provide additional information if you feel it's relevant to your case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **HOA Dues** (`1`, `text`, required/optional: false)
Enter a brief description of the current HOA dues status.

### **Service Value** (`2`, `text`, required/optional: false)
Describe the current service value, including any benefits or improvements.

### **Fee Reduction Needs** (`3`, `text`, required/optional: false)
Provide details about the fee reduction needs, including any specific requests.

### **Service Value Improvement** (`4`, `text`, required/optional: false)
Explain how the service value can be improved to address fee reduction requests.

### **HOA Fee Reduction Request** (`5`, `text`, required/optional: false)
Enter a description of the HOA fee reduction request, including any supporting information.

### **Fee Reduction Impact** (`6`, `text`, required/optional: false)
Describe the impact of the fee reduction request on the HOA's finances or operations.

### **HOA Dues Impact** (`7`, `text`, required/optional: false)
Explain how the fee reduction request will affect the HOA dues, including any potential changes.

### **Fee Reduction Request Status** (`8`, `text`, required/optional: false)
Enter the current status of the fee reduction request, including any updates or progress.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear when providing information in each field.
* Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
