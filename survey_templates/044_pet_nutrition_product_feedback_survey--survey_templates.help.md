# Pet Nutrition Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about our pet nutrition products. We want to understand how you use our products, what you like or dislike about them, and how we can improve them for you and your pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question honestly and thoroughly. If you have any questions or need help with a particular field, please don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you hear about our product?** (`intro_question`): This field asks where you first learned about our pet nutrition product. Select all that apply from the options provided:
	+ I saw an ad on social media
	+ I saw an ad on a website
	+ I got a recommendation from a friend
	+ Other (please specify in the field below)
* **What was the name of the product you used?** (`product_name`): Please enter the name of the product you used, as this will help us to identify the specific product you are providing feedback on.
* **How did you find our product?** (`question1`): This field asks for your honest opinion of our product. Select one of the options:
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Bad
	+ Very bad
* **How would you rate the quality of our product?** (`question2`): Please enter a numerical value from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **What was the price of the product?** (`question3`): Please enter the price you paid for the product.
* **How satisfied were you with the product?** (`satisfaction`): This field asks for your level of satisfaction with the product. Select one of the options:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **How did you use our product?** (`question4`): This field asks how you used the product. Select all that apply:
	+ As a food for my pet
	+ As a treat for my pet
	+ As a supplement for my pet
	+ Other (please specify in the field below)
* **What type of pet do you have?** (`question5`): Please select all that apply from the options provided:
	+ Dog
	+ Cat
	+ Bird
	+ Fish
	+ Other
* **What do you like or dislike about the product?** (`question6`): Please enter your honest opinion about the product, including anything you liked or disliked about it.
* **Can we use this information for marketing purposes?** (`question7`): Please select one of the options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **How did you contact us?** (`contact_info`): This field is optional and allows you to specify any contact method you used to reach out to us.
* **Is this your first time purchasing from us?** (`question9`): Please select one of the options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Can we follow up with you about your experience?** (`question10`): Please select one of the options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **How likely is it that you will recommend our product?** (`question11`): Please select one of the options:
	+ Very likely
	+ Likely
	+ Not likely
	+ Not likely at all
* **How did you contact us?** (`contact_info_2`): This field is optional and allows you to specify any contact method you used to reach out to us.
