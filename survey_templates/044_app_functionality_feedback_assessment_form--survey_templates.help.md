# App Functionality Feedback Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on the app's functionality, helping us understand its performance and areas for improvement. Please provide honest and detailed feedback to help us create a better user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the assessment date when you last used the app.
2. Enter your name and email address (optional).
3. Identify your user level (Free user, Paid subscriber, Premium member, Enterprise user, or Administrator).
4. Rate the app's core functionality, feature completeness, responsiveness, crash frequency, and bugs encountered.
5. Provide details about any bugs or crashes.
6. Evaluate the data accuracy and integrity, synchronization between devices, offline functionality, and integration with other services (if applicable).
7. Rate your overall satisfaction with the app's functionality and likelihood to continue using it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
The following is a detailed explanation of each field:

* **Assessment date** (1: Assessment date): Date when you last used the app.
* **User name** (2: User name): Your name (optional for anonymity).
* **User ID** (3: User ID): Your account identifier (if applicable).
* **Email address** (4: Email address): Your email address (optional).
* **App being assessed** (5: App name): The title of the app you're providing feedback on.
* **App version** (6: App version): The version of the app you last used.
* **User level** (7: User level): Your user level (Free user, Paid subscriber, Premium member, Enterprise user, or Administrator).
* **Core functionality rating** (8: Core functionality rating): Rate the app's core functionality from Excellent to Not functional.
* **Feature completeness** (9: Feature completeness): Rate the completeness of the app's features from All features present to Significantly incomplete.
* **Responsiveness rating** (10: Responsiveness rating): Rate the app's response time from Excellent - instant response to Very slow - frustrating.
* **Crash frequency** (11: Crash frequency): Rate how often the app crashes from Never crashes to Constantly crashes.
* **Crash details** (12: Crash details): Describe situations where the app crashes.
* **Bugs encountered** (13: Bugs encountered): Rate the frequency and severity of bugs from No bugs found to Numerous critical bugs.
* **Bug description** (14: Bug description): Describe any bugs you've encountered.
* **Data accuracy** (15: Data accuracy): Rate the data's reliability from Excellent - always accurate to Cannot rely on the data.
* **Sync reliability** (16: Sync reliability): Rate the synchronization between devices from Syncs perfectly to Does not sync.
* **Offline functionality** (17: Offline functionality): Rate the app's offline capability from Works well offline to Requires internet connection.
* **Integration quality** (18: Integration quality): Rate the integration with other services from Integrations work flawlessly to Integrations do not work.
* **Functionality satisfaction** (19: Functionality satisfaction): Rate your overall satisfaction with the app's functionality from Very satisfied to Very dissatisfied.
* **Likely to continue use** (20: Likely to continue use): Rate your likelihood to continue using the app from Very likely to Very unlikely.
