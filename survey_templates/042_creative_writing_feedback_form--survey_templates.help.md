# Creative Writing Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Creative Writing Feedback Form is designed to gather information about your creative writing needs. This form will help you provide feedback on the content, length, and tone of your story, as well as give you space to suggest characters and settings that would enhance your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by writing a brief description of your story in the "What is this story about" field.
2. Indicate how long you expect the story to be in the "How long do you expect the story to be" field.
3. Choose the type of story you are looking for in the "What is this a" field.
4. Describe the characters in your story in the "Can you describe the characters in the story" field.
5. Specify the setting for your story in the "What is the setting" field.
6. Provide any additional feedback you have in the "What feedback do you have" field.
7. Enter your email address to receive any updates or follow-ups.
8. Add any additional comments you have in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is this story about** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This field is meant for a brief description of your story. Please provide a short summary of the story you have in mind.
* **How long do you expect the story to be** (`story_length`, `number`, required: false): This field is for indicating the expected length of your story. Please enter a number to help writers tailor their writing to your needs.
* **What is this a** (`story_type`, `text`, required: false): This field is for choosing the type of story you are looking for. Please select from the options provided to help writers understand the tone and style of your story.
* **Can you describe the characters in the story** (`characters`, `text`, required: false): This field is for describing the characters in your story. Please provide any details about the characters that would help writers bring your story to life.
* **What is the setting** (`setting`, `text`, required: false): This field is for specifying the setting for your story. Please provide any details about the setting that would help writers create an immersive experience.
* **What feedback do you have** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional feedback you have about the story. Please share any specific likes or dislikes you have to help writers improve your story.
* **Your Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This field is for entering your email address. This will help us get in touch with you for any follow-ups or updates.
* **Your Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering your name. Please provide your name to help writers address you personally.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have about the story. Please share any thoughts or ideas that come to mind.

Note: The fields marked as required: false can be left blank if you do not have information for them. However, providing as much information as possible will help writers tailor their work to your specific needs.
