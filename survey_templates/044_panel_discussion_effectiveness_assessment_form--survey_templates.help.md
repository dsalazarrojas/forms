# Panel Discussion Effectiveness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from panel discussion participants on the effectiveness of the discussion. The questions are meant to be answered based on your personal experience and opinion.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question as truthfully and comprehensively as possible.
2. Provide detailed explanations and examples to support your answers.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Topic**: This field is for you to provide feedback on the topic of the discussion. Please describe the topic and how it was presented.
* **Presentation**: In this field, describe the presentation style and content. Was it engaging, informative, or both?
* **Panelists**: Please provide feedback on the panelists' performance, including their expertise, presentation skills, and interaction with each other.
* **Engagement**: How engaging was the discussion? Was it interactive, stimulating, or both?
* **Overall**: Rate your overall experience and impression of the discussion.
* **Conclusion**: Summarize your understanding of the main points discussed.
* **Feedback**: This is your chance to provide any feedback, suggestions, or criticism.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback that didn't fit in the previous sections, please add them here.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering each question.
* Use examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, contact the form creators at form@gic.mx.
