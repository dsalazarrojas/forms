# Cartoon Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you understand how to complete the Cartoon Survey form. The form is designed to gather information about your preferences and opinions related to cartoons and anime.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions that are marked as "required" to ensure that your responses are complete.
2. Choose the options that best fit your preferences for each question.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Age Range
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand the demographics of fans. Please choose your age range from the options provided.

### Gender
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): This question is used to gather information on the gender identity of fans.

### How Often Do You Watch Cartoons or Anime?
* **How often do you watch cartoons or anime?** (`frequency_of_watching`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand your frequency of watching cartoons or anime.

### Favorite Cartoon Genre
* **Favorite Cartoon Genre** (`favorite_genre`, select_multiple, required): This question allows you to select all the genres you enjoy.

### Preferred Art Style
* **Preferred Art Style** (`preferred_art_style`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand your preference for art styles.

### Primary Platform for Watching
* **Primary Platform for Watching** (`favorite_platform`, select_one, required): This question is used to gather information on your primary platform for watching cartoons or anime.

### What Do You Look for in a Main Character?
* **What do you look for in a main character?** (`character_preference`, select_multiple, required): This question helps us understand your preferences for a main character.

### Rate the Importance of a Deep Plot
* **Rate the importance of a deep plot** (`plot_importance`, number, required): This question is used to gather information on the importance of a deep plot.

### Favorite Sidekick Archetype
* **Favorite Sidekick Archetype** (`favorite_sidekick_archetype`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand your preference for sidekick archetypes.

### List Your Top 3 Favorite Cartoons of All Time
* **List your top 3 favorite cartoons of all time** (`top_3_cartoons`, text, required): This question allows you to list your top 3 favorite cartoons.

### Favorite Animation Studio
* **Favorite Animation Studio** (`favorite_animation_studio`, select_one, required): This question is used to gather information on your favorite animation studio.

### Opinion on Modern Reboots of Classic Cartoons
* **Opinion on modern reboots of classic cartoons** (`cartoon_reboots_opinion`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand your opinion on modern reboots of classic cartoons.

### Importance of Voice Acting
* **Importance of voice acting** (`voice_acting_importance`, number, required): This question is used to gather information on the importance of voice acting.

### Do You Purchase Cartoon-Related Merchandise?
* **Do you purchase cartoon-related merchandise?** (`purchase_merch`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand your purchase behavior related to cartoon merchandise.

### Do You Participate in Fan Communities or Forums?
* **Do you participate in fan communities or forums?** (`community_engagement`, select_one, required): This question is used to gather information on your participation in fan communities or forums.

### Have You Ever Cosplayed as a Cartoon Character?
* **Have you ever cosplayed as a cartoon character?** (`cosplay_interest`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand your interest in cosplaying.

### What Would You Like to See More of in the Cartoon Industry?
* **What would you like to see more of in the cartoon industry?** (`industry_suggestions`, text, required): This question is used to gather information on your suggestions for the cartoon industry.

### Thank You for Your Responses
* **Thank you for your responses** (`feedback_confirmation`, text, required): This is a confirmation question to thank you for your participation.
