# Digital Marketing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Digital Marketing Survey form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Introduction section with your brief description of your work experience and job function.
2. In the Demographic Questions section, enter your age and select your income level.
3. In the Email section, enter your email address.
4. In the Phone section, enter your phone number (optional).
5. In the Marketing Channels section, select all the marketing channels you use.
6. In the Social Media Channels section, select all the social media platforms you use for advertising.
7. In the Digital Tools section, select all the digital tools you use.
8. In the Email Marketing Channel section, select your preferred email marketing channel.
9. In the Social Media Ads section, select all the social media platforms you use for advertising.
10. In the Preferences section, select your preferred digital tool.
11. You can skip the Email Marketing Strategy section as it's optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction**: (`text`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your work experience and job function. This will help us understand your background and perspective on digital marketing.

### Demographic Questions
* **What is your demographic information** (`number`, `number`, required): Please provide your demographic details: Age, income, etc.

### Age
* **Age** (`number`, `number`, required): Enter your age.

### Income Range
* **Income** (`select_multiple`, `Low`, `Medium`, `High`, required): Select your income level: Low, Medium, or High.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Marketing Channels
* **What marketing channels do you follow** (`select_multiple`, `Social media`, `Email marketing`, `SMS`, `None`, required): Select all the marketing channels you use.

### Social Media Channels
* **What social media channels do you use** (`select_multiple`, `Twitter`, `Facebook`, `Instagram`, `Reddit`, required): Select all the social media platforms you use.

### Digital Tools
* **What digital tools do you use** (`select_multiple`, `Google AdWords`, `Google Analytics`, `Facebook Ads Manager`, required): Select all the digital tools you use.

### Email Marketing Channel
* **How do you prefer to use email marketing?** (`select_multiple`, `Email marketing`, `SMS`, required): Select your preferred email marketing channel.

### Social Media Ads
* **Which social media platforms do you use for advertising** (`select_multiple`, `Twitter`, `Facebook`, `Instagram`, `None`, required): Select all the social media platforms you use for advertising.

### Preferences
* **Which digital tool do you prefer?** (`select_multiple`, `Google Analytics`, `Google AdWords`, required): Select your preferred digital tool.

### Email Marketing Strategy
* **Email marketing strategy** (`select_multiple`, `Good`, `Fair`, optional): Select your preferred email marketing strategy (optional). 

Note that I followed the original fields exactly as described in the YAML specification, but some fields might be better reworded or merged into single fields as mentioned in the "internal reasoning block".
