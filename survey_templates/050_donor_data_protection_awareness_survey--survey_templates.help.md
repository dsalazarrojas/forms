# Donor Data Protection Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from donors about their awareness and understanding of data protection practices. The form is intended to gauge the level of awareness and compliance of donors with respect to their data protection practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each section of the form carefully, and do not hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need clarification on any of the sections.
2. Make sure to answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
3. The form is divided into the following sections:
	* General Info: This section gathers information about you as a donor.
	* Privacy Practices: This section asks about your understanding of data protection practices in our organization.
	* Data Collection: This section inquires about how you collect data from us.
	* Form Submission Results: This section is only visible to our staff and is used for our internal review.
	* Time Completed: This section is also only visible to our staff and is used for our internal review.
	* Email: This section is for your contact information.
	* Phone: This section is for your contact information.
	* Note: This section is for any additional comments or notes.
	* Donor Type: This section is about your type of donor (Individual, Company, or Foundation).
	* Donor Category: This section is about your donor category (Healthcare, Non-profit, Charity, or Education).
	* Data Collected: This section is about the data we collect from you.
	* Data Used: This section is about the data we use from you.
	* Data Retain: This section is about whether we retain your data.
	* Data Share: This section is about whether we share your data.
	* Data Breach: This section is about whether you are aware of any data breaches.
	* General Ack: This section is about your acknowledgment of our general data protection practices.
	* Specific Ack: This section is about your acknowledgment of our specific data protection practices.
	* Specific Ack Notice: This section is about your notice regarding our specific data protection practices.
	* General Notice: This section is about your notice regarding our general data protection practices.
