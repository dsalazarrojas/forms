# Healthcare Disparities Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect demographic information about patients for healthcare disparities analysis and service provision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide the patient's name and date of birth.
2. Choose the most appropriate contact method for the patient (phone, email, or in-person).
3. Select the clinic where the patient received care.
4. Identify the person the patient would like to be contacted.
5. Provide the contact person's phone number.
6. Enter the contact person's email address.
7. Enter the patient's address and ZIP code.
8. Select the patient's race and ethnicity.
9. Select the patient's preferred language.
10. Provide any additional comments or notes about the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their ID or identification card.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Method** (`patient_contact_method`): Select the method of contact the patient prefers to receive information. Phone, email, or in-person are available options.
* **Clinic Name** (`clinic_name`): Enter the name of the clinic where the patient received care.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Choose the person the patient would like to be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the patient's address in the format "Street Address", "City", "State", and "ZIP Code".
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`): Enter the patient's ZIP code.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the patient's city.
* **State** (`state`): Select the patient's state.
* **Race and Ethnicity** (`race_and_ethnicity`): Select the patient's race and ethnicity from the provided options.
* **Language** (`language`): Select the patient's preferred language.
* **Language Other** (`language_other_description`): Enter any other languages spoken by the patient (if not listed as a standard option).
* **Race Other** (`race_other_description`): Enter any other race or ethnicity not listed as a standard option.
* **Gender** (`patient_gender`): Select the patient's gender.
* **Gender Other** (`gender_other_description`): Enter any other gender not listed as a standard option.
* **Age** (`patient_age`): Enter the patient's age.
* **Age Group** (`patient_age_group`): Select the patient's age group.
* **Gender ID** (`patient_gender_id`): Enter any gender identity not listed as a standard option.
* **Race** (`patient_race`): Select the patient's race.
* **Race Other** (`race_other_description`): Enter any other race not listed as a standard option.
* **Ethnicity** (`patient_ethnicity`): Select the patient's ethnicity.
* **Ethnicity Other** (`ethnicity_other_description`): Enter any other ethnicity not listed as a standard option.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or information about the patient. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If a field has options, select the most accurate one for the patient.
* If a field requires an additional description, use clear and concise language to provide more information.
* Be respectful of patient's identity and privacy.
