# Sports Event Vendor Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from sports event vendors, helping event organizers understand their experiences and interactions. It's meant for vendors who have worked with event organizers to collect their satisfaction and communication experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you're a vendor who has interacted with event organizers.
2. Read the form carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
3. Complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
4. Choose your answers carefully, selecting the option that best describes your experience.
5. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**:
 	* Event Details is a text field where you can provide additional context about your experience with the event.
 	* Please be as specific as possible about the event you're referring to.
* **How would you rate your overall experience with the event?**:
 	* This question asks you to rate your overall experience with the event on a scale from Very good to Very bad.
	+ Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **What were the outcomes of your experience?**:
 	* This question asks you to describe the outcomes or results of your experience with the event.
 	* Please provide as much detail as possible about what happened as a result of your interaction with the event.
* **How was the communication with event organizers?**:
 	* This question asks you to rate the quality of communication you received from event organizers.
	+ Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **How was the level of support you received from event vendors?**:
 	* This question asks you to rate the level of support you received from event vendors.
	+ Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Overall experience**:
 	* This question asks you to rate your overall experience with the event on a scale from Very good to Very bad.
	+ Choose all options that apply to your experience.
* **Do you have any additional feedback?**:
 	* This question asks you to provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience with the event.
 	* Please use this space to provide as much detail as possible.
* **Contact Info**:
 	* This question asks you to provide any contact information if you'd like to be contacted.
 	* Please enter your email address or phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Email**:
 	* This question asks you to enter your email address.
	+ Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**:
 	* This question asks you to enter your phone number.
	+ Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Date**:
 	* This question asks you to enter the date you interacted with the event.
	+ Please use the date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Time**:
 	* This question asks you to enter the time you interacted with the event.
	+ Please use the time format (HH:MM).
* **Submit**:
 	* This question asks you to submit your completed form.
	+ Please click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback.
* **Cancel**:
 	* This question asks you to cancel your submission.
	+ Please click on the "Cancel" button to discard your changes.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by vendors only, so please make sure to fill it out carefully and truthfully.
