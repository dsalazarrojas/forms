# Student Reflection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Reflection Form is a tool used to capture student reflections on their current grade, class, and other relevant information. It helps students reflect on their learning progress, identify areas of improvement, and set goals for future growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **What is your current grade?**: This is a select-one field that asks you to select your current grade level. Choose the correct grade from the provided options.

2. **What class are you in?**: This is another select-one field that asks you to choose the current class you are in. Select the correct class from the provided options.

3. **What is your name?**: This is a text field where you should enter your full name.

4. **Which school do you attend?**: This is a text field where you should enter the name of the school you attend.

5. **Which subjects do you like?**: This is a select-multiple field where you can choose multiple subjects you are interested in.

6. **What do you want to reflect on?**: This is a text field where you can write a reflection on your current progress and goals.

7. **Date Submitted**: This is a date field where you should enter the date you submitted this form.

8. **Time Submitted**: This is a time field where you should enter the time you submitted this form.

9. **Submission ID**: This is a number field where you should enter a unique submission ID.

10. **Email**: This is an email field where you should enter your email address.

11. **Phone**: This is a text field where you can enter your phone number (optional).

12. **Additional Comments**: This is a text field where you can add any additional comments.

13. **Submitted By**: This is a text field where you should enter the name of the person submitting this form.

14. **Time Zone**: This is a select-one field where you should choose your current time zone.

15. **Additional Notes**: This is a text field where you can add any additional notes.

16. **Teacher Comments**: This is a text field where the teacher can add comments.

17. **Your Reflection Score**: This is a number field where you can rate your reflection (optional).

18. **Reflection Status**: This is a select-one field where you can choose the status of your reflection.

19. **Teacher Comments Status**: This is a select-one field where the teacher can choose the status of their comments.

20. **Submission Status**: This is a select-one field where you can choose the status of your submission.

21. **Teacher Suggestions**: This is a text field where the teacher can add suggestions.

22. **Submission Date**: This is a date field where you should enter the date you submitted this form.

23. **Submission Time**: This is a time field where you should enter the time you submitted this form.

24. **Submission Time Zone**: This is a select-one field where you should choose your current time zone.

## Tips

* Please make sure to complete all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, please use the corresponding fields to add them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your teacher or school administrator.
