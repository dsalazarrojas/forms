# Vacation Rental Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect inquiries from potential vacation rental clients. Fill out this form to provide detailed information about your stay preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Enter your contact details (inquiry name, email, and contact number - if available).
3. Choose your arrival and check-out dates.
4. Provide your arrival date if different from your check-in date.
5. Enter your property ID, name, and address.
6. Select the type of property you are interested in (e.g., single, double, triple, etc.).
7. Choose the number of guests in your party.
8. Add any additional inquiry message (optional).
9. Finally, select the user who will be responsible for handling your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry Name** (inquiry_name, text, required): Enter your name, so we know who to contact.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address, so we can respond to your inquiry.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer contact via phone.
* **Check-in Date** (check_in_date, date, required): Choose the date you plan to check-in for your stay.
* **Check-out Date** (check_out_date, date, required): Choose the date you plan to check-out for your stay.
* **Arrival Date** (arrival_date, date, required): Choose the date you plan to arrive at the property.
* **Property ID** (property_id, number, required): Enter the ID of the property you are interested in.
* **Property Name** (property_name, text, required): Enter the name of the property you are interested in.
* **Property Address** (property_address, text, required): Enter the address of the property you are interested in.
* **Property Type** (property_type, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply (e.g., single, double, triple, etc.).
* **Guests** (guests, select_one, required): Choose the number of guests in your party (1-6).
* **Inquiry Message** (inquiry_message, note, optional): Add any additional comments or messages about your stay.
* **Assigned User** (assigned_user, text, required): Select the user who will be responsible for handling your inquiry.
