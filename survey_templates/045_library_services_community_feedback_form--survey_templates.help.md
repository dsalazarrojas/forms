# Library Services Community Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users to provide feedback on library services. Please take a few minutes to share your thoughts and suggestions on how we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant branch location from the list.
2. Provide feedback on the facilities provided at the branch, such as cleanliness, equipment, and overall comfort.
3. Report any issues with library hours, including any problems with scheduling or access.
4. Share information about the library staff, such as their friendliness, helpfulness, and responsiveness.
5. Describe any library programs or activities that you have participated in or would like to see offered.
6. If you have attended an event at the branch, let us know about your experience.
7. Choose the services that are most important to you, and provide any feedback or suggestions.
8. If you have any other suggestions or comments, please provide them here.
9. Select any additional contact information you would like us to use to follow up on your feedback.
10. Finally, select any branch services that you consider to be important or essential.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Branch Location**: Please select the branch location where you are providing feedback.
* **Facilities**: Describe any issues or concerns you have with the facilities at the branch.
* **Hours**: Report any problems with library hours, including scheduling or access issues.
* **Staff**: Share any positive or negative experiences you have had with library staff.
* **Programs**: Describe any library programs or activities you have participated in or would like to see offered.
* **Events**: If you attended an event at the branch, share your experience and any feedback you have.
* **Branch Services**: Choose the services that are most important to you, such as programs, events, or other services.
* **Suggestion**: Provide any other suggestions or comments you have about library services.
* **Contact Info**: If you would like us to follow up with you on your feedback, please provide your contact information.
* **Branch Services Important**: Select any branch services that you consider to be important or essential.
* **Branch FAQ**: If you have any questions or concerns about branch services, please provide them here.

Tips: Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, and try to focus on one issue or suggestion per field. If you have multiple issues or suggestions, please fill out the form multiple times.
