# Women's Self Defense Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants of the Women's Self-Defense Course. It's intended to help instructors evaluate the effectiveness of the course and identify areas for improvement. Please ensure you are filling out this form for a session you have attended.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all fields that are marked as required.
2. Provide honest and detailed feedback about your experience with the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Women's Self-Defense Course Feedback** (`form_title`, text, required: false)
This field is for a brief title of your feedback. If you want to give a short summary of your thoughts about the course.

### 2. **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the instructor who led your session.

### 3. **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false)
Select the date of the session you attended.

### 4. **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required: false)
Select the time of the session you attended.

### 5. **Course Level** (`course_level`, text, required: false)
Indicate which course level you are currently at or would like to progress to.

### 6. **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required: false)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5.

### 7. **Specific Feedback** (`specific_feedback`, text, required: false)
Share any specific feedback you have about the course. This could include what you enjoyed, disliked, or would like to see changed.

### 8. **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false)
Add any additional comments about your experience with the course.

### 9. **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, number, required: false)
Rate your instructor's performance on a scale of 1-5.

### 10. **Instructor Comments** (`instructor_comments`, text, required: false)
Share any feedback about your instructor's teaching style or behavior.

### 11. **Will Attend Next** (`will_attend_next`, select_one, required: false)
Indicate whether you would attend the course again. Choose from "Yes" or "No".

### 12. **Will Recommend** (`will_recommend`, select_one, required: false)
Indicate whether you would recommend the course to others. Choose from "Yes" or "No".
