# gym_member_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from gym members about their overall satisfaction and experience at the gym.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (if any). Most fields are not required, so you can complete as much or as little as you like.
2. Answer each question according to your honest opinion about your experience at the gym.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Feedback" field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gym Member Satisfaction Survey** (`survey_title`, text, not required): This is the title of the form, which is just a label. You can ignore this field and proceed to the next section.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your experience at the gym. Choose one of the options: 5 (Excellent), 4 (Good), 3 (Neutral), 2 (Poor), or 1 (Very Poor).
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the quality of the gym facilities. Choose one or more of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Services** (`services`, select_one, not required): Rate the quality of the services offered by the gym. Choose one of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the cleanliness of the gym facilities. Choose one or more of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, not required): Rate the value for money of the gym membership. Choose one of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Staff Attitude** (`staff_attitude`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the attitude of the gym staff. Choose one or more of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Staff Helpfulness** (`staff_helpfulness`, select_one, not required): Rate the helpfulness of the gym staff. Choose one of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Facilities Quality** (`facilities_quality`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the quality of the gym facilities. Choose one or more of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the condition of the gym equipment. Choose one or more of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Parking** (`parking`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the parking facilities. Choose one or more of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Amenities** (`amenities`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the quality of the amenities provided by the gym. Choose one or more of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Overall Impression** (`overall_impression`, select_one, not required): Rate your overall impression of the gym experience. Choose one of the options: Positive, Neutral, or Negative.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience at the gym.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, not required): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience at the gym.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): If you'd like to be contacted by the gym management, please provide your contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): If you'd like to be contacted by the gym management, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): If you'd like to be contacted by the gym management, please provide your phone number.
* **Member Since** (`member_since`, number, not required): How long have you been a member of the gym?
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, not required): How often do you visit the gym? Choose one of the options: Daily, 2-3 times a week, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, number, not required): How long do you typically visit the gym for?
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, select_one, not required): How many times have you visited the gym? Choose one of the options: First time, Second time, Third time, or Fourth or more times.
* **Staff Interaction** (`staff_interaction`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the interaction with the gym staff. Choose one or more of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Staff Helpfulness Rating** (`staff_helpfulness_rating`, select_one, not required): Rate the helpfulness of the gym staff. Choose one of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select_one, not required): What is your current membership status? Choose one of the options: Active, Inactive, or Canceled.
