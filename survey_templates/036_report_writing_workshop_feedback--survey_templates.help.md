# Report Writing Workshop Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on their experiences in the Report Writing Workshop. Your feedback will help us improve future workshop sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply fill out each of the sections as instructed below.
- You may choose to complete all or some of the fields, as indicated by the "required" status of each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Writing Workshop Feedback** (`report_writing_workshop_feedback`, Note, Optional): 
  This section is a text area where you can provide your overall comments and feedback on the workshop.
* **How would you rate this workshop?** (`rating`, Number, Optional): 
  Please rate this workshop on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Optional): 
  This section is for any additional comments or feedback you want to provide. You can use this space to share your thoughts and suggestions.
* **Trainer** (`trainer_name`, Text, Optional): 
  Please enter the name of the workshop's trainer.
* **School** (`school_name`, Text, Optional): 
  Please enter the name of the school you attended the workshop at.
* **Date Attended** (`date_attended`, Date, Optional): 
  Enter the date you attended the workshop in MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by "required: true" status).
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so we can understand your experiences.
* You can skip fields if you are unsure or do not have information to provide.
