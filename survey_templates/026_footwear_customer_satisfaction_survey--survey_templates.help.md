# Footwear Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect customer feedback on a footwear product. The goal is to gather information on customer satisfaction, rating of the product's quality, and other relevant details to understand the customer's perspective.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the name of the person who purchased the product (Product Purchaser).
2. Rate your level of satisfaction with the product (rating of satisfaction).
3. Rate the product's quality (rating of quality).
4. Rate the product's value for money (rating of value for money).
5. Indicate if you would recommend the product (Recommend product).
6. Choose your purchase intent (Purchase intent).
7. Write any additional feedback (Feedback).
8. Enter your email address (Email address).
9. Enter your phone number (Phone number).
10. Enter the date of purchase (Date of purchase).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Purchaser**: Enter the name of the person who purchased the product. This is a required field and must be filled out.
* **Rating of Satisfaction**: Rate your level of satisfaction with the product on a scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Rating of Quality**: Select one of the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) to rate the product's quality.
* **Rating of Value for Money**: Enter a numerical rating (e.g., 1-10) for the product's value for money.
* **Recommend Product**: Indicate if you would recommend the product (True or False).
* **Purchase Intent**: Choose one or more of the options (Definitely Will Purchase, Probably Will Purchase, Not Sure, Definitely Will Not Purchase) to indicate your purchase intent.
* **Feedback**: Write any additional comments or feedback about the product. This field is optional.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you if needed.
* **Date of Purchase**: Enter the date you purchased the product (e.g., mm/dd/yyyy).
