# orange_challenge_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Orange Challenge Evaluation Survey is designed to collect feedback and evaluation from participants on their overall experience and satisfaction with the Orange Challenge program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the form, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure you complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
3. Review your responses before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, required*): Provide a brief statement expressing your thoughts and feelings about the Orange Challenge program.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required*): Rate your overall satisfaction with the Orange Challenge program on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in this field.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **More Suggestions** (`more_suggestions`, text, optional): If you have additional suggestions, please provide them in this field.
* **Event Suggestion** (`event_suggestion`, select multiple, required*): Indicate whether you would like to participate in events similar to the Orange Challenge.
* **Date Last Attended** (`date_last_attended`, date, optional): Enter the date you last attended the Orange Challenge.
* **Time Last Attended** (`time_last_attended`, time, optional): Enter the time you last attended the Orange Challenge.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required*): Rate your satisfaction with the Orange Challenge program on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required*): Rate your overall rating of the Orange Challenge program on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Suggestions for Events** (`suggestions_for_events`, text, required*): Provide suggestions for future events or programs.
* **Suggestions for Wellness** (`suggestions_for_wellness`, text, required*): Provide suggestions for future wellness programs.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in this field.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required*): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required*): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required*): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Attended** (`event_attended`, select one, required*): Indicate whether you attended the Orange Challenge event.
* **Event Frequency** (`event_frequency`, select multiple, required*): Indicate how often you would like to participate in future events like the Orange Challenge.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select one, required*): Indicate the type of event you attended (e.g., physical, online).
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select one, required*): Indicate your age group.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select one, required*): Indicate your gender.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required*): Enter your occupation.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select one, required*): Indicate your level of education.
* **Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, select one, required*): Indicate whether you have a good work-life balance.

This guide is intended to be a general help document and should be reviewed and updated as the form evolves.
