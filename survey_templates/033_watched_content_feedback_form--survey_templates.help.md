## Purpose
The Watched Content Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with the content they have watched. This form is used to understand how well the content meets the users' expectations and needs. The feedback collected will help improve the content and provide valuable insights for future content creation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the content being watched in the "Form Title" field.
2. Rate the content by selecting the number of stars that best represents your experience.
3. In the "Feedback" field, provide your comments about why you rated the content as you did.
4. Answer the second question by selecting the correct option from the options provided.
5. If you have additional comments about the content, you can provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "E-mail" field, if you would like to receive a copy of your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the content you are providing feedback on. If you do not know the title, please enter "Unknown".
* **How would you rate this content?** (`feedback_question_1`, number, optional): Rate the content by selecting a number of stars that best represents your experience. The more stars, the better the content.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_answer_1`, text, optional): Provide your comments about why you rated the content as you did. Be as specific as possible.
* **Was this content what you were looking for?** (`feedback_question_2`, select_one, required): Answer this question by selecting one of the options provided. This will help us understand if the content met your expectations.
* **Why did you find this content helpful or not?** (`feedback_answer_2`, text, optional): Provide your comments about why you found the content helpful or not.
* **How likely are you to recommend this content?** (`feedback_question_3`, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to your experience with the content. This will help us understand your level of enthusiasm for recommending the content.
* **Additional Comments** (`form_comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or comments about the content, please provide them here.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to receive a copy of your feedback.
