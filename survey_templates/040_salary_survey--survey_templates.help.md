# Salary Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salary Survey form collects information about an employee's job details, including job title, job grade, and bonuses. This data is used for internal processes and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Employee" field with the employee's name or ID.
2. Enter the "Job Title Salary" with the employee's current salary for their job title.
3. Select the correct "Job Grade Salary" from the provided options.
4. For fields "Job Title Bonus", "Job Grade Bonus", "Job Stock Options", "Job Stock Ownership", "Employee Stock Options", "Employee Stock Ownership", and "Employee Bonus", you can enter any applicable values or leave them blank if none.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee
* **Employee** (`employee_details`, text, optional): Enter the employee's name or ID.

### Job Title Salary
* **Job Title Salary** (`job_title_salary`, number, required): Enter the employee's current salary for their job title.

### Job Grade Salary
* **Job Grade Salary** (`job_grade_salary`, number, required): Select the correct salary based on the employee's job grade from the provided options.

### Job Title Bonus
* **Job Title Bonus** (`job_title_bonus`, number, optional): Enter any bonus the employee receives for their job title.

### Job Grade Bonus
* **Job Grade Bonus** (`job_grade_bonus`, number, optional): Enter any bonus the employee receives for their job grade.

### Job Stock Options
* **Job Stock Options** (`job_stock_options`, text, optional): Enter any stock options or equity granted to the employee.

### Job Stock Ownership
* **Job Stock Ownership** (`job_stock_ownership`, text, optional): Enter the employee's stock ownership or equity.

### Job Stock
* **Job Stock** (`job_stock`, number, optional): Enter the total value of the employee's job stock or equity.

### Employee Stock Options
* **Employee Stock Options** (`employee_stock_options`, text, optional): Enter any stock options or equity granted to the employee.

### Employee Stock Ownership
* **Employee Stock Ownership** (`employee_stock_ownership`, text, optional): Enter the employee's stock ownership or equity.

### Employee Stock
* **Employee Stock** (`employee_stock`, number, optional): Enter the total value of the employee's stock or equity.

### Employee Bonus
* **Employee Bonus** (`employee_bonus`, number, optional): Enter any bonus the employee receives.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- For optional fields, only enter values that are applicable to the employee.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
