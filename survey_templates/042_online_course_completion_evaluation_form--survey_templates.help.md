# Online Course Completion Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Course Completion Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with an online course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and fields as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. For questions with numerical or select options, choose the answer that best reflects your experience.
3. For open-ended fields (e.g. feedback and suggestions), provide as much detail as possible.
4. Make sure to fill out each field completely, as this will help us assess the course's effectiveness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Questions** (1) (note): This section is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Course Satisfaction** (2) (number): Rate your overall satisfaction with the course (e.g. 1-5).
* **Instructors' Performance** (3) (select_one): Choose how satisfied you are with the instructors' performance:
	+ 1: Very Satisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat Satisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
* **Teaching Materials** (4) (select_one): Choose how satisfied you are with the teaching materials:
	+ 1: Very Satisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat Satisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
* **Overall Effectiveness** (5) (select_multiple): Choose the aspects of the course that were effective for you:
	+ 1: Very Effective
	+ 2: Somewhat Effective
	+ 3: Not Very Effective
* **Feedback** (6) (text): Share any positive or negative comments about the course.
* **Suggestions** (7) (text): Provide any suggestions or ideas for improvements to the course.

Note: Please fill out all the fields completely, as this will help us assess the course's effectiveness.
