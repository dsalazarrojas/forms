# immunoglobulin_therapy_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and data from patients who have undergone immunoglobulin therapy. Your input will help healthcare professionals understand your experience and provide better care in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to provide valuable feedback.
2. Ensure you enter your email address and phone number correctly.
3. Select the relevant medical department and location from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the date and time of treatment, as well as your date and time of birth.
5. Provide your satisfaction level with the treatment and staff rating (if applicable).
6. If you have any additional comments, use the "additional_comments" field.
7. Review and submit your form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and experiences about the treatment you received.
* **Immunoglobulin Treatment** (`immunoglobulin_treatment`, text, optional): Describe the treatment you received, including any notable details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Share any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your healthcare team.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with the treatment (very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you strongly recommend or not recommend the treatment.
* **Open Comments** (`open_comments`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any follow-up information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, number, required): Enter your unique patient ID number.
* **Staff ID** (`staff_id`, number, required): Enter the ID number of the healthcare staff member who treated you.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Choose the medical department that treated you (cardiology, endocrinology, nephrology, oncology, pulmonology).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you received the treatment.
* **Date of Treatment** (`date_of_treatment`, date, required): Enter the date you received the treatment.
* **Time of Treatment** (`time_of_treatment`, time, required): Enter the time you received the treatment.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required): Enter your time of birth.
* **Treatment Duration** (`treatment_duration`, number, required): Enter the length of your treatment.
* **Treatment Frequency** (`treatment_frequency`, text, optional): Describe the frequency of your treatment.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you have or have had.
* **Treatment Outcomes** (`treatment_outcomes`, text, optional): Share any notable outcomes or results from your treatment.
* **Staff Rating** (`staff_rating`, number, optional): Rate the quality of care provided by your healthcare staff.
* **Patient Satisfaction** (`patient_satisfaction`, text, optional): Share your overall satisfaction with the treatment.
* **Treatment Side Effects** (`treatment_side_effects`, text, optional): Describe any side effects you experienced during treatment.
* **Follow-up Instructions** (`follow_up_instructions`, text, optional): Follow any post-treatment instructions provided by your healthcare team.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Provide any further comments or feedback about your experience.

## Tips

* Ensure you have the required fields completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information (email and phone number) for accuracy.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your experience and providing feedback.
* If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact our support team.
