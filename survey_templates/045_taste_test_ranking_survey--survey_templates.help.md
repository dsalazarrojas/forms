# Taste Test Ranking Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Taste Test Ranking Survey is designed to collect feedback and ratings from respondents about a particular dish. The survey aims to assess the taste and quality of the dish, and gather additional comments and contact information from the respondent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "Dish Title" field, which is a text field where you can enter the name of the dish being tested.
2. In the "Dish Description" field, provide a brief description of the dish, including any relevant details such as ingredients or cooking methods.
3. In the "How Delicious?" field, rate the dish on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not delicious at all and 5 is extremely delicious.
4. If you have more ratings or opinions, you can skip the "How Good?" field, as it is optional.
5. The "Comment" field is where you can provide additional feedback or comments about the dish. This is a free-text field where you can enter your thoughts or opinions.
6. In the "Rankings" field, select one of the options provided (Delicious, Good, Okay, Not Delicious) to rate the overall taste and quality of the dish.
7. Enter the date and time of the taste test in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
8. Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dish Title** (text, required): Enter the name of the dish being tested.
* **Dish Description** (text, required): Provide a brief description of the dish, including any relevant details such as ingredients or cooking methods.
* **How Delicious?** (number, required): Rate the dish on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not delicious at all and 5 is extremely delicious.
* **How Good?** (number, optional): If you have an opinion on the quality of the dish, rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not good at all and 5 is extremely good.
* **Comment** (text, optional): Provide additional feedback or comments about the dish.
* **Rankings** (select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided (Delicious, Good, Okay, Not Delicious) to rate the overall taste and quality of the dish.
* **Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the taste test.
* **Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the taste test.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Taste Test Ranking Survey Deliciousness Rating 1** (number, optional): This is a duplicate field for rating the dish's deliciousness. If you've already rated the dish's deliciousness in the "How Delicious?" field, you can skip this field.
* **Taste Test Ranking Survey Goodness Rating 1** (number, optional): This is a duplicate field for rating the dish's goodness. If you've already rated the dish's goodness, you can skip this field.
* **Taste Test Ranking Survey Deliciousness Rating 2** (number, optional): Another duplicate field for rating the dish's deliciousness.
* **Taste Test Ranking Survey Goodness Rating 2** (number, optional): Another duplicate field for rating the dish's goodness.
* **Taste Test Ranking Survey Deliciousness Rating 3** (number, optional): Yet another duplicate field for rating the dish's deliciousness.
* **Taste Test Ranking Survey Goodness Rating 3** (number, optional): Another duplicate field for rating the dish's goodness.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the dish.

## Tips
* Please be honest and objective when rating the dish's taste and quality.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at your email address provided.
