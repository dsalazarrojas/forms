# product_testing_results_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teams report and track the results of product testing. It gathers essential information about the product, its testing status, and the outcomes of the testing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the **Product Name** field, where you can enter the name of the product being tested.
2. Choose the **Testing Status** from the dropdown menu: Completed, In Progress, or Pending.
3. Enter the **Testing Duration** in numerical format, which represents the time spent testing the product.
4. Provide any **Testing Notes** you want to record, which can be useful for future reference.
5. Select the **Testing Feedback** you want to provide, which can be Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
6. Identify the **Testing Outcomes** you observed, which can be Expected, Unexpected, or Unknown.
7. Enter any **Testing Recommendations** you have, which can help improve the product or testing process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name**: A text field where you can enter the name of the product being tested.
* **Testing Status**: A dropdown menu that allows you to choose the current status of the product testing: Completed, In Progress, or Pending.
* **Testing Duration**: A numerical field where you can enter the time spent testing the product.
* **Testing Notes**: A text field where you can add any notes or comments about the testing process.
* **Testing Feedback**: A dropdown menu where you can select the feedback you want to provide: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **Testing Outcomes**: A dropdown menu where you can identify the outcomes of the testing process: Expected, Unexpected, or Unknown.
* **Testing Recommendations**: A text field where you can provide suggestions for improvement or recommendations for the product or testing process.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate reporting.
* Use the **Testing Duration** field to record actual time spent testing, not estimated time.
* When providing **Testing Feedback**, choose the option that best describes your overall experience.
* In **Testing Outcomes**, select all outcomes that apply, separated by commas.
* Use the **Testing Recommendations** field to record specific, actionable suggestions for improvement.
* Review the form for accuracy before submitting to ensure completeness and accuracy.
