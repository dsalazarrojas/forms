# Fabric Color Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect user preferences for fabric colors, textures, and weaves to inform product development and design decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select your favorite fabric color from the list of options provided.
* Choose your favorite fabric texture from the list of options provided.
* Choose your favorite fabric weave from the list of options provided.
* Select your preferred fabric weight from the list of options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Favorite Color** (`My Favorite Color`, select_multiple): Choose one or more colors that you like from the list of options provided.
* **Favorite Texture** (`Favorite Fabric Texture`, select_multiple): Select one or more textures that you find appealing from the list of options provided.
* **Favorite Weave** (`Favorite Fabric Weave`, select_multiple): Choose one or more weaves that you like from the list of options provided.
* **Favorite Weight** (`Favorite Fabric Weight`, select_one): Select your preferred fabric weight from the list of options provided.

## Tips
* You can select multiple options for each field, as indicated by the "(select_multiple)" label.
* Make sure to complete all fields, even if you have no preference, to provide more comprehensive feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to include them in the text area provided (although this form does not include a text field, it's mentioned as a potential future enhancement).
