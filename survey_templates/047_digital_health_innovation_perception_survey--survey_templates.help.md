# Digital Health Innovation Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form, called the "Digital Health Innovation Perception Survey," is designed to gather insights and feedback from users on the current state of digital health innovation and its potential applications. The survey aims to collect information from a diverse group of users, including those who work in the healthcare industry and those who do not.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Page 1: Introduction**
    *   This is the first page of the form, which serves as an introduction to the rest of the survey.
2.  **Page 2: Contact Info**
    *   On this page, you will find a single question asking for your contact information. If you want to provide your email address, please fill in the email field. This information will help us contact you if we need to follow up on any questions.
3.  **Page 3: Age Range**
    *   On this page, you will find two questions about your age range. Please select the age range that best describes you.
4.  **Page 4: Years of Experience**
    *   On this page, you will also find two questions asking about your years of experience. Please answer with the number of years you have been in your current profession or role.
5.  **Page 5: Gender**
    *   On this page, you will find a question asking about your gender. Please select the option that best describes you.
6.  **Page 6: Occupation**
    *   On this page, you will find a question asking about your occupation. Please select all the options that apply to you.
7.  **Page 7: Current Tool Usage**
    *   On this page, you will find a question asking about how frequently you use digital tools. Please select the option that best describes how often you use digital tools.
8.  **Page 8: Digital Tool Usage**
    *   On this page, you will find a question asking about your digital tool usage. Please select all the options that apply to you.
9.  **Page 9: Innovation**
    *   On this page, you will find a question asking about your perception of innovation. Please select the option that best describes your current understanding of innovation.
