# Justice And Dignity Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect feedback from users about their service experience, focusing on justice and dignity aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide a comprehensive feedback, follow these steps:

1. Choose one of the given options for each question.
2. Select the options that best match your experience.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled, as indicated by the label "required" in the field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. How was your service treatment?
* **How was your service treatment?** (`service_treatment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks how well you were treated during your service interaction. Choose all the options that apply to your experience:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Bad
	+ Very Bad

### 2. How fair were you treated?
* **How fair were you treated?** (`fairness`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate how fair you felt during your service interaction:
	+ Very Fair
	+ Fairly Fair
	+ Somewhat Unfair
	+ Unfair
	+ Very Unfair

### 3. Were you approached for follow-up?
* **Were you approached for follow-up?** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you were approached for follow-up:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 4. Would you like to contact the service staff?
* **Would you like to contact the service staff?** (`contact_request`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you want to contact the service staff:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback is valuable to the service.
* You can select multiple options for the first question, but ensure you choose only one option for the second and fourth questions.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the open-ended section (not shown here).
