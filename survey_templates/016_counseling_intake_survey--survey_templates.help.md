# Counseling Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a client's demographics, mental health history, treatment goals, and medical information, as well as their history with counseling and crisis services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Please provide as much detail as you feel comfortable sharing.
3. If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to ask the counselor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Client Info** (`client_info`, `text`, required: false)
* This field is intended to collect general information about the client, such as their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### 2. **Demographics** (`demographics`, `text`, required: false)
* This field is meant to gather basic demographic information, such as age, sex, and ethnicity.

### 3. **Mental Health History** (`mental_health_history`, `text`, required: false)
* This field is designed to understand the client's mental health history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or medications.

### 4. **Counseling History** (`counseling_history`, `text`, required: false)
* This field is intended to gather information about the client's previous experiences with counseling or therapy.

### 5. **Treatment Goals** (`treatment_goals`, `text`, required: false)
* This field is meant to help the client and counselor establish clear treatment goals and objectives.

### 6. **Mental Health Status** (`mental_health_status`, `text`, required: false)
* This field is designed to understand the client's current mental health status, including any symptoms or concerns.

### 7. **Crisis Services** (`crisis_services`, `text`, required: false)
* This field is intended to gather information about any crisis or emergency situations the client may be experiencing.

### 8. **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, `text`, required: false)
* This field is meant to collect any relevant medical information that may impact the client's mental health or treatment.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask the counselor for clarification.
* Remember, this form is intended to be a tool to help the counselor understand your needs, not to cause you any distress.
* If you need help or support, please do not hesitate to reach out to the counselor or your healthcare provider.
