# Role Rotation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Role Rotation Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from individuals about their role rotation experience. This feedback helps identify areas of improvement for the rotation process, ensuring future rotations are smoother and more effective.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, providing detailed and specific feedback about your experience.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. If you have additional comments or concerns, please use the "Comments" field.
4. Make sure to fill in your Email and Phone (if available) so we can get in touch with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role Rotation Experience** (`role_rotation_experience`, Text, optional): Share your overall experience with the rotation process.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, Select One, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the rotation process, choosing from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Role Rotation Goals** (`role_rotation_goals`, Text, optional): Describe your goals and expectations for the rotation process.
* **Communication** (`communication`, Select Multiple, optional): Indicate how effective or ineffective you found communication during the rotation process.
* **Role Rotation Process** (`role_rotation_process`, Text, optional): Share your thoughts on the effectiveness of the rotation process.
* **Training and Resources** (`training_and_resources`, Select Multiple, optional): Rate the adequacy of training and resources provided during the rotation process.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or insights you have about your experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, optional): Add any comments or concerns you have about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, optional): Enter your work email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, optional): Enter your work phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.

Please note that while some fields are optional, it's still important to provide as much detail as you can, as this feedback will help us improve the rotation process for future team members.
