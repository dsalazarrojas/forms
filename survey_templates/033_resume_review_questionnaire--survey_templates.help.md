# resume_review_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather relevant information from job candidates about their background, experience, and qualifications in order to facilitate a thorough review of their resume.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the requested information about your job experience, education, and other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **job_title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current or most recent job title. This will help the reviewer get a sense of your current or recent work.
* **work_experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your previous work experience in this field. This can include internships, part-time or full-time jobs, or any other relevant work experience.
* **resume_format** (`resume_format`, select_one, optional): Select the type of resume format you are using for this application (e.g., Chronological, Functional, Combination).
* **job_search** (`job_search`, text, optional): If you are currently searching for a job, describe the type of job you are looking for and any specific qualifications or experiences you have for it.
* **work_environment** (`work_environment`, select_multiple, optional): Select the environments in which you have worked or would like to work in (e.g., Office, Remote, Hybrid, Field).
* **contact_information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, such as email address or phone number.
* **resume_upload** (`resume_upload`, note, optional): Upload your resume or CV in PDF or DOC format.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or explanations about your application.
* **job_type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of job you are applying for (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
* **job_status** (`job_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select your current employment status (e.g., Employed, Unemployed, Not looking).
* **industry** (`industry`, text, optional): Enter the industry you are interested in or currently working in.
* **job_level** (`job_level`, select_one, optional): Select your current or desired job level (e.g., Entry Level, Mid-level, Senior).
* **job_function** (`job_function`, select_multiple, optional): Select the functions or departments you have experience in (e.g., Marketing, Sales, Engineering, Operations).
* **job_seniority** (`job_seniority`, select_one, optional): Select your current or desired job seniority (e.g., Junior, Senior, Lead).
* **job_description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your job responsibilities and duties.
* **job_industry** (`job_industry`, select_one, optional): Select the industry you are working in or interested in (e.g., Tech, Finance, Marketing).
* **job_type_industry** (`job_type_industry`, text, optional): Enter any other information about your job type within your industry.
* **job_location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you are or would like to work.
* **job_experience_years** (`job_experience_years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have worked in your job or field.
* **education** (`education`, select_multiple, optional): Select your educational level (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, PhD).
* **job_status_industry** (`job_status_industry`, text, optional): Enter any other information about your current or desired job status within your industry.
