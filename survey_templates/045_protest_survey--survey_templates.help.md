## Protest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Protest Survey form is designed to collect information from individuals who have participated in protests or demonstrations. This data is used to better understand the scope, size, and characteristics of protests, which can help us understand social and political trends.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
3. Leave blank fields that are not applicable or irrelevant to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, note, required/optional)
	+ Enter your name, email address, and phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Event Details** (Event Details, text, required/optional)
	+ Describe the protest, including the location, purpose, and any notable events or incidents that occurred.
* **Protest Date** (Protest Date, date, required/optional)
	+ Enter the date of the protest, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Time** (Event Time, time, required/optional)
	+ Enter the time of the protest, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Participant Count** (Participant Count, number, required/optional)
	+ Estimate the number of participants at the protest, including yourself.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate and honest as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about the form, feel free to contact us for clarification.
* Remember to review each page carefully before submitting the form to ensure you have all the necessary information.
