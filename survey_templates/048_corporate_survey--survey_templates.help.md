# Corporate Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Corporate Survey form, helping you understand its purpose and how to complete it correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Company Name field with the name of your company.
2. Select your company's marketing strategy from the options provided in the Marketing Strategy field.
3. Enter the name of your product in the Product Name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your company. This will help us understand the context of your responses.
* **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, select_one, required: false): Choose the marketing strategy your company follows. Select one of the options: "Good" or "Fair".
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the product or service your company offers. This will help us understand the specifics of your marketing strategy.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all fields to ensure your survey responses are complete and accurate.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
