# International Transport Agreement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is used to collect information about international transport agreements between shippers and customers. The purpose of this survey is to ensure that both parties are aware of the terms and conditions of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in your basic information: 
  * Customer Name and Email
  * Shipper Name and Email/Phone Number
  * Shipment Date and Shipment Time (if known)

- Select the relevant transport method and shipment mode
- Provide any specific remarks or special conditions that should be noted

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of the customer involved in the shipment.
* **Shipper Name** (`shipper_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of the shipper involved in the shipment.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): This field is used to collect the email address of the contact person for the shipment.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the phone number of the contact person for the shipment.
* **Shipment Date** (`shipment_date`, date, optional): This field is used to collect the date of the shipment.
* **Shipment Time** (`shipment_time`, time, optional): This field is used to collect the time of the shipment.
* **Shipment Duration** (`shipment_duration`, number, optional): This field is used to collect the duration of the shipment.
* **Transport Method** (`shipper_transport_method`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the transport method used for the shipment (e.g. Air, Land, Sea, Rail, or Mixed).
* **Shipment Mode** (`shipment_mode`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the shipment mode (e.g. Door-to-door, Door-to-door with customs clearance, Port-to-port, Store-to-store, or Mixed).
* **Shipper Address** (`shipper_address`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the address of the shipper involved in the shipment.
* **Customer Notice** (`customer_notice`, number, optional): This field is used to collect a notice or code about the customer.
* **Shipper Notice** (`shipper_notice`, number, optional): This field is used to collect a notice or code about the shipper.
* **Shipment Status** (`shipment_status`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the status of the shipment (e.g. Pending, Confirmed, Rejected).
* **Shipment Tracking Number** (`shipment_tracking_number`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the tracking number for the shipment.
* **Shipper Comments** (`shipper_comments`, text, optional): This field is used to collect any comments from the shipper about the shipment.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, optional): This field is used to collect any comments from the customer about the shipment.
* **Shipment Delivery Date** (`shipment_delivery_date`, date, optional): This field is used to collect the date of delivery of the shipment.
* **Shipment Delivery Time** (`shipment_delivery_time`, time, optional): This field is used to collect the time of delivery of the shipment.
* **Shipment Delivery Status** (`shipment_delivery_status`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the status of delivery (e.g. Pending, Confirmed, Rejected).
* **Shipper Remarks** (`shipper_remarks`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select any special remarks or conditions about the shipment (e.g. Customs, Insurance, Other).
* **Customer Remarks** (`customer_remarks`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select any special remarks or conditions about the shipment (e.g. Customs, Insurance, Other).
* **Shipper Agreement** (`shipper_agreement`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select whether the shipper agrees to the terms and conditions of the agreement.
* **Customer Agreement** (`customer_agreement`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select whether the customer agrees to the terms and conditions of the agreement.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in the form accurately and completely to avoid any discrepancies in the transport process.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can always reach out to the contact person for clarification.
- This form is used solely for the purpose of collecting information about international transport agreements. Any changes to the form should be made in consultation with relevant stakeholders.
