# Food Festival Top Chef Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from Food Festival attendees about their favorite dishes and chefs. The form is used to help identify the most popular dishes and chefs of the festival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and favorite dish and chef.
2. Choose your rating for each dish and chef.
3. If you want to, provide any additional comments.
4. Select the "Submit Form" option if you are ready to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Chef Name (<name>, <type: text, required: true>):** The name of your favorite chef from the festival.
* **Dish Name (<name>, <type: text, required: true>):** The name of your favorite dish from the festival.
* **Rating (<name>, <type: number, required: true>):** A rating between 1-5 for your favorite dish.
* **Chef (<name>, <type: select_one, required: true>):** A simple "yes/no" question to confirm your favorite chef.
* **Dish (<name>, <type: select_multiple, required: true>):** A multiple select field to choose multiple favorite dishes.
* **Favorite (<name>, <type: select_one, required: false>):** A simple "yes/no" question to confirm if this dish is your favorite.
* **Comments (<name>, <type: text, required: false>):** Any additional comments about your experience at the festival.
* **Feedback (<name>, <type: text, required: false>):** Any additional feedback or suggestions for the festival.
* **Submit Form (<name>, <type: select_one, required: true>):** Confirm that you are ready to submit your feedback.
