# EdTech App Feature Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EdTech App Feature Feedback Survey is a tool for users to share their thoughts, opinions, and suggestions about the features of the EdTech app.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read the introduction and review the sections below.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Fill out all the mandatory fields.
4. Save your progress as you go along, as you may need to revisit some sections.
5. Once completed, submit the form to help us improve the app.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feature Feedback** (`1`, `text`, `not required`): This field is where you can provide feedback on the features of the EdTech app. Please describe your thoughts, opinions, or experiences with the features in the app.
* **Satisfaction Comparison** (`2`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Here, you can compare your satisfaction levels with the EdTech app's features. Select all that apply to express your level of satisfaction.
* **Feature Satisfaction** (`3`, `number`, `not required`): Please rate your satisfaction level with the EdTech app's features on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Improvements** (`4`, `text`, `not required`): If you think there are areas for improvement in the EdTech app, please describe them in this field.
* **User Comments** (`5`, `text`, `not required`): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the EdTech app or its features.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, `not required`): If you'd like to provide your email address, we can contact you for further feedback or to thank you for your input.
* **Phone** (`7`, `text`, `not required`): Please provide your phone number (optional) if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Feature Feedback Frequency** (`8`, `number`, `not required`): Indicate how often you use the EdTech app's features on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is never and 10 is very frequently.
