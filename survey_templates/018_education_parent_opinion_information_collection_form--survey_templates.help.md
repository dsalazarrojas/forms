# Education Parent Opinion Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is created to collect your opinions and feedback about your experience with our educational institution. Your input will help us to understand your satisfaction with our services and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with options, select one or more of the provided answers.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the text fields to provide them.
4. Provide your contact information if you want to be contacted for follow-up discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What's your relationship with the school?**: Please describe your relationship with the school, e.g., your child's name, class/grade, etc.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Parent Satisfaction with School Services**: How satisfied are you with our school services?
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Options: Satisfied, Dissatisfied
	+ Required: No
* **School Satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with our school?
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: Satisfied, Dissatisfied
	+ Required: No
* **How can we improve services?**: If you have any suggestions, please provide them here.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Parent Opinion on Service Quality**: Please rate the quality of our services (1-5).
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **Other Comments**: Do you have any other comments or suggestions for us?
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Contact Info for Follow-up**: If you want to be contacted for follow-up discussions, please provide your contact information.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Date**: Please provide the date of your visit or the time period you are providing feedback about.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: No
* **Time**: Please provide the time of your visit or the time period you are providing feedback about.
	+ Type: Time
	+ Required: No
* **Email Address**: Please provide your email address for follow-up discussions.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No
