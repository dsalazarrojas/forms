> # Video Game Romance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about players' experiences and preferences regarding video games. It's intended to be used by players to share their opinions and feedback about games they have played.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your player feedback on the game you have played.
2.  Enter the Game Id, which is a unique code assigned to each game.
3.  Enter your age in whole numbers.
4.  Select your gender from the list provided.
5.  Choose your game's romance rating out of 5.
6.  Select all the game options that best describe your experience (yes or no).
7.  Add any additional comments you have about your experience.
8.  Rate how satisfied you are with the game.
9.  Select how satisfied you are with the game out of 5.
10.  Indicate whether you recommend the game.
11.  Indicate whether you recommend the game out of 5.
12.  Select all the games you have played (yes or no).
13.  Select all the games you have loved (yes or no).
14.  Select all the games you recommend (yes or no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Player Feedback** (`player_feedback`, text, required): This is where you can provide your feedback about the game you have played. Be as detailed and honest as possible.
*   **Game Id** (`game_id`, number, required): Enter the unique code assigned to each game.
*   **Game Name** (`game_name`, text, optional): This is where you can enter the name of the game you have played, if you have not already done so.
*   **Player Gender** (`player_gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the list provided.
*   **Player Age** (`player_age`, number, required): Enter your age in whole numbers.
*   **Player Ethnicity** (`player_ethnicity`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the ethnicities that best describe you.
*   **Game Romance Rating** (`game_romance_rating`, number, required): Choose your game's romance rating out of 5.
*   **Game Options** (`game_romance_options`, select_multiple, required): Select all the game options that best describe your experience (yes or no).
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments you have about your experience.
*   **Game Satisfaction** (`game_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate how satisfied you are with the game out of 5.
*   **Player Satisfaction** (`player_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate how satisfied you are with the game out of 5.
*   **Player Recommend** (`player_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you recommend the game.
*   **Game Recommend** (`game_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you recommend the game out of 5.
*   **Player Interest** (`player_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the games you are interested in (yes or no).
*   **Game Interest** (`game_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the games you are interested in (yes or no).
*   **Player Favourite Games** (`player_favourite_games`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the games you have loved (yes or no).
*   **Game Favourite Games** (`game_favourite_games`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the games you have loved (yes or no).
*   **Player Favourite Game** (`player_favourite_game`, text, optional): Enter the name of your favourite game.
*   **Game Favourite Game** (`game_favourite_game`, text, optional): Enter the name of your favourite game.
*   **Player Games Played** (`player_games_played`, number, optional): Enter the number of games you have played.
*   **Game Games Played** (`game_games_played`, number, optional): Enter the number of games you have played.
*   **Player Games Loved** (`player_games_loved`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the games you have loved (yes or no).
*   **Game Games Loved** (`game_games_loved`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the games you have loved (yes or no).

## Tips

*   Please be honest and detailed in your responses.
*   If you have any additional comments, please add them in the Comments field.
*   If you have any questions or need help, please contact us.
