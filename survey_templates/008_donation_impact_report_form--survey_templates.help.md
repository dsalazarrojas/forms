# Donation Impact Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donation Impact Report Form is designed to collect information about the impact of donations on your organization. This form will help you understand how donations have made a difference in the lives of individuals or communities supported by your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have received the donation and are filling out this form for actual donations only.
2. Enter the donor's name in the "Donor Name" field.
3. Enter the date the donation was received in the "Donation Date" field.
4. Enter the amount of the donation in the "Donation Amount" field.
5. Select how often the donor donates by choosing one of the options in the "Frequency of Donations" dropdown.
6. Choose the method(s) by which the donor made the donation in the "How Made Donation" and "How Made Payment" sections.
7. Select the method(s) by which the donor heard about the donation in the "How Found Out About Donation" section.
8. Choose the reason(s) the donor made the donation in the "Why Make Donation" section.
9. Enter any additional contact information for the donor in the "Contact Information" field.
10. Provide a detailed description of the impact of the donation in the "Impact Statement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the donor who made the donation.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, required): Enter the date the donation was received.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the donation.
* **Frequency of Donations** (`frequency_of_donations`, select_one, required): Select how often the donor donates, such as monthly, quarterly, annually, or one-time.
* **How Made Donation** (`how_made_donation`, select_multiple, required): Select the method(s) by which the donor made the donation, such as online, phone, mail, or in-person.
* **How Found Out About Donation** (`how_found_out_about_donation`, select_multiple, required): Select the method(s) by which the donor heard about the donation, such as social media, email, newsletter, or word of mouth.
* **Why Make Donation** (`why_make_donation`, select_multiple, required): Select the reason(s) the donor made the donation, such as to support a cause or support a friend or family member.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the donor.
* **Impact Statement** (`impact_statement`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the impact of the donation.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
* Ensure you are filling out this form for actual donations only.
* Use clear and concise language in the "Impact Statement" field.
