### How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. If you have trouble understanding a question, feel free to contact the form owner or administrator for clarification.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, marked as "required".
4. You can skip optional fields, but be aware that incomplete responses might affect the form's results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel about yourself** (`page: 1`, `label: How do you feel about yourself`, `type: text`): This question serves as an introduction to the self-esteem assessment. Please type in your thoughts or feelings about yourself in a brief sentence or phrase.
* **Self-esteem** (`page: 2`, `label: Self-esteem`, `type: select_one`): Choose one of the four options:
	+ I feel confident
	+ I feel uncertain
	+ I feel unhappy
	+ I feel neutral
* **Confidence** (`page: 3`, `label: Confidence`, `type: select_multiple`): Choose one or more of the options:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **Self-Esteem 2** (`page: 4`, `label: Self-Esteem 2`, `type: text`): Please provide a detailed explanation or answer to this question. This field is required.
* **Self-Esteem 3** (`page: 5`, `label: Self-Esteem 3`, `type: text`): Please provide a detailed explanation or answer to this question. This field is required.
* **Self-Esteem 4** (`page: 6`, `label: Self-Esteem 4`, `type: text`): Please provide a detailed explanation or answer to this question. This field is required.
* **Self-Esteem 5**, **Self-Esteem 6**, ..., **Self-Esteem 9** (`page: 7-11`, `label: Self-Esteem 5`, `type: text`): Please provide a detailed explanation or answer to this question. These fields are not required, but feel free to answer them if you have something to share.

## Tips
