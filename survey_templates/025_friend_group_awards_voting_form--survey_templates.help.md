# Friend Group Awards Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to participate in friend group awards voting system.

## How To Complete This Form
Follow the instructions above to fill out the form correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **voting_category**: Select a category under which the award will be given.
* **friend_group_name**: Enter the name of the friend group being awarded.
* **award_name**: Enter a name of a person, team, or group to be awarded.
* **reason_for_nominating**: Enter a description of why the person/group deserves the award.
* **nomination_points**: Enter the number of points to be given to the nominee.
* **nomination_points_max**: Set the maximum points that can be given to a single nominee, if available.
* **friend_group_voting_option**: Select one or more options from the list to determine the award.
* **submit**: Choose to submit your nomination, but it's optional.
* **email**: Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted about your nomination.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted about your nomination.
