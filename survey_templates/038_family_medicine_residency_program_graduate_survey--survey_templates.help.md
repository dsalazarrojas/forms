# Family Medicine Residency Program Graduate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is a confidential and anonymous assessment tool designed to gather feedback from graduates of the Family Medicine Residency Program. Your responses will help us understand your overall experience, satisfaction, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the appropriate options that best fit your experience.
3. Be as detailed as possible in the open-ended questions.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Program** (`program_rating`, number): Rate your overall experience in the program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Education** (`education_quality`, number): Rate the overall quality of education you received during your program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Clinical Exposure** (`clinical_exposure`, select_one): Select the level of clinical exposure you had during your program: Clinical Exposure, Not Enough, or Too Much.
* **Clinical Exposure Rating** (`clinical_exposure_rating`, select_one): Select your level of satisfaction with clinical exposure: Very satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
* **Program Flexibility** (`program_flexibility`, select_one): Select the level of program flexibility you experienced: High, Moderate, or Low.
* **Program Flexibility Rating** (`program_flexibility_rating`, select_one): Select your level of satisfaction with program flexibility: Very satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
* **Education Fundamentals** (`education_fundamentals`, select_one): Select the strengths or weaknesses of your education: Strengths or Weaknesses.
* **Program Flexibility Reasons** (`program_flexibility_reasons`, text): Explain the reasons for your program flexibility rating.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one): Select your overall experience in the program: Positive or Negative.
* **Overall Experience Reasons** (`overall_experience_reasons`, text): Explain the reasons for your overall experience.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text): Suggest any improvements or recommendations for the program.
* **Program Rating** (`program_rating_2`, number): Rate your overall experience in the program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Program Rating Reasons** (`program_rating_reasons`, text): Explain the reasons for your program rating.
* **Clinical Exposure Reasons** (`clinical_exposure_rating_reasons`, text): Explain the reasons for your clinical exposure rating.
* **Education Quality Reasons** (`education_quality_rating_reasons`, text): Explain the reasons for your education quality rating.
* **Education Rating 2** (`education_rating_2`, number): Rate the overall quality of education you received on a scale of 1-5.
* **Program** (`program`, select_one): Select the name of the program you graduated from: Family Medicine Residency Program or Other.
* **Year Completed** (`year_completed`, number): Enter the year you completed your residency.
* **Education** (`education`, select_one): Select your medical degree: MD/PhD, DO, DNP, or Other.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one): Select your gender: Male or Female.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, select_one): Select your ethnicity: White, Black or African American, Asian or Pacific Islander, Hispanic or Latino, or Other.
* **Race** (`race`, select_one): Select your race: White, Black or African American, Asian or Pacific Islander, Hispanic or Latino, or Other.
* **Age** (`age`, number): Enter your age.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one): Select your current employment status: Employed, Unemployed, Retired, or Student.
* **Year of Residency** (`year_of_residency`, number): Enter the year you completed your residency.
