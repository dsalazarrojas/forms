# Arbitration Highlights Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect voting data for arbitration highlights. It allows voters to provide their name, email, and affiliation or organization, followed by three separate highlights with a description and impact rating. The form then asks the voter to select their top choice from the three options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Voter Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your affiliation or organization in the "Affiliation or Organization" field (optional).
4. For each of the three highlights, enter a brief description in the corresponding "Highlight Option 1", "Highlight Option 2", and "Highlight Option 3" fields.
5. For each highlight, select the impact rating in the corresponding "Impact Rating for Option 1", "Impact Rating for Option 2", and "Impact Rating for Option 3" fields.
6. Select your top choice from the three options in the "Select your top highlight" field.
7. If you wish to provide additional highlights, enter them in the "Additional highlights to consider" field (optional).
8. Finally, select your level of confidence in the selection in the "Confidence in this selection" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Voter Name** (`voter_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`voter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation purposes.
* **Affiliation or Organization** (`voter_affiliation`, text, optional): Enter your affiliation or organization (optional).
* **Highlight Option 1** (`highlight_one_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the first key achievement or event.
* **Impact Rating for Option 1** (`highlight_one_impact`, select_one, required): Select the impact rating for the first highlight (Minimal, Moderate, Significant, Major, or Critical).
* **Highlight Option 2** (`highlight_two_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the second key achievement or event.
* **Impact Rating for Option 2** (`highlight_two_impact`, select_one, required): Select the impact rating for the second highlight (Minimal, Moderate, Significant, Major, or Critical).
* **Highlight Option 3** (`highlight_three_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the third key achievement or event.
* **Impact Rating for Option 3** (`highlight_three_impact`, select_one, required): Select the impact rating for the third highlight (Minimal, Moderate, Significant, Major, or Critical).
* **Select your top highlight** (`top_highlight_choice`, select_one, required): Select the option that you think is most important.
* **Why is this your top choice?** (`choice_justification`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your selection.
* **Additional highlights to consider** (`additional_highlights`, text, optional): Enter any other highlights you think are worth considering.
* **Confidence in this selection** (`voting_confidence`, select_one, required): Select your level of confidence in the selection (Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, or Very High).
