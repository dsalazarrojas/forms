# Life Vest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your interaction with life vests, including how many you own, how frequently you use them, and for how long.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the number of life vests you own.
2. Choose how often you use your life vests from the options provided.
3. Select the duration of use for your life vests.
4. Indicate the frequency of use for your life vests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Life Vests Owned** (customer_survey, Text, Optional): Enter the number of life vests you own, or indicate that you do not own any.
* **Life Vests Use Frequency** (life_vests_owned, Number, Optional): This field is currently empty and only serves as a placeholder. Please disregard it.
* **Years of Use** (years_of_use, Select One, Optional): Select the duration of use for your life vests from the options provided:
	+ years 0-5
	+ years 5-10
	+ years 10-20
	+ years 20 plus
* **Frequency of Use** (frequency_of_use, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the frequency options that apply to your use of life vests:
	+ daily
	+ weekly
	+ monthly
	+ never

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to help us better understand life vest usage.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
