# Car Dealership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience at a car dealership.
## How To Complete This Form
Please see the steps in the "How to Complete This Form" section above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating (2):** Overall Satisfaction Rating (`overall_satisfaction_rating`, number, true) Rate your overall satisfaction with the experience at the dealership.
* **Dealership Cleanliness and Presentation (3):** Dealership Cleanliness and Presentation (`dealership_cleanliness_rating`, select_one, true) Rate the dealership's physical appearance.
* **Staff Friendliness and Professionalism (4):** Staff Friendliness and Professionalism (`staff_friendliness_rating`, select_one, true) Rate the friendliness and professionalism of the staff.
* **Wait Time for Assistance (5):** Wait Time for Assistance (`wait_time_rating`, select_one, true) Rate how long you had to wait for assistance.
* **Quality and Selection of Vehicles (6):** Quality and Selection of Vehicles (`vehicle_selection_rating`, select_one, true) Rate the quality and selection of vehicles available.
* **Transparency of the Sales Process (7):** Transparency of the Sales Process (`sales_process_rating`, select_one, true) Rate the transparency of the sales process.
* **Efficiency of the Finance Department (8):** Efficiency of the Finance Department (`finance_process_rating`, select_one, true) Rate the efficiency of the finance department.
* **Likelihood of Recommending This Dealership (9):** Likelihood of Recommending This Dealership (`purchase_likelihood`, number, true) Rate your likelihood of recommending the dealership to others.
* **Future Purchase Intent (10):** Future Purchase Intent (`future_purchase_intent`, select_one, true) Indicate whether you plan to purchase from the dealership again.
* **Specific Feedback or Concerns (11):** Specific Feedback or Concerns (`specific_feedback`, text, false) Share any specific feedback or concerns you may have.
* **Full Name (12):** Full Name (`customer_full_name`, text, false) Enter your full name (if desired).
* **Email Address (13):** Email Address (`customer_email_address`, email, false) Enter your email address (if desired).
* **Phone Number (14):** Phone Number (`customer_phone_number`, text, false) Enter your phone number (if desired).
* **Date of Survey (15):** Date of Survey (`survey_date`, date, true) Enter the current date.
* **Type Your Name for Survey Confirmation (16):** Type Your Name for Survey Confirmation (`survey_signature_confirmation`, text, true) Type in your name for survey confirmation.
