# Net User Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Net User Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect user feedback on the Net service. It's essential to provide accurate and honest information to help us improve the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2.  Enter your Email Address to help us contact you if needed.
3.  Select how you feel about our service (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor) in the "How do you feel about our service?" field.
4.  Indicate if you have encountered any issues with our service (True or False).
5.  Fill in the Date of last use and Time of last use if you've interacted with the service recently.
6.  Add any Comments you'd like to share about your experience.
7.  If desired, provide your Phone Number for future contact (this is optional).
8.  Add any Additional feedback about your experience with the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **How do you feel about our service?** (select_one, required): Select how you feel about our service (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
*   **Have you encountered any issues with our service?** (select_multiple, required): Indicate if you've encountered any issues (True or False).
*   **Date of last use** (date, required): Fill in the Date of last use if you've interacted with the service recently.
*   **Time of last use** (time, required): Fill in the Time of last use if you've interacted with the service recently.
*   **Comments** (note, optional): Add any Comments about your experience.
*   **Phone Number** (text, optional): Provide your Phone Number for future contact if desired.
*   **Additional feedback** (text, optional): Add any Additional feedback about your experience with the service.
