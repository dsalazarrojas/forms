# Open Education Resource Usage Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the usage and experiences of open education resources (OER) in teaching and learning. This survey aims to gather data on how individuals have used OER, their satisfaction with OER, and their experiences with the benefits and challenges of OER usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the **Institution information** field with your institution's details.
2. Answer the question **What is OER?** to familiarize yourself with the concept of OER.
3. Select the ways in which you have used OER in teaching and learning activities in the **How have you used OER in teaching and learning?** field.
4. Let us know if you have ever used OER in the **Have you ever used OER?** field.
5. If you have used OER, specify the OER you have used in the **What OER have you used?** field.
6. Rate your satisfaction with your OER usage in the **How satisfied are you with your OER usage?** field.
7. Describe the benefits of OER you have experienced in the **What are the benefits of OER you have experienced?** field.
8. Describe the challenges of OER you have experienced in the **What are the challenges of OER you have experienced?** field.
9. Fill in your **Job title/position** and **Contact email** fields to provide contact information.
10. Provide your **Contact phone number**, **Job department**, **Job institution**, and **Job organizational unit** fields if applicable.
11. Select your **Job level** from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Institution information**: Please fill in your institution's name and any other relevant details.
* **What is OER?**: This is a brief explanation of open education resources (OER) for your reference. OER are educational materials that are licensed under Creative Commons or other open licenses, allowing for free and open access.
* **How have you used OER in teaching and learning?**: Select all the ways in which you have used OER, such as using OER in course materials, with students, or in other teaching and learning activities.
* **Have you ever used OER?**: Answer this question with either 'True' or 'False' to indicate your experience with OER.
* **What OER have you used?**: If you have used OER, specify the OER you have used. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **How satisfied are you with your OER usage?**: Rate your satisfaction with your OER usage on a scale of Satisfied, Not satisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **What are the benefits of OER you have experienced?**: Describe the benefits you have experienced from using OER, such as cost savings, ease of access, or improved student engagement.
* **What are the challenges of OER you have experienced?**: Describe the challenges you have faced when using OER, such as technical issues, copyright concerns, or lack of support.
* **Job title/position**: Fill in your job title and position in your institution.
* **Contact email**: Provide your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Contact phone number**: Provide your contact phone number.
* **Job department**: Indicate your department within the institution.
* **Job institution**: Fill in the name of your institution.
* **Job organizational unit**: Specify your job organizational unit within the institution.
* **Job level**: Select your job level as Entry-level, Mid-level, or Senior-level.
* **Thank you for participating in this survey!**: This is a confirmation message at the end of the form.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, feel free to ask.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
