# Theology And History Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Theology And History Survey" form is designed to collect information from users on their background in Theology and History. The survey aims to gather insights into a user's knowledge and experience in these fields.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the main page.
2. Answer each question on this page.
3. If you need to add or remove any questions, click the "Add" or "Delete" buttons.
4. Once you have completed this page, click "Next" to proceed to the next page.
5. Repeat this process until you have completed all pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Theology (1)** (`number`, required/optional): This question assesses your level of knowledge in Theology.
	+ Please enter a number between 0 and 10, where 0 means you have no knowledge and 10 means you have an advanced degree in Theology.
* **History (2)** (`text`, required/optional): This question asks about your experience in History.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your background in History.
* **No further questions on this page.**

## Tips

* Please be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form will only take a few minutes to complete.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask and we will be happy to assist.
