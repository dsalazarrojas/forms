# Employee Workload Check In Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Workload Check In Survey" is a tool for employees to report their current workload and stress levels, as well as provide feedback on manager and team support. This information will help managers and team leads to identify areas that require improvement and provide necessary support to maintain a healthy work-life balance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Select the best option that describes your current situation.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field if you wish to receive a copy of your survey response.
5. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Manager Comments" field if you wish to provide comments to your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`1`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional context or information about your current workload.
* **How often do you feel stressed?** (`2`, select_one, optional): This field asks how often you feel stressed about your current workload. Please select from the options:
	+ Never
	+ Slightly
	+ Somewhat
	+ A lot
	+ A lot of the time
* **How often do you have the resources to complete your work?** (`3`, select_one, optional): This field asks how often you have the necessary resources to complete your work. Please select from the options:
	+ Always
	+ Most of the time
	+ Slightly
	+ Never
* **How many hours have you worked in the last 7 days?** (`4`, number, optional): This field asks for the total number of hours you have worked in the last 7 days.
* **Does your team have enough capacity?** (`5`, select_one, optional): This field asks if your team has enough capacity to complete their tasks. Please select from the options:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
	+ 'Unsure'
