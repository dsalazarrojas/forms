# Social Care Digital Transformation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Care Digital Transformation Survey is designed to help Social Care professionals like you identify their digital transformation needs and provide feedback on how to improve the digital tools used in their practice. This guide will help you complete the form and understand what information we need from you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please take a few minutes to read the guide and understand each field's purpose.
2. Answer each field according to your needs and interests.
3. Provide as much detail as you need to answer each field.
4. Click submit when you are finished and we will review your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Digital Transformation** (id: 2, label: Digital Transformation, hint: What area of digital transformation would you like to help with?, type: select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is used to select the area of digital transformation you are interested in. Choose one of the options: Digital Tools, Skills Development, or Priorities.
* **Please describe your chosen transformation** (id: 3, label: Please describe your chosen transformation, hint: Provide more details about your chosen area, type: text, required: false)
	+ Please provide a brief description of the chosen area you selected in the previous field.
* **Skills Development** (id: 4, label: Skills Development, hint: What level of skills development do you need?, type: select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is used to select the level of skills development you need. Choose one of the options: Basic Skills, Intermediate Skills, or Advanced Skills.
* **Please provide more details about your chosen skills development** (id: 5, label: Please provide more details about your chosen skills development, hint: Describe how you will use these skills to achieve your goals, type: text, required: false)
	+ Please provide a brief description of how you will use the skills you selected in the previous field to achieve your goals.
* **Frequency of Use** (id: 6, label: Frequency of Use, hint: How often would you use the new skill?, type: select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This field is used to select how often you would use the new skill. Choose one or more of the options: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Feedback** (id: 7, label: Feedback, hint: Do you have any other feedback on our digital tools?, type: text, required: false)
	+ Please provide any additional feedback you have on our digital tools.
* **Please provide your feedback** (id: 8, label: Please provide your feedback, hint: Share your thoughts on how we can improve our digital tools, type: text, required: false)
	+ Please share your thoughts on how we can improve our digital tools.
* **Thank you!** (id: 9, label: Thank you, hint: Thank you for taking the time to provide us feedback, type: text, required: false)
	+ This is a thank you message. Please take a moment to read it and then submit your feedback.
