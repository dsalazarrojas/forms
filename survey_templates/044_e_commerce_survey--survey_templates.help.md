# E Commerce Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The E Commerce Survey is designed to collect information from e-commerce customers to gain insights into their shopping habits, purchase decisions, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out each page with the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *)
- Enter the correct information in each field, following the instructions for each type of field
- If you are not sure about any field, leave a comment in the "Additional Comments" section

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): This page asks for the customer's basic information such as their name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): This page will ask for more contact information, such as the customer's address and other contact preferences.
* **Order History** (`order_history`, text, optional): This page asks about the customer's past purchases and shopping history.
* **Product Purchases** (`product_purchases`, text, required): This page will ask about the customer's recent purchases and preferences.
* **Demographics** (`demographics`, text, optional): This page will ask about the customer's demographic information such as age, income, and occupation.
* **Shopping Habits** (`shopping_habits`, text, required): This page will ask about the customer's shopping habits, such as how often they shop online.
* **Purchase Decisions** (`purchase_decisions`, text, required): This page will ask about the factors that influence the customer's purchase decisions.
* **Customer Experience** (`customer_experience`, text, optional): This page will ask about the customer's experience with the store, including satisfaction and preferences.
* **E Commerce Survey** (`e_commerce_survey`, text, required): This page is a general page for customers to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This page will ask customers to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field asks for the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field asks for the customer's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): This field asks for the customer's date of birth.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This field asks for the customer's preferred shopping time.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required): This field asks the customer to select one option from the list of options provided.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): This field asks the customer to select multiple options from the list provided.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This field is for the customer to provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Integer Field** (`integer_field`, number, optional): This field asks for a numerical value.
* **Decimal Field** (`decimal_field`, number, optional): This field asks for a decimal value.
* **Select Three** (`select_three`, select_one, required): This field asks the customer to select one option from the list provided.
* **E Commerce Suggestions** (`e_commerce_suggestions`, text, required): This field asks for any additional suggestions or ideas the customer has for the store.
* **Shopping Frequency** (`shopping_frequency`, number, required): This field asks how often the customer shops online.
* **Average Order Value** (`average_order_value`, number, required): This field asks for the customer's average order value.
