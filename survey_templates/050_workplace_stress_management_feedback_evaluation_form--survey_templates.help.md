# Workplace Stress Management Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workplace stress management feedback evaluation form is designed to collect information from employees on their current stress levels, areas of stress, causes of stress, and suggestions for improvement. This form will help administrators understand where to focus their efforts to reduce stress in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the questions and options carefully before answering.
2. Select all the options that apply to you for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for improving stress management in the workplace.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title**: workplace_stress_management_feedback_evaluation_form - This is the title of the form.
* **Introduction** (`intro`): This is a text field where you can provide any background information about your stress experience. Please type a brief introduction to help us understand your situation.
* **Current Stress Levels** (`stress_levels`): Select all the levels that apply to your current stress situation. Please choose one or multiple options.
	+ Option 1: High levels of stress
	+ Option 2: Medium levels of stress
	+ Option 3: Low levels of stress
* **Stress Areas** (`stress_areas`): Select all the areas where you are experiencing stress. Please choose one or multiple options.
	+ Option 1: Work-related stress
	+ Option 2: Family-related stress
	+ Option 3: Financial stress
* **Stress Causes** (`stress_causes`): Select all the causes that apply to your stress situation. Please choose one or multiple options.
	+ Option 1: Work-life balance issues
	+ Option 2: Lack of job control
	+ Option 3: Unclear expectations
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`): This is a text field where you can provide any suggestions for improving stress management in the workplace.
* **Stress Reduction Methods** (`stress_reduction`): Select all the methods that you think can help reduce stress. Please choose one or multiple options.
	+ Option 1: Regular exercise
	+ Option 2: Meditation and mindfulness
	+ Option 3: Time management training
* **Stress Reduction Methods Used** (`stress_reduction_methods`): Select all the methods that you have used or are willing to use to reduce stress. Please choose one or multiple options.
	+ Option 1: Yoga
	+ Option 2: Journaling
	+ Option 3: Seeking support from HR
* **Support Services** (`support_services`): Select all the support services that you have used or are willing to use to reduce stress. Please choose one or multiple options.
	+ Option 1: Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
	+ Option 2: Counseling services
	+ Option 3: Mental health resources
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions for follow-up.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`): Select all the evaluation criteria that you think are important for assessing stress management. Please choose one or multiple options.
	+ Option 1: Communication
	+ Option 2: Support systems
	+ Option 3: Resources and tools

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Suggestions" and "Follow-up" fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please choose "Not Applicable" or "Not Sure".
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact your HR representative or manager.
