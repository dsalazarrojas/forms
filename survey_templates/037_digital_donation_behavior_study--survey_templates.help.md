# Digital Donation Behavior Study - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your digital donation behavior, including your online giving habits, attitudes towards digital donations, and demographic information. This will help the organization understand the trends and patterns of digital donations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and fill in the required information.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Enter dates in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter numbers in the correct format (e.g., 123.45 for decimal numbers).
5. Enter text in a clear and concise manner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1, text, optional): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please provide a short description of yourself and your experience with digital donations.
* **Demographic Information** (2, text, optional): This section is for you to provide your demographic information such as your age, location, and occupation. Please be as accurate as possible.
* **Online Giving Habits** (3, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about your online giving habits. Please select all options that apply to you.
	+ Option 1: I give online regularly
	+ Option 2: I give online occasionally
	+ Option 3: I do not give online
* **Attitudes** (4, select_one, optional): This question asks about your attitudes towards digital donations. Please select one option that best describes your attitude.
	+ Option 1: Digital donations are a convenient way to give
	+ Option 2: Digital donations are a secure way to give
	+ Option 3: Digital donations are not a preferred way to give
* **Donation Frequency** (5, date, optional): This question asks about your donation frequency. Please enter the date of your last donation in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Donation Amount** (6, number, optional): This question asks about your typical donation amount. Please enter a number in the correct format (e.g., 123.45 for decimal numbers).
* **Contact Information** (7, text, optional): This is for you to provide your contact information, such as your address and phone number. Please be as accurate as possible.
* **Email** (8, email, optional): This is for you to provide your email address.
* **Phone** (9, text, optional): This is for you to provide your phone number.
* **Website** (10, text, optional): This is for you to provide the website of the organization you support with digital donations.
* **Social Media** (11, text, optional): This is for you to provide your social media information, such as your social media handle.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* If you need help with any question, please contact us.
