# Beauty Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your beauty habits, preferences, and routines. Your feedback will help us understand your needs and interests in the beauty industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question accurately and honestly. You can select multiple options for certain questions, so take your time to review each question carefully. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your age group?** (`age_group`, select_one): Select the age group that best describes you. This will help us understand how beauty preferences and habits vary across different age groups.
* **How would you describe your skin type?** (`skin_type`, select_one): Choose the skin type that best describes your skin. This will help us understand your skin concerns and preferences.
* **How many steps are in your daily skincare routine?** (`skincare_steps_count`, number): Answer with the actual number of steps in your daily skincare routine. This will help us understand the complexity of your skincare routine.
* **How often do you wear makeup?** (`makeup_usage_frequency`, select_one): Select how often you wear makeup, from "Daily" to "Never". This will help us understand your makeup habits.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how important is hair care to you?** (`hair_care_importance`, number): Rate the importance of hair care to you, from 1 (not important at all) to 10 (very important).
* **Where do you primarily buy your beauty products?** (`shopping_location`, select_multiple): Choose all applicable places where you primarily buy your beauty products, from drugstores to subscription boxes.
* **What influences your beauty purchases the most?** (`purchase_influencers`, select_one): Select the factor that influences your beauty purchases the most, from social media influencers to brand reputation.
* **Approximately how much do you spend on beauty products monthly?** (`monthly_spend`, select_one): Choose the approximate monthly amount you spend on beauty products, from under $50 to over $250.
* **How important is it that your products are "Clean" or "Natural"?** (`ingredient_priority`, select_one): Rate the importance of products being "Clean" or "Natural" to you, from "Very Important" to "Not Important at all".
* **What is your favorite beauty category?** (`favorite_beauty_category`, select_one): Select your favorite beauty category, from skincare to haircare.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to share about your beauty journey?** (`open_feedback`, text): Write any additional comments or feedback about your beauty journey.
* **Date** (`survey_date`, date): Please enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
