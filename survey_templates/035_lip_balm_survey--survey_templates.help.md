# Lip Balm Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lip Balm Survey is designed to collect your basic contact information and preferences to help us better understand your needs and preferences for our lip balm products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name
2. Provide your Last Name
3. Enter your Email
4. Enter your Phone number

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you have access to and regularly check.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you for follow-up questions.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that we can contact you effectively.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at your preferred contact method.
