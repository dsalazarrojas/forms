# Bocce Score Card - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bocce Score Card is a form used for recording and tracking scores in Bocce games. It helps maintain accurate and organized records of matches, including team details, scores, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Bocce Score Card form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the match date and time in the "Match Date" field.
2. Enter the name of the league in the "League Name" field.
3. Select the current season in the "Season" field.
4. Choose the division in the "Division" field (if applicable).
5. Enter the court number where the match took place.
6. List the players for both Team 1 and Team 2 in their respective "Team 1 Players" and "Team 2 Players" fields.
7. Select the type of match in the "Game Type" field.
8. Record the winner of each frame from 1 to 4 in the corresponding "Frame 1 Winner" to "Frame 4 Winner" fields.
9. Enter the score for each frame from 1 to 4 in the "Frame 1 Score" to "Frame 4 Score" fields.
10. Record the total score for Team 1 and Team 2 in the "Team 1 Total Score" and "Team 2 Total Score" fields.
11. Select the overall match winner in the "Match Winner" field.
12. Enter your name as the scorekeeper in the "Scorekeeper Name" field.
13. If applicable, sign on behalf of Team 1 in the "Team 1 Captain Signature" field or Team 2 in the "Team 2 Captain Signature" field.
14. Enter any special circumstances or notes about the match in the "Match Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Match Date** (`match_date`): Enter the date of the game in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Match Time** (`match_time`): Enter the start time of the game in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **League Name** (`league_name`): Enter the name of the league where the game took place.
* **Season** (`season`): Enter the current season for the game.
* **Division** (`division`): If applicable, enter the division number or name.
* **Court Number** (`court_number`): Enter the number of the court where the game took place.
* **Team 1 Name** (`team_1_name`): Enter the name of the team playing as home.
* **Team 1 Players** (`team_1_players`): List all the players from Team 1.
* **Team 2 Name** (`team_2_name`): Enter the name of the team playing as away.
* **Team 2 Players** (`team_2_players`): List all the players from Team 2.
* **Game Type** (`game_type`): Select the type of match, such as Singles, Doubles, Triples, or Quadruples.
* **Frame 1 Winner** (`frame_1_winner`): Select the winner of Frame 1.
* **Frame 1 Score** (`frame_1_score`): Enter the score for Frame 1.
* **Frame 2 Winner** (`frame_2_winner`): Select the winner of Frame 2.
* **Frame 2 Score** (`frame_2_score`): Enter the score for Frame 2.
* **Frame 3 Winner** (`frame_3_winner`): Select the winner of Frame 3.
* **Frame 3 Score** (`frame_3_score`): Enter the score for Frame 3.
* **Frame 4 Winner** (`frame_4_winner`): Select the winner of Frame 4 (if applicable).
* **Frame 4 Score** (`frame_4_score`): Enter the score for Frame 4 (if applicable).
* **Team 1 Total Score** (`total_team_1`): Enter the total score for Team 1.
* **Team 2 Total Score** (`total_team_2`): Enter the total score for Team 2.
* **Match Winner** (`match_winner`): Select the overall winner of the match.
* **Scorekeeper Name** (`scorekeeper_name`): Enter your name as the scorekeeper.
* **Team 1 Captain Signature** (`team_1_signature`): If applicable, sign on behalf of Team 1.
* **Team 2 Captain Signature** (`team_2_signature`): If applicable, sign on behalf of Team 2.
* **Match Notes** (`notes`): Enter any special circumstances or notes about the match.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately record the match date and time to ensure correct reporting.
* Be clear and concise when entering team names and player lists.
* Double-check scores for each frame to ensure accuracy.
* If a frame is not played, leave the corresponding score field blank.
