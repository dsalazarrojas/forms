# Fun Group Dynamics Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about team dynamics, including collaboration, communication, and leadership. It will help teams identify areas where they can improve and provide insights on how to strengthen their interactions and relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the option that best describes your experience with each team dynamic.
2. Answer all questions honestly, and feel free to provide as much detail as you'd like in the text fields.
3. If you're unsure of an answer, choose the option that best fits your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Collaboration** (`group_dynamics`, number): This question asks about your team's overall collaboration style. Choose a number that reflects how often your team collaborates with each other.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, select_one): Select the size of your team: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Feedback Style** (`feedback_style`, text): Think of a team that gives constructive, specific feedback, but also acknowledges and appreciates each other's feelings and emotions. Please describe this team dynamic in a few sentences.
* **Team Culture** (`team_culture`, note): In this field, you can add any additional information about your team's culture. This is an optional and open-ended field, use it to provide any context that might be helpful.
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, select_multiple): Choose all the ways your team communicates with each other: Direct and Clear, Indirect and Clear, Direct and Vague, or Indirect and Vague.
* **How Often Do You Collaborate** (`collaboration_frequency`, select_one): Select the frequency of collaboration within your team: Almost Never, Rarely, Occasionally, Often, or Almost Always.
* **Fun Moments** (`fun_moments`, select_multiple): Choose all the fun moments you experience within your team: Celebrating Successes, Playing Games, Team Lunches, or Team Challenges.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text): What do you think would improve team collaboration and morale? Please provide any additional suggestions or ideas you have.
* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership`, select_one): Think of a team leader or manager who has positively impacted your team experience. Choose how often this leader leads your team: Always, Often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.
