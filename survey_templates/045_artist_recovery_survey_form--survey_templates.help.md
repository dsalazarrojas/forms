# Artist Recovery Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help collect data on artists' challenges, their impact, and potential support systems for recovery. It is intended to be filled out by artists who are facing difficulties and seeking support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your information:
	* Respondent Name: This is your full name or pseudonym.
	* Email Address (optional): Your primary contact email.
2. Answer the questions about your challenges:
	* What is Your Primary Challenge?: Select one from the list of options provided.
	* Describe Your Challenge in Detail: Explain the situation and its impact.
	* How Long Have You Been Facing This Challenge?: Select one from the list of duration options.
3. Assess the impact on your creative work and overall wellbeing:
	* Impact on Creative Work: Select one from the list of options.
	* Impact on Overall Wellbeing: Select one from the list of options.
4. Identify contributing factors:
	* What Factors Contributed to This Challenge?: Select all that apply from the list.
	* Personal or Internal Factors: Provide a short description of personal challenges that contributed.
5. Seek support:
	* Support or Help Already Received: Describe any assistance you have accessed.
	* What Support Would Help Most?: Select all that apply from the list.
6. Check your recovery progress:
	* Are You Currently Recovering?: Select one from the list of options.
7. Provide feedback:
	* What Recommendations Would You Make?: Share your ideas for improving support systems.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (1): Provide your full name or pseudonym.
* **Email Address** (2): Your primary contact email (optional).
* **Primary Artistic Discipline** (3): Your main area of creative work.
* **Date Completing This Survey** (4): When you are filling this out.
* **What is Your Primary Challenge?** (5):
	+ Select one from the list: Financial Difficulties, Health or Wellness Issues, Burnout or Exhaustion, Loss of Creativity or Inspiration, Relationship or Personal Problems, Career Uncertainty, Mental Health Concerns, Access to Resources, Other.
* **Describe Your Challenge in Detail** (6): Explain the situation and its impact.
* **How Long Have You Been Facing This Challenge?** (7):
	+ Select one from the list: Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2+ years.
* **What Factors Contributed to This Challenge?** (9):
	+ Select all that apply: Economic Hardship, Lack of Support System, Health Crisis, Mental Health Struggles, Family or Relationship Issues, Lack of Opportunities, Discrimination or Barriers, Loss or Grief, Pandemic or External Crisis.
* **Personal or Internal Factors** (10): Provide a short description of personal challenges that contributed.
* **Support or Help Already Received** (8): Describe any assistance you have accessed.
* **What Support Would Help Most?** (11):
	+ Select all that apply: Financial Assistance, Mental Health Support, Creative Community, Job or Income Opportunity, Skills Training, Mentorship or Guidance, Medical Care, Legal Assistance, Social Connection.
* **Are You Currently Recovering?** (12):
	+ Select one from the list: Actively recovering and improving, Slowly improving, Stable but not improving, Worsening, Just beginning to seek help.
* **Are You Aware of Available Resources?** (13):
	+ Select one from the list: Yes, well informed, Somewhat informed, Not very informed, Not at all informed.
* **What Prevents You from Getting Help?** (14): Describe obstacles to accessing support.
* **What Recommendations Would You Make?** (15): Share your ideas for improving support systems.
* **We May Contact You for Follow-up Support** (16):
	+ Select one from the list: Yes, contact me, No, do not contact, Email only.
