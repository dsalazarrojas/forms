# Study Streams Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Study Streams Assessment Survey form is designed to collect feedback on the quality of teaching and learning within study streams. It aims to help identify areas of improvement and provide insights for future development of educational programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer them based on your experience and knowledge of the study stream.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Fill out the form only once per study stream.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **study_streams_assessment_survey** (id: 1, type: note, required: false): This is a note field where you can provide general comments or observations about the study stream.
* **teaching_methods** (id: 2, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select all the teaching methods that apply to your study stream. For example, "Yes" for "Yes, I use this method" or "No" for "I don't use this method".
* **teaching_formats** (id: 3, type: select_one, required: false): Select the format of your teaching method. For example, "Yes" for "Yes, I use this format" or "No" for "I don't use this format".
* **student_feedback** (id: 4, type: text, required: false): Describe any feedback you have received from students about your teaching.
* **stream_feedback** (id: 5, type: text, required: false): Provide any general feedback you have about the study stream.
* **stream_rating** (id: 6, type: number, required: false): Rate the overall quality of the study stream, using a scale of 1-5.
* **teaching_quality** (id: 7, type: select_one, required: false): Evaluate the quality of your teaching, using the options "Good" or "Poor".
* **teaching_support** (id: 8, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the types of support you provide to your students, such as "Yes, I provide this type of support" or "No, I don't provide this type of support".
* **teaching_climate** (id: 9, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the teaching climate you strive to maintain, such as "Yes, I strive for this climate" or "No, I don't strive for this climate".
* **training_providers** (id: 10, type: select_one, required: false): Select whether you work with training providers, using the options "Yes" or "No".
* **teaching_method_format** (id: 11, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the teaching method formats you use, such as "Yes, I use this format" or "No, I don't use this format".
* **teaching_method_type** (id: 12, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the teaching method types you use, such as "Yes, I use this type" or "No, I don't use this type".
* **teaching_method_topic** (id: 13, type: text, required: false): Provide the topic of your teaching method, if applicable.
* **teaching_method_style** (id: 14, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the teaching method style you use, such as "Yes, I use this style" or "No, I don't use this style".
* **teaching_method_context** (id: 15, type: text, required: false): Describe the context in which you teach, if applicable.
* **course_materials** (id: 16, type: text, required: false): List any course materials you use.
* **course_format** (id: 17, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the course format you use, such as "Yes, I use this format" or "No, I don't use this format".
* **teaching_outcomes** (id: 18, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the teaching outcomes you strive for, such as "Yes, I strive for this outcome" or "No, I don't strive for this outcome".
* **teaching_goals** (id: 19, type: text, required: false): Describe your teaching goals for the course.
* **student_goals** (id: 20, type: text, required: false): Describe the goals you set for your students.
* **course_length** (id: 21, type: text, required: false): Provide the length of your course.
* **student_feedback_satisfies** (id: 22, type: text, required: false): Describe how your student feedback satisfies your needs.
* **feedback_frequency** (id: 23, type: text, required: false): Describe how often you receive feedback from your students.
* **course_materials_used** (id: 24, type: text, required: false): List the course materials you use.
* **course_materials_available** (id: 25, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select whether the course materials you use are available, such as "Yes, they are available" or "No, they are not available".
