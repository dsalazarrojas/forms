# Customer Satisfaction Survey Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify that customers have received and responded to a satisfaction survey or questionnaire. It allows customers to provide additional comments or feedback about their experience and helps the organization to gauge the effectiveness of the survey process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have received a survey or questionnaire from our organization.
2. Review the questions and options provided in the form carefully.
3. Answer the questions truthfully, selecting the options that best reflect your experience.
4. Use the free-text areas to provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Survey Form**:
	+ A text input field where you can enter the survey or questionnaire you received.
* **Customer Feedback Questions**:
	+ Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the survey or questionnaire was provided.
* **Customer Service Quality**:
	+ Select one of the options to rate the quality of service you received.
* **Feedback Frequency**:
	+ Enter a number to indicate how often you provide feedback to us.
* **Customer Service Improvement Plan**:
	+ Enter a date and time to indicate when you would like to see an improvement to our service.
* **Customer Survey Response**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Customer Survey Response 2**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Customer Survey Response 3**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Customer Service Quality 2**:
	+ Select one of the options to rate the quality of service you received this time.
* **Customer Service Quality 3**:
	+ Select one of the options to rate the quality of service you received this time.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Use clear and concise language when providing additional comments or feedback.
* If you have any concerns or issues that you would like to bring to our attention, please do so in the free-text areas.
