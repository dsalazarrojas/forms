# American Dream Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The American Dream Survey is a comprehensive questionnaire designed to gather insights from individuals about their experiences and perceptions of the American Dream. This survey aims to understand what the American Dream means to different people, how achievable it is, and what challenges they face in pursuing it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand what questions are being asked.
2. Answer all the questions truthfully, based on your own experiences and perceptions.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. For open-ended questions, provide detailed and honest answers.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
