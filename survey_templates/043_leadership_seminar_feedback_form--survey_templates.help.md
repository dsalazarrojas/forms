# Leadership Seminar Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Seminar Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from attendees about their experience in a leadership seminar. Your responses will help us understand what you thought of the seminar and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with your honest opinions.
* Rate the session, speakers, and venue based on the scales provided.
* Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Rating (id: 1)**: Rate the seminar on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Session Content (id: 2)**: Share your thoughts about the seminar content, what you liked or disliked, and any suggestions for improvement.
* **Speaker Rating (id: 3)**: Rate the speaker's performance, presentation, and overall effectiveness.
* **Speaker Content (id: 4)**: Provide feedback on the speaker's content and presentation style.
* **Venue Rating (id: 5)**: Rate the seminar venue, including its condition, facilities, and overall comfort.
* **Value Added (id: 6)**: Share any additional benefits or value you obtained from the seminar.
* **Suggestions (id: 7)**: Offer any suggestions for improvements, changes, or future seminar topics.
* **Email (id: 8)**: Provide your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone (id: 9)**: Enter your phone number, if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Name (id: 10)**: Enter your name, as we'd like to address you personally.
* **Organization (id: 11)**: Share your organization or affiliation.
* **Session Title (id: 12)**: Select the seminar title from the options provided.
* **Suggestion (id: 13)**: Offer any additional suggestions or comments.
* **Rating (id: 14)**: Rate the overall seminar experience on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Feedback (id: 15)**: Share any additional comments or feedback you may have about the seminar.

Note: Some fields are optional, but we appreciate your feedback on all aspects of the seminar.
