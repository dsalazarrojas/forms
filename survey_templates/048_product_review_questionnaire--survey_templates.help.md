# product_review_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback about a product, allowing us to gauge their satisfaction and identify areas for improvement. Your responses will help us better understand your experience and improve the product.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly to provide the most valuable feedback.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field name.
3. You can leave optional fields blank if you're unsure or don't have information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **product_name* (text): Enter the name of the product you are reviewing.
* **rating* (number): Rate your satisfaction with the product, on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **review_date* (date): Enter the date you made the purchase or used the product. This helps us track your experience over time.
* **review_time* (time): If possible, enter the time of day you made the purchase or used the product. This can help us understand your usage patterns.
* **rating_comment** (text): Share any thoughts or comments about your rating.
* **product_rating** (number): Another opportunity to rate your satisfaction with the product.
* **purchase_method* (select_one): How did you buy the product? Choose from: product bought, service bought, or other.
* **purchased_from* (select_multiple): Where did you buy the product from? Choose from: physical store, online store, or other.
* **rating_reason** (select_multiple): Why do you think you gave the rating you did? Choose from: Good, Fair.
* **rating_satisfied* (select_one): Are you satisfied with the product? Choose from: satisfied, not satisfied.
* **product_recommendation* (select_one): Would you recommend this product? Choose from: recommended, not recommended.
* **review_comment** (text): Add any additional comments about your experience with the product.
* **review_id** (number): This is a unique identifier for your review, but you can leave it blank if you don't know it.
