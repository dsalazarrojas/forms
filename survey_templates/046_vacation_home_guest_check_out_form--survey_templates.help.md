# Vacation Home Guest Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback, comments, and other relevant details from guests staying at the vacation home. It helps us improve our services and ensure that you have a great experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your information and comments. Make sure to include your stay date, check out date, and any comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Home Address** (`home_address`, text, optional): Please enter your address or the address of the vacation home where you stayed. 
* **What was your stay like?** (`feedback_rating`, text, optional): Share your experience with us. What did you think of the vacation home? 
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to share your email address with us, please enter it here. This will help us contact you for any follow-up questions or future promotions. 
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by us. 
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out`, date, optional): Please enter the date you're checking out. 
* **Check Out Time** (`check_out_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to check out. 
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback you may have. 
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): If you'd like to identify yourself, please enter your name. 
* **Check Out Staff** (`check_out_staff`, text, optional): If you interacted with a staff member, please let us know their name. 
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number if you'd like us to reach you. 
* **Check Out Note** (`check_out_note`, note, optional): Enter any other details about your check out, such as how we can contact you or any specific needs you may have. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure we can follow up with you.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback is appreciated and helps us improve our services.
