# Soccer Coach Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the performance of soccer coaches. It assesses their experience, communication, leadership, sportsmanship, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one or more of the options for each field.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (fields with a "required" flag).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **player_experience** (Yes, No): Select whether the coach has positive or negative experience with the players.
* **communication** (Yes, No): Choose whether the coach communicates effectively with the players.
* **leadership** (Yes, No): Determine if the coach shows strong leadership skills.
* **sportsmanship** (Yes, No): Evaluate the coach's demonstration of fair and respectful behavior towards the players.
* **coach_satisfaction** (Satisfied, Dissatisfied): Rate your overall satisfaction with the coach's performance.
* **positive_athlete_experiences** (Yes, No): Choose whether the players have had positive experiences with the coach.
* **feedback_on_coach_behaviors** (Yes, No): Determine if the coach's behaviors are open to feedback.
* **athlete_involvement** (Yes, No): Evaluate the level of involvement and engagement from the athletes.
* **coach_support** (Yes, No): Rate the level of support from the coach.
* **coach_development** (Yes, No): Determine if the coach shows willingness to learn and grow professionally.
* **overall_satisfaction** (Satisfied, Dissatisfied): Rate your overall satisfaction with the coach's performance.
* **player_satisfaction** (Satisfied, Dissatisfied): Rate the satisfaction of the players with the coach's performance.
* **feedback_from_coach** (Yes, No): Evaluate if the coach is open to feedback.
* **feedback_from_athlete_parents** (Yes, No): Determine if the coach seeks feedback from athlete parents.
* **coach_satisfaction_2** (Satisfied, Dissatisfied): Rate your overall satisfaction with the coach's performance.
* **athlete_satisfaction** (Satisfied, Dissatisfied): Rate the satisfaction of the athletes with the coach's performance.
* **overall_satisfaction_2** (Satisfied, Dissatisfied): Rate your overall satisfaction with the coach's performance.
* **feedback_from_coach_2** (Yes, No): Evaluate if the coach is open to feedback.
* **feedback_from_athlete_parents_2** (Yes, No): Determine if the coach seeks feedback from athlete parents.

Note: The fields marked with a "2" at the end of their label are duplicates of fields with the same functionality, and can likely be ignored or removed.
