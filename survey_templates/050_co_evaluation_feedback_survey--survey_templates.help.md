# Co Evaluation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Co Evaluation Feedback Survey is a tool to gather feedback from team members or collaborators who have worked on a specific project or team. This form helps evaluate their performance, identify areas of improvement, and provide recommendations for future collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information:
	* Your Name: Enter your name as a collaborator or team member.
	* Email Address: Enter your email address.
	* Evaluation Date: Enter the date of the evaluation.
	* Project or Team Name: Enter the name of the collaboration or project being evaluated.
	* Collaborator Being Evaluated: Enter the name of the team member or collaborator being evaluated.
2. Evaluate the collaborator's performance:
	* Communication Skills: Rate their communication skills as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
	* Reliability and Dependability: Rate their reliability and dependability as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
	* Teamwork and Cooperation: Rate their teamwork and cooperation as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
	* Problem-Solving Contribution: Rate their problem-solving skills as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
	* Initiative and Proactivity: Rate their initiative and proactivity as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
	* Quality of Work: Rate their quality of work as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
3. Identify areas of improvement:
	* Areas for Improvement: Suggest areas where the collaborator could improve.
4. Recommend for future collaboration:
	* Recommend for Future Collaboration: Choose whether you would recommend this collaborator for future projects.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a collaborator or team member.
* **Your Email Address** (`evaluator_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Project or Team Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the collaboration or project being evaluated.
* **Collaborator Being Evaluated** (`collaborator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team member or collaborator being evaluated.
* **Collaboration Period** (`collaboration_period`, text, required): Enter the duration of the collaboration.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_rating`, select_one, required): Rate their communication skills as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Reliability and Dependability** (`reliability_rating`, select_one, required): Rate their reliability and dependability as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Teamwork and Cooperation** (`teamwork_rating`, select_one, required): Rate their teamwork and cooperation as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Problem-Solving Contribution** (`problem_solving_rating`, select_one, required): Rate their problem-solving skills as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Initiative and Proactivity** (`initiative_rating`, select_one, required): Rate their initiative and proactivity as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Quality of Work** (`quality_work_rating`, select_one, required): Rate their quality of work as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Conflict Resolution and Maturity** (`conflict_management`, select_one, not required): Rate their conflict resolution and maturity as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Key Strengths as a Collaborator** (`strengths_list`, text, required): Suggest their main strengths.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, not required): Suggest areas for improvement.
* **Recommend for Future Collaboration** (`recommend_collaboration`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would recommend this collaborator for future projects.
