## Pulse Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pulse Survey is a tool designed to gather opinions and feedback from users about their work. It's meant to be a brief and straightforward questionnaire that helps us understand what you like most and least about your work and work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.

3. You can complete the form at your own pace, but please answer all questions, even if they seem straightforward or easy.
