# real_estate_purchase_satisfaction_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your satisfaction with the real estate purchase experience, providing an opportunity for you to share your feedback on the service provided by the real estate agent. Your input will help us improve our service and better serve future clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the corresponding answers to the questions provided, taking into account your honest experience with the real estate agent. Make sure to answer all questions thoroughly, especially the ones marked as required (denoted by "Yes" for select_one options or checked for select_multiple options).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How was your experience with the real estate agent?** (select_multiple): Select the level of satisfaction with your overall experience with the real estate agent, from 1 - Unsatisfactory to 5 - Highly Extremely Satisfactory.
- **What was the transaction ID?** (text): Enter the unique transaction ID associated with your purchase. This is a mandatory field, as it helps us track your specific purchase.
- **What location did you purchase the real estate property in?** (text): Provide the name or address of the location where you purchased the real estate property.
- **What was the price of the real estate property you purchased?** (number): Enter the exact price of the property you acquired.
- **How would you rate the real estate agent's performance?** (select_multiple): Assess the agent's performance based on your experience, with ratings from 1 - 1 to 5 - 5.
- **How clear was the real estate agent's explanation of the property's condition?** (select_one): Evaluate the clarity of the agent's explanation, choosing between 'Yes', 'No', and 'Other' (if not listed).
- **How would you rate the real estate agent's overall performance?** (select_one): Determine whether the agent's performance was satisfactory ('Yes'), not satisfactory ('No'), or something else ('Other').
- **How often would you say you interacted with the real estate agent?** (select_multiple): Choose how frequently you interacted with the agent, from 1 - Never to 5 - Almost All.
- **How would you rate the overall satisfaction with the real estate purchase?** (select_multiple): Evaluate your satisfaction with the entire purchase experience, from 1 - Not at All to 5 - Almost All.
- **How would you rate the real estate agent's expertise in the field?** (select_one): Determine if the agent demonstrated expertise ('Yes'), not ('No'), or something else ('Other').
- **How would you rate the real estate agent's responsiveness to your questions and concerns?** (select_one): Assess the agent's responsiveness, choosing 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'.
- **Is there anything else you'd like to share about your experience with the real estate agent?** (note): If you have additional comments or concerns, please provide them here. This field is optional, but your feedback will be valuable to us.

## Tips
- Answer all required fields thoroughly, as they are essential for us to understand your experience.
- If you need more time or want to add additional comments, use the 'Is there anything else you'd like to share...' field.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
