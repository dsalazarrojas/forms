# network_performance_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback on the current state of your network performance. Your input will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, providing honest and detailed feedback.
2. You can select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.
3. If you have any additional comments or details about the issue, please provide them in the text field after each question.
4. Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the form and its purpose.
	+ This is the first page of the form and provides a general overview of the questions you will be answering.
* **What is the current state of your network quality?**: This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the current state of your network.
	+ Choose "Excellent" if you are extremely satisfied with the network quality.
	+ Choose "Fair" if you are neutral or average.
	+ Choose "Poor" if you are not satisfied with the network quality.
* **Please identify any issues you have with your network**: This question allows you to describe any problems or issues you are experiencing with the network.
	+ Please provide detailed feedback on any issues or problems you are experiencing.
* **How often do you encounter network issues?**: This question helps us understand how frequently you are experiencing network issues.
	+ Choose "Very often" if you are frequently experiencing network issues.
	+ Choose "Occasionally" if you are occasionally experiencing network issues.
	+ Choose "Rarely" if you are rarely experiencing network issues.
* **Can you provide more details about the issue?** (optional): This question allows you to provide any additional comments or details about the issue.
	+ Please use the text field to provide more information about the issue.
* **How would you rate your network quality?**: This question helps us understand your overall assessment of the network quality.
	+ Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 to rate your network quality.
* **How satisfied are you with your network?**: This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the network.
	+ Choose "Very satisfied" if you are extremely satisfied with the network.
	+ Choose "Satisfied" if you are fairly satisfied.
	+ Choose "Neutral" if you are neutral.
	+ Choose "Dissatisfied" if you are not satisfied.
	+ Choose "Very dissatisfied" if you are extremely dissatisfied.
* **Submit**: Once you have answered all the questions, click this button to submit your feedback.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide detailed feedback.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is invaluable and will help us improve the network performance.
