<thinking>**Workplace Cycling Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide**

The Workplace Cycling Program Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from employees about their participation and satisfaction with the program. This form is intended to evaluate the effectiveness and impact of the program on employee wellness and engagement. Before filling out this form, please ensure that you have participated in the program and have a genuine understanding of its benefits and challenges.

To avoid duplication, please check if you have already filled out this form before.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. For the "Overall Satisfaction" question, please rate your satisfaction on a scale of 0-100.
3. For the "Frequency of Participation" question, select the option that best describes how often you participate in the program.
4. If you do not participate in the program, choose "Never" for the "Frequency of Participation" question.
5. For the "Reasons for Non-Participation" question, select all that apply.
6. For the "Improvement Ideas" question, provide any suggestions you have for enhancing the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program participation** (`program_participation`, number, optional): This question asks about your level of participation in the program. Please answer based on your actual involvement.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Please rate your satisfaction with the program on a scale of 0-100.
* **Improvement ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional): Share any ideas you have for improving the program.
* **Frequency of participation** (`frequency_participation`, select_one, required): Select how often you participate in the program:
	+ 1-2 times a week
	+ 3-4 times a week
	+ Daily
	+ Less than once a month
	+ Never
* **Reasons for non-participation** (`reasons_for_non_participation`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply to the reasons you do not participate in the program:
	+ Health reasons
	+ Lack of interest
	+ Other (please specify)
	+ Schedule conflicts
	+ Work reasons

## Tips

* Answer honestly and sincerely to provide valuable feedback.
* Provide specific examples and details if possible.
* If you are unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Please submit the form once you have completed it.
