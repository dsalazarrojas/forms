# University Faculty Employment Workload Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the employment workload of university faculty members to help administrators understand the distribution of work among faculty members and make informed decisions about resource allocation and support.

## How To Complete This Form
The form is divided into multiple pages, each covering a different aspect of a faculty member's employment workload. Follow the instructions below to complete the form:

### Page 1: Teaching Workload
*   **Teaching Workload** (`teaching_workload`, `number`, `required`): Report your current teaching workload, including the number of hours you spend in the classroom or online.
*   **Total Teaching Hours** (`total_teaching_hours`, `number`, `required`): Enter the total number of hours you spend teaching.
### Page 2: Research Workload
*   **Research Workload** (`research_workload`, `number`, `required`): Report your current research activities and time spent on research projects.
*   **Total Research Hours** (`total_research_hours`, `number`, `required`): Enter the total number of hours you spend on research projects.
### Page 3: Service Workload
*   **Service Workload** (`service_workload`, `number`, `required`): Report your service activities, such as committee work, community service, or other service-related work.
*   **Total Service Hours** (`total_service_hours`, `number`, `required`): Enter the total number of hours you spend on service activities.
### Page 4: Teaching Workload Duration
*   **Teaching Workload Duration** (`teaching_workload_duration`, `time`, `required`): Report the duration of your teaching workload in hours and minutes.
### Page 5: Research Workload Duration
*   **Research Workload Duration** (`research_duration`, `time`, `required`): Report the duration of your research workload in hours and minutes.
### Page 6: Service Workload Duration
*   **Service Workload Duration** (`service_duration`, `time`, `required`): Report the duration of your service workload in hours and minutes.
### Page 7: Total Teaching Hours
*   **Total Teaching Hours** (`total_teaching_hours`, `number`, `required`): Enter the total number of teaching hours.
### Page 8: Total Research Hours
*   **Total Research Hours** (`total_research_hours`, `number`, `required`): Enter the total number of research hours.
### Page 9: Total Service Hours
*   **Total Service Hours** (`total_service_hours`, `number`, `required`): Enter the total number of service hours.
### Page 10: Other Workload
*   **Other Workload** (`other_workload`, `select_one`, `required`): Select all that apply to your other workload categories, such as Academic Service or Administrative Work.
### Page 11: Other Workload Details
*   **Other Workload Details** (`other_workload_details`, `text`, `optional`): Provide a brief description of your other workload.
### Page 12: Academic Leader
*   **Academic Leader** (`academic_leader`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate whether you hold an academic leadership position, such as department chair or director.
### Page 13: Comments
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Provide any additional comments or information about your workload that may be relevant to the survey.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and honest when reporting your workload.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your department or university administration for guidance.
*   If you have any other workload categories not listed, please select "Other (please specify)" on the "Other Workload" page and provide a description on the "Other Workload Details" page.
