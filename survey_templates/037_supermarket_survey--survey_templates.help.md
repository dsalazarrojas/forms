# supermarket_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience at the supermarket, including their satisfaction levels with various aspects of the store and their purchasing habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the welcome message to familiarize yourself with the purpose of the form and the information it collects.
2. Provide your customer information, including any relevant details that you feel comfortable sharing.
3. Answer the questions about the products you purchased, such as whether you bought any items or not.
4. Rate your satisfaction levels with different aspects of the store, using the numbers provided.
5. Add any additional comments you have about your visit.
6. Finally, submit the form to share your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Welcome to the Supermarket Survey** (`1`, `note`, optional): A brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
- **Customer Info** (`2`, `text`, optional): This field is for customers to enter any relevant details they feel comfortable sharing about themselves.
- **Store Info** (`3`, `text`, optional): Please describe your experience at the store, including any notable interactions or events.
- **Products Purchased** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check which products you purchased during your visit.
- **Customer Preferences** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you are a frequent customer or not.
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`6`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the supermarket experience on a scale of 1-5.
- **Satisfaction with Service** (`7`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the service provided by the store staff.
- **Satisfaction with Value for Money** (`8`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the value for money you received from your purchases.
- **Satisfaction with Price** (`9`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the prices of the items you purchased.
- **Satisfaction with Quality of Items** (`10`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the quality of the items you purchased.
- **Satisfaction with Store Atmosphere** (`11`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the store atmosphere and environment.
- **Satisfaction with Staff Friendliness** (`12`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the friendliness of the store staff.
- **Satisfaction with Staff Service** (`13`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the service provided by the store staff.
- **Satisfaction with Parking and Facilities** (`14`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the store's parking and facilities.
- **Additional Comments** (`18`, `text`, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your visit.
- **Contact Info** (`19`, `text`, optional): Provide any contact information you feel comfortable sharing.
- **Email** (`20`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
- **Phone** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
- **Date** (`22`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your visit.
- **Time** (`23`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your visit.
- **Submit** (`24`, `text`, optional): Confirm that you've completed the form.
