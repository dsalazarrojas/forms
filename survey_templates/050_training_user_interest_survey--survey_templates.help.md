# Training User Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from users about their interest in training programs. It will help the training program creators understand your needs, preferences, and skills, which will guide them in designing a suitable training program for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply follow the questions on each page and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Each question is designed to gather specific information from you, so make sure to read the explanations below to understand each question's purpose.
3. Fill out the form in a way that is comfortable for you, and don't hesitate to ask for help if you need any.

* **What is your job title?** (`question_1`, `text`, optional): Please provide your job title or position in the organization.
* **What are you interested in learning?** (`question_2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one option that best describes your areas of interest or what you'd like to learn.
