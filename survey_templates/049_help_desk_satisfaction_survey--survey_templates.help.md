# help_desk_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the Help Desk service. It helps the company identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the questions and understanding what each one is asking.
2. Answer each question based on your honest feedback.
3. Be as specific as possible when answering the free-text questions.
4. Use the provided options for the multiple-choice questions.
5. Make sure to answer all the questions, as they are all optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel about the support service provided by our Help Desk?** (`question_1`, text, optional): This question asks about your overall feelings towards the support service provided by the Help Desk. Please answer this question based on your subjective experience.
* **How satisfied are you with the support service you received?** (`question_2`, select_one, optional): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the support service you received. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **What did we do wrong?** (`question_3`, text, optional): This question is an opportunity for you to provide specific feedback about any issues or problems you encountered with the Help Desk service. Be as detailed as possible.
* **How satisfied are you with the resolution process?** (`question_4`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the resolution process. Choose all the options that apply to your experience.
* **What did we do right?** (`question_5`, text, optional): This question is an opportunity for you to provide specific feedback about any good experiences or positive aspects of the Help Desk service. Be as detailed as possible.
* **How likely are you to recommend our company to a friend or family member?** (`question_6`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about your likelihood of recommending the company to others. Choose all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Have you used our Help Desk service previously?** (`question_7`, select_one, optional): This question asks about your past experience with the Help Desk service. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Do you have any suggestions or comments?** (`question_8`, text, optional): This question is an opportunity for you to provide any additional feedback, suggestions, or comments about the Help Desk service. Be as detailed as possible.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when answering the questions.
* Use specific examples or details when providing feedback.
* Make sure to answer all the questions, as they are all optional.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
