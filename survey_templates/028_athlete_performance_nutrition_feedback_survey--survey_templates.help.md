# Athlete Performance Nutrition Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather athlete's feedback on their current nutrition habits, experiences, and preferences. The information collected will be used to create a personalized nutrition plan tailored to your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into several sections, each designed to gather specific information about your nutrition habits and experiences. Please complete each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully to ensure that the nutrition team can provide you with accurate and effective guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Daily Nutrition Habits --**: This section asks about your daily nutrition habits, including how many full meals you consume and when you eat them.
* **Athlete Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Primary Sport**: Select the primary sport you participate in.
* **Nutritionist or Advisor Name**: If you have a nutritionist or advisor, please enter their name. If not, please leave this field blank.
* **How many full meals do you consume daily?**: Estimate the number of full meals you consume on a typical day.
* **Do you consistently eat a balanced breakfast?**: This question asks about your breakfast habits. Select how often you eat a balanced breakfast.
* **Typical Pre-Training Meal or Snack**: Describe what you usually eat before training.
* **Timing of Pre-Training Meal**: Select when you typically eat before training.
* **Typical Post-Training Meal or Snack**: Describe what you usually eat after training.
* **Timing of Post-Training Meal**: Select when you typically eat after training.
* **Estimated Daily Water Intake - Liters**: Enter your estimated daily water intake in liters.
* **Do you use sports drinks or electrolytes during training?**: Select how often you use sports drinks or electrolytes during training.
* **List any Supplements you currently take**: Describe any supplements you are currently taking.
* **Overall Satisfaction with your current Nutrition Plan**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your current nutrition plan.
* **Ease of Following the Nutrition Plan**: Rate how easy or difficult you find it to follow your current nutrition plan.
* **What is your main challenge in maintaining your diet?**: Select all that apply.
* **Do you feel your current nutrition supports your training energy levels?**: Select how you feel about your current nutrition plan's energy support.
* **Specific Nutrition Topic you want to learn more about**: Describe the specific nutrition topic you would like to learn more about.
* **Any additional comments or feedback for the nutrition team?**: Use this space to provide any additional comments or feedback for the nutrition team.
* **Date of Survey Completion**: Please enter the date you completed this survey.

Tips:
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly to help the nutrition team provide you with accurate and effective guidance.
* If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Please note that the information collected from this form will be used to create a personalized nutrition plan tailored to your needs and preferences.
* The date of survey completion is optional, but it helps the nutrition team track progress and changes over time.
