# pet_nutrition_awareness_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your pet's nutrition awareness. This form is designed to help you understand your pet's dietary habits, feeding habits, and overall health. It is used to assess your pet's nutrition awareness and provide personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them truthfully.
2. For each question, select the most accurate option from the provided choices.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions to ensure a complete assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page provides a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Do you feed your pet commercial food or home cooked meals?**
	+ This question asks about the type of food you feed your pet, which can impact their nutrition awareness.
	+ Commercial food: Pet owners who feed their pet commercial food are more likely to follow a standard nutritional guideline.
	+ Home cooked meals: Pet owners who cook meals for their pet have a better understanding of the ingredients and nutritional content.
	+ Both: Pet owners who feed a mix of commercial and home-cooked meals have a moderate understanding.
* **How many hours a day does your pet spend outside?**
	+ This question is relevant to the pet's activity level and potential exposure to external factors that can impact their nutrition awareness.
* **How often do you consult with a veterinarian?**
	+ Regular check-ups with a veterinarian provide valuable insights into the pet's health and nutrition status.
	+ Emergency visits: This option is likely due to a pet emergency, which may not reflect regular nutritional care.
	+ Never: This indicates a lack of interaction with a veterinarian, which can lead to poor pet nutrition awareness.
* **What is your pet's age?**
	+ Pet age can impact nutrition needs and requirements.
* **Do you have any pets that are pregnant or nursing?**
	+ This question is relevant to the pet's nutritional needs during pregnancy and lactation.
* **Do you have any pets that are on medication or supplements?**
	+ This question is relevant to the pet's overall health and nutrition status.
* **What type of food do you primarily feed your pet?**
	+ This question helps to understand the pet's food preferences and nutrient intake.
* **What is your pet's activity level?**
	+ This question helps to understand the pet's energy needs and potential impact on their nutrition awareness.
* **Do you give your pet treats?**
	+ Treats can add extra calories to a pet's diet, potentially leading to overfeeding and negative health effects.
* **How long do you give your pet treats for?**
	+ This question helps to understand the treat duration and potential impact on the pet's nutrition awareness.

## Tips
- Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- Make sure to answer all required questions to ensure a complete assessment.
- This form is designed to provide valuable insights into your pet's nutrition awareness.
