# Cultural And Entertainment Insights Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your cultural and entertainment habits, preferences, and demographics. This information will help us better understand your interests and needs, and improve our services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information about your preferences and habits.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by the required label).
3. Select multiple options for fields that allow multiple selections (e.g., Audience Demographics, Preferred Frequency, Preferred Method, Preferred Device, Preferred Platform, Preferred Browser, Preferred OS).
4. Enter any additional comments or information in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cultural Institutions Name** (`cultural_institutions_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to mention the name of any cultural institutions you are familiar with or have visited recently.
* **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to mention the name of event organizers you have interacted with or have been associated with.
* **Audience Demographics** (`audience_demographics`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the demographic options that apply to you (Male, Female, Other).
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_multiple, optional): Select your age group (18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65 or older).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This field is for any additional information or comments you would like to share.
* **Preferred Format** (`preferred_format`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred format (Online, Print, Both).
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, Mandarin).
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select your preferred time for our services.
* **Preferred Day** (`preferred_day`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred days (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Preferred Start Time** (`preferred_start_time`, time, optional): Select your preferred start time for our services.
* **Preferred End Time** (`preferred_end_time`, time, optional): Select your preferred end time for our services.
* **Cultural Habits** (`cultural_habits`, text, optional): This field is for you to mention any cultural habits you have or would like to develop.
* **Preferred Frequency** (`preferred_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you would like to receive our services (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Rarely).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any feedback or comments about our services.
* **Preferred Method** (`preferred_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred method of interaction (In person, Online, Mobile app, Phone call).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or information.
* **Preferred Device** (`preferred_device`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred device (Computer, Mobile, Tablet).
* **Preferred Platform** (`preferred_platform`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred platform (iOS, Android, Windows).
* **Preferred Browser** (`preferred_browser`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox).
* **Preferred OS** (`preferred_os`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred operating system (macOS, Windows, Linux).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information about your preferences or habits.

Note: The required fields are indicated by the "required" label in the field description. Please fill in all required fields accurately to ensure we can better understand your preferences and habits.
