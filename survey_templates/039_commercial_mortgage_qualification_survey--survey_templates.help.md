# Commercial Mortgage Qualification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commercial Mortgage Qualification Survey is a form used to gather information from brokers referring commercial mortgage clients to our organization. This information will be used to evaluate the client's eligibility for a commercial mortgage and to provide personalized support to the broker and client.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Referral/Broker Information**: If you are a broker, please provide your name in the "Name of Referring Broker" field. If you are not a broker, please leave this field blank.
2. **Borrower and Property Details**: Provide the name of the entity or individual borrower in the "Entity or Individual Borrower Name" field.
3. **Property Details**: Enter the subject property address in the "Subject Property Address" field and select the correct property type from the "Property Type" dropdown menu. You can choose from the following options:
	* Multi-Family
	* Office
	* Retail
	* Industrial/Warehouse
	* Mixed-Use
	* Hotel/Hospitality
	* Self-Storage
	* Other
4. **Property Status**: Select the current status of the property from the "Property Status" dropdown menu. You can choose from the following options:
	* Owner Occupied
	* Investment/Leased
	* Development/Construction
5. **Property Value and Loan Amount**: Enter the estimated current value of the property in the "Estimated Current Property Value" field and the desired loan amount in the "Desired Loan Amount" field.
6. **Transaction Type**: Select the type of transaction from the "Transaction Type" dropdown menu. You can choose from the following options:
	* Purchase
	* Refinance (Rate and Term)
	* Cash-Out Refinance
	* Bridge Loan
7. **Credit Score**: Estimate the borrower's credit score from the "Borrower Estimated Credit Score" dropdown menu. You can choose from the following options:
	* 740+ (Excellent)
	* 700-739 (Good)
	* 660-699 (Fair)
	* Below 660
8. **Contact Information**: Enter the primary contact's full name, email address, and phone number in the "Primary Contact Full Name", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" fields, respectively.
9. **Additional Loan Notes**: If you have any additional information about the loan or property, please provide it in the "Additional Loan or Property Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Referring Broker (If applicable)**: If you are a broker, enter your name in this field.
* **Entity or Individual Borrower Name**: Enter the name of the entity or individual borrower.
* **Subject Property Address**: Enter the address of the property being financed.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (e.g., Multi-Family, Office, etc.).
* **Property Status**: Select the current status of the property (e.g., Owner Occupied, Investment/Leased, etc.).
* **Estimated Current Property Value**: Enter the estimated current value of the property.
* **Desired Loan Amount**: Enter the amount of money you are seeking to borrow.
* **Transaction Type**: Select the type of transaction (e.g., Purchase, Refinance, etc.).
* **Borrower Estimated Credit Score**: Estimate the borrower's credit score.
* **Primary Contact Full Name**: Enter the full name of the primary contact.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact.
* **Additional Loan or Property Details**: Provide any additional information about the loan or property.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your broker or our organization for assistance.
* This form is intended to gather information and provide a starting point for the loan application process. Additional information may be required after the form is submitted.
