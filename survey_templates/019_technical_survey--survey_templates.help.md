# Technical Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your project, contact details, and technical skills. Your input will help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your project name in the "Project Name" field.
2. Provide your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Describe your technical requirements in the "Technical Requirements" field.
5. Select one or more programming languages you are familiar with from the "Software Development Skills" dropdown menu.
6. Indicate whether you have experience with "Programming Languages" by selecting "Yes" or "No".
7. Choose the operating system(s) you primarily use for development from the "Operating Systems" dropdown menu.
8. Describe your hardware requirements in the "Hardware Requirements" field.
9. Enter your project budget as a numerical value in the "Project Budget" field.
10. Select a date for your project deadline from the "Project Deadline" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Provide your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Technical Requirements** (`technical_requirements`, text, required: false): Describe any technical requirements or constraints for your project.
* **Software Development Skills** (`software_development_skills`, select_one, required: false): Select one or more programming languages you are familiar with (e.g., Python, Java, C++).
* **Programming Languages** (`programming_languages`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you have experience with programming languages (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Operating Systems** (`operating_systems`, select_one, required: false): Choose the operating system(s) you primarily use for development (e.g., Windows, Linux, macOS).
* **Hardware Requirements** (`hardware_requirements`, text, required: false): Describe any specific hardware requirements for your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, required: false): Enter your project budget as a numerical value.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, date, required: false): Select a date for your project deadline.
