# Chore Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the household management systems and experiences of users. It is meant to provide insight into how users manage their household chores, who does them, and what tools or systems they use to manage tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Household Information**: Provide general information about your living situation.
2. **Your Name**: Enter your name as the person completing the form.
3. **Type of Household**: Choose the type of household you live in (e.g., family with children, roommates, etc.).
4. **Number of Residents**: Enter the number of people living in your household.
5. **Number of Children**: If applicable, enter the number of children in your household.
6. **Current Chore System**: Describe how you currently manage your chores.
7. **Have a Current Chore System?**: Indicate if you have a current system or not.
8. **How Chores are Distributed**: Choose how chores are distributed in your household (e.g., assigned, rotating schedule, etc.).
9. **Satisfaction with Current System**: Rate your satisfaction with your current system.
10. **Chore Frequency**: Describe how often chores are done in your household.
11. **Daily Chores**: Select all tasks that are typically done daily.
12. **Weekly Chores**: Select all tasks that are typically done weekly.
13. **Monthly Chores**: Select all tasks that are typically done monthly.
14. **Fairness Assessment**: Assess how fair you feel the chore distribution is in your household.
15. **Who Does Most Chores**: Choose who does most of the chores in your household.
16. **Chore Burden**: Rate how much burden you feel from doing chores.
17. **Challenges**: Select all challenges your household faces with chore management.
18. **Main Chore Challenge**: Describe the main challenge with chore management in your household.
19. **Conflict Frequency**: Rate how often conflicts arise from chores.
20. **Desired Improvements**: Describe what would help improve your current system.
21. **Would you Like a Better System?**: Indicate if you would like a better system.
22. **Helpful Tools**: Select all tools that would be helpful for you.
23. **Will you Try a New System?**: Indicate if you would be willing to try a new system.
24. **Children Involvement**: If applicable, describe how children participate in household chores.
25. **Children Have Chores?**: Indicate if children have assigned chores.
26. **Children's Chores List**: Describe the chores children do if applicable.
27. **Reward System?**: Indicate if there is a reward system for children.
28. **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your home's cleanliness.
29. **Stress Level**: Rate how much stress chores cause you.
30. **Time Spent on Chores**: Choose how much time you spend on chores per week.
31. **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional thoughts or comments about your household's chore management.
32. **What Works Well**: Describe what works well in your household's chore management.
33. **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions for improving chore management.
34. **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your household's experience.
