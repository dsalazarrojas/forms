# Housing Investment Criteria Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Housing Investment Criteria Survey is designed to collect information from investors about their preferences, location, budget, and other relevant details. This survey is intended to be completed by individual investors, not organizations or other entities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
2. Enter your text responses in the text fields.
3. Ensure that you have provided accurate and honest information.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investor Preferences** (`investor_preferences`, `text`, optional):
	+ Please provide a brief description of your investment preferences, including any specific requirements or priorities you have for your investment.
* **Property Location** (`property_location`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all locations that are relevant to your investment interests.
* **Investment Objective** (`investment_objective`, `text`, optional):
	+ Describe your investment objective, including your financial goals and risk tolerance.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the minimum and maximum budget for your investment.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are interested in investing in real estate, 'No' otherwise.
* **Investor Experience** (`investor_experience`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all areas of experience relevant to your investment interests.
* **Investment Horizon** (`investment_horizon`, `time`, optional):
	+ Enter the time period you are interested in investing for.
* **Investor Expectations** (`investor_expectations`, `note`, optional):
	+ Please describe your expectations from the investment, including any specific requirements or priorities.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Property Price** (`property_price`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the price range of the property you are interested in.
* **Investment Status** (`investment_status`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all statuses that are relevant to your investment interests.
* **Property Size** (`property_size`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the size of the property you are interested in.
* **Investor Preferences 1** (`investor_preferences_1`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide any additional preferences or priorities you have for your investment.
* **Property Features** (`property_features`, `text`, optional):
	+ Describe any specific features you are interested in or require for your investment.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Investment Return** (`investment_return`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the expected return on your investment.
* **Investment Duration** (`investment_duration`, `text`, optional):
	+ Describe the duration of your investment.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Investment Location** (`investment_location`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the location of your investment.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your address.
* **Investment Type** (`investment_type`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are interested in investing in a specific type of investment, 'No' otherwise.
* **Investor Interest** (`investor_interest`, `text`, optional):
	+ Describe your investment interests.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your title or position.
* **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the amount of your investment.
