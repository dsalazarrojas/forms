# Community Listening Session Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback on the community listening session you attended. Your input will help us improve future sessions and ensure that they meet the needs and expectations of our community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing honest and detailed responses.
2. Select the options that best match your opinion or experience for each question.
3. Enter your name and contact information so we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of the Listening Session** (`session_date`): Please enter the date of the listening session you attended.
* **Listening Session Topic or Theme** (`session_topic`): Enter the topic or theme of the session, as it was presented to you.
* **Your Role as an Attendee** (`attendee_role`): Select the group you primarily represent, as listed:
	+ Resident / Individual Member
	+ Local Business Owner
	+ Student or Youth Member
	+ Local Government Official
	+ Community Advocate or Leader
* **Rate the Clarity of the Information Shared** (`information_clarity`): Rate how clear you found the information shared during the session, using the scale:
	+ 1 - Not Clear at All
	+ 2 - Somewhat Clear
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Very Clear
	+ 5 - Very Clear
* **Opportunity to Speak and Contribute** (`contribution_opportunity`): Select how you felt about the opportunity to speak and contribute, using the same scale as above.
* **Were your Concerns Heared?** (`concerns_heard`): Choose how well you felt your concerns were heard and understood:
	+ Yes, completely
	+ Partially
	+ No, not at all
* **Primary Takeaway** (`key_takeaway`): Enter the main thing you learned or observed during the session.
* **Change for Future Sessions** (`session_change`): If you have any suggestions for improvements, enter them here.
* **Overall Satisfaction with the Listening Session Format** (`overall_format_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with the format of the session, using the same scale as above.
* **Interested in Future Listening Sessions?** (`future_attendance_interest`): Select if you're interested in attending future listening sessions:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Best Way to Reach You** (`follow_up_method`): Choose the best way to contact you for session follow-up and notes:
	+ Email (Preferred)
	+ SMS Text Message
	+ Phone Call
	+ Postal Mail
* **Your Contact Details** (`respondent_contact`): Enter your email or phone number, as selected above.
