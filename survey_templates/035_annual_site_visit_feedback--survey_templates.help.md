# Annual Site Visit Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Annual Site Visit Feedback form, a tool designed to collect essential information on site visit experiences. Completing this form will help you provide valuable insights to site managers, enhancing their understanding of site performance and facilitating improvements.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions thoroughly, ensuring accurate and detailed responses.
2. For fields marked as required, please provide a full and complete answer.
3. For optional fields, feel free to provide any additional comments or insights you may have.
4. When selecting options for multiple-choice questions, choose the one that best reflects your experience or observation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Name** (`visitor_name`, text, required): Provide your full name.
* **Visitor Email** (`visitor_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Site Name** (`site_name`, text, required): Enter the site's name.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date you visited the site.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, optional): Provide the approximate time of visit.
* **Duration of Visit** (`visit_duration`, text, optional): Describe the length of your visit.
* **Site Manager or Contact** (`site_manager`, text, optional): Name of the site manager or contact person.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`visit_purpose`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options for the visit's purpose.
	+ "Routine inspection"
	+ "Compliance check"
	+ "Problem investigation"
	+ "Quality assurance"
	+ "New site evaluation"
	+ "Follow-up visit"
	+ "Other"
* **Overall Impression of the Site** (`overall_impression`, select_one, required): Evaluate the site's overall performance.
	+ "Excellent"
	+ "Good"
	+ "Satisfactory"
	+ "Poor"
	+ "Needs improvement"
* **Cleanliness and Organization** (`cleanliness`, select_one, required): Assess the site's cleanliness and organization.
	+ "Excellent"
	+ "Good"
	+ "Satisfactory"
	+ "Poor"
	+ "Unacceptable"
* **Safety Conditions Observed** (`safety`, select_one, required): Evaluate the site's safety conditions.
	+ "Excellent - no safety concerns"
	+ "Good - minor concerns only"
	+ "Satisfactory - some issues"
	+ "Poor - significant concerns"
	+ "Dangerous - immediate action needed"
* **Equipment Condition and Functionality** (`equipment_condition`, select_one, required): Examine the site's equipment condition and functionality.
	+ "Excellent"
	+ "Good"
	+ "Satisfactory"
	+ "Poor"
	+ "Non-functional"
* **Staff Presence and Engagement** (`staff_presence`, select_one, optional): Assess the site's staff presence and engagement.
	+ "Excellent"
	+ "Good"
	+ "Satisfactory"
	+ "Poor"
	+ "Inadequate staffing"
* **Records and Documentation** (`documentation`, select_one, optional): Evaluate the site's record-keeping and documentation practices.
	+ "Complete and organized"
	+ "Mostly complete"
	+ "Incomplete"
	+ "Missing critical records"
* **Compliance with Standards** (`compliance_status`, select_one, required): Assess the site's compliance with standards and policies.
	+ "Fully compliant"
	+ "Mostly compliant"
	+ "Partially compliant"
	+ "Non-compliant"
	+ "Critical violations"
* **Positive Observations** (`positive_observations`, text, optional): Share any positive observations or comments about the site visit.
* **Concerns or Issues Identified** (`concerns_identified`, text, optional): List any concerns or issues observed during the visit.
* **Safety Hazards Identified** (`hazards_found`, text, optional): Report any identified safety hazards or risks.
* **Recommendations for Improvement** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Suggest actions for the site to address identified issues.
* **Priority Level for Follow-up** (`priority_level`, select_one, required): Determine the urgency of follow-up actions.
	+ "Critical - immediate action"
	+ "High - within 30 days"
	+ "Medium - within 90 days"
	+ "Low - within 6 months"
	+ "None - no action needed"
* **Recommended Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Suggest a date for the next visit or follow-up.
* **Satisfaction with Site Visit** (`visitor_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Evaluate your overall satisfaction with the site visit.
	+ "Very satisfied"
	+ "Satisfied"
	+ "Neutral"
	+ "Dissatisfied"
	+ "Very dissatisfied"

**Tips**

* Be thorough and honest in your answers to provide valuable feedback.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to contact the site manager or contact person.
* Your feedback is instrumental in helping site managers improve site performance and address potential issues.
