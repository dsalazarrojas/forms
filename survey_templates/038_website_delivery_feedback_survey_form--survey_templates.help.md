# website_delivery_feedback_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with website deliveries. It helps us understand their satisfaction with the delivery process, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Choose the relevant options from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter your email address to help us contact you if we need further clarification.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you get here** (Intro Text, text, optional): This field is where you can describe how you received the delivery, such as how you ordered it or how you accessed the website.
* **Overall rating** (Rating, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the delivery experience, on a scale from 1-5. You can choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What was the quality of the delivery** (Feedback, select_one, optional): Choose the quality of the delivery from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need further clarification.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **How long did it take for delivery** (Delivery Time, select_one, optional): Choose the time it took for the delivery from the options: 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-6 hours, or More than 6 hours.
* **What was the delivery date** (Delivery Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the delivery.
* **What was the delivery time** (Delivery Time, time, optional): Enter the time of the delivery.
* **What type of delivery** (Delivery Type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of delivery from the options: Home delivery, Office delivery, or Other (please specify).
* **Who made the delivery** (Delivery Person, select_one, optional): Choose who made the delivery from the options: Customer, Driver, or Other (please specify).
* **What type of vehicle used for delivery** (Delivery Vehicle, select_one, optional): Choose the type of vehicle used for delivery from the options: Car, Van, or Other (please specify).
* **Where was the delivery made** (Delivery Location, select_one, optional): Choose the location where the delivery was made from the options: Home, Office, or Other (please specify).
* **How do you usually contact us** (Contact Method, select_one, optional): Choose how you usually contact us from the options: Phone call, Email, Online support, or Other (please specify).

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions to help us understand your experience better.
* If you have any further comments or questions, feel free to add them in the Comments field.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact us at [support email].
