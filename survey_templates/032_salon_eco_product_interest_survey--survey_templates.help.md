# salon_eco_product_interest_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The salon eco product interest survey is a form used to collect information from clients about their interest in eco-friendly products. This information will help the salon understand client preferences and improve their business strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. If you have any other notes or comments, enter them in the "Any other notes" field.
5. Select the date and time of your visit by entering it in the "Date of visit" and "Time of visit" fields.
6. For each of the "Eco-friendly product" fields (1-5), select "Yes" if you are interested in purchasing or using that product, and "No" otherwise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client interest in eco-friendly products**: This field is where you can enter your interest in eco-friendly products. Please type a brief description of your interest in such products.
* **Name**: Please enter your name to help us identify you as a client.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for any reason.
* **Any other notes**: If you have any other notes or comments, please enter them here.
* **Date of visit**: Enter the date of your last visit to the salon.
* **Time of visit**: Enter the time of your last visit to the salon.
* **Eco-friendly product 1**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in purchasing or using this product, and "No" otherwise.
* **Eco-friendly product 2**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in purchasing or using this product, and "No" otherwise.
* **Eco-friendly product 3**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in purchasing or using this product, and "No" otherwise.
* **Eco-friendly product 4**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in purchasing or using this product, and "No" otherwise.
* **Eco-friendly product 5**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in purchasing or using this product, and "No" otherwise.

## Tips
