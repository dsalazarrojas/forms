# Corporate Diversity Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees who have attended corporate diversity training sessions. It helps the organization understand how to improve future sessions, ensuring that they are effective and engaging for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions according to your honest and genuine feelings and opinions about the training.
2. For select-one and select-multiple questions, choose one option that best reflects your answer.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text field provided at the end.
