# Student Baptism Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students interested in being baptized to gather information about their contact details, faith background, and preferences for baptism.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your contact details: First name, Last name, Church name (if applicable), and Address.
2. Answer questions regarding your faith background, such as "What's your faith background?" and "How often do you attend church?"
3. Select your preferred date, time, and location for the baptism ceremony.
4. Choose your preferred baptism type and pastor for the ceremony.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes you have about your baptism.
6. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (id: 1): Provide your first and last name and church name if you have one.
* **What's your faith background?** (id: 2): Explain your faith background and how it relates to baptism.
* **When do you want to be baptized?** (id: 3): Select your preferred date for the baptism ceremony.
* **What time of day would you like to be baptized?** (id: 4): Choose your preferred time for the baptism ceremony.
* **Does your parent/guardian know you're interested in baptism?** (id: 5): Select whether your parent/guardian has been informed about your interest in baptism.
* **How often do you attend church?** (id: 6): Answer how frequently you attend church, if at all.
* **Do you attend a local youth group?** (id: 7): Answer whether you attend a local youth group or not.
* **When would you like to be contacted about baptism?** (id: 8): Select when you would like to be contacted about the baptism ceremony.
* **Would you like to schedule a meeting with a pastor to discuss baptism?** (id: 9): Answer whether you would like to schedule a meeting with a pastor to discuss baptism.
* **I consent to share this information with church staff.** (id: 10): Confirm whether you consent to share your information with church staff.
* **Which church would you like to schedule a baptism?** (id: 11): Select your preferred church for the baptism ceremony.
* **What is your church's address?** (id: 12): Provide your church's address.
* **Which pastor would you like to schedule a baptism?** (id: 13): Choose your preferred pastor for the baptism ceremony.
* **When would you like to be baptized?** (id: 14): Select your preferred date for the baptism ceremony.
* **What time would you like to be baptized?** (id: 15): Choose your preferred time for the baptism ceremony.
* **Where would you like to be baptized?** (id: 16): Provide the location for the baptism ceremony.
* **What type of baptism would you like?** (id: 17): Select the type of baptism you prefer, such as infant or immersion.
* **Father's name?** (id: 18): Enter your father's name, if applicable.
* **Mother's name?** (id: 19): Enter your mother's name, if applicable.
* **Guardian's name?** (id: 20): Enter your guardian's name, if applicable.
* **What is your address?** (id: 21): Enter your residential address.
* **What is your phone number?** (id: 22): Enter your phone number.
* **What is your email?** (id: 23): Enter your email.
* **Is there anything else we should know?** (id: 24): Provide any additional comments or notes about your baptism.
* **Additional comments?** (id: 25): Enter any additional comments about your baptism.

## Tips
* Please be honest when answering the questions.
* Make sure to include any necessary information to ensure a smooth baptism process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact church staff.
