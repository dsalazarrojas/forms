# Research Funding Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Research Funding Consent Form is used to obtain participant consent for a research study. It is a required document that must be filled out by every participant to ensure they understand their rights and responsibilities in the study.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant information form with your details.
2. Enter the study name and research institution where the study is being conducted.
3. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
4. Check the study title and ensure it accurately represents the research.
5. Give your explicit consent to participate in the study by selecting "Yes" on the participant consent field.
6. Fill out the date and sign the form as the participant.
7. As the investigator, sign and date the form to confirm you have obtained participant consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information Form**: Enter your personal details, including your name and any other relevant information.
* **Study Name**: Enter the name of the study you are participating in.
* **Research Institution**: Enter the name and address of the research institution where the study is being conducted.
* **Investigator Name**: Enter the name of the investigator or researcher responsible for the study.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the study.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Research Study Title**: Enter a brief description of the study you are participating in.
* **Participant Consent**: Select "Yes" to give your explicit consent to participate in the study.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date you gave your consent.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name as the participant to confirm you have read and understand the form.
* **Investigator Signature**: As the investigator, sign your name to confirm you have obtained participant consent.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date the participant and investigator signed the form.

## Tips

* Ensure you are filling out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Read and understand the participant information sheet before filling out this form.
* Give your explicit consent to participate in the study by selecting "Yes" on the participant consent field.
* Sign the form as the participant and investigator to confirm you have read and understand the form.
