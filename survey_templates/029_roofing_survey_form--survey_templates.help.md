# Roofing Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Roofing Survey Form is a comprehensive tool used by roofing contractors to gather essential information from customers about their roofing needs. This form ensures accurate and complete data collection, facilitating efficient project execution and improving customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with the customer's details, including their company name, contact number, and email.
2.  Select the appropriate job location (Job Site, Home, or Other).
3.  Describe the job details and roofing services required (Roofing Repair, Roofing Installation, or Other).
4.  Indicate the project type (New Construction, Re-roof, or Repair).
5.  Enter the job start and end dates, as well as the start and end times (if applicable).
6.  Include any job-specific notes and signatures from the customer and contractor.
7.  Enter the date and time the form was signed.
8.  Specify whether the customer has any goals for the project.
9.  Enter the roof size (if applicable).
10. Add any customer comments or additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's company name.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's contact number.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's email address.
*   **Job Location** (`job_location`, `select_one`, required): Select the job location (Job Site, Home, or Other).
*   **Job Details** (`job_details`, `text`, required): Describe the job specifics.
*   **Roofing Services** (`roofing_services`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the services required (Roofing Repair, Roofing Installation, or Other).
*   **Project Type** (`project_type`, `select_one`, required): Specify the project type (New Construction, Re-roof, or Repair).
*   **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the job start date.
*   **Job Start Time** (`job_start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the job start time (if applicable).
*   **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the job end date.
*   **Job Notes** (`job_notes`, `text`, required): Include any job-specific notes.
*   **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, `text`, required): Obtain the customer's signature.
*   **Contractor Signature** (`contractor_signature`, `text`, required): Obtain the contractor's signature.
*   **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date the form was signed.
*   **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, `time`, required): Enter the time the form was signed.
*   **Completed By** (`completed_by`, `text`, required): Specify who completed the job.
*   **Completed Date** (`completed_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the job was completed.
*   **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, `text`, required): Record any review notes.
*   **Review Rating** (`review_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the job's quality.
*   **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any customer comments.
*   **Goals** (`goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Specify whether the customer has any goals for the project (Yes or No).
*   **Roof Size** (`roof_size`, `number`, required): Enter the roof size (if applicable).
*   **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, `text`, required): Include any additional customer comments.

The Roofing Survey Form is a vital tool for gathering crucial information about customer's roofing needs. By following this guide, you'll ensure accurate and efficient data collection, leading to better project execution and enhanced customer satisfaction.
