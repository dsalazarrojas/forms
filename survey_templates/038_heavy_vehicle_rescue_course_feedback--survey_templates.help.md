# Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from course participants on their experience with the Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course. It will help training administrators evaluate the course's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Rate your overall experience with the course in the "course_rating" field.
3. Rate your experience with the rescue training provided in the "rescue_training_rating" field.
4. Rate your experience with the instructor in the "instructor_rating" field.
5. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the "improvement_notes" field.
6. If you would like to provide more information on the date and time you participated in the course, you can do so in the "date_of_participation" field and "time_of_participation" field.
7. If you would like to specify the location where the course took place, you can do so in the "location_of_participation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name (participant_name)** (`Text`, Required: no): Please fill in your name as a course participant.
- **Course Rating (course_rating)** (`Number`, Required: no): Rate your overall experience with the course (e.g., 1-5).
- **Rescue Training Rating (rescue_training_rating)** (`Number`, Required: no): Rate your experience with the rescue training provided (e.g., 1-5).
- **Instructor Rating (instructor_rating)** (`Number`, Required: no): Rate your experience with the instructor (e.g., 1-5).
- **Improvement Notes (improvement_notes)** (`Note`, Required: no): If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them here.
- **Date of Participation (date_of_participation)** (`Date`, Required: no): If you would like to provide more information on the date you participated in the course, please enter it here.
- **Time of Participation (time_of_participation)** (`Time`, Required: no): If you would like to provide more information on the time you participated in the course, please enter it here.
- **Location of Participation (location_of_participation)** (`Text`, Required: no): If you would like to specify the location where the course took place, please enter it here.
