# Catalyst Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey template is designed to help evaluators assess the performance of a catalyst in various applications. The form collects data on study details, project or study ID, catalyst code, and performance metrics such as efficiency, selectivity, and durability. It also includes sections on stability and cost considerations, as well as recommendations for pilot scaling.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the study details at the top of the page.
2. Enter the project or study ID and catalyst code as required.
3. Enter your name and date of evaluation.
4. Select the primary application area of the catalyst.
5. Evaluate the catalyst's performance metrics:
	* Conversion Efficiency: Rate from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
	* Reaction Selectivity: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, or Poor.
	* Catalyst Durability/Lifespan: Provide a text description of the catalyst's durability.
	* Optimal Operating Temperature Range: Enter the range in e.g. 200-400 C.
6. Evaluate the catalyst's stability and cost:
	* Thermal Stability: Highly Stable, Stable, Marginal, or Unstable.
	* Estimated Cost-Effectiveness: High, Moderate, or Low.
7. Compare this catalyst to the previous benchmark:
	* Provide a text description of the comparison.
8. Provide a recommendation for pilot scaling:
	* Highly Recommended, Recommended with modifications, or Not Recommended.
9. Add any additional technical observations:
	* Enter any additional technical details about the catalyst.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Study and Catalyst Details --**: This section is a brief summary of the study or project details. 
* **Project or Study ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the project or study. 
* **Catalyst Identification Code**: Enter the code for the catalyst being evaluated. 
* **Evaluator Name**: Enter your name and title. 
* **Date of Evaluation**: Enter the date of evaluation. 
* **Primary Application Area**: Select the primary application area of the catalyst. 
* **Conversion Efficiency Rating**: Evaluate the catalyst's conversion efficiency on a scale from 1 (Low) to 5 (High). 
* **Reaction Selectivity Rating**: Evaluate the catalyst's selectivity as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, or Poor. 
* **Catalyst Durability/Lifespan**: Provide a text description of the catalyst's durability. 
* **Optimal Operating Temperature Range**: Enter the optimal operating temperature range of the catalyst in e.g. 200-400 C. 
* **-- Stability and Cost --**: This section is a summary of the catalyst's stability and cost considerations. 
* **Thermal Stability**: Evaluate the catalyst's thermal stability as Highly Stable, Stable, Marginal, or Unstable. 
* **Estimated Cost-Effectiveness**: Evaluate the estimated cost-effectiveness of the catalyst as High, Moderate, or Low. 
* **Additional Technical Observations**: Enter any additional technical details about the catalyst. 
* **Comparison with Previous Benchmark**: Provide a text description of the comparison with the previous benchmark. 
* **Recommendation for Pilot Scaling**: Provide a recommendation for pilot scaling as Highly Recommended, Recommended with modifications, or Not Recommended. 
* **Additional Technical Observations**: Enter any additional technical details about the catalyst.
