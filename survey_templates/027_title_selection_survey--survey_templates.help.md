# Title Selection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Title Selection Survey" form is designed to collect information about your preferences regarding titles or names. Please fill out the form to provide your input and contribute to our efforts to improve our understanding of your title preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a title from the list provided in the "Select a Title" field.
2. If you have specific preferences regarding titles, indicate your preferences in the "What are your title preferences" field.
3. If this title is for a project or event, select all that apply from the "What is this title for" field.
4. If you have any additional comments, type them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Select a Title
* **Select a Title** (`select_a_title`, Select One, Required: No): Choose a title from the list of options to provide your selection.
### What are your title preferences?
* **What are your title preferences** (`title_preferences`, Number, Required: No): Type a number to indicate your preferences. For example, if you select 5, this means you prefer 5 options.
### What is this title for?
* **What is this title for** (`project_or_event`, Select Multiple, Required: No): Select all that apply to indicate the purpose of this title.
### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`notes`, Note, Required: No): Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about the title in this field.
