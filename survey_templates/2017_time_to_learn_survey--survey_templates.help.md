# 2017 Time To Learn Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback and opinions from users on the impact and effectiveness of the 2017 Time To Learn Survey. It will help us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Select your role in the context of the 2017 Time To Learn Survey.
4. Choose the date you submitted this response.
5. Answer questions about your experience with the survey, such as how satisfied you are and what aspects of it you found easy or confusing.
6. Select all the ways high-stakes testing has affected your classroom time, if applicable.
7. Choose all the consequences you've observed as a result of high-stakes testing, if applicable.
8. Decide if you consent to share your anonymized responses with partner organizations.
9. Optionally, provide suggestions on how to improve the 2017 Time To Learn Survey.
10. Optionally, provide any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name for our record-keeping purposes.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, Email, Required): Enter an email address we can use for follow-up, ensuring our responses remain anonymized.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, Phone, Optional): Provide your phone number with your country code for further communication, if needed.
* **Role** (`respondent_role`, Select One, Required): Choose the single best option that describes you in the context of this survey:
	+ Teacher
	+ Parent/Guardian
	+ Student
	+ Community member
	+ Union representative
	+ Other
* **Date of response** (`date_submitted`, Date, Required): Choose the date on which you completed this survey.
* **Years working in education** (`years_in_education`, Integer, Optional): If applicable, enter the number of years you've been working in education.
* **Hours per week on high-stakes testing prep** (`hours_per_week_testing`, Decimal, Optional): Enter an estimate of the average hours you spend per week on high-stakes testing preparation.
* **Satisfaction level** (`satisfaction`, Select One, Required): Rate your satisfaction level with the 2017 Time To Learn Survey:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, Integer, Required): Rate your overall experience with the 2017 Time To Learn Survey, from 1 (worst) to 10 (best).
* **Experience with the survey** (`experience_aspects`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all the aspects that applied to your experience with the 2017 Time To Learn Survey:
	+ Easy to use
	+ Fast
	+ Reliable
	+ Missing feature
	+ Confusing
	+ Well designed
* **Impact on classroom time** (`test_impact_areas`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all the ways high-stakes testing has affected your classroom time:
	+ Reduced instructional time for projects
	+ Less time for arts and music
	+ Increased test prep
	+ Longer school days
	+ Reduced formative assessment
	+ Increased homework required
* **Observed consequences** (`observed_consequences`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all the consequences you've observed as a result of high-stakes testing:
	+ Narrowed curriculum
	+ Student stress/anxiety
	+ Teaching to the test
	+ Lowered staff morale
	+ Reduced special education support
	+ Increased dropout risk
* **Consent to share** (`consent_share`, Select One, Required): Choose whether you consent to share your anonymized responses with partner organizations:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Improvement suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, Text, Optional): Provide specific suggestions on how to improve or change the 2017 Time To Learn Survey.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about the 2017 Time To Learn Survey.
