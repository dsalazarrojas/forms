# Company Car Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the company car program, specifically regarding the vehicle assigned to each employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question.
2. Answer all required fields. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Enter accurate and honest information for each field.
4. Once you have completed the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make and Model** (Vehicle Make and Model, text, \*): Enter the make and model of the company car you are currently using.
* **Vehicle Year** (Vehicle Year, number, \*): Enter the year of manufacture of the vehicle.
* **Average Monthly Mileage** (Average Monthly Mileage, number, \*): Enter approximate miles driven per month.
* **Overall Vehicle Condition** (Overall Vehicle Condition, select_one, \*): Rate the current mechanical and cosmetic condition of the vehicle.
	+ Excellent: The vehicle is in excellent condition with no issues.
	+ Good: The vehicle is in good condition but has minor issues.
	+ Fair: The vehicle has noticeable issues but is still operational.
	+ Poor: The vehicle is in poor condition with major issues.
* **How Satisfied Are You With the Fuel Card Process?** (How Satisfied Are You With the Fuel Card Process?, select_one, \*): Rate your satisfaction with the fuel card process.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are extremely satisfied with the fuel card process.
	+ Satisfied: You are satisfied with the fuel card process.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral or neither satisfied nor dissatisfied.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are dissatisfied with the fuel card process.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You are extremely dissatisfied with the fuel card process.
* **Maintenance Service Responsiveness** (Maintenance Service Responsiveness, select_one, \*): Rate the speed of maintenance and service handling.
	+ Very Fast: Maintenance and service are handled very quickly.
	+ Reasonable: Maintenance and service are handled reasonably quickly.
	+ Slow: Maintenance and service are handled slowly.
	+ Very Slow: Maintenance and service are handled very slowly.
	+ Not Applicable: This field does not apply to you.
* **Are the Safety Features in Your Vehicle Adequate for Your Role?** (Are the Safety Features in Your Vehicle Adequate for Your Role?, select_one, \*): Rate the adequacy of safety features in your vehicle.
	+ True: The safety features are adequate for your role.
	+ Mostly: The safety features are mostly adequate for your role.
	+ False: The safety features are not adequate for your role.
* **What Features Would You Like to See in Future Company Vehicles?** (What Features Would You Like to See in Future Company Vehicles?, text, ): If you have any features you would like to see in future company vehicles, enter them here.
* **Any Other Comments Regarding the Company Car Program?** (Any Other Comments Regarding the Company Car Program?, text, ): If you have any other comments or suggestions regarding the company car program, enter them here.
