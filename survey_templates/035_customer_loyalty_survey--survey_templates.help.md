# Customer Loyalty Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This help guide explains how to complete the customer loyalty survey. The survey aims to collect your opinions and feedback to help us understand your level of satisfaction with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly. The survey is designed to be easy and quick to complete.
2. For questions that require a numerical value, please enter a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is strongly disagree and 5 is strongly agree.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Loyalty Survey** (`customer_loyalty_survey`, text, not required): This is the title of the survey. Please do not fill this field.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our services, using the scale from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).
* **Customer Loyalty Survey Page 2** (`customer_loyalty_survey_page_2`, text, not required): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our services, using the scale from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, select_one, not required): Please select how likely you are to recommend our services to others.
	+ Strongly agree
	+ Somewhat agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat disagree
	+ Strongly disagree
* **Customer Loyalty Survey Page 3** (`customer_loyalty_survey_page_3`, text, not required): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Satisfaction Score** (`satisfaction_score`, number, not required): Rate your satisfaction score with our services, using the scale from 1 to 5.
* **Customer Loyalty Survey Page 4** (`customer_loyalty_survey_page_4`, text, not required): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Please enter your email address so we can get back to you.
* **Customer Loyalty Survey Page 5** (`customer_loyalty_survey_page_5`, text, not required): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Contact** (`contact`, note, not required): This is a note field for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Customer Loyalty Survey Page 6** (`customer_loyalty_survey_page_6`, text, not required): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have.

Note: All fields are optional, but please fill in as much information as possible to help us better understand your needs and preferences.
