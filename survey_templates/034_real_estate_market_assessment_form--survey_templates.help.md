# real_estate_market_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form

### How To Complete This Form
This form has several sections that will guide you through the process:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Property Type
• **What type of property** (`What type of property`, select_one, required): The type of property being assessed, e.g., House, Apartment, Commercial.

### Address
• **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property.

### Location
• **Location** (`location`, text, required): Provide the location of the property, e.g., City, State, Country.

### Market Condition
• **Market Condition** (`market_condition`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply: Yes, No.

### Property Age
• **Property Age** (`property_age`, number, required): Enter the age of the property in years.

### Property Status
• **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one, required): Active, Inactive.

### Property Price
• **Property Price** (`property_price`, number, required): Enter the current price of the property.

### Property Price Range
• **Property Price Range** (`property_price_range`, text, required): Describe the price range of the property, e.g., "Low to Medium".

### Property Area
• **Property Area** (`property_area`, text, required): Enter the area of the property, e.g., sq. ft..

### Assessment Date
• **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.

### Assessment Time
• **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the assessment.

### Seller Contact
• **Seller Contact** (`seller_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the seller.

### Buyer Contact
• **Buyer Contact** (`buyer_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the buyer.

### Investor Contact
• **Investor Contact** (`investor_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the investor.

### Other Contacts
• **Other Contacts** (`other_contacts`, text, required): Enter any other contacts related to the property.

### Notes
• **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the property.

### Email
• **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the property owner or representative.

### Phone
• **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the property owner or representative.

### Assessment Comments
• **Assessment Comments** (`assessment_comments`, note, required): Enter any comments about the property.

### Follow-up Actions
• **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, select_one, required): Yes, No.

### Follow-up Date
• **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required): Enter the date for follow-up actions.

### Follow-up Time
• **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, required): Enter the time for follow-up actions.

### Follow-up Status
• **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, required): Active, Inactive.

### Follow-up Notes
• **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, note, required): Enter any notes about the follow-up actions.

### Follow-up Contacts
• **Follow-up Contacts** (`follow_up_contacts`, text, required): Enter any follow-up contact information.

## Tips
* Please fill out each field with accurate and truthful information.
* Use this form for new property assessments only.
* Update existing assessments accordingly.
* If you have any questions, contact the relevant authority for assistance.
