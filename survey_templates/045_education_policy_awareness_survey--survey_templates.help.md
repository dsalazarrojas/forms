# Education Policy Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Policy Awareness Survey is a tool designed to gather insights from students, educators, and organizations about their understanding of educational policies. This form aims to collect information that will help organizations make informed decisions and improve their educational programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability. Please provide honest and accurate feedback.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each question.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Templates** (`survey_template`, `text`, **optional**): This field is for you to choose a specific survey template for the education policy awareness.
* **Education Policy Awareness Survey** (`education_policy_awareness_survey`, `text`, **optional**): This field is for providing a brief description of the education policy awareness survey.
* **Collects insights from students and educators about their understanding of educational policies** (`collects_insights`, `text`, **optional**): This field is for explaining how the survey will collect insights from students and educators about their understanding of educational policies.
* **Helping organizations make informed decisions** (`makes_informed_decisions`, `text`, **optional**): This field is for describing how the survey will help organizations make informed decisions.
* **Understanding of educational policies** (`understanding_of_policies`, `text`, **optional**): This field is for providing information about the understanding of educational policies.
* **Students** (`students`, `text`, **optional**): This field is for providing feedback about students' understanding of educational policies.
* **Educators** (`educators`, `text`, **optional**): This field is for providing feedback about educators' understanding of educational policies.
* **Organizations** (`organizations`, `text`, **optional**): This field is for providing feedback about organizations' understanding of educational policies.
* **Informed decisions** (`informed_decisions`, `text`, **optional**): This field is for describing how the survey will help organizations make informed decisions.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and honest feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form maintainer at form@gic.mx.
