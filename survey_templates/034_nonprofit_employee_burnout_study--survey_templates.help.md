# Nonprofit Employee Burnout Study - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on nonprofit employee burnout, including burnout levels, causes, symptoms, prevention strategies, and support systems. The goal is to help nonprofits understand burnout prevalence, causes, and prevention methods to create a supportive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form instructions carefully and complete each section accordingly.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your knowledge.
3. Use the options provided for each question, and select all that apply.
4. Be specific when providing answers, especially for text fields.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
