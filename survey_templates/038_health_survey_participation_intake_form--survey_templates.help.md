# Health Survey Participation Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about patient health survey participation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your **patientName** in the text box provided.
2.  Enter your **age** in the number field provided.
3.  Enter your **phoneNumber** in the text box provided.
4.  Enter your **email** in the format of [your email address]@example.com.
5.  Select your **surveyType** from the dropdown options provided.
6.  Type any additional **questions** in the text box provided.
7.  Add any additional **notes** about your health survey participation in the text box provided.
8.  Click the **submit** button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patientName** (Text, Optional)
	+ This field is where you should enter your name, as it will be displayed throughout the survey.
* **age** (Number, Optional)
	+ This field is where you should enter your age in numbers.
* **phoneNumber** (Text, Optional)
	+ This field is where you should enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **email** (Email, Optional)
	+ This field is where you should enter your email address in the format [your email address]@example.com.
* **surveyType** (Select, Required)
	+ This field is where you should select your survey type from the provided options (typeA or typeB).
* **questions** (Note, Optional)
	+ This field is where you can type any additional questions you may have or comments about your health survey participation.
* **notes** (Note, Optional)
	+ This field is where you can add any additional notes or comments about your health survey participation.
* **submit** (Text, Optional)
	+ This field is where you click to submit your form after completing all the above fields.

## Tips
* Ensure you have already filled out this form before attempting to complete it again. 
* Make sure you provide accurate and truthful information in the fields above. 
* If you have any issues with the form, contact the survey team.
