# Scientific Claim Verification Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scientific Claim Verification Submission Form is designed for users to submit their scientific claims for review and verification by experts. This form collects information about the claim, including user inputs, reviewer inputs, and verification details. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth verification process.

## How To Complete This Form

- Ensure you have all the required information before starting to fill out the form.
- Review each section carefully to understand what information is being requested.
- Enter your information accurately and clearly in the relevant fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User inputs**: Select one or multiple options from the given choices to describe your scientific claim. This section allows you to provide a brief overview of your claim.
  * Type: Select multiple options
  * Required: No
* **Reviewer inputs**: This field is for the reviewer to provide their comments and feedback on your claim. Enter your thoughts, observations, and any necessary details.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
* **Reviewer feedback**: Similar to the previous field, this is a text field for the reviewer to provide additional comments or feedback on your claim.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
* **Reviewer acceptance**: Indicate whether the reviewer accepts or rejects your claim. Select one of the two options provided.
  * Type: Select one option
  * Required: No
* **Submission ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your submission, if applicable.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
* **Claim status**: Describe the current status of your claim. This can include any relevant information about its progression or outcome.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
* **Evidence intake**: Provide any relevant evidence or supporting materials for your claim.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
* **Reviewer name**: Enter the name of the reviewer who evaluated your claim.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
* **Reviewer email**: Enter the email address of the reviewer who evaluated your claim.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
* **Reviewer title**: Provide the title or position of the reviewer.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
* **Review date**: Enter the date when the reviewer evaluated your claim.
  * Type: Date field
  * Required: No
* **Submission date**: Enter the date when you originally submitted your claim.
  * Type: Date field
  * Required: No
* **Reviewer signature**: Include the digital signature of the reviewer for verification purposes.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any section or field, consult the relevant guidelines or contact your designated representative.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields, if any, are completed correctly to avoid form rejection or delays in the verification process.
