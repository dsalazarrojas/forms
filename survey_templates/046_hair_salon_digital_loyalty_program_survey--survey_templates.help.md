# hair_salon_digital_loyalty_program_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by a hair salon to gather feedback from customers about their experiences and preferences. It helps the salon to understand how customers found out about them, what services they are satisfied with, and what rewards they are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information such as your email and phone number.
2. Choose how you heard about the salon.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the services you received.
4. Select the rewards you are interested in.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback you have about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **salon_name** (text): This field is used to record the name of the salon you visited.
* **email** (email): This is the email address you can be reached at.
* **phone** (text): This is your phone number.
* **how_did_you_hear** (select_one): This field is used to record how you found out about the salon, such as a friend, social media, or online search.
* **how_did_you_hear_other** (text): This field is used to provide additional information on how you heard about the salon.
* **satisfaction** (select_multiple): This field is used to rate your satisfaction with the services you received, such as "Very Satisfied", "Satisfied", "Neutral", "Dissatisfied", or "Very Dissatisfied".
* **desired_rewards** (select_multiple): This field is used to select the rewards you are interested in, such as discounts, free services, or exclusive offers.
* **comments** (text): This field is used to provide any additional feedback or comments about your visit.
* **consent** (select_one): This field is used to record your consent to receive future communications from the salon.

Tips:

* Make sure to fill in the required information such as your email and phone number.
* Be honest about how you heard about the salon.
* Choose your satisfaction rating and reward preferences honestly.
* Provide any additional feedback or comments about your visit to help the salon improve their services.
* Consent to receive future communications from the salon to stay up-to-date on their latest offers and promotions.
