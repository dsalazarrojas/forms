# Benefits Satisfaction Insight Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights on employee satisfaction with the benefits package offered by a company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the survey in the "Survey Date" field.
2. Provide your employee name in the "Employee Name" field (optional).
3. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field (optional).
4. Select your tenure with the company from the options provided in the "How Long Have You Been with the Company" field.
5. Select your overall satisfaction with the benefits package from the options provided in the "Overall Satisfaction with Benefits Package" field.
6. Select your satisfaction with each type of insurance (Health, Dental, Vision, Retirement, and Life) from the options provided.
7. Rate the quality of benefits communication and ease of enrollment process from the options provided.
8. Select your agreement with the statement "Benefits Provide Good Value for Cost" from the options provided.
9. Select your agreement with the statement "Benefits Are Competitive" from the options provided.
10. Select your agreement with the statement "Benefits Meet Your Personal Needs" from the options provided.
11. Select the most valued benefit from the options provided.
12. Select the least valued benefit from the options provided (if applicable).
13. Provide suggestions for missing benefits in the "What Benefits Are Missing" field.
14. Provide areas for improvement in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
15. Express your willingness to recommend the benefits to others in the "Would You Recommend These Benefits to Others" field.
16. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.
