# Cricket Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cricket Knowledge Quiz form is a tool to gather information from participants who have taken the cricket quiz. The data collected will help us understand who our participants are, their knowledge about cricket, and how they found out about the quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (although most fields on this form are optional).
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Cricket Participant Details**
* (`participant_details`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your name and other basic details about yourself. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **Cricket Knowledge Quiz Questions**
* (`questions`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide any additional questions or comments you may have about the quiz.

### 3. **Cricket Participant Score**
* (`participant_score`, number, required: false): Please enter the number of questions you answered correctly.

### 4. **How many of the questions did you answer correctly? 0 or 1**
* (`participant_score_details`, number, required: false): This field is similar to the previous one, but please enter your response as either 0 or 1.

### 5. **School or Club Name**
* (`school_or_club`, text, required: false): If you're representing a school or club, please enter its name here.

### 6. **How did you hear about us**
* (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, select_one, required: false): Please select how you found out about the quiz.

### 7. **Any comments**
* (`any_comments`, text, required: false): If you have any comments or suggestions about the quiz, please enter them here.

### 8-10 **Optional fields**
* **Participant Image**
* (`participant_image`, note, required: false): You can upload a picture of yourself, if you wish.
* **Participant Audio**
* (`participant_audio`, note, required: false): You can record a audio message, if you wish.
* **Participant Video**
* (`participant_video`, note, required: false): You can record a video message, if you wish.

## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all required fields.
* If you need help with any field, please refer to this guide or contact us.
* We appreciate your participation and feedback!
