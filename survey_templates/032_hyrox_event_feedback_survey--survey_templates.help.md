# hyrox_event_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hyrox_event_feedback_survey form is designed to collect feedback from event participants to improve future events at the specified location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. If you have a suggestion or additional comment, please type it in the corresponding field.
4. If you have any further questions, contact the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
  This question asks about your general feelings towards the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): Enter the name or location of the event venue.
  This field helps us understand where the event took place.
* **Location Quality** (`location_score`, number, required: false): Rate the quality of the event location on a scale of 1-5.
  This question is used to gauge the quality of the venue.
* **Event Organization** (`event_organization`, select_one, required: false): Rate the event organization on a scale from Excellent to Very poor.
  This question assesses how well the event was organized.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience at the event.
  This question gauges the overall quality of the event.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, required: false): Share any suggestions for improvement.
  Use this field to provide specific ideas for improving future events.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): Enter any other suggestions or ideas you have.
  This field is for any additional suggestions that didn't fit in the previous field.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Share any other comments or thoughts about the event.
  This field is for any other thoughts or comments about the event that didn't fit elsewhere.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for further discussions).
  If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your email address here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter any other contact information (if you'd like to be contacted).
  If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, you can enter any other contact information here.

## Tips
- Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
- If you have any further questions, contact the event organizers.
- Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback! Your input is invaluable in helping us improve future events.
