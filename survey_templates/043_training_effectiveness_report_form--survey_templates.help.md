# Training Effectiveness Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from trainees to help us understand the effectiveness of a training program. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve the quality and effectiveness of our training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete this form accurately and effectively:

* Fill out all mandatory fields as indicated by the `*` symbol.
* Enter your training program name in the `Training Program Name` field.
* Enter the date you completed the training in the `Completed Date` field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* Select your attendance status in the `Attended` field.
* Select the effectiveness of the training in the `Effectiveness` field.
* Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
* Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field (optional).
* Enter the number of training attendees in the `Attendance` field.
* Enter the name of the trainer in the `Trainer` field.
* Rate the training in the `Rating` field (1-10).
* If you wish to provide additional comments, you can do so in the `Comments` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Training Program Name
* **Training Program Name** (`training_program_name`, text, required): This field is where you enter the name of the training program you attended. Please type the exact name of the program as it was given to you.

### 2. Completed Date
* **Completed Date** (`date_completed`, date, required): This field requires you to enter the date you completed the training program. Please use the YYYY-MM-DD format for accurate representation.

### 3. Attended
* **Attended** (`training_attended`, select_multiple, required): Please select one of the options to indicate your attendance status. You can select multiple options if you have attended the training.

### 4. Effectiveness
* **Effectiveness** (`training_effectiveness`, select_one, required): This field helps us understand how effective the training program was for you. Please select one of the options provided.

### 5. Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have additional comments or feedback about the training program, you can enter them here.

### 6. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field requires your email address. Please ensure that you enter a valid email address.

### 7. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is not mandatory, but it's helpful if you have a phone number associated with your training attendance.

### 8. Attendance
* **Attendance** (`training_attendance`, number, required): Please enter the number of trainees who attended the training program.

### 9. Trainer
* **Trainer** (`trainer_name`, text, required): This field requires you to enter the name of the trainer who delivered the training program.

### 10. Rating
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Please rate the training program on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### 11. Completed By
* **Completed By** (`survey_completed`, time, optional): This field is not mandatory but if you complete the form at a specific time, this field helps us to track the completion time.

## Tips
* Ensure that you complete all mandatory fields.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to us for assistance.
