# International Women's Day Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "International Women's Day Survey" is a brief, user-friendly form designed to collect information about your occupation and work experience. It's intended to be completed by women in the workplace to help us better understand their experiences and perspectives. Please ensure that you're taking this survey in a relevant context and not duplicating your responses if you've already completed a similar survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "International Women's Day Survey" form.
2. Fill out each field as prompted, providing accurate and honest information about your occupation and work experience.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): This is your name, which will be used to personalize the survey results and maintain anonymity.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your work email address. This will help us contact you for follow-up or clarification.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required): Please describe your job title, profession, or field of work.
* **Years of Work** (`years_of_work`, number, required): The number of years you've been working in your current occupation.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, number, required): Your current salary range (in dollars). This will help us understand your work environment and compensation.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer honestly and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* If you've already completed a similar survey, please do not duplicate your responses.

Note: This form is designed to be completed anonymously, but if you'd like to be contacted about your responses, please let us know.
