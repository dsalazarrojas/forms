# Organizational Culture Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the organizational culture of your team or department. It will help us understand what works well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your honest feedback and answers.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your responses.
3. Select only one response for multiple-choice questions, and select all that apply for check-all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Information** (`company_info`, text, required: false)
	+ This is the first section of the form. Please provide a brief description of your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false)
	+ Please enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your department name.
* **Team** (`team`, text, required: false)
	+ Describe your team.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false)
	+ If applicable, please enter your manager's name.
* **Team Member Information** (`team_member_info`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide any additional information about your team members.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, text, required: false)
	+ On a scale of 1-10, how effectively do you think communication is within your team?
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide your thoughts on collaboration within the team.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, select_one, options: [Excellent, Sufficient, Insufficient], required: false)
	+ How effectively do you receive feedback in your team?
* **Trust** (`trust`, select_one, options: [Excellent, Sufficient, Insufficient], required: false)
	+ How much trust do you have in your team members?
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_multiple, options: [Highly Satisfied, Satisfied, Slightly Satisfied, Neutral, Slightly Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Highly Dissatisfied], required: false)
	+ Select all that apply to describe your level of satisfaction with your work environment.
* **Values** (`values`, select_multiple, options: [Respect, Fairness, Openness, Honesty, Flexibility, Innovation, Autonomy, Recognition, Empathy], required: false)
	+ Select all that apply to describe the values you hold important in your work environment.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, options: [Highly Satisfied, Satisfied, Slightly Satisfied, Neutral, Slightly Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Highly Dissatisfied], required: false)
	+ How satisfied are you with your work environment overall?
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, required: false)
	+ If you have any suggestions for improvement, please write them here.

Please note that the form is designed to gather honest and constructive feedback. All responses will be kept confidential and used to help improve the organizational culture within your team or department. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
