# Gym Facility Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from gym facility users about their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the fields as accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to enter your email address in the "email" field, as this is required for us to contact you.
3. Select the options that best describe your experience in each of the multiple-choice fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Rating** (`facility_rating`): Rate the overall cleanliness and maintenance of the facility. Scale: 1-5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Cleanliness Rating** (`cleanliness_rating`): Rate the cleanliness of the facility. Scale: 1-5, where 1 is very dirty and 5 is very clean.
* **Staff Rating** (`staff_rating`): Rate the helpfulness and friendliness of the staff. Scale: 1-5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Facilities** (`facilities`): Provide your feedback about the facilities provided by the gym, such as equipment, showers, etc.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates about our actions on your feedback.
* **Staff Helpfulness** (`staff_helpfulness`): Select the options that best describe how helpful the staff was during your visit.
