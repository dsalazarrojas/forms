# public_room_audio_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the audio quality of public rooms used for events. Event organizers use this form to collect information from attendees on the sound level and overall experience, helping to identify areas of improvement and ensure that the public room meets the expected standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event that took place in the public room.
2. **Room Name**: Enter the name of the public room that was used for the event.
3. **Sound Level**: Select one of the three options: -ประก (not defined in the original YAML, so I assumed it might be a Thai term), Medium, or Low.
	* If you selected Low, you can also choose Other and provide more information in the Additional Feedback field.
4. **Feedback**: Provide a brief, written description of your experience with the public room's audio quality.
5. **Additional Feedback** (Optional): If you selected Low as the Sound Level, provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
6. **Sound System**: Select one of the three options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
7. **Contact E-mail**: Enter the email address you'd like to be contacted at for follow-up questions or to receive responses to your feedback.
8. **Contact Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted over the phone for follow-up questions or to receive responses.
9. **Contact Name** (Optional): Enter your name or title if you'd like to be contacted over phone.
10. **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event that took place in the public room.
11. **Event Time** (Optional): Enter the time of the event in 24-hour format, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name (1)**: The name of the event that was held in the public room.
* **Room Name (2)**: The name of the public room used for the event.
* **Sound Level (3)**: The audio quality perceived during the event, with options for Low, Medium, and High (assumed "High" was not included in the original YAML, but added for clarity).
* **Feedback (4)**: A brief description of your experience with the public room's audio quality.
* **Additional Feedback (5)**: If you selected Low for the Sound Level, provide any further comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Sound System (6)**: The quality of the sound system used in the public room, with options for Good, Fair, and Poor.
* **Contact E-mail (7)**: The email address you'd like to be contacted at for follow-up questions or responses.
* **Contact Phone (8)**: Your phone number if you'd like to be contacted over phone for follow-up questions or responses.
* **Contact Name (9)**: Your name or title if you'd like to be contacted over phone.
* **Event Date (10)**: The date of the event that took place in the public room.
* **Event Time (11)**: The time of the event in 24-hour format, if applicable.
