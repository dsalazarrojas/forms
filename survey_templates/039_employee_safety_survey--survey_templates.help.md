# Employee Safety Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about employee safety concerns, experiences, and suggestions for improvement. It's essential to provide feedback on workplace safety, report incidents, and share ideas on how to enhance safety procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly, and remember that all responses are anonymous.
2. Read each question carefully, and choose the options that best fit your experiences and opinions.
3. You can select multiple options for questions that allow it (indicated by "select_multiple" type).
4. For open-ended questions (indicated by "text" type), provide as much detail as you like.
5. When finished, review your answers for completeness and accuracy.
