# packaging_design_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "packaging_design_feedback_questionnaire" is a form designed to gather feedback and suggestions from stakeholders on the design quality and overall experience of a product's packaging.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide an overall rating of the packaging design on the "overall_rating" page.
2. Enter the name of the product on the "product_name" page.
3. Select the type of packaging used for the product on the "packaging_type" page.
4. Provide any feedback on the packaging design on the "feedback" page.
5. Suggest any improvements or changes on the "suggestion" page.
6. If you have any attachments that support your feedback, please upload them on the "attachment" page.
7. Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, and website, on the "email", "phone", and "website" pages.
8. Specify if you want to be contacted for follow-up on the "follow_up" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating (rating_scale)**: This is a scale to rate the overall quality of the packaging design, with options to choose from (e.g., 1-5 stars).
* **Product Name (product_name)**: Please enter the name of the product being reviewed.
* **Packaging Type (packaging_type)**: Select the type of packaging used for the product from the options provided.
* **Feedback (feedback)**: Provide any specific feedback or comments on the packaging design.
* **Suggestion (suggestion)**: Suggest any improvements or changes to the packaging design.
* **Attachment (attachment)**: If you have any supporting documents or images, please upload them here.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Website (website)**: Enter your website URL for contact purposes.
* **Social Media (social_media)**: Enter your social media handles for contact purposes.
* **Social Media URL (social_media_url)**: Enter the URL for your social media profile.
* **Follow-up (follow_up)**: Specify if you want to be contacted for follow-up on this feedback.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback and suggestions.
* Make sure to attach any relevant documents or images to support your feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrator.
