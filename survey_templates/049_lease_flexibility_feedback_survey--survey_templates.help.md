# lease_flexibility_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the lease flexibility offered by the organization. It gathers information on various aspects of the lease, such as flexibility in terms, frequency, and duration, to help improve the services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields (marked 'required') to the best of your ability.
2. For select fields, select one or multiple options from the pre-defined list.
3. Be honest and accurate when providing feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **lease_flexibility_survey** (`1`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide general feedback on the lease flexibility.
* **lease_flexibility_questions** (`2`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options to indicate which questions or topics you would like to have more flexibility in the lease.
* **lease_flexibility_terms** (`3`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional feedback or comments on the lease terms.
* **lease_flexibility_suggestions** (`4`, text, optional): This field is for suggesting improvements or changes to the lease flexibility.
* **lease_flexibility_frequency** (`5`, select_one, required): Select one option to indicate how often you would like to have flexibility in the lease.
* **lease_flexibility_duration** (`6`, number, optional): This field is for indicating how long you would like to have flexibility in the lease.
* **lease_flexibility_start_date** (`7`, date, required): Enter the date when the lease flexibility starts.
* **lease_flexibility_end_date** (`8`, date, required): Enter the date when the lease flexibility ends.
* **lease_flexibility_start_time** (`9`, time, required): Enter the start time of the lease flexibility.
* **lease_flexibility_end_time** (`10`, time, required): Enter the end time of the lease flexibility.
* **prophets** (`11`, text, optional): This field is not clearly defined in the form, so please provide any feedback or comments you may have.
* **lease_flexibility_phone** (`12`, email, optional): This is not a standard phone field, but it is possible that it is meant to be an email field instead.
* **lease_flexibility_email** (`13`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **lease_flexibility_address** (`14`, text, optional): This field is for providing your physical address, if you feel it's necessary.
* **lease_flexibility_city** (`15`, text, required): Enter the city where you are located.
* **lease_flexibility_state** (`16`, select_one, required): Select one option for your state/region.
* **lease_flexibility_zip** (`17`, number, required): Enter your postal code.
* **lease_flexibility_lease_type** (`18`, select_one, required): Select one option for the type of lease you have.
* **lease_flexibility_flexibility** (`19`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options to indicate which aspects of the lease you would like to have more flexibility in.
* **lease_flexibility_other_terms** (`20`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional feedback or comments on the lease terms.
* **lease_flexibility_other_suggestions** (`21`, text, optional): This field is for suggesting improvements or changes to the lease flexibility.
* **lease_flexibility_flexibility_terms** (`22`, text, optional): This field is for providing feedback or comments on the lease flexibility terms.
* **lease_flexibility_flexibility_other_terms** (`23`, text, optional): This field is not clearly defined in the form, so please provide any feedback or comments you may have.
* **lease_flexibility_flexibility_other_suggestions** (`24`, text, optional): This field is not clearly defined in the form, so please provide any feedback or comments you may have.
* **lease_flexibility_flexibility_other** (`25`, text, optional): This field is not clearly defined in the form, so please provide any feedback or comments you may have.
