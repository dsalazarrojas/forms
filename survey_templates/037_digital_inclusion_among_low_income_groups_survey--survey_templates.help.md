# Digital Inclusion Among Low Income Groups Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the "Digital Inclusion Among Low Income Groups Survey". This survey is part of a research project aiming to understand the barriers and challenges faced by low-income groups in accessing digital technologies. Your input will help us better understand your experiences and provide valuable insights for creating more inclusive digital environments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your household's situation.
2. Select the correct answers from the provided options, or type in your response if the option "Other (please specify)" is available.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the "Additional comments" and "Comments" fields.
4. If you're unsure or need help, please contact the research team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Page 1)
* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the survey and its purpose.
* This page is a starting point for the survey, and we encourage you to read on to understand the questions and their relevance.

### What is the monthly household income? (Page 2)
* **What is the monthly household income?**: Please report the average monthly income of your household. This will help us understand how income affects digital inclusion.
* Please enter a numerical value for the monthly household income.

### How many people live in your household?
* **How many people live in your household?**: Tell us how many individuals are part of your household. This will help us understand household dynamics.
* Please enter a numerical value for the number of people living in your household.

### Which devices do you own? (Page 4)
* **Which of the following devices do you own?**: Choose all the devices you or your household members own or have access to.
* Select one or more of the following:
	+ Laptop
	+ Desktop
	+ Mobile phone
	+ Tablet
	+ Smart TV

### How do you access the internet? (Page 5)
* **How do you access the internet?**: Choose all the methods you use to access the internet. This will help us understand your internet connectivity options.
* Select one or more of the following:
	+ Mobile phone data
	+ Wi-Fi (home or public)
	+ Wi-Fi (school or work)
	+ Other (please specify)

### How often do you experience internet issues? (Page 6)
* **How often do you experience internet issues?**: Choose the frequency of internet issues you face.
* Select one of the following:
	+ Never
	+ Occasionally
	+ Often
	+ Almost always

### What are the primary barriers to digital inclusion? (Page 7)
* **What are the primary barriers to digital inclusion?**: Choose all the challenges you face in accessing digital technologies.
* Select one or more of the following:
	+ Cost
	+ Language
	+ Lack of skills
	+ Digital literacy
	+ Other (please specify)

### Comments (Page 8)
* **Comments**: Share any additional thoughts, experiences, or suggestions you have about digital inclusion among low-income groups.
* Please enter any information you think will help us better understand the challenges you face.

### Additional comments (Page 9)
* **Additional comments**: If you have any other information you'd like to share, please enter it here.
* This is an opportunity to provide any further insights or details that didn't fit into previous questions.
