# Primary Care Speaker Presentation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Primary Care Speaker Presentation Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from attendees after a presentation, focusing on the speaker's performance and the session's overall rating. This feedback will help us to improve our speakers' skills and tailor future presentations to better suit our audience's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Session Details**: Enter a brief description of the session you attended, including the topic(s) covered and any notable aspects.
2.  **Session Rating**: Choose how you would rate the session overall, using one of the provided options (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, or Unsatisfactory).
3.  **Speaker Performance**: Provide any additional comments about the speaker's performance, such as their ability to communicate complex ideas, engage the audience, etc.
4.  **Session Topics**: Select all the topics that were covered during the session.
5.  **Speaker Experience**: If applicable, provide the speaker's level of experience with the topic, using a numerical value (e.g., 1-10 years).
6.  **Speaker Rating**: Choose how you would rate the speaker's performance, using one of the provided options (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Unsatisfactory).
7.  **Session Notes**: Add any additional comments or observations about the session, such as suggestions for improvement or areas of focus for future presentations.

*   **Session Details** (Required: No, Type: Text): Enter a brief description of the session you attended, including the topic(s) covered and any notable aspects.

*   **Speaker Performance** (Required: No, Type: Note): Provide any additional comments about the speaker's performance, such as their ability to communicate complex ideas, engage the audience, etc.

*   **Speaker Experience** (Required: No, Type: Number): If applicable, provide the speaker's level of experience with the topic, using a numerical value (e.g., 1-10 years).
