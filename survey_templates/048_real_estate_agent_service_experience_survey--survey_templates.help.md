# Real Estate Agent Service Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our real estate agent services. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question according to your genuine experience with our real estate agent.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Enter your contact details, including your email address and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate your overall experience with our real estate agent? (id: 1)**
	+ This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with our real estate agent services.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **How would you rate the communication from our real estate agent? (id: 2)**
	+ This question evaluates the quality of communication from our real estate agent.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **How responsive was your real estate agent? (id: 3)**
	+ This question assesses the agent's responsiveness to your needs and concerns.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Responsive" and 5 is "Very Responsive".
* **Did our real estate agent follow up on our offer? (id: 4)**
	+ This question checks if the agent followed up on our offer as expected.
	+ Select one of the options: "Not Satisfied", "Satisfied", or "Very Satisfied".
* **Did our real estate agent help with negotiations? (id: 5)**
	+ This question evaluates the agent's ability to facilitate successful negotiations.
	+ Select one or more of the options: "Not Satisfied", "Satisfied", or "Very Satisfied".
* **How knowledgeable was your real estate agent? (id: 6)**
	+ This question assesses the agent's knowledge about the subject matter.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Knowledgeable" and 5 is "Very Knowledgeable".
* **How professional was your real estate agent? (id: 7)**
	+ This question evaluates the agent's professionalism in handling your case.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Professional" and 5 is "Very Professional".
* **How satisfied are you with the overall service? (id: 8)**
	+ This question gauges your overall satisfaction with the service provided.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Is there anything else you'd like to share with us? (id: 9)**
	+ This field is for any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
	+ Write your thoughts and suggestions in this space.
* **What are your contact details? (id: 10)**
	+ Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.
	+ Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **What is your email address? (id: 11)**
	+ Enter your email address.
* **What is your phone number? (id: 12)**
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Should we follow up with you via email? (id: 13)**
	+ Select one of the options: "Not Now", "True", or "Later".
	+ This question helps us know when to follow up with you via email.
* **Should we follow up with you via phone? (id: 14)**
	+ Select one of the options: "Never", "Only Sometimes", or "True".
	+ This question helps us know when to follow up with you via phone.
