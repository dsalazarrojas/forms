# Building and Space Generation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive survey to gather information about your experience and satisfaction with your working space.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions as honestly as possible.
2. Select the appropriate response for each question from the provided options.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Please enter your department or team name.
* **Your Role** (`role`, text, required): Please enter your job title or position.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your official email address.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): The date you are completing this survey.
* **Which Building** (`building_name`, text, required): The name of the building you work in.
* **Floor Level** (`floor_level`, text, required): The floor where you work on.
* **Workspace Type** (`workspace_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of workspace you occupy.
	+ Private Office
	+ Shared Office
	+ Cubicle
	+ Open Desk
	+ Hot Desk
	+ Laboratory
	+ Workshop
* **Space Satisfaction** (`space_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with your space.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Space Size Adequacy** (`space_size`, select_one, required): Please rate if your space is the right size.
	+ Too Large
	+ Slightly Large
	+ Just Right
	+ Slightly Small
	+ Too Small
* **Lighting Quality** (`lighting_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of lighting in your space.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Temperature Comfort** (`temperature_comfort`, select_one, required): Rate the temperature control in your space.
	+ Very Comfortable
	+ Comfortable
	+ Neutral
	+ Uncomfortable
	+ Very Uncomfortable
* **Ventilation Quality** (`ventilation_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the air quality in your space.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Noise Level** (`noise_level`, select_one, required): Rate the noise level in your space.
	+ Very Quiet
	+ Quiet
	+ Moderate
	+ Noisy
	+ Very Noisy
* **Privacy Level** (`privacy_level`, select_one, required): Rate your privacy level in your space.
	+ Very Private
	+ Private
	+ Moderate
	+ Limited Privacy
	+ No Privacy
* **Storage Adequacy** (`storage_adequacy`, select_one, required): Rate if there is enough storage in your space.
	+ More Than Enough
	+ Adequate
	+ Somewhat Limited
	+ Very Limited
	+ No Storage
* **Collaboration Spaces** (`collaboration_spaces`, select_one, required): Rate the availability and quality of collaboration spaces.
	+ More Than Enough
	+ Adequate
	+ Somewhat Limited
	+ Very Limited
	+ None Available
* **Common Areas Quality** (`common_areas`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of common areas (e.g., meeting rooms, break rooms).
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
	+ Not Available
* **Technology Infrastructure** (`technology_infrastructure`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of technology infrastructure (e.g., IT, connectivity).
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Accessibility Rating** (`accessibility_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the ease of access to your space.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Most Used Spaces** (`most_used_spaces`, select_multiple, required): Select the spaces you use most.
	+ My Workspace
	+ Meeting Rooms
	+ Break Room
	+ Collaboration Areas
	+ Library/Quiet Zone
	+ Outdoor Spaces
	+ Reception Areas
	+ Other
* **Underutilized Spaces** (`underutilized_spaces`, text, optional): If you have any underutilized spaces, please describe them.
* **Improvement Priorities** (`improvement_priorities`, select_multiple, required): Select the aspects of your space that need improvement.
	+ More Private Spaces
	+ Better Lighting
	+ Temperature Control
	+ More Meeting Rooms
	+ Better Technology
	+ Improved Break Areas
	+ More Storage
	+ Better Accessibility
	+ Enhanced Aesthetics
	+ Outdoor Spaces
* **Future Space Needs** (`future_needs`, text, optional): If you anticipate any changes to your space, please describe them.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Please provide any other feedback or comments about your space.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Select the correct option for each question based on your personal experience.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
