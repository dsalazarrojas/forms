# Audit Checklist Review Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the audit checklist used in the field, helping us improve its effectiveness and usability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each section carefully and provide thorough explanations where necessary.
3. Answer all questions truthfully and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name and title or position.
* **Audit Title/Reference** (`audit_title`, text, required): Please enter the title of the audit and its corresponding reference number.
* **Checklist Name/Version** (`checklist_version`, text, required): Please enter the name and version of the checklist you used.
* **Ease of Use of the Checklist** (`ease_of_use`, select_one, required): Evaluate the ease of navigation and completion of the checklist, rating it as:
	+ Extremely Easy
	+ Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Difficult
* **Clarity of Checklist Items/Questions** (`clarity_of_items`, select_one, required): Evaluate the clarity of the checklist items, rating it as:
	+ Very Clear
	+ Mostly Clear
	+ Somewhat Ambiguous
	+ Unclear
* **Approximate time spent using this checklist (Hours)** (`time_to_complete`, number, optional): If you have an idea of the time spent on the checklist, enter it here.
* **Are all items in the checklist relevant?** (`relevance_of_items`, select_one, required): Evaluate the relevance of the checklist items, rating it as:
	+ All Relevant
	+ Mostly Relevant
	+ Many Irrelevant Items
* **Was the checklist comprehensive?** (`comprehensive_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the comprehensiveness of the checklist, rating it as:
	+ Yes, very thorough
	+ Mostly, but missed minor points
	+ No, significant gaps
* **What items or sections should be added?** (`missing_items`, text, optional): If there are any items or sections that should be added to the checklist, describe them here.
* **Which items were most confusing or difficult to verify?** (`confusing_items`, text, optional): If there are any items that were confusing or difficult to verify, describe them here.
* **Overall Rating of the Audit Checklist** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the overall rating of the audit checklist, rating it as:
	+ 5 - Excellent
	+ 4 - Good
	+ 3 - Average
	+ 2 - Fair
	+ 1 - Poor
* **Date of Review** (`review_date`, date, required): Please enter the date of the review.

## Tips
1. Please take your time and fill out the form carefully.
2. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
3. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input.
