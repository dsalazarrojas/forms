# School Pastoral Care Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Pastoral Care Survey is a tool to help schools gather feedback from students, parents, and staff on their pastoral care experiences. This feedback is essential for schools to understand how well they are supporting their students' overall well-being and providing a nurturing environment. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, please ensure you have access to the survey form. If you are a student, you should have received a link to the survey via email or be given a paper copy from your teacher/parent/teacher. If you are a parent or staff member, please contact your school to obtain access.
2.  Select the relevant section based on your role in the school. If you are a student, please fill out the sections on student feedback and wellbeing support. If you are a parent, fill out the sections on parent feedback and wellbeing support. If you are a staff member, fill out the sections on staff feedback and wellbeing support.
3.  Answer all the questions based on your honest opinion, providing as much detail as you can in each section.
4.  Ensure you fill out all mandatory fields before submitting the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`): This section is for students to provide their personal feedback on their experience with the school.
* **Parent Feedback** (`parent_feedback`): This section is for parents to share their thoughts about their child's experience at school.
* **Staff Feedback** (`staff_feedback`): This section is for staff members to share their thoughts on how well the school is supporting students' well-being.
* **Wellbeing Support** (`wellbeing_support`): This section is to gather information on the types of support provided by the school to students.
* **Student Feedback Frequency** (`student_feedback_frequency`): This section is to determine how often students receive feedback from the school.
* **Staff Feedback Frequency** (`staff_feedback_frequency`): This section is to determine how often staff members receive feedback from the school.
* **Parent Feedback Frequency** (`parent_feedback_frequency`): This section is to determine how often parents receive feedback from the school.
* **Student Support Frequency** (`student_support_frequency`): This section is to gather information on how often students receive support from the school.
* **Staff Feedback Frequency (2)** (`staff_feedback_frequency`): This section is to determine how often staff members receive feedback from the school and colleagues.
* **Date of Feedback** (`date_of_feedback`): This section is for you to select the date when you last received feedback from the school.
* **Time of Feedback** (`time_of_feedback`): This section is for you to select the time when you last received feedback from the school.
* **Email** (`email`): This is the contact email where the school can reach out to you if needed. 
* **Phone** (`phone`): This is the contact phone number where the school can reach out to you if needed.
* **Note** (`note`): This is an optional section for you to provide any additional comments.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): This is the name of the school where you are providing feedback.
* **Department** (`department`): This is the department where you are providing feedback from.
* **Student Feedback Frequency Frequency** (`student_feedback_frequency_frequency`): This section is to gather information on how often students receive feedback from the school on their feedback frequency.
* **Parent Feedback Frequency Frequency** (`parent_feedback_frequency_frequency`): This section is to gather information on how often parents receive feedback from the school on their feedback frequency.
* **Staff Feedback Frequency Frequency** (`staff_feedback_frequency_frequency`): This section is to gather information on how often staff members receive feedback from the school on their feedback frequency.
* **Wellbeing Support Frequency** (`wellbeing_support_frequency`): This section is to gather information on how often students receive wellbeing support from the school.
* **School Pastoral Care Score** (`school_pastoral_care_score`): This is a text field where you rate the school's pastoral care. 

Please note that some fields are marked as required, meaning that you must fill them out to complete the form.
