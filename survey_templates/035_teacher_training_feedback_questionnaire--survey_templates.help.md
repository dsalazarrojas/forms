# Teacher Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Training Feedback Questionnaire is a survey designed to gather feedback from teachers on their training sessions. This feedback is used to evaluate the effectiveness of our training programs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Teacher Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the training program you attended in the "Training Program" field.
3. Select the date of the training session you attended in the "Session Date" field.
4. Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the session in the "Trainer Name" field.
5. Specify the length of the session in the "Session Length" field.
6. Rate your overall experience in the "Rating" field.
7. Provide feedback on your experience in the "Feedback" field.
8. Select how you would rate the training evaluations (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor) in the "Training Evaluations" field.
9. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name**: Enter your name so we can identify who provided the feedback.
* **Training Program**: Enter the name of the training program you attended, such as "Introduction to Teaching Methodologies" or "Effective Lesson Planning".
* **Session Date**: Select the date of the training session you attended, using the date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the session.
* **Session Length**: Specify the length of the session, e.g., "half-day" or "2 hours".
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback**: Provide feedback on your experience, e.g., what you liked, what you didn't like, or suggestions for improvement.
* **Training Evaluations**: Select how you would rate the training evaluations, using the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
