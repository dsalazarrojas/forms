## Family Holiday Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about family members' holiday experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the tour organization you're associated with.
2. Provide the name of your school or company you're working with.
3. Enter your family's name.
4. Describe your holiday experience.
5. Specify the location of your holiday.
6. Choose the start date of your holiday.
7. Choose the end date of your holiday.
8. (Optional) Leave feedback about your holiday experience.
9. (Optional) Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
10. (Optional) Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tour Organization** (text, required): Enter the name of the tour organization you're associated with.
* **School/Company** (text, required): Enter the name of your school or company you're working with.
* **Family Name** (text, required): Enter your family's name.
* **Holiday Experience** (text, required): Describe your holiday experience in a few sentences.
* **Location** (text, required): Specify the location of your holiday.
* **Date Start** (date, required): Choose the start date of your holiday.
* **Date End** (date, required): Choose the end date of your holiday.
* **Feedback** (note, optional): Leave feedback about your holiday experience.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

Please note that all required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) symbol for clarity.
