# sports_league_fan_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from sports league fans regarding their experience with the league, focusing on aspects such as game day satisfaction, team performance, engagement, and other related factors.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, select the corresponding page with the relevant fields for your answer.
2.  For general feedback questions (game_day_satisfaction, team_performance, league_engagement, fan_frustration, improvement_feedback, overall_experience), type in your answer in the provided text input field.
3.  For select_one fields (select_game_day_satisfaction, select_team_performance, select_league_engagement, select_fan_frustration, select_improvement_feedback, select_overall_experience), select one of the provided options that best matches your experience.
4.  For select_multiple fields (select_game_day_location, select_game_time, select_game_date, select_player_performance, select_game_length, select_score), select all the options that apply to your experience.
5.  Make sure to complete all required fields, as indicated by the required status in each field.

*   Make sure to provide as much detail as possible when answering the general feedback questions.
