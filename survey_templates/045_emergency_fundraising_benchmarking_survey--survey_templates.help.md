# Emergency Fundraising Benchmarking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Fundraising Benchmarking Survey is a tool to help organizations track and compare their emergency fundraising metrics with other organizations in their industry. By collecting data from various organizations, we can gain insights into the most effective emergency fundraising strategies and tactics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your organization's official records.
2. Enter your organization's name as it appears on your official records.
3. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
4. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
5. Report the time spent on emergency fundraising activities in the past quarter.
6. Report the date of your last emergency fundraising survey.
7. Select the frequency at which you conduct emergency fundraising activities (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
8. Report the total donations received during the past quarter.
9. Report the average amount of each donation.
10. Report the largest donation received during the past quarter.
11. Report the total number of donors.
12. Report the average frequency of donations.
13. Report the largest frequency of donations.
14. Report the average donation amount.
15. Report the total donations received during the past quarter.
16. Report the average donation amount received.
17. Report the highest donation received during the past quarter.
18. Report the lowest donation received during the past quarter.
19. Report the number of donations received during the past quarter.
20. Report the number of donors.
21. Report the largest donation received during the past quarter.
22. Report the smallest donation received during the past quarter.
23. Report the percentage of total donations.
24. Report the average percentage of donations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This field should be filled with the name of the person filling the form as it appears on your organization's official records.
* **Organization**: This field should be filled with the name of your organization as it appears on your official records.
* **Email**: This field should be filled with your organization's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: This field should be filled with your organization's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Time Spent**: This field should be filled with the time spent on emergency fundraising activities in the past quarter.
* **Date Last Survey**: This field should be filled with the date of your last emergency fundraising survey.
* **Frequency of Survey**: This field should be filled with the frequency at which you conduct emergency fundraising activities (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Total Donations**: This field should be filled with the total donations received during the past quarter.
* **Average Gift**: This field should be filled with the average amount of each donation.
* **Largest Gift**: This field should be filled with the largest donation received during the past quarter.
* **Total Donors**: This field should be filled with the total number of donors.
* **Average Gift Frequency**: This field should be filled with the average frequency of donations.
* **Largest Gift Frequency**: This field should be filled with the largest frequency of donations.
* **Average Donation Amount**: This field should be filled with the average donation amount.
* **Total Donations Received**: This field should be filled with the total donations received during the past quarter.
* **Average Donation Amount Received**: This field should be filled with the average donation amount received.
* **Highest Donation Received**: This field should be filled with the highest donation received during the past quarter.
* **Lowest Donation Received**: This field should be filled with the lowest donation received during the past quarter.
* **Number of Donations**: This field should be filled with the number of donations received during the past quarter.
* **Number of Donors**: This field should be filled with the number of donors.
* **Largest Donation**: This field should be filled with the largest donation received during the past quarter.
* **Smallest Donation**: This field should be filled with the smallest donation received during the past quarter.
* **Total Donations Percentage**: This field should be filled with the total donations percentage.
* **Average Donation Percentage**: This field should be filled with the average donation percentage.

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.
- Only fill out the form if you are a legitimate organization that conducts emergency fundraising activities.
- Do not fill out duplicate submissions.
