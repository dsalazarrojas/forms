# Doorbell Camera Product Comparison Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from users about their doorbell camera experience. The data collected will help us improve our product and better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
2. Select "No answer" or "Not applicable" if a question does not apply to your situation.
3. Fill out all required fields.
4. Ensure you have completed all necessary fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current doorbell camera model?** (`camera_model`, text, required: false)
	+ Please enter your current doorbell camera model, e.g. "Ring Pro", "Blink", etc.
* **What features do you like about your current doorbell camera?** (`camera_features_1`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select all features that you like about your current camera, e.g. Motion Detection, Night Vision, etc.
* **How would you rate your experience with your doorbell camera's connectivity?** (`connectivity_rating`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select how satisfied you are with your camera's connectivity, e.g. Very Satisfied, Satisfied, etc.
* **Which of the following features would you like to see improved in future doorbell cameras?** (`future_features_1`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select all features that you would like to see improved, e.g. Motion Detection, Night Vision, etc.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with your doorbell camera?** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select how satisfied you are with your camera overall, e.g. Very Satisfied, Satisfied, etc.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback about your doorbell camera?** (`feedback`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback about your camera.
* **What is your email address?** (`contact_info_1`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address, e.g. john.doe@example.com
* **What is your phone number?** (`contact_info_2`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number, e.g. 123-456-7890
* **What is your name?** (`contact_info_3`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name, e.g. John Doe
* **How can we contact you?** (`contact_info_4`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any other contact information, e.g. website, social media handle, etc.

## Tips
* Please complete all required fields.
* If a question does not apply to your situation, select "No answer" or "Not applicable".
* Please be honest and accurate with your answers.
* If you need help or clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
