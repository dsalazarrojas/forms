# Football Cleat Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your preferences for football cleats. Your responses will help us understand your needs and preferences for cleats.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to you. The form is divided into several sections, each asking a specific question or set of questions. You can navigate through the sections using the buttons at the bottom of the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cleat Brands**: Please select all the football cleat brands you have used or are interested in. You can choose from Adidas, Nike, Under Armour, Reebok, and New Balance. This will help us understand the brands you prefer or are familiar with.
* **Preferred Features**: Select the features that matter most to you when it comes to football cleats. You can choose from stud length, stud type, and stud arrangement. This will help us understand your specific needs and preferences.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Please rate your overall satisfaction with your current or previous cleats. You can choose from Satisfied and Dissatisfied. This will help us understand how satisfied you are with your cleats.
* **First Cleat Used**: Indicate if you have used a cleat before. You can choose from Yes and No.
* **First Cleat Model**: Indicate if you have used a specific model of cleat before. You can choose from Yes and No.
* **First Cleat Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with your first cleat. You can choose from Satisfied and Dissatisfied.
* **Second Cleat Used**: Indicate if you have used a second cleat. You can choose from Yes and No.
* **Second Cleat Model**: Indicate if you have used a specific model of second cleat. You can choose from Yes and No.
* **Second Cleat Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with your second cleat. You can choose from Satisfied and Dissatisfied.
* **Third Cleat Used**: Indicate if you have used a third cleat. You can choose from Yes and No.
* **Third Cleat Model**: Indicate if you have used a specific model of third cleat. You can choose from Yes and No.
* **Cleat Purchasing Priority**: You can enter a numerical value indicating your priority for purchasing a cleat. This is a free-form entry.
* **Cleat Price**: You can enter a numerical value indicating the price of your cleat.
* **Cleat Recommended**: Select cleats that you would recommend to others. You can choose from Adidas, Nike, Under Armour, Reebok, and New Balance.
* **Coach's Cleat**: Enter your coach's name, if applicable.
* **Coach Name**: Enter the name of your coach, if applicable.
* **Team Abbreviation**: Enter your team's abbreviation, if applicable.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of your program, if applicable.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your cleat preferences or experiences.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Date Submitted**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Timestamp**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Form Completion**: Indicate if you have completed the survey.
* **Coach Survey Completed**: Indicate if you've completed a coach's survey.
* **Program Survey Completed**: Indicate if you've completed a program's survey.

Note: Please fill out as many fields as possible to help us better understand your preferences and needs.
