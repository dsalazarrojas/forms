# Commuter Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commuter Survey is designed to gather information about your daily commute habits and experiences. This data will help us understand how you travel, what you like about your current commute, and what could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Choose the best answer that fits your situation for each question.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply if the answer applies to you.
4. Use the text area to provide specific details or suggestions about improving your commuting experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Mode of Transportation** (`primary_transport_mode`, select_one): What is your primary mode of transportation for commuting? Choose the mode you use most frequently.
* **Commute Frequency** (`commute_frequency`, number): How many days per week do you travel to your primary destination?
* **Average Commute Distance** (`average_commute_distance`, number): Enter the approximate one-way distance of your commute in miles.
* **Average Commute Time** (`average_commute_time`, number): Enter the time it takes for your one-way commute in minutes.
* **Typical Departure Time** (`typical_departure_time`, time): What time do you usually leave for your commute? (Leave blank if not applicable.)
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`commute_satisfaction`, select_one): Rate your overall satisfaction with your daily commute, from very dissatisfied to very satisfied.
* **Stress Level** (`commute_stress_level`, select_one): Rate the stress level of your commute, from low to high.
* **Alternative Mode Consideration** (`alternative_mode_consideration`, select_one): Have you considered switching to a more sustainable mode of transportation?
* **Barriers to Public Transport** (`barriers_to_public_transport`, select_multiple): Select all the barriers that apply to you when using public transportation.
* **Improvement Suggestion** (`improvements_suggestion`, text): Share your thoughts on a single improvement that would enhance your commuting experience.
