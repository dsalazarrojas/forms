# gaming_platform_user_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the gaming platform, helping us to identify areas for improvement and make necessary adjustments to enhance the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing honest and constructive feedback.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. If a field is optional, you can choose to leave it blank if you do not have any information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Satisfaction Level
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, `number`, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with our platform on a scale of 1-10.
### Recommendations
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the features or aspects of our platform you would recommend to others.
### Improvement Opportunities
* **Improvement Opportunities** (`improvement_opportunities`, `text`, required: false): Share any areas where you feel we could improve our platform.
### Favorite Features
* **Favorite Features** (`favorite_features`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the feature that you find the most enjoyable or useful on our platform.
### Feedback Message
* **Feedback Message** (`feedback_message`, `note`, required: false): Write any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experience with our platform.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
### Feedback Date
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you used the platform (if known).
### Feedback Time
* **Feedback Time** (`feedback_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you used the platform (if known).

## Tips
* Please be honest and constructive with your feedback.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please let us know so we can address them.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback, it is greatly appreciated!
