# Home Office Innovation Award Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from Home Office employees on the innovation award voting process. We value your input and encourage you to complete the form to help us improve the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Voting Process" field, which asks you to choose how you would like to vote. Select one of the options provided.
2. If you have an idea for an innovation that you would like to submit, please fill in the "Description of Innovation" field with a brief description.
3. For each innovation you submit, indicate whether or not it is related to the "Home Office Setup" by selecting the relevant option.
4. If you would like to participate in the voting process, select "Yes" in the "Voting Participants" field.
5. Finally, submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Templates** (`survey_templates_form`, text, Optional): This field is not a required field and is not used in the form's functionality. Please ignore it.
* **Voting Process** (`voting_process`, select_one, Optional): Choose how you would like to vote: Award, Innovation, or Award and Innovation. This field is required.
* **Home Office Setup** (`home_office_setup`, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate whether your innovation is related to the Home Office setup by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Description of Innovation** (`description_of_innovation`, text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your innovation, up to 500 characters.
* **Voting Participants** (`voting_participants`, select_multiple, Optional): Select "Yes" if you would like to participate in the voting process.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, Required): Click this button to submit your feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about your submission.
