# Bloodsucker Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is an interactive quiz designed to test your knowledge on blood-feeding animals and insects. It consists of multiple-choice questions that cover various topics related to blood-feeding creatures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the correct answer from the provided options.
2. Fill in your participant information (name and age group).
3. Answer all the questions to the best of your knowledge.
4. Submit your quiz to get the results.
