# Tech Software Productivity Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the impact of various tools on their productivity in the tech software industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question in as much detail as possible.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all the relevant options that apply to you.
3. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please use the "Summary" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`intro_label`, note, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully and then proceed to answer the questions.
* **Tool List** (`tool_list_label`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the tools you use for your work. This will help us understand which tools have the most significant impact on your productivity.
* **Question 1** (`question_1_label`, text, required: false): How has the tool changed your workflow?
* **Question 2** (`question_2_label`, text, required: false): What benefits have you experienced since using the tool?
* ...
* **Question 20** (`question_20_label`, text, required: false): What is your overall satisfaction with the tool?
* **Summary** (`summary_label`, note, required: false): This is your chance to add any additional thoughts or comments you may have.

Please note that you can answer any of the questions, and it is not mandatory to answer all of them. Your feedback is highly valued, and we appreciate your participation in this survey.
