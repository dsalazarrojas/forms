# Client Health Consultation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your health status, goals, and expectations for potential consultation services. This information will help healthcare professionals understand your needs and create a personalized plan for your consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, taking care to provide your most up-to-date information.
2. Use a pen or digital instrument of choice to fill out the form.
3. Read and understand each question before answering.
4. Enter the required information in the correct format (e.g., date in MM/DD/YY format).
5. If you are unsure about any information, please seek clarification before continuing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal first name as it appears on your ID or government-issued documentation.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal last name as it appears on your ID or government-issued documentation.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your best contact number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YY format (e.g., 02/25/1990).
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Choose your gender from the available options (Male, Female, Non-Binary, Prefer Not to Say, Other).
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, optional): Enter your current job or occupation, if applicable.
* **Type of Consultation** (`consultation_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of consultation you are seeking (e.g., General Health, Nutrition Counseling, Fitness Consultation, etc.).
* **Primary Health Goal** (`primary_health_goal`, `text`, required): Enter the primary health goal you are seeking to achieve.
* **Current Health Concerns** (`health_concerns`, `text`, required): Describe your current health concerns or worries.
* **Existing Medical Conditions** (`existing_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any diagnosed medical conditions you have.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, optional): List any current medications or supplements you are taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): List any allergies you have (e.g., medication, food, environmental).
* **Previous Surgeries or Hospitalizations** (`previous_surgeries`, `text`, optional): List any past surgeries or hospitalizations relevant to your health.
* **Family Health History** (`family_health_history`, `text`, optional): Share any relevant family health history.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you exercise (Never, 1-2 times per week, 3-4 times per week, 5-6 times per week, Daily).
* **Type of Exercise** (`exercise_type`, `text`, optional): Describe the type of exercise you do (e.g., running, swimming, yoga, etc.).
* **Dietary Habits** (`dietary_habits`, `text`, required): Describe your typical diet.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate your sleep quality (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Average Hours of Sleep Per Night** (`sleep_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the average hours of sleep you get per night.
* **Current Stress Level** (`stress_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current stress level (Low, Moderate, High, Very High).
* **Sources of Stress** (`stress_sources`, `text`, optional): Describe the sources of stress in your life.
* **Smoking Status** (`smoking_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your smoking status (Never Smoked, Former Smoker, Current Smoker).
* **Alcohol Consumption** (`alcohol_consumption`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you consume alcohol (Never, Rarely, Occasionally, Regularly, Daily).
* **Caffeine Consumption** (`caffeine_consumption`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you consume caffeine (None, 1-2 cups per day, 3-4 cups per day, 5+ cups per day).
* **Daily Water Intake** (`water_intake`, `select_one`, optional): Select your daily water intake (0-2 glasses, 3-5 glasses, 6-8 glasses, 9+ glasses).
* **Primary Care Physician Name** (`health_provider_name`, `text`, optional): If you have a primary care physician, enter their name.
* **Primary Care Physician Phone** (`health_provider_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a primary care physician, enter their phone number.
* **Have You Had Health Consultations Before?** (`previous_consultations`, `select_one`, required): Choose yes or no if you have had health consultations before.
* **What Do You Expect from This Consultation?** (`consultation_expectations`, `text`, required): Enter your expectations for this consultation.
* **I Consent to Health Consultation Services** (`consent_to_consultation`, `select_one`, required): Choose to consent to the health consultation services.
* **I Confirm All Information Is Accurate** (`information_accuracy`, `select_one`, required): Choose to confirm that all information provided is accurate.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the survey in MM/DD/YY format (e.g., 02/25/1990).

## Tips

* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any information, please seek clarification before continuing.
* This survey will help healthcare professionals understand your needs and create a personalized plan for your consultation.
* Please review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the health care provider before submitting the form.
