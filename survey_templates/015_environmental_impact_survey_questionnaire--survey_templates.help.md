# Environmental Impact Survey Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Impact Survey Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information about users' environmental awareness, attitudes, and practices. It is intended to provide insight into their perception of environmental issues and identify areas where they can adopt sustainable practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page of the form carefully to ensure you have filled in all the required fields.
2. For each field, select the answer that best reflects your thoughts and opinions.
3. Make sure to enter your contact information so we can contact you for further discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, text, not required): This is the first page of the form where you can provide any additional comments or questions about the survey.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select_multiple, not required): Select the options that best describe your contact information. Please note that this field is not required, but it will help us contact you for further discussions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter your contact number so we can contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date when you filled this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time when you filled this form.
* **Note** (`note`, note, not required): This is a text area where you can provide any additional comments or notes about the survey.
* **Sustainability Attitudes** (`sustainability_attitudes`, select_one, not required): Select how strongly you agree or disagree with the following statements:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Awareness** (`awareness`, select_one, not required): Select how strongly you agree or disagree with the following statements:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Sustainability Practices** (`sustainability_practices`, select_one, not required): Select how strongly you agree or disagree with the following statements:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Environmental Issues** (`environmental_issues`, select_multiple, not required): Select the environmental issues that you are concerned about:
	+ Air Quality
	+ Water Quality
	+ Noise Pollution
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, not required): This is a text area where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the survey.
