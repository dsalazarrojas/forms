# Cargo Delivery Simulation Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the cargo delivery simulation experience. It helps us understand your perceptions on the simulation's objectives, realism, difficulty level, and overall experience. Your feedback will aid us in improving the simulation and providing better experiences for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct answer or option for each question, following the scale or options provided.
2. For questions with numerical ratings (e.g., 1-5), use the scale as instructed.
3. Be as specific as possible when answering open-ended questions (e.g., "What was the biggest challenge you faced during the simulation?").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Simulation Exercise Name** (`exercise_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the simulation exercise you participated in.
* **Date of Simulation** (`simulation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the simulation in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Your Role in the Simulation** (`role_played`, select_one, required): Choose the role you occupied during the simulation from the provided options (Driver, Dispatcher, Warehouse Manager, Customer, Compliance Officer, Observer).
* **Were the simulation objectives clear?** (`objectives_clarity`, select_one, required): Choose how clear the objectives were to you from the provided options (Very Clear, Clear, Neutral, Unclear, Very Unclear).
* **Rate the realism of the simulation scenario** (`scenario_realism`, number, required): Rate the realism of the simulation from 1 (not realistic) to 5 (very realistic).
* **Rate the difficulty level of the simulation** (`difficulty_level`, select_one, required): Choose the difficulty level of the simulation from the provided options (Too Easy, Appropriate, Challenging, Too Difficult).
* **How effective was the communication within your team?** (`team_communication`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of team communication from 1 (poor) to 5 (exceptional).
* **How effectively did your team handle simulation challenges?** (`problem_solving`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of problem-solving from 1 (not effective) to 5 (very effective).
* **Rate the simulation tools and technology used** (`tools_and_tech`, number, required): Rate the quality of simulation tools and technology from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **What was the biggest challenge you faced during the simulation?** (`biggest_challenge`, text, required): Describe the biggest challenge you faced during the simulation.
* **Would you like to participate in similar simulations in the future?** (`future_simulation_interest`, select_one, required): Choose your interest in participating in similar simulations from the provided options (True, False, Maybe).
* **Overall rating of the simulation experience** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).

Note: All fields marked as required must be completed for the form to be considered valid.
