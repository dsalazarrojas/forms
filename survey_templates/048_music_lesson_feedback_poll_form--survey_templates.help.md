# Music Lesson Feedback Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their recent music lessons. It helps us understand how you perceive the quality of our lessons, identify areas for improvement, and gather suggestions from students like you! Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and help us make our music lessons even more enjoyable and effective for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select the relevant options for each select_one field.
4. Enter any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.
5. Click on the "Submit" button again to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Feedback For Last Session
* **Feedback For Last Session** (`session_feedback`, text): This field is for your general feedback about the last session. Please provide any comments, suggestions, or criticisms about the lesson.

### Rating
* **Rating** (`teacher_rating`, select_one): Rate the quality of the lesson on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### Lesson Quality
* **Lesson Quality** (`lesson_quality`, select_one): Rate the lesson's overall quality on a scale of Good, Fair, or Poor.

### Will Attend Next Session
* **Will Attend Next Session** (`will_attend_next_session`, select_one): Indicate whether you plan to attend the next session.

### Date Of Session
* **Date Of Session** (`date_of_session`, date): Enter the date of the last lesson.

### Time Of Session
* **Time Of Session** (`time_of_session`, time): Enter the time of the last session.

### Music Style
* **Music Style** (`music_style`, select_one): Choose the type of music you enjoyed during the last session, such as Pop, Rock, Jazz, Classical, or Other.

### Instrument
* **Instrument** (`instrument`, select_one): Select the instrument played during the last session, such as Piano, Guitar, Drums, Violin, or Other.

### Student Age Group
* **Student Age Group** (`student_age_group`, select_one): Choose your age group, such as 0-5, 6-12, 13-18, 19-25, or Other.

### Teacher Name
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text): Enter your teacher's name.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Session Duration
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number): Enter the duration of the last lesson in minutes.

### Total Students
* **Total Students** (`total_students`, number): Enter the total number of students present during the last session.

### Total Sessions
* **Total Sessions** (`total_sessions`, number): Enter the total number of sessions you've attended.

### Session Date
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date): Enter the date of the last session.

### Time In Minutes
* **Time In Minutes** (`time_in_minutes`, number): Enter the total time in minutes you've spent on lessons so far.

### Music Lesson Fee
* **Music Lesson Fee** (`music_lesson_fee`, number): Enter the fee paid for the last lesson.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one): Choose the method through which you paid for the last lesson, such as Online, Offline, or Other.

### Student Suggestion
* **Student Suggestion** (`student_suggestion`, text): Suggest any ideas or improvements for future lessons.

### Teacher Suggestion
* **Teacher Suggestion** (`teacher_suggestion`, text): Suggest any ideas or improvements for future lessons.

### Student Comments
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text): Enter any comments or feedback about the last session.

### Teacher Comments
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text): Enter any comments or feedback for the teacher.
