# AI Sales Workflow Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Sales Workflow Impact Survey is a tool designed to gather insights from sales professionals on the impact of AI on their sales workflows. This survey aims to understand how AI affects different aspects of sales processes, including lead qualification, forecasting accuracy, manual data entry time, sales cycle length, customer interaction quality, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing your name and company name if you wish to do so. 
   This information is optional and will not affect your responses.
2. Choose your industry and job role to provide context for the survey.
3. Select all AI tools currently used in your sales workflow.
4. Indicate how long AI has been part of your sales workflow.
5. Evaluate the impact of AI on lead qualification speed, forecasting accuracy, and other aspects of your sales process.
6. Choose how much time AI saves on manual data entry each week.
7. Rate the impact of AI on your sales cycle length and customer interaction quality.
8. Select up to three biggest challenges faced with AI integration.
9. Rate the overall satisfaction with AI tools among your team.
10. Provide suggestions for improving AI sales workflows.
11. Finally, indicate whether you plan to increase AI tool investment in the next year.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Optional)**: Provide your name if you wish to do so. This is an optional field and will not affect your responses.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name.
* **Industry**: Choose the industry you belong to:
	+ Technology/SaaS
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance/Insurance
	+ Retail/E-commerce
	+ Real Estate
	+ Professional Services
* **Job Role**: Select your job role:
	+ Sales Executive
	+ Sales Manager
	+ Sales Development Rep (SDR)
	+ Sales Operations
	+ Marketing/Enablement
* **AI Sales Tools Currently Used**: Select all AI tools used in your sales workflow:
	+ AI-powered CRM (e.g., Salesforce Einstein)
	+ Lead Generation & Scoring AI
	+ Conversational Intelligence (e.g., Gong, Chorus)
	+ Automated Email/Follow-up AI
	+ Sales Forecasting AI
	+ Chatbots for Inbound Leads
* **How long has AI been part of your sales workflow?**: Indicate how long AI has been part of your sales process:
	+ Less than 3 months
	+ 3 to 6 months
	+ 6 to 12 months
	+ Over 1 year
* **Impact on Lead Qualification Speed**: Evaluate the impact of AI on lead qualification speed:
	+ Highly Improved
	+ Improved
	+ No Change
	+ Slightly Slower
	+ Significantly Slower
* **Impact on Sales Forecasting Accuracy**: Evaluate the impact of AI on sales forecasting accuracy:
	+ Much More Accurate
	+ More Accurate
	+ No Change
	+ Less Accurate
* **Manual data entry time saved weekly (hours)**: Estimate the time saved on manual data entry each week:
	+ None
	+ 1-2 hours
	+ 3-5 hours
	+ 5-10 hours
	+ 10+ hours
* **Impact on overall sales cycle length**: Evaluate the impact of AI on your sales cycle length:
	+ Significantly Shorter
	+ Shorter
	+ No Change
	+ Longer
* **Impact on customer interaction quality**: Evaluate the impact of AI on customer interaction quality:
	+ Significantly Better
	+ Slightly Better
	+ No Change
	+ Slightly Worse
* **Biggest Challenges faced with AI integration**: Select up to three challenges faced with AI integration:
	+ Integration with existing tech stack
	+ Team training & adoption
	+ Data privacy/security concerns
	+ High cost of tools
	+ Inaccurate AI outputs
	+ Lack of transparency (Black box)
* **Overall team satisfaction with AI tools**: Rate the overall satisfaction with AI tools among your team:
	+ 1 (Low) to 5 (High)
* **Suggestions for improving AI sales workflows**: Provide any suggestions for improving AI sales workflows.
* **Do you plan to increase AI tool investment in the next year?**: Indicate whether you plan to increase AI tool investment in the next year:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Unsure/Under Review
