# Management Consulting Value Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture feedback from management consulting clients about the value they have gained from the consulting relationship. It's meant to be completed by clients to provide their perspective on how the consulting services helped them achieve their goals and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Select the appropriate options for each question, using the provided dropdown menus where available.
3. For text fields, enter your answers in the provided space.
4. Make sure to fill in all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) next to the field label.
5. Once you've completed the form, submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Value** (`project_value`, number, required: false): Please enter a number to indicate the value you have gained from our management consulting services. This will help us understand the perceived value of our services.
* **Satisfaction with the Project** (`satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false): Select from the options provided to indicate your level of satisfaction with the project. This will help us understand your perception of our work.
* **Main Outcome of Our Consulting Relationship** (`project_outcome`, select_one, required: false): Select from the options provided to indicate the main outcome of our consulting relationship. This will help us understand the outcome you achieved with our help.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or to provide additional support.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it so we can contact you with any urgent matters.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, text, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Date of Completion** (`date`, date, required: true): Please select the date you completed this survey.
* **Preferred Time of Day** (`time`, time, required: false): If you have a preferred time of day, please select it.
* **Value of Our Management Consulting Relationship** (`management_consulting_value`, select_one, required: false): Select from the options provided to indicate the value of our management consulting relationship. This will help us understand your perception of our work.
* **Outcome 1** (`project_outcome_1`, text, required: false): Please enter any relevant outcomes you achieved with our help.
* **Outcome 2** (`project_outcome_2`, text, required: false): Please enter any additional outcomes you achieved with our help.
* **Outcome 3** (`project_outcome_3`, text, required: false): Please enter any further outcomes you achieved with our help.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you have any urgent matters to discuss, please provide your phone number.
* Please select a valid date and time for the "Date of Completion" and "Preferred Time of Day" fields.
* For text fields, please provide detailed answers to help us understand your feedback better.
