# Family Menu Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a family's menu preferences and needs. It is meant to be used for the purpose of understanding their goals, dietary restrictions, and special requests, as well as gathering some basic information about the family members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "Family Menu Selection Form" link to access the form.
* Scroll through the form pages to review and select the options for each field.
* For each field, select the options that apply to your family or enter a value in the text field.
* Once you have completed all the fields, click "Submit" to review and submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Goals (`family_menu_form`)** (`select_multiple`, Required/Optional: Not Required): Select "Yes" if your family has specific goals for their meal planning, such as trying new foods or following a specific diet.
* **Dietary Restrictions (`dietary_restrictions`)** (`select_one`, Required/Optional: Required): Select "Yes" if your family has any dietary restrictions, such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, etc.
* **Preferred Dishes (`preferred_dishes`)** (`select_multiple`, Required/Optional: Required): Select "Yes" for any dishes that your family particularly enjoys or would like to have more often, such as pizza or stir-fry.
* **Special Requests (`special_requests`)** (`text`, Required/Optional: Not Required): Enter any special requests or comments about your family's menu preferences.
* **Family Name (`family_name`)** (`text`, Required/Optional: Required): Enter the name of your family or household.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting your answers to ensure that you have selected all applicable options.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrator for assistance.
