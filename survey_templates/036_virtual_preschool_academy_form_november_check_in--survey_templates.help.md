# Virtual Preschool Academy Form November Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for students and parents to report their attendance and satisfaction with Virtual Preschool Academy's November classes. It helps us understand how well our virtual classes are serving our students and their families.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please select the page that best matches your situation.
2. Fill in your student's name and your parent's name in the text fields provided.
3. Choose the class you attended from the dropdown list.
4. Select your date of attendance using the calendar or date picker.
5. Choose your level of satisfaction with the class.
6. Click the submit button to send your information to the academy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction 
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This page is a general welcome and introduction to the form, providing background information about the survey.

### Student Information 
* **Student name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your child's name as it appears on their school records.
* **Parent name** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.

### Class Information 
* **Class attended** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose which virtual classroom your child attended.
	+ Options: Virtual Classroom A, Virtual Classroom B, Virtual Classroom C, Virtual Classroom D

### Attendance and Date 
* **Date attended** (`5`, `date`, required: false): Please select the date you attended the virtual class using the calendar or date picker.
* **Satisfaction level** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction level with the class.
	+ Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied

### Tips
* Please be honest and accurate with your responses to help us better serve you and your child.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
