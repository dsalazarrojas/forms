# Third Party Risk Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Third Party Risk Assessment Survey" form is designed to evaluate the risk level of third-party vendors. It helps assess the potential risks associated with working with external parties that handle sensitive data or sensitive business processes. The survey is used to gather information about the vendor's IT infrastructure, security practices, and overall risk posture to determine the level of risk they pose to your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions according to your assessment.
2. Ensure that you select the correct option for each question based on the information provided.
3. For fields with options, select the most appropriate choice for your assessment.
4. Provide clear and concise answers in the required text fields.
5. Rate the vendor's overall risk level based on your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Vendor Information** (id: 13)
	* This field is for providing additional information about the vendor. Please include any relevant details that may impact the assessment.
### 2. **Risk Factors** (id: 4)
	* This field is for listing any risk factors associated with the vendor. Please include any concerns or issues that may impact their risk level.
### 3. **Has the vendor been vetted and cleared by your company's security team?** (id: 5)
	* This question is to determine if the vendor has undergone the necessary security clearance procedures.
### 4. **Is the vendor's IT infrastructure in accordance with our security and compliance policies?** (id: 6)
	* This question assesses the vendor's adherence to your organization's security and compliance policies.
### 5. **Does the vendor meet our IT security requirements?** (id: 7)
	* This question evaluates the vendor's ability to meet your organization's IT security requirements.
### 6. **Does the vendor meet our IT compliance requirements?** (id: 8)
	* This question determines if the vendor meets the required compliance standards.
### 7. **What is the vendor's management structure?** (id: 9)
	* This question is to understand the vendor's management structure, which may impact their risk posture.
### 8. **What is the risk level for the vendor's IT infrastructure?** (id: 10)
	* Select the most accurate risk level based on your assessment.
### 9. **What is the risk level for the vendor's data handling?** (id: 11)
	* Assess the risk associated with the vendor's data handling practices.
### 10. **What is the risk level for the vendor's security practices?** (id: 12)
	* This question determines the risk level associated with the vendor's security practices.
### 11. **Overall Vendor Rating** (id: 15)
	* Rate the vendor's overall risk level based on your assessment.
### 12. **Rating Rationale** (id: 16)
	* Provide a brief explanation for the overall rating.
### 13. **Vendor Comments** (id: 17)
	* Add any additional comments or observations about the vendor's risk posture.
### 14. **Additional Notes** (id: 14)
	* Enter any additional information or comments about the vendor.
### 15. **Vendor Rating** (id: 13)
	* This field is not a field to be filled out.
