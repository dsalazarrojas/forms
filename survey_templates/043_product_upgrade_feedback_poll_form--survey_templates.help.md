# Product Upgrade Feedback Poll Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers regarding product upgrades. It aims to gather information about any issues customers have encountered and their priority and impact on the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Provide your feedback about the product in the "Feedback" field.
4. Describe the issue you are experiencing with the product in the "Issue Description" field.
5. Select the priority level of the issue in the "Issue Priority" dropdown menu (Critical, High, Medium, Low, None).
6. Select the impact of the issue on the product in the "Impact" dropdown menu (High, Medium, Low, None).
7. Finally, type any additional feedback you want to share with the "Feedback Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name**: A text field to collect the customer's name. Please fill this in with your name as it appears on your official identity documents.

### Customer Email
* **Customer Email**: A field to collect the customer's email address. This will help us contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates about your case.

### Feedback
* **Feedback**: A field to collect any comments or suggestions you have about the product. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your concerns.

### Issue Description
* **Issue Description**: A field to describe the specific problem you are experiencing with the product. This will help us understand the issue better and provide a more accurate solution.

### Issue Priority
* **Issue Priority**: A dropdown menu to select the level of severity of the issue. Please choose from: Critical, High, Medium, Low, None. This will help us prioritize your case.

### Impact
* **Impact**: A dropdown menu to select the effect of the issue on the product. Please choose from: High, Medium, Low, None. This will help us understand the urgency of your issue.

### Feedback Message
* **Feedback Message**: A field to share any additional comments or feedback you want to provide. This is an opportunity to provide more context or information about your case.
