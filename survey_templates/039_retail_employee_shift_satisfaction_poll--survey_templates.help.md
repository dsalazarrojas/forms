Retail Employee Shift Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
=====================================================

## Purpose
The "Retail Employee Shift Satisfaction Poll" form is designed to gather feedback from retail employees about their shift experiences. This form allows managers to collect information on shift satisfaction, work status, and suggestions for improvement. The collected data can help managers identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance employee satisfaction and overall work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
2.  Select the shift you worked on (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, or Night).
3.  Enter the start and end times of your shift.
4.  Choose your work status for the shift (e.g., On Time, Late, or Absent).
5.  Select your satisfaction level for the shift (e.g., Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, etc.).
6.  Provide reasons for your satisfaction or dissatisfaction in the text fields.
7.  Offer suggestions for improvement for the manager to consider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name who supervised your shift.
*   **Shift**: Select the shift you worked on.
*   **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your shift.
*   **End Time**: Enter the end time of your shift.
*   **Shift Length**: Enter the length of your shift (number of hours worked).
*   **Work Status**: Choose your work status for the shift (e.g., On Time, Late, or Absent).
*   **Satisfaction Level**: Select your satisfaction level for the shift (e.g., Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, etc.).
*   **Reasons For Satisfaction**: Provide reasons why you were satisfied with your shift.
*   **Reasons For Dissatisfaction**: Provide reasons why you were dissatisfied with your shift.
*   **Suggestions For Improvement**: Suggest ways the manager can improve future shifts.

## Tips

*   Be honest and thorough in your responses.
*   Take a few minutes to think about your shift experience before submitting the form.
*   If you have any comments or suggestions, make sure to include them in the "Suggestions For Improvement" field.
*   If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field label for guidance.
