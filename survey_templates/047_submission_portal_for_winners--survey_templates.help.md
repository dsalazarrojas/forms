# Submission Portal For Winners - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Submission Portal for Winners is a form designed to collect and manage information about submissions and winners. It's intended to be a versatile tool for collecting and organizing various types of submission information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **User Info** page with your personal details.
2. Provide your **Contact Details** to ensure we can reach you.
3. Enter the **Entry Details** for the submission, including the **Entry Name**, **Entry Description**, and any other relevant information.
4. Choose the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** for the submission.
5. Add any necessary files for the submission using **Submission Files**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (required: false): Please fill in your name, email, and any other personal information you'd like to share.
* **Contact Details** (required: false): Provide your contact information, such as phone number and email, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Entry Details** (required: false): Enter the name, description, and any other details about the submission.
	+ **Entry Name** (required: false): The title or name of the submission.
	+ **Entry Description** (required: false): A brief description of the submission.
* **Submission Date** (required: false): Choose the date you'd like to associate with the submission.
* **Submission Time** (required: false): Choose the time of the submission.
* **Entry Image** (required: false): If you have an image related to the submission, upload it here.
* **Entry Description** (required: false): A longer description of the submission.
* **Submission Files** (required: false): Upload any relevant files for the submission.

* **Contact Details** (required: false): Please provide your contact details, such as phone number and email.
* **Winner Name** (required: false): The name of the winner (if applicable).
* **Winner Email** (required: false): The email of the winner (if applicable).

* **Winner Image** (required: false): An image related to the winner (if applicable).
* **Winner Video** (required: false): A video related to the winner (if applicable).
* **Assigned Tool** (required: false): Choose the tool assigned to the winner (if applicable).
* **Assigned Tool Other** (required: false): If the assigned tool is not in the list, provide an alternative tool.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all required fields and ensure that all fields are accurately filled in. If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to us for assistance.
