# Waiting Room Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waiting Room Feedback Form is designed to gather information from patients about their experience in the waiting room. This feedback will help us improve our services to ensure a more comfortable and pleasant experience for our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please answer the following questions:
	* What is your experience like in the waiting room? (Field 1)
	* How satisfied are you with our waiting room? (Field 2)
	* How would you rate your comfort level while waiting? (Field 3)
	* How long did you wait? (Field 4)
	* What time of day was your visit? (Field 5)
	* How long was your visit? (Field 6)
	* Do you have any contact information you'd like to provide? (Field 7)
	* Do you have any additional comments or suggestions? (Field 8)
	* Do you have an email address or phone number we can contact you at? (Field 9 and Field 10)
2. Please be as detailed as you can when answering these questions, as your feedback will help us improve our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your experience like in the waiting room? (1)** (`waiting_room_feedback_form`, text, optional): This field is where you can describe your experience in the waiting room. You can tell us about your wait time, comfort level, and any other observations or suggestions you might have.
* **How satisfied are you with our waiting room? (2)** (`satisfaction_survey`, number, optional): This field is where you rate your satisfaction with the waiting room on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being not satisfied and 10 being very satisfied.
* **How would you rate your comfort level while waiting? (3)** (`comfort_level`, number, optional): This field is where you rate your comfort level while waiting on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being not comfortable and 10 being very comfortable.
* **How long did you wait? (4)** (`waiting_time`, number, optional): This field is where you estimate the length of time you waited in the waiting room.
* **What time of day was your visit? (5)** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): This field is where you select the time of day you visited the healthcare facility.
* **How long was your visit? (6)** (`visit_length`, number, optional): This field is where you estimate the length of time your visit took.
* **Do you have any contact information you'd like to provide? (7)** (`contact_information`, text, optional): This field is where you provide your phone number or email address if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions? (8)** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): This field is where you provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your visit.
* **Do you have an email address we can contact you at? (9)** (`email`, email, optional): This field is where you provide your email address if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Do you have a phone number we can contact you at? (10)** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is where you provide your phone number if you'd like us to contact you.
