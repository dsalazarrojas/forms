# Teen Screen Time Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teen Screen Time Survey is designed to collect information about teenagers' screen time habits and parental involvement to inform and develop strategies for responsible screen use among this age group.
## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by answering the questions in the "Screen habits", "Screen time", and "Consequences" sections, which are all required fields.
2. In the "Parental involvement" section, answer the questions about parental involvement and monitoring only if you have a parent or guardian with whom you live.
3. Please answer all questions honestly and accurately, and if unsure, choose "Other (please specify)" for any field that does not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. What is your age** (`survey_title`): Please type your age.
* **2. How often do you use a screen** (`survey_screen_time`): This question is about how often you use a screen on a typical day.
*   * 1-2 hours: You use a screen for 1-2 hours on a typical day.
*   * 3-4 hours: You use a screen for 3-4 hours on a typical day.
*   * 5 or more hours: You use a screen for 5 or more hours on a typical day.
* **3. Screen habits** (`survey_habits`): Please select all the screens you use on a typical day from the options:
	+ Watching TV/Videos: You watch TV or videos on a screen.
	+ Playing games: You play games on a screen.
	+ Listening to music: You listen to music on a screen.
	+ Browsing the web: You browse the web on a screen.
	+ Chatting with friends: You chat with friends on a screen.
	+ Watching movies: You watch movies on a screen.
	+ Playing video games: You play video games on a screen.
	+ Listening to music or podcasts: You listen to music or podcasts on a screen.
* **4. How often do you use a phone or tablet?** (`survey_frequency`): This question is about how often you use a phone or tablet.
	+ Never: You never use a phone or tablet.
	+ Occasionally (1-2 times a week): You use a phone or tablet occasionally.
	+ Almost every day: You use a phone or tablet almost every day.
	+ Several times a day: You use a phone or tablet several times a day.
*   Please select the correct answer.
* **5. What screens do you have access to?** (`survey_screen_habit_type`): Please select all the screens you have access to.
	+ Phone: You have access to a phone.
	+ Tablet: You have access to a tablet.
	+ Computer: You have access to a computer.
	+ TV: You have access to a TV.
	+ Gaming Console: You have access to a gaming console.
*   Please select all that apply.
* **6. Frequency of device use** (`survey_frequency_device`): This question is about how often you use a device on a screen.
	+ Never: You never use a device on a screen.
	+ Less than 1 hour: You use a device on a screen for less than 1 hour.
	+ 1-2 hours: You use a device on a screen for 1-2 hours.
	+ 2-4 hours: You use a device on a screen for 2-4 hours.
	+ 4-6 hours: You use a device on a screen for 4-6 hours.
	+ More than 6 hours: You use a device on a screen for more than 6 hours.
*   Please select the correct answer.
* **7. Device screen time** (`survey_screen_time_device`): This question is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have any information to provide.
*   Please type the total screen time you spend on devices.
* **8. Educational screen time** (`survey_frequency_educational`): Please select all the educational screen activities you participate in on a typical day.
	+ Watching videos: You watch educational videos.
	+ Browsing the web: You browse the web for educational purposes.
	+ Chatting with friends: You chat with friends for educational purposes.
	+ Playing games: You play educational games.
	+ Other (please specify): There are other educational screen activities that apply to you.
*   Please select all that apply.
* **9. Consequences of screen time** (`survey_consequences`): This question is about the potential consequences of excessive screen time.
	+ Negative impact on relationships: You experience a negative impact on relationships due to screen time.
	+ Negative impact on grades: You experience a negative impact on grades due to screen time.
	+ Negative impact on physical health: You experience a negative impact on physical health due to screen time.
	+ Negative impact on mental health: You experience a negative impact on mental health due to screen time.
	+ Negative impact on social life: You experience a negative impact on social life due to screen time.
	+ Other (please specify): There are other negative consequences of screen time that apply to you.
*   Please select all that apply.
* **10. Comments** (`survey_comments`): This question is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have any comments or concerns.
*   Please type your comments or concerns.
* **11. Other consequences** (`survey_consequence_other`): This question is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have any other consequences of screen time.
*   Please type any other consequences of screen time.
* **12. Why do you use a screen?** (`survey_why_screen_time`): This question is about why you use a screen on a typical day.
	+ To relax: You use a screen to relax.
	+ To socialize: You use a screen to socialize.
	+ To learn: You use a screen to learn.
	+ To be creative: You use a screen to be creative.
	+ Other (please specify): There are other reasons why you use a screen that apply to you.
*   Please select all that apply.
* **13. Other reasons for screen time** (`survey_other_why_screen_time`): This question is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have any other reasons for screen time.
*   Please type your other reasons for screen time.
* **14. Parental involvement** (`survey_parent_involvement`): This question is about your parent's involvement in your screen time.
	+ None: Your parent is not involved in your screen time.
	+ Some involvement: Your parent is somewhat involved in your screen time.
	+ Significant involvement: Your parent is significantly involved in your screen time.
	+ Very significant involvement: Your parent is very significantly involved in your screen time.
*   Please select the level of parental involvement.
* **15. Have you discussed screen time with your parents?** (`survey_parent_conversation`): This question is about whether you have discussed screen time with your parents.
	+ Never: You have never discussed screen time with your parents.
	+ Some of the time: You have discussed screen time with your parents occasionally.
	+ Almost every time: You have discussed screen time with your parents almost every time.

* **16. How often do your parents set limits on screen time?** (`survey_parent_screen_time`): This question is about how often your parents set limits on screen time.
	+ Never: Your parents never set limits on screen time.
	+ Occasionally: Your parents set limits on screen time occasionally.
	+ Almost every time: Your parents set limits on screen time almost every time.

	+ True: Your parents set clear rules for screen time.
	+ Yes, partially: Your parents set some clear rules for screen time.

* **18. Do parents monitor screen time?** (`survey_parent_monitoring`): This question is about whether your parents monitor screen time.

* **19. Do parents approve screen time?** (`survey_parent_approve`): This question is about whether your parents approve screen time.

* **20. Parental screen time** (`survey_parent_screen_time`): This question is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have any information to provide.
*   Please type the screen time of your parents.

	+ Never: Your parents never set a screening time for screen time.

*   Please type the screen time limit hours set by your parents.
*   Please note that you can only enter whole numbers.
