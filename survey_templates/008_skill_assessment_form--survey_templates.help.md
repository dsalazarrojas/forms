# Skill Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skill Assessment Form is designed to collect information about a candidate's skills and abilities. It helps assessors to evaluate candidates for various roles or positions within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the candidate's information, including their first name, last name, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the assessment date and time.
3. Fill in the scorecard section with any relevant scores or feedback.
4. Review and fill in any questions related to the candidate's performance or behavior.
5. Add any additional notes or information about the candidate.
6. Finally, select one or multiple options from the select multiple field to provide further assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information (`candidate_info`, text, optional):** Enter the first name and last name of the candidate.
* **Assessment Information (`assessment_info`, text, optional):** Enter the assessment date and time.
* **Scorecard (`scorecard`, text, optional):** Enter any scores or feedback related to the candidate's performance.
* **Review Questions (`review_questions`, text, optional):** Answer any review questions related to the candidate's performance or behavior.
* **Review (`review`, text, optional):** Enter any additional review comments or feedback.
* **Select Multiple (`select_multiple_1`, select_multiple, required):** Select one or multiple options from the provided list to provide further assessment.
* **Note (`note`, text, optional):** Enter any additional information about the candidate.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are filling out the form for its intended purpose and that the candidate's information is up-to-date.
* Complete all required fields to ensure a thorough assessment.
* Provide accurate and detailed information to ensure that the assessment is fair and reliable.
* Use this form for skill assessment purposes only, as it is designed to gather specific information about a candidate's skills and abilities.
