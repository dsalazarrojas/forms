# Event Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Satisfaction Survey Form is designed to gather feedback from attendees about their recent event experience. This form helps us understand your thoughts, feelings, and opinions about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section completely and accurately.
2. For fields with multiple choices (like "Speakers"), select all that apply.
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location**: Please provide the name of the location where the event took place. This will help us understand the setting and context of your experience.
* **Content**: Share your thoughts about the content of the event, such as what you learned, what you enjoyed, or what could be improved.
* **Price**: If you're willing, share your opinion on the event price. This will help us gauge attendee satisfaction with cost.
* **Speakers**: Indicate which speakers you found engaging, entertaining, or helpful. Select all that apply.
* **Organization**: If you'd like to provide additional comments or feedback about the event organization, feel free to do so here.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers. Your feedback is valuable to us!
* If you're unsure about any field, just do your best to provide a reasonable answer.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
