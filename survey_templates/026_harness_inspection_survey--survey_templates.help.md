# Harness Inspection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Harness Inspection Survey is used to gather information about the inspection process of a harness, ensuring the quality and integrity of industrial equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and ensure you have the necessary permissions and qualifications to conduct the inspection.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately, providing all relevant information about the inspection process.
3. If you are unsure about any question or have any concerns, please do not hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **General Information** (`general_information`, `text`, required: false)
* This section is for providing general information about the inspection process.
* Please enter any relevant details about the harness being inspected, such as its location, type, or any notable features.

### 2. **Inspect Items** (`inspection_items`, `number`, required: false)
* This section is for listing the items that were inspected during the process.
* Please enter the number of items that were inspected, and provide details about any issues or concerns you encountered during the inspection.
