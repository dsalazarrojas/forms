# Community Project Selection Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather input from community members on their preferences and priorities for various community projects. By providing this feedback, you will help the community decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name (optional). This will help us understand who is providing feedback.
2. Select your residency status (required): Resident, Non-Resident Property Owner, Business Owner, or Other.
3. Enter your neighborhood or general address (required).
4. Select your age range (required).
5. Choose up to three project types that are most important to you (required).
6. Select your top priority project (required).
7. Choose the project that is your lowest priority (required).
8. Rate each of the proposed projects on a scale of 1 to 5, with 5 being High Priority and 1 being Low Priority.
9. Provide a brief reason for your top priority project (required).
10. If you have other project ideas, feel free to share them (optional).
11. Select if you would attend a town hall meeting to discuss these projects (required). If yes, please provide your email address so we can follow up with you (optional).
12. Select a date for submission (required).
