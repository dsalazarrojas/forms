# Change Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Change Readiness Assessment" form is a tool to help individuals and teams assess their readiness for an upcoming change initiative. It evaluates the personal and team readiness for the change, including understanding, preparedness, and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure you are completing this form in the context of the specific change initiative being assessed.
3. If you are unsure about a question, please answer based on your best judgment or ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): This is the date on which the assessment is being completed.
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Your name, if completing the form as an individual, or the team name, if completing the form as a team.
* **Department or Division** (`department`, text, optional): The department or division you work in, if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Your job title.
* **Change Initiative Being Assessed** (`change_initiative`, text, required): The specific name of the change initiative being assessed.
* **Description of Change** (`change_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the change.
* **Your Personal Readiness for Change** (`personal_readiness`, select_one, required): Rate your personal readiness for the change on a scale of:
	+ Very Ready
	+ Ready
	+ Somewhat Ready
	+ Not Ready
	+ Resistant
* **Team Readiness for Change** (`team_readiness`, select_one, required): Rate your team's readiness for the change on a scale of:
	+ Very Ready
	+ Ready
	+ Somewhat Ready
	+ Not Ready
	+ Resistant
* **Organization Readiness for Change** (`organization_readiness`, select_one, required): Rate the organization's readiness for the change on a scale of:
	+ Very Ready
	+ Ready
	+ Somewhat Ready
	+ Not Ready
	+ Resistant
* **Confidence in Skills Needed** (`skills_readiness`, select_one, optional): Rate your confidence in having the necessary skills on a scale of:
	+ Very Confident
	+ Confident
	+ Somewhat Confident
	+ Not Confident
* **Knowledge of the Change Initiative** (`knowledge_of_change`, select_one, required): Rate your understanding of the change initiative on a scale of:
	+ Excellent Understanding
	+ Good Understanding
	+ Some Understanding
	+ Little Understanding
	+ No Understanding
* **Adequacy of Communication About Change** (`communication_adequacy`, select_one, required): Rate the adequacy of communication about the change on a scale of:
	+ Very Adequate
	+ Adequate
	+ Somewhat Adequate
	+ Inadequate
* **Leadership Support for Change** (`leadership_support`, select_one, optional): Rate the level of leadership support for the change on a scale of:
	+ Strong Support
	+ Moderate Support
	+ Minimal Support
	+ No Support
* **Available Resources for Change** (`resource_availability`, select_one, optional): Rate the availability of resources for the change on a scale of:
	+ Very Adequate
	+ Adequate
	+ Somewhat Adequate
	+ Inadequate
* **Training or Support Available** (`training_availability`, select_one, optional): Rate the availability of training or support for the change on a scale of:
	+ Yes, Comprehensive
	+ Yes, Some
	+ Maybe
	+ No
* **Understanding of Change Urgency** (`change_urgency_understanding`, select_one, optional): Rate your understanding of the urgency of the change on a scale of:
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Not Clear
* **Main Concerns About Change** (`concerns_about_change`, text, optional): List any major concerns or worries you have about the change.
* **Probability of Change Success** (`success_probability`, select_one, optional): Rate the likelihood of change success on a scale of:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **Support Needed for Readiness** (`support_needed`, text, optional): List any additional support or resources you or your team may need to be fully prepared for the change.
* **Overall Readiness Score** (`overall_readiness_score`, number, required): Rate your overall readiness for the change on a scale of 1-10.

Please ensure to answer all questions honestly and accurately to get a comprehensive assessment of your readiness for the change initiative.
