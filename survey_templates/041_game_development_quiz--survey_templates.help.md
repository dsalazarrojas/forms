# Game Development Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Development Quiz form is used to gather information about game development experience and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the form is relevant to you.
2. Provide a brief description of yourself.
3. Rate your experience with game development (Very basic, Basic, Average, Proficient, Expert).
4. Enter your name and email address.
5. Enter your phone number (optional).
6. Provide additional details about yourself.
7. Choose the era of game development you focus on (Action, Action-Adventure, Action RPG, RPG, Strategy).
8. Select the game genres you are interested in (Adventure, Action, Role-Playing, Strategy, Sports, Survival).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (Yes/No)** (`category`, `select_one`): Indicate whether this form is relevant to you.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`): Provide a brief description of yourself and your experience with game development.
* **How would you rate your experience with game development?** (`game_development_quiz_form_description`, `select_multiple`): Rate your experience with game development (Very basic, Basic, Average, Proficient, Expert).
* **What is your name?** (`participant_name`, `text`): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, `email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Additional details** (`participant_details`, `note`): Provide any additional details about yourself.
* **What era of game development do you focus on?** (`game_development_era`, `select_multiple`): Choose the era of game development you focus on (Action, Action-Adventure, Action RPG, RPG, Strategy).
* **What game genre do you focus on?** (`game_genres`, `select_multiple`): Choose the game genres you are interested in (Adventure, Action, Role-Playing, Strategy, Sports, Survival).
