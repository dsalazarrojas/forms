# Growth Potential Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Growth Potential Assessment Form is designed to help us understand your company's growth potential and dynamics. It's meant to be filled out by the company representative, and will help us assess the company's size, industry, product or service, and relationships with contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form honestly and thoroughly.
2. Answer all the questions, even if you're not sure of the answer.
3. Don't worry if you don't know something, just make an educated guess.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1, Note, Required: False): This is a note field where you can introduce your company and provide any additional context.
* **Company Name** (2, Text, Required: False): Please enter your company's name as it's known to the public.
* **Contact Name** (3, Text, Required: False): Please enter the name of the person filling out the form.
* **Contact Email** (4, Email, Required: False): Please enter the email address of the person filling out the form.
* **Contact Phone** (5, Text, Required: False): Please enter the phone number of the person filling out the form.
* **Company Size** (6, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please select the size of your company from the options provided.
* **Industry** (7, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please select the industry of your company from the options provided.
* **Product/Service** (8, Text, Required: False): Please describe the product or service your company offers.
* **Description** (9, Text, Required: False): Please describe your company in a few sentences.
* **Growth Opportunities** (10, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please select the growth opportunities you see for your company from the options provided.
* **Current Challenges** (11, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please select the current challenges your company is facing from the options provided.
* **Solutions** (12, Text, Required: False): Please describe the solutions or steps your company is taking to address growth opportunities and current challenges.
* **Next Steps** (13, Text, Required: False): Please describe the next steps your company is planning to take.
* **Comments** (14, Text, Required: False): Please provide any additional comments about your company.
* **Company Rating** (15, Select One, Required: False): Please rate your company's overall performance from the options provided.
* **Contact Approvals** (16, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please select the people who approve of the contact process from the options provided.
* **Approval Process** (17, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please select the approval process your company uses from the options provided.
* **Decision Making Process** (18, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please select the decision making process your company uses from the options provided.
* **Company Culture** (19, Text, Required: False): Please describe your company's culture.
* **Relationship with Contact** (20, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please rate your relationship with the contact from the options provided.
* **Communication** (21, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please rate your company's communication from the options provided.
* **Time Saving** (22, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please rate your company's time saving from the options provided.
* **Collaboration** (23, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please rate your company's collaboration from the options provided.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (24, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please rate your company's employee satisfaction from the options provided.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (25, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please rate your company's overall satisfaction from the options provided.
