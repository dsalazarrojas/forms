# Accounting Accuracy Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gauge the accuracy of financial records within your company. Your feedback will help us understand the current state of accounting accuracy and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each page to navigate through the form.
2. Answer each question based on your personal experience and knowledge.
3. Select only the options that apply to you.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Suggestions" and "Additional Comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Respondent Information --** (`id: 1`): This is the first section of the form where you will be asked to provide some basic demographic information about yourself.
	+ **Name (Optional)** (`id: 2`): This is an optional field where you can input your name if you prefer to be identified in the survey results.
	+ **Department** (`id: 3`): Please select the department you belong to from the list.
	+ **Role Level** (`id: 4`): Choose the level of your role within the company from the options.
	+ **Years with the Company** (`id: 5`): Select the number of years you have been with the company.
* **-- Accuracy Perception --** (`id: 6`): This section inquires about your views on the accuracy and timeliness of financial reports.
	+ **How Would You Rate the Overall Accuracy of Financial Records** (`id: 7`): Choose your rating for the overall accuracy of financial records.
	+ **How Much Do You Trust the Financial Reports You Receive** (`id: 8`): Select your level of trust in the financial reports you receive.
	+ **How Often Do You Encounter Errors in Accounting Records** (`id: 9`): Choose how often you encounter errors in accounting records.
	+ **Types of Errors Encountered** (`id: 10`): Select all the types of errors you have encountered.
	+ **Are Financial Reports Delivered on Time** (`id: 11`): Choose how often financial reports are delivered on time.
* **-- Process Feedback --** (`id: 12`): This section inquires about your views on the process of accounting.
	+ **How Effective Are Current Reconciliation Processes** (`id: 13`): Choose how effective you rate the current reconciliation processes.
	+ **How Well Does the Finance Team Communicate Issues or Changes** (`id: 14`): Select how well the finance team communicates issues or changes.
	+ **What Area Needs the Most Improvement** (`id: 15`): Choose the area that needs the most improvement.
* **Suggestions for Improving Accounting Accuracy** (`id: 16`): If you have any suggestions, please input them here.
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 17`): If you have any additional comments, please input them here.
