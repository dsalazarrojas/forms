# New Year's Eve Post Event Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a New Year's Eve event. It will help us understand your experience, satisfaction, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question truthfully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for multiple-choice questions.
3. Provide your email address so we can contact you for any follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.
4. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How was the event?** (`new_years_eve_post_event_survey`): This question is designed to gather your overall impression of the event. Please provide a brief description of your experience, including any notable moments or surprises.
* **Did you attend the event?** (`event_attended`): This question helps us understand if you were present at the event or not. If you selected "True", please answer the subsequent questions to provide more details.
* **What time did the event start?** (`event_start_time`): If you attended the event, please provide the start time. This will help us synchronize our records with your experience.
* **What time did the event end?** (`event_end_time`): If you attended the event, please provide the end time. This will help us understand the duration of the event.
* **Overall rating of the event** (`overall_rating`): Please rate the event on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction.
* **Music rating** (`music_rating`): Rate the music played during the event on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Food and beverage rating** (`food_beverage_rating`): Rate the food and beverages provided during the event on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **What can we improve on?** (`suggest_improvement`): If you have any suggestions or criticisms, please provide them in this text field. This will help us understand areas where we can improve.
* **Email** (`email`): Please provide your email address so we can contact you for any follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`): This field is optional, but please provide your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Additional feedback** (`feedback`): If you have any additional thoughts or comments, please provide them in this text field.
* **What suggestions do you have?** (`suggestions`): If you have any suggestions for future events, please provide them in this text field.
* **How often do you attend this type of event?** (`event_frequency`): Please select how often you attend events of this type (e.g., music festivals, concerts, parties, conferences).
* **What type of event is this?** (`event_type`): Select the type of event this survey is referring to (e.g., music festival, concert, party, conference).
* **Did you attend alone?** (`attend_alone`): If you attended the event with others, please select the correct option.
* **Would you recommend this event?** (`recommend`): Please select if you would recommend this event to others.

By filling out this form, you will help us improve future events and provide better experiences for our attendees. Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
