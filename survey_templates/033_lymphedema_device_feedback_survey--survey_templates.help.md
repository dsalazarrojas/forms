# Lymphedema Device Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users about their experience with their lymphedema device. This information will help us understand how the device is being used and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that most accurately describe your experience with the device.
3. Provide detailed descriptions in the text fields (e.g., "usage_patterns" and "improvement_ideas") for a more comprehensive feedback.
4. If you have any additional feedback or concerns, please use the "Additional feedback or concerns?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief welcome message.
* **How often do you use your device?**: Select how often you use your device, such as "Every day", "Almost every day", "Several times a week", etc.
* **What is your typical usage pattern with your device?**: Provide a brief description of your usage pattern, such as "I use it for daily exercises", "I wear it at night", etc.
* **How satisfied are you with your device?**: Select how satisfied you are with the device, ranging from "Very satisfied" to "Very dissatisfied".
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?**: Provide detailed suggestions or ideas for improving the device.
* **Pain management during usage**: Select the level of pain management issues you experience during usage, ranging from "No issues" to "Extremely serious issues".
* **How would you rate the comfort of your device?**: Select how comfortable you find the device, ranging from "Not at all comfortable" to "Very uncomfortable".
* **Would you recommend this device to a friend or family member?**: Select if you would recommend the device to others.
* **Additional feedback or concerns?**: Provide any additional feedback or concerns you have about the device.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions).
* **Date**: Enter the date (if you want to be able to track when the feedback was given).
* **Time**: Enter the time (if you want to be able to track when the feedback was given).
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your experience with the device.
* **Category**: Select any relevant categories that apply to your feedback.
* **Tags**: Select any relevant tags that apply to your feedback.
