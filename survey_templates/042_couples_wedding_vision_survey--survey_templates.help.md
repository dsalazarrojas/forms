# Couples' Wedding Vision Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Couples' Wedding Vision Survey is a tool to gather information about your wedding ceremony and reception preferences. This form helps wedding planners and coordinators understand your personal tastes and expectations, ensuring a more personalized and enjoyable planning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Go through each page, reading the page-specific instructions and hints.
2. Answer each question based on your personal preferences and needs.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers, as this will help us understand your vision better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Welcome to the Survey
* **welcome_to_the_survey** (Note): This is the welcome page, introducing you to the survey.
### Page 2: Wedding Details
* **wedding_details** (Text): This field is for sharing any specific details about your wedding, such as the date, location, or theme.
### Page 3: Ceremony Preferences
* **ceremony_preferences** (Select One): Choose the type of ceremony you prefer:
	+ Outdoor ceremony: A ceremony held outside, surrounded by nature.
	+ Indoor ceremony: A ceremony held indoors, often in a more controlled environment.
	+ Other (please specify): If you have a different preference, please describe it here.
### Page 4: Reception Preferences
* **reception_preferences** (Select Multiple): Choose the types of reception that appeal to you:
	+ Outdoor reception: A reception held outside, often with a more casual atmosphere.
	+ Indoor reception: A reception held indoors, often with a more formal atmosphere.
	+ Other (please specify): If you have a different preference, please describe it here.
### Page 5: Guest Preferences
* **guest_preferences** (Select One): Choose the dress code for your guests:
	+ Formal: A formal, black-tie or cocktail dress code.
	+ Cocktail dress: A dressy, but less formal dress code.
	+ Casual: A more relaxed, casual dress code.
### Page 6: Budget
* **budget** (Number): Enter your estimated wedding budget.
### Page 7: Timeline
* **timeline** (Date): Enter your desired wedding date or a range of dates.
### Page 8: Venue Preferences
* **venue_preferences** (Select Multiple): Choose the type of venue that appeals to you:
	+ Historic mansion: A venue with a luxurious, classic feel.
	+ Beach location: A venue with a beach or ocean view.
	+ Mountain view: A venue with a mountain or scenic view.
### Page 9: Vendor Preferences
* **vendor_preferences** (Text): List the vendors you've worked with or would like to work with, such as caterers, photographers, or florists.

## Tips
* Answer as detailed as possible to help us understand your preferences better.
* If you have any special requests or specific needs, please let us know.
* This survey is a guide, and we'll work with you to tailor your wedding to your tastes and preferences.
