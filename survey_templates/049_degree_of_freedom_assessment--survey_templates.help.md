# Degree Of Freedom Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Degree Of Freedom Assessment form is used to collect data for assessing the degree of freedom (DoF) of a system or process. This form is designed to gather information from trainers to score and evaluate the freedom of movement or flexibility of a particular entity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the introduction and trainer name fields (intro1, trainer_name).
2. Enter the degree of freedom values (degree, flexibility, mobility) and their corresponding scores (flexibility_score, mobility_score) for the entity being assessed.
3. If additional comments or information are available, enter them in the comments and additional_comments fields.
4. Confirm the trainer name and email (trainer_name_2, trainer_email) and enter their contact information (phone, date, time).
5. Answer select questions (select_one, select_multiple) regarding the entity's freedom of movement or flexibility.
6. Review and verify the entered information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **intro1 (Introduction)** (`<text>`, required/optional): A brief introduction or description of the entity being assessed.
* **trainer_name (Trainer Name)** (`<text>`, required/optional): The name of the trainer who is assessing the entity.
* **degree_of_freedom (Degree of Freedom)** (`<number>`, required/optional): A numerical value representing the degree of freedom of the entity.
* **flexibility (Flexibility)** (`<number>`, required/optional): A numerical value representing the flexibility of the entity.
* **mobility (Mobility)** (`<number>`, required/optional): A numerical value representing the mobility of the entity.
* **flexibility_score (Flexibility Score)** (`<number>`, required/optional): A numerical value representing the score for the entity's flexibility.
* **mobility_score (Mobility Score)** (`<number>`, required/optional): A numerical value representing the score for the entity's mobility.
* **comments (Comments)** (`<text>`, optional/optional): Any additional comments or information regarding the entity being assessed.
* **additional_comments (Additional Comments)** (`<text>`, optional/optional): Any additional comments or information from the trainer.
* **trainer_name_2 (Trainer Name 2)** (`<text>`, required/optional): The name of the second trainer who may be involved in the assessment.
* **trainer_email (Trainer Email)** (`<email>`, required/optional): The email address of the second trainer who may be involved in the assessment.

* **date (Date)** (`<date>`, required/optional): The date of the assessment.
* **time (Time)** (`<time>`, required/optional): The time of the assessment.
* **select_one (Select One)** (`<select_one>`, required/optional): Select one of the options (Yes, No) to indicate the entity's freedom of movement or flexibility.

* **additional_comments1 (Additional Comments 1)** (`<text>`, optional/optional): A text field for any additional information or comments.
* **select3 (Select 3)** (`<select_one>`, required/optional): Select one of the options (Yes, No) to indicate the entity's freedom of movement or flexibility.
