# Global Warming Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Warming Survey is designed to gather information about your perception of global warming and climate change. Your honest responses will help inform policy and public awareness initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully to understand what it's asking.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. For select and multiple-choice questions, choose from the provided options.
4. If a question does not apply to you, select "Not Applicable" or skip it (if allowed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional context or information about yourself that you think is essential to your responses.
- **How Do You Feel?**: This question is asking about your emotional state regarding global warming. Please select one of the options provided that best describes your feelings.
  - **Option 1**: You're concerned about global warming.
  - **Option 2**: You're somewhat concerned, but not very much.
  - **Option 3**: You're not concerned about global warming.
- **Global Warming Awareness**: This question is about your understanding of global warming. Please select all that apply from the provided options.
  - **Option 1**: You're well-informed about global warming.
  - **Option 2**: You have some knowledge, but not a lot.
  - **Option 3**: You're not very aware of global warming.
- **Climate Change Impact**: This question is asking about the impact of global warming on your daily life. Please enter a numerical value that best describes your answer.
- **Global Warming Concern**: This is an open-ended question where you can express your concern about global warming in your own words.
- **Climate Change Solutions**: This question is asking about the actions you think can be taken to mitigate global warming. Please select one of the provided options.
  - **Option 1**: You think more research is needed.
  - **Option 2**: You support government policies and regulations.
  - **Option 3**: You believe in individual actions, like recycling and reducing carbon footprint.
- **Survey Conclusion**: This is a note field where you can provide any final thoughts or comments about the survey.
