# Character Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you gain a deeper understanding of your character and behavioral traits. It's meant to be a self-awareness exercise, helping you recognize your strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by completing the form section by section, as each section is designed to guide you through specific areas of character development.
2. For each field, read the label carefully and answer honestly.
3. When completing numerical fields (e.g., Integrity Self-Rating, Resilience Self-Rating), rate yourself on a scale from 1 to 10.
4. For select_one fields (e.g., Primary Motivation Driver, Response to Conflict Style), select the option that best describes you.
5. Text fields (e.g., Recent Example of Personal Integrity, Additional Reflection Comments) require a brief description or example.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name (2)**: Enter your first and last name as you prefer to be addressed.
* **Integrity Self-Rating (4)**: Rate how consistently you act according to your values.
* **Empathy Self-Rating (5)**: Rate how well you understand and share the feelings of others.
* **Resilience Self-Rating (6)**: Rate how quickly you recover from setbacks.
* **Honesty Self-Rating (7)**: Rate how truthful you are with yourself and others.
* **Self-Discipline Rating (8)**: Rate how well you control your impulses and stay focused.
* **Primary Motivation Driver (10)**: Select what drives your actions most often. Choose from: Personal Achievement, Helping Others, Seeking Knowledge, Security and Stability, or Creativity and Expression.
* **Response to Conflict Style (11)**: Select how you typically handle disagreement. Choose from: Collaborative (Win-Win), Competitive (Win-Lose), Avoidant, Accommodating, or Compromising.
* **Area of Character Needing Growth (13)**: Identify which trait you'd most like to improve.
* **Recent Example of Personal Integrity (14)**: Describe a situation where you stood by your values.
* **Impact on Others - Self-Perception (15)**: Describe how you think others would describe your character.
* **Accuracy Confirmation Self-Evaluation (17)**: Confirm that you've answered these questions thoughtfully and honestly. Select: I Confirm and Submit, or Other (if you have a compelling reason to do so).
* **Date of Completion (18)**: Enter the current date.
* **Additional Reflection Comments (19)**: Share any other thoughts on your personal character development.
* **Personal Motto or Guiding Principle (20)**: Enter a phrase that encapsulates your values and guiding principles.

Note: Remember to submit the form after reviewing and confirming your answers.
