# new_product_launch_consumer_opinion_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data from consumers regarding their opinion on the new product launch.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your product launch information in the "Product Launch Info" field on page 1.
2. Select your purchase intention from the options in the "Purchase Intent" field on page 1.
3. Enter your segment information in the "Segment Info" field on page 1.
4. Provide feedback details in the "Feedback Details" field on page 1.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field on page 1.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field on page 1.
7. Enter the website in the "Website" field on page 1.
8. Enter the name of the product in the "Product Name" field on page 2.
9. Describe the product in the "Product Description" field on page 2.
10. Enter the launch date of the product in the "Launch Date" field on page 2.
11. Enter the time spent on the product in the "Time Spent" field on page 2.
12. Indicate how frequently you purchase the product in the "Purchase Frequency" field on page 2.
13. Enter the amount you spend on the product in the "Purchase Amount" field on page 2.
14. Rate the product from the options in the "Product Rating" field on page 3.
15. Indicate where you purchased the product in the "Purchase Location" field on page 3.
16. Indicate whether you purchased the product in the "Product Purchased" field on page 3.
17. Indicate whether you would recommend the product in the "Product Recommended" field on page 3.
18. Indicate your purchase satisfaction level in the "Purchase Satisfaction" field on page 3.
19. Indicate whether you would recommend the product in the "Product Would Recommend" field on page 3.
20. Enter the launch date of the product in the "Product Launch" field on page 4.
21. Describe the type of product launch in the "Product Launch Type" field on page 4.
22. Describe the stage of product launch in the "Product Launch Stage" field on page 4.
23. Describe the phase of product launch in the "Product Launch Phase" field on page 4.
24. Indicate the status of the product launch in the "Product Launch Status" field on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Launch Info** (1): Enter a brief description of the product launch.
* **Purchase Intent** (2): Select your intentions for purchasing this product.
	+ Strongly Agree: You intend to purchase this product.
	+ Somewhat Agree: You somewhat intend to purchase this product.
	+ Neither Agree Nor Disagree: You don't intend to purchase this product.
	+ Strongly Disagree: You don't intend to purchase this product.
* **Segment Info** (3): Enter information about the segment this product is targeted at.
* **Feedback Details** (4): Provide any feedback or suggestions about the product.
* **Email** (5): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (6): Enter your phone number.
* **Website** (7): Enter the website where you purchased the product.
* **Product Name** (8): Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Description** (9): Describe the product.
* **Launch Date** (10): Enter the launch date of the product.
* **Time Spent** (11): Enter the time spent on the product.
* **Purchase Frequency** (12): Indicate how frequently you purchase the product.
* **Purchase Amount** (13): Enter the amount spent on the product.
* **Product Rating** (14): Rate the product from the options.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Purchase Location** (15): Indicate where you purchased the product.
* **Product Purchased** (16): Indicate whether you purchased the product.
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Product Recommended** (17): Indicate whether you would recommend the product.
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Purchase Satisfaction** (18): Indicate your purchase satisfaction level.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Product Would Recommend** (19): Indicate whether you would recommend the product.
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Product Launch** (20): Enter the launch date of the product.
* **Product Launch Type** (21): Describe the type of product launch.
* **Product Launch Stage** (22): Describe the stage of product launch.
* **Product Launch Phase** (23): Describe the phase of product launch.
* **Product Launch Status** (24): Indicate the status of product launch.
	+ On Track
	+ Delayed
	+ Unknown
	+ Completed
	+ Abandoned
