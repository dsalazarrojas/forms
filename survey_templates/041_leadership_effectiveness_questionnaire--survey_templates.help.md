# Leadership Effectiveness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Effectiveness Questionnaire is designed to gather information about an individual's leadership behaviors, skills, and characteristics. This form helps assess an individual's effectiveness in leading their team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully to understand its purpose and relevance to the questionnaire.
2. For each section, select or enter the relevant information as instructed.
3. Ensure you answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (1)** (`Introduction`, `note`): This section is a note-taking field where you can write any comments or additional information about yourself or your leadership role.
* **Leadership Behaviors (2)** (`leadership_behaviors`, `select_multiple`): Select all the leadership behaviors you frequently exhibit, such as `Leading by Example`, `Empowering and Motivating`, and `Adapting and Adapting`. Choose as many or as few as apply.
* **Leadership Skills (3)** (`leadership_skills`, `select_one`): Choose one of the following leadership skills that you believe you possess: `Analytical`, `Conceptual`, `Interpersonal`, or `Results-Oriented`.
* **Leader Characteristics (4)** (`leader_characteristics`, `select_multiple`): Select the leader characteristics that you embody, such as `Authentic`, `Charismatic`, or `Results-Driven`. 
* **Feedback Sources (5)** (`feedback_sources`, `select_multiple`): Choose the sources from whom you typically receive feedback, including `Colleagues`, `Direct Reports`, and `Peers`.
* **Leadership Development (6)** (`leadership_development`, `number`): Enter a number to represent how many times you receive feedback on your leadership skills, such as `1`, `2`, `3`, and so on.
* **Feedback Frequency (7)** (`feedback_frequency`, `select_one`): Choose the frequency at which you receive feedback, including `Less than once a month`, `More than once a month`, `More than once a week`, and `More than once a day`.
* **Communication Methods (8)** (`communication_methods`, `select_multiple`): Select the methods you use to communicate with your team, including `One-on-ones` and `Meetings`. Choose as many or as few as apply.
* **Leadership Skills Gap (9)** (`leadership_skills_gap`, `text`): Enter a brief description of areas where you feel you have skills gaps or areas that need improvement.
* **Action Steps (10)** (`action_steps`, `text`): Enter a brief description of steps you plan to take to improve your leadership skills.
* **Action Plan (11)** (`action_plan`, `text`): Enter a brief description of your plan to develop your leadership skills.
