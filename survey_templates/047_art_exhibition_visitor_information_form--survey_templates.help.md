# Art Exhibition Visitor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about visitors to the art exhibition, helping us understand their experiences and improve future exhibitions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will help us contact you if needed.
3. Fill out the "Date of Visit" field with the date you visited the exhibition.
4. Select your age group from the "Age Group" dropdown list.
5. Choose the type of visitor you are (e.g., first-time visitor, regular visitor, etc.) from the "Type of Visitor" dropdown list.
6. Select how long you spent in the exhibition from the "How Long Did You Spend in Exhibition" dropdown list.
7. Share your favorite pieces or artists in the "Your Favorite Pieces or Artists" field.
8. Rate your overall impression of the exhibition (e.g., excellent, fair, etc.) from the "Overall Impression of Exhibition" dropdown list.
9. If you have any feedback, suggestions, or comments about the exhibition, please use the "Additional Comments or Feedback" field.
10. Finally, select your interest in returning for future exhibitions and whether you'd like to subscribe to our newsletter and be tagged on social media.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`visitor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date of Visit** (`visit_date`, date, required): Fill out the date you visited the exhibition.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age group from Under 18, 18-25, 26-35, etc.
* **Type of Visitor** (`visit_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of visitor you are (e.g., first-time visitor, regular visitor, etc.).
* **How Long Did You Spend in Exhibition** (`visit_duration`, select_one, required): Select how long you spent in the exhibition from Less than 30 minutes, 30 minutes to 1 hour, etc.
* **Your Favorite Pieces or Artists** (`favorite_pieces`, text, required): Share your favorite pieces or artists you enjoyed during the exhibition.
* **Overall Impression of Exhibition** (`overall_impression`, select_one, required): Rate your overall impression of the exhibition from Excellent, Very good, Fair, etc.
* **Did You Learn Something New** (`learned_something`, select_one, required): Indicate if you learned something new during the exhibition.
* **Educational Value of Exhibition** (`educational_value`, select_one, required): Rate the exhibition's educational value from Very educational, Somewhat educational, etc.
* **Subcribe to Newsletter or Updates** (`newsletter_subscription`, select_one, required): Select whether you'd like to subscribe to our newsletter or updates.
* **May We Tag You on Social Media** (`social_sharing`, select_one, required): Indicate if you're willing to be tagged on social media about your visit.
* **Suggestions for Future Exhibitions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, required): Share any suggestions you have for future exhibitions.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the exhibition.
* **Interest in Returning for Future Exhibitions** (`return_interest`, select_one, required): Select your level of interest in returning for future exhibitions from Very interested, Interested, etc.
* **Interest in Returning for Future Exhibitions** (`return_interest`, select_one, required): Select your level of interest in returning for future exhibitions from Very interested, Interested, etc.
