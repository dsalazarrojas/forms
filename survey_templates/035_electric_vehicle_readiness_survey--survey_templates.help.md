# Electric Vehicle Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electric Vehicle Readiness Survey is designed to gather information about your interest, circumstances, and preferences related to adopting an electric vehicle. This data will help us better understand the current state of electric vehicle adoption and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question carefully and honestly, as this information will help us tailor our resources and services to your needs.
2.  Please select all that apply when asked to choose multiple options.
3.  For numerical fields, please provide your approximate value (e.g., 50 for "50" or 200 for "200,000").
4.  If you're unsure or do not know the answer to a question, choose the "Not Very Interested" or "Not Available" option where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the form, explaining its purpose and instructions.
*   **What is the primary interest for wanting to adopt an electric vehicle?**: Please select your level of interest in adopting an electric vehicle, ranging from "Not Interested" to "Very Interested."
*   **What is your current charging situation?**: Please choose all applicable charging locations you have access to (e.g., "Home", "Work", "Public").
*   **What outreach needs do you have regarding electric vehicles?**: Please select all applicable ways you would like to receive more information about electric vehicles (e.g., "Educational Materials", "Events", "Workshops").
*   **What type of electric vehicle do you own or interested in owning?**: Please select the type of electric vehicle that suits your needs (e.g., "Car", "Van", "Truck").
*   **What is your monthly household income?**: Enter your approximate monthly household income (e.g., 50,000 for "50,000" or 200,000 for "200,000").
*   **What is the approximate cost of the electric vehicle you are interested in?**: Enter the approximate cost of the electric vehicle you're interested in (e.g., 50,000 for "50,000" or 200,000 for "200,000").
*   **How much time do you have available to charge your vehicle daily?**: Please select the amount of time you have to charge your vehicle each day.
*   **Do you have a vehicle warranty?**: Please select whether you have a vehicle warranty or not.
*   **What is your vehicle's current mileage?**: Enter your vehicle's current mileage (e.g., 50,000 for "50,000" or 200,000 for "200,000").
*   **Where do you park your vehicle currently?**: Please choose all applicable parking locations (e.g., "Home", "Work", "Public").
*   **What insurance coverage do you have on your vehicle?**: Please select all applicable insurance coverages for your vehicle (e.g., "Full Coverage", "Liability Only", "None").
*   **How do you plan to purchase an electric vehicle?**: Please select how you plan to purchase an electric vehicle (e.g., "Lease", "Buy", "Other").
*   **How would you prefer to purchase an electric vehicle?**: Please select your preferred method of purchasing an electric vehicle (e.g., "Direct Purchase", "Dealership", "Online", "Other").
*   **What are you willing to pay for a new electric vehicle?**: Enter the maximum amount you're willing to pay for a new electric vehicle.
*   **How far is the charging station from your home?**: Enter the distance of the nearest charging station from your home.
*   **What would you prefer as a charging rate?**: Enter the preferred charging rate.
*   **How often do you charge your vehicle?**: Please select the frequency of charging your vehicle.
*   **What is the preferred location for charging?**: Please select the preferred charging location.
*   **How ready are you to adopt an electric vehicle?**: Please select your level of readiness to adopt an electric vehicle.
*   **Would you be willing to take an electric vehicle on a road trip?**: Please select whether you're willing to take an electric vehicle on a road trip.
*   **Number of household members living in the household?**: Enter the approximate number of household members living with you.
*   **What is the approximate annual household income (USD)?**: Enter your approximate annual household income (e.g., 50,000 for "50,000" or 200,000 for "200,000").
*   **What type of electric vehicle would you be interested in?**: Please select the type of electric vehicle you're interested in (e.g., "Car", "Van", "Truck").
*   **What is the approximate cost of the electric vehicle you are interested in?**: Enter the approximate cost of the electric vehicle you're interested in (e.g., 50,000 for "50,000" or 200,000 for "200,000").
