# Contemporary World Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contemporary World Survey is designed to collect information from individuals on their perspectives on global issues, demographics, and educational background. This survey aims to gather diverse views and opinions on various topics to help us better understand the global community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section and ensure you have completed all required fields.
2. For select_one fields (e.g., Age Range, Gender, Global Region), select the option that best matches your answer.
3. For select_one fields with numerical options (e.g., Climate Change, Outlook on Global Economic Trends, Perspective on International Relations), select the option that best represents your perception.
4. For the "Higest Level of Education Completed" field, select the option that matches your highest level of education achieved.
5. Answer "True" or "False" for questions that ask for a yes/no response.
6. If you have any additional comments or details, please use the "Any additional details or comments?" text field.
7. Finally, confirm that the information provided is accurate, and indicate your preference for follow-up research and anonymity in our reports.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Demographics --**
	+ **Age Range (1)**: Select the range that best matches your age.
	+ **Gender (3)**: Choose your gender identity.
	+ **Global Region (4)**: Select the region where you are located.
* **-- Global Issues and Perspectives --**
	+ **How high is climate change as a global priority? (6)**: Rate how high you consider climate change as a priority, from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
	+ **Outlook on Global Economic Trends (7)**: Select your perception of global economic trends.
	+ **Perspective on International Relations (8)**: Rate how you view international relations, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
	+ **Importance of Cultural Diversity and Inclusion (9)**: Rate how important you consider cultural diversity and inclusion, from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **-- Educational Insights and Research --**
	+ **Highest Level of Education Completed (11)**: Select the highest level of education you have achieved.
* **-- Global Issues Awareness --**
	+ **How aware are you of various global issues? (13)**: Rate how aware you are of global issues, from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **-- Confirmation --**
	+ **Survey Completed? (15)**: Confirm that you have completed the survey.
* **-- Additional Information --**
	+ **Any additional details or comments? (16)**: Share any additional comments or details you would like to provide.
* **Confirmation and Preferences**
	+ **I confirm that the information provided is accurate (17)**: Confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
	+ **May we contact you for follow-up research? (19)**: Indicate if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up research.
	+ **Would you like to remain anonymous in our reports? (20)**: Choose if you prefer to remain anonymous in our reports.
* **Survey Submission Date (18)**: Enter the date you submitted the survey.
* **Interest in Future Research Participation (12)**: Indicate if you are interested in participating in future research.
* **Signature Confirmation (17)**: Agree or disagree with the survey terms.
* **Would you like to remain anonymous (20)**: Choose if you prefer to remain anonymous in our reports.
