# Abortion Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about respondents' attitudes, experiences, and perspectives on abortion. It covers various aspects, including demographic details, attitudes and beliefs, personal experiences, and policy perspectives. Please answer honestly and accurately to provide valuable insights for the research.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select your age range** (`age_range`): Choose from the available age ranges: 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, or 65 or older.
2. **Choose your gender** (`gender`): Select one of the available options: Female, Male, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say.
3. **Indicate your highest level of education completed** (`education_level`): Select from the available options: High school or equivalent, Some college, Associate degree, Bachelor's degree, Graduate or professional degree, or Prefer not to say.
4. **Express your general stance on abortion** (`general_stance`): Choose one of the available options: Should be legal in all cases, Should be legal in most cases, Should be illegal in most cases, Should be illegal in all cases, or Unsure or no opinion.
5. **Select circumstances where abortion should be permitted** (`acceptable_circumstances`): Choose all that apply from the available options: Risk to the life of the mother, Severe fetal abnormality, Cases of rape or incest, Economic hardship, Personal choice at any time, or None of the above.
6. **Rate the importance of access to reproductive healthcare services** (`access_importance`): Select one of the available options: Very important, Somewhat important, Neutral, Not very important, or Not important at all.
7. **Identify your primary source of information about reproductive health topics** (`information_source`): Choose all that apply from the available options: Healthcare provider, Online sources, Friends or family, News media, Religious or community organizations, or Academic or research publications.
8. **Do you personally know someone who has had an abortion?** (`know_someone`): Select one of the available options: Yes, No, or Prefer not to say.
9. **Assess the availability of counseling and support services** (`counseling_availability`): Choose one of the available options: Yes, adequate services exist, Some services exist but not enough, No, services are lacking, or I am not sure.
10. **Indicate the government's role in regulating abortion** (`government_role`): Select one of the available options: No regulation - personal decision only, Some regulation with exceptions, Strict regulation, Complete prohibition, or Unsure.
11. **Support for comprehensive reproductive health education programs** (`education_programs`): Choose one of the available options: Strongly support, Somewhat support, Neutral, Somewhat oppose, or Strongly oppose.
12. **Share any additional thoughts or perspectives** (`additional_thoughts`): Type any additional comments or insights you would like to share.
