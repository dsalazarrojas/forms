# Judaism Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Judaism Knowledge Quiz is a form designed to assess the knowledge of individuals about Judaism. This form aims to gather information about the user's basic knowledge of Judaism, including their name and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please use the standard format for email addresses (e.g., user@example.com).
4. If you wish to, provide your score on the "score" field, which is not required.
5. If you prefer, submit the date and time you completed the form in the "date_submitted" and "time_submitted" fields. This is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email Address** (email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the standard format (e.g., user@example.com).
* **Score** (score, number, required/optional): Enter your score on a scale of 0-100, if you wish to report one.
* **Date Submitted** (date_submitted, date, required/optional): If you submit a date, it will be recorded as the date you completed the form. This is not required.
* **Time Submitted** (time_submitted, time, required/optional): If you submit a time, it will be recorded as the time you completed the form. This is not required.
