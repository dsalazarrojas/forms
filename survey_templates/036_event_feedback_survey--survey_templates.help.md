# event_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on events to help organizers improve their events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your honest feedback.
2. Make sure to provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
3. Be as detailed as possible when answering each question.
4. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event feedback** (event_feedback, text, required): Describe your experience at the event.
* **Venue satisfaction** (venue_satisfaction, text, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the venue.
* **Speaker satisfaction** (speaker_satisfaction, text, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the speakers.
* **Overall satisfaction** (overall_satisfaction, text, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
* **Event satisfaction** (event_satisfaction, text, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the event's organization.
* **Event organizer** (event_organizer, text, required): Provide the name of the event organizer.
* **Event participants** (event_participants, text, required): Provide a list of attendees you interacted with.
* **Event date** (event_date, date, optional): Select the date of the event.
* **Event time** (event_time, time, optional): Select the time of the event.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Feedback comment** (feedback_comment, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback about the event.

Please submit your feedback, and we'll get in touch with you to follow up on your comments.
