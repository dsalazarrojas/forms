# Industrial Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is intended to measure employees' perceptions of their work environment, job satisfaction, communication, manager support, employee satisfaction, engagement, and communication channels. Your honest and accurate responses will help the organization better understand their internal dynamics and make informed decisions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure the survey is completed accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and respond accordingly to the best of your ability.
2. Please select the most applicable options for each field based on your personal experience and knowledge.
3. Do not hesitate to skip any questions that do not apply to your situation.
4. Answer each field truthfully and without bias.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, text, optional): Describe your current work environment. This includes your physical workspace, work culture, and overall atmosphere.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your job satisfaction by choosing "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional): Provide a short description of your communication experience within the organization. Please note that this field is for your personal account of how you feel about the communication style within your workplace.
* **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether your manager provides the support you need to perform your job effectively by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each aspect.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your overall employee satisfaction by selecting all applicable options.
* **Engagement** (`engagement`, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of engagement with your work by choosing "Yes" or "No".
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select the communication channels you use to stay updated and informed about organizational news and policies.
