# Secret Santa Rescheduling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to collect information from participants for Secret Santa rescheduling purposes. It helps organizers or administrators to understand individual participant availability and preferences, making it easier to reschedule events or appointments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the participant name.
2. Select the days you are available.
3. (Optional) If applicable, enter the start and end dates of the event/appointment.
4. (Optional) Choose the format of the event (In-person or Virtual).
5. Add any additional notes or information (if needed).
6. Enter your preferred date for the event/appointment.
7. Enter your email address for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant.
* **Availability**: Select the days you are available for the event/appointment. This can be multiple days.
* **Start Date**: (Optional) If you know the start date of the event/appointment, enter it here.
* **End Date**: (Optional) If you know the end date of the event/appointment, enter it here.
* **Event Format**: (Optional) Choose whether the event is In-person or Virtual.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your availability or preferences.
* **Preferred Date**: Enter your preferred date for the event/appointment.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Check the event format to ensure you select the correct format.
- Double-check your availability and preferred date to ensure accuracy.
- Enter your email address correctly to avoid any communication issues.
