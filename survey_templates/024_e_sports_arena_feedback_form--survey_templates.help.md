# E Sports Arena Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The E Sports Arena Feedback Form is a tool used to gather feedback from customers about their experiences at E Sports Arena events. This form will help us understand what we are doing well and where we can improve to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Event Name, Event Date, Event Attendees, Email, Contact Number, and Name. These fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and are essential for us to understand who is providing the feedback.
2. Provide feedback in the Feedback field about your experience with the E Sports Arena.
3. If you wish to provide suggestions or comments, type them in the Suggestions and Comments fields respectively.
4. Rate your experience by selecting one of the five available options in the Rate Your Experience field.
5. Optionally, provide a Review of your experience in the Review field.
6. Finally, submit the form to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **E-Sports Event Details**: This field is not directly related to your feedback. You can skip it if you wish.
* **Event Name\***: This field requires the name of the event you attended. Please enter the exact name of the event as it was displayed or announced.
* **Event Date\***: Enter the date of the event you attended. Use the YYYY-MM-DD format to ensure accurate entry.
* **Event Time\***: This field is optional, but if you know the start and end time of the event, you can enter it here.
* **Venue\***: If you know the name of the venue where the event took place, you can enter it here.
* **Event Attendees\***: Please list the number of attendees you saw at the event.
* **Feedback**: This is where you provide detailed feedback about your experience with the E Sports Arena. Be as specific as possible about what happened, and what we could improve on.
* **E-Sports Game Details**: This field is optional, but if you want to provide additional details about the game or event, you can enter them here.
* **Overall Experience**: If you wish to provide a brief summary of your experience with the E Sports Arena, this is the place to do so.
* **Suggestions**: This field is for any suggestions you have for improving our services.
* **Comments**: This field is for any additional comments you may want to make about your experience.
* **Email\***: Please provide your email address so we can get back to you about your feedback.
* **Contact Number\***: Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you.
* **Name\***: Enter your name so we can associate it with your feedback.
* **Rate Your Experience**: Select one of the five available options to rate your overall experience with the E Sports Arena.
* **Review**: This field is optional, but it's a great place to provide more detailed feedback about your experience.
* **Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Unsatisfied, Very Unsatisfied**: These options represent your overall experience. Select one that best describes how you felt about your experience.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and detailed feedback to help us improve our services.
* If you have any concerns or issues that you didn't report in the Feedback field, please mention them in the Comments field.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please enter them in the Suggestions field.
* If you want to be contacted back, please provide your email address and contact number.
* If you have any further comments or questions, please use the Comments field.
