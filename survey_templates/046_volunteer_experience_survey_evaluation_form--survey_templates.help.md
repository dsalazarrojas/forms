# Volunteer Experience Survey Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants or volunteers about their experiences with a program or initiative. The goal is to evaluate and improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct response for each question.
3. Provide additional comments if you have any feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant
* **Participant** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_participant`, `number`, required/optional): Identify yourself as a participant or volunteer.

### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_participant_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.

### What is Your Role?
* **What is Your Role?** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_what_is_your_role`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the following roles:
	+ Participant
	+ Volunteer
	+ Staff

### Program Name
* **Program Name** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_program_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the program you participated in.

### Program Duration
* **Program Duration** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_program_duration`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of months or years you participated in the program.

### Program Type
* **Program Type** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_program_type`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the program's type.

### Program Frequency
* **Program Frequency** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_program_frequency`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the frequency of your program participation (e.g., weekly, monthly, etc.).

### Program Frequency Other
* **Program Frequency Other** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_program_frequency_other`, `text`, required/optional): If the above options do not apply, enter another frequency.

### Were You a Participant Last 12 Months?
* **Were You a Participant Last 12 Months?** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_were_you_a_participant_last_12_months`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unsure

### What Made Your Experience Worth It?
* **What Made Your Experience Worth It?** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_what_made_your_experience_worth_it`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your thoughts on what made your experience with the program valuable.

### What Could We Improve?
* **What Could We Improve?** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_what_could_we_improve`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any suggestions you have for improving the program.

### Were You a Veteran?
* **Were You a Veteran?** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_were_you_a_veteran`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False

### Were You Participating?
* **Were You Participating?** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_were_you_participating`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more of the following:
	+ True
	+ False

### What Made it Hard for You?
* **What Made it Hard for You?** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_what_made_it_hard_for_you`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any challenges you faced while participating in the program.

### What Do You Like?
* **What Do You Like?** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_what_do_you_like`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any positive aspects of your experience with the program.

### What Do You Dislike?
* **What Do You Dislike?** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_what_do_you_dislike`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any negative aspects of your experience with the program.

### Would You Participate Again?
* **Would You Participate Again?** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_would_you_participate_again`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unsure

### Would You Recommend?
* **Would You Recommend?** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_would_you_recommend`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`volunteer_experience_survey_evaluation_form_additional_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional thoughts or feedback you have for the program.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you are unsure about any question, choose the corresponding option.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter it in the "Additional Comments" field.
