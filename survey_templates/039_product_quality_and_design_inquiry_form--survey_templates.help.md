**Product Quality And Design Inquiry Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Product Quality And Design Inquiry Form is a tool used by customers to provide feedback and input on our products. This form helps us assess the quality and design of our products, allowing us to make improvements and better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your honest feedback.
2. Select the product you are inquiring about.
3. Choose a quality rating between 1 and 5 for the product.
4. Choose a design rating between 1 and 5 for the product.
5. Describe your experience with the product and its design in the Product Description and Design Description fields.
6. Provide any feedback you may have in the Feedback field.
7. Upload a product image (if applicable).
8. Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (product_name, text, optional): Please enter the name of the product you are inquiring about.
* **Quality Rating** (quality_rating, number, optional): Choose a rating between 1 and 5 to indicate the overall quality of the product.
* **Design Rating** (design_rating, number, optional): Choose a rating between 1 and 5 to indicate the overall design of the product.
* **Product Description** (product_description, text, optional): Describe your experience with the product, including its features, functionality, and any notable features or issues you've encountered.
* **Design Description** (design_description, text, optional): Describe your experience with the product's design, including its aesthetic, usability, and overall visual appeal.
* **Feedback** (feedback, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have for our team.
* **Product Image** (product_image, text, optional): If you have an image of the product, please upload it to help us better understand your concerns.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed to follow up on your feedback.
