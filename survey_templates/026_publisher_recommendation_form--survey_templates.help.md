# Publisher Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Publisher Recommendation Form is a tool to collect contact information of potential publishers for internal communication and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form to start filling it out.
2. Fill in the required fields with your information.
3. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Author Name** (`author_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name to be associated with the recommendation.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to be contacted for further information or discussion.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to be contacted for further information or discussion.
* **Contact Website** (`contact_website`, text, required: false): Enter your website or portfolio URL to showcase your work.
* **Contact Twitter** (`contact_twitter`, text, required: false): Enter your Twitter handle to be contacted via direct message.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Only submit the form once, as duplicate submissions will lead to confusion and potential issues with communication.
* This form is for internal use only, so do not share your contact information with others without permission.
