# Contract Negotiation Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants in a contract negotiation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the "Lead negotiator" that best describes your role in the negotiation process.
2. Enter your department or team in the "Department" field.
3. Provide the date you received feedback in the "Feedback Date" field.
4. Enter the name of the contract in the "Contract Title" field.
5. Enter the start and end dates of the negotiation process in the "Negotiation Start Date" and "Negotiation End Date" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the name of the other party involved in the negotiation process.
7. Select the final outcome of the negotiation process from the options provided.
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the negotiation process, the preparation quality, communication effectiveness, team collaboration, negotiation strategy, time management, and outcome assessment from the options provided.
9. If you encountered any challenges during the negotiation process, please describe them in the "Challenges Encountered" field.
10. Share any positive aspects of the negotiation process in the "What Went Well" field.
11. Identify areas for improvement for future negotiations in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
12. Provide any key takeaways or lessons learned from the negotiation process in the "Lessons Learned" field.
13. Suggest any recommendations for future negotiations in the "Recommendations" field.
14. If you would use the same approach in future negotiations, indicate your level of certainty from the options provided.
15. Finally, provide any additional feedback or comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (1)**: Enter your full name as you are known in the negotiation process.
* **Participant Role (2)**: Select the role that best describes your position in the negotiation process.
* **Department (3)**: Enter the name of the department or team you belong to.
* **Feedback Date (4)**: Enter the date you received feedback or the date of the feedback session.
* **Contract Title (5)**: Enter the name of the contract in question.
* **Negotiation Start Date (6)**: Enter the date the negotiation process began.
* **Negotiation End Date (7)**: Enter the date the negotiation process ended.
* **Other Party (8)**: Enter the name of the other party involved in the negotiation process.
* **Negotiation Outcome (9)**: Select the final outcome of the negotiation process.
* **Overall Satisfaction (10)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the negotiation process from the provided options.
* **Preparation Quality (11)**: Rate the quality of the preparation for the negotiation process from the provided options.
* **Communication Effectiveness (12)**: Rate the effectiveness of the communication during the negotiation process from the provided options.
* **Team Collaboration (13)**: Rate the level of collaboration between team members during the negotiation process from the provided options.
* **Negotiation Strategy (14)**: Rate the effectiveness of the negotiation strategy from the provided options.
* **Time Management (15)**: Rate the time management during the negotiation process from the provided options.
* **Outcome Assessment (16)**: Rate the overall outcome of the negotiation process from the provided options.
* **Challenges Encountered (17)**: Describe any challenges or difficulties encountered during the negotiation process.
* **What Went Well (18)**: Share any positive aspects of the negotiation process.
* **Areas for Improvement (19)**: Identify areas that could be improved for future negotiations.
* **Lessons Learned (20)**: Provide any key takeaways or lessons learned from the negotiation process.
* **Recommendations (21)**: Suggest any recommendations for future negotiations.
* **Would Use Same Approach (22)**: Indicate your level of certainty about using the same approach in future negotiations.
* **Additional Comments (23)**: Provide any additional feedback or comments.
