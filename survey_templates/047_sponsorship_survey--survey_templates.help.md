# Sponsorship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about sponsorship agreements, including contact details, budget, and terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Email address to ensure we can contact you.
3. Enter your Phone Number, including your office and mobile numbers.
4. Choose the type of sponsorship you are applying for (Sports, Cultural, Educational, Non-profit, or Other).
5. Enter the name of your sponsor.
6. Select the start and end dates of your sponsorship period.
7. Enter the purpose of your sponsorship.
8. Describe your project or event in the Description field.
9. Enter your budget for the sponsorship.
10. Enter the contact person's name and details.
11. Enter your organization's name and address.
12. Enter your website.
13. Enter the phone number of your office.
14. Enter your mobile phone number.
15. Enter your office email address.
16. Enter your mobile email address.
17. Add any additional Notes about your sponsorship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (Office)**: Enter your office phone number.
* **Phone Number (Mobile)**: Enter your mobile phone number.
* **Sponsor Type**: Choose the type of sponsorship you are applying for (Sports, Cultural, Educational, Non-profit, or Other).
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of your sponsor.
* **Start Date**: Select the start date of your sponsorship period.
* **End Date**: Select the end date of your sponsorship period.
* **Purpose**: Enter the purpose of your sponsorship.
* **Description**: Describe your project or event.
* **Budget**: Enter your budget for the sponsorship.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Address**: Enter your organization's address.
* **Website**: Enter your website.
* **Phone (Office)**: Enter the phone number of your office.
* **Phone (Mobile)**: Enter your mobile phone number.
* **Email (Office)**: Enter your office email address.
* **Email (Mobile)**: Enter your mobile email address.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your sponsorship.
* **Terms and Conditions 1**: Enter the terms and conditions of your sponsorship.
* **Terms and Conditions 2**: Enter the terms and conditions of your sponsorship.
* **Terms and Conditions 3**: Enter the terms and conditions of your sponsorship.
* **Terms and Conditions 1**: This field is for terms and conditions 1.
* **Terms and Conditions 2**: This field is for terms and conditions 2.
* **Terms and Conditions 3**: This field is for terms and conditions 3.
