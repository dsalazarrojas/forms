# Team Building Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights into team members' experiences and perceptions about their work team. It will help you understand how well you know your team members, how you collaborate, and what you value most about your team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question with a brief description or a few words.
2. You can use the "Hint" field as a guide if you need help with your answer.
3. You can add any additional comments or observations about your team in the "Additional Comments" section at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **How well do you know your team members** (`team_building_survey_1`): This question asks about the level of familiarity and understanding you have with your team members. 
- **How well do you work with your team** (`team_building_survey_2`): This question is about the quality of collaboration and communication within your team. 
- **How often do you communicate with your team** (`team_building_survey_3`): This question explores the frequency and effectiveness of team communication. 
- **How often do you collaborate with your team** (`team_building_survey_4`): This question is about the level of teamwork and collaboration on tasks and projects. 
- **How satisfied are you with your team** (`team_building_survey_5`): This question measures your level of satisfaction with your team's performance and dynamics. 
- **What do you value most about your team** (`team_building_survey_6`): This question helps to identify what aspects of the team are most valuable or important to you. 
- **How do you resolve conflicts with your team** (`team_building_survey_7`): This question is about conflict resolution and handling disagreements within the team. 
- **How do you handle feedback from team members** (`team_building_survey_8`): This question is about how you receive and use feedback from your team members. 

## Tips
Please answer honestly and thoroughly, and provide as much detail as possible for each question. This will help you and your team gain a better understanding of each other's perspectives and improve your team's performance.
