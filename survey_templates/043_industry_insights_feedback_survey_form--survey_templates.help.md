# industry_insights_feedback_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on your business and industry experiences. It is essential that you answer all questions truthfully and accurately to help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1.  Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are marked as required.
2.  Select the correct options for each field, according to your experiences.
3.  Leave any notes or comments in the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **demographic_info** (`demographic_info`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes" if your company has been in operation for more than 2 years.
*  **company_size** (`company_size`, select_multiple, required): Choose all services your company offers.
*  **company_type** (`company_type`, select_multiple, optional): You may choose from a variety of services your company provides. This option is not required.
*  **industry_experience** (`industry_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the services your company offers or has experience with.
*  **services** (`services`, select_multiple, optional): If your company offers any of these services, please mark them accordingly.
*  **areas_of_focus** (`areas_of_focus`, select_multiple, required): Please indicate the areas your company focuses on.
*  **pain_points** (`pain_points`, select_multiple, required): Please select all areas your company faces pain points.
*  **suggestions** (`suggestions`, select_multiple, required): Please suggest solutions or improvements your company would like to see in our industry.
*  **open_ended** (`open_ended`, text, required): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.
*  **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any further comments or questions, please feel free to add them.
*  **contact_info** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, required): Please provide your company's contact information.
*  **confirm** (`confirm`, select_one, required): Please confirm that your responses are accurate.
