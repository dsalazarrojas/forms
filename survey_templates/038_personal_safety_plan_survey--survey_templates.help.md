# Personal Safety Plan Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals assess their personal safety plan, identifying potential dangers, coping strategies, and emergency contacts to ensure their safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Review the options carefully and select the ones that apply to your situation.
3. If you're unsure about any question, you can always ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional)
Enter a brief title or description of your personal safety plan.

### 2. **Name** (`name`, text, optional)
Please provide your name or the name of the person you're completing this form for.

### 3. **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all that apply:
* Single
* Partnersed
* Married
* In a domestic partnership

### 4. **Safety Needs** (`safety_needs`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all that apply:
* Food and water
* Shelter
* Safety from domestic violence
* Safety from natural disasters
* Safety from mental illness
* Safety from physical illness
* Safety from substance abuse
* Safety from legal problems
* Safety from economic abuse

### 5. **Danger Situations** (`danger_situations`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all that apply:
* Home
* School
* Work
* Community center
* Public spaces
* Other

### 6. **Coping Strategies** (`coping_strategies`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all that apply:
* Talk to a friend or family member
* Call a crisis hotline
* Text a crisis hotline
* Visit a local emergency room
* Visit a police station
* Go to a shelter
* Visit a friend or family member
* Visit a neighbor
* Call a crisis line
* Visit a community center

### 7. **Emergency Contact 1** (`emergency_contact1`, text, optional)
Please provide the name and contact information for your primary emergency contact.

### 8. **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact2`, text, optional)

### 9. **Emergency Contact 3** (`emergency_contact3`, text, optional)

### 13. **Emergency Contact 1 Email** (`emergency_contact_email1`, text, optional)

### 14. **Emergency Contact 2 Email** (`emergency_contact_email2`, text, optional)
Please provide the email address of your secondary emergency contact.

### 15. **Emergency Contact 3 Email** (`emergency_contact_email3`, text, optional)

### 16. **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional)
Please describe the next steps you plan to take to ensure your safety and well-being.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate completion of your personal safety plan.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask.
* This form is for your personal safety and well-being; please share it with trusted contacts as needed.
