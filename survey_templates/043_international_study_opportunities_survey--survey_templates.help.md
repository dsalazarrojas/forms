# International Study Opportunities Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students or potential students about their interests and preferences for studying abroad. The information collected will help institutions or organizations provide study opportunities tailored to the needs and interests of the respondents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the country of interest from the list provided.
2. Choose the type of study (Undergraduate, Graduate, Postgraduate, or Other).
3. Select all languages you are proficient in or interested in studying.
4. Choose the reasons for studying abroad that apply to you.
5. Select your preferred location from the list provided.
6. Enter the duration of your intended study period.
7. Provide your date of birth.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Enter the name of your university (if applicable).
11. Enter the name of the program you are interested in (if applicable).
12. Select the semester you would like to start your study.
13. If applicable, enter any other information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_country** (Country, select_one): 
  This field is used to select the country in which you are interested in studying.
* **type_of_study** (Type of Study, select_one): 
  This field is used to select the type of study (Undergraduate, Graduate, Postgraduate, or Other) you are interested in.
* **what_language** (Language, select_multiple): 
  This field is used to select all languages you are proficient in or interested in studying.
* **why_study_abroad** (Reason for Studying Abroad, select_multiple): 
  This field is used to select the reasons why you are interested in studying abroad (Cultural Experience, Personal Growth, Career Advancement, Networking, Financial Gain, Language Skills, Academic Experience, Other, Personal Interest, Family Obligation, Financial Support).
* **preferred_location** (Preferred Location, select_one): 
  This field is used to select your preferred location for studying.
* **study_duration** (Study Duration, number): 
  This field is used to enter the duration of your intended study period.
* **date_of_birth** (Date of Birth, date): 
  This field is used to enter your date of birth.
* **your_email** (Email, email): 
  This field is used to enter your email address.
* **your_phone** (Phone Number, text): 
  This field is used to enter your phone number.
* **name_of_university** (University Name, text): 
  This field is used to enter the name of your university (if applicable).
* **name_of_program** (Program Name, text): 
  This field is used to enter the name of the program you are interested in (if applicable).
* **semester** (Semester, select_one): 
  This field is used to select the semester you would like to start your study.
* **any_other_info** (Any Other Information, note): 
  This field is used to provide any other information that may be relevant to your situation.

## Tips

- Please ensure that you are providing accurate information to the best of your ability.
- If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact the institution or organization providing this form for assistance.
- Double-check your selections before submitting the form to avoid errors.
