# Volunteer Readiness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Readiness Assessment Form is designed to help us understand your volunteer characteristics and preferences. Your responses will help us to determine your suitability for volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all that apply from the options provided for questions with multiple choices.
3. Use the space provided to write a brief answer to the questions that ask for text responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **title**: Title of the form (not editable).
* **skills**: What skills you have or possess. Select all that apply.
	+ Software Development
	+ Data Analysis
	+ Project Management
	+ Communication
	+ Event Planning
	+ Grant Writing
	+ Marketing
* **availability**: How often you are available to volunteer. Select all that apply.
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Flexible
* **interests**: What interests you have. Select all that apply.
	+ Volunteer Management
	+ Community Development
	+ Event Planning
	+ Grant Writing
	+ Marketing
	+ Education
* **volunteer_experience**: Briefly describe your previous volunteer experience.
* **why_volunteer**: Briefly explain why you want to volunteer.
* **volunteer_goals**: What motivates you to volunteer. Select one of the following options:
	+ Help others
	+ Meet new people
	+ Gain experience
* **available_hours**: How many hours you are available to volunteer per week. Select all that apply.
	+ 1-5 hours
	+ 6-10 hours
	+ More than 10 hours
* **contact**: How would you like to be contacted. Select all that apply.
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Text message
* **preferred_communication**: What is your preferred communication style. Select one of the following options:
	+ In-person
	+ Phone
	+ Email
* **volunteer_type**: What type of volunteer you would be interested in. Select all that apply.
	+ One-time event volunteer
	+ Ongoing volunteer
	+ Group volunteer
* **volunteer_frequencies**: How often you would like to volunteer. Select all that apply.
	+ One-time
	+ Ongoing
	+ As needed
* **volunteer_frequency**: How frequently you would like to volunteer. Select all that apply.
	+ 1-2 times per month
	+ 3-4 times per month
	+ More than 4 times per month
* **emergency_contact**: In case of an emergency, who should we contact.
* **volunteer_referral**: If you heard about us through someone, please mention their name or contact information.
* **volunteer_skills**: Briefly describe any skills you have that are not mentioned in the "skills" field.
* **volunteer_interests**: Briefly describe any interests not mentioned in the "interests" field.
* **volunteer_availability**: Briefly describe your availability in a way that is not covered by the "availability" field.
* **volunteer_goals**: Briefly describe what you hope to achieve or gain from volunteering.
* **volunteer_preferences**: Briefly describe any other preferences you have that are not covered by the other fields.
* **volunteer_experience**: Briefly describe any other volunteer experience not covered by the "volunteer_experience" field.
* **volunteer_frequency**: Briefly describe your ideal volunteer frequency that is not covered by the other fields.
* **volunteer_type**: Briefly describe any other volunteer type not covered by the "volunteer_type" field.
* **contact**: Briefly describe any other contact information or preferences not covered by the "contact" field.
* **volunteer_readiness**: This field is for notes and comments from the user. Do not fill this field.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your time and participation in this assessment.
