## Public Speaking Fear Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess your level of public speaking fear. The survey consists of a series of questions that will help you identify your areas of concern and fear when speaking in public.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Be as detailed as possible when answering open-ended questions.
4. Take as much time as you need to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**What fear level do you speak in public?** (2, Select One, Optional)
Indicate how you would describe your level of fear when speaking in public.

* **none**: You do not experience any fear or anxiety when speaking in public.
* **mild**: You experience some anxiety or discomfort when speaking in public, but it is manageable.
* **high**: You experience significant anxiety or discomfort when speaking in public.
