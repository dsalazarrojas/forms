# Event Time Selection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Event Time Selection Survey" is a form designed to gather information about users' preferences regarding events. This form is intended to help organizers understand users' availability and schedule constraints, allowing them to make informed decisions about event timing and format.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the first page of the form, labeled "What date would you like to attend".
2. Choose a date from the available options.
3. Click on the "What time would you like to attend at this date" page.
4. Select the time of day you prefer (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
5. Click on the "Format for this meeting/training/event" page.
6. Select the formats you are available to attend (In-person, Virtual, or Phone).
7. Click on the "What time of day would you prefer for this event" page.
8. Choose the time of day you prefer (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
9. Click on the "How long would you like this event to last" page.
10. Fill in the duration (if applicable).
11. Click on the "What is your availability from 8am-9am" page.
12. Select your availability for that time slot (Available, Busy, or Unknown).
13. Click on the "How many days advance notice do you need for events" page.
14. Select the amount of advance notice you need (1 day, 2 days, or More than 2 days).
15. Click on the "Do you prefer AM or PM" page.
16. Choose your preferred time of day (AM or PM).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What date would you like to attend** (`event_time_selection_survey_first_page`, `date`, required/optional): This field allows you to select a date you would like to attend an event. Choose a date from the available options provided.
* **What time would you like to attend at this date** (`event_time_selection_survey_second_page`, `time`, required/optional): This field is related to the previous one and asks for the time of day you prefer to attend. Select the time of day you are available.
* **Format for this meeting/training/event** (`event_time_selection_survey_third_page`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Here, you can select the formats in which you are available to attend the event (In-person, Virtual, or Phone).
* **What time of day would you prefer for this event** (`event_time_selection_survey_fourth_page`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is about choosing the time of day you prefer for the event. Select either Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **How long would you like this event to last** (`event_time_selection_survey_fifth_page`, `number`, required/optional): If applicable, fill in the duration you prefer for the event.
* **What is your availability from 8am-9am** (`event_time_selection_survey_sixth_page`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Here, select your availability for the time slot 8am-9am (Available, Busy, or Unknown).
* **How many days advance notice do you need for events** (`event_time_selection_survey_seventh_page`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the amount of advance notice you need for events (1 day, 2 days, or More than 2 days).
* **Do you prefer AM or PM** (`event_time_selection_survey_eighth_page`, `select_one`, required/optional): Finally, select your preferred time of day (AM or PM).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately, as the information gathered will help event organizers make informed decisions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the event organizers.
* The form is designed to be completed once for each event, so make sure to fill it out for each event separately.
