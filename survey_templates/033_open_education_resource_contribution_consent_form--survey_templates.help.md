# Open Education Resource Contribution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from contributors to open educational resources, ensuring their consent and details are accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your contributor details in the **Contributor Details** field.
2. Enter the details of the open educational resource you would like to contribute in the **Resource Details** field.
3. Select your preferred options for consent in the **Consent** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
5. Optionally, provide any additional comments in the **Additional Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contributor Details**: This field is where you can enter your name, affiliation, and other details relevant to your contribution.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To collect your personal and professional details for our records.
* **Resource Details**: This field is where you can provide more information about the open educational resource you would like to contribute, such as its title, description, and any relevant links or attachments.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To help us understand the resource you're contributing and how it will be used.
* **Consent**: Here, you can select which options you consent to:
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To indicate your agreement to share your contribution and personal details with us and other users.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to help us contact you with any questions or updates.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To enable us to contact you regarding your contribution.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any further comments or suggestions, please add them here.
	+ Type: Note
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To provide an opportunity for you to share any additional information or feedback.
* **Submit**: Select this option to finalize your submission and confirm your consent.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To complete and submit your form.
* **Cancel**: Select this option to cancel your submission and start over.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To undo your submission and begin again.
