# Beauty Fragrance Memory Association Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to explore the relationship between fragrances and memories, aiming to understand how scents evoke emotions, associations, and experiences in respondents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the corresponding options for each field according to your personal experiences.
3. Use the text fields to provide detailed answers when necessary.
4. Ensure you complete all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information** (`id: 1`): This section is not required, and you can skip it if you're not comfortable providing personal details.
* **Age Group** (`id: 2`): Select the age group that best represents you from the provided options (18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, 56+).
* **Gender** (`id: 3`): Choose the gender that aligns with your identity from the options (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer Not to Say).
* **Fragrance Use Experience** (`id: 4`): Select how frequently you use fragrances, ranging from Minimal (Rarely Use) to Heavy User.
* **Scent Sensitivity Level** (`id: 5`): Indicate your level of scent sensitivity from Low to Very High.
* **Favorite Scent Families** (`id: 7`): Select all the scent families that you enjoy or frequently use (Floral, Fruity, Oriental, Fresh, Woody, Aromatic, Spicy, Citrus).
* **Strongest Scent Memory** (`id: 8`): Describe the scent that strongly comes to mind when thinking about fragrance memories.
* **When Did This Scent Memory Occur** (`id: 9`): Choose the time frame when the scent memory occurred from the options (Childhood, Adolescence, Early Adulthood, Recent Years).
* **What Context or Situation Associated with This Scent** (`id: 11`): Provide a descriptive answer to the context or situation associated with this scent (e.g., person, place, event, emotion).
* **Emotional Response to This Scent** (`id: 12`): Choose the emotional response you associate with this scent from the options (Very Negative, Negative, Neutral, Positive, Very Positive).
* **Level of Nostalgia Associated** (`id: 13`): Indicate the level of nostalgia you experience when thinking about this scent from Not Nostalgic to Extremely Nostalgic.
* **Vividness of Memory When Encountering This Scent** (`id: 14`): Select the clarity of your memory from Vague to Extremely Vivid.
* **How Reliable Is Your Memory of This Scent** (`id: 15`): Choose how reliable you think your memory of this scent is from Not Reliable to Very Reliable.
* **Does This Scent Evoke Multiple Memories** (`id: 17`): Select yes or no, indicating whether this scent evokes multiple memories.
* **Does Your Favorite Fragrance Match Your Personality** (`id: 20`): Choose how well your favorite fragrance matches your personality from Not at All to Completely.
* **Does Fragrance Boost Your Confidence** (`id: 21`): Indicate how significantly fragrance boosts your confidence from Not at All to Very Significantly.
* **Frequency of Involuntary Memory Recall from Scents** (`id: 23`): Choose how often you experience involuntary memory recall from scents from Never to Very Often.
* **Has Scent-Based Marketing Influenced Your Purchase** (`id: 25`): Select whether scent-based marketing has influenced your purchase decisions from Yes, Significantly to No, Never.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Take your time to reflect on your experiences and memories associated with fragrances.
* If you have any concerns or need clarification, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
