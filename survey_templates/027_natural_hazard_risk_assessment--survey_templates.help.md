# Natural Hazard Risk Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Natural Hazard Risk Assessment form is designed to collect information about natural hazards that pose risks to specific areas. This form helps identify potential hazards, their impact, and the necessary actions to mitigate or respond to them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing the form and familiarize yourself with the sections and fields.
2. Click on the "Submit Form" button to begin.
3. Fill out the form section by section, and use the provided hints for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Natural Hazard Risk Assessment (id: 1)** (`note`): This section provides a brief introduction to the form.
* **Event Information (id: 2)** (`text`): Describe the event that triggered this assessment.
* **Natural Hazards (id: 3)** (`select_multiple`):
	+ Select all natural hazards that are present in the affected area.
	+ Examples: earthquake, landslide, flood
* **Affected Areas (id: 4)** (`select_multiple`):
	+ Select all areas that have been affected by the event.
	+ Examples: urban, suburban, rural, industrial, agricultural
* **Risk Level (id: 5)** (`number`): Enter a numerical value representing the level of risk associated with the event.
* **Risk Factors (id: 6)** (`select_one`):
	+ Select the factor that best describes the risk factors.
	+ Examples: physical, social, economic
* **Risk Characteristics (id: 7)** (`text`): Describe the specific characteristics of the risk.
* **Risk Consequences (id: 8)** (`text`): Describe the potential consequences of the event.
* **Mitigation Actions (id: 9)** (`text`): Describe the actions taken to mitigate the risk.
* **Risk Management Plan (id: 10)** (`text`): Describe the plan in place for risk management.
* **Risk Monitoring (id: 11)** (`text`): Describe the monitoring process for risk.
* **Emergency Preparedness (id: 12)** (`text`): Describe the emergency preparedness plan.
* **Response Capacity (id: 13)** (`select_multiple`):
	+ Select the capacity level of the response team.
	+ Examples: high, medium, low
* **Recovery Planning (id: 14)** (`text`): Describe the recovery plan.
* **Review Date (id: 15)** (`date`): Select a date for the form review.
* **Review Time (id: 16)** (`time`): Select a time for the form review.
* **Form Submission (id: 17)** (`select_one`):
	+ Select the status of the form submission.
	+ Examples: submitted, not submitted, submitted pending
* **Submitter (id: 18)** (`email`): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter Phone (id: 19)** (`text`): Enter the submitter's phone number.
* **Submitter Email (id: 20)** (`email`): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter Comments (id: 21)** (`text`): Add any comments about the submission.
* **Submitter Name (id: 22)** (`text`): Enter the submitter's name.
* **Submitter Organization (id: 23)** (`text`): Enter the submitter's organization.
* **Submitter Title (id: 24)** (`text`): Enter the submitter's title.
* **Submitter Notes (id: 25)** (`text`): Add any additional notes.
