# University Admissions Process Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and ratings from applicants on their experience with the university's admissions process. Your responses will help us identify areas of improvement and enhance the overall student experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question based on your actual experience with the university's admissions process.
2. Make sure to select the relevant options that best describe your experience.
3. If you're unsure or cannot find the answer to a question, please provide a brief explanation in the text box provided.
4. Please do not duplicate responses.
