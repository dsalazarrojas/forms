# Stakeholder Engagement Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from stakeholders about their engagement experience with our survey templates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please choose the level of engagement you feel about your stakeholder engagement experience. (Page 1)
	* Select one of the options:
		+ Very engaged
		+ Somewhat engaged
		+ Not very engaged
		+ Not at all engaged
2. Evaluate the form:
	* Select one or multiple options that best describe your experience:
		+ Excellent
		+ Good
		+ Fair
		+ Poor
3. Provide any essential feedback about your experience:
	* Please type your thoughts and suggestions.
4. Choose a survey type that you would like to see next:
	* Select one option:
		+ More of this type of survey
		+ Less of this type of survey
		+ None
5. Customize this form:
	* Select one or multiple options that you would like to see changed:
		+ Customize questions
		+ Add new questions
		+ Change question types
6. Choose data collection methods:
	* Select one or multiple options that you would like to see implemented:
		+ Streamline data collection
		+ Anonymize data
		+ Add new data fields
7. Rate our survey templates:
	* Select one or multiple options that best describe your experience:
		+ Excellent
		+ Good
		+ Fair
		+ Poor

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How engaged do you feel about your stakeholder engagement?** (`stakeholder_engagement`, select_one): This question measures your level of engagement with our survey templates.
* **How would you evaluate this form?** (`evaluation_form`, select_multiple): This question asks you to rate your experience with the form itself.
* **What essential feedback do you have?** (`essential_feedback`, text): Please type your thoughts and suggestions to help us improve our survey templates.
* **Which survey would you like to see next?** (`stakeholder_survey`, select_one): This question helps us know which type of survey you would prefer to see next.
* **How would you like to see this form customized?** (`stakeholder_evaluation_form_customization`, select_multiple): You can suggest changes to the form's questions, add new questions, or change the question types.
* **How would you like to see data collected?** (`stakeholder_data_collection`, select_multiple): You can suggest ways to streamline data collection, anonymize data, or add new data fields.
* **How do you rate our survey templates?** (`stakeholder_survey_templates`, select_multiple): This question asks you to rate your experience with our survey templates.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide constructive feedback to help us improve our survey templates.
* Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time in helping us create better experiences for our stakeholders.
