# Volunteer Needs Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Needs Assessment Survey is a tool used to collect information about your volunteer needs and availability. This survey will help us understand your capacity for volunteer work and match you with opportunities that suit your skills and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly about your volunteer needs and interests.
2. Choose your answers from the provided options.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Availability (2)**: Select the number of hours you are available to volunteer per week.
* **Volunteer Role Interests (3)**: Select all roles you are interested in, such as "Yes", "No", etc.
* **Volunteer Support Feedback (4)**: Share any additional feedback about your volunteer experience or any specific needs you have.
* **Volunteer Support Feedback (5)**: This is a text area where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about your volunteer experience.
* **Volunteer Availability (6)**: Select a specific time range for when you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Needs Assessment Survey (7)**: Select which assessment to complete, for example "Volunteer Needs Assessment 1", "Volunteer Needs Assessment 2", etc.
* **Volunteer Training Placement (8)**: Select all training programs you are interested in, such as "Training 1", "Training 2", etc.
