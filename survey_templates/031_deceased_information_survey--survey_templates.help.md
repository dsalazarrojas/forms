# Deceased Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive details about a deceased individual, helping us maintain accurate and complete records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form carefully and accurately.
2. Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled in to ensure the form can be submitted properly.
3. Date fields (e.g., Date of Death, Date of Birth, Date of Last Known Contact) should be in the format MM/DD/YY.
4. Time fields (e.g., Time of Discharge) should be in the format HH:MM.
5. Text fields can be filled in using the standard keyboard characters without any specific formatting requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id: 1, text, required): Fill in the first name of the deceased individual.
* **Last Name** (id: 2, text, required): Fill in the last name of the deceased individual.
* **Date of Death** (id: 3, date, required): Enter the date of the deceased individual's passing using the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Place of Death** (id: 4, text, required): Specify the place where the deceased individual passed away.
* **Cause of Death** (id: 5, text, required): Provide a brief description of the reason for the deceased individual's passing.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 6, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the deceased individual using the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Birth Place** (id: 7, text, required): Specify the place of birth of the deceased individual.
* **Age at Death** (id: 8, number, required): Fill in the age of the deceased individual at the time of passing.
* **Occupation** (id: 9, text, required): Provide the occupation of the deceased individual.
* **Place of Last Residence** (id: 10, text, required): Specify the last known address of the deceased individual.
* **Time of Death** (id: 11, time, required): Enter the time of the deceased individual's passing using the format HH:MM.
* **Date of Last Known Contact** (id: 12, date, required): Enter the date of last known contact with the deceased individual using the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Place of Last Known Contact** (id: 13, text, required): Specify the place where last contact with the deceased individual occurred.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 14, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (id: 15, text, required): Specify the relationship between the emergency contact and the deceased individual.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 16, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Deceased** (id: 17, text, required): Specify the relationship between the person filling out the form and the deceased individual.
* **Cause of Death Hospitalization** (id: 18, text, required): Provide a brief description of the reason for the deceased individual's hospitalization.
* **Time of Discharge** (id: 19, time, required): Enter the time of discharge from the hospital using the format HH:MM.
* **Place of Discharge** (id: 20, text, required): Specify the place of discharge from the hospital.
* **Medical Condition Before Death** (id: 21, text, required): Describe the medical condition of the deceased individual before passing.
* **Medical Condition at Death** (id: 22, text, required): Specify the medical condition of the deceased individual at the time of passing.
* **Cause of Death** (id: 23, text, required): Provide the reason for the deceased individual's passing.
* **Place Where Death Occurred** (id: 24, text, required): Specify the place where the deceased individual passed away.
* **Additional Comments** (id: 25, note, optional): Provide any additional information or details about the deceased individual, if necessary.

Note: If you need further assistance or clarification on any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us.
