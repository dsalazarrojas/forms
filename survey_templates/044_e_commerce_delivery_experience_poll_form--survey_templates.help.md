We're creating a form called "e_commerce_delivery_experience_poll_form" to collect customer feedback on their delivery experience. This form is designed to gather information on various aspects of their order, including the condition of the packaging and products, timeliness, communication, and overall satisfaction. Please ensure that you are using this form for its intended purpose, and that your responses are honest and respectful of the customer's experience.

# e_commerce_delivery_experience_poll_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect customer feedback on their delivery experience, which will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better services to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose from the options for "Delivery Experience", "Timeliness", "Packaging Condition", and "Overall Experience" (if applicable).
2. Enter your contact information (phone number, email, and name) if you wish to be contacted for further discussion.
3. Provide any additional comments you have about your experience.
4. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Experience** (`delivery_experience`, select_multiple): Choose all the options that apply to your delivery experience:
	+ Yes: The product was delivered on time and as expected.
	+ No: The product was not delivered on time or not as expected.
* **Timeliness** (`timeliness`, number): Rate your experience with the timeliness of the delivery:
	+ Enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very slow" and 5 is "Very fast".
* **Packaging Condition** (`packaging_condition`, select_one): Choose the condition of the packaging:
	+ Yes: The packaging was in good condition.
	+ No: The packaging was not in good condition.
* **Communication** (`communication`, number): Rate your experience with the communication:
	+ Enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very poor" and 5 is "Very good".
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, note): Share your overall experience with the product and delivery.
* **Suggestions For Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text): Provide any suggestions or comments you have for us to improve our services.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, email): Enter your customer email for us to contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter your contact email.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text): Enter your customer name.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date): Enter the date you received the product.
* **Shipping Time** (`shipping_time`, time): Enter the time you received the product.
* **Received Date** (`received_date`, date): Enter the date you received the product.
* **Received Time** (`received_time`, time): Enter the time you received the product.
* **Packaging** (`packaging`, text): Describe the condition of the packaging.
* **Packaging Condition** (`packaging_condition`, text): Describe the condition of the packaging.
* **Product Condition** (`product_condition`, text): Describe the condition of the product.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text): Enter the name of the product.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, text): Enter the ID of the order.
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select_one): Choose your customer type:
	+ Yes: You are a customer.
	+ No: You are not a customer.
* **Customer Gender** (`customer_gender`, select_one): Choose your gender:
	+ Yes: You are male.
	+ No: You are female.
* **Customer Age** (`customer_age`, number): Enter your age.
* **Customer Location** (`customer_location`, text): Enter your location.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Add any additional comments about your experience.
