# Civic Engagement In Youth Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on how youth engage in civic activities, what motivates or demotates them, and how they learn about civic and political issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section according to your experiences and attitudes. You can select all that apply for multiple-choice questions. Don't worry, it's anonymous and confidential.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### -- Youth Information --
- **Age**: Please select your current age.
- **Grade Level or Education Status**: Please select your current grade level or education status.
- **Name**: Please enter your name (optional).
- **Email Address**: Please enter your email address (optional).

### -- Civic Engagement Activities --
- **Civic activities you have participated in**: Select all the civic activities you have participated in.
- **How often do you participate in civic activities**: Select how often you participate in civic activities.
- **What causes or issues interest you most**: Please enter the causes or issues that interest you most.
- **Do you have civic role models or mentors**: Select if you have civic role models or mentors.
- **How do you learn about civic and political issues**: Select all the sources you use to learn about civic and political issues.

### -- Attitudes and Beliefs --
- **Do you believe youth can impact government decisions**: Select your level of agreement.
- **Do you feel a responsibility to participate in civic life**: Select your level of agreement.
- **Do you plan to vote when eligible**: Select your voting intention.

### -- Barriers to Engagement --
- **Barriers to civic engagement**: Select all the barriers that prevent you from participating in civic activities.
- **What support would help you engage more in civic activities**: Please enter any additional support you would like to receive.

### -- Inspiration and Role Models --
- **Do you have civic role models or mentors**: Select if you have civic role models or mentors.

### -- Additional Information --
- **What would increase your civic involvement in the future**: Please enter any additional information on what would increase your civic involvement.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and accurately.
- You can select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.
