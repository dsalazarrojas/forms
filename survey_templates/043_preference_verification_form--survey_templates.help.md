# Preference Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect preference verification data from team members for a team setting. It is used to understand and verify team preferences for contact and updates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, select the team from the options provided in the first page.
2. Fill in your name in the "Member Name" field.
3. Choose your contact preferences from the options provided in the "Contact Preferences" field.
4. Select the type of update you require in the "Update Type" field.
5. Optionally, provide your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team** (`Team`): Select the team you belong to.
	+ Select the team from the provided options (Team A, Team B, Team C).
* **Member Name** (`Member Name`): Please provide your name.
	+ Enter your name in this field.
* **Contact Preferences** (`Contact Preferences`): Choose your preferred contact method.
	+ Select one or more options (Email, Phone, Text).
* **Update Type** (`Update Type`): Select the type of update you require.
	+ Select one or more options (Membership, Service, Other).
* **Email** (`Email`): Provide your email address.
	+ Enter your email address in this field (if you have not already provided it).
