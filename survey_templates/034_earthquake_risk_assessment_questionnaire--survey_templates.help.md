# Earthquake Risk Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Earthquake Risk Assessment Questionnaire is designed to collect data about an earthquake event to determine the level of risk it poses. This form will help identify potential hazards and guide subsequent actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question carefully and honestly to ensure accurate data collection.
2.  Select the correct response for each question, based on your observations and experience.
3.  Make sure to provide detailed descriptions where needed.
4.  If a field is not applicable or not relevant, please mark it as such.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Earthquake Risk Assessment Questionnaire** (Note, required: false): This is the title of the form and serves as a heading.
* **Risk Level** (id: 2, required: false): Select the risk level that best describes the earthquake event. Options are High, Low.
* **Damage Level** (id: 3, required: false): Select multiple options that describe the damage level of the earthquake event. Options are High, Low.
* **Inspection Date** (id: 4, required: false): Enter the date the inspection was performed.
* **High Risk** (id: 5, required: false): Answer Yes or No to indicate if there is a high risk associated with the earthquake event.
* **Medium Risk** (id: 6, required: false): Answer Yes or No to indicate if there is a medium risk associated with the earthquake event.
* **Low Risk** (id: 7, required: false): Answer Yes or No to indicate if there is a low risk associated with the earthquake event.
* **No Risk** (id: 8, required: false): Answer Yes or No to indicate if there is no risk associated with the earthquake event.
* **Damage Description** (id: 9, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the damage caused by the earthquake event.
* **Damage Location** (id: 10, required: false): Provide the location of the damage caused by the earthquake event.
* **Prophets Location** (id: 11, required: false): Provide the location of the Prophet.
* **Prophets Damage** (id: 12, required: false): Provide a description of the damage caused by the Prophet.
* **Earthquake Date** (id: 13, required: false): Enter the date the earthquake event occurred.
* **Earthquake Time** (id: 14, required: false): Enter the time the earthquake event occurred.
* **Inspection Time** (id: 15, required: false): Enter the time the inspection was performed.
* **Earthquake Duration** (id: 16, required: false): Enter the duration of the earthquake event.
* **Magnitude** (id: 17, required: false): Enter the magnitude of the earthquake event.
* **Inspectors Name** (id: 18, required: false): Enter the name of the inspectors who performed the inspection.
* **Inspectors Title** (id: 19, required: false): Enter the title of the inspectors who performed the inspection.
* **Inspectors Organization** (id: 20, required: false): Enter the organization of the inspectors who performed the inspection.
* **Inspectors Phone** (id: 21, required: false): Enter the phone number of the inspectors who performed the inspection.
* **Inspectors Email** (id: 22, required: false): Enter the email of the inspectors who performed the inspection.
* **Prophets** (id: 23, required: false): Enter the location of the Prophet.
* **Safety Team Organization** (id: 24, required: false): Enter the organization of the safety team.
* **Safety Team Phone** (id: 25, required: false): Enter the phone number of the safety team.

Note: This form is for data collection purposes only, and the information gathered will be used to determine the level of risk posed by the earthquake event.
