# Tech Gadget Usability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the usability of a tech gadget. It's a survey to help us understand how well users like our products. This form should only be used for internal purposes within your company. Please do not submit feedback on behalf of others unless you are certain it's their genuine feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief introduction to provide context about your interaction with the tech gadget.
2. Enter the product ID if you have it handy.
3. Enter the name of the tech gadget.
4. Enter the model of the tech gadget, if available.
5. Indicate your level of satisfaction with the tech gadget (Not Satisfied, Satisfied, Very Satisfied, Extremely Satisfied).
6. Provide any suggestions for improvement.
7. Specify any areas where you feel the tech gadget needs improvement.
8. Add any additional comments about your experience.
9. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
10. Enter your phone number for contact purposes (if you have one).
11. Enter your name for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your experience or interaction with the tech gadget.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, text, optional): Enter the product ID if you have it handy. This will help us identify the product you're referring to.
* **Gadget Name** (`gadget_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tech gadget you're providing feedback on.
* **Gadget Model** (`gadget_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the tech gadget, if available. This can help us narrow down the specific version or type you're interacting with.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the tech gadget. This will help us understand how well our product meets your needs.
	+ Not Satisfied: We did not meet your expectations.
	+ Satisfied: You're generally happy with the product, but see room for improvement.
	+ Very Satisfied: You're very happy with the product and its features.
	+ Extremely Satisfied: You love the product and its features.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the tech gadget or our company's products in general.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Specify areas where you feel the tech gadget needs improvement. Choose from:
	+ Hardware
	+ Software
	+ Usability
	+ User Experience
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the tech gadget.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you about your feedback.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Enter your name or alias for contact purposes. This will help us get back to you if we need more information or clarification.
