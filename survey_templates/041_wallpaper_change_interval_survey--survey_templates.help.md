# Wallpaper Change Interval Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on your habits and preferences when it comes to changing your wallpaper.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select how often you change your wallpaper from the options provided.
2. If you've never changed your wallpaper, you can select "Never". If you've changed it monthly, quarterly, yearly, or randomly, you can select the corresponding option.
3. If you've changed your wallpaper, type in a brief description of why you do it.
4. If you're influenced by any of the options provided (e.g. personal preference, decorating style, etc.), select all that apply.
5. If you've changed your wallpaper for any other reason not listed, type it in the "Other" field.
6. Finally, enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you change your wallpaper (`wallpaper_change_interval_id`)**: Select from the options provided how often you change your wallpaper. If you've never changed it, select "Never". If you've changed it monthly, quarterly, yearly, or randomly, select the corresponding option.
* **Why do you change your wallpaper (`reason_for_change`)**: If you've changed your wallpaper, type in a brief description of why you do it. This can include anything from personal preference to decorating style.
* **What influences your choice of wallpaper (`what_influences_choice`)**: Select all that apply from the options provided. This can include personal preference, decorating style, color trends, budget, space constraints, etc.
* **How often do you influence your choice of wallpaper (`how_influenced_by`)**: Type in a brief description of how you're influenced by each of the options provided (e.g. personal preference, decorating style, etc.).
* **How often do you influence your family/friends' choice of wallpaper (`how_influenced_by_family_friends`)**: Type in a brief description of how you're influenced by your family and friends when it comes to choosing wallpaper.
* **How often do you influence your choice of wallpaper by social media (`how_influenced_by_social_media`)**: Type in a brief description of how you're influenced by social media when it comes to choosing wallpaper.
* **How often do you influence your choice of wallpaper by interior designers (`how_influenced_by_interior_designers`)**: Type in a brief description of how you're influenced by interior designers when it comes to choosing wallpaper.
* **Comments (`comments`)**: Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
* **Email (`email`)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (`phone`)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Name (`name`)**: Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Location (`location`)**: Enter your location for contact purposes.
* **Industry (`industry`)**: Enter your industry for contact purposes.
* **Job Title (`job_title`)**: Enter your job title for contact purposes.
* **Company Name (`company_name`)**: Enter your company name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Number (`contact_number`)**: Enter your contact number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`)**: Enter your contact email for contact purposes.
* **Organization (`organization`)**: Enter your organization for contact purposes.
* **Website (`website`)**: Enter your website for contact purposes.
* **How often do you change your wallpaper (`how_often`)**: Type in how often you change your wallpaper.
* **How often do you change your wallpaper frequency (`how_often_frequency`)**: Select from the options provided how often you change your wallpaper.
* **Why do you change your wallpaper (`why`)**: Type in a brief description of why you change your wallpaper.
* **What influences your choice of wallpaper (`what_influences`)**: Type in a brief description of what influences your choice of wallpaper.
