# Self Assessment Tool Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Assessment Tool Form is a tool used to help individuals assess their personal and professional development needs, set goals, and create a roadmap for their self-improvement journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, and Self-Assessment (a numerical value).
2. Select the relevant options for each field, taking into consideration your current situation and goals.
3. Choose one or more options for the select_one fields (Career Goals, Personal Growth, Career Development, Personal Development Goals, Career Development Goals, Strengths Used, and Self-Development Goals).
4. Choose one or more options for the select_multiple fields (Self-Development Topics, Personal Values, Self-Development Topics Used, and Personal Development Topics Used).
5. Enter a numerical value for the Self-Rating Roadmap and Self-Development Plan (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, optional): Please enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Self-Assessment** (5, number, required): Rate your self-assessment (on a scale of 1-5).
* **Career Goals** (6, select_one, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Career advancement, Financial stability, Personal growth.
* **Self-Development Topics** (7, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Communication, Teamwork, Leadership, Time management, Problem solving.
* **Personal Values** (8, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Respect, Honesty, Integrity, Other.
* **Self-Description** (9, note, optional): Enter a short description of yourself.
* **Strengths** (10, select_one, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Creativity, Problem solving, Other.
* **Goals** (11, select_one, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Career advancement, Financial stability, Personal growth, Other.
* **Self-Rating** (12, number, optional): Rate your self-assessment (on a scale of 1-5).
* **Self-Development** (13, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Communication, Teamwork, Leadership, Time management, Problem solving.
* **Career Development** (14, select_one, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Career advancement, Financial stability, Personal growth.
* **Personal Growth** (15, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Communication, Teamwork, Leadership, Time management, Problem solving.
* **Self-Development Roadmap** (16, note, optional): Enter a short description of your self-improvement plan.
* **Strengths Used** (17, select_one, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Creativity, Problem solving, Other.
* **Self-Rating Roadmap** (18, number, optional): Rate your self-improvement plan (on a scale of 1-5).
* **Self-Development Plan** (19, note, optional): Enter a short description of your self-improvement plan.
* **Self-Development Topics Used** (20, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Communication, Teamwork, Leadership, Time management, Problem solving.
* **Self-Development Goals** (21, select_one, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Career advancement, Financial stability, Personal growth, Other.
* **Career Development Goals** (22, select_one, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Career advancement, Financial stability, Personal growth, Other.
* **Personal Development Goals** (23, select_one, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Career advancement, Financial stability, Personal growth, Other.
* **Career Development Plan** (24, note, optional): Enter a short description of your career development plan.
* **Self-Development Plan Used** (25, select_one, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Used, Not Used.

Note: The above fields can be filled out in any order, and the user can skip any field if it is not relevant to their situation. The numerical values for Self-Assessment and Self-Rating Roadmap are on a scale of 1-5. The numerical values for Self-Development Plan and Self-Rating Roadmap are on a scale of 1-5. The other fields require a short text or select one/multiple options input.
