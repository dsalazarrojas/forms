# Men's Health Weight Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on men's health weight management habits. It is intended for men who are looking to manage their weight and improve their overall health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Age (2)**: Enter your age in numbers.
* **Gender (3)**: Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Weight (4)**: Enter your current weight in numbers.
* **Exercise (5)**: Choose how often you exercise from the provided options.
* **Motivation (6)**: This field is a note field for you to write any comments or notes on your motivation for weight management.
* **Sleep Duration (7)**: Enter your average sleep duration in hours.
* **Meal Frequency (8)**: Select how often you eat meals from the provided options.
* **Water Intake (9)**: Enter your average water intake in numbers.
* **Goal (10)**: Choose your current weight goal from the provided options.
* **Deadline (11)**: Enter the date by when you would like to achieve your weight goal.
* **Notes (12)**: This field is for you to write any additional notes on your weight management journey.
* **Gender (3)**: (This field appears to be a duplicate. Please ensure to only fill out this field once)
*   Please note that while this form is designed to be helpful, it is not a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have concerns about your health, please consult a healthcare professional.
