# Gender Inequality Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your experiences and perceptions of gender inequality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your gender identity from the options provided.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability, keeping in mind that the answers will be used for research purposes.
3. Enter any relevant information or comments in the text fields, if applicable.
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you identify as** (`section_1`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to select your gender identity from the options provided.
* **How often do you feel like** (`gender_inequality_questionnaire_form_section_2`, `number`, required: false): This question asks you to rate how often you feel like you are treated unfairly or discriminated against due to your gender. You can select a number between 0 and 4 to indicate the severity of your feelings.
* **Gender inequality is** (`gender_inequality_questionnaire_form_section_3`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to rate the level of gender inequality you feel you experience. You can select one of the options provided.
