# Stratification Theories Assessment - Help Guide

This form assesses your opinions and experiences with social stratification. It's designed to help us understand how you perceive and feel about social stratification in your daily life. Your responses will aid us in identifying areas of concern and inform our efforts to promote equality and fairness.
