# Chess Skill Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your chess skills, experience, and goals. The information collected will be used to tailor a training program or provide guidance to improve your skills.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Select your age and country of residence.
3. Rate your chess rating (official rating) and indicate what type of rating you have.
4. Indicate how long you have been playing chess and select your preferred time control.
5. Rate your skills in tactics, strategy, endgames, openings, and calculation.
6. Indicate how often you study chess and select your study methods.
7. Report your tournament experience and whether you receive chess coaching.
8. Select your chess goals (e.g., improving rating, winning tournaments).
9. Identify your biggest weakness and strength in chess.
10. Express your interest in a training program and indicate areas where you need improvement.
