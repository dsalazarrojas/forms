# Specialty Space Naming Vote - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect opinions from users on how to name a specialty space. The goal is to gather a variety of suggestions and ensure that the final name chosen is well-represented and representative of the community's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as prompted. Each page will ask you to provide a rating or selection. Please make sure to submit your vote and any feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select 1**: Choose your first choice for the specialty space name. You can select one of the three options provided.
	+ Label: Select 1
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Select 2**: Choose your second choice for the specialty space name. You can select one of the three options provided.
	+ Label: Select 2
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Date**: Please enter the date in YYYY-MM-dd format (e.g. 2022-01-01).
	+ Label: Date
	+ Type: date
* **Time**: Please enter the time in HH:MM format (e.g. 14:00).
	+ Label: Time
	+ Type: time
* **Feedback**: If you have any additional comments or feedback regarding the naming process, please enter them here. You can provide text input.
	+ Label: Feedback
	+ Type: note
* **Specialty Space Naming Vote**: Enter your suggested name for the specialty space. Please make sure to follow the guidelines for submission.
	+ Label: Speciality Space Naming Vote
	+ Type: text
* **Rank 1**: Provide your ranking for the first choice. Please rate this item 1-5.
	+ Label: Rank 1
	+ Type: number
* **Rank 2**: Provide your ranking for the second choice. Please rate this item 1-5.
	+ Label: Rank 2
	+ Type: number
* **Rank 3**: Provide your ranking for the third choice. Please rate this item 1-5.
	+ Label: Rank 3
	+ Type: number

## Tips
* Please ensure that your submission is original and not a duplicate of another user's entry.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
