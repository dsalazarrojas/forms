# Tableau Customer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about Tableau customers, aiming to gather feedback and understand their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your title in the "first page" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "contact info" field.
3. Answer the question "customer type" with either "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are a Tableau customer.
4. If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, you can provide more details about the type of customer you are in the "other" field.
5. Enter the year you started using Tableau in the "since" field.
6. Select the month you started using Tableau in the "since month" field.
7. If you want to, select the year you started using Tableau in the "since year" field.
8. Indicate if you have received support from Tableau in the "customer support" field, selecting one or more options from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page (first_page)**: Enter a brief description about yourself.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Provide your contact information, such as your email address and phone number.
* **Customer Type (customer_type)**: Answer "Yes" if you are a Tableau customer, or "No" otherwise.
* **Other (customer_type_other)**: Provide more details about the type of customer you are, if you answered "Yes" to the previous question.
* **Since (customer_since)**: Enter the year you started using Tableau.
* **Since Month (customer_since_month)**: Select the month you started using Tableau.
* **Since Year (customer_since_year)**: Select the year you started using Tableau, if it's not the current year.
* **Customer Support (customer_support)**: Select all the support services you have received or are interested in receiving from Tableau, including "Yes" and/or "No" options.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Answer truthfully to provide valuable feedback.
* If you need help or clarification about any question, feel free to contact us.
