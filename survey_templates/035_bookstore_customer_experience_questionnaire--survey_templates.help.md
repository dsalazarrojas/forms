# Bookstore Customer Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience at the bookstore. Your input is invaluable to help us improve and better serve you and our community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, please select the date you visited the bookstore.
2. If you'd like to provide an email address for follow-up, you can add your email. This is optional.
3. Indicate if this is your first visit or if you're a regular customer.
4. Let us know how often you visit us.
5. Rate the cleanliness and organization of the store.
6. Rate the helpfulness of our staff.
7. Evaluate the book selection and variety.
8. Rate the prices and value of the products.
9. Assess the checkout experience.
10. Indicate your overall satisfaction with your visit.
11. Let us know if you'd recommend our store to others.
12. If you have any areas where we could improve, please select all that apply.
13. If you'd like to provide additional feedback or comments, you can do so in the text field below.
14. If you're okay with us following up with you, please let us know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Visit** (`visit_date`, `date`, required): Please select the date you visited the store.
* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your name, you can do so here. This is optional.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to receive follow-up communication from us, you can add your email address.
* **Is this your first visit** (`first_time_visitor`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if this is your first visit or if you're a regular customer.
* **How often do you visit** (`visit_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you visit us.
* **Store Cleanliness and Organization** (`store_cleanliness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the cleanliness and organization of the store.
* **Staff Helpfulness** (`staff_helpfulness`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the helpfulness of our staff.
* **Product Selection and Variety** (`product_selection`, `select_one`, required): Rate the book selection and variety.
* **Pricing and Value** (`pricing`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the prices and value of the products.
* **Checkout Experience** (`checkout_experience`, `select_one`, required): Rate the checkout experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your overall satisfaction with your visit.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likely_to_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you'd recommend our store to others.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have any areas where we could improve, please select all that apply.
* **Additional Feedback or Comments** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide additional feedback or comments, please do so here.
* **May we contact you** (`contact_permission`, `select_one`, optional): If you're okay with us following up with you, please let us know.
