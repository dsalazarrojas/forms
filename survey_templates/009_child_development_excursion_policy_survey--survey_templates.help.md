# Child Development Excursion Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's participation in child development excursions, ensuring their safety and well-being are considered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Parent Full Name**, which is your name as the person completing this survey.
2. Enter your child's **Child Full Name**, as their name appears on their records.
3. Review and confirm you have read and understood the **Excursion Policy** by selecting "Yes - Read and Understood".
4. Confirm your understanding of **safety protocols** by selecting "True".
5. Select all excursions your child is willing to participate in from the **Excursion Types** field, such as "Walking excursions in local area", "Bus excursions to regional sites", "Visits to local parks", or "Library and community visits".
6. Provide an **Emergency Contact Number** that can be reached during excursion hours.
7. If necessary, provide any **Specific Medical Needs** your child may have, such as an inhaler or epipen.
8. Finally, select the current **Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Full Name**: Enter your name as the person completing this survey.
* **Child Full Name**: Enter your child's name as it appears on their records.
* **Excursion Policy**: Confirm you have read and understood the policy by selecting "Yes - Read and Understood".
* **Safety Protocols**: Confirm your understanding of safety protocols, which include staff ratios and risk assessment.
* **Excursion Types**: Select all excursions your child is willing to participate in. You can select multiple excursions.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Provide a phone number that can be reached during excursion hours.
* **Specific Medical Needs**: If necessary, provide any specific medical needs your child may have.
* **Date**: Select the current date to confirm the date of consent.

**Tips**: Make sure to review each field carefully and fill out any required fields. If you are unsure about any field, you can contact your child's program administrators for clarification.
