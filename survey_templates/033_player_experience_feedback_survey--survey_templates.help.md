# Player Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Experience Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from players to help us improve the gameplay experience. By providing feedback, you will help us understand your experience and make the game more enjoyable for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the fields marked as "required" (marked with an *).
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields (e.g., Feedback Comments).
3. Select only one rating for the "Rating" and "Satisfaction Level" fields.
4. Enter your email address if you'd like us to contact you for follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name**: This is the name of the player, as it appears in our records. Please enter your real name or a nickname you'd like to use.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address if you'd like us to contact you for follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Satisfaction Level**: Rate your satisfaction with the game on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Feedback Comments**: Share your thoughts and suggestions about your experience. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Game Version**: Enter the version of the game you played (e.g., "Version 1.2").
* **Date**: Enter the date you played the game (format: MM/DD/YY).
* **Game Environment**: If you played on a PC, console, or other platform, please specify the environment.
* **Playtest Length**: If you have played for a long time, you can rate how many minutes you played. This will help us understand how long you played the game.
