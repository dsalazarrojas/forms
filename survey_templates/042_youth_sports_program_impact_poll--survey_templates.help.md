# Youth Sports Program Impact Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Youth Sports Program Impact Poll form to provide feedback on your experience with the program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question honestly, selecting the best option from the provided choices.
- For questions with multiple options, you can select one or more answers.
- If you have additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional comments" field.
- If you have any contact information, please include it in the "Your name and contact info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How satisfied were you with the program?** (`participant_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, optional):
    This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the program. Select "Yes" if you were satisfied with the program, and "No" if you were not.
- **How did the program impact your athletic performance?** (`program_outcomes`, `select_one`, optional):
    This question asks about the effect of the program on your athletic performance. Select "Yes" if you felt the program helped improve your performance, and "No" if it didn't.
- **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, optional):
    Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale. This will help us understand how you felt about your experience.
- **How would you rate your coach's communication?** (`communication`, `number`, optional):
    Rate your coach's communication on a scale. This will help us understand how well they kept you informed about the program.
- **Feedback on the program** (`feedback`, `text`, optional):
    Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback about the program. This will help us understand what we can improve.
- **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional):
    Use this field to provide any further comments or suggestions about the program.
- **Your name and contact info (optional)** (`contact_info`, `email`, optional):
    If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or if you'd like to provide more information, please provide your name and contact info.
