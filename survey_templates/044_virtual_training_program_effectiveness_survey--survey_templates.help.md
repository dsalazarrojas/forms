# Virtual Training Program Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on various aspects of virtual training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form for your respective session.
2. Ensure that you are honest and thorough in your responses.
3. Select the relevant options for each question based on your experience.
4. Provide any additional comments or recommendations in the "Recommendations" and "Additional Comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Title**: Enter the title of the session you participated in.
* **Participant Title**: Enter your title or position within your department.
* **Satisfaction With Session Content** (rating 1): Rate your level of satisfaction with the content of the session, using the scale:
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
* **Satisfaction With Session Delivery** (rating 2): Rate your level of satisfaction with the delivery of the session, using the same scale as above.
* **Satisfaction With Trainer** (rating 3): Rate your level of satisfaction with the trainer, using the same scale as above.
* **Satisfaction With Training Materials** (rating 4): Rate your level of satisfaction with the training materials, using the same scale as above.
* **Session Goals**: Select the options that apply to your experience with the session goals:
	+ Yes, completely
	+ Yes, mostly
	+ No, somewhat
	+ No, not at all
* **Session Challenges**: Select the options that apply to any challenges you faced during the session:
	+ Yes, completely
	+ Yes, mostly
	+ No, somewhat
	+ No, not at all
* **Recommendations**: Provide any recommendations for future sessions.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback you would like to share.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (for communication purposes).
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name.
* **Department**: Enter your department or affiliation.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by one participant per session. If you have participated in multiple sessions, please fill out the form multiple times, once for each session.
