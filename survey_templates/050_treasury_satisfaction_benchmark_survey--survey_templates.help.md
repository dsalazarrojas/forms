# Treasury Satisfaction Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Satisfaction Benchmark Survey is a form designed to gauge the satisfaction of employees with various aspects of the treasury department. It is meant to collect feedback on general questions, operations, performance metrics, and other relevant factors. The survey aims to provide valuable insights for the treasury team to improve their services and processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the **General Questions** section with your thoughts and comments.
3. For **Operations**, select the relevant options that best describe the current state of treasury operations.
4. In **Performance Metrics**, enter numerical values to gauge performance indicators.
5. Provide feedback in the **Feedback** section.
6. For **Communication**, select multiple options that you feel are relevant.
7. Choose the overall **Satisfaction** rating.
8. Select the relevant option for **Follow Up**.
9. Enter the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** for tracking purposes.
10. Enter your **Email** address for contact information.
11. Add any additional **Notes** you may have.
12. Select the relevant option for **Assigned To**, **Team**, **Manager**, **Region**, **Country**, **Location**, **Department**, **Division**, **Team Member**, **Reporting Manager**, **Team Lead**, **Job Function**, and **Submitted By**.
