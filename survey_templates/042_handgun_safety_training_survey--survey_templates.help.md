# Handgun Safety Training Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect your honest feedback on the handgun safety training session you attended. Your input is crucial in helping us identify areas of improvement and provide better service in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
2. If a question does not apply to your experience, leave the field empty.
3. Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, use the "Comments" field to express them.
5. If you want to recommend our course, use the "Would you recommend this course to a friend?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your overall impression of the training session?** (text, required: false)
  Please describe your overall impression of the training session.
* **How knowledgeable did you find the instructor about handgun safety?** (1-5 scale, required: false)
  Rate the instructor's knowledge on a scale of 1 (not knowledgeable) to 5 (very knowledgeable).
* **How effectively did the instructor convey the information?** (1-5 scale, required: false)
  Rate the instructor's teaching technique on a scale of 1 (not effective) to 5 (very effective).
* **The course materials were helpful?** (text, required: false)
  Indicate whether the course materials were helpful or not.
* **Were we adequately equipped?** (text, required: false)
  Describe your experience with the equipment provided during the training session.
* **Where was the training held?** (text, required: false)
  Provide the location of the training session.
* **How long was the training session?** (text, required: false)
  Indicate the length of the training session.
* **How would you rate the training session's quality?** (text, required: false)
  Rate the quality of the training session.
* **Overall experience** (text, required: false)
  Describe your overall experience with the training session.
* **Comments** (text, required: false)
  Use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Rate the instructor** (1-5 scale, required: false)
  Rate the instructor's performance on a scale of 1 (not effective) to 5 (very effective).
* **Rate the course** (1-5 scale, required: false)
  Rate the course on a scale of 1 (not good) to 5 (very good).
* **Would you recommend this course to a friend?** (text, required: false)
  Indicate whether you would recommend the course to a friend.
* **Would Recommen 2** (text, required: false)
  Duplicate of "Would you recommend this course to a friend?" field.
* **Would Recommen2** (text, required: false)
  Duplicate of "Would you recommend this course to a friend?" field.
* **Would Recom** (text, required: false)
  Duplicate of "Would you recommend this course to a friend?" field.
* **Would** (text, required: false)
  Duplicate of "Would you recommend this course to a friend?" field.
* **Would 2** (text, required: false)
  Duplicate of "Would you recommend this course to a friend?" field.

Please note that fields marked as required: false are optional and you can skip them if you do not feel comfortable answering or if they do not apply to your experience.
