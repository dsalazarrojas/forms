# Dramatic Structure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you evaluate the structure of a dramatic work, such as a film, play, or book. The survey assesses your familiarity with key elements of dramatic structure and their effect on the audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. For each page, read the question and the provided options carefully.
2. Answer each question truthfully based on your understanding of the work.
3. Be sure to read the entire form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction to Dramatic Structure** (`introduction_to_dramatic_structure`, text, optional): A brief introduction to the structure of the story. Please provide a short description of the work's dramatic structure.
* **Dramatic Elements** (`dramatic_elements`, select_multiple, required): Indicate which of the following dramatic elements are present in the story: Yes/No
	+ Yes: The story includes this element.
	+ No: The story does not include this element.
* **Scene and Sequel** (`scene_and_sequel`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select one of the following options for this element in the story:
	+ Yes: The story effectively uses scene and sequel.
	+ No: The story does not effectively use scene and sequel.
* **Character Arcs** (`character_arcs`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which of the following character arcs are present in the story: 
	+ Yes: The story includes this arc.
	+ No: The story does not include this arc.
* **Plot Summaries** (`plot_summaries`, note, optional): Provide a brief summary of the story's overall plot.
* **Storytelling Choices** (`storytelling_choices`, text, optional): Briefly describe any storytelling choices that were made in creating the story.
* **Dramatic Techniques** (`dramatic_techniques`, select_multiple, required): Indicate which of the following dramatic techniques are used in the story: 
	+ Yes: The story includes this technique.
	+ No: The story does not include this technique.
* **Dramatic Structure Influence** (`dramatic_structure_influence`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which of the following dramatic structures influence the story: 
	+ Yes: The story includes this structure.
	+ No: The story does not include this structure.

## Tips

* Please answer each question truthfully based on your understanding of the story.
* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about an element, feel free to leave a note in the corresponding field for clarification.
