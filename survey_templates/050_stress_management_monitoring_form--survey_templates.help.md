# Stress Management Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals assess and monitor their stress levels, identify potential stressors and triggers, and develop coping mechanisms and support systems to manage their stress.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of 11 pages, each with its own set of questions. To complete the form, simply navigate through the pages, and for each page:

*   Fill in the information requested in the relevant fields. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
*   Select the options that best fit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **intro** (Page 1: intro, label: intro, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form. It's not a required field, so feel free to skip it if you're short on time.
*   **stress_levels** (Page 2: stress_levels, label: stress_levels, required: false): This field allows you to report your current stress levels on a scale of High, Medium, or Low. Select the option that best describes your current stress levels.
*   **emotional_states** (Page 3: emotional_states, label: emotional_states, required: false): This field offers a choice of three emotional states: High, Medium, or Low. Select the option that best reflects your current emotional state.
*   **triggers** (Page 4: triggers, label: triggers, required: false): This field is used to identify the sources of stress. Choose all that apply from the options provided:
    *   Work
    *   Personal
    *   Financial
*   **stressors** (Page 5: stressors, label: stressors, required: false): This field is similar to the triggers field, but with different options:
    *   Work
    *   Family
    *   Health
*   **coping_mechanisms** (Page 6: coping_mechanisms, label: coping_mechanisms, required: false): This field helps you identify the strategies you use to cope with stress. Choose all that apply from the options provided:
    *   Exercise
    *   Meditation
    *   Journaling
*   **support_systems** (Page 7: support_systems, label: support_systems, required: false): This field is used to report your support system:
    *   Friend
    *   Family
    *   Therapist
*   **comments** (Page 8: comments, label: comments, required: false): This field provides an open-ended space for you to share any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to add.
*   **submitted_by** (Page 9: submitted_by, label: submitted_by, required: false): This field indicates whether you are a wellness professional or a client.
*   **submitted_on** (Page 10: submitted_on, label: submitted_on, required: false): This field automatically records the date the form was submitted.
*   **submitted_at** (Page 11: submitted_at, label: submitted_at, required: false): This field automatically records the time the form was submitted.

Note: The form is designed to be self-explanatory, and most fields are optional, so don't worry if you're not sure how to fill in some of the fields. Just complete the form at your own pace, and you'll be done in no time!
