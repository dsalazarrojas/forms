# Distillery Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distillery Selection Survey is designed to gather information from visitors about their preferences for a distillery experience.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your preferences for a distillery experience. Please select the options that best fit your interests and needs.
2. For fields with multiple options (such as "Tasting Room Preferences" and "Tour Participants"), select all options that apply.
3. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") carefully, as they are essential for us to understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tasting Room Preferences** (Select Multiple):
	+ This question is asking about your preferences for the tasting room experience. Choose all options that apply to you.
* **Distillery Experience** (Select One):
	+ This question is asking about your experience level with distilleries. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Tour Participants** (Select Multiple):
	+ This question is asking about your willingness to participate in tours. Choose all options that apply to you.
* **Event Attendees** (Select Multiple):
	+ This question is asking about your interest in attending events at the distillery. Choose all options that apply to you.
* **Distillery Selection** (Select One):
	+ This question is asking about your preference for the distillery selection process. Please select the option that best suits your needs.
* **Comments and Suggestions**:
	+ This field is for you to add any additional comments or suggestions you have for the distillery experience.
* **Name** (Text):
	+ Please enter your full name.
* **Email** (Email):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Distillery Location** (Select One):
	+ This question is asking about the location of the distillery. Please select the option that best describes the location you prefer.
* **Distillery Type** (Select One):
	+ This question is asking about the type of distillery you are interested in. Please select the option that best suits your preferences.
* **Tasting Room Hours** (Time):
	+ This question is asking about your preferred hours for visiting the tasting room. Please select the time that best fits your schedule.
* **Tour Hours** (Time):
	+ This question is asking about your preferred hours for attending tours. Please select the time that best fits your schedule.
* **Events** (Select One):
	+ This question is asking about your interest in attending events at the distillery. Please select the option that best describes your interest.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully, as the information collected will directly influence the distillery's offerings and services.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments and Suggestions" field to share them with us.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [contact email or phone number].
