# Cultural Festival Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cultural Festival Feedback Form is designed to collect data from attendees about their experiences at the festival. This data will be used to make improvements to future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page. You will be asked to provide some personal information, rate your experience, and provide any comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your first name. This is a required field. If you are an organization or group, please list the representative's first name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Please provide your email address. This is not required but is recommended in case we need to follow up with you.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Share any comments, thoughts, or suggestions you have about the festival.
- **Event** (`event`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the event you attended. Choose from the list to ensure we accurately credit the event you participated in.
- **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your overall experience at the festival on a scale of 0-5.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): The date you attended the festival.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): The time you attended the festival.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Any additional thoughts or suggestions you have about the festival.

## Tips
- Please be as thorough as possible with your feedback.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask a festival staff member for assistance.
- We appreciate your time and input!
