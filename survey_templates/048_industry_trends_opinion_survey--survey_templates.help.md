# industry_trends_opinion_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions on industry trends and their impact on a business.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read through each field carefully before providing your response.
2.  Fill out each field according to your best judgment.
3.  Provide as much information as you can in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false):
    This is an introductory section where you can provide a brief context or background information about your company and its industry.
* **Industry Trends** (`Industry Trends`, `text`, required: false):
    Please describe the current state of your industry. This can include recent developments, changes, or notable events.
* **Trend Impact** (`Trend Impact`, `select_one`, required: true):
    Select the level of impact you think the current industry trend is having:
    *   High: The trend is having a significant and immediate impact on our operations.
    *   Medium: The trend is having a moderate impact on our operations.
    *   Low: The trend is not having a significant impact on our operations.
* **Trend Impact Reason** (`Trend Impact Reason`, `select_multiple`, required: true):
    Choose the reasons why you think the trend is impacting your operations:
    *   Increased Revenue
    *   Reduced Costs
    *   Improved Efficiency
    *   Competitor Pressure
    *   Regulatory Changes
    *   Market Trends
* **Trend Impact Other** (`Trend Impact Other`, `text`, required: false):
    If none of the above reasons apply, please provide a brief explanation of why the trend is impacting your operations.
* **Industry Trends** (`Industry Trends`, `text`, required: false):
    Provide a description of the current trends in the industry, if not already covered in the `Industry Trends` field.
* **Trend Impact** (`Trend Impact`, `select_one`, required: true):
    Select the level of impact you think the current industry trend is having:
    *   High: The trend is having a significant and immediate impact on our operations.
    *   Medium: The trend is having a moderate impact on our operations.
    *   Low: The trend is not having a significant impact on our operations.
* **Industry Trends Opinion** (`Industry Trends Opinion`, `note`, required: false):
    Add any additional comments or insights about the current state of the industry.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, required: false):
    Provide any additional comments or thoughts you may have about the industry trends or this form.

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled out. If you skip any required fields, your response will not be recorded.
