# Promotional Offer Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers about a promotional offer they have experienced. Please take a few minutes to share your thoughts and help us improve our offers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Please be as specific and detailed as possible in your responses.
3. If you have any issues or concerns, select the option that best describes your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What did you think about the offer** (Text Field, Optional): Please share your thoughts and impressions about the promotional offer you received.
- **Was it what you expected** (Select One, Required): Indicate how closely the promotional offer matched your expectations.
	+ Not at all
	+ Somewhat
	+ Very
- **Why did you choose this option** (Text Field, Optional): If you selected a particular option, please explain your reasoning behind it.
- **How did you like the design** (Number Field, Optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate how you liked the design of the promotional offer.
- **Did you have any issues with the process** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check if you experienced any issues during the promotional offer process.
	+ Yes
	+ No
- **How did you feel about the price** (Number Field, Optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate how you felt about the price of the promotional offer.
- **Do you have any recommendations** (Text Field, Optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving our offers in the future.
- **Would you recommend this offer to a friend** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if you would recommend the promotional offer to a friend.
	+ Yes
	+ No
