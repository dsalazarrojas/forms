# Wedding Theme Finder Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Theme Finder Quiz is designed to help couples discover the perfect theme for their wedding celebration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of wedding theme you're interested in from the "Wedding Theme" dropdown menu (Page 1).
2. Enter the estimated number of guests attending your wedding (Page 2).
3. Choose a venue type that resonates with you (Page 3).
4. Select the type of event you're planning (Page 4).
5. Indicate the season your wedding will take place (Page 5).
6. Estimate your budget for the wedding (Page 6).
7. Enter the date of your wedding day (Page 7).
8. Choose the time of your wedding ceremony (Page 8).
9. Enter the year of your wedding (Page 9).
10. Enter the date of your wedding day (Page 10).
11. Select any relevant themes you're interested in from the "Wedding Theme" list (Page 11).
12. Provide your email address (Page 12).
13. Enter a phone number (Page 13).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Theme** (`theme_name`): Select a theme that resonates with you and your partner. Choose from Fantasy, Rustic, Vintage, or Beach.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`): Estimate the number of guests you plan to invite. This will help us narrow down the theme options based on the capacity of your chosen venue.
* **Venue** (`venue_location`): Select a venue type that fits your style, such as a park, garden, or castle.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Choose the type of event that suits your needs, like a party, conference, or banquet.
* **Wedding Month** (`wedding_month`): Pick the season of your wedding: Summer, Spring, Autumn, or Winter.
* **Budget** (`budget`): Estimate your wedding budget. This will help us suggest themes that fit within your means.
* **Wedding Day** (`wedding_day`): Enter the date of your special day.
* **Wedding Time** (`time`): Choose the time of your wedding ceremony.
* **Wedding Year** (`wedding_year`): Enter the year of your wedding.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`): Enter the date of your wedding day again, for our records.
* **Wedding Theme** (`wedding_theme`): Select any themes that resonate with you, such as beach, rustic, or vintage.
* **Email** (`email`): Provide your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter a phone number for any further discussion or questions.

## Tips
Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions above, as this will help us provide you with more tailored suggestions. If you have any concerns or need further clarification, feel free to reach out to us.
