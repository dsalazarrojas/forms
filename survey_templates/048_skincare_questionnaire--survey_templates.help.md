# Skincare Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help skincare professionals understand your skincare habits, preferences, and concerns. The information you provide will help us create personalized skincare recommendations tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your true preferences and habits.
2. Select the options that best fit your situation.
3. Provide detailed answers where necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Skincare Questionnaire** (`introduction`): This page welcomes you to the form and explains its purpose.
* **Which of the following products are you interested in?** (`product_interest`): Choose all the products that you are interested in using.
* **What type of skin do you have?** (`skin_type`): Select the type of skin you have (Dry, Oily, Combination, Normal).
* **Which of the following skin concerns do you face?** (`skin_concerns`): Choose all the skin concerns you experience (Acne, Aging, Dark spots, Hyper-pigmentation, Pores, Rosacea, Sensitive skin, Tightening skin).
* **How often do you care for your skin?** (`skin_frequency`): Select how often you care for your skin (Daily, A few times a week, Once a week, Less than once a week).
* **Which skincare products do you currently use?** (`skin_product`): Choose all the skincare products you currently use (Moisturiser, Serum, Mask, Eye Cream, Cleanser, Sunscreen, Exfoliant, Toner).
* **Do you have any skin concerns that cause you pain?** (`skin_pain`): Choose all the skin concerns that cause you pain (Acne, Hyper-pigmentation, Redness, Pores, Sensitive skin, Tightening skin).
* **How often do you use skincare products?** (`skincare_frequency`): Select how often you use skincare products (Daily, A few times a week, Once a week, Less than once a week).
* **Are you loyal to any skincare brand?** (`skincare_brand`): Choose all the brands you are loyal to (Neutrogena, Olay, L'Oreal, Lancôme, Estee).
* **What is your average monthly skincare budget?** (`skincare_budget`): Enter your average monthly skincare budget.
* **How often do you purchase skincare products?** (`skincare_purchase_frequency`): Select how often you purchase skincare products (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
* **What is the most common pain point you experience with your current skincare products?** (`skincare_pain`): Choose all the pain points you experience with your current skincare products (Pore size, Acne, Hyper-pigmentation, Sensitive skin, Tightening skin, Lack of moisturizing, Lack of exfoliation, Other).
* **What do you look for in a skincare product?** (`skincare_preferences`): Choose all the features you look for in a skincare product (Moisturising, Anti-aging, Acne treatment, Hydration, Sensitive skin treatment, Gentle exfoliation, Anti-redness, Other).
* **Is there anything else you'd like to mention about your skincare preferences?** (`skincare_preferences_list`): Enter any additional skincare preferences or concerns you have.
* **How often do you use skincare products?** (`skincare_frequency`): Select how often you use skincare products (Daily, A few times a week, Once a week, Less than once a week).
* **How do you usually purchase skincare products?** (`skincare_purchase_method`): Choose how you usually purchase skincare products (In-store, Online, Social media, Other).
* **How satisfied are you with your current skincare products?** (`skincare_product_satisfaction`): Choose how satisfied you are with your current skincare products (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
* **Would you recommend your current skincare products to others?** (`skincare_recommendation`): Choose whether you would recommend your current skincare products to others (True, False, Not sure).
