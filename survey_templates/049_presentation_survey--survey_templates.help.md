# Presentation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Presentation Survey form, explaining its purpose and how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions on each page and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Select your rating for the presentation from the options provided (Good, Excellent, Fair, Poor, Undecided).
3. Provide any additional comments or questions you have about the presentation in the text fields.
4. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) so that we can follow up with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
- **Page 1** (`page1`, `number`, optional): This page is a placeholder page to start the survey.
- **Presentation Title** (`presentation_title`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief title of the presentation that you are providing feedback on.
- **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, optional): Select your rating for the presentation from the options provided (Good, Excellent, Fair, Poor, Undecided).
- **Comment** (`comment`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the presentation.
- **Page 2** (`page2`, `number`, optional): This page is another placeholder page to ensure you are ready for the next question.

### Page 2
- **Question 1** (`question1`, `text`, optional): What do you think about the presentation? Provide any feedback or comments about the content or style of the presentation.
- **Question 2** (`question2`, `text`, optional): What do you think about the slides? Provide any feedback or comments about the slides or the way they were presented.
- **Question 3** (`question3`, `text`, optional): This is another question for any additional feedback or comments about the presentation.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you if needed.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.

**Tips**

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the presentation, feel free to mention them in the "Comment" field.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, we would love to hear them!
* You can skip any questions or fields that do not apply to you.
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and valid contact information.
