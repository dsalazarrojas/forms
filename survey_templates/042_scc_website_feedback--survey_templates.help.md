# SCC   Website Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SCC website feedback form is designed to collect your thoughts and comments on the quality and usability of our website. Please take a moment to review each field and provide constructive feedback that will help us improve your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (marked with an asterisk *)
2. Select your rating of the website (Fair or Good)
3. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable)
4. Enter your name
5. Provide any additional comments you may have

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Website Feedback** (`scc_website_feedback`, text, required *): This is the main feedback field where you can type in your thoughts and comments about the website.
* **Website Rating** (`scc_website_rating`, select_multiple, required *): Please select either Good or Fair to rate your experience on the website.
* **Email** (`scc_website_email`, email, required *): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone** (`scc_website_phone`, text, required *): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Name** (`scc_website_name`, text, required *): Please enter your name so we can address you in our response.
* **Comments 1-15** (`scc_website_comments1`, `scc_website_comments2`, ..., `scc_website_comments15`, text, required *): Please provide any additional comments you may have about the website. You can type in up to 15 comments if needed.
* **Submit** (`scc_website_submit`, text, required *): Once you've filled out all the required fields, click this button to submit your feedback.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please include them in the comments section.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, we appreciate it!
