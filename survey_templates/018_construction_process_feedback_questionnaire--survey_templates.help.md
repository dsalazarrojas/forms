# Construction Process Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on the construction process of a project you have completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions based on your experience with the project. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on different aspects of the project process. Please answer all questions truthfully, and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Completed Project** (project_name, text, required): Please enter the name of the project you are providing feedback on.
* **Your Role in the Project** (project_role, select_one, required): Please select the role you played in the project from the list:
	+ Client/Owner
	+ General Contractor
	+ Subcontractor
	+ Architect/Engineer
	+ Consultant
	+ Project Manager
* **Approximate Date of Completion** (completion_date, date, required): Please enter the approximate date the project was completed.
* **Rate your overall satisfaction with the project (1-10)** (overall_rating, number, required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the project, where 1 is Very Dissatisfied and 10 is Very Satisfied.
* **Primary reason for your rating** (satisfaction_reason, text, required): Please explain the reason for your rating in the space provided.
* **Accuracy and Adherence to Project Schedule** (scheduling_rating, number, required): Please rate the project schedule from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Budget and Financial Management** (budget_adherence_rating, number, required): Please rate the project budget from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Overall Quality of Final Workmanship** (quality_of_workmanship, number, required): Please rate the quality of the final work from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Communication and Responsiveness During Project** (communication_rating, number, required): Please rate the communication and responsiveness from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **What was the single biggest challenge during the project?** (biggest_challenge_encountered, text, required): Please describe the biggest challenge you faced during the project.
* **How well was this challenge handled by the team?** (challenge_handling_rating, number, required): Please rate how well the team handled the challenge from 1 (Poorly) to 5 (Exceptionally well).
* **Efficiency of Change Order/Revision Process** (change_order_process_rating, number, required): Please rate the change order/revision process from 1 (Very inefficient) to 5 (Very efficient).
* **Perceived Prioritization of Site Safety** (safety_prioritization_rating, number, required): Please rate the prioritization of site safety from 1 (Low Priority) to 5 (High Priority).
* **If you could change one thing about the process, what would it be?** (improvement_suggestion_desc, text, required): Please describe one thing you would change about the project process.
* **How likely are you to work with this project team again?** (refer_others_likelihood, number, required): Please rate how likely you are to work with the project team again from 1 (Not Likely) to 5 (Very Likely).
* **Additional Comments or Observations** (additional_comments, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or observations about the project.
* **Date of Questionnaire Completion** (submission_date, date, required): This is the date you are completing the questionnaire. 

## Tips
Please answer all questions truthfully and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact [Your Name] at [Your Contact Info].
