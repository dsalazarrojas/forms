# Open House Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about visitors and events for attendance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the form with your details and information about the event you're attending.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Info** (Page 1)
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief title of the event you're attending.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time of the event.
* **Event Time Zone** (`event_time_zone`, `select_one`, optional): Choose a time zone for the event.
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the length of the event in hours.

### 2. **Visitor Info** (Page 2)
* **Visitor Name** (`visitor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Visitor Gender** (`visitor_gender`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your gender.
* **Visitor Age** (`visitor_age`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your age group.
* **Visitor Employment Status** (`visitor_employment_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your employment status.
* **Visitor Education Level** (`visitor_education_level`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your education level.
* **Visitor Occupation** (`visitor_occupation`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your occupation.
* **Visitor Hobbies** (`visitor_hobbies`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your hobbies.

### 3. **Contact Info** (Page 3)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the event title.

### 4. **Notes** (Page 4)
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the event.

### 5. **Visitor Travel History** (Page 5)
* **Visitor Travel History** (`visitor_travel_history`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your travel history.

### 6. **Visitor Count** (Page 6)
* **Visitors Count** (`visitors_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of visitors attending the event.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, and make sure to review each field before submitting.
