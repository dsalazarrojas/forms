# parent_interest_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a parent's contact information, relationship with the child, preferred contact method, extracurricular interests, occupation, work status, income, and work hours. The data collected will be used to create a comprehensive profile of the parent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Indicate your relationship with the child.
3. Choose your preferred method of contact.
4. Select any extracurricular interests that you or your child participate in.
5. Select your occupation from the available options.
6. Choose your current work status.
7. Enter your annual income, if applicable.
8. Enter the number of work hours you have per week, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your contact information, such as your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Relationships** (`contact_relationships`, text, required/optional): This field is used to indicate your relationship with the child.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to choose your preferred method of contact, which can be Text, Phone, Email, or In Person.
* **Extracurricular Interests** (`extracurricular_interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to select any extracurricular activities that you or your child participate in, such as Drama, Chorus, Instrumental, Sports, or Clubs.
* **Parent Occupation** (`parent_occupation`, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to select your occupation from the available options.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to choose your current work status, which can be Employed, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Retired, or Student.
* **Income** (`income`, number, required/optional): This field is used to enter your annual income, if applicable.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required/optional): This field is used to enter the number of work hours you have per week, if applicable.
