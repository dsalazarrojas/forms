# Real Time Feedback Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect real-time feedback from customers about their interactions with our service. It helps us understand the customer's experience, gather suggestions, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
2. Fill in all the required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
3. Provide as much detail as you can in the text fields.
4. If you have any concerns or issues, please mention them in the respective text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, `optional`): This section is just a brief welcome message to help you get started.
* **Customer Experience** (`2`, `text`, `optional`): This field is a text box where you can describe your overall experience with our service. Please be as detailed as you can.
* **What was the purpose of your visit or interaction with our service today?** (`3`, `text`, `required`): This question helps us understand why you visited or interacted with our service. Please provide a brief explanation.
* **How would you rate the quality of our service today?** (`4`, `select_one`, `required`): This question is about your overall satisfaction with our service. Please choose one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Which departments or teams did you interact with today?** (`5`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): If you interacted with any departments or teams, please select all that apply.
* **What is your email address so we can follow up with you?** (`6`, `email`, `required`): Please provide your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Can you share any feedback about your visit or interaction with our service today?** (`7`, `text`, `required`): This question is about your overall feedback and suggestions.
* **Do you have any suggestions for our service?** (`8`, `text`, `optional`): If you have any ideas on how we can improve, please share them.
* **How would you rate the service time today?** (`9`, `time`, `required`): This question is about the time it took to complete your service. Please provide the time in a format like "HH:MM".
* **How would you rate the service quality today?** (`10`, `select_one`, `required`): This question is similar to the previous one, but about the overall quality of service.
* **When did you visit our service today?** (`11`, `date`, `required`): Please provide the date and time of your visit.
* **When did you last contact our service?** (`12`, `date`, `required`): This question helps us understand when you first contacted our service.
* **Can you suggest any improvements to our service?** (`13`, `text`, `required`): If you have any ideas on how we can improve, please share them.
* **Are there any specific issues you faced during your interaction with our service?** (`14`, `text`, `required`): If you faced any issues, please describe them.
* **Do you have any other comments or concerns about our service?** (`15`, `text`, `required`): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them.
* **Can you suggest any features or services we could offer that would improve your experience?** (`16`, `text`, `required`): If you have any ideas on what we can offer, please suggest them.
* **Are there any other issues or concerns you would like to mention?** (`17`, `text`, `optional`): If you have anything else you'd like to add, please do so.
* **Question 16** (`18`, `text`, `required`): This question is currently empty and not used. Please disregard it for now.
