# Youth Empowerment Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Youth Empowerment Assessment is a form designed to evaluate the effectiveness and quality of youth empowerment programs and services offered by organizations. This form helps to identify areas of strength and weakness in these programs and inform decisions on how to improve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them accurately based on your knowledge and experience with youth empowerment programs.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your response.
3. For questions that ask for a numerical value (e.g., Participation Rate, Participation Rate Percentage), enter the exact value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Youth Education Programs** (`youth_education_programs`)
* This question asks whether your organization offers education programs that target youth empowerment.
* Select 'Yes' if your organization offers such programs, and 'No' otherwise.

### 2. **Program Partnerships** (`program_partnerships` )
* This question asks whether your organization partners with other organizations to provide services to youth.
* Select 'Yes' if your organization has partnerships, and 'No' otherwise.

### 3. **Youth Services Offered** (`youth_services_offered` )
* This question asks about the services offered by your organization to empower youth.
* Select the services that are offered by your organization.

### 4. **Program Participation** (`program_participation` )
* This question asks whether your organization has a participation framework to engage youth in the programs offered.
* Select 'Yes' if your organization has a participation framework, and 'No' otherwise.

### 5. **Empowerment Outcomes** (`empowerment_outcomes` )
* This question asks whether your organization measures the outcomes of youth empowerment programs.
* Select 'Yes' if your organization measures outcomes, and 'No' otherwise.

### 6. **Empowerment Levels** (`empowerment_levels` )
* This question asks about the level of empowerment achieved by youth in your organization's programs.
* Select 'High' if the level of empowerment is high, 'Low' if it's low, or choose 'No' if you don't measure empowerment levels.

### 7. **Participation Risks** (`participation_risks` )
* This question asks about the risks associated with youth participation in your organization's programs.
* Select the risks that are present in your organization's programs.

### 8. **Participation Barriers** (`participation_barriers` )
* This question asks about the barriers that prevent youth from participating in your organization's programs.
* Select the barriers that are present in your organization's programs.

### 9. **Service Provisions** (`service_provisions` )
* This question asks about the services provided by your organization to empower youth.
* Select the services that are provided by your organization.

### 10. **Program Capacity** (`program_capacity` )
* This question asks about the capacity of your organization's programs to effectively empower youth.
* Select the aspects that are present in your organization's programs.

### 11. **Staff Training Programs** (`staff_training_programs` )
* This question asks whether your organization offers training programs for staff to better serve youth.
* Select 'Yes' if your organization offers such programs, and 'No' otherwise.

### 12. **Community Resources** (`community_resources` )
* This question asks about the resources provided by your organization to support the community.
* Select the resources that are provided by your organization.

### 13. **Evaluation Framework** (`evaluation_framework` )
* This question asks whether your organization has a framework to evaluate the effectiveness of youth empowerment programs.
* Select 'Yes' if your organization has such a framework, and 'No' otherwise.

### 14. **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency` )
* This question asks about the frequency of evaluations conducted by your organization.
* Select the frequency of evaluations conducted by your organization.

### 15. **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date` )
* This question asks for the date of the last assessment conducted by your organization.
* Enter the date in the correct format.

### 16. **Staff Training** (`staff_training` )
* This question asks about the training provided to staff to better serve youth.
* Select 'Yes' if your organization provides such training, and 'No' otherwise.

### 17. **Training Programs** (`training_programs` )
* This question asks about the types of training programs offered by your organization.
* Select the types of training programs offered by your organization.

### 18. **Staff Satisfaction** (`staff_satisfaction` )
* This question asks about the level of satisfaction among staff with respect to youth empowerment programs.
* Select the level of satisfaction.

### 19. **Evaluation Outcomes** (`evaluation_outcomes` )
* This question asks about the outcomes of evaluations conducted by your organization.
* Select the outcomes that are present in your organization's programs.

### 20. **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions` )
* This question asks about the follow-up actions taken by your organization after evaluations.
* Select the actions that are taken by your organization.

### 21. **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency` )
* This question asks about the frequency of follow-up actions taken by your organization.
* Select the frequency of follow-up actions.

### 22. **Staff Participation** (`staff_participation` )
* This question asks about the participation of staff in youth empowerment programs.
* Select 'Yes' if staff participate, and 'No' otherwise.

### 23. **Participation Rate** (`participation_rate` )
* This question asks for the participation rate of youth in your organization's programs.
* Enter the exact participation rate.

### 24. **Participation Rate Percentage** (`participation_rate_percentage` )
* This question asks for the participation rate percentage of youth in your organization's programs.
* Enter the exact participation rate percentage.

### 25. **Assessment Status** (`assessment_status` )
* This question asks about the status of the assessment conducted by your organization.
* Select 'Active' if the assessment is active, and 'Inactive' if it's inactive.

Note: This help guide assumes that the form is self-explanatory and that users understand the questions and options provided.
