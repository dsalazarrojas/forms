# Caregiver Support Workshop Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness and quality of a caregiver support workshop that you have attended.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information and answer each question honestly.
2. Make sure to choose the correct options for each question that best describes your experience.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Workshop Date Attended**: Enter the date you attended the workshop in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Workshop Session**: Select the session you attended during the workshop (Morning Session, Afternoon Session, Evening Session, or All-Day Workshop).
* **Facilitator Name**: Enter the name of the workshop facilitator.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Please evaluate your overall satisfaction with the workshop.
* **Overall Workshop Rating**: Rate the quality of the workshop on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Likely Recommend**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend this workshop to other caregivers.
* **Content Relevance**: Evaluate how relevant the workshop content was to your caregiving situation.
* **New Skills Learned**: Indicate whether you learned new skills or strategies during the workshop.
* **Content Clarity**: Rate how clear the workshop content was.
* **Facilitator Effectiveness**: Evaluate the facilitator's expertise and knowledge.
* **Facilitator Engagement**: Indicate how effectively the facilitator engaged with you and other participants.
* **Facilitator Approachability**: Rate how approachable and supportive the facilitator was.
* **Venue Comfort**: Evaluate how comfortable and accessible the workshop venue was.
* **Workshop Duration**: Rate the duration of the workshop as too short, just right, or too long.
* **Materials Quality**: Evaluate the quality of handouts and materials provided.
* **Feedback for Improvement**: Share any suggestions or concerns you have about the workshop.
* **Future Topics**: Suggest any topics you would like to see covered in future workshops.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have.

Please answer each question honestly, and remember that your feedback is valuable to us!
