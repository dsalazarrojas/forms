# Employee Belonging Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees reflect on their sense of belonging within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read each question carefully and respond honestly.
*   Answer each question on the page without skipping or rushing through them.
*   Use the provided options or answer choices as a guide, but feel free to elaborate on your responses if needed.
*   Save your progress as you go and review your answers at the end to ensure completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Intro Question** (`Intro Question`, text, optional): A brief reflection on your current state of mind, providing a starting point for your responses.

    This question is meant to be a personal reflection of your current state, allowing you to express yourself freely. Your response will serve as a starting point for the subsequent questions.

- **Department** (`Department`, select_one, optional): Choose the department you are most closely associated with.

    This field is intended to help identify your primary departmental allegiance. Please select the department that you are most connected to, even if you feel you belong to multiple departments.

- **Question 1** (`Question 1`, select_multiple, optional): How do you perceive your departmental belonging?

    Answer this question by selecting from the options provided, or type your own response if none of the options fit your feeling. Be honest about your perceptions of belonging within your department.

- **Question 2** (`Question 2`, select_one, optional): How would you rate your team belonging?

    This question seeks to gauge your level of connection with your team. Choose an option that best reflects your feelings about your team's sense of belonging.

- **Question 3** (`Question 3`, text, optional): Describe your current feelings on the team's sense of belonging.

    This open-ended question allows you to freely express your thoughts on your team's sense of belonging. Be honest, and use as much or as little space as you need to elaborate.

- **Question 4** (`Question 4`, time, optional): How often do you spend time socializing with your department/team?

    This question looks into your social interaction with your department or team. Choose a time range that best describes your level of social interaction.

- **Question 5** (`Question 5`, date, optional): When was the last time you celebrated a team achievement or milestone?

    This question aims to find out about your recent team celebrations or milestones. Select a date if you can remember one, or describe a recent occasion if none comes to mind.

- **Question 6** (`Question 6`, text, optional): Describe a recent team celebration or achievement that made you feel part of the team.

    This question allows you to share a recent experience that made you feel like you belong to your team. Be as detailed as you need to express your feelings.

- **Question 7** (`Question 7`, text, optional): What would you say to someone who feels like they don't belong in their department?

    This question seeks to understand your thoughts on others who may be struggling with a sense of belonging. Respond from your perspective, using your own experiences and feelings.

- **Question 8** (`Question 8`, email, optional): Would you like to be contacted by your HR or departmental representative for any further questions or clarifications?

    This question offers an opportunity for HR or departmental representatives to follow up on your responses and provide further support if needed. Check this box if you'd like to be contacted.

- **Question 9** (`Question 9`, number, optional): How many hours per week do you spend working on departmental projects or initiatives outside of core work hours?

    This question aims to gauge your involvement in departmental projects beyond regular working hours. Enter a number that reflects your actual work hours spent on such projects.

- **Question 10** (`Question 10`, number, optional): How many hours per week do you spend working on departmental projects or initiatives outside of core work hours with your team?

    This question seeks to understand your collaboration with your team outside of regular work hours on departmental projects. Enter a number that represents this time spent with your team.

- **Question 11** (`Question 11`, text, optional): Describe a recent experience where you felt a strong sense of belonging in your department.

    This open-ended question allows you to share a recent experience where you felt a strong sense of belonging in your department. Be as detailed as you'd like to elaborate on your feelings.

- **Question 12** (`Question 12`, text, optional): What steps would you recommend for improving the departmental sense of belonging?

    This question seeks to gather your thoughts on how to enhance the departmental sense of belonging. Respond with suggestions, ideas, or recommendations that come to mind.

- **Question 13** (`Question 13`, text, optional): Do you have any further comments or suggestions on departmental belonging?

    This question offers an opportunity to add any additional thoughts or comments on departmental belonging. Use this space to express anything that hasn't been covered by previous questions.

- **Question 14** (`Question 14`, text, optional): If there's anything that you would like to add or mention regarding your sense of belonging, please do so here.

    This question allows you to add any final thoughts or observations about your sense of belonging. Be as detailed or brief as you'd like, depending on your needs.

- **Question 15** (`Question 15`, text, optional): Is there anything else you'd like to mention or discuss regarding your sense of belonging?

    This question provides a last chance to bring up any additional points or concerns about your sense of belonging. Use this space as needed to complete your thoughts.

Tips:

*   Be honest in your responses; the goal is to understand your feelings and perceptions.
*   Take your time and don't rush through the questions.
*   Use clear and concise language in your answers.
*   Don't worry if you're unsure about a question – simply answer to the best of your ability.

You are doing this survey to help improve your department's sense of belonging. Your input is invaluable to making this initiative a success. Thank you for taking the time to reflect on your feelings and experiences.
