# Soccer Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Soccer Experience Feedback Survey, a form designed to collect your thoughts and opinions on your recent soccer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply follow the steps below:

1. Answer each field based on your genuine feelings and thoughts.
2. Provide as much or as little detail as you want.
3. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction (2)** (`number`, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your recent soccer experience. Use the number keys (1-5) to indicate your level of satisfaction.

* **What did you most enjoy? (3)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the following options to express what you enjoyed most about your soccer experience:
	+ The coaching staff's knowledge and expertise
	+ The playing conditions (lighting, temperature, etc.)
	+ The overall experience
	+ The food and drinks provided
	+ The social atmosphere
	+ The facilities (restrooms, locker rooms, etc.)
	+ Other (feel free to specify)

* **Do you have any suggestions? (4)** (`text`, required/optional): Share any ideas or suggestions you have for improving the soccer event.

* **Email (5)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for future events or for any other reason.

* **Phone (6)** (`text`, required/optional): Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for future events or for any other reason.

* **Additional Comments (7)** (`note`, required/optional): Share any further thoughts or comments about your soccer experience.

* **Event Information (8)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the event, such as the date, time, or location.
