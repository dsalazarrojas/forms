# Disaster Evacuation Plan Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of a disaster evacuation plan in a facility or organization. It's essential to understand the purpose and goals of the plan, which is to ensure that participants, employees, and emergency contact persons are safe and prepared in the event of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Ensure you have filled out all relevant sections accurately.
3. Review and understand each field before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing emergency contact information such as a name, phone number, and address for contact.
	+ Please provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Evacuation Plan** (`evacuation_plan`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing the details of the emergency plan.
	+ Please provide a brief description of the evacuation plan, including emergency procedures and protocols.
* **Communication Plan** (`communication_plan`, `text`, required: false): This field is for describing the communication methods and protocols in place during the emergency.
	+ Please provide information about how participants will be informed and updated during the emergency.
* **Participant Safety** (`participant_safety`, `text`, required: false): This field is for evaluating the safety measures taken for participants during the emergency.
	+ Please describe the safety measures taken, such as emergency exits, fire alarms, and emergency lighting.
* **Emergency Exit Routes** (`emergency_exit_routes`, `text`, required: false): This field is for listing the emergency exit routes and procedures.
	+ Please describe the emergency exit routes and procedures, including any necessary safety precautions.
* **Exit Signs** (`exit_signs`, `text`, required: false): This field is for describing the exit signs and their locations.
	+ Please provide information about the location and description of exit signs.
* **Alarm System** (`alarm_system`, `text`, required: false): This field is for evaluating the alarm system's functionality and safety measures.
	+ Please describe the alarm system's features and its effectiveness during emergencies.
* **Emergency Lighting** (`emergency_lighting`, `text`, required: false): This field is for describing emergency lighting measures, such as backup power and lighting plans.
	+ Please provide information about emergency lighting procedures and protocols.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, `text`, required: false): This field is for describing fire extinguisher locations and procedures.
	+ Please describe the locations and procedures for using fire extinguishers.
* **Emergency Power Outage Procedures** (`emergency_power_outage_procedures`, `text`, required: false): This field is for describing procedures for power outages during emergencies.
	+ Please provide information about power outage procedures and protocols.
* **Evacuation Drill** (`evacuation_drill`, `text`, required: false): This field is for describing the evacuation drill procedures and protocols.
	+ Please describe the procedures and protocols for conducting evacuation drills.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing emergency contact person's name and information.
	+ Please provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing additional emergency contact information.
	+ Please provide any additional information that may be necessary for emergency contact.
* **Participant Participation** (`participant_participation`, `text`, required: false): This field is for evaluating participant participation in emergency drills and procedures.
	+ Please describe the level of participant participation and engagement during emergency drills.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, `text`, required: false): This field is for evaluating participant satisfaction with the emergency plan and procedures.
	+ Please describe participant satisfaction with the emergency plan and procedures.
* **Participant Safety Rating** (`participant_safety`, `number`, required: false): This field is for rating participant safety during emergencies.
	+ Please rate participant safety on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Participant Satisfaction Rating** (`participant_satisfaction_rating`, `number`, required: false): This field is for rating participant satisfaction with the emergency plan and procedures.
	+ Please rate participant satisfaction on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Employee/Participant Rating** (`employee_participant_rating`, `number`, required: false): This field is for rating employee/participant safety and satisfaction.
	+ Please rate employee/participant safety and satisfaction on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Emergency Procedures Rating** (`emergency_procedures_rating`, `number`, required: false): This field is for rating the emergency procedures and protocols.
	+ Please rate the emergency procedures and protocols on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
