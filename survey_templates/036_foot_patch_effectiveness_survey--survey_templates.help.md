# Foot Patch Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback and data from users about their experiences with foot patches.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your responses to each question by choosing from the provided options.
2. If you have used the foot patches, fill out the required and optional fields according to your experience.
3. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
