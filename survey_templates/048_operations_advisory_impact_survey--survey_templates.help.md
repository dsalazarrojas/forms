# Operations Advisory Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from end-users on the impact of operational advisories within their teams or departments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the survey and ensure you have the necessary information to complete the form.
2. For each page, select all applicable options for the "advisory engagements" field, as this is a multiple-choice question.
3. In the "perceived impact" field, enter a numerical value indicating the level of impact you perceive the advisory has had on your operations.
4. In the "suggestions for improvement" field, provide any comments or ideas you have for improving the operational advisory process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Advisory Engagements** (`advisory_engagements`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable options that describe the types of advisory engagements you have participated in or have knowledge of. For example, if you have participated in multiple advisory engagements, select all relevant options.
* **Perceived Impact** (`perceived_impact`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10, where 1 represents a low impact and 10 represents a high impact.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, required: false): Provide any comments or ideas you have for improving the operational advisory process. This can include suggestions on how to better engage stakeholders, improve communication, or streamline the process. 

## Tips

* Ensure you are providing accurate and honest feedback.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions or options, please do not hesitate to reach out to the survey administrators for clarification. 
* Please complete this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure we can gather useful insights to improve the operational advisory process.
