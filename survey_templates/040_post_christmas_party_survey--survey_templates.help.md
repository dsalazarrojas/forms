# Post-Christmas Party Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post-Christmas Party Survey is designed to gather feedback and ratings from participants about their experiences at the post-Christmas party. This information will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Party Details" and provide a brief description of your experience at the party.
2. Rate the overall quality of the party using the "Overall Rating" field.
3. Rate the quality of the food, drinks, games, and music using the "Food Rating", "Drink Rating", "Games Rating", and "Music Rating" fields, respectively.
4. Indicate if you received any party favors during the party.
5. Check if there was music played during the party.
6. Check if the party location was suitable for the event.
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Party Details**: Provide a brief description of your experience at the party.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate the overall quality of the party out of 5.
* **Food Rating**: Rate the quality of the food served at the party, out of 5.
* **Drink Rating**: Rate the quality of the drinks served at the party, out of 5.
* **Games Rating**: Rate the enjoyment of the games and activities at the party, out of 5.
* **Music Rating**: Rate the quality of the music played during the party, out of 5.
* **Party Favors**: Indicate if you received any party favors during the party (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
* **Music**: Indicate if there was music played during the party (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
* **Party Location**: Indicate if the party location was suitable for the event (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience in this field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
