# Financial Governance Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to assess the confidence level of users regarding the financial governance practices of your organization. Please answer this survey honestly to help identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the survey questions carefully and answer them based on your honest opinion.
2. For each question, select one of the available options to indicate your confidence level.
3. Fill in any contact information you'd like to be reached back for further discussion.
4. Once you've completed the survey, review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your opinion.
5. Submit the survey to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Financial Governance Confidence Survey** (`survey_start`, note, not required): This is the title of the survey, which is here to introduce you to the purpose of the form.
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, not required): Please provide a brief introduction or any additional comments you'd like to share about your financial governance confidence level.
* **Financial Governance Confidence** (`financial_governance_confidence`, number, not required): On a scale from 0-100, rate your confidence level regarding the financial governance practices of the organization.
* **Financial Governance Confidence 2** (`financial_governance_confidence_2`, number, not required): On a scale from 0-100, rate your confidence level regarding the financial governance practices of the organization.
* **Financial Governance Confidence 3** (`financial_governance_confidence_3`, time, not required): How many hours have you spent reviewing financial records or attending meetings to understand financial governance practices.
* **Financial Governance Confidence 4** (`financial_governance_confidence_4`, date, not required): Date of your last review of financial records or meetings regarding financial governance practices.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, email, not required): Please enter your email address or other contact information so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
* **Contact Information 2** (`contact_information_2`, text, not required): Enter any additional contact information you'd like.
* **Submission Confirmation 1** (`submission_confirmation`, select_one, not required): How confident are you in the financial governance of this organization?
	+ I am confident in the financial governance of this organization
	+ I am not confident in the financial governance of this organization
	+ I do not know
* **Submission Confirmation 2** (`submission_confirmation_2`, select_multiple, not required): How confident are you in the financial governance of this organization?
	+ I am confident in the financial governance of this organization
	+ I am not confident in the financial governance of this organization
	+ I do not know

## Tips
* Be honest in your answers, as this survey is designed to help identify areas of improvement.
* If you are not confident in the financial governance of the organization, please provide any additional comments or explanations you'd like to share.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
