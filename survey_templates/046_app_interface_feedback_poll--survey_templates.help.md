# App Interface Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the app interface, covering aspects like poll date, user experience, and areas for improvement. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the date of the poll you're responding to.
2. Enter your name and email address (if you'd like to provide it).
3. Specify the app name being evaluated.
4. Identify the interface element being evaluated (e.g. menu, buttons, layout).
5. Assess how familiar you are with the interface.
6. Indicate the ease of navigation.
7. Evaluate the interface layout and organization.
8. Assess the placement of buttons and icons.
9. Rate the text readability.
10. Assess the menu and option intuitiveness.
11. Rate the icon clarity and meaning.
12. Evaluate the color scheme and visual appeal.
13. Check how well the app works on mobile devices.
14. Assess the gesture controls if applicable.
15. Rate the accessibility features.
16. Assess the consistency across the interface.
17. Evaluate the loading states and feedback.
18. Provide suggestions for interface improvements.
19. Rate your overall satisfaction with the interface.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Poll date (1)**: Select the date of the poll you're responding to.
* **Your name (2)**: Enter your name for identification. (Optional)
* **Email address (3)**: Provide your email address if you'd like. (Optional)
* **App name (4)**: Specify the name of the app interface being evaluated.
* **Interface element being evaluated (5)**: Indicate the specific part of the app interface you're evaluating (e.g. menu, buttons, layout).
* **How familiar are you with this interface? (6)**: Rate your level of familiarity with the interface. Options: Very familiar, Familiar, Somewhat familiar, New to the interface, First time seeing it.
* **Ease of navigation (7)**: Rate how easy it is to find what you need in the interface. Options: Very easy, Easy, Moderate, Difficult, Very difficult.
* **Interface layout and organization (8)**: Evaluate if the interface layout is logically organized. Options: Excellent - very logical, Good - mostly logical, Fair - somewhat confusing, Poor - very confusing, Chaotic.
* **Button and icon placement (9)**: Rate the placement of buttons and icons in the interface. Options: Perfect placement, Good placement, Adequate placement, Poor placement, Confusing placement.
* **Text readability (10)**: Rate the font size and color contrast of the text in the interface. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very difficult to read.
* **Menu and option intuitiveness (11)**: Assess the clarity of menu options and their labels. Options: Very intuitive, Intuitive, Somewhat intuitive, Not intuitive, Confusing.
* **Icon clarity and meaning (12)**: Rate the understandability of icons in the interface. Options: Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, Confusing.
* **Color scheme and visual appeal (13)**: Evaluate the visual appeal of the app interface's color scheme. Options: Excellent - visually appealing, Good - nice colors, Fair - acceptable, Poor - unappealing, Very unappealing.
* **Mobile responsiveness (14)**: Check how well the app interface works on mobile devices. Options: Excellent on mobile, Good on mobile, Okay on mobile, Poor on mobile, Not usable on mobile.
* **Gesture controls if applicable (15)**: Check if gesture controls (e.g. swipe, tap, pinch) work as expected. Options: Work perfectly, Mostly work, Partially work, Rarely work, Do not work, N/A.
* **Accessibility features (16)**: Rate the presence and effectiveness of accessibility features like font size adjustment and dark mode. Options: Excellent accessibility, Good accessibility, Fair accessibility, Poor accessibility, Not accessible.
* **Consistency across the interface (17)**: Assess the consistency of the app interface's design elements. Options: Very consistent, Mostly consistent, Somewhat inconsistent, Very inconsistent, Chaotic.
* **Loading states and feedback (18)**: Check if loading states and feedback are clear and informative. Options: Always clear, Usually clear, Sometimes clear, Often unclear, Never clear.
* **Suggestions for interface improvements (19)**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving the app interface.
* **Overall interface satisfaction (20)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the app interface. Options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.

## Tips

- Be honest and thorough in your responses, as the feedback will be used to improve the app interface.
- If you're unsure about any of the questions, try to answer based on your general feelings and experiences.
- Take your time to fill out the form, but keep in mind that providing detailed and thoughtful feedback will be more helpful.
- Don't hesitate to provide any additional comments or suggestions that are not included in the questions.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
