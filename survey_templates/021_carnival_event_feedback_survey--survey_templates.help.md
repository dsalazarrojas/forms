# Carnival Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Carnival Event Feedback Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from attendees on their experience at the carnival. The feedback collected will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve on for next year's event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with your honest and accurate responses. Please note that some fields are required, and you will need to provide information in these fields to submit the form. You can leave fields blank if you are not sure or do not wish to provide information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Date Attended
**Date Attended** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Date Attended</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): The date you attended the carnival. This field is required and should be filled out with the correct date.

### 2. Your Name (optional)
**Your Name** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Your Name</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">optional</span>): Your name, or you can leave this field blank if you'd like to be anonymous.

### 3. Email Address (optional)
**Email Address** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Email Address</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">optional</span>): If you'd like to be entered into our prize draw, please provide your email address. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.

### 4. Age Group
**Your Age Group** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Age Group</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): This field will help us understand our audience. Please select the age group that best suits you.

### 5. Overall Experience
**Overall rating of the carnival** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Overall Experience</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): Rate your overall experience at the carnival, with 1 being poor and 10 being exceptional.

### 6. Favorite Attraction
**What was your favorite attraction or ride?** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Favorite Attraction</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): Please name one specific item that you enjoyed the most.

### 7. Least Favorite Attraction
**What was your least favorite part of the event?** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Least Favorite Attraction</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">optional</span>): If you have any feedback on what we could improve on, please let us know.

### 8. Atmosphere and Decor
**Rate the atmosphere and decor** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Atmosphere and Decor</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): Rate the atmosphere and decor of the carnival, with 1 being poor and 5 being exceptional.

### 9. Quality of Food and Beverages
**Quality of food and beverages** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Quality of Food and Beverages</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): Rate the quality of food and beverages, with 1 being poor and 5 being exceptional.

### 10. Variety of Food Options
**Variety of food options** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Variety of Food Options</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): Rate the variety of food options available, with 1 being poor and 5 being exceptional.

### 11. Friendliness and Helpfulness of Staff
**Friendliness and helpfulness of staff** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Friendliness and Helpfulness of Staff</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of staff, with 1 being poor and 5 being exceptional.

### 12. Cleanliness of the Venue and Restrooms
**Cleanliness of the venue and restrooms** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Cleanliness of Venue and Restrooms</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): Rate the cleanliness of the venue and restrooms, with 1 being poor and 5 being exceptional.

### 13. Value of the Ticket Price
**Rate the value for the ticket price** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Value of the Ticket Price</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): Choose how you feel about the value of the ticket price, with options for Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair, and Poor Value.

### 14. Wait Times for Attractions
**Were the wait times for attractions acceptable?** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Wait Times for Attractions</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): Choose how you feel about the wait times for attractions, with options for True, Mostly, No - Too long.

### 15. Navigation of the Event
**Was the event easy to navigate?** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Navigation of the Event</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): Choose how easy you found it to navigate the event, with options for Very Easy, Easy, Neutral, and Difficult.

### 16. Future Plans
**How likely are you to recommend this carnival to others?** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">How Likely to Recommend</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): Rate how likely you are to recommend the carnival to others, with 1 being not likely, 10 being very likely.

### 17. Plans to Attend Next Year
**Do you plan to attend next year?** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Plans to Attend Next Year</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">required</span>): Choose whether you plan to attend next year, with options for True, False, and Maybe.

### 18. Additional Feedback
**Any other feedback?** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Additional Feedback</span> (<span style="font-weight: bold;">optional</span>): If you have any other comments or suggestions, please let us know.
