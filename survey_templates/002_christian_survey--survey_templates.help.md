# Christian Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects demographic and spiritual information from participants to help the church community understand its members better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the **demographics page**, which includes the following fields:
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
	* Age Group: Select your age group from the provided options.
2. Move on to the **spiritual background page**, where you'll find:
	* Christian Denomination: Select your Christian denomination from the list.
	* How many years have you been Christian?: Enter the number of years you've been following Christianity.
3. The **spiritual practices page** includes:
	* How often do you pray?: Select how often you pray.
	* How often do you read the Bible?: Select how often you read the Bible.
	* How would you rate your spiritual growth in the last year?: Rate your spiritual growth from the options provided.
4. On the **community involvement page**, you'll find:
	* What is your level of church involvement?: Select your level of involvement from the options.
	* In what areas do you serve?: Select all the areas you're involved in.
5. Finally, you'll have the option to provide feedback on the **community feedback page**.
