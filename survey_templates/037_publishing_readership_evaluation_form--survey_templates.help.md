# Publishing Readership Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the readership of a publication, helping us improve its content, format, and distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the pages below and provide your honest feedback on each.
2. Answer each question carefully, considering the specific aspects of the publication being evaluated.
3. Use the options provided for each page to select your response.
4. Once you've completed all pages, submit your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form 1 Page** (`form_1_page`, text, optional): Please provide a brief summary of your experience with the publication. This can include any thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about its overall quality and relevance.
* **Page 2** (`form_2_page`, select_multiple, optional): On a scale of 1-2, how relevant was the publication to your needs or interests?
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Form 3 Page** (`form_3_page`, number, optional): How many times have you accessed the publication in the past?
* **Page 4** (`form_4_page`, select_one, optional): How would you rate the publication's accessibility?
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Form 5 Page** (`form_5_page`, number, optional): How many hours per week do you spend reading the publication?
* **Page 6** (`form_6_page`, date, optional): When did you first start reading the publication?
* **Form 7 Page** (`form_7_page`, select_one, optional): How likely are you to recommend the publication to others?
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Form 8 Page** (`form_8_page`, time, optional): How much time per week do you spend reading the publication?
* **Page 9** (`form_9_page`, text, optional): Any additional feedback or comments about the publication?
* **Page 10** (`form_10_page`, select_multiple, optional): On a scale of 1-2, how would you rate the publication's user experience?
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Form 11 Page** (`form_11_page`, text, optional): Please describe any difficulties or challenges you faced while reading the publication.
* **Page 12** (`form_12_page`, text, optional): Do you have any suggestions for improving the publication?
* **Form 13 Page** (`form_13_page`, text, optional): Have you read any other publications related to this one?
* **Page 14** (`form_14_page`, text, optional): Do you have any other comments or suggestions about the publication?
* **Page 15** (`form_15_page`, text, optional): Are there any other publications you would recommend for similar topics?
* **Page 16** (`form_16_page`, text, optional): Is there anything else you would like to add or mention about the publication?
