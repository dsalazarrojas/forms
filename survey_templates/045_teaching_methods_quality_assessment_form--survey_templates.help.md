# Teaching Methods Quality Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the quality of teaching methods used in educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the teaching method that was used (Lecture, Demonstration, Guided Practice, or Independent Practice) from the "Methods of Teaching" fields on pages 1 and 4.
2.  Enter your rating for the method's quality on pages 5, 6, and 7. Each rating field has an associated number value from 0 to 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1:  <page_1>
*   **Methods of Teaching** (`method_1`, select_one, **optional**): Select a method that was used (Lecture, Demonstration, Guided Practice, or Independent Practice) to teach.
*   **Prophets 1** (`prophets_1`, note, **optional**): Please include any relevant notes about the teaching method or any notable experiences with it.
### Page 2:  (No fields)
### Page 3:  (No fields)
### Page 4:  <page_4>
*   **Methods of Teaching** (`method_2`, select_one, **optional**): Select another method that was used (Lecture, Demonstration, Guided Practice, or Independent Practice) to teach.
### Page 5:  <page_5>
*   **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, number, **optional**): Rate the quality of the method selected on page 1 on a scale of 0 to 5.
### Page 6:  <page_6>
*   **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, number, **optional**): Rate the quality of the method selected on page 4 on a scale of 0 to 5.
### Page 7:  <page_7>
*   **Rating 3** (`rating_3`, number, **optional**): Rate the overall quality of the teaching experience on a scale of 0 to 5.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of the teaching method's effectiveness before completing the ratings.
*   If a method's quality rating is 0, please explain the reasoning in the "Prophets 1" note field.
*   The optional fields are meant for additional information or notes about the teaching experience. However, they do not affect the overall assessment.
