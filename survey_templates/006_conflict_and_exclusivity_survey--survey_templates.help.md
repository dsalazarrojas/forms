# Conflict And Exclusivity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about potential conflicts of interest that may affect your work or relationships with the company. It is crucial to provide accurate and honest answers to help the organization maintain a fair and transparent work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Read each question carefully and answer truthfully
- Fill out all the required fields (marked with an asterisk *)
- Provide details for any outside employment or relationships
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, *): Provide your full name as an employee of the company.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, *): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Department** (`department`, text): Select your department or division within the company.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, *): Enter your current job title.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, *): Enter the date of this survey.
* **Do You Have Outside Employment?** (`outside_employment`, select_one, *):
	+ Select `True` if you have work outside of your primary job.
	+ Select `False` if you do not have outside employment.
* **Outside Employer Details** (`outside_employer_details`, text, optional): If you selected `True` for the previous question, provide the name and nature of your outside employer.
* **Does Outside Work Conflict with Company Hours?** (`outside_hours_conflict`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select `True` if your outside work conflicts with company hours.
	+ Select `False` if it does not conflict.
* **Do You Have Financial Interest in Competitors?** (`financial_interest`, select_one, *):
	+ Select `True` if you have a financial interest in competing businesses.
	+ Select `False` if you do not have a financial interest.
* **Financial Interest Details** (`financial_interest_details`, text, optional): If you selected `True` for the previous question, describe the nature and extent of your financial interest.
* **Do You Serve on Any Board of Directors?** (`board_membership`, select_one, *):
	+ Select `True` if you serve on a board of directors for another company.
	+ Select `False` if you do not serve on a board of directors.
* **Board Membership Details** (`board_details`, text, optional): If you selected `True` for the previous question, list the organizations and your role on the board.
* **Do Family Members Work for Competitors?** (`family_employment_conflict`, select_one, *):
	+ Select `True` if your family members are employed by competing organizations.
	+ Select `False` if they are not employed by competing organizations.
* **Family Conflict Details** (`family_conflict_details`, text, optional): If you selected `True` for the previous question, describe the relationship and organization.
* **Do You Have Relationship with Company Vendors?** (`vendor_relationship`, select_one, *):
	+ Select `True` if you have a personal connection to suppliers or vendors.
	+ Select `False` if you do not have a relationship with vendors.
* **Vendor Relationship Details** (`vendor_relationship_details`, text, optional): If you selected `True` for the previous question, describe the nature of the relationship.
* **Do You Have Interest in Company Clients or Suppliers?** (`client_supplier_interest`, select_one, *):
	+ Select `True` if you have financial or personal interest in business partners.
	+ Select `False` if you do not have an interest.
* **Client or Supplier Interest Details** (`client_supplier_details`, text, optional): If you selected `True` for the previous question, describe the interest and organization.
* **Have You Used Confidential Information for Personal Gain?** (`confidential_information_use`, select_one, *):
	+ Select `True` if you have misused company confidential information.
	+ Select `False` if you have not misused company confidential information.
* **Confidential Information Details** (`confidential_info_details`, text, optional): If you selected `True` for the previous question, explain the circumstances.
* **Are You Subject to an Exclusivity Agreement?** (`exclusivity_agreement`, select_one, *):
	+ Select `True` if you are under an agreement limiting your work with others.
	+ Select `False` if you are not under an agreement.
* **Exclusivity Agreement Details** (`exclusivity_details`, text, optional): If you selected `True` for the previous question, describe the terms and parties involved.
* **Are You Bound by a Non-Compete Agreement?** (`non_compete_agreement`, select_one, *):
	+ Select `True` if you are under a non-compete agreement from previous employment.
	+ Select `False` if you are not under a non-compete agreement.
* **Non-Compete Agreement Details** (`non_compete_details`, text, optional): If you selected `True` for the previous question, describe the scope and duration.
* **Additional Disclosures** (`additional_disclosures`, text, optional): If you have any other potential conflicts to disclose, provide them in this field.
* **I Declare This Information is Accurate** (`declaration_accuracy`, select_one, *):
	+ Select `Yes - I Confirm` if you confirm the accuracy of the information.
	+ Select `False` if you do not confirm the accuracy of the information.
