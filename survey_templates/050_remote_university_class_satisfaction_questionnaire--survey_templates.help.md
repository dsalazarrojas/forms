# Remote University Class Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote University Class Satisfaction Questionnaire is a form designed to collect feedback from students about their experience in a remote university class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please select the course you are providing feedback about from the "Course" field.
2.  Rate the instructor's performance, communication, and overall quality using the provided rating scales.
3.  Enter your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields.
4.  Provide any additional feedback in the "Additional Feedback" field.
5.  Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Remote University Class Satisfaction Questionnaire** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your experience in the remote university class.
* **Course Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the course you are providing feedback about.
* **Remote Learning Experience** (Select One, Required): Rate your experience in the remote class as Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, or Excellent.
* **Instructor Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
* **Instructor Performance** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the ways in which the instructor performed well or poorly.
* **Course Materials** (Select One, Required): Rate the quality of the course materials as Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, or Excellent.
* **Communication** (Select One, Required): Rate the communication from the instructor as Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, or Excellent.
* **Additional Feedback** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have for improving the course.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any follow-up questions.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Course Rating** (Number, Required): Rate the overall quality of the course using a numerical score out of 10.
* **Instructor Rating** (Number, Required): Rate the quality of the instructor using a numerical score out of 10.
* **Communication Rating** (Number, Required): Rate the communication from the instructor using a numerical score out of 10.
* **Course Materials Rating** (Number, Required): Rate the quality of the course materials using a numerical score out of 10.
* **Overall Experience** (Number, Required): Rate your overall experience in the course using a numerical score out of 10.
* **Course** (Text, Required): Enter the course name and number.
* **Instructor** (Text, Required): Enter the instructor's name and ID.
* **Course ID** (Text, Required): Enter the course ID number.
* **Remote University** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the remote university.
* **Course Section** (Text, Required): Enter the course section number.
* **Course Number** (Number, Required): Enter the course number.
* **Semester** (Select One, Required): Select the semester in which you took the course.
* **Year** (Number, Required): Enter the year you took the course.
* **Instructor ID** (Text, Required): Enter the instructor's ID number.
