# Historical Preservation Interest Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Historical Preservation Interest Poll" form is designed to gather information on your interests and preferences in historical preservation activities. Your responses will help us better understand your needs and priorities in this field.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select all that apply for each of the following questions:
	* Preservation Interest (Question 1)
	* Participation Interest (Question 2)
	* Participation Frequency (Question 3)
	* Participation Methods (Question 4)
	* Conservation Priority (Question 5)
2. If you have any other preservation interests or methods not listed, please provide them in the "Other Preservation Interests" and "Other Preservation Methods" fields.
3. Finally, please provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preservation Interest (1)**: This question is about your level of interest in preserving historical sites and objects.
* **Participation Interest (2)**: This question is about your willingness to participate in historical preservation activities. Select all methods that apply to you.
* **Participation Frequency (3)**: This question is about how often you participate in historical preservation activities.
* **Participation Methods (4)**: This question is about the methods you prefer to participate in preservation activities. Select all that apply to you.
* **Conservation Priority (5)**: This question is about identifying your top priority for preserving historical preservation. Select one option that best represents your priority.
* **Contact Information (6)**: Please provide your email address and/or phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Other Preservation Interests (7)**: This field is for you to describe any other preservation interests you may have that are not listed.
* **Other Preservation Methods (8)**: This field is for you to describe any other preservation methods you may be interested in that are not listed.
* **Other Preservation Methods (8)**: This field is for you to describe any other preservation methods you may be interested in that are not listed.
