# PTSD Symptom Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PTSD Symptom Checklist is a form designed to help individuals document and track symptoms associated with post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD). It is not a diagnostic tool, but rather a tool to help individuals become more aware of their symptoms and potentially discuss them with healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by clicking on the first page labeled "Screening". This page has a text field where you can briefly describe your situation and any symptoms you're experiencing.
2. Proceed to the second page labeled "Symptoms", where you will select from a list of various symptoms commonly associated with PTSD. Please choose all symptoms that apply to you.
3. You can also add any additional notes or comments about your symptoms on the third page labeled "Notes".
4. If you have a contact email, you can optionally provide it on the fourth page labeled "Contact Email".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Screening
• **Screening** (text field): Briefly describe your current situation, including any symptoms you're experiencing or concerns you have.

### Symptoms
• **Affective Symptoms** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Feeling of guilt or regret
  - Loss of interest in activities
  - Loss of pleasure or enjoyment
  - Irritability or anxiety
  - Difficulty in concentrating
  - Feeling of hopelessness or despair

• **Anhedonia** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Feeling that nothing gives me pleasure or enjoyment
  - Feeling of emotional numbness or detachment
  - Feeling of disconnection from life or activities

• **Anxiety** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Feeling of fear or anxiety in response to reminders of the trauma
  - Physiological symptoms of anxiety (e.g., rapid heartbeat, sweating)
  - Feeling of unease or discomfort in social situations

• **Anxious Arousal** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Feeling of being "on the edge" or hyper-vigilant
  - Difficulty falling or staying asleep
  - Concentration problems
  - Feeling of irritability or restlessness

• **Argasias** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Feeling of being overwhelmed or burdened
  - Feeling of being trapped or stuck

• **Compassion** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Feeling of difficulty with empathizing with others
  - Feeling of difficulty showing emotions

• **Dissociation** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Feeling of detachment from one's body or senses
  - Feeling of being disconnected from one's emotions or thoughts
  - Feeling of being in a different place or time

• **Dissociative Symptoms** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Feeling of amnesia (memory loss)
  - Feeling of depersonalization (feeling like you're not in control)
  - Feeling of feeling like you're observing yourself from outside your body

• **Fear** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Feeling of persistent and generalized fear
  - Avoidance behaviors (e.g., avoiding people, places, activities)
  - Physiological symptoms of fear (e.g., rapid heartbeat, trembling)

• **Hypervigilance** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Feeling of being "on the lookout" for potential threats
  - Difficulty with concentration
  - Feeling of irritability or restlessness

• **Intrusion** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Unwanted, distressing thoughts or images
  - Feeling of reliving the traumatic event
  - Feeling of emotional numbness or detachment

• **Memory Problems** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Difficulty remembering events surrounding the trauma
  - Difficulty remembering things that happened before the trauma
  - Difficulty remembering things that happened after the trauma

• **Nightmares** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Frequent nightmares about the trauma
  - Night terrors or night sweats

• **Numbing of Affective Responses** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Feeling of emotional numbness or disconnection
  - Feeling of difficulty experiencing pleasure or joy

• **Panic** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Feeling of panic or anxiety in response to reminders of the trauma
  - Physiological symptoms of panic (e.g., rapid heartbeat, trembling)

• **Rumination** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Excessive and intrusive thinking about the trauma
  - Difficulty with letting go of the trauma

• **Somatic Symptoms** (select multiple): Choose all that apply: 
  - Headaches
  - Fatigue or low energy
  - Sleep disturbances
  - Gastrointestinal problems

### Notes
• **Notes** (note field): Add any additional comments or notes about your symptoms.

### Contact Email
• **Contact Email** (email field): Optionally provide your contact email for any follow-up questions or communication.
