# Online Bookstore Upgrade Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on the online bookstore upgrade experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Online Bookstore Upgrade Feedback** (1): Enter your thoughts and comments about the online bookstore upgrade experience.
* **Product Id** (2): Enter the product ID number that you were interacting with during the upgrade experience.
* **Rating** (3): Choose one of the two rating options: "Good" or "Fair".
* **Feedback** (4): Provide more detailed comments about the positive or negative aspects of the upgrade experience.
* **Suggestions** (5): Enter any suggestions for how we can improve the online bookstore.
* **Email** (6): Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Contact Number** (7): Provide your contact number if you prefer to be contacted.
* **Website** (8): Enter your website URL if you have a website.
* **Social Media** (9): If you are active on social media and would like to be contacted, check the box for "Yes".
* **Other Comments** (10): If you have any other comments or thoughts about the upgrade experience, please enter them here.
* **Submit** (11): Once you have completed all the fields, click "Submit" to send your feedback to us.
