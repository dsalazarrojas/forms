# Epic Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Epic Feedback Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from participants about their experience with our organization's services. The survey aims to gather information that will help us identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance our services. By completing this survey, you will be helping us to better understand your needs and preferences, which will allow us to create more effective and satisfying experiences for our participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To get started, navigate to the Epic Feedback Survey page.
2. The form consists of six sections, each with its own set of questions. Please answer all the questions in each section to provide a comprehensive feedback.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). Although it's optional, we encourage you to provide detailed comments in the "comments" field, as your input is valuable to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* This is a brief introduction to get you started on the survey. You can simply type in a few words to let us know that you're ready to proceed.

### Participant Name
* This field is for you to provide your name. It's not required, but please use your name for the sake of identity and accountability. 

### Participant Email
* This field is for you to enter your email address. This will help us contact you back if we need more information or clarification on your responses. It's not required, but please use a valid email address.

### Service Name
* Please select all the services you have interacted with during your experience. This will help us understand the scope of your feedback. 

### Rating
* This field is for you to rate your experience with each selected service. You can use the provided numerical rating scale. This will help us gauge the satisfaction level of our services.

### Comments
* This is an optional field where you can provide detailed comments or feedback about your experience. It's a great place to give us more context about your thoughts and feelings.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as you like. Your feedback is valuable to us, and it will help us to make informed decisions about our services.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey! Your contribution is greatly appreciated.
