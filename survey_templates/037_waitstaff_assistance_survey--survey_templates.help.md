# Waitstaff Assistance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers about their experiences with the waitstaff. This feedback will help us improve our service and provide better assistance to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form if you have had a recent interaction with our waitstaff.
2. If you've filled out this form before, please do not fill it out again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. How friendly are our waitstaff?
* **How friendly are our waitstaff?** (`waitstaff_friendliness`, Select Multiple, Optional): 
This question is asking if our waitstaff were friendly during your interaction. You can choose one or more of the options "Yes" or "No" that best describe your experience.

### 2. How responsive are our waitstaff?
* **How responsive are our waitstaff?** (`responsiveness`, Select Multiple, Optional): 
This question is asking if our waitstaff responded quickly and effectively to your needs. You can choose one or more of the options "Yes" or "No" that best describe your experience.

### 3. How quickly do you resolve issues?
* **How quickly do you resolve issues?** (`issue_resolution`, Select Multiple, Optional): 
This question is asking if our waitstaff were able to resolve any issues you had during your interaction. You can choose one or more of the options "Yes" or "No" that best describe your experience.

### 4. What issues do you have?
* **What issues do you have?** (`feedback`, Text, Optional): 
This question is asking for any issues or problems you experienced during your interaction with our waitstaff. Please provide a brief description of your experience.

### 5. Your email
* **Your email** (`email`, Email, Optional): 
This question is asking for your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback. Please provide a valid email address.
