# Conference Planning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your preferences and needs for a conference planning process. It will help us understand your ideal conference format, location, and content to create a more enjoyable and productive experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your preferences and choices. You can select one or multiple options for each question, depending on the type of question. Don't worry, you can't go wrong!

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Please provide your name, if you'd like to be contacted or have any personal preferences.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes, if needed.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Provide your company name, if you'd like to be contacted or have any company-specific preferences.
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, select_one, required): Choose your job title or position (e.g., Executive, Manager, Individual Contributor, etc.).
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select the sector or field you work in (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Education, etc.).
* **Conference Preferences** (`conference_preferences`, note, optional): Write down any specific preferences or ideas you have for the conference.
* **Preferred Format** (`preferred_format`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to attend the conference (In Person Only, Virtual Only, Hybrid Both Options).
* **Preferred Duration** (`preferred_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the conference that suits you best (1 Day, 2 Days, 3 Days, 4-5 Days, Week Long).
* **Preferred Time of Year** (`preferred_time_of_year`, select_one, optional): Choose the time of year when you'd like to hold the conference (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter).
* **Preferred Days** (`preferred_day_of_week`, select_multiple, optional): Select the weekdays or days you prefer to attend (Weekdays Only, Include Weekend, Friday and Saturday, Saturday and Sunday).
* **Content Preferences** (`content_preferences`, note, optional): Write down any specific topics or areas of interest you'd like to have covered in the conference.
* **Preferred Session Types** (`session_types`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of sessions you'd prefer (Keynote Speeches, Panel Discussions, Breakout Sessions, Hands-on Workshops, etc.).
* **Topic Areas of Interest** (`topic_areas`, select_multiple, required): Choose the areas of interest you'd like to have covered in the conference (Industry Trends, Best Practices, Case Studies, Research Findings, etc.).
* **Networking Preferences** (`networking_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select how you'd like to connect with others (Structured Networking Sessions, Informal Mixers, Speed Networking, etc.).
* **Venue Preferences** (`venue_preferences`, note, optional): Write down any specific venue preferences or ideas you have.
* **Preferred Venue Type** (`venue_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of venue you'd prefer (Convention Center, Hotel, University, etc.).
* **Location Preference** (`location_preference`, select_one, optional): Select the geographic area you'd prefer for the conference (Downtown Urban, Suburban, etc.).
* **Pricing Preferences** (`pricing_preferences`, note, optional): Write down any specific pricing considerations or ideas you have.
* **Acceptable Price Range** (`price_range`, select_one, required): Choose the price range you'd be willing to pay (Budget, Moderate, Premium, Luxury).
* **What Should Be Included** (`what_includes`, select_multiple, optional): Select what you'd like to have included in the registration fee (All Meals, Coffee Breaks, Welcome Reception, etc.).
* **Past Conference Experience** (`past_experience`, note, optional): Write down any past conference experiences or preferences.
* **Conferences Attended Per Year** (`conferences_attended`, select_one, optional): Choose how many conferences you attend per year (1-2, 3-5, 6-10, More than 10).
* **What Matters Most** (`what_matters_most`, select_one, required): Select the top priority for you (Content Quality, Networking Opportunities, etc.).
* **Biggest Challenges** (`biggest_challenges`, text, optional): Write down any biggest challenges that prevent you from attending a conference.
* **Additional Suggestions** (`additional_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any additional ideas or suggestions you have for the conference.
