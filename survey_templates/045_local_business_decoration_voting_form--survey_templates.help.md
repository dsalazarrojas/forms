# Local Business Decoration Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users on local business decorations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Introduction: This page is the introduction to the form, and it provides an overview of what the form is for and how it works.
2. Decoration Type: Choose one of the three options for the type of decoration you are rating.
3. Color Scheme: Select multiple color schemes that you think are present in the decoration.
4. Decoration Name: Enter the name of the decoration you are rating.
5. Category: Choose a category that best fits the decoration.
6. Decoration Image: Enter a brief description of the decoration image.
7. Decoration Details: Select multiple options that describe the details of the decoration.
8. Vote: Choose your vote for the decoration.
9. Submit Vote: Submit your vote to complete the form.
10. Comments: Provide any additional comments about the decoration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Page 1)
* **Introduction** (`text`, optional): This is the introduction to the form.

### Decoration Type (Page 2)
* **Decoration Type** (`select_one`, optional): Choose one of the three options for the type of decoration you are rating. This will help us understand what kind of decoration you are referring to.

### Color Scheme (Page 3)
* **Color Scheme** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple color schemes that you think are present in the decoration. This will help us understand the color palette used in the decoration.

### Decoration Name (Page 4)
* **Decoration Name** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the decoration you are rating.

### Category (Page 5)
* **Category** (`select_one`, optional): Choose a category that best fits the decoration. This will help us understand how to group similar decorations.

### Decoration Image (Page 6)
* **Decoration Image** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the decoration image.

### Decoration Details (Page 7)
* **Decoration Details** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple options that describe the details of the decoration. This will help us understand the features of the decoration.

### Vote (Page 8)
* **Vote** (`select_one`, optional): Choose your vote for the decoration. This will help us understand your opinion of the decoration.

### Submit Vote (Page 9)
* **Submit Vote** (`select_one`, optional): Submit your vote to complete the form. This is the final step to ensure your opinion is recorded.

### Comments (Page 10)
* **Comments** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional comments about the decoration.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are only completing this form once per business.
* Be honest and provide constructive feedback to help us understand your opinion.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
