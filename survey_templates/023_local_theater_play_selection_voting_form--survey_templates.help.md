# Local Theater Play Selection Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user opinions and preferences on local theater plays. It's essential to complete this form to help us better understand what plays our community is interested in seeing. Your input will be used to decide which plays to feature at the theater.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred local theater play on the first page. You can choose multiple options by selecting "Yes" for each play you like.
2. On the second page, you can add any additional information you'd like to share about the selected play.
3. On the third page, enter your contact information, including your email address.
4. On the fourth page, select "Yes" next to your preferred play to submit your vote.
5. On the fifth page, enter any other contact information you'd like to share.
6. On the sixth page, select the date you would like to attend the selected play (if it's scheduled).
7. On the seventh page, select the time you would like to attend the selected play (if it's scheduled).
8. On the eighth page, provide any more information about why you're interested in the selected play.
9. On the ninth page, enter any other details you'd like to add.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Play Selection**: Select your preferred local theater play. You can choose multiple options by selecting "Yes" for each play you like.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share about the selected play.
* **Contact Info (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Vote**: Select "Yes" next to your preferred play to submit your vote.
* **Contact**: Enter any other contact information you'd like to share.
* **Date**: Select the date you would like to attend the selected play (if it's scheduled).
* **Time**: Select the time you would like to attend the selected play (if it's scheduled).
* **More Information**: Provide any more information about why you're interested in the selected play.
