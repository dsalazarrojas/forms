# Santa Breakfast Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from attendees of a breakfast event hosted by Santa Claus. The survey aims to collect information on the event's date, time, location, attendees' satisfaction, food quality, and other aspects of the event. By completing this form, you will be providing valuable insights to help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct date for the event from the calendar.
2. Choose the correct time for the event from the time slots.
3. Enter the location of the event.
4. Fill in your name as it is on your ID or business card.
5. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
6. Enter your phone number for contact.
7. Optionally, add any comments about the event.
8. Choose how satisfied you were with the event, food, and food quality.
9. Indicate if you would attend the event again.
10. Make any additional recommendations for the event.
11. Choose how often you attend events like this.
12. If you have any comments, feel free to add them in the space provided.
13. Check if you would like to opt-in for email communication.
14. Select the social media platforms you would like to be published on.
15. Check if you would like your photo to be taken or shared on social media.
16. Check if you would like your photo to be published online.
17. Confirm your consent to be featured on social media.
18. Indicate your consent to be contacted by email.
19. Confirm if you would like to be published on social media.
20. Check the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time of the event from the time slots.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it is on your ID or business card.

* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
