# Wildfire Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildfire Risk Assessment Form is a tool used to assess the risk of wildfire in a household. It is designed to gather detailed information about the household's physical properties, safety measures, and emergency preparedness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please proceed through each page, answering all questions to the best of your ability. Be sure to answer each question honestly, and do not leave any fields blank unless explicitly instructed to do so. If you are unsure about any question, please make an educated guess or seek assistance from a relevant authority.
