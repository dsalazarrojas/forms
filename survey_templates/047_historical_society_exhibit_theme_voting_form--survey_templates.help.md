# historical_society_exhibit_theme_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and preferences on exhibit themes and dates from historical society exhibit stakeholders. It's crucial to ensure that each submission is unique and not a duplicate, as it will impact the exhibit's overall theme and schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all relevant themes from the "Historical Society Exhibit Theme" section.
2. Choose a preferred date and time slot for the exhibit from the "Exhibit Date" and "Opening Time" and "Closing Time" fields.
3. Enter the exhibit duration in hours, using the "Exhibit Length" field.
4. Select a theme preference from the "Theme Preferences" section.
5. Enter a valid email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Enter a phone number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Historical Society Exhibit Theme** (`survey_theme`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all relevant themes for the exhibit. This field allows you to choose multiple themes from the provided options.
* **Exhibit Date** (`preferred_exhibit_date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose a date for the exhibit. This field is used to determine the preferred date for the exhibit.
* **Opening Time** (`opening_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the opening time for the exhibit. This field is used to determine the opening time slot for the exhibit.
* **Closing Time** (`closing_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the closing time for the exhibit. This field is used to determine the closing time slot for the exhibit.
* **Exhibit Length** (`exhibit_length`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the length of the exhibit in hours.
* **Theme Preferences** (`theme_preferences`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select a preference for the theme of the exhibit.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a phone number for follow-up communication.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all relevant themes for the exhibit.
* Enter a valid email address and phone number for follow-up communication.
* Ensure that each submission is unique and not a duplicate.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
