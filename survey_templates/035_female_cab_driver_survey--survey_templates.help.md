# Female Cab Driver Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from female cab drivers on their experiences, challenges, and opportunities in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any difficulties or need clarification on a question, feel free to reach out to us.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- You can skip questions that are marked as optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your job title** (1, text, required)
	+ This is the first question, which asks about your current job title as a cab driver.
* **How many hours do you drive per week** (2, number, optional)
	+ This question is asking about the number of hours you drive per week. Please answer with a specific number.
* **What type of vehicle do you drive** (3, select_one, optional)
	+ This question is asking about the type of vehicle you drive. Please select one of the available options: Sedan, SUV, or Van.
* **What is your income range** (4, select_multiple, optional)
	+ This question is asking about your income range. Please select all that apply from the available options: Low, Medium, or High.
