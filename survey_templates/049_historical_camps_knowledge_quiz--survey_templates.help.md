# Historical Camps Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about learners' knowledge on historical camps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and other required personal information in the appropriate fields.
2. Choose categories related to the historical camp in question.
3. Select the correct dates of historical significance related to the camp.
4. Provide your email address for communication and feedback.
5. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the optional text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: `Historical Camps Knowledge Quiz` (optional): This is the title of the quiz that will be displayed on the results page.
* **Learner Information** (`survey_template_2`, text, optional): Enter your name, any relevant details about yourself, or your team's name and department.
* **Category** (`survey_template_3`, select_one, optional): Choose a category that best describes the historical camp in question. For example, 'Ancient', 'Medieval', 'Modern'.
* **Category** (`survey_template_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple categories that best describe the historical camp. For example, 'Ancient', 'Medieval', 'Modern', and 'Colonial'.
* **Date** (`survey_template_5`, date, optional): Enter the date of the historical event or time period related to the camp.
* **Time** (`survey_template_6`, time, optional): Enter the time of day or period of the historical event or time period related to the camp.
* **Email** (`survey_template_7`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and feedback.
* **Phone** (`survey_template_8`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication and feedback.
* **Historical Camps Knowledge Quiz** (`survey_template_9`, note, optional): This is an optional field for you to provide additional comments or feedback.
