# Job Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on their job satisfaction, work-life balance, and company policies. This survey helps employers understand what they can improve on to create a better work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly, considering your current job and work situation.
2. Select one or multiple options for each question, depending on the type of question (select_one or select_multiple).
3. For numerical questions (Job Satisfaction 10, Job Satisfaction 10.5, Time Spent 5), enter a whole number.
4. For Time Spent, enter the number of hours spent working per week.
5. You can add any additional comments or notes you'd like in the "Note" field.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.
7. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Satisfaction Page** (`job_satisfaction_page`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any comments or feedback about your job.
* **Work Life** (`work_life`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes your work-life balance:
	+ Option 1: Work-life balance is ideal
	+ Option 2: Somewhat satisfied with work-life balance
	+ Option 3: Somewhat dissatisfied with work-life balance
	+ Option 4: Dissatisfied with work-life balance
	+ Option 5: Not sure
	+ Option 6: Prefer not to answer
	+ Option 7: Other
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional): Enter any feedback about your communication with colleagues, managers, or other team members.
* **Manager** (`manager`, select_multiple, optional): Select all managers you have at your work (e.g., team leader, supervisor, direct manager):
	+ Option 1: Team Leader
	+ Option 2: Supervisor
	+ Option 3: Direct Manager
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify)
	+ Option 5: Don't have a manager
	+ Option 6: Prefer not to answer
	+ Option 7: Other (please specify)
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, text, optional): Enter any feedback about your job satisfaction, including positive or negative comments.
* **Career Growth** (`career_growth`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes your career growth:
	+ Option 1: Satisfying growth opportunities
	+ Option 2: Somewhat satisfying growth opportunities
	+ Option 3: Somewhat dissatisfying growth opportunities
	+ Option 4: Dissatisfying growth opportunities
	+ Option 5: Not sure
	+ Option 6: Prefer not to answer
	+ Option 7: Other
* **Company Culture** (`company_culture`, select_multiple, optional): Select all aspects of company culture you experience:
	+ Option 1: Collaborative culture
	+ Option 2: Flexible work hours
	+ Option 3: Open communication
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify)
	+ Option 5: Prefer not to answer
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes your benefits package:
	+ Option 1: Satisfying benefits package
	+ Option 2: Somewhat satisfying benefits package
	+ Option 3: Somewhat dissatisfying benefits package
	+ Option 4: Dissatisfying benefits package
	+ Option 5: Not sure
	+ Option 6: Prefer not to answer
	+ Option 7: Other
* **Job Security** (`job_security`, select_multiple, optional): Select all aspects of job security you experience:
	+ Option 1: Job security is high
	+ Option 2: Job security is low
	+ Option 3: Somewhat secure in my job
	+ Option 4: Somewhat insecure in my job
	+ Option 5: Prefer not to answer
	+ Option 6: Other
* **Job Fulfillment** (`job_fulfillment`, text, optional): Enter any comments about your job fulfillment, including positive or negative feelings.
* **Job Ambition** (`job_ambition`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes your career ambition:
	+ Option 1: High ambition to advance in the company
	+ Option 2: Medium ambition to advance in the company
	+ Option 3: Low ambition to advance in the company
	+ Option 4: No ambition to advance in the company
	+ Option 5: Prefer not to answer
	+ Option 6: Other
* **Company Mission** (`company_mission`, select_multiple, optional): Select all aspects of company mission you align with:
	+ Option 1: Fully align with company mission
	+ Option 2: Somewhat align with company mission
	+ Option 3: Somewhat misalign with company mission
	+ Option 4: Not align with company mission
	+ Option 5: Prefer not to answer
	+ Option 6: Other
* **Job Satisfaction 10** (`job_satisfaction_10`, number, optional): Enter a whole number from 1 to 10 to rate your job satisfaction.
* **Job Satisfaction 10.5** (`job_satisfaction_10_5`, number, optional): Enter a whole number from 1 to 10.5 to rate your job satisfaction.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, time, optional): Enter the number of hours spent working per week.
* **Time Spent 5** (`time_spent_5`, number, optional): Enter a whole number from 0 to 60 to rate your time spent working.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

Tips: 

* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Note" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative.

This help guide should provide end-users with a clear understanding of the Job Satisfaction Survey form's purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means.
