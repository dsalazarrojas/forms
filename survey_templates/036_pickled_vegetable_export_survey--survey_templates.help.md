# Pickled Vegetable Export Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your pickled vegetable export business. Please complete this form to help us better understand your export activities and provide us with valuable data to improve our services. This survey will only take a few minutes to complete.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
2. Choose the correct options from the provided lists.
3. Be accurate when filling out the form, as any incorrect information may affect the accuracy of our survey results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your export survey?** (`export_survey`, `text`, required)
	+ This is the title of your export survey, which is used to describe your export business.
	+ Provide a brief description of your export survey.
* **Which destinations do you export to?** (`destinations`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Select all the countries or regions you export to, such as USA, Mexico, Canada, China, Japan, Korea, Europe, or Other Country.
* **What pickled vegetable products do you export?** (`export_products`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select the pickled vegetable products you export, such as Pickled Cucumbers, Pickled Peppers, Pickled Olives, Pickled Tomatoes, Pickled Beets, Pickled Onions, or Pickled Carrots.
* **How much do you export per shipment?** (`quantity`, `number`, required)
	+ Enter the quantity of pickled vegetables you export per shipment.
* **Do you face any operational issues?** (`operational_issues`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Select yes or no if you face any operational issues.
* **Which export channels do you use?** (`export_channels`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select one export channel you use, such as Trade Shows, Online Marketplaces, Retailers, or Exporters Associations.
* **What is the export value per shipment?** (`export_value`, `number`, required)
	+ Enter the export value per shipment.
* **Where do you export?** (`exported_to`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Select all the countries or regions you export to.
* **Do you face any export issues?** (`export_issues`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Select yes or no if you face any export issues.
* **Export Products 2** (`export_products_2`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Select one pickled vegetable product you export, such as Pickled Cucumbers, Pickled Peppers, Pickled Olives, Pickled Tomatoes, Pickled Beets, Pickled Onions, or Pickled Carrots (this question is optional).
* **How many total exports per shipment?** (`total_exports`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter the total number of exports per shipment (this question is optional).
