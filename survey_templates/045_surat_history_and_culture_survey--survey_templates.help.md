# Surat History And Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surat History And Culture Survey is designed to collect information about Surat's history and culture from users. This form will help to create a comprehensive picture of Surat's cultural heritage and inform decision-making processes related to research, education, and local programming.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes your experience with Surat's history and culture.
2. Provide a brief description of your experience or perspective on Surat's history and culture.
3. Share any community feedback or comments you have about Surat's history and culture.
4. Indicate whether you engage in heritage and cultural activities related to Surat.
5. Describe your decisions related to research, education, and local programming related to Surat.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Template** (`survey_template`, text, not required): This field is where you can provide any additional information or context about your experience with Surat's history and culture.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, not required): Please select the category that best describes your experience with Surat's history and culture. This will help us to group your responses and create a comprehensive picture of Surat's cultural heritage.
* **Description** (`description`, text, not required): In this field, please provide a brief description of your experience or perspective on Surat's history and culture. This will help us to understand your thoughts and opinions on the subject.
* **Community Feedback** (`community_feedback`, text, not required): If you have any community feedback or comments about Surat's history and culture, please share them here. This will help us to create a comprehensive picture of the community's perspective on the subject.
* **Heritage And Cultural Engagement** (`heritage_and_cultural_engagement`, select_multiple, not required): Please indicate whether you engage in heritage and cultural activities related to Surat. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Research, Education, And Local Programming Decisions** (`research_education_programming`, text, not required): In this field, please describe your decisions related to research, education, and local programming related to Surat. This will help us to create a comprehensive picture of the subject matter.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and clear information.
* Do not duplicate any information that you have already provided in previous forms or surveys.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
