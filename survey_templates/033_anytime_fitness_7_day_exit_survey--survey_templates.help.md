# Anytime Fitness 7 Day Exit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anytime Fitness 7 Day Exit Survey is a critical tool to gather valuable insights from departing members to improve the overall member experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully to ensure you understand the question and select the most accurate answer.
2.  Answer every question honestly, and to the best of your ability.
3.  If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification or skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Member Name (<name>, <type>: text, required)
Member Name: Your full name.

### 2. Member ID (<name>, <type>: text, required)
Member ID: Your membership number.

### 3. Email Address (<name>, <type>: email, required)
Email Address: Your contact email.

### 4. Home Club Location (<name>, <type>: text, required)
Home Club Location: The gym where you joined.

### 5. Join Date (<name>, <type>: date, required)
Join Date: The date you joined the gym.

### 6. Cancellation Date (<name>, <type>: date, required)
Cancellation Date: The date you cancelled your membership.

### 7. Membership Type (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Membership Type: The type of membership you held.

### 8. Facility Usage (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Facility Usage: How often you visited the gym.

### 9. Fitness Goals (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, required)
Fitness Goals: Select all the goals you aimed to achieve while at the gym.

### 10. Goals Acheived (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Goals Achieved: Indicate the extent to which you achieved your fitness goals.

### 11. Overall Experience Rating (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Overall Experience Rating: Rate your experience at the gym.

### 12. Facility Cleanliness (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Facility Cleanliness: Rate the cleanliness of the gym.

### 13. Equipment Quality and Availability (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Equipment Quality and Availability: Rate the quality and availability of equipment.

### 14. Staff Friendliness (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Staff Friendliness: Rate the friendliness of the staff.

### 15. Staff Helpfulness (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Staff Helpfulness: Rate the helpfulness of the staff.

### 16. Value for Money (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Value for Money: Rate the gym's value for money.

### 17. Reason for Cancellation (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Reason for Cancellation: Choose the reason for cancelling your membership.

### 18. Cancellation Reason Details (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
Cancellation Reason Details: Provide more details about your reason for cancellation.

### 19. Used Personal Training (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Used Personal Training: Indicate whether you used personal training services.

### 20. Personal Training Rating (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Personal Training Rating: Rate your experience with personal training, if applicable.

### 21. Used Group Classes (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Used Group Classes: Indicate whether you attended group fitness classes.

### 22. Group Classes Rating (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Group Classes Rating: Rate your experience with group classes, if applicable.

### 23. Experience During Peak Hours (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Experience During Peak Hours: Describe your experience during peak hours.

### 24. Would Recommend (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Would Recommend: Indicate whether you would recommend the gym to friends or family.

### 25. Consider Returning (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Consider Returning: Indicate your willingness to return to the gym.

### 26. Suggestions for Improvement (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
Suggestions for Improvement: Provide any suggestions you have for improvement.

### 27. Additional Comments (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
Additional Comments: Share any additional thoughts or feedback.

### 28. Survey Completion Date (<name>, <type>: date, required)
Survey Completion Date: The date you submitted this survey.

## Tips

*   Take your time to answer each question carefully.
*   Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
*   If unsure, feel free to ask for clarification or skip it.
*   Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve the member experience.
