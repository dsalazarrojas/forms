# Aerospace Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Aerospace Interest Form is designed to gather information about you and your interest in the aerospace field. It is intended for students and professionals looking to explore, engage, or advance in the field of aerospace.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Personal Details** : 
   - Click on the Personal Details section. 
   - Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name." 
   - Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name." 
   - Enter your Email Address in the field labeled "Email Address." 

2. **Current Status** : 
   - Select your current status from the options provided: High School Student, Undergraduate Student, Graduate Student, Researcher, Industry Professional, or Hobbyist. 

3. **Areas of Interest** : 
   - This is a header section to indicate you are moving on to the next part of the form. It is not a field that requires input. 

4. **Primary Interest Area** : 
   - Select your primary interest area from the options provided: Propulsion Systems, Aerodynamics, Space Exploration, Avionics & Systems, Materials Science, or Drone Technology (UAVs). 

5. **Type of Opportunity Sought** : 
   - Select all opportunities that interest you from the options provided: Internship / Co-op, Full-time Employment, Research Collaboration, Mentorship, Educational Workshops, or Networking Events. 

6. **Experience Level** (optional): 
   - If applicable, select your experience level from the options provided: No experience yet, Beginner (some projects), Intermediate (academic/internship exp), Advanced (professional exp). 

7. **Why Aerospace?** : 
   - If you have space, briefly tell us about your passion for the aerospace field. 

8. **Newsletter Subscription** : 
   - Select whether you wish to be subscribed to the newsletter for aerospace news.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Current Status** (select_one, required): Your current status in the aerospace field.
* **Primary Interest Area** (select_one, required): Select the area that excites you most in the aerospace field.
* **Type of Opportunity Sought** (select_multiple, required): Choose all opportunities that you are interested in.
* **Experience Level** (select_one, optional): If applicable, select your current level of experience in the aerospace field.
* **Why Aerospace?** (text, optional): Briefly describe why you are interested in the aerospace field.
* **Newsletter Subscription** (select_one, optional): Choose whether you wish to be subscribed to the newsletter for aerospace news.

## Tips

- Please ensure that you are submitting genuine information as it will be used to tailor opportunities and information according to your submitted answers.
- Ensure that you enter a valid email address to be contacted and receive the newsletter if you opt-in. 
- This form is intended to gauge your interests and help you find relevant opportunities in the aerospace field.
