# Customization Effects Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect personal information from users for customization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
- Enter your First Name and Last Name as they appear on your official identification documents.
- Enter a valid Email address that your team can use to contact you.
- If applicable, select your favorite option for the "Favorite Option" question.
- Enter your Date of Birth in the standard format (mm/dd/yyyy) and ensure it reflects your actual date of birth.
- Select a time that represents your current time (HH:MM AM/PM).
- Provide a short description of your goals for the team.
- Select the name of the person you are assigned to from the list provided.
- Enter the output of your customization effects, if any.
- Select your favorite food from the list provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1)
* First Name: The field to input your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
### Last Name (2)
* Last Name: The field to input your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
### Email (3)
* Email: The field to input a valid email address that your team can use to contact you.
### Favorite Option (4)
* Favorite Option: Select your favorite option from the provided list.
### Date of Birth (5)
* Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the standard format (mm/dd/yyyy).
### Time (6)
* Time: Select your current time (HH:MM AM/PM).
### Goals (7)
* Goals: Provide a short description of your goals for the team.
### Assigned (8)
* Assigned: Select the name of the person you are assigned to from the provided list.
### Output (9)
* Output: Enter the output of your customization effects, if any.
### Favorite Food (10)
* Favorite Food: Select your favorite food from the provided list.
### Phone (11)
* Phone: The field to input a phone number, if necessary (not required).
