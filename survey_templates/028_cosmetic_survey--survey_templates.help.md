# Cosmetic Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the "Cosmetic Survey" form, designed to collect information from respondents about their personal characteristics and preferences related to cosmetics. This survey aims to collect demographic data and gather opinions on cosmetic products and trends.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "Cosmetic Survey" form, follow these steps:

1. The form consists of three sections: an introduction, demographic information, and a question about your age.
2. Please read the form carefully and respond to each section as instructed.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cosmetic Survey** (note, required: false): This section is an introduction to the survey, providing context and background information. It is not a question to be answered, but rather a read-only section to set the stage for the rest of the form.
* **Demographic Info** (text, required: false): Please provide your demographic information, including any relevant details about yourself. This will help us understand who you are and what your preferences might be.
* **Age** (number, required: false): Enter your age to help us narrow down our results and get a better understanding of our audience. Please provide a whole number (e.g., 25 or 35, not 25-30 or "around 30").

This help guide assumes you have no prior knowledge of the form or its purpose. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please feel free to ask for clarification.
