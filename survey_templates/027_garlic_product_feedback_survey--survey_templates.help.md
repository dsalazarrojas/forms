# Garlic Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on your experience with garlic products. It will help us understand your opinion on the quality, price, and other aspects of our products. Your honest feedback will contribute to improving our products and customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For each question, choose the relevant option that best describes your experience.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the garlic product you are providing feedback on.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Choose a rating that reflects your experience with the product (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share any comments or suggestions about the product. This is your chance to tell us what we could improve on.
* **Price Range** (`product_price`, text, optional): If you'd like to share, please specify the price range you paid for the product.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number (if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion).
* **Upload Product Image** (`product_image`, date, optional): You may upload a photo of the product for us to reference your feedback.
* **Where Do You Usually Buy This Product?** (`purchase_channel`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the places where you usually buy this product (Store, Online, Other).
* **How Often Do You Buy This Product?** (`purchase_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how frequently you buy this product (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely).
* **Age** (`age_range`, number, optional): If you're comfortable sharing, please indicate your age.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address so we can respond to your feedback.
* **Comments** (`comments`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you have any comments or suggestions about our product (True, False, Undecided).
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of product you are providing feedback on (Garlic, Onion, Shallots).
* **Location** (`location`, number, optional): If you'd like to share, please indicate your location.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name so we can address you by name in our response.
* **Product Name 2** (`product_name_2`, text, required): Enter the name of the garlic product you are providing feedback on (this is a duplicate field for product identification).

Note: Please make sure to fill out all required fields and provide as much information as possible to help us better understand your feedback.
