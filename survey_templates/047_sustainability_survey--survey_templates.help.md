# Sustainability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather responses on various sustainability topics, helping to understand people's perceptions and actions related to environmental and social responsibility. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by scrolling down to the first section, "what_did_you_see".
2. In this section, you will see a text field where you can enter your response to the question "what_did_you_see".
3. Continue to the next sections, "sustainability_topics", "question_2_1", "question_2_2", and "question_2_3", where you will see more text fields for you to enter your responses.
4. For each field, type your response directly into the text field.
5. Make sure to enter your responses accurately and thoroughly to provide useful information.
6. Once you have finished entering your responses, click submit to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### what_did_you_see
• **what_did_you_see** (`what_did_you_see`, text, required: false): Please describe what you have seen in your daily life that relates to sustainability.

### sustainability_topics
• **sustainability_topics** (`sustainability_topics`, text, required: false): Please list topics that you think relate to sustainability.

### question_2_1
• **what_did_you_see** (`question_2_1`, text, required: false): Describe another event or occurrence related to sustainability you have witnessed.

### question_2_2
• **what_did_you_do** (`question_2_2`, text, required: false): Share an instance where you made a sustainable decision or took an action that promoted a sustainable practice.

### question_2_3
• **other_info** (`question_2_3`, text, required: false): If you have any additional information or thoughts about sustainability that don't fit into the previous questions, please provide them here.
