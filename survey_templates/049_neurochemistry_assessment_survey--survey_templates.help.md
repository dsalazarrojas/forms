# Neurochemistry Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess your current mental health status, self-care habits, and emotional regulation skills. The goal is to help you better understand your overall well-being and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this survey, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read through each question carefully and select the best answer that reflects your current state.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Be as accurate and honest as possible.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the questions.
