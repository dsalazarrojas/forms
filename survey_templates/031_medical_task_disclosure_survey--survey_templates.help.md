# Medical Task Disclosure Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form, "Medical Task Disclosure Survey", aims to collect information from medical staff regarding their clinical tasks, training needs, and other related responsibilities. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose the relevant clinical responsibilities that apply to your task on page 1.
2. Select the clarity level of the task on page 1, which reflects the degree of clarity in performing the task.
3. Indicate the training needs you require for completing the task on page 1.
4. (Optional) Record the actual hours spent on the clinical tasks for your convenience on page 1.
5. (Optional) Provide any staff feedback on page 1 for your supervisor or team leader.
6. (Optional) Enter your email, phone, name, or date for your contact information on page 1.
7. (Optional) Record the time spent on the clinical tasks on page 1.
8. On page 2, choose the clinical responsibilities that apply to your task.
9. Select the training needs you require for completing the task on page 2.
10. (Optional) Record the actual hours spent on the clinical tasks on page 2.
11. (Optional) Provide any staff feedback on page 2 for your supervisor or team leader.
12. (Optional) Enter your email, phone, name, or date for your contact information on page 2.
13. (Optional) Record the time spent on the clinical tasks on page 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinical Responsibilities** (`clinical_responsibilities`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the clinical responsibilities that apply to your task.
* **Task Clarity** (`task_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Select the clarity level of the task.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the training needs you require for completing the task.
* **Clinical Tasks** (`clinical_tasks`, `number`, optional): (Optional) Record the actual hours spent on the clinical tasks.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `number`, optional): (Optional) Record the actual hours spent on the clinical tasks.
* **Staff Feedback** (`staff_feedback`, `note`, optional): Provide any staff feedback on your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, optional): Enter your email for your contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for your contact information.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name for your contact information.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for your contact information.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Record the time spent on the clinical tasks.
* **Clinical Responsibilities 2** (`clinical_responsibilities_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the clinical responsibilities that apply to your task.
* **Training Needs 2** (`training_needs_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the training needs you require for completing the task.
* **Work Hours 2** (`work_hours_2`, `number`, optional): (Optional) Record the actual hours spent on the clinical tasks.
* **Staff Feedback 2** (`staff_feedback_2`, `note`, optional): Provide any staff feedback on page 2.
* **Task Clarity 2** (`task_clarity_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the clarity level of the task on page 2.
* **Clinical Tasks 2** (`clinical_tasks_2`, `number`, optional): (Optional) Record the actual hours spent on the clinical tasks.
