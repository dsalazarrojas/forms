# Energy Efficiency Compliance Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Energy Efficiency Compliance Verification Form is a tool used to verify energy efficiency compliance in businesses. It helps gather information about a company's energy consumption, equipment efficiency, and compliance status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form consists of 8 sections, each with its own set of questions. Please complete each section according to your needs.
2. For each section, provide detailed and accurate information about your business or company.
3. Ensure that you have all the required information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Info (Page 1)**: This section is for general information about your company. Please provide details about your business, including its name, address, and contact information.
* **Usage Info (Page 1)**: Here, you can provide information about the usage patterns and schedules of your company's energy-consuming equipment.
* **Equipment Efficiency (Page 1)**: Describe the efficiency of your energy-consuming equipment, including its type, model, and age.
* **Energy Consumption (Page 1)**: Report your company's energy consumption data, including the amount of energy used and the sources of energy used.
* **Certification Info (Page 1)**: Provide any relevant certification or training information related to energy efficiency.
* **Energy Audit Results (Page 1)**: Report any energy audit results, including recommended improvements and implemented measures.
* **Usage Savings (Page 1)**: Estimate any energy savings achieved through efficiency measures and describe how they were implemented.
* **Certification Status (Page 2)**: Confirm your energy efficiency compliance status, including any relevant certifications or approvals.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all the required information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
