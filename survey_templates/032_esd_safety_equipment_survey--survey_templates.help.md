# ESD Safety Equipment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the safety equipment used by an employee. It is designed to gather details about the equipment's last use, such as the duration of use, location, and the user who last used it. This data will help in identifying patterns and trends in safety equipment usage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions based on your last use of the safety equipment.
2. Select the correct option for each question, and fill in any additional details if required.
3. Complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the collected data is reliable and useful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ESD Safety Equipment Survey** (`esd_safety_equipment_survey`, `select_one`, required/optional): This is the title of the form, and it is always "ESD Safety Equipment Survey". You do not need to answer this question, but it is a required field.
* **ESD Controls Used** (`esd_controls_used`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the controls used during your last use of the safety equipment. For example, "Control 1", "Control 2", "Control 3", etc.
* **Safety Gear Used** (`safety_gear_used`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you used safety gear during your last use of the safety equipment, or "No" if you did not.
* **ESD Safety Equipment Survey Notes** (`esd_safety_equipment_survey_notes`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your last use of the safety equipment.
* **Date of Last Use** (`date_of_last_use`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last use of the safety equipment.
* **Time of Last Use** (`time_of_last_use`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your last use of the safety equipment.
* **Last Use Duration** (`last_use_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your last use of the safety equipment in any of the following units: minutes, hours, days, years, or months.
* **Last Use Location** (`last_use_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where you last used the safety equipment.
* **Was last use at work?** (`was_last_use_at_work`, `select_one`, optional): Select "True" if you last used the safety equipment at work, or "False" if you did not.
* **User Last Use** (`user_last_use`, `select_one`, optional): Select who last used the safety equipment: "Self", "Other", or "Both".
* **Other User Last Use** (`user_last_use_other_user`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who last used the safety equipment if you selected "Other" in the previous question.
* **Last Use By** (`last_use_by`, `select_one`, optional): Select who last used the safety equipment: "Self", "Another".
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or notes about your last use of the safety equipment.
* **Last Use Duration (minutes)** (`last_use_duration_minutes`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your last use of the safety equipment in minutes.
* **Last Use Duration (hours)** (`last_use_duration_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your last use of the safety equipment in hours.
* **Last Use Duration (days)** (`last_use_duration_days`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your last use of the safety equipment in days.
* **Last Use Duration (years)** (`last_use_duration_years`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your last use of the safety equipment in years.
* **Last Use Duration (months)** (`last_use_duration_months`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your last use of the safety equipment in months.
* **Last Use Duration (weeks)** (`last_use_duration_weeks`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your last use of the safety equipment in weeks.
* **Last Use Duration (days days)** (`last_use_duration_days_days`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your last use of the safety equipment in days.
* **ESD Safety Equipment Survey Form Completed By** (`esd_safety_equipment_survey_form_completed_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who completed the survey form.
* **ESD Safety Equipment Survey Form Completed** (`esd_safety_equipment_survey_form_completed`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the survey form was completed.
* **ESD Safety Equipment Survey Form Completed Time** (`esd_safety_equipment_survey_form_completed_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when the survey form was completed.
* **ESD Safety Equipment Survey Form Completed At Work** (`esd_safety_equipment_survey_form_completed_at_work`, `select_one`, optional): Select "True" if the survey form was completed at work, or "False" if it was not.
