# Neighborhood Price Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from residents of a neighborhood on various topics related to their household income, neighborhood, and survey details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Provide your home address in the "Home Address" field.
3. Enter your zip code in the "zip code" field.
4. Report your household income in the "Household Income" field.
5. Select your neighborhood from the list of options in the "Neighborhood" field.
6. Choose a corresponding cost level for your neighborhood from the "Cost" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, if applicable.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Address** (text, required): Enter the address of your residence.
* **zip code** (number, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Household Income** (number, optional): Report your household income.
* **Neighborhood** (select multiple, optional): Select your neighborhood from the list.
* **Household Income** (number, optional): Note: This field is optional and has the same label as the previous one; it is unclear if it's intended to be used for a different purpose. Please disregard it if it's not relevant.
* **Survey Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the survey.
* **Survey Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the survey.
* **Nighborhood Cost** (note, optional): Enter additional comments or notes about the neighborhood cost.
* **Cost** (select one, optional): Choose a corresponding cost level for your neighborhood.
* **Email Address** (email, optional): Enter your email address, if applicable.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Enter additional comments about your neighborhood.
* **Comments** (select multiple, optional): Select additional comments from the list, if applicable.

Please note that the fields are optional, but it is encouraged to fill them out completely for a more comprehensive survey.
