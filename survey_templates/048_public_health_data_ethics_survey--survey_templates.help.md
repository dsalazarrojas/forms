# Public Health Data Ethics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an individual's attitudes, concerns, and priorities regarding data sharing in the context of public health.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Public Health Data Ethics Survey, please follow these steps:

1.  Read the optional introduction section if you wish to learn more about the purpose and importance of the survey.
2.  Answer the questions in the demographic information section to provide information about your contact details and demographic characteristics.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or questions about the survey, please contact the survey administrator.
* The survey is designed to be completed in a short amount of time, so please take your time and answer the questions thoroughly.
