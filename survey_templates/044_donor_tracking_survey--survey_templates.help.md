## Donor Tracking Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donor Tracking Survey is a form used to collect information from donors about their donations. This form helps us to understand donor demographics, donation amounts, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct date of your donation.
2. Provide your donor information, including your name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number (if you have one).
4. Specify the amount of your donation.
5. Indicate the funds you are donating (e.g., general, specific project, etc.).
6. Add any additional information you would like to share with us.
7. Finally, let us know how you heard about us (e.g., social media, friend referral, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Date (1)**: Enter the date you made your donation.
* **Donor Information (2)**: Enter your name, which will be used to identify you as a donor.
* **Name (3)**: Enter your name again, which will be used for communication and recognition.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number if you would like us to be able to contact you.
* **Donation Amount (6)**: Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Donation Funds Used (7)**: Specify which funds you are donating to (e.g., general, specific project, etc.).
* **Additional Info (8)**: Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your donation.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (9)**: Let us know how you heard about us, so we can improve our outreach efforts.
