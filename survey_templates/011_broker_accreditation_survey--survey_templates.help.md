# Broker Accreditation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from brokers about their experience with the accreditation program. It will help identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for better service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Fill in any additional comments in the text fields.
3. Review each section carefully and provide detailed explanations when required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Broker Name** (`broker_name`, `text`, *): Enter your name as a broker.
* **License Number** (`broker_license_number`, `text`, *): Enter your broker license number.
* **Firm Name** (`firm_name`, `text`, *): Enter the name of your current firm.
* **Rate the Accreditation Process** (`accreditation_process_rating`, `select_one`, *): Rate the overall accreditation process from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Clarity of Requirements** (`clarity_of_requirements`, `select_one`, *): Rate the clarity of the accreditation requirements from Not Clear at All to Very Clear.
* **Application Ease** (`application_ease`, `select_one`, *): Rate the ease of the application process from Very Difficult to Very Easy.
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, `select_one`, *): Rate your satisfaction with the response time from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, `select_one`, *): Rate the quality of communication during the process from Very Poor to Excellent.
* **Support Staff Rating** (`support_staff_rating`, `select_one`, *): Rate the helpfulness of support staff from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Technical Support** (`technical_support`, `select_one`, *): Rate the technical support if used, from Did Not Use to Very Satisfied.
* **Resources Provided** (`resources_provided`, `select_one`, *): Rate the helpfulness of provided resources from Not Helpful to Very Helpful.
* **Training Quality** (`training_quality`, `select_one`, *): Rate the quality of any training provided, from Did Not Use to Excellent.
* **Perceived Value of Accreditation** (`accreditation_value`, `select_one`, *): Rate the perceived value of the accreditation from Not Valuable to Very Valuable.
* **Professional Benefits** (`professional_benefits`, `select_one`, *): Rate the professional benefits from No Benefits to Major Benefits.
* **Would You Recommend Accreditation** (`recommend_accreditation`, `select_one`, *): Rate the likelihood of recommending the accreditation to others from Definitely Not to Definitely Yes.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (`overall_experience_rating`, `select_one`, *): Rate your overall experience from 1 - Poor to 5 - Excellent.
* **Likelihood to Renew** (`likelihood_return`, `select_one`, *): Rate the likelihood of renewing your accreditation from Definitely Not to Definitely Yes.
* **Biggest Strength** (`biggest_strength`, `text`, *): What is the biggest strength of the accreditation program.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, *): What areas could be improved.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, *): Any other feedback you would like to share.
* **May we Contact You for Follow-up** (`follow_up_consent`, `select_one`, *): Select if you can be contacted for follow-up.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, *): Enter your email for follow-up if you selected "Yes" for the previous question.

## Tips
1. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Provide detailed explanations when required.
3. Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
