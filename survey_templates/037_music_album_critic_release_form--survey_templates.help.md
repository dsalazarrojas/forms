# Music Album Critic Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect metadata for music albums to facilitate efficient and organized data management and review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by ensuring you have the required information for the music album in question. Then, fill out the form fields accordingly, following the explanations provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Album Name** (`album_name`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the name of the music album.
* **Album URL** (`album_url`, `text`, required: true)
  Enter the URL where the music album can be accessed or downloaded.
* **Music Genre** (`music_genre`, `select_one`, required: true)
  Select the genre of the music album (e.g. Electronic, Classical, Folk, etc.).
* **Music Style** (`music_style`, `select_one`, required: true)
  Select the style of the music album (e.g. Electronic, Classical, Folk, etc.).
* **Critic Name** (`critic_name`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the name of the music critic or reviewer.
* **Music Label** (`music_label`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the label associated with the music album.
* **Music Artist** (`music_artist`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the artist associated with the music album.
* **Music Album Release Date** (`music_album_release_date`, `date`, required: true)
  Enter the release date of the music album.
* **Music Album Rating** (`music_album_rating`, `number`, required: true)
  Enter a rating for the music album (e.g. 1-5).
* **Music Album Release Status** (`music_album_release_status`, `select_one`, required: true)
  Select the release status of the music album (e.g. Unreleased, In Progress, Published).
* **Critic Feedback** (`critic_feedback`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter any feedback or comments from the music critic.
* **Music Publisher** (`music_publisher`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the publisher associated with the music album.
* **Publication Permissions** (`publication_permissions`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
  Select the permissions for publication (e.g. Print, Web, Audio, Video, Digital).
* **Critic Contact Info** (`critic_contact_info`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the contact information of the music critic.
* **Music Album Review** (`music_album_review`, `text`, required: true)
  Enter a review of the music album.
* **Music Album Rating Score** (`music_album_rating_score`, `number`, required: true)
  Enter a rating score for the music album (e.g. 1-5).
* **Publication Notes** (`publication_notes`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter any additional notes about publication.
* **Music Album Release Version** (`music_album_release_version`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the release version of the music album.
* **Music Album Tracklist** (`music_album_tracklist`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the tracklist of the music album.
* **Music Release Date Notes** (`music_release_date_notes`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter any notes about the release date.
* **Music Album Length** (`music_album_length`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the length of the music album.
* **Music Album Format** (`music_album_format`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
  Select the format of the music album (e.g. Audio, Video, CD, Vinyl, Digital, LP).
* **Music Album Cover Art** (`music_album_cover_art`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the cover art of the music album.
* **Music Album Release Notes** (`music_album_release_notes`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter any additional release notes about the music album.
* **Music Album Additional Notes** (`music_album_additional_notes`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter any additional notes about the music album.

## Tips
Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure accurate and comprehensive data collection. If you have any questions or need further clarification, refer to the field explanations above.
