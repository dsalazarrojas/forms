The "Administration Intranet Survey" form aims to collect feedback from internal stakeholders on the intranet's features, usability, and overall experience. This data will help the intranet team identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions on feature enhancements and training needs. By completing this form, you'll be contributing to the intranet's growth and development.

**Administration Intranet Survey - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form collects information on your experience and preferences regarding the intranet, helping us improve it for everyone's benefit.
## How To Complete This Form
- Follow each step below to provide your feedback and suggestions on the intranet.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Respondent information** (`respondent_information`, `note`, Optional): Please provide your name or any other relevant identifying information so we can contact you if needed.
- **Department** (`department`, `text`, Required): Select your department from the list.
- **Frequency of intranet use** (`frequency_of_use`, `select_one`, Required): Choose how often you use the intranet (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
- **Overall satisfaction** (`satisfaction_overall`, `number`, Required): Rate your satisfaction on a scale from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
- **Primary purpose for using intranet** (`primary_purpose`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the main reason you use the intranet (News, HR forms, Knowledge base, Collaboration).
- **Improvement suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, Optional): Share ideas on how to make the intranet more useful.
- **Training needed** (`training_needed`, `select_one`, Optional): Indicate whether training would improve your intranet usage.
- **Features missing from intranet** (`features_missing`, `text`, Optional): List features you would like to see added to the intranet.
- **Contact for follow up** (`contact_for_followup`, `email`, Optional): If you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions, provide your email address.
- **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, Optional): Any other feedback or suggestions you'd like to share.

Please note that all required fields are marked with **Required**. Make sure to complete them all to provide a comprehensive feedback. Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort!
