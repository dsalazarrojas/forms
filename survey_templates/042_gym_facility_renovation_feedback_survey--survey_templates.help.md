# Gym Facility Renovation Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with our gym facility after the renovation. Your input will help us understand what we did well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. If you haven't used our facility in the past week, please let us know in the space provided for "Additional Comments".
3. Answer the questions in the format specified for each type of question (select multiple for questions 1 and 2, single select for questions 3-6).
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please type them in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Which of our facilities have you used in the past week?** (`question_1`, select_multiple, not required): Please select all the facilities you've used in the past week. You can choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Gym
	+ Pool
	+ Sauna
* **What do you think of our renovation?** (`question_2`, select_one, not required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **How likely are you to recommend our facility to a friend or family member?** (`question_3`, select_one, not required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **What would you suggest we improve?** (`question_5`, text, not required): Please type in any suggestions or ideas you have for our facility.
* **Would you like to be contacted for further information about our facility?** (`question_6`, select_one, not required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **How satisfied are you with our facility overall?** (`question_7`, select_one, not required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Additional Comments** (`question_8`, note, not required): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please type them in here.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
- If you have any concerns or issues, please let us know in the "Additional Comments" section.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact us.
