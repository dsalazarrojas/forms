# Civic Participation Readiness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Civic Participation Readiness Survey aims to assess your preparedness for active community participation, understanding your comfort level with civic activities, and identifying areas where you might need support or resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Select the correct options for each question.
* For questions with options, choose one or more that apply to you.
* If you have trouble with any question, feel free to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Civic Readiness Assessment --** (Readiness Intro, Note, required: false)
This introduction serves as a reminder of the purpose of the survey.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required: true)
This question helps us understand your age group, which is important for assessing your readiness for civic participation.
* **How often do you vote in local (municipal) elections?** (`voting_frequency_local`, select_one, required: true)
Please select the frequency with which you participate in local elections.
* **Rate your knowledge of how your local government functions** (`local_govt_knowledge`, number, required: true)
This question helps us gauge your understanding of local governance, from none to expert knowledge.
* **How comfortable are you speaking at a public meeting?** (`advocacy_comfort_level`, number, required: true)
This question measures your comfort level when discussing public issues.
* **-- Skills and Barriers --** (civic_skills_header, Note, required: false)
This section is a reminder that this is where we will discuss your civic skills.
* **Which of these civic skills do you currently possess?** (`current_civic_strengths`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose all skills you have experience with.
* **Which skills would you like to develop?** (`desired_skills_learning`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select skills you'd like to acquire or improve.
* **What are your biggest barriers to active participation?** (`participation_barriers`, select_multiple, required: true)
Identify the challenges that hinder your civic participation.
* **I believe I can influence decisions in my local community** (`community_influence_belief`, select_one, required: true)
Select your confidence level in influencing community decisions.
* **In the past 12 months, have you contacted a local official?** (`previous_year_participation`, select_one, required: true)
This question checks if you have engaged in civic activities recently.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how "ready" do you feel to take a leadership role?** (`readiness_score_self`, number, required: true)
Assess your confidence in leading community efforts.
* **Would you like a toolkit on how to get started in civic leadership?** (`further_resources_interest`, select_one, required: false)
Let us know if you need resources for further assistance.
* **Your Email (for toolkit delivery)** (`email_for_toolkit`, email, required: false)
If you selected "Yes" for the previous question, provide your email address to receive the toolkit.
* **Date of Assessment** (`assessment_date`, date, required: true)
Provide the date for this assessment, which will help us monitor your progress.
