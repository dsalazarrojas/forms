# Energy Drink Consumption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Drink Consumption Survey form is designed to gather data on energy drink consumption habits from individuals to better understand their behavior and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of energy drink you consume from the options provided.
2. Choose how often you consume energy drinks from the provided options.
3. If you experience any side effects, select them from the options provided.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, you can provide them in the text fields.
5. Finally, submit the form to share your energy drink consumption habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Demographics**: This is the first section of the form. You can provide your age, gender, or any other demographic information you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Energy Drink Type**: Please select the type of energy drink you consume from the dropdown options. This will help us understand your preferences.
* **Frequency of Consumption**: Choose how often you consume energy drinks from the options provided.
* **Side Effects**: If you experience any side effects after consuming energy drinks, select them from the options provided.
* **Perceptions**: Please indicate whether you agree or disagree with any statements about energy drinks.
* **Survey Completion**: Provide any additional comments or concerns you have about the survey.
* **Additional Information**: If you have any additional information you would like to share about yourself, feel free to type it here.
* **Additional Side Effects**: If you experience any additional side effects not listed in the previous question, feel free to type them here.
* **Energy Drink Consumption Frequency**: If you consume energy drinks at any frequency other than the options provided, select "Other" and type in your answer.
* **Energy Drink Amount**: If you consume a different amount of energy drinks than the provided options, type it here.
* **Thank You**: This is the last section of the form. Please take a moment to review your answers and submit the form.
