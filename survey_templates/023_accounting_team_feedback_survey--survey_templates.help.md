# Accounting Team Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Team Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from accounting team members to assess their experience, satisfaction, and challenges. The goal is to identify areas of improvement and provide insights for the management to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form honestly and thoroughly, taking about 10-15 minutes to complete.
2. Select the options that best describe your current state of mind.
3. Provide specific examples or details when asked.
4. Submit the form to ensure your feedback is recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role Title**: Please enter your job title or position within the accounting team.
* **Job Satisfaction**: This question measures your overall satisfaction with your current role. Select the option that best describes your current feelings.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You are extremely unhappy with your current role.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are somewhat unhappy with your current role.
	+ Neutral: You are neither happy nor unhappy with your current role.
	+ Satisfied: You are somewhat happy with your current role.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are extremely happy with your current role.
* **Work-Life Balance**: This question measures how well you feel your work and personal life are balanced. Select the option that best describes your current state.
	+ Poor: Your work and personal life are not balanced.
	+ Fair: Your work and personal life are somewhat balanced.
	+ Good: Your work and personal life are fairly balanced.
	+ Excellent: Your work and personal life are well balanced.
* **Biggest Challenges**: Select your top 2-3 hurdles that you face in your role. You can choose multiple options.
	+ Tight Deadlines: You have a lot of pressure to meet deadlines.
	+ Software Issues: You experience technical difficulties with software or tools.
	+ Communication Gaps: You have difficulty communicating with colleagues or management.
	+ High Workload: You have too much work to do.
	+ Lack of Training: You need more training or guidance to perform your job.
* **I feel Valued at Work**: This question measures how valued you feel in the organization. Select the option that best describes your current state.
	+ Strongly Disagree: You do not feel valued.
	+ Disagree: You somewhat do not feel valued.
	+ Neutral: You are not sure if you feel valued or not.
	+ Agree: You somewhat feel valued.
	+ Strongly Agree: You strongly feel valued.
* **Specific Achievements This Quarter**: Provide specific examples of what you are proud of accomplishing this quarter. This can include personal or team achievements.
* **Career Growth Opportunities**: This question measures how many opportunities you have for career growth and development. Select the option that best describes your current state.
	+ Not Available: You do not see any opportunities for growth.
	+ Limited: You see some opportunities for growth, but they are limited.
	+ Plentiful: You see a lot of opportunities for growth.
* **Tools Needed to Improve Performance**: Provide specific examples of tools, software, or equipment that you need to improve your performance.
* **General Feedback for Management**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for the management.
* **Survey Completion Date**: Please enter the date you completed this survey.

Note: The form will automatically capture the date and time you completed the survey.
