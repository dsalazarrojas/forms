# Travel Agent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on the challenges faced by travel agents during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. If you are unsure of any question, please choose "Not Applicable" or "Unknown".
3. If you have any additional information or comments, please use the space provided at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **travel_agent_survey (Text)**: This is the main field where you can type your answer to the question about challenges you faced during the pandemic.
* **travel_agent_survey_challenges (Text)**: Describe the specific challenges you faced during the pandemic. This can include anything from operational difficulties to financial losses.
* **travel_agent_survey_affected_areas (Select Multiple)**: Choose all the areas that were affected by the pandemic, such as Travel Agents, Tour operators, or Travel Agencies.
* **travel_agent_survey_travel_agency (Text)**: If you are affiliated with a travel agency, please provide the name of the agency.
* **travel_agent_survey_revenue_lost (Number)**: Estimate the revenue lost due to the pandemic. You can type a number, such as "10,000" or "50,000".
* **travel_agent_survey_support_measures (Select One)**: Choose how you obtained support during the pandemic, such as from Travel Agents, Tour operators, Government support, or Other.
* **travel_agent_survey_affected_people (Select Multiple)**: Choose all the types of people affected by the pandemic, such as CEO, Managers, Employees, or Customers.
* **travel_agent_survey_financial_loss (Number)**: Estimate the financial loss incurred due to the pandemic.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering each question.
* If you are unsure of any question, it's okay to say "Not Applicable" or "Unknown".
* If you have any additional information or comments, please use the space provided at the end of the form.
