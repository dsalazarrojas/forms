# Child Screen Time Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Screen Time Survey aims to gather information about your child's screen time habits, including their age, screen usage patterns, types of screens used, and how screen time affects their behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form for each child who needs a survey.
- Enter the child's name in the `Child Name` field.
- Select the child's age group from the options in the `Age of Child` field.
- Enter the average hours of screen time per day in the `Average hours of screen time per day` field.
- Select all the types of screens the child uses in the `Types of screens used` field.
- Choose the primary activities the child engages in on screens in the `Primary Screen Activities` field.
- Select how you use parental controls in the `Do you use parental controls?` field.
- Choose whether you set a daily time limit for the child's screen time in the `Do you set a specific daily time limit?` field.
- Select how screen time affects the child's behavior in the `How does screen time affect child behavior?` field.
- Provide any additional comments or observations about the child's screen habits in the `Additional comments on screen habits` field.
- Enter the survey completion date in the `Today Date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name**: Enter the name of the child for whom you are completing this survey.
* **Age of Child**: Select the age group of the child from the available options.
* **Average hours of screen time per day**: Enter the average number of hours the child spends on screens per day. This field requires a whole number.
* **Types of screens used**: Select all the types of screens the child uses, such as smartphones, tablets, televisions, computers, or gaming consoles.
* **Primary Screen Activities**: Choose the activities the child engages in most on screens, such as educational games or videos.
* **Do you use parental controls?**: Select whether you use parental software or apps to monitor or limit the child's screen time.
* **Do you set a specific daily time limit?**: Choose whether you set a daily time limit for the child's screen time.
* **How does screen time affect child behavior?**: Select how screen time affects the child's behavior, such as positively or negatively.
* **Additional comments on screen habits**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the child's screen habits.
* **Today Date**: Enter the date on which you are completing this survey.
