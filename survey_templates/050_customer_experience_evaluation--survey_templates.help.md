# Customer Experience Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from customers on their experience with your company, helping to identify areas of improvement and areas of success in customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely, answering all questions and providing any additional comments or information.
2. Select the correct options for each question, as prompted.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, as this will be used to contact you for any follow-up questions.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, as this will be used to contact you for any follow-up questions.
5. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Feedback** (<form_1_name>, note, required/optional): This is a text field where you can provide any general feedback or comments about your experience with the company.
* **Customer Expectations** (<form_2_title>, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all the options that apply to the experience you had with the company.
* **Rating 1 - 5** (<form_3_name>, select_one, required/optional): Please rate your experience with the company, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Email** (<form_4_email, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address so that we can contact you for any follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (<form_5_phone, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you for any follow-up questions.
* **Additional Comments** (<form_6_name, note, required/optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your experience with the company.
