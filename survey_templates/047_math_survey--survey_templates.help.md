# math_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The math_survey form is a collection of questions designed to gather information from students about their math abilities and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the math_survey form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form as instructed by your teacher or administrator.
2. Ensure that you are using the correct format for each field.
3. Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Math Survey Form**: This is the main section of the form where you will answer the questions about your math abilities and interests.
* **student_name**: This field is where you will enter your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your records.
* **math_abilities**: In this section, you will select all the math abilities you possess. Please choose all the options that apply to you.
* **math_interests**: Here, you will select one interest that best describes you. Choose the option that aligns with your preferences.
* **teacher_comments**: If you have any comments or feedback for your teacher, you can write them here. This is a text area where you can type your thoughts.
* **teacher_email**: If you want to provide your teacher's email address for follow-up, you can enter it here.
* **student_email**: Please enter your own email address for contact purposes.
* **phone_number**: This field is for your phone number. Please enter your correct phone number if you want to be contacted.
* **time_of_day**: In this field, please enter the time of day you are most likely to do math-related activities.
* **date**: This is a field where you can enter your date of birth or the date you last did something related to math.

Note: Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields (if any), before submitting the form.
