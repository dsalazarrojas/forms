# Celebrity Endorsement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Celebrity Endorsement Survey is designed to help brands and marketing teams evaluate potential celebrity endorsements. It collects information about a proposed celebrity or influencer, their relevance to the brand, their fit with the brand's values, and potential risks and benefits associated with the endorsement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the proposed celebrity or influencer, including their name, email address, organization or company, role, and any other relevant details.
2. Select the brand or product category that best fits the celebrity or influencer's expertise or field of work.
3. Choose the target audience for the celebrity or influencer's appeal, such as their age range, demographics, or interests.
4. Evaluate the celebrity or influencer's relevance to the brand, including their values alignment, appeal to the target audience, and credibility rating.
5. Discuss potential risks and benefits associated with the endorsement, including perceived benefits and potential risks or concerns.
6. Provide an estimated compensation range for the proposed endorsement campaign.
7. Offer an overall recommendation for whether or not to proceed with the endorsement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Organization or Company** (`respondent_organization`, `text`, optional): If you're representing a company or organization, enter it here.
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, `text`, required): Select your role in the marketing team or organization.
* **Brand or Product Name** (`brand_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the brand or product.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category that best fits the brand or product, such as Consumer Goods, Technology, Fashion, etc.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, required): Describe the target audience for the celebrity or influencer's appeal, including age range, demographics, or interests.
* **Proposed Celebrity or Influencer** (`proposed_celebrity`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the proposed celebrity or influencer.
* **Celebrity Field or Industry** (`celebrity_field`, `select_one`, required): Select the celebrity's field or industry, such as Sports, Entertainment, Music, etc.
* **Relevance to Brand** (`celebrity_relevance`, `text`, required): Explain why this celebrity or influencer is relevant to the brand.
* **Brand Values Alignment** (`brand_alignment`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate how well the celebrity or influencer's values align with the brand, on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Appeal to Target Audience** (`audience_appeal`, `select_one`, required): Assess how well the celebrity or influencer appeals to the target audience, on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Celebrity Credibility** (`credibility_rating`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the celebrity or influencer's credibility, on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Proposed Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`, `text`, required): Enter the proposed duration of the endorsement campaign, in months or years.
* **Estimated Compensation Range** (`compensation_range`, `select_one`, required): Select the estimated compensation range for the proposed endorsement campaign.
* **Perceived Benefits of Endorsement** (`perceived_benefits`, `text`, required): Discuss the perceived benefits of the endorsement, such as brand awareness or sales impact.
* **Potential Risks or Concerns** (`potential_risks`, `text`, optional): Identify potential risks or concerns associated with the endorsement, such as controversies or fit issues.
* **Alternative Celebrity Options** (`alternative_celebrities`, `text`, optional): Provide backup choices for alternative celebrities or influencers.
* **Overall Recommendation** (`recommendation`, `select_one`, required): Offer an overall recommendation for whether or not to proceed with the endorsement, on a scale from Highly Recommended to Strongly Not Recommended.

Note: This form is a tool to help you evaluate potential celebrity endorsements. The information provided will be used to inform your decision-making process.
