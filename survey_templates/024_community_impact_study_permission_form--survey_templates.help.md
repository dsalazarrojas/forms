# Community Impact Study Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is required to be completed by participants before starting a community impact study. It ensures that you understand the study's purpose, agree to participate, and provide consent for data collection and use. Please take a few minutes to review the form carefully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Participant Full Name
2. Provide the Title of the Study
3. Enter your Name as the Principal Researcher
4. Confirm you understand the study's purpose
5. Agree to participate in the study
6. Choose how you want your data to be stored and protected
7. Confirm your understanding of your rights to withdraw from the study
8. Sign your name electronically
9. Date your signature

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Title of the Study**: Type the name of the research project you are participating in.
* **Name of Principal Researcher**: Enter your name as the person conducting or leading the study.
* **I confirm I have been informed of the study purpose**: Choose "Yes, I understand" to confirm you've been informed of the study's purpose.
* **Do you consent to participate in this study?**: Choose "Yes, I consent" to agree to participate.
* **Permission to Record Interviews/Sessions**: Choose how you want your data to be handled, either "Yes, record session" or "No, notes only".
* **Permission to Use Direct Quotes**: Choose how you want to be credited in the study, either "Yes, use my real name", "Yes, use a pseudonym (fake name)", "Yes, use anonymously", or "No, do not use quotes".
* **I understand how my data will be stored and protected**: Choose "I Understand" to confirm you're aware of the data storage and protection.
* **I understand I can withdraw from the study at any time**: Choose "I Understand my rights" to confirm you know you can withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
* **Electronic Signature**: Type your name to sign the form electronically.
* **Date of Signature**: Date and time of your signature, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
