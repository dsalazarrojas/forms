# Open Evening Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about an event, helping us understand their thoughts and feelings about the evening's activities, food, and overall experience. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey to provide us with valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select a page to view the available fields.
2. Fill out each field with the required information. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Make sure to review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title (\*)**
	+ This field is for you to enter the title of the event you attended.
* **Name (\*)**
	+ Please provide your name for us to associate your feedback with you.
* **Email (Optional)**
	+ If you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive a confirmation email, provide your email address here. If you don't want to be contacted, you can leave this field blank.
* **Comments (**)**
	+ Share your thoughts, opinions, or suggestions about the event. This is your chance to tell us what we did well and what we could improve on.
* **Rating**
	+ Rate your overall experience at the event, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
