# ginger_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Ginger Knowledge Quiz** is designed to collect demographic information from users to gather insights for research purposes. This form is not intended for real-time feedback or direct communication. The collected data will be used to improve our services and provide better experiences for our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the **Introduction** page with your brief background or introduction.
2. Select your answers to the **Questions** page. You can choose multiple answers from the provided options.
3. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** on the **Email** and **Phone** page.
4. Confirm your **Email** and **Phone** on the **Confirm Email** and **Confirm Phone** page.
5. Enter your **Name**, **Date**, and **Age** on the **Name**, **Date**, and **Age** page.
6. Select your **Gender**, **Occupation**, and **Education Level** on the **Gender**, **Occupation**, and **Education Level** page.
7. Enter any additional **Comments** on the **Comments** page.
8. Confirm your **Comments** on the **Confirm Comments** page.
9. Select your **Age Group** and **Gender Group** on the **Age Group** and **Gender Group** page.
10. Select your **Education Level Group** and **Occupation Group** on the **Education Level Group** and **Occupation Group** page.
11. Enter the **Submit Date** and **Submit Time** on the **Submit Date** and **Submit Time** page.
12. Confirm your submission on the final **Confirm** page.
