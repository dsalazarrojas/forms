# Product Packaging Design Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and assessment on the design of product packaging. It helps in evaluating the overall appearance, design elements, and other key aspects of the packaging.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form's title.
2. Answer the questions in the following sections:
   - **Overall Appearance**
   - **Design Elements**
   - **Packaging Components**
   - **Color Palette**
   - **Material Use**
   - **Packaging Feel**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Packaging Design Assessment Form** (`title`, `text`, **Required**: No)
  - This is the title of the form. It's the heading that appears at the top of the form.
* **Overall Appearance** (`overall_appearance`, `text`, **Required**: No)
  - Provide a brief description of the overall appearance of the packaging.
* **Design Elements** (`design_elements`, `select_multiple`, **Required**: No)
  - Select all the design elements that you think are relevant to the product packaging. Please choose one or more options.
  - Options: option 1, option 2, option 3
* **Product Identity** (`product_identity`, `text`, **Required**: No)
  - Describe the unique features or aspects that make the product identifiable.
* **Packaging Components** (`packaging_components`, `select_one`, **Required**: No)
  - Select one of the following options that best fits the product's packaging:
    - option 1
    - option 2
    - option 3
* **Color Palette** (`color_palette`, `text`, **Required**: No)
  - Enter the primary and secondary colors used in the packaging design.
* **Material Use** (`material_use`, `text`, **Required**: No)
  - Describe the materials used in the packaging, e.g., glass, plastic, paper, etc.
* **Packaging Feel** (`packaging_feel`, `text`, **Required**: No)
  - Enter a brief description of the texture and finish of the packaging.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when providing answers to each section.
- Use the provided options for select fields when applicable.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
