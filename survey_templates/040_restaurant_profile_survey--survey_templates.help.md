# Restaurant Profile Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Profile Survey form collects information about restaurants to provide a comprehensive overview of each location.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start Survey" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field with the relevant information about the restaurant.
3. Ensure that all required fields (marked as "required") are filled out.
4. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the restaurant as it appears on its sign or on official documents.
- **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the street address or city where the restaurant is located.
- **Restaurant Type** (`restaurant_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of restaurant, such as American, Chinese, etc.
- **Cuisines** (`cuisine`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of cuisine the restaurant serves, such as Sushi, Seafood, etc.
- **Price Range** (`price_range`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value representing the restaurant's price range (1-10).
- **Hours** (`hours`, time, optional): Enter the hours of operation for the restaurant.
- **Staff Rating** (`staff_rating`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value representing the quality of the restaurant's staff.
- **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness_rating`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value representing the cleanliness of the restaurant.
- **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value representing the overall experience of the restaurant.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or remarks about the restaurant.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the restaurant's email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the restaurant's phone number.
- **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the restaurant's website URL.
- **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple, optional): Select the social media platforms where the restaurant is present, such as Facebook, Twitter, etc.
- **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, required): Select a numerical value representing the overall rating of the restaurant from 1-10.
