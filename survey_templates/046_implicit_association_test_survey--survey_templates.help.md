# Implicit Association Test Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Implicit Association Test Survey is a tool used to assess implicit attitudes and associations. It is designed to measure the unconscious associations between concepts, potentially helping researchers and testers understand underlying psychological processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the welcome message on page 1.
2.  Enter your participant information on page 2.
3.  On page 3, select your answer for each question in the Implicit Association Test.
4.  On page 4, select your answer to confirm your participation.
5.  Click submit to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to the Implicit Association Test** (`intro`, `note`, required: `false`): This is a brief introduction to the test, providing context and instructions.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, required: `true`): This is a required field where you will need to enter your participant information. Please ensure the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Implicit Association Test** (`iat_survey`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the answers that best reflect your thoughts and feelings about the given pairs of concepts.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Confirm your participation and submission of the survey.

## Tips
Please be honest and accurate in your responses. The success of this test depends on your honest answers. If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification.
