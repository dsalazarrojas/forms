# pcb_design_knowledge_quiz--survey_templates - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and general information from users about the PCB design knowledge quiz. This will help us improve the quiz and make it more effective for users.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully before starting to complete it.
- Enter your response in the space provided for each field.
- Make sure to complete all required fields (if any) to ensure your response is recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (text, text, optional): This field is for providing any general comments or observations about the quiz.
* **Design Knowledge** (design_knowledge, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of knowledge about PCB design. Select 'Yes' if you feel confident in your knowledge, 'No' if you don't feel confident.
* **Design Process** (design_process, select_multiple, optional): Check all the design process areas that you've gone through or have experience with. For example, if you've designed a PCB, check 'Yes' for 'Yes, I have experience designing PCBs.'
* **Review** (review, note, optional): Share your thoughts about the quiz and any areas for improvement.
* **Feedback** (feedback, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the quiz.
* **Date and Time** (date_and_time, date, optional): Select the date and time you completed the quiz.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Select the time you completed the quiz.

Note: Please be as thorough and specific as possible when providing your responses, as this will help us improve the quiz.
