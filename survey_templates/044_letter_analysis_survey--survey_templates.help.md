# Letter Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Letter Analysis Survey is designed to gather feedback on the clarity, tone, and overall effectiveness of a letter. It helps evaluate the communication skills of writers or the quality of a letter sent to stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the letter and gather your thoughts on its clarity, tone, and overall effectiveness.
2. Select the relevant options for each field to provide feedback on the letter.
3. Provide detailed comments in the "overall impression" and "team feedback" fields if you have specific thoughts or suggestions.
