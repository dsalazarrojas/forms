# ABC Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ABC Analysis Form is designed to gather information about customer orders, including personal details, order specifics, delivery preferences, and payment information. This form is intended for customers to provide all necessary details for their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details: Enter your full name and email address.
2. Provide order details: Enter the date of the order, item or product name, quantity, unit price, and total value (which will be auto-calculated).
3. Choose your delivery preferences: Select your preferred delivery method and date.
4. Add any special instructions or notes: Enter any additional comments or instructions you have.
5. Provide payment information: Select your preferred payment method and supplier details (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your full legal name.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone number**: Optional contact number.
* **Order date**: Date when the order is placed.
* **Item or product name**: Full product name.
* **Quantity**: Enter number of items.
* **Unit price**: Price per item in local currency.
* **Total value**: Auto-calculated as quantity × unit price.
* **ABC Category**: Classification based on inventory importance.
* **Current stock status**: Current availability level.
* **Reorder point**: Minimum quantity before reordering (optional).
* **Preferred delivery method**: Choose the most suitable option (Standard, Express, Pickup, or Local).
* **Delivery address**: Street, city, postal code.
* **Requested delivery date**: Preferred date for delivery.
* **Special instructions or notes**: Any special requests.
* **Payment method**: Select how you will pay (Credit card, Cash, Bank transfer, PayPal, or Invoice).
* **Supplier name**: Name of the supplier (optional).
* **Supplier contact**: Supplier contact number or email (optional).
* **Additional comments or notes**: Any other information.
