# ABC Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ABC Analysis Form is designed to gather information about customer orders, including personal details, order specifics, delivery preferences, and payment information. This form is intended for customers to provide all necessary details for their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details: Enter your full name and email address.
2. Provide order details: Enter the date of the order, item or product name, quantity, unit price, and total value (which will be auto-calculated).
3. Choose your delivery preferences: Select your preferred delivery method and date.
4. Add any special instructions or notes: Enter any additional comments or instructions you have.
5. Provide payment information: Select your preferred payment method and supplier details (if necessary).

* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone number**: Optional contact number.
* **Order date**: Date when the order is placed.
