# Reminder Setup Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences for reminders, including your preferred time of day, notification method, reminder day, reminder frequency, time of day, reminder message, email, and phone number. The goal is to help us set up your reminders according to your specifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred time of day from the options provided.
2. Choose your notification method (Email, SMS, Phone, or Push).
3. Select which days of the week you would like to receive reminders (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.).
4. Choose how frequently you would like to receive reminders (e.g., every day, every two days, etc.).
5. Specify the time of day you would like to receive reminders (in 24-hour format, e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
6. Enter a friendly reminder message to be sent to you (optional).
7. Provide your email address (if you want to receive reminders via email).
8. Enter your phone number (if you want to receive reminders via phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reminder Preferences** (`reminder_preferences`, text, **optional**): This is a free-form text area where you can provide any additional information about your reminders preferences.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select your preferred time of day from the provided options (e.g., 08:00-11:00, 11:00-13:00, etc.).
* **Notification Method** (`notification_method`, select_one, **optional**): Choose how you want to be notified (Email, SMS, Phone, or Push).
* **Reminder Day** (`reminder_day`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select which days of the week you want to receive reminders (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.).
* **Reminder Frequency** (`reminder_frequency`, select_multiple, **optional**): Choose how frequently you want to receive reminders (e.g., every day, every two days, etc.).
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, **optional**): Specify the time of day you want to receive reminders in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **Reminder Message** (`reminder_message`, text, **optional**): Enter a friendly reminder message to be sent to you (e.g., "Hey, it's time to check in with your team!").
* **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address if you want to receive reminders via email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your phone number if you want to receive reminders via phone.
