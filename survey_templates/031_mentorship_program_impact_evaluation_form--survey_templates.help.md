# Mentorship Program Impact Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentorship Program Impact Evaluation Form is designed to assess the effectiveness of a mentorship program. It is meant to be completed by program participants, mentors, or program administrators to provide an honest evaluation of the program's goals, outcomes, and areas for improvement. This form will help us identify the strengths and weaknesses of the program, ultimately allowing us to refine and enhance the mentorship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information for each field, which is marked as "required" in the form.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the mentorship program being evaluated.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the program started.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date the program ended.
* **Program Length** (`program_length`, number, required): Enter the length of the program in months or weeks.
* **Mentorship Goals** (`mentorship_goals`, select_one, required): Choose one of the three goals listed:
	+ Goal 1: Provide support and guidance to mentees
	+ Goal 2: Foster a culture of continuous learning and development
	+ Goal 3: Develop a sense of community and belonging among participants
* **Mentorship Outcomes** (`mentorship_outcomes`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the outcomes that apply:
	+ Outcome 1: Improved self-confidence
	+ Outcome 2: Enhanced professional skills
	+ Outcome 3: Better communication skills
* **Section 1 - Introduction** (`section_1_1`, text, optional): Provide a brief introduction to the program.
* **Section 1 - Mentor** (`section_1_2`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the mentor(s).
* **Section 2 - Feedback** (`section_2_1`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the program's strengths and weaknesses.
* **Section 2 - Evaluation** (`section_2_2`, text, optional): Evaluate the program's overall effectiveness.
* **Section 2 - Improvement** (`section_2_3`, text, optional): Suggest areas for improvement for the program.
* **Section 2 - Recommendations** (`section_2_4`, text, optional): Offer recommendations for future program development.
* **Section 3 - Impact** (`section_3_1`, text, optional): Describe the program's overall impact on you or the organization.
* **Section 3 - Evaluation** (`section_3_2`, text, optional): Evaluate the program's overall impact.
* **Section 3 - Improvement** (`section_3_3`, text, optional): Suggest areas for improvement for the program.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Use specific examples when possible to support your answers.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
* This form will help us improve the mentorship program for future participants.
