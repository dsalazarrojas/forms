# digital_care_communication_experience_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather demographic and experiential information from users about their digital care communication experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please answer all required fields (Gender, Age, Occupation, and Experience).
2. If you have any additional comments, feel free to provide them in the "Comments" field.
3. Once you have completed all fields, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Please select your gender from the options provided.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age to help us better understand the demographics of our users.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required): Please enter your occupation or job title.
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply regarding your digital care experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional feedback or comments, please feel free to provide them here.
