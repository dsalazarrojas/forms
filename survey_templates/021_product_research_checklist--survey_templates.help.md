# Product Research Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for collecting and comparing product data. It helps to gather essential information about products, such as name, description, pricing, ratings, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the product's basic information, including the product name and description.
2. Enter the product's price and rating.
3. Select the team members involved in the product development process.
4. Decide on the buying decision, merchandising decision, and marketing decision related to the product.
5. Add any additional product notes, if necessary.
6. Enter contact information, including email and phone numbers.
7. Provide a URL for the product and upload an image (if available).
8. Enter the product's URL and image upload date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required false): Enter the name of the product you are researching.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required false): Describe the product in detail, including its features and benefits.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required false): Enter the product's price, including any applicable currency.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, select_one, required false): Select the product's rating from the given options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required false): Choose the team members involved in the product development process (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Buying Decision** (`buying`, select_one, required false): Decide on the buying decision for the product (True or False).
* **Merchandising Decision** (`merchandising_decision`, select_one, required false): Decide on the merchandising decision for the product (True or False).
* **Marketing Decision** (`marketing_decision`, select_one, required false): Decide on the marketing decision for the product (True or False).
* **Product Notes** (`product_notes`, note, required false): Add any additional product-specific notes.
* **Email Contact** (`email_contact`, email, required false): Enter the email address of the product contact person.
* **Phone Contact** (`phone_contact`, text, required false): Enter the phone number of the product contact person.
* **Product URL** (`product_url`, text, required false): Enter the product's URL.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, date, required false): Upload the product image (if available).
* **Product Time** (`product_time`, time, required false): Enter the date and time of product development.
