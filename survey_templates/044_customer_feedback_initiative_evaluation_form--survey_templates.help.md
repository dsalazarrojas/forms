# Customer Feedback Initiative Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers on our services to help us improve and better serve them in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill out all the required fields (marked as "required").
3. Provide truthful and honest feedback.
4. If you have any complaints or concerns, use the "Do You Have a Complaint?" field.
5. If you would recommend our services, select "True" in the "Will You Recommend" field.
6. Provide additional comments or explanations in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. Fill out your contact information (name, email, and phone number) if you wish to be contacted for further follow-up.
