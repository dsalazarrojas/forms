# Self Help Group Impact Interview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants in a self-help group, evaluating their overall experience and satisfaction with the group. The purpose of this form is to measure the effectiveness of the self-help group, identify areas of improvement, and provide valuable insights for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand the questions and fields it asks.
2. Answer each question accurately and truthfully, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, which are marked as 'required'.
4. If you are unsure about a particular question or field, ask your group facilitator or leader for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief description of the self-help group, including its purpose and goals. This field is not required.
* **Group Information**: Enter a brief description of the self-help group, including its name, purpose, and any other relevant details. This field is not required.
* **Group Outcomes**: Select all the outcomes that you experienced as a result of being part of the self-help group. These outcomes are:
	+ **Positive**: I experienced positive emotions or thoughts.
	+ **Negative**: I experienced negative emotions or thoughts.
	+ **Mixed**: I experienced a mix of both positive and negative emotions or thoughts.
* **Participant Information**: Enter any other comments or concerns you have about your participation in the self-help group. This field is not required.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, such as your email address, so that we can follow up with you. This field is not required.
* **Participant Notes**: Any additional comments or thoughts about your experience in the self-help group. This field is not required.
* **Group Notes**: Any additional comments or thoughts about your experience in the self-help group. This field is not required.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Select how often you would like to be followed up with:
	+ 1: Once
	+ 2: Twice
	+ 3: Three times
	+ 4: Four times
	+ 5: Five times
* **Follow-up Duration**: Select the length of time you would like each follow-up to be:
	+ 1: 1 week
	+ 2: 2 weeks
	+ 3: 3 weeks
	+ 4: 4 weeks
	+ 5: 5 weeks
	+ 6: 6 weeks
* **Participant Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with the self-help group:
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
* **Group Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with the group as a whole:
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
