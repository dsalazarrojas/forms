# Tournament Safety Measures Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the safety measures in place during a tournament. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure that future events are safe and enjoyable for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the options that best match your experience and opinion.
3. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
4. Provide your contact information (name, email, phone number, and address) so that we can follow up on your feedback.
