# Corporate Team Building Activity Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants and evaluate the effectiveness of the team building activity. Your responses will help the organizers improve future team building events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to answer the questions based on your personal experiences and perceptions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and any other identifying information you'd like to share with the organizers.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required: false): Please indicate the number of people in your team that participated in the activity.
* **Team Diversity** (`team_diversity`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply from the options 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your team had a diverse mix of:
	+ 'Yes' - Your team had a diverse mix of people with different backgrounds, skills, or perspectives.
	+ 'No' - Your team did not have a diverse mix.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): Please rate the activity on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Write any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the activity.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for follow-up contact or to receive updates about future team building activities.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact in case we need to follow up with you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date you participated in the activity.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask the organizers for clarification.
* If you have any further feedback or suggestions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
