# Film Festival Screening Audience Feedback Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from audience members after attending a screening of a film. It helps the film festival organizers to understand what they do well and where they need to improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the required fields, marked as "Required" in the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and honestly, based on your experience at the screening.
3. Please only fill out the form once per screening to avoid duplication of effort and ensure accurate data collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Screening**: This field is not currently used by the form, but is likely intended to be an ID for the screening being evaluated. Please enter the screening ID if you are able.
* **Overall Rating**: This field allows you to rate your overall experience at the screening, on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **feedback**: This field is for providing more detailed feedback about your experience at the screening. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions about your experience, please enter them here.
* **Name**: Please enter your name so that the organizers can contact you if they need to follow up with you.
* **Email**: If you would like the festival organizers to contact you with any follow-up questions, please enter your email address.
* **Phone**: If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Comments**: This field is not used in the form, but is likely intended for additional comments or suggestions. Please use the field above for your comments about the screening.

Note: The fields with "Name" and "Phone" are redundant, as only one "Comments" field is used, likely intended for both additional comments and suggestions. For clarity, I have included a note in the **Comments** field to use the field above for comments, and this field for suggestions. 

**Tips**: 
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback and comments.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the festival organizers.
