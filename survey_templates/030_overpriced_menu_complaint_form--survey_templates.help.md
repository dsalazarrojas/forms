# overpriced_menu_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about overpriced menu items from customers. It helps to identify menu items that are too expensive and gather customer feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact number to let us know who you are and how to reach you.
2. Describe the menu item that you think is overpriced.
3. Provide a brief description of why you think it's overpriced.
4. List the items that you think are overpriced by selecting the relevant options from the list.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
6. Select the person to whom you want this issue to be assigned to (chatjimmy or jenny).
7. Fill in your timestamp so we can keep track of when you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name so we can get in touch with you.
* **Menu Item Description** (`menu_item_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the menu item that you think is overpriced.
* **Price Description** (`price_description`, text, required/optional): Explain why you think it's overpriced (e.g. too expensive, too small portion).
* **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the restaurant where you visited.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number so we can follow up with you.
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`, date, required/optional): If you can remember, please enter the date you visited the restaurant.
* **Time Visited** (`time_visited`, time, required/optional): If you can remember, please enter the time you visited the restaurant.
* **Overpriced Menu Items** (`overpriced_menu_items`, select multiple, required/optional): Select the overpriced menu items that you experienced.
* **Additional Comments** (`submit_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select one, required): Select the person to whom you want this issue to be assigned to.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required/optional): Not used in this form.
* **Timestamp** (`timestamp`, date, required/optional): Not used in this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all the overpriced menu items that you experienced.
* Be specific when describing the menu item and why it's overpriced.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please add it in the "Additional Comments" field.
