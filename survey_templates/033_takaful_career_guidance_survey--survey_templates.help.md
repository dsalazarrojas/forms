# Takaful Career Guidance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Takaful Career Guidance Survey is a form designed to collect information about your career aspirations, interests, and background. The information collected will help career advisors provide personalized guidance to help you make informed decisions about your career.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your preferred format: Job Interests, Career Readiness, or other options. 
2. Choose your preferred options for each category based on your current situation.
3. Fill out the remaining fields with the necessary information.
4. Review and submit your completed survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Interests**: Please select all the jobs that interest you. This will help us understand your career aspirations.
* **Career Readiness**: Please select your level of career readiness. This will help us understand your current level of preparation for your career.
* **Support Needs**: Please select one option that best describes your support needs. This will help us understand your needs for career guidance.
* **Job Ready**: This field is not applicable as it seems to be a time field without any options.
* **Education Level**: Please select your current education level. This will help us understand your educational background.
* **Job Status**: Please select one option that best describes your job status. This will help us understand your employment situation.
* **Job Type**: Please enter a brief description of the type of job you are looking for.
* **Job Sector**: Please select one option that best describes the job sector you are interested in. This will help us narrow down job opportunities.
* **Job Level**: Please select one option that best describes the job level you are looking for. This will help us understand the level of responsibility.
* **Job Position**: Please enter a brief description of the job position you are looking for.
* **Education Field**: Please select one option that best describes your education field. This will help us understand your academic background.
* **Job Location**: Please enter a brief description of the location you are looking for a job in.
* **Job Duration**: This field seems to be a select one field without any options, please select one that best describes the job duration you are looking for.
* **Job Salary**: Please enter your expected salary range for the job.
* **Job Employment**: Please select one option that best describes your employment status. This will help us understand your work situation.
* **Job Responsibilities**: Please enter a brief description of the job responsibilities you are looking for.
* **Job Description**: Please enter a brief description of the job you are looking for.
* **Job Skills**: Please enter a list of skills you possess that are relevant to the job.
* **Job Requirements**: Please enter a list of requirements for the job.
* **Job References**: Please enter a list of people who can vouch for your skills and experience.
* **Job References Employed**: Please enter a list of people who can vouch for your skills and experience.
* **Education Institution**: Please enter the name of the institution where you received your education.
* **Program Coordinator**: Please enter a brief description of your role or responsibilities as a program coordinator.
* **Educator Name**: Please enter the name of your educator or instructor.

Note: This help guide assumes that some fields are missing options or have an unclear label. Please provide the correct information or update the help guide to reflect the actual fields and options.
