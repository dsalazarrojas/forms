# Remote Work Experience Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your experiences with remote work. Your input will help us understand the pros and cons of remote work and make informed decisions about future work arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question in the space provided. You can type as much or as little as you like.
2. If you're unsure about a question, try to do your best to answer it based on your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or insights that didn't fit into one of the specific questions, feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the form. Please take a moment to read it and understand the purpose of the form.
* **What was the reason**: Please explain in a few words why you chose to work remotely. For example, "I wanted more flexibility" or "I wanted to reduce my commute".
* **Job title**: Select the job title that best describes your job during your remote work experience. This will help us understand the types of roles that are well-suited for remote work.
* **How long did you work**: Enter the number of months or years you worked remotely. This will help us understand the duration of your remote work experience.
* **What was the company**: Please enter the name of the company you worked for while working remotely.
* **What was the work arrangement**: Select one or more of the work arrangements you had during your remote work experience. For example, "Full-time", "Part-time", "Freelance", "Remote", "On-site", or "Flexible".
* **Rate your experience**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very negative" and 5 is "Very positive".
* **Why did you choose**: Select one or more reasons why you chose to work remotely. For example, "Flexibility", "Better work-life balance", "Reduced commuting time", "Increased productivity", "Improved relationships", or "Other".
* **How did you feel**: Select one or more emotions that describe how you felt while working remotely. For example, "Focused", "Energized", "Motivated", "Calm", "Bored", "Stressed", or "Other".
* **Would you recommend**: Rate how likely you would recommend working remotely to others. For example, "Strongly agree", "Somewhat agree", "Neutral", "Somewhat disagree", or "Strongly disagree".
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or insights that didn't fit into one of the specific questions, feel free to add them here.
* **Job satisfaction**: Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very unsatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".
* **Would you like to continue**: Select whether you would like to continue working remotely in the future. For example, "Yes", "No", or "No opinion".
* **Why didn't you**: Select one or more reasons why you didn't continue working remotely. For example, "Lack of job security", "No opportunities for advancement", "Limited career growth", or "Other".
* **How did it affect**: Select how working remotely affected your work or personal life. For example, "Yes", "No".
* **How many hours**: If relevant, enter the number of hours you worked remotely. This will help us understand the impact of remote work on work hours.
* **How long did you commute**: If relevant, enter the number of hours you commuted while working remotely.
* **What didn't work**: If you experienced any issues or challenges while working remotely, please explain them here.
* **Would you**: Select whether you would like to continue working remotely in the future. For example, "True", "False", or "No opinion".
* **What was your experience**: Select whether you had a positive or negative experience with remote work. For example, "Yes", "No".
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or insights, feel free to add them here.
* **Job satisfaction**: Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very unsatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".
* **Would you like to continue**: Select whether you would like to continue working remotely in the future. For example, "Yes", "No", or "No opinion".
* **Additional Comments 2**: If you have any additional comments or insights, feel free to add them here.
