# Workshop Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from participants in a workshop or training session. It consists of 8 questions that assess their knowledge and satisfaction with the workshop, as well as their rating of the trainer's performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the participant comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before starting to fill out the form.
* **Quiz questions** (`quiz_questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply to answer the quiz questions. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Participant rating** (`participant_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop, using the scale provided.
* **Participant feedback** (`participant_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the workshop.
* **Participant satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the workshop, using the scale provided.
* **Participant comments** (`participant_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience in the workshop.
* **Trainer rating** (`trainer_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the trainer's performance, using the options provided.
* **Participant rating scale** (`participant_rating_scale`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop, using the scale provided.
* **Quiz score** (`quiz_score`, `number`, required: false): If applicable, provide the number of correct answers you obtained in the quiz.
