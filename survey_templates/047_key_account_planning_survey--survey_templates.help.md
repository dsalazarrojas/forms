# key_account_planning_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "key_account_planning_survey" form is designed to collect crucial information about a customer's account, enabling effective key account planning and management. This form aims to gather essential details about the account's objectives, stakeholders, challenges, growth opportunities, and other relevant information. By completing this form, you'll be able to assess the account's status, priority, and other vital characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure accurate and complete information, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. For optional fields, provide as much detail as possible to get a comprehensive view of the account.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for select_multiple, select_one, and number fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Objective (Text)** (`objective`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the objective of the account.
- **Stakeholders (Select Multiple)** (`stakeholders`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether stakeholders are involved with the account.
	+ 'Yes': Stakeholders are involved.
	+ 'No': No stakeholders are involved.
- **Challenges (Text)** (`challenges`, `text`, optional): Describe any challenges related to the account.
- **Growth Opportunities (Text)** (`growth_opportunities`, `text`, required): Identify potential growth opportunities for the account.
- **Key Performance Indicators (Text)** (`key_performance_indicators`, `text`, optional): List specific Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) relevant to the account.
- **Account Manager Info (Text)** (`account_manager_info`, `text`, optional): Provide information about the account manager.
- **Account Manager Email (Email)** (`account_manager_email`, `text`, required): Enter the email address of the account manager.
- **Account Manager Phone (Text)** (`account_manager_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the account manager.
- **Sales Team (Text)** (`sales_team`, `text`, optional): List the members of the sales team involved with the account.
- **Customer Contact Info (Text)** (`customer_contact_info`, `text`, optional): Provide contact information for the customer.
- **Customer Decision Maker (Select One)** (`customer_decision_maker`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether the customer has a decision maker.
	+ 'Yes': Customer has a decision maker.
	+ 'No': No decision maker is involved.
- **Account Status (Select One)** (`account_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current status of the account.
	+ 'Active': The account is active.
	+ 'Inactive': The account is inactive.
- **Key Account Priority (Number)** (`key_account_priority`, `number`, required): Specify the priority of the account, from 1 to 10.
- **Customer Account ID (Number)** (`customer_account_id`, `number`, optional): Enter the unique ID of the customer account.

- **Customer Account Country (Text)** (`customer_account_country`, `text`, optional): Enter the country of the customer account.
- **Sales Team Members (Text)** (`sales_team_members`, `text`, optional): List the members of the sales team involved with the account.
