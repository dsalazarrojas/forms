# Charity Fundraising Strategy Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions and preferences from various stakeholders, including board members, staff, volunteers, and community members, on potential fundraising strategies for a charity organization. The goal is to understand their voting preferences for different strategies and gather information on their available resources, risk tolerance, and community impact considerations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your name and email address (fields 1 and 2).
2. Select your role within the organization (field 3).
3. Enter the name of your organization (field 4).
4. Choose your preferred voting period (field 5).
5. Select your top three preferred fundraising strategies (fields 6, 7, and 8).
6. Choose your preferred budget range for each strategy (field 9).
7. Select your preferred timeline for implementation (field 10).
8. Identify your available resources for each strategy (field 11).
9. Indicate your risk tolerance level (field 12).
10. Assess the importance of community visibility for each strategy (field 13).
11. Provide reasoning for your strategy choices (field 14, optional).
12. Share any additional strategy suggestions (field 15, optional).
13. Mention any implementation concerns (field 16, optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Your Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **2. Email Address**: Enter your work or personal email address for confirmation purposes.
* **3. Your Role**:
	+ Select one of the following:
		- Board member
		- Staff member
		- Volunteer
		- Major donor
		- Community member
		- Other (Please specify)
* **4. Organization Name**: Enter the name of your organization or charity.
* **5. Voting Period**: Choose the cycle for which you want to vote:
	+ Q1 planning
	+ Q2 planning
	+ Q3 planning
	+ Q4 planning
	+ Annual planning
* **6. First Choice Fundraising Strategy**: Select one of the following strategies:
	+ Annual gala event
	+ Charity auction
	+ Fun run or walk
	+ Online crowdfunding
	+ Grant writing
	+ Corporate partnerships
	+ Monthly donor program
	+ Capital campaign
* **7. Second Choice Fundraising Strategy**: Select one of the following strategies:
	+ Annual gala event
	+ Charity auction
	+ Fun run or walk
	+ Online crowdfunding
	+ Grant writing
	+ Corporate partnerships
	+ Monthly donor program
	+ Capital campaign
* **8. Third Choice Fundraising Strategy**: Select one of the following strategies:
	+ Annual gala event
	+ Charity auction
	+ Fun run or walk
	+ Online crowdfunding
	+ Grant writing
	+ Corporate partnerships
	+ Monthly donor program
	+ Capital campaign
* **9. Preferred Budget Range**: Choose your preferred budget range for each strategy:
	+ Under $10,000
	+ $10,000 to $25,000
	+ $25,001 to $50,000
	+ $50,001 to $100,000
	+ Over $100,000
* **10. Preferred Timeline**: Choose your preferred timeline for implementation:
	+ Immediate within 1 month
	+ Short-term 1 to 3 months
	+ Medium-term 3 to 6 months
	+ Long-term 6 to 12 months
* **11. Available Resources**: Select all resources you can contribute:
	+ Time volunteering
	+ Financial donation
	+ Professional skills
	+ Venue access
	+ Network connections
	+ In-kind donations
	+ None at this time
* **12. Risk Tolerance**: Choose your risk tolerance level:
	+ High risk high reward
	+ Moderate risk
	+ Low risk stable return
	+ Very conservative
* **13. Importance of Community Visibility**: Assess the importance of community visibility:
	+ Very important
	+ Important
	+ Neutral
	+ Not important
* **14. Reasoning for Your Choices** (Optional): Provide reasoning for your selected strategies.
* **15. Additional Strategy Suggestions** (Optional): Share any additional strategy suggestions.
* **16. Implementation Concerns** (Optional): Mention any implementation concerns you have.

Note: This form is for gathering opinions and preferences. Please answer honestly, and feel free to provide reasoning for your choices.
