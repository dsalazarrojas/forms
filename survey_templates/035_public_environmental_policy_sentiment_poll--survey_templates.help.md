# Public Environmental Policy Sentiment Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users on their opinions and concerns regarding public environmental policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. You can select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. For open-ended questions, please provide as much detail as possible.
4. Make sure to enter your email address to submit your response.
