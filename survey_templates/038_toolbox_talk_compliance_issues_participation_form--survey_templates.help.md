# Toolbox Talk: Compliance Issues Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about compliance issues that have been addressed in the "Toolbox Talk" meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Introduction" field with a brief description of the compliance issue.
2.  Select the nature of the compliance issue (New or Ongoing) in the "Nature of the Compliance Issue" field.
3.  Select whether the compliance issue was addressed ("Yes" or "No") in the "Addressed by the Toolbox Talk" field.
4.  Enter the total number of compliance issues in the "Total Compliance Issues" field.
5.  Enter the average severity of the compliance issues in the "Average Severity of Compliance Issues" field.
6.  Enter the date the compliance issue was first noticed in the "When was the Compliance Issue First Noted" field.
7.  Enter the expected resolution date in the "Expected Resolution Date" field.
8.  Enter any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
9.  Enter the response to general question 5 in the "General Question 5" field.
10. Enter the response to general question 6 in the "General Question 6" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (1, text, required): A brief description of the compliance issue.
* **Nature of the Compliance Issue** (2, select_one, required): The nature of the compliance issue, whether it is New or Ongoing.
* **Was the Compliance Issue New or Ongoing** (3, select_one, required): Whether the compliance issue was new or ongoing.
* **Addressed by the Toolbox Talk** (4, select_one, required): Whether the compliance issue was addressed ("Yes" or "No").
* **Total Compliance Issues** (6, number, required): The total number of compliance issues.
* **Average Severity of Compliance Issues** (7, number, required): The average severity of the compliance issues.
* **When was the Compliance Issue First Noted** (8, date, required): The date the compliance issue was first noticed.
* **Expected Resolution Date** (9, date, required): The expected resolution date.
* **Additional Comments** (5, text, required): Any additional comments about the compliance issue.
* **General Question 5** (10, text, required): The response to general question 5.
* **General Question 6** (11, text, required): The response to general question 6.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form for any errors before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your team members for help.
