# Compensation And Benefits Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your satisfaction with compensation and benefits provided by your employer. The purpose is to help us improve our compensation packages and better understand your needs and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your answers from the provided options and provide any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, optional): This is a unique identifier that helps us track your feedback. Please feel free to leave this blank if you'd rather not disclose your ID.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Choose the department you belong to.
* **Length of Employment** (`tenure`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how long you've worked here.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your employment status (full-time, part-time, contract, etc.).
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current job level (entry-level, junior, mid-level, etc.).
* **Satisfaction with Base Salary** (`base_pay_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with your base salary.
* **Base Pay is Fair** (`base_pay_fairness`, `select_one`, required): Rate your agreement with the fairness of your base pay.
* **Base Pay is Competitive** (`base_pay_market`, `select_one`, required): Rate how competitive your base pay is with the market.
* **Satisfaction with Bonus or Incentive Pay** (`bonus_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): If applicable to your role, rate your satisfaction with your bonus or incentive pay.
* **Satisfaction with Health Insurance** (`health_insurance_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with your health insurance.
* **Satisfaction with Dental and Vision** (`dental_vision_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with your dental and vision coverage.
* **Satisfaction with Retirement Benefits** (`retirement_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with your retirement benefits.
* **Satisfaction with Paid Time Off** (`pto_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with your paid time off.
* **Satisfaction with Sick Leave** (`sick_leave_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with your sick leave.
* **Satisfaction with Parental Leave** (`parental_leave_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): If applicable, rate your satisfaction with your parental leave.
* **Satisfaction with Professional Development** (`professional_development`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with your professional development opportunities.
* **Satisfaction with Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with your work-life balance.
* **Satisfaction with Other Perks** (`other_perks_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with any other benefits or perks you receive.
* **You Feel Valued** (`total_compensation_value`, `select_one`, required): Rate whether you feel valued through your compensation.
* **Pay Transparency** (`pay_transparency`, `select_one`, required): Rate how well you understand how your pay is determined.
* **Raise Frequency** (`raise_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Rate how often you receive raises.
* **Benefits Communication** (`benefits_communication`, `select_one`, required): Rate how well you understand your benefits.
* **Most Valued Benefit** (`most_valued_benefit`, `select_one`, required): Choose the benefit you value most.
* **Least Valued Benefit** (`least_satisfied_benefit`, `select_one`, required): Choose the benefit you value least.
* **Improvements Suggested** (`improvements_suggested`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions you may have for improving benefits.
* **Would Recommend Employer** (`recommend_employer`, `select_one`, required): Rate whether you would recommend this employer based on compensation and benefits.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional feedback you may have.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out.
* Your feedback is appreciated and will help us improve our compensation packages.
