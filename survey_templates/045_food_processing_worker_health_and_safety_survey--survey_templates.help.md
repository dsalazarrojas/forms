# Food Processing Worker Health And Safety Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Food Processing Worker Health And Safety Survey" is designed to gather feedback from food processing industry employees on their working conditions, hazards, and suggestions for improvement. It will be used to identify areas that require attention and enhance the working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please answer each question as honestly as possible, providing specific examples or descriptions when requested.
2. Select all applicable workplace hazards from the list provided.
3. If you have experienced a near miss, describe it in the "Near Misses" field.
4. Choose the root cause of any near miss from the list provided.
5. In the "Corrective Actions" field, describe any actions taken to address the near miss or hazard.
6. In "Employee Suggestions," share any ideas or recommendations for improving workplace safety and efficiency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, optional): This field is for employees to provide any general feedback or comments about their working conditions, concerns, or suggestions.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all departments where you work, such as Food Processing, Packaging, Warehousing, Manufacturing, or Quality Control.
* **Workplace Hazards** (`workplace_hazards`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all applicable hazards that you have experienced or witnessed, including Slip, trip, or fall, Chemicals or chemicals-related, Biological agents, Machinery or equipment, Violence or threats, Weather, Fire, or Other.
* **Near Misses** (`nearmisses`, note, optional): If you have experienced a near miss, describe the incident in detail, including what happened, where it occurred, and any contributing factors.
* **Root Causes** (`rootcauses`, select_one, optional): If a near miss occurred, choose the root cause from the list provided, such as Equipment malfunction, Human error, Lack of training, Inadequate procedures, or Other.
* **Corrective Actions** (`correctiveactions`, text, optional): Describe any actions taken to address the near miss or hazard.
* **Employee Suggestions** (`employee_suggestions`, text, optional): Share any ideas or recommendations for improving workplace safety and efficiency.
