# Digital Tracking Features Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Digital Tracking Features Survey" is a form used to gather information about users' preferences and interactions with digital features. It helps inform product development or monitor user behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred tracking features.
2. Choose your preferred level of privacy.
3. Indicate the features you find acceptable to use.
4. (Optional) Write your opinions about the features.
5. Enter your email preferences.
6. Enter your phone preferences.
7. Select the date and time of your last interaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tracking Preferences**: Select all the features you are willing to be tracked.
	+ This field allows you to indicate your willingness to be tracked for certain features.
* **Privacy Preferences**: Choose your preferred level of privacy.
	+ This field is required, so please select one option.
* **Acceptable Use Cases**: Select all the features you consider acceptable to use.
	+ This field allows you to indicate which features you feel are acceptable to use.
* **User Opinions**: If you have any opinions about the features, please feel free to write them here.
	+ This field is optional, so feel free to write as little or as much as you like.
* **Email Preferences**: Enter your email address if you want to receive notifications or updates.
	+ This field is optional, so you can leave it blank.
* **Phone Preferences**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
	+ This field is optional, so you can leave it blank.
* **Date Last Interaction**: Select the date of your last interaction with the service.
	+ This field is optional, so feel free to leave it blank.
* **Time Last Interaction**: Select the time of your last interaction with the service.
	+ This field is optional, so feel free to leave it blank.

## Tips

* Please be honest in your responses, as this information will help us improve the service.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
