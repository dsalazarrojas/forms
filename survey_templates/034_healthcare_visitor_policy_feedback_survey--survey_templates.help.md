# Healthcare Visitor Policy Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback on the hospital's visitor policy, to help us improve and make your experience better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the answer that best matches your experience for each question.
2. If you have any comments or suggestions, use the "Do you have any comments or suggestions?" field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How do you feel about how well the hospital communicated the visitor policy to you?** (communication, select_one)
	+ Please select one of the options that best describes your feelings about how well the hospital communicated the visitor policy.
* **How would you rate your overall experience?** (experience, number)
	+ Use a number from 1 to 5 to rate your overall experience at the hospital.
* **How would you rate the responsiveness of hospital staff?** (staff_response, number)
	+ Use a number from 1 to 5 to rate the responsiveness of hospital staff.
* **Were the visitation rules clear to you?** (rules, select_multiple)
	+ Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions?** (comments, note)
	+ If you have any comments or suggestions about the hospital's visitor policy, please share them here.
