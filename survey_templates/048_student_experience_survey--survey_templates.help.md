# Student Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Experience Survey is designed to gather feedback from students about their experience with the course, instructor, and overall learning environment. The survey aims to collect information on the student's perceptions, feelings, and opinions about the course, which will help improve the teaching and learning process. To ensure you provide valuable feedback, please take a few minutes to answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the questions carefully and answer each one to the best of your ability.
2.  For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3.  Enter your responses in the text fields and select your answers from the dropdown menus.
4.  If you have additional comments, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
5.  Fill in your contact information for us to reach out to you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to the Survey** (Page 1): This section welcomes you to the survey and provides an introduction to the questions that follow.
* **How do you enjoy your class?** (Course Feedback): This question allows you to share your feelings about the class. Please select one of the options that best describes your experience.
	+ Option 1: The class was excellent.
	+ Option 2: The class was good.
	+ Option 3: The class was average.
* **What did you learn from the instructor?** (Instructor Feedback): This question helps us understand what you gained from the instructor's teaching. Please select all that apply from the options below.
	+ Option 1: The instructor was engaging and motivating.
	+ Option 2: The instructor was knowledgeable and clear.
	+ Option 3: The instructor was helpful.
	+ Option 4: The instructor was unhelpful.
* **What was your overall experience?** (Overall Experience): This question allows you to rate your overall experience with the course on a numerical scale.
* **Instructor Name**: This field is for you to enter the name of your instructor, if you wish to provide it.
* **Course Name**: This field is for you to enter the name of the course, if you wish to provide it.
* **Date**: This field is for you to enter the date you took the course, if you wish to provide it.
* **Time**: This field is for you to enter the time you took the course, if you wish to provide it.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments): If you have any further feedback or comments, please use this field to share them.
* **Email**: This field is for you to enter your email address, if you wish to provide it.
* **Phone**: This field is for you to enter your phone number, if you wish to provide it.
