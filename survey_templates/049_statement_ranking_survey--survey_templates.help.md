# Statement Ranking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Statement Ranking Survey is a form designed to collect user input on various statements, allowing users to rank their preferences or opinions on a scale. This form is intended for collecting data on user opinions, experiences, or preferences regarding specific statements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the introduction page and familiarize yourself with the survey questions.
2. Read each statement carefully and select your preferred answer for each one. For select_one fields, choose the option that best reflects your opinion or preference. For select_multiple fields, select all applicable options.
3. Answer the number fields (question1, question2, question3, etc.) with the corresponding numeric values.
4. Complete all required fields. You cannot submit the form with incomplete data.
