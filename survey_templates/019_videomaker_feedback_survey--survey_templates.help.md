# Videomaker Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with Videomaker. The goal is to understand their satisfaction with our services and gather insights to improve future projects.

## How To Complete This Form

*   This form consists of 7 fields that ask for client feedback and contact information.
*   Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to provide us with valuable feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *Client Feedback* (`client_feedback`, text, optional)
    This field is for you to share any thoughts or comments about your experience with Videomaker.
*   *Production Services* (`production_services`, select_multiple, optional)
    Please select from the options to indicate which production services you received from us. This will help us understand the scope of your project.
*   *Rating* (`rating`, number, optional)
    Rate your overall satisfaction with our services on a scale of 1-5.
*   *Comments* (`comments`, text, optional)
    Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
*   *Email* (`email`, email, optional)
    If you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions, please provide your email address.
*   *Phone* (`phone`, text, optional)
    If you would like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
*   *Assigned To* (`assigned_to`, text, optional)
    If you know who you were working with during your project, please let us know who it was.
