# fence_marketing_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about a company's marketing strategy and tactics. It will help us understand your marketing goals, target audience, and competitive analysis, among other aspects of your marketing efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your company in the "Company Description" field (optional).
3. Select your marketing strategy from the dropdown menu in the "Marketing Strategy" field.
4. Choose your target market from the dropdown menu in the "Target Market" field. You can select multiple options.
5. Describe your goals and objectives in the "Goals and Objectives" field.
6. Describe your competitive analysis in the "Competitive Analysis" field.
7. Enter a number to represent your competitor's marketing budget in the "Competitor Marketing Budget" field (optional).
8. Enter a number to represent your competitor's pricing in the "Competitor Pricing" field (optional).
9. Select your company's product/service from the dropdown menu in the "Company Products" field.
10. Describe your target audience in the "Target Audience" field.
11. Describe your marketing channels in the "Marketing Channels" field.
12. Enter a number to represent your marketing budget in the "Marketing Budget" field.
13. Describe your pricing strategy in the "Pricing Strategy" field (optional).
14. Describe your competitor's analysis in the "Competitor Analysis" field.
15. Describe your marketing strategy description in the "Marketing Strategy Description" field (optional).
16. Describe your target market description in the "Target Market Description" field (optional).
17. Enter your website URL in the "Company Website" field (optional).
18. Enter your social media handle in the "Company Social Media" field (optional).
19. Enter your company's email address in the "Company Email" field.
20. Enter your company's phone number in the "Company Phone" field.
21. Enter your company's fax number in the "Company Fax" field (optional).
22. Enter your competitor's phone number in the "Competitor Phone" field (optional).
23. Enter your competitor's fax number in the "Competitor Fax" field (optional).
24. Enter your submission information in the "Submitted By" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (1, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Company Description** (2, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your company (optional).
* **Marketing Strategy** (3, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Survey Strategy
	+ Digital Strategy
	+ Traditional Strategy
* **Target Market** (4, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Homeowners
	+ Businesses
	+ Both
* **Goals and Objectives** (5, text, required): Describe your marketing goals and objectives.
* **Goals and Objectives Description** (6, text, optional): Provide more information about your goals and objectives (optional).
* **Competitive Analysis** (7, text, required): Describe your competitor's marketing analysis.
* **Competitor Marketing Budget** (8, number, optional): Enter a number to represent your competitor's marketing budget (optional).
* **Competitor Pricing** (9, number, optional): Enter a number to represent your competitor's pricing (optional).
* **Company Products** (10, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Fence
	+ Deck
	+ Gate
* **Target Audience** (11, text, required): Describe your target audience.
* **Marketing Channels** (12, text, required): Describe your marketing channels.
* **Marketing Budget** (13, number, required): Enter a number to represent your marketing budget.
* **Pricing Strategy** (14, text, optional): Describe your pricing strategy (optional).
* **Competitor Analysis** (15, text, required): Describe your competitor's analysis.
* **Marketing Strategy Description** (16, text, optional): Provide more information about your marketing strategy (optional).
* **Target Market Description** (17, text, optional): Provide more information about your target market (optional).
* **Company Website** (18, text, optional): Enter your company's website URL (optional).
* **Company Social Media** (19, text, optional): Enter your company's social media handle (optional).
* **Company Email** (20, email, required): Enter your company's email address.
* **Company Phone** (21, text, required): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Fax** (22, text, optional): Enter your company's fax number (optional).
* **Competitor Phone** (23, text, optional): Enter your competitor's phone number (optional).
* **Competitor Fax** (24, text, optional): Enter your competitor's fax number (optional).
* **Submitted By** (25, text, optional): Enter submission information (optional).
