# Union Officer Election Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from union members on the union officer election process. The goal is to gain insight into the members' experiences and opinions on the election's fairness, satisfaction, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select one or more of the options for each question to express your opinions on the union officer election process.
2. If you have additional feedback or comments, you can type them in the "member_feedback" field.
3. If you want to provide your contact information, you can enter your email address and/or phone number in the "contact_email" and "contact_phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Union Officer Election Survey** (Select Multiple):
	+ Select one or more of the options to express your likelihood of voting in the union officer election.
* **Election Fairness** (Select One):
	+ Choose how fair you think the election process is.
* **Election Satisfaction** (Select One):
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with the election process.
* **Officer Candidate Support** (Select One):
	+ Choose how supportive you think the union officer candidates were.
* **Member Feedback** (Note):
	+ Provide any additional feedback or comments about the election process.
* **Contact Email** (Email):
	+ Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Contact Phone** (Text):
	+ Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Election Date** (Date):
	+ Choose the date of the union officer election.
* **Election Time** (Time):
	+ Choose the time of the union officer election.
* **Election Location** (Text):
	+ Enter the location of the union officer election.
* **Election Venue** (Text):
	+ Enter the name of the venue where the union officer election will be held.
* **Union Address** (Text):
	+ Enter the union's address.
* **Union Phone** (Text):
	+ Enter the union's phone number.
* **Election Committee** (Text):
	+ Enter the name of the election committee.
* **Officer Election Type** (Text):
	+ Enter the type of the union officer election.
* **Officer Terms** (Number):
	+ Enter the term of the union officers.
* **Officer Election Date** (Date):
	+ Choose the date of the next union officer election.
* **Election Recurring** (Select One):
	+ Choose whether the union officer election will be recurring.
