# Client Feedback Usage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Feedback Usage Consent Form is designed to collect client feedback and gather information about their experience with your company. This form is essential for improving your services and products and ensuring that you can use client feedback to create valuable content for marketing and other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  First, please fill in your personal details: **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address**. These are required fields.
2.  Then, choose the **Type of Feedback Provided** from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
3.  Fill in the **Date Feedback Was Provided** to let us know when you submitted your feedback.
4.  Write down your **Testimonial or Feedback** in the text area. This is an essential part of your feedback.
5.  Next, choose how you wish to be attributed in our feedback use: **How Should We Attribute Your Feedback?**
6.  Select whether we may use your feedback in **Marketing**, **Website**, **Social Media**, and **Print Materials**.
7.  If applicable, choose whether we may use your **Company Logo**.
8.  Choose whether we may use your **Photo**.
9.  If you provided a **Video Testimonial**, choose whether we may use it.
10. Select the **Geographic Scope** where we may use your feedback.
11. Choose how long we may use your feedback: **Indefinitely**, **1 Year**, **2 Years**, or **5 Years**.
12. Select whether we may **Edit Your Feedback for Clarity**.
13. Choose whether your feedback is **Exclusive to Us**.
14. Select whether you received any **Compensation for this Feedback**.
15. If applicable, choose whether you have a **Relationship with Our Company**.
16. Check whether you **Agree to the Terms of This Consent Form**.
17. Finally, choose your **Consent Date**.
18. Fill in your **Electronic Signature** with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your legal first name. This is a required field.
* **Last Name**: Your legal last name. This is a required field.
* **Email Address**: Your primary contact email. This is a required field.
* **Type of Feedback Provided**: Choose the type of feedback you provided (e.g., testimonial, case study, product review, etc.). This is a required field.
* **Date Feedback Was Provided**: The date when you submitted your feedback. This is a required field.
* **Your Testimonial or Feedback**: Write down your feedback in this text area. This is an essential part of your feedback.
* **How Should We Attribute Your Feedback?**: Choose how you wish to be attributed in our feedback use (full name and company, full name only, etc.).
* **May We Use Your Feedback for Marketing?**: Choose whether we may use your feedback in marketing materials.
* **May We Use Your Feedback on Our Website?**: Choose whether we may use your feedback on our website.
* **May We Use Your Feedback on Social Media?**: Choose whether we may use your feedback on social media.
* **May We Use Your Feedback in Print Materials?**: Choose whether we may use your feedback in print materials.
* **May We Use Your Company Logo?**: If applicable, choose whether we may use your company logo.
* **May We Use Your Photo?**: If you provided a photo, choose whether we may use it.
* **May We Use Your Video Testimonial?**: If you provided a video testimonial, choose whether we may use it.
* **Where May We Use Your Feedback?**: Choose the geographic scope where we may use your feedback.
* **How Long May We Use Your Feedback?**: Choose how long we may use your feedback.
* **May We Edit Your Feedback for Clarity?**: Choose whether we may edit your feedback for clarity.
* **Is This Feedback Exclusive to Us?**: Choose whether your feedback is exclusive to us.
* **Did You Receive Compensation for This Feedback?**: Choose whether you received any compensation for this feedback.
* **Do You Have Any Relationship with Our Company?**: If applicable, choose whether you have a relationship with our company.
* **I Agree to the Terms of This Consent Form**: Check whether you agree to the terms of this consent form.
* **Consent Date**: Date of signing.
* **Electronic Signature**: Type your full name. This is a required field.
