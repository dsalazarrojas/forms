# Anxiety Self Reflection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals reflect on their current state of mind, identify patterns and triggers of their anxiety, and develop strategies to cope with it. It's a self-assessment tool to help you better understand your anxiety levels, identify areas for improvement, and find effective coping mechanisms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form at the current time of day (morning, afternoon, evening, or night).
2. Rate your anxiety level from 1 to 10.
3. Describe your current mood and energy level.
4. Select the physical symptoms you're experiencing today.
5. Describe your primary worries and their intensity.
6. Identify any thought patterns that might be contributing to your anxiety.
7. Describe any triggers or situations that triggered your anxiety.
8. Choose how you responded to these triggers.
9. Indicate whether you avoided any situations.
10. List any coping strategies you used to manage your anxiety.
11. Rate the effectiveness of these strategies.
12. Indicate if you tried any new strategies.
13. Describe any new strategies you tried.
14. Indicate your level of social interaction today.
15. Describe if you sought support from others.
16. Rate the helpfulness of the support you received.
17. List any accomplishments or things you're grateful for today.
18. Describe any self-care activities you did for yourself.
19. Reflect on what worked well today and what was challenging.
20. Describe any insights you gained about your anxiety.
21. Set goals for tomorrow.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **reflection_date** (Date of Reflection): Enter the current date in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **time_of_day** (Time of Day): Choose the time of day when you're completing this form (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening, or night).
* **current_mood** (Current Mood): Select how you're feeling right now (e.g., very calm, calm, neutral, anxious, very anxious).
* **anxiety_level_1_to_10** (Anxiety Level): Rate your anxiety level from 1 to 10.
* **sleep_quality** (How well did you sleep last night): Select how well you slept last night (e.g., excellent, good, fair, poor, very poor).
* **sleep_hours** (Hours of Sleep): Enter the approximate number of hours you slept last night.
* **energy_level** (Energy Level Today): Choose how your energy level is today (e.g., very high, high, normal, low, very low).
* **physical_symptoms** (Physical Symptoms Today): Select all the physical symptoms you're experiencing today.
* **thought_patterns** (Thought Patterns Noticed): Identify any thought patterns that might be contributing to your anxiety.
* **anxiety_triggers_today** (What triggered your anxiety today): Describe any triggers or situations that triggered your anxiety.
* **trigger_response** (How did you respond to triggers): Choose how you responded to these triggers.
* **situations_avoided** (Did you avoid any situations): Indicate whether you avoided any situations.
* **coping_strategies** (Coping Strategies Used Today): Select all the coping strategies you used to manage your anxiety.
* **strategy_effectiveness** (How effective were these strategies): Rate the effectiveness of these strategies.
* **new_strategies_tried** (Did you try any new strategies): Indicate if you tried any new strategies.
* **new_strategy_description** (Describe New Strategy): Describe any new strategies you tried.
* **social_interaction** (Social Interaction Today): Select your level of social interaction today.
* **support_sought** (Did you seek support from others): Describe if you sought support from others.
* **support_received** (Was support helpful): Rate the helpfulness of the support you received.
* **accomplishments_today** (What did you accomplish today): List any accomplishments or things you're grateful for today.
* **gratitude_items** (What are you grateful for today): List at least one thing you're grateful for today.
* **self_care_activities** (Self-Care Activities): Describe any self-care activities you did for yourself.
* **what_worked_well** (What worked well today): Reflect on what worked well today.
* **what_was_challenging** (What was challenging today): Describe any difficulties you faced today.
* **what_did_you_learn** (What did you learn about your anxiety): Reflect on any insights you gained about your anxiety.
* **tomorrow_goals** (Goals for Tomorrow): Set goals for tomorrow.
* **encouragement_to_self** (Words of Encouragement to Yourself): Offer words of encouragement to yourself.
