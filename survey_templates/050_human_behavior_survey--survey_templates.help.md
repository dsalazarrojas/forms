# human_behavior_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an individual's human behavior, including their daily routines, stress levels, relationships, work-life balance, and self-care practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
2. Select the most suitable option for each multiple-choice question.
3. Enter text responses for open-ended questions, such as "What are your work-life balance goals?"
4. Complete all questions that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your daily routines?** (Text)
	+ This question is designed to understand your daily routines and habits.
* **How often do you experience stress?** (Select One: Low, Moderate, High)
	+ This question helps to understand how frequently you experience stress.
* **How would you rate your work-life balance?** (Select One: Excellent, Needs improvement, No time for self)
	+ This question measures how balanced you feel between your work and personal life.
* **How many hours do you usually work per week?** (Number)
	+ This question measures your work hours and helps to understand your work-life balance.
* **How many hours do you usually sleep per night?** (Number)
	+ This question measures your sleep quality and helps to understand your self-care practices.

	+ This question helps to understand your physical activity level.
* **Which of the following self-care practices do you usually practice?** (Select Multiple: Mindfulness, Journaling, Meditation)
	+ This question measures your self-care practices and habits.

	+ This question measures whether you have good time management skills.
