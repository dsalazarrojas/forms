# Financial Consulting Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a self-reporting tool designed to gather data about your experience as a financial consultant. It will help us understand your processes, approaches, and client outcomes. Your feedback will inform our training and support efforts to improve your efficiency and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant answers from the provided options or enter a free-text response as required.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, only select one option per question.
3. For select_multiple fields, you can select multiple options.
4. For select_one fields, only select one option.
5. Enter clear and concise answers in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Financial Survey Form Data
* **Financial Survey Form Data** (`financial_survey_form_data`, `note`, required: false): This is an optional field for any additional comments or information you'd like to provide about the engagement.

### Financial Consultant Details
* **Financial Consultant Details** (`consultant_details`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your name and title as a financial consultant.

### Client Details
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, required: true): Please enter the name and contact information of the client you worked with.

### Survey Questions
* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the questions that apply to your engagement.

### What was the primary reason for the engagement?
* **What was the primary reason for the engagement?** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the primary reason for the engagement from the provided options.

### What was your approach to the engagement?
* **What was your approach to the engagement?** (`question_2`, `text`, required: true): Describe your approach to the engagement in a few words or sentences.

### What were the outcomes of the engagement?
* **What were the outcomes of the engagement?** (`question_3`, `text`, required: true): Describe the outcomes of the engagement in a few words or sentences.

### How would you rate the client's satisfaction with the service?
* **How would you rate the client's satisfaction with the service?** (`question_4`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the client's satisfaction with the service using the provided options.
