# employee_orientation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employee_orientation_survey" form is designed to collect information about new hires to create a comprehensive employee profile and facilitate a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as `required` in the form.
2. Select the relevant information from the provided options (if applicable).
3. Ensure that all fields are accurately filled out to avoid errors or discrepancies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Survey** (`new_hire_survey`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the new hire.
* **Employee Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the new employee.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department where the new employee is currently working.
	+ Options: Marketing, Sales, Support
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the new employee's manager.
* **Date Hired** (`date_hired`, date, optional): Enter the date the new employee was hired.
* **Onboarding Process** (`onboarding_process`, note, optional): Describe the onboarding process for the new employee.
* **Training** (`training`, text, optional): Specify any training needs for the new employee.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the new employee.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the new employee's supervisor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the new employee.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting this form.

Note: All fields marked as `required` must be filled out accurately to ensure that the information is up-to-date and useful for future references.
