# Animal Network Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on your experience within the Animal Network, understanding your satisfaction with its quality, leadership, and overall usefulness. The survey aims to identify areas for improvement and help shape the network to better serve its members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select an answer for each question based on your genuine experience in the Animal Network.
2.  Ensure that all required fields (marked as `true` in the form) are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Member Organization
*   **Member Organization** (`Member Organization`, `text`, required): This field requires your current or former organization's name where you hold or held membership in the Animal Network. Enter the name of your organization to help us associate your response to your institution. 

### How long have you been a member? 
*   **How long have you been a member?** (`How long have you been a member?`, `select_one`, required): This question asks for the duration of your membership within the network. Choose the time period that best describes your engagement with the network from Less than 1 year to 5 plus years.

### Frequency of Communication with the Network
*   **Frequency of Communication with the Network** (`Frequency of Communication with the Network`, `select_one`, required): This question seeks to know the frequency with which you interact with the network. Indicate how often you engage with the network from Daily to Rarely.

### Quality of Collaboration within the Network
*   **Quality of Collaboration within the Network** (`Quality of Collaboration within the Network`, `select_one`, required): This asks for your assessment of the quality of collaboration within the Animal Network. Rate it from Excellent to Poor.

### Usefulness of Network Resources
*   **Usefulness of Network Resources** (`Usefulness of Network Resources`, `select_one`, required): Choose how useful you find the network's resources, including toolkits and data sharing. Rate it from Very Useful to Not Useful.

### What is the primary benefit of your membership? 
*   **What is the primary benefit of your membership?** (`What is the primary benefit of your membership?`, `text`, optional): This field is optional, but if you have a primary reason for being part of the network, please describe it to help us understand the network's value proposition. 

### Satisfaction with Network Leadership
*   **Satisfaction with Network Leadership** (`Satisfaction with Network Leadership`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the leadership of the network from Very Satisfied to Dissatisfied.

### Areas for Improvement
*   **Areas for Improvement** (`Areas for Improvement`, `text`, optional): This field allows you to suggest areas where the network could improve, such as better communication or resource distribution. It is not a required field, but your insights are valuable for us.

### Would you recommend this network to other organizations?
*   **Would you recommend this network to other organizations?** (`Would you recommend this network to other organizations?`, `select_one`, required): Lastly, this question helps us determine if you would recommend the Animal Network to other organizations, from Definitely to Not at all. 

This form aims to gather your honest feedback to improve the Animal Network for its members. Your input is crucial to us, and we will use it to enhance the network's quality and value proposition.
