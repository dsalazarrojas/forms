# Municipality Transition Plan Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the municipality transition plan from various stakeholders. Your input will help us refine the plan to better meet the community's needs and address any concerns you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your feedback on the transition plan (Page 1).
2. Share your concerns about the transition plan (Page 2).
3. Indicate your level of readiness with the transition plan (Page 3).
4. Suggest any ideas for improvement (Page 4).
5. If you have any questions, you can provide them on Page 5.
6. If you have any additional comments, you can provide them on Page 6.
7. Finally, provide your contact information so we can follow up with you (Page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your feedback on our transition plan?** (`introduction`, text, **Required**): Please provide a brief written feedback on our transition plan. This could include positive comments, suggestions, or concerns.
* **What are your concerns about our transition plan?** (`concerns`, text, **Required**): Share any specific concerns or issues you have with the transition plan. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your perspective.
* **How ready do you feel about our transition plan?** (`readiness`, select_one, **Required**): Choose one of the following options to indicate your level of readiness:
	+ Very Unprepared
	+ Somewhat Prepared
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Unprepared
	+ Very Prepared
* **What ideas do you have to improve our transition plan?** (`improvement_ideas`, text, **Required**): Share any suggestions or ideas you have to enhance the transition plan. Think about what would make the plan more effective or efficient.
* **Questions** (`questions`, text, **Optional**): If you have any questions about the transition plan, please provide them here. This will help us address any confusion or misunderstandings.
* **Any other comments?** (`comments`, text, **Optional**): Share any additional thoughts or comments you have about the transition plan. This could include suggestions, concerns, or ideas not covered in the previous questions.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, **Required**): Please provide your contact information so we can follow up with you to discuss your feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Other** (`other`, text, **Optional**): If you have any other information you'd like to share with us, please enter it here.
