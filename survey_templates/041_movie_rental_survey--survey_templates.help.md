# Movie Rental Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to help you complete the Movie Rental Survey form. The form is meant to gather information about your movie rental preferences and behaviors. Please follow the instructions carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant responses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully and only answer questions that apply to your own movie rental experiences.
2. Start by reviewing the form pages and questions.
3. Fill out each page and field according to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Page**: A general introduction to the survey.
* **movie-preference** (`movie_preference`, select_multiple): Do you prefer to rent movies? 
	+ Select "Yes" if you regularly rent movies.
	+ Select "No" if you rarely or never rent movies.
* **favorite-movies** (`favorite_movies`, select_multiple): What are your favorite movies? 
	+ Select "Yes" if you enjoy watching these movies.
	+ Select "No" if you do not enjoy watching these movies.
* **watch-again** (`watch_again`, select_one): Would you watch this movie again? 
	+ Select "Yes" if you would watch the movie again.
	+ Select "No" if you would not watch the movie again.
* **rating** (`rating`, number): What is your rating for the movie? 
	+ Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the movie.

## Tips

* Only answer questions that apply to your own movie rental experiences.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure the survey results are meaningful.
* If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification or skip it.
