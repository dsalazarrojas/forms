# Internet Of Things Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internet of Things (IoT) survey is designed to gather information about your experiences with smart devices and technologies. Your input will help us better understand the trends, challenges, and opportunities in the field of IoT.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, if any.
3. Provide detailed answers for text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Internet of Things Survey** (`internet_of_things_survey`, text, optional): This is the title of the survey. You can leave this field blank.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes the context in which you are using or have used IoT devices or technologies. Options:
	+ Home
	+ Work
	+ School
* **Application** (`application`, text, optional): Describe the specific application or use case of your IoT device or technology.
* **Device Type** (`device_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Smart Thermostat
	+ Smart TV
	+ Smart Lock
	+ Smart Speaker
* **Applications of IoT** (`applications_survey`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Smart Home Automation
	+ Energy Management
	+ Security and Surveillance
	+ Home Entertainment
* **No Coding Required** (`no_coding`, text, optional): If you have used or are using an IoT device or technology that does not require programming or coding, describe it here.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your experience with the IoT device or technology.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you have used a specific tool or platform to manage or interact with your IoT device or technology, describe it here.
