# Road Safety Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Road Safety Audit Form is a tool used by road safety auditors to assess and document the safety conditions of roads and highways. This form helps auditors to identify potential hazards and take corrective actions to improve road safety.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant section for the road safety audit from the drop-down menu.
2. Choose the condition of the road being audited (High, Medium, or Low) in the "Road Safety Condition" field.
3. Identify any hazards present on the road in the "Road Hazards" field.
4. Provide any notes and recommendations in the "Notes and Recommendations" field.
5. Enter your contact information, including a contact number and email, in the "Contact Number" and "Contact Email" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Section**: (`section_road_safety`, select_one, **required**)
Select the relevant section for the road safety audit. This will help to categorize the audit and focus on the specific area being evaluated.
* **Road Safety Condition**: (`road_safety_condition`, select_multiple, **required**)
Choose the condition of the road being audited. This is a key factor in determining the severity of the audit and any necessary actions.
* **Road Hazards**: (`road_hazards`, text, **required**)
Identify any hazards present on the road, including but not limited to potholes, uneven pavement, and debris.
* **Notes and Recommendations**: (`notes_and_recommendations`, note, **required**)
Provide any notes or recommendations for improving road safety.
* **Contact Number**: (`contact_number`, text, **required**)
Enter your contact information, including a phone number, to ensure that you can be reached for follow-up questions or actions.
* **Contact Email**: (`contact_email`, email, **required**)
Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further information or actions.
