# point_of_view_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The point of view survey is designed to collect information about users' opinions and experiences with a specific topic. This form is meant to help us understand different perspectives and opinions on various subjects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the required fields marked as "required".
3. Select relevant options for each field as requested.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Survey Information
* **Survey Information** (`survey_information`, `text`, optional): Please enter a brief description of the survey or topic you are answering about.

### Survey Group
* **Survey Group** (`survey_group`, `select_one`, required): Please select the group that best describes the context of the survey or topic.

### Survey Topic
* **Survey Topic** (`survey_topic`, `text`, optional): Describe the specific topic or subject you are answering about.

### Demographic Information
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_information`, `note`, required): Please provide any additional demographic information you feel is relevant to your answer.

### Age
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, optional): If applicable, please enter your age.

### Gender
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Please select your gender.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you have a professional email, please enter it.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, please enter the date you are answering about.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): If applicable, please enter the time you are answering about.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review your answers carefully before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
