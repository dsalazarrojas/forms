# Performing Ensembles Equipment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the instruments and equipment used by performing ensembles in schools or organizations. This data will be used to maintain an inventory of equipment, schedule maintenance, and potentially inform future purchasing decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all instruments and equipment that your school or organization owns.
2. Provide the name and brand of each instrument/equipment selected.
3. Choose the type of instrument/equipment (e.g., Acoustic, Electric, Bass).
4. Enter the serial number of each instrument/equipment.
5. Assess the condition of each instrument/equipment (Good, Fair, Poor).
6. Record the date each instrument/equipment was purchased.
7. Note the last maintenance date and frequency (if applicable).
8. Enter the cost of the last maintenance (if applicable).
9. Predict the next maintenance cost (if applicable).
10. Estimate the date when the instrument/equipment will need replacement.
11. Enter the estimated cost of replacement (if applicable).
12. Provide contact information for the person responsible for maintenance and upkeep.
13. Add any additional instruments and equipment not listed.
14. Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instruments and Equipment**: Select all instruments and equipment that your school or organization owns. This will be the starting point for the rest of the form.
* **Name of Instrument and Equipment**: Enter the name of each instrument/equipment selected. This will help identify and distinguish between similar items.
* **Brand**: Enter the brand of each instrument/equipment.
* **Type**: Choose the type of instrument/equipment (e.g., Acoustic, Electric, Bass).
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of each instrument/equipment. This is often necessary for warranty or maintenance purposes.
* **Condition**: Assess the condition of each instrument/equipment as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Purchased Date**: Record the date each instrument/equipment was purchased.
* **Last Maintenance Date**: Note the last date each instrument/equipment was maintained.
* **Maintenance Frequency**: If applicable, select the maintenance frequency (e.g., Annual, Bi-annual, Rarely).
* **Last Maintenance Cost**: Enter the cost of the last maintenance performed on each instrument/equipment.
* **Next Maintenance Cost**: Predict the cost of the next maintenance (if applicable).
* **Need Replacement Date**: Estimate the date when the instrument/equipment will need replacement.
* **Need Replacement Cost**: Enter the estimated cost of replacement (if applicable).
* **Contact Name**: Provide the name of the person responsible for maintenance and upkeep.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email for the person responsible.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number for the person responsible.
* **Contact Department**: Enter the department of the person responsible.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes about each instrument/equipment.
* **Additional Instruments and Equipment**: Select any additional instruments and equipment not listed initially.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the additional instruments and equipment.
* **Purchased From**: Enter where each instrument/equipment was purchased from.
* **Last Maintained By**: Note the person or organization that last performed maintenance on each instrument/equipment.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about each instrument/equipment.
