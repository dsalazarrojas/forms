# Community Club Feedback Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from members of our community club to help us understand their experiences and improve our services and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions that apply to you.
2. Make sure to provide your honest feedback, as it will help us improve the club.
3. Fill out all required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
4. Select all that apply for "Which club activities have you participated in recently".
5. Rate your satisfaction with the club and its activities using the numbers provided.
6. Provide your digital signature by typing your full name.
7. Date of submission is required for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Member** (`member_full_name_fed`, `text`, Required):
	+ Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on our records.
* **Member ID (If assigned)** (`member_id_fed`, `text`, Optional):
	+ If you have been assigned a member ID, enter it here.
* **Email Address** (`member_email_fed`, `email`, Required):
	+ Enter your email address where we can contact you and send you feedback results.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (`member_phone_fed`, `text`, Required):
	+ Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Which club activities have you participated in recently** (`club_activity_participation_fed`, `select_multiple`, Required):
	+ Select all the activities you have participated in recently.
* **Overall Satisfaction with the club** (`overall_club_satisfaction_fed`, `number`, Required):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the club on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Satisfaction with club activities** (`club_activity_satisfaction_fed`, `number`, Required):
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the club's activities on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Satisfaction with club communication** (`club_communication_satisfaction_fed`, `number`, Required):
	+ Rate your satisfaction with club communication on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **How long have you been a member of the club** (`club_membership_duration_fed`, `select_one`, Required):
	+ Select the length of time you have been a member of the club.
* **Primary reason for joining the club** (`primary_reason_for_joining_fed`, `text`, Required):
	+ Enter the primary reason you joined the club.
* **Key interests or expertise you'd like to share** (`key_club_interests_expertise_fed`, `text`, Required):
	+ Enter your key interests or expertise you'd like to share with the club.
* **What could we do to improve the club** (`club_improvement_suggestions_fed`, `text`, Optional):
	+ Enter any suggestions you have for improving the club.
* **Digital Signature** (`member_signature_digital_fed`, `text`, Required):
	+ Type your full name as a digital signature.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date_final_fed`, `date`, Required):
	+ Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **I would like to receive the community center's newsletter** (`newsletter_opt_in_fed`, `select_one`, Required):
	+ Select "Yes, sign me up!" if you'd like to receive our newsletter.
* **How did you hear about this feedback collection form** (`referral_source_fed`, `select_one`, Optional):
	+ Select how you heard about this form.
* **Digital Signature** (`member_signature_digital_fed`, `text`, Required):
	+ Type your full name as a digital signature.
