# Virtual Assistant Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help match clients with suitable virtual assistants who meet their needs and expectations. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure the best possible match.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please go through each page of the form, answering the questions in order.
2. For text fields, provide a brief but descriptive answer.
3. For select_one fields, choose the most suitable option.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
