# Reception Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reception Check Out Form is designed to gather feedback from guests and collect billing information from customers after they have checked out of your establishment or service. This form will help you understand their overall experience, any issues they faced, and provide an opportunity to collect billing details for any outstanding payments or services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Guest Feedback (text)**: Please describe your overall experience during your stay or service with us. Try to provide detailed feedback, including both positive and negative comments.
2. **Billing Information (text)**: If you have any outstanding payments or services due, please provide the necessary billing information for us to process your account.
3. **Payment Terms (text)**: Please review and agree to our payment terms, which include [insert payment terms here, e.g., due date, payment method, etc.]. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
4-9. **Payment Terms 2-5 (text)**: Please review and agree to each of our payment terms, which may include specific details about each payment, e.g., due date, payment method, etc.
10. **Billing Information 2-5 (text)**: Please provide additional billing information for each of our services or payments due.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Feedback (Guest Feedback, text, required: false)**: A text field for guests to describe their overall experience.
* **Billing Information (Billing Information, text, required: false)**: A text field for guests to provide billing information for any outstanding payments or services.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms, text, required: false)**: A text field for guests to review and agree to the payment terms.
* **Payment Terms 2 (Payment Terms 2, text, required: false)**: A text field for guests to review and agree to the second payment term.
* **Payment Terms 3 (Payment Terms 3, text, required: false)**: A text field for guests to review and agree to the third payment term.
* **Payment Terms 4 (Payment Terms 4, text, required: false)**: A text field for guests to review and agree to the fourth payment term.
* **Payment Terms 5 (Payment Terms 5, text, required: false)**: A text field for guests to review and agree to the fifth payment term.
* **Billing Information 2 (Billing Information 2, text, required: false)**: A text field for guests to provide billing information for services or payments due.
* **Billing Information 3 (Billing Information 3, text, required: false)**: A text field for guests to provide billing information for services or payments due.

## Tips
- Please be specific when providing billing information to ensure accurate processing of payments.
- Review each payment term carefully and agree to the terms before proceeding.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
