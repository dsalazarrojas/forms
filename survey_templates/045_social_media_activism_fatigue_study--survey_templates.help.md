# social_media_activism_fatigue_study - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your social media behavior and habits. Your input will help us understand and address social media fatigue. Please answer these questions honestly, and thank you for participating.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that apply to you.
3. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **how_do_you_use_facebook** (`how_do_you_use_facebook`, select_one, required): This question asks about your usage habits on Facebook.
* **how_did_you_feel_before_using_facebook_last_week** (`emotional_state`, note, required): Describe your emotions before using Facebook last week.
* **how_did_you_feel_after_using_facebook_last_week** (`emotional_state_after`, note, required): Describe your emotions after using Facebook last week.
* **how_often_do_you_use_facebook** (`survey_participation_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how often you use Facebook.
* **what_motivates_you_to_engage_with_social_media** (`engagement_mechanism`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best motivates you to use social media.
* **what_type_of_content_motivates_you_to_engage_with_social_media** (`activism_content`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of content that motivate you to use social media.
* **on_a_scale_of_1_to_10** (`fatigue_level`, number, required): Rate your level of social media fatigue on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **why_do_you_participate_in_this_study** (`survey_participation_reasons`, select_one, required): Choose a reason for participating in this study.
* **which_social_media_platforms_do_you_use** (`social_media_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select all social media platforms you use.
* **how_long_do_you_plan_to_participate** (`survey_participation_duration`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how long you plan to participate.
* **how_often_do_you_participate_in_social_media** (`survey_participation_frequency_2`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how often you participate in social media.
* **what_motivates_you_to_engage_with_social_media_2** (`engagement_mechanism_2`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best motivates you to use social media.
* **on_a_scale_of_1_to_10_2** (`fatigue_level_2`, number, required): Rate your level of social media fatigue on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **why_do_you_participate_in_this_study_2** (`survey_participation_reasons_2`, select_one, required): Choose a reason for participating in this study.
* **which_social_media_platforms_do_you_use_2** (`social_media_platforms_2`, select_multiple, required): Select all social media platforms you use.
* **how_long_do_you_plan_to_participate_2** (`survey_participation_duration_2`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how long you plan to participate.
* **will_your_results_be_shared_with_others** (`survey_participation_report`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want your results shared with others.
* **how_do_you_prefer_your_survey_results_to_be_delivered** (`survey_participation_surveyor`, select_one, required): Choose how you prefer your survey results to be delivered.
* **how_do_you_prefer_your_results_to_be_delivered_2** (`survey_participation_surveyor_2`, select_one, required): Choose how you prefer your survey results to be delivered.
* **Thank You** (`thank_you`, note, required): Thank you for participating!

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Make sure to select all relevant options for each question.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Your participation is greatly appreciated.
