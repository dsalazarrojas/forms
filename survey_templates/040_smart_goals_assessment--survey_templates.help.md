# SMART Goals Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SMART Goals Assessment form is designed to help you evaluate whether a goal meets the SMART criteria: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. This form will guide you through a series of questions to help you assess each aspect of your goal.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer based on your goal.
2. Select the most appropriate answer for each select_one question.
3. Provide clear and specific answers to each text question.
4. Make sure to read the hints for each question for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`id: 1`): This is an introduction to the form. Read the hint to understand the purpose of this form.

### Goal Clarity
* **Is the goal clear?** (`id: 2`): Answer this question with a clear and specific sentence that describes your goal. This will help you understand if your goal is well-defined and easy to understand.

### Goal Achievability
* **Is the goal achievable?** (`id: 3`): Choose "Yes" if you have the resources and skills to achieve the goal. Consider the resources and challenges you may face.

### Goal Relevance
* **Is the goal relevant?** (`id: 4`): Select "Yes" if your goal aligns with your overall objectives. This will help you understand if the goal is worthwhile and relevant to your goals.

### Goal Measurability
* **How will you measure progress towards the goal?** (`id: 5`): Provide specific metrics or indicators to track progress towards your goal.

### Goal Attainability
* **Is the goal attainable?** (`id: 6`): Choose "Yes" if you have the resources and skills to achieve the goal. Consider the resources and challenges you may face.

### Goal Timeliness
* **Is the goal time-bound?** (`id: 7`): Select "Yes" if you have a specific deadline or timeframe for achieving the goal.

### Goal Specificity
* **Is the goal specific?** (`id: 8`): Choose "Yes" if your goal is clearly defined and easy to understand.

### Goal Outcome
* **What is the expected outcome or result of achieving the goal?** (`id: 9`): Describe the benefits or impact of achieving your goal.

### Goal Progress Tracking
* **How will you track and report progress towards the goal?** (`id: 10`): Describe your process for tracking progress.

### Goal Impact
* **What is the expected impact or benefit of achieving the goal?** (`id: 11`): Describe the effects on your work or life.

### Additional Comments or Feedback
* **Additional comments or feedback** (`id: 12`): Share any additional thoughts or insights you have about your goal.

Remember, this form is designed to help you assess the SMARTness of your goal, and your answers are for your own reference and use.
