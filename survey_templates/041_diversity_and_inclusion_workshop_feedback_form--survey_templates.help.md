# Diversity And Inclusion Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience with the diversity and inclusion workshop they attended. The information collected will be used to improve future workshop content and facilitation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
- Select the correct Workshop Attended option for the workshop you attended.
- Enter the Session Date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- Choose the facilitator from the list provided or type "Other" if they are not listed.
- Briefly describe what you learned during the workshop in the text area.
- Select the Session Length of the workshop.
- Indicate your level of satisfaction with the workshop by selecting one of the options provided.
- Provide any recommendations for future workshop topics.
- Select the level of value you perceive the workshop to be.
- Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Attended** (`workshop_attended`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of workshop you attended from the options provided.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the workshop you attended in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Who was your workshop facilitator?** (`workshop_facilitator`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the facilitator who led the workshop from the list provided, or select "Other" if they are not listed.
* **What did you learn during the workshop?** (`workshop_content`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of what you learned during the workshop.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, `number`, required/optional): Select the length of the workshop you attended.
* **How satisfied are you with the workshop?** (`workshop_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the workshop by selecting one of the options provided.
* **Do you have any recommendations for future workshop topics?** (`recommendations`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any suggestions or recommendations for future workshop topics.
* **How valuable do you think this workshop was?** (`workshop_value`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of value you perceive the workshop to be.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you submit one response per page per participant.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
