# Facility Upgrade Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Upgrade Voting Form is designed to collect opinions and preferences from employees or stakeholders on which facility upgrades they would like to see implemented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully, as each page contains questions related to facility upgrades.
2. Answer each question on the page as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
3. You can skip questions that are not relevant to you or your preferences.
4. Once you have completed all the pages, review your answers before submitting the form.
