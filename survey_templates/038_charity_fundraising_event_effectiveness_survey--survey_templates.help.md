# Charity Fundraising Event Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to gather data on the effectiveness of our charity's fundraising events. Your responses will help us understand what worked well, what didn't, and how we can improve future events to better serve our mission and community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully to provide honest and accurate feedback.
2. Answer each question based on your personal experience with the event.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields, such as "Key Success Factors" and "Main Challenges Faced."
4. Save your progress and return to the survey at a later time if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (1): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address for follow-up contact or to receive future event invitations.
* **Your Role** (3):
	+ Event organizer: Person responsible for planning and executing the event.
	+ Staff member: Someone working behind the scenes to support the event.
	+ Volunteer: Individual who contributed their time and skills to the event.
	+ Donor: Someone who donated funds or resources to the event.
	+ Attendee: Person who attended the event.
	+ Sponsor: Organization or individual that provided financial support.
	+ Other: Select this option if your role doesn't fit the above categories.
* **Event Name** (4): Enter the name of the event you participated in.
* **Event Date** (5): Enter the date the event was held.
* **Fundraising Goal** (6): Enter the target amount of funds raised.
* **Amount Actually Raised** (7): Enter the total amount of funds actually raised.
* **Was the Goal Met** (8):
	+ Exceeded significantly: The goal was far exceeded.
	+ Exceeded slightly: The goal was slightly exceeded.
	+ Met exactly: The goal was met exactly.
	+ Fell short slightly: The goal was slightly missed.
	+ Fell short significantly: The goal was significantly missed.
* **Total Event Expenses** (9): Enter the total costs associated with the event.
* **Net Proceeds** (10): Enter the amount of funds left over after expenses.
* **Return on Investment Rating** (11):
	+ Excellent ROI: The event was very profitable.
	+ Good ROI: The event was somewhat profitable.
	+ Average ROI: The event had an average return.
	+ Poor ROI: The event had a negative return.
	+ Very poor ROI: The event had a very negative return.
* **Attendee Satisfaction Level** (12):
	+ Very high: Event attendees were very satisfied.
	+ High: Event attendees were satisfied.
	+ Average: Event attendees were neutral.
	+ Low: Event attendees were dissatisfied.
	+ Very low: Event attendees were very dissatisfied.
* **Volunteer Engagement Level** (13):
	+ Very engaged: Volunteers were very committed.
	+ Engaged: Volunteers were committed.
	+ Neutral: Volunteers were neutral.
	+ Disengaged: Volunteers were not committed.
	+ Very disengaged: Volunteers were very uncommitted.
* **Most Effective Fundraising Strategy** (14):
	+ Ticket sales: Event ticket sales were the most effective strategy.
	+ Silent auction: Silent auction raised the most funds.
	+ Live auction: Live auction raised the most funds.
	+ Direct appeals: Direct appeals were the most effective.
	+ Sponsorships: Event sponsorships were the most effective.
	+ Raffle: Raffle was the most effective.
	+ Merchandise: Selling merchandise was the most effective.
	+ Other: Select this option if you have a different effective strategy.
* **Least Effective Strategy** (15):
	+ Ticket sales: Event ticket sales were the least effective.
	+ Silent auction: Silent auction was the least effective.
	+ Live auction: Live auction was the least effective.
	+ Direct appeals: Direct appeals were the least effective.
	+ Sponsorships: Event sponsorships were the least effective.
	+ Raffle: Raffle was the least effective.
	+ Merchandise: Selling merchandise was the least effective.
	+ Other: Select this option if you have a different least effective strategy.
* **Key Success Factors** (16): Enter the key factors that contributed to the event's success.
* **Main Challenges Faced** (17): Enter the main obstacles you encountered during the event.
* **Would You Repeat This Event** (18):
	+ Yes exactly the same: You would repeat the event exactly as it was.
	+ Yes with modifications: You would repeat the event with some changes.
	+ No try something else: You would not repeat the event.
* **Recommendations for Future Events** (19): Enter your suggestions for future events.
* **Overall Event Effectiveness** (20):
	+ 5 - Highly effective: The event was very effective.
	+ 4 - Effective: The event was somewhat effective.
	+ 3 - Moderately effective: The event was moderately effective.
	+ 2 - Slightly effective: The event was slightly effective.
	+ 1 - Not effective: The event was not effective at all.

## Tips

* Be as specific and honest as possible when answering each question.
* If you're unsure about a particular question, please provide the best answer you can.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Recommendations for Future Events" field.
* Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in helping us improve our events!
