# Sales Associate Certification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from sales associates about their certification, sales experience, and performance. The survey aims to collect data to improve sales associate development and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each section carefully and honestly, as your input will help us understand your needs and improve our training programs.
2. You can complete the form in any order, but please make sure to answer all questions in each section before moving on.
3. If you're unsure about any question or feel that it's not applicable to you, please provide feedback or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (1): Enter your name, job title, or any other relevant identification information.
	+ This will help us keep track of your responses and associate them with your certification.
* **Sales Experience** (2): Select the options that apply to your sales experience.
	+ This will help us understand your level of sales exposure and experience.
* **Certification Info** (3): Enter any relevant certification information or details.
	+ This will help us understand your certification status and any relevant certifications you've obtained.
* **Training Impact** (4): Enter the number of training sessions or hours you've attended in the past year.
	+ This will help us understand the impact of our training on your performance.
* **Skill Confidence** (5): Select the level of confidence you have in your sales skills.
	+ This will help us understand your self-assessment of your sales abilities.
* **Future Programs** (6): Enter any future training programs or courses you're planning to attend.
	+ This will help us understand your goals and needs for future development.
* **Overall Experience** (7): Enter a number between 1-5 to rate your overall sales experience in the past 6 months.
	+ This will help us understand your overall performance and satisfaction.
* **Feedback** (8): Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about our training programs or services.
	+ This will help us understand your thoughts and concerns.
* **Training Evaluation** (9, 10, 11): Select the length of time you've spent on each of the three training sessions.
	+ This will help us understand your engagement and effectiveness of our training programs.
* **Sales Performance** (12, 13, 14, 15): Select the level of performance you've demonstrated in each of the four areas.
	+ This will help us understand your performance and strengths.
* **Training Evaluation 4** (16): Select the length of time you've spent on the training session.
	+ This will help us understand your engagement and effectiveness of our training programs.
* **Training Performance** (17): Select the length of time you've spent on the training session.
	+ This will help us understand your performance and progress.
* **Sales Performance 5** (18): Select the level of performance you've demonstrated.
	+ This will help us understand your performance and strengths.

## Tips

* Make sure to save your progress and review your answers before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any question or field, don't hesitate to ask for help or clarification.
* Your feedback and input are valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation in this survey.
