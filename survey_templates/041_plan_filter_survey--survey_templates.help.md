# Plan Filter Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plan Filter Survey is a form designed to gather information from users about their plans, including plan types, benefits, budget, and schedule. This information will be used to filter and prioritize plans based on the user's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Plan Filter Survey, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user name and email address in the "User Name" and "User Email" fields, respectively.
2. Select the type of plan you are interested in from the "Plan Type" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the benefits you are interested in from the "Plan Benefit" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your plan's budget in the "Plan Budget" field.
5. Enter your schedule and start time in the "Plan Schedule" and "Plan Start Time" fields, respectively.
6. Enter any additional comments you have about your plan in the "User Comments" field.
7. Finally, select the duration of your plan in the "Plan Duration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to be associated with your plan.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to be contacted about your plan.
* **Plan Type** (`plan_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of plan you are interested in (e.g., Plan A, Plan B, Plan C).
* **Plan Benefit** (`plan_benefit`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the benefits of your plan (e.g., Benefit A, Benefit B, Benefit C).
* **Plan Budget** (`plan_budget`, text, optional): Enter your plan's budget.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted about your plan.
* **User Comments** (`user_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments you have about your plan.
* **Plan Schedule** (`plan_schedule`, date, optional): Enter your plan's schedule.
* **Plan Start Time** (`plan_start_time`, time, optional): Enter your plan's start time.
* **Plan Duration** (`plan_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of your plan.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing more information will help us better understand your plan and filter it accordingly.
