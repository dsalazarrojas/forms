# Snack Product Sensory Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a snack product from customers, helping the product development team to understand how the product is received and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the form with your honest and detailed feedback on the snack product.
- Answer each question as accurately as possible, using the options provided.
- For the open-ended comments field, write as much or as little as you would like, depending on what you think is relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Taste Test** (`taste_test`, text, optional): Describe your immediate taste experience when consuming the snack product.
* **Texture Test** (`texture_test`, text, optional): Describe your impression of the snack product's texture.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction level with the snack product on a scale (not specified in the form, but implied to be a 1-5 or 1-10 scale).
* **Favorite Taste** (`favorite_taste`, select_one, optional): Select your favorite taste of the snack product (Sweet, Salty, Savory).
* **Favorite Texture** (`favorite_texture`, select_one, optional): Select your favorite texture of the snack product (Crunchy, Smooth, Soft).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the snack product (Highly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Highly Dissatisfied).
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you would recommend the snack product (Yes, No, Undecided).
* **Open-Ended Comments** (`open_ended_comments`, note, optional): Share any additional thoughts, comments, or suggestions about the snack product.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (for contact purposes, if needed). 

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and accurately to provide valuable feedback.
- You can write as much or as little as you would like in the open-ended comments field.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, contact us at the email address you provided.
