# Office Cleanliness Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Office Cleanliness Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from employees about the state of their office workspace. This information is used to inform the facilities team's cleaning and maintenance practices, ensuring that the office environment remains clean and healthy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the options that best represent your experience with the office cleanliness.
3. Be specific when providing additional contact information if you'd like to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you clean your workspace** (`office_cleanliness`): Please rate how often you clean your workspace, on a scale of 0 (daily) to 5 (never). This will help the facilities team understand the frequency at which cleaning is performed in your area.
* **Which area of the office is the cleanest** (`work_area`): Select the area of the office that you think is the cleanest. This could be the Break Room, Kitchen, Meeting Room, or Other.
* **What's the level of cleanliness you'd rate** (`cleanliness_level`): Rate the overall level of cleanliness in the office, using the options 1 - Very Dirty, 2 - Somewhat Dirty, 3 - Neutral, 4 - Somewhat Clean, or 5 - Extremely Clean.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to tell us about the state of the office?** (`concerns`): Use this field to provide any additional comments or concerns you have about the office cleanliness.
* **Would you like to provide additional contact information?** (`contact_info`): If you'd like to be contacted by the facilities team, enter your email address here.
* **How often do you think the office cleanliness level changes?** (`feedback_frequency`): Select how often you think the office cleanliness level changes, using the options Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Never.
* **Which of the following is the main reason you rate the office cleanliness?** (`cleanliness_priority`): Select the main reason you rate the office cleanliness, using the options Trash and Recycling, Disinfecting and Sanitizing, Cleaning Equipment and Supplies, or Other.
