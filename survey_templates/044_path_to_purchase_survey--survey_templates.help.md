# Path To Purchase Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Path To Purchase Survey is designed to collect information about customers' buying habits, preferences, and demographic details. This form helps businesses understand their target audience and tailor their products or services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the general questions in the first section.
2. Select your demographic details, such as age range, gender, marital status, education level, and occupation.
3. Provide your contact information and indicate your purchase frequency.
4. Answer the questions about your path to purchase and product details.
5. Leave any additional feedback or comments in the customer feedback section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Questions** (General Questions, text, optional): This section is where you can provide any general information about yourself.
* **Demographics** (Demographics, select_one, optional): Please select your demographic details from the options provided, such as Yes or No for age range, gender, etc.
* **Location** (Location, text, optional): Enter your location or city.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, select_multiple, optional): Select your contact information options, such as Yes or No.
* **Purchase Frequency** (Purchase Frequency, time, optional): Select your purchase frequency, e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.
* **Path To Purchase** (Path To Purchase, select_one, optional): Please select Yes or No to indicate whether you follow a path to purchase.
* **Product Details** (Product Details, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the products you purchase.
* **Customer Feedback** (Customer Feedback, note, optional): Leave any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Purchase History** (Purchase History, select_multiple, optional): Select your purchase history options, such as Yes or No.
* **Age Range** (Age Range, select_multiple, optional): Select your age range, e.g., 18-25, 26-35, etc.
* **Gender** (Gender, select_one, optional): Please select your gender.
* **Marital Status** (Marital Status, select_one, optional): Select your marital status, e.g., Active or Inactive.
* **Income Range** (Income Range, select_multiple, optional): Select your income range options, e.g., Low, High, etc.
* **Education Level** (Education Level, select_one, optional): Please select your education level, e.g., High or Low.
* **Occupation** (Occupation, select_multiple, optional): Select your occupation options, e.g., Yes or No.
* **Purchase Frequency Again** (Purchase Frequency Again, select_multiple, optional): Select your purchase frequency options, e.g., Yes or No.
* **Path to Purchases** (Path to Purchases, select_multiple, optional): Select your path to purchases options, e.g., Yes or No.
* **Customer Type** (Customer Type, select_multiple, optional): Select your customer type options, e.g., Yes or No.

Note: Please answer each question according to your best knowledge and understanding. If you're unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.
