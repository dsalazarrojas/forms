# pre-conference-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-conference survey is a form designed to gather information from attendees to help us tailor the conference experience to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in the required fields (none required in this form) and providing as much detail as you can in the optional fields.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and complete information.
- Once you've filled out the entire form, click submit to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **attendee-expectations** (`attendee_expectations`, text, optional): Briefly describe your expectations from the conference, such as learning outcomes or specific goals you hope to achieve.
    - Please provide a clear and concise statement of what you're hoping to get out of the conference.
    - This will help us understand your needs and provide more relevant content and experiences.
- **prophets** (`general_feedback`, text, optional): Leave any general feedback or comments you have about the conference.
    - This is an open-ended field for any additional thoughts or comments you might have about the conference.
    - Your feedback will help us improve the conference experience.
- **conference-boosting** (`conference_boosting`, text, optional): If you're interested in boosting the conference (e.g., sponsoring, promoting), provide your name and contact information.
    - This will help us get in touch with you if there are opportunities for you to get involved.
    - Please provide a clear and accurate contact method.
- **session-schedule** (`session_schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the sessions you're interested in attending.
    - Select all the sessions that interest you from the provided options.
    - This will help us plan the schedule to meet your needs.
- **conference-goals** (`conference_goals`, select_one, optional): Choose the primary reason you're attending the conference.
    - Select the option that best describes your main reason for attending the conference.
    - This will help us tailor the conference experience to your goals.
