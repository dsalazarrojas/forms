# Academic Recognition Fairness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Recognition Fairness Survey is a feedback form designed to gather information on the perceived fairness of recognition processes within the institution. This survey will help the institution identify areas of improvement and make adjustments to ensure that recognition processes are fair and unbiased.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your primary role within the institution (Student, Faculty, Staff, or Administrator).
2. Rate the fairness of recognition processes (Very fair, Somewhat fair, Neutral, Somewhat unfair, or Very unfair).
3. Rate the diversity among awardees (Very diverse, Somewhat diverse, Not diverse).
4. Rate the transparency of the recognition process (Very transparent, Somewhat transparent, Not transparent).
5. Select any barriers you experience in receiving recognition (Lack of awareness, Bias or favoritism, Limited nomination channels, or Unequal resource distribution).
6. Suggest actions that would increase fairness (text input).
7. Share specific examples of unfair or fair experiences (text input).
8. Choose whether you'd like your response to be anonymous (True or False).
9. Provide any additional comments on recognition processes (text input).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent role** (Respondent role, select_one):
  Select your primary role within the institution.
* **Perception of fairness** (Perception of fairness in recognition, select_one):
  Rate the fairness of recognition processes.
* **Diversity among awardees** (Diversity among awardees, select_one):
  Rate the diversity of recipients.
* **Transparency of process** (Transparency of recognition process, select_one):
  Rate the transparency of the recognition process.
* **Barriers to recognition** (Barriers to receiving recognition, select_multiple):
  Select barriers you experience in receiving recognition.
* **Suggested changes** (Suggested changes to improve fairness, text):
  Suggest actions that would increase fairness.
* **Examples of unfair or fair experiences** (Examples of unfair or fair experiences, text):
  Share specific examples if comfortable.
* **Preference for anonymity** (Preference for anonymity, select_one):
  Choose whether you'd like your response to be anonymous.
* **Additional comments** (Additional comments, text):
  Provide any other feedback on recognition processes.
