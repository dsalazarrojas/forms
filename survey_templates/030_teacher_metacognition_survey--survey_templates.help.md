# Teacher Metacognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Metacognition Survey is designed to collect information from teachers about their metacognitive processes and experiences. This form is used to understand how teachers think and regulate their own thought processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully and ensure you provide accurate information.
2. For text fields, provide a clear and concise answer in the space provided.
3. For select_one fields, choose one option that best describes your situation.
4. For select_multiple fields, select all applicable options.
5. For number fields, enter a numerical value.
6. For date fields, select a date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
7. For email fields, enter a valid email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher Metacognition Survey 1** (`text`, required/optional): This is the first question of the survey. Please provide a brief answer to the question.
* **What do you do 1** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose one option that best describes your actions or habits related to metacognition.
* **Teacher Metacognition Survey 3** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all options that apply to your metacognitive experiences.
* **Teacher Metacognition Survey 4** (`number`, required/optional): Enter a numerical value to indicate the extent of your metacognitive processes.
* **How long did you spend** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the amount of time spent on metacognitive activities.
* **Teacher Metacognition Survey 6** (`date`, required/optional): Select a date to indicate when you last engaged in metacognitive activities.
* **What do you do 2** (`text`, required/optional): Provide a description of your actions or habits related to metacognition.
* **What do you do 3** (`text`, required/optional): Provide another description of your actions or habits related to metacognition.
* **Teacher Metacognition Survey 9** (`number`, required/optional): Enter a numerical value to indicate the extent of your metacognitive processes.
* **Teacher Metacognition Survey 10** (`email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to provide additional information or context.
