# Tripod Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your thoughts and feelings about your experience with the Tripod product. It helps us understand what we did well and what we can improve to make your experience even better. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time in filling out this survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the brief introduction.
2. Rate your experience with Tripod using the scale from 0 to 5.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them in the comment field.
4. If you want to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your email address.
5. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Intro Page** (`intro_page`, note, not required)
This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance. Please take a moment to read it carefully before starting.

### 2. **Rate Your Experience** (`rating_0_to_5`, number, not required)
Rate your experience with Tripod using the scale from 0 to 5. Think about your overall satisfaction with the product and services.

### 3. **Additional Comments** (`comment`, text, not required)
If you have any additional comments or suggestions about your experience with Tripod, please share them here. This is your chance to provide more details about your thoughts and feelings.

### 4. **Email** (`email`, email, not required)
If you want to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up, please provide your email address. We'll be happy to reach out and talk more about your experience.

### 5. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required)
If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number. This is optional, and we'll only use this to follow up on your feedback. 

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* You can skip any question if you don't feel comfortable providing the information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time in filling out this survey.
