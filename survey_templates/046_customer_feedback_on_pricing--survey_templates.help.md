# Customer Feedback On Pricing - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects customer feedback on pricing strategies and product offerings.
## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, ensure you have the product or service you are providing feedback on handy. Take a few moments to think about your experience with the product or service, and answer each question honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **price_perception** (`price_perception`, select_one, required): This question asks whether you believe the product's price is reasonable. Select 'Yes' if you think the price is fair, and 'No' if you think it's not.
* **value_for_money** (`value_for_money`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you about the value you get from the product or service. Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **pricing_clarity** (`pricing_clarity`, text, required): Describe how clear the pricing information is for you. For example, was it easy to understand the pricing structure?
* **price_sensitivity** (`price_sensitivity`, select_one, required): This question asks about your sensitivity to price changes. Select 'Yes' if you'd be sensitive to price changes, and 'No' if not.
* **customer_experience** (`customer_experience`, select_multiple, required): This question asks about your overall experience with the product or service. Select all the options that apply.
* **pricing_clarity_2** (`pricing_clarity_2`, text, required): Similar to **pricing_clarity**, describe how clear the pricing information is for you.
* **value_for_money_2** (`value_for_money_2`, select_multiple, required): Similar to **value_for_money**, select all the options that apply to your experience with the product or service.
* **customer_satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required): How satisfied are you with your experience? Select 'Satisfied' or 'Dissatisfied'.
* **product_price** (`product_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the product or service you are providing feedback on.
* **product_value** (`product_value`, select_multiple, required): This question asks about the value you get from the product or service. Select all the options that apply.
* **time_of_day** (`time_of_day`, time, required): If you're providing feedback on a specific product or service, enter the time of day you interacted with it.
* **product_time** (`product_time`, select_multiple, required): This question asks about the time of day you interacted with the product or service. Select all the options that apply.
* **date_of_purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, date, required): Enter the date you purchased the product or service.
* **customer_service** (`customer_service`, note, required): If you have any comments or feedback about customer service, type them in here.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you or keep you informed about our products or services.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **customer_name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name so we can know who's providing the feedback.
* **customer_id** (`customer_id`, select_one, required): This question asks whether you identify with a specific customer group. Select 'Yes' if you do.
* **customer_id_2** (`customer_id_2`, select_one, required): Similar to **customer_id**, select 'Yes' if you identify with this group.
* **customer_id_3** (`customer_id_3`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you identify with this group.
* **customer_id_4** (`customer_id_4`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you identify with this group.
* **customer_id_5** (`customer_id_5`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you identify with this group.
* **customer_id_6** (`customer_id_6`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you identify with this group.
* **customer_id_7** (`customer_id_7`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you identify with this group.
* **customer_id_8** (`customer_id_8`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you identify with this group.
* **product_name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product or service you are providing feedback on.
* **product_name_2** (`product_name_2`, text, required): Enter another product name if required.
* **product_name_3** (`product_name_3`, text, required): Enter yet another product name if needed.

## Tips
Take your time, and answer each question honestly. The more detailed and specific your feedback, the more useful it will be for us to improve our products or services. If you have any comments or questions, feel free to contact us.
