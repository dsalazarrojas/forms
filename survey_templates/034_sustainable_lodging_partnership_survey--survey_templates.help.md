# Sustainable Lodging Partnership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Sustainable Lodging Partnership Survey form. Please read through each section carefully to ensure you complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This is an optional introduction note for the partnership. You may use this field to provide any additional information about your partnership.
* **Sustainability Practices** (`sustainability_practices`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the following options to describe your sustainable practices:
	+ Sustainable Tourism: Emphasis on minimizing the impact of tourism on the environment and local communities.
	+ Ecotourism: Focus on promoting nature-based tourism experiences.
	+ Responsible Tourism: Prioritize fair labor practices and respect for local cultures.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all certifications that apply to your partnership:
	+ GOTS (Global Organic Tourism Standards)
	+ IOTC (International Organization for Tourism Certification)
	+ ISO 20118
	+ ISO 14001
	+ ISO 22000
* **Partnership Interests** (`partnership_interests`, select_one, required: true): Choose one of the following options to describe your partnership interests:
	+ Marketing
	+ Sales
	+ Training
	+ Other
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required: true): Choose one of the following options to describe your accommodation type:
	+ Hotel
	+ Resort
	+ Hostel
	+ Other
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to describe your location.
* **Sustainability Certification** (`sustainability_certification`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all certifications that apply to your sustainable practices:
	+ GOTS (Global Organic Tourism Standards)
	+ IOTC (International Organization for Tourism Certification)
* **Sustainability Practices** (`sustainability_practices`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all sustainability practices your partnership has implemented:
	+ Sustainable Tourism
	+ Responsible Tourism
	+ Ecotourism
	+ Conservation
* **Sustainability Rating** (`sustainability_rating`, number, required: false): You may rate your sustainability practices out of 100.
* **Year Established** (`year_established`, number, required: false): This is an optional field for you to describe the year your partnership was established.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_one, required: true): Choose one of the following options to describe your contact person:
	+ CEO
	+ Sustainability Officer
	+ Manager
	+ Other
* **Email** (`email`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide your phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide your website URL.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to describe your address.
* **Sustainability Focus Areas** (`sustainability_focus_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all sustainability focus areas your partnership has:
	+ Environmental Impact
	+ Community Engagement
	+ Conservation
	+ Biodiversity
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, required: false): This is an optional field for you to describe the date of your certification.
* **Partnership Status** (`partnership_status`, select_one, required: true): Choose one of the following options to describe your partnership status:
	+ Ongoing Partnership
	+ Inactive Partnership
* **Last Evaluation** (`last_evaluation`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options to describe the last time you were evaluated:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Sustainability Focus Area Comments** (`sustainability_focus_area_comments`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional comments about your sustainability focus areas.
* **Certification Date Comment** (`certification_date_comment`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional comments about your certification date.
* **Partnership Since** (`partner_since`, date, required: false): This is an optional field for you to describe the date you started your partnership.
* **Partnership Status Comment** (`partnership_status_comment`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional comments about your partnership status.
* **Last Evaluation Comment** (`last_evaluation_comment`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional comments about your last evaluation.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional notes about your partnership.
