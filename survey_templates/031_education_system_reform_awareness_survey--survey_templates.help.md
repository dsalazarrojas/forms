# education_system_reform_awareness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations regarding their awareness and opinions on education system reforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the survey in the "Survey Title" field.
2. Select one or more categories from the dropdown menu that best describe the area where education system reform is needed.
3. Provide a short description of the proposed reform in the "Description" field.
4. Enter your contact number and email address in the corresponding fields.
5. Enter the date you are submitting the form.
6. Enter your name and email address in the "Submitter Name" and "Submitter Email" fields.
7. Optionally, provide any additional comments about the reform proposal in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Title** (text, required): A brief title for the survey.
- **Category** (select multiple, required): Select one or more categories from the dropdown menu that best describe the area where education system reform is needed.
- **Description** (text, not required): A short description of the proposed reform.
- **Contact Number** (text, required): Enter your contact number.
- **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter your contact email address.
- **Date of Submission** (date, required): Enter the date you are submitting the form.
- **Submitter Name** (text, required): Enter your name.
- **Reform Area** (select one, required): Select the area where education system reform is needed.
- **Comments** (text, not required): Optionally, provide any additional comments about the reform proposal.
- **Submitter Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Check that you have not filled out this form before.
