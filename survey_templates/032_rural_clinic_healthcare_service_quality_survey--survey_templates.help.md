# rural_clinic_healthcare_service_quality_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the quality of services provided at the rural clinic. Your input will help us improve our services and better serve our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your recent experience with the clinic.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your answers, as this will help us understand your thoughts and feelings.
3. If a question has a rating scale, please use the given numbers to rate your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your overall impression of your recent appointment?** (`introduction`, `text`, optional)
	+ This is an open-ended question where you can share your general feeling about your recent visit.
* **How would you rate the Doctor?** (`doctor`, `number`, optional)
	+ Rate the Doctor's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How would you rate the Nurse?** (`nurse`, `number`, optional)
	+ Rate the Nurse's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How long did you wait in the waiting room?** (`waiting_time`, `number`, optional)
	+ Please enter the actual time you waited in the waiting room.
* **How would you rate the staff?** (`staff`, `number`, optional)
	+ Rate the staff's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How would you rate the facilities?** (`facilities`, `number`, optional)
	+ Rate the facilities' performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **What time did you arrive for your appointment?** (`appointment_start_time`, `time`, optional)
	+ Enter the actual time you arrived for your appointment.
* **What time did your appointment end?** (`appointment_end_time`, `time`, optional)
	+ Enter the actual time your appointment ended.
* **How would you rate the communication with your Doctor?** (`communication`, `text`, optional)
	+ Share your thoughts about the communication with your Doctor.
* **Were pain medications prescribed?** (`pain_medications`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Check the option that best describes your experience.
* **Did you receive lab results?** (`lab_results`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Check all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Do you have a follow up appointment?** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Check all the options that apply to your experience.
* **How do you think you would rate the medication adherence of your Doctor or Nurse staff?** (`medication_adherence`, `number`, optional)
	+ Rate the medication adherence on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How long did your appointment last?** (`appointment_length`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter the actual length of your appointment.
* **How would you rate the schedule efficiency?** (`appointment_schedule`, `number`, optional)
	+ Rate the schedule efficiency on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Was the Doctor present during your appointment?** (`doctor_attendance`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Check the option that best describes your experience.
* **Was the Nurse present during your appointment?** (`nurse_attendance`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Check the option that best describes your experience.
* **When did you receive lab results?** (`lab_results_received`, `date`, optional)
	+ Enter the actual date you received lab results.
* **What type of appointment was this?** (`appointment_type`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Check the option that best describes your appointment type.
* **What was the Doctor's specialty?** (`doctor_specialty`, `text`, optional)
	+ Share your understanding of the Doctor's specialty.
* **How satisfied were you with the service?** (`patient_satisfaction`, `text`, optional)
	+ Share your feelings about the service.
* **Any comments or suggestions?** (`comments`, `text`, optional)
	+ Share any thoughts or ideas you have about the clinic's services.
* **Overall rating** (`rating`, `number`, optional)
	+ Rate your overall experience on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Rating scale** (`rating_scale`, `text`, optional)
	+ Share the scale you used to rate your experience.
* **Why did you rate the service this way?** (`rating_reason`, `text`, optional)
	+ Share your reasoning behind your rating.
