# RTSM System Design Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The RTSM System Design Experience Survey is designed to collect feedback and experience from users who have used the RWS system. Your participation in this survey will help us identify areas of the system that require improvement and provide a better experience for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the appropriate response options for each field, and if a field is not applicable to your experience, feel free to leave it blank.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your answers, as this will help us provide a more accurate picture of your experience.
3. If you need help with a field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the title of your project?** (Required: false)
	* This field is where you can enter the title of your project. Enter the name of the project you are providing feedback on.
- **Project Manager's Name** (Required: false)
	* This field is for entering the name of the project manager who oversaw the project.
- **Describe your project** (Required: false)
	* Please enter a brief description of your project, including any relevant details that might be helpful for us to know.
- **How satisfied are you with the RWS system?** (Required: false)
	* Select one or more of the following options:
		+ Very Satisfied
		+ Somewhat Satisfied
		+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
		+ Very Dissatisfied
- **What are some challenges you faced during the project?** (Required: false)
	* Please enter any challenges you faced during the project, and any suggestions you might have for improvement.
- **What are some ideas for improvement?** (Required: false)
	* Enter any ideas you have for improving the RWS system, such as features or functionality you would like to see added.
- **Date of Project** (Required: false)
	* Enter the date of your project. This will help us understand the context of your experience.
- **Time of Project** (Required: false)
	* Enter the time of your project. This will help us understand the timeline of your experience.
- **Email** (Required: false)
	* Enter your email address, so we can contact you if we need more information or clarification on your feedback.
- **Phone** (Required: false)
	* Enter your phone number, so we can contact you if we need more information or clarification on your feedback.
- **Project Team** (Required: false)
	* Select the team that worked on your project:
		+ Team A
		+ Team B
		+ Team C
- **Project Phase** (Required: false)
	* Select the phase of your project:
		+ Phase 1
		+ Phase 2
		+ Phase 3
		+ Phase 4
		+ Phase 5
- **Has the RWS system been used?** (Required: false)
	* Select one of the following options:
		+ Yes
		+ No
- **How often do you use the RWS system?** (Required: false)
	* Select one of the following options:
		+ Daily
		+ Weekly
		+ Monthly
		+ Rarely
- **How valuable is the RWS system?** (Required: false)
	* Select one of the following options:
		+ Very Valuable
		+ Somewhat Valuable
		+ Neutral
		+ Somewhat Not Valuable
		+ Not Very Valuable
