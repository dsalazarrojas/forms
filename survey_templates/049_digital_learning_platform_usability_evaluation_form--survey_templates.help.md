# digital_learning_platform_usability_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the digital learning platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Answer the questions on the User Experience page to provide detailed feedback.
3. Enter your email address on the Feedback page to allow us to contact you for further discussion.
4. Click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`page 1`, `note`, not required):
	+ This page provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
	+ You can read it to understand what we are trying to accomplish with this form.
* **User Experience** (`page 2`, `text`, not required):
	+ Please share your thoughts about how you navigated the platform.
	+ What did you like or dislike about it?
	+ What features or functionalities would you like to see added or improved?
* **Feedback** (`page 3`, `email`, not required):
	+ Enter your email address if you would like us to follow up with you about your feedback.
	+ If you have any further questions or concerns, this is the best way for us to get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you have any specific examples or screenshots of the platform, please attach them to your email.
* If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, please use the provided email address to get in touch with us.
