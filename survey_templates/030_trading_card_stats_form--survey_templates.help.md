# Trading Card Stats Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Trading Card Stats Form is a tool to collect and manage information about your trading cards. Fill out this form to record details about your cards, such as rarity, condition, and price, to help you and others keep track of your collection.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field to identify yourself as the owner of the card.
2. Provide your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field, if desired.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field to allow others to contact you about trade or sale opportunities.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field, if you wish to be contacted directly.
5. Enter the **Card ID** number in the "Card ID" field, which might be a unique identifier assigned to the card.
6. Select the **Rarity** of the card from the options: Common, Rare, Very Rare, Legendary, or Mythical.
7. Enter the **Set ID** number, which might be a unique identifier for the set the card belongs to.
8. Select the **Condition** of the card from the options: Perfect, Near Perfect, Fair, or Poor.
9. Enter the **Price** at which you are willing to trade or sell the card.
10. Enter the **Card Name** to identify the card's name.
11. Enter the **Set Name** to identify the set the card belongs to.
12. Select the **Year** of the card from the options: 2005, 2006, 2007, 2008, or 2009.
13. Select the **Month** the card was released in from the options: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, or Dec.
14. Enter the **Card Number** of the card, if applicable.
15. Enter the **Image URL** where the card's image can be found.
16. Add any **Notes** about the card, such as its history or special features.
17. Select who **Submitted By** from the options: Hobbyist, Shop, or Club.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name to identify yourself as the owner of the card.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name, if desired.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address to allow others to contact you about trade or sale opportunities.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to be contacted directly, if you wish.
* **Card ID** (`card_id`, text, required: false): Enter the unique ID number assigned to the card.
* **Rarity** (`rarity`, select_one, required: false): Select the card's rarity from the options: Common, Rare, Very Rare, Legendary, or Mythical.
* **Set ID** (`set_id`, text, required: false): Enter the unique ID number of the set the card belongs to.
* **Condition** (`condition`, select_one, required: false): Select the condition of the card from the options: Perfect, Near Perfect, Fair, or Poor.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required: false): Enter the price at which you are willing to trade or sell the card.
* **Card Name** (`card_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the card to identify it.
* **Set Name** (`set_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the set the card belongs to.
* **Year** (`set_year`, select_one, required: false): Select the year the card was released from the options: 2005, 2006, 2007, 2008, or 2009.
* **Month** (`set_month`, select_one, required: false): Select the month the card was released from the options: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, or Dec.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, text, required: false): Enter the card's number, if applicable.
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the card's image.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the card.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required: false): Select who submitted the card from the options: Hobbyist, Shop, or Club.
