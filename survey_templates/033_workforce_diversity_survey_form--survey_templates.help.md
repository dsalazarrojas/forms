# Workforce Diversity Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect demographic information about the workforce to help the organization better understand and address diversity, equity, and inclusion issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the required information for each field. You can select multiple options for fields that allow multiple selections. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Demographic Information (Page 1)
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is intended to capture any other demographic information that is not included in the other fields. Please provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your demographic profile.
### Job Title (Page 1)
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional):
	+ This field is intended to capture the title of your job, if applicable. Please enter your job title as it appears on your official job title document.
### Age (Page 1)
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional):
	+ This field is intended to capture your age. Please enter your age in years.
### Race (Page 2)
* **Race** (`race`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field is intended to capture your racial identity. Please select one of the options provided.
### Ethnicity (Page 2)
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field is intended to capture your ethnicity. Please select all that apply from the options provided.
### Gender (Page 3)
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field is intended to capture your gender identity. Please select one of the options provided.
### Disability Status (Page 3)
* **Disability Status** (`disability_status`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field is intended to capture any disabilities or conditions that you have. Please select all that apply from the options provided.
### Language (Page 4)
* **Language** (`language`, text, optional):
	+ This field is intended to capture the languages you speak. Please list all languages you speak.
### Job Performance (Page 5)
* **Job Performance** (`job_performance`, time, optional):
	+ This field is intended to capture your job performance. Please provide a time period.
### Work-Life Balance (Page 6)
* **Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, date, optional):
	+ This field is intended to capture your work-life balance. Please provide a date when you last felt balanced.
