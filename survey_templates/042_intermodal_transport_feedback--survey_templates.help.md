# Intermodal Transport Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users who have experienced an intermodal transport experience. It will help us understand their satisfaction with the service, identify areas for improvement, and gather information about their journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed and submit it. You can select one or more options for each field, depending on your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Transfer Number
* **Transfer Number** (`transfer_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique number assigned to your transfer, if you know it.

### Transfer Date
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you used the transport service.

### Overall Rating
* **Overall Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience, from 1 to 5 stars.

### Why did you rate your experience this way?
* **Why did you rate your experience this way?** (`rating_reason`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reasons why you gave your experience this rating, from the options: Good - on time and convenient, Good - but some issues with timing, Bad - too much delay, Bad - other.

### Comments on transfer
* **Comments on transfer** (`comment`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_id`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, if you'd like to be contacted back about your feedback.

### Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name, if you'd like to be contacted back about your feedback.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted back about your feedback.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted back about your feedback.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted back about your feedback.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share any additional feedback or comments about your experience.

### Why did you not have a positive experience?
* **Why did you not have a positive experience?** (`feedback_reason`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reasons why you didn't have a positive experience, from the options: Lack of information, Bad communication, Bad service, Bad service staff, Unhelpful staff, Other.

### Transfer time
* **Transfer time** (`transfer_time`, number, optional): Enter the time it took for your transfer.

### Transfer type
* **Transfer type** (`transfer_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of transfer you experienced, from the options: Bus, Train, Ferry, Other.

### Travel mode
* **Travel mode** (`mode`, select_one, optional): Select the mode of transport you used, from the options: Car, Train, Bus, Bike, Walk, Taxi, Other.

### Mode 2
* **Mode 2** (`mode_2`, select_one, optional): Select the second mode of transport, from the options: Car, Train, Bus, Bike, Walk, Taxi, Other.

### Route information
* **Route information** (`route`, select_one, optional): Select the type of route you used, from the options: Bus, Train, Ferry, Other.

### Route id
* **Route id** (`route_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique id of the route, if you know it.

### Route name
* **Route name** (`route_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the route, if you know it.

### Origin
* **Origin** (`origin`, select_one, optional): Select the origin of your transfer, from the options: Bus, Train, Ferry, Other.

### Destination
* **Destination** (`destination`, select_one, optional): Select the destination of your transfer, from the options: Bus, Train, Ferry, Other.

### Mode type
* **Mode type** (`mode_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the modes of transport you used, from the options: Bus, Train, Ferry, Other.

### Transfer Time 2
* **Transfer Time 2** (`transfer_time_2`, number, optional): Enter the transfer time for your second transfer.

### Route type
* **Route type** (`route_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of route you used, from the options: Direct, Indirect.

### Transfer Number (2)
* **Transfer Number (2)** (`transfer_number`, number, optional): Enter the unique number assigned to your second transfer, if you know it.

### Transfer Date 2
* **Transfer Date 2** (`transfer_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of your second transfer, if you know it.

Note: Please only fill out the fields that are relevant to your experience. You can skip any fields that don't apply to you.
