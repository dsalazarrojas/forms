# Product Launch Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers after a product launch. The survey aims to gather information about customers' satisfaction and preferences regarding the product, its launch date, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, which includes:
	* Product launch date
	* Your contact number
	* Email
2. Answer the survey questions by selecting the relevant options from the drop-down menus.
3. Provide any additional feedback in the text field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Launch Survey** (Text): Enter a brief description of the product launch, such as "New Product X was launched on January 1st, 2024".
* **Product Launch Feedback** (Text): Share your thoughts and feedback about the product launch.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address to receive any follow-up information or feedback.
* **Contact Number** (Text): Enter your contact number for any communication.
* **Product Launch Date** (Date): Select the date when the product was launched.
* **Time of Day** (Time): Select the time of day when you experienced the product launch.
* **Select Time of Day** (Select One): Choose the correct time of day based on the options provided.
* **Satisfaction** (Select One): Choose how satisfied you are with the product launch, from "Not Satisfied" to "Very Satisfied".
* **Feedback** (Text): Share any additional feedback or comments about the product launch.
* **Product Launch Feedback Survey 1-16** (Select Multiple): Choose the relevant options that best describe your experience with the product launch.

Note: Please answer the multiple-choice questions based on your honest experience and preferences. If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us via email.
