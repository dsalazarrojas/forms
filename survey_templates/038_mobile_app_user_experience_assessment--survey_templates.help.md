# Mobile App User Experience Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile App User Experience Assessment form is designed to gather feedback from users on various aspects of a mobile app. This feedback will help improve the app's design and functionality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your honest and accurate responses. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Main** (`main`, `text`, not required)
The main field is a free-form text field where you can provide any general feedback about the app.

### 2. **User Usability** (`usability`, `text`, not required)
In this field, describe your experience with the app's usability. For example, how easy or difficult was it to navigate the app?

### 3. **User Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `text`, not required)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the app. For example, how likely are you to recommend the app to others?

### 4. **Feature Needs** (`features`, `text`, not required)
Suggest any new features you would like to see in the app or any features that are missing.

### 5. **User Demographics** (`demographics`, `text`, not required)
Provide some basic information about yourself, such as your age, location, or occupation, so we can better understand your perspective.

### 6. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required)
If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use this field to share them with us.

### 7. **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required)
Provide your contact information, such as your email or phone number, so we can follow up with you if needed.

### 8. **App Version** (`app_version`, `number`, not required)
If you know the version of the app you are using, please enter it here.

### 9. **Operating System** (`operating_system`, `select_one`, not required)
Select the operating system you are using to use the app (e.g., iOS, Android, etc.).

### 10. **Phone Model** (`phone_model`, `text`, not required)
If you have a phone, describe your phone model.

### 11. **E-mail** (`email`, `email`, not required)
Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

### 12. **Mobile Number** (`mobile_number`, `text`, not required)
Enter your mobile number so we can contact you if needed.
