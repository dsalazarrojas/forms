# Manufacturing Consent Reading Comprehension Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about participants in a manufacturing consent reading comprehension questionnaire.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all relevant class participation options that apply to you.
2. Choose the reading groups you have completed.
3. Indicate whether you have received training sessions or not.
4. Write a short note to describe your understanding of the training content.
5. If applicable, enter the number of hours you have spent on manufacturing consent training.
6. Enter the date of your most recent training session.
7. Enter the duration of each class you have attended.
8. Select your preferred time of day for training sessions.
9. If applicable, enter your contact information.
10. Enter your name, age, gender, education, and participant notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Class Participation** (`class_participation`, `select_multiple`): Select all relevant class participation options that apply to you.
* **Reading Groups** (`reading_groups`, `select_multiple`): Choose the reading groups you have completed.
* **Training Sessions** (`training_sessions`, `select_one`): Indicate whether you have received training sessions or not.
* **Participant Understanding** (`participant_understanding`, `note`): Write a short note to describe your understanding of the training content.
* **Manufacturing Consent** (`manufacturing_consent`, `number`): Enter the number of hours you have spent on manufacturing consent training. (If not applicable, leave blank.)
* **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`, `number`): Enter the number of hours you have spent on manufacturing consent training. (If not applicable, leave blank.)
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, `number`): Enter the number of hours you have spent on manufacturing consent training. (If not applicable, leave blank.)
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, `date`): Enter the date of your most recent training session.
* **Class Duration** (`class_duration`, `time`): Enter the duration of each class you have attended. (If not applicable, leave blank.)
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`): Enter the start time of your next training session. (If not applicable, leave blank.)
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`): Enter the end time of your next training session. (If not applicable, leave blank.)
* **Class Duration** (`class_duration`, `time`): Enter the duration of each class you have attended. (This field is identical to the one above. If not applicable, leave blank.)
* **Training Schedule** (`training_schedule`, `select_multiple`): Select your preferred time of day for training sessions.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`): If applicable, enter your contact number. (If not applicable, leave blank.)
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`): If applicable, enter your contact address. (If not applicable, leave blank.)
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`): Enter your name.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, `text`): Enter your age.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, `text`): Enter your gender.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`): If applicable, enter your participant phone number. (If not applicable, leave blank.)
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`): If applicable, enter your participant email. (If not applicable, leave blank.)
* **Participant Education** (`participant_education`, `text`): Enter your education level.
* **Participant Work Experience** (`participant_work_experience`, `text`): If applicable, enter your work experience. (If not applicable, leave blank.)
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`): Enter any additional notes about yourself. (This field is optional.)
