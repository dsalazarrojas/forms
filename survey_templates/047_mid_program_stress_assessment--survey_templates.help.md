**Mid-Program Stress Assessment - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the level of stress experienced by individuals or teams during a mid-program review. It helps to identify potential stress factors and their impact on overall performance. Completing this form ensures that we can better understand program stressors and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page, which provides general information about the form and its purpose.
2. Answer the questions on the subsequent pages, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select all applicable options for each question, as required.
4. Enter any additional comments or suggestions in the last section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1): This page provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose. You can read it once to understand the context.
* **Current Stress Level** (2): Please answer with a brief statement describing your current stress level. This can be written in a few words or short paragraph.
* **Stressors** (3): Select all stressors that you are currently experiencing. These are factors that contribute to your stress, such as workload, deadlines, or lack of resources.
	+ This field is required. Select all that apply.
* **Impact on Stressors** (4): Choose the option that best describes how the selected stressors are affecting your work. Options include "Significant", "Some", or "Not at all".
	+ This field is required.
* **Current Support** (5): Check all support options that you currently have in place to manage your stress. This can include colleagues, mentors, or training programs.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Suggestions for Support** (6): If you have any suggestions for additional support or resources that could help alleviate stress, check the relevant options. This can include training programs, equipment, or personnel.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Overall Experience** (7): Rate your overall experience with a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "very difficult" and 10 is "very easy".
	+ This field is required.
* **Comments** (8): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your stress experience or any ideas for improvement. This field is optional.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* Select all applicable options when choosing stressors or current support.
* Remember that this form is a snapshot of your current stress experience, so provide accurate and honest answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
