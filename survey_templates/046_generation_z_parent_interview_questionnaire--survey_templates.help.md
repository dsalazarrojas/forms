# Generation Z Parent Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Generation Z Parent Interview Questionnaire is designed to gather information about the online habits and behaviors of parents of children born between 1997 and 2012. This form is intended to help understand how parents interact with technology, set limits on screen time, and monitor their child's online activities.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into different sections, each addressing a specific aspect of parental interaction with technology. To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please read each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Ensure you select the most accurate options for each question.
3. Fill out all the questions, but do not repeat any section unnecessarily.
4. If you have questions or doubts, refer to the field-by-field explanations for further clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Participation** (`parent_participation`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first question of the form. Please provide an open-ended answer about your participation in your child's online life.
* **Technology Usage** (`technology_usage`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks how often you let your child use technology. Select the option that best describes your behavior.
* **Online Safety** (`online_safety`, `text`, required/optional): Provide an open-ended answer about your approach to ensuring your child's online safety.
* **Screen Time** (`screen_time`, `number`, required/optional): Estimate the amount of time your child spends on screens each day.
* **Digital Habits** (`digital_habits`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your child's digital habits, such as gaming or social media usage.
* **Parental Controls** (`parental_controls`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks how often you use parental controls on your child's technology. Select the option that best describes your behavior.
* **Parental Involvement** (`parental_involvement`, `text`, required/optional): Provide an open-ended answer about how much you involve yourself in your child's online life.
* **Digital Habits (Parent)** (`digital_habits_parental`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your own digital habits, such as your social media usage or online shopping.
* **Technology Awareness** (`technology_awareness`, `text`, required/optional): This question is about your awareness of your child's online activities. Provide an open-ended answer about what you know and how you stay informed.
* **Screen Time Goals** (`screen_time_goals`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks about your goal for your child's screen time. Select the option that best describes what you aim for.
* **Digital Media** (`digital_media`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the types of digital media your child consumes, such as games, videos, or social media.
* **Technology Monitoring** (`technology_monitoring`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks about how you monitor your child's online activities. Provide an open-ended answer about your approach.
* **Digital Media (Parent)** (`digital_media_parents`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your own digital media usage, such as games or videos.
* **Screen Time Limits** (`screen_time_limits`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks about the limits you set for your child's screen time. Provide an open-ended answer about your approach.
* **Digital Media (Parental)** (`digital_media_parental`, `text`, required/optional): Describe how often you set limits on your own screen time.
* **Technology Usage (Parental)** (`technology_usage_parental`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks about your own technology usage. Provide an open-ended answer about your behavior.
* **Screen Time Goals (Parental)** (`screen_time_goals_parental`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks about your goal for your own screen time. Select the option that best describes what you aim for.
* **Digital Media (Parental Controls)** (`digital_media_parental_controls`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks about how often you use controls on your own digital media. Select the option that best describes your behavior.
* **Technology Usage (Parental Controls)** (`technology_usage_parental_controls`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks about how often you use controls on your own technology. Select the option that best describes your behavior.
* **Parent Participation (Parental)** (`parent_participation_parental`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks about your participation in your own online activities. Provide an open-ended answer about your involvement.
