# Volunteer Exit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information from departing volunteers about their experiences, reasons for leaving, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions in the form as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. Provide specific examples or explanations to help us understand your reasons for leaving.
3. Be as detailed as you can be in your answers, but keep your responses concise.
4. Don't hesitate to provide any suggestions or comments you have for how we can improve.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Why did you leave
* **Why did you leave** (text): Please explain why you are leaving the organization. This will help us understand the reasons behind your departure.
### Volunteer ID (text): Please enter your Volunteer ID as provided to you by the organization.
### Organization ID (text): Please enter your Organization ID as provided to you by the organization.
### Why did you choose to leave (text): This section is meant to help us understand why you decided to leave our organization.
### What will you do next (text): Please let us know what you plan to do after leaving our organization.
### Suggestions for Improvement (text): If you have any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve, please share them with us.
### How would you rate the level of communication with your supervisor (number): Rate your experience with communication with your supervisor on a scale of 1-5.
### How would you rate the level of support given to you (number): Rate your experience with support given to you on a scale of 1-5.
### How would you rate the level of engagement in your role (number): Rate your experience with engagement in your role on a scale of 1-5.
### Overall satisfaction (number): Rate your overall satisfaction with your experience in our organization on a scale of 1-5.
### How would you rate your relationship with your manager (number): Rate your experience with your relationship with your manager on a scale of 1-5.
### How would you rate your relationship with your team (number): Rate your experience with your team on a scale of 1-5.
### How would you rate the benefits you received (number): Rate your experience with the benefits you received on a scale of 1-5.
### Why did you choose this organization (text): This section is meant to help us understand why you chose to join our organization.
### What did you gain from your role (text): Please describe what you gained from your experience in our organization.
### Why did you stay (text): This section is meant to help us understand why you chose to stay with our organization.
### Why did you quit (text): Please explain why you chose to leave our organization.
### Overall comments (text): If you have any further comments or thoughts, please share them with us.
### Recommendations for improvement (text): If you have any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve, please share them with us.
### Final comments (text): If you have any final comments or thoughts, please share them with us.
### Email (email): Please provide your email address so we can follow up with you.
### Phone (text): Please provide your phone number so we can follow up with you.
### Manager name (text): If you had a manager during your time with our organization, please let us know their name.
### Manager title (text): If you had a manager during your time with our organization, please let us know their title.
### Manager email (email): If you had a manager during your time with our organization, please provide their email address.
