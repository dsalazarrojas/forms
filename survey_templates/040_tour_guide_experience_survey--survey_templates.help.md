# Tour Guide Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers who have taken a guided tour to help us understand their experience and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions according to your experience on the tour. Please provide your honest feedback, and make sure to fill out all the required fields (denoted by *). For each question, select only one option for select fields (e.g. "Tour Condition"), and select multiple options for select multiple fields (e.g. "Would you Recommend?").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Introduction (Text)**: Please provide a brief introduction to your experience on the tour. This will give us an idea of what to expect in your responses.
* **Rating for Tour Guide (Number)**: Rate the tour guide's performance, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Tour Rating (Number)**: Rate the tour itself, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Overall Rating (Number)**: Rate your overall experience on the tour, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Guide's Communication (Number)**: Rate the tour guide's communication, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Helpfulness of the Guide (Number)**: Rate the tour guide's helpfulness, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Guide's Knowledge Level (Number)**: Rate the tour guide's knowledge, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Punctuality of the Guide (Number)**: Rate the tour guide's punctuality, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Tour Condition (Select One)**: Select the overall condition of the tour, where "Excellent", "Good", or "Poor" are options.
* **Would you Recommend? (Select Multiple)**: Select all the reasons why you would or would not recommend the tour, where "True" or "False" are options.
* **Comments (Text)**: Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the tour.
* **Additional Comments (Text)**: Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the tour guide or the tour itself.
