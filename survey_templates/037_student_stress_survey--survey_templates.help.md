> # Student Stress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Stress Survey is designed to collect information about students' stress levels and areas of concern in various aspects of their lives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the questions and answers.
2. Answer each question honestly, selecting the options that best reflect your experiences and concerns.
3. For each question, select all that apply from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Intro** (Intro, note, required: false): This section is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
- **Student Information** (Student Information, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks whether you are experiencing any stress related to your student status. Please select all that apply.
- **What are you stressed about?** (What are you stressed about?, text, required: false): This open-ended question allows you to share any additional stressors not listed above.
- **Academic Performance** (Academic Performance, select_one, required: false): This question inquires about your level of stress related to academic performance. Select "Yes" if you are experiencing stress in this area.
- **Social Relationships** (Social Relationships, select_one, required: false): This question asks about stress related to your social relationships. Select "Yes" if you are experiencing stress in this area.
- **School Workload** (School Workload, select_one, required: false): This question inquires about stress related to your school workload. Select "Yes" if you are experiencing stress in this area.
- **Time Management** (Time Management, select_one, required: false): This question asks about stress related to time management. Select "Yes" if you are experiencing stress in this area.
- **Emotional Issues** (Emotional Issues, select_one, required: false): This question inquires about stress related to emotional issues. Select "Yes" if you are experiencing stress in this area.
- **Other** (Other, note, required: false): This section allows you to share any other stressors not listed above.
- **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, required: false): This is a text box where you can provide any additional comments or insights.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough when answering the questions.
- Select all that apply for each question.
- Use the space provided for Additional Comments to elaborate on any points you want to make.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant support services.
