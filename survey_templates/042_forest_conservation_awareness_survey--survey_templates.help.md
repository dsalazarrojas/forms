# Forest Conservation Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about forest conservation awareness, including users' knowledge, actions, and attitudes towards forest conservation. It also aims to gather details about the target audience, communication channel, campaign length, funding, location, target, goals, and start and end dates of forest conservation campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability, providing honest and accurate information.
2. Select the options that best match your answers.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.
4. Double-check the form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Forest conservation knowledge** (`forest_conversation_knowledge`, text, required/optional): This field is designed to gauge your understanding of forest conservation. Please answer this question with a brief description of your knowledge.
* **Conservation actions** (`conservation_actions`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check all the actions you have taken to support forest conservation:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Forest conservation attitude** (`forest_conservation_attitude`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your attitude towards forest conservation:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Forest conservation importance** (`forest_conversation_importance`, number, required/optional): Rate the importance of forest conservation:
	+ 1-10
* **Target audience** (`target_audience`, select_one, required/optional): Who is the target audience of the campaign:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Communication channel** (`communication_channel`, select_one, required/optional): What is the primary channel used for campaign communication:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Campaign length** (`campaign_length`, number, required/optional): How long will the campaign last:
	+ 1-30 days
* **Campaign funding** (`campaign_funding`, select_one, required/optional): Is the campaign funded:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Campaign location** (`campaign_location`, select_multiple, required/optional): Where is the campaign located:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Campaign target** (`campaign_target`, select_one, required/optional): What is the target of the campaign:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Forest conservation goals** (`forest_conversation_goals`, text, required/optional): Describe the goals of the campaign.
* **Campaign start date** (`campaign_start_date`, date, required/optional): When will the campaign start:
	+ dd/mm/yyyy
* **Campaign end date** (`campaign_end_date`, date, required/optional): When will the campaign end:
	+ dd/mm/yyyy
* **Campaign hours** (`campaign_hours`, time, required/optional): How many hours will the campaign last:
	+ 1-24 hours
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, required/optional): What is the budget for the campaign:
	+ $

**Tips**

* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to contact us for support.
* Double-check the form before submission to ensure you have not missed any fields.
