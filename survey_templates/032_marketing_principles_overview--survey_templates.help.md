# Marketing Principles Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about marketing principles and strategies from customers or stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the text input field and type your answer. You do not need to select a specific order or follow a specific sequence, as the form is designed to be flexible. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **1. introduction** (`introduction`, text, required/optional): This field is meant to provide an introduction to your marketing strategy or principles. You may describe the background or context of your marketing efforts.

- **2. marketing_strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, text, required/optional): In this field, describe the overall strategy behind your marketing efforts. What approach are you taking to reach your target audience?

- **3. target_market** (`target_market`, text, required/optional): Identify the specific market or audience you are targeting with your marketing efforts. Be as specific as possible.

- **4. competitive_advantage** (`competitive_advantage`, text, required/optional): Describe what sets your business or product apart from the competition. What unique value do you offer?

- **5. market_segment** (`market_segment`, text, required/optional): Describe the specific market or segment that you are targeting with your marketing efforts.

- **6. customer_journey** (`customer_journey`, text, required/optional): Outline the steps a customer takes when interacting with your business or product. What are the key touchpoints and interactions that you aim to have with your customers?

- **7. customer_painpoint** (`customer_painpoint`, text, required/optional): Identify the pain points or challenges that your customers face when interacting with your business or product. What are the areas where you can improve?

- **8. marketing_objectives** (`marketing_objectives`, text, required/optional): Describe the specific marketing objectives that you aim to achieve. What are your goals and targets?

- **9. marketing_message** (`marketing_message`, text, required/optional): Craft a message that effectively communicates your brand's value proposition to your target audience. What key messages do you want to convey?

- **10. market_tactics** (`market_tactics`, text, required/optional): Describe the tactics or strategies you are using to achieve your marketing objectives.

- **11. market_measures** (`market_measures`, text, required/optional): Outline the metrics or measures that you will use to gauge the success of your marketing efforts.

- **12. competitive_composition** (`competitive_composition`, text, required/optional): Analyze the competitive landscape and describe the strengths and weaknesses of your competitors.
