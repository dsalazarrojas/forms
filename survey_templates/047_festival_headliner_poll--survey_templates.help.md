# Festival Headliner Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Headliner Poll is a simple form designed to collect your thoughts and opinions about the festival headliners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Select your favorite headliner from the options provided. If you're unsure, you can choose "none of the above."
2. If you wish, select multiple headliners as your favorites.
3. Share your thoughts and feedback about the headliners in the provided text area.
4. Submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Headliner 1 (Select One)** (`headliner_1`, Select One, Optional): Choose one headliner as your favorite among the options.
* **Headliner 2 (Select Multiple)** (`headliner_2`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select multiple headliners as your favorites. You can choose one or all of the options.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Note, Optional): Provide your thoughts and feedback about the headliners. This is an optional field where you can share your opinions and comments.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your responses.
* Select only those headliners you genuinely prefer, not just because you think it's a popular option.
* Feel free to provide feedback, but remember that it's not a comprehensive evaluation tool.
* If you've already submitted feedback, please do not repeat your previous response.
