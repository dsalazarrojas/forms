# School Extracurricular Activities Parent Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from parents about their child's extracurricular activities at the school. Your input will help us understand the effectiveness of our programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into 8 sections, each of which corresponds to a specific field. Please read each section carefully and answer all the fields that apply to you. You can skip fields that are not relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Parent Information (<name>, <type>, required/optional: false)
Please provide some basic information about yourself as the parent.

### 2. Student Information (<name>, <type>, required/optional: false)
Please provide some basic information about your child who is participating in the extracurricular activity.

### 3. Activity Information (<name>, <type>, required/optional: false)
Please describe the extracurricular activity your child is participating in.

### 4. Feedback On Clubs (<label>, <type>, required/optional: false)
Please rate how well the club your child is participating in is meeting your expectations. You can choose from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### 5. Feedback On Programs (<label>, <type>, required/optional: false)
Please select all that apply to your experience with the programs provided by the extracurricular activities. You can choose from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### 6. Additional Comments (<name>, <type>, required/optional: false)
Is there anything else you would like to comment on regarding your child's extracurricular activity?

### 7. Email (<name>, <type>, required/optional: false)
Please enter a valid email address for communication purposes.

### 8. Phone Number (<name>, <type>, required/optional: false)
Please enter a valid phone number for communication purposes.

### 9. Time Preferences (<name>, <type>, required/optional: false)
Please indicate your preferred time for the extracurricular activity to take place. (Note: This field is typically used by staff to schedule activities, so feel free to provide any time that works best for you.)

### 10. Date Preferences (<name>, <type>, required/optional: false)
Please indicate your preferred date for the extracurricular activity to take place. (Note: This field is typically used by staff to schedule activities, so feel free to provide any date that works best for you.)
