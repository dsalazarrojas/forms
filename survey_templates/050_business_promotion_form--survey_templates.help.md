# Business Promotion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback and information to improve our business services and customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly to provide accurate feedback.
2. Ensure that you have visited the store location during the time period specified in the form (visit_date field).
3. Provide your contact information (customer_name, customer_email, customer_phone) so we can follow up with you.
4. Answer questions about your experience at the store, including how you found out about us, how often you visit, and what you think about our products and services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of your visit to the store.
* **Your Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Residential Address**: Enter your residential address if you would like to be contacted in the future. (Optional)
* **Store Location Visited**: Select the location you visited.
* **Is This Your First Visit?**: Answer yes if this is your first time visiting the store, no if you've visited before.
* **How Often Do You Visit?**: Select how often you visit the store.
* **How Did You Find Out About Us?**: Select how you found out about the store.
* **Store Location Rating**: Rate the store location out of 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Store Appearance Rating**: Rate the store appearance out of 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Product Variety**: Rate the product variety out of 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Product Quality Rating**: Rate the product quality out of 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Pricing Rating**: Rate the pricing out of 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Staff Courtesy Rating**: Rate the staff courtesy out of 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What Would You Like to Buy?**: Enter products you are interested in purchasing.
* **Products Purchased Today**: If you have made any purchases today, enter details about the products.
* **Interested in Future Promotions?**: Select yes if you would like to receive future promotions, no if not.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction out of 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: Enter any suggestions you have for improvement.
* **Would You Recommend to Others?**: Select yes if you would recommend the store to others, no if not.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other feedback or comments you would like to share.
