# seasonal_fashion_trends_customer_preferences_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer preferences on seasonal fashion trends. Your input will help us understand what customers are looking for in terms of clothing styles, categories, and price ranges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the page that best represents your current fashion preferences.
2. On each page, select your preferred clothing categories and styles.
3. Choose the seasonality that best describes your current fashion preferences.
4. If you have any notes or comments, please provide them at the end of the form.
5. Fill out the demographic information at the end of the form to help us better understand your profile.
