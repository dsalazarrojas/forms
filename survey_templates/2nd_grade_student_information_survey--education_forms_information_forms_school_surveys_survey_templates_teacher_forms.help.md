# 2nd Grade Student Information Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The 2nd Grade Student Information Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather essential information about 2nd-grade students, including demographic details, educational history, and family contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the sections and questions carefully before starting.
2. Enter the student's full name as it appears on school records.
3. Provide the student's date of birth, including the month and year.
4. If applicable, enter the student's school ID or identifier.
5. Select the student's grade level or class grouping.
6. Enter the student's school or institution name (if applicable).
7. Identify the student's classroom teacher.
8. Enter the parent or guardian's name.
9. Provide the parent or guardian's email address.
10. Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
11. If applicable, enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
12. If applicable, select all medical conditions or allergies that apply.
13. If applicable, list the student's current medications and dosing schedule.
14. Choose the student's preferred or observed learning style.
15. Select the primary language spoken at home.
16. Choose the level of internet access at home.
17. Select all devices available for schoolwork.
18. Estimate the student's average study or homework hours per week.
19. Enter the teacher's assessment of the student's reading level.
20. If applicable, indicate if the student has special education services in place.
21. Add any attendance considerations or schedule constraints.
22. Provide additional comments or important information about the student.

* **Full name**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on school records.

* **Parent / guardian email address**: Provide the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent / guardian phone number**: Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Emergency contact name**: If applicable, enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact phone**: If applicable, enter the phone number and relationship of the emergency contact.
* **Medical conditions or allergies**: If applicable, select all medical conditions or allergies that apply.
* **Current medications**: If applicable, list the student's current medications and dosing schedule.
* **Preferred learning style**: Choose the student's preferred or observed learning style.
* **Primary language spoken at home**: Select the primary language spoken at home.
* **Internet access at home**: Choose the level of internet access at home.
* **Devices available for schoolwork**: Select all devices available for schoolwork.
* **Average study / homework hours per week**: Estimate the student's average study or homework hours per week.
* **Reading level**: Enter the teacher's assessment of the student's reading level.
* **Special education services**: If applicable, indicate if the student has special education services in place.
* **Attendance considerations**: Add any attendance considerations or schedule constraints.
* **Additional comments**: Provide additional comments or important information about the student.
