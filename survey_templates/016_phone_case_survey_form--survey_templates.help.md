# Phone Case Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a collection of questions and suggestions to help us better understand your preferences and needs for phone cases.

## How To Complete This Form
- Simply follow the questions and answer them to the best of your ability.
- For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
- If you have any additional ideas or comments, please use the dedicated fields at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Are you currently using a phone case?** (`current_case_use`, `select_one`, optional)
  This question helps us understand if you're using a phone case currently.
- **What is your preferred phone case design?** (`preferred_design`, `select_multiple`, optional)
  Select all the designs you prefer, such as leather, rubber, plastic, or metal.
- **How much are you willing to pay for a phone case?** (`price_range`, `number`, optional)
  This question helps us understand your budget for a phone case.
- **How important is case quality to you?** (`case_quality`, `number`, optional)
  Rate the importance of case quality to you, from 1 to 5.
- **What case features do you want in a phone case?** (`case_functionalities`, `select_multiple`, optional)
  Select all the features you would like to see in a phone case, such as shockproof, waterproof, scratch resistant, or anti-bacterial.
- **What color do you prefer in a phone case?** (`case_color`, `select_one`, optional)
  Choose a color from the options provided.
- **What material do you prefer for a phone case?** (`case_material`, `select_one`, optional)
  Select a material from the options provided.
- **What size do you prefer in a phone case?** (`case_size`, `select_one`, optional)
  Choose a size from the options provided.

  Rate the importance of case protection to you, from 1 to 5.
- **What is your budget for a phone case?** (`case_price`, `number`, optional)
  Enter your budget for a phone case.
- **How satisfied are you with your current phone case?** (`case_satisfaction`, `number`, optional)
  Rate your satisfaction with your current phone case, from 1 to 5.
- **What do you think of case warranty?** (`case_warranty`, `select_one`, optional)
  Rate your opinion on case warranty, from "always", "sometimes", or "never".
- **Would you recommend a case to a friend?** (`case_recommend`, `select_one`, optional)
