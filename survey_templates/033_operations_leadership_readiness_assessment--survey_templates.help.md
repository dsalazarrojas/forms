# operations_leadership_readiness_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the readiness of operations leaders to take on new challenges and opportunities. The goal is to gauge the current state of their leadership skills and identify areas where they may need development or support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly. Your responses will be kept confidential and used to help inform training and development programs for operations leaders.
2. Answer the questions to the best of your ability. If you're unsure about any of the questions, don't hesitate to ask for clarification or support.
3. Select the options that best match your current situation and perspective.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Coaching Goals** (`coaching_goals`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ This question asks you to select one or more of the following options that best describe your current goals and objectives as an operations leader. Please choose the options that are most relevant to your situation.
* **Current Role** (`current_role`, `text`, **required**):
	+ This question asks you to describe your current role or position within the organization. Please provide a brief but accurate description of your job title, department, or team.
* **Operations Leadership Readiness Assessment** (`operations_leadership_readiness_assessment`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ This question asks for any additional comments or thoughts you may have about your current level of readiness as an operations leader. Please use this space to provide any relevant information or context that may not be covered in the other questions.
* **Operations Leadership Readiness Assessment Comments** (`operations_leadership_readiness_assessment_comments`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ This question asks for any additional comments or thoughts you may have about your current level of readiness as an operations leader. Please use this space to provide any relevant information or context that may not be covered in the other questions.

## Tips
* Take your time and answer the questions carefully. Your responses will help inform training and development programs for operations leaders.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, don't hesitate to ask for clarification or support.
* Remember to answer the questions based on your current situation and perspective as an operations leader.
