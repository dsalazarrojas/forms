# Cupping Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cupping Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from clients on the quality of service provided by our team. This helps us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Client Name in the first field.
2. Choose how you rate the quality of service in the second field.
3. Select how likely you are to recommend our service in the third field.
4. Enter your Email address in the fourth field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the fifth field.
6. Choose how you rate the value for money in the sixth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **How do you rate the quality of service** (`question1`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a rating for the quality of service provided:
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Fair
* **How likely are you to recommend** (`question2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply:
	+ Option 1: Likely
	+ Option 2: Unlikely
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address as it appears on your email account.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number as it appears on your phone bill or identification documents.
* **How do you rate the value for money** (`question3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a rating for the value for money:
	+ Option 1: Good
	+ Option 2: Fair
	+ Option 3: Poor
