# salon-appointment-hours-poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from salon customers about their preferred hours of operation and appointment scheduling preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **What are your preferred salon hours**:
	* Select a time frame that best fits your schedule from the options provided.
2. Select the day of the week you are available to schedule an appointment:
	* Select all the days of the week that apply to you.
3. Choose your preferred start time for your appointment:
	* Use the time input field to select a start time.
4. Choose your preferred end time for your appointment:
	* Use the time input field to select an end time.
5. Indicate how often you would like to schedule appointments:
	* Choose a frequency that best describes your needs.
6. Select your preferred appointment duration:
	* Choose a duration that best fits your needs.
7. Select your preferred language for the appointment:
	* Choose the language you are most comfortable with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What are your preferred salon hours**: This field is an open-ended text field where you can describe your preferred hours of operation for the salon.
- **Morning**: Select this option if you prefer to schedule appointments during the morning hours.
- **Afternoon**: Select this option if you prefer to schedule appointments during the afternoon hours.
- **Evening**: Select this option if you prefer to schedule appointments during the evening hours.
- **Monday**: Select this option if you are available to schedule an appointment on Monday.
- **Tuesday**: Select this option if you are available to schedule an appointment on Tuesday.
- **Wednesday**: Select this option if you are available to schedule an appointment on Wednesday.
- **Thursday**: Select this option if you are available to schedule an appointment on Thursday.
- **Friday**: Select this option if you are available to schedule an appointment on Friday.
- **Saturday**: Select this option if you are available to schedule an appointment on Saturday.
- **Sunday**: Select this option if you are available to schedule an appointment on Sunday.
- **Preferred start time**: This is a time input field where you can select a start time for your appointment.
- **Preferred end time**: This is a time input field where you can select an end time for your appointment.
- **How often would you like to schedule appointments**: This field allows you to choose how often you would like to schedule appointments (e.g., once, regularly, occasionally, or never).
- **Preferred appointment duration**: This field allows you to choose the length of time you would like your appointments to last (e.g., 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 60 minutes, or less than 15 minutes).
- **Preferred language**: This field allows you to choose the language you would like to use for your appointment (e.g., English, French, or Spanish).

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering each field.
- If a field does not apply to you, simply skip it.
- Take your time to review each field before submitting the form.
