# Peer To Peer Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect feedback and insights about your relationship with a peer, colleague, or partner. It will help us understand how often you interact with each other, how you feel about the relationship, and what can be done to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to provide the most valuable feedback.
2. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of the relationship.
3. You will be asked to provide information about the relationship, including your name, email, and the date you last met your partner.
4. You will also be asked about your level of satisfaction with the relationship, frequency of interaction, and any conflicts or dominant behaviors you've experienced.
5. Finally, you will have the opportunity to provide any additional comments or feedback about the relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First name** (user1): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **First name email** (user1_email): Enter your email address associated with your first name.
* **Second name** (user2): Enter the name of the person you are relating to (if you want to provide it).
* **Second name email** (user2_email): Enter the email address associated with the second name (if you want to provide it).
* **Do you know the person** (relationship_type): Select the options that describe how you know the person (e.g., colleague, friend, partner).
* **How long do you know the person** (relationship_length): Enter the number of years you've known the person.
* **How frequently do you meet or communicate with the person** (relationship_frequency): Select the frequency of interaction (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **How satisfied are you with your current relationship** (relationship_satisfaction): Select the level of satisfaction with the relationship (e.g., highly satisfied, somewhat satisfied, not satisfied).
* **What is the status of your current relationship** (relationship_status): Select the status of the relationship (e.g., romantic, professional, friendly).
* **Comments** (relationship_comments): Provide any additional comments about the relationship.
* **Your partner's comments** (user1_relationship_comments): Provide any comments from your partner about the relationship.
* **Your partner's comments** (user2_relationship_comments): Provide any comments from your partner about the relationship.
* **Who is more dominant** (relationship_dominance): Select who you feel is more dominant in the relationship.
* **Have you had conflict with your partner** (relationship_conflict): Select the level of conflict in the relationship (e.g., none, minor, severe).
* **Why do you feel like your partner is more dominant** (relationship_dominance_comments): Provide any reasons why you feel your partner is more dominant.
* **Why do you have conflict with your partner** (relationship_conflict_comments): Provide any reasons why you have conflict with your partner.
* **Why are you satisfied with your current relationship** (relationship_satisfaction_comments): Provide any reasons why you are satisfied with your relationship.
* **Why do you feel like your current relationship is** (relationship_status_comments): Provide any reasons why you feel like your current relationship is.
* **Date you last met** (date_last_met): Enter the date you last met your partner.
* **Time you last met** (time_last_met): Enter the time you last met your partner.
* **Location you last met** (location_last_met): Select the location where you last met your partner.
* **Where and when did you last meet your partner** (date_last_met_comments): Provide any details about the last time you met your partner.
* **What time did you last meet your partner** (time_last_met_comments): Provide any additional comments about the time you last met your partner.
* **Where did you last meet your partner** (location_last_met_comments): Provide any additional comments about the location where you last met your partner.
* **Submission** (submission): You can submit any additional comments or feedback about the form or the relationship.

Note: This form is intended to be filled out by one person at a time, and the information will not be shared with the other person without their consent.
