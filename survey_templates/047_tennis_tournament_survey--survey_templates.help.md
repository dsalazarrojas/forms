# Tennis Tournament Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tennis Tournament Survey is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions on your experience at a tennis tournament. This form is meant to be a quick and easy way to provide feedback and ratings on your overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your tournament name in the **Tournament Name** field.
2. Select the date of the tournament in the **Tournament Date** field.
3. Choose the time of the day you attended the tournament in the **Tournament Time** field.
4. Enter the location of the tournament in the **Tournament Location** field.
5. Select your favorite match in the **Favorite Match** field (optional).
6. Choose your favorite opponent in the **Favorite Opponent** field (optional).
7. Answer the question about your overall experience by selecting one or more options in the **Overall Experience** field.
8. Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10 in the **Rating** field.
9. Leave any additional feedback or comments in the **Feedback** field (optional).
10. Enter your email address in the **Email** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tournament Name** (text, Required): This is the name of the tennis tournament you attended.
* **Tournament Date** (date, Required): Enter the date of the tournament in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Tournament Time** (time, Required): Select the time of day you attended the tournament.
* **Tournament Location** (text, Required): Enter the location of the tournament.
* **Favorite Match** (text, Optional): If you had a favorite match, enter the name of the match.
* **Favorite Opponent** (text, Optional): If you had a favorite opponent, enter their name.
* **Overall Experience** (select multiple, Required): Select one or more of the following options to describe your overall experience:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Rating** (number, Required): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Feedback** (text, Optional): Leave any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Email** (email, Required): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
