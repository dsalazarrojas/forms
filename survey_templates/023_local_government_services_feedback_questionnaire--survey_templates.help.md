# local-gov-services-feedback-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on local government services. It helps us understand your satisfaction level, the service you used, and any issues you may have encountered.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly, and only fill out the form for a specific local government service usage event.
2. Select the rating for your current level of satisfaction with the local government services.
3. Choose the local government service you used.
4. If applicable, indicate how long it took you to use the service.
5. If applicable, specify how you contacted the service.
6. If applicable, describe any issue you would like to report.
7. If applicable, provide your email address or phone number for follow-up.
8. If applicable, specify the date and time of your service usage.
9. Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your current level of satisfaction with the local government services?** (`localgovservicesfeedback`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Rate your satisfaction level with the provided options: Very dissatisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat satisfied, or Very satisfied.
* **Which local government service did you use?** (`which_service_used`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Choose the service you used, or select "Other (please specify)" if it's not listed.
* **How long did it take?** (`how_long_did_it_take`, `time`, optional)
	+ If this field is visible, you can specify the time it took to use the service.
* **How did you contact the service?** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose the method you used to contact the service: Phone, Email, In-person, Online, or Other (please specify).
* **Is there an issue you would like to report?** (`issue_description`, `note`, optional)
	+ If you have an issue, describe it in the text field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional)
	+ If you'd like to provide your email address for follow-up, enter it here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
	+ If you'd like to provide your phone number for follow-up, enter it here.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional)
	+ If this field is visible, you can specify the date of your service usage.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional)
	+ If this field is visible, you can specify the time of your service usage.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional)
	+ Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
