# pharmaceutical_new_drug_feedback_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from patients who are taking a new drug to assess its overall effectiveness and any potential side effects. The feedback collected will help healthcare professionals to understand how patients are experiencing the new medication and make informed decisions for future patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have not previously submitted feedback for this medication.
3. Enter your comments in the provided text fields.
4. Choose an answer for each of the numerical fields based on your experience.
5. Enter your contact information so that we can follow up with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **new drug feedback** (`new_drug_feedback`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you will provide a brief description of your experience with the new medication.
* **patient name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you will enter your name.
* **healthcare professional name** (`healthcare_professional_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is not intended for use by patients but rather for healthcare professionals. Please do not enter a value in this field.
* **rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Select a rating based on your experience with the medication. The options are likely to be on a scale from 1-5.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide more detailed information about your experience with the medication.
* **contact information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you enter your contact information so that we can follow up with you if needed.
* **date started treatment** (`date_started_treatment`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you started treatment with the new medication.
* **dosage instructions** (`dosage_instructions`, `text`, required: false): If you were given specific dosage instructions, please enter them here.
* **side effects** (`side_effects`, `text`, required: false): List any side effects you are experiencing due to the medication.
* **effectiveness** (`effectiveness`, `number`, required: false): Select a number based on how effective the medication has been for you. The options are likely to be on a scale from 1-5.
* **overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Select a number based on your overall satisfaction with the medication. The options are likely to be on a scale from 1-5.
* **comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): This is an optional field for any additional comments you may have.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you if needed.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can follow up with you if needed.
