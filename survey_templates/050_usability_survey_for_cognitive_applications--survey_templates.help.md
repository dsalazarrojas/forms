# usability_survey_for_cognitive_applications - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the cognitive application. It helps us identify areas of improvement and enhancement for the application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and answer them honestly and concisely.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Use the provided space for any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **usability_survey_for_cognitive_applications** (`note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please review the questions and answer them honestly.
* **how_would_you_rate_this_feature** (`number`, required: false): Rate this feature from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **any_other_comments** (`text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the feature.
* **what_would_you_improve** (`text`, required: false): Suggest improvements for the feature.
* **how_would_you_rate_this_feature_part_2** (`number`, required: false): Rate this feature from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **additional_feedback** (`text`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback about the application.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Provide specific examples or details to support your answers.
* If you have any suggestions or improvements, please share them.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
