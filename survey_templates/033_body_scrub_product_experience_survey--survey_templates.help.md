# Body Scrub Product Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your honest feedback about your experience with the body scrub product. Your responses will help us improve the product and make it better for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question as accurately as possible.
- Make sure to complete all the required fields marked as `*Required` in the form.
- You can use the hints provided to help you with your answers.
- Take your time and read each question carefully before answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (Respondent Name, `text`, *Optional*): Enter your name to be identified as the respondent in the survey.
* **Email Address** (Respondent Email, `email`, *Optional*): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive a copy of the survey results or future updates. (Note: This will not be shared publicly.)
* **Date of Product Purchase** (Purchase Date, `date`, *Required*): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Product Name** (Product Name, `text`, *Required*): Enter the name of the product you purchased.
* **Product Scent** (Product Scent, `text`, *Optional*): Enter the scent of the product, if applicable.
* **Where Did You Purchase This** (Purchase Location, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose where you bought the product from the provided options.
* **Price Paid** (Price Paid, `number`, *Optional*): Enter the price you paid for the product.
* **Is this your first time using this product** (First Time User, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose whether this is your first time using the product.
* **Your Skin Type** (Skin Type, `select_one`, *Required*): Select your skin type from the provided options.
* **Packaging Quality** (Packaging Quality, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose how you rate the packaging quality of the product.
* **Product Appearance and Texture** (Product Texture, `select_one`, *Required*): Select how you rate the product's appearance and texture.
* **Fragrance Quality** (Fragrance Quality, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose how you rate the fragrance quality of the product.
* **Exfoliation Effectiveness** (Exfoliation Effectiveness, `select_one`, *Required*): Select how effective the product was at exfoliating your skin.
* **Gentleness on Skin** (Gentleness on Skin, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose how gentle the product was on your skin.
* **Moisturizing Quality** (Moisturizing Quality, `select_one`, *Required*): Select how moisturizing the product was.
* **How Your Skin Feels After Use** (Skin Feeling, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose how your skin feels after using the product.
* **Value for Money** (Value for Money, `select_one`, *Required*): Select how you rate the value for money of the product.
* **How Often Do You Use This Product** (Use Frequency, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose how often you use the product.
* **Would You Repurchase This Product** (Would Repurchase, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose whether you would repurchase the product.
* **Would You Recommend to Others** (Would Recommend, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose whether you would recommend the product to others.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (Improvement Suggestions, `text`, *Optional*): Enter any suggestions for improving the product.
* **What You Liked Most** (Positive Aspects, `text`, *Optional*): Enter what you liked most about the product.
* **What You Disliked** (Negative Aspects, `text`, *Optional*): Enter what you disliked about the product.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose your overall satisfaction with the product.
