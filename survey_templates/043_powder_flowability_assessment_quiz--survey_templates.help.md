# Powder Flowability Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Powder Flowability Assessment Quiz is a tool designed to evaluate the user's understanding of powder flowability, a critical property that affects the behavior and performance of powders in various applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the most suitable option that best describes your understanding of powder flowability.
2. For the user information section, provide your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Answer each question based on your knowledge and understanding of powder flowability.
4. If you need help with any question, please ask.
5. Finally, review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **What is powder flowability?** (`question_1`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to describe your understanding of powder flowability:
	+ Option 1: A measure of how well a powder flows through a container or system.
	+ Option 2: A measure of a powder's ability to stick to surfaces.
	+ Option 3: A measure of a powder's ability to flow through a specific diameter.
* **What is the primary purpose of powder flowability?** (`question_2`, select_one, required): Select the primary purpose of powder flowability:
	+ Option 1: To predict the flowability of a powder in a specific application.
	+ Option 2: To determine the flowability of a powder in a specific container or system.
	+ Option 3: To assess the flowability of a powder in various environments.
* **Which of the following is NOT included in the flowability test?** (`question_3`, select_multiple, required): Select all that do not belong in a flowability test:
	+ Option 1: Measuring the flow speed of a powder.
	+ Option 2: Measuring the flowability of a powder in different temperatures.
	+ Option 3: Measuring the flow pressure of a powder.
* **How confident are you with powder flowability?** (`question_4`, select_one, required): Select your level of confidence:
	+ Option 1: Very confident.
	+ Option 2: Somewhat confident.
	+ Option 3: Not confident at all.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes related to powder flowability.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required): Enter your work email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date you completed this assessment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time you completed this assessment.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Submit your completed form for review.
