# ventilation_planning_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for ventilation planning purposes. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure the most accurate results. If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification or guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, make sure you have all the necessary information and documents ready. The form consists of two types of questions: multiple-choice questions and text fields. Answer each question according to the options provided, and if a question doesn't fit your specific situation, please provide a brief explanation or note in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **space_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the space or area you're planning for.
  - **space_type** (select one, optional): Choose whether the space is a "Yes" or "No" for the type of space, although this field seems a bit unclear. Please respond based on your best understanding.
  - **space description** (text, optional): Provide a short description of the space or area.
  - **system_type** (select multiple, optional): Check all applicable "Yes" options for the type of system you have in the space.
  - **system status** (select one, optional): Indicate the status of the system as either "Active" or "Inactive".
  - **system description** (text, optional): Give a brief description of the system in the space.
  - **project details** (text, optional): Enter any relevant project details related to the space or system.
  - **project manager** (select multiple, optional): Check all applicable "Yes" options for the project manager's role.
  - **space location** (select one, optional): Choose whether the space is a "Yes" or "No" for the location, although this field's meaning is also unclear. Please respond based on your best understanding.
  - **space size** (number, optional): Enter the size of the space in square feet.
  - **space height** (number, optional): Enter the height of the space in feet.
  - **system capacity** (number, optional): Enter the capacity of the system.
  - **air exchange rate** (number, optional): Enter the air exchange rate.
  - **air temperature** (select multiple, optional): Check all applicable "Yes" options for the air temperature.
  - **humidity** (number, optional): Enter the humidity level.

## Tips
- Make sure to be thorough but concise in your answers.
- If you're unsure about any question, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
- Please review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out.
