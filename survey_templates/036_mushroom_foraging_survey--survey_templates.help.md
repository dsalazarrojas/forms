# Mushroom Foraging Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mushroom Foraging Survey is a form designed to collect information about your foraging experiences, locations, and habits. This data will help us understand foraging practices and improve our knowledge on the subject.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing information about your foraging experiences.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Enter any additional comments or information that you think will help us understand your foraging practices.
4. Ensure you complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk \*.
5. Review your answers before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Foraging Experiences
* **Foraging Experiences** (`foraging_experiences`, text): Describe your foraging experiences, including the types of mushrooms you've found and any notable events or challenges you've faced.
* **Locations** (`locations_foraged`, select_one): Select the locations where you've foraged for mushrooms.
* **Time Spent Foraging** (`time_spent_foraging`, number): Enter the number of hours you've spent foraging for mushrooms.

### Page 2
* **Mushroom Type** (`mushroom_type`, select_multiple): Select the types of mushrooms you've foraged for.
* **Safety Practices** (`safety_practices`, select_one): Select the safety practices you follow when foraging.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note): Enter any additional comments or information about your foraging practices.

### Page 3
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text): Enter your name.
* **Contact Street Address** (`contact_street_address`, text): Enter your street address.
* **Contact City** (`contact_city`, text): Enter your city.
* **Contact State** (`contact_state`, text): Enter your state or province.
* **Contact Zip** (`contact_zip`, text): Enter your postal code.

### Page 4
* **Foraging Club** (`foraging_club`, select_multiple): Indicate if you're affiliated with a foraging club.
* **Foraging Interests** (`foraging_interests`, select_one): Select your primary foraging interest.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one): Select your age range.
* **Years of Experience** (`experience_years`, number): Enter the number of years you've been foraging.
* **Foraging Frequency** (`foraging_frequency`, select_one): Select how often you forage for mushrooms.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, and don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
