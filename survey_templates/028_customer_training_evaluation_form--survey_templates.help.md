# Customer Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers or trainees on their experience with the training session. This feedback will help us understand what worked well and what can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form with your honest feedback and ratings.
2. For select multiple fields, choose all that apply.
3. Ensure that you complete all required and optional fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Training Form** (`customer_training_form`, text, required: false): This is the main text field for any comments or feedback you'd like to provide about the training session.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required: false): This field is for rating your overall satisfaction with the training session. Please rate from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Training Session ID** (`training_session_id`, number, required: false): This field is for referencing the training session you attended. Please enter the session's unique ID if you have it.
* **Feedback Question 1** (`feedback_question_1`, text, required: false): This field is for providing feedback on the first question.
* **Feedback Question 2** (`feedback_question_2`, text, required: false): This field is for providing feedback on the second question.
