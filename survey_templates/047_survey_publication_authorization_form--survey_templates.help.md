# Survey Publication Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survey Publication Authorization Form is a crucial document that verifies the permission granted by participants for the publication of a survey. This form helps to ensure that the survey is published with the correct permissions and details of the participants involved.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the participant's name and ensure it matches the name of the participant you are representing.
2. Enter the participant's organization, if applicable.
3. Enter the participant's email address.
4. Enter the participant's phone number.
5. Select whether the participant has given their consent for the publication of the survey.
6. Select the status of the publication (Active or Inactive).
7. Enter any additional notes about the publication status.
8. If required, select the status of the participant's email and phone (Active or Inactive).
9. Enter any additional notes about the participant's email and phone status.
10. Finally, review and submit the form to complete the authorization process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Publication Authorization Form 1**: This is the main section of the form where you will enter the details of the survey publication authorization.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant who has given their consent for the publication of the survey.
* **Organization**: If the participant is affiliated with an organization, enter the name of the organization.
* **Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Publication Consent**: Select whether the participant has given their consent for the publication of the survey.
* **Publication Terms**: Enter any additional notes about the publication of the survey.
* **Date of Publication**: Enter the date on which the survey is to be published.
* **Time of Publication**: Enter the time of the publication.
* **Publication Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the publication.
* **Publication Status**: Select the status of the publication (Active or Inactive).
* **Publication Date Status**: Select the status of the publication date (Active or Inactive).
* **Publication Time Status**: Select the status of the publication time (Active or Inactive).
* **Publication Notes Status**: Select the status of the publication notes (Active or Inactive).
* **Publication Status Note**: Enter any additional notes about the publication status.
* **Participant Consent Status**: Select whether the participant has given their consent for the survey.
* **Participant Consent Note**: Enter any additional notes about the participant's consent.
* **Participant Name Status**: Select the status of the participant's name (Active or Inactive).
* **Participant Name Note**: Enter any additional notes about the participant's name status.
* **Participant Organization Status**: Select the status of the participant's organization (Active or Inactive).
* **Participant Organization Note**: Enter any additional notes about the participant's organization status.
* **Participant Email Status**: Select the status of the participant's email (Active or Inactive).
* **Participant Email Note**: Enter any additional notes about the participant's email status.
* **Participant Phone Status**: Select the status of the participant's phone (Active or Inactive).
* **Participant Phone Note**: Enter any additional notes about the participant's phone status.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have all the required information before filling out the form to avoid errors or duplication of effort.
* Review and review the form carefully to ensure that all the details are accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the participant for clarification or additional information.
* The form is designed to collect all the necessary details for the publication authorization process.
