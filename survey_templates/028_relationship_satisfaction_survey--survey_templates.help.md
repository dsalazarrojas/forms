# relationship_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to help us better understand your feelings and experiences in your relationship, gathering information about your satisfaction and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, simply follow the steps outlined below:
    1.  Answer each question honestly, taking into consideration your feelings and experiences.
    2.  Be as concise as possible while still providing enough detail to help us understand your relationship.
    3.  Please respond to each question with the corresponding options provided.
    4.  You may skip questions that do not apply to your relationship.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us in the comments section at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`This form is to help us better understand your feelings`, note, required): A brief note introducing the purpose of the form.
*   **What are your relationship expectations?** (`Expectations 1`, `Expectations 2`, `Expectations 3`, select_one, required): This question is asking about your expectations from your relationship, such as what you want to get out of it.
*   **How do you feel in your relationship?** (`Positive`, `Negative`, select_multiple, required): This question is about your emotions and feelings in your relationship, such as how you feel about your partner.
*   **What would improve your relationship?** (`text`, optional): This question is asking about areas where you feel your relationship could be improved.
*   **How long have you been in your relationship?** (`MM/DD/YY`, date, required): This question is asking about the length of time you've been in your relationship, so we can get a sense of its duration.
*   **How would you rate communication in your relationship?** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`, select_one, required): This question is about the quality of communication in your relationship, such as how well you and your partner communicate with each other.
*   **How would you rate intimacy in your relationship?** (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`, `None`, select_multiple, optional): This question is about the level of closeness and physical connection you feel in your relationship.
*   **How would you rate trust in your relationship?** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`, select_one, required): This question is about how much you trust your partner, such as whether you feel secure in your relationship.
*   **How would you rate emotional support in your relationship?** (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`, `None`, select_multiple, required): This question is about how much emotional support you receive from your partner, such as how much they care about your feelings.
*   **How would you rate physical contact in your relationship?** (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`, `None`, select_multiple, optional): This question is about the level of physical contact you have with your partner, such as how often you touch or kiss each other.
*   **What are your shared goals for your relationship?** (`text`, optional): This question is asking about any shared goals or aspirations you have for your relationship, such as what you want to achieve together.
*   **Do you think this relationship is headed towards marriage?** (`True`, `False`, `Maybe`, select_one, optional): This question is about whether you believe your relationship will lead to marriage.
*   **Do you have children?** (`True`, `False`, `Maybe`, select_one, optional): This question is about whether you have any children with your partner.
*   **What is your relationship status?** (`Married`, `In a relationship`, `Divorced`, `Widowed`, `It's complicated`, select_one, required): This question is about your current relationship status, such as whether you're married or single.
*   **Any comments?** (`note`, optional): This question is for any additional comments or thoughts you may have about your relationship.
