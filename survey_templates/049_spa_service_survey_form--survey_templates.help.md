# Spa Service Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Service Survey Form is a tool to gather feedback and ratings from clients who have received spa services. This form aims to improve the quality of services provided by the spa.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form once for each spa service received.
2. Answer all questions and fields accordingly.
3. If you have any questions, you can add feedback in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Spa Service** (`lobby_survey`, text, required): Describe the spa service you received (e.g., massage, facial, manicure, etc.).
* **Questions** (`questions`, number, optional): If you have any questions related to the service, please type them here.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Add any additional feedback about your experience at the spa. This can include suggestions for improvement or comments on the quality of service.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 stars, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest, to rate your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to receive future updates or promotions from the spa, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have any questions or would like to inquire about future services, you can provide your phone number.
* **Date of Service** (`date`, date, optional): If you know the date you received the service, please type it here.
* **Time of Service** (`time`, time, optional): If you know the time you received the service, please type it here.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully to help us improve our services.
* If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
