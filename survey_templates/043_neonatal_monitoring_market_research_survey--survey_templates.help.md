# neonatal_monitoring_market_research_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the neonatal monitoring market. Your input is crucial in helping us understand this market and identify areas for improvement or innovation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. If a field is not applicable to you, please select "N/A" or provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **usage** (`usage`, text, optional): Please describe how you currently use neonatal monitoring devices or systems in your practice.
- **satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, text, required): Rate your level of satisfaction with the current neonatal monitoring devices or systems you use.
- **buying_cycles** (`buying_cycles`, text, required): Describe your typical buying cycle for neonatal monitoring devices or systems.
- **adoption_barriers** (`adoption_barriers`, text, required): Identify any barriers you face when adopting or purchasing neonatal monitoring devices or systems.
- **device_characteristics** (`device_characteristics`, text, optional): Describe the characteristics of the neonatal monitoring devices or systems you use or would like to see in a new product.
- **market_trends** (`market_trends`, text, optional): Share any market trends you see affecting the neonatal monitoring market.
- **demographics** (`demographics`, select_one, required): Select your professional role from the list:
  • 18
  • male
  • healthcare provider
  • researcher
- **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your name, title, and contact information.
