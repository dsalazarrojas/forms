# Investment Policy Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gauge an employee's awareness of investment policies within the organization. Its primary purpose is to collect feedback and insights on employees' understanding of these policies, their department, location, and training needs. This information will help management identify areas where employees require additional training or resources.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your Employee Name.
2. Select your department from the options provided.
3. Check the box next to the locations where you currently perform your work.
4. Choose the investment policies you are familiar with from the options provided.
5. Select the areas where you have a good understanding from the options provided.
6. Provide any feedback or insights you have about the form or the survey process.
7. Check the boxes for any additional training needs you require.
8. Check the boxes for any comments you have for your manager.
9. Sign and date this form to confirm you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Department**: Select the department you are a member of.
* **Location**: Check the boxes next to the locations where you currently perform your work.
* **Investment Policies**: Choose the investment policies you are familiar with.
* **Understanding**: Select the areas where you have a good understanding.
* **Feedback**: Provide any comments or insights you have about the form or the survey process.
* **Training Needs**: Check the boxes for any additional training needs you require.
* **Manager Comments**: Provide any comments or insights you have for your manager.
* **Employee Signature**: Please sign and date this form to confirm you have completed it.
