# Peer Learning Facilitation Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from facilitators about their experiences with peer learning activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide a brief description of your facilitation experience in the "facilitation_experience" field. This should include any relevant details about the activity.
2. In the "facilitation_challenges" field, list any challenges you faced during the facilitation process.
3. In the "effectiveness" field, provide any comments or observations about the effectiveness of the facilitation process.
4. In the "suggestions" field, select all the ways in which the facilitation process could be improved.
5. In the "program_improvement" field, describe any improvements you would recommend for the program based on your experience.
6. In the "facilitator_support" field, indicate the level of support you received from the facilitator or other stakeholders.
7. In the "time_spent" field, estimate the time spent on the facilitation activity.
8. In the "facilitator_comments" field, provide any additional comments or feedback for the facilitator or program administrators.
9. In the "program_challenges" field, select all the challenges you faced with the program during the facilitation process.
10. In the "program_improvement_actions" field, describe any actions you would recommend to improve the program.
11. In the "facilitation_improvement" field, suggest any ways to improve the facilitation process itself.
