# Extracurricular Stipend Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extracurricular Stipend Survey is designed to gather feedback from users on their extracurricular activities and comments. This form is used to collect data on whether users have participated in extracurricular activities and any additional comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you are authorized to complete this form.
2. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
3. Select the correct answer for each question.
4. Provide any additional comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): This section is for users to provide any additional context or background information about themselves.
* **Extracurricular Stipend Activities** (`extracurricular_stipend_activities`, select_multiple, required): Check the activities that you have participated in. You may select multiple activities.
	+ 'Yes' indicates that you have participated in the activity.
	+ 'No' indicates that you have not participated in the activity.
* **Activities** (`activities`, select_one, required): Check the activity that best describes your participation in extracurricular activities.
	+ 'Yes' indicates that you have participated in extracurricular activities.
	+ 'No' indicates that you have not participated in extracurricular activities.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Please provide any additional comments or information you have about your extracurricular activities.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted for further information, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted for further information, please provide your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you have been assigned a tool or resource, please provide the name of the tool.
* **Comments (Other)** (`comments_other`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information that do not fit in the previous fields, please provide them here.
