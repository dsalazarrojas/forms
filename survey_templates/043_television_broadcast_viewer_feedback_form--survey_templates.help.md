# Television Broadcast Viewer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Television Broadcast Viewer Feedback Form" is designed to collect feedback from viewers of television broadcasts. The information collected will help the broadcaster improve future content and better understand their audience's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. **Be honest**: Provide your genuine opinions and thoughts about the broadcast.
3. **Use a valid email address**: Enter a valid email address that you own or have access to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide a brief introduction or context about your feedback.

### Your Name
* **Your Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the feedback report. This field is required.

### What is your opinion on the content of this broadcast?
* **What is your opinion on the content of this broadcast?**: Share your thoughts and opinions about the broadcast's content. This field is required.

### How satisfied are you with the content of this broadcast?
* **How satisfied are you with the content of this broadcast?**: Choose one of the options to indicate your level of satisfaction with the broadcast's content. This field is required.
   - Option 1: Strongly satisfied
   - Option 2: Moderately satisfied
   - Option 3: Somewhat satisfied
   - Option 4: Not satisfied

### How likely are you to recommend this broadcast to a friend or family member?
* **How likely are you to recommend this broadcast to a friend or family member?**: Select all that apply for your answer. This field is required.
   - Option 1: Strongly likely
   - Option 2: Moderately likely
   - Option 3: Not likely

### Your Email
* **Your Email**: Enter a valid email address that you own or have access to. This will be used to contact you for any further discussion. This field is required.

### Any additional comments?
* **Any additional comments?**: Provide any additional comments or thoughts you may have about the broadcast. This field is optional.
