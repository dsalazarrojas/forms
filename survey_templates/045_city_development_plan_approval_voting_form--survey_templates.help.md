# City Development Plan Approval Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from community members on a proposed city development plan. The form aims to collect information on individual's familiarity with the plan, their approval or disapproval of the plan, and any concerns or benefits they see associated with it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please ensure you are a community member affected by the development plan.
2.  Fill out your name and email address in the designated fields.
3.  Answer questions regarding your familiarity with the plan and your approval or disapproval of it.
4.  List any concerns or benefits you see associated with the plan.
5.  If you have suggestions for modifications or additional comments, feel free to include them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself as a community member.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address to facilitate potential follow-up contact.
*   **Will You Be Directly Affected by This Development?**: Indicate whether you will be directly affected by the development plan. If you don't know, please answer "Unsure."
*   **How Familiar Are You with the Development Plan?**: Answer how familiar you are with the development plan on a scale from Very Familiar to Not Familiar.
*   **Have You Reviewed the Plan Documents?**: Indicate whether you have reviewed the plan documents. If you haven't, you can select "No."
*   **Do You Approve of This Development Plan?**: Select your approval or disapproval of the plan on a scale from Strongly Approve to Strongly Disapprove.
*   **Main Reason for Your Vote**: If you've disapproved or partially disapproved the plan, provide the main reason for your vote.
*   **Major Concerns About This Plan**: Select all concerns that apply to you regarding the development plan.
*   **Describe Your Specific Concerns**: If you have additional concerns not listed, please describe them in this field.
*   **Potential Benefits You See**: Select all benefits that you see associated with the development plan.
*   **Would You Approve If Certain Conditions Were Met?**: If you wouldn't normally approve of the plan, indicate whether you would be willing to approve it under certain conditions.
*   **What Conditions Would You Require?**: If you selected "Yes" to the previous question, please provide conditions that would make you approve of the plan.
*   **Would You Prefer Modifications to the Plan?**: Indicate whether you would be willing to approve of the plan with modifications.
*   **Suggested Modifications**: If you would prefer modifications, suggest them in this field.
*   **Additional Comments or Concerns**: If you have any additional comments or concerns not covered by the previous fields, please include them here.
*   **How Did You Learn About This Development Plan?**: Select how you became aware of the development plan.
*   **Interested in Further Involvement in Planning?**: Indicate your level of interest in further involvement in planning processes.
