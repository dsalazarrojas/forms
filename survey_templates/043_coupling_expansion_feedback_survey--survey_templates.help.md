# Coupling Expansion Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coupling Expansion Feedback Survey is designed to collect your feedback and ratings on our product. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey to help us understand your experience with our product and identify areas for improvement. This survey is intended to be completed by end-users of our product, and your feedback will help us to provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "What is your email address" field. Please ensure that it is correctly formatted as an email address.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "What is your phone number" field.
4. Rate our product by selecting one of the options from the "How would you rate this product" field.
5. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback about our product in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (form_title, text, required: false): This field asks for your name, which will be used to personalize your feedback. Please type your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **What is your email address** (form_name, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address to allow us to contact you in case we need additional information or clarification.
* **What is your phone number** (form_phone, text, required: false): If you have a phone number, please enter it to help us contact you in case we need to follow up on your feedback.
* **How would you rate this product** (form_rating, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options below to rate our product:
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Fair
	+ Option 4: Poor
	+ Option 5: Very Poor
* **Comments** (form_comments, text, required: false): Share any additional thoughts, comments, or suggestions about our product. This is a great place to provide constructive feedback that will help us improve our services.
