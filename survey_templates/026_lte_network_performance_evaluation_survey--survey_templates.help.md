# lte_network_performance_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of LTE networks. It consists of a series of questions that assess the quality of your LTE network in different areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the starting point of the form by choosing from the options provided in the "start_form" field.
2. For the "lte_performance" field, select all the options that apply to your experience with the LTE network. This field is required to be completed.
3. In the "network_issues" field, select all the issues you have experienced with the LTE network. This field is not required.
4. For the "device_performance" field, select how your devices have performed on the LTE network. This field is not required.
5. In the "customer_experience" field, provide a brief description of your experience with the LTE network. This field is required.
6. Finally, select the completion status of your evaluation in the "submit_form" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **start_form** (`start_form`, `select_one`, required): This field determines the starting point of your evaluation. Select "Yes" to begin the evaluation and "No" to cancel.
- **lte_performance** (`lte_performance`, `select_multiple`, required): This field assesses your experience with the LTE network. Select all the options that apply to your experience.
- **network_issues** (`network_issues`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field identifies any issues you have experienced with the LTE network. Select all the issues that apply.
- **device_performance** (`device_performance`, `select_one`, optional): This field assesses how your devices have performed on the LTE network. Select how your devices have behaved.
- **customer_experience** (`customer_experience`, `note`, required): This field requires a brief description of your overall experience with the LTE network. Be as detailed as possible.
- **submit_form** (`submit_form`, `select_one`, required): This field confirms the completion of your evaluation. Select "Yes" to submit and "No" to cancel.
