# E Resource Provision Challenges Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback and information from library professionals about the challenges they face when providing resources to their users. This includes understanding the pain points they experience, their priorities for improvement, and potential access barriers they encounter. The survey is used by E-Resource Provision to better serve and support these professionals and their users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the page you want to respond to by clicking on the corresponding link.
2. Answer each question according to your honest and genuine response.
3. Use the text boxes to provide detailed answers.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, type them in the "E-Resource Provision Challenges Survey" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resource Provision Challenges**: This page is where you can describe any challenges you face when providing resources to your users. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Library Pain Points**: Describe the pain points or difficulties you experience when providing resources to your users.
* **Priorities for Improvement**: Indicate the areas that you would like to see improved in the resource provision process.
* **Access Barriers**: List any access barriers you encounter when providing resources to your users.
* **E-Resource Provision Challenges Survey**: This is an optional text field for any additional comments or suggestions you have about the resource provision process.

## Tips
* Be honest and genuine in your responses.
* Provide detailed answers to help us better understand your experiences.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please type them in the "E-Resource Provision Challenges Survey" field.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey and provide your valuable feedback!
