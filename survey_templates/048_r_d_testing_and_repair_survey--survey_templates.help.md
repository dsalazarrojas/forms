# R&D Testing And Repair Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by R&D teams to collect information about R&D Testing and Repair surveys.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Review each section carefully.
- Ensure you answer all required fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **1. What is this form for**: *What is this form for* (text, not required): Enter a brief description of the issue you are reporting.
*   **2. tester id** (number, not required): Enter your unique ID as a tester.
*   **3. tester name** (text, not required): Enter your name as a tester.
*   **4. location** (Select One, required): Select your location:
	+ QA
	+ Engineering
	+ Lab
*   **5. shift** (Select Multiple, not required): Select all shifts you worked during the issue:
	+ A
	+ B
	+ C
*   **6. date** (date, not required): Enter in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **7. time** (time, not required): Enter in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
*   **8. comments** (text, not required): Enter any additional comments about the issue.
*   **9. issue type** (Select One, required): Select the type of issue you are reporting:
	+ bug
	+ other
*   **10. issue description** (text, not required): Enter a detailed description of the issue.
*   **11. outcome** (Select One, required): Select the outcome of the action:
	+ resolved
	+ not resolved
	+ pending
*   **12. feedback** (text, not required): Enter any feedback about the issue.
*   **13. action plan** (text, not required): Enter an action plan to resolve the issue.
*   **14. follow up** (Select Multiple, not required): Select if follow up is needed:
	+ yes
	+ no
*   **15. reviewer** (text, not required): Enter the name of the reviewer.
*   **16. notes** (text, not required): Enter any additional notes about the issue.
*   **17. tester phone** (text, not required): Enter your phone number.
*   **18. tester email** (email, not required): Enter your email.
*   **17. tester notes** (text, not required): Enter any additional notes about the issue.

## Tips
- Review each field carefully.
- Ask your supervisor if you are unsure about any field.
- Ensure you answer all required fields.
