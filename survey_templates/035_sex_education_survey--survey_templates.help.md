# Sex Education Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to complete the Sex Education Survey form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Survey Template**: This is the main section of the form where you will answer questions about sex education.
2. **Sex Education Questions**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
3. **Sex Education Topics**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
4. **Teacher/Counselor Name**: Enter the name of the teacher or counselor who provided the sex education.
5. **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where the sex education took place.
6. **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
7. **Date Survey Given**: Select the date the survey was completed.
8. **Time Survey Given**: Select the time the survey was completed.
9. **Survey Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the sex education program.
10. **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
11. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
12. **Contact Information**: Add any additional contact information for follow-up.
13. **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the survey or sex education program.
14. **Email Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the survey or sex education program for the admin.
15. **Comments for Tech/Teacher**: Add any technical or logistical comments or feedback about the survey.
16. **Teacher/Counselor Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the teacher or counselor.
17. **Comments for Tech/Admin**: Add any technical or administrative comments or feedback about the survey.
18. **Contact Information for Tech**: Add any additional contact information for technical support.
19. **Sex Education Level**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
20. **Sex Education Topic**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
21. **Sex Education Level Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the sex education program level.
22. **Sex Education Comment**: Enter any comments or feedback about the sex education program.
23. **Email Comments for Admin**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the sex education program for the admin.
24. **Phone Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the sex education program.
25. **Comments for Tech**: Add any technical comments or feedback about the survey.
26. **Teacher/Counselor Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the teacher or counselor.
27. **Comments for Tech/Admin**: Add any technical or administrative comments or feedback about the survey.
