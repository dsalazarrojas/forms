# financial_coaching_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from clients after a coaching session, helping coaches to evaluate their performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field based on your coaching session.
2. Provide feedback on the client's overall satisfaction with the session.
3. Choose the relevant session topics discussed during the session.
4. Rate the coach's performance in areas such as communication, organization, planning, and problem-solving.
5. Select areas for improvement for the coach.
6. Provide any additional feedback or comments.
7. Enter the client's email address and phone number (if applicable).
8. Enter the coaching session date.
9. Enter the coaching session duration (if applicable).
10. Enter the coach's name and client's name.
11. Select the coaching session topic.
12. Enter the coaching program name.
13. Enter any coaching session notes (if applicable).
14. Enter the next steps for the client.
15. Enter the follow-up date and notes (if applicable).
16. Enter the next session topic (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, `note`, required): This field is for general feedback about the client's experience during the coaching session.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate the client's overall satisfaction with the session, on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Session Topics** (`session_topics`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all relevant session topics discussed during the session.
* **Feedback on Coach** (`feedback_on_coach`, `select_one`, required): Rate the coach's performance using the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feedback on Coaching** (`feedback_on_coaching`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate the coach's performance in areas such as communication, organization, planning, problem-solving, presentation, and listening.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose areas where the coach can improve their performance.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `note`, not required): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the session.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, not required): Enter the client's phone number (if applicable).
* **Coaching Session Date** (`coaching_session_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the coaching session.
* **Coaching Session Duration** (`coaching_session_duration`, `number`, not required): Enter the duration of the coaching session (if applicable).
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required): Enter the coach's name.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the client's phone number (if applicable).
* **Coaching Session Topic** (`coaching_session_topic`, `select_one`, required): Select the topic of the coaching session.
* **Coaching Program Name** (`coaching_program_name`, `text`, required): Enter the coaching program name.
* **Coaching Session Notes** (`coaching_session_notes`, `text`, not required): Enter any notes about the coaching session (if applicable).
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, not required): Enter the next steps for the client.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the follow-up date (if applicable).
* **Follow Up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, `text`, not required): Enter any follow-up notes (if applicable).
* **Next Session Topic** (`next_session_topic`, `text`, not required): Enter the topic for the next session (if applicable).
* **Follow Up Phone** (`follow_up_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the follow-up phone number (if applicable).
* **Follow Up Email** (`follow_up_email`, `email`, not required): Enter the follow-up email address (if applicable).
* **Follow Up Phone Number** (`follow_up_phone_number`, `text`, not required): Enter the follow-up phone number (if applicable).
