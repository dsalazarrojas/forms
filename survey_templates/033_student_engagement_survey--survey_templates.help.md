# Student Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from students about their experience in the class. It is intended to help instructors and course administrators understand student perspectives, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance the overall learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. You can respond to all or some of the questions, depending on your experience and feedback. Make sure to provide honest and constructive feedback, and feel free to elaborate on your answers if you have more to say.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (Note): This is a section where you can provide any general comments or suggestions about the class.
* **How would you rate your experience in this class so far?** (Text): Rate your experience in the class from 1-5, where 1 is "Not at all satisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".
* **How would you rate your experience of the online platform we use?** (Text): Rate your experience with the online platform used for this class from 1-5, where 1 is "Not at all satisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".
* **How would you rate the quality of the instructor?** (Number): Rate the quality of the instructor from 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the quality of the materials provided?** (Number): Rate the quality of the materials provided for the class from 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate your experience with the online tools provided?** (Number): Rate your experience with the online tools used for this class from 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate your experience with the online tools provided?** (Number): Rate your experience with the online tools used for this class from 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **What would you like to see improved in this class?** (Text): Share any specific changes or improvements you think would benefit the class.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (Text): Share any suggestions or ideas for improving the class.
* **Do you have any further comments or feedback?** (Text): Share any additional thoughts or concerns about the class.
* **How likely are you to take this class again?** (Select One): Rate how likely you are to take the class again, with options: "Very Likely", "Somewhat Likely", "Not Likely", or "Not at all Likely".
* **Would you like to be contacted for follow-up questions?** (Select Multiple): Choose "True" or "False" if you are willing or not willing to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **How many times have you attended this class?** (Date): Report the date of your last class attendance.
* **What was the last time you attended this class?** (Time): Report the time of your last class attendance.
* **How long have you been attending this class?** (Date): Report the start date of your class attendance.
* **How long do you plan to attend this class?** (Date): Report your planned duration of class attendance.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of this class?** (Number): Rate the overall quality of the class from 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **What do you think would improve this class?** (Text): Share specific ideas or suggestions for improving the class.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to add?** (Text): Share any final thoughts or comments about the class.
