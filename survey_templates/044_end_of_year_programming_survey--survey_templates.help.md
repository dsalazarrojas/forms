# end_of_year_programming_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The end-of-year programming survey is a tool to help teams reflect on their productivity, skills, and development strategies. It's a crucial part of the team's self-assessment process, allowing them to identify areas for improvement and set goals for the next year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Use the hints provided for each question to help guide you.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is an End of Year Programming Survey?** (`title`, `text`, required: false): This is a title field that briefly describes the purpose of the form. It's a good idea to write a few sentences to provide context.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `number`, required: true): Please enter the number of developers in your team.
* **How many developers are in your team?** (`team_size_hint`, `text`, required: false): This is a hint to help you remember to enter the team size.
* **Average Years of Experience** (`average_years_experience`, `number`, required: true): Please enter the average years of experience of your developers.
* **What is the average years of experience of your developers?** (`average_years_experience_hint`, `text`, required: false): This is a hint to help you remember to enter the average years of experience.
* **Skills Identified** (`skills_identified`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Please select all the programming skills your team has, such as Python, Java, C++, C#, C, Ruby, Swift, and SQL.
* **What programming skills does your team have?** (`skills_identified_hint`, `text`, required: false): This is a hint to help you remember to select the skills.
* **Productivity Improvement** (`productivity_improvement`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select one of the options to indicate how you want to improve productivity: Boost, Identify, Analyze, or Create.
* **How do you want to improve productivity?** (`productivity_improvement_hint`, `text`, required: false): This is a hint to help you remember to select a productivity improvement strategy.
* **Strategy Development** (`strategy_development`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select one of the options to indicate the development strategy you want to implement: Informed, Incremental, or Continuous.
* **What kind of development strategy do you want to implement?** (`strategy_development_hint`, `text`, required: false): This is a hint to help you remember to select a development strategy.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required: false): Please enter a brief description of your next steps for the next year.
* **What are your next steps for the next year?** (`next_steps_hint`, `text`, required: false): This is a hint to help you remember to enter your next steps.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: true): This is a submit button to finalize your responses.
* **Submit Hint** (`submit_hint`, `text`, required: false): This is a hint to help you confirm your submission.
