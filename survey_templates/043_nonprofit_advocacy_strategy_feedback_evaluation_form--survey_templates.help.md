# Nonprofit Advocacy Strategy Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from nonprofit organizations on their advocacy strategy, helping to identify areas of improvement and effective practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by selecting your role within the organization:
    * **What is your role in the organization?**: Please choose the role that best describes your position within the organization.
2.  Select the program your organization focuses on:
    * **What program does your organization focus on?**: Choose the program that your organization is primarily focused on.
3.  Describe the main objectives of your organization's advocacy strategy:
    * **What are the main objectives of your organization's advocacy strategy?**: Provide a brief description of the primary goals of your organization's advocacy efforts.
4.  Indicate how your organization implements its advocacy strategy:
    * **How does your organization implement its advocacy strategy?**: Select all methods your organization uses to implement its advocacy strategy.
5.  Specify the expected outcomes of your organization's advocacy strategy:
    * **What are the expected outcomes of your organization's advocacy strategy?**: Choose all the outcomes your organization expects to achieve through its advocacy efforts.
6.  Evaluate the effectiveness of your organization's advocacy strategy:
    * **Evaluation of the advocacy strategy**: Select how effective or ineffective you think the strategy is, or choose "Not sure" if you're unsure.
7.  Add any additional comments:
    * **Additional comments**: Provide any further information or thoughts you have about your organization's advocacy strategy.

## Tips
*   Please be as specific as possible when answering each question.
*   You may select multiple answers for questions 3, 5, and 6.
*   If you have additional comments, please provide any further information or thoughts on your organization's advocacy strategy.
