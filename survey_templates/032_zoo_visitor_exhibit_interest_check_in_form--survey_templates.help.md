# Zoo Visitor Exhibit Interest Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoo Visitor Exhibit Interest Check In Form is designed to gather information from zoo visitors about their interests, demographics, and preferences. This form helps the zoo staff understand the visitors' interests and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Exhibit" field with the exhibit you are interested in.
2. Answer the "Interest" field with your level of interest in the selected exhibit.
3. Answer the "Motivations" field with your reasons for visiting the exhibit.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
6. If you have any additional comments or questions, you can enter them in the "Comment" field.
7. If you are visiting with staff, answer "Yes" to the "Staff" field.
8. If you need to add any additional information for the staff, you can enter it in the "Staff Note" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibit (Select One)**: Select the exhibit that interests you the most.
* **Interest (Select Multiple)**: Answer with your level of interest in the selected exhibit.
* **Motivations (Select One)**: Choose your reasons for visiting the exhibit.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Comment (Text)**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Staff (Select One)**: Answer "Yes" if you are visiting with staff.
* **Staff Note (Note)**: Enter any additional information for the staff (optional).
* **Customer Note (Note)**: Enter any additional information for the customer (optional).
* **Customer Comment (Text)**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Customer Phone (Text)**: Enter your customer's phone number (optional).
* **Customer E-mail (Email)**: Enter your customer's email address (optional).
* **Customer Name (Text)**: Enter your customer's name.
* **Customer Age (Number)**: Enter your customer's age.
* **Customer Gender (Select One)**: Choose your customer's gender.
* **Customer Country (Select One)**: Choose your customer's country of origin.
* **Customer City (Select One)**: Choose your customer's city of residence.
* **Customer Postal Code (Select One)**: Choose your customer's postal code.
* **Customer Province (Select One)**: Choose your customer's province of residence.
* **Customer Zip (Select One)**: Choose your customer's zip code.

Note: Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
