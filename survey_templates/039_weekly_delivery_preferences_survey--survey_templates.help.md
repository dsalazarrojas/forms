# Weekly Delivery Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on a user's preferences for weekly deliveries. The form helps us understand individual preferences and tailor our services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the delivery days you are interested in for weekly deliveries.
2. Choose the time window that suits your schedule best.
3. If applicable, provide any additional delivery instructions.
4. Rate your satisfaction with our current delivery services.
5. Indicate your preference for delivery frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **delivery_day**: Select the days you prefer to receive weekly deliveries. Please choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Yes: You would like to receive deliveries on this day.
	+ No: You do not want to receive deliveries on this day.
* **time_window**: Choose the time of the day when you are available to receive deliveries. 
* **instruction**: If you have any specific delivery instructions, please provide them in this field.
* **satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with our current delivery services. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Satisfied: You are very satisfied with our services.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are not satisfied with our current delivery services.
* **delivery_instructions**: If you have any additional delivery instructions, please provide them in this field.
* **delivery_frequency**: Indicate your preference for the frequency of deliveries. 
	+ Yes: You would like to receive deliveries more frequently.
	+ No: You are happy with the current delivery frequency.

## Tips
Please take your time to review each field carefully and make sure you are selecting the options that best represent your preferences. If you have any doubts or questions, please do not hesitate to contact our support team.
