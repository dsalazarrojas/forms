# webinar_attendee_email_validation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The webinar_attendee_email_validation_form is used to collect and validate the email addresses of people who are interested in attending a webinar. This form is designed to be easy to fill out and requires minimal information to validate the email addresses of attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the webinar_attendee_email_validation_form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Attendee** field with a brief description of the attendee.
2. Enter the attendee's email address in the **Email** field. This field must be a valid email address.
3. If you are a person who is required to fill out this form, check the box next to **Required** to indicate that you are a required attendee.
4. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
5. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
6. Enter any additional email addresses you want to validate in the **Email 2** and **Email 3** fields.
7. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the **Submit** button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee**: Enter a brief description of the attendee. This field is not required.
* **Email**: Enter the attendee's email address. This field is required.
* **Required**: Check this box if you are a person who is required to fill out this form. This field is not required.
* **Name**: Enter your name. This field is not required.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This field is not required.
* **Email 2**: Enter any additional email address you want to validate. This field is optional.
* **Email 3**: Enter any additional email address you want to validate. This field is optional.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your form. This field is required.
