# Character Strengths Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to help participants reflect on their character strengths, values, and traits. It is designed to evaluate various aspects of a person's personality, including wisdom, courage, humanity, and transcendence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read each section carefully and answer the questions thoughtfully.
2. Enter your first and last name in the "Participant Full Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email Address" field.
4. Answer the questions in each section according to your thoughts and feelings.
5. For questions with numbers ratings, select a number from 1 to 7, with 1 being low and 7 being high.
6. For the "Confirmation of Survey Completion" field, select "I Confirm and Submit" to confirm that you have completed the survey thoughtfully and honestly.
7. If you have any additional notes or information, enter them in the "Additional Strengths Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full name, including your first and last names.
* **Participant Email Address**: Enter your email address, which will be used to send you the survey results and feedback.
* **Curiosity Self-Rating (5)**: How much do you enjoy exploring and discovering new things?
* **Love of Learning Rating (6)**: How much do you value acquiring new knowledge?
* **Critical Thinking Rating (7)**: How well do you analyze and evaluate information?
* **Bravery Self-Rating (9)**: How willing are you to face challenges or adversity?
* **Persistence Self-Rating (10)**: How well do you stay focused on long-term goals?
* **Integrity Self-Rating (8)**: How consistently do you act according to your values?
* **Love and Caring Rating (4)**: How much do you value close relationships and supporting others?
* **Kindness Self-Rating (5)**: How often do you perform acts of kindness for others?
* **Social Intelligence Rating (6)**: How well do you understand and navigate social situations?
* **Teamwork and Collaboration Rating (7)**: How well do you work with others toward a common goal?
* **Fairness and Justice Rating (8)**: How much do you value equal treatment and fair play?
* **Leadership Self-Rating (9)**: How well do you inspire and guide others?
* **Forgiveness Self-Rating (4)**: How easily do you let go of grudges or resentments?
* **Humility and Modesty Rating (6)**: How much do you value being humble and understated?
* **Prudence Self-Rating (8)**: How careful are you in making decisions and avoiding risks?
* **Appreciation of Beauty Rating (9)**: How much do you value excellence and beauty in the world?
* **Gratitude Self-Rating (5)**: How often do you feel and express thankfulness?
* **Hope and Optimism Rating (7)**: How much do you look forward to the future with confidence?
* **Confirmation of Survey Completion**: Confirm that you have completed the survey thoughtfully and honestly.
* **Date of Completion**: Enter the current date.
* **Additional Strengths Notes**: Enter any other relevant information or context.

Please note that this form is designed to be completed by participants who wish to reflect on their character strengths, values, and traits. It is not intended to be used for other purposes. Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
