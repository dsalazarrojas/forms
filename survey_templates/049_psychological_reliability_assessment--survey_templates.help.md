# Psychological Reliability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychological Reliability Assessment form is designed to gather information about an individual's mental state and coping mechanisms. It is likely used in settings where an individual's psychological well-being needs to be evaluated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each field and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
• **First Name** (text, required): Please provide your first name as it appears on your identification.

### Age
• **Age** (number, optional): Provide your age to give context to other fields.

### Contact Number
• **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter your contact number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.

### Email
• **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up or communication purposes.

### Date of Birth
• **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Provide your date of birth for age verification purposes.

### Time of Birth
• **Time of Birth** (time, optional): Provide your time of birth for age verification purposes. (Note: This field is less common, but some systems may request it.)

### Occupation
• **Occupation** (text, optional): Describe your job or current occupation to help contextualize the assessment results.

### Coping Mechanisms
• **Coping Mechanisms** (select multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple coping mechanisms you use to deal with stress or anxiety. Options include: 'Yes', 'No'.
• 
### Emotional Stability
• **Emotional Stability** (select one, optional): Indicate your emotional stability by selecting one option: 'Yes', 'No'.

### Notes
• **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or information that may be relevant to the assessment.

Tips: Please answer all fields honestly, and if you're unsure about any field, it's okay to ask for clarification. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
