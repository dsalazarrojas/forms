# Principle Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Principle Understanding Survey is a form designed to help teams assess their understanding of a specific principle and its implementation. It's meant to gather information on how well the team is aware of the principle, the training needed to grasp it, and the principle's impact on the team and other teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the principle and its purpose to ensure you understand what it's about.
* Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
* Choose the relevant options for each field, selecting one or multiple options depending on the type of question.
* Fill out all required fields, marked as "Required" in the field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is this principle about?** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This field is a note field where you can briefly describe the principle and its purpose.
* **How many team members are aware of this principle?** (`team_members_awareness`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more options that best describe the number of team members who are aware of this principle. Options include:
	+ Few
	+ Some
	+ Most
* **What kind of training do you need to understand this principle?** (`principle_training_needs`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option that best describes the kind of training you need to understand this principle. Options include:
	+ On-the-job training
	+ Classroom training
	+ Online training
	+ No training needed
* **Do you have any feedback for leadership on implementing this principle?** (`feedback_for_leadership`, `note`, required: false): This field is a note field where you can provide any feedback or suggestions to leadership on implementing this principle.
* **How has this principle been implemented in your team?** (`principle_implementations`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more options that best describe how this principle has been implemented in your team. Options include:
	+ Fully implemented
	+ Partially implemented
	+ Not yet implemented
* **What is the impact of this principle on your team?** (`principle_impact`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option that best describes the impact of this principle on your team. Options include:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ No impact
* **What benefits does this principle bring to your team?** (`principle_benefit`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more options that best describe the benefits this principle brings to your team. Options include:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ No benefits
* **What cost is associated with implementing this principle?** (`principle_cost`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more options that best describe the cost associated with implementing this principle. Options include:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ No cost
* **How does this principle impact other teams?** (`principle_impact_on_other_teams`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more options that best describe the impact of this principle on other teams. Options include:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ No impact

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields.
* Choose the options that best fit your team's situation.
* If you're unsure about any question, try to provide the best possible answer based on your understanding.
