# New Product Launch Consumer Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Product Launch Consumer Behavior Survey is designed to collect information about your thoughts, feelings, and preferences regarding a new product. Your responses will help us understand how to improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read each page carefully and fill out the information accurately and thoroughly.
2. For the "Demographics" page, select your response to the question "Have you ever purchased a product like this before?"
3. On the "Product" page, select all that apply if you have interest in our product.
4. On the "Behavior" page, please type your response to the question "What is your expected behavior when the product is launched?"
5. On the "Launch" page, choose a date from the calendar to indicate when you first learned about the product.
6. On the "Feedback" page, type any comments or suggestions you have about the product.
7. On the "Comments" page, type any additional comments or questions you have.
8. On the "Email" page, enter your email address to receive any follow-up information.
9. On the "Phone" page, enter your phone number if you'd like to receive follow-up calls.
10. On the "Rating" page, select "Good" if you have a positive experience with the product.
11. On the "Other" page, enter any other additional comments or information you'd like to share.
12. On the "Open-ended Question" page, answer the question "What would you like to see in the future from our company?"
13. On the "Open-ended Answers" page, type your response to the question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Demographics (Yes/No)** (`demographics_page`, select_one, required): This question is asking if you have ever purchased a product like this before.
* **Product (Yes/No)** (`product_page`, select_multiple, required): If you have any interest in our product, select all that apply.
* **Behavior (text)** (`behavior_page`, text, required): Please describe your expected behavior when our product is launched.
* **Launch (date)** (`launch_page`, date, required): When did you first learn about the product?
* **Feedback (text)** (`feedback_page`, text, required): Share any comments or suggestions you have about our product.
* **Comments (text)** (`comments_page`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please type them here.
* **Email (email)** (`email_page`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive any follow-up information.
* **Phone (text)** (`phone_page`, text, optional): If you'd like to receive follow-up calls, enter your phone number.
* **Rating (Good/Fair)** (`rating_page`, select_one, required): How would you rate your experience with our product?
* **Other (text)** (`other_page`, text, optional): Enter any other additional comments or information you'd like to share.
* **Open-ended Question (text)** (`open_ended_question_page`, text, required): What would you like to see in the future from our company?
* **Open-ended Answers (text)** (`open_ended_answers_page`, text, optional): Share your response to the question above.
