# Titanic Syndrome Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Titanic Syndrome Survey is a form designed to collect information about the symptoms, frequency of practice, and other relevant details related to the condition. This form will help identify potential issues and provide insight into the condition's progression.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of your last practice from the calendar.
2. Enter your full name.
3. Indicate the symptoms you have experienced, if any.
4. Specify how often you practice, if at all.
5. Describe your practice method.
6. Enter the time you spent practicing.
7. Enter the duration of practice.
8. Select the date of your last practice.
9. Describe any other symptoms you may be experiencing.
10. Provide any comments about your symptoms, if needed.
11. Enter the date of your previous practice.
12. Specify the method of your previous practice.
13. Enter the time spent on previous practice.
14. Select the symptoms you experienced previously.
15. Enter the name of your doctor.
16. Enter your doctor's phone number.
17. Enter your doctor's email.
18. Enter the department of your doctor.
19. Enter the name of the hospital.
20. Enter the address of the hospital.
21. Enter the phone number of the hospital.
22. Enter the email of the hospital.
23. Enter the department of the hospital.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Last Practice Date** (`practice_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your last practice.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the symptoms you have experienced, such as confusion, disorientation, difficulty concentrating, difficulty sleeping, dizziness, fatigue, headache, loss of interest, loss of speech, memory loss, muscle weakness, personality changes, ringing in ears, stomach upset, tingling in hands, vision loss.
* **Practice Frequency** (`practice_frequency`, `text`, optional): Specify how often you practice, if at all.
* **Method** (`practice_method`, `text`, required): Describe your practice method.
* **Practice Time** (`practice_time`, `number`, required): Enter the time you spent practicing.
* **Duration** (`practice_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of practice.
* **Last Practice Date** (`practice_date_last`, `date`, required): Select the date of your last practice.
* **Other Symptoms** (`symptoms_other`, `text`, optional): Describe any other symptoms you may be experiencing.
* **Please describe the symptoms** (`symptoms_description`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments about your symptoms.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Any comments about your symptoms.
* **Preceding Practice Date** (`practice_date_previously`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your previous practice.
* **Practice Frequency Preceding** (`practice_frequency_previously`, `text`, optional): Specify the frequency of your previous practice.
* **Method of practice preceding** (`practice_method_previously`, `text`, required): Describe the method of your previous practice.
* **Practice Time Preceding** (`practice_time_previously`, `number`, required): Enter the time spent on previous practice.
* **Symptoms Previously** (`symptoms_previously`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the symptoms you experienced previously.
* **Name of Doctor** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, `text`, optional): Enter your doctor's email.
* **Doctor Department** (`doctor_department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department of your doctor.
* **Name of hospital** (`hospital_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the hospital.
* **Address of hospital** (`hospital_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the hospital.
* **Phone number of hospital** (`hospital_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the hospital.
* **Email of hospital** (`hospital_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the email of the hospital.
* **Department of hospital** (`hospital_department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department of the hospital.
