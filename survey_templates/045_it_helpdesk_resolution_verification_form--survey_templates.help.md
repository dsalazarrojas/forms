# it_helpdesk_resolution_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The it_helpdesk_resolution_verification_form is used to verify the resolution of issues reported to the helpdesk. This form helps ensure that issues are resolved and customers are satisfied with the outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, please ensure you have completed any previous resolutions and that the issue is not being worked on by another technician. 

1. Provide feedback on the resolution in the `user_feedback` field.
2. Select the type of resolution that was made for the issue in the `resolution_type` field.
3. Add any additional notes on the resolution in the `resolution_notes` field.
4. Enter the date and time the issue was resolved in the `resolution_date` and `resolution_time` fields respectively.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the resolution in the `resolution_satisfaction` field.
6. Add any additional comments on the resolution in the `additional_comments` field.
7. Enter your contact information, including email address and phone number, in the `email_address` and `phone_number` fields respectively.
8. Select any follow-up actions that need to be taken in the `follow_up` field.
9. Select the technician that was assigned to work on the issue in the `assigned_technician` field.
10. Select the status of the issue resolution in the `resolution_status` field.
11. Choose any resolution categories that apply to the issue in the `resolution_category` field.
12. Select the priority of the issue resolution in the `resolution_priority` field.
13. Provide a detailed description of the resolution in the `resolution_description` field.
14. Select the type of resolution that was made for the issue in the `resolution_resolution` field.
15. Select the status update for the issue in the `resolution_status_update` field.
