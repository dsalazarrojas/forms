# treasury_forecast_confidence_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The treasury forecast confidence survey is designed to gauge the confidence of the current Chief Financial Officer (CFO) in the accuracy of the treasury forecast. This survey aims to capture the main reasons for the current level of confidence and identify potential improvement ideas for the forecasting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all relevant answers.
3. If you have an additional improvement idea that is not listed, please enter it in the "Additional Idea" field.
4. Once you have completed the form, click "Submit" to send your answers to the survey creators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (note, required: no):
	+ This is an introductory page that explains the purpose and instructions for the survey.
* **What is your current CFO confidence level?** (number, required: no):
	+ This question asks you to rate your current confidence level in the treasury forecast, on a scale of 1-10.
* **What is the main reason for your current CFO confidence level?** (select multiple, required: no):
	+ Select all the reasons that apply to your current confidence level. You can choose from:
		+ Accurate forecast
		+ Inaccurate forecast
		+ Uncertain forecast
		+ Other (please specify)
		+ Unsure
* **Which of the following are your top 3 risks?** (select multiple, required: no):
	+ Select up to 3 risks that you consider most important.
* **What improvement ideas do you have?** (text, required: no):
	+ This is a free-text field where you can enter any ideas you have for improving the treasury forecast.
* **Idea 1** (text, required: no):
	+ This is a secondary field where you can enter additional improvement ideas.
* **Do you have another idea?** (select one, required: no):
	+ If you have another improvement idea, select "True" and enter it in the "Additional Idea" field.
* **Additional Idea** (text, required: no):
	+ If you selected "True" for the previous question, enter your additional idea here.
* **Thank you** (note, required: no):
	+ This is a confirmation page that summarizes your answers. Review your responses before submitting the survey.
