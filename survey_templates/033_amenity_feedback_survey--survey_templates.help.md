# Amenity Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from residents and guests about our amenities, helping us to improve their quality and overall user experience. Your input will help us identify areas of strength and weakness, enabling us to make informed decisions and make necessary adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer options for each question based on your actual experience with our amenities.
2. For multiple-choice questions, choose the most accurate option from the available choices.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your thoughts and suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Section 1: Resident/Guest Information**
* **Resident/Guest Information**: This section is optional and helps us understand your affiliation with our community.
* **Length of Stay/Residency**: This question helps us understand how long you have been staying with us.
* **Unit/Building Number**: This question is optional and helps us track your specific unit or building.

### **Section 2: Amenity Usage**
* **Which Amenities Do You Use**: Select all the amenities you have used from the list below.
	+ Fitness Center/Gym
	+ Swimming Pool
	+ Community Room
	+ Business Center
	+ Library
	+ Outdoor Spaces/Parks
	+ Playground
	+ Tennis/Basketball Courts
	+ Spa/Sauna
	+ Clubhouse
	+ BBQ/Picnic Areas
	+ Walking Trails
	+ Pet Areas
	+ Other

### **Section 3: Amenity Ratings (Select One)**
* **Fitness Center**: Rate the condition of our fitness center equipment and cleanliness.
* **Swimming Pool**: Rate the condition, cleanliness, and operating hours of our swimming pool.
* **Community Room**: Rate the cleanliness and overall quality of our community room.
* **Outdoor Spaces**: Rate the quality of our outdoor spaces, such as parks and gardens.
* **Business Center**: Rate the quality and equipment of our business center.
* **Parking Facilities**: Rate the quality of our parking facilities.
* **Security Measures**: Rate the quality of our security measures.
* **Lighting in Common Areas**: Rate the lighting quality in our common areas.
* **Signage and Directions**: Rate the quality of our signage and directions.
* **Overall Amenity Cleanliness**: Rate the overall cleanliness of our amenities.
* **Maintenance Responsiveness**: Rate how responsive our maintenance team is to your requests.
* **Quality of Repairs**: Rate the quality of repairs to amenities and common areas.
* **Accessibility for Disabled Persons**: Rate the accessibility of our facilities for disabled persons.
* **Elevator Functionality**: Rate the functionality of our elevator, if applicable.
* **Amenity Operating Hours**: Rate the operating hours of our amenities.
* **Access Method**: Rate the ease of access to our amenities.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our amenities.
* **Value for Money**: Rate whether our amenities provide good value for the cost.
* **Recommend our Amenities**: Would you recommend our amenities to others?
* **Favorite Amenity**: What is your favorite amenity?
* **Least Favorite Amenity**: What is your least favorite amenity?
* **Improvements Needed**: What improvements would you like to see in our amenities?
* **New Amenities Suggested**: What new amenities would you like to see added?
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional feedback or suggestions you have.

### **Optional Fields**

* **Unit/Building Number**: Enter your unit or building number, if you wish to provide more specific feedback.
* **Email for Follow-up**: Provide your email address if you would like to receive follow-up questions or updates.
* **May We Contact You for Follow-up**: Choose 'True' if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up questions or updates.
* **Favorite Amenity**: Provide a specific amenity that you particularly enjoy.
* **Least Favorite Amenity**: Provide a specific amenity that you do not enjoy.
* **New Amenities Suggested**: Provide a specific amenity that you would like to see added.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have.
