# Event Attendee Experience Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Attendee Experience Assessment form is designed to gather feedback from event attendees. It is used to collect data about the attendee's experience, including their level of satisfaction, areas for improvement, and suggestions for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Event Attendee Experience Assessment" field with a brief description of your experience at the event.
2. Provide your answers to the questions about your experience.
3. Rate your experience on the "Event Attendee Rating Scale" using the options: Very Bad, Bad, Neutral, Good, or Very Good.
4. Suggest any improvements or ideas for future events.
5. Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you.
6. Provide your phone number for contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Attendee Experience Assessment** (`event_attendee_experience_assessment`): A brief description of your experience at the event. Please provide any comments, thoughts, or feelings you had during the event.
* **Event Attendee Answers** (`event_attendee_answers`): Please provide any answers to questions about your experience at the event. This is an open-ended field for any comments or feedback you may have.
* **Event Attendee Improvement** (`event_attendee_improvement`): Suggest any improvements or ideas you have for future events.
* **Event Attendee Rating** (`event_attendee_rating`): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is Very Bad and 5 is Very Good.
* **Event Attendee Suggestion** (`event_attendee_suggestion`): Suggest any ideas or improvements for future events.
* **Event Attendee Rating Scale** (`event_attendee_rating_scale`): Rate your experience on a scale of Very Bad, Bad, Neutral, Good, or Very Good.
* **Event Attendee Email** (`event_attendee_email`): Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you.
* **Event Attendee Telephone** (`event_attendee_telephone`): Enter your phone number for contact.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Provide any specific details about your experience that you think would be helpful to the event organizers.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas for future events, please share them with us.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact us at <insert contact information>.
