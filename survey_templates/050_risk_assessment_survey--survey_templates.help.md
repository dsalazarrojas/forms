# Risk Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Assessment Survey is a tool used to identify and evaluate potential risks in a workplace or similar setting. This form helps to gather information on hazards, risk assessment questions, risk evaluation, risk level, risk category, risk frequency, risk probability, and risk consequence. The data collected through this form will help in making informed decisions for risk management and ensuring a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Go through each page and answer the questions accordingly.
2.  For the "Workplace Hazards" page, describe any hazards you have identified in your workplace or similar setting.
3.  For the "Risk Assessment Questions" page, answer the questions regarding risk assessment in the space provided.
4.  On the "Evaluate Risks" page, select the appropriate options for the questions provided. Typically, select 'Yes' if the risk is present, and 'No' if it is not.
5.  On the "Risk Level" page, select the risk level for each identified hazard.
6.  On the "Risk Category" page, choose the category for the risk identified (Physical, Chemical, or Biological).
7.  On the "Risk Frequency" page, select the frequency with which you encounter the risk.
8.  On the "Risk Probability" page, enter a numerical value to assess the likelihood of the risk occurring.
9.  On the "Risk Consequence" page, describe the potential consequence of the identified risk in the space provided.

## Tips
*   Ensure you answer each question accurately and truthfully to provide reliable data for risk management.
*   If you're unsure about any of the questions, try to use your best judgment based on available information. 
*   If you encounter any issues during the submission process, contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
