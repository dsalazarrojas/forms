# Healthcare Employee Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from healthcare employees on their experiences and perceptions of their work environment. It aims to identify areas where employees feel supported and where improvements can be made.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all applicable answers for multiple-choice questions.
3. Use the text area to provide additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do patients say about our care?** (`patient_satisfaction`, text, required)
	+ Please describe your observations and experiences with patient satisfaction.
* **What do staff say about our care?** (`staff_satisfaction`, text, required)
	+ Describe your experiences and observations with staff satisfaction.
* **How do we communicate with patients?** (`communication`, text, required)
	+ Share your thoughts on how effectively we communicate with patients.
* **How well do you feel supported?** (`leadership_support`, text, required)
	+ Describe how you feel supported in your work environment.
* **What is your work life balance?** (`work_life`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel your work life balance is satisfactory, 'No' otherwise.
* **How satisfied are you with your team?** (`team_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all applicable answers that describe your level of satisfaction with your team.
* **How would you rate your manager's support?** (`manager_support`, select_one, optional)
	+ Rate your manager's support as 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **What are the biggest work life benefits that matter most to you?** (`work_life_benefits`, text, required)
	+ Describe the most important work life benefits for you.
* **How would you rate leaving the hospital on your days off?** (`leave_request`, date, required)
	+ Enter the date when you would like to take leave.
* **What does the patient care team do well?** (`patient_care_team`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select 'Yes' if the patient care team performs well, 'No' otherwise.
* **What does the staff care team do well?** (`staff_care_team`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all applicable answers that describe what the staff care team does well.
* **How do you rate the patient care team leadership?** (`patient_care_team_leadership`, select_one, optional)
	+ Rate the patient care team leadership as 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **How do you rate the staff care team leadership?** (`staff_care_team_leadership`, select_one, optional)
	+ Rate the staff care team leadership as 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **How would you rate the patient care team's skills?** (`patient_care_team_skills`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Rate the patient care team's skills as 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **How would you rate the staff care team's skills?** (`staff_care_team_skills`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Rate the staff care team's skills as 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **Patient Care Team Skills 2** (`patient_care_team_skills_2`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all applicable answers that describe the patient care team's skills.
* **What do you think about communication with your manager?** (`manager_communication`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel effectively communicated with, 'No' otherwise.
* **What do you think about communication with your team?** (`staff_communication`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel effectively communicated with, 'No' otherwise.
* **Staff Communication 2** (`staff_communication_2`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel effectively communicated with, 'No' otherwise.
* **Staff Communication 3** (`staff_communication_3`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel effectively communicated with, 'No' otherwise.
* **What do you think about communication with team?** (`staff_communication_4`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel effectively communicated with, 'No' otherwise.
* **Is there a way to improve communication with your team?** (`feedback`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select 'Yes' if there's room for improvement, 'No' otherwise.
* **Is there a way to improve communication with team?** (`feedback_2`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select 'Yes' if there's room for improvement, 'No' otherwise.
* **Is there a way to improve communication with staff?** (`feedback_3`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select 'Yes' if there's room for improvement, 'No' otherwise.
* **Feedback 4** (`feedback_4`, select_one, optional)

	+ Use this text area to provide any additional comments or feedback.
