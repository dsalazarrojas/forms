# Fitness Class Booking Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from fitness class attendees on their experience with the class, instructors, and facilities. The goal is to help the fitness studio or gym evaluate their services and make improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your experience with the class, including rating your instructors, class, and facilities.
2. Provide any additional feedback you may have about your experience.
3. Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you, if needed.
4. Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted for further feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructors** (`instructor_rating`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please rate your instructors' performance. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Class** (`class_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your experience with the class. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Facilities** (`facilities_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your experience with the facilities. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions about your experience with the class.
* **Email** (`email_input`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you, if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted for further feedback.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about your experience you would like to share.
