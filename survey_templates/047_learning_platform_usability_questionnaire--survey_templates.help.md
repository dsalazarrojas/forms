> # Learning Platform Usability Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Platform Usability Questionnaire is designed to gather user feedback on the usability of a learning platform. It assesses users' experiences with starting and navigating the platform, content quality, relevance, and overall satisfaction. By completing this form, you will be providing valuable input to help us improve the platform's usability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your experience on the learning platform.
2. Select the option that best describes your experience for each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you usually start a session on the platform (`survey_platform_use`)**: This question asks how you typically begin a session on the platform. Options include:
	+ Session Start: You usually click on a session link or button.
	+ Platform Home: You usually start on the platform's home page.
	+ Content Page: You usually land on a specific content page.
	+ Search Bar: You usually search for content on the platform.
	+ Link: You usually click on a link within the platform.
* **How do you usually find content on the platform (`survey_content_use`)**: This question asks how you usually locate content on the platform. Options include:
	+ Content Search: You usually search for content using the search bar.
	+ Content List: You usually browse a list of content on the platform.
	+ Recommendations: You usually view recommendations from the platform.
	+ Search Bar: You usually search for content using the search bar.
* **How do you usually navigate on the platform (`survey_navigation_use`)**: This question asks how you usually move around on the platform. Options include:
	+ Navigation Menu: You usually use the navigation menu to navigate.
	+ Search Bar: You usually use the search bar to find content.
	+ Content Page: You usually navigate through content pages.
	+ Links: You usually click on links within the platform.
* **What features do you usually use on the platform (`survey_platform_feature_use`)**: This question asks which features you usually use on the platform. Options include:
	+ Personalization: You usually use features that adapt to your interests.
	+ Recommendations: You usually view recommendations from the platform.
	+ Search Bar: You usually search for content using the search bar.
	+ Content Page: You usually view content pages.
* **What content quality do you usually expect on the platform (`survey_content_quality_use`)**: This question asks what quality of content you usually expect on the platform. Options include:
	+ High: You usually expect high-quality content.
	+ Medium: You usually expect medium-quality content.
	+ Low: You usually expect low-quality content.
* **How relevant is the content on the platform to your learning goals (`survey_content_relevance_use`)**: This question asks how relevant you find the content on the platform to your learning goals. Options include:
	+ Very Relevant: You usually find the content very relevant.
	+ Somewhat Relevant: You usually find the content somewhat relevant.
	+ Not at all: You usually do not find the content relevant.
* **What do you think about learning outcomes on the platform (`survey_learning_outcome_use`)**: This question asks about your satisfaction with learning outcomes on the platform. Options include:
	+ Strongly Agree: You usually strongly agree with learning outcomes.
	+ Somewhat Agree: You usually somewhat agree with learning outcomes.
	+ Neither Agree nor Disagree: You usually do not agree or disagree with learning outcomes.
	+ Strongly Disagree: You usually strongly disagree with learning outcomes.
* **How often do you complete sessions on the platform (`survey_completion_rate_use`)**: This question asks how often you complete sessions on the platform. Options include:
	+ Almost Always: You usually complete sessions almost every time.
	+ Often: You usually complete sessions often.

	+ Somewhat Satisfied: You usually have a somewhat positive experience.
