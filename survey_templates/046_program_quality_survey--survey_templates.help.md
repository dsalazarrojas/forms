# Program Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Program Quality Survey is designed to help us understand how well we're performing on our program. It's essential we hear from you to identify areas of improvement and what we do well.

## How To Complete This Form
Please select the pages that apply to the specific program you are surveying:
1. Page 1 - General Program Information
2. Page 2 - Program Goals and Objectives
3. Page 3 - Program Management and Organization
4. Page 4 - Program Operations and Resources
5. Page 5 - Program Risks and Challenges
6. Page 6 - Program Benefits and Outcomes
7. Page 7 - Program Improvement Suggestions
8. Page 8 - Additional Comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (page1_name, text, required): Enter the name of the program being surveyed.
* **Page 2 - Program Goals and Objectives** (page2_name, text, required): Describe the program's goals and objectives.
* **Page 3 - Program Management and Organization** (page3_name, text, required): Explain the program's organizational structure and management.
* **Page 4 - Program Operations and Resources** (page4_name, text, optional): Describe the program's operational procedures and available resources.
* **Page 5 - Program Risks and Challenges** (page5_name, text, optional): Identify any risks or challenges the program is facing.
* **Page 6 - Program Benefits and Outcomes** (page6_name, text, optional): Describe the program's benefits and outcomes.
* **Page 7 - Program Improvement Suggestions** (page7_name, text, optional): Provide suggestions for improving the program.
* **Page 8 - Additional Comments** (page8_name, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible for each page.
* Ensure you are providing feedback that is specific to the program being surveyed.
* If you have feedback for multiple pages, please fill out each page separately.
* You can fill out this form multiple times with different programs.
