## # evaluation_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The evaluation_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from participants after an event or program. This form helps to evaluate the quality and effectiveness of the event, identify areas of improvement, and inform future planning and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Evaluation Feedback Form Header** section, which provides a brief summary of the form.
2. Provide the name of the **Event Name** where you attended or participated in.
3. Enter the **Program Name** related to the event or program you're providing feedback on.
4. Select the date of the **Event Date** you attended or participated in.
5. Fill in your **Participant Name** for identification purposes.
6. Rate your **Feedback Score** on a scale from 1-5, indicating your overall satisfaction with the event or program.
7. Share your thoughts on what **went well** in the event or program.
8. Share your thoughts on what **did not go well** in the event or program.
9. Provide any **Additional Comments** you'd like to add to your feedback.
10. Suggest any **Suggestions** for improvement or future changes.
11. Enter your **Participant Email** for contact purposes.
12. Enter your **Participant Phone** for contact purposes.
13. Indicate the **Assigned Tool** used or relevant to the event or program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluation Feedback Form Header** (Text): A brief summary of the form.
* **Event Name** (Text): The name of the event or program you're providing feedback on.
* **Program Name** (Text): The name of the program related to the event or program.
* **Event Date** (Date): The date of the event or program you attended or participated in.
* **Participant Name** (Text): Your name for identification purposes.
* **Feedback Score** (Number): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event or program on a scale from 1-5.
* **What Went Well** (Text): Share your thoughts on what went well in the event or program.
* **What Didnt Go Well** (Text): Share your thoughts on what did not go well in the event or program.
* **Additional Comments** (Text): Provide any additional comments or thoughts on the event or program.
* **Suggestions** (Text): Suggest any improvements or changes for future events or programs.
* **Participant Email** (Email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text): Indicate the tool or resource assigned to you for the event or program.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback to help improve future events or programs.
* Ensure your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for clarification before proceeding.
* This form is for your feedback only, and your input is appreciated and valued.
