## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the overall direction of the market and expectations for various quarters. It is used to help forecasters understand the confidence levels and expectations of users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For each question, select the relevant option that best represents your answer.
3. If you are unsure or do not have an opinion on a particular question, select "Not at All Confident" or "No" as a placeholder.
