# Virtual Pre Conference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Pre Conference Survey is designed to help organizers and attendees gauge the interest and expectations of participants for an upcoming virtual conference. It will help in preparing for the conference by gathering information on attendee attendance, any physical conditions that might impact participation, and any additional thoughts or concerns that attendees may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the survey link to access the form.
2. Answer each question carefully, selecting the relevant options or entering your thoughts and comments.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Will you attend the conference?
* **Will you attend the conference?** (`attendance_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
This question asks about your plans for attending the conference. Select all the options that apply to you:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Physical Conditions
* **Physical Conditions** (`physical_conditions`, `text`, required: false)
Please describe any physical conditions that might affect your participation, such as mobility issues or other health considerations.

### Further Thoughts
* **Further Thoughts** (`further_thoughts`, `text`, required: false)
If you have any additional comments or concerns that are not covered in the other questions, please share them here.
