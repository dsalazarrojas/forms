# E Bike Customer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from customers about the E Bike service. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and where we need to improve.

1. Please fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For the "e_bike_product_rating" field, rate your experience with E Bike on a scale of 1-5.

5. In the "e_bike_improvement" field, type any suggestions or comments you have for how E Bike could improve.
6. Please enter your email address in the "e_bike_email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "e_bike_phone" field.

9. In the "e_bike_email_confirmation" field, re-enter your email address to confirm it's correct.
10. In the "e_bike_phone_confirmation" field, re-enter your phone number to confirm it's correct.
11. For the "e_bike_agree" field, select "True" if you agree with our terms and conditions, "False" if you don't, or "Decline" if you're unsure.
12. In the "e_bike_terms_and_conditions" field, provide any additional comments or feedback about our terms and conditions.
13. In the "e_bike_additional_comments" field, provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with E Bike.
14. Please select whether you want to upload any files or not in the "e_bike_uploads" field.
15. In the "e_bike_upload_uploads" field, select whether you want to upload files or not.

17. In the "e_bike_upload" field, select whether you want to upload files or not.

21. In the "e_bike_additional_comments_uploads" field, provide any additional comments or feedback about uploading files.
22. In the "e_bike_agree_terms_uploads" field, select "True" if you agree with our terms for uploading, "False" if you don't, or "Decline" if you're unsure.
23. In the "e_bike_terms_and_conditions_uploads" field, provide any additional comments or feedback about our terms and conditions for uploading.
