# food_delivery_app_usability_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and user experience from customers about the food delivery application. It helps us understand how users perceive our app's usability and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide valuable feedback, follow these steps:

*   Complete all the questions to the best of your ability.
*   Choose the correct rating or answer for each question based on your genuine experience with the app.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *What do you think of our app?* 
    This is a text field where you can describe your overall impression of the app.
*   *Rate our food app* 
    This is a rating field where you can rate the app on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   *How would you rate the quality of our food?* 
    This is a number field where you can rate the quality of food on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   *How would you rate the ordering process?* 
    This is a rating field where you can rate the ordering process on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   *Food quality rating* 
    This is a number field where you can rate the quality of food on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   *How often do you order from us?* 
    This is a multiple-select field where you can choose how often you order from us (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, or never).
*   *How many days do you order from us?* 
    This is a number field where you can specify how many days you order from us in a month.
*   *How much money do you spend in our app in a month?* 
    This is a number field where you can specify the average amount you spend in our app in a month.
*   *How would you rate our customer service?* 
    This is a rating field where you can rate the customer service on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   *Do you have any comments or suggestions?* 
    This is a text field where you can provide any comments or suggestions you have about the app.

## Tips
- Please be honest and genuine in your feedback.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please describe them in detail.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, please feel free to share them.
