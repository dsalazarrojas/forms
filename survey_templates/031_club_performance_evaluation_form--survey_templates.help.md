# Club Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Performance Evaluation Form is a tool for evaluating a club's performance in various areas, providing a comprehensive assessment of its progress towards its goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have access to relevant information about the club and its context.
2. Check that the club has not already undergone a similar evaluation recently.
3. Fill out the form with the required information, making sure to answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, required): Enter your name or the name of the person conducting the evaluation.
* **Evaluator Role** (`evaluator_role`, text, required): Describe your position within the club.
* **Club Name** (`club_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the club being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, text, required): Specify the start and end dates of the evaluation period.
* **Member Attendance Rate** (`member_attendance`, number, optional): Enter the percentage of meetings attended by members (if applicable).
* **Achievement of Club Goals** (`goals_achievement`, select_one, required): Rate the club's achievement of its goals, selecting from:
	+ Far Below Expectations
	+ Below Expectations
	+ Meets Expectations
	+ Exceeds Expectations
	+ Far Exceeds Expectations
* **Member Engagement and Participation** (`member_engagement`, select_one, required): Rate member engagement and participation, selecting from:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Quality of Leadership** (`leadership_quality`, select_one, required): Rate leadership effectiveness, selecting from:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Quality of Events and Activities** (`event_execution`, select_one, optional): Rate event execution, selecting from:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Financial Management** (`financial_management`, select_one, required): Rate financial management, selecting from:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate internal and external communication, selecting from:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Club Growth and Expansion** (`growth_metrics`, text, optional): Enter information about membership growth or expansion (if applicable).
* **Key Strengths** (`strengths`, text, required): Identify what the club is doing well.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, required): Identify areas that need improvement.
* **Recommendations for Next Period** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Suggest recommendations for future success (if applicable).

Note: The following fields are optional and may not be applicable in your evaluation: **Member Attendance Rate**, **Quality of Events and Activities**, **Club Growth and Expansion**.
