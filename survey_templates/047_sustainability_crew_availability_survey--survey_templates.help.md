# Sustainability Crew Availability Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about which crew members are available for sustainability initiatives. It helps us keep track of who is available and who needs to be asked to volunteer. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "Interested In" to indicate if you are interested in volunteering for sustainability initiatives.
2. If you are interested, select the date(s) you are available on the "Date" field.
3. Choose your availability time slots in the "Availability Time Slots" field.
4. Indicate your availability for sustainability initiatives on the "Sustainability Interest" field.
5. Confirm that you are available for coordination by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Coordination Consent" field.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information**: Enter any relevant information about yourself, such as your name and contact information.
* **Interested In**: Indicate if you are interested in volunteering for sustainability initiatives. Select "Yes" or "No" to answer this question.
* **Date**: If you selected "Yes" in the previous question, select the date(s) you are available.
* **Availability Time Slots**: Choose the specific time slots you are available.
* **Sustainability Interest**: Indicate your interest in sustainability initiatives.
* **Availability Time Slots**: Confirm that you have chosen specific time slots.
* **Coordination Consent**: Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you are available for coordination.
* **Form Submitted**: This field is a note to indicate that the form has been submitted.
* **Page Six to Page Fifteen**: These fields are for additional comments or notes. You can add any relevant information here.
