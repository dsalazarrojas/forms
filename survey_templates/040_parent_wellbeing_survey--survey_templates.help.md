# Parent Wellbeing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Wellbeing Survey is designed to assess the well-being of parents in various aspects of their lives, including their relationships with others, overall well-being, concerns, free time activities, sleep patterns, exercise habits, and dietary habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best fit your situation. Each question is optional, so feel free to answer only what you feel comfortable sharing. If you're not sure about any of the questions, you can also select 'Undecided' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How often do parents get together** (`survey_question_1`, number, optional): This question is about how frequently you interact with other parents. Select a number that represents how often you meet or communicate with other parents.
- **What is the quality of your relationship with other parents** (`survey_question_2`, select_one, optional): This question asks about the quality of your relationships with other parents. Select the answer that best describes your experience.
- **How would you rate your overall well-being** (`survey_question_3`, number, optional): This question is about your overall sense of well-being. Select a number that reflects your current well-being level.
- **Do you have any concerns** (`survey_question_4`, select_multiple, optional): This question is about any concerns you may have. Select all that apply from the options provided.
- **What is your concern** (`survey_question_5`, text, optional): This question is for you to elaborate on any concerns you selected in the previous question.
- **How do you usually spend your free time** (`survey_question_6`, select_multiple, optional): This question is about how you typically spend your free time. Select all that apply from the options provided.
- **What activities do you usually do in your free time** (`survey_question_7`, select_multiple, optional): This question is a follow-up to the previous one, where you can specify which activities you enjoy doing in your free time.
- **Do you usually get enough time to rest** (`survey_question_8`, select_multiple, optional): This question is about whether you feel you get enough rest.
- **What time do you usually go to bed** (`survey_question_9`, time, optional): This question is about your sleep schedule.
- **What time do you usually wake up** (`survey_question_10`, time, optional): This question is a follow-up to the previous one, about your wake-up time.
- **Do you usually exercise regularly** (`survey_question_11`, select_multiple, optional): This question is about your exercise habits.
- **How long do you exercise** (`survey_question_12`, number, optional): This question is about the duration of your exercise routine.
- **Do you usually eat well** (`survey_question_13`, select_multiple, optional): This question is about your eating habits.
- **Do you usually drink enough water** (`survey_question_14`, select_multiple, optional): This question is about your hydration habits.

Note: Remember to answer based on your genuine experience and feelings. If you need help or clarification, feel free to reach out for assistance.
