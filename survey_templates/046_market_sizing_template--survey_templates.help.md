# market_sizing_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for market sizing templates. The purpose of this form is to gather market sizing information for a particular market or product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields and ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each field as accurately as possible based on your knowledge of the market and product.
3. Make sure to check the required fields as they are marked.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Market_Sizing_Template** (1, text, optional): This is a placeholder title field. You can enter a descriptive title for your market sizing template.
* **Market Growth Rate** (2, text, optional): Enter the expected growth rate for the market or product. This will help inform future planning and decision-making.
* **Market Segment** (3, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options provided to indicate the market segment for your product or service.
* **Select Market Segment** (4, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply for the market segments relevant to your product or service. This field is used to gather more detailed information about the market segments.
* **Market Potential** (5, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for the market potential. This will help inform decisions about resource allocation and investment.
* **Market Entry Cost** (6, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for the market entry cost. This will help inform decisions about cost-benefit analysis.
* **Market Entry Barriers** (7, text, optional): Enter any obstacles or challenges that may hinder the market entry of your product or service.
* **Market Entry Barriers Details** (8, text, optional): Provide more detailed information about the market entry barriers. This will help identify potential areas for improvement.
* **Marketing Strategy** (9, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options provided to indicate the marketing strategy for your product or service.
* **Market Sizing** (10, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply for the market sizing relevant to your product or service.
* **Revenue Potential** (11, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for the revenue potential. This will help inform decisions about investment and growth.
* **Total Revenue** (12, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for the total revenue. This will help inform decisions about profit and loss statements.
* **Growth Rate** (13, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for the growth rate. This will help inform decisions about future projections and growth plans.
