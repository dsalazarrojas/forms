# employment_engagement_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employment engagement survey is designed to gather feedback from employees about their current job satisfaction, work-life balance, and overall happiness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions as honestly as possible.
2. For each question, select the most accurate answer from the provided options.
3. Be sure to include any additional comments or feedback in the "additional_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **overall_happiness (Very Happy, Neutral, Very Unhappy)**: Rate your overall happiness with your current job.
* **satisfaction_level (number)**: Indicate your current level of job satisfaction, with higher numbers indicating higher satisfaction.
* **work_life_balance (Very Good, Neutral, Very Bad)**: Rate your current work-life balance.
* **perception (time)**: This field is intended for employees to provide feedback on their perception of their work hours or schedule. Please enter a value in the format "HH:MM", e.g., "08:00".
* **email**: Enter your work email address.
* **phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **additional_comments (note)**: Include any additional comments or feedback you may have about your job or work environment.
