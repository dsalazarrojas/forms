# Weight Loss Journey Testimonial Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects testimonials from weight loss program participants to gather information about their weight loss journey. It is used to evaluate the effectiveness of our programs and create engaging content for our marketing efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2.  Enter your email address for verification and follow-up purposes.
3.  Select the name of the weight loss program you participated in.
4.  Enter your weight loss progress as a number (e.g., 10 lbs, 20 lbs, etc.).
5.  Select whether you had "Before" photos taken before starting the program (e.g., a photo taken before the program began).
6.  Select whether you had "After" photos taken after completing the program (e.g., a photo taken after your weight loss progress was noticed).
7.  Share your personal story or testimonial about your weight loss journey.
8.  Indicate your permission to share your story on social media or other marketing materials (yes, no, or other, and specify "other" if you'd like).
9.  If you selected "other" for sharing permission, specify your preferred social media platform or other medium for sharing your story.
10.  Enter the name of the clinic or location where you attended the weight loss program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email** (email): Enter your email address for verification and follow-up purposes.
*   **Program Name** (text): Select the name of the weight loss program you participated in.
*   **Weight Loss** (number): Enter your weight loss progress as a number (e.g., 10 lbs, 20 lbs, etc.).
*   **Before Photos** (select multiple): Select whether you had "Before" photos taken before starting the program.
*   **After Photos** (select multiple): Select whether you had "After" photos taken after completing the program.
*   **Testimonial** (text): Share your personal story or testimonial about your weight loss journey.
*   **Share Permission** (select one): Indicate your permission to share your story on social media or other marketing materials.
*   **Other Share Permission** (text): If you selected "other" for sharing permission, specify your preferred social media platform or other medium for sharing your story.
*   **Clinic Name** (text): Enter the name of the clinic or location where you attended the weight loss program.
