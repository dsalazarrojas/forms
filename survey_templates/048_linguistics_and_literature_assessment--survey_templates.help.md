# Linguistics And Literature Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's proficiency in languages and literatures, providing a comprehensive overview of their skills and knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Language Background** (`language_background`, text, optional): This field is a free-text field where you can provide details about your language skills and proficiency.
*   **Literature Preferences** (`literature_preferences`, select multiple, optional): Select the options that best match your interests in literature.
*   **Language Skills** (`language_skills`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value indicating your level of proficiency in languages.
*   **Language Interests** (`language_interests`, select multiple, optional): Select the options that best match your interests in languages.
*   **Education Level** (`education_level`, select one, optional): Select the option that best describes your level of education.
*   **Years of Education** (`years_of_education`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value indicating your level of education.
*   **Language Programs** (`language_programs`, select multiple, optional): Select the options that best match your participation in language programs.
*   **Language Certifications** (`language_certifications`, select multiple, optional): Select the options that best match your language certifications.
*   **Literature Programs** (`literature_programs`, select multiple, optional): Select the options that best match your participation in literature programs.
*   **Literature Certifications** (`literature_certifications`, select multiple, optional): Select the options that best match your literature certifications.
*   **Native Language** (`native_language`, select one, optional): Select the option that best describes your native language.
*   **Other Language** (`language_other`, text, optional): Provide details about any other language you are proficient in.
*   **Other Native Language** (`native_language_other`, text, optional): Provide details about your other native language.
*   **Other Language Description** (`language_other_description`, text, optional): Provide details about your other language proficiency.
*   **Other Literature** (`literature_other`, text, optional): Provide details about your other literature interests.
*   **Other Literature Description** (`literature_other_description`, text, optional): Provide details about your other literature interests.
*   **Language Native Language** (`language_native_language`, select one, optional): Select the option that best describes your native language.
*   **Other Language** (`language_native_language_other`, text, optional): Provide details about any other language you are proficient in.

## Tips

*   Please provide accurate and truthful information about your language and literature skills.
*   If you are unsure about any field, you can consult the provided options or ask for help.
*   Make sure to complete all fields that are marked as required.
*   If you have any other language or literature skills, please provide details in the corresponding fields.
