# Organizational Fairness Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand employees' perceptions of their organization's fairness and transparency, gathering insights to help identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly, reflecting your genuine opinions about your organization.
2. Choose the response that best reflects your feelings and experiences.
3. Fill in your email address and phone number (if you're comfortable sharing them).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organizational Fairness** (Section 1):
	+ This section is a note field where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments about your organization's fairness.
* **How often do you think your organization is transparent about its decision-making process?** (Question 1)
	+ Select the response that best reflects your experience and perceptions about your organization's decision-making transparency.
	+ Options: Always, Most of the time, Sometimes, Rarely, Never
* **How likely are you to recommend our organization as a good employer?** (Question 2)
	+ Choose the response that best reflects your confidence in recommending your organization to others.
	+ Options: Extremely Likely, Likely, Neutral, Not Likely, Not at All Likely
* **How would you rate our communication style in terms of openness and honesty?** (Question 3)
	+ Select the response that best reflects your experience with your organization's communication style.
	+ Options: Very Open and Honest, Somewhat Open and Honest, Neutral, Somewhat Closed and Dishonest, Very Closed and Dishonest
* **How satisfied are you with your manager or supervisor?** (Question 4)
	+ Choose the response that best reflects your satisfaction with your manager or supervisor.
	+ Options: Extremely Satisfied, Very Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Extremely Dissatisfied
* **How likely are you to suggest new ideas to your manager or supervisor?** (Question 5)
	+ Select the response that best reflects your willingness to suggest ideas to your manager or supervisor.
	+ Options: Extremely Likely, Likely, Neutral, Not Likely, Not at All Likely
* **How important is it for you to know the reasoning behind decisions made at work?** (Question 6)
	+ Choose the response that best reflects the importance of understanding the reasoning behind decisions.
	+ Options: Extremely Important, Very Important, Somewhat Important, Not Very Important, Not at All Important
* **How satisfied are you with your compensation?** (Question 7)
	+ Select the response that best reflects your satisfaction with your compensation.
	+ Options: Extremely Satisfied, Very Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Extremely Dissatisfied
* **How satisfied are you with your benefits?** (Question 8)
	+ Choose the response that best reflects your satisfaction with your benefits.
	+ Options: Extremely Satisfied, Very Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Extremely Dissatisfied
* **How likely are you to stay with our organization?** (Question 9)
	+ Select the response that best reflects your intention to stay with the organization.
	+ Options: Extremely Likely, Likely, Neutral, Not Likely, Not at All Likely
* **What is your role in the organization?** (Question 10)
	+ Choose the response that best reflects your role within the organization.
	+ Options: Manager or Supervisor, Team Member, HR or Recruiter, Other (please specify)
* **Email Address** (Field 11)
	+ If you're comfortable sharing it, enter your email address for feedback or follow-up purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Field 12)
	+ If you're comfortable sharing it, enter your phone number for feedback or follow-up purposes.
