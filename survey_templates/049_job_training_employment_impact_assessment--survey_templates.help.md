# job_training_employment_impact_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Training Employment Impact Assessment form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of job training programs on employment outcomes. This form collects data on job placement, retention, and outcomes to help administrators and managers identify areas for improvement and inform future training initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on your knowledge of the job training program and its outcomes.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Training Outcomes**: This field asks you to select all outcomes that apply to the job training program. Please choose the options that best describe the outcomes of the program.
* **Training Participation**: This field is used to describe your level of participation in the job training program. Enter your response in the text field.
* **Job Retention**: This field asks for a numeric value representing the job retention rate. Enter a whole number.
* **Employment Status**: This field is used to select the current employment status of the participants. Choose between "Active" and "Inactive".
* **Employment Outcomes**: This field is similar to "Job Training Outcomes" and asks you to select all outcomes that apply to the employment outcomes of the program.
* **Job Training Employment Impact**: This field asks for a numeric value representing the job training employment impact. Enter a whole number.
* **Job Training Outcomes 1-5**: These fields are identical to "Job Training Outcomes" and ask you to select all outcomes that apply to each specific job training outcome.
* **Job Placement**: This field is used to describe your job placement experiences with the training program. Enter your response in the text field.
* **Job Search Status**: This field is used to select the current job search status of the participants. Choose between "Active" and "Inactive".
* **Job Placement Type**: This field is used to select the type of job placement achieved through the training program. Choose between "Yes" and "No".
* **Job Status 1-7**: These fields are used to select the status of the job for each outcome. Choose between "Active" and "Inactive".
* **Job Placement Outcomes**: This field is used to select all outcomes that apply to the job placement of the program.
* **Employment Outcome Status**: This field is used to select the status of the employment outcome. Choose between "Active" and "Inactive".
* **Job Training Outcome Status**: This field is used to select the status of the job training outcome. Choose between "Active" and "Inactive".
* **Training Outcome Status**: This field is used to select the status of the training outcome. Choose between "Active" and "Inactive".
