# Cosmetic_Surgery_Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and satisfaction data from patients who have undergone cosmetic surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions as instructed. You may choose from the options provided for each question, or type in any additional comments or concerns you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience** (`1`): Please rate your overall experience with the surgery. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Patient Satisfaction** (`2`): Rate your satisfaction with the outcome of the surgery. Choose from Highly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Highly Dissatisfied.
* **Expectations** (`3`): What were your expectations before the surgery? Please provide any relevant information or comments.
* **On-Time Performance** (`4`): Was the surgery performed on time? Choose True or False.
* **Surgeon Expertise** (`5`): Rate the surgeon's expertise. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Recommend** (`6`): Would you recommend the surgery to others? Choose True or False.
* **Rating** (`7`): Rate your overall rating of the surgery out of 5.
* **Complications** (`8`): Were there any unexpected complications during the surgery? Choose True or False.
* **Recovery** (`9`): Rate your satisfaction with the recovery process. Choose from Highly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Highly Dissatisfied.
* **Post-Surgery Care** (`10`): Was the post-operative care adequate? Choose True or False.
* **Meet Expectations** (`11`): Did the surgery meet your expectations? Choose True or False.
* **Notes** (`12`): Please provide any additional comments or concerns.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them in the Notes field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
