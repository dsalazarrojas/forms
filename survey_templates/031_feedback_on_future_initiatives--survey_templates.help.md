# Feedback On Future Initiatives - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and input from end-users on proposed programs and future initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, please select the "Proposed Program" field and enter the name of the program you would like to provide feedback on.
2. Next, select the "Proposed Program Priority" field and choose the level of priority you assign to the program (Low, Medium, or High).
3. In the "Description" field, provide a brief description of the program.
4. Select the "Target Date" field and enter the date by which you would like to see the program implemented.
5. If applicable, enter your contact email and phone number in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
6. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional comments or feedback you have on the program.
7. If you are interested in being contacted about the proposed services associated with the program, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Proposed Services" field.
8. If you have specific services that you would like to prioritize, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Services Priority" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proposed Program (text)**: Enter the name of the proposed program you would like to provide feedback on.
* **Proposed Program Priority (select_one)**: Choose the level of priority you assign to the program (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Description (text)**: Provide a brief description of the program, including its objectives and goals.
* **Target Date (date)**: Enter the date by which you would like to see the program implemented.
* **Contact Email (email)**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about the program.
* **Contact Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about the program.
* **Comments (note)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have on the program.
* **Proposed Services (select_multiple)**: If you are interested in being contacted about the proposed services associated with the program, select "Yes".
* **Services Priority (select_one)**: If you have specific services that you would like to prioritize, select "Yes" or "No".
* **Submit (text)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have on the program and submit the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your feedback is submitted only once to avoid duplication of effort.
* Provide as much detail as possible in each field to ensure that your feedback is actionable and informative.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can reach out to the form administrators for guidance.
