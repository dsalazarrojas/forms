# Library Services Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather information about your experience with our library services. Your feedback will help us understand what we're doing well and where we need to improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each page carefully and thoughtfully. You can review the pages below to ensure you're providing the most useful information.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us. We're here to help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### page_1
 * **What were your experiences with our library services?** (`What were your experiences with our library services?`, `text`, required): 
   We want to know about your experiences with our library services. Please describe your visit, including any successes or challenges you encountered. Be as detailed as possible, and try to include specific dates and times if you can.

### page_2
 * **How friendly were the library staff?** (`How friendly were the library staff?`, `select_multiple`, required): 
   Rate how friendly you found our staff to be during your visit. You can select one or more of the following options:
   - Excellent
   - Good
   - Satisfactory
   - Poor

### page_3
 * **How much time did you spend at the library?** (`How much time did you spend at the library?`, `time`, required): 
   We're interested in knowing how long you spent at the library. Please enter the exact time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM` or `HH:MM 24-hour format`.

### page_4
 * **Do you have any suggestions for improving our library services?** (`Do you have any suggestions for improving our library services?`, `text`, optional): 
   If you have any ideas or suggestions for how we can improve our library services, please share them here. This is your chance to let us know how we can better serve you and our community.

### page_5
 * **Can we contact you?** (`Can we contact you?`, `email`, required): 
   We need to know if we can contact you for follow-up or additional information. Please enter your email address, and we'll be in touch.

### page_6
 * **What do you expect from our library in the future?** (`What do you expect from our library in the future?`, `text`, optional): 
   If you have any expectations or requests for our services in the future, please share them here. This will help us understand how to serve you better.

## Tips

- Be as detailed and specific as possible when providing feedback.
- If you have already provided feedback, try to think of any new experiences or changes you've noticed since your last visit.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out.
