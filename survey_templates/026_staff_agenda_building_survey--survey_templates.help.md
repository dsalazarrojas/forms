# staff_agenda_building_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect feedback from staff members on their meeting priorities, meeting scheduling, team leads, agenda planning, and manager leads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the meeting priorities that are relevant to you. You can choose one or more of the options provided.
2. Indicate your meeting scheduling preferences.
3. Choose the team lead(s) you work with.
4. Input your thoughts on agenda planning (if required).
5. Select the manager lead(s) you work with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meeting Priorities
* **Meeting Priorities** (`meeting_priorities`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the priorities that are most important to you when attending meetings, such as 'Yes' for priorities that are crucial and 'No' for those that are not.

### Meeting Scheduling
* **Meeting Scheduling** (`meeting_scheduling`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your preferred scheduling options, such as 'Yes' for scheduled meetings and 'No' for impromptu meetings.

### Team Leads
* **Team Leads** (`team_leads`, select_one, required/optional): Select the team lead(s) you primarily work with.

### Agenda Planning
* **Agenda Planning** (`agenda_planning`, number, required/optional): This field is likely for numerical input about the agenda planning process, if applicable.

### Manager Leads
* **Manager Leads** (`manager_leads`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the manager lead(s) you report to.
