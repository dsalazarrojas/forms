# Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clearance Form is used to gather information necessary for clearance requests within the organization. It is designed to collect personal and background information, as well as details about the type of clearance requested. This form is essential for ensuring that all necessary steps are taken to grant clearance, and it helps to streamline the clearance process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please start by filling out the **Personal Information** section, which includes:
    *   **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your identification.
    *   **Employee ID**: Enter your employee or identification number as per your organization's records.
    *   **Department**: Enter the department or division you belong to within the organization.
    *   **Position Title**: Enter your job title within the organization.
    *   **Email Address**: Enter your work or personal email address.
    *   **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
2.  Next, move on to the **Clearance Request** section, which includes:
    *   **Type of Clearance Requested**: Select the type of clearance you need, such as Background Check Clearance, Security Clearance, Financial Clearance, etc.
    *   **Clearance Level Required**: Select the level of clearance you require, such as Standard, Enhanced, Top Secret, etc.
    *   **Purpose of Clearance**: Describe the reason why you need the clearance.
    *   **Requested Clearance Start Date**: Enter the date you need the clearance to start.
3.  If you need to provide **Background Information**, answer the following:
    *   **Do you have any criminal history?**: Select 'True' if you have a criminal history, and 'False' otherwise.
    *   **If Yes, Please Provide Details**: If you have a criminal history, describe the type and date of conviction.
    *   **Are there any outstanding warrants against you?**: Select 'True' if you have outstanding warrants, and 'False' otherwise.
    *   **Outstanding financial or credit issues?**: Select 'True' if you have outstanding financial or credit issues, and 'False' otherwise.
    *   **If Yes, Please Provide Details**: If you have outstanding financial or credit issues, describe the details.
4.  In the **Medical and Health Information** section, answer the following:
    *   **Medical clearance required for this position?**: Select 'True' if medical clearance is required, and 'False' otherwise.
    *   **Physician Name**: If medical clearance is required, enter the name of your healthcare provider.
    *   **Any health conditions that could affect work?**: Select 'True' if you have health conditions that could affect your work, and 'False' otherwise.
    *   **Health Condition Details**: If you have health conditions that could affect your work, describe the details.
5.  Finally, in the **Verification and Agreement** section:
    *   **I certify that all information provided is true and accurate**: Select 'I Certify' to confirm that all information is true and accurate.
    *   **I consent to a background check and investigation**: Select 'I Consent' to agree to a background check and investigation.
    *   **Additional Information or Explanation**: Provide any additional information or explanation that may be relevant to your clearance request.
    *   **Submission Date**: The current date will be filled in automatically.
