# Bike MS Curbside Pick Up Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bike MS Curbside Pick Up Feedback form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the curbside pick up service. This form helps us understand their satisfaction and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer the questions based on your personal experience with the curbside pick up service.
2. Select the relevant options for each question to provide clear feedback.
3. Ensure you provide your contact information so that we can follow up with you regarding your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Curbside Pick Up Location (id: 2)**: Select the location from the options where you picked up your items. Choose the location that matches your experience.
* **How long did you wait for your items? (id: 3)**: Choose the time frame that best describes how long you waited for your items to be ready for pick up.
* **Staff Friendliness Rating (id: 4)**: Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of the staff that assisted you during the pick up process. Use the provided numbers to indicate the level of friendliness.
* **How efficient was the pick up process? (id: 5)**: Choose the option that best describes the efficiency of the pick up process.
* **Were all your items correct and present? (id: 6)**: Select the option that accurately represents your experience with regards to the items you received.
* **Did you feel safety protocols were followed? (id: 7)**: Choose the option that reflects whether you felt safety protocols were followed during the pick up process.
* **What is one thing we could do to improve the curbside experience? (id: 8)**: Provide a specific suggestion for improvement, keeping in mind that this is optional but will help us better understand your feedback.
* **Overall Satisfaction (id: 9)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the curbside pick up experience. Choose the option that best represents your level of satisfaction.
* **Would you use curbside pick up again? (id: 10)**: Select the option that best reflects your willingness to use the service again.
* **May we contact you regarding your feedback? (id: 11)**: If you would be willing to be contacted, select this option. We will follow up with you regarding your feedback.
* **Name (Optional) (id: 12)**: If you would like to provide your name for follow-up purposes, you can enter it here.
* **Email Address (Optional) (id: 13)**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback. This is optional but will allow us to follow up with you directly.

Please note that providing your email address is not mandatory, and you can choose to leave it blank if you prefer not to share it. However, it will help us follow up with you regarding your feedback and any improvements we will implement based on your suggestions.
