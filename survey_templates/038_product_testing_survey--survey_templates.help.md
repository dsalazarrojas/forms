# product_testing_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect product testing feedback from customers, helping us to evaluate the usability and overall satisfaction of a product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Enter the product title and provide a brief description of the product.
3. Rate the product on a scale of 1-5.
4. Answer the feedback questions, providing as much detail as possible.
5. Indicate whether or not you recommend the product.
6. Specify your design preferences, including whether the product should be minimalist and its color scheme.
7. Enter any additional product information or feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and the questions that follow.
* **product info**: A text field for entering a brief description of the product.
* **Feedback questions**: A text field for answering any feedback questions related to the product.
* **Product rating**: A numerical field where you rate the product on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product review**: A text field for providing a more detailed review of the product.
* **Product recommendation**: A select option to indicate whether or not you recommend the product.
* **Minimalist**: A select option to indicate whether or not the product should have a minimalist design.
* **color_scheme**: A select option to specify the color scheme of the product.
* **Product info 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10**: Text fields for entering additional product information.
* **Product image, URL, and description**: Text fields for entering the product image, URL, and description.
* **Feedback questions 2**: A text field for answering additional feedback questions.
* **product_rating**: A numerical field for rating the product ( duplicate of product_rating field 4).
* **Test**: A time field for conducting a test.
* **Product info 11, 12**: Text fields for entering additional product information.
