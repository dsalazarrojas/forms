# Market Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Analysis Survey is designed to gather data on customer preferences, market trends, and pain points to inform business decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide a title for your survey.
2. Answer the questions in the order they appear.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Title (`survey_title`, text, required/optional): Provide a title for your survey that accurately reflects its purpose and scope. This field is optional, but it helps identify the survey's topic and relevance.
* **Customer Preferences (`customer_preferences`, select multiple, required): Choose one or more from the list 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate customer preferences.
* **Customer Pain Points (`customer_pain_points`, select one, required): Select one option, 'Yes' or 'No', to indicate a specific customer pain point.
* **Market Trends (`market_trends`, text, required): Provide information about market trends relevant to your business or industry.
* **Customer Segment (`customer_segment`, text, required): Select one or more customer segments that align with your target market.
* **Market Segment (`market_segment`, text, required): Select one or more market segments relevant to your business or industry.
* **Customer Preferences 0-5 (`customer_preferences_0`, `customer_preferences_1`, `customer_preferences_2`, `customer_preferences_3`, `customer_preferences_4`, `customer_preferences_5`, text, required): Provide detailed information about customer preferences.
* **Customer Segment 0-2 (`customer_segment_0`, `customer_segment_1`, `customer_segment_2`, select one, required): Select one option, 'Yes' or 'No', for each customer segment.
* **Market Segment 0-1 (`market_segment_0`, `market_segment_1`, select one, required): Select one option, 'Yes' or 'No', for each market segment.
* **Customer Pain Points 0-3 (`customer_pain_points_0`, `customer_pain_points_1`, `customer_pain_points_2`, `customer_pain_points_3`, text, required): Provide detailed information about customer pain points.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in.
* Review the form carefully to avoid errors.
* Double-check the survey title and subtitles to ensure they accurately reflect the survey's purpose and scope.
* Use the customer preferences and customer segment fields to identify your target market.
* Use market trends and market segments to understand industry trends and adjust your strategy accordingly.
* Customer pain points and market trends help inform business decisions and customer segmentation can help tailor your products or services to meet market demands.
