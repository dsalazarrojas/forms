# Robot Safety Inspection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about robot facilities during safety inspections. It helps identify potential safety concerns and assigns responsibilities for maintenance and inspection tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Facility Name** (Field 1) to identify the inspected facility.
2. Select the **Inspection Date** (Field 2) when the inspection occurred.
3. Select the type of robot being inspected from the **Robot Type** (Field 3) dropdown menu.
4. Rate the safety of the robot facility from 1 to 5 using the **Safety Rating** (Field 4) field (optional).
5. Select the **Maintenance Status** (Field 5) of the robot facility (e.g., "In Good Condition," "Needs Maintenance," or "Defective").
6. If necessary, provide additional comments or observations about the facility's safety in the **Comments** (Field 6) field.
7. Enter the **Email Address** (Field 7) of the person who performed the inspection or will be responsible for maintenance.
8. Enter the **Phone Number** (Field 8) of the person who performed the inspection or will be responsible for maintenance.
9. Select the **Assigned Inspector** (Field 9) responsible for the robot facility's safety inspection.
10. Select the **Assigned Tool** (Field 10) required for the inspection.
11. Select the **Facility Type** (Field 11) of the robot facility being inspected.
12. Enter the **Facility ID** (Field 12) number for tracking and reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name** (Field 1): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Inspection Date** (Field 2): Select the date when the inspection took place.
* **Robot Type** (Field 3): Select the type of robot being inspected.
* **Safety Rating** (Field 4): Rate the facility's safety from 1 to 5.
* **Maintenance Status** (Field 5): Choose the maintenance status of the robot facility.
* **Comments** (Field 6): Provide any comments or observations about the facility's safety.
* **Email Address** (Field 7): Enter the email address of the person responsible for maintenance.
* **Phone Number** (Field 8): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for maintenance.
* **Assigned Inspector** (Field 9): Select the person responsible for the robot facility's safety inspection.
* **Assigned Tool** (Field 10): Select the tool required for the inspection.
* **Facility Type** (Field 11): Choose the type of facility being inspected.
* **Facility ID** (Field 12): Enter the facility's ID number for tracking and reference.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the assigned inspector or refer to the facility's manual for guidance.
* This form is for inspection purposes only; ensure all information is handled confidentially and securely.
