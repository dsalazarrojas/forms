# Exit Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exit Interview Form is a survey designed to gather information about the reasons behind an employee's departure from the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before starting.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If a question does not apply to you, simply leave it blank or note "Not applicable" in the relevant field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page**: This is the introduction page where you'll find general information about the form and its purpose.
* **Employee Info**: This section collects general employee information, such as title and department. Please fill in your relevant details.
* **Reason for Departure**: Choose the reason that best describes why you are leaving the company. This information helps us understand the reasons behind your departure.
* **Job Title**: If you have an official job title, please provide it. If not, you may leave this field blank.
* **Department**: Choose the department you were a part of while working with us. This helps us understand the context of your experience.
* **Manager Questions**: If you have any comments or concerns regarding your manager or their management style, please share them here.
* **Manager Comments**: If you have any additional comments for your manager, please share them here.
* **Employee Comments**: If you have any comments or suggestions for us, please share them here.
* **Exit Interview Date**: Please provide the date of your exit interview. This helps us track the timing of your departure.
* **Signature**: This is where you'll sign the form, confirming that you've completed it accurately and truthfully.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are completing this form for your current or previous role and not duplicating efforts by submitting the form for the same position or employee.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
* Remember to provide as much detail as possible in each field to help us understand your experience and reasons for leaving.
