# Sweet 16 Celebration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to assist guests in completing the Sweet 16 Celebration Survey form to ensure a successful and enjoyable celebration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the survey questions carefully and answer each field accordingly.
2. Select the options that best match your preferences and circumstances.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Guest Attendance
* **Guest Attendance** (`guest_attendance`, `select_multiple`, not required): Indicate if you will be attending the party.

### Party Preferences
* **Party Preferences** (`party_preferences`, `text`, not required): Note any special requests or preferences for the party.

### Guest Diet
* **Guest Diet** (`guest_diet`, `select_one`, not required): Specify the type of food that will be served at the party.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number.

### Party Time
* **Party Time** (`party_time`, `time`, not required): Specify the time of the party.

### Party Date
* **Party Date** (`party_date`, `date`, not required): Specify the date of the party.

### Celebration Location
* **Celebration Location** (`celebration_location`, `select_one`, not required): Specify the location of the party.

### Celebration Duration
* **Celebration Duration** (`celebration_duration`, `select_one`, not required): Select the length of the party.

### Celebration Theme
* **Celebration Theme** (`celebration_theme`, `text`, not required): Describe the theme of the party.

### Party Host
* **Party Host** (`party_host`, `text`, not required): Specify the host of the party.

### Party Color
* **Party Color** (`party_color`, `select_multiple`, not required): Specify if any party colors will be used.

### Party Invited Guests
* **Party Invited Guests** (`party_invited_guests`, `text`, not required): List the guests that will be invited.

### Party Favors
* **Party Favors** (`party_favors`, `select_multiple`, not required): Specify if party favors will be given.

### Party Games
* **Party Games** (`party_games`, `select_multiple`, not required): Specify if any party games will be played.

Please note that all fields are not required unless specifically marked as "required" in the form.
