# Best Content Creator Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Content Creator Nomination Form is designed to collect nominations for the "Best Content Creator" award. This form is used to gather information from users about their favorite content creators, including their name, email address, and nomination details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Nominator Information**: Please fill in your information to help us contact you for more details.
	* Your Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
	* Your Email Address: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your nomination.
2. **Nominee Information**: Provide details about the content creator you are nominating.
	* Content Creator Name: Enter the name of the content creator you are nominating, as they are known online.
3. **Nomination Details**: Please provide more context about your nomination.
	* Primary Content Platform: Select where the creator is most active (e.g. YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, etc.).
	* Link to Creator Profile or Content: Enter a URL to their main page or a specific video/post.
4. **Nomination Details (continued)**: Provide more information about your nomination.
	* Nomination Category: Select the category that best fits the creator's content (e.g. Educational and Tutorial, Entertainment and Comedy, etc.).
	* Most Impactful Piece of Content: Enter a title or description of a specific video or post that impressed you.
	* How Long Have You Followed This Creator: Select how long you have been following the creator (e.g. Less than 6 months, 6 to 12 months, etc.).
5. **Impact and Quality Ratings**: Rate the nominee's content based on your experience.
	* Consistency of Posting: Rate the creator's consistency of posting content.
	* Engagement with Audience: Rate the creator's engagement with their audience.
	* Uniqueness and Originality: Rate the creator's uniqueness and originality of content.
6. **Conclusion**: Provide any additional creators you would like to recommend.
	* Any Other Creators You Would Like to Recommend: Enter any other creators you think are worth considering for this award.
7. **Date of Nomination**: Enter the date of your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Your Email Address**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your nomination.
* **Content Creator Name**: Enter the name of the content creator you are nominating, as they are known online.
* **Primary Content Platform**: Select where the creator is most active (e.g. YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, etc.).

* **Nomination Category**: Select the category that best fits the creator's content (e.g. Educational and Tutorial, Entertainment and Comedy, etc.).
* **Most Impactful Piece of Content**: Enter a title or description of a specific video or post that impressed you.
* **How Long Have You Followed This Creator**: Select how long you have been following the creator (e.g. Less than 6 months, 6 to 12 months, etc.).
* **Consistency of Posting**: Rate the creator's consistency of posting content (from 1 to 5).
* **Engagement with Audience**: Rate the creator's engagement with their audience (from 1 to 5).
* **Uniqueness and Originality**: Rate the creator's uniqueness and originality of content (from 1 to 5).
* **Any Other Creators You Would Like to Recommend**: Enter any other creators you think are worth considering for this award.
