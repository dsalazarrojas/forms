# Research Data Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the research data release. It is intended to gather details about the research project, institution, and contact person in charge of the data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your researcher's name and institution's name in the "Researcher's Name" and "Institution's Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide a brief description of the research data and its purpose.
3. Enter the date of consent for the research data release.
4. Select the consent status from the options "Approved", "Declined", or "Pending".
5. Enter the version of the data and the date of data release.
6. Provide access information for the research data.
7. Enter the contact person's name and email address.
8. Fill in the contact person's phone number in the format 1XXX-XXX-XXXX.
9. Enter the institutional address, city, state/province, zip/postal code, and country.
10. Finally, select the institutional affiliation and the research funding source.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Researcher's Name
Researcher's Name (researcher_id): Enter your full name as the researcher responsible for the data.

### 2. Institution's Name
Institution's Name (institution_id): Enter the name of the institution where the data was collected.

### 3. Purpose of Data Use
Purpose of Data Use (purpose): Briefly describe the purpose of the research data.

### 4. Description of the Research Data
Description of the Research Data (research_data_description): Provide a detailed description of the research data.

### 5. Date of Consent
Date of Consent (consent_date): Enter the date when consent was given for the data release.

### 6. Consent Status
Consent Status (consent_status): Select one of the following options:
* Approved: The data release has been approved.
* Declined: The data release has been declined.
* Pending: The data release is pending.

### 7. Version of Data
Version of Data (research_data_version): Enter the version number of the research data.

### 8. Date of Data Release
Date of Data Release (data_release_date): Enter the date when the data was released.

### 9. Access Information
Access Information (research_data_access): Provide any necessary access information for the research data.

### 10. Contact Person
Contact Person (contact_person): Enter the name of the contact person in charge of the data.

### 11. Contact Email
Contact Email (contact_email): Enter the email address of the contact person.

### 12. Contact Phone
Contact Phone (contact_phone): Enter the phone number of the contact person in the format 1XXX-XXX-XXXX.

### 13. Institutional Address
Institutional Address (institution_address): Enter the address of the institution.

### 14. City
City (institution_city): Enter the city of the institution.

### 15. State/Province
State/Province (institution_state): Enter the state or province of the institution.

### 16. Zip/Postal Code
Zip/Postal Code (institution_zip): Enter the zip or postal code of the institution.

### 17. Country
Country (institution_country): Enter the country of the institution.

### 18. Institutional Affiliation
Institutional Affiliation (research_institutional_affiliation): Select the institutional affiliation.

### 19. Research Funding Source
Research Funding Source (research_funder): Enter the source of research funding.

### 20. Institutional Contact
Institutional Contact (research_institutional_contact): Enter the name of the institutional contact person.

### 21. Institutional Phone
Institutional Phone (research_institutional_phone): Enter the phone number of the institutional contact person in the format 1XXX-XXX-XXXX.

### 22. Institutional Country
Institutional Country (research_institutional_country): Enter the country of the institution.

### Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
