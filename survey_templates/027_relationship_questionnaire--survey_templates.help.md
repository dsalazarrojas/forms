# Relationship Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Relationship Questionnaire is designed to gather information about a person's relationship status and their perceptions of their relationship. This form is intended to collect anonymous and confidential information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relationship status that best describes your current situation.
2. Choose the length of your relationship from the provided options.
3. Select the type of relationship you are in (casual, romantic, friendly, family, etc.).
4. Rate how satisfied you are with your relationship on a scale of 1-10.
5. Answer questions about conflicts and support in your relationship.
6. Rate the quality of your relationship on a scale of 1-10.
7. Rate how dominant you feel in your relationship on a scale of 1-10.
8. If you are not sure or don't know, please answer "Not sure" or "Don't know".
9. For questions 5, 6, 7, 15, 16, 17, and 18, rate your answer on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer honestly and accurately.
* If unsure, choose the answer that best describes your situation.
* If you are not comfortable with any question, please do not fill out this form.
* If you have any concerns, please contact our support team.
