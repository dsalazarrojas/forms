# Energy Flow Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Energy Flow Assessment form is designed to collect information about the energy consumption and potential savings within a facility or building. This form is used to identify areas of improvement and provide recommendations for increasing energy efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), with accurate and relevant information.
* For select one fields, choose one of the provided options that best describes your situation.
* For select multiple fields, choose all the options that apply.
* For text inputs, provide a brief description or answer to the question.
* For number inputs, enter a numerical value.
* For date and time inputs, select a date or time that accurately represents the start and end of the energy assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### energy_flow_assessment (text)

* A brief description of the energy flow assessment.

### category (select one)

* Select one of the provided options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### description (text)

* A brief description of the energy consumption and potential savings.

### facility_id (number)

* A unique identifier for the facility.

### site_name (text)

* The name of the facility site.

### location_id (number)

* The location ID of the facility.

### start_date (date)

* The date when the energy assessment starts.

### end_date (date)

* The date when the energy assessment ends.

### start_time (time)

* The start time of the energy assessment.

### end_time (time)

* The end time of the energy assessment.

### facility_energy_used (number)

* The total energy used by the facility.

### total_energy_used (number)

* The total energy used by the facility.

### efficiency_loss_area (select multiple)

* Select all the areas where efficiency loss occurs:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### loss_area_description (text)

* A brief description of the efficiency loss areas.

### recommended_actions (text)

* Suggested actions for improving energy efficiency.

### notes (text)

* Additional notes about the energy assessment.

### facility_energy_savings (select one)

* Select one of the provided options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### energy_flow_assessment_form (select multiple)

	+ Yes
	+ No

### facility_id_1 (select multiple)
