# Health Product Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and opinions from customers about their experience with a health product.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the required fields to provide a complete and accurate representation of your experience.
2. Select your preferred answers for the multiple-choice questions.
3. Type in your comments and suggestions in the text fields.
4. Make sure to provide your email address and name so that we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`id: 1`): This field is for entering your general information, such as your name and contact details.
* **Product Rating** (`id: 2`): Rate your satisfaction with the product on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very bad and 5 is very good.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`id: 3`): Select your level of satisfaction with the product from Very good, Good, Medium, Bad, and Very bad.
* **Product Features** (`id: 4`): Select which features of the product are important to you, such as Product Design, Quality, Price, etc.
* **Additional Features** (`id: 5`): Select any additional features you would like to see in the product.
* **Overall Experience** (`id: 6`): Enter your overall experience with the product in the text field.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`id: 7`): Enter any improvement ideas you have for the product in the text field.
* **Comments and Suggestions** (`id: 8`): Enter any comments or suggestions you have for the product in the text field.
* **Email** (`id: 9`): Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 10`): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Name** (`id: 11`): Enter your name so that we can address you properly.
* **Feedback** (`id: 12`): This is an optional field for providing any additional feedback you have about your experience.
* **Assigned Tool** (`id: 13`): Select which tool was assigned to you for your feedback.
* **Output File** (`id: 14`): Select the output file type you would like to receive.
* **Assigned User** (`id: 15`): Select if the feedback was assigned to a specific user.

**Tips**
* Make sure to answer all the required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when answering the multiple-choice questions.
* Use clear and concise language when entering your comments and suggestions.
* Check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
