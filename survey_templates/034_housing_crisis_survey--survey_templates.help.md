# Housing Crisis Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather demographic information and data about the housing crisis experienced by individuals. It is used to better understand the impact of the housing crisis on various groups and individuals. The form is anonymous and all responses will be kept confidential.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
2. Make sure to select the correct answers for each question, if you are unsure, ask for help.
3. Enter your contact information to allow us to follow up on your response, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **demographic_data (1)**: This field is for demographic information about yourself. Please provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your housing crisis experience.
* **Housing Type (2)**: Select one of the options to indicate what type of housing you are experiencing. 
  • **rent**: If you are renting a home or apartment.
  • **own**: If you own your home.
  • **mortgage**: If you have a mortgage on your home.
  • **homeless**: If you are experiencing homelessness.
* **Housing Status (3)**: Select one or more of the options to indicate your housing status. 
  • **stable**: If your housing situation is stable.
  • **unstable**: If your housing situation is unstable.
  • **precarious**: If your housing situation is precarious.
* **Rent Amount (4)**: Please enter the amount of rent you are paying per month. This information will help us understand the economic impact of the housing crisis.
* **Housing Crisis Experience (5)**: Describe your experience with the housing crisis. This can be as detailed as you would like and will help us understand your personal experience.
* **Housing Crisis Impact (6)**: Please select one of the options to indicate the impact of the housing crisis on your daily life. 
  • **none**: The housing crisis has had no impact on your daily life.
  • **slight**: The housing crisis has had a slight impact on your daily life.
  • **significant**: The housing crisis has had a significant impact on your daily life.
* **Housing Crisis Solutions (7)**: Describe any solutions or suggestions you think would be beneficial in addressing the housing crisis. This can include policies, programs, or personal strategies.
* **Housing Crisis Comments (8)**: Please add any additional comments or thoughts about the housing crisis you would like to share.
* **Contact Details (9)**: Please provide your contact information so we can follow up on your response, if necessary.
* **First Name (10)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (11)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (12)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (13)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address (14)**: Enter your address.
* **Zip (15)**: Enter your zip code.
* **State (16)**: Select your state from the options.
* **City (17)**: Enter your city.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required. If you need help or clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask.
