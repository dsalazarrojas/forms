# Video Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Video Recommendation Form" is used to collect user opinions on video content. Please use this form to give feedback on videos you've watched. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the category that best describes the video you're giving feedback on.
2. Provide the sub-category that best describes the video.
3. Enter the length of the video in minutes.
4. Rate the video on a scale of 1-10.
5. Choose the type of video (e.g. tutorial, documentary, etc.).
6. Add any tags that describe the video.
7. Write a brief description of the video.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the category that best describes the video content.
* **Sub-Category** (`sub_category`, text, required/optional): Provide the sub-category that best describes the video content.
* **Video Length** (`video_length`, number, required/optional): Enter the length of the video in minutes.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the video on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Type** (`video_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of video (e.g. tutorial, documentary, etc.).
* **Tags** (`tags`, text, required/optional): Add any tags that describe the video content.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Write a brief description of the video content.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Make sure to select the correct category and sub-category for the video.
* If the video length is not provided, please estimate it based on your best judgment.
* The rating scale is from 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* Use the "Type" field to describe the type of video (e.g. tutorial, documentary, etc.).
* Use the "Tags" field to add any additional keywords or descriptions that are relevant to the video.
* Keep the "Description" field brief and to the point, describing the main idea or purpose of the video.
