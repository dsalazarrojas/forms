# employee_motivation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee motivation survey is a tool used to measure employee motivation levels and identify drivers of motivation. It is meant to be taken on a regular basis to track changes in employee motivation over time.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the following questions based on your current motivation level and drivers.
2.  Select the option that best describes your current state for each question.
3.  Fill in all necessary information, and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Motivation Score (<type>: <label>)** (`1`, `number`, required: `false`): 
  Rate your current motivation level on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **Motivation Level (<type>: <label>)** (`2`, `select_one`, required: `false`): 
  Select the level of motivation you currently feel: High, Medium, Low.
* **Engagement Level (<type>: <label>)** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): 
  Choose all that apply: High, Medium, Low.
* **Motivation Type (<type>: <label>)** (`4`, `select_one`, required: `false`): 
  Select the type of motivation that drives you: Self, Team, External.
* **Motivation Goal (<type>: <label>)** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): 
  Describe your primary motivation goal or objective.
* **Motivation Time Frame (<type>: <label>)** (`6`, `time`, required: `false`): 
  Enter a specific date and time when you expect to achieve your motivation goal.
* **Motivation Important (<type>: <label>)** (`7`, `select_one`, required: `false`): 
  Rate how important motivation is to you: Very Important, Somewhat Important, Not Important.
* **Motivation Important Reasons (<type>: <label>)** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): 
  Select all the reasons why motivation is important to you: Career Development, Financial Gain, Social Recognition.
* **Motivation Barometer (<type>: <label>)** (`9`, `number`, required: `false`): 
  Enter a numerical value to indicate your current motivation level, with higher numbers indicating higher motivation.
* **Motivation Barometer Scale (<type>: <label>)** (`10`, `number`, required: `false`): 
  Enter a numerical value to measure your motivation level, with higher numbers indicating higher motivation.
* **Motivation Score Scale (<type>: <label>)** (`11`, `number`, required: `false`): 
  Enter a numerical value to measure your motivation level.
* **Motivation Score (<type>: <label>)** (`12`, `number`, required: `false`): 
  Enter a numerical value to rate your current motivation level.
