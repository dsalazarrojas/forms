# Professional Development Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from attendees on their experience with professional development seminars. The feedback will be used to improve the quality and effectiveness of future seminars.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the seminars you attended and rate them as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
2. Rate the speaker's performance (1-5) to indicate how well they engaged and informed you.
3. Choose how informative or interesting you found the seminar content.
4. Rate the organization of the seminars, selecting all applicable options.
5. Rate the quality of food and beverages provided, selecting one option from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seminars** (`seminar_rating`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate all the seminars you attended. Please select all that apply.
* **Speaker Performance** (`speaker_performance`, number, required: false): Rate the speaker's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Seminars Content** (`seminar_content`, select_one, required: false): Rate how informative or interesting you found the seminar content.
* **Seminars Organization** (`seminar_organization`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the organization of the seminars. Please select all that apply.
* **Food and Beverages** (`food_and_beverages`, select_one, required: false): Rate the quality of food and beverages provided. Please select one option from the list.
