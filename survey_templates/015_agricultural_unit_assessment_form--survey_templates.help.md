## Agricultural Unit Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agricultural Unit Assessment Form is designed to provide a comprehensive assessment of the condition of agricultural units, such as farms, processing facilities, or equipment. This form helps to identify potential hazards, maintenance needs, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Enter Unit Information
1. **Unit ID**: Enter the building or structure identifier.
2. **Inspector Name**: Enter your name and contact information.
3. **Inspection Date**: Select the date of the inspection.
### Step 2: Unit Type
1. **Unit Type**: Select the function of the structure.
### Step 3: Condition Assessment
1. **Structural Condition**: Assess the overall condition of the unit's structure.
2. **Electrical System Check**: Check the status of the electrical systems (wiring, lights, panels).
3. **Water System Check**: Check the status of the water systems (pipes, pumps, troughs).
### Step 4: Safety Compliance
1. **Safety Signage**: Check if safety signs are visible.
2. **Fire Extinguishers**: Check if fire extinguishers are charged and accessible.
### Step 5: Final Assessment
1. **Cleanliness Rating**: Rate the unit's cleanliness (1-5).
2. **Maintenance Required**: Describe any specific repairs or maintenance needed.
3. **Urgency**: Rate the priority of repairs needed (Low, Medium, High, Immediate Safety Hazard).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Unit ID (ID 2)**: Enter the unique identifier of the building or structure.
* **Inspector Name (ID 3)**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Inspection Date (ID 4)**: Select the date of the inspection in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Unit Type (ID 5)**: Select the function of the structure from the provided options.
* **Structural Condition (ID 7)**: Assess the overall condition of the unit's structure, choosing from Good, Fair, Poor, or Critical.
* **Electrical System Check (ID 8)**: Check the status of the electrical systems, choosing from Pass, Fail, or Not Applicable.
* **Water System Check (ID 9)**: Check the status of the water systems, choosing from Pass, Fail, or Not Applicable.
* **Pest Control Status (ID 10)**: Check if pest management is current, choosing from Current, Overdue, or Evidence of Pests Found.
* **Safety Signage (ID 12)**: Check if safety signs are visible, choosing from True, False, or Needs Replacement.
* **Fire Extinguishers Checked (ID 13)**: Check if fire extinguishers are charged and accessible, choosing from True, False, or Missing.
* **Cleanliness Rating (ID 14)**: Rate the unit's cleanliness on a scale from 1 (Dirty) to 5 (Spotless).
* **Maintenance Required (ID 15)**: Describe any specific repairs or maintenance needed.
* **Urgency (ID 16)**: Rate the priority of repairs needed, choosing from Low, Medium, High, or Immediate Safety Hazard.
