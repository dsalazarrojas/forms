# Homeowner Property Survey Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homeowner Property Survey Questionnaire is a form designed to collect essential information about a homeowner's property. It will be used to gather accurate and complete data for property management and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2.  Begin by completing the Property Info page, providing a brief description of the property.
3.  Next, move on to the Property Details page, where you will provide more detailed information about the property.
4.  Continue to the Property Condition page, describing the current state of the property.
5.  On the Property Manager Info page, enter the contact information for the property's manager.
6.  Proceed to the Contact Info page, where you will enter the contact details for the property's contact person.
7.  On the Submission Review page, review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
8.  Move on to the Property Manager Teams page, select the relevant property manager teams.
9.  Enter the submission date and time on the Submission Date and Submission Time pages.
10.  Finally, review and complete the Property Address, Property Zip, and Contact Name fields.
11.  Enter the contact email and contact phone number on the Contact Email and Contact Phone pages respectively.
12.  Add any additional notes on the Additional Notes page if necessary.
13.  Before submitting, review all the fields carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Property Info** (Property Info)
    -   A brief description of the property (text).
*   **Property Details** (Property Details)
    -   A detailed description of the property (text).
*   **Property Condition** (Property Condition)
    -   A description of the property's current state (text).
*   **Property Manager Info** (Property Manager Info)
    -   Contact information for the property's manager (text).
*   **Contact Info** (Contact Info)
    -   Contact details for the property's contact person (text).
*   **Submission Review** (Submission Review)
    -   A review of your answers for accuracy and completeness (text).
*   **Property Manager Teams** (Property Manager Teams)
    -   Select the relevant property manager teams (Homeowners, Property Managers, Real Estate Teams) (select multiple).
*   **Submission Date** (Submission Date)
    -   The date of submission (date).
*   **Submission Time** (Submission Time)
    -   The time of submission (time).
*   **Property Address** (Property Address)
    -   The address of the property (text).
*   **Property Zip** (Property Zip)
    -   The zip code of the property (text).
*   **Contact Name** (Contact Name)
    -   The name of the contact person (text).
*   **Contact Email** (Contact Email)
    -   The email of the contact person (email).
*   **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone)
    -   The phone number of the contact person (text).
*   **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes)
    -   Any additional notes or comments (note).
