# Lighting Control Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lighting Control Preferences form is designed to collect information from users about their preferences and needs regarding lighting control in various settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact info" field. You can type any relevant details you'd like to share with us.
3. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have any lighting control needs in the "What's your first lighting control needs?" field.
4. Choose "True" or "False" to indicate if you have any other lighting control needs in the "Do you have any other lighting control needs?" field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" (or type "Other") to indicate your automation preference in the "What automation method do you prefer?" field.
6. Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your priority level for your lighting control needs in the "Do you have a priority level for your lighting control needs?" field.
7. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have any notes about your lighting control needs or preferences in the "Do you have any notes about your lighting control needs or preferences?" field.
8. If you have any additional comments or information, please type them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you enter your name. This is a required field.
* **Contact info** (`contact_info`, `note`, optional): This field is where you can provide any relevant contact information you'd like to share with us.
* **What's your first lighting control needs?** (`lighting_control_needs`, `text`, optional): This field is where you express your initial lighting control needs.
* **Do you have any other lighting control needs?** (`other_needs`, `select_one`, optional): Choose "True" if you have any other lighting control needs or "False" if you do not.
* **Do you prefer automation in your lighting control?** (`automation_preferences`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you prefer automation or "No" if you do not.
* **What automation method do you prefer?** (`automation_method`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose "Yes" if you prefer the selected method, "No" for no preference, or type "Other" if you have a different method in mind.
* **Do you have a priority level for your lighting control needs?** (`priority`, `select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes" if you have a priority level or "No" if you do not.
* **Do you have any notes about your lighting control needs or preferences?** (`notes`, `select_one`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you have any notes or "No" if you do not.
* **Notes** (`notes_2`, `note`, optional): This field is where you can type any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
