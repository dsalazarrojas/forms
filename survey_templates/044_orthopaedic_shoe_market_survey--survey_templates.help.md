# Orthopaedic Shoe Market Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Orthopaedic Shoe Market Survey aims to gather data on customers' preferences and habits in the orthopaedic shoe market.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly, and select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
2. Enter your honest answers, as they will help us better understand the market.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title**: 
	+ A text field where you can provide a brief title for your response.
* **What do you buy for purpose?**:
	+ A single-select question to select the reason for your purchase (e.g., medical, fashion, sport, etc.).
* **Which shoe type are you most likely to buy?**:
	+ A multi-select question to select all applicable shoe types (e.g., athletic, dress, orthopaedic, etc.).
* **Average number of times you've bought orthopaedic shoes**:
	+ A numerical field to input the average number of times you've purchased orthopaedic shoes.
* **What do you prefer in terms of shoe style?**:
	+ A single-select question to select your preferred shoe style (e.g., casual, formal, athletic, etc.).
* **What is your current shoe size?**:
	+ A single-select question to select your current shoe size.
* **Have you previously purchased from us?**:
	+ A single-select question to indicate if you have purchased from this company before.
* **Average amount you spent on orthopaedic shoes**:
	+ A numerical field to input the average amount you've spent on orthopaedic shoes.
* **Contact**:
	+ A text field where you can provide additional comments or contact information.
