# Healthcare Outreach Material Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare outreach teams collect information about the types of outreach materials healthcare professionals have, what they need, and how they want to receive them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of outreach material you have currently:
	* Please choose one of the options from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the type of outreach material you need:
	* Please enter the type of outreach material you require.
3. Specify the time and date you would like to receive the outreach material:
	* Please select the time and date you prefer for delivery.
4. Enter any additional comments or instructions for delivery:
	* Please provide any additional details or instructions that may be helpful for delivery.
5. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable):
	* Please enter your email address for future communications and your phone number (if you prefer to receive calls).
