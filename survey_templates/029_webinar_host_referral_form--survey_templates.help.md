# Webinar Host Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Webinar Host Referral Form is designed to help connect webinar hosts and their potential collaborators within the community. Fill out this form to share information about your upcoming webinars and connect with other hosts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Submit" to begin.
2. Fill out each field, providing the required and optional information as prompted.
3. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Webinar Host Referral Form** (`webinar_host_referral_form`, text, optional)
This is the main field to enter your referral information. Please provide a brief description of your upcoming webinars.

### **Email** (`email`, text, optional)
Enter your email address to allow others to contact you directly.

### **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional)
Enter your first name, which will be displayed when your referral is shared.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure successful referrals.
- Review your form carefully before submitting to avoid any mistakes.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact form@gic.mx.
