# radio_program_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the "radio_program_feedback_survey" form, which is designed to collect feedback from participants about a program. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section and ensure you have filled out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
2. Fill out the introduction section with a brief description of the program.
3. Rate your experience with the program using the rating field.
4. Share your positive feedback about the program in the "What did you like about the program?" field.
5. Share your negative feedback about the program in the "What didn't you like about the program?" field.
6. Provide any improvement ideas you have for the program in the "What improvement ideas do you have for this program?" field.
7. Enter your email address and phone number so we can follow up with you.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: A brief description of the program to get started.
- **How was the program?**: Share your experience with the program.
- **How would you rate the program?**: Rate your experience with the program on a scale (though the exact scale is not specified in the form).
- **What did you like about the program?**: Share your positive feedback about the program.
- **What didn't you like about the program?**: Share your negative feedback about the program.
- **What improvement ideas do you have for this program?**: Share any ideas you have to improve the program.
- **What's your email address?**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
- **What's your phone number?**: Enter your phone number so we can follow up with you.
- **Submit**: Click to send your completed form to us.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when sharing your feedback.
- Make sure to include any specific examples or anecdotes to help us understand your experience.
- If you have improvement ideas, be specific and realistic about what could be changed or added to the program.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
