# AI Prompt Engineering Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students who have taken the AI Prompt Engineering Course. Your input will help us improve the course and provide a better experience for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the course. Please complete each section according to your experience and opinion.
2. Be honest and thorough in your feedback, as your input is crucial for course improvement.
3. Fields marked as `required` are necessary for our analysis. Please make sure to fill them out accordingly.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the `improvement_suggestions` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Course Batch / Session ID**: Enter the ID of the course session you attended (e.g., "Batch 2023-01-01").
- **Student Name (Optional)**: If you'd like to share your name for feedback purposes, enter it here. Otherwise, leave it blank.
- **Overall Course Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale from 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
- **Instructor Effectiveness**: Rate how effective you found the instructor in guiding you through the course, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
- **Topic & Content Evaluation**: This section allows you to evaluate the quality of the course topics and materials.
  - **Technique Mastery Rating**: Check all techniques you feel you mastered after the course, or those you'd like to learn more about.
  - **Quality of Course Materials**: Rate the quality of the course materials, such as slides, examples, and exercises, on a scale from 1 to 5.
  - **Value of Practical Hands-on Exercises**: Rate the value of the practical exercises provided in the course, from 1 (Extremely Valuable) to 4 (Not very valuable).
  - **Most Valuable Topic**: Indicate which topic or technique was most valuable to you.
- **What Could be Improved**: Provide any suggestions or comments on how the course could be improved.
- **Course Pacing**: Rate how well-paced the course was for you, from Too Fast to Too Slow.
- **Feedback on AI Platforms**: Provide feedback on the AI platforms used in the course, such as ChatGPT or Claude.
- **Recommendation Likelihood**: Rate how likely you are to recommend this course to others, on a scale from 0 (Not likely) to 10 (Highly likely).
- **Advanced Course Interest**: Indicate whether you would be interested in an 'Advanced Prompt Engineering' follow-up course.
- **Certificate / Digital Badge Email**: If you would like to receive a certificate or digital badge, provide your email address.
- **Date of Submission**: Enter the date you are submitting this feedback.
