# Employee Attitude Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Employee Attitude Survey" is a tool designed to gather feedback from employees about their work environment, manager support, job satisfaction, and other aspects of their job. This survey aims to collect information that will help management make informed decisions to improve employee experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the best answer that reflects your feelings and opinions.
2. For questions with multiple-choice options, select one that best describes your sentiment.
3. For numerical questions, enter a valid number that represents your hours worked per week.
4. For open-ended questions, provide your thoughts and comments about your work environment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall, how would you rate your job satisfaction?** (`employee_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
Choose one of the options: Highly Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, that best describes your job satisfaction.
* **How satisfied are you with your job security?** (`job_security`, `select_one`, **required**):
Select one of the options: High, Medium, Low, None, that best describes your job security.
* **How would you rate your work-life balance?** (`work_life_balance`, `select_one`, **required**):
Choose one of the options: Very Good, Good, Neutral, Bad, Very Bad, that best describes your work-life balance.
* **How often do you feel stressed about work?** (`stress_level`, `select_one`, **required**):
Select one of the options: Often, Sometimes, Rarely, Never, that best describes your stress level.
* **How would you rate your manager's support?** (`manager_support`, `select_one`, **required**):
Select one of the options: High, Medium, Low, None, that best describes your manager's support.
* **How many hours do you typically work per week?** (`hours_worked`, `number`, **required**):
Enter a valid number that represents your average hours worked per week.
* **What are your working hours?** (`working_hours`, `time`, **required**):
Choose the best answer for you that describes your working hours.
* **How much autonomy do you have in your work?** (`autonomy`, `select_one`, **required**):
Select one of the options: High, Medium, Low, that best describes your level of autonomy.
* **How would you rate the level of feedback you receive from your manager?** (`feedback_manager`, `select_one`, **required**):
Select one of the options: High, Medium, Low, None, that best describes the level of feedback you receive.
* **Do you feel like your goals and expectations are clear?** (`clear_goals`, `select_one`, **required**):
Choose one of the options: True, False, that best describes your feelings about your goals and expectations.
* **How would you rate your collaboration with colleagues?** (`collaboration`, `select_one`, **required**):
Choose one of the options: Very Good, Good, Neutral, Bad, Very Bad, that best describes your collaboration with colleagues.
* **Any other comments or suggestions about your work environment?** (`comments`, `text`, **required**):
Please enter any thoughts or suggestions you have about your work environment in this text field.
