# Attunement Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey assesses your emotional awareness, regulation, and interpersonal attunement skills. It's essential to be honest in your responses, as they will help us understand your strengths and areas for improvement in these key areas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your basic information: name, email, role or position, and organization.
2. Answer the questions about your emotional awareness and regulation abilities (questions 8-14).
3. Describe your interpersonal attunement skills (questions 16-19).
4. Identify areas where you'd like to develop your attunement skills (question 24).
5. Add any additional feedback (question 25).
