# Healthcare Consumer Trust Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Consumer Trust Index Survey is a tool designed to gauge healthcare consumers' trust and satisfaction levels with various aspects of their care. It's essential to understand that this survey is for feedback purposes only and should not be used for any other reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please answer all questions honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select one option for each question, unless otherwise specified.
3. Enter your answers accurately and clearly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider or facility that you've interacted with.
* **Name**: Please enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Age Group**: Select how old you are:
	+ Yes: I'm 18-64 years old
	+ No: I'm older than 64 years old
* **Gender**: Select your gender:
	+ Yes: I'm female
	+ No: I'm male
* **Ethnicity**: Select your ethnicity:
	+ Yes: Yes, I'm Asian
	+ No: Not Asian (Other options may be available, but please select all applicable)
* **Trust in Provider**: Rate your trust in the healthcare provider or facility:
	+ 0-10: Scale of your trust level
* **Trust in Services**: Rate your trust in the quality of services provided:
	+ 0-10: Scale of your trust level
* **Trust in Clinical Staff**: Rate your trust in the clinical staff:
	+ 0-10: Scale of your trust level
* **Trust in Facility**: Rate your trust in the facility:
	+ 0-10: Scale of your trust level
* **Trust in Services Quality**: Rate your trust in the quality of services:
	+ 0-10: Scale of your trust level
* **Satisfaction with Care**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your care:
	+ 0-10: Scale of your satisfaction level
* **Communication with Provider**: Rate your satisfaction with communication with your provider:
	+ 0-10: Scale of your satisfaction level
* **Privacy and Security**: Rate your confidence in your privacy and security:
	+ 0-10: Scale of your satisfaction level
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your care:
	+ 0-10: Scale of your satisfaction level
* **Age**: Please enter your age for demographic purposes.
* **Gender**: Select your gender for demographic purposes:
	+ Yes: I'm female
	+ No: I'm male
* **Ethnicity**: Select your ethnicity for demographic purposes:
	+ Yes: I'm Asian
	+ No: Not Asian (Other options may be available, but please select all applicable)
* **Race**: Select your racial background for demographic purposes:
	+ Yes: I'm Asian
	+ No: Not Asian (Other options may be available, but please select all applicable)
* **Income**: Select your income level:
	+ Yes: I'm above the median income
	+ No: Below the median income
* **English Language**: Select your ability to speak English:
	+ Yes: I'm fluent
	+ No: Not fluent
* **Health Insurance Status**: Select your health insurance status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
