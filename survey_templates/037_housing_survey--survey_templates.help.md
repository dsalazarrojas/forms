# Housing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Housing Survey is a tool designed to collect feedback from users about their housing experiences. Your responses will help us improve our services and better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What Is Your Name" field.
2. Provide your phone number in the "What Is Your Phone Number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "What Is Your Email" field.
4. Share your thoughts about your housing experience in the "What Do You Think About Your Housing Experience" field.
5. If you have any suggestions for improvements, enter them in the "Do You Have Any Suggestions" field.
6. If you have any comments, please share them in the "Comments" field.
7. Rate your experience by selecting a rating from 1 to 5 in the "How Do You Rate Your Experience" field.
8. Indicate whether you would recommend this housing by selecting "Yes", "No", or "Maybe" in the "Would You Recommend This Housing" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What Is Your Name (Text)** (Required: False): Enter your name as it is written on your identification.
* **What Is Your Phone Number (Text)** (Required: False): Enter your phone number.
* **What Is Your Email (Email)** (Required: False): Enter your email address.
* **What Do You Think About Your Housing Experience (Text)** (Required: False): Share your thoughts about your housing experience.
* **Do You Have Any Suggestions (Text)** (Required: False): If you have any suggestions for improvements, enter them here.
* **Comments (Text)** (Required: False): Share any comments or feedback you have.
* **How Do You Rate Your Experience (Select One)** (Required: False): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to rate your experience.
* **Would You Recommend This Housing (Select Multiple)** (Required: False): Select "Yes" if you would recommend this housing, "No" if you would not, or "Maybe" if you are unsure.
* **Housing Experience (Text)** (Required: False): Share your thoughts about your housing experience.
* **Rating (Select One)** (Required: False): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to rate your experience.
* **Recommended (Select Multiple)** (Required: False): Select "Yes" if you would recommend this housing, "No" if you would not, or "Maybe" if you are unsure.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help us better understand your housing experiences and improve our services.
