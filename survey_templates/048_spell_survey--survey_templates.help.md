# Spell Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Spell Survey" form is designed to collect user responses to a series of questions related to a particular topic. This form is intended to gather data that will help us understand how to improve our services. By submitting your responses, you will be contributing to our research and helping us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by providing your answers to each question. The questions are designed to be clear and concise, and each one is labeled to help you understand what it's asking.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the response that best answers the question.
3. For text questions, provide a brief but complete answer.
4. Once you have completed all questions, click the "Submit" button to submit your responses.
5. If you need to return to any question, click the "Back" button to move up to the previous question.
