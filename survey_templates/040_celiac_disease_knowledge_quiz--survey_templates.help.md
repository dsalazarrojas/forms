# Celiac Disease Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Celiac Disease Knowledge Quiz is a self-assessment tool designed to gauge your knowledge about celiac disease. This quiz is meant to be a quick and engaging way to evaluate your understanding of the basics of celiac disease, its symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, and complications. The quiz is not a formal assessment instrument, but rather a tool for personal enrichment and understanding.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Celiac Disease Knowledge Quiz, follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully, and select the answer that best matches your understanding of the topic.
2.  For questions that ask "I do not know," select this option if you are unsure or lack information about the topic.
3.  If you have personal experience with Celiac Disease or have learned about it from other sources, use your knowledge to select the most accurate answer.
4.  You may select multiple options for questions that ask for more than one answer.
5.  Complete all required questions (indicated by a star symbol) before proceeding to the next section.
6.  Once you have completed all questions, review your results to understand your score and any feedback provided.

*   **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for the purpose of identifying your response. This field is optional and for personal identification purposes only.
*   **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further information or resources about Celiac Disease. This field is optional.
*   **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, optional): Choose your age group from the provided options to help us understand your demographic.
*   **What is Celiac Disease?** (`question_1_definition`, select_one, required): Select the definition that best describes Celiac Disease. This is a required question to gauge your understanding of the disease's nature.
*   **What triggers Celiac Disease?** (`question_2_trigger`, select_one, required): Choose the trigger that is most associated with Celiac Disease. This is a required question to evaluate your understanding of the disease's causes.
*   **Which of the following is NOT a symptom of Celiac Disease?** (`question_3_symptoms`, select_one, required): Select the symptom that is NOT typically associated with Celiac Disease. This is a required question to test your understanding of the disease's symptoms.

*   **What is the primary treatment for Celiac Disease?** (`question_5_treatment`, select_one, required): Select the primary method used to treat Celiac Disease. This is a required question to evaluate your knowledge of the disease's management.
