# Statue Discovery Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Statue Discovery Survey form is designed to collect information about statues from around the world. This form helps users document and share statues in a standardized way, making it easier for others to find and learn about them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the survey name, describing the statue or collection you're surveying.
2. Add a brief description of the statue or collection.
3. Upload or link photos of the statue or collection.
4. Provide the location where the statue can be found.
5. Specify the date the survey was completed or the date of the statue's discovery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Name** (`survey_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the survey or collection you're documenting. This will help others find your survey in the future.
* **Description** (`survey_description`, text, optional): Add a brief description of the statue or collection. Try to keep it concise but informative.
* **Photos** (`survey_photos`, text, optional): Upload or link photos of the statue or collection. This will help others visualize and understand your survey.
* **Location** (`survey_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the statue can be found. This will help others track down the statue.
* **Date** (`survey_date`, date, optional): Specify the date the survey was completed or the date of the statue's discovery.
