# Indirect Election Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of indirect election methods, which allow citizens to participate in the election process without directly voting. The survey aims to evaluate the experiences and perceptions of citizens who have participated in indirect elections and to identify areas for improvement. The questions in this survey are meant to be answered by participants who have recently participated in an indirect election.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer the questions as they apply to your recent participation in an indirect election. You can refer back to the questions and instructions as needed.
