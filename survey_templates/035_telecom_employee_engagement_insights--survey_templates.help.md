# Telecom Employee Engagement Insights - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect insights on employee engagement within the telecom industry. It gathers information about employees' job satisfaction, departmental alignment, and feedback. The collected data will help identify areas of improvement and opportunities for growth within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into multiple pages. Please select the "Next" button to navigate through each page. You can complete each page one at a time, and you can review and edit your answers as you go.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Please enter your manager's name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Please enter your job title within the telecom industry.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Please select your department from the list (IT, Marketing, Sales, Support).
* **Engagement Score** (`engagement_score`, number, optional): Please rate your level of engagement, with higher scores indicating higher engagement.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please provide any additional feedback about your experience as a telecom industry employee.
* **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, optional): Please provide any comments or suggestions you have for your manager.
* **Telecom Survey** (`telecom_survey`, select_multiple, optional): Please indicate if you have completed a telecom industry survey (Yes, No).
* **Telecom Survey Frequency** (`telecom_survey_frequency`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you complete telecom industry surveys (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
- You can complete the form at your own pace, but try to finish it within a reasonable time frame.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR department.
