# Caregiver Wellbeing Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Caregiver Wellbeing Index Survey is a tool designed to help organizations understand the wellbeing of caregivers. By completing this survey, you will be providing valuable information to help improve the support and resources available to caregivers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Please note that some questions are required, marked as "required: true".
3. You can choose from the available options or type in your answer in the designated fields.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Anything else you want to share about your wellbeing?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (optional)**: Enter your name if you are willing to be identified. If you prefer to remain anonymous, leave this field blank.
* **Caregiver Type**: Please select the category that best describes your role as a caregiver. This will help us understand your specific challenges and needs.
* **Average caregiving hours per week**: Enter the total number of hours you dedicate to caregiving per week. This will help us assess the demands of your role.
* **Stress Level**: Rate your current stress level from 1 to 10, with 1 being low stress and 10 being extremely high stress.
* **Burnout Frequency**: Select how often you experience physical or emotional exhaustion due to your caregiving role.
* **Mood Rating**: Rate your overall mood in the last 2 weeks, with 1 being very positive and 5 being very negative.
* **Sleep Quality**: Rate the quality of your sleep from 1 to 5, with 1 being poor and 5 being restful.
* **Physical Strain**: Rate the physical demands of your caregiving role from 1 to 5, with 1 being not demanding and 5 being very demanding.
* **Physical Health Impact**: Select the level of impact your caregiving role has on your physical health.
* **Health Maintenance**: Select how often you are able to attend to your own health needs.
* **Support Network**: Confirm if you have a reliable support system outside of work.
* **Organizational Support**: Rate the level of support you receive from your organization regarding your wellbeing.
* **Access to Counseling**: Confirm if you are aware of counseling or mental health resources available to you.
* **Self-Care Frequency**: Select how often you engage in self-care activities.
* **Preferred Self-Care**: Share your preferred way to recharge and relax.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Share any suggestions you have for improving the wellbeing of caregivers.

Note: Please answer each question carefully and honestly, as your input will help us better understand the wellbeing of caregivers and improve support and resources for you and others.
