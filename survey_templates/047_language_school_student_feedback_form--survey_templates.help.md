# Language School Student Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback about the course you took at the language school. Your input will help us improve the course content, instruction, and overall student experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the page you want to access by scrolling through the pages listed at the top of the form.
2. For each question, provide your honest feedback in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you think of the course content?** (`language_school_student_feedback_form_first_page`, `text`, optional)
	+ This is the first page of the form, where you will be asked to provide your thoughts about the course content.
* **Instructor** (`language_school_student_feedback_form_instructor`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ This question allows you to select the quality of the instructor who taught your course.
	+ Please choose one of the options provided that best reflects your opinion.
* **Facilities** (`language_school_student_feedback_form_facilities`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ This question allows you to select the facilities that were provided during your course.
	+ You can choose multiple options that best reflect your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (`language_school_student_feedback_form_additional_comments`, `text`, optional)
	+ This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience at the language school.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide thoughtful feedback that will help us improve our services.
* If a question is not required, you can still provide your feedback if you have something to say.
* You can scroll back and forth between pages to access all the questions.
* If you have any technical issues or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
