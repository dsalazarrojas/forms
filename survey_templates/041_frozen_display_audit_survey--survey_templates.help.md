# Frozen Display Audit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Frozen Display Audit Survey is designed to assess the condition of freezers in various stores, ensuring they are maintained at optimal display conditions. This form helps store managers and auditors evaluate the status of freezers, which is essential for food safety and customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Store Address** field with the store's address where the freezer is located.
2. Enter the **Store Name** in the **store name** field.
3. Record the **Audit Date**, when the audit was performed.
4. Evaluate the **Freezer Condition** by selecting one of the following options:
	* Good: The freezer is in excellent condition, with no signs of damage or malfunctions.
	* Fair: The freezer shows some signs of wear and tear, but it still functions properly.
	* Poor: The freezer is in a critical condition, with significant damage or malfunctions.
5. Check the **Freezer Temperature**:
	* Below 0°C: The freezer is maintained at a temperature below 0°C.
	* Above 0°C: The freezer is not maintained at a temperature below 0°C.
	* Not Applicable: The freezer is not applicable for this audit.
6. Describe the **Display Layout** in the designated field.
7. Record the **Store Manager Signature** in the designated field.
8. Fill in the **Store Manager Name** field.
9. Enter the **Store Phone** number.
10. Enter the **Store Email** address.
11. Enter the **Store Manager Email** address.
12. Take notes in the **Store Notes** field.
13. Take notes in the **Auditor Notes** field.
14. Record the **Audit Complete** status, which is not a numeric field but rather a checkbox or dropdown to confirm the audit is complete.
15. Enter the **Auditor Name** field.
16. Enter the **Store Number** field.
17. Enter the **Store Location** field.
18. Enter the **Store Region** field.
19. Enter the **Store Manager Title** field.
20. Enter the **Store Manager ID** field.
21. Evaluate the **Audit Frequency**:
	* Daily
	* Weekly
	* Monthly

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Address** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the store's address where the freezer is located.
* **store name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter the store's name.
* **Audit Date** (3, date, required/optional): Record the date when the audit was performed.
* **Freezer Condition** (4, select_multiple, required/optional): Evaluate the freezer condition, choosing from "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Freezer Temperature** (5, select_one, required/optional): Check the freezer temperature, choosing from "Below 0°C", "Above 0°C", or "Not Applicable".
* **Display layout** (6, text, required/optional): Describe the display layout of the freezer.
* **Store Manager Signature** (7, text, required/optional): Record the store manager's signature.
* **Store Manager Name** (8, text, required/optional): Enter the store manager's name.
* **Store Phone** (9, text, required/optional): Enter the store's phone number.
* **Store Email** (10, email, required/optional): Enter the store's email address.
* **Store Manager Email** (11, email, required/optional): Enter the store manager's email address.
* **Store Notes** (12, note, required/optional): Take notes on the store's condition.
* **Auditor Notes** (13, note, required/optional): Take notes on the audit process.
* **Audit Complete** (14, number, required/optional): Record the audit completion status.
* **Auditor Name** (15, text, required/optional): Enter the auditor's name.
* **Store Number** (16, text, required/optional): Enter the store's number.
* **Store Location** (17, text, required/optional): Enter the store's location.
* **Store Region** (18, text, required/optional): Enter the store's region.
* **Store Manager Title** (19, text, required/optional): Enter the store manager's title.
* **Store Manager ID** (20, text, required/optional): Enter the store manager's ID.
* **Audit Frequency** (21, select_multiple, required/optional): Evaluate the audit frequency, choosing from "Daily", "Weekly", or "Monthly".
