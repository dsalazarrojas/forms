# LED Mode Selection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The LED Mode Selection Survey is designed to collect information about your preferences and experiences with LED lighting. Your input will help us improve our product and service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Select one or multiple options for select_one and select_multiple questions, depending on your experience.
3. Enter your email, phone number, and name if you wish to be contacted for further information.
4. Enter your current usage frequency (if applicable).
5. Rate your satisfaction level (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **LED Mode Selection** (`led_mode_selection`): Select one option that best describes your LED mode selection:
	+ Brightness: How much brightness do you prefer in your LED lighting?
	+ Color: Do you prefer colored LED lighting?
	+ Color Temperature: Do you prefer warm or cool color temperature in your LED lighting?
	+ Dimmable: Can you dim your LED lighting?
	+ Smart Color: Do you prefer smart color changing or color adjusting LED lighting?
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`): How satisfied are you with your current LED lighting selection?
	+ Very Satisfied: You are very happy with your current selection.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: You are somewhat happy with your current selection.
	+ Not Satisfied: You are not happy with your current selection.
	+ Not at all Satisfied: You are not happy with your current selection.
* **Current Usage** (`current_usage`): How often do you use your LED lighting?
	+ High Usage: You use your LED lighting frequently.
	+ Medium Usage: You use your LED lighting occasionally.
	+ Low Usage: You use your LED lighting rarely.
	+ No Usage: You do not use LED lighting.
* **Reason Not Used** (`reason_not_used`): Why do you not use LED lighting?
	+ Not Needed: You do not need LED lighting.
	+ Not Required: You are not required to use LED lighting.
	+ Not Interested: You are not interested in LED lighting.
	+ Other: Other reasons not listed.
* **How Often** (`how_often`): How often do you use LED lighting?
	+ Daily: You use LED lighting every day.
	+ Weekly: You use LED lighting every week.
	+ Monthly: You use LED lighting every month.
	+ Rarely: You rarely use LED lighting.
* **Why Used** (`why_used`): Why do you use LED lighting?
	+ Brightness: You prefer the brightness of LED lighting.
	+ Color: You prefer the color of LED lighting.
	+ Energy Efficiency: You prefer the energy efficiency of LED lighting.
	+ Energy Saving: You prefer the energy saving features of LED lighting.
	+ Other: Other reasons not listed.
* **How Found** (`how_found`): How did you discover LED lighting?
	+ Internet: You discovered LED lighting on the internet.
	+ Social Media: You discovered LED lighting on social media.
	+ Recommendations: You were recommended to use LED lighting by a friend or family member.
	+ Online Search: You discovered LED lighting through an online search engine.
	+ Other: Other sources not listed.
* **Why Not Used** (`why_not_used`): Why do you not use LED lighting?
	+ Not Available: LED lighting is not available where you live or work.
	+ Not Interested: You are not interested in LED lighting.
	+ Not Needed: You do not need LED lighting.
	+ Other: Other reasons not listed.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Do you have any comments or suggestions about LED lighting?
* **Email** (`email`): Your email address (optional).
* **Phone** (`phone`): Your phone number (optional).
* **Name** (`name`): Your name (optional).
* **Usage Frequency** (`usage_frequency`): How often do you use LED lighting per day? (If applicable).
* **Satisfaction Score** (`satisfaction_score`): What is your satisfaction level with LED lighting? (If applicable).
