# Construction Efficiency Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help construction companies assess their operational efficiency and gather baseline data for future improvements. Please answer the questions accurately and thoroughly to provide a comprehensive view of your company's performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), as they are essential for a complete assessment.
2. Use the options provided for select_one fields to choose the most accurate description of your company's situation.
3. Enter numerical values for number fields, using the format as shown in the examples.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter your company's official name as it appears on your business cards or website.
* **Total Number of Employees** (`company_size`): Choose the correct category that represents the total number of employees in your organization.
	+ 1-20 Employees
	+ 21-100 Employees
	+ 101-500 Employees
	+ More than 500
* **Average Number of Projects per Year** (`annual_project_volume`): Estimate the average number of active projects within a 12-month period.
* **On-Time Completion Rate (%)** (`project_schedule_accuracy`): Calculate the percentage of projects completed on or before the original deadline.
* **Budget Adherence Rate (%)** (`budget_adherence_rate`): Calculate the percentage of projects completed without exceeding the baseline budget.
* **Material Waste Management Efficiency (1-10)** (`site_waste_reduction`): Assess your company's current material waste management efficiency on a scale of 1 to 10.
	+ 1 - Poor/No Program
	+ 2 - Basic/Ad-hoc
	+ 3 - Standardized
	+ 4 - Optimized
	+ 5 - Highly Efficient
	+ 6 - Excellent
	+ 7 - Best Practice
	+ 8 - Leading Practice
	+ 9 - Industry-leading
	+ 10 - Highly Efficient/Zero-Waste Goals
* **Estimated Average Rework Cost (%)** (`rework_cost_estimate`): Estimate the average percentage of total project cost spent on fixing errors/defects.
* **Level of Digital Tool Adoption** (`digital_adoption_maturity`): Choose the level of digital tool adoption in your daily operations:
	+ No Digital Tools
	+ Limited/Ad-hoc Use
	+ Standardized in Some Departments
	+ Fully Integrated/Paperless
* **BIM Implementation Level** (`bim_utilization`): Choose the frequency of Building Information Modeling use:
	+ Never Used
	+ Rarely/Experimental
	+ Most Projects (3D)
	+ All Projects (4D/5D Integrated)
* **Estimated Daily Non-Productive Time per Worker (Hours)** (`non_productive_time_estimate`): Estimate the average number of hours spent on non-productive activities per day.
* **Average Monthly Absenteeism Rate (%)** (`absenteeism_rate`): Calculate the average percentage of planned shifts missed within a month.
* **Annual Training Investment per Employee ($)** (`training_investment_annual`): Estimate the amount spent on professional development/certifications per employee per year.
* **Primary Goal for Efficiency Improvement in Next 12 Months** (`primary_improvement_target`): Choose the primary goal for improvement:
	+ Reducing Rework Costs
	+ Improving Schedule Accuracy
	+ Enhancing Site Safety
	+ Accelerating Digital Adoption
	+ Material Cost Management
* **Additional Context or Observations** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional context or observations that might be relevant to your company's efficiency assessment.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when answering the questions, using the provided options or formats.
* If you're unsure about a specific field, please consult with your colleagues or superiors to ensure the accuracy of your answers.
* Please keep in mind that this survey is designed to help your company improve its efficiency; be honest and thorough in your answers.
