# ESG Policy Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the ESG policy adoption by an individual or a company. It is meant to track progress of ESG policy implementation within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, fill out each section with accurate and detailed information. This form is not meant for multiple submissions, so ensure that you submit only once.
- Review each section carefully before proceeding to the next one.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ESG Policy Adoption Survey** (Page 1, Label: survey_header): This is the title of the form.
* **Name** (Page 1, Label: name): Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or identification.
* **Email** (Page 1, Label: email): Enter your work or company email address.
* **Company** (Page 1, Label: company): Enter your company name.
* **Position** (Page 1, Label: position): Enter your job title or position within your company.
* **Department** (Page 1, Label: department): Enter the department or team you belong to.
* **ESG Policy** (Page 2, Label: esg_policy): This question has two options: "True" or "False". Please answer according to whether your company has implemented an ESG policy.
* **ESG Policy Details** (Page 2, Label: esg_policy_details): Provide any additional details about your company's ESG policy.
* **Policy Adoption** (Page 3, Label: policy_adoption): Select the level of implementation of ESG policy within your company, which can be Not Implemented, Partially Implemented, or Fully Implemented.
* **Feedback** (Page 3, Label: feedback): Enter any additional feedback or comments about your company's ESG policy implementation.
* **Date** (Page 4, Label: date): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time** (Page 4, Label: time): Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Note** (Page 4, Label: note): Enter any additional notes or comments about your submission.
* **Submission Details** (Page 5, Label: submission_details): Enter any additional details about your submission.
* **Submission Comments** (Page 5, Label: submission_comments): Enter any additional comments about your submission.
* **Submit** (Page 6, Label: submit): Click this button to submit your completed form.
* **Back to Top** (Page 6, Label: back_to_top): Return to the top of the form.
* **Back to Previous Page** (Page 6, Label: back_to_prev_page): Return to the previous page of the form.
* **Next Page** (Page 6, Label: next_page): Go to the next page of the form.
