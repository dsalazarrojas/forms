# debrief_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The debrief survey is a form designed to gather feedback and evaluation of an event or project. It aims to collect information on team members, overall effectiveness, and any additional comments to facilitate post-event or post-project review and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are using it in the correct context.
2. If you are a team member, list the team members who were present during the event or project.
3. Provide detailed feedback about the event or project in the feedback field.
4. If applicable, rate the overall effectiveness of the event or project using the provided options.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the comments field.
6. Complete any other required fields, such as form version and created by fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, text, not required): A brief welcome message to users.
* **Team members** (`team_members`, select_one, not required): List the team members who were present during the event.
	+ Choose one of the following options: Team Lead, Event Coordinator, Project Manager, Team Member, Others
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, not required): Provide detailed feedback about the event or project.
* **Overall Effectiveness Rating** (`effectiveness_rating`, select_multiple, not required): Rate the overall effectiveness of the event or project.
	+ Choose one of the following options: Highly effective, Somewhat effective, Not effective
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, note, not required): Suggest improvements for future events or projects.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments or notes.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, not required): Not applicable for end-users.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, not required): Not applicable for end-users.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, not required): Not applicable for end-users.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, not required): Not applicable for end-users.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, not required): Not applicable for end-users.
* **Form Version ID** (`form_version_id`, number, not required): Not applicable for end-users.
