# Food Preferences Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Food Preferences Survey is a tool used to collect information about a patient's food preferences. It is designed to be completed by the patient or their representative. The survey helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's diet and eating habits, which can inform treatment plans or recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the survey questions carefully and answer them accurately.
2. Select the correct answers for each question.
3. Ensure you have permission from the patient or their representative before completing the form.
4. Complete each page of the survey, following the instructions provided.
5. Submit the completed survey to the relevant healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Preferences** (`id: 4`, `label: Food Preferences`): Select all the food items that you prefer to eat. This could be fruits, vegetables, or any other food item.
* **Eating Habits** (`id: 5`, `label: Eating Habits`): Choose when you usually eat, such as morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Food Allergies** (`id: 6`, `label: Food Allergies`): If you have any food allergies, please list them here.
* **Contact Number** (`id: 8`, `label: Contact Number`): Enter your contact number so that the healthcare professional can reach you for further information.
* **Email** (`id: 9`, `label: Email`): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Email 2** (`id: 10`, `label: Email 2`): Select whether you prefer to receive email updates or not.
* **Contact Address 1** (`id: 11`, `label: Contact Address 1`): Enter your contact address for postal communication.
* **Address Line 1** (`id: 12`, `label: Address Line 1`): Enter the address line 1 of your contact address.
* **Country** (`id: 13`, `label: Country`): Enter your country of residence.
* **State** (`id: 14`, `label: State`): Enter your state or region of residence.
* **City** (`id: 15`, `label: City`): Enter your city or town of residence.
* **Zip** (`id: 16`, `label: Zip`): Enter your zip code.
* **Address Line 2** (`id: 17`, `label: Address Line 2`): Enter the address line 2 of your contact address.
* **Address Line 3** (`id: 18`, `label: Address Line 3`): Enter the address line 3 of your contact address.
* **Contact Address 2** (`id: 19`, `label: Contact Address 2`): Enter your secondary contact address.
* **Contact Address 3** (`id: 20`, `label: Contact Address 3`): Enter your tertiary contact address.
* **Contact Number 2** (`id: 21`, `label: Contact Number 2`): Enter your secondary contact number.
* **Email 2** (`id: 22`, `label: Email 2`): Select whether you prefer to receive email updates or not from this address.
* **Email 3** (`id: 23`, `label: Email 3`): Select whether you prefer to receive email updates or not.
* **Contact Address 4** (`id: 24`, `label: Contact Address 4`): Enter your contact address for further communication.
* **Contact Number 3** (`id: 25`, `label: Contact Number 3`): Enter your tertiary contact number.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the healthcare professional for assistance.
