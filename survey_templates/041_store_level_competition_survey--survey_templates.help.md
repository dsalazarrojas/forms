# Store Level Competition Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the competitive landscape of stores in a market and gather information to inform business strategies and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing general information about your store in the **Store Information** field.
2.  Answer **Store Number**, which is the ID number assigned to your store.
3.  Enter your **Store Name**.
4.  In **Competitor Awareness**, indicate if you are aware of your competitors.
5.  Choose **Store Type**, whether your store is a physical store, an online store, or both.
6.  If you choose **Online** as your **Store Type**, answer **Price Point** indicating if you are at a high, medium, or low price point.
7.  Please provide a description of your store's market analysis in **Market Analysis**.
8.  In **Competitor Analysis**, describe your competitors' market analysis, focusing on price, quality, and product assortment.
9.  List actions you can take to improve your market position based on your competitor analysis in **Action Items**.
10. Add any additional information you think is relevant to your competitor analysis in **Additional Information**.
11. If you have a product line or assortment, provide a brief description in **Product Lineup**.
12. If you have a competitor store, provide its name in **Competitor Store**.
13. Describe your store's experience, including any unique features or benefits in **Store Experience**.
14. In **Competitor Analysis 2**, provide a description of your competitors' market analysis, focusing on price, quality, and product assortment.
15. List actions you can take to improve your market position based on your competitor analysis in **Action Items 2**.
16. Finally, review and submit your responses to complete the survey.
