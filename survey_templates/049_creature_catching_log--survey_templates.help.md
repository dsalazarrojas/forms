# Creature Catching Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on creatures caught for research purposes. It is intended to be a simple and structured way to record and document the capture of creatures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the species of the creature from the "Species Name" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date you captured the creature (YYYY-MM-DD format) in the "Capture Date" field.
3. Enter the time you captured the creature (HH:MM:SS format) in the "Capture Time" field.
4. You may attach a photo of the creature in the "Photo" field, or simply describe it.
5. Enter any additional details or comments about the creature and its capture in the "Details" field.
6. Record the location where the creature was caught in the "Location" field.
7. Enter the name or details of the person or team responsible for the capture in the "Researcher" field.
8. Record any additional notes or observations about the creature and its capture in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Species Name** (`species_name`, Select One, Optional): Select the species of the creature you caught from the dropdown menu.
* **Capture Date** (`capture_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you captured the creature (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Capture Time** (`capture_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time you captured the creature (HH:MM:SS format).
* **Photo** (`photo`, Note, Optional): Attach a photo of the creature, or describe it if a photo is not available.
* **Details** (`details`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional details or comments about the creature and its capture.
* **Location** (`location`, Text, Optional): Record the location where the creature was caught.
* **Researcher** (`researcher`, Text, Optional): Enter the name or details of the person or team responsible for the capture.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text, Optional): Record any additional notes or observations about the creature and its capture.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct species of the creature from the dropdown menu to ensure accurate data collection.
* Use the "Photo" field to attach a clear and legible photo of the creature.
* Be as detailed as possible when entering the "Details" and "Notes" fields to provide as much information as possible about the creature and its capture.
* If you are unable to attach a photo, use the "Photo" field to describe the creature in detail.
