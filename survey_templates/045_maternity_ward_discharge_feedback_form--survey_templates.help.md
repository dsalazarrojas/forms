# Maternity Ward Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients and their family members after discharge from the maternity ward. It helps us understand their experience and identify areas for improvement to provide better care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in all the required fields.
2. If you have any comments or feedback, please type them in the "Feedback" field.
3. If you have any additional comments or instructions for the ward staff, please type them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Maternity Ward Discharge Feedback Form** (`maternity_ward_discharge_feedback_form`, text, not required): This is the form title.
* **Ward Number** (`ward_number`, number, not required): Enter the ward number where you stayed.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, number, not required): Enter the patient ID number.
* **Date of Discharge** (`date_of_discharge`, date, not required): Enter the date of your discharge.
* **Time of Discharge** (`time_of_discharge`, time, not required): Enter the time of your discharge.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Share any feedback you have about your stay in the maternity ward.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): If you have any additional comments or instructions for the ward staff, please type them here.
* **Ward Staff Name** (`ward_staff_name`, text, not required): If you had a favorite nurse or staff member, please enter their name here.
* **Discharge Care Instructions** (`discharge_care_instructions`, text, not required): If you have any specific instructions for your discharge, please enter them here.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to be followed up with, if applicable.
