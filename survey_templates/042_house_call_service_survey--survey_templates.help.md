# House Call Service Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about house calls, including service provider details, visit frequency, and quality assessment. It will help identify areas for improvement and inform future visits.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Select the Service Provider
Fill in the "Service Provider" section, indicating if a service was provided. Choose the options that apply to your situation.

### 2. Choose the Service Type
Select the correct type of service that took place, whether it was a home visit or an office visit.

### 3. Enter Contact Information
Enter the contact person's name, phone number, and email address in the corresponding fields. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for these fields.

### 4. Set the Service Frequency
If applicable, indicate the frequency of the service visits.

### 5. Choose the Service Location
Indicate where the service visit took place, whether it was at a residential home, office location, or clinic location.

### 6. Schedule the Visit
Enter the date and time of the visit.

### 7. Add Any Additional Notes
If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Note" field to add them.

### 8. Evaluate Service Quality
Assess the quality of service provided, using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### 9. Identify Areas for Improvement
Indicate if there are any areas that need improvement.

### 10. Schedule the Next Visit
Let us know if there will be a next visit scheduled.

### 11. Additional Comments (Optional)
If you have any further comments or requests, use the "Service Request" field to add them.

### 12. Preferred Contact Method
Finally, select how you would like to be contacted for the next visit, whether by phone call, email, text message, or mail.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in the form to ensure accurate data collection.
* If a field is not applicable, simply leave it blank.
* Use the "Note" field to add any additional comments or feedback about the service visit.
* Take a moment to review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your experience or plans.
