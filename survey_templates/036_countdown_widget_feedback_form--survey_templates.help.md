# Countdown Widget Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Countdown Widget Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from users about their experience with the countdown widget. This form helps us to understand how the widget performed overall, what could be improved, and gather suggestions for future development.

## How To Complete This Form

To get the most out of this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Rate the overall performance of your countdown widget.
2. Provide a rating (1-5) of your experience with the widget.
3. Suggest areas for improvement and provide any additional comments about your experience.
4. Enter your Email address.
5. Enter your Phone Number.
6. Enter your Name.
7. Choose a category for your feedback (e.g., Bug, Suggestion, Feature Request).
8. If you would like to be contacted about this feedback, choose the appropriate response.
9. If you selected "Yes" for "Do you want to be contacted?", select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or None).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate the overall performance of your countdown widget?** (`widget_feedback`, number, required): This question asks you to rate the overall performance of the widget on a scale of 1-5.
* **What rating (1-5) would you give your countdown widget?** (`rating`, number, required): This question is a more specific rating of your experience with the widget.
* **What do you think could be improved?** (`suggestion`, text, required): Here, you can suggest specific areas for improvement and provide any additional comments about your experience with the widget.
* **Any comments about your experience with our countdown widget?** (`comments`, note, optional): This is an opportunity to provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the widget.
* **Your Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Please enter your Email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Your Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your Phone Number so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Your Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Provide your Name so we can reference your feedback in our records.
* **What category does this feedback belong to?** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose a category for your feedback (e.g., Bug, Suggestion, Feature Request).
* **Do you want to be contacted about this feedback?** (`contact_details`, select_multiple, optional): If you would like to be contacted, select "Yes" and choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or None).
* **If yes, how do you want to be contacted?** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): If you selected "Yes" for "Do you want to be contacted?", select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or None).
