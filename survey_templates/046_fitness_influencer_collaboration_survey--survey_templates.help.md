### Fitness Influencer Collaboration Survey - Help Guide

### Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from fitness influencers about their collaboration preferences and interests.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2.  Enter your Email address, which will be used to contact you.
3.  Indicate your Niche, which is the area of focus for your social media content.
4.  Select your Audience Type(s) to indicate the types of content you create.
5.  Enter your Audience Size.
6.  Enter your Social Media Platforms you have profiles on.
7.  Enter your Website, if you have one.
8.  Enter your links to your social media profiles, such as Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and more.
9.  Select the types of content you create (e.g. videos, articles, stories).
10.  Indicate your Content Frequency and Content Style.
11.  Select your Content Topics of interest.
12.  Enter the format of your content (e.g. videos, articles, stories).
13.  Enter your Bio.
14.  If you have any additional information you'd like to provide, enter it here.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Profile**: This field asks for your First Name and Last Name.
* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address for communication.
* **Niche**: Select the specific area of focus for your social media content. This could be fitness, wellness, nutrition, or any other specific area.
* **Audience Type**: Choose all the types of content you create (e.g. video, articles, stories).
* **Audience Size**: Enter the number of followers, viewers, or subscribers you have.
* **Social Media Platforms**: Enter the platforms you have profiles on.
* **Website**: Enter your website URL, if you have one.
* **Instagram Profile Link**: Enter the link to your Instagram profile.
* **TikTok Profile Link**: Enter the link to your TikTok profile.
* **YouTube Profile Link**: Enter the link to your YouTube profile.
* **Snapchat Profile Link**: Enter the link to your Snapchat profile.
* **Twitter Profile Link**: Enter the link to your Twitter profile.
* **LinkedIn Profile Link**: Enter the link to your LinkedIn profile.
* **Other Social Media Profile Link**: Enter the link to any other social media profile you have.
* **Content Types**: Select all the types of content you create (e.g. videos, articles, stories).
* **Content Frequency**: Indicate how often you post content.
* **Content Style**: Choose all the styles of content you create (e.g. serious, entertaining, informative).
* **Content Topic**: Enter the topic of your content.
* **Content Format**: Enter the format of your content (e.g. video, article, story).
* **Bio**: Enter a brief description of yourself.
* **Additional Info**: If you have any other information you'd like to share, enter it here.
