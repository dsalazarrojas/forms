# Event Attendance Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your availability and agreement to terms and conditions for events. Please complete this form carefully to ensure accurate and helpful data for event planning and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select your availability for the event by choosing "True" or "False" in the "Availability" field.
2. If you are available, select "True" in the "Terms and Conditions" field to accept the terms and conditions.
3. You can leave the "Agreement" field blank if you do not want to provide any additional comments.
4. Review your responses and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Availability** (`Availability`, select_one, required): This field asks if you are available to attend the event. Select "True" if you can attend, and "False" otherwise.
* **Option 1** (`availability_options`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information you want to provide about your availability.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to accept the terms and conditions of the event. Select "True" if you accept, and "False" otherwise.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or agreement you want to provide about the event.
* **Terms And Conditions Accept** (`terms_and_conditions_accept`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or agreement you want to provide about the terms and conditions. (Note: This field is likely a placeholder and its purpose is unclear.)
* **None** (`agreement_agreement`, text, optional): This field is a placeholder and its purpose is unclear. It is recommended to leave it blank. 

## Tips
* Please ensure you answer each field honestly to provide accurate data for event planning and management.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the event planner.
* This form is designed to be simple and easy to use. If you encounter any issues, please let us know so we can improve the form for your benefit.
