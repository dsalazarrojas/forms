# Park Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and information from park users to help us better understand their experiences and preferences regarding park usage. Your responses will help us improve the park's amenities and services to provide a better experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your response for each question by clicking on the corresponding button.
2. If a question is marked as required, make sure to provide an answer. If a question is optional, you can choose not to answer it.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. Enter your response in the text fields.
5. Once you've completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to send your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Park Usage (park_survey_1_label)** (`park_survey_1`, select_multiple, required): Select all the ways you use the park.
  - Choose from the options "Yes" or "No" to indicate the types of activities you do at the park.
* **Needs Improvement (park_needs_1_label)** (`park_needs_1`, text, optional): Share any specific needs or improvements you think the park could use.
  - Type in any thoughts or suggestions you have for making the park better.
* **Needs Improvement (park_needs_2_label)** (`park_needs_2`, text, optional): Share any other specific needs or improvements you think the park could use.
  - Type in any thoughts or suggestions you have for making the park better.
* **Needs Improvement (park_needs_3_label)** (`park_needs_3`, text, optional): Share any other specific needs or improvements you think the park could use.
  - Type in any thoughts or suggestions you have for making the park better.
* **Neighborhood (neighborhood_neighborhood)** (`neighborhood_neighborhood`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you live in the immediate neighborhood of the park.
  - Choose "Yes" if you live nearby, "No" otherwise.
* **Location (location_neighborhood)** (`location_neighborhood`, text, optional): If you live outside the immediate neighborhood, type in your location.
  - Provide the name of your neighborhood or city.
* **Park Neighborhoods (park_neighborhoods)** (`park_neighborhoods`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which other neighborhoods you've been to in the park.
  - Choose all that apply from the options "Yes" or "No".
* **Community (community_neighborhood)** (`community_neighborhood`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you feel a sense of community at the park.
  - Choose "Yes" if you do, "No" otherwise.
* **Neighborhoods (community_neighborhoods)** (`community_neighborhoods`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which neighborhoods you've seen in the park that have a sense of community.
  - Choose all that apply from the options "Yes" or "No".
* **Neighborhoods (neighborhoods)** (`neighborhoods`, text, optional): Share any observations or insights about the neighborhoods in the park.
  - Type in any thoughts or suggestions you have about the neighborhoods.
