# guess_the_value_challenge - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from teachers and teams for the "Guess the Value Challenge" project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Go through each page and fill out the required fields.
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **Teacher Name** (Teacher Name, text, required: false): Enter your name as a teacher.
* **Team Name** (Team Name, text, required: false): Enter your team's name.
* **Teacher Guess** (Teacher Guess, number, required: false): Enter your guess for the challenge.
* **Team Guess** (Team Guess, number, required: false): Enter your team's guess for the challenge.
* **Teacher Reasoning** (Teacher Reasoning, text, required: false): Explain why you made your guess.
* **Team Reasoning** (Team Reasoning, text, required: false): Explain why your team made its guess.
* **Teacher Contact Email** (Teacher Contact Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Team Contact Email** (Team Contact Email, email, required: false): Enter your team's email address for contact purposes.
* **Teacher Contact Phone** (Teacher Contact Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Team Contact Phone** (Team Contact Phone, text, required: false): Enter your team's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Reason** (Reason, text, required: false): Provide any additional reason for your guess.
* **Teacher Reason** (Teacher Reason, text, required: false): Provide any additional reason for your guess.
* **Teacher Comments** (Teacher Comments, text, required: false): Add any additional comments about your guess.
* **Team Comments** (Team Comments, text, required: false): Add any additional comments about your team's guess.

### Page 2
* **Teacher Consent** (Teacher Consent, select_one, required: true): Choose your role: I am a teacher, I am a team leader, or Other.
* **Team Consent** (Team Consent, select_one, required: true): Choose your role: I am a team leader, I am a team member, or Other.
* **Form Submission** (Form Submission, select_multiple, required: true): Confirm whether you submitted this form as a teacher or team member, and whether this submission is complete and accurate.

### Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* This form is for the purpose of the "Guess the Value Challenge" project.
* This form will help us gather data for this project.
* If you have any further questions about the form, please let us know.
