# Committee Meeting Scheduling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from committee members to schedule a meeting that suits everyone's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each section and fill in the required information. For multiple choice questions, select the option that best describes your preference or availability. For text fields, provide a brief description of your needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Committee Name** (`committee_name_survey`, text, **required**): Enter the full name of the committee you are a member of.
* **Your Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name.
* **Committee Member Role** (`participant_role`, text, **optional**): If you have a specific role within the committee, enter it here (e.g., Chair, Secretary, Member).
* **Weekly Availability and Patterns** (`section_general_availability`, note, **optional**): This section is a brief introduction to the rest of the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Which days of the week generally work best for committee meetings** (`best_days_pattern`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all the days of the week that you are generally available for meetings.
* **What is your preferred time of day for meetings** (`preferred_time_period`, select_one, **required**): Select the time of day that you prefer for meetings.
* **Proposed Specific Meeting Dates** (`section_proposed_dates`, note, **optional**): This section allows you to rate your availability for specific dates. Please select your availability for each date.
* **Monday, June 14** (`date_option_A`, select_one, **required**): Rate your availability for this date.
* **Tuesday, June 15** (`date_option_B`, select_one, **required**): Rate your availability for this date.
* **Thursday, June 17** (`date_option_C`, select_one, **required**): Rate your availability for this date.
* **Meeting Format and Logistics** (`section_format_logistics`, note, **optional**): This section is a brief introduction to the meeting format and logistics. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **What is your preferred meeting format for this session** (`meeting_format_preference`, select_one, **required**): Select the format you prefer for the meeting (in-person, online, or hybrid).
* **What is the maximum duration we should aim for** (`maximum_meeting_duration`, select_one, **required**): Select the maximum duration you are available for the meeting.
* **Are there any specific topics you would like to see on the agenda** (`agenda_suggestions`, text, **optional**): If you have any specific topics you would like to see on the agenda, enter them here.
* **Do you have any specific travel or accessibility requirements** (`travel_requirements`, text, **optional**): If you have any specific travel or accessibility requirements, enter them here.
* **Form Submission** (`section_submission`, note, **optional**): This section is a brief introduction to the form submission. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Date of Submission** (`survey_submission_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date and time you submit this form.
* **Can the committee chair contact you directly if there are scheduling conflicts** (`followup_contact`, select_one, **required**): Select whether you are available for the committee chair to contact you directly if there are scheduling conflicts.
