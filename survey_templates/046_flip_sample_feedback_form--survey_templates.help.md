# Flip Sample Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Flip Sample Feedback Form is used to collect feedback on demos or pilot tests of Flip Sample, a product or feature that is still in development. This feedback is crucial for the team to track progress and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of review that best describes the demo or pilot test.
2. Select the status of the review.
3. Describe your experience with the product X.
4. Share your suggestions for improvement.
5. Rate the overall quality of the product.
6. Choose who collected the feedback.
7. Enter the date and time of collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Demo or Pilot Review** (`demo_pilot_review`, `select_multiple`, required): 
Choose the type of review that best describes the demo or pilot test. Options are: Not good, Somewhat good, Very good.
* **Pre-launch Review** (`pre_launch_review`, `select_one`, required): 
Select the status of the review. Options are: Yes, No.
* **Product X Experience** (`product_x_experience`, `text`, optional): 
Describe your experience with the product. This field is optional but provides valuable insights.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, optional): 
Share your suggestions for improvement. This field is optional but helps the team make targeted changes.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required): 
Rate the overall quality of the product on a scale of 1-10.
* **Collected by** (`collected_by`, `select_one`, required): 
Choose who collected the feedback. Options are: Team A, Team B, Team C.
* **Collected at** (`collected_at`, `date`, required): 
Enter the date when the feedback was collected.
* **Collected at time** (`collected_at_time`, `time`, required): 
Enter the time when the feedback was collected.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form for every demo or pilot test to help us make data-driven decisions.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your experience with the product.
* Provide concrete suggestions for improvement to help us improve Flip Sample.
* Rate the overall quality of the product honestly to help us prioritize improvements.
