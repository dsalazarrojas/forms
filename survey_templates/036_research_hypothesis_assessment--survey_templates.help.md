# Research Hypothesis Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and assess the research hypothesis submitted by the researcher. This form helps to ensure that the hypothesis is clear, well-defined, and meets the necessary standards for further research and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Research Hypothesis" page to start filling out the form.
2. Enter a brief description of your research hypothesis in the "Research Hypothesis" field.
3. Provide your name and contact information as the "Reviewer Name" field.
4. If applicable, enter your rating and rating comment for the research hypothesis.
5. Optionally, provide the date when you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Hypothesis**: Enter a clear and concise statement of your research hypothesis. This is an optional field, but it's recommended that you provide a brief description to help others understand your research idea.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter your name as the reviewer or the person submitting this form. This is an optional field, but it's recommended that you provide your name to help with tracking and follow-up communication.
* **Rating**: If you've reviewed the research hypothesis, please rate it on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest. This rating is optional, but it provides a quick way for others to get a sense of your opinion.
* **Rating Comment**: If you'd like to provide more information about your rating, enter a brief comment in this field. This is optional, but it helps to give context to your rating.
* **Date Submitted**: If you're submitting this form for someone else, please indicate when it was submitted. This field is optional and helps with tracking and record-keeping.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all optional fields that are relevant to your submission.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- This form is designed to be simple and easy to use, so don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need help.
