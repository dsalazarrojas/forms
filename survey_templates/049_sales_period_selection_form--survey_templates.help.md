# Sales Period Selection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sales Period Selection Form is used to select the sales period for a survey, ensuring that the relevant data is captured and analyzed correctly. This form helps to identify the correct sales period, providing a clear and concise set of options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Sales Period Selection Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant sales period using the options provided.
2. Enter the start and end dates for the selected sales period.
3. Confirm your consent to the terms and conditions by selecting the appropriate option.
4. If you have any additional comments, you can enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Period** (`sales_period_selection_form`, select_multiple, required): Select the sales period for this survey using the options provided.
* **Start Date** (`sales_period_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the sales period.
* **End Date** (`sales_period_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the sales period.
* **Do you consent to the terms and conditions?** (`sales_period_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions by selecting the appropriate option.
* **Additional comments** (`sales_period_additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes related to the sales period.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct sales period to ensure accurate data capture.
* Enter the start and end dates accurately to avoid any errors.
* Carefully review the terms and conditions before selecting the consent option.
* Use the "Additional Comments" field to provide any additional information that may be relevant to the sales period.
