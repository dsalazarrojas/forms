# Future Generation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from the younger generation on their expectations, concerns, and experiences related to their future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. For questions that ask for multiple selections, you can select multiple options.
3. Enter your responses in the text fields as clearly and concisely as possible.
4. Make sure to submit your form completely before leaving.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief text field where you can introduce yourself and provide any relevant context or background information.
* **Generation**: A single selection to indicate if you believe you are a member of the younger generation (yes/no).
* **What do you expect from your future?**: A text field where you can describe your expectations from your future.
* **What do you expect from your future? answer**: A selection to confirm or deny your previous statement.
* **What are your main concerns in life?**: A text field where you can list your main concerns in life.
* **What are your main concerns in life? answer**: A multiple selection to confirm or deny your concerns.
* **What do you believe is the most important issue your generation faces today?**: A text field where you can describe the most important issue you believe your generation faces today.
* **What do you believe is the most important issue your generation faces today? answer**: A single selection to confirm or deny your statement.
* **What skills do you believe you will need to succeed in your future?**: A text field where you can describe the skills you believe you will need to succeed.
* **What skills do you believe you will need to succeed in your future? answer**: A multiple selection to confirm or deny your skills.
* **What skills do you believe are the most important for your future?**: A text field where you can describe the most important skills for your future.
* **What skills do you believe are the most important for your future? answer**: A single selection to confirm or deny your skills.
* **What do you believe is the role of technology in your future?**: A multiple selection to indicate the role of technology in your future.
* **What do you believe is the role of technology in your future? answer**: A single selection to confirm or deny your statement.
* **What do you believe is the likely impact on your future?**: A multiple selection to indicate the likely impact on your future.
* **What do you believe is the likely impact on your future? answer**: A single selection to confirm or deny your statement.
* **Any additional comments**: A text field where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Any additional comments answer**: A multiple selection to confirm or deny your comments.
* **Any further questions or concerns**: A text field where you can describe any further questions or concerns.
* **Any further questions or concerns answer**: A single selection to confirm or deny your statement.
* **Any other information**: A text field where you can provide any other information.
* **Any other information answer**: A multiple selection to confirm or deny your statement.

Note: This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions, so please answer honestly and concisely.
