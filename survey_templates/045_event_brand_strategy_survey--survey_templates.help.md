# Event Brand Strategy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your event's brand strategy. This information will help us understand your event's current branding state and identify areas for improvement. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form's sections and questions carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. You can skip questions that do not apply to your event.
5. If you need more information or clarification on a question, please reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Category (event_category)
* **Event Category** (`event_category`, select_one, \*): Select the category that best describes your event. This will help us understand the type of event you are promoting.

### Event Subcategory (event_subcategory)
* **Event Subcategory** (`event_subcategory`, select_multiple, \*): Select all subcategories that apply to your event. This will help us understand the specific details of your event.

### Event Name (event_name)
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, \*): Enter the name of your event.

### Brand Performance (brand_performance)
* **Brand Performance** (`brand_performance`, number, \*): Rate your event's current brand performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "poor" and 5 is "excellent".

### Perception of Brand (perception_of_brand)
* **Perception of Brand** (`perception_of_brand`, time, \*): Estimate how long it takes for people to develop a positive perception of your brand, on a scale of 1-10 minutes.

### Future Planning (future_planning)
* **Future Planning** (`future_planning`, select_one, \*): Select how often you plan to review and update your event's brand strategy in the future.

### Event Promotion (event_promotion)
* **Event Promotion** (`event_promotion`, text): Enter any details about how you promote your event. 

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* You can skip questions that do not apply to your event.
* If you need more information or clarification on a question, please reach out to us.
