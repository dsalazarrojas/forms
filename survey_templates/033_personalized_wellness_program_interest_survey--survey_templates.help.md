# Personalized Wellness Program Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your interests and needs, which will help us create a personalized wellness program tailored to your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the required fields marked with \*.
2. Select all the options that apply to you for each question.
3. Enter your email address for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client ID** (`client_id`, number, *): This is a unique identifier assigned to you as a client. Please enter your client ID if you have one.
* **Program Interests** (`program_interests`, select_multiple, false): Indicate which wellness programs you are interested in. You can select multiple options.
	+ Yes: You are interested in this program.
	+ No: You are not interested in this program.
* **Goals** (`goals`, select_multiple, false): Indicate your goals for participating in a wellness program. You can select multiple options.
	+ Yes: You are interested in this goal.
	+ No: You are not interested in this goal.
* **Program Goals** (`program_goals`, select_multiple, false): Indicate the specific goals you would like to achieve through the wellness program. You can select multiple options.
	+ Yes: You are interested in this goal.
	+ No: You are not interested in this goal.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, select_one, false): Indicate your current health status.
	+ Active: You are actively engaged in physical activities and exercises regularly.
	+ Inactive: You are not regularly engaged in physical activities and exercises.
* **Health Coach Referral** (`health_coach_referral`, select_one, false): Would you like to be referred to a health coach?
	+ Yes: You would like to be referred to a health coach.
	+ No: You do not want to be referred to a health coach.
* **Wellness Center** (`wellness_center`, select_one, false): Are you interested in visiting a wellness center?
	+ Yes: You are interested in visiting a wellness center.
	+ No: You are not interested in visiting a wellness center.
* **Survey Comments** (`survey_comments`, text, false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, *): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for further information. Please use a valid email address.
