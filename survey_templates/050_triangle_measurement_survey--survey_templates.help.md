# Triangle Measurement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a survey related to measuring a triangle. It has been created to gather information on the best way to measure a triangle, the ease of use of the method, the role of the user in using the survey, and other relevant details such as the accuracy of the data collected and the frequency of use. The form is divided into several pages, each addressing a specific aspect of the survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Start by reading the Introduction page, which provides some background information on the purpose of the form.
* On the "What is the best way to measure a triangle?" page, provide your answer in the text field. You can type your response freely.
* On the "How do you rate the ease of use of this method?" page, select one of the options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* On the "What is your role in using this survey?" page, select all the options that apply from the list.
* On the "How do you rate the accuracy of the data collected?" page, enter a number in the field to rate the accuracy.
* On the "When do you use the Triangle Measurement Survey?" page, enter a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

* **Introduction (Page 1)**: This page provides some background information on the purpose of the form.
