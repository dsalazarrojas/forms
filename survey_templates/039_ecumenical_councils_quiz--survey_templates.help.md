# Ecumenical Councils Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about ecumenical councils and create a quiz to test users' knowledge on the subject.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the survey question in the **Survey Question** field.
2. Enter the name of the instructor who will be asking the question.
3. Enter the name of the ecumenical council.
4. Select the type of council (Church, Ecumenical, Synod, or Other).
5. Enter the date of the council.
6. Enter the time of the council.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Question** (1): A text field to input the survey question you want to ask.
* **Instructor Name** (2): Enter the name of the person who will be asking the question.
* **Council Name** (3): Enter the name of the ecumenical council.
* **Council Type** (4): Select the type of ecumenical council (Church, Ecumenical, Synod, or Other).
* **Council Date** (5): Enter the date of the council in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Council Time** (6): Enter the time of the council in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* The council type field has four options: Church, Ecumenical, Synod, and Other.
* The date and time fields are for the date and time of the council, respectively.
* If you are unsure about a field, you can always contact form@gic.mx for more information.
