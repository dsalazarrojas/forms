# Open Ended Responses Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect open-ended responses from users for two questions, allowing for more in-depth information to be gathered in a free-formatted text format.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply type your answers to the two open-ended questions provided. You can respond in any style you prefer, and submit the form once you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (note, optional):
	+ This section is a brief introduction to the survey. You can read through it to understand the context and purpose of the questions.
* **Open Ended Question 1** (text, optional):
	+ Type your answer to the first question in the text box provided. You can respond in any style, including paragraphs or short answers.
* **Open Ended Question 2** (text, optional):
	+ Type your answer to the second question in the text box provided. You can respond in any style, including paragraphs or short answers.
* **Submit** (button, optional):
	+ Once you've completed the two questions, click the "Submit" button to submit your responses. 

## Tips
* Take your time to think through your answers and respond as thoroughly as possible.
* You can respond to both questions in one or more sessions, as the form does not have any specific time limits.
* If you have any trouble submitting the form, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
