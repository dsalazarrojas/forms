# user_experience_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The user_experience_questionnaire form is designed to collect feedback on a user's experience with a product or service. This form gathers both qualitative and quantitative data to help identify areas of improvement and provide suggestions for enhancement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all the options that apply to your experience.
2. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.
3. Rate your experience using the provided scales.
4. Answer the questions with the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Experience Survey** (`user_experience_survey`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **User Experience Notes** (`user_experience_notes`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **User Experience Score** (`user_experience_score`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience on a scale (likely to be defined by the form developer).
* **User Experience Improvement** (`user_experience_improvement`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one option to provide suggestions for improvement.
* **User Experience Comments** (`user_experience_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **User Experience Recommendation** (`user_experience_recommendation`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one option to provide a recommendation.
* **User Experience Rating** (`user_experience_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience on a scale (likely to be defined by the form developer).
* **User Experience Suggestions** (`user_experience_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional suggestions.
* **Additional Comments** (`user_experience_additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Likely to Recommend** (`user_experience_likely_to_recommend`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Would Recommend** (`user_experience_would_recommend`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one option to provide a recommendation.
* **Would Recommend Comments** (`user_experience_would_recommend_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Would Recommend Rating** (`user_experience_would_recommend_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one option to provide a rating.
* **Would Recommend Suggestions** (`user_experience_would_recommend_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional suggestions.
* **Time Spent** (`user_experience_time_spent`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one option to indicate the time spent.
* **Time Spent Hours** (`user_experience_time_spent_hours`, `number`, required: false): Provide the number of hours spent.
* **Time Spent Minutes** (`user_experience_time_spent_minutes`, `number`, required: false): Provide the number of minutes spent.
* **Time Spent Comments** (`user_experience_time_spent_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Pain Points** (`user_experience_pain_points`, `text`, required: false): Describe the pain points or issues you experienced.

* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`user_experience_suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, required: false): Provide any suggestions for improvement.

* **Likely to Recommend Again** (`user_experience_likely_to_recommend_again`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one option to provide a recommendation to others.
* **Additional Suggestions** (`user_experience_additional_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional suggestions.
