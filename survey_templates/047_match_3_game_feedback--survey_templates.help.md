# Match 3 Game Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from players on their experience with the match-3 puzzle game, allowing game developers or administrators to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about the game's development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the page you want to provide feedback on:
	* Page 1: "Gameplay" - Provide feedback on the overall gameplay experience.
	* Page 2: "Technical Issues" - Report any technical issues you encountered while playing the game.
	* Page 3: "Feature Preferences" - Select which game features you prefer or dislike.
	* Page 4: "Suggestions" - Provide suggestions for new features or improvements.
	* Page 5: "Comments" - Enter any additional comments or thoughts about the game.
2. Fill in the corresponding page:
	* On "Gameplay", describe your overall experience playing the game.
	* On "Technical Issues", describe any issues you encountered while playing the game.
	* On "Feature Preferences", select the features you like or dislike by choosing from the available options.
	* On "Suggestions", provide any ideas you have for new features or improvements.
	* On "Comments", enter any additional thoughts or comments about the game.
3. Click "Submit" to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gameplay** (`Gameplay`, text, required: false): Describe your overall experience playing the game.
* **Technical Issues** (`Technical Issues`, text, required: false): Report any technical issues you encountered while playing the game, such as bugs or glitches.
* **Feature Preferences** (`Feature Preferences`, select multiple, required: false): Select the features you like or dislike by choosing from the available options. You can choose one or more options.
* **Suggestions** (`Suggestions`, text, required: false): Provide any ideas you have for new features or improvements you'd like to see in the game.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional thoughts or comments about the game, such as what you liked or disliked about it.

## Tips
* Be honest and detailed in your feedback to help game developers understand your experience.
* Use the "Feature Preferences" field to indicate which features you enjoy or dislike, as this will help developers know what to focus on.
* If you have a suggestion, try to be specific and provide enough details so that developers can understand your idea.
* Remember to enter any additional comments or thoughts in the "Comments" field, even if they're not directly related to a specific feature or issue.
