# Animal Classification Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is an assessment tool designed to test your knowledge of animal taxonomy and classification. It consists of 16 questions that cover various aspects of animal classification, including kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, and species. The quiz aims to evaluate your understanding of the hierarchical structure of the animal kingdom and your ability to identify animals based on their characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions by selecting the correct options from the provided lists. Make sure to answer all questions, as some questions have multiple parts or require multiple selections.
