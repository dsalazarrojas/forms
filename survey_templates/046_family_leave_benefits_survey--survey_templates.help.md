# Family Leave Benefits Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is intended to gather information about employee awareness, usage, and satisfaction with family leave benefits policies within an organization. The survey aims to understand the current state of these policies and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field, providing as much detail as possible. If a field is not applicable, you can select "Not Applicable" or leave it blank. Please answer honestly, and do not hesitate to seek clarification if you have any questions or doubts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Leave Benefits Survey** (`family_leave_benefits_survey`, text, optional): This is the title of the survey, which is meant to be filled in with a descriptive title.
* **awareness** (`awareness`, select_one, optional): Select the type of policy you are aware of regarding family leave benefits:
	+ Family Leave Policy
	+ Family Leave Benefits Policy
	+ Other Company Policy
* **usage** (`usage`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the ways you use family leave benefits:
	+ Family Leave Policy
	+ Family Leave Benefits Policy
	+ Other Company Policy
* **satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with family leave benefits:
	+ Highly Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments about family leave benefits.
* **company policy** (`company_policy`, select_one, optional): Select the type of policy that best describes your company's approach to family leave benefits:
	+ Family Leave Policy
	+ Family Leave Benefits Policy
	+ Other Company Policy
* **benefits** (`benefits`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the benefits you receive from family leave:
	+ Family Leave Policy
	+ Family Leave Benefits Policy
	+ Other Company Policy
* **policy changes** (`policy_changes`, time, optional): How long do you think policy changes take:
	+ Less than 1 month
	+ 1-3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ More than 6 months
* **employee engagement** (`employee_engagement`, select_one, optional): How engaged are employees with family leave benefits:
	+ Highly Engaged
	+ Somewhat Engaged
	+ Not Engaged
* **employee satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Rate employee satisfaction with family leave benefits:
	+ Highly Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
* **manager satisfaction** (`manager_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Rate manager satisfaction with family leave benefits:
	+ Highly Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
* **manager engagement** (`manager_engagement`, select_multiple, optional): Rate manager engagement with family leave benefits:
	+ Highly Engaged
	+ Somewhat Engaged
	+ Not Engaged
* **employee participation** (`employee_participation`, select_one, optional): How often do employees participate in family leave:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low

Please note that this guide is intended to be a general explanation and may not cover every possible scenario. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact the form administrator.
