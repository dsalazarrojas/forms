# Mid Semester Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from students on their engagement and experiences with the course. The form is designed to help instructors understand the strengths and weaknesses of the course and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: This is a brief text box where you can provide any comments or feedback about the course.
2. **Question 1: What is your current level of engagement with the course?**: Select one of the following options:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
3. **Question 2: How do you rate the quality of instruction?**: Select one of the following options:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
4. **Question 3: What do you like most about the course?**: Select all that apply:
	* Lectures
	* Discussions
	* Readings
	* Assignments
	* Other
5. **Question 4: Are there any topics that you feel are missing or could be improved?**: Type in your thoughts and suggestions.
6. **Question 5: Would you like to be able to attend more lectures?**: Type in your thoughts and suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (1)**: This is a text box where you can provide any comments or feedback about the course.
* **What is your current level of engagement with the course (2)**: This question helps us understand how engaged you are with the course. Select the option that best describes your level of engagement.
* **How do you rate the quality of instruction (3)**: This question helps us understand how helpful and effective the instruction is. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **What do you like most about the course (4)**: This question helps us understand what aspects of the course you enjoy the most. Select all that apply.
* **Are there any topics that you feel are missing or could be improved (5)**: This question helps us understand any areas of the course that you think need improvement.
* **Would you like to be able to attend more lectures (6)**: This question helps us understand if there's anything you'd like to see change in the course.

## Tips

* Be as specific and honest as possible when answering the questions.
* If you select "Other" for Question 3, please provide more information about what you would like to see improved or added.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for the course, feel free to share them in Question 4 or Question 5.
