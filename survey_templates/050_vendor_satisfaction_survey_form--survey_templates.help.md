# Vendor Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from vendors on their experience with your company. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and areas of success, allowing your team to make data-driven decisions to enhance relationships and services with vendors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with the relevant information.
2. Select the desired rating for the quality of service provided by the vendor.
3. Enter any comments or feedback you have about the experience with the vendor.
4. Enter the vendor's name and contact information, if applicable.
5. Choose the person assigned to the vendor, if applicable.
6. Enter a brief description of the survey, if desired.
7. Enter the vendor's name and contact information, if applicable.
8. Date the survey was sent to the vendor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey_form** (Vendor Survey Form, text, required): Enter the survey questions and answers here.
* **rating** (Rating, select_one, required): This is the rating for the quality of service provided by the vendor.
  - Select the number that best corresponds with your level of satisfaction.
* **comment** (Comment, text, required): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the experience with the vendor.
* **overall_rating** (Overall Rating, select_one, required): This is the overall rating for the vendor's experience.
  - Select the number that best corresponds with your level of satisfaction.
* **email** (Email, email, required): Enter the vendor's email address.
* **phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **assigned_to** (Assigned To, select_multiple, required): Choose the person assigned to the vendor.
  - Select the names of the people who were assigned to the vendor.
* **description** (Description, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the survey, if desired.
* **vendor_name** (Vendor Name, text, required): Enter the vendor's name.
* **vendor_contact** (Vendor Contact, text, optional): Enter the vendor's contact information, if applicable.
* **survey_sent_on** (Survey Sent On, date, required): Date the survey was sent to the vendor.
