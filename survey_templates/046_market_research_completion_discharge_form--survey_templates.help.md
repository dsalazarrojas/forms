# Market Research Completion Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Research Completion Discharge Form is designed to collect information from market research participants after they have completed a study or survey. This form helps gather essential details about the participants, their experience, and any feedback they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide participant details if applicable.
2.  Fill in the survey details if you were part of a survey.
3.  Enter your contact details if you'd like to be contacted for further information.
4.  Describe the completion of the research study or survey if you were a part of it.
5.  Leave feedback on your experience with the study or survey.
6.  Indicate your current participant status.
7.  Sign the participant informed consent if you're confirming you were part of the study or survey.
8.  Share your demographics if you're willing.
9.  List the research study you participated in.
10. Provide the study institution where the study was conducted.
11. Describe the survey instruments used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Enter any additional details about yourself, such as your name, identification number, or any other relevant information. (optional)
* **Survey Details**: Provide a brief description of the survey you participated in. (optional)
* **Contact Details**: Enter a contact number and/or email address where you can be reached for further information. (optional)
* **Completion Details**: Describe the process and experience of completion of the research study or survey. (optional)
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions about the research study or survey. (optional)
* **Participant Status**: Indicate your current participant status, such as whether you completed the study or not. (optional)
* **Participant Informed Consent**: Sign this to confirm you were aware of the study and its requirements. (optional)
* **Participant Demographics**: Share your demographics if you'd like to be included in any further analysis or follow-up research. (optional)
* **Research Study**: Briefly describe the research study you participated in. (optional)
* **Study Institution**: Provide the name and location of the institution where the research study was conducted. (optional)
* **Survey Instruments**: List any tools or instruments used during the survey, such as questionnaires or question types. (optional)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out as much information as you're comfortable sharing.
* If unsure about any field, you can leave it blank, but please try to provide as much details as possible.
* This form is designed to collect feedback and information, so don't hesitate to share your honest thoughts.
* If you have any further questions, please contact form@gic.mx.
