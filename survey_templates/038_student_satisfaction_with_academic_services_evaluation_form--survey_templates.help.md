# Student Satisfaction With Academic Services Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on the academic services you have received. Your responses will help us understand your level of satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question carefully and select only the options that apply to you. Do not hesitate to add any additional comments if you think it will help us improve our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction** (1): Rate your overall satisfaction with the services you have received. Please select one of the options.
* **Academic Services Importance** (2): Select all the options that you consider important for your academic success.
	+ Very Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Not Important
* **Academic Services Quality** (3): Rate the quality of the services you received.
* **Comments** (4): Share any additional comments or concerns you have about the services.
* **Contact Information** (5): Enter your email address to help us contact you if needed.
* **Academic Services Response Rate** (6): Select the response rate you experienced with the services.
	+ 1-10
	+ 11-20
	+ 21-30
	+ 31-40
	+ 41-50
* **Overall Satisfaction (2)** (7): Rate your overall satisfaction with the services. (Note: This is a duplicate question of "Overall Satisfaction" field 1, please skip this field.)
* **Academic Services Importance Multiple** (8): Select all options that apply to your academic services. (Note: This field is optional and only required for future reference.)
* **Follow-up Frequency** (9): Select the frequency of follow-up communications you experienced with the services.
	+ 1-10
	+ 11-20
	+ 21-30
	+ 31-40
	+ 41-50

## Tips
* Please answer each question carefully to provide accurate feedback.
* Select only the options that apply to you to avoid confusion.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please add them in the "Comments" field.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, so please don't hesitate to provide it!
