# Employee Sentiment Summary Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Sentiment Summary Request Form is a tool used by managers to collect and record information about employee sentiment, which is the way employees feel about their work, workplace, or overall job satisfaction. This form is meant to be filled out by employees' managers to gather data on employee sentiment over a specific time period.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select the relevant information that you'd like to report for the employee.
*   Fill in the fields with the requested information.
*   Click 'Submit' to save your input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Sentiment Summary Request Form Page 1** (`employee_sentiment_summary_request_form_page_1`, text, optional): This field is a page title and doesn't require any specific input.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the manager responsible for this employee.
*   **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the manager responsible for this employee.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID number assigned to the employee.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee.
*   **Employee Department** (`employee_department`, text, optional): Enter the department that the employee belongs to.
*   **Employee Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the employee.
*   **Sentiment Start Date** (`sentiment_start_date`, date, optional): Select the start date of the sentiment period.
*   **Sentiment End Date** (`sentiment_end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of the sentiment period.
*   **Sentiment Frequency** (`sentiment_frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency at which the sentiment was experienced during the period.
*   **Sentiment Type** (`sentiment_type`, text, optional): Choose the type of sentiment that was experienced.
*   **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Write any additional comments about the employee's sentiment.
