# Business Income Reconstruction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Income Reconstruction Survey is a template designed to collect essential information about a business's income for accounting or auditing purposes. It helps businesses provide accurate financial data for tax or regulatory reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant reconstruction period for your business (Last 6 Months, Last Fiscal Year, Current Year-to-Date, or Specific Audit Period).
3. Choose the reason for reconstruction (Loss of Records (Fire/Theft), Data Corruption/IT Failure, Standard Audit Procedure, or Inaccurate Previous Reporting).
4. Estimate your average monthly sales in USD based on available indirect evidence.
5. Select the approximate breakdown of cash vs. card revenue.
6. Estimate the average daily transaction count.
7. Input the total supplier invoices for the period in USD for cost-of-goods-sold verification.
8. Choose the inventory valuation method used (FIFO, LIFO, Weighted Average, or Specific Identification).
9. Estimate the gross margin percentage.
10. Select the status of supporting documents (All Available, Partially Available, or Minimal Documentation Exists).
11. If necessary, provide an explanation for significant discrepancies.
12. Sign and date the form to certify the reconstruction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Please enter your business's name as registered with the relevant authority.
* **Reconstruction Period**: Select the time frame for which you are estimating your income. This could be the last 6 months, last fiscal year, current year-to-date, or a specific audit period.
* **Reason for Reconstruction**: Indicate the reason you are reconstructing your income data. This could be due to loss of records (fire or theft), data corruption or IT failure, standard audit procedure, or inaccurate previous reporting.
* **Estimated Average Monthly Sales (USD)**: Based on available indirect evidence, provide an estimate of your average monthly sales in USD. This will help in verifying your business's income.
* **Cash vs Card Revenue Ratio**: Select the approximate breakdown of your revenue coming from cash and card transactions.
* **Average Daily Transaction Count**: Estimate the number of customers you have per day.
* **Total Supplier Invoices for the Period (USD)**: Input the total invoices from your suppliers for the period in USD. This helps in cost-of-goods-sold verification.
* **Inventory Valuation Method**: Choose the method you use to value your inventory (FIFO, LIFO, Weighted Average, or Specific Identification).
* **Estimated Gross Margin Percentage**: Provide an estimate of your gross margin percentage.
* **Supporting Documents Status**: Select the status of your supporting documents. You could have all available, partially available, or minimal documentation exists.
* **Explanation of Significant Discrepancies**: If there are any major variations from expected results, please provide an explanation.
* **Signature of Preparer**: Type your full name to certify the reconstruction.
* **Date Signed**: Date and sign the form to complete the reconstruction process.
