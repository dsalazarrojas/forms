# Racism_Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experiences with racism and discrimination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct options for demographic information about yourself in the "demographic_info", "race", "ethnicity", "religion", and "gender" fields.
2. Choose the appropriate option for your age in the "age" field.
3. Select the relevant option for your education level in the "education" field.
4. Choose the correct option for your employment status in the "employment_status" field.
5. Select all the relevant options for your experience with racism in the "experience" field.
6. Choose the correct option for information about the perpetrator in the "perpetrator_info" field.
7. Select all the relevant options for the perpetrator's demographic information in the "perpetrator_relationship", "perpetrator_type", and "perpetrator_location" fields.
8. Enter a description of the incident in the "incident_details" field.
9. Enter a description of the perpetrator in the "perpetrator_description" field.
10. Enter the perpetrator's age in the "perpetrator_age" field.
11. Select the relevant option for the perpetrator's education level in the "perpetrator_education" field.
12. Choose the correct option for the perpetrator's employment status in the "perpetrator_employment_status" field.
13. Select all the relevant options for the perpetrator's relationship with you in the "perpetrator_relationship" field.
14. Select all the relevant options for the perpetrator's type in the "perpetrator_type" field.
15. Select all the relevant options for the perpetrator's location in the "perpetrator_location" field.
16. Enter a description of the incident involving the perpetrator in the "perpetrator_incident" field.
17. Enter any relevant information about yourself in the "respondent_info" field.
18. Select the relevant option for your education level in the "respondent_education" field.

* **experience**: Select all the options that describe your experience with racism.
* **perpetrator_info**: Choose the option that best describes the information about the perpetrator.
* **perpetrator_relationship**: Select all the options that best describe the relationship between you and the perpetrator.
* **perpetrator_type**: Select all the options that best describe the type of the perpetrator.
* **perpetrator_location**: Select all the options that best describe the location of the perpetrator.
* **incident_details**: Enter a description of the incident.
* **perpetrator_description**: Enter a description of the perpetrator.
* **perpetrator_age**: Enter the age of the perpetrator.
* **perpetrator_education**: Select the option that best describes the education level of the perpetrator.
* **perpetrator_employment_status**: Select the option that best describes the employment status of the perpetrator.
