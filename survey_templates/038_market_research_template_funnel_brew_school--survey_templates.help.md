## Purpose
This form template is designed to collect data on the company's background and team members' details for market research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the title of the company in the **Company Information** field.
2. Select all the roles that apply to the team member in the **Role** field.
3. Enter the team member's job title in the **Job Title** field.
4. Enter the team member's department in the **Department** field.
5. Enter a number in the **Team Size** field to indicate the team member's team size.
6. Enter the team member's contact email address in the **Contact Email** field.
7. Enter the team member's contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
8. Select the team member's job level from the options provided in the **Job Level** field.
9. Select one job function that best describes the team member's job function from the options provided in the **Job Function** field.
10. Indicate whether the company has industry relation in the **Industry Relation** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, `required`): This is the first page of the survey, where you can write a brief introduction to the company.
* **Company Information** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Please enter the name of the company.
* **Role** (`3`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the roles that apply to the team member. For example, "Yes" or "No".
* **Job Title** (`4`, `text`, `required`): Enter the job title of the team member.
* **Department** (`5`, `text`, `required`): Enter the department of the team member.
* **Team Size** (`6`, `number`, `required`): Please enter a number to indicate the size of the team.
* **Contact Email** (`7`, `email`, `required`): Enter the team member's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`8`, `text`, `required`): Enter the team member's phone number.
* **Job Level** (`9`, `text`, `required`): Select the job level of the team member from the options provided.
* **Job Function** (`10`, `select_one`, `required`): Select one job function that best describes the team member's job function.
* **Industry Relation** (`11`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate whether the company has industry relation by selecting "Yes" or "No".
