# it_support_service_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect feedback from users on the support service provided by your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your general feedback in the "General Feedback" field.
2. Rate your experience with the support service by selecting a number from 1 to 5 in the "Support Service Rating" field.
3. If you had an issue with the support service, describe it in detail in the "Issue Description" field.
4. If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up, provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Feedback** (`general_feedback`, text, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any general comments or thoughts about your experience with the support service.
* **Support Service Rating** (`support_service_rating`, select_one, required: false): Select a number from 1 to 5 to rate your experience with the support service. A higher number indicates a better experience.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required: false): If you had an issue with the support service, describe it in as much detail as possible.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up, provide your name, email address, and/or phone number.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you had an issue, try to describe it in a neutral tone, without expressing emotions or bias.
* If you have suggestions for improvement, consider sharing them in the "General Feedback" field.
* If you'd like to be contacted, make sure to provide your contact information.
* If you don't have any feedback, feel free to skip this form!
