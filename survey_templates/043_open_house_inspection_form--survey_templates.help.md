# Open House Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and feedback during an open house inspection, which is an essential step in the home buying or property-related process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information (Name and Contact Number) to ensure we can reach you for any questions or follow-ups.
2. Provide the date and time of the event you're referring to.
3. Answer the questions regarding event registration forms and survey templates to help us understand your preferences and needs.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to include them in the provided text fields.
