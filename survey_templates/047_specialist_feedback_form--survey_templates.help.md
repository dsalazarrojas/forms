# Specialist Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specialist Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from clients and specialists on a specific issue or case. This form helps in gathering valuable insights and comments from clients and specialists, which can be used to improve services, identify areas of improvement, and provide a better understanding of client needs and specialist feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Specialist Feedback Form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information and answers before submitting the form.
* Enter the client name and specialist role in the respective fields.
* Choose a feedback date, which is the date when the feedback was received.
* Provide a brief description of the feedback received from the client or specialist.
* Rate the client's satisfaction with the service (1-5).
* Choose whether you would recommend the client or specialist (True or False, or Maybe).
* Enter any comments or additional information about the feedback.
* Enter the specialist's email and phone number for easy contact.
* Enter the client's email and phone number for easy contact.
* Select the assigned tool or communication method (Chat, Email, Phone).
* Enter the name of the specialist assigned to the client.
* Enter a brief description of the feedback, if any.
* Enter any notes about the feedback.
* Enter the form title and category for reference.
* Enter a description of the form for reference.
* Enter the date and time when the feedback was submitted.
* Enter the date and time when the form was updated.
* Enter the date and time when the form was created.
* Enter the user ID for reference.
* Enter the form category and description for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (1): Enter the name of the client who provided the feedback.
* **Specialist Role** (2): Enter the role of the specialist who provided the feedback.
* **Feedback Date** (3): Enter the date when the feedback was received.
* **Feedback Details** (4): Enter a brief description of the feedback received.
* **Rating** (5): Rate the client's satisfaction with the service (1-5).
* **Recommendation** (6): Choose whether you would recommend the client or specialist (True or False, or Maybe).
* **Comments** (7): Enter any additional comments or information about the feedback.
* **Specialist Email** (8): Enter the email address of the specialist who provided the feedback.
* **Specialist Phone** (9): Enter the phone number of the specialist who provided the feedback.
* **Client Email** (10): Enter the email address of the client who provided the feedback.
* **Client Phone** (11): Enter the phone number of the client who provided the feedback.
* **Assigned Tool** (12): Select the communication method used (Chat, Email, Phone).
* **Assigned To** (13): Enter the name of the specialist assigned to the client.
* **Feedback** (14): Enter a brief description of the feedback received.
* **Notes** (15): Enter any notes about the feedback.
* **Form ID** (16): Enter a reference ID for the form.
* **Category** (17): Enter the category of the form for reference.
* **Description** (18): Enter a description of the form for reference.
* **Submitted At** (19): Enter the date and time when the feedback was submitted.
* **Updated At** (20): Enter the date and time when the form was updated.
* **Created At** (21): Enter the date and time when the form was created.
* **User ID** (22): Enter the ID of the user who filled the form.
* **Form Title** (23): Enter the title of the form for reference.
* **Form Category** (24): Enter the category of the form for reference.
* **Form Description** (25): Enter a description of the form for reference.
