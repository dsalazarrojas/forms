# Skin Treatment Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Treatment Feedback Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from users about their skin treatment experience with our team. We want to understand how well our services meet your expectations and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
2. Fill out all the required fields. If a field is marked as "optional," you can skip it if you do not have any information to provide.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers to help us understand your experience better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Skin Treatment Feedback** (`skin_treatment_feedback`, text): This is the main question where you can share your overall feedback about the skin treatment you received.
* **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, text): Please describe the type of skin treatment you received (e.g., facelift, peel, etc.).
* **Skin Feelings** (`skin_feelings`, text): Tell us about your feelings after the skin treatment (e.g., satisfied, unhappy, etc.).
* **Skin Condition** (`skin_condition`, select_one): Please select the best option that describes your skin condition after the treatment (e.g., improved, worsened, etc.).
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one): Choose the option that best describes your level of satisfaction with the skin treatment (e.g., very satisfied, not satisfied, etc.).
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text): If you have any recommendations for us to improve our services, please provide them here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please type them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email): If you want to be contacted back for further information or clarification, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): If you want to be contacted back for further information or clarification, please provide your phone number.
