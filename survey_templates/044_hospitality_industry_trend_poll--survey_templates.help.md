# Hospitality Industry Trend Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the process of completing the Hospitality Industry Trend Poll form, which aims to gather information on your experiences with trends and challenges in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select your role within the hospitality industry**: Choose your position from the options listed in the "Hotel Management" field.
2. **Identify your region**: Select all the regions that apply to you from the "Which region do you belong to?" field.
3. **Enter your organization type**: Choose the type of organization you work for from the "What type of organization do you work for?" field.
4. **Describe your biggest challenges**: Type in your response to the "What are your biggest challenges when it comes to trends and challenges?" field.
5. **Indicate the time spent on researching trends**: Enter the amount of time you spend on researching trends in minutes in the "How much time do you spend on researching and implementing trends?" field.
6. **Assess the impact of trends on your role**: Select all the options that apply to you from the "What is the impact of trends on your role?" field.
7. **Enter the time spent on implementing trends**: Enter the amount of time you spend on implementing trends in minutes in the "How much time do you spend on implementing trends?" field.
8. **Identify trend priorities**: Select all the options that apply to you from the "Which trend priorities are you most interested in?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hotel Management**: This field asks you to select your current role within the hospitality industry. Please choose your current position from the options listed.
* **Which region do you belong to?**: This field asks you to select all the regions that apply to you. Please choose all the regions you are affiliated with.
* **What type of organization do you work for?**: This field asks you to select the type of organization you work for. Please choose the option that best describes your work environment.
* **What are your biggest challenges when it comes to trends and challenges?**: This field asks you to describe your biggest challenges in the industry. Please provide a clear and concise answer.
* **How much time do you spend on researching and implementing trends?**: This field asks you to enter the amount of time you spend on researching and implementing trends. Please enter the time in minutes.
* **What is the impact of trends on your role?**: This field asks you to assess the impact of trends on your role. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **How much time do you spend on implementing trends?**: This field asks you to enter the amount of time you spend on implementing trends. Please enter the time in minutes.
* **Which trend priorities are you most interested in?**: This field asks you to select all the trend priorities that interest you. Please choose all the options that apply to you.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Ensure that you provide clear and concise answers.
- If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
