# E Learning Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

The E Learning Poll form is designed to collect opinions and feedback from users about their e-learning experiences. This form helps us understand users' preferences, opinions, and feedback about different e-learning platforms, enabling us to improve the overall e-learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the E-Learning Platform options (Option 1, 2, 3, or 4) in the first field.
2. Provide comments about your experience with the selected platform in the Comments field.
3. Select one or more of the platforms you use (Option 1, 2, 3, or 4) from the Platforms Used field.
4. Select your preferred platform (Option 1, 2, 3, or 4) from the Preferred Platform field.
5. Enter your email address in the Email field.
6. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
7. Provide any other comments about your experience in the Other Comments field.
8. Provide feedback about your experience in the Feedback field.
9. Provide any additional comments in the Comments (2) field.
10. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
11. Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
12. Provide any additional comments in the Comments (3) field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **E-Learning Platform (select_one)**: Select one of the options to indicate which e-learning platform you use (Option 1, 2, 3, or 4).
* **Comments**: Provide comments about your experience with the selected platform.
* **Faculty Member (select_one)**: Select one of the options to indicate which faculty member (Option 1, 2, 3, or 4) was involved with your e-learning experience.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Other Comments (note)**: Provide any other comments about your experience.
* **Faculty Comments (note)**: Provide feedback about the faculty member involved with your e-learning experience.
* **Platforms Used (select_multiple)**: Select one or more of the platforms you use (Option 1, 2, 3, or 4).
* **Preferred Platform (select_one)**: Select your preferred platform (Option 1, 2, 3, or 4).
* **E-Learning Platform Experience (note)**: Provide comments about your experience with e-learning platforms.
* **Positive Comments (note)**: Provide positive comments about your e-learning experience.
* **Negative Comments (note)**: Provide negative comments about your e-learning experience.
* **Faculty Experience (note)**: Provide comments about the faculty member's experience.
* **Feedback (note)**: Provide feedback about your e-learning experience.
* **Comments (2) (note)**: Provide any additional comments.
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Comments (3) (note)**: Provide any additional comments.

## Tips

* Make sure to select only one option from the E-Learning Platform field.
* Enter accurate information in the Email and Phone fields.
* Use the Other Comments field to provide any other comments or feedback.
* Be detailed and specific in your Comments, Faculty Comments, and Feedback fields.
* Review your selections and comments before submitting the form.
