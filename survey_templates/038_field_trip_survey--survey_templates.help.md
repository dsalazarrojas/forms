# Field Trip Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a simple survey designed to collect feedback from students about their field trip experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading this help guide to understand how to complete the form correctly.
2. Begin with the "intro" field, which is a short text box where you can briefly describe your experience.
3. In the "trip_details" field, provide more details about your trip, such as what you did, saw, or learned.
4. In the "student_name" field, enter your name as it appears on your student ID or other official documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro** (`text`, required/optional): This is a short text field where you can type a brief description of your experience on the field trip.
* **Trip Details** (`note`, required/optional): This field is where you can provide more information about your trip, such as what activities you participated in, what you saw or learned, or any notable events that occurred.
* **Student Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or other official documents.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer the questions clearly and concisely, so the survey administrator can easily understand your feedback.
* If you have any issues or concerns about the form, contact the survey administrator for assistance.
