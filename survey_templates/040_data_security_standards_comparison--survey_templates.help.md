# Data Security Standards Comparison - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect and document the evaluation of a system's or application's security standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the standard that best describes the security standards of the system or application being evaluated (Security Standards field)
2. Choose the method used to evaluate the system or application (Evaluation Method field)
3. Enter a score out of 10 to reflect the evaluated security standards (Evaluation Score field)
4. Identify any risk factors that were found during the assessment (Risk Factors field)
5. Indicate whether any risk factors were selected as potential concerns (Selected Risk Factors field)
6. Enter a decision or recommendation based on the assessment (Decision field)
7. Provide any additional comments on the assessment (Additional Comments field)
8. Enter the date and time the assessment was completed (Date Completed and Time Completed fields)
9. Enter the name and title of the evaluator (Evaluator and Evaluator Title fields)
10. Enter the name of the company and department the system or application belongs to (Company and Department fields)
11. Enter the contact email of the evaluator (Email field)
12. Enter the phone number of the evaluator (Phone field)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Security Standards**: This field is used to select the standard that best describes the security standards of the system or application being evaluated. Select one of the following options: **Confidentiality**, **Integrity**, or **Availability**.
* **Evaluation Method**: This field is used to choose the method used to evaluate the system or application. Select one of the following options: **Questionnaire**, **Checklist**, or **Framework**.
* **Selected Evaluation Method**: This field is used to indicate whether the selected method was actually used for the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Score**: Enter a score out of 10 to reflect the evaluated security standards.
* **Risk Factors**: Identify any risk factors that were found during the assessment. Select one or more of the following options: **Confidentiality**, **Integrity**, or **Availability**.
* **Selected Risk Factors**: Indicate whether any risk factors were selected as potential concerns.
* **Decision**: Enter a decision or recommendation based on the assessment.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments on the assessment.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date the assessment was completed in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
* **Time Completed**: Enter the time the assessment was completed.
* **Evaluator**: Enter the name of the evaluator.
* **Evaluator Title**: Enter the title of the evaluator.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company the system or application belongs to.
* **Department**: Enter the name of the department the system or application belongs to.
* **Email**: Enter the contact email of the evaluator.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the evaluator.

Please note that all fields are optional, but the evaluator's contact information (Email, Phone, Company, Department, Evaluator, Evaluator Title) should be provided if applicable.
