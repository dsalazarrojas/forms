# Corporate Internal Communication Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Internal Communication Effectiveness Survey is designed to gather feedback on the internal communication within the company, specifically regarding the frequency of communication, methods, and overall satisfaction with current communication methods. The feedback will help the organization identify areas of improvement and implement changes to enhance internal communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most suitable options or responses for each field.
3. If a field does not apply to you, choose "Other" or "Not Applicable" accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corporate Internal Communication Effectiveness Survey (1)**:
	+ (Optional) A text field for any comments or additional information you would like to provide.
* **How often do you receive information from your direct supervisor? (2)**:
	+ This question asks about the frequency of communication from your direct supervisor. Answer with a number to indicate how often you receive information from your supervisor.
* **How often do you feel informed about critical issues? (3)**:
	+ This question asks about the frequency of information about critical issues. Answer with a number to indicate how often you feel informed about such issues.
* **How often do you feel informed about non-critical issues? (4)**:
	+ This question asks about the frequency of information about non-critical issues. Answer with a number to indicate how often you feel informed about such issues.
* **What method of communication do you prefer? (5)**:
	+ This question asks about your preferred method of communication. Select one of the available options: Email, Phone Call, In-person Meeting, or Other.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with current communication methods? (6)**:
	+ This question asks about your satisfaction with current communication methods. Answer with a number to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **What do you like about current communication methods? (7)**:
	+ This question asks about your preferences about current communication methods. Select multiple options from the available choices: Fast, Clear, Frequent, Helpful, or Other.
* **What do you dislike about current communication methods? (8)**:
	+ This question asks about your dislikes about current communication methods. Select multiple options from the available choices: Slow, Unclear, Infrequent, Not Helpful, Not Clear, or Other.
* **What method of communication do you prefer for receiving information about critical issues? (9)**:
	+ This question asks about your preferred method of communication for critical issues. Select one of the available options: Email, Phone Call, In-person Meeting, or Other.
* **Additional comments (10)**:
	+ This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to provide.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If a field does not apply to you, select "Other" or "Not Applicable" accordingly.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the "Additional comments" field.
