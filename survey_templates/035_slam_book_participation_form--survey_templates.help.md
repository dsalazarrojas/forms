# Slam Book Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Slam Book Participation Form collects information about users' preferences, friends, and experiences related to their class or school activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions that apply to you.
2. For multi-select questions, select all that apply or up to 3 options.
3. Enter dates and times in the correct format.
4. Write any additional information in the "Is there anything else you want to share?" text box.
