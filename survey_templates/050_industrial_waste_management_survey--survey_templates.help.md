# Industrial Waste Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about waste management practices, challenges, and experiences from industrial organizations. It's essential to provide accurate and detailed answers to ensure a thorough assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Organization Name** field with your company or organization's name.
2. Provide the name and contact information of the **Contact Person** responsible for waste management.
3. Specify the **Role** of the contact person in the organization.
4. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.
5. Select the **Waste Type** relevant to your organization (e.g., chemical, physical, or hazardous waste).
6. Describe the **Waste Management Challenges** faced by your organization.
7. Rate your **Waste Management Satisfaction** level (Low, Medium, or High).
8. Choose the **Operations Teams Involved** in waste management (Engineering Team, Facilities Management Team, or EHS Team).
9. Indicate the **Review Frequency** for your organization (e.g., monthly or quarterly).
10. Select the **Data Collection Method** used in your organization (In-Person, Online, or Mobile App).
11. Choose the **Reporting Frequency** for your organization (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
12. Select the **Submission Method** for your reports (Email, Mobile App, or In-Person).
13. Specify the **Standardization Process** used in your organization.
14. Choose the **Submission Status** of your report (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
15. Enter any **Submission Notes** or comments.
16. Select the **Organization Size** of your organization (Small, Medium, or Large).
17. Choose the **Industry** your organization belongs to (Manufacturing or Services).
18. Select the **Location** of your organization (Americas, Europe, or Asia).
19. Enter the **Region** of your organization.
20. Enter the date of **Submission Date**.
21. Provide the name of the person who **Submitted By**.
22. Enter any **Comments**.
23. Enter the name of the person who **Reviewed By**.
24. Enter the date the **Review Completed**.
25. Enter any **Comments** about the submitted report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Organization Name (1)
The **Organization Name** field is where you should enter your company or organization's name.

### Contact Person (2)
The **Contact Person** field is where you should enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for waste management in your organization.

### Role (3)
The **Role** field is where you should specify the role of the contact person in your organization (e.g., Manager, Coordinator, or Team Leader).

### Email (4)
The **Email** field is where you should enter your organization's email address for contact purposes.

### Waste Type (5)
The **Waste Type** field is where you should select the type of waste relevant to your organization (e.g., chemical, physical, or hazardous waste).

### Waste Management Challenges (6)
The **Waste Management Challenges** field is where you should describe the challenges faced by your organization in waste management.

### Waste Management Satisfaction (7)
The **Waste Management Satisfaction** field is where you should rate your organization's satisfaction level with waste management (Low, Medium, or High).

### Operations Teams Involved (8)
The **Operations Teams Involved** field is where you should select the teams involved in waste management in your organization (Engineering Team, Facilities Management Team, or EHS Team).

### Review Frequency (9)
The **Review Frequency** field is where you should indicate how often your organization reviews its waste management practices (e.g., monthly or quarterly).

### Data Collection Method (10)
The **Data Collection Method** field is where you should select the method used for collecting data in your organization (In-Person, Online, or Mobile App).

### Reporting Frequency (11)
The **Reporting Frequency** field is where you should select how often your organization reports its waste management data (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).

### Submission Method (12)
The **Submission Method** field is where you should select the method used for submitting reports (Email, Mobile App, or In-Person).

### Standardization Process (13)
The **Standardization Process** field is where you should specify the process used for standardizing data in your organization.

### Submission Status (14)
The **Submission Status** field is where you should choose the status of your submitted report (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).

### Submission Notes (15)
The **Submission Notes** field is where you can enter any comments or notes about your submitted report.

### Organization Size (16)
The **Organization Size** field is where you should select the size of your organization (Small, Medium, or Large).

### Industry (17)
The **Industry** field is where you should select the industry your organization belongs to (Manufacturing or Services).

### Location (18)
The **Location** field is where you should select the location of your organization (Americas, Europe, or Asia).

### Region (19)
The **Region** field is where you should enter the region of your organization.

### Submission Date (20)
The **Submission Date** field is where you should enter the date of submission.

### Submitted By (21)
The **Submitted By** field is where you should enter the name of the person who submitted the report.

### Comments (22)
The **Comments** field is where you can enter any comments.

### Reviewed By (23)
The **Reviewed By** field is where you should enter the name of the person who reviewed the report.

### Review Completed (24)
The **Review Completed** field is where you should enter the date when the review was completed.

### Submission Comments (25)
The **Submission Comments** field is where you can enter any comments about the submitted report.
