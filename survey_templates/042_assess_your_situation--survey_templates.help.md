# Assess Your Situation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your current situation and challenges. It will help us better understand your specific needs and provide you with guidance and support tailored to your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your best effort to provide as much detail as possible.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
4. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_full_name_opt`, text, Optional): Your name will be used to address any follow-up discussions or correspondence.
* **Which sector or industry do you belong to** (`current_sector_situation`, select_one, Required): Please select the category that best describes your current sector or industry.
* **What is the primary challenge you are currently facing** (`primary_challenge_faced`, text, Required): Describe your primary challenge or problem in as much detail as possible.
* **Rate the impact level of this challenge** (`impact_level_rating`, number, Required): Rate the impact level of this challenge on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is minimal and 10 is critical.
* **Resources currently available to address this** (`resources_available_situation`, text, Optional): Describe the resources you have available to address this challenge, such as budget, staff, or existing software.
* **Previous solutions or actions attempted** (`previous_attempts_resolution`, text, Optional): If you have previously attempted to address this challenge, describe the steps you took.
* **What is your ideal desired outcome** (`desired_outcome_situation`, text, Required): Describe the outcome you hope to achieve by addressing this challenge.
* **Desired timeline for resolution** (`resolution_timeline_situation`, select_one, Required): Select the desired timeline for resolving this challenge.
* **What type of assistance is most needed** (`help_category_needed`, select_multiple, Required): Select the type of assistance you believe you need most.
* **Preferred method for follow-up consultation** (`follow_up_pref_situation`, select_one, Optional): Select your preferred method for any follow-up consultation.
