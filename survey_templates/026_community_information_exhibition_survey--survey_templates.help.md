# Community Information Exhibition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Information Exhibition Survey is designed to gather feedback from attendees on their experience at the exhibition. This information will be used to improve future exhibitions and better serve the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the exhibition or exhibition topic you attended.
2. Provide your full name, if you wish to be contacted for further information.
3. Choose how you found out about the exhibition (e.g., Social Media, Local News).
4. Rate your overall experience at the exhibition on a scale of 1-5.
5. Rate the clarity and readability of the information provided.
6. Choose the most interesting exhibit or display.
7. Choose the least interesting exhibit or display (optional).
8. Rate the professionalism and helpfulness of the staff.
9. Provide suggestions for future exhibitions.
10. Indicate how likely you are to attend a similar event in the future.
11. Enter the date of your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of the Exhibition or Exhibition Topic**: This is the name of the exhibition or exhibition topic you attended. Please enter the exact title or description used for the event.
* **Your Full Name**: If you wish to be contacted for further information, please provide your full name. This field is optional.
* **How did you find out about this exhibition?**: Choose how you found out about the exhibition. This will help us understand our reach and promotion effectiveness. (Social Media, Local News or Newspaper, Printed Flyer or Poster, Word of Mouth, Email Newsletter)
* **Overall Exhibition Rating**: Rate your overall experience at the exhibition on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is Poor and 5 is Excellent.
* **Clarity and Readability of Information Provided**: Rate the clarity and readability of the information provided at the exhibition, e.g., display boards, brochures. (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Most Interesting Exhibit or Display**: Choose the most interesting exhibit or display you encountered during the event.
* **Least Interesting Exhibit or Display**: If you wish, choose the least interesting exhibit or display. (This field is optional.)
* **Professionalism and Helpfulness of Staff**: Rate the professionalism and helpfulness of the staff during the event. (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, N/A)
* **Suggestions for Future Exhibitions**: Share any suggestions you have for future exhibitions, such as topics or formats you would like to see.
* **How Likely to Attend a Similar Event**: Indicate how likely you are to attend a similar event in the future. (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely)
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date you visited the exhibition.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled in for the form to be considered complete.
