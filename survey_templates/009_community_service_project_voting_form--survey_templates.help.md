# Community Service Project Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from community members on which community service projects they would like to support. The goal is to prioritize projects based on community input and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Full Name**: Enter your full name to identify yourself as a community member.
2. **Residency Status**: Choose your residency status from the provided options (Local Resident, Business Owner, Student, or Non-resident Visitor).
3. **Neighborhood/Area**: Enter the name of your neighborhood or ward.
4. **Top Priority Project**: Select which project is your top priority among the options (Park Renovation, Youth After-School Program, Community Garden, Senior Center Expansion, or Local Library Technology Upgrade).
5. **Additional Support**: Select all other projects that you find valuable.
6. **Reasoning for Choice**: Briefly explain why you chose your top priority project.
7. **Project Benefits**: Choose who you think will benefit most from these projects (Children and Youth, Seniors, Families, Local Businesses, or Entire Community).
8. **Environmental Impact**: Rate the importance of environmental sustainability for these projects (Very Important, Somewhat Important, Neutral, or Not Important).
9. **Volunteer Commitment**: Indicate if you are willing to volunteer for the winning project (Yes, No, or Maybe).
10. **Volunteer Email**: If you are willing to volunteer, provide your email address for updates.
11. **Additional Suggestions**: Share any other project suggestions you have.
12. **Updates**: Choose if you would like to receive project updates via email (Yes or No).
