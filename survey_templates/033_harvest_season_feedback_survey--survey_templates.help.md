# Harvest Season Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from farmers on their experience during the harvest season. This feedback will help us to understand their performance, planning, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability, providing honest and constructive feedback.
2. Select the correct options for each question, based on your experience during the harvest season.
3. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Harvest Season** (`season_feedback`): Enter a brief description of your experience during the harvest season.
* **Farm Experience** (`farm_experience`): Select "Yes" if you had a farm experience during the harvest season, "No" otherwise.
* **Team Performance** (`team_performance`): Please enter a number to rate the performance of your team during the harvest season.
* **Next Year Planning** (`next_year_planning`): Enter your plans for the next year, including any goals or objectives you hope to achieve.
* **End of Season Insights** (`end_of_season_insights`): Select all that apply for the insights you gained at the end of the harvest season.
* **Organized Data Collection** (`organized_data_collection`): Enter a time, in 24-hour format (HH:MM), representing when you typically collect your data.

Note: The `time` type for `organized_data_collection` implies that the user should enter a time of day in 24-hour format. If no specific format is provided, the default format will be assumed to be 24-hour format (HH:MM). 

**Tips**
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us to improve our services.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
