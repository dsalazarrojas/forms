# Modpack Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Modpack Development Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from users about the development process of Modpacks. This form is used to gather information on user satisfaction, development challenges, and submission quality. Your input is crucial in helping us improve the Modpack development process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. For the "Satisfaction" section, select the options that best describe your satisfaction with the development process.
3. In the "Development Challenges" field, type in any challenges you faced during development.
4. The "Feature Ideas" field is where you can share any ideas you have for improving the Modpack development process.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Feature Ideas Uploads" field, depending on whether you have any file uploads to attach with your feature ideas.
6. For the "Submission Rating" section, select the rating that best describes your overall satisfaction with the submission.
7. In the "Submission Comments" field, type any additional comments or feedback you have about the submission.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Submission Uploads" field, depending on whether you have any file uploads to attach with your submission comments.
9. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback you have in the "Submission Comments" field.
