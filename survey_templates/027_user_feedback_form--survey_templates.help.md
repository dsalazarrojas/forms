# User Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The User Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with our service. This form will help us understand how we can improve our services and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "Feedback" question, where you can provide any comments or issues you've encountered.
2. Provide a rating for your overall experience.
3. Fill out your contact information, including your email, phone number, and any additional information you'd like to share.
4. Review and agree to our terms and conditions.
5. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, `select_one`): Please answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are providing feedback or not.
* **Rate Us** (`rating`, `number`): Rate your experience from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Your Email** (`email`, `email`): Provide your email address so we can contact you with updates or follow-up questions.
* **Your Phone** (`phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`): Provide any additional comments or explanations about your feedback.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, `select_multiple`): Suggest any improvements or changes you think we can make.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, `select_one`): Please answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are providing your contact information or not.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`): Provide your email address so we can contact you with updates or follow-up questions.
* **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`, `text`): Enter your company or organization so we can understand your context.
* **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, `text`): Enter your job title or role so we can better understand your perspective.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, `text`): Enter your department or team so we can understand your context.
* **Submitter Additional Info** (`submitter_additional_info`, `note`): Provide any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Agree to Terms** (`agree_to_terms`, `select_one`): Please answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate that you agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Agree to Privacy** (`agree_to_privacy`, `select_one`): Please answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate that you agree to our privacy policy.
* **Submitter Agree to Terms** (`submitter_agree_to_terms`, `select_one`): Please answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate that you agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Submitter Agree to Privacy** (`submitter_agree_to_privacy`, `select_one`): Please answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate that you agree to our privacy policy.
