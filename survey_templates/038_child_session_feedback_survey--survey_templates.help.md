# Child Session Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Session Feedback Survey is designed to gather information from children about their experiences during a session. This form helps identify areas of satisfaction, engagement, and any challenges or concerns they may have had during the session. It also allows providers to rate their overall satisfaction and engagement level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Professional or Provider Name: Enter the name of the person who conducted the session.
	* Date of Session: Enter the date when the session occurred.
	* Child Full Name: Enter the child's full name.
	* Overall Satisfaction: Choose how satisfied the child was with the session (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied).
	* Engagement Level: Choose how engaged the child was during the session (High, Moderate, or Low).
	* Would You Like a Follow-up Call: Choose if you want to discuss the session in detail with the provider (Yes or No).
	* Today Date: Enter the current date.
2. Provide additional feedback:
	* What did the child enjoy most during the session? (Optional)
	* Were there any challenges or concerns during the session? (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Professional or Provider Name**: The name of the person who conducted the session.
* **Date of Session**: The date when the session occurred.
* **Child Full Name**: The child's full name.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied the child was with the session.
* **Engagement Level**: Choose how engaged the child was during the session.
* **Would You Like a Follow-up Call**: Choose if you want to discuss the session in detail with the provider.
* **Today Date**: The current date.
* **What did the child enjoy most during the session?**: Provide details about what the child liked most about the session.
* **Any challenges or concerns during the session?**: Provide details about any difficulties or worries the child had during the session.
* **Any other comments**: This field is not present in the original form, but if you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to add them.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about a question, try to answer based on your best understanding.
* This form is designed to help us improve our services, so your feedback is greatly appreciated!
