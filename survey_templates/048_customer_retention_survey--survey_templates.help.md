# customer_retention_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer retention survey aims to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the company. This feedback will help the company understand the customer's satisfaction with the services and products provided.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the customer retention survey, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2.  Select the options that best fit your experience with the company.
3.  Complete all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select_one, required): Please select whether you are a customer of the company (Yes or No).
* **Customer Segment** (`customer_segment`, select_one, required): Please select the segment of the customer you belong to (label1, label2, or label3).
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, number, required): Please rate the quality of the service provided by the company (e.g., 1-5).
* **Staff Interaction** (`staff_interaction`, number, required): Please rate the interaction with the company staff (e.g., 1-5).
* **Loyalty Program** (`loyalty_program`, select_multiple, required): Please select whether you are part of the loyalty program (Yes or No).
* **Communication Channel** (`communication_channel`, select_one, required): Please select whether you use the communication channel provided by the company (Yes or No).
* **Follow-up Action** (`follow_up_action`, select_one, required): Please select whether you would like the company to follow up on your experience (Yes or No).
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required): Please rate your overall experience with the company (e.g., 1-5).
* **Improvement Suggestion** (`improvement_suggestion`, text, optional): Please provide any suggestions or ideas for improvement.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, number, required): Please rate the customer service provided by the company (e.g., 1-5).
* **Product Quality** (`product_quality`, select_multiple, required): Please rate the quality of the products provided by the company (Good, Poor).
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, required): Please rate your satisfaction with the company (e.g., 1-5).
* **Retention Rate** (`retention_rate`, number, required): Please rate the retention rate of the company (e.g., 1-5).
* **Survey End** (`survey_end`, note, optional): Please use this field for any additional comments or feedback.

Note: The required fields are denoted by an asterisk (\*) symbol.
