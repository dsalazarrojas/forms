# Member Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from members of a community or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form by providing your first name and last name in the respective text fields.
2. In the "Feedback Comments" field, provide a brief description of your thoughts or concerns.
3. If you have any specific suggestions for improvement, type them in the "Feedback Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text): This is the field where you will provide your first name.
* **Last name** (text): This is the field where you will provide your last name.
* **Feedback Comments** (text): Use this field to provide a brief description of your thoughts or concerns.
* **Feedback Suggestions** (text): If you have any specific suggestions for improvement, type them in this field.

## Tips
* Please keep your feedback and suggestions concise and clear.
* If you have any specific concerns or issues, try to provide as much detail as possible.
* You can use the space provided in each field to provide as much information as you need.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
