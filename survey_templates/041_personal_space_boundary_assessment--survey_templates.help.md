# Personal Space Boundary Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Space Boundary Assessment is a form designed to help users assess their personal space boundaries. It is used to determine their comfort levels in certain situations and identify their preferences when it comes to boundaries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Personal Space Boundary Assessment Form.
2. Select your personal space comfort levels from the provided options.
3. Choose your boundary preferences from the options provided.
4. Identify who will complete this form among the options provided.
5. You can add any additional comments if needed.
6. Confirm if you have used Jotform before.
7. Enter your Email address.
8. Provide your Phone number.
9. Enter your Name.
10. Click submit or cancel as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Space Boundary Assessment Form** (Page 1, personal_space_boundary_assessment_form, Required): This field is a text-based input where you can describe your personal space boundary.
* **Personal Space Comfort Levels** (Page 2, comfort_levels, Required): Select your comfort levels by choosing one or multiple options provided.
  - High
  - Low
* **Boundary Preferences** (Page 3, boundary_preferences, Required): Select your preferred boundary preferences.
  - None
  - Other (you can specify what other preference you have).
* **Training, Intake, Workplace or Classroom Norms** (Page 4, training_intake_workplace_classroom_norms, Required): Describe your norms for training, intake, workplace, or classroom.
* **Who is the person completing this form?** (Page 5, counselor_or_educator, Required): Identify who is completing this form among the provided options.
  - Counselor
  - Educator
  - HR
  - Other
* **Additional Comments** (Page 6, additional_comments, Optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you may have.
* **Jotform** (Page 7, jotform_jotform, Required): Confirm if you have used Jotform before.
* **Email** (Page 8, email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Page 9, phone, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (Page 10, name, Required): Enter your name.
* **Submit** (Page 11, submit, Required): Confirm if you want to submit the form.
* **Cancel** (Page 12, cancel, Required): Confirm if you want to cancel the form.

Please note that the fields marked as "Required" must be filled out to submit the form. The fields marked as "Optional" do not require a completion to submit the form.
