# Friendsgiving Event Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a post-event survey designed to gather feedback from guests who attended the Friendsgiving event. Your responses will help us understand your experiences and opinions about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each question and enter any additional comments you have about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Feedback** (`form_1`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks for your overall feedback about the event. Choose one of the following options: Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neutral, Somewhat Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
* **How would you rate the food?** (`form_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply to describe your experience with the food: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Comments** (`form_3`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the event. This is a free-text field where you can write as much or as little as you'd like.
* **What did you enjoy most about the event?** (`form_4`, `text`, required: false): Describe what you enjoyed most about the event.
* **What did you enjoy least about the event?** (`form_5`, `text`, required: false): Describe what you enjoyed least about the event.
* **Would you attend the same event again?** (`form_6`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the following options: True, False, or Undecided.
* **How likely are you to recommend this event?** (`form_7`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the following options: Very Likely, Somewhat Likely, Not Likely.
* **Will you attend next year's Friendsgiving Event?** (`form_8`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the following options: True, False, or Undecided.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all the questions to help us understand your thoughts and opinions about the event.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to add them in the Comments field.
- Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve future events!
