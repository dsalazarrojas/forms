# Restaurant Opening Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Thinking
This form is designed to collect information about restaurants, including their name, address, operating hours, and contact details. It also includes fields for evaluating the restaurant's overall quality, such as cleanliness, ambiance, food quality, and service quality. Additionally, it allows for a manager's contact information and a comments section for any additional feedback. This form is intended for use by restaurant evaluators to assess and improve the quality of local eateries.

## Purpose
The Restaurant Opening Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather information about restaurants, their locations, and the quality of service and food they provide. It is designed to be completed by restaurant evaluators who visit a restaurant and gather data on its opening status, contact details, and overall evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the restaurant's name and address on page 1.
2. Choose the correct location type (e.g., Restaurant, Store, Food Truck, or Other) and provide a brief description of the location.
3. Specify the restaurant's operating hours.
4. Enter the restaurant's phone number and email address.
5. Provide the manager's name, phone number, and email address.
6. Select the days the restaurant is open (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday).
7. Rate the restaurant overall and rate its quality on various aspects (e.g., cleanliness, ambiance, food quality, service quality, and value for money).
8. Provide any additional comments.
9. Evaluate the restaurant overall.
10. Provide any evaluation comments.
11. Rate the restaurant's overall quality (on a scale of 1-5).
12. Enter the date and time of your visit.
13. Indicate if the staff was friendly or not.
14. Indicate the restaurant's cleanliness level.
15. Indicate the restaurant's ambiance level.
16. Indicate the restaurant's food quality level.
17. Indicate the restaurant's service quality level.
18. Indicate the restaurant's value for money level.
19. Indicate if you would recommend the restaurant.
20. Review and submit the form.
