# Homemade Soap Survey Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homemade Soap Survey Form Template is a tool to collect feedback and ratings from customers about your homemade soap products. Fill out this form to help us understand your experience and provide input for future product development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the order ID number in the "Order ID" field, if you have it.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
5. In the "Feedback" field, describe your thoughts about our homemade soap product. What did you like or dislike? What did you think about the texture, smell, or color?
6. In the "Rating" field, select a number from 1 to 5 to rate your overall satisfaction with our soap product.
7. In the "Characteristics of our soap" field, select the features that you felt were most notable about our homemade soap product. For example, if you thought the soap was moisturizing, bubbly, or had a nice color, select those options.
8. Finally, select "Yes" if you would like to see more soaps like this in the future, "No" if you're happy with our current product, or "Maybe" if you're unsure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name here.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, number, optional): If you have an order ID number, enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Describe your thoughts about our homemade soap product. What did you like or dislike? What did you think about the texture, smell, or color?
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Select a number from 1 to 5 to rate your overall satisfaction with our soap product.
* **Characteristics of our soap** (`soap_characteristics`, select_multiple, optional): Select the features that you felt were most notable about our homemade soap product, such as bubbles, fragrance, or moisturizing.
* **Would you like to see more soaps like this?** (`would_you_like`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you'd like to see more soaps like this, "No" if you're happy with our current product, or "Maybe" if you're unsure.

## Tips

* Be honest and constructive with your feedback. Your input helps us improve our products and services.
* If you have any specific issues or concerns, please provide them in the "Feedback" field.
* If you have any questions or comments, don't hesitate to reach out to us via email or phone.
