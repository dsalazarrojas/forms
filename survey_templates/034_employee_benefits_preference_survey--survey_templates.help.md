# Employee Benefits Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employee feedback on their benefits preferences. It will help us understand your needs and preferences for various employee benefits, such as health insurance, retirement plans, and paid time off. Your input will aid in creating a more inclusive and supportive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the survey.
2. Review each page carefully and fill out the required fields.
3. Use the provided options and input fields to answer the questions honestly and accurately.
4. Click "Submit" when finished.

* **Option 2 (Page 2: Time-off Policy)** (`employee_benefits_preference_survey_page_2_label`): This page is about your preferences for time-off policies, such as vacation days and sick leave. Please select the option that best suits your needs.
* **Option 3 (Page 3: Health Insurance)** (`employee_benefits_preference_survey_page_3_label`): Here, you will choose between different health insurance options.
* **Option 4 (Page 4: Retirement Plan)** (`employee_benefits_preference_survey_page_4_label`): This page is about your preferences for retirement plans. Choose the option that suits you best.
