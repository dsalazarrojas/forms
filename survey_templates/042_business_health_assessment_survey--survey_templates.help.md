# Business Health Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Health Assessment Survey is a tool to help business owners or managers evaluate their company's performance in various areas, including financial, operational, and market aspects. This survey aims to identify strengths and weaknesses, allowing for targeted improvements and growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information about your business.
2. For select-one fields, choose the option that best describes your current situation.
3. For text fields, provide a brief description or answer.
4. Make sure to save your progress and review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or stationery.
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person completing the survey.
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, text, required): Describe your role or position within the company.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, optional): If your business is new, enter 0 or the number of years it has been operating.
* **Revenue Trend** (`revenue_trend`, select_one, required): Choose how your revenue has been trending:
	+ Declining: Revenue is decreasing over time.
	+ Stagnant: Revenue has remained constant.
	+ Slight Growth: Revenue has increased slightly.
	+ Moderate Growth: Revenue has increased steadily.
	+ Strong Growth: Revenue has increased significantly.
* **Profitability Status** (`profitability`, select_one, required): Choose your current profitability status:
	+ Significant Loss: You are experiencing significant financial losses.
	+ Loss: You are losing money, but not significantly.
	+ Break-even: You are covering your costs, but not making a profit.
	+ Modest Profit: You are making a modest profit.
	+ Strong Profit: You are making a significant profit.
* **Cash Flow Health** (`cash_flow`, select_one, required): Choose how your cash situation is:
	+ Critical Issues: You are experiencing severe cash flow problems.
	+ Strained: You have some cash flow issues.
	+ Adequate: You have enough cash for current needs.
	+ Healthy: You have a comfortable cash reserve.
	+ Excellent: You have a strong cash reserve.
* **Debt Level Management** (`debt_level`, select_one, required): Choose how you manage your debt:
	+ Unsustainable: Your debt is becoming unmanageable.
	+ High Risk: You are concerned about your debt level.
	+ Moderate: Your debt is under control, but you have some concerns.
	+ Well Managed: You are effectively managing your debt.
	+ Strong Position: You have a robust debt management strategy.
* **Process Efficiency** (`process_efficiency`, select_one, required): Choose how efficient your processes are:
	+ Very Inefficient: Your processes are time-consuming and ineffective.
	+ Inefficient: Your processes are somewhat time-consuming.
	+ Adequate: Your processes are adequate, but not optimal.
	+ Efficient: Your processes are efficient, but could be improved.
	+ Highly Efficient: Your processes are highly efficient and effective.
* **Cost Control and Management** (`cost_control`, select_one, required): Choose how well you control costs:
	+ Poor Control: You are not effectively controlling costs.
	+ Weak: You are somewhat effective, but not consistently.
	+ Adequate: You have some control, but could be better.
	+ Good: You are effectively managing costs.
	+ Excellent: You have a robust cost management strategy.
* **Supply Chain and Logistics** (`supply_chain`, select_one, required): Choose how stable your supply chain is:
	+ Highly Unstable: Your supply chain is experiencing severe issues.
	+ Unstable: Your supply chain has some issues.
	+ Stable: Your supply chain is stable, but could be improved.
	+ Well Managed: You are effectively managing your supply chain.
	+ Optimized: You have a highly effective supply chain management strategy.
* **Quality Control Standards** (`quality_control`, select_one, required): Choose how well you manage product or service quality:
	+ Significant Issues: You are experiencing quality control problems.
	+ Inconsistent: Your quality control is somewhat inconsistent.
	+ Adequate: Your quality control is adequate, but not optimal.
	+ Good Standards: You have effective quality control measures.
	+ Excellent Standards: You have a robust quality control strategy.
* **Team Morale and Engagement** (`team_morale`, select_one, required): Choose how engaged your team is:
	+ Very Low: Your team is not engaged or motivated.
	+ Low: Your team is somewhat disengaged.
	+ Neutral: Your team has mixed levels of engagement.
	+ Good: Your team is engaged, but could be more so.
	+ Excellent: Your team is highly engaged and motivated.
* **Talent Retention** (`talent_retention`, select_one, required): Choose how well you retain top talent:
	+ High Turnover: You are experiencing high employee turnover.
	+ Moderate Turnover: You have moderate employee turnover.
	+ Acceptable: You are somewhat effective in retaining talent.
	+ Good Retention: You are effectively retaining top talent.
	+ Strong Loyalty: You have a strong talent retention strategy.
* **Skill Development and Training** (`skill_development`, select_one, required): Choose how well you invest in training:
	+ None: You do not invest in training.
	+ Minimal: You have limited training programs.
	+ Some programs: You have some training programs, but not many.
	+ Regular programs: You have regular training programs.
	+ Comprehensive: You have a robust training strategy.
* **Market Position** (`market_position`, select_one, required): Choose your market position:
	+ Losing Share: You are losing market share.
	+ Weak Position: You have a weak market position.
	+ Stable Position: You have a stable market position.
	+ Strong Position: You have a strong market position.
	+ Market Leader: You are the market leader.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied customers are:
	+ Very Dissatisfied: Customers are very dissatisfied.
	+ Dissatisfied: Customers are somewhat dissatisfied.
	+ Neutral: Customers are neutral.
	+ Satisfied: Customers are satisfied.
	+ Very Satisfied: Customers are very satisfied.
* **Innovation Capacity** (`innovation_capacity`, select_one, required): Choose how well you can innovate:
	+ Minimal: You are not innovating.
	+ Limited: You have limited innovation capacity.
	+ Adequate: You have some innovation capacity.
	+ Strong: You are effectively innovating.
	+ Exceptional: You have a highly innovative strategy.
* **Top Concerns** (`top_concerns`, text, optional): Briefly describe your top concerns about the business.
* **Priority Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Briefly describe areas where you need improvement.

## Tips

* Answer honestly and accurately to get a thorough assessment of your business's health.
* Review and revise your answers as needed to ensure accuracy.
* Use this survey as a starting point for discussions and improvements within your organization.
* Consider sharing your results with your team or partners to gain a more comprehensive understanding of your business.
