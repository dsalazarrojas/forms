# Game Day Meal Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Day Meal Planning Survey is used to gather information about the number of people being planned for, food preferences, contribution commitments, and contact details for a game day meal planning event. This form helps the event host plan and organize the meal arrangements for the game day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by a *required* label in each field description.
2. Enter the number of people you are planning for in the *How many people are you planning for?* field.
3. Select your preferred food type from the *What type of food do you like?* dropdown menu.
4. If you are able to contribute food to the meal, select the types of food you are able to contribute from the *What type of food are you able to contribute?* dropdown menu.
5. If there are any special dietary needs, select them from the *Are there any special dietary needs?* dropdown menu.
6. Provide more information about any special dietary needs in the *Provide more information* text field.
7. Enter the number of guests bringing a dish in the *How many guests are bringing a dish?* field.
8. Indicate if you are bringing a dish in the *Are you bringing a dish?* dropdown menu.
9. Enter the number of guests attending in the *How many guests are attending?* field.
10. Select a date and time for the meal in the *Date and time for meal* field.
11. Enter the host contact phone number in the *Host contact phone number* field.
12. Enter the host contact email in the *Host contact email* field.
13. Enter the host contact address in the *Host contact address* field.
14. Enter any additional notes in the *Additional notes* field.
15. If this is for a game day, select *true* in the *Is this for a game day?* dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* *How many people are you planning for?* (Number): Enter the total number of people you are planning for.
* *What type of food do you like?* (Select One): Select your preferred food type from the dropdown menu.
* *What type of food are you able to contribute?* (Select Multiple): If you are able to contribute food, select the types of food you are able to contribute from the dropdown menu.
* *Are there any special dietary needs?* (Select Multiple): Select any special dietary needs from the dropdown menu.
* *Provide more information* (Text): Provide more information about any special dietary needs.
* *How many guests are bringing a dish?* (Number): Enter the number of guests bringing a dish.
* *Are you bringing a dish?* (Select One): Indicate if you are bringing a dish.
* *How many guests are attending?* (Number): Enter the total number of guests attending.
* *Date and time for meal* (Date): Select a date and time for the meal.
* *Host contact phone number* (Text): Enter the host contact phone number.
* *Host contact email* (Email): Enter the host contact email.
* *Host contact address* (Text): Enter the host contact address.
* *Additional notes* (Text): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* *Is this for a game day?* (Select One): If this is for a game day, select *true* from the dropdown menu.
* *Game Day Meal Planning Survey Date 2* (Date): Select a date.
* *Game Day Meal Planning Survey Time 2* (Time): Select a time.
* *Game Day Meal Planning Survey Game Day Meal Planning Survey Additional Notes* (Text): Enter additional notes or comments.
* *Game Day Meal Planning Survey Headcount 4* (Number): Enter the total number of guests attending.
* *Game Day Meal Planning Survey Headcount 3* (Number): Enter the number of guests attending.
* *Game Day Meal Planning Survey Phone* (Text): Enter the host contact phone number.
* *Game Day Meal Planning Survey Email* (Email): Enter the host contact email.

* *Game Day Meal Planning Survey Date 2* (Date): Select a date.
* *Game Day Meal Planning Survey Time 2* (Time): Select a time.
