# drive_thru_customer_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The drive_thru_customer_experience_survey form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their recent drive-thru experience. The form aims to gauge the customer's satisfaction with service, product price, customer experience, staff friendliness, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to the first question: "Drive Thru Customer Experience"
2. Choose how you would rate the service at the drive-thru: "Thru Service"
3. Enter the price of the product: "Product Price"
4. Select how you experienced your visit: "Customer Experience"
5. Choose how friendly you found the staff: "Thru Staff"
6. Rate the cleanliness of the facilities: "Thru Facilities"
7. Select your overall satisfaction with the visit: "Overall Satisfaction"

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drive Thru Customer Experience**: Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you had a positive or negative experience with the drive-thru.
* **Thru Service**: Choose how you would rate the service at the drive-thru: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product you purchased. This will help us understand the value of our products.
* **Customer Experience**: Select how you experienced your visit: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Thru Staff**: Choose how friendly you found the staff at the drive-thru: Very Friendly, Friendly, Average, or Not Friendly.
* **Thru Facilities**: Rate the cleanliness of the facilities: Very Clean, Clean, Average, or Not Clean.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Select your overall satisfaction with the visit: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly to help us understand your experience.
* You can select more than one option for some questions.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please feel free to share them with us.
* This form will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers.
