# Award Theme Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects user preferences and ideas for future themes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Answer all the required fields.
2. **Rate each theme**: Indicate your rating for each of the four proposed theme options.
3. **Select your preferred color palette**: Choose all the color options that appeal to you.
4. **Submit your feedback**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
5. **Confirm your interest in the decorating committee**: Indicate your willingness to help with decorating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your official email address.
* **Department or Group**: Select your department or group affiliation.
* **Galactic Gala - Space and Future**: Rate this theme from 1 (Least favorite) to 5 (Most favorite).
* **Retro Rewind - 80s and 90s**: Rate this theme from 1 (Least favorite) to 5 (Most favorite).
* **Elegant Enigma - Masquerade and Mystery**: Rate this theme from 1 (Least favorite) to 5 (Most favorite).
* **Tropical Treasure - Beach and Island**: Rate this theme from 1 (Least favorite) to 5 (Most favorite).
* **Preferred Color Palette**: Select all the color options that appeal to you, such as Gold and Black (Classic), Blue and Silver (Cool), Red and White (Bold), Pastels (Soft), and Neon (Vibrant).
* **Do you have a theme idea to suggest?**: Provide a brief name and description of your theme idea.
* **Most Important Element of a Theme**: Select what makes a theme successful, such as Immersive Decoration, Fun Dress Code, Themed Music/Entertainment, or Specialized Food and Drink.
* **Will you help with the Decorating Committee?**: Indicate your willingness to participate in the decorating committee.
* **Previous Favorite Theme**: If you have a favorite previous theme, enter its name.
* **Any other comments on the theme selection?**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
* **Date of Submission**: This field is automatically populated with the submission date. You don't need to fill it out.
* **Will you help with decorating?**: Confirm your interest in helping with the decorating committee.
* **Any other comments on the theme selection?**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
