# Facility Management Workflow Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from facility management teams on their satisfaction with the current workflow process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. The form is divided into the following sections:

1. **Workflow Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with the current workflow process.
	* Satisfied or Dissatisfied
2. **Challenges Faced**: Describe any challenges you have faced while managing the facility.
	* Enter a brief description of your challenges
3. **Improvement Ideas**: Suggest any ideas you have for improving the workflow process.
	* Enter a brief description of your ideas
4. **Satisfaction Level**: Select your level of satisfaction with the current facility management workflow.
	* Satisfied or Dissatisfied
5. **Workflow Idea Submission**: Describe any workflow ideas you have for improving the facility management process.
	* Enter a brief description of your ideas
6. **Team Member Satisfaction**: Describe your overall satisfaction level with the team members involved in facility management.
	* Enter a brief description of your experiences with team members
7. **Location Satisfaction**: Describe your overall satisfaction level with the location or facility you manage.
	* Enter a brief description of your experiences with the location or facility
8. **Location Satisfaction Comments**: Additional comments or suggestions about the location or facility.
	* Enter any additional comments or suggestions
9. **Facility Satisfaction Comments**: Additional comments or suggestions about the facility management process.
	* Enter any additional comments or suggestions
10. **Team Members Involved**: Select whether you think team members are involved in the facility management workflow.
	* Yes or No
11. **Staff Feedback Frequency**: Select how often you receive feedback from staff on the facility management process.
	* Yes or No
12. **Staff Satisfaction Level**: Select your level of satisfaction with staff feedback on the facility management process.
	* Satisfied or Dissatisfied

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" fields for those.
* This form is designed to be completed by facility management teams, so please ensure that you are answering on behalf of your team.
