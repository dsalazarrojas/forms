# tips_and_advice_sharing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user-provided tips and advice on various topics. This form will help gather feedback and insights from end-users, which will be displayed as tips or advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the `Email` field. Please note that your email address will not be used for any promotional or marketing purposes.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the `Phone Number` field.
4. Select your answer for `Do you have a tip or advice to share?`. You have 3 options to choose from: A, B, or C.
5. Enter your **advice** in the `What is your advice?` field. This is an open field where you can provide your insights and suggestions.
6. Optionally, elaborate on your tip in the `Can you elaborate on your tip?` field.
7. Select your next tip from the `What is your next tip?` dropdown menu. You have 3 options to choose from: A, B, or C.
8. Select any additional tips or advice you have in the `Do you have any additional tips or advice?` field. You can choose one or multiple options from A, B, or C.
9. If you have an upcoming appointment, enter your **Appointment Date** in the `Can you provide a date for your next appointment?` field.
10. Enter your next available time for an appointment in the `What is your next available time for an appointment?` field.
11. Enter any additional notes you'd like to share in the `Can you share any further notes?` field.
12. Select your next tip from the `Do you have a tip for this question?` dropdown menu. You have 3 options to choose from: A, B, or C.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This is the field to enter your name, which will be displayed as your name.
*   **Email**: Enter your contact email address. Please be aware that your email address will not be used for any promotional or marketing purposes.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Do you have a tip or advice to share?**: Select A, B, or C from the dropdown menu to indicate if you have a tip or advice to share.
*   **What is your advice?**: Provide your insights and suggestions in this field. This is an open field where you can type as much as you'd like.
*   **Can you elaborate on your tip?**: Enter your expanded tip or advice to provide more details.
*   **What is your next tip?**: Select A, B, or C from the dropdown menu for your next tip.
*   **Do you have any additional tips or advice?**: Select one or more options from A, B, or C for any additional tips or advice you have.
*   **Can you provide a date for your next appointment?**: Enter your next appointment date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **What is your next available time for an appointment?**: Enter your next available time in 12-hour format (e.g., 09:00).
*   **Can you share any further notes?**: Enter any additional notes you'd like to share.
*   **Do you have a tip for this question?**: Select A, B, or C from the dropdown menu for your next tip.
