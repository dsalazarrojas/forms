# English Proficiency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The English Proficiency Assessment is a self-assessment tool designed to help you gauge your English language skills in various areas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the correct response for each question based on your self-assessment.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **English Proficiency Assessment**: This is the title of the form. Please do not answer this question.
* **What is your native language?**: Choose the option that best describes your native language.
	+ Native language: Your native language is the language you speak and write in your daily life.
	+ Non-native language: You learned English as a second language.
	+ Other: Choose this option if your native language is not English or any other language.
* **How would you rate your listening skills?**: Assess your ability to understand and comprehend spoken English.
	+ Excellent: You can understand spoken English with ease.
	+ Good: You can mostly understand spoken English, but may struggle occasionally.
	+ Average: You can understand spoken English, but may need to concentrate or ask for clarification.
	+ Poor: You struggle to understand spoken English.
	+ Very poor: You have difficulty understanding spoken English.
* **What is your current level of speaking proficiency?**: Assess your ability to speak English.
	+ Proficient: You can speak English fluently and with confidence.
	+ Basic: You can speak English, but may struggle to express yourself clearly.
	+ None: You are not proficient in speaking English.
* **Can you read and understand English texts with ease?**: Assess your ability to read and comprehend English texts.
	+ True: You can read and understand English texts with ease.
	+ False: You struggle to read and understand English texts.
	+ Somewhat: You can mostly read and understand English texts, but may need help occasionally.
* **What is your current level of writing proficiency?**: Assess your ability to write English.
	+ Excellent: You can write English with ease and confidence.
	+ Good: You can write English, but may struggle with grammar or vocabulary.
	+ Average: You can write English, but may need to review or seek help.
	+ Poor: You struggle to write English.
	+ Very poor: You have difficulty writing English.
* **Can you understand and use correct English grammar?**: Assess your ability to understand and use correct grammar in English.
	+ True: You can use correct grammar in English.
	+ False: You struggle with English grammar.
	+ Somewhat: You mostly use correct grammar in English, but may make occasional mistakes.
* **How would you rate your vocabulary?**: Assess your ability to use English vocabulary.
	+ Excellent: You have a large and accurate vocabulary.
	+ Good: You have a sufficient vocabulary, but may struggle with nuances or idioms.
	+ Average: You have a basic vocabulary, but may need to look up words frequently.
	+ Poor: You struggle to understand or use English vocabulary.
	+ Very poor: You have difficulty with English vocabulary.
* **How would you rate your pronunciation?**: Assess your ability to speak English with correct pronunciation.
	+ Excellent: You speak English with accurate pronunciation.
	+ Good: You mostly speak English with correct pronunciation, but may make occasional mistakes.
	+ Average: You mostly speak English with correct pronunciation, but may need to practice more.
	+ Poor: You struggle to speak English with correct pronunciation.
	+ Very poor: You have difficulty speaking English with correct pronunciation.
* **How would you rate your overall English language proficiency?**: Assess your overall ability to understand and use English.
	+ Excellent: You can use English with ease and confidence.
	+ Good: You can mostly use English, but may struggle with nuances or grammar.
	+ Average: You can use English, but may need to practice or review frequently.
	+ Poor: You struggle to use English.
	+ Very poor: You have difficulty using English.
* **Are there any areas where you struggle with the English language?**: Choose all areas where you struggle with English language.
	+ Speaking
	+ Listening
	+ Reading
	+ Writing
	+ Grammar
	+ Pronunciation
	+ Vocabulary
* **Is there anything else you would like to add about your English language proficiency?**: Share any additional comments or insights about your language skills.

Note: This help guide assumes that end users are self-assessing their own language skills. The results of this assessment are intended to be personal and informative, not used as a standardized measure of English proficiency.
