# App Name Selection Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **App Name Selection Voting Form** is designed to collect opinions and ratings from users on proposed names for an application or project. The form aims to gather data to make informed decisions on which name to select.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Input your demographic information:
    *   **Voting date**: Select the date you are voting.
    *   **Your name**: Enter your name.
    *   **Email address**: Enter your email address.
    *   **Your role in the organization**: Select your role from the list provided.
    *   **Department or team**: Select the department or team you belong to.
2.  Rate the proposed names:
    *   **Option 1 - App name candidate**: Rate the proposed name as **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Poor**, or **Very poor**.
    *   **Option 2 - App name candidate**: Rate the proposed name as **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Poor**, or **Very poor**.
    *   **Option 3 - App name candidate**: Rate the proposed name as **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Poor**, or **Very poor**.
    *   **Option 4 - App name candidate**: Rate the proposed name as **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Poor**, or **Very poor**. (Optional)
3.  Select your preferred name:
    *   **Most preferred name choice**: Select the name you prefer from the options provided.
4.  Provide feedback:
    *   **Selected name aligns with brand identity**: Rate how well the selected name aligns with the brand identity as **Strongly align**, **Align**, **Neutral**, **Misaligned**, or **Strongly misaligned**.
    *   **How memorable is the preferred name?**: Rate the memorability of the selected name as **Very memorable**, **Memorable**, **Moderately memorable**, **Hard to remember**, or **Very hard to remember**.
    *   **Is the name easy to pronounce and spell?**: Rate the ease of pronunciation and spelling of the selected name as **Very easy**, **Easy**, **Moderate**, **Difficult**, or **Very difficult**.
    *   **Does the name differentiate well in the market?**: Rate how well the selected name differentiates in the market as **Highly differentiated**, **Differentiated**, **Neutral**, **Similar to competitors**, or **Confusingly similar**.
5.  Optional feedback:
    *   **Any concerns or feedback**: Provide any concerns or feedback on the proposed names.
    *   **Alternative name suggestion**: Suggest an alternative name if you have one.
