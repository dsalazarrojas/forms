# Manager Of The Year Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Of The Year Survey is an annual evaluation form that helps us understand your performance, leadership skills, and qualifications for the Manager Of The Year award. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to provide us with valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. Use your real name as your name.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
5. Be honest and detailed in your answers, especially in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Name** (`survey_name`, text, required):
	+ This is the name of the survey you are taking, which is the Manager Of The Year Survey.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required):
	+ Please enter your real name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required):
	+ Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your work phone number (not required but recommended for contact purposes).
* **Department** (`department`, text, required):
	+ Enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required):
	+ Enter your job title.
* **Leadership Skills** (`leadership_skills`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all leadership skills that you have demonstrated:
		+ Leadership and Teamwork
		+ Communication
		+ Strategic Thinking
		+ Problem Solving
		+ Decision Making
		+ Time Management
		+ Adaptability
		+ Conflict Resolution
		+ Collaboration
* **Managerial Performance** (`managerial_performance`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate your own managerial performance:
		+ Excellent
		+ Good
		+ Fair
		+ Poor
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Share any additional comments or thoughts about your performance or leadership skills.
* **Date Of Last Promotion** (`date_of_last_promotion`, date, optional):
	+ If you've been promoted recently, enter the date of your last promotion.
* **Time Of Last Promotion** (`time_of_last_promotion`, time, optional):
	+ If you've been promoted recently, enter the time of your last promotion.
* **Manager Performance Rating** (`manager_performance_rating`, number, required):
	+ Rate your own performance on a scale from 1-10.
* **Manager Employees Count** (`manager_employees_count`, number, required):
	+ Enter the number of employees you manage.
* **Manager Years Of Service** (`manager_years_of_service`, number, required):
	+ Enter the number of years you've been working at this company.
* **Manager Job Description** (`manager_job_description`, text, optional):
	+ Briefly describe your job responsibilities.
* **Manager Last Performance Review** (`manager_last_performance_review`, date, optional):
	+ If you've had a performance review recently, enter the date.
* **Manager Next Evaluation Date** (`manager_next_evaluation_date`, date, optional):
	+ If you have a scheduled performance review, enter the date.
* **Manager Current Salary** (`manager_current_salary`, number, optional):
	+ Enter your current salary.
* **Manager Benefits** (`manager_benefits`, text, optional):
	+ Briefly describe your benefits.
* **Manager 403b Plan** (`manager_403b_plan`, select_one, required):
	+ Check if you are enrolled in the 403b plan:
		+ True
		+ False
* **Manager Hsa Plan** (`manager_hsa_plan`, select_one, required):
	+ Check if you are enrolled in the HSA plan:
		+ True
		+ False
* **Manager Flex Pension** (`manager_flex_pension`, select_one, required):
	+ Check if you are enrolled in the flex pension plan:
		+ True
		+ False
* **Manager Retirement Plan** (`manager_retirement_plan`, select_one, optional):
	+ Check if you have a retirement plan:
		+ True
		+ False
* **Manager Health Insurance** (`manager_health_insurance`, select_one, optional):
	+ Check if you have health insurance:
		+ True
		+ False
* **Manager Life Insurance** (`manager_life_insurance`, select_one, optional):
	+ Check if you have life insurance:
		+ True
		+ False
