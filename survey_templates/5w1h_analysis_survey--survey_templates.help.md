# 9 12 Academic Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information for creating an academic plan for students in grades 9-12. It is intended for use in educational settings, such as schools or school districts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the student's details, including their first and last name, date of birth, grade level, academic year, and total planned credits.
2. List the curriculum subjects the student will be taking, using the format "Subject:Course:Credits" (e.g., "Math:Algebra II:1.0").
3. Select all the extracurricular activities the student will participate in.
4. If necessary, provide details about any accommodations or services the student requires, such as IEP or 504 plans or tutoring needs.
5. Add any additional notes or explanations of the plan from the parent or guardian.
6. Sign and date the form with the parent's or guardian's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student name** (student_name): Enter the student's first and last name.
- **Date of birth** (date_of_birth): Enter the student's date of birth.
- **Grade level for plan** (grade_level): Select the grade level for which the academic plan is being created (9-12).
- **Academic year** (academic_year): Enter the academic year for which the plan is being created (e.g., 2026-2027).
- **Curriculum by subject** (curriculum_subjects): List the subjects and courses the student will be taking, using the format "Subject:Course:Credits" (e.g., "Math:Algebra II:1.0").
- **Total planned credits** (total_planned_credits): Enter the total number of planned credits for the student.
- **Extracurricular activities** (extracurricular_activities): Select all the extracurricular activities the student will participate in (e.g., Sports, Music/Band, Drama/Theater).
- **Accommodations or services** (accommodations_or_services): If the student requires any accommodations or services, such as IEP or 504 plans or tutoring, enter the details here.
- **Additional notes or parent explanation** (additional_notes): Add any additional notes or explanations from the parent or guardian about the plan.
- **Parent/guardian signature** (parent_signature): Sign and date the form with the parent's or guardian's signature.
