# Cybersecurity Awareness Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is created to collect information on cybersecurity awareness among employees, available training programs, incident response plans, and security policies in an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, select the answers that best describe your current state of cybersecurity awareness among your employees and your organization's policies and practices.
- Fill out the form carefully, as your responses will be used to assess your organization's preparedness and potential areas of improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the level of cybersecurity awareness among your employees?**
  `employee_knowledge` (`select_one`, required/optional): This question aims to gauge the extent of cybersecurity knowledge among your organization's employees. Please select the option that best matches your current state.
  - Basic knowledge: Employees have a limited understanding of cybersecurity concepts and practices.
  - Some knowledge: Employees have a general idea about cybersecurity, but may not fully understand its importance or implications.
  - Good knowledge: Employees possess a solid understanding of cybersecurity principles and concepts.
- **Are any employee training programs available to improve cybersecurity awareness?**
  `training_programs` (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all that apply to describe the types of training programs available to your employees:
  - Basic awareness training: Programs focused on teaching the basics of cybersecurity.
  - Advanced awareness training: Programs that provide in-depth knowledge and advanced understanding of cybersecurity.
  - No training: No programs are available.
  - Other: If other, please specify.
- **Does your organization have an Incident Response Plan?**
  `incident_response_plan` (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply to describe your organization's incident response plan:
  - True: A plan is in place.
  - False: No plan is available.
- **Are any security policies and procedures in place to prevent cybersecurity threats?**
  `security_policies` (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all that apply to describe your organization's security policies:
  - Password policies: Policies related to password management.
  - Data backup policies: Policies for data backup and recovery.
  - Network security policies: Policies for network access and security.
  - Other: If other, please specify.
- **Are security policies and procedures actually enforced in your organization?**
  `security_policies_enforced` (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply to describe policy enforcement in your organization:
  - Strongly enforced: Policies are strictly followed.
  - Somewhat enforced: Policies are somewhat followed, but with variations.
  - Not enforced: Policies are not followed.
- **How do you think you can improve policy enforcement?**
  `security_policies_enforced_further` (`text`, required/optional): Share your thoughts on how to improve policy enforcement in your organization.
- **Are there any plans for additional security training to be conducted within the next 6 months?**
  `security_training` (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all that apply to describe your organization's plans for additional security training:
  - Yes, within 6 months: Training is planned for the near future.
  - No, but planned for the future: Training is not immediate, but planned for the future.
  - No, not planned: No plans are in place.
