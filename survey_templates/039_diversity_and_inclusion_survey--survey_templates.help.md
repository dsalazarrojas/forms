# Diversity and Inclusion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Diversity and Inclusion Survey" is designed to gather information on employees' perceptions, experiences, and actions regarding diversity and inclusion within the organization. Your input will help the diversity and inclusion team better understand the current state of diversity and inclusion and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the most appropriate response for each question.
3. Provide additional comments if you want to elaborate on any of the questions.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is this survey about** (1): This question serves as an introduction to the survey, providing context and background information. You can briefly describe the purpose and goals of the survey.
* **What is your job title** (2): This question helps to understand your role and position within the organization. Please select the option that best represents your job title.
* **What is your department** (3): This question aims to capture your departmental affiliation. Select all departments that apply to your role.
* **What is your job level** (4): This question is about your current job level or position within the organization. Select the level that best describes your role.
* **What is your years of service** (5): Please provide your number of years of service within the organization. This information will help in understanding the diversity of the workforce.
* **How do you feel about being in a team where you are different** (6): This question is about your comfort level with being part of a team that may have different backgrounds, cultures, or perspectives. Please select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **How often do you share your opinions and ideas** (7): This question is about your willingness to share your thoughts and ideas within the team. Select all options that apply to your behavior.
* **How often do you feel you are able to be yourself** (8): This question is about feeling comfortable expressing your thoughts, feelings, and opinions within the team. Select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **What actions have you taken recently that promote diversity and inclusion** (9): This question is about specific actions you've taken to promote diversity and inclusion. Select all options that apply to your behavior.
* **What is your suggestion to promote diversity and inclusion** (10): This is an opportunity to provide a specific idea or suggestion on how to improve diversity and inclusion.
* **Have you experienced any negative or positive outcomes from diversity and inclusion actions** (11): This question is about any notable outcomes or events that you've experienced as a result of diversity and inclusion efforts.
* **Additional comments** (12-17): These questions provide an open-ended space for you to elaborate on any of the previous questions or share any additional thoughts or concerns you may have. Please use these fields to provide any additional context or information that may be helpful in improving diversity and inclusion within the organization.
