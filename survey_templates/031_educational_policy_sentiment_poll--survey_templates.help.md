# Educational Policy Sentiment Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the Educational Policy Sentiment Poll form, which is designed to collect users' opinions on various educational policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select the areas you want to provide opinions on from the multiple-choice questions.
*   Choose your response for each question, such as "Strongly Agree", "Somewhat Agree", "Not Sure", "Somewhat Disagree", or "Strongly Disagree".
*   Provide additional comments or feedback in the text fields.
*   If needed, upload a file or provide your name, email, phone number, or other required information.
