# Rollback Selection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on rollbacks, including the decision-making process, impact, and frequency. The data collected will help project managers and IT teams to better understand and manage rollbacks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the option that best describes the rollback decision made.
2. Provide a brief explanation of the reason for the rollback.
3. Select the business impact level of the rollback.
4. Indicate the frequency of the business impact.
5. Select the project manager involved in the rollback.
6. Select the IT team involved in the rollback.
7. Provide a brief explanation of the comments from the project manager.
8. Provide a brief explanation of the comments from the IT team.
9. Choose the business impact level of the rollback.
10. Choose the frequency of the business impact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the rollback decision?** (`rollback_decision`, select_one): This field asks you to select the option that best describes the decision made during the rollback. Choose the option that best fits your situation.
* **Reason for rollbacks** (`reason_for_rollbacks`, text): Enter a brief explanation of the reason why the rollback was performed. Be as specific as possible.
* **Business impact** (`business_impact`, text): Provide a brief explanation of the business impact of the rollback. This will help you to assess the severity of the impact.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, select_one): This field is for selecting the project manager involved in the rollback. Choose the option that best fits your situation.
* **IT Team** (`it_team`, select_one): This field is for selecting the IT team involved in the rollback. Choose the option that best fits your situation.
* **Project Manager Comments** (`project_manager_comments`, text): Enter a brief explanation of the comments from the project manager. Be as specific as possible.
* **IT Team Comments** (`it_team_comments`, text): Enter a brief explanation of the comments from the IT team. Be as specific as possible.
* **Business Impact Level** (`business_impact_level`, select_one): This field asks you to select the business impact level of the rollback. Choose the option that best fits your situation.
* **Business Impact Frequency** (`business_impact_frequency`, select_one): This field asks you to select the frequency of the business impact of the rollback. Choose the option that best fits your situation.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when completing this form.
* Avoid using placeholder text or incomplete answers.
* If you are unsure about any field, please feel free to ask for clarification.
* Ensure that you only complete this form for actual rollbacks, and not as a placeholder or for hypothetical scenarios.
* If you have any issues or questions while completing this form, please contact us for assistance.
