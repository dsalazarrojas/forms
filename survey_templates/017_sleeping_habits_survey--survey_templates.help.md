# sleeping_habits_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sleeping_habits_survey form is designed to collect information about your sleeping habits, including your sleep patterns, quality of sleep, and disturbances that may affect your sleep. Completing this form will help us understand your habits and patterns related to sleep.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Click on each page's "Continue" button to navigate through the form.
2. For each question, select the relevant option from the list, or enter the information in the text fields.
3. Make sure to complete all the questions as accurately as possible.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, click on the "Submit" button to submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* 1. What time do you typically go to sleep? (Time) 
  This question asks for the time you usually go to sleep at night.
* 2. What time do you wake up? (Time) 
  This question asks for the time you wake up in the morning.
* 3. Do you have any sleep patterns? (Select Multiple) 
  This question asks about your sleep patterns, such as whether you sleep with or without any specific habits.
* 4. How do you wake up? (Select Multiple) 
  This question asks about the circumstances that cause you to wake up during the day or night.
* 5. How much time do you spend exercising per day? (Number) 
  This question asks for the amount of time you spend engaging in physical activities that help improve your sleep quality.
* 6. How much screen time do you spend per day? (Number) 
  This question asks for the amount of time you spend on electronic devices such as phones, computers, or tablets.
* 7. How much time do you spend studying per day? (Select One) 
  This question asks for the amount of time you spend on educational activities that may affect your sleep.
* 8. What is the quality of your sleep? (Select Multiple) 
  This question asks about your sleep quality, whether it is good or poor.
* 9. How often do you sleep at night? (Select One) 
  This question asks about the frequency of your sleep at night.
* 10. Where do you sleep? (Select Multiple) 
  This question asks about your sleeping location, such as your home or other locations.
* 11. Have you been woken up by anything recently? (Text) 
  This question asks for any recent events that have caused you to wake up.
* 12. What was it that woke you up? (Text) 
  This question asks for a description of what woke you up.
* 13. How often do other disturbances happen? (Select One) 
  This question asks about the frequency of other disturbances that affect your sleep.
* 14. Describe other disturbances. (Text) 
  This question asks for a description of any other events that have disturbed your sleep.
* 15. What was the impact of the disturbance on your sleep? (Select One) 
  This question asks about the severity of the disturbance's impact on your sleep.
* 16. What type of disturbance was it? (Select Multiple) 
  This question asks about the type of event that caused you to wake up.
* 17. Describe other disturbances. (Text) 
  This question asks for a description of any other events that have disturbed your sleep.
* 18. How often do other disturbances happen? (Select One) 
  This question asks about the frequency of other disturbances that affect your sleep.
