# Transfer Learning Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your research on transfer learning. It will help us better understand your research interests, methods, and outcomes. Please provide accurate and detailed answers to help us create valuable resources for the transfer learning community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Choose the correct options for your research interests and methods.
3. Provide detailed answers for text fields.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **What is your research question** (`research_question`, `text`, `required`)
Your research question is the main topic or focus of your research. Please provide a brief and clear statement of your research question. 

### 2. **What is your field of research** (`research_area`, `select_multiple`, `true`)
Select all the fields of research that apply to your current or potential research projects, such as Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, etc.

### 3. **Which institution is funding your research** (`research_institution`, `select_one`, `true`)
Choose the institution that is currently funding or will be funding your research. You can select one institution from the provided options. 

### 4. **What are your interests in transfer learning** (`research_interests`, `text`, `true`)
Please list all your interests in transfer learning, and explain why they are important.

### 5. **How many years of research experience do you have?** (`research_experience`, `number`, `true`)
Provide your current research experience level, from 1 to 10 years.

### 6. **Other interests in transfer learning** (`research_interests_other`, `text`, `false`)
If there are other interests in transfer learning that are not listed, please describe them.

### 7. **How do you currently employ transfer learning?** (`transfer_learning_method`, `select_multiple`, `true`)
Select all the methods you currently use for transfer learning, such as Source Separation, Domain Adaptation, Multi-Task Learning, etc.

### 8. **Other transfer learning methods** (`transfer_learning_method_other`, `text`, `false`)
If there are other methods you use that are not listed, please describe them.

### 9. **What are the benefits of transfer learning** (`transfer_learning_benefits`, `select_multiple`, `true`)
Select all the benefits you have experienced or expect to experience with transfer learning, such as Improved model generalizability, Reduced overfitting, etc.

### 10. **What are the challenges faced in transfer learning** (`transfer_learning_barriers`, `select_multiple`, `true`)
Select all the challenges you have faced or expect to face with transfer learning, such as Data quality issues, Data scarcity issues, etc.

### 11. **What is your vision for the future of transfer learning?** (`transfer_learning_future`, `text`, `false`)
Please describe your expectations or aspirations for the future of transfer learning.

### 12. **Have you published any research on transfer learning?** (`transfer_learning_publication`, `select_one`, `false`)
Check if you have published any research on transfer learning.

### 13. **Link to publication** (`publication_link`, `text`, `false`)
If you have published research, provide a link to the publication.

### 14. **Institution where research was conducted** (`transfer_learning_institution`, `text`, `false`)
If you have published research, provide the institution where it was conducted.

### 15. **Supervisor of research** (`transfer_learning_supervisor`, `text`, `false`)
If you have published research, provide the supervisor or PI of your research.

### 16. **Other contributors** (`transfer_learning_contributors`, `text`, `false`)
If you have published research, list any other contributors to your research.

### 17. **Funding source** (`transfer_learning_funding`, `text`, `false`)
If you have published research, provide the funding source or grant.

### 18. **Project name** (`transfer_learning_project`, `text`, `false`)
If you have published research, provide the project name.

### 19. **Project completion year** (`transfer_learning_year`, `number`, `false`)
If you have published research, provide the completion year of the project.

### 20. **Project duration** (`transfer_learning_duration`, `number`, `false`)
If you have published research, provide the duration of the project.

### 21. **Research outcome** (`transfer_learning_outcome`, `select_one`, `true`)
Choose the outcome of your research, such as Positive, Negative, or Neutral.

### 22. **Contribution of this research** (`transfer_learning_contribution`, `text`, `false`)
Please describe the contribution of your research.

### 23. **References** (`transfer_learning_references`, `text`, `false`)
Provide any relevant references or citations for your research.

### 24. **Acknowledgement** (`transfer_learning_acknowledgement`, `text`, `false`)
Please provide any acknowledgement or thank you message for contributors.

### 25. **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, `true`)
Finally, submit your survey. Please ensure you have answered all the questions and provided all necessary information.
