# Remote Healthcare Services Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients on the effectiveness of remote healthcare services. It's essential to provide valuable insights for healthcare providers to improve their services. Please answer these questions honestly to help us understand your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and answer all questions that apply to you.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your experience.
3. Enter your responses in the spaces provided for free-text answers.
4. Make sure to answer all required fields (denoted by \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* No explanation needed (this is an introductory note)

### 2. patient_experience
* Patient Experience: A checkbox for patients to indicate if they have had a positive experience with remote healthcare services. 
    * (Select 'Yes' if you have had a positive experience, 'No' otherwise)

### 3. appointment_type
* This field allows you to select the types of remote appointments you've had.
    * (Select all types that apply to your experience)

### 4. provider_rating
* Provider Rating: A scale for patients to rate the quality of care provided by their healthcare provider.
    * A higher number indicates a higher rating (1-10)

### 5. services_used
* Services Used: A checkbox for patients to indicate which remote healthcare services they've used.
    * (Select all services that apply to your experience)

### 6. satisfaction
* Satisfaction: A scale for patients to rate their overall satisfaction with remote healthcare services.
    * A higher number indicates higher satisfaction (1-10)

### 7. comments
* Comments: A text field for patients to provide any additional comments or feedback about their experience.
    * Please enter any relevant comments about your experience

### 8. follow_up
* Follow-up: A checkbox for patients to indicate if they've had any follow-up interactions with their healthcare provider after the remote appointment.
    * (Select 'Yes' if you've had a follow-up, 'No' otherwise)

### 9. patient_satisfaction
* Patient Satisfaction: A dropdown menu to select the level of satisfaction.
    * (Select Satisfied or Dissatisfied)

### 10. provider_satisfaction
* Provider Satisfaction: A dropdown menu to select the level of satisfaction.
    * (Select Satisfied or Dissatisfied)

### 11. service_quality
* Service Quality: A scale for patients to rate the quality of remote healthcare services they've received.
    * A higher number indicates a higher rating (1-10)

### 12. patient_experience_follow_up
* Patient Experience Follow-up: A checkbox for patients to indicate if their experience has improved after follow-up interactions.
    * (Select 'Yes' if your experience has improved, 'No' otherwise)

### 13. follow_up_frequency
* Follow-up Frequency: A checkbox for patients to indicate if they've had follow-up interactions with their healthcare provider more than once.
    * (Select 'Yes' if you've had multiple follow-ups, 'No' otherwise)

### 14. follow_up_outcome
* Follow-up Outcome: A checkbox for patients to indicate if their condition has improved after follow-up interactions.
    * (Select 'Yes' if your condition has improved, 'No' otherwise)

## Tips
- Take your time to answer all questions carefully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
- Your feedback is valuable, so please be honest and provide as much information as you can.
