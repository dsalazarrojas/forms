# fitter_written_test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fitter Written Test" form is used to collect information from users on their written tests. This form is designed to gather feedback on the questions or other aspects of the written tests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. For questions with multiple-choice options, select the most accurate answer.
3. For open-ended questions (text fields), type your answer in the provided box.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **this-is-an-introduction** (`introduction`, text, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the written test.
* **written-test-question-1** (`written_test_question_1`, select_one, required: false): Select the most accurate answer for this question.
* **written-test-question-2** (`written_test_question_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply for this question.
* **written-test-question-2-answer-1** (`written_test_question_2_answer_1`, text, required: false): Provide more information for this question.
* **written-test-question-3** (`written_test_question_3`, date, required: false): Enter the date for this question.
* **written-test-question-4** (`written_test_question_4`, time, required: false): Enter the time for this question.
* **written-test-question-5** (`written_test_question_5`, text, required: false): Provide more information for this question.
* **written-test-question-6** (`written_test_question_6`, text, required: false): Enter more information for this question.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact information.

## Tips

* Answer each question carefully and honestly.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your email or phone number.
