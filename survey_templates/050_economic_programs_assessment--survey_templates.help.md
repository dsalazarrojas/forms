# Economic Programs Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Economic Programs Assessment form is a tool used to collect and analyze information about economic programs. This form is designed to provide insight into the outcomes, satisfaction, and other key aspects of economic programs, which can help administrators or researchers evaluate and improve these programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through each page carefully to understand the questions and what information is required.
* Fill in the answers to the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Be sure to select the most relevant options for each field.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Outcomes (<label>)**:
	+ This field is where you will list the outcomes of the economic program. Think of the expected results or achievements of the program.
* **Program Satisfaction (<label>)**:
	+ This field is used to measure the level of satisfaction of the participants in the program. Please enter a number to indicate their level of satisfaction.
* **Agency Partners (<label>)**:
	+ This field is a select multiple field where you can choose from a list of options. Select all the agency partners that are involved in the program.
* **Participant Feedback (<label>)**:
	+ This field is a text area where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the participant's experience with the program.
* **Program Goals (<label>)**:
	+ This field is a text area where you can describe the goals of the program. What are the objectives or targets of the program?
* **Program Objectives (<label>)**:
	+ This field is also a text area where you can describe the objectives of the program. What are the specific actions or steps taken to achieve the goals?
* **Program Indicators (<label>)**:
	+ This field is a text area where you can describe the indicators that measure the progress or achievement of the program's objectives.
* **Participant Satisfaction (<label>)**:
	+ This field is used to measure the level of satisfaction of the participants in the program. Please enter a number to indicate their level of satisfaction.
* **Participant Feedback 2 (<label>)**:
	+ This field is a text area where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the participant's experience with the program.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
