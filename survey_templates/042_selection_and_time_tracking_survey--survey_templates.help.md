# Selection And Time Tracking Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey template is designed to gather feedback from users about the selection and time tracking process. It is intended for users who have participated in the selection process and is used to gather their experiences, satisfaction, and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Select the options that apply to you from the "Activity Choices" section.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the activity using the "Satisfaction" and "Satisfaction Rating" fields.
3. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (this is optional, but helps us contact you if needed).
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (this is optional, but helps us contact you if needed).
6. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide a brief introduction or context about your experience with the selection process.
* **Activity Choices**: Select all the options that apply to you from the list of activity choices. This will help us understand your experience with the activities presented to you.
* **Time Allocation**: Enter the time allocated for the activity, if any.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the activity on a scale (e.g., 1-5 or 1-10).
* **Satisfaction Rating**: This is another field to rate your satisfaction with the activity, if not already rated in the previous field. 
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about your experience with the activity.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about the selection process.
