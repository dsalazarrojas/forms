# Vendor Reference Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Reference Check Form is used to gather contact and reference information from a vendor's client. This form helps us verify vendor references for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the vendor's **First Name** in the "first_name" field.
2.  Enter the vendor's **Company Name** in the "company_name" field.
3.  Enter the vendor's **Job Title** in the "job_title" field.
4.  Enter the vendor's **Contact Email** in the "contact_email" field, ensuring it's a valid email address.
5.  Enter the vendor's **Phone Number** in the "contact_phone" field.
6.  Indicate if the vendor has been a **Past Client** by selecting "True" or "False" from the dropdown menu, or choose "Unknown" if you're unsure.
7.  Enter the **Reference Name** in the "reference_name" field.
8.  Enter the **Reference Title** in the "reference_title" field.
9.  Enter the vendor's **Contact Email (Reference)** in the "reference_contact_email" field, ensuring it's a valid email address.
10. Enter the vendor's **Phone Number (Reference)** in the "reference_contact_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter the first name of the person you are referencing.
*   **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company that the person is associated with.
*   **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the person you are referencing.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact.
*   **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for the vendor's contact.
*   **Past Client**: Check if you have verified that the vendor has been a past client or not. If unsure, choose "Unknown".
*   **Reference Name**: Enter the name of the person you are referencing.
*   **Reference Title**: Enter the title of the person being referenced.
*   **Contact Email (Reference)**: Enter a valid email address for the person being referenced.
*   **Phone (Reference)**: Enter a valid phone number for the person being referenced.
