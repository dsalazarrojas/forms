# Go Kart Racing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from customers who have visited a kart racing facility. The goal is to gather information about their experience, satisfaction, and any suggestions they may have for improvement. This will help the facility to understand their strengths and weaknesses and make necessary adjustments to provide a better experience for future visitors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Read the form carefully**: Before starting, take a moment to read through the form and understand what each question is asking.
2. **Answer all questions**: Answer all the questions to the best of your ability. If you have no experience or opinion, simply write "N/A" or "No comment".
3. **Be honest and thorough**: Provide honest and detailed feedback, as this will help the facility to make informed decisions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the go kart racing survey**: This is a introductory page that welcomes you to the survey. You can skip this page and proceed to the next one.
* **How was your experience at the kart racing facility**: Share your honest feedback about your experience at the facility. Be specific about what happened during your visit.
* **How was the staff interaction during your visit**: Share your feedback about how the staff interacted with you during your visit. Were they friendly, helpful, etc.?
* **How were the facilities at the kart racing facility**: Describe your experience with the facilities, such as the cleanliness, organization, etc.
* **How would you rate the quality of racing provided**: Rate the quality of the racing experience on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **How would you rate the price of the experience**: Rate the price of the experience on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being too expensive and 5 being reasonable.
* **Overall satisfaction with your visit**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the visit on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 5 being very satisfied.
* **Any suggestions for the staff**: If you have any suggestions for the staff, such as improvements or changes, share them here.
* **Any suggestions for improvements**: If you have any suggestions for improvements, such as changes to the facility or services, share them here.
* **Would you like to provide a review**: If you would like to provide a review, select "Yes" and you will be able to provide a review message.
* **Email address for follow up**: If you would like to be contacted for follow up, provide your email address here.
* **Contact number**: If you would like to be contacted for follow up, provide your contact number here.
* **Review message**: If you selected "Yes" to provide a review, write a message about your experience with the facility.
