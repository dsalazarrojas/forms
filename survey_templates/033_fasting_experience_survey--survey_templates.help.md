# Fasting Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect user feedback and experiences about fasting, including their motivations, frequency, duration, and overall satisfaction. This information will help us to better understand user's perceptions and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information about your fasting experiences.
2. Select the correct options for each field, using the provided options for select fields.
3. If you have any other comments or experiences that are not listed in the provided options, please use the "Other Comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fasting Type** (`fast_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of fasting you have experienced, either "Yes" or "No".
* **Motivation for Fasting** (`motivation_for_fasting`, select_one, required: false): Select the reason(s) you have fasted, either "Yes" or "No". This field is designed to understand your motivations behind fasting.
* **Motivation for Fasting Other** (`motivation_for_fasting_other`, text, required: false): If the reason for fasting does not fit in the previous field, please provide the motivation for fasting in this text field.
* **Fasting Frequency** (`fasting_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the frequency at which you have fasted, either "Yes" or "No". This field is designed to understand how often you have fasted.
* **Duration of Fasting** (`duration_of_fasting`, number, required: false): If applicable, please provide the duration of your fasting experience.
* **Time of Fasting** (`time_fasting`, time, required: false): If applicable, please provide the time of your fasting experience.
* **Challenges Fasting** (`challenges_fasting`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the challenges you have faced during fasting, either "Yes" or "No". This field is designed to understand any difficulties you have faced during fasting.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Select your overall satisfaction with fasting, either "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Fasting Type Other** (`fasting_type_other`, text, required: false): If the type of fasting does not fit in the previous field, please provide the type of fasting in this text field.
* **Motivation for Fasting Other Details** (`motivation_for_fasting_other_details`, text, required: false): If the motivation for fasting does not fit in the previous field, please provide the motivation for fasting in this text field.
* **Fasting Frequency Other** (`fasting_frequency_other`, text, required: false): If the frequency of fasting does not fit in the previous field, please provide the frequency of fasting in this text field.
* **Duration of Fasting Other** (`duration_of_fasting_other`, text, required: false): If the duration of fasting does not fit in the previous field, please provide the duration of fasting in this text field.
* **Challenges Fasting Other** (`challenges_fasting_other`, text, required: false): If the challenges of fasting do not fit in the previous field, please provide the challenges of fasting in this text field.
* **Motivation for Fasting Other Details** (`motivation_for_fasting_other_details`, text, required: false): If the motivation for fasting does not fit in the previous field, please provide the motivation for fasting in this text field.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, required: false): If you have any additional comments or experiences about fasting, please provide them in this text field.
* **Fasting Experience** (`fasting_experience`, text, required: false): If you have any additional experiences about your fasting experience, please provide them in this text field.
