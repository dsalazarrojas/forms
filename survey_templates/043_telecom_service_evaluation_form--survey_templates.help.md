# Telecom Service Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather your feedback about your experience with our telecom services. Your input will help us improve our services and better serve you and other customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form if you have a direct experience with our telecom services.
2. The form is divided into several fields. Each field is labeled with a brief description.
3. Fill out the form completely, but do not worry if you do not have an answer for a particular field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Telecom Service Evaluation** (`telecom_service_evaluation`, Text, Optional): A brief description of your experience with our telecom services. Please type out your thoughts.
* **Rate Our Telecom Services** (`rating_telecom_services`, Number, Optional): Rate the quality of our telecom services on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, Number, Optional): Rate the value for money of our telecom services on a scale from 0 to 10.

* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, Number, Optional): Rate the quality of our customer service on a scale from 0 to 10.

* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Note, Optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions about our telecom services, please write them down here.
