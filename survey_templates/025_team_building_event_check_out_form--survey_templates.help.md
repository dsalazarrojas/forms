# Team Building Event Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and evaluations from team members after a team building event. It helps event organizers to assess the effectiveness of the event, identify areas of improvement, and understand the team's dynamics, skills, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event name and participant name in the respective fields.
2. Rate the event and team performance on a scale of 1-5 for each of the listed skills and attributes.
3. Provide any feedback, suggestions, or comments about the event in the "Feedback" field.
4. Complete any other fields as needed.
