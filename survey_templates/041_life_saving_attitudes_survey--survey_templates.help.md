# Life Saving Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Saving Attitudes Survey is designed to assess individuals' attitudes towards life-saving techniques, such as CPR and first aid. The survey consists of multiple-choice questions that aim to understand respondents' knowledge, confidence, and practices related to life-saving situations. This guide will walk you through each section of the survey, ensuring you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reviewing the instructions carefully and answering all questions truthfully.
2. For select-one and select-multiple questions, choose the relevant options that best describe your attitudes or practices.
3. For questions with options, select only one option that best fits your response.
4. Make sure to answer all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: `false`): This section is an introduction to the survey, providing a brief overview of the questions you will be asked.
* **Questions** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Please answer "Yes" or "No" to the following questions:
	+ Do you think CPR is essential in life-saving situations?
	+ Do you have a good understanding of CPR techniques?
* **Training History** (`3`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Yes, I have received training in CPR
	+ No, I have not received training in CPR
* **Confidence** (`4`, `number`, required: `false`): This question is optional and allows you to rate your confidence level in performing CPR on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being not confident at all and 5 being very confident.
* **Follow Instructions** (`5`, `number`, required: `false`): This question is optional and asks you to rate how well you follow instructions in a life-saving situation, on a scale of 1-5.
* **First Aid Knowledge** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Please answer "Yes" or "No" to the following questions:
