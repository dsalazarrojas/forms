# Interdisciplinary Learning Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Interdisciplinary Learning Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback from learners who have participated in the course.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Carefully review each page of the form to ensure you answer every question accurately and thoroughly.
* Select the answer option that best reflects your thoughts and feelings for each question.
* For multiple-choice questions, choose only one option that best represents your response.
* For numerical questions, provide a value between 0 and 10, where 0 indicates "very dissatisfied" and 10 indicates "very satisfied".
* For open-ended questions (such as "Comments"), please provide any additional information or suggestions you may have.
* Make sure to submit your completed form to provide the most valuable feedback.
