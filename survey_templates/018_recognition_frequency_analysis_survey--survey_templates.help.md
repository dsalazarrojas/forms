# Recognition Frequency Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to collect data on employee recognition frequency and its relation to employee performance. This will help employers and HR managers understand how often employees receive recognition, what barriers might prevent them from receiving recognition, and how to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form is divided into several sections. Please read each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2.  You can select "Other" in some fields if your answer is not among the provided options.
3.  If you are unsure about an answer, choose "Don't know what to say" and provide further comments if possible.
4.  Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
5.  Make sure to submit your answers as accurately and completely as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Recognition Frequency Analysis Survey**

    \*   This is a title field and does not require an answer.
*   **Do you give recognition to your employees?**

    \*   This question asks whether you give recognition to your employees.
    \*   Select "Yes" if you regularly give recognition to your employees.
    \*   Select "No" if you do not give recognition to your employees.
    \*   Select "Sometimes" if your recognition frequency varies.
*   **How often do you give recognition?**

    \*   This question asks about the frequency of your recognition.
    \*   Select "Never" if you do not give recognition.
    \*   Select "Rarely" if you give recognition very infrequently.
    \*   Select "Sometimes" if your recognition frequency varies.
    \*   Select "Often" if you regularly give recognition.
    \*   Select "Always" if you constantly give recognition.
*   **Why don't you give more recognition?**

    \*   This question asks about the reasons for lack of recognition.
    \*   Select "Don't know what to say" if you are unsure.
    \*   Select "Lack of time" if you lack time to give recognition.
    \*   Select "Don't want to be seen as favoritism" if you are concerned about being seen as favoring some employees over others.
    \*   Select "Other" if your reason is not among the provided options.
*   **What are barriers to giving recognition?**

    \*   This question asks about the challenges you face when giving recognition.
    \*   Select "Don't know what to say" if you are unsure.
    *   Select "Lack of feedback" if you lack feedback from employees.
    *   Select "Don't want to appear insincere" if you are concerned about appearing insincere.
    *   Select "Other" if your challenge is not among the provided options.
*   **Ideas to Improve Recognition**

    \*   This is a field where you can provide suggestions for improving recognition.
*   **How often do you receive recognition?**

    \*   This question asks about the frequency of recognition you receive.
    \*   Select "Never" if you never receive recognition.
    \*   Select "Rarely" if you receive recognition very infrequently.
    \*   Select "Sometimes" if your recognition frequency varies.
    \*   Select "Often" if you regularly receive recognition.
    \*   Select "Always" if you constantly receive recognition.
*   **Why don't you receive recognition?**

    \*   This question asks about the reasons you don't receive recognition.
    \*   Select "Not noticed" if your achievements go unnoticed.
    \*   Select "Not worthy" if you feel your work is not valuable.
    \*   Select "Not enough to do" if you feel you do not meet expectations.
    \*   Select "Other" if your reason is not among the provided options.
*   **What recognition means to you?**

    \*   This is a field where you can describe what recognition means to you.
*   **Suggestions for improved recognition**

    \*   This is a field where you can provide suggestions for improving recognition.

## Tips

*   Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
*   If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the relevant field.
*   Please submit your answers accurately and completely.

This help guide is intended to assist you in understanding and completing the Recognition Frequency Analysis Survey. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
