# moving_crew_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with a moving company's moving crew.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your move in the "Moving Date" field.
2. Enter the time of your move in the "Moving Time" field.
3. Enter the location you were moving from in the "Moving From" field.
4. Enter the location you moved to in the "Moving To" field.
5. Select the service level of the moving crew in the "Moving Crew Service Level" field.
6. Enter any comments or feedback about communication with the driver in the "Communication with Driver" field.
7. Rate the punctuality of the moving crew in the "Moving Crew Punctuality" field.
8. Rate the attentiveness of the moving crew in the "Moving Crew Attentiveness" field.
9. Rate the quality of equipment used by the moving crew in the "Moving Crew Equipment Quality" field.
10. Rate the cleanliness of the moving crew in the "Moving Crew Cleanliness" field.
11. Rate the overall satisfaction with the moving crew in the "Moving Crew Overall Satisfaction" field.
12. Enter the name of the moving company in the "Moving Company Name" field.
13. Enter the website of the moving company in the "Moving Company Website" field.
14. Enter the name of the person who completed the survey in the "Survey Admin" field.
15. Enter the status of the survey in the "Survey Status" field.
16. Rate the moving crew's performance in the "Moving Crew Rating" field.
17. Enter any additional feedback in the "Feedback" field.
18. Enter the contact number of the person who completed the survey in the "Contact Number" field.
19. Enter the email of the person who completed the survey in the "Contact Email" field.
20. Enter the date the survey was created in the "Date Created" field.
21. If you had a second move, enter the date of the second move in the "Moving Date 2" field.
22. If you had a second move, enter the time of the second move in the "Moving Time 2" field.
23. If you had a second move, enter the location you were moving from in the "Moving From 2" field.
24. If you had a second move, enter the location you moved to in the "Moving To 2" field.
25. If you had a second move, select the service level of the second moving crew in the "Moving Crew Service Level 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Moving Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your move.
* **Moving Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of your move.
* **Moving From** (Text, Optional): Enter the location you were moving from.
* **Moving To** (Text, Optional): Enter the location you moved to.
* **Moving Crew Service Level** (Select One, Required): Select the service level of the moving crew.
* **Communication with Driver** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments or feedback about communication with the driver.
* **Moving Crew Punctuality** (Text, Optional): Rate the punctuality of the moving crew (1-5).
* **Moving Crew Attentiveness** (Text, Optional): Rate the attentiveness of the moving crew (1-5).
* **Moving Crew Equipment Quality** (Text, Optional): Rate the quality of equipment used by the moving crew (1-5).
* **Moving Crew Cleanliness** (Text, Optional): Rate the cleanliness of the moving crew (1-5).
* **Moving Crew Overall Satisfaction** (Text, Optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the moving crew (1-5).
* **Moving Company Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the moving company.
* **Moving Company Website** (Text, Optional): Enter the website of the moving company.
* **Survey Admin** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person who completed the survey.
* **Survey Status** (Text, Optional): Enter the status of the survey.
* **Moving Crew Rating** (Number, Optional): Rate the moving crew's performance (1-5).
* **Feedback** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional feedback.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact number of the person who completed the survey.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email of the person who completed the survey.
* **Date Created** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the survey was created.
* **Moving Date 2** (Date, Optional): If you had a second move, enter the date of the second move.
* **Moving Time 2** (Time, Optional): If you had a second move, enter the time of the second move.
* **Moving From 2** (Text, Optional): If you had a second move, enter the location you were moving from.
* **Moving To 2** (Text, Optional): If you had a second move, enter the location you moved to.
* **Moving Crew Service Level 2** (Select One, Optional): If you had a second move, select the service level of the second moving crew.
