# TV Pilot Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from viewers on a TV pilot. The goal is to understand their impressions, preferences, and demographic information.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading through each question carefully.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the form, explaining what it's about and why you're taking this survey.
* **What do you think of the pilot's title?**: Rate your overall impression of the title of the TV pilot. This question is required and allows us to gauge your initial response.
* **Rate your impressions of the pilot:** Rate your overall impression of the pilot, using a numerical scale.
* **What was your favorite part of the pilot?** (Optional): If you enjoyed a particular segment, let us know what it was.
* **Which character did you enjoy the most?** (Optional): Choose the character that resonated with you the most.
* **Other character:** (Optional, if you didn't choose above): If you didn't select a character from the previous question, describe the character that stood out to you.
* **Enter your email:** (Optional): If you'd like to be entered into our feedback draw, please enter your email address. We won't share your email with anyone.
* **Enter your phone:** (Optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be reached out for further discussion.
* **Demographics** (Optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes (you're interested in this type of content)
	+ No (you're not interested in this type of content)
* **Gender:** (Required): Select your gender.
* **Age:** (Required): Enter your age.
* **Location:** (Required): Enter the region you're from.
* **Occupation:** (Required): Enter your occupation.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your feedback is submitted properly. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask and we'll be happy to help!
