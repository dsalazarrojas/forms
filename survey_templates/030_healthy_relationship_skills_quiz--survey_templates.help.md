# Healthy Relationship Skills Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's skills and knowledge related to building and maintaining healthy relationships. The quiz is composed of 20 questions, each asking about specific aspects of relationships, such as communication, conflict resolution, trust, and emotional intelligence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the space provided for each page.
2. Be honest and sincere in your responses, as they will help you gain a better understanding of your relationship skills.
3. Complete all 20 questions, as they are all important for a comprehensive assessment.
4. Once you have finished, submit your answers to review your results.
