# Critical Practice Program Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Critical Practice Program Survey is a form designed to collect data about programs under the Critical Practice Program. This data will be used to evaluate and improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you fill out all required fields.
2. Enter the name of your program in the "What is the name of your program?" field.
3. Provide a brief description of your program in the "What is the description of your program?" field.
4. Select your role in the program from the options provided in the "What is your role in this program?" field.
5. Enter the start and end dates of your program in the "Start date of your program?" and "End date of your program?" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the name of the main contact person for your program in the "Name of main contact?" field.
7. Enter the contact email and phone number of the main contact person in the "Contact E-mail" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
8. Enter the location of your program in the "Location" field.
9. Enter the number of participants in your program in the "Number of participants" field.
10. Provide any participant feedback or comments in the "Participant feedback" field.
11. Enter the goals of your program in the "Program goals" field.
12. Describe the participant involvement in your program in the "Participant involvement" field.
13. Specify the evaluation criterion for your program in the "Evaluation criterion" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the name of your program?** (`program_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your program.
* **What is the description of your program?** (`program_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your program.
* **What is your role in this program?** (`role`, select_one, required: false): Select your role in the program from the options provided.
* **Start date of your program?** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your program.
* **End date of your program?** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of your program.
* **Name of main contact?** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the main contact person for your program.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the main contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the main contact person.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your program.
* **Number of participants** (`number_of_participants`, number, required: false): Enter the number of participants in your program.
* **Participant feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, required: false): Provide any participant feedback or comments.
* **Program goals** (`program_goals`, text, required: false): Enter the goals of your program.
* **Participant involvement** (`participant_involvement`, text, required: false): Describe the participant involvement in your program.
* **Evaluation criterion** (`evaluation_criterion`, text, required: false): Specify the evaluation criterion for your program.
