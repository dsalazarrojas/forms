# product_metrics_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and metrics from Prophets on the usage and satisfaction with the product. This information will be used to improve the product and enhance user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the product usage frequency from the options provided.
2. Choose your level of satisfaction with the product.
3. Enter any improvement ideas you may have for the product.
4. Select the features of the product you have used.
5. Choose the ideas for improvement from the options provided.
6. Select the level of satisfaction you have with the product.
7. Enter your comments on the product.
8. Enter your Prophet ID.
9. Enter your Prophet Name.
10. Enter your Prophet email address.
11. Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
12. Enter a brief description of the product.
13. Enter the name of the product (again, this field seems to be a duplicate).
14. Enter the product version.
15. Select the features of the product you have used.
16. Select the ideas for improvement you have.
17. Select the level of satisfaction you have with the product.
18. Enter your comments on the product.
19. Enter the name of the person submitting the feedback.
20. Enter the email address of the person submitting the feedback.
21. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the feedback.
22. Enter the name of the company of the person submitting the feedback.
23. Enter the job title of the person submitting the feedback.
24. Enter the department of the person submitting the feedback.
25. Enter the job function of the person submitting the feedback.
26. Enter the location of the person submitting the feedback.
27. Enter the job title of the person submitting the feedback (again, this field seems to be a duplicate).
28. Enter any comments from the person submitting the feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Usage** (`1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the frequency of your product usage from the options provided.
	+ Select the option that best describes your usage frequency.
* **Satisfaction** (`2`, `number`, required: false): Enter your level of satisfaction with the product.
	+ Use a number to express your level of satisfaction.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter any improvement ideas you may have for the product.
	+ Write your ideas here.
* **Prophet ID** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your Prophet ID.
	+ This is an identifier for you as a Prophet.
* **Prophet Name** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter your Prophet Name.
	+ This is your name as a Prophet.
* **Prophet E-mail** (`6`, `email`, required: false): Enter your Prophet email address.
	+ This is your email address.
* **Product Name** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
	+ This is the name of the product.
* **Product Description** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the product.
	+ This is a brief description of the product.
* **Prophet Name** (`9`, `text`, required: false): This field seems to be a duplicate, please ignore.
* **Product Version** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Enter the product version.
	+ This is the version of the product.
* **Feature Usage** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the features of the product you have used.
	+ Select the features you have used.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the ideas for improvement you have.
