# Energy Audit Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Audit Check Out Form is a crucial tool to assess users' energy-efficient habits and provide insights for optimizing energy consumption.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the fields with accurate information about your daily habits.
2. Be honest in your responses, as these will influence the accuracy of the assessment.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk).
4. Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure you have provided complete and accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What time of day do you turn off the lights? (energy_usage_1)**: Indicate the typical time you turn off the lights in your home or office.
* **Energy-efficient devices at home or office? (energy_usage_2)**: Choose all the energy-efficient devices you use daily, such as LED light bulbs or energy-efficient washers and dryers.
* **Preferred lighting type (lighting_type)**: Select your preferred type of lighting, like LED, Incandescent, or Halogen.
* **Hours spent on electronic devices per day (electronic_usage)**: Enter the average number of hours you spend on electronic devices like computers, TVs, or gaming consoles.
* **How do you commute? (commute_method)**: Indicate if you use public transportation, walk, bike, or drive a car to get to work or school.
* **How often do you wash clothes? (laundry_method)**: Choose the frequency of washing your clothes, such as always using an energy-intensive washing machine or hand-washing and air-drying.
* **Preferred thermostat temperature (winter) (thermostat_setting)**: Select the temperature you prefer to keep your thermostat set to in the winter months.
* **Solar panels or other renewable energy sources? (solar_panels)**: Choose if you have solar panels or any other renewable energy sources installed.
* **How often do you use a water heater during winter? (water_heater_usage)**: Indicate the frequency of using a water heater during winter months.
* **Energy-efficient appliances? (energy_efficient_appliances)**: Choose if all your appliances are energy-efficient, like Energy Star rated ones.
* **Average electricity bill per month (electricity_bill)**: Enter your average electricity bill for the current month.
* **Plans to switch to renewable energy sources? (renewable_energy_plans)**: Indicate your plans to switch to renewable energy sources like solar, wind, or hydro power.

## Tips
* Be honest in your responses to get accurate results.
* If you have any additional information or habits that are not listed, feel free to include them in the notes section.
* Review your answers before submission to ensure accuracy.
