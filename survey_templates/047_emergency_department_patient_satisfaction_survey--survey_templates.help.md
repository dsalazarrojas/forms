# Emergency Department Patient Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients who have visited the emergency department. The survey aims to collect their impressions of the department's staff, facilities, and overall experience. The information collected will help us improve our services and ensure that we provide the best possible care for our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the text fields provided.
2. Rate the communication and professionalism of the doctor and nurse using the options below each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Comments" field.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to save your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prophet** (`prophets`, text, optional): Please describe your experience in the emergency department.
* **How was your emergency department visit structured?** (`patient`, text, optional): Please explain how your visit was organized, including the waiting time, treatment process, and any other relevant details.
* **ID** (`patient_id`, number, optional): Please enter your patient ID number.
* **Doctor** (`doctor`, text, optional): Rate the communication skills of the doctor who treated you. (Scale: 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent)
* **Staff** (`staff`, text, optional): Rate the professionalism of the emergency department staff. (Scale: 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent)
* **Nurse** (`nurse`, text, optional): Rate the communication skills of the nurse who treated you. (Scale: 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent)
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, text, optional): Please comment on the cleanliness, comfort, and overall condition of the emergency department facilities.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, optional): Please describe your overall experience in the emergency department, including any positive or negative aspects.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional feedback or suggestions for improvement, please share them here.
* **Doctor ID** (`doctor_id`, number, optional): Please enter the doctor's ID number (if known).
* (Note: All fields are optional, but your feedback is greatly appreciated!)
