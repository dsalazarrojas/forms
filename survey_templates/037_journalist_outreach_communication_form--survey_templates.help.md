# Journalist Outreach Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Journalist Outreach Communication Form is designed to collect information about journalists we want to collaborate with or provide content to. This form helps us tailor our communication and content creation to their specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (this must be a valid email address).
4. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Enter the name of the organization you work for in the "Organization" field.
6. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
7. If you have a journal you write for, select the answer in the "Journal" field.
8. If you write articles regularly, enter the number of articles you write per month in the "How often do you write articles per month?" field.
9. Enter a brief description of the topic you're interested in in the "Topic" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name you go by.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your role.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the organization you work for.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Journal** (`journal`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the name of the journal you write for.
* **How often do you write articles per month?** (`journal_frequency`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of articles you write per month.
* **Topic** (`topic`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the topic you're interested in.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Make sure your email address and phone number (if provided) are up to date and correct.
* If you're unsure about the journal or topic fields, leave them blank.
