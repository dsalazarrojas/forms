# Leadership Transformation Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Transformation Impact Survey is a tool to collect feedback and information from participants in leadership transformation training programs. The survey helps us understand the effectiveness of these programs in enhancing leadership skills, communication, and project management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. If you're unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or trainer for guidance.
3. Fill out the form honestly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Survey Title (1)
• **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, required: false): This is the title of the training program that you're participating in. Please enter the actual title of the program.

### Training Area (2)
• **Training Area** (`training_area`, text, required: false): This is the department or area where you work. Please enter the department name.

### Training Programs (3)
• **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the training programs that you've participated in. Examples: Leadership Development, Communication, Project Management, Time Management, etc.

### Training Method (4)
• **Training Method** (`training_method`, select_one, required: false): How do you think you'll be trained? Examples: Face-to-Face, Online, Virtual, In-person, Other (please specify).

### Training Length (5)
• **Training Length** (`training_length`, number, required: false): This is the length of the training program. Please enter the number of days or hours.

### Training Hours (6)
• **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, required: false): This is the total number of training hours you've participated in. Please enter the total number of hours.

### Training Participation (7)
• **Training Participation** (`training_participation`, select_one, required: false): How frequently do you participate in training programs? Examples: Highly, Moderately, Not at all.

### Impact on Performance (8)
• **Impact on Performance** (`impact_on_performance`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the ways the training program has impacted your performance. Examples: Increased productivity, Improved time management, Enhanced leadership skills, Better relationships with team members, Improved communication.

### Impact on Engagement (9)
• **Impact on Engagement** (`impact_on_engagement`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the ways the training program has impacted your engagement. Examples: Increased job satisfaction, Improved work-life balance, Better relationships with team members, Improved communication, Enhanced sense of purpose.

### Impact on Job Satisfaction (10)
• **Impact on Job Satisfaction** (`impact_on_job_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the ways the training program has impacted your job satisfaction. Examples: Increased job satisfaction, Improved work-life balance, Better relationships with team members, Improved communication, Enhanced sense of purpose.

### Training Frequency (11)
• **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, select_one, required: false): How often do you participate in training programs? Examples: Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annual, Annually, Never.

### Training Focus (12)
• **Training Focus** (`training_focus`, select_one, required: false): What is the focus of the training program? Examples: Leadership development, Communication, Project Management, Team Building.

### Training Goals (13)
• **Training Goals** (`training_goals`, text, required: false): What are the goals of the training program? Please enter the actual goals.

### Survey Comments (14)
• **Survey Comments** (`survey_comments`, text, required: false): Please add any additional comments or feedback about the training program.

### Participant Name (15)
• **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Your actual name. Please enter your actual name.

### Department (16)
• **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Your department or work area. Please enter the department name.

### Supervisor Name (17)
• **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required: false): Your supervisor's name. Please enter your supervisor's actual name.

### Supervisor Title (18)
• **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, required: false): Your supervisor's title. Please enter your supervisor's actual title.

### Training Location (19)
• **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required: false): Where did you participate in the training program? Please enter the actual location.

### Training Date (20)
• **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required: false): What date did you participate in the training program? Please enter the actual date.

### Training Time (21)
• **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required: false): What time did you participate in the training program? Please enter the actual time.

### Training Duration (22)
• **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, required: false): How long did the training program last? Please enter the number of hours or minutes.

### Evaluation Comments (23)
• **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, required: false): Please add any additional comments or feedback about the training program.

### Follow-up (24)
• **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required: false): Do you want to be contacted for follow-up questions? Examples: 'True', 'False'.

### Participant Signature (25)
• **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required: false): Your actual signature. Please enter your actual signature.

Note: Please fill out the form honestly and accurately.
