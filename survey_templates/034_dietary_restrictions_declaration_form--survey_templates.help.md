# dietary_restrictions_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual's dietary restrictions and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Survey Header** (page 1) section, which provides an introduction to the form.
2. In the **What do you often eat?** section (page 2), select the types of food that you typically eat from the list of options provided. You can choose one or more of the following options:
	* Vegetarian
	* Vegan
	* Omnivore
3. In the **Food Restrictions** section (page 3), enter any specific foods that you are restricted from eating due to medical reasons.
4. In the **Specific Foods** section (page 4), enter any other specific foods that you are restricted from eating.
5. In the **Dietary Restrictions** section (page 5), select the type of dietary restriction you have:
	* None
	* Fish and Seafood
	* Red Meat
	* Poultry
	* Eggs
	* Dairy
	* Fruits
	* Nuts
	* Soy
6. In the **Other Dietary Restrictions** section (page 6), enter any other dietary restrictions you have that are not listed above.
7. Review the **Survey Footer** (page 7) section, which provides a summary of the information you've entered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Header** (page 1, note): This section provides an introduction to the form.
* **What do you often eat?** (page 2, select_multiple):
	+ **Vegetarian**: A person who eats a diet that includes vegetables and fruits but not meat.
	+ **Vegan**: A person who eats a diet that is plant-based and excludes all animal products.
	+ **Omnivore**: A person who eats a diet that includes a variety of foods, including meat and plants.
* **Food Restrictions** (page 3, text): Enter any specific foods that you are restricted from eating due to medical reasons.
* **Specific Foods** (page 4, text): Enter any other specific foods that you are restricted from eating.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (page 5, select_one):
	+ **None**: No dietary restrictions.
	+ **Fish and Seafood**: Restriction from eating fish and seafood.
	+ **Red Meat**: Restriction from eating red meat.
	+ **Poultry**: Restriction from eating poultry.
	+ **Eggs**: Restriction from eating eggs.
	+ **Dairy**: Restriction from eating dairy products.
	+ **Fruits**: Restriction from eating fruits.
	+ **Nuts**: Restriction from eating nuts.
	+ **Soy**: Restriction from eating soy products.
* **Other Dietary Restrictions** (page 6, text): Enter any other dietary restrictions you have.
* **Survey Footer** (page 7, note): Review the information you've entered.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact a healthcare professional.
* This form is intended to be used for medical purposes, so please only answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
