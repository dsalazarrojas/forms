# Dishwasher Installation Site Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the dishwasher installation site, which will be used to prepare for the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the necessary fields with the required information:

1. **User Details**: Provide your personal contact information and any other relevant details.
2. **Site Details**: Enter the address of the site where the dishwasher will be installed.
3. **Dishwasher Details**: Enter the type and model number of the dishwasher being installed.
4. **Installation Details**: Describe the installation specifics, including any notable requirements or constraints.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (User Details, Text, Optional): Enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Site Details** (Site Details, Text, Optional): Enter the exact address of the site where the dishwasher will be installed, including apartment number and floor.
* **Dishwasher Details** (Dishwasher Details, Text, Optional): Enter the type and model number of the dishwasher being installed.
* **Installation Details** (Installation Details, Text, Optional): Describe the installation specifics, including any notable requirements or constraints, such as water supply and drainage conditions.
