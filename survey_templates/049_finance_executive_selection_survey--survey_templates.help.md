# Finance Executive Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a candidate's experience, skills, and abilities in the finance department. It is intended to be used by HR professionals or hiring managers for the selection of finance executives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Provide honest and accurate information about the candidate's experience and skills.
3. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly, as it will help in making an informed decision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Finance Executive Selection Survey** (id: 1, text): A brief introduction to the survey, providing context and purpose.
* **Finance Experience** (id: 2, number): The candidate's level of finance experience, where 'Yes' is 5 or more years of experience and 'No' is less than 5 years.
* **Finance Communication** (id: 3, select_one): The candidate's ability to communicate complex finance concepts, where 'Yes' indicates they can and 'No' indicates they cannot.
* **Finance Leadership** (id: 4, select_multiple): The candidate's leadership skills, where 'Yes' indicates they have led teams and 'No' indicates they have not.
* **Finance Problem Solving** (id: 5, select_one): The candidate's ability to solve finance-related problems, where 'Yes' indicates they can and 'No' indicates they cannot.
* **Finance Decision Making** (id: 6, select_one): The candidate's ability to make finance-related decisions, where 'Yes' indicates they can and 'No' indicates they cannot.
* **Finance Team Working** (id: 7, select_multiple): The candidate's ability to work with a team, where 'Yes' indicates they can and 'No' indicates they cannot.
* **Finance Time Management** (id: 8, select_multiple): The candidate's ability to manage time effectively in finance-related tasks, where 'Yes' indicates they can and 'No' indicates they cannot.
* **Finance Adaptability** (id: 9, select_one): The candidate's ability to adapt to new finance-related situations, where 'Yes' indicates they can and 'No' indicates they cannot.
* **Finance Financial Reporting** (id: 10, select_multiple): The candidate's ability to create and present financial reports, where 'Yes' indicates they can and 'No' indicates they cannot.
* **Finance Presentation** (id: 11, select_one): The candidate's ability to present financial information effectively, where 'Yes' indicates they can and 'No' indicates they cannot.
* **Finance Communication Skills** (id: 12, select_multiple): The candidate's communication skills relevant to finance, where 'Yes' indicates they possess them and 'No' indicates they do not.
* **Finance Executive Selection** (id: 13, text): Optional text to provide additional feedback or comments about the candidate's executive skills.
* **Feedback Comments** (id: 14, text): Optional text to provide feedback or comments about the candidate's finance-related experience or skills.
* **Final Recommendation** (id: 15, select_multiple): The final decision or recommendation, where 'Yes' indicates they are recommended and 'No' indicates they are not.
