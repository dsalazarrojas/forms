# real_time_control_consultant_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather personal and work-related information from individuals for real-time consulting purposes. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and honestly, providing accurate information about yourself and your work environment.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Review your information before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information** (`personal_info`, `text`, optional): Please provide a brief description of yourself.
* **Work Scheduling** (`work_scheduling`, `text`, optional): Share your work schedule and availability.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, `text`, optional): Describe your work environment, including any notable features or requirements.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, optional): Indicate your availability for consulting sessions.
* **Note** (`note`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your work or availability.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, `text`, optional): Provide the location where you work.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Share your contact details, such as email or phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Add any further details that may be helpful for the consultant to know. 

## Tips
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, contact the consultant directly. 

Note: The "real_time_control_consultant_survey" title was used for the purpose of this form as specified in the inputSchema.yaml file.
