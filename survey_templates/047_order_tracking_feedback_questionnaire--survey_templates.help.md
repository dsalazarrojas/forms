# Order Tracking Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Tracking Feedback Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from customers on their order tracking experiences. This feedback will help us understand how we are doing in terms of customer satisfaction and order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Order Id** in the "Order Id" field. This is a required field.
3. Select how satisfied you are with your order in the "Feedback" field. Choose from the options Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
4. (Optional) If you would like to select multiple reasons why your order was cancelled, please select from the "Order Status" field. Select all that apply.
5. (Optional) If your order was cancelled, please enter the reason for cancellation in the "Reason for Cancel" field.
6. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
7. (Optional) Enter the date and time of your order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
8. (Optional) If you have any additional feedback, please enter it in the "Additional Feedback" field.
9. (Optional) If you would like to provide a contact number, please enter it in the "Contact Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Order Id** (text, required): Enter the order number that you are providing feedback on.
* **Feedback** (select_one, optional): Select how satisfied you are with your order.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are extremely happy with your order.
	+ Satisfied: You are generally happy with your order.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about your order.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are not happy with your order.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You are extremely unhappy with your order.
* **Order Status** (select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply.
	+ Ordered: Your order was successfully placed.
	+ Shipped: Your order has been shipped.
	+ Delivered: Your order has been delivered.
	+ Cancelled: Your order was cancelled.
* **Reason for Cancel** (text, optional): Enter the reason for cancellation.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Order Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your order.
* **Order Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of your order.
* **Additional Feedback** (text, optional): Enter any additional feedback you have about your order.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter a contact number if you would like to be reached out about your order.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form for each order you would like to give feedback on separately.
* Only submit this form if you have an actual order to provide feedback on. If you have already given feedback on this order, do not submit this form again.
