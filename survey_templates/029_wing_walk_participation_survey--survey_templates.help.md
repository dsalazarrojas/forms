# Wing Walk Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from participants about their experience at the Wing Walk event, focusing on their overall satisfaction and perception of safety measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your event details, such as the event name, date, and any relevant information about your participation.
2. Indicate your perceived level of safety during the event (Perceived Safety).
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event (Overall Satisfaction).
4. Select any suggestions for improvement you may have (Suggestions).
5. Choose which safety measures you found effective (Event Safety Measures).
6. Indicate your understanding and adherence to safety guidelines during the event (Participant Safety).
7. Rate how much you enjoyed the event (Event Satisfaction).
8. Share any additional comments or feedback (Comments).
9. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number (Email and Phone Number).
10. Express your satisfaction with the overall experience (Submission).
11. Let us know if you're interested in being followed up with regarding this survey (Follow up).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (event_details, text, required: false)
	+ Please provide a brief description of your participation in the Wing Walk event, including any relevant details.
* **Perceived Safety** (perceived_safety, number, required: false)
	+ Rate your level of safety during the event on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not safe at all" and 5 is "Very safe".
* **Overall Satisfaction** (overall_satisfaction, number, required: false)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".
* **Suggestions** (suggestions, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select any suggestions for improvement you may have for future events.
* **Event Safety Measures** (event_safety_measures, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose which safety measures you found effective during the event.
* **Participant Safety** (participant_safety, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Indicate which safety guidelines you followed during the event.
* **Event Satisfaction** (event_satisfaction, number, required: false)
	+ This field has been removed as it was deemed not necessary.
* **Comments** (comments, note, required: false)
	+ Share any additional comments or feedback you have about the event.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false)
	+ Provide your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required: false)
	+ Provide your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Submission** (submission, select_one, required: false)
	+ Express your satisfaction with the overall experience.
* **Follow up** (follow_up, select_one, required: false)
	+ Let us know if you're interested in being followed up with regarding this survey.
