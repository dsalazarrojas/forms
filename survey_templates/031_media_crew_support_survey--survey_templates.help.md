# media_crew_support_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The media_crew_support_survey_form is designed to collect information from media crews about their support requests. It aims to gather details about the issue they are experiencing, its urgency, and their expected resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing a brief description of your issue in the **Introduction** field.
2.  Fill in your **Name** and **Email** fields for us to contact you for further information or resolution updates.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the issue in the **Issue** field.
4.  Select the correct **Issue Type** from the dropdown: either "Problem" or "Feature".
5.  Indicate the level of urgency using the **Urgency** dropdown: Low, Medium, or High.
6.  Let us know if you need to attach any files related to your issue in the **Files** field.
7.  If you have any additional comments, provide them in the **Comment** field.
8.  Share your **Feedback** if you have any suggestions or ideas.
9.  If you have any further information to support your request, enter it in the **Support Request** field.
10. Attach any supporting documents or files using the **Attachment** field.
11. Set your desired **Priority** using the dropdown: Low, Medium, or High.
12. Enter the expected **Deadline** for resolution.
13. Finally, provide the expected outcome or resolution of your request in the **Resolution** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your name to allow us to contact you about your request.
*   **Email** (`id: 3`): Enter your email address to contact you with further information or resolution updates.
*   **Department** (`id: 4`): Identify the department you are submitting the request from.
*   **Issue** (`id: 5`): Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
*   **Issue Type** (`id: 6`): Select "Problem" or "Feature" to help us understand your issue.
*   **Urgency** (`id: 7`): Indicate the level of urgency using the dropdown: Low, Medium, or High.
*   **Files** (`id: 8`): Attach any relevant files to support your request.
*   **Comment** (`id: 9`): Share any additional comments or details.
*   **Feedback** (`id: 10`): Provide any feedback or suggestions you have.
*   **Support Request** (`id: 11`): Enter any further information to support your request.
*   **Attachment** (`id: 12`): Attach supporting documents or files.
*   **Priority** (`id: 13`): Set your desired priority using the dropdown: Low, Medium, or High.
*   **Deadline** (`id: 14`): Enter the expected deadline for resolution.
*   **Resolution** (`id: 15`): Provide the expected outcome or resolution of your request.
