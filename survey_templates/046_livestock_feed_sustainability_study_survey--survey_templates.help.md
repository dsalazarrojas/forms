# Livestock Feed Sustainability Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather information about your practices and challenges related to sustainable feed production and management. Your input will help us better understand the current state of the industry and support future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the corresponding options for each field. You can choose multiple options when prompted, so feel free to select all that apply. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, optional): This field is intended to provide a brief overview of the survey's purpose and your expected response time. You can skip this section if you are short on time.
* **Feed Practices** (`feed_practices`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the feed practices you currently use or have used on your farm, such as Wheat, Corn, Soybean, Alfalfa, Oats, Barley, Sorghum, or Other.
* **Sustainability Measures** (`sustainability_measures`, select_one, optional): Choose one or more measures you have implemented or plan to implement to increase sustainability, such as implementing integrated farm management practices, using certified sustainable feed sources, reducing manure management, or increasing crop rotations.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the challenges you face on a daily or weekly basis, such as managing climate change, water scarcity, labor shortages, disease management, or Other.
* **Demographic** (`demographic`, select_one, optional): Please select your gender, Male or Female.
* **Contact Information** (`contact`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you are available for contact, Yes or No.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are currently at your farm location, Yes or No.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are optional, so users are encouraged to fill out as much or as little information as they are comfortable with. If they need assistance or clarification, please contact us.
