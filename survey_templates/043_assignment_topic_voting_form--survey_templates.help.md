# Assignment Topic Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for students to vote on their preferred topics for assignments and projects. The form helps instructors understand the collective preferences of students, enabling them to tailor assignments to meet the needs and interests of their class. By submitting this form, you are providing valuable feedback that will help shape the assignment topics and projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name, student ID, and email address in the required fields.
2.  Select your preferred voting period from the given options.
3.  Choose up to three top-pick topics from the list of options provided. These topics should reflect your interests and areas of study.
4.  Briefly explain why you chose these topics in the text box below.
5.  If you have a suggested topic, provide it in the text box.
6.  Justify why you think your suggested topic should be added to the course.
7.  Indicate your level of experience with the topics offered.
8.  Specify what you hope to learn from the assignment.
9.  Select your preferred project type from the options.
10.  Indicate your preferred group size for the project.
11.  Choose your preferred timeline for the assignment.
12.  Select your preferred difficulty level for the assignment.
13.  Check if you are available for in-class presentations.
14.  If applicable, select the days you are available for presentations.
15.  Provide any additional comments or requests.
16.  Acknowledge that your vote helps shape the assignment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official university documents.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Enter your student ID number as it appears on your university registration documents.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for confirmation purposes.
*   **Course Name** (`course_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the course you are taking.
*   **Course Section** (`course_section`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific course section, enter it here.
*   **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your instructor for the course.
*   **Voting Period** (`voting_period`, `select_one`, required): Select the voting period for which you are voting. This is the period during which topics are selected.
*   **First Choice Topic** (`topic_preference_1`, `select_one`, required): Choose your top-pick topic from the list of options provided.
*   **Second Choice Topic** (`topic_preference_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose your second preferred topic from the list of options provided.
*   **Third Choice Topic** (`topic_preference_3`, `select_one`, optional): If you have a third preferred topic, choose it from the list of options provided.
*   **Why Did You Choose These Topics** (`preference_reason`, `text`, required): Briefly explain why you chose your preferred topics.
*   **Do You Have a Topic Suggestion** (`topic_suggestion`, `select_one`, required): If you have a suggested topic, answer "Yes" and provide a description in the text box below.
*   **Your Suggested Topic** (`suggested_topic`, `text`, optional): If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, describe your suggested topic.
*   **Why Should This Topic Be Added** (`suggested_topic_rationale`, `text`, optional): If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, justify why you think your suggested topic should be added.
*   **Your Experience Level with Topics** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your level of experience with the topics offered.
*   **What Do You Hope to Learn** (`learning_goals`, `text`, required): Specify what you hope to learn from the assignment.
*   **Preferred Project Type** (`project_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred project type.
*   **Preferred Group Size** (`group_size_preference`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your preferred group size.
*   **Preferred Timeline** (`timeline_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred timeline for the assignment.
*   **Preferred Difficulty Level** (`difficulty_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred difficulty level for the assignment.
*   **Available for In-Class Presentation** (`availability_for_presentation`, `select_one`, required): Check if you are available for in-class presentations.
*   **Which Days Work for Presentations** (`presentation_days`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you are available for presentations, select the days you are available.
*   **Additional Comments or Requests** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or requests.
*   **I Understand My Vote Helps Shape the Assignment** (`vote_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that your vote helps shape the assignment.
*   **Vote Date** (`vote_date`, `date`, required): The date on which you submitted your vote.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are needed to be filled out, while fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if not needed.
