# Warmup Activity Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Warmup Activity Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback on the warm-up activities conducted during events, meetings, or training sessions. This survey aims to gather insights from participants to improve the effectiveness of the warm-up activities and enhance the overall experience for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete all the required fields marked with an asterisk (*). The form is divided into sections with clear labels and instructions.
2. Select the correct answer or input for each field based on your experience with the warm-up activity.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please provide a comment or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warm-up Questions 1**: Answer the first set of questions that were asked during the warm-up activity. Please provide a clear and concise answer.
* **Warm-up Questions 2**: Answer the second set of questions that were asked during the warm-up activity. Be as specific and detailed as possible.
* **Warm-up Session**: *Required* Enter the title of the warm-up session that you participated in.
* **Participants**: *Optional* List the names and roles of the participants in the warm-up activity.
* **Feedback**: *Optional* Share any additional feedback or comments about the warm-up activity.
* **Date**: *Required* Enter the date when the warm-up activity took place.
* **Time**: *Optional* Enter the start and end time of the warm-up activity.
* **Warm-up Activity**: *Required* Enter a brief description of the warm-up activity.
* **Rating**: *Required* Rate your experience with the warm-up activity using the options "Good" or "Fair".
* **Warm-up Questions 3-17**: These fields are for additional questions that were asked during the warm-up activity. Answer each question as clearly and concisely as possible.

Please note that the form is designed to be flexible and accommodate any warm-up activities. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
