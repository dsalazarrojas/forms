# Event Food And Beverage Services Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Food And Beverage Services Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback on the food and beverage services provided at an event. This form helps event organizers evaluate the quality of these services and make informed decisions for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields (event_date, category, event_type).
2. If applicable, choose the correct options for food_services and venue.
3. Enter a rating for food and beverage rating and venue rating.
4. Provide a suggestion for food and beverage service improvements, if any.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Food and Beverage Services Questionnaire** (`event_food_and_beverage_services_questionnaire`, text): This field is the title of the form, please do not fill it out. It's only an informational heading.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one): Select the category of your event (e.g., wedding, conference, concert).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date): Enter the date of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Venue** (`venue`, text): Enter the name of the venue where the event took place.
* **Food Services** (`food_services`, select_multiple): Check all the services that were provided (e.g., catering, bar service, buffet).
* **Food and Beverage Rating** (`food_beverage_rating`, number): Rate the quality of food and beverage services on a scale of 1-10.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one): Select the type of event (e.g., corporate, public).
* **Venue Rating** (`venue_rating`, number): Rate the overall quality of the venue on a scale of 1-10.
* **Food and Beverage Service Suggestion** (`food_beverage_service_suggestion`, text): If there were any issues with food and beverage services, provide a suggestion for improvement here.
* **Overall Event Satisfaction** (`overall_event_satisfaction`, number): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10.

This form is designed to be completed by attendees of events that had food and beverage services provided. Please fill out the form carefully and honestly to help event organizers improve their services.
