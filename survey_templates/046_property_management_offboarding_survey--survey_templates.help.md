# Property Management Offboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Offboarding Survey is designed to gather feedback and information from tenants who are offboarding from a property managed by the Property Management department. This helps to ensure a smooth transition and gather essential details for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the relevant page for your offboarding experience.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required".
3. For optional fields, provide any additional information you deem relevant.
4. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Feedback** (`tenant_feedback`, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather your thoughts on the offboarding experience. Please provide any feedback, positive or negative, about your experience with the property.
* **Transition Comments** (`transition_comments`, text, required/optional): Share any comments or concerns you have about the offboarding process or your experience with the property.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, note, required/optional): Provide any relevant details about the property, such as its location, size, or any distinctive features.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the property manager who managed your property. This helps us understand who was in charge of your offboarding experience.
* **Tenant Email** (`tenant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email, which will be used to follow up on any additional information or clarification needed.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in thoroughly.
- If you have any concerns or questions, reach out to the Property Management department.
- If you've offboarded from multiple properties, please complete this form for each property separately.
