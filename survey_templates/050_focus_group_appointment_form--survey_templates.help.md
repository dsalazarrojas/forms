# Focus Group Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Focus Group Appointment Form is designed for users to input their availability and provide feedback. It's intended for users who have been invited to participate in a focus group session, and it helps facilitators schedule appointments with them at convenient times.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Scroll through the pages until you reach "Select Time Slots".
2. Check the boxes corresponding to the time slots you are available for.
	* You can select one or more time slots that suit you best.
3. Provide feedback on your availability in the text field "Provide Feedback" if you want to communicate anything to the facilitators.
4. Enter your email address in the field "User Email" so we can reach out to you for further details.
5. If you have a phone number, you can enter it in the field "User Phone" to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Time Slots** (`user_input_1`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the boxes corresponding to the time slots you are available for. Select one or more time slots that suit you best.
* **Provide Feedback** (`user_input_2`, `note`, optional): This field is for providing any additional information or feedback about your availability. If you have any questions or concerns, you can type them in this field.
* **User Email** (`user_input_3`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you for further details about the focus group session.
* **User Phone** (`user_input_4`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you for any last-minute updates or reminders.
