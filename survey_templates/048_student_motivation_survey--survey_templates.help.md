# student_motivation_survey--survey_templates - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help educators and administrators understand student motivation, strengths, weaknesses, and goals. It's an essential tool for identifying areas of support and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Select the options that best apply to your situation.
3. Be thorough in your answers, as they will help educators and administrators provide targeted support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro`, `note`, not required): This section serves as an introduction to the form and provides some background information.
* **What motivates you?** (`survey_type`, `select_one`, not required): This question helps identify what drives you to take action. Select the option that best describes your primary motivator.
* **Strengths** (`strengths`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose from the list of options what you feel are your areas of strength.
* **Weaknesses** (`weaknesses`, `select_multiple`, not required): Identify areas where you may need improvement or extra support.
* **Needs** (`need`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select what you require to achieve your goals or overcome obstacles.
* **Comments** (`comment`, `note`, not required): If you have any additional thoughts or comments, feel free to share them here.
* **Goal** (`question1`, `select_one`, not required): Do you have a goal that you are currently working towards? Select "Yes" or "No".
* **Obstacles** (`question2`, `select_multiple`, not required): If you have any obstacles preventing you from achieving your goal, select from the list.
* **Overcome obstacles** (`question3`, `select_multiple`, not required): What would you do to overcome the obstacles you've identified?
* **Plan for achieving goal** (`question4`, `select_one`, not required): Do you have a plan for achieving your goal? Select "Yes" or "No".
* **Seek help** (`question5`, `select_multiple`, not required): If you were unable to achieve your goal, who would you seek help from? Select from the list.
* **Action if unable to achieve goal** (`question6`, `select_multiple`, not required): What would you do if you were unable to achieve your goal? Select from the list.
