# teenage_life_music_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your music preferences and habits. It's completely optional and will not affect your participation in any future surveys.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your favorite music genre from the list provided.
2. Type in the name of a favorite artist.
3. Indicate how many hours of music you listen to per week.
4. Type in the name of a favorite album.
5. Type in the name of a favorite song.
6. Indicate if you have ever been to a concert.
7. Select if you buy music.
8. Choose how you usually listen to music (e.g., streaming, purchasing, etc.).
9. Select your age group.
10. Select your school level.
11. Indicate who influenced you to get into music (e.g., friends, family, online, etc.).
12. Select music-related activities that interest you (e.g., playing an instrument, creating playlists, etc.).
13. Indicate how important music is in your life.
14. Select how often you listen to music.
15. Select which musical instrument you play (if any).
16. Select your favorite music genre.
17. Select how often you purchase music.
18. Select which music streaming platforms you use.
19. Select the type of music you usually listen to (e.g., MP3, MP2, etc.).
20. Select where you usually listen to music (e.g., smartphone, laptop, etc.).
21. Indicate how important music is to you.
22. Select how much music influences your culture.
23. Indicate if you have attended a music festival.
24. Select music activities you participate in (e.g., listening, dancing, etc.).
25. Indicate how many hours of music you listen to per day.
