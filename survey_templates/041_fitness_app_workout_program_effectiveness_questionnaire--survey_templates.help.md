# Fitness App Workout Program Effectiveness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness App Workout Program Effectiveness Questionnaire is designed to gather your thoughts and feedback about your experience with our fitness program. This survey will help us understand your satisfaction, comfort, and adherence to the program. Your input will help us improve the program to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the answer that best describes your experience.
3. Provide additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" section if you have any thoughts or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program, using the scale: 1 - Very dissatisfied, 2 - Somewhat dissatisfied, 3 - Neutral, 4 - Somewhat satisfied, 5 - Very satisfied.
* **Program Outcomes** (`Program Outcomes`, text, required: false): Please describe any specific outcomes or results you've achieved through the program.
* **How often do you train?** (`How often do you train?`, select_one, required: false): Indicate how often you train using the scale: Less than once a week, 1-2 times a week, 3-4 times a week, 5 or more times a week.
* **Workout Duration** (`Workout Duration`, number, required: false): Enter the average duration of your workout sessions.
* **How intense is your workout?** (`How intense is your workout?`, select_one, required: false): Rate the intensity of your workout using the scale: Low, Medium, High.
* **Training Location** (`Training Location`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the locations where you train, using the options: Home, Gym, Outdoor, Other (please specify).
* **Training Frequency outside of Work** (`Training Frequency outside of Work`, number, required: false): Enter how often you train outside of work hours.
* **Program Satisfaction** (`Program Satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the program, using the scale: Very dissatisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat satisfied, Very satisfied.
* **Program Outcome Expectation** (`Program Outcome Expectation`, select_one, required: false): Choose the outcome you expect from the program, using the options: Improve physical appearance, Improve overall health, Improve mental health, Other (please specify).
* **Physical Comfort** (`Physical Comfort`, select_one, required: false): Rate your physical comfort level, using the scale: Very uncomfortable, Somewhat uncomfortable, Neutral, Somewhat comfortable, Very comfortable.
* **Mental Comfort** (`Mental Comfort`, select_one, required: false): Rate your mental comfort level, using the scale: Very uncomfortable, Somewhat uncomfortable, Neutral, Somewhat comfortable, Very comfortable.
* **How many weekdays do you train?** (`How many weekdays do you train?`, number, required: false): Enter how many weekdays you train.
* **How many weekends do you train?** (`How many weekends do you train?`, number, required: false): Enter how many weekends you train.
* **Program Outcome Importance** (`Program Outcome Importance`, select_one, required: false): Rate the importance of the program outcomes, using the scale: Very low, Somewhat low, Neutral, Somewhat high, Very high.
* **Type of Training** (`Type of Training`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of training you perform, using the options: Weightlifting, Cardio, Flexibility, Core strength, Balance and flexibility.
* **Has your training goal been achieved?** (`Has your training goal been achieved?`, select_one, required: false): Rate your progress toward your training goal, using the scale: Very dissatisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat satisfied, Very satisfied.
* **How likely are you to stick to the program?** (`How likely are you to stick to the program?`, select_one, required: false): Rate your likelihood of sticking to the program, using the scale: Very unlikely, Somewhat unlikely, Neutral, Somewhat likely, Very likely.
* **Program Satisfaction Frequency** (`Program Satisfaction Frequency`, select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the program, using the scale: Very dissatisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat satisfied, Very satisfied.
* **Program Outcome Frequency** (`Program Outcome Frequency`, select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the program outcomes, using the scale: Very dissatisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat satisfied, Very satisfied.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional thoughts or feedback about the program.
* **Mornings** (`Mornings`, number, required: false): Enter how often you train in the morning.
* **Evenings** (`Evenings`, number, required: false): Enter how often you train in the evening.
* **Days** (`Days`, number, required: false): Enter how often you train on weekdays.
* **Sundays** (`Sundays`, number, required: false): Enter how often you train on Sundays.

Note: Please answer honestly, and remember that your feedback is invaluable to us in improving the program.
