# Retail Customer Service Effectiveness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Retail Customer Service Effectiveness Assessment form is designed to evaluate the quality of service provided by a retail store. This assessment helps customers, employees, or management to identify areas of excellence and areas for improvement in customer service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions honestly based on your recent visit to the store.
2. Please note that all answers are optional, but we encourage you to provide as much information as possible to help us improve our service.
3. Use the provided options as a guide, and feel free to elaborate if you have a specific experience or comment about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Location** (<code>store_location</code>): Enter the address of the store you visited.

* **Service Quality** (<code>service_quality</code>): Rate the quality of service provided by the store staff. Choose from:
