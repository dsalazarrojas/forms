# Pediatric Health Awareness Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect participant information and their responses to a set of quiz questions. The collected data will help us better understand pediatric health awareness and provide valuable insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information section, including any relevant details about the participant.
2. Answer the quiz questions in the space provided.
3. Provide submission information, including your name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
4. Enter the submission date and time.
5. Review all fields carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information (participant_info)

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, optional): This field allows you to provide any relevant details about the participant.
* **Hint: Please include any relevant information about the participant, such as age, gender, or medical conditions.**

### Quiz Questions (quiz_questions)

* **Quiz Questions** (`quiz_questions`, `text`, optional): This field is for answering the quiz questions provided. Please answer each question as accurately as possible.
* **Hint: Please review the questions carefully and answer each one before proceeding.**

### Quiz Answers (quiz_answers)

* **Quiz Answers** (`quiz_answers`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering your answers to the quiz questions.
* **Hint: Please be as accurate as possible when providing your answers.**

### Submission Information (submission_info)

* **Submission Information** (`submission_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing any additional information about the submission.
* **Hint: Please include any relevant details about the submission, such as any medical conditions or medications.**

### Submission Date (submission_date)

* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, optional): This field is for selecting the date of the submission.
* **Hint: Please select the date on which the submission is made.**

### Submission Time (submission_time)

* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `text`, optional): This field is for selecting the submission time.
* **Hint: Please select the time of the submission.**

### Submission Email (submission_email)

* **Submission Email** (`submission_email`, `email`, optional): This field is for entering the email address associated with the submission.
* **Hint: Please enter a valid email address.**

### Submission Phone (submission_phone)

* **Submission Phone** (`submission_phone`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering the phone number associated with the submission.
* **Hint: Please enter a valid phone number.**
