# Product Feature Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Feature Voting Form is a tool for users to provide feedback on which product features they would like to see included in future products or updates. By filling out this form, you are helping us to understand what features are most important to you and our community, and we will use this information to prioritize our development roadmap.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please make sure you are a relevant user and have a genuine interest in providing feedback.
2.  Fill out the form completely, but do not submit duplicate responses.
3.  Select the features you are most interested in and rate your preferences accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief and descriptive title for your feature voting feedback.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your desired feature. This will help us better understand your idea.
* **Category Selection** (`category_select`, select multiple, optional): Select all the categories that your feature belongs to. Please choose all relevant categories.
* **Feature Selection** (`feature_select`, select multiple, optional): Select the features you are interested in. You can choose all the features that apply to you.
* **Feature Rating** (`feature_rating`, number, optional): Rate your preference for each selected feature from 1-10. A higher rating indicates a stronger desire.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, none, optional): This is not a field to fill out. Please click the submit button to finalize your vote.

## Tips
*   Please be accurate and honest in your feedback.
*   You may select all categories and features that apply to you, but do not over-select or select non-relevant items.
*   Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help shape the direction of our product development roadmap.
