# Compensation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The compensation survey is designed to collect feedback on various aspects of employee compensation, including base salary, bonuses, benefits, paid time off, and overall job satisfaction. This survey aims to gather insights from employees on their perceptions and experiences with their compensation, helping HR professionals to identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the relevant department you belong to from the list provided.
2. Choose your length of employment with the organization.
3. Select your employment type (full-time, part-time, etc.).
4. Choose your current job level within the organization.
5. Rate your satisfaction with your base salary.
6. Rate your salary's competitiveness compared to similar roles.
7. Rate your perception of pay equity across the organization.
8. Select how often you receive raises.
9. Rate your satisfaction with raise amounts.
10. Indicate if you are bonus eligible and rate your satisfaction with the bonus program.
11. Rate your health benefits.
12. Rate your retirement benefits.
13. Choose your level of satisfaction with paid time off.
14. Indicate your understanding of benefits information.
15. Rate your perception of compensation communication.
16. Rate how valued you feel through your compensation.
17. Indicate your level of awareness about your market value.
18. Rate your view on pay transparency.
19. Rate the overall value of the total rewards package.
20. Select the most important factor for you in compensation.
21. Indicate if you have considered leaving for better pay in the past year.
22. Indicate if you would recommend this employer based on compensation.
23. Select any improvements needed in compensation.
24. Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department**: Your current department. This helps us understand which areas of the organization need improvement.
* **Employee ID**: Optional - for tracking purposes only. Please do not worry about filling this out if you don't have a company-issued ID.
* **Department**: Select your current department (e.g., Human Resources, Finance, Marketing).
* **Length of Employment**: Choose your length of employment with the organization.
* **Employment Type**: Select your current employment type (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract, etc.).
* **Job Level**: Choose your current job level (e.g., Entry Level, Junior, etc.).
* **Satisfaction with Base Salary**: Rate your satisfaction with your base salary. This will help us understand how well your base salary meets your expectations.
* **Salary Competitiveness**: Rate your salary's competitiveness compared to similar roles.
* **Pay Equity**: Rate your perception of pay equity across the organization.
* **Raise Frequency**: Select how often you receive raises.
* **Raise Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with raise amounts.
* **Bonus Eligibility**: Indicate if you are bonus eligible. If you are not sure, please answer "Not sure".
* **Bonus Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the bonus program. Please note that this question only applies if you are bonus eligible.
* **Health Benefits**: Rate your satisfaction with health benefits.
* **Retirement Benefits**: Rate your satisfaction with retirement benefits.
* **PTO Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with paid time off.
* **Benefits Understanding**: Indicate your understanding of benefits information. This will help us understand how clear the information is.
* **Compensation Communication**: Rate your perception of compensation communication.
* **Feeling Valued**: Rate how valued you feel through your compensation. This will help us understand your overall worth perception.
* **Market Awareness**: Indicate your level of awareness about your market value.
* **Pay Transparency**: Rate your view on pay transparency.
* **Total Rewards Value**: Rate the overall value of the total rewards package.
* **Most Important Factor**: Select the most important factor for you in compensation.
* **Consider Leaving**: Indicate if you have considered leaving for better pay in the past year.
* **Recommend Employer**: Indicate if you would recommend this employer based on compensation.
* **Improvements Needed**: Select any improvements needed in compensation. Please choose all that apply.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback.
