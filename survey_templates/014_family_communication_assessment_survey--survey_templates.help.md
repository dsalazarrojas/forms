# Family Communication Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Communication Assessment Survey is a tool used to assess the level of communication and conflict resolution within a family member's relationship. This survey helps identify areas of improvement and provides a better understanding of the relationship dynamics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each question carefully and choose the best answer that reflects your relationship with your family member.
2. Select the corresponding option that best describes your experience.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions (marked as "required") for accurate results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Communication Assessment Survey**: This is the title of the survey, and it is not a question to be answered.
* **How would you describe your family member’s communication level?** (id: 2): Select the option that best describes how often your family member communicates with you, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **Does your family member provide emotional support?** (id: 3): Choose "True" if your family member provides emotional support, such as comfort, guidance, or reassurance. Choose "False" if they do not provide emotional support.
* **Conflict Level** (id: 4): Select the option that best describes how often you get into arguments or conflicts with your family member, such as "0-1 time in a month", "2-3 times in a month", or "4 or more times in a month".
* **Communication skills** (id: 5): Choose the option that best describes your family member's communication skills, such as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Family Dynamics** (id: 6): Select the option that best describes your family member's relationship dynamics, such as "Cooperative", "Competitive", "Independent", or "Authoritative".
* **Conflict Resolution** (id: 7): Choose the option that best describes how you and your family member resolve conflicts, such as "Collaborative", "Individual", "Avoiding", or "Frustrating".
* **Emotional Maturity** (id: 8): Select the option that best describes your family member's emotional maturity, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **How often do you get into conflicts with your family member?** (id: 9): Select the option that best describes how often you get into conflicts with your family member, such as "0-1 time in a month", "2-3 times in a month", or "4 or more times in a month".
* **Family Participation** (id: 10): Choose the option that best describes your family member's level of participation, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low". This is a required question.
* **Support Measurement** (id: 11): Select the options that best describe the level of emotional and practical support your family member provides, such as "High", "Medium", "Low" (this is a select_multiple type, so you may choose one or more options).

## Tips
* Answer honestly and accurately to ensure reliable results.
* Complete the form in a quiet and private environment to minimize distractions.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure you have answered all required questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the administrator for assistance.
