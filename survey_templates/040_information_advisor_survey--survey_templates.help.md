# information_advisor_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users who have interacted with an advisor, in order to improve the quality of the service provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, providing as much information as possible.
2. For each field, make sure to enter the required information, which is denoted by a (*) symbol next to the label.
3. If a field is optional, feel free to enter any additional information you feel is relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the purpose of our organization?** (`title`, `text`, optional): This field is not directly applicable to this form, as it is a general question meant for the organization's description, not the user's experience. However, it is included here for consistency with the form's purpose.
* **What type of interaction were you having with the advisor?** (`interaction_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the options that best describe the type of interaction you had with the advisor. Select all that apply.
* **What is your satisfaction with the service provided by the advisor?** (`satisfaction`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **Can you provide any additional feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, optional): Use this field to add any comments, suggestions, or concerns you may have about the service provided.
* **How satisfied were you with the interaction?** (`interaction_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the interaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **How would you rate the quality of the interaction?** (`interaction_quality`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the option that best describes your rating of the interaction's quality. Select one option.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted for follow-up or further communication.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted by phone.
* **Date of interaction** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you had the interaction with the advisor.
* **Time of interaction** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you had the interaction with the advisor.
* **Advisor name** (`advisor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the advisor you interacted with.
* **Team name** (`team_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the team that provided the service.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, `text`, optional): Enter the name or identifier of the person or team assigned to you for the interaction.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, `text`, optional): Enter your name or identifier for submission record keeping.
* **Submitted at** (`submitted_at`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **How do you rate your interaction with the advisor?** (`interaction_type_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your rating of the interaction type.
* **How do you rate the quality of the interaction?** (`interaction_quality_2`, `number`, optional): Rate the quality of the interaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **How satisfied were you with the service provided by the advisor?** (`interaction_satisfaction_2`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the service on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **Can you provide any additional feedback?** (`feedback_2`, `note`, optional): Use this field to add any comments, suggestions, or concerns you may have about the service provided.
* **Phone number of interaction** (`phone_number_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the interaction.
* **Date of interaction 2** (`date_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the second interaction.
* **Time of interaction 2** (`time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the second interaction.
* **Advisor name 2** (`advisor_name_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the advisor of the second interaction.
* **Team name 2** (`team_name_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the team that provided the service for the second interaction.
* **Assigned to 2** (`assigned_to_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name or identifier of the person or team assigned to you for the second interaction.
* **Submitted by 2** (`submitted_by_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your name or identifier for submission record keeping.
* **Submitted at 2** (`submitted_at_2`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you are submitting this form for the second interaction.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
- If you have any additional feedback, please use the "Can you provide any additional feedback" field.
- If you have any questions or need help, please contact us at [support email].
- We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our services.
