# parent_feedback_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "parent_feedback_contact_form" is a survey template used to gather feedback from parents or guardians of students. This form is designed to collect relevant information from parents to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please provide the required information in the corresponding fields. You can find detailed explanations for each field below.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked as (required), before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Feedback** (`parent_feedback`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide any feedback or comments about your experience with us.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to help us contact you in case we need more information.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Provide your contact number to allow us to contact you easily.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): If you are associated with a school, please enter the name of the school.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, number, required: false): If applicable, please enter your grade level or the grade level of your child.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: true): Choose how you would like to submit your feedback:
	+ 1: Submit with comments
	+ 2: Submit without comments
	+ 3: Do not submit
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): If you have additional comments, please provide them here.
* **Rating (1-5)** (`rating_score`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience with us (1-5).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): This field will be populated automatically when you submit the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): This field will also be populated automatically when you submit the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your input carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
