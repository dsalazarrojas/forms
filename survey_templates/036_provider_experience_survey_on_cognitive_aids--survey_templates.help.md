# Provider Experience Survey On Cognitive Aids - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed, open-ended responses from providers about their experiences with cognitive aids. The responses will be used to gain a better understanding of how these aids are being used in practice and identify areas for improvement or further development.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each page carefully before starting to fill it out.
2.  Type your responses in the text fields provided, being as detailed and clear as possible.
3.  Make sure to complete all pages, as they are part of the survey.
4.  If you encounter any issues or have questions, you can contact support at form@gic.mx.
