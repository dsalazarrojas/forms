# Conference Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from conference attendees on their experience at the conference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the first two fields.
2. Rate the overall conference in the "Overall event rating" field.
3. Provide feedback on the session relevance, organization and logistics, value for money, and networking value.
4. Share your comments about the speakers and topics.
5. Optionally, provide suggestions for next year's conference.
6. If you wish to receive follow-up emails from the conference organizers, check the "Consent to follow up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Contact email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Overall event rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the overall conference using one of the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Session relevance** (`session_relevance`, select_one, required): Feedback on the relevance of the sessions to your needs:
	+ Very relevant
	+ Somewhat relevant
	+ Not relevant
* **Organization and logistics** (`organization_rating`, select_one, required): Feedback on the conference's organization and logistics:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Value for money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, optional): Feedback on the value of the conference for the price:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor (leave blank if not applicable)
* **Networking value** (`networking_value`, select_one, optional): Feedback on the quality of networking opportunities:
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low (leave blank if not applicable)
* **Speaker feedback** (`speaker_feedback`, text, optional): Share your comments about the speakers and topics:
* **Suggestions for next year** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide suggestions for next year's conference:
* **Consent to follow up** (`follow_up_consent`, select_one, optional): Check this box if you consent to receive follow-up emails from the conference organizers:
	+ True
	+ False
