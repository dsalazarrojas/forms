# Student Concentration Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help educators, researchers, or anyone interested in understanding the impact of concentration on students' learning experience. It gathers information about the focus areas, classroom distractions, study habits, and other factors that affect students' concentration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all the questions according to your experience.
2. For each question, select the most relevant option from the list provided.
3. Provide detailed descriptions for the "note" fields where required.
4. Do not leave any field blank, as it is essential to provide accurate and comprehensive data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Focus Areas
* **Focus Areas** (`focus_areas`, `text`, required): Describe the areas where you usually focus your attention in class. Please provide a detailed account of the topics or subjects you find most engaging and relevant.

### Classroom Distractions
* **Classroom Distractions** (`classroom_distractions`, `text`, required): Describe the distractions you usually encounter in the classroom, such as noise, peers' conversations, or other factors that affect your concentration.

### Study Habits
* **Study Habits** (`study_habits`, `note`, optional): Describe your regular study habits, such as the time of day you study, the amount of time you spend studying, or any other relevant information about your study routine.

### Student Focus
* **Student Focus** (`student_focus`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that describe your focus in the classroom. You can choose more than one option if applicable:
	+ Focus on one task
	+ Focus on multiple tasks
	+ Distracted

### Classroom Environment
* **Classroom Environment** (`classroom_environment`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes the classroom environment where you usually sit or work. You can choose one option:
	+ Quiet
	+ Loud
	+ Both
	+ Other

### Time Management
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your time management in the classroom. You can choose one option:
	+ On time
	+ Late
	+ Both
	+ Other

### Student Attention
* **Student Attention** (`student_attention`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that describe where you focus your attention in the classroom. You can choose more than one option:
	+ Focus on lectures
	+ Focus on notes
	+ Focus on reading
	+ Focus on other
	+ Distracted

### Study Spaces
* **Study Spaces** (`study_spaces`, `note`, optional): Describe the physical space where you usually study, such as a designated study room, a library, or a specific area in your home.

### Time Management
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your time management in the classroom. You can choose one option:
	+ On time
	+ Late
	+ Both
	+ Other

### Focus on Tasks
* **Focus on Tasks** (`focus_on_tasks`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes how you focus on tasks in the classroom. You can choose one option:
	+ Focus on one task
	+ Focus on multiple tasks

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any question, please consult with the survey administrator or provide additional information in the "notes" field.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions for improving the survey, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
