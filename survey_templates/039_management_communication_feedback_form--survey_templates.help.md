# Management Communication Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from managers on the communication effectiveness of their interactions with team members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select a communication channel**: Choose how you typically communicate with your team member (e.g., email, phone call, meeting, etc.). This will help us understand the context of your interaction.
2. **Rate the communication effectiveness**: On a scale of 1-3, rate how effective you found the communication to be, where 1 is "Not Very Effective" and 3 is "Highly Effective".
3. **Provide positive comments**: Share any positive comments or feedback you received from the team member regarding the communication.
4. **Identify areas for improvement**: Describe any areas where you felt the communication could be improved.
5. **Respond to feedback**: If you received any feedback from the team member, please respond to it here.
6. **Date and time of last interaction**: Provide the date and time of your last interaction with the team member.
7. **Additional comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Communication Channel**: This field asks you to select the way you typically communicate with your team member.
	+ Example: Select "Email" if you usually communicate via email.
* **Communication Effectiveness**: This field asks you to rate how effective you found the communication to be, on a scale of 1-3.
	+ 1 (Not Very Effective): The communication was not helpful or clear.
	+ 2 (Somewhat Effective): The communication was somewhat clear and helpful, but could be improved.
	+ 3 (Highly Effective): The communication was clear, concise, and helpful.
* **Positive Comments**: This field is for any positive feedback or comments you received from the team member.
	+ Example: "They were very receptive to my feedback and ideas."
* **Areas for Improvement**: This field is for any areas where you felt the communication could be improved.
	+ Example: "I felt that the communication could be improved by being more specific and detailed."
* **Manager Response**: If you received any feedback from the team member, please respond to it here.
	+ Example: "I acknowledged their concerns and provided additional information to clarify the situation."
* **Date of Last Interaction**: This field asks you to provide the date of your last interaction with the team member.
* **Time of Last Interaction**: This field asks you to provide the time of your last interaction with the team member.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Tips

* Please complete the form for each interaction with the team member.
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback and comments.
* Remember to keep the feedback objective and focused on the communication process, not on personal opinions or feelings.
