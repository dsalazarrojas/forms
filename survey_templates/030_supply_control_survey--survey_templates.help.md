# Supply Control Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about stock levels, ordering performance, and other relevant information to help organizations optimize their supply chain management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing each page of the form carefully.
2. For each question, provide the requested information as accurately as possible.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stock Level** (`stock_level`, number, required: false): This field is asking for the current stock level of the item or product. Please enter the quantity in stock.
* **Stockout** (`stockout`, text, required: false): Describe the reasons for stockouts or shortages.
* **Order Performance** (`ordering_performance`, select_one, required: false):
	+ **Low**: Indicate if the ordering performance is low.
	+ **Medium**: Indicate if the ordering performance is medium.
	+ **High**: Indicate if the ordering performance is high.
* **Frequency Of Ordering** (`ordering_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ **Daily**: Indicate if orders are received daily.
	+ **Weekly**: Indicate if orders are received weekly.
	+ **Monthly**: Indicate if orders are received monthly.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Enter the delivery time for the product or item.
* **Order Amount** (`order_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the total order amount for the product or item.
* **Stock out Reason** (`stock_out_reason`, text, required: false): Describe the reasons for stockouts or shortages.
* **Stock Level Frequency** (`stock_level_frequency`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the stock level was last checked.

## Tips
- Take your time and answer each question carefully to ensure accurate results.
- If you need help with any of the questions, please refer back to the form's purpose and instructions.
- Remember to review your answers before submitting the form.
