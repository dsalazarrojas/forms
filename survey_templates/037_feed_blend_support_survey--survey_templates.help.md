# feed_blend_support_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their support experience with Feed Blend. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose one response for each question based on your experience with Feed Blend.
2. For questions with multiple options, you can select more than one answer if applicable.
3. Enter your name and email or phone number so we can contact you for follow-up.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Your Message" field.
5. Choose when you'd like to be followed up with a response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with our service** (`How satisfied are you with our service`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with our service, on a scale from very satisfied to very dissatisfied.
* **Have you experienced any common issues with Feed Blend** (`Have you experienced any common issues with Feed Blend`, text, required): Describe any issues you've encountered while using Feed Blend.
* **How would you rate your support experience with us** (`How would you rate your support experience with us`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of excellent, good, average, or poor to describe your support experience.
* **What would you like to see improved in our support** (`What would you like to see improved in our support`, note, required): Type any suggestions or comments about how we can improve our support services.
* **How would you like to be contacted** (`How would you like to be contacted`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted, such as email, phone, or chat.
* **Your email** (`Your email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you.
* **Your phone number** (`Your phone number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Would you like to be followed up with a response** (`Would you like to be followed up with a response`, select_one, required): Choose yes or no if you'd like us to follow up with a response.
* **When would you like to be followed up with a response** (`When would you like to be followed up with a response`, select_multiple, required): Choose when you'd like to be followed up with a response, such as immediately, within a few hours, or within a week.
* **Your Name** (`Your Name`, text, required): Enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Your Message** (`Your Message`, text, required): Type any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to share with us.
