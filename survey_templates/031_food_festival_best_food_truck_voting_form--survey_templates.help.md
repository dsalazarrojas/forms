# food_festival_best_food_truck_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide explains how to complete the Best Food Truck Voting Form and what each field means.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has 11 fields that need to be filled out. Please read through each field carefully and answer accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Best Food Truck Voting Form** (`1`, `text`, `false`): This is the title of the form.
* **Food Truck Name** (`2`, `text`, `false`): Enter the name of the food truck that served the best dish.
* **Favorite Dish** (`3`, `text`, `false`): Describe your favorite dish from the food truck.
* **Rating** (`4`, `number`, `false`): Rate the dish from 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Comments** (`5`, `note`, `false`): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Would Recommend?** (`6`, `select_one`, `false`): Answer 'Yes' or 'No', indicating whether you would recommend the food truck.
* **Rating Time** (`7`, `number`, `false`): Rate how long you had to wait in line, from 1-5, with 1 being the shortest and 5 being the longest.
* **Food Quality** (`8`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Rate the food quality with Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Staff Quality** (`9`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Rate the quality of the staff with Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Value for Money** (`10`, `select_one`, `false`): Answer 'Yes' or 'No', indicating whether the price was worth it.
* **Overall Experience** (`11`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Rate your overall experience with Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the food truck?** (`12`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Rate the food truck with Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Take your time to answer each question carefully.
* Your feedback is valuable to help us improve the food festival.
