# return_to_church_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the church understand the reasons for your return and gather information on your last visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the reasons for leaving from the list, if applicable.
3. Provide the date and time of your last attended church, if you remember.
4. Add any comments or additional information in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **return to church survey** (return to church survey, text, required/optional): A field for you to write any comments or answers about why you've returned to the church.
* **reason for leaving** (reason for leaving, select multiple, required/optional): Select all the reasons that apply to why you returned to the church. You can select more than one option.
	+ Returned to church due to the love of God's work.
	+ Returned to church due to a new opportunity that presented itself.
	+ Returned to church to seek God's forgiveness.
	+ Returned due to a personal issue.
	+ Returned due to a family issue.
	+ Returned due to a change of heart.
	+ Returned due to health issues.
	+ Returned due to other reasons not listed. Please specify.
	+ Returned due to other (please specify)
* **time since last attended church** (time since last attended church, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your last visit.
* **time of last attended church** (time of last attended church, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your last visit.
* **hour of last attended church** (hour of last attended church, text, required/optional): Enter the hour of your last visit.
* **minute of last attended church** (minute of last attended church, text, required/optional): Enter the minute of your last visit.
* **AM/PM of last attended church** (AM/PM of last attended church, select one, required/optional): Select AM or PM for your last visit.
* **date of last attended church** (date of last attended church, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your last visit.
* **day of week of last attended church** (day of week of last attended church, select one, required/optional): Select the day of the week of your last visit.
* **day of month of last attended church** (day of month of last attended church, select one, required/optional): Select the day of the month of your last visit.
* **month of last attended church** (month of last attended church, select one, required/optional): Select the month of your last visit.
* **year of last attended church** (year of last attended church, number, required/optional): Enter the year of your last visit.
* **day of the week of last attended church** (day of the week of last attended church, number, required/optional): Enter the day of the week of your last visit.
* **day of month of last attended church** (day of month of last attended church, number, required/optional): Enter the day of the month of your last visit.
* **comments** (comments, text, required/optional): Add any additional information or comments about your return to the church.
* **other reason** (other reason, text, required/optional): If there is another reason for your return that is not listed, add it here.

Please note that some fields may not be displayed or required depending on your specific circumstances. If a field is not required, it will not be visible.
