# Water Heater Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Water Heater Assessment Survey is designed to collect information about the condition, location, age, brand, model, type, size, temperature setting, last maintenance performed, and comments regarding a water heater. This information will help in assessing the overall health and performance of the water heater.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the condition of the water heater from the provided options (Excellent, Fair, Poor).
2. Choose the location(s) where the water heater is installed (Kitchen, Bathroom, Utility).
3. Enter the age of the water heater in years.
4. Choose the brand of the water heater from the provided options (A.O. Smith, American, Rheem).
5. Enter the model number of the water heater (if known).
6. Select the type of water heater you have (Tankless, Tank-type, Hybrid).
7. Choose the size of the water heater in liters or gallons.
8. Set the temperature setting of the water heater in degrees Celsius.
9. Enter the date of the last maintenance performed in YYYY-MM-DD format.
10. Provide any additional comments or information about the water heater.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the condition of the water heater?**
  * This question helps assess the overall performance and condition of the water heater.
  * Select Excellent if the water heater is in good working condition.
  * Select Fair if the water heater is showing some signs of wear and tear.
  * Choose Poor if the water heater is not functioning properly or is nearing the end of its lifespan.
* **Location**
  * This question helps determine where the water heater is installed.
  * Select all applicable locations where the water heater is used (e.g., Kitchen, Bathroom, Utility).
* **How old is the water heater?**
  * Enter the age of the water heater in years to help assess its maintenance and replacement needs.
* **Which of the following brands is your water heater?**
  * Choose the brand of the water heater from the provided options.
* **Water Heater Model**
  * Enter the model number of the water heater (if known).
* **What type of water heater do you have?**
  * Select the type of water heater you have (Tankless, Tank-type, Hybrid).
* **What size is the water heater?**
  * Enter the size of the water heater in liters or gallons to help determine its capacity and maintenance needs.
* **What is the temperature setting?**
  * Set the temperature setting of the water heater in degrees Celsius.
* **When was the last maintenance performed?**
  * Enter the date of the last maintenance performed in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Comments**
  * Provide any additional comments or information about the water heater.
* **Contact Email**
  * Enter the email address of the person responsible for the water heater (if known).
