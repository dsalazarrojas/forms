# Campus Sustainability Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users on the campus sustainability initiatives, awareness, and satisfaction. The form aims to understand users' levels of awareness, participation, and satisfaction with current sustainability initiatives, as well as their suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review each section carefully**: The form is divided into several sections, each with a specific set of questions. Please take your time to review each section and answer the questions accurately.
2. **Answer all required fields**: Some questions are marked as required, which means they are necessary to complete the form. Please make sure to answer all required fields.
3. **Select options carefully**: When selecting options, choose the one that best describes your feelings or actions.
4. **Be honest and sincere**: We value your input and encourage you to be honest about your experiences and opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How aware are you of campus sustainability initiatives?**: This question measures your level of awareness about campus sustainability initiatives. Please select one of the options below:
	+ Very Aware
	+ Somewhat Aware
	+ Slightly Aware
	+ Not Aware at All
* **Which sustainability programs have you heard about?**: This question asks about the sustainability programs you are aware of. Please select all that apply:
	+ Recycling Program
	+ Energy Conservation
	+ Water Conservation
	+ Sustainable Dining
	+ Renewable Energy
	+ Waste Reduction
	+ Green Building Practices
	+ Environmental Education
* **How satisfied are you with current sustainability efforts?**: This question measures your satisfaction level with current sustainability initiatives. Please select one of the options below:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Are sustainability programs easily accessible to you?**: This question asks about the accessibility of sustainability programs. Please select one of the options below:
	+ Very Accessible
	+ Accessible
	+ Neutral
	+ Difficult to Access
	+ Very Difficult to Access
* **How well are sustainability initiatives communicated to students?**: This question asks about the communication of sustainability initiatives. Please select one of the options below:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Do you currently participate in any sustainability programs?**: This question asks about your participation status in sustainability programs. Please select one of the options below:
	+ Actively Participate
	+ Sometimes Participate
	+ Rarely Participate
	+ Never Participate
* **What prevents you from participating more?**: This question asks about any obstacles you face in participating in sustainability programs. Please select all that apply:
	+ Lack of awareness
	+ Inconvenience
	+ Lack of interest
	+ Time constraints
	+ Limited access
	+ No barriers - I'm satisfied
	+ Other reasons
* **Would you be interested in participating if barriers were removed?**: This question asks about your willingness to participate in sustainability programs. Please select one of the options below:
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Probably yes
	+ Unsure
	+ Probably not
	+ False
* **What improvements would make the greatest impact?**: This question asks about your suggestions for improvement. Please select up to three options that you think would have the greatest impact:
	+ Better communication
	+ More accessible programs
	+ Incentive programs
	+ More frequent events
	+ Better facilities
	+ More education
	+ Transparency in goals
* **What specific actions should the campus take?**: This question asks about your specific suggestions for the campus. Please provide your ideas in the text field.
* **What is your role at the campus?**: This question asks about your role at the campus. Please select all that apply:
	+ Student
	+ Faculty
	+ Staff
	+ Administrator
	+ Other
* **How long have you been at this campus?**: This question asks about your tenure at the campus. Please select one of the options below:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 3-4 years
	+ 5-6 years
	+ 7 or more years
* **May we contact you for follow-up questions?**: This question asks about your availability for follow-up questions. Please select one of the options below:
	+ Yes, email me
	+ Yes, call me
	+ No, thank you
* **Email Address (if you want follow-up)**: This question asks for your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions. Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (if you want follow-up)**: This question asks for your phone number if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions. Please enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please take your time to complete the form accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask.
* Your input is valuable, and we appreciate your feedback.
