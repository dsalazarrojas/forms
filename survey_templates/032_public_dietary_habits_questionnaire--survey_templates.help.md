# Public Dietary Habits Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to collect information about your public dietary habits. Your responses will help us understand your eating patterns and behaviors. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question according to the options provided. If you have any other food intake or eating habits not listed here, please use the "Other" options to describe them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the questionnaire.
* **Household Members**: Please select all the members who live with you.
	+ **One**: 1 member
	+ **Two**: 2 members
	+ **Three or more**: 3 or more members
* **Age**: If you're under 18, this field is optional. For other ages, please enter your age.
* **Gender**: Please select your gender or choose "Other (please specify)" if you don't identify with the provided options.
	+ **Male**: Male
	+ **Female**: Female
	+ **Other (please specify)**: Please enter your preferred gender.
* **Height**: Please enter your height in inches or centimeters (you can select the unit in the "Height Units" field).
* **Weight**: Please enter your weight in pounds or kilograms (you can select the unit in the "Weight Units" field).
* **Height/Weight Units**: Please select the units you use for height and weight (e.g., Feet and Inches, Centimeters).
* **Food Intake Frequency**:
	+ **Daily**: You eat food once a day
	+ **Weekly**: You eat food once a week
	+ **Monthly**: You eat food once a month
	+ **Rarely**: You eat food rarely
* **Food Intake Frequency Other**: If you don't fit into the above categories, please describe your food intake frequency.
* **Food Intake Frequentest**: Please select all the times of the day when you typically eat food.
	+ **Breakfast**: In the morning
	+ **Lunch**: In the afternoon
	+ **Dinner**: In the evening
* **Typical Meal Time**:
	+ **Early**: Early in the day
	+ **Late**: Late in the day
	+ **No set time**: You don't have a regular meal time
* **Typical Breakfast Time**:
	+ **Morning**: In the morning
	+ **Late**: Late in the day
	+ **No set time**: You don't have a regular breakfast time
* **Typical Dinner Time**:
	+ **Early**: Early in the day
	+ **Late**: Late in the day
	+ **No set time**: You don't have a regular dinner time
* **Meal Frequency**:
	+ **1-2 times a day**: You eat 1-2 meals a day
	+ **3-4 times a day**: You eat 3-4 meals a day
	+ **5 or more times a day**: You eat 5 or more meals a day
* **Meal Frequency Other**: If you don't fit into the above categories, please describe your meal frequency.
* **Typical Snack Time**:
	+ **Between meals**: Between meals
	+ **Morning**: In the morning
	+ **Afternoon**: In the afternoon
	+ **Evening**: In the evening
* **Typical Snack Time Other**: If you don't fit into the above categories, please describe your snack time.
* **Meal Frequency**:
	+ **1-2 times a day**: You eat 1-2 meals a day
	+ **3-4 times a day**: You eat 3-4 meals a day
	+ **5 or more times a day**: You eat 5 or more meals a day
* **Meal Frequency Other**: If you don't fit into the above categories, please describe your meal frequency.
* **Other Food Intakes**: If you have any other food intakes or eating habits not listed, please describe them.
