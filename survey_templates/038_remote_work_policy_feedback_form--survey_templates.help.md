# Remote Work Policy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Work Policy Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their experiences and concerns regarding remote work policies within the organization. This form helps gather insights and suggestions for improvement to enhance the remote work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the remote work policy feedback form.
2. Provide your manager's name and email address.
3. Enter your department.
4. Select the date and time you are providing feedback.
5. Enter the location where you are providing feedback.
6. Enter the number of hours you spent on the task or project.
7. Describe your feedback and concerns regarding the remote work policy in the text field.
8. Select the options for feedback and important issues.
9. Provide any additional comments or concerns.
10. Select the submission satisfaction level.
11. Enter any additional comments on important issues.
12. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Remote Work Title** (`remote_work_title`, text, required): Enter a title for your feedback or concern related to remote work policies.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name as this is the person who will receive the feedback.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Employee Department** (`employee_department`, text, optional): Enter your department or team.
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, optional): Select the date when you provided feedback.
* **Feedback Time** (`feedback_time`, time, optional): Select the time when you provided feedback.
* **Feedback Hours** (`feedback_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours spent on the task or project.
* **Feedback Location** (`feedback_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you provided feedback.
* **Feedback Comment** (`feedback_comment`, note, required): Describe your feedback and concerns regarding remote work policy in this text field.
* **Feedback Satisfaction** (`feedback_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select your level of satisfaction with the remote work policy.
* **Important Issues** (`feedback_important_issues`, select_multiple, optional): Select any important issues you'd like to highlight.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or concerns.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person providing feedback.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the person providing feedback.
* **Employee Department** (`employee_department`, text, optional): Enter your department or team.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Select the date when you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Select the time when you submitted the form.
* **Submission Hours** (`submission_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours spent submitting the form.
* **Submission Location** (`submission_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you submitted the form.
* **Submission Comment** (`submission_comment`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or concerns.
* **Important Issues Comments** (`submission_important_issues_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments on important issues.
* **Submission Satisfaction** (`submission_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select your level of satisfaction with the submission process.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or concerns.
