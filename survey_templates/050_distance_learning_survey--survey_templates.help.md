# Distance Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their distance learning experiences with our service. It helps us identify areas of improvement and better serve our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest in your feedback.
3. Provide as much detail as possible.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **grade level** (`grade_level`, select_one, **required**): Please select the level of your grade or course that you are taking.
* **email** (`email`, email, **optional**): If you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or questions, please provide your email address. 
* **integration** (`integration`, select_multiple, **optional**): If you've integrated our service with any other platforms or apps, please select them.
* **best part** (`best part`, text, **optional**): Please describe your favorite part of our distance learning service.
* **suggest improvements** (`suggest improvements`, text, **optional**): Share any suggestions for how we can improve our service.
* **overall satisfaction** (`overall satisfaction`, number, **optional**): Please rate your overall satisfaction with our service on a scale of 1-10.
* **teacher feedback** (`teacher feedback`, text, **optional**): Please tell us how you feel about our virtual classes.
* **student feedback** (`student feedback`, text, **optional**): Please describe your experience with our virtual classes from a student perspective.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
* **introduction** (`introduction`, note, **optional**): This is a placeholder field, please do not answer it.
