# Governance Effectiveness Measurement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Governance Effectiveness Measurement Survey is designed to evaluate the performance of a board of directors in a corporate setting. The survey assesses various aspects of board governance, including decision-making processes, communication, and evaluation practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the questions carefully and answer each one based on your knowledge and experience with the board.
2. For textual questions, provide detailed descriptions in the text box.
3. For multiple-choice and select-one questions, select the most accurate option.
4. You may refer to the board's documentation, records, and meeting minutes to answer the questions accurately.
5. Be as specific and detailed as possible in your answers.
