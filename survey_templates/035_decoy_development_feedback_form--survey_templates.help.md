# decoy_development_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The decoy_development_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from users involved in decoy development projects. This form helps us improve the quality of our developments by providing us with insights and suggestions for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide effective feedback, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a clear and concise title that describes the issue or suggestion you're addressing.
2. Rate your feedback on a scale of 0-10, where 0 indicates no issues and 10 indicates a severe issue.
3. Provide a brief description of your feedback in the text field.
4. If applicable, attach images or screenshots that demonstrate the issue or suggest a possible solution.
5. Optionally, add comments to elaborate on your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise title that describes the issue or suggestion you're addressing. This title will help us quickly identify the topic of your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your feedback on a scale of 0-10, where 0 indicates no issues and 10 indicates a severe issue.
* **Form Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your feedback in this text field. Be as clear and concise as possible.
* **Attach Images** (`image`, text, optional): If applicable, attach images or screenshots that demonstrate the issue or suggest a possible solution. This can help us better understand your feedback.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Optionally, add comments to elaborate on your feedback. This is an opportunity to provide additional context or details that might be helpful in addressing your suggestion or issue.
