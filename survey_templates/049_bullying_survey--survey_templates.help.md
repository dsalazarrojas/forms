# Bullying Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bullying Survey is designed to collect data on bullying experiences and perceptions within schools and organizations. This data will help inform anti-bullying efforts and improve school climate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. For each question, choose the most accurate answer from the provided options.
3. When answering questions regarding the frequency or impact of bullying, please provide the most honest and accurate response possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, required/optional): Enter the date you are completing this survey.
	+ This will help track the progress and trend of bullying incidents over time.
* **Your Age** (`respondent_age`, required/optional): Select your age or age range.
	+ This will help understand the demographics of the respondents.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, required/optional): Enter the name of the school or organization you are affiliated with.
	+ This will help identify the specific school or organization in the data collected.
* **Grade or Education Level** (`grade_or_level`, required/optional): Enter your current grade level or education level.
	+ This will help understand the educational context of the respondents.
* **Have You Experienced Bullying?** (`personal_experience`, required/optional): Select if you have ever experienced bullying.
	+ This question is used to gather information on the prevalence of bullying.
* **Type of Bullying Experienced** (`bullying_type_experienced`, required/optional): Select all types of bullying you have experienced.
	+ This question is used to gather information on the types of bullying.
* **How It Affected You** (`bullying_impact`, required/optional): Select all the ways bullying has affected you emotionally.
	+ This question is used to gather information on the impact of bullying.
* **Have You Witnessed Bullying?** (`witnessed_bullying`, required/optional): Select if you have witnessed bullying.
	+ This question is used to gather information on the prevalence of bullying as a witness.
* **What Did You Do When You Witnessed It?** (`witness_response`, required/optional): Select what you did when you witnessed bullying.
	+ This question is used to gather information on responses to bullying as a witness.
* **Have You Ever Bullied Others?** (`bullied_others`, required/optional): Select if you have bullied others.
	+ This question is used to gather information on the prevalence of bullying as a perpetrator.
* **Feel Safe at School** (`school_safety`, required/optional): Select how safe you feel at school.
	+ This question is used to gather information on school safety.
* **How School Handles Bullying** (`school_response`, required/optional): Select how effective you think your school handles bullying.
	+ This question is used to gather information on school's anti-bullying efforts.
* **School Staff Aware of Bullying Issues** (`staff_awareness`, required/optional): Select how aware school staff is of bullying issues.
	+ This question is used to gather information on school staff's awareness of bullying.
* **Easy to Report Bullying** (`reporting_process`, required/optional): Select how easy it is to report bullying.
	+ This question is used to gather information on the reporting process of bullying.
* **Trust Reporting Will Be Handled Well** (`trust_reporting`, required/optional): Select how much you trust that bullying reports will be handled well.
	+ This question is used to gather information on the trust in the reporting process.
* **Peers Support Anti-Bullying Efforts** (`peer_support`, required/optional): Select how much you support peers' involvement in anti-bullying efforts.
	+ This question is used to gather information on peers' support for anti-bullying efforts.
* **Suggestions to Reduce Bullying** (`improvement_suggestions`, required/optional): Enter any suggestions you have to reduce bullying.
	+ This question is used to gather information on potential suggestions to reduce bullying.
* **Need Mental Health Support?** (`support_services`, required/optional): Select if you need mental health support.
	+ This question is used to gather information on the need for mental health support.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts.
	+ This question is used to gather any other information that may be relevant to bullying.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure or have concerns, please contact the administrator before completing the survey.
* This survey is designed to gather information, not to judge or punish. All responses are kept confidential.
