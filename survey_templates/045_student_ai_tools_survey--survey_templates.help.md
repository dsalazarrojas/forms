# Student AI Tools Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about students' experiences with AI tools, aiming to understand their perceptions, interests, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page of the form, and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields, as indicated by the red star (*) symbol.
3. If you have used other AI tools not listed in the options, please specify them in the "Other" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_aitools** (Page 1, required: false): This field is the title of the survey.
* **student_aitools_used** (Page 1, required: true): Select the AI tools you have used for your academic work. Options: google app, microsoft office, other.
* **student_aitool_satisfaction** (Page 1, required: true): Rate your satisfaction with the AI tools you have used, on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **student_aitool_future_interest** (Page 1, required: true): Indicate whether you are interested in using more AI tools in the future. Options: True, False, not sure.
* **student_aitool_note** (Page 1, required: false): Provide any additional comments about AI tools.
* **student_email** (Page 1, required: false): Enter your student email address.
* **student_phone** (Page 1, required: false): Enter your student phone number.
* **student_aitoolscomments** (Page 1, required: false): Indicate whether you are interested in using more AI tools. Options: Yes, No.
* **student_name** (Page 1, required: true): Enter your name.
* **student_school** (Page 1, required: true): Indicate whether you are a student at the current school. Options: Yes, No.
* **student_grade** (Page 1, required: true): Enter your grade level.
* **student_tool_rating** (Page 1, required: true): Rate the tool's helpfulness on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **student_comments** (Page 1, required: false): Provide any additional comments about AI tools.
* **student_aitool_other** (Page 1, required: false): If you have used other AI tools not listed, specify them.
* **student_aitool_other_comments** (Page 1, required: false): Provide any additional comments about other AI tools.
* **student_aitool_other_info** (Page 1, required: false): If you have used other AI tools, provide more information about them.
* **student_interest** (Page 1, required: false): Indicate your interest in using more AI tools.
* **student_aitool_other_interest** (Page 1, required: false): If you have used other AI tools, indicate your interest in using more of them.
* **student_aidiscovery** (Page 1, required: true): Indicate whether you have discovered AI tools through the current school's tools. Options: True, False.
* **student_aidiscovery_other** (Page 1, required: false): If you have discovered AI tools through the current school's tools, provide more information.
* **assigned_tool** (Page 1, required: true): Select the AI tool assigned to you. Options: chatjimmy, chatbot, other.
* **student_date_created** (Page 1, required: false): Enter the date you created the AI tool.
* **student_time** (Page 1, required: false): Enter the time you spent using the AI tool.
* **student_date_completed** (Page 1, required: true): Enter the date you completed the AI tool.
* **student_time_completed** (Page 1, required: false): Enter the time you spent using the AI tool.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by students who have used AI tools in their academic work. If you have not used AI tools, you should not complete this form.
