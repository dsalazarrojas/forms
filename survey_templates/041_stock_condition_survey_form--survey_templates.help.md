# Stock Condition Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Condition Survey Form is a tool used to gather data on the condition of stock items. It helps maintain accurate records and informs decisions about inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you are filling this form for a specific inventory item only.
2. Answer the questions based on your inspection and observation of the item.
3. Select one or multiple options for each question, depending on the field type (select_one or select_multiple).
4. Add any additional notes you may have about the item.

* Be accurate and precise when answering the questions.
* Ensure that you're not over- or under-reporting the condition of the item.
* If unsure, select "Other" or add a note to explain the condition.
* This form is designed to be filled out by inventory management professionals only.
