# Sports Facility Accessibility Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Facility Accessibility Improvement Survey is a tool designed to collect feedback and ideas from staff, athletes, and visitors on the accessibility features and resources of sports facilities. This survey aims to identify areas of improvement and suggest solutions to enhance the accessibility of these facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and answer the questions and fill out the fields as instructed.
2. Select the correct type of venue from the dropdown options (e.g. school, sports centre, etc.).
3. Describe any accessibility challenges you have faced or witnessed at the venue.
4. Provide any improvement ideas you have for the venue.
5. Share your feedback and thoughts on the accessibility resources available at the venue.
6. Fill out the contact information for the staff member who is responsible for the venue.
7. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback on the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): Provide a title for the survey that will be visible to others.
* **Venue Type** (`venue_type`, text, optional): Select the type of venue (e.g. school, sports centre, etc.).
* **Accessibility Challenges** (`accessibility_challenges`, text, optional): Describe any accessibility challenges you have faced or witnessed at the venue.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional): Provide any improvement ideas you have for the venue.
* **Athlete Feedback** (`athlete_feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback or thoughts on the accessibility features at the venue from an athlete's perspective.
* **Visitor Feedback** (`visitor_feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback or thoughts on the accessibility features at the venue from a visitor's perspective.
* **Staff Feedback** (`staff_feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback or thoughts on the accessibility features at the venue from a staff member's perspective.
* **Accessibility Features** (`accessibility_features`, text, optional): Describe the accessibility features available at the venue.
* **Venue Address** (`venue_address`, text, optional): Provide the address of the venue.
* **Venue Phone** (`venue_phone`, text, optional): Provide the phone number of the venue.
* **Venue Email** (`venue_email`, text, optional): Provide the email address of the venue.
* **Venue Website** (`venue_website`, text, optional): Provide the website of the venue.
* **Accessibility Resources** (`accessibility_resources`, text, optional): Describe the accessibility resources available at the venue.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the staff member responsible for the venue.
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, text, optional): Provide the email address of the staff member.
* **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`, text, optional): Provide the phone number of the staff member.
* **Staff Title** (`staff_title`, text, optional): Provide the title of the staff member.
* **Staff Notes** (`staff_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes on the staff member.
* **Athlete Survey Completion** (`athlete_survey_completion`, text, optional): Provide information on the completion of the athlete survey.
* **Visitor Survey Completion** (`visitor_survey_completion`, text, optional): Provide information on the completion of the visitor survey.
* **Staff Survey Completion** (`staff_survey_completion`, text, optional): Provide information on the completion of the staff survey.
* **Survey Completion Notes** (`survey_completion_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments on the survey completion process.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, optional): Provide the date of the survey.
* **Survey Time** (`survey_time`, time, optional): Provide the time of the survey.
* **Survey Comments** (`survey_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the survey.
