# Family Dynamics Self Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Dynamics Self Assessment Survey is designed to help family members understand their relationships and dynamics within the family. This survey is a tool for self-assessment and exploration of family interactions, conflict resolution methods, and emotional intelligence.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select a page or section from the navigation menu to begin filling out the form.
2.  For each field, choose an answer that best describes your situation or feelings about your family relationships.
3.  Answer each question honestly, taking as much time as needed.
4.  Move on to the next section after completing each page.
5.  Review your answers before submitting the form for final review and submission.
