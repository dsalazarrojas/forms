# mystery_data_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **mystery_data_collection_form** is a simple form designed to collect basic demographic data from users. It contains fields for collecting personal information such as first and last name, email address, age, gender, occupation, and company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** if required fields, even though they are not required by default.
2. Enter your **Age** if you wish to provide this information.
3. Select one or multiple **Gender** options that apply to you.
4. Enter your **Occupation**, if you have one.
5. If you work for a company, enter your **Company** name.
6. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`name`, `text`): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`name`, `text`): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter your email address in the standard format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
* **Age** (`age`, `number`): Enter your age if you wish to provide this information.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_multiple`): Select one or multiple gender options that apply to you.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`): Enter your occupation if you have one.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`): Enter your company name if you have one.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact your supervisor or support team.
* If you have any suggestions for improving this form, provide feedback using the contact information provided.
