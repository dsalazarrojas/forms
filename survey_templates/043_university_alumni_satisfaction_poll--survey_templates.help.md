# University Alumi Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from alumni about their satisfaction with their university experience. The goal is to collect data on various aspects of their time at university, including academic experience, communication, support services, and graduation outcomes. The data collected will help the university to understand the strengths and weaknesses of their programs and make informed decisions to improve the overall student experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. The form is divided into several sections, each addressing a different aspect of your university experience. You can navigate between sections using the page navigation buttons at the top of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This section collects basic information about you, such as your email address and phone number.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Academic Experience**: This section is about your time as a student at the university.
	+ **Academic Experience**: Select the type of academic experience that best describes your time at the university.
* **Course Outcomes**: This section asks about your expectations and experiences with course-related outcomes.
	+ **Course Outcomes**: Select one of the provided options for your expectations and experiences with course-related outcomes.
* **Communication**: This section is about how you felt about communication from the university.
	+ **Communication**: Select all that apply for how you felt about communication from the university.
* **Support Services**: This section asks about your experiences with university support services.
	+ **Support Services**: Select one of the provided options for your experiences with university support services.
* **Graduation Information**: This section is about your graduation experience.
	+ **Graduation Date**: Enter your graduation date.
	+ **Graduation Outcome**: Select one of the provided options for your graduation outcome.
* **University Information**: This section is about your university experience.
	+ **University Name**: Enter the name of your university.
	+ **University Location**: Enter the location of your university.
	+ **University Year**: Enter the year you attended the university.
	+ **University Program**: Select one of the provided options for your university program.
* **Degree Information**: This section is about your degree.
	+ **Degree Level**: Select one of the provided options for your degree level.
	+ **Degree Title**: Enter your degree title.
	+ **Degree Specialization**: Enter your degree specialization.
	+ **Degree Area**: Enter your degree area.
	+ **Degree Field**: Enter your degree field.
	+ **Degree Course**: Enter your degree course.
	+ **Degree Major**: Enter your degree major.

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled in to submit the form.
