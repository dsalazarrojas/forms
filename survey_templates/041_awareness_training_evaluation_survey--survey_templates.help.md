# Awareness Training Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of an awareness training session. The survey asks about the participant's information, training experience, and feedback on the session's content and organization. It helps trainers and training teams assess the effectiveness of their training programs and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer or rating for each question according to your experience.
2. Fill in any required information, such as your full name and email address.
3. For select_one questions, choose the answer that best represents your opinion or rating.
4. For text fields, provide your answer in a clear and concise manner.
5. If you're unsure about any question, you can always ask for clarification or skip the question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your official email address to contact you for any follow-up questions or to share your feedback.
* **Awareness Training Topic**: Select the primary subject of the training session you attended.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date of the training session.
* **Trainer or Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the trainer or instructor who delivered the training session.
* **Knowledge Level BEFORE Training**: Rate your understanding of the topic before the training session on a scale of 1 (Low) to 5 (Expert).
* **Knowledge Level AFTER Training**: Rate your understanding of the topic after the training session on a scale of 1 (Low) to 5 (Expert).
* **Trainer Performance Rating**: Rate the trainer's performance on a scale of 1 (Unsatisfactory) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Course Content Quality**: Rate the quality of the training content on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Course Material Usefulness**: Rate the usefulness of the training materials on a scale of 1 (Not Useful) to 5 (Highly Useful).
* **Duration of Training Session**: Choose how you felt about the session's length on a scale of Too Short (Need more time) to Too Long (Boredom set in).
* **Most Important Key Learning Point**: Briefly describe the single most important thing you learned during the session.
* **Areas to Improve**: If you have any suggestions or areas where the training could be improved, list them here.
* **Would you recommend this training to a colleague?**: Choose whether you would or would not recommend the training to a colleague.
* **Final Comments or Suggestions**: Add any additional thoughts or suggestions for the training team.

Note: This form is for your feedback and opinions. Please answer honestly and provide constructive feedback to help improve the training programs.
