# platform-engagement-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information about your experience with our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. You can skip questions that do not apply to you or that you are unsure about. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Any additional comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: `false`): This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining the purpose and importance of your feedback.
* **What is your overall experience with our platform** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your overall experience with our platform. This can include your satisfaction, frustrations, or anything else you'd like to share.
* **How likely are you to recommend our platform** (`3`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose how likely you are to recommend our platform to others.
* **When did you first use our platform** (`4`, `date`, required: `false`): Please enter the date you first started using our platform.
* **What features would you like to see added to our platform** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Suggest any new features you think would improve our platform.
* **Have you used our platform in the past week** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Check yes or no if you have used our platform in the past week.
* **How often do you use our platform** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose how often you use our platform.
* **What is your age** (`8`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your age.
* **What is your email** (`9`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address.
* **What is your phone** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number.
* **How can we contact you** (`11`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose how we can contact you.
* **Have you used our platform in the past 30 days** (`12`, `date`, required: `false`): Please enter the date you last used our platform.
* **Is this your first use of our platform** (`13`, `text`, required: `false`): Check yes or no if this is your first time using our platform.
* **What is your occupation** (`14`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your occupation.
* **What is your education** (`15`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your level of education.
* **How did you hear about us** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose how you heard about our platform.
* **What is your relationship status** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose your relationship status.
* **What time of day do you use our platform** (`18`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the time of day you usually use our platform.
* **What device do you use to access our platform** (`19`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose the devices you use to access our platform.
* **How many times have you used our platform in the past week** (`20`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the number of times you've used our platform in the past week.
* **What is your favorite platform feature** (`21`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose your favorite feature of our platform.
* **How likely are you to recommend our platform** (`22`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose how likely you are to recommend our platform.
* **How satisfied are you with our platform** (`23`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose your level of satisfaction with our platform.
* **What is your feedback about your experience with our platform** (`24`, `text`, required: `false`): Share your thoughts about your experience with our platform.
* **Do you think your experience with our platform has been good** (`25`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check yes or no if you think your experience with our platform has been good.
* **Any additional comments** (`26`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional comments or feedback about our platform.
