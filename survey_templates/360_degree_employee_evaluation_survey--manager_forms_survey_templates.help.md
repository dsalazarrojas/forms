# 360 Degree Employee Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 360-degree employee evaluation survey is designed to gather feedback from various raters on an employee's performance across different dimensions, including communication skills, leadership, teamwork, problem-solving, reliability, adaptability, technical competence, and observed behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

### Section 1: Employee Information

*   Enter the employee's full name in the "Full name" field.
*   Enter the employee's email address in the "Email address" field.
*   Select the employee's department from the provided options in the "Department" field.
*   Enter the employee's current job title in the "Job title" field.
*   If applicable, select the employee's date of hire from the calendar picker in the "Date of hire" field.

### Section 2: Rater Relationship & Context

*   Select the relationship you have with the employee from the provided options in the "Your relationship to the employee" field.
*   If you prefer to remain anonymous, leave the "Rater name" field blank.
*   If you have a contact email, enter it in the "Rater contact email" field.
*   Rate the employee's communication skills by selecting one of the provided options in the "Communication skills" field.
*   Rate the employee's leadership and influence by selecting one of the provided options in the "Leadership and influence" field.
*   If the employee's role is applicable, rate their leadership and influence.
*   Rate the employee's teamwork and collaboration by selecting one of the provided options in the "Teamwork and collaboration" field.
*   Rate the employee's problem-solving and decision-making by selecting one of the provided options in the "Problem-solving and decision-making" field.
*   Rate the employee's reliability and accountability by selecting one of the provided options in the "Reliability and accountability" field.

### Section 3: Feedback and Recommendations

*   Select all that apply from the provided options in the "Observed behaviors" field.
*   List up to three specific strengths of the employee in the "Strengths" field.
*   Provide concrete suggestions for development in the "Areas for improvement" field.
*   Select one of the provided options in the "Overall recommendation" field.
*   Enter a number from 1 to 10 in the "Overall performance score" field.
*   Provide any additional comments or remarks in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's full name.
*   **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the employee's work email address.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select the employee's department.
*   **Job title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the employee's current job title.
*   **Date of hire** (`hire_date`, date, optional): If applicable, select the employee's date of hire.
*   **Years employed** (`years_employed`, integer, optional): If applicable, select the employee's years of employment.
*   **Your relationship to the employee** (`rater_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the correct relationship category that best describes your role or position within the organization.
*   **Rater name (optional)** (`rater_name`, text, optional): If desired, enter your name.
*   **Rater contact email (optional)** (`rater_contact`, email, optional): If applicable, provide your contact email for follow-up.
*   **Communication skills** (`communication_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the employee's communication skills.
*   **Leadership and influence** (`leadership_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the employee's leadership and influence.
*   **Teamwork and collaboration** (`teamwork_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the employee's teamwork and collaboration.
*   **Problem-solving and decision-making** (`problem_solving_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the employee's problem-solving and decision-making.
*   **Reliability and accountability** (`reliability_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the employee's reliability and accountability.
*   **Adaptability to change** (`adaptability_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the employee's adaptability to change.
*   **Technical competence** (`technical_competence_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the employee's technical competence.
*   **Observed behaviors** (`observed_behaviors`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the provided list.
*   **Strengths** (`strengths`, text, optional): List up to three specific strengths of the employee.
*   **Areas for improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Provide concrete suggestions for development.
*   **Overall recommendation** (`overall_recommendation`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options.
*   **Overall performance score** (`overall_score`, integer, required): Enter a number from 1 to 10.

## Tips

*   Please answer each question honestly and thoughtfully.
*   If you are unsure about any aspect of the employee's performance, please select "Not observed" or "Not applicable" as appropriate.
*   Be specific and concrete when providing feedback, especially in areas for improvement.
*   Ensure that your feedback is respectful and professional.
*   Review and complete the form carefully to provide accurate and comprehensive feedback.

The 360-degree employee evaluation survey is a tool designed to gather feedback from various raters on an employee's performance. By completing this form, you will provide valuable insights to help the employee grow and develop professionally.
