# Sailing Club Racing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sailing Club Racing Survey is designed to gather valuable information from sailing club members to better understand their racing schedule, event quality, and participant experience. This feedback will help the club organizers to make informed decisions to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the link to access the form.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, or type your answers in the text fields.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
4. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Racing Schedule** (race_schedule, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide information about your racing schedule, including the dates and times of upcoming events. You can type in any relevant details, such as the name of the event, the location, or any specific dates you'd like to attend.
* **Event Quality** (event_quality, text, required/optional): In this field, please rate the quality of past events you've attended. This will help us understand what works well and what could be improved. You can use this space to provide specific feedback, such as what made an event enjoyable or what aspects could be changed for future events.
* **Participant Experience** (participant_experience, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts about your experience as a participant in past events. What did you enjoy most? What could be improved? This field is your chance to share any suggestions or ideas to enhance future events.
