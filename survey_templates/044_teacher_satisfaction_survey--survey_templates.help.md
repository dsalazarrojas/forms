# teacher_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The teacher_satisfaction_survey form is used to collect feedback from teachers on their satisfaction with the course or educational program. It's intended to gather valuable information to improve the program and enhance the teaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the introduction page if provided.
2.  Select the relevant options that apply to your experience and feedback.
3.  Provide specific comments and suggestions in the Comments and Suggestions section.
4.  Enter your email address or phone number if applicable.
5.  Select the completed status.
6.  Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Introduction**: A brief overview of the form's purpose and content.
*  **Questions**: Select the options that best describe your experience and feedback.
    *  **More Questions**: Select the additional questions that apply to your experience.
    *  **Even More Questions**: Select the further additional questions that apply to your experience.
*  **Comments and Suggestions**: Provide specific comments and suggestions to help improve the program and teaching experience.
*  **Email Response**: Enter your email address if you wish to receive follow-up communication.
*  **Phone Response**: Enter your phone number if you wish to receive follow-up communication.
*  **Date**: Enter the date you completed the form.
*  **Time**: Enter the time you completed the form.
*  **Email Response 2**: Enter your email address if you wish to receive follow-up communication (optional).
*  **Phone Response 2**: Enter your phone number if you wish to receive follow-up communication (optional).
*  **Date 2**: Enter the date you completed the form (optional).
*  **Time 2**: Enter the time you completed the form (optional).
*  **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback.
*  **Completed**: Select 'True' if you have completed the form, 'False' otherwise.
*  **Email Response 3**: Enter your email address if you wish to receive follow-up communication (optional).
*  **Phone Response 3**: Enter your phone number if you wish to receive follow-up communication (optional).
*  **Date 3**: Enter the date you completed the form (optional).
*  **Time 3**: Enter the time you completed the form (optional).
