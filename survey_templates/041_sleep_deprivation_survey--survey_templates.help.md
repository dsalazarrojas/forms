# Sleep Deprivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sleep Deprivation Survey aims to gather information about your sleep habits, difficulties, and experiences. Your input will help us better understand sleep-related patterns and trends.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Be as accurate as possible when filling out numerical fields (e.g., Age, Sleep Duration).
3. Select the relevant option for each field that best describes your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): A brief description of your current sleep situation is optional but welcome if you feel it will provide context for your answers.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, optional): Enter your age in years.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the options: Male, Female, or Other.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, required): Enter your current occupation or profession.
* **Bedtime** (`bedtime`, `time`, optional): If you're asked about your bedtime, please enter the time you usually go to bed (e.g., 10:00 PM).
* **Wake Time** (`wake_time`, `time`, optional): If applicable, enter the time you usually wake up (e.g., 6:00 AM).
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of hours you usually sleep per night.
* **Sleep Difficulties** (`sleep_difficulties`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the difficulties you experience with sleep from the options: Difficulty sleeping, Difficulty falling asleep, Staying asleep, Waking up too early, Other (please specify).
* **Sleep Impact** (`sleep_impact`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes how sleep deprivation affects you: Significant impact, Some impact, No impact.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional information about your sleep experiences or difficulties.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
- If you're unsure about a field, you can always ask for clarification or skip it if you're unable to provide a meaningful answer.
- If you have any previous experience with this form, you can skip it if your circumstances have changed.
- Thank you for participating in this survey and helping us better understand sleep deprivation!
