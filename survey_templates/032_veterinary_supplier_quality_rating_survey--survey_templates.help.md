# Veterinary Supplier Quality Rating Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Veterinary Supplier Quality Rating Survey is designed to collect feedback from veterinarians about their experience with a supplier. The survey assesses the quality of service, facility, communication, and response from the supplier. The information collected will help improve the supplier's performance and ensure that they continue to meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Enter the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Rate the quality of service by selecting one of the options in the "Quality of Service" field.
3. If applicable, rate the quality of facility by selecting one or more options in the "Quality of Facility" field.
4. Indicate if you have had direct contact with the supplier's facility by selecting one or more options in the "Direct Contact" field.
5. Rate the quality of communication by selecting one or more options in the "Quality of Communication" field.
6. Rate the quality of response by selecting one or more options in the "Quality of Response" field.
7. Enter your total score out of 10 in the "Total Score" field.
8. Provide any additional comments about your experience with the supplier in the "Comments" field.
9. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter a valid phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
11. Select the date of your last contact with the supplier in the "Last Contact" field.
12. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (text): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Quality of Service** (select_one): Rate the quality of service as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Quality of Facility** (select_multiple): Rate the quality of facility as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. You can select one or more options.
* **Direct Contact** (select_multiple): Indicate if you have had direct contact with the supplier's facility as True or False.
* **Quality of Communication** (select_multiple): Rate the quality of communication as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. You can select one or more options.
* **Quality of Response** (select_multiple): Rate the quality of response as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. You can select one or more options.
* **Total Score** (number): Enter a score between 1 and 10 to evaluate the supplier's overall performance.
* **Comments** (note): Provide any additional comments about your experience with the supplier.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address to ensure that feedback is sent to the correct address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter a valid phone number (optional).
* **Last Contact** (date): Select the date of your last contact with the supplier.
* **Submit** (text): Click this button to send the form.

## Tips
- Please rate each field based on your actual experience with the supplier.
- If you have had multiple contacts with the supplier, select the most recent date in the "Last Contact" field.
- Ensure that your email address is correct to ensure that feedback is sent to the correct address.
