# wealth_management_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your financial situation, goals, and preferences to help us provide you with personalized advice and services tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by reading through each section carefully and answering the questions honestly.
2. Fill in all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the field label.
3. If you have any difficulty understanding a question or need clarification, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, not required): This is a brief description of who you are and what you need help with. Please provide a concise and clear summary of your financial situation and goals.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, select_multiple, not required): Please select all the ways you would like to be contacted:
	+ Yes: We will contact you via phone.
	+ No: We will not contact you via phone.
*   Note: If you select "Yes" but do not provide a valid phone number, we will not be able to contact you effectively.

### Financial Goals
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, select_one, not required): What are your primary financial goals? Please select one or more of the options below:
	+ Yes: I'm focused on saving for a specific goal (e.g., buying a house, paying off debt).
	+ No: I'm not focused on saving for a specific goal.
*   Note: If you have more specific financial goals not listed, please provide them in the 'Financial History' field below.

### Financial History
* **Financial History** (`financial_history`, text, not required): If you have a unique financial situation or specific financial goals not listed above, please provide a brief description here.

### Financial Institutions
* **Financial Institutions** (`financial_institutions`, select_multiple, not required): Which financial institutions do you have experience with?
	+ Yes: I have experience with banks.
	+ No: I do not have experience with banks.
*   Note: If you select "Yes" but do not provide valid information, we may not be able to assist you effectively.

### Investment Experience
* **Investment Experience** (`investment_experience`, text, not required): If you have experience with investments (e.g., stocks, bonds, real estate), please describe it here.

### Financial Advice
* **Financial Advice** (`financial_advice`, text, not required): Are you looking for general financial advice or specific investment advice?
	+ Yes: I'm looking for general financial advice.
	+ No: I'm looking for specific investment advice.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure we can provide you with the best possible service.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on a question, do not hesitate to contact us.
