# Usage Context Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Usage Context Survey is a tool designed to gather information on the usage patterns and habits of users. This survey aims to collect detailed insights on when, where, why, and how users interact with a product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that apply to your usage context.
3. Provide detailed feedback in the text fields where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Usage Context Survey Page** (`usage_context_survey_page`, `text`): This is the introduction page of the survey.
* **When Used** (`when_used`, `select_one`): Answer this question with the frequency you use the product or service. Choose from:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Where Used** (`where_used`, `select_multiple`): Indicate all the locations or contexts where you use the product or service. Choose from:
	+ Yes
* **Why Used** (`why_used`, `text`): Please provide a brief explanation of why you use the product or service.
* **Frequency Used** (`frequency_used`, `number`): If you can provide a numerical answer, please indicate how often you use the product or service.
* **Improve Feedback** (`improve_feedback`, `text`): If you have any suggestions or ideas to improve the product or service, please share them in the text field.
* **Product Roadmap** (`product_roadmap`, `note`): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional feedback or insights on the product or service.
* **User Context** (`user_context`, `select_one`, 1-8): These questions are about the context in which you use the product or service. Choose from:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **User Context 2-18** (`user_context_2` to `user_context_18`, `select_one`): These questions are also about the context in which you use the product or service. Choose from:
	+ Yes
	+ No
Note: Questions 2-18 are repeated variations of question 1, each targeting a different context.
