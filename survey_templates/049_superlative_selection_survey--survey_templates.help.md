# Superlative Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Superlative Selection Survey" form is used to collect information about awards and nominations. This form is part of an evaluation process for recognizing and appreciating outstanding individuals within an organization or school.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. For each field, read the explanations provided below to understand what information is required.
3. Enter the relevant details for each field, paying attention to any required fields.
4. Fill out the form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School name** (text): Enter the name of the school or institution nominating the award.
	+ Example: "Anytown High School"
* **School email** (email): Enter the email address associated with the school or institution.
	+ Example: "school@email.com"
* **Name nominated** (text): Enter the name of the person being nominated for the award.
	+ Example: "John Doe"
* **Award Category** (text): Enter a brief description of the award category.
	+ Example: "Outstanding Teacher"
* **Name of nominator** (text): Enter the name of the person nominating the award.
	+ Example: "Jane Doe"
* **School address** (text): Enter the address of the school or institution.
	+ Example: "123 Anytown Road, Anytown, USA 12345"
* **Nominator email** (email): Enter the email address of the nominator.
	+ Example: "nominator@email.com"
* **Nominator phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the nominator.
	+ Example: "123-456-7890"
* **Nominator note** (text): Enter any additional information about the nominator.
	+ Example: "Chair of the Science Department"
* **Award Nominator title** (text): Enter the title of the award nominator.
	+ Example: "Chair of the Science Department"
* **Award Nominator position** (text): Enter the position of the award nominator.
	+ Example: "Science Department Chair"
* **Award Nominator organisation** (text): Enter the organization or department of the award nominator.
	+ Example: "Anytown High School, Science Department"
* **Award Nominator phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the award nominator.
	+ Example: "123-456-7890"
* **Award Nominator email** (email): Enter the email address of the award nominator.
	+ Example: "awardnominator@email.com"
* **Award Nominator note** (text): Enter any additional information about the award nominator.
	+ Example: "Dedicated teacher with over 10 years of service"
* **Award Nominated name** (text): Enter the name of the person being awarded.
	+ Example: "John Smith"
* **Award Nominated position** (text): Enter the position of the person being awarded.
	+ Example: "Teacher of the Year"
* **Award Nominated organisation** (text): Enter the organization or department of the person being awarded.
	+ Example: "Anytown High School, Science Department"
* **Award Nominated email** (email): Enter the email address of the person being awarded.
	+ Example: "john.smith@email.com"
* **Award Nominated phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the person being awarded.
	+ Example: "123-456-1234"
* **Award Nominated note** (text): Enter any additional information about the person being awarded.
	+ Example: "Dedicated teacher with over 10 years of service"
* **Nominator nominator name** (text): Enter the name of the person nominating the award.
	+ Example: "Jane Doe"
* **Nominator nominator email** (email): Enter the email address of the person nominating the award.
	+ Example: "nominatornominator@email.com"

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your entries for spelling and formatting errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.
* Contact your supervisor or the survey administrator if you need further assistance.
