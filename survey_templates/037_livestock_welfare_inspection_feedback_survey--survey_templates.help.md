# Livestock Welfare Inspection Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Welfare Inspection Feedback Survey is a tool used to gather feedback on the welfare of livestock during inspections. It is designed to be completed by inspectors to report on the conditions and treatment of animals on farms, slaughterhouses, and other facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any other relevant contact information in the "Inspector Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the facility being inspected in the "Facility Name" field.
3. Enter the address of the facility in the "Facility Address" field.
4. Enter the contact information for the facility in the "Facility Contact" field.
5. Enter the date you visited the facility in the "Date Visited" field.
6. Select the type of facility from the options provided in the "Facility Type" field.
7. Select the species of animals being kept at the facility from the options provided in the "Animal Species" field.
8. Answer the questions regarding the conditions and treatment of the animals based on the provided options in the "Condition of Housing", "Handling and Training", "Food and Water", "Shelter and Shade", "Veterinary Care", and "Animal Behavior" fields.
9. If you have any additional comments or observations, enter them in the "Observation" field.
10. If you have any recommendations for improvement, enter them in the "Recommendation" field.
11. Finally, indicate whether you have completed the survey in the "Survey Complete" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Name (Text)**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Facility Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Facility Address (Text)**: Enter the address of the facility.
* **Facility Contact (Text)**: Enter the contact information for the facility.
* **Date Visited (Date)**: Enter the date you visited the facility.
* **Facility Type (Select One)**: Select the type of facility from the options provided.
* **Animal Species (Select Multiple)**: Select the species of animals being kept at the facility from the options provided.
* **Condition of Housing (Select Multiple)**: Answer based on the conditions of the animal housing.
* **Handling and Training (Select Multiple)**: Answer based on the handling and training of the animals.
* **Food and Water (Select Multiple)**: Answer based on the food and water provided to the animals.
* **Shelter and Shade (Select Multiple)**: Answer based on the shelter and shade provided to the animals.
* **Veterinary Care (Select Multiple)**: Answer based on the veterinary care provided to the animals.
* **Animal Behavior (Select Multiple)**: Answer based on the animal behavior observed during the inspection.
* **Observation (Text)**: Enter any additional comments or observations.
* **Recommendation (Text)**: Enter any recommendations for improvement.
* **Comments (Text)**: Enter any additional comments.
* **Survey Complete (Select One)**: Indicate whether you have completed the survey.
