# Music And Play Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and insights on music and play knowledge. The goal is to assess users' familiarity with various music-related topics, including instruments, genres, and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start on the first page and read each question carefully.
2. Answer each question according to your genuine knowledge and experience.
3. For select multiple and select one fields, choose the options that apply to you.
4. Ensure you complete all required fields, even if it takes a bit more time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Music Knowledge** (`1`, `number`, optional): This field is intended to gauge your basic familiarity with music-related concepts. Please enter a number between 1-10, indicating your level of knowledge, with 1 being not very familiar and 10 being very familiar.
* **Play Concepts** (`2`, `text`, optional): This is a text field meant to capture your thoughts and insights on play concepts related to music.
* **Musical Instruments** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the musical instruments you have knowledge about, such as guitar, drums, violin, etc.
* **Music Genres** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the music genre that best describes your favorite music category, such as rock, pop, classical, jazz, etc.
* **Quiz Question** (`5`, `text`, optional): This field is meant to capture any specific music-related question or topic you have in mind, or anything you'd like to ask about.
* **Event Organizers** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all event organizers you have experience with, such as schools, theaters, festivals, studios, etc.
* **Educators** (`7`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of educator you have worked with or have knowledge about, such as teachers, professors, artists, etc.
* **Event Location** (`8`, `text`, optional): This field is meant to capture any specific location related to music events, such as a concert hall, music studio, etc.

## Tips
- Answer honestly and accurately to provide valuable insights.
- If you are unsure about a particular field, try your best to provide a reasonable answer.
- This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so take your time and focus on providing quality responses.
