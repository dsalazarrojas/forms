# Sales Team Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sales team survey form is designed to collect information about the sales team's performance, goals, and targets. This form is intended for sales team members to provide insights into their sales performance, quotas, and targets.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Sales Performance" page to start.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible in the required fields.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Performance (id: 1)** (`text`, required): Provide a brief description of your sales performance.
* **Sales Quotas (id: 2)** (`select_one`, required): Select the sales quota that best describes your sales performance. Options: Quota 1, Quota 2, Quota 3.
* **Sales Target (id: 3)** (`select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether you have achieved your sales target. Options: Yes, No, Other.
* **Target Time (id: 4)** (`time`, optional): Enter the target time to be achieved (if applicable).
* **Performance Date (id: 5)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of your last performance review (if applicable).
* **Target Hours (id: 6)** (`number`, required): Enter the target hours to be worked.
* **Sales Performance Rating (id: 7)** (`select_one`, required): Evaluate your sales performance. Options: Excellent, Good, Needs Improvement.
* **Sales Team Size (id: 8)** (`number`, optional): Enter the number of people in the sales team.
* **Sales Quotas Distribution (id: 9)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the sales quotas that apply to your performance. Options: Quota 1, Quota 2, Quota 3.
* **Target Days (id: 10)** (`number`, required): Enter the target days to be worked.
* **Sales Performance Evaluation (id: 11)** (`text`, required): Evaluate your sales performance in this section.
* **Target Percentage (id: 12)** (`number`, required): Enter the target percentage to be achieved.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.
* Be as specific and accurate as possible when providing answers.
* Review your answers before submission to ensure they align with your actual performance and goals.
