# skill_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess an individual's skills and knowledge in various areas. It is meant to be completed by a subject matter expert (SME) to provide an evaluation of the individual's proficiency in different skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section, which covers a specific skill area.
2. Answer each question with a 'Yes' or 'No' response, depending on whether you possess the skill or not.
3. Fill in any additional information required in the 'Email' and 'Phone' fields.
4. Review your answers and submit the form.
