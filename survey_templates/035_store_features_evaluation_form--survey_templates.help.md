# Store Features Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users evaluate the features of a store. It contains questions to assess the store's hours, location, ambiance, product, service, price, cleanliness, layout, parking, and security features. The form will help users to provide an unbiased opinion on how well a store meets their expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select an option for each field. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank if you wish. The form has multiple pages, each with a few fields. You can navigate between pages by clicking the "Next" or "Previous" buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Store Hours (#1)
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the store's hours of operation. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### Store Location (#2)
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, `text`, required: false): Describe the store's location, including its address, distance from your location, and any other notable features.

### Store Ambiance (#3)
* **Store Ambiance** (`store_ambiance`, `text`, required: false): Describe the store's overall atmosphere, including its lighting, decor, and any other environmental features.

### Store Product (#4)
* **Store Product** (`store_product`, `text`, required: false): Describe the products or services offered by the store.

### Store Service (#5)
* **Store Service** (`store_service`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the store's service, including customer support and assistance. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### Store Price (#6)
* **Store Price** (`store_price`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the store's pricing, including value for money. Choose from the options: Low, Medium, High.

### Store Cleanliness (#7)
* **Store Cleanliness** (`store_cleanliness`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the store's cleanliness, including the state of the floors, counters, and any other notable features. Choose from the options: Very Clean, Somewhat Clean, Somewhat Dirty, Extremely Dirty.

### Store Layout (#8)
* **Store Layout** (`store_layout`, `text`, required: false): Describe the store's layout, including its organization and ease of navigation.

### Store Parking (#9)
* **Store Parking** (`store_parking`, `checkbox`, required: false): Check if the store has adequate parking options for customers. Select one or both of the options: Option A, Option B.

### Store Security (#10)
* **Store Security** (`store_security`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the store's security features, including its surveillance system and staff presence. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective when answering each question.
- Try to evaluate each feature based on your personal experience or observation.
- If you have any comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section (not shown here).
- Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form!
