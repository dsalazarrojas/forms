# Pro Bono Agent Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pro Bono Agent Feedback Form is used to gather information about your experience with the services you have received from our organization. This feedback is crucial in helping us understand what we do well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Make sure you have received Pro Bono services and only submit this form once.
* Select the services you have received by choosing an option from the "pro_bono_services" field.
* Answer the "how_satisfied" question by selecting one of the provided options.
* Identify areas where you were satisfied or dissatisfied with our services in the "feedback_areas" field.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them in the "additional_feedback" field.
* Enter the number of services you received in the "number_of_services" field.
* Provide a score out of 10 for the services you received in the "service_satisfaction_score" field.
* Enter the time spent on each service in the "time_spent" field.
* Select areas for improvement in the "areas_for_improvement" field.
* If you have any other areas for improvement, enter them in the "other_areas" field.
* Enter your email address in the "email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
* Select the type of Pro Bono service you received in the "service_type" field.
* Enter the date and time you received the services in the "date" and "time" fields.
* Enter the location where you received the services in the "location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pro Bono services** (`pro_bono_services`, text, optional): Select the services you have received from our organization.
* **How Satisfied** (`how_satisfied`, select_one, optional): Answer how satisfied you are with our services using one of the provided options.
* **Feedback areas** (`feedback_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Identify areas where you were satisfied or dissatisfied with our services.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Number of services** (`number_of_services`, number, optional): Enter the number of services you received.
* **Service Satisfaction Score** (`service_satisfaction_score`, number, optional): Provide a score out of 10 for the services you received.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, time, optional): Enter the time spent on each service.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, select_multiple, optional): Select areas for improvement for our organization.
* **Other Areas** (`other_areas`, text, optional): Enter any other areas for improvement.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of Pro Bono service you received.
* **Date** (`date`, text, optional): Enter the date you received the services.
* **Time** (`time`, text, optional): Enter the time you received the services.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you received the services.
