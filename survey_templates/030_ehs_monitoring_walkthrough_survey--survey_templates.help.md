# ehs_monitoring_walkthrough_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from employees for EHS (Employee Health and Safety) monitoring, including name, role, location, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: `name`, `location`, `department`, and `phone`.
2. If necessary, fill in any additional fields that you think are relevant to your situation.
3. Review your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (form_title)**: Please enter your title/position in the company.
* **Description (form_description)**: Briefly describe your role and responsibilities.
* **Company (company)**: Enter your company name.
* **Name (name)**: Your full name.
* **Location (location)**: Enter your work location (e.g., floor, building, department).
* **Role (role)**: Briefly describe your role and responsibilities.
* **Department (department)**: Enter your department name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your work email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Comments (comments)**: Use this field to add any additional comments or information related to EHS monitoring.
