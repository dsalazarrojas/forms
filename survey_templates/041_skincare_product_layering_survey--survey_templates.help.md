# Skincare Product Layering Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about your skincare habits and preferences. Your responses will help us understand how you use and layer skincare products.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the following questions to help us understand your skincare habits:

1. **How often do you use skincare products?**
	* Select your response from the options provided:
		+ Daily
		+ Several times a week
		+ Rarely
		+ Never
2. **What type of product do you use?**
	* Select all that apply:
		+ Serums
		+ Moisturizers
		+ Cleansers
		+ Toner
3. **What is your skin condition?**
	* Select the option that best describes your skin:
		+ Normal
		+ Combination
		+ Dry
		+ Oily
		+ Sensitive
		+ Acne-prone
		+ Rosacea
		+ Other
4. **How often do you apply skincare products?**
	* Select your response:
		+ Morning and night
		+ Morning only
		+ Night only
		+ Rarely
		+ Never
5. **How do you typically layer skincare products?**
	* Select all that apply:
		+ Always
		+ Sometimes
		+ Rarely
		+ Never
6. **How consistent is your skincare routine?**
	* Select your response:
		+ Very consistent
		+ Somewhat consistent
		+ Not very consistent
		+ Not consistent at all
7. **How long do you keep using a skincare product until you stop?**
	* Select your response:
		+ Until it runs out
		+ Until it expires
		+ 1-2 weeks
		+ 1-2 months
		+ More than 2 months

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skincare Routines (Page 1)**: This page is an introduction to the survey, and it's not a field to fill.
* **How often do you use skincare products?** (`skincare_routine_frequency`):
	* This question helps us understand how often you use skincare products.
* **What product type do you use?** (`product_application`):
	* Select all the product types you use.
* **What is your skin condition?** (`skin_condition`):
	* Choose the option that best describes your skin condition.
* **How often do you apply skincare products?** (`product_frequency`):
	* Select how often you apply skincare products.
* **How do you typically layer skincare products?** (`product_ordering`):
	* Select all the ways you layer your products.
* **How consistent is your skincare routine?** (`routine_consistency`):
	* Choose how consistent your routine is.
* **How long do you keep using a skincare product until you stop?** (`product_persistence`):
	* Select how long you keep using a product.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can select multiple options for some questions, so feel free to do so.
