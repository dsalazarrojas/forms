# Join Our Holiday Greetings - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form allows you to participate in a shared holiday event by filling out a few simple fields. Please review each section below to learn more about how to fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your personal message and any relevant details you'd like to share with your team.
2. Upload a photo to show off your holiday spirit.
3. If you'd like to participate in a shared card, let us know by selecting "Agree" in the consent field.
4. If you'd like to contribute to our digital wall, select "True" in the share card field.
5. If you have any special notes or comments, type them in the "organized submission" field.
6. If you have any community contributions, such as a relevant link or resource, enter it in the "community contribution" field.
7. Finally, you can upload a video message to share with others, or a timestamp for when you're available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **holiday message**: <h2>Enter a personal message for the holiday season.</h2>
* **holiday photo**: <h2>Upload a photo that shows your holiday spirit.</h2>
* **consent**: <h2>Do you want to participate in a shared card?</h2>
	+ Select "Agree" to join the shared card.
	+ Select "Decline" to keep your card private.
* **share card**: <h2>Do you want to contribute to our digital wall?</h2>
	+ Select "True" to join the digital wall.
	+ Select "False" to keep your contribution private.
* **video**: <h2>Upload a video message for others to see.</h2>
* **digital wall**: <h2>Enter a timestamp for when you are available.</h2>
* **organized submission**: <h2>Enter any special notes or comments you'd like to share.</h2>
* **community contribution**: <h2>Enter any relevant links or resources to share with the community.</h2>

## Tips

* Make sure to review your input carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to your team.
* You can only submit one entry per form.

Note: This guide is for internal use only, and the fields and instructions may change as needed to accommodate project updates.
