# Networking Event Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from attendees of networking events. This feedback will be used to improve future events and provide a better experience for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the event you attended from the "Event attended" dropdown list.
5. Provide your feedback on the event in the "Feedback" field.
6. Rate the event on a scale of 1-5 in the "Rating" field.
7. Provide any additional comments on your rating in the "Rating comment" field.
8. Add any suggestions for improvement in the "Suggestions for improvement" field.
9. Enter any contact information you would like to be used to follow up with in the "Contact information" field.
10. Indicate whether you would like to be contacted for follow up in the "Would you like to be contacted for follow up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Event attended**: Select the event you attended from the list.
* **Feedback**: Provide your thoughts on the event, including what you liked and what you didn't.
* **Rating**: Rate the event on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Rating comment**: Provide any additional comments on your rating.
* **Suggestions for improvement**: Add any ideas you have for improving future events.
* **Contact information**: Enter any contact information you would like to be used to follow up with.
* **Would you like to be contacted for follow up**: Indicate whether you would like to be contacted for follow up after the event.
