## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Project Title" field with the title of the project.
2. Select multiple initiatives for each "Initiative X" field (e.g., "Initiative 1", "Initiative 2", "Initiative 3", etc.) from the available options.
3. Enter a brief description of the project in the "Project Description" field.
4. Provide an impact assessment in the "Impact Assessment" field.
5. Select the committee name and email in the "Committee Name" and "Committee E-mail" fields.
6. Enter the committee phone number and notes in the "Committee Phone" and "Committee Notes" fields.
7. Provide a brief comment for the committee in the "Committee Comments" field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Submit" field to indicate whether the form is being submitted.
9. If submitting, select the date and time of submission in the "Submit Date" and "Submit Time" fields.
10. Enter any additional comments in the "Submit Comment" field.
11. Select the department for the project in the "Department X" fields (e.g., "Department 1", "Department 2", "Department 3", etc.).
12. Enter the project status in the "Project Status" field.
13. Provide a project impact and priority in the "Project Impact" and "Project Priority" fields.
14. Enter the committee ranking and feedback in the "Committee Ranking" and "Committee Feedback" fields.
14. Select the committee score in the "Committee Score" field.
