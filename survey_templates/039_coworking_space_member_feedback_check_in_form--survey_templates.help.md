# Coworking Space Member Feedback Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coworking Space Member Feedback Check In Form is a simple survey designed to collect feedback from members on their experience at the coworking space. It's a quick and easy way to get a pulse on how the space is being used and what can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read the instructions on the first page.
2.  Rate your experience by selecting one of the options under "How do you feel today?".
3.  Provide a brief explanation for your rating in the text field below.
4.  If you have any suggestions or comments, feel free to add them in the text field below.
5.  Enter your email address and phone number (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).
6.  Enter your name.
7.  Enter your team name (if you're part of a team).
8.  Enter the name of the coworking space you're checking in from.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a text field to read and understand the purpose of the form.
* **Feedback**: This is a text field where you can provide your comments and feedback on your experience at the coworking space. Please be as specific as possible.
* **How do you feel today?**: Choose one of the options to rate your experience. This is a select-one field with the following options: Good, Fair, Bad.
* **Why?**: This field is where you can provide more details on why you rated your experience the way you did. Please be specific and provide as much context as possible.
* **Rating**: This is another rating field, but with a different scale. Choose one of the options: Very High, High, Medium, Low, Very Low.
* **Suggest**: If you have any suggestions or comments on how the space can be improved, please add them here.
* **Email**: This is an optional field for your email address. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your email address.
* **Phone**: This is another optional field for your phone number. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your phone number.
* **Name**: This is a required field for your name.
* **Team**: This is an optional field for your team name. If you're part of a team, please enter your team name.
* **Space**: This is an optional field for the name of the coworking space you're checking in from. If you're checking in from a specific space, please enter the name of that space.
