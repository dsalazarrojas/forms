# Messaging Coordination Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from users about their experience with messaging tools and overall satisfaction with their messaging coordination setup. It will help our team to better understand their messaging needs and improve our services.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will need to go through each page and answer all the questions according to your experience. Please select the answers that best describe your experience with the messaging tools and your overall satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (`company_survey`, select_one, optional): Please select whether you use messaging tools within your company. This will help us to understand if your organization uses messaging tools or not.
* **Messaging Tools** (`messaging_tools`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the messaging tools you use within your company. This will help us to know what types of tools are being used and how they are used.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the challenges you face while using messaging tools. This will help us to identify potential issues and areas for improvement.
* **Improvements** (`improvements`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvements to our messaging services, please provide them here.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): If you have any feedback or comments about your experience with our messaging services, please provide them here.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number you'd like to be contacted on, please enter it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Date Used** (`date_used`, date, optional): Please enter the date you first started using our messaging services.
* **Time Used** (`time_used`, time, optional): Please enter the time of day you most often use our messaging services.
* **Tool 1 Frequency** (`tool_1_frequency`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you use Tool 1. This will help us to understand how frequently you use our services.
* **Tool 1 Quality** (`tool_1_quality`, select_one, optional): Please select the quality of Tool 1.
* **Tool 2 Frequency** (`tool_2_frequency`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you use Tool 2.
* **Tool 2 Quality** (`tool_2_quality`, select_one, optional): Please select the quality of Tool 2.
* **Tool 3 Frequency** (`tool_3_frequency`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you use Tool 3.
* **Tool 3 Quality** (`tool_3_quality`, select_one, optional): Please select the quality of Tool 3.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Please select how satisfied you are with our services.
* **Recommended** (`recommended`, select_multiple, optional): Please select whether you would recommend our services to others.
* **Tool Suggestions** (`tool_suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for new tools or features, please provide them here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number you'd like to be contacted on, please enter it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you most recently used our services.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please enter the time of day you most often use our services.
