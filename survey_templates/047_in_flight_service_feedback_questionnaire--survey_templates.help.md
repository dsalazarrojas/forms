# In Flight Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In Flight Service Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from passengers about their recent flight experience. This form helps airlines collect valuable insights into their service quality, cabin conditions, food and beverage, seat comfort, and overall satisfaction. Your feedback will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for future passengers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for your recent flight, including the flight details, cabin class, and flight number.
2. Rate your experience for each category, such as flight rating, cabin rating, food and beverage, seat comfort, service quality, and overall satisfaction.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
4. Enter your email address and contact number so we can get in touch with you.
5. Finally, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Flight Details
* **Flight Details** (`flight_details`, text, required): Enter the details of your recent flight, such as flight number, departure date, and arrival date.
* **Cabin** (`cabin`, select_multiple, required): Select the cabin class you traveled in (First Class, Business Class, Economy Class).
* **Flight Rating** (`flight_rating`, number, required): Rate your flight experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Cabin Rating** (`cabin_rating`, number, required): Rate the cabin experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Food and Beverage** (`food_and_beverage`, select_multiple, required): Select your experience with food and beverage from the options Very Good, Good, or Poor.
* **Seat Comfort** (`seat_comfort`, select_one, required): Rate the seat comfort as Very Comfortable, Comfortable, Fair, or Uncomfortable.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_multiple, required): Select your experience with service quality from the options Very Good, Good, or Fair.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction as Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Airline Name** (`airline_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the airline you flew with.
* **Aircraft Model** (`aircraft_model`, select_one, optional): Select if you flew with a special aircraft model.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, number, required): Enter the flight number for your recent flight.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, required): Enter the departure date of your recent flight.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): Enter the arrival date of your recent flight.
* **Flight Duration** (`flight_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your flight in hours.
* **Crew Name** (`crew_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the crew member who took care of you.
* **Seat Number** (`seat_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of your seat.
* **Seat Letter** (`seat_letter`, text, optional): Enter the letter of your seat (e.g. A1, B2, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`comments2`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Flight Number (again)** (`flight_number`, text, optional): Enter the flight number for your recent flight (if different from the first field).
* **Cabin Number** (`cabin_number`, text, optional): Enter the cabin number (e.g. C1, C23, etc.).
* **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the passenger (if you are submitting on behalf of someone else).

Note: This form is designed to be completed by passengers who have recently flown with our airline.
