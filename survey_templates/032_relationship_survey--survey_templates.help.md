# relationship_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The relationship survey is a tool used to gather information on various aspects of relationships, including communication, conflict resolution, and emotional support. This guide will walk you through the form and explain each field in detail.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with your responses to the questions provided. Make sure to answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How long have you been in a relationship?**
  (<label> Relationship Status, <type> number, required/optional: optional)
  This question is asking how many years you have been in your current relationship. Please enter the number of years.

* **How do you usually communicate with your partner?**
  (<label> Communication, <type> select_multiple, required/optional: optional)
  This question asks how you typically communicate with your partner. Please select all the methods that apply to you, such as phone, video call, or in person.

* **How do you usually resolve conflicts with your partner?**
  (<label> Conflict Resolution, <type> select_one, required/optional: optional)
  This question is asking how you usually handle conflicts with your partner. Select the option that best describes your approach.

* **How would you rate your level of intimacy with your partner?**
  (<label> Intimacy, <type> number, required/optional: optional)
  This question asks you to rate your level of intimacy with your partner on a scale (presumably, but not specified).

* **How much support do you receive from your partner?**
  (<label> Partner Support, <type> number, required/optional: optional)
  This question asks about the level of support you receive from your partner.

* **How important is personal space to you?**
  (<label> Personal Space, <type> number, required/optional: optional)
  This question asks about the importance of personal space in your relationship.

* **How do you usually approach sex and intimacy?**
  (<label> Sex Life, <type> select_multiple, required/optional: optional)
  This question asks about your approach to sex and intimacy in your relationship. Please select all the options that apply.

* **How would you rate your partner's emotional support?**
  (<label> Emotional Support, <type> number, required/optional: optional)
  This question asks you to rate your partner's emotional support.

* **How often do you have conflicts with your partner?**
  (<label> Conflict Frequency, <type> number, required/optional: optional)
  This question asks about the frequency of conflicts in your relationship.

* **How often do you feel stressed about your relationship?**
  (<label> Relationship Stress, <type> number, required/optional: optional)
  This question asks about the frequency of stress in your relationship.
