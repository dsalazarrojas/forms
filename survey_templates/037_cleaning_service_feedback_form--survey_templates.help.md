# Cleaning Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to provide feedback on your recent cleaning service experience. Your input will help us understand how we performed, identify areas for improvement, and ensure that our services meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. Select all applicable options from the drop-down menus.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to share them in the "Additional Comments or Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, not required): Enter your contact phone number. This is optional and will only be used if needed.
* **Service Address** (`service_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the cleaning service was provided.
* **Date of Service** (`service_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the cleaning service was performed.
* **Type of Service Received** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of cleaning service you received:
	+ Regular Cleaning
	+ Deep Cleaning
	+ Move-In or Move-Out Cleaning
	+ Post-Construction Cleaning
	+ Spring Cleaning
	+ Office Cleaning
	+ Other
* **Cleaner Name (If Known)** (`cleaner_name`, `text`, not required): If you had a specific cleaner or team member, enter their name here.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service:
	+ Very Satisfied - Exceeded expectations
	+ Satisfied - Met expectations
	+ Neutral - Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied
	+ Dissatisfied - Below expectations
	+ Very Dissatisfied - Did not meet expectations
* **Cleanliness Rating** (`cleanliness_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the cleanliness of the service:
	+ Excellent - Spotless
	+ Very Good - Very clean
	+ Good - Clean
	+ Fair - Adequately clean
	+ Poor - Not clean enough
* **Punctuality Rating** (`punctuality_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the punctuality of our team:
	+ Early - Arrived before scheduled time
	+ On Time - Arrived as scheduled
	+ Slightly Late - Less than 15 minutes late
	+ Late - 15-30 minutes late
	+ Very Late - Over 30 minutes late
* **Professionalism Rating** (`professionalism_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the professionalism of our staff:
	+ Excellent - Very professional
	+ Very Good - Quite professional
	+ Good - Acceptable professionalism
	+ Fair - Some concerns
	+ Poor - Unprofessional behavior
* **Attention to Detail** (`attention_to_detail`, `select_one`, required): Rate the attention to detail:
	+ Excellent - Very thorough
	+ Very Good - Quite thorough
	+ Good - Adequately thorough
	+ Fair - Missed some areas
	+ Poor - Many areas missed
* **Communication Rating** (`communication_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of communication:
	+ Excellent - Very clear and responsive
	+ Very Good - Good communication
	+ Good - Adequate communication
	+ Fair - Some communication issues
	+ Poor - Poor communication
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, required): Rate whether you feel the service was worth the cost:
	+ Excellent Value - Worth more than paid
	+ Good Value - Fair price for service
	+ Fair Value - Acceptable for the price
	+ Poor Value - Overpriced for service received
* **Areas That Were Cleaned** (`areas_cleaned`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all areas that were cleaned:
	+ Living Room
	+ Dining Room
	+ Kitchen
	+ Bedrooms
	+ Bathrooms
	+ Hallways
	+ Stairs
	+ Basement
	+ Garage
	+ Office Space
	+ Other
* **Missed Areas or Concerns** (`missed_areas`, `text`, not required): If there were any areas that were not cleaned properly, please describe them here.
* **What Did You Like Best** (`best_aspect`, `text`, not required): Share what you liked best about our service.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, not required): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them here.
* **Would You Recommend Our Services** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Would you recommend our services to friends or family:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Not Sure
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Would You Use Our Services Again** (`would_use_again`, `select_one`, required): Would you book our services again:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Not Sure
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No

	+ 10 - Extremely Likely

	+ 7 - Somewhat Likely
	+ 6 - Neutral
	+ 5 or Below - Unlikely
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, not required): How did you find our services:
	+ Website

	+ Friend or Family Referral
	+ Online Search
	+ Advertisement
	+ Direct Mail
	+ Previous Customer
	+ Other

	+ Carpet Cleaning

	+ Pressure Washing
	+ Floor Waxing or Polishing
	+ Organizing Services
	+ Laundry Services

* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Share any other comments or feedback about your experience.
