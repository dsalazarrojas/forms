# Energy Solutions Feedback Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and information from customers about their energy solutions experiences. Please provide your honest feedback and comments to help us improve our services and products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Provide Comments about your energy solutions experience.
5. Indicate your Permission to Reuse Comments for various purposes, including:
	* Promotional Materials.
	* Info Materials.
6. Enter Date and Time of your energy solutions experience, if applicable.
7. Provide additional Notes, if needed.
8. Enter other contact information (e.g., Phone) if necessary.
9. Specify other Email addresses if necessary.
10. Enter other Comments if needed.
11. Confirm your Permission to Share with various groups, including:
	* Other Organizations.
	* Energy Providers.
	* Other Groups.
	* Customers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Comments** (Note, Optional): Provide any comments about your energy solutions experience.
* **Permission to Reuse Comments** (Select One, Required): Confirm if you allow us to reuse your comments for various purposes.
* **Permission for Promotional Materials** (Select One, Required): Confirm if you allow us to reuse your comments for promotional materials.
* **Permission for Info Materials** (Select One, Required): Confirm if you allow us to reuse your comments for info materials.
* **Date** (Date, Required): If applicable, enter the date of your energy solutions experience.
* **Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of your energy solutions experience, if needed.
* **Note** (Note, Optional): Provide additional information if needed.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter an email address, if you want to provide a different one than what you already entered.
* **Comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments if needed.
* **Permission to Reuse Comments for Promotional Materials** (Select One, Required): Confirm if you allow us to reuse your comments for promotional materials.
* **Permission to Reuse Comments for Info Materials** (Select One, Required): Confirm if you allow us to reuse your comments for info materials.
* **Permission to Reuse Comments for Other Materials** (Select One, Optional): Confirm if you allow us to reuse your comments for other materials.
* **Other Materials** (Text, Optional): Enter other materials that are not listed above.
* **Description of Other Materials** (Note, Optional): Provide a description of other materials that are not listed above.
* **Permission to Share with Other Organizations** (Select One, Optional): Confirm if you allow us to share your comments with other organizations.
* **Permission to Share with Energy Providers** (Select One, Optional): Confirm if you allow us to share your comments with energy providers.
* **Permission to Share with Other Groups** (Select One, Optional): Confirm if you allow us to share your comments with other groups.
* **Other Groups** (Text, Optional): Enter other groups that are not listed above.
* **Description of Other Groups** (Note, Optional): Provide a description of other groups that are not listed above.
* **Permission to Share with Customers** (Select One, Optional): Confirm if you allow us to share your comments with customers.
* **Description of Customers** (Note, Optional): Provide a description of customers that are not listed above.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use the same email address for all email fields.
* Enter a valid email address for email fields.
* Confirm your permission settings carefully.
