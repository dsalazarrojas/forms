# Audiovisual Culture Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather information about your demographics, viewing habits, and opinions on audiovisual culture.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the correct answers for each field based on your personal experiences and opinions.
2. Ensure that you answer all the required fields.
3. You can select multiple options for some fields if applicable.
4. For text fields, provide clear and concise answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, Required): This question asks about your age group to help us understand the audience's age distribution.
* **Highest Level of Education Completed** (`education_level`, Required): Select your highest level of education completed.
* **How Often Do You Watch Movies or TV Series?** (`consumption_frequency`, Required): This question assesses your viewing frequency.
* **Primary Platform for Viewing Content** (`preferred_platform`, Required): Select your primary platform for watching movies or TV series.
* **Importance of Audio Quality** (`audio_importance`, Required): Indicate how important you think audio quality is for you when watching movies or TV series.
* **Importance of Video Quality** (`video_importance`, Required): Indicate how important you think video quality is for you when watching movies or TV series.
* **Most Significant Genres** (`favorite_genres`, Required): Select your top three favorite genres that you find culturally most significant.
* **Attend or Follow Film Festivals?** (`film_festival_interest`, Required): Answer whether you attend or follow film festivals regularly.
* **Interest in Virtual Reality as a Narrative Medium** (`future_tech_interest`, Required): Select your level of interest in virtual reality as a narrative medium.
* **Primary Purpose of Cinema in Your Opinion** (`cinema_purpose`, Required): Select the primary purpose of cinema in your opinion.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Optional): Feel free to provide any further thoughts on audiovisual culture.

## Tips
* Answer honestly and accurately.
* Select only options that apply to you.
* Be concise in the text field.
* If unsure, select the most appropriate option.
* Take your time to answer the questions thoughtfully.
