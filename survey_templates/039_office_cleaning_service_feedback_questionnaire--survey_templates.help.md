# Office Cleaning Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from office users about the office cleaning service. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the user's satisfaction with the cleaning service, how often they receive updates about the schedule, and any additional comments or feedback they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all necessary information before completing it.
2. Select the relevant answer for each field based on your experience with the office cleaning service.
3. Fill out any optional fields that you feel are relevant to your experience.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Office Cleaning Service Feedback Questionnaire - Field 1 (Introduction)
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, note, required: false): This field is intended to provide a brief introduction to the form. It is not a required field and you can skip it.

### Office Cleaning Service Feedback Questionnaire - Field 2 (How often do you get the office cleaning service?)
* **How often do you get the office cleaning service?** (`cleaning_service`, select_multiple, required: true): This field asks if you receive regular cleaning services for your office. Select 'Yes' if you do, 'No' otherwise.

### Office Cleaning Service Feedback Questionnaire - Field 3 (How do you rate the quality of the office cleaning service?)
* **How do you rate the quality of the office cleaning service?** (`cleaning_service_quality`, select_one, required: true): This field asks you to rate the quality of the cleaning service on a scale from Excellent to Poor.

### Office Cleaning Service Feedback Questionnaire - Field 4 (How satisfied are you with the office cleaning service?)
* **How satisfied are you with the office cleaning service?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required: true): This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with the cleaning service on a scale from Very Satisfied to Dissatisfied.

### Office Cleaning Service Feedback Questionnaire - Field 5 (How often do you get updates about the office cleaning schedule?)
* **How often do you get updates about the office cleaning schedule?** (`communication`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks if you receive regular updates about the cleaning schedule. Select 'Yes' if you do, 'No' otherwise.

### Office Cleaning Service Feedback Questionnaire - Field 6 (Do you have any feedback about the office cleaning service?)
* **Do you have any feedback about the office cleaning service?** (`feedback`, note, required: false): This field is intended for you to provide any comments or feedback you may have about the office cleaning service. If you don't have any feedback, you can skip this field.

### Office Cleaning Service Feedback Questionnaire - Field 7 (Additional Comments)
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): This field is intended for you to provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the office cleaning service. If you don't have any comments, you can skip this field.

## Tips
* Please answer each field based on your actual experience with the office cleaning service.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the form administrator for assistance.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our services.
