# School Facilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the condition of school facilities. It assesses various aspects of facility condition, such as building condition, equipment, safety, accessibility, cleanliness, hygiene, and waste management. 

## How To Complete This Form
* Read each page of the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
* For select fields, choose the best option that describes the current state of the facility.
* For rating fields, select the rating that best describes your assessment.
* Make sure to complete all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facilities Condition** (Select One)
	+ This field assesses the overall condition of the facility.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if the facilities are in good condition, or 'No' if they are not.
* **Facilities Type** (Select One)
	+ This field is meant to identify the type of facility, such as the condition of the school building.
	+ Choose the option that best describes the current state.
* **Facilities Rating** (Select One)
	+ This field rates the overall condition of facilities, with '4' being excellent and '1' being poor.
	+ Choose the rating that best describes your assessment.
* **Facilities Equipment** (Select Multiple)
	+ This field selects the types of facilities that are available, such as classrooms, technology, and libraries.
	+ Choose all that apply.
* **Facilities Safety** (Select Multiple)
	+ This field assesses the safety of facilities, such as fire safety and emergency services.
	+ Choose all that apply.
* **Facilities Safety Rating** (Number)
	+ This field rates the overall safety of facilities.
	+ Enter a number between 1 and 5 that best describes your assessment.
* **Facilities Accessibility** (Select One)
	+ This field assesses the accessibility of facilities, such as ramps and elevators.
	+ Choose 'Fully Accessible' if all facilities are accessible, or 'Not Accessible' if they are not.
* **Facilities Accessibility Rating** (Select One)
	+ This field rates the accessibility of facilities.
	+ Choose '5 - Fully Accessible' for fully accessible facilities, or '1 - Very Not Accessible' for facilities that are not accessible at all.
* **Facilities Cleanliness** (Select Multiple)
	+ This field assesses the cleanliness and hygiene of facilities, such as ventilation and temperature control.
	+ Choose all that apply.
* **Facilities Hygiene** (Select Multiple)
	+ This field assesses the hygiene of facilities, such as hand sanitization stations and waste disposal.
	+ Choose all that apply.
* **Facilities Health and Safety** (Select Multiple)
	+ This field assesses the health and safety of facilities, such as first aid kits and emergency phone availability.
	+ Choose all that apply.
* **Facilities Health and Safety Rating** (Number)
	+ This field rates the health and safety of facilities.
	+ Enter a number between 1 and 5 that best describes your assessment.
* **Facilities Waste Disposal** (Select Multiple)
	+ This field assesses the waste disposal practices of facilities, such as regular waste collection and clean storage.
	+ Choose all that apply.
* **Facilities Waste Disposal Rating** (Number)
	+ This field rates the waste disposal practices of facilities.
	+ Enter a number between 1 and 5 that best describes your assessment.
* **Facilities Ventilation** (Select Multiple)
	+ This field assesses the ventilation of facilities, such as well-ventilated and adequate ventilation.
	+ Choose all that apply.
* **Facilities Ventilation Rating** (Number)
	+ This field rates the ventilation of facilities.
	+ Enter a number between 1 and 5 that best describes your assessment.
* **Facilities Access Ramp and Elevators** (Select Multiple)
	+ This field assesses the accessibility of facilities with ramps and elevators.
	+ Choose all that apply.
* **Facilities Access Ramp and Elevators Rating** (Select One)
	+ This field rates the accessibility of facilities with ramps and elevators.
	+ Choose '5 - Fully Accessible' for fully accessible facilities, or '1 - Very Not Accessible' for facilities that are not accessible at all.

Note: The fields with numbers are used for rating purposes, and the fields with select multiple and select one are used for selecting options. The required fields are marked with 'required: true'.
