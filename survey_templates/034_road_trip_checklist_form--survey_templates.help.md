# Road Trip Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Road Trip Checklist Form is a tool to help you prepare and enjoy your road trip. It collects information about your vehicle, driver, and trip route to ensure a safe and memorable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section carefully, providing accurate details.
2. Start with "Checklist" (Page 1) and move down to "First Aid Kit" (Page 11).
3. Provide information in the relevant fields for each section.
4. Make sure to save your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist** (road_trip_checklist_form)
	+ A text field for you to add a checklist of items to pack or prepare for your trip.
* **Vehicle Details** (vehicle_details)
	+ Enter details about your vehicle, such as its make, model, and year.
* **Driver Information** (driver_info)
	+ Fill in your name, address, and contact information.
* **Route Info** (route_info)
	+ Enter information about your trip route, including the starting and ending points.
* **Vehicle Documents** (vehicle_documents)
	+ Upload or provide details about any required vehicle documents, such as insurance cards or vehicle registrations.
* **Vehicle Equipment** (vehicle_equipment)
	+ List any essential vehicle equipment, such as a spare tire or emergency kit.
* **Driver License** (driver_license)
	+ Provide a scanned or uploaded copy of your driver's license.
* **Vehicle Registration** (vehicle_registration)
	+ Upload or provide details about your vehicle's registration document.
* **Vehicle Insurance** (vehicle_insurance)
	+ Enter information about your vehicle's insurance coverage.
* **First Aid Kit** (first_aid_kit)
	+ List items to include in your first aid kit, such as pain relievers or bandages.
* **Road Map** (road_map)
	+ Upload or create a digital copy of your road map.
* **Weather Info** (weather_info)
	+ Gather weather forecast information for your trip route.
