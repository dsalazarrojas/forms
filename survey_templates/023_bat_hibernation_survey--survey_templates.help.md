# Bat Hibernation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on bat hibernation sites. Surveyors use it to capture metadata and observation details about the sites where bats hibernate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the **Observer Name** field, which should include your name and contact information.
2. Enter the **Site Name or ID** field, which should be the specific name or ID of the hibernation site.
3. Select the **Date of Observation** using the date picker.
4. Choose the **Survey Start Time** from the time picker.
5. Fill out the **External Temperature (Celsius)** field with the current temperature outside the site.
6. Fill out the **Internal Site Temperature (Celsius)** field with the temperature inside the hibernation site.
7. Enter the **Relative Humidity (%)** field with the current humidity reading.
8. Select the **Primary Bat Species Observed** from the dropdown list.
9. Enter the **Estimated Total Count of Bats** with an estimated number of bats present.
10. Select how the observation was made from the **Observation Method** dropdown list.
11. Answer **Are visible symptoms of White-Nose Syndrome present?** with one of the three options provided.
12. Add any **Additional Field Notes** if necessary.
13. Answer **Were survey photos captured?** with one of the two options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Observer Name**: Your name and contact information.
* **Site Name or ID**: The specific name or ID of the hibernation site.
* **Date of Observation**: The date when the survey was conducted.
* **Survey Start Time**: The time when the survey began.
* **External Temperature (Celsius)**: The current temperature outside the site.
* **Internal Site Temperature (Celsius)**: The temperature inside the hibernation site.
* **Relative Humidity (%)**: The current humidity reading at the site.
* **Primary Bat Species Observed**: The species of bat observed at the site.
* **Estimated Total Count of Bats**: An estimated number of bats present at the site.
* **Observation Method**: How the observation was made.
* **Are visible symptoms of White-Nose Syndrome present?**: Answer with one of the three options provided.
* **Additional Field Notes**: Any additional information about the site or observation.
* **Were survey photos captured?**: Whether photos were taken during the survey.
 
Tips: 
* Ensure that the data provided is accurate and consistent with other records.
* If this form has been used previously, ensure that data is not redundant.
* Add any additional field notes if necessary to provide context to your observation.
* If photos were taken, they should be stored with the submitted form data.
* If White-Nose Syndrome symptoms are observed, report them as soon as possible to prevent further spread of the disease.
