# Gratitude Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of the classroom environment and teacher support. Your input will help identify areas of improvement and inform strategies to enhance the learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please take a few minutes to read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
* For questions with multiple-choice options, select all that apply.
* You can skip questions that don't apply to you or your situation.
* Submit your completed form when you've finished.
