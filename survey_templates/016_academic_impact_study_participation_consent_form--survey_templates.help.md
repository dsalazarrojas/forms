# Academic Impact Study Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides instructions on how to complete the Academic Impact Study Participation Consent Form, which is designed to collect participant consent for an academic study. The form aims to ensure that participants understand the study's aims, procedures, and risks involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the study overview to understand the purpose and procedures of the study.
2.  Enter your name in the "Participant name" field to identify yourself as a participant.
3.  Provide your email address in the "Participant email" field for study communications.
4.  Read the "Brief study description" to understand the study's objectives and procedures.
5.  Estimate the "Expected participation duration" if applicable.
6.  Review the "Summary of risks and benefits" to understand any potential risks or benefits associated with the study.
7.  Read the "Confidentiality measures" to know how your data will be protected.
8.  Select the correct option for "Voluntary participation acknowledgement" to confirm your understanding of the study's voluntary nature.
9.  Sign your name in the "Electronic signature name" field to confirm your consent.
10.  Enter the date in the "Consent date" field to record the date of your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant name** (`Participant name`, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself as a participant.
*   **Participant email** (`Participant email`, email, required): Provide your email address for study communications.
*   **Brief study description** (`Brief study description`, text, required): Read this section to understand the study's objectives and procedures.
*   **Expected participation duration** (`Expected participation duration`, text, optional): Estimate the time you expect to participate in the study.
*   **Summary of risks and benefits** (`Summary of risks and benefits`, text, required): Understand any potential risks or benefits associated with the study.
*   **Confidentiality measures** (`Confidentiality measures`, text, optional): Learn how your data will be protected.
*   **Voluntary participation acknowledgement** (`Voluntary participation acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Select the correct option to confirm your understanding of the study's voluntary nature.
*   **Electronic signature name** (`Electronic signature name`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
*   **Consent date** (`Consent date`, date, required): Enter the date of your consent.

## Tips

*   Make sure to read and understand all the provided information before signing the form.
*   Ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form author at form@gic.mx.
