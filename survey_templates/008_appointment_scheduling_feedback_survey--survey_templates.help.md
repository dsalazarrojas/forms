# Appointment Scheduling Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback about your experience with our appointment scheduling process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form and answering each question to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple-choice questions (e.g. "How satisfied were you with the booking process"), select one of the available options that best reflects your experience.
3. For open-ended questions (e.g. "Additional Comments or Feedback"), provide a brief description of your thoughts and experiences.
4. Make sure to provide honest and constructive feedback, as it will help us to improve our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **About Your Appointment**: This is a brief description of your appointment, including the date, time, and any other relevant details.
* **Appointment Date**: Select the date of your appointment from the calendar or enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Type of Service Received**: Describe the type of service you received during your appointment.
* **How Satisfied Were You with the Booking Process**: Rate your satisfaction with the booking process using one of the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Was the Scheduling Process Easy to Use**: Rate your experience with the scheduling process using one of the following options:
	+ Very Easy
	+ Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **Were the Available Time Slots Suitable for You**: Rate your experience with the available time slots using one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Staff Professionalism During Booking**: Rate your experience with the staff's professionalism during the booking process using one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Quality of Communication About Your Appointment**: Rate your experience with the communication about your appointment using one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **What Could We Improve About Appointment Scheduling**: Provide any suggestions or comments on how we can improve our appointment scheduling process.
* **Would You Recommend Our Scheduling System to Others**: Rate your willingness to recommend our scheduling system to others using one of the following options:
	+ Definitely
	+ Probably
	+ Maybe
	+ Unlikely
	+ Definitely Not
* **Additional Comments or Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our scheduling system.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so we can better understand your experience.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us to improve our services.
