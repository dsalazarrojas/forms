# Driving Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driving Survey is designed to collect information from individuals about their driving habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages below and fill in the required information for each page.
2. Click "Submit" to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you drive? (Page 1)** (`driving_survey_page_1`, text, optional)
  This field is for you to describe how you typically drive. Please provide a brief answer in the text field.
* **When do you drive? (Page 2)** (`driving_survey_page_2`, text, optional)
  This field is for you to describe when you usually drive. Please provide a brief answer in the text field.
* **Do you drive to work? (Page 3)** (`driving_survey_page_3`, select_one, required)
  This field is for you to select whether you drive to work or not. Please choose one of the options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **How many times do you drive per week? (Page 4)** (`driving_survey_page_4`, number, optional)
  This field is for you to indicate how many times you drive per week. Please enter a number.
* **How often do you drive? (Page 5)** (`driving_survey_page_5`, number, optional)
  This field is for you to indicate how often you drive. Please enter a number.
* **Driving License (Page 6)** (`driving_survey_page_6`, select_multiple, required)
  This field is for you to select all the relevant options for your driver's license. Please choose one or more of the options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **How far do you drive from work? (Page 7)** (`driving_survey_page_7`, select_one, optional)
  This field is for you to select how far you drive from work. Please choose one of the options: Option 4 or Option 5.
* **Driving Time (Page 8)** (`driving_survey_page_8`, time, optional)
  This field is for you to indicate how much time you spend driving per day. Please use the time format.
* **Driving Day (Page 9)** (`driving_survey_page_9`, date, optional)
  This field is for you to select the day you drive. Please choose a date.
* **Email (Page 10)** (`driving_survey_page_10`, email, optional)
  This field is for you to provide your email address.
* **Phone Number (Page 11)** (`driving_survey_page_11`, text, optional)
  This field is for you to provide your phone number.

## Tips
* Please answer each field as accurately and truthfully as possible.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at your own pace.
* We value your input and thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
