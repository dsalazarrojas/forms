# Couples Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Couples Survey form is designed to gather information about the dynamics of romantic relationships between two individuals. It's essential to fill out this form if you're part of a couple or two individuals in a romantic relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the most suitable option for each field.
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is in a relationship** (1): Check if you're currently in a relationship.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you and your partner are romantically involved.
	+ Select 'No' if you're not in a romantic relationship.
* **Relationship type** (2):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you're aware of your partner's feelings and needs.
	+ Select 'No' if you're not aware of your partner's feelings and needs.
* **Partner awareness** (3):
	+ Select 'Yes' if your partner is aware of your feelings and needs.
	+ Select 'No' if your partner is not aware of your feelings and needs.
* **Partner influence** (4):
	+ Answer in a few words how much influence does your partner have on your life.
* **Communication style** (5):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you and your partner have a good communication style.
	+ Select 'No' if you and your partner don't have a good communication style.
* **Conflict resolution** (6):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you and your partner can resolve conflicts effectively.
	+ Select 'No' if you and your partner struggle to resolve conflicts.
* **Emotional intelligence** (7):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you and your partner have high emotional intelligence.
	+ Select 'No' if you and your partner don't have high emotional intelligence.
* **Emotional support** (8):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you and your partner provide emotional support to each other.
	+ Select 'No' if you and your partner don't provide emotional support.
* **Intimacy level** (9):
	+ Answer a number to indicate the level of intimacy in your relationship (e.g., 1-10).
* **Conflicts frequency** (10):
	+ Select 'Yes' if conflicts occur frequently in your relationship.
	+ Select 'No' if conflicts are rare in your relationship.
* **Communication channel** (11):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you and your partner use different communication channels (e.g., phone, email, social media).
	+ Select 'No' if you and your partner use the same communication channel (e.g., only phone).
