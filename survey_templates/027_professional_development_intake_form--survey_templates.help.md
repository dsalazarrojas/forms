# Professional Development Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Development Intake Form is used to gather information about an employee's professional development needs and interests. This form is intended for employees who are looking to develop their skills and knowledge in their current role or career.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the training programs you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose your departmental interest from the dropdown menu.
3. Select your work location from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter your job function in the text box.
5. Enter your reporting manager's name and email address.
6. Enter the number of people you supervise.
7. Enter the number of years you have been in your role.
8. Enter the amount of time you have available for training per week.
9. Enter your email address and phone number.
10. Enter your manager's email address and phone number.
11. Enter your training budget per month.
12. Enter your training goals and objectives.
13. Enter your training recommendations and suggestions.
14. Enter any concerns you have about training.
15. Enter your training expectations.
16. Enter your training preferences.
17. Enter your training commitments.
18. Enter any other training opportunities available to you.
19. Check if there are other trainings available to you.
20. Add any additional notes.
21. Finally, sign the form with your employee signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Programs**:
	+ This field allows you to select the training programs that interest you. Please choose the programs that align with your career goals and development needs.
* **Departmental Interest**:
	+ This field allows you to select the department that aligns with your work interests. Please choose the department that best fits your role and responsibilities.
* **Work Location**:
	+ This field allows you to select the location where you work. Please choose your work location from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Function**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your job function. Please enter a brief description of your current job function.
* **Reporting Manager**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your reporting manager's name and email address. Please enter your manager's name and email address.
* **Team Size**:
	+ This field allows you to enter the number of people you supervise. Please enter the number of team members you are responsible for.
* **Years in Role**:
	+ This field allows you to enter the number of years you have been in your role. Please enter the number of years you have been in your current position.
* **Available Time for Training**:
	+ This field allows you to enter the amount of time you have available for training per week. Please enter the number of hours you have available for training.
* **Email**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your email address. Please enter your work email address.
* **Phone**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your phone number. Please enter your work phone number.
* **Manager Email**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your manager's email address. Please enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your manager's phone number. Please enter your manager's phone number.
* **Training Budget**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your training budget per month. Please enter the amount of money allocated for training.
* **Training Goals**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your training goals and objectives. Please enter a brief description of your training goals.
* **Training Recommendations**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your training recommendations. Please enter any training recommendations you have.
* **Training Suggestions**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your training suggestions. Please enter any training suggestions you have.
* **Training Concerns**:
	+ This field allows you to enter any concerns you have about training. Please enter any concerns you have about training.
* **Training Expectations**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your training expectations. Please enter a brief description of your training expectations.
* **Training Preferences**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your training preferences. Please enter any training preferences you have.
* **Training Commitments**:
	+ This field allows you to enter your training commitments. Please enter any training commitments you have.
* **Other Trainings**:
	+ This field allows you to select if there are other trainings available to you. Please choose 'True' if there are other trainings available.
* **Additional Notes**:
	+ This field allows you to add any additional notes. Please enter any additional information you would like to share.
* **Employee Signature**:
	+ This field allows you to sign the form. Please sign the form with your employee signature.
