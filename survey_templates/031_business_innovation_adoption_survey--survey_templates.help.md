# Business Innovation Adoption Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess the current state of innovation adoption within your department or team. It aims to help us understand your perceptions on innovation culture, barriers to innovation, and potential areas for improvement. Your responses will help us better understand your needs and preferences, enabling us to develop more effective strategies for promoting innovation adoption.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly. This will help us get an accurate understanding of your department's or team's innovation adoption.
2. For questions marked as required, please provide a response. If a question is not required, you can skip it or leave it blank.
3. For questions with numerical ratings (e.g., 1-5), rate from 1 (low) to 5 (very high).
4. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
5. For questions with a single select option, choose one option that best describes your perspective.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department or Team** (department_team_name_innovation, text, required): Please enter the name of your department or team.
* **Innovation Awareness Score** (innovation_awareness_rating_score, number, required): Rate from 1 to 5 how well you understand the company's innovation goals, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Ease of Adopting New Tools** (ease_of_adopting_new_tools_rating, number, required): Rate from 1 to 5 the process of integrating new software or methods, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Management Support for New Ideas** (management_support_for_ideas_score, number, required): Rate from 1 to 5 how much your manager encourages experimentation, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Primary Barriers to Innovation** (barriers_to_innovation_selection, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to your current workflow.
* **Most Effective Innovation Channel** (most_effective_innovation_channel_choice, select_one, required): Choose the channel where the best new ideas come from.
* **Training Quality for New Tech** (training_quality_for_new_tech_rating, select_one, required): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 4 (Excellent) how effective the current training programs are for new technology.
* **Recommended Tool for Adoption** (recommended_tool_for_adoption_text, text, optional): If applicable, suggest a specific tool or method you believe should be adopted.
* **Personal Willingness to Experiment** (willingness_to_experiment_choice, select_one, required): Choose how comfortable you are with testing unproven methods.
* **Suggestion for Improving Innovation Culture** (innovation_improvement_suggestion_text, text, optional): Provide one change that would make a difference in innovation culture.
* **Survey Date** (survey_completion_date_innovation, date, required): This field is for your survey completion date.
