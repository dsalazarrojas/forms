# Nail Polish Purchase Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about your nail polish purchase behavior, including your buying frequency, preferred shopping channels, favorite brands, color preferences, budget, brand loyalty, type of nail polish, reasons for purchase, and purchasing frequency. Your responses will help us better understand your habits and preferences when it comes to nail polish.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. For each question, select the answer that best fits your behavior.
2. You can select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, but you don't have to answer them all in one session.
4. You can save your progress and come back later to continue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you buy nail polish? (buying_frequency)**: How often you buy nail polish, e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, or never.
* **Which online store do you usually buy nail polish from? (shopping_channels)**: Select all online stores you have purchased nail polish from, such as Amazon, eBay, Google Express, Sephora, or Ulta.
* **Which nail polish brand do you prefer? (preferred_brand)**: Choose your favorite nail polish brand from the list, e.g., OPI, Essie, Sally Hansen, LVMH, or Deborah Lippmann.
* **What color is your favorite nail polish color? (color_choice)**: Choose your favorite nail polish color from the list, e.g., red, pink, coral, purple, blue, or yellow.
* **What is your budget for a bottle of nail polish? (price_range)**: Select the price range you usually pay for a bottle of nail polish, e.g., $0, $100.
* **How loyal are you to your favorite nail polish brand? (brand_loyalty)**: Choose a number that represents how loyal you are to your favorite nail polish brand, e.g., 1-5.
* **What type of nail polish do you usually buy? (nail_polish_type)**: Choose the type of nail polish you usually buy, e.g., gel, cream, mousse, dipping, brush, or sponge.
* **What motivates you to buy nail polish? (nail_polish_reason)**: Select all reasons that motivate you to buy nail polish, such as wanting to look good, feel good, feel confident, express yourself, or other.
* **How many bottles of nail polish have you purchased in the last 30 days? (nail_polish_purchased)**: Enter the number of bottles of nail polish you have purchased in the last 30 days.
* **How many bottles of nail polish have you shopped for in the last 30 days? (nail_polish_shopped)**: Enter the number of bottles of nail polish you have shopped for in the last 30 days.
