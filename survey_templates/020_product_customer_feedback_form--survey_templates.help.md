# Product Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Customer Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from customers about a product that they are experiencing issues with. This form should only be filled out by customers who are experiencing difficulties with a specific product and are willing to provide detailed feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the product that you are experiencing issues with.
2. Answer the following questions about your experience:
	* What is your overall rating of this product?
	* How would you rate the quality of this product?
3. Choose how likely you are to recommend this product to others:
	* Very Likely to Recommend
	* Somewhat Likely
	* Not Likely
	* Not At All Likely
4. Enter your email address (if you would like to be contacted for further discussion).
5. Enter your phone number (if you would like to be contacted for further discussion).
6. Add any additional notes about your experience with the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product that you are experiencing issues with.
* **What is your overall rating of this product?** (`feedback_question_1`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the product, on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the quality of this product?** (`feedback_question_2`, number, optional): Rate the quality of the product, on a scale of 1-5.
* **How likely are you to recommend this product?** (`feedback_question_3`, select, optional): Choose how likely you are to recommend this product to others:
	+ Very Likely to Recommend
	+ Somewhat Likely
	+ Not Likely
	+ Not At All Likely
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your experience with the product.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form only if you are experiencing issues with the product.
* Be honest and detailed in your feedback, as it will help us improve the product.
* If you have any follow-up questions or discussions, we may contact you via email or phone based on the information you provide.
