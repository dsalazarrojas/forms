> ## Community SWOT Analysis Survey - Help Guide
### Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to help us identify our community's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. Your input will provide valuable insights to guide our strategic planning and decision-making.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the required fields and answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible. 

- Select the primary focus area for the analysis and choose the most relevant category from the list.
- Rate your current satisfaction level with our community on a scale of 1 to 5.
- Suggest one thing that could make our community stronger.
- Let us know if you would be interested in participating in a community asset mapping workshop.
- Enter your name and current contact email address (optional).
- Finally, select a date for submission.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Community SWOT Analysis --**: A brief introduction to the survey.
* **Name of Respondent**: Your name for reference purposes.
* **Your Neighborhood or Community**: The specific community you are representing.
* **Strengths (S)**: What does our community do well? What are our internal assets?
* **Weaknesses (W)**: What could be improved? What are our internal limitations?
* **Opportunities (O)**: What external trends or changes can we take advantage of?
* **Threats (T)**: What external factors or risks could harm our community?
* **Primary Focus Area for Analysis**: Select the most important category for this analysis. 
  - **Infrastructure and Services**: e.g., transportation, public utilities, community facilities
  - **Economy and Jobs**: e.g., local businesses, job market, economic development
  - **Environment and Sustainability**: e.g., natural resources, climate change, conservation
  - **Education and Youth**: e.g., schools, youth programs, education system
  - **Public Safety and Health**: e.g., healthcare, crime prevention, emergency services
  - **Culture and Community Engagement**: e.g., arts and culture, community events, social cohesion
* **Current Community Satisfaction Level**: Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 5.
  - **5 - Extremely Satisfied**: Very satisfied with our community.
  - **4**: Satisfied.
  - **3**: Neutral.
  - **2**: Dissatisfied.
  - **1 - Not Satisfied**: Very dissatisfied.
* **What is one thing that would make our community stronger?**: Suggest a concrete idea or improvement.
* **Would you participate in a community asset mapping workshop?**: Let us know if you'd like to be part of the workshop.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter your current email address for follow-up and workshop invitations.
* **Today's Date**: Select the current date for submission. 

### Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
- You can skip the optional fields.
- The survey will help inform our strategic planning and decision-making.
