# Playdate Partner Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback on your experience with our partner. Your input will help us understand how we can improve our partnership and better serve you and your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is organized into several sections, each with a series of questions or fields. Please read each section carefully and follow the instructions below:

1. **Introduction**: This section is a brief introduction to the survey.
2. **Partner Information**: This section is where you will enter information about the partner you are surveying.
	* **Partner Name**: Enter the name of the partner you are surveying.
	* **Partner Email**: Enter the email address of the partner you are surveying. (Note: This field is not used for communication purposes, but rather to help us verify the partner's identity.)
	* **Partner Phone**: Enter the phone number of the partner you are surveying.
	* **Partner Website**: Enter the website of the partner you are surveying.
3. **Experience with Partner**: This section is where you will answer questions about your experience with the partner.
	* **How many months ago did you start working with this partner?**: Select the number of months since you started working with the partner.
	* **How many children did you work with this partner?**: Select the number of children you worked with this partner.
	* **What type of activities did you do with this partner?**: Select all activities that apply to your experience with the partner.
4. **Feedback**: This section is where you will provide feedback on your experience with the partner.
	* **How satisfied are you with this partner?**: Select your level of satisfaction with the partner.
	* **What could this partner do to improve?**: Select all areas where the partner could improve.
	* **What did you like most about this partner?**: Enter a brief description of what you liked most about the partner.
	* **What did you like least about this partner?**: Enter a brief description of what you liked least about the partner.
5. **Partnership Potential**: This section is where you will answer questions about the potential for future partnership.
	* **Will you partner with us in the future?**: Select whether you think the partner will partner with us in the future.
6. **Additional Comments**: This section is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the partner.
