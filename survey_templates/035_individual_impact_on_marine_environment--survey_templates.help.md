# Individual Impact On Marine Environment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the impact of individual actions on the marine environment. It helps to understand how human activities influence the health and well-being of marine ecosystems, including coral reefs, estuaries, mangrove forests, open ocean, rocky coastlines, and seagrass. Your input will contribute to identifying areas where human actions have the most significant impact and inform strategies for mitigating these effects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide a brief introduction to your current or past activities related to the marine environment.
2. Choose the type of marine habitat that you have the most interaction with or are most concerned about.
3. Rate the health of the ocean (1-5) and describe your reasons for this assessment.
4. Select all marine habitats that you think have been impacted by your actions.
5. Briefly describe how you have become aware of ocean protection efforts and conservation practices.
6. Provide a personal account of how your actions have affected the marine environment.
7. Describe any ocean protection actions you have taken or would like to take.
8. Share your level of awareness about ocean protection efforts and conservation practices.
9. List any marine conservation participants you have been involved with or would like to be involved with.
10. Add any additional comments or information about your interaction with the marine environment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
	+ A text field to provide a brief introduction to your current or past activities related to the marine environment.
* **Marine habitat**:
	+ A select-one field asking you to choose a type of marine habitat with which you have the most interaction or concern.
	+ Options: coral reefs, estuary, mangrove forests, open ocean, rocky coastlines, seagrass
* **Ocean health**:
	+ A number field asking you to rate the health of the ocean on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
	+ Please explain your reasons for this assessment.
* **Impact on marine habitats**:
	+ A select-multiple field asking you to select all marine habitats that you think have been impacted by your actions.
	+ Options: coral reefs, estuary, mangrove forests, open ocean, rocky coastlines, seagrass
* **Awareness of ocean protection**:
	+ A text field asking you to describe how you have become aware of ocean protection efforts and conservation practices.
* **Personal impact on marine environment**:
	+ A text field asking you to provide a personal account of how your actions have affected the marine environment.
* **Ocean protection actions**:
	+ A text field asking you to describe any ocean protection actions you have taken or would like to take.
* **Ocean protection awareness**:
	+ A text field asking you to share your level of awareness about ocean protection efforts and conservation practices.
* **Marine conservation participants**:
	+ A text field asking you to list any marine conservation participants you have been involved with or would like to be involved with.
* **Additional comments**:
	+ A text field for you to add any additional comments or information about your interaction with the marine environment.
