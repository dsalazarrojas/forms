# Sustainable Product Self Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Product Self Assessment Questionnaire is a form used to gather information about a product's sustainability features and characteristics. This form helps users evaluate and compare products based on their environmental impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Product Title
	* Product Description
	* Materials
	* Manufacturing Process
	* Energy Consumption
	* Packaging
	* End-Of-Life (select multiple options)
	* Production Location
	* Company
	* Contact Person
2. Select the corresponding options for the End-Of-Life field.
3. Leave the optional fields blank, unless you have specific information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Product Title** (text, required)
Enter the title of the product being assessed.
### 2. **Product Description** (text, required)
Describe the product and its features.
### 3. **Materials** (select_one, required)
Select the type of materials used in the product:
	* Materials
	* Recycled
	* Biodegradable
	* Compostable
### 4. **Manufacturing Process** (text, required)
Describe the process used to manufacture the product.
### 5. **Energy Consumption** (number, required)
Enter the energy consumption of the product (e.g., 10 kWh).
### 6. **Packaging** (text, required)
Describe the packaging material and design used for the product.
### 7. **End-Of-Life** (select_multiple, required)
Select the end-of-life options that apply to the product:
	* Landfill
	* Recycled
	* Composting
	* Biodegradable
### 8. **Production Location** (text, required)
Enter the location where the product is produced.
### 9. **Company** (text, required)
Enter the company name and address.
### 10. **Contact Person** (text, required)
Enter the contact person's name, title, and contact information.
### 11. **Email** (email, optional)
Enter the contact person's email address (if available).
### 12. **Phone** (text, optional)
Enter the contact person's phone number (if available).
### 13. **Production Date** (date, optional)
Enter the production date (if available).
### 14. **Notes** (note, optional)
Add any additional comments or information about the product.

Note: This form is not intended to be filled out by the same user for the same product multiple times. Any existing entries for the same product will be considered duplicates and ignored.
