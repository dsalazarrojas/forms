# e-learning-program-evaluation-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluations from participants in an e-learning program. It helps us understand your experience, satisfaction, and suggestions for improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question carefully, and provide as much detail as possible. You can skip any question that is not relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **E-learning Program Evaluation Questionnaire**: This is a text box where you can provide your thoughts, comments, or suggestions about the e-learning program.
* **Online Course Feedback**: Please provide any feedback about the online course you took, including any positive or negative comments.
* **Participant Feedback**: This is a text box where you can provide feedback about your overall experience in the program.
* **How did you find the course content?**: This question is asking about your opinion on the quality of the course content.
* **How would you rate the course difficulty?**: Rate the difficulty of the course on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is easy and 5 is hard.
* **How would you rate the instructor's performance?**: Rate the performance of the instructor on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How would you rate the course materials?**: Rate the quality of the course materials on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How would you rate the course overall?**: Rate the overall quality of the course on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Date**: Please provide the date you completed the course.
* **Time**: Please provide the time you completed the course.
* **Email**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address.
* **Phone Number**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your phone number.
* **Would you like to provide your contact information?**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **E-learning Program Evaluation**: This is a text box where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about the e-learning program.
* **How often do you take online courses?**: Please select the frequency of your online course participation.
* **Do you take online courses?**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **How many online courses do you usually take in a month?**: Please provide the average number of online courses you take in a month.

Note: The fields with 'Program Evaluation Questionnaire' or 'E-learning Program Evaluation Questionnaire' in their label are for general comments or descriptions. They are not required fields and can be skipped if not relevant.
