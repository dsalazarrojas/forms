# School Withdrawal Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student's withdrawal from school. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details that can help in understanding the circumstances surrounding a student's departure. The form is part of an internal process to maintain accurate records and ensure a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant details.
3. Provide thoughtful answers to the open-ended questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the student's full name as it appears on their official records.
* **Grade Level** (`grade`, select_one, required: false): Select the grade level that the student was at during their time at the school. This can be Elementary, Middle School, or High School.
* **Reason For Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the reasons why the student is leaving the school. You can select one or more of the options provided.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the school the student attended.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher who is most closely associated with the student's departure.
* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, required: false): Provide any additional personal information that you feel is relevant to the student's withdrawal.
* **Thoughts** (`thoughts`, text, required: false): Share any thoughts or comments you may have regarding the student's departure.
* **Reason For Leaving** is a select_multiple field with two options: **I agree** and **I do not agree**.
*   Please note that this field is used to capture the student's agreement or disagreement with a specific reason for leaving. 

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or indicate that it is not relevant.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities. 

**Note**: The fields marked as "required: false" are optional and can be left blank or completed at your discretion.
