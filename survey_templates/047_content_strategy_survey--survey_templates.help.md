# Content Strategy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your online content preferences and behavior, helping us create more relevant and engaging content for our audience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
2. Use a single response per question, as applicable.
3. You can choose multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Respondent Information
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Please provide your full name.
* **Email** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Your email address (not required for submission, but it helps us contact you if needed).
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): The date of this survey completion.

### Page 2: Demographics
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age group: 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65 or older.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Choose your gender identity: Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer to self-describe, Prefer not to say.

### Page 3: Occupation and Industry
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required): Your current occupation or job title.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your industry: Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Education, Retail, Marketing, Media, Manufacturing, Other.

### Page 4: Content Preferences
* **Content Consumption Frequency** (`content_consumption_frequency`, select_one, required): How often do you consume online content: Multiple times daily, Once daily, Several times weekly, Once weekly, Rarely.
* **Preferred Content Types** (`preferred_content_types`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of content you prefer: Blog Articles, Videos, Infographics, Podcasts, Social Media Posts, E-books, Webinars, Case Studies.
* **Content Length Preference** (`content_length_preference`, select_one, required): What content length do you prefer: Short (under 500 words), Medium (500-1500 words), Long (1500+ words), Depends on topic.
* **Content Topics of Interest** (`content_topics_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select the topics that interest you most: Industry News, How-To Guides, Product Reviews, Case Studies, Thought Leadership, Trends and Insights, Company Updates, Educational Content.

### Page 5: Content Discovery and Sharing
* **Content Discovery Method** (`content_discovery_method`, select_one, required): How do you usually find content: Search Engines, Social Media, Email Newsletters, Recommendations, Direct Website Visits, RSS Feeds.
* **Social Media Platforms Used** (`social_media_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select the social media platforms you use: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Reddit.
* **Content Engagement Level** (`content_engagement_level`, select_one, required): How often do you engage with content: Always engage (like, comment, share), Sometimes engage, Rarely engage, Never engage, just consume.

### Page 6: Content Perception and Sharing
* **Content Format Device** (`content_format_device`, select_one, required): What device do you primarily use for content: Desktop/Laptop, Mobile Phone, Tablet, Multiple devices equally.
* **Email Subscription Willingness** (`email_subscription_willingness`, select_one, required): Would you subscribe to email content: Yes, regularly subscribe, Yes, but selectively, Maybe, No, prefer other channels.
* **Content Sharing Behavior** (`content_sharing_behavior`, select_one, required): How often do you share content: Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, Never.

### Page 7: Content Quality and Pain Points
* **Content Quality Factors** (`content_quality_factors`, select_multiple, required): What makes content high quality: Accurate Information, Well-Written, Visually Appealing, Actionable Insights, Original Ideas, Easy to Understand, Entertaining, Timely.
* **Content Pain Points** (`content_pain_points`, text, required): What frustrates you about online content?
* **Content Expectations** (`content_expectations`, text, required): What do you expect from brand content?

### Page 8: Additional Feedback
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Any other thoughts about content?
