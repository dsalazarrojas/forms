# Disaster Recovery Plan Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the readiness and response of your organization to disasters or critical incidents. It helps identify areas of concern and determines the level of preparedness to recover from such incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information to provide a point of contact for the assessment.
2. Evaluate the business criticality of your organization by selecting the relevant options.
3. Choose the options that best describe your current recovery planning processes.
4. Indicate the incident response processes you have in place.
5. Estimate the time it takes to recover from a disaster.
6. Identify the business continuity processes that are critical to your organization.
7. Select the critical assets that are essential to your organization's operations.
8. Determine the business criticality score based on your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (1)**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address to provide a point of contact for the assessment.
* **Business Criticality (2)**: Evaluate the criticality of your organization by selecting one or more of the options, which describe the potential impact of a disaster on your organization's operations.
* **Recovery Planning (3)**: Choose the options that best describe your current recovery planning processes. This will help identify areas of concern and determine the level of preparedness.
* **Incident Response (4)**: Indicate the incident response processes you have in place, such as communication protocols, resource allocation, and recovery procedures.
* **Disaster Recovery Plan (5)**: This is not a field for actual planning, but rather a field for you to write any additional information that you feel is relevant to your disaster recovery plan.
* **Data Loss (6)**: Select the option that best describes the potential loss of data in the event of a disaster. This will help in identifying potential risks and vulnerabilities.
* **Recovery Time (7)**: Estimate the time it takes to recover from a disaster. This will help in identifying areas of concern and determining the level of preparedness.
* **Business Continuity (8)**: Identify the business continuity processes that are critical to your organization. This will help in determining the level of preparedness and identifying areas of concern.
* **Critical Assets (9)**: Select the critical assets that are essential to your organization's operations. This will help in identifying potential risks and vulnerabilities.
* **Business Criticality Score (10)**: Determine the business criticality score based on your assessment. This will help in evaluating the level of preparedness and identifying areas of concern.
