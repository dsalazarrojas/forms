# Handmade Product Style Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Handmade Product Style Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers on their preferences and expectations when it comes to handmade products. This form will help us better understand what customers are looking for in a handmade product and tailor our product offerings accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and answer them to the best of your abilities.
2. Choose one or more options that suit your preferences.
3. If a question is not applicable to you, please mark the "N/A" option.
4. Fill out as many or as few questions as you feel necessary.
5. If you need help, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.
