# Privacy And Information Security Training Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the Privacy And Information Security Training session you attended. Your input will help us improve the training program and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please select "Yes" if you attended the training session, and "No" if you did not.
2.  If you selected "Yes", provide the date of attendance in the "Date of Attendance" field in mm/dd format (e.g., 01/20).
3.  Enter the time of attendance in the "Time of Attendance" field.
4.  Enter the name of the instructor in the "Name of Instructor" field.
5.  Rate the performance of the instructor in the "Instructor Performance" field. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Session Details**: This field is not applicable as it is a placeholder for a page title and not a field to fill out.
* **Have you attended this training?**: This question asks whether you attended the training session. Please select "Yes" if you did.
* **Date of Attendance**: If you selected "Yes" in the previous field, provide the date of attendance in the format mm/dd.
* **Time of Attendance**: Enter the time of attendance. This field is expecting a time in 12-hour format.
* **Name of Instructor**: Enter the name of the instructor who conducted the training session.
* **Length of Session**: This field is expecting the duration of the training session in minutes.
*   Please note that this field is not used in this form but is likely part of a larger form used for multiple sessions.
*   **Instructor Performance**: Rate the performance of the instructor based on how well they conducted the session. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   This field helps us understand the quality of the session and identify areas for improvement.
* **Tips**: Please provide specific details about your experience in the training session. What did you find helpful or unhelpful?
