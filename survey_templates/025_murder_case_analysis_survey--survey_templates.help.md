# Murder Case Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a murder case, including details about the suspect, crime scene, and investigation's progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*). These fields are essential for a comprehensive analysis of the murder case.
2. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus to provide specific information about the suspect and crime scene.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments in the text fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Murder Case
* **Murder Case**: A brief description of the case, including the crime scene and any relevant details.

* **Suspect Info**:
	+ A: Select the suspect's name or alias.
	+ B: Select any additional suspect information.
	+ C: Select any other suspect information.

* **Crime Scene**: A description of the crime scene, including any relevant details.

* **Evidence Collected**: A list of evidence collected from the crime scene.

* **Investigation Progress**:
	+ Not Started: The investigation has not yet begun.
	+ Ongoing: The investigation is currently underway.
	+ Completed: The investigation has been completed.

* **Suspect Interview**: A description of the suspect's interview, including any relevant details.

* **Case Status**:
	+ Active: The case is currently active and ongoing.
	+ Closed: The case has been resolved.

* **Team Member**:
	+ A: Select the team member responsible for the investigation.
	+ B: Select any additional team member information.
	+ C: Select any other team member information.
	+ D: Select any other team member information.
	+ E: Select any other team member information.

* **Email**: Enter the email address of the investigator or team member.

* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the investigator or team member.

* **Case Number**: A unique identifier for the case.

* **Case Description**: A brief description of the case, including the crime scene and any relevant details.

* **Suspect Name**: Enter the suspect's name or alias.

* **Suspect Address**: Enter the suspect's address.

* **Suspect Phone**: Enter the suspect's phone number.

* **Suspect Relationship**: A description of the suspect's relationship to the victim.

* **Suspect Distinguishing Features**: A list of the suspect's distinguishing features.

* **Suspect Age**:
	+ 18-30: The suspect is between 18-30 years old.
	+ 31-45: The suspect is between 31-45 years old.
	+ 46-60: The suspect is between 46-60 years old.

* **Suspect Gender**:
	+ Male: The suspect is male.
	+ Female: The suspect is female.
	+ Other: The suspect's gender is other or prefer not to say.

* **Suspect Race**:
	+ Asian: The suspect is Asian.
	+ Black or African American: The suspect is black or African American.
	+ White: The suspect is white.

* **Suspect Weight**:
	+ Less than 120: The suspect weighs less than 120 pounds.
	+ 120-180: The suspect weighs between 120-180 pounds.
	+ Over 180: The suspect weighs over 180 pounds.

* **Suspect Height**:

	+ 5'11-6'1: The suspect is between 5'11-6'1 tall.
	+ 6'2-6'6: The suspect is between 6'2-6'6 tall.

* **Suspect Eye Color**:
	+ Blue: The suspect has blue eyes.
	+ Brown: The suspect has brown eyes.
	+ Green: The suspect has green eyes.
	+ Hazel: The suspect has hazel eyes.
	+ Other: The suspect's eye color is other.

	+ Blonde: The suspect has blonde hair.
	+ Brown: The suspect has brown hair.
	+ Black: The suspect has black hair.
	+ Gray: The suspect has gray hair.
	+ Other: The suspect's hair color is other.

## Tips
