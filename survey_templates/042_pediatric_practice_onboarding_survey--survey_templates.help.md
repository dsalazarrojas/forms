# Pediatric Practice Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Practice Onboarding Survey is designed to gather essential information about new patients and their families, streamlining the onboarding process and improving patient experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Fill out the form completely, including all required and optional fields.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Patient Info (1)**: Enter the new patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Parent Info (2)**: Provide information about the patient's parents or guardians, including their names, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Emergency Contact (3)**: Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Consent (4)**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the practice's policies and procedures.
* **New Patient Parent Info (5)**: Enter information about the patient's parent or guardian, including their contact details.
* **Patient Details (6)**: Enter the patient's medical details, such as any allergies or medical conditions.
* **Medical History (7)**: Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any previous treatments or conditions.
* **Medical History Info (8)**: Enter any additional information about the patient's medical history.
* **Allergy Info (9)**: List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Medication Info (10)**: Enter any medication information, including dosage and frequency.
* **Insurance Info (11)**: Enter the patient's insurance details, including policy number and provider.
* **Emergency Contact Info (12)**: Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Ensure you have the patient's permission to share this information with the practice.
* Review the form with the patient to ensure they understand and agree to share this information.
* Keep the patient's confidentiality and HIPAA compliance in mind when completing this form.
