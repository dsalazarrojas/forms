# Paleo Diet Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paleo Diet Assessment Survey is a tool used to gather information about a person's paleo diet background, goals, and restrictions. It helps in assessing their suitability for a paleo diet plan and identifying potential challenges or areas of support needed. This survey is designed to be completed by individuals who are considering or have started a paleo diet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin with the introduction page, which provides a brief overview of the paleo diet.
2. On the "Paleo Background" page, provide a short description of your background and experience with the paleo diet.
3. For "Diet Restrictions," select the options that apply to you, such as "Yes" or "No," to indicate any dietary restrictions you have.
4. On the "Paleo Goals" page, describe your goals for adopting the paleo diet, such as weight loss, improved health, or increased energy.
5. In the "Paleo Diary" section, provide any relevant medical or health-related information about your paleo diary experiences.
6. For "Paleo Support," select the professionals who currently support your paleo diet, such as a paleo dietitian or doctors.
7. If you have other health professionals (doctors or practitioners) who are not listed above, please enter their names in the "Other Professions" section.
8. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback about your paleo diet journey in the "Paleo Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: `false`): This page provides a brief introduction to the paleo diet and its principles.
* **Paleo Background** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Share a short description of your background and experience with the paleo diet.
* **Diet Restrictions** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Indicate any dietary restrictions you have, such as vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free.
* **Paleo Goals** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your goals for adopting the paleo diet, such as weight loss, improved health, or increased energy.
* **Paleo Diary** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide any relevant medical or health-related information about your paleo diary experiences.
* **Paleo Support** (`6`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the professionals who currently support your paleo diet, such as a paleo dietitian or doctors.
* **Paleo Recommend** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Indicate if you recommend the paleo diet to others, such as friends or family.
* **Paleo Comments** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your paleo diet journey.
* **Paleo Dietitian** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of your paleo dietitian.
* **Paleo Doctor** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of your paleo doctor.
* **Other Doctors** (`11`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the names of other doctors who are not listed above.
* **Other Professions** (`12`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the names of other health professionals, such as nutritionists or coaches, who are not listed above.
* **Other Professions (o)** (`13`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is a duplicate of the "Other Professions" field with a different label.
* **Other Professions (1)** (`14`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is another duplicate of the "Other Professions" field.
* **Other Professions (2)** (`15`, `text`, required: `false`): Another duplicate of the "Other Professions" field.
* **Other Professions (3)** (`16`, `text`, required: `false`): Yet another duplicate of the "Other Professions" field.
* **Other Professions (4)** (`17`, `text`, required: `false`): Another duplicate of the "Other Professions" field.
* **Other Professions (5)** (`18`, `text`, required: `false`): The final duplicate of the "Other Professions" field.

Please note that some fields are duplicates, and you should only fill in one of them.
