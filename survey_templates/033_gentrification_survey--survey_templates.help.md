# Gentrification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gentrification Survey is a tool designed to collect feedback from community members about their experiences with gentrification in their neighborhood. This survey aims to understand the changes they have observed, their concerns, and their perceptions about gentrification. This information will help us better understand the impact of gentrification on community members and inform future development policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as honestly as you can.
2. You can select multiple options from the "gentrification_impacts" field if applicable.
3. Take your time to carefully consider your responses before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What changes have you noticed in the neighborhood?** (neighbourhood_changes)
	+ This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe any changes you have observed in your neighborhood, such as new businesses, housing developments, or demographic shifts.
* **On a scale of 0-10, how much do you think gentrification has changed the neighborhood?** (gentrification_level)
	+ This question asks you to rate the level of gentrification in your neighborhood, with 0 being no change and 10 being a significant change.
* **What are your concerns about gentrification?** (gentrification_concerns)
	+ This is a free-text field where you can list any concerns or worries you have about gentrification, such as housing costs, displacement, or loss of community character.
* **In what ways has gentrification impacted you?** (gentrification_impacts)
	+ Select all that apply:
	+ Increased housing costs
	+ Changes in community composition
	+ New businesses and amenities
	+ Other (please specify)
	+ This question allows you to identify the specific ways in which gentrification has affected you personally.
* **What are your perceptions about gentrification?** (gentrification_perceptions)
	+ Select one that best fits your view:
	+ Negative
	+ Positive
	+ Neutral
	+ Don't know/No answer
	+ This question asks you to express your general attitude or opinion about gentrification.
* **How satisfied are you with gentrification in the neighborhood?** (gentrification_satisfaction)
	+ This question is a scale from 0-10, where 0 means very dissatisfied and 10 means very satisfied.
* **How aware are you of gentrification?** (gentrification_awareness)
	+ Select one that best fits your level of awareness:
	+ Highly aware
	+ Somewhat aware
	+ Not very aware
	+ Not at all aware
	+ This question asks you to report your level of awareness about gentrification.
* **Would you recommend gentrification to someone else?** (gentrification_recommendation)
	+ Select one that best fits your opinion:
		- Yes
		- No
	+ This question asks you to share your opinion about gentrification with others.

# Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your responses to provide valuable insights.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to write "Don't know" or "Not sure" and we'll follow up to clarify.
* If you have any further comments or thoughts, you can include them in the "gentrification_concerns" field.
