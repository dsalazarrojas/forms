# Care Coordination Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Care Coordination Effectiveness Survey is a tool to evaluate the effectiveness of care coordination in a healthcare setting. It helps care teams understand what works well and what can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the care coordination process by choosing from the options provided under "Participant Role".
2. Enter the name of the facility or program you are evaluating in the "Facility or Program Name" field.
3. For each question, rate your experience using the numbers provided, ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
4. If applicable, provide feedback in the "Areas for Improvement" and "Success Stories or Positive Feedback" fields.
5. Select "Yes, I am open to it" if you are willing to be contacted for further feedback, and enter your email address if so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Role** (Participant Role, select_one, required): Select the role that applies to you, such as Patient, Family Member/Caregiver, Healthcare Provider/Staff, or Care Manager.
* **Facility or Program Name** (Facility or Program Name, text, required): Enter the name of the clinic or program you are evaluating.
* **Timeliness of Communication** (Timeliness of Communication, number, required): Rate how quickly the team responded to your needs, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Ease of Access to Services** (Ease of Access to Services, number, required): Rate how simple it was to get the help you needed, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Clarity of Care Plan** (Clarity of Care Plan, number, required): Rate if you understood the next steps and treatment goals, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Support from Care Manager** (Support from Care Manager, number, optional): If applicable, rate the support you received from your primary coordinator, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Coordination with Specialists** (Coordination with Specialists, number, required): Rate how well different doctors and therapists worked together, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Patient and Family Engagement** (Patient and Family Engagement, number, required): Rate if you were included in the decision-making process, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Care Coordination** (Overall satisfaction with care coordination, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the care coordination process, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Areas for Improvement** (Areas for Improvement, text, optional): Share any ideas you have for improvement, if applicable.
* **Success Stories or Positive Feedback** (Success Stories or Positive Feedback, text, optional): Share a moment where care coordination worked well, if applicable.
* **May we contact you for further feedback?** (May we contact you for further feedback?, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I am open to it" if you are willing to be contacted for further feedback, and enter your email address if so.
* **Email Address for follow-up** (Email Address for follow-up, email, optional): Enter your email address if you selected "Yes, I am open to it".

**Tips**

* Please rate each question seriously, but honestly. The goal is to improve care coordination.
* Remember, this form is for your benefit – the care team wants to know how to better serve you.
