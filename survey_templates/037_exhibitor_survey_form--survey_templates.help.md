# exhibitor_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from exhibitors about their events or webinars. It helps us determine the type of survey that will be conducted and the frequency of follow-up surveys.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
    1.  Select the type of survey you would like to conduct.
    2.  Provide information about the event or webinar.
    3.  Answer questions about the follow-up survey schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Survey Type (Event/Webinar)** (`exhibitor_survey_form_survey_type`, select_one, required): This field asks you to select the type of survey you would like to conduct. Please choose between "Event" or "Webinar".
*   **Event Name** (`exhibitor_survey_form_event_name`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter the name of the event or webinar.
*   **Event Date** (`exhibitor_survey_form_event_date`, date, optional): This field allows you to enter the date of the event or webinar.
*   **Event Time** (`exhibitor_survey_form_event_time`, time, optional): This field allows you to enter the time of the event or webinar.
*   **Survey Questions** (`exhibitor_survey_form_survey_questions`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter any additional survey questions you would like to ask.
*   **Comments** (`exhibitor_survey_form_comments`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
*   **Follow-up Survey** (`exhibitor_survey_form_follow_up_survey_frequency`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to select the frequency of follow-up surveys. Please choose one or more of the following options: 1 Month, 2 Months, 3 Months.
*   **Follow-up Survey Frequency 2** (`exhibitor_survey_form_follow_up_survey_frequency_2`, select_multiple, required): This field also asks you to select the frequency of follow-up surveys. Please choose one or more of the following options: 1 Month, 2 Months, 3 Months.
*   **Next Survey Date** (`exhibitor_survey_form_next_survey_date`, date, optional): This field allows you to enter the date of the next follow-up survey.
*   **Next Survey Time** (`exhibitor_survey_form_next_survey_time`, time, optional): This field allows you to enter the time of the next follow-up survey.
*   **Next Follow-up Survey Type** (`exhibitor_survey_form_next_followup_survey_type`, select_one, required): This field asks you to select the type of next follow-up survey. Please choose between "Event" or "Webinar".
*   **Exhibitor Name** (`exhibitor_survey_form_exhibitor_name`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter the name of the exhibitor.
*   **Exhibitor Survey Questions** (`exhibitor_survey_form_exhibitor_survey_questions`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter any additional survey questions for the exhibitor.
*   **Additional Comments** (`exhibitor_survey_form_additional_comments`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
*   **Follow-up Frequency 2** (`exhibitor_survey_form_follow_up_frequency_2`, select_multiple, required): This field also asks you to select the frequency of follow-up surveys. Please choose one or more of the following options: 1 Month, 2 Months, 3 Months.
*   **Exhibitor Name 2** (`exhibitor_survey_form_exhibitor_name_2`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter the name of the second exhibitor.
*   **Additional Comments 2** (`exhibitor_survey_form_additional_comments_2`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
*   **Follow-up Frequency 3** (`exhibitor_survey_form_follow_up_frequency_2`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to select the frequency of follow-up surveys. Please choose one or more of the following options: 1 Month, 2 Months, 3 Months.
*   **Additional Comments 3** (`exhibitor_survey_form_additional_comments_3`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to provide.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you select the correct survey type and follow-up frequency for your event or webinar.
*   Make sure to provide all required fields.
*   If you have any additional comments or information, please enter them in the comments fields.
