# Startup Quarterly Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help startup teams track and report their progress on a quarterly basis, providing essential information to mentors and investors to guide them in making informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select "Yes" or "No" for each field**, indicating whether the progress was made or not.
   - **Track quarterly progress**: Select "Yes" if you've made progress in the quarter, otherwise select "No".
   - **Company information**: Provide a brief description of your company, its products/services, and any relevant details.
   - **Quarterly progress update**: Write a clear and concise update on the progress made in the quarter.
   - **Financial progress**: Include any relevant financial metrics, such as revenue, expenses, or funding received.
   - **Next steps**: Describe the next steps you plan to take in the quarter.
   - **Current challenges**: List any challenges you're currently facing.
   - **Key milestones**: Select "Yes" if you've achieved any key milestones in the quarter, otherwise select "No".
   - **Mentors and investors**: Identify the mentors and investors who are supporting your startup.
   - **Progress rating**: Rate your progress on a scale of 1-10, indicating how well you've performed in the quarter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Track quarterly progress**: This field is used to indicate whether progress was made in the quarter.
  * **Type:** Select_multiple
  * **Required:** No
* **Company information** (`startup_info`): This field is used to provide a brief description of your company, its products/services, and any relevant details.
  * **Type:** Text
  * **Required:** No
* **Quarterly progress update** (`progress_update`): Write a clear and concise update on the progress made in the quarter.
  * **Type:** Text
  * **Required:** No
* **Financial progress** (`financial_progress`): Include any relevant financial metrics, such as revenue, expenses, or funding received.
  * **Type:** Text
  * **Required:** No
* **Next steps** (`next_steps`): Describe the next steps you plan to take in the quarter.
  * **Type:** Text
  * **Required:** No
* **Current challenges** (`current_challenges`): List any challenges you're currently facing.
  * **Type:** Text
  * **Required:** No
* **Key milestones** (`key_milestones`): Select "Yes" if you've achieved any key milestones in the quarter, otherwise select "No".
  * **Type:** Select_multiple
  * **Required:** No
* **Mentors and investors** (`mentors_investors`): Identify the mentors and investors who are supporting your startup.
  * **Type:** Select_one
  * **Required:** No
* **Progress rating** (`progress_rating`): Rate your progress on a scale of 1-10, indicating how well you've performed in the quarter.
  * **Type:** Number
  * **Required:** No

## Tips

* Be clear and concise in your responses to ensure accurate and easy-to-understand information.
* Provide any relevant attachments or supporting documents to back up your claims.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for guidance or clarification.
* This form is for your progress updates, so please be honest and accurate in your reporting.
