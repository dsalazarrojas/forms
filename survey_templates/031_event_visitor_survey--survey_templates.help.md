# Event Visitor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Visitor Survey form is designed to collect feedback and gather information from visitors to your events. This form helps to understand their experience, preferences, and demographics, which can be used to improve future events and tailor them to the audience's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant options for each field and provide any required information. Here's a brief overview of the fields:

- **Event Visitor Survey**: Select the type of survey that best describes your experience.
- **Feedback**: Provide any comments or suggestions you'd like to share about the event.
- **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you and share results.
- **Event ID**: Enter the event ID number you received during the event.
- **Attended with**: Indicate who you attended the event with (alone, friends, family, colleagues, etc.).
- **Attended before**: If you've attended this event before, select how many times.
- **Rated**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event (very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).
- **Rated why**: Provide reasons for your rating (optional).
- **Event type**: Choose the type of event you attended (online or in-person).
- **Visited from**: Indicate how you found out about the event (online, social media, email, or offline promotion).
- **Visited before**: If you've visited this event before, select how many times.
- **Attended with**: Indicate who you attended the event with (alone, friends, family, colleagues, etc.).
- **Visited at**: Enter the date you visited the event.
- **Visited before at**: Enter the date you first visited the event (if you've visited multiple times).
- **Rated at**: Enter the date you rated the event.
- **Time spent**: Optionally, enter the amount of time you spent at the event (in minutes).
- **Visited from others**: Provide any additional information about how you found out about the event from others.
- **Visited from others other**: Provide any other information about how you found out about the event from others.
