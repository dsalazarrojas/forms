# Pet Brand Loyalty Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data on pet owners' behaviors, preferences, and perceptions related to their pet ownership, in order to develop more effective marketing strategies for pet brands.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each page of the form thoroughly, focusing on one page at a time.
2. Review each field carefully, taking into account the options provided.
3. If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Pet Owners Segment
* **Pet Owners Segment** (`pet_owners_segment`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to identify which segment of pet owners you belong to: Cat Owners, Dog Owners, or Other. Please select all that apply.

### Survey Purpose
* **Survey Purpose** (`survey_purpose`, select_one, optional): This field asks you to select the primary reason for taking this survey. Please choose one of the options: Brand Awareness, Purchase Frequency, or Customer Satisfaction.

### Satisfaction Questions
* **Satisfaction Questions** (`satisfaction_questions`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to select the satisfaction questions that are relevant to your pet ownership. Please choose all that apply.

### Loyalty Drivers
* **Loyalty Drivers** (`loyalty_drivers`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to identify the factors that drive your loyalty to a brand. Please choose all that apply: Price, Quality, or Service.

### Demographic Questions
* **Demographic Questions** (`demographic_questions`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to select the demographic questions that are relevant to your pet ownership. Please choose all that apply: Pet Type, Age Range, or Household Income.

### Frequency Purchases
* **Frequency Purchases** (`frequency_purchases`, number, optional): This field asks you to indicate how frequently you purchase pet-related products. Please select a number between 1-10.

### Loyalty Programs
* **Loyalty Programs** (`loyalty_programs`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to confirm if you participate in any loyalty programs. Please choose all that apply: True or False.

### Purchase Frequencies
* **Purchase Frequencies** (`purchase_frequencies`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to indicate how often you purchase pet-related products. Please select one of the options: 1-2, 3-5, or 6-10.
