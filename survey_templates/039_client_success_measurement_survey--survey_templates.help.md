# Client Success Measurement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Client Success Measurement Survey is designed to help us understand our clients' experiences and evaluate our success in supporting them. This survey is essential in helping us identify areas for improvement and ensure that we are meeting your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions that apply to you, and provide as much context as possible in your responses.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best describes your experience.
3. For open-ended text fields, type your answers in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as the client representative.
* **Your Account Manager** (`account_manager`, `text`, optional): If you have a dedicated account manager, please mention their name.
* **How Long Have You Been a Client?** (`relationship_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select the duration of your relationship with us.
	+ Less than 3 months: Your relationship with us is still in its early stages.
	+ 3-6 months: You've been working with us for a few months.
	+ 6-12 months: You've been a client for about a year.
	+ 1-2 years: You've been working with us for at least a year and a half.
	+ 2+ years: You've been a long-term client.
* **Have We Helped You Achieve Your Goals?** (`goals_achievement`, `select_one`, required): Choose how much we've helped you achieve your goals.
	+ Exceeded goals: We've exceeded your goals and expectations.
	+ Achieved goals: We've met your goals and expectations.
	+ Partially achieved: We've made progress towards your goals, but there's still work to be done.
	+ Not yet achieved: You're still working towards your goals.
	+ Goals changed: Your goals have changed, and we've adapted to new requirements.
* **Return on Investment (ROI)** (`roi_achieved`, `select_one`, optional): Select the ROI you've seen.
	+ Significant ROI: You've seen a substantial return on investment.
	+ Good ROI: You've seen a decent return on investment.
	+ Modest ROI: The ROI is moderate.
	+ Minimal ROI: The ROI is minimal.
	+ Negative ROI: You've seen a negative return on investment.
	+ Too early to tell: You're still evaluating the ROI.
* **Success Metrics Met** (`success_metrics_met`, `text`, optional): Describe the specific measures of success you've achieved.
* **Product or Service Adoption Level** (`product_adoption`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of adoption.
	+ Full adoption: You've fully adopted our service.
	+ High usage: You use our service frequently.
	+ Moderate usage: You use our service occasionally.
	+ Low usage: You use our service minimally.
	+ Minimal usage: You've barely used our service.
* **Team Satisfaction with the Service** (`team_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Choose how satisfied your team is with our service.
	+ Very satisfied: Your team is extremely satisfied.
	+ Satisfied: Your team is satisfied.
	+ Neutral: Your team is neutral about our service.
	+ Dissatisfied: Your team is dissatisfied.
	+ Very dissatisfied: Your team is extremely dissatisfied.
* **Overall Customer Health Score** (`customer_health_score`, `number`, required): Rate your overall health score on a scale of 1-10.
* **Any Risk Factors or Concerns** (`risk_factors`, `text`, optional): Mention any potential issues that might affect our relationship.
* **Expansion or Upsell Opportunity** (`expansion_opportunity`, `select_one`, optional): Select your interest in additional services or products.
	+ Yes, definitely interested: You're interested in expanding our services.
	+ Possibly interested: You might be interested in expanding our services.
	+ Not interested at this time: You're not interested in expanding our services.
	+ Currently evaluating: You're evaluating our services for potential expansion.
* **Likelihood to Continue Service** (`retention_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Choose how likely you are to continue with us.
	+ Definitely yes: You're certain you'll continue with us.
	+ Probably yes: You're likely to continue with us.
	+ Uncertain: You're not sure about continuing with us.
	+ Probably not: You're unlikely to continue with us.
	+ Definitely not: You're certain you won't continue with us.
* **Likelihood to Recommend Us** (`recommendation_likelihood`, `number`, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend us on a scale of 1-10.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, optional): Suggest areas where we could improve.
* **What Enabled Your Success?** (`success_enablers`, `text`, optional): Describe the key factors that contributed to your success.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about our service.

Please answer this survey as accurately and thoroughly as possible to help us better serve you.
