# Adhesive Performance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback and performance evaluation data from users who have tested an adhesive formulation. Your input will help us improve the formulation and performance of our adhesives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your information as a product tester.
2. Provide details about the adhesive product you tested, including its name and batch ID.
3. Answer the questions about the test conditions and performance of the adhesive.
4. Provide your overall recommendation for the formulation for the application you tested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`tester_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it should appear on the survey report.
* **Company and Job Role** (`organization_role`, `text`, required): Enter your company name and job role as related to the test.
* **Adhesive Product Name / Batch ID** (`adhesive_product_id`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the adhesive product and its batch ID that you tested.
* **Substrate Material (What were you bonding?)** (`substrate_material`, `select_one`, required): Select from the following options:
	+ Metal to Metal
	+ Plastic to Plastic
	+ Metal to Plastic
	+ Wood / Composites
	+ Glass / Ceramics
	+ Other
* **Application Method** (`application_method`, `select_one`, required): Select from the following options:
	+ Manual Dispensing
	+ Automated / Robotic Spray
	+ Brush / Roller
	+ Film / Tape Application
* **Rating: Ease of Application / Workability** (`rating_workability`, `number`, required): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) how easy or difficult it was to apply the adhesive.
* **Rating: Curing / Set Time Satisfaction** (`rating_cure_time`, `number`, required): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) how satisfied you are with the curing time of the adhesive.
* **Rating: Initial Bond Strength** (`rating_bond_strength`, `number`, required): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) the initial bond strength of the adhesive.
* **Observed Failure Mode (if tested to failure)** (`failure_mode_observed`, `select_one`, required): Select from the following options:
	+ Cohesive Failure (Adhesive split)
	+ Adhesive Failure (Clean separation from substrate)
	+ Substrate Failure (Material broke before bond)
	+ No failure observed
* **Was the bond exposed to environmental stress?** (`environmental_testing`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ High Temperature
	+ High Humidity / Moisture
	+ Chemical / Solvent Exposure
	+ UV Light Exposure
	+ None
* **Would you recommend this formulation for this application?** (`overall_recommendation`, `select_one`, required): Select from the following options:
	+ Highly Recommend
	+ Recommend with modifications
	+ Do not recommend
* **Additional Observations or Improvement Suggestions** (`qualitative_feedback`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about the adhesive or the testing process.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you have tested the adhesive to failure, please provide as much detail as possible about the failure mode.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please select "Other" or "Unknown" and provide a brief explanation.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey! Your input is valuable to us.
