# Tech Training Program Effectiveness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from participants to assess the effectiveness of the tech training program. It will help us understand what worked well and what areas need improvement to enhance future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into several sections, each representing an aspect of the training program. To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and select the best answer that reflects your experience with the program.
2. Fill in all the required fields (indicated by the * symbol).
3. Use the provided options to select your responses.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all sections.
