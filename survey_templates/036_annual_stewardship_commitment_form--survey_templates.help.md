# Annual Stewardship Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Stewardship Commitment Form is designed to help our organization understand your annual support and engagement with our programs and initiatives. This form will help us tailor our communication and stewardship efforts to best meet your needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your information accurately and completely.
2. Choose your preferred communication method and frequency.
3. Select the type of supporter you are (e.g., individual, corporate sponsor, foundation, etc.).
4. Indicate your annual commitment level and specify the amount if applicable.
5. Choose how you'd like to be recognized for your support.
6. If interested, express your willingness to participate in naming opportunities or provide information for future opportunities.
7. Confirm your commitment agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`supporter_name`): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`supporter_email`): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`supporter_phone`): Enter your contact phone number (optional but helpful for regular updates).
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`): If you are a corporate sponsor, foundation, or other business-related supporter, please enter your organization's name.
* **Type of Supporter** (`supporter_type`): Select one of the provided options (individual, corporate sponsor, foundation, volunteer, partner, or other).
* **History with our organization** (`engagement_history`): Choose how long you've been involved with our organization (new supporter or 1-10+ years).
* **Annual Commitment Level** (`commitment_level`): Select the level of your annual commitment (major gift, significant, substantial, regular, friend, in-kind, or volunteer commitment).
* **Specific Commitment Amount** (`commitment_amount`): If you've selected a commitment level, you can specify the exact amount.
* **Commitment Timing** (`commitment_timing`): Choose how you'd like to provide your commitment (lump sum, quarterly installments, monthly, year-end, or flexible timing).
* **Purpose or Area of Support** (`giving_purpose`): Specify the program or initiative you'd like to support.
* **Preferred Impact Area** (`impact_area`): Choose one of the provided areas of interest (education, healthcare, environment, community services, research, arts, or other).
* **Annual Volunteer Hours Commitment** (`volunteer_hours`): If you are committing to volunteer, specify the number of hours.
* **Volunteer Roles or Activities** (`volunteer_roles`): Describe the types of volunteer activities you are interested in or willing to participate in.
* **In-Kind Donation** (`in_kind_donation`): If you are offering goods, services, or expertise, describe them here.
* **What Motivates Your Support** (`motivation`): Explain why you support our organization.
* **Preferred Recognition** (`impact_recognition`): Choose how you'd like to be acknowledged for your support (public, private, no recognition, or confidential donor).
* **Interest in Naming Opportunity** (`naming_opportunity`): If interested, express your willingness to participate in naming opportunities.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`communication_preference`): Select your preferred method of communication (email, phone, mail, in-person, or no preference).
* **Preferred Update Frequency** (`communication_frequency`): Choose how often you'd like to hear from us (monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, annually, or only impact reports).
* **Interest in Legacy or Planned Giving** (`legacy_giving`): If interested, express your willingness to explore legacy or planned giving opportunities.
* **Commitment Agreement** (`commitment_agreement`): Confirm your agreement to this stewardship commitment.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_notes`): Share any additional information or ask questions about your support.
