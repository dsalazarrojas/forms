# Remote Support Tool Selection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Remote Support Tool Selection Survey is designed to help us choose the best remote support tool for your team's needs. This survey will guide you through a series of questions to help us understand your preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages below, and fill out each field according to the instructions provided. Make sure to read each field's explanation to ensure you provide accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Template** (`Select Template`, `select_one`, required): Choose the template that best suits your needs from the list of available templates.
* **Tool Name** (`Tool Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the tool you are selecting.
* **Key Features** (`Key Features`, `text`, optional): Describe the key features you are looking for in the tool.
* **Selection Criteria** (`Selection Criteria`, `text`, optional): Describe the criteria you will use to select the tool.
* **Decision Guidelines** (`Decision Guidelines`, `text`, optional): Describe any guidelines that will influence your tool selection decision.
* **Current Tools** (`Current Tools`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the tools currently being used by your team.
* **IT Lead** (`IT Lead`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the IT lead responsible for the tool selection process.
* **Business Lead** (`Business Lead`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the business lead responsible for the tool selection process.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `text`, required): Enter the tool that will be assigned to your team.

Note: This form is intended to gather information to help make an informed decision about which remote support tool to select. Please ensure the information provided is accurate and complete.
