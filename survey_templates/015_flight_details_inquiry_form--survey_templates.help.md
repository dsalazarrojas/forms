# flight_details_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The flight_details_inquiry_form is designed to collect data about flight details. This form is useful when you need to gather information related to flights, including passenger or traveler information, flight numbers, or dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the form pages to the desired page.
2. Enter the required information for each field.
3. Submit the form when you have finished filling out all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **traveler_info** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter information about the traveler(s) on the flight.
* **flight_details** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter any additional details about the flight.
* **passenger_info** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter information about the passengers on the flight.
* **flight_number** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter the flight number (flight number or airline code).
* **flight_date** (Date, Optional)
	+ Enter the flight date in YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* If you have any questions, contact form@gic.mx.
