# Online Platform User Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation from users about their experience with an online platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Platform Name** field with the name of the online platform you are evaluating.
2. Provide your **Overall Rating** on a scale of 1-5.
3. Describe your **User Experience** in the text field.
4. List any **Issues Encountered** during your use of the platform in the text field.
5. Provide any **Suggestions** you have for improvement in the text field.
6. Enter your **Email** address.
7. Provide your **Phone Number**.
8. Rate your **Overall Satisfaction** on a scale of 1-5.
9. Enter your **Additional Feedback** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Platform Name** (`platform_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the online platform you are evaluating.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the platform on a scale of 1-5.
* **User Experience** (`user_experience`, text, required): Describe your experience with the platform.
* **Issues Encountered** (`issues_encountered`, text, required): List any issues you encountered during your use of the platform.
* **Suggestions** (`any_suggestions`, text, required): Provide any suggestions you have for improvement.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, required): Enter any additional feedback you have about the platform.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select one, required): Select one option that best describes your experience.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last use of the platform.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your last use of the platform.
