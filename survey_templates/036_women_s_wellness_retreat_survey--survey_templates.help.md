# Women's Wellness Retreat Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from participants to help improve future wellness retreats.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked as such.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
3. Take your time to answer each question and do not rush.
4. Use a computer or device with internet access to access the form.
5. Submit your form when you are finished.
