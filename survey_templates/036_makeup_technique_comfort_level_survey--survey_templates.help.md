# Makeup Technique Comfort Level Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the comfort level of clients with various aspects of a makeup service, including their skill level, confidence, learning interests, comfort level, service planning, product recommendations, content planning, class scheduling, client feedback, survey completion, tool usage, email preferences, contact preferences, client communication, and communication frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and select the options that best fit your preferences and comfort level with the corresponding aspects of the makeup service. If a field is not relevant to you, feel free to skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Skill Level** (`Client Skill Level`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the level of skill you feel comfortable with: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced.
* **Client Confidence** (`Client Confidence`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Rate your confidence level with the makeup service: High, Medium, Low.
* **Learning Interests** (`Learning Interests`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check all that apply: Techniques, Products, Trends, Business.
* **Comfort Level** (`Comfort Level`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Rate your comfort level with the makeup service: Very High, High, Medium, Low.
* **Service Planning** (`Service Planning`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the type of service planning you prefer: Classes, Consultations, Workshops.
* **Product Recommendations** (`Product Recommendations`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check all the types of products you are interested in: Skincare, Makeup, Hair, Accessories.
* **Content Planning** (`Content Planning`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the type of content you prefer: Blog Posts, Videos, Social Media.
* **Class Scheduling** (`Class Scheduling`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the time of day for classes: Morning, Afternoon, Evening.
* **Client Feedback** (`Client Feedback`, `note`, required: false)
	+ Please leave a note with any feedback or suggestions you have about the makeup service.
* **Survey Completion** (`Survey Completion`, `date`, required: false)
	+ Select the date you completed this survey.
* **Tool Usage** (`Tool Usage`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Rate how frequently you use the tools provided by the service: Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely.
* **Email Preferences** (`Email Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check all the times of day you prefer to receive emails: Morning, Afternoon, Evening.
* **Contact Preferences** (`Contact Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check all the ways you prefer to be contacted: Phone, Email, Social Media.
* **Client Communication** (`Client Communication`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Rate the quality of communication with the service: Very Good, Good, Fair.
* **Communication Frequency** (`Communication Frequency`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Rate how frequently you receive communication from the service: Often, Occasionally, Rarely.
