# Friendship Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your friendships, focusing on various aspects such as the type of friendships you have, what you value in friendships, and how you maintain and support each other.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please read each question carefully and answer according to your experiences and feelings. You can select multiple options for questions that allow for multiple selections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What kind of friendships do you have?** (`friendship_type`): Please select all the types of friendships you have, e.g. "Yes" for close friends, acquaintances, or online friends.
* **Do you need support from friends?** (`support_needs`): Select "Yes" if you often need emotional, financial, or other types of support from your friends.
* **What social skills do you use in friendships?** (`social_skills`): Describe some of the social skills you use to maintain and deepen your friendships, such as active listening, empathy, or conflict resolution.
* **How do you perceive yourself in a friendship?** (`self_concept`): Select "Yes" if you feel understood, valued, and accepted by your friends.
* **Do you feel supported in your friendships?** (`friendship_support`): Select "Yes" if you feel that your friends support you emotionally, financially, or in other ways.
* **How do you communicate with friends?** (`communication`): Select "Yes" for methods like phone, messaging, or video calls.
* **How much trust do you have in your friends?** (`trust`): Select "Yes" if you trust them completely.
* **How much do you disclose to friends?** (`self_disclosure`): Select "Yes" if you share your thoughts, feelings, or experiences with your friends.
* **Self-awareness in friendships** (`self_awareness`): Select "Yes" if you have a good understanding of yourself and your relationships with others.
* **How much do you share with friends?** (`self_disclosure_2`): Select "Yes" if you share your thoughts, feelings, or experiences with your friends.
* **How do you resolve conflicts with friends?** (`conflict_resolution`): Select "Yes" if you use methods like active listening, compromise, or mediation.
* **What values in a friendship are important to you?** (`friendship_quality`): Select "Yes" for qualities like loyalty, trust, or honesty.
* **How do you maintain friendships?** (`self_maintenance`): Describe how you make time, effort, or other resources available to maintain your friendships.
* **Do you get support from friends?** (`support_from_friends`): Select "Yes" if you receive emotional, financial, or other types of support from your friends.
* **How often do you meet with friends?** (`friendship_frequency`): Select a date for how often you meet with friends, e.g. daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Do you care about what friends think?** (`self_care`): Select "Yes" if you value and consider your friends' opinions.
* **How satisfied are you with your friendships?** (`friendship_satisfaction`): Select "Yes" for a high level of satisfaction.
* **Do you get support from others?** (`support_from_others`): Select "Yes" if you receive emotional, financial, or other types of support from others.
* **How helpful are your friends?** (`self_helpfulness`): Select "Yes" if your friends are supportive and helpful.
* **Friendship Quality 2** (`friendship_quality_2`): Select "Yes" for qualities like loyalty, trust, or honesty.
* **How helpful are you to friends?** (`self_helpfulness_2`): Select "Yes" if you are supportive and helpful to your friends.
* **Support Needs 2** (`support_needs_2`): Select "Yes" for needs like emotional, financial, or other types of support.
* **Self Concept 2** (`self_concept_2`): Select "Yes" if you feel understood, valued, and accepted by your friends.
* **Self Maintenance 2** (`self_maintenance_2`): Describe how you make time, effort, or other resources available to maintain your friendships.

Note: Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
