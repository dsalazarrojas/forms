# Remote Employee Connectivity And Morale Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help remote employees check in and provide information about their work environment and connectivity. It aims to facilitate communication and understanding between employees, managers, and teams. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate representation of your work situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Enter your name in the "name" field.
3. Select your department from the "department" dropdown menu.
4. Answer the "work_location" question by selecting the relevant option(s).
5. Enter your manager's contact information in the "manager_contact" field.
6. Select the team leads you are working with in the "team_leads" field.
7. Select the colleagues you are working with in the "colleagues" field.
8. Select the collaborators you are working with in the "collaborators" field.
9. Select the team members you are working with in the "team_members" field.
10. Select the direct reports you are responsible for in the "direct_reports" field.
11. Select the indirect reports you are responsible for in the "indirect_reports" field.
12. Select the person you report to in the "reports_to_manager" field.
13. Answer the "work_from_home" question by selecting "True" or "False".
14. If you work from home, enter your work hours in the "work_hours" field.
15. If you work from home, enter your work hours start time in the "work_hours_start" field.
16. If you work from home, enter your work hours end time in the "work_hours_end" field.
17. If you work from home, enter your work hours break time in the "work_hours_break" field.
18. Select your time zone from the "time_zone" dropdown menu.
19. Provide any additional comments in the "comments" field.
20. Finally, provide any feedback in the "feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief welcome message explaining the purpose of the form.
* **Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to, e.g., Marketing, Sales, or Customer Support.
* **Department**: (Select One) Marketing, Sales, Customer Support
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title, if applicable.
* **Work Location**: Select the location(s) where you work, e.g., On-site, Remote, or Hybrid.
* **Manager Contact**: Enter your manager's contact information, if applicable.
* **Team Leads**: Select the team leads you are working with.
* **Colleagues**: Select the colleagues you are working with.
* **Collaborators**: Select the collaborators you are working with.
* **Team Members**: Select the team members you are working with.
* **Direct Reports**: Select the people you are directly responsible for.
* **Indirect Reports**: Select the people you are indirectly responsible for.
* **Reports to Manager**: Select the person you report to.
* **Work from Home**: Answer whether you work from home, "True" or "False".
* **Work Hours**: If you work from home, enter your work hours.
* **Work Hours Start**: If you work from home, enter your work hours start time.
* **Work Hours End**: If you work from home, enter your work hours end time.
* **Work Hours Break**: If you work from home, enter your work hours break time.
* **Time Zone**: Select your time zone, e.g., EST, CST, MST, or PST.
* **Team Meetings**: Provide any additional comments about team meetings.
* **Team Meetings Frequency**: Select the frequency of team meetings, e.g., Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Team Meetings Time**: If you have any additional comments about team meetings time.
* **Team Meetings Date**: If you have any additional comments about team meetings date.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your work situation.
