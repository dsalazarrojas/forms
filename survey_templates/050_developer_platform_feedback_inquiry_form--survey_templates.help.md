# Developer Platform Feedback Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from developers who use our platform. Your input will help us improve the platform and provide a better experience for all developers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose the category that best describes your feedback (e.g., Bug, Feature, or Feedback).
2. Briefly describe your inquiry in the text field.
3. Specify what you would like to see improved in the text field.
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
5. Optionally, provide your phone number for follow-up.
6. Choose how often you visit our website (e.g., Every day, Few times a week, or Rarely).
7. If you have any other comments or questions, please provide them in the text field.
8. Finally, submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Please select the category that best describes your feedback (e.g., Bug, Feature, or Feedback).
* **Please describe your inquiry** (`question_one`, text, required): Type a brief description of the issue or suggestion you have for our platform.
* **What would you like to see improved?** (`question_two`, text, required): Specify what you would like to see improved in our platform.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Optionally, provide your phone number for follow-up.
* **How often do you visit our website?** (`question_three`, select_one, required): Choose how often you visit our website (e.g., Every day, Few times a week, or Rarely).
* **Do you have any other comments or questions?** (`question_four`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click this button to submit your feedback. (Note: This is a required field in the sense that you must click the button to complete the form, but it is not a field that requires input.)
