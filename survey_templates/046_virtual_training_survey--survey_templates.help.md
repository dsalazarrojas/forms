# Virtual Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Training Survey form is designed to gather feedback and input from learners on the virtual training session. This form allows you to understand what learners thought of the training content, experience, and any suggestions they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the **Survey Title** field with a brief title of the virtual training session.
2. In the **Input Table 1** field, enter any relevant information you wish to provide about the training session.
3. In the **Input Table 2** field, enter any additional information you wish to provide.
4. The **Long Text 1** field is for you to enter any detailed comments or feedback on the training session.
5. Select one option for the **Single Choice 1** field that best describes your opinion or experience.
6. Select one option for the **Single Choice 2** field that best describes your opinion or experience.
7. Enter your email address in the **Email** field if you would like to receive any further communication about the training session.
8. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field if you would like to be contacted by phone.
9. Select a date and time of completion from the **Date** and **Time** fields.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments in the **Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Title**: Enter a brief title of the virtual training session.
* **Input Table 1**: Enter any relevant information you wish to provide about the training session.
* **Input Table 2**: Enter any additional information you wish to provide.
* **Long Text 1**: Enter any detailed comments or feedback on the training session.
* **Single Choice 1**: Select one option that best describes your opinion or experience.
* **Single Choice 2**: Select one option that best describes your opinion or experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to receive any further communication about the training session.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Date**: Select a date of completion of the virtual training session.
* **Time**: Select a time of completion of the virtual training session.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the training session.
