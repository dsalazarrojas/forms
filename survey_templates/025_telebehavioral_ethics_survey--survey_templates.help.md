# Telebehavioral Ethics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telebehavioral Ethics Survey is a tool to collect feedback from healthcare providers on their experiences and challenges faced while working with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question in the spaces provided.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Organization**: <small>Please enter a brief description of your organization (e.g., name, location, etc.)</small>
* **Healthcare Provider**: <small>Please enter your name and title/position (e.g., Dr., MD, RN, etc.)</small>
* **Patient ID**: <small>Please enter the patient's ID number or any relevant identifying information.</small>
* **Contact Name**: <small>Please enter your name and title/position (e.g., Dr., MD, RN, etc.)</small>
* **Contact Phone**: <small>Please enter your contact phone number in a format like "123-456-7890".</small>
* **Contact Email**: <small>Please enter your work email address in a format like "johndoe@example.com".</small>
* **Challenge Faced**: <small>Please enter a brief description of the challenge you faced while working with our organization.</small>
* **Challenge Description**: <small>Please provide a detailed description of the challenge you faced, including any relevant details or context.</small>
* **Challenge Frequency**: <small>Please enter a brief description of how often you faced the challenge, e.g., "daily", "occasionally", "rarely" etc.</small>

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* If you have already submitted a survey, please do not repeat the process.
