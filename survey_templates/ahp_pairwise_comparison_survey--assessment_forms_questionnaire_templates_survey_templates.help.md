# AHP Pairwise Comparison Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the AHP Pairwise Comparison Survey, a method used in decision-making scenarios. Its purpose is to evaluate the experience and effectiveness of this method in real-world applications.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill in your complete name in the **Full name** field.
*   Enter your professional email in the **Email address** field.
*   Select the date you completed the survey in the **Date** field.
*   Briefly describe the decision problem you were evaluating in the **Decision context** field.
*   Enter the number of criteria you were evaluating in the **Number of criteria being evaluated** field.
*   Select the most important criterion for your decision in the **Which criterion is most important in your decision?** field.
*   On a scale of 1-9, rate how strongly you favor the most important criterion over the least important in the **On a scale of 1-9, how strongly do you favor the most important criterion over the least important?** field.
*   Indicate which method you used for pairwise comparisons in the **Which method did you use for pairwise comparisons?** field.
*   Enter your calculated consistency ratio in the **What was your calculated consistency ratio?** field. (Leave this field blank if you didn't calculate it.)
*   Rate how confident you are in your judgments in the **How confident are you in your judgments?** field.
*   Select all factors that influenced your pairwise comparisons in the **Which factors influenced your pairwise comparisons?** field.
*   Rate your level of satisfaction with the AHP Pairwise Comparison Survey in the **How satisfied are you with AHP Pairwise Comparison Survey?** field.
*   Rate your overall experience of the survey on a scale of 1-10 in the **Overall rating (1-10)** field.
*   Select all aspects that applied to your experience with AHP Pairwise Comparison Survey in the **Which aspects applied to your experience with AHP Pairwise Comparison Survey?** field.
*   Enter the approximate time you spent completing the survey in the **Approximately how much time did you spend completing the pairwise comparisons?** field.
*   Indicate how likely you are to recommend the AHP Pairwise Comparison Survey to others in the **How likely are you to recommend this method to others?** field.
*   Finally, add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement in the **What would you improve or change about AHP Pairwise Comparison Survey?** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name**: Enter your complete name.
*   **Email address**: Enter your professional email.
*   **Date**: Select the date you completed the survey.
*   **Decision context**: Briefly describe the decision problem you were evaluating.
*   **Number of criteria being evaluated**: Enter the number of criteria you were evaluating.
*   **Which criterion is most important in your decision?**: Select the most significant factor for your decision.
*   **On a scale of 1-9, how strongly do you favor the most important criterion over the least important?**: Rate your preference on a scale of 1-9.
*   **Which method did you use for pairwise comparisons?**: Indicate which method you used for pairwise comparisons.
*   **What was your calculated consistency ratio?**: Enter your calculated consistency ratio.
*   **How confident are you in your judgments?**: Rate your confidence level in your judgments.
*   **Which factors influenced your pairwise comparisons?**: Select all factors that influenced your comparisons.
*   **How satisfied are you with AHP Pairwise Comparison Survey?**: Rate your satisfaction level with the survey.
*   **Overall rating (1-10)**: Rate your overall experience with the survey.
*   **Which aspects applied to your experience with AHP Pairwise Comparison Survey?**: Select all aspects that applied to your experience.
*   **Approximately how much time did you spend completing the pairwise comparisons?**: Enter the approximate time you spent on the survey.
*   **How likely are you to recommend this method to others?**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend the survey.
*   **What would you improve or change about AHP Pairwise Comparison Survey?**: Add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
*   **Additional comments or notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your experience with the survey.

## Tips
