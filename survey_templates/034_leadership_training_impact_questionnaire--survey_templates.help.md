# Leadership Training Impact Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Training Impact Questionnaire is a form designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness and impact of the leadership training session. The form helps us to understand how the session was received by participants and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible. You can use the hints provided for each question for guidance. If you're unsure about any of the questions, please ask for help or clarification. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date**: The date of the leadership training session.
* **Session Time**: The time of the leadership training session.
* **Training Session ID**: A unique identifier for the leadership training session.
* **Participant Name**: The name of the participant who attended the leadership training session.
* **Participant Email**: The email address of the participant who attended the leadership training session.
* **Participant Phone**: The phone number of the participant who attended the leadership training session.
* **Feedback on Content**: Choose one option to provide feedback on the content of the leadership training session.
* **Feedback on Presentation**: Select all the options that apply for feedback on the presentation of the leadership training session.
* **Feedback on Trainer**: Choose one option to provide feedback on the trainer of the leadership training session.
* **Feedback on Location**: Choose one option to provide feedback on the location of the leadership training session.
* **Feedback on Equipment**: Choose one option to provide feedback on the equipment used in the leadership training session.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Choose one option to rate your overall satisfaction with the leadership training session.
* **Likelihood to Recommend**: Choose one option to rate your likelihood to recommend the leadership training session.
* **Will Participate Again**: Choose one option to indicate whether you would participate in the leadership training session again.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about the leadership training session.
* **Participant Organization**: The name of the participant's organization.
* **Participant Title**: The title of the participant.
* **Participant Department**: The department of the participant.
* **Participant Job Title**: The job title of the participant.
* **Participant Manager**: The participant's manager's name.
* **Participant Reporting Manager**: The participant's reporting manager's name.
* **Participant Supervisor**: The participant's supervisor's name.
* **Participant Division**: The division of the participant.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by the participant who attended the leadership training session. 

Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and data required to complete the form before proceeding. If you need help or clarification on any of the questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the form administrators.
