# Pet Care Product Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on pet care products from customers to help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the pet care product you are providing feedback on in the **Pet Care Product Name** field.
2. If you have an image of the product, please upload it in the **Product Image** field.
3. Rate the product's quality using the **Product Rating** field, selecting from the options: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. Share your review of the product using the **Product Review** field, selecting from the options: Excellent, Satisfactory, Average, or Unsatisfactory.
5. If you want to provide more detailed feedback on the product's quality, you can enter a numerical value in the **Product Rating (Scale)** field.
6. If the product's price is relevant to your feedback, you can enter it in the **Product Price** field.
7. Finally, provide feedback on the **Product Quality** field, indicating how long it takes to deliver the product's quality (e.g., minutes, hours, days, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Care Product Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the pet care product you are providing feedback on.
* **Product Image (Text)**: Upload an image of the product to help us better understand your feedback.
* **Product Rating (Select Multiple)**: Rate the product's quality using one or more of the following options: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Product Review (Select One)**: Share your review of the product, selecting from the options: Excellent, Satisfactory, Average, or Unsatisfactory.
* **Product Rating (Scale) (Number)**: If you want to provide more detailed feedback on the product's quality, enter a numerical value here.
* **Product Price (Number)**: Enter the product's price if it's relevant to your feedback.
* **Product Quality (Time)**: Provide feedback on the product's quality by indicating how long it takes to deliver it (e.g., minutes, hours, days, etc.).
