# Hotel Guest Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Guest Check Out Form is designed to collect feedback from hotel guests to improve the quality of service and amenities provided during their stay. It's essential to provide accurate and honest feedback to help us understand your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the necessary information about your stay, including your arrival and expected departure dates.
2. If you have any comments or feedback, please share them in the text fields provided.
3. Rate your satisfaction with our room cleanliness, room service, and overall stay.
4. Select the contact method you prefer to receive communication from us.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you arrived at the hotel.
* **Expected Departure Date** (`expected_departure_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you expect to depart from the hotel.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, required): Enter the number of the room you were assigned during your stay.
* **Check Out Time** (`check_out_time`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to check out of the hotel.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted by.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person you would like to be contacted by.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Share any comments or feedback about your stay.
* **Room Cleanliness Survey** (`room_cleanliness_survey`, select_multiple, required): Rate the cleanliness of your room.
* **Room Service Survey** (`room_service_survey`, select_multiple, required): Rate the quality of our room service.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your stay.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Share any additional feedback or comments.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Share any other comments or feedback.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select_one, required): Select the contact method you prefer.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, text, required): Enter the contact method you prefer (e.g., phone, email, etc.).
* **Contact Method Details** (`contact_method_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about your preferred contact method.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Share any additional comments.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Please sign this form to confirm your identity.
* **Photo ID** (`photo_id`, number, required): Enter your identification number.
* **Photo ID Details** (`photo_id_details`, text, required): Enter any details about your identification number.
* **Photo Upload** (`photo_upload`, text, required): Upload a photo of your identification.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of contact information you are comfortable sharing.
* **Check-out Date** (`check_out_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to check out of the hotel.
* **Contact Method 2** (`contact_method_2`, text, required): Enter the method of contact you prefer.
* **Survey ID** (`survey_id`, text, required): Enter your survey ID.

Note: Some fields are marked as "required" but may not be necessary for every user to fill out. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
