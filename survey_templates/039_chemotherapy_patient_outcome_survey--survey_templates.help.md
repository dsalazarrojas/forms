# Chemotherapy Patient Outcome Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chemotherapy Patient Outcome Survey is designed to collect information about patients' physical and emotional well-being, treatment effectiveness, and overall experience with chemotherapy. This survey helps healthcare teams understand patient outcomes, identify potential issues, and provide personalized support to improve care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient identification number in the "Patient ID" field.
2. Select the date of this survey from the calendar.
3. Enter your birth date in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Select your gender from the provided options.
5. Choose the type of cancer being treated.
6. Indicate the stage of cancer at diagnosis.
7. Enter the date you started chemotherapy.
8. Select the current treatment cycle.
9. If applicable, provide the name of your chemotherapy regimen.
10. Select how often you receive treatment.
11. Rate your fatigue level over the past week.
12. Rate your nausea level over the past week.
13. Rate your pain level over the past week.
14. Indicate if you have experienced appetite changes.
15. Describe any changes in your weight.
16. Indicate if you have experienced hair loss.
17. Select how your sleep quality has been.
18. Indicate if you have experienced mood changes.
19. Select how your work has been affected by treatment.
20. Describe how your daily activities have been affected by treatment.
21. Choose how you feel about your support system.
22. Select all the side effects you have experienced.
23. Rate how well side effects are being managed.
24. Rate how well you communicate with your care team.
25. Rate your quality of life.
26. Select how effective you feel treatment is.
27. Indicate if you would recommend this treatment center.
28. Provide any additional support you may need.
29. Share any concerns or questions with your care team.
30. Add any additional comments about your experience.
