# exhibition_visitor_experience_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Exhibition Visitor Experience Assessment Form** is used to gather feedback from exhibition visitors on their experience. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions below and provide honest and detailed feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer all the questions, even if you're not sure or have no opinion.
3. Use the provided options as a guide, but feel free to provide more detailed explanations if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibition Visitor Experience Assessment** (`exhibition_visitor_experience_assessment`):
	+ This is a text field where you can describe your overall experience at the exhibition.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`):
	+ Rate your overall experience at the exhibition on a scale from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Exhibition Stand** (`exhibition_stand`):
	+ Choose how good you found the exhibition stand, with options:
		- Excellent
		- Average
		- Poor
* **Event Attendant** (`event_attendant`):
	+ Indicate if you attended the event, with options:
		- True
		- False
		- Not Sure
		- Prefer not to say
* **Exhibition Stand Rating** (`exhibition_stand_rating`):
	+ This field is not applicable as it's not present in the form. You can skip it.
* **Catering** (`catering`):
	+ Provide any comments or feedback on the catering services, if you have any.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`):
	+ Indicate how likely you are to recommend this exhibition, with options:
		- Highly Likely
		- Somewhat Likely
		- Not at all Likely
* **Event Attendance** (`event_attendance`):
	+ Describe your attendance at the exhibition, if you have any comments or feedback.
* **Exhibition Visitor Experience 5 points** (`exhibition_visitor_experience_5_points`):
	+ Rate your experience at the exhibition on a scale from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Exhibition Visitor Experience 5 points 2** (`exhibition_visitor_experience_5_points_2`):
	+ This field is identical to the previous one, it seems there's a duplication. You can skip it.

## Tips
* Be honest and detailed in your answers.
* If you're unsure or have no opinion, please provide a "Not sure" or "No opinion" answer.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please feel free to add them in the "Catering" field.
