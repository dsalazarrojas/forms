# Business Profit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a survey for business owners to provide information about their business profit data. It will help our internal staff understand your business needs and provide guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as requested and submit. If you need help with any of the fields, feel free to ask our staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Owner Name** (`business_owner_name`, text, required)
  Your full name as the business owner.
* **Email Address** (`owner_email`, email, required)
  The contact email of the business owner.
* **Phone Number** (`owner_phone`, text, required)
  The contact phone number of the business owner.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required)
  The name of the business.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, select one, required)
  Select the type of business: Retail, Service, Manufacturing, Technology, Consulting, or Other.
* **Years in Operation** (`years_in_operation`, number, required)
  The number of years the business has been in operation.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, optional)
  The current number of employees in the business.
* **Current Annual Revenue** (`current_annual_revenue`, number, required)
  The last year's revenue of the business.
* **Annual Operating Costs** (`annual_operating_costs`, number, required)
  The total operating costs of the business.
* **Current Profit Margin** (`current_profit_margin`, number, optional)
  The current profit margin as a percentage.
* **Revenue Growth Rate** (`revenue_growth_rate`, number, optional)
  The year-over-year revenue growth rate as a percentage.
* **Primary Revenue Sources** (`primary_revenue_sources`, text, required)
  The primary sources of revenue for the business.
* **Cost Structure Analysis** (`cost_structure_analysis`, text, required)
  A description of the major expenses of the business.
* **Profitability Challenges** (`profitability_challenges`, text, required)
  A description of the challenges the business is facing with profit.
* **Cost Reduction Opportunities** (`cost_reduction_opportunities`, text, optional)
  Description of areas where costs can be reduced.
* **Revenue Increase Strategies** (`revenue_increase_strategies`, text, optional)
  Description of strategies for revenue increase.
* **Profit Goals - Next Year** (`profit_goals_next_year`, number, optional)
  The target profit for the next year.
* **Profit Goals - 3 Years** (`profit_goals_three_years`, number, optional)
  The target profit for 3 years.
* **Current Pricing Strategy** (`pricing_strategy`, text, optional)
  Description of the current pricing strategy of the business.
* **Competitive Positioning** (`competitive_positioning`, text, optional)
  Description of the competitive position of the business.
* **Business Operational Efficiency** (`business_efficiency`, number, optional)
  A rating of the business operational efficiency (1-10).
* **Type of Financial Help Needed** (`financial_help_needed`, text, optional)
  Description of the kind of financial help the business needs.
