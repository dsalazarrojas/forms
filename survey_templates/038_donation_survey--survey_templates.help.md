# Donation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Donation Survey" form is designed to collect information from donors, including their contact details, gift details, and preferred language for communication. This form helps us understand donor behavior and preferences, enabling us to better serve them and tailor our communication accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Donor Information" section with your details.
2. Select your preferred language for communication.
3. Choose your contact method and provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
4. Indicate your gift frequency and amount.
5. Enter the gift details, including the date and time of the gift.
6. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information (donation_survey)**: Enter your name and any other relevant donor information.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Provide your contact information, including any additional contact details you'd like to share with us.
* **Gift Details (gift_details)**: Describe your gift, including any specific details you'd like us to know.
* **Preferred Language (preferred_language)**: Select your preferred language for communication (EN, ES, FR, ZH, ZH HANT).
* **Contact Method (contact_method)**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone).
* **Contact Name (contact_name)**: Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Provide your phone number.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Address (contact_address)**: Enter your address.
* **Gift Frequency (gift_frequency)**: Choose how often you make gifts (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually).
* **Gift Amount (gift_amount)**: Enter the amount of your gift.
* **Gift Date (gift_date)**: Enter the date of your gift.
* **Time of Day (time_of_day)**: Enter the time of your gift.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy.
- If you have any additional comments or information, feel free to add them to the "Donor Information" field.
