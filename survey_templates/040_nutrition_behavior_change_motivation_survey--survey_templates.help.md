# Nutrition Behavior Change Motivation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Nutrition Behavior Change Motivation Survey is designed to gather information about your motivation level and responses regarding the nutrition behavior change program. This survey is an important part of the program, and your participation is essential to help us understand how to improve the program and support your goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form completely and honestly.
2. Provide your personal information, such as your name and contact details, in the "User Information" section.
3. In the "Survey Responses" section, describe your current behavior and challenges in relation to the nutrition program.
4. Indicate your motivation level by selecting one or more of the options in the "Motivation Level" section.
5. Add any additional comments or thoughts about your motivation level in the "Motivation Comment" section.
6. If applicable, provide the date and time of your survey completion in the "Survey Date" and "Survey Time" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **User Information** (user_info)
	* A text field where you can enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.

### 2. **Survey Responses** (survey_responses)
	* Describe your current eating habits, physical activity levels, and any challenges you're facing with the nutrition program.

### 3. **Motivation Score** (motivation_score)
	* A numerical field where you can enter a score between 1 and 10 to indicate your current motivation level.

### 4. **Motivation Level** (motivation_level)
	* A multiple-choice field where you can select one or more of the following options:
		+ High: You're extremely motivated and committed to the program.
		+ Medium: You're somewhat motivated but face some challenges.
		+ Low: You're struggling to stay motivated or need additional support.

### 5. **Motivation Comment** (motivation_comment)
	* A text field where you can add any additional comments or thoughts about your motivation level.

### 6. **Survey Date** (survey_date)
	* A date field where you can enter the date you completed the survey.

### 7. **Survey Time** (survey_time)
	* A time field where you can enter the time of day you completed the survey.
