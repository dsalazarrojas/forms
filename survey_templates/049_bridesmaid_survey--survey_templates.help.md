# Bridesmaid Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bridesmaid Survey is designed to gather information about your preferences and characteristics as a bridesmaid. This information will help the bride understand your strengths, interests, and fit for the bridal party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and thoughtful in your responses.
3. If a question does not apply to you, select "N/A" or "Not applicable".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bridesmaid Name** (`bridemaid_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a bridesmaid.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Bride Name** (`bride_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the bride you're supporting.
* **How Long Have You Known the Bride** (`how_long_know_bride`, select_one): Choose how long you've known the bride:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ 10-15 years
	+ 15+ years
* **Favorite Activities** (`favorite_activities`, select_multiple): Choose your favorite activities:

	+ Bars and nightlife
	+ Outdoor activities
	+ Sports
	+ Games and puzzles
	+ Arts and crafts
	+ Movies
	+ Music

	+ Spontaneous and fun
	+ Mix of everything
* **Alcohol Preferences** (`alcohol_preference`, select_multiple): Choose your drink preferences:
	+ Beer

	+ Cocktails
	+ Liquor
	+ Non-alcoholic drinks
	+ No alcohol preference
* **Food Preferences** (`food_preferences`, select_multiple): Choose your food preferences:

* **Physical Fitness Interest** (`physical_fitness_interest`, select_one): Choose your fitness level:

	+ Lightly active

* **Budget Comfort Level for Events** (`budget_comfort_level`, select_one): Choose your budget range for events:
