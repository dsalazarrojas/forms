# Candidate Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from candidates who have participated in an interview. Your responses will help us understand your experience and improve our hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full name.
2. Enter the Job ID as listed in the vacancy announcement.
3. Specify the position for which you have applied.
4. Answer the feedback questions honestly to help us improve our hiring process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job ID**: Enter the Job ID as listed in the vacancy announcement.
* **Position Applied For**: Specify the position for which you have applied.
* **Date of Interview Feedback**: Enter the date when the interview took place. Please use the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* 
* **Rate Overall Interview Experience**: Rate your experience from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Accuracy of Job Description**: Choose whether the job description was accurate or not.
* **Interviewer's Preparation**: Rate the interviewers' preparation from Very Poor to Excellent.
* **Enough Time to Ask Questions**: Choose whether you had enough time to ask questions or not.
* **Professionalism of Recruitment Team**: Rate the professionalism of the recruitment team from Very Poor to Excellent.
* **Ease of Application**: Rate the ease of application on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Clarity of Next Steps**: Choose whether the next steps were clear or not.
* 
* **Communication via Email**: Rate how helpful you found the email communication from our team.
* **Interview Enjoyment**: Specify what you enjoyed most about the interview. 
* **Suggestion for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions you have for improvement.
* 
* **Recommend as Employer**: Choose whether you would recommend our company as an employer.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Final Thoughts**: Please use this field to provide any final thoughts or feedback.
