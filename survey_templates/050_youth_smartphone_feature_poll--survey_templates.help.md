## # Youth Smartphone Feature Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect opinions and preferences from young individuals on their smartphone usage and desired features.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your age group from the options provided.
2. Choose your current smartphone brand from the options provided.
3. Indicate your primary reason for buying a smartphone.
4. Select all the features that are priority to you when it comes to a new smartphone.
5. Answer the questions in the "Do you agree with the following" section.
6. If you have any additional comments, type them in the "Additional comments" field.
7. Click "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **What is your age group?** (`what_is_your_age_group`, Select, required)
Select your age group from the provided options: 13-15, 16-18, 19-24, 25-29, 30+.

### **What is your current smartphone?** (`poll_question1`, Select, required)
Choose your current smartphone brand from the options: Apple, Samsung, Other, None.

### **What is your main reason for buying a smartphone?** (`poll_question2`, Select, optional)
Select all that apply: Social media, Entertainment, Productivity, Camera, Gaming, Other, Music, Messaging.

### **What is your smartphone feature priority?** (`poll_question3`, Multi-select, optional)
Select all that apply: Camera, Battery, Storage, Performance, Price, Durability, Display, Other.

### **Do you agree with the following?** (`poll_question4`, Multi-select, optional)
Select all that apply: True, False.

### **Additional comments** (`poll_question5`, Note, optional)
Type any additional comments or thoughts you have about smartphones.

## Tips
* Please select all that apply for each question, and only select what is relevant to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
* This form is for collecting data and opinions, so please be honest and accurate in your responses.
