# DJ Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and DJ recommendations for specific events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the title of the event in the `event_title` field.
2. Select the date of the event using the `event_date` field.
3. Choose your favorite music style from the options provided in the `music_style` field.
4. Select up to two DJs from the list of options in the `djs` field.
5. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
6. Provide your phone number in the `phone` field.
7. Mention your favorite band in the `favorite_band` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_title** (`event_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event that you are recommending.
* **event_date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **music_style** (`music_style`, select_one, required): Choose the music style you prefer for the event.
* **djs** (`djs`, select_multiple, required): Select up to two DJs that you recommend for the event.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **favorite_band** (`favorite_band`, text, required): Mention your favorite band for reference.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your recommendations.

## Tips
- Ensure the event title and date fields are accurate to help us provide the best possible recommendations.
- Choose the music style that best fits the event's atmosphere.
- Select up to two DJs that you trust to perform at the event.
- Enter a valid email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you for further information.
- If you have a favorite band, mention it to help us understand your musical preferences.
