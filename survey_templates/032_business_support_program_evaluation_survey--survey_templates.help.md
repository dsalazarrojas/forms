# Business Support Program Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from business participants about their experience in a support program. Your input will help us understand how well the program met your needs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, business name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the name of the program you attended and the dates you participated.
3. For each question, select the best answer that reflects your experience.
4. If you have any suggestions or recommendations for improvement, type them in the "Recommendations or Suggestions" field.
5. Finally, click the submit button to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please fill in your name as a participant in the program.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your business contact email address.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program you attended.
* **Program Dates Attended** (`program_dates`, text, optional): Enter the dates you participated in the program.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Fill in the current date to ensure accurate tracking.
* **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, select_one, required): Select the level of relevance the content had to your business.
	+ Highly Relevant: The content was highly relevant to my business.
	+ Relevant: The content was somewhat relevant to my business.
	+ Not Relevant: The content was not relevant to my business.
	+ Not Applicable: The content was not applicable to my business.
* **Instructor or Facilitator Quality** (`instructor_quality`, select_one, required): Select the quality of the instructor or facilitator.
	+ Excellent: The instructor was very experienced and knowledgeable.
	+ Good: The instructor was knowledgeable but could improve on presentation.
	+ Satisfactory: The instructor was somewhat knowledgeable and helpful.
	+ Fair: The instructor was somewhat unprepared or lacking.
	+ Poor: The instructor was unqualified or unhelpful.
* **Program Organization** (`program_organization`, select_one, required): Select the level of organization of the program.
	+ Excellent: The program was very well organized and smooth.
	+ Good: The program was somewhat well organized but could improve on timing.
	+ Satisfactory: The program was somewhat disorganized or confusing.
	+ Fair: The program was very disorganized or confusing.
	+ Poor: The program was unorganized or confusing.
* **Quality of Learning Materials** (`materials_quality`, select_one, required): Select the quality of the materials provided.
	+ Excellent: The materials were very high quality and relevant.
	+ Good: The materials were somewhat relevant and useful.
	+ Satisfactory: The materials were somewhat useful but could improve.
	+ Fair: The materials were somewhat confusing or unhelpful.
	+ Poor: The materials were unhelpful or irrelevant.
* **Practical Application Value** (`practical_application`, select_one, required): Select the level of practical application value.
	+ Highly Applicable: I can apply what I learned immediately.
	+ Applicable: I can somewhat apply what I learned.
	+ Not Applicable: I did not apply what I learned.
* **Networking Opportunities** (`networking_value`, select_one, required): Select the level of networking opportunities.
	+ Excellent Opportunities: I made many valuable connections.
	+ Good Opportunities: I made some valuable connections.
	+ Some Opportunities: I made a few connections.
	+ Few Opportunities: I made no valuable connections.
	+ None: I made no connections.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Select the level of value for money.
	+ Excellent Value: I got a very good value for money.
	+ Good Value: I got a somewhat good value for money.
	+ Adequate Value: I got a somewhat poor value for money.
	+ Poor Value: I got a very poor value for money.
	+ Not Worth It: I did not get a good value for money.
* **Expected Business Impact** (`business_impact`, select_one, required): Select the expected impact on your business.
	+ Significant Positive Impact: The program will have a significant positive impact on my business.
	+ Positive Impact: The program will have a positive impact on my business.
	+ Some Impact: The program will have a small positive impact on my business.
	+ Limited Impact: The program will have a small impact on my business.
	+ No Impact: The program will have no impact on my business.
* **Recommendations or Suggestions** (`recommendations_provided`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or recommendations for improvement, type them here.
* **Future Program Participation** (`future_participation`, select_one, optional): Select your willingness to participate in future programs.
	+ Definitely Yes: I will definitely participate in future programs.
	+ Probably Yes: I probably will participate in future programs.
	+ Maybe: I might participate in future programs.
	+ Probably No: I probably will not participate in future programs.
	+ Definitely No: I will not participate in future programs.
* **Would You Recommend This Program** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Select if you would recommend the program to others.
	+ Strongly Recommend: I strongly recommend the program to others.
	+ Recommend: I recommend the program to others.
	+ Neutral: I have no opinion on the program.
	+ Do Not Recommend: I do not recommend the program to others.
	+ Strongly Oppose: I strongly oppose recommending the program to others.
* **Additional Support or Topics** (`additional_support_needed`, text, optional): If there are any additional support or topics you think would be helpful, type them here.
