# Histology Techniques Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the techniques used in histology labs, including microscopy, microtome, histology, scanning, and transmission techniques. It also collects information on training and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully to ensure you have selected the correct techniques used in your lab.
2. For each technique, select all that apply from the options provided.
3. Enter the number of hours, days, weeks, months, or years of training and experience, as applicable.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "comments" section.
5. Select the correct submission option from the options provided.
