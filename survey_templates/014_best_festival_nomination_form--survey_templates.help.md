# Best Festival Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about festivals that you would like to nominate for recognition. Please fill out this form with as much detail as possible to help us understand why your festival is deserving of recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read through each section carefully and fill out all required fields.
*   Enter your name and email address to help us contact you with any further questions.
*   Provide the name, location, and category of the festival you are nominating.
*   Rate the quality of the festival, including its organization and logistics, value for money, local community impact, environmental sustainability, and any unique features that set it apart.
*   Explain why you think this festival deserves recognition.
*   Provide notable highlights from the most recent edition of the festival.
*   Finally, indicate whether or not you would recommend this festival to others and submit your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Nominator Information
*   **Nominator Full Name**: Please enter your full name as the person submitting this nomination.
*   **Nominator Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any further questions or updates.
*   **Date of Submission**: Indicate the current date as the date of submission.
*   Nominator Information is used to ensure that we can contact and follow up with you in case of any questions or further information needed.

### Festival Details
*   **Festival Name**: Enter the official name of the festival.
*   **Festival Location**: Enter the city and state or country where the festival is held.
*   **Festival Website**: Provide the URL for the festival's official website.
*   **Festival Category**: Choose the primary focus of the festival from the given options.
*   **Last Event Date**: Enter the date of the most recent edition of the festival.

### Quality and Impact Assessment
*   **Organization and Logistics**: Rate how well the festival was managed, from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
*   **Value for Money**: Rate the value of money, from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
*   **Local Community Impact**: Rate the local community impact, from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
*   **Environmental Sustainability**: Rate the festival's environmental sustainability, from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).

### Nomination Support
*   **Describe Unique Features**: In a few sentences, describe the unique features of this festival that set it apart from others.
*   **Win Justification**: Write a short explanation of why you think this festival deserves recognition.
*   **Recent Highlights**: List key performers, vendors, or moments that made the most recent edition of the festival notable.

### Conclusion
*   **Would You Recommend**: Finally, indicate whether or not you would recommend this festival to others.

Please note that the fields marked as "required" need to be completed to ensure a successful submission. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting your nomination.
