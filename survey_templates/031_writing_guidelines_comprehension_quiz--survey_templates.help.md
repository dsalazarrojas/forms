# Writing Guidelines Comprehension Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Writing Guidelines Comprehension Quiz is designed to assess a user's understanding of writing guidelines and rules. The quiz consists of several questions that test the user's ability to comprehend and apply writing conventions and best practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the quiz, follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page, which provides an overview of the writing guidelines and rules being tested.
2. Answer each question according to your understanding of the writing guidelines and rules.
3. For select_one and select_multiple questions, select the correct answer from the options provided.
4. For date, time, and email questions, enter the correct information if required.
5. Use the note field to provide additional information or context if necessary.
6. Finally, conclude your answers in the conclusion page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This page provides an overview of the writing guidelines and rules being tested.
* **question_1** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the correct answer that best demonstrates your understanding of the writing guidelines.
	+ Options: 
		- option 1
		- option 2
* **question_2** (`question_2`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all correct answers that demonstrate your understanding of the writing guidelines.
	+ Options: 
		- option 1
		- option 2
* **question3** (`question_3`, `date`, required: false): Enter the correct date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **question4** (`question_4`, `time`, required: false): Enter the correct time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **question5** (`question_5`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional information or context related to the writing guidelines.
* **question6** (`question_6`, `email`, required: false): Enter the correct email address associated with the writing guidelines.
* **question7** (`question_7`, `text`, required: false): Enter any other information or context related to the writing guidelines.
* **conclusion** (`conclusion`, `text`, required: false): Summarize your answers and provide any final thoughts or comments.
