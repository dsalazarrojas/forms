# labour_market_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, "labour_market_survey", is designed to collect information about respondents' employment status, education level, occupation, and other relevant details from the labour market. The purpose of this form is to gather data on how respondents perceive their employment and skills training, which can help in understanding the current labour market trends and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2.  Fill in the answers to each question based on your personal experiences and information.
3.  Make sure to answer all questions, as indicated by the "required" status of each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### How do you know the respondent?
*   **How do you know the respondent?** (`introduction`, text, optional): This field is a text field where you can provide any relevant information about how you know the respondent. This can be a name, a company, or a specific project.

### Survey Area
*   **Survey Area** (`survey_area`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select the area where you live or work. You can choose between "Rural", "Urban", or "Suburban" based on your location.

### What is your employment status?
*   **What is your employment status?** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): This field is used to determine your current employment status. You can select from the following options:
    *   Employed
    *   Unemployed
    *   Self-employed
    *   Student
    *   Retiree
    *   Other (please specify)

### What is your highest level of education completed?
*   **What is your highest level of education completed?** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): This field is used to determine your highest level of education completed. You can select from the following options:
    *   High School
    *   Vocational/Trade Certificate
    *   College Diploma
    *   University Degree
    *   Master's Degree
    *   Doctoral Degree
    *   Other (please specify)

### What type of work do you do?
*   **What type of work do you do?** (`employment_type`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select multiple types of work that you do. You can choose from the following options:
    *   Full-time
    *   Part-time
    *   Freelance/Contract
    *   Self-employed
    *   Retiree
    *   Other (please specify)

### How many years do you have of work experience?
*   **How many years do you have of work experience?** (`work_experience`, number, optional): This field is used to determine the number of years you have of work experience. Enter a numerical value that represents the number of years.

### What is your annual income range?
*   **What is your annual income range?** (`income_range`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to determine your annual income range. You can select from the following options:
    *   Less than 20k
    *   20-49k
    *   50-79k
    *   80k and above

### What is your occupation?
*   **What is your occupation?** (`occupation`, text, optional): This field is a text field where you can provide your occupation.

### Have you received any skills training or certification in the last 5 years?
*   **Have you received any skills training or certification in the last 5 years?** (`skills_training`, text, optional): This field is a text field where you can provide any information about skills training or certification you have received in the last 5 years.

## Tips

*   Make sure to answer all required fields before submitting the form.
*   If you need further clarification on any question, feel free to contact us.
*   This form is intended to gather general information, so please provide your honest answers.
