# unique_experience_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your feedback about your unique experience. This feedback will be used to improve future events and provide better experiences for users in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Event Details section to describe the event you attended.
2. Enter the Date and Time of the event.
3. List the participants or individuals involved in the event.
4. Share your feedback about the event.
5. Choose a number between 1-10 to indicate your satisfaction level with the event.
6. Provide any suggestions for improvement (optional).
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details (1)**: Enter a brief description of the event you attended, such as its title, location, and any notable details.
* **Date (2)**: Enter the date when the event took place.
* **Time (3)**: Enter the time when the event took place.
* **Participants (4)**: List all the individuals involved in the event, including speakers, performers, or any notable attendees.
* **Feedback (5)**: Share your honest feedback about the event, including what you liked or disliked.
* **Satisfaction Level (6)**: Choose a number between 1-10 to indicate how satisfied you are with the event, with 1 being the least satisfied and 10 being the most satisfied.
* **Suggestions (7)**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them here (this is optional).
* **Email (8)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone (9)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
