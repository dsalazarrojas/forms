# Trusts Legal Knowledge Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trusts Legal Knowledge Survey is designed to help legal firms assess their knowledge and understanding of trusts. It is used to identify areas where they may need improvement and provide guidance for further education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the survey template section, which asks about the definition and key facts about trusts.
2. Select the appropriate options for each question in the key facts answers section.
3. Choose the best answer for the legal knowledge assessment question.
4. Provide any improvement recommendations for the firm's knowledge and practices.
5. Fill out the educator comments section with any additional thoughts or comments.
6. Enter the name and contact information of the legal firm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Template**: Please answer the questions about the definition and key facts of trusts to the best of your ability. This will help us understand your firm's current knowledge level.
* **What is a trust?**: Choose the best answer from the options provided. This question is meant to gauge your understanding of the basic definition of a trust.
* **What are some key facts about trusts?**: Please provide any additional information you have about trusts that you think are relevant. This is a free-form text field, so you can write as much or as little as you need.
* **Key Facts Answers**: Select all the key facts that apply to your firm's understanding of trusts. These will be used to evaluate your firm's knowledge level.
* **Legal Knowledge Assessment**: Choose the best answer to assess your firm's overall knowledge of trusts. This question will help us understand where your firm may need additional education.
* **Improvement Recommendations**: If you have any suggestions for areas where you would like additional education or support, please provide those here.
* **Educator Comments**: This section is for any additional comments or thoughts you have about your firm's knowledge and practices.
* **Legal Firm Name**: Please enter the name of your law firm.
* **Legal Firm Email**: Please enter the email address of your law firm.
* **Legal Firm Phone**: Please enter the phone number of your law firm.
