# Parent Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and satisfaction ratings from parents about their experience with a program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select your ratings for each question using the scale provided.
3. You can skip any questions that do not apply to your experience.
4. Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Introduction
* **Parent Satisfaction Survey**: This is the title of the form, which explains its purpose.
* **intro**: This is a text input field where you can provide any additional context or information about your experience.

### Page 2:
* **what_program**: This is a selection field where you can choose the program you are providing feedback on. Please select "English Language and Literacy" or "Mathematics and Science" from the options provided.
* **satisfaction_level**: This is a rating field where you can indicate your level of satisfaction with the program. Please use the scale from 1 to 5 to answer this question.
* **communication**: This is a numeric field where you can indicate the frequency of communication with the program staff.
* **communication_frequency**: This is a numeric field where you can indicate the quality of communication with the program staff.
* **parent_support**: This is a numeric field where you can indicate the level of support from parents in the program.
* **teacher_support**: This is a numeric field where you can indicate the level of support from teachers in the program.
* **classroom_support**: This is a numeric field where you can indicate the level of support from classroom staff in the program.
* **school_support**: This is a numeric field where you can indicate the level of support from school staff in the program.
* **communication_quality**: This is a numeric field where you can indicate the overall quality of communication with the program staff.
* **overall_satisfaction**: This is a numeric field where you can indicate your overall satisfaction with the program.

### Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please include it in the text input field on the first page.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback! Your input is valuable to us.
