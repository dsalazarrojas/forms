# Art Gallery Visitor Experience Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback and opinions about your visit to the art gallery. Your input will help us improve the quality of our exhibitions and create a better experience for our visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
2. Provide your honest feedback and opinions about your visit.
3. Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Visit Summary
*   **-- Visit Summary --** (Note): A brief introduction to the form, explaining the purpose of your visit and any relevant comments.

### Your Information (Optional)
*   **Name (Optional)**: If you wish to identify yourself for any reason, please provide your name. This information will be kept confidential.
*   **Date of Your Visit**: The date you visited the gallery. Please use the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Which exhibition did you primarily come to see?**: The name of the exhibition that brought you to the gallery.
*   **How would you rate your overall experience today?**: Rate your overall experience out of 5 stars.

### Exhibition and Gallery Ratings
*   **Quality of Curation and Exhibition Theme**: Rate the quality and theme of the exhibition from Excellent, Good, Fair, to Poor.
*   **Variety and Interest of Artworks Displayed**: Rate the variety and interest of the artworks displayed from Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, to Dissatisfied.
*   **Gallery Layout and Movement Flow**: Rate the ease of navigating the gallery from Easy to Navigate, Somewhat Confusing, to Difficult to Navigate.
*   **Cleanliness and Comfort of the Gallery Facilities**: Rate the cleanliness and comfort of the gallery facilities from 5 - Outstanding, 4 - Good, 3 - Average, 2 - Poor, to 1 - Very Poor.

### What We Want to Know from You
*   **What was the highlight of your visit?**: Describe the most enjoyable or memorable part of your visit.
*   **One specific improvement you would recommend?**: Suggest one thing we could improve to make your next visit better.
*   **How likely are you to recommend this gallery to a friend or colleague?**: Rate your likelihood on the 0-10 NPS Scale.
*   **Do you plan to attend our next exhibition?**: Choose whether you plan to attend our next exhibition.
*   **Would you like to subscribe to our monthly newsletter?**: Choose whether you would like to stay connected with us.
*   **Your Email Address (Optional)**: If you would like to subscribe to our newsletter, please provide your email address.

**Tips**

*   Please be honest and provide your genuine opinions.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out to provide a complete response.
*   If you have any further questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to ask.
