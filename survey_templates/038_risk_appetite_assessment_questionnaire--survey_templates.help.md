# risk_appetite_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about users' risk appetite, investment objectives, and other related factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the text fields provided.
2. Make sure to answer all questions, as the form is not automatically saved.
3. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Financial Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, `text`, required: false): This field asks about your comfort level with taking financial risks.
* **Investment Objective** (`investment_objective`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your investment goals, such as long-term growth or short-term income.
* **Investment Horizon** (`investment_horizon`, `text`, required: false): Indicate the time frame for your investment goals.
* **Time Frame** (`time_frame`, `text`, required: false): Describe the time period you are considering for your investment.
* **Risk Sensitivity** (`risk_sensitivity`, `text`, required: false): Share your level of comfort with risk in your investments.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, `text`, required: false): Share your financial goals, such as saving for a specific purpose.
* **Current Assets** (`current_assets`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current financial situation.
* **Financial Obligations** (`financial_obligations`, `text`, required: false): Share any financial responsibilities or debts.
* **Investment Experience** (`investment_experience`, `text`, required: false): Describe your level of experience in investing.
* **Risk Appetite** (`risk_appetite`, `text`, required: false): Share your risk tolerance level for investments.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any doubts or need clarification, contact the form creators at form@gic.mx.
* You can modify or resubmit the form as needed.
