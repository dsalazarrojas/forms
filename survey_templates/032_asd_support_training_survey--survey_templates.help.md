# ASD Support Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ASD Support Training Survey is a tool for gathering feedback from participants of the ASD training session. This form helps us understand your experience with the training and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your participant information: Provide your name and email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).
2. Answer the evaluation questions: Rate and rank your experience with the training session.
   - Use the provided options for each question.
   - Choose the option that best represents your experience.
3. Add any additional comments or suggestions: If you have any further comments or suggestions, feel free to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, which will help us understand your feedback.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, date, required): The date of the training session you attended.
* **Training Session Topic** (`training_session_topic`, text, required): The topic of the training session.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required): The name of the training facilitator.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training session.
	+ Choose one of the following options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
* **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, select_one, required): Rate the relevance of the training content to your work with individuals with ASD.
	+ Choose one of the following options: Very Relevant, Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Very Relevant, Not Relevant.
* **Knowledge Gained** (`knowledge_gained`, select_one, required): Rate the amount of new knowledge you gained from the training session.
	+ Choose one of the following options: Significant Amount, Moderate Amount, Little Amount, No New Knowledge.
* **Practical Applicability** (`practical_applicability`, select_one, required): Rate the applicability of the strategies taught during the training session.
	+ Choose one of the following options: Very Applicable, Applicable, Somewhat Applicable, Not Very Applicable, Not Applicable.
* **Training Pacing** (`training_pacing`, select_one, required): Rate the pace of the training session.
	+ Choose one of the following options: Too Fast, Appropriate, Too Slow.
* **Materials Quality** (`materials_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the training materials.
	+ Choose one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor.
* **Interaction Opportunities** (`interaction_opportunities`, select_one, required): Rate the opportunities for questions and discussion during the training session.
	+ Choose one of the following options: More Than Enough, Adequate, Insufficient, None Needed.
* **Additional Topics of Interest** (`additional_topics_interest`, text, optional): Suggest any additional topics you'd like to see covered in future training sessions.
* **Recommendation Likelihood** (`recommendation_likelihood`, select_one, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend this training to your colleagues.
	+ Choose one of the following options: Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Add any other comments or suggestions you have about the training session.

# Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the evaluation questions.
* Take a moment to reflect on your experience with the training session before answering the questions.
* You can add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Feedback Comments" field.
* If you have any suggestions or requests for future training topics, feel free to share them in the "Additional Topics of Interest" field.
