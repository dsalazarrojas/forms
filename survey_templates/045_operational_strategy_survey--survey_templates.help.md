# operational-strategy-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help organizations gather information about their operational strategy, which is the process of planning and organizing business activities to achieve goals and objectives. This survey aims to collect data on various aspects of an organization's operational strategy, such as company size, operational strategy score, business models' impacts, communication technologies, decision-making procedures, operational capacity, business continuity, operational planning, operational strategy score 2, feedback sources, and feedback mechanisms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review each field and select the most appropriate options for your organization's current situation.
*   Provide accurate and truthful information to ensure the data collected will be reliable and useful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, **required: false**):
    • This field helps us understand the size of your organization. Please select the category that best describes your organization: 
    • Small: Less than 10 employees.
    • Medium: 10-50 employees.
    • Large: More than 50 employees.
*   **Operational Strategy Score** (`operational_strategy_score`, number, **required: false**):
    • This field asks for your organization's score for operational strategy. Please enter a number between 0 and 100. A higher score indicates a more effective operational strategy.
*   **Business Models' Impacts** (`business_models_impacts`, select_multiple, **required: false**):
    • This field is about how your business models impact your operational strategy. Please choose all that apply:
    • High or Medium or Low: Different levels of impact.
    • Other: Specify other impacts not listed.
*   **Communication Technologies** (`communication_technologies`, select_one, **required: false**):
    • This field helps us understand the technologies used for communication within your organization. Please select one that best describes your organization:
    • Low: Less frequently used.
    • Medium: Moderately used.
    • High: Frequently used.
*   **Decision Making Procedures** (`decision_making_procedures`, select_multiple, **required: false**):
    • This field is about the procedures used for decision making within your organization. Please choose all that apply:
    • Formal or Informal or Flexible: Different approaches to decision making.
    • Other: Specify other procedures not listed.
*   **Operational Capacity** (`operational_capacity`, number, **required: false**):
    • This field asks for your organization's operational capacity. Please enter a number indicating your organization's capacity.
*   **Business Continuity** (`business_continuity`, select_one, **required: false**):
    • This field helps us understand the level of business continuity in your organization. Please select one that best describes your organization:
    • High: Strong continuity.
    • Medium: Moderate continuity.
    • Low: Weak continuity.
*   **Operational Planning** (`operational_planning`, select_multiple, **required: false**):
    • This field is about the planning approach used within your organization. Please choose all that apply:
    • Formal or Informal or Flexible: Different approaches to planning.
    • Other: Specify other approaches not listed.
*   **Operational Strategy Score 2** (`operational_strategy_score_2`, number, **required: false**):
    • This field asks for your organization's operational strategy score 2. Please enter a number indicating your organization's operational strategy score 2.
*   **Feedback Sources** (`feedback_sources`, select_one, **required: false**):
    • This field helps us understand the sources of feedback in your organization. Please select one that best describes your organization:
    • Stakeholders: Feedback from stakeholders.
    • Employees: Feedback from employees.
    • Customers: Feedback from customers.
*   **Feedback Mechanisms** (`feedback_mechanisms`, select_multiple, **required: false**):
    • This field is about the mechanisms used for feedback in your organization. Please choose all that apply:
    • Stakeholders or Employees or Customers: Feedback from different sources.
    • Other: Specify other mechanisms not listed.

## Tips

*   Please answer all the questions truthfully and accurately, as this information will be used to understand your operational strategy better.
*   If you're unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.
*   Please note that this form is for informational purposes only, and the data collected will be used to improve operational strategy within your organization.
