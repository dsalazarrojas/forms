# interest_in_enhanced_products_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interest in enhanced products form is used to gather feedback and information from customers about their interests and preferences in various enhanced products. This form helps us understand what customers want and need in order to provide better service and tailor our offers accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the enhanced products that interest you.
2. Enter your customer feedback and comments.
3. Select the frequency of when you would like to receive feedback or communication from us.
4. Choose your age group.
5. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).
6. Enter the date and time you would like to receive communication from us.
7. Enter any additional comments or feedback about our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Feedback** (customer_feedback, text, optional): Enter your feedback about our products or services.
* **Product Interest** (product_interest, select multiple, optional): Select the enhanced products that interest you (e.g., Enhanced Product 1, Enhanced Product 2, Enhanced Product 3).
* **Improved Product** (improved_product, number, optional): Not applicable (this field seems to be unused in the form).
* **Feedback Frequency** (feedback_frequency, number, optional): Enter the frequency at which you would like to receive feedback or communication from us (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Product Preferences** (product_preferences, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your product preferences.
* **Demographic Info** (demographic_info, select one, optional): Choose your age group (e.g., Age Group 1, Age Group 2, Age Group 3).
* **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, optional): Enter your contact information (e.g., address, phone number).
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Not applicable (this field seems to be unused in the form).
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Not applicable (this field seems to be unused in the form).
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about our services.
