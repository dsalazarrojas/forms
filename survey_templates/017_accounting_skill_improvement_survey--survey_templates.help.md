# Accounting Skill Improvement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about your current accounting skills, knowledge, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. If a field is marked as "required", please provide a response. If it's not marked as required, you may choose to skip it.
3. Be as accurate and honest as possible when answering the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Respondent Name** (text)
Enter your name as it appears on your official records.

### 2. **Department** (text)
Enter your department name as it appears on your official records.

### 3. **Current Role** (select one)
Select the job title that best describes your current role in the company.

*   Junior Accountant
*   Senior Accountant
*   Controller
*   CFO
*   Audit Associate

### 4. **Proficient Skills** (select multiple)
Select the areas where you feel you are already proficient in.

*   General Ledger
*   Financial Reporting
*   Tax Compliance
*   Cost Accounting
*   IFRS / GAAP Knowledge

### 5. **Desired Training Areas** (select multiple)
Select the areas you would like to develop further in the future.

*   Advanced Excel
*   Data Analytics for Finance
*   Ethical Leadership
*   International Taxation
*   Automation Tools

### 6. **Preferred Learning Style** (select one)
Select the learning style that suits you best.

*   Self-paced Online
*   Live Webinar
*   In-person Workshop
*   One-on-one Mentoring

### 7. **Hours Per Week Available for Training** (number)
Enter the realistic number of hours you can dedicate to training per week.

### 8. **How much do current skill gaps impact your daily productivity?** (select one)
Select the level of impact your current skill gaps have on your daily productivity.

*   Low
*   Moderate
*   Significant

### 9. **Specific Tools or Software to Learn** (text)
Enter the specific tools or software you would like to learn more about.

### 10. **Developmental Feedback --** (note)
Enter any developmental feedback or suggestions you have for your growth and improvement.

### 11. **Manager Comments (if applicable)** (text)
Enter any comments or feedback from your supervisor or manager.
