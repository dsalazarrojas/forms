# which_raptor_suits_you_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user preferences for raptors. It consists of three questions: which raptor suits you, what's your question, and how can we improve. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Which raptor suits you?" question and select as many raptors as you like from the list of options. 
2. Click on the "What's your question?" text field and input your question. 
3. Click on the "How can we improve?" text field to provide your feedback or suggestions. 
4. Click submit to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which raptor suits you?** (Raptor 1, Select Multiple, required):
	+ This question is asking you to select all the raptors that you like or suit your needs. You can select as many or as few raptors as you like. 
* **What's your question?** (Text, required):
	+ This is a text field where you can ask a question related to raptors. 
* **How can we improve?** (Text, optional):
	+ This is a text field where you can provide feedback or suggestions on how the company can improve its raptor services or products.
