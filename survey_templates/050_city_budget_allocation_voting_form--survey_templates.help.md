# City Budget Allocation Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect individual residents' budget allocation priorities for various city services. The collected data will help the city government make informed decisions on how to allocate resources.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive a confirmation of your submission and any future updates.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up or confirmation purposes.
4. **Home Address**: Enter your current home address in the format of street, city, state, and zip code.
5. **City District or Ward**: Select your city district or ward where you live.
6. **How Long Have You Been a Resident**: Select the length of time you have been a resident of the city.
7. **Total Budget to Allocate**: Allocate a virtual budget of $100 among the various service categories.
8. **Education Budget Allocation**: Enter the amount or percentage of budget you would allocate to Education.
9. **Public Safety Budget Allocation**: Enter the amount or percentage of budget you would allocate to Public Safety.
10. **Infrastructure Budget Allocation**: Enter the amount or percentage of budget you would allocate to Infrastructure.
11. **Parks and Recreation Budget Allocation**: Enter the amount or percentage of budget you would allocate to Parks and Recreation.
12. **Healthcare Budget Allocation**: Enter the amount or percentage of budget you would allocate to Healthcare.
13. **Transportation Budget Allocation**: Enter the amount or percentage of budget you would allocate to Transportation.
14. **Housing Budget Allocation**: Enter the amount or percentage of budget you would allocate to Housing.
15. **Environment Budget Allocation**: Enter the amount or percentage of budget you would allocate to Environment.
16. **Economic Development Budget Allocation**: Enter the amount or percentage of budget you would allocate to Economic Development.
17. **Social Services Budget Allocation**: Enter the amount or percentage of budget you would allocate to Social Services.
18. **Arts and Culture Budget Allocation**: Enter the amount or percentage of budget you would allocate to Arts and Culture.
19. **Budget Balance Check**: Confirm if your allocation equals 100%.
20. **What Is Your Top Priority**: Select the single most important area for budget allocation.
21. **Why Is This Your Top Priority**: Explain the reasoning behind your top priority selection.
22. **How Satisfied Are You with Current Budget**: Select how satisfied you are with the current budget.
23. **Any Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions on the budget.
24. **Would You Like to Participate in Future Budget Discussions**: Confirm if you would like to participate in future budget discussions.
25. **Voting Confirmation**: Confirm that your submission is your authentic vote.
