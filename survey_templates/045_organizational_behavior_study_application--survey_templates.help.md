# Organizational Behavior Study Application - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is a part of a larger research study on organizational behavior. It collects data from participants to gather insights and information for the study.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the general information section with your personal details.
2. Check your availability for participating in the study.
3. Confirm your consent to participate in the study.
4. Provide your participant details, including any relevant information about your role or position within the organization.
5. Fill in your contact information so we can reach out to you.
6. If you have any comments or feedback, please share them with us.
7. Sign the participant signature section to confirm your participation.
8. Our researcher will also sign to confirm their involvement in the study.
9. The university will sign to confirm their authorization of the study.
10. Finally, please fill in your participant declaration and researcher declaration.
11. Provide a brief description of how the study will be conducted.
