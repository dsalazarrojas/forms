# Labor Law Learning Tools Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Labor Law Learning Tools Survey is designed to gather feedback and information about your preferences and needs related to learning labor law concepts. Your input will help us improve the effectiveness and accessibility of our learning tools.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select all relevant options for each page to express your preferences.
3. Fill in any text fields with your comments or additional information.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1): This is a note field where you can read a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Labor Law Learning Resources** (2): Select all the resources that you are interested in using to learn about labor law. This could include books, videos, online courses, or other educational materials.
* **Learning Format** (3): Choose the type of learning format that you prefer, such as self-paced, instructor-led, or online courses.
* **Labor Law Resources** (4): Provide any additional information about labor law resources that you would like to see included in our learning tools.
* **Satisfaction** (5): Rate your satisfaction with our current learning tools and resources. Use the number to indicate your level of satisfaction, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **Feedback** (6): Share any feedback or comments about our current learning tools and resources.
* **Additional Info** (7): Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for us to know about your learning preferences.
* **Preferred Learning Language** (8): Select the language in which you would like to learn about labor law. This could be English, Spanish, or another language.
* **Learning Format** (9): Choose the type of learning format that you prefer, such as online, instructor-led, or self-paced courses.
* **Learning Style** (10): Select the type of learning style that you prefer, such as hands-on, theory-based, or interactive learning.
