# Community Health Trend Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the community's health trends by collecting information on various aspects of health and wellness. The goal is to gather data on factors that influence the health and well-being of individuals living in a particular neighborhood or region. This information will help identify areas of concern and inform initiatives to improve community health.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your residential zip code to help us track trends by neighborhood.
2. Identify the most pressing health concern in your neighborhood.
3. Indicate your frequency of physical activity, such as walking, sports, or visiting a gym.
4. Rate your access to healthy and affordable food options, such as fresh produce and grocery stores.
5. Indicate your regular use of public parks or community gyms.
6. Rate your awareness of local public health services, such as clinics and health fairs.
7. (Optional) If you have experienced any barriers to accessing healthcare, select all that apply.
8. (Optional) If a recent illness has occurred in your household, select all that apply.
9. (Optional) Identify any environmental concerns affecting your health, such as air quality or water quality issues.
10. If you have a suggestion for improving community health, feel free to share it with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Residential Zip Code (1)** (`respondent_zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code to help us track trends by neighborhood.
* **Primary Health Concern in Your Neighborhood (2)** (`primary_health_concern`, text, required): Describe the most pressing health concern in your neighborhood.
* **Frequency of Physical Activity (3)** (`activity_frequency`, select_one, required): How often do you engage in physical activity, such as:
	+ Daily
	+ 3-5 times a week
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely or Never
* **Access to Healthy and Affordable Food (4)** (`food_access`, select_one, required): Rate your access to healthy and affordable food options:
	+ Easy Access
	+ Somewhat Difficult
	+ Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **Use of Public Parks or Community Gyms (5)** (`public_resource_use`, select_one, required): How often do you use public parks or community gyms:
	+ Regularly
	+ Sometimes
	+ Never
* **Awareness of Local Public Health Services (6)** (`service_awareness`, select_one, required): Rate your awareness of local public health services:
	+ Very Aware
	+ Somewhat Aware
	+ Not Aware at All
* **Barriers to Accessing Healthcare (7)** (`healthcare_barriers`, select_multiple, optional): If you experience any barriers to accessing healthcare, select all that apply:
	+ High Cost
	+ Lack of Transportation
	+ Long Wait Times
	+ Language Barriers
	+ No Health Insurance
	+ Lack of Childcare
* **Recent Illness in Your Household (8)** (`household_illness`, select_multiple, optional): If a recent illness has occurred in your household, select all that apply:
	+ Flu or Cold
	+ Chronic Condition Flare-up
	+ Injury
	+ Mental Health Concerns
	+ COVID-19
	+ None
* **Environmental Concerns Affecting Health (9)** (`environmental_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): If you experience any environmental concerns affecting your health, select all that apply:
	+ Poor Air Quality
	+ Water Quality Issues
	+ Noise Pollution
	+ Sanitation and Trash
	+ Neighborhood Safety
* **One Suggestion to Improve Community Health (10)** (`health_improvement_suggestion`, text, optional): Share one suggestion to improve community health.

## Tips

* Please answer questions to the best of your ability.
* If you have any difficulties or concerns, feel free to contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
