# Social Gaming Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is meant to gather information about your experience and opinions about social gaming, helping us understand what features and experiences you'd like to see in our games.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all that apply from the list of player habits on page 1.
2. Choose your level of satisfaction with our current features on page 1.
3. Provide any new feature ideas you have for our games in the text field on page 1.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Habits** (`player_habits`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the player habits that apply to you, such as Casual gaming, Frequent gaming, or Heavy gaming.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select the level of satisfaction that best describes your experience with our current features, such as Highly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Highly Dissatisfied.
* **Feature Ideas** (`feature_ideas`, text, optional): Type in any new feature ideas you have for our games, such as suggestions for new modes, characters, or levels.
 
## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feature ideas.
* If you have multiple feature ideas, you can separate them with commas.
* If you're unsure what to write or need help, you can always reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
