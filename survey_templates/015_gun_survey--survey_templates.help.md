# gun_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form, "gun_survey", is to collect information and gather insights on various aspects related to gun control laws, firearms, and survey-related questions. The form is designed to be comprehensive, covering different areas of inquiry to provide a well-rounded understanding of the topic.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select a page to complete** by choosing the corresponding number from the page selector.
2. **Provide answers to each question** by selecting or typing responses in the corresponding fields. Some fields require a simple "Yes" or "No" response, while others ask for more detailed answers.
3. **Submit the form** once you've completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **gun_survey_page_1**: This page is the first part of the form. No explanation is provided for this field as it's just a page label.
* **gun_control_laws_label (gun_control_laws)**: This field asks if you are aware of or agree with gun control laws. You can select either "Yes" or "No" to respond.
* **survey_part_1_label (survey_part_1)**: This is a numerical field where you can enter your answer.
* **survey_part_2_label (survey_part_2)**: Similar to survey_part_1, this is another numerical field for you to enter your response.
* **survey_part_3_label (survey_part_3)**: This field is for date-related information.
* **survey_part_4_label (survey_part_4)**: This field is for time-related information.
* **survey_part_5_label (survey_part_5)**: This is a text field for you to provide a note or any additional comments.
* **survey_part_6_label (survey_part_6)**: This field is for an email address.
* **survey_part_7_label (survey_part_7)**: Another text field for you to enter your response.
* **survey_part_8_label (survey_part_8)**: This field is for multiple select options, where you can choose one or more from the provided options.
* **survey_part_9_label (survey_part_9)**: A text field for you to provide more information.
* **survey_part_10_label (survey_part_10)**: Another text field for additional comments.
* **gun_control_laws_2_label (gun_control_laws_2)**: Similar to gun_control_laws, this is another field asking if you are aware of or agree with gun control laws. You can select either "Yes" or "No".
* **survey_part_11_label (survey_part_11)**: A text field for more information.
* **survey_part_12_label (survey_part_12)**: Another text field for comments.
* **survey_part_13_label (survey_part_13)**: The final text field for any additional thoughts or comments.
