# Live Stream Content Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather your feedback on your experience with our live streams. Your input will help us improve future live streams to better meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date of the live broadcast you are providing feedback on.
2. Specify the time of the live broadcast.
3. Choose the type of content you watched during the live stream.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the content.
5. Select your overall satisfaction with the live stream.
6. Share any additional comments or suggestions for future live streams.
7. Provide your email address (optional).
8. Enter your phone number (optional).
9. Add any additional comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What date was the broadcast?**: Enter the date of the live broadcast you are providing feedback on.
* **What time was the broadcast?**: Enter the time of the live broadcast.
* **Type of content?**: Select the type of content you watched during the live stream. Choose from the options: Label 1, Label 2, or Label 3.
* **How satisfied were you with the content?**: Choose your level of satisfaction with the content. Select all that apply from: Satisfied, Unsatisfied, or Neither.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the live stream?**: Rate your satisfaction with the live stream on a scale of your preference.
* **What would you like to see more of in future live streams?**: Share any additional comments or suggestions for future live streams.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your experience with the live stream.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Additional comments**: Add any additional comments about your experience with the live stream.
