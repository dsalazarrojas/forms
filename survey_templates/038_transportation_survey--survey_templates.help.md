# Transportation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Survey is designed to collect information about your commuting habits and preferences. This survey will help us understand how people commute, what modes of transportation they prefer, and how frequently they use public transportation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as prompted. Most fields are optional, and you can skip any questions that don't apply to you. If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How Do You Commute**: This field is a text entry field where you can describe your typical commute.
- **What Is Your Preferred Mode Of Transportation**: This field is a dropdown menu where you can select your preferred mode of transportation (e.g., Bike, Car, Train).
- **How Many Days A Week Do You Travel For Work**: This field is a number field where you can enter the number of days you travel for work.
- **How Far You Travel For Work**: This field is a text entry field where you can describe the distance you travel for work.
- **What Time Do You Leave Home**: This field is a time field where you can enter the time you leave home in 24-hour format.
- **What Time You Arrive At Work**: This field is a time field where you can enter the time you arrive at work in 24-hour format.
- **How Frequently Do You Use Public Transportation**: This field is a multiple-select field where you can select how frequently you use public transportation (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
- **What Is Your Favorite Mode Of Transportation**: This field is a dropdown menu where you can select your favorite mode of transportation.
- **What Mode Of Transportation Do You Think Is The Most Cost-Effective**: This field is a dropdown menu where you can select the mode of transportation you think is the most cost-effective.
- **How Long Do You Travel In Your Commute**: This field is a number field where you can enter the duration of your commute.
- **Are You Satisfied With Your Current Mode Of Transportation**: This field is a select-one field where you can rate your satisfaction with your current mode of transportation.
- **Do You Have Any Comments About Transportation**: This field is a text field where you can provide any comments or feedback about your transportation experience.
- **Do You Have Any Additional Comments About Transportation**: This field is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your transportation experience.
- **Email**: This field is an email field where you can enter your email address.
- **Phone**: This field is a text field where you can enter your phone number.

Note that most fields are optional, and you can skip any questions that don't apply to you. If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to ask.
