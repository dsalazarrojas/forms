# Research Study Participation Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Study Participation Permission Form is designed to collect essential information from participants before initiating a research study. This form serves as a formal consent document, ensuring that participants understand their rights and responsibilities during the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the Participant Information section with your personal details.
2. In the Study Information section, provide a detailed description of the research study.
3. Check the boxes in the Informed Consent section to indicate your consent for the study.
4. Enter your rights as a participant in the Participant Rights section.
5. Select the research facilities that will be used during the study.
6. Choose the data collection options that apply to you.
7. Select the level of data security that suits your preferences.
8. Provide contact information for yourself and the research coordinator.
9. Enter the emergency contact person's name and contact information.
10. Check the boxes in the Participant Declaration section to declare your willingness to participate.
11. Select the facilities that will be used during the study.
12. Choose the data destruction period that applies to you.
13. Enter the date of consent and sign the form electronically or physically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`): Enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Study Information** (`study_info`): Describe the research study, including its purpose, methods, and expected outcomes.
* **Study Description** (`study_description`): Provide a detailed explanation of the research study and its objectives.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`): Check the boxes to indicate your consent for:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Decline
* **Participant Rights** (`participant_rights`): Enter your rights as a participant, including your expectations and limitations.
* **Research Facilities** (`research_facilities`): Select the research facilities that will be used during the study.
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`): Choose the data collection options that apply to you:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Data Security** (`data_security`): Select the level of data security that suits your preferences:
	+ Public
	+ Private
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your contact information.
* **Research Coordinator** (`research_coordinator`): Enter the research coordinator's name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the emergency contact person's name and contact information.
* **Participant Declaration** (`participant_declaration`): Check the boxes to declare your willingness to participate:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Facilities Used** (`research_facilities`): Select the facilities that will be used during the study.
* **Data Destruction** (`data_destruction`): Choose the data destruction period that applies to you:
	+ Within 1-2 years
	+ Within 5 years
	+ After 5 years
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`): Enter the date of consent.
* **Signature** (`consent_signature`): Sign the form electronically or physically.
