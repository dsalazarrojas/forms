# Sports Policy And Conduct Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from sports clubs about their policy and conduct.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the sports club information in the first text field.
2. Provide answers to the policy and conduct questions in the second text field.
3. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the third text field.
4. Indicate if you are the club's coach or manager by selecting the corresponding option in the fourth field.
5. Indicate if staff or coach is responsible for the club by selecting the corresponding option in the fifth field.
6. Confirm if training has been completed for the club by selecting the corresponding option in the sixth field.
7. Confirm if training is required for the club by selecting the corresponding option in the seventh field.
8. If you have any feedback, please enter it in the eighth text field.
9. Enter the club's email address in the ninth field.
10. Enter the club's phone number in the tenth field.
11. Enter the club's address (if applicable) in the eleventh text field.
12. Enter any additional comments in the last text field.
13. Select the date and time of the survey completion in the thirteenth and fourteenth fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sports Club Information** (`sports_club_info`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the sports club, including its name and location.
* **Sports Club Information Answers** (`sports_club_info_answers`, text, required/optional): Answer the policy and conduct questions related to the sports club.
* **Sports Club Comments** (`sports_club_info_comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the sports club.
* **Coach Or Manager** (`coach_or_manager`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you are the club's coach or manager, "False" otherwise.
* **Staff Or Coach** (`staff_or_coach`, select_one, required): Select "True" if staff or coach is responsible for the club, "False" otherwise.
* **Training Completed** (`training_completed`, select_one, required): Select "True" if training has been completed for the club, "False" otherwise.
* **Training Required** (`training_required`, select_one, required): Select "True" if training is required for the club, "False" otherwise.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required/optional): Enter any feedback or comments about the survey or the sports club.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the sports club's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the sports club's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the sports club's address (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the sports club.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of the survey completion.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time of the survey completion.
* **Coach Info** (`coach_info`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the sports club's coach or manager.

## Tips
Please make sure to complete all required fields. If a field is marked as required, it is essential for the survey completion.
