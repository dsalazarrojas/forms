# Agriculture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agriculture Survey form is a tool used to collect data about farming activities, resource management, and future plans for agricultural development. The data collected will be used to inform decisions and improve agriculture practices in the region.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by providing a brief Farm Name and Location.
*   Select the Primary Activity of your farm (Crop Farming, Livestock Rearing, Mixed Farming, Aquaculture, or Forestry).
*   Indicate the Main Water Source (Well / Groundwater, River / Stream, Rainfed, Municipal Supply, or Canal System).
*   Rate the Soil Quality (Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
*   Select the main type of Fertilizer usage (Chemical / Synthetic, Organic / Manure, Both, or None).
*   Choose the methods used for pest control (Chemical Pesticides, Biological Control, Crop Rotation, Manual Removal, or None).
*   Provide a description of the Annual Yield.
*   Identify the Major Challenges faced (Climate / Weather, Market Prices, Labor Shortage, Pest Infestations, Input Costs, or Access to Credit).
*   Indicate if there are plans to expand or invest in the farm in the future.
*   Finally, provide a contact email address if you wish to receive the survey results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, text, optional): Provide a brief name for your farm.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the state or region where your farm is located.
*   **Primary Activity** (`primary_activity`, select_one, required): Choose the main focus of your farm (Crop Farming, Livestock Rearing, Mixed Farming, Aquaculture, or Forestry).
*   **Main Water Source** (`water_source`, select_one, required): Indicate the primary method used for irrigation on your farm (Well / Groundwater, River / Stream, Rainfed, Municipal Supply, or Canal System).
*   **Soil Quality Rating** (`soil_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of your soil (Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
*   **Fertilizer Usage** (`fertilizer_usage`, select_one, required): Select the type of fertilizer used most often on your farm (Chemical / Synthetic, Organic / Manure, Both, or None).
*   **Pest Control Methods** (`pest_control`, select_multiple, required): Choose the methods used to control pests on your farm (Chemical Pesticides, Biological Control, Crop Rotation, Manual Removal, or None).
*   **Annual Yield Description** (`annual_yield`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the annual yield (e.g., 50 tons of corn).
*   **Major Challenges** (`challenges`, select_multiple, required): Identify the biggest obstacles faced by your farm (Climate / Weather, Market Prices, Labor Shortage, Pest Infestations, Input Costs, or Access to Credit).
*   **Future Investment Plans** (`future_plans`, select_one, required): Indicate if there are plans to expand or invest in the farm in the future (True, False, or Uncertain).
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_info`, email, optional): Enter a contact email address to receive the survey results.
