# Cognitive Reframing Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cognitive Reframing Worksheet is a personalized tool for identifying and challenging negative thoughts and emotions related to specific situations. It's designed to help you develop a more balanced and realistic perspective, leading to improved emotional regulation and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most out of this worksheet, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information about your situation.
2. Be as specific as possible when describing your thoughts, feelings, and experiences.
3. Rate your emotions and thoughts on the provided scales (e.g., "1 - Mild" to "5 - Overwhelming").
4. Review and reflect on your answers, and consider alternative perspectives and more realistic thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (id: 2, type: text, required: true): Enter your name to help us keep track of your progress.
- **Date of Reflection** (id: 3, type: date, required: true): Choose a date for when you started reflecting on this situation.
- **Describe the Situation** (id: 5, type: text, required: true): Clearly and concisely explain the situation that triggered your thoughts and emotions.
- **Your Emotional Response** (id: 6, type: text, required: true): Describe how you felt about the situation.
- **Emotion Intensity** (id: 7, type: select_one, required: true): Rate your current emotional intensity using the provided scale.
- **Automatic Thoughts** (id: 9, type: text, required: true): Write down any thoughts that came to mind immediately.
- **Thought Pattern Type** (id: 10, type: select_one, required: false): Choose from the provided options to help identify any thinking patterns that might be contributing to your distress.
- **Evidence Supporting This Thought** (id: 12, type: text, required: true): List any facts or experiences that support your current thoughts.
- **Evidence Contradicting This Thought** (id: 13, type: text, required: true): Describe any facts or experiences that contradict your thoughts.
- **Alternative Perspective** (id: 15, type: text, required: true): Consider a more balanced view of the situation.
- **More Realistic Thought** (id: 16, type: text, required: true): Write a more accurate and balanced thought about the situation.
- **Positive Aspect of the Situation** (id: 17, type: text, required: false): Identify any potential positive outcomes or opportunities related to the situation.
- **Coping Strategies** (id: 19, type: text, required: true): Describe the actions you'll take to cope with the situation and manage your emotions.
- **New Emotion Intensity** (id: 20, type: select_one, required: true): Rate your emotional intensity after reflecting on the situation.
- **Specific Action Steps** (id: 21, type: text, required: true): Outline concrete steps you'll take to address the situation.
- **Progress Notes** (id: 23, type: text, required: false): Record your progress and observations about your thought patterns and emotions over time.
