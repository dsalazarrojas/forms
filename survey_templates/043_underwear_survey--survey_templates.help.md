# Underwear Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences and habits related to wearing underwear. Please fill out this survey to help us understand how you live your daily life and what you wear under your clothes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Make sure to answer the questions honestly and accurately.
3. If a field is required, make sure to fill it out.
4. If you have any notes or comments, you can add them in the "Note" field.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Surname** (Surname, text, optional): Please enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Please enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (Date Of Birth, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/1990 or 01/01/01/1990).
* **What is your preferred underweard type?** (select multiple, optional): Select all that apply, Men, Women, or Unisex.
* **How often do you wear underwear?** (select one, optional): Select one of the following options, Often, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **Time of day you wear underweard?** (time, optional): Enter the time of day you wear your underwear in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 14:30).
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your underwear preferences or habits.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your underwear preferences or habits.
