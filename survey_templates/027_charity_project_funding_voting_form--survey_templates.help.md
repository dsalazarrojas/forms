# Charity Project Funding Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals on how to allocate funding to two separate charity projects, "Project A" and "Project B". The form aims to gather data on each project's alignment with the organization's mission, potential impact, feasibility of implementation, and voting preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your name and email address to ensure accurate voting records.
2. For each project, rate your alignment with the organization's mission, potential impact, and feasibility of implementation.
3. Choose which project to fund or not fund.
4. Optionally, specify which project should be given highest priority if both projects cannot be funded.
5. Provide a rationale for your voting decisions, if desired.
6. Add any additional comments or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`voter_name`, required): Enter your full name to complete this field.
* **Email Address** (`voter_email`, required): Enter your email address for voting records. This field is required for tracking and verification purposes.
* **Organization or Role** (`voter_organization`, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, please specify your role or relationship with the organization.
* **Voting Round** (`voting_round`, required): Indicate which funding cycle or round this vote is for.
* **Date of Vote** (`voting_date`, required): Specify the date on which you are voting.
* **Project Name** (`project_1_name`, `project_2_name`, required): Enter the names of the two projects under consideration.
* **Alignment with Mission** (`project_1_alignment`, `project_2_alignment`, required): Rate how well each project aligns with the organization's mission, using the scale: Excellent, Good, Moderate, Poor.
* **Potential Impact Score** (`project_1_impact`, `project_2_impact`, required): Rate the expected impact of each project, using the scale: Exceptional, High, Moderate, Low.
* **Feasibility of Implementation** (`project_1_feasibility`, `project_2_feasibility`, required): Rate how feasible each project is to be implemented, using the scale: Highly Feasible, Feasible, Somewhat Feasible, Not Feasible.
* **Budget Reasonableness** (`project_1_budget_rating`, optional): Rate the budget of each project, using the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Your Vote for This Project** (`project_1_vote`, `project_2_vote`, required): Choose to fund or not fund each project, using the options: Definitely Fund, Probably Fund, Undecided, Probably Not Fund, Definitely Not Fund.
* **Which Project Should Be Highest Priority** (`highest_priority`, optional): If both projects cannot be funded, choose which one should be given the highest priority.
* **Rationale for Voting Decisions** (`rationale`, optional): Provide a reason for your voting decisions, if desired.
* **Additional Comments or Concerns** (`additional_comments`, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns you have about the projects or voting process.
