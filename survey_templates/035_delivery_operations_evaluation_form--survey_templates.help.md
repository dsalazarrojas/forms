# Delivery Operations Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the delivery team and service. It helps identify areas of improvement and provides insights into customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and answer the questions based on your experience with the delivery team and service.
2. Select the options that best match your experience for each question.
3. Answer all questions, even if you have no issues with a particular aspect of the delivery service.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the delivery service in the "Other Comments" section.
