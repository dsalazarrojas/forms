# Drama Character Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about a character's traits, relationships, and themes in a story or drama.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the character's name and any other relevant information about the character.
2. Answer the questions about the character's motive, development, and motivation. This will help you understand the character's behavior and personality.
3. Identify the themes in the story or drama and select the options that best describe them.
4. Provide quotes that illustrate the character's personality or traits.
5. Add any additional comments or insights about the character.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing information about the character.
* Use quotes that are accurate and relevant to the character's personality or traits.
