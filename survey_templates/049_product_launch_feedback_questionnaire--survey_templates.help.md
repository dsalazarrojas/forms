# Product Launch Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with a new product launch.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each section and answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. You can skip any sections that don't apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Very Easy
* **How was your experience with our new product**: Please describe your overall experience with the new product, including any notable highlights or issues you encountered.
### Which area of our product do you think needs improvement
* **Product Design** (select one): Choose one or more areas where you think our product needs improvement.
	+ Product Design
	+ User Interface
	+ Functionality
	+ Performance
	+ Features
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How satisfied were you with our new product** (required): On a scale of 1-5, rate your overall satisfaction with the product.
* **How easy was it to use our new product** (required): Choose how easy or difficult it was to use the product.
	+ Very Easy
	+ Somewhat Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **What new features would you like to see in our product** (optional): If you have any ideas for new features that would improve the product, please share them here.
* **What triggered your purchase of our new product** (optional): Choose the reason(s) why you purchased the new product.
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Online advertisement
	+ Social media
	+ Friend or family member
	+ Other (please specify)
	+ None of the above
* **How much would you pay for our new product** (optional): If you could pay any price for the new product, how much would you be willing to pay?
* **When did you launch our new product** (optional): If you know the date when the new product was launched, please enter it here.
* **Where did you purchase our new product** (required): Choose where you purchased the new product.
	+ Online store
	+ Physical store
	+ Online market
	+ Other (please specify)
	+ None of the above
* **Would you return to our store for another product** (required): Please answer whether you would return to our store for another product.
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Do you have any suggestions for our new product** (optional): If you have any ideas for how we can improve the product, please share them here.
* **How would you rate our new product** (optional): Choose a rating for the new product.
	+ (1-5 stars)
