# Anti Infectives Key Opinion Leader KOL Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information from Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) on their expertise and opinions on anti-infective agents, treatment preferences, and willingness to participate in advisory activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Section 1: Respondent Information** 
   - This section gathers basic contact information from the KOL to ensure that their responses are associated with their correct professional affiliation.

2. **Section 2: Name** 
   - Please provide your name, or optionally, select the "anonymity" option to keep your response anonymous.

3. **Section 3: Organization or institution** 
   - Enter the name of your institution or company.

4. **Section 4: Specialty area or discipline** 
   - Choose the area of specialization in the field of Infectious Diseases Microbiology.

5. **Section 5: Familiarity with anti-infective agents** 
   - Self-assess your level of familiarity with anti-infective agents, which can be Low, Moderate, High, or Expert.

6. **Section 6: Perceived unmet needs in treatment** 
   - Select all the areas where you perceive there are unmet medical needs in treatment for anti-infective agents.

7. **Section 7: Treatment preferences and rationale** 
   - Provide a free-text description of your treatment preferences and the rationale behind them.

8. **Section 8: Willingness to participate in advisory activities** 
   - Indicate your willingness to participate in future advisory activities.

9. **Section 9: Additional comments or recommendations** 
   - Add any final comments or provide evidence references to support your opinions.

## Tips
* Please provide honest and informed responses to ensure that the collected data is valuable for decision-making.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the form administrator for guidance.
* Only submit your response once to avoid duplication of effort.
