# Public Space Usage Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences and preferences regarding public spaces. It's a way for you to share your opinions and help us understand how to improve public spaces for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. If a field is marked as optional, you can choose not to fill it out if you don't have an answer. Please answer honestly and provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you're from an organization, please enter your organization's name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experiences with public spaces.
* **Favorite Space** (`favorite_space`, `select_one`, optional): Choose which type of public space you like most (Library, Park, Museum, or Other).
* **Other Favorite Space** (`favorite_space_others`, `text`, optional): If you selected "Other" as your favorite space, please specify what type of public space it is.
* **Time In Public Space** (`time_in_public_space`, `time`, optional): Estimate how many hours you spend in public spaces per week.
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how often you visit public spaces (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Favorite Feature** (`favorite_feature`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the features that you like most about public spaces (Amenities, Aesthetics, Accessibility, Events, Safety, Other).
* **Other Favorite Feature** (`favorite_feature_others`, `text`, optional): If you selected "Other" as your favorite feature, please specify what it is.
* **Suggest Feature** (`suggest_feature`, `text`, optional): Suggest features that you would like to see added or improved in public spaces.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, optional): Please select "Submit" to finish filling out the form. If you selected "Cancel", your answers will not be saved.
