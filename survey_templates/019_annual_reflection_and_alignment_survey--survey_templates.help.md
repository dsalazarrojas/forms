# Annual Reflection And Alignment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather your thoughts, reflections, and feedback on your experiences, goals, and challenges within the company. It is essential to provide honest and open-ended responses to help us better understand your needs and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your personal details:
	* **Your Name**: Enter your first and last name.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
2. Answer the questions honestly, considering your experiences and feelings:
	* Select the best option that reflects your agreement or satisfaction level with each question.
	* For text fields, provide a brief but descriptive answer.
3. Be honest and open in your responses, and do not worry about giving negative feedback.

## Tips
* Be honest and open in your responses.
* Provide specific examples or descriptions when answering text fields.
* Do not worry about giving negative feedback; it helps us understand your concerns.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact your HR representative.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
