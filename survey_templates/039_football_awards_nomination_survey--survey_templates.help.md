# Football Awards Nomination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect nominations for football awards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have not already submitted a nomination for the same nominee and award category to avoid duplicate submissions.
2. Start by providing your name and title.
3. Choose the award category for the nominee.
4. Enter the nominee's name, position, and other relevant details.
5. Enter your name and position as the nominator.
6. Enter your email address and phone number.
7. Select the type of submission you are making (automatic or manual).
8. Choose the status of the nomination (pending, complete, or rejected).
9. Add any additional comments about the nomination or submission.
10. Confirm your submission and agree to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the first name of the nominee.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the last name of the nominee.

### School Name
* **School Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the school the nominee belongs to.

### Award Category
* **Award Category** (select one, required): Choose the category for which the nominee is being nominated (Football, Soccer, or Award).

### Award Year
* **Award Year** (number, required): Enter the year the nominee won the award.

### Nomination Reason
* **Nomination Reason** (text, optional): Provide any additional details about why the nominee deserves the award.

### Nominee Name
* **Nominee Name** (text, required): Enter the full name of the nominee.

### Nominee Position
* **Nominee Position** (select one, required): Choose the position of the nominee (Goalkeeper, Defender, Midfielder, or Forward).

### Nominator Name
* **Nominator Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as the nominator.

### Nominator Position
* **Nominator Position** (text, required): Enter your position as the nominator.

### Nominator Email
* **Nominator Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.

### Nominator Phone
* **Nominator Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.

### Nominator Institute
* **Nominator Institute** (text, required): Enter the name of your institution.

### Submission Date
* **Submission Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the submission.

### Submission Time
* **Submission Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the submission.

### Nomination Type
* **Nomination Type** (select one, required): Choose the type of nomination (Automatic, Manual, or Both).

### Nomination Status
* **Nomination Status** (select one, required): Choose the status of the nomination (Pending, Complete, or Rejected).

### Nomination Comment
* **Nomination Comment** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the nomination.

### Submission Comment
* **Submission Comment** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the submission.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Provide any additional information about the nomination or submission.

### Submission Confirmation
* **Submission Confirmation** (select one, required): Confirm that you have submitted the nomination correctly.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (text, optional): Provide any additional details that are not covered in the other fields.

### Nominator Note
* **Nominator Note** (text, optional): Add any notes from the nominator.

### Submission Note
* **Submission Note** (text, optional): Add any notes from the submission.

### Nominator Agreement
* **Nominator Agreement** (select one, required): Agree to the terms of the nomination.
