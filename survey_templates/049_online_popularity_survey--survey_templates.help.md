# Online Popularity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about online surveys, specifically their titles, descriptions, categories, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the survey in the "Form Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the survey in the "Description" field.
3. Select the category of the survey from the "Category" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the category ID (if required) in the "Category ID" field.
5. Enter the URL of the survey in the "URL" field (if required).
6. Enter the path to the survey URL in the "URL Path" field (if required).
7. Enter the email address of the person responsible for the survey in the "Email" field.
8. Enter any additional notes about the survey in the "Note" field.
9. Optionally, provide comments about the survey in the "Comments" field.
10. Select the status of the survey from the "Status" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the survey.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the survey.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the survey from the dropdown menu.
* **Category ID** (`form_category_id`, number, optional): Enter the category ID (if required).
* **URL** (`form_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the survey (if required).
* **URL Path** (`form_url_path`, text, optional): Enter the path to the survey URL (if required).
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the survey.
* **Note** (`form_note`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the survey.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Provide comments about the survey.
* **Comments ID** (`form_comments_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID for comments (if required).
* **Comments Count** (`form_comments_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of comments (if required).
* **Comments URL** (`form_comments_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL for comments (if required).
* **Status** (`form_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the survey from the dropdown menu.
* **Status ID** (`form_status_id`, number, optional): Enter the status ID (if required).
* **Status Count** (`form_status_count`, number, optional): Enter the status count (if required).
* **Created At** (`form_created_at`, date, optional): Enter the date the survey was created.
* **Updated At** (`form_updated_at`, date, optional): Enter the date the survey was last updated.
* **Deleted At** (`form_deleted_at`, date, optional): Enter the date the survey was deleted.
* **Created By** (`form_created_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the survey.
* **Created By ID** (`form_created_by_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the person who created the survey.
* **Updated By** (`form_updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who last updated the survey.
* **Updated By ID** (`form_updated_by_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the person who last updated the survey.
* **Deleted By** (`form_deleted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who deleted the survey.
* **Deleted By ID** (`form_deleted_by_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the person who deleted the survey.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult the relevant documentation or reach out to the survey administrators for clarification.
