# New Client Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Client Survey Form is used to collect relevant information about new clients, including their category of interest, location, and contact details. This data is used to categorize and organize client preferences for further analysis and potential business development opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. In the "Feedback" field, briefly describe why you would like to get in contact with us.
5. Select one or more categories that interest you the most (Software, Hardware, Web Development, or any other).
6. Enter your location (city, state, or region).
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (id: 1)**: Enter your first name here. This will be used to identify you as a client.
* **Last Name (id: 2)**: Enter your last name here. This will be used to identify you as a client.
* **Email (id: 3)**: Enter your email address here. This will be used for communication and potential follow-up contact.
* **Phone (id: 4)**: Enter your phone number here. This will be used for potential phone or text contact.
* **Feedback (id: 5)**: Briefly describe why you would like to get in contact with us. This will help us understand your needs and preferences.
* **Category (id: 6)**: Select one or more categories that interest you the most. These categories will be used to categorize and organize client preferences.
	+ Software: Interested in software solutions or services.
	+ Hardware: Interested in hardware solutions or services.
	+ Web Development: Interested in web development solutions or services.
* **Location (id: 7)**: Enter your location (city, state, or region). This will help us understand your regional preferences and needs.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- Ensure that you are filling out this form for a new client and not updating an existing one.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
