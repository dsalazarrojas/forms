# tech_conference_attendee_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a feedback survey for tech conference attendees. It aims to gather feedback on the attendee's experience, preferences, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the sections below to understand the questions and options.
2. Answer each question according to your thoughts and experiences.
3. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
4. Ensure you have completed all the questions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee_Info** (`Name`, `Email`, required/optional): This section asks for your name and email address. Please provide your name as it appears on your badge or business card, and your email address for communication purposes.
* **Feedback_Rating** (`Good`, `Fair`, `Bad`, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the conference. Please select one of the three options: Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Top_Session** (`Yes`, `No`, required/optional): Indicate if you attended the session with the highest rating. Please select either Yes or No.
* **Favorite_Session_Topic** (`Yes`, `No`, required/optional): Choose your favorite session topic. Select Yes if you enjoyed the topic, and No otherwise.
* **Favorite_Session_Time** (`time`, required/optional): Indicate your favorite session time. Please enter the time of your preferred session.
* **Satisfaction** (`rating`, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with the conference. Please enter a number between 1 and 5.
* **Comment** (`text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the conference.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone_Numb** (`number`, required/optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Session_Attended** (`Yes`, `No`, required/optional): Indicate if you attended the session. Select Yes or No.
* **Session_Preference** (`Yes`, `No`, required/optional): State your session preference. Select Yes or No.
* **Top_Three_Suggestions** (`text`, required/optional): Suggest three improvements for future conferences. Be as specific as possible.
* **Session_Length** (`Yes`, `No`, required/optional): Indicate if the session length was sufficient. Select Yes or No.
* **Session_Type** (`Yes`, `No`, required/optional): State your session type preference. Select Yes or No.
* **Session_Topic** (`Yes`, `No`, required/optional): Choose your session topic preference. Select Yes or No.
* **Additional_Feedback** (`text`, required/optional): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
* **Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Company** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your company or affiliation (optional).
* **Job_Title** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your job title or position (optional).
* **Position** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your job position or role (optional).
* **Session_1**, **Session_2**, **Session_3** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the names of your favorite sessions (optional).
* **Comments** (`text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts.

Note: All fields with `required/optional` label are optional unless otherwise stated. Please ensure to fill in all fields before submitting the form. 

Tips: Be honest and provide as much detail as possible. The more you provide, the more valuable the feedback will be to the conference organizers. 

Please submit the form after completing all questions. The survey is designed to take a few minutes to complete. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [support email].
