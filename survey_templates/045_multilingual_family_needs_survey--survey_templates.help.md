# multilingual_family_needs_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the language needs and support needs of a family. It is intended to be completed by a family member or guardian, and will help us better understand the language needs of your family. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the languages spoken by each family member from the options provided.
3. Describe any additional languages spoken by family members, if applicable.
4. Provide a count of the number of family members who took the language survey.
5. Report any language or support needs of the family.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Language Survey** (`family_language_survey`): This field is not directly related to the other fields. It seems to be a standalone field for collecting language survey information about the family. Please answer this question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
* **Family Member 0** (`family_member_0_name`): This field is for the name of the first family member.
* **Family Member 0 Language Survey** (`family_member_0_language_survey`): This field is for the language survey of the first family member.
* **Family Member 0 Support Needs** (`family_member_0_support_needs`): This field is for the support needs of the first family member.
* **Family Member 1** (`family_member_1_name`): This field is for the name of the second family member.
* **Family Member 1 Language Survey** (`family_member_1_language_survey`): This field is for the language survey of the second family member.
* **Family Member 1 Support Needs** (`family_member_1_support_needs`): This field is for the support needs of the second family member.
* **Family Member 2** (`family_member_2_name`): This field is for the name of the third family member.
* **Family Member 2 Language Survey** (`family_member_2_language_survey`): This field is for the language survey of the third family member.
* **Family Member 2 Support Needs** (`family_member_2_support_needs`): This field is for the support needs of the third family member.
* **Family Member 3** (`family_member_3_name`): This field is for the name of the fourth family member.
* **Family Member 3 Language Survey** (`family_member_3_language_survey`): This field is for the language survey of the fourth family member.
