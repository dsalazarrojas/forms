# Personal Injury Attorney Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who have suffered a personal injury and are seeking consultation from an attorney. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant details about the injury, including the location and date of the incident, the type of injury, and the involvement of witnesses or medical reports. This information will help the attorney to assess the case and provide guidance on the best course of action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address to identify yourself.
2. Enter your Phone Number to enable the attorney to contact you easily.
3. Provide the Date of Injury and Location of Injury to help the attorney understand the incident.
4. Describe the injury in detail, including any medical reports or treatments received.
5. Specify the Name of Attorney you are consulting with, along with their Phone and Email.
6. Choose your Insurance Provider from the list (if applicable).
7. If necessary, specify any Other Insurance Provider (if not listed).
8. Select any applicable options for Type of Injury, Witnesses, Medical Bills, Medical Reports, Police Report, Hospitalization, Lost Wages, Emotional Disturbance, Physical Disturbance, Property Damage, Vehicle, or Other Injuries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is known to the attorney.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is known to the attorney.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so the attorney can contact you via email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Date of Injury**: Enter the date of the injury in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Date of Last Treatment**: Enter the date of the last treatment for the injury in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Location of Injury**: Enter the location where the injury occurred.
* **Description of Injury**: Describe the injury in detail, including any medical reports or treatments received.
* **Name of Attorney**: Enter the name of the attorney you are consulting with.
* **Phone of Attorney**: Enter the phone number of the attorney.
* **Email of Attorney**: Enter the email of the attorney.
* **Insurance Provider**: Select the insurance provider from the list (Aetna, Blue Cross, Blue Shield, Cigna, Humana, or None) that covers the injury.
* **Other Insurance Provider**: If the insurance provider is not listed, select "Other" and enter the name of the other insurance provider.
* **Type of Injury**: Choose the type of injury (Car Accident, Slip and Fall, or Other) that occurred.
* **Witnesses**: Select any witnesses that were present during the incident.
* **Medical Bills**: Select any medical bills related to the injury.
* **Medical Reports**: Select any medical reports related to the injury.
* **Police Report**: Select if a police report was filed.
* **Hospitalization**: Select if you were hospitalized due to the injury.
* **Lost Wages**: Select if you lost wages due to the injury.
* **Emotional Disturbance**: Select if you experienced emotional disturbance due to the injury.
* **Physical Disturbance**: Select if you experienced physical disturbance due to the injury.
* **Property Damage**: Select if property was damaged during the incident.
* **Vehicle**: Select if a vehicle was involved in the incident.
* **Other Injuries**: Enter any other injuries or complications that occurred due to the injury.
