# digital_form_creation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from end-users on their experience of creating digital forms. Your input will help us understand areas for improvement and make the form creation process more efficient and user-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as accurately as possible. You can choose from the available options for each question, and if you need more time, you can take as long as you need. There's no time limit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Creating a digital form** (`form_creation_experience`): This question is asking about your overall experience creating the digital form. Please describe the steps you went through and any difficulties you faced.
* **How did you find the form design process?** (`form_design_process`): This question helps us understand how easy or difficult you found the form design process. Choose from the options: Easy, Difficult, or Not sure.
* **What would you like to provide feedback on?** (`user_feedback`): This question asks about specific areas where you'd like to give feedback on the form. Select from the available options: Form layout, Form design, or Other (please specify).
* **How satisfied were you with the form creation process?** (`overall_satisfaction`): This question assesses your overall satisfaction with the form creation process. You can choose from a range of numbers to indicate your satisfaction level.
* **How long did it take you to complete the form?** (`form_completion_time`): This question measures the time you spent on the form, but please note that this is a rough estimate and may not be accurate.
* **Hours spent on the form** (`form_completion_time_hours`): This is an optional question to help us better understand the time spent on the form. You can choose the number of hours you spent.
* **How would you rate your experience creating the form?** (`user_experience`): This is a space for you to provide any additional feedback or thoughts about your experience creating the form. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Minutes spent on the form** (`form_completion_time_minutes`): This is another optional question to help us gauge the time spent on the form. You can choose the number of minutes you spent.

## Tips
- Take as much time as you need to provide accurate answers.
- If you're unsure about an option, choose the one that best fits your experience.
- Please be as descriptive as possible when providing feedback in the "Other (please specify)" field.
