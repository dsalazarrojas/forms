# luxury_travel_experience_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback about your luxury travel experience.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your information in the Customer Information page.
2. Describe your travel experience in the Travel Details page.
3. Answer the Services & Amenities page.
4. Rate your experience on the Overall Experience page.
5. Provide suggestions for improvement on the Suggestions for Improvement page.
6. Rate your satisfaction on the Satisfaction page.
7. Enter your recommendations on the Recommendations page.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Travel Details** (`Travel Details`, text, optional): Tell us about your trip.
* **Services & Amenities** (`Services & Amenities`, select_multiple, optional): Choose services you used during your trip.
* **Overall Experience** (`Overall Experience`, select_one, required): Rate your experience as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`Suggestions for Improvement`, text, optional): Tell us anything you think we can do better.
* **Satisfaction** (`Satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your level of satisfaction.
* **Recommendations** (`Recommendations`, text, required): Enter any other recommendations you have.
## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you have a suggestion, please be specific.
* If you have any other recommendations, please provide as much detail as possible.
