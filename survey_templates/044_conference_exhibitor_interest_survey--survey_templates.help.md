This help guide is intended for conference exhibitors to understand the purpose and process of completing the Conference Exhibitor Interest Survey. 

The purpose of this form is to collect information from exhibitors about their interest in participating in the conference, specifically in regards to booth size and location, display requirements, anticipated leads, level of interest in sponsorship packages, marketing materials to provide, and other relevant details. 

This survey aims to avoid duplicating effort and minimize the risk of exhibitors being contacted by multiple parties about the same event. 

Please note that this guide is based on the provided YAML structure and field labels, and explanations will be made best-effort to understand the intended purpose of each field.

# Conference Exhibitor Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Exhibitor Interest Survey is designed to collect information from exhibitors about their interest in participating in the conference, including their booth preference, display requirements, and other relevant details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the `Organization name` field with the name of your company or organization. 
2. Provide the `Contact name` and `Contact email` fields with the name and email address of your primary contact person. 
3. Select your preferred booth type from the `Booth interest level` dropdown menu. 
4. If applicable, describe your display requirements in the `Display requirements` field. 
5. If you're expecting a certain number of leads from the event, enter that number in the `Expected number of leads` field. 
6. Indicate your level of interest in sponsorship packages in the `Sponsorship interest` dropdown menu. 
7. If you're willing to provide marketing materials, enter details in the `Marketing materials` field. 
8. If you have a preferred payment contact, provide their information in the `Payment contact` field. 
9. Use the `Additional notes` field to provide any further information for the organizers' consideration. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization name** (1, text, required): The name of your company or organization. 
* **Contact name** (2, text, required): The name of your primary contact person. 
* **Contact email** (3, email, required): The email address of your primary contact person, used for follow-up and contract details. 
* **Booth interest level** (4, select_one, required): Your preferred booth type: Standard, Corner, Premium, or Table top. 
* **Display requirements** (5, text, optional): Describe your display requirements, such as power or internet needs. 
* **Expected number of leads** (6, number, optional): The anticipated number of leads from the event. 
* **Sponsorship interest** (7, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of interest in sponsorship packages: Interested, Maybe, or Not interested. 
* **Marketing materials** (8, text, optional): Enter details of the marketing materials you're willing to provide, such as brochures or digital assets. 
* **Payment contact** (9, text, optional): Provide contact information for your billing contact. 
* **Additional notes** (10, text, optional): Share any further information for the organizers' consideration. 

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly. 
* Ensure that your contact email is up-to-date and valid. 
* Be specific when describing your display requirements and marketing materials. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the organizers.
