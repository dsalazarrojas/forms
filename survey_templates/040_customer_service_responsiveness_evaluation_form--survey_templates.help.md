# Customer Service Responsiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Responsiveness Evaluation Form is a tool used to evaluate the responsiveness of our customer service team. It helps us understand how well our representatives handle interactions and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the option that best describes your interaction with the customer service representative.
2. Enter your answer in the text fields provided.
3. Rate the customer service representative's communication and resolution rate.
4. Select the time it took to resolve the issue.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the interaction.
6. Enter any additional comments about the interaction.
7. Select "Submit" to complete the form and "Do not submit" to save your work for later.
