# Municipal Election Issues Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Municipal Election Issues Survey is designed to collect citizen feedback on municipal services and community priorities to inform decision-making and improve services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For the "service_satisfaction" question, select all that apply from the options provided.
3. In the "local_priorities" field, enter your top priorities for your local community, separated by commas (e.g., "public safety, education, and transportation").
4. If you have any comments or concerns, please enter them in the "community_involvement" field.
5. If you wish to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address in the "contact_us" field.
6. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "phone_number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Service Satisfaction

* **service_satisfaction** (`select_multiple`): How satisfied are you with the services provided by your local municipality? Select all that apply:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Not At All Satisfied

### Local Priorities

* **local_priorities** (`text`): What are your top priorities for your local community?
	+ Enter your priorities separated by commas (e.g., "public safety, education, and transportation")

### Community Involvement

* **community_involvement** (`note`): Do you have any comments or concerns about the community?
	+ Enter any thoughts or ideas you have about your local community

### Contact Us

* **contact_us** (`email`): Would you like to be contacted for follow-up?
	+ Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted

### Phone Number

* **phone_number** (`text`): Would you like to provide your phone number?
	+ Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted
