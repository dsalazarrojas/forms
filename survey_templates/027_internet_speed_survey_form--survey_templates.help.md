# Internet Speed Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internet Speed Survey Form is designed to collect information about end users' experiences with internet speeds. This data will help us understand and improve internet performance for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your **Place** where you are currently located.
2. Choose your **Internet Service Provider** from the list.
3. Enter your **Download Speed** in megabits per second.
4. Enter your **Upload Speed** in megabits per second.
5. Select your **Router Type** from the options provided.
6. Provide your **Location** for further analysis.
7. Select any relevant **Router Type 2** options (if applicable).
8. Optionally, provide any additional **Comments** about your experience.
9. Enter your **Contact Email** (if you'd like to receive follow-up information).
10. Enter your **Contact Phone** (if you'd like to receive follow-up information).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Place** (`place`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the city, state, or region where you are currently located.
* **Internet Service Provider** (`internet_service_provider`, `select_one`, required): Select the company that provides your internet service.
* **Download Speed** (`download_speed`, `number`, required): Enter your download speed in megabits per second. This will help us understand your connection's download capabilities.
* **Upload Speed** (`upload_speed`, `number`, required): Enter your upload speed in megabits per second. This will help us understand your connection's upload capabilities.
* **Router Type** (`router_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of router you are currently using.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter your location for further analysis and reference.
* **Router Type 2** (`router_type_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have multiple routers, select any relevant options that apply.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any comments or additional information, feel free to share them with us.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive follow-up information or updates.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to receive follow-up calls or updates.
