# Veneer Comfort And Speech Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veneer Comfort And Speech Survey is a tool used to gather feedback from patients about their experience with a veneer treatment or procedure. This form helps to assess the patient's comfort level, sensitivity, and overall satisfaction, as well as their ability to speak after the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Select your overall satisfaction with the veneer treatment or procedure from the "overall_satisfaction" dropdown menu.
3. Choose your comfort level with the veneer treatment or procedure from the "comfort_level" dropdown menu.
4. Select your level of sensitivity after the procedure from the "tooth_sensitivity" dropdown menu.
5. Choose if you have experienced any speech changes after the procedure from the "speech_changes" dropdown menu.
6. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "additional_comments" field.
7. If you have a contact email, please enter it in the "email" field.
8. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "phone_number" field.
9. Finally, enter the name and contact information of the person you would like to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (text, required/optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **overall_satisfaction** (select_one, required/optional): Please select one of the two options to express your overall satisfaction with the veneer treatment or procedure.
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **comfort_level** (select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all that apply to express your comfort level with the veneer treatment or procedure.
	+ Very comfortable
	+ Somewhat uncomfortable
	+ Not at all comfortable
* **tooth_sensitivity** (select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all that apply to express your level of sensitivity after the procedure.
	+ None
	+ Mild
	+ Moderate
	+ Severe
* **speech_changes** (select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all that apply to express any changes in your speech after the procedure.
	+ None
	+ Slightly changed
	+ Somewhat changed
	+ Significantly changed
* **email** (text, required/optional): If you have a contact email, please enter it here.
* **phone_number** (text, required/optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it here.
* **contact_person** (text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
