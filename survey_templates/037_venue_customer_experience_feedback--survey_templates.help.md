# Venue Customer Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
To collect feedback from customers about their experience at a venue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the context of the feedback.
2. Answer the questions below, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the venue.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback.
5. Enter your contact details so we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction**
*   This is a brief welcome message that introduces the purpose of the feedback form.

### 2. **Event Name**
*   **event_name (text, optional):** Enter the name of the event you attended at the venue.

### 3. **Event Date**
*   **event_date (date, optional):** Enter the date of the event you attended at the venue.

### 4. **Venue**
*   **venue (text, optional):** Enter the name of the venue you visited.

### 5. **Overall Rating**
*   **overall_rating (number, optional):** Rate your overall experience at the venue on a scale of 1-5.

### 6. **Satisfaction**
*   **satisfaction (select_one, optional):** Choose how satisfied you are with your experience at the venue.

### 7. **Comments**
*   **comments (text, optional):** Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

### 8. **Contact Details**
*   **contact_details (text, optional):** Provide your contact information so we can follow up with you.

## Tips
*   Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the comments section.
*   If you have any concerns or compliments about your experience, please include them in the comments section.
*   If you'd like to provide your contact information, please be sure to enter your name, email, and phone number.
