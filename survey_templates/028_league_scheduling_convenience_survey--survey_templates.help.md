# league_scheduling_convenience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's scheduling preferences and conflicts for a league game. It helps the game organizers understand player availability and preferences to schedule the game accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the introduction page to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Fill in your player name in the "Player Information" section.
3. Answer the "Availability" question by selecting either "Yes" or "No".
4. If you are available, answer the "Time Preferences" and "Date Preferences" sections by selecting your preferred time slots and dates.
5. For each "Conflict" section, select "Yes" if you have a conflict with another player, otherwise select "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Player Name (`player_name`, `text`, required):** 
Enter your name to be identified in the game.

*   **Availability (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required):** 
Select "Yes" if you are available for the game, otherwise select "No".

*   **Time Preferences (`time_preferences`, `select_one`, required):** 
Select your preferred time slot for the game.

*   **Date Preferences (`date_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required):** 
Select the dates you are available for the game.

*   **Conflicts 1-16 (`conflict_1`, `select_multiple`, required):** 
Select "Yes" if you have a conflict with other players for the specific time slot, otherwise select "No". 

*   **Submit (`submit`, `text`, optional):** 
Submit your response once you have answered all questions.
