# overdue_account_management_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The overdue_account_management_survey form is designed to collect information about account management, client responsiveness, communication practices, and collections workflows. This form is likely used to gather data on how efficiently the account management process is running, how clients are responding to follow-ups, and how workflows can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) in the field labels.
2. For each field, enter the requested information as accurately as possible.
3. Be sure to provide comments or additional information in the fields with comment labels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Management Survey** (`account_management_survey`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description or summary of the account management process.
* **Collection Workflows** (`collection_workflows`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Automated: Workflows are automated and executed without human intervention.
	+ Manual: Workflows are executed manually by a team member or team members.
	+ Hybrid: Workflows are a mix of automated and manual processes.
* **Communication Practices** (`communication_practices`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Automated: Communication is automated through emails or automated messages.
	+ Manual: Communication is done manually through phone calls or in-person meetings.
	+ Hybrid: Communication is a mix of automated and manual approaches.
* **Client Responsiveness** (`client_responsiveness`, `number`, required): Enter the client responsiveness score, where 1 is the least responsive and 5 is the most responsive.
* **Collections Workflows Improvement** (`collections_workflows_improvement`, `text`, required): Provide suggestions or ideas for improving the collections workflows.
* **Collections Follow-up** (`collections_follow_up`, `number`, required): Enter the number of follow-up activities performed per week.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, `time`, required): Enter the frequency of follow-up activities, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ SMS
* **Follow-up Response Rate** (`follow_up_response_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the response rate, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Collections Workflows Comments** (`collections_workflows_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving collections workflows.
* **Communication Practices Comments** (`communication_practices_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving communication practices.
* **Client Responsiveness Comments** (`client_responsiveness_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving client responsiveness.
* **Collections Improvement Comments** (`collections_improvement_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving collections workflows.
* **Collections Follow-up Comments** (`collections_follow_up_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving collections follow-up activities.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields.
* Provide as much detail as possible in each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field label or ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to gather data, so be honest and accurate in your responses.
