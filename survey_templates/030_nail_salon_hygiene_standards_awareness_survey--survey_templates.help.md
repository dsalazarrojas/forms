# Nail Salon Hygiene Standards Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gauge the cleanliness and safety standards of the nail salon, as well as your overall satisfaction and frequency of visits. Your input is essential to help the salon improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through the questions and answer each one honestly. Most questions have multiple options for you to choose from, while some are open-ended. Fill in your contact information at the end of the form if you wish to be contacted for further clarification or to receive a response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you rate our cleanliness?** (survey_title)
	+ This question asks about your overall impression of the salon's cleanliness. Choose a number that best represents your thoughts.
* **Rate the cleanliness of the salon** (cleanliness_scores)
	+ Select all that apply from the options below to rate the cleanliness of the salon.
	+ Very Clean
	+ Clean
	+ Somewhat Clean
	+ Not Clean
	+ Very Unclean
* **Rate the safety practices of the salon** (safety_score)
	+ Choose one option that best represents your impression of the salon's safety practices.
	+ Very Safe
	+ Somewhat Safe
	+ Not Safe
	+ Very Uncertain
* **How often do you interact with the staff?** (staff_wellness_scores)
	+ Choose one option that best represents how often you interact with the staff.
	+ Daily
	+ Several times a week
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction?** (overall_satisfaction)
	+ Choose one option that best represents your overall satisfaction with the salon.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **How often do you visit this salon?** (time_spent_in_salon)
	+ Choose one option that best represents how often you visit the salon.
	+ Daily
	+ Several times a week
	+ Weekly
	+ Rarely
* **Any comments or suggestions?** (comments)
	+ Write any comments or suggestions you have for the salon.
* **Email address** (email)
	+ If you wish to be contacted, please provide your email address.
* **Phone number** (phone)
	+ If you wish to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
* **Name** (name)
	+ Provide your name if you wish to be contacted or to have your response acknowledged.
