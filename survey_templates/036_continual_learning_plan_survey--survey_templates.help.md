# Continual Learning Plan Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help employees create a personalized learning plan that aligns with their career goals and objectives. The information collected through this form will be used to identify areas where the employee needs to develop new skills and provide recommendations on training and resources.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions carefully and to the best of your ability.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please seek clarification from your HR representative or manager.
3. The fields marked as "required" must be completed to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_full_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Current Job Title** (`current_job_title`, text, **required**): Enter your current job title as it is listed on your employment contract or job description.
* **Department/Team** (`department_team`, select_one, **required**): Select your department or team from the drop-down menu.
* **Identified Skill Gaps** (`current_skill_gaps`, text, **required**): List the skills you feel you need to develop to be more effective in your current or future role.
* **Preferred Learning Methods** (`preferred_learning_formats`, select_multiple, **required**): Select up to two methods from the drop-down menu that you prefer for learning.
* **Short-term Career Goals (Next 12 Months)** (`career_goals_short_term`, text, **required**): Enter your short-term career goals, which should be aligned with your job responsibilities and the company's objectives.
* **Hours per Week Available for Training** (`weekly_training_hours`, number, **required**): Estimate the number of hours you can realistically dedicate to training during your work hours.
* **Specific Resources or Software Needed** (`required_resources_software`, text, **optional**): If you need any specific resources or software to support your learning, enter them here.
* **Have you discussed these goals with your manager?** (`manager_discussion_status`, select_one, **required**): Confirm that you have discussed your goals with your manager.
* **Date of Completion** (`survey_completion_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date you have completed this survey.
* **Any other training suggestions for the team** (`additional_training_suggestions`, text, **optional**): If you have any additional ideas for team training or development, enter them here.

Please note that the fields with the "**required**" label must be completed to submit the form. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact your HR representative or manager.
