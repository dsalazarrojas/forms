# Class Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students or trainees about their experience in a class or training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form carefully, providing as much detail as possible for each section.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as required).
3. Select options for select fields (e.g. class duration, overall rating).
4. Be honest and thorough in your feedback, providing constructive suggestions for improvement.
5. Proofread your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address for results and follow-up (optional).
* **Class or Training Name** (`class_name`): Enter the name of the class or training you attended.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`): Enter the name of the instructor who taught the class.
* **Date of Class** (`class_date`): Enter the date you attended the class.
* **Duration of Class** (`class_duration`): Select the length of the class (e.g. Half day, Full day, etc.).
* **Overall Class Rating** (`overall_rating`): Rate your overall experience in the class (e.g. Excellent, Very good, etc.).
* **Quality of Content Rating** (`content_rating`): Rate the quality of the class material (e.g. Excellent, Very good, etc.).
* **Instructor Effectiveness Rating** (`instructor_rating`): Rate the effectiveness of the instructor (e.g. Excellent, Very good, etc.).
* **Were Learning Objectives Met** (`objectives_met`): Indicate whether the class met your learning objectives (e.g. Yes, completely, Yes, mostly, etc.).
* **Difficulty Level** (`difficulty_level`): Rate the difficulty level of the class for your skill level (e.g. Too easy, Somewhat easy, etc.).
* **Pace of Class** (`pace_rating`): Rate the speed of the class (e.g. Too slow, Just right, etc.).
* **Quality of Materials and Resources** (`materials_rating`): Rate the quality of materials and resources provided (e.g. Excellent, Very good, etc.).
* **Learning Environment Rating** (`environment_rating`): Rate the learning environment (e.g. Excellent, Very good, etc.).
* **What Skills Did You Gain** (`skills_gained`): Select all the skills you gained from the class (e.g. Technical skills, Communication skills, etc.).
* **How Likely Are You to Apply What You Learned** (`apply_learning`): Indicate how likely you are to apply what you learned in your work or studies (e.g. Very likely, Somewhat likely, etc.).
* **Would You Recommend This Class** (`recommend_class`): Indicate whether you would recommend this class to others (e.g. Definitely yes, Probably yes, etc.).
* **What Was the Best Aspect of the Class** (`best_aspect`): Describe the best aspect of the class.
* **What Could Be Improved** (`improvement_areas`): Provide suggestions for improvement.
* **What Additional Topics Would You Like** (`additional_topics`): Suggest additional topics for future classes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any other feedback or comments about the class.
