# Wildlife Health Outreach Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is part of the "wildlife health outreach effectiveness survey" initiative, aiming to collect feedback and opinions from users to better understand the effectiveness of wildlife health outreach programs.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Read through the form carefully and respond to each question as honestly as possible.
2.  For questions that ask for numerical values (e.g., "knowledge before" and "knowledge after"), please enter whole numbers.
3.  You can type your comments or answers in the text fields, and you can add as many lines as you need to provide detailed responses.
4.  Don't worry about grammar or spelling mistakes; we want to hear your real thoughts and opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** (1) (Text, Optional)
An introduction or a brief description of the topic or project you are working on related to wildlife health.

### 2. **Wildlife Health** (2) (Text, Optional)
Please describe the current state of wildlife health in your area or project context.

### 3. **Knowledge Before** (3) (Number, Optional)
Indicate your level of knowledge or understanding before starting the wildlife health outreach program. Use numbers 0-10, with 0 being no knowledge and 10 being perfect understanding.

### 4. **Knowledge After** (4) (Number, Optional)
Indicate your level of knowledge or understanding after participating in the wildlife health outreach program. Use numbers 0-10, with 0 being no knowledge and 10 being perfect understanding.

### 5. **Future Programming** (5) (Note, Optional)
Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for future programming and improvement of the wildlife health outreach program.

### 6. **Comments** (6) (Text, Optional)
Add any other thoughts, opinions, or comments about the wildlife health outreach effectiveness survey, our team, or your experience with the program.

Note: This form is for internal use only, and your responses will help us better understand your needs and improve our services.
