# usability_evaluation_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Usability Evaluation Checklist is a form designed to gather feedback and assess the usability of our product. It helps us understand how users interact with our product and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple choice options, select the one that best represents your experience.
3. For open-ended questions (text fields), provide a clear and concise description.
4. If a question is not applicable or does not pertain to your experience, please mark the "Not Applicable" or "N/A" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Note):
	+ This section is for any additional information you may want to provide about your experience with our product.
* **User Satisfaction** (Number):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with our product, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **User Frustration** (Text):
	+ Describe any frustrations or difficulties you experienced while using our product.
* **Usability Rating** (Number):
	+ Rate the usability of our product, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **User Experience** (Text):
	+ Describe your overall experience with our product.
* **Feedback** (Text):
	+ Share any additional feedback or comments about your experience with our product.
* **Product Usability** (Select One):
	+ Select one of the following options:
		- Very Poor
		- Neutral
		- Very Good
* **User Satisfaction 1** (Text):
	+ This question is part of our continued evaluation process. Please provide your thoughts on your most recent experience with our product.
* **User Satisfaction 2** (Text):

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Provide as much detail as possible for open-ended questions.
