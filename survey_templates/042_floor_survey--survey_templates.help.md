# Floor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Floor Survey" form is used to collect information about a building's floors, including its name, location, building type, size, and contact person's details. This form is used to create a comprehensive survey for the first and second floor of a building.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Floor 1" as the survey type.
2. Enter the "Building Name" of the surveyed building.
3. Select the "Location" of the building from the options provided.
4. Choose the "Building Type" by selecting one or multiple options (residential, commercial, industrial).
5. Enter the "Building Size" in numerical value.
6. Provide the "Building Address" of the surveyed building.
7. Enter the "Contact Person" details.
8. Enter the "Contact Phone" number (optional).
9. Enter the "Contact Email" address (optional).
10. Select any additional features of the building (elevator, parking, security).
11. (Optional) Upload a "Building Image", "Floor Plan Image", "Building Video", "Building Audios", "Building Videos", "Additional Info", "Building Images", "Building Audios2", "Floor Plan Videos", "Building Videos2", "Floor Plan Audios".
12. (Optional) Provide "Floor Survey Comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, *) - Enter the name of the building.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, *) - Select the location of the building from the options provided.
* **Building Type** (`building_type`, select_multiple, *) - Choose one or multiple building types (residential, commercial, industrial).
* **Building Size** (`building_size`, number, *) - Enter the size of the building in numerical value.
* **Building Address** (`building_address`, text, *) - Enter the building's address.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, *) - Enter the contact person's details.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional) - Enter the contact person's phone number (optional).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional) - Enter the contact person's email address (optional).
* **Building Features** (`building_features`, select_multiple, optional) - Select any additional features of the building (elevator, parking, security).
* **Building Image** (`building_image`, text, optional) - Upload a building image (optional).
* **Building Notes** (`building_notes`, text, optional) - Provide any additional comments about the building (optional).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional) - Provide any additional information about the building (optional).
* **Floor Plan Image** (`floor_plan_image`, text, optional) - Upload a floor plan image (optional).
* **Building Video** (`building_video`, text, optional) - Upload a building video (optional).
* **Building Audios** (`building_audios`, text, optional) - Upload building audios (optional).
* **Building Videos** (`building_videos`, text, optional) - Upload building videos (optional).
* **Building Images** (`building_images`, text, optional) - Upload building images (optional).
* **Building Audios2** (`building_audios2`, text, optional) - Upload building audios2 (optional).
* **Floor Plan Videos** (`floor_plan_videos`, text, optional) - Upload floor plan videos (optional).
* **Building Videos2** (`building_videos2`, text, optional) - Upload building videos2 (optional).
* **Floor Plan Audios** (`floor_plan_audios`, text, optional) - Upload floor plan audios (optional).
* **Floor Survey Comments** (`floor_survey_comments`, text, optional) - Provide any comments for the floor survey (optional).
