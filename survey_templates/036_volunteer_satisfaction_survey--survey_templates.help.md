# Volunteer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from volunteers about their experience on projects. Your input is valuable in helping us understand your preferences and satisfaction, allowing us to improve future projects and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Start by reviewing each field and its label to understand what information it requires.
2. Fill in each field carefully, providing accurate and relevant information.
3. Use the hints provided for each field as guidance when needed.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Experience**: A text field for you to describe your experience working on the project. Please provide a brief summary of your experience.
* **Rate Your Experience**: A number field to rate your experience on a scale. Please select a number that best represents your satisfaction.
* **Comments**: A text field for any additional feedback or comments you have about your experience.
* **Email**: An email field to provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: A text field for your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **How long you've volunteered for this project**: A select_one field with options for the duration of your project participation.
*   * Less than a week
*   * 1-2 weeks
*   * More than 2 weeks
*   This field helps us understand your project timeline and commitment.
* **How often you volunteer for projects**: A select_multiple field with options for your frequency of volunteering.
*   * Rarely
*   * Occasionally
*   * Frequently
*   * Almost Always
*   This field helps us understand your volunteering schedule and preferences.
* **What time of day do you prefer?**: A time field to select a preferred time of day for volunteering.
*   Note: This field might not have any options, it seems to be missing in the inputSchema.
* **What day of the week do you prefer?**: A select_one field with options for your preferred day of the week for volunteering.
*   * Monday
*   * Tuesday
*   * Wednesday
*   * Thursday
*   * Friday
*   * Saturday
*   * Sunday
*   This field helps us understand your scheduling preferences.
* **What type of project would you like to participate in?**: A select_multiple field with options for your preferred project type.
*   * Environment
*   * Community
*   * Education
*   * Animal
*   * Other
*   This field helps us understand your project interests.
* **How many hours per week do you volunteer?**: A number field to select the number of hours you volunteer per week.
*   Note: This field might not have any options, it seems to be missing in the inputSchema.
* **What days and times you're available?**: A note field for you to provide information about your availability.
*   Note: This field might not have any options, it seems to be missing in the inputSchema.
* **Any other comments**: A text field for you to provide additional feedback or comments.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form carefully and accurately to ensure your feedback is helpful.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* Your input is valuable, and we appreciate your time and effort in providing your feedback.
