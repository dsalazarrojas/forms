# Retail Seasonal Promotion Impact Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess the impact of a seasonal promotion, gathering information about the promotion's details, objectives, methods, budget, outcome, and lessons learned. It is intended to aid in evaluating the effectiveness of the promotion and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the seasonal promotion.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the promotion.
3. Specify the location of the promotion.
4. Enter the name and description of the product(s) involved in the promotion.
5. Outline the objectives and methods used for the promotion.
6. Provide details on the budget allocated for the promotion.
7. Describe the outcome of the promotion.
8. Summarize lessons learned from the promotion.
9. Offer recommendations for future promotions.
10. Provide any additional comments or attachments related to the promotion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seasonal Promotion Name** (1, text, optional): Enter the name of the seasonal promotion.
* **Start Date** (3, date, optional): Enter the start date of the promotion in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **End Date** (4, date, optional): Enter the end date of the promotion in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Location** (24, select_one, required): Select the location where the promotion took place (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Boston, Dallas).
* **Product Name** (6, text, optional): Enter the name of the product involved in the promotion.
* **Product Description** (7, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the product.
* **Objectives** (8, text, optional): Outline the objectives of the promotion.
* **Method** (9, text, optional): Describe the method used for the promotion.
* **Budget** (10, text, optional): Enter the budget allocated for the promotion.
* **Outcome** (11, text, optional): Describe the outcome of the promotion.
* **Lessons Learned** (12, text, optional): Summarize the lessons learned from the promotion.
* **Recommendations** (13, text, optional): Offer recommendations for future promotions.
* **Next Steps** (14, text, optional): Provide information on next steps or follow-up actions.
* **Impact Score** (15, number, optional): Enter a score out of 10 to indicate the impact of the promotion.
* **Impact Description** (16, text, optional): Describe the impact of the promotion.
* **Recommendations** (17, select_one, optional): Select whether the promotion was Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **Rating** (18, select_multiple, required): Select an evaluation of the promotion (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Comments** (19, text, optional): Provide any comments related to the promotion.
* **Additional Comments** (20, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations.
* **Attachments** (21, note, optional): Attach any relevant files, images, or documents related to the promotion.
* **Email** (22, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the promotion.
* **Phone** (23, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the promotion.
* **Product Name 2** (25, text, optional): Enter the name of the second product involved in the promotion, if applicable.
