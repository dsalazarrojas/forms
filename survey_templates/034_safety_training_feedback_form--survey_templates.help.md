# Safety Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Training Feedback Form is used to gather feedback from participants on the safety training programs provided by the organization. Your feedback will help us evaluate the effectiveness of the training and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate the overall effectiveness of the safety training by selecting a rating from 1 (Very Poor) to 4 (Very Good).
2. Provide the name of the training program you attended.
3. Share your thoughts on the safety training in the comments section.
4. Select a rating for the overall quality of the training program.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
6. Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
7. Enter the date you attended the safety training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **How would you rate the overall safety training?** (`safety_training_feedback`, number)
* Rate the overall safety training from 1 (Very Poor) to 4 (Very Good). This will help us gauge the effectiveness of the training session.

### 2. **Training Program** (`program_name`, text)
* Enter the name of the safety training program you attended. This helps us track which specific program you are providing feedback on.

### 3. **What did you think of the safety training?** (`feedback_comments`, note)
* Share your honest and constructive thoughts on the safety training. What did you like? What didn't you like?

### 4. **Overall Rating** (`rating_scale`, select_one)
* Select one of the following ratings: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor. This will give us an idea of your overall satisfaction with the training program.

### 5. **Your email** (`email`, email)
* Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed. We will keep your email address confidential.

### 6. **Your phone number** (`phone`, text)
* Enter your phone number for follow-up contact. We will only use this number to contact you regarding your feedback.

### 7. **Training Date** (`training_date`, date)
* Enter the date you attended the safety training. This helps us track when the training was delivered.

### 8. **Trainer's Name** (`trainer_name`, text)
* Enter the name of the trainer who delivered the safety training. This helps us keep track of our trainers and their performance.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and constructively.
* Ensure you enter accurate information, especially your email and phone number.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, feel free to share them with us.
