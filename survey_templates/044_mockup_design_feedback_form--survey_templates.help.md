# mockup_design_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on the mockup design from the user. The feedback collected will help the design team to understand what needs to be improved or changed in the design to make it more user-friendly and effective.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form questions on the first page (Form Questions).
2. Rate the usability of the mockup on a scale of 0 to 10.
3. Provide actionable comments on the design, including specific suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Questions**: This is where you will answer a series of questions about the mockup design. Please provide your honest feedback on the design.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Usability**: Rate the overall usability of the mockup design, with 0 being very poor and 10 being excellent.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: False
* **Actionable Comments**: Provide specific, actionable comments on the design, including suggestions for improvement.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide specific feedback on the design.
* Keep your comments clear and concise.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please include them.
