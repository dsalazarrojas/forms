# Fitness Weekly Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Weekly Check In Form is designed to help you provide regular check-ins and updates on your fitness progress, goals, and habits. This form is meant for tracking and monitoring progress, and providing a platform for the organization to offer personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in your User First Name, User Last Name, and Email (if required).
3. Choose your response for each of the following fields: Fitness Plan, Exercise Frequency, Fitness Level, and Check In Frequency.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes as necessary.
5. If you have specific goals, you can describe them in the Goals field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
- **User Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Fitness Plan** (`fitness_plan`, select_one, optional): Choose how you plan to approach your fitness goals (Yes/No).
- **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how often you exercise (Yes/No).
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your progress.
- **Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, select_one, optional): Describe your current fitness level (High/Low).
- **Check In Frequency** (`check_in_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how often you will check in (Yes/No).
- **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Describe any specific goals you're working towards.

## Tips
* Please ensure your answers are honest and accurate.
* Remember to regularly check in and update your progress.
* Use this form as a tool for tracking and monitoring your progress, and for receiving guidance and support from the organization.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask for clarification.
