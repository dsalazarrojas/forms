# Corporate Communications Strategy Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Communications Strategy Audit Form is designed to help organizations evaluate and improve their corporate communications strategy. This form provides a structured and systematic approach to identifying areas of improvement and making informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form instructions and ensure you understand the purpose and objectives of the audit.
2.  Carefully review each field and provide answers that accurately reflect your corporate communications strategy.
3.  Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, and provide any additional information requested in the "Key Findings" and "Recommendations" fields.
4.  Review the "Next Steps" field for action items and tasks to be completed after the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (1): This is a brief introduction to the form and the purpose of the audit. Please provide a short summary of the corporate communications strategy and its goals.
* **Messaging Consistency** (2): Select all the channels where your organization communicates with its stakeholders (e.g. email, phone, social media, etc.). Indicate if the messaging is consistent across all these channels.
* **Channel Performance** (3): Please enter the percentage of the time you spend on each communication channel in a typical week. This will help us understand where your organization is spending most of its time and energy.
* **Stakeholder Communication** (4): Select the channels where your organization communicates with your stakeholders. This will help us identify where to improve communication and engagement.
* **Online Data Collection** (5): This field is currently not relevant for this form. Please ignore it.
* **Communication Channels** (6): Select all the channels where your organization communicates with its stakeholders (e.g. email, phone, social media, etc.). Please note that this field is similar to "Messaging Consistency" and "Stakeholder Communication" but with a broader scope.
* **Key Findings** (7): Please provide any important points you've identified during the audit. This can be a summary of the main issues you've encountered and the implications for the corporate communications strategy.
* **Recommendations** (8): Based on the audit, please provide suggestions for improvement and recommendations to enhance the corporate communications strategy.
* **Next Steps** (9): Please describe the action items and tasks needed to implement the recommendations and improve the corporate communications strategy.
* **Conclusion** (10): This is a field to provide a summary of the audit, its key findings, and recommendations.

Note: Fields with a type of "note" are free text fields, where you can enter any information you think is important and relevant to the audit.
