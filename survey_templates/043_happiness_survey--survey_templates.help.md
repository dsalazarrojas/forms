# Happiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your opinions about your current level of happiness and your perception of the world as a fair place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each page as instructed below. Remember, all fields are optional, and you can skip any question that doesn't apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What's Your Current Level Of Happiness** (`page_1`, number, optional): Please select a numerical value from 0 to 10 to indicate your current level of happiness, where 0 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Do You Think The World Is A Fair Place** (`page_2`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the four options provided:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **On A Scale Of 0-10, How Happy Are You** (`page_3`, number, optional): Select a numerical value from 0 to 10 to indicate your happiness level.
* **email** (`page_4`, email, optional): Please enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted back.
* **phone** (`page_5`, text, optional): You can optionally enter your phone number for follow-up questions.
* **page_7** (`page_6`, note, optional): This is a text box for any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Tips
* If you have any trouble filling out this form, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your honesty and time in taking this survey.
* Your feedback is important to us, and we'll use it to improve the user experience.
