# Spring Break Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spring Break Survey is a brief questionnaire designed to gather information about your Spring Break plans. Your responses will help us better understand your preferences and habits during this time of the year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. **Answer each question thoroughly and accurately**. Make sure to provide the most up-to-date information.
2. **Select only one option for 'travel_purpose'**, as it is a select-one question.
3. **Choose all applicable options for 'travel_method'**, as it is a select-multiple question.
4. **Enter a valid date for 'date_of_travel'**. This should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. **Provide a numerical value for 'number_of_people'**. This should be a whole number (0-10).
6. **Enter a numerical value for 'pandemic_risk'**. This is a risk level score from 1-10.
7. **Enter any additional comments or information in 'comments'**. This is an optional field.
8. **Provide your email address in 'email'** (if applicable). This will help us contact you for follow-up questions.
9. **Enter your phone number in 'phone'** (if applicable). This will help us reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **spring_break_survey_1**: A unique identifier for this page.
* **travel_purpose**: Please select the primary reason for your Spring Break travel. Choose one option that best describes your main intention.
	+ Enjoy the weather: If your primary goal is to enjoy outdoor activities or natural surroundings.
	+ Visit family and friends: If your main goal is to spend time with loved ones.
	+ Other: If your primary goal is something else (please specify in 'comments').
* **travel_method**: Select all transportation methods you plan to use for your Spring Break trip. You can choose multiple options.
	+ By car: If you plan to drive or take a car ride.
	+ By train: If you plan to take a train.
	+ By plane: If you plan to fly.
* **date_of_travel**: Enter the date you plan to start your Spring Break trip.
* **number_of_people**: Please enter the number of people accompanying you on your Spring Break trip.
* **pandemic_risk**: Please rate your risk level for contracting the pandemic during your trip, from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **comments**: If you have any additional comments or information, please provide it here.
* **email**: If you have an email address, please enter it for any follow-up questions.
* **phone**: If you have a phone number, please enter it for any follow-up questions.
