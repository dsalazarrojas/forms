# Digital Wellness Education Series Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users who have attended the Digital Wellness Education Series, allowing them to evaluate the effectiveness and quality of the series.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of 8 questions, each designed to gather specific information about your experience with the Digital Wellness Education Series. Please answer each question honestly, and make sure to complete all required fields. If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop** (`workshop`, `select_one`, required): Check the box next to "Yes" if you attended the workshop. This question helps us understand if you found the workshop helpful.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience with the workshop on a scale of 1-5. Please keep in mind that this is a numerical rating, not a subjective description.
* **Future Workshop** (`future_workshop`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the boxes that apply to indicate if you are interested in attending future workshops in the series. Please be honest, and don't worry about the number of boxes you check.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `text`, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions about the workshop, feel free to type them in here. This is an optional field, so you can skip it if you don't have anything to say.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to receive future updates or information about upcoming workshops, please enter your email address here. We'll keep your information confidential and only use it for workshop-related purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have any urgent questions or need to schedule a call for a future workshop, feel free to provide your phone number here. Please don't worry about sharing your number if you don't want to, you can skip this field if you're not comfortable sharing.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): Do you have any other comments or suggestions that didn't fit in the above fields? If yes, type them in here.

Please submit your feedback, and we'll make sure to use it to improve the Digital Wellness Education Series. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
