# Post Travel Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from travelers about their experiences, preferences, and details related to their travel. It aims to improve travel-related services and experiences for future travelers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, and phone number in the relevant fields.
2. Rate your experience as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
3. Enter the date of your travel in the "Date of Prophets" field.
4. Select the time of your travel in the "Time of Travel" field.
5. Describe your travel experience in the "Travel Experience" field.
6. Add any additional comments about your travel in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. Choose the location of your travel from the options provided.
8. Enter the date of your travel in the "Travel Date" field.
9. Provide your age as a number.
10. Specify the number of days you spent traveling.
11. Select the type of travel you experienced (Domestic, International, Business).
12. Choose the modes of transportation you used for your travel (Train, Bus, Flight, Car).
13. Specify how often you travel (as a number).
14. Enter the cost of your travel as a number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Rate your experience as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Date of Prophets** (`date_of_prophet`, date, required): Enter the date of your travel.
* **Time of Travel** (`time_of_travel`, time, required): Select the time of your travel.
* **Travel Experience** (`travel_experience`, note, required): Describe your travel experience in this field.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments about your travel experience.
* **Travel Location** (`travel_location`, select_one, required): Choose the location of your travel from the options provided.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your travel.
* **Age of Traveler** (`age_of_traveler`, number, required): Provide your age as a number.
* **Number of days** (`number_of_days`, number, required): Specify the number of days you spent traveling.
* **Type of Travel** (`travel_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of travel you experienced (Domestic, International, Business).
* **Travel Mode** (`travel_mode`, select_multiple, required): Choose the modes of transportation you used for your travel (Train, Bus, Flight, Car).
* **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`, number, required): Specify how often you travel (as a number).
* **Cost of Travel** (`travel_cost`, number, required): Enter the cost of your travel as a number.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
- If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to reach out to us.
- Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve travel-related services for future travelers.
