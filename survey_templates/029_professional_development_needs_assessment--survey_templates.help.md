# Professional Development Needs Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Professional Development Needs Assessment form is used to collect information on an employee's skills, career path, and other relevant details to identify areas where an employee may need additional training or support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page and its corresponding fields to understand what information is required.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Use the "Submit" button to save your progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Below is a detailed explanation of each field in the form:

* **First Page: First Page Label** (id: 1, text, not required): This is the introduction page of the form. Please provide a brief overview of your professional development needs.
* **Skills Selection** (id: 2, select_multiple, not required): Please select all the skills that you are proficient in or would like to develop. The options are "Yes" and "No". You can select multiple options.
* **Career Path** (id: 3, select_one, not required): Please select "Yes" if you have a clear career path, and "No" otherwise.
* **Career Recommendation** (id: 4, text, not required): This field is for any additional information or suggestions you may have on your career path or professional development needs.
* **Note** (id: 5, note, not required): This field is for any additional comments or notes you may have regarding your professional development needs.
* **Email** (id: 6, email, not required): Please provide your email address so that we can contact you for further discussions or to provide updates on your professional development plan.
* **Phone Number** (id: 7, text, not required): Please provide your phone number so that we can contact you for further discussions or to provide updates on your professional development plan.
* **Date** (id: 8, date, not required): Please provide the date you would like to start your professional development plan or when you would like to be contacted for further discussions.
* **Time** (id: 9, time, not required): Please provide the time you would like to be contacted for further discussions or when you would like to start your professional development plan.
* **Submit Button** (id: 10, text, not required): Clicking this button will save your progress and submit your form for review.

## Tips
- Please answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
- Use the "Submit" button to save your progress.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the form administrator for further clarification.
