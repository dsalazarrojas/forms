# Television Pilot Episode Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings on a television pilot episode. It is intended for production team members and collaborators to provide their thoughts and opinions on various aspects of the episode, such as genre, director preference, and production budget.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Episode Title** and select the correct **Series Concept**.
2. Select the **Genre** of the episode (e.g., Drama, Comedy, Thriller).
3. Choose your preference for **Director** and **Producer** by selecting multiple options.
4. Enter a brief **Episode Synopsis** describing the episode's plot.
5. Add any **Production Team Notes** relevant to the episode.
6. Enter the **Episode Duration**, **Release Date**, and **Production Budget**.
7. Select the **Target Audience** (e.g., Adults 18-34, Adults 35-44).
8. Choose your **Episode Rating** (1/10 to 10/10).
9. Select the **Production Crew** members (e.g., John, Jane, James).
10. Provide any **Feedback Comments** and **Additional Notes**.
11. Enter the **Production Completion Date**.
12. Select the **Production Budget Range** and **Target Audience Size**.
13. Choose the **Production Duration** and **Release Duration**.
14. Select the **Production Schedule** (e.g., Weekday, Weekend, Weekday and Weekend).
15. Answer any **Additional Questions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Episode Title**: Enter a descriptive title for the episode.
* **Series Concept**: Enter a brief description of the series' concept.
* **Genre**: Select the primary genre of the episode (e.g., Drama, Comedy, Thriller).
* **Director Preference**: Select one or multiple directors you prefer for the episode.
* **Producer Preference**: Select one or multiple producers you prefer for the episode.
* **Episode Synopsis**: Enter a brief description of the episode's plot.
* **Production Team Notes**: Enter any notes relevant to the production team.
* **Episode Duration**: Enter the duration of the episode in minutes.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date the episode will be released.
* **Production Budget**: Enter the budget for the episode.
* **Target Audience**: Select the target audience for the episode (e.g., Adults 18-34, Adults 35-44).
* **Episode Rating**: Rate the episode from 1/10 to 10/10.
* **Production Crew**: Select one or multiple crew members you prefer for the episode.
* **Feedback Comments**: Provide any feedback or comments on the episode.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Production Completion Date**: Enter the date the episode will be completed.
* **Production Budget Range**: Enter the range of the production budget.
* **Target Audience Size**: Select the target audience size for the episode.
* **Production Duration**: Enter the duration of production in minutes.
* **Release Duration**: Enter the duration of release in minutes.
* **Production Schedule**: Select the production schedule (e.g., Weekday, Weekend, Weekday and Weekend).
* **Additional Questions**: Answer any additional questions you may have.
