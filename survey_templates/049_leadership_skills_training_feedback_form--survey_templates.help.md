# Leadership Skills Training Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from participants about the effectiveness of a leadership skills training session. It helps identify areas of improvement and informs future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Provide specific examples or details when answering the questions.
3. Use the "Overall Training Effectiveness" dropdown menu to select the level of effectiveness of the training.
4. In the "Learning Plans or Actions" field, describe the specific steps you will take based on your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leadership Skills Training Effectiveness** (`leadership_skills_training_effectiveness`, note, optional): This field is a general note about the effectiveness of the training session.
* **What are some key insights gained from the training?** (`participant_insights`, text, optional): Share your key takeaways or insights from the training session. Try to be specific about what you learned or realized.
* **What areas of the training could be improved?** (`participant_improvement_areas`, text, optional): Identify areas where the training could be improved, such as content, facilitation, or logistics.
* **Overall Training Effectiveness** (`overall_training_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate the level of effectiveness of the training:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not Effective
* **What learning plans or actions will be taken based on your feedback?** (`organization_learning_plan`, text, optional): Describe the specific steps you will take to improve future training sessions, such as implementing changes, providing feedback, or revisiting content.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions thoughtfully and provide specific examples.
- If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Leadership Skills Training Effectiveness" field.
- This form is meant to be completed by training participants, so please do not fill it out on behalf of someone else.
