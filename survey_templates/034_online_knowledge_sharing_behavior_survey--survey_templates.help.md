# Online Knowledge Sharing Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on how individuals share online knowledge with others, including their motivations and challenges. This information will help us better understand online knowledge sharing behavior and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions on each page, one at a time. You can select your answers from the options provided or type your response in the text fields. If you have any doubts, feel free to ask, but remember that each page is designed to be completed independently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Page** (`1`, text, required: false): This page is an introduction to the survey and is meant to be completed before proceeding.
* **What do you do when faced with new information?** (`2`, text, required: false): Describe your typical approach when you come across new information.
* **Do you share new information with others?** (`3`, select_one, required: false):
	+ **Yes**: If you select 'Yes', please choose one of the following options for the next question.
	+ **No**: If you select 'No', you will proceed to the next question.
* **Yes, always** (`4`, text, required: false): If you selected 'Yes' in the previous question, describe situations where you always share new information.
* **Sometimes** (`5`, text, required: false): If you selected 'Sometimes' in the previous question, describe situations where you sometimes share new information.
* **What motivates you to share?** (`7`, select_one, required: false):
	+ **Curiosity**: If you select 'Curiosity', describe why this motivates you to share.
	+ **To help others**: If you select 'To help others', describe why this motivates you to share.
	+ **For personal gain**: If you select 'For personal gain', describe why this motivates you to share.
* **What barriers do you face?** (`8`, text, required: false): Describe any challenges you face when sharing new information.
* **What is your role?** (`9`, select_one, required: false):
	+ **Researcher**: If you select 'Researcher', describe your role in research.
	+ **HR**: If you select 'HR', describe your role in human resources.
	+ **Educator**: If you select 'Educator', describe your role in education.
