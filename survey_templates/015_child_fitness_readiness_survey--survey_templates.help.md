# Child Fitness Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Fitness Readiness Survey is designed to gather information about a child's medical history and physical abilities to determine their fitness readiness for specific sports or activities. This survey is to be completed by parents or guardians to provide accurate and necessary information about their child's health and physical abilities.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Start by filling in the required information in the "Program and Participant Information" section.
- Then, proceed to the "Medical History" section to provide details about any known medical conditions that may affect the child's participation.
- In the "Activity Levels" section, indicate the child's current physical activity frequency and preferred sports or activities.
- Finally, in the "Parental Consent" section, confirm that you grant permission for your child's participation in fitness activities.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Program and Participant Information - Club Name
• **Club Name** (`club_name`, `text`, required): Name of the sports organization or program your child is applying to.
### Program and Participant Information - Child Full Name
• **Child Full Name** (`child_name`, `text`, required): Full legal name of your child, as stated on their birth certificate or ID.
### Medical History - Does the Child Have Any Known Heart Conditions?
• **Does the Child Have Any Known Heart Conditions?** (`heart_conditions`, `select_one`, required): If the child has any known heart conditions, including congenital issues, select "True". Otherwise, select "False".
### Medical History - Does the Child Experience Dizziness during Activity?
• **Does the Child Experience Dizziness During Activity?** (`dizziness_activity`, `select_one`, required): If the child experiences dizziness or fainting spells during physical activity, select "True". Otherwise, select "False".
### Medical History - Any Bone or Joint Problems?
• **Any Bone or Joint Problems That Could be Aggravated?** (`bone_joint_problems`, `select_one`, required): If the child has any known bone or joint problems that could be made worse by physical activity, select "True". Otherwise, select "False".
### Medical History - Is the Child Currently Taking Any Medications?
• **Is the Child Currently Taking Any Medications?** (`medications`, `select_one`, required): If the child is taking any prescription or non-prescription medications, select "True". Otherwise, select "False".
### Activity Levels - Frequency of Current Physical Activity
• **Frequency of Current Physical Activity** (`physical_activity_freq`, `select_one`, required): Select the typical frequency of your child's current physical activity, such as "Daily", "3-5 times a week", "1-2 times a week", or "Rarely".
### Activity Levels - Preferred Sports or Activities
• **Preferred Sports or Activities** (`preferred_sports`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the sports or activities your child prefers or enjoys.
### Parental Consent - I Grant Permission for Participation
• **I Grant Permission for Participation in Fitness Activities** (`fitness_participation_perm`, `select_one`, required): Select "I Agree" to confirm that you grant permission for your child's participation in fitness activities.
### Parental Consent - Date of Signature
• **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Date of this form is signed.
### Additional Tips
- This form is only meant to be filled out by the parent or guardian.
- Please fill out this form carefully and completely to provide accurate information.
- If you need help or clarification on any question, please contact the organization hosting this form.
