# Sync Settings Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about synchronization settings from users to better understand their synchronization preferences and issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the answers that best fit your needs for each question.
2. If you select "Other" in any of the select fields, provide a brief explanation in the corresponding text field.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, as this will help us understand your synchronization settings preferences and issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How Often Do You Sync?** (`how_often_do_you_sync`, number, required): This question asks how often you synchronize your settings. Select the number that best fits your habits.
* **What Are Synchronized Issues?** (`sync_issues`, note, optional): This question is optional and allows you to provide any issues or problems you've encountered with synchronization.
* **Sync Frequency Preference** (`sync_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency at which you prefer to sync your settings. Choose from Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **How Do You Synchronize?** (`sync_behavior`, select_multiple, required): Select all the methods you use to synchronize your settings. Choose from Automatic, Manual, Other, or Unknown.
* **How Do You Synchronize Other?** (`sync_behavior_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, provide a brief explanation of your synchronization method.
* **What Are The Details Of Sync Issues?** (`sync_issues_details`, note, optional): This question is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about the issues you've encountered with synchronization.
* **What Is The Largest Pain Point?** (`sync_pain_point`, note, optional): This question is optional and allows you to provide any pain points you've encountered with synchronization.
* **What Is The Largest Pain Point Sync Frequency?** (`sync_frequency_pain_point`, note, optional): This question is optional and allows you to provide any pain points you've encountered with synchronization frequency.
* **Has Synchronization Issues?** (`sync_synchronization_issues`, select_multiple, required): Select all the issues you've encountered with synchronization. Choose from True, False, or Unknown.
* **What Is The Nature Of Synchronization Issues?** (`sync_synchronization_frequency`, note, optional): This question is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about the issues you've encountered with synchronization.
* **What Is Your Sync Frequency Preference?** (`sync_synchronization_frequency`, number, required): Select the frequency at which you prefer to sync your settings. This is the frequency you selected in the previous question.
* **What Is The Largest Pain Point In Sync Frequency?** (`sync_synchronization_frequency_pain_point`, note, optional): This question is optional and allows you to provide any pain points you've encountered with synchronization frequency.
* **What Is The Impact Of Sync Issues?** (`sync_synchronization_issues_impact`, select_one, required): Select the impact of the issues you've encountered with synchronization. Choose from Performance, Security, Usability, or Other.
* **What Is The Specific Impact Of Sync Issues?** (`sync_synchronization_issues_impact_details`, note, optional): This question is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about the impact of the issues you've encountered with synchronization.
* **What Is The Specific Nature Of Sync Issues Impact Other?** (`sync_synchronization_issues_impact_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, provide a brief explanation of the impact of the issues you've encountered with synchronization.
* **What Is The Specific Reason For Sync Issues Impact Other?** (`sync_synchronization_issues_impact_other_reason`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, provide a brief explanation of the reason for the impact of the issues you've encountered with synchronization.
* **Are You Aware Of Synchronization Issues?** (`sync_synchronization_issues_awareness`, select_one, required): Select the option that best fits your awareness of synchronization issues. Choose from True, False, or Unknown.
* **What Do You Know About Synchronization Issues?** (`sync_synchronization_issues_awareness_details`, note, optional): This question is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about your awareness of synchronization issues.
* **What Do You Know About Synchronization Issues Other?** (`sync_synchronization_issues_awareness_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, provide a brief explanation of what you know about synchronization issues.
* **What Is The Specific Nature Of Synchronization Issues Other?** (`sync_synchronization_issues_awareness_other_details`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, provide a brief explanation of the specific nature of synchronization issues.
* **What Is The Reason For Sync Issues Other?** (`sync_synchronization_issues_awareness_other_reason`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, provide a brief explanation of the reason for the issues you've encountered with synchronization.
* **What Is Your Preferred Sync Frequency?** (`sync_synchronization_issues_preferred_frequency`, number, required): Select the frequency at which you prefer to sync your settings. This is the frequency you selected in the previous question.
* **What Is The Largest Pain Point In Sync Frequency?** (`sync_synchronization_issues_pain_point`, note, optional): This question is optional and allows you to provide any pain points you've encountered with synchronization frequency.
* **What Is The Nature Of Synchronization Issues?** (`sync_synchronization_frequency_details`, note, optional): This question is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about the issues you've encountered with synchronization frequency.
