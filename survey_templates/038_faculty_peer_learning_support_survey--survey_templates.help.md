# Faculty Peer Learning Support Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your experiences with peer learning support programs. Your input will help us understand how these programs are working for you and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question as thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the best option for each question from the provided options, or type your answer in the text field.
3. Make sure to provide as much detail as you can about your experiences and ideas for improvement.
4. Check the "I am finished" checkbox to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a placeholder field where you can provide any additional context or comments about your experiences with peer learning support programs.
* **Peer Learning Activities**: This question asks if you have participated in any peer learning activities. Select 'Yes' if you have participated, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Challenge Areas**: This question asks you to identify areas where you have faced challenges in your peer learning experiences. Select the option that best fits your experience.
	+ **Peer Mentoring**: You have had difficulties finding or accessing peer mentors.
	+ **Collaborative Projects**: You have had difficulties working on projects with your peers.
	+ **Peer Feedback**: You have had difficulties receiving useful feedback from your peers.
	+ **Other (Specify)**: Select this option if your challenge is not listed above.
* **Effectiveness**: This question asks about your overall effectiveness of the peer learning program. Select all options that apply.
	+ **Yes**: You have found the program to be effective.
	+ **No**: You have not found the program to be effective.
* **Improvement Ideas**: Provide any ideas you have for improving the peer learning program in this text field.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experiences with the peer learning program in this text field.
