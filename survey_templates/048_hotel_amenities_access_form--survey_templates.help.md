# Hotel Amenities Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Hotel Amenities Access Form is used to gather information about guests' preferences for accessing hotel amenities. This form helps hotel staff members to manage and provide services that meet the guests' needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please select the correct options for each field as they pertain to your situation.
2. Fill in the required fields (if any) with accurate and relevant information.
3. Review your selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Information (Text)** (`guest_information`, text, Optional): Please enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Room Number (Number)** (`room_number`, number, Optional): Enter the number of the room you are staying in.
* **Arrival Time (Time)** (`arrival_time`, time, Optional): Select your expected arrival time.
* **Room Rate Plan (Select One)** (`room_rate_plan`, select_one, Optional): Choose the rate plan you have booked (e.g., Option 1 or Option 2).
* **Checkin Status (Select Multiple)** (`checkin_status`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all the statuses that apply to your check-in status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3).
* **Room Type (Select Multiple)** (`room_type`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the type of room you have booked (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3).
* **Amenities Accessed (Select Multiple)** (`amenities_accessed`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all the amenities you plan to access during your stay (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Amenities Available (Select Multiple)** (`amenities_available`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all the amenities that are available for you to access during your stay (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Guest Notes (Note)** (`guest_notes`, note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your stay.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Text)** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Guest ID (Number)** (`guest_id`, number, Optional): Enter your guest ID.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact hotel staff for assistance.
