# Productivity And Wellness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess your self-perceived levels of productivity, wellness, and job satisfaction. It will help us understand your current status and areas where you might need support or resources to improve your overall job satisfaction and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your job role from the dropdown menu on the first page.
2. For each question, select the best response that matches your current feelings or experiences.
3. For questions with multiple options, choose all that apply.
4. You can add any additional comments in the last question if you'd like to share any thoughts or concerns.
