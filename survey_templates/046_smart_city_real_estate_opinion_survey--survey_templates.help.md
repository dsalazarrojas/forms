# Smart City Real Estate Opinion Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions from citizens on the features of a Smart City and understand their perception of its effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. You may be required to answer questions that appear multiple times, so please take note of the page numbers to ensure you answer each question only once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are the top 3 smart city features you think are most important for a city's success?** (`smart_city_features_1`, select_multiple, required)
    This question asks you to select up to 3 features that you think are most important for a city's success. Please choose from the options: Public Transportation, Green Spaces, and Community Engagement.
* **On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with the current state of smart city features in our city?** (`satisfaction_levels_1`, number, required)
    This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the current state of smart city features in our city. Please choose a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **What improvements would you like to see in our smart city?** (`desired_improvements_1`, text, required)
    This question asks you to provide any improvements you would like to see in the smart city features. Please type your thoughts in the text field.
* **How do you think smart city features have improved your daily life?** (`smart_city_features_2`, select_one, required)
    This question asks you to select one of the options: Significantly, Somewhat, or Not at all. Please choose how you think smart city features have improved your daily life.
* **How likely are you to recommend smart city features to others?** (`smart_city_features_3`, select_one, required)
    This question asks you to select one of the options: Very Likely, Somewhat Likely, or Not Likely. Please choose how likely you are to recommend smart city features to others.
* **How important is smart city features to you?** (`smart_city_features_4`, number, required)
    This question asks you to rate the importance of smart city features on a scale of 1-5. Please choose a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is "Not Important" and 5 is "Very Important".
* **How often do you use smart city features?** (`smart_city_features_5`, select_multiple, required)
    This question asks you to select up to 3 features that you use frequently. Please choose from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.
* **What do you think is the biggest challenge facing our city's smart city development?** (`smart_city_features_6`, text, required)
    This question asks you to provide any challenges you think are facing our city's smart city development. Please type your thoughts in the text field.
* **How do you rate the overall quality of smart city features in our city?** (`smart_city_features_7`, number, required)
    This question asks you to rate the overall quality of smart city features on a scale of 1-5. Please choose a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
