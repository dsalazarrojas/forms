# partner_organization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The partner_organization_form is designed to collect essential details about a partner organization's relationship with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Review the form to ensure all fields are filled out correctly before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **partner_organization_name** (text): Enter the name of the partner organization.
* **partner_name** (text): Enter the name of the partner (if applicable).
* **name_of_partnership** (text): Enter a descriptive name for the partnership.
* **partnership_type** (select_one): Choose whether the partnership is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **start_date** (date): Enter the start date of the partnership.
* **end_date** (date): Enter the end date of the partnership.
* **end_date_reason** (text): Provide a reason for the end of partnership (if applicable).
* **contact** (text): Enter the contact person's name and title.
* **organization** (text): Enter the partner organization's details (e.g., address, phone number, etc.).
* **contact_email** (email): Enter the contact person's email address.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting to avoid duplication or inconsistencies in the data collected.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant team for clarification.
