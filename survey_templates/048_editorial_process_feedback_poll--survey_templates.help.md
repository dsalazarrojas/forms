# Editorial Process Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from editors about their experience with the editorial process. This feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure that our processes are efficient and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the "editor_survey" page to provide your overall feedback and comments about the editorial process.
2. Choose the "feedback" page to provide specific feedback on the process.
3. Select "satisfaction" and choose one of the options to indicate your level of satisfaction with the process.
4. Provide any "pain_points" you experienced during the process.
5. If you have any "improvement" suggestions, enter them in the "improvement" field.
6. You can submit your feedback anonymously or provide your contact information if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
