# Lab Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lab Experience Feedback Survey aims to collect students' opinions and experiences about their lab sessions. This feedback will help lab managers and educators understand the strengths and weaknesses of their lab sessions, allowing them to make informed decisions for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Provide honest and detailed feedback about your lab experience.
3. Ensure that you answer all required questions.
4. If a question doesn't apply, mark it as "N/A" or "Not Applicable."
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
This section is an introduction to the form and serves as a guide. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### Facilities
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, text, required: false): Please describe the facilities provided during your lab session, such as the cleanliness, comfort, and overall condition of the lab space.

### Equipment
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, text, required: false): Describe the state of the equipment used during your lab session, including its functionality, condition, and any issues you encountered.

### Equipment Quality
* **Instruction Quality** (`instructional_quality`, text, required: false): Rate the quality of the instruction provided during your lab session, including the clarity of explanations, demonstrations, and overall guidance.

### Lab Session Date
* **Lab Session Date** (`lab_session_date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date of your lab session.

### Lab Session Time
* **Lab Session Time** (`lab_session_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start and end time of your lab session.

### Instructor
* **Instructor** (`instructor`, text, required: false): Describe the instructor's teaching style, approachability, and effectiveness.

### Student Comments
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments, suggestions, or ideas you have about the lab experience.
