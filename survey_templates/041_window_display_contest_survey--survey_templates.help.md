# Window Display Contest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from retailers participating in a window display contest. It is meant to gather details about the retailers' stores, such as their store name, phone number, email, website, and social media handles. This information will help the contest organizers contact the retailers and verify their contest entries.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section of the form with accurate and complete information about your store.
2. Make sure to double-check your entries for any errors or typos.
3. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retailer Information** (`contest_entry`, text, required)
	+ Enter your store's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Display Description** (`contest_description`, text, required)
	+ Describe your store's window display in a few sentences. This is your chance to tell us about the creativity and effort you put into your display.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required)
	+ Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Store Photo** (`store_image`, text, required)
	+ Upload a photo of your store's window display. This is a required field, but you can provide multiple photos if you'd like.
* **Store Photo 2** (`store_photo_alt`, text, required)
	+ Upload a second photo of your store's window display. This can be used if you have a lot to show.
* **Store Image Credit** (`store_image_credit`, text, required)
	+ Provide credit information for the photo you uploaded. This includes the photographer's name and any other relevant details.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter your business name as it appears on your store's signage and marketing materials.
* **Business Name 2** (`business_name_alt`, text, required)
	+ Enter an alternative name for your business, if applicable.
* **Phone** (`business_phone`, text, required)
	+ Enter your store's phone number.
* **Phone 2** (`business_phone_alt`, text, required)
	+ Enter an alternative phone number for your store, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required)
	+ Enter your business's email address.
* **Business Email 2** (`business_email_alt`, email, required)
	+ Enter an alternative email address for your business, if applicable.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter your store's name as it appears on your storefront and marketing materials.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, text, required)
	+ Enter your store's phone number.
* **Store Email** (`store_email`, text, required)
	+ Enter your store's email address.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, required)
	+ Enter your store's address.
* **Store Address 2** (`store_address_alt`, text, required)
	+ Enter an alternative address for your store, if applicable.
* **Store City** (`store_city`, text, required)
	+ Enter your store's city.
* **Store State** (`store_state`, text, required)
	+ Enter your store's state or region.
* **Store Zip** (`store_zip`, text, required)
	+ Enter your store's zip code.
* **Store Phone 2** (`store_phone`, text, required)
	+ Enter an alternative phone number for your store, if applicable.
* **Store Email 2** (`store_email`, text, required)
	+ Enter an alternative email address for your store, if applicable.
* **Store Website** (`store_website`, text, required)
	+ Enter your store's website.
* **Store Facebook** (`store_facebook`, text, required)
	+ Enter your store's Facebook handle.
