# Hallowfest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience at Hallowfest. It will help us understand your preferences, activities, and contact information. Your participation is crucial in helping us improve the festival experience for future attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name, contact number, and other relevant information.
2. Answer the questions honestly, and please select all the relevant options that apply.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **hallowfest_survey** (`hallowfest_survey_1`, text, required: false): This question is asking about your overall experience at Hallowfest. Please provide a brief description of your experience, including any notable events or activities you participated in.
* **how_many_visits** (`hallowfest_survey_2`, number, required: false): How many times have you attended Hallowfest in the past?
* **favorite_activities** (`hallowfest_survey_3`, select_one, required: false): Which activities did you enjoy most during the festival? You can choose one or more options that apply to you.
* **contact_number** (`hallowfest_survey_4`, text, required: false): Please provide your contact number so we can reach out to you for any further questions or updates.
* **number_of_visits** (`hallowfest_survey_7`, number, required: false): How many times have you visited Hallowfest in the past 3 years?
* **hallowfest_survey_5** (`hallowfest_survey_5`, select_multiple, required: false): Which of the following activities would you like to see more of during Hallowfest? Select all that apply.
* **hallowfest_survey_6** (`hallowfest_survey_6`, date, required: false): What date would you prefer to attend Hallowfest in the future? (Please note that this is a date field and should be in the format of YYYY-MM-DD)
* **hallowfest_survey_7** (`hallowfest_survey_7`, time, required: false): What time would you prefer to attend Hallowfest in the future? (Please note that this is a time field and should be in the format of HH:MM)
* **hallowfest_survey_8** (`hallowfest_survey_8`, note, required: false): Is there anything else you would like to share about your Hallowfest experience?
* **hallowfest_survey_9** (`hallowfest_survey_9`, text, required: false): Do you have any other comments or suggestions about Hallowfest?
