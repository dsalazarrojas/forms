# Subscription Service User Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather your thoughts and opinions on our subscription service. Your responses will help us understand your preferences and needs, which will inform our development and improvement of the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name, if you'd like to be identified.
2. Enter your Email address, as we may contact you with updates or questions.
3. Describe your experience with our subscription service by answering the questions and selecting your preferences below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name, if you'd like to provide it. This will help us address you personally.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name, if you'd like to provide it. This will help us identify you further.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. We may use this to contact you with updates or questions.
* **Subscription Service**: Describe the subscription service you're using. This will help us understand your specific context.
* **Feedback**: Share any thoughts, ideas, or comments about our service. Be as detailed as you like.
* **Frequency of Use**: Select how frequently you use our service:
	+ Monthly: If you use it monthly.
	+ Quarterly: If you use it quarterly.
	+ Annually: If you use it annually.
* **Satisfaction**: Indicate your overall satisfaction with our service:
	+ Very Satisfied: If you're extremely satisfied.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: If you're somewhat satisfied.
	+ Not Satisfied: If you're not satisfied.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: If you're very dissatisfied.
* **Product Updates**: Select whether you'd like to receive:
	+ True: Product updates and notifications.
	+ False: No updates or notifications.
* **Data Collection**: Indicate your preferences for data collection:
	+ True: Allow us to collect more data.
	+ False: Do not collect any additional data.
* **Retention**: Indicate your preference for data retention:
	+ High: Keep your data for a longer period.
	+ Medium: Keep your data for a moderate period.
	+ Low: Keep your data for a short period.
* **Submitted By**: Leave this field blank if you don't wish to be identified.
* **Submitted At**: Date and time of submission, automatically filled in.
* **User ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your account, if you have one.

## Tips
Please take a few minutes to answer these questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible. Your input will help us improve and develop our subscription service for your benefit.
