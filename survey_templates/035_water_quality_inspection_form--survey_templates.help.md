# water_quality_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The water_quality_inspection_form is a tool used to collect and document observations about water bodies such as lakes, rivers, or ponds. Its primary purpose is to gather data on physical parameters, sample date and time, and other attributes that contribute to a comprehensive understanding of water quality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of water body (lake, river, or pond) that you are inspecting.
2. Enter the specific location of the water body.
3. Choose the date of the sample collection (sample_date).
4. Enter the time of the sample collection (sample_time).
5. Record the pH level (ph_level) of the water body.
6. Note the turbidity (turbidity) of the water body.
7. Measure and record the temperature (temperature) of the water body.
8. Record the conductivity (conductivity) of the water body.
9. Describe the color (color) of the water body by selecting all applicable options (blue, green, brown).
10. Indicate the presence and intensity of the odor (odor) of the water body.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **water_body** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description of the water body being inspected.
* **water_body_type** (Select, Required): Choose from the options lake, river, or pond to describe the type of water body.
* **water_body_location** (Text, Required): Provide the exact location of the water body.
* **sample_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the sample collection.
* **sample_time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of the sample collection.
* **ph_level** (Number, Required): Record the pH level of the water body.
* **turbidity** (Number, Required): Note the level of turbidity.
* **temperature** (Number, Required): Measure and record the temperature of the water body.
* **conductivity** (Number, Required): Record the conductivity of the water body.
* **color** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all applicable colors (blue, green, brown) that describe the water body.
* **odor** (Select One, Required): Indicate the presence and intensity of the odor (none, mild, moderate, strong).
