# Health Region Change Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the current health region they are serving. It helps administrators identify areas that need improvement and make informed decisions.
 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question.
2.  Enter your responses in the text boxes or numerical fields as requested.
3.  Make sure to answer all required questions.
4.  Submit your responses when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Region Transfered**: Please enter the current region you are serving.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: no
*   **Satisfaction Level**: Please rate your satisfaction level on a scale of 1-10.
    *   Type: number
    *   Required: no
*   **Improvement Opportunities**: Please enter any areas you see for improvement in the current health region.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: no
*   **Region Transferred Satisfactory**: Please rate the transferred region's satisfactory level on a scale of 1-10.
    *   Type: number
    *   Required: no

## Tips

*   Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
*   If a field is marked as required, please make sure to fill it out.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your administrator for assistance.
