# Child Speech Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect information about a child's speech development, including their age when they first spoke, their ability to form multi-word sentences, and other factors that affect their language skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please answer each question carefully and accurately based on your observations and experiences with the child. Please provide the child's full name, date of birth, and other information requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Developmental History
- **Child Full Name** (`child_name`): Enter the child's full name as they are known in their daily life.
- **Date of Birth** (`child_dob`): Provide the child's date of birth to calculate their age.
- **Age when child said first word** (`first_word_age`): Indicate the approximate age when you first observed the child saying a word.
- **Does child use multi-word sentences?** (`multiword_sentence_status`): Select how often you see the child using multi-word sentences.
- **Approximate age when child said first word** (based on `first_word_age` and `child_dob`): Calculated age based on the child's date of birth and the age when they said their first word.

### Page 2: Speech Observations
- **Does child make eye contact during speech?** (`eye_contact_status`): Check if you frequently see the child maintaining eye contact while speaking.
- **Does child respond to their name?** (`name_response_status`): Select whether you often see the child responding to their name.
- **Describe primary concerns about child speech** (`primary_speech_concerns`): Share any concerns you have about the child's speech development, providing examples if possible.
- **Has child had a hearing test?** (`hearing_test_status`): Check if the child has recently undergone a hearing test to rule out auditory issues.

### Page 3: Clarity and Understanding
- **How much of child speech do you understand?** (`parent_understanding_level`): Rate how much of the child's speech you can understand.
- **How much of child speech do strangers understand?** (`stranger_understanding_level`): Rate how much of the child's speech strangers can understand.

### Page 4: Social Communication
- **Does child make eye contact during speech?** (`eye_contact_status`): Repeat from Page 2.
- **Does child respond to their name?** (`name_response_status`): Repeat from Page 2.

### Page 5: Primary Concerns
- **Has child had a hearing test?** (`hearing_test_status`): Repeat from Page 3.
- **Today's Date** (`survey_date`): Enter the date on which you're completing the survey.
