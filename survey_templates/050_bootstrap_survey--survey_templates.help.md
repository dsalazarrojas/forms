# Bootstrap Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your opinions and experiences about our website. Your feedback will help us understand how to improve and better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please answer each question with your honest and best judgment.
*   If you have not visited our website, please answer based on your general expectations.
*   If you have questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
*   You can skip optional questions, but answering them will provide more valuable insights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

    *   **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, Optional): Your email address for follow-up or further information. You can leave this field empty if you don't want to be contacted.
    *   **Date** (`survey_date`, date, Optional): Please enter today's date as per your system's display.

        +   Daily
        +   Several times a week
        +   Weekly
        +   Monthly
        +   Rarely
        +   First time

        +   Very easy

        +   Very difficult
    *   **Website Design Appeal** (`website_design_appeal`, select_one, Required): Rate the website's design appeal.
        +   Very appealing

        +   Neutral

        +   Very unappealing
    *   **Content Quality** (`content_quality`, select_one, Required): Rate the quality of the content on our site.
        +   Excellent
        +   Very Good
        +   Good
        +   Average
        +   Poor
    *   **Did You Find the Information You Needed** (`information_found`, select_one, Required): Rate how easily you found the information on our site.
        +   Yes, easily

        +   Partially
        +   False

    *   **Page Loading Speed** (`page_loading_speed`, select_one, Required): Rate how fast our site loads.
