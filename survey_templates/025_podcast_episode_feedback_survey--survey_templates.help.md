# Podcast Episode Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback from users who have listened to a specific podcast episode. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate you taking the time to share your thoughts!

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page. Each page will ask you to fill in a specific field with your feedback. Please make sure to answer all required fields to ensure that your feedback is collected correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name (optional).
* **Email**: Your email address. Please enter the same email address you used to subscribe to our podcast.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the podcast episode (1-5 stars).
* **Episode Number**: The number of the episode you're providing feedback for.
* **Feedback**: Share any comments or thoughts you have about the episode.
* **Episode Title**: The title of the episode you're providing feedback for.
* **Suggestion**: Any suggestions for future episodes or topics (optional).
* **Rating Why**: Why did you give your rating (e.g. good, bad, neutral).
* **Rating What**: What specifically about the episode made you give your rating (e.g. host, content, production quality).
* **Rating Where**: Where did you listen to the episode (e.g. car, home, office).
* **Rating When**: When did you listen to the episode (e.g. date).
* **Rating Who**: Who else listened to the episode with you (optional).
* **Rating Comments**: Any additional comments about your experience with the episode.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" must be filled in to ensure that your feedback is collected correctly.
