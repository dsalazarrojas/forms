# Historical Presentation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Historical Presentation Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and ratings on past presentations and events. This form helps to document and evaluate historical information, allowing for a structured assessment of the presentation's content, organization, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, including the presenter's name, event name, event date, and event location.
2. Rate the presentation on the evaluation scales provided (Presentation Score, Feedback Score, and Evaluation Scale).
3. Provide comments on the presentation and event in the respective fields (Feedback Comments, Evaluation Comments, and Evaluation Comments 2).
4. Fill in any additional information about the presentation and event (Presentation Topic, Presentation Length, and Presentation Notes).
5. Review and submit the form to complete the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the name of the person who presented.
* **E-mail**: Enter the email address of the presenter or event contact.
* **Presentation Score**: Rate the presentation's content, organization, and overall quality. (1-5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest)
* **Feedback Score**: Rate the presentation's feedback and comments. (1-5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest)
* **Overall Score**: Rate the overall quality of the presentation and event. (1-5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest)
* **Feedback Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback on the presentation.
* **Presentation Date**: Enter the date the presentation took place.
* **Presentation Time**: Enter the time the presentation took place (in 24-hour format).
* **Presentation Location**: Enter the location where the presentation took place.
* **Presentation Type**: Select the type of presentation (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Presenter Name**: Enter the name of the presenter.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event (in 24-hour format).
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Coordinator**: Enter the name of the event coordinator.
* **Event Speaker**: Enter the name of the event speaker.
* **Presentation Topic**: Enter the topic of the presentation.
* **Presentation Length**: Enter the length of the presentation.
* **Presentation Notes**: Enter any notes or additional information about the presentation.
* **Evaluation Scale 1**: Rate the presentation on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest.
* **Evaluation Comments 1**: Provide any comments or feedback on the presentation.
* **Evaluation Scale 2**: Rate the presentation on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest.
* **Evaluation Comments 2**: Provide any comments or feedback on the presentation.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete evaluation.
- Use a consistent rating system for all evaluations.
- Review and submit the form carefully before saving the evaluation.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your event administrator for assistance.
