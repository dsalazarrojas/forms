# Student Mental Health Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Mental Health Check Form is designed to collect information from students for mental health checks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your school psychologist's name on page 1.
2. Enter your student's name, student ID, and date of birth.
3. Select your gender from the provided options.
4. Check any mental health issues that apply to you.
5. Describe your symptoms in the text box.
6. Provide parent contact information, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Psychologist Name**: Enter the name of the school psychologist or mental health professional who will be using this form to assess your mental health.
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your records.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number or other identifying information.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the provided options (Male, Female, Other).
* **Mental Health Issue**: Check any mental health issues that you are currently experiencing, such as Anxiety, Depression, or ADHD.
* **Symptoms Description**: Describe your symptoms in as much detail as possible.
* **Parent Contact Info**: Provide any relevant parent contact information that may be necessary for your mental health support.

## Tips
Please be honest and accurate when completing this form. This information will be used to help support your mental health and well-being. If you are experiencing severe mental health concerns or suicidal thoughts, please seek immediate support from a mental health professional or call a crisis hotline.
