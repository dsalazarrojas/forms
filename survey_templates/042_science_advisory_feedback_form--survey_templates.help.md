# Science Advisory Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Science Advisory Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from participants in science advisory sessions to help improve future sessions and provide valuable insights to the session organizers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, follow these steps:
	*   Review the form fields and provide your information as instructed.
	*   Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
	*   Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant's Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the feedback report.
*   **Session ID**: Enter the session ID provided to you by the session organizer. This helps track your session.
*   **Your Feedback**: Provide your honest feedback about your experience in the session.
*   **How satisfied are you with your experience?**: Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 0 to 10.
*   **Do you have any recommendations for future sessions?**: Share any ideas or suggestions you have to improve future sessions.
*   **Any comments?**: Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experience.
*   **Participant's E-mail**: If you'd like to be contacted about future sessions or to receive more information about science advisory sessions, enter your email address here.
*   **Participant's Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
*   **UTM**: This is an automatically populated field for tracking and is not relevant for participant input.
*   **UTM Source**: This is an automatically populated field for tracking and is not relevant for participant input.
*   **UTM Medium**: This is an automatically populated field for tracking and is not relevant for participant input.
*   **UTM Campaign**: This is an automatically populated field for tracking and is not relevant for participant input.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the session organizer.
*   Your feedback is valuable and helps improve future science advisory sessions.
