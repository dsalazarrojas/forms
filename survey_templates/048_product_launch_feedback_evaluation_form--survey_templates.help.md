# product_launch_feedback_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The product launch feedback evaluation form is designed to gather feedback from end users about their experience with a newly launched product. The form is intended to collect information about the product's overall satisfaction, feedback, and any additional information or contact details that users may want to share. This information will help the product team to understand how users perceive the product and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the product you are evaluating.
2.  Enter the launch date of the product.
3.  Select your overall satisfaction with the product.
4.  Provide your feedback about the product.
5.  If you have any additional information, you can enter it here.
6.  Enter your email address.
7.  Enter your phone number.
8.  Provide a submission confirmation message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product you are evaluating.
* **Product Launch Date** (`product_launch_date`, date, required): Enter the launch date of the product.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your overall satisfaction with the product using the options "Very Satisfied", "Somewhat Satisfied", "Somewhat Dissatisfied", or "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Product Launch Feedback** (`product_launch_feedback`, text, required): Provide your feedback about the product.
* **Additional Information** (`product_launch_feedback_additional_info`, text, optional): If you have any additional information, you can enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, note, required): Provide a submission confirmation message that will be displayed after the submission.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide detailed feedback about your experience with the product.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at your email or phone number.
