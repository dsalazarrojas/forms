# Technology And Values Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather opinions and insights on the relationship between technology and social values. Your responses will help us better understand the public perception of technology and its impact on society.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them in a clear and concise manner.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in your responses.
3. Do not hesitate to elaborate on your answers if you feel the need to clarify your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what-is-tech-survey**: This question asks about the general perception of technology. Please describe your thoughts on the current state of technology and how it affects you or your community.
* **societal-impact**: This question explores the social implications of technology. Consider the effects of technology on your relationships, family, or community, and share your observations.
* **ethics-and-regulation**: This question delves into the ethics of technology. Please think about the moral implications of technological advancements and how they should be regulated.
* **future-impact**: This question looks into the potential future effects of technology. Consider how technology might shape the world in the years to come and its potential consequences.
* **tech-society**: This question is about the relationship between technology and society as a whole. Please share your thoughts on how technology influences and is influenced by social values.
* **innovation**: This question asks about the innovation that technology brings. Consider the advantages and disadvantages of new technologies and how they are developed.
* **regulation-1**: This question is about the regulation of technology. Think about the current laws and regulations that govern technological advancements and whether they are sufficient.
* **ethics-1**: This question explores the ethics of technology. Please reflect on the moral implications of technological advancements and how they should be managed.
* **future-1**: This question looks into the future of technology. Consider the potential effects of new technologies on your life, community, or society.
* **tech-regulation**: This question is about the regulation of technology in the future. Think about the laws and regulations that will govern technological advancements and how they should be adapted.

Tips:
* Be honest and open in your responses.
* Provide as much detail as possible.
* Do not worry about grammar or spelling errors, your thoughts are valuable regardless of format.
