# Backflow Tester Registration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Backflow Tester Registration Survey is used to register and verify Backflow testers for the municipality or utility service. This form is necessary for individuals who want to become authorized testers for the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Tester Full Name** field with your full name.
2. Next, enter your **Company/Employer Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide your **Business Email Address** in the required format.
4. Enter your **Business Phone Number** for contact purposes.
5. Select all the relevant **Specific Device Certifications** that apply to your experience.
6. Enter your **Years of Experience in Backflow Testing**.
7. Enter your **Primary Service Area (Counties/Cities)** where you will be performing Backflow tests.
8. Enter your **Main Test Kit Serial Number** for identification.
9. Select the **Last Test Kit Calibration Date**, which must be within the last 12 months.
10. Certify that you have **current liability insurance as required**.
11. Agree to **submit all test reports within 48 hours of inspection**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Certified Backflow Tester Registration --** (`header`): This is the title of the form and an introduction to the registration process.
* **Tester Full Name** (`tester_full_name_reg`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name for registration purposes.
* **Company/Employer Name** (`tester_company_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your company or employer.
* **Business Email Address** (`contact_email_tester`, `email`, `required`): Provide your business email address in the required format.
* **Business Phone Number** (`contact_phone_tester`, `text`, `required`): Enter your business phone number for contact purposes.
* **-- Credentials and Licensing --** (`license_and_cert_header`, `note`, `required`): This section is for your credentials and licensing information.
* **State Backflow Tester License Number** (`state_license_number`, `text`, `required`): Enter your state Backflow tester license number.
* **License Expiration Date** (`license_expiration_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date your Backflow tester license expires.
* **Years of Experience in Backflow Testing** (`years_experience_backflow`, `number`, `required`): Enter the number of years you have experience in Backflow testing.
* **Specific Device Certifications** (`certifications_held_tester`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the certifications that apply to your experience.
* **Primary Service Area (Counties/Cities)** (`service_area_tester`, `text`, `required`): Enter the primary service area where you will be performing Backflow tests.
* **Main Test Kit Serial Number** (`test_kit_serial_number`, `text`, `required`): Enter the main test kit serial number for identification.
* **Last Test Kit Calibration Date** (`test_kit_calibration_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the last test kit calibration date, which must be within the last 12 months.
* **I certify that I have current liability insurance as required** (`proof_of_insurance_ack`, `select_one`, `required`): Select "I certify" to confirm you have current liability insurance.
* **I agree to submit all test reports within 48 hours of inspection** (`tester_declaration_reg`, `select_one`, `required`): Select "I agree and understand" to confirm you agree to submit all test reports within 48 hours of inspection.
