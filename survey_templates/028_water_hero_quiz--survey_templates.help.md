# Water Hero Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from individuals who wish to engage with the water conservation program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant details section with your basic information such as name, email, and location.
2. Answer the quiz forms, which are a series of questions related to water conservation and sustainability.
3. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This section collects your basic information for tracking and identification purposes.
	+ **Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
	+ **Email**: Enter your valid email address for future correspondence.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further clarification.
	+ **Location**: Enter your location for geospatial identification.
* **Quiz Form 1-8**: These are a series of text-based questions related to water conservation and sustainability.
	+ Each question is designed to assess your understanding and preferences related to water conservation.
	+ Please answer each question accurately and honestly.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your response.
* **Quiz Results**: This section displays your results after submitting the form. Please review it carefully to ensure that your answers are accurate.
* **Quiz Result 1**: This field displays the result of one of the quiz questions. Please select the correct option based on your understanding.
	+ This field is optional and will only be displayed if the corresponding quiz form is submitted.
