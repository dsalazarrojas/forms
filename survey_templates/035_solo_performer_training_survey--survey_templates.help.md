# Solo Performer Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your training habits, performance frequency, and support needs as a solo performer. The collected data will help us better understand your needs and create more effective training programs for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form, which includes questions about your artist discipline, performance experience, training goals, support needs, and more.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, and make sure to select all applicable options for multiple-choice questions.
3. Don't worry if you're not sure about something - just fill out what you know and we'll do our best to help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Discipline (<artist_discipline>, text, optional)**: This is the primary discipline or area you specialize in as an artist (e.g., music, dance, theater, etc.).
* **Performance Experience (<performance_experience, number, optional)**: Indicate your level of experience performing in your discipline, on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **What are your training goals? (<training_goals, select_one, optional)**: Choose one or both of the following options:
	+ Improve technique
	+ Increase performance
	+ Both
* **What are your current training habits? (<training_habits, select_multiple, optional)**: Select all that apply:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times a week
	+ Rarely
	+ Not at all
* **What kind of support do you need? (<support_needs, select_one, optional)**: Choose one of the following:
	+ Technical
	+ Emotional
	+ Financial
	+ None
* **How frequently do you train in your discipline? (<discipline_frequency, select_multiple, optional)**: Select all that apply:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times a week
	+ Rarely
	+ Not at all
* **How frequently do you perform? (<performance_frequency, select_multiple, optional)**: Select all that apply:
	+ Frequently
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Note (<note, note, optional)**: Write any additional comments or notes about your training or performance.
* **Email (<email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further information or to receive updates about your training.
* **Phone (<phone, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.

## Tips

* Please be honest about your training habits and support needs - this will help us create more effective programs for you.
* If you have any specific questions or requests, feel free to ask and we'll do our best to help.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey - your feedback is invaluable to us!
