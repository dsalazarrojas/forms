# Pet Healthcare Financing Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from pet owners about their interest in pet healthcare financing options. It helps us understand what type of pet they have, what payment plans they are interested in, and other relevant details. This information will be used to tailor our services and provide better support to pet owners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pet you have from the options provided.
2. Enter your name as the owner of the pet.
3. Choose the payment plans that interest you from the options provided.
4. Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted.
5. Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your pet's healthcare needs.
7. Select a date for your appointment (if applicable).
8. Select a time for your appointment (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of pet do you have?** (`pet_type`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to gather information about the type of pet you have. Please select the type of pet that applies to you.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required: false): This is where you enter your name as the owner of the pet.
* **What payment plan would you like to see?** (`payment_plan`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to gather information about the payment plans you are interested in. You can select multiple options if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is used to collect your email address. If you would like to be contacted, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect your phone number. If you would like to be contacted, please enter your phone number here.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, note, required: false): This field is used to provide any additional comments or notes about your pet's healthcare needs.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): This field is used to select a date for your appointment (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): This field is used to select a time for your appointment (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all relevant fields to help us better serve you and your pet's needs.
* If you have any questions or need clarification about any of the fields, feel free to contact us.
* Please be specific and honest in your answers to help us understand your preferences and needs accurately.
