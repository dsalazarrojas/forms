# Parent Stress And Burnout Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help parents or caregivers gauge their level of stress and burnout, identifying potential factors contributing to these feelings. By completing this form, you'll be providing valuable insights into your personal situation.

## How To Complete This Form

### Introduction Page
The first page of the form, "Parent Stress And Burnout Survey," serves as an introduction to the survey questions that follow. Please take a moment to read this page before proceeding.

### Survey Questions
Each subsequent page will present a series of questions related to your experiences and feelings. Please answer these questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.

### Tips
- Take your time, and answer each question to the best of your ability.
- If a question doesn't seem relevant, please feel free to skip or comment about it in the "comments" section.
- You can answer "Other" when selecting options if none of the provided choices fit your experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **How would you rate your current stress level?**
  • Label: Caregiver Stress Level
  • Type: Select One
  • Purpose: This question helps us understand your subjective stress level, which can influence your responses to other questions.
  • Options: Low, Moderate, High, Not sure

- **What coping strategies do you currently use?**
  • Label: Coping Strategies
  • Type: Select Multiple
  • Purpose: This question explores your coping mechanisms, which can be effective or ineffective in managing stress.
  • Options: Exercise, Mindfulness, Talking to friends or family, Other

- **What are some of the main stressors in your life?**
  • Label: Stressors
  • Type: Text
  • Purpose: This question allows you to specify the specific challenges you face.
  • No options are provided as it's an open-ended question.

- **Have you experienced any stressful experiences in the past 2 weeks?**
  • Label: Stressful Experiences
  • Type: Select Multiple
  • Purpose: This question investigates recent stressful events that may be contributing to your overall stress level.
  • Options: Financial stress, Health concerns, Work stress, Relationship difficulties, Parenting stress, Other

- **How would you rate the supportiveness of systems for you as a caregiver?**
  • Label: Caregiver Support Systems
  • Type: Select One
  • Purpose: This question gauges the effectiveness of support systems in your life.
  • Options: Very supportive, Somewhat supportive, Not very supportive

- **What would you do to prevent burnout?**
  • Label: Burnout Prevention
  • Type: Select One
  • Purpose: This question helps identify strategies for mitigating burnout.
  • Options: Take more breaks, Prioritize tasks, Set boundaries, Practice self-care, Other

- **Who have you reached out to for help with stress?**
  • Label: Help Sources
  • Type: Select Multiple
  • Purpose: This question explores your support network.
  • Options: Family members, Friends, Mental health professional, Other

- **Do you have any comments or feedback about this survey?**
  • Label: Comments
  • Type: Note
  • Purpose: This is an open-ended question for any additional insights or thoughts.
  • No options are provided as it's an open-ended question.
