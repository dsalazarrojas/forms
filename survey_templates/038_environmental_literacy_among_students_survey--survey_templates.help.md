# Environmental Literacy Among Students Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on students' environmental literacy levels and experiences. It aims to collect feedback and opinions from students and their teachers to inform educators, researchers, and policymakers on how to improve environmental education and literacy programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form by providing the required and optional information listed below.
2. Ensure that you are filling out the form for your own school and as a student or teacher who is directly affiliated with the students.
3. If you are filling out the form for a student, please provide information about your own school and experiences.
4. If you are a teacher, please provide information about your school and students.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Id** (`school_id`, number, optional): Please enter your school's ID number. This helps us to keep track of which school you are affiliated with.
* **Survey Year** (`survey_year`, number, optional): Please enter the year you are filling out this survey.
* **Student Survey Id** (`student_survey_id`, number, optional): Please enter a unique ID number for the student survey.
* **Teacher Survey Id** (`teacher_survey_id`, number, optional): Please enter a unique ID number for the teacher survey.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Please enter your school's name.
* **Researcher Name** (`researcher_name`, text, optional): Please enter the researcher's name.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Please enter your school's email address.
* **Researcher Email** (`researcher_email`, email, optional): Please enter the researcher's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your school's phone number.
* **Researcher Phone** (`researcher_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the researcher's phone number.
* **School Website** (`school_website`, text, optional): Please enter your school's website.
* **Researcher Website** (`researcher_website`, text, optional): Please enter the researcher's website.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Please enter your school's physical address.
* **Researcher Address** (`researcher_address`, text, optional): Please enter the researcher's physical address.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Please enter your own name as a student.
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, optional): Please enter your age as a student.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, optional): Please enter your current grade as a student.
* **School Level** (`school_level`, select_multiple, optional): Please select one or more school levels you are affiliated with (e.g., High School, Middle School, Elementary, College/University, Other).
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, text, optional): Please enter the subject area you are interested in or related to (e.g., environmental science, biology, chemistry).
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as a teacher.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address as a teacher.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number as a teacher.
* **Teacher Survey Completed** (`teacher_survey_completed`, date, optional): Please enter the date you completed this survey.
* **Student Survey Completed** (`student_survey_completed`, date, optional): Please enter the date you completed this survey as a student.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are filling out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask your teacher or school administrator for guidance.
* This form is for your school and your own experiences, so please provide information that is specific to your school and your role as a student or teacher.
