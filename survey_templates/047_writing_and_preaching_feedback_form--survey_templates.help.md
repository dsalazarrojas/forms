# Writing And Preaching Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Writing And Preaching Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback on writing and preaching messages. This form helps to understand how to improve the quality and effectiveness of the messages shared by the speaker or writer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Writing And Preaching Feedback Form** title.
2.  Rate the message by selecting a number (e.g., 1-5) in the **How would you rate this message** field.
3.  Provide a brief **Message** about the content of the message.
4.  Suggest improvements for future messages by typing in the **Do you have any suggestions for the next message?** field.
5.  Select the **Category** of the message by choosing from the options **Sermon**, **Written Message**, or **Video**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Writing And Preaching Feedback Form** (1): Enter the title of the form, which should be a brief description of the purpose of the form.
* **How would you rate this message** (2): Rate the message on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Message** (3): Provide a brief description of the content of the message, including any positive or negative comments.
* **Do you have any suggestions for the next message?** (4): Suggest improvements or ideas for future messages, such as adjusting tone, content, or style.
* **Category** (5): Select the type of message being rated, which can be **Sermon**, **Written Message**, or **Video**.

**Tips**

* Be honest and constructive with your feedback to help improve the quality of the messages.
* Keep your message and suggestions brief and to the point.
* Choose the most suitable category for the message being rated.
