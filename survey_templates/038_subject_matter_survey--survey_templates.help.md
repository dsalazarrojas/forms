# Subject Matter Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subject Matter Survey form is designed to collect information about a subject matter. This form is intended for general inquiries and data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
### Page 1: First Name
* Complete the First Name field with your name as it is known to you.

### Page 2: Email
* Enter your valid email address.

### Page 3: What Subject
* Select "Yes" or "No" to answer the question.

### Page 4: Description
* Enter a brief description of the subject matter.

### Page 5: Category
* Select multiple categories from the provided options.

### Page 6: Date of Birth
* Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Page 7: Time of Last Contact
* Enter the time of last contact in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### Page 8: Note
* Enter any additional notes or comments about the subject matter.

### Page 9: Phone
* Enter your phone number.

### Page 10: Rating 1-5
* Provide a rating for each of the 5 questions. Ratings range from 1 to 5.

### Page 11-15: Select 1-5
* Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the 5 questions.
