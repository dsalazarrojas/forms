# Technology Survey For Remote Learning - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback on your experience with remote learning. Your responses will help us understand how we can improve the platform or system you're using.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the option that best describes your remote learning experience.
2. Choose all options that apply for the "Easy Of Use" and "Support" sections.
3. Type your overall rating or provide additional comments.
4. Enter your email and phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Remote learning experience (2)** (`remote_learning_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your overall remote learning experience. This question is used to gauge your satisfaction with remote learning.
* **Easy Of Use (2)** (`easy_of_use`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all options that apply to your experience with the ease of use of remote learning. This section helps us understand what makes the platform or system easy or difficult to use.
* **Support (2)** (`support`, `select_one`, optional): Select the option that best describes your experience with support for remote learning. This question helps us understand how well we're supporting you.
* **Overall rating** (`overall`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief rating of your overall experience with remote learning. This can be a number or a brief comment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the remote learning experience.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **Technology** (`technology`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the technology used for remote learning.
* **Technology (2)** (`technology_2`, `text`, optional): Another opportunity to provide feedback on technology.
