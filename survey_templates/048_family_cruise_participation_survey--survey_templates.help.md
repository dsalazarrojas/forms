# Family Cruise Participation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Family Cruise Participation Survey is designed to gather information about your family's preferences and needs for a cruise. This survey will help us understand your interests, cabin requirements, and other essential details to ensure a pleasant and comfortable experience for everyone on board.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "General Info" field with any relevant details about your family.
2. Provide the headcount for your family.
3. Select your preferred date for the cruise.
4. Choose your preferred cabin type and location.
5. If you have any special needs, such as a wheelchair, walker, or medical condition, please select all that apply.
6. Enter any additional family information in the "Family Info" field.
7. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
8. Add any comments or notes about your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (General Info, text, optional)**: This field is where you can enter any relevant information about your family, such as any special requirements or circumstances.
* **Headcount (Headcount, number, optional)**: Please enter the number of family members that will be traveling with you.
* **Date Preferences (Date, date, optional)**: Choose a date that works best for your family, taking into account any school or work commitments.
* **Cabin Preferences (Cabin Preferences, select one, optional)**: Select the type of cabin you prefer for your family, such as 2-person, 3-person, or 4-person cabin.
* **Special Needs (Special Needs, select multiple, optional)**: If you have any special needs, such as a wheelchair or walker, please select all that apply.
* **Cabin Type (Cabin Type, select one, optional)**: Choose the type of cabin you prefer, such as Balcony, Balcony Suite, or Non-smoking.
* **Cabin Location (Cabin Location, select multiple, optional)**: Select the location of the cabin you prefer, such as Starboard, Center Deck, or Aft Deck.
* **Family Info (Family Info, text, optional)**: Enter any additional information about your family that may be helpful for us to know.
* **Date (Date, date, optional)**: Enter the date you prefer for the cruise.
* **Time (Time, time, optional)**: Enter the time you prefer for the cruise, if applicable.
* **Email (Email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone (Phone, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Comment (Comment, text, optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your preferences.
