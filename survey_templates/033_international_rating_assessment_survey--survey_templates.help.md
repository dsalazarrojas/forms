# International Rating Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experience with our service. Your feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better services to our users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions based on your actual experience with our service.
2. Select an option for each field, and make sure to fill in all required fields.
3. Take your time, and answer accurately to the best of your abilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, Required: False): Please select the country that you used our service in.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with our service?** (`satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, Required: False): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service. Choose one or more of the options provided.
* **On a scale of 1-5, how likely is it that you would recommend our service to a colleague or friend?** (`recommendation_intent`, `select_one`, Required: False): Rate your likelihood of recommending our service.
* **How would you rate the quality of our service?** (`service_quality`, `select_multiple`, Required: False): Rate the quality of our service. Choose one or more of the options provided.
* **Do you have any suggestions for how we can improve?** (`improvement_suggestion`, `select_multiple`, Required: False): If you have any suggestions, select one or more of the options provided.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, Required: False): Please enter the date you used our service.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, Required: False): Please enter the time you used our service.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Required: False): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or for us to contact you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Required: False): If you would like to provide your phone number, enter it here.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, Required: False): Enter any additional comments or feedback you would like to provide.
* **Additional Comments** (`comment`, `text`, Required: False): If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them here.
* **Countries** (`country_list`, `select_multiple`, Required: False): Please select any countries that you used our service in.
