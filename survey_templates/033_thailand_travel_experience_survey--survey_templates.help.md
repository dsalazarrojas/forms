# Thailand Travel Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your recent travel experience to Thailand. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to help us understand your satisfaction and provide us with valuable feedback for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*) have a valid response before submitting the form.
2. Use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
3. For select fields (Team Leaders and Team Members), choose one or more options from the provided list.
4. For text fields (Activities and Recommendations), provide clear and concise answers.
5. You can submit the form once you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): This question measures your general satisfaction with our travel experience. Please rate your overall satisfaction level from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): Please select the date of your recent travel to Thailand. Use the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, \*): This question asks for the length of your recent travel to Thailand. Please answer in days.
* **Travel Team Size** (`travel_team_size`, number, \*): Please indicate the number of people in your travel team.
* **Team Leaders** (`team_leaders`, select_multiple, \*): Choose one or more team leaders from the provided list.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, \*): Choose one or more team members from the provided list. Please make sure to select team members who were actually present during your travel.
* **Destinations** (`destinations`, select_one, \*): Select the destination(s) you visited during your travel.
* **Activities** (`activities`, text, optional): Briefly describe some of the activities you participated in during your travel.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide suggestions on how we can improve our travel experience.
* **Satisfaction Comments** (`satisfaction_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about your overall satisfaction.
