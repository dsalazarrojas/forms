# Event Planning Effectiveness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Planning Effectiveness Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate the effectiveness of events in terms of event planning, communication, and follow-up. This form helps to assess the success of events, identify areas for improvement, and provide actionable feedback for event planners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and questions carefully.
2. Answer each question based on your experience and judgment.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
4. Follow the instructions provided for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Planning Assessment** (`event_planning_assessment`, `note`, required: false)
This section is used to evaluate the overall effectiveness of the event planning process. Please note down any observations, comments, or suggestions you have about the event planning process.

*   **Event Planning Assessment** is a free-form text field where you can provide your thoughts on the event planning process.

### 2. **Event Success Factors** (`event_success_factors`, `select_one`, required: false)
This question assesses whether the event was successful in achieving its objectives. Please select one of the options:

*   **Yes**: The event was successful in achieving its objectives.
*   **No**: The event was not successful in achieving its objectives.

### 3. **Event Outcomes** (`event_outcomes`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
This section is used to evaluate the outcomes of the event. Please select all applicable options:

*   **Yes**: The event had a positive outcome.
*   **No**: The event did not have a positive outcome.

### 4. **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, `number`, required: false)
This field is used to rate the effectiveness of communication during the event. Please rate on a scale of 1-10.

### 5. **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, required: false)
This section is used to provide any feedback or comments about the event. Please note down any thoughts or suggestions you have.

### 6. **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `date`, required: false)
This field is used to record the follow-up activity after the event. Please enter the date of the follow-up activity.

### 7. **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, `number`, required: false)
This field is used to record the time spent during the event. Please enter the number of hours or minutes spent.

### 8. **Event Outcome Rating** (`event_outcome_rating`, `number`, required: false)
This field is used to rate the outcome of the event on a scale of 1-10.

### 9. **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
This section is used to assess the frequency of feedback during the event. Please select all applicable options:

*   **Yes**: Feedback was frequent and timely.
*   **No**: Feedback was infrequent or not provided.

### 10. **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
This section is used to assess the communication channels used during the event. Please select all applicable options:

*   **Yes**: Communication channels were effective.
*   **No**: Communication channels were not effective.

### 11. **Communication Channels Rating** (`communication_channels_rating`, `number`, required: false)
This field is used to rate the effectiveness of communication channels on a scale of 1-10.

### 12. **Follow Up Status** (`follow_up_status`, `select_one`, required: false)
This question is used to determine the follow-up status after the event. Please select one of the options:

*   **Active**: Follow-up is ongoing.
*   **Inactive**: Follow-up has ceased.

## Tips
*   Be honest and objective in your responses.
*   Provide specific examples and details to support your answers.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
