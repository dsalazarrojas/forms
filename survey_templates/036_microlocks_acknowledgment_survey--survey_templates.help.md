# Microlocks Acknowledgment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microlocks Acknowledgment Survey is used to document and verify the completion of tasks or events related to microlocks. This form ensures that all parties involved are informed and acknowledged, and that all necessary steps are taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Salon Details** section with the relevant information about the salon.
2. Provide your **Client Details** in the corresponding section.
3. Select whether or not you have **Selected Service**.
4. Enter the **Appointment Date** and **Appointment Time** for the acknowledgment.
5. Enter your **Acknowledgment**, which is the main text body of the acknowledgment.
6. Add your **Signature** to the acknowledgment.
7. Enter your **Email** address, which will be used to send the acknowledgment.
8. Enter your **Phone** number, which will be used to contact you if needed.
9. Add any **Additional Comments** if necessary.
10. Select whether or not you **Consent** to the acknowledgment.
11. Enter the **Acknowledge Date** and **Acknowledge Time** for the acknowledgment.
12. Select the **Acknowledge Status** of the acknowledgment (Active or Inactive).
13. Add any **Acknowledge Notes** if necessary.
14. Add your **Acknowledge Signature** to the acknowledgment.
15. Select whether or not you have **Sent the Acknowledgment** to the relevant parties.
16. Select whether or not you have **Sent the Acknowledgment by** the relevant party.
17. Select whether or not you have **Copied the Acknowledgment** to the relevant parties.
18. Select whether or not you have **CC'd the Acknowledgment** to other parties.
19. Select whether or not you have **BCC'd the Acknowledgment** to other parties.
20. Add any **Acknowledge Comments** if necessary.
21. Enter the **Acknowledge Status Updated** date and time.
22. Select the **Acknowledge Status Updated By** status.
23. Enter the **Acknowledge Version** number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Salon Details
* **Salon Details** (text, not required): Enter the name and address of the salon where the microlock took place.

### Client Details
* **Client Details** (text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the client being acknowledged.

### Service Selected
* **Service Selected** (select one, required): Select whether or not you have selected the service for the microlock.

### Appointment Date
* **Appointment Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the appointment for the microlock.

### Appointment Time
* **Appointment Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the appointment for the microlock.

### Acknowledgment
* **Acknowledgment** (text, required): Enter the main text of the acknowledgment, describing the microlock and any necessary details.

### Signature
* **Signature** (note, required): Sign your name to the acknowledgment.

### Email
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the email address to which the acknowledgment will be sent.

### Phone
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number to which the acknowledgment will be sent if necessary.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (text, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about the microlock.

### Consent
* **Consent** (select one, required): Select whether or not you consent to the acknowledgment.

### Acknowledge Date
* **Acknowledge Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the acknowledgment.

### Acknowledge Time
* **Acknowledge Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the acknowledgment.

### Acknowledge Status
* **Acknowledge Status** (select one, required): Select the status of the acknowledgment (Active or Inactive).

### Acknowledge Notes
* **Acknowledge Notes** (note, not required): Add any additional notes or comments about the acknowledgment.

### Acknowledge Signature
* **Acknowledge Signature** (note, required): Sign your name to the acknowledgment.

### Acknowledge Sent To
* **Acknowledge Sent To** (select multiple, required): Select whether or not you have sent the acknowledgment to the relevant parties.

### Acknowledge Sent By
* **Acknowledge Sent By** (select multiple, required): Select whether or not you have sent the acknowledgment by the relevant party.

### Acknowledge Copy To
* **Acknowledge Copy To** (select multiple, required): Select whether or not you have copied the acknowledgment to the relevant parties.

### Acknowledge CC
* **Acknowledge CC** (select multiple, not required): Select whether or not you have CC'd the acknowledgment to other parties.

### Acknowledge BCC
* **Acknowledge BCC** (select multiple, not required): Select whether or not you have BCC'd the acknowledgment to other parties.

### Acknowledge Comments
* **Acknowledge Comments** (text, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about the acknowledgment.

### Acknowledge Status Updated
* **Acknowledge Status Updated** (date, not required): Enter the date and time when the acknowledgment status was updated.

### Acknowledge Status Updated By
* **Acknowledge Status Updated By** (select one, required): Select the status of the acknowledgment when it was updated.

### Acknowledge Version
* **Acknowledge Version** (number, required): Enter the version number of the acknowledgment.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill in all fields carefully and accurately.
* Review the form before submitting it.
* Ensure that all parties involved are informed and acknowledged.
* Keep the acknowledgment up to date with the latest version number.
