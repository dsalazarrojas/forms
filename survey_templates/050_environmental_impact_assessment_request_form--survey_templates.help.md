# environmental_impact_assessment_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Environmental Impact Assessment Request Form is used to collect information about a project's environmental impacts and potential hazards. This form is essential for assessing the potential risks and consequences of a project and ensuring that it complies with relevant regulatory frameworks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter project details, including its location.
2. Indicate the potential hazards associated with the project.
3. Specify the risk characteristics of the project.
4. Answer yes or no to questions about environmental impacts.
5. Select the current project status.
6. Identify the relevant regulatory frameworks that apply to the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (project_details, text, required): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Impact Categories** (impact_categories, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether the project has any environmental impact categories (yes or no).
* **Potential Hazards** (potential_hazards, select_one, required): Choose one potential hazard associated with the project.
* **Risk Characteristics** (risk_characteristics, select_one, required): Select the risk characteristic of the project (Risk 1, Risk 2, or Risk 3).
* **Project Location** (project_location, text, required): Enter the location of the project.
* **Environmental Impacts** (environmental_impacts, select_multiple, required): Answer yes or no to questions about environmental impacts.
* **Project Status** (project_status, select_one, required): Select the current status of the project (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Regulatory Frameworks** (regulatory_frameworks, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether the project is subject to any regulatory frameworks (yes or no).

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately to ensure the assessment is thorough.
* If unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant authorities or departments.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
