# Post Case Resolution Client Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Case Resolution Client Feedback Questionnaire form is designed to gather feedback from clients regarding the resolution of a case. This feedback is vital in identifying areas for service improvement, ensuring that the organization's services meet the clients' expectations and needs. 
## How To Complete This Form
### Required Steps

1. Enter the Case ID in the "Case ID" field. This is an identifier assigned to the case.
2. Select the "Case Type" that best describes the case.
3. Enter the "Case Date" when the case was resolved.
4. Select the "Case Manager" who managed the case.
5. Enter your feedback about the client in the "Client Feedback" field.
6. Mention specific areas that need improvement for service in the "Service Improvement Area" field.
7. Enter any comments about the case in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
9. Enter the client's phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter the "Case Manager Email" of the person who managed the case.
11. Indicate if a follow-up is required in the "Follow-up Required" field.
12. Enter any follow-up details in the "Follow-up Details" field.
13. Select the "Follow-up Manager" who will conduct the follow-up.
14. Enter the "Follow-up Date" when the follow-up will take place.
15. Enter any follow-up comments in the "Follow-up Comments" field.
16. Enter any contact details for the follow-up in the "Follow-up Contact" field.
17. Select the "Follow-up Case Manager" who will be involved in the follow-up.
18. Enter the "Follow-up Case Manager Email" for the person responsible for the follow-up.
19. Enter details about the follow-up case in the "Follow-up Case Details" field.
20. Select the current status of the case in the "Case Status" field.
21. Enter any comments about the case closure in the "Case Closure Comments" field.
22. Select the person who closed the case in the "Case Closing Manager" field.
23. Enter the date when the case was closed in the "Case Closing Date" field.
24. Enter any review comments about the case in the "Review Comments" field.
25. Enter the name of the reviewer in the "Reviewer" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Case ID** (number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the case.
- **Case Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of case that best describes it.
- **Case Date** (date, required): Enter the date when the case was resolved.
- **Case Manager** (select_one, required): Select the person who managed the case.
- **Client Feedback** (text, required): Enter any feedback from the client about the case resolution.
- **Service Improvement Area** (text, required): Enter areas where the service can be improved.
- **Comments** (text, optional): Enter any comments about the case.
- **Contact Details** (email, required): Enter your contact details.
- **Phone** (text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
- **Case Manager Email** (email, required): Enter the email of the person who managed the case.
- **Follow-up Required** (select_one, required): Indicate if a follow-up is required for the case.
- **Follow-up Details** (text, optional): Enter any follow-up details.
- **Follow-up Manager** (select_one, required): Select the person who will conduct the follow-up.
- **Follow-up Date** (date, required): Enter the date when the follow-up will take place.
- **Follow-up Comments** (text, optional): Enter any follow-up comments.
- **Follow-up Contact** (text, required): Enter any contact details for the follow-up.
- **Follow-up Case Manager** (select_one, required): Select the person who will be involved in the follow-up.
- **Follow-up Case Manager Email** (email, required): Enter the email of the person responsible for the follow-up.
- **Follow-up Case Details** (text, required): Enter details about the follow-up case.
- **Case Status** (select_multiple, required): Select the current status of the case.
- **Case Closure Comments** (text, required): Enter any comments about the case closure.
- **Case Closing Manager** (select_one, required): Select the person who closed the case.
- **Case Closing Date** (date, required): Enter the date when the case was closed.
- **Review Comments** (text, optional): Enter any review comments about the case.
- **Reviewer** (text, required): Enter the name of the reviewer.
