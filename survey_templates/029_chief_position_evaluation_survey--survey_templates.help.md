# Chief Position Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chief Position Evaluation Survey is a comprehensive tool for evaluating the performance of a Chief Executive or leader within an organization. It assesses their strengths and weaknesses across various critical areas, providing a detailed picture of their effectiveness in leading the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing a brief introduction and context for the survey, including the evaluation date, your name, and your role in the organization.
2. Select the Executive being evaluated and provide their title and position.
3. Choose the evaluation period that is relevant to your assessment.
4. Answer questions on the performance of the executive in various areas:
	* Ability to develop and articulate strategic vision
	* Overall leadership effectiveness
	* Quality of decision-making
	* Communication and transparency
	* Ability to develop and retain talent
	* Financial performance and results achievement
	* Stakeholder and customer relations management
	* Promoting innovation and continuous improvement
	* Ethical standards and integrity
5. Provide a few key achievements during the review period
6. Identify areas requiring improvement
7. Suggest recommended professional development areas for the executive
8. Rate the executive's overall performance and provide any additional feedback or comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluation date**: A required field to indicate the period being evaluated.
	+ Please enter the date when this survey is being completed.
* **Evaluator name**: A required field for your name as the evaluator.
	+ This will help identify the person submitting the evaluation.
* **Your role in the organization**: A required field to specify your relationship with the executive.
	+ This could be as a Board member, Senior executive, Direct report, or other.
* **Executive being evaluated**: A required field to identify the executive being surveyed.
	+ This could be a Chief Executive, CEO, or other similar leadership position.
* **Executive position/title**: A required field to specify the executive's position or title.
	+ This will help clarify the specific role being evaluated.
* **Evaluation period**: A required field to select the relevant evaluation period.
	+ This could be 6 months, Annual review, 2-year period, or Other.
* **Ability to develop and articulate strategic vision**: A required field to rate the executive's ability to create and share a clear, compelling vision.
	+ Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Overall leadership effectiveness**: A required field to rate the executive's performance as a leader.
	+ Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Quality of decision-making**: A required field to evaluate the executive's decision-making process.
	+ Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Communication and transparency**: A required field to assess the executive's communication with stakeholders.
	+ Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Ability to develop and retain talent**: A required field to evaluate the executive's talent management.
	+ Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Financial performance and results achievement**: A required field to rate the executive's financial achievements.
	+ Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Stakeholder and customer relations management**: A required field to assess the executive's relationships with stakeholders and customers.
	+ Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Promoting innovation and continuous improvement**: A required field to evaluate the executive's drive for innovation.
	+ Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Ethical standards and integrity**: A required field to assess the executive's behavior and ethics.
	+ Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Key achievements during review period**: A required field for notable achievements during the review period.
	+ This could be a list of specific accomplishments or successes.
* **Areas requiring improvement**: A required field for areas the executive needs to improve.
	+ Provide specific areas that require attention.
* **Recommended professional development areas**: An optional field for professional development advice.
	+ Suggest areas for the executive to focus on for improvement.
* **Overall performance rating**: A required field to rate the executive's overall performance.
	+ Rate as Needs improvement, Satisfactory, Exceeds expectations, or Far exceeds expectations.
* **Additional feedback or comments**: An optional field for any further comments or suggestions.
	+ Provide any additional insights or advice for the executive.
