# Integrative Program Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you gauge your understanding of a particular topic or program by creating an integrative program quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the program or topic you are taking the quiz on.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Enter your answer to each question in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Quiz Question** (`Program Quiz Question`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the text of the question you want to ask in the quiz.
* **Question Type** (`Question Type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of question you want to ask (e.g. multiple choice, short answer, essay question).
* **Question** (`Question`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the text of the question you want to ask in the quiz.
* **Answer** (`Answer`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the text of the answer to the question.
* **Answer Text** (`Answer Text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the text of the answer to the question. 

## Tips
- Make sure to keep the question and answer text clear and concise.
- Use this form to create quizzes that are relevant to your course, workshop, or topic.
- You can use this form to create a variety of question types, such as multiple choice, short answer, or essay questions.
- If you need help with formatting or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
