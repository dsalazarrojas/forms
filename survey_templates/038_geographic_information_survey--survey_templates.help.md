# Geographic Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Geographic Information Survey" form is designed to collect data on users' geographic locations, demographic characteristics, and personal preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, choosing the most suitable option from the provided list.
2. For each question, select all options that apply to your personal characteristics or preferences.
3. Make sure to review each field and its corresponding options carefully before submitting the form.
