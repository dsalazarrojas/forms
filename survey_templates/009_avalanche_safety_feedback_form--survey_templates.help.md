# Avalanche Safety Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Avalanche Safety Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users who have experienced an avalanche in the backcountry. This feedback is used to improve the accuracy and clarity of our safety information, ultimately reducing the risk of accidents and fatalities in the backcountry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have recently visited a ski resort or backcountry area.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately according to your experience.
3. Select the options that best match your experience.
4. If there's anything else you'd like to share or suggest for improvement, please use the "Additional Safety Concerns" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full Name
* **Full Name**: Your complete name that you wish to be known as.

### Email Address
* **Email Address**: Your email address for follow-up or contact.

### Experience Level
* **Backcountry Experience Level**: Choose the most accurate description of your experience level. This will help us understand your background and provide more relevant feedback.

### Region or Area Visited
* **Region or Area Visited**: Specify the exact mountain range or ski resort you visited.

### Safety Feedback 
* **Safety Feedback**: This section is optional but provides an opportunity for you to share any thoughts or concerns about how we can improve our risk communication.

### Avalanche Forecast Accuracy
* **Avalanche Forecast Accuracy**: Rate how well the forecast matched the conditions you experienced. This helps us improve our forecasting methods.

### Signs of Instability Observed
* **Signs of Instability Observed**: Select all the signs of instability you observed during your experience. This helps us understand potential risks and areas for improvement.

### Safety Gear Carried
* **Safety Gear Carried**: Select all the safety gear you carried during your experience. This helps us understand what tools and information users have access to.

### Safety Communication Quality
* **Safety Communication Quality**: Rate the clarity of the safety information provided. This helps us improve our communication methods.

### Overall Safety Rating
* **Overall Safety Rating**: Rate your overall sense of safety during your experience. This helps us understand the effectiveness of our safety measures.

### Suggested Improvements
* **Suggested Improvements for Risk Communication**: Share any suggestions or ideas on how we can better share safety information.

### Would You Use This Service or Area Again?
* **Would You Use This Service or Area Again?**: Rate your likelihood of returning to this location based on your experience.

### Additional Safety Concerns
* **Additional Safety Concerns**: Please share any other thoughts, concerns, or suggestions related to safety in the backcountry.

### Date of Feedback
* **Date of Feedback**: Your feedback will help us improve the accuracy and clarity of our safety information. To ensure we can track your feedback, please provide the date of your experience.
