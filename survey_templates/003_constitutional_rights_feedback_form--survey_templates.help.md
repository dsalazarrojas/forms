# Constitutional Rights Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Constitutional Rights Feedback Form is designed to collect information about the community's understanding and awareness of constitutional rights. This form aims to gauge the public's knowledge, attitudes, and opinions about constitutional rights and identify areas where education and awareness may be needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the category that best describes you.
* Choose your age group from the provided options.
* Choose your highest level of education completed.
* Rate your understanding of constitutional rights as very knowledgeable, somewhat knowledgeable, neutral, somewhat unknowledgeable, or very unknowledgeable.
* Select all the sources where you learned about constitutional rights.
* Indicate how well you understand specific constitutional rights, such as the First and Fourth Amendments.
* Share any personal experience with constitutional rights issues.
* Rate how well constitutional rights are protected in your community.
* Choose the most important constitutional right to you.
* Express your opinion on whether constitutional education should be improved.
* Suggest any improvements to constitutional education.
* Indicate your interest in participating in community engagement programs on constitutional rights.
* Provide any additional comments or feedback on constitutional rights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Type** (`respondent_type`, select_one, required): Choose a category that best describes you, such as Student, Educator, Legal Professional, or General Public.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, required): Select your age group from the provided options.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Choose your highest level of education completed, such as High School or Less, Some College, Associate Degree, or Graduate Degree.
* **Awareness of Constitutional Rights** (`awareness_level`, select_one, required): Rate your understanding of constitutional rights as very knowledgeable, somewhat knowledgeable, neutral, somewhat unknowledgeable, or very unknowledgeable.
* **How Did You Learn About Constitutional Rights** (`rights_knowledge_source`, select_multiple, required): Select all the sources where you learned about constitutional rights, such as School Education, College or University Courses, Self-Study or Reading, or Never Learned About Them.
* **First Amendment Understanding** (`first_amendment_understanding`, select_one, required): Indicate how well you understand First Amendment rights.
* **Fourth Amendment Understanding** (`fourth_amendment_understanding`, select_one, required): Indicate how well you understand Fourth Amendment rights.
* **Have You Experienced Rights Violation** (`rights_violation_experience`, select_one, required): Share any personal experience with constitutional rights issues.
* **Rights Protection** (`rights_protection_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how well constitutional rights are protected in your community.
* **Most Important Right** (`most_important_right`, select_one, required): Choose the most important constitutional right to you.
* **Should Constitutional Education Be Improved** (`education_improvement_needed`, select_one, required): Express your opinion on whether constitutional education should be improved.
* **Suggested Improvements** (`suggested_improvements`, text, required): Suggest any improvements to constitutional education.
* **Community Engagement Interest** (`community_engagement`, select_one, required): Indicate your interest in participating in community engagement programs on constitutional rights.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_feedback`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback on constitutional rights.
