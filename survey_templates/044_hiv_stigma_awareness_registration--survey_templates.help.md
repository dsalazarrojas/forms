# HIV Stigma Awareness Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about HIV stigma awareness events, their attendance, and the needs and details of the attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the categories that apply to your event:
   - **Event Signups**: Select all the event categories that you have signups for.
   - **Attendee Needs**: Indicate if attendees have specific needs.
   - **Session Planning**: Choose the session topics or categories that apply.
   - **Event Details**: Select all the event categories that you have details on.
   - **Attendee Info**: Indicate if attendees have provided their information.
   - **Contact Details**: Select if attendees have provided contact details.
   - **Event Signups 2** and **Event Signups 3**: These fields are duplicates of Event Signups and are not relevant for most cases.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Signups** (required): Check all the categories that apply to your event, such as "Yes" for each applicable category.
	+ This field is used to determine the types of events that are happening at your organization.
* **Attendee Needs** (optional): Indicate if your attendees have specific needs, such as "Yes" for each category.
	+ This field is used to determine if your attendees have any special requirements.
* **Session Planning** (optional): Choose the session topics or categories that apply to your event.
	+ This field is used to determine the types of sessions that are planned for your event.
* **Event Details** (optional): Select all the categories that you have details on, such as "Yes" for each category.
	+ This field is used to gather information about the event itself.
* **Attendee Info** (optional): Indicate if your attendees have provided their information, such as name, email, etc.
	+ This field is used to determine if attendees have shared their personal details.
* **Contact Details** (optional): Select if attendees have provided their contact details, such as phone number, etc.
	+ This field is used to determine if attendees have shared their contact information.
* **Event Signups 2** and **Event Signups 3** (optional): These fields are duplicates of Event Signups and are not relevant for most cases.

## Tips
- Please make sure to complete all the categories that apply to your event.
- Be as detailed as possible when selecting categories and providing attendee information.
- If you're unsure about any of the categories, please contact your event organizer for guidance.

This form is designed to be user-friendly and straightforward. If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact us for assistance.
