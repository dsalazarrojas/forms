## Purpose
The Access Flow Evaluation Form is designed to assess the accessibility of a physical facility. It collects information about your experience navigating the facility, including your relationship with the facility, mobility requirements, accessibility of entrances, interior navigation, restrooms, and overall accessibility. This data helps us understand how we can improve the facility's accessibility features to make it more inclusive for all users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each section, filling in the corresponding fields with your honest and accurate feedback. Make sure to select the relevant options for each question and provide specific details in the text fields.
