# Consumer Research Study Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consumer Research Study Consent Form is designed to obtain informed consent from potential participants in a consumer research study. This form ensures that participants understand the study's purpose, procedures, and data usage, and that they provide their consent to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the demographic information (fields 1-6)
	* **First Name**: Enter your full first name.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	* **Street Address**: Enter your mailing address.
2. Provide study details (fields 10-15)
	* **Study Title**: Enter the title of the research study.
	* **Principal Investigator**: Enter the name of the lead researcher.
	* **Organization**: Enter the name of the conducting organization.
	* **Study Purpose**: Briefly describe the purpose of the study.
	* **Study Procedures**: Describe what you will be asked to do during the study.
	* **Study Duration**: Describe how long the study will take.
	* **Time Commitment**: Describe the expected time per session.
3. Review and acknowledge potential risks and benefits (fields 17-20)
	* **Potential Risks**: Describe any potential risks or discomforts.
	* **Potential Benefits**: Describe any potential benefits.
	* **Compensation**: Check if you will receive compensation.
	* **Confidentiality**: Describe how your data will be protected.
4. Review and provide consent (fields 23-28)
	* **Voluntary Participation**: Confirm that your participation is voluntary.
	* **Right to Withdraw**: Confirm that you understand you can withdraw at any time.
	* **Audio Recording Consent**: Confirm if you consent to audio recording.
	* **Video Recording Consent**: Confirm if you consent to video recording.
	* **Data Sharing Consent**: Confirm if you consent to share anonymized data.
5. Provide contact information (fields 29-31)
	* **Future Contact Consent**: Confirm if you agree to be contacted for future studies.
	* **Participant Signature**: Sign your full name as the participant.
	* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of signing.
6. Additional signature (field 32)
	* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's signature and date.
