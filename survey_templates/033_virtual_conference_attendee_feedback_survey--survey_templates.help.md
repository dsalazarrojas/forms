# Virtual Conference Attendee Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of virtual conferences to help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions and provide your feedback honestly.
2. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your coordinator for clarification.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Attendance**: Please describe your experience attending the virtual sessions.
* **Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the conference on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Improvement Ideas**: Suggest any ideas for improvement for future conferences.
* **Session Quality**: Rate the quality of the sessions you attended as either Good or Poor.
* **Session Value**: Choose which sessions provided value to you, multiple selections are allowed.
* **Speaker**: Choose which speaker(s) you enjoyed the most.
* **Communication**: Rate our communication during the conference on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Venue**: Describe your experience with the venue.
* **Date & Time**: Select the date and time of the conference.
* **Session Length**: Choose the length of the sessions that worked best for you.
* **Session Scheduling**: Choose whether the session scheduling was well-organized or not.
* **Feedback**: Share any additional feedback you have about the conference.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes about your experience.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for us to contact you if needed.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes about your experience.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer honestly and provide constructive feedback.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
