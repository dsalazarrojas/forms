# Political Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Political Poll form is a survey designed to collect users' opinions on politics and government. It is not a formal scientific study, but rather a tool for gathering general feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Respond to each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the options provided in the text fields to express your opinion.
4. For numerical fields, enter a whole number between 0 and 100.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **political_views** (text, optional): This field is for your overall political views on a scale of 0 to 100.
* **political_party_1** (text, optional): Enter the name of your preferred political party.
* **political_party_2** (text, optional): Enter the name of your preferred political party.
* **political_party_3** (text, optional): Enter the name of your preferred political party.
* **political_candidate_1** (text, optional): Enter the name of your preferred political candidate.
* **political_candidate_2** (text, optional): Enter the name of your preferred political candidate.
* **political_candidate_3** (text, optional): Enter the name of your preferred political candidate.
* **political_interest_rate_1** (number, optional): Enter your level of interest in politics on a scale of 0 to 100.
* **political_interest_rate_2** (number, optional): Enter your level of interest in politics on a scale of 0 to 100.
* **political_interest_rate_3** (number, optional): Enter your level of interest in politics on a scale of 0 to 100.
* **political_scale_1** (number, optional): Enter your political scale on a scale of 0 to 100.
* **political_scale_2** (number, optional): Enter your political scale on a scale of 0 to 100.
* **political_scale_3** (number, optional): Enter your political scale on a scale of 0 to 100.
