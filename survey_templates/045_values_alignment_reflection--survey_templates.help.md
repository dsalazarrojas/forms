# values_alignment_reflection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "values_alignment_reflection" form is designed to help you reflect on your personal values, current goals, self-assessment, reflection, growth goals, obstacles, values alignment, values drivers, and values stretch. This form helps you identify areas for personal growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  **Personal Values**: Enter any personal values that are important to you in this field.
2.  **Current Goals**: Enter any current goals and objectives in this field.
3.  **Self-Assessment**: Enter your current self-assessment in this field.
4.  **Reflection**: Enter your current reflection in this field.
5.  **Growth Goals**: Enter any growth goals you have in this field.
6.  **Obstacles**: Enter any obstacles preventing you from achieving your goals in this field.
7.  **Values Alignment**: Enter your values alignment in this field.
8.  **Values Drivers**: Enter what drives your values in this field.
9.  **Values Stretch**: Enter how far you are currently stretching your values in this numerical field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Values** (personal_values, text, required:false): This field is where you can list your personal values.
* **Current Goals** (current_goals, text, required:false): Enter any current goals and objectives in this field.
* **Self-Assessment** (self_assessment, text, required:true): Please enter your current self-assessment here.
* **Reflection** (reflection, text, required:true): Enter your current reflection here.
* **Growth Goals** (growth_goals, text, required:false): Enter any growth goals you have in this field.
* **Obstacles** (obstacles, text, required:false): Enter any obstacles preventing you from achieving your goals in this field.
* **Values Alignment** (values_alignment, text, required:false): Enter your values alignment in this field.
* **Values Drivers** (values_drivers, text, required:true): Enter what drives your values in this field.
* **Values Stretch** (values_stretch, number, required:true): Enter how far you are currently stretching your values in the numerical field.
