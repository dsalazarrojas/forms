# relationship_accountability_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you gauge the quality of your relationship by asking questions about your communication style, conflict resolution, and accountability. It will provide you with insights on how you can improve your relationship dynamics and strengthen your bond with your partner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each question and choose the answer that best describes your relationship with your partner.
2. For select multiple-choice questions, choose all that apply.
3. Make sure to answer honestly, as this survey aims to provide a realistic representation of your relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page provides an introduction to the survey and its purpose.
* **How_do_you_communicate_with_your_partner**: This question assesses how you typically communicate with your partner. Choose one of the four options:
	+ I always try to listen to my partner's perspective
	+ I usually try to listen to my partner's perspective
	+ I rarely try to listen to my partner's perspective
	+ I never try to listen to my partner's perspective
* **Who_takes_responsibility_in_this_relationship**: This question determines who takes responsibility in your relationship. Choose all that apply:
	+ Both me and my partner
	+ Me
	+ My partner
	+ Neither
* **How_do_you_grow_as_a_couple**: This question explores how you grow together as a couple. Choose all that apply:
	+ Together
	+ I
	+ My partner
	+ Neither
* **What_type_of_feedback_do_you_prefer_from_your_partner**: This question asks about your preference for feedback from your partner. Choose all that apply:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral
* **What_type_of_conflict_do_you_prefer_to_resolve_first**: This question determines the type of conflict you prefer to resolve first. Choose all that apply:
	+ Emotional
	+ Physical
	+ Financial
	+ None
* **What_is_your_relationship_status**: This question assesses your current relationship status. Choose one of the four options:
	+ In a relationship
	+ Dating
	+ Engaged
	+ Married
* **How_do_you_prefer_to_communicate_with_your_partner**: This question asks about your preferred method of communication with your partner. Choose one of the five options:
	+ Text
	+ Call
	+ Video call
	+ Social media
	+ None
* **How_do_you_prefer_to_resolve_conflict**: This question determines your conflict resolution style. Choose all that apply:
	+ Collaborate
	+ Compromise
	+ Avoid
	+ None
* **What_do_you_believe_is_the_main_objective_of_your_relationship**: This question explores your main objective in your relationship. Choose all that apply:
	+ Love and support
	+ Personal growth
	+ Shared goals
* **Do_you_hold_your_partner_accountable**: This question asks about your level of accountability with your partner. Choose all that apply:
	+ Always
	+ Usually
	+ Rarely
	+ Never

## Tips
- Answer honestly to provide an accurate representation of your relationship dynamics.
- Take your time to carefully consider each question before making a choice.
- This survey is not a diagnostic tool, but rather a self-reflection exercise to help you grow as a couple.
