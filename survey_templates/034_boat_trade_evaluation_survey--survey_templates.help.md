# Boat Trade Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a survey designed to assess your experience and satisfaction when trading your boat with our company. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our services and provide better support for future customers. Please answer the questions honestly, and feel free to provide any additional comments or feedback.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions carefully, making sure to provide accurate information about your trade-in experience.
2. Select the correct options from the provided lists for each question.
3. Take your time to provide detailed answers for open-ended questions, such as "Additional Comments".
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the questions.
