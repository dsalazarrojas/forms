# Drafting Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drafting Preferences Survey is a tool to gather information from project stakeholders about their drafting preferences for upcoming projects. This helps to ensure that all stakeholders are on the same page and have a clear understanding of each other's expectations and priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure you are filling it out as a project stakeholder directly involved in the drafting process.
2. Select the project type that best describes your project (Project A, Project B, or Project C).
3. Choose the design style that suits your project (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
4. Select the color scheme and materials that you prefer for your project (Material 1, Material 2, or Material 3).
5. Enter your budget allocation for the project.
6. Set your timeline start and end dates.
7. Fill out the project leader and manager information (name, phone number, and email).
8. Enter the client's information (name, company, phone number, and email).
9. Set your design style priority (Priority 1, Priority 2, or Priority 3).
10. Choose the project location (Location 1, Location 2, or Location 3).
11. Set your budget allocation priority (Priority 1, Priority 2, or Priority 3).
12. Indicate if you have a timeline start date priority (Priority 1, Priority 2, or Priority 3).
13. Choose whether a timeline end date is a priority for your project (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Draft Preferences (Page 1)**: 
	+ `Project Type`: Select the project type that best describes your project. This will help us understand the nature of your project and allocate resources accordingly.
	+ `Design Style`: Choose the design style that suits your project. This will help us create a design that meets your expectations.
* **Project Details (Page 2)**:
	+ `Project Leader Name`: Enter the name of the project leader. This is the person responsible for leading your project.
	+ `Project Leader Phone`: Enter the phone number of the project leader.
	+ `Project Leader Email`: Enter the email of the project leader.
	+ `Project Manager Name`: Enter the name of the project manager. This is the person responsible for managing your project.
	+ `Project Manager Phone`: Enter the phone number of the project manager.
	+ `Project Manager Email`: Enter the email of the project manager.
* **Client Information (Page 3)**:
	+ `Client Name`: Enter the name of the client. This is the person or organization that commissioned your project.
	+ `Client Company`: Enter the company name of the client.
	+ `Client Phone`: Enter the phone number of the client.
	+ `Client Email`: Enter the email of the client.
	+ `Client Address`: Enter the physical address of the client.
* **Priorities (Page 4)**:
	+ `Design Style Priority`: Set the priority of the design style for your project. Choose how important the design style is for your project. (Priority 1, Priority 2, or Priority 3).
	+ `Project Location`: Choose the location where your project is taking place. (Location 1, Location 2, or Location 3).
	+ `Budget Allocation Priority`: Set the priority of budget allocation for your project. Choose how important budget allocation is for your project. (Priority 1, Priority 2, or Priority 3).
	+ `Timeline Start Date Priority`: Indicate if you have a priority for the timeline start date. (Yes or No).
	+ `Timeline End Date Priority`: Choose whether a timeline end date is a priority for your project. (Yes or No).
* **Additional Details (Page 5)**:
	+ `Project Leader Name Priority`: Set the priority of the project leader name. Choose how important the project leader name is for your project. (Priority 1, Priority 2, or Priority 3).

## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask your project manager or leader for guidance.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
