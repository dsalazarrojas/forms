# Film Festival Best Film Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to allow attendees of a film festival to provide their feedback and ratings on the films they have watched during the festival. It gathers information on the user's opinion on the best film of the festival, as well as some basic contact information and other details about themselves.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Choose the best film from the list of options provided.
2.  Enter your feedback and opinions about the selected film.
3.  Fill in your contact information (email address, phone number, and name) so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
4.  Submit your form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Best Film** (`best_film`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the film that you think is the best from the list of provided options.
*   **Audience Feedback** (`audience_feedback`, `note`, **optional**): Enter your feedback and opinions about the selected film.
*   **Film Title** (`film_title`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the title of the film you are providing feedback on.
*   **Year Released** (`film_year`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the year the film was released.
*   **Film Director** (`film_director`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the director of the film you are providing feedback on.
*   **Film Length** (`film_length`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the length of the film you are providing feedback on.
*   **Film Rating** (`film_rating`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Rate the film as **Highly Recommended**, **Good**, **Average**, or **Bad**.
*   **User Email** (`user_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
*   **User Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your phone number so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
*   **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your name so that we can associate it with your email address and phone number.
*   **User Feedback** (`user_feedback`, `note`, **optional**): Enter any additional feedback or comments about the film.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, **optional**): This button submits your feedback form once you have filled it out.
*   **Cancel** (`cancel`, `text`, **optional**): This button allows you to cancel your submission.
