# Medical Career Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to understand the motivations and experiences of individuals working in the medical field. It collects information on factors such as education, career challenges, job satisfaction, job security, and personal growth, to better understand what motivates healthcare professionals and what drives their career decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Choose the options that best match your experiences and feelings.
3. Select all relevant fields for your family background and role model.
4. If you have multiple role models, select all applicable ones.
5. Be specific and detailed in your answers to "text" fields.
6. Select only one option for "select_one" fields.
7. Select multiple options for "select_multiple" fields.
8. Do not leave any fields blank unless explicitly stated as optional.
9. Take as much time as you need, but try to complete the survey in one sitting.
10. Submit your responses for review and analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What motivated you to pursue a career in medicine** (Text): This field is for you to describe what motivated you to become a medical professional.
* **What level of education did you complete or are you currently completing** (Select One): This field asks for your level of education, from none to PhD.
* **What kind of family background do you have?** (Select Multiple): Choose all relevant fields that apply to your family background, such as biological, adoptive, foster, or other.
* **What are some of the biggest challenges you face in your career?** (Text): This field is for you to describe specific challenges you face in your medical career.
* **What role models do you have in your career?** (Select Multiple): Select all professionals who have inspired or motivated you in your career, such as physicians, researchers, or teachers.
* **Why do you pursue a career in medicine?** (Select One): This field is to understand your personal reasons for pursuing a medical career, such as personal interest or financial reasons.
* **What job satisfaction do you have with your career in medicine?** (Select Multiple): Rate your job satisfaction with options like "Very Satisfied" or "Somewhat Dissatisfied".
* **In which field of medicine do you work?** (Select Multiple): Choose all fields you are currently working or have worked in, such as primary care or specialty care.
* **What kind of job security do you feel in your career?** (Select Multiple): Rate your job security with options like "Very Secure" or "Somewhat Insecure".
