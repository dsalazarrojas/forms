# Division Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about each division within the organization, including its structure, responsibilities, size, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the most relevant choice.
3. If you have any additional comments or notes about the division, please use the "division_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **division_structure**: Describe the structure of your division, including the number of teams or departments, and any other relevant details.
* **division_responsibilities**: List the key responsibilities and objectives of your division.
* **division_challenges**: Identify any challenges or obstacles that your division is currently facing.
* **team_size**: Enter the number of team members in your division.
* **division_type**: Select the size of your division from the options provided (Small, Medium, Large, Custom).
* **division_employees**: Choose the appropriate size category for the number of employees in your division.
* **division_location**: Enter the location(s) where your division operates.
* **division_leaders**: Indicate whether you have division leaders (True/False).
* **division_challenge_scale**: Enter a numerical value to estimate the level of challenge or difficulty your division is facing.
* **division_employees_scale**: Enter a numerical value to estimate the number of employees in your division.
* **division_leaders_scale**: Enter a numerical value to estimate the number of leaders in your division.
* **division_comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your division.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately.
* For fields with multiple options, select the most relevant choice.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to reach out.
