# real_time_simulation_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on real-time simulation exercises. It helps us understand their experiences and perceptions during the simulation, which is crucial for improving the exercise's design and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please ensure that you have completed the real-time simulation exercise** before filling out this form.
2. **Fill out this form immediately after the simulation**. The sooner you provide feedback, the more accurate it will be.
3. Read each question carefully to ensure you answer accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Intro** (`form_intro`, note, required: false): A brief introduction to the form and the purpose of this feedback collection.
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the simulation session you participated in. This will help us identify the specific session you are providing feedback on.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: true): Please enter your name as it appears on your participant profile. This will ensure that your feedback is associated with your correct participant ID.
* **Scenario Rating** (`scenario_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the difficulty or challenge level of the simulation scenario on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is easy and 10 is extremely challenging.
* **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the helpfulness and relevance of the feedback provided during the simulation on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is not helpful at all and 10 is extremely helpful.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Share any observations, thoughts, or suggestions about the simulation experience.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know about the simulation.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Automatically populated with the date and time you submitted the form. This field is for record-keeping purposes only.
