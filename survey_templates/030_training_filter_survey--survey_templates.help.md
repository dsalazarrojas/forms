# Training Filter Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users on the training program. Your input will help the training team understand the strengths and weaknesses of the program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the company from the options provided.
2. Choose how long you have been at the company.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the training program.
4. Select the primary purpose of the training program (if applicable).
5. Answer whether you would recommend this training program to a colleague.
6. Describe the most valuable part of the training program (if you have a strong opinion).
7. Rate the quality of the materials used during the training program.
8. Rate the trainer's performance.
9. Rate how likely you are to recommend this training program to others.
10. Provide any additional comments you have about the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the company?**: This field asks for your job title or position within the company. Please select the option that best describes your role.
* **How long have you been at the company?**: This field asks for your tenure in the company. Choose the option that reflects how long you have been working with the company.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the training?**: This field asks for your opinion on the overall effectiveness of the training program. Please rate your satisfaction from "Very Dissatisfied" to "Very Satisfied".
* **What is the primary purpose of the training program?**: If you have a clear idea of the program's purpose, please select one of the available options. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Would you recommend this training program to a colleague?**: This field asks for your opinion on whether you would recommend the training program to a colleague. Please select "True" if you would recommend, and "False" otherwise.
* **What was the most valuable part of the training program?**: This field is for any comments or feedback you have on the most valuable aspect of the training program. Please be specific and provide as much detail as you can.
* **How would you rate the quality of the materials used during the training program?**: This field asks for your opinion on the quality of the materials used during the training program. Please rate the quality using a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the trainer's performance?**: This field asks for your opinion on the trainer's performance. Please rate the performance using a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **How likely are you to recommend this training program to others?**: This field asks for your opinion on how likely you are to recommend the training program to others. Please rate your likelihood using a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Likely" and 5 is "Very Likely".
* **Any additional comments?**: This field is for any additional thoughts or comments you have about the training program. Please feel free to share as much detail as you can.
