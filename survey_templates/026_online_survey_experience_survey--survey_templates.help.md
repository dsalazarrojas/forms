# online_survey_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your online experience, including your visiting frequency, device usage, satisfaction levels, common challenges, and contact information. The collected data will be used to improve online services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Provide your contact information (Email and Phone) if you wish to be contacted.
3. Fill in the Date field if it's applicable to your response.
4. If you have comments or challenges, type them in the "Comments" and "Common Challenges" fields.
5. Select the devices you use to access websites.
6. Choose your level of satisfaction with the current online experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How often do you visit websites** (`intro`, text, optional): Answer this question to indicate how frequently you visit websites.
* **Which devices** (`device`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the devices you use to access websites (e.g., Android, iOS, Windows, Mac, Others).
* **How satisfied are you** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select a level of satisfaction (very satisfied, fairly satisfied, not satisfied, not at all satisfied).
* **Common challenges** (`challenge`, text, optional): Describe any common challenges you face while using online services.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your online experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Fill in the Date field if it's relevant to your response.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Ensure that you fill in all relevant fields to provide a complete and accurate response.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
