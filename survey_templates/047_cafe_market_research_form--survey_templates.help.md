# Cafe Market Research Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather feedback and opinions from cafe-goers in your area on various aspects of their habits and preferences regarding local coffee shops. The data collected will help identify the needs and wants of the target audience and guide businesses in tailoring their services to meet those demands.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the required information. You can type or select the options that best match your preferences and habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Age Group (2/ Required)
* **Age Group**: Select the age group that best describes you (Under 18, 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55 plus).
### 2. Occupation Status (3/ Required)
* **Occupation Status**: Choose the occupation that most closely describes your current status (Student, Employed Full-time, Employed Part-time, Self-employed, Retired, Unemployed).
### 3. How often do you visit a cafe or coffee shop? (5/ Required)
* **Visit Frequency**: Select how often you visit a coffee shop or cafe (Daily, 2-3 times per week, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
### 4. Primary reason for visiting a cafe (6/ Required)
* **Primary Visit Reason**: Choose the main reason you visit a coffee shop or cafe (Coffee Quality, Work/Study Environment, Socializing, Quick Snack, Meeting Place).
### 5. Average Spend per Visit (7/ Required)
* **Average Spend per Visit**: Select the range that best describes your average spend per visit (Under $5, $5 to $10, $10 to $20, Over $20).
### 6. Your favorite coffee-based drink (9/ Required)
* **Favorite Coffee-based Drink**: Type in your favorite coffee-based drink.
### 7. Importance of Dietary Requirements (10/ Required)
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select how important it is to you to have dietary options (Very Important, Somewhat Important, Not Important).
### 8. Do you use cafe loyalty apps or cards? (11/ Required)
* **Loyalty App Usage**: Choose how often you use cafe loyalty apps or cards (Yes - Frequently, Yes - Occasionally, No).
### 9. Preferred Cafe Ambiance (12/ Required)
* **Ambiance Preference**: Select all that apply from the following options (Quiet and Cozy, Vibrant and Energetic, Modern and Minimal, Artistic and Quirky, Outdoor Seating).
### 10. Would you be interested in participating in a paid focus group? (18/ Required)
* **Focus Group Interest**: Select your interest level in participating in a paid focus group (Very Interested, Neutral, Not Interested).
### 11. Contact Email (19/ Not Required)
* **Contact Email**: Type in your email address if you're interested in participating in a paid focus group.
### 12. Additional Insights (17/ Not Required)
* **Additional Insights**: Share any other insights or comments you have about your cafe habits.
### 13. What's missing from the local cafe scene? (15/ Not Required)
* **Gap in Market**: Type in any gaps or shortcomings you've noticed in the local cafe scene.
### 14. How interested would you be in a new specialty coffee shop in this area? (16/ Required)
* **New Cafe Interest**: Select how interested you are in a new specialty coffee shop (Very Interested, Neutral, Not Interested).
