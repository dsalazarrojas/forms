# Return To In Person Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your thoughts and experiences on in-person learning. Your responses will help us understand your perception of in-person learning and inform future development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the answer that best describes your feelings or experiences.
3. You can skip any question that does not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction to In Person Learning**: This is a brief text field where you can provide any background information about your experience with in-person learning.
* **In Person Learning Experience**: This is a text field where you can describe your personal experience with in-person learning.
* **Agreement with In Person Learning**: This question asks about your level of agreement with in-person learning. Please select one of the four options: 
  A great deal, Somewhat, Not at all, or No opinion.
* **In Person Learning Satisfaction**: This question asks about your satisfaction with in-person learning. Please select one of the four options: 
  A great deal, Somewhat, Not at all, or No opinion.
* **Frequency of In Person Learning**: This question asks about how often you participate in in-person learning activities. Please select one of the four options: 
  A great deal, Somewhat, Not at all, or No opinion.
* **In Person Learning Benefits**: This is a text field where you can describe the benefits you have gained from in-person learning.
* **Open-Ended Question**: This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments on in-person learning.
* **Final Thoughts**: This is another open-ended question where you can provide any final comments or suggestions on in-person learning.
* **Survey Instructions**: This is a text field where you can read and acknowledge the survey instructions and guidelines. 

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to provide valuable insights.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any other comments or suggestions, please use the "Open-Ended Question" field.
