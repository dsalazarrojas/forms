# spa_wellness_perception_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Wellness Perception Survey is designed to collect feedback on the user's experience at the spa, focusing on their perception of the relaxation area, staff friendliness, and overall satisfaction with the service quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the visit date you visited the spa.
2.  Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 5.
3.  Choose whether you found the relaxation area suitable for your needs.
4.  Choose the level of relaxation you experienced during your visit (High or Low).
5.  Rate your satisfaction with staff friendliness, choosing from 1 to 5.
6.  Choose the quality of service you experienced (Option 1 to Option 6).
7.  Provide any additional comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date of your visit to the spa.

### 2. **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the spa experience on a scale from 1 to 5.

### 3. **Relaxation Area** (`relaxation_area`, `select_one`, required: false)
Choose whether the relaxation area was suitable for your needs.

### 4. **Relaxation Level** (`relaxation_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Choose the level of relaxation you experienced during your visit (High or Low).

### 5. **Staff Friendliness** (`staff_friendliness`, `select_one`, required: false)
Rate your satisfaction with staff friendliness on a scale from 1 to 5.

### 6. **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Choose the quality of service you experienced (Option 1 to Option 6).

### 7. **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false)
Provide any additional comments about your experience at the spa.
