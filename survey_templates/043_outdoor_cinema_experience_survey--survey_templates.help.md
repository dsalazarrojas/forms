# Outdoor Cinema Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback on the outdoor movie experience and user preferences for cinema events, aiming to improve the overall quality of movie nights and provide a better understanding of audience needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the options that best reflect your experience and preferences.
2.  Answer the questions according to your personal experience and feelings.
3.  For numerical fields (e.g., 1-2-3), choose the option that best matches your answer.
4.  For date fields (e.g., Date and Time), input the date you attended the cinema event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Movie Night** (`movie_night`, `number`, required/true): Answer this question with the number of movie nights you've attended recently.
* **Location Details** (`location_details`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Please indicate if you've attended an outdoor cinema recently.
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, `date`, required/false): Input the date and time you last attended an outdoor cinema event.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose a rating between 1-7 to reflect your overall experience.
* **Review** (`review`, `text`, required/false): Share your thoughts and feedback on the outdoor cinema experience.
* **Will You Recommend** (`recommend`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose if you would recommend the outdoor cinema to others.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/false): Add any additional comments you feel are relevant to the experience.
* **E-mail** (`email`, `email`, required/true): Provide your email address for contact or follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/false): Enter your phone number (not required but can be useful for future contact).
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/true): Enter your name for identification and follow-up.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required/true): Choose your age for demographics.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required/true): Select your gender for demographics.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, required/false): If you feel it's relevant, share your occupation for better understanding.
* **Favorite Cinema** (`favorite_cinema`, `text`, required/false): If you have a favorite cinema, enter its name or location.
* **Favorite Movies** (`favorite_movies`, `text`, required/false): If you have a favorite movie, list its title(s).
* **Favorite Genres** (`favorite_genres`, `text`, required/false): List your favorite movie genres (e.g., action, comedy, horror).
* **How Often Do You Attend Cinema** (`how_ofter`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose how often you visit cinemas in a year.
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, `text`, required/false): If you prefer a specific outdoor cinema location, enter it.
* **Preferred Format** (`preferred_format`, `text`, required/false): Share your preferred format for cinema events (e.g., 2D, 3D, IMAX).
* **Accessibility Issues** (`accessibility_issues`, `note`, required/false): If you have any accessibility issues, describe them in this field.
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, `text`, required/false): If you have a favorite food, enter its name or type.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose your preferred language for cinema events.
* **Preferred Subtitles** (`preferred_subtitles`, `select_one`, required/true): Select if you prefer subtitles for cinema events.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/false): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/true): Provide your email address for follow-up or contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/false): Enter your phone number (not required but can be useful for future contact).

Note: This form is designed to collect feedback on outdoor cinema experiences, and by filling it out, you're helping us improve these events for everyone. Please answer honestly and to the best of your abilities.
