# Financial Planning Service Opinion Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a tool for collecting opinions on the financial planning service provided to customers. It's designed to gather feedback on how customers feel about the service and any suggestions they might have for improving it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply read each question carefully and answer it truthfully. For the "Customer Satisfaction" question, select the level that best describes your experience with the financial planning service. For the "Financial Advisor Suggestion" question, type in any ideas you have for improving the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Financial Planning Service Opinion Poll Form** (`financial_planning_service_opinion_poll_form`, note, required: false): This form is the main section of the survey, where you can provide any general feedback or comments about the financial planning service.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the two options to indicate your level of satisfaction with the financial planning service: Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
* **Financial Advisor Suggestion** (`financial_advisor_suggestion`, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions for improving the financial planning service, type them in this field.
