# Animal Care Innovation Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Care Innovation Study Survey is designed to gather insights from animal care professionals on their awareness, adoption, and utilization of new innovations in the field.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form by answering each question to the best of your ability.
- Please select the most accurate option for each question.
- If you have any additional comments or insights, please provide them in the text fields.
- Take your time to review each question carefully before submitting your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, Optional): Please enter your full name. You can leave this field blank if you prefer to remain anonymous.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, Optional): If you have any questions or would like to follow up on this survey, you can enter your email address here.
* **Organization or Institution** (`organization`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of your organization or institution.
* **Animal Care Innovation Study Survey** (`survey_intro`, `note`, Optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **What is Your Professional Role?** (`professional_role`, `select_one`, Required): Select your professional role:
	+ Veterinarian
	+ Veterinary technician
	+ Animal researcher
	+ Shelter manager
	+ Animal care attendant
	+ Animal behaviorist
	+ Pet business owner
	+ Student
	+ Other
* **Years of Experience in Animal Care** (`years_experience`, `select_one`, Required): Select your years of experience:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 4-7 years
	+ 8-15 years
	+ More than 15 years
* **Primary Work Setting** (`work_setting`, `select_one`, Required): Select your primary work setting:
	+ Private veterinary practice
	+ Veterinary hospital
	+ Animal shelter
	+ Research institution
	+ Zoo or wildlife park
	+ Farm or agricultural setting
	+ Home-based care
	+ Other
* **How Would You Rate Your Awareness of New Innovations in Animal Care?** (`innovation_awareness`, `select_one`, Required): Rate your awareness of new innovations:
	+ Very aware - actively follow trends
	+ Somewhat aware - occasional reading
	+ Neutral
	+ Not very aware
	+ Not aware at all
* **How Quickly Do You Adopt New Technologies?** (`technology_adoption`, `select_one`, Required): Select your adoption rate:
	+ Early adopter - first to try
	+ Early majority - after proof of concept
	+ Late majority - after widespread use
	+ Laggard - resist change
* **Which Innovations Have You Implemented?** (`innovations_used`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select all the innovations you have implemented:
	+ Digital medical records
	+ Telemedicine
	+ Wearable health monitors
	+ Advanced imaging
	+ Laser therapy
	+ Regenerative medicine
	+ Pain management innovations
	+ Enrichment technologies
	+ None yet
* **What Barriers Prevent You from Adopting New Technologies?** (`technology_barriers`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select all barriers that prevent you from adopting new technologies:
	+ Cost
	+ Lack of training
	+ Lack of evidence
	+ Resistance from staff
	+ Technical complexity
	+ Time constraints
	+ No barriers
* **What Innovation Has Had the Greatest Impact on Your Work?** (`most_impactful_innovation`, `text`, Optional): Describe the innovation that has had the greatest impact on your work.
* **How Have Innovations Improved Animal Care in Your Setting?** (`care_improvements`, `text`, Optional): Describe how innovations have improved animal care in your setting.
* **Which Areas Interest You for Future Innovation?** (`future_interest`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select all areas where you are interested in future innovation:
	+ Diagnostic tools
	+ Treatment methods
	+ Pain management
	+ Behavioral health
	+ Nutrition
	+ Preventive care
	+ Remote monitoring
	+ Data analytics
	+ AI applications
* **Would You Participate in Research Studies?** (`research_participation`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your participation level:
	+ Yes - actively participate
	+ Yes - if relevant
	+ Maybe - depends on study
	+ False
* **Are You Interested in Collaborating with Other Professionals?** (`collaboration_interest`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your collaboration level:
	+ Yes - very interested
	+ Yes - somewhat interested
	+ No - prefer to work independently
* **How Do You Typically Learn About New Innovations?** (`knowledge_sharing`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select all sources where you learn about new innovations:
	+ Professional journals
	+ Conferences
	+ Online webinars
	+ Social media
	+ Colleagues
	+ Vendor presentations
	+ Continuing education
* **What Training Would Help You Adopt Innovations?** (`training_needs`, `text`, Optional): Describe any additional training you need to adopt new innovations.
* **How Do You Fund New Technology Adoption?** (`funding_sources`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your funding method:
	+ Organization budget
	+ Grants
	+ Donations
	+ Personal funds
	+ Do not adopt new technology
* **How Do You Measure the Success of New Innovations?** (`outcome_measurement`, `text`, Optional): Describe how you measure the success of new innovations.
* **What Ethical Considerations Are Important in Animal Care Innovation?** (`ethical_considerations`, `text`, Optional): Describe any ethical considerations that are important to you.
* **What Is the Biggest Challenge in Animal Care Today?** (`biggest_challenge`, `text`, Optional): Describe the biggest challenge in animal care that you face.
* **What Innovations Would You Like to See Developed?** (`innovation_suggestions`, `text`, Optional): Suggest new innovations that you would like to see developed.
* **Would You Be Willing to Share Your Experiences in an Interview?** (`willing_share`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your willingness to share your experiences:
	+ Yes - happy to share
	+ Yes - with conditions
	+ False
* **Additional Comments or Insights** (`additional_comments`, `text`, Optional): Provide any additional comments or insights about the survey or your experiences.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- Take your time to complete the survey, and do not hesitate to ask if you need any clarification.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
- Thank you for participating in this survey!
