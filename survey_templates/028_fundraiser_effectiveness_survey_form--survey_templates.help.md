# Fundraiser Effectiveness Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees on the effectiveness of a specific fundraiser event. Please provide your honest and constructive feedback to help us understand your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the corresponding option for each question that best describes your experience.
2. For the "Expectations" field, please enter a short description of your expectations before attending the event.
3. Select all applicable options for the "Future Attendance" and "Quality" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the fundraiser?** (`fundraiser_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
  Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Not satisfied, Not at all satisfied
* **How likely are you to recommend this event to a friend?** (`recommendation`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
  Very likely, Somewhat likely, Not likely, Not at all likely
* **How much did you enjoy the event?** (`enjoyment`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
  Very enjoyable, Somewhat enjoyable, Not enjoyable, Not at all enjoyable
* **What were your expectations before attending this fundraiser?** (`expectations`, `text`, required): Enter a short description of your expectations before attending the event.
* **How likely are you to attend a similar event in the future?** (`future_attendance`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable options: 
  Very likely, Somewhat likely, Not likely, Not at all likely
* **How would you rate the overall quality of this event?** (`quality`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable options: 
  Very good, Somewhat good, Not good, Not at all good
