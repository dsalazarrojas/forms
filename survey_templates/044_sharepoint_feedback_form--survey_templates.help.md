# sharepoint_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The <sharepoint_feedback_form> is a survey template designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with Sharepoint. This form is intended to help us understand how users interact with the platform and what features they find useful or need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "what_is_your_name" field.
2. Select your department from the "what_is_your_department" field.
3. Choose the features you use from the "which_sharepoint_features_do_you_use" field.
4. Provide your feedback on the features you use in the "sharepoint_features_feedback" field.
5. If applicable, enter your contact email in the "contact_email" field.
6. If applicable, enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
7. If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them in the "what_do_you_suggest_improvement" field.
8. Select a rating for your overall experience in the "rating" field.
9. Choose how you heard about Sharepoint in the "how_did_you_hear_about_sharepoint" field.
10. If you want to provide additional information, use the "additional_comments" field.
11. If you have any additional information, use the "additional_information" field.
12. Select the date you completed this form in the "date" field.
13. Select the time you completed this form in the "time" field.
14. If you have attended Sharepoint training, select the option that applies to you in the "have_you_attended_sharepoint_training" field.
15. If you have read Sharepoint blogs, select the option that applies to you in the "have_you_read_sharepoint_blogs" field.
16. Finally, select the submit option that best applies to your form completion in the "submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_is_your_name** (text, **required**): Enter your name to help us address you personally.
* **what_is_your_department** (text, **required**): Select your department to help us understand your role.
* **which_sharepoint_features_do_you_use** (select_multiple, **required**): Choose the Sharepoint features you use to help us understand your interaction with the platform.
* **sharepoint_features_feedback** (text, **required**): Provide your feedback on the features you use to help us improve Sharepoint.
* **contact_email** (email, **required**): Enter your contact email if you want to be contacted.
* **contact_phone** (text, **optional**): Enter your contact phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **what_do_you_suggest_improvement** (text, **required**): Suggest any improvements you would like to see in Sharepoint.
* **rating** (number, **optional**): Select a rating for your overall experience with Sharepoint.
* **how_did_you_hear_about_sharepoint** (select_one, **optional**): Choose how you heard about Sharepoint to help us track your discovery process.
* **other_sources** (text, **optional**): If you found Sharepoint through other means, enter the source here.
* **additional_comments** (note, **optional**): Use this field to provide additional comments.
* **additional_information** (note, **optional**): Use this field to provide any additional information.
* **date** (date, **optional**): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **time** (time, **optional**): Enter the time you completed this form.
* **have_you_attended_sharepoint_training** (select_one, **optional**): Select whether you have attended Sharepoint training.
* **have_you_read_sharepoint_blogs** (select_one, **optional**): Select whether you have read Sharepoint blogs.
* **submit** (select_one, **optional**): Select the submit option that best applies to your form completion.
