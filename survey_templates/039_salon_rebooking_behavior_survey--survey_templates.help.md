# salon_rebooking_behavior_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your rebooking behavior as a client of a salon. We want to understand how frequently you rebook your appointments, what types of services you usually rebook, and your satisfaction with your last appointment. This information will help us improve our services and tailor our approach to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the information you need.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the options that best match your experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **What is your rebooking frequency?** (`rebooking_frequency`, `select_one`, required)
Please select the frequency with which you rebook your appointments.
- **What type of service do you usually rebook?** (`service_type`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the types of services you usually rebook.
- **How often do you rebook your appointments?** (`rebooking_window`, `select_one`, optional)
Please select the frequency with which you rebook your appointments.
- **How would you rate your satisfaction with your last appointment?** (`appointment_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional)
 Rate your satisfaction with your last appointment.
- **Rebooking Habits** (`rebooking_habits`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select your habits regarding rebooking.
- **Why do you rebook your appointments?** (`rebooking_reason`, `note`, optional)
Provide a note about your reasons for rebooking.
- **What is your preferred contact method for rebooking?** (`contact_info`, `select_one`, optional)
Select your preferred method of contact for rebooking.
- **How long before your next appointment will be rebooked?** (`appointment_rebooking_window`, `select_one`, optional)
Please select the frequency with which your next appointment will be rebooked.
- **What unit do you use to measure your rebooking window?** (`rebooking_window_unit`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the unit you use to measure your rebooking window.
- **Do you have any additional comments about your rebooking behavior?** (`note`, `note`, optional)
Provide any additional comments about your rebooking behavior.
- **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
Please enter your phone number.
- **Is there anything else you'd like to comment about your rebooking behavior?** (`note_2`, `note`, optional)
Provide any additional comments about your rebooking behavior.
