# Tournament Venue Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your feedback about your experience at the tournament venue. Your responses will help us evaluate the venue's performance and make improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question as accurately as possible. For multiple-choice questions, choose the option that best describes your experience.
2. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, even if you are not satisfied with some aspects of the venue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parking** (`parking`, `select_one`, required: false)
    Please rate the quality of parking at the venue.
* **Staff** (`staff`, `text`, required: false)
    Describe your experience with the staff at the venue. For example, were they friendly and helpful?
* **Amenities** (`amenities`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
    Select all the amenities that you found useful during your visit. You can choose more than one option.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false)
    Rate your overall satisfaction with the venue. Please choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false)
    If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here. This is an opportunity to share any thoughts or ideas you have about the venue that are not covered in the previous questions.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses.
- If you are unsure about any question, please choose the option that best describes your experience.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Comments" field to provide more information.
