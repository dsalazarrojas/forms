# Student Behavior Observation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about a student's behavior during an observation session. It is likely used by educators or observers to document and record the student's behavior, providing a snapshot of their actions and decisions made during a specific time period. The purpose of this form is to collect data that can be used to inform instructional strategies or interventions for the student.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Behavior Observation Form**: This is the main page where you will document the student's behavior.
2. Fill in the **Student Name** field with the student's name.
3. Select the appropriate **Student Grade** level from the available options (e.g., Pre-K, Elementary, High School, etc.).
4. Enter the **Date of Observation** to specify the date of the observation session.
5. Choose the **Time of Observation** to specify the time of the observation session.
6. Identify the **Student Class** where the observation took place.
7. Document the **Observation Location** where the observation occurred.
8. Provide a brief description of the **Student Behavior Observation**.
9. Enter the **Educator Name** who conducted the observation.
10. If necessary, provide **Feedback to Student**, suggesting areas of improvement or encouragement.
11. Outline an **Action Plan** to address any observed behaviors.
12. Share **Additional Comments** about the observation.
13. Record any **Observation Notes** about the student's behavior.
14. Select the **Behavior Observation 1** to indicate whether the student was On-Task, Off-Task, or Other.
15. Select the **Behavior Observation 2** to indicate whether the student was Respectful, Not Respectful, or Other.
16. Select the **Behavior Observation 3** to indicate whether the student was On-Task, Off-Task, or Other.
17. Select the **Behavior Observation 4** to indicate whether the student was On-Task, Off-Task, or Other.
