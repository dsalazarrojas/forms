# Residential Soundproofing Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with residential soundproofing systems. The data collected will help the company understand customer satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question.
2. For each question, select the rating that best describes your experience.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
4. Submit the form to share your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Installation Date**: This is the date the soundproofing system was installed.
* **Soundproofing System**: Rate the quality of the soundproofing system. Select the rating that best describes your experience.
	+ Excellent: The soundproofing system was highly effective and met all of your expectations.
	+ Good: The soundproofing system was satisfactory, but had some minor issues.
	+ Fair: The soundproofing system had some noticeable problems, but was still functional.
	+ Poor: The soundproofing system was not effective and had significant issues.
	+ Very Poor: The soundproofing system was completely unacceptable.
* **Satisfaction Level**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the system.
* **Quality of Installation**: Rate the quality of the installation process.
* **Customer Support**: Rate the quality of customer support you received.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
