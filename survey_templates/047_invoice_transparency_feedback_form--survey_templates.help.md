# Invoice Transparency Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Transparency Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers on the transparency of invoices. This form helps us understand how well our customers understand their invoices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the page you wish to complete (Page 1 is the default page).
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
3. Provide additional comments if necessary.
4. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1** (`page_1`, note, required/optional): This is the introductory page to the form. Please review the form instructions and ensure you understand the purpose and scope of the form.
* **Invoice Number** (`invoice_number`, number, required/optional): Please enter the number of the invoice you are providing feedback on.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you received the invoice.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount of the invoice.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the method of payment you used to pay the invoice from the options provided.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the payment frequencies you have used to pay the invoice from the options provided.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of feedback you wish to provide from the options provided.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for further information.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the invoice.
