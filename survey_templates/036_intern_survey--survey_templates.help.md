# Intern Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intern Survey is a form used by employers to collect feedback from interns about their internship program. This survey aims to gather valuable insights to improve the internship experience and better support future interns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answers to the rating questions (if any) in the "Rating" page.
2. Provide your detailed feedback and comments about your internship experience in the "Comments" page.
3. Enter your name in the "Intern Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Intern Survey Form
* **Intern Survey** (`intern_survey_form`, text, optional): This is the main form section where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your internship experience.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): If applicable, please rate your overall experience on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide detailed feedback and comments about your internship experience.
* **Intern Name** (`intern_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to help us identify you in our records.

## Tips
* Please be honest and open in your feedback, as it will help us improve the internship program.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please include them in the comments section.
* If you have any concerns or complaints, please provide them in the comments section as well.
