# weekly_video_reflection_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for weekly progress tracking and feedback collection in an educational or training setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "video URL" field with the URL of the video you watched or attended this week.
2. In the "takeaways" field, describe the important points you took away from the video or activity.
3. In the "question" field, ask yourself what you would like to know more about or what you are trying to accomplish next.
4. In the "action steps" field, outline the steps you plan to take based on what you learned.
5. Enter your progress, if applicable, in the "progress" field.
6. In the "email" field, enter your email address so that we can contact you with any updates or feedback.
7. Select a "category" for your survey from the dropdown list.
8. Finally, click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **weekly_video_reflection_survey_intro** (`intro`, text, required/optional): A text field where you can enter a brief introduction to your survey.
* **enter_video_url** (`video_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the video you watched or attended this week.
* **weekly_takeaways** (`takeaways`, text, required): Describe the important points you took away from the video or activity.
* **weekly_question** (`question`, text, required): Ask yourself what you would like to know more about or what you are trying to accomplish next.
* **weekly_action_steps** (`action_steps`, text, required): Outline the steps you plan to take based on what you learned.
* **weekly_engagement** (`engagement`, text, optional): Describe your level of engagement with the video or activity.
* **weekly_progress** (`progress`, number, required): Enter your progress, if applicable.
* **weekly_feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback on your learning experience.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with any updates or feedback.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select a category for your survey from the dropdown list.
* **form_id** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a form ID, if applicable.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool you used for this activity.
* **output_file** (`output_file`, text, optional): Enter the output file, if applicable.
* **description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a description of your activity, if applicable.
