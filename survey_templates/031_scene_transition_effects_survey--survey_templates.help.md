# Scene Transition Effects Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on the effectiveness of scene transition effects in projects. Please fill out this survey to help us better understand how you perceive these effects and how we can improve them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Select the project type from the list of options.
3. Indicate your experience with scene transitions by selecting a value for "transition usage" on a scale of 1-10.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the current scene transition effects.
5. If you have any areas of improvement, type them in the "improvement needs" field.
6. Add any additional comments you have about the scene transition effects.
7. Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions).
8. Enter your phone number (optional, but useful for follow-up conversations).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **project_type** (`project_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of projects you've used scene transitions in (e.g., film, game, animation).
* **transition_usage** (`transition_usage`, number, optional): Rate your experience with scene transitions on a scale of 1-10.
* **satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the current scene transition effects.
* **improvement_needs** (`improvement_needs`, text, optional): Describe any areas you think need improvement.
* **comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments you have about the scene transition effects.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
