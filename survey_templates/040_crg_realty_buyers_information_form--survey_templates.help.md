# CRG Realty Buyers Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide the CRG Realty agency with information about your homebuying preferences and needs. This will help us to understand your requirements and provide you with suitable properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your name and contact information in the relevant fields.
3. Choose your preferred property type and location from the options provided.
4. Select your preferred contact method and provide any additional information that may be relevant to your search.
5. Read and agree to the consent terms at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Full Name** (\*): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Phone Number** (\*): Enter your preferred phone number for the agency to contact you.
* **Email Address** (\*): Enter your email address for the agency to send property information.
* **Co-Buyer Name** (Optional): If applicable, enter the name of your co-buyer.
* **Co-Buyer Phone Number** (Optional): If applicable, enter your co-buyer's phone number.
* **Current Street Address**: Enter your current street address.
* **City, State, ZIP Code**: Enter your current city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Desired Location or Area**: Enter the area or location where you are interested in buying a property.
* **Price Range**: Select your preferred price range for the home purchase.
* **Type of Property Interested In** (Multiple): Select all property types that interest you.
* **Number of Bedrooms**: Enter the number of bedrooms you prefer.
* **Number of Bathrooms**: Enter the number of bathrooms you prefer.
* **Desired Square Footage**: Enter the approximate size of the property you are interested in.
* **Must-Have Features**: Enter any must-have features you are looking for in a property.
* **When Are You Planning to Purchase**: Select your expected timeline for purchasing a property.
* **Pre-Approved for Financing**: Select whether you have pre-approved financing or not.
* **Currently Working with a Realtor**: Select whether you are currently working with a realtor or not.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred method of contact.
* **Additional Information or Preferences**: Enter any additional information that may be relevant to your search.
* **I Consent to Be Contacted About Properties**: Select whether you consent to be contacted about properties.
