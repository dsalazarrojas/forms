# survey_on_discovery_learning - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about a learning experience. It will ask questions about the category, subcategory, description of the experience, and ratings for both the teacher and student.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can briefly describe the purpose of the form and any important context related to the learning experience.
2. **Category**: Select the category that best describes the learning experience from the list of options.
3. **Subcategory**: Select the subcategory that best describes the learning experience from the list of options.
4. **Description**: Briefly describe the learning experience in this text field. 
5. **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher who facilitated the learning experience.
6. **Teacher Email**: Enter the email address of the teacher who facilitated the learning experience.
7. **Teacher Phone**: Enter the phone number of the teacher who facilitated the learning experience.
8. **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student who participated in the learning experience.
9. **Student Email**: Enter the email address of the student who participated in the learning experience.
10. **Student Phone**: Enter the phone number of the student who participated in the learning experience.
11. **Teacher Rating**: Select the rating of the teacher from the list of options, indicating how good or fair their teaching was.
12. **Student Rating**: Select the rating of the student for the learning experience from the list of options, indicating how good or fair it was.
