# Bidder Registration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bidder Registration Survey is a form designed to gather information about bidders who are interested in participating in auctions. The form will help us better understand your preferences, experiences, and expectations, which will help us tailor our services to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the required fields and select from the provided options. Each section is designed to help us understand different aspects of your bidding experience and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`bidder_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates and notifications about the auction.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number where we can reach you for any auction-related matters.
* **Auction or Event Name** (`auction_name`, `text`, required): Indicate which auction or event you are interested in participating in.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, optional): If you know the date of the auction, please provide it. This will help us tailor our notifications to you.
* **Bidder Status** (`bidder_status`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you are a first-time bidder, returning bidder, frequent bidder, or professional bidder.
* **Item Categories of Interest** (`item_categories`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all categories of items you are interested in bidding on.
* **Expected Bidding Budget** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your expected bidding budget range.
* **Specific Items You Are Interested In** (`specific_items`, `text`, optional): If you have specific items in mind, please provide them here.
* **Auction Frequency** (`auction_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how often you attend auctions.
* **Preferred Bidding Method** (`bidding_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred bidding method (in-person, online platform, phone, proxy or absentee bid, or no preference).
* **Past Auction Experience** (`past_experience`, `select_one`, required): Rate your past auction experience (very experienced, experienced, somewhat experienced, limited experience, or no prior experience).
* **Approximate Number of Successful Purchases** (`successful_purchases`, `number`, optional): If you have purchased items at auctions before, please approximate the number of successful purchases.
* **Preferred Event Timing** (`timing_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your preferred event timing (morning, afternoon, evening, weekend, weekday, or no preference).
* **Preferred Event Frequency** (`event_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your preferred event frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, as needed, or no preference).
* **Interest in Additional Services** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional services that interest you (appraisal services, shipping and handling, buyer's advisory, pre-auction estimates, lot previews, insurance, or none).
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`communication_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred communication method (email, phone, text message, or no preference).
* **Interested in Auction Newsletter** (`newsletter_interest`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you are interested in receiving our auction newsletter.
* **Quality of Past Auction Items** (`auction_quality`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the quality of past auction items you have purchased.
* **Overall Bidding Experience** (`bidding_experience`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your overall bidding experience (excellent, good, average, fair, or poor).
* **Suggested Improvements** (`improvements`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them here.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any other comments or feedback, please provide them here.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be completed. If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us.
