# Campus Climate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campus Climate Survey is a tool to gauge the perceptions and experiences of students, faculty, and staff on a campus community, providing insights into the climate and quality of campus life.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully to understand the questions and prompts.
2.  Answer each question honestly and accurately, based on your personal experiences and feelings.
3.  Select the most suitable options for each question, considering your perspective as a respondent.
4.  Provide clear and concise answers in the text fields, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Role on Campus** (`respondent_role`, `select_one`, required): This question determines your role on campus. Select the option that accurately represents your position, such as Student, Faculty, Staff, or Other.
*   **How Long Have You Been Part of This Campus Community** (`respondent_years`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your length of time on the campus. Choose the option that best describes your time on campus, such as Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 3-4 years, or 5+ years.
*   **Response Should Be Anonymous** (`respondent_anonymous`, `select_one`, optional): You can choose to remain anonymous or include your name with your response. If you choose to be anonymous, your responses will be treated as such.
*   **I Feel Valued and Respected on this Campus** (`feel_valued`, `select_one`, required): This question gauges your feelings about being valued and respected on campus. Select Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree, based on your actual feelings.
*   **I Feel a Strong Sense of Community** (`sense_of_community`, `select_one`, required): This question explores your sense of community on campus. Choose Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree, depending on your feelings.
*   **I Feel Supported in this Environment** (`supported_environment`, `select_one`, required): This question assesses your sense of support on campus. Select Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree, based on your experience.
*   **This Campus Feels Safe** (`safe_campus`, `select_one`, required): This question gauges your perceptions of campus safety. Select Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree, based on your actual feelings.
*   **This Campus is Inclusive and Welcoming** (`inclusive_environment`, `select_one`, required): This question determines your perception of the campus as an inclusive and welcoming environment. Select Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree, based on your feelings.
*   **I Have Experienced Discrimination on Campus** (`discrimination_experienced`, `select_one`, optional): If you have experienced discrimination, choose 'True', 'False', or 'Prefer not to say'. If you choose 'True', please provide details in the next field.
*   **Please Describe the Discrimination Experienced** (`discrimination_details`, `text`, optional): If you have experienced discrimination, provide a clear and concise description of the incident in this text field.
*   **Campus Facilities are Well-Maintained** (`facilities_quality`, `select_one`, required): This question evaluates the quality of campus facilities. Select Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree, based on your observations.
*   **Campus Communication is Clear and Effective** (`communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): This question assesses the effectiveness of campus communication. Choose Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree, based on your feelings.
*   **What are Your Main Concerns About Campus Climate?** (`main_concerns`, `text`, optional): Share any main concerns you have about the campus climate in this text field.
*   **What Changes Would Improve Campus Climate?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): If you have suggestions for improving campus climate, provide them in this text field.
*   **This Campus Adequately Represents Diverse Perspectives** (`diversity_adequate`, `select_one`, required): This question gauges the campus's representation of diverse perspectives. Select Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree, based on your perceptions.
*   **Do You Identify as an Underrepresented Group** (`underrepresented_groups`, `select_one`, optional): Check if you identify as an underrepresented group. Select 'True' if you do.
*   **What Support Is Needed for Underrepresented Groups?** (`support_needed`, `text`, optional): If you identify as an underrepresented group, provide the support needed in this text field.
*   **Overall Campus Climate Rating** (`overall_climate_rating`, `select_one`, required): Finally, rate the overall campus climate, selecting Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor, based on your feelings.
