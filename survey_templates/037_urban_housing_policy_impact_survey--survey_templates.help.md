# Urban Housing Policy Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect your opinions and feedback on the impact of our urban housing policies. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and develop more effective policies that suit the needs of our community.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer the questions according to your genuine experiences and opinions.
2.  For select multiple options, choose all the options that apply to you.
3.  For select one options, choose only one option that best represents your opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): A brief overview of the survey and its purpose.
*   **Resident Demographics** (`resident_demographics`, `number`, optional): A field to collect demographic information about yourself. Please fill in your age range and number of years living in the area.
*   **Housing Status** (`housing_status`, `select_one`, optional): A field to collect information about your current housing status. Please choose one option from the list:
    *   Option 1: Owner-occupied
    *   Option 2: Renting
    *   Option 3: Shared accommodation
    *   Option 4: Other (please specify)
    *   Option 5: No fixed abode
*   **Housing Needs** (`housing_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): A field to collect information about your current housing needs. Please choose all the options that apply to you:
    *   Option 1: Affordable housing
    *   Option 2: Housing for families
    *   Option 3: Housing for students
    *   Option 4: Housing for seniors
*   **Policy Impact** (`policy_impact`, `number`, optional): A field to collect your rating of our current urban housing policies. Please rate them from 1 to 10, where 1 is "very ineffective" and 10 is "very effective".
*   **Policy Effectiveness** (`policy_effectiveness`, `select_one`, optional): A field to collect information about how effective you think our current urban housing policies are. Please choose one option from the list:
    *   Option 1: Very effective
    *   Option 2: Somewhat effective
    *   Option 3: Not very effective
*   **Policy Awareness** (`policy_awareness`, `select_multiple`, optional): A field to collect information about how aware you are of our current urban housing policies. Please choose all the options that apply to you:
    *   Option 1: Very aware
    *   Option 2: Somewhat aware
    *   Option 3: Not very aware
*   **Policy Participation** (`policy_participation`, `select_multiple`, optional): A field to collect information about your participation in our urban housing policies. Please choose all the options that apply to you:
    *   Option 1: Fully participated
    *   Option 2: Partially participated
    *   Option 3: Not participated
*   **Policy Suggestions** (`policy_suggestions`, `text`, optional): A field for you to suggest any ideas or changes you would like to see in our urban housing policies.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): A field for you to add any additional comments or suggestions.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): A field for you to provide your contact information in case we need to contact you for further discussion.

This guide will help you complete the form in an informed and clear manner. If you have any further questions or clarifications, please do not hesitate to contact us.
