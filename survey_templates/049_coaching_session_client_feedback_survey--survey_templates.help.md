# Coaching Session Client Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients after a coaching session, aiming to understand their satisfaction, experience, and areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply go to each section and follow the instructions for each question:
1. Enter your **Client Name**. If you prefer not to provide your name, it's optional.
2. Enter your **Email Address**, which will allow the coach to contact you for further discussion or follow-up sessions.
3. Enter the **Session Date** when you had your coaching session.
4. Choose the **Session Number**, indicating how many coaching sessions you've had with this coach.
5. Rate your **Overall Session Satisfaction**, using the provided scale (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
6. Choose how **Helpful Was This Session** for you, using the provided scale (Extremely Helpful, Very Helpful, Somewhat Helpful, Not Very Helpful, Not Helpful).
7. Evaluate the coach's **Listening Skills** on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
8. Assess the coach's **Understanding of Your Issues** on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
9. Rate the quality of the coach's **Questions** on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
10. Evaluate the quality of the coach's **Feedback** on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
11. Choose the **Session Pace**, if it was too fast, just right, or too slow.
12. Select how **Focused** you felt during the session, using the scale (Very Focused, Mostly Focused, Somewhat Focused, Not Focused).
13. Choose your **Comfort Level** during the session, using the scale (Very Comfortable, Comfortable, Neutral, Uncomfortable, Very Uncomfortable).
14. In the **Insights or Breakthroughs Gained** section, describe any significant learnings or discoveries you gained from the session.
15. In the **Actions or Next Steps Identified** section, write down any actions or decisions you plan to take next.
16. Choose your **Progress Toward Your Goals**, using the scale (Significant Progress, Moderate Progress, Some Progress, Little Progress, No Progress).
17. Rate your **Confidence Level After Session**, using the scale (Very Confident, Confident, Neutral, Less Confident, Not Confident).
18. Choose your **Motivation Level After Session**, using the scale (Very Motivated, Motivated, Neutral, Less Motivated, Not Motivated).
19. In the **What Worked Well** section, describe anything the coach did particularly well.
20. In the **What Could Be Improved** section, suggest any areas where the coach could improve.
21. In the **Topics for Next Session** section, mention any topics you think should be covered in future sessions.
22. In the **Would You Recommend This Coach** section, indicate if you would recommend this coach to others.
23. Choose if you would **Continue with This Coach**, indicating if you plan to have more sessions.
24. In the **Additional Feedback** section, share any further thoughts or comments you'd like to make.
25. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Name (1)
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, Text, Optional): This is your full name. It's optional, but it helps the coach know who's providing feedback.
* 
### Email Address (2)
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your contact email. It allows the coach to get in touch with you for further discussion or follow-up sessions.
* 
### Session Date (4)
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, Date, Required): Record the date you had your coaching session.
* 
### Session Number (5)
* **Session Number** (`session_number`, Select One, Optional): Select which session this feedback is for.
* Options: Session 1, Session 2, Session 3, Session 4, Session 5, Session 6 or More
* 
### Overall Session Satisfaction (6)
* **Overall Session Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, Select One, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* Options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied
* 
### How Helpful Was This Session (7)
* **How Helpful Was This Session** (`session_helpfulness`, Select One, Required): Assess the session's helpfulness on a scale from Extremely Helpful to Not Helpful.
* Options: Extremely Helpful, Very Helpful, Somewhat Helpful, Not Very Helpful, Not Helpful
* 
### Coach Listening Skills (8)
* **Coach Listening Skills** (`coach_listening`, Select One, Required): Evaluate the coach's listening skills on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* Options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor
* 
### Coach Understanding of Your Issues (9)
* **Coach Understanding of Your Issues** (`coach_understanding`, Select One, Required): Assess the coach's understanding of your issues on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* Options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor
* 
### Quality of Coach Questions (10)
* **Quality of Coach Questions** (`coach_questions`, Select One, Required): Rate the coach's questions on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* Options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor
* 
### Quality of Coach Feedback (11)
* **Quality of Coach Feedback** (`coach_feedback`, Select One, Required): Evaluate the coach's feedback on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* Options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor
* 
### Session Pace (12)
* **Session Pace** (`session_pace`, Select One, Optional): Choose how fast or slow the session was, if it was too fast, just right, or too slow.
* Options: Too Fast, Just Right, Too Slow
* 
### Session Focus (13)
* **Session Focus** (`session_focus`, Select One, Required): Rate how focused you felt during the session, using the scale (Very Focused, Mostly Focused, Somewhat Focused, Not Focused).
* Options: Very Focused, Mostly Focused, Somewhat Focused, Not Focused
* 
### Your Comfort Level (14)
* **Your Comfort Level** (`comfort_level`, Select One, Required): Choose your comfort level, from Very Comfortable to Very Uncomfortable.
* Options: Very Comfortable, Comfortable, Neutral, Uncomfortable, Very Uncomfortable
* 
### Insights or Breakthroughs Gained (15)
* **Insights or Breakthroughs Gained** (`insights_gained`, Text, Optional): Describe any significant learnings or discoveries you gained from the session.
* 
### Actions or Next Steps Identified (16)
* **Actions or Next Steps Identified** (`actions_identified`, Text, Optional): Write down any actions or decisions you plan to take next.
* 
### Progress Toward Your Goals (17)

* 
### Confidence Level After Session (18)
* **Confidence Level After Session** (`confidence_after_session`, Select One, Required): Rate your confidence level after the session, using the scale (Very Confident, Confident, Neutral, Less Confident, Not Confident).
* Options: Very Confident, Confident, Neutral, Less Confident, Not Confident
* 
### Motivation Level After Session (19)

### What Worked Well (20)

* 
### What Could Be Improved (21)

* 
### Topics for Next Session (22)
* **Topics for Next Session** (`topics_for_next_session`, Text, Optional): Mention any topics you think should be covered in future sessions.
* 
### Would You Recommend This Coach (23)
* **Would You Recommend This Coach** (`would_recommend_coach`, Select One, Required): Indicate if you would recommend this coach to others.

### Additional Feedback (25)
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, Text, Optional): Share any further thoughts or comments you'd like to make.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere with your feedback, as it will help coaches improve and better serve their clients.
* Use the provided scales and options as a guide when answering questions.
* Provide as much or as little information as you feel comfortable sharing.
