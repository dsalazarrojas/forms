# dream_job_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dream_job_survey form is designed to help you identify your ideal job characteristics. It's a series of questions that will guide you through your preferences and aspirations in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select all the options that apply to you for each question.
3. Be as specific as possible in your answers, but don't worry about grammar or spelling mistakes.
4. Take your time and don't rush through the questions.
