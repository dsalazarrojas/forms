# Meeting Psychological Safety Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the psychological safety of a meeting or team environment. It helps to identify areas where team members feel comfortable speaking up and sharing their thoughts, and where they may feel uncomfortable or silenced. The information gathered from this survey will be used to inform future meeting facilitation strategies and improve the overall psychological safety of the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  For each of the following fields, answer with your honest response. The survey is anonymous, so your answers will not be attributed to you individually.
*   **Always, Often, Rarely, or Never**: Indicate the frequency with which you feel comfortable speaking up in meetings.
*   **Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neutral, Somewhat Disagree, or Strongly Disagree**: Express your level of agreement or disagreement with the statement "I feel more comfortable with better facilitation."
*   **Always, Often, Rarely, or Never**: Select the options that apply to you for the following questions:
    +  How often do you feel team leaders speak up?
    +  How often do team members speak up?
    +  In what way can the culture of the team be improved to foster better facilitation?

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Team Leaders Speak Up**: This field assesses whether team leaders frequently speak up in meetings. Please answer with one of the following options:
    *   **Always**: Team leaders consistently speak up in meetings.
    *   **Often**: Team leaders usually speak up in meetings.
    *   **Rarely**: Team leaders rarely speak up in meetings.
    *   **Never**: Team leaders never speak up in meetings.
*   **Team Members Speak Up**: This field evaluates the frequency with which team members speak up in meetings. Choose all options that apply:
    *   **Always**: Team members consistently speak up in meetings.
    *   **Often**: Team members usually speak up in meetings.
    *   **Rarely**: Team members rarely speak up in meetings.
    *   **Never**: Team members never speak up in meetings.
*   **Culture and Speak Up**: This field is open for additional comments on how the team culture can be improved to foster better facilitation.
*   **Better Facilitation**: Please select your level of agreement with the statement "I feel more comfortable with better facilitation." This will help us understand your perception of the current facilitation style.
*   **Inclusive Collaboration**: This field assesses the level of inclusive collaboration within the team. Select your level of agreement with the statement "I feel more comfortable with inclusive collaboration."
*   **Psychological Safety Rating**: This field is not currently being used in the provided YAML fields, but in a standard psychological safety survey, it would measure the overall psychological safety of the team.

## Tips
*   Answer honestly, and remember that the survey is anonymous.
*   Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
*   This survey is designed to gather your honest opinions and help us improve the meeting environment.
