# Material Property Data Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about materials, including their category, size, weight, color, chemical composition, safety rating, and hazard statement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct option for each question, or type in the required text field.
2. For each field, choose one of the options provided, or select "No" if the field does not apply.
3. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it, as any errors may affect the accuracy of the data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Material Category**: Choose the category that best describes the material. (Select One)
* **Material Size**: Select the size that best describes the material. (Select One)
* **Material Weight**: Select the weight that best describes the material. (Select One)
* **Material Color**: Choose the color that best describes the material. (Select One)
* **Material Chemical Composition**: Provide a brief description of the material's chemical composition. (Text)
* **Material Safety Rating**: Rate the material's safety level as "Good" or "Fair". (Select One)
* **Material Hazard Statement**: Provide any relevant hazard statements or warnings related to the material. (Text)

## Tips
* Make sure to be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about a field, try to select the option that best fits the information you have.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy. 

Please note that the explanations for each field are provided to help clarify their purpose and intended use. However, the specific requirements and definitions for each field may vary depending on your organization's specific needs and procedures.
