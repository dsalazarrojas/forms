# E Book Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with E-book content, helping us understand their perception and improve future developments.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Type in your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Rate your experience with the E-book on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
5. Provide any additional feedback on your experience with the E-book in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name, text, optional)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, optional)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, optional)**: Type in your valid email address to help us contact you if needed.
* **Rating (Rating, number, optional)**: Rate your experience with the E-book using a number from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Feedback (Feedback, text, optional)**: Share any thoughts or suggestions you have about your experience with the E-book.

## Tips

* Please fill out one feedback form per E-book.
* If you have already filled out this form for the same E-book, please do not fill it out again.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to reach out to us!
