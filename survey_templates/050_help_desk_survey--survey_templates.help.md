# Help Desk Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the quality of our help desk services. It consists of several questions that ask about your experience and rating of our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes your experience.
2. Provide a brief description of your experience.
3. Rate the help desk service.
4. Provide a rating for the help desk.
5. Enter any comments or feedback you have about your experience.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (Select One, required): This field allows you to categorize your experience as either "Survey Templates" or "Other".
* **Description** (Text, optional): Please enter a brief description of your experience with our help desk service. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Help Desk Service** (Select Multiple, required): This field allows you to rate the quality of our help desk service as "Good", "Fair", or "Bad".
* **Help Desk Rating** (Number, optional): This field is for you to provide a numerical rating of our help desk service. Please enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Comments** (Note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience with our help desk service.
* **Email** (Email, optional): Please enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (Text, optional): Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form as completely and accurately as possible.
- Be as descriptive as possible in the "Description" field to give us a clear understanding of your experience.
- Select "Other" in the "Category" field if your experience does not fit into "Survey Templates".
- If you have any issues or concerns, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
