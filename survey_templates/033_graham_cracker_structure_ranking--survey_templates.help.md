# graham_cracker_structure_ranking - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the structure of a Graham cracker, a type of cracker that is typically rectangular in shape and has a distinctive cracker shape.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please provide a rating for the structure of the Graham cracker.
2. Provide feedback on the structure of the cracker.
3. Select photos from the options that show the cracker organization (only applicable if "Photo Upload" is used).
4. Upload photos of the cracker.
5. Select the organizer(s) of the cracker (only one or multiple).
6. Select the "Submit" option to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graham Cracker Structure Rating** (`graham_cracker_rating`, number, required): Please rate the structure of the cracker from 1 to 5.
* **Challenge Rating** (`graham_cracker_challenge_rating`, number, optional): Provide an optional rating for the cracker challenge, if applicable.
* **Photo Upload** (`photo_upload`, text, optional): Upload photos of the cracker.
* **Score 1** (`graham_cracker_rating_1`, number, optional): Provide a score for the first challenge.
* **Score 2** (`graham_cracker_rating_2`, number, optional): Provide a score for the second challenge.
* **Score 3** (`graham_cracker_rating_3`, number, optional): Provide a score for the third challenge.
* **Score 4** (`graham_cracker_rating_4`, number, optional): Provide a score for the fourth challenge.
* **Score 5** (`graham_cracker_rating_5`, number, optional): Provide a score for the fifth challenge.
* **Organizer** (`graham_cracker_organizer`, select_one, required): Select the main organizer of the cracker.
* **Co-Organizer** (`graham_cracker_organizer_1`, select_multiple, required): Select the co-organizers of the cracker.
* **Photo Organization** (`graham_cracker_photo_organize`, select_multiple, optional): Select the photos to organize.
* **Upload Photo** (`graham_cracker_upload_photo`, select_multiple, optional): Select the photo(s) to upload.
* **Submit** (`graham_cracker_submit`, select_one, required): Confirm your submission.
* **Organizer 1** (`graham_cracker_organizer_1`, select_multiple, required): Select the first co-organizer.
* **Co-Organizer** (`graham_cracker_organizer_2`, select_multiple, required): Select the co-organizers.
* **Organize** (`graham_cracker_organizer_3`, select_multiple, required): Select the second organizer.
* **Upload** (`graham_cracker_upload_photo`, select_multiple, required): Select the photo(s) to upload.
* **Upload 1** (`graham_cracker_upload_photo_1`, text, optional): Upload photo 1.
* **Upload 2** (`graham_cracker_upload_photo_2`, text, optional): Upload photo 2.
* **Upload 3** (`graham_cracker_upload_photo_3`, text, optional): Upload photo 3.
* **Upload 4** (`graham_cracker_upload_photo_4`, text, optional): Upload photo 4.
* **Upload 5** (`graham_cracker_upload_photo_5`, text, optional): Upload photo 5.
