# Dish Search And Filter - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to input information for searching and filtering dishes based on their meal preferences and filter criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Go to the first page and input the title for your search and filter operation. This will help you to search for specific dishes.
2. On the second page, select your meal preferences from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Dish Search And Filter** (`dish_search_and_filter`, `text`, required/optional: false)
This is the title field where you can input the name of the dish you are looking for.

### 2. **Meal Preferences** (`meal_preferences`, `text`, required/optional: false)
In this field, select the meal preferences that apply to the dish you are searching for. You can choose more than one preference if needed.

### 3. **Filter Preferences** (`filter_preferences`, `text`, required/optional: false)
Here, you can select the filter preferences that apply to the dish you are searching for. You can choose more than one preference if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to input specific and accurate information in the title field to get the best results.
* Be specific with your meal and filter preferences for a more precise search result.
