# Preclinical Data Management Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from preclinical data management teams about their current tools, collaboration practices, and improvement priorities. The survey aims to understand the current state of preclinical data management and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the tools you currently use for preclinical data management.
2. Choose the collaboration practices you use with your data team.
3. Identify your priorities for future improvement of your data workflows.
4. Rate your preclinical data management tools (1-5).
5. Rate your satisfaction with your preclinical data management tools (1-5).
6. Rate your satisfaction with your data team collaboration practices (1-5).
7. Provide any improvement ideas you have for your preclinical data management workflows.
8. Add any additional comments or feedback.
9. Enter your email address, phone number, and date and time (if relevant).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select tools (Tool Selection)** (`tool_selection`, select_multiple): Choose the tools you currently use for preclinical data management. Select all applicable options.
* **What collaboration practices do you use with your data team? (Collaboration Practices)** (`collaboration_practices`, select_one): Choose one of the collaboration practices you use.
* **What priorities do you think are important for future improvement of your data workflows? (Improvement Priorities)** (`improvement_priorities`, select_multiple): List up to 3 priorities separated by commas (e.g., Time-to-market, Cost).
* **How do you rate your preclinical data management tools? (Tool Rating)** (`tool_rating`, number): Rate on a scale of 1-5.
* **Are you satisfied with your preclinical data management tools? (Tool Satisfaction)** (`tool_satisfaction`, number): Rate on a scale of 1-5.
* **Are you satisfied with your data team collaboration practices? (Collaboration Satisfaction)** (`collaboration_satisfaction`, number): Rate on a scale of 1-5.
* **Do you have any improvement ideas for your preclinical data management workflows? (Improvement Ideas)** (`improvement_ideas`, text): Provide any improvement ideas you have.
* **Additional feedback** (`feedback`, text): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date.
* **Time** (`time`, time): Enter the time.
* **Date & Time** (`datetime`, date): Enter both date and time.
* **Comments** (`main_section_comments`, note): Add any comments related to this form.
* **How many people collaborate on preclinical data management? (Collaboration Team)** (`collaboration_team`, select_one): Choose the number of people in your collaboration team.
* **Comment on tools used** (`tool_selection_comment`, text): Provide any comments on the tools you have selected.
* **Comment on collaboration practices** (`collaboration_comment`, text): Provide any comments on your collaboration practices.
