# Social Justice Metrics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Social Justice Metrics Survey, which aims to gauge your understanding and engagement in social justice practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and provide answers that reflect your genuine thoughts and experiences.
2. Answer all the questions that apply to you, and provide any additional comments or feedback in the designated fields.
3. Ensure that you answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Intro** (Note, Survey Intro, Not Required): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully to understand the context and purpose of the survey.
* **Awareness Scale** (Number, Awareness Scale, Optional): This question measures your level of awareness related to social justice practices. Please choose a number between 1-5 to indicate your level of understanding.
* **Experiences Index** (Number, Experiences Index, Optional): This question measures your personal experiences related to social justice practices. Please choose a number between 1-5 to indicate your level of engagement.
* **Perceptions Survey** (Select Multiple, Perceptions Survey, Optional): This question asks about your perceptions and beliefs related to social justice practices. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Equity Practices Scale** (Number, Equity Practices Scale, Optional): This question measures your level of engagement in equity practices related to social justice. Please choose a number between 1-5 to indicate your level of engagement.
* **Inclusion Practices Scale** (Number, Inclusion Practices Scale, Optional): This question measures your level of engagement in inclusion practices related to social justice. Please choose a number between 1-5 to indicate your level of engagement.
* **Survey Instructions** (Note, Survey Instructions, Not Required): This is an optional field for providing any additional comments or feedback you may have about the survey.
* **Survey Conclusion** (Note, Survey Conclusion, Not Required): This is an optional field for summarizing your thoughts or conclusions after completing the survey.
