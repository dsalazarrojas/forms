# media_consumption_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about users' media consumption habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the "media_consumption_survey_form" text field and enter your response.
2. Choose one of the options in the "how_do_you_consume_media" select field.
3. Enter the amount of time you spend on media consumption in the "how_much_time_do_you_spend" field.
4. Enter any additional notes about your media consumption in the "notes" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
7. Indicate whether you have an assigned tool for media consumption in the "assigned_tool" select field.
8. Enter a unique form ID in the "form_id" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **media_consumption_survey_form** (`media_consumption_survey_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your media consumption habits.
- **how_do_you_consume_media** (`how_do_you_consume_media`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you consume media, "No" if you don't.
- **how_much_time_do_you_spend** (`media_consumption_time`, time, optional): Enter the amount of time you spend consuming media on a daily basis.
- **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your media consumption habits.
- **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have an assigned tool for media consumption, "No" if you don't.
- **form_id** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter a unique form ID.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You may enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your media consumption habits in the "notes" field.
* The "assigned_tool" field is used to indicate whether you have a specialized tool or service for media consumption.
* The "email" and "phone" fields are used to gather contact information for follow-up purposes only.
