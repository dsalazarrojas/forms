# Vacation Package Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to collect feedback from users about their vacation experiences, allowing the organization to understand areas of improvement and identify what users liked or disliked about a specific vacation package.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "What did you experience" in the first page.
2. Select how satisfied you are with your vacation experience in the second page (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied).
3. Specify areas for improvement in the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you experience** (`introduction`, `text`, required):
	+ This is the first question where you will describe your vacation experience. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand what happened during your trip.

* **How satisfied are you** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Please select how satisfied you are with your vacation experience using the provided options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.

* **What could we improve** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, required):
	+ In this section, specify areas where we could improve our services. Be specific and provide concrete suggestions to help us better serve you in the future.
