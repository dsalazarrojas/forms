### Dreamer Profile Survey - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The Dreamer Profile Survey is a comprehensive form designed to collect information about an individual's sleep habits, frequency of dreaming, and overall sleep quality. It is intended to help users gain deeper insights into their sleep patterns and identify areas of improvement.

#### How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly. 
2. Use the provided options to select your responses.
3. For text fields, provide a clear and concise description of your answer.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief note to explain the purpose and objectives of the form.
* **Dream Frequencies**: Do you often have dreams? (Yes/No)
* **Sleep Habits**: Do you often have trouble falling asleep or staying asleep? (Yes/No)
* **Sleep Quality**: How would you rate your sleep quality? (Good/Poor)
* **Wake Up Time**: What time do you wake up? (Select a time)
* **Wake Up Time Hour**: Do you wake up exactly on time? (Yes/No)
* **Wake Up Time Minute**: Do you often wake up feeling rested or feeling tired? (Yes/No)
* **Sleep Duration**: How long do you sleep in a single session? 
* **Sleep Stages**: Do you often experience various sleep stages? (Yes/No)
* **Sleep Disorders**: Do you often experience sleep disorders? (Yes/No)
* **Dream Frequency**: Do you often have dreams? (Yes/No)
* **Most Common Dream**: Describe the most common dream you experience. 
* **Most Common Dream Frequency**: How often do you experience this dream? 
* **Most Common Dream Location**: Where does this dream usually occur?
* **Most Common Dream Character**: Who is usually in this dream?
* **Most Common Dream Theme**: What is the main theme of this dream?
* **Most Common Dream Emotion**: How do you feel during this dream?
* **Most Common Dream Objectives**: What are you trying to achieve in this dream?
* **Most Common Dream Consequences**: What happens as a result of this dream?
* **Most Common Dream Outcomes**: What do you gain from this dream?
* **Morning Mood**: Do you wake up feeling energized or refreshed? (Yes/No)
* **Energy Level**: How would you describe your energy level after waking up? (High/Low)
* **Sleep Quality Rating**: How would you rate your sleep quality? (1-5)
* **Wake Up Feeling**: Do you wake up feeling rested or feeling tired? (Yes/No)
* **Comments**: Any additional comments about your sleep habits and experiences.

Note: Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your abilities. This form is for your own personal insight and improvement.
