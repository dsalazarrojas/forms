# Irish Culture Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Irish Culture Awareness Survey is a set of questions designed to help us understand your knowledge, attitudes, and behaviors related to Irish culture. This survey will help us gauge your level of awareness, engagement, and interest in various aspects of Irish culture, including history, folklore, music, dance, art, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and select the answer that best reflects your feelings or experiences.
2. You can select only one option for each question.
3. Be honest in your responses, as this survey is designed to gather information to improve our understanding of Irish culture.
