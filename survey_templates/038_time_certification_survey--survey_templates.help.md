# time_certification_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to employee time certification. It is used to verify and track the time spent on projects or tasks by employees, ensuring that certification requirements are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee you are certifying from the list of available options.
2. Choose the time period for which you are certifying the employee's work hours.
3. Enter the certification date, if applicable.
4. Select the certification type from the list of available options.
5. Enter the total number of certification hours completed.
6. Add any additional notes about the certification.
7. Review and sign the form, indicating that you have verified the information provided.
8. Add any comments or feedback about the certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`Employee`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the employee whose time certification you are verifying. If you are not sure which employee, please do not select anyone.
* **Time period** (`Time period`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the time period for which you are certifying the employee's work hours. This can be a specific month or quarter, depending on your organization's requirements.
* **Certification Date** (`Certification Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the certification, if applicable.
* **Certification Type** (`Certification Type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of certification from the list of available options. This ensures that the certification requirements are met.
* **Certification Hours** (`Certification Hours`, number, required): Enter the total number of certification hours completed by the employee.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the certification.
* **Manager Comments** (`Manager Comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the certification.
* **Manager Signature** (`Manager Signature`, text, required): Review and sign the form, indicating that you have verified the information provided.
* **Employee Comments** (`Employee Comments`, text, required/optional): Add any comments or feedback from the employee about the certification.
* **Employee Signature** (`Employee Signature`, text, required): The employee's signature indicates that they have reviewed and agreed to the certification information. 

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct employee and time period to ensure accurate certification.
* Double-check the certification date and hours to ensure they match your records.
* Use the notes and manager comments fields to provide additional context or feedback about the certification.
* The manager's signature indicates that they have verified the information provided.
