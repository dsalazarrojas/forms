# research_study_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The research study approval form is used for researchers to submit their study proposals for approval. The form captures essential information about the proposal, including study details, institution, funding, and review status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information about the study, including study description, category, and study leader/coordinator details.
* Choose the correct research institution and its address (if applicable).
* Select the relevant funding options for your study.
* Provide accurate contact information for the study leader and coordinator (if applicable).
* Attach any relevant documentation or attachments to the proposal.
* Select the correct review and approval status for your study.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Research Study Proposal
* **Research Study Proposal** (`research_study_proposal`, text, required): A brief description of the research study, including its purpose, objectives, and methodology.

### Categories
* **Categories** (`categories`, select_one, required): Select the category under which the research study falls (Category A, Category B, Category C).

### Study Description
* **Study Description** (`study_description`, text, required): A detailed description of the study, including its objectives, methodology, and expected outcomes.

### Study Leader
* **Study Leader** (`study_leader`, text, optional): The name and contact information of the study leader.

### Study Coordinator
* **Study Coordinator** (`study_coordinator`, text, optional): The name and contact information of the study coordinator.

### Research Institution
* **Research Institution** (`research_institution`, select_one, required): Select the research institution associated with the study (Research Inst 1, Research Inst 2, Research Inst 3).

### Research Funding
* **Research Funding** (`research_funding`, select_multiple, optional): Select the relevant funding options for the study (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).

### Ethical Clearance
* **Ethical Clearance** (`ethical_clearance`, select_one, required): Select the ethical clearance type for the study (Clearance Type 1, Clearance Type 2, Clearance Type 3).

### Submission Date
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): The date the proposal was submitted.

### Review Status
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required): Select the current review status of the proposal (Review Status 1, Review Status 2, Review Status 3).

### Research Institution Address
* **Research Institution Address** (`research_institution_address`, text, optional): The address of the research institution (if applicable).

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): The contact email of the study leader/coordinator.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): The phone number of the study leader/coordinator.

### Study Leader Phone
* **Study Leader Phone** (`study_leader_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the study leader.

### Study Coordinator Phone
* **Study Coordinator Phone** (`study_coordinator_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the study coordinator.

### Research Institution Website
* **Research Institution Website** (`research_institution_website`, text, optional): The website of the research institution (if applicable).

### Research Proposal Attachment
* **Research Proposal Attachment** (`research_proposal_attachment`, note, required): Attach any relevant documentation or attachments to the proposal.

### Research Institutional Review
* **Research Institutional Review** (`research_institutional_review`, select_one, required): Select the review status of the research institutional review (Institutional Review 1, Institutional Review 2, Institutional Review 3).

### Research Institutional Approvement
* **Research Institutional Approvement** (`research_institutional_approvement`, select_one, required): Select the approval status of the research institutional review (Approvement 1, Approvement 2, Approvement 3).

### Research Institutional Date
* **Research Institutional Date** (`research_institutional_date`, date, required): The date of the research institutional review.

### Research Proposal Status
* **Research Proposal Status** (`research_proposal_status`, select_one, required): Select the proposal status (Proposal Status 1, Proposal Status 2, Proposal Status 3).

### Research Institutional Approval
* **Research Institutional Approval** (`research_institutional_approval`, select_one, required): Select the approval status of the research institutional review (Approval 1, Approval 2, Approval 3).

### Study Institutional Review
* **Study Institutional Review** (`study_institutional_review`, select_one, required): Select the review status of the study institutional review (Institutional Review 1, Institutional Review 2, Institutional Review 3).

### Study Institutional Approvement
* **Study Institutional Approvement** (`study_institutional_approvement`, select_one, required): Select the approval status of the study institutional review (Approvement 1, Approvement 2, Approvement 3).

### Research Institutional Date (Study)
* **Research Institutional Date (Study)** (`research_institutional_date`, date, required): The date of the study institutional review.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth review process.
* Attach any relevant documentation or attachments to the proposal.
* Select the correct options and statuses for your study.
* Review your information carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
