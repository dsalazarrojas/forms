# logic_puzzle_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "logic_puzzle_quiz" form is designed to collect user demographic and logical puzzle response information for analysis and assessment of user reasoning and problem-solving skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your User Name, Age, Email, and Phone (if applicable).
2. For each puzzle question (Puzzle 1-5), select a single answer from the provided options.
3. For Puzzle 6, select multiple answers from the provided options.
4. Fill in the Date and Time of submission.
5. Add any additional notes, if required or desired.
6. Finally, fill in your Submission Name for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (text, required: false)**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Age (number, required: false)**: Provide your age for demographic information.
* **Email (email, required: false)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (text, required: false)**: Provide your phone number (optional).
* **Puzzle 1 (select_one, required: false)**: Choose a single answer from the options for the first puzzle.
* **Puzzle 2 (select_one, required: false)**: Choose a single answer from the options for the second puzzle.
* **Puzzle 3 (select_one, required: false)**: Choose a single answer from the options for the third puzzle.
* **Puzzle 4 (select_one, required: false)**: Choose a single answer from the options for the fourth puzzle.
* **Puzzle 5 (select_one, required: false)**: Choose a single answer from the options for the fifth puzzle.
* **Puzzle 6 (select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose multiple answers from the options for the sixth puzzle.
* **Date (date, required: false)**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Time (time, required: false)**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Note (note, required: false)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Submitted By (text, required: false)**: Enter a name or identifier for your submission.
