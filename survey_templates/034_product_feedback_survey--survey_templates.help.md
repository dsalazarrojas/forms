# Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback and data from customers about a product. This information will help us improve product development, customer satisfaction, and overall product quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
	* **Feedbacker Name**: Enter your name as a feedback provider.
	* **Feedbacker Email**: Enter your email address.
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* **Rating**: Choose a rating for the product (if applicable).
	* **Message**: Provide any additional comments about the product.
	* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product (if applicable).
	* **Product Type**: Select the type(s) of the product (if applicable).
	* **Feedback Time**: Enter the time you used the product (if applicable).
	* **Feedback Date**: Enter the date you used the product (if applicable).
	* **Feedback Category**: Select a category for your feedback (if applicable).
	* **Feedbacker Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
	* **Company Phone**: Enter the company phone number (if applicable).
	* **Company Website URL**: Enter the company website URL (if applicable).
	* **Product Description**: Provide a brief description of the product (if applicable).
	* **Product Image URL**: Enter the URL of a product image (if applicable).
	* **Product Image Alt**: Enter a text alternative for the product image (if applicable).
	* **Company Address**: Enter the company address (if applicable).
	* **Company City**: Enter the company city (if applicable).
	* **Company State**: Select the state of the company (if applicable).
	* **Company Postal Code**: Enter the company postal code (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Name
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.

### Rating
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, optional): Choose a rating for the product (if applicable).

### Product Rating Comments
* **Product Rating Comments** (`product_rating_comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any comments about the product rating.

### Message
* **Message** (`feedback_message`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments about the product.

### Feedbacker Name
* **Feedbacker Name** (`feedbacker_name`, `email`, optional): Enter your name as a feedback provider.

### Feedbacker Email
* **Feedbacker Email** (`feedbacker_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.

### Product Price
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price of the product (if applicable).

### Product Type

### Feedback Time
* **Feedback Time** (`feedback_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you used the product (if applicable).

### Feedback Date
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you used the product (if applicable).

### Feedback Category

### Feedbacker Phone

### Company Phone
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the company phone number (if applicable).

### Company Website URL
* **Company Website URL** (`company_website_url`, `text`, optional): Enter the company website URL (if applicable).

### Product Description
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `note`, optional): Provide a brief description of the product (if applicable).

### Product Image URL
* **Product Image URL** (`product_image_url`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of a product image (if applicable).

### Product Image Alt
* **Product Image Alt** (`product_image_alt`, `text`, optional): Enter a text alternative for the product image (if applicable).

### Company Address

### Company City

### Company State

### Company Postal Code

Tips:
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
