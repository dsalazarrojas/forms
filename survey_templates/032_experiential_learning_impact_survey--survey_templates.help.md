# Experiential Learning Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the impact of experiential learning experiences, helping to identify the skills and habits gained from these experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction and understand the purpose of the survey.
2. Select all the outcomes that apply to your experience.
3. Choose the specific skills gained from your experience.
4. Provide suggestions or comments on how to improve the experience in the "Improvement Ideas" field.
5. If you have any additional comments, feel free to type them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (id: 1)**: A brief description of the form and its purpose.
* **Outcomes (id: 2)**: Choose all the skills and habits that you gained from the experiential learning experience. The options are:
  - Improved critical thinking
  - Developed problem-solving skills
  - Enhanced creativity
  - Better communication skills
  - More efficient work habits
  - Improved time management
  - Greater self-awareness
  - Better teamwork
  - Greater self-motivation
  - Greater self-discipline
* **Skills Gained (id: 3)**: Choose the specific skills you gained from your experience. The options are:
  - Leadership and management
  - Communication
  - Teamwork
  - Time management
  - Problem-solving
  - Critical thinking
  - Creativity
  - Adaptability
* **Improvement Ideas (id: 4)**: Type any suggestions or comments you have on how to improve the experience.
* **Comments (id: 5)**: If you have any additional comments or thoughts, feel free to type them here.
* **How do you justify this outcome (id: 6)**: Type a brief explanation on how you justified your outcome, if applicable.
* **How did you develop the skills above (id: 7)**: Type a brief explanation on how you developed the skills above, if applicable.
