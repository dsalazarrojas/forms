# Browsing History Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Browsing History Survey aims to collect information about your online habits and preferences. This survey will help us better understand your browsing patterns, device usage, and online activities. Please answer the questions honestly to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the corresponding answers for each field. For select fields, choose the option that best fits your habits.
2. If you have to provide a numerical answer, enter the number as instructed.
3. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Age Range** (respondent_age)
Age Range: 18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, 56-65, 65+

* This field asks for your age range to help us understand your demographic information.

### 2. **Education Level** (respondent_education)
Education Level: High School, College, Graduate Degree, Professional Certification

* This field asks about your level of education to help us gauge your educational background.

### 3. **Hours Spent Online Daily** (hours_online_daily)
* Enter the number of hours you spend online daily.

### 4. **Primary Device for Internet Access** (primary_device)
* Select the primary device you use to access the internet.

### 5. **Primary Web Browser Used** (browser_preference)
* Choose the primary web browser you use for browsing.

### 6. **Hours Spent on Social Media Weekly** (social_media_usage)
* Enter the number of hours you spend on social media weekly.

### 7. **Top 3 Websites You Visit Most** (favorite_websites)
* List or describe the top 3 websites you visit most.

### 8. **Website Categories You Visit Most** (website_categories)
* Select all the website categories you visit most (e.g., News, Entertainment, etc.).

### 9. **Primary Search Engine Used** (search_engine)
* Choose the primary search engine you use for searching.

### 10. **Monthly Online Shopping Frequency** (online_shopping)
* Select how often you shop online monthly.

### 11. **Do you use online banking services?** (online_banking)
* Choose whether you use online banking services.

### 12. **How concerned are you about online privacy?** (privacy_concern)
* Select your level of concern about online privacy.

### 13. **Your comfort level with cookies and tracking** (cookies_tracking)
* Choose your comfort level with cookies and tracking.

### 14. **How many unique passwords do you use?** (password_habits)
* Estimate or range the number of unique passwords you use.

### 15. **Weekly hours of online video watching** (video_watching)
* Enter the number of hours you spend watching online videos weekly.

## Tips
* Answer each question honestly to help us improve our services.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Please complete the form carefully to ensure accurate results.
