# Genetic Feedback Loop Research Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Genetic Feedback Loop Research Data Collection Form is designed to collect participant information for genetic research studies. Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Study Details**: Provide a brief description of the study, including its title, date, and any relevant details.
2. Select a participant type from the options (Participant Type, Control, or Donor).
3. **Demographic Data**: Choose the participant's age, study group, and gender from the options.
4. **Survey Data**: Provide any relevant survey data for the participant.
5. **Contact Info**: Enter the participant's contact information, including any relevant details.
6. Select the participant's relationship (if applicable) from the options.
7. **Genetic Data 1 & 2**: Select any relevant genetic information for the participant.
8. **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Study Details**: 
  * **title** (text): A brief description of the study.
  * **participant_type** (select_one): Select from Participant Type, Control, or Donor.
  * **date** (text): Date of study (not shown on the form).
* **participant_info**: 
  * **participant_type** (select_one): Select from Participant Type, Control, or Donor.
  * **participant_age** (select_multiple): Age of the participant.
  * **study_group** (select_multiple): Study group the participant is in.
  * **gender** (select_multiple): Gender of the participant.
* **survey_data**: Provide any relevant survey data for the participant.
* **contact_info**: Enter the participant's contact information.
  * **participant_relationship** (select_one): Select the participant's relationship (if applicable).
* **genetic_data_1 & 2**:
  * **genetic_info_1 & 2** (select_multiple & select_one): Select any relevant genetic information for the participant.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the participant.
