# health_equity_and_inclusion_perception_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Health Equity and Inclusion Perception Survey is designed to collect opinions and perceptions about health equity and inclusion. The survey aims to gauge respondents' understanding and attitudes on health-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please take a few minutes to read each section carefully before answering.
2. Fill in the required information for each field, marked as **required**.
3. If a field is **optional**, feel free to provide as much information as you deem necessary or relevant.
4. Once you've completed all fields, submit your responses.

* **Introduction (1)** (`text`, **required**): Briefly explain why you're taking this survey and what you expect from it.
* **Section 1: Demographics (2)** (`text`, **required**): Provide your demographic information, such as age, sex, occupation, or any other relevant details.
* **Section 2: Patient Characteristics (3)** (`text`, **required**): Describe your personal characteristics related to health, such as health status, medical conditions, or healthcare access.
* **Section 3** (`text`, **required**): This section is about health-related issues and concerns.
* **Section 4** (`text`, **required**): Describe your experiences with healthcare services and their impact on your health.
* **Section 5** (`text`, **required**): Discuss your opinions and perspectives on various health topics.
* **Section 6** (`text`, **required**): Describe any challenges you face when accessing health services.
* **Section 7** (`text`, **required**): This section is about healthcare providers' roles and behaviors.
* **Section 8** (`text`, **required**): Discuss your experiences with healthcare providers' communication and responsiveness.
* **Section 9** (`text`, **required**): Describe your perceptions on healthcare quality and effectiveness.
* **Section 10** (`text`, **required**): This section is about health equity and inclusion.
* **Section 11** (`text`, **required**): Describe your experiences with health-related policies and regulations.
* **Section 12** (`text`, **required**): Discuss your opinions on addressing health inequities.
* **Section 13** (`text`, **required**): This section is about your suggestions for improving health equity and inclusion.
* **Section 14** (`text`, **required**): Describe any personal stories or anecdotes related to health equity and inclusion.
* **Section 15** (`text`, **required**): This section is about your final thoughts and recommendations.
* **Section 16** (`text`, **required**): Summarize your understanding and opinions on health equity and inclusion.
* **Section 17** (`text`, **required**): This section is about your suggestions for future research or initiatives.
* **Section 18** (`text`, **required**): Describe any additional comments or questions.
* **Section 19** (`text`, **required**): This section is about your overall experience and satisfaction with the survey.
* **Section 20** (`text`, **required**): Provide any final thoughts or messages.
* **Section 21** (`text`, **required**): Discuss your understanding of health equity and inclusion's impact on your life.
