# Beta Tester Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beta Tester Registration Form is for individuals who want to participate in beta testing a product. It is used to collect demographic and interest information from potential beta testers to help the product team identify suitable candidates for the beta testing process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your contact information, including your Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Answer whether you have been a beta tester before and what is your Primary Operating System Used.
3. Provide your Specific Hardware Details and your Time Commitment Weekly.
4. Select the parts of the app you are most interested in testing (Preferred Testing Areas).
5. Describe your interest in the product (Why do you want to test our product).
6. Sign the Non-Disclosure Agreement and Privacy Policy Agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Full Name** (`tester_full_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
- **Email Address** (`tester_email_addr`, Email, Required): Enter your valid email address that you check regularly.
- **Phone Number** (`tester_phone_number`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
- **Mailing Address** (`tester_mailing_address`, Text, Optional): If you would like us to send you any information or products, please enter your mailing address.
- **Have you been a beta tester before** (`previous_tester_experience`, Select One, Required): Choose whether you have been a beta tester before or not.
- **Primary Operating Systems Used** (`primary_os_selection`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the operating systems you regularly use.
- **Specific Hardware Details** (`hardware_details_text`, Text, Required): Describe your hardware specifications.
- **Time Commitment Weekly** (`availability_hours_weekly`, Select One, Required): Choose how much time you can dedicate to testing.
- **Preferred Testing Areas** (`preferred_testing_areas`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the parts of the app you are most interested in testing.
- **Why do you want to test our product** (`motivation_narrative`, Text, Required): Describe your interest in the product.
- **Non-Disclosure Agreement** (`nda_agreement_choice`, Select One, Required): Sign the Non-Disclosure Agreement to keep the software confidential.
- **Privacy Policy Agreement** (`privacy_policy_agreement`, Select One, Required): Sign the Privacy Policy Agreement to understand how we handle your personal information.
- **Date of Registration** (`registration_date_stamp`, Date, Required): The date you are registering for beta testing.
