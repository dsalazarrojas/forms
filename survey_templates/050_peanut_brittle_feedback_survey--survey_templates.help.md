# Peanut_Brittle_Feedback_Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about a product. Your input will help us improve the product and provide a better experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please fill out all the required fields. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own purpose. Follow the instructions below to ensure you provide the necessary information.

1. **Peanut Brittle Feedback Survey**: This is a title section where you can provide a brief title for your feedback.
2. **Customer Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or account.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
4. **Product Rating**: Rate the product from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
5. **Product Quality**: Choose from the options provided to indicate the quality of the product (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
6. **Areas for Improvement**: Provide any areas where you think we can improve the product (optional).
7. **Where did you buy it**: If you'd like to share where you purchased the product (optional).
8. **Comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts about the product (optional).
9. **Form Submit**: You must click this button to submit your feedback.
