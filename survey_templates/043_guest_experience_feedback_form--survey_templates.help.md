# Guest Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from guests after their experience with our company. The purpose of this form is to gather information about their satisfaction and suggestions for improvement. By completing this form, you will be helping us understand how we can better serve our guests in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Guest Experience** field with a brief description of your experience.
2. Provide your **Feedback** regarding your experience.
3. Choose a **Rating** to express your satisfaction level (Excellent, Good, Average, Bad, or Terrible).
4. Share any **Suggestions** on how we can improve.
5. Enter your **Email** address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
6. Provide your **Phone** number (if you'd like to be contacted by phone).
7. Select the **Date** of your experience.
8. Choose the **Time** of your experience (if applicable).
9. Leave any additional **Note** that you'd like to share with us.
10. If assigned a case number, type it in the **Assigned** field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Experience**: Briefly describe your experience with our company.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts on how we can improve.
* **Rating**: Choose a rating from Excellent, Good, Average, Bad, or Terrible to express your satisfaction level.
* **Suggestions**: Share your ideas on how we can improve.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **Date**: Select the date of your experience.
* **Time**: Select the time of your experience (if applicable).
* **Note**: Leave any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Assigned**: If you were assigned a case number, enter it here (if applicable).
