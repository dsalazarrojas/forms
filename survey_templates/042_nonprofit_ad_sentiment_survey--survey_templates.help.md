# Nonprofit Ad Sentiment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is used to understand the sentiment of our nonprofit ad on social media. It asks questions about how often you have seen the ad, what your overall sentiment was, how it impacted you, if it inspired you to donate, if you will share it, and if you are interested in learning more about our nonprofit organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reading through each question carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have you seen our ad on social media?** (ad_views, number, required)
	+ Answer how many times you have seen our ad on social media.
* **How often have you seen our ad on social media?** (ad_views_views_frequency, select_multiple, required)
	+ Choose how often you have seen our ad on social media.
* **What was your overall sentiment when you saw the ad on social media?** (ad_views_views_sentiment, select_one, required)
	+ Choose your overall sentiment about the ad.
* **How did our ad on social media affect you?** (ad_views_views_impact, select_multiple, required)
	+ Choose how our ad impacted you.
* **Did you feel inspired to donate after seeing the ad on social media?** (ad_views_views_donate, select_one, required)
	+ Choose whether you were inspired to donate.
* **Will you share our ad on social media?** (ad_views_views_share, select_one, required)
	+ Choose whether you will share our ad on social media.
* **Are you interested in learning more about our nonprofit organization?** (ad_views_views_interest, select_one, required)
	+ Choose whether you are interested in learning more about our nonprofit organization.
* **What is your name?** (contact_info, text, required)
	+ Enter your name.
* **What is your email address?** (contact_info_email, email, required)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (contact_info_phone, text, required)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **What would you like to say to us?** (contact_info_message, text, optional)
	+ Enter any comments or feedback you have for us.

## Tips

Please answer each question truthfully and carefully. This survey is designed to help us improve our ad strategy and better serve our nonprofit organization. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
