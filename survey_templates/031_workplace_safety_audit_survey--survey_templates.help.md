The purpose of this form is to gather information about the workplace safety conditions and procedures in place within an organization. The Workplace Safety Audit Survey is intended to identify potential hazards and evaluate the effectiveness of the workplace safety procedures. This information will help the organization to improve workplace safety and reduce the risk of accidents and injuries.

Please note that this form is not a formal evaluation or assessment of the workplace. It is a survey meant to gather opinions and comments from employees about their experiences and concerns regarding workplace safety.

Before starting the survey, please make sure that you have reviewed the relevant sections of the workplace procedures and policies related to safety.
