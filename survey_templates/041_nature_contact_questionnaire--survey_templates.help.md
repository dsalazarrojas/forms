# Nature Contact Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your nature contact frequency and activities. Your responses will help us understand how people interact with nature and the impact of their activities on the environment.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately to the best of your ability.
- You can select one or multiple options depending on the question type.
- If you're unsure about any question, you can skip it if it's not required. However, if a question is marked as required, you will need to answer it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

## Page 1: Survey Start
* **Start Survey** (`survey_start`, note, required: false): This is a starting point for the survey. Please read the instructions carefully and let's get started.

## Page 2: How Do You Spend Your Time Outdoors
* **How Do You Spend Your Time Outdoors** (`contact_frequency`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your frequency of contact with nature. Please select one of the options that best answers your frequency.
  * 'Yes': I contact nature regularly.
  * 'No': I rarely contact nature.

## Page 3: What Do You Like to Do In Nature
* **What Do You Like to Do In Nature** (`favorite_nature_activity`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your favorite nature activities. Please select one of the options that best answers your preference.
  * 'Yes': I engage in this activity.
  * 'No': I do not engage in this activity.

## Page 4: Do You Contact Nature with Water Bodies
* **Do You Contact Nature with Water Bodies** (`contact_with_water`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your contact with water bodies. Please select one of the options that best answers your experience.
  * 'Yes': I contact nature with water bodies.
  * 'No': I do not contact nature with water bodies.

## Page 5: How Does Your Activity Impact Nature
* **How Does Your Activity Impact Nature** (`nature_environment_impact`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about the impact of your activities on nature. Please select all the options that best answer your experience.
  * 'Yes': This impacts nature positively.
  * 'No': This impacts nature negatively.

## Page 6: Other Nature Activities
* **Other Nature Activities** (`other_nature_activity`, text, required: false): This question allows you to provide any other nature activities not listed. Please describe your activities briefly.

## Page 7: How Many Hours Do You Spend Outdoors
* **How Many Hours Do You Spend Outdoors** (`hours_spend_outdoors`, number, required: false): This question asks about the amount of time you spend outdoors. Please enter the number of hours you spend outdoors per day/week.

## Page 8: Frequency of Contact with Nature
* **Frequency of Contact with Nature** (`nature_contact_frequency_per_week`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about how frequently you contact nature per week. Please select one of the options that best answers your experience.
  * 'Yes': I contact nature frequently.
  * 'No': I rarely contact nature.

## Page 9: Age Range
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your age range. Please select all the options that best answer your age range.

## Page 10: Where Do You Spend Most of Your Nature Time
* **Where Do You Spend Most of Your Nature Time** (`contact_location`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about where you spend most of your nature time. Please select all the options that best answer your experience.

## Page 11: End Survey
* **End Survey** (`survey_end`, note, required: false): This is the end of the survey. Thank you for your participation.
