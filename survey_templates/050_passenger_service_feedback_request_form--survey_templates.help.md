# Passenger Service Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Passenger Service Feedback Request Form is designed to gather information about your experience with our service. It helps us to understand what we do well and where we can improve. 

## How To Complete This Form
- To provide feedback on your recent travel experience, fill out each field as accurately and completely as possible.
- Select one or multiple answers for the questions depending on your experience.
- Do not hesitate to answer each field, as this will help us to gain a complete understanding of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Service Provider** (`passenger_service_provider`, text, **required**): Please provide the name of the service provider you used for your recent travel experience.
* **Route** (`route`, text, **required**): Please type the name of the route you took or the service you used.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, text, **required**): Please select the type of service you received, which could be bus, train, airplane, etc.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select one, **required**): Please select how satisfied you are with your overall experience on a scale from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select multiple, optional): Please select all that apply to describe the quality of service you received. You can choose one or more of the following:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Communication** (`communication`, select one, optional): Please select how you would rate the communication you received during your travel experience. You can choose one of the following:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, select one, optional): Please select how clean the facilities were during your travel experience. You can choose one of the following:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Punctuality** (`punctuality`, select one, optional): Please select how punctual you found the service to be during your travel experience. You can choose one of the following:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Late
	+ Very Late
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select one, optional): Please select how you think the price of the service compared to its quality. You can choose one of the following:
	+ Very cheap
	+ Somewhat cheap
	+ Average
	+ Somewhat expensive
	+ Very expensive
* **Comfort** (`comfort`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments about the comfort of your travel experience.

Note: This form should only be completed once for each travel experience. If you have any feedback on multiple services, please submit separate forms for each experience.
