# demographic_information_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information from respondents to support research or data collection purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first name** in the text field labeled "first_name".
2. Enter your **last name** in the text field labeled "last_name".
3. Select your **birthdate** by clicking on the date field labeled "birthdate".
4. Enter your **age** in the text field labeled "age".
5. Select your **ethnicity** from the list provided in the labeled "ethnicity" field.
6. Select your **gender** from the list provided in the labeled "gender" field.
7. Select your **marital status** from the multiple-choice options in the labeled "marital_status" field.
8. Select your **education level** from the list provided in the labeled "education_level" field.
9. Enter your **annual income** in the text field labeled "annual_income".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is typically spelled.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is typically spelled.
* **Birthdate** (date, required: false): Select your birthdate using the calendar tool. Please ensure that the date is accurate.
* **Age** (number, required: false): Enter your age as a numerical value.
* **Ethnicity** (select_one, required: false): Select one of the options provided to describe your ethnicity.
	+ Select "Other" if your ethnicity is not listed.
* **Gender** (select_one, required: false): Select one of the options provided to describe your gender.
	+ Select "Other" if your gender is not listed.
* **Marital Status** (select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply to describe your current marital status.
* **Education Level** (select_one, required: true): Select one of the options provided to describe your highest level of education.
* **Annual Income** (number, required: false): Enter your annual income as a numerical value.

## Tips

* Ensure that you provide accurate and honest information to support the reliability of the data.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the form owner or administrator for clarification.
* If you have provided this form before, ensure that you are not duplicating your submission.
* Take your time to ensure that you provide accurate and thoughtful responses.
