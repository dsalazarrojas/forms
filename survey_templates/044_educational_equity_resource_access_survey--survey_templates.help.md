# Educational Equity Resource Access Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about users' access to educational resources. Please answer the questions honestly, and make sure to complete all required fields. Required fields are marked with <code>**Required**</code>.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (<code>**Required**</code> label).
3. For select and date fields, choose the answer that best describes your situation.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please contact us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (<id: 1>, <type: text>): 
    This field is for your name and contact information. Please provide your name and a valid email address.
* **Do you have access to the following resources?** (<id: 2, <type: select_multiple>):
    Select all resources you have access to from the provided options.
* **If you do not have access, what is the reason?** (<id: 3, <type: text>):
    If you selected "no" or "limited" for the resources in the previous field, please explain why you don't have access. This will help us better understand your situation.
* **School Information** (<id: 4, <type: number>):
    This field is for providing school or institution information. Please enter your school number or name, or leave it blank if you are not associated with a school.
* **How often do you have access to these resources?** (<id: 5, <type: select_one>):
    Choose how often you access these resources from the provided options.
* **How long have you had access to these resources?** (<id: 6, <type: date>):
    If applicable, select the date when you first had access to these resources or when you started experiencing difficulties in accessing them.
* **Notes** (<id: 7, <type: text>):
    If you have any additional information you'd like to share, please type it in this field.
* **Email** (<id: 8, <type: email>):
    Please enter a valid email address where we can contact you if needed. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If you are unsure about any question, contact us for clarification.
* If you have completed this form before, your previous responses will not be overridden.
