# Property Climate Resilience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Property Climate Resilience Survey is designed to help us better understand your property's resilience to climate-related risks and challenges. Your participation in this survey will help us gather valuable insights and inform our future efforts to support properties like yours.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the following form, one field at a time.
2. Fill in your property's information accurately and thoroughly.
3. If you're unsure about any field or need help, please refer to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Building Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of your property.
* **Property Type** (2, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Residential
	+ Commercial
	+ Industrial
* **Address Line 1** (3, text, required): Enter your property's street address.
* **Address Line 2** (4, text, required): Enter your property's apartment or suite number (if applicable).
* **City** (5, text, required): Enter your property's city or locality name.
* **Region** (6, text, required): Enter your property's region or state name.
* **Province** (7, text, required): Enter your property's province or region name.
* **Country** (8, text, required): Enter your country.
* **Zip Code** (9, text, required): Enter your property's zip or postal code.
* **Contact Person** (10, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Title** (11, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Phone Number** (12, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (13, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (14, text, required): Enter the name of the person who will serve as the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Title** (15, text, required): Enter the title of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone Number** (16, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Climate Change Risk Category** (17, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following risks:
	+ Extreme Weather
	+ Sea Level Rise
	+ Water Scarcity
	+ Flood
	+ Drought
	+ Wildfire
* **Risk Severity** (18, select_one, required): Please select one of the following:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
	+ Very High
* **Adaptation or Mitigation Actions** (19, text, required): Describe any adaptation or mitigation actions your property has taken or plans to take.
* **Resilience Measures in Place** (20, text, required): Describe any resilience measures your property has in place.
* **Current Climate Change Impacts** (21, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following impacts:
	+ Increased Extreme Weather
	+ Sea Level Rise
	+ Water Scarcity
	+ Heat Wave
	+ Drought
	+ Wildfire
	+ Increased Flood
	+ Sea Ice
* **Current Climate Change Risk Concerns** (22, text, required): Describe any current climate change risk concerns your property faces.
* **Potential Future Risks** (23, text, required): Describe any potential future climate change risks your property may face.
* **Potential Future Adaptation and Mitigation Plans** (24, text, required): Describe any potential future adaptation and mitigation plans your property may have.
* **Additional Comments** (25, text, required): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

Please ensure that you complete all required fields. If you encounter any issues or have questions, please contact us at [support email/phone number]. We are here to help!
