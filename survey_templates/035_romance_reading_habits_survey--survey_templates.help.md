# Romance Reading Habits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Romance Reading Habits Survey is designed to collect information about your reading habits, specifically in the romance genre. This survey will help us understand how often you read, what types of books you enjoy, and how you discover new authors and books.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2.  Select the correct options for each question.
3.  Be accurate when providing your information.
4.  If a question does not apply to you, please select "Not Applicable" or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audience** (`audience`, `select_one`): Select the type of audience you usually read about. This can be authors, publishers, librarians, book communities, or any other type of audience that you enjoy.
* **Reading Frequency** (`reading_frequency`, `select_multiple`): Indicate how often you read. You can select multiple options, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Reading Length** (`reading_length`, `number`): This question is asking about the length of time you usually spend reading. Please select the corresponding option that best describes your reading habits.
* **How often do you usually read?** (`reading_length_hint`, `note`): This is a follow-up question to help clarify your reading length. Please provide more details about your reading habits in this text field.
* **Favorite Genre** (`favorite_genre`, `select_multiple`): Select the genres that you enjoy reading. You can choose multiple options, such as Romance, Fiction, Non-Fiction, Fantasy, or any other genre that you enjoy.
* **Favorite Author** (`favorite_author`, `select_multiple`): Select the authors that you like to read about. Please note that you can choose multiple options, such as "Yes" or "No" depending on whether you like a particular author.
* **Reading Format** (`reading_format`, `select_one`): Indicate your preferred book format. You can select one option, such as Hardcover, Paperback, E-Book, or Audiobook.
* **Recommended Source** (`recommended_source`, `select_multiple`): Tell us how you discover new books and authors. You can choose multiple options, such as Book Clubs, Online, Recommendation, or Friends.
* **Preferred Book Length** (`preferred_book_length`, `select_multiple`): Select the length of book that you prefer. You can choose multiple options, such as Short Story Collection, 100-200 pages, or any other option that you enjoy reading.
* **Preferred Format** (`preferred_format`, `select_multiple`): Indicate your preferred book format. You can select multiple options, such as Physical Book, E-Book, or Audiobook.
* **Reading Device** (`reading_device`, `select_multiple`): Tell us how you usually read your books. You can select multiple options, such as E-Reader, Tablet, Smartphone, or Paperback.
* **Reading Location** (`reading_location`, `select_multiple`): Select where you usually read. You can choose multiple options, such as Home, Work, School, or Public Place.
* **Time Spent Reading** (`time_spent_reading`, `time`): This question is asking about the amount of time you spend reading. Please select the correct option that describes your reading habits.
* **How Do You Get Your Books?** (`how_do_you_get_your_books`, `select_multiple`): Tell us how you obtain your books. You can select multiple options, such as Bookstore, Online, Recommendation, or Gift.
* **Why Do You Read?** (`why_do_you_read`, `text`): This question is asking about your motivation for reading. Please provide more details about why you enjoy reading in this text field.
* **How Much Do You Agree?** (`how_much_do_you_agree`, `select_multiple`): This question is asking about your level of agreement with certain statements. Select the correct options that reflect your agreement or disagreement.
* **Favorite Book Type** (`favorite_book_type`, `select_multiple`): Select the type of book that you enjoy reading. You can choose multiple options, such as Fiction, Non-Fiction, Comic Books, or Magazine.
* **How Do You Find New Authors?** (`how_do_you_find_new_authors`, `select_multiple`): Tell us how you discover new authors. You can select multiple options, such as Bookstore, Online, Recommendation, or Social Media.
* **What Do You Don't Like?** (`what_dont_you_like`, `select_multiple`): This question is asking about things that you don't like in books. Select multiple options that describe your dislikes.
* **Favorite Character Type** (`favorite_character_type`, `select_multiple`): Select the type of character that you usually enjoy reading about. You can choose multiple options, such as Romance, Fantasy, Science Fiction, or any other type of character that you like.
* **Reading Mood** (`reading_mood`, `select_multiple`): Indicate the mood that you usually get while reading. You can select multiple options, such as Relaxing, Exciting, Adventurous, or Emotional.
* **Reading Mood Reason** (`reading_mood_reason`, `select_multiple`): This question is asking about why you get the selected mood while reading. Select multiple options that describe your reasons.
* **How Do You Rate Your Reading Habits?** (`how_do_you_rate_your_reading_habits`, `select_one`): Select how you rate your reading habits. You can choose one option that describes your agreement or disagreement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`): Please provide any additional comments or insights that you'd like to share about your reading habits.

Please take the survey honestly, and remember that all answers will be kept confidential. Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
