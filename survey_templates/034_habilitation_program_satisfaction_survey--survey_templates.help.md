# Habilitation Program Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Habilitation Program Satisfaction Survey is a tool to gather feedback from participants who have experienced the habilitation program. It aims to understand their overall experiences, perceptions, and suggestions for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
This form is designed to be easy to use, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select the corresponding answer options for each question.
3. You can add additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Feedback" section at the end.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Overall Experiences
 * **Overall Experiences** (`overall_experiences`, text, not required): 
Please describe your overall experiences with the habilitation program.

### Facility Experience
 * **Facility Experience** (`facility_experience`, text, not required): 
Describe your experiences with the physical facility where the program was held.

### Instructor Rating
 * **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, number, not required): 
Rate the quality of the instructors that led the program, with higher numbers indicating better experiences.

### Instructor Support
 * **Instructor Support** (`instructor_support`, select, not required): 
Indicate if you felt supported by the instructors during the program.

### Instructor Guided Sessions
 * **Instructor Guided Sessions** (`instructor_guidance`, select, not required): 
Check all the sessions that you found helpful or relevant, or select 'No' if none were helpful.

### Program Fulfiment
 * **Program Fulfiment** (`program_fulfillment`, number, not required): 
Rate the level of program fulfillment you experienced, with higher numbers indicating better fulfillment.

### Additional Feedback
 * **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): 
Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the program.

### Contact Information
 * **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, email, not required): 
If you have any follow-up questions or would like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address.
