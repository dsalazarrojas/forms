# Meeting Content Suggestions Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is intended to collect your suggestions and ideas for meeting content, such as topics, agendas, or minute notes. Your input will help improve the quality and relevance of our meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Meeting Content Suggestions Survey** title to briefly describe the content you suggest.
2. Provide a **Description** of your suggestion, explaining the context and reasoning behind it.
3. Select a **Category** that best fits your proposal (e.g., Meeting Content, Meeting Agenda, Meeting Minutes).
4. Add any relevant **Tags** to help others quickly identify and find your idea.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them in the **Comments** field.
6. In the **Suggestions** field, enter your new content or idea.
7. Provide a rating (1-5) in the **Rate this suggestion** field to indicate your satisfaction with the idea.
8. If you'd like to explain your rating, enter a brief comment in the **Why did you rate this?** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Content Suggestions Survey** (`meeting_content_suggestions_survey_name`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for your content suggestion.
* **Description** (`meeting_content_suggestions_survey_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the content you suggest.
* **Category** (`meeting_content_suggestions_survey_category`, select_one, required: false): Select a category for your suggestion (e.g., Meeting Content, Meeting Agenda, Meeting Minutes).
* **Tags** (`meeting_content_suggestions_survey_tags`, text, required: false): Add relevant tags to help others find your idea.
* **Comments** (`meeting_content_suggestions_survey_comments`, text, required: false): Enter additional comments or feedback on your suggestion.
* **Suggestions** (`meeting_content_suggestions_survey_suggestions`, text, required: false): Enter your new content or idea.
* **Rate this suggestion** (`meeting_content_suggestions_survey_rating`, number, required: false): Provide a rating (1-5) to indicate your satisfaction with the idea.
* **Why did you rate this?** (`meeting_content_suggestions_survey_rating_comments`, text, required: false): Enter a brief comment on why you rated your suggestion this way.
