# Water Supply Drill Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on water supply drills from participants. It is used to gather information on different aspects of the drill, such as participant details, evaluation, and satisfaction. This feedback will help improve future water supply drills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Drill Participant Information** section with any relevant details about the participant.
2. In the **Evaluation** section, select the level of evaluation for the drill.
3. In the **Satisfaction** section, choose the level of satisfaction you experienced with the drill.
4. Select the **Drill Location** where the drill took place.
5. For **Drill Time**, enter the time of the drill.
6. If there were **Drill Participants**, enter the number of participants.
7. Choose the **Drill Water Source** that was used during the drill.
8. Evaluate the **Drill Water Quality** based on your experience.
9. Enter any **Drill Notes** about the drill in the text box below.
10. For **Drill Recommendations**, choose any recommendations you have for improving the drill.
11. If applicable, enter a **Drill Rating** and **Drill Score** to rate and score your experience.
12. Enter any additional **Drill Comments** about the drill.
13. If there are areas for improvement, choose the **Recommendation for Improvement** options that apply.
14. Select the **Drill Improvement Plan** that you think would be beneficial for future drills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drill Participant Information**: Enter any details about the participant, such as their name, age, or contact information.
* **Evaluation**: Choose the level of evaluation you experienced during the drill, from "Very Poor" to "Very Good".
* **Satisfaction**: Select the level of satisfaction you felt during the drill, from "Very Dissatisfied" to "Very Satisfied".
* **Drill Location**: Choose the location of the drill, from "North", "South", "East", or "West".
* **Drill Time**: Enter the time of the drill in a 24-hour clock format (e.g., 08:00 AM).
* **Drill Participants**: Enter the number of participants that took part in the drill.
* **Drill Water Source**: Choose the source of water used during the drill, from "Well", "Stream", "Reservoir", or "Other".
* **Drill Water Quality**: Evaluate the quality of water used during the drill, from "Very Poor" to "Very Good".
* **Drill Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the drill in this text box.
* **Drill Recommendations**: Choose any recommendations for improving the drill, from "Very Likely" to "Very Unlikely".
* **Drill Rating**: Enter a numerical rating for the drill (e.g., 1-5).
* **Drill Score**: Enter a numerical score for the drill (e.g., 1-5).
* **Drill Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the drill.
* **Recommendation for Improvement**: Choose any options that apply for areas of improvement.
* **Drill Improvement Plan**: Select the plan that you think would be beneficial for future drills, from "Very Likely" to "Very Unlikely".
