# Library Genre Highlight Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your reading preferences, so we can better understand what types of books you enjoy reading and what formats you prefer. This will help us provide better services and make informed decisions about our book collections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). You can leave the optional fields empty if you don't have a preference.
2. Select your favorite genres from the list provided.
3. Choose your preferred format for reading books.
4. Let us know if you have any additional comments or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Library Genre Highlight Poll Page 1 (\*):** This field is not used in the form, but it's a placeholder page with no functionality.
* **Favorite Genres (required):** Select up to 3 of your favorite book genres from the list provided. You can choose any combination of genres that you enjoy reading.
* **Reading Preferences (optional):** This is a text field for you to provide any additional comments or preferences you may have. You can leave this field blank if you don't have anything to add.
* **Preferred Format (optional):** Select your preferred format for reading books. You can choose from the following options: E-book, Paperback, Hardcover, Audiobook, Digital audiobook, or Other (please specify).
* **Preferred Length (optional):** This field is not used in the form, but it's another placeholder with no functionality.
* **Other Preference (optional):** If you have a different preference or format that is not listed, you can specify it here.
* **Other (optional):** This field is also not used in the form, but it's another placeholder with no functionality.
* **Additional Comments (optional):** If you have any additional comments or preferences, you can provide them here.
* **Email (optional):** If you want to be contacted about any new book releases or updates, please provide your email address.
* **Phone (optional):** This field is not used in the form, but it's another placeholder with no functionality.
