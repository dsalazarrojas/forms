# Volunteer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from volunteers on their experiences and opinions about the volunteer program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your name as the volunteer.
2. Provide a feedback score out of 10 to rate your experience.
3. Share your feedback in the text field provided.
4. Select the date and time you visited the location.
5. Indicate your volunteer type (e.g., volunteer, staff, etc.).
6. Select your current position within the volunteer program (e.g., Volunteer, Staff, etc.).
7. Choose how frequently you participate in feedback sessions (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
8. Provide your email address for contact.
9. Enter your phone number (optional).
10. Add any additional comments about your experience.
11. Select who submitted the feedback (e.g., User 1, User 2, etc.).
12. Choose the date and time you submitted the feedback.
13. Select the volunteer program you are associated with.
14. Choose the location where you volunteered.
15. Select the tool assigned to you for the volunteer work.
16. Enter the number of volunteer hours you contributed.
17. Add any additional comments about your volunteer hours.
18. Select the committee that managed your volunteer work.
19. Add any additional comments about your experience.
20. Confirm that the feedback was submitted correctly.
21. Choose the submission date and time.
22. Select your current status in the volunteer program (e.g., Active, Inactive, etc.).
23. Select who submitted the feedback (e.g., User 1, User 2, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the volunteer.
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, number, required): Provide a score out of 10 to rate your experience.
* **Feedback Text** (`feedback_text`, text, required): Share your feedback and opinions about the volunteer program.
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`, date, required): Select the date you visited the location.
* **Time Visited** (`time_visited`, time, required): Select the time you visited the location.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, select_one, required): Choose your type (e.g., volunteer, staff, etc.).
* **Volunteer Position** (`volunteer_position`, select_multiple, required): Select your current position within the volunteer program (e.g., Volunteer, Staff, etc.).
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose how frequently you participate in feedback sessions (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Choose who submitted the feedback (e.g., User 1, User 2, etc.).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Choose the date you submitted the feedback.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Choose the time you submitted the feedback.
* **Volunteer Status** (`volunteer_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current status in the volunteer program (e.g., Active, Inactive, etc.).
* **Submitted By 2** (`submitted_by_2`, select_one, optional): Choose who submitted the feedback (e.g., User 1, User 2, etc.).
* **Volunteer Program** (`volunteer_program`, select_one, required): Choose the volunteer program you are associated with.
* **Volunteer Location** (`volunteer_location`, select_one, required): Choose the location where you volunteered.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to you for the volunteer work.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of volunteer hours you contributed.
* **Volunteer Hours Comment** (`volunteer_hours_comment`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your volunteer hours.
* **Volunteer Committee** (`volunteer_committee`, select_one, optional): Select the committee that managed your volunteer work.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, select_one, required): Confirm that the feedback was submitted correctly.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Choose the submission date and time.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Choose the submission time.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to the form administrators for clarification.
* The data collected will be used to inform decisions on how to better support and engage volunteers in the future.
* Please provide accurate and honest feedback to help us improve the volunteer program.
