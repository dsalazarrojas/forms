# Grocery Store Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers about their experience with a grocery store. It is intended for internal use within the organization and is not meant to be shared publicly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, including the store name, location, date visited, time spent, and employee name.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the correct options for each question based on your experience at the store.
4. Provide additional comments if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Name** (`store_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the store you visited.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the store you visited.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the store's overall quality on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product and Price Rating** (`product_and_price_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the store's products and prices on a scale of 1-5.
* **Store Appearance and Cleanliness** (`store_appearance_and_cleanliness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the store's appearance and cleanliness on a scale of 1-5.
* **Store Layout** (`store_layout`, `select_one`, required): Rate the store's layout on a scale of 1-5.
* **Employee Attitude** (`employee_attitude`, `select_one`, required): Rate the store's employee attitude on a scale of 1-5.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, `number`, required): Enter the time you spent at the store.
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`, `date`, required): Enter the date you visited the store.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the employee you interacted with.
* **Employee Name 2** (`employee_name_2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of another employee you interacted with (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **How would you rate the store's products and prices?** (`question1`, `select_one`, required): Rate the store's products and prices on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the store's service?** (`question2`, `select_one`, required): Rate the store's service on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the store's store appearance and cleanliness?** (`question3`, `select_one`, required): Rate the store's appearance and cleanliness on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the store's products and prices?** (`question1_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that apply to the store's products and prices.
* **How would you rate the store's service?** (`question2_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that apply to the store's service.
* **How would you rate the store's store appearance and cleanliness?** (`question3_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that apply to the store's appearance and cleanliness.
