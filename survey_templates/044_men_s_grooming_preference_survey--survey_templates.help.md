# Men's Grooming Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your preferences for men's grooming products. Your input will help us create tailored product recommendations or surveys that cater to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and select the options that best fit your preferences. Don't worry if you need help – just let us know!

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Template** (`survey_template`, note, not required): This section is for you to review and understand the questions before you start answering. Please take a moment to read through this section carefully.
* **Hair Care** (`hair_care`, select_multiple, not required): Please select all the hair care options that interest you, such as Beard Care, Shaving, Hair Color, Hair Style, or Hair Texturization.
* **Face Care** (`face_care`, select_multiple, not required): Select all face care options that suit your needs, including Skin Care, Shaving, Skincare, or Beard Care.
* **Body Grooming** (`body_grooming`, select_multiple, not required): Choose all body grooming options that interest you, such as Shaving, Exfoliating, Moisturizing, Deodorants, or Fragrance.
* **Product Preferences** (`product_preferences`, select_one, not required): Please select one product preference from the list, such as Shaving Cream, Shaving Foam, Aftershave, Aftershave Balm, Moisturizer, Deodorant, or Fragrance.
* **Frequency of Use** (`frequency_of_use`, select_one, not required): Select how often you use your current product, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly.
* **How Often Do You Shave** (`how_often_do_you_shave`, select_one, not required): Choose how often you shave, such as Daily, Every Other Day, 2-3 times a week, 1 time a week, or Less than 1 time a week.
* **How Often Do You Trim** (`how_often_do_you_trim`, select_one, not required): Select how often you trim, such as Daily, Every Other Day, 2-3 times a week, 1 time a week, or Less than 1 time a week.
* **What Is Your Current Product Use** (`what_is_your_current_product_use`, select_multiple, not required): Please select all the current products you use, such as Shaving Cream, Shaving Foam, Aftershave, Aftershave Balm, Moisturizer, Deodorant, Fragrance, or Beard Oil.
* **What Is Your Ideal Frequency of Use** (`what_is_your_ideal_frequency_of_use`, select_one, not required): Select how often you'd like to use your ideal product, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly.
* **What Do You Prefer** (`what_do_you_prefer`, select_one, not required): Choose what you prefer, such as Shaving Cream, Shaving Foam, Aftershave, Aftershave Balm, Moisturizer, Deodorant, or Fragrance.
* **Product Preferences Description** (`product_preferences_description`, text, not required): If you'd like to provide additional details about your product preferences, please enter them in this text field.
* **Any Other Comments** (`any_other_comments`, text, not required): If you have any other comments or suggestions, please share them with us.
* **How Would You Rate Your Current Product** (`how_would_you_rate_your_current_product`, select_one, not required): Rate your current product, from 1-2 to 9-10.
* **How Would You Rate Your Ideal Product** (`how_would_you_rate_your_ideal_product`, select_one, not required): Rate your ideal product, from 1-2 to 9-10.
* **What Is Your Ideal Product Characteristics** (`what_is_your_ideal_product_characteristics`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the ideal product characteristics you'd like to see, such as Natural Ingredients, Easy to Apply, Long Lasting, Affordable, Gentle, Fragrance, or Easy to Lather.
* **Any Other Comments 2** (`any_other_comments_2`, text, not required): If you have any other comments or suggestions, please share them with us.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for contact or follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for contact or follow-up purposes.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, not required): Re-enter your email address for confirmation.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, not required): Re-enter your phone number for confirmation.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, text, not required): Re-enter your password for confirmation.
* **Password** (`password`, text, not required): Enter a password (not used on this form).
* **Confirm Password 2** (`confirm_password_2`, text, not required): Re-enter your password for confirmation.
* **Confirm Email 2** (`confirm_email_2`, email, not required): Re-enter your email address for confirmation.
