# Coaching Relationship Expectations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help you clearly communicate your expectations and preferences for your coaching relationship with your coach. It will help ensure that both you and your coach are on the same page, setting a strong foundation for a positive and productive partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Take a few minutes to carefully read each question and understand what is being asked.
2. Choose your answers based on your true preferences and expectations for your coaching relationship.
3. Be as specific and detailed as possible in your answers.
4. Don't worry about running out of space; you can elaborate on your answers in the "Additional Expectations" field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the client.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your coach.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of this survey.
* **Stage of Coaching Relationship** (`relationship_stage`, `select_one`, required): Choose where you are in the coaching process:
	+ Just Starting: You are just beginning your coaching relationship.
	+ 1-3 Sessions In: You have had 1-3 coaching sessions.
	+ 4-6 Sessions In: You have had 4-6 coaching sessions.
	+ Long-Term Client: You have been working with your coach for a while.
	+ Ending Relationship: You are nearing the end of your coaching relationship.
* **Primary Goal for Coaching** (`primary_goal`, `text`, required): Describe your main objective for coaching.
* **Expected Session Frequency** (`session_frequency_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you want to meet with your coach:
	+ Weekly: Regular sessions every week.
	+ Bi-Weekly: Sessions every other week.
	+ Monthly: Less frequent sessions.
	+ As Needed: Sessions based on your coach's availability.
	+ Intensive: Intensive, frequent sessions.
* **Expected Session Duration** (`session_duration_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long each session should last:
	+ 30 Minutes: Short, focused sessions.
	+ 45 Minutes: Standard sessions.
	+ 60 Minutes: Longer sessions.
	+ 90 Minutes: More in-depth sessions.
	+ Flexible: Sessions can be adjusted to your needs.
* **Communication Between Sessions** (`communication_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you want to communicate with your coach outside of sessions:
	+ Regular Check-Ins: Regular updates and communication.
	+ Only When Needed: Limited communication when necessary.
	+ Scheduled Calls Only: Only scheduled calls and meetings.
	+ Email Only: Communication is limited to email.
	+ No Contact Between Sessions: No communication outside of sessions.
* **Expected Response Time** (`response_time_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose how quickly you want your coach to respond:
	+ Within 24 Hours: Prompt responses.
	+ Within 48 Hours: Responses within 2 days.
	+ Within 1 Week: Responses within a week.
	+ No Urgency: Responses at your coach's convenience.
* **Coach Role** (`coach_role_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose the role you want your coach to play:
	+ Guide and Facilitator: Coach provides guidance and support.
	+ Advisor and Expert: Coach provides expert advice.
	+ Accountability Partner: Coach holds you accountable for your actions.
	+ Cheerleader and Supporter: Coach provides encouragement and support.
	+ Challenger: Coach challenges you to grow.
	+ Mixed Role: Coach plays a mix of roles.
* **Feedback Style Preference** (`feedback_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to receive feedback:
	+ Direct and Honest: Feedback is direct and straightforward.
	+ Gentle and Supportive: Feedback is gentle and supportive.
	+ Balanced Approach: Feedback is balanced between direct and gentle.
	+ Written Feedback: Feedback is written.
	+ Verbal Feedback: Feedback is verbal.
* **Accountability Level Expected** (`accountability_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of accountability you want:
	+ High Accountability: High level of accountability.
	+ Moderate Accountability: Moderate level of accountability.
	+ Light Accountability: Low level of accountability.
	+ Self-Directed: You are responsible for your own accountability.
* **Expectations for Between-Session Work** (`homework_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of work you expect to do outside of sessions:
	+ Significant Work Expected: Extensive work between sessions.
	+ Moderate Work: Moderate work between sessions.
	+ Light Work: Minimal work between sessions.
	+ No Homework: No work outside of sessions.
* **Progress Tracking Preference** (`progress_tracking_expectation`, `select_one`, not required): Choose how you prefer to track progress:
	+ Formal Assessments: Regular, formal assessments.
	+ Goal Milestones: Progress is measured by milestones.
	+ Informal Discussion: Progress is discussed informally.
	+ Journal Reviews: Progress is reviewed through journal entries.
	+ No Formal Tracking: No formal progress tracking.
* **Confidentiality Expectations** (`confidentiality_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of confidentiality:
	+ Complete Confidentiality: Your information is completely confidential.
	+ Confidential with Exceptions: Confidentiality with some exceptions.
	+ Open to Sharing Progress: Progress is shared openly.
	+ No Concerns: No confidentiality concerns.
* **How Much Do You Want to Be Challenged** (`challenge_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose your comfort level with being challenged:
	+ Push Me Hard: You want to be challenged often.
	+ Moderate Challenge: You want some challenge.
	+ Gentle Approach: You prefer a gentle approach.
	+ Let Me Lead: You want to be in control.
* **Session Structure Preference** (`structure_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose how structured you want sessions to be:
	+ Highly Structured: Sessions are highly structured.
	+ Moderately Structured: Sessions are moderately structured.
	+ Flexible Structure: Sessions are flexible.
	+ Completely Open: Sessions have no structure.
* **Goal Setting Approach** (`goal_setting_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to set goals:
	+ Coach Sets Goals: Coach sets the goals.
	+ Collaborative Goal Setting: Goals are set together.
	+ Client Sets Goals: You set the goals.
	+ Evolving Goals: Goals evolve as needed.
* **How Do You Define Success** (`success_definition`, `text`, required): Describe what success looks like to you.
* **Expected Relationship Duration** (`relationship_duration_expectation`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long you want the coaching relationship to last:
	+ 1-3 Months: Short-term relationship.
	+ 3-6 Months: Medium-term relationship.
	+ 6-12 Months: Long-term relationship.
	+ Over 1 Year: Relationship can last longer.
	+ Indefinite: No set end date.
* **How Should the Relationship End** (`termination_expectation`, `select_one`, not required): Choose how you want the relationship to end:
	+ Clear End Date: A clear end date is set.
	+ When Goals Are Met: Coaching ends when goals are achieved.
	+ Natural Fade Out: Relationship ends naturally.
	+ Open-Ended: Relationship can end at any time.
* **Any Concerns or Boundaries** (`concerns_or_boundaries`, `text`, not required): If you have any concerns or boundaries, enter them here.
* **Additional Expectations** (`additional_expectations`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional expectations, enter them here.

# Tips

* Be honest and specific when answering each question.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your coach.
* Remember that this form is a tool to help you and your coach understand each other's expectations better. It's not a contract, and you can always adjust your expectations as needed during the coaching process.
