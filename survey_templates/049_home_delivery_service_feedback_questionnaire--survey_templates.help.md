# Home Delivery Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their home delivery experience. We value your input and use it to continuously improve our service to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Use the provided options or type in your answer if you have a specific response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Areas** (`delivery_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select the delivery areas you received your order from. You can choose multiple options if applicable.
* **Overall Service** (`service_rating`, number, optional): Rate the overall service you received, from 1 to 5 stars.
* **Were your order picked up on time** (`order_picked_up`, date, required): Enter the date and time you received your order.
* **Delivery Person** (`delivery_person_nickname`, text, required): Enter the name of the delivery person who brought your order.
* **How would you rate the delivery area** (`delivery_area_rating`, number, required): Rate the delivery area from 1 to 5 stars.
* **How would you rate the service quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, optional): Select the service quality you received, either Good or Poor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **How long did you wait** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you waited for your delivery.
* **Date of Delivery** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Enter the date you received your delivery.
* **Delivery Area** (`delivery_area`, text, required): Enter the delivery area you received your delivery from.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date you placed your order.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with our service, either Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
* **Customer Satisfaction Comments** (`customer_satisfaction_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about your level of satisfaction.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name so we can contact you if needed.
* **Message** (`contact_message`, text, optional): Enter any additional message or feedback about your experience.
* **Contact Area** (`contact_area`, text, required): Enter the area you are contacting us from.
* **Delivery Person** (`delivery_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the delivery person who brought your order.
* **Delivery Person Email** (`delivery_person_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the delivery person.
* **Delivery Person Phone** (`delivery_person_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the delivery person.
* **Delivery Date & Time** (`delivery_date_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you received your delivery.
* **Delivery Time Rating** (`delivery_time_rating`, number, optional): Rate the delivery time from 1 to 5 stars.
* **Delivery Quality** (`delivery_quality`, select_one, required): Select the quality of the delivery, either Good or Poor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
