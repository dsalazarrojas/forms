# Presenter Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from presenters after a seminar or presentation has taken place. It helps us understand your experience, assess the quality of the seminar, and gather valuable insights to improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your feedback and information about the seminar.
2. Choose the relevant options for each field that best describe your experience.
3. Leave any additional comments or suggestions in the "Presenter feedback" and "Suggestions for improvement" fields.
4. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Presenter's Name** (`presenter_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the presenter.
* **Seminar Title** (`seminar_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the seminar.
* **Seminar Start Time** (`seminar_starting_time`, date, required): Select the start time of the seminar.
* **Seminar End Time** (`seminar_ending_time`, time, required): Select the end time of the seminar.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the seminar in minutes.
* **Presenter Experience** (`presenter_experience`, number, required): Enter the presenter's experience level (years).
* **Number of Attendees** (`number_of_attendees`, number, required): Enter the number of attendees.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required): Select your overall experience with the seminar.
* **Location and Facilities** (`feedback_on_location`, select_one, required): Select your experience with the location and facilities.
* **Content of Seminar Was Clear and Concise** (`feedback_on_content`, select_one, required): Select your experience with the seminar's content.
* **Presenter was Available for Questions** (`presenter_availability`, select_one, required): Select your experience with the presenter's availability.
* **Presenter Feedback** (`presenter_feedback`, text, required): Leave any feedback about the presenter.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, required): Leave any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or feedback.
* **Survey Conducted By** (`survey_conducted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the survey.
* **Survey Conductors Name** (`survey_conductors_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the survey.
* **Seminar Date** (`seminar_date`, date, required): Select the date of the seminar.
* **Seminar Location** (`seminar_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the seminar.
* **Seminar Topic** (`presentation_topic`, text, required): Enter the topic of the seminar.
* **Seminar Starting Time** (`seminar_starting_time`, time, required): Select the start time of the seminar.
* **Seminar Ending Time** (`seminar_ending_time`, time, required): Select the end time of the seminar.
* **Presenter Availability** (`presenter_availability`, select_one, optional): Select your experience with the presenter's availability.
* **Materials Used Were Relevant and Up-to-Date** (`feedback_on_materials`, select_one, optional): Select your experience with the materials used.
* **Presenter Performance** (`presenter_performance`, select_multiple, optional): Select your experience with the presenter's performance.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Leave any additional information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, email, required): Enter your contact details.
