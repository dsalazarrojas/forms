# Hamstring Exercise Instruction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants about their participation in hamstring exercises. It's meant to be completed by participants to confirm their participation and provide additional details about their experience with the exercise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate exercise type from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your participant name and any relevant comments.
3. Choose the date and time you attended the training session.
4. Select the training location.
5. Confirm whether the form has been submitted or not.
6. Optionally, enter your email address (this field is not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exercise Type** (`exercise_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of hamstring exercise you participated in from the list. 
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you'd like to be recognized in the training session.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you attended the training session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the training session.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the training session took place.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments you'd like to share about your experience with the exercise.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, select_multiple, required): Confirm whether this form has been submitted or not.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted with updates or further information, please enter your email address here.
