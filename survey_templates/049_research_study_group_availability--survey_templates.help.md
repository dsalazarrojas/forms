# research_study_group_availability - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "research_study_group_availability" form is a tool used by researchers to collect information about participant availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the meeting format that best suits your needs: **Meeting Formats**
2. Provide your schedule details, including your availability in hours: **Member Schedules**
3. Add your contact details: **Contact Details**
4. Choose which research session you are available for: **Research Sessions**
5. Provide the duration of your research sessions: **Session Durations**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Formats** (`Meeting Formats`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more meeting formats that you are available for, such as **Morning**, **Afternoon**, or **Evening**.
* **Member Schedules** (`Member Schedules`, number, required: false): Provide a numeric value representing your availability in hours, for example, 5 for a 5-hour block of time.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Research Sessions** (`Research Sessions`, select_one, required: false): Choose which research session you are available for, such as **Research Session 1** or **Research Session 2**.
* **Session Durations** (`Session Durations`, number, required: false): Provide the duration of your research sessions, measured in hours.
