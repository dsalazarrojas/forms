# Short Film Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Short Film Feedback Survey is a tool for gathering feedback from viewers after watching a short film. By filling out this survey, you'll help us improve future productions and better serve our audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide your feedback, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best fit your preferences.
3. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Intro
- **Intro** (`intro_page`, note, not required): This is a brief introduction to the survey, providing context and background information about the film.

### 2. Contact Information
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter your contact information if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or discussions.

### 3. Email
- **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates or notifications about our future productions.

### 4. Phone Number
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.

### 5. Favorite Part
- **Favorite Part** (`favorite_part`, select_one, not required): Select the part of the film that you enjoyed the most.

### 6. Least Favorite Part
- **Least Favorite Part** (`least_fav_part`, select_one, not required): Select the part of the film that you enjoyed the least.

### 7. Why Leave
- **Why Leave** (`why_leave`, text, not required): If you didn't finish watching the film, please explain why.

### 8. Overall Rating
- **Overall Rating** (`rating`, number, not required): Rate the film on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being the highest rating.

### 9. Would Rewatch
- **Would Rewatch** (`would_rewatch`, select_one, not required): Select if you'd like to re-watch the film.

### 10. Comments
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Share any additional comments or feedback about the film.

### 11. Would Recommend
- **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, not required): Let us know if you'd recommend the film to others.

### 12. Share on Social
- **Share on Social** (`share_on_social`, select_one, not required): Select how you'd like to share the film on social media.

### 13. Share on Email
- **Share on Email** (`share_on_email`, select_one, not required): Select if you'd like to share the film via email.

### 14. Watch on Streaming
- **Watch on Streaming** (`watch_on_streaming`, select_one, not required): Select if you'd like to watch the film on streaming platforms.

### 15. Watch on TV
- **Watch on TV** (`watch_on_tv`, select_one, not required): Select if you'd like to watch the film on TV.

### 16. Buy on DVD/BluRay
- **Buy on DVD/BluRay** (`buy_on_dvd_bluray`, select_one, not required): Select if you'd like to buy the film on DVD/BluRay.

### 17. Share on Facebook
- **Share on Facebook** (`share_on_facebook`, select_one, not required): Select if you'd like to share the film on Facebook.

### 18. Share on Twitter
- **Share on Twitter** (`share_on_twitter`, select_one, not required): Select if you'd like to share the film on Twitter.

### 19. Share on Instagram
- **Share on Instagram** (`share_on_instagram`, select_one, not required): Select if you'd like to share the film on Instagram.

### 20. Watch on VOD
- **Watch on VOD** (`watch_on_vod`, select_one, not required): Select if you'd like to watch the film on VOD platforms.

### 21. Watch on Other Platforms
- **Watch on Other Platforms** (`watch_on_other_platforms`, select_one, not required): Select if you'd like to watch the film on other platforms.

### 22. Other Platforms
- **Other Platforms** (`other_platforms`, text, not required): Enter any other platforms you'd like to watch the film on.

### 23. Comments
- **Comments** (`comments_other`, text, not required): Share any additional comments or feedback about the film.

### 24. Would Watch Again
- **Would Watch Again** (`watch_again`, select_one, not required): Let us know if you'd like to watch the film again.

**Tips**: Take your time and answer honestly, and feel free to add any additional comments or feedback. Your input is greatly appreciated, and it will help us improve future short film productions.
