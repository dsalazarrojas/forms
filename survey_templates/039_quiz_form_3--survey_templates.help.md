# Quiz Form 3 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Quiz Form 3" is a survey template designed to gather information from users in a structured and organized manner. This form is used to collect different types of responses, including text, multiple-choice, and numerical answers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your response to each question in the text fields (e.g., question_1, question_2, question_5, question_6, question_8, question_10, and question_11).
2. Select one of the options for the multiple-choice question (question_4).
3. Select multiple answers for the select_multiple question (question_5).
4. Enter a numerical answer for the number questions (question_6 and question_9).
5. Fill in the date for the date question (question_7).
6. Enter a note for the note question (question_9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title (1)** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for the form. This will be displayed at the top of the form.
* **Question 1 (2)** (`question_1`, text, required: false): Answer this question in the text field below.
* **Question 2 (3)** (`question_2`, text, required: false): Answer this question in the text field below.
* **Question 3 (4)** (`question_3`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options (A, B, C, D) for this question.
* **Question 4 (5)** (`question_4`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple answers (X, Y, Z) for this question.
* **Question 5 (6)** (`question_5`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical answer to this question.
* **Date Question (7)** (`question_7`, date, required: false): Fill in the date for this question.
* **Time Question (8)** (`question_8`, time, required: false): Enter a time for this question.
* **Note Question (9)** (`question_9`, number, required: false): Enter a note for this question.
* **Question 10 (10)** (`question_10`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Question 11 (11)** (`question_11`, text, required: false): Enter your answer to this question in the text field below.
