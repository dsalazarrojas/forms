# Focus Group Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants, researchers, and reviewers involved in a focus group study. The form includes various fields to collect data on participant demographics, focus group study details, consent, and review information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information in the "Participant Information" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section if required.
3. Add any additional focus group information in the "Focus Group Information" section if required.
4. Review the focus group study terms and conditions and check the "Participant Consent" box to confirm your understanding.
5. Add your statement of understanding in the "Participant Statement" section.
6. If applicable, add your signature in the "Participant Signature" section.
7. Fill in your researcher information in the "Researcher Information" section if required.
8. Review the focus group study terms and conditions and check the "Researcher Consent" box to confirm your understanding.
9. Add your statement of understanding in the "Researcher Statement" section.
10. If applicable, add your signature in the "Researcher Signature" section.
11. Fill in your reviewer information in the "Review Information" section if required.
12. Review the focus group study terms and conditions and check the "Reviewer Consent" box to confirm your understanding.
13. Add your statement of understanding in the "Reviewer Statement" section.
14. If applicable, add your signature in the "Reviewer Signature" section.
15. Add any review notes or comments in the "Review Notes" and "Review Comments" sections if required.
16. If applicable, add your review comments in the "Date Review Comments" and "Time Review Comments" sections.
17. Finally, add your review comments and notes in the "User Review Comments" and "Review Notes" sections if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide your personal and demographic information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide your contact details, if required.
* **Focus Group Information** (`focus_group_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide more details about the focus group you are participating in.
* **Study Information** (`study_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide information about the study you are participating in.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `text`, optional): This is a critical section where you must review and understand the focus group study terms and conditions.
* **Participant Statement** (`participant_statement`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to declare your understanding of the focus group study terms and conditions.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to sign and confirm your participation in the focus group study.
* **Researcher Information** (`researcher_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide your contact details and information about yourself as a researcher.
* **Researcher Consent** (`researcher_consent`, `text`, optional): This is a critical section where you must review and understand the focus group study terms and conditions.
* **Researcher Statement** (`researcher_statement`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to declare your understanding of the focus group study terms and conditions.
* **Researcher Signature** (`researcher_signature`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to sign and confirm your participation as a researcher in the focus group study.
* **Review Information** (`review_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide your contact details and information about yourself as a reviewer.
* **Reviewer Consent** (`reviewer_consent`, `text`, optional): This is a critical section where you must review and understand the focus group study terms and conditions.
* **Reviewer Statement** (`reviewer_statement`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to declare your understanding of the focus group study terms and conditions.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to add any review notes or comments.
* **Review Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to add any review comments.
* **Date Review Comments** (`date_review_comments`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to add any review comments on a specific date.
* **Time Review Comments** (`time_review_comments`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to add any review comments on a specific time.
* **User Review Comments** (`user_review_comments`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to add any review comments as a user.
* **User Signature** (`user_signature`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to sign and confirm your participation as a user in the focus group study.

## Tips

* Please review the focus group study terms and conditions carefully before signing any sections.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Sign and date all required sections.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrators.
* The form is designed to be completed by participants, researchers, and reviewers. Please ensure that you fill in the correct sections for your role.
