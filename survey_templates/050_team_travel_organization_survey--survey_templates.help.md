# Team Travel Organization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Travel Organization Survey is designed to collect information about team member travel plans, including flight details, travel agency used, travel class, and other relevant details. This data will help team managers and administrators better organize and budget for team travels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Team Travel Organization Survey, follow these steps:

* Enter the name of the team member(s) traveling (Team Traveler).
* Enter the person submitting the report (Reporter).
* Provide a brief note about the trip details (Trip Details).
* Select the travel dates for the trip.
* Enter the flight details, including the flight number.
* Choose the travel agency used for the trip (Travel Agency).
* Select the travel class for the team member(s) (Travel Class).
* Choose the travel cost range for the trip (Travel Cost).
* Select the number of team members traveling (Travelers Count).
* Specify the type of trip (Travel Type).
* Select the travel dates for the trip (Travel Dates).
* Enter any additional notes about the trip (Notes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Traveler**: Enter the name of the team member(s) traveling. This field is used to identify the team member(s) on the trip.
* **Reporter**: Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the report. This field is used to keep track of who submitted the report.
* **Trip Details**: Provide a brief note about the trip details, such as destination or purpose of the trip. This field is optional but helps in organizing the trip information.
* **Travel Dates**: Select the travel dates for the trip. This field is used to keep track of the start and end dates of the trip.
* **Flight Details**: Enter the flight details, including the flight number. This field is used to track the flight information.
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number for the trip. This field is used to track the flight number.
* **Travel Agency**: Choose the travel agency used for the trip (American, Enterprise, Delta, or Southwest). This field is required to track the travel agency used.
* **Travel Class**: Select the travel class for the team member(s) (Economy, First Class, or Business Class). This field is required to track the travel class.
* **Travel Cost**: Choose the travel cost range for the trip (0-$20, $20-$40, or $40-$60). This field is required to track the travel cost.
* **Travelers Count**: Enter the number of team members traveling. This field is optional but helps in organizing the trip details.
* **Travel Type**: Select the type of trip (Team Trip, Tournament, Retreat, or Other). This field is required to track the trip type.
* **Travel Dates 2**: Select the travel dates for the trip. This field is optional and duplicates the travel dates field.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the trip. This field is optional and helps in keeping track of any additional trip details.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about the team member(s) traveling and the trip details.
* Double-check the travel agency, travel class, and travel cost before submitting the report.
* Ensure that the travel dates and travel type are accurate to avoid any errors in trip organization and budgeting.
