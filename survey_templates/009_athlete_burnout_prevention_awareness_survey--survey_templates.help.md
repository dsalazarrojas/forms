# Athlete Burnout Prevention Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey helps to understand and prevent athlete burnout by gathering information from athletes about their experiences with burnout, perceptions of current prevention strategies, and potential initiatives that could be helpful.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section of the form carefully, and answer all questions thoroughly.
2. Select the options that best match your experiences and perspectives.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses, as they are confidential.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Introduction** (`survey_intro`): This section is a brief introduction to the survey and explains its purpose.
* **What is your primary sport?** (`primary_sport`): Please select the sport you primarily participate in.
* **What is your current level of competition?** (`competition_level`): Select the level of competition you are currently participating in.
* **In your own words, how would you define athlete burnout?** (`burnout_definition`): Please provide your definition of burnout in your sport.
* **How familiar are you with the clinical signs of burnout?** (`awareness_level`): Select how familiar you are with the clinical signs of burnout, from "Not familiar" to "Very familiar."
* **Have you felt physically or emotionally exhausted by your sport in the last 6 months?** (`exhaustion_frequency`): Select how frequently you have felt exhausted by your sport in the last 6 months.
* **Which factors contribute most to stress in your sport?** (`stress_factors`): Select all the factors that contribute to stress in your sport, such as performance pressure, time commitments, and coaching pressure.
* **Do you feel you have adequate support systems to manage sports-related stress?** (`support_system_rating`): Select how much support you feel you have to manage sports-related stress.
* **How many hours per week do you dedicate to training and competition?** (`training_hours`): Enter the number of hours per week you dedicate to training and competition.
* **Do you typically have at least one full day of rest per week?** (`rest_day_status`): Select whether you typically have at least one full day of rest per week.
* **What prevention strategies do you currently use?** (`current_strategies`): Select all the prevention strategies you currently use, such as meditation or mindfulness.
* **Which initiatives would be most helpful for burnout prevention in your program?** (`preferred_initiatives`): Select the initiatives that you believe would be most helpful for burnout prevention in your program.
* **Would you like to receive information about upcoming wellness workshops?** (`workshop_interest`): Select whether you would like to receive information about upcoming wellness workshops.
* **Contact Email Address (Optional)** (`contact_email`): If you would like to receive workshop information, please enter your email address.
* **Do you have any additional comments or concerns regarding athlete well-being?** (`additional_comments`): Please use this field to add any additional comments or concerns you have about athlete well-being.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses, as they are confidential.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the survey administrators.
* Your responses will help inform the development of programs and resources to support athlete well-being.
