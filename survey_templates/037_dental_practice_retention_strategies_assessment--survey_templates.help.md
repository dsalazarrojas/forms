# Dental Practice Retention Strategies Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the retention strategies and efforts made by your dental practice. It aims to identify areas where you can improve to retain patients and increase overall satisfaction. By filling out this form, you can gain insights into your practice's strengths and weaknesses, and create a better experience for both patients and staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the answer that best describes your current patient satisfaction level on page 1 (Patient Satisfaction).
2. Describe the efforts you've made to retain current patients on page 2 (Retention Efforts).
3. Enter your staff satisfaction score on page 3 (Staff Satisfaction). Note that this page is optional.
4. Select the scores for Patient Satisfaction Score and Patient Retention Score on pages 4 and 5, respectively.
5. Choose one or multiple retention strategies and efforts that you use on pages 6 (Retention Strategy) and 7 (Retention Efforts), respectively. These include Social Media, Referrals, Word of Mouth, Patient Education, Staff, and Other.
6. Describe areas where you can improve on page 8 (Areas of Improvement).
7. Choose one of the following action plans for your next steps on page 9 (Action Plan): Short-Term, Mid-Term, Long-Term, or None.
8. Select the next steps you'll take on page 10 (Next Steps). You can choose one or multiple options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Satisfaction** (Page 1): Select one of the following options:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied

This field measures your patients' overall satisfaction level with your dental practice.

* **Retention Efforts** (Page 2): Describe the efforts you've made to retain current patients. For example, you may have implemented a loyalty program, offered discounts, or provided exceptional customer service.

* **Staff Satisfaction** (Page 3): Enter a numerical score between 1-5, where 1 is Very Dissatisfied and 5 is Very Satisfied. This field is optional, but it helps to identify potential staff dissatisfaction that may affect patient retention.

* **Patient Satisfaction Score** (Page 4): Enter a numerical score between 1-5, where 1 is Very Dissatisfied and 5 is Very Satisfied.

This field measures your patients' overall satisfaction level, based on their responses to your practice's services.

* **Retention Strategy** (Page 6): Choose one or multiple options from the following:
	+ Social Media
	+ Referrals
	+ Word of Mouth
	+ Patient Education
	+ Staff
	+ Other

These are the strategies you use to retain patients and increase loyalty.

* **Retention Efforts** (Page 7): Choose one or multiple options from the following:
	+ Social Media
	+ Referrals
	+ Word of Mouth
	+ Patient Education
	+ Staff
	+ Other

These are the efforts you make to retain patients and increase loyalty.

* **Areas of Improvement** (Page 8): Describe areas where you can improve patient satisfaction and retention. For example, you may need to improve communication, provide better customer service, or enhance patient education.

* **Action Plan** (Page 9): Choose one of the following options for your next steps:
	+ Short-Term
	+ Mid-Term
	+ Long-Term
	+ None

This field determines the timeframe for your next steps.

* **Next Steps** (Page 10): Choose one or multiple options from the following:
	+ Short-Term
	+ Mid-Term
	+ Long-Term
	+ None

These are the next actions you'll take to improve patient retention and satisfaction.
