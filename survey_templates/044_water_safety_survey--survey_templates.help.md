# Water Safety Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who have participated in water activities to assess their safety knowledge and experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please select a location where the incident occurred**: Choose between "pool", "beach", or "river".
2. **Enter the participant's name**: Type in the name of the participant who is completing the form.
3. **Enter the participant's age**: Enter the age of the participant.
4. **Select the participant's location**: Choose the location where the participant was when the incident occurred.
5. **Select the participant's rescuer's location**: Choose the location where the participant's rescuer was when they arrived.
6. **Enter the participant's rescuer's phone number and email**: Provide the contact information of the participant's rescuer.
7. **Select whether the participant was rescued**: Choose "rescued" or "not rescued".
8. **Enter the time of rescue**: Type in the time when the participant was rescued.
9. **Enter the rescuer's information**: Type in the information provided by the rescuer when they arrived.
10. **Select the types of safety concerns**: Choose between "drowning", "entrapment", or "other" for the types of safety concerns experienced by the participant.
11. **Enter a description of other safety concerns**: If the participant experiences "other" safety concerns, provide a description of what happened.
12. **Enter the participant's safety knowledge and experience**: Rate the participant's safety knowledge and experience on a scale.
13. **Enter the type of watercraft used**: Type in the type of watercraft used by the participant.
14. **Enter the size and length of the watercraft**: Type in the size and length of the watercraft.
15. **Enter the type of vessel**: Type in the type of vessel used by the participant.
16. **Enter the length and units of the vessel**: Enter the length of the vessel and select the units.
17. **Enter the rescue method**: Type in how the participant was rescued.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name.
* **Participant Age**: Enter your age.
* **Location of the incident (pool, beach, river)**: Where did the incident occur? Choose between "pool", "beach", or "river".
* **Participant Rescuer's Location**: Where was your rescuer when they arrived?
* **Participant Rescuer's Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your rescuer.
* **Participant Rescuer's Email**: Enter the email of your rescuer.
* **Participant Rescued**: Were you rescued? Choose "rescued" or "not rescued".
* **Time of Rescue**: When were you rescued?
* **Participant Safety Concerns**: Choose between "drowning", "entrapment", or "other" for the types of safety concerns experienced by the participant.
* **Other Safety Concerns**: If you chose "other" safety concerns, describe what happened.
* **Participant Safety Knowledge**: Rate your safety knowledge and experience on a scale.
* **Type of Watercraft**: What type of watercraft did you use?
* **Watercraft Size**: Enter the size of the watercraft.
* **Watercraft Length**: Enter the length of the watercraft.
* **Watercraft Length Units**: Choose the units for the length of the watercraft.
* **Other Watercraft Length Units**: If you selected "other" units, enter the units used.
* **Rescue Method**: How were you rescued?
