# Chiropractic Referral Marketing Software Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness and value of a chiropractic referral marketing software. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each field with the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Evaluator Name** (`Evaluator Name`, `text`, required): The name of the person completing the evaluation.
- **Organization** (`Organization`, `text`, optional): The name of the clinic or practice.
- **Software Name** (`Software Name`, `text`, required): The name of the product being evaluated.
- **Ease of Use** (`Ease of Use`, `select_one`, required): Rate the software's user-friendliness, with options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
- **Referral Tracking Capability** (`Referral Tracking Capability`, `select_one`, required): Answer "True" if it tracks referrals effectively, "Partial" if it partially tracks referrals, or "False" if it doesn't track referrals at all.
- **Integration Options** (`Integration Options`, `text`, optional): Note any integrations supported, e.g. EHR, CRM.
- **Value for Money** (`Value for Money`, `select_one`, required): Rate the cost-effectiveness of the software, with options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
- **Overall Recommendation** (`Overall Recommendation`, `select_one`, required): Answer "True" if you would recommend this product, or "False" otherwise.
