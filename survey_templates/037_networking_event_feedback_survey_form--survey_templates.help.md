# Networking Event Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from attendees of a networking event. It helps us understand the attendees' experiences and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event you attended.
2. Fill in your name as an attendee.
3. Provide your contact email address.
4. Share your thoughts and feedback about the event.
5. Rate your experience by selecting a number between 1 and 5.
6. Add any comments you'd like to make about the event.
7. Indicate whether you attended or not by selecting one of the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Name (Field 1)
* **Event Name**: The name of the event you attended.
*   Type: Text field
*   Required: No

### Attendee Name (Field 2)
* **Attendee Name**: Your name as an attendee.
*   Type: Text field
*   Required: No

### Contact Email (Field 3)
* **Contact Email**: Your email address.
*   Type: Email field
*   Required: No

### Event Feedback (Field 4)
* **Event Feedback**: Share your thoughts and feedback about the event.
*   Type: Text field
*   Required: No

### Rate Event (Field 5)
* **Rate Event**: Select a number between 1 and 5 to rate your experience.
*   Type: Number field
*   Required: No

### Comments (Field 6)
* **Comments**: Add any comments you'd like to make about the event.
*   Type: Text field
*   Required: No

### Attended (Field 7)
* **Attended**: Indicate if you attended or not.
*   Type: Select multiple options
*   Required: No

## Tips
* Please be as concise as possible when filling in the feedback fields.
* Make sure to enter the correct event name if possible.
* Your feedback will help us improve future events.
* If you're not sure about a field, you can leave it blank.
