# Website User Acceptance Testing Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback on website usability, bugs, and overall satisfaction after a user acceptance testing (UAT) process. The feedback collected will help identify areas of improvement and provide a rating system for the website's functionality and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form after a user acceptance testing (UAT) process has been completed.
2. Answer the questions to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If a field is not relevant to your experience, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Usability** (`website_usability`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can describe any issues or concerns you have with the website's usability. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Bugs** (`bugs`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can report any bugs or issues you encountered during the testing process. Please describe the bug and provide any steps you took to reproduce it.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can express your overall satisfaction with the website. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Website Usability Rating** (`website_usability_rating`, number, optional):
	+ Please rate the website's usability on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
* **Website Usability Rating Value** (`website_usability_rating_value`, select_one, required):
	+ This field will ask you to provide a more detailed rating for the website's usability, with options 1-5 and 6-10. Please select the range that best fits your experience.
* **Bugs Rating** (`bugs_rating`, number, optional):
	+ Please rate the number of bugs you encountered during the testing process on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very few and 5 being many.
* **Bugs Rating Value** (`bugs_rating_value`, select_one, required):
	+ This field will ask you to provide a more detailed rating for the number of bugs, with options 1-5 and 6-10. Please select the range that best fits your experience.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, optional):
	+ Please rate your overall satisfaction with the website on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 5 being very satisfied.
* **Satisfaction Rating Value** (`satisfaction_rating_value`, select_one, required):
	+ This field will ask you to provide a more detailed rating for your satisfaction, with options 1-5 and 6-10. Please select the range that best fits your experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any additional feedback or comments, please enter them here. This is a great place to elaborate on any points you made in the previous fields or mention anything else that's important.
