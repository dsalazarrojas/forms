# Mobile Phone Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and preference data from mobile phone users for research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus and radio buttons.
3. Enter your mobile phone model in the text field.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name as you would like to be referred to.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your mobile phone number for contact purposes.
