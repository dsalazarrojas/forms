# net_promoter_score_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The net_promoter_score_questionnaire is a survey designed to measure customer satisfaction and the likelihood of customers recommending a business. By answering the following questions, you will help us understand how we can improve our services and offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you're answering all the questions truthfully.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience with the business.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Satisfaction
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks you to choose how satisfied you are with your recent visit or interaction with our business. Please select all options that apply to your experience, such as "Satisfied" and/or "Dissatisfied".

### How likely are you to recommend this business?
* **How likely are you to recommend this business?** (`question1`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to rate how likely you are to recommend our business on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is "not at all likely" and 10 is "extremely likely".

### How likely is it that you will continue to do business with us?
* **How likely is it that you will continue to do business with us?** (`question2`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to select whether you are likely or unlikely to continue doing business with us.

### What was the main reason for your recent visit or interaction with our business?
* **What was the main reason for your recent visit or interaction with our business?** (`question3`, `text`, required: false): This field is a free text field where you can provide a brief description of the main reason for your recent visit or interaction with our business.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can include them in the "What was the main reason for your recent visit or interaction with our business?" field.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification or skip it if you're unable to answer it.
