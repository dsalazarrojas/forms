# Contest Preparation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your preparation and expectations for the contest. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question as it appears, and make sure to provide the required information. If you need help or clarification, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name (1)
* **Participant Name** (text, required): This is your full name that we will use to address you.

### Email Address (2)
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.

### Contest Name (3)
* **Contest Name** (text, required): Please specify the name of the contest you are entering.

### Contest Category (4)
* **Contest Category** (select_one, required): Choose the category your contest falls under (Art, Photography, Writing, Music, Dance, Innovation, Sports).

### Experience Level (5)
* **Experience Level** (select_one, required): Select your experience level in this category (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional).

### Previous Contests (6)
* **Previous Contests** (select_one, required): Choose how many contests you have entered in the past.

### Preparation Time (7)
* **Preparation Time** (select_one, required): Indicate the amount of time you spent preparing for this contest.

### Preparation Areas (8)
* **Preparation Areas** (select_multiple, required): Select all the areas you have prepared for this contest (Practice Performances, Equipment setup, Costume or attire, Rehearsals, Research, Coaching, Material creation).

### Confidence Level (9)
* **Confidence Level** (select_one, required): Choose how confident you feel about your performance.

### Support Needed (10)
* **Support Needed** (select_multiple, not required): Select any support you may need (Technical assistance, Equipment rental, Practice space, Coaching, Transportation, Accommodation, None).

### Special Requirements (11)
* **Special Requirements** (text, not required): Enter any special requirements or needs you have (e.g., accessibility needs).

### Travel Distance (12)
* **Travel Distance** (select_one, not required): Indicate how far you are traveling for this contest (Local, Regional, Statewide, Out of state, International).

### Accommodation Needed (13)
* **Accommodation Needed** (select_one, not required): If you need lodging assistance, choose this option.

### Team or Individual (14)
* **Team or Individual** (select_one, required): Indicate if you are competing alone or with a team (Individual, Team, Duo).

### Team Size (15)
* **Team Size** (number, not required): Enter the number of team members (if applicable).

### Contest Goals (16)
* **Contest Goals** (select_multiple, required): Choose your goals for this contest (Win first place, Place in top 3, Gain experience, Network with others, Win a prize, Personal achievement).

### Concerns or Questions (17)
* **Concerns or Questions** (text, not required): Enter any concerns or questions you may have.

### Communication Preference (18)
* **Communication Preference** (select_one, required): Choose how you would prefer to be contacted (Email, Phone call, Text message, Social media).

This form is designed to be completed by the participant, and the information collected will help the contest organizers understand their preparation and expectations. Please fill out the form carefully and honestly, and do not hesitate to ask if you need any clarification.
