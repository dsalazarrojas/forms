# Travel Itinerary Planning Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is meant to collect feedback from travelers about their experiences with travel itineraries. We want to know how we can improve the planning process, what you liked or disliked about the itinerary, and whether you would recommend the travel planning services to others.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form with your honest feedback.
2. Answer all questions that apply.
3. Choose the best option from the select one or multiple fields.
4. Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Itinerary Planning Feedback Form** (`travel_itinerary_planning_feedback_form`): This is the title of the form, and you can use it as a prompt to start writing your feedback.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (`overall_experience_rating`): Please select how satisfied you were with your travel itinerary. You can choose between "Very satisfied", "Neutral", or "Dissatisfied".
* **Travel Itinerary Planning Feedback** (`travel_itinerary_planning_feedback`): Share any specific feedback you have about your travel itinerary.
* **Feedback Text** (`feedback_text`): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experience with us.
* **Would You Recommend This Travel Itinerary?** (`travel_itinerary_planning_feedback_form_2`): Indicate whether you would recommend our travel planning services to others. You can select "True", "False", or "Maybe".
* **Overall Experience Rating2** (`overall_experience_rating2`): Please select how satisfied you were with your travel itinerary. You can choose between "Very satisfied", "Neutral", or "Dissatisfied".
* **Travel Itinerary Planning Feedback2** (`travel_itinerary_planning_feedback2`): Share any specific feedback you have about your travel itinerary.
* **Feedback Text2** (`feedback_text2`): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experience with us.
* **Travel Itinerary Planning Feedback Form 3** (`travel_itinerary_planning_feedback_form_3`): This is another opportunity to share your feedback about our travel planning services.
