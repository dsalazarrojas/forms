# Laptop Research Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, titled "Laptop Research Survey", is designed to collect user input on laptop preferences and usage habits. Please refer to the project stakeholders or form creator to confirm the form's purpose and potential data requirements have not changed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select one of the pages to start filling in: <code>survey_laptop_usage</code>, <code>survey_laptop_preferences</code>, <code>survey_laptop_brand</code>, <code>survey_laptop_purchase_year</code>, <code>survey_laptop_usage_frequency</code>, <code>survey_laptop_satisfaction</code>, <code>survey_laptop_purchase_price</code>, <code>survey_laptop_recommended</code>
*   Fill out the form fields as per your knowledge of the subject.
*   Ensure all required fields (<code>required</code> fields) are filled before proceeding to the next page or submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Laptop Usage** (<code>survey_laptop_usage</code>, text, optional):
    This field is for you to describe your usage habits with the laptop. Please be as detailed as possible.
*   **Laptop Preferences** (<code>survey_laptop_preferences</code>, select multiple, optional):
    Select all the preferences that apply to your laptop, such as whether you have used the laptop, if it is a personal or work laptop, and so on.
*   **Laptop Brand** (<code>survey_laptop_brand</code>, select one, optional):
    Choose the brand of the laptop you use or have used.
*   **Purchase Year** (<code>survey_laptop_purchase_year</code>, number, optional):
    If you have purchased a laptop, provide the year you purchased it.
*   **Usage Frequency** (<code>survey_laptop_usage_frequency</code>, text, optional):
    Describe how often you use the laptop, such as daily, weekly, or less than once a week.
*   **Satisfaction** (<code>survey_laptop_satisfaction</code>, select one, optional):
    Select how satisfied you are with your laptop, from very satisfied to very dissatisfied.
*   **Purchase Price** (<code>survey_laptop_purchase_price</code>, number, optional):
    If applicable, provide the price you paid for the laptop. This field is not directly related to any option provided, so please enter the actual price or the range of prices if you are unsure.
*   **Recommended** (<code>survey_laptop_recommended</code>, select one, optional):
    If you think you would recommend the laptop to others, select true; otherwise, select false or if you are undecided.
