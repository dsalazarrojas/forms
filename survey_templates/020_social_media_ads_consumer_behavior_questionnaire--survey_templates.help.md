# social-media-ads-consumer-behavior-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about consumer behavior related to social media ads. The purpose of this form is to collect data on users' trust in social media ads, their engagement with ads, and factors that affect their purchase intent and engagement. This data can be used to improve the effectiveness of social media marketing campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introductory page carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly, keeping in mind that the data collected will be used for research purposes only.
3. Please note that the form has 20 questions in total, each one asking about a specific topic related to social media ads.
4. Make sure to answer each question according to the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **intro-page** (`intro_page`, `note`, required/optional): This is the introductory page of the form, providing a brief overview of the purpose and structure of the questionnaire.
* **trust in social media** (`trust_in_social_media`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks how much you trust social media ads in general. Answer according to your personal opinion and experience.
* **ad trust** (`ad_trust_ads`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks if you consider ads on social media trustworthy. Choose one of the options provided.
* **ad engagement** (`ad_engagement_ads`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks how often you engage with ads on social media. Choose all applicable options that best fit your behavior.
* **ad engagement 2** (`ad_engagement_ads_2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Similar to the previous question, this one asks how often you engage with ads on social media.
* **purchase intent** (`purchase_intent_ads`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks about the factors that affect your purchase intent when seeing ads on social media. Choose all applicable options.
* **purchase intent 2** (`purchase_intent_ads_2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Similar to the previous question, this one asks about how often you buy after seeing ads on social media.
* **ad trust factors** (`ad_trust_factors`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks about the factors that affect your trust in ads on social media. Choose all applicable options.
* **engagement factors ads** (`engagement_factors_ads`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks about the factors that affect your engagement with ads on social media. Choose all applicable options.
* **purchase factors ads** (`purchase_factors_ads`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks about the factors that affect your purchase intent on social media. Choose all applicable options.
* **demographics** (`demographics_1`, `note`, required/optional): This is the demographics page of the form, providing a brief overview of the questions related to your demographic information.
* **gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks about your gender. Choose one of the provided options.
* **age range** (`age_range`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks about your age range. Choose all applicable options that best fit your age.
* **location** (`location`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks about your location.
* **income range** (`income_range_1`, `number`, required/optional): This question asks about your income range.
* **occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks about your occupation.
* **purchase habit** (`purchase_habit_ads`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks about your purchase habit on social media.
* **purchase habit 2** (`purchase_habit_ads_2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Similar to the previous question, this one asks about how often you make purchases on social media.
* **satisfaction with ads** (`purchase_ads_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks about your satisfaction with ads on social media. Choose all applicable options.
* **satisfaction with ads 2** (`purchase_ads_satisfaction_2`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks about how satisfied you are with ads on social media. Choose one of the provided options.
