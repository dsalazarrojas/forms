# Appointment Reminder Method Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a poll to gather information about respondents' preferred methods for receiving appointment reminders. The data collected will help the organization better understand the needs and expectations of its clients and improve its services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by providing your name and email address (required fields).
2. Answer the questions about your appointment frequency, preferred reminder method, and preferred reminder timing.
3. Select all the information you would like to receive in the reminder (optional).
4. Indicate if you would like reminders to request appointment confirmation (optional).
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving appointment reminders (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`respondent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **How often do you have appointments?** (`appointment_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you have appointments. Options: Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Occasionally.
* **Which reminder method do you prefer?** (`preferred_reminder_method`, select_one, required): Select your top choice for reminder method. Options: Email, SMS text message, Phone call, App notification, Calendar invite, No reminder.
* **What is your second preference?** (`second_preference`, select_one, optional): Select your second choice for reminder method. Options: Email, SMS text message, Phone call, App notification, Calendar invite, No preference.
* **When would you prefer to be reminded?** (`reminder_timing`, select_one, required): Select when you would prefer to be reminded. Options: 24 hours before, 12 hours before, 2 days before, Week before, Day of appointment, Multiple times.
* **Language Preference** (`language_preference`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred language for reminders. Options: English, Spanish, French, German, Other.
* **Do you have accessibility needs?** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, optional): Check if you have any accessibility needs. Options: Yes - visual impairment, Yes - hearing impairment, Yes - other needs, No special needs.
* **Preferred accommodation for reminders** (`preferred_accommodation`, text, optional): Enter any specific accommodation you require for reminders.
* **What information should the reminder include?** (`content_preference`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the information you would like to receive in the reminder. Options: Appointment date and time, Provider name, Location and directions, Preparation instructions, Insurance information, Cancellation instructions.
* **Would you like reminders to request appointment confirmation?** (`confirmation_request`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you would like reminders to request appointment confirmation. Options: True, False, No preference.
* **How can we improve appointment reminders?** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improving appointment reminders.
* **Any additional comments or suggestions?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
