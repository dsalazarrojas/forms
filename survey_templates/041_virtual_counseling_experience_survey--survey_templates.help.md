# Virtual Counseling Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a collection of questions designed to gather feedback on your virtual counseling experience. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our users in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the appropriate options for each field and provide a response that best describes your experience. You can select multiple options for the "Improvement Areas" field. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please feel free to provide them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Survey Title**
* (`survey_title`, text, optional): This field is a text box where you can provide a brief title for this specific feedback submission. It is not required, so you can leave it blank if you prefer.

### 2. **Session Type**
* (`session_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of session you experienced. The options are:
	+ In-person
	+ Phone
	+ Video
	+ Online

### 3. **Technology Satisfaction**
* (`technology_rating`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the technology used during the session. Please enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".

### 4. **Session Satisfaction**
* (`session_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the session as a whole. Please enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".

### 5. **Improvement Areas**
* (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Identify areas for improvement in our services. Please select all that apply:
	+ Technology
	+ Communication
	+ Availability
	+ Other (please specify in the "Comments" field)

### 6. **Comments**
* (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or concerns you may have about your virtual counseling experience.
