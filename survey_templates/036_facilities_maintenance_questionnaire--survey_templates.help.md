# Facilities Maintenance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about the maintenance frequency and condition of facilities within our organization. The purpose of this form is to:

* Assess the current maintenance frequency of facilities
* Gather information on recent maintenance requests and their status
* Evaluate the overall condition of facilities and identify potential areas for improvement
* Record maintenance dates and schedules

Please ensure you are completing this form accurately and thoroughly to provide valuable insights for facility management and maintenance decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. Select the correct options for each question based on your experience and knowledge.
3. Enter your answers in the designated fields.
4. Submit the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the maintenance frequency of our facilities?** (Select One):
	+ Maintenance Weekly: Regular maintenance is performed on a weekly basis.
	+ Maintenance Monthly: Regular maintenance is performed on a monthly basis.
	+ Maintenance Yearly: Regular maintenance is performed on a yearly basis.
* **How often do you perform routine maintenance on our facilities?** (Select Multiple):
	+ Maintenance Weekly: Perform maintenance on a weekly basis.
	+ Maintenance Monthly: Perform maintenance on a monthly basis.
	+ Maintenance Yearly: Perform maintenance on a yearly basis.
* **Have you experienced any maintenance requests recently?** (Select Multiple):
	+ 'True': Yes, we have experienced maintenance requests.
	+ 'False': No, we have not experienced maintenance requests.
* **Describe any maintenance requests you have made in the past week.** (Text Field): Enter a brief description of recent maintenance requests.
* **How would you rate the overall condition of our facilities?** (Select One):
	+ 'True': Facilities are in good condition.
	+ 'False': Facilities are not in good condition.
* **What is the last maintenance date for our facilities?** (Date Field): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed on facilities.
* **What is the next scheduled maintenance date for our facilities?** (Date Field): Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance.
* **Describe the current condition of our facilities.** (Text Field): Enter a brief description of the current condition of facilities.
* **How would you rate the frequency of maintenance requests?** (Select Multiple):
	+ 'True': Yes, maintenance requests are frequent.
	+ 'False': No, maintenance requests are not frequent.
* **What is the current condition of our facilities?** (Select One):
	+ 'True': Facilities are in good condition.
	+ 'False': Facilities are not in good condition.
* **Facility Maintenance Questionnaire 11** (Date Field): Enter the date for this field is unclear and should be removed or clarified.
* **Facility Maintenance Questionnaire 12** (Select Multiple): This field is unclear. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Maintenance Weekly
	+ Maintenance Monthly
	+ Maintenance Yearly

**Tips**

* Be accurate and thorough in your answers.
* If unsure, refer to your knowledge and experience with facilities maintenance.
* Submit the form when completed.
