# Victimology Concepts Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about victimology concepts, challenges, and awareness. It is meant to be used to collect data on the subject matter of victimology, which focuses on the dynamics of crime and victimization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Victimology Knowledge" dropdown menu to indicate your level of knowledge about victimology. You can choose from "Victimology Basics", "Victimology Theories", or "Victimology Concepts".
2. In the "Key Concept 1" field, select all that apply from the options "Concept 1a", "Concept 1b", and "Concept 1c" that best describe your understanding of a specific key concept.
3. In the "Key Concept 2" field, type in your answer to the question, as it does not have a predefined option list.
4. Choose a single option from "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3" for the "Key Concept 3" field.
5. In the "System Challenges" field, type in your answer to describe any system-related challenges you are facing.
6. Enter your level of knowledge awareness in the "Victimology Knowledge Awareness" field, using a number from 1 to 10.
7. Indicate how you apply your victimology knowledge in the "Victimology Knowledge Application" field, using a number from 1 to 10.
8. In the "Victimology System Challenges" field, type in your answer to describe any system-related challenges you are facing.
9. Enter the date for the "Form Field 15" field.
10. Select all that apply from the options "Yes" and "No" for the "Form Field 14" field.
11. Select a single option from "Yes" and "No" for the "Form Field 17" field.

* **Victimology Concepts**: This field is the main topic of this form, where you can express your thoughts and ideas about victimology.
* **Victimology Knowledge**: Choose a level of knowledge about victimology to indicate your understanding of the subject matter.
* **Victimology Challenges**: Describe any challenges you are facing related to victimology.
* **Key Concept 1**: Select all that apply to describe a specific key concept.
* **Key Concept 2**: Answer this question with your understanding of the key concept.
* **Key Concept 3**: Choose a single option to describe the key concept.
* **System Challenges**: Describe any system-related challenges you are facing.
* **Victimology Knowledge Awareness**: Indicate your level of awareness of victimology knowledge, using a number from 1 to 10.
* **Victimology Knowledge Application**: Indicate how you apply your knowledge of victimology, using a number from 1 to 10.
* **Victimology System Challenges**: Describe any system-related challenges you are facing.
* **Form Field 13**: This field is not used in the current form, so do not fill it out.
* **Form Field 14**: Select all that apply from the options "Yes" and "No".
* **Form Field 15**: Enter the date for this field.
* **Form Field 16**: This field is not used in the current form, so do not fill it out.
* **Form Field 17**: Select a single option from "Yes" and "No".
