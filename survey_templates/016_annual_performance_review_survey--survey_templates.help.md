# Annual Performance Review Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to conduct annual performance reviews of employees. It gathers feedback on their accomplishments, areas for improvement, and overall performance. The form helps managers evaluate and provide constructive feedback to employees, promoting growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Reviewer: Fill in the Reviewer Name field with your full name.
2. Employee: Ensure the Employee Name field is accurate and matches the employee's ID in the HR system.
3. Progress through the form, filling out the fields as instructed:
	* Provide dates according to the format YYYY-MM-DD.
	* For select fields, choose one option from the dropdown list.
	* For text fields, provide a brief, clear description.
4. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Reviewer Name (1)
* **Reviewer Name**: Your full name as the person conducting the review.
* **Hint**: Full name of person conducting review

### Employee Name (3)
* **Employee Name**: Full name of the employee being reviewed.
* **Hint**: Name of employee being reviewed

### Department (6)
* **Department**: Identify the department the employee belongs to.
* **Hint**: null

### Review Date (7)
* **Review Date**: Date of this performance review.
* **Hint**: Date of this performance review

### Employment Start Date (8)
* **Employment Start Date**: Date the employee started in their current role.
* **Hint**: When employee started in current role

### Achievement of Annual Goals and Objectives (9)
* **Achievement of Annual Goals and Objectives**: Select from the dropdown list how the employee has met their annual goals.

* **Percentage of Goals Achieved**: Enter a numerical value (0-100) representing the percentage of goals achieved.

## Tips
- Ensure accurate and complete information throughout the form.
- Review and discuss results with the employee to foster growth and development.
- Consider the employee's feedback and areas for improvement when discussing performance and providing recommendations.
- Keep the tone professional and constructive.
