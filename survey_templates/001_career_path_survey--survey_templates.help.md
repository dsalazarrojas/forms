# Career Path Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Path Survey is designed to help you understand your current career situation and identify areas for improvement and growth. The survey will guide you through a series of questions to help you assess your current career status, skills, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your basic information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Describe your current occupation or student status.
3. Select all that apply for your primary career goals.
4. Choose your highest education level completed.
5. Enter your total years of work experience.
6. Select your primary industry interest.
7. Rate your proficiency in communication, technical, and leadership skills.
8. Identify any career challenges you are facing.
9. Select your preferred work environment.
10. Specify your expected annual salary range.
11. Choose your expected timeframe for achieving your career goals.
12. Provide any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information**: Please provide your basic information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address**: We will send results to this email address.
* **Phone Number**: Include area code.
* **Current Occupation or Student Status**: Describe your current role or field of study.
* **Career Goals**: Select all that apply to help us understand your goals.
* **Career Goals Options**: 
  - Career advancement in current field
  - Career change to new industry
  - Start own business
  - Professional certification
  - Higher education degree
  - Skill development
  - Work-life balance improvement
* **Education Level**: Select your highest completed education level.
* **Education Options**: 
  - High School Diploma
  - Some College
  - Associate Degree
  - Bachelor Degree
  - Master Degree
  - Doctorate
  - Professional Certification
* **Years of Experience**: Enter your total years in the workforce.
* **Industry Interest**: Select your primary interest area.
* **Industry Options**: 
  - Technology and IT
  - Healthcare and Medical
  - Business and Finance
  - Education and Training
  - Creative and Arts
  - Engineering and Manufacturing
  - Sales and Marketing
  - Public Service
  - Other
* **Skills Assessment**: Rate your proficiency in communication, technical, and leadership skills.
* **Communication Skills**: Select your level of proficiency in written and verbal communication.
* **Technical Skills**: Select your level of technical proficiency.
* **Leadership Skills**: Select your level of leadership proficiency.
* **Leadership Options**: 
  - Beginner
  - Intermediate
  - Advanced
  - Expert
* **Career Challenges**: Select all that apply to help us understand your challenges.
* **Career Challenges Options**: 
  - Lack of clear direction
  - Limited job opportunities
  - Insufficient qualifications
  - Work-life balance issues
  - Low compensation
  - Lack of mentorship
  - Fear of change
  - Geographic limitations
* **Preferred Work Environment**: Select where you see yourself working.
* **Preferred Work Environment Options**: 
  - Corporate office
  - Remote from home
  - Hybrid remote and office
  - Small business
  - Startup company
  - Government agency
  - Nonprofit organization
  - Self-employed
* **Salary Expectations**: Select your expected annual salary range.
* **Salary Expectations Options**: 
  - Under 30000
  - 30000 to 50000
  - 50000 to 75000
  - 75000 to 100000
  - 100000 to 150000
  - Over 150000
* **Expected Timeline**: Select your expected timeframe for achieving your career goals.
* **Expected Timeline Options**: 
  - Within 6 months
  - 6 to 12 months
  - 1 to 2 years
  - 2 to 5 years
  - 5 plus years
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or questions you have.

Note: This help guide is a comprehensive overview of the form and each field's purpose. Please review each field carefully and answer accurately to ensure the best possible outcome.
