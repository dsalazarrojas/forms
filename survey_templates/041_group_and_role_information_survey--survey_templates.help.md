# Group And Role Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about groups and their roles. It's intended to help organizations understand their team structure, member responsibilities, and group attributes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Details (page 1)**: This section is used to capture basic information about the group member, such as their name and contact details.
* **Member Roles (page 1)**: Select all roles that apply to the member from the available options.
* **Role Tenure (page 1)**: Enter the length of time the member has held their role.
* **Member Responsibilities (page 1)**: Select all responsibilities that apply to the member from the available options.
* **Group Name (page 1)**: Enter the name of the group.
* **Group Size (page 1)**: Enter the number of members in the group.
* **Group Formation (page 1)**: Enter the date the group was formed.
* **Member Email (page 2)**: Enter the member's email address.
* **Member Phone (page 2)**: Enter the member's phone number.
* **Group Description (page 2)**: Enter a brief description of the group.
* **Role Formation (page 2)**: Select whether the role was formed by the member or not.
* **Member Status (page 2)**: Select the member's status from the available options.
* **Member Category (page 2)**: Select the member's category from the available options.
* **Member Subcategory (page 2)**: Select the member's subcategory from the available options.
* **Member Address (page 3)**: Enter the member's address.
* **Group Address (page 3)**: Enter the group's address.
* **Group Formation Date (page 3)**: Enter the date the group was formed.
* **Group Start Date (page 3)**: Enter the date the group started.
* **Group End Date (page 3)**: Enter the date the group ended.
* **Role Start Date (page 4)**: Enter the date the role started.
* **Role End Date (page 4)**: Enter the date the role ended.
* **Group Status (page 4)**: Select the group's status from the available options.
