# Educational Technology Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to understand the educational technology needs of schools and educators. It will help us gather information on the level of educational technology, departmental needs, and other requirements to better support your institution's educational technology needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the educational level for which you are responding (High, Low).
2. If applicable, select the schools you are representing (Yes, No).
3. Enter the name of your school (if you selected "Yes" in the previous step).
4. Choose the departments that require technology support (Yes, No).
5. Select the technology needs (Yes, No) for your school.
6. Indicate the classroom needs (number) for your school.
7. Choose the teaching needs (yes, no) for your school's teachers.
8. Provide any other needs or comments you may have.
9. Optionally, you can add any additional comments or feedback.
10. Finally, enter your name and date to identify who submitted the survey and when.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Educational Level**: This is the first field in the form. Please select the educational level for which you are responding to this survey. This will help us understand the level of educational technology required.
	+ **High**: Select this option if your educational institution is a high school or higher.
	+ **Low**: Select this option if your educational institution is a low-level school (e.g., middle school, elementary).
* **School Name**: If you selected "Yes" in the previous field, enter the name of your school here.
* **Departments (e.g., IT, Library, Administration)**: Please select the departments that require technology support. This can include departments such as IT, Library, Administration, etc.
	+ **Yes**: Select this option if the department requires technology support.
	+ **No**: Select this option if the department does not require technology support.
* **Technology Needs**: Please select the technology needs of your school. This can include computers, internet, software, and other technical requirements.
	+ **Yes**: Select this option if your school requires the selected technology.
	+ **No**: Select this option if your school does not require the selected technology.
* **Classroom Needs**: Indicate the number of classrooms that require technology needs (e.g., 5, 10).
* **Teacher Needs**: Please select the teaching needs of your school's teachers. This can include professional development, training, and other requirements.
	+ **Yes**: Select this option if your school's teachers require the selected technology.
	+ **No**: Select this option if your school's teachers do not require the selected technology.
* **Other Needs**: If you have any other needs or comments, please provide them here.
* **Comments**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Submitted by**: Please enter your name to identify who submitted the survey.
* **Date Submitted**: This is an optional field where you can enter the date you submitted the survey.

## Tips

* Please answer all fields that are applicable to your situation.
* If a field is marked as required, please make sure to fill it out.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* The information collected will be used to better understand the needs of your educational institution and provide better support.
* Please submit the form only once for each school or educational institution.
