# Personal Story Sharing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Story Sharing Form is a simple, text-based form designed to collect personal stories from users. This form is ideal for gathering information about individuals, such as their experiences, thoughts, or feelings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **first name** in the first field. This will be your identifier in the form.
2.  Enter your email address in the second field. This will help us contact you if we need more information.
3.  Share your personal story in the third field. This is the main content of the form. Be as detailed and descriptive as you like.
4.  Enter your organization (if applicable) in the fourth field. This will help us understand your professional context.
5.  Enter your department (if applicable) in the fifth field. This will help us narrow down your context.
6.  Enter your role or position in the sixth field. This will help us understand your function within your organization.
7.  Finally, provide a title for your story in the last field. This will help us categorize and manage your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name here. This will be your identifier in the form.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address here. This will help us contact you if we need more information.
*   **Story** (`story`, text, optional): Share your personal story here. Be as detailed and descriptive as you like.
*   **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, enter it here. This will help us understand your professional context.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, enter your department here. This will help us narrow down your context.
*   **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Enter your role or position here. This will help us understand your function within your organization.
*   **Story Title** (`story_title`, text, optional): Provide a title for your story here. This will help us categorize and manage your submission.
