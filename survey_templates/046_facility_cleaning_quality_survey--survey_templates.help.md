# Facility Cleaning Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Cleaning Quality Survey is used to assess the quality of cleaning in different areas of the facility, including bathrooms, kitchen, hallways and corridors, common areas, and overall facility cleaning quality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the questions carefully and answer the required fields.
2. Rate the quality of cleaning for each area based on your assessment.
3. Provide comments for each area if you have any feedback.
4. Enter your name and email address (optional).
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Cleaning Quality Survey** (`Facility Cleaning Quality Survey`): The title of this form.
* **Area Cleaning Quality** (`Area Cleaning Quality`, 1-5): Rate the quality of cleaning in the specified area, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
	+ Note: This question is required but not necessarily answered by everyone.
* **Area Comments** (`Area Comments`, text): Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the area.
* **Bathroom Cleaning Quality** (`Bathroom Cleaning Quality`, 1-5): Rate the quality of cleaning in the bathrooms, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Bathroom Comments** (`Bathroom Comments`, text): Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the bathrooms.
* **Kitchen Cleaning Quality** (`Kitchen Cleaning Quality`, 1-5): Rate the quality of cleaning in the kitchen, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Kitchen Comments** (`Kitchen Comments`, text): Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the kitchen.
* **Hallways/Corridors Cleaning Quality** (`Hallways Corridors Cleaning Quality`, 1-5): Rate the quality of cleaning in the hallways and corridors, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Hallways/Corridors Comments** (`Hallways Corridors Comments`, text): Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the hallways and corridors.
* **Common Areas Cleaning Quality** (`Common Areas Cleaning Quality`, 1-5): Rate the quality of cleaning in the common areas, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Common Areas Comments** (`Common Areas Comments`, text): Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the common areas.
* **Facility Cleaning Quality Rating** (`Facility Cleaning Quality Rating`, 1-5): Rate the overall quality of cleaning in the facility, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Overall Quality Rating** (`Overall Quality Rating`, 1-5): Rate the overall quality of the facility, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Completed By** (`Completed By`, text): Enter your name.
* **Completed At** (`Completed At`, date): Enter the date and time you completed the form.
* **Completed By User** (`Completed By User`, text): Enter your name and email address (optional).
* **Email** (`Email`, email): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text): Enter any additional comments you may have about this survey.
* **Facility Cleaning Quality Score** (`Facility Cleaning Quality Score`, number): Enter the overall facility cleaning quality score.
* **Facility Cleaning Quality Comments** (`Facility Cleaning Quality Comments`, text): Enter any comments or suggestions you may have about the facility cleaning quality.
* **Area Cleaning Quality Rating** (`Area Cleaning Quality Rating`, 1-5): Rate the quality of cleaning in the specified area, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Completed By Inspector** (`Completed By Inspector`, text): Enter the name of the person who completed this form.
* **Completed At Inspector** (`Completed At Inspector`, date): Enter the date and time this form was completed.
* **Inspector Comments** (`Inspector Comments`, text): Enter any comments or suggestions by the inspector.
* **Completed By Staff** (`Completed By Staff`, text): Enter your name.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to provide clear and concise answers for each field.
* Rate the quality of cleaning honestly and objectively.
* Enter your name and email address if you want to be notified when the form is updated.
* You can skip any optional fields if you do not have any comments or suggestions.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have filled all required fields.
