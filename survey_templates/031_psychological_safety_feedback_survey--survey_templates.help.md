# Psychological Safety Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on psychological safety in the workplace. The goal is to understand how employees perceive their environment, identify areas of improvement, and promote a safe and supportive work culture.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please take a few minutes to read each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
- You can complete this form at your own pace and take breaks if needed.
- If you have any issues or concerns that you'd like to discuss, you can add them in the "Feedback" section.
- You can submit your feedback at any time, but it's recommended to complete the form in one session for the best results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. <strong>Welcome to the survey</strong>
- This is a greeting to welcome you to the survey.
- No further action is required.

### 2. **How do you feel about your job?**
- This section is for you to express your feelings about your current job.
- Please be honest and provide a clear answer.

### 3. **How safe do you feel?**
- This question helps us understand how safe you feel in your workplace.
- Consider answering on a scale or with a brief explanation.

### 4. **Open communication?**
- In this section, you can describe any open communication issues you've encountered in the workplace.
- Please be specific and provide examples if possible.

### 5. **Feeling supported?**
- This question is for you to express how supported you feel in your job.
- Please answer honestly and with examples if possible.

### 6. **What could the org do better?**
- In this section, you can provide any feedback or suggestions on how the organization can improve.
- Be specific and provide concrete ideas for improvement.

## Tips
- Please keep your answers brief and clear.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out.
- Your feedback is valuable and helps improve the workplace.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
