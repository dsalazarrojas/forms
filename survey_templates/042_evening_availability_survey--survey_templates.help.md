# Evening Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evening Availability Survey is a form designed to collect information about your evening availability for scheduling purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `user_name` field.
2. Select your preferred time of day in the `preferred_time` field.
3. Choose your preferred day of the week in the `preferred_day` field.
4. Enter your available time in the `available_time` field.
5. Select the days you are available for meetings in the `available_days` field.
6. Choose your preferred meeting duration in the `preferred_meeting_duration` field.
7. Select your preferred meeting time in the `preferred_meeting_time` field.
8. Choose how often you prefer to meet in the `preferred_meeting_frequency` field.
9. Select how often you are available for meetings in the `available_meeting_frequencies` field.
10. Choose your preferred meeting type in the `preferred_meeting_type` field.
11. Add any additional comments in the `additional_comments` field (optional).
12. Click the `Submit` button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name for reference.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Choose your preferred time of day (e.g., 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM).
* **Preferred Day** (`preferred_day`, select_one, required): Select your preferred day of the week (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday).
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, text, required): Enter your available time of day (e.g., 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM).
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, required): Select the days you are available for meetings (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday).
* **Preferred Meeting Duration** (`preferred_meeting_duration`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred meeting duration (e.g., 15 minutes, 30 minutes).
* **Preferred Meeting Time** (`preferred_meeting_time`, time, required): Select your preferred meeting time (e.g., 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM).
* **Preferred Meeting Frequency** (`preferred_meeting_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you prefer to meet (e.g., once, weekly, monthly).
* **Available Meeting Frequencies** (`available_meeting_frequencies`, select_multiple, required): Select how often you are available for meetings (e.g., once, weekly, monthly).
* **Preferred Meeting Type** (`preferred_meeting_type`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred meeting type (e.g., Video Call, Phone Call, In Person).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to complete the form.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field-by-field explanation.
