# Trade Opportunities And Challenges Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your business's trade opportunities and challenges. The information gathered from this form will enable us to provide you with the most suitable support and guidance to help you succeed in trading.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer it according to your best knowledge.
2. If you are unsure about a question or have any doubts, don't hesitate to contact us.
3. You can skip questions if you have already completed a similar form in the past.
4. Please provide the most accurate information about your business and its needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trade Opportunities and Challenges Survey** (`trade_page_1`, text, optional): This is the title of the form that you are currently filling out. Please do not make any changes to this field.
* **What are the main challenges that hinder your ability to trade?** (`trade_page_2`, text, optional): This question is asking you to describe the main challenges you face when trading. Please provide a brief but accurate description of these challenges.
* **What is the current average export value of your products/services?** (`trade_page_3`, number, optional): This question is asking for the average value of your products or services that are being exported. Please enter the current value in numbers (e.g., 100,000).
* **Do you have any other products/services with export value higher than $1,000,000?** (`trade_page_4`, select_multiple, optional): This question is asking if you have any products or services with an export value higher than $1,000,000. Please select all the options that apply to your business.
* **What is the current export value of these products/services higher than $1,000,000?** (`trade_page_5`, number, optional): This question is asking for the current export value of your products or services that are higher than $1,000,000. Please enter the current value in numbers (e.g., 500,000).
* **What kind of support do you need to trade?** (`trade_page_6`, select_multiple, optional): This question is asking about the type of support you need to trade. Please select all the options that apply to your business (Market access, Market research, Logistics, Other).
* **What is the primary support area you need?** (`trade_page_7`, select_one, optional): This question is asking about the primary support area you need. Please select one of the options that best describes your need (Trade Finance, Market Access, Export Promotion, Other).
* **Have you had any experience using a trade promotion program?** (`trade_page_8`, select_multiple, optional): This question is asking about your experience with trade promotion programs. Please select all the options that apply to your business (Yes, No).
