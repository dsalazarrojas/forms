# Daily Reflection Journal - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you reflect on your daily experiences, thoughts, and feelings. It is meant to be a personal and introspective tool for self-awareness and personal growth.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question in the provided fields, being as thorough or brief as you prefer.
2. For the "Morning Mood" field, select one of the provided options: "Yes" or "No".
3. In the text fields, write your thoughts, feelings, and reflections as you see fit.
4. For the "Goals" fields, write down your goals and progress on them.
5. Use the "Submit" button to save your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Morning Mood (1)** (`morning_mood`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate your morning mood by selecting "Yes" if you are feeling positive and alert, or "No" if you are not.
* **Reflection Topic (2)** (`reflection_topic`, text, optional): Write down the topic you want to reflect on for the day.
* **Other (3)** (`reflection_topic_other`, select_one, optional): If your reflection topic is not listed, select "Yes" to indicate that you have a different topic in mind.
* **Thoughts and Feelings (4)** (`thoughts_feelings`, text, optional): Write down any thoughts or feelings that came up for you during the day.

## Tips
* Try to be as honest and authentic in your responses as possible.
* Use the space provided to write down your thoughts and reflections freely.
* Don't worry about grammar or spelling errors - the focus is on your personal growth and self-awareness.
* Review your responses regularly to track your progress and identify areas for improvement.
