# Hospital Energy Efficiency Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Hospital Energy Efficiency Awareness Survey" is a form designed to gather information from hospital staff about their energy usage habits and awareness of energy-saving opportunities. This data will help hospital administrators identify areas where energy efficiency can be improved, reducing costs and environmental impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have filled in all required fields.
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly, as your responses will help inform hospital decision-making.
3. If you are unsure about any question, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**1. Hospital Energy Efficiency Awareness Survey Page 1**
This page is the introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and importance of completing this form.

**2. Staff Energy Use**
* **Staff Energy Use** (number): Report the amount of energy (in kilowatt-hours) used by your staff for work-related activities.
* This field is required to help us understand your energy usage habits.

**3. Staff Energy Use (kWh)**
* **Staff Energy Use (kWh)** (number): Report the amount of energy (in kilowatt-hours) used by your staff for work-related activities.
* This field is required to help us understand your energy usage habits.

**4. Energy Saving Potential**
* **Energy Saving Potential** (select multiple):
	+ Yes: You believe there are energy-saving opportunities in your hospital.
	+ No: You do not believe there are energy-saving opportunities in your hospital.
* This field is optional, but helps us understand your awareness of energy-saving potential.

**5. Staff Energy Awareness**
* **Staff Energy Awareness** (text): Share any comments or suggestions about how to improve energy efficiency in your hospital.
* This field is optional, but helps us understand your thoughts on energy awareness.

**6. Energy Saving Opportunities**
* **Energy Saving Opportunities** (select multiple):
	+ Yes: You believe there are energy-saving opportunities in your hospital.
	+ No: You do not believe there are energy-saving opportunities in your hospital.
* This field is optional, but helps us understand your awareness of energy-saving opportunities.

**7. Staff Energy Awareness Training**
* **Staff Energy Awareness Training** (text): Share any comments or suggestions about how to improve staff energy awareness training.
* This field is optional, but helps us understand your thoughts on energy awareness training.

**8. Comments**
* **Comments** (text): Share any additional comments or suggestions about hospital energy efficiency.
* This field is optional, but helps us understand your thoughts on energy efficiency.

**9. Email**
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* This field is optional, but helps us contact you if we need more information.

**10. Phone**
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* This field is optional, but helps us contact you if we need more information.

**11. Submitted By**
* **Submitted By** (time): This field will automatically be populated with the current date and time.
* This field is not editable, as it will be automatically filled in when you submit the form.

Note: Please review each page carefully and make sure to fill in all required fields. If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [insert contact information].
