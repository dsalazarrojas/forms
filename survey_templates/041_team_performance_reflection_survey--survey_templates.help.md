**team_performance_reflection_survey - Help Guide**
=============================================

## Purpose
The Team Performance Reflection Survey is a form designed to help teams reflect on their performance, goals, and progress. It provides an opportunity for team members to share their thoughts, ideas, and feedback on their individual and team performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the introduction page, which provides background information on the survey and its purpose.
2. Select all team members who will be taking the survey.
3. Answer each of the questions, providing thoughtful and honest feedback on your performance and progress.
4. Add any comments or observations you feel are important to share with the team or stakeholders.
5. Choose whether the submission status is Complete or Not Completed.
6. Provide comments on the submission status, if necessary.
7. Enter the date and time of submission.
8. Enter your contact email and phone number.
9. Add any additional notes or comments you feel are relevant.
