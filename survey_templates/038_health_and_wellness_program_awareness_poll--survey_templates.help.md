# Health And Wellness Program Awareness Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health And Wellness Program Awareness Poll is a survey aimed at gauging the level of awareness and participation in health and wellness programs among employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your current awareness level of health and wellness programs from the options provided.
2. Answer whether you participate in wellness programs.
3. Rate your satisfaction with current wellness programs.
4. Specify how often you participate in wellness programs.
5. Choose the benefits you receive from wellness programs.
6. Identify who funds the wellness programs.
7. Rate your satisfaction with wellness programs.
8. Indicate your awareness of wellness programs.
9. Select your preferred method of communication for wellness programs.
10. Provide any additional feedback you have about the programs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Awareness Level** (`current_awareness_level`): Select your current level of awareness of health and wellness programs. The options are:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Participation** (`participate_in_wellness_programs`): Indicate whether you participate in wellness programs. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Satisfaction with Wellness Programs** (`satisfaction_with_wellness_programs`): Rate your satisfaction with current wellness programs. The options are:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Initiative Participation** (`initiative_participation`): This field is not used in the form. It seems to be a leftover from previous version.
* **Frequency of Participation** (`how_frequent_do_you_participate`): Specify how often you participate in wellness programs. The options are:
	+ Never
	+ Less than monthly
	+ About once a month
	+ About once every two months
	+ About once every three months
	+ About once every six months
	+ About once a year
	+ More than once a year
* **Benefits Received** (`which_benefits_do_you_receive`): Choose the benefits you receive from wellness programs. The options are:
	+ Stress reduction
	+ Improved physical health
	+ Improved mental health
	+ Increased productivity
	+ Improved relationships
	+ Better work-life balance
	+ Improved overall health
	+ Other (please specify)
	+ None
* **Program Funding** (`who_funds_the_wellness_programs`): Identify who funds the wellness programs. The options are:
	+ Employer
	+ Insurance Company
	+ Union
	+ Employee contributions
	+ Both employer and insurance
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Satisfaction with Programs** (`satisfaction_with_programs`): Rate your satisfaction with wellness programs. The options are:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Program Awareness** (`program_awareness`): Indicate your awareness of wellness programs. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Not sure
* **Communication Preference** (`how_do_you_prefer_to_receive`): Select your preferred method of communication for wellness programs. The options are:
	+ Email
	+ In-person meetings
	+ Newsletters
	+ Print materials
	+ Text messages
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): This is a free-form field for you to provide any additional feedback you have about the wellness programs.

## Tips

* Take your time to carefully read and consider each question before answering.
* Make sure to select the options that best fit your experience and opinions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR or benefits administration team.
