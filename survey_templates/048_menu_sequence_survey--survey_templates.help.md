# menu_sequence_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your preferences for menu sequence.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and understand what information is being collected.
2. Click on "Select Menu Items" field to choose from the available options.
3. Select one option from the "Sequence" field to indicate your preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Menu Items** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the menu items you'd like to see in your sequence. Choose from the available options:
	+ Item 1
	+ Item 2
	+ Item 3
* **Sequence** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose one sequence type from the options:
	+ A
	+ B
	+ C

## Tips

- Make sure to choose options that align with your actual preferences.
- If you're unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask for help.
- Remember, your input will help us improve our services, so fill out the form accurately.
