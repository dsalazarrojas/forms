# Participant Study Feedback Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Participant Study Feedback Request Form" is a document that allows participants to provide feedback about their experiences and suggestions for future research sessions. This form is meant for participants who have completed our research sessions and are now being asked for their input on how we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out all the fields that are marked as "required" (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Take your time to think through each question before providing your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you find most enjoyable about our research sessions?** (`participant_study_feedback_request_form_1`, text, required: false):
  This question is an open-ended question that asks participants about their favorite part of our research sessions. Please share your thoughts and experiences in the space provided.
* **What topics would you like to see covered in future sessions?** (`participant_study_feedback_request_form_2`, text, required: false):
  This question allows participants to suggest topics or areas they would like to see explored in future research sessions. Your input will help us tailor future sessions to better meet the needs of all participants.
* **How likely are you to recommend our research sessions to others?** (`participant_study_feedback_request_form_3`, select_one, required: false):
  This question helps us gauge how satisfied participants are with our research sessions. Please select the option that best reflects your likelihood of recommending our sessions to others.
* **What time of day do you usually prefer for our sessions?** (`participant_study_feedback_request_form_4`, time, required: false):
  This question is about your preferred time of day for our research sessions. Please choose the time that works best for you.
* **How did you travel to our location?** (`participant_study_feedback_request_form_5`, select_multiple, required: false):
  This question is about your mode of transportation to our research location. Please select all that apply from the options provided.
* **How would you rate the parking at our location?** (`participant_study_feedback_request_form_6`, number, required: false):
  This question asks about your assessment of the parking facilities at our location. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How would you rate the comfort of our seating?** (`participant_study_feedback_request_form_7`, number, required: false):
  This question is about the comfort level of our seating arrangements. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is uncomfortable and 5 is very comfortable.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving our research sessions?** (`participant_study_feedback_request_form_8`, text, required: false):
  This question is an opportunity for participants to provide any suggestions they may have for improving our research sessions. Please take a moment to think about any ideas you may have and share them with us.
