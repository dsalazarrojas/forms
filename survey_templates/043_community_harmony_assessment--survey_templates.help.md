# Community Harmony Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Harmony Assessment form is a survey aimed at measuring the level of harmony within a community. It inquires about the respondent's relationship with their neighbors, communication, sense of belonging, appreciation for diversity, frequency and resolution of conflicts, participation in community events, satisfaction with these events, and perception of overall community safety and condition. The form is designed to help communities better understand their strengths and weaknesses and identify areas that require improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address (if you wish to share them).
2. Answer the questions regarding your relationship with your neighbors, communication, and sense of belonging.
3. Rate your appreciation for diversity in the community.
4. Indicate the frequency of conflicts and how they are typically resolved.
5. Describe your participation in community events and rate your satisfaction with these events.
6. Evaluate your perception of overall community safety and condition.
7. If you have any suggestions for improving community harmony, feel free to provide them.
8. Finally, rate the overall harmony within your community.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Optional)** (`respondent_name`, text, required: false): This is your name, which you can optionally provide for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): This is your email address, which can be useful for further communication.
* **Neighborhood or Area** (`neighborhood`, text, required: true): Indicate the neighborhood or area where you reside.
* **How Long Have You Lived Here** (`residency_duration`, select_one, required: true): Choose the duration of your residence in the area: Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, or More than 10 years.
* **Relationship with Neighbors** (`neighbor_relations`, select_one, required: true): Select how you would describe your relationship with your neighbors: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Communication Quality in Community** (`communication_quality`, select_one, required: true): Rate the effectiveness of communication within your community: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Do You Feel Included** (`inclusion_feeling`, select_one, required: true): Indicate how included you feel in your community: Very Included, Somewhat Included, Neutral, Somewhat Excluded, or Very Excluded.
* **Appreciation of Diversity** (`diversity_appreciation`, select_one, required: true): Select how well you appreciate diversity within your community: Very Well, Well, Neutral, Poorly, or Not Appreciated.
* **Frequency of Community Conflicts** (`conflict_frequency`, select_one, required: true): Select how often conflicts occur in your community: Very Rarely, Occasionally, Sometimes, Frequently, or Very Frequently.
* **Conflict Resolution Effectiveness** (`conflict_resolution`, select_one, required: true): Rate how well conflicts are resolved within your community: Very Well, Well, Fair, Poorly, or Very Poorly.
* **Community Events Participation** (`community_events`, select_one, required: true): Indicate how often you participate in community events: Regularly, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **Satisfaction with Community Events** (`event_satisfaction`, select_one, required: true): Rate your satisfaction with community events: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Safety Rating** (`safety_rating`, select_one, required: true): Select your perception of overall community safety: Very Safe, Safe, Neutral, Unsafe, or Very Unsafe.
* **Noise Level Satisfaction** (`noise_level`, select_one, required: true): Rate your satisfaction with noise levels in your neighborhood: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Shared Spaces Condition** (`shared_spaces`, select_one, required: true): Rate the condition of common areas within your community: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Barriers to Community Harmony** (`harmony_barriers`, select_multiple, required: false): Select from a list the barriers that prevent better harmony within your community. You can choose one or more of the following options: Lack of Communication, Cultural Differences, Language Barriers, Noise Issues, Parking Problems, or Pet Issues.
* **Suggestions for Better Harmony** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions for improving community harmony, feel free to provide them.
* **Overall Community Harmony Rating** (`overall_harmony_rating`, select_one, required: true): Rate the overall harmony within your community: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
