# Healthcare Policy Literacy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Policy Literacy Survey is designed to gather healthcare professionals' input and feedback on various aspects of healthcare policies. This survey is intended to improve policy understanding, provide status updates, and gather recommendations for policy improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections, each focused on a specific aspect of healthcare policies.
2. Fill in the required fields as prompted, which are marked as "required" in the field label.
3. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
4. Submit the form to provide your input and feedback.

* **Patient Information (page_one)** (`Patient Name`, `text`, not required): This field is for entering the patient's name. Please enter the patient's name as it appears in their medical records.
* **Policy Understanding (page_two)** (`Policy Understanding`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is for selecting the level of understanding regarding the healthcare policy. Choose the options that best describe your understanding.
* **Additional Information (page_three)** (`text`, not required): This field is for entering any additional comments or information related to the policy.
* **Policy Feedback (page_four)** (`text`, not required): This field is for entering any additional feedback or comments regarding the policy.

* **Policy Number (page_six)** (`Policy Number`, `text`, not required): This field is for entering the policy number (if applicable).
* **Contact Information (page_seven)** (`text`, `select_one`, required): This field is for selecting the contact information type (e.g., "Phone" or "Email").
* **Phone Number (page_eight)** (`Phone Number`, `text`, not required): This field is for entering your phone number (if applicable).
* **Email (page_nine)** (`Email`, `email`, not required): This field is for entering your email address (if applicable).
