# Charity Gala Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a charity gala event, helping the organizers to understand the strengths and weaknesses of the event and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Your Name**: Type your full name as an attendee of the gala event.
2. **Email Address**: If you'd like to receive updates or follow-up from us, please enter your email address.
3. **Gala Event Name**: Select the name of the gala event you attended.
4. **Gala Date**: Select the date you attended the gala event.
5. **Ticket Type**: Choose the type of ticket you purchased (General admission, VIP, Table sponsor, Event sponsor, or Complimentary).
6. **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
7. **Venue Rating**: Rate the quality of the event venue (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor).
8. **Ambiance and Decor Rating**: Rate the atmosphere and decor of the event (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor).
9. **Food Quality**: Rate the quality of the food served (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor, Did not eat).
10. **Beverage Service**: Rate the quality of the beverage service (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor, Did not drink).
11. **Entertainment Rating**: Rate the quality of the entertainment (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor).
12. **Auction Experience**: If you participated in the auction, rate your experience (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor, Did not participate).
13. **Program Flow and Timing**: Rate the pacing of the program (Very well paced, Well paced, Average, Rushed, Too slow).
14. **Highlight of the Evening**: Share the best moment of the event.
15. **Areas for Improvement**: Select areas where you'd like to see improvement (optional).
16. **Will You Attend Next Year**: Let us know if you plan to attend next year's event (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no).
17. **Would You Recommend to Others**: Rate if you would recommend the event to others (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no).
18. **Likelihood to Donate in Future**: Rate your likelihood to donate in the future (Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely).
19. **Additional Comments**: Share any other feedback or comments you have.
