# Pet Insurance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your pet and its insurance status. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to help us better understand your situation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting your insurance status for your pet.
	* Choose "Not Insured" if you don't have insurance for your pet.
	* Choose "Insured" if you have insurance for your pet.
	* Choose "Not Applicable" if you're not applicable for insurance.
2. Select the type(s) of pet you have.
	* Choose "cat" or "dog" if you have one of these pets.
	* Choose "other" for any other type of pet.
3. Enter your pet's age.
	* This is a numerical field, please enter a number.
4. Describe your pet's health condition.
	* Choose "No Health Issues" if your pet is healthy.
	* Choose "Minor Issues" if your pet has minor health issues.
	* Choose "Severe Issues" if your pet has severe health issues.
5. Enter the value of your pet.
	* This is a numerical field, please enter a number.
6. Select the method you'd like to contact us.
	* Choose "email", "phone", or "chat" to contact us.
7. Enter your contact number.
	* This is a text field, please enter a phone number.
8. Enter your email.
	* This is a text field, please enter a valid email address.
9. Enter your address.
	* This is a text field, please enter a valid address.
10. Enter your zip.
	* This is a numerical field, please enter a zip code.
11. Enter any additional comments you may have.
	* This is a text field, please enter any additional comments.
12. Select the insurance provider you use.
	* Choose "Trupanion", "Petplan", "Petco", or "Other" for your insurance provider.
13. Do you have a policy number?
	* Choose "True" if you have a policy number, otherwise choose "False" or "Not Applicable".
14. If you have a policy, select the provider you have it with.
	* Choose "Trupanion", "Petplan", "Petco", or "Other" for your insurance provider.
15. Enter the start date of your policy.
	* This is a date field, please enter a date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
16. Enter the end date of your policy.
	* This is a date field, please enter a date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
17. Enter the effective date of your policy.
	* This is a date field, please enter a date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
18. Enter the deductible of your policy.
	* This is a numerical field, please enter a number.
19. Enter the maximum deductible of your policy.
	* This is a numerical field, please enter a number.
20. Enter the minimum deductible of your policy.
	* This is a numerical field, please enter a number.
21. Enter the coverage of your policy.
	* This is a numerical field, please enter a number.
22. How would you like to renew your policy?
	* Choose "Automatic" to renew automatically or "Manual" to renew manually.
23. Enter any additional comments about your policy.
	* This is a text field, please enter any additional comments.
24. Select the status of your policy.
	* Choose "Active" if your policy is active, otherwise choose "Inactive".
