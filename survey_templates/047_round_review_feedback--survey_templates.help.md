# Round_Review_Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from participants after a round of training or review. It helps gather information to improve the quality and effectiveness of future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the participant's name as it appears on their ID badge or business card.
2. Enter the date of the event, in the format MM/DD/YY.
3. Rate the participant's overall experience using the rating options provided.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback for the participant.
5. If applicable, choose the assigned tool used during the event.
6. Decide whether to follow up with the participant and provide any necessary details.
7. Choose the review status to indicate whether the review is complete or not.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (participant_name)**: Enter the participant's name as it appears on their ID badge or business card.
* **Event Date (event_date)**: Enter the date of the event, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Overall Rating (overall_rating)**: Rate the participant's overall experience using the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feedback Comments (feedback_comments)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback for the participant.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)**: If applicable, choose the assigned tool used during the event.
* **Follow up with Participant (follow_up)**: Decide whether to follow up with the participant and provide any necessary details.
* **Follow up Details (follow_up_details)**: If you decided to follow up, provide additional details about the reason and plan for the follow-up.
* **Follow up Notes (follow_up_notes)**: Add any additional notes about the follow-up.
* **Follow up Contact Info (follow_up_contact)**: If you decided to follow up, provide the participant's contact information.
* **Next Steps (next_steps)**: Add any additional information about the next steps or actions taken as a result of the review.
* **Review Date (review_date)**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Status (review_status)**: Choose the review status to indicate whether the review is complete or not.
* **Review Comments (review_comments)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the review.
* **Recommendations (recommendations)**: Provide any suggestions or recommendations for improvement.
* **Participant Contact Info (participant_contact_info)**: Add any information about the participant's contact information.
* **Next Tool (next_tool)**: If you have a next tool, select it in the provided options.
* **Rating Comments (rating_comments)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the rating.
* **Follow-up Contact (follow_up_contact)**: If you have a follow-up contact information, add it in the field.
