# Media Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on media content. It will help you rate and provide feedback on individual items in a video series.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the page number corresponding to the item you'd like to provide feedback on from the list below:
	* Page 1 (media_feedback_request_form_page_1)
	* Page 2 (video_rating)
	* ... (list the other pages similarly)
2. Follow the instructions on each page to rate and provide feedback on the selected item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Media Feedback Request Form** (`media_feedback_request_form`): A brief description of the item being rated. (Optional, text field)
* **Video Rating** (`video_rating`): Rate the video on a scale. (Number field)
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`): Rate the first item in the series. (Number field)
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`): Rate the second item in the series. (Number field)
* **Rating 3** (`rating_3`): Rate the third item in the series. (Number field)
* **Rating 4** (`rating_4`): Rate the fourth item in the series. (Number field)
* **Rating 5** (`video_rating_5`): Rate the fifth item in the series. (Number field)
* **Rating 6** (`rating_6`): Rate the sixth item in the series. (Number field)
* **Rating 7** (`rating_7`): Rate the seventh item in the series. (Number field)
* **Rating 8** (`rating_8`): Rate the eighth item in the series. (Number field)
* **Rating 9** (`rating_9`): Rate the ninth item in the series. (Number field)
* **Rating 10** (`rating_10`): Rate the tenth item in the series. (Number field)
* **Video Feedback** (`video_feedback`): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the item. (Text field)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out every page that corresponds to an item in the series you'd like to provide feedback on.
* Use a scale of 1-5 to rate each item, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Video Feedback" field to provide them.
