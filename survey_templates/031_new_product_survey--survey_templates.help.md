# new_product_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their product preferences and purchasing experiences. It helps us understand what customers like and dislike about our products, and how they interact with our brand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Answer each question carefully and honestly**. We value your feedback and want to get an accurate picture of your opinions.
2. **Select one or multiple options** for questions with multiple-choice options.
3. **Enter your email address and phone number (if you have one)**. This will allow us to contact you if you'd like.
4. **Write any additional comments or suggestions** you have about the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your product name?** (Required): This is the name of the product you're interested in or have purchased. Please enter it exactly as it appears on the product packaging or in your marketing materials.
* **What is your age group?** (Required): Please select the age group that best fits your current age.
* **What type of product are you interested in purchasing?** (Required): Select one or multiple categories that interest you, such as Electronics, Fashion, or Home & Kitchen.
* **Which of the following features do you like most about the product?** (Required): Choose the feature that resonates with you the most, such as Design, Performance, or Price.
* **How likely are you to recommend the product to a friend or family member?** (Required): Select how likely you would recommend the product based on your experience.
* **What is your contact email address?** (Optional): Enter your email address if you have one. This will allow us to contact you if you'd like.
* **Do you have any phone number to contact you?** (Optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Would you like to provide any additional comments or suggestions about the product?** (Optional): Write any thoughts or ideas you have about the product.
* **Date of last purchase (if any)**: Enter the date of your last product purchase, if any.
* **Time of last purchase** (Optional): Enter the time of your last purchase, if any.
* **How did you find out about this product?** (Required): Select how you became aware of the product, such as online advertising or social media.
* **How would you rate the product's performance?** (Required): Choose how you rate the product's performance, such as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
