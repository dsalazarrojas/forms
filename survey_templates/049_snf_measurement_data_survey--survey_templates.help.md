# snf_measurement_data_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data related to SNF measurements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the site name in the "Site Name" field.
2. Enter the date of the survey in the "Survey Date" field (optional).
3. Enter the site address in the "Site Address" field.
4. Enter the measurement location in the "Measurement Location" field.
5. Enter the SNF measurements in the "SNF Measurements" field.
6. Select the measurement units in the "Measurement Units" field.
7. If applicable, enter the name of the person who performed the measurement in the "Measured By" field.
8. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field (optional).
9. Enter the tool assigned to the site in the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. Enter the email of the person who completed the form in the "Email" field (optional).
11. Enter the phone number of the site in the "Site Phone" field (optional).
12. Enter the email of the site in the "Site Email" field.
13. Select the person assigned to the site from the list in the "Assigned To" field.
14. Enter the unique survey ID in the "Survey ID" field.
15. Enter the site ID in the "Site ID" field.
16. Enter the date the form was completed in the "Date Completed" field.
17. Select the site survey count from the list in the "Site Survey Count" field.
18. Select the status of the survey from the list in the "Survey Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the site.
* **Survey Date** (2, date, optional): Enter the date of the survey.
* **Site Address** (3, text, required): Enter the address of the site.
* **Measurement Location** (4, text, required): Enter the location where the measurement was taken.
* **SNF Measurements** (5, text, required): Enter the measurements taken at the site.
* **Measurement Units** (6, text, required): Enter the units used for the measurement.
* **Measured By** (7, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the person who performed the measurement.
* **Notes** (8, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned Tool** (9, text, required): Enter the tool assigned to the site.
* **Email** (10, email, optional): Enter the email of the person who completed the form.
* **Phone** (11, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the site.
* **Site Phone** (12, text, required): Enter the phone number of the site.
* **Site Email** (13, email, required): Enter the email of the site.
* **Assigned To** (14, select_one, required): Select the person assigned to the site from the list.
* **Survey ID** (15, text, required): Enter the unique survey ID.
* **Site ID** (16, text, required): Enter the site ID.
* **Date Completed** (17, date, required): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Site Survey Count** (18, select_multiple, required): Select the site survey count from the list.
* **Survey Status** (19, select_one, required): Select the status of the survey from the list.

## Tips
Before completing this form, ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals to access and input data in this form. If you are unsure about any field or value, please seek clarification from your supervisor or team leader.
