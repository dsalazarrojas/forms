# Patient Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from patients on the quality of care provided by healthcare professionals. This form helps us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page to understand the purpose and importance of providing feedback.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Review the options carefully and choose the one that best represents your response.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed, as marked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (Note): A brief explanation of the form's purpose and importance.

### Questions
* **question_one** (Text): This question asks for your overall impression of the visit/service provided.
* **question_two** (Text): Describe your experience with the care provider(s).
* **question_three** (Text): If you had any issues or concerns during the visit, please elaborate here.
