# Stress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a stress survey designed to help users understand and manage their stress levels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each question and answer honestly.
2. Select the options that best describe your current state.
3. Answer all questions that apply to you.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.
