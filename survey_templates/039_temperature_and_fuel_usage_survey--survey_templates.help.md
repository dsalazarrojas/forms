# Temperature And Fuel Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is part of an initiative to collect information about your temperature and fuel usage habits to support sustainable practices in your home or community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select all applicable answers for each field and provide as much detail as necessary in the open-ended fields. Make sure to select the correct unit of measurement for your fuel consumption (e.g., Gallons, Kiloliters, Liters, Tonnes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Heating Fuel Type** (`home_heating_fuel_type`, select_one, required: false): Please select the type of fuel used for heating, such as Gas, Electricity, Oil, Coal, or Other.
* **Current Thermostat Setting** (`thermostat_setting`, select_one, required: false): Choose the current setting on your thermostat: Low, Medium, High, or Other.
* **Energy-Saving Habits** (`energy_saving_habits`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the energy-saving habits you practice, such as using energy-saving appliances, unplugging appliances when not in use, using natural light in the day, or having a smart thermostat.
* **Heating Fuel Consumption** (`heating_fuel_consumption`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of fuel used for heating.
* **Unit of Heating Fuel Consumption** (`heating_fuel_consumption_unit`, select_one, required: false): Choose the unit of measurement for your fuel consumption (e.g., Gallons, Kiloliters, Liters, Tonnes).
* **Energy-Saving Goals** (`energy_savings_goal`, text, required: false): Describe your goals for reducing energy consumption, such as improving home comfort or saving money.
* **How Do You Save Energy?** (`energy_savings_habits`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the habits that help you save energy, including using energy-efficient appliances, having a smart thermostat, using natural light in the day, or other practices.
* **Energy-Saving Habits 2** (`energy_savings_habits_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the habits you use for energy savings, such as using energy-efficient appliances, having a smart thermostat, or using natural light in the day.
* **Frequency of Energy-Saving Habits** (`energy_savings_habits_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how often you perform energy-saving habits, like always, often, sometimes, rarely, or never.
* **How Often Do You Use Fuel?** (`heating_fuel_consumption_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how often you use fuel for heating: Always, Often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or insights about your energy usage and saving practices.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, required: false): Additional comments or insights about your energy usage and saving practices.
* **Comments 3** (`comments_3`, text, required: false): More comments or insights about your energy usage and saving practices.
* **Comments 4** (`comments_4`, text, required: false): Yet more comments or insights about your energy usage and saving practices.
* **Comments 5** (`comments_5`, text, required: false): Last set of comments or insights about your energy usage and saving practices.
* **Comments 6** (`comments_6`, text, required: false): Final comments or insights about your energy usage and saving practices.
* **Comments 7** (`comments_7`, text, required: false): Additional final comments or insights about your energy usage and saving practices.
* **Comments 8** (`comments_8`, text, required: false): Final comments or insights about your energy usage and saving practices.
* **Comments 9** (`comments_9`, text, required: false): Last final comments or insights about your energy usage and saving practices.
* **Comments 10** (`comments_10`, text, required: false): Final and last comments or insights about your energy usage and saving practices.

**Tips**
* Please answer all applicable questions and select only one option per field when applicable.
* If a field is not applicable, please select "N/A" or "None" as the answer.
* You can provide as much detail as needed in the open-ended fields.
* Make sure to select the correct unit of measurement for your fuel consumption.

Please fill out this form and help us understand your energy usage and saving practices.
