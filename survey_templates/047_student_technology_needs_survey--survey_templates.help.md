# Student Technology Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information and self-reported technology needs and preferences from students. It is intended to help the student body and technology administrators better understand their technology requirements and infrastructure needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student ID number in the "What is your student ID" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Student Email" field. (Note: This field is required.)
4. Enter your phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
5. Describe your technology needs in the "Describe your technology needs" field.
6. Indicate whether you need or do not need the following technologies:
	* Laptops
	* Tablets
	* Smartphones

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your student ID**: Enter your student ID number as required for your student profile.
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your student records.
* **Student Email**: Enter your institutional email address. (Note: This field is required.)
* **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Describe your technology needs**: Provide a brief description of your technology needs and preferences.
* **Laptops**: Select "Yes" if you need a laptop for academic purposes, "No" otherwise.
* **Tablets**: Select "Yes" if you need a tablet for academic purposes, "No" otherwise.
* **Smartphones**: Select "Yes" if you need a smartphone for academic purposes, "No" otherwise.

## Tips
* Ensure your email address is accurate to receive any necessary notifications.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure the best possible outcome. 
* If you are unsure about any of the options, you can always contact your technology administrator or department for assistance.
