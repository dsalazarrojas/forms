# Solar Exploration Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Solar Exploration Survey form. The purpose of this form is to collect information about your exploration experiences, interests, and expertise in the field of solar energy. Your responses will help us understand your motivations for exploring solar energy and identify areas where you can contribute to the community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Survey Title**: Enter a brief title for your survey. (text, optional)
2. **Name**: Provide your name. (text, optional)
3. **Email**: Enter your email address. (email, optional)
4. **Role**: Select your role in the solar energy community. (select_one, optional)
	* Administrator: Someone who oversees or manages the solar energy community.
	* Educator: Someone who teaches or shares knowledge about solar energy.
	* Researcher: Someone who conducts research on solar energy.
	* Community Leader: Someone who leads or coordinates solar energy activities.
5. **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization. (text, optional)
6. **How many years**: Specify the number of years you've been exploring solar energy. (number, optional)
7. **Years exploring**: Enter the number of years you've been exploring solar energy. (number, optional)
8. **Why exploring**: Select all reasons that apply for why you are exploring solar energy. (select_multiple, optional)
	* Personal interest
	* Professional development
	* Research
	* Other (please specify)
9. **Solar interests**: Select all categories that interest you about solar energy. (select_multiple, optional)
	* Space
	* Environment
	* Energy
	* Science
10. **Solar expertise**: Select all levels of expertise you possess in solar energy. (select_multiple, optional)
	* Basic
	* Intermediate
	* Advanced
11. **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your exploration experiences. (note, optional)
12. **Date**: Enter the date of your submission. (date, optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title**: A short title for your survey submission.
* **Name**: Your name as you want to be listed in our records.
* **Email**: Your email address for future communication.
* **Role**: Your position within the solar energy community.
* **Organization**: Your affiliated organization, if any.
* **How many years**: The number of years you've spent exploring solar energy.
* **Years exploring**: Another way to ask for the number of years you've been exploring solar energy (same as "How many years").
* **Why exploring**: Select all reasons why you are interested in or explore solar energy.
* **Solar interests**: Choose categories that interest you about solar energy.
* **Solar expertise**: Select levels of expertise you possess in solar energy.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or thoughts about your exploration experiences.
* **Date**: The date of your submission, which is automatically populated.
