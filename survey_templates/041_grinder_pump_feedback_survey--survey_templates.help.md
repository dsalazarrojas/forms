# grinder_pump_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "grinder_pump_feedback_survey" form is a tool for customers to provide feedback on their experience with the grinder pump. This feedback is crucial to help us understand how our product performs and what improvements we can make to better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the survey completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
2.  Make sure to provide your contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.
3.  Answer truthfully about your experience with the grinder pump, providing details about any issues or concerns you may have.
4.  Take a moment to think about your experience and provide suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your survey entry, briefly describing your experience with the grinder pump. This can be something like "Excellent performance on our new pump install!".
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please provide your name as it appears on your identification or business cards. This will help us to contact you if we need more information.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter the name of your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter your job title.
* **Pump Model** (`pump_model`, text, optional): If you are experiencing issues with a specific pump model, please enter its model number or name.
* **Pump Location** (`pump_location`, text, optional): Please enter the location where the grinder pump is installed.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the performance of the grinder pump. This will help us understand if there are any areas for improvement.
* **Issue Report** (`issue_report`, text, optional): If you experienced any issues with the grinder pump, please describe them in detail. This will help us to identify and fix problems quickly.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvements to the grinder pump or our services, please share them with us.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address where we can reach out to you with follow-up questions or to discuss your feedback.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Take a moment to share any additional comments or information about your experience with the grinder pump.

Tips:
* Be honest and detailed in your feedback, as it will help us to better serve you and our other customers.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask and we will do our best to clarify the question.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
