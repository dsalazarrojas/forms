# App Store Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from users of our app to help us understand their experience and suggest areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose only one or multiple options that best describe your experience.
3. For text fields, provide as much detail as you can.
4. For note fields, type in any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know.
