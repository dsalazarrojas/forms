# model_registration_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The model registration survey is a form designed to collect information from models or talents for registration and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name and Last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
3. If you have a Contact phone number, enter it in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or "+X-X-XXXX" (e.g., "123-456-7890" or "+1-876-5432").
4. If you have an Email, enter it in the standard "username@domain.com" format.
5. Enter your Height and Weight in whole numbers.
6. Select your Hair color and Eye color from the provided options.
7. Select the Available models you would like to be considered for.
8. Select the Model type(s) you would like to be considered for.
9. Select the Agency(ies) you would like to be associated with.
10. Enter your Location.
11. Enter the Contact person's name.
12. Enter the Contact email.
13. Select the Portfolio(s) you would like to be associated with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Date of birth** (3, date, required): Enter your Date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Contact phone number** (4, text, required): Enter your Contact phone number, if applicable.
* **Email** (5, email, required): Enter your Email address.
* **Height** (6, number, required): Enter your height in centimeters or meters.
* **Weight** (7, number, required): Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Hair color** (8, select_one, required): Select your hair color from the provided options.
* **Eye color** (9, select_one, required): Select your eye color from the provided options.
* **Available models** (10, select_multiple, required): Select the models you would like to be considered for.
* **Model type** (11, select_multiple, required): Select the model type(s) you would like to be considered for.
* **Agency** (12, select_multiple, required): Select the agency(ies) you would like to be associated with.
* **Location** (13, text, required): Enter your location.
* **Contact person** (14, text, required): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact email** (15, email, required): Enter the contact email.
* **Portfolio** (16, select_multiple, required): Select the portfolio(s) you would like to be associated with.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have selected all relevant options for your available models, model type, agency, and portfolio.
