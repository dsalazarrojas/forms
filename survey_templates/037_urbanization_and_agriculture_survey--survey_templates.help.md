# Urbanization And Agriculture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the impact of urbanization on agriculture from individuals who have relevant information to provide on this topic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, starting from the first page.
2. For each question, select the answer that best describes your situation.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
4. Once you have filled out the form, review it carefully to ensure that all required questions are answered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What is the impact of urbanization?
* **what is the impact of urbanization** (`what_is_the_impact_of_urbanization`, select_one, required): This question is asking about the impact of urbanization on agriculture. Please select one of the options that best describes the impact you have observed.
  - Option 1: Positive impact (e.g. increased food availability)
  - Option 2: Negative impact (e.g. loss of land for agriculture)
  - Option 3: Neutral impact (e.g. no change)
  - Option 4: Uncertain impact (e.g. not sure)
  - Option 5: Other (please specify)

### How much time do you spend outside of work?
* **how much time do you spend outside of work** (`how_much_time_do_you_spend_outside_of_work`, number, required): This question is asking about how much time you spend outside of work. Please enter the number of hours or minutes you spend outside of work per day.
  - Example: 2-5 hours

### What are your employment status?
* **what are your employment status** (`what_are_your_employment_status`, select_multiple, required): This question is asking about your current employment status. Please select all that apply:
  - Option 1: Employed
  - Option 2: Self-employed
  - Option 3: Unemployed

### Urbanization and Agriculture Survey Form Page 2
* **Urbanization and Agriculture Survey Form Page 2** (`urbanization_and_agriculture_survey_form_page_2`, text, required): This is an additional question on the second page of the form. Please enter your answer in the text box.
  - Example: Please provide a brief description of the impact of urbanization on agriculture.

### How much time do you spend outside of work in urban?
* **how much time do you spend outside of work in urban** (`how_much_time_do_you_spend_outside_of_work_in_urban`, number, required): This question is asking about how much time you spend outside of work in urban areas. Please enter the number of hours or minutes you spend outside of work per day.
  - Example: 2-5 hours

### Age
* **age** (`age`, number, required): This question is asking about your age. Please enter the number of years you are.
  - Example: 25

**Tips**
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to ask.
