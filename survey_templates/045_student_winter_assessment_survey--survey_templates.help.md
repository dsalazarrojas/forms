# Student Winter Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Winter Assessment Survey is designed to collect your thoughts and opinions about your experience with the winter assessment in the program you're taking. This survey aims to provide valuable insights to your instructor and the program administrators, helping them understand the strengths and weaknesses of the assessment process and make it more effective for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions and fields carefully.
2. Select the option that best describes your experience with the winter assessment.
3. Add any additional comments you'd like to share about your experience.
4. Enter your name and student ID (if you're prompted to do so).
5. Select the program you're taking from the list.
6. Select the instructor teaching your program.
7. Submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This is an optional text field where you can briefly introduce yourself and your experience with the winter assessment.
* **survey questions** (`survey questions`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select how you felt about the winter assessment. Please choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **survey comments** (`survey comments`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments about the winter assessment, you can add them here. Please be as specific as possible.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): You can enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or to receive a copy of your survey response.
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **student id** (`student id`, `number`, required: false): You can enter your student ID number.
* **program** (`program`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the program you're taking from the list.
* **department** (`department`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the department you're taking from the list.
* **instructor** (`instructor`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the instructor teaching your program from the list.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- You can choose one option for the survey questions, so select the one that best fits your experience.
- If you have any concerns or suggestions, please include them in the comments field.
- You can skip any field if you're not comfortable providing the information.
- If you need help or have any questions, contact your instructor or the program administrators.
