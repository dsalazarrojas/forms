# team_swervex_clan_tryout_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The team_swervex_clan_tryout_form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from team members about their experiences and preferences related to the Swervex Clan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team's title in the text field labeled "Team".
2. Enter your name in the text field labeled "Name".
3. Enter your email address in the text field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your phone number in the text field labeled "Phone".
5. Select one category from the dropdown menu labeled "Category".
6. Provide a brief description of your answers in the text field labeled "Description".
7. Select multiple prophets from the dropdown menu labeled "Prophet".
8. Enter the date and time of the event in the date and time fields labeled "Date" and "Time", respectively.
9. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the text field labeled "Note".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team** (`team_swervex_clan_tryout_form_title`): Enter the team's title.
* **Name** (`team_swervex_clan_tryout_form_name`): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`team_swervex_clan_tryout_form_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`team_swervex_clan_tryout_form_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Category** (`team_swervex_clan_tryout_form_category`): Select one category from the dropdown menu.
	+ Options: Team, Clan, Tryout
* **Description** (`team_swervex_clan_tryout_form_description`): Enter a brief description of your answers.
* **Prophet** (`team_swervex_clan_tryout_form_prophet`): Select multiple prophets from the dropdown menu.
	+ Options: Prophet 1, Prophet 2, Prophet 3
* **Date** (`team_swervex_clan_tryout_form_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`team_swervex_clan_tryout_form_time`): Enter the time of the event.
* **Note** (`team_swervex_clan_tryout_form_note`): Enter any additional comments or notes.
