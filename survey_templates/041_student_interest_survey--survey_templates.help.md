# Student Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Student Interest Survey" is a short form that gathers information from students about their preferences and interests. It's a tool to help us get a better understanding of what you like to do or are interested in, so we can offer you suitable activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select up to 3 types of activities you enjoy from the list provided.
2. Enter your name in the format of "First Name Last Name" (e.g., John Doe).
3. Enter your email address in the format of "john.doe@example.com".
4. Enter your phone number in the format of "(123) 456-7890" or any other international format.
5. Select a date that you are interested in (this should be a specific date, e.g., "2024-02-15").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of activities do you enjoy**: This field allows you to choose up to 3 types of activities that you enjoy. Please select the ones that best describe your interests.
* **Your name**: Please enter your name in the format "First Name Last Name" (e.g., John Doe).
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format of "john.doe@example.com". This will help us get in touch with you if needed.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number in the format of "(123) 456-7890" or any other international format.
* **Date of interest**: Please select a specific date that you are interested in. This could be a date for an event or a specific day you would like to attend.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter valid email and phone number formats.
- Select only up to 3 activities you enjoy.
- Enter a specific date you are interested in.
- If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact us.
