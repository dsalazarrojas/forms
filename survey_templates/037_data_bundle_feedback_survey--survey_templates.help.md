# data_bundle_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The data_bundle_feedback_survey form is used to collect feedback and information from customers regarding data bundles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
- Enter the title of the data bundle being submitted on the "survey_title" field.
- Provide your name on the "customer_name" field.
- Select "Yes" or "No" on the "data_bundle_type" field to indicate whether the data bundle was successful or not.
- Enter any feedback on the "feedback" field.
- Select your level of satisfaction on the "satisfaction" field.
- Indicate if you would like to be contacted for follow-up on the "follow_up" field.
- Enter any additional feedback on the "additional_feedback" field.
- Enter your contact information on the "contact_info" field.
- Enter your phone number on the "phone_number" field.
- Select the date of delivery on the "date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **survey_title** (text, required: false): Enter the title of the data bundle being submitted.
* **customer_name** (text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **data_bundle_type** (select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if the data bundle was successful, "No" otherwise.
* **feedback** (text, required: false): Enter any feedback or comments about the data bundle.
* **satisfaction** (select_multiple, required: false): Select your level of satisfaction with the service.
* **follow_up** (select_one, required: false): Indicate if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **additional_feedback** (text, required: false): Enter any additional feedback or comments.
* **contact_info** (email, required: false): Enter your email address or contact information.
* **phone_number** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **date** (date, required: false): Select the date of delivery.
