# AI Assistant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Assistant Feedback Form is designed to collect user feedback on our AI Assistant product. This form helps us understand how you use our AI Assistant, identify areas for improvement, and gather suggestions for new features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your usage frequency, satisfaction level, and primary use case for our AI Assistant.
3. Choose your overall rating for our AI Assistant (from 1 to 10).
4. Select all the best aspects of our AI Assistant that apply to you.
5. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide specific feedback.
6. Rate the accuracy of responses and satisfaction with response speed.
7. Let us know if you would recommend our AI Assistant to others.
8. If you have any privacy concerns, select one of the options.
9. Share any additional comments or feature requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name to identify yourself in the feedback.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): We'll use this to follow up if needed.
* **Usage frequency** (`usage_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you use our AI Assistant (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or First time).
* **Satisfaction level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction level with our AI Assistant (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, integer, required): Rate our AI Assistant from 1 to 10.
* **Primary use case** (`primary_use_case`, select_one, required): Select your main use case for our AI Assistant (Customer support, Content creation, Data analysis, Learning/research, Automation, Other).
* **Best aspects** (`best_aspects`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the aspects of our AI Assistant that you find particularly useful (Easy to use, Fast responses, Accurate information, Helpful suggestions, Natural conversation, Reliable availability, Good integration).
* **Areas to improve** (`areas_to_improve`, text, optional): Provide specific feedback on areas where you think our AI Assistant can be improved.
* **Accuracy rating** (`accuracy_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the accuracy of responses (Very accurate, Mostly accurate, Somewhat accurate, Often inaccurate, Very inaccurate).
* **Response speed** (`response_speed`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with response speed (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
* **Would recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Let us know if you would recommend our AI Assistant to others (Definitely, Probably, Not sure, Probably not, False).
* **Privacy concerns** (`privacy_concerns`, select_one, optional): Select any privacy concerns you may have about using our AI Assistant (None, Minor concerns, Moderate concerns, Major concerns).
* **Feature requests** (`feature_requests`, text, optional): Share any additional feature requests you may have.
* **Customer support experience** (`customer_support_experience`, select_one, optional): If you have contacted customer support, select the response (Yes, and it was helpful, Yes, but it wasn't helpful, No, but I might in the future, No, and I don't need to).
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback.
