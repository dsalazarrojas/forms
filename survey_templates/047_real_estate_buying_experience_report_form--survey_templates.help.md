# Real Estate Buying Experience Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Buying Experience Report Form is a collection of questions designed to gather information about your real estate buying experience. Please answer these questions to provide us with valuable feedback and help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply work through each question and provide your honest and accurate responses. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification. You can select one or multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What drove your decision to buy a property?** (`what_drove_your_decision`, text, optional): Please explain what factors contributed to your decision to buy a property.
* **Why did you choose your current property?** (`why_did_you_choose`, text, optional): Describe the reasons why you selected your current property.
* **Were your expectations met?** (`were_your_expectations_met`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if your expectations were met, or 'No' if they were not.
* **What did you purchase?** (`what_did_you_purchase`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding the types of properties or services you purchased during your real estate transaction.
	+ Yes: You purchased a single-family home, condominium, townhouse, etc.
	+ No: You did not purchase a property
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the real estate buying process, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Was a real estate agent involved?** (`real_estate_agent`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if a real estate agent was involved in your transaction, or 'No' if they were not.
* **Was the real estate office involved?** (`real_estate_office`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the real estate office was involved in your transaction, or 'No' if they were not.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
- You can select all that apply for questions that allow multiple selections.
- The form is optional, but your feedback is invaluable to us.
