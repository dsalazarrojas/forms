# BIM Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to assess your experiences and perspectives on Building Information Modeling (BIM). The information gathered will help us understand your organization's BIM maturity level, challenges, and benefits realized. The insights will aid in identifying areas for improvement and developing targeted support and resources for your BIM practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Company or Organization**, and **Job Title**.
2. Enter your **Business Email** for follow-up communication.
3. Select your primary **Industry Sector** from the list provided.
4. Indicate your **Years of BIM Experience**.
5. Choose the **BIM Software Used** from the list.
6. Rate your organization's **BIM Implementation Level** based on your experience.
7. Select the **BIM Benefits Realized** by your organization, such as improved collaboration or cost savings.
8. Identify any **BIM Implementation Challenges** your organization faced.
9. Assess the **BIM Collaboration Effectiveness** within your team.
10. Choose the types of **Projects Using BIM** that your organization works on.
11. Indicate whether your organization follows **BIM Standards**.
12. Rate your organization's **Training Investment Level**.
13. State your plans for **Future BIM Adoption**.
14. Evaluate your **Overall Satisfaction** with BIM technology.
15. Provide any **Recommendations for BIM Improvement** if you see areas for enhancement.
16. Offer any **Additional Comments** about your BIM practices.

* **Full Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name to identify yourself.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_organization`, text, required): Type in the name of your organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your position or role.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email for follow-up.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, select_one, required): Choose your primary sector from the list provided:
	+ Architecture
	+ Engineering
	+ Construction
	+ Owner or Developer
	+ Facility Management

	+ Other

* **Recommendations for BIM Improvement** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Offer any recommendations for BIM improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your BIM practices.
