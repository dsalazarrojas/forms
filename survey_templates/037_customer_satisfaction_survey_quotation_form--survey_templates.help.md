# customer_satisfaction_survey_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a customer satisfaction survey designed to collect feedback and suggestions from customers. It is meant to help us understand how we can improve our service or product offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in each field. You can type your response in the text fields, select one or multiple options from the dropdown lists, and don't worry about saving your progress as you can complete the form in multiple steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Please provide your first name for the purpose of this survey.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Please provide your last name for the purpose of this survey.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please provide your email address to contact you back regarding any feedback or follow-up questions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a company, please provide the name of your company.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any specific feedback you have about our service or product.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve our service or product, please share them here.
* **Contact** (`contact`, `email`, optional): Your email address where we can contact you for any follow-up questions or feedback.
* **Manager Contacted?** (`manager`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if you have been contacted by a manager regarding your survey response.
* **Departmental Contact** (`department`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate which department(s) you would like to be contacted regarding your survey response.

Note: Fields with a type of `select_multiple` can have multiple selections made.
