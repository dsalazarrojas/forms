# Demographic Information & Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information and consent from participants in a study or research project.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read through the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2.  Answer all questions truthfully and accurately, ensuring that your responses reflect your actual experiences and circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Background
*   **participant_background**: A brief description of your background and context as a participant. This could include any relevant details about your identity, experiences, or circumstances.

### Gender
*   **gender**: Please select the gender that best describes you.
    *   Male
    *   Female
    *   Non-binary
    *   Prefer not to answer

### Date of Birth
*   **date_of_birth**: Provide your date of birth, if applicable.

### Participant Consent
*   **participant_consent**: This is a statement of consent for participation in the study. Please select 'True' if you consent, 'False' otherwise.
    *   'True'
    *   'False'

### Participant Email
*   **participant_email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication or follow-up.

### Participant Phone
*   **participant_phone**: Enter your phone number. (Note: This field is not required.)

### Country of Birth
*   **country_of_birth**: Select the country where you were born.
    *   United States
    *   Canada
    *   Mexico
    *   Other (Please specify)

### Consent Date
*   **participant_consent_date**: If you consented to participate, provide the date of your consent.

### Participant Consent Given
*   **participant_consent_given**: Please confirm if you have given consent for participation in the study.
    *   'True'
    *   'False'

## Tips
1.  Make sure to answer all fields truthfully and accurately.
2.  If you are unsure about a particular field, please contact the study administrators for guidance.
3.  If you have provided consent, you will be contacted for follow-up or further study participation.
