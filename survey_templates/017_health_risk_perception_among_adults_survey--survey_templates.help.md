# Health Risk Perception Among Adults Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the health perception among adults, with a focus on factors such as age, lifestyle, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions truthfully.
2. For select_one questions, select the correct response from the options provided.
3. For numerical questions, enter your response in the requested format (e.g., number of hours worked per week).
4. For questions that ask for a specific age group, select the corresponding option that best describes you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Age Group** (`demographic_info`): Please select the age group that best describes you.
* **18-24 years** (`age_group_0`): If you are 18-24 years old.
* **25-34 years** (`age_group_1`): If you are 25-34 years old.
* **35-44 years** (`age_group_2`): If you are 35-44 years old.
* **45-54 years** (`age_group_3`): If you are 45-54 years old.
* **55 years and above** (`age_group_4`): If you are 55 years or older.
* **What is your age?** (`question_1`): Enter your age in years.
* **What is your gender?** (`question_2`): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **What is your weight (in kg)?** (`question_3`): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Do you smoke?** (`question_4`): Select if you smoke.
* **Do you drink alcohol regularly?** (`question_5`): Select if you drink alcohol regularly.
* **Do you exercise regularly?** (`question_6`): Select if you exercise regularly.
* **What is your occupation?** (`question_7`): Enter your occupation.
* **What is your average monthly income?** (`question_8`): Enter your average monthly income.
* **How many hours do you usually work per week?** (`question_9`): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Do you get regular check-ups with a doctor?** (`question_10`): Select if you get regular check-ups with a doctor.
* **How many times did you get vaccinated this month?** (`question_11`): Select the number of times you got vaccinated this month.
* **How would you rate your overall health?** (`question_12`): Select the rating that best describes your overall health.
* **How would you rate your mental health?** (`question_13`): Select the rating that best describes your mental health.
* **How would you rate your physical activity level?** (`question_14`): Select the rating that best describes your physical activity level.
* **How would you rate your stress level?** (`question_15`): Select the rating that best describes your stress level.
* **Do you feel anxious or depressed?** (`question_16`): Select if you feel anxious or depressed.
* **Do you have any chronic illnesses?** (`question_17`): Select if you have any chronic illnesses.
* **How many times do you have been hospitalized in the last 12 months?** (`question_18`): Enter the number of times you have been hospitalized in the last 12 months.
