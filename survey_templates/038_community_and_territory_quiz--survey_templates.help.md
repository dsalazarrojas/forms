# Community And Territory Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge about community and territory concepts. It consists of 15 questions, divided into 5 sections: Community Basics, Territory Basics, Community Organization, Territorial Management, and Application. The quiz is intended to be a fun and informative way to learn about these topics.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by providing your participant information in sections 2-4 (Participant Name, Email, and Organization).
- For each section, read the questions and choose the best answer from the provided options.
- Make sure to answer all required questions (marked with a star \*).
- You can leave the bonus question (section 26) empty if you don't have any additional feedback.
- Click submit to complete the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Participant Email** (Participant Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Organization** (Organization, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, please enter its name.
* **Quiz Date** (Quiz Date, date, required): Enter the date you are taking this quiz.
* **Section 1 - Community Basics** (Section 1 - Community Basics, note, optional): Questions about community fundamentals.
*   **What best defines a community?** (Question 1 - What best defines a community, select_one, required): A group of people living in the same area, A group sharing common interests or characteristics, A political entity with defined borders, or All of the Above.
*   **Which is NOT a type of community?** (Question 2 - Which is NOT a type of community, select_one, required): Geographic, Interest-Based, Virtual, or Isolated.
*   **Who typically leads community initiatives?** (Question 3 - Who typically leads community initiatives, select_one, required): Elected Officials Only, Community Members and Organizations, External Consultants, or Government Agencies Only.
*   **Section 2 - Territory Basics** (Section 2 - Territory Basics, note, optional): Questions about territorial concepts.
*   **What is a territory?** (Question 4 - What is a territory, select_one, required): An area of land under jurisdiction, A region with defined boundaries, An area controlled by an entity, or All of the Above.
*   **What is the difference between territory and boundary?** (Question 5 - What is the difference between territory and boundary, select_one, required): They are the same, Territory is the area - Boundary is the edge, Boundary is larger than territory, or Territory is always political.
*   **What commonly causes territorial disputes?** (Question 6 - What commonly causes territorial disputes, select_one, required): Resource Access, Historical Claims, Strategic Location, or All of the Above.
*   **Section 3 - Community Organization** (Section 3 - Community Organization, note, optional): Questions about how communities organize.
*   **What is the purpose of community meetings?** (Question 7 - What is the purpose of community meetings, select_one, required): Social Gathering, Decision Making and Discussion, Entertainment, or Fundraising Only.
*   **Who are community stakeholders?** (Question 8 - Who are community stakeholders, select_one, required): Only Property Owners, Anyone Affected by Community Decisions, Only Business Owners, or Only Long-Term Residents.
*   **What are community resources?** (Question 9 - What are community resources, select_one, required): Only Financial Assets, Only Physical Infrastructure, Any Asset Available to the Community, or Only Government Services.
*   **Section 4 - Territorial Management** (Section 4 - Territorial Management, note, optional): Questions about managing territories.
*   **What is zoning?** (Question 10 - What is zoning, select_one, required): A type of fencing, Land Use Regulation, Property Tax Assessment, or Building Construction.
*   **What determines land use?** (Question 11 - What determines land use, select_one, required): Natural Features Only, Government Regulations, Community Needs and Planning, or All of the Above.
*   **What is territorial planning?** (Question 12 - What is territorial planning, select_one, required): Drawing Maps, Organizing Land Use for Future Development, Setting Property Taxes, or Building Roads.
*   **Section 5 - Application** (Section 5 - Application, note, optional): Questions applying concepts.
*   **Best way to engage community members?** (Question 13 - Best way to engage community members, select_one, required): Mandatory Attendance, Inclusive Communication and Participation, Financial Incentives Only, or Online Surveys Only.
*   **How should territorial conflicts be resolved?** (Question 14 - How should territorial conflicts be resolved, select_one, required): By Force, Through Negotiation and Mediation, By External Imposition, or By Ignoring the Issue.
*   **What is sustainable community development?** (Question 15 - What is sustainable community development, select_one, required): Building as much as possible, Meeting current needs without compromising future, Preserving everything as is, or Maximizing Profits.
*   **Bonus Question** (Bonus Question, text, optional): Leave empty if you don't have any additional feedback.
*   **Quiz Feedback** (Quiz Feedback, text, optional): Any feedback about this quiz.
