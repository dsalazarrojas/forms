# Transformation Success Factors Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transformation Success Factors Survey is a structured questionnaire designed to collect information about initiatives, their success factors, and other relevant details. This form helps in gathering data from individuals who are involved in initiatives or have relevant information about them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the type of initiative you are responding about.
2. Enter the name of the initiative.
3. Select all the relevant success factors related to the initiative.
4. Enter any comments or observations about the initiative.
5. Provide your contact information (email and phone number).
6. Select the date and time you submitted this form.
7. Choose your name as the submitter.
8. Enter your name as the submitter (optional but recommended).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of initiative?** (`initiative_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of initiative you are responding about.
* **Initiative name** (`initiative_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the initiative.
* **Another initiative name** (`initiative_name_2`, text, required: false): Enter another name related to the initiative (if applicable).
* **Goal** (`goal`, text, required: false): Enter the goal of the initiative.
* **Success Factors** (`success_factors`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the relevant success factors related to the initiative.
* **Another success factor** (`success_factors_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select another success factor related to the initiative (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or observations about the initiative.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, required: false): Enter another comment or observation about the initiative (if applicable).
* **Comments 3** (`comments_3`, text, required: false): Enter another comment or observation about the initiative (if applicable).
* **Another comments** (`comments_4`, text, required: false): Enter another comment or observation about the initiative (if applicable).
* **Contact** (`contact`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required: false): Select the date you submitted this form.
* **Time submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required: false): Select the time you submitted this form.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, required: false): Choose your name as the submitter.
* **Another submitter** (`submitter_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose another name as the submitter (if applicable).

## Tips
- Please answer all relevant questions to the best of your ability.
- Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the form owner or relevant personnel.
