# Organizational Agility Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Organizational Agility Evaluation Survey is designed to evaluate the agility of your organization, assessing areas such as leadership commitment, risk management, continuous improvement, and change management. The survey aims to help you identify areas that need improvement and provide valuable insights for developing targeted strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, selecting the best option that describes your current organizational state.
2. For questions with multiple options (Select One, Select Multiple, Number), select only one or multiple options that best fit your organization's situation.
3. For questions with text boxes (Description), provide a brief description of your response, elaborating on your answer.
4. Be accurate, and ensure your responses are consistent throughout.
