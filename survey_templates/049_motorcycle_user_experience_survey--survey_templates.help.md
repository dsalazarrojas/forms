# Motorcycle User Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from motorcycle users, focusing on their satisfaction levels and preferences. We'd like to understand their experiences to improve our services and better cater to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start with the introductory note, which provides background information about the survey.
2. Answer the questions about your motorcycle brand, model year, and riding hours to provide context for your feedback.
3. Express your satisfaction level with the overall experience on a scale from 1 to 5 (Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied).
4. Select all the aspects where you were satisfied or dissatisfied with your experience.
5. Provide any additional comments or information about your experience.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
7. Review and submit your form to help us improve.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro** (`1`, `note`, `not required`): Please read the introduction to this survey, which provides essential background information and instructions on how to complete this form.
* **Motorcycle Brand** (`2`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Indicate if you own or use the following motorcycle brands or types:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Model Year** (`3`, `number`, `not required`): Enter the year you bought or use your motorcycle for the first time.
* **Riding Hours** (`4`, `number`, `not required`): Report the approximate number of hours you've spent riding your motorcycle.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`5`, `select_one`, `not required`): Express your overall satisfaction level with your experience:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Satisfaction Questions** (`6`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Select all the aspects that you were satisfied or dissatisfied with:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Comments** (`7`, `text`, `not required`): Provide any additional comments or information about your experience.
* **Email** (`8`, `email`, `not required`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`9`, `text`, `not required`): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Additional Information** (`10`, `note`, `not required`): Leave any additional information that you think is relevant to your experience.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as you can when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure or don't know how to answer a question, that's okay. Just leave it blank or mention that in the comments section.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
