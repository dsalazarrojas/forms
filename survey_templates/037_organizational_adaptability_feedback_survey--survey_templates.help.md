# Organizational Adaptability Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess an individual's adaptability, open-mindedness, and willingness to challenge the status quo, all key components of a highly adaptable organization. It will help to identify areas of improvement and provide insights into how well an individual adapts to change, their ability to receive and act on feedback, and their willingness to innovate and learn from new experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review each question carefully and answer them honestly.
*   Select the options that best describe your current behavior and attitudes.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
*   Once finished, click submit to save your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How likely are you to adapt to change?** (`adaptability_question_1`, number, required): This question asks about your ability to adjust to new circumstances and changes in your work or environment.
*   **How confident are you in your ability to adapt to change?** (`adaptability_question_2`, number, required): This question assesses your self-assurance in adjusting to new situations.
*   **How open-minded are you to new ideas?** (`open_mindedness_question`, number, required): This question gauges your willingness to consider and explore new perspectives and suggestions.
*   **How willing are you to take calculated risks?** (`risk_taking_question`, number, required): This question measures your readiness to take measured risks to advance or improve your work or organization.
*   **How often do you seek feedback from others?** (`feedback_question_1`, number, required): This question evaluates your frequency of seeking input from colleagues, supervisors, or mentors.
*   **What is your preferred way of communication?** (`communication_question`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred method of communication (email, phone, or in-person) to indicate your preferred way of interacting with others.
*   **How comfortable are you with ambiguity and uncertainty?** (`ambiguity_question`, number, required): This question assesses your tolerance for unclear or uncertain situations.
*   **How willing are you to challenge the status quo?** (`challenge_status_quo_question`, number, required): This question measures your willingness to question existing procedures or norms.
*   **How often do you try new things?** (`innovation_question`, number, required): This question evaluates your inclination to experiment and innovate.
*   **How likely are you to adapt to new technologies?** (`tech_adaptability_question`, number, required): This question asks about your readiness to learn and incorporate new technological tools and methods.
*   **How often do you receive feedback from others?** (`feedback_question_1_2`, number, required): This question measures the frequency of feedback you receive from colleagues, supervisors, or clients.
*   **How do you rate your ability to learn from feedback?** (`feedback_rater_question`, number, required): This question assesses your capacity to learn from input and criticism.

## Tips

*   Please respond honestly and accurately to your experiences and perceptions.
*   Remember that all answers are private and anonymous.
*   This survey is designed to be a helpful tool for personal growth and improvement.
