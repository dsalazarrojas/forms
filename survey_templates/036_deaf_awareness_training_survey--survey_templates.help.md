# Deaf Awareness Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback and information about the Deaf Awareness Training Survey. It is meant to evaluate the effectiveness of the training program and gather data on participants' experiences and perceptions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have a clear understanding of each question and section.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction and Background** (1): This section is a brief introduction to the training program and its purpose. You may want to include any relevant background information or context about your experience with the training.
* **Knowledge and Understanding** (2): This section asks about your level of understanding of the training material. Please answer based on your actual knowledge and experience with the training.
* **Participation and Methods** (3): Describe how you participated in the training, including any methods or approaches you found helpful or challenging.
* **Satisfaction and Feedback** (4): Share your overall satisfaction with the training program and provide any feedback you may have. This is an opportunity to comment on what you liked or disliked about the program.
* **Contact and Follow-up** (5): If you would like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up, please provide your contact information.
* **Timing and Duration** (6): Share your thoughts on the timing and duration of the training program. Was it sufficient or did you feel rushed?
* **Additional Feedback** (7): Is there anything else you would like to share about your experience with the training program?

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation in this survey.
