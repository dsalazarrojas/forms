# Acrylic Signage Solutions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback about your experiences with our acrylic signage solutions. Your input will help us understand what we do well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form in a single session. You can save your progress and resume later.
2. Answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Select the best option for each question that reflects your honest opinion.
4. Do not leave any fields blank. If a question does not apply to you, select 'N/A' or 'None of the above'.
5. Submit your completed form once you have answered all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name or Facility** (`business_name_or_facility`, text, required): This is the name of your business or facility where the signage was installed.
* **Primary Use for Signage** (`primary_use_for_signage`, select_one, required): Please select the main purpose of the signage you received (e.g., Wayfinding, Branding, etc.).
* **Durability Satisfaction** (`durability_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate the durability of the signage from 1 (Fragile) to 5 (Very Durable).
* **Aesthetic Appeal Rating** (`aesthetic_appeal_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the aesthetic appeal of the signage from Poor to Excellent.
* **Preferred Finishes** (`preferred_finishes`, select_multiple, optional): Select the finishes you prefer for your signage (e.g., Clear, Frosted, etc.).
* **Ease of Installation** (`ease_of_installation`, select_one, required): Rate the ease of installation of the signage (e.g., Difficult, Moderate, Easy).
* **Current Provider Responsiveness** (`current_provider_responsiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the speed of communication and delivery from your current provider (e.g., Slow, Average, Fast).
* **Specific Improvement Opportunities** (`specific_improvement_opportunities`, text, optional): If there's anything you'd like to see improved, please specify.
* **Annual Signage Budget** (`annual_signage_budget`, number, optional): If you'd like to share, please provide your approximate annual signage budget in USD.
* **Frequency of Signage Updates** (`frequency_of_signage_updates`, select_one, required): Select how often you need signage updates (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, etc.).
