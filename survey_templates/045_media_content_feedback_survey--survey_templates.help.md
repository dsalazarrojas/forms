# Media Content Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from users on media content, specifically video, podcast, article, and campaign content. It allows content creators to understand how their content is perceived by users and make improvements for future content creation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Media Content Feedback Survey** field, which is the title of the form.
2. Provide a **Video Title** if the content you are providing feedback on is a video.
3. Select a **Rating** to indicate your satisfaction with the content (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
4. Enter a **Rating Value** if you wish to provide more detailed feedback.
5. Choose the **Media Type** of the content (Video, Podcast, Article, or Campaign).
6. Provide any additional **Comments** you would like to share about the content.
7. If you would like to be contacted for follow-up feedback, enter your **Email** address.
8. If you would like to be contacted for further feedback, provide your **Phone Number**.
9. Finally, select the **Date Submitted** when you completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Content Feedback Survey** (Text, Optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Video Title** (Text, Optional): If the content is a video, enter the title of the video.
* **Rating** (Select One, Optional): Select one of the options to indicate your satisfaction with the content.
* **Rating Value** (Number, Optional): Enter a numerical value to provide more detailed feedback.
* **Media Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of content (Video, Podcast, Article, or Campaign).
* **Comments** (Note, Optional): Share any additional comments about the content.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up feedback.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): If you would like to be contacted for further feedback, enter your phone number.
* **Date Submitted** (Date, Optional): Select the date when you completed the form.
