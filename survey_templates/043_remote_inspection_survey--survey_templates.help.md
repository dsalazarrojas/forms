# remote-inspection-survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information related to remote inspections and gather relevant details about the facilities, inspectors, and findings. It is used to ensure thoroughness and consistency in inspection processes.

## How To Complete This Form
### To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Start by filling out the facility information section, which includes any details relevant to the inspection, such as facility data.
- Next, provide contact information for the contact person responsible for the facility.
- In the inspector information section, fill out the details about the inspector, such as their name and any relevant notes.
- In the inspection findings section, describe any findings or issues observed during the inspection.
- You can also add additional inspection findings in the next section.
- If necessary, attach photos or evidence related to the inspection in the photos and evidence section.
- Finally, add any photos and evidence attachments and sign the form to complete it. Don't forget to select the inspection status and choose the findings and add any notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Information (Text)** (`facility_info`, Required: false): Enter any details about the facility that will be inspected.
* **Contact Information (Text)** (`contact_info`, Required: false): Provide details about the contact person for the facility, including name, phone and email.
* **Inspector Information (Text)** (`inspector_info`, Required: false): Enter details about the inspector, including any relevant notes.
* **Inspection Findings (Text)** (`inspection_findings`, Required: false): Describe any findings or issues observed during the inspection.
* **Additional Inspection Findings (Text)** (`inspection_findings_2`, Required: false): Add any additional findings or issues observed during the inspection.
* **Photos and Evidence (Text)** (`photos_and_evidence`, Required: false): Enter any photos or evidence related to the inspection.
* **Attach Photos and Evidence (Text)** (`photos_and_evidence_attachment`, Required: false): Upload photos or evidence related to the inspection.
* **Signature (Text)** (`signature`, Required: false): Sign and date the inspection report.
* **Date (Date)** (`date`, Required: false): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time (Time)** (`time`, Required: false): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Status (Select One)** (`select_one_inspection_status`, Required: false): Choose the status of the inspection.
* **Select Multiple Inspection Findings (Select Multiple)** (`select_multiple_inspection_findings`, Required: false): Select the findings and issues observed during the inspection.
* **Note (Note)** (`note`, Required: false): Add any additional notes about the inspection.

### Tips
- Please make sure all fields that are required are completed.
- Attach any relevant photos or evidence to support your findings.
- Choose the correct inspection status and findings accurately and honestly.
- Do not forget to sign the form.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
