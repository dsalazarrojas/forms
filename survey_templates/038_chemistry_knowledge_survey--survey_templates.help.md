# Chemistry Knowledge Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey aims to collect information on individuals' knowledge, experience, and attitudes towards chemistry. It will help to gather insights into various aspects of chemistry and provide a better understanding of how people perceive and learn chemistry.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
4. Take your time and don't rush through the form.
