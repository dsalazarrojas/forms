# Facility Maintenance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Maintenance Evaluation Form is used to assess the condition of a facility, including its physical state and maintenance procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "Introduction" section.
2. Provide information about the building in the "Building Info" section.
3. Evaluate the physical condition of the building and equipment in the "Building Condition" and "Facility Condition" sections, using numbers to indicate the condition (e.g. 1-5, where 1 is Very Poor and 5 is Excellent).
4. In the "Maintenance Plan" section, outline the current maintenance procedures and schedules.
5. Provide details about the equipment in the "Equipment Info" section.
6. Record any maintenance history in the "Maintenance History" section.
7. Describe the safety procedures in place in the "Safety Procedure" section.
8. Identify the emergency contact person and their information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
9. Provide contact information for the person who completed this form in the "Contact Person" section.
10. Leave any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" section.
11. Finally, evaluate the overall condition of the facility and select "Good", "Fair", "Poor", "Very Poor", or "Unusable" in the "Review" section.
12. Select the role of the person submitting this form (Admin, Manager, or User) in the "Submitted By" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: Provide a brief introduction to the facility and the purpose of this form.
* **Building Info**: Enter any relevant information about the building, such as its address, size, or occupancy.
* **Building Condition**: Evaluate the overall physical condition of the building on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is Very Poor and 5 is Excellent.
* **Facility Condition**: Evaluate the condition of the facility's equipment on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is Very Poor and 5 is Excellent.
* **Maintenance Plan**: Describe the current maintenance schedule and procedures for the facility.
* **Equipment Info**: Provide details about any equipment that requires maintenance or repair.
* **Maintenance History**: Record any notable maintenance events or repairs made to the facility.
* **Safety Procedure**: Describe the safety procedures in place to prevent accidents and injuries.
* **Emergency Contact**: Identify the emergency contact person and their information.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person completing this form.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments or notes about the facility's condition.
* **Review**: Evaluate the overall condition of the facility as Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor, or Unusable.
* **Submitted By**: Select the role of the person submitting this form (Admin, Manager, or User).

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* Use the numbers to evaluate the condition of the building and facility.
* Make sure to include any relevant information about the maintenance plan and safety procedures.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
