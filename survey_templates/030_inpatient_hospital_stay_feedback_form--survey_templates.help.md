# inpatient_hospital_stay_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your inpatient stay experience at our hospital. Your responses will help us understand your satisfaction with our ward, staff, and overall service quality. Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and thank you for your input.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct option for each field, using the provided options for select-one fields.
3. Enter your actual stay date in the date field.
4. Enter the ward number where you stayed.
5. Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you for further discussion.
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ward** (`ward`, text, required): Please enter the name of the ward where you stayed.
* **Stay Period** (`stay_period`, number, required): Enter the number of days you stayed in the hospital.
* **Ward Satisfaction** (`ward_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied were you with the ward's facilities and services? 
  • Very dissatisfied
  • Dissatisfied
  • Satisfied
  • Very satisfied
* **Hospital Satisfaction** (`hospital_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied were you with the hospital's overall service quality? 
  • Very dissatisfied
  • Dissatisfied
  • Satisfied
  • Very satisfied
* **Staff Satisfaction** (`staff_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied were you with the hospital staff's service quality? 
  • Very dissatisfied
  • Dissatisfied
  • Satisfied
  • Very satisfied
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied were you with your overall hospital stay experience? 
  • Very dissatisfied
  • Dissatisfied
  • Satisfied
  • Very satisfied
* **Ward Rating** (`ward_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the ward's facilities and services (1-5).
* **Hospital Rating** (`hospital_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the hospital's overall service quality (1-5).
* **Staff Rating** (`staff_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the hospital staff's service quality (1-5).
* **Hospital Suggestion** (`hospital_suggestion`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Ward Number** (`ward_number`, text, optional): Please enter the ward number where you stayed. If unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Date of Stay** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of your stay.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the hospital where you stayed.
* **Ward Name** (`ward_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the ward where you stayed.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Please do not leave any fields blank without a valid reason.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the hospital suggestion field.
- Your input will help us improve our services and provide better care for our patients. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
