# Ayurveda Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ayurveda Knowledge Quiz is a form designed to assess your knowledge of Ayurveda, a traditional system of traditional Indian medicine. This form will guide you through a series of questions and fields to evaluate your understanding of various aspects of Ayurveda, including its principles, practices, and philosophy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student and class information in the "Student and Class Information" section (Page 1).
2. Provide your full name in the "Full Name" field (Page 2).
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field (Page 2).
4. Select the date you attended the workshop or class from the calendar in the "Workshop or Class Date" field (Page 2).
5. Choose your level of previous experience with Ayurveda in the "Previous Experience with Ayurveda" field (Page 3).
6. Answer the following questions:
	* What are the three primary Doshas in Ayurveda? (Page 4)
	* What is the literal meaning of -Ayurveda-? (Page 5)
	* What is the main goal of Ayurvedic practice? (Page 5)
	* Which Dosha is primarily associated with the elements of Air and Space? (Page 6)
	* Which Dosha is primarily associated with the elements of Fire and Water? (Page 6)
	* Which Dosha is primarily associated with the elements of Earth and Water? (Page 7)
	* What is the primary focus of Ayurvedic nutrition? (Page 7)
7. Select your dominant Dosha type, if you have had an assessment (Page 8).
8. Rate the training content from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) in the "How would you rate the training content?" field (Page 9).
9. If you have any additional areas you would like to learn more about, type them in the "Areas you would like to learn more about" field (Page 10).
10. Provide any feedback or thoughts about the class in the "Participant Feedback" field (Page 10).
11. Finally, select the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student and Class Information (Page 1)** (`section_student_info`, note, required: false): This section is for providing student and class information. You can fill in your student name, class, or any other relevant details.
* **Full Name (Page 2)** (`full_name_ayu`, text, required: true): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address (Page 2)** (`email_address_ayu`, email, required: true): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further information or updates.
* **Workshop or Class Date (Page 2)** (`workshop_date_ayu`, date, required: true): Select the date you attended the workshop or class from the calendar.
* **Previous Experience with Ayurveda (Page 3)** (`ayu_experience_level`, select_one, required: true): Please select your level of experience with Ayurveda from the options provided:
	+ No previous experience
	+ Basic (General awareness)
	+ Intermediate (Some study/practice)
	+ Advanced (In-depth knowledge)
* **Knowledge Check (Page 4)** (`section_knowledge_check`, note, required: false): This section is for selecting the correct answers to the following questions. Please make sure to choose the most accurate option for each question.
* **What are the three primary Doshas in Ayurveda? (Page 4)** (`q1_primary_doshas`, select_one, required: true): The three primary Doshas in Ayurveda are:
	+ Vata, Pitta, and Kapha
	+ Fire, Water, and Earth
	+ Wind, Fire, and Stone
	+ Mind, Body, and Spirit
* **What is the literal meaning of -Ayurveda-? (Page 5)** (`q2_ayu_meaning`, select_one, required: true): The literal meaning of -Ayurveda- is:
	+ The Science of Life
	+ The Science of the Body
	+ The Science of Food
	+ The Science of Spirit
* **What is the main goal of Ayurvedic practice? (Page 5)** (`q3_ayu_goal`, select_one, required: true): The main goal of Ayurvedic practice is:
	+ Maintaining Balance and Harmony
	+ Curing Active Diseases
	+ Losing Weight and Building Muscle
	+ Predicting the Future
* **Which Dosha is primarily associated with the elements of Air and Space? (Page 6)** (`q4_vata_elements`, select_one, required: true): The Dosha primarily associated with the elements of Air and Space is:
	+ Vata
	+ Pitta
	+ Kapha
* **Which Dosha is primarily associated with the elements of Fire and Water? (Page 6)** (`q5_pitta_elements`, select_one, required: true): The Dosha primarily associated with the elements of Fire and Water is:
	+ Vata
	+ Pitta
	+ Kapha
* **Which Dosha is primarily associated with the elements of Earth and Water? (Page 7)** (`q6_kapha_elements`, select_one, required: true): The Dosha primarily associated with the elements of Earth and Water is:
	+ Vata
	+ Pitta
	+ Kapha
* **What is the primary focus of Ayurvedic nutrition? (Page 7)** (`q7_ayu_nutrition`, select_one, required: true): The primary focus of Ayurvedic nutrition is:
	+ Supporting Agni (Digestive Fire)
	+ Minimizing Caloric Intake
	+ High-Protein Concentration
	+ Fasting for Long Periods
* **What is your own dominant Dosha? (Page 8)** (`personal_dosha_ayu`, select_one, required: false): If you have had an assessment, please select your dominant Dosha type. If not, select "Not sure yet".
* **How would you rate the training content? (Page 9)** (`training_rating_ayu`, select_one, required: true): Rate the training content from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Areas you would like to learn more about (Page 10)** (`further_topics_ayu`, text, required: false): If you have any additional areas you would like to learn more about, please type them in.
* **Participant Feedback (Page 10)** (`feedback_text_ayu`, text, required: false): Please provide any feedback or thoughts about the class.
* **Date of Submission (Page 11)** (`submission_date_ayu`, date, required: true): Select the date of submission.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to complete the form.
- If you're unsure about any question or field, please ask for clarification.
- The form is designed to be completed in one session, so please make sure to have all the necessary information ready before proceeding.
