# Construction Defects Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Construction Defects Feedback Survey is designed to provide a structured and efficient way to collect information about defects found on construction sites or projects. This information is vital for identifying issues, scheduling repairs, and ultimately ensuring the quality and safety of the construction project. By completing this form, you're helping the maintenance or project management team to effectively manage and address defects in a timely and organized fashion.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Section 1: Property and Contact Information**
   - Fill in the property address where the issue has been noticed.
   - Provide your name and contact details for any further communication regarding this issue.
   - Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date as this information helps the repair team to access the property and address your concerns efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the property where the defect was observed. This helps the team locate the property easily.
- **Unit or Apartment Number** (`unit_number`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the unit or apartment number to help identify the exact location within the property.
- **Resident or Owner Name** (`resident_name`, text, required): Enter your name for easier communication and reference.
- **Phone Number** (`resident_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for further communication and updates about the progress.
- **Email Address** (`resident_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for updates and any additional information needed from the project team.
- **Defect Location (Room/Area)** (`defect_location`, text, required): Describe the exact location where the defect was found, e.g., kitchen, master bedroom, exterior wall.
- **Defect Category** (`defect_category`, select_one, required): Choose the type of issue from the options provided, such as structural, electrical, or plumbing.
- **Detailed Defect Description** (`defect_description`, text, required): Provide as much detail as possible about the issue observed, including any visual descriptions or photos.
- **Severity Level** (`severity_level`, select_one, required): Select the urgency of the issue, with options like low, medium, high, or critical.
- **Date Defect Was First Noticed** (`date_noticed`, date, required): Enter the date when the defect was first observed.
- **Access Instructions for Repair Team** (`access_instructions`, text, optional): If applicable, provide special instructions for the repair team, such as when to arrive or what access codes to use.
- **Has this issue been reported before?** (`previous_reports`, select_one, required): Confirm whether this is a new issue or if it has been previously reported.
- **Photos Attached to Submission** (`photos_attached`, select_one, required): Confirm if you've attached photos or will do so separately.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional information, use this field to provide it.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. This information is crucial for the maintenance or project team to address the issue efficiently.
* If you have any additional photos or information that you feel may be relevant, please attach them separately and note that in the `Photos Attached to Submission` field.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the defect in the `Detailed Defect Description` field, including visual descriptions or notes that can aid in its identification and repair.
