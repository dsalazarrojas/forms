# Quarterly Owner Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from property owners on their experience with our company and our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Rate your satisfaction with us in the "How satisfied are you with us" field by selecting one of the available options.
3. If you have any areas where you think we can improve, please list them in the "What can we improve" field.
4. If you'd like to provide your email address for future contact, please enter it in the "What is your email" field.
5. Select the type of property you own from the "What type of property is it" field.
6. Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have in the "additional_comments" field.
7. Provide the date and time you visited our property in the "date_visited" and "time_visited" fields.
8. Choose how you would like to contact us in the "contact_method" field.
9. Select any relevant options from the "comments" field.
10. Enter the name of the owner of the property in the "owner_name" field.
11. Enter the email address of the owner of the property in the "owner_email" field.
12. Enter the phone number of the owner of the property in the "owner_phone" field.
13. Enter the name of the company that owns the property in the "owner_company" field.
14. Enter the position of the owner of the property in the "owner_position" field.
15. Enter the website of the owner of the property in the "owner_website" field.
16. Enter the address of the owner of the property in the "owner_address" field.
17. Enter the city of the owner of the property in the "owner_city" field.
18. Enter the state of the owner of the property in the "owner_state" field.
19. Enter the zip code of the owner of the property in the "owner_zip" field.
20. Enter the country of the owner of the property in the "owner_country" field.
21. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "contact_person" field.
22. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "contact_email" field.
23. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "contact_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **How satisfied are you with us** (select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes your satisfaction with us.
  * Option 1: Very satisfied
  * Option 2: Somewhat satisfied
  * Option 3: Neutral
  * Option 4: Somewhat dissatisfied
  * Option 5: Very dissatisfied
* **What can we improve** (text, required): If there's anything you'd like to see changed or improved, please list it here.
* **What is your email** (email, optional): If you'd like to provide your email address for future contact, please enter it here.
* **What type of property is it** (select_one, optional): Select the type of property you own.
  * Option 1: Residential
  * Option 2: Commercial
  * Option 3: Other
* **additional_comments** (note, optional): If you'd like to add any additional comments or suggestions, please do so here.
* **date_visited** (date, optional): If you'd like to provide the date you visited our property, please enter it here.
* **time_visited** (time, optional): If you'd like to provide the time you visited our property, please enter it here.
* **contact_method** (select_one, optional): Select how you would like to contact us.
  * Option 1: Phone
  * Option 2: Email
  * Option 3: Mail
* **comments** (select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant comments or areas of improvement you'd like to provide.
  * Option 1: Option 1
  * Option 2: Option 2
  * Option 3: Option 3
* **owner_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the owner of the property.
* **owner_email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the owner of the property.
* **owner_phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the owner of the property.
* **owner_company** (text, optional): Enter the name of the company that owns the property, if applicable.
* **owner_position** (text, optional): Enter the position or title of the owner of the property, if applicable.
* **owner_website** (text, optional): Enter the website of the owner of the property, if applicable.
* **owner_address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the owner of the property, if applicable.
* **owner_city** (text, optional): Enter the city of the owner of the property, if applicable.
* **owner_state** (text, optional): Enter the state of the owner of the property, if applicable.
* **owner_zip** (text, optional): Enter the zip code of the owner of the property, if applicable.
* **owner_country** (text, optional): Enter the country of the owner of the property, if applicable.
* **contact_person** (text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person, if applicable.
* **contact_email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person, if applicable.
* **contact_phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person, if applicable.
