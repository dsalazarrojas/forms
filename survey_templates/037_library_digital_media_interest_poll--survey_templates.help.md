# Library Digital Media Interest Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on digital media usage and preferences among library users. The data collected will help library professionals understand user behavior and tailor services to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of library that you currently use (Public Libraries, School Libraries, Academic Libraries, Specialized Libraries, or Other).
2. Select all the digital media types that you use (e.g., Movies, Music, Podcasts, etc.).
3. Indicate how often you use digital media (Never, Less Than Once a Week, A Few Times a Week, A Few Times a Month, Daily).
4. Rate your satisfaction level with digital media (Not at All Satisfied, Slightly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Very Satisfied, Extremely Satisfied).
5. Choose all the digital media types that you would like to see more of (e.g., Movies, Music, Podcasts, etc.).
6. Provide your contact information (required).
7. Indicate your interest in future workshops on digital media (True, False, Undecided).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Public Libraries** (`Public Libraries`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks you to choose the type of library where you currently use digital media.
* **Digital Media Types** (`Digital Media Types`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to select all the digital media types you use, such as Movies, Music, Podcasts, etc.
* **How Often Do You Use Digital Media?** (`How Often Do You Use Digital Media?`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks you to indicate how often you use digital media, from Never to Daily.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`Satisfaction Level`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks you to rate your overall satisfaction level with digital media, from Not at All Satisfied to Extremely Satisfied.
* **What Would You Like to See More of in Digital Media?** (`What Would You Like to See More of in Digital Media?`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to choose all the digital media types that you would like to see more of, such as Movies, Music, Podcasts, etc.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required): This field asks you to provide your contact information, which is required for us to contact you.
* **Is There Interest in Future Workshops?** (`Is There Interest in Future Workshops?`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks you to indicate your interest in future workshops on digital media, with options True, False, or Undecided.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough when completing this form.
* If you select "Other" for any of the fields, please provide a brief description.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
