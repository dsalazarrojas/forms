# Agricultural Technology Adoption Farmer Insights Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather insights from farmers about their current and future adoption of agricultural technology.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by reviewing the form's sections carefully.
2. Ensure that you have a clear understanding of each field's purpose and relevance.
3. Complete each field accurately and completely, based on your real-life experiences and circumstances.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Demographics -- (1)**: This section collects general information about you as a farmer.
  - It is a note field, which means it serves as an introduction to the rest of the form.
  - Please do not fill out this field, but rather read it carefully to understand the context of the survey.

* **Age Range (2)**: This section asks about your age range as a farmer.
  - Please select one of the provided age ranges that best describes you.
  - This information helps us understand your background and experiences as a farmer.

* **Years Farming (3)**: This section asks about your years of farming experience.
  - Please select one of the provided experience levels that best describes your background.
  - This information helps us understand your level of expertise and familiarity with farming practices.

* **Farm Type (4)**: This section asks about the primary type of farm you operate.
  - Please select one of the provided farm types (Crop, Specialty Crops, Livestock, Dairy, or Mixed).
  - This information helps us understand the specific challenges and opportunities you face in your farm type.

* **-- Technology Adoption -- (5)**: This section serves as an introduction to the technology adoption section.
  - It is a note field, which means it does not require a response.
  - Please read it carefully to understand the focus on technology adoption.

* **Current Technology Usage (6)**: This section asks about the agricultural technologies you currently use.
  - Please select all the technologies that you are currently using from the provided options.
  - This information helps us understand your current level of technology adoption.

* **Barriers to Adoption (7)**: This section asks about the challenges that prevent you from adopting more agricultural technologies.
  - Please select all the barriers that apply to you from the provided options.
  - This information helps us understand the obstacles to technology adoption.

* **Information Sources (8)**: This section asks about where you learn about new agricultural technologies.
  - Please select all the sources that apply to you from the provided options.
  - This information helps us understand how you stay informed about new technologies.

* **Likelihood to Adopt New Tech (9)**: This section asks about your likelihood to adopt new technologies in the next 2 years.
  - Please select one of the provided likelihood levels (1-5).
  - This information helps us understand your potential for technology adoption.

* **Specific Technology Interest (10)**: This section asks about specific agricultural technologies that you are interested in.
  - Please enter a brief description or name of the technologies that interest you.
  - This information helps us understand your specific interests and needs.

* **Annual Technology Budget (11)**: This section asks about your estimated annual investment in agricultural technologies.
  - Please select one of the provided budget ranges.
  - This information helps us understand your financial capabilities and priorities.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and completely.
* If you have any difficulties or concerns, refer to this guide for help.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey, your input is invaluable.
