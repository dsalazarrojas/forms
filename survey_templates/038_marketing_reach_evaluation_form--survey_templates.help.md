# Marketing Reach Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Reach Evaluation Form is designed to assess the marketing reach and performance of your company or brand. This form helps you evaluate your marketing channels, audience, and growth challenges, providing valuable insights for future marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate answer for each field based on your marketing strategies and current situation.
2. Choose the channel(s) that best describe your marketing efforts.
3. Provide a brief answer to the questions about your audience, performance, and growth challenges.
4. Ensure you have a valid answer for each required field (marked with \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Channel Name** (2, select_one): Select the specific channel(s) you use for your marketing efforts, such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, or LinkedIn. This helps us understand your marketing focus.
* **Audience Reach** (3, number): Estimate the number of people you have reached through your marketing efforts. This can include the number of followers, subscribers, or viewers.
* **Marketing Channels** (4, select_multiple): Choose all channels you have used for marketing, such as social media, email, content marketing, paid media, and events.
* **Performance** (5, text): Briefly describe your overall performance indicators, such as website traffic, sales, or engagement.
* **Growth Challenges** (6, text): Identify any challenges you're facing in terms of marketing growth.
* **Analysis** (7, text): Summarize your analysis of the effectiveness of your marketing efforts, if any.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when answering questions.
- If you're unsure about a channel, select "Other" and provide a brief description.
- Ensure you provide valid answers for all required fields.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
