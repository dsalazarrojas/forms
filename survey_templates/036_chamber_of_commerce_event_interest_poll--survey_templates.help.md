# Chamber Of Commerce Event Interest Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather feedback from the respondents on their preferences for various aspects of chamber events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, and select from the provided options.
3. Ensure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Poll Date** (`poll_date`, date, required): This field is used to record the date of poll submission.
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): This field is for your full name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): This field is for your email address, which will be used for event updates.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): This field is for the name of your business.
* **Chamber Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select_one, required): Please select whether you are a current member, former member, non-member, or considering membership.
* **Event Type Preference** (`event_type_preference`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of events that interest you, such as networking mixers, educational seminars, or community service.
* **Preferred Day of Week** (`preferred_day`, select_multiple, required): Choose the days of the week that work best for you.
* **Preferred Time of Day** (`preferred_time`, select_multiple, required): Select the time of day that works best for you.
* **Event Frequency** (`event_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like events to occur.
* **Topic Interests** (`topic_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select the topics that interest you, such as business growth strategies or marketing and branding.
* **Speaker Preferences** (`speaker_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of speakers you prefer, such as industry experts or local business leaders.
* **Venue Preference** (`venue_preference`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred event location.
* **Acceptable Price Range** (`price_range`, select_one, required): Select the price range you are willing to pay for events.
* **Meal Preference** (`meal_preference`, select_one, optional): Select the meal option you prefer, such as breakfast or lunch.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary requirements, please specify them.
* **Childcare Needed** (`childcare_needed`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you would need childcare.
* **Virtual Attendance Option** (`virtual_attendance`, select_one, required): Choose whether you prefer virtual, hybrid, or in-person events.
* **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you would be interested in volunteering to help with events.
* **Sponsorship Interest** (`sponsorship_interest`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether your business is interested in sponsoring events.
* **Additional Suggestions** (`additional_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any other event ideas or suggestions you may have.
* **Likelihood to Attend** (`likely_to_attend`, select_one, required): Indicate how likely you are to attend chamber events.
