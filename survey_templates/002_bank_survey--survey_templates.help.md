# Bank Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bank Survey form is designed to understand your experiences and perceptions of our banking services. Your feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better services to you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Fill in all required fields (marked as **Required**).
4. Use the text field for free-form comments or suggestions.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, Optional): This field is used to capture your full name for reference purposes. If you wish to provide your name, please type it here.
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select_one, **Required**): Please select the category that best describes your relationship with our bank:
	+ Personal
	+ Business
	+ Small Business
	+ Corporate
* **Primary Banking Channel Used** (`primary_channel`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the channel you use most often for banking services:
	+ Branch
	+ Online Banking
	+ Mobile App
	+ ATM
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_overall`, select_one, **Required**): Rate your overall satisfaction with our services:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Service Timeliness** (`service_speed`, select_one, **Required**): Rate the speed of service you experienced:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Product Satisfaction** (`product_satisfaction`, select_one, Optional): How satisfied are you with the products you use:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likely_to_recommend`, select_one, **Required**): Would you recommend us to others?
	+ Definitely
	+ Probably
	+ Not sure
	+ Probably not
	+ Definitely not
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, Optional): Share any comments or suggestions for improvement:
	+ Please type your suggestions in this text field.
* **Permission to Contact** (`contact_permission`, select_one, Optional): May we contact you to follow up on feedback:
	+ True
	+ False

## Tips

* Please answer the questions based on your honest experience with our bank.
* If you have any specific comments or suggestions, please provide them in the Suggestions for Improvement field.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation in helping us improve our services.
