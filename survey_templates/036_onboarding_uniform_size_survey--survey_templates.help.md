# Onboarding Uniform Size Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onboarding Uniform Size Survey is a form designed to collect information about an employee's onboarding process, specifically their uniform size and any special requirements they may have. This information is used to ensure that the employee receives the correct uniform and any necessary equipment or accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by scrolling down to the first section, "Employee Information", which is the first page of the form.
2. Complete the "Employee Email" and "Employee Phone" fields with your own information. Note that these fields are not required, so you can leave them blank if you don't have this information readily available.
3. Proceed to the "Select Uniform Size" section, where you can select your uniform size from the available options.
4. If you have any special requirements, such as specific equipment or accommodations, select all that apply in the "Special Requirements" section.
5. Move on to the "Date" section, where you can enter the date you started or expect to start your onboarding process. Note that this field is not required.
6. In the "Time" section, enter the time you expect to start your onboarding process. Again, this field is not required.
7. You can add any additional notes or comments about your onboarding process in the "Note" section.
8. Finally, select your "Onboarding Status" from the available options, and add any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (`Employee Email`, `Employee Phone`, required: false): This section is for your contact information. Please enter your email and phone number to ensure that we can reach you for any updates or follow-ups.
* **Select Uniform Size** (`Select Uniform Size`, required: false): Choose your uniform size from the available options: Large, Medium, Small, or Extra Small.
* **Special Requirements** (`Special Requirements`, required: true): If you need any special equipment or accommodations, select all that apply from the available options.
* **Date** (`Date`, required: false): Enter the date you started or expect to start your onboarding process. This will help us track your progress.
* **Time** (`Time`, required: false): Enter the time you expect to start your onboarding process. Again, this field is not required.
* **Note** (`Note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Status** (`Onboarding Status`, required: false): Select your current onboarding status from the available options: Pending, Onboarding, or Completed.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about your onboarding experience.
