# electric_vehicle_charging_station_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about electric vehicle charging stations. It is intended to be completed by customers, clients, or users of electric vehicle charging services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **business_name**: This field is used to collect the name of the business providing the electric vehicle charging station.
* **name**: This field is used to collect the name of the person completing the form.
* **email**: This field is used to collect the contact email of the person completing the form.
* **phone**: This field is used to collect the phone number of the person completing the form. Please select one of the provided options to indicate the purpose of the call.
* **location**: This field is used to collect additional information about the location of the charging station.
* **comments**: This field is an open-ended text area where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the charging station's services.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (\*).
- Keep your comments and feedback clear and concise.
- If you are unsure about any field or have a question, contact the form owner for assistance.
