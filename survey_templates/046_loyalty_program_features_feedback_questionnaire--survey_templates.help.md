# Loyalty Program Features Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the "Loyalty Program Features Feedback Questionnaire," a form designed to gather feedback and insights from customers about their experiences with our loyalty programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each page carefully and select the relevant options or enter the required information.
2. Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Don't hesitate to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Intro Page
* **Intro Page** (`intro_page`, none, optional): This page serves as an introduction to the form and explains its purpose. Please note that this page is optional, and you can skip it if you're short on time.
* **Loyalty Program Features** (`program_features`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the loyalty programs you have experience with from the list provided. This will help us understand which programs you're referring to in your feedback.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with our loyalty program on a scale of 0-10. This will give us an idea of your overall experience.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improvement. This could be anything from changing program features to improving communication.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with our loyalty program again on a scale of 0-10. This helps us gauge your experience.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Rate our communication regarding the loyalty program. Options are: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
* **Program Enrollment** (`program_enrollment`, number, optional): Rate how easy it was to enroll in our loyalty program. Please note that this question is optional and only applies to customers who have actually enrolled.
* **Reward Claims** (`reward_claims`, number, optional): Rate how easy it was to claim rewards from our loyalty program. Only apply this to customers who have claimed rewards.
* **Reward Delivery** (`reward_delivery`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the delivery of rewards from our loyalty program. Only apply this to customers who have received rewards.
* **Program Participation** (`program_participation`, select_one, optional): Rate how likely you are to continue participating in our loyalty program. Options are: Very Likely, Somewhat Likely, Neutral, Somewhat Unlikely, Very Unlikely.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback you may have about our loyalty program.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, email, optional): Please provide your contact details if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or discussion.

**Tips**
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
* Don't forget to review your answers carefully before submitting the form!
