# Household Chore Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Household Chore Survey form is designed to collect information about household chores distribution among family members and gather feedback on how fair is the split of household chores. This form is intended for family members to provide an honest assessment of their contribution to household tasks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the number of household members from the options provided in the "Household Members" field.
2. Choose the member who performs the most household chores from the "Which member does the most household chores?" field.
3. Select the household chores that each member should have from the "Which household chores do you think each member should have?" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Contact" field.
5. Provide any suggestions or comments in the "Do you have any suggestions?" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Members** (`select_one`, required): Select the number of household members that live in the house. This will help us understand the dynamics of the household.
* **Which member does the most household chores?** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose the member who performs the most household chores in your household.
* **Which household chores do you think each member should have?** (`select_one`, required): Select the household chores that you believe each member should be responsible for. This will help us understand the expectations and responsibilities of each household member.
* **Do you have any suggestions?** (`note`, optional): Provide any suggestions or comments about the household chore distribution.
* **Contact** (`email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification or feedback.
* **Your Contact Information** (`text`, required): Enter your contact information so we can keep you updated about the survey results.
* **Fair Split of Household Chores** (`number`, optional): This field is not currently being used. Please ignore it.

**Tips**

* Be honest about your household's dynamic and the chores each member performs.
* Select the options that best fit your household situation.
* Make sure to enter your correct email address so we can contact you for further clarification or feedback.
