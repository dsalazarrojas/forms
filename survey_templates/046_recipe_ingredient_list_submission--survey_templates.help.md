**Recipe Ingredient List Submission - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about recipe ingredients for submission and review.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Recipe Name**, a brief title describing the recipe.
2. Enter the **Ingredient Name**, the name of the specific ingredient used in the recipe.
3. Enter the **Quantity** used in the recipe, if applicable.
4. Select the **Unit** of measurement for the ingredient (e.g., "grams", "milliliters", etc.).
5. Choose whether the ingredient is a **Yes/No** option for **Ingredient Type** (e.g., a key ingredient).
6. Select any **Ingredient Group** (e.g., "vegetable", "meat", etc.) that the ingredient belongs to.
7. Enter the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** of the recipe submission.
8. Enter **Submission Notes**, if any additional comments are needed.
9. Enter your **Email** address.
10. Enter your **Phone** number.
11. Enter your **Contributor Name**.
12. Enter your **Contributor Email**.
13. Select the **Submission Status** (Active/Inactive).
14. Enter the **Submission ID** number.
15. Enter the **Submission URL** of the recipe.
16. Add any **Submission Tags** that describe the recipe.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recipe Name**: Enter a brief title describing the recipe.
* **Ingredient Name**: Enter the name of the specific ingredient used in the recipe.
* **Quantity**: Enter the amount of the ingredient used (if applicable).
* **Unit**: Select the unit of measurement for the ingredient (e.g., "grams", "milliliters", etc.).
* **Ingredient Type**: Choose whether the ingredient is a key ingredient (Yes/No).
* **Ingredient Group**: Select any categories that the ingredient belongs to (e.g., "vegetable", "meat", etc.).
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of the recipe submission.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time of the recipe submission.
* **Submission Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contributor Name**: Enter your name.
* **Contributor Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contributor Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submission Status**: Select the status of the submission (Active/Inactive).
* **Submission Status Other (Specify)**: Enter any other status that applies.
* **Submission ID**: Enter the submission ID number.
* **Submission URL**: Enter the URL of the recipe submission.
* **Submission Tags**: Add any tags that describe the recipe.
* **Submission Comments**: Enter any comments about the submission.
