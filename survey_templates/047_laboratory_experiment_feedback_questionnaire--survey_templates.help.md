# laboratory_experiment_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from students on their experience in a laboratory experiment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each page and field. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of the experiment. Please answer each question honestly, and be as detailed as possible in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Experiment Details**: This section provides context for your feedback. Please enter the date and time of the experiment, as well as any additional comments you may have.
* **Teaching Method**: Indicate whether the teaching method used in the experiment was effective or not.
* **Learning Outcome**: Select all outcomes that apply to your experience.
* **Difficulty Level**: Rate the difficulty level of the experiment on a scale of 1-5.
* **Instructor Performance**: Assess the instructor's performance during the experiment.
* **Overall Experience**: Indicate your overall experience with the experiment.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Lab Experiment Date**: Enter the date of the experiment in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Lab Experiment Time**: Enter the time of the experiment in 12-hour clock format (e.g., 3:00 PM).
* **Student Involvement**: Select all areas where you were involved in the experiment.
* **Teaching Materials**: Indicate which teaching materials were used during the experiment.
* **Lab Environment**: Assess the lab environment during the experiment.
* **Instructor Support**: Evaluate the instructor's support during the experiment.
* **Lab Equipment**: Select all equipment that were used during the experiment.
* **Student Interest**: Indicate whether you were interested in the experiment.
* **Student Attention**: Select all areas where you felt your attention was focused during the experiment.
* **Teaching Style**: Evaluate the instructor's teaching style.
* **Student Participation**: Select all areas where you participated in the experiment.
* **Teaching Strategy**: Evaluate the instructor's teaching strategy used in the experiment.
* **Communication**: Select all areas where you felt you were able to communicate effectively with the instructor.
* **Organization**: Assess the organization of the experiment.
* **Student Satisfaction**: Indicate your level of satisfaction with the experiment.
* **Instructor Satisfaction**: Evaluate the instructor's satisfaction with the experiment.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

Tips:

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Be as detailed as possible in your responses.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the instructor or researcher who conducted the experiment.
