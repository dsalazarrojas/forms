# Property Investment Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Investment Confidence Survey is designed to gather information about your investment confidence and preferences for property investment. It will help us understand your investment habits and provide you with more tailored advice.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the most accurate options for each question.
3. Don't hesitate to answer "Other" if none of the provided options suit your needs.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Investment Confidence Survey Form**: This is the title of the survey.
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, select_one, optional): Choose your confidence level for investment (Low, Medium, High).
* **Time Frame** (`time_frame`, select_multiple, optional): Select how long you're considering for investment (Short Term, Medium Term, Long Term).
* **Investment Frequency** (`investment_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you invest (Quarterly, Bi-Annually, Annually).
* **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, text, optional): Enter the amount you're planning to invest.
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, select_multiple, optional): Select the financial institution you plan to use (Bank, Credit Union, Mortgage Company).
* **Investment Investment Adviser** (`investment_investment_adviser`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your investment adviser (Financial Advisor, Broker, Other).
* **Financial Investment Experience** (`financial_investment_experience`, select_one, optional): Select your level of financial investment experience (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of property you're considering (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
* **Investment Frequency Property** (`investment_frequency_property`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you invest in properties (Quarterly, Bi-Annually, Annually).
* **Confidence Score** (`confidence_score`, number, optional): Enter a score that represents your confidence level for investment (1-10).
* **Confidence Reasons** (`confidence_reasons`, text, optional): Enter reasons for your confidence level.
* **Confidence Justification** (`confidence_justification`, text, optional): Enter justification for your investment decisions.
* **Property Location** (`property_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the property you're considering.
* **Property Size** (`property_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of the property you're considering.
* **Investment Return** (`investment_return`, text, optional): Enter your expected investment return.
* **Investment Return Frequency** (`investment_return_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you expect investment returns (Quarterly, Bi-Annually, Annually).
* **Investment Return Amount** (`investment_return_amount`, number, optional): Enter the expected investment return amount.
* **Property Investment Risk** (`property_investment_risk`, text, optional): Enter your risk tolerance for property investment.
* **Investment Return Risk** (`investment_return_risk`, text, optional): Enter your risk tolerance for investment returns.
* **Investment Return Liquidity** (`investment_return_liquidity`, text, optional): Enter your liquidity expectations for investment returns.
* **Confidence Reasons Other** (`confidence_reasons_other`, text, optional): Enter other reasons for your confidence level.
* **Time Investment Return** (`time_investment_return`, text, optional): Enter your time expectations for investment returns.
* **Financial Institution Investment** (`financial_institution_investment`, select_multiple, optional): Select the financial institution you plan to use for investment (Bank, Credit Union, Mortgage Company).
* **Confidence Level Other** (`confidence_level_other`, text, optional): Enter other reasons for your confidence level.

## Tips

* Take your time, and answer honestly.
* If you're unsure, select "Other" and provide as much detail as possible.
* Review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your investment preferences.
* Don't hesitate to ask for help if you have any questions or concerns.
