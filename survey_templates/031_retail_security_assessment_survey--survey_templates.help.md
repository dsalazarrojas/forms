# Retail Security Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your store location's security measures and potential security risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your store location.
2. Enter your name and title (e.g., manager or security officer).
3. Enter your shift start and end times.
4. Enter your store hours.
5. Rate your store's safety score (e.g., 1-5).
6. List your store's security measures (e.g., CCTV cameras, alarms).
7. List any potential security risks (e.g., lack of lighting, suspicious activity).
8. Describe any security training provided to employees.
9. List any security equipment (e.g., access control systems, motion detectors).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Location**: Enter the address or name of your store location.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your name and title (e.g., manager or security officer).
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the start time of your shift in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 08:00 hours).
* **Shift End Time**: Enter the end time of your shift in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00 or 17:00 hours).
* **Store Hours**: Enter your store hours of operation.
* **Store Safety Score**: Rate your store's safety score from 1-5 (1 being lowest, 5 being highest).
* **Store Security Measures**: List any security measures in place at your store (e.g., CCTV cameras, alarms).
* **Potential Security Risks**: List any potential security risks or concerns (e.g., lack of lighting, suspicious activity).
* **Security Training**: Describe any security training provided to employees (e.g., regular training sessions).
* **Security Equipment**: List any security equipment used at your store (e.g., access control systems, motion detectors).

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Make sure to list any relevant security measures and potential risks.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, ask your supervisor or security officer for guidance.
* This form is meant for assessment purposes only and should not be used for actual security threat assessment or risk analysis.
