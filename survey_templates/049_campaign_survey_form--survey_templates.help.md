# Campaign Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from respondents about their opinions on a campaign, including their support level, key issues, main concern, voting intent, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Respondent Name** if you wish to provide it. If you prefer to remain anonymous, you can leave this field blank.
2.  Select your **Age Group** from the given options.
3.  Choose your **Support Level for Candidate** from the options provided.
4.  Select all the **Key Issues** that matter most to you.
5.  Provide your **Main Concern** about the current policy or candidate.
6.  Indicate whether you are **Voting Intent** in the upcoming election.
7.  Choose all the **Information Sources** where you get most of your political information.
8.  If you have any **Suggestions for the Campaign**, provide them in the text field.
9.  Choose whether the campaign can **Consent to Follow Up** with you.
10.  Finally, share any **Additional Comments** or feedback you have about the campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, Optional for anonymous responses): This field is optional, but it is recommended to fill it in to identify yourself. 
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one): Select the age group that best represents you.
* **Support Level for Candidate** (`support_level`, select_one): Choose how likely you are to support the candidate.
* **Key Issues** (`key_issues`, select_multiple): Select all the issues that matter most to you.
* **Main Concern** (`main_concern`, text): Provide a brief description of your main concern.
* **Voting Intent** (`voting_intent`, select_one): Indicate whether you will vote in the upcoming election.
* **Information Sources** (`information_sources`, select_multiple): Choose where you get most of your political information.
* **Suggestions for Campaign** (`suggestions`, text): Provide any suggestions you have for the campaign.
* **Consent to Follow Up** (`follow_up_consent`, select_one): Choose whether the campaign can contact you for more information.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Share any other feedback you have about the campaign.
