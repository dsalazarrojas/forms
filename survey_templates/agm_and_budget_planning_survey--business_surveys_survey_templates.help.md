# AGM And Budget Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from participants about their plans and opinions on the upcoming AGM and budget planning process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the options that best match your opinions and plans.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for follow-up and meeting materials.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, optional): Enter your phone number, including the country code if outside your local area.
* **Membership ID** (`membership_id`, `text`, optional): If you have a membership ID or account number, please enter it here.
* **AGM attendance intent** (`attendance_intent`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you plan to participate in the AGM:
	+ Will attend in person
	+ Will attend virtually
	+ Not attending
	+ Undecided
* **AGMs attended in past 5 years** (`past_agm_attendance_count`, `integer`, optional): Enter the number of AGMs you have attended in the past 5 years.
* **Date of response** (`response_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with recent AGM and budget planning:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate your overall rating of the planning process on a scale of 1-10.
* **Top budget priority areas** (`budget_priority_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select up to 3 areas you consider most important for budget allocation:
	+ Operating expenses
	+ Capital projects
	+ Reserve funds
	+ Membership services
	+ Events & programs
	+ Sustainability initiatives
* **Preferred annual budget change** (`preferred_budget_change_percent`, `decimal`, optional): Enter the percentage of budget change you prefer.
* **Acceptable funding sources** (`preferred_funding_sources`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the funding sources you consider acceptable:
	+ Membership fees
	+ Grants
	+ Sponsorships
	+ Fundraising events
	+ Service charges
	+ Reserve funds
* **Program priority level** (`program_priority_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate the priority level for member-facing programs:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ No opinion
* **Proposed policy changes** (`proposed_policy_changes`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any policy changes you would support:
	+ Change membership dues structure
	+ Introduce new service fees
	+ Reduce scope of certain programs
	+ Increase transparency/reporting
	+ Establish a designated reserve
	+ No changes
* **Satisfaction with last year's budget planning process** (`planning_process_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with last year's budget planning process:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **What would you improve or change about the AGM and budget planning process?** (`open_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide specific suggestions or examples of improvements.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
