# HIV Policy Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HIV Policy Feedback Survey is a tool designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness and implementation of HIV policies in different regions. The survey aims to identify areas of need, barriers to implementation, and priorities for improvement. Your honest and thorough feedback will help inform policy development and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the region with the greatest need for HIV policy support from the list provided.
2.  Rate the effectiveness of the HIV policy using the scale provided (Very Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Very Effective, Not Effective At All).
3.  Choose the barriers to implementing the HIV policy from the list (Lack of Funding, Lack of Community Engagement, Lack of Awareness, Lack of Policy Enforcement, Other (please specify)).
4.  Select the priorities for improving the HIV policy from the list (Increase Funding, Improve Awareness, Enhance Community Engagement, Improve Policy Enforcement, Other (please specify)).
5.  Provide your name and email address for contact purposes.
6.  Enter your phone number and address.
7.  Confirm that you have not already submitted this form.
8.  Enter the date and time you are submitting this form.
9.  Confirm your region's need for policy support.
10.  Provide any additional feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Which region has the greatest need for HIV policy support?** (Select from: Africa, Americas, Asia, Europe)
    This question is designed to identify the region with the greatest need for HIV policy support.
*   **How would you rate the effectiveness of the HIV policy?** (Scale: Very Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Very Effective, Not Effective At All)
    This question is to measure the perceived effectiveness of the HIV policy in your region.
*   **What are the barriers to implementing the HIV policy?** (Select from: Lack of Funding, Lack of Community Engagement, Lack of Awareness, Lack of Policy Enforcement, Other (please specify))
    This question is to identify the primary barriers to implementing the HIV policy.
*   **What are the priorities for improving the HIV policy?** (Select from: Increase Funding, Improve Awareness, Enhance Community Engagement, Improve Policy Enforcement, Other (please specify))
    This question is to determine the key areas for improvement in the HIV policy.
*   **What is your name?** (Text)
    This question is to collect your name for contact purposes.

    This question is to gather your email address for contact purposes.

*   **What is your address?** (Text)
    This question is to gather your address for contact purposes.

    This question is to avoid duplicate submissions.
*   **On what date did you submit this form?** (Date)
    This question is to record the date of submission.
*   **On what time did you submit this form?** (Time)
    This question is to record the time of submission.
*   **Is your region in need of policy support?** (Select: True, False)
