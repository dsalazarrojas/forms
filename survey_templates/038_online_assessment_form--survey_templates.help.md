# Online Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Online Assessment Form is a tool used to collect information from users for online assessments. It is designed to gather feedback and evaluate user responses to a series of questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: "Survey Question", "Date Submitted", and "Survey Type" are not required but "User Answer" is required.
2. Enter your "User Answer" in the text field.
3. Fill in the remaining fields as per your choice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Question** (text, required): This field is for entering a question that you want to be assessed. Please provide a clear and concise question that is free of any grammatical errors.
* **User Answer** (text, required): This field is for the user to provide their response to the question entered above. Please make sure that your answer is complete and free of grammatical errors.
* **Date Submitted** (date, required): This field is for the date when the assessment is submitted. Please enter the date in the required format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Email** (email, optional): This field is for your email address. This information will be used for future correspondence regarding the assessment.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): This field is for your phone number. This information will be used for future contact regarding the assessment.
* **Score** (number, optional): This field is for scoring the assessment. Please enter the score in the required format (e.g., 0-100).
* **Comments** (note, optional): This field is for any additional comments you may want to make regarding the assessment.
