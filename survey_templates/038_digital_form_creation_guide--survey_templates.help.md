# Digital Form Creation Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Digital Form Creation Guide" form is a tool used to create and configure digital forms for internal use. It provides a framework for users to design and manage their digital forms, enabling them to gather information, feedback, or data from other users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select a title for your digital form. This will be the main heading that appears at the top of the form.
2.  Choose a category for your form from the available options. This will help in organizing and filtering forms within the application.
3.  Provide a description of your form, which should briefly explain its purpose and usage.
4.  Select the category of your form, which is crucial for further categorization within the application.
5.  Optionally, add a tag to your form for easier identification and searching.
