# Peer Code Review Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from reviewers on code submissions. Reviewers will use this form to provide comments, recommendations, and ratings on code submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of feedback you want to provide (Review, Code Review, or Code Quality).
2. Choose the relevant submission(s) from the Code Submissions dropdown list.
3. Provide any relevant submission notes.
4. Select the submission status (In Progress, Done, or Archived).
5. Enter your comments, comments date, and comments time.
6. Provide your reviewer comments duration.
7. Enter the submitter's name, job title, and department.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Provide a signature for yourself and the submitter.
10. Review and review your submission date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Code Submissions**: This dropdown list allows you to choose which submission you are providing feedback on.
  - Select all relevant submissions that you want to provide feedback on.
  - If there are multiple submissions, separate them with commas.
- **Submission Notes**: This text box is for any additional information you want to provide about the submission.
  - Be as detailed as possible in your notes.
- **Feedback Type**: This dropdown list allows you to select the type of feedback you are providing.
  - Review: Provide general feedback on the submission.
  - Code Review: Provide in-depth feedback on the code quality.
  - Code Quality: Provide feedback on the code quality.
- **Reviewer Score**: This number box allows you to rate the submission on a scale.
  - Use a number from 1-5 to rate the submission.
- **Reviewer Comments**: This text box is for any additional comments you want to provide.
  - Be as detailed as possible in your comments.
- **Reviewer Recommendations**: This text box is for any recommendations you have for the submitter.
  - Be as detailed as possible in your recommendations.
- **Reviewer Signature**: This text box is for your signature.
  - Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID badge.
- **Submitter Signature**: This text box is for the submitter's signature.
  - Enter the submitter's name as it appears on their business card or ID badge.
- **Reviewer Name**: This text box is for your name.
  - Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID badge.
- **Reviewer Job Title**: This text box is for your job title.
  - Enter your job title as it appears on your business card or ID badge.
- **Reviewer Department**: This text box is for your department.
  - Enter your department as it appears on your business card or ID badge.
- **Reviewer Email**: This text box is for your email address.
  - Enter your email address in the format of "username@domain.com".
- **Submission Status**: This dropdown list allows you to select the submission status.
  - In Progress: The submission is still being worked on.
  - Done: The submission is complete.
  - Archived: The submission is no longer active.
- **Reviewer Comments Date**: This date box is for the date of your comments.
  - Enter the date in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD".
- **Reviewer Comments Time**: This time box is for the time of your comments.
  - Enter the time in the format of "HH:MM".
- **Reviewer Comments Duration**: This number box is for the duration of your comments.
  - Enter the time duration in the format of number of minutes.
- **Submission Date**: This date box is for the date of the submission.
  - Enter the date in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD".
- **Submission Time**: This time box is for the time of the submission.
  - Enter the time in the format of "HH:MM".
