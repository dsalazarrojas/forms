# Beverage New Drink Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The form is designed to gather feedback from customers who have tried a new drink, providing a thorough assessment of their experience, satisfaction, and preferences. This feedback will help identify areas for improvement and guide future product development.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field (if you want to receive follow-up information).
3.  Choose the date you tried the new drink from the calendar picker in the "Visit Date" field.
4.  Enter the name of the new drink in the "New Drink Name" field.
5.  Select the size of the drink you ordered from the options in the "Drink Size" field.
6.  Choose the temperature of the drink you received from the options in the "Drink Temperature" field.
7.  Rate your overall satisfaction in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.
8.  Rate the taste of the drink from the options provided in the "Taste Rating" field.
9.  Choose how you rate the sweetness level of the drink from the options in the "Sweetness Level" field.
10. Choose how you rate the flavor intensity of the drink from the options in the "Flavor Strength" field.
11. Rate the visual presentation of the drink from the options in the "Presentation Rating" field.
12. Rate the value for money of the drink from the options in the "Value for Money Rating" field.
13. Select whether you would order the drink again from the options in the "Would You Order This Drink Again" field.
14. Choose whether you would recommend the drink to others from the options in the "Would You Recommend to Others" field.
15. Enter what you liked most about the drink in the "What Did You Like Most" field.
16. (Optional) Enter what you liked least about the drink in the "What Could be Improved About This Drink" field.
17. Rate how this drink compares to existing menu items from the options in the "How Does This Compare to Existing Menu Items" field.
18. (Optional) Enter your suggestions for improvement in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.
19. (Optional) Enter any other comments about the new drink in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or business card.
*   **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes, if you wish.
*   **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you tried the new drink from the calendar picker.
*   **New Drink Name** (`drink_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the new drink you tried.
*   **Drink Size** (`drink_size`, `select_one`, required): Choose the size of the drink you ordered from the options: Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large.
*   **Drink Temperature** (`drink_temperature`, `select_one`, required): Choose the temperature of the drink from the options: Hot, Iced, Blended, Room Temperature.
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the drink from the options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
*   **Taste Rating** (`taste_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the taste of the drink from the options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
*   **Sweetness Level** (`sweetness_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you rate the sweetness level from the options: Too Sweet, Slightly Sweet, Just Right, Not Sweet Enough, Unsweetened.
*   **Flavor Strength** (`flavor_strength`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you rate the flavor intensity from the options: Too Strong, Slightly Strong, Just Right, Slightly Weak, Too Weak.
*   **Presentation Rating** (`presentation_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the visual presentation of the drink from the options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
*   **Value for Money Rating** (`value_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the value for money of the drink from the options: Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, Very Poor Value.
*   **Would You Order This Drink Again** (`would_order_again`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you would order the drink again from the options: Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No.
*   **Would You Recommend to Others** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you would recommend the drink to others from the options: Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No.
*   **What Did You Like Most** (`favorite_aspect`, `text`, required): Enter what you liked most about the drink.
*   **What Could be Improved About This Drink** (`least_favorite_aspect`, `text`, optional): (Optional) Enter what you liked least about the drink.
*   **How Does This Compare to Existing Menu Items** (`comparison_to_existing`, `select_one`, required): Rate how this drink compares to existing menu items from the options: Much Better, Somewhat Better, About the Same, Somewhat Worse, Much Worse.
*   **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): (Optional) Enter your suggestions for improvement.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): (Optional) Enter any other comments about the new drink.

## Tips

*   Be honest and specific when answering the questions.
*   Take your time to think about your experience and preferences before answering.
*   If you're unsure or have no preference, choose the middle option.
*   Enter your email address if you want to receive follow-up information.
*   Enter any other comments or suggestions you may have about the drink.
*   Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
