# Internet Addiction Disorder Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's internet usage habits and perceived level of addiction to the internet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. If you are unsure about how to answer a question, try to choose the answer that best reflects your typical behavior.
3. Please select all relevant options for multi-choice questions.
4. Enter your answers in the spaces provided.
5. Once you have completed the form, submit your responses for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **internet_use**: (text, optional)
  This question asks how often you use the internet. Please answer with a brief description of your typical internet usage habits.
* **internet_frequency**:
  This question asks about the frequency of your internet use. Please select all options that apply to you:
  + Option 1: I use the internet daily
  + Option 2: I use the internet several times a week
  + Option 3: I use the internet occasionally
  + Option 4: I do not use the internet regularly
  + Option 5: I do not use the internet at all
* **internet_duration**: (time, optional)
  This question asks how much time you spend on the internet. Please answer in the format HH:MM (e.g., 02:30).
* **internet_amount**: (number, optional)
  This question asks about the total amount of time you spend on the internet per week. Please enter a numerical value.
* **internet_addiction_score**: (number, optional)
  This question asks you to rate your level of internet addiction. Please rate from 1 to 10, with 1 being "not at all addicted" and 10 being "very addicted".
* **teacher_support**:
  This question asks how often you feel your teacher is supportive of your online activities. Please answer with the following options:
  + Option 1: Always
  + Option 2: Most of the time
  + Option 3: Sometimes

Note: This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used for self-diagnosis or self-treatment.
