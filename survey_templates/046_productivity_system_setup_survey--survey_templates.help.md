# Productivity System Setup Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Productivity System Setup Survey" form is designed to gather information about your team's productivity levels, current process improvements, and other aspects of the workplace that impact productivity. This information will help us understand areas for improvement and develop strategies to enhance the productivity system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the answers that best describe your team's productivity levels, current process improvements, and other aspects of the workplace from the provided options.
2. Enter your answers in the designated fields.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Work Patterns", "Team Structure", "Communication Channels", "Collaboration Tools", and "Decision Making Process" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Productivity Levels** (High, Low): Choose the best description of your team's current productivity levels.
* **Current Process Improvement** (Yes, No): Indicate if you think there are areas for process improvement in your current workflow.
* **Workplace Efficiency** (Yes, No): Select whether your workplace is efficient or not.
* **Productivity System Features** (Yes, No): Choose the features that you would like to see in a productivity system.
* **Work Patterns** (text): Enter your thoughts on work patterns that have contributed to your productivity.
* **Team Structure** (text): Describe your team's structure and how it impacts productivity.
* **Communication Channels** (text): List the communication channels you use in your team.
* **Collaboration Tools** (text): Enter the collaboration tools you use in your team.
* **Decision Making Process** (text): Describe your team's decision-making process.
* **Productivity System Implementation** (Yes, No): Indicate if you support or oppose implementing a productivity system.
* **Feedback Mechanisms** (Yes, No): Select whether you support or oppose having feedback mechanisms in place.
* **Additional Comments** (text): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
