# Matting Damage Cost Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about matting damage and estimate or report its cost. Please fill out the form to provide a detailed description of the damage and the total cost associated with it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and fill out the required information.
2. For the "Damage Description" section, provide a clear and concise description of the damage.
3. In the "estimate" section, enter the estimated cost of the damage if you're unsure about the exact cost.
4. In the "Total Cost" section, enter the total cost of the matting damage, if you have this information available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Damage Description**: Describe the nature of the matting damage. Be as detailed as possible, including the location, cause, and any other relevant information.
* **estimate**: Enter an estimated cost of the damage, or leave blank if you're unsure. This is a number field where you can input the estimated cost, if available.
* **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of the matting damage, if you have this information available. This is a number field where you can input the total cost.

## Tips

* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please do not leave them blank. If you're unsure, it's better to estimate or indicate that you're unsure rather than leaving it blank.
* If you have any additional information that you think would be helpful to provide, please include it in the "Damage Description" field.
