# Ministry Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ministry Satisfaction Survey is a tool to gather feedback from ministry stakeholders to evaluate their satisfaction level with the ministry's services, programs, and community events. This information will help the ministry to understand what they are doing well and where they can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and concisely.
2. Select the rating that best reflects your opinion for each question.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in the corresponding text box.
4. Make sure to submit your feedback before closing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel about our ministry?** (`ministry_satisfaction_survey`, text, optional): This question is an open-ended question where you can express your overall feeling about our ministry.
* **How do you rate the quality of our services?** (`services`, select_one, optional): This question asks you to rate the quality of our services using a scale of Very Poor, Poor, Average, Good, Excellent. Please select the rating that best reflects your opinion.
* **How do you rate the quality of our programs?** (`programs`, select_one, optional): Similar to the previous question, this one asks you to rate the quality of our programs.
* **How do you rate your participation in our community events?** (`community_engagement`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks you to rate your level of participation in our community events using a scale of Very Low, Low, Average, High, Very High.
* **Do you have any comments?** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted for further clarification or follow-up, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have any phone number that you would like to be reached at, please type it here.
* **What do you think are the biggest areas of improvement for our services?** (`services_quality`, text, optional): This question is open-ended, and you can type any suggestions or areas that you think we can improve in our services.
* **What do you think are the biggest areas of improvement for our programs?** (`programs_quality`, text, optional): Similar to the previous question, this one is also open-ended.
* **What do you think are the biggest areas of improvement for our community events?** (`community_events_quality`, text, optional): This question is open-ended, and you can type any suggestions or areas that you think we can improve in our community events.
* **What would you like to see improved in our services?** (`services_improvement`, text, optional): This question is open-ended, and you can type any suggestions or areas that you would like to see improved in our services.
* **What would you like to see improved in our programs?** (`programs_improvement`, text, optional): Similar to the previous question, this one is also open-ended.
* **What would you like to see improved in our community events?** (`community_events_improvement`, text, optional): This question is open-ended, and you can type any suggestions or areas that you would like to see improved in our community events.
* **Do you have any suggestions for our services?** (`services_comments`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions, please type them here.
* **Do you have any suggestions for our programs?** (`programs_comments`, text, optional): Similar to the previous question, this one is also open-ended.
* **Do you have any suggestions for our community events?** (`community_events_comments`, text, optional): This question is open-ended, and you can type any suggestions that you have for our community events.
