# in_person_meetings_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data on in-person meetings. It is intended for users who have participated in or attended in-person meetings to report on their goals, frequency, length, purpose, and feedback received during these meetings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select one or multiple options for each field, as indicated by the field's label.
* Ensure that you have the necessary information and permissions to provide honest and accurate feedback.
* Be as detailed as possible when answering questions with multiple options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Goals** (`goals`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Choose all the goals that you aimed to achieve or did achieve during the meeting. Select one or multiple options.
- **Meeting Frequency** (`meeting_frequency`, `select_one`, required: true):
    Choose how often you meet with this group/person. Select only one option.
- **Meeting Length** (`meeting_length`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Choose all the meeting lengths that you usually meet. Select one or multiple options.
- **Meeting Purpose** (`meeting_purpose`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Choose all the purposes of the meetings you attend. Select one or multiple options.
- **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Choose all the frequencies at which you give and receive feedback during meetings. Select one or multiple options.
- **Feedback Value** (`feedback_value`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Choose all the values you assign to feedback received during meetings. Select one or multiple options.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false):
    Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your in-person meetings.

## Tips

* Be honest and detailed in your answers.
* Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form author at <form@gic.mx>.
