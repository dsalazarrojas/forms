# Self Report Personality Test Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your personality traits and demographics in order to create a better understanding of your personality type and characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the survey.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, being as honest as possible.
3. Select your answer from the provided options for questions with select options.
4. Enter your text answers for questions that require text input (e.g. job title, company, email, and phone number).
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.
