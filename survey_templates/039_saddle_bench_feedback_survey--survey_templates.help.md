# Saddle Bench Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Saddle Bench Feedback Survey form. Please carefully read each section to ensure that you provide the necessary information for your feedback to be effectively recorded.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Enter your contact information (name, email, phone number, and department) to allow us to reach out to you for further clarification or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Saddle Bench Feedback Survey**:
	+ This is the introductory text that welcomes you to the survey.
* **Overall Satisfaction Level**:
	+ Please rate your overall satisfaction level with the Saddle Bench, on a numerical scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Comfort Level**:
	+ Indicate your comfort level with the Saddle Bench, also on a numerical scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Comfort Rated Saddle Bench**:
	+ Rate your comfort level with the Saddle Bench, on a numerical scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Improvement Ideas**:
	+ Share any ideas or suggestions you may have for improving the Saddle Bench.
* **Contact Name**:
	+ Enter your name as you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Contact Email**:
	+ Enter your email address where you can be reached for further clarification or follow-up.
* **Contact Phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further clarification or follow-up.
* **Contact Department**:
	+ Enter the department you are part of, this will help us to route your feedback accordingly.
* **Contact Title**:
	+ Enter your title or position in the company.
* **Comments**:
	+ Provide any additional comments or explanations related to your feedback.
* **Company**:
	+ Select the company you are associated with from the provided options.
* **Team**:
	+ Select all teams you are part of from the provided options.
* **Facility Name**:
	+ Select the facility you are referring to from the provided options.
* **Installation Number**:
	+ Enter the installation number associated with the Saddle Bench.
* **Survey Date**:
	+ Enter the date you completed this survey.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are essential for your feedback to be recorded correctly. Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately.
