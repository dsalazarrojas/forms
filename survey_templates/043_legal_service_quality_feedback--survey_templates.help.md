# Legal Service Quality Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Service Quality Feedback form is a tool used to gather feedback from clients who have received our legal services. This form is used to collect information on the quality of our services, allowing us to identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes to better serve our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each question to the best of your ability.
* Please be honest and provide specific feedback on your experience with our legal services.
* Make sure to answer all questions that apply to you.
* You may select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.

* **Feedback Question 2** (Select Multiple, Feedback Question 2, optional): This question allows you to select all applicable options that describe your experience with our legal services. Choose all that apply:

* **Feedback Question 3** (Text, Feedback Question 3, optional): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with our legal services.
* **Feedback Question 4** (Date, Feedback Question 4, optional): This question asks for the date you received our legal services. Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Feedback Question 5** (Time, Feedback Question 5, optional): This question asks for the time you received our legal services. Please enter the time in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Email** (Email, Email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Phone** (Phone, Phone, optional): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Name** (Name, Name, optional): Please enter your name so we can address you personally in our response.
