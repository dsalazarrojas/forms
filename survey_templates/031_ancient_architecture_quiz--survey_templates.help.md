# Ancient Architecture Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a quiz designed to test your knowledge of ancient architectural structures and their significance. It covers various civilizations and famous monuments, and is intended to be a brief and multiple-choice assessment of your understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct answer for each question.
3. When asked to choose multiple options, make sure to select all applicable choices.
4. If prompted for text, provide a brief answer.
5. Make sure to enter numbers only for the year of completion of the Great Pyramid of Giza.
6. When asked to describe the impact of the Roman Arch, provide a brief explanation.
7. Finally, decide whether you want to see your score immediately after submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Ancient Architecture Quiz --** (`section_quiz_header`, Note, None): This section is an introduction to the quiz.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, required): Enter your email address to receive your score and results.
* **-- Quiz Questions --** (`section_quiz_questions`, Note, None): This section contains the quiz questions.
* **In which city is the Parthenon located?** (`parthenon_location`, Select One, required): Select the city where the Parthenon is located. The options are: Athens, Rome, Alexandria, and Istanbul.
* **What was the primary use of the Roman Colosseum?** (`colosseum_primary_use`, Select One, required): Choose the primary use of the Roman Colosseum. The options are: Gladiatorial Games, Religious Ceremonies, Market Place, and Theater.
* **Which of these are features of Mesopotamian architecture?** (`mesopotamian_architectural_features`, Select Multiple, required): Select all features that apply to Mesopotamian architecture. The options are: Ziggurats, Mud Bricks, Arches, Glass Windows, and Steel Columns.
* **Which dynasty began the construction of the Great Wall of China?** (`great_wall_of_china_dynasty`, Select One, required): Choose the dynasty that started the construction of the Great Wall of China. The options are: Qin, Han, Ming, and Tang.
* **Approximately what year was the Great Pyramid of Giza completed (BC)?** (`year_the_great_pyramid_was_completed_bc`, Number, required): Enter the approximate year of completion of the Great Pyramid of Giza in BC.
* **What is a widely accepted theory for the purpose of Stonehenge?** (`purpose_of_stonehenge`, Select One, required): Choose a widely accepted theory for the purpose of Stonehenge. The options are: Astronomical Observatory, Burial Site, Market, and Palace.
* **Briefly describe the impact of the Roman Arch on ancient construction.** (`describe_the_roman_arch_impact`, Text, required): Provide a brief explanation of the impact of the Roman Arch on ancient construction.
* **-- Finalize --** (`section_finalize`, Note, None): This section indicates the end of the quiz.
* **Would you like to see your score immediately after submission?** (`send_score_immediately`, Select One, required): Choose whether you want to see your score immediately after submission. The options are: True and False.
