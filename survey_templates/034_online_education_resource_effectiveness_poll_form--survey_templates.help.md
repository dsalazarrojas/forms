# Online Education Resource Effectiveness Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback and input on online education resources you have used. Your responses will help us understand their effectiveness and identify areas for improvement. Your participation is greatly appreciated!

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply work through each page from start to finish. You do not need to be an expert in any of the fields, and you can answer each page as you see fit. You can submit the form at any time, and you can return to it later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Page (Optional)**: This is the introduction page to the form. You can skip this page if you are ready to proceed.
  * **Page Label (first_page)**: This page is a placeholder for the form's introduction.
- **Online Education Resource (second_page)**: Please enter the name or description of the online education resource you are providing feedback on.
  * **Label (online_education_resource)**: Enter the name or description of the online resource in a few words.
- **Resource Effectiveness (third_page)**: Please select a date when you last used the online education resource.
  * **Label (resource_effectiveness)**: Select a date when you last used the online resource. This will help us understand when you used it.
- **Feedback on Tool (fourth_page)**: Please select the time you spent on the online education resource.
  * **Label (feedback_on_tool)**: Select the time you spent using the online resource. This will help us understand its usability.
- **Impact of Tool (fifth_page)**: Please select all the options that describe the impact of the online education resource on your learning.
  * **Label (impact_of_tool)**: Select all the options that apply to how the online resource affected your learning.
  * Example: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3
- **Improvements to Make (sixth_page)**: Please select the option for the area that needs improvement in the online education resource.
  * **Label (improvements_to_make)**: Select one option for how the online resource could be improved.
- **Better Learning Decisions (seventh_page)**: Please enter an email address that we can contact you for further questions or discussions.
  * **Label (better_learning_decisions)**: Enter your email address for further contact. This will help us discuss your feedback if needed.
- **Comments Section (eighth_page)**: You can provide any additional comments or feedback on the online education resource.
  * **Label (comments_section)**: Use this space to share any further thoughts or comments on the online resource.

Note: You can save the form and complete it at a later time if needed.
