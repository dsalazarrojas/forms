# Recruitment Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on the recruitment process. Your input will help us understand what worked well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each question carefully and answer them based on your experience with the recruitment process.
2. For text fields, please provide as much detail as you can.
3. If you have any other comments or suggestions, use the "Do you have any other comments or suggestions?" field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Note)**: This is a placeholder section to acknowledge you've started the survey.
* **How would you rate the overall evaluation process? (Number)**: Please rate the overall evaluation process on a scale of 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good).
* **What criteria do you think are used to evaluate candidates? (Select Multiple)**: Choose the criteria you think are used to evaluate candidates.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **How can the evaluation process be improved? (Text)**: Share your thoughts on how we can improve the evaluation process.
* **How would you rate communication with the recruitment team? (Number)**: Rate communication with the recruitment team on a scale of 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good).
* **What could the recruitment team have done to improve communication? (Text)**: Suggest ways to improve communication with the team.
* **How can the evaluation process be improved? (Text)**: Share your thoughts on how we can improve the evaluation process.
* **How do you rate your overall satisfaction with the process? (Number)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the process on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **How could we improve to increase overall satisfaction? (Text)**: Suggest ways to improve overall satisfaction.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions? (Note)**: Share any additional thoughts or ideas you have about the recruitment process.
