# property_energy_audit_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "property_energy_audit_feedback_survey" form is used to gather feedback from property owners or managers on the energy consumption and audit results after the energy audit process. This information will help us better understand the property's energy profile and improve our energy audit process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the outcome of the energy audit (Yes/No) on page 1.
	* The "Yes" option indicates that the recommendations from the energy audit were implemented, and the outcome was positive.
	* The "No" option indicates that not all recommendations were implemented, and the outcome was not positive.
2. Provide your contact information on page 2.
	* Enter your contact details so that we can follow up with you regarding your energy audit feedback.
3. Write any additional comments about the audit on page 3.
	* Describe any issues or concerns you encountered during the audit, or anything you'd like to add.
4. Enter your energy consumption information for [number of years] on page 4-6.
	* Enter the energy consumption values for each year.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_energy_audit (`select_one`, page 1):** 
  This field asks you to confirm if the energy audit recommendations were implemented.
* **contact_information (`note`, page 2):** 
  Please enter your contact information so that we can follow up with you regarding your energy audit feedback.
* **audit_comments (`text`, page 3):** 
  Add any comments or concerns you may have about the energy audit process or outcome.
* **energy_consumption (`number`, page 4):** 
  Enter the energy consumption value for [number of years].
* **energy_consumption_2 (`number`, page 5):** 
  Enter the energy consumption value for [number of years].
* **energy_consumption_3 (`number`, page 6):** 
  Enter the energy consumption value for [number of years].

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the energy consumption fields.
* Use decimal points for energy consumption values.
