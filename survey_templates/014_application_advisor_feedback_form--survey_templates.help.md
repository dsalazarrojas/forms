# Application Advisor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Application Advisor Feedback Form" is designed to collect feedback from students about their experiences with their application advisors. This form is intended to gather information about the advisor's performance, service quality, and overall experience. Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve our student support services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by reading each field description carefully and fill in the required fields accordingly.
2. The form is divided into different sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of your experience, such as service quality and overall experience.
3. Fill in the "Student ID Number" and "Name of the Application Advisor" fields, as they are required.
4. Select the ratings for the advisor's responsiveness, knowledge, clarity of instructions, and overall experience based on your honest opinions.
5. Answer the "helpfulness_score" question on a scale of 1-10, indicating the helpfulness of the advising session.
6. If you had a good experience, select "Would you recommend this advisor to other students" accordingly.
7. Share any strengths or improvements the advisor showed during the session in the "Advisor Strengths" field.
8. In the "Improvement Suggestions" field, suggest how the advisor or the advising process can be improved.
9. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can share them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (Student Name, Text, Optional): Please fill in your full name as per your university records.
* **Student ID Number** (Student ID Number, Text, Required): Enter your student ID number, which can be found on your university card or email.
* **Name of the Application Advisor** (Advisor Name, Text, Required): Type in the name of your application advisor.
* **Date of Your Last Advising Session** (Date, Required): Fill in the date of your last advising session with the advisor.
* **How responsive was your advisor to your inquiries?** (Select One, Required): 
  • Very Responsive
  • Somewhat Responsive
  • Neutral
  • Unresponsive
* **Rate the advisor's knowledge of the application process** (Select One, Required):
  • Excellent
  • Good
  • Fair
  • Poor
* **Were the advisor's instructions clear and easy to follow?** (Select One, Required):
  • Very Clear
  • Clear
  • Somewhat Unclear
  • Very Unclear
* **Rate the advisor's professionalism and courtesy** (Select One, Required):
  • Highly Professional
  • Professional
  • Needs Improvement
  • Unprofessional
* **On a scale of 1-10, how helpful was the advising session?** (Number, Required): Rate your experience based on a scale of 1-10.
* **Would you recommend this advisor to other students?** (Select One, Required):
  • True
  • False
  • Maybe
* **What did the advisor do particularly well?** (Text, Optional): Describe any strengths or improvements the advisor showed during the session.
* **How can the advisor or the advising process be improved?** (Text, Optional): Suggest any improvements the advisor or the process can have.
* **Any other comments or feedback?** (Text, Optional): Share any additional comments or feedback you might have about the advisor or the advising process.
