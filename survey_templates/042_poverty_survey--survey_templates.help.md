# poverty_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects demographic and socioeconomic data from families to help identify areas of financial struggle and inform policy-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
2. Fill out all required fields, indicated by a star (*) symbol.
3. Select all applicable options for select multiple questions.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **household_income** (Number): Enter the total monthly household income.
* **household_size** (Number): Enter the number of family members living in your household.
* **age_range_start** (Select One): Choose the age range of the head of the household (0-20, 21-40, 41-60, 61-80, Above 80).
* **household_head** (Text): Enter the name of the household head or primary breadwinner.
* **employment_status** (Select One): Choose the employment status of the household head (Unemployed, Working, Retired, Student).
* **family_relationship** (Select Multiple): Select all applicable family relationships (Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed).
* **family_income** (Number): Enter the total income per family member.
* **household_assets** (Text): Enter a brief description of your household's assets.
* **family_education** (Select One): Choose the highest level of education in the family (Primary, Some Secondary, High School, University).
* **family_language** (Text): Enter the language(s) spoken at home.
* **employment_sector** (Select Multiple): Select all applicable employment sectors (Public Sector, Private Sector, Non-profit, Self-Employed).
* **poverty_level** (Select One): Choose the approximate poverty level of your household (0-20%, 21-40%, 41-60%, 61-80%, Above 80%).
* **family_members_with_disability** (Number): Enter the number of family members with a disability.
* **family_members_with_disability_type** (Select Multiple): Select all applicable disability types (Visual, Hearing, Speech, Mobility, Intellectual, Mental Health).
* **family_members_with_disability_level** (Select One): Choose the approximate level of disability in your family members (0, 1-25%, 26-50%, 51-75%, 76-100%).
* **family_members_with_disability_income** (Number): Enter the total income of family members with disabilities.
* **family_members_without_disability_income** (Number): Enter the total income of family members without disabilities.
* **family_members_without_disability_assets** (Text): Enter a brief description of the assets owned by family members without disabilities.
* **family_members_without_disability_education** (Select One): Choose the highest level of education in family members without disabilities (Primary, Some Secondary, High School, University).
* **family_members_without_disability_employment** (Select Multiple): Select all applicable employment sectors for family members without disabilities (Public Sector, Private Sector, Non-profit, Self-Employed).
* **family_members_without_disability_pov_level** (Select One): Choose the approximate poverty level of family members without disabilities (0-20%, 21-40%, 41-60%, 61-80%, Above 80%).
* **poverty_area** (Text): Enter the region or area where your household resides.
* **family_members_with_disability_income_type** (Text): Enter a brief description of the income of family members with disabilities.
* **household_location** (Text): Enter the name of your household's neighborhood or location.
* **employment_sector_other** (Text): Enter a brief description of your employment sector or industry.
