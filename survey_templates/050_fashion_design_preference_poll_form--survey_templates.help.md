# Fashion Design Preference Poll Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your fashion preferences, including your favorite style, color, fabric, and shopping habits. The data collected will help us understand your tastes and preferences, and enable us to provide you with more personalized fashion advice and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select your answers from the provided options.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options that best fit your preferences.
3. For select_one fields, only select one option.
4. For select_multiple fields, select multiple options.
5. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the note fields.
7. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is a brief overview of the form. You can read through it at your own pace.
* **What is the most important style in fashion?** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Select the style that you think is most important to you. This could be a classic, modern, or another style that you prefer.
* **What is your favorite color?** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or multiple colors that you like best.
* **What is your age?** (`4`, `number`, required): Enter your age to help us understand your demographic.
* **What do you usually wear?** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Select your usual outfit style, such as casual, formal, or business casual.
* **How much experience do you have with the fashion industry?** (`6`, `number`, required): Enter your level of experience with fashion, from 1 to 10.
* **What is your favorite brand?** (`7`, `note`, optional): This is a free-text field where you can write your favorite brand name or a brief description.
* **What is your favorite color for shoes?** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Select the color of your favorite shoes.
* **What is your favorite fabric?** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or multiple fabrics that you like best.
* **How many hours do you spend per week on fashion?** (`10`, `time`, optional): Enter the number of hours you spend on fashion-related activities, if any.
* **Your email address** (`11`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with your results.
* **What is your favorite season?** (`12`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or multiple seasons that you like best.
* **What is your favorite activity?** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): Select an activity related to fashion that you enjoy, such as shopping or creating.
* **Your phone number** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you, if needed.
* **Do you have any suggestions or comments?** (`15`, `note`, optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional thoughts or suggestions.
* **What is your favorite style?** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Select the style that you think best represents you.
* **How often do you buy new clothes?** (`17`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you last bought new clothes or how often you buy new clothes.
* **How many clothes do you usually buy per month?** (`18`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of clothes you buy each month.
* **What is your favorite material?** (`19`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or multiple materials that you like best.
* **Do you use any fashion apps?** (`20`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the fashion apps you use, if any.
* **How much do you spend on fashion?** (`21`, `select_one`, required): Select the amount you spend on fashion, from 1 to 100.
* **How many clothes do you usually keep?** (`22`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of clothes you usually keep.
* **Do you usually buy second-hand clothes?** (`23`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you buy second-hand clothes, yes or no.
* **What is your favorite store?** (`24`, `select_one`, optional): Select your favorite store.
* **Do you usually shop online?** (`25`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you shop online, yes or no.
* **Do you have any other questions or suggestions?** (`26`, `note`, optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional thoughts or suggestions.
