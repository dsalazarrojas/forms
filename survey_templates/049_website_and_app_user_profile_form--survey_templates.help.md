# Website And App User Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about users of our website and app in order to better understand their demographics and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields with your information as requested.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and relevant information.
3. Submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (Text, Optional): This field is intended to capture any additional information you would like to share about yourself.
* **name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **name_other** (Text, Optional): If you have a nickname or alias, enter it here.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **email_other** (Email, Optional): Enter any other email address you would like to associate with your profile.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **phone_other** (Text, Optional): Enter any other phone number you would like to associate with your profile.
* **website** (Text, Optional): Enter the URL of your website or blog.
* **company** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of company or organization you are associated with (Website, App, or Other).
* **industry** (Select One, Mandatory): Select the industry you are working in (Technology, Marketing, Finance, or Other).
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to share.
* **date_of_birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **time_of_visit** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of your visit.
* **time_of_visit_other** (Time, Optional): Enter any other time of visit you would like to associate with your profile.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the provided instructions or contact our support team for assistance.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and help us better understand our users!
