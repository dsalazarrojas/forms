# Academic Peer Feedback Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Peer Feedback Culture Survey is designed to gather information about the culture of peer feedback within an institution. It will help the institution identify its strengths and weaknesses regarding peer feedback and provide insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please select the option that best describes your role in the institution (Faculty, Postdoc, Graduate student, Undergraduate student, or Staff).
2. Choose the frequency with which you give or receive peer feedback (Weekly, Monthly, Per term, or Rarely).
3. Rate the openness and constructiveness of feedback culture within the institution (Very positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, or Very negative).
4. Select any barriers that limit peer feedback (Lack of time, Fear of retaliation, Lack of training, Hierarchical culture, or any other option that applies).
5. Select any topics you are interested in for feedback training (Giving feedback, Receiving feedback, Facilitating peer review, Conflict resolution, or any other option that applies).
6. Describe any successful practices you have observed within the institution that promote peer feedback (e.g. regular feedback sessions, structured feedback tools).
7. Provide any recommendations for improving the peer feedback culture within the institution (e.g. more feedback training, anonymous feedback options).
8. Finally, indicate whether you prefer anonymity when receiving feedback (True or False).
9. If there are any additional comments or suggestions you would like to share, please use the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role in Institution (1)**: Select the option that best describes your position within the institution.
* **Frequency of Peer Feedback (2)**: Choose the option that best reflects how often you give or receive feedback. This will help us understand the institutional dynamics of feedback.
* **Rating of Feedback Culture (3)**: Rate the openness and constructiveness of feedback within the institution. This will help us assess the perceived quality of feedback.
* **Barriers to Peer Feedback (4)**: Select any barriers that limit feedback, such as lack of time or fear of retaliation.
* **Interest in Feedback Training (5)**: Choose any topics you would like to learn more about through feedback training.
* **Successful Practices (6)**: Describe any successful practices you have observed that promote peer feedback.
* **Recommendations for Improvement (7)**: Share any practical suggestions you have for enhancing the peer feedback culture within the institution.
* **Preference for Anonymity (8)**: Indicate whether you prefer anonymous feedback options.
* **Additional Comments (9)**: Share any further thoughts or suggestions you have about the peer feedback culture within the institution.

Note: Please be as detailed and specific as possible when answering each field, as this will help us gain a better understanding of the institution's peer feedback culture.
