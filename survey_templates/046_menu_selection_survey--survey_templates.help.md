# Menu Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Menu Selection Survey is a form designed to gather information from users about their menu preferences and eating habits. It is meant to be a simple and user-friendly way to collect data on menu selection, eating habits, and personal details such as email and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your menu items of interest from the first question.
2. Choose your favorite menu item in the second question.
3. Answer the third question with your typical time of day you eat.
4. Provide a numerical rating of your experience in the fourth question.
5. Answer the fifth question with the number of times you have visited this restaurant.
6. Enter your email address in the sixth question.
7. Provide your phone number in the seventh question.
8. Type any additional comments or notes in the eighth question.
9. Choose your typical time to eat in the ninth question.
10. Select your eating frequency in the tenth question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Menu Items** (`Select Multiple`, required: false):
	+ This question asks you to select all the menu items that interest you. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **What's your favorite menu item?** (`Select One`, required: false):
	+ This question asks you to select your favorite menu item from the options provided.
* **What time of day do you typically eat?** (`Time`, required: false):
	+ This question asks for your typical time of day when you eat. Please answer with a 24-hour clock format (e.g., 12:00, 13:00, etc.).
* **Rate your experience** (`Number`, required: false):
	+ This question asks you to provide a numerical rating of your experience. Please answer with a number between 1 and 5.
* **How many times have you been to this restaurant?** (`Number`, required: false):
	+ This question asks you to provide the number of times you have visited this restaurant.
* **Email** (`Email`, required: false):
	+ This question asks you to enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Text`, required: false):
	+ This question asks you to provide your phone number.
* **Do you have any other comments?** (`Note`, required: false):
	+ This question allows you to provide any additional comments or notes you have.
* **When do you usually eat?** (`Date`, required: false):
	+ This question asks for your typical time to eat. Please answer with a date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **How often do you eat here?** (`Select One`, required: false):
	+ This question asks you to select how often you eat at this restaurant. Please choose one of the options provided.
