# Church Guest Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from church guests to help us improve our services and facilities. Your responses will help us understand your experience and provide better services for future visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as accurately as possible. Make sure to select the correct options for each field and provide as much detail as you can in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`guest_name_questionnaire`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Age Range** (`guest_age_range`, select_one, optional): Select your age range to help us understand our demographic.
* **Email Address** (`guest_email_questionnaire`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted, please enter your email address.
* **How You Found Us** (`discovery_source`, select_one, required): Select how you found the church, e.g., Friend/Family, Social Media, Website, Local Ad, Driven By, or Other.
* **Rate your first impression upon entering the building** (`first_impression_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your first impression of the building's atmosphere, e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Rate the music/worship experience** (`music_experience_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the music and worship experience, e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Not for me.
* **Rate the teaching/sermon quality** (`teaching_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the teaching and sermon quality, e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **What did you like the best? Why?** (`best_part_reason`, text, required): Share with us what you liked best about your experience and why.
* **Did someone greet you or speak with you?** (`greeting_experience`, select_one, required): Indicate if someone greeted you or spoke with you, e.g., Yes - Very friendly, Yes - Brief, No - I was not greeted.
* **Was it easy to find your way around?** (`signage_finding_way`, select_one, required): Rate how easy or confusing it was to find your way around the building, e.g., Very Easy, Easy, Confusing.
* **If you have children - how was their experience?** (`kids_ministry_rating`, select_one, optional): If you have children, rate their experience, e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, N/A.
* **What is one suggestion you have for us?** (`suggestion_box_guest`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or feedback you have for us.
* **Would you like someone from our team to contact you?** (`future_contact_permission`, select_one, required): Indicate if you would like someone to contact you, e.g., Yes - Phone, Yes - Email, No thanks.
* **Do you have a question for the Pastor?** (`question_for_pastor`, text, optional): If you have any questions for the Pastor, share them with us.
* **Today's Date** (`submission_date_guest`, date, required): Please select the current date.

Note: Fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled in to complete the form.
