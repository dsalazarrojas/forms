# Gardening Program Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey used to understand interest in various gardening-related activities and programs. It will help us understand how many people are interested and when they are available to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of activity you are most interested in from the "Survey Template Selection" field. You can choose from "Gardening Club", "Gardening Class", or "Other".
2. Indicate your interest in each of the activities listed in the "What are you interested in" field. You can select multiple options.
3. Enter the months you are available to participate in the "When are you available" field.
4. Choose your learning preferences in the "What kind of learning preferences do you have?" field. You can select multiple options.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Add any additional details about your interest or availability in the "Other Details" field.
8. Choose how you would like to be contacted for this survey in the "How would you like to be contacted for this survey?" field.
9. Provide a response in the "Response" field (this is a text entry field for additional comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Template Selection** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the type of activity you are most interested in.
* **What are you interested in** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate your interest in each of the activities listed.
* **When are you available** (`text`, required: false): Enter the months you are available to participate.
* **What kind of learning preferences do you have?** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your learning preferences, such as visual, interactive, or interactive and visual.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Other Details** (`text`, required: false): Add any additional details about your interest or availability.
* **How would you like to be contacted for this survey?** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or both).
* **Response** (`note`, required: false): Provide a response or additional comments.
