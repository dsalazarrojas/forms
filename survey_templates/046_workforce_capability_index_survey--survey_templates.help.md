# Workforce Capability Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and data on the workforce capability index of the organization, allowing for the assessment and improvement of departmental and role development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to complete this form before proceeding.
2. Review each field carefully and enter your responses as instructed.
3. Fill in the demographic information (page 1) with your details.
4. On page 2, select the manager who has provided guidance or support to you.
5. On page 3, list any skills and training needs you have.
6. On page 4, provide any feedback or comments you have about your department or role.
7. On page 5, rate your departmental development.
8. On page 6, rate your role development.
9. On page 7, rate your overall satisfaction with your work.
10. On page 8, enter your contact information (email and phone number).
11. On page 9, select the date that best describes your work hours.
12. On page 10, provide any additional comments or notes.
13. On page 11, select the departments you are currently working in.
14. On page 12, select the roles you are currently in.
15. On page 13, select the manager who has provided guidance or support to you.
16. On page 14, select multiple dates that apply to you.
17. On page 15, rate your overall satisfaction with your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **demographic_info** (`demographic_info`, text, optional): Enter your demographic information, such as name, title, and department.
* **manager_info** (`manager_info`, text, optional): Select the manager who has provided guidance or support to you.
* **employee_skills** (`employee_skills`, text, optional): List any skills and training needs you have.
* **training_needs** (`training_needs`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or comments you have about your department or role.
* **departmental_development** (`departmental_development`, text, optional): Rate your departmental development.
* **role_development** (`role_development`, text, optional): Rate your role development.
* **overall_rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your work.
* **manager_rating** (`manager_rating`, number, optional): Rate your manager's support and guidance.
* **department_rating** (`department_rating`, number, optional): Rate your department's support and resources.
* **overall_satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your work.
* **manager_satisfaction** (`manager_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your manager's support and guidance.
* **department_satisfaction** (`department_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your department's support and resources.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date that best describes your work hours.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time that best describes your work hours.
* **note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes.
* **select_one_department** (`select_one_department`, select_one, optional): Select the department you are currently working in.
* **select_multiple_departments** (`select_multiple_departments`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple departments you are currently working in.
* **select_one_role** (`select_one_role`, select_one, optional): Select the role you are currently in.
* **select_multiple_roles** (`select_multiple_roles`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple roles you are currently in.
* **select_one_manager** (`select_one_manager`, select_one, optional): Select the manager who has provided guidance or support to you.
* **select_multiple_managers** (`select_multiple_managers`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple managers who have provided guidance or support to you.
* **select_one_date** (`select_one_date`, select_one, optional): Select the date that best describes your work hours.
* **select_multiple_dates** (`select_multiple_dates`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple dates that apply to you.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or comments you have about your department or role.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Select the departments you are currently working in.
