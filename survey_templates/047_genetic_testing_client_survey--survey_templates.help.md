# Genetic Testing Client Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients who have undergone genetic testing. It helps healthcare providers understand their services and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Introduction
This form consists of multiple sections, each designed to collect specific information about your experiences with genetic testing. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability, and remember that all fields are optional unless marked as required.

### Step 2: Complete Each Section
This form is divided into sections with clear labels and instructions. Each section is designed to collect specific information. You will find the following sections:
* **Testing Experiences**: This section asks about your experiences with genetic testing. Please respond with the number of times you've had genetic testing in the past year.
* **Communication Clarity**: This section asks about how clear the communication was with providers before, during, and after genetic testing. Please respond with the time taken to understand information about your genetic testing.
* **Satisfaction Level**: This section asks about your satisfaction with the genetic testing services. Please choose one of the three options: Client, Provider, or Both.
* **Personal Information**: This section collects your contact information and any additional comments or notes you'd like to share.

### How to Respond

* For **Testing Experiences** and **Communication Clarity** fields: Please respond with a number or time value.
* For **Satisfaction Level** fields: Please choose one of the three options: Client, Provider, or Both.
* For **Personal Information** fields: Please respond with your name, email, phone number, and any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Testing Experiences 1**: This field asks about the number of times you've had genetic testing in the past year.
	+ **Label:** `Testing Experiences 1`
	+ **Label (Internal):** Number of times you've had genetic testing in the past year
	+ **Type:** `number`
* **Communication Clarity 1**: This field asks about how clear the communication was with providers before genetic testing.
	+ **Label:** `Communication Clarity 1`
	+ **Label (Internal):** How clear was the communication with providers before genetic testing?
	+ **Type:** `time`
* **Satisfaction Level 1**: This field asks about your satisfaction with the genetic testing services.
	+ **Label:** `Satisfaction Level 1`
	+ **Label (Internal):** Satisfaction level with genetic testing services
	+ **Type:** `select_one`
* **Notes**: This field is for any additional comments or notes you'd like to share.
	+ **Label:** `Notes`
	+ **Label (Internal):** Additional comments or notes
	+ **Type:** `note`

## Tips

* Please take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you're unsure about any question, please leave it blank or ask for clarification.
* Your responses are greatly appreciated and will help healthcare providers improve their services.
