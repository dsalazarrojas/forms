# Film Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback on films, allowing us to understand your viewing preferences and improve our movie selection. We appreciate your honesty and insights!

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the movie you're providing feedback on in the "Movie" field.
2. Rate the movie by selecting a number from 1 to 5 in the "Rate the Movie" field.
3. Share your written reflections about the movie in the "Written Reflections" field.
4. If you'd like to receive further information or updates from us, provide your email address in the "Email" field.
5. If you'd like to be contacted about future movie screenings or promotions, provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Select the category of the movie from the list in the "Category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Movie**: Enter the title of the movie you're providing feedback on. This will help us track the specific film you're referring to.
  - Required: No
  - Type: Text
  - Hint: Enter the title of the movie as shown on the cinema's poster or marquee.
- **Rate the Movie**: Rate your experience with the movie on a scale from 1 to 5. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction.
  - Required: No
  - Type: Select One (Scale 1-5)
  - Hint: 1 is "Did not enjoy", 2 is "Okay", 3 is "Enjoyable", 4 is "Great!", 5 is "Fantastic!"
- **Written Reflections**: Share your thoughts about the movie. What did you like or dislike about it? What would you recommend or not recommend?
  - Required: No
  - Type: Note
  - Hint: Share your thoughts and opinions about the movie.
- **Email**: If you'd like to receive further information or updates from us, enter your email address here.
  - Required: No
  - Type: Email
  - Hint: This will allow us to contact you with special promotions, new movie releases, and upcoming events.
- **Phone Number**: If you'd like to be contacted about future movie screenings or promotions, enter your phone number here.
  - Required: No
  - Type: Text
  - Hint: This will allow us to reach out to you with special offers and reminders.
- **Category**: Select the genre of the movie from the list to help us categorize it for future reference.
  - Required: No
  - Type: Select One
  - Hint: Choose the most fitting category from the list.
