# Retail Store Experience Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their retail store experience. We value your input and want to hear about your thoughts on our store's product quality, service, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct answer from the provided options for each question.
3. If you have visited the store before, select "True" for "Visited Before".
4. If you have visited other locations, select "True" for "Visited Other Locations".
5. If you are a first-time customer, select "True" for "First Time Customer".
6. If you wish to provide your contact details, add them in the "Contact Details" field.
7. If you have any additional comments, add them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Store Experience
* **Store Experience**: Please describe your overall experience at the store.

### 2. Product Quality
* **Product Quality**: How satisfied were you with the product you purchased? (Select one of the following options)
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Not Satisfied

### 3. Service Quality
* **Service Quality**: How would you rate the service you received during your visit? (Select all that apply)
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### 4. Employee Friendliness
* **Employee Friendliness**: How friendly were the employees during your visit? (Select one of the following options)
	+ Very Helpful
	+ Somewhat Helpful
	+ Not Helpful

### 5. Store Cleanliness
* **Store Cleanliness**: How clean was the store during your visit? (Select one of the following options)
	+ Excellent

	+ Poor

### 6. Employee Knowledge

	+ Not Knowledgeable

### 7. Store Location
* **Store Location**: Which location did you visit? (Select one of the following options)
	+ Google Maps

### 8. Store Hours
* **Store Hours**: If you visited during regular store hours, what day and time did you visit? (Please enter the date)

### 9. Visited Before

### 10. Visited Other Locations
