# Technology Integration Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Technology Integration Needs Survey" is designed to collect information about your organization's technology needs. It will help us understand your current usage and requirements for various technology tools and services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you are unsure about a particular field or section, please feel free to ask for clarification.
3. Once you have completed all the questions, submit the form to proceed.
