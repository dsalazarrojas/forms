# Healthcare Conference Attendee Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and suggestions from attendees of the healthcare conference. Your input will help us understand what we did well and what we can improve on for future conferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
2. You may skip any questions that do not apply to your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text area for feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`<no label>` , <note> , optional): This is the beginning of the form, where you can briefly explain what you did at the conference and why your feedback is relevant.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating` , <number> , optional): Rate your overall experience at the conference on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Session 1** (`session1` , <select multiple> , optional): Choose the speakers who presented at Session 1 (Speaker1, Speaker2, Speaker3).
* **Speaker Rating** (`speaker_rating` , <number> , optional): Rate your overall experience with the speakers on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Feedback** (`feedback` , <text> , optional): Write any additional comments or suggestions about the conference.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions` , <select one> , optional): Suggest ways we can improve the conference (Suggestion1, Suggestion2, Suggestion3).
* **Email** (`email` , <email> , optional): Enter your email address to be informed about future conferences or for us to contact you for further discussion.

Note: The fields with a type of `note` or `text` are for free-form comments, where you can write as much or as little as you like. The fields with a type of `number` are for numerical ratings. The fields with a type of `select_multiple` are for selecting multiple options, while the fields with a type of `select_one` are for selecting one option.
