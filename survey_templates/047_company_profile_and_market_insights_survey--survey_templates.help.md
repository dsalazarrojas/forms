# Company Profile And Market Insights Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a company's profile and market insights. The data collected will help in creating a detailed survey template.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company's name in the "Company name" field (page 1, field 1).
2. Select the primary industry sector from the "Industry" field (page 1, field 2).
3. Describe the company's main products or services in the "Main products or services" field (page 1, field 3).
4. Choose the market geography from the options in the "Market geography" field (page 1, field 4). You can select one or more regions the company serves (Local, Regional, National, or International).
5. If applicable, describe the key challenges the company faces in its growth in the "Main challenges to growth" field (page 1, field 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the company's official trading or legal name.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, required): Select the primary industry sector the company operates in.
* **Main products or services** (`main_products_services`, text, required): Describe the company's main products or services offered.
* **Market geography** (`market_geography`, select_multiple, required): Choose the regions the company serves (Local, Regional, National, or International).
* **Main challenges to growth** (`growth_challenges`, text, optional): Describe the key obstacles the company faces in its growth, if applicable.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have permission to fill out this form on behalf of the company.
