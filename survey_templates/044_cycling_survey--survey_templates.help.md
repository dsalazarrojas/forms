# Cycling Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about your level of expertise and physical and technical skills in various areas related to cycling. This will help us to better understand your strengths and weaknesses in cycling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the introduction page to understand the purpose of the survey.
2. Answer all the required fields by selecting one or multiple options for each field.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Level of Expertise** (`Level of Expertise`, select_one, required): This field asks about your level of expertise in cycling. Choose the option that best describes your level of expertise.
* **Technical Skills** (`Technical Skills`, select_multiple, required): This field asks about your technical skills in cycling. Select all the skills that apply to you.
* **Technical Skills 2** (`Technical Skills 2`, select_multiple, required): This field is similar to the Technical Skills field but asks about different technical skills.
* **Physical Fitness** (`Physical Fitness`, select_multiple, required): This field asks about your physical fitness in cycling. Select all the skills that apply to you.
* **Physical Fitness 2** (`Physical Fitness 2`, select_multiple, required): This field is similar to the Physical Fitness field but asks about different physical skills.
* **Physical Fitness 3** (`Physical Fitness 3`, select_multiple, required): This field is similar to the Physical Fitness 2 field but asks about different physical skills.
* **Physical Fitness 4** (`Physical Fitness 4`, select_multiple, required): This field is similar to the Physical Fitness 3 field but asks about different physical skills.
* **Technical Skills 2** (`Technical Skills 2`, select_multiple, required): This field is similar to the Technical Skills field but asks about different technical skills.
* **Technical Skills 3** (`Technical Skills 3`, select_multiple, required): This field is similar to the Technical Skills 2 field but asks about different technical skills.
* **Technical Skills 4** (`Technical Skills 4`, select_multiple, required): This field is similar to the Technical Skills 3 field but asks about different technical skills.
* **Technical Skills 5** (`Technical Skills 5`, select_multiple, required): This field is similar to the Technical Skills 4 field but asks about different technical skills.

## Tips
