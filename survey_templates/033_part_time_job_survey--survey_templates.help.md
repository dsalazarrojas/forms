# Part Time Job Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Part Time Job Survey is designed to collect information about part-time job opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information (page 2)
2. Enter the job title (page 3)
3. Enter your contact number (page 4)
4. Choose your start date (page 5)
5. Choose your work hours (page 6)
6. Select your work schedule (page 7)
7. Choose your work days (page 8)
8. Enter your work hours range (page 9)
9. Enter your pay rate (page 10)
10. Enter your work location (page 11)
11. Choose your job type (page 12)
12. Choose your work status (page 13)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (page 1, **required: false**): A brief introduction about the purpose of this form.
* **Contact Information** (`contact`, **required: false**): Enter your contact information, including a way for us to reach out to you about potential job opportunities.
* **Job Title** (`job`, **required: false**): Enter the title of the part-time job you are interested in or currently working.
* **Contact Number** (`contact`, **required: false**): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you about potential job opportunities.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, **required: false**): Choose the date you want to start your part-time job.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, **required: false**): Choose the hours you are available to work per day.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, **required: false**): Select the days you are available to work.
* **Work Days** (`work_days`, **required: false**): Select the specific days you are available to work.
* **Work Hours Range** (`work_hours_range`, **required: false**): Enter the range of hours you are available to work per day.
* **Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, **required: false**): Enter your requested pay rate for the part-time job.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, **required: false**): Enter the location where you are able to work.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, **required: false**): Choose the type of job you are interested in (Part Time, Full Time, Internship, or Volunteer).
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, **required: false**): Choose your current status (Active or Inactive).
