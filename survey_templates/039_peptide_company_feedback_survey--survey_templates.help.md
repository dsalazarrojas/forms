# Peptide Company Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our company's peptide products and services. The goal of this survey is to gather information on how well our products meet your needs and how we can improve our services to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your contact information.
2. Select all the peptide products you used during your experience with our company.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the product and our services on a scale of 1-10.
4. Submit the form to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, Required: false): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Required: false): Enter the email address associated with your contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, Required: false): Enter the phone number of your contact person.
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, text, Required: false): Enter the department you are contacting us from.
* **Product Used** (`product_used`, select_multiple, Required: false):
	+ Choose all the peptide products you used during your experience with our company.
	+ Select as many options as applicable.
* **Product Satisfaction** (`product_satisfaction`, number, Required: false):
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the product on a scale of 1-10.
	+ A higher number indicates higher satisfaction.
* **Service Satisfaction** (`service_satisfaction`, number, Required: false):
	+ Rate your satisfaction with our services on a scale of 1-10.
	+ A higher number indicates higher satisfaction.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, number, Required: false):
	+ Rate the quality of our services on a scale of 1-10.
	+ A higher number indicates higher quality.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling in the fields to help us understand your experience better.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the space provided.
