# Hurricane Damage Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to assess the damage caused by hurricanes to properties. It is designed to gather data on the extent of damage to various components of a property, such as the roof, walls, electrical systems, heating systems, plumbing systems, foundation, and floors. This information will help insurance companies, government agencies, and other organizations to make informed decisions regarding property restoration and recovery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the property address field (`property_address`).
2. Select the type of property you are assessing from the list of options (`property_type`).
3. Choose the extent of damage to each category of systems or components, as applicable:
   - Roof (`roof_damage`)
   - Walls (`wall_damage`)
   - Electrical systems (`electrical_systems`)
   - Heating systems (`heating_systems`)
   - Plumbing systems (`plumbing_systems`)
   - Foundation (`foundation_damage`)
   - Floors (`floor_damage`)
   - Wall (`wall_damage`)
   - Roof status (`roof_status`)
   - Wall status (`wall_status`)
   - Electrical status (`electrical_status`)
   - Heating status (`heating_status`)
   - Plumbing status (`plumbing_status`)
   - Foundation status (`foundation_status`)
   - Floor status (`floor_status`)
4. Fill out the status of each system or component as applicable.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the property (`comments`).
6. Enter your name and title/position (`submitted_by`).
7. Record the time and date you submitted the form (`submit_time` and `submitted_date`).
8. Enter the date you submitted the form (`submitted_at`).
9. Add any comments from the inspector or assessor (`inspector_comments`).
10. Enter the name of the inspector or assessor (`inspector`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_address**: Enter the address of the property being assessed. This can include the street address, city, and state.
* **property_type**: Select the type of property being assessed, such as single-family home, apartment, condominium, etc.
* **roof_damage**: Select the extent of damage to the roof, such as minor, moderate, or severe.
* **wall_damage**: Select the extent of damage to the walls, such as minor, moderate, or severe.
* **electrical_systems**: Select the extent of damage to electrical systems, such as minor, moderate, or severe.
* **heating_systems**: Select the extent of damage to heating systems, such as minor, moderate, or severe.
* **plumbing_systems**: Select the extent of damage to plumbing systems, such as minor, moderate, or severe.
* **foundation_damage**: Select the extent of damage to the foundation, such as minor, moderate, or severe.
* **floor_damage**: Select the extent of damage to floors, such as minor, moderate, or severe.
* **wall_status**: Select the status of the walls, such as secure, unstable, or collapsed.
* **roof_status**: Select the status of the roof, such as secure, unstable, or collapsed.
* **electrical_status**: Select the status of electrical systems, such as operational, non-operational, or severely damaged.
* **heating_status**: Select the status of heating systems, such as operational, non-operational, or severely damaged.
* **plumbing_status**: Select the status of plumbing systems, such as operational, non-operational, or severely damaged.
* **foundation_status**: Select the status of the foundation, such as secure, unstable, or collapsed.
* **floor_status**: Select the status of floors, such as secure, unstable, or collapsed.
* **comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the property.
* **submitted_by**: Enter your name and title/position.
* **submit_time**: Record the time you submitted the form.
* **submitted_date**: Record the date you submitted the form.
* **submitted_at**: Select the date you submitted the form, such as yesterday, today, or yesterday or today.
* **inspector_comments**: Enter any comments from the inspector or assessor.
* **inspector**: Enter the name of the inspector or assessor.
* **submitted_date**: Enter the date you submitted the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the damage to each category.
* Take note of any additional comments or notes you may want to add.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* Review and edit your form before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
