# Team Building Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Building Preference Survey is designed to gather information about team preferences and availability for team-building activities. This information will be used to schedule and plan team-building events that are convenient and appealing to team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. For each question, select or enter the information required.
2. Fill in each field according to your preferences and availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Preferences** (`survey_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you would like to participate in team-building activities.
* **Availability** (`availability_date`, date, required: false): When would you like to participate in team-building activities? Choose a date from the calendar.
* **Availability Time** (`availability_time`, time, required: false): When would you like to participate in team-building activities? Choose a time that suits you best.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): If you have any comments or suggestions about team-building activities, please share them here.
* **Preferred Team Size** (`preferred_team_size`, number, required: false): What size team would you prefer for team-building activities?
* **Preferred Meeting Frequency** (`preferred_meeting_frequency`, number, required: false): How often would you like to participate in team-building activities?
* **Availability Email** (`availability_email`, email, required: false): If you have any specific email preferences or requirements for communication, please provide them here.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please choose the most likely or preferred response.
* If you have any questions or need help with completing the form, please reach out to your team lead or HR representative.
