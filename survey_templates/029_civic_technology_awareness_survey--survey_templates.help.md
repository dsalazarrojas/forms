# Civic Technology Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to understand how citizens interact with and perceive civic technology in their city. It aims to gather information on awareness, usage, and perceptions of civic technology, which will help us improve our services and better serve the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Answer all required fields**: Make sure to fill in all fields marked as "required." These fields are indicated with a * in the label.
2.  **Select the correct options**: For select-type fields, choose the answer that best describes your thoughts or experiences.
3.  **Provide honest answers**: Your feedback and opinions are valuable to us, so please answer honestly and accurately.
4.  **Save and submit**: Once you've completed the form, click on the "Submit" button to save and send your answers to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Awareness Intro**: This is a note field that provides a brief introduction to the topic of civic technology.
*   **Are you familiar with the term "Civic Technology"?**: Please select the answer that best describes your level of familiarity with the term. This will help us understand how well-known this term is among citizens.
*   **Have you ever used a mobile app provided by the city government?**: Choose the answer that reflects your experience with city-provided mobile apps. This will help us understand how citizens use these tools.
*   **Which platforms are you aware of?**: Select all platforms you are aware of, such as City Official Portal, Neighborhood Discussion Boards, and more. This will help us understand which platforms are most accessible to citizens.
*   **Technology makes it easier for me to participate in local government**: Please rate your agreement level with this statement. This will help us understand citizens' perceptions of the value of technology in participating in local government.
*   **Are you aware of the city's Open Data Initiative?**: Choose the answer that reflects your awareness of the city's Open Data Initiative. This will help us understand how citizens interact with and use open data.
*   **If yes, have you ever used city data for your own project or research?**: If you answered "True" to the previous question, please choose whether you've used city data for your own project or research.
*   **What is your preferred way to provide feedback to the city?**: Select the method you prefer to provide feedback, such as mobile app, web form, social media, or in-person meeting.
*   **What stops you from using more civic tech tools?**: Choose all the reasons that prevent you from using more civic tech tools, such as privacy concerns or lack of internet access.
*   **Do you feel tech tools have improved government transparency?**: Rate your agreement level with this statement. This will help us understand how citizens perceive the impact of tech tools on government transparency.
*   **What one digital tool is missing from our city services?**: If you feel a digital tool is missing, please describe it in the text field provided.
*   **How would you rate your own digital literacy?**: Rate your level of digital literacy, with 1 being very low and 5 being very high.
*   **Would you join a "Beta Tester" group for new city digital services?**: Choose whether you would be interested in joining such a group.

By following these steps and understanding each field's purpose, you will be able to complete this form accurately and provide valuable feedback to help us improve our civic technology services.
