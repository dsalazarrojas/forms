# Exam Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from students about their experience with the exam process. This information will help us improve the exam process and make it more efficient for future students.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form as completely and accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to provide as much detail as you can about your experience with the exam process.
3. For select multiple-choice questions, choose all that apply.
4. For select one questions, choose only one option.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Comments" field to provide them.
