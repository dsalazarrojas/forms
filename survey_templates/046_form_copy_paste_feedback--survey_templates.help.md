# Form Copy Paste Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experiences with copying and pasting content from our application.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out the form with as much detail as possible to help us understand your experience.
* Be honest and provide accurate information to help us improve our copy paste functionality.
* Make sure to answer all required fields to ensure we have a complete understanding of your issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, `note`, required: false): This field is for you to share any additional information or context about your issue.
* **Issue On** (`issue_device_browsers`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Please select all the devices and browsers where you experienced the issue.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required: true): Please rate your satisfaction with our copy paste functionality on a scale of 1-10.
* **Issue On Devices** (`issue_devices`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the devices where you experienced the issue.
* **Issue with Feature** (`issue_feature`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select the feature where you experienced the issue.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: true): Share your feedback and suggestions on how we can improve our copy paste functionality.
* **Browser Version** (`browser_version`, `text`, required: false): If you're using a specific browser version, please share it with us.
* **Issue On Browsers** (`issue_browsers`, `select_one`, required: false): If you're using a specific browser, please select it.
* **Issue with Copy** (`issue_copy`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the issues you experienced with copying and pasting content.
* **Device Version** (`device_version`, `text`, required: false): If you're using a specific device version, please share it with us.
* **Issue Other** (`issue_other`, `text`, required: false): If your issue doesn't fit in any of the above categories, please share more details about it.
* **Support Contact** (`support_contact`, `email`, required: false): If you'd like to get in touch with our support team, please provide your email address.
* **Issue with Satisfaction** (`issue_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Please rate your satisfaction with our copy paste functionality on a scale of 1-3.
* **Feedback Email** (`feedback_email`, `email`, required: false): If you'd like to get in touch with our feedback team, please provide your email address.
* **Issue with Time** (`issue_time`, `time`, required: false): If you experienced an issue with timing, please share more details about it.
* **Issue Other Details** (`issue_other_details`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional details about your issue, please share them.
* **Issue with Copy Version** (`issue_copy_version`, `text`, required: false): If you're using a specific copy version, please share it with us.
* **Support Phone** (`support_phone`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to get in touch with our support team, please provide your phone number.
* **Issue with Support** (`issue_support`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the issues you experienced with our support team.
* **Issue with Copy and Paste** (`issue_copy_paste`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select if you experienced any issues with copying and pasting content.
* **Issue with Device** (`issue_device`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select if you experienced any issues with your device.
* **Issue with Feature Version** (`issue_feature_version`, `text`, required: false): If you're using a specific feature version, please share it with us.
* **Issue Other Issue** (`issue_other_issue`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional issues, please share them with us.
* **Support URL** (`support_url`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to get in touch with our support team, please provide the URL.
* **User Message** (`user_message`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them with us.

Note: The required fields are marked as "required: true" in the YAML schema.
