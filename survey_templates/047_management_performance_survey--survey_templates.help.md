# Management Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides an overview of the Management Performance Survey form, which is designed to collect feedback on management performance, skills, and areas of improvement. The form is used to gather information on management strengths and weaknesses, allowing for informed decision-making and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Management Performance Survey, follow these steps:

*   Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, as the information will be used to inform management decisions.
*   Please answer all questions, even if you're not sure or don't know the answer.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the survey administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What do you think?** (`what_do_you_think`, text, optional): This question is a free-text field where you can provide your thoughts on management performance.
*   **What is your rank?** (`what_is_your_rank`, number, optional): Please enter your rank or position in the organization.
*   **What skills areas?** (`what_skills_areas`, text, optional): This field is for selecting skills areas, but the options are not provided in the original YAML.
*   **Do you agree?** (`do_you_agree`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you agree or disagree with a given statement.
*   **Skills you find most challenging?** (`skills_you_find_most_challenging`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the skills you find challenging.
*   **What skills areas?** ( repeated fields, text, optional): These fields seem to be duplicates of the previous one, but they might be used for a different purpose.

Note: The fields with repeated names might be an error in the original YAML structure. It's possible that the original intention was to have a more complex form, but it seems to be a simple repetition.
