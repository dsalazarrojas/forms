# Facility Cleaning Service Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the quality and satisfaction of facilities cleaning services. The collected data will help evaluate and improve these services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the navigation buttons or links provided to move between pages. Each page is labeled with a corresponding number. Fill in all applicable fields and make sure to provide clear and concise responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Facility Cleaning Service Benchmark Survey** (`title`, required/optional): This is the title of the survey. It's not a field that needs to be filled but rather a heading to guide you through the survey.
- **Page 1** (`first_page`, optional): This page is an introduction or a welcome page. You can skip it if you're not interested in reading the survey instructions.
- **Cleaning Quality** (`cleaning_quality`, optional): Please select the best description that suits your experience with the facilities cleaning services.
  - Very good
  - Good
  - Average
  - Fair
  - Poor
- **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, optional): Indicate your overall satisfaction with the facilities cleaning services.
  - Very satisfied
  - Somewhat satisfied
  - Neutral
  - Somewhat dissatisfied
  - Very dissatisfied
- **Improvement Areas 1** (`improvement_areas`, optional): Please describe any areas where the facilities cleaning services could be improved.
- **Improvement Areas 2** (`improvement_areas_2`, optional): Another section to describe areas where the facilities cleaning services could be improved.
- **Improvement Areas 3** (`improvement_areas_3`, optional): A third section to describe areas where the facilities cleaning services could be improved.
- **Comments 1** (`comments`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about the facilities cleaning services.
- **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, optional): Another section for additional comments or feedback.
- **Comments 3** (`comments_3`, optional): Yet another section for additional comments or feedback.
- **...** (up to `Comments 14`): There will be 13 more sections for comments or feedback if needed.
- **Comments 15** (`comments_15`, optional): The last section for comments or feedback.
- **Facility Cleaning Service Benchmark Survey Comments** (`facility_cleaning_service_benchmark_survey_comments`, optional): This is the final comment section. Please use it to summarize any key points or suggestions.

## Tips
- Try to fill in all sections that apply to your experience with the facilities cleaning services.
- Be specific when providing improvement areas or comments.
- If unsure about a section, feel free to skip it if you're not comfortable answering or commenting.
- Navigate through the pages using the provided buttons or links.
