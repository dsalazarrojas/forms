# Economic Indicators Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Economic Indicators Survey is a form designed to collect data on various economic indicators. This form is intended for internal use within the organization and should not be used for external purposes without proper authorization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate audience that best describes your response.
2. Choose the countries that apply to your response.
3. Provide your economic sentiment in a brief text description.
4. Select the sentiment change you have observed.
5. Enter your current outlook on the economic situation.
6. Enter a confidence level for your assessment (0-100).
7. Enter the impact you expect the economic indicators to have.
8. Choose the likelihoods that apply to your response.
9. Select the industry that best describes your response.
10. Enter any additional comments or feedback.
11. Select the person or entity assigned to review your response.
12. Select the person or entity that created this survey.
13. Enter the date the survey was created.
14. Enter the date the survey was last updated.
15. Enter the date the survey was submitted.
16. You may also enter any custom data if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audience** (select_one): Select the audience that best describes your response.
* **Country** (select_multiple): Choose all the countries that apply to your response.
* **Economic Sentiment** (text): Enter a brief description of your current economic sentiment.
* **Sentiment Change** (select_one): Select the sentiment change you have observed.
* **Outlook** (text): Enter your current outlook on the economic situation.
* **Confidence** (number): Enter a confidence level for your assessment (0-100).
* **Impact** (number): Enter the impact you expect the economic indicators to have.
* **Likelihood** (select_multiple): Choose all the likelihoods that apply to your response.
* **Industry** (select_one): Select the industry that best describes your response.
* **Comments** (note): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Assigned To** (select_one): Select the person or entity assigned to review your response.
* **Created By** (select_one): Select the person or entity that created this survey.
* **Last Updated** (date): Enter the date the survey was last updated.
* **Last Viewed** (time): Not applicable.
* **Views** (number): Not applicable.
* **Comments Count** (number): Not applicable.
* **Ratings Count** (number): Not applicable.
* **Ratings Sum** (number): Not applicable.
* **Ratings Average** (number): Not applicable.
* **Form ID** (select_one): Select the form ID.
* **Created At** (date): Enter the date the survey was created.
* **Updated At** (date): Enter the date the survey was last updated.
* **Submitted At** (date): Enter the date the survey was submitted.
* **Form Data** (note): Not applicable.
* **Custom Data** (note): Not applicable.

Note: Fields marked as "not applicable" are not visible in the form and do not require entry.
