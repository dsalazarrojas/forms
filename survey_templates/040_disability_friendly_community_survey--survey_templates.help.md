# Disability-Friendly Community Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is the Disability-Friendly Community Survey, designed to collect valuable information from community members to better understand their experiences, needs, and suggestions for improvement. By filling out this form, you can help us create a more inclusive and accessible community for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you have filled out all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the required fields with your honest and accurate responses.
3. Use the "Other" option when answering questions that don't fit the provided options.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, note, optional): This section is not a multiple-choice question, but rather a note for you to write your thoughts and experiences about our community.
* **Accessibility Barriers** (`accessibility_barriers`, text, optional): Please list any barriers you have faced or are facing related to accessibility in our community.
* **Barriers Experienced** (`barriers_experienced`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the barriers you have experienced or are experiencing from the list:
	+ None
	+ Other
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_improvement`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving our community's accessibility and inclusivity.
* **Community Services** (`community_services`, select_one, optional): Choose the service you are most interested in:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Service Areas** (`service_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the service areas you are interested in:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Service Areas Other** (`service_areas_other`, text, optional): If none of the above options fit, please describe your service area of interest.
* **Demographic Data** (`demographic_data`, select_one, optional): Choose your demographic:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Demographic Data Other** (`demographic_data_other`, text, optional): If none of the above options fit, please provide your demographic information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Share your contact information so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Email Addresses** (`email_addresses`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your form.
* **Confirm Submission** (`confirm_submission`, select_one, optional): Confirm your submission:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2

Note: This form is intended to be completed by community members to provide feedback and suggestions for improvement. Please answer honestly and accurately to help us create a more inclusive and accessible community for everyone.
