# label_management_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The label management survey is a general form to collect information about a label management process. It aims to gather insights on various aspects related to the label, including workflow type, challenges, feature needs, operations, manufacturing, logistics, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a workflow type that best describes the current state of the label management process.
2. Describe any challenges you are currently facing or have faced in the past related to the label.
3. Outline the feature needs that are essential for the successful implementation of the label management process.
4. Specify the operations involved in the current label management process.
5. Detail the manufacturing steps or processes used to produce the label.
6. Describe the logistics associated with the label management process.
7. Provide any comments or feedback you'd like to share about the process.
8. If you have a specific person in mind who would be the best person to address any questions or concerns, select their name from the dropdown list or select "Other" and provide the relevant contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workflow Type** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the current workflow type associated with the label management process.
* **Challenges** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): List any challenges you are facing or have faced in the past related to the label management process.
* **Feature Needs** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): Specify the essential feature needs for the successful implementation of the label management process.
* **Operations** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): Describe the operations involved in the current label management process.
* **Manufacturing** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): Detail the manufacturing steps or processes used to produce the label.
* **Logistics** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): Describe the logistics associated with the label management process.
* **Comments** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the label management process.
* **Assigned To** (`<name>`, `select_one`, optional): Select a person to contact for any questions or concerns about the label management process. You can choose from the list of suggested options or select "Other" and provide the relevant contact information.
