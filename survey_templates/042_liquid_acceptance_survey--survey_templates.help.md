# liquid_acceptance_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "liquid_acceptance_survey" form is used to gather information about the acceptance and use of liquid-based products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to initiate the form.
2. Fill out the form sections below with your responses.
3. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Brand (Page 1)
* **Brand** (`brand`, text, optional): Enter the name of the brand associated with the product or service.

### Liquid Acceptance (Page 1)
* **Liquid Acceptance** (`liquid_acceptance`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the product or service is accepted or not. You can select 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.

### Product Name (Page 1)
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product or service being evaluated.

### Acceptance Levels (Page 2)
* **Acceptance Levels** (`acceptance_levels`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the levels of acceptance that apply to the product or service. You can choose 'Yes' for each level as needed.
  - Level 1: Level 1
  - Level 2: Level 2
  - Level 3: Level 3

### Influencing Factors (Page 2)
* **Influencing Factors** (`influencing_factors`, note, optional): This field is not applicable as it is a note field without any specific options. You can use this space to provide any additional information that may be relevant to the form.

Tips:
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field's label for clarification.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
