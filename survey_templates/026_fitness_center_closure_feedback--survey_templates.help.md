# Fitness Center Closure Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with the temporary closure of our fitness center. Your input will help us understand the impact of the closure and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best describe your experience and provide any additional comments you'd like to share. You can choose from the options provided for each question, and you can also add additional comments in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was the impact of the closure on your workouts?** (`feedback_question_1`, select_one, required: false): Please select the option that best describes how the closure affected your workouts.
* **How would you rate the communication quality from the gym about the closure?** (`communication_quality_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the options that apply to describe how well we communicated about the closure.
* **Intent to return after the gym reopens?** (`intent_to_return_1`, select_one, required: false): Select the option that indicates your intention to return to our fitness center once it reopens.
* **Additional comments** (`comment_1`, note, required: false): If you'd like to share any additional thoughts or concerns, please type them in this field.
* **Email** (`email_1`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number_1`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.

## Tips
Please note that all fields are optional, so you can skip any question that doesn't apply to your experience. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
