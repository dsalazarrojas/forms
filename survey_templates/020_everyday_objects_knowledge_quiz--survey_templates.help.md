# everyday_objects_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge about everyday objects. It contains 12 questions that cover various aspects of these objects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and its questions carefully.
2. Answer each question according to your knowledge and understanding.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
