# Chips Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Chips Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our chip products. This form is used to gather information about the freshness, crispiness, flavor, and delivery of our chips, as well as any other comments or suggestions customers may have.

This form is used to help us understand how we can improve our products and services to better meet the needs and expectations of our customers. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey and provide us with your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Answer each question or rating according to your experience.
3. If a question is not applicable to you, please select "Not applicable" or "Not sure".
4. You may add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Any suggestions for improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, text, required): Enter the number of your order, if applicable.
* **How fresh were the chips?** (`freshness`, select_one, required): Rate the freshness of the chips you received:
	+ Very Fresh
	+ Fresh
	+ Stale
* **Rate the crispiness level** (`crispiness`, select_one, required): Rate the crispiness of the chips you received:
	+ Perfectly Crunchy
	+ Good
	+ Soggy
* **How was the saltiness?** (`salt_level`, select_one, required): Rate the saltiness of the chips:
	+ Too Salty
	+ Just Right
	+ Not Salty Enough
* **Rate the flavor profile** (`flavor_rating`, number, required): Rate the flavor of the chips:
	+ (Use the number keys to input your rating)
* **Was the portion size adequate?** (`portion_size`, select_one, required): Rate the portion size:
	+ Too Small
	+ Just Right
	+ Very Generous
* **Rate the speed of delivery** (`delivery_speed`, select_one, required): Rate the speed of delivery:
	+ Faster than expected
	+ On time
	+ Delayed
* **Was the packaging intact upon arrival?** (`packaging_condition`, select_one, required): Was the packaging of the chips intact upon arrival:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Would you recommend our chips to others?** (`recommend_friends`, select_one, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend our chips:
	+ Definitely
	+ Probably
	+ Not sure
	+ False
* **Any suggestions for improvement?** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any other comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
