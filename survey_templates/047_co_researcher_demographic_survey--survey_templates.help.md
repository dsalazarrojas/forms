# Co-Researcher Demographic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information from co-researchers participating in a project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the title of the research project you are participating in.
2. In the **-- Co-Researcher Profile --** section, add a brief note about yourself.
3. In the **Age Range** field, select your current age range.
4. In the **Gender Identity** field, select your gender identity.
5. In the **Highest Level of Education Completed** field, select your highest level of education completed.
6. In the **Primary Field of Expertise** field, enter your primary field of expertise.
7. In the **Total Years of Research Experience** field, enter the total number of years you have been conducting research.
8. In the **-- Professional Background --** section, add a brief note about your professional background.
9. In the **Current Institutional Affiliation** field, enter your current institution of affiliation.
10. In the **Country of Residence** field, enter the country where you currently reside.
11. In the **-- Collaboration Information --** section, add a brief note about your collaboration with the team.
12. In the **Primary Role in this Specific Project** field, select your primary role in this project.
13. In the **Have you collaborated with this team before?** field, select whether you have collaborated with the team before.
14. In the **I acknowledge this information is used for research demographic analysis only** field, select whether you acknowledge that this information will be used for research purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Project Title** (`project_title`, text, **required**): Enter the title of the research project you are participating in.
* **-- Co-Researcher Profile --** (`section_profile`, note, **false**): This section is a note field for you to add a brief description about yourself.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, **required**): Select your current age range from 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, or 65 or older.
* **Gender Identity** (`gender_identity`, select_one, **false**): Select your gender identity from Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say.
* **Highest Level of Education Completed** (`highest_education`, select_one, **false**): Select your highest level of education completed from Bachelor's, Master's, PhD, MD, or Other.
* **Primary Field of Expertise** (`expertise_field`, text, **required**): Enter your primary field of expertise.
* **Total Years of Research Experience** (`years_experience`, number, **required**): Enter the total number of years you have been conducting research.
* **-- Professional Background --** (`section_professional`, note, **false**): This section is a note field for you to add a brief description about your professional background.
* **Current Institutional Affiliation** (`institutional_affiliation`, text, **required**): Enter your current institution of affiliation.
* **Country of Residence** (`residence_country`, text, **required**): Enter the country where you currently reside.
* **-- Collaboration Information --** (`section_collaboration`, note, **false**): This section is a note field for you to add a brief description about your collaboration with the team.
* **Primary Role in this Specific Project** (`project_role`, select_one, **required**): Select your primary role in this project from Lead Researcher, Associate Researcher, Data Collector, Analyst, or Consultant.
* **Have you collaborated with this team before?** (`previous_team_collaboration`, select_one, **false**): Select whether you have collaborated with the team before.
* **I acknowledge this information is used for research demographic analysis only** (`demographic_ack`, select_one, **required**): Select whether you acknowledge that this information will be used for research purposes.
