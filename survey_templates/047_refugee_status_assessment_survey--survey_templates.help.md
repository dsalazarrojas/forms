# Refugee Status Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refugee Status Assessment Survey is a form used to collect information about refugees' personal and biographical details, which will help in assessing their status and provide necessary support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and fill in all required fields. If you are having trouble understanding any question, please don't hesitate to ask for help.
2. Start by filling out the intake information page, which is the first page of the form.
3. Provide your name on the "Name" field.
4. Enter your age on the "Age" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intake Information**: This page is meant to gather some initial data from you. Please fill out this page completely before moving on to the next part of the form.
* **Name**: Please provide your name as it should be written on your identification documents.
* **Age**: Enter your age to help us understand your age-related requirements and eligibility.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to verify your age and provide context to your age-related information.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to provide context to your age-related information. 

Note: The date of birth field type is set to "date" which means you must enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format. 

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure we can provide you with the best possible services.
- If you are unsure about any field or question, please don't hesitate to ask for help.
- Please note that the information collected through this form will be used for the purpose of assessing your refugee status. 

Remember, the form is a tool to help us better understand your needs and circumstances. Please feel free to ask if you have any questions or need further assistance.
