# Community Insights Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Community Insights Survey is designed to gather honest feedback from community members about their experiences and perceptions of their local community. Your input will help us understand what matters most to you and what you would like to see improved or changed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select all that apply for multiple-choice questions. Be as specific as possible in your responses, and feel free to provide detailed thoughts and suggestions in the text field. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Profile** (`section_resident`, note, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form.
* **Number of years you have lived in this community** (`residency_duration`, number, required: true): Please select the number of years you have lived in this community.
* **-- Satisfaction and Safety --** (`section_satisfaction`, note, required: false): This section provides a header for the next two questions.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Local Government Services** (`government_satisfaction`, select_one, required: true): Please rate your overall satisfaction with local government services, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Perception of Quality of Local Public Schools** (`school_quality`, select_one, required: true): Please rate your perception of the quality of local public schools, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **General Sense of Safety in the Neighborhood** (`safety_perception`, select_one, required: true): Please select how you feel about the general sense of safety in the neighborhood.
* **Top Priority for Community Improvement** (`improvement_priority`, select_one, required: true): Please select what you believe should be the first focus for community improvement.
* **Awareness of Local Community Events and Meetings** (`event_awareness`, select_one, required: true): Please rate your awareness of local community events and meetings, with High being very aware and Low being not aware at all.
* **Use of Public Amenities in the last 6 months** (`amenity_usage`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the public amenities you have used in the last 6 months.
* **Frequency of Interaction with Neighbors** (`neighbor_interaction`, select_one, required: true): Please select how often you interact with your neighbors.
* **Suggestions for Better Community Planning and Growth** (`planning_suggestions`, text, required: false): Please provide any specific thoughts or suggestions you have for improving the community.
* **I consent to share my insights for community research** (`participation_consent`, select_one, required: true): Please confirm that you consent to share your insights for community research purposes.
