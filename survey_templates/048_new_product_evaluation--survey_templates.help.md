# New Product Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information about new products. It's essential to provide accurate and honest responses to help us better understand your thoughts and experiences with the product.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For each field, provide your genuine feedback and comments.
3. Don't hesitate to skip any fields that are not relevant to your experience with the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, no): This field is for any additional general information about the product you'd like to share.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, no): Describe the product's features, specifications, or any other relevant details.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, text, no): Share your experience with the product's customer support or any interactions you had with their team.
* **Product Features** (`product_features`, text, no): List the product's key features that impressed or disappointed you.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, no): Share any notable images or screenshots that you think showcase the product's design or functionality.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, no): Provide a brief description of the product and how it fits your needs or preferences.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, no): Share your overall satisfaction with the product and any areas for improvement.
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, no): If you're an employee, share your experience with using or interacting with the product in your work environment.
* **Company Info** (`company_info`, text, no): Share any information you'd like to share about the company that produces or markets the product.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, no): Provide any contact information you'd like to share, such as email or phone numbers.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, no): Share any additional details or information about the product that you think is essential to share.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, no): This field is for any final comments or suggestions you'd like to provide.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Keep your answers concise and clear.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask.
* We appreciate your time and input in helping us improve the product and provide better service to our customers.
