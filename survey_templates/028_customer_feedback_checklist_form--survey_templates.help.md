# Customer Feedback Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer feedback checklist form is designed to collect customer feedback and experiences.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name on page 1.
2. Select your contact information (Yes/No) and provide your email address on page 2.
3. Enter your feedback on page 2.
4. Select your preferred date for feedback submission on page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`form_page_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name, which will be used to identify you in our records.
* **Contact Info** (`form_page_2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select whether you have a website, social media, or email address.
* **Email** (`form_page_2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you for follow-up.
* **Feedback** (`form_page_2`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your feedback about your experience with our organization.
* **Date** (`form_page_3`, `date`, required/optional): Select a date for your feedback submission.
