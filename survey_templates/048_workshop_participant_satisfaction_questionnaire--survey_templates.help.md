# workshop_participant_satisfaction_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather participant feedback about the workshop they attended. It helps us understand what the participant learned, how they felt about the content and presentation, and what they would recommend this workshop to others. Your input will help us improve the quality of our workshops and better serve you and your colleagues in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant questions by choosing one of the options provided.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, type your response in the provided text field.
4. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
