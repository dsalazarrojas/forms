# online_screen_addiction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to explore your screen use habits, experiences, and perceptions to help you better understand your relationship with screens and identify areas for personal improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question.
2. Select the most accurate response for each question, based on your experiences and perceptions.
3. Fill out the form completely, but feel free to skip questions that do not apply to you or if you are unsure of the answer.
