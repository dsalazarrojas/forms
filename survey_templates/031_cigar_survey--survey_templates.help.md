# Cigar Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a person's preferences and behaviors related to cigars, including their name, email, demographics, smoking habits, and purchasing habits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the required questions (indicated by *) as thoroughly as possible.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions (e.g. "Select all that apply").
3. List up to 3 favorite brands for the "Favorite Brands" question.
4. Answer all text questions as thoroughly as possible (e.g. "Additional Comments").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, but feel free to remain anonymous if you wish.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for survey results only.
* **Your Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **How Long Have You Been Smoking Cigars** (`smoking_experience`, select_one, required): Choose how long you have been smoking cigars from the provided options.
* **How Often Do You Smoke Cigars** (`smoking_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you smoke cigars from the provided options.
* **Preferred Cigar Size** (`preferred_size`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred cigar size from the provided options.
* **Preferred Strength** (`preferred_strength`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred cigar strength from the provided options.
* **Preferred Wrapper Type** (`preferred_wrapper`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred cigar wrapper type from the provided options.
* **Preferred Country of Origin** (`preferred_origin`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred country of origin for cigars from the provided options.
* **Typical Price Range Per Cigar** (`price_range`, select_one, required): Choose your typical price range for cigars from the provided options.
* **Where Do You Usually Purchase Cigars** (`purchase_location`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the places where you typically purchase cigars from the provided options.
* **How Do You Store Your Cigars** (`storage_method`, select_one, required): Choose your method for storing cigars from the provided options.
* **Which Accessories Do You Use** (`accessories`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the accessories you use for smoking cigars from the provided options.
* **What Do You Like to Pair with Cigars** (`pairing_preference`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the things you like to pair with cigars from the provided options.
* **Favorite Brands** (`favorite_brands`, text, optional): List up to 3 of your favorite cigar brands.
* **Where Do You Typically Smoke** (`smoking_location`, select_one, required): Choose where you typically smoke cigars from the provided options.
* **Do You Enjoy Cigars Socially** (`social_aspect`, select_one, required): Choose how often you enjoy smoking cigars with others from the provided options.
* **How Loyal Are You to Specific Brands** (`brand_loyalty`, select_one, required): Choose your level of loyalty to specific cigar brands from the provided options.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Cigar Selection Available** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Choose your overall satisfaction with the available cigar selection from the provided options.
* **What Cigars Would You Recommend** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Share your favorite cigars with others.
* **Any Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any comments or experiences about cigar preferences.
