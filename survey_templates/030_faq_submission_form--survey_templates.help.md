# faq_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and questions from users, which will be reviewed and responded to by the support team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of question you would like to ask (General, Technical, or Billing) from the "question_type" dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief description of the question in the "question_description" field.
3. Type your answer to the question in the "question_answer" field.
4. Enter your name and any relevant information about yourself in the "submitter_info" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "submitter_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Frequently Asked Questions** (submission_form): This field is the main section of the form where you can type or paste your question. Please provide a clear and concise description of the question you would like to ask.
* **Question Type** (question_type): Please select one of the three options (General, Technical, or Billing) that best describes the type of question you are asking.
* **Question Description** (question_description): Provide a brief description of the question in this field. This will help the support team understand the context of your question.
* **Question Answer** (question_answer): Type your answer to the question in this field.
* **Submitter Info** (submitter_info): Enter your name and any relevant information about yourself in this field. This will help the support team to contact you if needed.
* **Submitter Email** (submitter_email): Enter your email address in this field so that we can contact you if we need more information about your question.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields (question_type and question_description).
* Please provide clear and concise questions that accurately describe your issue.
* If you are submitting multiple questions, please fill out this form multiple times.
* Please note that all submitted information will be reviewed and responded to by the support team.
