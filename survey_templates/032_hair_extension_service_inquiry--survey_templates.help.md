# Hair Extension Service Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your hair extension service inquiries, which will be reviewed by our staff to determine the best course of action for your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name (first name and last name) in the respective fields.
2. Provide your email address in the email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
4. Select the type of service you are inquiring about from the service type dropdown menu.
5. Enter the location where you would like to receive the service.
6. Write a brief message about your inquiry in the message field.
7. If applicable, select the staff member you would like to be assigned to your case from the assigned to dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name to help us identify you accurately.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to contact you about your inquiry.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can call or text you about your request.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of hair extension service you are inquiring about (Hair Extensions, Hair Transplant, Hair Restoration, or Other).
* **Location**: Enter the location where you would like to receive the service.
* **Message**: Write a brief message about your inquiry, so we can understand your needs better.
* **Assigned To**: If you have a preferred staff member to be assigned to your case, select their name from the list.
