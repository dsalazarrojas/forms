# Body And Facial Treatment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients who have received body and facial treatments. Your responses will help us understand your experience and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Choose options from select_one fields by clicking on the desired answer.
4. Select multiple options for select_multiple fields by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on a Mac) and clicking on multiple answers.
5. Type in text fields to provide more information.
