# Recruitment Process Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from candidates about their experience with the recruitment process. We want to understand how they feel about the communication, interview process, and overall hiring experience. This information will help us improve our recruitment process and provide better experiences for future candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each page as you move through the survey.
2. Answer every question that applies to your experience.
3. Use the provided hints to guide your answers.
4. Don't hesitate to add any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Application** (`candidate_application`, text, not required): This is a text field where you can briefly describe your experience with the initial application process. You can mention any difficulties you faced, how clear the instructions were, or any other relevant details.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, not required): Please rate how you feel about the communication with the recruiter. Was it good, bad, or somewhere in between? You can also mention specific instances where communication was effective or ineffective.
* **Interview Process** (`interview_process`, select_multiple, not required): This is a multiple-choice field where you can select the type of interview you participated in (Phone, Video, In-person). If you had multiple interviews, feel free to select all that apply.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, select_one, not required): Please rate your experience with feedback from the recruiter. Was it positive, negative, or neutral? This will help us understand where we can improve.
* **Candidate Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the hiring process on a scale of 1-5. This will help us gauge our success in meeting your needs.
* **Time to Hire** (`time_to_hire`, date, not required): If you have an idea of when your hiring process took place, please provide the start and end dates.
* **Time to First Interview** (`time_to_first_interview`, time, not required): If you had an interview, please estimate the time from when you applied to when you received your first interview invitation.
* **Recruiter Skills** (`recruiter_skills`, select_multiple, not required): What skills do you think a recruiter should have to excel in their job? This could be things like communication, empathy, or problem-solving.
* **Candidate Preferences** (`candidate_preferences`, select_multiple, not required): What preferences do you think a recruiter should have? This could be openness to new ideas, flexibility in the work schedule, or respect for the candidate's time.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions about your experience, please share them here.
