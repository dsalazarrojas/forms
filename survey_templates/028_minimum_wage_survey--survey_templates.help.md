# Minimum Wage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Minimum Wage Survey is a form designed to collect information about an individual's current salary and their feelings about it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions honestly about your current salary and your feelings about it.
2.  Provide accurate and clear responses to each question.
3.  Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current salary?**: This question asks for your current salary. Please enter your actual salary, including any relevant details such as bonuses or commissions.
* **Do you feel you are underpaid?**: Choose one of the options that best describes your feelings about your current salary. If you feel you are underpaid, select "very underpaid" or "fairly underpaid." If you feel you are paid fairly, select "fairly paid." If you feel you are overpaid, select "very not paid" or "fairly not paid."
* **What would you like to do to earn more money?**: In this free-text field, you can provide any ideas or suggestions you have about earning more money. This could include seeking a promotion, taking additional courses or training, or pursuing a different career path.
* **Do you think you are overpaid?**: Similar to the previous question, choose one of the options that best describes your feelings about your current salary. If you feel you are overpaid, select "very overpaid" or "fairly overpaid." If you feel you are paid fairly, select "fairly not paid." If you feel you are underpaid, select "very not paid" or "fairly not paid."
