# Reunion Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Reunion Survey is designed to collect details about an event or reunion. This form helps in gathering essential information about the event, such as date, time, venue, and estimated number of guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Choose the Event Time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
3. Enter the Venue name where the event will take place.
4. Fill in the Event Name, e.g., "Family Reunion 2023."
5. If known, specify the Expected Guests attending the event.
6. After the event, enter the Actual number of guests who attended.
7. Mention any Food and Drinks that will be served.
8. If necessary, provide accommodation details.
9. Enter the contact number for any urgent matters.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Date <br />
**Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): The date of the event. Please enter in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Event Time <br />
**Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): The time of the event. Use 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### Venue <br />
**Venue** (`venue`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the place where the event will take place.

### Event Name <br />
**Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required: false): This is the name of the event, e.g., "Reunion Party."

### Expected Guests <br />
**Expected Guests** (`expected_guests`, `text`, required: false): Estimated number of guests attending the event, e.g., 50, 200, etc.

### Actual Guests <br />
**Actual Guests** (`actual_guests`, `text`, required: false): Actual number of guests who attended the event.

### Food and Drinks <br />
**Food and Drinks** (`food_and_drinks`, `text`, required: false): Mention any food and drinks that will be served during the event.

### Accommodation <br />
**Accommodation** (`accommodation`, `text`, required: false): If the event requires accommodation, provide details here.

### Contact Number <br />
**Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number for any urgent matters.

## Tips
* Ensure the event details are accurate and up-to-date.
* Enter the actual number of guests after the event.
