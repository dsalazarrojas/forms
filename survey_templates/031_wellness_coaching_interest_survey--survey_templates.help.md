# Wellness Coaching Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Coaching Interest Survey is designed to help coaches understand potential clients' needs and provide relevant coaching services. This survey is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any form platform and is an original adaptation created using AI.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, selecting the options that best fit your responses.
2. You can select multiple options for certain questions, so feel free to choose as many as apply.
3. You can skip any questions that don't apply to you.
4. When you've completed the form, click "Submit" to send it in.
