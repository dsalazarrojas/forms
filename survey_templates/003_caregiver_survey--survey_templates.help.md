# Caregiver Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide is intended to assist users in completing the "Caregiver Survey" form, which is used to evaluate and provide feedback on the quality of care provided by caregivers. The survey collects information about the respondent's relationship with the care recipient, their interaction frequency and duration, and their opinions on various aspects of care provided.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and questions carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. For questions with multiple choice options, select the answer that best reflects your opinion or experience.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your thoughts and suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Respondent Information

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`): Enter your name for reference purposes.

### Respondent Relationship

* **Your Relationship to the Care Recipient** (`respondent_relationship`): Choose the relationship you have with the care recipient. Options are:
	+ Family Member
	+ Friend
	+ Healthcare Provider
	+ Social Worker
	+ Other Professional

### Date

* **Today's Date** (`survey_date`): Enter the current date in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Caregiver Information

* **Caregiver Full Name** (`caregiver_full_name`): Enter the full name of the caregiver you are evaluating.

### Care Duration

* **How long have you known this caregiver?** (`care_duration`): Choose the duration of your interaction with the caregiver. Options are:
	+ Less than 1 month
	+ 1 to 3 months
	+ 3 to 6 months
	+ 6 months to 1 year
	+ More than 1 year

### Interaction Frequency

* **How frequently do you interact with this caregiver?** (`care_frequency`): Choose the frequency of your interaction with the caregiver. Options are:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times per week
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Less frequently

### Compassion and Attitude

### Professional Competence

* This section assesses the caregiver's professional competence. Please answer the following questions based on your experiences.

### Safety and Reliability

* This section assesses the caregiver's punctuality and reliability. Please answer the following questions based on your experiences.

### Communication and Hygiene

* This section assesses the caregiver's communication and personal hygiene. Please answer the following questions based on your experiences.

### Overall Assessment

* **Overall satisfaction with this caregiver** (`overall_satisfaction`): Choose how satisfied you are with the care provided by the caregiver. Options are:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### Overall Recommendation

* **Would you recommend this caregiver to others?** (`would_recommend`): Choose whether you would recommend the caregiver to others. Options are:
	+ Yes, highly
	+ True
	+ Neutral
	+ False
	+ No, would not recommend

### Additional Comments

* **Additional comments or suggestions for improvement** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have for the caregiver.

Please note that the form is intended for evaluation and feedback purposes, so please answer honestly and provide as much detail as possible to help us improve the care provided. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
