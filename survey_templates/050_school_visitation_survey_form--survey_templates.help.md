# School Visitation Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on visitors to the school, including their names, contact information, and any comments or feedback they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Type your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
4. Provide any additional Comments about your visit in the Comments field.
5. Select the Facilities that you visited from the Facilities dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents, such as ID cards or government-issued IDs.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents, such as ID cards or government-issued IDs.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for any questions or follow-up.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any questions or follow-up.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any comments or feedback you may have about your visit. This will help us improve our services.
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, select_one, required/optional): Select the facilities you visited during your tour. Please choose one or more facilities that apply to your visit.
