# online_exam_platform_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user feedback on various aspects of the online exam platform, including usability, reliability, overall experience, and more. Your input will help us understand user experiences and improve the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page (usability, reliability, overall experience, etc.) as instructed below.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, please type them in the "feedback" field.
4. If you want to provide your contact information, please fill out the "email" and "phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **usability** (1, number, required: false): This question asks about your overall impression of the platform's user interface and ease of use. Please rate it on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very poor and 10 is very good.
* **reliability** (2, text, required: false): This question asks about your perception of the platform's reliability. Please write a brief description of your experience (e.g., "very reliable", "somewhat reliable", etc.).
* **satisfaction** (3, number, required: false): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the platform. Please rate it on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very poor and 10 is very good.
* **overall_experience** (4, number, required: false): This question is similar to "satisfaction", but asks about your overall experience with the platform.
* **platform_usability** (5, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your impression of the platform's usability. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Very good
	+ Somewhat good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **platform_reliability** (6, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your perception of the platform's reliability. Please select all the options that apply to your experience:
	+ Very reliable
	+ Somewhat reliable
	+ Fairly reliable
	+ Not reliable
* **user_experience** (7, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your overall experience with the platform. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **time_management** (8, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your impression of the platform's time management features. Please select all the options that apply to your experience:
	+ Very good
	+ Somewhat good
	+ Fair
	+ Not good
* **feedback** (9, note, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **email** (10, email, required: false): This is a field for you to provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **phone** (11, text, required: false): This is a field for you to provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.
