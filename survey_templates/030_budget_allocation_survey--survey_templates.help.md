# Budget Allocation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Budget Allocation Survey is designed to gather information from respondents about their priorities and concerns related to budgeting within an organization. This survey will help management understand how different departments or teams would like to allocate their budget across various categories.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please fill out the required fields with your information.
2. For each section, please enter your answer in the provided text or select a relevant option.
3. Please ensure that you are answering based on your department or team's budget priorities and concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Your Name** (Required)

*   **Your Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **Email Address** (Required)

*   **Email Address**: Enter your official email address associated with your department or team.

### 3. **Department** (Required)

*   **Department**: Enter the name of your department or team within the organization.

### 4. **Your Role or Title** (Optional)

*   **Your Role or Title**: If applicable, enter your job title or role within your department or team.

### 5. **Budget Year Being Assessed** (Required)

*   **Budget Year Being Assessed**: Enter the fiscal year for which this budget is being assessed.

### 6. **How should overall budget be allocated** (Optional)

*   **How should overall budget be allocated**: Provide a brief description or explanation of how you think the overall budget should be allocated.

### 7. **Personnel and Salaries - Allocation Percentage** (Required)

*   **Personnel and Salaries - Allocation Percentage**: Enter the percentage value between 0-100% for personnel and salaries allocation.

### 8. **Feedback on Personnel Budget** (Optional)

*   **Feedback on Personnel Budget**: Enter any feedback or concerns you have about the personnel and salaries budget.

### 9. **Technology and Equipment - Allocation Percentage** (Required)

*   **Technology and Equipment - Allocation Percentage**: Enter the percentage value between 0-100% for technology and equipment allocation.

### 10. **Feedback on Technology Budget** (Optional)

*   **Feedback on Technology Budget**: Enter any feedback or concerns you have about the technology and equipment budget.

### 11. **Operations and Facilities - Allocation Percentage** (Required)

*   **Operations and Facilities - Allocation Percentage**: Enter the percentage value between 0-100% for operations and facilities allocation.

### 12. **Feedback on Operations Budget** (Optional)

*   **Feedback on Operations Budget**: Enter any feedback or concerns you have about the operations and facilities budget.

### 13. **Marketing and Outreach - Allocation Percentage** (Required)

*   **Marketing and Outreach - Allocation Percentage**: Enter the percentage value between 0-100% for marketing and outreach allocation.

### 14. **Feedback on Marketing Budget** (Optional)

*   **Feedback on Marketing Budget**: Enter any feedback or concerns you have about the marketing and outreach budget.

### 15. **Training and Development - Allocation Percentage** (Required)

*   **Training and Development - Allocation Percentage**: Enter the percentage value between 0-100% for training and development allocation.

### 16. **Feedback on Training Budget** (Optional)

*   **Feedback on Training Budget**: Enter any feedback or concerns you have about the training and development budget.

### 17. **Contingency or Reserve - Allocation Percentage** (Required)

*   **Contingency or Reserve - Allocation Percentage**: Enter the percentage value between 0-100% for contingency or reserve allocation.

### 18. **Are there funding constraints or limitations?** (Required)

*   **Are there funding constraints or limitations**: Select whether there are significant, some, or no constraints.

### 19. **Budget Concerns or Challenges** (Optional)

*   **Budget Concerns or Challenges**: Enter any budget concerns or challenges you have that were not previously mentioned.

### 20. **Any Additional Budget Priorities** (Optional)

*   **Any Additional Budget Priorities**: Enter any additional budget priorities that you feel are important but not listed above.
