# Recreational Program Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this questionnaire is to gather feedback from participants about their experience in recreational programs. This will help in understanding what works well and what areas need improvement, ultimately enhancing the program's quality and participant satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer each question truthfully, providing as much detail as you can.
2. Select all that applies or choose your rating for each question.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the * required * label.
4. Enter your contact information at the end, so we can get back to you with any follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **program_participation** (`program_participation`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the programs you have participated in, as listed.
* **program_participation_program_part** (`program_participation_program_part`, select_one, required): Choose the specific part of the program you participated in (if applicable).
* **overall_satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program (Option 1: Very Satisfied, Option 2: Somewhat Satisfied, Option 3: Not Satisfied).
* **program_satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, text, optional): Share any thoughts about your experience in the program.
* **program_satisfaction_value** (`program_satisfaction_value`, number, optional): If you want to provide a numerical rating of your satisfaction, type it in.
* **comments** (`comments`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your experience and any comments or suggestions.
* **comments_comments** (`comments_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments, feel free to share.
* **comments_comments_program_part** (`comments_comments_program_part`, select_one, optional): If it's relevant to your comments, choose the part of the program that relates to your comments.
* **comments_comments_value** (`comments_comments_value`, number, optional): If you want to numerically rate the importance of your comments, type it in.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you with any follow-up questions.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to ensure we know who you are.
* **participant_email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted via email, enter your email address here.

Please note that some fields are marked as "optional" but it's still beneficial to fill them out for a more comprehensive feedback.
