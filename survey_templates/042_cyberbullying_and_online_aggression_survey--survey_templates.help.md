# cyberbullying_and_online_aggression_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect anonymous data on the prevalence of cyberbullying and online aggression within and outside of institutions. The purpose of this form is to help identify trends and patterns in these issues, allowing for more effective strategies to be developed to address them. Please note that this form is for internal use only and the data collected will not be shared with any third parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Please answer all the questions truthfully, as this information will be used for research purposes only.
2. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.
3. You can provide additional information or context where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: `false`):
	+ This is a note field where you can provide any additional information or context before proceeding with the survey.
* **Bullying within institution** (`bullying_within_institution`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ This question asks about the frequency of bullying or harassment you experienced within an institution (e.g., school, work, etc.). Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Bullying outside institution** (`bullying_outside_institution`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ This question asks about the frequency of bullying or harassment you experienced outside of an institution (e.g., online, in public, etc.). Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Self-Cyberbullying** (`self_cyberbullying`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ This question asks about the frequency of online harassment or bullying you have experienced from others towards yourself. Please select the option that best describes your experience.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
* If you experience any issues or have any questions, please contact our support team.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey! Your input is greatly appreciated.
