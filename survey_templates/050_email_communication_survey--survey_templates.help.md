# Email Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Email Communication Survey is designed to collect information about the frequency and effectiveness of your email communications. This survey will help us understand how often you interact with emails and what the target audience for your emails is, allowing us to better understand the impact of our communication efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the frequency of your email interactions (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, Less than 1/month) in the "How often do you interact with emails?" field.
2. Choose the target audience for your emails from the list (Email, Social media, Phone, Other, Text message, Website).
3. Enter the open rate of your emails as a percentage in the "What is your open rate (as a percentage)?" field.
4. Enter the open rate of your second set of emails as a percentage in the "What is the open rate of your emails?" field.
5. Enter the reply rate of your emails as a percentage in the "What is your reply rate (as a percentage)?" field.
6. Enter the reply rate of your second set of emails as a percentage in the "What is the reply rate of your emails?" field.
7. Enter the click rate of your emails as a percentage in the "What is your click rate (as a percentage)?" field.
8. Enter the click rate of your second set of emails as a percentage in the "What is the click rate of your emails?" field.
9. Enter the target audience for your second set of emails from the list (Email, Social media, Phone, Other, Text message, Website).
10. Enter the frequency of your second set of emails in the "How often do you send emails?" field.
11. Enter the subject line of your emails in the "What is your email subject line like?" field.
12. Enter the body of your emails in the "What is the content of your email body like?" field.
13. Enter the body of your second set of emails in the "What is the content of your email body like?" field.
14. Enter the click rate of your second set of emails as a percentage in the "What is the click rate of your emails?" field.
15. Enter the reply rate of your third set of emails as a percentage in the "Email Reply Rate 3" field.
16. Enter the frequency of your third set of emails in the "How often do you interact with emails?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How often do you interact with emails?** (`email_frequency_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how often you interact with emails, which can be Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or Less than 1/month.
* **What is the target audience for your emails?** (`email_target_audience_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose the target audience for your emails, which can be Email, Social media, Phone, Other, Text message, or Website.
* **What is your open rate (as a percentage)?** (`email_open_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the open rate of your emails as a percentage.
* **What is the open rate of your emails?** (`email_open_rate_2`, `number`, required): Enter the open rate of your emails as a percentage.
* **What is your reply rate (as a percentage)?** (`email_reply_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the reply rate of your emails as a percentage.
* **What is the reply rate of your emails?** (`email_reply_rate_2`, `number`, required): Enter the reply rate of your emails as a percentage.
* **What is the click rate (as a percentage)?** (`email_click_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the click rate of your emails as a percentage.
* **What is the click rate of your emails?** (`email_click_rate_2`, `number`, required): Enter the click rate of your emails as a percentage.
* **Do you have a call to action in your emails?** (`email_call_to_action`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether you have a call to action in your emails, which can be 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **What is the content of your email body like?** (`email_body`, `text`, required): Enter the content of your email body.
* **What is the content of your email body like?** (`email_body_2`, `text`, required): Enter the content of your email body.
* **What is the target audience for your second set of emails?** (`email_target_audience_2_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose the target audience for your second set of emails, which can be Email, Social media, Phone, Other, Text message, or Website.
* **What is the target audience for your third set of emails?** (`email_target_audience_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose the target audience for your third set of emails, which can be Email, Social media, Phone, Other, Text message, or Website.
* **How often do you send emails?** (`email_frequency_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how often you send emails, which can be Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, Less than 1/month.
* **What is your email subject line like?** (`email_subject`, `text`, required): Enter the subject line of your emails.
* **What is the content of your second set of email body like?** (`email_body_2`, `text`, required): Enter the content of your second set of email body.
