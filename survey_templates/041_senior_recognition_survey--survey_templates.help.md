# Senior Recognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Recognition Survey form is used to collect information for senior recognition. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the senior who is being recognized.
2. Provide the Achievement Story for the senior's accomplishment.
3. Add Contact Info for the senior.
4. Enter their Email for communication purposes.
5. Upload a Photo of the senior (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Senior Recognition Survey Form** (`senior_recognition_survey_form`, text, required/optional): This field is the title of the form.
* **Achievement Story** (`achievement_story`, text, required/optional): Enter the senior's achievement or accomplishment that is being recognized.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the senior's contact information for follow-up or communication purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the senior's email address for communication purposes.
* **Photo** (`photo`, text, required/optional): Upload a photo of the senior being recognized.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for the senior being recognized.
* Ensure that the senior is aware that this form is being used for their recognition.
* Use an up-to-date contact info for the senior.
* Use a clear and recent photo of the senior.
