## in_store_shopping_experience_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your in-store shopping experience. It collects data on various aspects of your visit, including the store ID, employee interaction, time of visit, products viewed or purchased, and overall satisfaction with the store and its staff. The goal is to gather feedback and insights to improve the shopping experience for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Intro**: Please provide a brief introduction to your shopping experience, including any relevant context or background information.
2. Fill out the **Store ID** field if it applies to your visit. This is not a required field.
3. Enter your **Employee ID** if you interacted with a specific employee during your visit. This is not a required field.
4. Select the **Time of Visit** for your visit, using the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
5. Choose the **Product ID** from the dropdown list of available options, selecting the product you interacted with or considered purchasing.
6. Choose the **Category ID** from the dropdown list of available options, selecting the category of the product you interacted with or considered purchasing. This is not a required field.
7. Answer **question1**, **question2**, **question3**, **question4**, **question5**, **question6**, **question7**, **question8**, **question9**, **question10**, **question11**, **question12**, and **question13** with your thoughts and comments about your shopping experience.
8. Provide feedback in the **question14** field with any additional comments or suggestions.
9. Enter your overall satisfaction ratings for the **Store Score**, **Employee Score**, **Product Score**, and **Overall Score** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro**: 
	+ (Required)
	+ Type: Text
	+ Explanation: A brief introduction to your shopping experience.
* **Store ID**: 
	+ (Optional)
	+ Type: Text
	+ Explanation: The ID of the store where you visited.
* **Employee ID**: 
	+ (Optional)
	+ Type: Text
	+ Explanation: The ID of the employee who interacted with you during your visit.
* **Time of Visit**: 
	+ (Required)
	+ Type: Date
	+ Explanation: The date and time of your visit.
* **Product ID**: 
	+ (Required)
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Explanation: The product you interacted with or considered purchasing.
* **Category ID**: 
	+ (Optional)
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Explanation: The category of the product you interacted with or considered purchasing.
* **question1**, **question2**, **question3**, **question4**, **question5**, **question6**, **question7**, **question8**, **question9**, **question10**, **question11**, **question12**, and **question13**:
	+ (Required)
	+ Type: Text
	+ Explanation: Open-ended questions to gather your thoughts and comments about your shopping experience.
* **question14**:
	+ (Optional)
	+ Type: Text
	+ Explanation: Additional comments or suggestions for the store.
* **Store Score**: 
	+ (Optional)
	+ Type: Text
	+ Explanation: Your overall satisfaction rating for the store.
* **Employee Score**: 
	+ (Optional)
	+ Type: Text
	+ Explanation: Your overall satisfaction rating for the employee.
* **Product Score**: 
	+ (Optional)
	+ Type: Text
	+ Explanation: Your overall satisfaction rating for the product.
* **Overall Score**: 
	+ (Optional)
	+ Type: Text
	+ Explanation: Your overall satisfaction rating for the entire shopping experience.
