# Investment Advice Quality Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Advice Quality Inquiry Form is designed to gather feedback from clients on the quality of investment advice they received. Please use this form to provide your honest opinion on the advice provided by our advisors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Company (if applicable)
	* Email (make sure it's valid)
	* Rating (Overall Satisfaction)
2. Choose the correct answers for the advisor's performance:
	* Advisor's Name
	* Advisor Rating
	* Additional Feedback for Advisor
3. Rate the service quality:
	* Overall Satisfaction
	* Service Quality
	* Communication
	* Professionalism
	* Value for Money
	* Clarity
	* Reliability
	* Responsiveness
	* Communication Style
4. Indicate if you would like to be followed up on:
	* Follow Up
	* Follow Up Date (if applicable)
	* Follow Up Time (if applicable)
5. Add any additional comments or feedback.
