# Equity And International Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about participants' demographics, experiences, and perceptions related to equity and international issues. The form is meant to be a starting point for further investigation and analysis of these topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Introduction" page, where you'll find a brief overview of the survey.
2. On the "Demographics" page, select your answer for each question:
	* Participant demographics: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your demographic status.
3. On the "Experience" page, select your answer for each question:
	* Participant experience: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your experience with equity and international issues.
4. On the "Perceptions" page, select your answer for each question:
	* Participant perceptions: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your perceptions about equity and international issues.
5. On the "Suggestions" page, type your thoughts and ideas in the text field. This is an optional field, so you don't have to fill it out if you don't have any suggestions.
6. Finally, on the "International" page, select your answer for each question:
	* International participation: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your international participation status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Page 1, note type):
	* This page provides a brief overview of the survey and its purpose.
* **Participant Demographics** (Page 2, select_multiple type):
	* This question asks you to select your demographics status for the participant.
	* Please choose "Yes" if you identify with this demographic, and "No" otherwise.
* **Participant Experience** (Page 3, select_one type):
	* This question asks you to select your experience with equity and international issues.
	* Please choose "Yes" if you have experience with this, and "No" otherwise.
* **Participant Perceptions** (Page 4, select_multiple type):
	* This question asks you to select your perceptions about equity and international issues.
	* Please choose "Yes" if you have these perceptions, and "No" otherwise.
* **Participant Suggestions** (Page 5, text type):
	* This is an optional field where you can type any suggestions you have about equity and international issues.
* **International** (Page 6, select_multiple type):
	* This question asks you to select your international participation status.
	* Please choose "Yes" if you have international participation, and "No" otherwise.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* You can save your progress and come back to the form later if you need to.
