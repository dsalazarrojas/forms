# 24 Hour Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants after a 24-hour experience event. Your input will help us evaluate and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, including all required fields.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as you can in the optional fields.
3. Use local currency and time formats when filling out optional fields (e.g., phone number, time).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (2, `Full name`, required): Enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Email address** (3, `Email address`, required): We may contact you for follow-up, so ensure this is a valid email address.
* **Phone number** (4, `Phone number`, optional): Include your phone number, including the country code (if applicable).
* **Attendance type** (5, `Attendance type`, required): Select one of the following:
	+ In-person
	+ Virtual
	+ Hybrid
* **Event date** (6, `Event date`, required): Enter the date you attended the 24-hour experience.
* **Session start time** (7, `Typical session start time you attended`, optional): Enter the local time the session started.
* **How satisfied were you with the 24-hour experience?** (9, `How satisfied were you with the 24-hour experience?`, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Overall rating** (10, `Overall rating`, required): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the worst and 10 being the best.
* **Organization** (11, `How well was the event organized?`, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below average
	+ Poor
* **Most valuable sessions** (12, `Which sessions did you find most valuable?`, optional): List the session titles, speakers, or topics that you found most valuable.
* **Topics for future events** (13, `Topics you want covered in future events (select all)`, optional): Select all the topics that interest you for future events:
	+ Leadership
	+ Technology
	+ Marketing
	+ Finance
	+ Wellness
	+ Networking
	+ Other
* **Would you attend again?** (14, `Would you attend this event again?`, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Definitely
	+ Probably
	+ Not sure
	+ Probably not
	+ False
* **Perceived value for cost** (15, `Perceived value for cost`, optional): Enter the approximate value you received for the experience in your local currency.
* **Facilities and logistics** (16, `Facilities and logistics rating`, optional): Rate your experience with facilities and logistics on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **Accessibility issues** (17, `Accessibility issues experienced`, optional): Select all the accessibility issues you experienced:
	+ Parking
	+ Venue signage
	+ Wheelchair access
	+ Hearing support
	+ Visual aids
	+ None
* **Networking opportunities** (18, `How would you rate networking opportunities?`, optional): Select one of the following:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Limited
	+ None
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (19, `Additional comments or suggestions`, optional): Share any specific suggestions, issues, or highlights about the 24-hour experience.
* **Consent to be contacted** (20, `Consent to be contacted for follow-up`, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Yes — contact me
	+ No — do not contact
	+ Maybe later
