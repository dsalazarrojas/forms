# smart_home_feature_value_survey - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select "Yes" or "No" for the first question about your interest in the feature.
2. On the second question, select a number to indicate how important the feature is to you.
3. For the third question, choose one of the options to express your pricing expectations.
4. In the "Additional Comments" field, provide any additional thoughts or insights you have about the feature.
5. Enter your email address in the format "user@example.com" if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
6. Provide your phone number if you'd like to be reached about your feedback.
7. Select a date for when you first started using the feature.
8. Choose a time for when you usually use the feature.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are aware of the feature.
10. Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate how often you use the feature.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" to express your level of satisfaction with the feature.
12. In the "What do you expect from this feature?" field, provide a brief description of your expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How interested are you in the feature?** (`feature_interest`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your level of interest in the feature. Please select "Yes" or "No" from the provided options.
* **How important is the feature to you?** (`feature_importance`, number, required: false): This question measures how important the feature is to you. Select a number to express your level of importance.
* **What are your expectations on the pricing?** (`feature_pricing`, select_one, required: false): This question is about your expectations regarding the feature's pricing. Choose one of the options to express your thoughts.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): This field is for providing additional thoughts or insights about the feature.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address in the format "user@example.com" if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be reached about your feedback, provide your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): This field is for selecting the date when you first started using the feature.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Choose a time for when you usually use the feature.
* **Are you aware of this feature?** (`feature_awareness`, select_one, required: false): This question asks if you are aware of the feature. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **How often do you use the feature?** (`how_often_used`, select_one, required: false): This question measures how often you use the feature. Choose "Yes" or "No".
* **How satisfied are you with the feature?** (`how_satisfied`, select_one, required: false): This question is about your level of satisfaction with the feature. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **What do you expect from this feature?** (`feature_expectation`, text, required: false): This question is for expressing your expectations from the feature. Provide a brief description of your expectations.
