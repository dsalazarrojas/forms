# Meeting Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Evaluation Survey is a tool used to gather feedback and insights about a meeting. It helps to identify what worked well and what could be improved upon.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your honest feedback about the meeting.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.

* **Attendee Count** (`meeting_attendee_count`, number, not required): Please enter the number of attendees that attended the meeting.
* **Topic** (`meeting_topic`, text, not required): Please enter a brief description of the meeting topic.
