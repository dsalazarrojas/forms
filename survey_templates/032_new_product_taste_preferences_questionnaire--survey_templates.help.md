# New Product Taste Preferences Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your preferences for a new product, specifically your taste preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go to each page and fill out the questions according to your preferences. You can select one or multiple options for each question, depending on your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Preferences** (`product_preferences`, text, optional): Please tell us about your preferences for the new product. This is a free text field where you can provide any information you think is relevant.
* **Appearance** (`appearance`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like the new product to appear: Light, Dark, or None.
* **Color** (`color`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple colors you would like to see in the new product: Red, Blue, Green, None.
* **Scent** (`scent`, select_one, optional): Select how strong or mild you would like the scent of the new product to be: Strong, Mild, None.
* **Taste** (`taste`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like the new product to taste: Sweet, Bitter, Sour.
* **Texture** (`texture`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple textures you would like to see in the new product: Smooth, Rough, None.
* **Finish** (`finish`, select_one, optional): Select how long you would like the finish of the new product to be: Short, Long, None.
* **Packaging** (`packaging`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like the new product to be packaged: Bottle, Can, None.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Please enter a price range you would like to see for the new product.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Please enter a rating (out of 10) for the new product.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments about the new product.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`comments_2`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments about the new product.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Email Address 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number 2** (`phone_2`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Additional Comments 3** (`comments_3`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments about the new product.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your form.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, text, optional): Please confirm your submission.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, optional): Click this button to cancel your submission.
* **Back** (`back`, text, optional): Click this button to return to the previous question.
* **Save and Submit** (`submit_2`, text, optional): Click this button to save and submit your form.
* **Save** (`save`, text, optional): Click this button to save your form without submitting it.
* **Cancel and Quit** (`cancel_2`, text, optional): Click this button to cancel and quit the form.
* **Return to Previous Question** (`back_2`, text, optional): Click this button to return to the previous question.
