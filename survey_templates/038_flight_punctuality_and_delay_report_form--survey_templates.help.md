# Flight Punctuality And Delay Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flight Punctuality And Delay Report Form is a tool to collect information about flight schedules and their performance. Its primary goal is to gather data that can help analyze and improve flight schedules, identify potential issues, and make informed decisions to reduce delays and cancellations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Review the information carefully before submitting the form.
3. Ensure that you have permission to report this flight.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`flight_punctuality_and_delay_report_form`, note, optional): This field is an informational page that explains the purpose and usage of the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### Flight Number
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of the flight being reported.
 
### Flight Date
* **Flight Date** (`flight_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the flight took place.
 
### Flight Time
* **Flight Time** (`flight_time`, time, optional): Enter the scheduled flight time.
 
### Departure Airport
* **Departure Airport** (`departure_airport`, text, optional): Enter the airport of departure.
 
### Arrival Airport
* **Arrival Airport** (`arrival_airport`, text, optional): Enter the airport of arrival.
 
### Flight Delayed Time
* **Flight Delayed Time** (`flight_delayed_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the flight was delayed.
 
### Reason for Delay
* **Reason for Delay** (`reason_for_delay`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the delay. This could be due to weather, mechanical issues, or other factors.
 
### Flight Status
* **Flight Status** (`flight_status`, select_one, optional): Select whether the flight was active (operational) or inactive (canceled or diverted).
 
### Service Quality
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_multiple, optional): Select the service quality of the flight. Options are Good and Poor.
 
### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the flight.
 
### Submission Type
* **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, select_one, optional): Select whether this report is for submission or not.
