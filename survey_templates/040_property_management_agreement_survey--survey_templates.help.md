# Property Management Agreement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record the terms and conditions of a property management agreement, including property details, questions, instructions, tenant feedback, and other relevant information. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all parties involved are aware of their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Property Details**: Fill in the property name, address, and any other relevant details about the property.
2. **Questions**: Answer the questions related to the property management agreement.
3. **Instructions**: Provide any additional instructions or information about the property.
4. **Tenant Feedback**: Enter the tenant's feedback or comments about the property.
5. **Date**: Enter the date of the property management agreement.
6. **Time**: Enter the time of the property management agreement.
7. **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the property.
8. **Tenant Email**: Enter the tenant's email address.
9. **Tenant Phone**: Enter the tenant's phone number.
10. **Checkbox 1** and **Checkbox 2**: Select one of the options provided for each checkbox.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details**: This field is for entering the name, address, and any other details about the property.
* **Questions**: This field is for answering questions related to the property management agreement.
* **Instructions**: This field is for providing any additional instructions or information about the property.
* **Tenant Feedback**: This field is for entering the tenant's feedback or comments about the property. This field is required.
* **Date**: This field is for entering the date of the property management agreement. Although not required, it's essential to enter an accurate date to keep records up-to-date.
* **Time**: This field is for entering the time of the property management agreement. Although not required, it's a good practice to include the time for reference purposes.
* **Note**: This field is for entering any additional notes or comments about the property. Although not required, it's helpful to include any relevant information about the property.
* **Tenant Email**: This field is for entering the tenant's email address. This field is not required but is a useful way to stay in touch with the tenant.
* **Tenant Phone**: This field is for entering the tenant's phone number. This field is not required but is a good way to stay in touch with the tenant.
* **Checkbox 1** and **Checkbox 2**: These fields are for selecting one of the options provided. Please choose the option that best applies to the property management agreement.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the information entered for each field to avoid errors.
* If you have any doubts or questions about this form, please contact the property management team for assistance.
