# Team Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from team members on their experience working on a team. It's a simple and concise tool to gather insights from team members and improve team collaboration and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* team_member_name: Enter the name of the team member you're providing feedback for.
2. Answer the questions about the team member:
	* Communication: How would you rate the team member's communication style?
	* time_management: How would you rate the team member's time management skills?
	* skills: What skills do you think the team member is strongest in? (Problem solving, Teamwork, or Time management)
3. Rate the team lead:
	* team_lead_name: Enter the name of the team lead.
	* lead_feedback: How would you rate the team lead's performance? (Excellent, Average, or Needs improvement)
4. Additional comments:
	* additional_comments: Any additional comments or suggestions about the team member or team lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_member_name** (text): Enter the name of the team member you're providing feedback for.
* **Communication** (select_multiple): How would you rate the team member's communication style? (Open and inclusive, Respectful, Helpful)
* **time_management** (select_multiple): How would you rate the team member's time management skills? (On time, Late, Early)
* **skills** (select_one): What skills do you think the team member is strongest in? (Problem solving, Teamwork, Time management)
* **would_continue** (select_multiple): Would you continue to work with this team member? (True, False)
* **lead_feedback** (select_multiple): How would you rate the team lead's performance? (Excellent, Average, Needs improvement)
* **team_lead_name** (text): Enter the name of the team lead.
* **additional_comments** (text): Any additional comments or suggestions about the team member or team lead.

Note: All fields are optional, so feel free to fill in as much or as little information as you like. However, be aware that some fields are required, as marked in the form.
