# Gym Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Reopening Survey is designed to gather feedback and insights from users to help the gym management make informed decisions on how to effectively reopen and cater to their customers' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the form's purpose and sections.
2. Answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Choose the relevant options for each field as instructed.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your thoughts** (`What are your thoughts`, `note`, required: false): This field is a text box for you to share your thoughts and opinions about the gym's reopening plans. You can write as much or as little as you like.
* **Our business** (`Our business`, `number`, required: false): This field is asking you to provide any specific services or amenities that you think the gym should offer upon reopening. Please select from the options provided or type in your own suggestion.
* **Reopening plans** (`Reopening plans`, `text`, required: false): In this field, describe your expectations for the gym's reopening plans. Be as detailed as possible about what you would like to see changed or improved.
* **Facilities** (`Facilities`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options that best describes the facilities you would like the gym to have upon reopening. This could be a Gym, Pool, Sauna, Tennis court, Swimming pool, Playground, or Fitness classes.
* **Fitness classes** (`Fitness classes`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose multiple options from the provided list of fitness classes that you would like to see offered upon the gym's reopening. Select from Yoga, Weightlifting, Cardio, Dance, Golf, or Martial arts.
* **Services** (`Services`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to suggest any additional services you would like the gym to offer upon reopening. You can type in as much or as little as you like.
* **Amenities** (`Amenities`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options that best describes the amenities you would like the gym to have upon reopening. Choose from Free Wi-Fi, Parking, Snack bar, Restaurant, Locker room, or Shower.
* **Contact** (`Contact`, `email`, required: false): Provide your email address if you would like to be contacted about this survey.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about this survey.
* **Follow up** (`Follow up`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the options provided to indicate when you would like to be followed up with about this survey. Choose from Follow up with you about this survey prophets or Follow up with you about this survey.
* **Survey time** (`Survey time`, `time`, required: false): This field is for you to indicate how long you took to complete this survey.
* **Survey date** (`Survey date`, `date`, required: false): This field is for you to indicate the date you completed this survey.

Note: Some fields are optional, but providing accurate and honest answers will help the gym management make informed decisions.
