# Community Voting Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Voting Poll form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from community members on a specific proposal or issue. This form helps gather data on community views, allowing for informed decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your Full Name and Email Address, which are required fields.
2. If you have a Voter ID or Registration Number, please provide it, but it's not mandatory.
3. Choose a proposal or topic for the poll from the options listed.
4. Select a proposal title and provide a brief description of the proposal being voted on.
5. Choose how you feel about the proposal by selecting one of the options.
6. Rank the priority level of the issue, from Top Priority to Not a Priority.
7. Provide a brief explanation for your vote, if desired.
8. If you have an alternative suggestion, feel free to provide it.
9. Indicate if you would be willing to help implement the proposal.
10. Select the expected community impact of the proposal.
11. Finally, provide any additional comments or thoughts about the proposal.
