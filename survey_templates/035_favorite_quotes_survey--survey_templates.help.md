# Favorite Quotes Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your favorite quotes, including their authors, the context in which you came across them, and your personal experiences with them. The data collected will help us understand your preferences and behaviors related to inspirational quotes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the first page of the form.
2. In the "What is your favorite quote?" field, enter your favorite quote.
3. In the "Who is the author of your favorite quote?" field, enter the name of the quote's author.
4. In the "When did you come across this quote?" field, select the date on which you first encountered the quote.
5. In the "Why is this quote meaningful to you?" field, explain the significance of the quote in your life.
6. If you'd like to, list up to three more quotes that are similar to your favorite quote in the "List 3 more quotes that are similar to this one" field.
7. Select "True" or "False" to answer "Do you think this quote has the potential to inspire others?".
8. In the "Can you explain why you think this quote is inspiring?" field, provide a brief explanation of why you believe the quote has the potential to inspire others.
9. Rate the frequency with which you come across quotes like this one using the "How often do you come across quotes like this one?" field.
10. Select all applicable options for "Have you ever shared this quote with someone else?"
11. Choose the tone of the quote from the options provided in "What is the tone of the quote?".
12. Rate the impact of the quote on you using the "Rate the impact of this quote on you?" field.
13. Categorize the quote into one or more categories from the options provided in "Categorize the quote (e.g., inspirational, motivational, humorous)?".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your favorite quote?**: This is the main field where you enter your favorite quote.
* **Who is the author of your favorite quote?**: Enter the name of the author of your favorite quote.
* **When did you come across this quote?**: Select the date on which you first encountered the quote.
* **Why is this quote meaningful to you?**: Explain the significance of the quote in your life.
* **List 3 more quotes that are similar to this one**: If you'd like to, list up to three more quotes that are similar to your favorite quote. 
* **Do you think this quote has the potential to inspire others?**: Select "True" or "False" to answer this question.
* **Can you explain why you think this quote is inspiring?**: Provide a brief explanation of why you believe the quote has the potential to inspire others.
* **How often do you come across quotes like this one?**: Rate the frequency with which you come across quotes like this one using the provided scale.
* **Have you ever shared this quote with someone else?**: Select all applicable options for this question.
* **What is the tone of the quote?**: Choose the tone of the quote from the provided options.
* **Rate the impact of this quote on you?**: Rate the impact of the quote on you using the provided scale.
* **Categorize the quote (e.g., inspirational, motivational, humorous)?**: Categorize the quote into one or more categories from the provided options.
