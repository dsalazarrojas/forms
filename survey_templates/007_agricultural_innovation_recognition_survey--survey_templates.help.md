# Agricultural Innovation Recognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on new products, processes, or sustainable practices in the agricultural industry. It aims to assess the impact, scalability, and environmental sustainability of these innovations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide nominator information**: Fill in your full name, the name of your organization, and your contact email.
2. **Choose a category**: Select the primary area of innovation from the options provided (Biotechnology, Machinery/Hardware, Digital/Software, Sustainable Practices, Supply Chain, or Processing).
3. **Describe the innovation**: Enter a detailed explanation of the innovation, including its title, problem it solves, and description.
4. **Rate sustainability**: Rate the environmental impact of the innovation using the Sustainability Rating scale.
5. **Assess scalability**: Choose the level of potential for widespread adoption (Local, Regional, National, or Global).
6. **Submit**: Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Name**: Your full name.
* **Organization**: The name of your company or institution.
* **Contact Email**: The email address where you can be contacted.
* **Innovation Title**: The name of the product or process.
* **Category**: The primary area of innovation (Biotechnology, Machinery/Hardware, Digital/Software, Sustainable Practices, Supply Chain, or Processing).
* **Problem Solved**: A description of the issue the innovation addresses.
* **Innovation Description**: A detailed explanation of how the innovation works.
* **Impact**: Measurable benefits or results of the innovation.
* **Sustainability Rating**: The environmental impact of the innovation (Low, Medium, High, or Transformative).
* **Scalability**: The potential for widespread adoption (Local, Regional, National, or Global).
* **Supporting Documents**: You can upload supporting documents to awards@ag-innovate.org, or simply provide the email address where they can be found.
