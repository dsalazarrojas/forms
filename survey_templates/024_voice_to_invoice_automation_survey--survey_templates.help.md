# Voice To Invoice Automation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voice To Invoice Automation Survey is designed to gather information about your current workflow and readiness for automation in handling invoices. This survey will help us understand how you handle invoices and identify potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select a page by clicking on the "Next" button at the bottom of the page.
2. Fill out the form fields for each page, using the options provided.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields, indicated by a *asterisk* (*).
4. Once you've completed all the pages, click "Submit" to send your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Invoice Workflows** (`1`): Describe your current workflow for handling invoices.
	+ Please provide a detailed explanation of your current process for handling invoices.
* **Is your team ready for automation?** (`2`): 
	+ Select "Yes" if your team has experience with automation and is ready to adopt new tools and processes.
	+ Select "No" if your team is not ready or needs more information before adopting automation.
* **Concerns about automation adoption** (`3`): 
	+ If you're not ready to adopt automation, please explain the reasons behind your concerns.
* **How do you see your team adopting automation in the future?** (`4`):
	+ Select all the ways in which your team might adopt automation in the future, for example "Yes" for one-time integrations or "Yes" for continuous integrations.
* **Current Process for Handling Invoices** (`5`):
	+ Select "Yes" if you have a manual process for handling invoices.
	+ Select "No" if you don't have a process for handling invoices.
* **Invoice Management** (`6`):
	+ Select all the ways in which you currently manage your invoices, for example "Yes" for manual tracking or "Yes" for automated tracking.
* **Data Sensitivity** (`7`):
	+ Select "Yes" if you will be sharing sensitive data about your company's invoicing practices.
	+ Select "No" if you won't be sharing sensitive data.
* **Company Size** (`8`):
	+ Select the size of your company, with "Yes" indicating your company is of that size. 

Note: The size is likely to be the number of employees in your company.
