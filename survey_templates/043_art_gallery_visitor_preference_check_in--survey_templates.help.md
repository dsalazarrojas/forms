# Art Gallery Visitor Preference Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from art gallery visitors to better understand their preferences and interests. The data collected will help the art gallery tailor its offerings and communications to suit the needs and tastes of its visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the welcome message and providing your name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Select your preferred art mediums and styles.
4. Indicate how often you visit art galleries.
5. Choose how you would like to be contacted.
6. Optionally, enter your zip code if you wish to receive special offers or updates.
7. Select a date for your check-in.
8. Finally, add any additional notes you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`visitor_name`, text, required): Please enter your name for our records.
* **Email Address** (`visitor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can send you updates and invitations.
* **Preferred Art Mediums** (`preferred_mediums`, select_multiple, required): Select all the art mediums that interest you, such as painting, photography, sculpture, digital art, or sketching.
* **Which styles resonate with you?** (`artistic_styles`, select_multiple, required): Choose the art styles that you enjoy, such as abstract, impressionism, surrealism, pop art, or classical realism.
* **How often do you visit art galleries?** (`frequency_of_visits`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you visit art galleries, whether it's your first time, once or twice a year, every few months, monthly, or if you're a professional.
* **Would you like to receive invitations to VIP exhibition openings?** (`receive_opening_invites`, select_one, required): Choose whether you'd like to receive invitations to special events and exhibitions.
* **Are you interested in participating in art workshops?** (`workshop_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate if you're interested in attending art workshops or not.
* **How would you like us to contact you?** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select how you'd like us to contact you, whether via email, SMS, WhatsApp, or not at all.
* **Zip - Postal Code** (`visitor_zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code if you'd like to receive special offers or updates from us.
* **Check-in Date** (`check_in_timestamp`, date, required): Select a date for your check-in.
* **Anything else you want us to know?** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when selecting your preferred art mediums and styles.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
