# Introductory Questionnaire Sheet For Graphics - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a graphic or visual aid used in a presentation or document. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the graphic, such as its title, description, and any relevant contact information for the person who created or commissioned it. This information will help us better understand the context and requirements of the graphic, ensuring that it is used effectively in our work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully and fill in all required fields.
2. If a field is optional, provide as much detail as you can if you feel it is relevant.
3. Use the provided options for select fields to choose the most accurate answer.
4. Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **introduction** (Page 1): This is a note field where you can provide any general information about the graphic.
- **questionaire_survey_form** (Page 2): Enter a title for the graphic, which will be displayed with it.
- **requesting_assistance_with_graphics** (Page 3): Describe the graphic, including its purpose and any relevant context.
- **this_is_an_important_section** (Page 4): Select all that apply from the options provided. This field is for important information about the graphic.
- **email** (Page 5): Enter your email address to contact you about the graphic.
- **phone** (Page 6): Enter your phone number for further contact.
- **this_is_optional** (Page 7): If you have any further information to provide, select one of the options that best applies.
- **date** (Page 8): Enter the date the graphic was created or is relevant to.
- **time** (Page 9): Enter the time the graphic was created or is relevant to.
- **more_information** (Page 10): Provide any additional information that is not covered in other fields.
- **please_provide_more_details** (Page 11): Enter any additional information about the graphic.
- **please_provide_more_details_2** (Page 12): Enter any further information about the graphic.
- **this_is_an_other_section** (Page 13-20): These fields are optional and can provide any additional information not covered in other fields.
- **more_details_3** to **more_details_14** (Page 21-25): These fields are also optional and can provide any additional information not covered in other fields.
