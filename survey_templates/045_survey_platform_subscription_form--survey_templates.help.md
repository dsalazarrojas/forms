# Survey Platform Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survey Platform Subscription Form is designed to collect information from users about their subscription to the survey platform. This form helps us understand the user's preferences and categorize them accordingly. It is intended for new users who have not yet subscribed to the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Answer all required questions truthfully and accurately.
3. If you need help or have questions, contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Platform Subscription** (`survey_platform_subscription_form_1`, text, required: false): This is the main field for you to enter your subscription information. Please type your answer in the text field provided.

* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: true): Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes: You are interested in a specific category.
	+ No: You are not interested in a specific category.

* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): This field is optional, but it's a good opportunity to provide more details about your subscription preferences.

* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: true): Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes: You are interested in being assigned a tool.
	+ No: You are not interested in being assigned a tool.

## Tips

* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure correct categorization.
* If you are already subscribed to the platform, this form is not intended for you.
