# Customer Digital Banking Trust Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect customer feedback about their level of trust in various aspects of digital banking services. The information collected will help us improve the services to better meet customer needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the "Start Form" button to begin.
2. Each section represents a separate question or prompt.
3. Complete the required questions, which are indicated by an asterisk (*) symbol.
4. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
5. When finished, click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_1** (Text, Optional): Please provide any comments or thoughts about your experience with digital banking services.
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_2** (Text, Optional): What do you think is missing from your digital banking experience?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_3** (Text, Optional): Have you encountered any issues with digital banking services in the past?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_4** (Select Multiple, Required): Which of the following aspects of digital banking services do you trust the most? (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3)
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_5** (Date, Optional): When did you first use digital banking services?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_6** (Time, Optional): What time of day do you usually use digital banking services?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_7** (Number, Optional): On a scale of 1-10, how satisfied are you with digital banking services?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_8** (Number, Optional): How many times have you used digital banking services in the past week?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_9** (Select One, Required): Which of the following features do you trust the most? (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3)
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_10** (Text, Optional): Please provide any additional feedback about digital banking services.
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_11** (Text, Optional): Do you have any suggestions for improving digital banking services?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_12** (Text, Optional): Have you used any other banking services besides digital banking?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_13** (Text, Optional): How do you usually access digital banking services? (e.g., mobile app, website, etc.)
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_14** (Text, Optional): What do you think is the most important feature of digital banking services?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_15** (Text, Optional): Are there any features that you would like to see added to digital banking services?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_16** (Text, Optional): Are there any features that you would like to see removed from digital banking services?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_17** (Text, Optional): Are there any issues that you have encountered while using digital banking services that you have not reported before?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_18** (Text, Optional): Do you have any other comments or suggestions about digital banking services?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_19** (Text, Optional): Are you satisfied with the overall experience of using digital banking services?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_20** (Text, Optional): Do you think digital banking services are convenient?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_21** (Text, Optional): Are you able to easily navigate digital banking services?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_22** (Text, Optional): Do you think digital banking services are secure?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_23** (Text, Optional): Do you think digital banking services are reliable?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_24** (Text, Optional): Do you think digital banking services are easy to use?
* **customer_digital_banking_trust_poll_form_25** (Text, Optional): Are you likely to continue using digital banking services?

## Tips
