> # Academic Advising Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation

> 
This form is designed to assess your satisfaction with academic advising services. The purpose is to gather feedback from students about their experiences with their advisors, aiming to improve the quality of advising services. Your responses will be kept confidential and used to inform future improvements. Before starting, please ensure you have completed this form for the correct academic year and have not already submitted a similar survey.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please select the corresponding answer options for each question.
2.  If a question is optional, feel free to skip it.
3.  Answer each question based on your actual experience and feelings.
4.  Please be honest and concise in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student name** (2): This field is used for you to provide your name.
    *   If you prefer not to disclose your name, you can leave it blank.
*   **Student email** (3): This field is used for you to provide your email address.
    *   If you don't have a university email or prefer not to disclose it, you can leave it blank.
*   **Current academic year** (4): This field allows you to select your current academic year.
    *   Please choose the corresponding option that best describes your current academic standing (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, or Graduate).
*   **Major or program** (5): This field is for you to enter your major or academic program.
    *   Please type your actual major or academic program.
*   **How many times did you meet with your advisor this semester** (7): This question is about your advising experience.
    *   Select the number of times you met with your advisor in the current semester.
*   **How easy was it to schedule an advising appointment** (8): This question is about your experience with scheduling advising appointments.
    *   Choose how easy or difficult it was to schedule an appointment with your advisor.
*   **My advisor was available when I needed help** (9): This question is about your advisor's availability.
    *   Select your level of agreement with the statement.
*   **My advisor was knowledgeable about degree requirements and course options** (11): This question is about your advisor's knowledge.
    *   Choose how much you agree with the statement about your advisor's knowledge.
*   **My advisor listened to my concerns and questions** (12): This question is about your advisor's listening skills.
    *   Select your level of agreement with the statement about your advisor's listening skills.
*   **The advising I received helped me make informed academic decisions** (13): This question is about the effectiveness of the advising.
    *   Choose how much you agree with the statement about the advising's impact on your academic decisions.
*   **My advisor provided useful career or post-graduation guidance** (14): This question is about career guidance.
    *   Select your level of agreement with the statement about the career guidance received.
*   **Overall, how satisfied are you with your academic advising experience** (15): This question is about your overall satisfaction.
    *   Choose the best description of your overall satisfaction.
*   **Would you recommend your advisor to other students** (16): This question is about recommending your advisor.
    *   Select your level of agreement with the statement about recommending your advisor.
*   **What did you find most helpful about your advising experience** (17): This question is about your positive experiences.
    *   Type the aspect that you found most helpful.
*   **What could be improved about the advising process** (18): This question is about areas for improvement.
    *   Type your suggestions for improvement.
