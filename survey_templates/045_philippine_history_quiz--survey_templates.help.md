# philippine_history_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their knowledge and interests related to the Philippines' history. The form will use the data collected to create a quiz or assessment about the country's past.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that you have not already submitted the form.
2. Review the fields below and fill them out according to your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction 
- **Introduction** (`title`, text, optional): This is a brief introduction page that allows you to provide a short description of your background and interests. 

### Category 
- **Native Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Please select the category that best describes your native category.

### Age 
- **Age** (`age`, number, required): Please enter your age. This will help us determine your eligibility for certain questions.

### Historical Periods 
- **Favorite Historical Periods** (`historical_periods`, select_multiple, required): Select the historical periods that interest you. Please choose as many or as few as you like.

### Favorite Event 
- **Favorite Historical Event** (`favorite_event`, select_one, required): Please select the historical event that interests you the most.

### Birthdate 
- **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, required): Please enter your birthdate in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).

### Goals 
- **Short-term Goals** (`goals`, select_multiple, required): Select the short-term goals that interest you. Please choose as many or as few as you like.

### Email 
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

### Phone 
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

### Submit 
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Please confirm that you have filled out the form correctly before submitting it.

Note: Please ensure that you have filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.
