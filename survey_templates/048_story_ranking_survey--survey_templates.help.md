# Story Ranking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Story Ranking Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about a story, gathering information on various aspects such as story title, ranking, rating, and demographic details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required fields, such as story title, ranking, and rating.
2. Answer questions about your demographics, including age, gender, education, occupation, income, location, and story length preferences.
3. Provide any comments or feedback you may have about the story.
4. Fill in your email address to allow us to contact you for further discussion, if necessary.
5. Select your preferred time of day and date of birth for our records.
6. Finally, review and submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Story Title (`story_title`, text, required)**: Enter a brief description of the story you are ranking.
* **How would you rank the story? (`ranking`, select_one, optional)**: Choose how you would rank the story on a scale from 1 to 3.
* **How would you rate the story? (`rating`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select multiple options to rate the story on various aspects.
* **Do you have feedback for the author? (`feedback`, note, optional)**: Share any thoughts or comments you may have about the story.
* **Your Email (`email`, email, optional)**: Enter your email address for further discussion, if needed.
* **Your Phone (`phone`, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for further discussion, if needed.
* **Date (`date`, date, optional)**: Enter your date of birth for our records.
* **Time (`time`, time, optional)**: Select your preferred time of day for our records.
* **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, optional)**: Enter your date of birth for our records.
* **Time of Day (`time_of_day`, time, optional)**: Select your preferred time of day for our records.
* **Comments (`comments`, text, optional)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Score (`score`, number, optional)**: Enter a numerical score for the story.
* **Average Score (`score_average`, number, optional)**: Enter the average score for the story.
* **Score Count (`score_count`, number, optional)**: Enter the count of scores provided.
* **How long would you like the story? (`story_length`, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose your preferred story length.
* **Story Type (`story_type`, select_one, optional)**: Select the type of story you are ranking.
* **Target Audience (`target_audience`, select_one, optional)**: Choose the target audience for the story.
* **Age (`age`, select_one, optional)**: Select your age group.
* **Gender (`gender`, select_one, optional)**: Select your gender.
* **Education (`education`, select_one, optional)**: Select your level of education.
* **Occupation (`occupation`, select_one, optional)**: Select your occupation.
* **Income (`income`, select_one, optional)**: Select your income level.
* **Location (`location`, select_one, optional)**: Select your location.
* **Comments 2 (`comments_2`, text, optional)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Comments 3 (`comments_3`, text, optional)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
