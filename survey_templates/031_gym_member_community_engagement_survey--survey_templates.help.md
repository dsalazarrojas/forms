# Gym Member Community Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your level of engagement with your gym community. It's meant to be a quick and easy way to provide us with insights on how we can improve our services and events for you.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
- You can select multiple options for questions that allow multiple answers.
- Be as specific as possible when answering questions that allow for text input.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask!

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gym Member Community Engagement Survey** (Text input, optional)
    This is the title of the form, which you can fill in as you see fit.
* **Event Interest** (Select multiple, optional)
    Please select all the events that interest you. This will help us understand what types of events you'd like to see more of.
* **Communication Frequency** (Select one, optional)
    How often do you like to receive communication from us? Select the frequency that best describes your preference.
* **Event Participation** (Select multiple, optional)
    How often do you participate in our events? This will help us understand how we can better cater to your needs.
* **Communication Channels** (Select multiple, optional)
    Which communication channels do you prefer to receive information from us? Please select all that apply.
* **Event Frequency** (Select one, optional)
    How often would you like to see events happening? This will help us tailor our events to your liking.
* **Feedback** (Text input, optional)
    If you have any suggestions or feedback about our gym or services, please let us know!
* **Suggestions** (Text input, optional)
    Do you have any other suggestions or ideas for us to improve our services or events?
