# Mentorship Program Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help participants in the mentorship program report their progress, challenges, and next steps, providing valuable feedback to their mentors and facilitating a more effective mentorship experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field and provide any additional comments or information as needed. You can choose from the provided options or type in your own responses. If you have any questions or need clarification on a field, feel free to ask your mentor or the form administrator for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Current Status** (`program_status`, `select_one`): Choose how your program is progressing: On Schedule, Behind Schedule, or At Risk.
- **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, `text`): Describe any challenges you have faced during the program.
- **Mentor Support** (`mentor_support`, `select_multiple`): Rate your mentor's support as Good, Average, or Bad.
- **Feedback From Mentor** (`feedback_from_mentor`, `text`): Share any feedback from your mentor.
- **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `select_multiple`): Choose the next steps you plan to take in the program.
- **Check In Frequency** (`check_in_frequency`, `select_one`): Report how often you expect to check in with your mentor: Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`): Add any additional comments or information about your progress.
- **Program Improvement** (`program_improvement`, `text`): Suggest any improvements for the program.
- **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, `text`): Add any other comments or information.
- **Next Program Steps** (`next_program_steps`, `select_multiple`): Choose the next steps you plan to take in the program.
- **Current Status (Again)** (`program_status_2`, `select_one`): Again, choose how your program is progressing: On Schedule, Behind Schedule, or At Risk.
- **Challenges Faced 2** (`challenges_faced_2`, `text`): Describe any challenges you have faced during the program.
- **Feedback From Mentor 2** (`feedback_from_mentor_2`, `text`): Share any feedback from your mentor.
- **Check In Frequency 2** (`check_in_frequency_2`, `select_one`): Report how often you expect to check in with your mentor: Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly.
- **Program Improvement 2** (`program_improvement_2`, `text`): Suggest any improvements for the program.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, but please provide any relevant information to give the most accurate picture of your progress and needs.
