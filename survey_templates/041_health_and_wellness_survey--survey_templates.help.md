# Health And Wellness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on your health and wellness. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible to help us better understand your health status and provide you with personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page and section carefully before answering.
2. For multiple-select questions, choose all that apply.
3. For true or false questions, select the correct answer.
4. For time fields, enter your answer in the format HH:MM.
5. For text fields, provide a brief description or answer.
