# Dispatch Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dispatch Availability Survey is a form designed to gather information about the availability of dispatchers for scheduling purposes. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that dispatchers' availability is correctly reflected in the scheduling process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the available options on the first page.
2. On the second page, enter your details as a dispatcher.
3. Choose your preferred shifts from the available options.
4. If you have any scheduling constraints, select "Yes" or "No" on the third page.
5. On the fourth page, select a date for your scheduling constraints.
6. On the final page, select a time for your scheduling constraints.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Page** (`first_page`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  * This page asks whether you are available for the following options: 
    * Yes
    * No
  * Select "Yes" if you are available for all options, or "No" if you are not available for any options.
- **Dispatcher Details** (`dispatcher_details`, `number`, required: false): 
  * This field is for entering your name and other details as a dispatcher.
  * Enter your name, and any other relevant information.
- **Preferred Shifts** (`preferred_shifts`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  * This field asks you to select your preferred shifts from the available options.
  * Choose your preferred shifts from the options provided.
- **Scheduling Constraints** (`scheduling_constraints`, `date`, required: false): 
  * This field asks you to select a date for any scheduling constraints.
  * Select a date that works for you, if you have any constraints.
- **Scheduling Constraints 2** (`scheduling_constraints_2`, `time`, required: false): 
  * This field asks you to select a time for any scheduling constraints.
  * Select a time that works for you, if you have any constraints.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out this form accurately to avoid any errors in the scheduling process.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your administrator for assistance.
- Ensure that this form is filled out only once per individual.
