# Gadget Prototype Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gadget Prototype Evaluation Form is a survey designed to gather feedback and evaluations from users on gadget prototypes. It helps the development team understand the user's perception of the prototype's usability, design, and overall rating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "user-name" field with your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
2. Enter your "user-email" in the format of your business email address.
3. In the "form-title" field, briefly describe the prototype you are evaluating.
4. Type a short description of the prototype in the "form-description" field.
5. Rate the overall performance of the prototype with a score from 1-5 in the "overall-rating" field.
6. For each feature rating field (e.g., "rating-1", "rating-2", etc.), select one or more of the ratings that best describe your experience with the prototype.
7. Provide feedback on your experience with the prototype in the "feedback" field.
8. If you spent time reviewing the prototype, enter the date and time in the "Submit" field.
9. Enter the time spent reviewing the prototype in the "time-spent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Questions** (`form_questions`, text, optional): This field is not used in this form, it's just a placeholder for potential future questions.
* **user-name** (`user_name`, text, required): This field is where you enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **user-email** (`user_email`, email, optional): This field is where you enter your business email address.
* **form-title** (`form_title`, text, optional): This field is where you enter a brief description of the prototype you are evaluating.
* **form-description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Type a short description of the prototype.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate the overall performance of the prototype with a score from 1-5.
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, select_one, optional): Select one or more ratings that best describe your experience with the prototype's feature 1.
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more ratings that best describe your experience with the prototype's feature 2.
* **Rating 3** (`rating_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more ratings that best describe your experience with the prototype's feature 3.
* **Rating 4** (`rating_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more ratings that best describe your experience with the prototype's feature 4.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide feedback on your experience with the prototype.
* **Submit** (`submit`, date, optional): If you spent time reviewing the prototype, enter the date and time in this field.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, time, optional): Enter the time spent reviewing the prototype.
