**Student Interest Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

The Student Interest Form is designed to collect information from students about their academic interests, extracurricular activities, career goals, hobbies, personal and student information, and emergency contact details. This form is meant to be completed by students to provide a snapshot of their interests, goals, and circumstances, which will be used for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the "Academic Interests" tab.
2. In the "Academic Interests" field, type your short-term and long-term academic goals, interests, or subjects you'd like to study.
3. Move to the "Extracurricular Activities" tab.
4. In the "Extracurricular Activities" field, describe your extracurricular activities, hobbies, or clubs you participate in.
5. Proceed to the "Career Goals" tab.
6. In the "Career Goals" field, specify your long-term career goals.
7. Move to the "Hobbies and Interests" tab.
8. In the "Hobbies and Interests" field, list your hobbies, interests, or activities outside of academics.
9. Move to the "Personal Info" tab.
10. In the "Personal Info" field, provide your personal details such as address, email, or any other relevant information.
11. Next, move to the "Student Info" tab.
12. In the "Student Info" field, fill in your student ID, school name, or other relevant student details.
13. Then, move to the "Parent Info" tab.
14. In the "Parent Info" field, provide your parent's contact information, such as name, phone number, or email.
15. Finally, move to the "Emergency Contact" tab.
16. In the "Emergency Contact" field, list the contact information of a person to contact in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Academic Interests** (`academic_interests`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your short-term and long-term academic goals, interests, or subjects you'd like to study.
* **Extracurricular Activities** (`extracurricular_activities`, text, required/optional): This field is used to describe your extracurricular activities, hobbies, or clubs you participate in.
* **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, text, required/optional): This field is used to specify your long-term career goals.
* **Hobbies and Interests** (`hobbies_and_interests`, text, required/optional): This field is used to list your hobbies, interests, or activities outside of academics.
* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide your personal details such as address, email, or any other relevant information.
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to fill in your student ID, school name, or other relevant student details.
* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide your parent's contact information, such as name, phone number, or email.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): This field is used to list the contact information of a person to contact in case of an emergency.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough when answering each question.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
* You can use this form to provide any additional information not covered in the questions.
* This form will help us better understand your interests and provide you with relevant information and services.
* Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
