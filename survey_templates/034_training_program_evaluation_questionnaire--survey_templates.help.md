# Training Program Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on a training program. It is used to evaluate the effectiveness, satisfaction, and overall experience of the training program. This evaluation helps the training team to identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments for future programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Provide the name of the training program that you participated in.
2. Answer the questions regarding the length of the program.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
4. Choose how satisfied you are with the program from the provided options.
5. Write any additional comments or feedback about the program.
6. Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further feedback.
7. Provide your phone number if you want to be contacted for further feedback.
8. Date and time you completed the survey.
9. If you are finished, click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the name of the training program?**: Please enter the name of the program you participated in. If you are unsure, refer to any materials provided or your memory.
* **How long was the training program?**: Please enter the length of time you spent on the program, in hours or days.
* **Rate the program**: This is a numerical rating of your overall satisfaction with the program, from 1 to 5. A higher number indicates a higher level of satisfaction.
* **How Satisfied Are You With The Program?**: Choose from the options below to express your level of satisfaction with the program:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Do You Have Any Additional Comments?**: Please use this text field to provide any feedback, suggestions, or concerns about the program.
* **Participant Email**: If you want to be contacted for further feedback or clarification, please enter your email address here.
* **Participant Phone**: If you want to be contacted for further feedback, please enter your phone number.
* **Date Completed**: Please enter the date and time you completed this survey.
* **Time Completed**: Please enter the time you completed this survey.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information helps us to better understand your experience.
