# Gamer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your gaming habits and preferences. We will use this information to improve our services and products for gamers like you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select "Game Developer Survey" on the first page.
2. If you are a game developer, please answer accordingly. If not, you can skip this page.
3. On the second page, describe your gaming habits, e.g., how often do you play games, which games do you play, etc.
4. On the third page, indicate whether you are a gamer or not.
5. On the fourth page, enter your email address so we can contact you about our services.
6. On the fifth and final page, enter your phone number (optional) and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Game Developer Survey (Page 1)
* **Game Developer Survey**: (`game_developer_survey`, text, optional): This is an optional question for game developers to provide information about their games. If you are not a game developer, you can skip this page.

### Gaming Habits (Page 2)
* **Gaming Habits**: (`gaming_habits`, text, optional): Describe your gaming habits, e.g., how often do you play games, which games do you play, etc.

### Gamer Info (Page 3)
* **Gamer Info**: (`gamer_info`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are a gamer or not. Choose 'Yes' if you play games regularly, and 'No' if you do not.

### Email (Page 4)
* **Email**: (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about our services. This will help us keep you informed about new developments and updates.

### Phone Number (Page 5)
* **Phone Number**: (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
