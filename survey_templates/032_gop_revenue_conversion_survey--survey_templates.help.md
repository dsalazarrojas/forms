# GOP Revenue Conversion Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The GOP Revenue Conversion Survey is designed to gather information from users about their opinions and experiences with GOP revenue conversion. This form helps collect data that can inform decisions and improve processes related to GOP revenue conversion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields, marked as 'true' in the form.
3. Answer each question based on your genuine experience and opinion.
4. Use the provided options to select your answer.
5. Proceed to the next section when you've completed the current one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please provide a short description of your experience or opinion on GOP revenue conversion.
* **Effectiveness** (`effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): This field asks whether you think the effectiveness of GOP revenue conversion is 'Yes' or 'No'. Please select one of the options based on your genuine experience.
* **GOP-Process** (`gop-process`, `select_one`, required): This field asks whether you think the current GOP process is 'Yes' or 'No'. Please select one of the options based on your genuine experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide about GOP revenue conversion. You can type as much or as little as you'd like.
* **GOP-Solutions** (`gop-solutions`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks whether you think the current GOP solutions are 'Yes' or 'No'. Please select one of the options based on your genuine experience.
* **Finance** (`finance`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks whether you think finance is a 'Yes' or 'No' concern in GOP revenue conversion. Please select one of the options based on your genuine experience.
* **GOP-Challenges** (`gop-challenges`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks whether you think GOP challenges are a 'Yes' or 'No' concern. Please select one of the options based on your genuine experience.
* **Operations** (`operations`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks whether you think operations are a 'Yes' or 'No' concern. Please select one of the options based on your genuine experience.

Note: The fields marked as 'optional' can be skipped, but please make sure to complete all 'required' fields to proceed.
