# data_utilization_capability_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "data_utilization_capability_survey" form is designed to help organizations assess their data utilization capabilities and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully to understand the purpose and scope of the form.
2. Select all the data sources your organization uses (multiple selection is allowed).
3. Identify the primary challenge your organization faces with data management (single selection is required).
4. Enter your organization's training priorities for data management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Data Sources** (Data Sources, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the data sources your organization uses, such as Data Warehouse, Data Lake, Data Mart, or other (e.g., spreadsheets, databases, etc.).
- **Challenges** (Challenges, Select One, Required): Identify the primary challenge your organization faces with data management. Options include Data Quality Issues, Data Security and Compliance, Lack of Resources, Lack of Technical Skills, or Other (please specify).
- **Training Priority** (Training Priority, Text, Required): Enter your organization's training priorities for data management. This is a free-text field where you can provide detailed information on your organization's training needs and goals.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough when completing this form, as the information gathered will help identify areas for improvement.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the form administrator for guidance.
- The information provided will be used to create a comprehensive report on your organization's data utilization capabilities.
