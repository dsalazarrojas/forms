# employee_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their overall satisfaction with their role in the organization and areas where they would like to see improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best fit your experience and opinions. You can select one or multiple options depending on the type of question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?** (`Very satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`, single): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your role. Choose the option that best reflects your feelings.
* **How would you rate communication from management?** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`, single): This question evaluates the communication you receive from management. Select the option that best represents your experience.
* **Do you feel supported in your professional development?** (`true`, `Mostly`, `Somewhat`, `Not really`, `false`, single): This question checks if you feel supported in your growth and development within the organization.
* **How would you describe your current work-life balance?** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`, single): This question assesses your current work-life balance. Choose the option that best represents your experience.
* **Are you satisfied with the opportunities for career growth?** (`Very satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`, single): This question evaluates opportunities for career growth. Select the option that best represents your feelings.
* **How valued do you feel by the company?** (`Highly valued`, `Valued`, `Neutral`, `Undervalued`, `Not valued`, single): This question asks about how valued you feel by the company.
* **How would you rate the level of collaboration within your team?** (`Always`, `Mostly`, `Sometimes`, `Rarely`, `Never`, single): This question assesses collaboration within your team. Choose the option that best represents your experience.
* **Which aspects of our company culture do you appreciate most? (Select all that apply)** (`Teamwork`, `Innovation`, `Flexibility`, `Inclusivity`, `Transparency`, `Recognition`, multiple): This question asks about the company culture aspects you appreciate. Select all that apply.
* **How satisfied are you with your compensation and benefits package?** (`Very satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`, single): This question evaluates your satisfaction with compensation and benefits.
* **Do you feel comfortable discussing issues with your direct manager?** (`Extremely likely`, `Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`, `Extremely unlikely`, single): This question assesses your comfort in discussing issues with your manager.
* **How likely are you to recommend this organization as a place to work?** (`Always`, `Usually`, `Sometimes`, `Rarely`, `Never`, single): This question evaluates your likelihood to recommend the organization as a place to work.
* **What is one thing that could significantly improve your daily work experience?** (text): This question asks about a specific improvement that could enhance your daily experience.
* **What motivates you to continue working at this company?** (text): This question evaluates the factors motivating you to stay at the company.
* **In what areas do you believe the company could improve upon? (Select all that apply)** (`Communication`, `Leadership`, `Workload`, `Benefits`, `Culture`, `Training`, multiple): This question asks about areas for improvement in the organization. Select all that apply.
* **Do you feel your ideas and suggestions are genuinely considered?** (`Strong`, `Moderate`, `Neutral`, `Weak`, `Non-existent`, single): This question evaluates the consideration of your ideas and suggestions.
* **How would you describe your overall sense of belonging at the company?** (single): This question assesses your overall sense of belonging at the company.
* **Any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to share?** (text): This question allows for any additional comments or suggestions.

## Tips
Remember to be honest and sincere in your answers. Your feedback will help the organization understand and improve your experience.
