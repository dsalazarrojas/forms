# exit_ticket - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exit_ticket form is designed to collect feedback and insights from students or trainees after a training session. This information will help trainers and educators evaluate the effectiveness of the training and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Input field with any relevant comments or thoughts about the training session.
2. Enter the Date Completed field with the date you completed the training session.
3. Provide any additional Comments about the training session.
4. Enter the Trainer Name of the person who conducted the training session.
5. Enter the Student Name of the person who participated in the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (User Input, text, optional):
  Provides a space for students or trainees to share their thoughts or comments about the training session.
- **Date Completed** (Date Completed, date, optional):
  Enter the date you completed the training session.
- **Comments** (Comments, text, optional):
  Enter any additional comments or insights you have about the training session.
- **Trainer Name** (Trainer Name, text, optional):
  Enter the name of the person who conducted the training session.
- **Student Name** (Student Name, text, optional):
  Enter your name as the student or trainee who participated in the training session.
