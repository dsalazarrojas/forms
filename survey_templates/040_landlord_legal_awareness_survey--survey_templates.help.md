# Landlord Legal Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Landlord Legal Awareness Survey is designed to assess your level of awareness about landlord-tenant laws and regulations. The survey is intended to help us better understand your knowledge of these laws and regulations, and to identify areas where additional education or support may be needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer to the best of your ability.
* For questions with multiple options, select all applicable answers.
* Take as much time as you need to complete the survey, but try to keep your answers concise and accurate.
* For questions requiring numerical answers, please enter numbers only.
* When asked to specify reasons or comments, type your response in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Legal Awareness Survey**: This is the title of the survey, which should be read and understood before proceeding.
* **Introduction**: This section is an introductory note, explaining the purpose of the survey and its importance.
* **Landlord Name**: Enter your full name as a landlord.
* **Property Address**: Enter your property address.
* **Are you aware of all applicable landlord-tenant laws and regulations?**: Answer "Yes" if you are aware of all relevant laws and regulations, or "No" if you are not.
* **How many years have you been a landlord?**: Enter the number of years you have been a landlord.
* **How often do you review the lease agreement with your tenant?**: Select how often you review the lease agreement, or choose "Never" if you never do.
* **What is the main reason you would ask a tenant to move out?**: Select all reasons that apply (Non-payment of rent, Damage to the property, Breach of lease, or Other (please specify)).
* **Have you ever had to deal with an eviction?**: Answer "Yes" if you have dealt with an eviction, or "No" if you have not.
* **What type of education or training would you find most helpful?**: Select all types of education or training that would be helpful to you (Landlord-tenant law, Property management, Real estate investing, or Other (please specify)).
* **How many properties do you currently manage?**: Enter the number of properties you currently manage.
* **What is the main reason you use a property manager?**: Select all reasons that apply (I need help with tenant relations, I need help with rent collection, I need help with property maintenance, or Other (please specify)).
* **Have you ever had any complaints from tenants?**: Answer "Yes" if you have had complaints from tenants, or "No" if you have not.
* **How do you typically handle complaints from tenants?**: Select all methods of handling complaints (Meet with them in person, Call or text them, Email or online portal, or Other (please specify)).
* **Do you have any upcoming events or activities planned for your tenants?**: Answer "Yes" if you have upcoming events or activities, or "No" if you do not.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for us?**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!**: This is a note of appreciation for your participation in the survey.
* **How long did it take you to complete this survey?**: Enter the number of minutes it took you to complete the survey.
* **How would you rate this survey?**: Select your rating of the survey (Very good, Good, Fair, or Bad).
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback for this survey?**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Is there anything else you would like to add?**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
