# study guide preparation survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about study guide preparation preferences from users. It is intended to be used by educators and instructors to better understand their students' needs and tailor their teaching methods accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
2. Select your preferred learning styles from the list.
3. Choose your preferred study format from the available options.
4. If you have any additional study challenges, please describe them in the "Study Challenges" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your school or institution where you currently study. This information will help us understand your context and learning environment.
* **Preferred Learning Styles** (`learning_styles`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the learning styles that you prefer. These may include Lecture, Textbook, Video, or Other. You can select multiple styles if needed.
* **Preferred Study Format** (`study_format`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you prefer to learn through. Your options are Textbook, Video, Online, or Other. Select only one option that best describes your preferred format.
* **Study Challenges** (`study_challenges`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional challenges that you face while studying, please describe them here. This will help us understand your specific needs and concerns.
