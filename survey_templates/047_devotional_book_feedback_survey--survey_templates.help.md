# Devotional Book Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Devotional Book Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from customers on a devotional book. This feedback will help the publishing team understand the overall rating and satisfaction of the book, as well as identify specific areas for improvement. By providing feedback, customers will be able to help shape the future of the devotional book and improve the reading experience for others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the rating scale for the book by choosing a number from 1 to 5.
2. Provide a brief overall rating for the book in the "Overall Rating" field.
3. Share any feedback or suggestions in the "Feedback" field.
4. Let us know if you would recommend any other books in the "Recommended Books" field. Select all books that you have read and liked.
5. If applicable, provide your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields for further communication.
6. If desired, enter a date and time when you finished reading the book.
7. Write any additional notes about your reading experience in the "Note" field.
8. Select the category that best fits the book's genre in the "Category" field. Choose from Fiction, Nonfiction, Romance, Mystery, Science Fiction, or Fantasy.
9. Finally, provide the name of the author of the book.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Rating** (book_rating): A number from 1 to 5 that represents your overall rating of the book.
* **Rating Scale** (rating_scale): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to rate the book.
* **Feedback** (feedback): A brief text field where you can provide any feedback or suggestions about the book.
* **Recommended Books** (recommended_books): A list of books you have read and liked, select all that apply.
* **Email** (email): If you want to be contacted for further communication, provide your email address here.
* **Phone** (phone): If you want to be contacted by phone, provide your phone number here.
* **Date** (date): The date you finished reading the book.
* **Time** (time): The time you finished reading the book.
* **Note** (note): Any additional notes about your reading experience.
* **Category** (category): Select the category that best fits the book's genre, choose from Fiction, Nonfiction, Romance, Mystery, Science Fiction, or Fantasy.
* **Author** (author): The name of the author of the book.
