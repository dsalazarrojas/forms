# Festival Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from festival attendees on their overall experience. Your input will help us improve future festivals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. 
2. Select the rating scale as instructed for each question.
3. If you have any ideas for improvement, provide them in the "Improvement Idea" field.
4. If you have a favorite part of the festival, describe it in the corresponding field (e.g., "Favorite Food").
5. If you have any suggestions or comments about the festival, they can be included in the "Improvement Idea" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Rating (Page 1)**: Rate your overall experience at the festival on a scale of 1-5.
* **Favorite Aspect (Page 1)**: Describe your favorite part of the festival. This could be a performance, an activity, or a food option.
* **Improvement Idea (Page 1)**: Suggest ways we can improve the festival for next year's event. Be as specific as possible.
* **Food and Beverage (Page 1)**: Rate the quality of the food and drinks offered at the festival on a scale of 1-5.
* **Entertainment (Page 1)**: Rate the quality of the performances and activities offered at the festival on a scale of 1-5.
* **Atmosphere and Decorations (Page 1)**: Rate the overall ambiance and visual appeal of the festival on a scale of 1-5.
* **Favorite Food (Page 1)**: Describe your favorite food or drink from the festival.
* **Favorite Performance (Page 1)**: Describe your favorite performance or activity from the festival.
* **Favorite Decorations (Page 1)**: Describe your favorite decorations or visual aspect of the festival.
* **Staff (Page 1)**: Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of the festival staff on a scale of 1-5.
