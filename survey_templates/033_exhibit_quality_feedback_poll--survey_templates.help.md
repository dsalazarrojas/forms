# Exhibit Quality Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users on the quality of an exhibit, allowing for the evaluation of its overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Exhibit Quality Feedback Poll Form**
	* This is the main section where you will provide feedback on the exhibit.
2. **Visit**
	* Enter the date of your visit to the exhibit.
3. **Rate**
	* Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your overall satisfaction with the exhibit.
4. **Comment**
	* Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about the exhibit.
5. **Contact us**
	* If you would like to get in touch with us regarding the exhibit, enter your email address.
6. **Visit duration (hours)**
	* Enter the duration of your visit to the exhibit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibit Quality Feedback Poll Form**: This is the main section where you will provide feedback on the exhibit.
* **Visit**: Please enter the date of your visit to the exhibit using the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Rate**: Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your overall satisfaction with the exhibit. A higher rating indicates a more positive experience.
* **Comment**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about the exhibit. This is optional, but provides valuable feedback for improvement.
* **Contact us**: If you would like to get in touch with us regarding the exhibit, enter your email address. This will allow us to follow up with you and discuss your feedback in more detail.
* **Visit duration (hours)**: Please enter the duration of your visit to the exhibit in hours. This helps us understand how engaging the exhibit was for you.

## Tips

* Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve the exhibit.
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields to ensure your feedback is complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us through the "Contact us" field.
