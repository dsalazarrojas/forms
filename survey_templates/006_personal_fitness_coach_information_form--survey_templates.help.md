# personal_fitness_coach_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from clients about their personal fitness goals, habits, and preferences to help personal fitness coaches or trainers understand their needs and tailor their services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number in the Client Information section.
2. Provide your Birth Date.
3. Choose how often you currently exercise in the Workout Frequency field.
4. Select your preferred workout time in the Preferred Workout Time field.
5. Choose your preferred workout duration in the Preferred Workout Duration field.
6. Select your primary fitness goal in the Goal Weight Loss, Goal Gain Muscle, Goal Improve Athletic Performance, Goal Enjoy Activities, Goal Enjoy Life, Goal Health, and Goal Wellness fields.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the Client Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: `false`): This section is used to collect basic client information such as First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number.
* **First Name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: `false`): This field is used to collect the client's first name.
* **Last Name** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: `false`): This field is used to collect the client's last name.
* **Email** (`id: 4`, `type: email`, required: `false`): This field is used to collect the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: `false`): This field is used to collect the client's phone number.
* **Birth Date** (`id: 6`, `type: date`, required: `false`): This field is used to collect the client's birth date.
* **Workout Frequency** (`id: 7`, `type: select_one`, required: `true`): This field is used to select how often the client currently exercises. Possible options include Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday, Daily, Occasionally, and Rarely.
* **Preferred Workout Time** (`id: 8`, `type: select_multiple`, required: `true`): This field is used to select the client's preferred workout time. Possible options include Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Anytime.
* **Preferred Workout Duration** (`id: 9`, `type: select_multiple`, required: `true`): This field is used to select the client's preferred workout duration. Possible options include 30-60 minutes, 60-90 minutes, 90-120 minutes, Less than 30 minutes, and More than 120 minutes.
* **Goal Weight Loss** (`id: 10`, `type: select_one`, required: `true`): This field is used to select the client's primary fitness goal related to weight loss.
* **Goal Gain Muscle** (`id: 11`, `type: select_one`, required: `true`): This field is used to select the client's primary fitness goal related to muscle gain.
* **Goal Improve Athletic Performance** (`id: 12`, `type: select_one`, required: `true`): This field is used to select the client's primary fitness goal related to improving athletic performance.
* **Goal Enjoy Activities** (`id: 13`, `type: select_one`, required: `true`): This field is used to select the client's primary fitness goal related to enjoying activities.
* **Goal Enjoy Life** (`id: 14`, `type: select_one`, required: `true`): This field is used to select the client's primary fitness goal related to enjoying life.
* **Goal Health** (`id: 15`, `type: select_one`, required: `true`): This field is used to select the client's primary fitness goal related to health.
* **Goal Wellness** (`id: 16`, `type: select_one`, required: `true`): This field is used to select the client's primary fitness goal related to wellness.
* **Client Notes** (`id: 18`, `type: note`, required: `false`): This field is used to collect any additional notes or comments from the client.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields accurately to provide the personal fitness coach or trainer with the necessary information.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, please use the Client Notes field.
* Please note that some fields might not be visible or accessible based on your selections in other fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or options, please ask your personal fitness coach or trainer for clarification.
