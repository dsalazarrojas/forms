# E Commerce UX Design Study Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in an e-commerce UX design study. By filling out the form, you will be helping us understand how you think about and interact with e-commerce platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the first field. This is the name that you go by.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the second field. This is the name of your family.
3. Enter your **Email** in the third field. This should be your primary email address that you check regularly.
4. Enter your **Phone** in the fourth field. This is a good contact method in case we need to reach you.
5. Enter your **Company** in the fifth field. This is your current or previous employer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (text, optional)**: Enter your name that you go by.
* **Last name (text, optional)**: Enter the name of your family.
* **Email (email, required)**: Enter your primary email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter a contact method in case we need to reach you.
* **Company (text, optional)**: Enter your current or previous employer.
