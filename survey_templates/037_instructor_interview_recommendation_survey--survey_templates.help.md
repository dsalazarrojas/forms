# Instructor Interview & Recommendation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Instructor Interview & Recommendation Survey form is designed to collect information from instructors about their experiences, recommendations, and evaluations. It's essential to understand that this form is a unique creation, generated using AI and public survey topic metadata, and its use should be responsible and not for malicious purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information and answers to the questions provided.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor Information** (`instructor_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for providing your instructor name, email, or any other relevant contact information.
* **Instructor Survey** (`instructor_survey`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for sharing your thoughts, experiences, or opinions about the instructor or the course.
* **School Information** (`school_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for providing information about the school or institution where you are affiliated with.
* **Training Program Information** (`training_program_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for sharing details about the training program or course you are involved with.
* **Instructor Recommendation** (`instructor_recommendation`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for providing recommendations or suggestions for instructors or training programs.
* **Instructor Evaluation** (`instructor_evaluation`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for evaluating or rating your experience with the instructor or training program.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a submit button to finalize your answers and submit the form.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Use clear and concise language in your answers.
- If you're unsure about any field, it's okay to ask for clarification or assistance.
