# Carbon Emissions Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help organizations collect and report their carbon emissions data in order to conduct a carbon audit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering your organization's details:
	* Organization name: Enter the name of your organization.
	* Organization size: Select the size of your organization from the provided options.
	* Industry sector: Choose the industry sector your organization operates in.
2. Provide audit details:
	* Audit date: Enter the date of the audit.
	* Audit scope: Select the scope of the audit from the provided options.
3. Report emissions data:
	* Stationary combustion (boilers, furnaces) - mtCO2e: Enter the amount of emissions from stationary combustion sources.
	* Mobile combustion (fleet vehicles) - mtCO2e: Enter the amount of emissions from mobile combustion sources.
	* Refrigerant/air conditioning emissions - mtCO2e: Enter the amount of emissions from refrigerant/air conditioning sources.
	* Chemical processes or manufacturing - mtCO2e: Enter the amount of emissions from chemical processes or manufacturing sources.
	* Purchased electricity - mtCO2e: Enter the amount of emissions from purchased electricity.
	* Purchased steam/heating - mtCO2e: Enter the amount of emissions from purchased steam/heating.
	* Electricity source: Select the source of electricity used by your organization.
4. Calculate and report total emissions:
	* Total estimated emissions (mtCO2e): Calculate and enter the total amount of emissions.
5. Review and provide final details:
	* Emissions intensity (mtCO2e per employee): Calculate and enter the emissions intensity.
	* Recommended emissions reduction strategies: Provide any recommended strategies for reducing emissions.
	* Auditor notes and observations: Enter any additional notes or observations from the auditor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Organization size** (`organization_size`, `select_one`, required): Select the size of your organization from the provided options.
* **Industry sector** (`industry_sector`, `select_one`, required): Choose the industry sector your organization operates in.

* **Audit scope** (`audit_scope`, `select_one`, required): Select the scope of the audit from the provided options.
* **Stationary combustion (boilers, furnaces) - mtCO2e** (`stationary_combustion`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of emissions from stationary combustion sources.
* **Mobile combustion (fleet vehicles) - mtCO2e** (`mobile_combustion`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of emissions from mobile combustion sources.
* **Refrigerant/air conditioning emissions - mtCO2e** (`refrigerant_emissions`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of emissions from refrigerant/air conditioning sources.
* **Chemical processes or manufacturing - mtCO2e** (`chemical_processes`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of emissions from chemical processes or manufacturing sources.
* **Purchased electricity - mtCO2e** (`purchased_electricity`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of emissions from purchased electricity.
* **Purchased steam/heating - mtCO2e** (`purchased_steam`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of emissions from purchased steam/heating.
* **Electricity source** (`electricity_source`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the source of electricity used by your organization.
* **Total estimated emissions (mtCO2e)** (`total_emissions`, `number`, required): Calculate and enter the total amount of emissions.
* **Emissions intensity (mtCO2e per employee)** (`emissions_intensity`, `number`, required): Calculate and enter the emissions intensity.
