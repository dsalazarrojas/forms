# marketing_personality_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "marketing_personality_quiz" form is designed to help marketers and businesses understand their marketing personality traits, customer behavior, and other related metrics. This form collects data on various aspects of marketing, including customer preferences, behavior, and satisfaction. The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive analysis of marketing personality and customer metrics, which can be used to develop targeted marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reading through the form carefully and understanding the purpose of each section.
2. Answer the questions honestly and accurately, based on your knowledge and experience.
3. Select the options that best fit your answers for each field.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **intro_question** (`intro_question`, text, required: false): This field is used to introduce yourself and provide some background information about your marketing role or experience.
* **marketing_preferences** (`marketing_preferences`, select_one, required: false): Here, you can answer whether you prefer marketing strategies focused on product, brand, or customer satisfaction.
* **customer_behavior** (`customer_behavior`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the customer behaviors that apply to you, such as customer engagement, lead generation, and lead conversion.
* **lead_generation** (`lead_generation`, select_one, required: false): Answer whether you have a lead generation strategy, and which specific channels you use for this purpose.
* **tailored_strategies** (`tailored_strategies`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the marketing strategies that apply to you, such as customer targeting, channel mix, and content creation.
* **marketing_channels** (`marketing_channels`, select_one, required: false): Choose the marketing channels you use for your marketing efforts.
* **customer_engagement** (`customer_engagement`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the customer engagement activities that you perform, such as social media, email marketing, and customer service.
* **target_audience** (`target_audience`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the target audience groups that you cater to, such as demographics, preferences, and behaviors.
* **marketing_strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the marketing strategies that apply to you, such as brand awareness, lead generation, and customer conversion.
* **customer_satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the customer satisfaction metrics that you track, such as customer retention, satisfaction, and engagement.
* **customer_value_proposition** (`customer_value_proposition`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the customer value propositions that apply to you, such as product value, customer experience, and brand value.
* **marketing_personality** (`marketing_personality`, text, required: false): Write a brief description of your marketing personality and approach.
* **lead_gen_channels** (`lead_gen_channels`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the lead generation channels that you use, such as email, social media, and content marketing.
* **target_marketing_channels** (`target_marketing_channels`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the marketing channels that you target, such as demographics, firmographic, and firmographic data.
* **marketing_strategy_channels** (`marketing_strategy_channels`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the marketing strategy channels that apply to you, such as lead generation, channel mix, and content creation.
* **marketing_channels_used** (`marketing_channels_used`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the marketing channels that you use, such as email, social media, and content marketing.
* **customer_value_proposition** (`customer_value_proposition`, text, required: false): Write a brief description of your customer value proposition.
* **customer_satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the customer satisfaction metrics that you track, such as customer retention, satisfaction, and engagement.
* **customer_personality** (`customer_personality`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the customer personality traits that apply to you, such as customer behavior, preferences, and demographics.
* **marketing_personality_score** (`marketing_personality_score`, number, required: false): Enter a score for your marketing personality, based on your answers.
* **customer_value_score** (`customer_value_score`, number, required: false): Enter a score for your customer value, based on your answers.
* **customer_personality_score** (`customer_personality_score`, number, required: false): Enter a score for your customer personality, based on your answers.
* **customer_engagement_score** (`customer_engagement_score`, number, required: false): Enter a score for your customer engagement, based on your answers.
* **marketing_strategy_score** (`marketing_strategy_score`, number, required: false): Enter a score for your marketing strategy, based on your answers.
* **customer_satisfaction_score** (`customer_satisfaction_score`, number, required: false): Enter a score for your customer satisfaction, based on your answers.

Note: The scores for the fields `marketing_personality_score`, `customer_value_score`, `customer_personality_score`, `customer_engagement_score`, `marketing_strategy_score`, and `customer_satisfaction_score` are intended to be subjective scores based on your answers and understanding of the marketing personality traits and customer metrics.
