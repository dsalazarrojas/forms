# Dance Studio Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our dance studio. We value your opinion and encourage you to be honest and thorough in your responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please fill out the form with your honest and genuine feedback about your experience with our dance studio.
2.  For each field, please provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your thoughts and feelings.
3.  Please submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dance Studio Name** (`dance_studio_name`, `text`, required: false): 
    This is where you can input the name of the dance studio that you visited.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): 
    Please enter your name, or the name of the person who visited the studio.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): 
    If you would like to be contacted by the studio, please provide your email address here.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): 
    If you would like to be contacted by the studio, please provide your phone number here.
* **Performance** (`Performance`, `number`, required: false): 
    Please rate how you feel about the overall performance of our dance studio, using the scale provided.
* **Instructors** (`Instructors`, `text`, required: false): 
    If you have any comments or feedback about the instructors you worked with, please provide them here.
* **Class Scheduling** (`Class Scheduling`, `select_one`, required: false): 
    Please select one of the options provided to let us know how you feel about our class scheduling.
* **Facilities** (`Facilities`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Please select all the options that apply to your experience with our facilities.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): 
    If there is anything you would like to say about your experience with our dance studio, please provide it here.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, required: false): 
    If there is anything else you would like to say about your experience, please provide it here.

## Tips
* Please be as honest and thorough as possible in your responses.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please don't hesitate to share them.
* We value your feedback and look forward to hearing about your experience!
