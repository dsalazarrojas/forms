# Digital Content Consumption Preference Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is a collection of questions designed to understand individual's preferences and habits when consuming digital content.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all that apply for each question.
2. You can select multiple options for each question, as indicated by the type of question (e.g., select_multiple).
3. Be honest and provide the most accurate information possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of content do you consume?**: Choose all types of content you consume, such as news, social media, videos, podcasts, and blogs.
    * 'Yes' - You consume this type of content.
    * 'No' - You do not consume this type of content.
    * 'Other' - Specify other types of content not listed.
* **How often do you consume digital content?**: Choose the frequency of your digital content consumption.
    * 'Yes' - You consume content at this frequency.
    * 'No' - You do not consume content at this frequency.
    * 'Other' - Specify a different frequency.
* **Which platform do you use to consume digital content?**: Select all platforms you use, such as Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and Twitter.
    * 'Yes' - You use this platform.
    * 'No' - You do not use this platform.
    * 'Other' - Specify other platforms not listed.
* **On which device do you consume digital content?**: Choose all devices you use to consume digital content.
    * 'Yes' - You use this device.
    * 'No' - You do not use this device.
    * 'Other' - Specify other devices not listed.
* **How long do you usually spend on digital content?**: Enter the average amount of time you spend consuming digital content in minutes.
* **What sources do you use to find digital content?**: Select all sources you use to find content.
    * 'Yes' - You use this source.
    * 'No' - You do not use this source.
    * 'Other' - Specify other sources not listed.
* **What format do you prefer for digital content?**: Select all formats you prefer for digital content, such as text, video, and audio.
    * 'Yes' - You prefer this format.
    * 'No' - You do not prefer this format.
    * 'Other' - Specify other formats not listed.
* **Content Frequency**: Choose how often you consume content of this type.
    * 'Yes' - You consume content at this frequency.
    * 'No' - You do not consume content at this frequency.
    * 'Other' - Specify a different frequency.
* **Content Source 2**: Select all sources you use to find this type of content.
    * 'Yes' - You use this source.
    * 'No' - You do not use this source.
    * 'Other' - Specify other sources not listed.
* **Content Format 2**: Select all formats you prefer for this type of content.
    * 'Yes' - You prefer this format.
    * 'No' - You do not prefer this format.
    * 'Other' - Specify other formats not listed.
* **Content Frequency 2**: Choose how often you consume content of this type.
    * 'Yes' - You consume content at this frequency.
    * 'No' - You do not consume content at this frequency.
    * 'Other' - Specify a different frequency.
* **Other platform(s) do you use?**: Select all other platforms you use for digital content.
    * 'Yes' - You use this platform.
    * 'No' - You do not use this platform.
    * 'Other' - Specify other platforms not listed.
* **Other sources you use to find digital content?**: Select all other sources you use to find content.
    * 'Yes' - You use this source.
    * 'No' - You do not use this source.
    * 'Other' - Specify other sources not listed.
* **Other formats do you prefer for digital content?**: Select all other formats you prefer for digital content.
    * 'Yes' - You prefer this format.
    * 'No' - You do not prefer this format.
    * 'Other' - Specify other formats not listed.
