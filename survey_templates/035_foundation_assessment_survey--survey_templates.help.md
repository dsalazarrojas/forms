# Foundation Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, the "Foundation Assessment Survey", is a tool used to gather detailed information about the condition and status of foundations for new builds and renovations. Its primary purpose is to help construction teams assess and prioritize necessary repairs or upgrades based on accurate and factual data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure you have personal knowledge or experience with the foundation you are submitting information about.
* Fill out the "Foundation Type" field with the correct option that best describes the foundation (Shallow or Other).
* For the "Condition" field, select all that apply from the list of options provided (Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, Not Applicable).
* If you have additional details about the foundation type (e.g., its material composition), fill out the "Foundation Type Details" field with a brief description.
* Choose the correct size of the foundation from the options provided (Small, Medium, Large).
* If you know the age of the foundation in years, enter the correct number in the "Foundation Age" field.
* If you have information on the depth of the foundation, enter the correct number in the "Foundation Depth" field.
* The "Foundation Condition Rating" field is used to give a numerical assessment of the foundation's condition, out of a hypothetical scale (e.g., 0-10, where 0 is the worst condition and 10 is the best condition).
* The "Foundation Condition Comment" field is for any additional comments or observations about the foundation's condition that are not covered by the other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Foundation Type** (`Foundation Type`, select_one, required): This field is used to categorize the foundation type. Choose from Shallow foundation or Other if it doesn't fit into the shallow category.
* **Condition** (`Condition`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select all that apply from the list of options provided. Select all conditions that apply to the foundation.
* **Foundation Type Details** (`Foundation Type Details`, text, optional): This field is for additional details about the foundation type, if needed.
* **Foundation Size** (`Foundation Size`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the correct size of the foundation.
* **Foundation Age** (`Foundation Age`, number, optional): This field is for the age of the foundation in years, if known.
* **Foundation Depth** (`Foundation Depth`, number, optional): This field is for the depth of the foundation, if known.
* **Foundation Condition Rating** (`Foundation Condition Rating`, number, optional): This field is a numerical assessment of the foundation's condition, out of a hypothetical scale.
* **Foundation Condition Comment** (`Foundation Condition Comment`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or observations about the foundation's condition. 

Note: Please ensure all fields are completed as accurately as possible to provide a comprehensive assessment of the foundation's condition.
