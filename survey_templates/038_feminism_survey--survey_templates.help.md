# Feminism Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions and experiences from users on various aspects of feminism. Users will be asked about their beliefs, knowledge, and experiences related to feminism. Please provide your honest and thoughtful responses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each page carefully and complete all required and optional fields.
2. For multiple-choice questions (e.g. beliefs, impact on society), select all applicable options.
3. For text fields, provide clear and concise answers.
4. Skip or leave blank any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **what_is_feminism**: This is a text field where you can provide your understanding of what feminism means to you.

### Feminism Beliefs
* **feminism_beliefs**: In this field, select all that apply to your beliefs about feminism:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Knowledge about Feminism
* **knowledge_about_feminism**: Please provide a brief description of your knowledge about feminism, e.g. what you've read, learned, or experienced.

### How it Affects Me
* **how_it_affects_me**: Select how you feel about the impact of feminism on your life:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Personal Experience with Feminism
* **personal_experience_with_feminism**: Share a brief account of your personal experiences related to feminism, if any.

### Impact on Society
* **impact_on_society**: Select all that apply to how feminism has impacted society:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Actions to Support Feminism
* **actions_to_support_feminism**: Describe any actions you have taken or would take to support feminism.

### Recommendations for Feminism
* **recommendations_for_feminism**: Suggest any recommendations or suggestions for promoting or supporting feminism.

### Final Thoughts
* **final_thoughts**: Share any final thoughts or reflections on feminism in your own words.
