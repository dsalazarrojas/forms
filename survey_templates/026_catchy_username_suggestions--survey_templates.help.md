# Catchy Username Suggestions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users to generate catchy username suggestions for their social media handle or online presence.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your full name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is where we'll send your generated username suggestions.
3. In the "Username Preferences" section, select the primary platform you'll be using this username on.
4. Choose the desired vibe or style of your username from the options provided.
5. If you have any specific keywords or themes you'd like to include in your username, type them in the "Specific Keywords or Themes to Include" field.
6. Select the maximum length of your desired username.
7. Choose whether numbers, symbols, or both are allowed in your username.
8. Provide any previous usernames or handles you've liked in the past for us to get inspiration from.
9. In the "Your Niche" section, specify your primary interest or area of focus.
10. Select who your target audience is for your username.
11. Choose how quickly you need the suggestions, with options ranging from ASAP to No Rush.
12. Finally, provide any additional information or context that might help us better understand your requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where you want us to send your generated username suggestions.
* **Primary Platform** (`primary_platform`, `select_one`, required): Choose the main site you will use this for (e.g. Gaming, Social Media, Professional, Community/Forum, Personal/Blog).
* **What is the desired vibe for your username** (`username_vibe`, `select_one`, required): Select the desired style or theme of your username (e.g. Cool and Edgy, Cute and Aesthetic, Professional and Clean, Funny and Punny, Mysterious/Abstract).
* **Specific Keywords or Themes to Include** (`keyword_preferences`, `text`, optional): Provide any specific keywords or themes you'd like to include in your username (e.g. space, cats, coffee, tech).
* **Maximum Character Length** (`character_limit`, `number`, optional): Select the maximum length of your desired username.
* **Are numbers or symbols allowed** (`allow_numbers_symbols`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether numbers or symbols are allowed in your username (e.g. user_123 or user.name).
* **Previous useranmes or handles you have liked** (`previous_usernames_liked`, `text`, optional): Provide any previous usernames or handles you've liked for inspiration.
* **Primary Interest or Niche** (`primary_interest`, `text`, required): Specify your primary interest or area of focus (e.g. Minecraft, Cooking, Coding, Photography).
* **Who is your target audience** (`target_audience`, `select_one`, optional): Select who your target audience is for your username (e.g. General Public, Niche Community, Professional/Clients, Friends and Family).
* **How quickly do you need these suggestions** (`urgent_request`, `select_one`, required): Choose how quickly you need the suggestions, with options ranging from ASAP to No Rush.
* **Anything else we should consider** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information or context that might help us better understand your requirements.
