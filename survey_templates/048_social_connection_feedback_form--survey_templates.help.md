This form aims to assess the social connections within our community, gathering feedback on how often members interact with each other, their sense of belonging, satisfaction levels, barriers to connection, self-expression, positive experiences, and the adequacy of our support system. The form collects data on individual experiences to help us identify areas for improvement and better serve our community members.

# Social Connection Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from community members on their social connections within our community. Your input will help us understand how well our community is meeting your needs and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best match your experiences and feelings. For each question, choose one of the provided options, and type in any additional comments or thoughts if desired. Don't worry, your feedback is anonymous and confidential.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you regularly visit our community?** (`community_vibes`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand how often you interact with our community. Select "Frequently" if you visit regularly, "Occasionally" if you visit occasionally, or "Rarely" if you hardly ever visit.
* **How would you rate your belonging feelings?** (`belonging_scale`, number, required): This question asks you to rate your sense of belonging on a scale, where a higher number indicates a stronger sense of belonging.
* **What is your satisfaction level?** (`satisfaction_survey`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our community by selecting one of the provided options: "Very Satisfied", "Satisfied", "Neutral", "Dissatisfied", or "Very Dissatisfied".
* **What prevents you from connecting with others?** (`barriers_to_connection`, text, required): Share any challenges you face in connecting with others, such as language barriers, cultural differences, or lack of interest.
* **Can you be yourself in our community?** (`self_expression`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand if you feel comfortable expressing yourself in our community. Select "True" if you feel at ease, "False" if you don't.
* **Have you had a positive experience in our community?** (`feedback_form`, select_one, required): Share your positive experiences, successes, or moments of joy in our community.
* **Is our community support system adequate?** (`support_system`, select_one, required): This question helps us assess the effectiveness of our support system. Select "True" if you feel supported, "False" if you don't.
* **Do you have any comments or feedback?** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions on how we can improve our community.
