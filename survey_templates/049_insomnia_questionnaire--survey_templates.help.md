# Insomnia Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insomnia Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about a person's sleep patterns, habits, and experiences. It is meant to gather data for research or study purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select each page and answer the corresponding questions.
2. Fill in your sleep history, quality, onset, duration, and efficiency.
3. Select the sleep disorders that apply to you.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sleep History** (1) (Text): Enter a brief description of your sleep history, such as your sleep schedule, bedtime routine, or any notable events that affect your sleep.
* **Sleep Quality** (2) (Text): Rate your sleep quality, such as how well you rested, how refreshing you felt, or any difficulties you experienced during sleep.
* **Sleep Onset** (3) (Time): Enter the time you typically go to bed or start feeling sleepy.
* **Sleep Duration** (4) (Number): Enter the amount of time you usually sleep or rest each night.
* **Sleep Efficiency** (5) (Number): Rate your sleep efficiency, such as how well you were able to fall asleep, stay asleep, and wake up feeling rested.
* **Sleep Discomfort** (6) (Select One): Select one of the sleep disorders listed that apply to you.
* **Sleep Disorder 1** (7) (Select Multiple): Select all sleep disorders that apply to you, specifically Disorder 1.
* **Sleep Disorder 2** (8) (Select Multiple): Select all sleep disorders that apply to you, specifically Disorder 2.
* **Sleep Disorder 3** (9) (Select Multiple): Select all sleep disorders that apply to you, specifically Disorder 3.
* **Sleep Disorder 4** (10) (Select Multiple): Select all sleep disorders that apply to you, specifically Disorder 4.
