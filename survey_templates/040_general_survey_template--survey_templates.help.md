# General Survey Template - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is a general template for creating surveys to collect data from users. It is a versatile template that can be used for various types of surveys, including market research and customer feedback surveys.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of your survey (e.g. "Market Research" or "Customer Feedback").
2. Provide a brief description of your survey template.
3. Upload a file with the name "040_general_survey_template--survey_templates.yaml".
4. Assign a tool to your survey (e.g. "chatjimmy" or "Other").
5. Fill in any additional information required for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Category** (`survey_templates`)
Select the category of your survey from the dropdown menu.

*   This field is optional and allows you to choose the type of survey you're creating.

### 2. **Survey Template Description** (`survey_template_description`)
Enter a brief description of your survey template.

*   This field is optional and provides a short text description of your survey template.

### 3. **Output File** (`output_file`)
Upload a file with the name "040_general_survey_template--survey_templates.yaml".

*   This field is not required and is used to upload a file with the survey template data.

### 4. **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`)
Select the tool assigned to your survey from the dropdown menu.

*   This field is optional and allows you to choose the tool associated with your survey.

### 5. **Form ID** (`form_ids`)
Enter the ID number for your form.

*   This field is not required and is used to input a unique form ID.

### 6. **Short Description** (`description_short`)
Enter a short description of your survey template.

*   This field is optional and provides a short text description of your survey template.

### 7. **Long Description** (`description_long`)
Enter a longer description of your survey template.

*   This field is optional and is used to provide more detailed information about your survey template.

### 8. **Time Spent Manual Input** (`time_spent_manual_input`)
Estimate the time spent on manual input for your survey.

*   This field is not required and is used to input the time spent on manual input.

### 9. **Description of Manual Input** (`description_manual_input`)
Enter a description of the manual input process for your survey.

*   This field is not required and is used to provide more information about the manual input process.

### 10. **Time Spent Survey Process** (`time_spent_survey_process`)
Estimate the time spent on the survey process.

*   This field is not required and is used to input the time spent on the survey process.

## Tips

- Make sure to select the correct category for your survey template.
- Ensure that the output file is correctly named and uploaded.
- Choose the correct tool for your survey.
- Be as detailed as possible when providing descriptions for your survey template.
- If required fields are marked as optional, feel free to fill them in or leave them blank.
