# support_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The support_satisfaction_survey form is a tool for customers to provide feedback on their experiences with a company's support team. It helps identify areas of improvement and measures the effectiveness of customer service efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following information:
	* **Customer Name**: Your name or the name of the company representative completing the form.
	* **Email**: Your email address or the email address of the company representative completing the form.
2. Answer the questions by selecting one of the provided options:
	* **How likely are you to recommend our company?**: Choose from the options very unlikely, unlikely, neutral, likely, or very likely.
	* **How do you rate our customer service team?**: Choose from the options very poor, poor, neutral, good, or very good.
	* **How would you rate our responsiveness?**: Choose from the options very slow, slow, neutral, fast, or very fast.
	* **How would you rate our resolution rate?**: Choose from the options very low, low, neutral, high, or very high.
	* **How would you rate our communication?**: Choose from the options very poor, poor, neutral, good, or very good.
	* **How would you rate our expertise?**: Choose from the options very low, low, neutral, high, or very high.
	* **How would you rate our knowledge?**: Choose from the options very low, low, neutral, high, or very high.
3. Add any additional comments or suggestions in the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name or the name of the company representative completing the form.
* **Email**: Enter your email address or the email address of the company representative completing the form.
* **How likely are you to recommend our company?**: Choose how likely you are to recommend the company to others.
* **How do you rate our customer service team?**: Rate the performance of the customer service team.
* **How would you rate our responsiveness?**: Rate the company's responsiveness to customer inquiries or issues.
* **How would you rate our resolution rate?**: Rate the speed and efficiency of resolving customer issues.
* **How would you rate our communication?**: Rate the quality and effectiveness of communication from the company.
* **How would you rate our expertise?**: Rate the level of knowledge and competence of the company's support team.
* **How would you rate our knowledge?**: Rate the amount and relevance of information provided by the company.
* **What could we improve on?**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide clear feedback.
* If you are unsure about any question, choose the option that best reflects your opinion.
* You can add any additional comments or suggestions in the **Comments** field.
* This form will help us understand your experiences and improve our support services.
