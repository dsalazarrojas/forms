# Concert Hall Encore Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from concert attendees about their experience at the concert hall.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. The form is divided into multiple sections. Please complete each section according to the instructions provided.
3. For each field, provide your honest and thoughtful response.
4. You can submit the form anonymously, so feel free to share your feedback without sharing your identity.
5. Once you have completed all the sections, click on the "Submit" button to send your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to Encore Voting** (`welcome_message`, `note`, required: false): This section is an introduction to the form and an invitation to vote for your favorite performance.
* **Your Name** (`voter_name`, `text`, required: false): Your optional name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`voter_email`, `email`, required: false): Your email address for confirmation and results notification.
* **Concert Date** (`concert_date`, `date`, required: true): Select the date of the concert you attended.
* **Performance Section** (`performance_section`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the section of the concert you are voting for (First Half, Second Half, Full Program).
* **Encore Performance Choice** (`encore_choice`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the performance you would like to hear again (Opening Overture, First Symphony Movement, Second Symphony Movement, etc.).
* **Favorite Composer of the Evening** (`composer_preference`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the composer whose work you enjoyed most.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the overall performance of the concert (Exceptional, Excellent, Very Good, Good, Satisfactory).
* **Orchestra Performance** (`orchestra_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the orchestra's performance (Outstanding, Excellent, Very Good, Good, Needs Improvement).
* **Conductor Performance** (`conductor_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the conductor's leadership (Outstanding, Excellent, Very Good, Good, Needs Improvement).
* **Soloist Performance** (`soloist_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the soloist's performance (Outstanding, Excellent, Very Good, Good, No Soloist, Not Applicable).
* **Venue Acoustics** (`venue_acoustics`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the sound quality of the hall (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Seating Satisfaction** (`seating_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with your seat location (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Would You Attend Again** (`would_attend_again`, `select_one`, required: true): Indicate if you would attend future concerts at this hall (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Future Program Suggestions** (`program_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Provide any suggestions for future concert programs.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any other feedback about the performance.

## Tips
* Please be honest and sincere in your responses.
* You can provide as much or as little information as you like.
* If you have any suggestions for future concerts, please share them in the "Future Program Suggestions" field.
* If you have any other comments about the performance, please share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
