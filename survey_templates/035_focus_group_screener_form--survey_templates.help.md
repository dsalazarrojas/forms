# Focus Group Screener Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Focus Group Screener Form is designed to collect demographic and contact information for a focus group or market research purpose. This form is a general template for gathering various categories of information and is not specific to a particular business or organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill out the form with your demographic and contact information.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and truthful responses to the required fields.
4. Submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, required/optional): A brief introduction or description of your contact information and demographic data. This field is optional.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to enter your name.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): A text field for you to enter your email address.
* **Other Contact Info** (`other_contact_info`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to enter any other contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Demographic Background** (`demographic_background`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to describe your demographic background.
* **Income Category** (`income_category`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Less than 20k
	+ 20-40k
	+ 40-60k
	+ 60-80k
	+ More than 80k
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Employed
	+ Self-employed
	+ Retired
	+ Unemployed
	+ Student
	+ Disabled
* **Age** (`age`, number, required/optional): A number field for you to enter your age.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to enter your occupation.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Some high school
	+ Bachelor's degree
	+ Master's degree
	+ PhD
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ African American
	+ Caucasian
	+ Asian
	+ Hispanic
	+ Mexican
	+ Pacific Islander
	+ Other
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Single
	+ Married
	+ In a relationship
	+ Divorced
	+ Widowed
* **Children** (`children`, number, required/optional): A number field for you to enter the number of children you have.
* **Household Income** (`household_income`, number, required/optional): A number field for you to enter your household income.
* **Education Years** (`education_years`, number, required/optional): A number field for you to enter the number of years you've been educated.
* **Highest Education Level** (`highest_education_level`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to enter your highest level of education.
* **Languages** (`languages`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to list languages you speak.
* **Languages Known** (`languages_known`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to list languages you know.
* **Languages Read/Write** (`languages_read_write`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to list languages you can read and write.
* **Other Languages** (`other_languages`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to list any other languages.
* **Other Languages Known** (`languages_other_known`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to list any other languages you know.
* **Other Languages Read/Write** (`languages_other_read_write`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to list any other languages you can read and write.
* **Other Languages Other** (`other_languages_other`, text, required/optional): A text field for you to enter any other languages information.
