# Best Venue Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from the community about exceptional venues that deserve recognition and appreciation. Users are invited to nominate a venue they have experience with and share their thoughts on what makes it stand out.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and contact information as the nominator, providing your full name, email address, and your role at the venue or event.
2. Select your role at the venue or event from the options provided.
3. Provide the name of the venue you are nominating.
4. Select the category that best describes the venue type.
5. Rate the venue's atmosphere and design, staff service quality, technical facilities, accessibility, and catering and food quality.
6. Describe the best thing about the venue and what makes it unique.
7. Summarize why this venue should win the award for Best Venue.
8. Enter your professional reference contact information for verification.
9. Provide the date of nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`nominator_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the nominator.
* **Your Role** (`nominator_role_select`, `select_one`, required): Select your role at the venue or event from the options provided.
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name_text`, `text`, required): Enter the official name of the venue.
* **Venue Location** (`venue_location_city_state`, `text`, required): Enter the city and state/province of the venue.
* **Venue Type** (`venue_type_select`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary category for the venue from the options provided.
* **Overall Atmosphere and Design** (`atmosphere_design_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the venue's overall atmosphere and design from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Staff Service Quality** (`staff_service_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the staff's service quality from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Technical Facilities** (`technical_facilities_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the technical facilities from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Accessibility for All Guests** (`accessibility_rating_score`, `select_one`, required): Rate the venue's accessibility from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Catering and Food Quality** (`catering_food_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the catering and food quality from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Best Thing About This Venue** (`best_venue_thing_desc`, `text`, required): Describe the best thing about the venue.
* **Unique or Standout Feature** (`standout_feature_desc`, `text`, required): Describe what makes the venue unique or standout.
* **Why Should This Venue Win** (`award_win_justification_detailed`, `text`, required): Summarize why this venue should win the award for Best Venue.
* **Professional Reference** (`venue_professional_reference`, `text`, required): Enter the name and email of someone familiar with the venue details for verification.
* **Date of Nomination** (`nomination_final_date_input`, `date`, required): Enter the date of nomination.
