# Customer Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect customer feedback and needs information to improve the customer experience and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Select all that apply for each of the customer needs questions (Do you need help with a project? Do you need assistance with a task? etc.).
4. Choose your preferred communication channel from the options provided.
5. Select all that apply for the product or service preference.
6. Enter any additional feedback or comments you may have in the feedback field.
7. If applicable, provide feedback on how the company can improve in the review field.
8. Confirm your answers by checking the "Confirm your answers" checkbox.
9. Finally, review the terms and conditions, and if you agree, check the "I agree" checkbox.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Do you need help with a project?**: Select all that apply if you need help with a project.
* **Do you need assistance with a task?**: Select all that apply if you need assistance with a task.
* **Do you need information about a service?**: Select this checkbox if you need information about a service.
* **Do you need help with a product?**: Select this checkbox if you need help with a product.
* **Do you need to be contacted?**: Select this checkbox if you need to be contacted.
* **Do you need help with an issue?**: Select this checkbox if you need help with an issue.
* **Which channel do you prefer to communicate?**: Choose your preferred communication channel (Phone, Email, Social Media, or In-person).
* **Which product or service would you like to receive?**: Select all that apply for the product or service you would like to receive.
* **Any additional feedback?**: Enter any additional feedback or comments you may have.
* **How can we improve?**: Provide feedback on how the company can improve.
* **Confirm your answers**: Confirm your answers by checking this checkbox.
* **I confirm that I have read and agree to the terms and conditions**: Review the terms and conditions and check this checkbox if you agree.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by customers who are looking to get help or information from the company. It's essential to complete this form carefully to ensure that your needs are accurately recorded. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
