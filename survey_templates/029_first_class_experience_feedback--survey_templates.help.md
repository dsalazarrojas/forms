# first_class_experience_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from passengers who have experienced first class travel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have recently taken a first class flight and can provide honest feedback about your experience.
2. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus and text fields as accurately as possible.
3. Provide a rating for your overall experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Class Experience**: This field is for describing your first class experience in your own words. Please provide a detailed account of what happened during your flight.
* **Route**: Please select the route you took for your first class flight.
* **Airline**: Select all the airlines you flew with during your first class experience.
* **Flight Number**: If you want to provide additional information about your flight, you can enter the flight number here.
* **Departure Date**: If you want to provide the date you departed for your flight, you can enter it here.
* **Return Date**: If you want to provide the date you returned from your flight, you can enter it here.
* **Seat Class**: Please select the seat class you booked for your flight (First Class, Business Class, Economy Class).
* **Cabin Service**: Please rate your experience with the cabin service (e.g. flight attendants, in-flight entertainment).
* **Food Quality**: Please rate the quality of the food you were served on your flight.
* **Overall Experience**: Please select your overall experience of flying first class (e.g. very good, good, fair, poor).
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions about your first class experience, please provide them here.
* **Rating**: Provide a rating for your overall experience (1-5 stars).
