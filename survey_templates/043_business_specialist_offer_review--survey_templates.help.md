# Business Specialist Offer Review - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback and evaluation of business specialists' offers. Reviewers will assess the offer's market alignment, competitive advantage, value proposition clarity, financial viability, and overall recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
2.  Review each field carefully and provide detailed responses where applicable.
3.  Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
4.  If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the form creators for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Reviewer Information

*   **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, `text`, required): This field is for you to enter your name as the reviewer.
*   **Department** (`reviewer_department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department or affiliation.
*   **Title or Role** (`reviewer_title`, `text`, required): Provide your title or role.
*   **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your review.

### Offer Details

*   **Offer or Proposal Name** (`offer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the offer or proposal being reviewed.
*   **Type of Offer** (`offer_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category or classification of the offer from the provided options.
*   **Brief Offer Description** (`offer_description`, `text`, required): Provide an overview of the offer being reviewed.
*   **Target Audience or Segment** (`target_audience`, `text`, required): Describe who this offer is intended for.
*   **Brief Offer Description** was replaced by **Target Audience or Segment** to make space and provide a clear overview of the form structure.

### Evaluation Assessment

*   **Market Alignment** (`market_alignment`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well this offer aligns with market needs.
*   **Competitive Advantage** (`competitive_advantage`, `select_one`, required): Select how this offer compares to competitors.
*   **Value Proposition Clarity** (`value_proposition`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate how clear the value proposition is.
*   **Financial Viability** (`financial_viability`, `select_one`, required): Assess the financial viability of the offer.
*   **Overall Recommendation** (`overall_recommendation`, `select_one`, required): Choose the recommended course of action.

### Recommendation and Feedback

*   **Overall Recommendation** (`overall_recommendation`, `select_one`, required): This field is actually part of the evaluation assessment section and is not needed here as a separate option. It has been merged with the previous section.
*   **Key Strengths** (`strengths`, `text`, optional): Describe the strengths or positive aspects of the offer.
*   **Key Weaknesses or Concerns** (`weaknesses`, `text`, optional): Identify the weaknesses or concerns with the offer.
*   **Suggested Improvements** (`suggested_improvements`, `text`, optional): Provide suggestions on how the offer could be improved.
*   **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, `text`, optional): Consider any risks associated with the offer.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any further feedback or comments.

Note: The form structure and field explanations are adjusted for clarity and conciseness.
