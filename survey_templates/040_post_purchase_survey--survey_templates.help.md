# Post Purchase Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Purchase Survey form is designed to gather feedback from customers after they've made a purchase. This feedback helps us improve the customer experience and the overall quality of our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the "Customer Information" section at the top of the page.
2. Select one or multiple options for each question in the "Product Feedback" section.
3. Review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first section of the form where you can enter your demographic information.
* **What type of product did you purchase?** (`question1`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or multiple options that best describe the type of product you purchased.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you purchased the product.
	+ Select 'No' if you did not purchase the product.
* **How would you rate the quality of the product?** (`question2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the available options to rate the quality of the product.
	+ Select 'Poor' if the product was of poor quality.
	+ Select 'Fair' if the product was of fair quality.
	+ Select 'Good' if the product was of good quality.
	+ Select 'Excellent' if the product was of excellent quality.
* **What would you rate the price of the product?** (`question3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or multiple options that best describe your opinion on the price of the product.
	+ Select 'Too High' if you thought the price was too high.
	+ Select 'Too Low' if you thought the price was too low.
	+ Select 'Just Right' if you thought the price was just right.
