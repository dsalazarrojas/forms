# public_policy_opinion_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects users' opinions on public policy issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter your E-mail address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. If you select an issue in "Select Policy Issue", you can also select any number of issues applicable in "Select Issues".
6. If you want to provide more details about specific issues, use the text fields "Issue 1", "Issue 2", and "Issue 3".
7. Enter your opinions on "Opinion" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **E-mail** (email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Select Policy Issue** (select_one, optional): Select one policy issue from the list.
* **Select Issues** (select_multiple, optional): Select all policy issues that apply.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter a date.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter a time.
* **Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Issue 1** (text, optional): Enter details about issue 1.
* **Issue 2** (text, optional): Enter details about issue 2.
* **Issue 3** (text, optional): Enter details about issue 3.
* **Issue 4** (text, optional): Enter details about issue 4.
* **Issue 5** (text, optional): Enter details about issue 5.
* **Opinion** (text, optional): Enter your opinion.
* **Rating 1** (number, optional): Rate issue 1 (1-5).
* **Rating 2** (number, optional): Rate issue 2 (1-5).
* **Rating 3** (number, optional): Rate issue 3 (1-5).
* **Decimal 1** (number, optional): Enter decimal value 1.
* **Decimal 2** (number, optional): Enter decimal value 2.
* **Decimal 3** (number, optional): Enter decimal value 3.
* **Date 2** (date, optional): Enter a date.
* **Time 2** (time, optional): Enter a time.
* **E-mail 2** (email, optional): Enter an email address.
* **Note 2** (note, optional): Enter additional comments.
* **Issue 4** (text, optional): Enter details about issue 4.
* **Issue 5** (text, optional): Enter details about issue 5.
