# Field Research Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from researchers and participants for field research purposes. It is meant to be used by researchers to gather necessary contact information and details about their field research, and by participants to provide their consent and information about their involvement in the research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Researcher Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information in this section.
2. **Participant Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
3. **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information so that the researcher can reach out to you.
4. **Field Details**: Describe the field research you are participating in, including any relevant dates, times, and locations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Researcher Information (Page 1)
- **Researcher Information** (text field): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information, such as your email address and phone number.

### Participant Information (Page 1)
- **Participant Information** (text field): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself, such as your age and occupation.

### Contact Information (Page 1)
- **Contact Information** (text field): Enter your contact information so that the researcher can reach out to you, such as your email address and phone number.

### Field Details (Page 1)
- **Field Details** (text field): Describe the field research you are participating in, including any relevant dates, times, and locations. This can include any necessary details to ensure you can participate or be contacted.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure that you can be contacted and participate in the field research.
- Review each field carefully before submitting to ensure that you have included all necessary information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the researcher directly.
