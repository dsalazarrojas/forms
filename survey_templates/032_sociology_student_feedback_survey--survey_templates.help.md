# Sociology Student Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from sociology students on their experience with a course. The purpose of this form is to gather information on what students liked and disliked about the course, what they found most and least useful, and their overall opinion of the course.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Answer the questions about your experience with the course, rating and providing feedback as instructed.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the course and instructor.
4. Submit your completed form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your name as a student.
* **How would you rate this course?**: Choose how you would rate the course using the options provided.
	+ Excellent: I was highly satisfied with the course.
	+ Average: The course was neutral and met my expectations.
	+ Poor: The course was not satisfactory.
* **What did you like best about the course?**: Provide a short answer on what you liked most about the course.
* **What did you dislike about the course?**: Provide a short answer on what you disliked most about the course.
* **How would you rate the instructor?**: Choose how you would rate the instructor using the options provided.
	+ Excellent: I was highly satisfied with the instructor.
	+ Average: The instructor met my expectations.
	+ Poor: The instructor was not satisfactory.
* **What did you like/dislike about the instructor?**: Provide a short answer on what you liked or disliked most about the instructor.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of the course?**: Choose how you would rate the overall quality of the course using the options provided.
	+ Excellent: The course was highly satisfactory.
	+ Average: The course met my expectations.
	+ Poor: The course was not satisfactory.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving the course?**: Provide a short answer on any suggestions you have for improving the course.
* **How likely are you to take this course again?**: Choose how likely you are to take the course again using the options provided.
	+ Very Likely: I would definitely take the course again.
	+ Somewhat Likely: I might take the course again.
	+ Not Likely: I would not take the course again.
* **What was the most useful part of the course?**: Choose the most useful part of the course using the options provided.
	+ Lecture: The lectures were informative and helpful.
	+ Assignments: The assignments were challenging and engaging.
	+ Discussions: The discussions were interactive and insightful.
	+ Quizzes: The quizzes were challenging and fun.
	+ Other: Other (please specify).
* **Why was the most useful part of the course?**: Provide a short answer on why the most useful part of the course was helpful to you.
* **What was the least useful part of the course?**: Choose the least useful part of the course using the options provided.
	+ Lecture: The lectures were not informative or helpful.
	+ Assignments: The assignments were not challenging or engaging.
	+ Discussions: The discussions were not interactive or insightful.
	+ Quizzes: The quizzes were not challenging or fun.
	+ Other: Other (please specify).
* **Why was the least useful part of the course?**: Provide a short answer on why the least useful part of the course was not helpful to you.
* **Overall, how would you rate the course?**: Choose how you would rate the course using the options provided.
	+ Excellent: The course was highly satisfactory.
	+ Average: The course met my expectations.
	+ Poor: The course was not satisfactory.
* **Why?**: Provide a short answer on why you would rate the course as you did.
* **Do you have any suggestions for the instructor?**: Provide a short answer on any suggestions you have for the instructor.
* **Do you have any suggestions for the course improvement?**: Provide a short answer on any suggestions you have for improving the course.
* **Would you recommend this course to a friend?**: Choose whether you would recommend the course to a friend using the options provided.
	+ True: Yes, I would recommend the course.
	+ False: No, I would not recommend the course.
* **Why or why not?**: Provide a short answer on why you would or would not recommend the course to a friend.
* **Do you have any other comments about this course?**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the course.
* **Do you have any other comments about the instructor?**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the instructor.
* **Do you have any other comments about the course structure?**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the course structure.
* **Would you like to take this course again?**: Choose whether you would like to take the course again using the options provided.
	+ True: Yes, I would like to take the course again.
	+ False: No, I would not take the course again.
* **Why?**: Provide a short answer on why you would or would not like to take the course again.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* Keep your answers concise and clear.
* Do not provide personal attacks or criticisms.
* Do not discuss unrelated topics or issues.
* This form is designed to be completed by sociology students, but your feedback is valuable to anyone who has taken the course.
