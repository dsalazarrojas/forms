# Workplace Wellness Program Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and information from employees about their perceptions of the workplace wellness program. It aims to understand how employees experience their work-life balance, physical and mental health, and their overall well-being. This information will help us to better understand the effectiveness of our wellness program and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, based on your personal experience.
2. Choose one option per question that best describes your situation.
3. For questions with multiple options (e.g., wellness initiatives), select all that apply.
4. In the "Feedback Comments" field, feel free to provide any additional comments or thoughts you may have about the workplace wellness program.
