# Road Safety Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Road Safety Workshop Feedback Form is a tool for gathering feedback and suggestions from workshop participants. Completing this form will help us improve the content and structure of future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter the title of the workshop you attended.
3. Enter the date and time of the workshop.
4. Enter the length of the session (in hours).
5. Rate your satisfaction with the workshop on a scale of 1-5.
6. If you would like to suggest areas for improvement, please provide any thoughts or comments you think will help us enhance the workshop.
7. Add any additional comments or suggestions you think would be beneficial for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Participant Name** (`workshop_participant_name`): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`): Enter the title of the workshop you attended, such as "Defensive Driving Course" or "Workshop on Road Safety".
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`): Enter the location where the workshop took place.
* **Date of Workshop** (`date_of_workshop`): Enter the date you attended the workshop in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Time of Workshop** (`time_of_workshop`): Enter the time you attended the workshop in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Session Hours** (`session_hours`): If you are asked to select the length of the workshop, please enter the number of hours you attended.
* **Rating of Workshop** (`rating_of_workshop`): Rate your satisfaction with the workshop on a scale from 1 to 5.
	+ 1: Very Dissatisfied
	+ 2: Dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Satisfied
	+ 5: Very Satisfied
* **How Satisfied with Workshop** (`session_satisfaction`): Select the level of satisfaction from the options provided.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`): Enter any suggestions or comments on how the workshop could be improved.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional thoughts or comments you think would be beneficial for us to know.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and honest as possible with your answers to provide us with valuable feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas, feel free to include them in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
* Your input will help us improve the quality and effectiveness of future workshops.
