# Scrum Task Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scrum Task Management Survey is a tool designed to gather information on team performance, team lead, and other relevant factors. Its purpose is to aid teams in improving their processes and workflows.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Scrum Task Management Survey form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team name in the **Team Name** field.
2. Enter the task ID in the **Task ID** field, if applicable.
3. Select the team lead from the list in the **Team Lead** field.
4. Enter the team lead's name in the **Team Lead Name** field.
5. Enter the team lead's email address in the **Team Lead Email** field.
6. Enter the team lead's phone number in the **Team Lead Phone** field.
7. Enter the team lead's title in the **Team Lead Title** field.
8. Select up to three team members from the list in the **Team Members** field.
9. Enter the collaboration level in the **Collaboration** field.
10. Provide feedback on task tracking in the **Feedback on task tracking** field.
11. Rate the process satisfaction in the **Process satisfaction** field.
12. Select the date for continuous improvement in the **Continuous Improvement** field.
13. Enter any relevant Jira tickets in the **Jira Ticket** field.
14. Enter the team's workload in the **Team Workload** field.
15. Provide feedback on Jotform in the **Feedback on Jotform** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of your team.
* **Task ID** (number, required: false): Enter the ID of the task if applicable.
* **Team Lead** (select_one, required: false): Select the team lead from the list.
* **Team Lead Name** (text, required: false): Enter the team lead's name.
* **Team Lead Email** (email, required: false): Enter the team lead's email address.
* **Team Lead Phone** (text, required: false): Enter the team lead's phone number.
* **Team Lead Title** (text, required: false): Enter the team lead's title.
* **Team Members** (select_multiple, required: false): Select up to three team members from the list.
* **Collaboration** (number, required: false): Rate the level of collaboration.
* **Feedback on task tracking** (text, required: false): Provide feedback on task tracking.
* **Process satisfaction** (number, required: false): Rate the process satisfaction.
* **Continuous Improvement** (date, required: false): Select the date for continuous improvement.
* **Jira Ticket** (text, required: false): Enter any relevant Jira tickets.
* **Team Workload** (number, required: false): Enter the team's workload.
* **Feedback on Jotform** (text, required: false): Provide feedback on Jotform.

Tips:

* Please ensure that you are providing accurate and honest feedback.
* Use the **Continuous Improvement** field to schedule a date for future improvements.
* If you have any feedback on task tracking, process satisfaction, or Jotform, please provide it in the respective fields.
* You can select up to three team members in the **Team Members** field.
* You can rate the collaboration level on a scale of 1-5.
* You can rate the process satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* You can rate the team's workload on a scale of 1-5.
* If you have any Jira tickets related to the task, please enter them in the **Jira Ticket** field.
