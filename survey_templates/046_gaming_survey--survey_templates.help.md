# gaming_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about games, including game titles, names, platforms, types, genres, player levels, ratings, and other relevant details for market analysis, user experience analysis, or game development projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Game Name** in the "Game Name" field.
2. Select the **Game Type** from the dropdown menu (e.g., MMORPG, FPS, MOBA, RPG).
3. Choose the **Game Genre** by selecting multiple options from the list (e.g., action, adventure, arcade, racing, role-playing).
4. Enter the **Player Level** in the designated field.
5. Enter the **Player Rating** in the designated field.
6. Enter your **E-Mail** address in the "E-Mail" field.
7. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
8. Provide a brief **Note** in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Title**: Enter the title of the game in this text field.
* **Game Name**: Enter the name of the game in this text field.
* **Platform**: Select the platform on which the game is played (Desktop, Mobile, Console, Web).
* **Game Type**: Select the category of the game (e.g., MMORPG, FPS, MOBA, RPG).
* **Game Genre**: Choose multiple genres that apply to the game (e.g., action, adventure, arcade, racing, role-playing).
* **Player Level**: Enter the player's level of experience or skill.
* **Player Rating**: Enter the player's rating or review of the game.
* **E-Mail**: Enter your email address for contact or follow-up purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact or follow-up purposes.
* **Note**: Provide a brief note about the game or player experience.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure valuable data collection.
