# Follow Up Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers who have interacted with the company for follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Choose the correct answer for each question.
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: Select multiple options that apply to your interaction with our company.
	+ This question helps us understand what type of interaction you had with our company.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions about your experience.
	+ This is an open-ended question where you can provide any feedback you think would be helpful to us.
* **Follow Up Survey**: This is a free-text field for any follow-up questions or comments.
	+ Use this space to ask or comment on anything that was not already covered in previous questions.
* **Customer Service**: Choose one of the provided options to rate your experience with our customer service.
	+ This question helps us gauge how well we served you during your interaction.
* **Company**: This is an optional field for you to share your thoughts about our company.
	+ You can provide any comments or suggestions you think would be helpful to us about our company.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully.
* Only select "Yes" for Customer Info if you actually interacted with our company.
* If you're unsure about an answer, choose "Neutral" in the Customer Service field.
* This form should be filled out once per interaction with our company.
