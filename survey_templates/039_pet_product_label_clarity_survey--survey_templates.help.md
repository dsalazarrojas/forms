# Pet Product Label Clarity Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from pet owners on the clarity and effectiveness of product labels on pet food products. Your input will help us improve the clarity and usefulness of product labels, making it easier for you to make informed decisions about your pet's food.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your pet type (e.g., Cat, Dog, Other)
2. Answer the questions about the product label clarity, effectiveness, and overall satisfaction
3. Provide your contact information (email and contact number)
4. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Type** (`What type of pet do you have` (required)): Select the type of pet you own, such as Cat or Dog.
* **Product Label Clarity** (`product_label_clarity` (required, number)): Rate how clear the product label was on your pet food, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Label Effectiveness** (`product_label_effectiveness` (required)): Select how effective the product label instructions were in guiding you.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction` (required, select multiple)): Choose the level of satisfaction you felt with the product label (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral).
* **Compliance** (`compliance` (required, select one)): Indicate whether you followed all the instructions on the product label.
* **Email** (`email` (required)): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number` (required)): Enter your contact number.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`comments`, `feedback` (optional)): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the product label. 

Remember, your input will help us improve the product label clarity and effectiveness for pet owners like you!
