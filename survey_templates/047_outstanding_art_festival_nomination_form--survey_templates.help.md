# Outstanding Art Festival Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for an Outstanding Art Festival nomination. The form is intended to gather details about the artist and their artwork, including any relevant information that supports the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Artist Information field with the artist's name and contact details.
2. Enter the Artwork Information field with a brief description of the artwork, including any relevant details that support its nomination.
3. In the Nomination Details field, provide any additional information that you would like to share about the artwork and its relevance to the festival.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Information (Artist)**: This field is where you can enter the name of the artist and their contact information. Please make sure to include any relevant details about the artist's contact information.
* **Artwork Information (Artwork)**: This field is for a brief description of the artwork. Please include as much detail as possible about the artwork, including medium, size, and any other relevant information that you feel is important.
* **Nomination Details (Nomination Details)**: This field is for any additional information that you would like to provide about the artwork and its relevance to the festival. Please be as detailed as possible.

## Tips
* Please make sure to proofread your entries carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
