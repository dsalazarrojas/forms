# Retail Home Improvement Preferences Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your preferences and habits related to retail home improvement. The data collected will help us better understand your needs and provide you with more tailored services and products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through each page carefully and make sure you understand what is being asked.
* Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* Select the options that best match your current habits and preferences.
* Make sure to enter your email address and phone number (if applicable) accurately.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shopping Habits (1)**: A note section where you can write about your shopping habits, including how often you shop and what you typically buy.
* **Satisfaction With Products (2)**: This is a text field where you can express your satisfaction with our current products and services.
* **Improvement Preferences (3)**: Select all the improvement areas you would like to see in your home. You can choose from:
	+ Improvement of Kitchen
	+ Improvement of Bathroom
	+ Improvement of Bedroom
	+ Improvement of Living Room
	+ Improvement of Outdoor Space
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Budget (4)**: If you are willing to disclose your budget for home improvements, please enter a number. This information will help us better understand your budget constraints.
* **Shopping Frequency (5)**: Indicate how often you shop for home improvement products:
	+ Less than once a week
	+ 1-2 times a week
	+ 3-4 times a week
	+ 5 or more times a week
* **Contact Method (6)**: Do you prefer to be contacted by:
	+ Yes (phone or email)
	+ No
* **Email Address (7)**: If you would like to be contacted via email, please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (8)**: If you would like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Home Improvement Goals (9)**: A note section where you can express your goals and aspirations for home improvements.
* **Customer Insights (10)**: A note section where you can provide any additional insights about our services and products that could help us improve.
* **Demographics (11)**: Indicate your demographic information:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Other
* **Age (12)**: If you are willing to disclose your age, please enter a number.
* **Income (13)**: If you are willing to disclose your income, please select from:
	+ Below $25,000
	+ $25,000-$50,000
	+ $50,000-$100,000
	+ $100,000+
* **Occupation (14)**: If you are willing to disclose your occupation, please enter a text description.
