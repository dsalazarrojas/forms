# Program Member Satisfaction Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from program members about their overall satisfaction with the program. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the answer that best reflects your experience with the program.
2. Be honest and specific with your answers.
3. If you have additional comments or suggestions, please use the "What feedback do you have for the program?" field.
4. If you have participated in the program multiple times, please answer accordingly to the questions about participation duration, start date, end date, and frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Member Satisfaction**: This section is where you can rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
	+ **Program Member Satisfaction** (`program_member_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false)
		- This question allows you to rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **How likely are you to recommend our program to a friend?**: This section is where you can rate how likely you are to recommend the program to a friend.
	+ **How likely are you to recommend our program to a friend?** (`recommendation`, select_multiple, required: false)
		- Choose all options that apply to your likelihood of recommending the program.
* **How satisfied are you with the program overall?**: This section is where you can rate your satisfaction with the program's overall experience.
	+ **How satisfied are you with the program overall?** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false)
		- Choose the option that best reflects your experience with the program.
