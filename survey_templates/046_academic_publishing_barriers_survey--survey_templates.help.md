# Academic Publishing Barriers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Academic Publishing Barriers Survey form. The survey aims to collect information about your experiences and attitudes towards open access publishing, with the goal of identifying key barriers and gathering insights on how to mitigate or resolve them.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer each question based on your personal experience and expertise.
2. Select the appropriate options for each question, using the provided options for select_one and select_multiple questions.
3. Provide a brief description for text questions, such as `mitigation_strategies` and `suggestions_for_publishers`.
4. Answer all questions that are marked as "required" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role in publishing**: This question asks about your role in the academic publishing process. Select the option that best describes your position, which can be Author, Editor, Reviewer, Librarian, or Publisher.
* **Experience level**: This question asks about your level of experience in publishing or research. Select the option that best describes your experience, which can be Less than 2 years, 2 to 5 years, 6 to 10 years, or More than 10 years.
* **Key barriers to publishing**: This question asks you to select all the barriers that apply to your publishing experience. Select all the options that you have faced or know about, which can be Access to funding, High publication fees, Peer review delays, Lack of mentorship, or Language barriers.
* **Attitude toward open access publishing**: This question asks you to rate your position on open access publishing. Select your attitude, which can be Strongly support, Support, Neutral, or Oppose.
* **Institutional support available**: This question asks about the availability of resources for publishing within your institution. Select the option that best describes your situation, which can be Extensive, Moderate, Limited, or None.
* **Strategies to mitigate barriers**: This question asks you to provide a brief description of the strategies that would help you overcome the barriers you have selected. Think about how you have overcome obstacles in the past and what would help you in the future.
* **Training needs to support publishing**: This question asks you to select the areas where you need training or support to improve your publishing experience. Select all the options that apply, which can be Writing and editing, Grant writing, Navigating peer review, Open access publishing.
* **Suggestions for publishers and editors**: This question asks you to provide a brief description of any ideas you have to improve the publishing process. Think about how you would like to see changes or improvements.
* **Additional comments**: This question is an open-ended field for any other insights or comments you have about publishing barriers or suggestions.
