# school-climate-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a School Climate Survey designed to collect data on students' perceptions of their school environment. The survey will help administrators and teachers understand students' needs and make informed decisions to improve the school climate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please select the corresponding answer for each question. You can choose one or more of the options provided for each question. Remember that all questions are optional, and you can skip any question that you feel uncomfortable answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Environmental Satisfaction**: This question asks about your satisfaction with the school building and facilities. Please select "Satisfied" if you are generally happy with the school environment, and "Dissatisfied" if you have concerns or issues with the school facilities.
* **Academic Expectations**: This question is about your expectations from your teachers and school staff. Please select "Yes" if you expect a high level of support and guidance from teachers, and "No" if you feel that teachers are not meeting your expectations.
* **Student Teacher Ratio**: This is a numerical question where you can enter a number representing the ideal student-teacher ratio you think is suitable for your school. The higher the number, the better.
* **School Climate Satisfaction**: This question is similar to the first question, asking about your satisfaction with the school climate. Please select "Satisfied" if you feel that the school climate is positive and supportive, and "Dissatisfied" if you have concerns.
* **Teacher Student Relationship**: This question is about your relationship with teachers. Please select "Yes" if you have a positive relationship with teachers, and "No" if you feel that the relationship is not good.
* **Student Engagement**: This question is about your level of engagement with school activities and events. Please select "Yes" if you are actively participating and engaged, and "No" if you feel that you are not engaged.
* **Peer Acceptance**: This question is about how well you feel accepted by your peers. Please select "Yes" if you feel that your peers accept you, and "No" if you feel that you are not accepted.
* **Student Safety**: This question is about your safety and well-being within the school. Please select "Yes" if you feel safe and supported, and "No" if you feel that you are not safe.
