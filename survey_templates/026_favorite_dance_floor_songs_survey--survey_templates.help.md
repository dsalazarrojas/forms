# favorite_dance_floor_songs_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your favorite songs on the dance floor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and select your answers accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This page is a brief overview of the form and explains the purpose of the survey.
* **Favorite songs** (`favorite_songs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the songs that you enjoy dancing to.
* **Favorite genres** (`favorite_genres`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the genres of music that you enjoy dancing to.
* **Favorite artists** (`favorite_artists`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the artists that you enjoy dancing to.
* **Favorite songs 2** (`favorite_songs_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the songs that you enjoy dancing to in a different setting (e.g. home, car, etc.).
* **Favorite genres 2** (`favorite_genres_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the genres of music that you enjoy dancing to in a different setting.
* **Favorite artists 2** (`favorite_artists_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the artists that you enjoy dancing to in a different setting.
* **Favorite songs 3** (`favorite_songs_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the songs that you enjoy dancing to in yet another setting (e.g. beach, etc.).
* **Favorite genres 3** (`favorite_genres_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the genres of music that you enjoy dancing to in yet another setting.
* **Favorite artists 3** (`favorite_artists_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the artists that you enjoy dancing to in yet another setting.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Type in your email address or phone number to be entered into our survey and get notified when we publish the results.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive notifications when we publish the results.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number to receive notifications when we publish the results.

Note: Some fields are optional, but we encourage you to provide as much information as you can.
