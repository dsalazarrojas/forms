# Reaction Time Score Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit scores for the "Reaction Time" game.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure you have not already submitted scores.
2. Fill out each page with your scores. For each page, simply input your score for the corresponding entry.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have filled out all pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Entry** (first_entry, number, required: false): Enter your score for the first entry.
* **Second Entry** (second_entry, number, required: false): Enter your score for the second entry.
* **Third Entry** (third_entry, number, required: false): Enter your score for the third entry.

## Tips
- Please submit only one score per page.
- Ensure your scores are accurate and legible.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
