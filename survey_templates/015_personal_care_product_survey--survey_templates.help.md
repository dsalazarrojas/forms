# Personal Care Product Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about customers' personal care products usage and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
2. **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
3. **Product Type**:
	* Select the type of product that you have purchased or used.
	* Choose from: Toothpaste, Soap, or Shampoo.
4. **Satisfaction**: Choose a number that best describes your level of satisfaction with the product.
	* This is a scale from 0 to 10, where 0 is not satisfied at all and 10 is extremely satisfied.
5. **Feedback**: Share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about the product.
6. **Date Last Purchase**: Enter the date when you last purchased the product.
7. **Time Last Purchase**: Enter the time when you last purchased the product.
8. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (if you have one).
