# cultural_exhibitions_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and assessment data from users after they have visited a cultural exhibition.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your feedback in the "how_did_you_find_exhibition" field.
2. Provide a rating of your experience by selecting a number in the "rating" field.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the "suggestion" field.
4. If you would like to be contacted, fill in your email and phone number in their respective fields.
5. Specify how much time you spent at the exhibition by using the time input.
6. If you have a favorite part of the exhibition, select one of the options in the "favorite_part" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibition Name** (`exhibition_name`, text, not required): Please fill in the name of the exhibition you visited.
* **how_did_you_find_exhibition** (`feedback_question`, text, not required): Please describe your experience at the exhibition. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, not required): Rate your experience by selecting a number between 1 and 10. A higher number indicates a more enjoyable experience.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, not required): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please let us know in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): If you would like to be contacted, please fill in your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): If you would like to be contacted, please fill in your phone number.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, time, not required): Please specify how much time you spent at the exhibition.
* **Favorite Part** (`favorite_part`, select one, not required): Select your favorite part of the exhibition. The options are Exhibition, Artist, or Other.
## Tips
* Please be as honest as possible when filling in this form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
