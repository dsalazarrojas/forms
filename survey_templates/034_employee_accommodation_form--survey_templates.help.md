# Employee Accommodation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Accommodation Form is designed to collect information about employee accommodations, allowing for the efficient and organized handling of such requests within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*). These include:
    * Employee Name (\*)
    * Employee Email (\*)
    * Employer Name (\*)
    * Employer Email (\*)
    * Employer Phone (\*)
    * Job Title (\*)
    * Accommodation Location (\*)
2.  Provide the requested details for each field as instructed, making sure to include any necessary supporting information or attachments if required.
3.  Submit the form once all fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name (\*)** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the employee submitting the accommodation request.
*   **Employee Email (\*)** (`employee_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the employee submitting the accommodation request.
*   **Employee Phone (\*)** (`employee_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the employee submitting the accommodation request.
*   **Accommodation Location (\*)** (`accommodation_location`, text, required/optional): Specify the location for the accommodation request.
*   **Job Title (\*)** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the job title of the employee submitting the accommodation request.
*   **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, optional): Choose an option to indicate the type of accommodation being requested (Yes/No).
*   **Accommodation Status** (`accommodation_status`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate the accommodation's status (Active/Inactive/Pending).
*   **Employer Name (\*)** (`employer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company or organization providing the accommodation.
*   **Employer Email (\*)** (`employer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the company or organization providing the accommodation.
*   **Employer Phone (\*)** (`employer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the company or organization providing the accommodation.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee's manager if applicable.
*   **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the employee's manager if applicable.
*   **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee's manager if applicable.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options to indicate departmental involvement or relevance to the accommodation request (Yes/No).
*   **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options to indicate if team members are involved in the accommodation request (Yes/No).
*   **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate the manager's approval status (Yes/No).
*   **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the manager's approval if applicable.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the accommodation request.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, text, optional): Enter the end date of the accommodation request if applicable.
*   **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, text, optional): Enter the employee's address for contact purposes, if applicable.
*   **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, optional): Enter the employer's address for contact purposes, if applicable.
*   **Reason for Accommodation** (`reason_for_accommodation`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the accommodation request, if applicable.

Note: The asterisk (\*) denotes a required field. Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
