# Teaching Innovation Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your school's current stage in the innovation adoption cycle, the biggest barriers you face when adopting new ideas or teaching methods, and the support you need from your district or school. This data will help us understand the challenges and needs of schools in adopting innovations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully to understand the questions and options.
2. Select the stage your school is currently in (Early Stage, Established Stage, or Maturation Stage) for each question.
3. Identify the biggest barriers you face when adopting new ideas or teaching methods. You can select more than one option.
4. Indicate the support you need from your district or school. You can select more than one option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teaching Innovation Adoption Survey**: This is the title of the form.
* **Which stage of the innovation adoption cycle is your school currently in?**: This question helps us understand your school's current stage in adopting innovations. Please select one of the three options: Early Stage, Established Stage, or Maturation Stage.
* **What is the biggest barrier you face when adopting new ideas or teaching methods?**: This question helps us understand the challenges you face when adopting new ideas or teaching methods. You can select more than one option. The options are:
	+ Difficulty getting support from superiors
	+ Lack of resources
	+ Lack of training
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What kind of support do you currently need from your district or school?**: This question helps us understand the support you need to adopt innovations. You can select more than one option. The options are:
	+ Coaching or mentoring
	+ Financial support
	+ Materials or equipment
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Other (please specify)**: This is a text field where you can provide additional information if none of the options above apply to you.
* **Other (barrier)**: This is a text field where you can specify other barriers you face when adopting new ideas or teaching methods.
* **Other (support)**: This is a text field where you can specify other types of support you need from your district or school.

Note: The questions are repeated with slight variations to gather data at different stages of the innovation adoption cycle. Please answer each question accordingly, and don't worry if the options seem similar.
