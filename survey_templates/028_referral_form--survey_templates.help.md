# referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The referral form is designed to collect essential information about patients, including their demographics, insurance details, and contact information. This data will help healthcare professionals to quickly and efficiently process referrals and ensure that patients receive timely and quality care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's details, including name, address, insurance information, and contact details.
2. Choose the source of the referral (e.g., doctor, family member, friend, social media).
3. Select the type of insurance the patient has (e.g., medical, medicare, private, medicaid).
4. Enter the patient's insurance number (if applicable).
5. Fill in the patient's address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
6. Enter the patient's phone number and email address.
7. Indicate whether the patient has consent to share their information.
8. Read and agree to the privacy statement.
9. Assign the referral to a specific healthcare professional (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details**: Enter the patient's name, age, and any other relevant demographic information.
* **Referrers Source**: Choose from the following options:
	+ Patient: The patient is referring themselves.
	+ Family: A family member is referring the patient.
	+ Friend: A friend is referring the patient.
	+ Social Media: The patient was referred through social media.
	+ Other: Specify the other source of the referral.
* **Insurance Details**: Enter the patient's insurance information, including the insurance type and number.
* **Insurance Type**: Select from the following options:
	+ Medical
	+ Medicare
	+ Private
	+ Medicaid
	+ Other
* **Insurance Number**: Enter the patient's insurance number (if applicable).
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Street**: Enter the patient's street address.
* **City**: Enter the patient's city name.
* **State**: Select from the following options:
	+ New York
	+ California
	+ Florida
	+ Other
* **Zip**: Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Consent to Share**: Indicate whether the patient has consented to share their information.
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional who will process the referral.
* **Privacy Statement**: Read and agree to the privacy statement, acknowledging that the patient's information will be kept confidential and secure.
