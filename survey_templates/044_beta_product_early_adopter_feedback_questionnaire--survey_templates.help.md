# Beta Product Early Adopter Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beta Product Early Adopter Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from early adopters about their experience with the beta product. This form will help the product team understand the product's strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and ensure the final product meets the needs and expectations of its users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and email address for follow-up questions.
2. Answer the questions about your experience with the beta product, including your usage duration, impressions, and ratings for ease of setup, user interface, and performance.
3. Describe any technical issues you encountered and the features that were useful or less useful to you.
4. Rate the product's stability, speed, and responsiveness.
5. Provide feedback on the product's value proposition and areas for improvement.
6. Indicate your willingness to pay for the final version of the product.
7. Finally, provide any other comments or suggestions for the product team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`beta_user_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Your Email Address** (`beta_user_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for follow-up questions. Please ensure it is accurate to receive any further communication about the product.
* **Beta Product Name** (`beta_product_name_text`, `text`, required): Describe the beta product you are providing feedback on.
* **How long have you been using the beta product** (`usage_duration_select`, `select_one`, required): Choose the duration you have been using the beta product:
	+ Less than 1 week
	+ 1 to 2 weeks
	+ 3 to 4 weeks
	+ Over 1 month
* **Initial Impressions and UX** (`initial_impressions_header`, `note`, required): Rate your initial impressions of the product from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Ease of Setup and Onboarding** (`onboarding_ease_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the ease of setup and onboarding from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **User Interface (UI) Design** (`ui_design_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the product's visual appeal and layout from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Most Useful Feature** (`most_useful_feature_desc`, `text`, required): Identify the feature that you use most often.
* **Least Useful or Confusing Feature** (`least_useful_feature_desc`, `text`, required): Describe the least useful or most confusing feature to you.
* **Overall Product Stability** (`stability_assessment_choice`, `select_one`, required): Rate the product's stability from:
	+ Very Stable - No issues
	+ Minor Bugs - Occasional Glitches
	+ Frequent Issues - Needs work
* **Speed and Responsiveness** (`speed_responsiveness_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the product's speed and responsiveness from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Describe any technical issues you encountered** (`technical_issues_narrative`, `text`, required): Share any technical issues you faced while using the product.
* **Value Proposition and Improvement** (`value_improvement_header`, `note`, required): Rate the product's value proposition and indicate areas for improvement.
* **How well does the product solve your core problem** (`core_problem_solving_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the product's effectiveness in solving your core problem from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **One feature you would like to see** (`missing_feature_request`, `text`, required): Identify the top feature you would like to see in the next release.
* **Likelihood to recommend after official launch** (`net_promoter_score_proxy`, `select_one`, required): Rate your likelihood to recommend the product after its official launch from 1 (Not likely) to 5 (Extremely likely).
* **Willingness to Pay for Final Version** (`willingness_to_pay_status`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your willingness to pay for the final version:
	+ Very Likely - I would pay
	+ Likely
	+ Neutral - depends on price
	+ Unlikely
* **Any other feedback for the product team** (`product_team_feedback_text`, `text`, required): Provide any final thoughts or encouragement for the product team.
* **Date of Questionnaire** (`feedback_completion_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are filling out this questionnaire.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions thoroughly to provide the most valuable feedback.
- If you have any technical issues, please describe them in detail so we can understand the problem clearly.
- If you have suggestions for improvement, please be specific and realistic.
- Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort to complete this questionnaire.
