# Personal Clarity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Clarity Assessment is a tool for individuals to gain insights into their goals, values, and progress towards achieving success. It helps you to reflect on your current situation, identify obstacles, and plan your next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each page carefully and follow the instructions.
2. Answer the questions honestly, taking into account your current situation.
3. Select the options that best describe your situation.
4. Move to the next page by clicking the "Next" button or by selecting the next option in the page list.
5. Finally, review your results and submit your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Personal Clarity Assessment** (`intro_page`, `note`, required: `false`): This page is an introduction to the assessment. Please read it carefully and understand the purpose of the form.
* **What are your values and priorities** (`life_direction`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all the values and priorities that are important to you. This will help you identify your core values and prioritize your goals.
* **What obstacles prevent you from achieving your goals** (`obstacles`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all the obstacles that hinder your progress. Be honest, and try to identify specific challenges.
* **Reflect on your current situation** (`self_reflection`, `date`, required: `false`): Choose a date to reflect on your current situation. This is an opportunity for you to think about your current situation and identify areas for improvement.
* **What are your long term goals** (`goals`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose one of the options to describe your long-term goals. This will help you focus on what you want to achieve.
* **Obstacles to achieving goals** (`obstacles_2`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select one of the obstacles that prevent you from achieving your goals.
* **What are your core values** (`values`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all the core values that are important to you. This will help you understand what drives you and your decisions.
* **Reflect on your current situation** (`self_reflection_2`, `date`, required: `false`): Choose a date to reflect on your current situation. This is another opportunity for you to think about your current situation and identify areas for improvement.
* **Reflect on your current situation** (`self_reflection_3`, `date`, required: `false`): Choose a date to reflect on your current situation. This is the third and final opportunity for you to think about your current situation and identify areas for improvement.
* **What are your next steps** (`next_steps`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all the next steps you plan to take. This will help you plan your future actions.
* **Finalize your assessment** (`final_steps`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose one of the options to finalize your assessment. This will help you understand your progress and areas for improvement.
* **View your results** (`results`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select one of the options to view your results. This will provide you with a summary of your assessment.
* **Submit your assessment** (`submit`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose one of the options to submit your assessment. This will complete the assessment process.
