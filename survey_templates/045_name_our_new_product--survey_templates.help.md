# name_our_new_product - Help Guide

## Purpose

We are excited to learn about your new product! The <b>name_our_new_product</b> form is designed to help our team better understand your product and its features. Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your willingness to share your product information. Please answer the questions honestly, and make sure to fill in all required fields. Your feedback will help us improve our services and better serve you and our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Choose the relevant category and product type from the provided options.
3. Add any additional tags that describe your product.
4. Provide any comments or suggestions you have about our service.
5. Fill in your personal contact information to help us understand who you are.
6. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your product.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your product in a few sentences.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, `note`, required): Please provide us with your information, and rest assured that we will not share it with anyone.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, not required): If you'd like to share your contact number with us.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category that best fits your product.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the types that apply to your product.
* **Tags** (`tags`, `select_multiple`, not required): Add any additional keywords that describe your product.
* **Comments** (`submitters_comments`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them with us.
* **Company** (`submitters_company`, `text`, not required): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`submitters_job_title`, `text`, not required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`submitters_department`, `text`, not required): Enter your department.
* **Job Location** (`submitters_job_location`, `text`, not required): Enter your job location.
* **Date & Time** (`submitters_date_time`, `date`, not required): If you'd like to specify a date and time for your submission.
* **Time** (`submitters_time`, `time`, not required): If you'd like to specify a time for your submission.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Country** (`submitter_country`, `text`, not required): Enter your country of residence.
* **Submitter State** (`submitter_state`, `text`, not required): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **Submitter Postal** (`submitter_postal`, `text`, not required): Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Submitter Language** (`submitters_language`, `text`, not required): Enter the language you speak.
* **Submitter IP Address** (`submitter_ip_address`, `text`, not required): Enter your IP address.
* **Submitter Browser** (`submitter_browser`, `text`, not required): Enter the browser you are using.
* **Submitter OS** (`submitter_os`, `text`, not required): Enter your operating system.
* **Submitter Screen Resolution** (`submitter_screen_resolution`, `text`, not required): Enter your screen resolution.
* **Submitter Device Type** (`submitter_device_type`, `text`, not required): Enter your device type.
