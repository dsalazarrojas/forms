# Portfolio Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Portfolio Evaluation Survey is a tool for evaluators to assess the quality, effectiveness, and overall value of a portfolio.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the first page with the "Employer Rating" field.
2. Enter a rating from 1 to 5 for the portfolio, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
3. Proceed to the "Evaluation Date" field and select a date to indicate when the evaluation was performed.
4. Move to the "Performance Score" field and input a numeric value to represent the portfolio's performance.
5. Enter any additional feedback in the "Feedback" field.
6. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employer Rating (Required: False)**: Enter a rating from 1 to 5 to represent the overall quality of the portfolio.
* **Evaluation Date (Required: False)**: Select a date to indicate when the evaluation was performed.
* **Performance Score (Required: False)**: Enter a numeric value to represent the portfolio's performance.
* **Feedback (Required: False)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the portfolio.

## Tips
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback.
* Ensure the feedback is relevant to the evaluation.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you need to fill out multiple evaluations for the same portfolio, use this form for each unique evaluation.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your supervisor or administrator for assistance.
