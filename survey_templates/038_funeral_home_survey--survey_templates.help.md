# Funeral Home Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Home Survey is designed to collect information about funeral homes that have been visited or will be visited by our organization. This survey aims to gather data on services attended, ratings, and comments from funeral homes, which will help us improve our services and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Complete the form**: Fill in all required fields, which are marked as "Required".
2. **Enter your name and email**: Please enter your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields respectively.
3. **Provide the funeral home name**: Enter the name of the funeral home you visited or will be visiting.
4. **Indicate services attended**: Choose the services you attended or plan to attend from the list under "Services Attended" (Yes or No).
5. **Rate your experience**: Rate your experience from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest, in the "Rating" field.
6. **Share your comments**: Provide any additional comments you have about your experience with the funeral home.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to our Funeral Home**: This field is a text entry field to record a brief message or welcome note from the funeral home.
* **Name**: This is a required field where you must enter your name to submit the survey.
* **Email**: This is a required field where you must enter a valid email address associated with your name.
* **Funeral Home Name**: This is a required field where you must enter the name of the funeral home you visited or will be visiting.
* **Services Attended**: This field is a select multiple field where you must choose the services you attended or plan to attend. Options are Yes or No.
* **Rating**: This is a required field where you must rate your experience from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments about your experience with the funeral home.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or difficulties while completing the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
* We appreciate your feedback and look forward to improving our services based on your input.
