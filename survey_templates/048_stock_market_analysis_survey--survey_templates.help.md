# Stock Market Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your insights and opinions about the stock market, helping to inform our analysis and decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, considering your current knowledge and understanding of the stock market.
2. Select the options that best match your response for each question.
3. If you have additional comments or notes, you can include them in the "What is your investment strategy?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Group (Text)** (`form_group`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your current market analysis group or topic.
* **Section 1 (Text)** (`section_1`, text, optional): Add any additional context or information about your analysis.
* **What is the current stock market trend? (Text)** (`question_1`, text, optional): Describe the current trend of the stock market. For example, "Bullish/Bearish" or "Uncertain".
* **How many months of data do you need access to?** (`question_2`, select_one, optional) 
  + 1 month: Choose this if you need access to recent data.
  + 3 months: Choose this if you need access to slightly more recent data.
  + 6 months: Choose this if you need access to moderately recent data.
  + 12 months: Choose this if you need access to a full year's data.
* **What is the current market performance?** (`question_5`, select_multiple, optional) 
  + Positive: Select this if the market is trending upwards.
  + Negative: Select this if the market is trending downwards.
  + Neutral: Select this if the market is stable.
* **Which asset class do you focus on?** (`question_6`, select_one, optional) 
  + Stocks: If you primarily analyze or invest in stocks.
  + Bonds: If you primarily analyze or invest in bonds.
  + Commodities: If you primarily analyze or invest in commodities.
* **What is your investment strategy?** (`question_7`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your investment approach.
* **What is the risk tolerance?** (`question_8`, select_multiple, optional) 
  + High risk: If you are willing to take high-risk investments.
  + Low risk: If you prefer low-risk investments.
  + Medium risk: If you take moderate risk in your investments.
