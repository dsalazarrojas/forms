# Car Wash Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Car Wash Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience at our car wash facility. This survey aims to gather information on the cleanliness of our facility, the quality of our service, and overall satisfaction with our pricing and value. By providing your feedback, you will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer the questions as accurately as possible, and provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your name**: Please enter your name for reference purposes.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone number**: Provide your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Date of wash service**: Enter the date you visited our car wash facility.
* **Which wash package did you choose?**: Select the wash package you chose for your vehicle.
* **How clean and well-maintained was our facility?**: Rate our facility's cleanliness and maintenance.
* **How friendly and professional was our staff?**: Rate the friendliness and professionalism of our staff.
* **How well was your vehicle washed?**: Rate the quality of the wash job.
* **Was all the cleaning you requested completed?**: Rate the completeness of the cleaning job.
* **Was the service completed in a timely manner?**: Rate the time it took to complete the service.
* **How would you rate the water quality used?**: Rate the quality of the water used.
* **How well was your vehicle dried?**: Rate the quality of the drying job.
* **Was the price reasonable for the service?**: Rate the reasonableness of the price.
* **Did you feel you received good value?**: Rate your overall value perception.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with our service?**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our service.
* **Would you use our car wash again?**: Rate your likelihood of returning to our car wash.
* **Would you recommend us to friends or family?**: Rate your likelihood of recommending us to others.
* **What did we do well?**: Enter any strengths or positives about your experience.
* **What could we improve?**: Enter any areas for improvement or suggestions.
* **Did you experience any issues during your visit?**: Enter any problems or concerns you experienced.
* **Any additional comments or suggestions?**: Enter any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.

Note: All fields marked as required must be completed in order to submit the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
