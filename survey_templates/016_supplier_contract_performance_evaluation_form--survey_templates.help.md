# Supplier Contract Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Contract Performance Evaluation Form is designed to assess the performance of a supplier contract based on various parameters such as communication, quality, delivery, payment, and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions according to your experience and perception of the supplier's performance.
2. Use the options provided to select the ratings and comments as necessary.
3. Fill in the text fields with your thoughts and observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your experience with the supplier?**: Describe your experience with the supplier, including any interactions or transactions you have had with them.
* **Rate the supplier**: Provide a rating on a scale of 1-5 to indicate your satisfaction with the supplier's performance.
* **Contract start date**: Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract end date**: Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Supplier performance**: Rate the supplier's performance using the provided options (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Comments on the supplier contract**: Provide any comments or observations you have on the supplier's performance.
* **Communication**: Rate the supplier's communication (1-5).
* **Quality**: Rate the supplier's quality (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Delivery**: Rate the supplier's delivery (1-5).
* **Payment**: Rate the supplier's payment (1-5).
* **Supplier Contract Review Comments 2**: Provide any additional comments or observations you have on the supplier's performance.
* **Category**: Select the categories that apply to the supplier's performance (High, Medium, Low).
* **Remarks on the supplier contract**: Provide any additional comments or observations you have on the supplier's performance.
* **Contract value**: Rate the value of the supplier's contract using the provided options (High, Medium, Low).
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the supplier's performance using the provided options (High, Medium, Low).
* **Would you recommend?**: Indicate if you would recommend the supplier (True, False, Not sure).
* **Contract type**: Select the type of contract (Service contract, Goods contract).
* **Contract value (2)**: Enter the contract value.
* **Is the supplier important?**: Indicate if the supplier is important (True, False).
* **Is the supplier recurring?**: Indicate if the supplier is recurring (True, False).
* **Contract term**: Enter the contract term.
* **Contract period**: Enter the contract period.
* **Contract Start Date 2**: Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date 2**: Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Contract date**: Enter the contract date.
* **Payment term**: Enter the payment term.
* **Contract amount**: Enter the contract amount.
* **Contract amount currency**: Enter the contract amount currency.
* **Review the supplier contract**: Provide any additional comments or observations you have on the supplier's performance.
* **Supplier contract status**: Select the status of the supplier contract (In progress, Completed, Canceled).
