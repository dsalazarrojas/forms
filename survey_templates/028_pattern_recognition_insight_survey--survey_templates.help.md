# Pattern Recognition Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pattern Recognition Insight Survey is a tool designed to collect insights on pattern recognition from educators, researchers, and training teams. It aims to gather information on their habits, confidence in pattern recognition, and involvement in training teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the introduction page (page 1) if necessary.
2. Select your level of confidence in pattern recognition (page 2).
3. Choose the researchers involved in your work (page 3).
4. List your training teams (page 4).
5. Choose your pattern recognition habits (page 5).
6. Select your level of involvement in training teams (page 6).
7. Choose your confidence level in pattern recognition (page 7).
8. Select your method of data collection (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educators**: Select the level of confidence you have in your pattern recognition skills. Please choose one of the options: educators 1, educators 2.
* **Researchers**: List the researchers involved in your work. You can select multiple researchers.
* **Habits**: Select the pattern recognition habits you employ. You can select multiple habits.
* **Training Teams**: List the training teams involved in your work. You can select multiple teams.
* **Pattern Recognition**: Select your confidence level in pattern recognition. Please choose one of the options: high, medium, low.
* **Training Teams**: Select your involvement level in training teams. Please choose one of the options: high, medium, low.
* **Data Collection**: Select your method of data collection. Please choose one of the options: high, medium, low.
* **Confidence**: Select your confidence level in pattern recognition. Please choose one of the options: high, medium, low.
