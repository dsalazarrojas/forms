# Wall Extraction Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wall Extraction Assessment Survey is designed to collect comprehensive data on the characteristics and conditions of a building's walls for evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by reviewing the form's pages. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the wall's characteristics and conditions.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If a field is marked as required, it is essential to provide a response. If a field is optional, provide information only if it is relevant to the wall being assessed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Site Conditions (id: 1)
Site Conditions: This field is used to describe the overall condition of the site where the wall is located. Please provide a brief description of the site's condition, including any notable features or issues.

### Site Photos (id: 2)
Site Photos: This field is used to upload or provide a link to photos of the site where the wall is located. Please ensure that the photos are clear and relevant to the wall being assessed.

### Wall Feasibility (id: 3)
Wall Feasibility: This field is used to indicate the feasibility of constructing on the wall's site. Please provide a numerical value (1-10) that reflects the wall's feasibility.

### Wall Location (id: 4)
Wall Location: This field is used to describe the location of the wall. Please provide a brief description of the wall's location, including the area and any surrounding structures.

### Wall Characteristics (id: 5)
Wall Characteristics: This field is used to describe any notable characteristics of the wall, such as its material, size, or condition. Please provide a brief description of the wall's characteristics.

### Wall Condition (id: 6)
Wall Condition: This field is used to select the current condition of the wall. Please choose one or more options from the list: Concrete, Brick, Wood, Metal, Glass, or Other.

### Wall Height (id: 7)
Wall Height: This field is used to measure the height of the wall. Please provide a numerical value (in feet).

### Wall Materials (id: 8)
Wall Materials: This field is used to select the materials used in the wall's construction. Please choose one or more options from the list: Concrete, Wood, Metal, Plastic, or Other.

### Wall Width (id: 9)
Wall Width: This field is used to measure the width of the wall. Please provide a numerical value (in feet).

### Wall Thickness (id: 10)
Wall Thickness: This field is used to measure the thickness of the wall. Please provide a numerical value (in inches).

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete responses for all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance.
* Please upload clear and relevant photos to support your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the form administrator.
