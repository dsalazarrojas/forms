# Staff Of The Month Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help identify an employee who stands out for their exceptional work and deserves to be recognized as "Employee of the Month".

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by rating each employee based on their performance in various aspects such as job knowledge, performance, helpfulness, dedication, communication, and email address (if available).
2. For each rating, choose a number between 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
3. Optionally, provide a comment or reason for why the employee deserves to be recognized as "Employee of the Month".
4. Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to review and submit your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you rate the job knowledge of the employee?** (`job_knowledge`, number, required: false)
	+ Rate the employee's job knowledge, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How would you rate the employee's performance?** (`performance`, number, required: false)
	+ Rate the employee's performance, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How would you rate the employee's helpfulness?** (`helpfulness`, number, required: false)
	+ Rate the employee's helpfulness, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How would you rate the employee's dedication?** (`dedication`, number, required: false)
	+ Rate the employee's dedication, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How would you rate the employee's communication?** (`communication`, number, required: false)
	+ Rate the employee's communication, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Employee Email Address** (`email`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter the employee's email address so that we can reach out to them with the news of their nomination.
* **Why does the employee deserve to be employee of the month?** (`comment`, note, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief reason or comment on why the employee deserves to be recognized as "Employee of the Month".
