# Athlete Motivation Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent and gather information from athletes about their motivation and participation in sports research. It is a crucial step in ensuring that athletes understand the study's purpose, requirements, and risks involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal information, such as your full name, age, and sport.
2. Select the correct options for your gender and years of experience in the sport.
3. Read through the research participation terms and confirm your understanding by selecting the corresponding options.
4. Sign the form with your electronic signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Study Title** (id: 2, required, text): Enter the title of the research study you are participating in.
* **Research Institution or University** (id: 3, required, text): Enter the name of the institution or university conducting the research.
* **Lead Researcher Name** (id: 4, required, text): Enter the name of the researcher in charge of the study.
* **Athlete Full Name** (id: 6, required, text): Enter your full name as an athlete.
* **Age** (id: 7, required, number): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (id: 8, required, select_one): Select your gender from the given options.
* **Primary Sport** (id: 9, required, text): Enter the name of the sport you participate in.
* **Years Participating in Sport** (id: 10, required, number): Enter the number of years you have been participating in the sport.
* **Purpose Acknowledgment** (id: 12, required, select_one): Confirm that you understand the purpose and objectives of this motivation research.
* **Requirements Acknowledgment** (id: 13, required, select_one): Confirm that you understand the requirements for participation, such as surveys and interviews.
* **Risks Disclosure** (id: 14, required, select_one): Acknowledge the potential risks and benefits of participation.
* **Privacy Acknowledgment** (id: 15, required, select_one): Confirm that you understand how your data will be stored and kept confidential.
* **Publication Consent** (id: 16, required, select_one): Consent to the use of your anonymized data in research publications.
* **Voluntary Agreement** (id: 17, required, select_one): Confirm that you understand your participation is voluntary and you can stop at any time.
* **Withdrawal Rights** (id: 18, required, select_one): Acknowledge your right to request data removal within the study window.
* **Reason for Participation** (id: 20, required, text): Explain your primary reason for participating in the research.
* **Overall Motivation** (id: 21, required, number): Rate your overall motivation in your sport today.
* **Electronic Signature** (id: 22, required, text): Sign the form with your full name.
* **Date of Signature** (id: 23, required, date): Enter the date of your signature.

Please note: This form is a sample and may not be exhaustive. Always review and update the form according to your specific research study's needs.
