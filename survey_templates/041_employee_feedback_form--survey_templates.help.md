# Employee Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Feedback Form is a tool used by our organization to gather employee feedback and suggestions. It's designed to help us understand how we can improve our work environment and processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into four sections. Please complete each section as instructed below.
2. For the "Overall Satisfaction" section, select a number from 1 to 5 that reflects your current level of satisfaction with your work experience.
3. In the "Who is your Manager?" section, select the name of your supervisor from the list.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in the "Additional Feedback" section.
5. Finally, in the "Have Any Suggestions?" section, type in any ideas or improvements you think would benefit our organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`feedback_score`, Number, Not Required): This field asks for your current level of satisfaction with your work experience, on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Who is your Manager?** (`manager`, Select One, Required): Please select the name of your direct supervisor from the list provided.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback_message`, Note, Optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have regarding your work experience.
* **Have Any Suggestions?** (`feedback_suggestion`, Note, Optional): Provide any ideas or improvements you think would benefit our organization.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when answering the questions.
* Provide concrete suggestions and ideas where possible.
* Your feedback and suggestions will be reviewed by management, and we appreciate your input.

Please note: The form is designed to be completed by employees for internal use only. Your feedback and suggestions will help us improve our work environment and processes.
