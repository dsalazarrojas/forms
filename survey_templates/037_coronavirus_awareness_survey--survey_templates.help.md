# Coronavirus Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Coronavirus Awareness Survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question truthfully and carefully.
2. Select the relevant option from the given choices for each question.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.
4. Submit the survey once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Questions**: This is the title of the survey that appears on the top of the page. Please leave it unchanged.
* **How would you rate your level of concern about Coronavirus?**: This question is a select-one question where you will be given four options: Very High, High, Low, None. Choose the one that best describes your level of concern.
* **Have you ever had any symptoms?**: This question is a select-one question where you will be given two options: Yes, No. Choose the one that is true for you.
* **Prevention methods used**: This question is a select-multiple question where you will be given four options: Wearing face mask, Washing hands frequently, Keeping social distancing, Getting vaccinated. Choose all the options that apply to you.
* **Note**: This is a note field where you can add any additional information or comments about your answer. Please use this field to provide any additional context or details.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your administrator or support team for clarification.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the note field to provide them.
* Thank you for participating in this survey! Your input will help inform public health initiatives.
