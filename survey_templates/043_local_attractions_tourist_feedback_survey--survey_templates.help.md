# local_attractions_tourist_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from tourists who have visited local attractions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Rate your experience at the attraction using the "Rating" field.
4. Provide any comments or feedback you have about your visit in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter the date you visited the attraction in the "Visit Date" field.
6. Select the time you visited the attraction in the "Visit Time" field.
7. Choose the location you visited from the "Visited Location" field.
8. Indicate whether you used any facilities during your visit in the "Facilities" field.
9. Rate the interaction with the staff at the attraction in the "Staff Interaction" field.
10. Select any facilities that were available during your visit in the "Visited Facility" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`note`, optional): This is a placeholder for any introductory information about the form.

### Name
* **Name** (`text`, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to be displayed on any communication with us.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Please enter your email address for us to contact you if necessary.

### Rating
* **Rating** (`select_one`, optional): Please rate your experience at the attraction, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your visit.

### Visit Date
* **Visit Date** (`date`, optional): Please enter the date you visited the attraction.

### Visit Time
* **Visit Time** (`time`, optional): Please select the time you visited the attraction.

### Visited Location
* **Visited Location** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the location you visited from the options provided.

### Visited Facility
* **Visited Facility** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select any facilities you used during your visit.

### Staff Interaction
* **Staff Interaction** (`select_multiple`, optional): Rate the interaction with the staff at the attraction.

### Facilities
* **Facilities** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select any facilities that were available during your visit.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have visited the attraction before completing this form.
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any further feedback or suggestions, please contact us at [insert contact information].
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
