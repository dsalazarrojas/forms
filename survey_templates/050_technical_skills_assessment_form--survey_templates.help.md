# technical skills assessment form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assess an individual's technical skills in various areas. It helps to gather information on your name, contact details, technical experience, and proficiency in programming languages and operating systems. The form is meant to be completed by individuals who wish to showcase their technical skills and expertise.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
	* Email: Enter your valid email address that you check regularly.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further clarification.
	* Experience: If you have any technical experience, please specify the number of years you have been working in the field.
2. Assess your technical skills:
	* Skill Level: Choose one of the two options (High or Low) that best describes your skill level in technical areas.
	* Programming Languages: Indicate your proficiency in programming languages (Yes or No).
	* Operating Systems: Indicate your proficiency in operating systems (Good or Fair).
	* Repeat the same for Programming Languages 2 and Operating Systems 2 (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further clarification.
* **Experience** (number, required/optional): If you have any technical experience, please specify the number of years you have been working in the field.
* **Skill Level** (select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the two options (High or Low) that best describes your skill level in technical areas.
* **Programming Languages** (select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate your proficiency in programming languages (Yes or No).
* **Operating Systems** (select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate your proficiency in operating systems (Good or Fair).
* **Programming Languages 2** (select_multiple, required/optional): Repeat the same as above (Programming Languages).
* **Operating Systems 2** (select_multiple, required/optional): Repeat the same as above (Operating Systems 2).
* **Programming Languages 3** (select_multiple, required/optional): Repeat the same as above (Programming Languages 3).

## Tips

* Please be honest while answering the questions, as this form will help us get a better understanding of your technical skills.
* You can skip any field if you are not comfortable sharing that information.
* If you have any additional comments or clarification, please use the space provided at the end of the form.
