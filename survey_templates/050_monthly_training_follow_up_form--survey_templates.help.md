# Monthly Training Follow Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Training Follow Up Form is designed to track and record details about training sessions, including participants, programs, dates, times, tools, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that the training follow-up process has not been completed already.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address in this format: `example@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, optional): Provide any feedback or comments about the training session.
* **Training Participants** (`training_participants`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether the training has participants.
* **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the training programs that were conducted during the session.
* **Date of Training** (`date_of_training`, `date`, optional): Choose the date of the training session.
* **Time of Training** (`time_of_training`, `time`, optional): Choose the time of the training session.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, optional): Select the tool assigned to the training session.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the category of the training session.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the training session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Leave any additional notes about the training session.
* **Email Sent** (`email_sent`, `text`, optional): Enter the email sent to participants.
* **Next Follow-up** (`next_follow_up`, `date`, optional): Choose the next follow-up date.
* **Next Follow-up Time** (`next_follow_up_time`, `time`, optional): Choose the next follow-up time.
* **Next Follow-up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): Choose the next follow-up date.
* **Next Follow-up Note** (`next_follow_up_note`, `note`, optional): Leave any additional notes about the next follow-up.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the frequency of follow-ups.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the method used for follow-ups.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, `text`, optional): Leave any additional comments about the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, `note`, optional): Leave any additional notes about the follow-up.
* **Follow Up Next Follow Up** (`follow_up_next_follow_up`, `date`, optional): Choose the next follow-up date.
* **Follow Up Follow Up Method** (`follow_up_follow_up_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the method used for next follow-up.
* **Follow Up Follow Up Status** (`follow_up_follow_up_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of next follow-up.
* **Follow-up Next Follow-up** (`next_follow_up_follow_up`, `date`, optional): Choose the next follow-up date.

Note: Some fields are optional, but it is recommended to fill out as much information as possible for accurate tracking.
