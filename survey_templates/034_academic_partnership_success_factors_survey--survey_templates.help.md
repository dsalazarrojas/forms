# Academic Partnership Success Factors Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect feedback and insights from partners on the success factors of academic partnerships. It will help identify areas for improvement and provide a platform for partners to share their experiences and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your role in the partnership from the options provided.
2. Choose the duration of the partnership from the available options (if applicable).
3. Rate the communication effectiveness between partners.
4. Rate the alignment of goals and expectations between partners.
5. Choose the adequacy of resource sharing and support.
6. Describe the key success indicators or metrics of the partnership.
7. List or describe the major barriers or issues faced during the partnership.
8. Provide practical suggestions for improving the partnership.
9. Rate your overall satisfaction with the partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Role** (`partner_role`, select_one, required): This field helps us understand your role in the partnership. Select the option that best describes your position or affiliation.
* **Duration of Partnership** (`duration_of_partnership`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter the duration of the partnership. This will help us understand the longevity and evolution of the partnership.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of communication between partners. Choose from Excellent, Good, Adequate, or Poor.
* **Alignment of Goals and Expectations** (`alignment_of_goals`, select_one, required): Rate how well goals and expectations are aligned between partners. Choose from Very aligned, Somewhat aligned, Neutral, or Misaligned.
* **Resource Sharing and Support** (`resource_sharing`, select_one, optional): Rate the adequacy of resource sharing and support. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Success Metrics and Outcomes** (`success_metrics`, text, optional): Describe or list the key success indicators or metrics of the partnership. This will help us understand the partnership's achievements and progress.
* **Challenges Encountered** (`challenges_encountered`, text, optional): List or describe the major barriers or issues faced during the partnership. This will help us identify areas for improvement.
* **Improvement Recommendations** (`improvement_recommendations`, text, optional): Provide practical suggestions for improving the partnership. This will help us understand your ideas and recommendations for growth.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the partnership. Choose from Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can skip any fields that do not apply to your partnership.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please feel free to describe them in the relevant fields.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey! Your feedback is invaluable to us.
