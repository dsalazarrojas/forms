The Character Dynamics Analysis Survey is designed to help you analyze the interactions and relationships between characters in a story, project, or scenario. This guide will walk you through the purpose and requirements of the form.

**Note:** Before proceeding, ensure you have a clear understanding of the characters' relationships and dynamics. This analysis is best suited for stories, projects, or scenarios where character interactions play a significant role in the narrative.

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the key relationships and interactions between characters in a given story, project, or scenario. The analysis helps identify the power balance, conflicts, and emotional arcs between characters, providing a deeper understanding of their dynamics.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start with a brief overview** of the story, project, or scenario.
2.  **Provide basic details** about the characters involved, such as their names and titles.
3.  **Rate the power balance** between characters to understand their relative influence and control.
4.  **Assess the level of conflict** between characters to determine the intensity of their interactions.
5.  **Describe key interactions** that changed the characters' dynamics.
6.  **Identify the primary source of friction** between characters.
7.  **Map the emotional arcs** of each character to understand their emotional growth and development.
8.  **Confirm the accuracy** of your analysis and provide any additional comments or context.
9.  **Select your role** as the analyst to help with any further support or resources.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Analyst Full Name** (`analyst_full_name`, text, **Required**):
    The name of the person performing the analysis.
*   **Project or Story Title** (`project_or_story_title`, text, **Required**):
    The title of the story, project, or scenario being analyzed.
*   **Character A Name** (`first_character_name`, text, **Required**):
    The name of the first character in the dynamic.
*   **Character B Name** (`second_character_name`, text, **Required**):
    The name of the second character in the dynamic.
*   **Primary Relationship Type** (`type_of_relationship`, select_one, **Required**):
    Select the most accurate description of the primary relationship between characters.
*   **Power Balance Rating** (`power_balance_rating`, number, **Required**):
    Rate from 1 to 5 to determine which character has more influence or control.
*   **Levels of Conflict Rating** (`levels_of_conflict_rating`, number, **Required**):
    Rate from 1 to 5 to determine the level of friction or conflict between characters.
*   **Shared Goals Status** (`shared_goals_status`, select_one, **Required**):
    Determine if characters have identical, overlapping, or opposing goals.
*   **Turning Point Interaction Summary** (`turning_point_interaction_summary`, text, **Required**):
    Describe one interaction that significantly changed the characters' dynamics.
*   **Primary Source of Friction** (`primary_source_of_friction`, select_one, **Required**):
    Identify the main cause of conflict between characters.
*   **Character A Initial Feelings for B** (`initial_emotional_state_a_to_b`, text, **Required**):
    Describe how Character A feels about Character B at the beginning.
*   **Character B Initial Feelings for A** (`initial_emotional_state_b_to_a`, text, **Required**):
    Describe how Character B feels about Character A at the beginning.
*   **Character A Final Feelings for B** (`final_emotional_state_a_to_b`, text, **Required**):
    Describe how Character A feels about Character B at the end.
*   **Character B Final Feelings for A** (`final_emotional_state_b_to_a`, text, **Required**):
    Describe how Character B feels about Character A at the end.
*   **Confirmation of Analysis Completion** (`accuracy_confirmation_analyst`, select_one, **Required**):
    Confirm that the analysis is complete and accurate.
*   **Date of Analysis** (`date_of_analysis_log_submission`, date, **Required**):
    The date of the analysis.
*   **Additional Analysis Comments** (`additional_analysis_comments`, text, **Optional**):
    Additional context or information about the analysis.
*   **Role as Analyst** (`analyst_role_type`, select_one, **Optional**):
    Select your role as the analyst to help with further support or resources.

**Tips:**

*   Be clear and concise in your answers.
*   Focus on the characters' feelings and relationships.
*   Use the provided options when rating and selecting.
*   Double-check your responses for accuracy.
*   Add any additional comments or context to help other analysts understand your analysis.
*   Confirm your analysis to ensure it is complete and accurate.
*   Select your role as analyst for future reference.
*   Date and time stamp your analysis completion for record-keeping purposes.
