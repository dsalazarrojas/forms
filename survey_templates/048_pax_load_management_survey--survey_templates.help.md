# PAX Load Management Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The PAX Load Management Survey Form is a tool used to collect and gather information about passengers' load management experiences on flights. This form is designed to capture specific details about passengers' flight preferences and load management, ensuring a smooth and comfortable travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant flight information, such as flight number and departure date.
2. Provide your passenger information, including your name, contact details, and any relevant travel history.
3. Answer questions about your load management experience, paying attention to any specific instructions and requirements listed in the "load_management" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **flight_information** (`flight_information`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is used to collect details about the flight you are taking. Enter the flight number and departure date to help us better understand your travel experience.
* **passenger_information** (`passenger_information`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is designed to gather your passenger information, including your name and contact details. Please ensure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate efficient communication.
* **load_management** (`load_management`, `text`, required: true):
	+ This field is where you will describe your load management experience. Please answer this question in as much detail as possible, providing any relevant facts, figures, or observations about your experiences with load management.
