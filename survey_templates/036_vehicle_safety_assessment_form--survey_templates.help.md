# Vehicle Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Safety Assessment Form is used to assess the safety of a vehicle, gathering information on various aspects such as vehicle condition, damage, inspection, maintenance, and safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to complete it.
2. Answer all the required questions (marked as "required") to ensure a thorough assessment.
3. For each question, select the correct answer based on the vehicle's condition.
4. Be sure to answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
