# Energy Concepts Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is intended to assist you in completing the Energy Concepts Assessment form. The form is designed to collect your thoughts, opinions, and knowledge about energy concepts, which will help us better understand how you perceive and think about energy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully read through each question and understand what is being asked.
2. For each question, select the most appropriate answer from the provided options. For text fields, describe your answer in 100 words.
3. Ensure you fill out all required fields.
4. Review your answers for completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section is an optional introduction to the form. Feel free to describe any background or context that might be helpful for us to understand your answers.
* **Do you think energy is important?**: Select whether you think energy is important or not. This helps us understand your perspective on energy's significance.
* **How does energy affect your daily life?**: Choose how much energy impacts your daily life. This helps us understand the practical implications of energy on your daily activities.
* **Energy is an important part of our lives**: Select your level of agreement or disagreement on the statement. This helps us understand your opinion on energy's importance.
* **What is your understanding of energy?**: Describe your knowledge or perception of energy in 100 words. This gives us a clear picture of your understanding.
* **Energy is what?**: Describe your comprehension of what energy is in 100 words. This helps us assess your knowledge of basic energy concepts.
* **Energy is renewable or non-renewable?**: Select all the types of energy that you consider renewable or non-renewable. This helps us understand your classification of energy sources.
* **What is your understanding of energy efficiency?**: Describe your knowledge or perception of energy efficiency in 100 words. This helps us gauge your grasp on energy conservation.
* **What is your understanding of energy sources?**: Describe your knowledge or perception of energy sources in 100 words. This helps us assess your understanding of energy sources.
* **What is your understanding of energy conversion?**: Describe your knowledge or perception of energy conversion in 100 words. This helps us gauge your comprehension of energy transformation.
* **What is your understanding of energy storage?**: Describe your knowledge or perception of energy storage in 100 words. This helps us understand your understanding of storing energy.
* **Energy sources can be harnessed to generate electricity**: Select all the energy sources that you consider can be used to generate electricity. This helps us understand your opinion on energy sources that can produce electricity.
* **Page 2** to **Page 4**: These sections are intended to provide additional context or space for you to elaborate on your answers. Please fill them out if necessary.

## Tips

* Answer honestly, and please take your time to provide clear and concise answers.
* For text fields, keep your answers within the 100-word limit.
* Make sure to review your answers before submitting to ensure completeness.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to ask.
