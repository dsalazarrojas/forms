# Topic Suggestion Form For Sharing Session - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Topic Suggestion Form for Sharing Session is designed to collect relevant information from users about their suggested topics for sharing during a session. This form ensures that the session organizers are aware of potential discussion topics and can plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Session Details section with the relevant information about the session you are participating in.
2. In the Topics section, provide a brief description of the topics you would like to share during the session.
3. In the Session Topics section, indicate whether the session topics are relevant or not.
4. In the Topic Suggestions section, provide any additional topic suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details (session_details)**: A text field where you can input any relevant information about the session you are participating in. This could include the session name, date, or any other details that might be helpful.
* **Topics (topics)**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of the topics you would like to share during the session. Keep this concise and focused on the main ideas you want to discuss.
* **Session Topics (session_topics)**: A multiple-choice field where you must indicate whether the session topics are relevant or not. Select 'Yes' if the topics are relevant, or 'No' if they are not.
* **Topic Suggestions (topic_suggestions)**: A text field where you can provide any additional topic suggestions you may have. This is an optional field, but feel free to use it to suggest any further ideas you think would be valuable for the session.
