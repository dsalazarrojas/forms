# Heat Stress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about employee experiences with heat stress in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*), with your relevant details.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure the information is accurate and complete.
3. For select_one, select_multiple, and select_multiple fields, choose the relevant options that apply to your situation.
4. For number fields, enter the numerical value as instructed.
5. For time fields, enter the start time of your shift in the format HH:MM or 24-hour format.
6. For email fields, enter your work email address.
7. Fill in the submitter information at the end of the form, including your name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Heat Stress Feedback** (1, User Input): This field is for providing feedback on your personal experience with heat stress. Please provide any relevant comments or observations about your heat stress experience.
* **Role** (2, Select One): Choose your role in the workplace, such as Manager, Crew leader, Worker, or Other.
* **Workplace Location** (3, Select One): Indicate where you work, such as Factory Floor, Office, Warehouse, or Other.
* **Shift Length** (4, Number): Enter the length of your shift in hours.
* **Shift Start Time** (5, Time): Enter the start time of your shift in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Break Length** (6, Number): Enter the length of your break in minutes.
* **Body Temperature** (7, Number): Enter your body temperature in degrees Celsius (°C).
* **Heat Stress Score** (8, Number): Rate your level of heat stress from 0 (no stress) to 10 ( extreme stress).
* **Heat Exposure** (9, Select Multiple): Check all the times you were exposed to heat, such as True or False.
* **Heat Stress Symptoms** (10, Select Multiple): Check all the symptoms you experienced, such as Headache, Dizziness, Nausea, Fatigue, Confusion, Irritability, or Other.
* **Heat Exposure Prevention Methods** (11, Select Multiple): Check all the methods you used to prevent heat exposure, such as Staying hydrated, Wearing lightweight, loose-fitting clothing, Taking regular breaks, or Other.
* **Comments** (12, Text): Add any additional comments about your heat stress experience.
* **Submitter Name** (13, Text): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter E-mail** (14, Email): Enter your work email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (15, Text): Enter your phone number.
