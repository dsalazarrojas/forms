# OB GYN Event Attendance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and data on Obstetrics and Gynecology (OB GYN) events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Name" field with your name.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3.  Select the sessions you attended using the "Session Attendance" dropdowns.
4.  Rate your overall satisfaction on the scale.
5.  If desired, provide feedback on your experience.
6.  List any additional attendees in the "List of Attendees" field.
7.  Select any attendees who attended from the "Attendees" dropdowns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (id: 9)**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email (id: 7)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Session Attendance (id: 2)**: Select the sessions you attended using the dropdowns.
* **Select all sessions attended (id: 3)**: Confirm your selection of sessions.
* **Overall Satisfaction (id: 4)**: Rate your overall satisfaction on the scale.
* **Scale (id: 5)**: Choose your rating using the provided scale.
* **Additional Feedback (id: 6)**: Provide any additional feedback on your experience.
* **List of Attendees (id: 10)**: List any additional attendees who attended the event.
* **Attendees (id: 11)**: Select any attendees who attended the event.
* **Select all attendees (id: 12)**: Confirm your selection of attendees.
