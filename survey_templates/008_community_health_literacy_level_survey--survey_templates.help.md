# Community Health Literacy Level Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Health Literacy Level Survey is designed to gauge the level of health literacy among community members. This survey will help us understand how well community members understand health and nutrition concepts, their preferences for health information sources, and their level of comfort in interacting with healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in a clear and honest manner. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully, as your responses will help us better understand the needs and preferences of our community.
2. For questions that require a selection, choose the option that best describes your situation or preference.
3. If you have any questions or concerns while filling out the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age (Page 2)**: Please enter your age in numerical format.
* **Primary Language Spoken at Home (Page 3)**: Enter the language(s) you primarily speak at home.
* **Self-rated Knowledge of Nutrition and Healthy Eating (Page 5)**: Rate your knowledge of nutrition and healthy eating on a scale from Very High to Very Low.
* **Self-rated Knowledge of Chronic Disease Prevention (Page 6)**: Rate your knowledge of chronic disease prevention on a scale from Very High to Very Low.
* **Where do you primarily find health information? (Page 7)**: Select all sources where you usually find health information, such as the Internet, family doctor, or social media.
* **How easy is it for you to understand medical labels or prescriptions? (Page 8)**: Rate your understanding of medical labels or prescriptions on a scale from Very Easy to Very Difficult.
* **How comfortable are you asking your doctor questions? (Page 9)**: Rate your comfort level in asking questions to your doctor on a scale from Very Comfortable to Uncomfortable.
* **Are you interested in attending health literacy workshops? (Page 10)**: Indicate your interest in attending health literacy workshops.
* **Highest Level of Education Completed (Page 12)**: Select the highest level of education you have completed.
* **Preferred health topics for future workshops (Page 11)**: Describe the health topics you would like to learn about in future workshops.
