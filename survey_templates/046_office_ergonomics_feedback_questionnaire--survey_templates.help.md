## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How Comfortable Are You?** (comfort_level, number, optional): This question asks you to rate how comfortable you feel in your current workspace. Please select a number from 1 to 10, where 1 means "very uncomfortable" and 10 means "very comfortable".
* **What Do You Think Is Best Implemented?** (workplace_ergonomics, select_multiple, optional): This question asks you to select from the options provided what you think would be the best implementation to improve the ergonomics of your workspace.
* **Are There Any Ergonomic Issues In Your Workspace?** (workplace_ergonomics_issues, select_one, optional): Please select "Yes" if you have any ergonomic issues in your workspace, "No" if you don't, or "Not Sure" if you're not sure.
* **What Improvements Do You Suggest For Your Workspace?** (ergonomic_improvements, text, optional): This is a free-text field where you can suggest any improvements you think would make your workspace more comfortable and efficient.
* **Suggestions For Improvement** (suggestions_for_improvement, text, optional): Similar to the previous question, please provide any suggestions you have for improving your workspace.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): If you want to provide any additional comments or feedback, please feel free to do so.
* **Name** (name, text, optional): Please provide your full name.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Please provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): If you want to provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Job Title** (job_title, select_one, optional): Please select your job title from the options provided.
* **Department** (department, select_one, optional): Please select your department from the options provided.
* **Supervisor Name** (supervisor_name, text, optional): Please provide the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (supervisor_email, email, optional): Please provide the email address of your supervisor.
* **Employee ID** (employee_id, text, optional): Please provide your employee ID.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Please provide the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Please provide the time you completed this form.
* **Submitted At** (submitted_at, time, optional): Please provide the time you submitted this form.
* **Completed On** (completed_on, date, optional): Please provide the date you completed this form.
* **Submitted On** (submitted_on, date, optional): Please provide the date you submitted this form.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): If you want to provide any additional comments or feedback, please feel free to do so.
* **Ergo Score** (ergo_score, number, optional): If you want to provide a score for your comfort level.
* **Ergo Score Comment** (ergo_score_comment, text, optional): Please provide any comments for your ergo score.
* **Submitted** (submitted, select_one, optional): Please select "Yes" if you have submitted this form, "No" if you haven't, or "Not Sure" if you're not sure.
