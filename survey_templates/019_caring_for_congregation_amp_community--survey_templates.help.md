# Caring For Congregation & Community - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate communication and assistance between members of the congregation and community. It helps to gather information about personal details, needs or offers, and other relevant information to provide effective support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the relevant sections for your purpose: If you are seeking assistance, please choose the "Assistance" sections, and if you wish to offer help, please choose the "Volunteering" sections.
2. Fill out the required fields for your chosen section, making sure to provide accurate and detailed information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Full Name
* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address, ensuring we can contact you for further information or assistance.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): This is your contact number where we can reach out for support or clarification.

### Physical Address
* **Physical Address** (`Physical Address`, `text`, optional): This will be helpful if you require assistance or volunteering that is location-based, such as transportation or delivery services.

### Type of Interaction
* **How would you like to interact today?** (`Type of Interaction`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options: 'I need assistance', 'I would like to volunteer or offer help', or 'I have a general inquiry'.

### Assistance Needed
* **If you need help, what category?** (`Assistance Category`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all that apply from the options: 'Grocery or Pharmacy delivery', 'Prayer request', 'Financial assistance', 'Transportation', 'Home maintenance', 'Mental health support'.

### Detailed Information
* **Please provide more details about your request** (`Detailed Need`, `text`, optional): Provide a clear and specific description of the help you require.

### Urgency Level
* **How urgent is this request?** (`Urgency Level`, `select_one`, optional): Choose from the options: 'Standard', 'Urgent', 'Immediate'.

### Volunteering and Offers
* **If you are offering help, what categories do you have skills or resources for?** (`Volunteer Category`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all that apply from the options: 'Food preparation', 'Driving', 'Professional services', 'Labor or Manual work', 'Admin support', 'Friendly check-ins'.

### Availability
* **When are you available to help?** (`Availability`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all that apply from the options: 'Weekdays', 'Evenings', 'Weekends', 'On-call'.

### Skills Description
* **Brief description of skills or resources you can offer** (`Skills Description`, `text`, optional): Describe your skills or resources in a few sentences.

### Connection
* **Are you a member of this congregation?** (`Congregation Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options: 'True', 'False', 'Regular Attendee', 'Visiting'.

### Mailing List
* **Would you like to be added to our mailing list?** (`Connection Interest`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options: 'True', 'False'.

### Referral Source
* **How did you hear about this program?** (`Referral Source`, `text`, optional): Provide details on how you were introduced to the program.

### Additional Comments
* **Any other comments or prayer requests?** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional thoughts or requests you have.

### Privacy Confirmation
* **I agree to have my information shared with the relevant parish team** (`Privacy Confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options: 'True', 'False'.
