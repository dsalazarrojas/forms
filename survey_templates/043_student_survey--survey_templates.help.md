# student_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The student survey is designed to capture your feedback on your educational experience. It's used to help us understand your satisfaction and opinions on various aspects of your education, which will inform improvements to enhance your learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly to provide a comprehensive view of your experience.
2. Select the relevant option for each question.
3. If you need help with a question, you can refer to the field explanations below.
4. Submit your answers when you've completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_survey_1** (`text`, optional): This is the title of your comment or answer to the question on page 1.
* **curriculum** (`select_one`, optional): Select one of the options to describe your level of satisfaction with the curriculum: 
  - Very Satisfied
  - Somewhat Satisfied
  - Not Satisfied
  - Very Dissatisfied
* **facilities** (`text`, optional): Describe your experience with the facilities, such as classrooms, equipment, etc.
* **materials** (`text`, optional): Share your thoughts on the materials and resources provided, such as textbooks, online resources, etc.
* **time** (`time`, optional): Provide a specific time or date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` to reflect on your experience at that time.
