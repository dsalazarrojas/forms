# Chat Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback about your experience with our chat app. Your responses will help us understand what features you use most, your satisfaction with the app's performance and usability, and how we can improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information and answers.
2. Use the dropdown menus to select your responses for each question.
3. Type your answers in the text fields.
4. Click on "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be identified in this feedback.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **How Often Do You Use Chat** (`usage_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you use the chat app.
	+ Daily
	+ Several Times a Week
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ First Time
* **Most Frequently Used Feature** (`most_used_feature`, select_one, required): Select the feature you use most often.
	+ Direct Messages
	+ Group Chats
	+ Voice Calls
	+ File Sharing
	+ Search Function
	+ Other
* **Satisfaction with Features** (`feature_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the features.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Missing Features or Functions** (`missing_features`, text, optional): Enter any features or functions you think are missing from our chat app.
* **Ease of Use** (`ease_of_use`, select_one, required): Select how easy you find the app to use.
	+ Very Easy
	+ Easy
	+ Moderate
	+ Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **Interface Design and Layout** (`interface_design`, select_one, required): Rate the app's design and layout.
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
* **Navigation and Menu Structure** (`navigation`, select_one, optional): Select how easy it is to navigate the app.
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
* **App Speed and Responsiveness** (`app_speed`, select_one, required): Select how fast the app is.
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Slow
* **App Stability and Reliability** (`stability`, select_one, required): Rate the app's stability and reliability.
	+ Very Reliable
	+ Reliable
	+ Mostly Reliable
	+ Unreliable
* **Crash or Technical Issue Frequency** (`crash_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you experience problems with the app.
	+ Never
	+ Rarely
	+ Sometimes
	+ Often
	+ Very Often
* **Overall Satisfaction with Chat App** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the chat app.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Likelihood to Recommend to Others** (`likelihood_recommend`, select_one, required): Select how likely you are to recommend the app to others.
	+ Definitely
	+ Probably
	+ Maybe
	+ Unlikely
* **Best Aspects of the Chat App** (`best_aspects`, text, optional): Enter what you like most about the app.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Enter any suggestions you have for improving the app.

Note: This form is for your feedback, and your responses will be kept confidential.
