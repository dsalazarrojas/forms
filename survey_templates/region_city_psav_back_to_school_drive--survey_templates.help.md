# (Region/City) PSAV Back To School Drive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The (Region/City) PSAV Back To School Drive form is designed to gather participant information, learning style, and needs for an upcoming back-to-school drive. The collected data will help us provide the right support and resources to students in need.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and fill out each section according to the instructions.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as 'true' in the form metadata).
3. Provide accurate and clear information in each field.
4. If you need help or have questions, do not hesitate to reach out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, phone, optional): Include your country code if you're outside the US. Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Provide your date of birth if you're registering a minor.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, text, required): Enter your current grade level, e.g., '3rd Grade', 'Year 10', 'Freshman'.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your school or institution name if applicable.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, optional): Enter your street address for delivery purposes, if applicable.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, integer, optional): Enter your postal/zip code for delivery purposes.
* **Primary Subject Area** (`subject_area`, select_one, optional): Choose the subject area most relevant to your needs.
* **Learning Style** (`learning_style`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred learning style.
* **Average Study Hours Per Week** (`hours_studied`, integer, optional): Enter your approximate study hours per week.
* **Items Requested** (`items_requested`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the supplies you need, e.g., 'Notebooks', 'Pens / Pencils', etc.
* **Total Quantity of Items Requested** (`total_items_requested`, integer, optional): Enter the total quantity of each item requested.
* **Will You Pick Up the Supplies or Need Delivery** (`pickup_needed`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to receive the supplies.
* **Preferred Pickup Location** (`preferred_pickup_location`, select_one, optional): Select the most convenient distribution site if picking up.
* **Interest in Volunteering with the Drive** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you're interested in volunteering.
* **Preferred Volunteer Time** (`volunteer_time`, time, optional): Provide a time range if you're available to volunteer.
* **Consent to be Acknowledged in Publicity** (`photo_publicity_consent`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' to allow recognition posts with your first name and city.
* **Additional Comments or Special Needs** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Include any accessibility needs, allergy info, or delivery notes.
