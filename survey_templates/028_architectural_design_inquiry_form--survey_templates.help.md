# Architectural Design Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Architectural Design Inquiry Form is designed to gather essential information about your project, allowing us to provide tailored design services that meet your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer each question thoroughly, and if you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask.
2.  Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can serve your project needs effectively.
3.  If you have any attachments or documents that support your project details, please attach them where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, text, required): Enter your professional email address where you'd like us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Provide your primary contact number for project discussions.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you're representing a company or organization, please provide their name.
* **Project Category** (`project_category`, select_one, required): Choose the type of project you're initiating (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Institutional, Mixed-use, Other).
* **Specific Project Type** (`specific_project_type`, select_one, required): Select the specific type of project you're undertaking (New building, Renovation, Addition, Interior redesign, Feasibility study, Master planning).
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Briefly describe your project, including its objectives and any relevant details.
* **Primary Goals for This Project** (`primary_goals`, text, required): Describe the primary goals and objectives of your project.
* **Target Users or Occupants** (`target_users`, text, optional): Specify who will use or occupy the space.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, required): Enter the city, region, or address where the project will take place.
* **Estimated Square Footage** (`estimated_area`, number, optional): Provide the estimated square footage of the project.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, number, optional): Enter your estimated budget for the project.
* **Desired Project Timeline** (`timeline`, date, optional): Specify when you'd like to begin the project.
* **Preferred Design Style** (`design_style`, text, optional): Describe your preferred design style (e.g., Modern, Traditional, Contemporary).
* **Sustainability or Green Building Priorities** (`sustainability_priorities`, text, optional): Specify any sustainability or green building priorities for your project.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Indicate what you'd like to discuss further or what you'd like us to focus on next.
