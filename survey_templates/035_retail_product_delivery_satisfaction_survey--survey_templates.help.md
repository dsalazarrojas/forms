# Retail Product Delivery Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their delivery experience with a retail product.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out each field as instructed.
3. Submit the form when you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How was your delivery experience? (1)**: Describe your overall experience with the delivery process.
* **What is your impression of the product quality? (2)**: Rate your impression of the product quality (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **How quickly was your order delivered? (3)**: Choose the time frame in which your order was delivered (1-2 business days, 3-4 business days, 5-6 business days, 7 or more business days).
* **How satisfying was the delivery experience in terms of speed? (4)**: Rate how satisfying your delivery experience was in terms of speed.
* **How improving was the delivery process compared to last time? (5)**: Rate how improving the delivery process was compared to last time.
* **Overall delivery experience rating (6)**: Rate your overall delivery experience using a number.
* **Who was your contact person? (7)**: Type the name of the contact person.
* **How did you contact the person? (8)**: Choose how you contacted the person (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Overall satisfaction score? (9)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the product and delivery process using a number.
* **Do you have any suggestions? (10)**: Type any suggestions you may have.
* **How likely are you to recommend our store to a friend or colleague? (11)**: Rate how likely you are to recommend the store.
* **What is the name of the store where you received your order? (12)**: Type the name of the store.
* **What is the store address where you received your order? (13)**: Type the store address.
* **What is the store number where you received your order? (14)**: Type the store number.
* **Date received? (15)**: Type the date you received your order.
* **Time received? (16)**: Type the time you received your order.
* **Email address? (17)**: Type your email address.
* **Phone number? (18)**: Type your phone number.
