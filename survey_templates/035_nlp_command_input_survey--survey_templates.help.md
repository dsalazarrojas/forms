# NLP Command Input Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from NLP team members and users about their experiences with natural language commands.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your role from the list of options provided in the "What is your role" field. This will help us understand your perspective on natural language commands.
2. Indicate how often you use natural language commands in your work by selecting a number from the options provided in the "How often do you use natural language commands in your work" field.
3. Choose all the challenges you face while using natural language commands from the list of options provided in the "Most significant challenge faced while using natural language commands" field. You may select multiple options.
4. In the "What would you like to see improved in natural language commands" field, describe your suggestion in as much detail as possible. This field is optional.
5. Enter a number to indicate how important it is to understand the context in which natural language commands are used in the "How important is it to understand the context in which natural language commands are used" field.
6. Finally, select how important it is to you that commands are well-documented by choosing a number from the options provided in the "How important is it to you that commands are well-documented" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your role** (Select One): This field is used to identify your role in the NLP team or your perspective on natural language commands.
* **How often do you use natural language commands in your work** (Select One): This field helps us understand how frequently you use natural language commands in your work.
* **Most significant challenge faced while using natural language commands** (Select Multiple): This field allows you to indicate the difficulties you face while using natural language commands.
* **What would you like to see improved in natural language commands** (Text): This field is optional and allows you to provide suggestions for improvements.
* **How important is it to understand the context in which natural language commands are used** (Number): This field requires you to enter a number to indicate the importance of understanding the context in which natural language commands are used.
* **How important is it to you that commands are well-documented** (Select One): This field helps us understand how important it is for you that commands are well-documented.
