# Public Service Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the quality of public services provided to you. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the services we offer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your **Name** in the first field. This is a required field and should be filled in with your full name.
2. Enter your **Email** in the second field. This is also a required field, which will be used to contact you if we need more information or clarification on your feedback.
3. Rate your **Rating Score** in the third field. This rating is out of 5 points, and you can choose a number between 0 and 5 to reflect your level of satisfaction with the service.
4. Choose your **Service Quality** rating from the options in the fourth field. This helps us understand your experience with the service provider's quality of service.
5. Select all that apply for **Service Satisfaction** in the fifth field. Choose from the options to indicate your level of satisfaction with the service.
6. If you have any **Improvement Areas**, please select all that apply from the options in the sixth field. This helps us identify areas where we can improve the service.
7. Enter your **Comments** in the seventh field. This is a required field where you can provide detailed feedback on your experience with the service.
8. Finally, enter your overall **Rating** in the eighth field. This is out of 5 points, and you can choose a number between 0 and 5 to reflect your overall satisfaction with the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (required, text): Enter your full name to help us identify you and understand who is providing feedback.
* **Email** (required, email): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Rating Score** (required, number): Rate your experience with the service on a scale of 0 to 5 points.
* **Service Quality** (select one, optional): Choose Excellent, Good, Poor, or Very Poor to reflect your experience with the service provider's quality.
* **Service Satisfaction** (select multiple, optional): Select all that apply from Highly Satisified, Satisified, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied to indicate your level of satisfaction with the service.
* **Improvement Areas** (select multiple, optional): Select all that apply from Communication, Responsiveness, Timeliness, or Other to help us understand areas for improvement.
* **Comments** (required, note): Provide detailed feedback on your experience with the service.
* **Rating** (required, number): Enter a number between 0 and 5 to reflect your overall satisfaction with the service.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback to help us improve our services.
* If you have already filled out this form for the same service provider, do not fill it out again to avoid duplication of effort.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us at [insert contact information].
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in providing it.
