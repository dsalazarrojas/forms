# Team Process Mapping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Process Mapping Survey is designed to help teams map and document their existing processes, identify challenges, and share improvement ideas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand the purpose and questions being asked.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the text fields to provide explanations and examples to support your answers.
4. If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for clarification or help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Process Mapping Survey** (`1`, `text`, optional): This is the main section title, where you can provide a brief description of the process you are mapping.
* **Team Process Mapping Survey Form** (`2`, `text`, optional): This is the form title, which can be a short and descriptive name for the process you are mapping.
* **Team Process Mapping Survey Workflow** (`3`, `text`, optional): Here, describe the high-level steps of your current process, using bullet points or numbered lists as needed.
* **Team Process Mapping Survey Capture Challenges** (`4`, `text`, optional): Identify any challenges or issues you are facing in your current process, and describe the impact they have on your team or organization.
* **Team Process Mapping Survey Improvement Ideas** (`5`, `text`, optional): Share your ideas and suggestions for improving your process, including any changes you would like to make, new tools or methods to adopt, or new policies to implement.
* **Team Process Mapping Survey Capture Improvement Ideas** (`6`, `text`, optional): This is similar to the previous field, where you can share your ideas and suggestions for improving your process.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as this will help the team get a accurate picture of their processes.
* Use examples and anecdotes to illustrate your points and make your answers more engaging.
* If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for clarification or help.
* Remember that this form is a tool to help the team improve, so be open-minded and willing to share your thoughts and ideas.
