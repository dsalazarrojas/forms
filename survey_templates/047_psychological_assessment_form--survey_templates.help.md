# Psychological Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on a person's mental state, goals, and experiences. It helps identify patterns and trends in mental health status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
* Answer each question to the best of your ability.
* You can skip questions that do not apply to you or that you do not feel comfortable answering.
* If you need help with a question, you can ask the person administering the form for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: What is your current mental state?
* **What is your current mental state?** (`text`, optional): This question asks about your current emotional state, such as feeling sad, anxious, or happy.
*   Please describe your current mental state as accurately as possible.

### Page 2: What are your goals?
* **What are your goals?** (`text`, optional): This question asks about your objectives, aspirations, or things you hope to achieve in the near future.
*   Write down your short-term and long-term goals.

### Page 3: Answers to questions on page 2
* **Answers to questions on page 2** (`text`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or explanations related to your goals.
*   Please type any additional thoughts, insights, or context that may help clarify your goals.

### Page 4: How long have you been experiencing these feelings?
* **How long have you been experiencing these feelings?** (`text`, optional): This question asks about the duration of your current emotional state or feelings.
*   Please specify the approximate time frame during which you have been experiencing these feelings.

### Page 5: Answers to questions on page 4
* **Answers to questions on page 4** (`text`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or insights related to the duration of your feelings.
*   Please provide any relevant context or details about how long you have been experiencing these feelings.

### Page 6: What are you hoping for?
* **What are you hoping for?** (`text`, optional): This question asks about your desires, aspirations, or expectations for the future.
*   Describe your hopes and expectations in as much detail as possible.

### Page 7: Answers to questions on page 6
* **Answers to questions on page 6** (`text`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or insights related to your hopes and expectations.
*   Please provide any relevant context or details about what you are hoping for.

### Page 8: How long have you been feeling this way?
* **How long have you been feeling this way?** (`text`, optional): This question asks about the duration of your hopes and expectations.
*   Please specify the approximate time frame during which you have been feeling this way.

### Page 9: Answers to questions on page 8
* **Answers to questions on page 8** (`text`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or insights related to the duration of your hopes and expectations.
*   Please provide any relevant context or details about how long you have been feeling this way.
