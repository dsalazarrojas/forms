# Mobile Network Connectivity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback on mobile network connectivity in a specific area. It will help identify areas where users are experiencing issues and provide insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each question and answer the questions truthfully. You can choose from the provided options or type in your response if none of the options match your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mobile Network Connectivity (1)** (`mobile_network_connectivity`, text, required: false): Describe your current mobile network connectivity experience in this area.
* **Location (2)** (`location`, text, required: false): Provide the location where you experienced your mobile network issues.
* **Mobile Coverage (3)** (`mobile_coverage`, number, required: false): On a scale of 1-5, rate your satisfaction with your mobile coverage in this area. A rating of 1 means poor coverage, while 5 means excellent coverage.
* **Mobile Reliability (4)** (`mobile_reliability`, number, required: false): On a scale of 1-5, rate your satisfaction with the reliability of your mobile network in this area. A rating of 1 means frequent disconnections, while 5 means no disconnections.
* **Mobile Satisfaction (5)** (`mobile_satisfaction`, number, required: false): On a scale of 1-5, rate your overall satisfaction with your mobile network in this area. A rating of 1 means very dissatisfied, while 5 means very satisfied.
* **Issue Description (6)** (`issue_description`, text, required: false): If you experienced any issues with your mobile network in this area, describe the problems you faced.
* **Mobile Network Issue (7)** (`mobile_network_issue`, select_one, required: false): If you experienced issues with your mobile network, what type of issue did you encounter? Choose one of the following options: coverage, data, voice, video, or voice video.
* **Network Type (8)** (`network_type`, select_multiple, required: false): What type of network do you have in this area? Choose one or more of the following options: 4G, 3G, 2G, Wi-Fi, or WiMAX.

## Tips
* Answer truthfully to provide valuable insights.
* Choose the most accurate response for each question.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Issue Description" field.
