# Emergency Room Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a patient's experience in an emergency room. It is intended to help medical providers understand and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "emergency_room_experience_survey" page to begin the form.
2. Choose the category of care the patient received (Cardiac, Neurologic, Surgical, or Other).
3. Enter the name of the provider who delivered the care.
4. Select the date and time of the patient's visit.
5. Rate the patient's experience as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
6. Provide any additional feedback about the patient's experience.
7. Choose whether the patient received care (Yes or No).
8. Enter the patient's email address (if available).
9. Enter the patient's phone number (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (id: 2): Choose the category of care the patient received.
* **Provider Name** (id: 3): Enter the name of the provider who delivered the care.
* **Visit Date** (id: 4): Select the date the patient visited the emergency room.
* **Visit Time** (id: 5): Select the time the patient visited the emergency room.
* **Rating** (id: 6): Rate the patient's experience as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feedback** (id: 7): Provide any additional feedback about the patient's experience.
* **Patient Name** (id: 8): Choose whether the patient received care (Yes or No).
* **Email** (id: 9): Enter the patient's email address (if available).
* **Phone** (id: 10): Enter the patient's phone number (if available).

## Tips

* Please ensure that the form is only completed by authorized personnel.
* The form should only be accessed to gather data about a patient's experience in an emergency room.
* If a field is not relevant, please leave it blank.
* Be honest and accurate when completing the form.
