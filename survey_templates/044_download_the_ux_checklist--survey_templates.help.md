# Download The UX Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and provide a download link for the UX design checklist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the "First name" field. This field is not required but helps us get a better understanding of your name.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This field is required to ensure we can contact you with the download link.
3. Enter your **Phone number** (if any) in the "Phone number" field. This field is not required but helps us get in touch with you in case of any further questions.
4. Enter the **Download link** in the "Download link" field. This field is not required but helps us understand where you'd like to download the checklist from.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address here. This will help us get in touch with you about the download link.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): If you have a phone number, enter it here so we can get in touch with you.
* **Download link** (`download_link`, text, not required): If you know the link to download the checklist, enter it here. This is optional, we can also send it to you.
