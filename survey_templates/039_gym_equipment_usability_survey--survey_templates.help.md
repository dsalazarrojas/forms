# Gym Equipment Usability Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is to gather feedback from gym users on their interaction with the equipment and facilities. Your input will help the gym management to better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience.
3. You can also provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction**
(`Introduction`, `note`, required: `false`)

This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### 2. **How often do you use the equipment?**
(`equipment_usability`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)

This question asks about your frequency of use of the gym equipment. Please select all the options that apply to your experience:

* Rarely
* Occasionally
* Often

### 3. **How often is the equipment available?**
(`equipment_availability`, `select_one`, required: `false`)

This question inquires about the availability of the equipment. Please select the option that best describes your experience:

* Always
* Sometimes
* Never

### 4. **What would you like to see improved?**
(`facility_improvements`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)

This question asks for your suggestions on potential improvements to the gym facilities. Please select all the options that apply:

* Equipment
* Flooring
* Hours of operation
* Other (please specify below)

### 5. **Other Improvement Suggestions**
(`other_improvement`, `text`, required: `false`)

If you have any other suggestions that are not listed above, please provide them here. This is your chance to share any thoughts or ideas that are not included in the previous question.

### 6. **Introduction** (second part)
(`introduction2`, `note`, required: `false`)

Another brief note to reiterate the purpose of this survey.

### 7. **Thank you**
(`thank_you`, `note`, required: `false`)

Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
