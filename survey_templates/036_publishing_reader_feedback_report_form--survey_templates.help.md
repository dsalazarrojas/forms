# Publishing Reader Feedback Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to collect feedback from readers on the publication's content, including their opinions on the magazine's name, the current issue, and specific articles. The feedback will help the publication's team to understand reader opinions and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the magazine's name in the "Magazine Name" field.
2. Answer the question "What do you think about our current issue?" in the "What do you think about our current issue?" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
4. Rate the current issue in the "How would you rate this issue?" field.
5. Provide comments on the current issue in the "Comments" field.
6. Indicate whether you agree or disagree with the publication's content in the "Do you agree with what we publish?" field.
7. Choose your favorite section from the list of options in the "What section is your favorite?" field.
8. Enter your feedback on the date you received the current issue.
9. Enter the time you received the current issue.
10. Indicate whether you agree or disagree with the publication's content in the "Do you generally agree or disagree with what we publish?" field.
11. Choose how you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
12. Choose your preferred method of contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Magazine Name**: Enter the name of the magazine you are providing feedback on.
* **What do you think about our current issue?**: Share your thoughts on the current issue of the magazine.
* **Email**: If you would like to be contacted for further discussion, please enter your email address.
* **How would you rate this issue?**: Rate the current issue on a scale of 0-5.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback you have on the current issue.
* **Do you agree with what we publish?**: Indicate whether you generally agree or disagree with the publication's content.
* **What section is your favorite?**: Choose a section from the list of options (News, Reviews, Features).
* **Date**: Enter the date you received the current issue.
* **Time**: Enter the time you received the current issue.
* **Do you generally agree or disagree with what we publish?**: Indicate whether you generally agree or disagree with the publication's content.
* **Would you like to be contacted for further discussion?**: Choose whether you would like to be contacted or not.
* **How would you like to be contacted?**: Choose your preferred method of contact (Email, Phone, None).
