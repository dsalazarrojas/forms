# Board Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Board Performance Survey is a self-assessment tool designed to evaluate the performance of a board of directors or governing body in an organization. The survey aims to gather information on various aspects of the board's functioning, including:

* Attendance and engagement
* Effectiveness in decision-making
* Communication and clarity
* Financial oversight
* Alignment with the organization's mission and values

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Board Member Name**: This is a text field where you can provide your name as a board member.
2. **Assessment Header**: This is a note field providing context and introduction to the survey questions.
3. **Attendance Rating**: Rate your attendance at board meetings over the last year using the scale:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
4. **Board Effectiveness Header**: This is a note field providing context and introduction to the board effectiveness questions.
5. **Strategic Focus Rating**: Rate your opinion on whether the board spends enough time on strategic issues rather than operational details using the scale:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
6. **Communication Rating**: Rate your opinion on the clarity and timeliness of communication between board members and management using the scale:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
7. **Decision Making Rating**: Rate your opinion on whether board decisions are made efficiently and effectively using the scale:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
8. **Organizational Health Header**: This is a note field providing context and introduction to the financial oversight questions.
9. **Financial Oversight Rating**: Rate your opinion on whether the board provides effective oversight of the organization's health using the scale:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
10. **Mission Alignment Rating**: Rate your opinion on whether organizational activities are aligned with the board's mission and values using the scale:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
11. **Open Feedback Header**: This is a note field providing context and introduction to the open feedback questions.
12. **Board Strength**: Provide your answer to the question "What is the board's greatest strength?"
13. **Board Improvement**: Provide your answer to the question "What is one area where the board could improve its performance?"
14. **Additional Feedback**: Provide any other comments or feedback you may have for the board leadership.
15. **Survey Date**: Select a date when you completed this survey.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when answering the questions.
* Use the provided scale for the ratings questions.
* Provide specific examples or explanations when answering the open-ended questions.
* Ensure all questions are answered before submitting the survey.

This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to complete. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
