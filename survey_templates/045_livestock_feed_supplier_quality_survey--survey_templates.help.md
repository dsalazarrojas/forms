# Livestock Feed Supplier Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback about the quality of services provided by livestock feed suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely for each supplier you are evaluating.
2. Enter the supplier's name in the `Supplier Name` field.
3. Enter the contact name and email address of the person to contact for more information in the `Contact Name` and `Email` fields respectively.
4. Enter the phone number of the supplier in the `Phone` field.
5. Rate the quality of the supplier's services on a scale of 1-5 in the `Quality Rating` field.
6. Select the delivery frequency of the supplier's services in the `Delivery Frequency` field.
7. Select all relevant product information (Type, Weight, Ingredients, Price, Quality, Shelf Life, Packaging) in the `Product Information` field.
8. Enter any customer comments or feedback about the supplier's services in the `Customer Comments` field.
9. Rate the supplier's services on a scale of 1-5 in the `Supplier Rating` field.
10. Enter the date of the last delivery from the supplier in the `Last Delivery Date` field.
11. Enter the date of the next expected delivery from the supplier in the `Next Delivery Date` field.
12. Enter any comments or feedback from the supplier in the `Supplier Comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of the supplier being evaluated.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the supplier.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person for the supplier.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supplier.
* **Quality Rating**: Rate the quality of the supplier's services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Delivery Frequency**: Select the frequency of the supplier's deliveries (Livestock Feed, Every 2 weeks, Every 4 weeks, Every 8 weeks, Every 12 weeks).
* **Product Information**: Select all relevant product information (Type, Weight, Ingredients, Price, Quality, Shelf Life, Packaging).
* **Customer Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the supplier's services.
* **Supplier Rating**: Rate the supplier's services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Last Delivery Date**: Enter the date of the last delivery from the supplier.
* **Next Delivery Date**: Enter the date of the next expected delivery from the supplier.
* **Supplier Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the supplier.
