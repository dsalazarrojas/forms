# Library Digital Resource Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a feedback mechanism to collect your thoughts on our digital collection, enabling us to refine and improve it for better user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide valuable feedback, please follow these steps:
1. Start with the first question, "What do you think about our digital collection?" This is an open-ended text field where you can express your opinions and thoughts about the resources we offer.
2. The next section, "Resource Type," allows you to select one or more types of resources that you interacted with during your visit. You can choose from a variety of options including eBook, article, database, document, journal, newspaper, thesis, and video.
3. The "Rating" question is a numerical scale from 1 to 5, where you can rate your overall experience with our digital collection. This helps us gauge your satisfaction level.
4. In the "Comments" field, you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience, even if you've already rated it or selected resource types.
5. The "Email" and "Phone" fields are optional, but if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or have specific questions, please provide your contact information.
6. "Additional Comments" is another field where you can provide any additional feedback or suggestions for improvement.
7. "Date of Visit" and "Time of Visit" fields will help us track your visit, but they are optional and not strictly required.
8. "How would you improve our digital collection?" is a text field where you can express your ideas on how we can enhance our resources.
9. "Which services would you like us to add to improve user experience?" is a select multiple field where you can choose from Search functionality, Recommendations, Personalization, Accessibility features, or Other.
10. "Issue tracking" and "Resource access issues" are select multiple fields where you can report any technical or resource-related problems you encountered during your visit.
11. "Service improvement suggestions" and "Other service improvement suggestions" are text fields where you can propose specific ideas on how we can improve our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about our digital collection?** (`digital_resource_feedback_form`, text, required): This is an open-ended question where you can share your thoughts, opinions, or suggestions about our digital collection.
* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more types of resources that you interacted with during your visit. You can choose from eBook, article, database, document, journal, newspaper, thesis, and video.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience with our digital collection on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience, even if you've already rated or selected resource types.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or have specific questions, provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or have specific questions, provide your phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for improvement.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): This field will help us track your visit, but it's not strictly required.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): This field will help us track your visit duration, but it's not strictly required.
* **How would you improve our digital collection?** (`digital_collection_improvement`, text, required): Express your ideas on how we can enhance our resources.
* **Which services would you like us to add to improve user experience?** (`services`, select multiple, required): Choose from Search functionality, Recommendations, Personalization, Accessibility features, or Other to suggest improvements.
* **Issue tracking** (`issue_tracking`, select multiple, optional): Report any technical or resource-related problems you encountered during your visit.
* **Resource access issues** (`resource_access_issues`, select multiple, optional): If you experienced any issues accessing our resources, select from Authentication errors, Authorization errors, Network errors, or Other.
* **Service improvement suggestions** (`service_improvement`, text, required): Propose specific ideas on how we can improve our services.
* **Other service improvement suggestions** (`other_service_improvement`, text, optional): Provide any additional ideas or suggestions for service improvement.

## Tips

* Please provide constructive and specific feedback to help us improve our digital collection.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing feedback, especially for open-ended questions.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask, and we'll do our best to clarify.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback; it's invaluable to us!
