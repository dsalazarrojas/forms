# pslf_waiver_eligibility_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's employment status and work-related details for waiver eligibility assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form section by section, ensuring that each field is accurate and complete.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing the correct information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your administrator or relevant authority for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing contact information, such as name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ **Active employment**: Currently employed.
	+ **Inactive employment**: Previously employed.
	+ **No employment**: Not employed.
* **Income Employed** (`income_employed`, number, optional): Enter your annual income if you are employed.
* **Income Not Employed** (`income_not_employed`, number, optional): Enter the amount of income you have from a non-employment income source, such as investments or government benefits.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ **Full time**: Full time employed.
	+ **Part time**: Part time employed.
	+ **Seasonal**: Seasonal employment (e.g., temporary work).
	+ **Other**: Other (please specify in the field below).
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ **Full time**: Full time employed.
	+ **Part time**: Part time employed.
	+ **Seasonal**: Seasonal employment (e.g., temporary work).
	+ **Other**: Other (please specify in the field below).
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" for Employment Type or Work Status, provide additional information about your income source.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): If you selected "Active employment" for Employment Status, provide the location of your work place.
* **Work Start Date** (`work_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you started working (if applicable).
* **Work End Date** (`work_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you stopped working (if applicable).
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you work per week (if applicable).
* **Pay Frequency** (`pay_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ **Hourly**: Paid hourly.
	+ **Bi-Weekly**: Paid bi-weekly.
	+ **Monthly**: Paid monthly.
	+ **Yearly**: Paid yearly.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you work per week (if applicable).

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your administrator or relevant authority for clarification.
* Please review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing the correct information.
