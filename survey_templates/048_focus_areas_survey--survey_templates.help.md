# focus_areas_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The focus_areas_survey form is used to gather information on your focus areas, which are specific aspects of your work or life that you want to improve or concentrate on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the focus_areas_survey form, follow these steps:

1. Read each page carefully and answer the questions based on your actual focus areas.
2. For the "focus_areas" page, select all the areas that apply to your work or life.
3. For "question1", "question2", "question3", and "question4", select the relevant options that best describe your focus areas.
4. On the "conclusion" page, provide a brief summary of your focus areas.
5. In "additional_comments", you can provide any additional information that is not covered in the other questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Introduction, Note, Optional)
* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance.

### Focus Areas (Focus_areas, Select Multiple, Optional)
* **Focus Areas**: Select all the areas that apply to your work or life.
  + Examples: Work, personal, relationships, health, finance, etc.

### Question 1 (Question1, Text, Optional)
* **What is a focus area?**: Describe what a focus area means to you.
  + Be specific and concise in your answer.

### Question 2 (Question2, Select One, Optional)
* **What are your focus areas?**: Select the answer that best describes your focus areas.
  + Examples: Yes, No

### Question 3 (Question3, Select Multiple, Optional)
* **What focus areas are most important?**: Select all the focus areas that are most important to you.
  + Examples: Very Important, Not Important

### Question 4 (Question4, Select Multiple, Optional)
* **What focus areas would you prioritize?**: Select all the focus areas that you would prioritize if you had to choose.
  + Examples: Yes, No

### Conclusion (Conclusion, Note, Optional)
* **Conclusion**: Summarize your focus areas in a few sentences.
  + Be brief and concise in your answer.

### Additional Comments (Additional Comments, Text, Optional)
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information about your focus areas.
  + This is an open-ended field for any additional comments or thoughts.
