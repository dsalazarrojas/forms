# Cafeteria Menu Improvement Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback and suggestions from students, staff, and visitors on how to improve the cafeteria menu.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please go through the following steps:

1. Select your role as a respondent (student, staff, or visitor).
2. Choose your preferred meal type (Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner).
3. Provide any dietary requirements you may have.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the menu variety from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
5. Rate your satisfaction with the price from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
6. Suggest new menu items you would like to see.
7. If you visit the cafeteria, select how often you visit (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely).
8. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent role** (Student, staff or visitor): Please select your role as a respondent to help us understand your perspective.
* **Preferred meal type** (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner): Choose the meal type you prefer to help us tailor the menu to your needs.
* **Dietary requirements** (text): If you have any food allergies or preferences (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, halal), please let us know.
* **Satisfaction with menu variety** (1-5): Rate your satisfaction with the current menu variety from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Satisfaction with price** (1-5): Rate your satisfaction with the price from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Suggested menu items** (text): Provide any new menu item suggestions you may have.
* **Frequency of use** (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely): If you visit the cafeteria, let us know how often you come.
* **Additional comments** (text): Provide any other feedback or comments you may have.
