# New Academic Programs Opening Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Academic Programs Opening Survey is designed to gather information and feedback from users about new academic programs opening at your institution. This survey is an essential tool for understanding user interests and needs regarding academic programs, and it helps to improve the quality and relevance of these programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Select all the programs that interest you from the "Course Offerings" field.
3. Choose the correct answers for "Demographic Questions" that best describe your situation.
4. In the "Program Details" field, provide a brief description of the programs that interest you.
5. If you have any additional information that might be helpful to provide, please use the "Additional Information" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Select the programs that interest you from the "Program Interest" field.
8. Choose the programs that interest you from the "Other Programs Interest" field.
9. Estimate the time you are willing to invest in the "Time Invested" field.
10. If you have any comments or feedback, please use the "Comments" field.
11. Enter your name and institution in the "Name" and "Institution" fields.
12. Finally, select the correct completion options for "Survey Completion" fields.
