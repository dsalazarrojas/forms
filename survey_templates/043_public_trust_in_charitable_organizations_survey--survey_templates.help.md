# public_trust_in_charitable_organizations_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "public_trust_in_charitable_organizations_survey," is designed to measure the level of trust people have in charitable organizations. Its purpose is to collect information about how much people trust these organizations, allowing for evaluation or research on this topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read through the form carefully to understand the questions asked.
2. Answer each question honestly to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Introduction, Note, Optional): This is a note field where you can provide any background information or context about your experience with charitable organizations. You can write anything that comes to mind, and it will not affect your answers to other questions.
* **Trust Drivers** (Trust Drivers, Select One, Optional): This question asks about your level of trust in the factors that drive trust in charitable organizations. Please select the option that best describes your level of trust. The options are:
	+ Very Low
	+ Low
	+ High
	+ Very High
* **Confidence** (Confidence, Number, Optional): This question asks you to rate your confidence in your answers. Please enter a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Charitable Organizations** (Charitable Organizations, Select Multiple, Optional): This question asks you to select all the types of charitable organizations you trust. You can choose multiple organizations from the given options.
	+ Nonprofit
	+ Government
	+ Hybrid
	+ Other (Please specify)
* **Feedback** (Feedback, Text, Optional): This is an open text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about charitable organizations.

## Tips
* Answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Please select all applicable options for Charitable Organizations.
* You can provide any background information in the Introduction field.
* If you are unsure about any question, you can leave it blank or skip it.
