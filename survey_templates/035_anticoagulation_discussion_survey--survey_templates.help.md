# Anticoagulation Discussion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on anticoagulation discussions. It will help us understand how clear and concise the information provided to patients was, how confident patients and clinicians were in the treatment decisions, and overall satisfaction with the discussion.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please answer the questions as honestly as possible. You may choose more than one option for the select_multiple question (Topics covered in discussion). 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### -- Discussion Context --
* **-- Discussion Context --** (Note): This is a text field for you to describe the context of the discussion. You may enter any relevant details about the consultation.

### Patient Age Group
* **Patient age group** (Select One): 
  + Under 18
  + 18 to 44
  + 45 to 64
  + 65 or older
  Please select the age bracket that best fits your age.

### Topics covered in discussion
* **Topics covered in discussion** (Select Multiple): 
  + Indications for therapy
  + Bleeding risk
  + Medication adherence
  + Monitoring and follow up
  + Reversal strategies
  Select all topics that were covered during your discussion.

### Clarity of information provided
* **Clarity of information provided** (Select One): 
  + Not clear
  + Somewhat clear
  + Clear
  + Very clear
  Please rate how clear you found the information provided during the discussion.

### Confidence in treatment decision
* **Confidence in treatment decision** (Select One): 
  + Low
  + Moderate
  + High
  Please rate how confident you are in the treatment decision made after the discussion.

### Recommended follow-up actions
* **Recommended follow-up actions** (Text): This is an optional field for you to describe any recommended tests, monitoring, or referrals that were suggested after the discussion.

### Overall satisfaction
* **Overall satisfaction with discussion** (Number): Please enter a number from 1 to 5 to rate your overall satisfaction with the discussion.

## Tips

Please keep the answers as brief and clear as possible to facilitate easier analysis. If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to ask.
