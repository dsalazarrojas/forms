# Time Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your time management habits and preferences. This survey will help employees understand their time usage patterns and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to you. Each question is designed to be straightforward, and you can fill it out at your own pace. Make sure to answer all the required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What time does your workday start?** (time_management_survey_page_1): This question is asking for the time you usually start your workday. Choose the time that best represents your normal start time.
* **Time Management** (time_management_survey_page_2): This field is for you to describe your approach to managing your time effectively.
* **What time do you usually take a break?** (time_management_survey_page_4): This question is asking for the time you typically take a break during the day.
* **What time management technique have you ever used?** (time_management_survey_page_6): This question allows you to share a time management technique you've tried or recommend.
* **What time do you usually leave your work place?** (time_management_survey_page_7): This question asks for the time when you usually leave your workplace.
* **How many hours do you usually work per day?** (time_management_survey_page_8): This question is asking for the average number of hours you work per day.
* **Do you usually take a break?** (time_management_survey_page_9): This question asks if you take a break during your work hours.
* **How long do you usually take breaks?** (time_management_survey_page_10): This question is asking for the average length of breaks you take.
* **What time do you usually have a meeting?** (time_management_survey_page_11): This question is asking for the typical time of your meetings.
* **Do you have meetings?** (time_management_survey_page_12): This question asks if you have meetings at all.
* **How many hours do you usually spend on meetings?** (time_management_survey_page_13): This question is asking for the average time spent on meetings.
* **Do you usually have a lunch break?** (time_management_survey_page_14): This question asks if you take a lunch break during your work hours.

**Tips**
- Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
- If you're unsure about a question, try your best to provide an accurate answer.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
