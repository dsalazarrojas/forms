# Christian Dating Perspectives Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christian Dating Perspectives Survey is designed to gather insights on various aspects of Christian dating, including personal information, faith and beliefs, and relationship goals. This survey aims to create a comprehensive understanding of the Christian community's perspectives on dating.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the personal information section, which includes First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Date of Birth.
2. In the "Faith and Beliefs" section, select the options that best describe your Christian denomination or faith background and how often you attend church.
3. For "Relationship Goals," select all options that apply to your primary goals in dating.
4. In the "Values Alignment" section, choose how important you think values alignment is in a relationship.
5. Finally, provide any additional thoughts or perspectives on Christian dating in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Current Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, select_one, required): Select one option that best describes your current relationship status.
* **What is your Christian denomination or faith background?** (`christian_denomination`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best represents your Christian denomination or faith background.
* **How often do you attend church?** (`church_attendance`, select_one, required): Select how often you attend church services.
* **How important is faith in your daily life?** (`faith_importance`, select_one, required): Choose the importance level of faith in your daily life.
* **Would you date someone from a different Christian faith?** (`interfaith_dating`, select_one, required): Select your willingness to date someone from a different Christian faith.
* **Would you date someone who is not Christian?** (`non_christian_dating`, select_one, required): Choose your willingness to date someone who is not Christian.
* **What are your primary goals in dating? (Select all that apply)** (`relationship_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select all the goals that are relevant to your primary dating objectives.
* **How important is values alignment in a relationship?** (`values_alignment`, select_one, required): Choose the level of importance you place on values alignment in a relationship.
* **Please share any additional thoughts or perspectives on Christian dating** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any thoughts or insights you have on Christian dating that you would like to share.
