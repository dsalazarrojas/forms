# local-governance-feedback-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
<thinking>This form is designed to collect feedback on local governance matters. The primary purpose is to understand the concerns or issues reported to the local government. This form is intended to collect data that will help us improve the services provided by the local government. If you have experienced any issues or concerns with the local government, please use this form to report them. Please note that if the issue has already been reported, your feedback will not be considered a new instance of the issue.</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. If you have a phone number, you can enter it in the "Phone" field.
4. Describe your experience or concern in the "Feedback" field.
5. Enter the date you encountered the issue in the "Issue Date" field.
6. Select "Yes" if you are willing to provide feedback, or "No" if you are not.
7. Click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification or to keep you updated on the progress.
* **Phone** (text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you easily.
* **Feedback** (text, required): Describe your experience or concern in detail. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Issue Date** (date, required): Enter the date you encountered the issue.
* **Submit** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are willing to provide feedback, or "No" if you are not.
