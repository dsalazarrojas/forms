# Divorce Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Divorce Survey form is designed to collect information from respondents about their divorce experience. This form aims to gather data on various aspects of divorce, such as reasons for divorce, number of children, assets, and income.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details and experiences regarding your divorce.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
3. Make sure to provide accurate information for the best results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Divorce Survey** (text, required/optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Respondent Name** (text, required/optional): Please enter your name as the respondent.
* **Respondent Information** (text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about yourself as the respondent.
* **Date of Divorce** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of your divorce, if applicable.
* **Reason for Divorce** (select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the following reasons for your divorce:
	+ Divorce
	+ Separation
	+ Annulment
	+ Judicial
	+ Judicial and Contested
* **Number of Children** (number, required/optional): Enter the number of children involved in the divorce.
* **Spouse Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your spouse's name.
* **Spouse Age** (text, required/optional): Enter your spouse's age.
* **Spouse Income** (number, required/optional): Enter your spouse's income.
* **Divorce Counselor** (select_multiple, required/optional): Check if you had a divorce counselor:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Other Issues** (text, required/optional): If you have any other issues related to your divorce, please describe them.
* **Respondent Income** (number, required/optional): Enter your income.
* **Assets** (select_multiple, required/optional): Check if you have any assets:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Liabilities** (select_multiple, required/optional): Check if you have any liabilities:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Assets Type** (select_multiple, required/optional): Check the type of assets you have:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Property Ownership** (select_multiple, required/optional): Check if you own any properties:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Property Description** (text, required/optional): Describe any properties you own.
* **Assets Value** (number, required/optional): Enter the value of your assets.
* **Assets Location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location of your assets.
* **Property Type** (select_multiple, required/optional): Check the type of properties you own:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Assets Type Description** (text, required/optional): Describe the type of assets you have.
* **Assets Other** (text, required/optional): If you have any other assets, please describe them.
* **Spouse Occupation** (text, required/optional): Enter your spouse's occupation.
* **Divorce Status** (select_one, required/optional): Choose your divorce status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth.

Note: This help guide is for reference only and should be reviewed and updated if any fields or options change.
