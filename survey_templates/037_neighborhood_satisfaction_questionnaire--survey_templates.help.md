# Neighborhood Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure neighborhood satisfaction from residents' perspectives, aiming to gather insights into various aspects of community life.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and answer the questions according to your experiences and feelings about your neighborhood. The form is divided into several pages, and each page focuses on a specific aspect of neighborhood satisfaction. You may answer all or some of the questions, as indicated by the "required" status for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: neighborhood_satisfaction_questionnaire
* **category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your neighborhood, such as "Family-Friendly" or "Affluent".
* **description** (`description`, text, optional): Describe your neighborhood in a few words.
* **resident_satisfaction** (`resident_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with resident services, such as community events, maintenance, and safety.
* **community_experience** (`community_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Select your level of involvement in community activities and events.
* **community_leadership** (`community_leadership`, number, optional): Rate your level of confidence in community leadership.
* **community_participation** (`community_participation`, number, optional): Rate your level of participation in community activities.
* **resident_engagement** (`resident_engagement`, number, optional): Rate your level of engagement in resident activities.
* **communication_with_leaders** (`communication_with_leaders`, text, optional): Describe your experiences with communication from community leaders.
* **community_feelings** (`community_feelings`, text, optional): Describe your feelings about your neighborhood.
* **neighborhood_satisfaction** (`neighborhood_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Select your level of satisfaction with neighborhood amenities.
* **community_involvement** (`community_involvement`, select_multiple, optional): Select your level of involvement in neighborhood activities.
* **resident_ownership** (`resident_ownership`, select_multiple, optional): Select your level of ownership and responsibility towards your neighborhood.
* **community_pride** (`community_pride`, select_multiple, optional): Select your level of pride in your neighborhood.
* **resident_satisfaction_score** (`resident_satisfaction_score`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with resident services.
* **community_score** (`community_score`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with community services.
* **overall_satisfaction_score** (`overall_satisfaction_score`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your neighborhood.
* **resident_engagement_score** (`resident_engagement_score`, number, optional): Rate your overall engagement in resident activities.
* **communication_score** (`communication_score`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with communication from community leaders.
* **community_feelings_score** (`community_feelings_score`, number, optional): Rate your overall feelings about your neighborhood.
* **community_involvement_score** (`community_involvement_score`, number, optional): Rate your overall involvement in neighborhood activities.
* **resident_ownership_score** (`resident_ownership_score`, number, optional): Rate your overall ownership and responsibility towards your neighborhood.
* **community_pride_score** (`community_pride_score`, number, optional): Rate your overall pride in your neighborhood.
* **resident_satisfaction_score_avg** (`resident_satisfaction_score_avg`, number, optional): Rate your average satisfaction with resident services.
* **overall_satisfaction_score_avg** (`overall_satisfaction_score_avg`, number, optional): Rate your average satisfaction with your neighborhood.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" can be skipped, but completing all fields will provide a more comprehensive understanding of your neighborhood's performance.
