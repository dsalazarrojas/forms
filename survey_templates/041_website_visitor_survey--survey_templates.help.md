# Website Visitor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience with our website. Your responses will help us understand our website's strengths and weaknesses and make targeted improvements to enhance your user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer each question with your best judgment.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Your email address" field, which is required for us to contact you with your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to our website** (`page_1`, text, optional): A text field where you can briefly introduce yourself and state the purpose of your visit.
* **What brought you to this website?** (`page_2`, select_one, required): Please select whether you visited our website for a specific reason, such as looking for information or making a purchase.
* **How easy was it to find what you were looking for?** (`page_3`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the ways that made it difficult or easy for you to find the information you needed on our website.
* **What would you like to see improved?** (`page_4`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on what we can improve on our website to better serve our visitors.
* **How likely are you to recommend this website?** (`page_5`, select_one, optional): Let us know how likely you are to recommend our website to others.
* **What would you like to see improved?** (`page_6`, text, optional): Another opportunity to share your suggestions and feedback on our website.
* **Additional comments** (`page_7`, note, optional): If you have any other comments or suggestions, please include them in this field.
* **Your email address** (`page_8`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with your survey results.
* **Your phone number** (`page_9`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please enter your number here.
