# Knife Crime Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Knife Crime Awareness Survey is a tool designed to collect data on knife crime awareness and attitudes among individuals. This survey aims to gather information on respondents' perceptions, safety measures, and prevention efforts related to knife crime.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. For the "Submission Info" questions, select the relevant options that apply to your situation.
3. Make sure to submit the form only once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Note`): This is a brief introduction to the form, providing context and instructions.
* **Safety Perceptions** (`Text`): Please respond with your thoughts on the safety of your community.
* **Safety Measures** (`Select One`): Do you take any safety measures when carrying a knife? (`Yes`/`No`)
* **Knowledge Level** (`Number`): How knowledgeable are you about knife crime? Please respond with a numerical value.
* **Awareness Level** (`Number`): How aware are you of knife crime in your community? Please respond with a numerical value.
* **Prevention Efforts** (`Select Multiple`): Which prevention efforts are being taken by your community to address knife crime? (`Yes`/`No`)
* **Community Engagement** (`Text`): Describe your engagement with your community regarding knife crime awareness.
* **Submission Info 1-8** (`Select One/Text`): Answer the questions with the following options or your personal response.

Note: Please submit the form only once, as indicated in the "Submission Info" questions.
