# Rural Urban Migration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rural Urban Migration Survey is designed to collect information on migration from rural to urban areas. This survey aims to understand the reasons behind such migration, the challenges faced during the process, and the plans for settlement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. For the "Migration Reasons" section, select all the reasons that apply to you.
3. For the "Migration Destination" section, select all the areas where you plan to or have migrated to.
4. For all other sections, please provide a brief explanation or answer the question as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Migration Reasons**: A list of possible reasons for migration (Rural Area, Urban Area, Suburban Area, International Area, Other).
* **Challenges Encountered**: Describe the challenges you have faced or expect to face during the migration process.
* **Settlement Plan**: Describe your plans for settling in the urban area.
* **Migration Year**: The year you migrated or plan to migrate.
* **Migration Destination**: The area where you migrated to or plan to migrate to.
* **Migration Duration**: The length of time you stayed in the urban area.
* **Other (please specify)**: If none of the above reasons apply, describe your reason for migration here.
* **Economic factors**: Describe any economic factors that contributed to your migration.
* **Environmental factors**: Describe any environmental factors that contributed to your migration.
* **Family factors**: Describe any family factors that contributed to your migration.
* **Politics or social unrest**: Describe any politics or social unrest that contributed to your migration.
* **Education or marriage**: Describe any education or marriage factors that contributed to your migration.
* **Settlement Location**: The location where you plan to settle in the urban area.
* **Settlement Status**: Describe your current or planned settlement status (e.g., homeowner, renter, etc.).
* **Settlement Size**: Describe the size of your settlement (e.g., small, medium, large).
* **Settlement Type**: Describe the type of settlement (e.g., single-family home, apartment building, etc.).
* **Contact Phone**: Provide your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Contact Email**: Provide your contact email address (if applicable).
* **Contact Address**: Provide your contact address (if applicable).
* **Migration Source**: Describe the location you migrated from.
* **Migration Destination**: Describe the location you migrated to.
* **Migration Distance**: The distance between the migration source and destination (if applicable).
* **Migration Cost**: The cost associated with your migration (if applicable).
* **Migration Frequency**: Describe your migration frequency (e.g., one-time, seasonal, etc.).
* **Health reasons**: Describe any health reasons that contributed to your migration.
* **Other (please specify)**: If none of the above reasons apply, describe your reason for migration here.

Note: Please fill out this form as honestly and accurately as possible, as the information collected will be used to understand rural-urban migration patterns and provide insights for policymakers and researchers.
