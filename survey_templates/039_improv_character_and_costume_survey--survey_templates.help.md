# improv_character_and_costume_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `improv_character_and_costume_survey` form is designed to collect information about characters and costumes for improv and theatrical performances. It aims to gather details that help us better understand the requirements for an improv show and ensure a great experience for both the actor and the audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the character details, if applicable.
2. Enter the character's name.
3. Choose the character type (e.g., 'True' or 'False').
4. Select any necessary costumes and their corresponding needs (e.g., 'True', 'False', or 'Maybe').
5. Choose the experience level of the character (e.g., 'High' or 'Low').
6. Enter the name of the costume.
7. Select the type of costume (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
8. Enter any additional rehearsal or show needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Character Details** (`character_details`, 1, Optional): This section is where you can add any additional details about the character, if needed.
* **Character Name** (`character_name`, 2, Required): Enter the name of the character.
* **Character Type** (`character_type`, 3, Optional): Choose the type of character (e.g., 'True' or 'False').
* **Costume Needs** (`costume_needs`, 4, Optional): Select any necessary costumes and their corresponding needs (e.g., 'True', 'False', or 'Maybe').
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, 5, Optional): Choose the experience level of the character (e.g., 'High' or 'Low').
* **Costume Name** (`costume_name`, 6, Optional): Enter the name of the costume.
* **Costume Type** (`costume_type`, 7, Optional): Select the type of costume (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Rehearsal Needs** (`rehearsal_needs`, 8, Optional): Enter any additional rehearsal needs.
* **Show Needs** (`show_needs`, 9, Optional): Enter any additional show needs.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible for each character and costume.
- If unsure about a character type or costume type, choose 'Maybe' or 'No' accordingly.
- Use the experience level to indicate the actor's experience level with the character or costume.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy.
