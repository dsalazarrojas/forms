# Employee Monthly Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Monthly Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their job satisfaction, department, and other relevant details. This form helps us understand how we can improve our work environment and ensure that our employees are satisfied with their jobs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the introduction carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of the form.
2.  Select your department from the drop-down menu.
3.  Rate your overall job satisfaction using the provided scale.
4.  Choose your available work hours from the list provided.
5.  Select your specific job satisfaction level.
6.  Choose how frequently you'd like to submit feedback.
7.  Enter the submission date.
8.  Select the person you're assigned to for feedback.
9.  Select which employee submitted the feedback.
10.  Enter their email address.
11.  Enter their phone number.
12.  Add any comments or suggestions you'd like to make.
13.  Enter the submission date again for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section provides a brief background on the form's purpose and importance. Please read it carefully before starting the form.
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to so we can tailor our feedback accordingly.
* **Overall Job Satisfaction**: Rate your overall job satisfaction on a scale of Highly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, and Highly Dissatisfied.
* **Available Work Hours**: Choose your available work hours from the list provided.
* **Specific Job Satisfaction**: Select your specific job satisfaction level for the current month.
* **Feedback Frequency**: Choose how frequently you'd like to submit feedback.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you're submitting this feedback.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person you're assigned to for feedback.
* **Submitted By**: Select the person submitting this feedback.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting this feedback.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting this feedback.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Submission Date 1**: Enter the date this feedback was submitted.
* **Submission Date 2**: (This field is for internal record-keeping purposes only.)
 
**Tips**

* Please only submit this form once a month.
* Ensure you're submitting the correct department and job satisfaction levels.
* If you've already submitted feedback for this month, don't resubmit.
* If you're not an employee, don't submit this form.
