# Competition Feedback Form 2017 PITCH COMPETITION - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and evaluations from individuals who participated in the 2017 Pitch Competition. It is meant to be filled out by judges, fellow competitors, audience members, and mentors who attended the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the role that best describes you in the competition (Judge, Fellow Competitor, Audience Member, or Mentor).
2. Provide the name of the team you are evaluating.
3. Rate the clarity of the problem being solved, the viability and innovation of the solution, market size and potential, team confidence and ability to execute, and overall pitch delivery and impact.
4. Select the most memorable part of the pitch.
5. Provide specific, constructive feedback for the team to improve their pitch or business model.
6. If you were an angel investor, rate the likelihood to invest in the presented idea.
7. Select today's date to confirm the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Pitcher / Startup Team** (`pitcher_name_eval`, text, required): Please enter the name of the team you are evaluating.
* **-- 2017 Pitch Competition Evaluation --** (`pitch_feedback_header`, note, required): This is a header for the evaluation form.
* **Clarity of the Problem being Solved** (`problem_clarity_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how clearly the team articulates the problem they are solving. 
    + Very Clear: The team very clearly explains the problem they are solving.
    + Clear: The team clearly explains the problem they are solving.
    + Vague: The team somewhat ambiguously explains the problem they are solving.
    + Not Defined: The team does not clearly explain the problem they are solving.
* **Viability and Innovation of the Solution** (`solution_viability_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how viable and innovative the solution presented is. 
    + 1 - Not Viable: The solution is not practical or unique.
    + 2: The solution is somewhat viable and innovative.
    + 3: The solution is moderately viable and innovative.
    + 4: The solution is highly viable and innovative.
    + 5 - Highly Viable: The solution is extremely practical and unique.
* **Market Size and Potential** (`market_potential_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the scale of the business opportunity. 
    + Large Global Potential: The market has a large potential and global reach.
    + Moderate National Potential: The market has a moderate potential within a national context.
    + Local Niche Market: The market is limited to a local or niche context.
    + Underspecified: The market potential is not well specified.
* **Confidence in Team's Ability to Execute** (`team_confidence_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how confident you are in the team's ability to execute their idea. 
    + High Confidence: You have very high confidence in the team's ability to execute.
    + Moderate Confidence: You somewhat confident in the team's ability to execute.
    + Low Confidence: You somewhat doubt the team's ability to execute.
* **Overall Pitch Delivery and Impact** (`pitch_delivery_impact`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the presentation and slides. 
    + Professional / Inspiring: The presentation is professional and inspiring.
    + Good / Clear: The presentation is good and clear.
    + Fair / Needs Work: The presentation is somewhat fair but needs improvement.
    + Poor / Unclear: The presentation is poorly and unclear.
* **What was the most memorable part of the pitch?** (`memorable_pitch_element`, text, optional): Highlight or unique factor that made the pitch stand out.
* **Specific Constructive Feedback for the team** (`pitch_improvement_feedback`, text, required): Provide specific and constructive feedback for the team to improve their pitch or business model.
* **Likelihood to Invest (Hypothetical)** (`investment_likelihood_pitch`, select_one, required): Rate how likely you would be to invest in the presented idea. 
    + Definitely Invest: You would definitely invest in the idea.
    + Would consider with more data: You would consider investing with more data.
    + Unlikely to Invest: You do not see investing in the idea.
* **Evaluator Role** (`evaluator_role_type`, select_one, required): Select your role in the competition (Judge, Fellow Competitor, Audience Member, Mentor).
* **Date** (`pitch_eval_date`, date, required): Select today's date to confirm the submission date.
