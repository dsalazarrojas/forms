# Self Driving Car Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Driving Car Survey is designed to collect your thoughts, experiences, and preferences about self-driving cars. Your input will help us to better understand your needs and preferences for future development of self-driving cars.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form fields as instructed below.
2. Be honest and provide your true thoughts and experiences.
3. You can skip any field that you're not comfortable answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Car Owner** (`car_owner`, text, optional): Please provide your name or alias as the car owner.
* **Driving Experience** (`driving_experience`, time, optional): Share your experience with self-driving cars, e.g., how long you've been using or test-driving one.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience with self-driving cars on a scale of 1-10.
* **Favorite Feature** (`favorite_feature`, select_one, optional): Choose the feature you like most about self-driving cars.
	+ Options: Feature 1, Feature 2, Feature 3, Feature 4
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about self-driving cars.
* **Contact** (`contact`, email, optional): Provide your email address for any follow-up questions or surveys.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any further communication.
* **Survey Comments** (`self_driving_car_survey_comments`, text, optional): Share your overall thoughts and feelings about self-driving cars.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide accurate information.
* You can skip any field if you're not comfortable answering.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
