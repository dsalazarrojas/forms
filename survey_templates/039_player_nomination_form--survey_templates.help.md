# Player Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Nomination Form is used to collect player nomination data for team selection and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team name you are nominating or denoting a player for.
2. Enter the player's name.
3. Select one or multiple positions for the player.
4. Choose whether you are nominating or denoting the player.
5. Enter any additional information or notes about the player.
6. Optionally, enter the player's email and phone number.
7. Optionally, enter a note for the player.
8. Click "Submit" to save your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_name** (Required, Text): Enter the team name for which you are nominating or denoting the player.
* **player_name** (Required, Text): Enter the player's name.
* **position** (Required, Select Multiple): Select one or multiple positions for the player. Options:
	+ Defensive Line
	+ Defensive Back
	+ Defensive Lineman
	+ Quarterback
	+ Kicker
* **player_nomination** (Required, Select One): Choose whether you are nominating or denoting the player.
	+ Nominate a player
	+ Denote a player
	+ Nominate and Denote a player
* **age** (Optional, Number): Enter the player's age.
* **email** (Optional, Email): Enter the player's email address.
* **phone** (Optional, Text): Enter the player's phone number.
* **note** (Optional, Note): Enter a note for the player.
* **assigned_tool** (Optional, Text): Enter any additional information or tools assigned to the player.
* **category** (Optional, Text): Enter any additional category information about the player.
