<thinking> FTIR Equipment Usage Survey - Help Guide

This guide is for users who want to complete the FTIR Equipment Usage Survey. To be eligible to complete this survey, you must have been previously trained on the FTIR equipment and have access to the equipment. This guide will walk you through each field and help you understand what information to input.

## Purpose
The FTIR Equipment Usage Survey is a tool used to gather feedback and usage information from users who have previously been trained on the FTIR equipment. This information helps us understand how often and when users are using the equipment, as well as what aspects of it they are satisfied with and what areas for improvement there are.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through it page by page, filling out the required information in each field. You can navigate through the pages using the links at the top of the form. If you have any questions or need help, you can contact your supervisor or support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Email** (Email, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
  This is the email address we will use to follow up with you about your submission and any other important information.
- **Instrument Type** (Instrument Type, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you have used the FTIR equipment for this survey.
  This field ensures we know you have experience with the FTIR equipment.
- **Frequency Range** (Frequency Range, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you use the equipment frequently.
  This field helps us understand how often you use the equipment.
- **Usage Frequency** (Usage Frequency, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any frequencies you have used the equipment.
  This is where you can specify multiple frequencies you have used the equipment.
- **Usage Time** (Usage Time, `time`, optional): Enter the time of day you usually use the FTIR equipment.
  This helps us understand when you use the equipment.
- **Satisfaction** (Satisfaction, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the FTIR equipment on a scale from 1 to 10.
  This is where you can give your honest feedback on how satisfied you are with the equipment.
- **Training Needs** (Training Needs, `text`, optional): Describe any additional training you need for the FTIR equipment.
  This field is for any extra training you feel you need.
- **Improvement Feedback** (Improvement Feedback, `text`, optional): Describe any areas for improvement with the FTIR equipment.
  This field is for any suggestions you have for improving the equipment.
- **Improvement Areas** (Improvement Areas, `text`, optional): Describe any areas where you would like to improve the FTIR equipment.
  This field is another place for any suggestions you have for improving the equipment.
- **Improvement Description** (Improvement Description, `text`, optional): Describe why you want to improve the specified areas.
  This field is a detailed explanation of your improvement suggestions.
- **Training Needs Urgent** (Training Needs Urgent, `text`, optional): Describe any urgent training needs you have for the FTIR equipment.
  This field is for any training needs you feel are urgent.
- **Notes** (Notes, `text`, optional): Add any other important information.
  This field is for any other important information about your experience with the FTIR equipment.
- **Submission Date** (Submission Date, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting this survey.
  This is the date and time you are submitting this form.
- **Submission Time** (Submission Time, `time`, optional): Enter the time you are submitting this survey.
  This field is optional, but helps us understand when you submitted the form.
- **Facility** (Facility, `text`, optional): Enter the facility where you use the FTIR equipment.
  This field is for the location where you use the equipment.
- **Lab Core** (Lab Core, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the lab core you use for the FTIR equipment.
  This field is for the lab core you use for the equipment.
- **User Rating** (User Rating, `number`, optional): Rate your experience with the FTIR equipment.
  This field is for your rating of the equipment.
- **Submission ID** (Submission ID, `text`, optional): Enter any submission ID for the survey.
  This field is for any unique identifier for this submission.
- **User Name** (User Name, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
  This field is for your name.
- **Submission Tracking** (Submission Tracking, `text`, optional): Enter any submission tracking information.
  This field is for any tracking information.

Please note that the required fields are marked 'required' in the form and should be filled out before submitting.
