# Earnings Improvement Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Earnings Improvement Quiz is a tool designed to help assess individuals' financial stability and security. It gathers information about demographic and financial data to provide insights into users' earnings and financial well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Select your income goal from the available options.
5. Choose your income sources (select multiple options).
6. Indicate how often you make money (select one option).
7. Rate your income certainty (select one option).
8. Confirm if you save for the future (select multiple options).
9. Check if you have debt (select multiple options).
10. Indicate your credit score (select one option).
11. Evaluate your income stability (select multiple options).
12. Rate your income security (select multiple options).
13. Indicate your income certainty 2 (select one option).
14. Select your income frequency 2 (select one option).
15. Choose your education level (select one option).
16. Choose your main career field (select multiple options).
17. Enter your years of work experience.
18. Enter your gross income.
19. Enter your net income.
20. Select your income frequency 3 (select one option).
21. Enter your age.
22. Enter your income.
23. Enter your savings goal.
24. Confirm if you have emergency funds (select multiple options).
25. Indicate if income is a stress factor (select multiple options).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Income Goal** (`income_goal`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the provided income goal ranges that best represents your current or future income aspirations.
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, `select_multiple`, required): Please choose all the income sources that apply to you (e.g. Wages, Investments, Other).
* **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Please select the frequency with which you make money (e.g. Monthly, Weekly, Biweekly).
* **Income Certainty** (`income_certainty`, `select_one`, required): Please rate your level of certainty about your income (e.g. 0-20%, 21-40%, etc.).
* **Do you Save for the Future?** (`income_savings`, `select_multiple`, required): Please indicate if you do or do not save for the future (select multiple options).
* **Do you Have Debt?** (`income_debt`, `select_multiple`, required): Please confirm if you have any debt.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, `select_one`, required): Please select your credit score range (e.g. 300-399, 400-499, etc.).
* **Income Stability** (`income_stability`, `select_multiple`, required): Please evaluate the stability of your income (select multiple options).
* **Income Security** (`income_security`, `select_multiple`, required): Please rate your income security (select multiple options).
* **Income Certainty 2** (`income_certainty_2`, `select_one`, required): Please rate your income certainty again (select one option).
* **Income Frequency 2** (`income_frequency_2`, `select_one`, required): Please select the frequency with which you make money again (select one option).
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Please choose your highest completed education level.
* **Career Field** (`career_field`, `select_multiple`, required): Please choose all the career fields that apply to you.
* **Years of Work Experience** (`work_exp`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of years you have worked.
* **Gross Income** (`income_gross`, `text`, required): Please enter your current gross income.
* **Net Income** (`income_net`, `text`, required): Please enter your current net income.
* **Income Frequency 3** (`income_frequency_3`, `select_one`, required): Please select the frequency with which you make money again (select one option).
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Please enter your age.
* **Income** (`income`, `text`, required): Please enter your income.
* **Savings Goal** (`savings_goal`, `text`, required): Please enter your savings goal.
* **Emergency Funds** (`emergency_funds`, `select_multiple`, required): Please confirm if you have 3-6 months emergency funds (select multiple options).
* **Is Income a Stress Factor?** (`income_stress`, `select_multiple`, required): Please confirm if income is a stress factor in your life (select multiple options).
