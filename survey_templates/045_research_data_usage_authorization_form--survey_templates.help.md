# research_data_usage_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The research_data_usage_authorization_form is a critical form used to declare the usage of research data. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that data is handled and used according to the guidelines and regulations set forth by your institution and research ethics board.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field and its relevance to your research project.
2. Answer each field truthfully, providing accurate information about your research data usage.
3. Make sure to save a copy of the completed form for your records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (Page 1)** (`Participant Information`, select_multiple, not required)
    * Choose 'Yes' if you are authorizing the use of participant data for research purposes.
    * Choose 'No' if you are not authorizing the use of participant data for research purposes.
* **Research Data Usage (Page 1)** (`Research Data Usage`, select_one, not required)
    * Choose 'Yes' if you want to authorize the use of research data for your current research study.
    * Choose 'No' if you are not authorizing the use of research data for this study.
* **Research Data Usage Date** (`Research Data Usage Date`, date, not required)
    * Enter the date you want to start or authorize the use of research data.
* **Participant Consent (Page 2)** (`Participant Consent`, select_one, not required)
    * Choose 'Yes' if the participant has given their consent to the use of their data.
    * Choose 'No' if the participant has not given their consent to the use of their data.
* **Researcher ID** (`Researcher ID`, text, not required)
    * Enter your researcher ID or username.
* **Participant ID** (`Participant ID`, text, not required)
    * Enter the participant ID or username.
* **Research Data** (`Research Data`, text, not required)
    * Enter a description of the research data you are using.
* **Participant Consent Date** (`Participant Consent Date`, date, not required)
    * Enter the date the participant gave their consent.
* **Research Data Deidentified** (`Research Data Deidentified`, select_multiple, not required)
    * Choose 'Yes' if the data has been deidentified or de-identified.
    * Choose 'No' if the data has not been deidentified or de-identified.
* **Research Data Anonymized** (`Research Data Anonymized`, select_multiple, not required)
    * Choose 'Yes' if the data has been anonymized.
    * Choose 'No' if the data has not been anonymized.
* **Research Data Pseudonymized** (`Research Data Pseudonymized`, select_multiple, not required)
    * Choose 'Yes' if the data has been pseudonymized.
    * Choose 'No' if the data has not been pseudonymized.
* **Data Access** (`Data Access`, select_one, not required)
    * Choose 'Yes' if you have access to the data.
    * Choose 'No' if you do not have access to the data.
* **Data Access Description** (`Data Access Description`, text, not required)
    * Enter a description of your data access.
* **Research Data Retention** (`Research Data Retention`, select_one, not required)
    * Choose 'Yes' if you want to retain research data.
    * Choose 'No' if you do not want to retain research data.
* **Research Data Retention Description** (`Research Data Retention Description`, text, not required)
    * Enter a description of your data retention policies.
* **Research Data Destruction** (`Research Data Destruction`, select_one, not required)
    * Choose 'Yes' if you want to destroy the research data.
    * Choose 'No' if you do not want to destroy the research data.
* **Research Data Destruction Description** (`Research Data Destruction Description`, text, not required)
    * Enter a description of your data destruction policies.
* **Participant Contact** (`Participant Contact`, text, not required)
    * Enter the participant's contact information.
* **Participant Contact Description** (`Participant Contact Description`, text, not required)
    * Enter a description of the participant's contact information.
* **Research Data Sharing** (`Research Data Sharing`, select_one, not required)
    * Choose 'Yes' if you want to share the research data.
    * Choose 'No' if you do not want to share the research data.
* **Research Data Sharing Description** (`Research Data Sharing Description`, text, not required)
    * Enter a description of your data sharing policies.
* **Participant Informed Consent** (`Participant Informed Consent`, select_one, not required)
    * Choose 'Yes' if the participant has given their informed consent.
    * Choose 'No' if the participant has not given their informed consent.
* **Participant Informed Consent Description** (`Participant Informed Consent Description`, text, not required)
    * Enter a description of the participant's informed consent.
* **Research Data Usage Authorizer** (`Research Data Usage Authorizer`, select_multiple, not required)
    * Choose 'Yes' if you are authorizing the use of research data.
    * Choose 'No' if you are not authorizing the use of research data.
* **Researcher Information** (`Researcher Information`, text, not required)
    * Enter any relevant information about yourself as a researcher.

## Tips
* Ensure you have reviewed and understood the form instructions before proceeding.
* Answer each field truthfully and accurately.
* Save a copy of the completed form for your records.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult your research team or department head.
* This form is a critical component of your research data usage, and its accuracy is essential for ensuring compliance with regulations and guidelines.
