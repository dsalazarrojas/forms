# Chronic Disease Coaching Outcome Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Disease Coaching Outcome Survey is designed to evaluate the progress, satisfaction, and areas for improvement of individuals who have received coaching for chronic diseases. The survey aims to gather data on the effectiveness of the coaching program and identify potential improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly. For each field, provide the information as instructed, and make sure to select only one option for the select-one fields. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): This field is for your convenience, and you can input your full name or any other name you prefer to be referred to in the survey.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Please input your email address for survey follow-up and potential future communications.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, `text`, optional): If you have a participant ID, please input it here. This is helpful for our internal tracking purposes.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, `date`, required): Please input the date when you started the coaching program.
* **Coaching Sessions Completed** (`coaching_sessions_completed`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of coaching sessions you have completed from the provided options.
* **Chronic Condition** (`chronic_condition`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary chronic condition you are managing.
* **Current Health Status** (`health_status_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall health status from the provided options.
* **Health Status Change** (`health_status_change`, `select_one`, required): Select how your health has changed since the start of the program.
* **Symptom Management** (`symptom_management`, `select_one`, required): Select how well you are managing your symptoms.
* **Medication Adherence** (`medication_adherence`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you take your medications as prescribed.
* **Lifestyle Changes Made** (`lifestyle_changes`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the lifestyle changes you have made since the start of the program.
* **Confidence in Self-Management** (`confidence_level`, `select_one`, required): Select how confident you feel in managing your condition.
* **Knowledge Improvement** (`knowledge_improvement`, `select_one`, required): Select how much your knowledge has improved since the start of the program.
* **Goal Achievement** (`goal_achievement`, `select_one`, required): Select how well you are meeting your health goals.
* **Coach Support Rating** (`coach_support_rating`, `select_one`, required): Select how you rate your coach's support.
* **Coach Helpfulness** (`coach_helpfulness`, `select_one`, required): Select how helpful your coach was.
* **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, `select_one`, required): Select the quality of communication with your coach.
* **Overall Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Select how satisfied you are with the program.
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Select if you would recommend the program to others.
* **Continue in Program** (`continue_program`, `select_one`, required): Select if you want to continue in the program.
* **Most Helpful Aspect** (`most_helpful_aspect`, `text`, optional): If you have any aspect of the program that was most helpful, please describe it here.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please describe them here.
* **Additional Support Needed** (`additional_support_needed`, `text`, optional): If you need any additional support, please describe here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any other feedback or comments, please describe them here.
