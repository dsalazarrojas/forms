# workshop_knowledge_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the knowledge gained by workshop participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by rating your experience with each session on a scale of 1-10.
2. Describe your takeaways from each session.
3. Provide feedback on the workshop overall.
4. Enter your contact information so that we can follow up with you.
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you learn?** (`1`, text, optional): Briefly describe the key concepts or ideas that you gained from the workshop.
* **Session 1** (`2`, select_one, optional): Choose how well you understand the material from Session 1.
* **How would you rate Session 1 on a scale of 1-10?** (`3`, number, optional): Rate your experience with Session 1.
* **What did you take away from Session 1?** (`4`, text, optional): Describe your takeaways from Session 1.
* **Session 2** (`5`, select_one, optional): Choose how well you understand the material from Session 2.
* **How would you rate Session 2 on a scale of 1-10?** (`6`, number, optional): Rate your experience with Session 2.
* **What did you take away from Session 2?** (`7`, text, optional): Describe your takeaways from Session 2.
* **Overall** (`8`, select_one, optional): Choose how well the workshop met your expectations.
* **Feedback** (`9`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or comments about the workshop.
* **Enter your email** (`10`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you.
* **Enter your phone number** (`11`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can follow up with you.
* **Additional comments** (`12`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Participant name** (`13`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Participant email** (`14`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Participant phone** (`15`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Trainer name** (`16`, text, optional): Enter your trainer's name.
* **Trainer email** (`17`, email, optional): Enter your trainer's email address.
* **Trainer phone** (`18`, text, optional): Enter your trainer's phone number.
* **Organization** (`19`, text, optional): Enter your organization name.
* **Role** (`20`, text, optional): Enter your role.
* **Location** (`21`, text, optional): Enter your location.
* **Session length** (`22`, number, optional): Enter the session length in hours.
* **Additional session length information** (`23`, text, optional): Add any additional session length information.
* **Session notes** (`24`, text, optional): Add any session notes.
