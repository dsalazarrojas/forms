## knee_pain_treatment_options_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the treatment options provided to patients with knee pain. The form helps to document the effectiveness and outcome of various treatments, as well as gather feedback from patients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant information for each field based on your interaction with the patient.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields, indicated by `required: true` in the field's metadata.
3. Use the available options to select the best answer for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_feedback** (`patient_feedback`, `note`, optional): A note-taking field to document any relevant comments or information from the patient's feedback.
* **treatment_options** (`treatment_options`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the available treatment options: "Knee Pain Injections", "Steroid Shots", or "Other".
* **pain_level** (`pain_level`, `number`, optional): A numeric field to measure the patient's pain level. Please enter a number from 1 to 10, where 1 is minimal pain and 10 is maximum pain.
* **treatment_effectiveness** (`treatment_effectiveness`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple options from the list to indicate the effectiveness of the treatment: "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **treatment_outcome** (`treatment_outcome`, `time`, optional): A date field to record the treatment's outcome. You can enter any date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **patient_satisfaction** (`patient_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the available options: "Very Satisfied", "Somewhat Satisfied", or "Not Satisfied".
* **medical_history** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): A text field to document the patient's medical history.
* **follow_up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether the patient needs a follow-up visit: "True" or "False".
* **medication_side_effects** (`medication_side_effects`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple options from the list to report any medication side effects: "Nausea", "Dizziness", "Itching".
* **follow_up_date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): A date field to record the follow-up visit date. You can enter any date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the available options to provide accurate and relevant information.
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
