# course_management_tools_instructor_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from instructors about their experiences and opinions on the course management tools they use. Your input will help us understand how these tools are being used and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. You can select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Instructor Inquiry** (`course_management_tools_instructor_inquiry`, text, optional): 
This field is where you can provide any general comments or questions you have about the course management tools.

### 2. **Email** (`email`, email, optional): 
Enter your email address so we can contact you with any updates or follow-up questions.

### 3. **Instructor** (`instructor`, text, optional): 
Please provide your name or title as an instructor.

### 4. **School** (`school`, text, optional): 
Enter the name of your school or institution where you are using the course management tools.

### 5. **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): 
This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the course management tools.

### 6. **Platform Evaluations** (`platform_evaluations`, select_one, optional): 
Select one option from the list: 
  * Platform A
  * Platform B
  * Platform C

### 7. **Follow Up Outreach** (`follow_up_outreach`, select_multiple, optional): 
Select all that apply: 
  * Platform A
  * Platform B
  * Platform C

### 8. **Training Teams** (`training_teams`, select_one, optional): 
Select one option from the list: 
  * Training Team 1
  * Training Team 2
  * Training Team 3

### 9. **Feature Planning** (`feature_planning`, text, optional): 
Provide your thoughts on how to improve or feature to be implemented in the course management tools.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions that apply to you.
- If you have already submitted this form, please note that your new submission will be treated as an update and will not be processed as a duplicate.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact us.
