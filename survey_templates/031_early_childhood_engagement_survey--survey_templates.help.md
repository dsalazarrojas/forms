# early_childhood_engagement_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather opinions and experiences from preschool or kindergarten teachers on their current engagement with their work and potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your response to the survey title in the "Early Childhood Engagement Survey" field.
2. Provide a brief introduction to your response in the "Introduction" field.
3. Answer the question "How do you feel about your current experience as a preschool or kindergarten teacher?" in the "How do you feel about your current experience as a preschool or kindergarten teacher?" field by selecting one or more of the options (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
4. Provide any suggestions or ideas you have on how to improve your experience in the "What could be done to improve your experience?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Early Childhood Engagement Survey** (`survey_title`, `text`, **Required**): Enter a brief response to the survey title.
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, **Required**): Provide a brief introduction to your response.
* **How do you feel about your current experience as a preschool or kindergarten teacher?** (`teacher_experience`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select one or more of the options (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor) that best describes your current experience.
* **What could be done to improve your experience?** (`experience_improvement`, `text`, **Required**): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have on how to improve your experience.
