# PMDD Screening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PMDD Screening Survey is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information from patients about their medical history, symptoms, and current health status. This data is used to assess the presence or absence of PMDD symptoms, which can significantly impact a patient's mental health and well-being. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly is vital for healthcare professionals to provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each field carefully and provide honest answers to the best of your ability.
2.  Ensure you have the necessary permissions or approvals from your healthcare provider to complete this form.
3.  Fill out each field as instructed, taking care to provide the required information for each section.
4.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy or mm/dd/yy (e.g., 06/12/1990).
* **Home Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your home address.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all relevant medical conditions from the dropdown list (High Blood Pressure, Diabetes, Allergies, Cancer).
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose any medications you are currently taking from the list provided (Aspirin, Ibuprofen, Acetaminophen, Lisinopril, Fosinopril, Amlodipine).
* **Primary Care Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your primary care provider.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your appointment with your healthcare provider.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your appointment with your healthcare provider.
* **Screening Date** (`screening_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of this screening survey completion.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required: false): Describe your symptoms in as much detail as possible.
* **Medical History Notes** (`medical_history_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments about your medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): List any allergies you may have.

## Tips

* Be truthful and accurate when answering each field, as this information will be used to guide your healthcare provider in assessing your PMDD symptoms.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by you, the patient, or by a healthcare professional on your behalf with your explicit permission.
