# New Member Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from new members in our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips
* Please enter your name and email address accurately to ensure you can be contacted and recognized in our organization.
* The phone number is not mandatory but recommended for communication purposes.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
