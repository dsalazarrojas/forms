# Energy Community Feedback Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Community Feedback Information Collection Form is a tool used to gather feedback from members of the energy community. It helps us understand your experiences, concerns, and opinions about our services and programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Member Name and Email address (if available).
2. Answer the Feedback Question, providing your thoughts on your experience with our energy community.
3. Share your Comments, if you have any additional thoughts or suggestions.
4. Select a Rating to indicate your level of satisfaction (on a scale).
5. Add a comment to explain your Rating selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Name**: Enter your name so we can address you personally in our communication.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Optional
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: Optional
* **Feedback Question**: Describe your experience or concern with our energy community.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Optional
* **Comments**: Add any additional thoughts or suggestions you have.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Optional
* **Rating**: Choose a number to indicate your level of satisfaction (on a scale).
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: Optional
* **Rating Comment**: Explain your Rating selection.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Optional

## Tips
* Be honest and open in your feedback, as it will help us improve our services and programs.
* You can fill in as much or as little information as you want, depending on your needs.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please report them so we can address them.
* If you have suggestions or ideas, we'd love to hear them!
