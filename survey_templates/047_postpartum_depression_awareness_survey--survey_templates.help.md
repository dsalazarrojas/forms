# Postpartum Depression Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your postpartum health and well-being. Please answer the questions honestly to help us understand the postpartum depression awareness and support needs of new mothers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each page's questions in sequence.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose one or more of the provided options.
3. For numeric questions (New Mother, New Baby Age, New Mother Age), please enter a whole number.
4. For text questions (Suggestions for Improvement, Additional Comments), please provide a short answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Mother** (`id: 1`, `label: New Mother`, `type: number`): Please enter the number of months since giving birth.
* **New Baby Age** (`id: 2`, `label: New Baby Age`, `type: number`): Please enter the age of your baby in months.
* **New Mother Age** (`id: 3`, `label: New Mother Age`, `type: number`): Please enter your age in years.
* **Depression Symptoms** (`id: 4`, `label: Depression Symptoms`, `type: select_multiple`): Please select all that apply from the list of depression symptoms provided.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`id: 5`, `label: Healthcare Provider`, `type: select_one`): Do you have a healthcare provider?
* **Support Systems** (`id: 6`, `label: Support Systems`, `type: select_multiple`): Please select all that apply from the list of support systems provided.
* **Postpartum Care Services** (`id: 7`, `label: Postpartum Care Services`, `type: select_one`): Have you received postpartum care services?
* **Feelings About Postpartum Depression** (`id: 8`, `label: Feelings About Postpartum Depression`, `type: select_multiple`): Please select all that apply from the list provided.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`id: 9`, `label: Suggestions for Improvement`, `type: text`): Please provide your suggestions on how we can improve postpartum depression awareness and support.
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 10`, `label: Additional Comments`, `type: text`): Please add any additional comments or concerns you may have.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are experiencing any postpartum depression symptoms, please seek help from a healthcare provider.
* Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve postpartum depression awareness and support.
