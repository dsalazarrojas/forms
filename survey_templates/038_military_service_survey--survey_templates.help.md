# Military Service Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Military Service Survey is a form designed to collect information about your military service. It is intended to gather data on your contact information, military experience, and support services. This information will help us better understand your needs and provide you with the necessary resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Answer the questions in the "Military Experience" section, which will ask about your military dates of service.
3. Indicate whether you have experience in any of the following areas:
	* Branch of service (e.g., Army, Navy, Air Force)
	* Deployment information (e.g., were you deployed to a specific location?)
	* Discharge information (e.g., how you were discharged from the military)
	* Contact frequency (e.g., how often do you like to be contacted?)
	* Support services (e.g., do you need help with job training or education?)
	* Military medical career counselor referral
	* Other resources (e.g., are you interested in receiving information on VA benefits?)
	* Veterans services (e.g., do you need help with employment assistance?)
	* Other services (e.g., are you interested in receiving information on healthcare?)
4. Finally, provide any additional comments or information you would like to share with us in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (Contact Information, note, required: false)
	+ Enter your name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you.
* **Military Experience (Page 2)** (Military Experience, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the dates you served in the military (start and end dates).
* **Branch Of Service (Page 3)** (Branch Of Service, select_one, required: false)
	+ Indicate whether you have experience in the Army, Navy, Air Force, or other branch.
* **Unit ID (Page 4)** (Unit ID, number, required: false)
	+ Enter your unit ID number, if applicable.
* **Role ID (Page 4)** (Role ID, number, required: false)
	+ Enter your role ID number, if applicable.
* **Date Of Service (Page 5)** (Date Of Service, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the dates you served in the military.
* **Deployment Information (Page 6)** (Deployment Information, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Indicate whether you were deployed to a specific location.
* **Discharge Information (Page 7)** (Discharge Information, select_one, required: false)
	+ Indicate how you were discharged from the military.
* **Contact Frequency (Page 8)** (Contact Frequency, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Indicate how often you would like to be contacted.
* **Support Services (Page 9)** (Support Services, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Indicate whether you are interested in receiving help with job training or education.
* **Military Medical Career Counselor Referral (Page 10)** (Military Medical Career Counselor Referral, select_one, required: false)
	+ Indicate if you would like to receive a referral to a military medical career counselor.
* **Other Resources (Page 11)** (Other Resources, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Indicate whether you are interested in receiving information on VA benefits or other topics.
* **Veterans Services (Page 12)** (Veterans Services, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Indicate whether you need help with employment assistance or other veterans' services.
* **Other Services (Page 13)** (Other Services, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Indicate whether you are interested in receiving information on healthcare or other services.
* **Additional Comments (Page 14)** (Additional Comments, note, required: false)
	+ Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.
