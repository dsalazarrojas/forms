# Donor Campaign Outcome Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on the performance and impact of Donor Campaigns, including donor feedback and budget information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select all applicable options for "Campaign Performance", describing the success of the campaign.
2.  Answer "Donor Feedback" questions with "Yes" or "No" based on whether the donor provided feedback on the campaign.
3.  Choose one of the provided "Outcome Measures" that best describe the campaign's outcome.
4.  Enter any relevant information on "Outcome Measures" in the "Notes" field.
5.  Report the campaign's budget and target amount.
6.  Record the actual amount received from the campaign.
7.  If you have any further comments or feedback from the donor, please enter them in the "Donor Survey" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Campaign Performance** (`campaign_performance`, text, optional)
    This field is used to describe the performance of the campaign.
    
### 2. **Donor Feedback** (`donor_feedback`, select_multiple, optional)
    This field is used to collect feedback from donors, with "Yes" or "No" options.
    
### 3. **Outcome Impact** (`outcome_impact`, text, optional)
    This field is used to describe the campaign's impact.
    
### 4. **Outcome Measures** (`outcome_measures`, select_one, optional)
    This field is used to select one of the provided outcome measures that best describe the campaign's outcome.
    
### 5. **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional)
    This field is for entering any additional comments or information on the campaign.
    
### 6. **Campaign Budget** (`campaign_budget`, number, optional)
    This field is used to report the campaign's budget.
    
### 7. **Target Amount** (`target_amount`, number, optional)
    This field is used to report the campaign's target amount.
    
### 8. **Actual Amount** (`actual_amount`, number, optional)
    This field is used to report the actual amount received from the campaign.
    
### 9. **Donor Survey** (`donor_survey`, text, optional)
    This field is for entering any comments or feedback from the donor.
    
### 10. **Donor Survey 2** (`donor_survey_2`, text, optional)
    This field is for entering any additional comments or feedback from the donor.
    
### 11. **Donor Survey 3** (`donor_survey_3`, text, optional)
    This field is for entering any further comments or feedback from the donor.

## Tips
Make sure to complete all applicable fields, as this form will aid in reporting and analyzing campaign performance. Review all fields carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
