# Educational Session Topic Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback on preferred topics for an educational session. By filling out the form, you can indicate which topics you'd like to attend or are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the topics listed on each page.
2. Fill in the text field next to each topic to indicate your preference.
3. If you have an email address you'd like to be contacted about the session, fill in the corresponding email field.
4. Submit the form to cast your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Topic 1 Label** (`topic_1`, text, optional): Enter your preference for Topic 1, the first topic listed.
* **Topic 2 Label** (`topic_2`, text, optional): Enter your preference for Topic 2, the second topic listed.
* **Topic 3 Label** (`topic_3`, text, optional): Enter your preference for Topic 3, the third topic listed.
* **Topic 4 Label** (`topic_4`, text, optional): Enter your preference for Topic 4, the fourth topic listed.
* **Topic 5 Label** (`topic_5`, text, optional): Enter your preference for Topic 5, the fifth topic listed.
* **Topic 6 Label** (`topic_6`, text, optional): Enter your preference for Topic 6, the sixth topic listed.
* **Email 1 Label** (`email_1`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about the session, enter your email address here.
* **Email 2 Label** (`email_2`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about the session, enter your email address here.
* **Email 3 Label** (`email_3`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about the session, enter your email address here.
* **Email 4 Label** (`email_4`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about the session, enter your email address here.

## Tips
* Please fill in all the topics you'd like to attend or are interested in.
* You can select one or multiple topics, depending on your preferences.
* If you'd like to be contacted about the session, enter a valid email address.
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure you receive updates about the session.
