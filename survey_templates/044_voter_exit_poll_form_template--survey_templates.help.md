# Voter Exit Poll Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for conducting exit polls to gather information on specific topics, such as voting behavior, opinions, and demographic data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a page to start from the page dropdown or click "Next" to navigate through the pages.
2. Fill out each page according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1: Page 1**
	+ **Page 1 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_1_label`, text, not required)
	+ This is the first page of the form, and it serves as an introduction to the survey.
* **Page 2: Page 2**
	+ **Page 2 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_2_label`, email, not required)
	+ Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive the survey results.
* **Page 3: Page 3**
	+ **Page 3 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_3_label`, time, not required)
	+ Report your time of exit from the voting location, in the format HH:MM.
* **Page 4: Page 4**
	+ **Page 4 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_4_label`, number, not required)
	+ Report the number of voters you interacted with.
* **Page 5: Page 5**
	+ **Page 5 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_5_label`, select_one, not required)
	+ Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you voted or not.
* **Page 6: Page 6**
	+ **Page 6 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_6_label`, select_multiple, not required)
	+ Select all the reasons why you voted or did not vote.
* **Page 7: Page 7**
	+ **Page 7 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_7_label`, date, not required)
	+ Report the date of the exit poll.
* **Page 8: Page 8**
	+ **Page 8 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_8_label`, number, not required)
	+ Report the number of hours spent waiting in line.
* **Page 9: Page 9**
	+ **Page 9 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_9_label`, note, not required)
	+ Take a note of any additional comments or remarks about the voting experience.
* **Page 10: Page 10**
	+ **Page 10 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_10_label`, text, not required)
	+ Report any other details about your experience.
* **Page 11: Page 11**
	+ **Page 11 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_11_label`, number, not required)
	+ Report the number of people you interacted with at the voting location.
* **Page 12: Page 12**
	+ **Page 12 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_12_label`, select_one, not required)
	+ Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you felt informed about the voting process.
* **Page 13: Page 13**
	+ **Page 13 Label** (`voter_exit_poll_form_template_page_13_label`, select_multiple, not required)
	+ Select all the reasons why you felt informed or not informed.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions honestly and accurately.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing your responses.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact us.
* This form is a template for exit polls, and your responses will help us gather information on specific topics.
* Please take your time to fill out the form carefully.
