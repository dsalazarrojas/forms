# Donor Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is designed to provide an overview of the Donor Experience Survey, ensuring that end-users understand its purpose and functionality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the survey questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **donor_info** (Text, required: False): This is the space for you to provide any additional information about your donor experience. Please type your thoughts in this field.
* **donation_history** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate whether you have a donation history with us (Yes/No).
* **donation_frequency** (Select Multiple, required: False): Select all the frequencies at which you have made donations (e.g., weekly, monthly, quarterly, etc.).
* **donation_amount** (Number, required: False): Please enter the amount of your donation.
* **giving_channel** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate the channel through which you made your donation (e.g., online, offline, etc.).
* **contact_method** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate whether you prefer to contact us through (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
* **contact_frequency** (Select Multiple, required: False): Select all the frequencies at which you prefer to contact us (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **communication_channel** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate whether you prefer to communicate with us through (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
* **communication_method** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate your preferred method of communication with us (e.g., phone, email, etc.).
* **communication_frequency** (Select Multiple, required: False): Select all the frequencies at which you prefer to communicate with us (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **experience_rating** (Number, required: False): Rate your overall experience with us (e.g., 1-5).
* **feedback** (Text, required: False): Please provide any feedback or suggestions you have about our services.
* **suggestions** (Text, required: False): Please provide any suggestions you have for improving our services.
* **follow_up** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up (Yes/No).
* **communication_channel_2** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate whether you would like to use this communication channel (Yes/No).
* **experience_rating2** (Number, required: False): Rate your experience with this communication channel (e.g., 1-5).
* **communication_channel_3** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate whether you would like to use this communication channel (Yes/No).
* **follow_up2** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up (Yes/No).
* **communication_channel_4** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate whether you would like to use this communication channel (Yes/No).
* **follow_up3** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up (Yes/No).
* **experience_rating3** (Number, required: False): Rate your experience with this communication channel (e.g., 1-5).
* **communication_channel_5** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate whether you would like to use this communication channel (Yes/No).
* **follow_up4** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up (Yes/No).
* **communication_channel_6** (Select One, required: False): Please indicate whether you would like to use this communication channel (Yes/No).
* **experience_rating4** (Number, required: False): Rate your experience with this communication channel (e.g., 1-5).
