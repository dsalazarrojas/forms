# Municipal Sludge Disposal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Municipal Sludge Disposal Survey is designed to gather information about the practices, procedures, and challenges related to municipal sludge disposal in your area. This form will help you assess the current state of sludge disposal, identify areas for improvement, and provide insights for policy-making and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
2. Be aware that fields are optional, unless marked as required.
3. Use clear and concise language in your responses.
4. If you need help or clarification, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Page

* **Municipal Sludge Disposal Survey** (`title`, `text`, optional): This is the title of the form, which will be displayed at the top of the page.
* **Section 1** (`first_page_section_1`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide a brief description of your municipal sludge disposal practices.
* **Section 2** (`first_page_section_2`, `text`, optional): In this section, please describe the types of sludge disposal methods used in your area.
* **Section 3** (`first_page_section_3`, `text`, optional): Describe the benefits and advantages of the sludge disposal methods used in your area.
* **Section 4** (`first_page_section_4`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to identify any challenges or limitations encountered in the sludge disposal process.
* **Section 5** (`first_page_section_5`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving sludge disposal practices in your area.
* **Section 6** (`first_page_section_6`, `text`, optional): In this final section, you can provide any relevant supporting documents, such as references or resources.

### Second Page

* **Sludge Disposal Methods** (`second_page`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to describe the methods and techniques used for sludge disposal in your area.
* **Methods Used** (`second_page_section_1`, `text`, optional): In this section, please list the specific sludge disposal methods used in your area, including their advantages and disadvantages.
