# Subscription Content Quality Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the quality of subscription content. Please use this form to share your thoughts, satisfaction, and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions below to the best of your ability. You can skip any question that does not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What did you think of the content**: This is a text field where you can express your overall opinion about the content. Keep it brief and concise.
* **How satisfied were you with the content**: Please select one of the three options that best describe your level of satisfaction:
	+ Option 1: Satisfied
	+ Option 2: Neutral
	+ Option 3: Dissatisfied
* **What would you recommend we improve**: This is a text field where you can suggest ideas for how we can improve the content in the future. Think about what you would find most helpful or engaging in the content, and type it in.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us refine our content and better serve our audience.
* Use plain language and clear sentences to express your thoughts.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
