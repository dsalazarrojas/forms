# Rule Of Law Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey to assess users' knowledge and understanding of the Rule of Law concept. The Rule of Law refers to a system in which all individuals are subject to the law and the laws are applied equally to all. It is essential for ensuring that the government and its institutions respect individual rights and liberties. This form aims to gauge awareness and understanding of the Rule of Law among the users, allowing us to better understand the current level of awareness and identify areas for improvement in education and advocacy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all questions that apply to you (indicated by "required: false" in the options).
2. For questions with multiple options, select all the options that you believe to be correct.
3. For question "q4", provide your answer in the text area.
4. For question "q5", rate your confidence in the Rule of Law from 1 to 10, where 1 is not confident at all and 10 is very confident.
5. For question "submission", just click on the "Submit" button after filling all the other questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (1, text, required: false): This is the starting point of the form. Read through the introduction to get familiar with the concept of the Rule of Law. Although not required, it's essential to understand the background before answering the subsequent questions.
- **What is the Rule of Law?** (2, select_one, required: false): Select one of the four options that best describe the Rule of Law. These options are:
  - The rule of law is a set of rules that govern a particular country or region.
  - The rule of law is a set of principles that guide the actions of a government.
  - The rule of law is a system of laws and regulations that protect individual rights.
  - The rule of law is a concept that refers to the idea of a just and impartial application of the law.
- **Who is most likely to be aware of the Rule of Law?** (3, select_one, required: false): Think about who might be more familiar with the Rule of Law concept in your daily life. You can choose one of the four options:
  - Judges and lawyers
  - Politicians and policymakers
  - Community members and citizens
  - Students and educators
- **Which of the following is a key component of the Rule of Law?** (4, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the options that you believe to be a key component of the Rule of Law. You can choose one or more of the four options:
  - Separation of powers
  - Independent judiciary
  - Legislative supremacy
  - Checks and balances
- **What is the primary purpose of the Rule of Law?** (5, text, required: false): In a few sentences, describe the primary purpose of the Rule of Law in your own words. Think about what it's all about.
- **How would you rate your confidence in the Rule of Law?** (6, number, required: false): Rate your confidence in the Rule of Law from 1 to 10, where 1 is not confident at all and 10 is very confident.
- **Submission** (7, time, required: false): After filling all the above questions, click on the "Submit" button. This will help us to understand your level of awareness and understanding of the Rule of Law.
