# Sports Program Accessibility Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Program Accessibility Survey is designed to collect information from participants in sports programs about their accessibility needs and experiences. This data will help improve the accessibility of sports programs to make them more enjoyable and inclusive for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact method on page 1.
2. Answer the demographic questions (e.g. gender, age, experience level) on page 1.
3. Select the accessibility features you require on page 2.
4. Provide feedback on the program on page 3.
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions on page 4.
6. Sign and date the form on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where you participated in the sports program.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility where you participated in the sports program.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the sports program you participated in.
* **Participant Gender**: Select your gender (Female, Male, Non-Binary).
* **Participant Age**: Enter your age.
* **Participant Experience**: Select your level of experience with sports (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Contact Method**: Select how you would prefer to be contacted (Phone, Email, In-Person).
* **Accessibility Features**: Select any accessibility features that you require (Wheelchair Accessibility, Sign Language Interpretation, Large Print or Audio Materials, Accessible Parking).
* **Feedback on Program**: Provide any feedback or comments about the sports program.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions on how to improve the program.
* **Accessibility Barriers**: Select any accessibility barriers you experienced (Physical, Communication, Social, Cultural).
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions on how to improve the program.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign and date your name to confirm that this is your response.
* **Staff Signature**: Sign and date the signature of the staff member who assisted with completing this form.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you need assistance completing the form, don't hesitate to ask a staff member for help.
* Please keep the form to yourself and do not share it with others.
* If you have already completed a survey on the same topic, please do not complete this form again.
