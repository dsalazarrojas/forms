# Conference Panel Discussion Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees on a conference panel discussion. It assesses various aspects of the panel, including the overall quality, content relevance, and effectiveness of the panelists.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address (optional).
2. Provide the name of the conference and the panel discussion you are evaluating.
3. Rate the panel discussion on overall quality, content relevance, and audience engagement.
4. Provide feedback on the panelists' expertise, effectiveness, and interaction.
5. Share your favorite panelist and any improvement suggestions.
6. Answer questions about whether you would recommend this panel and whether you would attend similar events in the future.
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback.
