# Team Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on team performance, leadership, and dynamics. It will help teams identify areas of strength and weakness, and provide insights for growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as you feel is necessary to help your team understand its performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **team_leader_survey**: A description of the team leader's performance, strengths, and areas for improvement. (Required)
* **team_member_survey**: A description of the team member's performance, strengths, and areas for improvement. (Required)
* **feedback**: Any feedback or comments about the team or its performance. (Optional)
* **team_leader_feedback**: Feedback about the team leader's performance. (Optional)
* **team_members**: A list or description of team members, including their roles or functions. (Optional)
* **team_diversity**: A description of the team's diversity, including demographics, backgrounds, or perspectives. (Optional)
* **team_inclusion**: A description of how the team values and promotes inclusion. (Optional)
* **team_sustainability**: A description of the team's sustainability efforts, if any. (Optional)
* **team_leadership**: A description of the team's leadership style, strengths, and areas for improvement. (Optional)
* **team_skills**: A description of the team's skills, including technical, soft, or adaptive skills. (Optional)
* **team_work_environment**: A description of the team's work environment, including workspace, culture, or policies. (Optional)
* **communication**: A description of the team's communication style, strengths, and areas for improvement. (Optional)
* **decision_making**: A description of the team's decision-making process, strengths, and areas for improvement. (Optional)
* **collaboration**: A description of the team's collaboration style, strengths, and areas for improvement. (Optional)
* **conflict_resolution**: A description of the team's conflict resolution process, strengths, and areas for improvement. (Optional)
* **adaptability**: A description of the team's adaptability, including ability to pivot or adjust to changing circumstances. (Optional)
* **team_adaptability**: A description of the team's adaptability, including ability to pivot or adjust to changing circumstances. (This field is a duplicate of the previous field, and may be removed or merged with it.) (Optional)
* **team_spirit**: A description of the team's esprit de corps, or team spirit. (Optional)
* **feedback_frequency**: A description of how often the team receives feedback, and its quality. (Optional)
* **team_safety**: A description of the team's safety protocols, or measures to ensure member safety. (Optional)
* **team_trust**: A description of the team's level of trust, among members or between members and leaders. (Optional)
* **team_wellness**: A description of the team's focus on member well-being, including physical and mental health. (Optional)
* **submit**: A submission button to complete the survey. (Required)

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can choose to answer any or all of them, depending on your team's specific needs and circumstances.
