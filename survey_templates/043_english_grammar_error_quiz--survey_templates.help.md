# English Grammar Error Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The English Grammar Error Quiz is a tool designed to test your knowledge of English grammar rules and identify areas where you need improvement. This quiz is meant to be a fun and interactive way to assess your understanding of English grammar and syntax.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the English Grammar Error Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your age in the **Age** field.
4. Rate your performance on each of the 7 grammar quiz questions using the **Score 1** to **Score 7** fields.
5. Enter a valid email address in the **Email** field if you'd like to be contacted with your results or further instructions.
6. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field if you'd like to receive a call-back with your results or further instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age in years.
* **Score 1**, **Score 2**, **Score 3**, **Score 4**, **Score 5**, and **Score 7** (`score_1`, `score_2`, `score_3`, `score_4`, `score_5`, `score_7`, number, optional): Rate your performance on each of the 7 grammar quiz questions. Choose a score from 0 to 100.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address if you'd like to be contacted with your results or further instructions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to receive a call-back with your results or further instructions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is valid and functional to receive any follow-up communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your results, feel free to contact us using the email or phone number provided.
* If you're having trouble submitting the form, try checking that all required fields are filled out.
