# Holiday Office Hours Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on holiday office hours. It helps us understand how employees feel about the current office hours and gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the office hours that you are aware of.
2. Indicate your satisfaction with the current state of office hours.
3. Choose your preferred office hours.
4. Enter your email address (optional).
5. Enter your phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Office Hours Awareness**
  (Office Hours Awareness, select_multiple, no): Select all the office hours that you are aware of. Please choose all the options that apply.
  - Option 1: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
  - Option 2: 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  - Option 3: 11:00 AM - 7:00 PM
- **Satisfaction**
  (Satisfaction, select_one, no): Indicate how satisfied you are with the current state of office hours.
  - Highly Satisfied
  - Somewhat Satisfied
  - Not Satisfied
- **Preferred Hours**
  (Preferred Hours, select_one, no): Choose your preferred office hours.
  - 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
  - 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  - 11:00 AM - 7:00 PM
- **Email**
  (Email, email, no): Enter your email address (optional).
- **Phone**
  (Phone, text, no): Enter your phone number (optional).
