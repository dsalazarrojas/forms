# Event Production Scheduling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from event organizers to schedule their event production needs. The form is used to gather details about the event, including venue information, event dates, and production requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your event information, including venue details and event dates.
2. Specify your event preferences, such as audio-visual equipment requirements.
3. Share your contact details for follow-up communication.
4. Describe your production needs, including personnel and equipment requirements.
5. Outline your production resources, including any in-house or external services.
6. Establish a timeline for your event production.
7. Confirm your event production preferences, including any specific requirements for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Venue Information** (id: 1, `text`, required: false): Enter the name, address, and any other relevant details about the event venue.
* **Event Details** (id: 2, `text`, required: false): Describe the event you are organizing, including its type, size, and expected attendance.
* **Event Dates** (id: 3, `text`, required: false): Specify the start and end dates of your event, including any setup or tear-down requirements.
* **Event Preferences** (id: 4, `text`, required: false): List any special requirements or preferences for your event, such as catering options or security needs.
* **Contact Details** (id: 5, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, email, and phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Production Needs** (id: 6, `text`, required: false): Describe your production requirements, including personnel and equipment needs.
* **Production Resources** (id: 7, `text`, required: false): List any in-house or external resources you will be using for production, such as staff or rental equipment.
* **Timeline** (id: 8, `text`, required: false): Establish a detailed timeline for your event production, including key milestones and deadlines.
* **Event Production Preferences** (id: 9, `text`, required: false): Confirm any specific production preferences or requirements for your event.
