# Educator Testimonial Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect testimonials from educators, providing a platform for institutions to gather feedback, opinions, and experiences from their teaching staff. The collected data will help institutions understand their educators' perspectives, improving overall teaching quality and student outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the **Title** field with the name of the program or initiative you are providing feedback on.
2. Enter your **Name** as it appears on official documents or records.
3. Provide a valid **Email** address that is associated with your institutional account.
4. Select the relevant **Program** that you are providing feedback on from the list of options.
5. Choose the **Department** you are affiliated with.
6. Write a detailed **Testimonial** describing your experience, feedback, or opinion on the program.
7. Enter the **Date of Testimonial**, when you provided your feedback or opinion.
8. Select the current **Approval Status** of your testimonial from the list of options.
9. If your testimonial has been approved, enter the **Approval Date** it was approved on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Educator Testimonial Collection Form**: This is the main section of the form where all other fields are located.
* **Title**: A brief name for the program or initiative you are providing feedback on.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents or records.
* **Email**: A valid email address associated with your institutional account.
* **Program**: Select the relevant program from the list of options.
* **Department**: Choose the department you are affiliated with.
* **Testimonial**: Write a detailed description of your experience, feedback, or opinion on the program.
* **Date of Testimonial**: Enter the date when you provided your feedback or opinion.
* **Approval Status**: Select the current approval status of your testimonial from the list of options.
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date your testimonial was approved on, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not already submitted a testimonial for this form to avoid duplication.
* Be honest and provide your genuine feedback and opinions.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling this form, please contact the form administrators at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
