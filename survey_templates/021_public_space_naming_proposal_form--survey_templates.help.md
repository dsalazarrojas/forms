# Public Space Naming Proposal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect community suggestions for proposed names for public spaces within the organization. Submissions will be reviewed and considered for approval. Please only submit one proposal per form, and ensure that all required fields are completed accurately. Duplicate submissions will be rejected.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your proposed name in the "Proposed Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the location in the "Describe the location and propose a name for the park or plaza" field.
3. Explain the reasoning behind your proposed name in the "Reason for proposed name" field.
4. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, in the "Your Name", "Your Email", and "Your Phone" fields.
5. Choose whether you agree to the terms of use for the submitted proposal.
6. Add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional comments" field.
7. Finally, submit the form with all required fields completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Community Suggestions** (`public_space_naming_form`, text, not required): This is the primary field where you can submit your proposal. Please enter a suggested name for a public space within the organization.
* **Parks and Plazas** (`parks_plazas`, text, not required): This field is not currently used in the form, but you can enter additional information about the park or plaza if you like.
* **Park Name** (`park_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the park or plaza you are proposing.
* **Describe the location and propose a name for the park or plaza** (`park_plaza_description`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the location and propose a name for the park or plaza.
* **Proposed Name** (`proposed_name`, text, not required): Enter your proposed name for the public space.
* **Reason for proposed name** (`reason_for_choice`, text, not required): Explain the reasoning behind your proposed name.
* **Your Name** (`submitter_name`, text, not required): Enter your name, so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Submission Date** (`submit_date`, date, not required): The date and time you submit this form.
* **Submission Time** (`submit_time`, time, not required): The time you submit this form.
* **Your Email** (`submitter_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Your Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **I agree to terms of use** (`agree_tos`, select_one, not required): Choose whether you agree to the terms of use for the submitted proposal.
* **Additional comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, not required): Add any additional comments or suggestions for the proposal.
