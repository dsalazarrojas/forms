# Customer Loyalty Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experience with your company's customer loyalty program. It's crucial to understand their satisfaction and identify areas for improvement to enhance the program's effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for each field to provide your feedback.
2. For fields that allow multiple selections, choose all applicable options.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Recommendations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Loyalty Program**
**<label>`Customer Loyalty Program`</label> (Select One, Required): 
Indicate whether the customer has participated in your customer loyalty program.

*   **Select 'Yes'** if the customer is or was a participant in your loyalty program.
*   **Select 'No'** if the customer has not participated in your program.

### 2. **Customer Satisfaction**
**<label>`Customer Satisfaction`</label> (Select One, Required): 
Rate your overall satisfaction with your experience with our company.

*   **Select 'Satisfied'** if you are generally satisfied with your experience.
*   **Select 'Dissatisfied'** if you are not satisfied with your experience.

### 3. **Overall Experience**
**<label>`Overall Experience`</label> (Select Multiple, Required): 
Select all that apply to describe your overall experience with our company.

*   **Select 'Yes'** for any of the following experiences that apply:
    *   Quality of products or services
    *   Responsiveness of customer service
    *   Timeliness of delivery
    *   Other (please specify in the "Recommendations" field)

### 4. **Recommendations**
**<label>`Recommendations`</label> (Optional, Text): 
Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving our customer loyalty program.

*   If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving the program, type them here.

### 5. **Program Improvement**
**<label>`Program Improvement`</label> (Optional, Text): 
Please provide any specific areas where you think our customer loyalty program can be improved.

*   Be as specific as possible with your suggestions or ideas for improvement.

### 6. **Satisfaction Level**
**<label>`Satisfaction Level`</label> (Optional, Number): 
Rate your satisfaction level with our customer loyalty program (1-5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest).

*   Use the scale to rate your overall satisfaction with the program.

### 7. **Program Value**
**<label>`Program Value`</label> (Optional, Number): 
Rate the value you perceive in our customer loyalty program (1-5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest).

*   Use the scale to rate the value you derive from the program.

### 8. **Customer Service**
**<label>`Customer Service`</label> (Select One, Required): 
Indicate whether you have received satisfactory customer service from our company.

*   **Select 'Yes'** if you have received satisfactory customer service.
*   **Select 'No'** if you did not receive satisfactory customer service.
