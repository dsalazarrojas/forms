# Sustainability Certification Value Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sustainability Certification Value Survey is a tool used to gauge the importance of different sustainability certifications in an industry or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose one of the available certifications from the drop-down menu on **Certification** page 1.
2. Provide any additional information or comments in the **Notes** field on page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Certification** (page 1, select_one, required: false):
    Select the certification that is most important or relevant to you. This will help us understand which certification has the highest value.
* **Value** (page 2, text, required: false):
    Use this field to elaborate on the reasons why this certification is important or provide any additional context about your answer.
* **Notes** (page 3, note, required: false):
    Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share about the certification you selected.

## Tips
* Please select only one certification per page.
* You can provide more details in the **Notes** field if you need to elaborate on your answer.
* If you are unsure about any of the certifications, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
