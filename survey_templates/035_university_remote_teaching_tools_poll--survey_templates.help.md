# university_remote_teaching_tools_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the usage patterns, improvement priorities, and opinions on remote teaching platforms from university stakeholders. It will help us understand their experiences and suggestions to enhance the effectiveness of these platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read each question carefully and answer them truthfully to the best of your abilities.
2. Fill in the required information for each field, marked as "required".
3. Select the options that apply to your experiences and opinions.
4. For fields with "Select one" or "Select multiple" options, choose the one(s) that best match your answers.
5. You can add any additional comments or feedback in the "Do you have any additional comments" field.
6. Finally, click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, text, optional): Please provide a brief introduction to your answers. This can be a personal story, a description of your experience with remote teaching platforms, or any other relevant information.
* **What are the usage patterns of remote teaching platforms?** (`What_are_the_usage_patterns_of_remote_teaching_platforms`, text, optional): Describe how you typically use remote teaching platforms.
* **What are the improvement priorities?** (`What_are_the_improvement_priorities`, text, optional): What do you think should be improved in remote teaching platforms?
* **Which teaching methods are used most frequently?** (`Which_teaching_methods_are_used_most_frequently`, select_one, optional): Select the teaching methods you use most frequently when using remote teaching platforms.
	+ **Remote lectures**
	+ **Video recording**
	+ **Live campus**
* **What do you feel about the current status of remote teaching?** (`What_do_you_feel_about_the_current_status_of_remote_teaching`, text, optional): Share your opinions or concerns about the current status of remote teaching platforms.
* **Do you have any additional comments?** (`Do_you_have_any_additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): If you have an email address where you can be contacted for further discussion, please provide it.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please provide it.
* **Email/Phone** (`Email_Phone`, select_multiple, optional): Select which of the above fields you have (Email, Phone, or both).
	+ **1**
	+ **2**
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, optional): Click to submit your response.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate in your answers.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Do you have any additional comments" field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or administrator for guidance.
