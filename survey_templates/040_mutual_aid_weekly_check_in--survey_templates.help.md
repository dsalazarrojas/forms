# Mutual Aid Weekly Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mutual Aid Weekly Check In form is a tool for mutual aid groups to check in with each other on a weekly basis. This form is meant to be filled out by each group member to provide a brief summary of their current wellbeing, the help they have provided or received, and their plans and goals for the upcoming week.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as necessary to give your group a clear picture of your wellbeing.
2. Be honest and accurate when reporting your help provided or received.
3. Choose the correct options for the 'Total connections' and 'Total help given' fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weekly Check-in** (`weekly_checkin`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your current wellbeing and any notable events or challenges from the past week.
* **How are you doing this week?** (`wellbeing`, `text`, required: false): Share your current emotional state, how you're coping with stressors, and any self-care activities you've engaged in.
* **What help have you been able to give or receive this week?** (`wellbeing_what_helps`, `text`, required: false): Report any help you've provided or received, including emotional support, practical assistance, or other forms of aid.
* **What stressors have you encountered this week?** (`wellbeing_what_stressors`, `text`, required: false): Describe any challenges or stressors you've faced, including personal, social, or environmental factors.
* **What support do you have this week?** (`wellbeing_what_support`, `text`, required: false): Share any resources or support systems you have in place, including family, friends, therapy, or other forms of aid.
* **What plans have you made for next week?** (`wellbeing_plans`, `text`, required: false): Outline your goals and plans for the upcoming week, including any appointments, deadlines, or tasks you need to complete.
* **What goals or tasks do you have for next week?** (`wellbeing_goals`, `text`, required: false): Share any specific goals or tasks you aim to achieve in the next week.
* **Any new connections or relationships made this week?** (`wellbeing_connections`, `text`, required: false): Report any new connections or relationships you've formed, including friends, family, or colleagues.
* **Notes from the group** (`wellbeing_notes`, `text`, required: false): Leave any additional comments or concerns for the group to discuss.
* **Total connections** (`wellbeing_connections_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the number of connections you've made this week, using the following options: 0-1, 1-2, 2-3, 3+.
* **Total help given** (`wellbeing_helped`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the amount of help you've given out this week, using the following options: 0-1, 1-2, 2-3, 3+.
