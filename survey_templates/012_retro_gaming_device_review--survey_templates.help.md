# Retro Gaming Device Review - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers review and provide feedback on retro gaming devices, which are games consoles or machines from past years. It is used to gather information about a specific retro gaming device, including the model, year of production, availability, condition, price range, and serial number. The form is designed to be as clear and concise as possible while collecting the most relevant and useful information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
3. If a field is not required but you want to provide additional information, you can add it in the "What is your comment" field.
4. If you have an image or video that you want to share with us, you can add the URL in the corresponding fields.
5. Once you have filled out all the fields, click the submit button to send your review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retro Gaming Device** (`title`, required: false): This field is for the title of the retro gaming device you are reviewing.
* **What is your name** (`question1`, required: false): Please enter your name or nickname.
* **What is your email** (`question2`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **What is your phone** (`question3`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **What is your rating** (`question4`, required: false): Rate the device from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What is your review** (`question5`, required: false): Describe your experience with the device.
* **What is the model of the device** (`question6`, required: false): Enter the model number or name of the device.
* **What is the year of production** (`question7`, required: false): Enter the year the device was produced.
* **What is the availability** (`question8`, required: false): Select from "available", "unavailable", or "not sure".
* **What is the condition** (`question9`, required: false): Select from "working", "not working", or "not tested".
* **What is the price range** (`question10`, required: false): Select from "under 100", "100-500", "501-1000", "1000-2000", or "2000-5000".
* **What is the serial number** (`question11`, required: false): Enter the serial number of the device.
* **What is the region of sale** (`question12`, required: false): Select from "us", "eu", "asia", "au", or "other".
* **What is your rating2** (`question14`, required: false): Rate the device from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What is your comment** (`question15`, required: false): Add any additional comments about the device.
* **What is your image** (`question16`, required: false): Add the URL of an image of the device.
* **What is the URL of your image** (`question17`, required: false): Add the URL of an image of the device.
* **What is the URL of your video** (`question18`, required: false): Add the URL of a video about the device.
* **What is your screenshot of your console** (`question19`, required: false): Add the URL of a screenshot of the console.

Please note that all fields are optional except for the "Retro Gaming Device" field, which is used to identify the title of the device. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
