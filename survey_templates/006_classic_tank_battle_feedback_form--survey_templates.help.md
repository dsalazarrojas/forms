# Classic Tank Battle Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from players about their experience with the classic tank battle game.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your in-game username to help us identify your feedback.
2. If you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please enter your email address.
3. Provide the date of your playing session and the date you are submitting this feedback.
4. Rate your overall experience with the game.
5. Share your thoughts on the gameplay mechanics.
6. Let us know if you encountered any bugs or technical issues.
7. Describe any bugs or issues you experienced.
8. (Optional) Rate the severity of the issue.
9. Explain the steps to reproduce the bug (if applicable).
10. Share your suggestions for new features or improvements.
11. Indicate the priority of your suggested improvements.
12. Tell us if you're interested in multiplayer features.
13. Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Username (1)**: Enter your in-game username to help us identify your feedback.
* **Email Address (2)**: If you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback, enter your email address. (Optional)
* **Date of Feedback (3)**: Provide the date of your feedback.
* **Session or Playtest Date (4)**: Enter the date of your playing session.
* **Overall Game Experience Rating (6)**: Rate your overall experience with the game, from Excellent (5/5) to Poor (1/5).
* **Gameplay Mechanics (7)**: Share your thoughts on the gameplay mechanics, such as the tank controls and overall gameplay feel.
* **Game Difficulty Balance (8)**: Let us know if the difficulty was too easy, just right, or too difficult.
* **Graphics and Visual Quality (9)**: Rate the visual presentation of the game. (Optional)
* **Audio and Sound Effects Quality (10)**: Rate the sound design and music in the game. (Optional)
* **Bug Reports and Issues (12)**: Indicate if you encountered any bugs or technical issues.
* **Did you encounter any bugs or technical issues? (13)**: Choose 'True' if you encountered any issues.
* **Please describe the bugs or issues (14)**: Be as specific as possible about what happened.
* **Severity of Issues (15)**: (Optional) Rate the severity of the issue, from Critical - Game Breaking to Cosmetic - Visual Only.
* **Steps to Reproduce the Bug (16)**: Explain the steps to reproduce the issue, if applicable.
* **Feature Suggestions and Improvements (17)**: Share your suggestions for new features or improvements.
* **Feature Suggestions (18)**: Provide a brief description of your suggested features or improvements.
* **Priority of Suggested Improvements (19)**: Indicate the priority of your suggested improvements, from Critical Priority to Nice to Have.
* **Would you be interested in multiplayer features? (20)**: Tell us if you're interested in multiplayer features.
* **Additional Comments (21)**: Provide any other feedback or suggestions you have. (Optional)
