# Nile River Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience on a Nile River cruise. Please take a few minutes to answer the following questions and provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill out the required fields. You may skip questions that do not apply to you.
- Each page will have one or more questions, and you will be able to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.
- Use the provided options to select your answers, unless otherwise specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info (Page 1)
- **User Info** (`user_info`, text, Optional): This is a text field where you can optionally provide any additional information about yourself, such as your name, contact information, or other details about your experience.

### Have you been on a Nile River cruise before? (Page 2)
- **Have you been on a Nile River cruise before?** (`question_1`, select_multiple, Optional): This question asks about your previous experience with Nile River cruises. Please select 'Yes' if you have taken a cruise before, and 'No' if you haven't. 

### What type of experience did you have? (Page 2)
- **What type of experience did you have?** (`question_2`, select_multiple, Optional): This question allows you to select one or more types of experiences you had on the cruise, such as 'River cruises', 'On land tours', or 'Other'.

### How satisfied are you with your experience? (Page 3)
- **How satisfied are you with your experience?** (`question_3`, select_one, Optional): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your experience. Please select one of the three options: 'Very satisfied', 'Neutral', or 'Very dissatisfied'.

### What would you like to see improved? (Page 4)
- **What would you like to see improved?** (`question_4`, text, Optional): This is a text field where you can provide any suggestions or feedback on how we can improve our services.

### Additional comments (Page 5)
- **Additional comments** (`question_5`, note, Optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience. 

### Do you recommend our Nile River cruises to others? (Page 6)
- **Do you recommend our Nile River cruises to others?** (`question_7`, select_multiple, Optional): This question asks about your willingness to recommend our cruises to others. Please select one or both of the options: 'True' or 'False'.

### How many days did you spend on the cruise? (Page 7)
- **How many days did you spend on the cruise?** (`question_8`, number, Optional): This question asks about the duration of your cruise. Please enter the number of days you spent on the cruise.

## Tips
- Take your time to carefully review each question and provide accurate and honest feedback.
- If you have any trouble with the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
