# naturalDisasterIncidentForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The naturalDisasterIncidentForm is used to collect information about incidents caused by natural disasters, such as floods, fires, earthquakes, and hurricanes. This form helps us to gather data on incidents that affect organizations and individuals, including damage assessments and relief efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field that apply to the incident.
3. If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or indicate that it is not available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (1): Please enter the date of the incident, if known.
* **incidentName** (2): Enter the name of the incident, e.g., "Flood on Main Street".
* **incidentLocation** (3): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Affected Organizations** (4): Select all organizations affected by the incident. You can choose multiple organizations from the list.
* **incidentType** (5): Select the type of incident that occurred, such as "Flood", "Fire", "Earthquake", etc.
* **Damage Assessment** (6): Enter a numerical value to assess the damage caused by the incident.
* **Relief Assessment** (7): Enter a numerical value to assess the relief efforts required.
* **Damage Description** (8): Provide a brief description of the damage caused by the incident.
* **Relief Description** (9): Provide a brief description of the relief efforts provided.
* **Affected People** (10): Enter the number of people affected by the incident.
* **Affected Infrastructure** (11): Enter the infrastructure affected by the incident.
* **Notes** (12): Enter any additional information or notes about the incident.

## Tips
* Please be thorough and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities or experts for guidance.
* Remember to save your progress and review the form before submitting it.
* If you need to make changes to the form, please do not duplicate efforts by submitting multiple forms for the same incident.
