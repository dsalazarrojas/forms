# work_from_home_policy_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from employees about their experiences with the work-from-home policy. This includes feedback on how often they work from home and their satisfaction with the policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best match your experience. If you have not worked from home before, select "Never." If you have any comments or suggestions for improvement, please include them in the "Any comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How often do you work from home (1)**: This question asks about your typical work-from-home frequency. Please select the option that best matches your experience.
* **Where do you work from (2)**: Please choose the location where you typically work from home.
* **How satisfied are you with your current work from home policy (3)**: Rate your satisfaction with the policy on a scale of 1-10.
* **Any comments (4)**: If you have any thoughts or suggestions about the policy, please include them here.
* **Satisfaction with work from home policy (5)**: This field is for providing additional comments about your satisfaction with the policy.
* **Frequency of work from home (6)**: This field is not currently used. Please skip this question.
* **Work from home frequency (7)**: This question is similar to (1). Please select the option that best matches your experience.
* **Where do you work from (8)**: This question is similar to (2). Please choose the location where you typically work from home.
* **Satisfaction with work from home (9)**: This field is for providing additional comments about your satisfaction with the policy, similar to (5).

## Tips
* Be honest in your answers, as this will help us understand the effectiveness of the work-from-home policy.
* If you have not worked from home before, please select "Never" for question (1) and skip the rest of the questions related to work-from-home frequency.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact your supervisor or HR department.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, so please take a few minutes to complete this form carefully.
