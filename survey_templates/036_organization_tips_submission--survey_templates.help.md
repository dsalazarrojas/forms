To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Organization Tips Submission**: This is the main section where you can submit your organization tips.
2. **Supporting Document**: If you have any supporting documents, such as images or videos, you can upload them here.
3. **Submission Comments**: Add any additional comments or explanations for your submission, if needed.
4. **Submission Date**: Enter the date when you submitted your organization tips.
