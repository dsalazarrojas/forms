# Student Clubs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about student clubs, including their activities, status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form to provide information about your student club.
- You can skip any questions that do not apply to your club.
- If you need assistance or clarification, please don't hesitate to contact the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. What is the purpose of your club?
* **What is the purpose of your club?** (`title`, text, required/optional): This field is a free-text field where you can describe the main purpose or objective of your student club. Please provide a brief but clear explanation of what your club does.

### 2. What activities do your club members participate in?
* **What activities do your club members participate in?** (`activities`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more activities from the list to describe what your club members engage in. You can choose one or multiple activities that apply to your club.

### 3. What is the current condition of your club?
* **What is the current condition of your club?** (`club_condition`, select_one, required/optional): Choose from the available options (Active, Inactive, Unknown) to indicate the current status of your club.

### 4. How many members are in your club?
* **How many members are in your club?** (`number_of_members`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of members in your club.

### 5. Who is the advisor or sponsor of your club?
* **Who is the advisor or sponsor of your club?** (`advisor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the advisor or sponsor of your club.

### 6. What is the advisor's email address?
* **What is the advisor's email address?** (`advisor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the advisor or sponsor of your club.

### 7. What is the advisor's phone number?
* **What is the advisor's phone number?** (`advisor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the advisor or sponsor of your club.

### 8. How often do your club members meet?
* **How often do your club members meet?** (`club_meeting_time`, select_one, required/optional): Choose from the available options (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Never) to indicate how often your club members meet.

### 9. Where do your club members meet?
* **Where do your club members meet?** (`meeting_location`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more locations from the available options (Cafeteria, Library, Classroom, Other) to describe where your club members meet.

### 10. What is the name of your club?
* **What is the name of your club?** (`student_club_name`, text, required/optional): Enter a clear and concise name for your student club.

### 11. What is the email address for your club?
* **What is the email address for your club?** (`student_club_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address for your club.

### 12. What is the phone number for your club?
* **What is the phone number for your club?** (`student_club_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number for your club.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields if you can.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out for help.
