# Final Expense Quote Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a client's insurance policy details. It should only be filled out once for each policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Client Information section with the client's personal details.
2. Enter the Policy Details section with the relevant information about the policy.
3. Select the Policy Type and Policy Status to accurately represent the policy.
4. Enter the Premium Frequency and Premium Amount for the policy.
5. Select the Premium Frequency 2 and Premium Amount 2 for the second policy, if applicable.
6. Enter the Premium Start Date and Premium End Date for both policies.
7. Enter the Policy Effective Date and Policy Expiration Date for both policies.
8. Select the Premium Frequency 2 for both policies.
9. Finally, review all the information entered to ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information (1)**: Enter the client's name, company name, and address to identify the policy holder.
- **Policy Details (2)**: Enter a brief description of the policy, its policy number, and any other relevant details.
- **Contact Information (3)**: Enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number for the policy holder.
- **Policy Type (4)**: Select the type of policy (Life, Health, or Annuity) to accurately categorize the policy.
- **Policy Status (5)**: Select the status of the policy (Active, Inactive, or Pending) to reflect its current state.
- **Premium Frequency (6)**: Select the frequency of premium payments (Annually, Monthly, or Quarterly) for the policy.
- **Premium Amount (7)**: Enter the amount of premium to be paid for the policy.
- **Premium Start Date (8)**: Enter the start date of premium payments for the policy.
- **Premium End Date (9)**: Enter the end date of premium payments for the policy.
- **Policy Effective Date (10)**: Enter the effective date of the policy.
- **Policy Expiration Date (11)**: Enter the expiration date of the policy.
- **Premium Frequency 2 (12)**: Select the frequency of premium payments for the second policy, if applicable.
- **Premium Amount 2 (13)**: Enter the amount of premium to be paid for the second policy, if applicable.
- **Premium Start Date 2 (14)**: Enter the start date of premium payments for the second policy, if applicable.
- **Premium End Date 2 (15)**: Enter the end date of premium payments for the second policy, if applicable.
- **Policy Type 2 (16)**: Select the type of policy for the second policy.
- **Policy Status 2 (15)**: Select the status of the second policy (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
- **Policy Expiration Date 2 (16)**: Enter the expiration date of the second policy.

## Tips
- Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct policy.
- Double-check all entered information for accuracy and completeness.
- If you are not sure about any information, please contact the policy holder or their representative.
