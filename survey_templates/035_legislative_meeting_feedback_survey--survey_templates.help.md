# Legislative Meeting Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on legislative meetings, helping meeting organizers and administrators to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant ID, which is usually your name or a unique identifier assigned to you by the meeting organizers.
2. Provide the date and time of the meeting.
3. Enter the location of the meeting.
4. Indicate whether you were a participant in the meeting.
5. Choose whether you attended the meeting.
6. Provide your feedback on the meeting, including any suggestions or comments.
7. Check if you'll need any follow-up actions from the meeting organizers.
8. Enter the date and time of the next meeting, if applicable.
9. Provide any additional comments or feedback on the follow-up actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Participant Id (Number)
* **Participant Id** (`participant_id`, Number, Required: False): Enter your unique identifier or your name to help track your feedback.
### 2. Session Date (Date)
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, Date, Required: False): Enter the date of the meeting.
### 3. Session Time (Time)
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, Time, Required: False): Enter the time of the meeting.
### 4. Session Location (Text)
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, Text, Required: False): Enter the location of the meeting.
### 5. Meeting Participants (Select Multiple)
* **Meeting Participants** (`meeting_participants`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Check all that apply to indicate which meeting participants you interacted with during the meeting.
### 6. Did Participant Attend (Select One)
* **Did Participant Attend** (`participant_attended`, Select One, Required: False): Choose how you attended the meeting.
### 7. Participant Feedback (Note)
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, Note, Required: False): Provide your feedback on the meeting, including any suggestions or comments.
### 8. Follow-up Actions (Select Multiple)
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Check if you need any follow-up actions from the meeting organizers.
### 9. Next Meeting (Date)
* **Next Meeting** (`next_meeting`, Date, Required: False): Enter the date and time of the next meeting, if applicable.
### 10. Follow-up Comments (Text)
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, Text, Required: False): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the follow-up actions.
