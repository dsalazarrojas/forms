# Devotion Sharing Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This guide is to help you complete the Devotion Sharing Survey form. The form is designed to gather your thoughts, habits, and beliefs related to devotion and prayer practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and as thoroughly as possible.
2. For questions with select options, choose the answer that best matches your feelings or experiences.
3. For free-form text fields, provide a brief description or reflection on your response.
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Additional Comments" text field.
5. Please provide your Email Address and Phone Number if you feel comfortable sharing them for any follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How Do You Pray**:
	+ (1) `how_do_you_pray` (`text`, `required: false`)
	+ A place where you can describe your current prayer practices or habits.
* **Time Spent Praying**:
	+ (2) `time_spent_praying` (`number`, `required: false`)
	+ Please enter the number of minutes you spend praying per day.
* **Where Do You Pray**:
	+ (3) `where_do_you_pray` (`select_multiple`, `required: false`)
	+ Choose all the places where you typically pray, such as Praying with family, Alone, In church or place of worship, or Other.
* **Reflection on Your Prayer Practice**:
	+ (4) `reflection_on_your_prayer_practice` (`select_one`, `required: false`)
	+ Please select how often you reflect on your prayer practice: Not at all, Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently, Almost every day.
* **How Do You Reflect on Your Devotion**:
	+ (5) `how_do_you_reflect_on_your_devotion` (`text`, `required: false`)
	+ Please describe how you reflect on your devotion, such as through journaling, meditation, or prayer.
* **Why Do You Believe Devotion Matters**:
	+ (6) `why_do_you_believe_devotion_matters` (`text`, `required: false`)
	+ Explain why you believe devotion is important or matters to you.
* **What Helps Your Devotion**:
	+ (7) `what_helps_your_devotion` (`text`, `required: false`)
	+ Describe what helps you maintain or grow in your devotion, such as prayer, scripture, or community connections.
* **Challenges to Your Devotion**:
	+ (8) `challenges_to_your_devotion` (`text`, `required: false`)
	+ Discuss any challenges you face in your devotion, such as time constraints, doubts, or fears.
* **How Can We Support Your Devotion**:
	+ (9) `how_can_we_support_your_devotion` (`select_multiple`, `required: false`)
	+ Choose how you would like to be supported in your devotion, such as through Prayer and spiritual guidance, Community connection, Personal growth, or Other.
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ (10) `additional_comments` (`note`, `required: false`)
	+ If you have any additional thoughts or comments, please share them here.
* **Email Address**:
	+ (11) `email_address` (`email`, `required: false`)
	+ Please provide your email address if you would like to receive any follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number**:
	+ (12) `phone_number` (`text`, `required: false`)
	+ If you would like to receive phone calls for any follow-up communication, please provide your phone number.

Note: This help guide is for reference only, and the actual questions and fields on the form may vary based on the input schema provided.
