# Physical Fitness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your physical fitness habits, health concerns, and demographics to inform the development of a personalized fitness program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each field as accurately and truthfully as possible.
2. Select the options that best match your physical fitness habits and demographics.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exercise Habits** (`exercise_habits`, text, optional): Describe your regular exercise habits, such as the types of exercise you enjoy or do regularly.
* **Health Concerns** (`health_concerns`, text, optional): Share any health concerns or conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goals`, text, optional): Outline your short-term and long-term fitness goals, such as weight loss, weight gain, or improving overall health.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_one, optional): Indicate how often you exercise, whether it's `Regularly`, `Occasionally`, `Rarely`, or `Never`.
* **Exercise Duration** (`exercise_duration`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable durations for your exercise sessions, such as `Less Than 30 minutes`, `30-59 minutes`, `60-89 minutes`, `90-119 minutes`, or `More than 2 hours`.
* **Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, select_one, optional): Choose your current fitness level, such as `Beginner`, `Intermediate`, `Advanced`, or `Expert`.
* **Physical Activities** (`physical_activities`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable physical activities you participate in, such as `Walking`, `Running`, `Swimming`, `Cycling`, or `Other`.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate your overall health status, such as `Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, or `Poor`.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable medical conditions you have, such as `None`, `High Blood Pressure`, `Diabetes`, `Heart Disease`, or `Other`.
* **Doctor Consulted** (`doctor_consulted`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you have consulted a doctor, whether it's `True` or `False`.
* **Exercise Type** (`exercise_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable types of exercise you enjoy or practice, such as `Aerobics`, `Weightlifting`, `Stretching`, `Yoga`, or `Other`.
* **Physical Fitness Survey** (`physical_fitness_survey`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you have taken this survey before, whether it's `True` or `False`.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Provide your age.
* **Height** (`height`, number, optional): Provide your height.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): Provide your weight.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Choose your gender, such as `Male`, `Female`, or `Other`.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable ethnicities you identify with, such as `White`, `Black or African American`, `Asian`, `Hispanic or Latino`, or `Other`.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your marital status, such as `Married`, `Single`, `Divorced`, or `Separating`.
* **Smoker** (`smoker`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you are a smoker, whether it's `True` or `False`.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you have health insurance, whether it's `True` or `False`.
* **Income** (`income`, number, optional): Provide your income.
* **Education** (`education`, select_one, optional): Choose your education level, such as `High School`, `College`, `University`, or `Some College`.
* **Survey Completed** (`survey_completed`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you have completed this survey before, whether it's `True` or `False`.
