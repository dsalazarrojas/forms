# Protective Gloves Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Protective Gloves Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback and ratings on the effectiveness, comfort, and overall satisfaction of a specific type of protective glove.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user response in the "User Response" field. This is a free-text field where you can write any comments or thoughts you have about your experience with the gloves.
2. Enter your name and ID in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
3. If you are an employee, enter your Employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
4. Rate the effectiveness of the gloves in the "Glove Effectiveness" field. Select from the options provided.
5. Select all that apply in the "Comfort Level" field, as it may be a subjective experience.
6. Provide feedback in the "Feedback" field, if you have any comments or thoughts that didn't fit in the "User Response" field.
7. Rate the overall condition of the gloves in the "Glove Condition" field.
8. Select any safety concerns in the "Safety Concerns" field.
9. Rate your overall safety experience in the "User Safety Rating" field.
10. Rate your overall satisfaction with the gloves in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.
11. Enter a numerical rating for the gloves in the "Rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Response**: A free-text field for you to provide any comments or thoughts you have about your experience with the gloves.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your Employee ID, if you are an employee.
* **Glove Effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of the gloves in terms of performance and protection.
* **Comfort Level**: Select all that apply regarding comfort and fit.
* **Feedback**: Provide feedback on your experience with the gloves.
* **Glove Condition**: Rate the overall condition of the gloves, including wear and tear.
* **Safety Concerns**: Select any safety concerns you experienced while using the gloves.
* **User Safety Rating**: Rate your overall safety experience with the gloves.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the gloves.
* **Rating**: Enter a numerical rating for the gloves.
## Tips

- Be honest and provide specific feedback about your experience with the gloves.
- Take the time to rate each field accurately, as it will help us understand your experience better.
- If you have any additional comments or thoughts that didn't fit in any of the fields, include them in the "Additional Comments" field.
