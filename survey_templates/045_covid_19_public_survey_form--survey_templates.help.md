# COVID 19 Public Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and opinion data from respondents regarding the COVID-19 pandemic and the COVID-19 vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your email address to enable survey submission tracking.
3. Select your age group from the provided options.
4. Enter your state or location.
5. Answer questions about your opinion on the COVID-19 vaccine.
6. Indicate your current vaccination status.
7. Choose whether you plan to get vaccinated or have already been vaccinated.
8. If hesitant, select the primary reason for your hesitation.
9. Rate your trust in government health guidance.
10. Select sources of COVID-19 information you use.
11. Describe how the pandemic has impacted your life.
12. Rate your concern level about COVID-19.
13. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
14. Enter the date of your survey submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address to enable survey submission tracking.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group from the provided options.
* **State or Location**: Enter your state or location where you reside.
* **What is your opinion on the COVID-19 vaccine?**: Select your opinion from the provided options.
* **Do you believe the COVID-19 vaccine is effective?**: Select your opinion from the provided options.
* **Do you believe the COVID-19 vaccine is safe?**: Select your opinion from the provided options.
* **What is your current vaccination status?**: Select your current vaccination status from the provided options.
* **Do you plan to get vaccinated?**: Select your intention to get vaccinated from the provided options.
* **If hesitant, what is your main concern?**: Select the primary reason for your hesitation, if applicable.
* **How much do you trust government health guidance?**: Select your level of trust from the provided options.
* **Where do you get COVID-19 information? (select all)**: Select all sources you use for COVID-19 information from the provided options.
* **How has COVID-19 impacted your life?**: Describe how the pandemic has affected you in the text field.
* **How concerned are you about COVID-19?**: Select your concern level from the provided options.
* **Additional comments or suggestions**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Survey Submission Date**: Enter the date of your survey submission.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure or do not know the answer to a question, select "Unsure" or "Not applicable" accordingly.
* Please ensure that your email address is valid and up-to-date.
* You can submit this form only once.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
