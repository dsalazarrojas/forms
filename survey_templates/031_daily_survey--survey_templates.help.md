# Daily Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from users on a daily basis. It is meant to be a quick and easy way to capture information that can help us improve our services and products. Please note that this form is intended for internal use only and is not meant for public distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name on the first page.
2. Provide your email address on the second page.
3. Answer the three text questions (What do you do, What do you like, and What do you dislike) on the third page.
4. Select one option for the "Select Question 1" question on the fourth page.
5. Select multiple options for the "Select Question 2" question on the fifth page.
6. Enter a date and time on the sixth and seventh pages respectively.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in this format: `user@example.com`.
* **What do you do** (`question1`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe what you do on a daily basis.
* **What do you like** (`question2`, text, required/optional): Tell us about what you enjoy or like.
* **What do you dislike** (`question3`, text, required/optional): Share any dislikes or things that bother you.
* **Select Question 1** (`select_question1`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one option from the list provided.
* **Select Question 2** (`select_question2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple options from the list provided. You can choose as many or as few as you like.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes you may have.
* **Email 2** (`email2`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address again for verification purposes.
