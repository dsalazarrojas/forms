# mandated_reporter_training_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mandated reporter training survey aims to collect demographic and feedback data from participants in the mandated reporter training program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and completely.
2. If a question is not applicable to you, select "Not Applicable" or leave it blank.
3. If you have any difficulties in filling out this form, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required): A brief introduction to provide context for your survey answers.

### survey_participant_info
* **Survey Participant Info** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all applicable boxes to indicate your status as a mandated reporter:
	+ 'Yes' if you are a mandated reporter
	+ 'No' if you are not a mandated reporter

### Training Experience
* **Training Experience** (`3`, `number`, optional): If you have completed the mandated reporter training, please enter the number of hours or days of the training.

### Feedback Comments
* **Feedback Comments** (`4`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about your training experience.

### Confidence Rating
* **Confidence Rating** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Choose your confidence rating after the training:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low

### Participant Rating
* **Participant Rating** (`6`, `number`, optional): If you feel confident, enter a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate how confident you are in reporting.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all applicable boxes to indicate your comments:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'

### Participant Email
* **Participant Email** (`8`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you have one.

### Trainer Comments
* **Trainer Comments** (`9`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments about your trainer.

### Participant Phone
* **Participant Phone** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.

### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### Participant Job Title
* **Participant Job Title** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter your job title as it appears on your identification.

### Training Location
* **Training Location** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of your training.

### Date of Training
* **Date of Training** (`14`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your training.

### Participant Employed
* **Participant Employed** (`15`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all applicable boxes to indicate your employment status:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### Participant School
* **Participant School** (`16`, `text`, optional): Enter your school or university name if applicable.

### Participant Non-Profit
* **Participant Non-Profit** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter your non-profit organization name if applicable.

### Participant Care Team
* **Participant Care Team** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter your care team name if applicable.

### Participant Other
* **Participant Other** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter any other information if applicable.

### Trainer Name
* **Trainer Name** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter your trainer's name.

### Trainer Title
* **Trainer Title** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter your trainer's job title.

### Trainer Phone
* **Trainer Phone** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter your trainer's phone number.

### Trainer Email
* **Trainer Email** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter your trainer's email address.

### Training Provider
* **Training Provider** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your training provider.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`25`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments or feedback.

Please note that you are not required to fill out all fields, but please complete as much as possible to provide accurate and comprehensive data.
