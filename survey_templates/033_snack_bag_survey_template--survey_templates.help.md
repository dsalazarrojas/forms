# Snack Bag Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is used to collect user feedback or ratings on various snack options. Please answer the questions according to your snack preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, follow the instructions for each section below.
2.  The form consists of multiple-choice questions and free-text areas for additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Snack Bag Survey Template** (`title`, `note`, not required): This is the title of the form. It's only for display purposes and doesn't require any input from the user.
*   **Which snack do you like?** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the snack options (Apple, Banana, Cherry) to express your preference.
*   **Which snack do you like? (Multi-select)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select multiple snack options from the list (Apple, Banana, Cherry) to express your preferences.
*   **Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date in a standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Additional comments** (`note`, required): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the snack options.
*   **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Time** (`time`, required): Enter the time in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
*   **Note 1** (`note`, not required): This field is for additional comments or notes. You can add any information that you think is relevant.
*   **Text** (`text`, not required): Enter any additional text or comments about the snack options or your preferences.
