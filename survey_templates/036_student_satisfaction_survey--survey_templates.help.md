# Student Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from students about their overall experience in the course. Your responses will help us understand what you liked or disliked about the course, and what we can improve for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best describe your experience in each category. You can choose only one answer for select options and multiple answers for select multiple options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What did you think of the course?**: This field is for your general opinion about the course. Please type in your thoughts, and be as detailed as possible.
* **2. Overall Rating**: This field asks you to rate the course as a whole, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent). Choose a number that best reflects your overall satisfaction.
* **3. How would you rate your instructor?**: This is an assessment of your instructor's performance. You can select multiple answers to provide a more nuanced view. Please choose from Excellent, Good, Average, or Poor.
* **4. How would you rate your course?**: Similar to question 3, this field is for your assessment of the course. You can select multiple answers to provide a detailed view.
* **5. How would you rate the course materials?**: This is your evaluation of the course materials provided. You can select multiple answers to provide a more detailed view.
* **6. How would you rate online support?**: This field is for your assessment of the support you received from online resources. You can select multiple answers to provide a more nuanced view.
* **7. How would you rate your instructor's communication?**: This field is for your assessment of how well your instructor communicated with you. You can select multiple answers to provide a more detailed view.
* **8. How would you rate student resources?**: This field is for your evaluation of the resources provided to you as a student. You can select multiple answers to provide a more nuanced view.
* **9. How long was the course?**: This field is for you to report the length of the course in your local date format (e.g., MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY).
* **10. How much time did you spend learning?**: This field is for you to report the amount of time you spent learning in the course. Use the format "HH:MM:SS" to report your answer.
* **11. How satisfied are you with the course?**: This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with the course on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **12. Why are you satisfied?**: This field is for you to provide a detailed explanation of why you are satisfied with the course. Please be as detailed as possible. 

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question honestly and accurately.
- If you have any comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them to the "Why are you satisfied?" field.
