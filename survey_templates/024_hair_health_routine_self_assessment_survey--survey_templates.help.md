# Hair Health Routine Self Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your hair health routine, including your hair type, condition, and any problems you're experiencing. This will help us create a personalized hair care plan tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question carefully and accurately. You can select multiple options for each field as needed. If you have any additional information that is not listed in the options, please use the "Other" option and provide a brief description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Hair Health Routine Self Assessment Survey (Page 1)
* **Hair Health Routine Self Assessment Survey** (`hair_health_routine_self_assessment_survey`, text, not required)
	+ This is the title of the survey. Please do not fill in this field.

### 2. Hair Health Routine Survey Date (Page 1)
* **Hair Health Routine Survey Date** (`hair_health_routine_survey_date`, date, not required)
	+ Please enter the date you completed this survey.

### 3. Hair Health Routine Survey Time (Page 1)
* **Hair Health Routine Survey Time** (`hair_health_routine_survey_time`, time, not required)
	+ Please enter the time you completed this survey.

### 4. Client Name (Page 2)
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, not required)
	+ Please enter your name.

### 5. Client Age (Page 2)
* **Client Age** (`client_age`, number, not required)
	+ Please enter your age.

### 6. Hair Type (Page 2)
* **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, select_one, required)
	+ Please select the type of your hair:
		- African Hair
		- African American Hair
		- Asian Hair
		- Curly Hair
		- Dry Hair
		- Fine Hair
		- Gray Hair
		- Mixed Ethnicity
		- Other

### 7. Hair Condition (Page 2)
* **Hair Condition** (`hair_condition`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Please select all conditions that apply to your hair:
		- Damaged
		- Dry
		- Fine
		- Gray
		- Oily
		- Other

### 8. Scalp Condition (Page 2)
* **Scalp Condition** (`scalp_condition`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Please select all conditions that apply to your scalp:
		- Itchy
		- Oily
		- Other
		- Sensitive
		- Tender
		- Too Tight

### 9. Hair Routine (Page 3)
* **Hair Routine** (`hair_routine`, text, not required)
	+ Please describe your current hair care routine.

### 10. Hair Problems (Page 3)
* **Hair Problems** (`hair_problems`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Please select all problems you are experiencing with your hair:
		- Color Fading
		- Dryness
		- Frizziness
		- Greying
		- Other
		- Split Ends
		- Thinning

### 11. Current Hair Products Used (Page 3)
* **Current Hair Products Used** (`current_hair_products_used`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Please select all hair products you are currently using:
		- Conditioner
		- Hairspray
		- Hair Oil
		- Hair Serum
		- Other
		- Shampoo
		- Styling Cream
		- Styling Mousse
		- Treatments

### 12. Current Hair Products Used (Specify) (Page 3)
* **Current hair products used (please specify)** (`current_hair_products_used_other`, text, not required)
	+ Please describe any additional hair products you are using.

### 13. Additional Notes (Page 3)
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, not required)
	+ Please add any additional comments or information about your hair.

### 14. Hair Treatment Services Used (Page 4)
* **Hair Treatment Services Used** (`hair_treatment_services_used`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Please select all services you have used:
		- Coloring
		- Cut
		- Hair Treatment
		- Other
		- Style

### 15. Hair Treatment Services Used (Specify) (Page 4)
* **Hair treatment services used (please specify)** (`hair_treatment_services_used_other`, text, not required)
	+ Please describe any additional hair services you have used.

### 16. Hair Treatment Services Price (Page 4)
* **Hair Treatment Services Price** (`hair_treatment_services_price`, number, not required)
	+ Please enter the price of the services you have used.

### 17. Number of Services (Page 4)
* **Number of Services** (`number_of_services`, number, not required)
	+ Please enter the number of services you have used.

### 18. Hair Treatment Services Duration (Page 4)
* **Hair Treatment Services Duration** (`hair_treatment_services_duration`, time, not required)
	+ Please enter the duration of the services you have used.

## Tips
* Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
* If you have any additional information that is not listed in the options, please use the "Other" option and provide a brief description.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
