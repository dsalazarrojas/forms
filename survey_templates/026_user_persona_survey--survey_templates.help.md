# User Persona Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information to create a user persona. The purpose of this survey is to gather data about the user's demographics, occupation, education, and income.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
2.  Select the correct options for the questions that ask for select options.
3.  Enter your age and income as numbers.
4.  Make sure to select all the relevant occupation options that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your age?**
    *   This field is not required. Please enter your age as a number.
*   **What is your job title?**
    *   This field is not required. Please enter a brief description of your job title.
*   **What is your income?**
    *   This field is not required. Please enter your income as a number.
*   **What is your education level?**
    *   This field is not required. Please select one of the following options:
        +  High school
        +  Some college
        +  Bachelor's
        +  Master's
        +  Ph.D.
*   **What is your occupation?**
    *   This field is not required. You can select one or more of the following options:
        +  Business
        +  Software developer
        +  Engineer
        +  Marketing
        +  Other
*   **What is your annual income?**
    *   This field is not required. Please enter your income as a number.
