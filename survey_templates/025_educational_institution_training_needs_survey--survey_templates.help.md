# Educational Institution Training Needs Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the training needs of educational institution staff.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Select the options that apply to your role and training needs.
* Be as detailed as possible in the comments section if you have any specific training needs or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ This field asks about your role within the institution. Please select the option that best describes your role.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, `number`, required: false):
	+ This field asks about the number of years you have been working at the institution.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field asks about your current job title or position within the institution.
* **Current Program** (`current_program`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field asks about the program you are currently working on or responsible for.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ This field allows you to select the types of training you need or are interested in. Please select all that apply.
* **Priority** (`priority`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ This field asks about your priority level for training. Please select the option that best describes your priority.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or information you want to provide about your training needs or requests.
* **Institution** (`institution_survey_form`, `note`, required: false):
	+ This field is a placeholder for future development. Please do not fill it out. 

Note: The "Institution" field is likely a note field for future development and should not be filled out by users.
