# Match Day Experience Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the Match Day Experience event. This form is meant to be completed by attendees to provide their honest thoughts and opinions on the event, its organization, staff, and overall quality. Your feedback will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
* Choose the best fit for each option in the select_one, select_multiple, and select_one fields.
* Use the number fields to provide numerical feedback.
* Use the text fields to write your comments or suggestions.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
