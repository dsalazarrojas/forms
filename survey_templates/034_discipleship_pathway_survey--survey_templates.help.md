# Discipleship Pathway Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Discipleship Pathway Survey" is a tool designed to collect feedback and data from program participants to help us better understand their experiences and areas for improvement. Your honest and accurate responses will assist us in refining the program to meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each question, ensuring you answer all required fields and provide accurate and truthful information.
2. Select one or multiple options for questions that have multiple options (e.g., "Do You Need More Support?" or "How Often Do You Participate in Program Events?").
3. Enter your answer in the text fields (e.g., "What Do You Think About the Program?" or "Additional Comments").
4. Fill in your email address and phone number in the corresponding fields.
5. Click submit to send your completed form.
