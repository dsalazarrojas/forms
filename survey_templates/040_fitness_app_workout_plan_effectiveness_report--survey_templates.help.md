# Fitness App Workout Plan Effectiveness Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fitness App Workout Plan Effectiveness Report is a form designed to gather feedback and information from users about their workout plans. This report aims to understand how users perceive the effectiveness of the workout plans provided by the app and to gather insights about their progress and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user workout feedback by filling in the **User Workout Feedback** field.
2. Share your **User Progress Insights** in the next field.
3. Select the type of **Workout Plan Type** that you have followed.
4. Choose the exercises you have performed in the **Exercises Selected** field.
5. Rate your **Feedback Rating** from 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
6. Add any comments about your experience in the **Feedback Comments** field.
7. Fill in your **Progress Bar** (percentage completed).
8. Report your **User Workout Progress** in the field.
9. Provide feedback comments from your trainer in the **Trainer Comments** field.
10. Add any additional information in the **Additional Info** field.
11. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.
12. Enter your **Phone** number for further contact.
13. Select the **Date Completed** when you finished the workout plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Workout Feedback** (`User Workout Feedback`, text, optional): Share your overall feedback about the workout plan.
* **User Progress Insights** (`User Progress Insights`, text, optional): Provide any insights about your progress.
* **Workout Plan Type** (`Workout Plan Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of workout plan you followed (e.g., weightlifting, cardio, etc.).
* **Exercises Selected** (`Exercises Selected`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the exercises you performed during the workout.
* **Feedback Rating** (`Feedback Rating`, number, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the workout plan from 1-5.
* **Feedback Comments** (`Feedback Comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Progress Bar** (`Progress Bar`, number, optional): Enter the percentage of progress you made.
* **User Workout Progress** (`User Workout Progress`, number, optional): Report your progress.
* **Trainer Comments** (`Trainer Comments`, text, optional): Feedback comments from your trainer.
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, text, optional): Add any additional information.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Date Completed** (`Date Completed`, date, optional): Select the date when you completed the workout plan.
