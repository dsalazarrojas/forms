# Air Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Air Quality Survey is a tool to help management and facilities teams understand employee perceptions of the air quality in their workspace. This information can inform decisions to improve the indoor environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Employee Name and Department / Floor information, which will help us understand your specific work environment.
2. Answer the questions about your experience with air quality, including rating the overall air quality, symptoms you experience, and when symptoms occur.
3. Indicate if you've noticed any odors and provide a description if you've selected "Yes".
4. Rate your comfort with the temperature and humidity.
5. Add any additional comments or concerns you have about the air quality.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your employee badge or in the company directory.
* **Department / Floor**: This is where you work. For example, "Marketing Department" or "Floor 2".
* **How would you rate the air quality?**:
	+ Poor: You have severe symptoms or strong odors.
	+ Fair: You have noticeable symptoms or moderate odors.
	+ Good: You have minimal symptoms or slight odors.
	+ Excellent: You have no symptoms or no odors.
* **Do you experience symptoms at work?**: Check all that apply:
	+ Headache: You experience headaches.
	+ Fatigue / Drowsiness: You feel tired or drowsy.
	+ Dry / Itchy Eyes: You experience dry or itchy eyes.
	+ Throat Irritation: You experience throat irritation.
	+ Coughing: You cough or have respiratory issues.
	+ Dizziness: You feel dizzy.
	+ None: You do not experience any symptoms.
* **When do symptoms occur?**: This is when you experience symptoms. For example, "Morning", "Afternoon", or "All Day".
* **Have you noticed any odors?**: Check "Yes" if you have noticed any strange smells.
* **Description of Odors**:
	+ If you selected "Yes", please provide a description of the odors you've noticed.
* **Temperature Comfort**:
	+ Too Cold: You feel too cold.
	+ Comfortably Cool: You feel comfortable.
	+ Just Right: You feel just right.
	+ Comfortably Warm: You feel comfortably warm.
	+ Too Hot: You feel too hot.
* **Humidity Comfort**:
	+ Too Dry: You feel too dry.
	+ Just Right: You feel comfortable.
	+ Too Humid / Muggy: You feel too humid or muggy.
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ This is the space for any additional comments or concerns you have about the air quality.

Note: This form has a mix of multiple-choice and text fields. Be sure to provide clear and concise answers for the select fields, and detailed descriptions for text fields.
