# School Lunch Menu Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Lunch Menu Voting Form is designed to allow students to vote for their favorite school lunch options. By participating in this survey, students help inform the school's culinary decisions, ensuring that the school lunch menu caters to their tastes and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "School Lunch Menu Voting Form" page from the form navigation bar.
2. Review the available options on each page and select your favorite choices.
3. For the "Select Your Favorite" page, choose one option from the provided list.
4. On the "Rate Your Meal" pages, enter a number rating for each meal option. Ratings can range from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
5. Complete all pages of the form and submit your votes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Lunch Menu Voting Form** (`school_lunch_menu_voting_form_page_1`, text, Optional): This is the introduction page of the form. It provides a brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions on how to complete it.
* **Select Your Favorite** (`school_lunch_menu_voting_form_page_2`, select_one, Optional): This page allows you to choose your favorite school lunch option from the list provided. Select one option from the available choices.
* **Rate Your Meal** (`school_lunch_menu_voting_form_page_3`, number, Optional): This page is for rating your meal options. Enter a number rating for each meal option, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Rate Your Meal** (`school_lunch_menu_voting_form_page_4`, time, Optional): This page is also for rating your meal options. Enter a time rating for each meal option, but please note that this field is not currently being used. 

## Tips
* Please rate each meal option separately to ensure that your preferences are accurately represented.
* Make sure to complete all pages of the form to provide comprehensive feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the school administration or support team.
