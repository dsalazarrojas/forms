# Caesar's Campaigns Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Caesar's Campaigns Quiz is a form designed to test your knowledge of Julius Caesar's military campaigns. It consists of a series of questions that cover various aspects of Caesar's conquests, from his battles and territories to his strategies and tactics. By completing this quiz, you'll be able to demonstrate your understanding of Caesar's military prowess and the historical context of his campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the quiz, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the `student_name` field, which asks for your name.
2. Next, enter the `quiz_date`, which should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Choose the correct `class_section` (or school) from the drop-down menu.
4. Answer each question carefully, using the correct options provided:
	* For questions 1-7, select the correct territory, battle, or phrase using the drop-down menus.
	* For questions 8 and 9, enter your answer in the text field.
5. If you have any additional comments or questions for the instructor, you can enter them in the `additional_comments` field, which is optional.
6. Finally, confirm that the submitted work is your own by selecting the correct answer for the `submission_ack` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded in the quiz results.
* **Date** (`quiz_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`, e.g., `2022-07-18`.
* **Class Section** (`class_section`, `text`, required): Choose the class or section that you are in.
* **Question 1** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required): Select the territory where Caesar fought the Helvetii at the start of the Gallic Wars. Answer: Gaul.
* **Question 2** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose the person who was Caesar's primary opponent at the Siege of Alesia. Answer: Vercingetorix.
* **Question 3** (`question_3`, `select_one`, required): Select the river that marked Caesar's declaration of civil war against the Senate. Answer: Rubicon.
* **Question 4** (`question_4`, `select_one`, required): Select the battle where Caesar decisively defeated Pompey the Great. Answer: Pharsalus.
* **Question 5** (`question_5`, `text`, required): Enter the phrase that Caesar used to describe his swift victory in Pontus. Answer: Battle of Zela.
* **Question 6** (`question_6`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of bridge that Caesar used to cross the Rhine. Answer: Timber Bridge.
* **Question 7** (`question_7`, `select_one`, required): Select the Gallic tribe known for their fierce resistance and coastal sailing prowess. Answer: Veneti.
* **Question 8** (`question_8`, `text`, required): Describe the 'Testudo' formation used by the Roman Legions. Answer: a shield wall formation.
* **Question 9** (`question_9`, `text`, optional): If you know it, enter the primary motivation for Caesar's second invasion of Britannia. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
* **Bonus Question** (`bonus_question`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the members of the First Triumvirate. Answer: Julius Caesar, Pompey the Great, and Crassus.
* **Feedback** (`quiz_feedback`, `select_one`, required): Rate the difficulty of the quiz for you. Answer: Easy, Moderate, Difficult, or Extremely Challenging.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have for the instructor.
* **Confirmation** (`submission_ack`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the submitted work is your own. Answer: True.
