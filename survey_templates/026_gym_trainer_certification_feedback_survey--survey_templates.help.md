# Gym Trainer Certification Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from gym trainers on their certification, professionalism, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your experience.
2. If you are not certified, please mark "Not Applicable" for the corresponding question.
3. For numerical questions, please enter a number between 1-100.
4. For free text questions, please type in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainer Certification** (`trainer_certification`, select_one, required: false): 
    Please select whether you have a current certification.
* **Professionalism** (`professionalism`, select_multiple, required: false): 
    Please choose all the ways in which you felt I was professional (e.g. On time, Helpful, Unhelpful).
* **Training Quality** (`training_quality`, number, required: false): 
    Please rate the quality of the training you received on a scale of 1-100.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): 
    Please type in any additional feedback you have about your experience.
* **Trainer Professionalism Score** (`trainer_professionalism_score`, number, required: false): 
    Please rate how professional you found the trainer's behavior on a scale of 1-100.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, required: false): 
    Please type in any additional comments you have about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): 
    Please enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): 
    Please enter your phone number so we can follow up with you.
 
## Tips
- Please answer truthfully and accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us. 
- If you have a similar experience to this one, please let us know so we can learn from your feedback!
