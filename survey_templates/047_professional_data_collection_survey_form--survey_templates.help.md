# Professional Data Collection Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect professional data for internal business use. Please fill out this form to provide information about yourself or your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form title and category to accurately describe the form and its purpose.
2. Write a short description of the form in the Description field.
3. Select the correct category and assigned tool for this form.
4. Enter your input data in the designated fields (Input 1-5).
5. Enter your contact information (Email and Phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Professional Data Collection Survey Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the form, e.g., "Survey for Employee Data".
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category of the form, e.g., "Survey Templates".
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the form, e.g., "Form for collecting employee data".
* **Assigned Tool** (`form_assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Choose the tool assigned to this form, e.g., "chatjimmy".
* **Input 1** (`form_input_1`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value, e.g., "10".
* **Input 2** (`form_input_2`, number, optional): Enter another numerical value, e.g., "20".
* **Input 3** (`form_input_3`, date, optional): Enter a date, e.g., "2022-01-01".
* **Input 4** (`form_input_4`, time, optional): Enter a time, e.g., "14:00".
* **Input 5** (`form_input_5`, note, optional): Enter a short text note, e.g., "This is a test note."
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, e.g., "john@example.com".
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, e.g., "123-456-7890".
* **Date of Birth** (`form_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth, e.g., "1990-01-01".
