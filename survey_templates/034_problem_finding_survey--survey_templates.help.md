# Problem Finding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Problem Finding Survey is designed to collect information from users about their experience with problems they have encountered. This data will help us better understand the problem finding process and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Select your company from the list of options in the "company" field.
4. Choose your role from the list of options in the "role" field.
5. Select your department from the list of options in the "department" field.
6. Answer each question by selecting all relevant options from the list of choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`name`, `text`, required): This field is where you will enter your name.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): This field is where you will enter your email address.
* **company** (`company`, `select_one`, required): This field is where you will select the name of your company from the list of options.
* **role** (`role`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is where you will select all applicable roles you hold.
* **department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): This field is where you will select your department from the list of options.
* **question1** (`question1`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks about your experience with Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3. Select all applicable options.
* **question2** (`question2`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks about your experience with Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3. Select all applicable options.
* ...
* **question16** (`question16`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks about your experience with Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3. Select all applicable options.
* **question17** (`question17`, `select_one`, required): This field asks about your experience with Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3. Select one applicable option.
* **question18** (`question18`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks about your experience with Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3. Select all applicable options.

Note: The questions are designed to be specific and focused on different aspects of problem finding. Be as precise as possible when answering each question.
