# Neighborhood Environmental Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the environmental quality of your neighborhood. It will help us understand the current state of your neighborhood's conditions, such as air quality, noise levels, and presence of pests. This information will help us improve the livability and appeal of your neighborhood.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Street Name** and **Address Line 1** and **Address Line 2**. These will help us identify the specific location you are surveying.
2. Enter the **Number of Photos** taken in the area.
3. Indicate the **Quality of Photos** taken, whether they are Yes, No, or Other.
4. Describe the **Photos** you took by providing a brief summary.
5. Indicate if there are any **Environmental Conditions** such as **Air Quality**, **Noise**, **Odor**, **Litter**, or **Pests** present.
6. Choose the **Noise Level** and **Odor Level** of your neighborhood.
7. Report the **Trash Count** and provide a brief **Description of Trash**.
8. Indicate the **Pests Count** and provide a brief **Description of Pests**.
9. Select whether photos are taken **Always**, **Rarely**, **Sometimes**, or **Never**.
10. Choose the **Taken At** time of day and **Taken On** date of the photos.
11. Provide a **Neighborhood Rating** and **Rating Reason**.
12. Enter any additional comments or thoughts in the **Rating Comment** field.
13. Finally, provide a **Taken Date** and **Neighborhood Rating Reason**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Street Name**: The name of the street where you took the photos.
* **Address Line 1**: The first line of your address where you took the photos.
* **Address Line 2**: The second line of your address where you took the photos.
* **Number of Photos**: The number of photos you took in the area.
* **Quality of Photos**: Indicate whether you took high-quality photos or not.
* **Photos**: A brief description of the photos you took.
* **Environmental Condition**: Check all the conditions you observed, such as Air Quality, Noise, Odor, Litter, or Pests.
* **Noise Level**: Indicate whether there is a high, medium, or low noise level in the area.
* **Odor Level**: Indicate whether there is a high, medium, or low odor level in the area.
* **Trash Count**: The number of trash found in the area.
* **Description of Trash**: A brief description of the trash found.
* **Pests Count**: The number of pests observed in the area.
* **Description of Pests**: A brief description of the pests observed.
* **Taken Photo**: Indicate whether photos are taken **Always**, **Rarely**, **Sometimes**, or **Never**.
* **Taken At**: The time of day when you took the photos.
* **Taken On**: The date when you took the photos.
* **Neighborhood Rating**: Your rating of the neighborhood's quality.
* **Rating Reason**: Your reason for the neighborhood rating.
* **Rating Comment**: Additional comments or thoughts about your rating.
* **Taken Date**: The date you took the photos.
* **Neighborhood Rating Reason**: Additional comments or thoughts about your neighborhood rating.

Note: Please fill out the form accurately and to the best of your ability. If you have any questions or need help, please contact us.
