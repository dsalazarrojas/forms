# Partnership Program Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partnership Program Effectiveness Evaluation Form is a self-assessment tool for partners to evaluate their own partnership programs. It helps identify strengths and weaknesses, areas for improvement, and provides a basis for continuous improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Go through each field carefully, answering questions based on your own experience and involvement in the partnership.
2. Fill out the form in its entirety, as this will provide the most comprehensive insights into the partnership's performance.
3. Be honest and thorough in your answers, as this will help identify areas that need improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Program**: This field is for you to describe your partnership program in brief.
* **Program Goals**: Select all the goals that have been set for the partnership. These goals should align with the partnership's objectives and your own expectations.
* **Program Outcome**: Choose the outcome of the partnership from the given options. This will help determine if the partnership has achieved its set goals.
* **Evaluation Period**: This field is for you to specify the time period over which you are evaluating the partnership. This will help in comparing the performance of the partnership over time.
* **Partner Involvement**: Describe the level of involvement you had in the partnership's decision-making and planning processes.
* **Partner Communication**: Describe the communication channels used in the partnership and the level of satisfaction with the communication process.
* **Partner Goals Alignment**: Describe how your own goals aligned with the partnership's goals.
* **Outcome Expectation**: Describe your expected outcome from the partnership.
* **Outcome Achieved**: Describe the actual outcome of the partnership.
* **Lessons Learned**: Describe the lessons you learned from the partnership.
* **Recommendations**: Provide any recommendations for future improvements or changes to the partnership.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or feedback about the partnership.
* **Form Completion**: Check if the form is complete and accurate.
* **Partnership Program Effectiveness**: Rate the partnership's effectiveness based on your experience.
* **Partnership Program Effectiveness Score**: Rate the partnership's effectiveness score.
* **Partner Satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you are with the partnership.
* **Partner Satisfaction Score**: Rate your satisfaction with the partnership.
* **Partner Commitment**: Describe the level of commitment you had in the partnership.
* **Partner Support**: Describe the level of support you received from the partner.
* **Partner Collaboration**: Describe the level of collaboration you experienced in the partnership.
* **Program Value**: Describe the value added to the partner and the partnership.
* **Program Return on Investment (ROI)**: Describe how the partnership has provided a return on investment.
* **Program Return on Investment (ROI) Score**: Rate the return on investment of the partnership.
