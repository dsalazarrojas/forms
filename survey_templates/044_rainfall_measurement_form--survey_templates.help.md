# Rainfall Measurement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rainfall Measurement Form is designed to collect rainfall measurements from various locations to aid in the understanding of weather patterns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the location field and select the correct option that represents the location where you are taking the measurement.
2. Enter the rainfall measurements in the corresponding fields, using the following format: `measurement 1`, `measurement 2`, `measurement 3`, etc.
3. Fill in the `Time of Measurement` field with the exact time of day and date of the measurement.
4. Enter the `Timezone Offset` fields to account for the time difference between your location and the standard time zone.
5. Add any additional notes or remarks about the location in the `Notes` and `Location Notes` fields.
6. Finally, click on the `Submit` button to submit your data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location**: Select the location where you are taking the measurement from the provided options.
* **Rainfall Measurement 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6**: Enter the rainfall measurements in the corresponding fields, using the following format: `measurement 1`, `measurement 2`, `measurement 3`, etc.
* **Time of Measurement**: Enter the exact time of day and date of the measurement.
* **Timezone Offset**: Enter the time offset between your location and the standard time zone.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or remarks about the location.
* **Latitude**: Enter the latitude of the location where you are taking the measurement.
* **Longitude**: Enter the longitude of the location where you are taking the measurement.
* **Timezone**: Select `Yes` or `No` to indicate whether your location is within a timezone or not.
* **Timezone Offset Hours**, **Timezone Offset Minutes**, **Timezone Offset Seconds**: Enter the time offset in hours, minutes, and seconds, respectively.
* **Timezone Offset Am/PM**: Select `Yes` or `No` to indicate whether your time zone uses Am/PM or not.
* **Location Notes**, **Location Notes 2, 3, 4, 5**: Add any additional notes or remarks about the location (optional).

Note: The `Location Notes 2, 3, 4, 5` fields are optional and can be used to provide more detailed information about the location.
