# Internal Communication Staff Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects your opinions on internal communication within the organization. Please provide your honest feedback on the effectiveness and channels of communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply select the relevant answers and type in the required information. You can choose one or multiple answers for the select_multiple and select_one fields. For text fields, type in your thoughts and suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Feedback**: Describe your experience with internal communication.
* **Communication Channels**: Select all the channels that you use for internal communication (e.g., email, phone calls, team meetings, etc.).
* **Effectiveness**: Choose how effective you think internal communication is for you (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Frequency**: If possible, estimate the frequency with which you receive internal communication (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).
* **Time Spent**: If you can estimate, please indicate the amount of time you spend on internal communication.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions on internal communication.
* **Manager Feedback**: Share any feedback you received from your manager or any comments about your manager's communication style.
* **Employee Satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you are with internal communication (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions for improving internal communication.
* **Channel Effectiveness**: Choose the channel that you think is most effective for internal communication (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Communication Skills**: Choose the communication skill that you think is most important for managers (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Communication Channels to Improve**: Choose the internal communication channel that you think needs improvement (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Channels to Improve**: If you have any other channels that you think need improvement, please describe them here.
