# Behavior And Work Pressure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on your work pressure and behavior, including your workload manageability, stress factors, and suggestions for improvement. Your responses will help us understand your experiences and identify areas for organizational development.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
- Select "Never" if you have not experienced a particular situation or feeling.
- Choose "Daily" for daily occurrences, "Weekly" for weekly occurrences, "Monthly" for monthly occurrences, and so on.
- For numerical ratings (e.g., 0 to 10 scales), rate your agreement with the given statements.
- For questions with options, select the one that best describes your experience or opinion.
- You can provide additional comments if you have any other thoughts or ideas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Department** (`department_name`, required): This question is to help us understand which department you belong to.
* **Years with the Organization** (`years_with_company`, required): This question is to gauge your level of familiarity with the organization and its policies.
* **My current workload is manageable within my contracted hours** (`workload_manageability`, required): This question helps us understand how well you feel you can manage your workload.
* **How often do you feel stressed by tight deadlines?** (`deadline_stress_frequency`, required): This question aims to identify the frequency of stress caused by deadlines.
* **I have the necessary tools and resources to do my job effectively** (`resource_adequacy`, required): This question is to assess how well-equipped you feel you are to perform your tasks.
* **I feel supported by my direct supervisor when work pressure is high** (`support_from_management`, required): This question is to evaluate the level of support you receive from your supervisor during stressful times.
* **Have you experienced physical symptoms of stress in the last month?** (`physical_symptoms_of_stress`, required): This question is to identify if you have experienced any physical symptoms of stress.
* **Satisfaction with your current work-life balance** (`work_life_balance_satisfaction`, required): This question is to rate your satisfaction with your current work-life balance.
* **Do you feel at risk of burnout in your current role?** (`burnout_risk_self_perception`, required): This question is to gauge your self-assessment of burnout risk.
* **What is the single biggest factor contributing to your work pressure?** (`top_stress_factor`, required): This question is to identify the primary cause of your work pressure.
* **Which measures would help reduce your work pressure?** (`desired_support_measures`, optional): This question is to gather suggestions for alleviating work pressure.
* **Any other comments regarding workplace behavior or pressure?** (`additional_comments_work_pressure`, optional): This question is to allow you to share any other thoughts or ideas you may have.
* **Date** (`survey_completion_date`, required): This question records the date when you completed the survey.
