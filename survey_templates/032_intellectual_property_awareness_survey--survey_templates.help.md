# Intellectual Property Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to gauge your awareness and understanding of intellectual property concepts. This form is designed to gather information about your opinions and experiences regarding patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, and general intellectual property-related topics. Your responses will help us assess your level of understanding and provide insights for future improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review each page**: The form is divided into multiple pages, each covering a different aspect of intellectual property.
2. **Choose your options**: For the "Patents", "Copyrights", "Trademarks", and "Trade Secrets" pages, select all the options that apply to you. This will help us understand your familiarity with each concept.
3. **Enter your response**: For the "General Questions" page, type your answers in the text fields provided.
4. **Submit your survey**: Once you have completed all the pages, click the "Submit" button to save your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patents** (`patent_section`, `select_multiple`): This page is about patents, which are grants of exclusive rights to make, use, and sell inventions. Select all the options that apply to you:
	+ Patents are essential for innovation
	+ Patents help innovation
* **Copyrights** (`copyright_section`, `select_multiple`): This page is about copyrights, which protect creative works such as literature, music, and art. Select all the options that apply to you:
	+ Copyrights are essential for creative works
	+ Copyrights protect creative works
* **Trademarks** (`trademark_section`, `select_multiple`): This page is about trademarks, which prevent confusion among consumers and are used to gain competitive advantage. Select all the options that apply to you:
	+ Trademarks prevent confusion among consumers
	+ Trademarks are used to gain competitive advantage
* **Trade Secrets** (`trade_secret_section`, `select_multiple`): This page is about trade secrets, which are used to gain unfair advantage or protect competitive advantage. Select all the options that apply to you:
	+ Trade secrets are used to gain unfair advantage
	+ Trade secrets are used to protect competitive advantage
* **General Questions** (`general_questions`, `text`): This page is for you to enter any additional comments or thoughts about intellectual property.
* **Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey** (`survey_header`, `text`): A friendly message acknowledging your participation in the survey.

Tips

* Take your time to carefully review each page before submitting your responses.
* If you're unsure about any option, select "Not applicable" to show that you don't identify with that concept.
* Please be honest and provide your genuine opinions and experiences.
* Thank you for participating in this survey, and your contributions will help us improve our understanding of intellectual property concepts.
