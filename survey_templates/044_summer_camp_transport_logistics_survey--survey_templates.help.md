# Summer Camp Transport Logistics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your transportation needs for the summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" if you need transportation to the summer camp.
2. If you need transportation, choose your preferred pick-up location from the options listed.
3. Enter the time you would like to be picked up.
4. If you have any special requirements (e.g., wheelchair accessibility), please enter them in the "Special Requirements" field.
5. Choose the type of transportation you would like to use (Bus, Train, Drive, or Other).
6. Enter your contact information in case we need to communicate with you about your transportation arrangements.
7. Select the drop-off location from the options listed.
8. Enter the time you would like to be dropped off.
9. If you have any additional instructions or comments, please enter them in the "Special Instructions" field.
10. Finally, include any additional notes or comments about your transportation arrangements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transportation Needs** (`Transportation Needs`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks if you need transportation to the summer camp. Select "Yes" if you need assistance, and we will provide transportation arrangements accordingly.
* **Preferred Pick Up Location** (`Preferred Pick Up Location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where you would like to be picked up.
* **Pick Up Time** (`Pick Up Time`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to be picked up.
* **Special Requirements** (`Special Requirements`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any special requirements you have, such as wheelchair accessibility.
* **Transportation Arrangements** (`Transportation Arrangements`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of transportation you would like to use.
* **Transportation Contact Information** (`Transportation Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information in case we need to communicate with you.
* **Drop Off Location** (`Drop Off Location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where you would like to be dropped off.
* **Drop Off Time** (`Drop Off Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to be dropped off.
* **Special Instructions** (`Special Instructions`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or instructions about your transportation arrangements.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your transportation arrangements.
