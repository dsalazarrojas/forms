# Impact Of Isolation On Life Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about your experience with isolation, including your feelings, experiences, and coping strategies. This information will help us better understand the impact of isolation on individuals and identify areas for improvement in support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Take a moment to read through each page and understand the questions being asked.
2.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3.  For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply or one option that best describes your experience.
4.  For open-ended questions, provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share.
