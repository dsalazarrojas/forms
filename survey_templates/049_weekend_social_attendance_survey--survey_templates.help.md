# Weekend Social Attendance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track weekend social events, such as concerts, festivals, or meetups. It helps event organizers and administrators understand who will be attending and make necessary arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by providing the event name, start and end times, name of the event organizer, and location of the event.
- Select the time of your availability for attending the event.
- Indicate whether you will or will not attend the event.
- Choose the weekend days you are available to attend.
- Provide any additional comments or notes about the event.
- Repeat steps 1-5 for each event you wish to report on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

	+ Choose whether you will or will not attend the event.

	+ Enter the time when the event starts.
