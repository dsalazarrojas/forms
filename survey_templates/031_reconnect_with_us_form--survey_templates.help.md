# Reconnect With Us Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Reconnect With Us Form is a user-friendly tool designed to help users reconnect with our services. It's a simple form that allows you to let us know that you'd like to reestablish contact or reengage with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "What do you want to do?" on the first page. This is your chance to briefly explain why you'd like to reconnect with us.
2. Provide your email address on the second page. This will help us get in touch with you.
3. Enter your phone number on the third page. This is optional, but it will help us contact you if needed.
4. Type a message on the fourth and final page. This is your chance to add any additional context or ask questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you want to do?** (`what_do_you_want_to_do`, text, optional): A brief explanation of why you'd like to reconnect with us. Please be as clear and concise as possible.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here. This is optional, but it will help us reach out to you if needed.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Add any additional context or ask questions you have. This is your chance to elaborate on your reasons for reconnecting with us.
