# Quarterly Team Meeting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from team members about their experiences during the quarterly team meeting. This feedback will help the team and management understand what works well and what can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and provide detailed answers.
2. For each question, answer truthfully about your experiences during the meeting.
3. When rating a question, select the best option that represents your honest opinion.
4. Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What were the key takeaways from the meeting** (`quarterly_team_meeting_survey_form`, text, **required**): 
This question asks about the main points or outcomes from the meeting that you found valuable or important. Please describe the key takeaways, such as new ideas discussed, action items agreed upon, or decisions made.

* **How would you rate the effectiveness of the meeting** (`meeting_effectiveness`, number, **required**): 
Rate the meeting's effectiveness on a scale, where 1 is the lowest (least effective) and 5 is the highest (most effective). Choose the number that best reflects your honest opinion.

* **How could we improve the meeting** (`meeting_effectiveness_how`, text, **optional**): 
Share your thoughts on how the meeting could be improved. Be as specific as possible, and consider suggestions such as improving time management, adjusting the agenda, or enhancing engagement techniques.

* **What did you think about the team members' participation?** (`team_member_satisfaction`, select_one, **required**): 
Rate the level of preparation and engagement from team members during the meeting. Choose one of the options that best describes your experience:
	+ Team members were well-prepared
	+ Team members were somewhat prepared
	+ Team members were not prepared

* **What were the communication challenges you faced?** (`communication`, text, **required**): 
Describe any communication difficulties you experienced during or after the meeting. This could include unclear messages, misunderstandings, or other obstacles that hindered your understanding or progress.

* **How could we improve the communication?** (`communication_how_to_improve`, text, **optional**): 
Share your suggestions on how the communication process could be enhanced, such as adjusting meeting protocols, improving messaging tools, or encouraging better active listening.

* **What action items will you take on?** (`action_items`, select_multiple, **required**): 
Choose from the options that apply to you, indicating what action items you will take on or have already taken on:
	+ Action items are clear and achievable
	+ Action items are unclear or unachievable

* **How could we improve the action items?** (`action_items_how`, text, **optional**): 
Consider sharing your thoughts on how the action items could be improved, such as making them more actionable, providing clearer instructions, or setting realistic deadlines.
