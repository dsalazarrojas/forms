# Study Environment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Study Environment Survey is a form designed to gather information about your experience in the study environment. This form is intended to help us better understand your needs and expectations in this setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant options for each question from the provided options.
2.  Choose 'Option 1' or 'Other' where relevant, depending on your preference.
3.  Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
4.  Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Study Settings 
*   **Study Settings** (`study_environment_settings`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks you to select multiple options that best describe your study environment. Choose 'Option 1', 'Option 2' or select 'Other'.

### Satisfaction 
*   **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_level`, `number`, required/optional): This question asks for your level of satisfaction with the study environment. Choose a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your satisfaction level.

### Student Resources 
*   **Student Resources** (`student_resources`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks you to select one option that best describes your availability of resources during studies. Choose 'Option 1', 'Option 2' or select 'Other'.

### Time Spent Learning 
*   **Time Spent Learning** (`time_spent_learning`, `time`, required/optional): This question asks for the average time you spend learning during your studies. Enter a time value to indicate your average study time.

### Student Satisfaction 
*   **Student Satisfaction** (`student_satisfaction`, `number`, required/optional): This question asks for your overall satisfaction with your studies. Choose a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your satisfaction level.

### Learning Outcome 
*   **Learning Outcome** (`learning_outcome`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks you to select multiple options that describe your learning outcomes. Choose 'Option 1', 'Option 2' or select 'Other'.

### Student Resources Fulfillment 
*   **Student Resources Fulfillment** (`student_resources_fulfillment`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks you to select multiple options that describe your fulfillment with resources during studies. Choose 'Option 1', 'Option 2' or select 'Other'.

### Instructor Support 
*   **Instructor Support** (`instructor_support`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks you to select multiple options that describe your satisfaction with instructor support. Choose 'Option 1', 'Option 2' or select 'Other'.

### Classroom Environment 
*   **Classroom Environment** (`classroom_environment`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks you to select one option that best describes your classroom environment. Choose 'Option 1', 'Option 2' or select 'Other'.

### Study Environment Satisfaction 
*   **Study Environment Satisfaction** (`study_environment_satisfaction`, `number`, required/optional): This question asks for your overall satisfaction with the study environment. Choose a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your satisfaction level.

### Student Fulfillment 
*   **Student Fulfillment** (`student_fulfillment`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks you to select multiple options that describe your fulfillment with studies. Choose 'Option 1', 'Option 2' or select 'Other'.

## Tips
*   Make sure to answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
*   If you're unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.
*   You can review and modify your answers before submitting the form.
