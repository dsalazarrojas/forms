# Mobile App Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Mobile App Usage Survey aims to gather information on users' experiences, preferences, and pain points related to mobile apps. This information will help the development team create more user-friendly and efficient mobile apps.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Take a few minutes to carefully consider each question.
2. Select the most accurate answers for each question based on your personal experience.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, as they are all optional and important for providing a complete picture.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mobile App Used** (2): This question asks about your experience with mobile apps within the last 30 days. Select "Yes" if you have used a mobile app, and "No" otherwise.
* **How Often Do You Use Mobile Apps?** (3): This question inquires about your frequency of mobile app usage. Choose the option that best reflects your usual behavior.
* **Which of the Following Apps Do You Use Most Frequently?** (4): Select all the apps that you use most often. Commonly used apps include social media, entertainment, productivity, gaming, and navigation.
* **Why Do You Use Mobile Apps?** (5): This question seeks to understand your motivations for using mobile apps. Choose all the reasons that apply to you, such as convenience, entertainment, productivity, or other reasons.
* **What Features Do You Usually Look for When Using a Mobile App?** (6): This question helps identify what aspects of a mobile app you value most. Select features like navigation, design, performance, functionality, and user interface that you usually consider when choosing or using an app.
* **What Do You Expect When Using a Mobile App?** (7): This question aims to understand your expectations from mobile apps. Choose all the expectations that you commonly have, like ease of use, intuitiveness, speed, and simplicity.
* **What Do You Dislike When Using a Mobile App?** (8): This question seeks to uncover potential pain points. Select all the reasons that you commonly dislike, such as slow performance, confusing design, or difficulty in use.
* **How Much Time Do You Usually Spend on Mobile Apps?** (9): This question inquires about your typical time spent on mobile apps. Choose the time frame that best represents your average usage.
* **Which of the Following Platforms Do You Use to Access Mobile Apps?** (10): This question is about the platforms you use to access mobile apps, such as iOS, Android, or both.
* **What Type of Device Do You Most Frequently Use to Access Mobile Apps?** (11): This question asks about your typical device of choice for mobile app usage. Select between mobile phone, tablet, or other devices.
* **What Type of Device Do You Typically Use to Access Mobile Apps?** (12): This question is similar to the previous one but focuses on your typical device usage. Select all the devices you commonly use, like mobile phones, tablets, or others.
