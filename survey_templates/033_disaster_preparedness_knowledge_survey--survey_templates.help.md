# Disaster Preparedness Knowledge Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your emergency plans and preparedness in the event of a disaster. Please provide accurate and thorough information to ensure that you are adequately prepared.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and provide accurate information for each section.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as "Required".
3. You can leave fields marked as "Optional" blank if you prefer.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that everything is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `text`, Required): This is a short section where you can briefly introduce yourself and provide any additional information that might be relevant to your emergency plans.
* **Category** (`Category`, `select_one`, Optional): Please select the category that best describes your emergency contact. This will help us provide you with targeted information and resources.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, Optional): Provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you in case of an emergency.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, Optional): Please provide your name so that we can refer to you by name in our records.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, Optional): If you have an email address that you would like to be contacted at in case of an emergency, please provide it here.
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, Optional): Please provide your location so that we can provide you with information and resources specific to your area.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, Optional): Provide the name and contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Info** (`Emergency Info`, `text`, Optional): Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful in case of an emergency.
* **Questions 1** (`Questions 1`, `text`, Optional): Please provide any relevant information that you think would be helpful in case of an emergency.
* **Questions 2** (`Questions 2`, `text`, Optional): Please provide any additional information that you think would be helpful in case of an emergency.
* **Questions 3** (`Questions 3`, `text`, Optional): Please provide any more information that you think would be helpful in case of an emergency.

## Tips

* Please review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that everything is accurate.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
