# Tool User Feedback Poll Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on your experience with our tool. Your input will help us understand your needs and improve the tool for better usability and functionality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title to describe your feedback.
2. Choose how you felt about your last interaction with our tool (Very positive, Somewhat positive, Neutral, Somewhat negative, or Very negative).
3. Select any issues you encountered during your last interaction with our tool (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5, or Other - please specify).
4. Provide any suggestions on what we could improve on.
5. Enter the date of your last interaction with our tool.
6. If you'd like to be contacted for further feedback, provide your email address.
7. If you'd like, enter your phone number for us to reach out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): A brief title to describe your feedback.
* **How was your experience with our product?** (`question1`, select_one, optional): Choose how you felt about your last interaction with our tool.
* **What issue did you encounter during your last interaction?** (`question2`, select_multiple, optional): Select any issues you encountered during your last interaction with our tool.
* **What do you think we could improve on?** (`question3`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions on what we could improve on.
* **Date of last interaction** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last interaction with our tool.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further feedback, provide your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like, enter your phone number for us to reach out.
