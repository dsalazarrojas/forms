# College Student Service Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from college students about their satisfaction with various campus services. Your input will help the institution understand what's working well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Each question is designed to gather specific information about your satisfaction with campus services.
3. For questions with a select_one type, choose the answer that best fits your experience.
4. For questions with a select_multiple type, select up to three services that you think are most important.
5. When answering the survey, consider your overall experience with each service, not just your most recent interaction.
6. Take a few minutes to think about each question before moving on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID Number (Optional)**: This is an optional field for tracking purposes only. If you'd like to provide your Student ID Number, it will help us understand your specific experience.
* **Email Address (Required)**: Enter your college email address to verify your identity and ensure your responses are counted.
* **Year in School (Required)**: Select the current year you're in school to help us understand your perspective.
* **Dining Services Satisfaction (Required)**: Rate your satisfaction with dining services, including cafeteria food quality and overall experience.
* **Library Services Satisfaction (Required)**: Rate your satisfaction with library services, including resources, staff, and study spaces.
* **IT and Technology Support Satisfaction (Required)**: Rate your satisfaction with IT and technology support, including computer labs and WiFi.
* **Health and Wellness Services (Required)**: Rate your satisfaction with health and wellness services, including the health center, counseling, and fitness programs.
* **Student Housing Services (Required)**: Rate your satisfaction with student housing, including dorms, maintenance, and overall conditions.
* **Academic Advising Services (Required)**: Rate your satisfaction with academic advising, including advisor availability and helpfulness.
* **Career Services and Job Placement (Required)**: Rate your satisfaction with career services, including internships, job fairs, and resume help.
* **Student Activities and Clubs (Required)**: Rate your satisfaction with student activities and clubs, including variety and engagement opportunities.
* **Recreation and Athletics (Required)**: Rate your satisfaction with recreation and athletics, including gym facilities and sports programs.
* **Service Improvement Priorities (Required)**: Select up to three services that you think are most important for improvement.
* **Most Needed New Service or Resource (Required)**: Suggest a new service or resource that you think would benefit students most.
* **Overall Quality of Student Services this Year (Required)**: Compare your experience with last year, choosing how it has improved or declined.
* **Effectiveness of Service Communications (Required)**: Rate how well services are promoted and communicated to you.
* **Additional Feedback or Suggestions (Optional)**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them here.
* **May We Contact You for Follow-up Questions (Required)**: Allow us to contact you for follow-up questions, and we'll do our best to address them.
* **Survey Completion Consent (Required)**: Confirm that you've answered honestly and completely.

Please take a few minutes to complete this form and help us improve your college experience!
