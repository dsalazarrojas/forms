# University Distance Learning Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The University Distance Learning Feedback Survey is a tool used by the university to gather feedback from students about their experiences with distance learning. The data collected will help us improve our online courses and services to better support your learning needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form) and provide as much detail as you can in the optional fields. You can select your answers using the options provided for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Online Learning Quality (1)**: Rate the quality of online learning in this university. Choose one or more of the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Communication (2)**: Describe how you felt about the communication with university staff, instructors, or other students. Choose one of the options:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Support Services (3)**: Rate the support services provided by the university. Choose one or more of the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Overall Experience (4)**: Rate your overall experience with distance learning at this university. Enter a number to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Comments (5)**: If you have any comments or suggestions about your experience with distance learning, please enter them here.
* **Email (6)**: If you would like us to contact you about your feedback, please enter your email address.
* **Phone (7)**: If you would like us to contact you about your feedback, please enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when completing the form.
* If you have difficulty accessing any of the fields, contact your instructor or the university's distance learning team for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to ask us!
