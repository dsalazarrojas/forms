# Cognitive Wellbeing Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cognitive Wellbeing Assessment Survey is a tool designed to assess an individual's cognitive wellbeing, including their mental health, emotional regulation, social connections, physical health, and lifestyle factors. This survey is intended to help identify areas of strength and weakness in cognitive wellbeing, providing insights for personal development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form sections, which are divided into categories such as mental health, emotional wellbeing, social connections, physical health, and lifestyle factors.
2. For each question, select the answer that best describes your current state or experience. You may choose from a variety of options for each question.
3. Ensure that you fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Respondent Information
* **Your Name**: This is the name of the person taking the survey. This field is optional.
* **Email Address**: This is your email address, which will be used to contact you for any follow-up questions or to provide you with results. This field is required.
* **Age Range**: This question asks for your age range to help us understand your demographics. Please select the range that best describes your age.
* **Date of Assessment**: This is the date you are taking the survey. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Mental Health and Cognitive Function
* **Overall Mental Health**: This question asks for your overall mental health, rated from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Mental Clarity and Focus**: This question asks for your level of mental clarity and focus, rated from 1 (Very unclear and unfocused) to 5 (Very clear and focused).
* **Memory Function**: This question asks for your memory function, rated from 1 (Very poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Decision Making Ability**: This question asks for your ability to make decisions, rated from 1 (Very difficult) to 5 (Very easy).
* **Stress Level**: This question asks for your current stress level, rated from 1 (Very low) to 5 (Very high).
* **Anxiety Level**: This question asks for your anxiety level, rated from 1 (None) to 5 (Severe).
* **Overall Mood**: This question asks for your overall mood, rated from 1 (Very depressed) to 5 (Very positive).
* **Ability to Regulate Emotions**: This question asks for your ability to regulate emotions, rated from 1 (Very difficult) to 5 (Very good).

### Social and Relational Wellbeing
* **Sense of Social Connection**: This question asks for your sense of social connection, rated from 1 (Very isolated) to 5 (Strong connection).
* **Quality of Important Relationships**: This question asks for the quality of your relationships, rated from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).

### Physical Health Impact on Cognition
* **Sleep Quality**: This question asks for your sleep quality, rated from 1 (Very poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Energy Level**: This question asks for your energy level, rated from 1 (Exhausted) to 5 (High).
* **Overall Physical Health**: This question asks for your overall physical health, rated from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).

### Lifestyle Factors
* **Exercise Frequency**: This question asks for your exercise frequency, rated from Never to Very regularly.
* **Nutritional Habits**: This question asks for your nutritional habits, rated from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).

### Overall Assessment
* **Overall Cognitive Wellbeing**: This question asks for your overall cognitive wellbeing, rated from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Changes in Cognitive Wellbeing**: This question asks for any positive or negative changes in your cognitive wellbeing recently.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* This survey is a tool for personal development and improvement. Your responses will be kept confidential and anonymous.
* You may take as much time as you need to complete the survey.
* You can save your progress and come back to complete the survey later if needed.
* We recommend taking the survey in a quiet and private area.
* If you experience any difficulties with the survey or need assistance, please contact us.

This form should take approximately 15-20 minutes to complete, depending on your pace. If you need any help, please don't hesitate to reach out. Your input will help us better understand your cognitive wellbeing. Thank you for participating in this survey.
