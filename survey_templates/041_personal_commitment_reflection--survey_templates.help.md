# Personal Commitment Reflection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Personal Commitment Reflection" form is designed to help users reflect on their personal growth, goals, and values. It is meant to be filled out by individuals who are part of a specific team or program within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Describe your current goals and aspirations in the "goals" field.
6. List your core values that drive your personal growth and development in the "values" field.
7. Reflect on your current state of being accountable in the "accountability" field.
8. Share how you are committed to personal growth and development in the "growth" field.
9. Write a brief reflection on yourself in the "self_reflection" field.
10. State your personal commitment to your growth and development in the "commitment" field.
11. Describe your current goals and values that drive your personal growth in the "goals_values" field.
12. Share your next steps for personal growth and development in the "next_steps" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **goals** (text, optional): Describe your current goals and aspirations.
* **values** (text, optional): List your core values that drive your personal growth and development.
* **reflection** (text, optional): Reflect on your current state of being accountable.
* **accountability** (text, optional): Describe how you are committed to personal growth and development.
* **growth** (text, optional): Share how you are committed to personal growth and development.
* **self_reflection** (text, optional): Write a brief reflection on yourself.
* **commitment** (text, optional): State your personal commitment to your growth and development.
* **goals_values** (text, optional): Describe your current goals and values that drive your personal growth.
* **next_steps** (text, optional): Share your next steps for personal growth and development.
* **review** (text, optional): This field is not currently utilized.
