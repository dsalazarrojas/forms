# Voting Closure Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Voting Closure Survey is designed to gather feedback from project participants to assess the team's performance and identify areas for improvement. It helps us understand your experience and preferences, which enables us to provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out each page completely and honestly.
2.  Answer the questions according to your actual experience with our team.
3.  Use the available options for select questions to choose the best fit.
4.  Use the "Additional comments" field to provide any other feedback or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in this project** (`page_1`, text, optional): Please describe your role in the project, e.g., team member, client, sponsor, etc.
* **How fair do you think the process was** (`page_2`, select_one, optional): Choose how fair you think the process was in your opinion.
* **How well did the organization meet your expectations** (`page_3`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to how well the team met your expectations.
* **Additional comments** (`page_4`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you may have about your experience with our team.
* **How likely would you recommend our team to others** (`page_5`, select_one, required): Choose how likely you would recommend our team to others.
* **What did you find most valuable about our team** (`page_6`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to what you found most valuable about our team.
* **What did you find least valuable about our team** (`page_7`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to what you found least valuable about our team.
* **What did you find most challenging about our team** (`page_9`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to what you found most challenging about our team.
* **How likely do you feel our team has helped you** (`page_10`, select_one, required): Choose how likely you feel our team has helped you.
* **What could we improve on** (`page_12`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to areas where we could improve.
* **How much time do you have available for future projects** (`page_13`, number, optional): Enter the amount of time you have available for future projects.
* **How much time did you spend on this project** (`page_14`, number, optional): Enter the amount of time you spent on this project.
* **What is your role in this project** (`page_15`, text, optional): Describe your role in this project.
* **What did you think of our team's communication style** (`page_16`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about our team's communication style.
* **What time of the day do you like to work** (`page_17`, time, optional): Choose a time of day that suits you best.
* **What kind of projects do you like to work on** (`page_18`, text, optional): Describe the type of projects you prefer to work on.
* **Do you have any additional comments** (`page_19`, note, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments you may have.
* **How likely do you want to participate in future projects** (`page_20`, select_one, optional): Choose how likely you are to participate in future projects.
* **What kind of projects would you be interested in working on** (`page_21`, select_multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to the types of projects you would be interested in working on.
* **What time of the day is best suited for you** (`page_22`, time, optional): Choose a time of day that suits you best.
* **What kind of projects you prefer to work on** (`page_23`, text, optional): Describe the type of projects you prefer to work on.
* **Do you have any feedback** (`page_24`, note, optional): Share any feedback you may have.
* **What kind of team structure** (`page_25`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of team structure you prefer.
