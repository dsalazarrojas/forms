# Science And Literature Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about scientific and literary concepts from students, teachers, and other stakeholders. The collected data will support educational research, policy-making, or resource allocation within schools or universities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the classroom for which you are completing the form.
2. Choose the subject that is being taught in the classroom.
3. Provide a brief description of the question or topic being addressed.
4. Enter your score or assessment of the student's or teacher's response.
5. Add any additional comments if necessary.
6. Enter your email address to serve as a submitter identifier.
7. Enter your phone number for verification purposes.
8. Enter the submit time and date.
9. Add any comments about the classroom or submitter.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Classroom** (`classroom_id`, required): This field is used to select the classroom from which the data is being collected.
  - Enter the unique identifier of the classroom.

- **Subject** (`subject_id`, required): Select one from the dropdown list of available subjects.
  - This field is used to specify the subject matter being taught in the classroom.

- **Question** (`question_id`, required): This field is used to describe the question or topic being addressed.
  - Enter a brief description of the question or topic.

- **Answer** (`answer_id`, required): Enter your response or assessment of the student's or teacher's response.
  - This field is used to record the student's or teacher's answer to the question or topic.

- **Score** (`score_id`, required): Enter your score or assessment of the student's or teacher's response.
  - This field is used to measure the student's or teacher's understanding or performance.

- **Comments** (`comments_id`, optional): Provide any additional comments about the student or teacher's response.
  - This field is used to collect additional feedback or comments.

- **Submitter Email** (`submitter_id`, required): Enter your email address for verification purposes.
  - This field is used to identify the submitter.

- **Phone** (`submitter_phone_id`, required): Enter your phone number for verification purposes.
  - This field is used for phone verification.

- **Submit Time** (`submitter_time_id`, required): Enter the time at which you are submitting the form.
  - This field is used to record the submit time.

- **Submit Date** (`submitter_date_id`, required): Enter the date at which you are submitting the form.
  - This field is used to record the submit date.

- **School** (`school_id`, required): Enter the name of the school associated with this form.
  - This field is used to collect the school name.

- **Grade** (`grade_id`, required): Enter the grade level of the classroom.
  - This field is used to specify the grade level.

- **Section** (`section_id`, required): Enter the section of the classroom.
  - This field is used to specify the classroom section.

- **Student** (`student_id`, required): Enter the student's name or ID.
  - This field is used to identify the student.

- **Teacher** (`teacher_id`, required): Enter the teacher's name or ID.
  - This field is used to identify the teacher.

- **Course** (`course_id`, required): Enter the course name or ID.
  - This field is used to specify the course.

- **Subject Teacher** (`subject_teacher_id`, required): Enter the name of the subject teacher.
  - This field is used to identify the subject teacher.

- **Classroom** (`classroom_id`, required): Enter the name of the classroom.
  - This field is used to specify the classroom.

- **School Year** (`school_year_id`, required): Enter the school year.
  - This field is used to record the school year.

- **School Grade** (`school_grade_id`, required): Enter the school grade.
  - This field is used to record the school grade.

- **Student Count** (`student_count_id`, required): Enter the number of students.
  - This field is used to count the number of students.

- **Teacher Count** (`teacher_count_id`, required): Enter the number of teachers.
  - This field is used to count the number of teachers.

- **School Comments** (`school_comments_id`, optional): Add any comments about the school.
  - This field is used to collect comments about the school.

## Tips

- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check your input data for any errors before submitting the form.
- Use the provided fields carefully to avoid any confusion or misinterpretation of the data.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to this guide or contact your administrator for assistance.
