# Attraction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a candidate's attraction to an opportunity for job matching purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
2. Provide specific details for fields like email, phone number, current position, industries worked in, key skills, job titles interested, and availability to start.
3. Select the best-suited options for fields like will to relocate, ideal company type, preferred work arrangement, and why you're open to new opportunities.
4. Leave notes for fields like job preferences, motivation assessment, and additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email Address** (`email`, required): Provide your primary email for contact purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
- **LinkedIn Profile URL** (`linkedin_profile`, optional): Share your professional LinkedIn profile URL if you have one.
- **Current Location** (`current_location`, required): Specify your city and state or country.
- **Will to Relocate?** (`willingness_to_relocate`, required): Select if you are willing to relocate or not.
- **Employment Background** (`employment_background`, optional): Add any relevant background information about your previous employment.
- **Current Position** (`current_position`, required): Describe your current job title and company.
- **Years of Professional Experience** (`years_experience`, required): Enter the total number of years you have spent in your industry.
- **Industries Worked In** (`industry_experience`, required): List any previous industries or sectors you have worked in.
- **Key Skills and Expertise** (`key_skills`, required): Specify your top 5-10 key skills.
- **Job Preferences** (`job_preferences`, optional): Add any notes about your job preferences.
- **Job Titles Interested In** (`job_titles_interested`, required): List the roles you would consider.
- **Ideal Company Type** (`ideal_company_type`, required): Choose the type of company you would like to work for.
- **Salary Expectation Range** (`salary_expectation`, required): Enter your expected annual salary range.
- **Preferred Work Arrangement** (`work_arrangement`, required): Select your preferred working arrangement.
- **Motivation Assessment** (`motivation_assessment`, optional): Add any notes about your motivation.
- **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, required): Describe where you want to be in your career.
- **Why Open to New Opportunities?** (`job_change_motivation`, required): Explain why you're open to new opportunities.
- **Company Culture and Values** (`culture_values`, optional): Speculate on what matters to you in a company culture and values.
- **Availability to Start** (`availability`, required): Select when you could start a new role.
- **Notice Period Required** (`notice_period`, optional): Specify the amount of notice you would require.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Add any additional information we should know.
