# Hunting Experience Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Hunting Experience Feedback" form is a tool for guided hunting trip participants to provide their honest feedback on their experience. It aims to help outfitters understand what worked well and what could be improved, ultimately enhancing the quality of their services.

## How To Complete This Form

- To begin, select the appropriate rating for your overall experience (1-5) in the "Overall Rating" field.
- For each field that is not marked as required, you may choose to answer or leave it blank, as you see fit.
- Use the "Comments" field to provide any additional information or insights about your experience.
- In the "Photo" field, you may upload a photo related to your experience.
- Choose your level of satisfaction with the experience using the "How Satisfied Are You With Your Experience?" field.
- Select "True" or "False" for "Outfitter" if you had a dedicated guide or outfitter during your trip.
- Choose whether you would recommend this experience to others using the "Recommendations" field.
- Provide any additional comments you may have about your experience in the "Additional Comments" field.
- Enter your email address and phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or to receive future offers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating (1-5)** (`rating`, select_one, required: false): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information or insights about your experience.
* **Photo** (`photo`, text, required: false): Upload a photo related to your experience.
* **How Satisfied Are You With Your Experience?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Choose your level of satisfaction (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Outfitter** (`outfitter`, select_one, required: false): Select "True" if you had a dedicated guide or outfitter during your trip.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you would recommend this experience to others (True, False).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or to receive future offers.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or to receive future offers.

Note: The required fields are not marked as required in the form schema but are necessary to gather the most valuable insights from users.
