# WillRobinsGolfLessonRequest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The WillRobinsGolfLessonRequest form is designed to collect user feedback and requests for golf lessons at WillRobins Golf.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number (optional, but recommended for follow-up purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be addressed in our communication.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up on your lesson request.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you directly (recommended for follow-up purposes).
