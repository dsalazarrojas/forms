# post_event_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and communication information from users after an event. It should only be used for events where the user has not already received communication from the organizer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have already created an event and have not received communication from the organizer.
2. Fill out the form with your event details, including your thoughts on the event.
3. Select any questions that apply to your experience.
4. Enter any additional comments you have about your experience.
5. Enter your email address and/or phone number for us to get in touch with you.
6. Rate your experience with the provided options.
7. Provide any feedback you have about the event.
8. Click the submit button to submit your feedback.
