# Photo Limit Efficiency Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Limit Efficiency Quiz is a survey designed to evaluate the efficiency of a photography service or product in terms of photo limit, storage efficiency, and quality of photo.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
* Leave your phone number in the "Phone" field if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* Select the appropriate photo limit from the dropdown menu in the "Photo Limit" field.
* Check all that apply for the storage efficiency level in the "Storage Efficiency" field.
* Rate the quality of the photo from the options in the "Quality of Photo" field.
* Indicate whether the photo balance point meets your expectations in the "Balance Point" field.
* If you have any additional comments, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
* Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Leave your phone number if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Photo Limit** (`photo_limit`, select_one, required): Select the maximum number of photos you can upload at once.
* 	**Storage Efficiency** (`storage_efficiency`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to rate the storage efficiency of the photo service or product.
* **Quality of Photo** (`quality_of_photo`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the photo from excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Balance Point** (`balance_point`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the photo balance point meets your expectations.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the photo service or product.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to submit the form and complete the evaluation.
