# workshop_session_quality_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather feedback on the quality of a workshop, providing a comprehensive evaluation of its organization, content, and facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Respond to each question honestly and thoroughly, providing your genuine experience with the workshop.
2. For questions with "select_one" type, choose the option that best reflects your opinion.
3. For questions with "text" type, provide a brief description or answer in the space provided.
4. Make sure to answer all questions, as they are all optional unless marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was your overall experience with this workshop?** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): Please share your general impression of the workshop, describing your experience in a few words.
* **How would you rate the presenter's knowledge on the subject?** (`introduction_2`, `number`, required: false): Rate the presenter's knowledge on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being not knowledgeable and 5 being extremely knowledgeable.
* **Was the workshop well-organized?** (`introduction_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the workshop was well-planned and executed, and "No" if it was not.
* **What did you enjoy most about the workshop?** (`content`, `text`, required: false): Describe the aspect that you found most engaging or enjoyable about the workshop.
* **What was the most valuable thing you learned?** (`content_2`, `text`, required: false): Share the key takeaway or insight you gained from the workshop.
* **Was the workshop well-structured?** (`content_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the workshop had a good balance of activities and time management, and "No" if it did not.
* **Was the workshop well-facilitated?** (`content_5`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the workshop was facilitated smoothly, and "No" if it was not.
* **Were the facilities clean and well-maintained?** (`content_8`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the facilities were clean and well-maintained, and "No" if they were not.
* **Was the food and beverages provided satisfactory?** (`content_11`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the food and beverages were good, and "No" if they were not.
* **Were the breaks well-maintained?** (`content_13`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the breaks were well-organized, and "No" if they were not.
* **Was the overall pace of the workshop well-balanced?** (`content_15`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the pace of the workshop was good, and "No" if it was not.
* **Was the workshop well-attended?** (`content_17`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the workshop was well-attended, and "No" if it was not.
* **Was the workshop well-planned?** (`content_19`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the workshop was well-planned, and "No" if it was not.
* **Was the workshop well-executed?** (`content_21`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the workshop was well-executed, and "No" if it was not.
* **Was the workshop enjoyable?** (`content_18`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you found the workshop enjoyable, and "No" if you did not.
* **How likely are you to recommend this workshop to others?** (`content_20`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the likelihood of recommending the workshop, ranging from "Not likely at all" to "Extremely likely".
