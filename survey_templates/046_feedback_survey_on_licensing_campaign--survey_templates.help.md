# Feedback Survey On Licensing Campaign - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on the effectiveness and communication of our licensing campaign. Your input will help us improve our services and better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Read the survey questions and choose the most relevant response for each question.
* Select one option for each question unless otherwise specified.
* Provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted about this survey.
* Add any additional comments you'd like to share.
* Choose one or more methods you'd like to be contacted about this survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Survey On Licensing Campaign** (`<title>`): This is the title of the form, not a field to fill out. Please ignore it.
* **How did you hear about this survey?** (`question1`, select_one): How did you learn about this survey? Choose the most relevant option from the list.
* **Your Email** (`email`, email): If you'd like to be contacted about this survey, please enter your email address here.
* **How satisfied are you with our services?** (`question2`, select_one): Please rate your satisfaction with our services on a scale of 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Additional Comments** (`comment`, text): If you have any additional thoughts or comments about the survey or our services, please share them here.
* **How long do you take to respond to a request?** (`question3`, time): This question is not applicable if you don't have any experience with our services. Please answer in a format like "X minutes" or "X hours" if possible.
* **Would you like to be contacted about this survey?** (`question4`, select_one): Do you want us to contact you about this survey?
* **Name** (`name`, text): This is an optional field to input your name.
* **What is your preferred method of contact?** (`question5`, select_multiple): Choose one or more of the following methods to be contacted: Phone, Email, Other, In-person.
