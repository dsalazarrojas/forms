# Communications Goal Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey aims to gather information about your department's communication goals, priorities, and challenges. It will help us understand the current state of communication in your organization and identify areas for improvement. Your feedback is crucial in defining and prioritizing communication goals that will have the most significant impact.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely**: Ensure that you answer all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. **Read each field carefully**: Take a moment to understand the question and options before selecting an answer.
3. **Be honest and specific**: Provide accurate and detailed answers to help us better understand your needs and goals.
4. **Save and submit the form**: Once you have completed the form, review your answers carefully and submit the form when ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Introduction**: A brief introduction to the purpose of this survey.
* **Participant Name**: A field to provide your name, if you wish to be associated with your answers.
* **Participant Email**: An optional field to provide your email address, if you want to be contacted for further information or clarification.
* **Department**: Select the department you work in from the provided options (e.g., Executive, Sales, Marketing, etc.).
* **Role Level**: Choose your level in the organization from the provided options (e.g., Individual Contributor, Team Lead, Manager, etc.).
* **Current State Assessment**: This field is for your personal assessment of the current communication state in your organization.
* **Current Effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of current organizational communication (e.g., Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, etc.).
* **Current Clarity**: Rate how clear current communication objectives are (e.g., Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, etc.).
* **Current Alignment**: Rate how well teams are aligned on communication goals (e.g., Very Well Aligned, Well Aligned, Somewhat Aligned, etc.).
* **Goal Priorities**: A field for your thoughts about communication goal priorities.
* **Top Priority Goal**: Select the top priority goal for communication improvement.
* **Priority Goals**: Choose your top three priority goals for communication improvement.
* **Internal Communication Goals**: Select internal communication improvement goals (e.g., more frequent updates, clearer messaging, etc.).
* **External Communication Goals**: Select external communication improvement goals (e.g., brand consistency, message clarity, etc.).
* **Timeline Expectations**: Think about the timeline for achieving communication goals.
* **Short-Term Goals**: Describe what you want to achieve in the next 3 months.
* **Medium-Term Goals**: Describe what you want to achieve in 6-12 months.
* **Long-Term Goals**: Describe what you want to achieve in 1-3 years.
* **Success Metrics**: Select how success should be measured (e.g., employee satisfaction, engagement rates, etc.).
* **Resource Needs**: Select the resources needed to achieve communication goals (e.g., additional staff, training, etc.).
* **Barriers and Challenges**: Identify potential barriers and challenges to achieving communication goals.
* **Accountability**: Choose who should be accountable for communication goals.
* **Support Needed**: Describe any additional support you need to contribute to achieving communication goals.
* **Additional Input**: Any other thoughts or comments about communication goals.
* **Survey Date**: Date and time you completed this survey.

Note: This help guide is intended to be a general guide and may not cover every possible scenario or question. If you have any doubts or questions about a specific field, please don't hesitate to ask.
