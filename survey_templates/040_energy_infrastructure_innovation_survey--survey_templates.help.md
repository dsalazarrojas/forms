# Energy Infrastructure Innovation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from survey participants about their organization's energy infrastructure innovation priorities and challenges. The data collected will help us better understand the energy infrastructure innovation landscape and identify key areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them accurately.
2. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
3. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Participant Info (Survey Participant Info, text, required: no)**: This field is used to collect general information about the participant, such as their name, title, and organization. This will help us understand who is taking the survey and their affiliation with the organization.
* **Organization Info (Organization Info, text, required: no)**: This field is used to collect information about the organization taking the survey, such as its name and contact details. This will help us understand the organization's context and capabilities in the energy sector.
* **Project Participants (Project Participants, select_multiple, required: no)**: This field asks about the number of people involved in project development, delivery, and maintenance. Select all options that apply to your organization.
* **Innovation Priorities (Innovation Priorities, select_one, required: no)**: Choose the top three innovation priorities for your organization. These priorities will help us understand what drives energy infrastructure innovation.
* **Barriers To Innovation (Barriers To Innovation, select_multiple, required: no)**: Identify the top three barriers to innovation for your organization. This will help us understand the challenges that hinder energy infrastructure innovation.
* **Successful Projects (Successful Projects, text, required: no)**: Describe a successful innovation project in your organization. This will give us valuable insights into what works well and what doesn't.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, text, required: no)**: This field is used to collect contact information for follow-up questions or further communication. Please provide your name, title, and email address.
