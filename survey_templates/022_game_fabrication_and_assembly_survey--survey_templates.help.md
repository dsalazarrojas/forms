# Game Fabrication And Assembly Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from stakeholders about the game fabrication and assembly process to help us improve the overall quality and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- Take as much time as you need, but be sure to complete the survey within a reasonable time frame.
- If you're unsure about a question or need clarification, feel free to ask for help.
- Submit your answers and be sure to review them for accuracy before saving.
