# product-category-management-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users about their preferences and ratings for specific product categories. This information will help us better understand user behavior and develop products that cater to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions carefully and honestly.
2. For the "Category Questions" section, select all the categories that apply.
3. In the "Preference Ratings" section, choose the rating that best matches your preference.
4. In the "Open-Ended Questions" section, provide as much detail as possible in the text box.
5. Finally, fill out the "Survey Finish" section with any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Intro** (`survey_intro`, text, optional): This is a text field for any additional comments or questions you may have before starting the survey.
* **Category Questions** (`category_questions`, select_multiple, required): Select all the categories that apply to your preferences. This will help us understand which categories you are most interested in.
* **Preference Ratings** (`preference_ratings`, select_one, optional): Choose the rating that best matches your preference. This will help us understand your level of interest in the category.
* **Open-Ended Questions** (`open_ended_questions`, text, optional): Provide as much detail as possible in this text field to help us better understand your thoughts and preferences.
* **Survey Finish** (`survey_finish`, text, optional): This is a text field for any additional comments or feedback you may have after completing the survey.

## Tips
Please be honest and thorough in your responses. Your input will help us develop products that are tailored to your needs. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask!
