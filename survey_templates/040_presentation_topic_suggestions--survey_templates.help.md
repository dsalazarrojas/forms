# Presentation Topic Suggestions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Presentation Topic Suggestions" form is used to collect information about presentation topics, such as title, category, description, and other details. This information will be used to suggest presentation topics for presentations, lectures, or other events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the "Presentation Topic" field with a clear and concise title of the presentation topic.
2. Select a category from the list of options provided in the "Category" field.
3. Add a description of the presentation topic in the "Description" field.
4. Choose the type of presentation you are suggesting in the "Presentation Type" field.
5. Select the target audience for the presentation in the "Target Audience" field.
6. Optionally, provide the date and time of the presentation in the "Date" and "Time" fields respectively.
7. Add any additional notes about the presentation in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Optionally, provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to submit your suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Presentation Topic** (`title_input`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise title of the presentation topic.
* **Category** (`category_input`, select_one, required): Select a category for the presentation topic from the options provided.
* **Description** (`description_input`, text, required): Add a description of the presentation topic.
* **Presentation Type** (`presentation_type_input`, select_one, required): Choose the type of presentation you are suggesting.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience_input`, select_multiple, required): Select the target audience for the presentation.
* **Date** (`date_input`, date, optional): Optionally, provide the date of the presentation.
* **Time** (`time_input`, time, optional): Optionally, provide the time of the presentation.
* **Notes** (`notes_input`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the presentation.
* **Email** (`email_input`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone_input`, text, optional): Optionally, provide your phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit_form`, text, required): Click the submit button to submit your suggestion.
