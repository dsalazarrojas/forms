# Team Building Activity Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to help team members vote on team building activities, allowing team leaders to plan activities that best suit the team's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available team building activities and select your preferred activities.
2. Choose the activities that you are interested in by selecting "Yes" next to each activity.
3. Fill out the submission date and time when you would like to submit your preferences.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments about your preferences.
5. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
6. Confirm your submission status (Active, Inactive, or Completed).

* **Activity Type** (`id: 2`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select "Yes" if you are interested in the activity type, otherwise select "No".

* **Ranked Choice 2** (`id: 4`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select all the activities you are interested in for the second ranked choice.
