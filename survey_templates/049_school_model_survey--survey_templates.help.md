# School Model Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended for end users who want to complete a School Model Survey form. The School Model Survey is a tool used to gather information about school preferences for online or offline learning environments. Completing this form will help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer each question based on your school's learning environment.
3. Select the options that best fit your school's needs.
4. If a question is not relevant to your school, feel free to skip it.
5. Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Model Survey** (`school_model_survey_first_page`): This is the introduction page to the School Model Survey.
- **How do you prefer (school_model_survey_first_page_question_1)** (`select_one`): Choose how you prefer your learning environment: Online, Offline, or Hybrid.
    * Select Online if your school is primarily online-based.
    * Select Offline if your school is primarily offline-based.
    * Select Hybrid if your school has both online and offline components.
- **Options for How do you prefer (school_model_survey_first_page_question_1_options)** (`select_multiple`): Select any additional options that apply to your school's learning environment. For example, Online and Hybrid.
- **How do you prefer (school_model_survey_second_page_question_1)** (`select_one`): Choose how you prefer your learning environment on this page. This is similar to the previous question.
- **Options for How do you prefer (school_model_survey_second_page_question_1_options)** (`select_multiple`): Select any additional options that apply to your school's learning environment on this page. For example, Online and Hybrid.
- **How do you prefer (school_model_survey_second_page_question_2)** (`date`): Provide a date that represents when your school prefers to be online or offline.
- **How do you prefer (school_model_survey_second_page_question_3)** (`number`): Enter a number that represents the ratio of online to offline learning time.
- **Contact Information (school_model_survey_second_page_question_4)** (`email`): Enter your email address or the email address of your school's contact person.
- **Additional Comments (school_model_survey_second_page_question_5)** (`text`): Add any additional comments about your school's learning environment.

Please note that the form is divided into three pages, and each page has a "School Model Survey" label. These are just introductory labels and do not affect the form's functionality. You can complete each page independently.
