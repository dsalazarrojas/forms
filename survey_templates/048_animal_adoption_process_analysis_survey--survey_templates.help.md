# Animal Adoption Process Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect information on the animal adoption process from respondents, providing insights on their experiences, pain points, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the **Role in adoption process** that best describes your involvement: Staff, Volunteer, or Adopter.
2. Choose the **Process stage** being evaluated: Application, Screening, Match, or Follow up.
3. Describe the **Main pain points** you have experienced or observed during the adoption process.
4. If you have any **Suggestions for improvement**, please provide practical recommendations.
5. If you wish to be contacted for follow-up, provide your **Contact email**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Responder name
• **Responder name** (text): This field is optional and allows you to provide your name for reference purposes. If you prefer to remain anonymous, you can leave this field blank.

### Role in adoption process
• **Role in adoption process** (select_one):
	+ **Staff**: Staff members involved in the adoption process.
	+ **Volunteer**: Volunteers who assist in the adoption process.
	+ **Adopter**: Individuals or families adopting animals.

### Process stage
• **Process stage being evaluated** (select_one):
	+ **Application**: The initial stage where adopters submit their application.
	+ **Screening**: The stage where adopters are screened for suitability.
	+ **Match**: The stage where adopters are matched with animals.
	+ **Follow up**: The stage where adopters are followed up on after adoption.

### Main pain points
• **Main pain points** (text): Describe the key issues you have experienced or observed during the adoption process.

### Suggestions for improvement
• **Suggestions for improvement** (text): Provide practical recommendations for improving the adoption process.

### Priority for action
• **Priority for action** (select_one):
	+ **Low**: Low priority for action.
	+ **Medium**: Medium priority for action.
	+ **High**: High priority for action.

### Contact for follow up
• **Contact for follow up** (email): If you wish to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up, provide your email address.

### Suggestions for improvement (continued)
• **Suggestions for improvement** (text): This field is optional and allows you to provide additional practical recommendations for improving the adoption process.
