# crime_control_support_fund_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your level of support for crime control and your preferences for engagement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide your contact details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, optional):
	* Fill in your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information in this field.
2. **Indicate your level of support for crime control** (`Support Level`, `select_one`, optional):
	* Choose your level of support from the options provided:
		+ High
		+ Low
3. **Select your engagement preferences** (`Engagement Preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	* Choose all the ways in which you would like to be engaged with regarding crime control:
		+ Yes
		+ No
4. **Enter your email address** (`Email`, `email`, optional):
	* Fill in a valid email address for any follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details (text)**:
	+ This field collects any additional contact information you'd like to provide to be contacted.
* **Support Level (select_one)**:
	+ This question is asking about your level of support for crime control.
* **Engagement Preferences (select_multiple)**:
	+ This field allows you to select the methods by which you would like to be engaged with regarding crime control.
* **Email**:
	+ This field is for you to input a valid email address.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in accurate information.
- Only fill out this form if you support crime control and have not submitted it before.
- The form can be completed without selecting any fields if you prefer.
- If you have any questions, please contact [insert contact info].
