# Vaccination Schedule Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to evaluate users' knowledge about vaccination schedules, their benefits, safety concerns, administration methods, and potential side effects. It is an educational tool for anyone interested in this topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. For fields that are not mandatory (marked as "optional" or not required), you can skip them if you're unsure or don't have the information.
3. Ensure you're filling out the correct information for each field, especially for fields with specific options or choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is a vaccine?** (`vaccination_schedule_knowledge_quiz`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is a text input where you can describe your understanding of what a vaccine is.
* **Vaccines benefits include** (`vaccine_benefits`, text, required: true):
	+ This field is a text input where you can list the benefits of vaccines. Please answer concisely and accurately.
* **What are the potential risks of vaccines?** (`vaccination_safety`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is a text input where you can discuss potential risks or side effects associated with vaccines.
* **What is the recommended vaccination schedule for a newborn baby?** (`vaccination_schedules`, select_one, required: true):
	+ This field is a dropdown menu asking for the recommended vaccination schedule for a newborn baby. Choose one of the provided options that best answers your knowledge.
* **How are vaccines administered?** (`vaccine_administration`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This field is a dropdown menu asking about the methods of vaccine administration. You can select multiple answers if you're familiar with different methods.
* **What are the common side effects of vaccines?** (`vaccine_side_effects`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is a text input where you can describe any common side effects of vaccines you are aware of.
* **What are some potential interactions between vaccines?** (`vaccine_interactions`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is a text input where you can discuss any known interactions between vaccines.
* **What is the summary of this quiz?** (`quiz_summary`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is a text input where you can briefly summarize your knowledge about vaccination schedules in general.
