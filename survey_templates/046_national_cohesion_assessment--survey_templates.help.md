# National Cohesion Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The National Cohesion Assessment form is designed to gauge the level of national cohesion and sense of belonging among its citizens. It aims to understand the feelings and attitudes of citizens towards their region and the institutions that represent it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions in the following pages:
	* Introduction: A brief description of your region and its institutions.
	* Region: Select the region you belong to.
	* Institution: Select the institutions you trust.
	* Belonging: Rate your sense of belonging to your region.
	* Trust: Rate your trust in your institutions.
	* Unity: Rate your sense of unity with your region.
	* Additional comments: Any additional comments or feedback you have about your region.
	* Contact information: Provide your contact information (Email, Phone, Date, Time) if you want to be contacted for follow-up surveys.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, optional): Please provide a brief description of your region and its institutions.
* **Region** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the region you belong to from the list of options.
* **Institution** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the institutions you trust from the list of options.
* **Institution** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the institutions you trust from the list of options.
* **Belonging** (`5`, `number`, optional): Rate your sense of belonging to your region from 0 (not at all) to 5 (very much).
* **Trust** (`6`, `number`, optional): Rate your trust in your institutions from 0 (not at all) to 5 (very much).
* **Unity** (`7`, `number`, optional): Rate your sense of unity with your region from 0 (not at all) to 5 (very much).
* **Additional Comments** (`8`, `note`, optional): Any additional comments or feedback you have about your region.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, optional): Provide your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up surveys.
* **Phone** (`10`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number if you want to be contacted for follow-up surveys.
* **Date** (`11`, `date`, optional): Provide your date of birth (or any other date you would like to provide).
* **Time** (`12`, `time`, optional): Provide your time of day (or any other time you would like to provide).

Note: Please answer all the questions in the pages above, and then submit your form when you are finished.
