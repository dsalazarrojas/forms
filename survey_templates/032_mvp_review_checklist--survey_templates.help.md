# mvp_review_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review and rate the MVP of a product or service. It is designed to gather feedback from users on various aspects of the product, including its usability, technical features, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form, which includes an introduction to the product, and sections on usability, product fit, decision making process, user experience, and overall usability rating.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing feedback on your experience with the product.
3. Select the relevant options for each question, such as selecting the best answer for a question or rating a feature on a scale of 1-5.
