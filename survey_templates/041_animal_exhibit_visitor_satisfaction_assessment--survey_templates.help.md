# Animal Exhibit Visitor Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from visitors to our animal exhibit. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions to help us improve your experience and the overall quality of our exhibit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each field carefully, as indicated by the required status of each question.
2. The form is divided into sections, each focusing on a different aspect of your visit.
3. Use the dropdown menus to select your answers, and provide text responses where applicable.
4. Take a moment to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Please enter your name, which will help us address you by name in any follow-up communications.
	+ Optional
* **Email Address**: Provide your email address for us to follow up on your feedback, if needed.
	+ Optional
* **Date of Visit**: Please select the date you visited our animal exhibit.
	+ Required
* **What Type of Group Are You With**: Describe your group composition (e.g., solo visitor, couple, family with children, etc.).
	+ Required
* **How Many People in Your Group**: Select the size of your group.
	+ Required
* **Are There Children in Your Group**: Indicate if there are children present in your group.
	+ Required
* **How Long Did You Spend at the Exhibit**: Choose the duration of your visit.
	+ Required
* **Overall How Satisfied Were You with Your Visit**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your visit.
	+ Required
* **How Would You Rate the Quality of the Exhibits**: Select the quality of our exhibits.
	+ Required
* **How Would You Rate the Apparent Welfare of the Animals**: Rate the welfare of the animals you observed.
	+ Required
* **How Would You Rate the Educational Value**: Select the educational value of your visit.
	+ Required
* **How Would You Rate the Cleanliness of the Facility**: Rate the cleanliness of our facilities.
	+ Required
* **How Would You Rate Staff Friendliness**: Rate the friendliness of our staff.
	+ Required
* **How Would You Rate Staff Knowledge**: Rate the knowledge of our staff, if you interacted with them.
	+ Optional
* **How Would You Rate the Value for Money**: Select the value for money of your visit.
	+ Required
* **What Was Your Favorite Exhibit or Animal**: Describe your favorite exhibit or animal.
	+ Optional
* **What Could Be Improved**: Suggest areas for improvement.
	+ Optional
* **How Would You Rate Accessibility**: Rate the accessibility of our exhibit.
	+ Optional
* **How Would You Rate Facilities and Amenities**: Rate the quality of our facilities and amenities.
	+ Optional
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend This Exhibit**: Rate the likelihood of recommending our exhibit.
	+ Required
* **How Likely Are You to Return**: Rate the likelihood of returning to our exhibit.
	+ Required
* **Is This Your First Visit**: Indicate if this is your first visit.
	+ Optional
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select how you heard about our exhibit.
	+ Optional
* **Any Additional Comments or Suggestions**: Share any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
	+ Optional
