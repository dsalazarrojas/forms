# Regional Rural Banks Branding Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gauge the familiarity and knowledge of our regional bank's branding among users. It's designed to assess how well users recognize our logo, recall our history, and rate their satisfaction with our services. The results will help us improve our branding strategy and provide valuable insights for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question honestly, and make sure to fill out all required fields.
2.  For fields that ask for multiple selections, choose all that apply.
3.  For fields that ask for a single selection, choose the best response for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is the name of our regional bank?** (`branding_knowledge`, text, required: false): This field is to test your familiarity with our regional bank's name.
* **2. Which of the following logos are recognized as our regional bank?** (`logo_recognition`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is designed to test your recognition of our logo.
* **3. How many years have you been familiar with our regional bank?** (`years_of_familiarity`, select_one, required: false): This field measures the length of time you've been acquainted with our regional bank.
* **4. What is your age group?** (`age_group`, select_one, required: false): This field asks for your demographic information.
* **5. What is the score you think you would get out of 100 if you were to take the test?** (`expected_score`, select_one, required: false): This field gauges your self-assessment of your knowledge about our regional bank.
* **6. How do you rate your knowledge of our regional bank's history?** (`knowledge_of_history`, select_one, required: false): This field tests your knowledge of our regional bank's history.
* **7. Which of the following describes your relationship with our regional bank?** (`relationship_with_bank`, select_one, required: false): This field measures your level of satisfaction with our bank.
* **8. Which of the following services does our regional bank offer?** (`service_offered`, select_multiple, required: false): This field assesses your awareness of our services.
* **9. What are your comments about our regional bank?** (`comments`, note, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or feedback.
* **10. What is your score out of 100?** (`quiz_submission`, number, required: true): This field asks for your actual score.
* **11. What is your email address?** (`regional_rural_banks_branding_quiz_form_submitted`, email, required: true): This field is to collect your contact information.
