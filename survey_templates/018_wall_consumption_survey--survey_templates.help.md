# Wall Consumption Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Wall Consumption Survey" form aims to gather information about the usage and consumption habits of walls by asking a series of questions. It will provide useful insights for analysis and help in making informed decisions related to wall management.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, follow these steps:
    1.  Start by answering the questions on the "Main Page" section.
    2.  For the "Wall type" question, select one of the provided options: "Yes" or "No".
    3.  In the "Usage volume" field, describe the volume of wall usage you have experienced.
    4.  For the "Purchasing channel" question, select all the channels through which you have purchased wall-related products or services (if applicable).
    5.  In the "Satisfaction level" field, enter a numerical value to indicate your level of satisfaction with the wall-related services or products.
    6.  Finally, for the "Wall material type" question, select all the types of wall materials you have used (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Main Page** (`main_page`, `text`, required/optional): Answer the questions on this page to start the survey.
*   **Wall type** (`wall_type`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks whether you have consumed wall or not.
*   **Usage volume** (`usage_volume`, `text`, optional): Describe the volume or amount of wall you have consumed.
*   **Purchasing channel** (`purchasing_channel`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have purchased wall-related products or services, choose all the channels through which you have done so.
*   **Satisfaction level** (`satisfaction_level`, `number`, optional): Enter a numerical value to indicate your level of satisfaction with wall-related products or services.
*   **Wall material type** (`wall_material_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have used any wall materials, select all the types you have used.
