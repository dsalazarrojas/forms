# Data Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users to input metadata about their form. The goal is to collect a variety of information to help categorize and manage forms in a system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the desired information:

1. Enter a title for your form in the "Form Title" field.
2. Add a description for your form in the "Form Description" field.
3. Choose a category for your form from the "Form Category" dropdown menu.
4. Add any relevant tags for your form in the "Form Tags" field.
5. Upload or paste in a form image in the "Form Image" field.
6. Add a form video in the "Form Video" field.
7. Add a form audio in the "Form Audio" field.
8. Upload or link a form document in the "Form Document" field.
9. Enter a URL for your form in the "Form URL" field.
10. Enter a date for your form in the "Form Date" field.
11. Select a time for your form in the "Form Time" field.
12. Enter an email address for your form in the "Form Email" field.
13. Enter a phone number for your form in the "Form Phone" field.
14. Add any notes about your form in the "Form Note" field.
15. Select options for "Form Checkbox" and "Form Checkbox 2".
16. Select an option from the "Form Select One 1" dropdown menu.
17. Select multiple options for "Form Select Multiple 1" and "Form Select Multiple 2".
18. Enter a date range for your form in the "Form Date Range" field.
19. Enter an integer value for your form in the "Form Integer" field.
20. Enter a decimal value for your form in the "Form Decimal" field.
21. Enter a text area for your form in the "Form Text Area" field.
22. Select options for "Form Checkbox 3".
23. Enter a rating for your form in the "Form Rating" field.
24. Select options for "Form Checkbox 3" and "Form Submit".

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your form, a brief description to identify it.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Add a description for your form, a longer text that explains its purpose.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Choose a category for your form from the dropdown menu.
* **Form Tags** (`form_tags`, text, optional): Add relevant tags for your form to categorize it.
* **Form Image** (`form_image`, text, optional): Upload or paste an image for your form.
* **Form Video** (`form_video`, text, optional): Add a video for your form.
* **Form Audio** (`form_audio`, text, optional): Add an audio file for your form.
* **Form Document** (`form_document`, text, optional): Upload or link a document for your form.
* **Form URL** (`form_url`, text, optional): Enter a URL for your form.
* **Form Date** (`form_date`, date, optional): Enter a date for your form.
* **Form Time** (`form_time`, time, optional): Select a time for your form.
* **Form Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter an email address for your form.
* **Form Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number for your form.
* **Form Note** (`form_note`, note, optional): Add any notes about your form.
* **Form Checkbox** (`form_checkbox`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more checkboxes for your form.
* **Form Select One 1** (`form_select_one_1`, select_one, optional): Select an option from the dropdown menu.
* **Form Select Multiple 1** (`form_select_multiple_1`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Form Date Range** (`form_date_range`, text, optional): Enter a date range for your form.
* **Form Integer** (`form_integer`, number, optional): Enter an integer value for your form.
* **Form Decimal** (`form_decimal`, number, optional): Enter a decimal value for your form.
* **Form Text Area** (`form_text_area`, text, optional): Enter a text area for your form.
* **Form Checkbox 2** (`form_checkbox_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more checkboxes for your form.
* **Form Rating** (`form_rating`, text, optional): Enter a rating for your form.
* **Form Checkbox 3** (`form_checkbox_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more checkboxes for your form.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, text, optional): Click the submit button to complete and save your form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
- Use clear and concise text for your form title and description.
- Select an appropriate category and tags for your form.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to upload or link files.
- Test your form before saving it to ensure it functions as expected.
