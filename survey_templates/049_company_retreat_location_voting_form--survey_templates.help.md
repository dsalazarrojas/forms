# Company Retreat Location Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Company Retreat Location Voting Form is designed to gather information from employees about their preferred locations, activities, and accommodation preferences for company retreats. This form helps the retreat planning team to create a more enjoyable and inclusive experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name**: In this field, please type in your full name as you would like to be addressed during the retreat.
2. **Choose your preferred location**: Select one of the four options:
	* Resort A: A luxurious retreat venue offering scenic views and relaxing amenities.
	* Conference Center B: A professional and productive retreat venue with state-of-the-art facilities.
	* Beach Venue: A fun and relaxing retreat venue with direct access to the beach.
	* Mountain Lodge: A secluded and scenic retreat venue with breathtaking views.
3. **Select your preferred activities**: You can choose one or multiple activities from the list:
	* Workshops: Engage in educational and professional development sessions.
	* Team building: Participate in group activities to strengthen team bonds.
	* Outdoor activities: Enjoy activities like hiking, biking, or water sports.
	* Networking events: Meet and connect with colleagues in a relaxed setting.
4. **Specify your accommodation preference**: You can choose one of the three options:
	* Single: A private room with personal amenities.
	* Shared: A shared room with one or more colleagues.
	* No preference: You're okay with any accommodation arrangement.
5. **List any dietary restrictions**: If you have any dietary requirements or restrictions, please list them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee name**: This field is optional for tallies and participation tracking. Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed during the retreat.
- **Preferred retreat location**: Choose one location that you would like to attend for the company retreat.
- **Preferred activities**: Select one or multiple activities that you enjoy or would like to participate in during the retreat.
- **Accommodation preference**: Specify your preferred accommodation type for the retreat.
- **Dietary restrictions**: List any dietary requirements or restrictions you may have, such as vegetarian or vegan.
