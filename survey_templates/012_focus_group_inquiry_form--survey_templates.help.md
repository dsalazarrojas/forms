# Focus Group Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Focus Group Inquiry Form" is a tool used to collect information from potential participants for a focus group inquiry event. This form will help event organizers understand who is attending and what their expectations are for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the participant in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
4. Select the type of event you are participating in from the available options (Event, Seminar, Webinar, Conference, or Workshop).
5. Enter the date and time of the event (if applicable).
6. If required, enter the duration of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (Participant Name, Text, Optional)**: Enter your name as the participant. This will help the event organizers keep track of who is attending the event.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, Text, Optional)**: Please enter your contact information so that the event organizers can easily reach out to you.
* **Event Name (Event Name, Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Type (Event Type, Select-One, Required)**: Select the type of event you are participating in from the available options. This will help the event organizers prepare accordingly.
* **Event Date (Event Date, Date, Optional)**: Enter the date of the event (if applicable).
* **Event Time (Event Time, Time, Optional)**: Enter the time of the event (if applicable).
* **Event Duration (Event Duration, Number, Optional)**: If required, enter the duration of the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to help the event organizers plan and prepare for the event.
* If you have any specific requests or questions, please contact the event organizers directly.
* This form is for internal tracking purposes only and will not be shared publicly.
