# Freedom Advocacy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freedom Advocacy Feedback Form is used to collect information about individuals or groups supporting or advocating for a cause or campaign. This data helps organizations better understand their target audience and tailor their advocacy strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as accurately and completely as possible. You will be asked for demographic information, preferences, and experiences. If you are unsure about a field, you can always ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`id: 1`, `text`, `not required`): Write a brief introduction about your relationship with the cause or campaign.

### Campaign details
* **Campaign details** (`id: 2`, `text`, `not required`): If applicable, describe any specific campaign or initiative you are supporting.

### Perceived Impact
* **Perceived Impact** (`id: 3`, `text`, `not required`): Describe how you believe this campaign will impact the cause or community you are supporting.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`id: 4`, `text`, `not required`): If you have any comments or feedback on the campaign or cause, please share them here.

### Future Outreach
* **Future Outreach** (`id: 5`, `text`, `not required`): Describe how you think the campaign team can better engage with you in the future.

### Supporter Demographics
* **Supporter Demographics** (`id: 6`, `select_one`, `not required`): Select your gender.
	+ **Male**
	+ **Female**
	+ **Other**

### Supporter Location
* **Supporter Location** (`id: 7`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Select where you can be reached:
	+ **Online**
	+ **In-person**

### Supporter Interests
* **Supporter Interests** (`id: 8`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Select the causes you are interested in:
	+ **Environmentalism**
	+ **Social Justice**
	+ **Human Rights**

### Supporter Occupation
* **Supporter Occupation** (`id: 9`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Select your occupation:
	+ **Student**
	+ **Employee**
	+ **Retiree**

### Supporter Income
* **Supporter Income** (`id: 10`, `number`, `not required`): If applicable, enter your income.

### Supporter Education
* **Supporter Education** (`id: 11`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Select your highest level of education:
	+ **High School**
	+ **Some College**
	+ **Bachelor's**
	+ **Master's**

### Supporter Job Title
* **Supporter Job Title** (`id: 12`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Select your job title:
	+ **Student**
	+ **Employee**
	+ **Manager**

### Supporter Work Hours
* **Supporter Work Hours** (`id: 13`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Select your work hours:
	+ **1-10 hours**
	+ **11-20 hours**
	+ **More than 20 hours**

### Supporter Email
* **Supporter Email** (`id: 14`, `email`, `not required`): If applicable, enter your email.

### Supporter Phone
* **Supporter Phone** (`id: 15`, `text`, `not required`): If applicable, enter your phone number.

### Supporter Notes
* **Supporter Notes** (`id: 16`, `note`, `not required`): If you have any additional comments or notes about yourself, please share them here.

### Supporter Website
* **Supporter Website** (`id: 17`, `text`, `not required`): If applicable, enter your website.

### Supporter Facebook
* **Supporter Facebook** (`id: 18`, `text`, `not required`): If applicable, enter your Facebook.

### Supporter Twitter
* **Supporter Twitter** (`id: 19`, `text`, `not required`): If applicable, enter your Twitter.

### Supporter Instagram
* **Supporter Instagram** (`id: 20`, `text`, `not required`): If applicable, enter your Instagram.

### Supporter Other
* **Supporter Other** (`id: 21`, `text`, `not required`): If applicable, enter any other social media handles or websites.

### Campaign Start Date
* **Campaign Start Date** (`id: 22`, `date`, `not required`): If applicable, enter the start date of the campaign.

### Campaign End Date
* **Campaign End Date** (`id: 23`, `date`, `not required`): If applicable, enter the end date of the campaign.

### Campaign Start Time
* **Campaign Start Time** (`id: 24`, `time`, `not required`): If applicable, enter the start time of the campaign.

### Campaign End Time
* **Campaign End Time** (`id: 25`, `time`, `not required`): If applicable, enter the end time of the campaign.

## Tips

* If you are having trouble completing a field or have a question, feel free to ask for help.
* Please answer truthfully to provide the most accurate information.
* All fields are optional, but filling in as much detail as possible will help organizations better tailor their advocacy strategy to you.
