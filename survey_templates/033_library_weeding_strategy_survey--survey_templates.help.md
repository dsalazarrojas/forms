# Library Weeding Strategy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Weeding Strategy Survey is a tool designed to gather information about your library's weeding strategy. This survey helps us understand your library's weeding frequency, decision-making criteria, challenges, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. Review your responses before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weeding Frequency** (1, number, required:false): 
This question asks about how often you weed your library's collection. Please provide a numerical value (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually) to indicate the frequency of weeding in your library.

* **Weeding Decision Criteria** (2, select_one, required:false): 
Select one or more criteria that you use to decide which items to weed. Please choose from the following options:
  - Weeding Criteria 1
  - Weeding Criteria 2
  - Weeding Criteria 3

* **Weeding Challenges** (3, text, required:false): 
Describe any challenges you face when weeding your library's collection.

* **Weeding Improvement Ideas** (4, text, required:false): 
Share any ideas you have to improve your weeding strategy.

* **Library Types** (5, select_multiple, required:false): 
Select all types of libraries that apply to your institution (check all that apply):
  - Urban
  - Rural
  - Academic
  - Public
  - School
  - Special

* **Staff Feedback** (6, text, required:false): 
Provide any feedback or comments from your staff about weeding your library's collection.
