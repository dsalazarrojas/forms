# wildlife_safari_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your wildlife safari experience, including the guide's performance, safety measures, and overall experience. It will help us improve our services and provide a better experience for future clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for each section that best describes your experience. If you didn't experience something, select "No" for that particular section.
2. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
3. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Safari Experience** (`safari_experience`): Select "Yes" if you had a good experience during the safari tour, and "No" otherwise.
* **Guide Performance** (`guide_performance`): Select "Yes" for each aspect of the guide's performance that you felt was good, such as communication, knowledge, and overall attitude. Select "No" for any aspects where you felt the guide performed poorly.
* **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`): Select "Yes" if you felt the safety measures were adequate, and "No" otherwise.
* **Overall Safari Rating** (`overall_safari_rating`): Rate your overall experience on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Tour Name** (`tour_name`): Enter the name of the tour you took, if you'd like to provide more specific feedback.
* **Date** (`tour_date`): Enter the date you took the tour, if you'd like to provide more specific feedback.
* **Guide Name** (`guide_name`): Enter the name of your guide, if you'd like to provide more specific feedback.
* **Tour Operator** (`tour_operator`): Enter the name of the tour operator, if you'd like to provide more specific feedback.
* **Safari Experience 2** (`safari_experience_2`): Select "Yes" if you had a good experience during the second part of the tour, and "No" otherwise.
