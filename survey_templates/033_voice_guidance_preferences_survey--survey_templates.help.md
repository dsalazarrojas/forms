# Voice Guidance Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on user preferences regarding voice guidance in a specific application or system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Start" button at the top of the form.
2. Answer the questions regarding voice guidance used, satisfaction, accessibility preferences, feature priorities, and voice guidance features as you see fit. You can select multiple answers for each question.
3. If you have any comments or feedback regarding the voice guidance features, enter them in the "Voice Guidance Comments" field. This is an optional section.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback regarding the form, enter them in the "Form Submission" field. This is also an optional section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Start
* **Start** (`text`, required: false): This is the starting point of the form. Clicking this button will allow you to begin answering the questions.

### 2. Voice Guidance Used
* **Voice Guidance Used** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all that apply regarding the use of voice guidance in our application or system. You can choose from "Yes" and "No" options.

### 3. Voice Guidance Satisfaction
* **Voice Guidance Satisfaction** (`number`, required: false): Please rate your satisfaction with our current voice guidance features on a scale of 1-5.

### 4. Accessibility Preferences
* **Accessibility Preferences** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply regarding your accessibility preferences. You can choose from "Yes" and "No" options.

### 5. Feature Priorities
* **Feature Priorities** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all that apply regarding your feature priorities. You can choose from "Yes" and "No" options.

### 6. Voice Guidance Features
* **Voice Guidance Features** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply regarding our voice guidance features. You can choose from "Yes" and "No" options.

### 7. Voice Guidance Comments
* **Voice Guidance Comments** (`note`, required: false): If you have any comments or feedback regarding our voice guidance features, please enter them here.

### 8. Voice Guidance Submission
* **Voice Guidance Submission** (`note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or feedback regarding the form, enter them here.

### 9. Form Submission
* **Form Submission** (`text`, required: false): Please enter any final comments or feedback regarding the form submission process.
