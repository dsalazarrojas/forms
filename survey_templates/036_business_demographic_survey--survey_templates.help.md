# Business Demographic Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your business demographics. It aims to collect data on your personal and business characteristics, such as age, education level, years of experience, industry, location, and revenue range. This information will help us better understand your business needs and provide you with relevant insights and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each field. You will be asked for information about your personal demographics, business characteristics, and customer demographics.
2. For select fields, choose the option that best describes your business. If you don't find a suitable option, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation in the text field.
3. Be as accurate as possible when providing your age range, education level, and years of experience.
4. If you have multiple locations, select all that apply.
5. If you have any other business challenges or opportunities that you would like to share, use the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for your age range to help us understand your personal background.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for your gender identity.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for your highest level of education completed.
* **Years of Business Experience** (`years_experience`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for your total business experience.
* **Primary Business Industry** (`business_industry`, text, **required**): This field asks for the industry of your business.
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for the geographic location of your business.
* **Business Age** (`business_age`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for the age of your business.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for the number of employees in your business.
* **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue_range`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for the approximate annual revenue of your business.
* **Business Ownership Structure** (`ownership_structure`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for the structure of your business.
* **Primary Customer Age Group** (`primary_customer_age`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for the age range of your main customers.
* **Primary Customer Gender** (`primary_customer_gender`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for the gender of your primary customers.
* **Customer Income Level** (`customer_income`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for the income range of your customers.
* **Customer Location** (`customer_location`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for the location of your customers.
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for the type of customers you primarily serve.
* **Biggest Business Challenge** (`biggest_challenge`, text, **required**): This field allows you to share your main business challenge.
* **Biggest Market Opportunity** (`market_opportunity`, text, **required**): This field allows you to share your main market opportunity.
* **Competition Level in Your Market** (`competition_level`, select_one, **required**): This field asks for the level of competition in your market.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when providing your information.
* If you're unsure about any field, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
* If you have any other business challenges or opportunities that you would like to share, use the text fields.
