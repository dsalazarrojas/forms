This is a help guide for the Exterior Shade Expectation Survey form. 

This form is intended to gather information about exterior shades, including contact details, shade type and condition, customer expectations, and follow-up information. The form is designed to be completed by a contact person with relevant details and is optional. The form is meant to be used in a survey setting, where data is collected to understand customer expectations and preferences.

### Exterior Shade Expectation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Exterior Shade Expectation Survey is used to collect information about exterior shades, including contact person details, shade type and condition, customer expectations, and follow-up information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into various pages with different fields to be completed.
2. You can navigate through the pages using the page navigation buttons at the top of the page.
3. Fill in all required fields (indicated by an *) and optional fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Survey Template

* **Survey Template** (`survey_template`, note): Enter any comments or notes about the survey here.
 
### Page 2: Contact Person

* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
 
### Page 3: Contact E-mail

* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_e_mail`, email, required): Enter the email of the contact person.
 
### Page 4: Contact Phone

* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
 
### Page 5: Location

* **Location** (`location`, number, required): Enter the location number.
 
### Page 6: Contact Address

* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the contact person.
 
### Page 7: Shade Type

* **Shade Type** (`shade_type`, text, required): Enter the type of shade.
 
### Page 8: Shade Condition

* **Shade Condition** (`shade_condition`, select_one, required): Select the condition of the shade. Options: Shade Free, Partially Shaded, Fully Shaded.
 
### Page 9: Customer Expectation

* **Customer Expectation** (`customer_expectation`, select_multiple, required): Select the customer's expectation. Options: Good, Fair, Poor.
 
### Page 10: Customer Expectation Note

* **Customer Expectation Note** (`customer_expectation_note`, note, required): Enter any notes about the customer's expectation.
 
### Page 11: Shade Description

* **Shade Description** (`shade_description`, note, required): Enter any notes about the shade.
 
### Page 12: Follow-up

* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required): Select whether to follow-up. Options: Yes, No, Maybe.
 
### Page 13: Additional Comments

* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments.
 
### Page 14: Shade Color

* **Shade Color** (`shade_color`, text, required): Enter the shade color.
 
### Page 15: Follow-up Time

* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, date, required): Enter the follow-up time.
 
### Page 16: Customer Expectation 2

* **Customer Expectation 2** (`customer_expectation2`, text, required): Enter the customer's expectation 2.
 
### Page 17: Shade Material

* **Shade Material** (`shade_material`, text, required): Enter the shade material.
 
### Page 18: Additional Comments 2

* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments2`, note, required): Enter any additional comments 2.
 
### Page 19: Follow-up Time 2

* **Follow-up Time 2** (`follow_up_time2`, date, required): Enter the follow-up time 2.
 
### Page 20: Contact Name

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
 
### Page 21: Contact Name 2

* **Contact Name 2** (`contact_name2`, text, required): Enter the name of the second contact person.
 
### Page 22: Contact Address 2

* **Contact Address 2** (`contact_address2`, text, required): Enter the address of the second contact person.
 
### Page 23: Contact Phone 2

* **Contact Phone 2** (`contact_phone2`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the second contact person.
 
### Page 24: Contact E-mail 2

* **Contact E-mail 2** (`contact_e_mail2`, email, required): Enter the email of the second contact person.
 
### Page 25: Contact Address 3

* **Contact Address 3** (`contact_address3`, text, required): Enter the address of the third contact person.
 
Please ensure that all fields are filled in completely to ensure accurate data collection. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us.
