# Building Compliance Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your compliance awareness and understanding of the building's policies, procedures, and safety protocols. It will help identify areas where you may need additional training or support to ensure you are working safely and effectively within the building.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field as completely and accurately as possible.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, select one or multiple options that best describe your answer.
3. For text fields, provide your answer in a brief but clear description.
4. Do not leave any fields blank, as this may impact the accuracy of the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Your Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **2. Department**: Type the name of the department you belong to or work with.
* **3. Your Role**: Enter your job title or position.
* **4. Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
* **5. Survey Date**: Enter the date you completed the survey.
* **6. Building Name**: Type the name of the building where you work or are most frequently in.
* **7. Time in Building**: Select how long you have been working or present in the building.
	+ Less than 6 months
	+ 6 months to 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ Over 5 years
* **8. Compliance Training Received**: Select if you have received compliance training within the last year.
	+ Yes - Within Past Year
	+ Yes - Over 1 Year Ago
	+ No - Never Received
	+ Not Sure
* **9. Training Topics Covered**: Select all that apply from the list of compliance topics.
* **10. Emergency Procedures Knowledge**: Rate your understanding of emergency procedures.
	+ Very Knowledgeable
	+ Knowledgeable
	+ Somewhat Knowledgeable
	+ Not Very Knowledgeable
	+ Not Knowledgeable at All
* **11. Evacuation Route Knowledge**: Select how well you know the evacuation routes.
	+ Yes - All Routes
	+ Yes - Primary Routes
	+ Somewhat
	+ No - Not Sure
* **12. Safety Equipment Location Knowledge**: Select if you know where safety equipment is located.
	+ Yes - All Equipment
	+ Yes - Most Equipment
	+ Somewhat
	+ No - Not Sure
* **13. Compliance Documentation Access**: Select how easy it is for you to access compliance documentation.
	+ Yes - Easy Access
	+ Yes - But Difficult to Find
	+ No - Not Available
	+ Not Sure
* **14. Incident Reporting Knowledge**: Select how clear you understand the incident reporting process.
	+ Yes - Very Clear
	+ Yes - Somewhat Clear
	+ No - Not Clear
	+ Not Sure
* **15. Safety Signage Clarity**: Select how clear and visible you find the safety signs.
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Very Unclear
* **16. Compliance Communication**: Select how well you are informed about compliance.
	+ Very Well Informed
	+ Well Informed
	+ Somewhat Informed
	+ Poorly Informed
	+ Not Informed at All
* **17. Building Policy Awareness**: Select how aware you are of building policies.
	+ Very Aware
	+ Aware
	+ Somewhat Aware
	+ Unaware
	+ Very Unaware
* **18. Compliance Concerns**: Select all that apply from the list of concerns.
* **19. Training Effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of compliance training.
	+ Very Effective
	+ Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Ineffective
	+ Very Ineffective
	+ No Training Received
* **20. Additional Training Needed**: Select all that apply from the list of topics.
* **21. Preferred Training Format**: Select your preferred training format.
	+ In-Person Sessions

	+ Video Tutorials

* **22. Compliance Confidence Level**: Rate your confidence level in compliance.
	+ Very Confident
	+ Confident
	+ Somewhat Confident
	+ Not Very Confident
	+ Not Confident at All
* **23. Suggestions for Improvement**: Share any suggestions or recommendations for improving compliance awareness and training.
* **24. Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about compliance and safety within the building.

## Tips
- Answer honestly and accurately, as your input will help identify areas for improvement and support.
- If you are unsure or unclear about a question, select the option that best reflects your understanding.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the provided text fields to share them.
