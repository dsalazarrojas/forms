# Weekly Meetings Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Meetings Feedback Form is used to gather feedback about weekly meetings. Please fill out the form below to provide your feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and make sure you have all the required information before starting to fill out the form.
2. Start with the "meeting_feedback_form" page, where you can input the title of the meeting and any other relevant details.
3. On the "meeting_participants" page, select all participants who attended the meeting.
4. For the "participant_attended" field, choose who was a participant in the meeting from the options provided.
5. On the "meeting_topic" page, enter the topic discussed during the meeting.
6. On the "meeting_outcome" page, describe the outcome or results of the meeting.
7. For the "participant_feedback" field, provide any feedback or comments from the meeting participants.
8. On the "meeting_notes" page, input any additional notes about the meeting.
9. If applicable, select whether the next meeting is planned on the "next_meeting_planned" page.
10. Rate your feedback on the "feedback_rating" page.
11. Finally, add any additional feedback on the "feedback_comment" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Feedback Form (1)**: This is the main section of the form, where you can input the meeting title and any other relevant details.
* **Meeting Participants (2)**: Select all participants who attended the meeting. 
* **Participant Attended (3)**: Choose who was a participant in the meeting from the options provided.
* **Meeting Topic (4)**: Enter the topic discussed during the meeting.
* **Meeting Outcome (5)**: Describe the outcome or results of the meeting.
* **Participant Feedback (6)**: Provide any feedback or comments from the meeting participants.
* **Meeting Notes (7)**: Input any additional notes about the meeting.
* **Next Meeting Planned (8)**: If applicable, select whether the next meeting is planned.
* **Feedback Rating (9)**: Rate your feedback on the meeting.
* **Additional Feedback (10)**: Add any additional feedback or comments about the meeting.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise feedback to help others understand the meeting's progress and outcome.
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback, especially for fields like "Meeting Outcome" and "Participant Feedback".
* If you're unsure about a field, you can always ask for clarification or guidance from the form's administrator or team lead.
