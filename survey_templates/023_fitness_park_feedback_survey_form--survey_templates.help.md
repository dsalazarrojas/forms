# Fitness Park Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Park Feedback Survey Form is designed to collect feedback from Fitness Park users on their overall fitness experience, including their ratings of the equipment, cleanliness, and overall satisfaction. Your input will help us improve the park and provide a better experience for all users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all answers that apply to your experience at Fitness Park.
2. Choose one of the options for each field, or select "N/A" if the question does not apply.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey_form_1**: This field asks about your overall satisfaction with the equipment at Fitness Park. Choose all the options that apply to your experience:
	+ Yes: You were satisfied with the equipment.
	+ No: You were not satisfied with the equipment.
* **equipment_rating_1**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the equipment at Fitness Park. Choose a number from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **how_did_you_find_equipment**: This field provides more detail about your experience with the equipment. Choose one of the options:
	+ Poor: The equipment was not satisfactory.
	+ Fair: The equipment was just okay.
	+ Good: The equipment was satisfactory.
	+ Excellent: The equipment was excellent.
* **cleanliness_rating_1**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the cleanliness at Fitness Park. Choose a number from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **how_did_you_find_cleanliness**: This field provides more detail about your experience with the cleanliness at Fitness Park. Choose one of the options:
	+ Poor: The park was not clean.
	+ Fair: The park was somewhat clean, but could be better.
	+ Good: The park was clean.
	+ Excellent: The park was very clean.
* **overall_satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your experience at Fitness Park. Choose a number from 1 (Very Unsatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
* **how_satisfied_are_you**: This field provides more detail about your overall satisfaction. Choose one of the options:
	+ Very Unsatisfied: You were very dissatisfied.
	+ Somewhat Unsatisfied: You were somewhat dissatisfied.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: You were somewhat satisfied.
	+ Very Satisfied: You were very satisfied.

## Tips
* Be honest with your answers, as they will help us improve the park and provide a better experience for all users.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please include them in the space provided below.
