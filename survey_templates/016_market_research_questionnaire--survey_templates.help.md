# Market Research Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect valuable insights into customers' demographics, product usage, and experiences. Your responses will help us understand your needs and preferences, which will inform our product development and customer service decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill in the relevant fields according to the instructions provided. You can select multiple options for most fields, so be sure to review each page carefully to ensure you've provided the desired information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### What is your age? (Page 1)

* **Age** (`number`, **required**: true): Please enter your age to provide demographic information.

### How long have you been using our product? (Page 2)

* **Product use** (`select_multiple`, **required**: false): Select one of the following options to indicate how long you've been using our product:
	+ Less than 1 month
	+ 1-3 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2-5 years
	+ More than 5 years

### How do you rate our current product offerings? (Page 3)

* **Product ranking** (`select_one`, **required**: false): Select one of the following options to rate our product offerings:
	+ 1 (Poor)
	+ 2 (Fair)
	+ 3 (Good)
	+ 4 (Excellent)

### How much do you know about market trends in the industry? (Page 4)

* **Market trends** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter any relevant information about market trends in this field. This will help us understand your level of awareness about industry developments.

### Have you recently purchased a similar product? (Page 5)

* **Recent purchase** (`select_one`, **required**: false): Select one of the following options to indicate if you've recently purchased a similar product:
	+ True
	+ False

### Which product attributes are most important to you? (Page 6)

* **Product attributes** (`select_multiple`, **required**: false): Select all that apply to indicate the product attributes that are most important to you:
	+ Price
	+ Quality
	+ Performance
	+ Design
	+ Brand

### How do you rate our customer service? (Page 7)

* **Customer service** (`select_one`, **required**: false): Select one of the following options to rate our customer service:
	+ 1 (Poor)
	+ 2 (Fair)
	+ 3 (Good)
	+ 4 (Excellent)

### Who are the top 3 competitors in the market? (Page 8)

* **Competitors** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter the names of the top 3 competitors in the market, separated by commas.

### What are the key factors that influence your purchasing decisions? (Page 9)

* **Purchasing factors** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter any relevant information about the key factors that influence your purchasing decisions.

### How often do you use our product? (Page 10)

* **Product usage** (`select_multiple`, **required**: false): Select one of the following options to indicate how often you use our product:
	+ Never
	+ Occasionally
	+ Frequently
	+ Almost Daily

### What are your impressions of our product? (Page 11)

* **Product impressions** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter your impressions of our product in this field.

### Do you have any feedback or suggestions for our company? (Page 12)

* **Feedback** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter any feedback or suggestions you may have for our company.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Review each page carefully to ensure you've provided the desired information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
