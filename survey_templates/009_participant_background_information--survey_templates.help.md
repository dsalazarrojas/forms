# Participant Background Information - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic participant background information for a study or program. It is intended to gather details about the participant, their contact information, and other relevant details. This form is meant to be filled out by participants or their representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Carefully read each section and ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
2. For each field, enter your answers accurately and thoroughly.
3. Please ensure all required fields are filled out, if any.
4. Review your answers for completeness and correctness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (Participant Details)**: This section is where you can enter your name, contact information, and other basic details about yourself.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter your relevant contact details, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Study and Program Information (Study and Program Information)**: Provide details about the study or program you are participating in or managing.
* **Demographics (Demographics)**: Enter any additional demographic information about yourself, such as age, occupation, or education level.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)**: Enter the name, relationship, and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Other Programs (Other Programs)**: If you are participating in other programs or studies, please list them here.
* **Additional Information (Additional Information)**: Enter any other relevant information about yourself or your participation that is not covered by the other fields.
* **Participant Statement (Participant Statement)**: This is a text field for you to enter any statements or comments you would like to make about your participation.
* **Participant Signature (Participant Signature)**: Please sign your name to confirm your participation.
* **Program Manager Signature (Program Manager Signature)**: If you are a program manager, please sign your name to confirm your management of this participant.
* **Additional Information (Additional Information)**: Please enter any other information that is relevant to your participation or management.
* **Participant Signature (Participant Signature)**: Please sign your name to confirm your participation.
* **Program Manager Signature (Program Manager Signature)**: Please sign your name to confirm your management of this participant.

Note: Some fields are not required, so please only fill out the fields that are relevant to you or your participation.
