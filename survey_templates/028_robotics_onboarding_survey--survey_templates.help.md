# robotics_onboarding_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The robotics_onboarding_survey form is designed to gather information from new employees onboarding into a company that deals with robotics, such as their new member details, interests, experience levels, goals, roles, training plans, and additional information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, indicated by a star (*) next to the label. If a field is not marked as required, you can leave it blank.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and truthful information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### New Member Details (new_member_details)
* **New Member Details** (`new_member_details`, text, required): Enter your name and any other details that you think would be helpful for your onboard team to know.

### Interests (interests)
* **Interests** (`interests`, text, required): Describe your interests, hobbies, or areas of expertise that are relevant to the field of robotics.

### Experience Levels (experience_levels)
* **Experience Levels** (`experience_levels`, text, required): Indicate your level of experience in robotics, for example, "beginner", "intermediate", or "advanced".

### Goals (goals)
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required): Define your short-term and long-term goals related to the field of robotics, for example, "Complete robotics training program", "Develop a new robotic arm".

### Roles (roles)
* **Roles** (roles, select_one, optional): Do you have any experience or training in robotics? 
  * Yes
  * No

### Training Plans (training_plans)
* **Training Plans** (`training_plans`, text, optional): If you have a plan in place for training or professional development, describe it.

### Additional Info (additional_info)
* **Additional Info** (additional_info, note, optional): This field is for any additional information that you want to share with your onboard team, such as your background, experience, or qualifications.
