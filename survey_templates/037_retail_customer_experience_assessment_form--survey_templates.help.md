# Retail Customer Experience Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their retail experience. The data collected will help our team understand customer satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, follow the instructions for each page.
2. Review each page carefully to ensure you've answered all required questions.
3. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Visits** (`store_visits`, `number`, **optional**): Please estimate the total number of times you have visited our store.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate the quality of service you received from our staff. Choose from: Very Good, Good, Average, or Needs Improvement.
* **Retail Customer Experience** (`retail_customer_experience`, `note`, **optional**): Please provide your overall impression of your retail experience. This is an optional note field for any additional feedback or comments.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your answers.
- Make sure to provide specific examples or details to support your feedback.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
