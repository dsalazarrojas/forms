# Accounting System Usability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting System Usability Survey is a form designed to collect feedback on the usability of an accounting system. It helps us understand the strengths and weaknesses of the system, allowing us to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about system development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please follow these steps:
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For select-one and select_multiple questions, choose the answer that best fits your experience with the system.
3. For text questions, provide a brief but clear explanation.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **System Name** (`system_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the software being evaluated. Please enter the exact name as it appears on your screen.
* **Ease of Navigation** (`ease_of_navigation`, `select_one`, required): Rate how easy or difficult it is to navigate the system. Choose from: 1 - Very Difficult, 2, 3, 4, or 5 - Very Easy.
* **Speed of Task Completion** (`speed_of_task_completion`, `select_one`, required): Rate how fast the system responds to your actions. Choose from: Very Slow, Slow, Average, Fast, or Very Fast.
* **Clarity of Error Messages** (`clarity_of_error_messages`, `select_one`, required): Rate the clarity of error messages. Choose from: Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent.
* **Frequency of System Crashes** (`frequency_of_system_crashes`, `select_one`, required): Rate how often the system crashes. Choose from: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.
* **Most Used Features** (`most_used_features`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select up to three features that you use most frequently. Choose from: Dashboard, Reporting, Data Entry, Reconciliation, or User Management.
* **Likelihood to Recommend to a Colleague** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, `number`, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend the system to a colleague. Choose a number between 0 and 10.
* **Biggest Pain Point** (`biggest_pain_point`, `text`, optional): Describe the most frustrating part of using the system.
* **Feature Request or Improvement** (`feature_request_or_improvement`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving the system, please provide them in this field.
* **-- Thank you for helping us improve our tools --** (`footer_note`, `note`, required): This is a thank you note and not a field to fill out. However, your feedback is greatly appreciated, and we are committed to making our tools better for you.
