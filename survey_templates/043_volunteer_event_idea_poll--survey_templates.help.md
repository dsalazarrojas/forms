# Volunteer Event Idea Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect ideas for a volunteer event. It's intended for users to provide information about a potential event they'd like to organize. Users can input details such as the title, description, date, and target audience of the event. The form also allows for the selection of various options for the event's frequency, location, and target interest. Users can also provide additional contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your event idea in the **Event Title** field.
2. Provide a brief description of your event idea in the **Description** field. You can type in as much detail as you'd like.
3. Select the type of event you have in mind (**Event**, **Activity**, or **Project**) in the **Event Type** field.
4. Enter the date for your event in the **Date** field. Use the date format (MM/DD/YY).
5. Select the time for your event in the **Time** field. Choose the time you think would be best for your event.
6. Choose one or more locations where the event could take place from the **Location** field. Select from the options **Home**, **School**, **Community Center**, **Park**, or **Other**.
7. Select one or more ideas for your event from the **Ideas** field. Choose from the options **Event 1**, **Event 2**, **Event 3**, **Event 4**, or **Event 5**.
8. Choose the frequency of your event (**One Time**, **Regularly**, or **Irregularly**) in the **Frequency** field.
9. Select the target audience for your event (**Children**, **Adults**, or **Both**) in the **Target Audience** field.
10. Select one or more target age ranges for your event (**All Ages**, **Children (5-12)**, **Teenagers (13-19)**, **Young Adults (20-39)**, or **Adults (40+**) from the **Target Age Range** field.
11. Select one or more target interests for your event from the **Target Interest** field. Choose from the options **Interest 1**, **Interest 2**, **Interest 3**, **Interest 4**, or **Interest 5**.
12. Enter any additional information about your event in the **Other Info** field.
13. Provide your contact email in the **Contact Email** field.
14. Enter your contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Title**: A brief and descriptive title of your event idea.
* **Description**: A brief description of your event idea. This can include details such as the purpose, goals, and any notable features of the event.
* **Event Type**: The type of event you're proposing, such as a **Event**, **Activity**, or **Project**.
* **Date**: The date you're proposing for the event. Use the date format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time**: The time you think would be best for your event.
* **Location**: Choose one or more locations where the event could take place. Select from the options **Home**, **School**, **Community Center**, **Park**, or **Other**.
* **Idea**: Select one or more ideas for your event. Choose from the options **Event 1**, **Event 2**, **Event 3**, **Event 4**, or **Event 5**.
* **Frequency**: Choose the frequency of your event (**One Time**, **Regularly**, or **Irregularly**).
* **Target Audience**: Select the target audience for your event (**Children**, **Adults**, or **Both**).
* **Target Age Range**: Choose one or more target age ranges for your event (**All Ages**, **Children (5-12)**, **Teenagers (13-19)**, **Young Adults (20-39)**, or **Adults (40+**)**.
* **Target Interest**: Select one or more target interests for your event from the options **Interest 1**, **Interest 2**, **Interest 3**, **Interest 4**, or **Interest 5**.
* **Other Info**: Enter any additional information about your event.
* **Contact Email**: Provide your contact email.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to provide a clear and accurate event idea.
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field to ensure that your event idea is considered and executed effectively.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
