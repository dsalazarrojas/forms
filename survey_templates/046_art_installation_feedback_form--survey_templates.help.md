# Art Installation Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors about their experience with public art installations. Their input helps us improve future installations and create more engaging experiences for the public.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). If you'd like to provide additional comments, you may also fill out the optional fields.
2. The form is divided into sections with headers. Please scroll down to each section and fill out the relevant questions.
3. Be honest and specific in your feedback, as it will help us improve our future work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of the Installation (\*)**: Select the name of the installation you are providing feedback about from the dropdown list.
* **Date of Your Visit (\*)**: Enter the date you visited the installation.
* **How would you rate the visual appeal of the installation? (\*)**: Rate the visual appeal of the installation on a scale from Extremely Appealing to Unappealing.
* **Was the intended message or concept clear to you? (\*)**: Choose how clear the message or concept was to you.
* **If the work was interactive, how was the user experience? (\*)**: Rate the interaction level from Seamless and Intuitive to Technical issues encountered.
* **How did the installation affect the atmosphere of the space? (\*)**: Rate the impact on the space from Enhanced the space significantly to Felt out of place.
* **What was your favorite part of the installation? (\*)**: Describe your favorite part of the installation.
* **Was there anything that didn't work for you?**: If you'd like to provide additional comments, you may write about any issues you encountered.
* **How would you rate the lighting of the installation? (\*)**: Rate the lighting on a scale from Perfect to Too Dim.
* **Was the space around the installation easy to navigate? (\*)**: Choose how easy or difficult it was to access the space.
* **Suggestions for future art installations in this space**: If you have any suggestions or ideas, please share them here.
* **Would you like to be notified about our next installation? (\*)**: Choose if you'd like to receive updates about our future work.
* **Your Email Address**: If you'd like to receive updates about our future work, please provide your email address.

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required fields. Please fill out all required fields to ensure your feedback is complete and helpful.
