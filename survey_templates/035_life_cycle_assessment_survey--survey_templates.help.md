# Life Cycle Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Cycle Assessment Survey is designed to help assess the life cycle of a product or company. This form will help you gather information on various stages of the product life cycle, from development to disposal or recycling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for "Product Leader" from the dropdown list.
2. Select all relevant options for "Sustainability Team" from the dropdown list. You can select multiple options if applicable.
3. Select the relevant option for "Consultant" from the dropdown list.
4. Enter any relevant information for "Product Processes" in the text field.
5. Select the relevant option for "Sustainability Leader" from the dropdown list.
6. Select the relevant option for "Company" from the dropdown list.
7. Enter any relevant information for "Role" in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Product Leader**
Product Leader: This field is used to select the person or team responsible for leading the product development and production process. Please choose one of the provided options.
### 2. **Sustainability Team**
Sustainability Team: This field allows you to select multiple teams that are involved in the sustainability efforts of the product. Please select all relevant options.
### 3. **Consultant**
Consultant: This field is used to select the consultant responsible for implementing sustainability practices in the product development and production process. Please choose one of the provided options.
### 4. **Product Processes**
Product Processes: This field is used to enter any additional information about the product's production process. Please provide any relevant details.
### 5. **Sustainability Leader**
Sustainability Leader: This field is used to select the person or team responsible for leading the sustainability efforts of the product. Please choose one of the provided options.
### 6. **Company**
Company: This field is used to select the company associated with the product. Please choose one of the provided options.
### 7. **Role**
Role: This field is used to enter any additional information about the product's role or purpose. Please provide any relevant details.
