# Utility Survey And Excavation Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from providers and contacts for utility survey and excavation training registrations. It's a straightforward form meant to gather the necessary details for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "provider" field with the name of the provider or organization associated with the training.
2. Provide the "contact" field with the name and contact information of the person responsible for the training.
3. Choose the type of training the provider offers by selecting "Utility Survey", "Excavation", or "Both" from the "training_type" field.
4. Select the type of provider by choosing "Training Provider" or "Training Contractor" from the "provider_type" field.
5. Enter the contract start date in the "contract_start_date" field, if applicable.
6. Choose the start date of the training in the "training_start_date" field.
7. Select the start time of the training in the "training_start_time" field.
8. If applicable, enter the contract end date in the "contract_end_date" field.
9. Enter the end date of the training in the "training_end_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider** (`provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the provider or organization associated with the training.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for the training.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the contract if applicable. This field is only required if your organization has a contract with the provider.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of training the provider offers by selecting "Utility Survey", "Excavation", or "Both".
* **Provider Type** (`provider_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of provider by choosing "Training Provider" or "Training Contractor".
* **Training Start Date** (`training_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the training. If you're unsure, check with the provider directly.
* **Training Start Time** (`training_start_time`, time, optional): Select the start time of the training. Be as specific as possible to avoid confusion.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the contract if applicable.
* **Training End Date** (`training_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the training. If you're unsure, check with the provider directly.
