# Access Control Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Access Control Feedback Form is designed to collect user feedback on the access control system, aiming to identify areas of improvement and suggest changes to enhance security and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide a clear and comprehensive feedback, please follow these steps:

* Enter your name (if you'd like us to follow up with you)
* Enter your email address (if you'd like us to follow up with you)
* Select your role (e.g., Employee, Contractor, Visitor, etc.)
* Provide your department or location
* Indicate how often you use the access control system
* Rate the ease of use of the system
* Rate the reliability of the system (e.g., card readers, biometric scanners)
* Select any issues you've experienced with the system
* Report the response time for access issues
* Rate how safe you feel with the current access control measures
* Identify which aspects of access control are most important to improve (select up to 2)
* Rate your overall satisfaction with the access control system

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Your name** (optional)
* (`respondent_name`): Enter your name if you'd like us to follow up with you.

### 2. **Email address** (optional)
* (`respondent_email`): Enter your email address if you'd like us to follow up with you.

### 3. **Your role**
* (`respondent_role`): Select your role, e.g., Employee, Contractor, Visitor, etc.

### 4. **Department or location**
* (`department_or_location`): Enter the department or location you are affiliated with.

### 5. **How often do you use the access control system**
* (`frequency_of_use`): Select how often you use the access control system: Multiple times daily, Once daily, A few times a week, Occasionally, Rarely.

### 6. **Ease of use**
* (`ease_of_use`): Rate the ease of use of the system: Very difficult, Difficult, Neutral, Easy, Very easy.

### 7. **Reliability**
* (`reliability_rating`): Rate the reliability of the system (e.g., card readers, biometric scanners): Very unreliable, Unreliable, Neutral, Reliable, Very reliable.

### 8. **Issues experienced**
* (`issues_experienced`): Select any issues you've experienced with the system (select all that apply): Card or badge not recognized, Slow response time at entry points, System downtime or outages, Difficulty obtaining or replacing badges, Unclear access permissions, None - no issues experienced.

### 9. **Response time for access issues**
* (`response_time_security`): Report how quickly your access issue was resolved: Within minutes, Within an hour, Same day, Next day or longer, Issue was never resolved, Never reported an issue.

### 10. **Safety feeling**
* (`safety_feeling`): Rate how safe you feel with the current access control measures: Very unsafe, Somewhat unsafe, Neutral, Safe, Very safe.

### 11. **Improvement priorities**
* (`improvement_priorities`): Select up to 2 aspects of access control that are most important to improve: Speed of entry, System reliability, Badge replacement process, Visitor check-in experience, After-hours access, Communication about access policies.

### 12. **Overall satisfaction**
* (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the access control system: Very dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very satisfied.

### 13. **Additional feedback**
* (`additional_feedback`): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the access control system.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can select multiple issues experienced with the system.
* You can select up to 2 aspects of access control that are most important to improve.
* You can provide any additional comments or suggestions about the access control system.
