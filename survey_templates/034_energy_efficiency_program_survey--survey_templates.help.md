# energy_efficiency_program_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and suggestions from participants in the energy efficiency program. The form is used to collect demographic and contact information and evaluate the program's effectiveness and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions in the introduction page.
2. Provide your participation information in the program participation section.
3. Share your satisfaction with the program and suggest improvements.
4. Add your contact details so we can follow up with you.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (text): This is the first page of the form that provides a brief background on the energy efficiency program and its purpose.

### Program Participation
* **Program Participation** (text, required): Please indicate your level of participation in the energy efficiency program, e.g., "I am actively participating" or "I am interested but have not yet participated".

### What do you think about the program?
* **What do you think about the program?** (select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the program, choosing one of the options: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### What improvements do you suggest?
* **What improvements do you suggest?** (text, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have to improve the program.

### Additional Feedback
* **Additional Feedback** (text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the program, please share them here.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (select_multiple, required): Please select how you would like to be contacted: Email, Phone.
* **Name** (text, required): Your name.
* **Email** (email, required): Your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Your phone number.
* **Address** (text, optional): Your address (if you'd like to be contacted via mail).
* **Department** (text, optional): Your department or office.
* **Position** (text, optional): Your position or title.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback and suggestions.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the program administrators.
* If you have already completed this form, please do not submit again, as your feedback will not be recorded.
