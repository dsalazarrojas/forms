# Environmental Chemistry Knowledge Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your understanding, attitudes, and preferences related to environmental chemistry. Your input will help us better understand your knowledge and provide more effective support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Please ensure you have a stable internet connection and a compatible browser.
* Scroll through each page and read the explanations for each field.
* Fill in the required information for each field, marked as "required" in the explanations.
* Use the options provided for select multiple fields where applicable.
* Click "Submit" when you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Note, not required)**: This is a text field for you to provide any additional context or information about your background or experience with environmental chemistry.
* **Survey Chemistry Understanding (Text, not required)**: Describe your current understanding of environmental chemistry, its principles, and concepts.
* **Attitudes towards Environmental Chemistry (Text, not required)**: Share your thoughts and feelings about environmental chemistry, its importance, and its applications.
* **Learning Needs (Text, not required)**: Specify what you'd like to learn or need help with in environmental chemistry.
* **Educational Background (Select Multiple, not required)**: Choose all the options that apply to your educational background in environmental chemistry.
	+ Option 1: Bachelor's degree in Environmental Chemistry
	+ Option 2: Master's degree in Environmental Chemistry
	+ Option 3: Ph.D. in Environmental Chemistry
* **Years of Teaching Chemistry (Number, not required)**: Provide the number of years you have taught chemistry, specifically environmental chemistry.
* **Department Name (Text, not required)**: Enter the name of the department you are associated with, if applicable.
* **Contact Name (Text, not required)**: Provide your name and any other contact information if you'd like to be reached out for follow-up questions or discussions.

## Tips
* Be honest and concise in your answers to provide the most accurate information.
* Use proper formatting and grammar to ensure your responses are easy to read.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to your department or contact the form owner.

This help guide is designed to assist you in completing the form accurately and effectively. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
