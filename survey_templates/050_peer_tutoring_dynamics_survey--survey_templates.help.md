# Peer Tutoring Dynamics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experience with peer tutoring in a school setting. Your feedback will help us better understand the dynamics of peer tutoring and how to improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your School Name in the first field.
* Enter your Tutor Name in the second field.
* Select your role frequency (e.g., 1-2 times a month, Daily, Weekly, etc.) from the dropdown list in the third field.
* Select one or more of the options that best describe your role effectiveness (e.g., Helping student, Improving student, Supporting student, Teaching student) from the dropdown list in the fourth field.
* If you have encountered any challenges, enter them in the fifth field.
* If you have any suggestions, enter them in the sixth field.
* Enter your Email address in the seventh field.
* Enter your Phone number in the eighth field.
* If you have any additional comments, please enter them in the ninth field.
* Select how often you go to the school for tutoring from the dropdown list in the tenth field.
* Select how effective you find school tutors from the dropdown list in the eleventh field.
* If you have any comments about school tutors, please enter them in the twelfth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name**: This is the name of your school where you have experience with peer tutoring.
* **Tutor Name**: This is your name as a peer tutor.
* **Role Frequency**: This field asks about how often you engage in peer tutoring. Select the option that best describes your frequency.
* **Role Effectiveness**: This field asks about your effectiveness as a peer tutor. Select one or more of the options that best describe your effectiveness.
* **Challenges**: If you have faced any challenges while being a peer tutor, please enter them here.
* **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for improving the peer tutoring program, please enter them here.
* **Email**: This is your email address that will be used for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: This is your phone number that will be used for contact purposes.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments, please enter them here.
* **How often do you go to the school for tutoring?**: This field asks about how often you visit the school for tutoring.
* **How effective is your experience with school tutors?**: This field asks about your experience with school tutors. Select one or more of the options that best describe your effectiveness.
* **Comments on school tutors**: If you have any comments about your experience with school tutors, please enter them here.
