# Angles And Scales Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate a student's knowledge and understanding of angles and scales. It includes questions on definitions, measurement, and calculation, as well as opportunities for students to reflect on their learning.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by carefully reading each question and instruction on the form.
2.  Answer each question or complete each task as directed.
3.  Make sure to provide answers or explanations for all required fields.
4.  Use the hint text to help guide your response if needed.
5.  Double-check your work before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or other identification.
* **Grade Level** (Grade Level, text, required): Enter your current grade level in school.
* **-- Angles Identification --** (Note, required): This section is for notes or comments from the instructor.
* **What is the definition of an acute angle?** (Select One, required): Choose from the options: Less than 90 degrees, Exactly 90 degrees, More than 90 but less than 180, Exactly 180.
* **What is the range for a reflex angle?** (Select One, required): Choose from the options: 180 to 360 degrees, 90 to 180 degrees, 0 to 90 degrees.
* **How many degrees are in a right angle?** (Number, required): Enter a numerical value.
* **-- Scales and Measurements --** (Note, required): This section is for notes or comments from the instructor.
* **Explain what a 1 to 100 map scale means.** (Text, required): Provide a clear and concise explanation.
* **Calculate actual distance if 2cm on map equals 5km and map distance is 6cm.** (Number, required): Use the formula: actual distance = (map distance x actual scale) / map scale. Enter a numerical value.
* **Which instrument is primarily used for measuring angles?** (Select One, required): Choose from the options: Protractor, Compass, Ruler, Set Square.
* **Rate your confidence with scale drawings.** (Select One, required): Choose from the options: Very Confident, Confident, Needs Practice, Confused.
* **Show your working for a multi-step angle calculation problem.** (Text, optional): This is an opportunity for you to show your thought process and work.
* **-- Assessment Reflection --** (Note, optional): This section is for notes or comments from the instructor.

Note: Make sure to complete all required fields.
