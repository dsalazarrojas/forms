# Audit Communication Flow Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Audit Communication Flow Survey is designed to gather feedback from auditors on their experiences with communication during audits. This helps identify areas for improvement in the audit communication flow and enables data-driven decisions to enhance the audit process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Survey Participants**: This is a placeholder section to introduce the survey participants and provide context for the following questions.
2. **Name of Auditor**: Enter your name as the auditor who participated in the audit.
3. **Department Audited**: Enter the department that was being audited.
4. **Audit Project Reference**: Enter the reference number or name of the audit project.
5. **Communication Channels**: This is a placeholder section to introduce the communication channels used during the audit.
6. **Primary Method of Communication Used**: Select the primary method of communication used during the audit (e.g., Email, Face-to-Face Meetings, Video Calls, Shared Audit Platform/Portal, Phone Calls).
7. **Frequency of Communication**: Rate the frequency of communication during the audit (e.g., Just Right, Too Frequent (Disruptive), Not Frequent Enough).
8. **Timeliness of Responses from the Other Party**: Rate the timeliness of responses from the other party (e.g., Very Prompt, Acceptable, Sometimes Delayed, Consistently Late).
9. **Communication Quality**: This is a placeholder section to introduce the quality of communication.
10. **Clarity of Information and Document Requests**: Rate the clarity of information and document requests (e.g., Extremely Clear, Mostly Clear, Often Vague, Very Unclear).
11. **Communication Barriers**: Describe any communication barriers encountered during the audit (e.g., Technical issues, language barriers, siloed information).
12. **Professionalism of Communication**: Rate the professionalism of communication (e.g., 5 - Highly Professional, 4 - Professional, 3 - Neutral, 2 - Needs Improvement, 1 - Unprofessional).
13. **Suggestions for Flow Improvement**: Enter any suggestions for improving the audit communication flow.
14. **Date of Survey Completion**: Select the date on which you completed this survey.

**Tips:**

* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* For the professionalism of communication and clarity of information and document requests questions, select the rating that best describes your experience.
* For the communication barriers question, provide a detailed description of any issues you encountered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Auditor** (`auditor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the auditor who participated in the audit.
* **Department Audited** (`auditee_department`, text, required): Enter the department that was being audited.
* **Audit Project Reference** (`audit_project_ref`, text, optional): Enter the reference number or name of the audit project.
* **Primary Method of Communication Used** (`primary_comm_method`, select_one, required): Select the primary method of communication used during the audit (e.g., Email, Face-to-Face Meetings, Video Calls, Shared Audit Platform/Portal, Phone Calls).
* **Frequency of Communication** (`frequency_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the frequency of communication during the audit (e.g., Just Right, Too Frequent (Disruptive), Not Frequent Enough).
* **Timeliness of Responses from the Other Party** (`timeliness_of_responses`, select_one, required): Rate the timeliness of responses from the other party (e.g., Very Prompt, Acceptable, Sometimes Delayed, Consistently Late).
* **Clarity of Information and Document Requests** (`clarity_of_requests`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of information and document requests (e.g., Extremely Clear, Mostly Clear, Often Vague, Very Unclear).
* **Communication Barriers** (`communication_barriers`, text, optional): Describe any communication barriers encountered during the audit (e.g., Technical issues, language barriers, siloed information).
* **Professionalism of Communication** (`professionalism_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the professionalism of communication (e.g., 5 - Highly Professional, 4 - Professional, 3 - Neutral, 2 - Needs Improvement, 1 - Unprofessional).
* **Suggestions for Flow Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Enter any suggestions for improving the audit communication flow.
* **Date of Survey Completion** (`completion_date`, date, required): Select the date on which you completed this survey.
