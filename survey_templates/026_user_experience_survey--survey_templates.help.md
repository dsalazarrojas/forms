# User Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the user experience of your product or service. It's divided into several sections that cover Accessibility, Content, and Presentation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and determine which sections you would like to provide feedback on. You can choose from Accessibility, Content, and Presentation.
2. Enter your feedback in the corresponding sections. You can type in your comments in the text fields or select a score (0-5) for the numerical fields.
3. Submit your feedback when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Accessibility** (`Accessibility`, text, optional): This field is for providing any comments or thoughts you have about the accessibility of the product or service.
* **Accessibility Score** (`accessibility_score`, number, optional): If you'd like to provide a more quantitative assessment of the accessibility, select a score from 0 to 5, with 0 being "not accessible" and 5 being "very accessible".
* **Content** (`content_label`, text, optional): This field is for providing any comments or thoughts you have about the content of the product or service.
* **Content Score** (`content_score`, number, optional): If you'd like to provide a more quantitative assessment of the content, select a score from 0 to 5, with 0 being "not relevant" and 5 being "very relevant".
* **Presentation** (`presentation_label`, text, optional): This field is for providing any comments or thoughts you have about the presentation of the product or service.
* **Presentation Score** (`presentation_score`, number, optional): If you'd like to provide a more quantitative assessment of the presentation, select a score from 0 to 5, with 0 being "not pleasing" and 5 being "very pleasing".

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible with your feedback, providing concrete examples or situations where you encountered issues or had positive experiences.
* If you have any suggestions or recommendations, include them in the corresponding section.
* You can leave any of the sections blank if you don't have any feedback or comments to provide.
