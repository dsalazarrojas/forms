# Volunteer Program Impact Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and data on the impact of your participation in our volunteer program. It will help us understand your experiences, engagement, and any outcomes or achievements you've experienced through the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose your level of participation in the program from the options provided in **how_did_you_participate**.
2. Indicate how long you were involved in the program by selecting all that apply from **how_long_did_you_participate**.
3. Describe any notable outcomes or achievements you've experienced from your involvement in the program in **comments**.
4. If you'd like to stay updated on future program developments or opportunities, provide your email address in **receive_updates**.
5. If you have a phone number where we can reach you, please provide it in **contact_phone**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **program_impact**: This is the main section of the form where you can provide any thoughts, feedback, or suggestions on your experiences with the volunteer program.
* **how_did_you_participate**: Choose one or more of the options to describe how you participated in the program. This could be as a volunteer, a participant, or both.
* **how_long_did_you_participate**: Select all that apply to indicate how long you were involved in the program.
* **how_did_we_do**: Describe any notable outcomes or achievements you've experienced from your involvement in the program.
* **how_did_we_help**: Choose one or more of the options to describe how you helped others through the program.
* **comments**: Share any additional thoughts, feedback, or suggestions you have about your experiences with the program.
* **receive_updates**: If you'd like to stay updated on future program developments or opportunities, enter your email address here.
* **contact_phone**: If you have a phone number where we can reach you, please provide it so we can get in touch with you about program updates or opportunities.
