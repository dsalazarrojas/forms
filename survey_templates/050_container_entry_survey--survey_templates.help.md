# Container Entry Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Container Entry Survey is a form used to inspect and document the condition of a container's entry, including its seal, contents, and any issues or defects observed during the inspection. This form helps verify the accuracy of its contents and determine the need for corrective actions or follow-up inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, such as survey date, survey time, inspector name, and inspector ID.
2. Enter the container number and seal number.
3. Record the condition of the container's seal, including whether it is intact, broken, missing, or tampered with.
4. Describe the container's type and size.
5. Evaluate the exterior condition of the container, choosing from excellent, good, fair, poor, or damaged.
6. If necessary, describe any exterior damage.
7. Assess the interior condition of the container, selecting from clean, mostly clean, some debris, dirty, or contaminated.
8. If necessary, describe any interior damage.
9. Check if there are any unusual odors present and, if so, describe them.
10. Indicate whether evidence of pests is present and, if so, describe them.
11. Verify that the contents match the documentation and, if not, describe the discrepancies.
12. Enter the number of items inside the container.
13. If the container is refrigerated, record the temperature reading.
14. Indicate if photos were taken during the inspection.
15. Determine the inspection result, choosing from passed with no issues, passed with minor issues, failed with major issues, or failed with critical issues.
16. If necessary, specify the corrective actions required.
17. Indicate if a follow-up inspection is needed and, if so, schedule the date for the follow-up inspection.
18. Sign the form with your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Survey Time**: Enter the time of the inspection in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification card.
* **Inspector ID**: Enter your employee number or ID.
* **Container Number**: Enter the unique ID of the container.
* **Seal Number**: Enter the security seal number of the container.
* **Seal Condition**: Select whether the seal is intact, broken, missing, or tampered with.
* **Container Type**: Choose the type of container (e.g., dry van, refrigerated, open top, flat rack, tank, or other).
* **Exterior Condition**: Evaluate the overall state of the container's exterior, choosing from excellent, good, fair, poor, or damaged.
* **Exterior Damage Details**: Describe any exterior damage if necessary.
* **Interior Condition**: Assess the interior state of the container, selecting from clean, mostly clean, some debris, dirty, or contaminated.
* **Interior Damage Details**: Describe any interior damage if necessary.
* **Odor Present**: Indicate whether there are any unusual odors present.
* **Odor Description**: If odors are present, describe them in detail.
* **Pests Evidence**: Indicate whether evidence of pests is present.
* **Pest Details**: If pests are present, describe them in detail.
* **Moisture Present**: Indicate whether there is any moisture or water damage present.
* **Cargo Matches Documentation**: Verify that the cargo matches the documentation and, if not, describe the discrepancies.
* **Cargo Description**: Enter a description of what is inside the container.
* **Cargo Quantity**: Enter the number of items inside the container.
* **Temperature Reading**: If the container is refrigerated, enter the temperature reading.
* **Photos Taken**: Indicate whether photos were taken during the inspection.
* **Inspection Result**: Determine the inspection result, choosing from passed with no issues, passed with minor issues, failed with major issues, or failed with critical issues.
* **Corrective Action**: If necessary, specify the corrective actions required.
* **Follow-up Needed**: Indicate if a follow-up inspection is required.
* **Follow-up Date**: If a follow-up inspection is needed, schedule the date for the inspection.
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign the form with your digital signature.
