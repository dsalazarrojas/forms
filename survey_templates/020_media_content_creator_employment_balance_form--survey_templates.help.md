# media_content_creator_employment_balance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The media content creator employment balance form is a survey designed to gather information about the balance between work and personal life among media content creators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the introduction on Page 1.
2. On Page 2, enter your thoughts on "creator_balance" in the text field.
3. On Page 3, enter your thoughts on "work_life_balance" in the text field.
4. On Page 4, enter the number of hours you spend on work in the "work_hours" field.
5. On Page 5, enter the number of hours you have available for personal activities in the "available_time" field.
6. On Page 6, enter the average number of hours you spend on work in the "average_hours" field.
7. On Page 7, enter your thoughts on "work_life_ratio" in the text field.
8. On Page 8, you may optionally enter any additional comments.
9. On Page 9, select one of the available options for "submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (`introduction`, text, required): Please enter a brief introduction or context about your work as a media content creator. This will help us understand your perspective and background.
* **creator_balance** (`creator_balance`, text, required): Describe your current work-life balance as a media content creator. This could include your thoughts on your work schedule, workload, or any other factors that impact your work-life balance.
* **work_life_balance** (`work_life_balance`, text, required): Share your thoughts on the balance between your work and personal life. How do you feel about the demands of your work and how it affects your personal life?
* **work_hours** (`work_hours`, number, required): Enter the total number of hours you spend on work each week.
* **available_time** (`available_time`, number, required): Enter the number of hours you have available for personal activities each week.
* **average_hours** (`average_hours`, number, required): Enter the average number of hours you spend on work in a typical week.
* **work_life_ratio** (`work_life_ratio`, text, required): Please enter your thoughts on the balance between your work and personal life. This could include your goals or aspirations for improvement.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or comments, please enter them here.
* **submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select one of the available options to submit your response.
