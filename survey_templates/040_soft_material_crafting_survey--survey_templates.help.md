# Soft Material Crafting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soft Material Crafting Survey is designed to collect information on users' soft material crafting projects. This data will help us better understand user behavior and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. If you're unsure about a field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Soft Material Crafting Survey Form** (`soft_material_crafting_survey_form`, text, required): This is the main form section where you'll provide your project information.
* **Soft Crafting Project Details** (`soft_crafting_project_details`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your soft crafting project.
* **Fabric Used** (`fabric_used`, select_multiple, required): Select all the fabrics you've used for your project. Choose from the following options:
	+ Yarn
	+ Thread
	+ Felt
	+ Fabric
	+ Cotton
	+ Lycra
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Yarn Weight** (`yarn_weight`, select_one, required): Choose the weight of the yarn you used for your project:
	+ Medium weight
	+ Lightweight
	+ Heavyweight
	+ Unknown
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of projects you're working on. Choose from:
	+ Handmade item
	+ Custom item
	+ Gift item
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, time, optional): If you'd like to share how much time you spent on your project, enter the time spent in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Soft Material Crafting Survey Comments** (`soft_material_crafting_survey_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your project.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`, text, optional): Share any other relevant details about your project.
* **Project Photo URL** (`project_photo_url`, text, optional): If you'd like to share a photo of your project, enter the URL of the image.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled in for the form to be submitted. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
