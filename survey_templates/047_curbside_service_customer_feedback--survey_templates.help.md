# Curbside Service Customer Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers regarding the curbside service experience. This form helps us understand the customer's satisfaction with the service, identify areas of improvement, and provide opportunities for follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Curbside Service Customer**: Type a brief description of the customer who received the curbside service.
2. **Rating - 1-5**: Rate your overall experience with the curbside service, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
3. **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the service.
4. **Contact Permission**: Check "Yes" if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up purposes, otherwise check "No".
5. **Contact Phone**: If you want to be contacted by phone for follow-up, provide your phone number.
6. **Contact Email**: If you want to be contacted by email for follow-up, provide your email address.
7. **Follow Up**: Check "Yes" if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up, otherwise check "No".
8. **Follow Up Reason**: If you checked "Yes" for follow-up, explain the reason(s) why you want to be contacted.
9. **Service Date**: Enter the date the curbside service was provided.
10. **Service Time**: Enter the time the curbside service was provided.
11. **Service Location**: Type the location where the curbside service was provided.
12. **Service Vehicle**: Type the make of the vehicle that received the curbside service.
13. **Vehicle Make**: Select the make of the vehicle from the list.
14. **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
15. **Vehicle Model**: Type the model of the vehicle.
16. **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the service.
17. **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the service.
18. **Service Area**: Type the service area (e.g., front of house, back of house, both).
19. **Service Area Label**: Select the correct label for the service area from the list.
20. **Service Type**: Select the type of service provided (e.g., curbside, in-home, other).
21. **Service Type Label**: Select the label for the service type from the list.
22. **Follow Up Method**: Check "Yes" for each method of follow-up you are willing to receive (e.g., phone, email, text).
23. **Follow Up Method Label**: Select the correct label for each follow-up method from the list.

## Tips
- Please provide honest and constructive feedback about your experience.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
- Remember to provide your contact information if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up purposes.
