# Best Friend Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Friend Survey is a comprehensive tool to gather information about your best friend, assessing the depth and quality of your relationship. This survey will guide you through various aspects of your friendship, helping you reflect on what makes your friend special and meaningful to you.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Your Name**: Please fill out your full name to identify yourself in this survey.
2.  **Friend's Name**: Fill out your friend's full name, to whom the survey is about.
3.  Complete each subsequent question by selecting or typing your responses. Ensure you answer all required fields, marked as 'True/Required'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Your Name** (`your_full_name`, text, **Required**): Your full name to identify yourself in this survey.
- **Friend's Name** (`friend_full_name`, text, **Required**): Your friend's full name, who this survey is about.
- **Do you know their middle name?** (`middle_name_check`, select_one, **Required**): Check 'True' if you are familiar with your friend's middle name, or choose 'False' otherwise.
- **Do you know their favorite movie?** (`favorite_movie_check`, select_one, **Required**): Check 'True' if you know your friend's favorite movie, or choose 'False' otherwise.
- **Do you know their favorite musician?** (`favorite_musician_check`, select_one, **Required**): Check 'True' if you are familiar with your friend's favorite musician, or choose 'False' otherwise.
- **How often do you talk?** (`communication_frequency`, select_one, **Required**): Choose how often you have communication with your friend, including messaging and phone calls.
- **How do you usually communicate?** (`communication_methods`, select_multiple, **Required**): Select all methods you usually use to communicate with your friend, such as text messaging, phone calls, social media, or in-person conversations.
- **How much do you trust them?** (`trust_rating`, select_one, **Required**): Rate your level of trust with your friend, on a scale from 1 (not at all) to 5 (completely).
- **Do they make you a better person?** (`personhood_impact`, select_one, **Required**): Check 'Yes - definitely' if your friend positively impacts your life, 'Sometimes' for mixed results, or 'False' otherwise.
- **What is the most important quality in a friend?** (`friendship_quality_priority`, select_one, **Required**): Select the quality that matters most in a friend, such as loyalty, honesty, or kindness.
- **If your friend was in trouble, how would you help?** (`helper_scenario`, text, **Required**): Describe how you would support your friend in a challenging situation.
- **What do you appreciate most about this friend?** (`appreciation_description`, text, **Required**): Express what you value and enjoy about your friend's qualities or behaviors.
- **If you were both characters in a show, which ones would you be?** (`show_characters`, text, **Required/Optional**): Choose a TV show and characters you would like to be for fun and creativity.
- **What is a song that reminds you of each other?** (`friendship_song`, text, **Optional/Required**): Name a song that evokes memories or feelings connected to your friendship.
- **Anything else you would like to share about your friendship?** (`additional_comments_text`, text, **Optional/Required**): Add any final thoughts or feelings about your friend or relationship.
- **Date of Survey** (`survey_completion_date`, date, **Required**): Fill out the date you completed this survey for record-keeping.

Note: Ensure all required fields are filled out.
