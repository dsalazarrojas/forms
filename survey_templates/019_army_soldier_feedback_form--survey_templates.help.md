# Army Soldier Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from Army soldiers on their experiences and perceptions of their service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the answers that best match your experiences and opinions.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses, as they will help the Army improve the service experience for its soldiers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Name** (`respondent_name`, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide your name, if you feel comfortable doing so. Responses are confidential.
- **Rank** (`respondent_rank`, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide your rank.
- **Unit or Command** (`respondent_unit`, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide your unit or command.
- **Years of Service** (`years_service`, select_one, Optional): This field asks you to select the number of years you have served in the Army.
- **Date of Feedback** (`feedback_date`, date, Required): Please select the date of the feedback, ensuring it is accurate and complete.
- **Commander effectiveness** (`commander_effectiveness`, select_one, Required): This field asks you to rate the effectiveness of your commander, using the options: Excellent, Good, Average, Below average, Poor.
- **Leadership style is clear and fair** (`leadership_style`, select_one, Required): This field asks you to rate the leadership style, using the options: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly disagree.
- **Quality of communication from leadership** (`communication_quality`, select_one, Required): This field asks you to rate the quality of communication from leadership, using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very poor.
- **Satisfaction with working conditions** (`working_conditions_satisfaction`, select_one, Required): This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with working conditions, using the options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
- **Adequate resources** (`resources_adequate`, select_one, Required): This field asks you to rate whether you have adequate resources for your role, using the options: Yes, more than adequate, Yes, adequate, Somewhat adequate, No, inadequate, No, very inadequate.
- **Quality of training and development** (`training_quality`, select_one, Required): This field asks you to rate the quality of training and development, using the options: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor.
- **Overall unit morale** (`morale_level`, select_one, Required): This field asks you to rate the overall unit morale, using the options: Very high, High, Average, Low, Very low.
- **Job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, Required): This field asks you to rate your job satisfaction, using the options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
- **Mental health support availability** (`mental_health_support`, select_one, Required): This field asks you to rate the mental health support availability, using the options: Excellent, Good, Adequate, Poor, Not available.
- **Main concerns or challenges** (`main_concerns`, select_multiple, Optional): This field asks you to select the main concerns or challenges you face in your service.
- **Describe your main concerns in detail** (`concerns_description`, text, Optional): This field is for you to describe your main concerns in detail, if you feel comfortable doing so.
- **How can the Army improve your service experience** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide suggestions on how the Army can improve your service experience.
- **Would you recommend Army service to others** (`would_recommend`, select_one, Optional): This field asks you to rate whether you would recommend Army service to others, using the options: Yes, definitely, Yes, probably, Maybe, Probably not, Definitely not.
- **Additional feedback or comments** (`additional_feedback`, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide any additional feedback or comments you may have.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Select the options that best match your experiences and opinions.
- If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the "Additional feedback or comments" field.
