# Cellular Balance Treatment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help the clinic understand your experience with our treatment services. Your feedback will help us evaluate the effectiveness of our services and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name and contact information at the top of the form.
2. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to select the correct answer for each question.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the treatment session you are providing feedback about.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the therapist who provided your treatment, if you can remember their name.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Treatment** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the treatment, using the scale:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Energy Level Before Treatment** (`energy_level_before`, number, optional): Rate your energy level on a scale of 1-10 before the treatment.
* **Energy Level After Treatment** (`energy_level_after`, number, optional): Rate your energy level on a scale of 1-10 after the treatment.
* **Pain or Discomfort Relief** (`pain_relief`, select_one, optional): Rate the pain or discomfort relief provided by the treatment, using the scale:
	+ Significant Relief
	+ Moderate Relief
	+ Slight Relief
	+ No Change
	+ Worse
* **Sleep Quality Improved** (`sleep_quality_improved`, select_one, optional): Rate whether your sleep quality improved since the treatment, using the scale:
	+ Much Better
	+ Better
	+ Same
	+ Worse
	+ Much Worse
* **Would You Recommend This Treatment** (`recommend_treatment`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend the treatment to friends or family, using the scale:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Unsure
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **How Effective Was the Treatment** (`treatment_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the treatment on a scale:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Minimally Effective
	+ Ineffective
* **Therapist Professionalism** (`therapist_professionalism`, select_one, optional): Rate the therapist's professionalism and demeanor, using the scale:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Clinic Environment and Cleanliness** (`clinic_environment`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the facility, using the scale:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Ease of Scheduling Appointment** (`appointment_scheduling`, select_one, optional): Rate how easy it was to book your session, using the scale:
	+ Very Easy
	+ Easy
	+ Average
	+ Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **Desired Frequency of Future Treatments** (`next_appointment_frequency`, select_one, optional): Indicate how often you would like to return for treatment, using the scale:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Every 2-3 months
	+ As Needed
* **Specific Improvements Noticed** (`specific_improvements`, text, optional): List any specific improvements you noticed since the treatment.
* **Areas for Improvement at the Clinic** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Provide any feedback for service improvements.
* **Interest in Additional Services** (`additional_services_interest`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are interested in additional services, using the scale:
	+ Yes, Very Interested
	+ Maybe
	+ Not Interested
* **May We Contact You for Follow-up** (`contact_permission`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are willing to be contacted for follow-up, using the scale:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Indicate how you would like to be contacted, using the scale:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Text
	+ No Preference
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the treatment and service.
