# High Scores Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High Scores Survey form is designed to collect information from players about their game experiences. This form helps game developers understand player performance and make informed decisions to improve the game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please enter the following information about your game session:
	+ Game Information (text field)
	+ Your High Score (number field)
	+ Player Name (text field)
	+ Feedback (text field)
	+ Rating (select one option from 1 to 5)
	+ Game Type (select multiple options: Online, Offline, or Both)
* Fill in the required information and submit the form
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Information**: Enter a brief description of the game you played.
* **Your High Score**: Enter your high score, if you have one.
* **Player Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your in-game profile or a nickname you'd like to be known by.
* **Feedback**: Share any thoughts or suggestions about your game experience. This is a chance for us to hear from you!
* **Rating**: Choose how you would rate your game experience. Options range from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Game Type**: Select one or more of the game types you experienced (Online, Offline, or Both).
* **Playtime**: If you'd like to share how long you played, enter the time in 24-hour clock format.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your game session.
