# Moodboard Creation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Moodboard Creation Survey is designed to gather essential information about a moodboard, a visual tool used in design projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, please fill out the **Project Title** field with a brief description of your project.
2. In the **Project Description** field, provide a more detailed explanation of your project.
3. Select one or multiple color schemes that best represent your project's visual identity.
4. Choose the **Target Audience** for your project (e.g., Men, Women, or Children).
5. In the **Inspiration Sources** field, describe the sources of inspiration for your project.
6. Select a **Design Style** that suits your project (e.g., Modern, Minimalist, or Vintage).
7. Choose your **Preferred Color** for the project.
8. Enter a **Moodboard Name** that reflects the project's identity.
9. Provide a **Project URL** where the project can be found or accessed.
10. Upload a **Project Image** that represents the project's visual identity.
11. If relevant, select the **Target Age Group** for your project (e.g., Men, Women, or Children).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): A brief description of your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): A more detailed explanation of your project.
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple color schemes that represent your project's visual identity.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_one, required): Choose the target audience for your project (e.g., Men, Women, or Children).
* **Inspiration Sources** (`inspiration_sources`, text, required): Describe the sources of inspiration for your project.
* **Design Style** (`design_style`, select_one, optional): Select a design style that suits your project (e.g., Modern, Minimalist, or Vintage).
* **Preferred Color** (`preferred_color`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred color for the project.
* **Moodboard Name** (`moodboard_name`, text, required): Enter a moodboard name that reflects the project's identity.
* **Project URL** (`project_url`, text, required): Provide a URL where the project can be found or accessed.
* **Project Image** (`project_image`, text, required): Upload a project image that represents the project's visual identity.
* **Target Age Group** (`target_age_group`, select_one, optional): If relevant, choose the target age group for your project (e.g., Men, Women, or Children).

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- For fields with options, select the most suitable choice that reflects your project's characteristics.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to the form's purpose and use your best judgment.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
