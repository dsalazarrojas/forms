# Home Organization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your home organization habits. It is intended for individuals who want to understand their own habits and preferences regarding home organization. Please ensure that you are completing this form for personal, non-commercial use and not for any other purpose without explicit permission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction, which provides some background information about the form's purpose.
2. Answer the question about your home organization habits.
3. Answer the question about cluttered places in your home.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, text, optional): This is the beginning of the survey, which gives you some context about what the survey is about.
* **Home Organization** (`2`, text, optional): This question is about your habits and preferences regarding home organization. Please answer in a way that best describes your thoughts and feelings about organizing your home.
* **Cluttered Places** (`3`, text, optional): This question is about identifying areas in your home that tend to be cluttered or disorganized. Please be as specific as possible when answering.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* This form is for personal use only and not for any commercial purposes.
