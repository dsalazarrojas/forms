# Innovative Product Design Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovative Product Design Voting Form is designed to collect feedback and ratings on the design of new product concepts. The form helps to gauge user preferences and opinions on various product design options, allowing our team to refine and improve the design based on your input. This form is typically filled out after reviewing and interacting with our product designs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred options for each of the product design concepts presented to you. Make sure to choose all that apply.
2. Provide feedback on any of the product designs by typing in the 'share_your_feedback' field.
3. Rate the product designs using the numbered rating fields (rating_1 to rating_10).
4. Fill out all required fields marked as required.
