# Festival Experience Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback and suggestions on your experience at the festival.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Read each question carefully and answer all the required fields (marked as "required").
*   Please answer all the questions truthfully and accurately, as your feedback is valuable to us.
*   If you have any suggestions or ideas for next year's festival, please share them in the "any_suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required): Please rate your overall experience at the festival on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Entertainment Experience** (`entertainment_experience`, number, optional): If you have a specific experience with entertainment, please rate it on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Music Experience** (`music_experience`, number, optional): If you have a specific experience with music, please rate it on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Artist Performance** (`artist_performance`, number, optional): If you have a specific experience with the artist's performance, please rate it on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Art Venue Experience** (`art_venue_experience`, number, optional): If you have a specific experience with the art venue, please rate it on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Food and Beverage Experience** (`food_beverage`, number, optional): If you have a specific experience with food and beverages, please rate it on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Festival Staff Experience** (`festival_staff`, number, optional): If you have a specific experience with festival staff, please rate it on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Facilities Experience** (`facilities`, number, optional): If you have a specific experience with festival facilities, please rate it on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the festival on a scale of 1-5.
*   **What you would like to see next year** (`festival_experience_request_1`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas for next year's festival, please share them here.
*   **Any Suggestions** (`any_suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any other suggestions or feedback, please share them here.
*   **Name** (`name`, email, required): Please enter your name and email address to ensure we can contact you about your feedback.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you about your feedback.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by us, please enter your address.
*   **City** (`city`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by us, please enter your city.
*   **State** (`state`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by us, please enter your state.
*   **Country** (`country`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by us, please enter your country.
*   **Postal Code** (`postal`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by us, please enter your postal code.
*   **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter your company name.
*   **Job Title** (`job`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter your job title.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter your department name.
*   **Title** (`title`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by us, please enter your title.
*   **Fax** (`fax`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by us, please enter your fax number.

Note: Please ensure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
