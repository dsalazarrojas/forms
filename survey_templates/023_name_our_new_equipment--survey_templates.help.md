# Name Our New Equipment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Name Our New Equipment" form is designed to help users provide information about a new equipment, its name, role, and context.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the second name field.
3. Enter the title of the equipment in the title field.
4. Provide the context of the equipment in the context field.
5. Specify the role of the equipment in the role field.
6. Explain the reasoning behind the equipment's purpose in the reasoning field.
7. Suggest a name for the equipment in the name suggestion field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (first_name_input)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name (last_name_input)**: Enter your last name.
* **Title (title_input)**: Enter the title of the equipment.
* **Context (context_input)**: Describe the context in which the equipment is used.
* **Role (role_input)**: Specify the role or function of the equipment.
* **Reasoning (reasoning_input)**: Explain the reasoning or purpose behind the equipment's purpose.
* **Name Suggestion (name_suggestion_input)**: Suggest a name for the equipment.
