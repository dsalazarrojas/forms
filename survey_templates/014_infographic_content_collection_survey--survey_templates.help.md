# Infographic Content Collection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about infographics, including their status, target audience, deadline, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the infographic.
2. Describe the project in detail, including its purpose and goals.
3. Provide the name of the person or team responsible for designing the infographic.
4. Specify the type of infographic you are creating, such as Informational Graphic, Data Visualization, or Presentation Graphic.
5. Identify the target audience for the infographic.
6. Enter the location where the infographic will be displayed or used.
7. Enter the deadline for completing the infographic.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the project.
9. Enter the email address of the person responsible for the project.
10. Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the project.
11. Enter the website associated with the project.
12. Enter the name of the company or organization associated with the project.
13. Enter the location of the project.
14. Enter the job title of the person responsible for the project.
15. Enter the department or team responsible for the project.
16. Enter the role or position of the person responsible for the project.
17. Enter the name of the person who should be contacted about the project.
18. Enter the name of the team or group working on the project.
19. Enter the name of the project lead.
20. Select the current status of the project, such as In Progress, Completed, or On Hold.
21. Enter any comments or feedback about the project.
22. Select the person or team member assigned to the project.
23. Enter the date the project was created.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, text): Enter a brief title for the infographic.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the project.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the project.
* **Designer** (`designer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for designing the infographic.
* **Creative Direction** (`creative_direction`, text, optional): Enter the direction or style of the infographic.
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of infographic you are creating, such as Informational Graphic, Data Visualization, or Presentation Graphic.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): Enter the target audience for the infographic.
* **Content Location** (`content_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the infographic will be displayed or used.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, optional): Enter the deadline for completing the infographic.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the project.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the project.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the website associated with the project.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company or organization associated with the project.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the project.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the person responsible for the project.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department or team responsible for the project.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Enter the role or position of the person responsible for the project.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who should be contacted about the project.
* **Team** (`team`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team or group working on the project.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the project lead.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for leading the project.
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of the project, such as In Progress, Completed, or On Hold.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the project.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select the person or team member assigned to the project.
* **Created Date** (`created_date`, time, optional): Enter the date the project was created.
