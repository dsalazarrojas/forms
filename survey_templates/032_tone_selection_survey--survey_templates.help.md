# Tone Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to understand your perception of different tones in various contexts. This will help us in creating more relevant and effective content for your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each of the text boxes with your answer. You will be presented with a series of questions about your perception of different tones. Answer each question as accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tone Selection Survey Form** (`tone_selection_survey_form`, text, required): 
Please fill out a brief description of the tone you are perceiving. This will help us understand your context.
* **Tone Selection Survey Form Context 1** (`tone_selection_survey_form_context_1`, text, required): 
Describe the context in which you perceive this tone.
* **Tone Selection Survey Form Context 2** (`tone_selection_survey_form_context_2`, text, required): 
Describe the context in which you perceive this tone.
* ... (Context 3-10)
* **Tone Selection Survey Form Context 10** (`tone_selection_survey_form_context_10`, text, required): 
Describe the context in which you perceive this tone.

## Tips
Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible. This will help us in better understanding your preferences and needs. You can fill out this form at your own pace and can stop and save your progress at any time. If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
