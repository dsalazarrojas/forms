# Language Learning Styles Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language Learning Styles Survey is designed to gather information about your language learning preferences and styles to help develop more effective language learning strategies and materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the options that best fit your learning style and preferences.
3. Fill in any additional comments or suggestions you may have in the "Additional comments or suggestions?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome** (`introduction`, text, not required): This is a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Which learning styles do you use most frequently?** (`learning_styles`, select_one, not required):
	+ **Visual**: You prefer learning through images, diagrams, and videos.
	+ **Auditory**: You prefer learning through listening to lectures, podcasts, or discussions.
	+ **Kinesthetic**: You prefer learning through hands-on activities, experiments, and demonstrations.
	+ **Tactile**: You prefer learning through touch and hands-on activities.
	+ **None**: You do not have a preferred learning style.
* **Which teaching methods do you enjoy most?** (`teaching_methods`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ **Storytelling**: You enjoy learning through stories and narratives.
	+ **Videos**: You enjoy learning through videos and online tutorials.
	+ **Games**: You enjoy learning through interactive games and activities.
	+ **Textbooks**: You enjoy learning through traditional textbooks and reading.
* **Which teaching methods do you prefer?** (`teaching_preferences`, select_one, not required):
	+ **Fast-paced**: You prefer learning at a faster pace.
	+ **Self-paced**: You prefer learning at your own pace.
	+ **Collaborative**: You prefer learning in a group setting.
	+ **Individualized**: You prefer learning individually.
* **Additional comments or suggestions?** (`note`, note, not required): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address if you would like to receive any further updates or information related to this survey.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further information or clarification.
* **Time of day?** (`time`, time, not required): Choose your preferred time of day to be contacted for further information or clarification.
