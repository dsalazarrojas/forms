# Messages And Souvenir Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Messages And Souvenir Feedback" form is used to gather feedback on a souvenir item's presentation, story, and overall experience. This feedback will help us improve our souvenir products and provide a better experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions below. Each page has one field for you to fill out. Follow the instructions on each page for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Souvenir Name** (`souvenir_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the souvenir item you are providing feedback on.
  * This is the name of the souvenir that we will use to reference in our feedback and improvement processes.
  * Please enter the name of the souvenir as it is written on the item or as we have listed it on our website.
- **Your Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate the overall presentation and quality of the souvenir.
  * You will be able to select a number from 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
  * Please select the number that best represents your opinion of the souvenir.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about the souvenir.
  * This is your opportunity to share any extra information about your experience with the souvenir.
  * Be as detailed as you would like, but please keep it concise.
- **Story** (`story`, text, optional): Share a brief story about your experience with the souvenir.
  * This could be a personal story about how you used the souvenir, or a story about the souvenir's history or significance.
  * Keep it brief and relevant to the souvenir.
- **Photo** (`photo`, text, optional): Share a photo of the souvenir.
  * This could be a photo of the souvenir itself, or a photo of you with the souvenir.
  * Please ensure the photo is clear and legible.
- **Souvenir URL** (`souvenir_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the souvenir's online presence.
  * This could be a URL to the souvenir's product page on our website, or a URL to its social media page.
  * Please ensure the URL is correct and accessible.

## Tips
* Please be honest and clear in your feedback.
* Be as detailed as you would like, but keep it concise.
* If you have previously submitted feedback on this souvenir, do not submit again.
* If you have any other comments or questions, feel free to contact us.
