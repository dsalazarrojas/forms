# social_care_market_failure_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences and opinions on the social care market.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Answer all questions to the best of your ability, providing detailed and honest feedback.
3. Review and confirm your submission status before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`): Enter your name and any other relevant information that you think would be helpful for us to know about you.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`): Provide any demographic information that you think is relevant to your feedback, such as age, location, or occupation.
* **What Are Your Feedback?** (`survey_questions`): Share your thoughts and opinions on any aspect of the social care market that you think we should know about.
* **Which Resources?** (`community_resources`): Select all resources that you think are available or needed in the social care market.
* **Policy Effectiveness** (`policy_effectiveness`): Choose the option that best describes your opinion on the effectiveness of social care policies.
* **Gaps** (`gaps_and_gaps`): Select all gaps that you think exist in the social care market.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or thoughts that you think are relevant to your feedback.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter your phone number or email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Submission Info** (`submission_info`): This is a placeholder field and does not require an answer.
* **Submission Terms** (`submission_terms`): Choose the option that best describes your agreement to the submission terms.
* **Form Completion** (`form_completion`): Select all options that describe your completion status.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`): This is a placeholder field and does not require an answer.
* **Submission Acknowledgement** (`submission_acknowledgement`): Enter your email address to confirm your submission.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): Choose the option that best describes your submission status.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Select all dates that you think are relevant to your feedback.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`): This is a placeholder field and does not require an answer.
* **Submission Location** (`submission_location`): Enter the location where you submitted your feedback.
* **Submission Language** (`submission_language`): Choose the option that best describes the language of your submission.
* **Submission File** (`submission_file`): Select all files that you attached to your submission.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`): Provide any additional notes or comments about your submission.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`): Select all comments that you think are relevant to your feedback.
* **Submission Agreement** (`submission_agreement`): Choose the option that best describes your agreement to the submission terms.
* **Submission Consequences** (`submission_consequences`): Enter a number that describes the consequences of your feedback.
* **Submission Recommendations** (`submission_recommendations`): Provide any recommendations or suggestions on how to improve the social care market.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your feedback to help us improve the social care market.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always ask for clarification.
