# Non Profit Program Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather feedback from participants of non-profit programs to assess their effectiveness. It's used to gather information on various aspects of the program, including participant feedback, outcomes measured, program evaluation, and participant satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by carefully reading each question to understand what information is being asked.
2. For each question, provide a response that best answers the question.
3. If a question allows multiple responses, select all applicable options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Profit Program Effectiveness Survey** (`1`): This is the title of the survey. Please ignore this field as it's for internal reference only.
* **Program Description** (`2`): Provide a brief description of the program, including its goals and objectives.
* **Participant Feedback** (`3`): Share any feedback or comments you have about the program, such as what worked well or what didn't.
* **Outcomes Measured** (`4`): Describe the outcomes or results that were measured as a result of the program.
* **Program Evaluation** (`5`): Share your evaluation of the program, including its strengths and weaknesses.
* **Program Improvement** (`6`): Suggest areas for improvement for the program.
* **Participant Engagement** (`7`): Rate your level of engagement with the program, on a scale from 1-5.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`8`): Rate your level of satisfaction with the program, on a scale from 1-5.
* **Program Satisfaction** (`9`): Rate the overall satisfaction with the program, on a scale from 1-5.

* **Program Retention** (`11`): Rate the percentage of participants who were retained in the program, on a scale from 1-5.
