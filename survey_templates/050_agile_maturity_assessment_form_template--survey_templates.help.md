# Agile Maturity Assessment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the Agile maturity level of an organization. It helps to identify areas of improvement and provide recommendations for growth. The assessment covers various aspects such as Agile practices, leadership support, measurement maturity, team autonomy, cultural readiness for change, and suggested maturity level.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the organization's name and the period covered by the assessment (if applicable).
* Select all Agile practices that are currently being used by the organization.
* Choose the level of visible executive sponsorship, from Strong to Weak.
* Indicate the current level of measurement maturity, from Advanced to None.
* Assess the degree of team autonomy, from High to Low.
* Choose the readiness level for cultural change, from High to Low.
* Suggest a maturity level after assessment, if desired.
* Provide brief action plans for improvement and next steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Organization name
* **Organization name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization being assessed.

### Assessment period
* **Assessment period** (`assessment_period`, text, optional): Enter the dates covered by this assessment, if applicable.

### Agile practices
* **Agile practices in use** (`agile_practices`, select_multiple, required): Check all Agile practices currently being used by the organization:
	+ Scrum
	+ Kanban
	+ XP
	+ Lean
	+ DevOps

### Leadership support
* **Leadership support** (`leadership_support`, select_one, required): Choose the level of visible executive sponsorship:
	+ Strong
	+ Moderate
	+ Weak

### Measurement maturity
* **Measurement maturity** (`measurement_maturity`, select_one, required): Indicate the current level of measurement maturity:
	+ Advanced
	+ Established
	+ Developing
	+ None

### Team autonomy
* **Team autonomy level** (`team_autonomy`, select_one, required): Assess the degree of team autonomy:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low

### Cultural readiness for change
* **Cultural readiness for change** (`cultural_readiness`, select_one, required): Choose the readiness level for cultural change:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low

### Suggested maturity level
* **Suggested maturity level** (`maturity_level`, select_one, optional): Suggest a maturity level after assessment, if desired:
	+ Level 1
	+ Level 2
	+ Level 3
	+ Level 4
	+ Level 5

### Recommendations and next steps
* **Recommendations and next steps** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide brief action plans for improvement and next steps.
