# Casual Conversion Policy Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Casual Conversion Policy Survey is designed to gather feedback and insights from employees who have undergone the casual to permanent conversion process. The survey aims to understand their experiences, awareness, and perceptions of the policy, as well as identify areas for improvement to enhance the conversion process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question in the survey as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. For questions with options, select the one that best represents your experience or opinion.
3. Provide detailed answers in the text fields, if applicable.
4. If you have any suggestions or comments, feel free to add them in the "Improvement Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department**: Select the department you belong to, which will help us understand the conversion process across different teams.
	+ Human Resources
	+ Operations
	+ Sales and Marketing
	+ Finance
	+ IT and Engineering
	+ Customer Support
* **Length of Service with the Company**: Choose the duration of your employment with the company, which will help us understand your conversion experience in different periods.
	+ Less than 6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 years
	+ More than 2 years
* **Policy Awareness**: Indicate your level of awareness about the casual-to-permanent conversion policy.
	+ Yes, very familiar
	+ Yes, somewhat aware
	+ No, not at all
* **How did you first learn about this policy**: Select the source where you got information about the policy, which will help us understand how the information is disseminated.
	+ Employee Handbook
	+ HR Information Session
	+ Direct Manager
	+ Company Intranet
	+ Colleague
* **Have you ever requested or been offered a conversion**: Share your experience with the casual-to-permanent conversion, including whether you were offered and accepted or declined.
	+ Yes, I was offered and accepted
	+ Yes, I was offered and declined
	+ Yes, I requested it
	+ No, not yet
* **Rate the ease of the conversion process**: Evaluate the ease or difficulty you experienced during the conversion process, on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1 - Very Difficult
	+ 2
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4
	+ 5 - Very Easy
* **Was the information provided during the process clear**: Share your perception of the clarity of information provided during the conversion process.
	+ Yes, completely
	+ Mostly clear
	+ Somewhat confusing
	+ No, very unclear
* **Approximately how long did the conversion process take**: Provide an estimate of the time it took to finalize the conversion.
	+ Less than 1 week
	+ 1-2 weeks
	+ 2-4 weeks
	+ More than 1 month
* **Did you encounter any barriers during the process**: If yes, describe any issues or delays you faced.
* **How can we improve the casual conversion process**: Share your suggestions on how to enhance the conversion process.
* **If not converted, are you interested in a permanent role**: Indicate your interest in taking on a permanent role if not already converted.
	+ Yes, very interested
	+ No, I prefer casual work
	+ Not sure at this time

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the relevant department or the form creator.
* Your feedback and suggestions are valuable to us, and we appreciate your contribution to the improvement of our conversion process.
