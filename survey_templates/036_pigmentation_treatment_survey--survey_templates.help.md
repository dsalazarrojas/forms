# Pigmentation Treatment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and expectations related to pigmentation treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
3. If you have any medical concerns or previous treatments, please describe them in as much detail as possible.
4. Indicate how often your concerns occur, and how long they last.
5. Answer all questions, even if they seem unrelated or repetitive.
