# Character Stats Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Character Stats Tracker Form is a comprehensive tool to track and record your character's progress in a campaign or game. It helps you keep track of their attributes, stats, and progress, providing a clear and organized record of your character's evolution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Section 1: Basic Stats and Information**
	* Enter your **Player Name**: This is your in-game name or the name of the character you're tracking.
	* Enter the **Character Name**: This is the name of the character you're tracking.
	* Choose **Character Class or Profession**: Select the class or profession of your character, such as Fighter, Rogue, or Wizard.
	* Enter the **Current Character Level**: Update the level of your character as of this update.
2. **Section 2: Core Attributes and Stats**
	* Enter the **Strength Attribute Score**: Record the current value of your character's strength attribute.
	* Enter the **Dexterity Attribute Score**: Record the current value of your character's dexterity attribute.
	* Enter the **Constitution Attribute Score**: Record the current value of your character's constitution attribute.
	* Enter the **Intelligence Attribute Score**: Record the current value of your character's intelligence attribute.
	* Enter the **Wisdom Attribute Score**: Record the current value of your character's wisdom attribute.
	* Enter the **Charisma Attribute Score**: Record the current value of your character's charisma attribute.
3. **Section 3: Combat and Health Update**
	* Enter the **Maximum Hit Points (HP)**: Record your character's total health points.
	* Enter the **Current Hit Points (HP)**: Record the current health points of your character.
	* Enter the **Armor Class (AC) Value**: Record the defensive capability of your character.
4. **Section 4: XP and Wealth Update**
	* Enter the **Total Experience Points (XP)**: Record the cumulative experience points earned by your character.
	* Enter the **Gold and Currency Total Update**: Record the cumulative wealth of your character.
5. **Finalization**
	* Confirm the accuracy of your logged stats by selecting **I Confirm and Log Stats**.
	* Choose the **Date of Update**: Select today's date for the update.
	* Optionally, add **Additional Stats Notes** for any context or information not covered in the form.
	* Check if you are ready to **Level-Up Pending** and select the corresponding option.

This guide will help you complete the form accurately and ensure that your character's stats are up-to-date and organized for further tracking and referencing.
