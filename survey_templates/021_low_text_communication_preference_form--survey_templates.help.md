# Low Text Communication Preference Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Low Text Communication Preference Form is designed to collect information about individual users' communication preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Input**: Enter your name or ID to identify yourself.
2. **Communication Preferences**: Select how you would like to be contacted: Email, Phone, or Both.
3. **Preferred Communication Channel**: Choose your preferred communication channel(s): Email, Phone, or Both.
4. **Preferred Communication Time**: Select your preferred time of day for communication: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
5. **Additional Notes**: Type any additional information or notes you'd like to share with us.
6. **Email**: Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted via email).
7. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted via phone).
8. **Date**: Select your preferred date for communication (if applicable).
9. **Time**: Select your preferred time for communication (if applicable).
10. **Custom Fields**: Enter any custom information in the dedicated fields (Custom 1-16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: Enter your name or ID to identify yourself.
* **Communication Preferences**: This field allows you to select how you would like to be contacted, such as Email, Phone, or Both.
	+ Select Email to receive communication through email.
	+ Select Phone to receive communication through phone calls.
	+ Select Both to receive communication through both email and phone.
* **Preferred Communication Channel**: Choose the channel(s) through which you would like to receive communication, such as Email, Phone, or Both.
	+ Select Email to prefer communication through email.
	+ Select Phone to prefer communication through phone.
	+ Select Both to prefer communication through both email and phone.
* **Preferred Communication Time**: Select your preferred time of day for communication, such as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
	+ Select Morning to prefer communication during the morning.
	+ Select Afternoon to prefer communication during the afternoon.
	+ Select Evening to prefer communication during the evening.
* **Additional Notes**: Type any additional information or notes you'd like to share with us.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted via email.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone.
* **Date**: Select your preferred date for communication (if applicable).
* **Time**: Select your preferred time for communication (if applicable).
* **Custom Fields 1-16**: Enter any custom information in these dedicated fields.

## Tips

* Ensure you've filled out the form only once for each user.
* Use the same user ID or handle for updating information.
* This form is designed for individual users, not for group or company-wide communications.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to this help guide or reach out to your administrator.
