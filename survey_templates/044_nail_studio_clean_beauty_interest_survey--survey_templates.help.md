# Nail Studio Clean Beauty Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your interests in nail-related topics. Your responses will help us understand your preferences and behaviors in the beauty industry.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this survey, navigate through each page and select the options that best represent your interests.
- You can select multiple options for "Nail Type" and "Nail Art" fields.
- Make sure to fill out every field, as they are all optional.
- Use the "Submit" button to send your responses once you have completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Nail Type** (`Nail Type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or multiple nail types that interest you, such as Gel, Acrylic, or Nail Polish.
  - This field is meant to gauge your interest in different types of nail treatments.
  - You can select as many options as apply to you.
- **Nail Shape** (`Nail Shape`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the shape of nails that interest you the most.
  - This field is intended to understand your preference for specific nail shapes.
  - Choose the option that best describes your preferred nail shape.
- **Nail Color** (`Nail Color`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the nail color that you are most interested in.
  - This field is designed to gather information on your preferred nail color.
  - Choose the option that best represents your favorite nail color.
- **Nail Polish Name** (`Nail Polish Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your favorite nail polish.
  - This field is meant to collect information on the name of specific nail polish you like.
  - Type in the exact name of the polish if you have one in mind.
- **Nail Polish Brand** (`Nail Polish Brand`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the brand of your favorite nail polish.
  - This field is intended to understand your preference for specific nail polish brands.
  - Choose the option that best represents your favorite brand.
- **Nail Art** (`Nail Art`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of nail art that interests you the most.
  - This field is designed to gather information on your interest in different types of nail art.
  - Choose the option that best describes your preferred type of nail art.
- **Nail Art Name** (`Nail Art Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your favorite nail art design.
  - This field is meant to collect information on the name of specific nail art designs you like.
  - Type in the exact name of the design if you have one in mind.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer honestly and accurately, as your responses will help us better understand your interests in the beauty industry.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
