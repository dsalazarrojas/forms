# Data Driven Care Coordination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand how care coordination teams use data in their work and gather feedback on how to improve data-driven care coordination practices. Your input will help us identify areas of strength and areas for improvement in data usage and care coordination processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Read the survey questions carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you if needed.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" section.
5. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Introduction** (`survey_intro`, `note`, not required): This is an introductory note to provide context to the survey.
* **Questions and Challenges** (`questions_and_challenges`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	+ Select all that apply to indicate the areas you face challenges with data usage in care coordination.
* **How do you use data in care coordination?** (`data_usage`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ Choose one option that best describes how you use data in care coordination.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, not required):
	+ Type any suggestions you have for improving data usage and care coordination processes.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	+ Select all that apply to indicate the next steps you would take to improve data usage and care coordination.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required):
	+ Share any additional thoughts or comments you have about data-driven care coordination.
* **Staff Involvement** (`staff_involvement`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ Choose one option that best describes the involvement of staff in data-driven care coordination.
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ Choose one option that best describes how often you provide feedback on data-driven care coordination.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required):
	+ Enter your name and contact information so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, not required): This is a submit button. Clicking on this will complete your survey.
* **Survey Conclusion** (`survey_conclusion`, `note`, not required): This is a concluding note to finalize your survey.

Note: Please keep in mind that this survey is completely anonymous and for research purposes only. We do not collect any identifiable information, so your responses will help us improve data-driven care coordination without compromising your or your organization's confidentiality.
