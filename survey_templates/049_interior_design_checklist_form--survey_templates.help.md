# Interior Design Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Interior Design Checklist Form is a tool used to collect information and preferences from clients for interior design projects. This form helps designers understand the client's project requirements, communication preferences, and design goals, ensuring a clear understanding of the project's scope and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the project title and ensure it accurately represents the project.
2. Provide an overview of your project preferences, including any specific requirements or needs.
3. Describe your project brief, focusing on the design goals and objectives.
4. Specify your budget for the project, including any budget range if applicable.
5. Select your primary and secondary design goals and objectives.
6. Indicate your preferred communication method (e.g., email, phone, in-person, virtual meetings).
7. Select your preferred meeting frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
8. Choose the type of meeting you would like to have (e.g., initial meeting, design meeting, construction meeting).
9. Select the design deliverables you require (e.g., 2D design, 3D design, design report, etc.).
10. If you have any other design deliverables not listed, please specify.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Preferences (Text, optional)**: Please provide a brief overview of your project's specific requirements or needs.
* **Project Brief (Note, optional)**: Describe your project's design goals and objectives in detail.
* **Budget (Number, optional)**: Enter your project budget, including any range if applicable (e.g., 1000-5000).
* **Budget Range Start/End (Numbers, optional)**: If you have a budget range, enter the start and end values.
* **Design Goals (Select multiple, optional)**: Select all the design goals and objectives that apply to your project:
	+ Home Staging
	+ Interior Design
	+ Architecture
	+ Furniture Selection
	+ Space Planning
	+ Lighting Design
	+ Color Scheme
* **Other Design Goals (Text, optional)**: If you have any other design goals not listed above, please describe them here.
* **Communication Plan (Select multiple, optional)**: Select your preferred communication methods:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ In Person
	+ Virtual Meetings
* **Communication Preference (Select one, optional)**: Indicate if you would like to communicate with your designer directly or not.
* **Meeting Frequency (Select one, optional)**: Select how often you would like to meet with your designer:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Biweekly
	+ Monthly
* **Meeting Type (Select one, optional)**: Select the type of meeting you would like to have:
	+ Initial Meeting
	+ Design Meeting
	+ Construction Meeting
	+ Final Walk-through Meeting
* **Design Deliverables (Select multiple, optional)**: Select the design deliverables you require:
	+ 2D Design
	+ 3D Design
	+ Design Report
	+ Design Presentation
	+ Renderings
	+ CAD Files
* **Other Design Deliverables (Text, optional)**: If you have any other design deliverables not listed above, please describe them here.
