# qsr_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a restaurant experience survey designed to collect information about a user's dining experience.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Provide Restaurant Information
- Click on the "Restaurant Name" field and type in the name of the restaurant where you had your experience.
- Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.

### Step 2: Share Your Feedback
- Type your feedback and observations about your visit in the "Feedback" field.
- If you have additional observations, you can add them in "Observation 2", "Observation 3", and "Observation 4" fields.

### Step 3: Choose Your Location
- Select the correct location of the restaurant from the options in "Restaurant Location" field.

### Step 4: Date and Time
- Enter the date and time of your visit in the "Date" and "Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Restaurant Name**: Enter the name of the restaurant where you had your experience. This will help us identify the restaurant you visited.
*   **Email**: Enter your Email address so we can contact you for further information. Please note that your Email address will not be shared with the restaurant.
*   **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions about your visit. This will help us understand your experience and improve our services.
*   **Observation 2**: If you have any additional observations or comments, you can add them here.
*   **Observation 3**: Another opportunity to add more observations or comments.
*   **Feedback 2**: Add any additional feedback you may have.
*   **Observation 4**: More space to add any further comments or thoughts.
*   **Restaurant Location**: Please select the location of the restaurant from the provided options (e.g., Restaurant A, Restaurant B, Restaurant C).
*   **Date**: Enter the date of your visit.
*   **Time**: Enter the time of your visit.

## Tips

*   Please be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out this form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
*   Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our services, so thank you for taking the time to fill out this form!
