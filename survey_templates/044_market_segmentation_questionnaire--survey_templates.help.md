# Market Segmentation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Segmentation Questionnaire is a tool used to help businesses identify and segment their target market. It is designed to gather information about the preferences, behaviors, and needs of potential customers to help inform product and market strategies. This questionnaire is crucial for understanding who your ideal customer is, what their needs are, and how to tailor your products and services to meet those needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. On each page of the form, select the corresponding response from the dropdown options or type in the text fields.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label next to each field.
3. Once you have completed the form, submit it to generate your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Segment** (select_one)
*   Segment: Select one of the available options, either "Yes" or "No".
    * This field helps us understand whether you are interested in segmenting your market.
### 2. **Audience Insights** (select_multiple)
*   Audience Insights: Select all applicable options from the list, either "Yes" or "No".
    * This field helps us understand what insights about your audience you have already gathered.
### 3. **Preferences** (text)
*   Preferences: Enter any relevant information about your customer's preferences.
    * This field allows you to specify any specific preferences your customers have.
### 4. **Customer Segmentation** (select_one)
*   Customer Segmentation: Select one of the available options, either "Yes" or "No".
    * This field helps us understand whether you have customer segmentation in place.
### 5. **Data Collection** (select_multiple)
*   Data Collection: Select all applicable options from the list, either "Yes" or "No".
    * This field helps us understand how you collect data about your customers.
### 6. **Product Decisions** (text)
*   Product Decisions: Enter any information regarding product decisions that have been made.
    * This field allows you to specify any product-related decisions made based on customer preferences.
### 7. **Brand Decisions** (text)
*   Brand Decisions: Enter any information regarding brand decisions that have been made.
    * This field allows you to specify any brand-related decisions made based on customer preferences.
### 8. **Campaign Decisions** (text)
*   Campaign Decisions: Enter any information regarding campaign decisions that have been made.
    * This field allows you to specify any campaign-related decisions made based on customer preferences.

## Tips
*   Ensure you have a clear understanding of your target market before completing this form.
*   Be as specific as possible when answering each field, and avoid vague or general responses.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
