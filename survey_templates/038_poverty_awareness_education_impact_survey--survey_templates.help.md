# Poverty Awareness Education Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the impact of poverty awareness education sessions. It aims to evaluate the quality of sessions, the attendees' knowledge gains, behavior intentions, and attendance numbers. The form's purpose is to collect data on the effectiveness of these sessions, which can help inform future improvements and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For the "Session Quality" section, select a rating from 1 (Poor) to 4 (Very Good) based on your experience.
3. For the "Knowledge Gains" section, select the answer that best describes the attendees' understanding after the session.
4. For the "Behavior Intentions" section, select all the answers that best describe the attendees' intentions after the session.
5. In the "Session Attendance" section, enter the number of attendees.
6. In the "Total Attendance" section, enter the total number of attendees for all sessions.
7. In the "Total Number of Sessions" section, enter the total number of sessions conducted.
8. In the "Date" section, select the date of the session.
9. In the "Time" section, select the time of the session.
10. In the "Notes" section, enter any additional comments or observations about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Poverty Awareness Education Impact Survey Form** (Page 1):
  This page serves as an introduction to the survey.
- **Session Quality** (Page 2):
  This question asks for your evaluation of the session's quality, from 1 (Poor) to 4 (Very Good).
  1. Poor: The session was not well-structured or engaging.
  2. Fair: The session was average, but could be improved.
  3. Good: The session was well-structured and engaging.
  4. Very Good: The session was exceptional and met all its objectives.
- **Knowledge Gains** (Page 3):
  This question asks for your evaluation of the attendees' knowledge gains after the session.
  Select the answer that best describes the attendees' understanding:
  1. Very Good: The attendees gained a high level of understanding.
  2. Good: The attendees gained a moderate level of understanding.
  3. Fair: The attendees gained a low level of understanding.
  4. Poor: The attendees gained no understanding.
- **Behavior Intentions** (Page 4):
  This question asks for your evaluation of the attendees' behavior intentions after the session.
  Select all the answers that best describe the attendees' intentions:
  1. Very Good: The attendees are likely to implement the changes discussed in the session.
  2. Good: The attendees are somewhat likely to implement the changes discussed in the session.
  3. Fair: The attendees are not likely to implement the changes discussed in the session.
  4. Poor: The attendees are not likely to implement the changes discussed in the session.
- **Session Attendance** (Page 5):
  This question asks for the number of attendees who participated in the session.
  Enter the number of attendees who participated.
- **Total Attendance** (Page 6):
  This question asks for the total number of attendees for all sessions.
  Enter the total number of attendees.
- **Total Number of Sessions** (Page 7):
  This question asks for the total number of sessions conducted.
  Enter the total number of sessions conducted.
- **Date** (Page 8):
  This question asks for the date of the session.
  Select the date of the session.
- **Time** (Page 9):
  This question asks for the time of the session.
  Select the time of the session.
- **Notes** (Page 10):
  This question asks for any additional comments or observations about the session.
  Enter any comments or observations about the session.
- **Email** (Page 11):
  This question asks for your email address.
  Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, consult with the session facilitator or educator.
- The information collected will be used to improve future poverty awareness education sessions.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, feel free to share them with us.
