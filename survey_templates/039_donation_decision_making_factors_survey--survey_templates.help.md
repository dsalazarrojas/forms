# Donation Decision Making Factors Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donation Decision Making Factors Survey is a tool designed to help organizations understand what drives donors' decisions when considering making a donation. It aims to gather information on various factors that influence donor behavior, including donor type, age, motivation, barriers, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Please select the correct options for each question based on your actual experience and preference.
3. Be as detailed as possible when providing information in the text fields (e.g., motivation, barriers, and note).
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or skip it.
5. Ensure that you have filled in all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) before submitting your survey response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Donation Decision Making Factors Survey** (text field, 1): A brief introduction to this survey.
- **Donor Type** (select one, 2): Indicate whether you are an individual or an organization making the donation.
  \* If you are an individual, select 'Yes'. If you are an organization, select 'No'.
- **Donor Age** (number, 3): Your age.
- **Motivation** (text field, 4): Briefly describe the reasons why you decided to make a donation. (e.g., charitable cause, social responsibility, tax benefits, etc.)
- **Barriers** (text field, 5): If you have encountered any obstacles or challenges when making a donation, please describe them here.
- **Preference** (select multiple, 6): Indicate your preferred methods of giving:
  \* 'Yes' for Online, 'No' for Offline (bank transfer, mail, etc.)
- **Frequency** (select one, 7): How often do you make donations:
  \* 'Yes' for regularly (at least once a month), 'No' for rarely or never.
- **Amount** (number, 8): The amount you typically donate.
- **Impact** (text field, 9): How do you feel that your donations impact the charity or non-profit organization? (e.g., immediate impact, long-term benefits, etc.)
- **Confidence** (number, 10): On a scale of 0 to 10, how confident are you that the charity or non-profit organization will use your donation effectively? 
- **Note** (note, 11): Additional information or comments about your donation experience (e.g., positive or negative interactions with the organization).
- **Email** (email, 12): Your email address (if you would like to be contacted for follow-up or updates).
- **Phone** (text, 13): Your phone number (if you would like to be contacted for follow-up or updates).
- **Time** (time, 14): The time of day you are most likely to make a donation (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
- **Date** (date, 15): Your birthday (not required but can be used for data analysis).
- **Submit Your Response** (text field, 16): A summary of your answers.
- **Email Confirmation** (email, 17): Your email address for confirmation.
- **Phone Confirmation** (text, 18): Your phone number for confirmation.
- **Time Confirmation** (time, 19): The time of day for confirmation.
- **Date Confirmation** (date, 20): Your birthday for confirmation.
- **Note Confirmation** (note, 21): Additional comments or information for confirmation.
- **Email Verification** (email, 22): Your email address for verification.
- **Phone Verification** (text, 23): Your phone number for verification.
- **Time Verification** (time, 24): The time of day for verification.
- **Note Verification** (note, 25): Additional comments or information for verification.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or skip it.
* Ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting your survey response.
* You can ask the survey administrator or help desk if you need any assistance or have any questions.
