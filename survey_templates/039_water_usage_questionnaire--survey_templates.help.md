# Water Usage Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your water usage habits and practices. It will help us understand your water consumption patterns, source, and conservation efforts, which will aid in identifying areas for improvement and optimizing water resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide accurate and helpful information, please answer the questions in this form as accurately and thoroughly as possible. You can select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections, and use the text field for any additional comments or insights you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of building do you live or work in?** (`house_type`, select_multiple): This question helps us understand the type of building you are in, which can affect water usage patterns. Please select all that apply, including Single family residence, Apartment complex, Commercial building, or Other (please specify).
* **How many people live in your household?** (`household_size`, number): This question helps us estimate the average water usage per person in your household.
* **What is your household income (in USD)?** (`household_income`, number): This question can help us understand the potential impact of income on water usage habits and expenses.
* **Where is your water tap/well/meter located?** (`usage_location`, select_one): This question helps us understand the location of your water source, which can influence water usage patterns. Please select one option, such as Front yard, Backyard, Kitchen, Bathroom, or Other (please specify).
* **Where is your water source?** (`water_source`, select_multiple): This question helps us identify the source of your water supply. Please select all that apply, including Public tap, Private well or borehole, Wellpoint or rainwater collection system, or Other (please specify).
* **How often do you use water?** (`water_usage_frequency`, select_multiple): This question helps us understand your water usage habits. Please select all that apply, including Daily, Several times a week, A few times a month, Rarely, or Never.
* **What water conservation practices have you implemented in your household?** (`conservation_practices`, select_multiple): This question helps us identify the measures you take to conserve water. Please select all that apply, including Low-flow showerhead, Low-flow faucet, Dual-flush toilet, Greywater use, or Other (please specify).
* **What insights do you have about water usage in your household?** (`water_usage_insights`, text): This question is an opportunity to provide any additional comments or insights you may have about your water usage habits and experiences.
* **Do you have any additional comments or questions?** (`additional_comments`, note): This question is a chance to provide any additional information or ask questions about your water usage or this form.
