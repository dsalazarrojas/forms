# Agricultural Land Use Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about agricultural land use, specifically for surveys conducted by a surveyor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the surveyor conducting the survey in the `Surveyor Name` field.
2. Enter the date of the observation in the `Survey Date` field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Select the location or region of the land area being surveyed in the `Location or Region` field.
4. Enter the total size of the surveyed area in the `Total Acreage` field.
5. Select the primary land use of the area in the `Primary Land Use` field.
6. Choose the primary irrigation method used for the land in the `Irrigation Method` field.
7. Choose the dominant soil type of the land in the `Soil Type` field.
8. Select all relevant conservation practices observed in the `Conservation Practices` field, if applicable.
9. Enter any key challenges or issues affecting the land use in the `Challenges` field.
10. Enter any future plans for the land use in the next 5 years in the `Future Plans` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Surveyor Name**: Enter your name as the surveyor conducting the survey.
* **Survey Date**: Enter the date of the observation in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Location or Region**: Enter the county, district, or GPS coordinates of the land area being surveyed.
* **Total Acreage**: Enter the total size of the surveyed area.
* **Primary Land Use**: Choose the dominant activity or use of the land, e.g., Row Crops, Orchards/Vineyards, etc.
* **Irrigation Method**: Choose the primary method of water source used for irrigation, e.g., None (Rainfed), Drip, Sprinkler/Center Pivot, etc.
* **Soil Type**: Choose the dominant soil texture of the land, e.g., Sandy, Clay, Loam, etc.
* **Conservation Practices**: Select all relevant conservation practices observed, e.g., Cover Crops, No-Till, Buffer Strips, etc.
* **Challenges**: Enter any key challenges or issues affecting the land use.
* **Future Plans**: Enter any expected changes in land use in the next 5 years.
