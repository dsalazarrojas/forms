# Chamber Of Commerce Award Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit votes for different categories of awards. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure your nominations are counted.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Vote Date**: Select the date of your vote submission. This is a required field.
2. **Voter Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID or passport. This is a required field.
3. **Email Address**: Enter your email address for verification purposes. This is a required field.
4. **Phone Number**: If you have a phone number, please enter it for contact purposes. This is an optional field.
5. **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business or individual you are nominating for each category. This is a required field.
6. **Business Address**: Enter the full address of the business or individual you are nominating. This is a required field.
7. **Chamber Membership Status**: Select your current membership status with the Chamber of Commerce. This is a required field.
8. **Membership ID Number**: If you are a member, enter your Chamber membership ID number. This is an optional field.
9. **Years as Member**: Select how long you have been a member of the Chamber of Commerce. This is an optional field.
10. **Award Categories**:
	* **Award Category 1**: Select a nominee for the first category. This is a required field.
	* **Nominee for Category 1**: Enter the name of the business or individual you selected as the nominee for Category 1. This is a required field.
	* **Award Category 2** (if available): Select a nominee for the second category. This is an optional field.
	* **Nominee for Category 2** (if available): Enter the name of the business or individual you selected as the nominee for Category 2. This is an optional field.
	* **Award Category 3** (if available): Select a nominee for the third category. This is an optional field.
	* **Nominee for Category 3** (if available): Enter the name of the business or individual you selected as the nominee for Category 3. This is an optional field.
	* **Write-in Category**: If the category you wish to nominate for is not listed, enter the category name and the name of your nominee. This is an optional field.
	* **Write-in Nominee**: Enter the name of your write-in nominee. This is an optional field.
11. **Nomination Reason**: Explain why you are nominating this business or individual. This is an optional field.
12. **Voter Eligibility**: Confirm that you are eligible to vote. This is a required field.
13. **One Vote Per Category**: Confirm that you are voting only once per category. This is a required field.
14. **Voter Signature**: Sign your name as the voter. This is a required field.
15. **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your vote submission. This is a required field.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YY).
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the Chamber of Commerce staff.

Note: This help guide is a condensed version of the form's YAML fields. Please review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field and its purpose before submitting your vote.
