# Job Fair Organization Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on your experience at the job fair event. Please provide your honest feedback on the event's organization and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer the questions according to your actual experience at the job fair event.
2.  Fill in the blanks with the requested information and select the most suitable options.
3.  For open-ended questions, provide detailed and constructive feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Header**: This is a header section that introduces the form and explains its purpose.
* **Event Name**: Please enter the name of the job fair event you attended. If you're unsure, check the event's promotional materials or contact the event organizers.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Event Time**: Enter the start and end time of the event.
* **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the event, if applicable.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you attended (Yes/No). This will help us understand the type of event that took place.
* **Event Category**: Select all categories that apply to the event (Yes/No). This will give us an idea of the different types of events held at the location.
* **Event Organizer**: Select all organizations that organized the event (Yes/No). This will help us identify who was responsible for the event's organization.
* **Event Location**: Select the location where the event was held (Yes/No). This will give us an idea of the venue's suitability for future events.
* **Event Setup**: Select all aspects of the event's setup that were satisfactory or problematic (Yes/No). This will help us understand the event's overall organization.
* **Feedback Participants**: Select all aspects of participant feedback that were satisfactory or problematic (Yes/No). This will help us gauge participant satisfaction.
* **Feedback Exhibitors**: Select all aspects of exhibitor feedback that were satisfactory or problematic (Yes/No). This will help us gauge exhibitor satisfaction.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event, out of 5 stars.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Enter any suggestions you have for improving the event in this text field.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about your experience at the event.

Please provide your feedback as truthfully as possible. Your input will help us improve future job fair events.
