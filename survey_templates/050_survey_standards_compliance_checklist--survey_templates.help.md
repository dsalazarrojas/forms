# Survey Standards Compliance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survey Standards Compliance Checklist is a form designed to help ensure that your projects adhere to the standards set by your organization. It's essential to complete this form accurately to receive credit for your project and demonstrate your compliance with these standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct project phase from the options provided.
2. Indicate whether the current project document exceptions apply or not.
3. Enter the date of the exception if applicable.
4. Select the status of the project leader, team members, and team lead.
5. Select the status of the project.
6. Choose the standard awareness status.
7. Indicate the status of the project documentation.
8. Select the status of the project phase.
9. Select the standard compliance status for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Phase** (`project_phase`, text, required): Describe the current phase of your project.
- **Document Exception** (`document_exception`, select_multiple, required): If the project documents are not up-to-date or accurate, select "Yes" to indicate that exceptions apply. If they are up-to-date and accurate, select "No".
- **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, date, required): Enter the date of the latest data collection.
- **Project Leader** (`project_leader`, text, optional): Enter the name of the project leader.
- **Team Member** (`team_member`, text, optional): Enter the names of team members involved in the project.
- **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team lead.
- **Team Member 2** (`team_member_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of another team member involved in the project.
- **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the project manager.
- **Survey Coordinator** (`survey_coordinator`, text, optional): Enter the name of the survey coordinator.
- **Other** (`other`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant details about the project.
- **Exception Date** (`exception_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the exception if "Document Exception" is selected as "Yes".
- **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of the project from the options provided: "Active" or "Inactive".
- **Team Lead Status** (`team_lead_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of the team lead from the options provided: "Active" or "Inactive".
- **Project Lead Status** (`project_lead_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of the project lead from the options provided: "Active" or "Inactive".
- **Team Member Status** (`team_member_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of team members from the options provided: "Active" or "Inactive".
- **Standard Awareness** (`standard_awareness`, note, required): Enter a note explaining the standard awareness level of the project team.
- **Documentation Status** (`documentation_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of the project documentation from the options provided: "Active" or "Inactive".
- **Project Phase Status** (`project_phase_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of the project phase from the options provided: "Active" or "Inactive".
- **Standard Compliance** (`standard_compliance`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of standard compliance from the options provided: "Yes" or "No".
