# Customer Tip Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on their tip experiences, helping businesses understand their customers' satisfaction levels, tip habits, and perceptions of staff behavior. Your responses will help us improve our services and make your experience better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name and contact information (if you choose to provide it).
2. Answer questions about your satisfaction level, tip amount, and tip frequency.
3. Rate your experience with our staff, including their friendliness, professionalism, and quality of service.
4. Provide feedback on our tip policies, including whether you always, sometimes, or never follow them.
5. If you've completed the survey within a certain period, tell us about it (e.g., "just now," "5 minutes ago").
6. Optionally, if you have more time to spare, feel free to add any additional comments or details about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (Customer Name)**: Please enter your name as a customer.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number)**: If you're comfortable sharing your contact number, enter it here. This is optional.
* **Email Address (Email Address)**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or for us to send you updates, enter your email address.
* **Satisfaction Score (Satisfaction Score)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with a number between 1 and 5 (1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - 5). A higher score means a more positive experience.
* **Tip Amount (Tip Amount)**: If you've received a tip, enter the amount you gave.
* **Tip Frequency (Tip Frequency)**: Choose how often you give tips:
	+ **Never**: You've never given a tip in this location.
	+ **Occasionally**: You give tips occasionally.
	+ **Regularly**: You give tips regularly.
* **Service Quality Score (Service Quality Score)**: Rate the quality of service you received with a number between 1 and 5 (1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - 5). A higher score means better service.
* **Staff Friendliness (Staff Friendliness)**: Rate how friendly the staff were with a number between 1 and 5 (1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - 5). A higher score means more friendly staff.
* **Staff Professionalism (Staff Professionalism)**: Rate how professional the staff were with a number between 1 and 5 (1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - 5). A higher score means more professional staff.
* **Tip Policies (Tip Policies)**: Choose how you adhere to our tip policies:
	+ **Always**
	+ **Sometimes**
	+ **Never**
* **Tip Frequency Policies (Tip Frequency Policies)**: Choose when you follow our tip frequency policies:
	+ **Daily**
	+ **Weekly**
	+ **Monthly**
	+ **Other** (If you select "Other," please specify in the comments below.)
* **Survey Completion Time (Survey Completion Time)**: If you're willing, tell us when you completed the survey. This is optional.
* **Additional Comments (Survey Completion Time)**: If you'd like to add any additional comments or details, you can do so here.
