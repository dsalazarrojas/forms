# Personal Styling And Fitting Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client information for styling and fitting purposes. Please fill out this form to provide detailed information about the client, including their contact and measurement details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Click on the 'Submit' button to open the form in a new tab.
*   Scroll through the form pages using the 'Prev' and 'Next' buttons to access each field.
*   Fill in the requested information for each field, making sure to select relevant options for fields with dropdown menus.
*   Click on the 'Submit' button when you have filled out all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information**: This section is for general details about the client, including their name, email, and other personal details.
    *   **Client Name**: Enter the client's full name.
    *   **Email**: Enter the client's email address.
*   **Communication Preferences**: This field allows you to select how you would like to communicate with the client (Mobile, Landline, or Work).
    *   **Select one or more communication methods**: Choose one or more ways you would like to contact the client.
*   **Address Details**: Enter the client's address for styling and fitting purposes.
    *   **Address**: Enter the client's full address.
*   **Location**: Enter the client's city or town.
*   **State**: Select the state or region where the client is located (NSW, QLD, VIC, or Other).
*   **Zip/Postcode**: Enter the client's postcode.
*   **Postcode Area**: Enter the postal code area for the client's location.
*   **Measurement Information**: Enter any relevant measurement details.
*   **Style Information**: Enter any additional styling information for the client.
