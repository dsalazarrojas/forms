# market_research_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a market research survey designed to collect information about customers and their market segments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. If applicable, enter the customer's contact number.
3. Choose the relevant target market segment (Commercial, Residential, or Industrial).
4. Enter the customer's email address.
5. If necessary, add a note or comment in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. Optionally, select a date for the survey in the "Survey Date" field.
7. If required, select a time of day for the survey in the "Time of Day" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the customer's contact number (e.g., phone number or mobile number).
* **Target Market Segment** (`market_segment`, select_multiple, required): Choose the relevant market segment for your business from the options provided (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the survey (if required).
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): Select the time of day for the survey (if required).
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the survey.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (Target Market Segment, Survey Date, Time of Day) accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact the relevant authorities.
