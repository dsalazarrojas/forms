# podcast_topic_suggestions - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects podcast topic suggestions for new episodes or discussions. Please submit your ideas to help us create engaging content for our audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Select one of the suggested types for your idea (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. Provide a clear and concise title for your suggestion.
4. Describe your idea in detail, focusing on the main points and key points.
5. Select relevant categories for your suggestion, using one or more of the options provided.
6. Choose keywords related to your suggestion, again using one or more of the options provided.
7. Finally, provide a clear and concise submit message indicating that your form has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to this form** (`introduction`, text, required: false): A short introduction to get you started.
* **Suggestion Type** (`suggestion_type`, select_one, required: true): Select one of the suggested types for your idea (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Suggestion Title** (`suggestion_title`, text, required: true): A clear and concise title for your suggestion.
* **Description** (`suggestion_description`, text, required: true): A detailed description of your idea, focusing on the main points and key points.
* **Category** (`suggestion_category`, select_multiple, required: true): Select relevant categories for your suggestion, using one or more of the options provided.
* **Keywords** (`suggestion_keywords`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose relevant keywords related to your suggestion, again using one or more of the options provided.
* **Submit Suggestion** (`submit_suggestion`, text, required: false): A message indicating that your form has been completed.
