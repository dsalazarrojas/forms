# Scenario Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Scenario Selection Form" is designed to collect information about participants for workshops, training events, or other organizational events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the scenarios (workshop type) from the list.
2. Choose a training type from the list.
3. Select the event type from the list.
4. Fill out the participant's contact information: 
   * Organization
   * Business Name
   * Participant Email
   * Participant Phone
   * Participant Phone Ext
   * Participant Organization
   * Participant Job Title
   * Participant Position
   * Participant Department
   * Participant Job Title Ext
   * Participant Department Ext
   * Participant Position Ext
   * Participant Organization Ext
5. Fill out the participant's date information:
   * Workshop Date
   * Training Date
   * Event Date
   * Event Date Ext
   * Training Date Ext
6. Add any comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
7. Finally, fill out the participant's name if not already provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Type**: Select the type of workshop you wish to participate in.
* **Training Type**: Select the type of training you need.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are interested in.
* **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date of the training you wish to attend.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you wish to attend.
* **Organization**: Enter the participant's organization name.
* **Business Name**: Enter the participant's business name.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Phone Ext**: Enter the participant's phone extension number.
* **Participant Job Title**: Enter the participant's job title.
* **Participant Position**: Enter the participant's job position.
* **Participant Department**: Enter the participant's department name.
* **Participant Job Title Ext**: Enter the participant's job title extension.
* **Participant Department Ext**: Enter the participant's department extension.
* **Participant Position Ext**: Enter the participant's position extension.
* **Participant Organization**: Enter the participant's organization name.
* **Participant Organization Ext**: Enter the participant's organization extension.
* **Event Date Ext**: Enter the extended date of the event you wish to attend.
* **Training Date Ext**: Enter the extended date of the training you wish to attend.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your participation.
* **Notes**: Add any notes or feedback about the form.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Phone Ext**: Enter the participant's phone number extension.
