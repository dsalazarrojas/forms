# Community Promotion Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in promoting our community. By filling out this form, you'll be helping us understand your areas of interest and your ability to help with promotional efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Select all that apply for each multiple-choice question.
- Use the provided hints as guidance for each field.
- If you're unsure about any field, reach out to us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`promoter_full_name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`promoter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`promoter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Associated Organization or Business** (`promoter_organization`, text, optional): If you're associated with an organization or business, please let us know.
* **Which initiatives are you interested in promoting?** (`initiative_interest_selection`, select_multiple, required): Select all the initiatives you're interested in promoting, such as Upcoming Local Events, Volunteer Opportunities, etc.
* **How can you help us with promotion?** (`promotional_method_support`, select_multiple, required): Select all the ways you can help with promotion, such as Social Media Posting, Flyer and Poster Distribution, etc.
* **Tell us more about your background in promotion or community work** (`background_experience_text`, text, required): Share with us your experience in promotion or community work.
* **Weekly hours you can dedicate to promotion** (`weekly_hours_dedication`, select_one, required): Choose how many hours you can dedicate to promotion per week.
* **Do you have a significant social media following?** (`social_media_following_check`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have a significant social media following.
* **Please provide your social media handles** (`social_media_handles_info`, text, optional): If you answered 'True' above, please provide your social media handles.
* **Would you like to join our promotional strategy team?** (`strategy_team_interest`, select_one, required): Help us plan and coordinate campaigns by answering 'Yes - I'm in!'.
* **Other ways you'd like to support the community** (`additional_support_ideas_text`, text, optional): Share with us any other ways you'd like to support the community.
* **I agree to be contacted for promotional opportunities** (`contact_consent_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you're willing to be contacted for promotional opportunities.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps_note`, note, optional): Read the note carefully for next steps and further instructions.
* **Today's Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Fill in the current date to confirm submission.

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to help us understand your interests and abilities for promoting our community. We'll be in touch within 48 hours to discuss how we can work together.
