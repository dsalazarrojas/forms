# Level Creation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about level creation, including the name, description, difficulty level, and theme of a level. The data collected will be used to improve the level creation process and provide insights for game developers. Please complete this survey to help us better understand your experience with level creation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the title of the level you are creating or have created.
2. Add a description of the level, including its features and mechanics.
3. Choose the difficulty level of the level on a scale from 1-10.
4. Select the type of level (e.g., puzzle, platformer, etc.).
5. Choose the theme of the level (e.g., fantasy, sci-fi, etc.).
6. Add any relevant artwork or graphics for the level.
7. Rate your satisfaction with the level creation process on a scale from 1-10.
8. Leave any additional feedback or comments about the level creation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Level Name** (`level_name`): Enter the name of the level, e.g., "Level 1: Castle Dungeon".
- **Level Description** (`level_description`): Describe the features and mechanics of the level, e.g., "A platformer level set in a medieval castle, with a focus on navigation and combat".
- **Level Difficulty** (`level_difficulty`): Choose a difficulty level from 1-10, where 1 is easy and 10 is very challenging.
- **Level Type** (`level_type`): Select the type of level, e.g., puzzle, platformer, etc.
- **Level Theme** (`level_theme`): Choose the theme of the level, e.g., fantasy, sci-fi, etc.
- **Level Art** (`level_art`): Upload any relevant artwork or graphics for the level.
- **Satisfaction** (`level_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with the level creation process, where 1 is not satisfied and 10 is satisfied.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`): Leave any additional feedback or comments about the level creation process.
- **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or thoughts about the level creation process.
