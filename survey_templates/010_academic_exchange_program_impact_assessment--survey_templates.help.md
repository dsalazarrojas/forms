# Academic Exchange Program Impact Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants about their experience in an academic exchange program. This information will help us assess the impact of the program on participants and make it more effective for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your name**: In the "Participant name" field, enter your full name as it appears on your ID or as you would like to be addressed.
2. **Enter your home institution**: In the "Home institution" field, enter the name of the institution you are from.
3. **Enter your host institution**: In the "Host institution" field, enter the name of the institution you visited during the exchange.
4. **Rate your learning outcomes**: In the "To what extent were learning outcomes met" field, select one of the options to rate how well your objectives were achieved during the exchange.
5. **Rate your cultural experience**: In the "Cultural experience rating" field, select one of the options to rate your cultural integration and experiences during the exchange.
6. **Select any challenges faced**: In the "Challenges faced during exchange" field, select all the challenges you faced during the exchange.
7. **Describe any notable effects on your career or studies**: In the "Impact on career or studies" field, describe any notable effects the exchange had on your career or studies.
8. **Provide suggestions for program improvement**: In the "Suggestions for program improvement" field, provide any ideas you have to enhance future exchanges.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: Your full name as it appears on your ID or as you would like to be addressed.
* **Home institution**: The name of the institution you are from.
* **Host institution**: The name of the institution you visited during the exchange.
* **Program duration**: The length of time you spent in the exchange program. (Note: This field is not required, but it's a good idea to fill it in if you can.)
* **To what extent were learning outcomes met**: Rate how well your objectives were achieved during the exchange. Select one of the options:
	+ Exceeded expectations
	+ Met expectations
	+ Partially met
	+ Did not meet
* **Cultural experience rating**: Rate your cultural integration and experiences during the exchange. Select one of the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Challenges faced during exchange**: Select all the challenges you faced during the exchange. Options are:
	+ Language barriers
	+ Housing difficulties
	+ Financial constraints
	+ Administrative delays
	+ Health or safety concerns
* **Impact on career or studies**: Describe any notable effects the exchange had on your career or studies.
* **Suggestions for program improvement**: Provide any ideas you have to enhance future exchanges.
