# Call To Action Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on marketing assets' call-to-action (CTA) performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide information about the marketing asset, including its title or name and type.
2. Evaluate the CTA's placement, visual clarity, relevance to the surrounding content, perceived value, and likelihood of users clicking on it.
3. Suggest improvements to make the CTA more effective.
4. Compare your approach to that of competitors.
5. Provide your name and date of review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Asset Title or Name** (`asset_title`, text, required): Enter the title or name of the marketing asset, e.g., "Spring Promo Landing Page" or "Q3 Newsletter".
* **Marketing Asset Type** (`asset_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of marketing asset, such as Landing Page, Email Campaign, Social Media Post, or Banner Ad.
* **Primary CTA Text** (`cta_text`, text, required): Enter the main button or link text of the CTA.
* **CTA Placement and Visibility** (`cta_placement`, select_one, required): Evaluate how easily the user can find the CTA, with options: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Visual Clarity** (`visual_clarity`, select_one, required): Assess how well the CTA stands out visually, with options: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Relevance to Content** (`relevance_to_content`, select_one, required): Determine how well the CTA matches the surrounding copy, with options: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Perceived Value** (`value_proposition`, select_one, required): Evaluate how clear it is what the user gets by clicking on the CTA, with options: Very Clear, Somewhat Clear, or Unclear.
* **Likelihood to Click** (`likelihood_to_click`, select_one, required): Assess how effective the CTA is in encouraging users to click, with options: Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, or Very Unlikely.
* **Suggested Improvements** (`suggested_improvements`, text, optional): Suggest ways to make the CTA more effective.
* **Comparison to Competitor CTAs** (`competitor_comparison`, text, optional): Compare your approach to that of competitors.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Date of Review** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the review.
