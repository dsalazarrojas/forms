# OMR Sheet Design Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your preferences and needs for designing an OMR sheet. Your input will help us understand what elements and processes are most suitable for OMR sheet design.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions in the form as honestly and accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. For select and multiple-choice questions, choose one or multiple options that best fit your preferences.
4. For text and number fields, enter your answer or number accordingly.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **OMR Sheet Design Survey**: This is the title of this survey.
* **Preferred Color Scheme**: Please select one of the following options for your preferred color scheme for the OMR sheet:
	+ Blue
	+ Red
	+ Green
* **Design Element Preferences**: Check all that apply for your preferred design elements:
	+ Font
	+ Layout
	+ Color
* **Design Element Priorities**: Check all that apply for your most important design elements:
	+ Font
	+ Layout
	+ Color
* **Design Process**: Please provide a brief description of your preferred design process.
* **Assessment Team Size**: Please enter the number of team members you expect to work with on the assessment process.
* **Educator Experience**: Please enter the number of years of experience you have in education.
* **Assessment Process**: Please provide a brief description of your assessment process.
* **Assessment Tool**: Please select one of the following options for your preferred assessment tool:
	+ Jotform
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Additional Comments**: Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your OMR sheet design needs.
