# Study Abroad Personality Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users identify their preferred study abroad destinations based on their individual preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Answer each question in the order it appears.
* Choose the options that best fit your preferences.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Abroad Preferences** (`country_preferences`, text, optional): This field is where you can briefly describe your preferred country for studying abroad.
* **Education Goals** (`education_goals`, text, optional): Here you can outline your main goals for studying abroad, such as career advancement, personal growth, or cultural immersion.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred language, which will help us understand your language proficiency level and communication needs.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Indicate your budget for studying abroad, which will help us suggest affordable options that fit your financial constraints.
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, select_multiple, required): Select all countries where you would like to study, such as Australia, Austria, Belgium, etc.
* **Preferred Duration** (`preferred_duration`, select_one, optional): Choose the length of your study period, from less than 6 months to more than 3 years.
* **Preferred Program Type** (`preferred_program_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of study program that suits your needs, such as academic, internship, or work and travel.
* **Preferred Language of Study** (`preferred_language_of_study`, text, optional): If you have a preferred language for studying, select it here.
* **Preferred Language of Work** (`preferred_language_of_work`, text, optional): If you have a preferred language for working, select it here.
* **Preferred Culture** (`preferred_culture`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the culture that best fits your needs, such as traditional or western.
* **Preferred Academic Level** (`preferred_academic_level`, select_one, optional): Select the academic level you are looking for, such as high school, university, or master's or doctoral.
* **Preferred Academic Field** (`preferred_academic_field`, select_multiple, optional): Select all academic fields that interest you, such as arts, business, engineering, etc.
* **Preferred Academic Field Subfield** (`preferred_academic_field_subfield`, text, optional): If you have a specific subfield within your preferred academic field, select it here.
* **Preferred Academic Institution** (`preferred_academic_institution`, text, optional): If you have a preferred academic institution, select it here.
* **Preferred Academic Degree** (`preferred_academic_degree`, text, optional): If you have a preferred degree level, such as bachelor's or master's, select it here.
* **Preferred Academic Certificate** (`preferred_academic_certificate`, text, optional): If you have a preferred academic certificate, select it here.
* **Preferred Language of Proficiency** (`preferred_language_of_proficiency`, text, optional): If you have a preferred language for proficiency, select it here.
* **Preferred Language of Proficiency Level** (`preferred_language_of_proficiency_level`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred language proficiency level, from beginner to proficient.
* **Preferred Language of Study Level** (`preferred_language_of_study_level`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred language level for study, from basic to advanced.
* **Preferred Language of Work Level** (`preferred_language_of_work_level`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred language level for work, from basic to advanced.
* **Preferred Academic Career Field** (`preferred_academic_career_field`, select_multiple, optional): Select all academic career fields that interest you, such as arts, business, engineering, etc.
* **Preferred Academic Career Field Subfield** (`preferred_academic_career_field_subfield`, text, optional): If you have a specific subfield within your preferred academic career field, select it here.
* **Preferred Academic Field of Interest** (`preferred_academic_field_of_interest`, text, optional): If you have a preferred academic field of interest, select it here.
* **Preferred Academic Field of Interest Subfield** (`preferred_academic_field_of_interest_subfield`, text, optional): If you have a specific subfield within your preferred academic field of interest, select it here.
* **Preferred Academic Field of Interest Subfield 2** (`preferred_academic_field_of_interest_subfield_2`, text, optional): If you have a specific subfield within your preferred academic field of interest, select it here.
