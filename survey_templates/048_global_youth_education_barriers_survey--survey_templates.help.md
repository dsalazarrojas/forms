# Global Youth Education Barriers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the barriers to education faced by individuals, particularly youth. It aims to gather data on various aspects of education, including demographics, education status, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, choosing the best option from the provided list.
2. If you have a different answer that is not listed, you can choose "Other" and provide more details in the "Other Info" field.
3. Fill out all the fields that apply to you, but do not feel obligated to fill out all the fields.
4. Submit your answers when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This field is not required and is meant to provide additional information if you want to provide it.
* **Barriers**: Choose all the barriers to education that you face. These might include things like limited access to education, financial constraints, or other barriers.
* **Education Status**: Select the current education status of the individual.
* **Needed Support**: If you have faced any obstacles to education, describe them briefly.
* **Country**: Choose the country where you are from.
* **School**: Choose the type of school you attend or attended.
* **Other School**: If you attend or attended a school that is not listed, select "Other" and provide more details in the "Other Info" field.
* **Gender**: Choose your gender.
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Education System**: Choose the type of education system you are part of.
* **Language**: Choose your primary language.
* **Education Level**: Enter the level of education you have completed.
* **School Year**: Enter the year you completed your education.
* **Family Income**: Choose the income level of your family.
* **Region**: Choose the region where you live.
* **School Environment**: Choose the condition of the school you attend or attended.
* **School Condition**: Choose the condition of the school you attend or attended.
* **Family Wealth**: Choose the wealth level of your family.
* **Family Status**: Choose the status of your family (married, single, etc.).
* **Family Type**: Choose the type of family you come from.
* **Family Size**: Enter the number of people in your family.
* **Education Awareness**: Choose how aware you are of education.
* **Education Interest**: Choose how interested you are in education.
* **Family Support**: Choose how much support you receive from your family for education.
* **Other Info**: If you want to provide more information that is not captured in the other fields, enter it here.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- If you are unsure of an answer, choose the option that best fits.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
