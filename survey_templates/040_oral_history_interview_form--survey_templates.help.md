# Oral History Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect oral history interview information, gathering essential details about an interview, the person being interviewed, and the context of the story being shared.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the personal information section on page 1.
2.  Move on to page 2 and provide the details of the interview.
3.  On page 3, write a brief narrative about the story being shared.
4.  On page 4, offer a historical account of the events described in the narrative.
5.  On page 5, fill out the researcher's information.
6.  On page 6, submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Personal Info** (Personal Info, text, required: false):
    This field is intended to collect basic personal details such as name, contact information, and any other relevant personal information.
*   **Interview Details** (Interview Details, text, required: false):
    This field is meant to capture the specifics of the interview, including the date, time, and location.
*   **Narrative** (Narrative, text, required: false):
    In this section, please provide a brief account of the story being shared, including any relevant background information.
*   **Historical Account** (Historical Account, text, required: false):
    This field is designed to gather a more in-depth historical account of the events described in the narrative.
*   **Researcher Info** (Researcher Info, text, required: false):
    This field is intended for the researcher to provide their contact information and any other relevant details about themselves.
*   **Submission** (Submission, text, required: false):
    In this final section, please submit your completed form, ensuring all fields are filled out accurately.
