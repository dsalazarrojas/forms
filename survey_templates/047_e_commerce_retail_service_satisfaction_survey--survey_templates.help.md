# E Commerce Retail Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and satisfaction data from customers who have made a purchase through our e-commerce retail service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields (marked with an asterisk \*) accurately and thoroughly.
2. Leave blank any optional fields that you do not have information for.
3. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
4. Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_name** (1, text, \*): Please enter your name as it appears on your purchase receipt or ID.
* **customer_email** (2, text, \*): Please enter your email address as it is associated with your purchase.
* **purchase_id** (3, number, \*): Please enter the purchase ID or order number associated with your purchase.
* **product_name** (4, text, \*): Please enter the name of the product you purchased.
* **purchase_date** (5, date, \*): Please enter the date of your purchase.
* **purchase_time** (6, time, \*): Please enter the time of your purchase.
* **rating** (7, select_one, \*): Please select your overall satisfaction with our service.
* **comment** (8, select_multiple, \*): Please select all that apply from the list of options for your comment.
* **question** (9, text, \*): Please enter any questions or concerns you have about your purchase.
* **issue** (10, text, \*): Please enter any issues you experienced with your purchase.
* **improvement_suggestions** (11, text, \*): Please enter any suggestions for improvement for our service.
* **contact_email** (12, text, ): This is an optional field for entering your contact email address.
* **phone_number** (13, text, ): This is an optional field for entering your phone number.
* **address** (14, text, ): This is an optional field for entering your address.
* **feedback** (15, text, ): This is an optional field for providing feedback about our service.
* **device** (16, select_multiple, ): Please select all the devices you used to access our service.
* **store** (17, text, ): This is an optional field for entering the store where you made your purchase.
* **website** (18, text, ): This is an optional field for entering the website where you made your purchase.
* **device_type** (19, select_multiple, ): Please select all the types of devices you used to access our service.
* **purchase_amount** (20, number, ): This is an optional field for entering the amount you spent on your purchase.
* **purchase_location** (21, text, ): This is an optional field for entering the location where you made your purchase.
* **visited_other** (22, text, ): This is an optional field for entering any other store or location you visited.
* **other_store** (23, text, ): This is an optional field for entering any other store where you made your purchase.
* **other_purchase_location** (24, text, ): This is an optional field for entering any other location where you made your purchase.
* **survey_comments** (25, text, ): This is an optional field for entering any additional comments about your experience.

# Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us through the contact email address provided.
* We value your feedback and appreciate your time in taking this survey.
* Thank you for your participation!
