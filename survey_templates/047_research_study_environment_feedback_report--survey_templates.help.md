# research_study_environment_feedback_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for researchers to provide feedback on the environment and settings of their research studies. The form is used to collect information on the research studies conducted, site conditions, cohorts involved, milestone completion, time spent, and any additional feedback or comments. This information will be used to improve the study environment and settings for future research endeavors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are providing feedback on a research study.
2. Select the relevant information from each field, either by typing or selecting from the provided options.
3. Complete all required fields, but feel free to leave fields with "false" information if no action is taken.
4. Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label: Research Studies</label>
* The Research Studies field is a text field for you to describe the research studies you are providing feedback on. Please provide a brief description of the study, including its title, objectives, and any relevant details.

### <label: Settings</label>
* The Settings field is a text field for you to describe the settings used during the research study, such as equipment, software, or other relevant details.

### <label: Site</label>
* The Site field is a select-one field where you can choose either "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the research was conducted on-site or remotely. If you choose "Yes", please provide further details on the site conditions.

### <label: Cohorts</label>
* The Cohorts field is a select-multiple field where you can choose one or more "Yes" or "No" options to indicate which cohorts were involved in the research study. If you choose "Yes", please provide further details on the cohorts.

### <label: Milestone</label>
* The Milestone field is a date field where you can input the completion date of the research milestone.

### <label: Time Spent</label>
* The Time Spent field is a time field where you can input the total time spent on the research study.

### <label: Feedback</label>
* The Feedback field is a note field for you to provide any additional comments or feedback on the research study environment and settings.

### <label: Email</label>
* The Email field is an email field where you can input your email address for any follow-up communication or to receive updates on the study environment and settings.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and clear information to ensure that your feedback is helpful in improving the study environment and settings.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
