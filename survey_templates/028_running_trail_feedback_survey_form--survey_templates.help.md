# running_trail_feedback_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "running_trail_feedback_survey_form" is designed to gather feedback from event participants about their experience at the running trail event. This feedback will help event organizers understand what they did well and what they can improve on for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you answer all the questions that apply to your experience.
2. Fill out all the questions that relate to your experience, and feel free to skip any that don't.
3. Provide honest and specific feedback in the text fields.
4. Select the options that best fit your experience for the select_one questions.
