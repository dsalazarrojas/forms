# telecom_customer_preference_information_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects customer preference information for telecom services. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form header section.
2. Provide your Customer Name, Customer Number, Customer Email, and Customer Phone in the respective fields.
3. Select your Service Type, Service Level of Service, and Preferred Language options.
4. Choose your billing cycle from the list of available options.
5. Select the follow-up method and date for future follow-ups.
6. Provide the Customer Service Representative's name and phone number (if applicable).
7. Enter any Follow-up Notes (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This section is for general information and is not required.
* **Customer Name**: Your name as it appears on your account.
* **Customer Number**: Your telecom account or reference number.
* **Customer Email**: Your email address for communication with our team.
* **Customer Phone**: Your phone number for contact.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of telecom service you use (e.g., mobile, fixed line, internet).
* **Service Level of Service**: Choose the level of service you require (e.g., standard, premium, business).
* **Billing Cycle**: Select the billing cycle that suits your needs.
* **Preferred Language**: Select your preferred language for communication.
* **Feedback**: Choose from the available feedback options to provide feedback on our service.
* **Follow up**: Select the follow-up method for our team to contact you (e.g., phone, email, SMS).
* **Next Follow up Date**: Schedule a follow-up date for our team to contact you.
* **Follow up Method**: Select the follow-up method for our team to contact you (e.g., phone, email, SMS).
* **Customer Service Representative**: Enter the name of your customer service representative (if applicable).
* **Customer Service Representative Phone**: Enter the phone number of your customer service representative (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments for our team to reference during follow-ups.
