## League Team Scheduling Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is divided into the following sections:
- **Availability**: Please indicate when you are available to play during the week. 
- **Preferred Days**: Indicate which days of the week you prefer for league games. 
- **Preferred Times**: Indicate which times of the day you prefer for league games. 
- **Blackout Dates**: If you have any dates when you are not available, please provide them here. 
- **Conflict Avoidance**: Do you want to play against a specific team or avoid specific teams? 
- **Team Conflict Avoidance**: Do you want to avoid playing against specific teams? 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your availability status for each day of the week.
2. For preferred days and times, choose the days and time slots that you prefer.
3. If you have specific dates when you are not available, provide them in the "Blackout Dates" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for conflict avoidance questions based on your preferences.
5. Save your responses when you've completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_availability**: Indicate when you are available to play during the week.
* **preferred_days**: Choose which days you prefer for league games.
* **preferred_times**: Choose which times of the day you prefer for league games.
* **blackout_dates**: If you have specific dates when you are not available, provide them here.
* **conflict_avoidance**: Select "Yes" to avoid playing against a specific team, "No" otherwise.
* **team_conflict_avoidance**: Choose which teams you prefer to avoid playing against.

This help guide aims to make it easy for users to understand the form's purpose and how to fill it out correctly. By following these steps and explanations, users can provide accurate and helpful feedback for league team scheduling decisions.
