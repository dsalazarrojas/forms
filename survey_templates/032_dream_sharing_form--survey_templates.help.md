# Dream Sharing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dream Sharing Form is a tool for users to share their dreams and experiences with others in a research or educational setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Decide if you would like to share your dream with others. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for the first question.
2. Select the category you think best describes your dream.
3. Write your dream narrative in the text field provided.
4. Add any reflections or thoughts you have about your dream.
5. Enter the date you had your dream.
6. If you are a researcher, educator, or counselor, enter your corresponding ID number (if applicable).
7. If you are a dream collector, indicate if you are a participant or collector (yes/no).
8. If you are a dream collector, enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dream Sharing?** (`dream_sharing_form`, select_one, required): Decide if you would like to share your dream with others.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category you think best describes your dream.
* **Dream Narrative** (`description`, text, required): Write your dream narrative in this text field.
* **Reflections** (`Reflections`, text, required): Add any reflections or thoughts you have about your dream.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you had your dream.
* **Researcher ID** (`researcher_id`, text, optional): If you are a researcher, enter your Researcher ID number.
* **Educator ID** (`educator_id`, text, optional): If you are an educator, enter your Educator ID number.
* **Counselor ID** (`counselor_id`, text, optional): If you are a counselor, enter your Counselor ID number.
* **Dream Collector** (`dream_collector`, select_one, optional): If you are a dream collector, indicate if you are a participant or collector.
* **Dream Collector Email** (`dream_collector_email`, email, optional): If you are a dream collector, enter your email address.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required).
- If you are unsure about a field, you can always contact the dream collector or administrator for guidance.
- Remember to review your submission before submitting it to ensure it accurately represents your dream.
