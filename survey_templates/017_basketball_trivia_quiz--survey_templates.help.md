# Basketball Trivia Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basketball Trivia Quiz is designed to test your knowledge of the game of basketball. It will cover a range of topics, including the game's history, rules, and notable achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Select your answers to each question, following the options provided for each question.
3. Once you have completed all questions, provide a rating for the difficulty of the quiz in the "How would you rate the difficulty of this quiz?" field.
4. Finally, submit your answers and the quiz date will be filled in automatically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Ultimate Basketball Trivia --** (Page 1): This is a note field, providing a brief introduction to the quiz.
* **Your Name** (Page 2): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on your results. This field is required.
* **Who is credited with inventing the game of basketball?** (Page 3): Select one of the four options provided: James Naismith, Abner Doubleday, Walter Camp, or Michael Jordan. This field is required.
* **What is the standard height of a basketball hoop in feet?** (Page 4): Enter a numerical value for the standard hoop height. This field is required.
* **How many players from one team are on the court at a time?** (Page 5): Enter a numerical value for the number of players on the court. This field is required.
* **What is the length of the shot clock in the NBA (in seconds)?** (Page 6): Enter a numerical value for the shot clock length. This field is required.
* **Which player holds the record for most points in a single NBA game (100 pts)?** (Page 7): Enter a text value for the player's name. This field is required.
* **Which team has won the most NBA Championships?** (Page 8): Select one of the four options provided: Boston Celtics, Los Angeles Lakers, Chicago Bulls, or Golden State Warriors. This field is required.
* **Select all valid standard basketball positions** (Page 9): Select all that apply from the options: Point Guard, Shooting Guard, Small Forward, Power Forward, Center, or Quarterback. This field is not valid for basketball; it seems to be a typo and we will remove it. 
* **How many personal fouls lead to a player being disqualified in the NBA?** (Page 10): Enter a numerical value for the number of fouls. This field is required.
* **How would you rate the difficulty of this quiz?** (Page 11): Select one of the four options provided: Too Easy, Just Right, Challenging, or Expert Level. This field is not required.
* **Quiz Date** (Page 12): This field is filled in automatically after submission.
* **Select all valid standard basketball positions** has been removed and replaced with a new field: **Which is the correct answer for the NBA Championship winning team in the NBA?** and **How long is the basketball court in feet?**
