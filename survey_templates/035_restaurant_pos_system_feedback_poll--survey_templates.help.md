# Restaurant POS System Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant POS System Feedback Poll form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with the Restaurant POS system. Your input will help us improve the system and provide better service in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the location of your store from the drop-down menu.
2. Choose your role within the store from the drop-down menu.
3. Provide your feedback on the POS system performance.
4. Select a date and time of your visit (or use the current date and time).
5. Rate your overall experience with the system (1-5).
6. Add any additional comments or feedback.
7. Enter your contact email address.
8. Enter your contact phone number.
9. Select the categories you belong to.
10. Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the location of your store?**: Select the location of your store from the drop-down menu.
* **What is your role in the store?**: Choose your role within the store from the drop-down menu.
* **What is your feedback on POS performance?**: Enter any specific feedback or issues you experienced with the POS system.
* **Date and Time**: Select the date and time of your visit. Note: This field is optional.
* **How would you rate your overall experience?**: Select a rating from 1 to 5 to express your satisfaction with the system.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Which of the following categories do you belong to?**: Select all categories that apply to you.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Category**: Enter the category name of your store (this field is optional).
* **Role**: Enter your role within the store (this field is optional).
* **Store**: Enter the name of your store (this field is optional).
* **Location**: Enter the location of your store (this field is optional).
* **How long do you have experience with our POS system?**: Select how long you have been using the POS system.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback.
* **How would you rate our POS system?**: Rate the system (1-5).
* **Submit**: Click to submit your feedback.

Please note that some fields are optional and can be left blank. However, providing more information will help us better understand your feedback.
