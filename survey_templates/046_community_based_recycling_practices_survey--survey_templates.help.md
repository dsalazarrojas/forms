# Community Based Recycling Practices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your recycling habits, practices, and suggestions for improving recycling in our community. The goal is to better understand the needs and challenges of our community members and identify opportunities for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read the introduction carefully.
2. Provide your name and email address, if you wish (optional).
3. Enter your address and city to help us understand where you live.
4. Provide your household size and type of housing to help us understand your living situation.
5. Answer questions about your recycling habits, including the frequency, materials recycled, and services used.
6. Indicate how satisfied you are with current recycling services and what barriers prevent you from recycling more.
7. Let us know if you are aware of recycling contamination issues and what education you need.
8. Express interest in additional recycling programs and suggest ways to improve recycling.
9. Tell us about your waste reduction practices and if you would participate in community programs.
10. Finally, share any suggestions or comments you have for improving recycling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Your full name (optional).
	+ Please enter your full name if you wish to be identified in the results.
* **Participant Email**: Your email address (optional).
	+ Enter your email address if you wish to receive updates or participate in future surveys.
* **Address**: Your street address.
	+ This will help us understand where you live in our community.
* **City**: The city where you live.
	+ This will help us understand the local context of your recycling habits.
* **Household Size**: How many people live in your household.
	+ This will help us understand your family's size and dynamics.
* **Housing Type**: The type of housing you live in.
	+ This will help us understand your living situation.
* **Recycling Frequency**: How often do you recycle?
	+ This will help us understand your commitment to recycling.
* **Materials Recycled**: What materials do you recycle?
	+ This will help us understand what you currently recycle.
* **Recycling Service**: Do you have curbside recycling service?
	+ This will help us understand your access to recycling facilities.
* **Satisfaction Level**: How satisfied are you with current recycling services?
	+ This will help us understand your experience with recycling services.
* **Recycling Barriers**: What prevents you from recycling more?
	+ This will help us understand your challenges with recycling.
* **Contamination Awareness**: Are you aware of recycling contamination issues?
	+ This will help us understand your knowledge of contamination and its implications.
* **Education Needs**: What recycling education would be helpful to you?
	+ This will help us understand what information you need to improve your recycling habits.
* **Program Interest**: Which additional programs interest you?
	+ This will help us understand what programs you would like to see offered.
* **Waste Reduction**: What do you do to reduce waste?
	+ This will help us understand your efforts to reduce waste.
* **Community Involvement**: Would you participate in community recycling programs?
	+ This will help us understand your willingness to engage in community activities.
* **Volunteer Interest**: Would you volunteer for recycling initiatives?
	+ This is optional and will help us understand your interest in contributing to the community.
* **Suggestions**: Any suggestions for improving recycling in our community?
	+ This will help us gather feedback and suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other comments about recycling in our community.
	+ This is an optional field for providing any additional thoughts or suggestions.
* **Survey Date**: The date you completed this survey.
	+ This will help us track when you completed the survey.

Please note that all answers will be kept confidential and anonymous. Your responses will help us improve recycling services and programs in our community. If you have any questions or need clarification on any question, feel free to contact us. Thank you for participating in this survey!
