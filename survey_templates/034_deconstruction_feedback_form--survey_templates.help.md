# Deconstruction Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Deconstruction Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers or users regarding the deconstruction process. This information will be used to identify areas for improvement and enhance the quality of our deconstruction services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please make sure you are filling out this form for a single deconstruction project.
2. Fill out each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Crew name** (`crew_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the crew that performed the deconstruction work.
* **Crew size** (`crew_size`, number, required): Please enter the number of crew members who were involved in the deconstruction process.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions regarding the deconstruction process, please enter them here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Please click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

Note: All fields with the "required" flag set to true must be completed before submitting the form.
