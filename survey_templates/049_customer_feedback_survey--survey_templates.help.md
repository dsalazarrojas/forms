# customer_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and suggestions from customers, allowing the company to understand their experience and preferences and improve their products or services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the option that best describes your reason for filling out this form.
2. Answer the questions as they arise on each page.
3. Enter your email address in the "Your Email" field.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Your Comment" field.
5. Select all that apply to answer the "Do you have any feedback..." questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What would you like to do** (`intro_page`, `select_one`, required): This section asks you to choose the purpose of your feedback, whether it's to provide general feedback or report an issue.
* **What do you like?** (`question_1`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks you to select all the things you like about the company's products or services.
* **What do you not like?** (`question_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the things you do not like about the company's products or services.
* **Do you have any suggestions?** (`suggestion`, `text`, optional): If you have any ideas or suggestions for the company, enter them here.
* **How likely are you to recommend us?** (`rating_1`, `select_one`, required): Rate your likelihood of recommending the company's products or services to others.
* **Your Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Your Comment** (`comment`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the company.
* **Do you have any feedback about our website?** (`question_8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply if you have any feedback about the website.
* **Do you have any feedback about our customer support?** (`question_9`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply if you have any feedback about the customer support.
