# sports_league_rule_change_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a proposed rule change within a sports league. Your input will help inform the decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the introduction carefully to understand the context of the rule change.
2. Answer each question carefully, as your response will contribute to the overall discussion.
3. Fill in the required fields (question2, question3, question14, question15, question16, question19) with your honest answers.
4. Leave optional fields (introduction, question1, question4, question6, question7, question9, question10, question11, question12, question13, question20, question21, question22, question23, question24, question25) blank if you do not have relevant information or if they are not applicable to your answer.
5. Submit your response to contribute to the discussion.
