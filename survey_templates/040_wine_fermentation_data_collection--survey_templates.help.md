# Wine Fermentation Data Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wine Fermentation Data Collection form is designed to collect data on wine fermentation to help wine producers and researchers understand the various factors that affect wine quality and production.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options that apply to your wine fermentation data.
2. Enter the required information in the corresponding fields.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wine Fermentation Data Collection Form** (`wine_fermentation_data_collection`, select_multiple): Please select all the options that apply to your wine fermentation data.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Winery Name** (`winery_name`, text): Enter the name of the winery or vineyard.
* **Vintage** (`vintage`, number): Enter the vintage of the wine (e.g., 2018).
* **Wine Style** (`wine_style`, select_one): Select the type of wine that was fermented (e.g., Chardonnay, Merlot, Cabernet).
	+ Chardonnay
	+ Merlot
	+ Cabernet
	+ Pinot
	+ Other
* **Fermentation Temperature** (`fermentation_temperature`, number): Enter the temperature of the fermentation process (e.g., 15°C).
* **Fermentation Duration** (`fermentation_duration`, number): Enter the duration of the fermentation process (e.g., 7 days).
* **Yeast Used** (`yeast_used`, select_multiple): Select the type of yeast used in the fermentation process.
	+ Red Wine Yeast
	+ White Wine Yeast
	+ Rapid Yeast
	+ Champagne Yeast
	+ Other
* **Wine Address** (`wines_address`, text): Enter the address of the winery or vineyard.
* **Wine Color** (`wine_color`, select_one): Select the color of the wine (e.g., white, red, rosé).
	+ white
	+ red
	+ rosé
	+ pink
	+ Other
* **Wine Grape Varietal** (`wine_grape_varietal`, text): Enter the grape variety used in the wine production (e.g., Chardonnay, Cabernet, Merlot).
* **Fermentation Tank Size** (`fermentation_tank_size`, number): Enter the size of the fermentation tank used (e.g., 1000L).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the fermentation process.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date of the fermentation process.
* **Time** (`time`, time): Enter the time of the fermentation process.
* **Wine Type** (`wine_type`, select_one): Select the type of wine produced (e.g., still, sparkling, dessert).
	+ Still Wine
	+ Sparkling Wine
	+ Dessert Wine
	+ Fortified Wine
	+ Other
* **Wine PH Level** (`wine_ph_level`, number): Enter the pH level of the wine (e.g., 7.1).
* **Wine Titratable Acid** (`wine_titratable_acid`, number): Enter the acid level of the wine (e.g., 1.1).
* **Winery** (`winery`, text): Enter the name of the winery or vineyard.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Wine Location** (`wines_location`, text): Enter the location of the winery or vineyard.
* **Region** (`region`, select_one): Select the region where the wine is produced (e.g., North America, Europe).
	+ North America
	+ South America
	+ Europe
	+ Asia
	+ Africa
	+ Oceania
	+ Other
* **Country** (`country`, select_one): Select the country where the wine is produced (e.g., United States, France).
	+ United States
	+ Australia
	+ France
	+ Italy
	+ Spain
	+ Portugal
	+ Chile
	+ Other
