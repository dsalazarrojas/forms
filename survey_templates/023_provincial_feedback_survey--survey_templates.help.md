# Provincial Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about their experience with the provincial agency, specifically regarding public services, contact methods, and geographic location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Select your province of residence from the list of available options.
* Indicate how satisfied you are with public services in your province.
* Choose your primary mode of contact (phone, email, in-person, or other) for the agency to contact you.
* Provide any additional feedback or comments you have for the agency.
* If desired, you can specify how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, in-person, or not at all).
* You will be asked to provide the province you serve (text field).
* If you want, you can also specify how you would like to be contacted for this purpose (phone, email, in-person, or not at all).
