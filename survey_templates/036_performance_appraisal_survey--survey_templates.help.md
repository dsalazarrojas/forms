# Performance Appraisal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Appraisal Survey is used to evaluate an employee's performance, gathering information about their strengths, areas for improvement, and overall performance. This survey is typically completed by a manager or supervisor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Enter the manager's name in the **Manager Name** field.
3. Specify the performance area that the employee is being evaluated in the **Performance Area** field.
4. Select the evaluation date from the calendar picker or enter it manually in the **Evaluation Date** field.
5. Enter a numeric value for the **Performance Rating** field, where 1 is the lowest rating and 10 is the highest rating.
6. Optional fields are **Strengths**, **Areas for Improvement**, **Recommendations**, and **Evaluation Comments**. You can enter any relevant information in these fields.
7. Enter a numeric value for the **Overall Performance** field, where 1 is the lowest rating and 10 is the highest rating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the employee's manager or supervisor who is completing the evaluation.
* **Performance Area**: Specify the performance area being evaluated, such as sales, customer service, or project management.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the performance evaluation.
* **Performance Rating**: Enter a numeric value to rate the employee's performance, where 1 is the lowest rating and 10 is the highest rating.
* **Strengths**: Optionally enter any strengths or positive attributes of the employee.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Optionally enter any areas where the employee needs improvement.
* **Recommendations**: Optionally enter any recommendations for the employee's future growth and development.
* **Evaluation Comments**: Optionally enter any additional comments about the employee's performance.
* **Overall Performance**: Enter a numeric value to rate the employee's overall performance, where 1 is the lowest rating and 10 is the highest rating.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy and completeness of the data entered.
* This form is intended for current performance evaluations, not past or retroactive evaluations.
