# Casino Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Casino Survey" form is designed to gather feedback from casino visitors about their experiences and preferences. This form aims to collect information about respondents' demographics, visit details, and opinions on various aspects of the casino. The goal is to understand how to improve the casino's facilities, services, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Respondent Information**: This form is not required but provides an opportunity to share some basic information about yourself.
2. **Survey Date**: Please enter the date of your recent visit to the casino in MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. **Survey Time**: Enter the time of your visit in HH:MM AM/PM format.
4. **Age Group**: Select your age group from the available options.
5. **Visit Information**: This form is not required but provides space to share any additional details about your visit.
6. **Visit Frequency**: Indicate how often you visit the casino (First time, Once a month, Several times a month, Weekly, Multiple times per week, Daily).
7. **Party Size**: Enter the number of people in your party.
8. **Visit Duration**: Choose how long you spent at the casino (Less than 1 hour, 1-3 hours, 3-5 hours, 5-8 hours, More than 8 hours).
9. **Visit Purpose**: Select your primary reason for visiting the casino (Gaming and Gambling, Dining, Entertainment or Shows, Social Gathering, All of the above, Other).
10. **Gaming Preferences**: Select all games you played during your visit (Slot Machines, Blackjack, Roulette, Poker, Baccarat, Sports Betting, Other).
11. **Playing Time Allocation**: Share how you allocated your gaming time.
12. **Gaming Budget**: Indicate your typical gaming budget per visit (Under $50, $50 - $100, $100 - $250, $250 - $500, $500 - $1000, Over $1000).
13. **Facilities and Services**: This form is not required but provides space to share any comments about facilities and services.
14. **Gaming Floor Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the gaming floor layout and equipment (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
15. **Dining Experience**: Rate the quality of dining options available (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Did not dine).
16. **Entertainment Quality**: Rate the quality of entertainment and shows (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Did not attend).
17. **Staff and Service**: This form is not required but provides space to share any comments about staff friendliness and helpfulness.
18. **Staff Friendliness**: Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of staff (Very Friendly, Friendly, Neutral, Unfriendly, Very Unfriendly).
19. **Service Quality**: Rate the overall service quality during your visit (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
20. **Overall Experience**: This form is not required but provides space to share any general comments about your visit.
21. **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the visit (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
22. **Would Return**: Indicate if you would return to this casino (Definitely, Probably, Maybe, Probably Not, Definitely Not).
23. **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions for improvement with the casino.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Take a few minutes to answer each question carefully.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, use the space provided for them.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback!
