# corporate_training_material_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the corporate training material.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Use the provided options for select_multiple questions, and feel free to add any additional comments or suggestions.
3. Do not worry about grammar or spelling errors, just provide your honest feedback.
4. If a question does not apply to you, feel free to skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome To The Survey (1)**: This page is an introduction to the form.
* **What did you like most about this training material? (2)**: Please share your positive thoughts about the training material.
* **What did you dislike about this training material? (3)**: Please share your negative thoughts about the training material.
* **What suggestions do you have for improvement? (4)**: Think about how the training material can be improved and provide your suggestions.
* **Do You Need More Information On A Topic? (5)**: If you think the training material was lacking in some areas, let us know what you would like to see more of.
* **Was your training material relevant? (6)**: Please select one or more of the options that apply to you: True, False, Not Enough Information.
* **Was this training material easy to understand? (7)**: Please select one or more of the options that apply to you: True, False, Not Enough Information.
* **Do you have any other comments about this training material? (8)**: This is a free-text area where you can add any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Would you recommend this training material? (9)**: Please select one or more of the options that apply to you: True, False.
* **Is this material suitable for your training needs? (10)**: Please select one or more of the options that apply to you: True, False, Not Enough Information.
* **What is the most important information you want the course creators to know? (11)**: Please provide your thoughts on what the course creators should know.
* **Is there anything else you would like to add? (12)**: This is a free-text area where you can add any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Do you have any other comments? (13)**: This is a free-text area where you can add any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Is your feedback important for our organization? (14)**: Please select one or more of the options that apply to you: True, False.
* **How likely is it that you will recommend this training material to colleagues? (15)**: Please enter a number between 0 and 100 to indicate your likelihood of recommendation.
* **What is your email address? (16)**: Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further feedback or discussion.

**Tips**

* Be honest and provide your real name and email address if you would like to be contacted for further feedback or discussion.
* If you are unable to complete the form at once, please save your progress and come back to it later. 
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
