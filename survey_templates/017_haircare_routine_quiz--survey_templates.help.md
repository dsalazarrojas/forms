# Haircare Routine Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Haircare Routine Quiz is designed to help us understand your hair characteristics and habits so we can provide you with personalized hair care recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, and choose the best option that describes your hair.
2. If a question does not apply to you, you can skip it.
3. Fill out as much or as little information as you need to.
4. When you have finished, click submit to save your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, Select One, Optional): Choose the type of hair you have, such as normal, wavy, or curly.
* **Hair Conditioner** (`hair_conditioner`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the conditions that apply to your hair, such as normal, dry, or damaged.
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, Select One, Optional): Choose the natural color of your hair, such as black, blonde, or red.
* **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the lengths that apply to your hair, such as short, medium, or long.
* **Hair Routine** (`routine`, Text, Optional): Describe your current hair care routine, including any products or practices you use.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, Time, Optional): If you have any follow-up questions or would like to schedule a follow-up, enter the date and time you would like to follow up.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, Date, Optional): If you would like to schedule a follow-up appointment, enter the date and time you would like to schedule.
* **Review** (`review`, Note, Optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, you can enter them here.
