# Baby Names Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your opinions and suggestions on baby names. We value your input and appreciate your participation in this research.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in its entirety or submit a partial response if you are unsure or need to leave.
- You can provide one or multiple name suggestions per form.
- Each name suggestion requires a brief description or explanation.
- Be honest about your preferences and opinions.
- Submit the form to share your thoughts!

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **<label>Help us choose a name for our baby!** (`title`, note, required: false): A brief note about the purpose of this survey. 
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required: false): Optional field for you to identify yourself.
* **First Name Suggestion** (`name_suggestion_1`, text, required: true): Enter a name that you think would be great for our baby.
* **Best Suited For** (`baby_gender_preference`, select_one, required: true): Choose whether you think the name is best suited for a boy, girl, or is neutral.
* **How much do you love this name?** (`rating_suggestion_1`, number, required: true): Rate how much you like the name, from 1 to 5.
* **Do you know the meaning of this name?** (`name_meaning`, text, required: false): If you are familiar with the meaning of the name, provide it here.
* **Second Name Suggestion** (`name_suggestion_2`, text, required: false): Optional second name suggestion.
* **What style of name do you prefer?** (`name_style`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply from Classic and Traditional, Modern and Trendy, Unique and Rare, Nature-inspired, or Family names.
* **Any middle name ideas?** (`middle_name_ideas`, text, required: false): Suggest any middle name ideas you have.
* **Why do you think these names fit us?** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Explain why you think these names fit our criteria.
