# dissertation_data_collection_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from participants and researchers involved in various studies, ensuring a clear and comprehensive data collection process. It gathers data on participant and researcher details, study specifics, and agreements to facilitate a smooth and accurate record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant Information, including title and contact details.
2. Provide Study Details, including research study titles and dates.
3. Indicate your consent and participant agreement to participate in the study.
4. Enter researcher contact information, including email and title.
5. Specify any other research studies you're involved in.
6. Add any necessary notes for participants and researchers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your title and contact details to be recorded in the data collection process.
* **Study Details** (`study_details`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a clear and concise description of the study, including title, start and end dates.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select one, optional):
	+ Indicate your consent to participate in the study. Select "True" to confirm your agreement.
* **Research Studies** (`research_studies`, select multiple, optional):
	+ List the research studies you are involved in. Select "Research Study 1," "Research Study 2," or type "Other (please specify)" and enter the study title.
* **Participant Contact** (`participant_contact`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your title and contact details for the participant.
* **Researcher Contact** (`researcher_contact`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address for researcher contact.
* **Study Start Date** (`study_start_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the start date of the study.
* **Study End Date** (`study_end_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the end date of the study.
* **Researcher Institution** (`researcher_institution`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your institution or affiliation for the researcher.
* **Participant Institution** (`participant_institution`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your institution or affiliation for the participant.
* **Study Location** (`study_location`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the location of the study.
* **Researcher Title** (`researcher_title`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your title or position for the researcher.
* **Participant Title** (`participant_title`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your title or position for the participant.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional notes or comments for the participant.
* **Researcher Notes** (`researcher_notes`, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional notes or comments for the researcher.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select one, optional):
	+ Indicate your agreement to participate in the study. Select "True" to confirm your agreement.
* **Researcher Agreement** (`researcher_agreement`, select one, optional):
	+ Indicate your agreement to conduct the study. Select "True" to confirm your agreement.
* **Participant Declaration** (`participant_declaration`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any declaration or statement you'd like to make as a participant.
* **Researcher Declaration** (`researcher_declaration`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any declaration or statement you'd like to make as a researcher.
* **Other Research Studies** (`research_studies_other`, text, optional):
	+ List any other research studies you're involved in, if not already listed.
