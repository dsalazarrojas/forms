# Sports League Administrative Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports League Administrative Efficiency Survey is designed to help sports league administrators gather insights into their performance, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance their operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to understand its purpose and relevance.
2. Provide honest and accurate answers to each question.
3. Ensure you answer all questions, as some are marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **League Administration** (`admin_efficiency`, `text`, required: false):
This field is for general information about your league's administration processes. Please provide a brief description of your current administration procedures.
* **Communication** (`communication`, `text`, required: false):
This field is for feedback on your communication strategies within the league. Share your thoughts on how well you communicate with your staff, players, and other stakeholders.
* **Staff Resources** (`staff_resources`, `text`, required: false):
Here, describe your current staff structure and resource allocation for the league. This will help you assess the balance between human resources and administrative tasks.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false):
Share any constructive feedback on areas where the league can improve its administrative efficiency. Be specific about what works well and what doesn't.

## Tips
- Take your time to answer each question thoughtfully.
- Be as specific as possible when describing your processes and experiences.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and consistency.

By following this guide, you'll be able to provide valuable insights into your sports league's administrative efficiency and make informed decisions to improve your operations.
