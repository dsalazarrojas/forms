# Judging Workshop Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants to improve the judging process. Your input is crucial in helping us understand how to make the process better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply read each section carefully and answer the questions according to your honest feedback. You don't have to complete every question, as most are optional. If you have any comments or recommendations, please feel free to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Welcome to the feedback survey** (Introduction, note, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
- **How would you rate the judging workshop** (Rating, number, required: false): Rate your experience with the workshop from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest. Be honest!
- **What comments do you have** (Comments, text, required: false): Share any thoughts, observations, or suggestions you think would be helpful to us.
- **Any recommendations or suggestions** (Recommendations, text, required: false): If you have any ideas for improving the judging process, please share them with us.
- **How did you like the judging idea** (Rating for the judging idea, number, required: false): Rate how much you liked the judging idea, from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

## Tips
- Please be as honest as possible when providing feedback.
- You can skip any questions that you don't feel comfortable answering.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
- Your input is greatly appreciated!
