# Client Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with our service or product. Your input is valuable to us, and we would love to hear about your thoughts, suggestions, and opinions. Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose how you would prefer us to contact you.
3. Describe the service or product you received in the "Service or Product Received" field.
4. Enter the date of your transaction.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction, quality of service, value for money, staff courtesy, and timeliness.
6. Answer "yes" or "no" questions about returning and recommending our service.
7. Share what you liked most about your experience in the "What Did You Like Most?" field.
8. Tell us about areas where we could improve in "What Could We Improve?".
9. Add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, required): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **How Would You Prefer We Contact You?** (`contact_method`, optional): Choose how you would like us to contact you: Email, Phone, Text message, or Not required.
* **Service or Product Received** (`service_received`, required): Describe the service or product you received.
* **Date of Transaction** (`transaction_date`, optional): Enter the date of your transaction.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Quality of Service** (`service_quality`, required): Rate the quality of our service: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, required): Rate the value of our service for you: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very poor.
* **Staff Courtesy and Professionalism** (`staff_courtesy`, required): Rate the courtesy and professionalism of our staff: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor.
* **Timeliness of Service** (`timeliness`, required): Rate the timeliness of our service: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor.
* **Would You Use Our Services Again?** (`would_return`, required): Answer yes or no if you would return to our service.
* **Would You Recommend Us to Others?** (`would_recommend`, required): Answer yes or no if you would recommend our service to others.
* **What Did You Like Most?** (`positive_aspects`, optional): Share what you liked most about your experience.
* **What Could We Improve?** (`improvement_areas`, optional): Tell us about areas where we could improve.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions.
