# Pet Product Marketing Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the effectiveness of a marketing campaign for pet products. It's crucial that you answer these questions honestly and accurately to provide valuable insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, carefully read each question and provide your honest responses. For select fields, choose the option that best represents your answer. For numerical fields, enter the exact number that applies to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Pet Product Name** (`pet_product_name`, text, **Required**): 
Enter the name of the pet product you are currently using or have used in the past.

### 2. **Type of Pet** (`pet_type`, select_one, **Required**): 
Choose the type of pet you own or care for (cat, dog, or other).

### 3. **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, number, **Required**): 
Enter the age of your pet.

### 4. **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, select_multiple, **Required**): 
Select all the marketing channels you've encountered or seen while buying or using pet products (Social Media, Email, Print, Online Advertising).

### 5. **Customer Awareness** (`customer_awareness`, number, **Required**): 
Enter the level of awareness you have about pet products (on a scale).

### 6. **Referral Intent** (`referral_intent`, select_one, **Required**): 
Choose whether you have referred or plan to refer others to pet products (True or False).

### 7. **Customer Referral Amount** (`customer_referral_amount`, number, **Required**): 
Enter the number of people you've referred or plan to refer to pet products.

### 8. **Campaign Impact** (`campaign_impact`, number, **Optional**): 
Enter the level of impact you've experienced from the pet marketing campaign (if applicable).

### 9. **Customer Awareness Scale** (`customer_awareness_scale`, select_one, **Required**): 
Select how aware you are of pet products (Yes or No).

### 10. **Customer Awareness Score** (`customer-awareness_score`, number, **Required**): 
Enter a score reflecting your awareness level of pet products.

### 11. **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, select_multiple, **Optional**): 
Select all the sources where you've seen or encountered pet product referrals (Social Media, Email, Online Advertising).

### 12. **Satisfied** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_multiple, **Required**): 
Choose how satisfied you are with pet products (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Not Satisfied).

Tips:
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Select only what applies to your situation.
- If unsure, refer to the provided options or choose 'Other' for select fields.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the relevant team for assistance.
