# health_program_self_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "health_program_self_evaluation_form" is used to gather feedback from participants in a health program. It is designed to be filled out by individuals who have participated in the program to provide their perspective and assessment of the program's effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Review each field carefully and fill out the required fields before proceeding.
3. Make sure to save your work regularly as you complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction (1)**: A brief note that introduces the form and provides context for the questions that follow.
* **participant_info (2)**: Enter a brief description about yourself and your participation in the program.
* **program_participation (3)**: Select one of the following options:
	+ **Yes**: You participated in the program.
	+ **No**: You did not participate in the program.
	+ **Maybe**: You are unsure if you participated in the program.
* **wellness_clinics (4)**: Select all the clinics you visited or interacted with during the program.
* **program_topics (5)**: Enter a brief description of the topics you covered in the program.
* **program_topics_2 (6)**: Select all the topics you are interested in or would like to learn more about.
* **rating (7)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-10.
* **overall_satisfaction (8)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-10.
* **additional_comments (9)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the program.
* **Submit (10)**: Select this option to submit your feedback.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
* This form should only be completed by individuals who have participated in the program.
