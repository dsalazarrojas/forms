# Consumer Pulse Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from consumers on their overall satisfaction, brand perception, and experience with our products and services. It is intended to provide insights that will help us improve our offerings and better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions to the best of your abilities.
2. Ensure you have the required information before proceeding.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please select "I don't know" or "Not Applicable".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent ID** (`respondent_id`, text, Optional): Enter your identification number, if available. This will help us keep track of your response for future reference.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, Required): Enter the date you completed this survey.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, Required): Select the age range that best describes you.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, Optional): Select your gender. If you prefer not to select, please select "Prefer Not to Say".
* **Location** (`location`, text, Optional): Enter your city and state.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our brand.
* **Brand Perception** (`brand_perception`, select_one, Required): Rate how you perceive our brand.
* **Product Quality** (`product_quality`, select_one, Required): Rate the quality of our products.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, select_one, Required): Rate the quality of our customer service.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, Required): Rate the value of our products or services.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_recommend`, select_one, Required): How likely are you to recommend our brand to others?
* **Recent Experience** (`recent_experience`, text, Optional): Describe your recent experience with our brand.
* **Purchase Intention** (`purchase_intent`, select_one, Required): How likely are you to purchase from our brand in the next 3 months?
* **Trust Level** (`trust_level`, select_one, Required): How much do you trust our brand?
* **Communication Rating** (`communication_rating`, select_one, Optional): Rate our communication efforts.
* **Website Experience** (`website_experience`, select_one, Optional): Rate your experience on our website.
* **Social Media Engagement** (`social_media_engagement`, select_one, Optional): Do you follow us on social media?
* **Improvement Priorities** (`improvement_priorities`, select_multiple, Optional): Select what aspects of our brand need most improvement.
* **Top Satisfaction Driver** (`top_satisfaction_driver`, select_one, Required): What matters most to you when making a purchase?
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, Optional): Any other thoughts or comments about our brand?

## Tips
- Please answer all questions truthfully to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any question, please select "I don't know" or "Not Applicable".
- This survey is designed to be a snapshot of your current feelings and perceptions, and is intended to inform business decisions and product development.
- If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the "Additional Feedback" field.
