# Favorite Holiday Tree Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their favorite holiday trees. It is intended for a voting or poll purpose, where users can provide their input and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Holiday Tree Voting Name and Enter Name.
2. Enter your email address in the Holiday Tree Voting Email field.
3. Answer the Are you eligible question by selecting either 'True' or 'False'.
4. Optionally, provide comments in the Comments field.
5. Select either 'I'm not a robot' or 'I'm a robot' in the Captcha field.
6. Finally, click on the Submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Tree Voting Name**: Enter the name of the holiday tree voting you are participating in.
* **Holiday Tree Voting Email**: Enter your email address associated with the holiday tree voting.
* **Are you eligible**: Indicate if you are eligible to participate in the holiday tree voting. Select 'True' if you are eligible, and 'False' if you are not.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share about your favorite holiday tree. This field is optional.
* **Captcha**: Select 'I'm not a robot' if you are a human, and 'I'm a robot' if you are not. This is to prevent automated form submissions.
* **Enter Name**: Enter your name. This is the name that will be displayed next to your vote.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete and submit your form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are having trouble accessing the form, try clearing your browser's cache or using a different browser.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
