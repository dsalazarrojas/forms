# Bed And Breakfast Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from guests about their stay at the bed and breakfast, with a focus on various aspects of their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the date of check-in and check-out from the calendar provided.
2. Enter your name, if you choose to do so.
3. Rate the cleanliness and condition of the room and common areas, comfort, quality of breakfast, staff friendliness, and location.
4. Provide detailed feedback about your stay.
5. Select whether or not you would recommend the bed and breakfast to others.
6. Enter the review submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Stay Details
* **Section 1: -- Stay Details --**: This section is a note that serves as an introduction to the form.
### Guest Name
* **Guest Name**: Optional - if you wish to share your name, enter it here. If not, you can leave it blank.
### Stay Dates
* **Check-in Date**: Select the date you arrived at the bed and breakfast.
* **Check-out Date**: Select the date you departed from the bed and breakfast.
### Room Information
* **Room Name or Number**: Enter the name or number of the room you stayed in.
### Experience Ratings
* **Section 2: -- Experience Ratings --**: This section is a note that serves as a header for the ratings.
### Cleanliness Rating
* **Cleanliness of Room and Common Areas**: Rate the cleanliness of your room and the common areas from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
### Comfort Rating
* **Bed and Room Comfort**: Rate the comfort of your room from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
### Breakfast Rating
* **Quality and Selection of Breakfast**: Rate the quality and selection of the breakfast served from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
### Staff Rating
* **Hospitality and Staff Friendliness**: Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of the staff from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
### Location Rating
* **Location and Ease of Access**: Rate the location and ease of access to the bed and breakfast from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
### Detailed Feedback
* **Section 3: -- Detailed Feedback --**: This section is a note that serves as a header for the detailed feedback.
### Highlight of Stay
* **What was the highlight of your stay?**: Enter the highlight of your stay.
### Areas for Improvement
* **Any areas where we could improve?**: Enter any areas that the bed and breakfast could improve on.
### Recommendation Likelihood
* **How likely are you to recommend us to others?**: Rate how likely you are to recommend the bed and breakfast to others on a scale from 0 to 10.
### Intent to Return
* **Would you stay with us again?**: Select whether or not you would stay at the bed and breakfast again.
### Review Date
* **Review Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this review.
