# Municipal Policy Public Opinion Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Municipal Policy Public Opinion Survey Form successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have enough time to complete it without feeling rushed.
2. Read each question carefully and answer honestly, based on your experiences and opinions.
3. If you need help with any question or field, you can refer to this guide for further clarification.
4. Complete the form in one sitting, if possible, to ensure your responses are consistent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Feedback** (`resident_feedback`, text, optional): Please share your thoughts, suggestions, and comments about municipal policies and services.
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to indicate your experience with the following services:
	+ Yes: You have used the service
	+ No: You have not used the service
* **Priorities** (`priorities`, text, optional): Share your top priorities for municipal policies and services.
* **Demographics** (`demographics`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following demographics that best describes you:
	+ Yes: You identify as part of this demographic
	+ No: You do not identify as part of this demographic
* **Policy Priorities** (`policy_priorities`, text, optional): Share your top policy priorities for the municipality.
* **Municipal Services** (`municipal_services`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to indicate your experience with the following municipal services:
	+ Yes: You have used the service
	+ No: You have not used the service
* **Public Input** (`public_input`, note, optional): This is a free-text field for any additional information or comments you'd like to share.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Please provide your name and any other contact information you'd like to share, so we can get back to you.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): If you'd like to receive updates or have any follow-up questions, please enter your email address here.
* **Community Groups** (`community_groups`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to indicate your membership or involvement with the following community groups:
	+ Yes: You are a member or involved with this community group
	+ No: You are not a member or involved with this community group
