# Cybersecurity Awareness Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Awareness Subscription Form is used to collect information from customers who want to subscribe to our cybersecurity awareness program. This form ensures that customers do not duplicate their subscription, and it helps our team to understand who is subscribed and for what purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, indicated by asterisks (\*) in their labels.
2. Make sure to enter your First Name, Email, and Company Name correctly.
3. If you have a valid Phone Number, please enter it for better communication.
4. Fill out your Job Title for our records.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1)
• **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email (2)
• **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications.

### Phone Number (3)
• **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number for better communication and in case of emergencies.

### Company Name (4)
• **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of your company.

### Job Title (5)
• **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position in your company.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid errors in our records.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
