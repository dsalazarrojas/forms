## Auto Repair Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the fields of the Auto Repair Feedback Survey, designed to help us better understand your experience and provide valuable feedback on our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. If you're unsure what to write, refer to the help text for guidance. If you need further assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (Customer Name)** (`customer_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Repair Order Number (repair_order_number)** (`repair_order_number`, text, **optional**): If available, please provide the number found on your invoice.
* **Date of Service (date_of_service)** (`date_of_service`, date, **required**): Enter the date when the repair was performed.
* **Vehicle Serviced (vehicle_serviced)** (`vehicle_serviced`, text, **required**): Enter the year, make, and model of your vehicle.
* **Quality of Repair (quality_of_repair)** (`quality_of_repair`, select_one, **required**): Rate the quality of the repair, using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Timeliness of Service (timeliness_of_service)** (`timeliness_of_service`, select_one, **required**): Select the correct option for how the work was completed: Ahead of Schedule, On Time, or Delayed.
* **Advisor Communication (advisor_communication)** (`advisor_communication`, select_one, **required**): Rate how well your advisor kept you informed, using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Value for Price (value_for_price)** (`value_for_price`, select_one, **required**): Assess the balance of cost and quality using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What we did Well (what_well)** (`what_well`, text, **optional**): Share your positive experiences with our service.
* **Areas for Improvement (areas_improvement)** (`areas_improvement`, text, **optional**): Provide suggestions on how we can serve you better.
* **Likely to Return (likely_to_return)** (`likely_to_return`, select_one, **required**): Select how likely you are to return for services: Very Likely, Likely, Unlikely, or Not at all.
* **May We Contact You (may_contact)** (`may_contact`, select_one, **required**): Select if you're willing to allow us to contact you to discuss your feedback further: True or False.

Please fill out this form as thoroughly and accurately as possible, and we will use your feedback to make necessary adjustments to improve our service. Thank you for taking the time to provide valuable feedback!
