# Entry Filter Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Entry Filter Survey form is used to collect and track information about entry filter surveys. This form is designed to gather data on various aspects of the survey process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, starting from the "Program Name" field.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, such as "Yes" or "No" for select fields.
3. Enter date and time information in the respective fields.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
5. Ensure that you have selected the correct options for each field accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program being surveyed.
* **research_area** (`research_area`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey is related to research, "No" otherwise.
* **contact_method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if the contact method is used, "No" otherwise.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address associated with the survey.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number associated with the survey.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the survey.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the survey.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the survey.
* **team_member** (`team_member`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey is related to a team member, "No" otherwise.
* **team_members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey is related to multiple team members, "No" otherwise.
* **priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey has high priority, "No" otherwise.
* **submission_type** (`submission_type`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey is a submission type, "No" otherwise.
* **submission_status** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional): Select "Active" if the survey is active, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **submission_date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the submission date of the survey.
* **submission_time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the submission time of the survey.
* **submission_note** (`submission_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the submission.
* **team_lead** (`team_lead`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey is related to a team lead, "No" otherwise.
* **team_leads** (`team_leads`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey is related to multiple team leads, "No" otherwise.
* **program_lead** (`program_lead`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey is related to a program lead, "No" otherwise.
* **program_leads** (`program_leads`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey is related to multiple program leads, "No" otherwise.
* **submission_id** (`submission_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique submission ID of the survey.
* **created_by** (`created_by`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey was created by the user, "No" otherwise.
* **created_by_team** (`created_by_team`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey was created by a team, "No" otherwise.
* **updated_by** (`updated_by`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey was updated by the user, "No" otherwise.
* **updated_by_team** (`updated_by_team`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the survey was updated by a team, "No" otherwise.
