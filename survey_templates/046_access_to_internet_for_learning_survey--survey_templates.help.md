# Access To Internet For Learning Survey - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select only the options that apply to you.
3. If you have additional comments or feedback, feel free to share them in the optional "Any other comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Grade Level**: Select your current grade level of schooling, whether Elementary, Middle School, High School, or College/University.
* **Primary Device for Schoolwork**: Describe your primary device you use for school-related activities, e.g., Laptop, Tablet, Smartphone, or Desktop.
* **Reliable Internet Access at Home**: Indicate how often you have reliable internet access at home, e.g., Always, Most of the time, Sometimes, or Never.
* **Primary Internet Connection Type**: Choose the type of internet connection you use at home, such as High-speed Fiber or Cable, DSL, Mobile Hotspot, or Satellite.
* **Internet Speed**: Describe your experience with internet speed, e.g., Fast, Sufficient for basic tasks, Slow, or Very Slow.
* **Average Hours Spent Online**: Estimate the average hours you spend online for schoolwork each day.
* **Online Learning Activities**: Select all the activities you perform online for school, including Watching Video Lectures, Attending Live Classes, Researching Projects, Submitting Assignments, and Collaborative Writing.
* **Impact of Slow Internet**: Indicate how often slow internet prevents you from completing schoolwork, e.g., Daily, Weekly, Rarely, or Never.
* **Alternative Locations for Internet Access**: If you don't have internet at home, select other locations where you access the internet, such as Public Library, School or Campus, Friends or Relatives Home, or Coffee Shop/Public Space.
* **Interest in Low-Cost Internet Programs**: If you're interested in programs offering low-cost internet, select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Additional Comments**: Feel free to share any other comments or feedback about your internet access for learning.
