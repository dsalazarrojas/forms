# Brand Perception Analysis Customer Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your perception of our brand and help us understand how we can improve our relationship with you, our customers. By participating in this survey, you will be contributing to the development of our brand and helping us create a better experience for everyone. Please answer the questions as honestly as possible to ensure you're not duplicating any existing data and that the responses are relevant to the survey questions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions based on your genuine opinions.
2. Fill in the required fields for each section.
3. Make sure to select one option per question, even if it doesn't perfectly fit your answer.
4. Enter any additional comments in the "One thing we could change to improve your perception of us?" field, if you'd like to provide more detailed feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Group**: This question helps us understand your age range, so we can tailor our marketing strategies to better suit your needs.
* **How often do you buy from us?**: Please select how often you buy from us, so we can understand your purchase frequency.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group to help us understand your demographics.
* **When you think of our industry, is our brand the first that comes to your mind?**: This question checks if our brand is prominent in your thoughts when thinking about our industry.
* **Which other brand do you consider our main competitor?**: This question will help us compare our brand to others in the same market.
* **How reliable do you find our brand?**: Choose an option that best describes your perception of our reliability.
* **Does our brand feel more traditional or modern?**: Select how modern or traditional you think our brand is.
* **If our brand was a person, how would you describe their personality?**: This is a fun question to understand our brand persona.
* **How likely are you to recommend us to a colleague?**: Please choose a score between 0 and 10 to rate your willingness to recommend us.
* **One thing we could change to improve your perception of us?**: This is an open-ended question to gather your suggestions on how we can improve.
* **Date of Survey**: This is a system-generated field to record the date you completed the survey.

Note: The date field will be filled in automatically, but please make sure to answer all other questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
