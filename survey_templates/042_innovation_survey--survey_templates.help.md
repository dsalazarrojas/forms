# innovation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a voluntary survey to collect information from employees and managers about innovation and communication within the team. It is not mandatory, so you can choose to complete it or not.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the first page "Employee Survey" to answer questions about innovation and communication within the team.
2. Select the options that best fit your answers for each question.
3. Enter your answers for the questions in the "Answers" field.
4. Repeat steps 1-3 for the second page "Questions", selecting options and entering answers as needed.
5. On the third page "Manager Comments", enter any comments or feedback you have.
6. On the fourth page "Questions for Manager", select options for what questions you think the manager should answer.
7. Repeat steps 5-6 for the remaining pages with similar fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey** (`Employee Survey`, `note`, optional): This page asks you about innovation and communication within the team. Answer the questions that best fit your experiences and opinions.
* **Questions** (`Questions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the options that best fit your answers for each question.
* **Answers** (`Answers`, `note`, optional): Enter your answers for the questions on this page.
* **Manager Comments** (`Manager Comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have for the manager.
* **Questions for Manager** (`Questions for Manager`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the options that you think the manager should answer.
* **Answers for Manager** (`Answers for Manager`, `note`, optional): Enter answers for the questions on this page.
* **Employee Comments** (`Employee Comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the form or the questions.
* **Manager Comments for Employees** (`Manager Comments for Employees`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the options that you think should be answered by employees.
* **Questions for Employees** (`Questions for Employees`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the options that you think employees should answer.
* **Answers for Employees** (`Answers for Employees`, `note`, optional): Enter answers for the questions on this page.
* **Employee Comments for Manager** (`Employee Comments for Manager`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have for the manager.
* **Questions for Employees Comments** (`Questions for Employees Comments`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the options that you think employees should answer.
* **Manager Comments for Employee** (`Manager Comments for Employee`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have for employees.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure the survey results are accurate.
* Select the options that best fit your answers for each question.
* Enter your answers clearly and concisely to help others understand your thoughts.
* Be honest and respectful in your comments and feedback.
