# gym_class_cancellation_policy_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from gym members about their experiences with cancelling classes at the gym. Your responses will help us to understand the reasons and frequency of cancellations, which will enable us to improve our services and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully to understand the purpose and scope of the form.
2. Answer each question based on your actual experience.
3. Be as specific as possible when providing your answers.
4. If you need to provide additional comments, use the "Additional Comments" section.
5. Finally, provide your email and phone number in case we need to contact you for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section is a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Cancellation Reason**: This question asks you to choose the reason why you cancelled your class. Please select the option that best describes your reason.
	+ **Cancellation due to personal reason**: If you cancelled your class because of personal reasons, such as health issues or scheduling conflicts.
	+ **Cancellation due to business reason**: If you cancelled your class because of business-related reasons, such as work or travel.
	+ **Other**: If none of the above options apply to you, please select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
* **Cancellation Date**: This question asks for the date you cancelled your class.
* **Cancellation Time**: This question asks for the time you cancelled your class.
* **Cancellation Rule**: This question asks you to describe the cancellation rule that applies to your cancellation.
* **Cancellation Awareness**: This question asks you to indicate whether you were aware of the cancellation rule before cancelling your class. Please select all that apply.
	+ **Aware of cancellation rule**: If you were aware of the cancellation rule before cancelling your class.
	+ **Aware of consequences**: If you were aware of the consequences of cancelling your class.
	+ **Not aware**: If you were not aware of the cancellation rule or consequences.
* **Satisfaction**: This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the cancellation policy.
	+ **Very dissatisfied**: If you are very dissatisfied with the cancellation policy.
	+ **Somewhat dissatisfied**: If you are somewhat dissatisfied with the cancellation policy.
	+ **Neutral**: If you are neutral about the cancellation policy.
	+ **Somewhat satisfied**: If you are somewhat satisfied with the cancellation policy.
	+ **Very satisfied**: If you are very satisfied with the cancellation policy.
* **Additional Comments**: This section is for any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **Cancellation Frequency**: This question asks you to indicate how often you cancel your classes.
	+ **Less than once a month**: If you do not cancel your classes more than once a month.
	+ **About once a month**: If you cancel your classes approximately once a month.
	+ **About once a week**: If you cancel your classes approximately once a week.
	+ **More than once a week**: If you cancel your classes more than once a week.
* **Email**: This is your email address, which we will use to contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone Number**: This is your phone number, which we will use to contact you if we need more information.
* **Member ID**: This is your member ID, which is not required but will help us to identify you in our records.
