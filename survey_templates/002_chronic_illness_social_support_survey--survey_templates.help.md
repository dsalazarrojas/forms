This help guide is for the "Chronic Illness Social Support Survey" form. This form is designed to gather information about individuals' experiences with chronic illness and their perceived level of social support. The purpose of this form is to understand how chronic illness affects social connections and relationships, and to identify areas where individuals may need more support. This form will ask about demographics, types of support received, barriers to getting support, and other relevant questions.

This form will not ask for any sensitive or protected information, and all questions are designed to be clear and concise. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.

This form is intended to be completed by individuals with chronic illness, and all responses will be kept anonymous and confidential. If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Purpose
The Chronic Illness Social Support Survey is designed to gather information about your experiences with chronic illness and your perceived level of social support. This survey aims to understand how chronic illness affects social connections and relationships, and to identify areas where individuals may need more support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. For select questions, choose the response that best describes your situation.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.
