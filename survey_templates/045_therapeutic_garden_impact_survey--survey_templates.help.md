# Therapeutic Garden Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is used to gather information about the impact of a therapeutic garden on patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the answer that best describes your experience with the garden visit.
2. If prompted, select all applicable outcomes that describe your well-being.
3. Provide your feedback about your experience with the garden.
4. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.
5. Enter the name of the hospital where you visited the garden.
6. Enter the name of the department within the hospital where you visited the garden.
7. Enter the name of the staff member who facilitated the garden visit.
8. Enter the staff member's position or title.
9. Enter the date and time of your garden visit.
10. Select whether the garden is used for a specific therapeutic area.
11. Enter the name of the program or initiative that the garden is part of.
12. Enter the date of the evaluation or assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Garden Visit**: Select the answer that best describes your experience with the garden visit. This field is required.
* **Well Being Outcomes**: Select all applicable outcomes that describe your well-being after visiting the garden. These outcomes are required.
	+ This field is intended to gather information about the participant's emotional or psychological state.
* **Participant Feedback**: Provide your feedback about your experience with the garden. This field is optional.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for follow-up. This field is optional.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for follow-up. This field is optional.
* **Hospital Name**: Enter the name of the hospital where you visited the garden. This field is optional.
* **Hospital Department**: Enter the name of the department within the hospital where you visited the garden. This field is optional.
* **Staff Name**: Enter the name of the staff member who facilitated the garden visit. This field is optional.
* **Staff Position**: Enter the staff member's position or title. This field is optional.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your garden visit. This field is required.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your garden visit. This field is required.
* **Therapeutic Area**: Select whether the garden is used for a specific therapeutic area. This field is optional.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program or initiative that the garden is part of. This field is optional.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation or assessment. This field is required.
* **Garden Size**: Select all applicable sizes that describe the garden. This field is optional.
* **Garden Description**: Enter a brief description of the garden. This field is optional.

Note: This form is designed to be user-friendly and intuitive, but if you are unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification.
