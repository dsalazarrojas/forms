# Quarterly Business Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your quarterly business performance, including client feedback, goal alignment, and next steps for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the client whose review you are completing.
2.  Provide feedback on their performance.
3.  Align the goals for the next quarter.
4.  Outline the next steps for improvement.
5.  Enter the review date and time.
6.  Add any additional notes or comments.
7.  Enter the contact email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client** (`client_review`, `select_one`): Select the client whose review you are completing.
*   **Provide Feedback** (`feedback_question`, `text`): Enter any feedback or comments about the client's performance.
*   **Alignment Goals** (`goal_alignment`, `select_multiple`): Select the goals that are being aligned for the next quarter.
*   **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`): Outline the next steps or actions that will be taken to improve performance.
*   **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`): Enter the date of the review.
*   **Review Time** (`review_time`, `time`): Enter the time of the review.
*   **Notes** (`note`, `note`): Add any additional notes or comments about the review.
*   **Contact Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter the contact email for further communication.
*   **Contact Phone** (`phone`, `text`): Enter the contact phone number for further communication.
*   **Review Status** (`review_status`, `select_one`): Select the status of the review (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
*   **Review Period** (`review_period`, `select_one`): Select the review period (Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4).
*   **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, `select_multiple`): Select the frequency of reviews (Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly).
*   **Review Type** (`review_type`, `select_one`): Select the type of review (Online, In-Person, or Group).

## Tips

*   Make sure to select the correct client and review period for accurate tracking.
*   Keep the feedback and next steps clear and concise.
*   Use the correct format for dates and times to ensure easy tracking.
*   Use the email and phone number for further communication or follow-up.

Remember to review the form carefully and provide accurate and complete information to ensure a successful quarterly business review.
