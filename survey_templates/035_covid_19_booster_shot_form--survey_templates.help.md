# COVID 19 Booster Shot Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from patients prior to their COVID-19 booster shot appointment. The goal is to ensure that medical staff has the necessary information to prepare for your visit and provide you with the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
2. Make sure to answer all the required questions.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the medical staff.
4. Once you have completed the form, submit it to the medical staff before your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Required)
	+ Full legal name of the patient.
* **Date of Birth** (Required)
	+ Date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Gender** (Required)
	+ Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Email Address** (Required)
	+ Enter your email address for appointment confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Required)
	+ Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Street Address** (Required)
	+ Enter your complete address for scheduling purposes.
* **City** (Required)
	+ Enter the city of your residence.
* **State** (Required)
	+ Enter the state or province where you live.
* **ZIP Code** (Required)
	+ Enter your postal code.
* **Insurance Provider** (Optional)
	+ If you have an insurance provider, enter their name.
* **Insurance ID** (Optional)
	+ Enter your member ID number if you have an insurance provider.
* **Primary Care Physician** (Optional)
	+ Enter your primary care physician's name.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination History** (Required)
	+ Select your vaccination history from the options provided.
* **First Dose Date** (Optional)
	+ Enter the date of your first COVID-19 vaccine dose if applicable.
* **Second Dose Date** (Optional)
	+ Enter the date of your second COVID-19 vaccine dose if applicable.
* **Previous Vaccination Location** (Optional)
	+ Enter the location where you received your previous vaccines.
* **Booster Eligibility** (Required)
	+ Select your eligibility status based on time since last dose.
* **High Risk Condition** (Required)
	+ Select if you have any high-risk medical conditions.
* **High Risk Condition Details** (Optional)
	+ If yes, specify your high-risk condition.
* **High Risk Occupation** (Required)
	+ Select your high-risk occupation.
* **Previous COVID-19 Infection** (Required)
	+ Select if you have had a previous COVID-19 infection.
* **Infection Date** (Optional)
	+ Enter the date of your previous COVID-19 infection.
* **Current COVID-19 Symptoms** (Required)
	+ Select your current symptoms status.
* **Vaccine Preference** (Required)
	+ Select your preferred vaccine type for the booster dose.
* **Reasons for Getting Booster** (Required)
	+ Select your reasons for getting the booster dose.
* **Concerns About Booster** (Optional)
	+ If you have any concerns or worries about the booster dose, enter them here.
* **Previous Vaccine Side Effects** (Required)
	+ Select your experience with previous vaccine side effects.
* **Allergies** (Optional)
	+ If you have any known allergies, especially to vaccines, enter them here.
* **Appointment Preference** (Optional)
	+ Select your preferred appointment time.
* **Transportation Method** (Optional)
	+ Select how you plan to arrive at the appointment.
* **Post-Vaccination Plan** (Required)
	+ Select your plan for post-vaccination.
* **Consent for Vaccination** (Required)
	+ Select your consent status for the booster dose.
* **Patient Signature** (Required)
	+ Type your full name as the patient.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (Optional)
	+ If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's signature.
* **Date Signed** (Required)
	+ Enter the current date.

Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully, and if you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the medical staff.
