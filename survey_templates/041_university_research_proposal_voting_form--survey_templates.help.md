# University Research Proposal Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the evaluation and voting process for university research proposals. It collects general information, reviews proposal details, and allows users to vote on proposals. The form ensures that each proposal is evaluated based on specific criteria and that users provide feedback through comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information section with the proposal's title, research team, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the Research Proposal ID for the proposal being evaluated.
3. Provide a brief description of the Principal Investigator and their role in the research project.
4. List the members of the research team, including their names and roles.
5. Enter the Research Title and provide a brief description of the project.
6. Describe the Research Objectives, including the project's goals and expected outcomes.
7. Outline the Research Methodology, including the methods and procedures used.
8. Enter the estimated Research Budget and provide a breakdown of the costs.
9. Describe the Research Impact, including the expected benefits and outcomes.
10. Select the Review Criteria that will be used to evaluate the proposal.
11. Enter an Evaluation Scale, with a score between 1 and 10, to rate the proposal.
12. Select the Evaluation Result, either Pass or Fail, based on the evaluation.
13. Choose the Vote Option, either Approve or Reject, based on the evaluation.
14. Provide Review Comments on the proposal, including any concerns or suggestions.
15. Enter Additional Comments, any other relevant information or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`): Enter a brief description of the proposal, including its title, research team, and other relevant details.
* **Research Proposal ID** (`research_proposal_id`): Enter the unique identifier for the proposal being evaluated.
* **Principal Investigator** (`principal_investigator`): Describe the Principal Investigator and their role in the research project.
* **Research Team** (`research_team`): List the members of the research team, including their names and roles.
* **Research Title** (`research_title`): Enter a brief description of the project, including its objectives and expected outcomes.
* **Research Objectives** (`research_objectives`): Describe the project's goals and expected outcomes.
* **Research Methodology** (`research_methodology`): Outline the methods and procedures used in the project.
* **Research Budget** (`research_budget`): Enter the estimated budget for the project, including a breakdown of costs.
* **Research Impact** (`research_impact`): Describe the expected benefits and outcomes of the project.
* **Review Criteria** (`review_criteria`): Select the criteria that will be used to evaluate the proposal, from the options listed.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`): Enter a score between 1 and 10 to rate the proposal.
* **Evaluation Result** (`evaluation_result`): Select the evaluation result, either Pass or Fail, based on the evaluation.
* **Vote Option** (`vote_option`): Choose the vote option, either Approve or Reject, based on the evaluation.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`): Provide feedback on the proposal, including any concerns or suggestions.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any other relevant information or feedback.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language in your responses.
* Review and edit your responses carefully before submitting.
* Use the Review Criteria to guide your evaluation and voting process.
* Keep your Review Comments and Additional Comments concise and focused on the proposal's strengths and weaknesses.
