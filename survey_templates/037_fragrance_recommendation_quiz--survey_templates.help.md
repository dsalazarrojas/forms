# Fragrance Recommendation Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand your preferences and habits related to fragrances. It will ask you a series of questions about your scent preferences, frequency of use, budget, and other relevant factors to provide a personalized fragrance recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each question and select your answers from the provided options. You can choose as many or as few options as relevant to your preferences. If you're unsure or do not have an answer for a particular question, feel free to leave it blank. Once you've completed all the questions, click the submit button to get your personalized fragrance recommendation.
