# Consumer Feedback Event Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Consumer Feedback Event Attendance Form is designed to collect detailed feedback from attendees of events, conferences, or workshops. The data collected from this form will help event organizers improve their events, speakers, and overall attendee experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal details, such as **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email Address**, **Event Name**, **Event Date**, **Event Location**, **Attendance Type**, **Registration Status**, and **Overall Satisfaction**.
2. Answer the rating questions, such as **Content Quality**, **Speaker Quality**, **Venue Rating**, **Organization Rating**, and **Networking Opportunities**.
3. Select the **Sessions Attended** that you found valuable.
4. Write your thoughts on **Key Takeaways**, **Most Valuable Session**, **Areas for Improvement**, and **Future Topics** that you would like to see.
5. Answer the recommendation questions, including **Would You Recommend This Event** and **Would You Attend Future Events**.
6. Provide **Additional Comments** if you have any other feedback to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name (required)
* **Last Name**: Your last name (required)
* **Email Address**: Your email address for follow-up (required)
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number (optional)
* **Company Name**: Your organization (optional)
* **Job Title**: Your position (optional)
* **Event Name**: Name of the event attended (required)
* **Event Date**: Date of the event (required)
* **Event Location**: Where the event was held (required)
* **Attendance Type**: How you attended the event (required)
* **Registration Status**: Whether you were registered for the event (required)
* **Sessions Attended**: Select the sessions you found valuable (required)
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction (required)
* **Content Quality**: Rate the quality of content (required)
* **Speaker Quality**: Rate the speakers (required)
* **Venue Rating**: Rate the venue if you attended in-person (optional)
* **Organization Rating**: Rate the event organization (required)
* **Networking Opportunities**: Rate networking chances (optional)
* **Key Takeaways**: Write what you learned from the event (required)
* **Most Valuable Session**: Identify the most valuable session (optional)
* **Areas for Improvement**: Suggest areas for improvement (required)
* **Future Topics**: Suggest topics you would like to see in future events (optional)
* **Would Recommend This Event**: Select if you would recommend the event (required)
* **Would You Attend Future Events**: Select if you would attend again (required)
* **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback (optional)

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled in, while optional fields are not mandatory.
