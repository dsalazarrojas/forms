# Caravan Booking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a survey to help us understand your caravan travel preferences and habits. Your input will aid us in providing a better experience for you and your fellow caravan enthusiasts.

## How To Complete This Form
- Follow each step to fill out the form, and be as accurate as possible.
- For select_one questions, choose one option that best fits your preference.
- For select_multiple questions, choose all that apply.
- For select_all questions, choose all that apply.
- For number questions, enter a numerical value.
- For text questions, provide a brief answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your name** (full_name, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email address** (email_address, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone number** (phone_number, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Preferred travel season** (preferred_travel_season, select_one, required): Choose a season you typically travel in (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter, Year-round, No Preference).
* **Typical trip duration** (typical_trip_duration, select_one, required): Choose a duration for your typical trip (Weekend Getaway, 1 Week, 2 Weeks, 3 Weeks, 1 Month, Longer Than 1 Month, Varies).
* **How often do you travel by caravan?** (how_often_caravan_travel, select_one, required): Choose how often you travel by caravan (First Time, Rarely, Several Times a Year, Regularly, Very Frequently).
* **Caravan experience level** (caravan_experience, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of caravan driving experience (Beginner, Some Experience, Very Experienced, Expert Driver).
* **Number of travelers typically** (travel_companion_count, number, required): Enter the number of people traveling with you (e.g. 1, 2, 3).
* **Will children travel with you?** (children_traveling, select_one, optional): Indicate if children will be traveling with you (True, False, Sometimes).
* **Pet-friendly needed** (pet_friendly_needed, select_one, optional): Check if you need a pet-friendly caravan (True, False, Not Sure).
* **Preferred caravan size** (caravan_size, select_one, required): Choose the preferred size of the caravan (Small - Up to 18ft, Medium - 18-24ft, Large - 24-30ft, Extra Large - 30ft+, No Preference).
* **Minimum sleeping capacity needed** (sleeping_capacity, number, required): Enter the minimum number of people that need to sleep in the caravan.
* **Must-have amenities** (must_have_amenities, select_multiple, optional): Choose the amenities you must have in the caravan (Kitchen Facilities, Bathroom/Shower, Heating System, Air Conditioning, TV/Entertainment, WiFi, Solar Panels, Backup Generator).
* **Budget per night** (budget_range, select_one, required): Choose a budget range for your caravan stay (Under 50, 50-100, 100-150, 150-200, 200-300, 300+, Flexible).
* **Preferred destinations** (preferred_destinations, select_multiple, optional): Choose the types of destinations you prefer (Beach, Mountains, National Parks, Cities, Rural Areas, Countryside, Desert, Lakes, Campgrounds).
* **Domestic or international travel** (domestic_or_international, select_one, required): Choose where you prefer to travel (Domestic Only, International Only, Both, No Preference).
* **Maximum distance willing to travel** (distance_willing_travel, select_one, optional): Choose the maximum distance you are willing to travel for a caravan stay (Within 200 miles, Within 500 miles, Within 1000 miles, Across Country, International, No Limit).
* **Scenic priority** (scenic_priority, select_one, optional): Indicate the importance of scenic views to you (Very Important, Important, Somewhat Important, Not Important).
* **Advance booking preference** (advance_booking_preference, select_one, optional): Choose how far in advance you typically book (Last Minute, 1-2 Weeks, 1-3 Months, 3-6 Months, 6+ Months, Varies).
* **Additional information** (additional_information, text, optional): Enter any additional information or special requests you may have.
