# Route Efficiency Rider Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Route Efficiency Rider Inquiry Form is a tool used to collect feedback and data from riders on the efficiency of routes. This form helps us understand the experiences and perspectives of our riders, which is essential for identifying areas of improvement and providing better service. Please fill this form only once for each ride, and make sure to provide as much detail as possible to help us better understand your experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the feedback type you want to provide (Rider Feedback, Route Delay, Route Efficiency, or Rider Satisfaction).
2. Answer the Route Delay question with a note (text) explaining the delay you experienced.
3. Choose how you rate the Route Efficiency (Very Efficient, Somewhat Efficient, Somewhat Inefficient, or Very Inefficient) and Rider Satisfaction (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied).
4. Fill in the Rider Comments with any additional thoughts on your experience.
5. Enter your name in the Contact Name field.
6. Enter your email address in the Contact Email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the Contact Phone field.
8. Choose your department in the Contact Department field (e.g., Operations, Management, etc.).
9. Enter your title in the Contact Title field (e.g., Manager, Rider, etc.).
10. Enter the location you were at during the ride in the Contact Location field.
11. Provide any additional comments in the Additional Comments field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Route Feedback** (`route_efficiency`):
	+ Select this field to provide general feedback on your ride.
	+ Options: Route Delay, Route Efficiency, Rider Satisfaction
* **Route Delay** (`route_efficiency_1`):
	+ Answer this question with a note explaining the delay you experienced.
	+ Required field
* **Route Efficiency** (`route_efficiency_2`):
	+ Choose how you rate the efficiency of your ride.
	+ Options: Very Efficient, Somewhat Efficient, Somewhat Inefficient, Very Inefficient
	+ Required field
* **Rider Satisfaction** (`route_efficiency_3`):
	+ Choose how satisfied you were with your ride.
	+ Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied
	+ Required field
* **Rider Comments** (`route_efficiency_4`):
	+ Fill in any additional comments about your experience.
	+ Required field
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`):
	+ Enter the name of the contact who completed this form.
	+ Required field
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`):
	+ Enter the email address of the contact who completed this form.
	+ Required field
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`):
	+ Enter the phone number of the contact who completed this form.
	+ Required field
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`):
	+ Choose the department of the contact who completed this form.
	+ Required field
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`):
	+ Enter the title of the contact who completed this form.
	+ Required field
* **Contact Location** (`contact_location`):
	+ Enter the location where the contact was during the ride.
	+ Required field
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`):
	+ Provide any additional comments about your experience.
	+ Optional field
