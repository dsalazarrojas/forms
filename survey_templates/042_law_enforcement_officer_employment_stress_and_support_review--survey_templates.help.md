# law_enforcement_officer_employment_stress_and_support_review - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from law enforcement officers about their employment stress and support experiences to inform the development of support programs and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each field as prompted. You will be asked to select one or multiple options for several questions, and some questions will ask for free-text answers. Make sure to select all applicable options and provide clear and concise answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your job title?** (`job_title`, select_one): This question is asking for your official title or position within the agency.
* **How many years of service do you have?** (`years_of_service`, number): This question is asking for the number of years you have served in law enforcement.
* **Which of the following stressors do you experience frequently in your work?** (`stressors`, select_multiple): The options for this question are:
	+ High stress of work
	+ Unclear goals
	+ Unrealistic expectations
	+ Lack of resources
* **What support resources do you have at work?** (`support_resources`, select_multiple): The options for this question are:
	+ EAP (Employee Assistance Program)
	+ ESO (Employee Support Organization)
	+ Peer support
	+ Supervisory support
	+ Employee assistance programs
* **Are you enrolled in our agency's wellness program?** (`wellness_programs`, select_one): This question is asking if you are enrolled in our agency's wellness program.
* **What factors affect your likelihood to leave the agency?** (`retention_factors`, select_multiple): The options for this question are:
	+ Work-life balance
	+ Career advancement opportunities
	+ Pay and benefits
	+ Work environment
	+ Other
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions?** (`open_ended_comments`, note): This is a free-text field for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Do you have any contact information you would like us to have?** (`contact_information`, text): This is a free-text field for you to provide any contact information you would like us to have.
* **Who is your emergency contact?** (`emergency_contact`, select_one): This question is asking for the contact information of an emergency contact.

**Tips**: Make sure to answer all fields truthfully and to the best of your ability. If you are unsure about any of the options, select all that apply or choose "Other" and provide a clear explanation.
