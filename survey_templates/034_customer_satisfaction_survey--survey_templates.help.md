# Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

This form is designed to collect customer feedback and satisfaction data. The purpose of this form is to gauge customer experience and identify areas for improvement in our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the option that best fits your experience.
3. For date and number fields, enter the date or number in the specified format.
4. For text fields, enter your thoughts, feelings, or comments in the space provided.
