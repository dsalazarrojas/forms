# Product Durability Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about the durability of a product, including their level of satisfaction and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the product you are providing feedback on.
2. Choose how satisfied you are with the product's durability.
3. Share any suggestions you have for improving the product.
4. Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Durability Feedback** (`product_durability_feedback_form`, text, required): Please provide a detailed account of your experience with the product, including any issues you've encountered and why you think it may not be durable.
* **Satisfaction Survey** (`satisfaction_survey`, select_one, required: false): Select the level of satisfaction you feel about the product's durability, using the options provided:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Not at all Satisfied
* **Product Improvement Suggestions** (`product_improvement_suggestions`, text, required: false): Share any suggestions you have for improving the product's durability.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how you would like to be contacted for follow-up:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Social Media
