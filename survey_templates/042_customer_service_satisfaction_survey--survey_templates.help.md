# Customer Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer service satisfaction survey is designed to help us understand how well we've met your expectations. Your feedback will help us improve our service quality, communication, and overall experience.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select your answer based on your honest opinion.
2. Rate your satisfaction with each question using the scale provided (e.g., 1-5).
3. Provide a brief description in the "What was the primary reason for your contact with us?" and "What was the primary issue or concern during your experience with our service?" text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with our service quality?** (`service_quality`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction with our service quality, where 1 means very dissatisfied and 5 means very satisfied.
* **How satisfied are you with our communication?** (`communication`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction with our communication, where 1 means very dissatisfied and 5 means very satisfied.
* **How would you rate your overall experience with our service?** (`overall_experience`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience with our service, where 1 means very poor and 5 means very good.
* **How satisfied are you with our service satisfaction?** (`service_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction with our service satisfaction, where 1 means very dissatisfied and 5 means very satisfied.
* **What was the primary reason for your contact with us?** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): Please provide a brief description of your contact with us, such as a phone call, email, or visit to our website.
* **What was the primary issue or concern during your experience with our service?** (`concern_or_issue`, `text`, required): Please provide a brief description of the issue or concern you experienced with our service, such as a problem with a product or a billing issue.
