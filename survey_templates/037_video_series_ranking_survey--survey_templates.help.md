# Video Series Ranking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your opinions and ratings on a video series. Your feedback will help us understand what you think about the series and provide a more accurate representation of user engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. If you want to be contacted for any reason, provide your email address in the "Email" field. If you don't want to provide your email, you can skip this field.
3. Answer the ranking questions in the "Ranking Questions" field by selecting up to 6 options that best fit your opinion.
4. If you want to leave a comment or message, type it in the "Message" field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`text`, required): Fill in your name.
* **Ranking Questions** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose up to 6 options that best fit your opinion on the video series.
	+ 1. Best episode overall
	+ 2. Favorite episode
	+ 3. Most memorable scene
	+ 4. Most surprising moment
	+ 5. Most quotable line
	+ 6. Favorite character development
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Fill in your email address if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Message** (`text`, optional): Leave a comment or message about the video series.
* **Submit** (`text`, required): Click this button to send your feedback.
