# Audio Production Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audio Production Skills Assessment is designed to evaluate your audio production skills and experience. Please answer the questions truthfully to help us understand your abilities and background in audio production.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer the options that best describe your skills and experience.
* For multiple-choice questions, select only one option that best fits your knowledge or proficiency level.
* For text fields, enter your answers clearly and concisely.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (*): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Years of Professional Audio Experience** (*): Please enter the number of years you have spent working in the audio production industry.
* **Primary Digital Audio Workstation (DAW)** (*): Select the software you use most frequently for audio production. This could be Avid Pro Tools, Apple Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Adobe Audition, or FL Studio.
* **Secondary DAWs (Proficient in)**: Select all the secondary DAWs you are proficient in. You can select multiple options.
* **Knowledge of Signal Processing** (*): Select your level of knowledge in signal processing, which includes compression, EQ, gating, and reverb.
* **Experience with Analog Hardware** (*): Select your level of experience with analog hardware, including consoles, outboard gear, and patchbays.
* **Familiarity with Microphone Techniques** (*): Enter any techniques you are familiar with, such as XY, Mid-Side, Decca Tree, or Blumlein.
* **Signal Routing and Patching** (*): Select your ability to navigate a complex studio patchbay. Options are Yes, with ease, Yes, with some guidance, and False.
* **Mixing Specialization** (*): Select all areas where you specialize in mixing, such as music, podcast/voice, sound for film/TV, or game audio/middleware.
* **Basic Mastering** (*): Select if you have experience with basic mastering or not.
* **Link to Portfolio or Work Samples** (*): Enter a link to your portfolio or work samples.
* **List any relevant certifications** (*): Enter any relevant certifications you have, such as Pro Tools Certified User or Dante Level 2.
* **Contact Email** (*): Enter your contact email address.

Note: Please make sure to review each field carefully and answer truthfully to ensure an accurate assessment of your audio production skills.
