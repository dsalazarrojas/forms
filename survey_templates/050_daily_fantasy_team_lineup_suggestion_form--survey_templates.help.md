# Daily Fantasy Team Lineup Suggestion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for daily fantasy team lineup suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief title for your lineup suggestion.
2. Select the **Category** field. Choose from the provided options: Sports or Templates.
3. Provide a brief **Description** of your lineup suggestion (optional).
4. Select the **Lineup Preferences** field. Choose from the provided options: Sports and Contest Types.
5. Select the sports you are interested in for your lineup suggestion. Choose from the provided options: NFL, NBA, MLB, and NHL.
6. Select the **Contest Types** field. Choose from the provided options: Cash, Free, and Roster.
7. Enter the **League Size** field with the number of players in your suggested lineup.
8. Select the **League Type** field. Choose from the provided options: Pick'em, H2H, and Roto.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title (Form Title, text, required)**: Enter a brief title for your lineup suggestion.
* **Category (Category, select_one, required)**: Choose the category for your lineup suggestion. This could be Sports or Templates.
* **Description (Description, text, optional)**: Provide a brief description of your lineup suggestion (optional).
* **Lineup Preferences (Lineup Preferences, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the sports and contest types you are interested in for your lineup suggestion.
* **Sports (Sports, select_multiple, required)**: Select the sports you are interested in for your lineup suggestion. Choose from the provided options: NFL, NBA, MLB, and NHL.
* **Contest Types (Contest Types, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the contest types you are interested in for your lineup suggestion. Choose from the provided options: Cash, Free, and Roto.
* **League Size (League Size, number, required)**: Enter the number of players in your suggested lineup.
* **League Type (League Type, select_one, required)**: Choose the league type for your lineup suggestion. Choose from the provided options: Pick'em, H2H, and Roto.

## Tips
- Be brief and clear in your form title and description.
- Select all applicable sports and contest types for your lineup preferences.
- Ensure the league size is accurate for your suggested lineup.
- Choose the correct league type for your lineup suggestion.
