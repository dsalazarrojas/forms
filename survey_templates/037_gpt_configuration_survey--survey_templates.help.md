# GPT Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about your GPT model configuration preferences and requirements. Please answer these questions to help us better understand your needs and provide optimal configuration settings for your model.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your desired preferences and settings. You can select from the provided options for select multiple and select one fields. For text fields, enter your desired text. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and must be filled out to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your GPT model configuration.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a short description of your GPT model configuration.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, text, optional): Select a category for your GPT model configuration.
* **Category ID** (`form_category_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the category you selected.
* **Select Subcategories** (`form_subcategories`, select_multiple, optional): Select the subcategories you are interested in for your GPT model configuration.
* **Select Category IDs** (`form_subcategories_list_options`, select_one, optional): Select the category ID you want to associate with your subcategories.
* **Targeted Audience** (`form_target_audience`, select_one, optional): Select the target audience for your GPT model configuration.
* **Audience ID** (`form_target_audience_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the target audience you selected.

## Tips
* Please select the options that best suit your needs and preferences.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can leave them blank or select "No" or "Yes" as a placeholder.
* Remember to submit the form to save your preferences and settings.
