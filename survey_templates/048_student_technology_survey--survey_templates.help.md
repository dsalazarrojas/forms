# Student Technology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Technology Survey is designed to collect information about student technology usage, teacher support, and other related aspects of technology integration in the classroom.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the most accurate answer from the provided options.
2. Fill out the form with the relevant information, and feel free to add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
3. Make sure to choose the correct answer for each question and do not leave any fields blank unless you are not sure or do not have an answer.
4. When filling out the "Other Technology Tools Used" fields, please specify the tool and its purpose.
5. When filling out the "Submitted By" field, choose the correct option that represents your role (Student, Teacher, Parent, etc.).
