# Educational Content Source Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Content Source Evaluation Form is a tool for evaluating the quality and effectiveness of educational content sources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the educational content source in the `Educational Content` field.
2. Rate the content using the scale of 1-5 in the `Rating` field.
3. Provide a comment on the rating in the `Rating Comment` field.
4. Choose the category under which the content falls in the `Recommendation` field.
5. Explain why you recommended the content in the `Why` field.
6. Specify the educational level of the content in the `Educational Level` field.
7. Estimate the time spent on the content in the `Time Spent` field.
8. Choose the difficulty level of the content in the `Difficulty Level` field.
9. Rate your satisfaction with the content in the `Satisfaction` field.
10. Select the reason for your satisfaction in the `Satisfaction Scale` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educational Content** (`form_1_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the educational content source.
* **Rating** (`form_1_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the content using a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating Comment** (`form_1_rating_comment`, text, required/optional): Provide a comment on the rating.
* **Recommendation** (`form_1_recommendation`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the category under which the content falls.
* **Why** (`form_1_educational_level_why`, text, required/optional): Explain why you recommended the content.
* **Learning Objectives** (`form_1_learning_objectives`, text, required/optional): Describe the learning objectives of the content.
* **Content Type** (`form_1_content_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the type of content (e.g., Lecture, Video, Article, Other).
* **Content Source** (`form_1_content_source`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the source of the content (e.g., Online Course, Offline Course, Other).
* **Content Format** (`form_1_content_format`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the format of the content (e.g., HTML, PDF, Image, Video).
* **Content Length** (`form_1_content_length`, number, required/optional): Estimate the length of the content.
* **Educational Level** (`form_1_educational_level`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the educational level of the content (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Time Spent** (`form_1_time_spent`, number, required/optional): Estimate the time spent on the content.
* **Difficulty Level** (`form_1_difficulty_level`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the difficulty level of the content (e.g., Easy, Medium, Hard).
* **Satisfaction** (`form_1_satisfaction`, number, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with the content using a scale of 1-5.
* **Satisfaction Scale** (`form_1_satisfaction_scale`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the reason for your satisfaction.
* **Recommendation Satisfied** (`form_1_recommendation_satisfied`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether the recommendation is satisfied or not.
* **Content Source Why** (`form_1_content_source_why`, text, required/optional): Explain why the content source is relevant.
* **Educational Level Why** (`form_1_educational_level_why`, text, required/optional): Explain why the educational level is relevant.
* **Content Length Why** (`form_1_content_length_why`, text, required/optional): Explain why the content length is relevant.
* **Learning Objectives Why** (`form_1_learning_objectives_why`, text, required/optional): Explain why the learning objectives are relevant.
* **Content Format Why** (`form_1_content_format_why`, text, required/optional): Explain why the content format is relevant.
* **Difficulty Level Why** (`form_1_difficulty_level_why`, text, required/optional): Explain why the difficulty level is relevant.
* **Time Spent Why** (`form_1_time_spent_why`, text, required/optional): Explain why the time spent is relevant.
* **Satisfaction Why** (`form_1_satisfaction_why`, text, required/optional): Explain why your satisfaction is relevant.
* **Recommendation Why** (`form_1_recommendation_why`, text, required/optional): Explain why you recommended the content.
* **Content Length Why** (`form_1_content_length_why`, text, required/optional): Explain why the content length is relevant.
