# Home School Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home School Survey is a tool used to collect data about home schools for administrative, educational, or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by clicking on the "Home School Survey" page.
2. Fill out the form by providing the required and optional information in the corresponding fields.
3. Review and submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (2)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Grade Level (3)**: Select the grade level of your home school from the provided options: High or Low.
* **Date of Survey (4)**: Enter the date on which you're filling out the survey.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information in the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the form administrator for assistance.
* This form is for information purposes only and will not be used for any other purpose.
