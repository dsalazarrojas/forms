## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your thoughts and opinions about your experience with our platform. It's a quick and easy way for us to get to know you better and understand how to improve your experience.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. Each page has a specific set of questions that are designed to gather information on a particular aspect of your experience.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Platform User Experience Survey** (`1`, `note`, required/optional): This is an introductory page that sets the tone for the entire form. It's not a question, but an explanation of the purpose of the form.
- **What features do you use most frequently?** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the features you use most often from the options provided. You can choose one feature that best describes your behavior on the platform.
	+ For example, if you often use the "Search" feature, select that option.
- **What do you like about our platform?** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Share your thoughts on what you like about the platform. This can be anything from the user interface to the features or services offered.
- **What do you dislike about our platform?** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Similarly, share your thoughts on what you dislike about the platform. This will help us identify areas that need improvement.
- **How can we improve our platform?** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Think about what we can do to make the platform better for you. Share your ideas and suggestions.
- **How likely are you to recommend our platform?** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all the options that apply to how likely you are to recommend the platform to others. You can select one, some, or all the options.
- **How often do you use our platform?** (`7`, `date`, required/optional): If you're asked about how often you use the platform, choose a specific date range or a frequency of use.
- **How satisfied are you with our platform?** (`8`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with the platform on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
- **How many users do you use our platform with?** (`9`, `number`, required/optional): If you use the platform with multiple users, enter the number of users you interact with regularly.
- **Contact Information** (`10`, `email`, required/optional): Provide your contact email address if you'd like us to follow up with you about your feedback.
- **Contact Phone Number** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Share your contact phone number if you'd like to receive phone calls or follow-ups about your feedback.
