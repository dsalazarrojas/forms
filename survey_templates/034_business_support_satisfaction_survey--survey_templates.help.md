# Business Support Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your experience with our support services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the appropriate response for each question based on your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Company Name** (`client_company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`respondent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact number (optional).
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): This field will automatically populate with the current date.
* **Service Category** (`service_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category of service you received (e.g., Technical Support, Customer Service, etc.).
* **Support Representative Name** (`support_representative`, text, optional): If you had a representative, enter their name.
* **How Did You Contact Us** (`interaction_channel`, select_one, required): Choose how you initially contacted us (e.g., phone, email, chat, etc.).
* **Response Time Rating** (`response_time`, select_one, required): Rate how quickly we responded to your issue.
* **First Contact Resolution** (`first_contact_resolution`, select_one, required): Was your issue resolved on the first contact? Choose the best option.
* **Support Representative Knowledge** (`representative_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the representative's knowledge of the issue.
* **Representative Courtesy and Respect** (`representative_courtesy`, select_one, required): Rate the representative's courtesy and respect.
* **Quality of Solution** (`solution_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the solution provided.
* **Clarity of Explanation** (`explanation_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate how clear the explanation was.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required): Rate the follow-up provided.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the support experience.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_recommend`, select_one, required): Would you recommend our support services?
* **Feedback for Improvement** (`improvement_feedback`, text, optional): If you have any feedback for us, please share it here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share it here.
