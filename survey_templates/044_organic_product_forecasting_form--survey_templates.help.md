# Organic Product Forecasting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the organic products forecasted for farming, including their type, quantity, and distribution frequency.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of organic product being forecasted (e.g., Organic Apple, Organic Carrot, or Organic Broccoli).
2. Enter the quantity of the product being forecasted.
3. Choose the distribution frequency for the product (e.g., weekly, biweekly, or monthly).
4. Enter any additional notes about the product (if needed).
5. Provide your email address for any follow-up questions or communications.
6. Enter your phone number for any urgent contact.

* **organic_product** (`organic_product`, select_one, optional): Select the type of organic product being forecasted (e.g., Organic Apple, Organic Carrot, or Organic Broccoli).
* **product_type** (`product_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of product (e.g., Juice, Dried Fruit, Frozen, or Fresh).
* **quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the product being forecasted.
* **production_date** (`production_date`, date, optional): This field is for selecting the production date (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **distribution_frequency** (`distribution_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the frequency of distribution (e.g., '1', '2', or '3', likely indicating weekly, biweekly, or monthly).
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the product (if needed).
