# Menu Price Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data about menu prices from restaurants. This form is intended to help restaurants understand their menu price strategy and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Provide accurate and honest information in all fields.
4. If you need to add additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Menu Price Survey** (`title`): A brief introduction to the survey.
- *Customer Feedback* (`customer_feedback`, **text**): Provide your honest feedback about menu prices at this restaurant.
- *Price Comparison* (`price_comparison`, **select_one**): Do you compare prices at this restaurant with other restaurants in the area? 
  - If you answered "Yes" to this question, you may also be asked about the prices of other restaurants in your response.
- *Competition Prices* (`competition_prices`, **select_multiple**): If you answered "Yes" to the "Price Comparison" question, list which other restaurants in the area you compare prices with.
- *Restaurant Prices* (`restaurant_prices`, **select_multiple**): List which prices you have noticed at this restaurant.
- *Rating* (`rating`, **number**): Rate your overall satisfaction with the menu prices at this restaurant on a scale from 1 to 5.
- *Rating Scale* (`rating_scale`, **select_one**): Select the scale that best describes your rating (e.g., Scale A, Scale B).
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, **text**): Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback.
- **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, **text**): Enter the name of the restaurant you are providing feedback about.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, **text**): Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.
- **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, **date**): This field will automatically populate with the current date.

## Tips
- Please answer all required fields accurately and honestly.
- If you need more time to complete the form, you can save it and return later. 
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact info].
