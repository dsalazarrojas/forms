# Enterprise LLM Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather user feedback about your interaction with the Enterprise LLM. This will help us understand how well the LLM is performing and where we can improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your company's name in the `company_name` field.
2. Type in the name of the LLM you are evaluating in the `llm_name` field.
3. Choose your rating for the LLM from the options provided in the `rating` field.
4. If you have any comments about your interaction with the LLM, type them in the `comment` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **company_name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **llm_name** (`llm_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the LLM you are evaluating.
* **rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Choose one of the four rating options: `excellent`, `good`, `neutral`, or `bad`.
* **comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Add any comments you have about your interaction with the LLM.
