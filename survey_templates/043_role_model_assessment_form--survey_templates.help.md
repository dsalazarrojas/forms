# Role Model Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Role Model Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the performance and behavior of leaders within an organization. This form is used to assess the qualities and skills of the leader, providing feedback for professional development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Leader's ID number to identify the leader being assessed.
2. Provide the Leader's first name and last name.
3. Rate the leader's performance in six different areas: Self, Other, Leader, Team, Organization, and Overall.
4. Enter any additional comments about the leader's performance.
5. Enter the leader's email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Leader Id**: Enter the unique ID number assigned to the leader being assessed.
* **Leader First Name**: Enter the first name of the leader being assessed.
* **Leader Last Name**: Enter the last name of the leader being assessed.
* **Rating 1: Self**: Rate the leader's self-assessment, using numbers 1-5, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Rating 2: Self**: Rate the leader's self-assessment, using numbers 1-5, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Rating 3: Other**: Rate the leader's performance by others, using numbers 1-5, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Rating 4: Leader**: Rate the leader's performance as a leader, using numbers 1-5, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Rating 5: Team**: Rate the leader's performance as a team player, using numbers 1-5, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Rating 6: Organization**: Rate the leader's performance as an organization, using numbers 1-5, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Rating 7: Overall**: Rate the leader's overall performance, using numbers 1-5, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the leader's performance.
* **Leader Email**: Enter the leader's email address for contact purposes.
* **Leader Phone**: Enter the leader's phone number for contact purposes.
