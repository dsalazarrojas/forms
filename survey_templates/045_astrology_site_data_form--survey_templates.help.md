# Astrology Site Data Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for generating astrology-related horoscopes. It is intended to be a comprehensive tool for capturing user birth data, relationship status, and preferences for horoscope types and focus areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address for horoscope delivery.
2. Select your gender.
3. Enter your date and time of birth.
4. Enter your city, state/province, and country of birth.
5. If you have a partner, enter their first name, last name, date and time of birth, city, state/province, and country.
6. Choose the type of horoscope you are interested in (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, annual, love, career, or compatibility).
7. Select areas of focus for your horoscope (e.g., love and relationships, career and finances, health and wellness, etc.).
8. Add any additional notes or requests you may have.
9. Enter the date you would like to receive your horoscope.
10. Opt-in or opt-out of receiving astrology updates and horoscopes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Your Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Your Email Address**: Please enter a valid email address for receiving horoscope delivery.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY (month/day/year).
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM AM/PM (12-hour clock).
* **City of Birth**: Enter the city where you were born.
* **State/Province of Birth**: Enter the state or province where you were born.
* **Country of Birth**: Enter the country where you were born.
* **Partner First Name**: If you have a partner, enter their first name.
* **Partner Last Name**: If you have a partner, enter their last name.
* **Partner Date of Birth**: If you have a partner, enter their date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY (month/day/year).
* **Partner Time of Birth**: If you have a partner, enter their time of birth in the format HH:MM AM/PM (12-hour clock).
* **Partner City of Birth**: If you have a partner, enter the city where your partner was born.
* **Partner State/Province of Birth**: If you have a partner, enter the state or province where your partner was born.
* **Partner Country of Birth**: If you have a partner, enter the country where your partner was born.
* **Time Zone at Birth**: Enter your birth time zone.
* **Partner Sun Sign**: If you have a partner, this field will be calculated from their birth date.
* **Sun Sign (Zodiac Sign)**: This field will be calculated from your birth date.
* **Partner Sun Sign**: If you have a partner, this field will be calculated from their birth date.
* **Type of Horoscope Requested**: Choose the type of horoscope you are interested in (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, annual, love, career, or compatibility).
* **Areas of Focus**: Select one or more areas of focus for your horoscope (e.g., love and relationships, career and finances, health and wellness, etc.).
* **Additional Notes or Requests**: Enter any specific interests or requests you may have.
* **Form Submission Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your horoscope.
* **Receive Astrology Updates and Horoscopes**: Opt-in or opt-out of receiving astrology updates and horoscopes.
