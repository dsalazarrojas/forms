# Smartphone Addiction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smartphone Addiction Assessment is a form designed to help individuals understand their smartphone usage habits and potential impact on their well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your average smartphone use habits.
2. Answer each question truthfully, using the available options for each field.
3. For open-ended fields, provide a brief description of your thoughts and habits related to the question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Screen time** (`screen_time`, number, optional): Please select the approximate number of hours you spend on your smartphone each day.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_one, optional): Indicate how often you use social media on your smartphone.
	+ Never: I rarely or never use social media on my smartphone.
	+ Always: I constantly check social media throughout the day.
	+ Rarely: I check social media occasionally, but not frequently.
	+ Occasionally: I check social media a few times a day.
	+ Frequently: I check social media several times a day.
* **Smartphone use** (`phone_use`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your daily smartphone use habits.
	+ Never: I don't use my smartphone at all.
	+ Less than 1 hour: I use my smartphone for less than an hour a day.
	+ 1-2 hours: I use my smartphone for 1-2 hours a day.
	+ 2-4 hours: I use my smartphone for 2-4 hours a day.
	+ More than 4 hours: I use my smartphone for more than 4 hours a day.
* **Smartphone distractions** (`distractions`, text, optional): Describe any frequent distractions you experience while using your smartphone (e.g., phone notifications, social media alerts, emails).

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully, and try to think critically about your smartphone use habits.
* If you select "Not a problem" for any field, consider whether that is truly the case or if you might be overlooking potential issues.
* Be mindful of your answers and reflect on any patterns or habits that might be causing problems in your life.
* Use this assessment as a starting point for self-reflection and discussion with loved ones or a healthcare professional if needed.
