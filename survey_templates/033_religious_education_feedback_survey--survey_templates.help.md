# Religious Education Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with religious education programs. Your input will help us improve the quality and effectiveness of these programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please start by reading each question carefully and providing your honest feedback.
2. Fill in all the required fields, marked with a red star.
3. Use the provided options for select multiple-choice questions.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.
5. Finally, review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, not required): This is an open-ended question where you can briefly describe your experience with the religious education program.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, not required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the program using the scale provided. Your rating will help us gauge the program's effectiveness.
* **Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the areas that you are most satisfied with or least satisfied with. Select all that apply.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, not required): Describe in detail the areas that you think the program can be improved.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Please provide your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, not required): Provide your phone number so we can reach out to you for more information.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, not required): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the program.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, not required): Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your feedback.
