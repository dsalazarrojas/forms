# Competition Rituals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your pre-competition routines and habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability. Each question is designed to provide insight into your competitive habits and rituals. Please select the options that best fit your practices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Primary Sport or Competitive Activity** (`primary_sport_activity`): Please select the sport or competitive activity in which you most frequently participate. For example, if you play tennis, chess, or engage in gaming, running, or other activities.
    - Type: `text`
    - Required: `true`

- **How often do you participate in competitions?** (`competition_frequency_rituals`): Select the frequency of your competitive events. This helps us understand how often you engage in competitive activities.
    - Type: `select_one`
    - Options: `Weekly`, `Monthly`, `A few times a year`, `Once a year or less`
    - Required: `true`

- **Average duration of your pre-competition routine** (`ritual_duration`): Choose how long your routine typically lasts from start of preparation to the start of the event.
    - Type: `select_one`
    - Options: `Less than 15 minutes`, `15-30 minutes`, `30-60 minutes`, `1-2 hours`, `More than 2 hours`
    - Required: `true`

- **Which physical rituals do you perform?** (`physical_rituals_select`): Select all the physical practices that you perform as part of your routine.
    - Type: `select_multiple`
    - Options: `Specific Warm-up / Stretching`, `Breathing Exercises`, `Eating a Specific Meal`, `Wearing "Lucky" Clothing`, `Listening to specific music`, `Equipment Check / Cleaning`
    - Required: `true`

- **Which mental rituals do you perform?** (`mental_rituals_select`): Select all the mental practices that you perform as part of your routine.
    - Type: `select_multiple`
    - Options: `Visualization / Mental Rehearsal`, `Positive Self-Talk / Affirmations`, `Meditation / Quiet Time`, `Studying Opponents / Game Tape`, `Goal Setting`
    - Required: `true`

- **How does it impact your performance if you skip your ritual?** (`ritual_skipping_impact`): Indicate how skipping your pre-competition routine might affect your performance.
    - Type: `select_one`
    - Options: `Significant negative impact`, `Slight negative impact`, `No impact at all`, `Depends on the competition level`
    - Required: `true`

- **If you listen to music, which genre is most effective?** (`music_genre_ritual`): If music is part of your routine, choose the genre that you find most beneficial.
    - Type: `select_one`
    - Options: `Rock / Metal`, `Hip Hop / Rap`, `Electronic / EDM`, `Classical / Calm`, `Pop`, `Not applicable`
    - Required: `false`

- **Do you consider any part of your ritual to be a superstition?** (`superstition_presence`): Indicate if you believe any part of your routine has a supernatural or lucky element.
    - Type: `select_one`
    - Options: `Yes, definitely`, `Somewhat`, `False`
    - Required: `true`

- **Rate the correlation between ritual consistency and success** (`ritual_success_correlation`): Assess the relationship between sticking to your routine and achieving success.
    - Type: `select_one`
    - Options: `1 (low)`, `2`, `3`, `4`, `5 (high)`
    - Required: `true`

- **How has your ritual changed over the years?** (`ritual_evolution_comments`): Briefly describe any changes or evolution in your pre-competition routine.
    - Type: `text`
    - Options: `[]`
    - Required: `false`

- **Date** (`survey_completion_date_rituals`): Please select today's date for tracking purposes.
    - Type: `date`
    - Options: `[]`
    - Required: `true`
