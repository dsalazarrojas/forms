# Casual Gathering Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of gatherings or events to improve future events and provide better experiences for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your honest feedback. Most fields are self-explanatory, but if you need further clarification, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name (Optional)** (`guest_name`, `text`, required/optional): If you'd like to provide your name, please fill it in; otherwise, you can leave it blank.
- **Date of the Gathering** (`gathering_date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date of the gathering you're providing feedback about.
- **How would you rate the overall event** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the event from Excellent to Poor.
- **How easy was it to find the location and settle in** (`arrival_experience`, `select_one`, optional/true): Rate the ease of finding the location and settling in from Very Easy to Difficult.
- **Food Quality and Variety** (`food_quality_rating`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the quality and variety of the food from Highly Satisfied to Dissatisfied.
- **Beverage Selection** (`drinks_variety_rating`, `select_one`, optional/true): Rate the selection of beverages from Excellent to Could be improved.
- **What was your favorite dish or drink** (`favorite_food_item`, `text`, optional/true): If you had a favorite, please let us know!
- **Music and Noise Level** (`music_volume_rating`, `select_one`, optional/true): Rate the music and noise level from Just Right to Too Loud or Too Quiet.
- **Rating of the venue or location** (`venue_suitability`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the suitability of the venue from 5 (Perfect) to 1 (Not suitable).
- **What was the most enjoyable part of the gathering** (`most_enjoyable_part`, `text`, optional/true): If there was a standout moment, please share!
- **Suggestions for future gatherings** (`future_suggestions`, `text`, optional/true): Do you have any themes, locations, or activities you'd like to see in future gatherings?
- **Would you attend a similar event in the future** (`next_time_interest`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate your interest in attending a similar event from Definitely to Unlikely.
- **Would you recommend our events to a friend** (`referrals`, `select_one`, optional/true): Let us know if you'd recommend our events to a friend from Yes to No.
