# Educational Outreach Impact Study - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Outreach Impact Study form is designed to assess the effectiveness of educational outreach activities in engaging participants and promoting learning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the Participant Details section with your name, email, and other relevant contact information (if applicable).
* Provide ratings for each activity on a scale from Good to Fair.
* Select your level of agreement or disagreement with the statements in the Participant Disagree, Participant Indifferent, and Educational Outreach Impact sections.
* Provide comments for each activity and participant in the Comments sections.
* Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) next to the field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This section collects basic contact information and participant details.
	+ **Name**: Your name as a participant in the study.
	+ **Email**: Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Activity Ratings**: Rate each activity on a scale from Good to Fair.
	+ **Activity 1 Rating**: Rate the first activity you participated in.
	+ **Activity 2 Rating**: Rate the second activity you participated in.
	+ **Activity 3 Rating**: Rate the third activity you participated in.
* **Feedback**: Share any feedback or comments about the activities or the study.
* **Contact Info**: Provide any additional contact information or notes (optional).
* **Participant Comments**: Share any comments or feedback about the study or activities (optional).
* **Participant Agree**: Select your level of agreement with the statements.
	+ **Participant Agree**: Agree or disagree with the statements (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Participant Disagree**: Select your level of disagreement with the statements.
	+ **Participant Disagree**: Disagree or strongly disagree with the statements (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Participant Indifferent**: Select your level of neutrality with the statements.
	+ **Participant Indifferent**: Neither agree nor disagree with the statements (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Educational Outreach Impact Scale**: Rate the educational outreach impact on a scale (optional).
	+ **Educational Outreach Impact**: Agree or disagree with the statements (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Participant Rating**: Rate each participant on a scale from Good to Fair.
	+ **Participant 1 Rating**: Rate the first participant you rated.
	+ **Participant 2 Rating**: Rate the second participant you rated.
	+ **Participant 3 Rating**: Rate the third participant you rated.
* **Participant Rating 1, 2, 3**: Select your level of agreement with the statements for each participant.
	+ **Participant 1 Rating**: Agree or disagree with the statements for the first participant (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
	+ **Participant 2 Rating**: Agree or disagree with the statements for the second participant (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
	+ **Participant 3 Rating**: Agree or disagree with the statements for the third participant (e.g., "Yes" or "No").

Note: The required fields are indicated by an asterisk (*) next to the field label.
