# Team Building Activity Success Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help team leaders and organizers assess the success of a team-building activity. It is meant to be filled out after the event has concluded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the relevant information. The form is divided into several sections. Please make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the * symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): This field is meant to record the name of the event, for reference and record-keeping purposes.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): This field is used to record the date on which the team-building activity took place.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): This field is used to record the time at which the team-building activity occurred.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required/optional): This field is meant to record the name of the team that participated in the activity.
* **Team Leader Name** (`team_leader_name`, text, required/optional): This field is used to record the name of the team leader or person in charge of the activity.
* **Team Leader Email** (`team_leader_email`, email, required/optional): This field is used to record the email address of the team leader or person in charge of the activity.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, text, required/optional): This field is meant to list the members of the team that participated in the activity.
* **Activities Conducted** (`activities_conducted`, text, required/optional): This field is used to record a brief description of the activities that took place during the team-building activity.
* **Team Morale** (`team_morale`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is used to rate the team's morale after the activity, using the following options:
	+ Happy
	+ Unhappy
	+ Neither
* **Team Collaboration** (`team_collaboration`, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to rate the team's collaboration level after the activity, using the following options:
	+ High
	+ Low
	+ Medium
* **Team Engagement** (`team_engagement`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is used to rate the team's engagement level after the activity, using the following options:
	+ High
	+ Low
	+ Medium
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): This field is used to record any additional feedback or comments about the activity.

Note: Please fill out this form with as much detail as possible, and make sure to review the results with your team to identify areas of improvement.
