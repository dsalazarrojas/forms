# Monthly Performance Nomination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Performance Nomination Survey is a tool used to identify and recognize team members who have demonstrated exceptional performance in various categories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the nominee you wish to recognize.
2. Enter the nominee's first name, last name, and date of nomination.
3. Select the scores for the nominee's performance and behavior in the following categories:
	* Performance
	* Potential
	* Teamwork
	* Leadership
	* Innovation
	* Communication
	* Adaptability
	* Problem Solving
	* Initiative
	* Leadership
	* Collaboration
	* Quality
	* Continuous Improvement
	* Teamwork
	* Adaptability
	* Problem Solving
	* Initiative
4. Provide any additional comments about the nominee's performance or achievements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the nominee.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the nominee.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Nomination Time** (`nomination_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the nomination.
* **Nominee's Name** (`nominee_name`, text, optional): Enter the nominee's name (if different from first and last names).
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the manager or supervisor.
