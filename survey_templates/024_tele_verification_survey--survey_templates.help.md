# Tele Verification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and document phone calls that require verification.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each field with the relevant information about the phone call.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Call Details** (`call_details`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional information about the call, such as the call duration or any notable events that occurred during the call.
* **Outcome** (`outcome`, select_one, optional): Choose the outcome of the phone call from the options:
	+ Outcome 1
	+ Outcome 2
	+ Outcome 3
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, optional): This field is reserved for any additional notes or comments about the call. This can be used to capture any necessary information that doesn't fit into other fields.
* **Phone Verification Details** (`details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the phone call, such as the reason for the call or any other relevant details.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the phone call.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the phone call.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who made or received the phone call.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who made or received the phone call.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If a field is not required, it is optional and can be left blank.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose and instructions above.
