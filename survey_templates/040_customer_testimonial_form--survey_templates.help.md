# Customer Testimonial Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Testimonial Form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the business. This form helps us understand their satisfaction with the quality of work, recommendation of our business, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title, which is the title of the form itself.
2. For the **testimonial_form_input** field, provide a brief testimonial or comment about your experience with our business.
3. Check any relevant **business_services** that apply to your experience with us.
4. Rate your experience on the **rating** field, with higher numbers indicating higher satisfaction.
5. Share your submission and testimony in the **submission** and **testimony** fields, respectively.
6. Select the **employee** who interacted with you during your experience.
7. Indicate whether you **recommend_business** to others based on your experience.
8. Enter your **experience** with our business, with higher numbers indicating more experience.
9. Click the **form_submit** button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **testimonial_form_input** (`testimonial_form_input`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can enter any comments or feedback about your experience with our business.
* **business_services** (`business_services`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Check the box that best describes the services you received from our business. This could include any services such as consulting, training, or other services we offer.
* **quality_of_work** (`quality_of_work`, number, optional):
	+ If you could rate our work on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being poor and 10 being excellent, enter the number here.
* **recommend_business** (`recommend_business`, select one, optional):
	+ Check this box if you would recommend our business to others based on your experience.
* **experience** (`experience`, number, optional):
	+ If you could rate your level of experience with our business on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being none and 10 being extensive, enter the number here.
* **employee** (`employee`, select one, optional):
	+ Select the employee who interacted with you during your experience with our business.
* **testimony** (`testimony`, text, optional):
	+ Share your testimony or story about your experience with our business.
* **rating** (`rating`, number, optional):
	+ If you could rate your satisfaction with our business on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being poor and 10 being excellent, enter the number here.
* **submission** (`submission`, text, optional):
	+ Share any additional comments or information about your submission.
* **form_submit** (`form_submit`, text, optional):
	+ Click this button to submit your feedback and complete the form.
