# Product Defectiveness Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Defectiveness Assessment Questionnaire is used to collect information about a product defect, including contact details, product information, and defect characteristics. This information will help identify the defect type, product category, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the Contact Information section.
2. Describe the product issue in the Product Issue section.
3. Enter the product name and choose the product category and subcategory that best fit the product.
4. Select the type of defect in the Defect Type section.
5. Choose multiple subcategories that apply to the product in the Product Subcategory section.
6. Enter the date and time the report was received in the Date Report Received and Time Report Received sections.
7. Enter a detailed description of the issue in the Description section.
8. Attach any relevant files or documents to the report in the Attachment section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (id: 1, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and other contact details to allow us to get back to you with updates and questions.
* **Product Issue** (id: 2, text, required/optional): Describe the issue with the product in as much detail as possible.
* **Product Name** (id: 3, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the product that is experiencing the issue.
* **Defect Type** (id: 4, select one, required/optional): Select the type of defect that best describes the issue (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Product Category** (id: 5, select one, required): Choose the category that best fits the product (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Product Subcategory** (id: 6, select multiple, required/optional): Choose multiple subcategories that apply to the product (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Date Report Received** (id: 7, date, required/optional): Enter the date the report was received.
* **Time Report Received** (id: 8, time, required/optional): Enter the time the report was received.
* **Description** (id: 9, text, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the issue.
* **Attachment** (id: 10, note, required/optional): Attach any relevant files or documents to the report.
