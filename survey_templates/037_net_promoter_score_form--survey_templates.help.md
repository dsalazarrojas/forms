# net_promoter_score_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The net promotor score form is a tool used to collect feedback on how customers perceive a company's products or services. It's a simple four-question form that aims to measure a customer's likelihood to recommend and rate the product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill out the following questions to the best of your knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: (`net_promoter_score_form_1`, text, required: false)
  This is the first question of the form, which asks for your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **How did you rate our product?** (`net_promoter_score_form_2`, number, required: false)
  This question asks you to rate your experience with our product or service on a scale of 1 to 10. Try to provide a fair and honest rating based on your experience.
* **Would you recommend our product?** (`net_promoter_score_form_3`, select_one, required: false)
  Choose one of the options that best describes your likelihood to recommend our product to friends or family:
  * Option 1: Very likely
  * Option 2: Somewhat likely
  * Option 3: Not likely
* **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** (`net_promoter_score_form_4`, number, required: false)
  This is a calculated score based on your answers to the previous questions. The score is calculated as a result of your response to the "How did you rate our product?" and "Would you recommend our product?" questions.

## Tips
- Be honest and provide accurate information about your experience with our product or service.
- Take your time and answer each question carefully, as the results will be more accurate with more thoughtful responses.
