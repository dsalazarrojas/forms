# mental_health_service_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health service assessment form is used to evaluate an individual's mental health needs and goals. This information helps professionals provide personalized support and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please provide your basic contact information in the "Client Information" section.
2. In the "Mental Health Background" section, describe your mental health history, including any relevant experiences, diagnoses, or treatment plans.
3. For "Mental Health Symptoms," list any current symptoms you're experiencing related to mental health issues.
4. "Medical History" should include any relevant medical conditions, treatments, or allergies.
5. In "Medication List," list any medications you're currently taking.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Mental Health Service Goals," indicating if you're interested in seeking services.
7. In "Mental Health Service Goals 2," select all that apply from the list of options.
8. Provide a brief description of your mental health service plan in "Mental Health Service Plan."
9. Describe the next steps you plan to take to address your mental health concerns in "Next Steps."
10. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Mental Health Service Outcome," indicating if you think seeking services will be helpful.
11. In "Mental Health Service Outcome 2," select all that apply from the list of options.
12. Finally, provide any additional information or thoughts in "Mental Health Service Outcome 3."
