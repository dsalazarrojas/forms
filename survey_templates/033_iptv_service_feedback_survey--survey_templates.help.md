# IPTV Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IPTV Service Feedback Survey is a tool used to gather information from customers about their experience with the IPTV service. This survey is designed to collect feedback on the quality, usability, and overall satisfaction with the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is required to allow us to contact you with any follow-up questions or to provide you with updates on your feedback.
3. Select your rating of the service in the "Rating" field. Choose from the options "Good" or "Fair".
4. In the "Feedback" field, tell us more about your experience with the service. This is an opportunity to share your thoughts and suggestions.
5. Choose which IPTV service you use from the "Which IPTV Service Do You Use" dropdown menu. Select one or more of the options "Amazon", "Fubud", or "DazsSAT".
6. Indicate if you watch channels with the service by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "How Many Channels Do You Watch" field.
7. If you do watch channels, estimate the amount of time you spend watching IPTV per week in the "How Much Time Do You Spend Watching IPTV" field.
8. In the "Improvement Ideas" field, share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Write your name here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address here.
* **Rating**: Select from "Good" or "Fair" to rate your experience with the IPTV service.
* **Which IPTV Service Do You Use**: Choose from the available options "Amazon", "Fubud", or "DazsSAT".
* **How Many Channels Do You Watch**: Indicate if you watch channels with the service by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **How Much Time Do You Spend Watching IPTV**: If you watch channels, estimate the amount of time you spend watching IPTV per week in hours.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and suggestions about your experience with the service.
* **Improvement Ideas**: Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the service.
