# share_your_special_moments - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect and share special moments from users. It's a place to record and cherish your memories, big or small.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by writing a short description of your special moment in the **share_your_special_moment** field.
2. Next, describe what happened **after** the special moment in the **what_happened_next** field.
3. Think about who was involved in the special moment and select **all** the people involved in the **people_involved** field.
4. If anything significant happened, select **yes** in the **thing_happened** field.
5. If the moment occurred on a specific **date**, enter the date in the **date** field.
6. If the moment happened at a specific **time**, enter the time in the **time** field.
7. Choose the type of **memory_type** by selecting from the options.
8. Finally, add any **comments** you'd like to share about the special moment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **share_your_special_moment**: A short description of your special moment.
* **share_your_story**: Describe what happened after the special moment.
* **what_happened_next**: Continue the story about the special moment.
* **people_involved**: Select all the people involved in the special moment.
* **thing_happened**: Was anything significant that happened? Select "yes" if yes.
* **place_visited**: A specific location where the special moment occurred.
* (Note: The "place_visited" field is not present in the form, but it was mentioned in the original YAML, so I assumed it's a field that should be included in the field-by-field explanation for user understanding.)
* **time**: A specific time when the special moment occurred. Note that this field is usually not required and should not be confused with the **date** field.
* **memory_type**: Choose the type of memory by selecting from the options.
* **comments**: Add any additional comments you'd like to make about the special moment.

Tips

* Be creative and share as much detail as you'd like about your special moment.
* Remember, this form is for sharing your memories, so don't be afraid to be honest and open.
