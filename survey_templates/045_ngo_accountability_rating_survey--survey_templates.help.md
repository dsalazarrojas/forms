# NGO Accountability Rating Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NGO Accountability Rating Survey is a tool to measure the performance of Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) in their transparency, engagement, accountability, governance, and financial practices. This survey helps stakeholders, such as donors, investors, or beneficiaries, to evaluate the level of accountability of NGOs and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully to understand the context and requirements.
2. Rate each question on a scale from 1-5, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very much".
3. Fill in the ratings for each question, ensuring that you answer each question truthfully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### How transparent is the organization
* **Transparency Rating (1-5)**: How would you rate the organization's transparency in terms of its decision-making processes, information sharing, and communication?
* This question is optional, but it will help to evaluate the organization's openness and honesty in its practices.

### How engaged are stakeholders
* **Engagement Rating (1-5)**: How engaged are stakeholders in the organization's decision-making processes and activities?
* This question helps to evaluate the level of involvement and participation of stakeholders in the organization's work.

### How accountable are decisions
* **Accountability Rating (1-5)**: How accountable are the decisions made by the organization, including financial management and governance practices?
* This question helps to evaluate the level of responsibility and answerability of the organization's actions.

### How well is governance managed
* **Governance Rating (1-5)**: How well is the organization's governance managed, including its management and organizational structure?
* This question helps to evaluate the effectiveness and efficiency of the organization's governance practices.

### How transparent are financials
* **Transparency Rating (2) (Financial)**: How transparent are the organization's financial practices, including information sharing and reporting?
* This question helps to evaluate the level of openness and honesty in the organization's financial dealings.

### How engaged are team members
* **Engagement Rating (2) (Team)**: How engaged are team members in the organization's activities and decision-making processes?
* This question helps to evaluate the level of involvement and participation of team members in the organization's work.
