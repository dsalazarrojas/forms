# Skin Tone Finder Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Tone Finder Quiz is designed to help users discover their skin tone type by guiding them through a series of questions and providing recommendations based on their answers.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Start at the beginning**: Begin with the "Introduction" page, where you'll find a brief overview of the quiz and its purpose.
- **Answer each question**: Move on to the "Undertone Questions" page, where you will be asked to choose how your skin undertone looks in the sun, then move on to the "Undertone Answers" page, where you'll be asked to choose how your skin undertone looks in certain lighting conditions.
- **Get your result**: Once you have answered all questions, you'll be taken to the "Quiz Result" page, where you'll find your skin tone type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction**: (`title`, note, optional) This page provides an overview of the quiz and its purpose.

### Undertone Questions
* **Undertone Questions**: (`undertone_questions`, select_one, optional) On this page, you will be asked to choose how your skin undertone looks in the sun. Choose the answer that best describes your skin.

### Undertone Answers
* **Undertone Answers**: (`undertone_answers`, select_multiple, optional) On this page, you will be asked to choose how your skin undertone looks in different lighting conditions. Choose the answers that best describe your skin.

### Skin Tone Recommendations
* **Skin Tone Recommendations**: (`skin_tone_recommendations`, text, optional) Here, you can enter any additional information about your skin tone, such as its color, undertone, or any other characteristics you'd like to describe.

### Quiz Result
* **Quiz Result**: (`quiz_result`, select_one, optional) On this final page, you'll be presented with your skin tone type based on your previous answers.
