# Food Truck Market Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on customer experiences with food trucks, including frequency of visits, favorite items, satisfaction, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Enter your information in the relevant fields.
3. Submit the form when you have completed all the questions.
