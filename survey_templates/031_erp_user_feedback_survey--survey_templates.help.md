# ERP User Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about their experience with our ERP system. Your input is invaluable in helping us understand what works well and what can be improved, ensuring that the system meets your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions according to your experience with the ERP system. You can choose from a dropdown menu for certain questions and type in your answers for others. If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to express them in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Department (`<name>`, `select_one`, not required)
This question asks about the department you belong to. Please select the one that best matches your role or affiliation.

### 2. name (`name`, text, not required)
This is your name or identifier that will be used to associate this feedback with your account. You can enter your full name, initials, or any other identifier you prefer.

### 3. Email (`email`, email, not required)
This is your email address that we may use to contact you if needed. Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.

### 4. Feedback (`feedback`, text, not required)
In this field, please provide any issues, suggestions, or comments you have about the ERP system. This is your chance to tell us about your positive and negative experiences.

### 5. Satisfaction (`satisfaction`, number, not required)
Rate your level of satisfaction with the ERP system. You can choose any number between 1 (very dissatisfied) and 5 (very satisfied).

### 6. Adoption (`adoption`, number, not required)
Rate the level of adoption or usage of the ERP system within your department or workgroup. You can choose any number between 1 (not at all) and 5 (very high).

### 7. Improvement Priorities (`improvement_priorities`, text, not required)
In this field, please suggest areas for improvement or features that would enhance your experience with the ERP system.

### 8. Submit (`Submit`, select_one, not required)
Choose this option to submit your feedback or cancel it without submitting. If you choose to cancel, your feedback will not be submitted.

## Tips
- Please answer all the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any attachments or supporting documents, you can share them with us separately.
- We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve the ERP system for everyone.
