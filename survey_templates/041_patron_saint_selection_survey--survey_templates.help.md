# Patron Saint Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patron Saint Selection Survey is designed to collect information from patrons about their patron saint preferences. This data will help us better understand their interests and tailor our services to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each field.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3.  Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your full address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address. Please use a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
*   **Patron Saint** (`patron_saint`, `select_one`, required): Choose your patron saint from the available options.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional information or comments about your patron saint selection, please include them here.
*   **School Group** (`school_group`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you are affiliated with a school, select the corresponding group.
*   **Church Group** (`church_group`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you are affiliated with a church, select the corresponding group.
*   **Local Group** (`local_group`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have a local group affiliation, select the corresponding group.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, optional): Enter your time of birth in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
*   **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of death of your patron saint, if applicable.
*   **Time of Death** (`time_of_death`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of death of your patron saint, if applicable.
*   **Saint Date** (`saint_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of recognition as a patron saint, if applicable.
*   **Saint Time** (`saint_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of recognition as a patron saint, if applicable.

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the form administrators.
