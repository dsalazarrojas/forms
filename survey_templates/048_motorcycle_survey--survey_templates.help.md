# Motorcycle Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their motorcycle experiences, preferences, and behaviors to gather actionable insights that can be used to improve the overall experience of motorcycle users.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill out the form by answering each question honestly. 

- Start by providing a brief description of your motorcycle (Field 1).
- Share your motorcycle preferences, including any notable features or characteristics (Field 2).
- Rate the performance of your motorcycle (Field 3).
- Indicate how much you enjoy riding your motorcycle (Field 4).
- Specify the average number of kilometers you drive per week (Field 5).
- Tell us how many motorcycles you own (Field 6).
- Enter the date you purchased or acquired your current motorcycle (Field 7).
- Indicate the time you typically spend riding your motorcycle (Field 8).
- Add any additional comments or remarks about your motorcycle experiences (Field 9).
- Provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted about this survey or for any further updates (Field 10).
- Finally, enter any other information or comments about your motorcycle experiences (Field 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your motorcycle** (`motorcycle_survey_1`, Text, Required: False): This field is where you can describe your motorcycle, including its make, model, and any notable features.
* **Your motorcycle preferences** (`motorcycle_survey_2`, Text, Required: False): Share with us your favorite features, color, or any unique characteristics of your motorcycle.
* **How do you rate the performance of your motorcycle** (`motorcycle_survey_3`, Text, Required: False): Rate your motorcycle's performance from your perspective, considering its acceleration, handling, and overall feel.
* **Do you enjoy riding your motorcycle** (`motorcycle_survey_4`, Select One, Required: False): Choose how much you enjoy riding your motorcycle, whether it's very much, sometimes, or not at all.
* **How many kilometers you drive per week** (`motorcycle_survey_5`, Number, Required: False): Enter the average number of kilometers you drive per week on your motorcycle.
* **How many motorcycles do you own** (`motorcycle_survey_6`, Number, Required: False): Tell us how many motorcycles you own, and provide details about them if you'd like.
* **motorcycle_survey_7** (`motorcycle_survey_7`, Date, Required: False): Enter the date you purchased or acquired your current motorcycle.
* **motorcycle_survey_8** (`motorcycle_survey_8`, Time, Required: False): Indicate the time you typically spend riding your motorcycle.
* **motorcycle_survey_9** (`motorcycle_survey_9`, Note, Required: False): Add any comments or remarks about your experiences with your motorcycle.
* **motorcycle_survey_10** (`motorcycle_survey_10`, Email, Required: False): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about this survey or for any further updates.
* **motorcycle_survey_11** (`motorcycle_survey_11`, Text, Required: False): Provide any other information or comments about your motorcycle experiences.
