# Auto Mode Configuration Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback and opinions on the usage and configuration of our Auto Mode feature. Your input will help us improve the usability, performance, and overall experience of this automated tool. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey, and let us know your honest thoughts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. You will be asked questions about your experience with Auto Mode, including your usage frequency, ease of setup, navigation, and performance. You can select your answers from the provided options or type in your thoughts in the text boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- User Profile --**: This page is a header to introduce the survey.
* **User ID or Display Name**: Enter your unique identifier or display name for tracking and identification purposes.
* **Frequency of Auto Mode Use**: Choose how often you use the automated features, from "Daily" to "Rarely".
* **-- Configuration Usability --**: This page is a header to introduce the usability section.
* **Ease of Initial Setup**: Rate your experience with the initial setup of Auto Mode, from "Very Easy" to "Very Difficult".
* **Clarity of Configuration Menu**: Evaluate how easy it is to navigate the settings, from "Excellent" to "Poor".
* **Setup Barriers Encountered**: If you faced any technical issues or confusing steps, list them here.
* **-- Feature Performance --**: This page is a header to introduce the performance section.
* **Features Most Used**: Choose which automated tools you rely on most, from a list of options.
* **Auto Mode Reliability Rating**: Rate the reliability of Auto Mode on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Does Auto Mode save you time?**: Evaluate the impact of Auto Mode on your workflow efficiency, from "Significantly" to "Not at all".
* **Feature Improvement Suggestions**: Share your thoughts on what you would like to see improved in future updates.
* **Likelihood to Recommend Auto Mode**: Indicate how likely you are to suggest Auto Mode to others, from "Extremely Likely" to "Unlikely".
