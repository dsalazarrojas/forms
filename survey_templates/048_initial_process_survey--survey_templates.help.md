# Initial Process Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and insights about the project's progress, goals, and outcomes. Your input will help us improve future project management and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and answer them as honestly as possible.
2. For text fields, provide a brief but clear description of your answer.
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options that best match your feelings about the project.
4. Submit your completed form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was the initial project goal?** (`start_of_project`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the project's initial objectives and objectives.
* **What were your expectations for this project?** (`project_expectations`, `text`, required/optional): Share what you expected from the project in terms of deliverables, timeline, and resources.
* **What were your experiences with this project?** (`project_experiences`, `text`, required/optional): Reflect on your interactions with the project team, stakeholders, and any challenges you faced.
* **How do you feel about this project?** (`overall_feelings`, `text`, required/optional): Express your feelings about the project's progress, quality, and overall impact.
* **What are your next steps?** (`next_steps`, `text`, required/optional): Outline the next actions or decisions you'll take based on the project's outcome.
* **What was the project scope?** (`project_scope`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the project's scope, including its objectives, deliverables, and timelines.
* **What are the project phases?** (`project_phases`, `text`, required/optional): Break down the project into its individual phases and describe each phase's objectives and key activities.
* **What were the project deliverables?** (`project_deliverables`, `text`, required/optional): List the specific deliverables that were expected from the project.
* **What was the project timeline?** (`project_timing`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the project's schedule, including deadlines and milestones.
* **What were the project resources?** (`project_resources`, `text`, required/optional): Identify the people, materials, and equipment required for the project.
* **Who were the project participants?** (`project_participants`, `text`, required/optional): List the individuals or teams involved in the project.
* **How satisfied are you with this project?** (`project_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the project.
* **What can be improved in this project?** (`project_improvements`, `text`, required/optional): Suggest areas for improvement for future project iterations.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with this project?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or multiple options that best describe your overall satisfaction with the project.

Note: Please answer all fields to provide a comprehensive feedback.
