# Financial Advisor Annual Review Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Advisor Annual Review Survey is designed to gather information from financial advisors to assess their performance and identify areas for improvement. This form collects data on various aspects of a financial advisor's work, including their contact information, professional experience, and specific areas of expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
3. **Email**: Enter your official email address used for communication.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
5. **Address**: Enter your work address or the address where you can be reached for work-related purposes.
6. **Performance**: Briefly describe your performance and accomplishments during the review period.
7. **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your work or areas for improvement.
8. **Category**: Select one of the following categories that best describes your area of expertise:
	* Financial Planning
	* Retirement Planning
	* Estate Planning
	* Investment Management
	* Tax Planning
	* Risk Management
9. **Other (please specify)**: If you don't see your area of expertise listed above, select "Other" and describe it in the field below.
10. **Other category**: Enter the specific area of expertise you selected in the field above.
11. **Year**: Enter the year(s) you have worked in this field.
12. **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth.
13. **Time worked**: Enter the total amount of time you have worked in this field.
14. **Date of last review**: Enter the date of your last review.
15. **Financial institution**: Enter the name of the financial institution you work with or are associated with.
16. **Bank account**: Enter any relevant bank account information.
17. **Investment account**: Enter any relevant investment account information.
18. **Retirement account**: Enter any relevant retirement account information.
19. **Social Security number**: **Note:** This field is not actually a standard form field in the US. This field might not be required for international users. In the US, it's unlikely to use this field for security or privacy reasons.

## Tips
* Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Complete the form honestly and thoroughly, as it will help in evaluating your performance and providing feedback.
