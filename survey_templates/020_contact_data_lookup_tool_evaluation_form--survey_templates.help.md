# Contact Data Lookup Tool Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate contact data lookup tools used by your organization. It will help us understand their performance, usability, and overall effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the name of the tool being evaluated.
2. Select the primary purpose of using the tool.
3. Rate the tool's data accuracy and completeness.
4. Rate the tool's lookup speed and response time.
5. Evaluate the tool's ease of use and interface.
6. Assess the tool's integration capabilities with existing systems.
7. Choose the tool's pricing model.
8. Rate the tool's value for money.
9. Provide a final recommendation for the tool.
10. Enter your name and the date of evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Lookup Tool Being Evaluated** (`lookup_tool_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the tool being evaluated.
* **Tool Vendor Name** (`tool_vendor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the tool's vendor or provider. (Optional)
* **Primary Purpose of Tool Usage** (`tool_usage_purpose`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the reasons why you are using this tool (Lead Generation, Data Enrichment, Sales Prospecting, Marketing Campaigns, Customer Research).
* **Data Accuracy and Completeness** (`rating_data_accuracy`, `select_one`, required): Rate the tool's data accuracy and completeness (Poor, 2, 3, 4, 5 - Excellent).
* **Lookup Speed and Response Time** (`rating_lookup_speed`, `select_one`, required): Rate the tool's lookup speed and response time (1 - Slow, 2, 3, Average, 4, 5 - Fast).
* **Ease of Use and Interface** (`rating_ease_of_use`, `select_one`, required): Rate the tool's ease of use and interface (1 - Difficult, 2, 3 - Neutral, 4, 5 - Easy).
* **Integration with Existing Systems** (`integration_capabilities`, `select_one`, required): Rate the tool's integration with existing systems (Very Poorly, Poorly, Moderately, Well, Very Well).
* **Tool Pricing Model** (`tool_pricing_model`, `select_one`, required): Choose the tool's pricing model (Pay-per-lookup, Monthly Subscription, Annual Subscription, Credit-based System).
* **Value for Money Assessment** (`rating_value_for_money`, `select_one`, required): Rate the tool's value for money (Poor Value, Below Average, Good Value, Excellent Value).
* **Overall Tool Rating** (`overall_tool_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the tool overall (1 - Not Recommended, 2, 3 - Neutral, 4, 5 - Highly Recommended).
* **Biggest Strength of Tool** (`biggest_strength_of_tool`, `text`, optional): Describe the tool's biggest strength.
* **Biggest Weakness of Tool** (`biggest_weakness_of_tool`, `text`, optional): Describe the tool's biggest weakness.
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of evaluation.
