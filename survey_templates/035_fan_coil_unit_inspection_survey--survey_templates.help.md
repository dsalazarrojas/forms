# Fan Coil Unit Inspection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fan Coil Unit Inspection Survey is a tool used to collect and record data about fan coil units in a building. This information can help maintenance personnel or facilities staff identify potential issues and make informed maintenance decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the fan coil unit being inspected.
2. Select the location of the fan coil unit within the building.
3. Enter the model number of the fan coil unit.
4. Record the capacity of the fan coil unit.
5. Enter the date of the inspection.
6. Select the current status of the fan coil unit (Active or Inactive).
7. (Optional) Report any defects found during the inspection.
8. (Optional) Enter any comments or notes about the fan coil unit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fan Coil Unit Name (1)**: Enter the name of the fan coil unit being inspected.
* **Location (2)**: Select the location within the building where the fan coil unit is installed.
* **Model Number (3)**: Enter the model number of the fan coil unit.
* **Capacity (4)**: Record the capacity of the fan coil unit.
* **Inspection Date (5)**: Enter the date the inspection was performed.
* **Status (6)**: Select the current status of the fan coil unit (Active or Inactive).
* **Defects (7)**: If any defects were found during the inspection, select the corresponding option(s) that apply.
* **Comments (8)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the fan coil unit.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter the correct information for each field to ensure accurate data collection.
- Use the "Defects" field to report any issues found during the inspection, but only if applicable.
- Keep the "Comments" field for additional notes or explanations that may not fit into the other fields.
