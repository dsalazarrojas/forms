# Earth In The Solar System Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Earth In The Solar System Survey is a form designed to gather information about your preferences related to the planets in our solar system. It consists of questions that will help us understand your favorite planet, the planet you were born on, and other personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a title for your survey.
2. Enter your name and email address.
3. Select one or more planets that you are interested in.
4. Choose your favorite planet among the options.
5. Answer question 1 (note: this is an open-ended question).
6. Provide your time of birth (if you're willing to disclose this information).
7. Enter your birth year and month.
8. Provide your birth date and time (if you're willing to disclose this information).
9. Select the size of the planet you're interested in (note: the options for this field are not clearly defined; we'll assume it's a numerical value representing the size of the planet in some unit, e.g., kilometers or miles).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`): This is the title of your survey. You can enter any text that you'd like to describe your response.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your full name as you'd like to be identified.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your valid email address for us to contact you (if you're willing to share your email).
* **Planet** (`planet_selected`): Select one or more planets from the list that you are interested in (Mars, Jupiter, Earth, Venus, Saturn, Uranus, and Pluto).
* **Favorite Planet** (`favorite_planet`): Choose your favorite planet among the options.
* **Question 1** (`question_1`): Answer question 1 in the provided space. This is an open-ended question, so feel free to write anything you'd like.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_born`): If you're willing to disclose your time of birth, enter it here. This field is for your convenience; you can leave it blank if you'd rather not share this information.
* **Year Born** (`year_born`): Enter the year you were born.
* **Month Born** (`month_born`): Enter the month you were born.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_born`): If you're willing to disclose your date of birth, enter it here. This field is optional.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_born`): If you're willing to disclose your time of birth, enter it here. This field is optional.
* **Planet Size** (`planet_size`): Select the size of the planet you're interested in (note: the units of measurement are not defined; assume a numerical value).

## Tips
* Please answer questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or leave the field blank.
* Remember to submit your form when you're ready to complete it.
