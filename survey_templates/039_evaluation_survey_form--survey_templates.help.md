# Evaluation Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evaluation Survey Form is designed to gather information about your experience and perceptions at this school. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand your needs and make improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required").
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable options.
4. For open-ended questions, provide detailed and honest answers.
5. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (id: 1, text, required: false): Briefly introduce yourself, if you wish.
	+ This field is optional and only necessary if you want to provide a personal context for your answers.
* **What do you think about your experience at this school?** (id: 2, text, required: true): Share your thoughts and feelings about your experience at the school.
	+ Be honest and provide detailed feedback about your experience.
* **What do you think about the quality of the education offered by this school?** (id: 3, text, required: true): Rate the quality of education you have received.
	+ Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your rating.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (id: 4, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them.
	+ Your suggestions will help us make informed decisions about future changes.
* **Rating** (id: 5, number, required: true): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to rate your overall experience.
	+ Be honest and provide a rating based on your experience.
* **Email Address** (id: 6, email, required: true): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
	+ This will help us contact you for further discussions or follow-up.
* **Phone Number** (id: 7, text, required: false): If you wish to be contacted by phone, please provide your number.
	+ This is optional, but it will help us reach you more efficiently.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 8, date, required: false): If you wish to provide this information for further reference.
	+ This is optional and only for internal use.
* **Time of Completion** (id: 9, time, required: false): If you wish to provide this information for further reference.
	+ This is optional and only for internal use.
* **Select all that apply** (id: 10, select_multiple, required: true): Select all options that apply to your experience.
	+ Be honest and select all applicable options.
* **Select one** (id: 11, select_one, required: false): Select one option that best represents your experience.
	+ Choose the option that best matches your opinion.
* **Note** (id: 12, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes you have about your experience.
	+ This is optional and for your convenience to add further thoughts.
* **Submit** (id: 13, text, required: false): Submit your answers and complete the survey.
* **Cancel** (id: 14, text, required: false): Cancel your answers and exit the survey.
* **Back** (id: 15, text, required: false): Return to a previous page in the survey.
* **Next** (id: 16, text, required: false): Proceed to the next page in the survey.
* **Previous** (id: 17, text, required: false): Return to the previous page in the survey.
* **Submit and quit** (id: 18, text, required: false): Submit your answers and exit the survey.
* **Save and exit** (id: 19, text, required: false): Save your answers but exit without submitting.
* **Continue with survey** (id: 20, text, required: false): Continue with the survey without submitting your answers.
* **Submit and email** (id: 21, text, required: false): Submit your answers and email them for further reference.
