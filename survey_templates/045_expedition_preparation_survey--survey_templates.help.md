# Expedition Preparation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your past experiences and health status to help us tailor the expedition to your needs and ensure your safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. For each question, select the option that best describes your situation.
2. If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please provide details in the text fields (participant_medical_info_1 to participant_medical_info_17).
3. Make sure to provide accurate and honest information, as it will help us provide the best possible experience for you.
