# Group Exercise Class Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about participant experiences and feedback in group exercise classes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with relevant information for the group exercise class you are reporting on.
2. Ensure you are honest and thorough in your responses.
3. Complete all required fields, indicated by a `*`.
