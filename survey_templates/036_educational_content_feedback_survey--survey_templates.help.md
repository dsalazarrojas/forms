# Educational Content Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the educational content presented to you. Your input will help us improve the content and ensure it meets the needs of our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* The "educational_content" field: This is a text box where you can provide detailed feedback about the educational content presented to you.
2. Optional fields are not required, so you may choose to fill them in or skip them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Educational Content**: Enter your feedback about the educational content presented to you. This could include any issues you encountered, suggestions for improvement, or comments on what worked well.
* **Feedback**: Enter any additional feedback you may have about the educational content.
* **Name**: Enter your name if you would like to be credited for your feedback.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to receive a copy of your feedback or if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone**: This field is optional and not required. You can enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **Select One**: Choose "Yes" if you found the educational content helpful, or "No" if it was not helpful.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose one or more options that describe why the content was not helpful (e.g. "too complex", "too simple", etc.).
* **Date**: Enter the date you took the educational content (e.g. a course, tutorial, etc.).
* **Time**: Enter the time you took the educational content.
* **Integer**: This field is currently unused, please do not fill it in.

## Tips
* Please keep your feedback concise and clear.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback is highly appreciated and will help us improve the educational content.
