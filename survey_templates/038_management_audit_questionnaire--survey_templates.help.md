# Management Audit Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your management performance in order to evaluate and improve it. It will take about 15-20 minutes to complete, and will help us understand your current management practices, goals, and performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
2. Please fill out one page at a time, following the guide below for each page.
3. Use the provided options in the select_multiple fields, unless you have a different option that is not listed.
4. You can type in the text fields as much or as little as you like, but please ensure that your answer is clear and concise.

* **What are your long-term goals?** (long_term_goals, text, required): This field is where you will specify your long-term goals. These goals are expected to be achievable within a long period of time, e.g., a year or more. Please provide a brief description of your long-term goals.
* **How do you currently achieve your goals?** (how_goals_are_achieved, select_multiple, required): This field will ask you about your current methods of achieving your goals. Please select all the methods that apply to you, such as Regularly, Sporadically, etc.
* **How often do you plan to achieve your goals?** (how_goals_are_planned, select_multiple, required): This field will ask you about your planned frequency of achieving your goals. Please select all the frequencies that apply to you, such as Monthly, Quarterly, etc.
* **What are your current evaluation methods?** (evaluation_methods, text, required): This field is where you will specify your current evaluation methods. Please provide a brief description of your evaluation methods, e.g., regular meetings, project progress reports, etc.
* **How much do you agree or disagree with the following statements?** (agreement_statements, select_multiple, required): This field will ask you to select how much you agree or disagree with a series of statements. Please select all the options that apply to you for each statement.

* **What are your comments about your performance?** (performance_comment, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any comments about your performance. Please feel free to provide any feedback or thoughts about your performance.
* **What are your suggestions for improvement?** (suggestion_for_improvement, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any suggestions for improvement. Please feel free to provide any ideas or suggestions about how you could improve your performance.
* **How do you rate your overall performance?** (self_rating, select_multiple, required): This field will ask you to rate your overall performance. Please select one of the options, e.g., Good, Fair, etc.
* **What are your comments?** (end_comments, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback. Please feel free to provide any final thoughts or comments about your performance.
