# Program Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Program Evaluation Survey is designed to gather your feedback about a program. Your input will help us understand your participation, the effectiveness of the program, and areas that need improvement. Please take a few minutes to share your honest feedback, and we'll use it to refine the program for better results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and make sure you understand each question before answering.
2. Answer each question in a honest and thorough manner.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Enter your contact information if you wish to receive follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Survey Participation
* **Survey Participation** (`survey_participation`, note, optional): Briefly describe your participation in the program, including how you engaged with it.

### 2. Program Effectiveness
* **Program Effectiveness** (`program_effectiveness`, note, optional): Share your overall assessment of the program's effectiveness in meeting your needs.

### 3. Participant Feedback
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, note, optional): Provide your thoughts on what you liked or disliked about the program.

### 4. Program Changes
* **Program Changes** (`program_changes`, note, optional): If you have suggestions for program improvements, describe them here.

### 5. Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, note, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.

### 6. Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Share any additional feedback or thoughts you have about the program.

### 7. Program Improvement
* **Program Improvement** (`program_improvement`, note, optional): Suggest ways to improve the program.

### 8. Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, note, optional): Please provide your name, email, and phone number (if you wish to receive follow-up communication).

### 9. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to receive follow-up communication from us.

### 10. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to receive follow-up communication from us.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing feedback is greatly appreciated!
