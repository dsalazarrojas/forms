# iso_9001_gap_analysis_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data for a survey on the gap analysis between employee and manager perceptions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the questions carefully and answer truthfully. Ensure that all required fields are filled in. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction (1)** (`text`, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of this form.
  - Please provide a short introduction about the purpose and scope of this form.
- **Company (2)** (`text`, required): Enter your company name.
  - This field is used to identify the company being surveyed.
- **CompanyAddress (3)** (`text`, optional): Enter your company address.
  - This field is used to provide the company's address for reference purposes.
- **ManagerName (4)** (`text`, optional): Enter your manager's name.
  - This field is used to identify your manager's name for reference purposes.
- **Department (5)** (`text`, optional): Enter your department.
  - This field is used to identify the department being surveyed.
- **ManagerTitle (6)** (`text`, optional): Enter your manager's title.
  - This field is used to identify your manager's title for reference purposes.
- **Practice (7)** (`text`, required): Enter the practice or activity you are familiar with.
  - This field is used to identify a specific practice or activity that the survey is focused on.
- **GapAnalysis (8)** (`text`, required): Enter your perception of the gap between employee and manager best practices.
  - This field is used to provide your perception of the gap and any comments you have.
- **Comments (9)** (`text`, optional): Enter any comments about your experience.
  - This field is for any additional comments or notes about your experience.
- **SubmittedBy (10)** (`select_one`, required): Select who submitted this form.
  - Select from the provided list of managers to identify who submitted this form.
- **SubmittedAt (11)** (`date`, required): Enter the date this form was submitted.
  - This field is used to record the date and time the form was submitted.
- **SubmittedTime (12)** (`time`, required): Enter the time this form was submitted.
  - This field is used to record the time of submission.
- **CompanyPhone (13)** (`text`, optional): Enter your company phone number.
  - This field is used to provide your company's phone number for reference purposes.
- **CompanyEmail (14)** (`email`, optional): Enter your company email.
  - This field is used to provide your company's email address for reference purposes.
- **PracticeFrequency (15)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the frequency of practice.
  - Select from the provided list to identify how often the practice or activity is performed.
- **SubmittedByName (16)** (`text`, optional): Enter your name.
  - This field is used to identify your name for reference purposes.
- **SubmittedByEmail (17)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email.
  - This field is used to identify your email for reference purposes.
- **SubmittedByPhone (18)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
  - This field is used to identify your phone number for reference purposes.
- **Comments (19)** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional comments.
  - This field is for any additional comments or notes about your experience.
- **SubmittedAtDate (20)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
  - This field is not required but will be used to record the date and time the form was submitted.
- **SubmittedTime (21)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.
  - This field is not required but will be used to record the time of submission.
- **SubmittedFrequency (22)** (`text`, optional): Enter the frequency of submission.
  - This field is used to identify the frequency of submission.
- **SubmittedLocation (23)** (`text`, optional): Enter the location where the form was submitted.
  - This field is used to provide the location where the form was submitted.
- **SubmittedDepartment (24)** (`text`, optional): Enter the department where the form was submitted.
  - This field is used to identify the department where the form was submitted.
- **SubmittedByTitle (25)** (`text`, optional): Enter your title.
  - This field is used to identify your title for reference purposes.
