# training_pre_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help employees complete the training pre-assessment survey form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to understand its purpose and relevance.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Ensure you have the necessary information before filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Survey Questions (1)** (`text`, required)
* This field is a text input where you can provide any additional comments or questions related to your training experience.

### **Employee ID (2)** (`number`, required)
* This field is for your employee ID number, which is a unique identifier assigned to you by the organization.

### **Training Program (3)** (`text`, optional)
* This field is for you to specify the training program you are currently undergoing.

### **Skills Assessed (4)** (`select_one`, required)
* Select the skills that are being assessed during your training. 

### **Knowledge Area (5)** (`select_multiple`, required)
* Choose the areas of knowledge that you are currently assessing during your training.

### **Training Status (6)** (`select_one`, required)
* Select the current status of your training, such as completed or not completed.

### **Training Start Date (7)** (`date`, required)
* Enter the start date of your training.

### **Training End Date (8)** (`date`, required)
* Enter the end date of your training.

### **Training Hours (9)** (`number`, optional)
* If applicable, enter the total training hours.

### **Completed Training Hours (10)** (`number`, optional)
* If applicable, enter the completed training hours.

### **Training Hours Remaining (11)** (`number`, optional)
* If applicable, enter the training hours remaining.

### **Training Percentage Complete (12)** (`number`, required)
* Enter the percentage of training completed.

### **Training Completion Date (13)** (`date`, required)
* Enter the completion date of your training.

### **Employee Comments (14)** (`note`, optional)
* Provide any additional comments or feedback about your training experience.

### **Manager Comments (15)** (`note`, optional)
* If applicable, provide comments from your manager about your training experience.

### **Assigned Tool (16)** (`select_one`, required)
* Select the tool assigned to you for training.

### **Completed Training (17)** (`select_one`, required)
* Select whether your training is completed or not completed.
