# library_visitor_new_service_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users who have interacted with the library service, helping the library to understand their experiences, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions about service development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**
   - This is a note section to provide an introduction or brief context about the form. You can read through this section to understand the form's purpose and what it aims to achieve.

2. **Which library service did you use today?**
   - Select the service you interacted with from the options provided: Service 1, Service 2, or Service 3.
   - If none of the provided services match what you used, you can select 'Other' and provide more details in the next field.

3. **Other (if applicable)**
   - If you selected 'Other', provide a brief description of the service you used in this text field. This is to ensure that the library can identify and understand your experience.

4. **How would you rate this service?**
   - Rate the service on a scale from 0.5 to 5, where 0.5 is the lowest and 5 is the highest rating.

5. **Rating Scale**
   - This scale shows the options you can choose from when rating the service.

6. **Do you have any suggestions for this service?**
   - Share any suggestions or comments you have regarding the service in this text field. This could include anything from improvements, new features, or just general comments.

7. **Contact details**
   - If you wish to be contacted by the library or have any follow-up questions, provide your contact information (email) here. This is optional but can aid in any further communication regarding your feedback.

8. **How long did you spend using the service?**
   - Provide an estimate of the time you spent using the service in this field. This gives the library insight into your interaction duration.

9. **Date of Visit**
   - Select the date you visited the library or used the service. This is to help the library track the timeline of your interaction.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and honest as possible in your feedback, as this helps the library to better understand your experience and make informed improvements.
- Ensure that you select the correct service you used, and if you used an 'Other' service, provide an accurate description in the next field.
- The rating scale is there to give a clear indication of your satisfaction level with the service.
