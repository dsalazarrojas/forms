# Hospital Care Models Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on hospital care models, aiming to improve the quality and effectiveness of care services provided to patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on different aspects of the care models. Answer each section carefully, providing as much detail as needed.
2. If you have experience with more than one care model, you can select multiple options for the "Category" and "Care Models" fields.
3. For the "Description" field, please provide a brief explanation of your experience with the care model.
4. The "How would you rate your overall experience?" question has four rating options: Very Good, Good, Fair, and Bad. Select the option that best describes your experience.
5. In the "Which of the following care models have you been a part of?" section, select all care models that apply to your experience.
6. If you have comments or suggestions for the care models, use the "Do you have any comments about our care models?" field.
7. For "How satisfied are you with our care models?", select the option that best describes your level of satisfaction.
8. Finally, if you have any suggestions for improvements in the care models, use the "What would you like to see improved in our care models?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hospital Care Models Feedback Survey** (survey_title, text, optional): This is the title of the form. Please enter your name or a relevant title to help us understand your feedback.
* **Category** (category, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more categories that apply to your experience with the care models. This will help us filter and analyze your feedback.
* **Description** (description, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your experience with the care models. This will help us understand your context and feedback.
* **How would you rate your overall experience?** (patient_feedback, select_one, optional): Rate your overall experience with the care models using the provided options.
* **Which of the following care models have you been a part of?** (care_models, select_multiple, optional): Select all care models that apply to your experience.
* **Do you have any comments about our care models?** (care_model_comments, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **How satisfied are you with our care models?** (patient_satisfaction, select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes your level of satisfaction.
* **What would you like to see improved in our care models?** (care_model_improvement, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvements, please provide them here.
