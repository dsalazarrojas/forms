# Skydiving Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skydiving Event Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from participants after a skydiving event. This survey aims to assess overall participant satisfaction, instructor performance, and event details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Follow the numbered pages as they appear on your screen.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Submit your survey once you have completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Participant Rating
* **Overall Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_rating`, number, required: false):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the event out of 5.
### Page 2: Additional Comments
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_rating`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe your experience with the event in a short statement.
### Page 3: Instructor Evaluation
* **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, number, required: false):
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the instructor out of 5.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the instructor who led the event.
### Page 4: Event Details
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the event as provided by the instructor.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the location where the event took place.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the start and end time of the event.
### Page 5: Additional Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional feedback or comments about the event.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
- If you are unsure of the correct answer, please leave it blank.
- Please do not submit your survey until you have completed all pages.
