# Educational Workshop Quality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the Educational Workshop Quality Assessment form, which evaluates the quality of educational workshops by gathering participant feedback and ratings on key aspects of the session.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please go through each page and fill in the required information for each field. Make sure to answer honestly and thoroughly, providing detailed feedback where possible.
2.  Start with the "Session Feedback" page, where you'll provide a brief description of your experience during the session.
3.  Move on to the "Session Satisfaction" page, where you'll rate your level of satisfaction with the session using a 1-5 scale.
4.  In the "Next Workshop" page, indicate if you are planning to attend another workshop in the series or if you are not sure.
5.  On the "Comments" page, feel free to include any additional comments or suggestions you might have about the workshop.
6.  In the "Session Improvement" page, suggest areas where the workshop could be improved.
7.  The "Session Improvement Score" page allows you to rate your rating of the session's overall improvement.
8.  On the "Session Improvement Plan" page, suggest concrete actions that could be taken to address your suggested improvements.
9.  Finally, in the "Session Follow-up" page, rate your experience with follow-up activities after the session.
10. Complete this form by providing a rating for "Session Follow-up Score" using the 1-5 scale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Session Feedback (1)** (required): A space to provide a brief description of your experience during the session.
*   **Session Satisfaction (2)** (required): Rate your level of satisfaction with the session using the 1-5 scale.
*   **Next Workshop (4)** (required): Indicate if you plan to attend another workshop in the series or if you're not sure.
*   **Comments (5)** (optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about the workshop.
*   **Session Improvement (6)** (optional): Suggest areas where the workshop could be improved.
*   **Session Improvement Score (7)** (optional): Rate your rating of the session's overall improvement.
*   **Session Improvement Plan (8)** (optional): Suggest concrete actions that could be taken to address your suggested improvements.
*   **Session Follow-up (9)** (optional): Describe your experience with follow-up activities after the session.
*   **Session Follow-up Score (10)** (optional): Rate your experience with follow-up activities after the session.

## Tips

*   Please be as specific as possible in your feedback and ratings.
*   Use the provided scales and hints for guidance when answering.
*   If a question is not applicable or does not apply to you, feel free to mark it as "Not Applicable" or "Not Sure" to move forward with the form.
