# MultiPage Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of a course in various aspects, such as subject matter, materials, instructing techniques, and overall experience. It is a multi-page form that allows course participants to provide feedback on different sections of the course, including introduction, subject matter, materials, instructing techniques, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the different pages and fill in the relevant sections. Each page has a specific section that you are required to fill in, as denoted by the red asterisk (*) symbol next to the page name. You can type in your answers in the text boxes provided, select from the options for multiple-choice questions, or provide a date/time in the designated fields.
