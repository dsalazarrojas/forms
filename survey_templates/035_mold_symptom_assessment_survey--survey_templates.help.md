# Mold Symptom Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mold Symptom Assessment Survey is designed to collect information about mold symptoms, exposure history, and related health effects. This form helps healthcare professionals or researchers understand the extent of mold exposure and its impact on individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each question and understand its purpose.
2. Answer each question truthfully and as detailed as possible.
3. For some fields, you may be asked the same type of question multiple times. Please provide separate and specific answers for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is an optional introduction or description field where you can briefly describe your situation or any relevant information you'd like to share.
* **Building Information** (`Building Information`, `note`, required: false): Similar to the introduction, this field is also optional and serves as a place to provide any additional information about your building or environment where you suspect mold exposure.
* **Mold Symptoms** (`Mold Symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the symptoms you have experienced due to mold exposure. The options are `Yes` or `No`.
* **Symptoms Description** (`Symptoms Description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your mold symptoms, including how they started, when you first noticed them, and any patterns or triggers you've observed.
* **Exposure History** (`Exposure History`, `text`, required: false): Describe your mold exposure history, including when and where you were exposed, and any relevant details about the exposure.
* **How often do you experience mold symptoms?** (`How often do you experience mold symptoms?`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how often you experience mold symptoms from the options: `Daily`, `Occasionally`, `Rarely`, or `Never`.
* **Location of exposure** (`Location of exposure`, `text`, required: false): Provide the location(s) where you were exposed to mold.
* **Duration of symptoms** (`Duration of symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if you have experienced mold symptoms for a duration of `Yes` or `No`.
* **Exposure duration** (`Exposure duration`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose if your exposure to mold was for a duration of `Yes` or `No`.
* **Health effects** (`Health effects`, `text`, required: false): Describe any health effects you've experienced due to mold exposure.
* **Have you sought medical attention due to mold exposure?** (`Have you sought medical attention due to mold exposure?`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose if you've sought medical attention due to mold exposure from the options: `True` or `False`.
* **Medical results** (`Medical results`, `text`, required: false): Describe the medical results or outcomes of your treatment or consultation.
* **Will you follow up with your healthcare provider?** (`Will you follow up with your healthcare provider?`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose if you plan to follow up with your healthcare provider from the options: `True` or `False`.
* **Follow-up scheduled** (`Follow-up scheduled`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your scheduled follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up completed** (`Follow-up completed`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your follow-up appointment if you've already been.
* **Follow-up outcome** (`Follow-up outcome`, `text`, required: false): Describe the outcome of your follow-up appointment.
* **Next steps** (`Next steps`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about next steps you plan to take or actions you've already taken.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, required: false): This is an optional field where you can sign your name to confirm your responses.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number for any follow-up communication.
* **Follow-up doctor name** (`Follow-up doctor name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the doctor who will follow up with you.
* **Doctor's specialty** (`Doctor's specialty`, `text`, required: false): Enter the doctor's specialty who will follow up with you.
* **Doctor's phone number** (`Doctor's phone number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the doctor's phone number who will follow up with you.
* **Doctor's notes** (`Doctor's notes`, `text`, required: false): This is an optional field where the doctor can provide any additional notes.
* **Doctor's diagnosis** (`Doctor's diagnosis`, `text`, required: false): Enter the doctor's diagnosis.
* **Doctor's prescription** (`Doctor's prescription`, `text`, required: false): Enter the doctor's prescription.

By following this guide, you'll be able to fill out the Mold Symptom Assessment Survey accurately and effectively, providing valuable information to healthcare professionals or researchers.
