# Calmness Coaching Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to help us understand your current situation, stress levels, and goals for coaching. It will help us tailor our approach to provide the best possible support for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information, and make sure to answer all required questions truthfully.
2. Select all applicable options for each field.
3. If you need any help or clarification, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_full_name`, `text`, required): This is the first step in getting to know you. Please fill out your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, required): We will use this email to communicate with you and send you resources and scheduling information.
* **Rate your current daily stress level** (`current_stress_level`, `number`, required): This is a self-assessment of your current stress level, from 1 (Very Low) to 10 (Extremely High).
* **Primary Sources of Stress** (`stressors_list`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all sources of stress that apply to you, such as Career and Work, Family and Relationships, etc.
* **How do you currently handle stress?** (`current_coping_methods`, `text`, optional): Please share with us how you usually cope with stress, if you'd like.
* **Physical Symptoms of Stress** (`physical_symptoms_stress`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to share, please list any physical symptoms of stress you're experiencing.
* **Coaching Goals** (`coaching_goals_header`, `note`, required): This section is for us to get a better understanding of your goals and expectations from coaching.
* **What is your primary goal for coaching?** (`primary_goal`, `text`, required): Please describe your main objective for working with us.
* **Tools you are interested in exploring** (`preferred_tools`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all tools you'd like to explore during our coaching sessions.
* **Commitment Level** (`commitment_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose how ready you are to make changes, from Ready for immediate action to Hesitant but willing to try.
* **General Weekly Availability** (`availability_sessions`, `text`, required): Please indicate your general availability for coaching sessions.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_wellness`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to share any other relevant information, feel free to add it here.
* **How did you find our coaching service?** (`referral_info`, `text`, optional): If you were referred to us, how did you find our service?
