# Personal Reset And Realignment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal information and insights from users about the category they belong to. It will help us understand their thoughts and preferences to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category that you belong to.
2. Provide a brief description of your category.
3. Enter a valid email address.
4. Enter a valid phone number.
5. Share any next steps or additional information you think would be helpful.
6. Optionally, include any reflection insights or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category that you belong to. This will help us understand your context and provide relevant information.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your category. This will help us understand your specific context and needs.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address. This will help us communicate with you more effectively.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter a valid phone number. This will help us contact you if needed.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required: false): Share any next steps or additional information you think would be helpful.
* **Reflection Insights** (`reflection_insights`, note, required: false): Optionally, include any reflection insights or comments you have.
