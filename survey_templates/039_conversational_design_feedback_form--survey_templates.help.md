# Conversational Design Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users who have interacted with our bot to help us improve its performance and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form, answering questions based on your experience with our bot.
2. Take your time, and answer honestly - your feedback is invaluable to us!
3. If a question doesn't apply to your specific interaction, just skip it and move on to the next one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (`respondent_name`, text, optional)**: Your name, which will be used for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address (`email`, email, optional)**: Your email address, which will be used for follow-up purposes.
* **Name of Bot or Assistant (`bot_name`, text, required)**: The name of the bot you interacted with. This is a crucial field to help us identify the specific bot.
* **Platform Used (`platform_used`, select_one, required)**: The platform where you interacted with our bot (e.g., Website, Mobile App, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, etc.).
* **Date of Interaction (`interaction_date`, date, required)**: The date and time when you interacted with our bot.
* **What task were you trying to complete (`task_attempted`, text, required)**: A brief description of the task you were trying to accomplish.
* **Ease of interaction (`ease_of_interaction`, select_one, required)**: How easy or difficult you found it to interact with our bot.
* **How well did the bot understand your requests (`understood_requests`, select_one, required)**: How well our bot understood your requests and questions.
* **Clarity of bot responses (`response_clarity`, select_one, required)**: How clear were our bot's responses.
* **Relevance of responses (`response_relevance`, select_one, required)**: How relevant our bot's responses were.
* **How natural did the conversation feel (`naturalness`, select_one, required)**: How natural did you feel the conversation was.
* **Pleasant personality of the bot (`personality`, select_one, required)**: Did our bot have a pleasant personality.
* **How well did it handle conversation context (`context_handling`, select_one, required)**: How well our bot handled conversation context.
* **Did you complete your task (`task_completed`, select_one, required)**: Were you able to complete your task.
* **How long did it take (`time_to_complete`, select_one, required)**: How long it took to complete your task.
* **Number of steps or messages exchanged (`steps_required`, number, optional)**: An approximate number of steps or messages exchanged.
* **Did you encounter any errors (`errors_encountered`, select_one, required)**: Did you encounter any errors.
* **Describe the errors (`error_description`, text, optional)**: If you encountered errors, describe them here.
* **How were errors handled (`error_resolution`, select_one, optional)**: How were errors handled.
* **Did you need to speak to a human (`needed_human_agent`, select_one, optional)**: Did you need to speak to a human during the interaction.
* **How smooth was the handoff process (`handoff_smoothness`, select_one, optional)**: If you needed to speak to a human, how smooth was the handoff process.
* **Overall satisfaction (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required)**: Your overall satisfaction with our bot.
* **Would you use this bot again (`would_use_again`, select_one, required)**: Would you use our bot again.
* **Would you recommend to others (`would_recommend`, select_one, required)**: Would you recommend our bot to others.
* **What did you like best (`best_aspects`, text, optional)**: What did you like best about our bot.
* **What needs improvement (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional)**: What areas our bot needs improvement in.
* **Features you wish it had (`feature_requests`, text, optional)**: What features you would like to see in our bot.
* **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, text, optional)**: Any additional feedback or comments you would like to share.
