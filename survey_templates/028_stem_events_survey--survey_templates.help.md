# STEM Events Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The STEM Events Survey is a tool to gather information about events related to science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) fields. This form helps us understand the scope and demographics of these events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event title in the "Event Title" field.
2. Select the participant type from the options provided in the "Participant Type" field.
3. Choose the participant's interests from the list in the "Participant Interests" field.
4. Enter any feedback you may have in the "Feedback" field.
5. Select the participant's needs from the options provided in the "Participant Needs" field.
6. Enter the participant's contact information in the "Participant Contact" field.
7. Fill in the name of the event's educator or organizer in the "Educator Name" field, if applicable.
8. Select the department related to the event's educator or organizer in the "Department" field, if applicable.
9. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
10. Select the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
11. Fill in the event's location in the "Event Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the event, briefly describing what it's about.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of participant you are or will be, such as "Event organizer", "Educator", "Participant", or "Others".
* **Participant Interests** (`participant_interests`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the interests of the participant, such as "Robotics", "Coding", "Science", "Art", or "History".
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Leave any feedback or comments about the event.
* **Participant Needs** (`participant_needs`, select_one, required: false): Select the needs of the participant, such as "Food", "Drinks", "Accommodation", or "Others".
* **Participant Contact** (`participant_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's contact information, such as email or phone number.
* **Educator Name** (`educator_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event's educator or organizer, if applicable.
* **Department** (`educator_department`, select_one, required: false): Select the department related to the event's educator or organizer, if applicable.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Select the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): Fill in the location of the event.
