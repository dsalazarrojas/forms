# provider_network_onboarding_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The provider network onboarding survey is used to collect information from providers during the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your provider organization from the available options.
2. Enter your contact name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select the onboarding date for your organization.
6. Select the current status of your onboarding process.
7. Provide any feedback about your experience with the onboarding process.
8. Add any additional notes about your onboarding process.
9. Select your readiness score out of 10.
10. Provide additional insights about your readiness for the network.
11. Select your onboarding team members.
12. Select the date for follow-up communication.
13. Select the frequency of follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider Organization** (`provider_organization`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more options from the list of available provider organizations.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name to be contacted for follow-up communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, required): Select the date you started the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your onboarding process (e.g., pending, in progress, completed).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required): Provide any feedback or comments about your experience with the onboarding process.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your onboarding process.
* **Readiness Score** (`readiness_score`, number, required): Rate your readiness for the network out of 10.
* **Readiness Insights** (`readiness_insights`, text, required): Provide additional insights about your readiness for the network.
* **Onboarding Team** (`onboarding_team`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more members of your onboarding team.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date, required): Select the date for follow-up communication.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of follow-up communication (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct provider organization and onboarding team members.
* Enter accurate contact information, including email and phone number.
* Select the correct onboarding status and date.
* Provide clear feedback and notes about your experience.
* Rate your readiness score accurately.
* Select the correct follow-up frequency.
