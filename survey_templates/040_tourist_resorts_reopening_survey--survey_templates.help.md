# Tourist Resorts Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about how tourists were informed about the reopening of tourist resorts. Your input is crucial in helping us understand what worked best and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact the support team.
3. Answer each question to the best of your abilities, and make sure to submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1): This is a note-based field where you can provide any additional information or comments about the survey.
* **How did you hear about resorts reopening?** (2): Select the most relevant option that describes how you were informed about the reopening of tourist resorts. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Social media
	+ Option 2: News articles
	+ Option 3: Travel agency
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify in the introduction)
* **What services do you expect to implement?** (3): Choose all the services that you would like to implement for the reopening of tourist resorts. Please select all that apply:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Question 3 (no label)** (4): Please provide a short text-based answer to this question.
* **Question 4 (no label)** (5): Enter a numerical value to answer this question.
* **When would you like to visit?** (6): Select a date to answer this question.
* **Question 7 (no label)** (7): Please enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Additional comments** (8): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Answer all questions to the best of your abilities.
* Provide as much detail as possible when answering questions 3 and 8.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
