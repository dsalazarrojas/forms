# Return To Office Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect information about your work habits and preferences. This information will help us understand your needs and make informed decisions about work arrangements that support employee productivity and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select your department from the "Department" field. If you work in a different department, select "Other" and type in the department name.
3. Choose your preferred office hours from the "Office Hours" field.
4. Indicate if you plan to work from home on Fridays by answering "True" or "False" in the "Work From Home Fridays" field.
5. Select the number of days you plan to work from home from the "Work From Home Days" field.
6. Answer "True" or "False" in the "Work From Client Location" field, depending on whether you usually work from a client location.
7. Choose your preferred work location from the "Work Location" field.
8. Indicate the frequency of your manager's conversation with you, from the "Manager Conversation" field.
9. Write any additional feedback you may have in the "Feedback" field.
10. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
11. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
12. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Type in your employee name to identify yourself in the survey results.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list. If your department is not listed, choose "Other" and type in your department name.
* **Office Hours**: Choose your preferred office hours from 8am-5pm, 9am-4pm, 10am-3pm, or 11am-2pm.
* **Work From Home Fridays**: Indicate if you usually work from home on Fridays by answering "True" or "False".
* **Work From Home Days**: Select the number of days you plan to work from home.
* **Work From Client Location**: Answer "True" or "False" depending on whether you usually work from a client location.
* **Work Location**: Choose your preferred work location from Home, Office, or Remote.
* **Manager Conversation**: Select the frequency of your manager's conversation with you.
* **Feedback**: Write any feedback you have about your work arrangements.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your work experience.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Fill in your phone number.
