# Personal Hygiene Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Personal Hygiene Survey" is a form designed to collect information about an individual's personal hygiene practices. This information will be used to gather data on the effectiveness of personal hygiene habits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions in this form as honestly and consistently as possible.
2. For questions with options (e.g. deodorant use, soap use), select one of the options that best describes your personal preference.
3. For questions asking for frequency (e.g. hand washing frequency), enter a numerical value (e.g. 1, 2, 3, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your personal hygiene practices** (`personal_hygiene_practices`): This question is an open-ended question that asks for a brief description of your personal hygiene practices. Please provide a concise and honest answer.
* **Hand washing frequency** (`handwashing_frequency`): This question asks how often you wash your hands. Please enter a numerical value indicating how many times you wash your hands in a typical day.
* **Tooth brushing frequency** (`toothbrushing_frequency`): This question asks how often you brush your teeth. Please enter a numerical value indicating how many times you brush your teeth in a typical day.
* **Mouth washing frequency** (`mouth_washing_frequency`): This question asks how often you wash your mouth. Please enter a numerical value indicating how many times you wash your mouth in a typical day.
* **Hair washing frequency** (`hair_washing_frequency`): This question asks how often you wash your hair. Please enter a numerical value indicating how many times you wash your hair in a typical day.
* **Body washing frequency** (`body_washing_frequency`): This question asks how often you wash your body. Please enter a numerical value indicating how many times you wash your body in a typical day.
* **Showering frequency** (`showering_frequency`): This question asks how often you take showers. Please enter a numerical value indicating how many times you take showers in a typical day.
* **Deodorant use** (`deodorant_use`): This question asks if you use deodorant. Please select one of the options: Deodorant User, Non-User, or Unsure.
* **Soap use** (`soap_use`): This question asks about your soap usage. Please select one of the options: Always, Sometimes, Never, or Unsure.
* **Hand sanitizer use** (`hand_sanitizer_use`): This question asks if you use hand sanitizer. Please select multiple options that apply to you.
* **Face washing frequency** (`face_washing_frequency`): This question asks how often you wash your face. Please enter a numerical value indicating how many times you wash your face in a typical day.
* **Armpit washing frequency** (`armpit_washing_frequency`): This question asks how often you wash under your arms. Please enter a numerical value indicating how many times you wash under your arms in a typical day.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions as honestly and consistently as possible.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select the "Unsure" option.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities.
* Please complete the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible to ensure the data collected is useful for its intended purpose.
