# Researcher Type Identification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Researcher Type Identification Survey is designed to collect information from researchers to better understand their research types, habits, motivations, focus areas, and goals. This survey will help in identifying and categorizing researchers for various programs and initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Research Type**: Select one of the given options to identify your research type.
2. **Institution**: Enter your institution's name where you work.
3. **Department**: Enter your department's name (optional).
4. **Research Habits**: Answer the time-related question as accurately as possible.
5. **Research Motivations**: Type in your research motivations.
6. **Research Focus Areas**: Select all the applicable research focus areas that apply to you.
7. **Research Interest Areas**: Enter any other research interest areas not listed in the provided options.
8. **Research Goals**: Enter your research goals in a date format (e.g., January 2025).
9. **Research Objectives**: Add any notes about your research objectives (optional).
10. **Researcher Type**: Select all the applicable researcher types that apply to you.
11. **Research Focus Areas Other**: Enter any other research focus areas not listed in the provided options.
12. **Research Interest Areas Other**: Enter any other research interest areas not listed in the provided options.
13. **Research Goals Other**: Enter any other research goals not listed in the provided options.
14. **Email**: Enter your email address.
15. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
16. **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
17. **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
18. **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
19. **Department Email**: Enter the department's email address.
20. **Department Phone**: Enter the department's phone number.
21. **Department Contact Name**: Enter the name of the department contact person.
22. **Notes**: Add any additional notes about yourself or your research (optional).
23. **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes about yourself or your research (optional).
24. **Submit**: Confirm that you have completed the survey.
25. **Submitted**: Confirm that you have submitted the survey.

Note: Please complete all required fields to ensure accurate and comprehensive information about yourself.
