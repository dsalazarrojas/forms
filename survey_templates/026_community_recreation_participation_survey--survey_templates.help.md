# Community Recreation Participation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from community members to help us improve local parks and recreation services. Your input will help us understand your participation habits, preferences, and concerns, which will guide our decisions on how to best serve the community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions to the best of your ability. If you're unsure about a question, feel free to provide additional comments or feedback in the "Additional comments or suggestions" section.
2. Select the most accurate options for each question. If none of the options fit your situation, select "Other" and describe your answer in the text field.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional comments or suggestions" field to provide feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Community Recreation Participation --**:
	+ A brief introduction to the survey.
* **Your Neighborhood or General Address**:
	+ Enter your current neighborhood or general address to help us understand where you live.
* **Your Age Group**:
	+ Select your age group for demographic purposes.
	+ Options:
		- Under 18
		- 18-24
		- 25-34
		- 35-44
		- 45-54
		- 55-64
		- 65+
* **What recreational activities have you participated in?**:
	+ Select all activities you have participated in.
	+ Options:
		- Public Parks
		- Community Center Events
		- Local Sports Leagues
		- Public Pools
		- Hiking and Biking Trails
		- Fitness Classes
* **Frequency of Participation**:
	+ Select how often you participate in recreational activities.
	+ Options:
		- Daily
		- Weekly
		- Monthly
		- Once a Year
		- Never
* **Overall Satisfaction with Local Recreation Services**:
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with local recreation services on a scale of 1-5.
	+ Options:
		- 5 - Extremely Satisfied
		- 4
		- 3
		- 2
		- 1 - Not Satisfied
* **What are the main barriers to your participation?**:
	+ Select all barriers that prevent you from participating in recreational activities.
	+ Options:
		- High Cost
		- Inconvenient Location
		- Poor Timing
		- Lack of Information
		- Transportation Issues
		- Accessibility Concerns
		- No Interest
* **If other barriers - please specify**:
	+ If none of the options above apply, describe other barriers that prevent you from participating.
* **Rate the maintenance of our recreation facilities**:
	+ Rate the maintenance of our recreation facilities.
	+ Options:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
* **What new activities or programs would you like to see?**:
	+ Suggest new activities or programs you would like to see in the community.
* **Do you feel our recreation programs are generally affordable?**:
	+ Select whether you think our recreation programs are affordable.
* **How do you primarily find out about recreation programs?**:
	+ Describe how you usually find out about recreation programs.
* **Additional comments or suggestions**:
	+ Add any additional comments or suggestions for the Parks and Rec team.
* **Would you like to join a recreation advisory committee?**:
	+ Select whether you're interested in joining a recreation advisory committee.
* **Contact Email Address**:
	+ Enter your email address if you're interested in joining the committee.
* **Today's Date**:
	+ This field is automatically populated and not editable.
