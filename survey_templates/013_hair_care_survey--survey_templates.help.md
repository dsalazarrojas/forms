# Hair Care Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your hair care habits and preferences. Your input will help us understand your opinions and behaviors related to hair care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the designated text field.
2. If a field is not required, you can skip it if you wish.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and honest answers to help us better understand your hair care habits.

* **Hair Care Survey Form 1 (1)**:
	+ **label:** `hair_care_survey_form_1`
	+ **type:** `text`
	+ **required:** `false`
	+ This field is a text input field where you can provide any information related to your hair care habits. You can type in your thoughts, opinions, or any other relevant details.
* **Hair Care Survey Form 2 (2)**:
	+ **label:** `hair_care_survey_form_2`
	+ **type:** `text`
	+ **required:** `false`
	+ This field is similar to the first field, where you can provide any additional information related to your hair care habits.
* **Hair Care Survey Form 3 (3)**:
	+ **label:** `hair_care_survey_form_3`
	+ **type:** `text`
	+ **required:** `false`
	+ This field is another text input field where you can share any other thoughts or opinions about hair care.
