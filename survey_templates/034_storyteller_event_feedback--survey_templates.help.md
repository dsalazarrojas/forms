# Storyteller Event Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the Storyteller event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each field as accurately and completely as possible. You can skip fields that are not relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you think** (storytellereventfeedback): This field is for your general feedback about the event. Tell us about your experience and what we did well or poorly.
* **Overall rating** (storytellereventfeedbackrating): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not enjoyable" and 5 is "very enjoyable".
* **How would you like to see this story improve?** (storytellereventfeedbackquestion): Share your thoughts on how we could improve the story or event.
* **Rating for this Storyteller** (storytellereventfeedbackratingforstory): Rate the Storyteller on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not helpful" and 5 is "very helpful".
* **Your Email** (storytellerfeedbackemail): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.
* **Your Phone Number** (storytellereventfeedbackphonenumber): If you have a phone number, you can enter it here in case we need to contact you.
* **Date of visit** (storytellereventfeedbackdatevisited): If you remember the date of your visit, please enter it.
* **Time of visit** (storytellereventfeedbacktimevisited): If you remember the time of your visit, please enter it.
* **Additional comments** (storytellerfeedbacknotes): Use this field to add any additional comments or thoughts that didn't fit in the previous fields.
