# Establishment Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your establishment's location, operating hours, service requirements, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Select the correct page to complete (Location Details, Operating Hours, Service Requirements, or Contact Details).
3. Fill in the required fields for each page.
4. Review your submission before clicking "Submit" to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Location Details
* **Location Details** (`location_details`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the establishment's location information, such as the address, city, state, or zip code.
### Operating Hours
* **Operating Hours** (`operating_hours`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the establishment's operating hours, including the days of the week and time ranges when the establishment is open to the public.
### Service Requirements
* **Service Requirements** (`service_requirements`, text, required/optional): This field is for specifying any special service requirements or restrictions that the establishment needs to operate, such as wheelchair accessibility or credit card acceptance.
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the establishment's contact information, including the name, email, phone number, and social media handles.

## Tips
* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form to ensure accurate submission.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the relevant authorities or the form creators for clarification.
* You can review your submission before clicking "Submit" to ensure accuracy.
* If you have already completed this form for the same establishment, please do not proceed. Instead, contact the relevant authorities or the form creators for guidance.
