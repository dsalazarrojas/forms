# survey_preparation_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The survey preparation checklist form is used to create a survey for data collection in an academic or research setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your survey.
2. Choose a template for your survey from the available options.
3. Define the objective of your survey.
4. Select the questions you want to include in your survey.
5. Choose the scale for your survey.
6. Select the language for your survey.
7. Enter the expected length of your survey.
8. Add any additional notes about your survey.
9. Enter the email and phone number of the person administering the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Preparation Checklist Form** (`survey_preparation_checklist_form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your survey that summarizes its purpose and scope.
* **Survey Template** (`survey_preparation_checklist_form_survey_template`, select_one, optional): Choose a template for your survey from the available options to ensure consistency in format and structure.
* **Survey Objective** (`survey_preparation_checklist_form_survey_objective`, text, optional): Define the objective of your survey, which should clearly state the research question or problem being addressed.
* **Survey Questions** (`survey_preparation_checklist_form_survey_questions`, select_multiple, optional): Select the questions you want to include in your survey, which should be relevant to the objective and survey topic.
* **Survey Scale** (`survey_preparation_checklist_form_survey_scale`, select_one, optional): Choose the scale for your survey, which should be consistent with the questions and objective.
* **Survey Language** (`survey_preparation_checklist_form_survey_language`, select_one, optional): Select the language for your survey to accommodate respondents with language barriers.
* **Survey Length** (`survey_preparation_checklist_form_survey_length`, number, optional): Enter the expected length of your survey in terms of time or responses.
* **Survey Notes** (`survey_preparation_checklist_form_survey_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your survey, such as instructions or reminders.
* **Survey Email** (`survey_preparation_checklist_form_survey_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person administering the survey.
* **Survey Phone** (`survey_preparation_checklist_form_survey_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person administering the survey.
