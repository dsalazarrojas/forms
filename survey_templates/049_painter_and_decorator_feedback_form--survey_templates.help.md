# Painter And Decorator Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for gathering feedback from customers about their experience with a painter and decorator's service. Please fill it out only once you have received the service and have had a chance to think about it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Start Form" to begin filling out the form.
2. Follow the instructions for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Your name will be displayed for the painter and decorator who will be serving you.
* **Email**: Your email address will be used to contact you for any follow-up questions or to provide you with further information.
* **Phone**: Your phone number will be used to contact you for any urgent matters.
* **Service Type**: Please select the type of service you received from the painter and decorator:
	+ Interior Painting
	+ Exterior Painting
	+ Both
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the service (1-5 stars).
* **Comments**: Provide any feedback or comments you may have about the service you received. This is your chance to let us know what we did well and what we could improve on.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- Do not fill out the form on behalf of someone else.
- Only fill out the form once you have received the service and have had a chance to think about your experience.
