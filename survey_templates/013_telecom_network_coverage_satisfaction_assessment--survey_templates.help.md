# Telecom Network Coverage Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback on your experience with your telecom network coverage. Your input will help us understand areas where we need to improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select all applicable coverage areas that you have experienced issues with.
3. If you have specific areas with issues, describe them in the text field.
4. Provide any additional notes about your experience.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the telecom network coverage on a scale from 0 to 100.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Telecom Network Coverage Satisfaction Assessment** (`1`, text, required): This is the title of the form, and you are not required to enter anything here. You can simply type in any comments or questions you have about the form.
* **Coverage Areas** (`2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the coverage areas where you have experienced issues with. You can select one or more of the following:
	+ Home
	+ Work
	+ Public Place
	+ Other (Specify in the notes field)
* **Any issues with these coverage areas?** (`3`, select_multiple, optional): If you have experienced issues with any of the coverage areas selected above, choose one or more of the following:
	+ Poor Coverage
	+ No Signal
	+ Dropped Calls
	+ Slow Data Speeds
* **Specific Areas with Issues** (`4`, text, optional): If there are specific areas with issues that are not listed above, please describe them in this text field.
* **Other notes about your experience** (`5`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the telecom network coverage.
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`6`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the telecom network coverage on a scale from 0 to 100. A higher score indicates a more positive experience.
