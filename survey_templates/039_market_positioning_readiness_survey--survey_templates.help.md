# market_positioning_readiness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses determine their market positioning readiness. It is a crucial step in identifying areas of improvement and developing a strategy to strengthen their market position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions carefully and thoroughly. Each question is important in assessing market positioning. If you're unsure about any question or option, please contact your business advisor for guidance. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, Not Required): This section provides a brief introduction to market positioning. Please provide a short description of your current market position.
* **Target Segments** (`target_segments`, `select_multiple`, Required): Identify the market segments you're currently targeting. Please select all applicable segments from the list.
	+ Segment 1
	+ Segment 2
	+ Segment 3
* **Brand Positioning** (`brand_positioning`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the best description of your brand's current market positioning.
	+ Value 1
	+ Value 2
	+ Value 3
* **Strategy Maturity** (`strategy_maturity`, `number`, Required): Assess your current strategy maturity level. Please select a number that best describes your strategy's level of development.
* **Market Positioning Guidance** (`market_positioning_guidance`, `text`, Required): Provide guidance on your current market positioning. Please describe your current market positioning in a few sentences.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`, `select_multiple`, Required): Choose a plan of action to improve your market positioning. Please select all applicable options.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `date`, Required): Plan your next steps to improve your market position. Please enter a date for your next step.
