# i_o_module_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about Input/Output (IO) modules from users. The form is composed of six questions that cover the basics of IO modules, their uses, types, and design principles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, click on the 'Submit' button.
2. Select the relevant answers for each question based on your knowledge about IO modules.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked as 'required'.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is an IO Module** (`question_1`, text, required: false):
	* This question asks for a brief description or definition of what an IO module is.
* **What are IO modules used for** (`question_2`, text, required: false):
	* This question requests an explanation of the common uses or applications of IO modules.
* **What are the differences between SRMs** (`question_3`, text, required: false):
	* This question is asking for an understanding of the differences between different types of IO modules, specifically SRMs (Single-Resource Modules).
* **What is IO safety concepts** (`question_4`, text, required: false):
	* This question seeks to understand the safety considerations or guidelines related to IO modules.
* **What are the types of IO modules** (`question_5`, select_multiple, required: false):
	* This question asks you to choose from a list of available types of IO modules. Select all the relevant options that apply to your knowledge.
* **What is IO module design** (`question_6`, text, required: false):
	* This question requests an explanation of the design principles or considerations for IO modules.

## Tips
* Please be thorough and accurate in your responses.
* Use specific examples or references when possible to support your answers.
* If unsure about any question, it's okay to ask for clarification or guidance.
