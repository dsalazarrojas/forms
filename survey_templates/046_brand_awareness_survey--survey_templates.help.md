# Brand Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about your level of brand awareness, perception, and preferences. Your responses will help us understand your familiarity with our brand and inform our marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing demographic information, including your age range and gender.
2. Answer questions about your awareness of our brand, such as how you heard about us and what makes our brand unique.
3. Share your thoughts on our brand products, services, and values.
4. Express your opinion on our brand's perception, including your overall impression and likelihood of purchase.
5. Finally, provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### -- About You --
* **Respondent ID (Optional)**: This field is for tracking purposes. If you want to provide a unique identifier, you can enter it here.
* **Age Range**: Please select your age group from the options provided.

### -- Brand Awareness --
* **Are You Aware of Our Brand**: Please select how familiar you are with our brand.
* **Where Have You Heard About Our Brand**: Select all the channels through which you heard about our brand.
* **What You Know About Our Brand**: Please describe what you know about our brand's offerings and value proposition.

### -- Brand Perception --
* **What Is Your Overall Impression of Our Brand**: Please select how positive or negative your impression of our brand is.
* **Likelihood of Purchasing From Us**: Select how likely you are to purchase from us.
* **How Does Our Brand Impact Your Decisions**: Please choose how much influence our brand has on your decisions.
* **Would You Recommend Us to Others**: Select how likely you are to recommend our brand to others.

### -- Competitive Awareness --
* **How Aware Are You of Competing Brands**: Please select how familiar you are with competing brands.

### -- Additional Feedback --
* **Any Additional Comments or Suggestions**: Please share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about our brand.
