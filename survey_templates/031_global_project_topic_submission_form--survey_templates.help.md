# Global Project Topic Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Project Topic Submission Form is used to submit proposals for global projects. Each proposal must meet the requirements specified in this form and be approved by the project team and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of your proposal.
2. Select the relevant information about your proposal (proposal information).
3. Enter your organization's name.
4. Provide a detailed description of your proposal.
5. Enter your name and contact information (contact person, email, and phone number).
6. Specify the status of your proposal (active, not active, pending, or canceled).
7. Set a deadline for your project.
8. Choose the priority level of your proposal (low, high, or medium).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **title** (text, required): Enter a clear and concise title of your proposal.
* **proposal_information** (select_one, required): Select the relevant information about your proposal (option 1, option 2).
* **organization** (text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **proposal_details** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of your proposal.
* **contact_person** (text, required): Enter your name.
* **contact_email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **contact_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **project_status** (select_multiple, required): Select the status of your proposal (active, not active, pending, or canceled).
* **deadline** (date, required): Set a deadline for your project.
* **priority** (select_one, required): Choose the priority level of your proposal (low, high, or medium).

Note: The project team and stakeholders will review and verify all the information provided in this form before making a final decision.
