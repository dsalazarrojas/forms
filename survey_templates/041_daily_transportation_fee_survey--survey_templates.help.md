# Daily Transportation Fee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Transportation Fee Survey is designed to collect information from users about their daily transportation habits, including the frequency and mode of commutes, commute time, and user satisfaction. This data can be used to inform policy decisions and improve public transportation services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name and contact information (email and phone number) so we can get in touch with you if needed.
2. Answer the questions about your daily commute, including how often you commute, the mode of transportation you use, and how much time you spend commuting.
3. Rate your level of satisfaction with current transportation fees.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your name so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Commute Frequency** (`commute_frequency`, number, required: true): How often do you usually commute daily? Select from 1 to 10, with 1 being "Never" and 10 being "Daily".
* **Transportation Mode** (`transportation_mode`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the transportation modes you use for your daily commute, such as driving, walking, cycling, or taking public transport.
* **Commute Time** (`commute_time`, time, required: true): Enter the average time you spend on your daily commute.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, text, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with current transportation fees, from "Very Dissatisfied" to "Very Satisfied".
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or concerns about transportation fees, please share them with us.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **More Comments** (`comments2`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them with us.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* You can add any additional comments or concerns in the "Comments" or "More Comments" fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
