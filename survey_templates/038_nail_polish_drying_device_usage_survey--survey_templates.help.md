# Nail Polish Drying Device Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about users' experiences with different nail polish drying devices. It aims to understand how frequently users employ these devices and what issues they have encountered during use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction page, which will guide you through the survey's purpose and instructions.
2. Select the devices you use for drying nail polish from the options provided.
3. Indicate your usage frequency of these devices, choosing from the provided frequency options.
4. Choose any satisfaction issues you have experienced with the devices from the options provided.
5. Optionally, you can provide any additional issues you have encountered with the devices in the text field.
6. If you have any comments or feedback, you can type them in the text field at the end of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required/optional): This page explains the purpose and instructions of the survey.
* **Devices Used** (`devicesUsed`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the nail polish drying devices you use. Examples: Nail Polish Drying Device A, Nail Polish Drying Device B, Nail Polish Drying Device C.
* **Usage Frequency** (`usageFrequency`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how often you use the selected devices. Choose one of the following options: Less than 3 times a week, 3-4 times a week, 5-6 times a week, More than 6 times a week.
* **Satisfaction Issues** (`satisfactionIssues`, `select_multiple`, required): Check any satisfaction issues you have experienced with the devices. Examples: High satisfaction, Low satisfaction, No issues.
* **Issues Encountered** (`issuesEncountered`, `text`, optional): If you have experienced any issues with the devices that are not listed, please describe them in this text field.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any general comments or feedback about the survey or the devices, you can type them in this field.
