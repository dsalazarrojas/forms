# Post Session Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a survey form designed to gather feedback and data from attendees after a seminar has concluded. It is used to track the progress and quality of the seminars.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your seminar name in the `Seminar Name` field.
2.  Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
3.  Provide your overall impression of the seminar in the `What was your overall impression of the seminar?` field.
4.  Enter any additional comments about the seminar in the `Comments` field.
5.  Rate your overall experience with the seminar in the `Rating` field.
6.  Enter the date and time of your attendance in the `Date` and `Time` fields.
7.  Select one option from the `Select One` field.
8.  Select multiple options from the `Select Multiple` field.
9.  Enter any additional notes in the `Additional Notes` field.
10. Enter your name in the `Submitted By` field.
11.  Enter the date and time when you submitted your feedback in the `Submit Date` and `Submit Time` fields.
