# Theme Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences regarding themes, styles, colors, textures, and other related categories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best describe your preferences for each category. You can choose as many or as few options as you like. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Theme** (`survey_theme`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have a theme that you would like to be associated with your account or project. Choose 'No' if you do not have a specific theme in mind.
* **Styles** (`theme_style`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the styles that you would like your theme to have. You can choose as many or as few as you like.
* **Colors** (`theme_color`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have a specific color in mind for your theme. Choose 'No' if you do not have a specific color preference.
* **Textures** (`theme_texture`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have a specific texture in mind for your theme. Choose 'No' if you do not have a specific texture preference.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Write any additional notes or comments you would like to provide about your theme preferences.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about your theme preferences.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your theme preferences.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your theme preferences.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose a time for your theme preferences.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have a specific category in mind for your theme. Choose 'No' if you do not have a specific category preference.
* **Sub Category** (`sub_category`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have a specific sub category in mind for your theme. Choose 'No' if you do not have a specific sub category preference.
