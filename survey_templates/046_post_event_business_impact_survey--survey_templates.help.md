# Post Event Business Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from event attendees to understand the business impact of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question based on your honest opinion.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the name of the event?** (event_name, text, required)
	+ Please provide the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (event_date, date, required)
	+ Please provide the date of the event.
* **Who organized the event?** (event_organizer, text, required)
	+ Please specify the name of the event organizer.
* **How would you rate the event?** (rating, number, required)
	+ Please rate the event from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What areas could we improve?** (improvement_areas, text, required)
	+ Please specify the areas for improvement.
* **Do you have any recommendations?** (recommendations, text, required)
	+ Please provide any recommendations.

	+ Please provide any additional comments.

	+ Please provide your email address.

	+ Please provide your phone number.
