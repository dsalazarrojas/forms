# healthy_eating_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthy_eating_survey form is designed to collect information about users' eating habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions on the first page, which provides an introduction to the survey.
2. On the following pages, select your food preferences for each category. You will be asked to choose one or multiple options for each question.
3. Enter the number of times you consume each of the first three food items.
4. Select the best option that describes your first, second, and third dietary preferences.
5. Choose the first and second food group that best describe your eating habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a text-based question that provides context to the survey.
* **Food Frequencies**: Enter the number of times you consume a specific food item within a week. This question has three fields for different food items.
* **First Food Item**: Select the number of times you consume a specific food item within a week.
* **Second Food Item**: Select the number of times you consume a specific food item within a week.
* **Third Food Item**: Select the number of times you consume a specific food item within a week.
* **First Dietary Preference**: Choose one option that best describes your dietary preference.
* **Second Dietary Preference**: Choose one option that best describes your dietary preference.
* **First Food Group**: Choose one option that best describes your eating habits.
* **Second Food Group**: Choose one option that best describes your eating habits.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the survey administrator.
* This form is designed to be completed within a reasonable time frame, so please be concise in your answers.
