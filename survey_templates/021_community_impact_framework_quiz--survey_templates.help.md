# Community Impact Framework Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gauge your understanding of the Community Impact Framework, a tool used to evaluate and improve community development programs. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability, using your knowledge from the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name and the name of your organization in the respective fields.
2. Select the correct answer to each question, and provide a brief explanation if necessary.
3. For questions with a "select_one" type, choose only one option from the list.
4. For the "definition_question" field, provide a brief definition using examples from the training session.
5. Enter your final quiz score, if applicable.
6. Provide feedback on the training session in the "training_feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`trainee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official records.
* **Name of Your Organization** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization as it appears on official records.
* **Date of Training Session** (`training_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training session in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **What are the three primary pillars of the Community Impact Framework?** (`pillar_question`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Social, Economic, and Environmental Impact
	+ Planning, Execution, and Measurement
* **Which foundational tool is most effective for community impact planning?** (`tool_question`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Logic Model
	+ Project Gantt Chart
	+ Financial CRM
	+ Marketing Funnel
* **Why is collecting community feedback considered essential for the framework?** (`feedback_question`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ To prove program success to donors
	+ To facilitate continuous program improvement
	+ To fulfill legal reporting requirements
	+ To increase social media engagement
* **Briefly define the difference between an Outcome and an Output** (`definition_question`, text, required): Provide a brief definition using examples from the training session.
* **What does the SMART acronym stand for in goal setting?** (`smart_goal_question`, text, required): Enter a brief explanation of what the SMART acronym stands for.
* **Final Quiz Score** (`score_placeholder`, number, optional): Enter the score assigned by the instructor, if applicable.
* **Feedback on the Training Session** (`training_feedback`, text, optional): Provide feedback on what was the most useful part of the training session for you.
