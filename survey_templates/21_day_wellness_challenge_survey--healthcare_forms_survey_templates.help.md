# 21 Day Wellness Challenge Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 21 Day Wellness Challenge Survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and progress after completing the 21-Day Wellness Challenge. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve the challenge for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each question and answer honestly about your experiences during and after the challenge.
2. Ensure you have completed all required fields, marked as "Required: true" above each field.
3. Select all options that apply when prompted to select multiple choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section: Participant Information
* **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address for challenge updates and follow-up communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for urgent communications, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Gender Identity**: Select your gender identity from the provided options.
* **Section: Before the Challenge** 
* **When did you start the 21-Day Challenge?**: Enter the start date of your challenge in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Primary Challenge Goal**: Describe your primary goal for participating in the challenge, e.g., "Increase energy", "Lose weight", "Improve sleep".
* **Rate your overall well-being before the challenge (1-10)**: Rate your overall well-being on a scale from 1 (very poor) to 10 (excellent).
* **Which of these were most common for you before the challenge? (Select all)**: Choose all issues that were most common for you before starting the challenge.
* **Section: Your Experience During the Challenge** 
* **Did you follow the challenge plan consistently?**: Select how well you followed the challenge plan.
* **Which activities/practices had the most positive impact? (Select all)**: Choose all activities that had a positive impact on your well-being.
* **Did you experience significant cravings or withdrawal symptoms?**: Select whether you experienced cravings or withdrawal symptoms during the challenge.
* **Section: After the Challenge** 
* **When did you complete the 21-Day Challenge?**: Enter the completion date of your challenge in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Do you feel you achieved your primary goal?**: Select how well you achieved your primary goal.
* **Rate your current overall well-being (1-10)**: Rate your overall well-being after completing the challenge on a scale from 1 (very poor) to 10 (excellent).
* **How satisfied are you with your challenge experience?**: Select how satisfied you are with your experience.
* **Do you plan to continue the new wellness practices?**: Select whether you plan to continue some or all of the practices you learned during the challenge.
* **Section: General Feedback & Next Steps** 
* **Would you recommend this 21-Day Wellness Challenge to others?**: Select whether you would recommend the challenge to others.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving the challenge?**: Share any suggestions you have for future improvements.
* **Any additional comments or thoughts?**: Enter any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.

Note: This survey is intended to be completed by participants who have finished the 21-Day Wellness Challenge.
