# Home Staging Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback about the property staging process to help improve its quality and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to provide accurate feedback.
2. Fill out the form completely, as every field is an essential part of the survey.
3. If a field is marked as required, make sure to fill it out accurately.
4. You can provide suggestions for improvement in the "Suggestions" field.
5. Make sure to check the provided options for fields like "Overall Rating", "Staged By", and "Staged For" as they are crucial for analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staged Property**: This field is used to provide the name or address of the property that has been staged. Please enter the property's name or address accurately.
* **Overall Rating**: This field is used to rate your overall experience with the staging process. Please select one of the provided options (Good, Fair, Poor) that best describes your experience.
* **Satisfaction**: Select the level of satisfaction you experienced with the staging process.
* **Feedback**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the staging process. This is an optional field, but it's a great opportunity to suggest improvements.
* **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in this field.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person or company that coordinated the staging process.
* **Contact Email**: Provide the email address of the contact person or company.
* **Staged By**: Select whether the property was staged by a human or not.
* **Staged Date**: Enter the date when the property was staged.
* **Viewed On**: Select the date when you viewed the property.
* **Staged For**: Select whether the property was staged for sale or rent.
* **Staged Price**: Enter the price of the property at the time of staging.
* **Staging Type**: Select the type of staging services used (Yes or No).
* **Viewed Price**: Select whether the property was viewed at its current price or not.
* **Staging Services**: Select the staging services used during the process (Yes or No for multiple options).
* **Staging Company**: If you would like to provide the name of the staging company used.
* **Viewed By**: If you would like to provide the name of the person or company that viewed the property.
* **Realtor**: If you would like to provide the name of the realtor involved.
* **Manager**: If you would like to provide the name of the property manager.
* **Comments**: This field is for any additional comments you may have.
* **Viewed On**: Enter the date when you viewed the property.
* **Staging Services 2**: Select the staging services used during the process (Yes or No for multiple options).

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be filled out if you have additional information to provide, but it's not required.
