# Aerospace Program Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from program participants to help us improve the quality of our aerospace programs. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and share your thoughts.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in its entirety, as indicated by the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
- Use the provided hints to help guide you through each section.
- Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, as your feedback is essential for us to make improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program / Workshop Name** (`program_name`, text, *): This is the title of the program or workshop you attended. Please enter the actual name of the program, e.g., "Intro to Rocketry".
* **Date Attended** (`date_attended`, date, *): This is the date you attended the program. Please select the date from the calendar or type it in the correct format.
* **Instructor / Facilitator** (`instructor_name`, text, ): This is an optional field, but if you'd like to provide feedback about the instructor, please do so here.
* **Quality of Content** (`content_quality`, select_one, *): Please rate the quality of the content presented during the program. Use the scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Instructor Effectiveness** (`instructor_rating`, select_one, *): Rate the effectiveness of the instructor in delivering the content. Again, use the scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Hands-on Activities** (`hands_on_activities`, select_one, *): Please evaluate the usefulness of the hands-on activities. Use the scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) or N/A if there were no hands-on activities.
* **Facilities / Equipment** (`facilities_rating`, select_one, *): Rate the quality of the facilities and equipment used during the program. Use the scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Most Valuable Takeaway** (`key_learning`, text, ): Share your most valuable takeaway from the program. What did you learn or take away?
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvements`, text, ): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for how we could improve the program in the future.
* **Recommend** (`recommend`, select_one, *): Will you recommend this program to others? Please select the option that best reflects your opinion.

Note: Remember to save your answers and submit the form when complete.
