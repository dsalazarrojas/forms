# Hazmat Facility Inspection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a facility's hazardous materials handling and management practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the location of the facility in the "Facility Location" field.
2. Provide the name of the facility in the "Facility Name" field.
3. Enter the contact person's name and phone number in the "Contact Person" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
4. If applicable, provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact Info" field.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Are there hazardous substances present?".
6. Describe the facility's containment procedures in the "Containment" field.
7. Describe the personal protective equipment (PPE) available for handling chemicals in the "Personal Protect Equipment" field.
8. Describe how chemicals are handled in the "Chemical Handling" field.
9. Describe chemical labeling procedures in the "Chemical Labeling" field.
10. Describe the storage procedures for chemicals in the "Chemical Storage" field.
11. Describe the facility's chemical handling procedures in the "Chemical Handling Procedures" field.
12. Describe waste disposal procedures in the "Waste Disposal" field.
13. Describe fire prevention procedures in the "Fire Prevention Procedures" field.
14. Describe spill response procedures in the "Spill Response" field.
15. Describe emergency exit locations in the "Emergency Exit Locations" field.
16. Describe safety equipment available for use in the "Safety Equipment" field.
17. Describe fire fighting equipment available for use in the "Fire Fighting Equipment" field.
18. Describe fire extinguishers available for use in the "Fire Extinguishers" field.
19. Describe fire suppression systems in the "Fire Suppression Systems" field.
20. Describe fire alarm systems in the "Fire Alarm Systems" field.
21. Describe sprinkler systems in the "Sprinkler Systems" field.
22. Describe fire suppression systems in the "Fire Suppression Systems" field.
23. Describe spill response procedures in the "Spill Response Procedures" field.
24. Describe training and drill procedures in the "Training and Drills" field.

* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): The phone number of the contact person.
