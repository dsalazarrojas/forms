# Performance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Performance Evaluation Survey" form is used to solicit feedback from employees on their performance. It helps managers to identify strengths and areas for improvement, and provides a way to evaluate employee performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu for "Performance Evaluation Survey".
2. Enter your employee's name in the "employee name" field.
3. Rate the employee's feedback on a scale from 0 to 10 using the "Feedback Rating" field.
4. Choose one option from the "Feedback Rating Scale" dropdown menu.
5. Enter any notable strengths in the "Strengths" field.
6. List any areas for improvement in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
7. Enter your name as the "Manager Name".
8. Fill in the "Date" field with the date you completed the form.
9. Enter the time you completed the form in the "Time" field.
10. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performance Evaluation Survey** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or multiple options to indicate how you evaluate the employee's performance.
* **employee name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their personnel record.
* **Feedback Rating** (`number`, required: false): Rate the employee's feedback on a scale from 0 to 10. This will help the manager to get an idea of how the employee performed.
* **Feedback Rating Scale** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose one option from this dropdown menu to indicate the quality of the employee's feedback.
* **Strengths** (`text`, required: false): List any strengths you want to highlight for the employee.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`text`, required: false): List any areas where the employee needs to improve.
* **Manager Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name as the manager who filled out this form.
* **Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time you completed the form.
* **Notes** (`note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the employee's performance.

Please fill in this form carefully, providing honest and constructive feedback.
