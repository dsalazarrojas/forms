# Bankruptcy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and opinions about the bankruptcy process. Your responses will help us understand how we can improve our services to better support individuals in similar situations.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the following questions to the best of your ability. You can skip any questions that are not applicable to your situation.

1. **Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
4. **Age Range**: Select your age bracket.
5. **Marital Status**: Choose your current marital status.
6. **Household Size**: Enter the number of people living in your household.
7. **Bankruptcy Chapter Filed**: Choose the chapter under which you filed.
8. **Filing Date**: Enter the date you filed for bankruptcy (optional).
9. **Primary Reason for Bankruptcy**: Select the main reason for your bankruptcy filing.
10. **Total Debt Before Filing**: Choose your approximate total debt amount before filing.
11. **Debt Categories**: Select all the debt categories that apply to you (e.g. Credit Cards, Medical Bills).
12. **Annual Income Before**: Select your annual income before filing.
13. **Current Annual Income**: Select your current annual income.
14. **Home Ownership Status**: Choose your current housing situation.
15. **Financial Counseling Received**: Indicate if you received financial counseling before or after filing.
16. **Did You Use an Attorney**: Choose if you hired an attorney or filed pro se.
17. **Process Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the bankruptcy process.
18. **Financial Situation Now**: Select your current financial situation compared to before.
19. **Credit Rebuilding Progress**: Rate your progress in rebuilding your credit.
20. **Would You File Again**: Indicate if you would file for bankruptcy again.
21. **Would Recommend to Others**: Choose if you would recommend the process to others in similar situations.
22. **Biggest Challenge Faced**: Describe the main difficulty you faced during the bankruptcy process.
23. **Advice for Others**: Share any advice you would give to others in similar situations.
24. **Additional Services Needed**: Select any additional services you believe would be helpful to others.
25. **Additional Comments**: Provide any other feedback or comments you have about the bankruptcy process.
