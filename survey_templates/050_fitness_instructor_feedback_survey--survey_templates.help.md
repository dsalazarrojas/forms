# Fitness Instructor Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help fitness instructors complete the Fitness Instructor Feedback Survey form accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form title at the top.
2. Provide your name and email address.
3. Rate your experience with the program.
4. Share any comments about your experience with the program.
5. Specify the date you attended the program.
6. Mention the name of the program you attended.
7. Choose the level of the program you attended.
8. Select the type of program you attended.
9. Rate your satisfaction with the program.
10. Provide any additional comments about the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`survey_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief title for your feedback.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Rating** (`instructor_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience with the program on a scale (we assume 1-5).
* **Comments** (`instructor_comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any comments about your experience with the program.
* **Program Date** (`program_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you attended the program in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the program you attended.
* **Program Level** (`program_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the level of the program you attended (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of program you attended (Bootcamp, Private Session, Group Session).
* **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, `text`, required: false): Rate your experience with the instructor.
* **Instructor Experience** (`instructor_experience`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of years you've been working with the instructor.
* **Instructor Feedback** (`instructor_feedback`, `note`, required: false): Provide any feedback about the instructor.
* **Instructor Satisfaction** (`instructor_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the instructor (Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied).
* **Instructor Comments** (`instructor_comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any comments about the instructor.
* **Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the program (Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied).
* **Phone Number** (`instructor_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`instructor_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`instructor_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Share any comments about the program.

**Tips**
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Double-check your answers to ensure they are correct.
* If you have any additional comments, please include them in the comments fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
