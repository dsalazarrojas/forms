# Opportunities Assessment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Opportunities Assessment Checklist is a form used to gather information about opportunities, including their category, location, description, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the opportunity name and category in the respective fields.
2.  If applicable, fill in the location where the opportunity will be located.
3.  Provide a brief description of the opportunity.
4.  If necessary, set a follow-up date and time for the opportunity.
5.  Fill in any other required or optional fields as needed.
6.  Review and verify the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### opportunity_name
Opportunity Name (text): Enter the name of the opportunity.

### Category
Category (select_one): Select the category of the opportunity from the options:
*   Opportunity
*   Partnership
*   Investment

### location
Location (text): Enter the location where the opportunity will be located.

### Description
Description (note): Provide a brief description of the opportunity.

### Date
Date (date): Enter the date of the opportunity.

### Time
Time (time): Enter the time of the opportunity.

### assigned_tool
Assigned Tool (select_one): Select the tool assigned to the opportunity from the options:
*   chatjimmy
*   other
*   default

### Opportunity Level
Opportunity Level (select_one): Select the level of the opportunity from the options:
*   High
*   Medium
*   Low

### Opportunity Score
Opportunity Score (number): Enter a score for the opportunity, with higher scores indicating a more promising opportunity.

### Comments
Comments (note): Provide any additional comments or notes about the opportunity.

### Follow-up
Follow-up (select_multiple): Select whether a follow-up is necessary:
*   True
*   False
*   Maybe

### Follow-up Date
Follow-up Date (date): Enter the date to follow up on the opportunity.

### Follow-up Time
Follow-up Time (time): Enter the time to follow up on the opportunity.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields for a thorough assessment.
*   Use clear and concise language in the description and comments fields.
*   If unsure about any field, leave it blank or consult with a colleague or supervisor.
*   Save and submit the form only when all information is accurate and complete.
