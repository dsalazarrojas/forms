# Cheerleader Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from cheerleaders about their experience in the program, helping us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your role in the program from the options provided.
2. Enter your full name (optional).
3. Rate your satisfaction with the coaching quality, team spirit, and progress in skills development.
4. Share what part of the program you enjoy most and least.
5. Suggest ideas for team bonding activities.
6. Rate the clarity and timeliness of communication from the program administrators.
7. Provide an overall rating for the program.
8. Indicate whether you would recommend the program to a friend.
9. Add any additional comments or concerns.
10. Finally, select the date you are providing feedback on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role_type`, select_one, required): Please select your role in the program from the options provided.
* **Your Full Name** (`respondent_full_name_opt`, text, optional): Enter your full name if you're comfortable sharing it for this feedback.
* **Satisfaction with Coaching Quality** (`coaching_effectiveness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how satisfied you are with the coaching quality of the program.
* **Team Spirit and Morale Rating** (`team_spirit_rating_score`, select_one, required): Rate the team's spirit and morale.
* **Satisfaction with Skills Development** (`skill_development_progress_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the progress in skills development.
* **What part of the program do you enjoy most** (`most_enjoyed_program_activity`, text, required): Share what part of the program you enjoy most, e.g. competitions, practice, games.
* **What part of the program do you enjoy least** (`least_enjoyed_program_activity`, text, optional): Share what part of the program you enjoy least.
* **Suggestions for Team Bonding Activities** (`bonding_activity_suggestions`, text, optional): Suggest ideas for team bonding activities.
* **Rating of Program Communication** (`communication_rating_admin`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity and timeliness of communication from the program administrators.
* **Overall Program Rating** (`overall_experience_rating`, select_one, required): Provide an overall rating for the program.
* **Would you recommend this program to a friend?** (`recommended_likelihood_friend`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend the program to a friend.
* **Any other comments or concerns** (`additional_comments_survey`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **Date of Feedback** (`survey_completion_timestamp`, date, required): Select the date you are providing feedback on.
