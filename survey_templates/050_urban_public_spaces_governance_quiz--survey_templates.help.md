# urban_public_spaces_governance_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to provide their thoughts and input on urban public spaces governance, including public space management, participation, and usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and provide your thoughts or answers as required.
2. Answer all questions marked as "required" as they are essential for the form submission.
3. You can submit your answers by selecting the "Submit" button at the end of the form.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure you have provided all required information.
