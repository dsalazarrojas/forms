# Post Presentation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Presentation Survey form is used to collect feedback and comments from users after a presentation, allowing presenters to understand their audience's experience and gather valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the feedback questions, selecting the appropriate response for each question.
2. Enter any comments or suggestions you have in the comments field.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can also enter them in the comments_and_suggestions fields.
4. Enter your email address (if you have one) in the email field.
5. Enter your phone number (if you have one) in the phone field.
6. Click submit to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Questions** (`feedback_questions`, `select_one`, required): This question allows you to choose your level of agreement with a particular statement. Please select the option that best reflects your opinion.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you have about the presentation.
* **Comments and Suggestions 1** (`comments_and_suggestions_1`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have, which will be helpful for the presenter to improve future presentations.
* **Comments and Suggestions 2** (`comments_and_suggestions_2`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have, which will be helpful for the presenter to consider.
* **Comments and Suggestions 3** (`comments_and_suggestions_3`, `note`, optional): This field is for any final additional comments or suggestions you have, which will be helpful for the presenter to take into account.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address, please enter it so the presenter can contact you for further discussion or to follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it so the presenter can contact you for further discussion or to follow up on your feedback.

Note: Please keep in mind that some fields are optional, but providing feedback and comments is encouraged.
