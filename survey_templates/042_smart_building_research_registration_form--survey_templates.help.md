# Smart Building Research Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to gather information from participants in smart building research projects. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, start from the beginning and fill out each field as you proceed.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, \*) Enter your first name for registration purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, \*) Enter your email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, \*) Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Participant Type** (Participant Type, select_multiple, \*) Select all relevant participant types (Participant, Researcher, Real Estate Team).
* **Research Interests** (Research Interests, select_multiple, \*) Choose one or more research interests (Smart Building Technologies, Sustainable Design, Building Materials, Energy Efficiency).
* **Building Details** (Building Details, text, ) Enter details about the building you're registering (optional).
* **University** (University, text, ) Enter your university affiliation (optional).
* **Lab** (Lab, text, ) Enter your lab or department (optional).
* **Team Name** (Team Name, text, ) Enter your team's name (optional).
* **Research Area** (Research Area, text, ) Enter your research area of interest (optional).
* **Study Details** (Study Details, text, ) Enter additional study details (optional).
* **Select start date** (Select start date, date, \*) Choose the start date of your research project.
* **Select end date** (Select end date, date, \*) Choose the end date of your research project.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your research coordinator for assistance.
