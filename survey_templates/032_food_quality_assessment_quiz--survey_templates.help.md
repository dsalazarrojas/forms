# Food Quality Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Food Quality Assessment Quiz is designed to gauge your perception of the food quality in a given establishment. It will ask you to rate the quality of food on a scale of Excellent, Very good, Average, or Poor. Your responses will help us improve the quality of food served in this establishment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the rating for each question using the scale: Excellent, Very good, Average, or Poor.
3. You can answer all questions, or as many as you like, depending on your experience with the food.
4. Use the text boxes for any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **food-quality-questions** (`food-quality-questions`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate the overall quality of the food at this establishment. Please select the best answer from the scale: Excellent, Very good, Average, or Poor.
* **food-quality-questions-1** (`food-quality-questions_1`, text, optional): This question asks about the taste of the food. If you have specific comments or feedback about the taste, you can enter it here.
* **food-quality-questions-2** (`food-quality-questions_2`, text, optional): This question is about the aroma of the food. Please let us know if the food had a pleasant or unpleasant smell.
* **food-quality-questions-3** (`food-quality-questions_3`, text, optional): This question is about the texture of the food. Do you have any comments or feedback about the texture?
* **food-quality-questions-4** (`food-quality-questions_4`, text, optional): This question is about the appearance of the food. Please let us know if the food looked appetizing or not.
* **food-quality-questions-5** (`food-quality-questions_5`, text, optional): This question is about the presentation of the food. Was the food presented well?
* **food-quality-questions-6** (`food-quality-questions_6`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to rate the quality of multiple aspects of the food. Please select all that apply from the scale: Excellent, Very good, Average, or Poor.
* **food-quality-questions-7** (`food-quality-questions_7`, text, optional): This question is about any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **food-quality-questions-8** (`food-quality-questions_8`, text, optional): This question is for any further comments or feedback.
* **food-quality-questions-9** (`food-quality-questions_9`, text, optional): This question is also for any additional comments or feedback.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please use this text box for any general comments or feedback about your experience with the food.

Note: The fields with `optional` label can be left blank if you don't have anything to add.
