# sports_club_social_responsibility_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about your participation in sports clubs, your level of commitment, and your enjoyment of the club's activities. It will help us understand how you engage with our sports club and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the sport you participate in (Page 1).
2. If you want to provide more information, you can write about the purpose of your participation (Page 2).
3. Indicate how often you participate in the sport (Page 3).
4. Choose a time that represents when you usually participate in the sport (Page 4).
5. Select the level of commitment you have for the sport (Page 5).
6. Answer how much you enjoy the club's activities (Page 6).
7. Answer if you support the club's sustainability (Page 7).
8. Answer if you support the club's charities (Page 8).
9. Answer if you support the club's volunteering activities (Page 9).
10. Add any comments you want to share with us (Page 10).
11. Provide your email address (Page 11).
12. Enter your contact phone number (Page 12).
13. Enter your contact website (Page 13).
14. Answer how much you enjoy the club's sustainability activities (Page 14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What sport do you participate in** (`club_name`, `select_one`): Please select the sport you participate in from the list provided. This will help us understand which sports are most popular among our members.
* **What is the purpose of your participation?** (`what_is_the_purpose_of_your_participation`, `text`): If you want to provide more information about why you participate in the sport, you can write it here.
* **How often do you participate?** (`how_often_do_you_participate`, `select_one`): Please select how often you participate in the sport. Options include Less than once a week, Once a week, Several times a week, and Every day.
* **What time do you participate?** (`what_time_do_you_participate`, `time`): Choose a time that represents when you usually participate in the sport. This will help us understand your schedule and preferences.
* **What level of commitment?** (`what_level_of_commitment`, `select_multiple`): Select all the levels of commitment that apply to you. Options include Casual, Competitive, Social, and Recreational.
* **How much do you enjoy?** (`how_much_do_you_enjoy`, `number`): Answer on a scale from 1 to 10 how much you enjoy the club's activities.
* **Do you support the club's sustainability?** (`do_you_support_the_club_sustainability`, `select_one`): Please answer Yes or No if you support the club's sustainability activities.
* **Do you support the club's charities?** (`do_you_support_the_club_charities`, `select_one`): Please answer Yes or No if you support the club's charities.
* **Do you support the club's volunteering activities?** (`do_you_support_the_club_volunteering`, `select_one`): Please answer Yes or No if you support the club's volunteering activities.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`): Add any comments or feedback you want to share with us.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Please provide your email address so we can contact you.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact website** (`contact_website`, `text`): Enter your contact website.
* **How much do you enjoy our sustainability?** (`how_much_do_you_enjoy_our_sustainability`, `number`): Answer on a scale from 1 to 10 how much you enjoy the club's sustainability activities.

Please note that all fields are not required, and you can answer as much or as little as you like.
