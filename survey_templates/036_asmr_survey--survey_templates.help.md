# ASMR Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about your experiences and preferences related to Autonomous Sensory Processing Disorder (ASMR). The goal of this survey is to help researchers understand how individuals interact with ASMR content and identify factors that contribute to ASMR sensations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2.  Select the options that best match your experiences and preferences.
3.  Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): This question is about your age group, which will help us understand the demographics of our respondents. Choose the age range that applies to you.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): This question is about your gender identity. Select the option that best describes you.
* **How Familiar Are You with ASMR** (`asmr_familiarity`, select_one, required): This question is about how well you know about ASMR triggers and content. Choose how familiar you are with ASMR.
* **How Often Do You Listen to ASMR Content** (`asmr_frequency`, select_one, required): This question is about how frequently you listen to ASMR content. Select your typical listening frequency.
* **Which ASMR Triggers Affect You Most (select all that apply)** (`asmr_triggers`, select_multiple, required): This question is about the triggers that consistently give you ASMR sensations. Choose all the triggers that apply to you.
* **On a Scale of 1-10, How Effective Is ASMR for Relaxation for You** (`asmr_effectiveness_rating`, number, required): This question is about how effective ASMR is for you to relax. Rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Does ASMR Help Improve Your Sleep Quality** (`sleep_improvement`, select_one, required): This question is about whether you use ASMR to improve your sleep quality. Select "yes" if you regularly use ASMR for sleep.
* **Do You Use ASMR Primarily for Stress Relief** (`stress_relief`, select_one, required): This question is about your primary reason for listening to ASMR. Select "true" if you use ASMR primarily for stress relief.
* **How Intense Are Your ASMR Sensations** (`trigger_intensity`, select_one, required): This question is about the physical intensity of your ASMR experiences. Rate the intensity of your sensations.
* **What Is Your Preferred Duration for ASMR Videos** (`preferred_duration`, select_one, required): This question is about the length of ASMR videos you prefer. Select the duration that works best for you.
* **Do You Have a Preference for ASMR Content Creators** (`content_creator_preference`, select_one, required): This question is about your preference for content creators. Select your preferred type of creator.
* **Additional Comments About Your ASMR Experience** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This question is an opportunity to share any other thoughts or comments about your ASMR experience.
