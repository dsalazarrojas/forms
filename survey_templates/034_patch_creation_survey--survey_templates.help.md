# Patch Creation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your workflow, communication methods, and overall satisfaction. Please answer the questions to help us better understand your team's workflow and provide insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please answer the questions to the best of your ability**.
2. **Be honest and accurate in your responses**.
3. **You can skip any questions that don't apply to your situation**.
4. **Use the options provided for select fields, or type your answer in the text fields**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workflow Title (workflow_title)**: A brief title of your current workflow.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Which Communication Method Do You Use? (communication_method)**: Choose the methods you use for communication within your team.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: Email, Phone call, Chat, Slack
* **What Type Of Workflow Are You In? (workflow_type)**: Select the type of workflow you are currently using.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Code Review, Code Review and Deploy, Code Review and Deploy and Verify
* **How Often Do You Communicate? (communication_frequency)**: Choose how often you communicate with your team.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Less than once a week, Once a week, 2-3 times a week, More than 3 times a week
* **What Tool Do You Use For Communication? (tooling)**: Choose the tools you use for communication.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: Jotform, Slack, Trello, Email
* **What Type Of Feedback Do You Give? (feedback_type)**: Select the type of feedback you give during the code review process.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: Code Review, Code Review and Deploy, Code Review and Deploy and Verify
* **How Often Do You Give Feedback? (feedback_frequency)**: Choose how often you give feedback.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Less than once a week, Once a week, 2-3 times a week, More than 3 times a week
* **How Big Is Your Team? (team_size)**: Enter the number of people in your team.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: false
* **Team Communication (team_communication)**: Describe your team's communication dynamics.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Team Tooling (team_tooling)**: Describe the tools your team uses for collaboration.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **How Satisfied Are You With Your Current Tooling? (tooling_satisfaction)**: Rate your satisfaction with the tools you use for communication.
	+ Type: number
	+ Options: 1-5
* **How Satisfied Are You With Your Current Communication? (communication_satisfaction)**: Rate your satisfaction with your current communication methods.
	+ Type: number
	+ Options: 1-5
* **How Satisfied Are You With Your Current Feedback? (feedback_satisfaction)**: Rate your satisfaction with your current feedback methods.
	+ Type: number
	+ Options: 1-5
* **Overall Satisfaction (overall_satisfaction)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your current workflow.
	+ Type: number
	+ Options: 1-5
* **Comments (comments)**: Add any additional comments about your workflow or communication methods.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false

By following these instructions, you'll be able to complete the form accurately and provide valuable insights about your team's workflow and communication methods.
