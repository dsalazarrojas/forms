# Annual Service Plan Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experience with the service provider. The goal is to improve the quality and overall satisfaction of the service, ensuring it meets and exceeds customer expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in your name and email address.
2.  Choose the type of service you have been using.
3.  Select how long you have been a customer, from the options provided.
4.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
5.  Rate the quality of the service provided.
6.  Choose whether the service provides good value for money.
7.  Rate the reliability and consistency of the service.
8.  Rate the quality of customer support.
9.  Rate the responsiveness of customer support.
10. Rate the quality of communications, such as updates and information provided.
11.  Choose whether the service meets your expectations.
12.  List the most valued service features.
13.  Identify areas for improvement.
14.  Specify any desired new features or services.
15.  Rate whether the price is fair for the value received.
16.  Choose whether you are willing to pay more for enhanced service.
17.  Rate whether you have considered switching to a competitor.
18.  Rate your likelihood to renew the service.
19.  Choose whether you would recommend the service to others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Your name, first and last. This is the field where you should enter your personal name.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): This is your email address for follow-up communication. Please ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
* **Customer or Account ID** (`customer_id`, text, required/optional): If you have a customer or account ID, please enter it if available. This helps the service provider to identify and track your account.
* **Type of Service** (`service_type`, text, required/optional): Please choose the type of service you have been using.
* **How long have you been a customer** (`subscription_length`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how long you have been a customer from the options provided.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Service** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Quality of Service Provided** (`service_quality`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the quality of the service provided, from excellent to very poor.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether the service provides good value for money.
* **Reliability and Consistency** (`reliability`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the service's reliability and consistency, from very reliable to very unreliable.
* **Quality of Customer Support** (`customer_support`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the quality of customer support, from excellent to very poor.
* **Responsiveness of Customer Support** (`support_responsiveness`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the speed and responsiveness of customer support, from excellent to very slow.
* **Quality of Communications** (`communication_quality`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the quality of communications, such as updates and information provided, from excellent to very poor.
* **Service Meets Expectations** (`expectations_met`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether the service meets your expectations, from exceeds to below expectations.
* **Most Valued Service Features** (`most_valued_features`, text, required/optional): List the features that you value most about the service.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, required/optional): Identify areas that you think the service could improve.
* **Desired New Features or Services** (`feature_requests`, text, required/optional): Specify any new features or services that you would like to see added.
* **Price Fairness for Value Received** (`price_fairness`, select_one, required/optional): Rate whether the price is fair for the value received, from strongly agree to strongly disagree.
* **Willingness to Pay More for Enhanced Service** (`price_change_willingness`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you would be willing to pay more for enhanced service, from yes to already too expensive.
* **Considered Switching to Competitor** (`competitor_consideration`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you have considered switching to a competitor, from never to frequently.
* **Likelihood to Renew Service** (`likelihood_renew`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your likelihood to renew the service, from very likely to very unlikely.
* **Would Recommend to Others** (`recommend_others`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you would recommend the service to others, from definitely to definitely not.
