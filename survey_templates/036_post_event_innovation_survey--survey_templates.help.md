# Post Event Innovation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from attendees and participants on the recently concluded event. It aims to understand your opinions on the event's organizer, content, organization, and overall experience. Your input will help improve future events and provide valuable insights for the event planners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). The fields marked as "required" are essential for us to receive your feedback.
2. Answer the multiple-choice questions with a single selection from the available options.
3. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible to help us improve our events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event organizer, if known.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, \*): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Rating Organizer** (`rating_organizer`, number, \*): Rate the event organizer's performance. Please use a scale of 1 to 10, with 1 being poor and 10 being excellent.
* **Rating Content** (`rating_content`, number, \*): Rate the event content's quality. Please use a scale of 1 to 10, with 1 being poor and 10 being excellent.
* **Rating Organization** (`rating_organization`, number, \*): Rate the event organization's performance. Please use a scale of 1 to 10, with 1 being poor and 10 being excellent.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions about the event.
* **Suggestion Ideas** (`rating_suggestion_ideas`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest new ideas for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Suggestion Topics** (`rating_suggestion_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest topics for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Suggestion Venues** (`rating_suggestion_venues`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest venues for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Suggestion Durations** (`rating_suggestion_durations`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest durations for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Suggestion Dates** (`rating_suggestion_dates`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest dates for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Suggestion Formats** (`rating_suggestion_formats`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest formats for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Suggestion Locations** (`rating_suggestion_locations`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest locations for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Suggestion Participants** (`rating_suggestion_participants`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest participants for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Suggestion Tools** (`rating_suggestion_tools`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest tools for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Suggestion Vendors** (`rating_suggestion_vendors`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest vendors for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Suggestion Others** (`rating_suggestion_others`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest others for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Suggestion Ideas** (`rating_suggestion_ideas`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest new ideas for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Would you like to be contacted for follow up questions? Choose between "True" and "False".
* **Next Event** (`next_event`, select_one, optional): Are you interested in attending the next event? Choose between "True" and "False".
* **Recommend to Organizer** (`recommend_to_organizer`, select_one, optional): Would you recommend the event to the organizer? Choose between "True" and "False".
* **Recommend to Others** (`recommend_to_others`, select_one, optional): Would you recommend the event to others? Choose between "True" and "False".
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, text, optional): A text field for submitted.

Note: The fields without an asterisk (\*) are optional.
