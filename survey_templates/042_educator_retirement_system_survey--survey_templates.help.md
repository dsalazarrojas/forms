# Educator Retirement System Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educator Retirement System Survey is designed to gather information from educators about their experiences and concerns related to retirement benefits, communication, and perceived fairness in the retirement process. Your input will help administrators or policymakers make informed decisions to improve the retirement system for educators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the correct answer for each question, if applicable.
3. If a question does not apply to you, simply mark the "N/A" option.
4. Take your time to ensure that you have completed all the questions accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Retirement Benefits** (`retirement_benefits`, text, required: false)
This question asks about the retirement benefits you have received or expect to receive when you retire. Please describe your experiences and any concerns you have about these benefits.

### **Communication** (`communication`, text, required: false)
This question inquires about your experiences with communication from your school or district regarding retirement benefits, such as notifications, updates, or changes. Describe any challenges or successes you have had in this area.

### **Perceived Fairness** (`perceived_fairness`, text, required: false)
This question seeks your opinion on how fair you perceive the retirement benefits and process to be. Describe any feelings of fairness or unfairness you have experienced or observed.

## Tips
- Please answer each question thoroughly and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
- If you have any concerns or suggestions for improvement, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
