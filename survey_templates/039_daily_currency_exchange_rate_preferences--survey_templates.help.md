# Daily Currency Exchange Rate Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Currency Exchange Rate Preferences form is designed to collect user's preferences for currency exchange rates updates. It allows users to select the currencies they are interested in, the frequency at which they want to receive updates, and the method they prefer to receive these updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the currencies you are interested in from the list of available options.
2. Choose the frequency at which you want to receive updates (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
3. Select the method you prefer to receive updates (Email, SMS, or Mobile App).
4. Optionally, enter any notes you'd like to include.
5. Enter your email address if you want to receive updates via email.
6. Enter your phone number if you want to receive updates via SMS or Mobile App.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preferred Currencies** (`Preferred Currencies`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all currencies you are interested in for which you want to receive updates. You can choose as many or as few currencies as you prefer.
* **Update Frequency** (`Update Frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how often you want to receive updates for your selected currencies (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Update Timing** (`Update Timing`, time, required/optional): Note: This field is not currently populated with options. If you want to specify a custom timing for your updates, please contact your administrator.
* **Delivery Method** (`Delivery Method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the method you prefer to receive updates (Email, SMS, or Mobile App).
* **Required Update** (`Required Update`, number, required/optional): Note: This field is not currently populated with options. Please contact your administrator for further information.
* **Preferred Exchange Rates** (`Preferred Exchange Rates`, number, required/optional): Note: This field is not currently populated with options. Please contact your administrator for further information.
* **Required Update Frequency** (`Required Update Frequency`, number, required/optional): Note: This field is not currently populated with options. Please contact your administrator for further information.
* **Required Delivery Method** (`Required Delivery Method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the method you prefer to receive required updates (Email, SMS, or Mobile App).
* **Required Delivery Frequency** (`Required Delivery Frequency`, number, required/optional): Note: This field is not currently populated with options. Please contact your administrator for further information.
* **Required Exchange Rates** (`Required Exchange Rates`, number, required/optional): Note: This field is not currently populated with options. Please contact your administrator for further information.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments you'd like to include with your updates.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive updates via email.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you want to receive updates via SMS or Mobile App.
* **Required Phone** (`Required Phone`, text, required/optional): Note: This field is not currently populated with options. Please contact your administrator for further information.
