# Shoe Purchase Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shoe Purchase Feedback Form is a questionnaire designed to collect feedback from customers who have purchased shoes from our store. The form is used to gauge customer satisfaction with their purchase, including the quality of the shoe, the overall purchasing experience, and any suggestions the customer may have for improving our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the form by selecting one of the provided options or entering your response in the text field.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Enter your accurate contact email address and phone number so that we can follow up on your feedback and respond to any questions or concerns you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How likely were you to recommend this shoe to a friend?**
	+ This question is used to gauge your level of satisfaction with the shoe and our store's overall quality.
* **What was the price of the shoe?**
	+ This question is used to help us understand your perception of the value of the shoe in relation to its price.
* **How would you rate the quality of the shoe?**
	+ This question is used to assess your satisfaction with the shoe's quality and material.
* **How would you rate your overall purchasing experience?**
	+ This question is used to understand how easy it was for you to purchase the shoe from our store.
* **What suggestions do you have for us?**
	+ This question is an open-ended field where you can provide any suggestions or comments you may have about your purchase or our service.
* **Your contact email**
	+ Please enter your accurate email address so that we can follow up on your feedback and respond to any questions or concerns you may have.
* **Your contact phone number**
	+ Please enter your accurate phone number so that we can follow up on your feedback and respond to any questions or concerns you may have.
