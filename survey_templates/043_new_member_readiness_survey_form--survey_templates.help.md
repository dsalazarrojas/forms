# New Member Readiness Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about new members' background, interests, and preferences to ensure a smooth transition into our community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each page carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Fill out all the required fields marked with '*'.
3. Select the options that apply to you for the relevant questions.
4. Save and return to the form if needed.
5. Submit your answers when finished.
