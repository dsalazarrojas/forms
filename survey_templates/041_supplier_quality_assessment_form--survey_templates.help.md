# supplier_quality_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The supplier quality assessment form is designed to gather information about a supplier's performance, including their quality of service, communication, reliability, and response time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Choose a rating for the supplier's overall quality of service from the "supplier_rating" dropdown menu.
3. Select one or more of the following for the "quality_of_service" field:
	* excellent
	* good
	* fair
	* poor
4. Choose a rating for the supplier's communication from the "communication" dropdown menu.
5. Choose a rating for the supplier's reliability from the "reliability" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the supplier's average response time (in minutes) in the "response_time" field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "note" field (optional).
8. Enter the supplier's email address in the "supplier_email" field (optional).
9. Enter the supplier's phone number in the "supplier_phone" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name**: The name of the supplier being assessed.
* **supplier_rating**: A subjective rating of the supplier's overall quality of service, with options: High, Medium, Low.
* **quality_of_service**: A selection of one or more of the following: excellent, good, fair, poor.
* **communication**: A subjective rating of the supplier's communication, with options: excellent, good, fair, poor, very poor.
* **reliability**: A subjective rating of the supplier's reliability, with options: excellent, good, fair, poor.
* **response_time**: The supplier's average response time (in minutes).
* **note**: Any additional comments or notes about the supplier.
* **supplier_email**: The supplier's email address (optional).
* **supplier_phone**: The supplier's phone number (optional).
