# document_management_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The document management survey is designed to gather user feedback on the document management process. This form aims to understand user experiences and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through each page (or section) of the form.
2. For each page, answer the questions and provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
3. Once you have completed all pages, review your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Header** (`survey_header`, note, required: false): This page serves as an introduction to the survey and provides an overview of the form's purpose.
* **How do you rate your overall experience with our document management process?** (`header_question`, select_one, required: false): Please select the rating that best describes your experience with our document management process. 
* **How would you rate the ease of use of our document management process?** (`rating_scale`, number, required: false): Rate the ease of use of our document management process on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comment_section`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about our document management process.
* **Email** (`email_section`, email, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to discuss your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone_section`, text, required: false): If you would prefer to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Date** (`date_section`, date, required: false): If you would like to provide a specific date for when you last interacted with our document management process, please enter it here.
* **Time** (`time_section`, time, required: false): If you would like to provide a specific time for when you last interacted with our document management process, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Your input is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our document management process.
