# Online Slam Book Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Slam Book Form is a tool used to gather feedback and information from individuals who have interacted with your organization or business. This form collects details about the person, their experience, and any additional comments or feedback they wish to provide.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please provide the required information in the corresponding fields.
- Ensure you fill out each field completely and accurately.
- If you need help or clarification on any of the fields, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name (1)
* **First name**: text input field
    * This field is meant for the individual to enter their first name. Please provide your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last name (2)
* **Last name**: text input field
    * This field is meant for the individual to enter their last name. Please provide your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email (3)
* **Email**: email input field
    * This field is for the individual to provide their email address. This will help us contact them regarding any further communication or follow-up.

### Phone (4)
* **Phone**: text input field
    * This field is for the individual to input their contact phone number. This will help us contact them in case of any urgent matters.

### Sentiment (5)
* **Sentiment**: select one option
    * Please choose how you felt about your interaction with our organization or business. You can select either "Yes" or "No" based on your sentiment.

### Memory (6)
* **Memory**: text input field (note type)
    * This field is for the individual to provide any memorable details about their interaction with our organization or business. Please write down any notable events or experiences you wish to recall.

### Category (7)
* **Category**: select one option
    * Please choose a category that best describes your interaction with our organization or business. You can select either "Yes" or "No" based on your experience.

### Goals (8)
* **Goals**: select multiple options
    * Please choose all the goals that apply to your interaction with our organization or business. You can select multiple options based on your experience.

### Comments (9)
* **Comments**: text input field (note type)
    * This field is for any additional comments or feedback you wish to provide about your interaction with our organization or business. Please be as detailed as possible, but keep your comments professional and respectful.

## Tips
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- Please ensure you are filling out this form accurately and completely.
- This form is designed to gather information for the betterment of our organization or business, so your participation is greatly appreciated.
