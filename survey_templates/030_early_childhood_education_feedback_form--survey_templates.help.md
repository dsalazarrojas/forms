## Early Childhood Education Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Early Childhood Education Feedback Form is designed to gather information about your experience with our early childhood education programs. Your feedback will help us to improve our services and provide the best possible education and care for children.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the parent(s) who have provided input for this form.
2. Please answer the question about whether the child is enrolled in our education program.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, please provide a detailed description in the "Program Type" field.
4. Please select the type of program you have experience with (Preschool, Daycare, Elementary School, or another option).
5. Finally, please provide any additional feedback or comments you may have in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parents Survey (<select_multiple>, parent_survey, optional)**: Please select all parents who have provided input for this form. You can choose from three options: Parent 1, Parent 2, Parent 3.
* **Child Survey (<select_one, child_survey, optional)**: Please answer the question about whether the child is enrolled in our education program. You can choose from two options: Yes or No.
* **Education Program (<text, education_program, optional)**: If you have any comments or feedback, please provide a detailed description about your experience with our education programs.
* **Program Type (<select_one, program_type, optional)**: Please select the type of program you have experience with. You can choose from three options: Preschool, Daycare, or Elementary School.
* **Feedback (<text, feedback, optional)**: Please provide any additional feedback or comments you may have about our education programs.

## Tips

* Be honest and detailed in your feedback, as it will help us to improve our services.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
* We appreciate your contributions and look forward to hearing about your experience with our early childhood education programs.
