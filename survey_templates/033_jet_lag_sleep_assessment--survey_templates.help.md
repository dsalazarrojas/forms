# Jet Lag Sleep Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your jet lag experience and sleep patterns. Please answer the questions honestly to provide the most accurate data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the options that apply to your travel history.
2. Choose one option that best describes your sleep patterns.
3. Describe your current symptoms.
4. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each symptom that you are experiencing.
5. Provide a brief description of your follow-up reminders, if any.
6. Choose the option that best describes your current jet lag symptoms.
7. Enter a brief description of your sleep assessment.
8. Enter any comments about your sleep assessment.
9. Enter your follow-up contact information, if applicable.
10. Provide your travel date.
11. Enter the time zone of your destination.
12. Enter the number of days post-travel you are currently experiencing jet lag.
13. Enter the total sleep time you have received during your travel.
14. Enter a value between 0 and 10 that describes your sleep quality.
15. Enter the wake-up time you are experiencing jet lag symptoms.
16. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each sleep schedule you currently have.
17. Enter the destination of your recent travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Travel History
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the options that apply to your travel history.
	+ If you have recently traveled, select all the options that apply to your travel experience.

### Sleep Patterns
* **Sleep Patterns** (`sleep_patterns`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one option that best describes your current sleep patterns.
	+ Select the option that most accurately represents your current sleep patterns during your travel period.

### Symptoms
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current symptoms.
	+ Be honest and provide details about your symptoms, including their severity and duration.

### Jet Lag Symptoms
* **Jet Lag Symptoms** (`jet_lag_symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each symptom you are experiencing.
	+ Only select the symptoms that apply to your current jet lag experience.

### Follow-Up Reminders
* **Follow-Up Reminders** (`follow_up_reminders`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your follow-up reminders, if any.
	+ If you have follow-up reminders, provide details about the reminders you are using to stay on schedule.

### Follow-Up Contact
* **Follow-Up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter your follow-up contact information, if applicable.
	+ Provide details about the contact information you prefer to receive follow-up reminders.

### Jet Lag Sleep Assessment
* **Jet Lag Sleep Assessment** (`jet_lag_sleep_assessment`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option that best describes your current jet lag symptoms.
	+ Select the option that most accurately represents your current jet lag experience.

### Sleep Assessment Comments
* **Sleep Assessment Comments** (`sleep_assessment_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments about your sleep assessment.
	+ Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your sleep assessment.

### Follow-Up Contact
* **Follow-Up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter your follow-up contact information, if applicable.
	+ Provide details about the contact information you prefer to receive follow-up reminders.

### Travel Date
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your recent travel.
	+ Enter the date you traveled, in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.

### Time Zone
* **Time Zone** (`travel_time_zone`, `number`, required: false): Enter the time zone of your destination.
	+ Enter the time zone of your destination, using the numerical code.

### Days Post-Travel
* **Days Post-Travel** (`jet_lag_days_post_travel`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of days post-travel you are experiencing jet lag symptoms.
	+ Enter the number of days since your travel that you are experiencing jet lag symptoms.

### Sleep Duration
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total sleep time you have received during your travel.
	+ Enter the total sleep time you have received since your travel, in hours.

### Sleep Quality
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, `number`, required: false): Enter a value between 0 and 10 that describes your sleep quality.
	+ Enter a value between 0 and 10 that represents your current sleep quality.

### Wake-Up Time
* **Wake-Up Time** (`wake_up_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the wake-up time you are experiencing jet lag symptoms.
	+ Enter the time you typically wake up, in the format 'HH:MM'.

### Sleep Schedule
* **Sleep Schedule** (`sleep_schedule`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each sleep schedule you currently have.
	+ Select the sleep schedules that apply to your current jet lag experience.

### Travel Destination
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, `text`, required: false): Enter the destination of your recent travel.
	+ Enter the destination of your recent travel.
