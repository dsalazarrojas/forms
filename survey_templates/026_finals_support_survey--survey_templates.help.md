# Finals Support Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from students regarding their experience with finals support. Your responses will help our support team identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions for better supporting students during finals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form with your honest and accurate answers.
3. Be sure to provide specific examples or details where possible.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Info
* **School Info** (`school_info`, text, false): 
This field is for you to provide your school information, such as your school name or code, if you are unsure of what to enter, please just type 'Unknown'.

### Finals Resources
* **Finals Resources** (`finals_resources`, select_one, false): 
Use this field to rate the quality of resources provided during finals. Please select one of the following options: 
* Good
* Fair
* Poor

### Finals Resources Stress
* **Finals Resources Stress** (`resource_stress`, number, false): 
Please rate how stressful you felt about finals resources from 1 to 10. This will help us understand the level of stress you experienced.

### Improvement Ideas
* **Improvement Ideas** (`support_ideas`, text, false): 
If you have any improvement ideas for finals support, please share them here. This is an optional field, so feel free to skip it if you don't have any ideas.

### Submission
* **Submission** (`submission`, select_multiple, false): 
Use this field to rate the submission of your work during finals. Please select one or multiple of the following options: 
* Good
* Fair
* Poor
