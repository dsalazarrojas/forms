# In Car Communication Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In Car Communication Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from employees about their experiences with in-car communication during company-provided rides. This form will help us understand how well we're communicating with you while you're on the road.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions below and select the answers that best fit your experience.
2. For each question, choose the answer that best reflects your thoughts and feelings.
3. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Communication Clarity** (`form_questions_1`, number, required: false):
	+ This question asks how clear the communication was during the ride. Choose a number that represents how easy or difficult it was to understand what was being communicated.
* **Comfort Level** (`form_questions_2`, number, required: false):
	+ This question asks how comfortable you felt during the ride. Choose a number that represents how pleasant or uncomfortable your experience was.
* **Overall Experience** (`form_questions_3`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This question asks for your general impression of the in-car communication experience. Choose an option that best describes your overall feeling.
* **Communication Style** (`form_questions_4`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This question asks about the communication style used by the driver during the ride. Choose all options that apply to your experience.
* **Frequency** (`form_questions_5` & `form_questions_6`, time, required: false):
	+ These questions ask about the frequency of communication during different parts of the ride. Choose a time of day or situation that best fits your experience.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure or have no preference, choose the option that best reflects your neutral feeling.
* Submitting this form is completely optional.
