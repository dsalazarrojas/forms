# Fashion New Collection Popularity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on a new fashion collection, aiming to understand their opinions, preferences, and demographic information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate the new fashion collection on a scale of 1-10.
2. Indicate your likelihood of purchasing from the collection.
3. Select the category that interests you the most in the collection.
4. Choose your preferred frequency of purchasing from the collection (if applicable).
5. Select your price range for the collection.
6. Select your preferred brand for the collection (if applicable).
7. Share any opinions or comments about the collection (optional).
8. Provide any additional comments or suggestions (optional).
9. Identify your demographic information (gender, age, etc.).
10. Indicate if you'd like to be contacted for further follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate this new fashion collection? (1)**: Rate the collection on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "not interested" and 10 is "very interested".
* **How likely are you to purchase from this collection?**: Indicate your likelihood of purchasing from the collection, with options such as "Very Likely", "Somewhat Likely", "Not Likely", etc.
* **Which category do you find this collection interesting?**: Select one category that interests you the most.
* **How often do you plan to purchase from this collection?**: Choose your preferred frequency of purchasing from the collection, such as "Once a week", "Every two weeks", "Once a month", etc.
* **What price range are you looking for?**: Select a price range for the collection, such as "Under $20", "$20-$50", "$50-$100", etc.
* **Which brand do you prefer?**: Select your preferred brand for the collection (if applicable).
* **Do you have any opinions about this collection?**: Share your thoughts, comments, or suggestions about the collection.
* **Additional comments or suggestions?**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Do you identify as?**: Select your gender, age range, or other demographic information.
* **Would you like to be contacted?**: Indicate if you'd like to be contacted for further follow-up, such as by phone, email, or postal mail.
* **Age**: Provide your age range or specific age.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **Address**: Enter your address (if you'd like to be contacted by postal mail).
* **ZIP**: Enter your postal code or zip code.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Take as much time as you need to complete the form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.

Note: This form is designed to be completed once per user, to ensure accurate and reliable results.
