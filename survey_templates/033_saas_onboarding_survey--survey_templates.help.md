# SaaS Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their onboarding experience with a SaaS application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and labels carefully.
2. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
3. Enter your contact and user information in the fields provided.
4. Submit the form when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (User Info) (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant user details.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info) (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact details, such as phone number and address.
* **Subscription Info** (Subscription Info) (`subscription_info`, text, optional): Enter your subscription-related details, such as subscription start date and plan type.
* **User Subscription History** (User Subscription History) (`user_subscription_history`, text, optional): Describe your subscription history with the SaaS application.
* **Billing Cycle** (Billing Cycle) (`billing_cycle`, text, optional): Specify your billing cycle, if applicable.
* **Subscription License** (Subscription License) (`subscription_license`, text, optional): Enter any relevant subscription license information.
* **Billing Frequency** (Billing Frequency) (`billing_frequency`, text, optional): Specify your billing frequency, if applicable.
* **Subscription Plan** (Subscription Plan) (`subscription_plan`, text, optional): Enter your subscription plan details.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any question, try to provide as much information as possible.
