# Healthcare Policy Impact Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information on recent policy changes in the healthcare system and their effects on healthcare providers and costs. Your input will help us better understand how these changes impact our services and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For the "What are the most recent policy changes you are aware of in the healthcare system?" question, please provide a brief summary of the changes you have observed or heard about (e.g., changes in laws, regulations, or guidelines).
3. When answering "What do you think are the biggest impacts of these policy changes?", consider any notable effects you have noticed or expect from these changes (e.g., changes in patient care, provider workload, or costs).
4. In the "category" field, select all the categories that you believe the policy changes affect (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
5. For the "age" field, please enter a numerical value representing your age.
6. In the "provider" field, enter the name of the health insurance provider you are associated with.
7. For the "cost" field, enter a numerical value representing any observed or expected changes in costs due to the policy changes.
8. When selecting "health insurance type" and "health insurance provider type", please choose the options that best describe your current health insurance arrangement.
9. Finally, review and submit your answers to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Healthcare Policy Impact Awareness Survey** (id: 1): This is an introductory note to remind you that you are about to complete a survey on healthcare policy changes.
* **What are the most recent policy changes you are aware of in the healthcare system?** (id: 2): Please provide a brief summary of the policy changes you have observed or heard about.
* **What do you think are the biggest impacts of these policy changes?** (id: 3): Consider any notable effects you have noticed or expect from these changes.
* **category** (id: 4): Select all categories that you believe the policy changes affect. These categories are predefined and should match your understanding of the changes' effects.
* **age** (id: 5): Enter your age to help us better understand how the policy changes might affect different age groups.
* **provider** (id: 6): Enter the name of the health insurance provider you are associated with to help us understand their involvement with the policy changes.
* **cost** (id: 7): Enter a numerical value representing any observed or expected changes in costs due to the policy changes.
* **health insurance type** (id: 8): Select the type of health insurance you have, as it may be affected by the policy changes.
* **health insurance provider type** (id: 9): Select the type of health insurance provider you have, as it may be affected by the policy changes.
* **Survey Complete** (id: 10): This is a confirmation note that you have finished the survey.

## Tips

* Be as concise and clear as possible when answering the questions.
* Focus on the most significant policy changes you are aware of.
* Select all applicable categories for the policy changes' effects.
* Enter your age accurately to help us better understand the age factor.
