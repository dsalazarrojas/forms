# Social Media Engagement Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to help you gather insights into your company's social media presence and engagement levels. It asks about your usage of various social media platforms, the frequency of your engagement, and the types of content you post.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the social media platforms you use (yes or no).
2. Choose the frequency of your engagement on these platforms (yes or no).
3. Select the types of interactions you have with your audience (yes or no).
4. Choose the types of content you post on these platforms (yes or no).
5. Enter the frequency of your content (number of posts per week).
6. Enter the average length of your content (in minutes).
7. Select the channels you use for engagement (yes or no).
8. Choose whether you post on your social media platforms (yes or no).
9. Choose whether you allow comments on your social media platforms (yes or no).
10. Select the types of content you post (yes or no) (this is a repeat of the content types field with a different label).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Social media platforms**: This question asks if you use any social media platforms, such as Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram. Please indicate "yes" if you have a presence on any of these platforms.
* **Engagement frequency**: This question asks about the frequency of your engagement with your audience on social media. Choose a number to represent how often you interact with your audience, such as 1-5 times a week.
* **Interaction types**: This question asks about the types of interactions you have with your audience, such as responding to comments or posting photos. Please select all that apply.
* **Content types**: This question asks about the types of content you post on social media, such as text, images, or videos. Please select all that apply.
* **Content frequency**: This question asks about the frequency of your content posts, such as 5 posts per week.
* **Content length**: This question asks about the average length of your social media posts, such as 5 minutes.
* **Engagement channels**: This question asks about the channels you use for engagement, such as private messages or public posts. Please select all that apply.
* **Social media posts**: This question asks whether you post on social media platforms, such as Facebook or Twitter.
* **Social media comments**: This question asks whether you allow comments on your social media platforms, such as Facebook or Twitter.
* **Content types 2**: This question asks about the types of content you post (this is a repeat of the content types field with a different label).
 
## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question accurately to get the most useful insights into your social media presence.
- You can choose "no" if you don't use a particular platform or don't engage with your audience on a particular channel.
- If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask your administrator for clarification.
