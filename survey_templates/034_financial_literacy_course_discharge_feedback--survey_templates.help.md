# Financial Literacy Course Discharge Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants who have completed the Financial Literacy Course. Your input will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
- Complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
- You can skip optional fields if you don't have an opinion or comment to share.
- Submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Course Satisfaction** (`course_satisfaction`, Select One, \*): How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the course? Select one of the following options:
  - Satisfied
  - Not Satisfied
  - Neutral
  - Dissatisfied

- **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, Optional): Share any thoughts, ideas, or concerns you have about the course. This is your chance to provide detailed comments and suggestions for improvement.

- **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, Text, Optional): If you have any suggestions for improving the course, please share them here.

- **Instructor Feedback** (`instructor_feedback`, Select One, \*): How would you rate your instructor's performance? Select one of the following options:
  - Good
  - Not Good
  - Neutral Instructor Feedback
  - Not Good Instructor

- **Outcome Improvement** (`outcome_improvement`, Text, Optional): If you have any ideas for how we can improve the outcomes of the course, share them here.

- **Track Outcomes** (`track_outcomes`, Date, Optional): If you have a specific date for when you'd like to track the outcomes of the course, provide it here.

- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): If you have any other comments or thoughts to share, please do so here.

## Tips

- Be honest and sincere with your feedback. Your input is invaluable to us.
- If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to share your thoughts.
- We value your time and appreciate your participation.
