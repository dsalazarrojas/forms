# Ethnicity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your ethnic background to help us better serve and understand the diversity of our users.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form by answering each question carefully and honestly.
- Select all that apply for each question.
- You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your background.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ethnicity Survey** (`introduction`, note, not required): This is a brief introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity_select`, select_multiple, not required): Please select all the ethnicities that apply to you, such as "Yes" for Asian, "Yes" for Black or African American, etc.
* **What is your race?** (`select_ethnic_race`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the races that apply to you, such as "Yes" for White, "Yes" for Asian, etc.
* **What is your ethnicity?** (`select_ethnic_ethnicity`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the ethnicities that apply to you, such as "Yes" for American Indian or Alaska Native, etc.
* **What is your culture?** (`select_ethnic_culture`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the cultures that apply to you, such as "Yes" for Hispanic or Latino, etc.
* **What is your preferred language?** (`select_ethnic_language`, select_multiple, not required): Select your preferred language.
* **What is your country of origin?** (`select_ethnic_country`, select_multiple, not required): Select your country of origin.
* **What is your ancestry?** (`select_ethnic_ancestry`, select_multiple, not required): Select your ancestry.
* **Select Ethnicity Group** (`select_ethnic_ethnicity_group`, select_multiple, not required): Select the ethnicity group that best applies to you.
* **Select Ethnicity Group Other** (`select_ethnic_ethnicity_group_other`, select_multiple, not required): If none of the above options apply, select "Other" and describe your ethnicity group.
