# information_and_enjoyment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `information_and_enjoyment_survey` form is used to gather user feedback on their experience with the information and services provided. It is designed to collect information about the user's thoughts, feelings, and perceptions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the `start` field.
2. Enter your name in the `name` field.
3. Provide your rating of the service or information in the `prophets_rating` field.
4. Optionally, enter your comment in the `comment` field.
5. Rate the service or information again in the `rating_2` field.
6. Optionally, enter another comment in the `comment_2` field.
7. Indicate whether you have finished with the service or information in the `finish` field.
8. (Optional) Enter your email address in the `email` field for further contact.
9. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the `phone` field for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **start**: This field is a text input where you should begin your feedback.
* **name**: This is your name, required for identification purposes.
* **prophets_rating**: This field is a number input where you rate the service or information, on a scale that is not specified.
* **comment**: This is an optional text input where you can provide further explanation or comments.
* **rating_2**: This field is another number input where you rate the service or information again.
* **comment_2**: This is another optional text input where you can provide further comments or explanation.
* **finish**: This field is a yes/no select_one input where you confirm whether you have finished with the service or information.
* **email**: This field is an email input, optional for further contact purposes.
* **phone**: This field is a text input, optional for further contact purposes.

Note: Please be aware that some fields are marked as "optional" and can be skipped if not relevant.
