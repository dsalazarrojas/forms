# Financial Well Being Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Well Being Survey is a form designed to collect information about a user's financial well-being. It asks questions about their financial status, income, debt, savings, risk tolerance, and investment goals. This information will help the user assess their current financial situation and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Survey Title field with a brief description of your current financial situation.
2. Select your Financial Status from the provided options.
3. Choose your Income Range from the provided options.
4. Select your Financial Goals from the provided options.
5. Indicate your Debt Level and prioritize your debts.
6. Choose your level of emergency funds.
7. Determine your Risk Tolerance from the provided options.
8. Select your Investment Goals from the provided options.
9. Select your Financial Education level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title (survey_title)**: A brief description of your current financial situation.
* **Financial Status (financial_status)**: Select the best description of your current financial status:
	+ Option 1: Desperate for money
	+ Option 2: Concerned but managing
	+ Option 3: Comfortable
* **Income Range (income_range)**: Select your current income range:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Financial Goals (financial_goals)**: Select your financial priorities:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Debt Level (debt_level)**: Select your level of debt:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Debt Priority (debt_priority)**: Select the priority level of your debts:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Financial Savings (financial_savings)**: Enter your current savings amount.
* **Emergency Funds (emergency_funds)**: Select the level of your emergency funds:
	+ Option 1

* **Risk Tolerance (risk_tolerance)**: Select your risk tolerance:
	+ Option 1

* **Investment Goals (investment_goals)**: Select your investment goals:
