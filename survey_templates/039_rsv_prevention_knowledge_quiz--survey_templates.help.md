# RSV Prevention Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a quiz to assess your understanding of RSV prevention strategies, transmission, symptoms, and communication plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the best answer based on your knowledge.
2. For select_multiple and select_one questions, choose one or more options from the list.
3. For text fields, provide a brief but clear answer.
4. Ensure that you fill out all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk).
5. If you're unsure about any question, try your best or mark it as unknown.

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the RSV prevention quiz.
* **RSV Prevention** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Describe your knowledge about RSV prevention strategies.

* **Transmission** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all possible ways RSV is transmitted.
* **Prevention** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Share your thoughts on preventing RSV.
* **Demographics** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your relevant demographic.
* **Age** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age.
* **RSV Prevention Familiarity Level** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate your familiarity with RSV prevention.
* **RSV Prevention Confidence Level** (`9`, `select_one`, required: false): Assess your confidence in RSV prevention.
* **RSV Prevention Interest Level** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your interest in RSV prevention.
* **RSV Prevention Behaviors** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your prevention behaviors.
* **RSV Prevention Barriers** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Describe any barriers to RSV prevention.
* **RSV Prevention Communication Plan** (`13`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred communication method for RSV prevention.
* **RSV Prevention Symptoms Tracking** (`14`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you track RSV symptoms.
* **RSV Prevention Symptoms Timeline** (`15`, `select_one`, required: false): Select a timeline for RSV symptoms.
* **RSV Prevention Symptoms Impact** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Assess the impact of RSV symptoms.
* **RSV Prevention Questions** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Ask any questions related to RSV prevention.
* **RSV Prevention Questions Answers** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Share your answers to the above RSV questions.
* **RSV Prevention Quiz** (`19`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the provided options for your RSV prevention quiz score.
* **RSV Prevention Quiz Score** (`20`, `text`, required: false): Enter your RSV prevention quiz score.

* **RSV Prevention Phone** (`25`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering questions.
- Review each question carefully and select the best response.
- Use the provided fields to provide clear and concise answers.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your administrator for assistance.
- This form is for RSV prevention knowledge assessment only and is not a medical diagnosis tool.
