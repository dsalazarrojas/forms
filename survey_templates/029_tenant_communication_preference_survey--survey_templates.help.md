# tenant-communication-preference-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect tenant communication preferences, allowing the tenant services team to tailor their interactions with tenants based on individual needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your preferred method of communication (e.g., email, phone, or other options).
2. Indicate your preferred language of communication (e.g., English, Spanish, or other options).
3. Select the best time of day to contact you (e.g., morning, afternoon, or evening).
4. Choose how often you would like to be contacted (e.g., daily, weekly, or monthly).
5. If you have any additional information you would like to share, you can enter it in the "Additional information" field.
6. If you have a preferred email address, you can enter it in the "Email" field.
7. If you have a preferred phone number, you can enter it in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you prefer to communicate with us** (`tenant_communication_method`, select_one): This field allows you to choose how you would like to communicate with us. Please select one of the available options.
* **Preferred language** (`preferred_language`, select_one): This field allows you to choose your preferred language of communication.
* **Best time of day to contact you** (`best_time_of_day`, select_multiple): This field allows you to select the best time of day to contact you. You can choose one or more options.
* **How often do you like to be contacted** (`preferred_contact_frequency`, select_multiple): This field allows you to choose how often you would like to be contacted. You can select one or more options.
* **Additional information** (`note`, note): This field is for any additional information you would like to share with us.
* **Email** (`email`, email): This field is for you to enter your preferred email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): This field is for you to enter your preferred phone number.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, you can enter it here to let us know how we can contact you.
