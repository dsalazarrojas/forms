# Bracket Challenge Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to register and complete a bracket challenge. It collects information about the participant, challenge preferences, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.
4. Ensure all fields are filled out completely and correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your identification documents. (<participant_first_name>)
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your identification documents. (<participant_last_name>)
* **Email Address**: The email address where you can be reached for challenge communications. (<email>)
* **Phone Number**: Your primary contact number. (<phone>)
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format, confirming you are 18 or older. (<date_of_birth>)
* **Age Verification**: Confirm that you are 18 or older. (<age_verification>)
* **Address**: Your complete home address. (<address>)
* **City**: The city of your residence. (<city>)
* **State**: The state of your residence. (<state>)
* **ZIP Code**: Your home ZIP code. (<zip_code>)
* **Challenge Name**: Select the bracket challenge you are participating in. (<challenge_name>)
* **Team Name**: A name for your bracket entry. (<team_name>)
* **Entry Type**: Select the type of entry (Individual, Team, Office pool, Family pool). (<entry_type>)
* **Team Members**: List other members of your team, if applicable. (<team_members>)
* **Entry Fee**: The amount paid for entry. (<entry_fee>)
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment (Credit card, Debit card, PayPal, Venmo, Cash, Check). (<payment_method>)
* **Payment Status**: Confirm payment status (Paid in full, Pending, Will pay later). (<payment_status>)
* **Bracket Submission**: Select the method of submitting your bracket (Online submission, Paper bracket, Mobile app, Email). (<bracket_submission>)
* **Bracket Deadline**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the deadline. (<bracket_deadline>)
* **Scoring System**: Confirm that you understand the scoring system. (<scoring_system>)
* **Prize Structure**: Confirm that you understand the prize structure. (<prize_structure>)
* **Rules Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the rules. (<rules_acknowledgment>)
* **Tiebreaker Understanding**: Confirm that you understand the tiebreaker rules. (<tiebreaker_understanding>)
* **Communication Preference**: Select how you prefer to be contacted (Email, Text message, Phone call, App notification). (<communication_preference>)
* **Newsletter Signup**: Confirm if you want to receive updates and promotions. (<newsletter_signup>)
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select how you heard about the challenge (Friend referral, Social media, Website, Advertisement, Previous participant, Other). (<referral_source>)
* **Bracket Challenge Experience**: Select your level of experience (First time, Some experience, Experienced, Very experienced). (<experience_level>)
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name as it appears on your identification documents. (<participant_signature>)
* **Signature Date**: Date of registration. (<signature_date>)
