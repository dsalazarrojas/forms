# gSCO Family Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your family's participation in events and your preferences for communication and event frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your family's level of comfort with participating in in-person events by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
2. Indicate how often you participate in in-person events by selecting a number.
3. Select the type of events your family is most likely to participate in.
4. Choose the methods you typically use to participate in events with your family.
5. Describe your typical family event participation in the text box.
6. Select the day(s) of the week you usually participate in family events.
7. Choose the time of day you usually participate in family events.
8. Select the day(s) of the week you usually participate in family events.
9. Choose the methods you use to communicate with family members.
10. Indicate how often you communicate with family members.
11. Enter the time zone you usually participate in family events.
12. Provide a description of your family's event participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family level of comfort with in-person events** (`family_level_of_comfort`, number, required: false): Rate your family's comfort level with participating in in-person events, where 1 is "not comfortable at all" and 5 is "very comfortable".
* **Frequency of in-person event participation** (`event_frequency`, number, required: false): Select a number that indicates how often you participate in in-person events.
* **Type of event** (`event_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of event your family is most likely to participate in (Event with friends, Event with family, Event with a mix of both, or No preference).
* **How do you usually participate in events with your family?** (`participation_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the methods you typically use to participate in events with your family (In-person, Virtual, Mixed, or No preference).
* **Typical family event participation** (`typical_participation`, note, required: false): Describe your typical family event participation.
* **Day of the week for family events** (`day_of_week`, date, required: false): Select the day of the week you usually participate in family events.
* **Time of day for family events** (`time_of_participation`, time, required: false): Choose the time of day you usually participate in family events.
* **Event Days** (`event_days`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the day(s) you usually participate in family events (Weekdays or Weekends).
* **Communication methods with family members** (`communication_methods`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the methods you usually use to communicate with family members (Calls, Messages, Emails, Video calls, Social media, In-person).
* **Frequency of communication with family members** (`communication_frequency`, number, required: false): Indicate how often you communicate with family members.
* **Time zone for family events** (`time_zone`, text, required: false): Enter the time zone you usually participate in family events.
* **Description** (`description`, note, required: false): Provide a description of your family's event participation.
