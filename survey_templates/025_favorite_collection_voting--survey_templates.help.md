# Favorite Collection Voting - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect data from participants for the "Favorite Collection Voting" activity, where participants choose their favorite participants or items from a collection and provide their votes for each selected participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `contest_id` field with the unique identifier for the contest or collection.
2. Enter your name in the `participant_name` field.
3. Select your favorite participants in the `favorite_participants` field by choosing from the available options.
4. Enter the number of votes for each of your favorite participants in the `votes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contest Id** (`1`, text, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the contest or collection.
* **Participant Name** (`2`, text, required: false): Enter your name for the purpose of this activity.
* **Favorite Participants** (`3`, select multiple, required: false): Choose your favorite participants or items from the available options.
	+ Select one or more options that best represent your favorite participants.
* **Votes** (`4`, number, required: false): Enter the number of votes for each of your selected favorite participants.
	+ Ensure you enter a valid numerical value for the votes field.
