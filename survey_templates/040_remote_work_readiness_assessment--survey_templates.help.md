# remote_work_readiness_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The remote Work Readiness Assessment is a form used to evaluate an employee's preparedness for remote work. It helps in determining whether an employee can work effectively from home and ensures that the employee is familiar with company policies, technology requirements, and other related aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer the questions honestly, providing information about your current remote work status, work-from-home frequency, and other relevant details.
* Be honest about your current work-from-home status, whether you're actively working from home or not.
* For select multiple-choice questions, choose the option that best represents your situation or status.
* Do not provide any sensitive or confidential information.
