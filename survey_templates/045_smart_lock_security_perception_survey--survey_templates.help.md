# Smart Lock Security Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gauge users' perceptions of their smart lock security, gathering information on how secure they feel with their smart locks, any concerns they may have, and their overall satisfaction with the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page and its corresponding questions carefully.
2. Answer all required questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the provided options to select your responses, and if none of the options match your answer, please provide a brief explanation in the text box.
4. Enter your contact information (email, phone number, and name) for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How secure do you feel with your smart lock?** (`trust_page`, select_one, required): How secure do you feel with your smart lock? Select one of the options:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **What are your concerns with your smart lock?** (`concerns_page`, select_multiple, required): Check all that apply:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **How satisfied are you with your smart lock?** (`satisfaction_page`, select_one, required): How satisfied do you feel with your smart lock? Select one of the options:
	+ 'Satisfied'
	+ 'Dissatisfied'
* **Comments** (`comments_page`, text, optional): If you have any comments or feedback about your smart lock, please enter them here.
* **Email** (`email_page`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone_page`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Date** (`date_page`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the survey.
* **Time** (`time_page`, time, optional): Enter the time you completed the survey.
* **Notes** (`notes_page`, note, optional): If you have any additional notes or comments, please enter them here.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_page`, text, optional): Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them here.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your address for follow-up purposes.
* **Trust Level** (`trust_level`, select_one, required): How would you rate the trust level of your smart lock? Select one of the options:
	+ High
	+ Low
* **Concern Frequency** (`concern_frequency`, select_one, required): How often do you experience concerns with your smart lock? Select one of the options:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Satisfaction Score** (`satisfaction_score`, select_one, required): How satisfied do you feel with your smart lock? Select one of the options:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
