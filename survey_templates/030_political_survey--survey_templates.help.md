# political_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a political survey. The form is a simple, 3-page survey with text fields for respondents to express their opinions and thoughts on various political topics. It's intended to gather data for research or analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction page, which provides some background information about the survey.
2. Answer each question on the subsequent pages by typing your thoughts and opinions in the text fields provided.
3. Make sure to fill in all the fields, as they are all optional.
4. Once you've completed all the pages, click submit to finish the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): This page provides some background information about the survey. You can read it at your convenience.
* **question_one** (`question_one`, `text`, optional): This question asks for your thoughts and opinions about a particular topic.
* **question_two** (`question_two`, `text`, optional): This question is another opportunity for you to share your thoughts and opinions.
* **question_three** (`question_three`, `text`, optional): This is the last question, where you can share your thoughts and opinions once more.

## Tips
* Make sure to be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you need more space to answer a particular question, feel free to add additional lines.
* You can use this form for your own research or analysis purposes, or simply to gather more information on the topics presented.
* If you have any difficulties or questions while filling out the form, please contact: form@gic.mx.
