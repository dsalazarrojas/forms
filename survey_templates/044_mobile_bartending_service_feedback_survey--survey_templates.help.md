# Mobile Bartending Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with a mobile bartending service. It helps us improve our services and build customer relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the event that you attended, including the date and any other details that you think are relevant.
2. Share your experience with the bartender who served you, including their name if you remember it.
3. Let us know the name of the venue where the event took place.
4. If you have any feedback about the event, share it with us in the "Feedback" field.
5. If you have any praise or compliments for the bartender, share it with us in the "Testimonial" field.
6. Finally, don't forget to let us know how we can contact you if we need more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): This field is a free-text area where you can provide any information about the event, such as the date, location, or any other relevant details.
* **Bartender Name** (`bartender_name`, text, required: false): This field is where you can share the name of the bartender who served you, if you remember it. Don't worry if you don't know their name; just leave it blank.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): This field is a date picker where you can select the date of the event that you attended. Make sure to format the date in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`, text, required: false): This field is where you can share the name of the venue where the event took place.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, text, required: false): This field is where you can specify the type of event (e.g., birthday party, corporate event, etc.).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): This field is a free-text area where you can provide any feedback about your experience with our mobile bartending service. Be as specific as possible, and let us know what we can do better.
* **Testimonial** (`testimonial`, text, required: false): This field is where you can share a positive comment or praise for the bartender who served you.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, text, required: false): This field is where you can provide your contact information, and we'll be happy to get back to you if we need more information.
