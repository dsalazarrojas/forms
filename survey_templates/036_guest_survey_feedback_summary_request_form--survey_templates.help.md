# Guest Survey Feedback Summary Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from guests on the events or incidents they have experienced at a location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each question and provide any additional comments or information you wish to share. Please note that all fields are optional, but providing as much information as possible will help us better understand your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What happened? (`guest_survey_feedback_summary_request_form`, select_multiple, optional)**: This question is asking you to describe the specific event or incident you experienced at the location. Select all the options that apply to provide a clear picture of what happened.
  - 'Yes' indicates that you experienced the event or incident.
  - 'No' indicates that you did not experience the event or incident.
  - You can also add any additional comments or details about your experience.
- **Feedback Type (`feedback_type`, select_one, optional)**: This question asks you to specify the type of feedback you wish to provide. Please select the option that best describes your feedback.
  - Option 1: General Feedback
  - Option 2: Suggestion
  - Option 3: Concern
- **Location (`location`, text, optional)**: If you are providing feedback about a specific location, please provide the address or name of the location.
- **Name (`contact_name`, text, optional)**: If you would like to be contacted or have your feedback attributed to your name, please provide your name here.
- **Email (`contact_email`, email, optional)**: If you would like to be contacted or have your feedback attributed to your email address, please provide your email address here.
- **Phone (`contact_phone`, text, optional)**: If you would like to be contacted or have your feedback attributed to your phone number, please provide your phone number here.
- **Date of Visit (`date_of_visit`, date, optional)**: If you are providing feedback about a specific date of visit, please provide the date of your visit.
- **Time of Visit (`time_of_visit`, time, optional)**: If you are providing feedback about a specific time of visit, please provide the time of your visit.
- **Comment (`comment`, note, optional)**: If you have any additional comments or information about your experience, please provide them here.
- **Submit (`submit`, text, not required)**: Clicking the submit button will send your feedback to us. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting.

## Tips
* Please provide as much information as possible to help us better understand your feedback.
* If you are unable to select all the options for the "What happened?" question, please add any additional comments or details in the "Comment" field.
* If you would like to be contacted or have your feedback attributed to your name, email address, or phone number, please provide that information in the respective fields.
