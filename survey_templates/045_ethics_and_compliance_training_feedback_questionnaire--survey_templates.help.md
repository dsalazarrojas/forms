# Ethics and Compliance Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from trainees on their experiences with the ethics and compliance training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions that apply to your specific training experience.
2. Select the options that best match your experiences.
3. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Program** (`page_1`, `note`, required: false): This section is a placeholder for notes or comments on the overall training program.
* **Training Participants** (`section_2_training_participants`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the participant(s) who delivered the training. You may select one or more of the options.
* **Training Topics** (`section_3_training_topics`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the topics that were covered during the training. You may select multiple options.
* **Training Value** (`section_4_training_value`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number that represents how much value you derived from the training.
* **Training Effectiveness** (`section_6_training_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the effectiveness of the training. You may select one option.
* **Training Participants** (`section_7_training_participants`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the participant(s) who participated in the training. You may select multiple options.
* **Undertaken** (`section_8_undertaken`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date on which you completed the training.
* **Time Spent** (`section_9_time_spent`, `time`, required: false): Enter the approximate time spent on the training.
* **Additional Comments** (`section_10_additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the training experience.
