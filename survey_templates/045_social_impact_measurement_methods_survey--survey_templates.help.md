# Social Impact Measurement Methods Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your organization's social impact measurement methods, helping us better understand your approach, data collection methods, and reporting frequency. Please answer these questions to the best of your ability, and provide any additional information that you feel relevant to your organization's social impact measurement methods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, using the provided options as a guide.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For questions with a date field, enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. If you need help or clarification on any question, feel free to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`id: 1`, `type: note`, required: false)
  * This section provides a space for you to introduce your organization's social impact measurement methods. Please use this space to provide any relevant context or information about your approach.
* **Measurement Approach** (`id: 2`, `type: select_one`, required: false)
  * This question asks about your organization's measurement approach. Select 'Yes' if your organization uses a measurement approach, or 'No' if not.
* **Social Impact Indicators** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false)
  * This question asks about the social impact indicators you use. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Data Collection** (`id: 4`, `type: date`, required: false)
  * This question asks for the date of your most recent data collection. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Data Analysis** (`id: 5`, `type: time`, required: false)
  * This question asks about the frequency of your data analysis. Please enter the time it takes for your organization to analyze your data.
* **Reporting Frequency** (`id: 6`, `type: number`, required: false)
  * This question asks how often your organization reports its social impact data. Please enter the number of times you report per year.
* **Challenges** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false)
  * Please provide any challenges your organization faces while measuring its social impact. This can be helpful for us to better understand your needs.
* **Insights** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false)
  * This question asks about any insights your organization has gained from its social impact measurement methods. Please provide any relevant information you feel is helpful.
* **Nonprofit Characteristics** (`id: 9`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false)
  * This question asks about your organization's characteristics as a nonprofit. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Foundation Characteristics** (`id: 10`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false)
  * This question asks about your organization's foundation characteristics. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Enterprise Characteristics** (`id: 11`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false)
  * This question asks about your organization's enterprise characteristics. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Impact Area Focus** (`id: 12`, `type: select_one`, required: false)
  * This question asks about the impact area your organization focuses on. Select 'Yes' if your organization focuses on this area, or 'No' if not.
