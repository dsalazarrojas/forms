# fair_work_laws_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help employees understand their rights and benefits under fair work laws, which are designed to protect employees' rights in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your own understanding and experience.
2. Select the options that best describe your situation.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please answer based on your best guess.
4. Once you've completed the form, review your answers to ensure you've answered all the questions and selected the correct options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employment Status** (`employed`, `unemployed`, `student`, `other`): Please select the option that best describes your current employment status.
* **Work Type** (`full-time`, `part-time`, `contract`, `other`): Please select all the types of work you perform.
* **Work Hours**: Please enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Overtime Pay**: Please enter the number of hours you receive overtime pay for.
* **Breaks** (`True`, `False`): Please select whether you receive regular breaks during the workday.
* **Fair Pay** (`True`, `False`): Please select whether you are paid fairly for your work.
* **Safe Harbor** (`True`, `False`): Please select whether you are protected by a safe harbor clause.
* **Paid Vacation** (`True`, `False`): Please select whether you receive paid vacation time.
* **Paid Sick Leave** (`True`, `False`): Please select whether you receive paid sick leave.
* **Workers Compensation** (`True`, `False`): Please select whether you have workers' compensation.
* **Minimum Wage** (`True`, `False`): Please select whether you receive minimum wage.
* **Equal Pay** (`True`, `False`): Please select whether you receive equal pay for equal work.
* **Paid Family Leave** (`True`, `False`): Please select whether you receive paid family leave.
* **Paid Family Leave Reasons**: Please enter a brief description of the reasons you take paid family leave.
* **Fair Work Laws Familiarity** (`familiar`, `not familiar`): Please select whether you are familiar with fair work laws.
* **Fair Work Laws Familiarity Reasons**: Please enter a brief description of why you are (or are not) familiar with fair work laws.

**Tips**

* Please answer each question based on your own understanding and experience.
* If you're unsure about any question, please answer based on your best guess.
* Once you've completed the form, review your answers to ensure you've answered all the questions and selected the correct options.
