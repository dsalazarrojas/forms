# Equine Learning Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on our equine learning sessions. Your input will help us create a more engaging and valuable experience for future participants. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the questions carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
2. Fill in all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, feel free to include them in the "Suggestions" field.
4. Enter your Email Address and Participant Name for contact purposes.
5. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief welcome message from us.
* **What was the date of your equine learning session? **: Please enter the date of the session you participated in.
* **What time was your equine learning session?** : Enter the time of the session, if you can.
* **Where did your equine learning session take place?** : Describe the location of the session, e.g. "Stable A".
* **Who was your instructor?** : Please enter the name of your instructor.
* **How would you rate your instructor?** : Choose a rating from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **What did you find most valuable in the session?** : Describe what you found most valuable or enjoyable in the session.
* **What did you find least valuable in the session?** : Describe what you found least valuable or challenging in the session.
* **Do you have any suggestions for future sessions?** : If you have any suggestions or ideas for how we can improve future sessions, please share them with us.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name so we can address you in our response.
* **Participant Contact Info**: If you have any additional contact information (e.g. phone number), please enter it here.
