# Social Movements And Their Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on social movements and their impact.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read through all the questions carefully to understand what information is being gathered.
2.  Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are completed, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.
4.  Select the most accurate options for each question from the provided choices.
5.  Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Awareness** (`awareness_question`, Number, Optional): This question assesses the level of awareness about the social movement.
    *   Please answer this question by selecting the number that best reflects your level of awareness.
*   **Participation** (`participation_question`, Select One, Optional): This question inquires about your level of participation in the social movement.
    *   Please choose 'Yes' if you have participated in the social movement, and 'No' if you haven't.
*   **Outcomes** (`outcomes_question`, Select Multiple, Optional): This question explores the outcomes or achievements of the social movement.
    *   Select all the options that best describe the outcomes of the social movement.
*   **Type** (`movement_type`, Select One, Optional): This question asks about the category or type of social movement.
    *   Please choose the option that best classifies the social movement.
*   **Name** (`movement_name`, Text, Optional): This question collects the name or title of the social movement.
    *   Please provide the full name or title of the social movement.
*   **Cause Area** (`cause_area`, Select Multiple, Optional): This question inquires about the area or sector of the cause being addressed by the social movement.
    *   Select all the options that best describe the cause area of the social movement.
*   **Description** (`movement_description`, Text, Optional): This question asks for a brief description of the social movement.
    *   Please provide a concise description of the social movement.
*   **Statement** (`impact_statement`, Select Multiple, Optional): This question explores the statement or slogan of the social movement.
    *   Select all the options that best describe the statement or slogan of the social movement.
*   **Participant** (`movement_participant`, Select Multiple, Optional): This question asks about the group or community participating in the social movement.
    *   Select all the options that best describe the participant group or community.
*   **Number of Participants** (`participant_number`, Number, Optional): This question inquires about the number of participants in the social movement.
    *   Please provide the number of participants.
*   **Location** (`location`, Text, Optional): This question asks about the location or region where the social movement took place.
    *   Please provide the location or region.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Optional): This question inquires about the start date of the social movement.
    *   Please provide the start date in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Optional): This question asks about the end date of the social movement.
    *   Please provide the end date in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
*   **Survey Response** (`survey_response`, Text, Optional): This is an open-ended question for any additional comments or feedback about the survey.
    *   Please provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
