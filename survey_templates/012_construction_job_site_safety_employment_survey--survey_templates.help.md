# Construction Job Site Safety Employment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on your experiences with safety on your job site. Your input will help us improve safety procedures and protocols to create a safer work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Employee Identification**: Enter your employee ID or name, or click "Skip" if you wish to remain anonymous.
2. Fill out the subsequent sections according to the instructions and options provided.
3. Answer all questions truthfully, as they will help us identify areas of improvement.
4. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best reflects your experience.
5. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID or Name**: A field for entering your employee ID or name. This information is optional and will help us track your responses if you choose to provide it.
* **Current Assigned Job Site**: Enter the name of your current job site or the location you are working on. (Required)
* **Current Job Role/Trade**: Enter your current job role or trade, e.g., carpenter, electrician, site manager. (Required)
* **My supervisor prioritizes safety over project deadlines**: Rate your supervisor's priority for safety, with 1 being Strongly Disagree and 5 being Strongly Agree. (Required)
* **I feel empowered to stop work if I see a safety hazard**: Rate your level of empowerment to stop work when faced with a safety hazard, with 1 being Strongly Disagree and 5 being Strongly Agree. (Required)
* **Does the company provide all necessary PPE on time?**: Select how often the company provides you with necessary PPE, with options to choose from: Always, Usually, Sometimes, Rarely, Never. (Required)
* **When was your last safety toolbox talk or meeting?**: Select when you last attended a safety-related meeting, with options to choose from: Today, Within the last week, Within the last month, More than a month ago, Never. (Required)
* **Rate the quality of the safety training you've received**: Rate the quality of your safety training, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent. (Required)
* **What additional safety training do you feel you need?**: Enter any additional safety training you feel you require. (Optional)
* **Have you witnessed a near-miss or incident in the last 30 days?**: Select if you've witnessed a near-miss or incident, with options to choose from: True or False. (Required)
* **Was the near-miss/incident reported to management?**: Select if the near-miss or incident was reported, with options to choose from: True, False, N/A. (Optional)
* **If not reported, what was the reason?**: Enter the reason why the near-miss or incident was not reported. (Optional)
* **Do you have any current safety concerns on your site?**: Enter any current safety concerns you have on your site. (Optional)
* **How can we make our job sites safer?**: Enter your suggestions on how to improve job site safety. (Optional)
* **Date of Completion**: Select the date you are completing this survey. (Required)

Please note that all required fields are marked as "Required" to ensure you provide the necessary information to help us improve job site safety.
