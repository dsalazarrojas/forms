# Concert Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather feedback from concert attendees about their experience. It is meant to help event organizers improve their events and provide a better experience for attendees in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Begin by filling in your full name in the "Full name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email address" field for follow-up purposes.
3.  Select the date of the concert you attended from the calendar in the "Event date" field.
4.  If you attended a concert in a venue with a specific name (e.g., "Madison Square Garden"), type it in the "Venue name" field.
5.  Choose where you were seated or standing from the options provided in the "Seating area" field.
6.  Rate your overall experience using the "Overall experience rating" field.
7.  Rate the sound quality by selecting the appropriate rating from the "Sound quality rating" field.
8.  Assess the crowd management and safety from the "Crowd management and safety rating" field.
9.  If you experienced any notable moments during the concert, describe them in the "Favorite moment" field.
10.  Use the "Additional comments or suggestions" field for any other feedback or suggestions you may have for future event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name** (`respondent_name`, text, **required**): This is your full name as you would like to be known in this feedback form. Please enter your first and last name.
- **Email address** (`email_address`, email, **required**): Your email address will be used for follow-up purposes if the event organizers need to contact you about your feedback or if you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions.
- **Event date** (`event_date`, date, **required**): This is the date of the concert you attended. Try to select the date as accurately as possible to help event organizers understand the timing of your experience.
- **Venue name** (`venue_name`, text, optional): If you attended a concert in a specific venue, please type its name. This will help event organizers know the specific venue of your experience.
- **Seating area** (`seating_area`, select_one, optional): Choose the area where you were seated or standing during the concert. This could be General admission, Floor, Lower level, Upper level, or VIP area.
- **Overall experience rating** (`overall_experience`, select_one, **required**): Rate your overall experience at the concert, using the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
- **Sound quality rating** (`sound_quality`, select_one, **required**): Rate the sound quality of the concert, using the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
- **Crowd management and safety rating** (`crowd_management`, select_one, optional): Assess the crowd management and safety, using the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
- **Favorite moment** (`favorite_moment`, text, optional): If there were any standout moments during the concert that you particularly enjoyed or would like to highlight, please describe them here.
- **Additional comments or suggestions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any other thoughts, suggestions, or comments about the concert or event, please add them here.
