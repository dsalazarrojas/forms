# Executive Director Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights from the Executive Director about their leadership style, experience, and team work. It is intended for internal use within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand the questions being asked.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the provided options to select the most accurate responses.
4. If you have additional comments or questions, use the "open-ended questions" field to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Executive Director Survey** (`executive_director_survey_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form. You can leave this field blank if you prefer.

* **Experience** (`experience`, note, optional): This field is for you to provide any relevant experience or background information about your role as the Executive Director.

* **Leadership** (`leadership`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you believe you have strong leadership skills and "No" if you do not.

* **Communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the ways you prefer to communicate with your team, such as "Yes" for email, "Yes" for phone calls, etc.

* **Team Work** (`team_work`, number, optional): Estimate the percentage of time you spend working collaboratively with your team (e.g., 50% for "Most" and 0 for "Never").

* **Leadership Style** (`leadership_style`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you consider yourself an effective leader and "No" if not.

* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you would like to provide.

* **Open-Ended Questions** (`open_ended_questions`, note, optional): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide that does not fit into the other questions.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Use clear and concise language in the "open-ended questions" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the form administrator.
