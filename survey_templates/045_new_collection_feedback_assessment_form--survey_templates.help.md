# New Collection Feedback Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a specific collection from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide a brief title for your feedback in the "New Collection Feedback" field.
2. Provide the name of the collection you are giving feedback on in the "Collection Name" field.
3. Add a brief description of the collection in the "Collection Description" field.
4. Share your thoughts on what you thought of when you saw the collection in the "What did you think of when you saw the collection?" field.
5. If you have something positive to say, please tell us what you liked most about the collection in the "What did you like most about the collection?" field.
6. Conversely, if you have any concerns, let us know what you liked least about the collection in the "What did you like least about the collection?" field.
7. Rate your overall satisfaction with the collection on a scale from 1 to 10 in the "Rate your satisfaction with the collection" field.
8. Let us know if you would recommend this collection to a friend or not in the "Would you recommend this collection to a friend?" field.
9. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them in the "What would you like to see improved in the collection?" field.
10. You can also provide an additional rating of your overall satisfaction with the collection on a scale from 1 to 10 in the "Form Collection Satisfaction 2" field.
11. Finally, if you'd like to add any additional comments, please do so in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Collection Feedback** (`form_title`, text, optional): A brief title for your feedback.
* **Collection Name** (`collection_name`, text, optional): The name of the collection you are providing feedback on.
* **Collection Description** (`collection_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the collection.
* **What did you think of when you saw the collection?** (`what_did_you_think_of_when_you_saw_the_collection`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on what you thought of when you saw the collection.
* **What did you like most about the collection?** (`what_did_you_like_most_about_the_collection`, text, optional): If you have something positive to say, tell us what you liked most about the collection.
* **What did you like least about the collection?** (`what_did_you_like_least_about_the_collection`, text, optional): If you have any concerns, let us know what you liked least about the collection.
* **Rate your satisfaction with the collection** (`form_collection_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the collection on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Would you recommend this collection to a friend?** (`would_you_recommend_this_collection_to_a_friend`, select_multiple, optional): Let us know if you would recommend this collection to a friend or not.
* **What would you like to see improved in the collection?** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improvement.
* **Form Collection Satisfaction 2** (`form_collection_satisfaction_2`, select_one, optional): An additional rating of your overall satisfaction with the collection on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Additional comments** (`form_question_1`, text, optional): Any additional comments you'd like to add about the collection.
* **Contact Information** (`form_question_1_2`, text, optional): Your contact information for follow up.
