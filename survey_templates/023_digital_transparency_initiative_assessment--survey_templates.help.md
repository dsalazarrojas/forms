# Digital Transparency Initiative Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the team's digital transparency initiative and gather information about their current state, which will help the organization identify areas for improvement and provide recommendations for increasing digital transparency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required".
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
3. Ensure that the team lead has authorized the completion of this form.
4. If there are any changes in the team's size or type, update the form accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date): The date when you are completing this form.
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time): The time when you are completing this form.
* **Assessment Location** (`assessment_location`, text): The location where the team is currently situated.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, select_one): The size of the team, choose one of the following options:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
* **Assessment Frequency** (`assessment_frequency`, text): This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional information about the team's assessment frequency.
* **Team Type** (`team_type`, select_one): The type of team, choose one of the following options:
	+ Internal
	+ External
	+ Other
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text): The name of the team.
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, email): The email address of the team lead.
* **Team Lead Name** (`team_lead_name`, text): The name of the team lead.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, text): The phone number of the team lead.
* **Team Lead Title** (`team_lead_title`, text): The title of the team lead.
* **Assessment Comments** (`assessment_comments`, text): This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional comments about the team's digital transparency state.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the team lead has authorized the completion of this form.
* If there are any changes in the team's size or type, update the form accordingly.
* Answer each question accurately and honestly.
* This form is designed to gather information about the team's digital transparency state, not to evaluate the team's performance.
