# Referee Of The Year Voting - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and ratings from users to identify the "Referee Of The Year" among the nominees. The form helps to gather information on the overall performance and characteristics of each nominee, including their first name, last name, email, overall rating, communication skills, knowledge of the game, positive attitude, and teamwork. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Overall Rating, Communication Skills, Knowledge of Game, and Referee Of The Year.
*   Enter your feedback and comments in the "Feedback" and "Comments" fields if desired.
*   Select the nominee(s) you wish to vote for in the "Referee Of The Year" and "Form 13" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`form_1`, text, required): Enter the first name of the nominee.
*   **Last Name** (`form_2`, text, required): Enter the last name of the nominee.
*   **Email** (`form_3`, email, required): Enter the email address of the nominee.
*   **Phone** (`form_4`, text, optional): Enter a phone number for the nominee if available.
*   **Overall Rating** (`form_5`, number, required): Rate the nominee's overall performance as a number between 1 and 5.
*   **Communication Skills** (`form_6`, number, optional): Rate the nominee's communication skills as a number between 1 and 5.
*   **Knowledge of Game** (`form_7`, number, required): Rate the nominee's knowledge of the game as a number between 1 and 5.
*   **Positive Attitude** (`form_8`, number, optional): Rate the nominee's positive attitude as a number between 1 and 5.
*   **Teamwork** (`form_9`, number, required): Rate the nominee's ability to work as part of a team as a number between 1 and 5.
*   **Nominee** (`form_10`, text, optional): Enter the name of an additional nominee if desired.
*   **Nomination Reason** (`form_11`, text, optional): Enter the reason why you nominated the referee.
*   **Referee Of The Year** (`form_12`, select_one, required): Select the nominee that you think should be "Referee Of The Year".
*   **Form 13** (`form_13`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple nominees that should also be considered for "Referee Of The Year" if desired.
*   **Feedback** (`form_14`, text, optional): Enter any additional feedback you may have.
*   **Comments** (`form_15`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments you may have.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate voting results.
*   Select only one nominee for "Referee Of The Year" unless you wish to provide multiple nominations for consideration.
*   Enter feedback and comments if you have any additional information to share about the nominees.
