# Cardiac Treatment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from patients about their experience with cardiac treatment in the facility. It helps identify areas of improvement and ensures that the care provided is of high quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
*   For questions with multiple options, select the one that best describes your experience.
*   If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Suggestions for improvement or additional comments" field.
*   Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your medical record.
*   **Patient ID or Medical Record Number** (`patient_id`, text, optional): If you have a patient ID or medical record number, please enter it here.
*   **Date of treatment** (`visit_date`, date, required): The date of your cardiac treatment.
*   **Type of cardiac treatment received** (`treatment_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of cardiac treatment you received (e.g., Consultation, Medication Management, Cardiac Rehabilitation, etc.).
*   **Provider name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): The name of your healthcare provider.
*   **Improvement in cardiac symptoms** (`symptom_improvement`, select_one, required): Please select how you felt after treatment (Significantly Improved, Improved, No Change, Worsened, Not Applicable).
*   **Treatment effectiveness** (`treatment_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Please select how effective you felt the treatment was (Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, Too Early to Tell).
*   **Improvement in exercise tolerance** (`activity_tolerance`, select_one, optional): Please select how your exercise tolerance improved after treatment (Significantly Improved, Improved, No Change, Worsened, Not Applicable).
*   **Overall quality of life improvement** (`quality_of_life`, select_one, required): Please select how your overall quality of life improved after treatment (Significantly Better, Better, No Change, Worse, Not Sure).
*   **Provider and care quality** (`provider_quality`, note, optional): Please use this field to provide any comments about the quality of care and provider you received.
*   **Provider knowledge and expertise** (`provider_knowledge`, select_one, required): Please select how knowledgeable and expert your provider was (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Quality of communication** (`communication_quality`, select_one, required): Please select how well your provider communicated with you (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Clarity of treatment explanation** (`treatment_explanation`, select_one, required): Please select how clear your provider explained your treatment (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Coordination with other healthcare providers** (`care_coordination`, select_one, optional): Please select how well your provider coordinated with other healthcare providers (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, Not Applicable).
*   **Staff courtesy and professionalism** (`staff_courtesy`, select_one, required): Please select how courteous and professional the staff were (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Satisfaction with wait times** (`wait_time`, select_one, required): Please select how satisfied you were with the wait times (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Facility cleanliness** (`facility_cleanliness`, select_one, optional): Please select how clean you thought the facility was (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Availability of necessary medical equipment** (`equipment_availability`, select_one, optional): Please select how available you thought necessary medical equipment was (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Overall satisfaction with cardiac care** (`overall_care_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Please select how satisfied you were with your cardiac care (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
*   **Would you recommend this facility for cardiac care?** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Please select if you would recommend this facility for cardiac care (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Unsure, Probably No, Definitely No).
*   **Suggestions for improvement or additional comments** (`improvement_comments`, text, optional): Please use this field to provide any suggestions or comments about your experience.
