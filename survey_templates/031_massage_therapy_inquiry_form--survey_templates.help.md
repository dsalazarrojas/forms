# Massage Therapy Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Therapy Inquiry Form is used to gather information from clients about their massage needs and preferences. It helps the business to provide a tailored experience for each client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information, such as Client Name, Client Age, Client Gender, and Preferred Massage Area.
2. Choose your preferred massage style and frequency from the options provided.
3. Select your preferred massage time and duration.
4. If you have any special requests, please mention them in the Special Requests field.
5. Finally, sign the Client Signature and Therapist Signature fields to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Preferred Massage Area** (`preferred_massage_area`, select_one, required): Choose the area of your body you would like to receive massage on. Options are:
	+ Abdomen
	+ Back
	+ Feet
	+ Head and neck
	+ Neck
* **Preferred Massage Style** (`preferred_massage_style`, select_multiple, required): Choose the massage style that suits your needs. Options are:
	+ Deep tissue
	+ Hot stone
	+ Massage oil
	+ Sports
	+ Swedish
* **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`, date, optional): If you would like to schedule an appointment on a specific date, please enter it here.
* **Preferred Massage Time** (`preferred_massage_time`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred massage time. Options are:
	+ 60
	+ 90
	+ 120
* **Massage Frequency** (`massage_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you would like to receive massages. Options are:
	+ One time
	+ Regular
	+ Sporadic
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests, such as allergies or preferences, please mention them here.
* **Therapist Notes** (`therapist_notes`, note, required): Please provide any information that may help the therapist tailor your massage experience.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, optional): Sign this field to confirm your submission.
* **Therapist Signature** (`therapist_signature`, text, required): Sign this field to confirm that you have reviewed the client's information.
* **Massage Therapist** (`massage_therapist`, select_one, required): Please confirm whether you will be receiving a massage from a therapist or not.
* **Massage Duration** (`massage_duration`, select_multiple, required): Choose the duration of your massage. Options are:
	+ 30
	+ 60
	+ 90
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your massage.
	+ Bank transfer
	+ Card
	+ Other
* **Special Requests 2** (`special_request_2`, text, optional): If you have any additional special requests, please mention them here.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information, please mention it here.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please mention them here.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for your appointment, please enter it here.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, select_one, optional): If you have a specific time in mind for your appointment, please choose it here.
* **Massage Type** (`massage_type`, select_multiple, optional): If you have a specific massage type in mind, please choose it here.
* **Client Age** (`client_age`, number, optional): If you would like to share your age, please enter it here.
* **Client Gender** (`client_gender`, select_one, required): Choose your gender.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please mention them here.
* **Massage Area 2** (`massage_area_2`, select_one, required): Choose the second area of your body you would like to receive massage on. Options are:
	+ Abdomen
	+ Arms
	+ Back
	+ Chest
	+ Face
	+ Feet
	+ Head and neck
	+ Hips
	+ Knees
	+ Lower back
	+ Legs
	+ Neck
	+ Scalp
	+ Shoulders
	+ Upper back
* **Massage Style 2** (`massage_style_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose the second massage style you would like to receive. Options are:
	+ Deep tissue
	+ Hot stone
	+ Massage oil
	+ Sports
	+ Swedish
