# Public Service Announcement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about public service announcements. Your input will help us understand what you recall from these announcements and how well we convey the message.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the answers to the questions. You can answer as many or as few questions as you like, and you don't have to answer any questions that you're not comfortable answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Recalls** (`survey_recalls`, Select Multiple, Required: False): This question asks whether you recall any specific information from the public service announcement. Please select all that apply from the options:
	+ Yes: You do recall something specific
	+ No: You don't recall anything specific
* **Message Quality** (`message_quality`, Select One, Required: False): This question asks how would you rate the quality of the message in the public service announcement.
	+ Good: The message was clear and well-communicated
	+ Poor: The message was not clear or was confusing
* **Intended Actions** (`intended_actions`, Select Multiple, Required: False): This question asks what actions you are likely to take as a result of hearing or seeing the public service announcement.
	+ Yes: You would take one or more of the actions mentioned
* **Date of Message** (`date_of_message`, Date, Required: False): This question asks for the date you heard or saw the public service announcement.
* **Time of Message** (`time_of_message`, Time, Required: False): This question asks for the time you heard or saw the public service announcement.
* **Recall Score** (`recall_score`, Number, Required: False): This question asks you to rate how well you recall the message from the public service announcement.
* **Message Quality Score** (`message_quality_score`, Number, Required: False): This question asks you to rate how well you think the message was communicated in the public service announcement.
* **Intended Actions Score** (`intended_actions_score`, Number, Required: False): This question asks you to rate how likely you are to take the actions mentioned in the public service announcement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Note, Required: False): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about the public service announcement.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text, Required: False): This field is for you to provide any contact information if you would like to be contacted for more information or follow-up questions.
