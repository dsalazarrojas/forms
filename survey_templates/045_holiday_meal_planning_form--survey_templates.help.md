# Holiday Meal Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Meal Planning Form is designed to collect information from users to plan a holiday meal that caters to everyone's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your preferred holiday meal type from the options provided on the first page.
2. Choose any dietary restrictions you have on the second page.
3. Enter the number of guests you are planning to invite on the third page.
4. Add any additional comments or questions you may have on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your preferred holiday meal?** (`main_page`, `text`, `optional`): This is the first page of the form where you will select your preferred holiday meal type. Please choose one of the options provided.
* **What kind of food do you prefer?** (`meal_preferences`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): This page allows you to select multiple types of food that you prefer. Please choose one or more of the options provided.
* **Do you have any dietary restrictions?** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): This page is for you to select any dietary restrictions you have, such as Gluten-Free, Lactose-Free, Nut-Free, Vegetarian, or Vegan.
* **How many guests are you planning to invite?** (`guest_counts`, `number`, `optional`): This page is where you will enter the number of guests you are planning to invite. Please enter a whole number.
* **Additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, `note`, `optional`): This is the last page where you can add any additional comments or questions you may have about the meal planning.
