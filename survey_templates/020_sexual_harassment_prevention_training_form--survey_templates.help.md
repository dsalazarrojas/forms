# Sexual Harassment Prevention Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your sexual harassment prevention training session. It will help us tailor the training to meet your needs and ensure a safe and respectful environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

*   Fill out the form completely.
*   Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the training session.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you with more information about the training session.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you have a preferred phone number to be reached at, please enter it here.
*   **Training Session** (`training_session`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    +   Choose the duration of your desired training session: 30 minutes, 60 minutes, or 90 minutes.
*   **Session Date and Time** (`session_date_time`, `time`, required: false):
    +   Select a specific date and time for your training session.
*   **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required: false):
    +   Enter a date for your training session.
*   **Training Location** (`training_location`, `text`, required: false): If you have a preferred location for the training, please enter it here.
*   **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, required: false):
    +   Select a specific time for your training session.
*   **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `text`, required: false): Enter a description of your desired training session duration.
*   **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, `time`, required: false):
    +   Select a specific start time for your training session.
*   **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, `time`, required: false):
    +   Select a specific end time for your training session.
*   **Session Location** (`session_location`, `text`, required: false): If you have a preferred location for the training, please enter it here.
*   **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional information or notes about your training session.
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your supervisor or the person you are requesting the training for.
*   **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of your supervisor or the person you are requesting the training for.
*   **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of your supervisor or the person you are requesting the training for.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate information will help us tailor the training to your needs and ensure a smooth process. Please review the form before submitting it to ensure you haven't missed any required information.
