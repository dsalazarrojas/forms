# Workplace Mentorship Initiative Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluate the effectiveness of the Workplace Mentorship Initiative. The initiative's goals, duration, and overall satisfaction are all relevant factors. This form is intended for mentors and mentees to provide their honest feedback, and it's a crucial part of the program's development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Mentorship Program Name` field with the name of the mentorship program you are evaluating.
2. Enter the duration of the program in the `Program Duration` field. This should be a numerical value representing the length of time the program has been active.
3. Describe the goals of the program in the `Program Goals` field. This is an opportunity to provide more detail about what the program aims to achieve.
4. Rate the effectiveness of the initiatives implemented by the program using the `Initiatives Effectiveness Rating` field. Use the numerical value to indicate how well the initiatives have performed.
5. Provide your overall satisfaction level with the program in the `Overall Satisfaction` field. Select one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
6. If you have any feedback about the program, type it in the `Feedback` field.
7. Enter your email address in the `Email` field, this will help us reach back to you for further communication.
8. Fill in your contact number in the `Contact Number` field, this will help us contact you in case we need more information.
9. Use the `Comments` field to provide any additional comments or remarks about the program.
10. Finally, type in your name in the `Submitted By` field so we know who submitted the feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mentorship Program Name** (mentorship_program_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the mentorship program you are evaluating.
* **Program Duration** (mentorship_program_duration, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the program, which should be a numerical value representing the length of time the program has been active.
* **Program Goals** (mentorship_program_goals, text, required: false): Describe the goals of the program, which is an opportunity to provide more detail about what the program aims to achieve.
* **Initiatives Effectiveness Rating** (mentorship_initiatives_effectiveness_rating, number, required: false): Rate the effectiveness of the initiatives implemented by the program using the numerical value to indicate how well the initiatives have performed.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (overall_satisfaction, select_one, required: false): Provide your overall satisfaction level with the program, select one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feedback** (feedback, text, required: false): If you have any feedback about the program, type it in this field.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter your email address, this will help us reach back to you for further communication.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, required: false): Fill in your contact number, this will help us contact you in case we need more information.
* **Comments** (comments, note, required: false): Use this field to provide any additional comments or remarks about the program.
* **Submitted By** (submitted_by, text, required: false): Type in your name so we know who submitted the feedback.

Note: The fields marked as required: false are optional, but it's still recommended to fill them in for more detailed feedback and for us to contact you.
