# Program Participation Summary - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Program Participation Summary" form is a survey that gathers information about a participant's engagement and experience with a program. It helps us understand how the participant perceives the program and their own feedback. This information is essential for internal use to monitor and improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that you have only one entry for each program.
2. Fill out the form for a specific participant and program.
3. Provide accurate information about your experience with the program.
4. Choose the program outcomes that apply to your experience.
5. Leave feedback and comments to help us improve.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Page 1)**: Enter a brief description about the participant.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant. This is the name of the person who completed the program.
* **Program Outcomes**: Select all the outcomes that apply to your experience with the program.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the program outcomes.
* **Participant Feedback**: Choose how you feel about the program.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments about your feedback.
* **Participant Outcome Score**: Enter a score between 0 and 100 to express your satisfaction with the program outcomes.
* **Outcome Scale**: Choose the scale that best represents your outcome score.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments about your outcome score.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to save your changes.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date you completed the program.
* **Time Completed**: Enter the time you completed the program.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number (optional).
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email (optional).

Note: The fields with asterisk (*) are optional. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.

Tips:

* Please ensure that you have only one entry for each program.
* Be honest about your feedback and comments.
* The form's data will be used internally to monitor and improve the program.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please let us know.
