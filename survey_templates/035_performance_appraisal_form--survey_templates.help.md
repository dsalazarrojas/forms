# Performance Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess and improve job performance by gathering feedback from employees. It covers various aspects of your role, including your current job title, self-rated performance, major accomplishments in the past quarter, and goals for the next quarter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your current job title in the field "What is your current job title?".
2.  Rate your performance in the field "How do you rate your performance in this role?" using the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
3.  List your major accomplishments in the past quarter in the field "List your major accomplishments in the past quarter".
4.  Select your top 3 goals for the next quarter in the field "What are your top 3 goals for the next quarter?".
5.  In the field "How do you plan to achieve these goals?", provide a clear plan of action.
6.  List the tools you use for your role in the field "What tools do you use for this role?".
7.  Identify the tools assigned to you in the field "Which tools have been assigned to you?".
8.  Provide feedback for your manager in the field "What feedback do you have for your manager?".
9.  Suggest areas for improvement in your role in the field "What areas for improvement do you suggest for your role?".
10. In the field "What is the expected output format for the appraisal?", select the format that best suits your needs.
11. Enter the name of the output file in the field "What is the name of the output file?".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `text`, not required): A brief introduction to your performance appraisal.
- **What is your current job title?** (`current_job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title.
- **How do you rate your performance in this role?** (`self_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your performance on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
- **List your major accomplishments in the past quarter** (`quarterly_accomplishments`, `select_multiple`, required): Describe your significant accomplishments in the past quarter.
- **What are your top 3 goals for the next quarter?** (`quarterly_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Identify your top 3 goals for the next quarter.
- **How do you plan to achieve these goals?** (`goal_strategy`, `text`, required): Provide a clear plan of action for achieving your goals.
- **What tools do you use for this role?** (`used_tools`, `select_multiple`, required): List the tools you commonly use for your job.
- **Which tools have been assigned to you?** (`assigned_tools`, `select_multiple`, required): Identify any tools assigned to you by management or team leaders.
- **What feedback do you have for your manager?** (`manager_feedback`, `text`, required): Offer feedback for your manager's improvement.
- **What areas for improvement do you suggest for your role?** (`role_improvement`, `select_multiple`, required): Suggest areas that could be improved for your role.
- **What is the expected output format for the appraisal?** (`output_format`, `select_one`, required): Choose the format in which you'd like to receive your appraisal.
- **What is the name of the output file?** (`output_file_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the output file.

Note: This guide is a general template, and specific fields might be skipped or modified based on your organization's needs and policies. Always refer to your supervisor or HR representative for any clarification or further assistance.
