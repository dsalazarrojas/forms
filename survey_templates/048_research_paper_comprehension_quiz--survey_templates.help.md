# research_paper_comprehension_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "research_paper_comprehension_quiz" form is a template used by instructors to create a quiz on research paper comprehension. This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Upload a research paper**: Click on the "Research Paper File Upload" field to upload a research paper related to the quiz.
2. Fill in the required fields: Fill in the fields marked as "required" such as "Research Paper Title", "Instructor Name", "Submission Status", "Quiz Completion Status", and "Date Submitted", and "Time Submitted".
3. Provide feedback: Fill in the fields for "Student Answer", "Instructor Response", "Instructor Comments", "Research Paper Title", and "Submission Status" as applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Paper Comprehension Quiz Questions**: This is the main field where you will upload the research paper related to the quiz. Please make sure to follow the submission guidelines.
* **question_number**: This field is used to keep track of the question number. Please enter the question number corresponding to the uploaded research paper.
* **Student Answer**: This field is for students to submit their answers to the quiz questions. Please enter their answer in this field.
* **Instructor Response**: This field is for instructors to provide feedback on student answers. Please enter your response in this field.
* **Quiz Completion Status**: This field is used to track whether the quiz has been completed or not. Please select whether the quiz has been completed or not.
* **Date Submitted**: This field is for tracking the date of submission. Please enter the date you submitted the quiz.
* **Time Submitted**: This field is for tracking the time of submission. Please enter the time you submitted the quiz.
* **Research Paper Title**: This field is for entering the title of the research paper. Please enter the title of the research paper.
* **Instructor Comments**: This field is for instructors to provide comments on student submissions. Please enter your comments in this field.
* **Submission Status**: This field is used to track the submission status of the quiz. Please select whether the submission has been accepted or rejected.
* **Research Paper File Upload**: This field is for uploading the research paper file.
* **Instructor Name**: This field is for entering your name as the instructor. Please enter your name in this field.
* **Submission Status**: This field is used to track the submission status of the quiz. Please select whether the submission has been accepted or rejected.
* **Instructor Name**: This field is for entering your name as the instructor. Please enter your name in this field.
* **Date Submitted**: This field is for tracking the date of submission. Please enter the date you submitted the quiz.
* **Time Submitted**: This field is for tracking the time of submission. Please enter the time you submitted the quiz.

Note: All fields with "required" attribute are marked as required in the form.
