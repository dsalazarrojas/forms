# Beck Depression Inventory Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your mental health and help identify potential signs of depression. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, and it's essential to consult a mental health professional if you're struggling with your mental health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, selecting the response that best describes your feelings and experiences over the past two weeks.
2. Please select one answer choice for each question.
3. Make sure to read each question carefully and choose the response that best fits your current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Initials** (`patient_initials`, `text`, required): Enter your initials for identification purposes only.
* **Date of Assessment** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required): Please select the date you're completing this form.
* **Clinical Disclaimer** (`section_clinical_disclaimer`, `note`, optional): This section is for informational purposes only and should not be taken as medical advice. Please read carefully before proceeding.
* **Mood and Outlook** (`section_mood_assessment`, `note`, optional): This section is a general introduction to the assessment, explaining that the questions are designed to help identify potential signs of depression.
* **Sadness** (`sadness_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of sadness you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I do not feel sad
	+ 1 - I feel sad much of the time
	+ 2 - I am sad all the time and I can't snap out of it
	+ 3 - I am so sad or unhappy that I can't stand it
* **Pessimism** (`pessimism_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of pessimism you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I am not discouraged about my future
	+ 1 - I feel more discouraged about my future than I used to be
	+ 2 - I do not expect things to work out for me
	+ 3 - I feel my future is hopeless and will only get worse
* **Past Failure** (`past_failure_perception`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of failure you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I do not feel like a failure
	+ 1 - I feel I have failed more than I should have
	+ 2 - As I look back, I see a lot of failures
	+ 3 - I feel I am a total failure as a person
* **Loss of Pleasure** (`pleasure_loss`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of pleasure you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I get as much pleasure as I ever did from the things I enjoy
	+ 1 - I don't enjoy things as much as I used to
	+ 2 - I get very little pleasure from the things I used to enjoy
	+ 3 - I can't get any pleasure from the things I used to enjoy
* **Guilty Feelings** (`guilty_feelings`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of guilt you feel over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I don't feel particularly guilty
	+ 1 - I feel guilty over many things I have done or should have done
	+ 2 - I feel quite guilty most of the time
	+ 3 - I feel guilty all the time
* **Punishment Feelings** (`punishment_feelings`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of punishment you feel over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I don't feel I am being punished
	+ 1 - I feel I may be punished
	+ 2 - I expect to be punished
	+ 3 - I feel I am being punished
* **Self-Dislike** (`self_dislike`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of self-dislike you feel over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I feel the same about myself as ever
	+ 1 - I have lost confidence in myself
	+ 2 - I am disappointed in myself
	+ 3 - I dislike myself
* **Self-Criticalness** (`self_criticalness`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of self-criticism you experience over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I don't criticize or blame myself more than usual
	+ 1 - I am more critical of myself than I used to be
	+ 2 - I criticize myself for all of my faults
	+ 3 - I blame myself for everything bad that happens
* **Suicidal Thoughts or Wishes** (`suicidal_thoughts`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of suicidal thoughts or wishes you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I don't have any thoughts of killing myself
	+ 1 - I have thoughts of killing myself, but I would not carry them out
	+ 2 - I would like to kill myself
	+ 3 - I would kill myself if I had the chance
* **Crying** (`crying_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select the frequency of crying you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I don't cry any more than I used to
	+ 1 - I cry more than I used to
	+ 2 - I cry over every little thing
	+ 3 - I feel like crying, but I can't
* **Agitation** (`agitation_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of agitation you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I am no more restless or wound up than usual
	+ 1 - I feel more restless or wound up than usual
	+ 2 - I am so restless or agitated that it's hard to stay still
	+ 3 - I am so restless or agitated that I have to keep moving or doing something
* **Loss of Interest** (`interest_loss`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of interest you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I have not lost interest in other people or activities
	+ 1 - I am less interested in other people or activities than I used to be
	+ 2 - I have lost most of my interest in other people or activities
	+ 3 - It's hard to get interested in anything
* **Indecisiveness** (`indecisiveness_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of indecisiveness you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I make decisions about as well as I ever could
	+ 1 - I find it more difficult to make decisions than I used to
	+ 2 - I have much greater difficulty in making decisions than I used to
	+ 3 - I have trouble making any decisions
* **Worthlessness** (`worthlessness_perception`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of worthlessness you feel over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I do not feel worthless
	+ 1 - I don't consider myself as worthwhile and useful as I used to
	+ 2 - I feel more worthless as compared to other people
	+ 3 - I feel utterly worthless
* **Loss of Energy** (`energy_loss`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of energy loss you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I have as much energy as ever
	+ 1 - I have less energy than I used to have
	+ 2 - I don't have enough energy to do very much
	+ 3 - I don't have enough energy to do anything
* **Changes in Sleeping Pattern** (`sleep_pattern_changes`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of change in your sleeping pattern over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I have not experienced any change in my sleeping pattern
	+ 1 - I sleep somewhat more than usual OR somewhat less than usual
	+ 2 - I sleep a lot more than usual OR a lot less than usual
	+ 3 - I sleep most of the day OR I wake up 1-2 hours early and can't get back to sleep
* **Irritability** (`irritability_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of irritability you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I am no more irritable than usual
	+ 1 - I am more irritable than usual
	+ 2 - I am much more irritable than usual
	+ 3 - I am irritable all the time
* **Appetite Changes** (`appetite_changes`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of change in your appetite over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I have not experienced any change in my appetite
	+ 1 - My appetite is somewhat less than usual OR somewhat greater than usual
	+ 2 - My appetite is much less than before OR much greater than usual
	+ 3 - I have no appetite at all OR I crave food all the time
* **Concentration Difficulty** (`concentration_difficulty`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of concentration difficulty you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I can concentrate as well as ever
	+ 1 - I can't concentrate as well as usual
	+ 2 - It's hard to keep my mind on anything for very long
	+ 3 - I find I can't concentrate on anything
* **Fatigue or Tiredness** (`fatigue_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of fatigue or tiredness you've experienced over the past two weeks:
	+ 0 - I am no more tired or fatigued than usual
	+ 1 - I get tired or fatigued more easily than usual
	+ 2 - I am too tired or fatigued to do a lot of the things I used to do
	+ 3 - I am too tired or fatigued to do most of the things I used to do

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly, and select the response that best fits your current situation.
* If you're unsure about any question, please don't hesitate to contact the administrator or a mental health professional for guidance.
* This form is not a substitute for professional medical advice, and it's essential to consult a mental health professional if you're struggling with your mental health.
