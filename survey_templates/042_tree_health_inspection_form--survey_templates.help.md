# Tree Health Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tree Health Inspection Form is designed to assess the overall health and condition of a tree. It is used to gather information about tree details, status, damage, and other relevant attributes to inform decision-making and ensure the tree's well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are familiar with the questions and options provided.
2. Select the relevant fields based on the specific needs and requirements of your organization or project.
3. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible for each field.
4. Save and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
This is a brief introduction to the form, which should be filled out by the inspector.

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the form and its purpose. This field is a note field and is intended to provide a general description of the form and its use.

### Tree Details
* **tree_details**: Enter a brief description of the tree being inspected, including its species, location, and any relevant features.

### Tree Status
* **tree_status**: Select the status of the tree (Active/Inactive).

### Root System
* **root_system**: Assess the health of the tree's root system and select one of the four options provided.

### Condition
* **tree_condition**: Assess the overall condition of the tree and select one of the two options provided.

### Damage
* **tree_damage**: List any damage or issues with the tree, selecting all that apply.

### Inspection Location
* **inspection_location**: Enter the location of the inspection, including any relevant coordinates or GPS points.

### Notes
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the tree or inspection.

### Tree Species
* **tree_species**: Select whether the tree species is identified or not.

### Tree Height
* **tree_height**: Enter the height of the tree in meters or feet.

### Tree Age
* **tree_age**: Enter the age of the tree.

### Tree Diameter
* **tree_diameter**: Enter the diameter of the tree in meters or feet.

### Tree Canopy
* **tree_canopy**: Enter a brief description of the tree's canopy, including its shape and features.

### Tree Condition Rating
* **tree_condition_rating**: Assess the overall condition of the tree and select one of the five options provided.

### Tree Canopy Rating
* **tree_canopy_rating**: Assess the shape and features of the tree's canopy and select one of the three options provided.

### Inspection Date
* **inspection_date**: Enter the date of the inspection.

### Inspector Name
* **inspector_name**: Enter the name of the inspector who completed the form.

### Notes
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the tree or inspection.

### Photos
* **photos**: Upload any photos of the tree or inspection area, if applicable.

### Video
* **video**: Provide a link to any video recordings of the tree or inspection area, if applicable.

### Tree Location
* **tree_location**: Enter the location of the tree, including any relevant coordinates or GPS points.

### Tree Notes
* **tree_notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the tree or inspection.

### Inspection Comments
* **tree_inspection_comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the inspection or tree condition.

### Tree Inspector
* **tree_inspector**: Enter the name of the inspector who completed the form.

### Inspection Status
* **tree_inspection_status**: Select the status of the inspection (Active/Inactive).

### Inspection Status Date
* **tree_inspection_status_date**: Enter the date of the inspection status.

### Inspection Status Time
* **tree_inspection_status_time**: Enter the time of the inspection status.

## Tips
* Ensure that the form is completed thoroughly and accurately, as this information will inform decision-making and tree management.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
* Use clear and concise language when entering notes or comments.
* Use the form as a guide for future inspections to track changes and monitor tree health over time.
