# share_your_recovery_journey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your recovery journey. It's meant to help us understand your experiences and challenges during your recovery process. Please answer the questions honestly, and feel free to provide as much detail as you need to share your story.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through each page and question. Make sure you understand what's being asked before answering.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability. If you're unsure about a question or feel it's not applicable, please leave it blank.
3. Use the provided options for select_multiple or select_one questions to choose the best answer(s) for you.
4. For text fields like "recovery_story" and "comments", feel free to write as much or as little as you need.
5. Once you've completed all questions, review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
