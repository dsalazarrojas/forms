# Staff Acknowledgement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to ensure that staff members are aware of and agree to comply with the company's policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each field carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. For fields that ask for multiple selections, choose all that apply.
3. For the acknowledgement date and time fields, enter the date and time you are completing this form.
4. Use the text fields to provide any additional information or feedback you may have.
5. Finally, review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Purpose** (`survey_purpose`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your understanding of the company's policies and procedures.
* **Staff Policies** (`staff_policies`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the policies that you are aware of or agree to comply with. This field is multiple-select, so you can choose one or more policies.
* **Acknowledgement Date** (`acknowledgement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are completing this form.
* **Acknowledgement Time** (`acknowledgement_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are completing this form.
* **Acknowledgement Notes** (`acknowledgement_notes`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional information or feedback you may have.
* **Staff Feedback** (`staff_feedback`, text, optional): Use this field to provide any feedback or suggestions you may have.
* **Staff Suggestions** (`staff_suggestions`, text, optional): Use this field to provide any suggestions you may have.
* **Staff Contact Info** (`staff_contact_info`, text, optional): Use this field to provide any contact information you may have, such as your email address or phone number.
* **Supervisor Acknowledgement** (`supervisor_acknowledgement`, select_one, optional): Select the level of acknowledgement you wish to provide, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete this form carefully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* This form is intended to be a one-time acknowledgement, so you should not need to complete it again unless your supervisor or HR representative requests it.
