# Nonprofit Communication Efficiency Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help nonprofit organizations assess the efficiency of their internal and external communication processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them truthfully.
2. Select all that apply for the first question regarding survey templates.
3. Answer each question in as much detail as possible.
4. Submit the form when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Templates Questionnaire** (survey_templates_questionnaire, select_multiple, required)
	+ Answer 'Yes' if your organization uses survey templates.
	+ Answer 'No' if your organization does not use survey templates.
* **Nonprofit Communication Efficiency Questionnaire** (nonprofit_communication_efficiency_questionnaire, text, required)
	+ This question is asking about your thoughts on the effectiveness of your organization's communication processes.
	+ Please describe in as much detail as possible what you think makes your communication processes efficient or inefficient.
* **Gather feedback on internal and external communication** (gather_feedback, text, required)
	+ This question is asking about what methods your organization uses to collect feedback from stakeholders on internal and external communication.
	+ Please describe these methods and any challenges you face in collecting feedback.
* **Making data collection and form submission simple** (make_data_collection_and_submission_simple, text, required)
	+ This question is asking about your organization's data collection and form submission processes.
	+ Please describe how your organization collects and submits data, and any challenges or difficulties you face.
* **Making data collection and form submission simple for teams using Jotform Form Templates** (teams_using_jotform_form_templates, text, required)
	+ This question is asking about your experience using Jotform Form Templates.
	+ Please describe any benefits or challenges you have faced while using these templates.
