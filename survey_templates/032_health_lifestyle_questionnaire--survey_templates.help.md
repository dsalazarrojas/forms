# Health And Lifestyle Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the completion of the Health And Lifestyle Questionnaire. Please read through each step carefully to ensure you fill out the form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the personal details sections, including First name, Last name, Email, and Phone number.
2. Provide your birth date and age.
3. Indicate your gender.
4. Report any medical conditions you have.
5. List your current medications and allergies.
6. Provide the details of your emergency contact.
7. If applicable, provide the name and contact information of your doctor.
8. Answer questions about your medical record and medical transport needs.
9. Select your emergency services and medical transport preferences.
10. Answer questions about your blood donation status and medical coverage plan.
11. Report your health insurance information and medical group affiliations.
12. Provide details about your health plan and emergency coverage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First name (`first_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last name (`last_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email (`email`, `email`, required: false)
Enter your personal email address.

### Phone number (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your phone number for emergency contact.

### Birth date (`birth_date`, `date`, required: false)
Enter your birth date in the format `month/day/year`.

### Age (`age`, `number`, required: false)
Enter your age.

### Gender (`gender`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select your gender from the options: Male, Female.

### Occupation (`occupation`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your current occupation or job title.

### Medical conditions (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false)
List any medical conditions you have.

### Medications (`medications`, `text`, required: false)
List any medications you are currently taking.

### Allergies (`allergies`, `text`, required: false)
List any allergies you have.

### Emergency contact (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false)
Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.

### Doctor name (`doctor_name`, `text`, required: false)
If applicable, provide the name of your doctor.

### Doctor contact (`doctor_contact`, `text`, required: false)
If applicable, provide the contact information of your doctor.

### Medical record (`medical_record`, `text`, required: false)
Indicate whether you have a medical record.

### Emergency services (`emergency_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select whether you have emergency medical services.

### Medical transport (`medical_transport`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select whether you have medical transport needs.

### Blood donation (`blood_donation`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Indicate whether you are able to donate blood.

### Medical coverage (`medical_coverage`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select who provides your medical coverage: Self, Spouse/partner, Parent/Child, Family member.

### Health insurance (`health_insurance`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your health insurance information.

### Medical group (`medical_group`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your medical group affiliation.

### Health plan (`health_plan`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your health plan details.

### Medical coverage plan (`medical_coverage_plan`, `select_one`, required: false)
Indicate whether you have a medical coverage plan.

### Emergency coverage (`emergency_coverage`, `select_one`, required: false)
Indicate whether you have emergency coverage.

### Health savings plan (`health_savings_plan`, `select_one`, required: false)
Indicate whether you have a health savings plan.
