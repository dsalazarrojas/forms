# SILO Ownership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about SILO (Storage In the Loop) ownership data for internal use.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field based on the provided choices.
2. Fill out each field with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Save the form after completion for future reference.
