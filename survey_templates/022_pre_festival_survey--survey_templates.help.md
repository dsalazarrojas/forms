# pre_festival_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your experiences, habits, and health status in relation to COVID-19. Please be honest when answering the questions to help us understand your circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each question and select the answer that best describes your situation. Make sure to answer all required questions and be accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Festival Details** (`intro`, `note`, not required)
This field is just a note for festival details, but it's not required to be filled out.

### 2. **How Many Festivals Attended In Last 2 Years** (`festival_frequency`, `number`, not required)
Please answer how many festivals you have attended in the last 2 years.

### 3. **How Do You Feel In The Last 24 Hours** (`feeling_temperature_checklist`, `select_multiple`, not required)

* `Yes`
* `No`
