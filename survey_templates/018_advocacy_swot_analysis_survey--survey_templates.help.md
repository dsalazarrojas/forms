# Advocacy SWOT Analysis Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help your advocacy group or organization identify and analyze its internal strengths and weaknesses, as well as external opportunities and threats. The goal is to derive a top strategic priority for your organization based on this analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the organization's name and primary advocacy focus.
2. List your organization's key internal strengths, their impact, and key internal weaknesses, their severity.
3. Identify and list key external opportunities and threats.
4. Based on your analysis, select your top strategic priority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Primary Advocacy Focus** (`advocacy_focus`, text, required): Identify your organization's main area of focus (e.g., Environment, Human Rights, Education).
* **List Key Strengths** (`key_strengths`, text, required): List your organization's internal strengths (e.g., resources, capabilities, reputation).
* **Impact of Strengths** (`strength_impact`, select_one, optional): Select the impact of your internal strengths (High, Moderate, Low).
* **List Key Weaknesses** (`key_weaknesses`, text, required): List your organization's internal weaknesses (e.g., gaps in resources, skills, reach).
* **Severity of Weaknesses** (`weakness_severity`, select_one, optional): Select the severity of your internal weaknesses (Critical, Major, Minor).
* **List Key Opportunities** (`key_opportunities`, text, required): List key external opportunities that can benefit your organization (e.g., policy changes, funding trends, partnerships).
* **List Key Threats** (`key_threats`, text, required): List key external threats to your organization (e.g., opposition, funding cuts, regulatory changes).
* **Top Strategic Priority** (`strategic_priorities`, text, required): Based on your analysis, select your top strategic priority.
