# Ethnographic Research Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethnographic Research Survey Template is a form designed to collect information about ethnographic research surveys. It is meant to be used when conducting research that involves observing and recording human behavior in its natural setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant research method from the options provided (Survey, Ethnographic, or Observational).
2. Describe the participant's behaviors, activities, and acts in the text fields.
3. If necessary, specify the research form's accessibility (PC, smartphones, or tablets).
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the notes field.
5. Enter the participant's email address if you want to contact them for further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Template** (`research_template`, select_multiple): Choose how you want to conduct your research (Yes, No).
* **Ethnographic Research Method** (`ethnographic_research_method`, select_one): Select the method you will use for your research (Survey, Ethnographic, or Observational).
* **Participant Behaviors** (`participant_behaviors`, text): Describe the participant's behaviors, such as what they do, how they interact with others, or any notable actions.
* **Participant Activities** (`participant_activities`, text): Describe the participant's activities, such as what they are doing, how they are engaging with their surroundings, or any notable events.
* **Participant Acts** (`participant_acts`, text): Describe any notable actions taken by the participant, such as gestures, expressions, or interactions.
* **Research Form Accessibility** (`research_form_accessibility`, select_one): Specify the device(s) on which the participant completed the research form (PC, smartphones, or tablets).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional comments or notes about the participant's behavior or activity.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email): Enter the participant's email address if you need to contact them for further information.

## Tips

* Be as specific and detailed as possible when describing participant behaviors, activities, and acts.
* Use clear and concise language when entering the participant's email address.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the research guidelines or consult with a researcher for guidance.
