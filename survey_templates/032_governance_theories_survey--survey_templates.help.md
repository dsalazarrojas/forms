# Governance Theories Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Governance Theories Survey is a form designed to collect information about governance theories and their implementation status within an organization. This form helps stakeholders assess the frequency and status of governance theories and practices, providing valuable insights for informed decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Add any additional note or information in the "note" field.
5. Indicate the name of the governance theory in the "theory_name" field.
6. Select the types of practices related to the theory in the "practice_type" field. You can choose one or multiple options.
7. If you selected "Other" in the "practice_type" field, provide more details in the "practice_type_other" field.
8. Describe the practice in the "practice_description" field.
9. Evaluate your familiarity with the theory in the "theory_familiarity" field.
10. Assess the importance of the theory in the "theory_importance" field.
11. Evaluate the impact of the theory in the "theory_impact" field.
12. Assess the impact of the practice in the "practice_impact" field.
13. Indicate your level of familiarity with the practice in the "practice_familiarity" field.
14. Evaluate the importance of the practice in the "practice_importance" field.
15. Assess the impact of the practice in the "practice_impact" field.
16. Select the level at which the practice is implemented in the organization in the "organization_level" field. You can choose one or multiple options.
17. If you selected "Other" in the "organization_level" field, provide more details in the "organization_level_other" field.
18. Indicate the level at which the theory is implemented in the organization in the "theory_level" field. You can choose one or multiple options.
19. If you selected "Other" in the "theory_level" field, provide more details in the "theory_level_other" field.
20. Select the frequency of the practice in the "frequency" field.
21. Evaluate the implementation status of the practice in the "implementation_status" field.
22. Evaluate the status of the theory in the "theory_status" field.
23. Evaluate the status of the practice in the "practice_status" field.
24. Select the submission status of the form in the "submission_status" field.
