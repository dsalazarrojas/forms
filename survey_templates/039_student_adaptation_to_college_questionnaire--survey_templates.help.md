# Student Adaptation To College Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information about students to help institutions understand their adaptation to college.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question according to your personal information.
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter your name in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Program**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Not Sure
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Grade Level**: Select all that apply:
	+ Sophomore
	+ Junior
	+ Senior
	+ Freshman
	+ Other
* **Academic History**: Describe your academic background, including any previous education or degrees earned.
* **Student Citizenship**: Select one of the following options:
	+ US Citizen
	+ Non-US Citizen
	+ US Resident
	+ Other
* **Language Proficiency**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Native
	+ Fluent
	+ Basic
	+ None
* **Student Residency**: Select one of the following options:
	+ US
	+ Non-US
	+ International
* **Age**: Enter your age in numeric form (e.g., 20).
* **Student Gender**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Other
* **Parent/Guardian Name**: Enter your parent's or guardian's name.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly to provide accurate information.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact the responsible party (e.g., college admin) for assistance.
