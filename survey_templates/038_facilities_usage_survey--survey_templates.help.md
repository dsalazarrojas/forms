# facilities_usage_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The facilities_usage_survey form is used to collect feedback from users about their experience with facilities. This information will help facility managers identify areas for improvement and optimize the facility usage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Facility Used** (facilty_used, text, required): Enter the name of the facility you used during your visit.
2. **Time Visited** (time_visited, time, optional): If you remember, please enter the time you visited the facility.
3. **Satisfaction Rating** (satisfaction_rating, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the facility's usage from 1-5, where 1 is not satisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
4. **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the facility.
5. **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
6. **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number, so we can contact you for further feedback or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Used**: This field is used to record which facility you visited. Please enter the name of the facility as it appears on your receipt or marketing material.
* **Time Visited**: If you're able to recall the time you visited the facility, please enter it. This will help us track any changes or trends over time.
* **Satisfaction Rating**: This rating will help us understand how satisfied you were with your experience at the facility. Please rate your satisfaction from 1 (not satisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Comments**: This field is optional, but please provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your experience. This will help us improve the facility and better serve our users.
* **Email**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or further questions about your feedback, please enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Similarly, if you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields (Facility Used and Satisfaction Rating).
* Try to be as specific as possible when filling out the Facility Used field.
* Remember that your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve the facility and provide better service to our users.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us via email or phone.
