# flavor_profile_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a customer's flavor preferences and dietary restrictions. It helps us understand their needs and preferences to provide a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the appropriate answers for each field based on your true preferences and restrictions. Make sure to answer all relevant questions and provide accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (select_one)**: Choose 'Yes' if you identify as a customer, 'No' if you don't.
* **What flavor do you like? (select_multiple)**: Select all the flavors that you enjoy. You can choose multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on Mac) while selecting multiple options.
* **Dietary Restrictions (text)**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please enter them here.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Notes (note)**: If you have any additional comments or requests, please include them here.
* **Restaurant Info (note)**: If you have any special requests or comments about the restaurant, please include them here.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly to ensure we get a clear understanding of your preferences and restrictions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
