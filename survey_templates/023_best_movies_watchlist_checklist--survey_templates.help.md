# Best Movies Watchlist Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from movie fans about their watchlist and preferences. It's intended to be a survey or quiz to gauge the audience's interest in various films and streaming services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Your Name" field (id: 2).
2. Choose your favorite movie genre from the "Your Favorite Movie Genre" field (id: 3).
3. For each of the four movie title fields (id: 5, 7, 11, and 14), type in the title of a film you're interested in watching. For example, "The Shawshank Redemption" or "Parasite".
4. If you're interested in any of the classic films listed, check the boxes next to their titles in the "Which of these classics have you already seen" field (id: 14). This is an optional section.
5. Select your preferred streaming platforms from the "Preferred Streaming Platforms" field (id: 17). Choose all platforms that apply.
6. Type in the email address you'd like to use for contact information in the "Your Email Address" field (id: 20).
7. Choose the date you're providing this information on from the "Date of Checklist" field (id: 21).
