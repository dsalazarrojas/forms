# School Partnership Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gauge your perceptions of our school partnership, helping us understand your views on various aspects of our collaboration. Your feedback will contribute to strengthening our partnership and improving our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the introduction page, which provides context about the survey.
2. Fill in your name and email address in the designated fields.
3. Rate your perceptions of our school partnership by selecting the corresponding options for each question.
4. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions in the "What would you like to tell us about our school partnership?" and "Do you have any additional suggestions for our school partnership?" fields.
5. If you have a contact person we can reach out to, please enter their name and email address in the "Is there a contact person we can reach out to?" field.
