# E Pal User Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a user experience survey designed to collect feedback about your interaction with the E Pal system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for "User Satisfaction" to express your level of satisfaction with the system.
2. Choose which features you have used in the "Feature Usage" section.
3. Provide any recommendations or comments in the "User Feedback" text field.
4. Enter your email address and phone number if you wish to be contacted for further follow-up.
5. Select a date and time of your most recent interaction with the E Pal system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Satisfaction
* **User Satisfaction** (`User Satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): This is a question about your overall satisfaction with the system. Select "Satisfied" if you are pleased with your experience, "Dissatisfied" if you are not.

### Feature Usage
* **Feature Usage** (`Feature Usage`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the features you have used from the list below:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Recommendations
* **Recommendations** (`Recommendations`, note, required: false): This is a text field for any suggestions or comments you may have about the system.

### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further follow-up.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further follow-up.

### Date
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required: false): Select the date of your most recent interaction with the E Pal system.

### Time
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required: false): Select the time of your most recent interaction with the E Pal system.

### User Feedback
* **User Feedback** (`User Feedback`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the E Pal system.
