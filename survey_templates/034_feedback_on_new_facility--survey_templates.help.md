# Feedback On New Facility - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Feedback On New Facility" form is a tool used to collect feedback from visitors to a new facility, aiming to gauge their overall satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form sections carefully and fill out the fields relevant to your experience.
* For fields that are not relevant, you can leave them blank.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and honest feedback to help us improve the facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Data Collection (1)** (`visitor_data_collection`, `text`, **Required: No**): This field is for general information about your visit. Please provide any details about your visit that you think would be helpful.
* **Satisfaction Level (2)** (`satisfaction_level`, `select_one`, **Required: No**): Rate your overall satisfaction level with the facility from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Identified Improvement (3)** (`identified_improvement`, `text`, **Required: No**): If you have any specific suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them here.
* **Recommendation (4)** (`recommendation`, `note`, **Required: No**): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have for the facility.
* **Visitor Type (5)** (`visitor_type`, `select_one`, **Required: No**): Choose the category that best describes your visit:
	+ New
	+ Returning
	+ Employee
	+ Guest
	+ Other
* **Visit Length (6)** (`visit_length`, `number`, **Required: No**): Enter the duration of your visit in minutes.
* **Visited With (7)** (`visited_with`, `select_multiple`, **Required: No**): Indicate who you visited with:
	+ Alone
	+ With a friend
	+ With family
	+ With colleagues
	+ With a guide
* **Visited At (8)** (`visited_at`, `time`, **Required: No**): Choose the time of your visit:
* **Email (9)** (`email`, `email`, **Required: No**): If you have an email address, please provide it if you'd like to receive any updates or follow-up questions.
* **Phone (10)** (`phone`, `text`, **Required: No**): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Visited Location (11)** (`visited_location`, `select_one`, **Required: No**): Choose the area of the facility you visited:
	+ Main Entrance
	+ Main Hall
	+ Side Entrance
	+ Side Hall
	+ Other
* **Visited Date (12)** (`visited_date`, `date`, **Required: No**): Enter the date of your visit in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Visited Time (13)** (`visited_time`, `time`, **Required: No**): Choose the time of your visit:
* **Visited Days (14)** (`visited_days`, `select_multiple`, **Required: No**): Select the days of the week you visited:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **Visited Hour (15)** (`visited_hour`, `select_multiple`, **Required: No**): Choose the hour of your visit:
	+ AM
	+ PM
* **Visited Minute (16)** (`visited_minute`, `select_multiple`, **Required: No**): Select the minute of your visit:
	+ 0
	+ 15
	+ 30
	+ 45
* **Visited Second (17)** (`visited_second`, `select_multiple`, **Required: No**): Choose the second of your visit:
	+ 0

	+ 20
	+ 25
	+ 30
	+ 35
	+ 40
	+ 45
	+ 50
	+ 55

	+ 0
