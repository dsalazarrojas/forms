# School District Calendar Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect proposals for changes to the school district calendar. Fill it out to suggest new schedule options or modifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the guidelines on the previous page to ensure this is a new proposal and not a duplicate.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* **Schedule Proposal**: Briefly describe your proposed schedule changes.
	* **Proposed Schedule Date**: Enter the date for the proposed schedule change.
	* **Schedule Type**: Choose the type of schedule change you propose (Option A, Option B, or Option C).
	* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
	* **Submit**: Confirm that you've filled out the form correctly and are ready to submit your proposal.
3. The following fields are optional:
	* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or details about your proposal.
	* **Phone**: This field is optional, but if you have a phone number, it can be helpful for further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Proposal** (`schedule_proposal`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your proposed schedule changes. This is the main body of your proposal.
* **Proposed Schedule Date** (`proposed_schedule_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the proposed schedule change.
* **Schedule Type** (`schedule_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of schedule change you propose (Option A, Option B, or Option C).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about your proposal.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Confirm that you've filled out the form correctly and are ready to submit your proposal.
