# Home Energy Audit Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Energy Audit Checklist Form is a tool to help you evaluate your home's energy usage and identify areas for improvement. It's a self-assessment exercise that will guide you through a series of questions to help you understand your energy consumption and potential opportunities for reducing energy waste.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the sequence of pages. Each page will present a new set of questions or prompts, and you should answer them according to your knowledge of your home and its energy usage. You can select one or multiple options from the provided lists or type in any additional information as prompted. Don't hesitate to skip any question that does not apply to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **home_energy_audit_checklist_form** (Page 1): This is an introductory page with no fields to fill out. It serves as a starting point for the assessment.
* **energy_use** (Page 2): Choose all the ways in which you use energy in your home. These include electricity, gas, and water. This helps us understand your current energy usage patterns.
	+ Option 1: Heating
	+ Option 2: Cooling
	+ Option 3: Water heating
* **energy_sources** (Page 3): Select the primary energy sources you use in your home. This could be electricity, gas, or other sources.
	+ Option 1: Electricity
	+ Option 2: Gas
	+ Option 3: Solar
* **energy_waste** (Page 4): Identify areas where energy is wasted in your home. This could be due to leaks, inefficient appliances, or poor insulation.
	+ Option 1: Leaks
	+ Option 2: Inefficient appliances
	+ Option 3: Poor insulation
* **potential_savings** (Page 5): Estimate the potential savings you could make by improving your energy usage. This can be money saved on bills or energy efficiency improvements.
* **home_improvements** (Page 6): Select potential improvements to your home's energy efficiency.
	+ Option 1: Insulation
	+ Option 2: Energy-efficient appliances
	+ Option 3: Solar panels
* **energy_affect** (Page 7): Consider how your energy usage affects your daily life and the environment.
	+ Option 1: Comfort and health
	+ Option 2: Environment
	+ Option 3: Budget
* **final_steps** (Page 8): Summarize your findings and note any additional actions you plan to take based on your assessment.
* **additional_comments** (Page 9): Enter any additional comments or observations not captured in the previous pages.
