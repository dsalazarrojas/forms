# Faith Based Study Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Faith Based Study Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from participants about their experiences in a study session. This feedback will help the organizers of the study sessions to better understand their effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Use the space provided to write any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, text, required): Please provide a brief summary of your thoughts and feelings about the session.
* **Session Evaluation** (`session_evaluation`, note, optional): Provide any specific feedback about the session's content, facilitation, or organization. 
* **What was the most memorable part of the session?** (`feedback_question_1`, text, required): What experience stood out to you the most during the session?
* **What do you think we could improve on in this session?** (`feedback_question_2`, text, required): Think about the session and suggest one or two areas that could be improved upon.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address so that we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Please enter the date of the session you attended.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Please enter the time of the session you attended.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): If you would like to provide the date of the session you attended.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): If you would like to provide the time of the session you attended.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, required): Where did you attend the session?
* **Session Leaders** (`session_leaders`, text, required): Who led the session?
* **Session Topics** (`session_topics`, text, required): What topics were discussed during the session?
* **Group Size** (`group_size`, number, required): How many people were in attendance at the session?
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, required): How long did the session last?
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments or notes about the session.

## Tips

* Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at the email address provided.
