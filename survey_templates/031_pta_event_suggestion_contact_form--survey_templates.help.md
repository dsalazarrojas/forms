# PTA Event Suggestion Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PTA Event Suggestion Contact Form is designed to collect event suggestions from users, allowing the Parent-Teacher Association to consider and plan events that cater to the community's needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field.
2. Provide a clear and concise event suggestion in the "Event Suggestion" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, so the PTA can contact you for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Suggestion Form** (`event_suggestion_form`, Text, Optional): This is the main section where you can provide your event suggestions. Be as specific and detailed as possible to help the PTA plan the event.
* **Name** (`name`, Text, Optional): This field is for your name, which will be used to address you if the PTA needs to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address to allow the PTA to contact you with more information about your event suggestion or to confirm receipt of your form.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, Note, Optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information or context about your event suggestion.
* **Event Suggestion** (`event_suggestion`, Text, Optional): This is another field for you to provide more details about your event suggestion, if needed.
