# Basement Inspection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to conduct a thorough inspection of a basement's conditions, including its foundation, walls, floor, and overall state. The survey is used to identify potential issues and provide recommendations for repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Inspector Name** and **Inspection Date** with the corresponding information.
2. Enter the **Property Address**, which is the complete street address of the inspected property.
3. Fill in the **Property Owner Name**.
4. Select the type of **Basement Type** from the available options: Full finished basement, Full unfinished basement, Partial basement, Crawl space, or Walk out basement.
5. Enter the approximate **Basement Size** in square footage.
6. Select the approximate **Age of Basement** from the available options: Less than 5 years, 5 to 10 years, 11 to 20 years, 21 to 40 years, or More than 40 years.
7. Select the **Foundation Type** from the available options: Poured concrete, Concrete block, Stone, Brick, Wood.
8. Rate the **Foundation Condition** based on the observed conditions: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
9. Check if **Cracks Observed** in the foundation are present, rating them as: No cracks, Hairline cracks, Small cracks, Large cracks, or Severe cracking.
10. Check for signs of **Water Damage Evidence**, rating them as: None observed, Minor staining, Moderate damage, Severe damage, or Active leaking.
11. Rate the **Moisture Level** as: Dry, Slightly damp, Damp, Wet, or Standing water.
12. Check if **Mold or Mildew Present**, rating them as: None observed, Minor growth, Moderate growth, Significant growth, or Extensive growth.
13. Rate the **Musty Odors** as: None, Slight, Moderate, Strong, or Overwhelming.
14. Check for **Efflorescence Present**, rating them as: None, Minor, Moderate, or Significant growth.
15. Confirm the presence and condition of a **Sump Pump** as: Yes, working, Yes, not working, False, or Unknown.
16. If a sump pump is present, rate its **Condition** as: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
17. Check if an **Drainage System** is present and working, or if it's not present, or if it's unknown.
18. Rate the **Walls Condition** as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
19. Rate the **Floor Condition** as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
20. Rate the **Ceiling Condition** as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
21. Rate the **Windows Condition** as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or No windows.
22. Rate the **Ventilation** as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
23. Rate the **Electrical System** as: Up to code, Minor issues, Major issues, or Hazardous.
24. Rate the **Visible Plumbing** as: Good condition, Minor issues, Leaks observed, or Major issues.
25. Check the condition of the **HVAC Equipment** as: Present, good condition, Present, needs attention, Not present, or Not inspected.
26. Check for signs of **Pest Evidence**, rating them as: None, Minor, Moderate, Significant, or Severe.
27. Confirm if **Radon Tested** as: Yes, negative, Yes, positive, No, not tested, or Unknown.
28. Rate the **Overall Basement Rating** as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
29. List the **Major Concerns** in the text field, describing any significant issues found.
30. Enter a list of **Recommended Repairs** in the text field, specifying what needs to be fixed.
31. Enter an **Estimated Repair Cost** in the text field, if any.
32. Enter **Additional Comments** in the text field, describing any other observations.
33. Sign your name as the **Inspector Signature**, and type your full name.
34. Attach **Photos** for reference, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Property Address**: Enter the complete street address of the inspected property.
* **Property Owner Name**: Enter the name of the property owner.
* **Basement Type**: Select the type of basement: Full finished basement, Full unfinished basement, Partial basement, Crawl space, or Walk out basement.
* **Basement Size**: Enter the approximate square footage of the basement.
* **Age of Basement**: Select the approximate age of the basement from the available options.
* **Foundation Type**: Select the type of foundation from the available options.
* **Foundation Condition**: Rate the condition of the foundation as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
* **Cracks Observed**: Check if cracks are present, and rate their severity.
* **Water Damage Evidence**: Check for signs of water damage, and rate them as: None observed, Minor staining, Moderate damage, Severe damage, or Active leaking.
* **Moisture Level**: Rate the general moisture level as: Dry, Slightly damp, Damp, Wet, or Standing water.
* **Mold or Mildew Present**: Check if mold or mildew is present, and rate its severity.
* **Musty Odors**: Rate the musty odors as: None, Slight, Moderate, Strong, or Overwhelming.
* **Efflorescence Present**: Check for efflorescence, and rate its severity.
* **Sump Pump**: Confirm the presence and condition of the sump pump.
* **Sump Pump Condition**: Rate the condition of the sump pump as: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Drainage System**: Check if a drainage system is present and working.
* **Walls Condition**: Rate the condition of the walls as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
* **Floor Condition**: Rate the condition of the floor as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
* **Ceiling Condition**: Rate the condition of the ceiling as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
* **Windows Condition**: Rate the condition of the windows as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or No windows.
* **Ventilation**: Rate the air circulation as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
* **Electrical System**: Rate the condition of the electrical system as: Up to code, Minor issues, Major issues, or Hazardous.
* **Visible Plumbing**: Rate the condition of the pipes as: Good condition, Minor issues, Leaks observed, or Major issues.
* **HVAC Equipment**: Confirm the presence and condition of the HVAC equipment.
* **Pest Evidence**: Check for signs of pests, and rate their severity.
* **Radon Tested**: Check if radon has been tested.
* **Overall Basement Rating**: Rate the overall condition of the basement as: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
* **Major Concerns**: List any significant issues found during the inspection in the text field.
* **Recommended Repairs**: List any recommended repairs in the text field.
* **Estimated Repair Cost**: Enter an approximate cost for the recommended repairs, if any.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other observations or comments.
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign your name as the inspector.
* **Photos Attached**: Attach any photos taken during the inspection, if any.

**Tips**:

* Be thorough and detailed when filling out this form to ensure accurate results.
* Take note of any potential issues or concerns found during the inspection.
* Provide clear and concise answers for each field.
* Attach photos to support your observations, if possible.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure it accurately reflects the condition of the basement.
