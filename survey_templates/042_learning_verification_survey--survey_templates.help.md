# learning_verification_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture feedback from participants about their learning experience. The goal is to assess the effectiveness of training programs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your learning experience, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes if necessary.
4. Fill in your participant information (name, email, phone number, and category).
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **participant_feedback**
(participant_feedback, text, optional)
Please provide feedback about your learning experience.

### 2. **learning_program**
(learning_program, select_one, optional)
Select 'Yes' if you have participated in a learning program.

### 3. **objectives_meeting**
(objectives_meeting, select_multiple, optional)
Select 'Yes' for each objective that you feel you have met during the training.

### 4. **effectiveness_rating**
(effectiveness_rating, number, optional)
Rate the effectiveness of the learning experience on a scale of 1-10.

### 5. **rating**
(rating, number, optional)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the learning experience on a scale of 1-10.

### 6. **additional_comments**
(additional_comments, text, optional)
Enter any additional comments or notes about your learning experience.

### 7. **participant_name**
(participant_name, text, optional)
Enter your participant name.

### 8. **participant_email**
(participant_email, email, optional)
Enter your participant email address.

### 9. **participant_phone**
(participant_phone, text, optional)
Enter your participant phone number.

### 10. **date**
(date, date, optional)
Enter the date of your learning experience.

### 11. **time**
(time, time, optional)
Enter the time of your learning experience.

### 12. **note**
(note, note, optional)
Enter any additional notes about your learning experience.

### 13. **form_id**
(form_id, text, optional)
This field is not used for participant input; please ignore.

### 14. **category**
(category, text, optional)
Enter the category of your learning experience.

### 15. **description**
(description, text, optional)
Enter a description of your learning experience.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required. If you have any questions or need help, please contact us.
