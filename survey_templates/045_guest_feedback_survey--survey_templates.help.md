# Guest Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from guests about their experiences at hotels, restaurants, rooms, or services. It helps us understand your opinions and suggestions, which are essential for improving our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant fields from the dropdown menus, if applicable.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Checkin Date
* `checkin_date`: Select the date of check-in.
### Checkout Date
* `checkout_date`: Select the date of checkout.
### Rating
* `rating`: Rate your overall experience, from 1 to 5.
### Comments
* `comments`: Share your thoughts and opinions about your experience.
### Email Address
* `email_address`: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
### Phone Number
* `phone_number`: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
### Category
* `category`: Select the category of your experience (Hotel, Restaurant, Room, or Service).
### Sub-Category
* `sub_category`: Select the subcategory of your experience (Very Good, Good, Neutral, Bad, or Very Bad).
### Comment Subcategory
* `comment_subcategory`: Select the subcategory of your comment (Quality, Cleanliness, Service, Food, or Ambiance).
### Comment Details
* `comment_details`: Provide more details about your comment (e.g., "The food was delicious but the service was slow").
### Rating Score
* `rating_score`: Rate your overall satisfaction, from 1 to 5.
### Comment
* `comment`: Share your thoughts about your experience.
### Assigned Tool
* `assigned_tool`: Enter the name of the tool or equipment used during your stay.
### Form ID
* `form_id`: This field is for internal reference only and should not be filled in by the user.
### Output File
* `output_file`: This field is not relevant for users to fill in, as it is an automated field for exporting survey results.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have already filled out this form previously, please do not submit it again.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please email us at [your_email_address].
