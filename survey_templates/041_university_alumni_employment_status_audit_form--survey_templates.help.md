# University Alumni Employment Status Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The University Alumni Employment Status Audit Form is designed to collect information from university alumni about their current employment status. This form aims to gather data on the employment trends and outcomes of graduates to inform the university's career services and provide valuable insights to alumni and the university community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the current employment status of the alumni.
* Enter the name of the current employer, if applicable.
* Enter the job title, if applicable.
* Choose the current occupation from the options provided.
* Select the industry sector of the current employment.
* Enter the duration of employment, if applicable.
* Select the level of job satisfaction.
* Enter the expected employment outcome, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employment Status**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Active
	+ Retired
	+ Disabled
	+ Deceased
	+ Not employed

The employment status field is used to track the current employment status of the alumni. This information will help the university to understand the career outcomes of graduates.

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the current employer, if applicable. This field is optional and is used to provide additional information on the current employment status of the alumni.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title, if applicable. This field is optional and is used to provide more details on the current employment status of the alumni.
* **Current Occupation**: Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Manager
	+ Executive
	+ Software Developer
	+ Marketing Manager
	+ Teacher
	+ Other

The current occupation field is used to track the current occupation of the alumni. This information will help the university to understand the career paths of graduates.
* **Current Employer**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Current employer
	+ Former employer
	+ Self-Employed

The current employer field is used to track the current employment status of the alumni. This information will help the university to understand the employment trends and outcomes of graduates.
* **Industry Sector**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Finance
	+ Government
	+ Healthcare
	+ Non-Profit
	+ Technology

The industry sector field is used to track the industry in which the alumni are currently employed. This information will help the university to understand the employment trends and outcomes of graduates in different industries.
* **Employment Duration**: Enter the duration of employment, if applicable. This field is optional and is used to provide more details on the current employment status of the alumni.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied

The job satisfaction field is used to track the level of satisfaction of the alumni with their current employment. This information will help the university to understand the job satisfaction trends and outcomes of graduates.
* **Expected Employment Outcome**: Enter the expected employment outcome, if applicable. This field is optional and is used to provide additional information on the future plans of the alumni.
* **Tips**: Please provide as much information as possible to help the university better understand the employment status and trends of graduates.
