# Fellowship Meeting Format Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a fellowship meeting. It helps assess the format and quality of the meeting, including feedback, attendance, and comments from participants.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

2. Enter the number of attendees who participated in the meeting.
3. Enter the duration of the meeting, including the start and end times if applicable.
4. Enter the name of the facilitator who led the meeting.
5. Enter any session topics or subjects discussed during the meeting.
6. Provide feedback about the meeting for each of the four sections, as applicable.

8. Select the outcome or outcomes of the meeting (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

16. Enter the name of the facilitator who led the session.
17. Enter any additional comments or suggestions about the session.
