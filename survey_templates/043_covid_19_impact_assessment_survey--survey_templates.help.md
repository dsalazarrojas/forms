# COVID 19 Impact Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on your life and business. Your responses will help us identify areas where support is needed and provide valuable insights for future assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the most appropriate answer from the provided options.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the survey results.
* **Role or Title** (`respondent_role`, text, required): Please enter your job title or position to help us understand your employment context.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Please select the date you are completing this survey.
* **-- Economic Impact --**
	+ **Income Lost Due to COVID-19** (`income_lost`, number, optional): If applicable, please estimate the percentage of income lost due to the pandemic.
	+ **Percentage of Income Lost** (`income_impact_percentage`, number, required): If you answered "Income Lost Due to COVID-19" above, please enter the corresponding percentage.
* **Business Operations Affected** (`business_affected`, select_one, required): Select the level of impact on your business operations.
* **Financial Hardship Level** (`financial_hardship`, select_one, required): Select the level of financial hardship you are experiencing.
* **-- Employment Impact --**
	+ **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Please select your current employment status.
	+ **Lost Job Due to COVID-19** (`job_loss`, select_one, required): Select if you lost your job due to the pandemic.
	+ **Current Work Arrangement** (`work_arrangement`, select_one, required): Select your current work arrangement.
* **-- Health Impact --**
	+ **COVID-19 Diagnosis** (`covid_diagnosis`, select_one, required): Select if you or a family member has been diagnosed with COVID-19.
	+ **Family Member Diagnosed with COVID-19** (`family_covid_diagnosis`, select_one, required): Select if a family member has been diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **Mental Health Affected** (`mental_health_impact`, select_one, required): Select the level of mental health impact you are experiencing.
* **-- Social Impact --**
	+ **Social Isolation Impact** (`social_isolation`, select_one, required): Select the level of social isolation you are experiencing.
	+ **Family Relationships Impact** (`family_relationships`, select_one, required): Select the impact on your family relationships.
* **-- Recovery and Support --**
	+ **Recovery Outlook** (`recovery_outlook`, select_one, required): Select your recovery outlook.
	+ **Support Received** (`support_received`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of support you have received or are receiving.
	+ **Additional Support Needed** (`support_needed`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any additional support you may need.
* **Overall COVID-19 Impact Rating** (`overall_impact_rating`, select_one, required): Select the overall impact of COVID-19 on your life.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns you may have.
