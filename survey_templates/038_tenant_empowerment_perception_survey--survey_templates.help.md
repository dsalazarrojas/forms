# Tenant Empowerment Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather your opinions and experiences about your living or working space to help improve the services provided by the landlord or property manager. Your input will help us understand what's working well and what areas need attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply fill in the corresponding fields with your thoughts and opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What does being a tenant mean to you** (`tenant_empowerment`, text, optional): In a few words, describe what being a tenant means to you, and how you feel about your relationship with the landlord or property manager.
* **How satisfied are you with the communication from your landlord/property manager** (`communication_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the communication from your landlord or property manager on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **How does your landlord/property manager support your needs** (`empowerment_level`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **What are some ideas for improvements you have for the property** (`improvement_ideas`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options from the list:
	+ Landlords/Property Manager
	+ Maintenance/Repairs
	+ Communication
	+ Rent
	+ Amenities
* **How satisfied are you with your rent** (`rent_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with your rent on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **How often do you communicate with your landlord/property manager** (`communication_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Never
	+ Rarely
	+ Sometimes
	+ Often
	+ Almost always

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your feedback, as it will help us improve your living or working space.
* You can skip questions if they are not relevant or applicable to your situation.
* Please rate your answers on a scale or choose one of the provided options.
