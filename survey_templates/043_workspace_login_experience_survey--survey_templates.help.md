# Workspace Login Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with logging into their workspace. This will help us understand how to improve the login process and provide better user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus to answer the questions about your login experience.
2. If you'd like to provide additional comments or details about your experience, use the note fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Login Frequency** (`Login Frequency`, `Select Multiple`, Required: No): Choose whether you've had to log in multiple times recently.
* **Devices** (`Devices`, `Select Multiple`, Required: No): Check all the devices you've used to log in.
* **Browsers** (`Browsers`, `Select Multiple`, Required: No): Select all the browsers you've used to log in.
* **Issues** (`Issues`, `Select Multiple`, Required: No): Check if you've experienced any issues during login.
* **Satisfaction** (`Satisfaction`, `Select Multiple`, Required: No): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the current login process.
* **Frequency Note** (`Frequency Note`, `Note`, Required: No): Use this field to provide any additional comments about your login frequency.
* **Devices Note** (`Devices Note`, `Note`, Required: No): If needed, add any details about the devices you've used to log in.
* **Browsers Note** (`Browsers Note`, `Note`, Required: No): If necessary, add any comments about the browsers you've used to log in.
* **Issues Note** (`Issues Note`, `Note`, Required: No): Share any details about issues you've encountered during login.
* **Satisfaction Note** (`Satisfaction Note`, `Note`, Required: No): Express your thoughts about your satisfaction with the current login process.
* **Email** (`Email`, `Email`, Required: No): If you're willing to share it, provide your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `Text`, Required: No): You may leave your phone number for us to contact you about your experience. 

Note: Please make sure to fill out as much information as you can, even if it's just a few sentences. The more details we have, the better we can understand and improve your login experience.
