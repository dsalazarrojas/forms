# ux_design_user_experience_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user experience information to help improve the overall design and usability of the application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title.
2. Choose the category that best describes the form.
3. Provide a brief description of the form.
4. Rate your satisfaction level.
5. Rate the usability of the form.
6. Choose your daily habits for using the application.
7. Provide any comments or suggestions.
8. If you would like to be contacted, please provide your phone number or email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): A brief title of the form.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Choose a category that best describes the form (e.g., "Survey", "Feedback", etc.).
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the form.
* **Form Satisfaction** (`form_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with the form (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Form Usability** (`form_usability`, select_one, optional): Rate the usability of the form (e.g., Very Usable, Usable, Somewhat Usable, Somewhat Unusable, Very Unusable).
* **Form Habits** (`form_habits`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your daily habits for using the application (e.g., Early Morning, Midday, Afternoon, Evening, Night).
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the form.
* **Form Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
* **Form Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted, please provide your email address.
* **Form Suggestions** (`form_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any further suggestions or comments you may have about the form.
