# Reading Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your reading habits and preferences. Your responses will help us better understand your reading interests, allowing us to tailor services and recommendations to suit your tastes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. This form consists of 5 questions that cater to your reading habits and preferences.
2. Read the instructions carefully, and select the options that best suit your response.
3. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully, as your responses will help us provide better services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Reading Interest**
* **Label: reading_interest** (`reading_interest_survey`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): This question asks about your general interest in reading. Please select all the options that apply to you:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 2. **How Often Do You Read?**
* **Label: reading_interest_survey_1** (`reading_interest_survey_1`, `select_one`, `required: false`): How often do you read? Please choose one of the following options:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4

### 3. **What Type of Reading Materials Do You Prefer?**
* **Label: reading_interest_survey_2** (`reading_interest_survey_2`, `select_one`, `required: false`): What type of reading materials do you prefer? Please choose one of the following:
	+ Fiction
	+ Nonfiction
	+ Both
	+ Neither

### 4. **What Format Do You Prefer?**
* **Label: reading_interest_survey_3** (`reading_interest_survey_3`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Which format do you prefer? Please choose one of the following:
	+ Book
	+ Article
	+ Both
	+ Neither

### 5. **How Much Time Do You Spend Reading Per Week?**
* **Label: reading_interest_survey_4** (`reading_interest_survey_4`, `number`, `required: false`): Please enter the number of hours you spend reading per week.

### 6. **What Do You Like to Read for What Purpose?**
* **Label: reading_interest_survey_5** (`reading_interest_survey_5`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): What do you like to read for? Please select all the options that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No

Tips:

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
* Your responses will help us provide better services and recommendations.
