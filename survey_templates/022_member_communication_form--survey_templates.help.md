# Member Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect member communication preferences and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please fill out the sections below, one at a time. You can navigate between pages using the 'Next' and 'Back' buttons.

1. **Member Communication Form** (`member_communication_form`, text, false)
	* This is the title of the form. Please leave this field as it is.
2. **First Name** (`first_name`, text, false)
	* Enter your first name.
3. **Email** (`email`, email, false)
	* Enter your email address.
4. **Preferred Communication** (`preferred_communication`, select_one, false)
	* Choose how you would like to be communicated with:
		+ Email
		+ Phone
		+ Both
		+ Never
5. **Preferred Communication Method** (`preferred_communication_method`, select_multiple, false)
	* Select the methods you would like to be communicated with:
		+ Email
		+ Phone
6. **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, select_one, false)
	* Choose how often you would like to be communicated with:
		+ Once a week
		+ 2-3 times a week
		+ Daily
7. **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, false)
	* Enter your preferred time (HH:MM format).
8. **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_time_slot`, select_one, false)
	* Select your preferred time slot:
		+ Morning
		+ Afternoon
		+ Evening
9. **Note** (`note`, note, false)
	* Leave any additional comments or feedback.
10. **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, false)
	* This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.
11. **Member ID** (`member_id`, text, false)
	* Enter your member ID.
12. **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, false)
	* Enter your email address.
13. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, false)
	* Enter your phone number.
14. **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, false)
	* Leave any feedback or comments.
15. **Submission ID** (`submission_id`, text, false)
	* This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.
16. **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, text, false)
	* This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.
17. **Communication Status** (`communication_status`, select_one, false)
	* Choose your current communication status:
		+ Communicating
		+ Not Communicating
18. **Communication Method** (`communication_method`, select_multiple, false)
	* Select the methods you currently use for communication:
		+ Email
		+ Phone
19. **Note 2** (`note_2`, note, false)
	* Leave any additional comments or feedback.
20. **Note 3** (`note_3`, note, false)
	* Leave any additional comments or feedback.
21. **Note 4** (`note_4`, note, false)
	* Leave any additional comments or feedback.
22. **Note 5** (`note_5`, note, false)
	* Leave any additional comments or feedback.
23. **Note 6** (`note_6`, note, false)
	* Leave any additional comments or feedback.
24. **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, false)
	* This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.
25. **Last Modified Date** (`last_modified_date`, date, true)
	* This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification before submitting the form.
* You can navigate between pages using the 'Next' and 'Back' buttons.
