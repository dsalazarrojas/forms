# Gadget User Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback on their experience with our gadget. It aims to understand how users feel about using the gadget, their level of satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field as honestly as possible, based on your actual experience with the gadget.
2. Please answer all sections, even if you're not satisfied with the device. Your feedback is valuable to us!
3. Use your best judgment when selecting options, even if it means selecting "Other" if none of our listed options apply.
4. Don't worry if you can't fill out every field; the required fields are marked as such.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How do you feel about using our gadget?** (`gadget_user_experience_questionnaire`, text, optional): Please write a brief description of your feelings about using our gadget. This will help us understand your overall sentiment towards the device.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): If you're satisfied, please enter a number between 1 and 5. If you're not satisfied, please enter 0.
* **What do you use our gadget for?** (`usage_habits`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the ways you use our gadget, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Improvement Opportunities** (`improvement_opportunities`, text, optional): If you have any ideas on how we can improve our gadget, please list them here.
* **How satisfied are you with your device?** (`device_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Please select your level of satisfaction, from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Reasons for Dissatisfaction** (`device_satisfaction_reasons`, select_multiple, optional): If you're dissatisfied, please select all the reasons why, including Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, optional): Please describe your overall experience with the gadget.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them here.

Note: Please answer these questions honestly and concisely, and don't worry about grammar or spelling. We just want to hear your genuine feedback!
