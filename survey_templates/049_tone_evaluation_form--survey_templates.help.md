# Tone Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the tone of a given text, allowing users to provide their subjective impression and feedback. It is intended to be completed by users who have read the text and are familiar with its content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the text carefully and consider your overall impression of the tone.
2. Choose the most accurate option for each question based on your impression.
3. Provide detailed feedback on the text in the "What are your overall feedback on the text?" field.
4. Answer the remaining questions to the best of your ability, considering your subjective interpretation of the text's tone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your overall impression of the text?** (Select One)
	+ This question helps us understand your overall tone impression of the text.
	+ Choose the option that best represents your impression: Very positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, or Very negative.
* **Select all the tones that you feel are present in the text.** (Select Multiple)
	+ Please choose all the tones that you perceive in the text, from Friendly to Very negative.
* **How would you rate the text's tone?** (Select One)
	+ This question asks for your rating of the text's tone, on a scale from Very positive to Very negative.
* **What are your overall feedback on the text?** (Text)
	+ Provide detailed feedback on the text, including any positive or negative aspects you observed.
* **What do you think about the text's content?** (Text)
	+ Share your thoughts on the content, any observations or opinions you have.
* **How does the text's tone make you feel?** (Select One)
	+ Choose how the text's tone affects you, from Very positive to Very negative.
* **Do you have any additional feedback about the text?** (Text)
	+ Share any additional thoughts or comments about the text.
* **Is the text's tone consistent with the content?** (Select One)
	+ Answer if you think the text's tone matches its content, or not.
* **How would you rate the text's tone if you were to read it again?** (Select One)
	+ Rate the text's tone again, considering your previous reading experience.
* **Would you recommend the text to a friend?** (Select One)
	+ Decide if you would recommend the text to someone else.
* **Why or why not?** (Text)
	+ Explain your reason for recommending or not recommending the text.
* **How would you rate the text overall?** (Select One)
	+ Rate the text overall, from Very positive to Very negative.
* **Is this the final version of the form?**

Note: Please ensure that you have read the text carefully before completing this form, and answer based on your subjective impression.
