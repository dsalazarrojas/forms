# Event Festival Attendee Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and preferences from attendees of events. Your responses will help us understand your experience and identify areas to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Select the Event Name you attended.
4. Choose the Date of the event.
5. Select the Time of the event.
6. Describe the highlights of the event in the "What were the highlights of the event?" field.
7. Share any Additional Comments you have.
8. Provide suggestions for future events in the "What suggestions do you have for future events?" field.
9. Rate the event overall by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
10. Indicate how likely you are to attend a similar event in the future by selecting a number from 2 to 5.
11. Rate how likely you are to recommend this event to others by selecting a number from 3 to 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This will help us contact you if we need to follow up on your response.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Choose the event you attended from the list provided.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of the event you attended.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time of the event you attended.
* **What were the highlights of the event?** (`preferences`, text, required): Describe the aspects of the event that stood out to you.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have about the event.
* **What suggestions do you have for future events?** (`suggestions`, text, required): Provide any ideas or recommendations for improving future events.
* **How would you rate the event overall?** (`ratings_1_to_5`, number, required): Rate the event's quality on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **How likely are you to attend a future event like this?** (`ratings_2_to_5`, number, required): Indicate how likely you are to attend a similar event in the future.
* **How likely are you to recommend this event to others?** (`ratings_3_to_5`, number, required): Rate how likely you are to suggest this event to others.
