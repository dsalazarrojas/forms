# Diagram Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Diagram Assessment Survey" form is used to collect feedback and assessment from team members, educators, or other stakeholders on a diagram-related project or initiative. It helps to gather opinions on the design, documentation, and overall quality of the diagram or project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Team or Educators**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
	* Yes: Select this option if you are part of a team or educator involved in the project.
	* No: Select this option if you are not part of a team or educator involved in the project.
2. **Feedback on Design**: Provide your feedback on the diagram's design.
3. **Design Reviews**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
	* Yes: Select this option if you have reviewed the diagram's design.
	* No: Select this option if you have not reviewed the diagram's design.
4. **Feedback on Documentation**: Provide your feedback on the diagram's documentation.
5. **Documentation Reviews**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
	* Yes: Select this option if you have reviewed the diagram's documentation.
	* No: Select this option if you have not reviewed the diagram's documentation.
6. **Training Reviews**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
	* Yes: Select this option if you have completed training related to the diagram.
	* No: Select this option if you have not completed training related to the diagram.
7. **Design Reviews Comments**: Provide any comments on the diagram's design.
8. **Documentation Comments**: Provide any comments on the diagram's documentation.
9. **Overall Comments**: Provide any overall comments on the diagram or project.
10. **Submit Form**: Click the "Submit" button to submit your assessment.
11. **Submit Comments**: Provide any additional comments on your submission.
12. **Name**: Enter your name.
13. **Department**: Enter your department (optional).
14. **Title**: Enter your title (optional).
15. **Name of Institution**: Enter the name of the institution you represent (required).
16. **Role**: Enter your role in the project (required).
17. **Job Title**: Enter your job title (optional).
18. **Comments**: Provide any additional comments on your submission.
19. **Submit Form Comments**: Provide any comments on your submission.
20. **Submit Button**: Click the "Submit" button to finalize your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team or Educators**: Check to indicate if you are part of a team or educator involved in the project.
* **Feedback on Design**: Provide feedback on the design of the diagram.
* **Design Reviews**: Check to indicate if you have reviewed the diagram's design.
* **Feedback on Documentation**: Provide feedback on the documentation of the diagram.
* **Documentation Reviews**: Check to indicate if you have reviewed the diagram's documentation.
* **Training Reviews**: Check to indicate if you have completed training related to the diagram.
* **Design Reviews Comments**: Provide comments on the diagram's design.
* **Documentation Comments**: Provide comments on the diagram's documentation.
* **Overall Comments**: Provide overall comments on the diagram or project.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Department**: Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Title**: Enter your title (if applicable).
* **Name of Institution**: Enter the name of the institution you represent (required).
* **Role**: Enter your role in the project (required).
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments on your submission.
* **Submit Form Comments**: Provide comments on your submission.
* **Submit Button**: Click the "Submit" button to finalize your submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Provide clear and concise feedback on each field.
* Ensure that you have reviewed and understood the purpose of this form before submitting your assessment.
