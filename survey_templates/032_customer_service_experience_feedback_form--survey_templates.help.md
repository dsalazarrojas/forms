# Customer Service Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Experience Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback and insights from customers on their experience with our customer service team. This form helps us understand how we're doing and where we can improve to better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in each section to provide your honest feedback on your experience with our customer service.
2. For open-ended fields like "If you could suggest one area for improvement", feel free to provide as much detail as you'd like.
3. Select one or multiple options for each field to indicate your response.
4. If you prefer to be contacted by a specific method, please indicate your preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Service Experience** (`customer_service_experience`): A text field where you can describe your experience with our customer service team.
* **How would you rate our customer support?** (`customer_support`): Rate our customer support as "Not Sufficient", "Sufficient", or "Highly Sufficient" to help us understand how we're doing.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of our service?** (`service_quality`): Rate the overall quality of our service as "Poor", "Fair", "Good", "Very Good", or "Excellent".
* **Overall satisfaction with our service?** (`satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service as "Not Satisfied", "Satisfied", or "Highly Satisfied".
* **If you could suggest one area for improvement** (`open_ended`): This field is for any additional feedback or suggestions you may have on how we can improve our service.
* **Would you like to be contacted for follow-up discussions?** (`contact_details`): Select "False" to not be contacted, "True" to be contacted, or choose a specific method (Phone, Email, Mail) to be contacted.
* **Which follow-up method would you like to be used?** (`follow_up_method`): Select one or multiple methods (Phone, Email, Mail) for us to contact you.
* **What is your email address or phone number?** (`contact_info`): Enter your email address or phone number if you've chosen to be contacted.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback for us?** (`additional_comments`): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you may have.
* **Would you prefer to contact us via phone, email or mail?** (`contact_preferred`): Select one or multiple methods (Phone, Email, Mail) for you to contact us.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
