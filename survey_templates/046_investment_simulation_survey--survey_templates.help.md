# Investment Simulation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you simulate and evaluate your investment strategy. It will guide you through a series of questions on four different pages, each focusing on a specific aspect of investment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate through the pages sequentially, completing each page's questions before proceeding to the next.
2. Review each page's label to understand what information it requests.
3. Enter your answers according to the question type (select, yes/no, time, or number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Page** (First Page, text, required):
  This first page is an introduction to the investment simulation, setting the stage for the questions to come.
  Please provide a brief description of your current investment strategy or goals.
- **First Page Questions** (First Page Questions, select multiple, required):
  Choose all the options that apply to your current investment strategy:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Second Page** (Second Page, text, required):
  Describe your current investment portfolio.
- **Second Page Questions** (Second Page Questions, select one, required):
  Which of the following questions best describes your investment goals:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Third Page** (Third Page, time, required):
  Estimate the number of hours you spend per week on investment research.
- **Third Page Questions** (Third Page Questions, number, required):
  How much money are you willing to invest per quarter:
  Answer with a numerical value.
- **Fourth Page** (Fourth Page, number, optional):
  If you have any additional investment funds available, estimate the amount.
- **Fourth Page Questions** (Fourth Page Questions, text, optional):
  Describe any other investment priorities or goals you have:
  This is an open-ended question for any additional information you'd like to provide.
