# Hospital Patient Care Initiative Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions and feedback from hospital staff on the patient care initiative. The form aims to gauge the overall support among staff for the initiative and gather insights on how to improve patient care quality. Please complete this form to contribute to the improvement of patient care services in your hospital.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each page of the form carefully before starting your response.
2.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly. For select fields, choose one or more options from the available answers.
3.  Provide detailed explanations for any free-text fields (Staff Comments and Hospital Comments).
4.  Use your email address and phone number to help us contact you for follow-up.
5.  Submit your response once you have completed all pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Staff Votes
* **Staff Votes** (`Staff Votes`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether you support or do not support the patient care initiative by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the options.

### Page 2: Vote Priority
* **Vote Priority** (`Vote Priority`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the available options to indicate your priority for the patient care initiative. Options are: Care Improvement and Other Priority.

### Page 3: Feedback
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `note`, optional): If you have any specific feedback or suggestions for improving patient care services, write them here.

### Page 4: Staff Comments
* **Staff Comments** (`Staff Comments`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts or experiences about the patient care initiative. Your input will help us understand the challenges and opportunities for improvement.

### Page 5: Email
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up on your response.

### Page 6: Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number if you would like us to contact you by phone for further discussion.

### Page 7: Hospital Comments
* **Hospital Comments** (`Hospital Comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or observations about the hospital's services or processes related to patient care.

Note: The form will be reviewed for relevance and non-duplication to ensure each response is unique and valuable to the patient care initiative. Please answer honestly and thoroughly to contribute to the improvement of patient care services.
