# Vehicle Interior Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about a vehicle's interior condition and other key attributes. This information will be used for inventory management, vehicle valuation, and other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing general details about the vehicle, including its model, color, and year of purchase.
2. Answer the questions about the vehicle's interior and exterior conditions, including rating and comments.
3. Provide the mileage and vehicle identification number (VIN) for the vehicle.
4. Answer questions about the vehicle's warranty status, interior type, transmission type, engine type, drive type, and airbag status.
5. Fill out the form completely, and double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Enter the make and model of the vehicle.
* **Mileage** (`mileage`): Enter the current mileage of the vehicle, including the unit of measurement (e.g., miles or kilometers).
* **VIN** (`vin`): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the year the vehicle was purchased or is expected to be purchased.
* **Interior Condition Rating** (`interior_condition_rating`): Rate the interior condition of the vehicle on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Very Good).
* **Interior Condition Comment** (`interior_condition_comment`): Enter a brief description of the interior condition of the vehicle.
* **Exterior Condition Rating** (`exterior_condition_rating`): Rate the exterior condition of the vehicle on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Very Good).
* **Exterior Condition Comment** (`exterior_condition_comment`): Enter a brief description of the exterior condition of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Registration Status** (`vehicle_registration_status`): Select the current registration status of the vehicle (e.g., Registered, Deregistered, Pending Registration).
* **Vehicle Warranty Status** (`vehicle_warranty_status`): Select the current warranty status of the vehicle (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending Warranty).
* **Interior Type** (`vehicle_interior_type`): Select the type of interior (e.g., Standard, Leather, Premium, Custom).
* **Transmission Type** (`transmission_type`): Select the type of transmission (e.g., Automatic, Manual, Semi-Automatic).
* **Engine Type** (`engine_type`): Select the type of engine (e.g., Gasoline, Diesel, Hybrid, Electric).
* **Drive Type** (`drive_type`): Select the drive type (e.g., Front Wheel Drive, Rear Wheel Drive, All Wheel Drive).
* **Interior Type** (`interior_type`): Select the type of interior (e.g., Cloth, Leather, Vinyl).
* **Airbag Status** (`airbag_status`): Select whether the vehicle has airbags or not.
* **Airbag Type** (`airbag_type`): Select the type of airbag system (e.g., Dual, Single, None).
* **Airbag Count** (`airbag_count`): Enter the number of airbags the vehicle has.
* **Airbag Location** (`airbag_location`): Select the location of the airbags (e.g., Front, Rear).
* **Airbag Condition** (`airbag_condition`): Select the condition of the airbags (e.g., Good, Poor, Average, Fair, Very Poor).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to provide a reliable record.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with a subject matter expert or seek further guidance.
* This form is designed to be completed once for each vehicle. Do not fill it out again for the same vehicle.
