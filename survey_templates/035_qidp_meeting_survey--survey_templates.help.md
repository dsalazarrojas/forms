# QIDP Meeting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The QIDP Meeting Survey form is designed to collect information about meetings, including the date, time, location, attendees, and any notes or comments. This form helps meeting organizers or attendees document and record meeting details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the meeting from the calendar picker.
2. Enter the time of the meeting.
3. Enter the location of the meeting.
4. Indicate whether or not attendees are expected.
5. If attendees are expected, list them in the Attendee List field.
6. Enter any notes or comments about the meeting.
7. Enter your email address (if you are the meeting organizer or attendee).
8. Enter any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Survey** (`meeting_survey`, text, required: no): Enter a brief description of the meeting, including its purpose and agenda.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: no): Select the date of the meeting from the calendar picker.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: no): Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: no): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, required: no): Select all attendees who are expected to attend the meeting.
* **Attendee List** (`attendees_list`, text, required: no): List the attendees by name, if they are expected to attend.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: no): Enter any notes or comments about the meeting.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: no): Enter your email address (if you are the meeting organizer or attendee).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: no): Enter your phone number (if you are the meeting organizer or attendee).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: no): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the meeting.
* **Questions** (`questions`, text, required: no): Enter any questions or topics that need to be discussed during the meeting.
