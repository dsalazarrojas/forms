# school_community_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from students, teachers, and parents in a school community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each page of the form carefully.
2.  Provide your feedback and suggestions in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Feedback (student_feedback, text, false)**: This field is for students to share their thoughts and opinions about their experience in the school community.
*   **Teacher Comments (teacher_comments, text, true\*)**: In this field, teachers can provide comments and feedback about their work in the school community.
*   **Parent Suggestions (parent_suggestions, select_multiple, true\*)**: This field allows parents to select multiple options for suggestions they have for the school community.
    • Parent A: This option is for parents to suggest ideas from their perspective.
    • Parent B: This option is for parents to suggest ideas from their point of view.
    • Parent C: This option is for parents to provide additional suggestions.
*   **School Activities (school_activities, select_one, true\*)**: In this field, you can select one option that best represents your suggestion for school activities.
    • Option A: This option is for a suggestion that focuses on extracurricular activities.
    • Option B: This option is for a suggestion that focuses on academic programs.
    • Option C: This option is for a suggestion that focuses on facilities and resources.
*   **Teacher Suggestions (teacher_suggestions, text, false)**: This field is for teachers to provide their suggestions and ideas for the school community.

## Tips
*   Make sure to review your submissions carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school administration.
