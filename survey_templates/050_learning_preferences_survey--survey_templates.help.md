# Learning Preferences Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to help us understand your preferences and needs when it comes to learning. By filling out this survey, you'll be helping us shape the development of learning resources and templates that suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page by clicking on the buttons at the bottom of the page. You can review and edit your answers as you go.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey_questions** (select multiple):
	+ This question is about whether you're comfortable with new questions or information.
	+ Please select all that apply.
* **learning_styles** (select one):
	+ This question is about your preferred method of learning.
	+ Please choose the option that best suits your learning style.
* **new_information** (text):
	+ Please enter your thoughts on what kind of new information you'd like to see in our learning resources.
* **new_tasks** (text):
	+ Please describe any new tasks or activities that you'd like to see in our learning resources.
* **survey_preferences** (select multiple):
	+ This question is about your preferences for new information and learning resources.
	+ Please select all that apply.
* **no_coding** (text):
	+ If you have any thoughts on why you wouldn't want to learn coding skills, please enter them here.
* **get_started** (select one):
	+ This question is about whether you've already started learning or working on a new project.
	+ Please choose the option that best describes your situation.
* **survey_templates** (text):
	+ If there's anything you'd like to see improved or added to our survey templates, please share your thoughts.
* **survey_preferences** (select multiple) [Duplicate of the same field]
Note: Please do not fill in this field as it has been duplicated in the form and will not be recorded as part of the survey.
