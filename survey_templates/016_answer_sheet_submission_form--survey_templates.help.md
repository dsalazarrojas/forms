# Answer Sheet Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect student submissions for assignments or quizzes. It is intended to be used as an intermediate step before submitting assignments or quizzes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student Name and Student ID in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the title of the assignment or quiz.
3. Provide the date of submission.
4. Answer each question or prompt as instructed.
5. If applicable, provide an extended response or essay answer.
6. If necessary, enter any source citations.
7. Enter the Instructor's name.
8. Add any additional notes for the instructor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or records.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Assignment or Quiz Title**: Enter the title of the assignment or quiz you are submitting.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date on which you are submitting this assignment or quiz.
* **Answer 1**, **Answer 2**, **Answer 3**, **Answer 4**, **Answer 5**: Answer each question or prompt as instructed.
* **Essay or Extended Response**: If you need to provide a longer answer, write it in this field.
* **Source Citations (if required)**: If you need to provide source citations, enter them in this field.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the instructor's name as it appears in your records.
* **Additional Notes for the Instructor**: Enter any additional comments or notes you have for the instructor.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If a question has an "if required" note, answer it only if instructed to do so.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* Use proper citation format for any source citations.
* Proofread your submission before submitting it.
