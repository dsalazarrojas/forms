# Patriotism And National Identity Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patriotism And National Identity Quiz is designed to measure an individual's level of national identity, their views on patriotism, and how they perceive their relationship with their country. This quiz is composed of 22 questions that cover various aspects of national identity, such as nationalism, patriotism, and national symbols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and select the best answer that aligns with your thoughts and feelings.
2. Fill in each question as instructed, choosing the options provided or writing a brief answer in the text fields.
3. Ensure that you complete all the questions, as skipping any of them may affect the accuracy of the quiz results.
