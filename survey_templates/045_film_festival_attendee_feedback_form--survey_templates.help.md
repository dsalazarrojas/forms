# Film Festival Attendee Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback from film festival attendees, allowing organizers to gauge the overall quality and appeal of the event. The goal is to gather data on attendee satisfaction, preferences, and ratings to improve future film festival experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide feedback, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Attendee Name" field.
2.  Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5 in the "Overall Rating" field.
3.  Indicate your level of satisfaction with the event in the "Satisfaction" field.
4.  Select all that apply from the list of interests provided in the "Preferences" field.
5.  If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendee Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on any follow-up communications or recognition.
*   **Overall Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
*   **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required): Indicate how satisfied you are with the event, with higher scores indicating greater satisfaction.
*   **Preferences** (`preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the things you enjoyed during the festival, such as movie premieres, live music, art exhibits, or food and drinks.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions, please share them here.
