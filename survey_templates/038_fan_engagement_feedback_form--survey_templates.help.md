# Fan Engagement Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Fan Engagement Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from fans about their experiences with your team, events, or content. This form helps identify areas of improvement and provides valuable insights for future event planning, content creation, and fan engagement strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the correct options from the drop-down menus (if applicable).
3. Enter your thoughts and comments in the text fields.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Fan Satisfaction

* **Satisfaction** (`<number>`, *optional*): Rate your level of satisfaction with your recent event experience, on a scale of 1-5.

### Content Preferences

* **Content Preferences** (`<select_multiple>`, *optional*): Select all the types of content you would like to see from us in the future. You can choose multiple options.

### Event Experience

* **Event Experience** (`<note>`, *required*): Share any comments or thoughts about your recent event experience.

### Contact Information

* **Email** (`<email>`, *required*): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`<text>`, *optional*): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.

### Feedback and Suggestions

* **Satisfaction** (`<number>`, *required*): Rate your level of satisfaction with your recent event experience, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Suggestions** (`<text>`, *optional*): Share any suggestions or feedback you have about your recent event experience.
* **Event Suggestion** (`<text>`, *optional*): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for future events.
* **Additional Comments** (`<text>`, *optional*): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your recent event experience.

### Follow-up Information

* **Follow-up** (`<date>`, *optional*): Select a date for your next follow-up meeting or action item.
* **Follow-up Time** (`<time>`, *optional*): Select a time for your next follow-up meeting or action item.
* **Follow-up Location** (`<text>`, *optional*): Enter the location for your next follow-up meeting or action item.
* **Follow-up Channel** (`<text>`, *optional*): Select the channel or method for your next follow-up (e.g., phone, email, in-person).
* **Follow-up Method** (`<text>`, *optional*): Select the method for your next follow-up (e.g., phone, email, in-person).
* **Follow-up Status** (`<select_one>`, *required*): Select the status of your next follow-up (e.g., Scheduled, In Progress, Completed).
* **Next Follow-up Date** (`<date>`, *optional*): Enter the date for your next follow-up.
* **Next Follow-up Time** (`<time>`, *optional*): Enter the time for your next follow-up.
* **Next Follow-up Location** (`<text>`, *optional*): Enter the location for your next follow-up.
* **Next Follow-up Notes** (`<text>`, *optional*): Enter any notes or next steps for your next follow-up.

### Fan Type and Social Media

* **Fan Type** (`<select_one>`, *required*): Select whether you are a Fan or Non-Fan.
* **Social Media** (`<select_multiple>`, *optional*): Select all the social media platforms you are active on (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Reddit).

## Tips

* Please be as honest and detailed as possible in your feedback and suggestions.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
