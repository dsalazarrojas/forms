# Multiple Choice Options Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect multiple choice options from users across multiple pages. It appears to be a template for a survey with a large number of questions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the survey.
2. Select the options for each question by clicking on the corresponding radio buttons.
3. Review and submit the completed survey once all questions are answered.
