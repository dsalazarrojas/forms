# Creative Writing Prompt Generator - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Creative Writing Prompt Generator form is designed to help writers input their preferences and generate a writing prompt tailored to their needs. This prompt can serve as the starting point for their creative endeavors, helping them overcome writer's block and get their imagination flowing.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to you.
2.  For each field, choose the option that best fits your preferences.
3.  You can fill out the form as much or as little as you like, depending on the information you wish to provide.
4.  Once you've filled out the form, click the "Generate Prompt" button to receive your generated writing prompt.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Writing Group** (`writing_group`, select_multiple, optional):
    - Select the types of writing that you are interested in. This could be fiction, non-fiction, poetry, or even playwriting.
*   **Writer Name** (`writer_name`, text, optional):
    - Enter your name or the name of your character. This can help us generate more personalized and accurate prompts.
*   **Writing Style** (`writing_style`, select_one, optional):
    - Choose the type of writing style you're in the mood for. For example, do you want a prompt for fantasy, romance, or horror?
*   **Writing Prompt** (`writing_prompt`, text, optional):
    - Enter any specific details about what you're looking for in a prompt. For example, do you want it to be set in a particular location, have specific themes, or focus on a certain style?
*   **Writing Prompt Type** (`writing_prompt_type`, select_one, optional):
    - Select the type of writing prompt you're looking for. This could be a simple idea, a scenario, or even a character concept.
*   **Story Length** (`story_length`, number, optional):
    - Choose a length for your story. This can help us generate prompts that fit your writing schedule and goals.
*   **Writing Prompt Details** (`writing_prompt_details`, text, optional):
    - Enter any additional details or preferences you have for your prompt. For example, do you want it to be funny, serious, or educational?
