# Competitor Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Competitor Research Survey form, designed to gather your thoughts on competitors' products or services you're using. Your input will help us understand their strengths and weaknesses, which will inform our product development and improvement process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form if you're currently using a competitor's product or service.
- Answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask, and we'll do our best to clarify.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Competitor Product Feedback --** (`research_intro_header`, note, not required): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Which competitor's product do you currently use?** (`competitor_brand_choice`, select_one, required): Select the competitor's product or service you're currently using. You can choose from the list of options or select "Other" if it's not listed.
* **Specific Product or Service Used** (`specific_product_used`, text, required): Enter the name of the competitor's product or service you're using.
* **How long have you been using this product?** (`usage_duration_comp`, select_one, required): Choose how long you've been using the competitor's product or service.
* **What was the primary reason for choosing them?** (`main_reason_for_choosing_competitor`, select_one, required): Select the primary reason you chose the competitor's product or service.
* **What is the one thing they do better than us?** (`competitor_advantage_feedback`, text, required): Be honest – this helps us improve our product or service.
* **Overall satisfaction with their product** (`competitor_satisfaction_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the competitor's product or service, from 1 (not satisfied) to 5 (highly satisfied).
* **How likely are you to switch to our product in the next 6 months?** (`likelihood_to_switch_to_us`, select_one, required): Rate your likelihood to switch to our product or service in the next 6 months.
* **Is there a key feature missing in their product?** (`missing_feature_competitor`, text, not required): Describe any features you feel are missing from the competitor's product or service.
* **Have you had any recent negative experiences with them?** (`negative_experience_competitor`, text, not required): Share any recent issues or problems you've encountered with the competitor's product or service.
* **Date** (`survey_date_research`, date, required): Select today's date. This will help us track the date of your submission.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions truthfully, and provide as much detail as possible.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask, and we'll do our best to clarify.
- Your input is invaluable, and we appreciate your time and effort in completing this form.
