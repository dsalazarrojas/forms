# pomodoro_technique_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with the Pomodoro Technique. It aims to understand users' habits, focus, productivity, and satisfaction levels during sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. You can submit it once you have finished filling out all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field where you can briefly introduce yourself and provide any context about your experience with the Pomodoro Technique.
* **background** (`background`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any background information about your experience with the Pomodoro Technique, such as how you learned about it or how you have been using it.
* **POMODORO_SESSIONS** (`pomodoro_sessions`, `number`, required: false): This question asks how many Pomodoro sessions you usually have per day. Please answer with the number of sessions you typically have.
* **session_length** (`session_length`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about the average length of your Pomodoro sessions. Please answer with the number of minutes you usually spend on each session.
* **BREAKS** (`breaks`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select how many breaks you usually take between your sessions. You can choose from the options: "Pomodoro sessions with no breaks", "Pomodoro sessions with 5-10 minute breaks", "Pomodoro sessions with 15-30 minute breaks", "Pomodoro sessions with 30-60 minute breaks", or "Other (please specify)".
* **breaks_frequency** (`breaks_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks how often you take breaks during your sessions. You can choose from the options: "Before each session", "Every 25% of sessions", "Every 50% of sessions", "Every session", or "Other (please specify)".
* **FOCUS** (`focus`, `select_one`, required: false): This question measures how focused you feel during your sessions. You can choose from the options: "Not at all", "Somewhat", "Somewhat focused", "Very focused", "Extremely focused".
* **productivity** (`productivity`, `select_one`, required: false): This question measures how productive you feel during your sessions. You can choose from the options: "Not productive at all", "Somewhat productive", "Very productive", "Extremely productive".
* **satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): This question measures how satisfied you are with the Pomodoro Technique. You can choose from the options: "Not satisfied at all", "Somewhat satisfied", "Very satisfied", "Extremely satisfied".
* **RECOMMEND** (`recommend`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks if you recommend the Pomodoro Technique to others. You can choose from the options: "True" or "False".
* **comments** (`comment`, `text`, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about the Pomodoro Technique.
