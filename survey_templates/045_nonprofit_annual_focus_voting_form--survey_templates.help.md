# Nonprofit Annual Focus Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help Nonprofit organizations set annual goals and priorities for their focus areas. The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from relevant stakeholders, including staff, board members, and external partners, to determine the most important focus areas for the Nonprofit in the upcoming year. The form is not intended to be a comprehensive survey, but rather a tool to gather high-level information to help guide decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For questions marked as "required", you must provide an answer.
3. Select the relevant options for the multiple-choice questions.
4. Provide clear and concise answers for the text fields.
5. If you have any additional comments or notes, use the "Comments" or "Note" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro`, text, required: false): This is a brief introduction page where you can provide any additional context or information about your response.
* **What is your priority** (`what_is_your_priority`, select_one, required: false): Please select one of the following options: A, B, or C. This question helps us understand your top priority for the Nonprofit in the upcoming year.
* **What are your goals** (`what_are_your_goals`, text, required: false): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any relevant goals or objectives you have for the Nonprofit. Please keep your answer concise.
* **Nonprofit focus areas** (`nonprofit_focus_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the relevant focus areas that are important to you. These focus areas are: A, B, or C.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you would like to be contacted regarding your response, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you have a phone number where you can be reached, please provide it.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): If you have a specific date or timeframe for your goals, please provide it in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): If you have a specific time or timeframe for your goals, please provide it in the standard time format (HH:MM).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): If you have any additional notes or comments that are not captured in the previous questions, please use this field.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): If you have any further comments or additional information, please use this field.
