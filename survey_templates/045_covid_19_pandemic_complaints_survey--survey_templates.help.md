This form is a survey designed to collect information about COVID-19 pandemic-related complaints. The form is intended for respondents who have experienced difficulties or concerns related to the pandemic and would like to share their experiences and suggestions for improvement. The purpose of this form is to gather data that will help identify areas where the pandemic response has been effective or ineffective, and to gather suggestions for improvement.

# COVID 19 Pandemic Complaints Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experiences and concerns related to the COVID-19 pandemic. Your responses will help us better understand how to improve our response to the pandemic.
## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the following questions carefully and honestly. You may answer all questions or just those that apply to your experience. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Respondent Information --** (`respondent_header`, `note`, not required): This section is for informational purposes only and does not affect the processing of your complaint.
* **Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, not required): Please enter your name or alias if you prefer not to be identified.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you for follow-up.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address if you would like us to contact you for follow-up.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code to help us better understand your location.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are completing this form.
* **-- Complaint Information --** (`complaint_header`, `note`, not required): This section is for informational purposes only and does not affect the processing of your complaint.
* **Type of Complaint** (`complaint_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of complaint you are experiencing, such as Public Health Response, Business Impact, etc.
* **Complaint Category** (`complaint_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of your complaint, such as Service Closure or Limitation, etc.
* **Detailed Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, `text`, required): Describe your complaint in as much detail as possible.
* **When Did This Complaint Arise** (`complaint_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date your complaint arose.
* **-- Impact of Complaint --** (`impact_header`, `note`, not required): This section is for informational purposes only and does not affect the processing of your complaint.
* **Personal Impact** (`personal_impact`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of impact you are experiencing due to the complaint.
* **Areas of Impact** (`impact_areas`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all areas where your complaint has had an impact.
* **-- Positive Feedback --** (`positive_header`, `note`, not required): This section is for informational purposes only and does not affect the processing of your complaint.
* **Any Positive Feedback** (`positive_feedback`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have had any positive aspects of the pandemic response.
* **Describe Positive Feedback** (`positive_details`, `text`, not required): Enter any positive aspects of the pandemic response you have experienced.
* **-- Suggestions and Improvements --** (`suggestion_header`, `note`, not required): This section is for informational purposes only and does not affect the processing of your complaint.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions`, `text`, not required): Enter any suggestions for how we can improve our response to the pandemic.
* **Action Requested or Resolution Sought** (`action_requested`, `text`, not required): Enter any action or resolution you would like to be taken.
* **Follow-up Response Desired** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you would like us to contact you for follow-up on your complaint or not.

Please note that some fields are optional but it is highly recommended that you fill out as much of the form as possible to help us better understand your experience and concerns.
