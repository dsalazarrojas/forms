# groundhog_day_opinion_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey about your opinions and thoughts about Groundhog Day. It will help us understand your familiarity, beliefs, interest, and favorite characters related to this event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
* Review the form and ensure you have a clear understanding of each question.
* Select the correct options for each field based on your genuine opinions.
* Fill out each field completely.
* Submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How well do you know Groundhog Day? (Page 1 - familiarity_with_groundhog_day)
* **How well do you know Groundhog Day?** (How familiar you are with this event)
	+ This question asks about your level of familiarity with Groundhog Day, which is a popular cultural event.
* Answer on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being "not at all familiar" and 10 being "very familiar".

### What do you believe about Groundhog Day? (Page 2 - beliefs_about_groundhog_day)
* **What do you believe about Groundhog Day?**
	+ This question asks about your personal beliefs and thoughts about Groundhog Day.
* Write your honest answer in the text field.

### How interested are you in Groundhog Day? (Page 3 - interest_in_groundhog_day)
* **How interested are you in Groundhog Day?**
	+ This question asks about your level of interest in this event.
* Answer on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being "not at all interested" and 10 being "very interested".

### Favorite Groundhog Day character? (Page 4 - favorite_groundhog_day_character)
* **Favorite Groundhog Day character?**
	+ This question asks about your favorite character associated with Groundhog Day.
* Select the option that best represents your favorite character. If your favorite character is not listed, please answer "Yes" and type in your favorite character in the text field.

### Favorite Groundhog Day character (if other) (Page 5 - favorite_groundhog_day_character_other)
* **Other (please specify)**
* This field is for if you selected "Yes" as your favorite character on the previous question.
* Type in your favorite Groundhog Day character here.
