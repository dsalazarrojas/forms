# University Faculty Research Impact Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the research activities and impact of faculty members within a university. The data collected will be used to evaluate the overall research impact of the university.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your department from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter your faculty member name in the "Faculty Member Name" field.
3. Provide a description of your research interests in the "Research Interests" field.
4. Enter the number of research publications you have made in the "Research Publications" field.
5. Provide a description of your research grants in the "Research Grants" field.
6. Enter a description of your research collaborations in the "Research Collaborations" field.
7. Enter a description of your research projects in the "Research Projects" field.
8. Enter a description of the departmental impact of your research in the "Departmental Impact" field.
9. Enter a description of the university impact of your research in the "University Impact" field.
10. Enter a description of the external impact of your research in the "External Impact" field.
11. Enter a Research Impact Statement that describes the overall impact of your research.
12. Enter a Departmental Impact Statement that describes the departmental impact of your research.
13. Enter a University Impact Statement that describes the university impact of your research.
14. Enter an External Impact Statement that describes the external impact of your research.
15. Enter a description of your impact measures in the "Impact Measures" field.
16. Enter a description of your evaluation process in the "Evaluation Process" field.
17. Enter a description of your review process in the "Review Process" field.
18. Enter a description of your reporting requirements in the "Reporting Requirements" field.
19. Enter a description of your review frequency in the "Review Frequency" field.
20. Enter a description of your review criterion in the "Review Criterion" field.
21. Enter a description of your review comments in the "Review Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department** (Department, Select One, Required): Select the department that you belong to.
* **Faculty Member Name** (Faculty Member Name, Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your faculty ID badge or business card.
* **Research Area** (Research Area, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of your research area or focus.
* **Research Interests** (Research Interests, Text, Required): Provide a description of your research interests.
* **Research Publications** (Research Publications, Number, Not Required): Enter the number of research publications you have made.
* **Research Awards** (Research Awards, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of your research awards or achievements.
* **Research Grants** (Research Grants, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of your research grants or funding.
* **Research Collaborations** (Research Collaborations, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of your research collaborations or partnerships.
* **Research Projects** (Research Projects, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of your research projects or initiatives.
* **Departmental Impact** (Departmental Impact, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of the departmental impact of your research.
* **University Impact** (University Impact, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of the university impact of your research.
* **External Impact** (External Impact, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of the external impact of your research.
* **Research Impact Statement** (Research Impact Statement, Text, Required): Enter a statement that describes the overall impact of your research.
* **Departmental Impact Statement** (Departmental Impact Statement, Text, Not Required): Enter a statement that describes the departmental impact of your research.
* **University Impact Statement** (University Impact Statement, Text, Not Required): Enter a statement that describes the university impact of your research.
* **External Impact Statement** (External Impact Statement, Text, Not Required): Enter a statement that describes the external impact of your research.
* **Impact Measures** (Impact Measures, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of your impact measures or assessment metrics.
* **Impact Statement** (Impact Statement, Text, Not Required): Enter a statement that describes the impact of your research.
* **Metrics** (Metrics, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of your metrics or assessment criteria.
* **Evaluation Process** (Evaluation Process, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of your evaluation process.
* **Review Process** (Review Process, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of your review process.
* **Reporting Requirements** (Reporting Requirements, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of your reporting requirements.
* **Review Frequency** (Review Frequency, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of your review frequency.
* **Review Criterion** (Review Criterion, Text, Not Required): Provide a description of your review criterion.
* **Review Comments** (Review Comments, Text, Not Required): Enter any comments or feedback from reviewers.

Please note that some fields are marked as "Not Required", but it is still recommended to provide as much information as possible to give a comprehensive picture of your research activities and impact. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
