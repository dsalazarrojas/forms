# world_cup_2026_viewing_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from viewers about their plans and excitement levels for the World Cup 2026 matches.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions about your plans for each match, selecting one or multiple options as relevant.
2. Rate your excitement levels for each match on a scale of 0 to 10.
3. Choose your favorite player and team from the provided options.
4. Submit the form to share your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How many matches will you watch** (`title`, `number`, optional): This question asks about your overall viewing plan for the World Cup 2026. You can choose any number of matches you plan to watch.
* **What are your plans for Match 1?** (`match_1_plan`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question helps us understand how you plan to watch Match 1. Choose one or multiple options that best describe your plans.
* **On a scale of 0 to 10 how excited are you about Match 1?** (`excitement_level_1`, `number`, optional): Rate your excitement level for Match 1 on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **What are your plans for Match 2?** (`match_2_plan`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question is similar to Match 1, but for Match 2.
* **On a scale of 0 to 10 how excited are you about Match 2?** (`excitement_level_2`, `number`, optional): Rate your excitement level for Match 2 on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **What are your plans for Match 3?** (`match_3_plan`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question is similar to Match 1, but for Match 3.
* **On a scale of 0 to 10 how excited are you about Match 3?** (`excitement_level_3`, `number`, optional): Rate your excitement level for Match 3 on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Who is your favorite player?** (`favorite_player`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your favorite player from the options provided.
* **Who is your favorite team?** (`favorite_team`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your favorite team from the options provided.
* **On a scale of 0 to 10 how excited are you about the World Cup 2026?** (`overall_excitement`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall excitement level for the World Cup 2026 on a scale of 0 to 10.
