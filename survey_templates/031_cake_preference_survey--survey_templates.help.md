# Cake Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from respondents about their preferences regarding cakes, including their favorite flavors, toppings, and consumption habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field (optional).
3. Select your age group from the "Age Group" dropdown menu.
4. Choose how often you eat cake from the "How Often Do You Eat Cake" dropdown menu.
5. Select your favorite cake flavor from the "Favorite Cake Flavor" dropdown menu.
6. Choose your second favorite flavor from the "Second Favorite Flavor" dropdown menu (optional).
7. Select your favorite frosting type from the "Favorite Frosting Type" dropdown menu.
8. Choose your frosting sweetness preference from the "Frosting Sweetness Preference" dropdown menu (optional).
9. Select your preferred filling from the "Favorite Filling" dropdown menu (optional).
10. Choose your preferred cake texture from the "Cake Texture Preference" dropdown menu (optional).
11. Select your preferred cake style from the "Preferred Cake Style" dropdown menu.
12. Choose when you most enjoy eating cake from the "Favorite Cake Occasion" dropdown menu.
13. Select any dietary preferences you may have from the "Dietary Preferences" dropdown menu (optional).
14. Indicate your willingness to try new flavors from the "Willingness to Try New Flavors" dropdown menu (optional).
15. Choose how often you purchase cake from the "How Often Do You Buy Cake" dropdown menu (optional).
16. Select where you prefer to buy cake from the "Preferred Bakery Type" dropdown menu (optional).
17. Choose how important price is to you from the "Price Sensitivity" dropdown menu (optional).
18. Provide any additional cake preferences you may have in the "Additional Preferences" text field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself in the survey results (optional).
* **Email Address**: Provide your email address for follow-up or survey results (optional).
* **Age Group**: Select your age group to help us understand your demographic.
* **How Often Do You Eat Cake**: Choose how often you enjoy eating cake.
* **Favorite Cake Flavor**: Select your top choice of cake flavor.
* **Second Favorite Flavor**: Choose your second favorite flavor (optional).
* **Favorite Frosting Type**: Select your preferred type of frosting.
* **Frosting Sweetness Preference**: Choose how sweet you like your frosting (optional).
* **Favorite Filling**: Select your preferred filling (optional).
* **Cake Texture Preference**: Choose your preferred cake texture (optional).
* **Preferred Cake Style**: Select your preferred style of cake.
* **Favorite Cake Occasion**: Choose when you most enjoy eating cake.
* **Dietary Preferences**: Select any dietary preferences you may have (optional).
* **Willingness to Try New Flavors**: Indicate how willing you are to try new flavors (optional).
* **How Often Do You Buy Cake**: Choose how often you purchase cake (optional).
* **Preferred Bakery Type**: Select where you prefer to buy cake from (optional).
* **Price Sensitivity**: Choose how important price is to you (optional).
* **Additional Preferences**: Enter any other cake preferences you may have (optional).
