# Self-Worth And Connection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self-Worth And Connection Survey is designed to assess your level of self-worth and social connection. It is a set of questions that aim to understand how you perceive your relationship with yourself and others. The survey will provide you with insights into your strengths and areas for improvement in these two areas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, with the options provided.
2. For questions that are type: number, select the number that best describes your answer.
3. For questions that are type: select_one, select the option that best describes your answer.
4. For questions that are type: select_multiple, select all options that best describe your answer.
5. If you are unsure about an option, please select the one that seems most accurate.
