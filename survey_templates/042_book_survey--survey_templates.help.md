# Book Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Book Survey form aims to collect information about your reading habits, preferences, and behaviors. It is designed to gather data on various aspects of reading, including favorite genres, reading frequency, preferred formats, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the `Full Name` field (page 1).
2. Provide your contact email address in the `Email Address` field (page 1).
3. Select your age group from the `Age Group` options (page 1).
4. Answer the question about how often you read books (page 2) and choose your preferred format (page 3).
5. Indicate how many books you read per year (page 3) and where you usually read (page 4).
6. Choose how you select books (page 4) and where you get them (page 5).
7. Answer the question about your monthly book budget (page 5).
8. Select methods for discovering new books (page 6) and whether you set reading goals (page 7).
9. Confirm your book club status (page 7).
10. If desired, provide your favorite book of the year (page 7) and any challenges you face while reading (page 8).
11. Indicate how often you use the library (page 8).
12. Confirm whether you recommend books to others (page 9).
13. Provide any additional comments about reading (page 10).
14. Select the date you completed the survey (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name (page 1)**: Enter your full name in this field.
- **Email Address (page 1)**: Provide your email address for communication purposes.
- **Age Group (page 1)**: Choose your age range from the options: Under 18, 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65 Plus.
- **How Often Do You Read Books (page 2)**: Select your reading frequency: Daily, Several times a week, Once a week, 2-3 times a month, Once a month, Rarely.
- **How Often Do You Use the Library (page 8)**: Indicate how often you visit the library: Weekly, Monthly, Few times a year, Rarely, Never.
- **How Much Do You Spend on Books Monthly (page 5)**: Choose your monthly book budget: Under $20, $20-$50, $50-$100, $100-$200, Over $200.
- **Where Do You Usually Read (page 4)**: Select all locations where you usually read: At Home, During Commute, At Work, At Library, At School, Outdoors, Before Bed.
- **How Do You Choose Books (page 4)**: Select all methods you use to choose books: Recommendations from Friends, Social Media, Book Reviews, Best Seller Lists, Library Displays, Bookstore Browsing, Author Reputation, Book Clubs.
- **Where Do You Get Books (page 5)**: Select all sources where you get books: Purchase New, Purchase Used, Public Library, School Library, Borrow from Friends, Book Subscription, eBook Subscription, Audiobook Subscription.
- **How Do You Discover New Books (page 6)**: Select all methods you use to discover new books: Social Media, Book Blogs, Podcasts, Friend Recommendations, Library, Bookstore, Book Clubs, Online Reviews.
- **Do You Recommend Books to Others (page 9)**: Choose how often you recommend books: Very Often, Often, Sometimes, Rarely, Never.
- **Do You Set Reading Goals (page 7)**: Confirm whether you set reading goals: Yes every year, Sometimes, No never.
- **Are You in a Book Club (page 7)**: Confirm your book club status: Yes currently, Yes in the past, No but interested, No not interested.
- **Favorite Book You Read This Year (page 7)**: If desired, provide the title and author of your favorite book of the year.
- **What Challenges Do You Face Reading (page 8)**: Select any challenges you face while reading: Not enough time, Too expensive, Difficulty concentrating, Limited book access, Eye strain, No challenges.
- **Favorite Book Genres (page 3)**: Choose your favorite book genres: Fiction, Mystery, Thriller, Romance, Science Fiction, Fantasy, Historical Fiction, Non-Fiction, Biography, Self-Help, Business, History, Science, Children's Books, Young Adult.
- **Preferred Reading Format (page 3)**: Select your preferred reading format: Physical Hardcover, Physical Paperback, eBook, Audiobook, No preference.
- **How Many Books Do You Read Per Year (page 3)**: Choose your reading rate: 0-5 books, 6-10 books, 11-20 books, 21-30 books, Over 30 books.
- **Survey Date (page 10)**: Date of survey completion.
- **Favorite Book Genres (page 3)**: Choose your favorite book genres; you can select multiple genres.
- **Additional Comments (page 10)**: Provide any other comments about reading.

Note: Please fill in all fields as accurately as possible.
