# Ethical Consumerism Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to understand consumers' awareness and behaviors related to ethical consumerism. The results will help businesses understand consumer attitudes and make informed decisions that promote responsible purchasing practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your genuine feelings and experiences.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best reflects your attitude or behavior.
3. For questions that ask you to select multiple options, choose all that apply.
4. Take your time and answer each question to the best of your ability.
