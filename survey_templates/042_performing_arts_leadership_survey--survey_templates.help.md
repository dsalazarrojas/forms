# Performing Arts Leadership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about performing arts leadership, including job titles, performance history, and performance measures. Your participation will help us understand the needs of performing arts leaders and inform future training and development programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Select your current leadership role (Executive Director, Department Head, or Other) in the "role" field.
4. Enter the number of years you have been in the leadership position in the "years experience" field (if applicable).
5. Select your highest level of education (Bachelor, Master, or PhD) in the "highest degree" field (if applicable).
6. Enter the name of your organization in the "organization" field.
7. Enter the name of your department in the "department" field.
8. Enter your job title in the "job title" field.
9. Enter the city or location where you work in the "location" field.
10. Select your performance history (Often, Occasionally, Rarely) in the "performance history" field.
11. Select the frequency of your performances (Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly) in the "performance frequency" field.
12. Enter the type of performances you are responsible for (e.g. concerts, plays, dance productions) in the "performance type" field.
13. Indicate if you have leadership experience (True or False) in the "leadership experience" field.
14. Indicate if you have participated in a leadership development program (True or False) in the "leadership development program" field.
15. Describe your performance evaluation process in the "performance evaluation process" field.
16. Select the frequency of your performance evaluations (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly) in the "performance evaluation frequency" field.
17. Enter your budgeted funds for performances in the "budgeted funds" field.
18. Enter your actual funds spent on performances in the "actual funds" field.
19. Describe the variance between your budgeted and actual funds in the "budget variance" field.
20. Enter your performance goals in the "performance goal" field.
21. Select the frequency of your performance evaluations (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly) in the "evaluation frequency" field.
22. Enter your performance measures in the "performance measures" field.
23. Describe any leadership support you receive in the "leadership support" field.
24. Enter any additional comments about your leadership role in the "additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Role** (select_one, required): Select your current leadership role (Executive Director, Department Head, or Other).
* **Years Experience** (number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been in the leadership position.
* **Highest Degree** (select_multiple, optional): Select your highest level of education (Bachelor, Master, or PhD).
* **Organization** (text, optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Department** (text, optional): Enter the name of your department.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Location** (text, optional): Enter the city or location where you work.
* **Performance History** (time, optional): Select your performance history (Often, Occasionally, Rarely).
* **Performance Frequency** (select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of your performances (Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly).
* **Performance Type** (text, optional): Enter the type of performances you are responsible for (e.g. concerts, plays, dance productions).
* **Leadership Experience** (select_one, required): Indicate if you have leadership experience (True or False).
* **Leadership Development Program** (select_one, required): Indicate if you have participated in a leadership development program (True or False).
* **Performance Evaluation Process** (text, optional): Describe your performance evaluation process.
* **Performance Evaluation Frequency** (select_one, required): Select the frequency of your performance evaluations (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly).
* **Budgeted Funds** (number, optional): Enter your budgeted funds for performances.
* **Actual Funds** (number, optional): Enter your actual funds spent on performances.
* **Budget Variance** (text, optional): Describe the variance between your budgeted and actual funds.
* **Performance Goal** (text, optional): Enter your performance goals.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (select_one, required): Select the frequency of your performance evaluations (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly).
* **Performance Measures** (text, optional): Enter your performance measures.
* **Leadership Support** (text, optional): Describe any leadership support you receive.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your leadership role.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and honest as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please contact us.
* Your participation is crucial in helping us better understand performing arts leadership and inform future training and development programs.
