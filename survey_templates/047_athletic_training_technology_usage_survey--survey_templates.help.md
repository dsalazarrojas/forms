# Athletic Training Technology Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from athletic trainers, sports medicine physicians, physical therapists, strength and conditioning coaches, sports coaches, administrators, students, and researchers on their usage and perceptions of various technologies in the field of athletic training. The information collected will help understand the current state of technology usage and its impact on patient care and efficiency in the athletic training field.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Demographic Information**: Enter your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. **Professional Information**: Select your role and organization type from the provided options.
3. **Technology Usage**: Answer the questions on how often you use various technologies (EHR, Injury Tracking Software, Video Analysis Tools, Wearable Technology) on a scale of Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.
4. **Perceptions**: Rate your satisfaction, effectiveness, ease of use, and training adequacy of the technologies you use.
5. **Future Interests**: Select the technologies you are interested in exploring in the future.
6. **Additional Comments**: Share any additional thoughts or comments on technology usage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Respondent Name**: Your first and last name.
- **Email Address**: Your contact email address.
- **Role**: Select your role from the provided options (Athletic Trainer, Sports Medicine Physician, Physical Therapist, etc.).
- **Organization Type**: Select your organization type from the provided options (High School, University, Hospital, etc.).
- **Sport Focus**: Select the sport you primarily work with from the provided options (Football, Basketball, etc.).
- **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been working in your current role.
- **Survey Date**: Enter the date you completed this survey.
- **Technology Categories Used**: Select all the technologies you use from the provided options (EHR, Injury Tracking Software, etc.).
- **EHR Usage**: Rate how often you use EHR systems on a scale of Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.
- **Injury Tracking Software Usage**: Rate how often you use injury tracking software on a scale of Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.
- **Video Analysis Tools Usage**: Rate how often you use video analysis tools on a scale of Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.
- **Wearable Technology Usage**: Rate how often you use wearable technology on a scale of Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.
- **Overall Technology Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the technologies you use on a scale of Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
- **Technology Effectiveness**: Rate how effective you find the technologies you use on a scale of Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, Very Ineffective.
- **Ease of Use**: Rate how easy or difficult you find the technologies you use on a scale of Very Easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, Very Difficult.
- **Technology Training Received**: Rate how much training you received on the technologies you use on a scale of Comprehensive Training, Basic Training, Minimal Training, No Training, Self-Taught.
- **Technology Adequacy**: Rate how adequate the training you received was on a scale of More Than Adequate, Adequate, Somewhat Inadequate, Very Inadequate, Not Applicable.
- **Barriers to Adoption**: Select all the barriers you face to adopting new technologies on a scale of Cost, Lack of Training, Technical Complexity, Time Constraints, Resistance to Change, Lack of Evidence, Privacy Concerns, Limited Access, No Barriers.
- **Technology Budget**: Rate your annual budget for technology on a scale of Under $1000, $1000 to $5000, $5000 to $10000, $10000 to $50000, Over $50000, No Dedicated Budget.
- **Future Technology Interests**: Select the technologies you are interested in from the provided options (AI-Powered Injury Prediction, etc.).
- **Data Privacy Concerns**: Rate how concerned you are about data privacy on a scale of Very Concerned, Concerned, Neutral, Not Very Concerned, Not Concerned At All.
- **Technology Impact on Patient Care**: Rate how technology has impacted patient care on a scale of Significantly Improved, Moderately Improved, Slightly Improved, No Impact, Negative Impact.
- **Technology Impact on Efficiency**: Rate how technology has impacted your efficiency on a scale of Significantly Improved, Moderately Improved, Slightly Improved, No Impact, Decreased Efficiency.
- **Most Valuable Technology**: Enter the technology that is most valuable to you.
- **Least Valuable Technology**: Enter the technology that is least valuable to you (optional).
- **Technology Recommendations**: Enter any technologies you would recommend.
- **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts on technology usage. 

Note: All fields are required, except **Least Valuable Technology**, **Technology Recommendations**, and **Additional Comments**, which are optional.
