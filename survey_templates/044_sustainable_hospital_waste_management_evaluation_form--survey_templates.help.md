# Sustainable Hospital Waste Management Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the current state of waste management in hospitals, focusing on identifying areas for improvement and suggesting recommendations for better waste management practices, processes, and policies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly and carefully.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
3. Navigate through the form's multiple pages, filling out each page completely before proceeding to the next.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1: Sustainable**
    (Select one):
    • **Yes** – Your hospital has a comprehensive waste management policy in place.
    • **No** – Your hospital does not have a waste management policy.
* **Page 2:**
    (Text input):
    Please describe your hospital's current waste management practices and procedures.
* **Page 3: Multiple Choice**
    (Select all that apply):
    • **Yes** – Staff is trained on waste management practices.
    • **No** – Staff is not trained on waste management practices.
* **Page 4: Time**
    (Time input):
    Please estimate the average time it takes for your hospital to process and manage waste per day.
* **Page 5: Date**
    (Date input):
    Please provide the date of your hospital's last waste management audit or review.

**Tips**

* Be honest and accurate in your answers to ensure the results are reliable and comprehensive.
* If you need help or clarification on any question, please ask.
* Please fill out each page completely before proceeding to the next page to ensure accurate results.
