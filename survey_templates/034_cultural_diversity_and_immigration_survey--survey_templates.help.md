# Cultural Diversity And Immigration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to aid in completing the Cultural Diversity And Immigration Survey form efficiently and accurately. The form is intended to collect information on cultural identity and immigration status of individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the survey.
* Fill out the form carefully and accurately, taking care to fill out all required fields.
* For select multiple fields (e.g., Language spoken at home, Languages spoken proficient), select all relevant options that apply.
* For numerical fields (e.g., Age, Work experience years, Immigration year), enter numbers only.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
* **First name**: This field is for your first name.
### Last name
* **Last name**: This field is for your last name.
### Email
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
### Phone number
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
### Age
* **Age**: Enter your current age.
### Birth date
* **Birth date**: Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
### Birth time
* **Birth time**: Enter your time of birth in the format hh:mm:ss.
### Gender
* **Gender**: Select your gender: Male, Female, Non-binary.
### Ethnicity
* **Ethnicity**: Please fill out this field according to your cultural identity.
### Country of birth
* **Country of birth**: Select where you were born.
### Country of residence
* **Country of residence**: Select where you currently reside.
### Language spoken at home
* **Language spoken at home**: Select all languages you can converse in at home.
### Languages spoken proficient
* **Languages spoken proficient**: Select all languages you are proficient in.
### Immigration status
* **Immigration status**: Select your current immigration status: Active, Inactive.
### Immigration year
* **Immigration year**: Enter the year you immigrated to your current country.

* **Years in country**: Enter the number of years you have lived in your current country.
