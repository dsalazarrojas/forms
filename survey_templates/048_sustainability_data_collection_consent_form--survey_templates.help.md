# Sustainability Data Collection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your engagement with sustainability practices, including your participation in initiatives, goals, and data collection methods. This will help us better understand our customers' or users' level of engagement with sustainability efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Scroll down to the first page (Introduction) and read through the form's purpose and instructions.
2. Review each page and answer the questions accordingly.
3. Provide your contact details and answer any questions that pertain to your engagement with sustainability practices.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction**: This is a text-based page that explains the form's purpose and instructions.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details**: This field is to collect your contact information, such as your name and email. Please provide your details if you wish to participate.

### Sustainability Initiatives
* **sustainability initiatives**: This field asks you to select all the initiatives you are currently participating in or have participated in, such as "Yes" or "No". Check all the options that apply to you.

### Sustainability Commitments
* **sustainability commitments**: This field asks you to select all the commitments you have made or are willing to make to sustainability practices, such as "Yes" or "No". Check all the options that apply to you.

### Sustainability Goals
* **sustainability goals**: This field asks you to select all the goals you have set or are working towards, such as "Yes" or "No". Check all the options that apply to you.

### Data Collection Frequency
* **Data Collection Frequency**: This field asks you to select how often you are willing to provide data to support sustainability efforts. Please select the frequency that best applies to you.

### Data Collection Methods
* **Data Collection Methods**: This field asks you to select all the methods you are comfortable with for data collection, such as "Yes" or "No". Check all the options that apply to you.

### Data Protection
* **Data Protection**: This field is an explanation of our data protection policy.

### Terms of Service
* **Terms of Service**: This field is an explanation of our terms of service.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or questions you may have.

Note: Please provide your consent by checking "Yes" in the consent field.
