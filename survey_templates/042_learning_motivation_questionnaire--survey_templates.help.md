# learning_motivation_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your motivation and preferences for learning. The collected data will be used to identify areas of interest for educational programs or resources.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow these steps:
  1. Read the form carefully and understand the purpose and questions asked.
  2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
  3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **what_motivates_you** (`what_motivates_you`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to describe what motivates you to learn or participate in learning activities.
  - Please type your answer in the provided text field.
- **what_do_you_prefer** (`what_do_you_prefer`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select multiple options that appeal to you.
  - Choose all that apply from the provided options.
- **what_motivation_level** (`what_motivation_level`, number, optional): This field is to rate your motivation level on a scale.
  - Select a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your motivation level.
- **submission** (`submission`, note, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information or comments.
  - Type your comments or thoughts in the provided text field.
- **class_or_program** (`class_or_program`, select_one, optional): This field is to select a preferred class or program.
  - Choose one option from the provided selection.
- **email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for you to enter your email address.
  - Type a valid email address.
- **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your phone number.
  - Type a valid phone number.
- **date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is for you to select a date.
  - Choose a valid date.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required fields.
- Be honest and thorough in your answers.
- If you have any questions, contact our support team for assistance.
