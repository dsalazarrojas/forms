# Marketing Campaign Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather insights on the effectiveness of a marketing campaign to help us refine and improve future campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the questions carefully and answer them honestly.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in your responses.
3. Do not skip any questions, but feel free to skip questions that don't apply to your situation.
4. Use the space provided to elaborate on your answers if needed.
5. If you're unsure about any question, use your best judgment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the main objective of this campaign?**: Please describe the main goal or objective of the campaign. This could include the target audience, products/services promoted, or specific business outcomes aimed for.
* **What is your job title?**: Provide your job title to help us understand your role in the campaign.
* **What is your role in this project?**: Choose from the available options: Project Manager, Marketing Manager, or Other. This will help us understand your involvement in the campaign.
* **What is your role in this organization?**: Select all that apply from the options: Sales, Marketing, Product, or Other. This will help us understand your department's involvement in the campaign.
* **When did this campaign start?**: Provide the start date of the campaign.
* **When did this campaign end?**: Provide the end date of the campaign.
* **What was the primary source of leads for this campaign?**: Please specify the primary source of leads for this campaign.
* **How many leads generated this campaign?**: Enter the number of leads generated by the campaign.
* **How would you rate the campaign's performance?**: Choose from the available options: Goals, Results, or Results Prophets.
* **Was your team involved in this campaign?**: If yes, please provide the team members' names.
* **Who is your go-to contact for this campaign?**: Provide the contact information for the person you worked with during the campaign.
* **What is the budget for this campaign?**: Select all that apply from the options: Under 50,000, 50,000-200,000, or Over 200,000.
* **How would you rate your campaign experience?**: Enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **How long would you rate the campaign length?**: Enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the shortest and 5 is the longest.
* **How many campaigns are you running currently?**: Select all that apply from the options: One campaign, Multiple campaigns, or None.
* **How much was your team involved in this campaign?**: Enter a number from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **How did you hear about this campaign?**: Select all that apply from the options: Social media, Email, Word of mouth, or Other.
* **Would you recommend this campaign to others?**: Enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What would you like to see as the next campaign?**: Provide your suggestions for the next campaign.
* **Have you been involved in other similar campaigns?**: Enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **How did this campaign help you?**: Provide your personal experiences and benefits from the campaign.
* **How did you use the campaign?**: Describe how you used the campaign materials or assets.
* **Have you been using this campaign?**: Provide your current usage of the campaign materials or assets.
* **How did this campaign help your organization?**: Provide the benefits and outcomes your organization gained from the campaign.
* **How did this campaign help your organization? (again)**: Provide the benefits and outcomes your organization gained from the campaign. (Duplicate question, please ignore)
