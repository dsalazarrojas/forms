# Antibiotic Use Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information on your recent antibiotic use and your attitudes towards antibiotics. Your responses will help us improve our understanding of antibiotic use and attitudes among our users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form only if you have used antibiotics in the past 12 months.
2. Select "True" if you have used antibiotics and fill out the remaining fields.
3. Select "False" if you haven't used antibiotics and do not fill out the remaining fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have you used antibiotics in the past 12 months** (used_antibiotics_recently):
	+ A yes/no question to determine if you have used antibiotics recently.
	+ If you answered yes, fill out the remaining fields.
* **Reason for antibiotic use** (reason_for_use):
	+ Please enter the health condition or diagnosis that led to your antibiotic use.
* **Did you complete the prescribed course** (completed_course):
	+ Please select True if you completed the full course of antibiotics as prescribed.
	+ You can also select "Not applicable" if you don't have a full course to complete.
* **Where did you get the prescription** (source_of_prescription):
	+ Choose where you received your antibiotic prescription:
		- General practitioner
		- Specialist
		- Emergency care
		- Pharmacy or over the counter
		- Other (please specify)
* **Awareness of antibiotic resistance** (knowledge_of_resistance):
	+ Please rate your awareness of antibiotic resistance:
		- Unaware
		- Somewhat aware
		- Aware
		- Very aware
* **Attitude toward receiving an antibiotic prescription** (attitude_toward_prescribing):
	+ Please select your attitude towards receiving an antibiotic prescription:
		- Prefer to avoid antibiotics
		- Neutral
		- Prefer to receive antibiotics
* **Suggestions for improving antibiotic stewardship** (suggestions_for_stewardship):
	+ Please provide any suggestions you have for improving antibiotic stewardship in the community.
* **Additional notes** (section_antibiotic_use):
	+ This is a general space for any additional comments or notes you would like to share.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form accurately and honestly.
- If you have any further comments or suggestions, you can include them in the "Additional notes" section.
- Your responses will help us better understand your experiences and attitudes towards antibiotics.
