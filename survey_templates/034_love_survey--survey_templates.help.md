## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your feelings, preferences, and habits related to online love and relationships. It's meant to be a fun and lighthearted way to understand your opinions and experiences in this area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to each question.
3. If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is accurate.
