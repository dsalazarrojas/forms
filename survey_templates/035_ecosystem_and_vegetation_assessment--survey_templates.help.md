# Ecosystem And Vegetation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ecosystem And Vegetation Assessment form is designed to collect information on the assessment of ecosystems and vegetation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, fill out all required fields and check relevant optional fields according to your needs. Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and relevant to the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (#1)**: This field collects general information, provide a brief description of the project or study. If this field is not required, you can leave it blank.
* **Species (#2)**: This field asks whether species are present or not. Choose 'Yes' if species are observed, otherwise choose 'No'.
* **Habitat (#3)**: This field asks whether the habitat of the species is affected or not. Choose 'Yes' if the habitat is affected, otherwise choose 'No'.
* **Region (#4)**: In this field, enter the region where the assessment is taking place.
* **Land Use (#5)**: This field asks whether the land use is affecting the ecosystem or not. Choose 'Yes' if land use is affecting the ecosystem, otherwise choose 'No'.
* **Date Observed (#6)**: Enter the date when the assessment was done.
* **Time Observed (#7)**: Enter the time when the assessment was done.
* **Note (#8)**: This field is for any additional notes you want to include in the assessment.
* **Assigned Tool (#9)**: Enter the tool used for this assessment (e.g. "Camera", "Binoculars", etc.).
* **Assigned Technician (#10)**: Enter the name of the technician who conducted this assessment.
* **Assigned Organization (#11)**: Enter the name of the organization where this assessment was conducted.

## Tips
This form is designed to collect data for the assessment of ecosystems and vegetation. Ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information to the best of your abilities. If you have any further questions or need clarification on any field, please refer to your project leader or team leader.
