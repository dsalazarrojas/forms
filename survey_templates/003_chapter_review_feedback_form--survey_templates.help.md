# Chapter Review Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chapter Review Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from attendees on their experience at chapter meetings. This form helps the chapter organizers understand what works well and what needs improvement to create a more engaging and valuable experience for attendees in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out your personal information, including your name, email address, and the chapter meeting you attended.
2. Provide feedback on the meeting's organization, content, and speakers.
3. Rate the meeting's overall quality and your level of satisfaction.
4. Share any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
5. Indicate your interest in attending future chapter meetings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (id: 2, required): Your name, as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (id: 3, required): Your email address, where we can contact you.
* **Chapter Meeting Attended** (id: 4, required): The name or number of the chapter meeting you attended.
* **Date of Meeting** (id: 5, required): The date of the chapter meeting you attended.
* **First Time Attending This Chapter** (id: 6, required): Select the correct option to indicate if this is your first time attending the chapter.
* **Organization and Structure of Meeting** (id: 8, required): Rate the meeting's organization and structure from Excellent to Poor.
* **Relevance of Content Discussed** (id: 9, required): Rate the relevance of the meeting's content from Very Relevant to Not Relevant.
* **Quality of Speakers or Presenters** (id: 10, optional): Rate the quality of the speakers or presenters from Excellent to Poor.
* **Was the Meeting Length Appropriate** (id: 11, required): Select the correct option to indicate if the meeting was too long, just right, or too short.
* **Venue Comfort and Facilities** (id: 12, optional): Rate the meeting venue and facilities from Excellent to Poor.
* **Opportunity to Network with Others** (id: 14, required): Select the correct option to indicate the level of networking opportunity you had.
* **Quality of Discussion and Q&A** (id: 15, optional): Rate the quality of the discussion and Q&A from Very Good to Fair.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (id: 16, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (id: 17, optional): Share any suggestions or comments for future meetings.
* **Will You Attend Future Chapter Meetings** (id: 18, required): Select the correct option to indicate if you intend to attend future chapter meetings.
* **Additional Comments** (id: 19, optional): Share any other feedback or comments.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.
* Select the correct option for each field, as it helps us understand your experience better.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, feel free to share them in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
