# Broadcasting Test Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with a broadcast test. Your input will help us improve the service and ensure a better experience for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date when the broadcast test was conducted in the "Test Date" field.
2. Select the time when the broadcast test took place in the "Test Time" field.
3. Enter the location where the broadcast test was conducted in the "Test Location" field.
4. Select the type of broadcast test performed in the "Broadcast Test Type" field.
5. Rate the video quality from 1 to 5 in the "Video Quality Rating" field.
6. Rate the audio quality from 1 to 5 in the "Audio Quality Rating" field.
7. Rate the stream stability from 1 to 5 in the "Stream Stability Rating" field.
8. Rate the latency from 1 to 5 in the "Latency Rating" field.
9. Select the devices you used to view the test in the "Device Used for Viewing" field.
10. Select the platform used to access the broadcast in the "Platform Used" field.
11. Select the type of internet connection used in the "Internet Connection Type" field.
12. Select any issues you experienced during the test in the "Issues Experienced" field.
13. Describe any issues in detail in the "Issues Description" field.
14. Rate your overall satisfaction with the broadcast service in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.
15. Rate your likelihood to recommend the service in the "Likelihood to Recommend" field.
16. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.
17. Enter your name for follow-up purposes in the "Tester Name" field.
18. Enter your email for follow-up purposes in the "Tester Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Test Date** (Date, required): Enter the date when the broadcast test was conducted.
- **Test Time** (Time, required): Select the time when the broadcast test took place.
- **Test Location** (Text, required): Enter the location where the broadcast test was conducted.
- **Broadcast Test Type** (Select One, required): Select the type of broadcast test performed.
- **Video Quality Rating** (Select One, required): Rate the overall video quality from 1 to 5.
- **Audio Quality Rating** (Select One, required): Rate the overall audio quality from 1 to 5.
- **Stream Stability Rating** (Select One, required): Rate how stable the stream was during the test.
- **Latency Rating** (Select One, required): Rate the delay in broadcast transmission.
- **Device Used for Viewing** (Select Multiple, required): Select the devices you used to view the test.
- **Platform Used** (Select One, required): Select the platform used to access the broadcast.
- **Internet Connection Type** (Select One, optional): Select the type of internet connection used.
- **Issues Experienced** (Select Multiple, required): Select any issues you experienced during the test.
- **Issues Description** (Text, optional): Describe any issues in detail.
- **Overall Satisfaction** (Select One, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the broadcast service.
- **Likelihood to Recommend** (Select One, required): Rate your likelihood to recommend the service.
- **Additional Comments** (Text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
- **Tester Name** (Text, optional): Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
- **Tester Email** (Email, optional): Enter your email for follow-up purposes.
