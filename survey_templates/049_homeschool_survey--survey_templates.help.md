# Homeschool Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from homeschooling students and their parents/guardians to gather feedback and data that can be used to improve the homeschooling experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
3. Select only relevant options for each field.
4. Double-check your email address for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Homeschool Survey Questions** (`homeschool_survey_questions`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or questions you have about the homeschooling program.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the interests that apply to you (e.g. Arts and Crafts, Drama, Music, Science).
* **Parent Interests** (`parent_interests`, select_one, optional): Please select the interest that best describes you (e.g. Arts and Crafts, Drama, Music, Science).
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the homeschooling program.
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, number, optional): Please rate your teacher's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Parent Comments** (`parent_comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the teacher's performance.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please enter the time you completed this form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with further questions or information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your attendance in the homeschooling program.
