# Youth And Eternity Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth And Eternity Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from individuals about their experience with the "Youth And Eternity" program. This form is intended to be completed by program participants to provide their thoughts, feelings, and feedback on the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read the introduction on the first page.
* Select your response to the question "How were you feeling?" on the second page.
* Write any comments or suggestions you have about the program on the third page.
* Fill in your name, email address, and age on the following pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This page provides a brief overview of the "Youth And Eternity" program. Please read this page to understand the context of the program before proceeding.
* **How Were You Feeling?** (`how_were_you_feeling`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that best describe how you were feeling during the program. You can choose one or more of the following options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Any Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Write any thoughts, suggestions, or feedback you have about the program. This is a free-text field where you can express your opinions and ideas.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Fill in your name to help us identify you in the feedback.
* **Name (Email)** (`name_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Name (Phone)** (`name_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required: false): Fill in your age to help us understand your demographics.
* **Age Date** (`age_date`, `date`, required: false): This field is not clearly defined in the YAML, so please disregard it. It seems to be a duplicate of the "Age" field.
