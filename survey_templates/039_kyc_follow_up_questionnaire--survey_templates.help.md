# kyc_follow_up_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a follow-up questionnaire for Know Your Customer (KYC) purposes, designed to collect additional information about an individual's or entity's beneficial ownership and control structure. It is intended to help our team assess the risk associated with a business or individual and ensure compliance with relevant regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the instructions and ensure you are familiar with the questions and fields.
2. Enter your information in the provided fields, following the required and optional fields marked.
3. Answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
4. Once you've completed the form, click submit to submit your response.
