# City Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect comprehensive information about cities, including their demographics, economy, and attractions. This data will help users make informed decisions and ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "City Identification" page, which provides an overview of the city's details.
2. Enter the official name of the city in the "City Name" field.
3. Select the country the city is located in from the "Country" dropdown menu.
4. If applicable, provide the state or province the city is located in in the "State or Province" field.
5. Proceed to the "Demographics Information" page, where you can provide information about the city's demographics.
6. Enter the current population of the city in the "Current Population" field.
7. Provide the annual population growth rate as a percentage in the "Population Growth Rate - percent" field.
8. Enter the median age of the city's residents in the "Median Age" field.
9. List the major ethnic groups that reside in the city, separated by commas, in the "Major Ethnic Groups" field.
10. Proceed to the "Economic Information" page, where you can provide economic details about the city.
11. List the major industries that drive the city's economy in the "Major Industries" field.
12. Enter the unemployment rate as a percentage in the "Unemployment Rate - percent" field.
13. Provide the average household income in the city in the "Average Household Income" field.
14. Proceed to the "Key Attractions and Features" page, where you can provide information about the city's attractions.
15. List the major tourist attractions in the city, separated by commas, in the "Major Tourist Attractions" field.
16. Select the primary climate type of the city from the "Primary Climate Type" dropdown menu.
17. Enter the year the city was founded in the "Year City Was Founded" field.
18. Select the government type of the city from the "Government Type" dropdown menu.
19. Provide any additional relevant information about the city in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **City Name** (`city_name`, text, required): Enter the official name of the city.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Select the country where the city is located.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, text, optional): Enter the state or province the city is located in (if applicable).
* **Current Population** (`population`, number, required): Enter the total number of residents in the city.
* **Population Growth Rate - percent** (`population_growth_rate`, number, optional): Provide the annual population growth rate as a percentage.
* **Median Age** (`median_age`, number, optional): Enter the median age of the city's residents.
* **Major Ethnic Groups** (`major_ethnic_groups`, text, optional): List the major ethnic groups that reside in the city, separated by commas.
* **Primary Languages Spoken** (`primary_languages`, text, optional): List the primary languages spoken in the city.
* **Major Industries** (`major_industries`, text, optional): List the major industries that drive the city's economy.
* **Unemployment Rate - percent** (`unemployment_rate`, number, optional): Enter the unemployment rate as a percentage.
* **Average Household Income** (`avg_household_income`, number, optional): Provide the average household income in the city in the local currency.
* **Primary Climate Type** (`climate`, select_one, optional): Select the primary climate type of the city (Tropical, Subtropical, Temperate, Continental, Arid, Polar).
* **Year City Was Founded** (`founding_year`, number, optional): Enter the year the city was founded.
* **Government Type** (`government_type`, select_one, optional): Select the government type of the city (Monarchy, Democracy, Republic, Other).
* **Major Tourist Attractions** (`major_attractions`, text, optional): List the major tourist attractions in the city, separated by commas.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information about the city.
