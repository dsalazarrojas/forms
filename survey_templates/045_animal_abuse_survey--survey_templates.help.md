# Animal Abuse Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect data on animal abuse and neglect incidents, awareness of local animal welfare laws, and opinions on education and penalties for animal abuse. Your responses will help us better understand the prevalence of animal abuse, identify knowledge gaps among community members, and inform policy decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions based on your personal experiences and observations.
2. If you have witnessed animal abuse or neglect in the past year, select 'True' in question 4.
3. Select all that apply in question 5 for the types of abuse you have observed.
4. Answer questions 8-11 based on your experience of reporting the incident.
5. For questions 10 and 13, please choose the option that best describes your level of awareness or support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Group or General Location**: This question asks for your age group or the city/state you reside in. Please enter a range or a specific location.
* **Have you witnessed animal abuse or neglect in the past year?**: Indicate if you have witnessed animal abuse or neglect within the past year. Please select 'True', 'False', or 'Unsure'.
* **Types of Abuse Observed**: If you have witnessed animal abuse or neglect, select all that apply from the types of abuse listed below:
	+ Physical Violence
	+ Neglect (No food / water)
	+ Abandonment
	+ Animal Hoarding
	+ Dog Fighting
	+ Overworking
* **Frequency of Observations**: If you have witnessed animal abuse or neglect, select the frequency of your observations:
	+ Frequently
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Once
* **Did you report the incident to authorities?**: If you reported the incident, select 'True'. If you didn't report, select 'False' or 'Intended to but didn't'.
* **If not reported, what was the primary reason?**: If you didn't report the incident, select the primary reason from the options below:
	+ Didn't know who to call
	+ Feared retaliation
	+ Thought it wasn't serious enough
	+ Didn't want to get involved
* **Level of awareness regarding local animal welfare laws**: Select the option that best describes your level of awareness:
	+ Well Informed
	+ Somewhat Informed
	+ Not Informed
* **Adequacy of current penalties for animal abuse**: Select the option that best describes your opinion:
	+ Too Lenient
	+ Just Right
	+ Too Harsh
* **Support for mandatory humane education in schools**: Select the option that best describes your level of support:
	+ Strongly Support
	+ Support
	+ Oppose
	+ Strongly Oppose
* **Suggestions for reducing animal neglect in your community**: Enter any suggestions you have for reducing animal neglect in your community.
