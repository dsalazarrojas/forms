# Teen Stress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Teen Stress Survey is designed to help identify potential sources of stress in a teenager's life. By providing honest and accurate information, you will be able to understand how different areas of your life are contributing to your stress levels.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as you can about your feelings and experiences.
3. If you are unsure about a particular question, try to answer based on your overall impression or how you typically feel in that situation.
4. Remember that this form is confidential and anonymous, so feel free to be as honest as you want.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, required/optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read through this section carefully and let us know if you have any questions or concerns.
* **School** (`school`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your feelings and experiences related to school. Think about your classes, teachers, and overall school environment.
* **Home Life** (`home life`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your feelings and experiences related to your home life, including your relationships with your family and friends.
* **Relationships** (`relationships`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your relationships with others, including friends, family, and romantic partners.
* **Health** (`health`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your physical and mental health, including any health concerns or issues you've experienced.
* **Finances** (`finances`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your financial situation, including money worries or concerns.
* **Self-Confidence** (`self_confidence`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your feelings of self-worth and confidence, including any self-doubt or low self-esteem.
* **Academic Pressure** (`academic_pressure`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about the stress you feel from academic expectations or pressures, including homework, grades, and exams.
* **Peer Pressure** (`peer_pressure`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about the influence of your peers on your feelings and experiences, including social media, peer relationships, and group dynamics.
* **Family Support** (`family_support`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about the support you receive from your family, including physical, emotional, and financial support.
* **Parent Involvement** (`parent_involve`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question is asking about your parent's involvement in your life, including how often you interact with them and how supportive they are.
* **School Support** (`school_support`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about the support you receive from your school, including teachers, counselors, and administrators.
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about the support you receive from your teachers, including feedback, guidance, and encouragement.
* **Peers Support** (`peers_support`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about the support you receive from your peers, including friends and classmate relationships.
* **Parents Support** (`parents_support`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about the support you receive from your parents, including physical, emotional, and financial support.
* **Friends Support** (`friends_support`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about the support you receive from your friends, including emotional and social support.
* **School Climate** (`school_climate`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about the overall feeling and atmosphere of your school, including safety, inclusivity, and overall environment.
* **Family Climate** (`family_climate`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about the overall feeling and atmosphere of your home, including relationships, communication, and overall environment.
* **Parent-Child Interaction** (`parent_child`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your relationships with your parents, including quality of interaction, communication, and conflict resolution.
* **Teacher-Child Interaction** (`teacher_child`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your relationships with your teachers, including quality of interaction, communication, and feedback.
* **Peers-Child Interaction** (`peers_child`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your relationships with your peers, including friendships, conflicts, and interactions.
* **Family-Child Interaction** (`family_child`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your relationships with your family members, including quality of interaction, communication, and conflict resolution.
* **School Environment** (`school_environment`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your school's overall environment, including safety, cleanliness, and overall feel.
* **Teacher Environment** (`teacher_environment`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your teacher's overall environment, including office hours, communication, and overall feel.
* **Peers Environment** (`peers_environment`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your peers' overall environment, including social dynamics, group projects, and overall feel.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Try to answer based on your overall feelings and experiences, rather than specific incidents.
* Remember that this form is confidential and anonymous, so feel free to be as honest as you want.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
