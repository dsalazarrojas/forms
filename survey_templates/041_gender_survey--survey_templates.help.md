# Gender Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about your perspective on gender identity and expression. Your responses will help us understand the community's view on these topics and enable us to better serve and support individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Choose the correct gender identity options that apply to you from the first field.
2. Select the most accurate expression of your gender from the second field.
3. Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have on the topics.
4. If you have a phone number, please provide it for follow-up contact.
5. If you have an email address, please enter it so we can reach out to you.
6. Finally, submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gender Identity** (`gender_identity`, select_multiple, required/optional):
    + Choose any of the following options that apply to you: Androgynous, Cisgender, Genderqueer, Genderfluid, Male, Non-binary, Other, Transgender, Two-spirit, Woman
* **Gender Expression** (`gender_expression`, select_one, required/optional):
    + Select the most accurate description of your expression: Masculine, Masculine and Feminine, Feminine, Non-binary, Transmasculine, Transfeminine
* **Gender Role** (`gender_role`, text, required/optional):
    + Describe your gender role in as much detail as you like
* **Gender Awareness** (`gender_awareness`, number, required/optional):
    + If you are aware of any gender-specific issues or concerns, please describe them here
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional):
    + Leave any additional comments or thoughts you have about gender identity and expression
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional):
    + If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your phone number
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional):
    + Enter your email address so we can reach out to you for follow-up or to provide more information
