# new_beverage_flavor_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_beverage_flavor_feedback_survey form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about new beverages. This includes information about the flavor, rating, and other characteristics of the new beverage.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in all the required fields, including the new_beverage_flavor_description field.
- Rate the flavor of the new beverage using the options provided in the flavor_rating field.
- Select the relevant options for Recommended For and Age Group fields.
- Enter your email address in the Email field.
- Choose your agree level with the options in the Agree field.
- Select the relevant options for New Beverage Image Url and New Beverage Video Url fields.
- Choose the submit option in the Submit field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **New Beverage Flavor Description**: Enter a brief description of the new beverage.
- **Flavor Rating**: Rate the flavor of the new beverage using the options provided.
- **Recommended For**: Select the groups of people that the new beverage is recommended for.
- **Email**: Enter your email address to receive a confirmation of your submission.
- **Age Group**: Select your age group.
- **Agree**: Rate your level of agreement with the options provided.
- **New Beverage Image Url**: Select the relevant options for the image URL of the new beverage.
- **New Beverage Video Url**: Select the relevant options for the video URL of the new beverage.
- **Submit**: Choose the submit option to complete the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that your submission is complete.
- Double-check the options selected to ensure they are accurate.
- If you have any other comments or feedback, use the notes or other comments field to provide additional information.
