# Corporate Leadership Readiness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Leadership Readiness Survey is designed to help corporate leaders assess their leadership skills, identify areas of strength and weakness, and create a plan for personal and professional growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you are unsure about the meaning of a question, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leader Self-Assessment** (Required): This field asks you to rate your own leadership skills and abilities. Please select 'Yes' if you feel you have the skills and abilities necessary to be a effective leader.
* **Team Leadership** (Required): This field asks you to assess your team's needs and challenges. Select all that apply.
* **Business Accmplishments** (Optional): Please enter any recent business accomplishments or successes you've achieved.
* **Personal Leadership** (Optional): This field asks you to select areas where you feel you excel as a leader.
* **Development Needs** (Required): This field asks you to identify areas where you need development and growth.
* **Action Planning** (Required): Please enter a plan for your professional growth and development over the next 30, 60, or 90 days.
* **Feedback** (Optional): Share any feedback or comments you have about your leadership development.
* **Next Steps** (Optional): Enter any next steps you plan to take as part of your leadership development.
* **Coaching Goals** (Optional): Enter any coaching goals or objectives you have.
* **Coaching Outcomes** (Optional): Please select all that apply.
* **Leadership Skills** (Optional): Enter any leadership skills you have or wish to develop.
* **Leadership Development** (Optional): Enter any leadership development opportunities or programs you've participated in.
* **Evaluation** (Required): Please enter the date you plan to evaluate your progress and growth.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (Required): This field asks you to select how often you'll evaluate your leadership growth.
* **Evaluation Scale** (Required): Please select the scale you'll use to evaluate your progress.
* **Evaluation Comments** (Optional): Enter any comments about your evaluation method.
* **Evaluation Frequency Comments** (Optional): Enter any comments about your evaluation frequency.
* **Evaluation Frequency Scale** (Required): Please select the frequency scale you'll use for your evaluation.
* **Evaluation Scale Comments** (Optional): Enter any comments about your evaluation scale.
* **Action Plan** (Required): Please enter a detailed plan for your action plan.
* **Follow Up** (Optional): Enter a date for your follow-up meeting or evaluation.
* **Feedback** (Optional): Share any feedback or comments about your progress.
* **Next Steps Follow Up** (Optional): Enter any next steps you plan to take as part of your follow-up meeting.
* **Completion Status** (Required): Please select your completion status.
