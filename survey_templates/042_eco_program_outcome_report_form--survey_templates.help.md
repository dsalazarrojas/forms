# Eco Program Outcome Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Program Outcome Report Form is designed to collect information about the outcomes of environmental programs and reports. This form helps to track and monitor the progress of eco-programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Select the program area or category of the eco-program being reported.
2. Enter the title of the eco-program.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the eco-program.
4. Describe the activities conducted during the eco-program.
5. List the outcomes or results achieved during the eco-program.
6. Describe the challenges faced during the eco-program.
7. Upload supporting evidence such as photos, videos, or documents to demonstrate the outcomes and progress of the eco-program.
8. Enter contact information for the person responsible for the eco-program, including their email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Program Area** (`program_area`): Choose the category or area where the eco-program operates, such as Eco-System, Community Engagement, or Climate Action.
- **Program Title** (`program_title`): Enter the title of the eco-program, which should be a brief description of the program.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of the eco-program, which is the date it began.
- **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of the eco-program, which is the date it ended.
- **Activities** (`activities`): Describe the activities or initiatives conducted during the eco-program.
- **Outcomes** (`outcomes`): List the outcomes or results achieved during the eco-program.
- **Challenges** (`challenges`): Describe any challenges faced during the eco-program.
- **Supporting Evidence** (`supporting_evidence`): Upload photos, videos, or documents to demonstrate the outcomes and progress of the eco-program.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the eco-program.
- **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the eco-program.
- **Program Area 2** (`program_area_2`): Choose an additional category or area for the eco-program, if applicable.
- **Supporting Evidence 2** (`supporting_evidence_2`): Upload additional photos, videos, or documents to demonstrate further outcomes and progress of the eco-program.
- **Phone 2** (`phone_2`): Enter an additional phone number of the person responsible for the eco-program, if applicable.
- **Email 2** (`email_2`): Enter an additional email address of the person responsible for the eco-program, if applicable.
