# decode_e_numbers_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "decode_e_numbers_survey" form is designed to collect feedback and insights from users on their awareness, concerns, behaviors, demographics, email, and comments related to a product or service. This form aims to identify areas where users may be struggling with certain numbers or concepts and gather information on how they interact with the product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "decode_e_numbers_survey" form, follow these steps:

1. Start on the "Intro Page" where you will find a brief introduction to the form.
2. On the "Awareness" page, select all that apply from the options provided regarding your level of awareness about certain numbers or concepts.
3. On the "Concerns" page, choose the option that best reflects your level of concern about the product or service.
4. On the "Behaviors" page, select the options that describe your typical behaviors related to the product or service.
5. On the "Demographics" page, enter your age in years, as this information is required for analysis.
6. On the "Email" page, enter your email address to allow us to contact you with any further questions or updates.
7. On the "Comments" page, provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the product or service.
8. On the "Contact" page, enter any contact information you would like to be reached out to, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro Page** (`intro_page`, note, optional): This page serves as an introduction to the form and provides a brief overview of what to expect.
* **Awareness** (`awareness_page`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the options provided regarding your level of awareness about certain numbers or concepts.
* **Concerns** (`concerns_page`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best reflects your level of concern about the product or service.
* **Behaviors** (`behaviors_page`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that describe your typical behaviors related to the product or service.
* **Demographics** (`demographics_page`, number, optional): Enter your age in years, as this information is required for analysis.
* **Email** (`email_page`, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with any further questions or updates.
* **Comments** (`comments_page`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the product or service.
* **Contact** (`contact_page`, text, optional): Enter any contact information you would like to be reached out to, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please fill out as much information as you can, but be as concise as possible.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
* Your feedback and insights are valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation in helping us improve the product or service.
