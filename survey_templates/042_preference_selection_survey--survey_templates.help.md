# Preference Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information on your personal preferences and feedback. Your responses will help us understand your likes and dislikes, which will enable us to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select a preference by clicking on the radio button next to the option of your choice.
2. In the "Preferences" section, you can add any additional comments or remarks you'd like to share.
3. If you have any email or phone number you'd like to include, you can do so in the respective fields.
4. For the "Date" and "Time" fields, select the date and time of your choice.
5. For "Select Multiple", choose as many options as you'd like.
6. In the "Integer" and "Decimal" fields, enter a numerical value.
