This form is used to administer the Superhero Trivia Quiz, a fun and interactive way to test users' knowledge about various superheroes. It is designed to gather information from participants about their favorite superheroes, colors, and demographics. The goal is to create a fun and engaging experience for end-users while collecting valuable data for analysis and research.

## Superhero Trivia Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Superhero Trivia Quiz is a form designed to collect data about users' preferences and knowledge about superheroes. The form is divided into multiple sections, each focused on different aspects of superheroes, such as favorite superheroes, colors, and demographics. The data collected will be used to analyze user preferences and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Answer the questions on each page, making sure to select your favorite superheroes, colors, and age group.
* On the **Questions** page, select all the superheroes you are familiar with.
* On the **Superhero** page, choose your favorite superhero.
* On the **Favorite Color** page, select your favorite colors.
* On the **Favorite Superhero** page, choose your favorite superhero among the options.
* On the **Age** page, select your age group.
* On the **Superhero Type** page, select the types of superheroes you are familiar with.
* Finally, review your answers on the **Submit** page and submit the form.

* **Superhero Trivia Quiz** (`title`, required: `false`): This is the title of the form. It is not a field to fill out, but rather a header for the form.
* **Questions** (`questions_page`, required: `false`): On this page, select all the superheroes you are familiar with. You can choose as many or as few as you like.
	+ **Batman**
	+ **Spider-Man**

	+ **Wonder Woman**
* **Favorite Superhero** (`superhero_page`, required: `false`): Choose your favorite superhero among the options.
	+ **Batman**

	+ **Superman**
	+ **Wonder Woman**
* **Favorite Color** (`favorite_color_page`, required: `false`): Select your favorite colors.
	+ **Blue**
	+ **Red**
	+ **Green**
	+ **Yellow**
* **Age** (`age_page`, required: `false`): Select your age group.
	+ This field is a number input, where you can select your age group.
* **Superhero Type** (`superhero_type_page`, required: `false`): Select the types of superheroes you are familiar with.
	+ **Action**
	+ **Magical**
	+ **Superhuman**
	+ **Human**
* **Submit** (`submit_page`, required: `false`): This is the final page where you can review your answers and submit the form.
