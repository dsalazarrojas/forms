## Seminar Attendee Feedback Check In Form - Help Guide
### Purpose

This form is for seminar attendees to check in and provide feedback on their experience. It's used to track attendance and collect suggestions for improving future events.

### How To Complete This Form

1. **To start, enter your First and Last names, email address, and confirm your attendance**. You can do this on the first page.
2. **Fill out any additional pages as needed**. Depending on the form, you may be asked to provide further information.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* ****FirstName** (Text): Enter your first name
* ****LastName** (Text): Enter your last name
* ****Email** (Email): Enter your email address. This will help us keep in touch with you.
* ****ConfirmedAttendance** (Number): If you have checked in, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'
* ****Feedback** (Text): Share any thoughts or concerns you may have about the seminar.
* ****Suggestions** (Text): If you have any ideas or suggestions for improving future events, enter them here.
* ****ContactUs** (Text): If you have any questions or would like more information, feel free to reach out to us. If you would like to be contacted, select the relevant option.
* ****Comments** (Text): Any additional comments you'd like to make about your experience.
* ****Date** (Date): The date you attended the seminar.
* ****Time** (Time): The time you attended the seminar.
* ****ConfirmedDate** (Date): The date you confirmed your attendance.
* ****ConfirmedTime** (Time): The time you confirmed your attendance.
* ****HasConfirmedAttendence** (Select One): If you have confirmed attending, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'. 
* ****CommentsConfirmed** (Text): Any comments about confirming your attendance. 
* ****HasReceivedSuggestions** (Select One): If you have received any suggestions, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'. 
* ****CommentsSuggestion** (Text): Any comments about receiving suggestions. 
* ****ContactUs** (Text): If you would like to be contacted, enter your contact information here.
* ****ContactMethod** (Text): If you would like to be contacted, enter the method of contact you prefer (e.g., email, phone).
* ****ContactEmail** (Email): If you would like to be contacted via email, enter your email address.
* ****ContactPhone** (Text): If you would like to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number.
* ****ContactWebsite** (Text): If you have a website where you can be contacted, enter the URL.
* ****HasConfirmedContact** (Select One): If you have confirmed you would like to be contacted, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'. 
* ****CommentsContact** (Text): Any comments about being contacted. 
* ****HasConfirmedWebsite** (Select One): If you have confirmed your website URL, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'. 
* ****HasConfirmedPhone** (Select One): If you have confirmed your phone number, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
