# safety_perception_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The safety_perception_survey form is used to collect data on the safety perceptions of users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the context and purpose of the form.
2. Answer question 1, "how_safe", by indicating how safe you feel about a certain situation on a scale from 1 to 5.
3. For question 2, "feel_safe", select one of the options that best describes how safe you feel about a certain situation.
4. For question 3, "feel_often", select how frequently you feel safe about a certain situation.
5. Answer the free-text questions "comments" and "safety_measures" to provide any additional information about your safety perception.
6. Identify any concerns or issues you have related to safety by typing in the "concerns" field.
7. Confirm whether you need to follow up on any of the issues you've raised by selecting "True" or "False" for "follow_up" and providing a brief message if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a brief overview of the form's purpose and context. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **How Safe** (`question_1`, `number`, optional): Answer on a scale from 1 to 5 how safe you feel about a certain situation.
* **Feel Safe** (`question_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the options: "very safe", "somewhat safe", "neutral", "somewhat uneasy", or "very uneasy" to describe how safe you feel.
* **Feel Often** (`question_3`, `select_one`, required): Select how frequently you feel safe about a certain situation: "very frequently", "sometimes", "rarely", or "never".
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about your safety perception or any concerns you have.
* **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, `text`, optional): Describe any measures you think are necessary to improve safety.
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, `text`, optional): Identify any concerns or issues you have related to safety.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm whether you need to follow up on any issues by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Comments (Follow Up)** (`comments_follow_up`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief message if you need to follow up on any issues.
* **Follow Up Message** (`follow_up_message`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief message if you need to follow up on any issues.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm whether you need to follow up on any issues by selecting "True" or "False". This field is required. 

Note: The fields marked as "required" should be completed. 

Tips: Be honest and thorough when answering the questions, and provide as much detail as possible in the free-text fields.
