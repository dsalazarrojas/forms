# Supplier Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Evaluation Questionnaire is used to assess and document the performance of a supplier in various areas such as supplier performance, reliability, price, delivery time, communication, supplier information, contact person, payment terms, and order processing. This form helps in making informed decisions regarding supplier selection, evaluation, and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions based on your experience and interaction with the supplier.
2. Fill in the required fields as per your knowledge and understanding.
3. If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the concerned authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Performance** (`supplier_performance`, `note`, required: false): This field is used to record the supplier's overall performance based on your experience with them.
* **Reliability** (`reliability`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to describe the supplier's reliability in fulfilling their obligations.
* **Price** (`price`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to record the supplier's price competitiveness.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required: false): This field is used to describe the supplier's delivery time and punctuality.
* **Communication** (`communication`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to describe the supplier's communication skills and responsiveness.
* **Supplier Info** (`supplier_info`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is a list of options to select the supplier's information.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is a list of options to select the contact person in the supplier's organization.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to describe the payment terms and conditions agreed upon with the supplier.
* **Order Processing** (`order_processing`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to describe the supplier's order processing and fulfillment efficiency.
* **Delivery Scheduling** (`delivery_scheduling`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to describe the supplier's delivery scheduling and organization.
* **Payment Scheduling** (`payment_scheduling`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to describe the payment terms and conditions agreed upon with the supplier.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to describe the status of the payment.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to describe the status of the order.
* **Payment Terms 1** to **Payment Terms 12** (`payment_terms_1` to `payment_terms_12`, `number`, required: false): These fields are used to record specific payment terms and conditions.

Note: Some fields may not be required, depending on your specific requirements and interactions with the supplier. Fill them in accordingly.
