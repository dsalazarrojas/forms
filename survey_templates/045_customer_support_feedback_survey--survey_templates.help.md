# Customer Support Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather your feedback about your experience with our support team. Your input will help us improve our service and better support you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the reason for your recent contact with our support team.
2. Rate your overall experience with our support team from 1 to 5.
3. Indicate whether you were satisfied with the resolution of your issue.
4. If you wish, select a preference for email notifications from our support team.
5. Rate the responsiveness of our support team from 1 to 5.
6. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the text field below.
7. If you have received support from our team in the past, please indicate yes or no.
8. If you have had any past interactions with our support team, please describe the main issue you contacted us about.
9. Rate your experience with the communication from our support team from 1 to 5.
10. Indicate whether you would recommend our support team to a friend or colleague.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the reason for your recent contact with our support team?** (`contact_reason`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select a reason for your recent contact with our support team, such as having trouble with a product/service, having a general inquiry, experiencing a technical issue, or needing help with an order.
* **How would you rate your overall experience with our support team?** (`experience_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Were you satisfied with the resolution of your issue?** (`issue_resolution`, `checkbox`, required: true): Indicate whether you were satisfied or not with the resolution of your issue.
* **Would you like to receive email notifications from our support team in the future?** (`email_notifications`, `select_one`, required: false): Select a preference for email notifications.
* **How would you rate the responsiveness of our support team?** (`responsiveness`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the responsiveness of our support team from 1 to 5, where 1 is very slow and 5 is extremely fast.
* **Do you have any additional feedback or suggestions for our support team?** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Have you received any support from our team in the past?** (`past_contact`, `checkbox`, required: false): Indicate whether you have received support from our team in the past.
* **What was the main issue you contacted us about?** (`main_issue`, `text`, required: false): Describe the main issue you contacted us about.
* **How did you feel about the communication from our support team during your recent contact?** (`communication`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your experience with the communication from our support team from 1 to 5.
* **Would you recommend our support team to a friend or colleague?** (`recommendation`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you would recommend our support team to a friend or colleague.
