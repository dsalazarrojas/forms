# Financial App Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from users about their experiences and opinions on financial app development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into multiple sections. Please fill out each section completely and honestly.
2. Each section is labeled with a title and contains multiple fields with labels and options.
3. Some fields are marked as "required", indicating that they must be filled out for the form to be submitted.
4. Use the options provided to select the most accurate response for each field.
5. If you have any comments or feedback, please use the "comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Note): This section is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Contact Information** (Text): Please provide your contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **app_name** (Text): Enter the name of the financial app you are using or plan to use.
* **platform** (Select One): Choose "Yes" if you use or plan to use a financial app, "No" otherwise.
* **Feedback** (Text): Share any feedback or comments about your experience with financial apps.
* **usability_rating** (Select Multiple): Rate the usability of financial apps in terms of Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **feature_requests** (Text): List any features you would like to see in financial apps.
* **pain_points** (Text): Share any pain points or challenges you face while using financial apps.
* **suggestions** (Text): Suggest any ideas or improvements for financial apps.
* **demographics** (Select One): Choose "Yes" if you are a financial professional, "No" otherwise.
* **financial_institution** (Select One): Choose "Yes" if you work for a financial institution, "No" otherwise.
* **job_function** (Select One): Choose "Yes" if you have a job function related to finance, "No" otherwise.
* **job_title** (Text): Enter your job title.
* **years_of_experience** (Number): Enter the number of years you have been working in finance.
* **monthly_income** (Number): Enter your monthly income.
* **frequency_of_use** (Select Multiple): Choose "Yes" if you frequently use financial apps, "No" otherwise.
* **time_spent_on_app** (Number): Enter the average time you spend on financial apps.
* **last_used_app_feature** (Select Multiple): Choose "Yes" for features you have used recently, "No" otherwise.
* **financial_institution_type** (Select One): Choose "Yes" if you are a financial professional working for a financial institution, "No" otherwise.
* **financial_institution_size** (Select One): Choose "Yes" if you are part of a financial institution with a small team, "No" otherwise.
* **team_size** (Number): Enter the number of people in your team.
* **average_hours_on_task** (Number): Enter the average hours spent on a task.
* **average_hours_on_task_unit** (Select One): Choose "Yes" if you work on tasks that require a unit of time, "No" otherwise.
* **time_spent_on_task** (Number): Enter the average time spent on tasks.
* **comments** (Text): Share any additional comments or feedback about financial apps.

Note: Please ensure you have read and understood the purpose and instructions before proceeding with the survey.
