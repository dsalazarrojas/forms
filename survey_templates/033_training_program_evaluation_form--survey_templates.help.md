# Training Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants about their training experience, to help the training team understand what worked well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Participant Name field with your name as it appears on your official records.
2. Select the date you attended the training session from the calendar in the Training Session Date field.
3. Choose the start time you attended the training session from the clock in the Training Session Time field.
4. Rate the overall quality of the training session from 1 to 4, where 1 is Poor, 2 is Fair, 3 is Good, and 4 is Excellent, in the Overall Rating field.
5. Provide any additional comments about the training session in the Comments field.
6. Answer the three questions in the Questions section with a brief description of your experience. You can type as much or as little as you like to answer each question.
7. Make sure to save your answers after completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, **Required**): Please fill in your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, date, **Required**): Select the date you attended the training session.
* **Training Session Time** (`training_session_time`, time, **Required**): Choose the start time you attended the training session.
* **Overall Rating** (`rating`, select_one, **Required**): Rate the overall quality of the training session on a scale from 1 to 4, where 1 is Poor, 2 is Fair, 3 is Good, and 4 is Excellent.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, **Required**): Provide any additional comments about the training session.
* **What was the most valuable part of the training session?** (`question_1`, text, **Required**): Describe the most valuable part of the training session.
* **What did you find most challenging about the training session?** (`question_2`, text, **Required**): Describe what you found most challenging about the training session.
* **What suggestions do you have for future training sessions?** (`question_3`, note, **Required**): Provide any suggestions you have for future training sessions.
