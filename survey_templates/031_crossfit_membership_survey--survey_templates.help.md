# Crossfit Membership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about Crossfit members and their experiences to help us improve our services and facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. If a question does not apply to you, select "N/A" (Not Applicable) or leave it blank.
3. Use the dropdown menus to select your answers for questions that have multiple options.
4. Enter your email address and name as it appears on your membership card.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Type** (`Membership Type`, select_one, required: false): Please select one of the following membership types:
	+ Free
	+ Premium
	+ Business
* **Age** (`Age`, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address as it appears on your membership card.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your membership card.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number as it appears on your membership card.
* **How did you find out about Crossfit?** (`How did you find out about Crossfit?`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all that apply:
	+ Social Media
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Online Ad
* **What were you looking for when you joined Crossfit?** (`What were you looking for when you joined Crossfit?`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all that apply:
	+ Workout routine
	+ Fitness classes
	+ Gym atmosphere
* **Favorite part of Crossfit?** (`Favorite part of Crossfit?`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all that apply:
	+ Workouts
	+ Community
	+ Equipment
* **What do you like most about Crossfit?** (`What do you like most about Crossfit?`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all that apply:
	+ Workouts
	+ Community
	+ Trainers
* **What do you like least about Crossfit?** (`What do you like least about Crossfit?`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all that apply:
	+ Crowdedness
	+ Long wait times
	+ Limited equipment
* **Will you renew your membership?** (`Will you renew your membership?`, select_one, required: false): Please select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
* **How satisfied are you with your membership?** (`How satisfied are you with your membership?`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all that apply:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, required: false): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience at Crossfit.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed this survey (optional).
