# Service Partner Payment Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your company's payment policies for service partners. It covers various aspects of payment terms, communication, and feedback management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review each section carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Fill out the form in sections, taking your time to provide detailed and thoughtful responses.
3. Use the options provided in each field, and if you need more space, you can include additional comments in the "Other Comments" fields.
4. Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this information will help us better understand your payment policies.
