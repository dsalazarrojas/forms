## product_prototype_inquiry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from customers who have interacted with our product prototype. The information gathered will help us improve the product and ensure it meets your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question to the best of your ability. If a question is not applicable to your experience, please provide a brief explanation for why.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details** (`company_details`, `text`, required: false):
This is where you can provide your company's name or the name of your organization. Please enter your company's name as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required: false):
Please describe the product or feature that you interacted with. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Feedback on Usability** (`feedback_on_usability`, `select_one`, required: false):
Rate your experience with the product's usability. Choose from the options:
	+ Very good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
* **What usability issues did you encounter?** (`feedback_on_usability_issues`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
If you experienced any issues with the product's usability, please select all that apply. Some common issues include:
	+ Difficulty navigating the interface
	+ Unclear instructions
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Product Improvement Recommendations** (`product_improvement_recommendations`, `text`, required: false):
Share your ideas on how we can improve the product. This could include suggestions on new features, changes to existing features, or anything else that you think would enhance the product.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required: false):
Let us know your preferred method of contact. We will use this to get in touch with you about your feedback:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ In-person
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false):
If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them here. This is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback or ask questions.

## Tips
Remember to be as specific as possible when providing feedback, and feel free to include screenshots or examples to illustrate your points. Your input is invaluable in helping us create a better product.
