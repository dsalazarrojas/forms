# software_and_device_needs_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess the software and device needs of your organization. It's meant to be a starting point for evaluating what tools and technologies your team requires to perform their tasks efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the text fields provided.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
3. If you're unsure about any question, you can always contact the form creators at form@gic.mx for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_is_it** (software_and_device_needs_assessment_1): This question asks you to describe the software or device that your team is using or planning to use. Try to be as specific as possible.
* **what_hardware** (hardware_needs_1): This field is asking about the types of hardware your team uses or will use. You can mention laptops, smartphones, servers, or any other hardware relevant to your team's tasks.
* **what_software** (software_needs_1): This field is for mentioning the software your team uses or plans to use. You can list specific software applications, platforms, or systems that are essential for your work.
* **technology_requirements** (technology_requirements_1): In this field, describe the specific technologies your team requires for their tasks, such as programming languages, frameworks, or databases.
* **other_hardware** (hardware_needs_2): If your team uses or plans to use any other hardware not mentioned earlier, list them here.
* **specific_technology** (technology_needs_2): If you need to use specific technologies for your tasks, describe them in this field.
* **employee_feedback** (employee_feedback_1): Share any feedback or concerns your team members have regarding software or device needs. This will help us understand their perspective and provide better support.
* **next_steps** (next_steps_1): Suggest the next steps or actions your team needs to take to fulfill their software and device needs.

## Tips
Remember to be as detailed as possible when providing answers to each question. This will help the form creators and your team better understand your software and device needs. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
