# Guided Tour Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user feedback and gauge their experience after a guided tour.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the optional "Feedback Questions" field with your thoughts and comments.
2. Rate your participation in the guided tour on a scale from 0 to 10 in the "Tour Participation Rating" field.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the guided tour on a scale from 0 to 10 in the "Satisfaction Rating" field.
4. Optionally, provide additional feedback in the "Feedback Text Area" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Questions**: A text input field for users to provide feedback about their experience. (Optional)
* **Tour Participation Rating**: Rate your participation in the guided tour on a scale from 0 to 10. (Required)
* **Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the guided tour on a scale from 0 to 10. (Required)
* **Feedback Text Area**: A text area for users to provide additional feedback. (Optional)
