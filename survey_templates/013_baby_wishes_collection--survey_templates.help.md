# Baby Wishes Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baby Wishes Collection form is a way for friends, family, and loved ones to share their heartwarming messages and wishes with the new parents of a baby. It's a special keepsake for the family to treasure and cherish.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by writing a heartfelt message for the baby's future in the "My wish for the baby's future is..." field.
2.  Choose the personality traits that you think the baby will develop and grow into.
3.  Share a special memory you have with the parents that you'd love the baby to know one day.
4.  Suggest a book that you think the baby would love to have in their library.
5.  Provide your email address so that the digital collection can be sent to you.
6.  Finally, write a final blessing or thought for the baby.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Heartfelt Wishes for the New Arrival --**: This field is an introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and inviting you to share your well-wishes with the new family.
*   **Your Name**: Please fill out your name so that the new parents know who's behind the well-wishes.
*   **Your Relationship to the Parents**: This field helps the new parents know who's sending the well-wishes, so they can personalize the keepsake.
*   **My wish for the baby's future is...**: Share your heartwarming message for the baby's future in this field.
*   **My best piece of advice for the new parents**: If you have any advice or words of encouragement for the new parents, share it here.
*   **I predict the baby will grow up to be...**: Choose the personality traits that you think the baby will develop and grow into.
*   **A special memory I have with the parents**: Share a special memory you have with the parents that you'd love the baby to know one day.
*   **A book I recommend for your first library**: Suggest a book that you think the baby would love to have in their library.
*   **Your Email Address**: Provide your email address so that the digital collection can be sent to you.
*   **Final Blessing or Thought**: Write a final blessing or thought for the baby.
