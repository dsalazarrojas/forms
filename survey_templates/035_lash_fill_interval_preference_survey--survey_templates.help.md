# Lash Fill Interval Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your experiences with lash fills, specifically how often you get them, how satisfied you are with them, and what influences your decisions when booking fills. Your input will help service providers better understand your needs and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. For each question, select the answer that best describes your experience or preference.
2. You can choose one answer option for questions that ask for a single response (select_one).
3. For questions that ask for multiple responses (select_multiple), select all that apply.
4. You can add any additional comments or thoughts in the notes fields if you want to provide more information.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
