# Cult Warning Signs Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information on warning signs associated with cult activities. It is an optional form that should only be completed by individuals with relevant experience or direct involvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by selecting the relevant warning signs and providing any additional comments as necessary.
2. Select from the options provided for each field, or type your own answer in the text fields.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Initial Contact** (`initial_contact`, `text`, required: false): Please describe the initial contact with the individual or group you are concerned about.
* **Concern Level** (`concern_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate the level of concern you have regarding cult involvement, selecting one or more of the options provided.
	+ Option 1: Low
	+ Option 2: Medium
	+ Option 3: High
* **Warning Signs** (`warning_signs`, `text`, required: false): List any specific warning signs you have observed, such as unusual behavior or suspicious activities.
* **Follow-up Support** (`follow_up_support`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the level of support you need to follow up on the situation, selecting one of the options provided.
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
	+ Option 3: Unknown
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): If additional information or explanations are needed, please provide them here.

## Tips

* Please ensure the accuracy of your input, as this information will be used to identify potential cult-related activities.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or authorized personnel for guidance.
* You can save your progress and come back to the form later if needed.
