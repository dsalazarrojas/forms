# Additional Notes Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to provide extra context or updates to an existing project record. It allows submitters to provide additional information that may be relevant to the project's progress or resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and title in the "Submitter Full Name" field.
2. Provide the reference ID, case number, or ticket ID related to the project in the "Reference ID / Case Number / Ticket ID" field.
3. Select the category of information being submitted (e.g., Progress Update, Clarification of Previous Data, etc.) in the "Nature of Information" field.
4. Enter any additional notes or information you have in the "Additional Notes / Information" field, being as specific as possible.
5. Indicate how critical the information is by selecting its impact level (e.g., Low, Medium, High) in the "How critical is this information?" field.
6. Enter your contact email address in case we need to reach out for further questions or clarification in the "Contact Email" field.
7. Decide if a response or confirmation is required in the "Do you require a response or confirmation?" field.
8. If you have supporting documents, select "True" in the "Do you have supporting documents to share?" field.
9. Finally, enter the date of submission in the "Date of Submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Supplementary Information Submission --**: This field is a header indicating that the form is for providing extra context or updates to an existing project record.
* **Submitter Full Name**: Enter your full name as the submitter of this information. This is a required field.
* **Reference ID / Case Number / Ticket ID**: Provide the reference ID, case number, or ticket ID related to the project record you are submitting notes for. This is a required field.
* **Nature of Information**: Select the category of information being submitted (e.g., Progress Update, Clarification of Previous Data, etc.). This is a required field.
* **Additional Notes / Information**: Enter any additional notes or information you have about the project. Please be as specific as possible.
* **How critical is this information?**: Indicate the level of criticality of the information you are submitting by selecting its impact level (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address in case we need to reach out for further questions or clarification. This is a required field.
* **Do you require a response or confirmation?**: Decide if a response or confirmation is required from us after submitting this information.
* **Do you have supporting documents to share?**: If you have supporting documents, select "True" in this field. If not, select "False".
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date on which you are submitting this information. This is a required field.
