# Cardiologists Event Log Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from cardiologists and other medical professionals about their experiences with events. Your input is invaluable in helping us create more valuable and engaging events for our community.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill out this form carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
*   Be honest and thorough in your answers, and avoid providing duplicate responses.
*   You can select multiple options when asked to rate or select items.
*   When providing text responses, be specific and clear in your answers.
*   If you have trouble with a particular question or section, feel free to ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Date** (`event_date`): The date the event took place.
*   **Event Name** (`event_name`): The title or name of the medical event.
*   **Event Location** (`event_location`): The location where the event was held.
*   **Department or Organizer** (`department_organizer`): The department that organized this event.
*   **Your Name** (`respondent_name`): Your full name as the person providing feedback.
*   **Your Role or Title** (`respondent_role`): Your job title or position (e.g., Cardiologist, Nurse, Staff, Student, Attendee).
*   **What was your role at this event?** (`attendance_status`): Select your participation level (Presenter or Speaker, Organizer or Staff, Attendee or Participant, Volunteer, Guest).
*   **Overall Event Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Unsatisfied, Very Unsatisfied).
*   **Organization and Logistics** (`organization_rating`): Rate the event's planning and execution (Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, Poor).
*   **Content Quality or Presentations** (`content_quality`): Rate the educational or informational value of the event (Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, Poor).
*   **Networking Opportunities** (`networking_opportunity`): Rate the chance to connect with colleagues (Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, Poor). (Note: This question is not required, and your answer is optional.)
*   **Venue and Facilities** (`venue_facilities`): Rate the location, space, and amenities (Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, Poor).
*   **How long did you attend?** (`attendance_duration`): Approximately how long you were present (Full event, 3-4 hours, 1-2 hours, Less than 1 hour). (Note: This question is not required, and your answer is optional.)
*   **Were the topics relevant to your practice?** (`topics_covered`): Select all the topics that were relevant to your practice (Heart disease prevention, Diagnostic techniques, Treatment options, Clinical research updates, Patient management, Professional development).
*   **Most Valuable Takeaway from This Event** (`key_takeaway`): What did you find most useful from this event? (Text response, optional).
*   **What could be improved?** (`improvement_areas`): Specific feedback on areas for enhancement (Text response, optional).
*   **Would you recommend this event to colleagues?** (`would_recommend`): Would you recommend this event to colleagues? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Unsure, Probably No, Definitely No).
*   **Topics for Future Events** (`future_topics`): What subjects would you like to see covered in future events? (Text response, optional).
*   **Speaker or Presentation Quality** (`speaker_feedback`): How would you rate the speakers? (Excellent - Very knowledgeable and engaging, Good - Informative and clear, Average - Adequate information, Fair - Could be improved, Poor - Not helpful). (Note: This question is not required, and your answer is optional.)
*   **How often should we hold events like this?** (`event_frequency`): How often should we hold events like this? (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annually, Annually, As needed). (Note: This question is not required, and your answer is optional).
*   **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_comments`): Any other feedback or suggestions? (Text response, optional).

## Tips

*   Please take the time to answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
*   If you have trouble with a particular question or section, don't hesitate to ask for help or clarification.
*   Your input is vital to creating better events for our community.
*   All answers will be kept confidential and used to improve event quality.
