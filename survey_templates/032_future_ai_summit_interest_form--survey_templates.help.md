# Future AI Summit Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Future AI Summit Interest Form is a crucial tool for us to understand your background and interests, which will help us tailor the summit experience to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and contact email.
2. Select your role (Attendee, Partner, or Speaker) and choose the areas of interest related to AI.
3. If you have any other interests or topics you'd like to focus on, let us know in the "Other interests" field.
4. Finally, tell us how you heard about the Future AI Summit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (<type:number>): This field is for entering your employee ID number. Please fill in your ID number for us to verify your identity.
* **Contact Email** (<type:email>): Enter your official email address associated with your organization. This will help us contact you with summit updates and information.
* **Contact Phone** (<type:text>): Enter your phone number to allow us to reach you more efficiently.
* **Organization** (<type:text>): Please enter your organization name or affiliation to help us understand your background.
* **Your Role** (<type:select_one>): Select your role at the organization, such as Attendee, Partner, or Speaker.
* **Interests** (<type:select_multiple>): Choose one or more areas of interest related to AI, such as AI Ethics, AI in Business, AI in Education, AI in Health, or AI in Research.
* **How did you hear about us?** (<type:note>): Briefly describe how you heard about the Future AI Summit. This will help us understand our marketing efforts.
* **Other interests** (<type:text>): If you have any other interests or topics related to AI that you'd like to focus on, please let us know in this field.
