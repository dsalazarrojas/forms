# Team Captain Leadership Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from team captains on their leadership skills and goals for the next season. Please take 5-10 minutes to complete this survey, and we appreciate your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Provide specific examples or details where possible to help the team's leadership understand your thoughts and feelings.
3. Be honest and sincere in your feedback, as it will be used to support your growth and development as a team captain.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Leadership Feedback** (`player_leadership_feedback`): Please describe your experiences and feedback from players regarding your leadership.
* **Current Position** (`current_position`): Please specify your current position within the team.
* **What will you do next year** (`next_year`): Describe your plans and goals for the next season.
* **What skills have you developed** (`skills_developed`): List the skills you have developed as a team captain.
* **What skills did you focus on in training** (`skills_in_training`): Describe the skills you focused on during training and how they have helped you in your role.
* **How would you rate your leadership** (`leadership_rating`): Rate your leadership skills from 1 to 10, with 1 being lowest and 10 being highest.
* **What do you expect from the captain** (`captain_expectations`): Describe your expectations from the team's captain.
* **What would you like to improve on leadership** (`leadership_improvement`): Identify areas where you would like to improve your leadership skills.
* **What will be your goal for next season** (`next_season_goal`): Set a goal for the next season that you would like to achieve.
* **What skills will you focus on in next season** (`next_season_skills`): Describe the skills you plan to focus on in the next season.
* **What skills will be required from you** (`required_skills`): Identify the skills that will be required from you in your role.
* **How long will it take for you to master these skills** (`master_time`): Estimate the time it will take for you to master the required skills.
* **What skills would be good for your position** (`good_skills`): Suggest skills that would be beneficial for your position.
* **What would be your preferred role** (`preferred_role`): Choose a preferred role from the options provided (Coach, Team Player, Assistant Coach, Other).
* **Do you have any other comments** (`additional_comments`): Use this field to provide any additional feedback, thoughts, or suggestions you may have.
