# Customer Feedback Survey Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Feedback Survey Release Form is a tool used to collect customer feedback and survey data from customers. This information will help the company to better understand customer experiences and opinions, which will enable us to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer feedback and survey data in the designated fields.
2. Fill in the customer name, email address, and phone number if required.
3. Choose a category that best describes your feedback.
4. Select one or multiple options from the available choices.
5. Enter a date and time for the feedback you are providing.
6. Add any additional notes you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as a customer. This will be used to identify you in the feedback form. If you're not comfortable sharing your name, you can leave it blank.
* **Customer Survey**: This is where you can provide your overall feedback about our services. Please be as detailed as possible in your response.
* **Feedback**: This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have. You can provide details about your experience or suggestions for improvement.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your feedback. This is optional but helps us to follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about your feedback. This is optional but helps us to follow up on your feedback.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with our services. Choose a number that best describes your opinion.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your feedback. This will help us to group your feedback with others with similar concerns.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose one or multiple options from the available choices that best describe your feedback. You can select all that apply.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your feedback. This will help us to track when your feedback was given.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your feedback. This will help us to track when your feedback was given.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments you may have. This is a free text field where you can provide any further details.
* **Customer Survey 2**: This is a duplicate of the customer survey field, where you can provide additional feedback.
