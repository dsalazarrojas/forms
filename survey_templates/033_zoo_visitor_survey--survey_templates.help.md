# Zoo Visitor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from visitors to the zoo. We value your opinions and want to hear about your experience. Your input will help us improve our services and ensure that your next visit is even more enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click the "Submit" button when you have finished filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form with your honest and accurate responses.
3. Review your answers before submitting to ensure they are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Zoo Visitor Survey (Page 1)** 
	+ Label: zoo_visitor_survey 
	+ Type: text 
	+ Explanation: Please enter a brief description of your visit to the zoo. This is the first question on the form.
* **Overall Experience** 
	+ Label: overall_experience 
	+ Type: number 
	+ Explanation: On a scale of 1-5, rate your overall experience at the zoo. A rating of 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **Favorite Animal** 
	+ Label: favorite_animal 
	+ Type: select_multiple 
	+ Explanation: Check all the animals you enjoyed seeing during your visit.
* **Zoo Staff Service** 
	+ Label: zoo_staff_service 
	+ Type: select_one 
	+ Explanation: Rate the service provided by the zoo staff. A "Yes" indicates good service and a "No" indicates poor service.
* **Favorite Section** 
	+ Label: favorite_section 
	+ Type: select_one 
	+ Explanation: Check the section of the zoo that you enjoyed the most.
* **Date of Visit** 
	+ Label: date_visit 
	+ Type: date 
	+ Explanation: Enter the date of your visit in the format DD/MM/YY.
* **Time of Visit** 
	+ Label: time_visit 
	+ Type: time 
	+ Explanation: Enter the time of your visit in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Feedback Message** 
	+ Label: feedback_message 
	+ Type: note 
	+ Explanation: Share any additional comments or suggestions about your visit.
* **Email** 
	+ Label: email 
	+ Type: email 
	+ Explanation: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for any further questions or to receive updates.
* **Phone** 
	+ Label: phone 
	+ Type: text 
	+ Explanation: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about any further information or updates.

**Tips**

* Please be as honest and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey.
