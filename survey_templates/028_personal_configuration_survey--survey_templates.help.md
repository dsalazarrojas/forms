# Personal Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Configuration Survey is a form designed to collect your personal preferences and configuration settings to help us tailor our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Configuration Survey**: This is the title of the form and does not require an answer.
* **Business Services**: Please enter any relevant business services you are using or have used with us. This can include information about specific services or projects.
* **Client Preferences**: Describe any specific preferences or requirements you have regarding our services. This can include things like communication preferences or specific features you need.
* **Service Expectations**: Explain your expectations from our services. This can include how often you expect to receive services, how you want to be contacted, or any specific outcomes you are looking for.
* **Communication Channels**: Please select all the ways in which you would like us to contact you:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Chat
* **Feedback Frequency**: Please select how often you would like to provide feedback:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Please select your preferred way of contacting you:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Chat
* **Feedback**: If you have any feedback or comments about our services, please enter them here.
* **Comments**: This is a text box for any additional comments or notes you would like to make about our services.
* **Contact Info**: Please enter any additional contact information you would like to provide, such as phone numbers or email addresses.

## Tips
* Please fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* If you've filled out this form before, you can log in and update your settings at any time.
