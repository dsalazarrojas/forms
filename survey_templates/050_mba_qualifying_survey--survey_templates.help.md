# mba_qualifying_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your qualification for an MBA program. It will guide you through a series of questions that will help us understand your background, work experience, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. If you are unsure of any of the questions, please refer to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your highest degree level** (`mba_qualifying_survey_page_1`, select_one, required)
	+ This question is asking about your level of education. Please select the option that best matches your highest degree earned, such as "Bachelors degree", "Masters degree", or "Doctorate degree".
* **What is your current occupation** (`mba_qualifying_survey_page_2`, select_one, required)
	+ This question is asking about your current job or occupation. Please select "Yes" if you are currently working, or "No" if you are not working.
