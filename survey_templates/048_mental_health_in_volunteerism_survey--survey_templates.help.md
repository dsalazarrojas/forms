# mental-health-in-volunteerism-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health in volunteerism survey is a tool for understanding how volunteering affects the mental health and well-being of individuals. It aims to gather information on various aspects of mental health, such as emotional stress, burnout, and self-care activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and answer the questions based on your experiences and feelings.
2. Select all that apply for each question, unless otherwise specified.
3. Be honest and accurate when answering, as your input will help shape the development of resources and support for volunteers in the future.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro** (`intro_page`, note, required: false): This is the introductory page, where you can read about the purpose and objectives of the survey.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your experiences as a volunteer. Select all types of activities or roles you've been involved in as a volunteer.
* **How has volunteering affected your mental health?** (`mental_health_effect`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about the impact of volunteering on your mental health. Choose one of the options that best describes your experience.
* **Emotional stress** (`emotional_stress`, number, required: false): This question asks about your level of emotional stress. Please enter a number that represents your level of emotional stress.
* **Burnout** (`burnout`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your burnout experiences. Select all that apply.
* **How often do you experience burnout?** (`burnout_frequency`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about the frequency of burnout experiences. Choose one of the options that best describes your experience.
* **Symptoms of burnout** (`burnout_symptoms`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about the symptoms you experience due to burnout. Select all that apply.
* **Self-care activities** (`self_care`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about the activities you do to take care of your mental health. Select all that apply.
* **Do you seek support for mental health concerns?** (`support_seeking`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your support seeking behavior for mental health concerns. Choose one of the options that best describes your experience.
* **How do you seek support for mental health concerns?** (`seeking_support`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your methods of seeking support for mental health concerns. Select all that apply.
* **What can we do to help you in the future?** (`feedback`, text, required: false): This question is an open-ended opportunity for you to share any comments or suggestions on how we can better support you as a volunteer.
* **What do you think about mental health involvement in volunteerism?** (`mental_health_involvement`, text, required: false): This question is an opportunity for you to reflect on your thoughts and feelings about mental health involvement in volunteerism.
