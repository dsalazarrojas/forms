# Production And Marketing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your production and marketing activities, such as marketing strategies, customer satisfaction, and budget allocation. This data will help us better understand your needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the instructions and guidelines for each field.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly, using the options provided or typing in your response.
3. Make sure to review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`production_and_marketing_survey`, `text`, not required): This field is intended for you to provide a brief overview of your production and marketing goals, if any.
* **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, `select_multiple`, not required): Please select all the marketing strategies that apply to your current or future production and marketing plans. You can choose more than one option.
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Product Feedback** (`product_feedback`, `text`, not required): This field is designed for you to share any product feedback or suggestions you have.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, `number`, not required): Please rate your level of satisfaction with our production and marketing services on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, `number`, not required): Enter your current or proposed marketing budget for the production and marketing activities.
* **Product Pricing** (`product_pricing`, `number`, not required): Provide the current or proposed pricing for your product.
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, `select_multiple`, not required): Please select all the marketing channels you currently use or plan to use.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Product Launch Date** (`product_launch_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the current or planned product launch date.
* **Marketing Strategy 2** (`marketing_strategy_2`, `text`, not required): This field is intended for you to describe your marketing strategy in more detail.
* **Customer Satisfaction 2** (`customer_satisfaction_2`, `number`, not required): Please rate your level of satisfaction with our services on a scale from 0 to 10.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Use specific numbers and dates when requested.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for support.
