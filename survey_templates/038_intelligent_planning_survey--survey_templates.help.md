# Intelligent Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the planning process, meeting frequency, contact person, and other relevant details to facilitate effective planning and communication. Please take a few minutes to fill out this survey to help us better understand your planning needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate options for each field based on your current planning process.
2. For text and date fields, enter the requested information as prompted.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
4. Once you've completed all fields, click the submit button to save your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Planning Process** (`planning_process`, `select_one`, required): This field asks about your typical planning frequency. Select the option that best describes how often you plan.
	+ Regularly: This is when you plan on a regular schedule, e.g., daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all departments that are involved in your planning process.
	+ Marketing: This department is responsible for marketing activities and campaigns.
	+ Sales: This department is involved in sales and revenue-related activities.
	+ IT: This department handles technology and infrastructure-related planning.
* **Meeting Frequency** (`meeting_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you hold meetings related to planning.
	+ Daily: This is when you hold meetings daily, often for urgent matters.
	+ Weekly: This is when you hold meetings weekly, for more in-depth planning.
	+ Monthly: This is when you hold meetings monthly, for long-term planning.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `select_multiple`, required): Identify the people directly involved in your planning process.
	+ Yes: This person is directly involved in the planning process.
	+ No: This person is not directly involved in the planning process.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Meeting Availability** (`meeting_availability`, `time`, required): Choose a time slot for regular meetings.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Share any feedback or suggestions related to the planning process.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required): Offer any suggestions for improvement to the planning process.
* **Communication Channel** (`communication_channel`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the channels you use for communication.
	+ Email: This is when you use email for communication.
	+ Phone: This is when you use phone for communication.
	+ In-person: This is when you have face-to-face communication.
* **Update Frequency** (`update_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you update the plan.
	+ Daily: This is when you update the plan daily.
	+ Weekly: This is when you update the plan weekly.
	+ Monthly: This is when you update the plan monthly.
* **Department 2** (`department_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all departments involved in the second part of the process.
* **Department 3** (`department_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all departments involved in the third part of the process.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required): Leave any additional comments or notes for the planning process.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `time`, required): Choose a time slot for regular meetings.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date for scheduling purposes.

Please take a few minutes to fill out this survey, and thank you for your time!
