# Health History General Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health History General Survey is a form designed to collect essential information about a patient's health history. This form aims to gather comprehensive details about their medical conditions, allergies, surgical history, and other relevant health-related information. Please ensure you have the patient's consent before sharing this form with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. Please ensure this is the patient's first name, not a title or surname.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. Again, this should be the patient's last name.
3. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field. Use the format: DD/MM/YYYY.
4. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field. Please provide a valid address where we can contact you.
5. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. We'll use this to contact you if needed.
6. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field. This will help us reach you quickly.
7. List your **Medical Conditions** in the "Medical Conditions" field. Please be as detailed as possible.
8. List any **Allergies** you might have in the "Allergies" field. Include any medications or substances you're allergic to.
9. Describe your **Surgical History** in the "Surgical History" field. Include any surgeries, dates, and procedures.
10. Enter your **Medical History** in the "Medical History" field. Be as detailed as possible about your medical history, including any relevant dates and procedures.
11. Share your **Family History** in the "Family History" field. This includes any relevant medical history of your family members.
