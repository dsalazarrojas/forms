# City Health Profile Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to gather your thoughts and opinions on various aspects of your city's health profile. It's designed to identify areas that require improvement and provide valuable insights for policymakers and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct response for each question.
3. If you have additional comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Information -- Respondent Name (Optional)** (`respondent_name`, `text`, Optional): This section is for you to provide your name, if you choose to do so.
* **Email Address (`email`, `email`, Optional)**: Please provide your email address, if you have one.
* **Age Group (`age_group`, `select_one`, Optional)**: Choose an age group that best describes you.
	+ 18-25
	+ 26-35
	+ 36-45
	+ 46-55
	+ 56-65
	+ 65+
	+ Prefer not to say
* **Your Neighborhood (`neighborhood`, `text`, Optional)**: Describe your neighborhood.
* **-- Overall City Health Assessment -- (`section_header_overall_health`, `note`, Optional)**: Read through this section before answering the next questions.
* **Overall Health of Our Community (`overall_health_rating`, `number`, Required)**: Rate the overall health of our city from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Perceived Health Trends in the City (`health_trends`, `select_one`, Optional)**: Choose how you perceive health trends in the city.

	+ Improving slightly
	+ Stable
	+ Declining slightly
	+ Declining significantly
* **-- Specific Health Areas -- (`section_header_specific_health`, `note`, Optional)**: Read through this section before answering the next questions.
* **Access to Mental Health Services (`mental_health_access`, `number`, Required)**: Rate the availability of mental health services in the city from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Access to Primary Care Physicians (`primary_care_access`, `number`, Required)**: Rate the availability of primary care physicians in the city from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Healthcare Affordability (`healthcare_affordability`, `number`, Required)**: Rate healthcare affordability in the city from 1 (very unaffordable) to 5 (very affordable).
* **Availability of Preventive Health Services (`preventive_services`, `number`, Required)**: Rate the availability of preventive health services, such as vaccinations, screenings, and wellness programs.
* **Substance Abuse and Addiction Services (`substance_abuse_services`, `number`, Optional)**: Rate the availability of substance abuse and addiction services from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Maternal and Child Health Services (`maternal_child_health`, `number`, Optional)**: Rate the availability of maternal and child health services from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **-- Health Risk Factors -- (`section_header_health_factors`, `note`, Optional)**: Read through this section before answering the next questions.

* **Concern About Substance Abuse (`substance_abuse_concern`, `number`, Optional)**: Rate your concern about substance abuse in the city from 1 (not concerned) to 5 (very concerned).
* **Concern About Chronic Disease Rates (`chronic_disease_concern`, `number`, Optional)**: Rate your concern about chronic disease rates in the city from 1 (not concerned) to 5 (very concerned).
