## Virtual Event Patron Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Event Patron Evaluation Form is a tool for us to gather feedback from patrons about their experience at virtual events. We use this feedback to identify areas for improvement and to provide better experiences for our patrons in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Name** field with the name of the virtual event you attended.
2. Enter the **Event Date** of the virtual event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Provide the **Event Location** where the virtual event took place.
4. Select the type of event you attended from the options: **Virtual Event**, **In-Person Event**, or **Online Event**.
5. If you attended the event with other people, enter the number of **Attendees**.
6. In the **Feedback** field, provide any thoughts or comments about your experience.
7. Share any **Suggestions** for how we could improve our virtual events.
8. Select how **Satisfied** you were with the event from the options: **Very Satisfied**, **Somewhat Satisfied**, **Somewhat Dissatisfied**, or **Very Dissatisfied**.
9. Choose whether you would **Recommend** our virtual events to others from the options: **True** or **False**.
10. Select whether you would like for us to **Follow-up** with you with further information from the options: **True** or **False**.
11. Enter your **Email** address so we can get in touch with you.
12. Enter your **Phone number** so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: The name of the virtual event you attended.
* **Event Date**: The date of the virtual event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Location**: The physical or virtual location where the virtual event took place.
* **Event Type**: The type of event you attended, such as Virtual Event, In-Person Event, or Online Event.
* **Attendees**: If you attended the event with others, enter the number of people who attended.
* **Feedback**: Provide any thoughts or comments about your experience.
* **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions for how we could improve our virtual events.
* **How Satisfied**: Select how satisfied you were with the event from the options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Recommend**: Choose whether you would recommend our virtual events to others from the options: True or False.
* **Follow-up**: Select whether you would like for us to follow-up with you with further information from the options: True or False.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
