# School Meal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Meal Survey is a tool to gather feedback from students about their experience with school meals. The data collected will help school administrators make informed decisions to improve the meal program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2.  Rate the quality of the food served in the school cafeteria using the "Food Quality" field.
3.  Provide your rating for the quality of your teacher using the "Teacher Rating" field.
4.  Indicate the time you usually eat at school using the "School Meal Time" field.
5.  Select how often you have school meals using the "School Meal Frequency" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your school records.
* **Food Quality** (`food_quality`, Select, Required: false): Rate the quality of the food served using the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, Select, Required: false): Rate the quality of your teacher using the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **School Meal Time** (`school_meal_time`, Select, Required: false): Choose the time when you usually eat at school:
	+ Before School
	+ Lunchtime
	+ After School
* **School Meal Frequency** (`school_meal_frequency`, Select, Required: false): Select how often you have school meals:
	+ Every Day
	+ 2-3 times a week
	+ 1-2 times a week
	+ Rarely

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Select the options that best match your experience.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to write them in the space provided.
