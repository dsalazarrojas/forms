# Academic Research Study Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from potential participants in an academic research study to help researchers target and tailor studies to their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name, contact email, and phone number.
2. Select your age group and gender identity.
3. Choose your highest level of education.
4. Select all research areas that interest you.
5. Choose your availability on weekdays and weekends.
6. Select your preferred study format and compensation interest.
7. Indicate your previous research participation experience.
8. If applicable, provide any special health conditions that might be relevant for research studies.
9. Specify how long you would be willing to participate in a research study.
10. If needed, provide any special accommodations for research participation.
11. Finally, provide any additional comments about your interests and availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, **Required**): Provide your full name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter your contact email for research opportunities.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your contact phone number for research opportunities.
* **Age Group** (`participant_age_group`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your age group (18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, or 65+).
* **Gender Identity** (`participant_gender`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your gender identity (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, or Other).
* **Education Level** (`participant_education_level`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose your highest level of education (High school, Some college, Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, Doctoral degree, or Other).
* **Research Interest Areas** (`research_interest_areas`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Select all areas that interest you (Health and medicine, Psychology, Education, Technology, Environmental science, Social sciences, Arts and humanities, or Other).
* **Availability on Weekdays** (`availability_weekdays`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Select the days you are generally available for research studies (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Morning only, Afternoon only, or Evening only).
* **Availability on Weekends** (`availability_weekends`, `select_one`, **Required**): Indicate your availability on weekends (Yes, Saturday, Yes, Sunday, or No, weekends unavailable).
* **Preferred Study Format** (`preferred_study_format`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose how you would prefer to participate in research studies (In-person, Online, Phone interview, Survey only, or Mixed methods).
* **Compensation Interest** (`compensation_interest`, `select_one`, **Required**): Indicate your interest in compensation for participating (Yes, I would participate for compensation, No, I prefer volunteer participation, It depends on the study, or Not interested in compensation).
* **Previous Research Participation** (`previous_research_participation`, `select_one`, **Required**): Indicate your experience with research studies (Yes, multiple times, Yes, once or twice, No, but interested, or No, not interested).
* **Special Health Conditions** (`special_health_conditions`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide any health conditions that might be relevant for research studies.
* **Research Availability Duration** (`research_availability_duration`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose how long you would be willing to participate in a research study (Less than 30 minutes, 30-60 minutes, 1-2 hours, Half day, Full day, or Multiple sessions over time).
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`special_accommodations_needed`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide any special accommodations you might need for research participation.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide any additional information about your interests and availability.
