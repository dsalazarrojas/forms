# Subscription Box Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey template designed to gather information from end-users about their preferences for a subscription box service. It aims to understand users' preferred contact methods and delivery frequencies for their subscription box products.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply go through each page, and for each field:

### First Page

1. **firstName**: Enter your first name as you'd like to be contacted.
2. **email**: Enter your email address as your preferred contact method.
3. **preferredContact**: Select your preferred contact method. You can choose one or multiple options from "Contact by phone", "Contact by email", and "Contact by mail".
4. **contactOption1**: Select your preferred contact method for this specific product. Please choose one of the options provided.
5. **contactMethod**: Select the method of contact you prefer for this specific product. You can choose one or multiple options from "Phone", "Email", and "Mail".

### Second Page

1. **contactFrequency**: Select how often you'd like to receive your subscription box products. You can choose one or multiple options from "Monthly", "Every 1 week", "Every 2 weeks", "Every 4 weeks", "Every 8 weeks".
2. **deliveryFrequency**: Select the frequency of delivery for your subscription box products. Please choose one of the provided options.
3. **deliveryFrequencyOption2**: Select the frequency of delivery for this specific product. Please choose one of the provided options.
4. **productPreferences**: Select your preferences for this product. You can choose one or multiple options from the provided list.
5. **productPreferenceOption1**: Select your preference for this specific product. Please choose one of the provided options.

### Subsequent Pages

Continue this pattern for the remaining pages, following the same instructions for each field.

## Tips

* Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* This form is intended to help us better understand your preferences, so your honesty is greatly appreciated.

Please note that this form may seem extensive, but each field is designed to provide detailed insights into your preferences. If you have any doubts or concerns, please feel free to ask.
