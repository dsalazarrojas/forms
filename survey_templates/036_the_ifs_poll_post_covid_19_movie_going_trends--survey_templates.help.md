# the_ifs_poll_post_covid_19_movie_going_trends - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "the_ifs_poll_post_covid_19_movie_going_trends" form is a survey that gathers data on post-COVID-19 movie-watching trends and habits. It's designed to be completed by users who are willing to share their movie preferences and spending patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page to understand the context and purpose of the form.
2. Answer the questions on how often you watch movies, selecting one of the three options provided.
3. Choose your favorite movie genres by selecting all that apply from the list of options.
4. Enter your favorite movie title(s) in the text field.
5. Report how much money you've spent on movies and how many movies you've watched per month.
6. Select your favorite movie type from the list of options.
7. Enter your favorite movie title (2) in the text field.
8. Report how much money you've spent on movies and money spent per month.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: `false`): This is a note field that provides an introduction to the form.
- **How Often Do You Watch Movies?** (`2`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose how often you watch movies: frequently, occasionally, or rarely.
- **Favorite Movie Genre** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all that apply from action, adventure, comedy, or other genres.
- **Favorite Movie Title** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your favorite movie title(s) in the text field.
- **How Much Money Spent On Movies?** (`5`, `number`, required: `false`): Report how much money you've spent on movies.
- **How Many Movies Watched Per Month** (`6`, `number`, required: `false`): Report how many movies you've watched per month.
- **Movie Type** (`7`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your favorite movie type from action, adventure, or comedy.
- **Favorite Movie Title (2)** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your favorite movie title (2) in the text field.
- **Money Spent On Movies** (`9`, `number`, required: `false`): Report how much money you've spent on movies.
- **Money Spent On Movies 2** (`10`, `number`, required: `false`): Report money spent on movies 2.
- **Favorite Genre** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all that apply from action, adventure, or comedy.
- **Money Spent Per Month** (`12`, `number`, required: `false`): Report money spent per month.
