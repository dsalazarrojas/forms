# Farrier Clinics And Competitions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and ratings from individuals who have attended Farrier Clinics and Competitions. Your input will help us improve the quality of our events and provide better services to our attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information in the form below.
2. Provide ratings and feedback for the relevant sections.
3. Make sure to submit your survey by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Info** (text): This field is for providing a brief description of the event you attended. Please write down a short summary of the event, including its date and time.
* **Attendee Rating** (number): This field is for rating the overall quality of the event. Please rate from 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Instructor Feedback** (text): This field is for providing any feedback or comments about the instructor who led the clinic or competition. Please be specific and provide constructive feedback.
* **Competitor Rating** (select one): This field is for rating the competitor who won the competition. Please select the name of the competitor from the dropdown list.
* **Competition Rating** (number): This field is for rating the overall quality of the competition. Please rate from 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (select multiple): This field is for rating your overall satisfaction with the event. Please select one or multiple options from the dropdown list (Very Good, Good, Fair).
* **Rating 1** (number): This field is for rating the first aspect of the event. Please rate from 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Rating 2** (number): This field is for rating the second aspect of the event. Please rate from 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Rating 3** (number): This field is for rating the third aspect of the event. Please rate from 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Comments** (note): This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback about the event. Please be specific and provide constructive feedback.
* **Email** (email): This field is for providing your email address. Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached for any follow-up questions or feedback.
* **Phone** (text): This field is for providing your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number where you can be reached for any follow-up questions or feedback.
* **Date of Event** (date): This field is for the date of the event you attended. Please select a date from the calendar.
* **Time of Event** (time): This field is for the time of the event you attended. Please select a time from the dropdown list.
* **Location of Event** (text): This field is for the location of the event you attended. Please enter a valid location.
* **Date of Submission** (date): This field is for the date of submission of this form. It will automatically be populated once you submit the form.
