# Math And AI Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on your experience with Math and AI.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and select the correct answer for each field.
2. Provide honest and accurate answers to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Math Proficiency** (`math_proficiency`, `number`, optional): This field measures your level of proficiency in Math, from 1 to 10.
* **AI Experience** (`ai_experience`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your experience with AI, including any relevant certifications, training, or projects.
* **Comfort Integrating AI** (`ai_integration`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply to describe your comfort level with integrating AI into your work or projects:

**Tips**

* Answer all fields truthfully and completely.
