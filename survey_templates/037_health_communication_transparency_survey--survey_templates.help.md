# Health Communication Transparency Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Communication Transparency Survey is designed to evaluate the transparency of health communication channels. It assesses the clarity, trust, and overall feelings of users towards health communication channels. This survey aims to provide insight into the effectiveness of health communication strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and provide honest answers to each question.
2. Select the most appropriate response for each question, taking into account your personal feelings and experiences.
3. Complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (1) (note, required: false): This is an introductory page explaining the purpose and instructions for the survey.
* **clarity** (2) (text, required: false): Please describe how clear you found the health communication to be. Be honest about any difficulties you experienced while understanding the information.
* **trust** (3) (text, required: false): Describe how much trust you have in the health communication channel. Provide your genuine feelings and opinions.
* **transparency** (4) (text, required: false): Explain how transparent you perceive the health communication channel to be. Be specific about any issues or concerns you experienced.
* **messaging_style** (5) (text, required: false): Describe the style of the health communication messages you received. Be honest about whether the messages were clear, concise, and easy to understand.
* **communication_channels** (6) (text, required: false): Enumerate the health communication channels you used (e.g., social media, email, website, phone). Explain how these channels contributed to your understanding and feelings.
* **feedback** (7) (text, required: false): Provide any feedback you may have about the health communication process. This can include suggestions for improvement or positive experiences.
* **trust_scale** (8) (number, required: false): Indicate your level of trust in the health communication channel, with 1 being Low and 5 being High.
* **clarity_scale** (9) (number, required: false): Rate the clarity of the health communication channel, with 1 being Low and 5 being High.
* **transparency_scale** (10) (number, required: false): Assess the transparency of the health communication channel, with 1 being Low and 5 being High.
* **perceived_transparency** (11) (number, required: false): Estimate how transparent you perceive the health communication channel to be, with 1 being Low and 5 being High.
* **trust_level** (12) (select_one, required: true): Choose one of the following levels of trust: High, Moderate, or Low.
* **clarity_level** (13) (select_one, required: true): Select one of the following clarity levels: High, Moderate, or Low.
* **transparency_level** (14) (select_one, required: true): Rate the transparency level as High, Moderate, or Low.
* **trustworthiness** (15) (select_multiple, required: true): Indicate whether the health communication channel's trustworthiness is High, Moderate, or Low.
* **trustworthiness_score** (16) (number, required: false): Provide a numerical score for the health communication channel's trustworthiness, with 1 being Low and 5 being High.
* **clarityworthiness** (17) (select_multiple, required: true): Select the clarityworthiness of the health communication channel as High, Moderate, or Low.
* **clarityworthiness_score** (18) (number, required: false): Rate the clarityworthiness score for the health communication channel, with 1 being Low and 5 being High.
* **transparencyworthiness** (19) (select_multiple, required: true): Indicate whether the health communication channel's transparency is High, Moderate, or Low.
* **transparencyworthiness_score** (20) (number, required: false): Provide a numerical score for the transparencyworthiness, with 1 being Low and 5 being High.
* **overall_feelingscale** (21) (number, required: false): Rate your overall feelingscale for the health communication channel, with 1 being Low and 5 being High.
* **overall_feelingscore** (22) (number, required: false): Provide a numerical score for your overall feelingscore, with 1 being Low and 5 being High.
* **perceived_transparency_2** (23) (select_multiple, required: true): Select one of the following perceived transparency levels: High, Moderate, or Low.
* **trustworthiness_important** (24) (select_multiple, required: true): Indicate whether trustworthiness is High, Moderate, or Low.
* **transparency_important** (25) (select_multiple, required: true): Rate the importance of transparency as High, Moderate, or Low.

This help guide is a concrete explanation of the survey's purpose and fields. It assumes the reader understands the context of the survey and its intended use.
