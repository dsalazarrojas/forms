# Feedback On Proposed Agenda - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on a proposed agenda. It's meant to be a platform for users to provide their thoughts, suggestions, and comments on the agenda.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the proposed agenda in the `Agenda Title` field.
2.  Enter a brief description of the proposed agenda in the `Description` field.
3.  Select the category of the proposed agenda from the `Category` select menu.
4.  Add relevant tags related to the proposed agenda in the `Tags` field.
5.  Enter the name of the proposed agenda in the `Agenda Name` field.
6.  Enter the start and end times of the proposed agenda in the `Agenda Start Time` and `Agenda End Time` fields.
7.  Select participants involved in the proposed agenda from the `Participants` select menu.
8.  Provide any additional feedback or comments on the proposed agenda in the `Feedback` field.
9.  Add any additional comments in the `Additional Comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Agenda Title**: Enter the title of the proposed agenda in this field.
*   **Description**: Enter a brief description of the proposed agenda to help users understand its purpose and context.
*   **Category**: Select the category of the proposed agenda from the options provided (e.g., Event, Category).
*   **Tags**: Add relevant tags related to the proposed agenda to help users find it more easily.
*   **Agenda Name**: Enter the name of the proposed agenda.
*   **Agenda Start Time**: Enter the start time of the proposed agenda. Please use the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
*   **Agenda End Time**: Enter the end time of the proposed agenda. Please use the 24-hour format (e.g., 16:00).
*   **Participants**: Select participants involved in the proposed agenda from the provided options.
*   **Feedback**: Provide any feedback or comments on the proposed agenda.
*   **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or thoughts on the proposed agenda.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you are familiar with the proposed agenda before filling out this form.
*   If you are not familiar with the proposed agenda, it's recommended to wait until you are before filling out this form.
*   This form is for gathering feedback, not for submitting the agenda for approval.
