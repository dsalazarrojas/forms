# Leadership Vs Management Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Vs Management Quiz is designed to distinguish between leadership and management styles in the workplace. This quiz will help you assess your leadership skills and identify areas where you can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction on the first page. This will give you a brief overview of the quiz and its objectives.
2. Move on to the "Leadership Questions" section. Here, you will select all the leadership questions that you think are relevant to your workplace.
3. After that, move to the "Management Questions" section. In this section, you will select all the management questions that are relevant to your workplace.
4. Finally, move to the "Total Score" section where you will enter your total score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Leadership Questions** (Leadership Questions, select_multiple, Optional): In this section, choose all the leadership questions that you feel are relevant to your workplace. Leadership questions are statements that reflect the characteristics and behaviors of a leader, such as "I motivate and inspire my team" or "I empower my team members to take ownership."
* **Management Questions** (Management Questions, select_multiple, Optional): In this section, choose all the management questions that you feel are relevant to your workplace. Management questions are statements that reflect the characteristics and behaviors of a manager, such as "I set clear goals and objectives for my team" or "I monitor and control the work of my team members."
* **Total Score** (Total Score, number, Optional): In this section, enter your total score, which represents the number of leadership and management questions you selected as relevant to your workplace.
