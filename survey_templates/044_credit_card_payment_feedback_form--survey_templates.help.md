# credit_card_payment_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the payment experience with your credit card. It helps us understand how well we're doing in processing credit card payments and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the best way you can.
2. If a question is not relevant to your experience, you can skip it or select "N/A" (Not Applicable) where possible.
3. Provide as much detail as you can about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### How was your payment experience? (1)
**payment_experience**: Describe your experience with payment processing. What was good, bad, or in between?

### What type of credit card did you use? (2)
**card_type**: Choose the type of credit card you used for the payment.

*   *Visa*: I used a Visa credit card
*   *Mastercard*: I used a Mastercard
*   *Amex*: I used an Amex credit card
*   *Diners*: I used a Diners credit card

### What was the amount you paid with your credit card? (3)
**amount_paid**: Enter the amount you paid with your credit card.

### Was the transaction successful? (4)
**transaction_success**: Select whether the transaction was successful or not.

*   *True*: The transaction was successful
*   *False*: The transaction was not successful

### Which payment method was used? (5)
**payment_method**: Choose the payment method used for the transaction.

*   *Online*: The payment was made online
*   *In-store*: The payment was made in-store
*   *Phone*: The payment was made over the phone

### What time of day was the transaction made? (6)
**time_of_day**: Enter the time of day the transaction was made.

### Date of the transaction (7)
**date_of_transaction**: Enter the date of the transaction.

### Do you want to provide your contact details? (8)
**contact_details**: Select whether you want to be contacted about this feedback or not.

*   *True*: Yes, contact me
*   *False*: No, don't contact me

### Additional comments (9)
**additional_comments**: Provide any additional comments or details about your experience.

### Email address (10)
**email**: Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted about this feedback.

### Phone number (11)
**phone**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted about this feedback.

### Comments (12)
**comments**: Provide any additional comments about your experience.

### Rating (13)
**rating**: Choose a rating for your experience.

*   *1-5*: Your rating for the experience

### Rating comment (14)
**rating_comment**: Provide a short description of your rating.

### Have you been contacted via email? (15)
**email_sent**: Select whether you've been contacted via email or not.

*   *True*: Yes, I've been contacted via email
*   *False*: No, I haven't been contacted via email

### Time contacted (16)
**time_contacted**: If you've been contacted via email, enter the time you were contacted.

### Method of contact (17)
**contact_method**: Choose the method used to contact you.

*   *Email*: Contacted via email
*   *Phone*: Contacted via phone

### What type of feedback is this? (18)
**feedback_type**: Choose the type of feedback.

*   *General feedback*: General feedback
*   *Transaction-specific feedback*: Feedback about a specific transaction

### How were you contacted via email? (19)
**email_sent_method**: If you were contacted via email, choose the method used.

*   *Automated email*: Contacted via automated email
*   *Human email*: Contacted via human email

### Phone number (20)
**phone_number**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted about this feedback.

### Contacted by (21)
**contacted_by**: Choose who contacted you.

*   *Automated system*: Contacted by an automated system
*   *Human representative*: Contacted by a human representative

### Contact Details Email (22)
**contact_details_email**: Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted about this feedback.

### Contact Details Phone (23)
**contact_details_phone**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted about this feedback.

### Other contact details (24)
**contact_details_other**: Provide any other contact details.

### Follow up (25)
**follow_up**: Select whether you'd like to be followed up on or not.

*   *True*: Yes, follow up on this feedback
*   *False*: No, don't follow up on this feedback

Note: This help guide assumes that you're a user who wants to provide feedback on their credit card payment experience. If you're a system administrator or have special access, you may need additional guidance or have different instructions.
