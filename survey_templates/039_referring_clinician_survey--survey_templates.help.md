# Referring Clinician Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referring Clinician Survey is a tool designed to capture information about referring clinicians and their practices. This guide will walk you through the process of completing the survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your practice in the **Practice Name** field.
2. Enter the name of the contact person for your practice in the **Contact Person Name** field.
3. Enter your practice's address in the **Practice Address** field.
4. Enter the city where your practice is located in the **Practice City** field.
5. Select your practice's state from the list in the **Practice State** field.
6. Enter your practice's zip code in the **Practice Zip** field.
7. Enter your contact person's phone number in the **Contact Person Phone** field.
8. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
9. Select the type of medical board certification your clinician holds from the list in the **Medical Board Cert** field.
10. Enter your medical license number in the **Medical License Number** field.
11. Select whether your practice specializes in any of the listed areas from the list in the **Specialty** field.
12. Select the type of practice you have (solo, partner, etc.) from the list in the **Practice Type** field.
13. Select the insurance payer for your practice from the list in the **Insurance Payer** field.
14. Select the location of your practice (urban, rural, etc.) from the list in the **Practice Location** field.
15. Enter the name of the contact person in the **Contact Person** field.
16. Enter your practice's National Provider Identifier (NPI) in the **Practice NPI** field.
17. Enter the address line 1 for your practice in the **Address Line 1** field.
18. Enter the address line 2 for your practice in the **Address Line 2** field.
19. Enter the city where your practice is located in the **City** field.
20. Select the state where your practice is located from the list in the **State** field.
21. Enter the fax number for your practice in the **Fax Number** field.
22. Enter the email address for your practice in the **Email** field.
