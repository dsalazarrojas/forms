# Audience Favorite Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect audience feedback and ratings for a show, event, or performance. It aims to gather information on the audience's favorite act or performance, overall show rating, and other aspects of the event such as venue satisfaction and how they heard about it. The collected data will help event organizers understand their audience's preferences and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and understand what each field is asking for.
2. Start by filling in the required fields:
	* **Name of Event or Show**: Enter the title of the event or show you attended.
	* **Which act was your absolute favorite?**: Enter the name of the act that impressed you the most.
3. Choose a rating for **Overall Show Rating**: Select a number of stars (1-5) to rate the overall quality of the event.
4. If you want to provide additional feedback, fill in **Favorite Moment**: This is a text field for any specific moment that you found particularly memorable.
5. For **Venue Satisfaction**, choose a rating for how satisfied you were with the venue:
	* Very Satisfied
	* Satisfied
	* Neutral
	* Dissatisfied
6. Select how you heard about the event:
	* Social Media
	* Word of Mouth
	* Poster or Flyer
	* Local News
	* Website
	* Other (please specify in **Additional Comments**)
7. Finally, decide if you would **Recommend this show to a friend**:
	* Definitely
	* Probably
	* Maybe
	* Not likely

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Audience Choice Award --**: This is an introductory note to the form. Please disregard and move on to the questions.
* **Name of Event or Show**: This is the title of the event or show you attended, and it's required for identification purposes.
* **Which act was your absolute favorite?**: Please enter the name of the act that impressed you the most during the event.
* **Overall Show Rating**: This is a rating for the overall quality of the event, with 1-5 stars representing poor, good, great, amazing, and best respectively.
* **Favorite Moment**: This is an optional text field for you to describe a specific moment that stood out to you during the event.
* **Venue Satisfaction**: This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with the venue, with options ranging from Very Satisfied to Dissatisfied.
* **How did you hear about this show?**: This field asks you to select how you found out about the event, with options including Social Media, Word of Mouth, Poster or Flyer, Local News, and Website.
* **Would you recommend this show to a friend?**: This field asks you to choose how likely you are to recommend the event to someone else.
* **Additional Comments**: This is a text field for any additional feedback or comments you would like to provide to the event organizers.
