# Educational Leadership Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Educational Leadership Assessment Survey is designed to evaluate the quality of educational leadership in a school or institution. It helps to identify areas of strength and weakness in leadership practices and policies, and to develop targeted strategies for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, select the response that best reflects your perception of the school's educational leadership.
3. If a question asks for multiple responses, select all that apply.
