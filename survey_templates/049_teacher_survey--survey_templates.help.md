# teacher_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from teachers about their professional background, including their title, department, salary range, years of experience, education level, language proficiency, area of interest, and employment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
3. If a field is optional, feel free to answer as much or as little as you like.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is your title** (text, required/optional): Enter your current job title or position.
* **2. What is your department** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of your department or area of specialization.
* **3. What is your department (select multiple)** (select multiple, required/optional): Select all areas of specialization you are involved in. You may select as many options as apply.
* **4. What is your salary range** (select multiple, required/true): Select a range of your annual salary, including all options that apply.
*  	* 1: Option 1-1 ($0 - $100)
*  	* 2: Option 2-1 ($101 - $200)
*  	* 3: Option 3-1 ($201 - $300)
*  	* 4: Option 4-1 ($301 - $400)
*  	* 5: Option 5-1 (over $400)
* 
* **5. What is your years of experience** (number, required/optional): Enter the number of years you have been teaching or working in your field.
* **6. What is your education level** (select one, required/optional): Select your highest level of education completed.
*  	* 1: Option 1
*  	* 2: Option 2
*  	* 3: Option 3
* **7. What is your language** (select multiple, required/true): Select all languages you are proficient in.
*  	* 1: Option 1
*  	* 2: Option 2
*  	* 3: Option 3
* **8. What is your area of interest** (text, required/optional): Enter your area of professional interest or specialization.
* **9. What are you prepared for** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your preparation or training.
* **10. What is your employment status** (select one, required/optional): Select your current employment status.
*  	* 1: Option 1
*  	* 2: Option 2
*  	* 3: Option 3
