# field-research-documentation-evaluation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help researchers document and evaluate their research projects, providing a standardized template for consistency and ease of reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your researcher name in the "researcher-name" field.
2. Describe your research question or topic in the "research-question" field.
3. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you used a specific data collection method (e.g., "Yes" for a method used, "No" for a method not used).
4. Select all data collection tools used (e.g., "Yes" for a tool used, "No" for a tool not used) in the "data-collection-tool" field.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have feedback sources.
6. Select the evaluation criteria used for your research project (e.g., "Good" or "Poor").
7. Describe your research outcomes in the "research-outcomes" field.
8. Identify any challenges faced during the research project in the "research-challenges" field.
9. List the benefits of your research project in the "research-benefits" field.
10. Identify any limitations of your research project in the "research-limitations" field.
11. Add any notes or comments about your research project in the "researcher-notes" field.
11. Specify the duration of your research project in the "research-duration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **researcher-name**: This field is for the researcher's name.
* **research-question**: This field is for the research question or topic.
* **data-collection-method**: This field is for indicating whether a specific data collection method was used. Select "Yes" for methods used and "No" for methods not used.
* **data-collection-tool**: This field is for selecting all data collection tools used. Select "Yes" for tools used and "No" for tools not used.
* **evaluation-criteria**: This field is for selecting the evaluation criteria used for your research project. Choose "Good" or "Poor" depending on your evaluation.
* **feedback-sources**: This field is for indicating whether you have feedback sources for your research project. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **data-quality**: This field is for evaluating the data quality of your research project. Choose "Good" or "Poor".
* **research-outcomes**: This field is for describing the outcomes of your research project.
* **research-challenges**: This field is for identifying any challenges faced during the research project.
* **research-benefits**: This field is for listing the benefits of your research project.
* **research-limitations**: This field is for identifying any limitations of your research project.
* **researcher-notes**: This field is for adding any notes or comments about your research project.
* **research-duration**: This field is for specifying the duration of your research project.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required fields.
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out each field.
- Use the provided examples as a guide for formatting and structuring your answers.
