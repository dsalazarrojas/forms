# Student Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students regarding their experiences and thoughts about school-related behavior. The information gathered from this form will be used to identify areas of improvement and enhance the overall student experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete this form in its entirety, as some sections may be required for data collection and analysis.
- Select the correct answers from the provided options or enter your text responses according to the specified format.
- If you have any additional comments or thoughts, feel free to include them in the "Any comments?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel about your school? (Introduction)** (`text`, required): 
  Please share your overall sentiment or feelings about the school. This field is required for us to gauge your emotional state or satisfaction level with the school.

* **Student ID (Student ID)** (`select_one`, required): 
  Please select 'Yes' if you have experienced or have had an incident related to your behavior at school. This field helps us identify which students have had issues or concerns.

* **School Name (School Name)** (`text`, optional): 
  If you feel comfortable sharing, please enter your school's name. This optional field helps us understand which school you are referring to.

* **What do you think about this question? (Questions)** (`text`, optional): 
  Please provide any thoughts or reflections you have about the questions asked in this survey. This field is for your personal comments or insights.

* **Answer (Answers)** (`select_multiple`, optional): 
  Choose all the options that apply to your situation. Select 'Yes' for each answer that you feel is relevant to you.

* **Any comments? (Any comments?)** (`note`, optional): 
  Feel free to share any additional thoughts, ideas, or concerns you may have. This field is for any further information you would like to provide.

* **Email (Email)** (`email`, optional): 
  If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or further discussion, please provide your email address. Your email will be kept confidential and used only for the purpose of this survey.

* **Phone (Phone)** (`text`, optional): 
  If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your phone number. Your phone number will be kept confidential and used only for the purpose of this survey.
