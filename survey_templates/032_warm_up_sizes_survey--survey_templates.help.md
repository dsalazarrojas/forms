# Warm Up Sizes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warm Up Sizes Survey is designed to collect information from users, specifically their warm-up sizes and preferences. This form aims to gather data for internal analysis or processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. For each field, select the relevant options or enter your details as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Home Page (Page 1)
* **Home Page** (Page: 1, Type: note, Required: false): This page serves as an introduction to the survey.
### Warm Up Sizes (Page 2)
* **Warm Up Sizes** (Page: 2, Type: select_multiple, Required: false): Select all the sizes that apply to you from the options provided.
### What is your size? (Page 3)
* **What is your size?** (Page: 3, Type: select_one, Required: false): Choose the specific size that best fits you from the options provided:
	+ 4XL
	+ 5XL
	+ 6XL
	+ 7XL
### Fit Preferences (Page 4)
* **Fit Preferences** (Page: 4, Type: select_multiple, Required: false): Select your fit preferences from the options provided.
### Do you prefer a loose or fitted fit? (Page 5)
* **Do you prefer a loose or fitted fit?** (Page: 5, Type: select_one, Required: false): Choose your preferred fit from the options provided:
	+ Loose
	+ Fitted
### Contact Details (Page 6)
* **Contact Details** (Page: 6, Type: note, Required: false): This page is for additional comments or notes.
### Email (Page 7)
* **Email** (Page: 7, Type: email, Required: false): Enter your email address.
### Phone (Page 8)
* **Phone** (Page: 8, Type: text, Required: false): Enter your phone number.
### Name (Page 9)
* **Name** (Page: 9, Type: text, Required: false): Enter your name.
### School or Club (Page 10)
* **School or Club** (Page: 10, Type: select_one, Required: false): Choose where you belong from the options provided:
	+ Coach
	+ School
	+ Club
### Sport (Page 11)
* **Sport** (Page: 11, Type: text, Required: false): Enter the name of the sport you play.
### Team Name (Page 12)
* **Team Name** (Page: 12, Type: text, Required: false): Enter your team's name.
### Order Details (Page 13)
* **Order Details** (Page: 13, Type: text, Required: false): Enter any additional order details.
### Order Notes (Page 14)
* **Order Notes** (Page: 14, Type: text, Required: false): Enter any additional notes about your order.
