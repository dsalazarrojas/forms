# post_festival_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect post-festival event information for internal use. Please fill it out thoroughly and accurately to ensure a smooth process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the festival name and event details in **Section 1**.
2. Enter your contact email, phone number, and other contact information in **Section 2**.
3. Confirm event timing, location, and other essential details in **Section 3**.
4. Review and submit the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival Name** (`festival_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the festival that has taken place.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for additional communication.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event that took place.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event in dd/mm/yyyy format.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the start and end time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00 - 17:00).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional event notes or comments.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, optional): Enter your title or job position.
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Contact Position** (`contact_position`, text, optional): Enter your position or role.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Company** (`contact_company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00).
* **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00).

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of all provided information.
- Ensure all required fields are filled in completely and accurately.
- If you have any questions or doubts, feel free to reach out to our support team.
