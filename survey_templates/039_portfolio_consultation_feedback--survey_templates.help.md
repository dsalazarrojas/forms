# Portfolio Consultation Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Portfolio Consultation Feedback form is designed to gather feedback from clients after a portfolio consultation session. This feedback is used to improve the quality of services provided by the consultants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to "Client Feedback" from the options provided.
2. Enter the date and time of the session you attended.
3. Provide the name of the consultant who led the session.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the session on a scale of 1-5.
5. Write any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about the session.
6. Enter your email address and phone number so that we can follow up with you if needed.
7. Finally, provide the name of the client who attended the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Feedback** (`portfolio_consultation_feedback_form`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options to indicate your satisfaction with the session. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you attended the session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the session you attended.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the consultant who led the session.
* **Rating (1-5)** (`consultant_rating`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the session on a scale of 1-5.
* **Qualitative Feedback** (`qualitative_feedback`, text, optional): Write any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about the session.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Name of Client** (`name_of_client`, text, optional): Provide the name of the client who attended the session.

## Tips
Please be as specific as possible when answering the "Qualitative Feedback" and "Additional Comments" fields, as they help us understand your thoughts and opinions about the session.
