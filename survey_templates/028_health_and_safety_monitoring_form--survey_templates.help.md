# health_and_safety_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employee health and safety incidents that occur during work hours. It helps in tracking and managing such incidents, ensuring a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you are filling out the form for an incident that occurred while you were working.
2. Enter your name and employee ID number in the corresponding fields.
3. Report the date of the incident.
4. Describe the incident that occurred in the incident description field.
5. Provide a brief description of any injuries sustained.
6. If treatment was provided, describe it in the treatment field.
7. Specify if a follow-up is required or not.
8. If medical certification is needed, select 'Yes' and provide details in the medical certification field.
9. Choose whether you are ready to return to work normally, on restricted duties, or on medical leave.
10. Enter your supervisor's name and their contact number.
11. Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you for any further information.
12. If you visited a medical provider, enter their name and contact number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required: false): Enter your employee ID number as per your personnel record.
* **Date of Occurrence** (`date_of_occurrence`, date, required: false): Report the date of the incident.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Describe the incident that occurred during work hours.
* **Injury Description** (`injury_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of any injuries sustained.
* **Treatment** (`treatment`, text, required: false): If treatment was provided, describe it here.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, required: false): Specify if a follow-up is required or not.
* **Medical Certification** (`medical_certification`, select_multiple, required: false): If medical certification is needed, select 'Yes' and provide details.
* **Return to Work** (`return_to_work`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you are ready to return to work normally, on restricted duties, or on medical leave.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required: false): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Contact Number** (`supervisor_contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your supervisor's contact number.
* **Employee Contact Number** (`employee_contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you for any further information.
* **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`, text, required: false): If you visited a medical provider, enter their name.
* **Medical Provider Contact Number** (`medical_provider_contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the medical provider's contact number.

## Tips
- Always fill out the form for incidents that occur while working.
- Be as detailed as possible in your descriptions and answers.
- If unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
