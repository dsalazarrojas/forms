# Yes Or No Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for creating Yes or No survey templates.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Yes or no questions** (`yes_or_no_survey_template_survey_questions`, select_multiple, required): Select all the questions that will be included in your survey.
* **Survey Type** (`yes_or_no_survey_template_survey_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of survey you're creating (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Description** (`yes_or_no_survey_template_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your survey template.
* **Category** (`yes_or_no_survey_template_category`, select_one, required): Select the category for your survey template (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Tags** (`yes_or_no_survey_template_tags`, text, required): Add any relevant tags or keywords to help identify your survey template.
* **Created by** (`yes_or_no_survey_template_created_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the user who created this survey template.
