# creative_characteristics_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The creative_characteristics_survey form is designed to gather information on an individual's creative characteristics, preferences, and skills. It is intended to be used by professionals such as psychologists, educators, or coaches to assess an individual's potential for creativity, problem-solving skills, and other cognitive abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the questions being asked.
2. Answer the questions honestly, based on your personal experiences and preferences.
3. For each field, select the options that best describe your characteristics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro`, `note`, not required): This is a introductory note that provides background information about the form.
* **Creative traits test** (`creative_traits_test`, `select_one`, not required): This question assesses your creative inclination. Choose 'Yes' if you consider yourself creative, and 'No' if you don't.
* **Preference for artistic activities** (`preference_for_artistic_activities`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about your preference for activities that involve art, music, or other creative pursuits. Choose all the activities that interest you.
* **Preference for adventurous activities** (`preference_for_adventurous_activities`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about your preference for activities that involve risk-taking, exploration, or challenge. Choose all the activities that interest you.
* **Preference for scientific activities** (`preference_for_scientific_activities`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about your preference for activities that involve scientific inquiry, experimentation, or research. Choose all the activities that interest you.
* **Preference for sports activities** (`preference_for_sports_activities`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about your preference for activities that involve physical exercise, competition, or strategy. Choose all the activities that interest you.
* **Self reported creativity** (`self_reported_creativity`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about your self-assessment of your creativity. Choose 'Yes' if you consider yourself creative, and 'No' if you don't.
* **Observed creativity** (`observed_creativity`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about your observed creativity by others. Choose 'Yes' if you consider yourself creative as observed by others, and 'No' if you don't.
* **Creative traits test result** (`creative_traits_test_result`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question summarizes the results of the creative traits test. Choose all the characteristics that you think apply to you.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Take your time to carefully consider each question before making a choice.
* If you are unsure about any question, you can ask for help or clarification.
