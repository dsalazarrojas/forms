# Tech Conference Attendee Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from attendees of the tech conference. Your responses will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answer that best matches your experience with the conference. For the "How satisfied are you with the conference?" question, choose one of the provided options (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Very Not Satisfied). For the "Which topics did you find most valuable?" question, select all that apply. For the "Do you have any additional feedback?" question, type as much as you'd like. For the "Your Email" and "Your Phone" fields, enter your contact information, which will be used for follow-up questions. For the "Your Name" field, enter your name as you would like to be addressed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How satisfied are you with the conference?** (`attendee_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): How satisfied were you with the overall experience at the tech conference? Choose one of the options provided.
  - **Very Satisfied**
  - **Somewhat Satisfied**
  - **Not Satisfied**
  - **Very Not Satisfied**
- **Which topics did you find most valuable?** (`conference_topics`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the topics you found most valuable during the conference. 
  - **Technical**
  - **Business**
  - **Social**
- **How would you rate the speaker performance?** (`speaker_performance`, select_one, required: false): Rate the speakers' performance during the conference.
  - **Excellent**
  - **Good**
  - **Fair**
  - **Poor**
  - **Very Poor**
- **Do you have any additional feedback?** (`feedback`, note, required: false): If you have any thoughts or suggestions about the conference, please type them here.
- **Your Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for follow-up questions.
- **Your Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you for follow-up questions.
- **Your Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.

## Tips
Please be honest and specific with your answers, and remember that your responses will help us create a better experience for future conferences.
