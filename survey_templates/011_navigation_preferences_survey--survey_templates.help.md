# Navigation Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Navigation Preferences Survey aims to gather your opinions about the navigation tools you've used in the past and help us understand your preferences for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of survey template you'd like to use (Survey Templates or Product Surveys).
2. Indicate how often you've used the Navigation Tool (a number from 0 to 10).
3. Choose your satisfaction level with the current navigation features (Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied).
4. Select the features you consider most important for future development (Navigation Bar, Navigation Menu, or both).
5. If you'd like, add any additional comments or notes about your experience (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Templates** (`survey_template_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of survey template you'd like to use. Select either "Survey Templates" or "Product Surveys" to help us understand what kind of navigation preferences you're interested in.
  - This field is required to ensure we understand the context of your survey.
- **Navigation Tool Usage** (`navigation_tool_usage`, number, required): Indicate how often you've used the Navigation Tool in the past. Think of a number between 0 (never) and 10 (very frequently).
- **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with the current navigation features. Select from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied to provide us with your honest opinion.
- **Feature Priorities** (`feature_priorities`, select_multiple, required): Select the features you consider most important for future development. Choose from Navigation Bar, Navigation Menu, or both.
  - This field helps us understand what features you value the most.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes about your experience, feel free to add them here. This is an optional field, so you can skip it if you don't have anything to say.

## Tips
- Please be honest in your answers, and remember that your feedback is valuable to us.
- If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
- This survey is a short and quick way for you to provide us with insights into your navigation preferences. Thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts!
