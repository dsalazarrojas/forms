# risk_perception_benchmark_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your perception of various risks that may impact your business operations. It will guide you through a series of questions to understand your risk factors, likelihood, impact, and confidence in your assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading each question carefully and answering accordingly.
2. Answer questions based on your actual experience and perception.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`page 1`, `text`, optional): Briefly introduce yourself and provide a background on your risk perception.
  - A good starting point for your risk assessment.
- **What are some of the risk factors you are concerned about?** (`risk_factors`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the risk factors that you consider significant.
  - This is where you will start identifying specific risks.
- **Any other risk factors you are concerned about** (`risk_factors_other`, `text`, optional): List any additional risk factors not captured in the previous question.
  - A chance to capture any other risks not already listed.
- **How frequently do you perceive these risks to be occurring?** (`frequency`, `number`, required): Provide a numerical value (0-100) that represents your perceived frequency of each risk.
  - This is a quantitative measure of how often you see these risks happening.
- **How long ago did you last experience this risk?** (`time_last_occurrence`, `date`, optional): If possible, provide a specific date or range of dates for the last time you experienced this risk.
  - This will help with understanding the recency of the risk.
- **What impact do you think this risk has on your business?** (`impact`, `text`, optional): Describe the impact of the risk on your operations.
  - A qualitative measure of the risk's effect on your business.
- **How much of a risk do you think this poses to you?** (`risk_precedence`, `number`, required): Provide a numerical value (1-10) that represents the level of risk posed by each risk.
  - A measure of how significant this risk is.
- **How likely is it that you will encounter this risk again?** (`likelihood`, `number`, optional): Provide a numerical value (0-100) that represents your perceived likelihood of encountering the risk again.
  - Another way to understand the risk's frequency.
- **What are the consequences of this risk?** (`consequence`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the best option that describes the consequences of the risk.
  - A clear understanding of what happens if the risk materializes.
- **How do you mitigate this risk?** (`mitigation`, `text`, optional): Describe how you or your organization plans to handle or mitigate the risk.
  - Your risk management strategy for this specific risk.
- **What is your risk score for this risk?** (`risk_score`, `number`, optional): Provide a numerical value (1-10) that represents your overall risk score.
  - Your assessment of how risky it is.
- **How confident are you about your risk assessment?** (`confidence`, `number`, optional): Provide a numerical value (0-100) that represents your confidence in your assessment.
  - A measure of how certain you are about your risk evaluation.
- **What level of risk do you think this poses?** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best option that describes the level of risk.
  - A clear categorization of the risk level.
- **What level of risk do you think this poses? (Other)** (`risk_level_other`, `text`, optional): If not captured in the previous question, describe your understanding of the risk level here.
  - An explanation of your risk level that wasn't covered in the previous question.
- **Describe the risk** (`risk_description`, `text`, optional): Give an in-depth description of the risk.
  - Further details on the specific risk.
- **Risk level Other 1** (`risk_precedence_other`, `text`, optional): Describe your risk level if it's not captured in the previous questions.
  - Another explanation of your risk level that might not have been captured.
- **Risk Likelihood Other 1** (`risk_likelihood_other`, `text`, optional): Describe your perceived likelihood of the risk if not covered in previous questions.
  - A qualitative measure of your risk likelihood.
- **Risk Consequence Other 1** (`risk_consequence_other`, `text`, optional): Describe the consequences of the risk if not captured in previous questions.
  - What happens if the risk materializes.
- **Risk Score Other 1** (`risk_score_other`, `text`, optional): Describe your overall risk score if not captured in previous questions.
  - Another way to evaluate the risk.
- **Risk Level Other 2** (`risk_level_other_2`, `text`, optional): Describe your risk level if not covered in previous questions.
  - An additional way to express your risk level.
- **Risk Likelihood Other 2** (`risk_likelihood_other_2`, `text`, optional): Describe your perceived likelihood of the risk if not captured in previous questions.
  - A qualitative measure of the risk's likelihood.
- **Risk Consequence Other 2** (`risk_consequence_other_2`, `text`, optional): Describe the consequences of the risk if not captured in previous questions.
  - What happens if the risk materializes.
- **Submission** (`submission`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the risk assessment process.
  - Last opportunity to share thoughts or concerns.
