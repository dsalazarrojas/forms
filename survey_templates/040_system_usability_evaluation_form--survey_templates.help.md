# System Usability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user input on the usability of a system, product, or experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Input Section with your thoughts and opinions about your experience.
2. Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5 in the Rating field.
3. Provide more detailed feedback in the Feedback field.
4. Enter your Email address (optional).
5. Enter your Phone Number (optional).
6. Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5 in the Usability Rating field (optional).
7. Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale of 1-5 in the Overall Satisfaction field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input Section** (`user_input_section`, `text`, optional): This field allows you to provide your thoughts and opinions about your experience. Be as detailed and specific as possible.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, optional): Please rate your experience on a scale of 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide more detailed feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Usability Rating** (`usability_rating`, `number`, optional): Please rate your experience on a scale of 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good), specifically regarding usability.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): Please rate your overall satisfaction on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
