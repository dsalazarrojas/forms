# Wedding DJ and Lighting Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a wedding DJ and lighting quote. It will help us understand your event details and provide a quote for our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out all the required fields with your wedding event details.
2. Select the correct options for each field where applicable.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Quote Details" field.
4. Review and submit your quote request form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Please enter the name of the person who will be the main contact for this event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, required): Enter the email address of the contact person. This email will be used to communicate with you about your quote.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, number, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the wedding event.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the event.
* **Wedding Time** (`wedding_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the wedding event.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, required): Select the location of the wedding event from the options provided.
* **Number of Hours** (`number_of_hours`, number, required): Enter the estimated number of hours you would like the services to be provided for.
* **Equipment Needed** (`equipment_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select the equipment you need for your event.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, required): Enter any special requests you may have for your event.
* **Music Preferences** (`music_preferences`, select_one, required): Select the type of music you would like played during the event.
* **Equipment Provided** (`equipment_provided`, select_multiple, required): Select which equipment you would like us to bring to the event.
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select which services you would like us to provide during the event.
* **Other Services** (`other_services`, select_multiple, required): Select any other services you would like us to provide.
* **Quote Details** (`quote_details`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or details about your quote.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Please sign this form to confirm your request.
* **Quote** (`quote`, text, required): Please review and provide any comments or feedback on the quote provided.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select the option to submit your quote request form.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, select_one, required): Select the option to cancel your quote request form.
* **Other Quote Details** (`other_quote_details`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or details about your quote.
* **Equipment Price** (`equipment_price`, text, required): Please enter the price of the equipment you need.
* **Total Quote** (`total_quote`, text, required): Please review and provide any comments or feedback on the total quote provided.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required): Please review and provide any comments or feedback on the payment terms.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Please enter any additional comments or details about your quote.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the payment status of your quote.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Select the payment method for your quote.

Note: The required fields are marked as required in the form. Please fill out all required fields to ensure that we have the necessary information to provide an accurate quote for your event.
