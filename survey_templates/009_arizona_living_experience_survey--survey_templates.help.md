# Arizona Living Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals about their living experience in Arizona. It collects data on their satisfaction with housing, community, and services, as well as their main concerns and future plans for the state.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Respondents need to start by providing their information at the beginning of the form, including their name and email address.
2.  Then, they are asked about their length of time living in Arizona, selecting one of the given options.
3.  Respondents then choose the region of Arizona they live in.
4.  The form inquires about the respondent's satisfaction with their current housing, selecting one of the given options.
5.  Next, they evaluate the housing affordability in their area, choosing one of the given options.
6.  The form asks about their connection to their community, choosing one of the given options.
7.  Respondents assess their overall satisfaction with living in Arizona, selecting one of the given options.
8.  Then, they express their feelings about Arizona's weather, choosing one of the given options.
9.  They rate the frequency of their outdoor activities, selecting one of the given options.
10.  Respondents are asked about their access to healthcare services, choosing one of the given options.
11.  They evaluate the quality of education in their area, choosing one of the given options.
12.  The form inquires about the availability of public transportation, choosing one of the given options.
13.  Respondents list their main concerns about living in Arizona, selecting multiple options.
14.  They state their plans for staying in Arizona, choosing one of the given options.
15.  Finally, they can add any additional comments about their living experience in Arizona.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for follow-up communications.
*   **How long have you lived in Arizona** (`years_in_arizona`, select_one, required): Select how long you have lived in Arizona:
    *   Less than 1 year
    *   1-3 years
    *   3-5 years
    *   5-10 years
    *   More than 10 years
    *   Entire life
*   **Which region of Arizona do you live in** (`current_region`, select_one, required): Choose the region of Arizona where you reside:
    *   Phoenix metro area
    *   Tucson area
    *   Northern Arizona
    *   Eastern Arizona
    *   Western Arizona
    *   Other

    *   Very satisfied
    *   Satisfied
    *   Neutral
    *   Dissatisfied
    *   Very dissatisfied
*   **Housing affordability in your area** (`housing_affordability`, select_one, required): Evaluate the affordability of housing in your area:
    *   Very affordable
    *   Affordable
    *   Moderate cost
    *   Expensive
    *   Very expensive
*   **How connected do you feel to your community** (`community_connection`, select_one, required): Assess your connection to your community:
    *   Very connected
    *   Connected
    *   Somewhat connected
    *   Not very connected
    *   Not connected at all
*   **Overall satisfaction with living in Arizona** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with living in Arizona:
    *   Very satisfied

    *   Neutral
    *   Dissatisfied
    *   Very dissatisfied
*   **How do you feel about Arizona weather** (`weather_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Express your feelings about Arizona's weather:
    *   Love it

    *   Neutral
    *   Dislike it

*   **Frequency of outdoor activities** (`outdoor_activities`, select_one, optional): Choose the frequency of your outdoor activities:
    *   Daily or almost daily

    *   Excellent

    *   Traffic and congestion
    *   Job opportunities
    *   Education quality
    *   Healthcare access

    *   Definitely yes
    *   Probably yes
    *   Undecided
    *   Probably no
    *   Definitely no
*   **Additional comments about Arizona living** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your living experience in Arizona.

## Tips

*   Please answer each question honestly, and do not leave any fields blank without a response.
*   If you have any additional comments, please type them in the "Additional comments" field.
*   This form is designed to gather information about your living experience in Arizona. Your responses will help us understand your thoughts and feelings about the state.
