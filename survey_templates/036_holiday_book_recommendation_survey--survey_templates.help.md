# Holiday Book Recommendation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about users' holiday book preferences and suggestions. It aims to collect data on users' preferred book genres, suggested books, and holiday interests to help create personalized book recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each page of the form carefully.
2.  On the **Reader Information** page, no input is required as it is not a form field.
3.  On the **Book Preferences** page, select up to 5 book genres that you enjoy reading from the given options.
4.  On the **Suggested Books** page, select up to 5 books from the listed options.
5.  On the **Holiday Suggestion** page, no input is required as it is a date field.
6.  On the **Suggested Holiday** page, select your preferred holiday from the given options.
7.  On the **Email** page, enter a valid email address in the format of `username@domain.com`.
8.  On the **Phone** page, enter your phone number.
9.  On the **Additional Comments** page, provide any additional comments or information you'd like to share about your holiday book preferences.
