# Agricultural Record Keeping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agricultural Record Keeping Survey is designed to gather information from farmers about their current practices and interests regarding agricultural record keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all the questions truthfully, as your responses will help us better understand your needs and challenges.
2. Select the correct answer for each question based on your current practices and experiences.
3. Be as specific as possible when answering the "Records Kept" and "Primary Challenges" questions.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please answer "None" or "Not Applicable" and explain briefly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farmer Name**: Your name as a farmer.
	+ This is an optional field where you can provide your name for identification purposes.
* **Farm Size**: Total acres managed by you or your farm operation.
	+ This field is required to understand your scale of operation.
* **Primary Record Keeping Method**: How do you currently store data?
	+ Select from the options: Paper / Notebooks, Spreadsheets (Excel), Specialized Farm Software, Mobile App, or None.
* **Frequency of Updates**: How often do you log data?
	+ Select from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Seasonally, or Only for tax time.
* **Types of Records Kept**: Select all that apply.
	+ This field allows you to indicate the types of records you currently keep, such as Financial / Expenses, Production / Yields, Labor / Payroll, Inventory, Machinery Maintenance, or Breeding / Genetics.
* **Primary Challenges**: What makes record keeping difficult for you?
	+ Select all that apply.
	+ This field helps us understand the challenges you face in keeping accurate records.
* **Interest in Training**: Would you like to learn better methods?
	+ Select from the options: True, False, or Maybe.
* **Preferred Training Format**: How do you learn best?
	+ Select from the options: In-person Workshop, Online Webinar, One-on-one Coaching, or Self-paced Video Tutorials.

Note: The questions are designed to be clear and concise, but if you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
