# Life Coaching Goal Planning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Life Coaching Goal Planning Survey is designed to help you assess your motivation levels, identify areas for personal growth, and set specific goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the multiple-choice questions for each section, selecting all that apply.
2. Provide detailed responses to the text fields, describing your thoughts and feelings.
3. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section serves as an optional introduction to the survey.
* **goals**: Please select all that apply.
	+ 'Yes' - You have set clear and specific goals.
	+ 'No' - You have not set clear and specific goals.
* **motivation_levels**: Please select one that applies.
	+ high - You are highly motivated.
	+ medium - You are moderately motivated.
	+ low - You are not motivated.
* **challenges**: Describe any challenges you are currently facing.
* **goals_1**: Please select all that apply.
	+ 'Yes' - You have set a specific goal related to goal 1.
	+ 'No' - You do not have a specific goal related to goal 1.
* **goals_2**: Please select all that apply.
	+ 'Yes' - You have set a specific goal related to goal 2.
	+ 'No' - You do not have a specific goal related to goal 2.
* **goals_3**: Please select all that apply.
	+ 'Yes' - You have set a specific goal related to goal 3.
	+ 'No' - You do not have a specific goal related to goal 3.
* **progress**: This field is for tracking progress, but is not required. Please enter your progress in the format:  [time] [description].
* **follow_up**: Describe any additional comments or feedback you would like to provide.

## Tips

* Be honest and take your time to answer each question.
* Review and reflect on your responses before submitting the form.
* This form is for personal use and will not be shared with anyone.
