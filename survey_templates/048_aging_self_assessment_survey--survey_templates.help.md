# Aging Self Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your physical and mental well-being, as well as your ability to perform daily activities. It will ask you about your health, daily routines, and technology use. Your input will help us provide better support and services for older adults like you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your name and current age.
2. Select your gender from the given options.
3. Rate your overall health and answer questions about chronic conditions, mobility, and vision/hearing difficulties.
4. Move on to the "Daily Activities" section, where you'll answer questions about bathing, dressing, meal preparation, and medication management.
5. In the "Mental Health" section, rate your mood and answer questions about anxiety frequency and memory concerns.
6. In the "Social Connections" section, answer questions about social activity frequency and loneliness.
7. Finally, answer questions about your living arrangement and technology use, including your comfort level with technology and any assistance you might need.
8. End by providing any additional comments or concerns you'd like to share about your aging experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Respondent Name
* **Respondent Name**: Enter your name, which is optional but helpful for our records.
### 2. Respondent Age
* **Respondent Age**: Enter your current age.
### 3. Gender
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the given options: Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say.
### 4. General Health Rating
* **General Health Rating**: Rate your overall health from the options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
### 5. Chronic Conditions
* **Chronic Conditions**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have any chronic health conditions.
### 6. Chronic Conditions List
* **Chronic Conditions List**: If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, list your chronic conditions.
### 7. Mobility Difficulty
* **Mobility Difficulty**: Select how much difficulty you experience with mobility or walking from the options: No difficulty, Some difficulty, Much difficulty, or Cannot do at all.
### 8. Vision/Hearing Difficulty
* **Vision/Hearing Difficulty**: Select how much difficulty you experience with vision or hearing from the options: No difficulty, Some difficulty, Much difficulty, or Cannot do at all.
### 9. Bathing Difficulty
* **Bathing Difficulty**: Select how much difficulty you experience with bathing or showering from the options: No difficulty, Some difficulty, Much difficulty, or Cannot do at all.
### 10. Dressing Difficulty
* **Dressing Difficulty**: Select how much difficulty you experience with dressing from the options: No difficulty, Some difficulty, Much difficulty, or Cannot do at all.
### 11. Meal Preparation Difficulty
* **Meal Preparation Difficulty**: Select how much difficulty you experience with preparing meals from the options: No difficulty, Some difficulty, Much difficulty, or Cannot do at all.
### 12. Medication Management Difficulty
* **Medication Management Difficulty**: Select how much difficulty you experience managing your medications from the options: No difficulty, Some difficulty, Much difficulty, or Cannot do at all.
### 13. Mental Health
* **Mental Health**: Rate your mental health from the options: Very happy, Happy, Neutral, Sad, or Very sad.
### 14. Anxiety Frequency
* **Anxiety Frequency**: Select how often you experience anxiety from the options: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, or Always.
### 15. Memory Concerns
* **Memory Concerns**: Select how much concern you have about your memory from the options: No concerns, Minor concerns, Moderate concerns, or Significant concerns.
### 16. Social Activity Frequency
* **Social Activity Frequency**: Select how often you participate in social activities from the options: Daily, Several times a week, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.
### 17. Loneliness Frequency
* **Loneliness Frequency**: Select how often you feel lonely from the options: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, or Always.
### 18. Living Arrangement
* **Living Arrangement**: Select your current living arrangement from the options: Alone, With spouse/partner, With family, In assisted living, In nursing home, or Other.
### 19. Technology Comfort Level
* **Technology Comfort Level**: Select how comfortable you are with using technology from the options: Very comfortable, Comfortable, Neutral, Uncomfortable, or Very uncomfortable.
### 20. Technology Assistance Needs
* **Technology Assistance Needs**: Select the types of technology assistance you need, if any, from the options: Smartphone usage, Computer skills, Video calling, Online shopping, Telehealth, Social media, None, or Other.
### 21. Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or concerns you'd like to add about your aging experience.
