# Socioeconomic Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Socioeconomic Registration Form is used to collect information on a family member's demographic details, employment status, education level, and language proficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the household income field with the current income of the household.
2. Enter the size of the household.
3. Select the employment status of the family member (unemployed, employed, retired, or student).
4. Enter the age of the family member.
5. Select the gender of the family member (male, female, or other).
6. Enter the education level of the family member (high school, bachelor, master, or doctor).
7. Enter the type of employment (part time, full time, contract, or self-employed).
8. Select the marital status of the family member (married, single, divorced, or widowed).
9. Enter the country of birth of the family member.
10. Enter the language spoken by the family member.
11. Select the migration status of the family member (citizen, non-citizen, or resident).
12. Enter the ethnic group of the family member.
13. Select the language proficiency of the family member (fluent, basic, or none).
14. Enter the employment history of the family member in the last 3 months.
15. Enter the duration of previous employment of the family member.
16. Select the education level of the family member (elementary, high school, vocational, bachelor, or master).
17. Enter the education field of the family member.
18. Enter the education institution of the family member.
19. Enter the country of education of the family member.
20. Enter any other relevant details about the family member's education.
21. Add any additional notes about the family member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Income**: Enter the current income of the household.
* **Household Size**: Enter the number of family members living in the household.
* **Employment Status**: Select the employment status of the family member (unemployed, employed, retired, or student).
* **Family Member Age**: Enter the age of the family member.
* **Family Member Gender**: Select the gender of the family member (male, female, or other).
* **Education Level**: Select the education level of the family member (high school, bachelor, master, or doctor).
* **Employment Type**: Select the type of employment of the family member (part time, full time, contract, or self-employed).
* **Family Member Marital Status**: Select the marital status of the family member (married, single, divorced, or widowed).
* **Family Member Country of Birth**: Enter the country of birth of the family member.
* **Family Member Language**: Enter the language spoken by the family member.
* **Family Member Residence Country**: Enter the country of residence of the family member.
* **Family Member Migration Status**: Select the migration status of the family member (citizen, non-citizen, or resident).
* **Family Member Ethnic Group**: Enter the ethnic group of the family member.
* **Family Member Language Spoken**: Enter the language spoken by the family member.
* **Family Member English Language**: Select the language proficiency of the family member (fluent, basic, or none).
* **Family Member Employment in Last 3 Months**: Enter the employment history of the family member in the last 3 months.
* **Family Member Previous Employment Duration**: Enter the duration of previous employment of the family member.
* **Family Member Education Level**: Select the education level of the family member (elementary, high school, vocational, bachelor, or master).
* **Family Member Education Field**: Enter the education field of the family member.
* **Family Member Education Institution**: Enter the education institution of the family member.
* **Family Member Education Country**: Enter the country of education of the family member.
* **Family Member Education Level Other**: Enter any other details about the family member's education.
* **Family Member Notes**: Add any additional notes about the family member.
