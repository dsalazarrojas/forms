# Health And Wellness Program Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your health and wellness habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer each question carefully and honestly. Most fields are optional, so you can skip them if you're not comfortable sharing that information. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **health_and_wellness_program_survey** (select_one): 
	+ This question is about your current status on the Health and Wellness Program. Please select 'Yes' if you are participating in the program.
* **daily_health_habits** (text): 
	+ This is an open-ended question where you can describe your daily health habits. Please provide a brief description of your daily routines and activities.
* **favorite_health_topics** (text): 
	+ In this field, please enter your favorite health topics or areas of interest (e.g., exercise, nutrition, stress management, etc.).
* **exercise_frequency** (select_one): 
	+ This question is about your current exercise frequency. Please select 'Yes' if you exercise regularly.
* **food_choices** (select_multiple): 
	+ This question is about your food choices. Please select 'Yes' for each type of food you consume regularly (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.).
* **sleep_pattern** (text): 
	+ This is an open-ended question where you can describe your sleep pattern. Please provide a brief description of your sleep schedule and quality.
* **health_insights** (note): 
	+ In this field, please provide any health insights or observations you'd like to share.
* **other_topics_of_interest** (text): 
	+ This is an open-ended question where you can describe any other topics of interest related to health and wellness.
* **demographics** (select_multiple): 
	+ This question is about your demographics. Please select 'Yes' for each demographic category that applies to you (e.g., male/female, age group, etc.).
