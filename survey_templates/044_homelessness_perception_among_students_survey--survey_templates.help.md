# Homelessness Perception Among Students Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homelessness Perception Among Students Survey is a tool designed to gather insights and opinions from students on homelessness. This survey aims to understand students' perceptions, knowledge, and experiences related to homelessness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by reading the introduction page, which provides a brief overview of the survey and its purpose.
2.  Answer the questions on the following pages, which are designed to gather more specific information from you.
3.  Select all the services and resources you are familiar with from the list on page 4.
4.  Choose the amount of time you've spent learning about homelessness from the options on page 5.
5.  On page 6, select all the demographics that apply to you.
6.  Finally, provide your contact information on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, optional): This page provides a brief overview of the survey and its purpose.
    *   This is a read-only page and you do not need to provide any input.
*   **How much do you know about homelessness?** (`How much do you know about homelessness?`, `text`, optional): 
    *   Please provide a brief description of your understanding or knowledge about homelessness.
*   **What does homelessness mean to you?** (`What does homelessness mean to you?`, `text`, optional): 
    *   Share your thoughts on what homelessness means to you and how you perceive it.
*   **What could be done to help people who are homeless?** (`What could be done to help people who are homeless?`, `text`, optional): 
    *   Share your ideas and suggestions on what could be done to support and help people who are homeless.
*   **How much do you know about services and resources?** (`How much do you know about services and resources for people who are homeless?`, `select_multiple`, required): 
    *   Select all the services and resources you are familiar with to help people who are homeless.
*   **How much time have you spent learning about homelessness?** (`How much time have you spent learning about homelessness?`, `select_one`, optional): 
    *   Choose the amount of time you've spent learning about homelessness from the options provided.
*   **Demographics** (`Demographics`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
    *   Select all the demographics that apply to you.
*   **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional): 
    *   Provide your contact information if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and sincere in your answers, as your responses will help in better understanding and addressing the issue of homelessness.
*   You can select all that apply from the options provided for questions with multiple-choice options.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the survey administrator for clarification.
