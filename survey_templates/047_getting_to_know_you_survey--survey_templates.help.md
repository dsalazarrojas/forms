# Getting To Know You Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal information and preferences from survey respondents to better understand the target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as requested. Each field has a specific purpose, and instructions are provided below to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Getting To Know You** (`intro_text`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can briefly introduce yourself. Please provide a short introduction about who you are and why you're interested in this survey.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please provide a valid email address associated with your name.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as a series of digits (without any special characters).
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Select your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Select your time of birth in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Which category are you interested in?** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the list that best describes your interest.
* **Which categories do you like?** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the list that best describe your interests.
* **Any additional information** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any further details or comments you'd like to share with the survey administrators.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all necessary information ready before filling out this form.
* Each field has a specific format or option list, please refer to the field-by-field explanation for guidance.
* You can submit this form only once, so please be thorough and accurate with your responses.
