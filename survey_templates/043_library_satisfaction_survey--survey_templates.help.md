# Library Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The library survey is designed to help us understand your experiences and perceptions about our services, staff, and facilities. Your feedback will help us make informed decisions to improve your library experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
2. You can skip questions if you are unsure or have no opinion.
3. Please be specific and provide examples when possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Library Satisfaction Survey Questions** (`library_satisfaction_survey_questions`, text, optional): This is the main question that asks about your overall satisfaction with the library. Feel free to write as much or as little as you like.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Staff Friendliness** (`staff_friendliness`, text, optional): Describe your experience with library staff. Were they helpful and welcoming?
* **Help Finding Information** (`help_finding_information`, text, optional): Describe any challenges you faced while finding information or help within the library.
* **Help Finding Materials** (`help_finding_materials`, text, optional): Describe any challenges you faced while finding materials or resources within the library.
* **Library Hours** (`library_hours`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the library's hours of operation.
* **Library Location** (`library_location`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the library's location or accessibility.
* **Library Services** (`library_services`, text, optional): Describe any services you would like us to offer or improve.
