# Pet Product Packaging Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on pet product packaging preferences from pet owners. The data collected will help inform product development and packaging design decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred packaging material from the options provided.
2. Rate the importance of eco-friendly packaging on a scale of 1-5.
3. Rate your current satisfaction with packaging on a scale of 1-100.
4. Rate your satisfaction with eco-friendly packaging on a scale of 1-100.
5. Provide open-ended comments about your thoughts on packaging.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
