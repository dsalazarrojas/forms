# Efficacy And Safety Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Efficacy And Safety Assessment Survey is a form designed to gather user feedback on the effectiveness and safety of a particular product or service. This feedback will help our business team to better understand how well the product or service meets its intended goals and whether it is safe for users to use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select all the pages you wish to respond to
* For each page, select all the options that apply to your experience
* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this will help us to better understand your experience

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Efficacy and Safety Assessment Survey** (page_1, select_multiple, required): This page asks you to indicate whether you have experienced the desired effects or goals of the product or service. Please select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Introduction** (page_2, text, required): This page is a text field for you to provide any additional information or comments about your experience. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Efficacy** (page_3, select_one, required): This page asks you to rate how well you think the product or service meets its intended goals. Please select one of the options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Safety** (page_4, select_multiple, required): This page asks you to indicate whether you think the product or service is safe to use. Please select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Demographic Information 1** (page_5, select_multiple, required): This page asks you to indicate your demographic information. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Demographic Information 2** (page_6, select_multiple, required): This page asks you to indicate your demographic information. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Demographic Information 3** (page_7, select_multiple, required): This page asks you to indicate your demographic information. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Demographic Information 4** (page_8, select_multiple, required): This page asks you to indicate your demographic information. Please select all the options that apply to you.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your responses, as this will help us to better understand your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or information that you would like to share, please use the text field on the introduction page.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
