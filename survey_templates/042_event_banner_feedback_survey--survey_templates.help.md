# event_banner_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event_banner_feedback_survey form is designed to collect feedback, ratings, and departmental information from event participants to gauge their overall experience and provide suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields (if any).
2. For each question, provide the requested information. For example:
	* For the "Rating" section, select a number from 1 to 5 to rate your overall experience.
	* For the "Feedback" section, choose one or multiple options to provide detailed feedback.
	* For the "Suggestions" section, type in any suggestions or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Banner Feedback** (Field 1): This is a note field where you can provide any feedback about the event.
* **Rating** (Field 2): Rate your overall experience with the event on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Rating Scale** (Field 3): Select the rating scale that best represents your overall experience.
* **Feedback** (Field 4): Choose one or multiple options to provide detailed feedback about your experience.
* **Suggestions** (Field 5): Type in any suggestions or comments you may have about the event.
* **Department** (Field 6): Select the department you work for.
* **Job Title** (Field 7): Enter your job title.
* **Email** (Field 8): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Field 9): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (Field 10): Enter your company name.
* **Location** (Field 11): Enter your current location.
* **Colloquium** (Field 12): This is a note field for any additional comments.
* **Comments** (Field 13): This is a note field for any additional comments.
* **Agreement** (Field 14): Select how you feel about the event.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide detailed feedback to help us improve the event.
* If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Make sure to include your contact information so we can follow up with you.
