# Online Banking Experience Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Banking Experience Inquiry Form is a tool designed to help us understand your satisfaction with our online banking services. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and enhance your overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the text field below.
2. Enter your **Email** in the corresponding field. Please use a valid email address to help us contact you if needed.
3. Answer the question about your **Satisfaction** with our online banking experience, using the number scale provided.
4. Choose your level of **Security** when accessing our online services. Please pick from the following options: 1 (Very Secure), 2 (Secure), 3 (Neutral), 4 (Somewhat Insecure), 5 (Very Insecure).
5. For the **Ratings** field, select all that apply from the list of options provided.
6. In the **Comments** field, describe what you expected to see in our online banking services. This will help us understand your expectations.
7. Answer the question about **Product Updates**. Please choose whether you are satisfied with our product updates or not.
8. For **Services**, select the services you use or have used with us, from the list provided.
9. If you have used any other services with us, select **Other Services** as required.
10. Finally, add any additional comments you have about your online banking experience in the **Other Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your account with us.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: false
* **Satisfaction**: Answer on a scale from 1 (Very Satisfied) to 5 (Very Dissatisfied).
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: false
* **Security**: How secure do you feel when accessing our online services?
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: false
* **Ratings**: Select all that apply.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: false
* **Comments**: Describe your expectations from our online banking services.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: false
* **Product Updates**: Are you satisfied with our product updates?
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: false
* **Services**: Select from the list of services provided.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: false
* **Other Services**: Select if you have used any other services with us.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: false
* **Other Comments**: Add any additional comments about your online banking experience.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
