# Customer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Survey form is designed to collect customer feedback and satisfaction data to help us understand your experiences with our product or service. Your responses will help us identify areas for improvement and provide better services to you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Provide any additional feedback in the "Feedback" field.
4. If you have any specific customer service improvements in mind, please list them in the "Customer Service Improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required):
	+ Select how satisfied you are with our overall service:
		- Satisfied
		- Dissatisfied
* **Product concerns** (`product_concerns`, text, optional):
	+ Describe any concerns or issues you have with our product.
* **Customer Service Satisfaction** (`customer_service_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select how satisfied you are with our customer service:
		- Satisfied
		- Dissatisfied
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Share any comments or suggestions you have about our service.
* **Customer Service Improvement** (`customer_service_improvement`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate whether you would like to see any improvements made to our customer service:
		- Yes
		- No
* **Other Customer Service Improvement** (`customer_service_improvement_other`, text, optional):
	+ If yes, please specify what improvements you would like to see.
* **Contact details** (`contact_details`, note, optional):
	+ Provide your contact information for us to follow up on your response.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional):
	+ If applicable, please let us know which tool was used to assist you.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, number, optional):
	+ Please enter your unique customer ID (if you have one).
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional):
	+ Please ignore this field, it's for internal use only.
