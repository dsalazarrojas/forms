# Secret Shopper Program Client Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Shopper Program Client Application Form is a form that helps us understand your business requirements and preferences for our secret shopper program. It allows you to provide us with information about your store, including its address, phone number, hours of operation, and manager's contact information. We will use this information to tailor our program to meet your unique needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the application process.
2. Fill in all required fields (indicated by *).
3. Provide accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
4. Review your submission for errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, text, optional)
This field allows you to provide any additional comments or information that might be relevant to your application.

### 2. **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional)
Enter the name of your store.

### 3. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.

### 4. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional)
Enter your store's phone number.

### 5. **Store Type** (`store_type`, text, optional)
Enter a brief description of your store type (e.g., retail, restaurant, etc.).

### 6. **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, optional)
Enter your store's physical address.

### 7. **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, text, optional)
Enter the hours your store is open.

### 8. **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional)
Enter the name of your store manager.

### 9. **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional)
Enter the phone number of your store manager.

### 10. **Store Experience** (`store_experience`, text, optional)
Describe your experience with our secret shopper program (if any).

### 11. **Why Shopper** (`why_shopper`, text, optional)
Explain why you're interested in participating in our secret shopper program.

### 12. **Shopper Hours** (`shopper_hours`, time, optional)
Enter the hours you are available to shop during the week.

### 13. **Shopper Address** (`shopper_address`, text, optional)
Enter your personal address.

### 14. **Shopper Phone** (`shopper_phone`, text, optional)
Enter your personal phone number.

### 15. **Shopper Email** (`shopper_email`, email, optional)
Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for shopping assignments.

Tips:
* Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us.
