# Best Telehealth Service Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on a telehealth service that you believe deserves recognition. Your input helps identify exceptional services that have a positive impact on access to healthcare.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you've chosen a service you've already interacted with and are not duplicating a nomination.
2.  Fill out the nominator's profile: Provide your full name and email address.
3.  Select your role: Choose how you interact with the service from the provided options.
4.  Enter the service's name and website or app link.
5.  Identify the types of care provided by the service.
6.  Choose the primary delivery platform used by the service.
7.  Rate the service's quality and security.
8.  Evaluate the service's ease of scheduling and registration, video and audio quality, patient data privacy and security, ease of use for non-technical users, and technical support availability.
9.  Describe what makes the service exceptional and a specific success story or positive outcome.
10.  Summarize why this service should be recognized as the Best Telehealth Service.
11.  Provide a professional reference contact for verification.
12.  Finally, confirm the date of your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name (2)**: Enter your complete name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Your Role (3)**: Choose how you interact with the service from the provided options.
*   **Your Email Address (4)**: Enter the email address associated with your professional or personal account.
*   **Telehealth Service Name (6)**: Enter the official name of the telehealth service you're nominating.
*   **Website or App Link (7)**: Enter the URL for accessing the service's website or app.
*   **Types of Care Provided (8)**: Select all types of care provided by the service you've experienced.
*   **Primary Delivery Platform (9)**: Choose the primary platform the service uses for delivery.
*   **Quality and Security Assessment (10)**: Rate the service's quality and security on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **Ease of Scheduling and Registration (11)**: Rate the ease of scheduling and registration on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **Video and Audio Clarity (12)**: Rate the video and audio quality during visits on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **Patient Data Privacy and Security (13)**: Rate the trustworthiness of the platform regarding patient data on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **Ease of Use for Non-technical Users (14)**: Rate the ease of use for non-technical users on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **Technical Support Availability (15)**: Rate the quality of help when technical issues arise on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **What Makes this Telehealth Service Exceptional (17)**: Describe the standout features of the service that make it exceptional.
*   **Describe a Specific Success Story or Positive Outcome (18)**: Share a story or outcome that showcases the service's impact.
*   **How Has This Service Improved Access to Healthcare (19)**: Describe how the service has improved access to healthcare, reaching underserved areas or populations.
*   **Why Should This Service Win the Best Telehealth Service Award (20)**: Summarize why this service should be recognized.
*   **Professional Reference (22)**: Provide a professional reference contact for verification.
*   **Date of Nomination (23)**: Confirm the date of your nomination.
