# Facilitator Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facilitator Feedback Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from facilitators on the quality of the training sessions they lead. The goal of this survey is to gather information that can be used to improve the training sessions and better support facilitators in their role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the options provided in the Training Title field to select the correct title of the training session you are providing feedback on.
2. Rate the facilitator's performance using the 1-5 rating scale provided in the facilitator_rating field.
3. Provide feedback on the facilitator's performance in the feedback field.
4. Select the rating that best reflects your opinion on the facilitator's performance.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the facilitator_comments field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Title**: This field allows you to select the title of the training session you are providing feedback on.
* **Facilitator Rating**: Use this rating scale to rate the facilitator's performance, where 1 is the lowest rating and 5 is the highest.
* **Facilitator Feedback**: In this field, you can provide detailed feedback on the facilitator's performance.
* **Rating Scale**: This is the scale used for the facilitator rating.
* **Rating 1, 2, 3, 4, 5**: These fields represent the individual ratings for each step of the rating scale.
* **Facilitator Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback on the facilitator's performance.
