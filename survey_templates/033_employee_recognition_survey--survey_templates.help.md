# Employee Recognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Recognition Survey is designed to collect your feedback on the recognition system within the organization. It helps us understand how you feel about being recognized for your contributions and how that affects your engagement and motivation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer the questions as honestly as possible. Please make sure to review each question carefully and select the most suitable option for your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey** (`employee_survey`, select_one, required/optional): This is the main question that asks if you have been recognized for your contributions recently. Please select "Yes" if you have been recognized, and "No" otherwise.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): This field is for your name, which will be used to identify you in the survey results.
* **Supervisor ID** (`supervisor_id`, number, optional): This is the ID of your supervisor, if applicable.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select the department(s) you belong to.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): This is your employee ID, if applicable.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, select_one, optional): This is the name of your manager, if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): This field is for your job title.
* **How do you feel about being recognized for your contributions to the team?** (`feedback_question_1`, text, optional): Please provide your honest feedback on how you feel about being recognized for your contributions.
* **How do you feel about being recognized for your contributions to the team?** (`feedback_question_2`, text, optional): This question is similar to the previous one, but it helps us understand if you feel more positive or negative about recognition.
* **Recognition Type** (`recognition_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of recognition you receive most often (if any).
* **Recognition Frequency** (`frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency with which you receive recognition (if any).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please provide any additional feedback you may have about recognition in the organization.
* **Employee Survey Completion Time** (`employee_survey_completion_time`, time, optional): This field is for you to estimate the time it took you to complete the survey.

Note: The optional fields are not required, but it's helpful for us to have the most accurate and complete feedback.
