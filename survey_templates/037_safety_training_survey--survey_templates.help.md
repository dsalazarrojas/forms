# Safety Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your experience with a recent safety training session. It helps us understand what you think about the topics covered, the effectiveness of the training, and areas for improvement. Your input is crucial in helping us refine our safety training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. For each page, select or enter the corresponding information according to the field type (select_one, select_multiple, number, text).
3. Make sure to answer all the required fields (if any).
4. You can leave optional fields blank or provide additional comments in the "Additional Comments" section.
5. Once you've completed all the pages, review your answers for accuracy and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Required: No): This is a brief introduction to get you started. Please take a moment to read the text and proceed when ready.
* **Safety Topics** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the safety topics that you think were covered in the training (e.g., Value 1, Value 2).
* **Effectiveness of the Training** (Number, Optional): Rate the effectiveness of the training on a scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Training Leaders** (Select One, Optional): Identify the training leader(s) who presented the training (e.g., John, Jane).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Number, Optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training on a scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Areas for Improvement** (Text, Optional): Provide any areas where you think the training could be improved.
* **Safety Culture** (Select Multiple, Optional): Describe the current state of the safety culture within your organization (e.g., Good, Fair).
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
