# Environmental Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the environmental implications of a particular action or decision. The form will help us to gauge the user's understanding of the environmental effects of this action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read the introduction page, which provides background information about the environmental impact survey.
2. For each section, choose one option from the list provided.
3. Enter the date and time in the respective fields.
4. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Page 1)
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This page provides background information about the environmental impact survey. Read this page carefully before proceeding.

### Section 1 (Page 2)
* **Section 1** (`section_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the options provided to indicate your understanding of the environmental implications of the action. Please select only one option.

### Section 2 (Page 3)
* **Section 2** (`section_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the text that best describes your understanding of the environmental implications of the action.

### Date (Page 4)
* **Date** (`section_3`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date on which you are taking this survey.

### Time (Page 5)
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time on which you are taking this survey.
