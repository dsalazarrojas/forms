# Resource Development Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the resource development within your team. It is designed to gather feedback and input on various aspects of resource development, including adequacy, challenges, priorities, and other relevant information. This form is intended for team members to provide their insights and help evaluate the effectiveness of resource development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading the form carefully and making sure you understand each section and question.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the "Submit" button to submit your assessment when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resource Development Assessment Form Template (1)**: This is the title of the form, which is not a field to be filled in but rather a heading to indicate that this is a template for assessing resource development.
* **Resource Development Adequacy (2)**: Assess the adequacy of resources developed in your team, using the scale: Not Applicable, Somewhat Applicable, Very Applicable, and Extremely Applicable.
* **Resource Development Challenges (3)**: Select the challenges you are facing with resource development in your team, using the scale: Not Applicable, Somewhat Applicable, Very Applicable, and Extremely Applicable.
* **Resource Development Priorities (4)**: Select the priorities you have for resource development in your team, using the scale: High, Medium, Low.
* **Resource Development Date (5)**: Enter the date of resource development (this field seems to be a placeholder and might not be used).
* **Resource Development 1-15 (6-20)**: These fields are for providing open-ended notes on various aspects of resource development. You can use these fields to provide as much detail as possible about your thoughts and experiences.
* **Submit (21)**: Select 'Yes' to submit your assessment, or 'No' to cancel.
