# IVD Machine Calibration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IVD Machine Calibration Survey form is used to collect information about machine calibration. This data is used for maintenance and quality control purposes to ensure optimal machine performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please enter your contact information for the person completing the form.
2.  Enter the machine information, including its current calibration status.
3.  If applicable, select the calibration date.
4.  Choose the calibration frequency, if specified.
5.  Select the type of calibration performed.
6.  Provide any additional comments or remarks about the machine.
7.  Enter the email address and phone number of the person completing the form.
8.  Sign the form to confirm completion.
9.  Enter any submission information, such as the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Contact Info:** Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information to help us contact you if any issues arise.
*   **Machine Information:** This field is for entering details about the machine being calibrated, including its current status.
*   **Calibration Date:** If the machine was calibrated, please enter the date it was calibrated.
*   **Calibration Frequency:** If the machine has a scheduled calibration frequency, enter it here.
*   **Calibration Type:** Select the type of calibration performed, such as maintenance, repair, or recalibration.
*   **Comments:** Enter any comments or remarks about the machine or the calibration process.
*   **Email:** Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Phone:** Enter your phone number for easy contact.
*   **Signature:** Sign the form to confirm that you have completed it accurately.
*   **Submission Info:** Enter any additional submission information, such as the date and time of submission.
