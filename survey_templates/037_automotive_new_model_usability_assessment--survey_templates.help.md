# Automotive New Model Usability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automotive New Model Usability Assessment form is designed to gather feedback on the user experience of a new car model. The form covers various aspects of the car, including its interior and controls, driving dynamics, safety features, and overall impression.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address to ensure you can receive a confirmation of receipt of your feedback.
2. Select the age group that best describes you.
3. Enter the model of the car you test-driven.
4. Enter the year of the car.
5. Select the date of the test drive.
6. Enter the duration of the test drive (in minutes).
7. Evaluate the dashboard layout and visibility, control placement and accessibility, infotainment system ease of use, seat comfort and support, handling and responsiveness, acceleration performance, braking performance, and overall visibility.
8. Rate the car's safety features and technology.
9. Provide an overall impression of the car and indicate if you would consider purchasing this model.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Please enter your full name.
	+ This field is required to ensure we can address any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
	+ This field is required to ensure we can contact you with any further questions or concerns.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
	+ This field is optional and will only be used for follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Age Group**: Select the age group that best describes you.
	+ The age group will help us to understand your demographic background.
* **Model Tested**: Enter the model of the car you test-driven.
	+ Please ensure you have test-driven the car before attempting to complete this form.
* **Model Year**: Enter the year of the car.
	+ This field is required to ensure we can accurately record your feedback.
* **Test Drive Date**: Select the date of the test drive.
	+ This field is required to record the date of the test drive.
* **Test Drive Duration**: Enter the duration of the test drive (in minutes).
	+ This field is required to record the duration of the test drive.
* **Dashboard Layout and Visibility**: Evaluate the dashboard layout and visibility.
	+ Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to describe your experience.
* **Control Placement and Accessibility**: Evaluate the control placement and accessibility.
	+ Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to describe your experience.
* **Infotainment System Ease of Use**: Evaluate the infotainment system's ease of use.
	+ Select Very Easy, Easy, Moderately Difficult, or Very Difficult to describe your experience.
* **Seat Comfort and Support**: Evaluate the seat comfort and support.
	+ Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to describe your experience.
* **Handling and Responsiveness**: Evaluate the handling and responsiveness.
	+ Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to describe your experience.
* **Acceleration Performance**: Evaluate the acceleration performance.
	+ Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to describe your experience.
* **Braking Performance**: Evaluate the braking performance.
	+ Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to describe your experience.
* **Overall Visibility**: Evaluate the overall visibility.
	+ Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to describe your experience.
* **Safety Features and Technology**: Evaluate the safety features and technology.
	+ Select Very Impressed, Impressed, Neutral, or Disappointed to describe your experience.
* **Overall Impression**: Provide an overall impression of the car.
	+ Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to describe your experience.
* **Would you Consider Purchasing this Model?**: Indicate if you would consider purchasing this model.
	+ Select Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably Not, or Definitely Not to describe your decision.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Ensure you have test-driven the car before attempting to complete this form.
