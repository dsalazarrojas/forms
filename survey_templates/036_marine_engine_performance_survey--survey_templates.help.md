# Marine Engine Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marine Engine Performance Survey form is designed to collect information on the performance of marine engines, helping users report on various aspects of engine operation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate engine type from the options provided.
2. Choose the engine capacity from the available options.
3. Evaluate the engine condition from the provided options.
4. Report the maintenance cost, annual hours, and fuel efficiency of the engine.
5. Assess various performance metrics such as temperature, pressure, leakage, noise, vibration, and other issues.
6. Use the final page to report any other performance problems not covered in the previous sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Engine Type** (`engine_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of marine engine from the options provided (2 Stroke, 4 Stroke, Hybrid, or Electric).
- **Engine Capacity** (`engine_capacity`, select_multiple, required): Select the capacity of the engine from the options provided (1000cc, 1200cc, 1500cc, or 2000cc).
- **Engine Condition** (`engine_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the condition of the engine from the options provided (Good, Fair, or Poor).
- **Maintenance Cost** (`maintenance_cost`, number, required): Report the maintenance cost of the engine.
- **Annual Hours** (`annual_hours`, number, required): Report the annual hours of engine operation.
- **Fuel Efficiency** (`fuel_efficiency`, select_multiple, required): Assess the fuel efficiency of the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Mechanical Condition** (`mechanical_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the mechanical condition of the engine from the options provided (Good, Fair, or Poor).
- **Engine Problem** (`engine_problem`, select_multiple, required): Report any problems with the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Oil** (`engine_oil`, select_one, required): Evaluate the oil condition of the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Lube** (`engine_lube`, select_one, required): Evaluate the lube condition of the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Fuel** (`engine_fuel`, select_one, required): Assess the fuel quality of the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Battery** (`engine_battery`, select_one, required): Evaluate the battery condition of the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Starting** (`engine_starting`, select_one, required): Evaluate the starting performance of the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Idling** (`engine_idling`, select_one, required): Assess the idling performance of the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine RPM** (`engine_rpm`, select_one, required): Evaluate the RPM performance of the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Temperature** (`engine_temperature`, select_one, required): Assess the temperature performance of the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Pressure** (`engine_pressure`, select_multiple, required): Evaluate the pressure performance of the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Leakage** (`engine_leakage`, select_multiple, required): Report any leakage problems with the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Noise** (`engine_noise`, select_multiple, required): Assess the noise performance of the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Vibration** (`engine_vibration`, select_multiple, required): Evaluate the vibration performance of the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Rust** (`engine_rust`, select_multiple, required): Report any rust issues with the engine from the options provided (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Engine Other Issue** (`engine_other_issue`, text, optional): Report any other performance problems not covered in the previous sections.
- **Marine Engine Performance Survey** (`marine_engine_performance_survey_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the engine performance.

## Tips
- Be thorough when assessing the performance of the engine to ensure accurate reporting.
- Make sure to report all relevant issues to provide a comprehensive picture of the engine performance.
- If unsure about any of the options, select the one that best fits the observed performance.
