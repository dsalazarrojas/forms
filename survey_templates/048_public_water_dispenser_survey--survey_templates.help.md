# Public Water Dispenser Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users to provide feedback on their experience with a public water dispenser. We're interested in hearing about your thoughts on the water quality and your contact information so we can better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "what_is_your_name" field.
2. Enter the address of the public water dispenser you used in the "Station Address" field.
3. Rate the water quality from "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" in the "How is the water quality" field.
4. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or have any additional comments, enter your contact information in the "contact_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_your_name** (text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Station Address** (text, required): Enter the address of the public water dispenser you used.
* **How is the water quality** (select_one, optional): Rate the water quality from "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **contact_info** (text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or have any additional comments, enter your contact information here.

## Tips
* Please be honest in your assessment of the water quality.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the contact_info field.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, don't worry! Just do your best to provide the information.
