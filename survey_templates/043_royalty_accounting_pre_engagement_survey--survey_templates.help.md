# royalty_accounting_pre_engagement_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Royalty Accounting Pre-Engagement Survey" is a form designed to gather information from clients about their pre-engagement details, royalty processes, and engagement expectations. This form helps us understand the client's needs and preferences, ensuring that the engagement process is tailored to their specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all the questions honestly.
2. Provide detailed information about your pre-engagement details in the "Pre-Engagement Details" field.
3. Indicate your current royalty processes and whether you have any new processes that you would like to implement.
4. Select your preferred royalty accounting and financial reporting options.
5. Indicate your engagement expectations and provide any additional details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pre-Engagement Details (1)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide detailed information about your pre-engagement details. Please provide a clear and concise description of your pre-engagement requirements.
* **Royalty Processes (2)** (`number`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate your current royalty processes. Please select the options that apply to your current processes.
* **Current Royalty Processes (3)** (`select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate whether you currently have royalty processes in place.
* **New Royalty Processes (4)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate whether you have any new royalty processes that you would like to implement.
* **Royalty Accounting (5)** (`select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to select your preferred royalty accounting option.
* **Financial Reporting (6)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to select your preferred financial reporting options.
* **Engagement Expectations (7)** (`select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate your engagement expectations.
* **Engagement Expectations Details (8)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide additional details about your engagement expectations.
* **Client Company (9)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate whether you are a client company or not.
* **Client Contacts (10)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate whether you have client contacts or not.
* **Accounting Firms (11)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate whether you use accounting firms or not.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please ask for clarification.
* This form should not be used for any other purpose other than gathering pre-engagement information.
