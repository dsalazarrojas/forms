# returnee_invitation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The returnee_invitation_survey form is used to collect feedback and survey responses from returnees of an event or session. This form is designed to gauge the returnees' experience, satisfaction, and engagement with the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please navigate through each page and answer the questions as relevant to your experience. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Returnee Invitation Survey** (`returnee_invitation_survey_page_1`, text, optional): This page is the introduction to the survey, please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. This will help us contact you if we need any further information.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Returnee Attendance** (`returnee_k_attendance`, number, optional): Please select the number of returnees you attended the session.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the session.
* **Returnee Engagement** (`returnee_engagement`, select_one, optional): Please select how engaged you were with the session, from the options: Strongly Engaged, Somewhat Engaged, Not Engaged.
* **Session Satisfaction** (`session_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your level of satisfaction with the session, from the options: Strongly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Neutral.
* **Returnee Satisfaction** (`returnee_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your level of satisfaction with the returnee, from the options: Strongly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Neutral.
* **Event Satisfaction** (`event_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your level of satisfaction with the event, from the options: Strongly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Neutral.
* **Returnee Impressions** (`returnee_impressions`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your impression of the returnee, from the options: Positive, Negative, Neutral.
* **Event Relevance** (`event_relevance`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your level of relevance of the event, from the options: Strongly Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Relevant, Neutral.
* **Returnee Experience** (`returnee_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your level of experience with the returnee, from the options: Positive, Negative, Neutral.
* **Returnee Opportunity** (`returnee_opportunity`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your level of opportunity with the returnee, from the options: Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Disagree.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please provide any feedback you may have about the session.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Please provide any suggestions you may have for future improvements.
* **Returnee Recommendation** (`returnee_recommendation`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your level of recommendation for the returnee, from the options: Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Disagree.
* **Returnee Attendance Date** (`returnee_attendance_date`, date, optional): Please select the date you attended the session.
* **Returnee Attendance Time** (`returnee_attendance_time`, time, optional): Please select the time you attended the session.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, number, optional): Please enter the ID of the session.
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the session.
* **Session Start Date** (`session_start_date`, date, optional): Please select the start date of the session.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, time, optional): Please select the start time of the session.
* **Session End Date** (`session_end_date`, date, optional): Please select the end date of the session.
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, time, optional): Please select the end time of the session.

Please complete each question carefully, and remember that all fields are optional. If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
