# Customer Attitudes Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Attitudes Survey Template is a tool designed to collect information on customer attitudes and behaviors, specifically their satisfaction with various aspects of the product or service. This template is meant to be used by customers to provide feedback on their experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose one or more from the list of Survey Templates:
	* Yes
	* No
2. Choose one from the list of Customer Satisfaction Evaluation Forms:
	* Satisfied
	* Dissatisfied
3. Choose one or more from the list of Marketer Forms:
	* Yes
	* No
4. Enter your answer in the Customer Insights Departments field (if available):
	* Type your answer in the text field.
5. Choose one from the list of Market Research Teams:
	* Yes
	* No
6. Choose one or more from the list of Customer Experience Teams:
	* Yes
	* No
7. Choose one or more from the list of Customer Insights Teams:
	* Yes
	* No

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Templates (1)**: This field allows you to choose one or more survey templates from the list.
	+ Select one or more options that apply to your experience.
* **Customer Satisfaction Evaluation Forms (2)**: This field allows you to choose one customer satisfaction evaluation form from the list.
	+ Select the form that best describes your level of satisfaction.
* **Marketer Forms (3)**: This field allows you to choose one or more marketer forms from the list.
	+ Select one or more options that apply to your experience.
* **Customer Insights Departments (4)**: This field is used to enter any additional information about the departments related to customer insights.
	+ Type your answer in this field.
* **Market Research Teams (6)**: This field allows you to choose one from the list.
	+ Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Customer Experience Teams (7)**: This field allows you to choose one or more from the list.
	+ Select one or more options that apply to your experience.
* **Customer Insights Teams (8)**: This field allows you to choose one or more from the list.
	+ Select one or more options that apply to your experience.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Make sure to select all the options that apply to your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please type them in the Customer Insights Departments field.
* Remember to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
