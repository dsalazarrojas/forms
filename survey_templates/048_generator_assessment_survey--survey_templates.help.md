# Generator Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generator Assessment Survey is designed to collect information about the performance and efficiency of a generator. This form will help you evaluate the generator's capabilities and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each field as accurately as possible based on your experience with the generator.
2. Be specific when providing information, especially for numerical fields like Generator Efficiency and Performance Metric.
3. If you have any comments or notes about the generator, use the Comments field to share your thoughts.
4. Ensure that you're not duplicating any previous assessments for the same generator.
