# Donor Preferences Survey For Fundraising Event - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Preferences Survey For Fundraising Event form is used to gather information about donors' preferences, habits, and contact preferences to improve the donor experience and tailor the communication style to each individual's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your name in the **name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
3. Choose your preferred frequency of donations from the options provided in the **donation_frequency** field.
4. Indicate your preferred amount for donations in the **donation_amount** field (optional).
5. Select your preferred contact method from the options provided in the **preferred_contact_method** field.
6. Answer yes or no to whether you would like to volunteer for the fundraising event in the **volunteer** field.
7. Choose your preferred language(s) from the options provided in the **preferred_language** field.
8. Select the times of day you are available to receive communication from us (24-hour clock format) in the **preferred_time** field.
9. Repeat step 8 for additional times you are available (up to two times can be selected) in the **preferred_time2** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: Your name, as you would like to be addressed in our communications.
* **email**: Your email address for receiving updates and communications about the fundraising event.
* **donation_frequency**: The frequency with which you would like to make donations, e.g., never, rarely, occasionally, regularly, or very often.
* **donation_amount**: If you would like to specify an amount for your donations, please enter it here. Note: this is optional.
* **preferred_contact_method**: Choose how you would like to receive communications from us: email, phone, mail, or no preference.
* **volunteer**: Would you be interested in volunteering for the event? Choose yes or no.
* **preferred_language**: Select your preferred language(s) for communication with us. You can choose one or more languages from the options provided.
* **preferred_time**: Choose times of day (24-hour clock format) when you are available to receive communications from us.
* **preferred_time2**: Repeat step 8 and select additional times you are available (up to two times can be selected).
