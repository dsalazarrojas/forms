# Customer Feedback Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Feedback Audit Form is designed to gather valuable insights from customers to improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, select the options that best describe your feedback.
3. Enter the correct date and time of your visit in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` for date and `HH:MM:SS` for time.
4. Fill in the required fields, such as customer name, customer ID, and feedback date.
5. Provide your email address and any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.
