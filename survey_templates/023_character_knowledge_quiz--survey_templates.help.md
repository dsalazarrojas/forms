# Character Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Character Knowledge Quiz is designed to test your knowledge of a story's characters, their relationships, and the plot. It's a series of questions that will help the creators of the story understand your understanding of the narrative.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the required questions (marked as "true" in the form).
2. Enter your full name and email address for future updates and results.
3. Select all the correct traits that describe the main character.
4. Choose the primary antagonist, the main character's origin, and their primary motivation.
5. Describe the key event that had the most significant impact on the main character.
6. List the main character's closest allies.
7. Select the nature of the rivalry between character A and character B.
8. Confirm your answers to the best of your knowledge.
9. Rate the difficulty of the quiz.
10. Express your interest in taking more quizzes like this.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Quiz Taker Information
* **Quiz Taker Full Name** (`quiz_taker_full_name`, text, optional): Enter your first and last name for future updates and results.
* **Quiz Taker Email Address** (`quiz_taker_email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address for future updates and results.
### General Character Knowledge
* **-- General Character Knowledge --** (`section_general_knowledge`, note, optional): This section is just an introduction and does not require any input.
### Story's Character Information
* **Who is the main protagonist of the story being analyzed?** (`quiz_identify_protagonist`, text, true): Identify the main character in the story.
* **Who is the primary antagonist in this narrative?** (`quiz_primary_antagonist`, text, true): Identify the primary antagonist in the story.
* **Which of the following traits describe the main character?** (`quiz_major_character_traits`, select_multiple, true): Select all the traits that describe the main character.
* **What is the main character's place of origin?** (`quiz_character_origin_location`, text, true): Enter the place where the main character was born or comes from.
* **What is the main character's primary motivation?** (`quiz_character_primary_motivation`, select_one, true): Choose the primary motivation of the main character.
* **Which event had the most significant impact on the main character?** (`quiz_key_event_impact`, text, true): Describe the pivotal moment that had the most significant impact on the main character.
* **Who are the main character's closest allies?** (`quiz_character_allies_list`, text, true): List the names of the main character's closest allies.
* **What is the nature of the rivalry between Character A and Character B?** (`quiz_nature_of_rivalry`, select_one, true): Select the most accurate description of the rivalry between the characters.
### Final Confirmation
* **Confirmation of Knowledge Assessment** (`accuracy_confirmation_quiz_taker`, select_one, true): Confirm that you have answered the questions to the best of your knowledge.
### Additional Information
* **Date of Completion** (`date_of_knowledge_quiz_log`, date, true): This field is automatically generated and shows the current date.
* **Additional Quiz Comments** (`additional_quiz_comments`, text, false): This section is optional and allows you to add any additional comments about the story or your experience with the quiz.
* **Quiz Difficulty Rating** (`quiz_difficulty_rating`, number, false): Rate the difficulty of the quiz, with 1 being the easiest and 5 being the hardest.
* **Interest in Future Quizzes** (`interest_in_future_quizzes`, select_one, false): Express your interest in taking more quizzes like this.
