# Employee Trust And Loyalty Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Trust And Loyalty Survey is a tool used to measure employee trust and loyalty towards their manager. It helps management understand employee perceptions and feelings, allowing them to identify areas of improvement and create a more positive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Intro**: This page serves as an introduction to the survey. Please read it thoroughly before proceeding.
2. For each question, choose your response from the provided options, such as 'None', 'Other', or the actual answer if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **trust** (`trust`, Select Multiple, Required: false)
	* This section asks about the level of trust employees have towards their manager. Select all options that apply to you.
### 2. **loyalty** (`loyalty`, Select One, Required: false)
	* This question probes into whether employees feel loyal to their manager. Choose the response that best represents your feelings.
### 3. **engagement** (`engagement`, Select Multiple, Required: false)
	* This section inquires about the level of engagement employees feel with their work. Select all options that apply to you.
### 4. **manager_awareness** (`manager awareness`, Select One, Required: false)
	* This question gauges the level of awareness employees have about their manager's expectations. Choose the response that best represents your feelings.
### 5. **employee_awareness** (`employee awareness`, Select One, Required: false)
	* This question probes into whether employees feel their manager is aware of their needs and feelings. Select the response that best represents your feelings.
### 6. **communication** (`communication`, Select Multiple, Required: false)
	* This section inquires about the quality of communication between employees and their manager. Select all options that apply to you.
### 7. **manager_support** (`manager support`, Select One, Required: false)
	* This question measures the level of support employees feel from their manager. Choose the response that best represents your feelings.
### 8. **job_security** (`job security`, Select Multiple, Required: false)
	* This section asks about the level of security employees feel about their job. Select all options that apply to you.
### 9. **job_performance** (`job performance`, Select Multiple, Required: false)
	* This question probes into whether employees feel their job performance is satisfactory. Select all options that apply to you.
### 10. **manager_effectiveness** (`manager effectiveness`, Select One, Required: false)
	* This section inquires about the effectiveness of their manager. Choose the response that best represents your feelings.
### 11. **feedback** (`feedback`, Select One, Required: false)
	* This question measures whether employees feel their manager provides them with feedback. Select the response that best represents your feelings.

## Tips
- Answer honestly and accurately to help management create a more positive work environment.
- Please select all options that apply to you for each question.
- If you are unsure about any question, please choose the response that best represents your feelings.
