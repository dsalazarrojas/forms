# Weight Loss Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their weight loss goals and progress. It will help users track their current status, understand their progress, and set realistic goals for the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability, even if you are not sure about some of the fields.
2. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
3. Select your sex in the "Gender" field.
4. Enter your height in centimeters in the "Height in cm" field.
5. Enter your weight in kilograms in the "Weight in kg" field.
6. Enter your blood pressure (systolic) in mmHg in the "Blood Pressure (Systolic) in mmHg" field.
7. Enter your blood pressure (diastolic) in mmHg in the "Blood Pressure (Diastolic) in mmHg" field.
8. Enter your blood pressure heart rate per minute in the "Blood Pressure Heart Rate" field.
9. Select any medications you are taking that may impact your weight loss in the "Medication List" field.
10. Select your physical activity level in the "Physical Activity Level" field.
11. Select the type of diet you follow in the "What Type of Diet Do You Follow" field.
12. Enter your Body Mass Index (BMI) in the "Body Mass Index (BMI)" field.
13. Enter your weight loss goal in the "What is Your Weight Loss Goal" field.
14. Select whether or not you have a weight loss plan in the "Do You Have a Weight Loss Plan" field.
15. Select the date when you plan to start your weight loss journey in the "When Do You Plan to Start Your Weight Loss Journey" field.
16. Enter how long you have been trying to lose weight in years in the "How Long Have You Been Trying to Lose Weight" field.
17. Select the methods you have used to lose weight in the past in the "What Method Did You Use to Lose Weight" field.
18. Enter why you want to lose weight in the "Why Do You Want to Lose Weight" field.
19. Enter how much weight you have lost in the past year in the "How Much Weight Have You Lost in the Past Year" field.
20. Enter how much weight you expect to lose in the next year in the "How Much Weight Do You Expect to Lose in the Next Year" field.
21. Enter how much weight you expect to lose in the next 2 years in the "How Much Weight Do You Expect to Lose in the Next 2 Years" field.
22. Enter how much weight you expect to lose in the next 5 years in the "How Much Weight Do You Expect to Lose in the Next 5 Years" field.
23. Enter your goal weight in the "What is Your Goal Weight" field.
