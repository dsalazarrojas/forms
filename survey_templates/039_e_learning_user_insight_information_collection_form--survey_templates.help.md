# e-learning-user-insight-information-collection-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about users who have participated in e-learning activities. The data collected will help in understanding user progress and performance in these activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Job Title.
5. Enter your Department.
6. Answer the question about whether you are reporting to a manager.
7. Enter your current Location.
8. Enter your Phone number.
9. Provide the Date of Joining (if applicable).
10. Enter the duration of your training in hours (if applicable).
11. Provide the Total Training Hours completed (if applicable).
12. Answer whether your Training Facilitator has approved your training (if applicable).
13. Select the status of your Training Facilitator's approval (if applicable).
14. Enter the name of your Training Facilitator (if applicable).
15. Enter the Email of your Training Facilitator (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your active email address that will be used for communication.
* **Job Title (4)**: Enter your title in your current role.
* **Department (5)**: Enter your department name.
* **Reporting Manager (6)**: Answer whether you are reporting to a manager. Yes or No.
* **Location (7)**: Enter your current location.
* **Phone (8)**: Enter your active phone number.
* **Date of Joining (9)**: If you have joined the organization, enter the date you joined. Otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Training Duration (10)**: If you have received training, enter the duration in hours.
* **Total Training Hours (11)**: If you have completed training, enter the total number of hours completed.
* **Training Facilitator Approval (12)**: Answer whether your Training Facilitator has approved your training. Yes or No.
* **Training Facilitator Approval Status (13)**: Select the status of your Training Facilitator's approval. Active or Inactive.
* **Training Facilitator Name (14)**: If your Training Facilitator has approved your training, enter their name.
* **Training Facilitator Email (15)**: If your Training Facilitator has approved your training, enter their email address.

Note: Some fields are optional, so you only need to fill in the relevant information.
