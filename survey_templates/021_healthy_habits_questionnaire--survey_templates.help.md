# Healthy Habits Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your healthy habits, covering various aspects of well-being, such as physical activity, sleep, diet, relationships, mental health, emotional intelligence, and self-awareness. It helps users understand their current habits and provides a comprehensive overview of areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your personal experiences and habits.
2. Select the relevant options for each question based on your preferences and habits.
3. Fill in any required text fields, such as the introduction and self-regulation motivation fields.
4. Review your answers to ensure you've covered all the necessary aspects of healthy habits.
