# research_conference_paper_quality_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The research_conference_paper_quality_evaluation_form is a tool used by assessors to evaluate the quality of research papers presented at conferences. This form helps assessors provide structured and systematic feedback on various aspects of the paper.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Paper Title field with the title of the paper being reviewed.
2. Indicate whether the paper has authors (Yes or No).
3. Select the quality of the paper (Good or Poor) from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose whether the paper's topic is relevant (Yes or No) or not.
5. Select whether the conference proceedings were clear (Yes or No).
6. Enter an overall score for the paper (a numerical value).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Paper Title** (`paper_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the paper being reviewed.
* **Authors** (`authors`, select_multiple, required): Check if the paper has authors. This helps assessors understand who contributed to the paper's research.
* **Paper Quality** (`paper_quality`, select_one, required): Evaluate the overall quality of the paper, with options being Good or Poor.
* **Relevance to Topic** (`relevance_to_topic`, select_multiple, required): Determine whether the paper's topic is relevant (Yes or No) or not.
* **Clarity of Conference** (`clarity_of_conference`, select_one, required): Assess whether the conference proceedings were clear (Yes or No).
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, number, required): Enter a numerical score for the paper, out of some unspecified range.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when completing this form, as the feedback will be used to improve the paper.
* Use the options provided for each field, and avoid adding additional text or comments that are not relevant to the field.
* Complete the form as thoroughly as possible, as the more information provided, the better the feedback will be.
