# financial_comfort_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial_comfort_survey form is designed to collect information about users' comfort with financial situations. It helps gauge users' confidence in their financial stability or their ability to manage financial challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by selecting the relevant pages (sections) to fill out. This form has three sections: "survey_questions", "financial_concerns", and "financial_situation".
*   For each section, carefully read the instructions and questions, and answer all the questions truthfully.
*   Make sure to review each field carefully and select the correct answer for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **survey_questions** (`survey_questions`, note, optional): This is a free-text field where you can enter any thoughts or concerns related to your financial situation.
*   **financial_concerns** (`financial_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): This is a multiple-choice field where you can select one or more of the following options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **financial_situation** (`financial_situation`, date, optional): This is a date field where you can select the date related to your financial situation.
