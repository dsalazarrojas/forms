# User Testing Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about users' opinions and experiences with a specific product. The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and insights from users to help improve the product.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer the ones that apply to you.
2. Enter your answers in the provided fields.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a asterisk \*).
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`user_testing_survey_1`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your results.
* **What type of device do you use** (`user_testing_survey_2`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of device you use (e.g. iPhone, Android, Other).
* **What is your age** (`user_testing_survey_3`, number, required: false): Please enter your age in years.
* **How do you rate your experience on this product** (`user_testing_survey_4`, number, required: false): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "very poor" and 10 is "excellent".
* **How did you get here** (`user_testing_survey_5`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the ways you got to this product (e.g. Social media, Referral, Search Engine, Other).
* **Date of birth** (`user_testing_survey_6`, date, required: false): Please enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g. 1990-01-01).
* **Time of day** (`user_testing_survey_7`, time, required: false): Please enter the time of day you completed this form (e.g. 10:00 AM).
* **What do you think about this product** (`user_testing_survey_8`, note, required: false): Enter any thoughts or comments about the product.
* **Email** (`user_testing_survey_9`, email, required: false): Please enter a valid email address for future communication.
* **phone** (`user_testing_survey_10`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and honest answers.
* Do not enter any confidential or sensitive information.
* This form is designed to be completed by users of the product, not for commercial or sales purposes.
