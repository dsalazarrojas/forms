# Jewelry Box Protection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jewelry Box Protection Survey is a research tool designed to gather information about users' jewelry storage habits and preferences. 

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the Jewelry Box Protection Survey:

1. Enter your name in the "User Input" field.
2. Select the storage method you currently use for your jewelry in the "Storage Method" field.
3. Choose how often you store your jewelry in the "How Often" field.
4. Select the protection features you consider most important for your jewelry in the "Protection Feature" field.
5. Share your overall satisfaction with your current storage setup in the "Customer Satisfaction" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (text): Enter your name to help us identify you as a respondent.
* **Storage Method** (text): Choose how you store your jewelry, e.g., in a jewelry box, on a velvet cloth, or in a safe deposit box.
* **How Often** (select one of the following):
	+ Label 1
	+ Label 2
	+ Label 3
* **Protection Feature** (select multiple, separated by commas):
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Customer Satisfaction** (text): Share your level of satisfaction with your current storage setup on a scale of 1-10.
