# corporate_event_success_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate_event_success_evaluation_form is used to gather feedback from event attendees and assess the overall success of corporate events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and detailed answers for each field.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **intro**: Briefly introduce the event, including its purpose and objectives.
* **event_type**: Indicate if this event was a 'Yes' or 'No' to help us evaluate its success.
* **event_date**: Enter the date of the event, in the format 'dd/mm/yyyy' (e.g., 01/02/2023).
* **event_time**: Enter the time of the event, in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **event_location**: Describe the event location, including any notable features or details.
* **event_attendance**: Provide an estimate of the number of attendees and their demographics (e.g., employees, clients, etc.).
* **event_success**: On a scale of 1-5, rate the overall success of the event, where 1 is 'very unsuccessful' and 5 is 'very successful'.
* **improvement_areas**: Identify areas that could be improved for future events.
* **suggestions**: Suggest any suggestions or ideas for future events.
* **feedback**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the event.
* **email**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication (this will help us keep you updated).
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up calls or notifications (this will help us keep you updated).
* **file**: Attach any supporting documents or files relevant to the event evaluation.

Note: If you need help with a specific field, refer to the 'Field-by-Field Explanation' section for more detailed guidance.
