# Annual Meeting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from attendees of an annual meeting. It covers various aspects of the meeting, including attendance details, overall satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field (Field 1).
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field (Field 2). Note that this field is optional.
3. Provide your organization name in the "Organization" field (Field 3). This is also optional.
4. Select the meeting year you attended (Field 4).
5. Choose how you attended the meeting (Field 5).
6. Indicate if this is your first time attending the meeting (Field 6).
7. Select the number of years you have attended the meeting (Field 7). Note that this field is optional.
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting (Field 8).
9. Rate the registration process (Field 9).
10. Rate the venue quality (Field 10). Note that this field is optional.
11. Rate the content quality (Field 11).
12. Rate the speaker quality (Field 12).
13. Rate the session variety (Field 13).
14. Rate the networking value (Field 14).
15. Rate the exhibit quality (Field 15). Note that this field is optional.
16. Rate the food and beverage (Field 16). Note that this field is optional.
17. Evaluate the value for money (Field 17).
18. Indicate if your expectations were met (Field 18).
19. Describe any key learnings you took away from the meeting (Field 19). This field is optional.
20. Identify the best aspect of the meeting (Field 20). This field is optional.
21. Note any worst aspects of the meeting (Field 21). This field is optional.
22. Suggest topics for future meetings (Field 22). This field is optional.
23. Indicate your likelihood to attend next year's meeting (Field 23).
24. Express your likelihood to recommend the meeting to others (Field 24).
25. Provide any suggestions for improvement for the meeting (Field 25). This field is optional.
26. Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the meeting (Field 26). This field is optional.
