# Community Supported Agriculture Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Supported Agriculture Order Form is a crucial document that helps us understand your preferences and needs for our produce delivery program. It's a comprehensive form that guides you through choosing the perfect share size, delivery frequency, and additional add-ons for your optimal produce experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each page carefully, ensuring you've answered every question.
2.  Select your preferred share size and delivery frequency based on your household size and schedule.
3.  Choose your preferred address type and delivery time.
4.  Specify your produce preferences and any dietary restrictions or allergies.
5.  Decide on your add-ons, such as eggs, honey, or flowers.
6.  Select your preferred payment method and payment frequency.
7.  Provide any special requests or comments.
8.  Confirm your order details and submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name (1)**: Enter your first and last name to be associated with your order.
*   **Email Address (2)**: Enter your email address for order confirmations and updates.
*   **Phone Number (3)**: Provide your phone number for delivery coordination.
*   **Delivery Address (4)**: Enter the address where you'd like to receive your produce deliveries.
*   **Address Type (5)**: Select the type of location where you'll be receiving your produce (e.g., home, work, community pickup point, farm market, or other).
*   **CSA Share Size (6)**: Choose the share size based on the number of people you're planning to serve (small: 1-2 people, medium: 2-4 people, large: 4-6 people, extra large: 6+ people).
*   **Delivery Frequency (7)**: Select how often you'd like to receive deliveries (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or one-time order).
*   **Preferred Start Date (8)**: Choose when you'd like your deliveries to begin.
*   **Produce Preferences (9)**: Select any produce preferences or restrictions you have (e.g., leafy greens, root vegetables, etc.).
*   **Organic Preference (10)**: Indicate if you prefer certified organic, organic, or no preference.
*   **Add Ons (11-13)**: Select if you'd like to add farm-fresh eggs, local honey, or fresh-cut flowers to your order.
*   **Dietary Restrictions (14)**: Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
*   **Delivery Window (15)**: Choose a preferred delivery time (morning, midday, afternoon, evening, or no preference).
*   **Special Delivery Instructions (16)**: Enter any special instructions for delivery (gate codes, leave at door, etc.).
*   **Payment Method (17)**: Select the method you'll use to pay for your order (credit/debit card, SNAP/EBT, check, cash on delivery, or farm credit if a member).
*   **Payment Frequency (18)**: Indicate how often you'll make payments (pay in full, monthly installments, per delivery).
*   **Farm Newsletter (19)**: Opt-in to receive our farm newsletter with recipes, updates, and news.
*   **Visit Interest (20)**: Indicate if you're interested in visiting the farm or attending events.
*   **Referral Source (21)**: Select where you heard about our CSA program (friend/family, social media, farmers market, website, community bulletin, local store, or other).
*   **Special Requests (22)**: Enter any additional information or comments you'd like to share with us.
*   **Order Date (23)**: Enter today's date to confirm your order.

Note: This help guide is designed to be concise and easy to follow. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us. We're here to help!
