# Personal Development Course Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students who have taken a personal development course. It aims to gather information on their course experience, including course name, type, length, rating, and any feedback they may have on the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the course name in the "Course Name" field.
2. Select the course type from the options provided in the "Course Type" field.
3. Enter the course length in the "Course Length" field. This can be a numerical value.
4. Rate your experience with the course in the "Course Rating" field.
5. Answer the feedback questions in the "Feedback Question 1", "Feedback Question 2", "Feedback Question 3", and "Feedback Question 4" fields. These questions are designed to gather specific insights from your experience.
6. Enter any additional comments you may have in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you would like to be contacted for more information.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you would like to be contacted by phone.
9. Enter the start date of the course in the "Course Start Date" field.
10. Enter the end date of the course in the "Course End Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Course Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the course you took.
- **Course Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of course you took (Online, Offline, or Hybrid).
- **Course Length** (number, optional): Enter the length of the course in hours, days, or any other unit of time that applies.
- **Course Rating** (number, optional): Rate your experience with the course on a scale of 1-5.
- **Feedback Question 1** (text, optional): Answer this question to provide specific feedback on the course.
- **Feedback Question 2** (text, optional): Answer this question to provide specific feedback on the course.
- **Feedback Question 3** (text, optional): Answer this question to provide specific feedback on the course.
- **Feedback Question 4** (text, optional): Answer this question to provide specific feedback on the course.
- **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments you may have about the course.
- **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for more information.
- **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
- **Course Start Date** (date, optional): Enter the start date of the course.
- **Course End Date** (date, optional): Enter the end date of the course.
