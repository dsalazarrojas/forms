# equity_in_digital_access_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the digital access and equity of a school. It is essential to have accurate and up-to-date data for making informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you are authorized to complete this form for your school.
2. Select the correct options for each field carefully, using the best knowledge available.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
4. If unsure about any question, use the 'Survey notes' field to provide additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School participants count** (1, number): Enter the total number of school participants.
- **School name** (2, text): Enter your school's name.
- **School city** (3, text): Enter your school's city.
- **School state** (4, text): Enter your school's state.
- **School county** (5, text): Enter your school's county.
- **Internet quality** (6, select_one): Choose the best description of your school's internet quality.
- **Device access** (7, select_multiple): Select all applicable devices your school has access to.
- **Barriers to connectivity** (8, select_one): Choose the main barrier to connectivity for your school.
- **Device type** (9, select_multiple): Select all applicable device types your school has.
- **Device access notes** (10, note): Provide any additional comments or notes about device access.
- **Device quality notes** (11, note): Provide any additional comments or notes about device quality.
- **Internet access notes** (12, note): Provide any additional comments or notes about internet access.
- **Barriers to connectivity notes** (13, note): Provide any additional comments or notes about barriers to connectivity.
- **Survey notes** (14, note): Provide any additional comments or notes about this survey.
- **Contact email** (15, email): Enter your contact email.
- **Contact phone** (16, text): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Contact person** (17, text): Enter your name or title.
- **Contact address** (18, text): Enter your school's address.
- **School district** (19, text): Enter your school's district.
- **School zip** (20, number): Enter your school's zip code.
- **School country** (21, select_one): Choose the country where your school is located.
- **City** (22, text): Enter your school's city.
- **State** (23, text): Enter your school's state.
- **County** (24, text): Enter your school's county.
- **School city** (25, text): This field should be left blank, as it is redundant with the 'City' field.
 
Note: Some fields may not be required but are still important for providing additional context and information. Please ensure that you answer all fields as accurately and completely as possible.
