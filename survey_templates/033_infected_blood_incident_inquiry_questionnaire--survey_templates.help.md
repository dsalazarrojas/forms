# Infected Blood Incident Inquiry Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents related to infected blood. It's used to gather details about each incident, including the date, location, and type of incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Review and edit your answers as needed.
3. Submit the form to ensure that all information is accurately recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **incident_date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **location** (`location`, text, optional): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **incident_type** (`incident_type`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **personal_account** (`personal_account`, text, optional): Share any personal information or details about the incident.
* **follow_up_status** (`follow_up_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options to indicate the follow-up status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Provide your name or initials.
* **organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization involved.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Share the contact number of the organization or individual involved.
* **follow_up_date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the follow-up.
* **follow_up_message** (`follow_up_message`, text, optional): Share any follow-up information or comments.
* **follow_up_status** (`follow_up_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options to indicate the follow-up status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **incident_review_date** (`incident_review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident review.
* **incident_review_status** (`incident_review_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options to indicate the incident review status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **organization_name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization.
* **organization_contact** (`organization_contact`, text, optional): Provide the contact information of the organization.
* **organization_email** (`organization_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the organization.
* **organization_phone** (`organization_phone`, text, optional): Share the phone number of the organization.
* **organization_address** (`organization_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the organization.
* **organization_website** (`organization_website`, text, optional): Enter the website URL of the organization.
* **group_name** (`group_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the group involved.
* **group_contact** (`group_contact`, text, optional): Provide the contact information of the group.
* **group_email** (`group_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the group.
* **group_phone** (`group_phone`, text, optional): Share the phone number of the group.
* **group_website** (`group_website`, text, optional): Enter the website URL of the group.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Review and edit your answers before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or the form's administrator.
* This form is designed to be completed by medical professionals or staff members, so be sure to use your professional judgment when filling it out.
