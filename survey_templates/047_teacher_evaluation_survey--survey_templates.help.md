# Teacher Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate teachers based on student feedback. The form has eight pages, each with its own purpose:
- Page 1: student_feedback_form: Collects feedback from students about a teacher.
- Page 2: parent_feedback_form: Collects feedback from parents about a teacher.
- Page 3: evaluation_score_form: Collects evaluation scores from students.
- Page 4: evaluation_comments_form: Provides space for students to leave comments about a teacher.
- Page 5: email_form: Collects email addresses from students (likely for follow-up).
- Page 6: phone_form: Collects phone numbers from students (likely for follow-up).
- Page 7: date_form: Collects the date the survey is completed.
- Page 8: time_form: Collects the time the survey is completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the page you want to complete.
2. Fill out each page according to its purpose and label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: student-feedback-form (Page 1)
* **student-feedback-form** (`student_feedback_form`, text, required/optional: required): Enter your honest feedback about the teacher. Be as detailed as possible.

### Page 2: parent-feedback-form (Page 2)
* **parent-feedback-form** (`parent_feedback_form`, text, required/optional: required): Enter your honest feedback about the teacher. Be as detailed as possible.

### Page 3: evaluation-score-form (Page 3)
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score_form`, select_one, required/optional: required): Select one of the following scores for each question:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Needs Improvement

### Page 4: evaluation-comments-form (Page 4)
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments_form`, note, required/optional: required): Leave any comments you may have about the teacher.

### Page 5: email-form (Page 5)
* **Email** (`email_form`, email, required/optional: required): Enter your email address. This will likely be used for follow-up.

### Page 6: phone-form (Page 6)
* **Phone Number** (`phone_form`, text, required/optional: required): Enter your phone number. This will likely be used for follow-up.

### Page 7: date-form (Page 7)
* **Date** (`date_form`, date, required/optional: required): Enter the date the survey is completed.

### Page 8: time-form (Page 8)
* **Time** (`time_form`, time, required/optional: required): Enter the time the survey is completed.

## Tips
- Be honest and detailed in your feedback.
- Select the correct scores for each question.
- Enter your actual contact information (email and phone number).
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- Make sure to complete all fields that are marked as required.
