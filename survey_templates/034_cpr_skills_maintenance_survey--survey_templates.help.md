# CPR Skills Maintenance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CPR Skills Maintenance Survey is designed to assess your skills and experiences related to CPR maintenance. The survey will help to identify areas of improvement and provide recommendations for training and certification maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in your name, email address, date of completion of your CPR training, name of the instructor, and the location of the training.
3. Indicate your current certification status and how long it has been since your certification was obtained.
4. Rate your confidence in performing CPR and your skills in areas such as compression, airway management, AED operation, and CPR practice frequency.
5. Provide feedback on whether you think a refresher is needed and what you think could be improved in the training.
6. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback on your experience with CPR training.
