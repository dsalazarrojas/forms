# Tech App Usability Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the usability of a tech app. It aims to gather information about users' experiences and opinions on the app, including their overall satisfaction and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read through each field carefully and answer the questions according to your thoughts and experiences with the app.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the answer that best describes your experience or opinion.
3. If you have any specific comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the "Comments" field.
4. Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** (Note)
This is a brief introduction to the form. It is not a required field, so you can skip it if you prefer.

### 2. **Rating** (Number)
Rate the app's usability on a scale from 1 to 10.

### 3. **Satisfaction** (Select Multiple)
Select all the categories that best describe your overall satisfaction with the app:

* Excellent
* Good
* Average
* Poor

### 4. **Improvement** (Text)
If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, type them in this field.

### 5. **Suggestions** (Select One)
What area of the app would you like to suggest for improvement:

* Feature
* User Interface
* Usability
* Performance
* Design
* Content
* Other

### 6. **Comments** (Text)
Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the app.

### 7. **Email** (Email)
If you have an email address, provide it so that we can contact you for further discussion.

### 8. **Phone** (Text)
If you have a phone number, provide it so that we can contact you for further discussion.

### 9. **Contact** (Select Multiple)
If you are willing to be contacted:

* False
* True

### 10. **Submit** (Text)
Type your name in this field to confirm your submission.

### 11. **Feedback** (Date)
Select the date you completed this form.

### 12. **Date** (Date)
Select the date you completed this form.

### 13. **Time** (Time)
Select the time you completed this form.

### 14. **Name** (Text)
Enter your name to confirm your identity.

This guide should help you complete the form efficiently and effectively. If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to ask.
