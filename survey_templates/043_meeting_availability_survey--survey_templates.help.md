# Meeting_Availability_Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about meeting availability, including start and end times, duration, and location. The goal is to create a clear and concise request for meetings and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form fields carefully and fill in the required information.
2.  Enter your start time in the "Start Time" field. Please use the 24-hour format (e.g., 13:00 for 1 pm).
3.  Enter your end time in the "End Time" field. Please use the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2 pm).
4.  Enter the duration of your meeting in the "Duration" field. This is the time you will be unavailable.
5.  Enter the location of your meeting in the "Location" field. This could be a room number, a specific office, or any other relevant location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Meeting Availabilty Survey** (`meeting_availability_survey`, text, required/optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide a brief description of the meeting or event. Please keep it short and concise.
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of your meeting using the 24-hour format. For example, 13:00 for 1 pm.
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the end time of your meeting using the 24-hour format. For example, 14:00 for 2 pm.
*   **Duration** (`duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the length of your meeting in minutes or hours. For example, 30 for a 30-minute meeting.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your meeting, such as a room number, office, or other relevant location.

## Tips
*   Be concise in your responses to ensure the meeting request is clear and easy to understand.
*   Use the 24-hour format for times.
*   If you have any issues or need clarification, don't hesitate to reach out.
