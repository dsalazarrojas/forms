# Anonymous Technology Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect anonymous feedback from users about a specific technology or software.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely, providing as much information as possible about your experience with the technology.
* Select the relevant options for each question from the provided list or rate the technology based on the scale provided.
* Make sure to provide specific examples or explanations when answering questions that ask for free-text responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tool or Software Name**: Enter the name of the technology you are providing feedback for.
* **Frequency of Use**: Select how often you use the technology. Options are:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times a week
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **Primary Purpose**: Enter the main reason you use the technology.
* **Ease of Use Rating**: Rate the ease of use of the technology on a scale of 1 (very hard) to 5 (very easy).
* **Reliability and Uptime Rating**: Select the reliability and uptime of the technology. Options are:
	+ Very Unreliable
	+ Unreliable
	+ Reliable
	+ Very Reliable
* **Speed and Performance Rating**: Select the speed and performance of the technology. Options are:
	+ Slow
	+ Average
	+ Fast
	+ Excellent
* **Satisfaction with Available Features**: Rate your satisfaction with the available features on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Known Issues or Bugs**: Describe any persistent issues or bugs you have encountered with the technology. This is a free-text field, so provide as much detail as possible.
* **Have you received adequate training for this tool?**: Select whether you have received adequate training for the technology. Options are:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Some
* **Technical Support Quality Rating**: Select the quality of technical support you have received. Options are:
	+ Poor
	+ Average
	+ Good
	+ N/A
* **Suggestions for Improvements or New Features**: Provide any suggestions you have for improvements or new features for the technology. This is a free-text field, so provide as much detail as possible.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions as accurately and honestly as possible.
* If you have any questions or need help with a specific question, contact the support team.
* Your feedback is appreciated and will help the technology developers to make informed decisions about future development.
