# Volleyball Team Snack Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volleyball Team Snack Preferences Survey is designed to collect information about the snack preferences of volleyball players on a team. The data collected will be used to provide personalized snack recommendations for each team member.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form by providing your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Indicate your dietary needs by selecting one of the options (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, or None) in the "Dietary Needs" field.
3. Choose your snack preferences by selecting all that apply from the list in the "Snack Preferences" field.
4. Enter your Team ID and Coach's name in the respective fields.
5. If you have a parent or guardian, please enter their name in the "Parent Name" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`athlete_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`athlete_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Dietary Needs** (`athlete_dietary_needs`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ None
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten-free
* **Snack Preferences** (`athlete_snack_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all that apply:
	+ Water
	+ Fruits
	+ Vegetables
	+ Cheese
	+ Granola
* **Team ID** (`team_id`, `number`, required): Please enter your team ID.
* **Coach's Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of your coach.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, optional): If you have a parent or guardian, please enter their name.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have reviewed the relevant policies and procedures for this team before completing this survey.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your coach or team administrator for assistance.
* Please complete this survey honestly and accurately to ensure that we can provide the best possible snack recommendations for you.
