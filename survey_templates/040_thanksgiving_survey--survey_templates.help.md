# thanksgiving_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about how participants heard about the organization and their preferences related to the holiday season. This will help us understand our outreach efforts and tailor our marketing strategies accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question and section.
2. Answer all required fields (indicated by a asterisk *) to the best of your ability.
3. Select the relevant options from the provided choices.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Page 1)** (`intro`, note, optional): This section is a general introduction to the form. Please read through it to understand the purpose and context of the questions that follow.
* **How did you hear about us** (`question1`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the ways you heard about our organization.
	+ *Email*: You heard about us through an email.
	+ *Phone*: You heard about us through a phone call.
	+ *Friend*: You heard about us through a friend.
	+ *Other*: You heard about us through another means not listed here.
* **Answer 1** (`answer1`, text, optional): This is a text field for you to provide additional information.
* **What is your favorite holiday dish?** (`question2`, select_one, required): Please select your favorite holiday dish from the options provided.
	+ *Turkey*: Turkey is a classic holiday choice.
	+ *Ham*: Ham is another popular option.
	+ *Potatoes*: Potatoes are a comforting holiday favorite.
	+ *Pumpkin Pie*: Pumpkin pie is a sweet holiday treat.
* **Answer 2** (`answer2`, text, optional): This is another text field for you to provide additional information.
* **How many guests will be at your table?** (`question3`, number, required): Please enter the number of guests you expect to have at your table.
* **Answer 3** (`answer3`, text, optional): This is the final text field for you to provide additional information.
