# Erasmus Consortium Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Erasmus Consortium Interest Survey is a tool for partner organizations to express their interest in participating in the Erasmus program. This form is intended to gather information about your organization and its readiness to partner with us.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Organization Name** field with the name of your organization.
2. Enter the name of the contact person for your organization in the **Contact Person** field.
3. Provide the email address of the contact person in the **Email** field.
4. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the **Phone** field.
5. Select your partner organization from the list of options in the **Partner Organization** field.
6. Choose the areas of interest for your partner organization from the list in the **Partner Organization Interests** field.
7. Select your partner organization's readiness level from the list in the **Partner Organization Readiness** field.
8. Select the status of your partner organization from the list in the **Partner Organization Status** field.
9. Enter the name of the person submitting the form in the **Submitter Name** field.
10. Enter the email address of the person submitting the form in the **Submitter Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name (1)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Person (3)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person to contact regarding your organization's interest in the Erasmus program.
* **Email (4)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone (5)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Partner Organization (6)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select your partner organization from the list of options.

* **Partner Organization Interests (7)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the areas of interest for your partner organization from the list.

* **Partner Organization Readiness (8)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select your partner organization's readiness level from the list.

* **Partner Organization Status (9)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the status of your partner organization from the list.
	+ Options:

* **Submitter Name (10)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email (11)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
