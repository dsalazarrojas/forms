# Pharmacy Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on your experience with our pharmacy services, focusing on various aspects such as service quality, staff interaction, and overall satisfaction. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make our services better for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please answer each question according to your personal experience. You can choose from the options provided for questions with multiple-choice answers. For text-based questions, provide your honest feedback in the space provided. Make sure to answer all required questions to ensure your feedback is taken into account.
