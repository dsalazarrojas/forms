# cultural_responsiveness_in_teaching_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the level of cultural responsiveness in teaching practices, teacher and school support, and resources. It helps to identify areas of improvement and opportunities for growth in promoting diversity and inclusion in the classroom and school environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Select the options that best describe your current practices or circumstances.
3. For fields with "None" as an option, select "None" if you do not have any experience or resources in that area.
