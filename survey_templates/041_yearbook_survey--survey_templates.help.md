# Yearbook Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Yearbook Survey is a form designed to collect information from students and teachers about their yearbook experiences. It helps school administrators and teachers to gather data for yearbook creation and planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student.
* **Grade** (`grade`, text, required: false): Enter the grade level of the student.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, text, required: false): Enter the student number or ID.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the student.
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the student.
* **Address** (`student_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the student.
* **Birth Date** (`student_birthdate`, date, required: false): Enter the birth date of the student.
* **Class** (`student_class`, text, required: false): Enter the class or group the student belongs to.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the teacher.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the teacher.
* **Teacher Address** (`teacher_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the teacher.
* **Teacher Birth Date** (`teacher_birthdate`, date, required: false): Enter the birth date of the teacher.
* **Start Date** (`student_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the student's involvement with the school.
* **End Date** (`student_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the student's involvement with the school.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the school.
* **Teacher School** (`teacher_school`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher's school.
* **School Address** (`teacher_school_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the teacher's school.
* **Teacher Grade** (`teacher_grade`, text, required: false): Enter the grade level of the teacher.
* **Message** (`yearbook_message`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or messages related to the yearbook.
* **Start Date/Time** (`student_start_date_time`, date, required: false): Enter the start date and time of the student's yearbook creation process.
* **End Date/Time** (`student_end_date_time`, date, required: false): Enter the end date and time of the student's yearbook creation process.
* **Start Time** (`teacher_start_date_time`, date, required: false): Enter the start time of the teacher's yearbook creation process.
* **End Time** (`teacher_end_date_time`, date, required: false): Enter the end time of the teacher's yearbook creation process.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use correct formatting for date fields (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* Double-check the information for accuracy before submission.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your school administrator or teacher for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only; please do not share it with external parties.
