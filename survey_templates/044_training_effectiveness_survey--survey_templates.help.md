# Training Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the effectiveness of past training sessions. Your input will help us improve future sessions and provide better learning experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience with the training session.
3. For questions that ask for comments or suggestions, please provide your thoughts and ideas.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Effectiveness** (`training_effectiveness_survey_1`, select_one, required: false)
	+ How would you rate the overall effectiveness of the training? Please choose one of the two options: Yes or No.
* **How would you rate the overall effectiveness of the training?** (`training_effectiveness_survey_2`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question is identical to the first, please choose one of the two options: Good or Fair.
* **How would you rate the trainer's ability to engage and motivate participants?** (`training_effectiveness_survey_3`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Please rate the trainer's ability to keep you engaged and motivated during the session.
* **How would you rate the relevance of the topics presented during the training?** (`training_effectiveness_survey_4`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Rate the relevance of the topics presented in the session.
* **Which of the following topics would you like to see presented in future sessions?** (`training_effectiveness_survey_5`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select the topics that you would like to see presented in future sessions.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving future sessions?** (`training_effectiveness_survey_6`, text, required: false)
	+ Please provide any thoughts or ideas on how we can improve future sessions.
* **Do you have any other comments or concerns about this session?** (`training_effectiveness_survey_7`, text, required: false)
	+ If you have any other thoughts or concerns, please share them with us.
* **What is your name?** (`training_effectiveness_survey_8`, text, required: false)
	+ Please enter your name for reference.
* **What is your email address?** (`training_effectiveness_survey_9`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **What is your phone number?** (`training_effectiveness_survey_10`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number for future contact.
* **Date of session** (`training_effectiveness_survey_11`, date, required: false)
	+ Please enter the date of the session you attended.
* **Time of session** (`training_effectiveness_survey_12`, time, required: false)
	+ Enter the time of the session.
* **Session start time** (`training_effectiveness_survey_13`, time, required: false)
	+ Enter the start time of the session.
* **Session end time** (`training_effectiveness_survey_14`, time, required: false)
	+ Enter the end time of the session.
* **Date of next session** (`training_effectiveness_survey_15`, date, required: false)
	+ If known, enter the date of the next session.

Note: If a field has the "required: false" property, it is not mandatory to fill it in. However, please provide your honest feedback wherever possible.
