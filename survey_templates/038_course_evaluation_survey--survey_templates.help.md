# course_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The course evaluation survey is a form used to gather feedback from students about their experiences in a course. It is designed to assess various aspects of the course, including its content, organization, difficulty, relevance, value, and satisfaction, as well as the quality and effectiveness of the instructor. This survey helps instructors and course administrators understand the strengths and weaknesses of the course and make necessary adjustments to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have a clear understanding of the questions and fields.
2.  Answer each question in as much detail as possible, providing specific examples and reasons for your feedback.
3.  Please note that some fields are optional, but we encourage you to provide feedback on all aspects of the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Organization** (`course_organization`, text, required: false): Describe the organization and structure of the course.
* **Course Content** (`course_content`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts on the content of the course, including any topics or material you found particularly useful or challenging.
* **Course Difficulty** (`course_difficulty`, text, required: false): Rate the difficulty level of the course, including any obstacles or challenges you faced.
* **Course Relevance** (`course_relevance`, text, required: false): Discuss the relevance of the course material to your academic or professional goals.
* **Course Value** (`course_value`, text, required: false): Share your overall impression of the course's value and how it contributed to your learning outcomes.
* **Course Satisfaction** (`course_satisfaction`, text, required: false): Express your level of satisfaction with the course, including any aspects that were satisfactory or unsatisfactory.
* **Instructor Quality** (`instructor_quality`, select multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply regarding the quality of the instructor, such as "Good", "Poor", or "Good".
* **Instructor Effectiveness** (`instructor_effectiveness`, select one, required: true): Select the level of effectiveness of the instructor, such as "Effective", "Needs Improvement", or "Not Effective".
* **Instructor Support** (`instructor_support`, text, required: false): Share any feedback on the support provided by the instructor, such as responses to questions or availability for guidance.
