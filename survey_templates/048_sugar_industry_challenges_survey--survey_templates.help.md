# sugar-industry-challenges-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather feedback on the major pain points in the sugar industry. Your input will help us better understand the challenges faced by industry professionals and inform our service improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the sections below, answering the questions as they apply to you and your organization. You can save your progress and come back later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are some of the major pain points in the sugar industry?** (`industry_pain_points`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the current pain points you are experiencing in the sugar industry. This section is meant to gather a list of major issues.
* **What is your role in the industry?** (`role`, select_one, optional): Please select the best description of your current role in the sugar industry:
	+ Yes, I am an industry professional
	+ No, I am not an industry professional
* **Which region do you belong to?** (`region`, select_one, optional): Please select your region of association:
	+ Yes, I am from this region
	+ No, I am not from this region
* **What are your top-reported priority for your company/organization?** (`priority_one`, select_multiple, optional): This field is meant to gather the top priority challenges for your organization. Please select all that apply:
	+ Yes, I have this priority
	+ No, I do not have this priority
* **What is your second-reported priority for your company/organization?** (`priority_two`, select_multiple, optional): Similarly to the previous field, please select all that apply:
	+ Yes, I have this priority
	+ No, I do not have this priority
* **Additional notes or comments** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional notes or comments, please include them here. This section is optional and meant for any extra information you may want to provide.

## Tips
* Take your time, and feel free to save your progress and come back later if needed.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your pain points and priorities.
* If you are unsure about any question, please do not hesitate to contact us for clarification.
