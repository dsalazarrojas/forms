# Educational Program Impact Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects feedback and evaluation data from participants about a training program. Please provide accurate and honest answers to help program administrators assess the program's impact and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) and any other relevant information as you see fit.
2. For multiple-choice questions (e.g., 'What impact did the training have on you?'), select all that apply.
3. If you have additional comments or feedback, use the 'Additional Comments' field.
4. Please ensure that you have correctly entered the training date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Title** (`training_title`, text, not required): Provide a brief title for the training program attended.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, not required): Provide the name of the program or course.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Share any additional comments or concerns you may have about the training.
* **Training Outcomes** (`training_outcomes`, text, not required): Describe the outcomes or benefits gained from the training.
* **What impact did the training have on you?** (`program_impact`, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply:
	+ Positive
	+ Somewhat positive
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat negative
	+ Negative
	+ Very negative
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your work or contact email.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you attended the training.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you attended the training.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, text, not required): Provide the location of the training program.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, text, not required): Describe the duration of the training program.
* **Training Facilitator** (`training_facilitator`, text, not required): Identify the facilitator or instructor.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Share any additional comments or feedback.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Enter the date you attended the training.
* **Program Length** (`program_length`, text, not required): Describe the length or duration of the program.
* **Training Length** (`training_length`, text, not required): Describe the length or duration of the training program.
* **Session Number** (`session_number`, number, required): Enter the number of sessions completed.
* **Total Sessions** (`sessions_total`, number, required): Enter the total number of sessions.
* **Completed Sessions** (`sessions_completed`, number, required): Enter the number of sessions completed.
* **Program ID** (`program_id`, text, required): Enter the program ID or identifier.

Please take a moment to review and fill out the form carefully. Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your feedback.
