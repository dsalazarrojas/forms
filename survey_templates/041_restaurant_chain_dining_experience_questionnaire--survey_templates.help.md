# restaurant_chain_dining_experience_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their dining experience at various restaurant chains. By providing their feedback, you'll help us improve our services and ensure that your next visit is even better.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reading through each question carefully.
2. Select your answers to each question based on your actual experience.
3. Fill in the required information for each field.
4. Once you've completed all the questions, submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1):
	+ This is a text field for you to provide any additional context about your visit.
* **Restaurant Experience** (Page 2):
	+ This is a numerical field for you to rate your overall experience at the restaurant.
* **Restaurant Chain** (Page 3):
	+ Select the restaurant chain you visited from the dropdown list.
* **Dining Experience** (Page 4):
	+ This is a text field for you to describe your dining experience.
* **Food Quality** (Page 5):
	+ This is a numerical field for you to rate the quality of the food.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Page 6):
	+ Select all that apply from the dropdown list to indicate your overall satisfaction with the restaurant.
* **Visit Date** (Page 7):
	+ Enter the date you visited the restaurant.
* **Visit Time** (Page 8):
	+ Enter the time you visited the restaurant.
* **Comment** (Page 9):
	+ This is a text field for you to provide any additional comments about your visit.
* **Overall Satisfaction (2)** (Page 10):
	+ Select all that apply from the dropdown list to indicate your overall satisfaction with the restaurant.
* **Comment 2** (Page 11):
	+ This is a text field for you to provide any additional comments about your visit.
* **Comment 3** (Page 12):
	+ This is a text field for you to provide any additional comments about your visit.
* ...
* **Comment 20** (Page 21):
