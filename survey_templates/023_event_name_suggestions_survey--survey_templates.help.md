# Event Name Suggestions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect suggestions for event names from users. Please use the form to propose names for events you are interested in, and we will review them for duplication and relevance before adding them to the list.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select an event name from the options provided in the "Event Name" section.
2. If you have additional suggestions, select them from the options provided in the "Additional Suggestions" section.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Optionally, enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (text, required): Enter a specific name for the event you are interested in. Please be as descriptive as possible.
* **Event Name Ideas** (select one, required): Choose the name you think best describes the event. If you don't see the name you are looking for, please contact us.
* **Additional Suggestions** (select multiple, optional): Provide any other ideas you may have for the event name. You can choose one or more options from the list.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your suggestion.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Optionally, enter your phone number so we can contact you about your suggestion.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide your email address so we can get in touch with you about your suggestion.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your email address provided.
* We will review your submission for duplication and relevance before adding it to the list.
