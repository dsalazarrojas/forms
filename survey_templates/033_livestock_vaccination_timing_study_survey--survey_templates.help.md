# Livestock Vaccination Timing Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the vaccination timing of livestock for a study. The form helps gather data for research or statistical analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide farmer information in the "Farmer Info" field, which is a text field where you can enter details about the farmer.
2. In the "Vaccines Administered" field, select all the vaccines that have been administered to the livestock, as they are presented in a multiple select field.
3. Enter the vaccination schedule in the "Vaccination Schedule" field, which is a date field where you can select a date.
4. In the "Challenges" field, enter any challenges or issues that have occurred during the vaccination process.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Practices" in the "Practices" field, which is a select one field.
6. In the "Monitoring" field, enter any monitoring information related to the livestock.
7. Select the frequency of monitoring in the "Monitoring Frequency" field, as it is a text field.
8. Enter the method of monitoring in the "Monitoring Method" field, which is a text field.
9. In the "Data Source" field, enter the source of the data being collected.
10. Add any additional information in the "Additional Info" field, which is a text field.
11. Enter details about the farmer's vaccination scheduling in the "Farmer Vaccination Scheduling" field.
12. In the "Health Provider Info" field, enter information about the health provider involved in the vaccination process.
13. Provide details about the animal's info in the "Animal Info" field.
14. In the "Vaccine Storage" field, enter details about the storage of the vaccines.
15. Answer questions about the vaccine distribution in the "Vaccine Distribution" fields.
16-21. Enter any challenges that occurred during the vaccination process in the "Challenge 1-5" fields.
22. Enter any other challenge information in the "Challenge 6-10" fields.
