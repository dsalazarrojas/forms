# Restaurant Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback about your recent visit to a restaurant. It will help us understand your experiences and identify areas for improvement. Your input will help us provide better services and improve the quality of our restaurants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and familiarize yourself with each field.
2. Answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
3. Select the corresponding options that best match your experience.
4. Submit your completed form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`General Info`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or information about your visit.
* **Food Quality** (`Food Quality`, `select_multiple`, optional): Rate the quality of the food you ordered. Select all that apply:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Order Accuracy** (`Order Accuracy`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the accuracy of your order. Choose:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Service Quality** (`Service Quality`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the quality of the service you received. Choose:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Cleanliness** (`Cleanliness`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the cleanliness of the restaurant. Choose:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Speed of Service** (`Speed of Service`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the speed of service you received. Choose:
	+ Fast
	+ Slow
	+ Average
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your visit. Choose:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your feedback.
* Please provide specific examples or details to help us understand your experience better.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, feel free to add them in the "General Info" field.
