# Emergency Preparedness Drill Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Preparedness Drill Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the effectiveness of emergency preparedness drills. It helps emergency response teams and stakeholders evaluate the drill's strengths and weaknesses, providing valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the drill's objectives and outcomes
2. Answer the questions based on your observations and experiences during the drill
3. Provide detailed explanations and justifications for your answers

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide a brief introduction or overview of the drill.
* **Was there a designated observation area?** (`observation_area_question`, `select_one`): This question assesses whether a designated observation area was present during the drill. Answer with either "True" or "False".
* **What was observed in the observation area?** (`observation_area_description`, `text`): If a designated observation area was present, describe what was observed there.
* **Was communication effective?** (`communication_question`, `select_multiple`): This question evaluates the effectiveness of communication during the drill. Answer with "True", "Partially", or "Not At All".
* **Can you explain?** (`communication_description`, `text`): Provide a detailed explanation of your answer to the previous question.
* **Was the observation area visible to all?** (`observation_area_visibility`, `select_one`): This question checks if the observation area was visible to all participants.
* **What was the size of the observation area?** (`observation_area_size`, `select_one`): If an observation area was present, describe its size as "Large", "Medium", "Small", or "Other".
* **Was the observation area visible to participants?** (`observation_area_visibility_to_participants`, `select_multiple`): This question checks if the observation area was visible to participants.
* **Was the communication questionnaire clear?** (`communication_questionnaire`, `select_multiple`): This question evaluates the clarity of the communication questionnaire. Answer with "True", "Partially", "Not At All", or "None".
* **What was in the observation area?** (`observation_area_equipment`, `text`): Describe any equipment or objects present in the observation area.
* **Were observer instructions clear?** (`observer_instructions`, `select_multiple`): This question checks if observer instructions were clear and understandable.
* **Observer count** (`observer_count`, `number`): Provide the number of observers present during the drill.
* **Drill time** (`drill_time`, `text`): Record the duration of the drill.
* **Observation prophets count** (`observation_prophets`, `number`): This is another field for recording the number of observation points.
* **Participant count** (`participants_count`, `number`): Provide the number of participants in the drill.
* **Was there an observation area for the communication questionnaire?** (`communication_questionnaire_type`, `select_multiple`): This question evaluates if there was an observation area for the communication questionnaire.
* **Location of the observation area** (`observation_area_location`, `text`): Describe the location of the observation area.
* **Equipment in the observation area** (`observation_area_equipment_description`, `text`): Describe any equipment or objects present in the observation area.
* **Was the observation area for the communication questionnaire visible to all?** (`communication_questionnaire_visibility`, `select_one`): This question checks if the observation area for the communication questionnaire was visible to all participants.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when answering questions and providing explanations.
* Use concrete examples from the drill to support your answers.
* Review and revise your answers before submitting the form.
