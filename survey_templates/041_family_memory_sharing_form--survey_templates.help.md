# Family Memory Sharing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Memory Sharing Form is a template for collecting family memories and stories. This form is designed to help you gather information about your family's history, experiences, and relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your family's name in the "Family Name" field.
2. Indicate your relationship to a family member in the "Relationship To Family Member" field.
3. Enter a title for your story in the "Story Title" field.
4. Type a description of your story in the "Story Description" field.
5. Choose a date for your story in the "Story Date" field (this can be any date in the past or present).
6. Choose a time for your story in the "Story Time" field (this can be any time of day).
7. Add any relevant media (e.g., images, videos) to your story in the "Story Media" field.
8. List the family members who are involved in your story in the "Family Members" field.
9. Add any additional notes about your story in the "Note" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field (this will be used to share the output document).
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (this will be used to contact you about the document).
12. Choose the tool that will be assigned to work on your story in the "Assigned Tool" field.
13. Finally, choose a file name for your output document in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Name** (`family_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your family.
* **Relationship To Family Member** (`relationship_to_family_member`, text, required: false): Indicate how you are related to a family member (e.g., parent, child, sibling).
* **Story Title** (`story_title`, text, required: false): Give your story a title that summarizes what it's about.
* **Story Description** (`story_description`, text, required: false): Type a description of your story.
* **Story Date** (`story_date`, date, required: false): Choose a date for your story (this can be any date in the past or present).
* **Story Time** (`story_time`, time, required: false): Choose a time for your story (this can be any time of day).
* **Story Media** (`story_media`, text, required: false): Add any relevant media (e.g., images, videos) to your story.
* **Family Members** (`family_members`, select_multiple, required: false): List all family members involved in your story.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your story.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to receive the output document.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to be contacted about the document.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Choose the tool that will be assigned to work on your story.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required: false): Choose a file name for your output document.
