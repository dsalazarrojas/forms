# Thanksgiving Potluck Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the upcoming Thanksgiving Potluck event, allowing us to better understand your preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of event you're attending or organizing. Choose from Friendsgiving Party, Family Gathering, Office Celebration, School Function, Community Event, or Church Potluck.
2. Enter the date and time of the event. Note that this is optional, as some events may not have specific dates or times set.
3. Fill in the address or location where the event will take place.
4. Estimate the number of guests you expect to attend.
5. Indicate the type of food you're willing to bring, choosing from Vegetarian, Vegan, Meat, Seafood, or Salad options. You can choose one or multiple options.
6. Add any additional comments or requests related to food.
7. Enter your email address and phone number so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Type**: Choose the type of event you're attending or organizing (Friendsgiving Party, Family Gathering, etc.)
  - This field helps us understand the type of event and plan accordingly.
- **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event (optional, only if you're sure of the date)
  - This helps us schedule the event and ensure everyone knows the timing.
- **Event Location**: Enter the address or location of the event
  - This ensures we know where to send the food and find the venue.
- **Number of Guests**: Estimate the number of guests you expect to attend
  - This helps us plan food and space requirements.
- **Potluck Food**: Choose from Vegetarian, Vegan, Meat, Seafood, or Salad options for the food you're willing to bring
  - This ensures we know what types of food to expect and can accommodate dietary needs.
- **Food Items**: Add any additional food items or comments
  - This allows you to provide more specific information about your dish.
- **Food Dietary Restrictions**: List any food restrictions or dietary concerns
  - This ensures we're aware of any special needs and can plan accordingly.
- **Note**: Add any additional remarks or requests
  - This is an open space for you to share anything else you'd like to mention.
- **Email**: Enter your email address
  - This helps us get in touch with you and ensure you receive important updates.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number
  - This allows us to contact you with last-minute details or requests.

## Tips
- Be accurate when filling in the event date and location to avoid confusion.
- If you're unsure about the event type or location, please let us know and we'll clarify.
- If you have specific dietary restrictions, please list them in the food dietary restrictions field.
- We appreciate your honesty and transparency in filling out this form.
