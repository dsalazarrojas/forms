## Waste Management Services Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers on the quality of our waste management services. The feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and ensure we provide the best possible service to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Rate the Quality of Our Services**:
	* Answer the question on the quality of our waste management services on a scale of 1-5.
2. **Report Any Issues**:
	* Describe any problems you've experienced with our waste management services.
3. **Suggest Improvements**:
	* Select all the areas where you think we can improve our waste management services.
	* If "Other" is selected, please specify the area of improvement in the text field.
4. **Get In Touch**:
	* If you'd like to be contacted by a member of our team, type your contact information in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate the quality of our waste management services? (service_quality)**:
	+ A scale of 1-5 to rate the quality of our waste management services.
* **What issues do you have with our waste management services? (service_issues)**:
	+ Describe any problems you've experienced with our waste management services.
* **What are some areas where we can improve our waste management services? (improvement_priorities)**:
	+ Select all the areas where you think we can improve our waste management services.
	+ If "Other" is selected, please specify the area of improvement in the text field.
* **Would you like to be contacted by a member of our team? (contact_info)**:
	+ If yes, type your contact information in the text field.
