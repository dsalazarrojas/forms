# MBA Employment Outcomes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the employment outcomes of MBA graduates. Please fill out the form to help us understand the current employment status of our MBA graduates, their job titles, industries, job functions, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply navigate through the pages and fill out the fields with the requested information. You can select from the available options in the select fields, and enter your answer in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mba Program** (`mba_program`, text, optional): This field is used to record the name of the MBA program completed.
* **Employment Outcome** (`employment_outcome`, text, optional): This field is used to record the employment outcome of the graduate, e.g. employed, searching, etc.
* **Current Employment Status** (`current_employment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to the current employment status of the graduate. Options include:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): This field is used to record the job title of the graduate.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, optional): This field is used to record the industry of the graduate's job.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): This field is used to record the job function of the graduate.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of employment for the graduate. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to the job type of the graduate. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional): This field is used to record the salary of the graduate.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to the job location of the graduate. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, text, optional): This field is used to record the job satisfaction of the graduate.
* **Employment Outcome Purpose** (`employment_outcome_purpose`, text, optional): This field is used to record the purpose of employment outcome.
* **Career Services Use** (`career_services_use`, text, optional): This field is used to record the career services use of the graduate.
* **Job Search Status** (`job_search_status`, select_one, optional): Please select the job search status of the graduate. Options include:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
