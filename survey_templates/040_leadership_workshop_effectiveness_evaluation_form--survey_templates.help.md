# Leadership Workshop Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Leadership Workshop Effectiveness Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback on the effectiveness of our Leadership Workshop, providing valuable insights to help us improve and refine our training program.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To start, please provide the workshop's title in the **workshop_title** field, which will serve as a unique identifier for the form.
*   Enter the name of the workshop you attended in the **workshop_name** field, ensuring that it accurately reflects the event's details.
*   In the **date** field, type the date of the workshop you attended. If you're unsure, you can also find this information on any promotional materials or emails from the workshop.
*   The **time** field is where you'll record the duration of the workshop, which will help us better understand how long you were engaged with the content.
*   To express your overall satisfaction, select a rating from 0 to 5 (Good, Fair, Good, Fair, Good) in the **rating** field. This will give us an idea of how impactful the workshop was for you.
*   Write down a brief description of what worked well and what did not in the **rating_label** field.
*   Select all the options that apply to you from the **rating_scale** field, which will help us understand your specific experiences and preferences.
*   Please provide your thoughts, opinions, and recommendations for future improvement in the **comments** field.
*   If available, please enter your email address in the **email** field so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or to share your feedback with our team.
*   Enter your phone number in the **phone** field for any further communication or follow-up discussions. Please ensure it is a valid phone number and is not used for telemarketing purposes.
*   In the **submitted_by** field, provide a unique identifier for yourself, such as your name and initials, so we can associate your response with your identity.
*   Finally, record the date and time you submitted this form in the **submitted_at** field, ensuring that your feedback is up-to-date and accurate.
