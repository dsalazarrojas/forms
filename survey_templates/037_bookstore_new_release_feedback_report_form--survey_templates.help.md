# Bookstore New Release Feedback Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from bookstore staff on new releases. The feedback is used to help decide which books to feature in the store's displays and suggest similar books that are currently selling well.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Staff Member Name" field.
2. Enter the date of your feedback in the "Report Date" field.
3. Enter the title of the new release in the "Book Title" field.
4. Enter the author or authors of the new release in the "Author Name" field.
5. Select the genre of the book from the options provided.
6. Rate the cover design of the book from the options provided.
7. Rate the book's quality and production from the options provided.
8. Indicate your expected reader reaction from the options provided.
9. Select the book's sales potential from the options provided.
10. Choose how you recommend displaying the book from the options provided.
11. Choose the target audience for the book from the options provided.
12. If applicable, enter similar books that are currently selling well.
13. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Member Name** (`staff_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter your name as a staff member to associate your feedback with your identity.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date of your feedback, which helps track the timing of your assessment.
* **Book Title** (`book_title`, text, required):
	+ Enter the title of the new release.
* **Author Name** (`book_author`, text, required):
	+ Enter the author or authors of the new release.
* **Genre** (`genre`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the primary genre of the book from the options provided.
* **Publisher Name** (`publisher_name`, text, optional):
	+ If applicable, enter the name of the publishing company.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional):
	+ If applicable, enter the official publication date.
* **Cover Design Rating** (`cover_design_rating`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate the cover design of the book from the options provided.
* **Book Quality and Production** (`book_quality`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate the book's quality and production from the options provided.
* **Expected Reader Reaction** (`reader_reaction`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate your expected reader reaction from the options provided.
* **Sales Potential** (`sales_potential`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the expected sales performance from the options provided.
* **Display Recommendation** (`display_recommendation`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose how you recommend displaying the book from the options provided.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the target audience for the book from the options provided.
* **Similar Books Already Selling** (`comparison_titles`, text, optional):
	+ If applicable, enter similar books that are currently selling well.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional feedback or comments.
* **Genre Options**: 
	+ Choose from the following genres: Fiction, Mystery, Romance, Science Fiction, Fantasy, Biography, Non-fiction, Self-help, History, Children, Niche audience.
* **Cover Design Rating Options**:
	+ Choose from the following: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Book Quality and Production Options**:
	+ Choose from the following: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Expected Reader Reaction Options**:
	+ Choose from the following: Very positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, Very Negative.
* **Sales Potential Options**:
	+ Choose from the following: High, Moderate, Low, Very Low.
* **Display Recommendation Options**:
	+ Choose from the following: Front display, Window display, Staff picks, Regular shelf, Not recommended.
* **Target Audience Options**:
	+ Choose from the following: Young adults, Adults, Seniors, Students, General public, Niche audience.
