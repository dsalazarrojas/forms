# Animal Product Quality Assurance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about the quality and compliance of animal products during inspections. It helps inspectors evaluate and provide feedback on the products' overall quality, packaging integrity, freshness or condition, compliance with safety standards, and more. This form is a crucial tool for tracking and monitoring the performance of product batches or lots.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Product Batch or Lot ID** in the first field. This is the unique identifier of the product batch or lot being inspected.
2. Select the **Product Type Category** from the list of options. This will help classify the type of product being inspected.
3. Provide an **Overall Quality Rating** by selecting one of the five available options (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
4. Assess the **Packaging Integrity** by selecting one of the three available options (Intact and Secure, Slightly Damaged - Acceptable, Heavily Damaged - Rejected).
5. Rate the **Freshness or Condition Score** by entering a scale of 1 to 10.
6. Select the **Compliance with Safety Standards** by choosing from the three available options (Fully Compliant, Minor Deviations - Correctable, Major Deviations - Non-Compliant).
7. Enter any **Observations or Identified Defects** in the text field, if applicable.
8. Enter your name as the **Quality Inspector Name**.
9. Select the **Date of Inspection** from the calendar.
10. Provide **Recommendations for Corrective Action**, if necessary, in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Batch or Lot ID** (Required): Enter the unique identifier of the product batch or lot being inspected.
* **Product Type Category** (Required): Select the category of the product being inspected (Raw Material, Semi-Finished Component, Finished Consumer Product, Packaging Material).
* **Overall Quality Rating** (Required): Evaluate the product's overall quality by selecting one of the five available options (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Packaging Integrity Assessment** (Required): Assess the packaging integrity by selecting one of the three available options (Intact and Secure, Slightly Damaged - Acceptable, Heavily Damaged - Rejected).
* **Freshness or Condition Score** (Required): Rate the product's freshness or condition by entering a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Compliance with Safety Standards** (Required): Select the level of compliance with safety standards (Fully Compliant, Minor Deviations - Correctable, Major Deviations - Non-Compliant).
* **Observations or Identified Defects**: Provide details on any issues found during the inspection (Required for submission, but not for initial data submission).
* **Quality Inspector Name** (Required): Enter your name as the quality inspector.
* **Date of Inspection** (Required): Select the date of inspection from the calendar.
* **Recommendations for Corrective Action** (Optional): Provide recommendations for corrective action, if necessary.
