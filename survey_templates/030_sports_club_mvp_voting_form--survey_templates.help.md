# sports-club-mvp-voting-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect votes for the Most Valuable Player (MVP) of a sports club. The form allows users to input their vote for their favorite team and provide optional comments or feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Enter the name of the player you would like to vote for in the "player-name" field.
3. Select the player's position in the "position" field.
4. Rate the player's performance on a scale from 1-10 in the "rating" field.
5. Choose one or more teams to vote for in the "vote" field. Select "Team A", "Team B", or "Team C" to show your support.
6. If you'd like to add any additional comments, type them in the "comment" field.
7. Finally, provide any feedback about the team or player in the "feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **player-name** (`player_name_field`, text, required): Enter the name of the player you would like to vote for.
* **position** (`position_field`, text, required): Select the player's position (e.g. goalie, forward, defender, etc.).
* **rating** (`rating_field`, number, required): Rate the player's performance on a scale from 1-10.
* **vote** (`vote`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more teams to vote for. Select "Team A", "Team B", or "Team C" to show your support.
* **comment** (`comment_field`, text, optional): If you'd like to add any additional comments about the player or team, type them here.
* **feedback** (`feedback_field`, text, optional): Provide any feedback about the team or player.
