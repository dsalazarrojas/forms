# Contemporary Art Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Contemporary Art Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess and evaluate contemporary art pieces. It helps art professionals make informed decisions about acquisition, exhibition, or recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the artwork's title in the "Artwork Title" field.
2. Enter the artist's full name in the "Artist Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the artwork's creation in the "Evaluation Date" field.
4. Enter your name and title/position in the "Evaluator Name" and "Evaluator Role" fields.
5. Select the correct medium used in the artwork from the "Artwork Medium" dropdown list.
6. Enter the dimensions of the artwork in the "Artwork Dimensions" field (if applicable).
7. Rate the artwork's conceptual strength, technical execution, originality, visual impact, emotional resonance, cultural relevance, craftsmanship, presentation quality, and overall rating using the provided scale.
8. Enter your recommendation for acquisition and suitability for exhibition.
9. Describe the main strengths and weaknesses of the artwork in the respective fields.
10. Provide any additional comments or observations in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Artwork Title
**Artwork Title (required)**: Enter the name or title of the artwork.

### Artist Name
**Artist Name (required)**: Enter the full name of the artist.

### Evaluation Date
**Evaluation Date (required)**: Enter the date of the artwork's creation.

### Evaluator Name
**Evaluator Name (required)**: Enter your name.

### Evaluator Role
**Evaluator Role (required)**: Select your position or title.

### Artwork Medium
**Artwork Medium (required)**: Select the primary medium used in the artwork.

### Artwork Dimensions
**Artwork Dimensions**: Enter the height, width, and depth of the artwork (if applicable).

### Year Created
**Year Created**: Enter the year the artwork was completed.

### Conceptual Strength
**Conceptual Strength (required)**: Rate the strength of the artwork's concept or idea.

### Technical Execution
**Technical Execution (required)**: Rate the technical skill demonstrated in the artwork.

### Originality
**Originality (required)**: Rate the originality and uniqueness of the artwork.

### Visual Impact
**Visual Impact (required)**: Rate the visual impact and presence of the artwork.

### Emotional Resonance
**Emotional Resonance (required)**: Rate the emotional impact on the viewer.

### Cultural Relevance
**Cultural Relevance (required)**: Rate the relevance to contemporary issues.

### Craftsmanship
**Craftsmanship (required)**: Rate the quality of craftsmanship.

### Presentation Quality
**Presentation Quality (required)**: Rate the quality of presentation.

### Overall Rating
**Overall Rating (required)**: Overall evaluate the artwork.

### Acquisition Recommendation
**Acquisition Recommendation (required)**: Would you recommend acquisition?

### Exhibition Suitability
**Exhibition Suitability (required)**: Is this artwork suitable for exhibition?

### Strengths
**Strengths (required)**: Describe the main strengths of this artwork.

### Weaknesses
**Weaknesses**: Describe areas for improvement.

### Additional Comments
**Additional Comments**: Any other observations or feedback.
