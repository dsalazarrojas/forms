# Digital Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information about your digital awareness and literacy.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in each field according to the instructions provided in the field explanations.
- Make sure to answer all fields that are marked as required.
- You can submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Title**: A text field where you can type in your own title for this survey response. 
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
- **Age**: A number field where you can enter your age. 
  - Type: number
  - Required: No
- **Gender**: A field to select your gender. 
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: No
- **Location**: A field to select your location, such as Urban or Rural. 
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: No
- **Awareness**: A field to select your level of awareness regarding digital literacy. 
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: No
- **What is your understanding of digital literacy?**: A field where you can provide a brief note about your understanding of digital literacy. 
  - Type: note
  - Required: No
- **How many hours a week do you spend on digital devices?**: A field where you can enter the number of hours you spend on digital devices per week. 
  - Type: time
  - Required: No
- **What are your thoughts about AI?**: A field to select your thoughts about Artificial Intelligence. 
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: No
- **How often do you use digital technologies?**: A field to select how often you use digital technologies. 
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: No
- **What is your favorite digital device?**: A field to select your favorite digital device. 
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: No
- **Thank you for taking the survey**: A text field where you can type a message for the survey administrator. 
  - Type: text
  - Required: No

## Tips
- Make sure to answer honestly and accurately to provide the survey administrator with reliable data.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the survey administrator.
