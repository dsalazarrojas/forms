# City Public Safety Campaign Response Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from residents about the city's public safety campaign. Your responses will help us understand how effectively the campaign was received and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions honestly to help us improve our public safety efforts.
2. If you don't have an opinion or answer to a question, select "Not sure" or the most suitable alternative.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply if applicable.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information --** (`name`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide your name and any additional information about yourself if you'd like.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Please provide your name for follow-up purposes, if needed.
* **What neighborhood do you live in** (`neighborhood`, `text`, required): Tell us the name of your neighborhood to help us understand your specific area.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional): Provide your email address, if you'd like to receive updates on future campaign initiatives.
* **-- Campaign Awareness --** (`campaign_header`, `note`, optional): This section is for context and introduction.
* **Were you aware of our recent public safety campaign?** (`campaign_awareness`, `select_one`, required): Answer how aware you were of the campaign.
* **How did you learn about the campaign?** (`campaign_methods`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the ways you learned about the campaign.
* **How clear was the safety message?** (`message_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Answer how clear the safety message was to you.
* **-- Safety Feedback --** (`feedback_header`, `note`, optional): This section is for context and introduction.
* **What are your top safety concerns in the neighborhood?** (`safety_concerns`, `text`, optional): Describe your top safety concerns in your neighborhood.
* **How safe do you feel in your neighborhood?** (`safety_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your safety level in your neighborhood on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **What areas need the most safety improvement?** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, optional): Suggest areas that need improvement.
* **How adequate is police presence in your area?** (`police_presence`, `select_one`, optional): Answer how adequate you think the police presence is in your area.
* **-- Community Involvement --** (`community_header`, `note`, optional): This section is for context and introduction.
* **Would you be willing to participate in neighborhood watch programs?** (`willing_to_participate`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you'd be willing to participate in neighborhood watch programs.
* **What recommendations do you have for public safety initiatives?** (`recommendations`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions you have.
* **How effective was the campaign?** (`campaign_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Answer how effective you think the campaign was.
* **May we contact you about future safety campaigns?** (`future_contact`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you'd like to be contacted about future campaigns.
