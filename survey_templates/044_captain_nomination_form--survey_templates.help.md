# Captain Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Captain Nomination Form is used to identify and recognize outstanding team captains for their leadership and dedication. This form helps to evaluate a team member's potential to lead and guide their team effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and email address in the "Your Name" and "Your Email" fields, respectively.
2.  Select the team name and sport/activity from the "Team Name" and "Sport or Activity" dropdown menus.
3.  Enter the nominee's name and other relevant details in the "Nominee Name", "Nominee Email" (if known), and "Jersey Number" fields.
4.  Indicate the nominee's class year or tenure in the "Class Year or Tenure" field.
5.  Choose the nominee's position on the team from the "Position on Team" field.
6.  Select all the leadership qualities that the nominee possesses from the "What leadership qualities does this nominee possess?" field.
7.  Describe why you are nominating this person and provide specific examples of their leadership skills in the "Why do you nominate this person?" and "Specific Examples of Leadership" fields.
8.  Explain how this nominee has positively impacted the team and how their experience will benefit the team in the "How has this person positively impacted the team?" field.
9.  Indicate whether the nominee has held leadership positions before and describe them in the "Has nominee held leadership positions before?" field (if applicable).
10. Select whether teammates will respect and follow this captain from the "Will teammates respect and follow this captain?" field.
11.  Choose whether this captain will improve team unity from the "Will this captain improve team unity?" field.
12. Confirm that this nomination is sincere and well-considered in the "I confirm this nomination is sincere and well-considered" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (id: 14, <name> <type>: text, required): Enter your full name as the nominator.
* **Your Email** (id: 5, <name> <type>: email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Team Name** (id: 10, <name> <type>: text, required): Select the name of the team you are nominating.
* **Sport or Activity** (id: 11, <name> <type>: select_one, required): Choose the sport or activity of the team.
* **Nominee Name** (id: 14, <name> <type>: text, required): Enter the full name of the nominee.
* **Nominee Email** (id: 8, <name> <type>: email, required): Enter the email address of the nominee (if known).
* **Jersey Number** (id: 15, <name> <type>: text, required): Enter the jersey number of the nominee (if applicable).
* **Class Year or Tenure** (id: 16, <name> <type>: select_one, required): Choose the class year or tenure of the nominee.
* **Position on Team** (id: 17, <name> <type>: text, required): Enter the position of the nominee on the team.
* **What leadership qualities does this nominee possess?** (id: 20, <name> <type>: select_multiple, required): Select all the leadership qualities that the nominee possesses.
* **Why do you nominate this person?** (id: 21, <name> <type>: text, required): Provide a detailed explanation for nominating this person.
* **Specific Examples of Leadership** (id: 22, <name> <type>: text, required): Share specific examples of the nominee's leadership skills.
* **How has this person positively impacted the team?** (id: 23, <name> <type>: text, required): Describe the positive impact of the nominee on the team.
* **Has nominee held leadership positions before?** (id: 25, <name> <type>: select_one, required): Indicate whether the nominee has held leadership positions before.
* **Prior Leadership Positions** (id: 26, <name> <type>: text, required): Describe the prior leadership positions held by the nominee (if applicable).
* **How will experience benefit the team?** (id: 27, <name> <type>: text, required): Explain how the nominee's experience will benefit the team.
* **Will teammates respect and follow this captain?** (id: 29, <name> <type>: select_one, required): Select whether teammates will respect and follow this captain.
* **Will this captain improve team unity?** (id: 30, <name> <type>: select_one, required): Choose whether this captain will improve team unity.
* **Additional Comments** (id: 31, <name> <type>: text, required): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **I confirm this nomination is sincere and well-considered** (id: 32, <name> <type>: select_one, required): Confirm that the nomination is sincere and well-considered.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Use specific examples to support your nomination.
* Ensure that the nominee meets all the necessary requirements for the captain position.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
