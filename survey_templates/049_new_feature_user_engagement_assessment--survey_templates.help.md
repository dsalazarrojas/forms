# new_feature_user_engagement_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "new_feature_user_engagement_assessment" form is used to gather feedback on the usage and satisfaction of a new feature. This form asks users about their experiences with the feature, their opinions on its adoption, and their suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the new feature and ensure you have a clear understanding of its functionality and purpose.
2.  Select your answers based on your genuine experiences with the new feature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How did you hear about the new feature**
    (`new_feature_adoption`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select how you found out about the new feature. You can choose from the following options: 'Yes' or 'No'.

    *   Note: This field aims to understand the source of information about the new feature.
*   **What do you think about the new feature?**
    (`new_feature_satisfaction`, `note`, required: false): Please share your thoughts and opinions about the new feature. You can type your answer in the text box.
*   **How does this feature improve your workflow?**
    (`new_feature_improvement`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the ways the new feature improves your workflow. You can choose from the following options: 'Yes', 'No'.
*   **How often do you use the new feature?**
    (`new_feature_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select how often you use the new feature. You can choose from the following options: 'Yes', 'No'.
*   **What difficulties do you encounter while using the new feature?**
    (`new_feature_difficulty`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the difficulties you face while using the new feature. You can choose from the following options: 'Yes', 'No'.
*   **How much joy do you derive from using the new feature?**
    (`new_feature_satisfaction_joy`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select how much joy you derive from using the new feature. You can choose from the following options: 'Yes', 'No'.
*   **Do you have any improvement ideas?**
    (`new_feature_improvement_ideas`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select whether you have any improvement ideas for the new feature. You can choose from the following options: 'Yes', 'No'.
*   **How would you rate your satisfaction with the new feature?**
    (`new_feature_satisfaction_likert`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your level of satisfaction with the new feature. You can choose from the following options: Satisfied, Dissatisfied.
*   **How would you rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5?**
    (`new_feature_satisfaction_scale`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your level of satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Can you provide more comments?**
    (`new_feature_satisfaction_comments`, `note`, required: false): Please type any additional comments or feedback you have about the new feature.
