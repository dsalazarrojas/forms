# Fire Safety Compliance Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on fire safety compliance from facilities and users. It helps the organization identify potential issues and areas for improvement. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide specific examples or details when possible.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide fire safety information about your facility.
2. Indicate whether your fire safety equipment is functional or non-functional.
3. Answer questions about fire drill frequency.
4. Describe your current safety practices.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback you have about fire safety in your facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Safety Facility Information** (`facility_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is a short description of your facility. Please provide a brief overview of your facility, including its name, location, and number of occupants.
* **Fire Safety Equipment** (`fire_equipment`, `select_one`, required: false): Here, you can choose whether your facility's fire equipment is functional, non-functional, or has not been tested. Please select the option that best describes your equipment's status.
* **Fire Drill Frequency** (`drill_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate how frequently fire drills are conducted at your facility. You can select multiple options that apply.
* **Safety Practices** (`safety_practices`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your current safety practices, such as regular equipment checks, fire alarm tests, or evacuation procedures.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional feedback or suggestions for improving fire safety at your facility, please add them here.
