# Exponent Learning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Exponent Learning Survey form is a crucial instrument for gathering feedback about our program. Your participation is essential in helping us refine our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your genuine feedback, and do not hesitate to provide specific suggestions for improvement.
2. Ensure that you complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
3. Double-check that your email address is accurate, as we may contact you for further information.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: This is the field where you should enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or as you prefer to be known. This information will help us understand who is providing the feedback.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall experience with Exponent Learning on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest. This will help us gauge your satisfaction level.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: In this text area, please provide specific suggestions on how we can improve our services. Your input is valuable to us.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address so we can contact you if we need more details or clarification on your feedback.
* **Phone Number**: If you have a preferred phone number where we can reach you, please enter it. This is optional but can be helpful for us to contact you directly.

## Tips

* Please be as honest and specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Remember that your input is crucial to helping us improve our services.
* If you have any difficulty or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We value your time and appreciate your contribution to Exponent Learning.
