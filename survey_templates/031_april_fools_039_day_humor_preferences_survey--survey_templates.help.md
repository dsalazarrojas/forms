# April Fools Day Humor Preferences Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about individuals' humor preferences and comfort levels with pranks on April Fools' Day. The survey aims to understand respondents' demographics, their favorite humor styles, and their opinions on the role of humor in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "About You" page and provide your age group and work environment.
2. On the "Humor Preferences" page, select the humor style that appeals to you most.
3. On the "Prank Comfort" page, choose your comfort level with pranks.
4. On the "Prank Preferences" page, select the types of pranks you enjoy.
5. On the "Workplace Pranks" page, indicate whether you think pranks should be part of a workplace culture.
6. On the "Benefits of Humor" page, select the benefits of humor you think are most important.
7. Finally, on the "Improvement Ideas" page, provide your suggestions for better humor in the workplace.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What humor style appeals to you most**
	+ Select the humor style that you find most enjoyable or relatable. Options include:
		- Sarcasm: Witty, often irreverent remarks.
		- Puns: Wordplay with multiple meanings.
		- Dark Humor: Humor that involves morbid or uncomfortable topics.
		- Physical Comedy: Humor that involves bodily movements or gestures.
		- Wordplay: Humor that relies on word meanings or double meanings.
		- Absurdist: Humor that is absurd or illogical.
* **Comfort Level with Pranks**
	+ Choose your level of comfort with pranks. Options include:
		- Love Them: You enjoy pranks and feel they bring laughter and joy.
		- Enjoy Them: You have a neutral or positive attitude towards pranks.
		- Neutral: You don't have strong feelings about pranks.
		- Prefer No Pranks: You don't enjoy pranks or find them intrusive.
		- Strongly Dislike: You strongly dislike pranks and find them annoying.
* **Type of Pranks You Enjoy**
	+ Select all the types of pranks you enjoy. Options include:
		- Harmless Jokes: Lighthearted, non-mean-spirited pranks.
		- Fake News Stories: Humorous, fake news-style pranks.
		- Office Pranks: Pranks that take place in a workplace setting.
		- Technology Pranks: Pranks that involve technology or gadgets.
		- Surprise Events: Unexpected, often playful pranks.
* **Should Pranks be Part of a Workplace Culture**
	+ Decide whether you think pranks should be part of a workplace culture. Options include:
		- Definitely Yes: You think pranks can be a positive aspect of workplace culture.
		- Probably Yes: You lean towards pranks being part of a workplace culture, but with reservations.
		- Neutral: You don't have an opinion on this matter.
		- Probably No: You don't think pranks should be part of a workplace culture.
		- Definitely No: You strongly oppose pranks in a workplace culture.
* **Benefits of Humor**
	+ Select all the benefits of humor you think are important. Options include:
		- Team Bonding: Humor can create connections and relationships among coworkers.
		- Stress Relief: Humor can help reduce workplace stress and anxiety.
		- Morale Boost: Humor can improve overall well-being and job satisfaction.
		- Creativity: Humor can foster creativity and innovative thinking.
		- Better Communication: Humor can improve communication and relationships among coworkers.
* **Ideas for Better Humor**
	+ Provide your suggestions for improving humor in the workplace.
