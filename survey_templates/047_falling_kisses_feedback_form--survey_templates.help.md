# Falling Kisses Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Falling Kisses Feedback Form is designed to gather information from customers who have received the falling kisses service. Your feedback will help us improve and refine our service to provide the best experience for you and our future customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service
2. Rate the quality of the service
3. Rate the value for money
4. Rate the presentation of the service
5. Rate the service experience
6. Rate the food and drinks quality
7. Share any comments about the service
8. Share any suggestions for improvement
9. Enter your email address
10. Enter your phone number
11. Provide any additional comments or questions

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating (1-5)** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required): This section asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the service. Please rate from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Satisfaction Rating (1-5)** (`rating_satisfaction`, `number`, required): This section asks you to rate how satisfied you were with the service. Please rate from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Quality Rating (1-5)** (`rating_quality`, `number`, required): This section asks you to rate the quality of the service. Please rate from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Value for Money Rating (1-5)** (`rating_value`, `number`, required): This section asks you to rate how much value you feel you got for the money you paid. Please rate from 1 (not worth it) to 5 (worth it).
* **Presentation Rating (1-5)** (`rating_presentation`, `number`, required): This section asks you to rate the presentation of the service. Please rate from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Service Experience Rating (1-5)** (`rating_service`, `number`, required): This section asks you to rate your overall experience with the service. Please rate from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Food and Drinks Quality Rating (1-5)** (`rating_food_and_drinks`, `number`, required): This section asks you to rate the quality of the food and drinks provided. Please rate from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to share any comments or thoughts about the service. Please feel free to share as much or as little as you like.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the service. We value your input and would love to hear from you.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): This section asks you to enter your email address so we can contact you with any updates or responses to your feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): This section asks you to enter your phone number so we can contact you with any updates or responses to your feedback.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide any additional comments or questions you may have. If you have anything else you'd like to share, please do so here.
