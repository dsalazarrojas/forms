# Beverage Packaging Design Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to collect opinions on beverage packaging design from respondents. Your feedback will help designers and manufacturers create more appealing and effective packaging.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read the instructions and review the questions carefully** before proceeding.
2. **Fill out the form with your honest and informed opinions**.
3. **Select one answer option for each question**.
4. **Answer all questions, even if you are unsure of some**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Respondent Name** (id: 1, label: Your Name, type: text, required: true)
Enter your name for follow-up surveys or internal use.

*   This is a required field to identify you and any follow-up surveys or internal use.
*   Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. **Email Address** (id: 2, label: Email Address, type: email, required: false)
For follow-up surveys or internal use, enter your email address.

*   This is an optional field. If you do not want to be contacted for follow-up surveys or internal use, you can leave this blank.
*   Please enter your email address as it appears on your official email.

### 3. **Age Range** (id: 3, label: Age Range, type: select_one, required: true)
Select your age range from the options provided.

*   This will help us understand your demographic.
*   Select the age group that best describes you.

### 4. **Gender** (id: 4, label: Gender, type: select_one, required: false)

*   This will help us understand your demographic.
*   Select the gender that best describes you.

### 6. **Design Version Reviewed** (id: 6, label: Design Version Reviewed, type: select_one, required: true)

### 7. **Overall Visual Appeal** (id: 7, label: Overall Visual Appeal, type: select_one, required: true)
Rate the overall visual appeal of the packaging.

*   Select your honest opinion.
*   Rate the overall design as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### 8. **Color Scheme Rating** (id: 8, label: Color Scheme Rating, type: select_one, required: true)

*   Select your honest opinion.
*   Rate the color scheme as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### 9. **Typography Rating** (id: 9, label: Typography Rating, type: select_one, required: true)

### 10. **Logo Placement Rating** (id: 10, label: Logo Placement Rating, type: select_one, required: true)

### 11. **Brand Recognition** (id: 11, label: Brand Recognition, type: select_one, required: true)

### 12. **Shelf Appeal** (id: 12, label: Shelf Appeal, type: select_one, required: true)

### 13. **Purchase Influence** (id: 13, label: Purchase Influence, type: select_one, required: true)

### 17. **Least Favorite Design Element** (id: 17, label: Least Favorite Design Element, type: text, required: false)

### 18. **Suggestions for Improvement** (id: 18, label: Suggestions for Improvement, type: text, required: false)
