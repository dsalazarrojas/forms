# Internship Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from interns who have completed an internship program. The information gathered will help us understand the strengths and weaknesses of the program, enabling us to improve future internships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Program Title** in the first field. This is the name of the internship program you participated in.
2. Enter the **Program Duration** in the second field. Please provide the length of time you spent in the internship program.
3. Rate the **Program Rating** in the third field. This is a subjective measure of your experience with the program.
4. Provide your **Program Satisfaction** rating in the fourth field. This helps us understand how satisfied you were with the program.
5. Rate your **Manager** in the fifth field. This helps us understand how effective your manager was during the program.
6. Rate your **Colleague** in the sixth field. This helps us understand how well you worked with your colleagues during the program.
7. Rate your **Supervisor** in the seventh field. This helps us understand how effective your supervisor was during the program.
8. Provide your **Overall Satisfaction** rating in the eighth field. This helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Title (Text)**: The name of the internship program.
* **Program Duration (Number)**: The length of time you spent in the internship program.
* **Program Rating (Number)**: A subjective measure of your experience with the program.
* **Program Satisfaction (Number)**: A measure of your satisfaction with the program.
* **Manager (Number)**: A rating of how effective your manager was during the program.
* **Colleague (Number)**: A rating of how well you worked with your colleagues during the program.
* **Supervisor (Number)**: A rating of how effective your supervisor was during the program.
* **Overall Satisfaction (Number)**: A measure of your overall satisfaction with the program.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve the program.
- If you have completed multiple internships, please fill out this form for each program separately.
- If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section (not included in this form, but you may want to provide this option for users).
