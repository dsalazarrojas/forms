# Customer Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Satisfaction Survey Form is designed to gauge your satisfaction with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a smooth completion experience:

1. Provide your Customer Name to help us understand who you are.
2. Choose a rating (1-5) to represent your overall satisfaction level.
3. Enter your Email address to ensure we can get in touch with you.
4. Answer question 1 by choosing one of the three provided options: Very Good, Somewhat Good, or Not Good.
5. Answer question 2 by selecting multiple options that best describe your experience, choosing from Very Good, Somewhat Good, or Not Good.
6. Select a Date Taken to record the exact date of your interaction.
7. Choose a Time Taken to document the exact time of your interaction.
8. Provide Additional Comments if you have any feedback or suggestions for improvement.
9. Answer question 3 by choosing multiple options that best describe your experience, choosing from Very Good, Somewhat Good, or Not Good.
10. Answer question 4 by selecting one of the three provided options: Very Good, Somewhat Good, or Not Good.

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Your full name, used to identify you.
* **Rating 1-5** (`rating`, `number`, required): Your level of satisfaction, with 1 being Not Good and 5 being Very Good.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, `email`, required): A valid email address, used for follow-up and correspondence.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Share any extra information or feedback to help us improve.
* **Date Taken** (`date`, `date`, required): Record the exact date when you took this survey.
* **Time Taken** (`time`, `time`, required): Record the exact time when you took this survey.
* **Question 1** (`question_one`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you feel about our service: Very Good, Somewhat Good, or Not Good.
* **Question 2** (`question2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable options to describe your experience with our service: Very Good, Somewhat Good, or Not Good.
* **Question 3** (`question_three`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all applicable options to describe your experience with our service: Very Good, Somewhat Good, or Not Good.
* **Question 4** (`question_four`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you feel about our service: Very Good, Somewhat Good, or Not Good.
