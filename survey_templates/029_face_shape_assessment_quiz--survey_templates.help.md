# Face Shape Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Face Shape Assessment Quiz is a form designed to collect information about a user's face shape and other facial characteristics. This information can be used for research, cosmetic, or medical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Face Shape Assessment Quiz, follow these steps:

* Review each page carefully and select the option that best describes your face shape or characteristics.
* Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Use the dropdown menus to select your face shape and other attributes from the given options.
* For numeric fields, provide a numerical value for your measurements.
* For free-text fields, describe your facial features briefly and concisely.
