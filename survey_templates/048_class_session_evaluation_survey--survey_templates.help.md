# Class Session Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on the quality and effectiveness of class sessions. Your responses will be used to identify areas for improvement and to provide instructors with constructive feedback for better teaching practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form in the order of the pages provided.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3.  Answer the questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Respondent Information**: 
    *   `Class Name`: Please enter the name of the class you are evaluating.
    *   `Instructor Name`: Enter the name of the instructor who led the class session.
    *   `Session Date`: Enter the date of the class session you are evaluating.
    *   **Optional**: `Student Name`: This is optional, but if you are a student, please enter your name. Otherwise, you can remain anonymous.
*   **Session Content and Learning**
    *   `The session content was clear and well-presented - scale 1-5`: Please rate how clear and well-presented the session content was.
    *   `The content was relevant to the course objectives - scale 1-5`: Please rate how relevant the session content was to the course objectives.
    *   `The session helped me learn the material - scale 1-5`: Please rate how helpful the session was in learning the material.
    *   `The pace of the session was appropriate - scale 1-5`: Please rate the speed of the session. 
*   **Instructor and Teaching Methods**
    *   `The instructor demonstrated subject expertise - scale 1-5`: Please rate the instructor's knowledge of the subject.
    *   `The instructor engaged students effectively - scale 1-5`: Please rate how effectively the instructor engaged with students.
    *   `The teaching methods were effective - scale 1-5`: Please rate the effectiveness of the teaching methods.
    *   `The instructor communicated clearly - scale 1-5`: Please rate how clearly the instructor communicated.
*   **Activities and Materials**
    *   `In-class activities were helpful - scale 1-5`: Please rate how helpful in-class activities were.
    *   `Quality of course materials provided - scale 1-5`: Please rate the quality of materials provided.
    *   `Resources were accessible and available - scale 1-5`: Please rate how easily accessible and available resources were.
*   **Feedback and Interaction**
    *   `Feedback from instructor was constructive - scale 1-5`: Please rate how constructive feedback was.
    *   `Opportunity for questions and discussion - scale 1-5`: Please rate how much opportunity there was for questions and discussion.
*   **Additional Comments**
    *   `What were the strongest aspects of this session`: Please provide any specific things you liked about the session.
    *   `What could be improved`: Please provide any specific things that could be improved.
    *   `Overall, I was satisfied with this session - scale 1-5`: Please rate your overall satisfaction with the session.
    *   `Additional Comments or Suggestions`: Please provide any additional comments or suggestions about the session.

Note: Please answer all required questions and provide as much detail as possible for optional questions.
