**Higher Education Policy Perception Survey**
==========================

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather opinions on the perception of higher education policies from various stakeholders in the higher education sector. The purpose of this survey is to understand the views and experiences of individuals working in different types of institutions and positions within the sector.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of university you are affiliated with.
2. Choose the department within the university where you are working.
3. If you have a job title, enter it in the corresponding field.
4. Indicate your job level within the university.
5. Select your job type within the university.
6. If your job status is active or inactive, choose the correct option.
7. If your job location is a specific institution, enter its name. If not, simply enter the location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### University Name (<name>: University Name
• **University Name (university_name)**: Please enter the full name of the university you are associated with.
### What type of university (<name>): What type of university?
• **What type of university (university_type)**: Select the type of university that aligns with your affiliation. Options:
  • Community College
  • Private University
  • Public University
  • Technical Institute
  • University System
### Department (<name>): Department
• **Department (department)**: Select all departments you are associated with. Options:
  • Faculty of Agriculture
  • Faculty of Arts and Humanities
  • Faculty of Engineering
  • Faculty of Health Sciences
  • Faculty of Mathematics and Physical Sciences
### Job Title (<name>): Job Title
• **Job Title (job_title)**: Enter your job title if applicable.
### Job Level (<name>): Job Level
• **Job Level (job_level)**: Choose your job level within the university. Options:
  • Entry-level
  • Mid-level
  • Senior-level
  • Executive-level
### Job Type (<name>): Job Type
• **Job Type (job_type)**: Select the job type that best describes your position. Options:
  • Academic Staff
  • Administrative
  • Research
  • Technician
### Job Status (<name>): Job Status
• **Job Status (job_status)**: Indicate your current job status. Options:
  • Active
  • Inactive
  • On Leave
### Job Location (<name>): Job Location
• **Job Location (job_location)**: Enter the location of your job. If not a specific institution, simply enter the location.
