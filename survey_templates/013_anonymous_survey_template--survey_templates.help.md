# Anonymous Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Anonymous Survey Template" is designed to collect feedback from employees on their experiences and perceptions of their company. This form will help the organization understand employee opinions on various aspects of the work environment, which will aid in making informed decisions to improve the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the questions honestly, as this feedback is anonymous.
2. Review each question carefully and provide detailed responses where possible.
3. If you need help or have questions, please ask your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Current Role** (`company_role`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your current role or position in the company. This will help us understand your context within the organization.
* **How long have you been with the company?** (`tenure_period`, `select_one`, required): Please select the length of time you have been with the company, ranging from Less than 1 year to 10+ years.
* **Satisfaction with Management** (`management_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction with management on a scale of 1 to 5, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent.
* **Work-Life Balance Rating** (`work_life_balance`, `select_one`, required): Choose your rating for work-life balance, with options ranging from Poor to Excellent.
* **Compensation and Benefits Satisfaction** (`compensation_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with compensation and benefits, from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Professional Growth Opportunities** (`growth_opportunities`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of professional growth opportunities you have within the company, with options ranging from Limited to Plentiful.
* **Company Culture Rating** (`company_culture`, `number`, required): Rate the company culture on a scale of 1 to 5, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent.
* **Are tools and resources adequate for your job?** (`resources_adequacy`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether the tools and resources you need for your job are adequate or not.
* **One thing you would change about the company** (`change_suggestion`, `text`, optional): Share one thing you would change about the company to improve the work environment.
* **One thing we should definitely keep doing** (`keep_suggestion`, `text`, optional): Suggest one thing that the company is doing well and should continue to do.
* **How would you describe team morale?** (`overall_morale`, `text`, optional): Describe the current team morale, providing any details or comments you have.
