# Survey On Relationships And Community - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of survey templates in building relationships and community engagement, and to understand how they have improved user's work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all that apply to answer the questions.
2. Enter your honest feedback in the text field.
3. Choose a numerical value to rate your level of agreement.
4. Select one option to answer the question.
5. Provide more details in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Relationships** (`survey_on_relationships_and_community`, select_multiple, required): This question asks about your experiences with relationships and whether you have had positive relationships with others.
	+ Select all that apply if you have had positive relationships.
* **Community** (`community`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide more details about your community engagement.
* **Relationships And Community** (`relationships_and_community`, number, optional): This field is intended to rate your level of agreement with the statement 'I have a good relationship with my community'.
* **Organization** (`organization`, select_one, optional): This question asks if you have a good relationship with your organization.
* **Gathering** (`survey_gathering`, time, optional): This field is intended to rate your level of time spent on survey gathering.
* **Engagement** (`community_engagement`, note, optional): This field is for you to provide more details about your community engagement.
* **Improving** (`survey_improving`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about the extent to which surveys have improved your work.
