# Audience Topic Suggestion Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audience Topic Suggestion Survey Form is a tool designed to help content creators understand what topics the audience is interested in. It collects suggestions on topics that would be engaging and valuable to the audience, helping to shape the future content of a platform or publication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your name, if you'd like to be included in the survey results.
2. Choose your current role or profession to help us understand your demographic.
3. Select the general categories that interest you the most.
4. Suggest a specific topic or theme that you'd like to learn about.
5. Explain why this topic is important to you.
6. Choose how you prefer to consume this content.
7. Decide on the desired depth of the topic.
8. Let us know if you'd be interested in participating as a guest speaker or panelist on this topic.
9. Provide your email address if you'd like to be notified when this topic is covered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, if you'd like to be included in the survey results.
* **Your Current Role or Profession** (`respondent_role`, `text`, optional): This field helps us understand your demographic.
* **Which general categories interest you most?** (`general_categories`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the categories that interest you, from Technology and AI, Business and Leadership, Creative Arts and Design, Personal Development, Health and Wellness, and Sustainability and Environment.
* **Suggest a specific topic or theme** (`specific_topic_suggestion`, `text`, required): Be as detailed as you like when suggesting a specific topic or theme.
* **Why is this topic important to you?** (`importance_reason`, `text`, optional): Explain why this topic is important to you, what problems it would help you solve.
* **How do you prefer to consume this content?** (`preferred_format`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you prefer to consume this content, from Live Webinar, In-Person Workshop, Recorded Video Series, Podcast Episode, or Written Article or Whitepaper.
* **Desired Depth of Topic** (`learning_depth`, `select_one`, required): Decide on the desired depth of the topic, from Beginner - Overview, Intermediate - Deep Dive, or Advanced - Technical/Expert level.
* **Would you be interested in being a guest speaker or panelist on this topic?** (`participant_interest`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you'd be interested in participating as a guest speaker or panelist on this topic.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to be notified when this topic is covered, provide your email address.
