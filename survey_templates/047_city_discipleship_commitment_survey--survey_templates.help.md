# City Discipleship Commitment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to help you assess your commitment to discipleship and spiritual growth. It will help you evaluate your current level of engagement, spiritual practices, and areas of growth. The survey is composed of 28 questions, divided into several sections: Personal Information, Faith Journey, Current Engagement, Spiritual Practices, Spiritual Growth, Discipleship Commitment, Mentoring and Support, and Discipleship Goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the most accurate answer that reflects your current situation.
2. For select-one questions, choose the option that best describes your current level of commitment or practice.
3. For select multiple questions, select all the options that apply to you.
4. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly to get an accurate assessment of your discipleship commitment.
5. When you finish, review your answers and make any necessary changes before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that you use for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further information or support.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, optional): Choose your age group:
	+ 18-25
	+ 26-35
	+ 36-45
	+ 46-55
	+ 56-65
	+ 65+
* **Faith Journey** (`faith_background`, select_one, required): Choose how you became a Christian:
	+ Lifelong Christian
	+ Recent conversion
	+ Returning to faith
	+ Exploring faith
	+ Uncertain
* **Years of Faith Commitment** (`years_faith_commitment`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have committed to your faith.
* **Discipleship Experience Level** (`discipleship_experience`, select_one, required): Choose your level of discipleship experience:
	+ New to discipleship
	+ Some experience
	+ Growing in discipleship
	+ Experienced disciple
	+ Disciple-maker
* **Church Attendance Frequency** (`church_attendance`, select_one, required): Choose how often you attend church:
	+ Every week
	+ 2-3 times per month
	+ Monthly
	+ A few times per year
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Bible Study or Small Group Participation** (`group_participation`, select_one, required): Choose how often you participate in a Bible study or small group:
	+ Regularly attend
	+ Attend sometimes
	+ Have attended
	+ Never attended
	+ Would be interested
* **Ministry or Service Involvement** (`ministry_involvement`, select_one, required): Choose your level of involvement in ministry or service:
	+ Active volunteer
	+ Regular volunteer
	+ Occasional volunteer
	+ Not currently involved
	+ Interested in getting involved
* **Daily Prayer Practice** (`prayer_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you practice daily prayer:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times per week
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Occasionally
	+ Never
* **Scripture Study or Bible Reading** (`scripture_study`, select_one, required): Choose how often you read or study Scripture:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times per week
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Occasionally
	+ Never
* **Spiritual Disciplines Practiced (Select all that apply)** (`spiritual_disciplines`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the spiritual disciplines you practice:
	+ Bible study
	+ Prayer
	+ Fasting
	+ Worship
	+ Journaling
	+ Meditation
	+ Confession
	+ Service
* **Assessment of Your Spiritual Growth** (`spiritual_growth_assessment`, select_one, required): Choose how you evaluate your spiritual growth:
	+ Growing significantly
	+ Growing steadily
	+ Growing slowly
	+ Stagnant
	+ Declining
* **Areas of Spiritual Growth (Select all that apply)** (`growth_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the areas where you are experiencing spiritual growth:
	+ Faith and trust
	+ Love and compassion
	+ Holiness and righteousness
	+ Knowledge of Scripture
	+ Prayer life
	+ Witnessing and evangelism
	+ Service and justice
	+ Community and relationships
* **Challenges to Spiritual Growth** (`growth_challenges`, text, optional): Enter any challenges you are facing in your spiritual growth.
* **Level of Commitment to Discipleship** (`commitment_level`, select_one, required): Choose your level of commitment to discipleship:
	+ Fully committed
	+ Mostly committed
	+ Somewhat committed
	+ Minimally committed
	+ Not committed
* **Obstacles to Deeper Commitment** (`commitment_obstacles`, text, optional): Enter any obstacles you are facing that prevent you from being more committed to discipleship.
* **Have a Spiritual Mentor or Discipler** (`spiritual_mentor`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have a spiritual mentor or discipler:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Would like one
* **Interest in Being a Disciple-Maker** (`mentoring_interest`, select_one, optional): Choose how interested you are in being a discipler:
	+ Very interested
	+ Interested
	+ Maybe
	+ Not interested
* **Spiritual Goals for Next Year** (`spiritual_goals`, text, optional): Enter your spiritual goals for the next year.

## Tips
