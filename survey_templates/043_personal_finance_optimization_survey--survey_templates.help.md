# Personal Finance Optimization Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help gather information about your personal finance goals and preferences. By completing this form, you will be providing valuable data that will help us understand your financial needs and optimize your financial situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: `Money Goal`, `Financial Goal`, `Savings Rate`, `Financial Institution`, `Money Source`, and `Money Use`.
2. Select the corresponding options for `Income Range`, `Financial Institution`, `Money Source`, `Money Use`, and `Financial Goal`.
3. Fill in the text field for `Survey Title`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for this survey.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Money Goal** (`money_goal`, number, optional): Enter your personal financial goal.
* **Income Range** (`income_range`, select_one, required): Select the range of your annual income.
	+ Less than $25,000
	+ $25,000 - $50,000
	+ $50,000 - $75,000
	+ $75,000 - $100,000
	+ More than $100,000
* **Savings Rate** (`savings_rate`, number, optional): Enter your savings rate (e.g., 0-10, 10-20, etc.).
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, select_multiple, required): Select the financial institution(s) you use.
	+ Bank
	+ Credit Union
	+ Other
* **Money Source** (`money_source`, select_one, required): Select where your money comes from.
	+ Savings
	+ Investments
	+ Other
* **Money Use** (`money_use`, select_one, required): Select how you use your money.
	+ Housing
	+ Transportation
	+ Food
	+ Other
* **Financial Goal** (`financial_goal`, select_one, required): Select your financial goal.
	+ Short term (less than 6 months)
	+ Medium term (6-12 months)
	+ Long term (more than 12 months)
* **Money Awareness** (`money_awareness`, select_one, required): Select your level of money awareness.
	+ Very low
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **Follow-Up** (`follow_up`, time, optional): Enter the follow-up duration.
* **Follow-Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the follow-up date.
* **Coach Id** (`coach_id`, number, optional): Enter your coach's ID.
* **Advisor Id** (`advisor_id`, number, optional): Enter your advisor's ID.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
- If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
- Please note that this form is for personal finance optimization, not for any other use.
