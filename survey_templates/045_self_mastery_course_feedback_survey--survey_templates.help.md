# self_mastery_course_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self_mastery_course_feedback_survey form is designed to collect feedback from users about a self-mastery course. This feedback will be used to improve future versions of the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question according to your honest opinion.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. If a field is not required, you may leave it blank or choose the "N/A" option if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, optional)
	+ This is a text area where you can write any additional comments or thoughts you have about the course.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`2`, `number`, optional)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale of 0-100.
* **Course Materials** (`3`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with the course materials:
		- Very Satisfied: The materials were excellent and very useful.
		- Somewhat Satisfied: The materials were somewhat satisfactory.
		- Somewhat Dissatisfied: The materials were somewhat dissatisfying.
		- Very Dissatisfied: The materials were poor.
* **Course Instructors** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with the course instructors:
		- Excellent: The instructors were excellent.
		- Good: The instructors were good.
		- Fair: The instructors were fair.
		- Poor: The instructors were poor.
* **Course Content** (`5`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Choose how relevant and well-organized you find the course content:
		- Relevant and Well-Organized: The content was highly relevant and well-organized.
		- Relevant but not Well-Organized: The content was somewhat relevant but not well-organized.
		- Somewhat Relevant: The content was somewhat relevant.
		- Somewhat Irrelevant: The content was somewhat irrelevant.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`6`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the course:
		- Very Satisfied: You are very satisfied with the course.
		- Somewhat Satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with the course.
		- Neutral: You are neutral about the course.
		- Somewhat Dissatisfied: You are somewhat dissatisfied with the course.
		- Very Dissatisfied: You are very dissatisfied with the course.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`7`, `note`, optional)
	+ This is a text area where you can provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the course.
* **Contact Information** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ How would you like to be contacted for future course updates or improvements:
		- Phone: We can contact you via phone.
		- Email: We can contact you via email.
		- Social Media: We can contact you via social media.
