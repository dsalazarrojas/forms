# Survey Participation Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survey Participation Permission Form is used to collect consent from participants for survey participation, ensuring you have the necessary permissions to include them in your survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and provide honest answers.
2. Answer questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Participation Permission** (`survey_participation_permission_form_1`, Note, required: false): This note provides a space for you to briefly describe the survey and its purpose. Be sure to include the survey ID and any other relevant details.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, Text, required: false): Enter the participant's details, such as their name, contact information, or any other identifying information.
* **Survey Participant Agreement** (`survey_participant_agreement`, Select Multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the participant has given their consent to participate in the survey, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, Date, required: false): Enter the date on which the participant gave their consent to participate in the survey.

## Tips
* Make sure to clearly explain the survey's purpose and any potential risks or benefits associated with participation.
* Ensure that the participant understands that their responses will be kept confidential and only used for the intended purpose.
* Review this form carefully before submitting it to the participant, and make sure they have a clear understanding of what they are agreeing to.
