# Guest Satisfaction Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Satisfaction Quiz is a form designed to collect feedback from guests after their visit. This form aims to understand their satisfaction with various aspects of their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Please select one answer for each question.
3. Do not worry about grammar or spelling; just provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Satisfaction Quiz** (`page_1`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which we will not be explaining here as it's a title and not a field.
* **Guest Satisfaction** (`page_2`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your experience. Please select one of the options: Option 1, Very dissatisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat satisfied, Very satisfied.
* **Feedback** (`page_3`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question allows you to select all that apply. Please choose from the options: Dissatisfied with the food, Not happy with the staff, Not satisfied with the ambiance, Other.
* **Date** (`page_4`, date, required/optional): This question asks for the date of your visit. Please enter the date in a standard format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Time** (`page_5`, time, required/optional): This question asks for the time of your visit. Please enter the time in a 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Note** (`page_6`, note, required/optional): This is an open-ended field for any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Email** (`page_7`, email, required/optional): This question asks for your email address, which will help us contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`page_8`, text, required/optional): This question asks for your phone number, which will help us contact you if needed.
* **Note 2** (`page_9`, note, required/optional): This is another open-ended field for any additional comments or suggestions.
