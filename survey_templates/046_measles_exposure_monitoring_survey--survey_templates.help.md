# Measles Exposure Monitoring Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Measles Exposure Monitoring Survey is a critical tool used to collect information about potential measles exposure cases. This form helps to identify and track individuals who may have been exposed to measles, allowing for prompt and effective action to prevent the spread of the disease.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Measles Exposure Monitoring Survey, follow these steps:

1. Select the survey date from the calendar picker.
2. Provide a brief description of the case (e.g., rash, fever, etc.).
3. Choose the type of case (e.g., Measles, Rash, Fever, etc.).
4. Enter the location where the exposure occurred.
5. Select the date and time of exposure.
6. Describe the symptoms exhibited by the individual.
7. Report the vaccination status of the individual.
8. Provide travel history, including travel dates and frequency.
9. Specify the travel type (e.g., domestic, international).
10. Assess the exposure risk level.
11. Describe the exposure source.
12. Enter a detailed description of the exposure.
13. Specify the follow-up action required.
14. Enter the date for the follow-up action.
15. Check whether the case is closed.
