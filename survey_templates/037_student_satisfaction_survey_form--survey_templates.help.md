# Student Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experiences and satisfaction with various aspects of the school or educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields below with your honest and sincere feedback. You can rate the options provided using the numbers 1, 2, or 3, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 3 is "very satisfied". You can also add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Satisfaction Survey Form** (`form_title`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, where you can enter a brief description of your overall feedback.
* **How would you rate the school's mission and vision?** (`mission_vision`, select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the school's mission and vision using the numbers 1, 2, or 3, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 3 is "very satisfied".
* **What do you think about the quality of the coursework?** (`coursework`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the coursework quality using the numbers 1, 2, or 3, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 3 is "very satisfied". Select all that apply.
* **How would you rate the facilities?** (`facilities`, select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the facilities using the numbers 1, 2, or 3, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 3 is "very satisfied".
* **What do you think about the quality of education?** (`education_quality`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the education quality using the numbers 1, 2, or 3, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 3 is "very satisfied". Select all that apply.
* **How would you rate the student support services?** (`student_support_services`, select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the student support services using the numbers 1, 2, or 3, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 3 is "very satisfied".
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about the school or educational institution.

Tips:
* Please answer the questions honestly and sincerely.
* You can select multiple options for the "What do you think about the quality of the coursework?" and "What do you think about the quality of education?" questions.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
