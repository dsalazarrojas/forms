The Office Space Feedback Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about your office experience, allowing us to understand your thoughts on the working environment. # Office Space Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Space Feedback Questionnaire is a form to collect your opinion on the office space. Your feedback is essential in helping us create a more comfortable and productive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. You can skip any question that does not apply to you.
2. Select the options that best match your experience.
3. If you have any specific comments or suggestions, please feel free to share them in the space provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Office Location** (`office_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the correct location of your office:
	+ 1: First Floor
	+ 2: Second Floor
	+ 3: Third Floor
	+ 4: Other (please specify)
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your department name (e.g., Sales, Marketing, IT).
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel file.
* **Work Stations** (`work_stations`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the work stations you frequently visit:
	+ 1: Cubicles
	+ 2: Desks
	+ 3: Collaboration Rooms
	+ 4: Other (please specify)
* **Office Layout** (`office_layout`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the layout that best describes your workspace:
	+ 1: Closed
	+ 2: Open
	+ 3: Semi-Open
	+ 4: Other (please specify)
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1-5, rate the temperature of your workspace:
	+ 1: Very cold
	+ 2: Cold
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Warm
	+ 5: Very warm
* **Lighting** (`lighting`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1-5, rate the lighting of your workspace:
	+ 1: Very dim
	+ 2: Dim
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Bright
	+ 5: Very bright
* **Noise Levels** (`noise_levels`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1-5, rate the noise levels of your workspace:
	+ 1: Very quiet
	+ 2: Quiet
	+ 3: Moderate
	+ 4: Loud
	+ 5: Very loud
* **Ergonomics** (`ergonomics`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1-5, rate the ergonomics of your workspace:
	+ 1: Very uncomfortable
	+ 2: Somewhat uncomfortable
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat comfortable
	+ 5: Very comfortable

Please note that some fields are optional and do not affect the overall form submission. However, your feedback is valuable to us, and every comment counts.
