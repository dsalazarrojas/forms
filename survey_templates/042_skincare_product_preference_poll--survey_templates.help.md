# Skincare Product Preference Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Product Preference Poll is a form designed to collect your opinions and preferences on skincare products. Your input will help us create better products that meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section of the form with your personal preferences and information.
2. Select only one answer for questions with "select_one" type fields.
3. Choose multiple answers for questions with "select_multiple" type fields.
4. Enter your email address in the format "example@domain.com".
5. Fill out your phone number in the format "123-456-7890".
6. Enter your age as a number.
7. Enter your income as a number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select_one, required=false): Please select the type of skin you have: Normal, Oily, or Dry.
* **Routine** (`routine`, select_multiple, required=false): Choose how often you perform your skincare routine: AM, PM, Both, or Never.
* **Product Decision** (`product_decision`, select_one, required=false): What factor is most important when making a skincare product purchase: Price, Brand, Ingredients, or Convenience.
* **Purchase** (`purchase`, select_one, required=false): How do you usually buy your skincare products: In-store, Online, Both, or Never.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required=false): Enter your email address (e.g., example@domain.com).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required=false): Enter your phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required=false): Enter your age.
* **Income** (`income`, number, required=false): Enter your income.
* **Skincare Expertise** (`skincare_expertise`, select_multiple, required=false): Choose your level of skincare expertise: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.

Note: This form does not require all fields to be completed. However, the more information you provide, the more accurate our results will be. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
