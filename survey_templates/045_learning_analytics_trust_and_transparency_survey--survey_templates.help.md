# Learning Analytics Trust And Transparency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to assess the level of trust and transparency that organizations have in the data they collect for learning analytics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the options that best match your experience and knowledge.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): Provide a brief title for this survey.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Survey Templates
	+ Form Examples
	+ Other
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Describe the purpose of this survey.
* **Data Use** (`data_use`, select_one, optional):
	+ Collecting anonymous feedback
	+ Collecting user data
	+ Analyzing educational data
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, select_one, optional):
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Yearly
* **Trust Level** (`trust_level`, select_one, optional):
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Transparency Level** (`transparency_level`, select_one, optional):
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Submission Frequency** (`submission_frequency`, select_one, optional):
	+ Frequently
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Never

## Tips
- Please answer the questions as accurately as possible.
- If you are unsure about any of the options, select the one that best fits your experience and knowledge.
- You can submit the form at any time, but please do not submit it multiple times.
