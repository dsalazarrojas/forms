# Residential Energy Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Residential Energy Efficiency Survey is a tool used to gather information about the energy efficiency and usage habits of your home. This data will be used for research purposes to understand energy consumption patterns and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your home's square footage.
3. Answer questions about the number of people living in your home and your energy usage habits.
4. Describe your home's characteristics, such as insulation type, window quality, and home age.
5. Indicate whether you have undergone an energy audit in your home.
6. Add any additional comments or information about your home and energy usage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your survey, if desired.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Your name as it should be displayed on the survey form.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Your email address where you would like to be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number where you would like to be contacted.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, optional): The address of your home.
* **Home Square Footage** (`home_square_footage`, number, optional): The square footage of your home.
* **Number of People** (`number_of_people`, number, optional): The number of people living in your home.
* **Heating Oil** (`heating_oil`, number, optional): The type and amount of heating oil used in your home.
* **Heating Gas** (`heating_gas`, number, optional): The type and amount of heating gas used in your home.
* **Heating Electric** (`heating_electric`, number, optional): The type and amount of heating electric used in your home.
* **Cooling Air** (`cooling_air`, number, optional): The type and amount of cooling air used in your home.
* **Cooling Electric** (`cooling_electric`, number, optional): The type and amount of cooling electric used in your home.
* **Insulation Type** (`insulation_type`, select one, optional): Choose whether your home has insulation.
* **Insulation Quality** (`insulation_quality`, select multiple, optional): Select the quality of your home's insulation.
* **Windows** (`windows`, text, optional): Describe the type and quality of your windows.
* **Doors** (`doors`, text, optional): Describe the type and quality of your doors.
* **Walls** (`walls`, text, optional): Describe the type and quality of your walls.
* **Roof** (`roof`, text, optional): Describe the type and quality of your roof.
* **Floors** (`floors`, text, optional): Describe the type and quality of your floors.
* **Appliances** (`appliances`, text, optional): Describe the type and quality of your home's appliances.
* **Home Type** (`home_type`, select one, optional): Choose whether your home is a single-family residence or a multi-unit building.
* **Home Age** (`home_age`, number, optional): The age of your home.
* **Home Energy Rating** (`home_energy_rating`, text, optional): Describe your home's energy rating, if applicable.
* **Energy Audit** (`energy_audit`, select one, optional): Choose whether your home has undergone an energy audit.
* **Survey Comments** (`survey_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your home and energy usage.
