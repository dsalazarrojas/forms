# Donor Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donor Feedback Questionnaire is a tool to gather information from donors about their experience with a specific campaign. This information will be used by the campaign team to improve future campaigns and ensure that donors feel valued and heard.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Provide as much detail as you are comfortable sharing.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed (indicated by the "required" label).
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Details** (Page 1): 
  * **Campaign Details** (`campaign_details`, text, required: false): This field is for you to describe your experience with the campaign. Please provide any comments or observations you have about the campaign.
* **Questions** (Page 2): 
  * **How likely are you to recommend this campaign?** (`questions`, select_one, required: false): This question will help us understand your level of interest in recommending our campaign to others.
* **Contact Details** (Page 3): 
  * **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide any other information you would like to share with us, such as your name and contact information.
* **Contact Method** (Page 4): 
  * **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: false): This question will help us understand how you would prefer to be contacted in the future.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 5): 
  * **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have regarding the campaign.
* **Email** (Page 6): 
  * **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can contact you in the future.
* **Name** (Page 7): 
  * **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your name.
* **Organization** (Page 8): 
  * **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your organization.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is invaluable and will help us improve future campaigns.
