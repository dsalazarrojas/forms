# Group Therapy Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from individuals who are interested in participating in group therapy sessions. The purpose of this form is to help facilitators understand the needs and preferences of potential participants, so they can create a suitable group environment and tailor the sessions to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information on page 1.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number on page 2.
3. Indicate your availability for group sessions on page 3.
4. Select the topics you are interested in discussing in group sessions on page 4.
5. Choose your preferred group size and facilitator on page 5.
6. Add any additional comments about your preferences or needs on page 6.
7. Enter your email address so we can send you reminders for sessions on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: Enter basic information about yourself, such as your name and a brief description of your goals for participating in group therapy.
* **Contact Information**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you with session reminders. We also invite you to share your phone number if you would like us to be able to reach you directly.
* **Availability**: Choose the days of the week when you are available to attend group sessions.
* **Therapy Topics**: Select one or more topics you are interested in discussing in group sessions, such as anxiety, depression, or relationships.
* **Preferred Group Size**: Indicate how many people you would like to be in the group with you.
* **Preferred Facilitator**: Choose whether you prefer a male, female, or non-binary facilitator.
* **Comments**: Share any additional thoughts or comments about your preferences or needs.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can send you session reminders.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted directly for sessions.
* **Availability (again)**: Please note that this field may not be necessary if you already filled out the availability field. 
* **Therapy Topics (again)**: Please note that some sessions may not accommodate all topics. 
* **Preferred Group Size (again)**: Please note that we will try to accommodate your preferred group size but cannot guarantee it. 
* **Preferred Facilitator (again)**: Please note that we will do our best to accommodate your preference but cannot guarantee it. 
* **Comments (again)**: This is your chance to add any additional comments or questions you may have about participating in group therapy. 

Note: Some fields are repeat questions, please only answer them if they are applicable to your situation. 

This guide is intended to be a general help guide for users. However, if you have any specific questions or need clarification on any part of the form, please do not hesitate to contact us.
