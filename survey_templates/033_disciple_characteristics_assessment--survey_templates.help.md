# Disciple Characteristics Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disciple Characteristics Assessment is a survey designed to evaluate a disciple's characteristics and behaviors related to their spiritual growth, relationships, and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the assessment sections, which cover different aspects of a disciple's character.
2. Answer each section based on your understanding and experiences as a disciple.
3. Select the options that best describe your characteristics and behaviors.
