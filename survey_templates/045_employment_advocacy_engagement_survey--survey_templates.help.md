# Employment Advocacy Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about employment advocacy engagement, covering aspects such as awareness, participation, communication, and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and categories.
2. Select the relevant answers for each question from the available options.
3. Provide additional comments where applicable.
4. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) symbol.
5. Ensure you answer all questions honestly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employment Advocacy Initiative** (`survey_page_1`, text, *optional*):
  This field is used to gather information about the employment advocacy initiative you are familiar with. Please provide a brief description or name of the initiative.
  
* **Awareness** (`awareness`, select_multiple, *optional*):
  Select all relevant awareness initiatives that apply to you or your entity. This will help us understand your level of awareness about employment advocacy.

* **Participation** (`participation`, number, *optional*):
  Estimate your level of participation in employment advocacy activities. Please choose a number rating from 1 to 10, where 1 is "Not at all" and 10 is "Very high".

* **Communication** (`communication`, text, *optional*):
  Describe how you communicate about employment advocacy, including methods or channels used.

* **Comments** (`comments`, note, *optional*):
  Provide any additional comments or observations about employment advocacy engagement.

* **Email** (`email`, email, *optional*):
  If you would like to be contacted about this survey or have any follow-up questions, please provide your email address.

* **Phone** (`phone`, text, *optional*):
  If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.

* **Category** (`category`, select_one, *optional*):
  Select the category that best describes your employment advocacy engagement.

* **Sub Category** (`sub_category`, select_one, *optional*):
  Select the sub-category that best describes your employment advocacy engagement.

* **Awareness Rating** (`awareness_rating`, number, *optional*):
  Rate your awareness of employment advocacy initiatives on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is "Not at all" and 10 is "Very high".

* **Participation Rating** (`participation_rating`, number, *optional*):
  Rate your participation in employment advocacy activities on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is "Not at all" and 10 is "Very high".

* **Communication Rating** (`communication_rating`, number, *optional*):
  Rate your communication about employment advocacy on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is "Not at all" and 10 is "Very high".

* **Comments Rating** (`comments_rating`, number, *optional*):
  Rate your comments or observations about employment advocacy engagement on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is "Not at all" and 10 is "Very high".

* **Date** (`date`, date, *optional*):
  If this is relevant to the survey, please provide the date of the most recent event or activity.

* **Time** (`time`, time, *optional*):
  If this is relevant to the survey, please provide the time of the most recent event or activity.

* **Additional Comments** (`comments_2`, note, *optional*):
  Provide any further comments or observations about your employment advocacy engagement.
