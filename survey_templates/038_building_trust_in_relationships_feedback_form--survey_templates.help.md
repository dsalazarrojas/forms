# Building Trust In Relationships Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you assess the dynamics of your relationships, focusing on trust, communication, and emotional support. It's a tool to reflect on your relationships and provide feedback on areas that can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your feedback date at the top of the form (Field 1: Feedback Date).
2. Enter your name or pseudonym (Field 2: Your Name).
3. Provide your email address (Field 3: Email Address).
4. Select the type of relationship you have with the person (Field 4: Type of Relationship).
5. Choose how long you've known them (Field 5: Relationship Duration).
6. Assess your current trust level with them (Field 6: Current Trust Level).
7. Indicate if your trust level has changed recently (Field 7: Has Trust Level Changed Recently).
8. Evaluate their reliability and dependability (Field 8: Person Is Reliable and Dependable).
9. Assess their honesty and truthfulness (Field 9: Person Is Honest and Truthful).
10. Rate their communication transparency (Field 10: Communication Is Transparent).
11. Assess their consistent behavior (Field 11: Behavior Is Consistent).
12. Evaluate the level of mutual respect (Field 12: Mutual Respect Exists).
13. Assess their level of emotional support (Field 13: Provides Emotional Support).
14. Rate how they handle conflicts (Field 14: Handles Conflict Constructively).
15. If applicable, report any past trust breaches (Field 15: Past Trust Breaches).
16. If applicable, indicate how well issues were resolved after a trust breach (Field 16: Recovery From Trust Breach).
17. Identify any barriers to building trust (Field 17: Barriers to Building Trust).
18. Suggest ways to improve trust (Field 18: Ways to Improve Trust).
19. Provide an overall assessment of your relationship (Field 19: Overall Relationship Assessment).
20. Indicate your willingness to work on building trust (Field 20: Willing to Work on Trust).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Date (1)**: Enter the date you are providing feedback on your relationship.
* **Your Name (2)**: Enter your name or a pseudonym to maintain anonymity.
* **Email Address (3)**: Provide your email address for follow-up or further communication.
* **Type of Relationship (4)**: Choose the type of relationship you have with the person (e.g., Romantic Partner, Spouse, Family Member, etc.).
* **Relationship Duration (5)**: Select how long you've known the person (e.g., Less than 1 Year, 1-2 Years, etc.).
* **Current Trust Level (6)**: Assess your current level of trust with the person (e.g., Completely Trust, Mostly Trust, Somewhat Trust, etc.).
* **Has Trust Level Changed Recently (7)**: Indicate if your trust level has increased, stayed the same, or decreased.
* **Person Is Reliable and Dependable (8)**: Evaluate how reliable and dependable you find the person to be.
* **Person Is Honest and Truthful (9)**: Rate their honesty and truthfulness.
* **Communication Is Transparent (10)**: Assess the level of transparency in their communication.
* **Behavior Is Consistent (11)**: Rate their consistent behavior.
* **Mutual Respect Exists (12)**: Evaluate the level of mutual respect in your relationship.
* **Provides Emotional Support (13)**: Assess their level of emotional support.
* **Handles Conflict Constructively (14)**: Rate how they handle conflicts.
* **Past Trust Breaches (15)**: If applicable, report any past trust breaches.
* **Recovery From Trust Breach (16)**: If applicable, indicate how well issues were resolved after a trust breach.
* **Barriers to Building Trust (17)**: Identify any barriers to building trust in your relationship.
* **Ways to Improve Trust (18)**: Suggest ways to improve trust.
* **Overall Relationship Assessment (19)**: Provide an overall assessment of your relationship (e.g., Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, etc.).
* **Willing to Work on Trust (20)**: Indicate your willingness to work on building trust.

Note that some fields are optional, and you can choose to skip them if not applicable to your relationship.
