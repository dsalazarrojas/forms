# inquest_session_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inquest_session_feedback_survey form is a tool designed to gather feedback from participants after an inquest session. The purpose of this form is to collect honest and accurate feedback to improve the facilitators and overall inquest experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Introduction section with your name and a brief description of the session you attended.
2.  Provide your participant information in the Participant Information section, including your name and any other details as requested.
3.  Enter session details in the Session Details section, including the session number and date.
4.  Rate your overall experience in the Rating section.
5.  Share your comments and feedback in the Comments and Facilitator Feedback sections.
6.  Provide your feedback on the next session in the Next Session Feedback section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Inquest Session Feedback Survey** (`introduction`, text, required: false): This section is a brief description of the form and its purpose.
*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name or the name of the participant you represent.
*   **Session Number** (`session_number`, number, required: false): Enter the number of the session you attended.
*   **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the session you attended.
*   **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts and opinions about the session.
*   **Facilitator Feedback** (`facilitator_feedback`, text, required: false): Provide feedback for the facilitator.
*   **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, required: false): Share your feedback about the session.
*   **Next Session Feedback** (`next_session_feedback`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts and suggestions for the next session.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate in your feedback.
*   Keep your responses concise and clear.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
*   If you have any concerns or suggestions, please do not hesitate to reach out to us directly.
