# Molecules And Compounds Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about molecules and compounds for a specific database. It will help us gather data about the properties of molecules and compounds, including their molecular weight, chemical formula, and category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the compound type: Choose the type of compound (Molecule, Atom, or Compound).
2. Enter the molecular weight range: Select the range of the molecular weight from the options provided.
3. Enter the molecular weight value: Enter the molecular weight value for the selected range.
4. Check the molecular weight error: Indicate if there are any errors in the molecular weight calculation.
5. Select the molecular weight unit: Choose the unit for the molecular weight (Dalton, Grams, Grams/Moles, or Kilograms).
6. Enter the molecular formula: Enter the chemical formula for the compound.
7. Check the molecular formula error: Indicate if there are any errors in the molecular formula calculation.
8. Select the chemical formula type: Choose the type of chemical formula (None, Inorganic, or Organic).
9. Enter the compound name: Enter the name of the compound.
10. Select the compound category: Choose the category of the compound (None, Inorganic, or Organic).
11. Enter the compound category other: Enter any other category that doesn't match the options above.
12. Enter additional notes: Enter any additional information about the compound.
13. Check the additional notes error: Indicate if there are any errors in the notes.
14. Enter the date of last update: Enter the date of the last update.
15. Check the date of last update error: Indicate if there are any errors in the date of last update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Compound Type** (`compound_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of compound (Molecule, Atom, or Compound).
* **Molecular Weight Range** (`molecular_weight_range`, `select_one`, required): Select the range of the molecular weight from the options provided.
* **Molecular Weight Value** (`molecular_weight_value`, `number`, required): Enter the molecular weight value for the selected range.
* **Molecular Weight Error** (`molecular_weight_error`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if there are any errors in the molecular weight calculation.
* **Molecular Weight Unit** (`molecular_weight_unit`, `select_one`, required): Choose the unit for the molecular weight (Dalton, Grams, Grams/Moles, or Kilograms).
* **Molecular Formula** (`molecular_formula`, `text`, required): Enter the chemical formula for the compound.
* **Molecular Formula Error** (`molecular_formula_error`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if there are any errors in the molecular formula calculation.
* **Chemical Formula Type** (`chemical_formula_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of chemical formula (None, Inorganic, or Organic).
* **Chemical Formula** (`chemical_formula`, `text`, required): Enter the chemical formula for the compound.
* **Chemical Formula Error** (`chemical_formula_error`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if there are any errors in the chemical formula calculation.
* **Chemical Formula Value** (`chemical_formula_value`, `text`, required): Enter the chemical formula value for the compound.
* **Compound Category** (`compound_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category of the compound (None, Inorganic, or Organic).
* **Compound Category Other** (`compound_category_other`, `text`, required): Enter any other category that doesn't match the options above.
* **Compound Name** (`compound_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the compound.
* **Compound Category Error** (`compound_category_error`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if there are any errors in the compound category calculation.
* **Compound Category Error** (`compound_category_error`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if there are any errors in the compound category calculation.
* **Compound Category Other Error** (`compound_category_other_error`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if there are any errors in the compound category other calculation.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about the compound.
* **Additional Notes Error** (`notes_error`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if there are any errors in the notes.
* **Date of Last Update** (`date_of_last_update`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the last update.
* **Date of Last Update Error** (`date_of_last_update_error`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if there are any errors in the date of last update.

Note: Some fields have error options to indicate if there are errors in the calculation or input. These errors should only be selected if there are actual errors in the compound's properties.
