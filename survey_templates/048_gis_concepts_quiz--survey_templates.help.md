# gis_concepts_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test the user's knowledge of GIS concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Each page has one text field where you will be asked to enter your response to a question about GIS concepts.
3. Type your response in the provided text field.
4. Once you have completed all four pages, click the "Submit" button to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Page 1 (gis_concepts_quiz_page_1)** (`page_1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first page of the form where you will be asked to provide your response to a question about a specific GIS concept. Please enter your answer in the provided text field.
- **Page 2 (gis_concepts_quiz_page_2)** (`page_2`, `text`, required/optional): This is the second page of the form where you will be asked to provide your response to a question about another GIS concept. Please enter your answer in the provided text field.
- **Page 3 (gis_concepts_quiz_page_3)** (`page_3`, `text`, required/optional): This is the third page of the form where you will be asked to provide your response to a question about another GIS concept. Please enter your answer in the provided text field.
- **Page 4 (gis_concepts_quiz_page_4)** (`page_4`, `text`, required/optional): This is the final page of the form where you will be asked to provide your response to a question about another GIS concept. Please enter your answer in the provided text field.

## Tips
- Please provide clear and concise answers to each question.
- You can type your responses directly into the text fields.
- If you need help or clarification on any question, please ask the form creator or administrator.
- Make sure to submit your responses before the form expires.
