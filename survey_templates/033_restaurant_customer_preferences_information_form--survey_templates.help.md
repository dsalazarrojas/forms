# restaurant_customer_preferences_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about restaurant customers' preferences and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the relevant options for each field as per your preferences and experiences.
2. Enter your Email Address and Phone number accurately.
3. Provide feedback and suggestions in the "Feedback And Suggestions" field.
4. Answer the rating question by selecting one of the available options.
5. Enter the Restaurant Name and other relevant details.
6. Enter the team details as per your interaction with the staff.
7. Fill in the other fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Dining Preferences**: Please select your preferences for dining, such as Gluten-free, Vegan, Vegetarian, or Other (specify).
* **Dietary Needs**: Select all that apply from Gluten-free, Vegan, Vegetarian, or Other (specify).
* **Preferred Meal Time**: Choose one of the options: Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner.
* **Feedback And Suggestions**: Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about your experience at the restaurant.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to allow us to communicate with you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you if needed.
* **Restaurant Visits**: Tell us how many times you've visited the restaurant.
* **Last Dining Visit**: Enter the date of your last visit to the restaurant.
* **Rate Your Experience**: Rate your experience as Very Good, Good, Average, or Bad.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Restaurant Name**: Enter the name of the restaurant where you had your dining experience.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the restaurant is situated.
* **Staff Name**: Enter the name of the staff member you interacted with.
* **Staff Email**: Enter the email address of the staff member.
* **Staff Phone**: Enter the phone number of the staff member. (Only if applicable)
* **Staff Position**: Enter the position of the staff member. (Only if applicable)
* **Department**: Enter the department of the staff member. (Only if applicable)
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team you interacted with.
* **Team Position**: Enter your position within the team.
* **Section**: Enter the section of the restaurant where you had your experience.
* **Section 1**, **Section 1 1**, **Section 1 1 1**, **Section 1 1 2**: Enter any additional details about your experience. (Only for specific sections)
*   **Section 2**: Enter any additional details about your experience in this section.
