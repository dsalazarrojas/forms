# Community Health Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Health Program Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from evaluators on the effectiveness of a community health program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the Evaluator Information section**, where you will provide your name (optional) and your role in the program.
2. Continue to the Program Content and Impact section where you will rate the clarity of the program's goals and objectives, and provide your assessment of the impact on community health knowledge and behavior.
3. Evaluate the Efficiency of Program Delivery and Logistics by selecting a rating.
4. In the Additional Feedback section, you can provide any major challenges you faced during the program and any success stories or highlights.
5. Finally, provide your overall rating of the program and any recommendations for future health programs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`Evaluator Name`, **text**, required/optional): Your name, which will help us get in touch with you in case we need more information.
* **Your Role in the Program** (`Evaluator Role`, **select_one**, required): Choose your role in the program (Participant, Program Staff, Volunteer, Community Stakeholder, Partner Organization).
* **Clarity of program goals and objectives** (`Goal Clarity`, **select_one**, required): Rate how clear the program's goals and objectives were to you.
* **Quality of educational resources provided** (`Resource Quality`, **select_one**, required): Rate the quality of materials and handouts provided.
* **Impact on community health knowledge and behavior** (`Community Impact`, **text**, required): Provide your assessment of how effective the program was in improving community health knowledge and behavior.
* **Efficiency of program delivery and logistics** (`Delivery Efficiency`, **select_one**, required): Rate how efficient the program's delivery and logistics were.
* **Major challenges faced during the program** (`Challenges Encountered`, **text**, optional): If you faced any significant challenges, please describe them.
* **Success stories or program highlights** (`Success Stories`, **text**, optional): Share any positive experiences or highlights from the program.
* **Recommendations for future health programs** (`Future Recommendations`, **text**, optional): If you have any suggestions for future health programs, please share them.
* **Overall Rating of the Health Program** (`Overall Rating`, **select_one**, required): Rate the program overall from 1 to 5 stars.

## Tips
- Please answer all required fields to ensure your feedback is complete.
- Be as specific as possible when answering questions.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
