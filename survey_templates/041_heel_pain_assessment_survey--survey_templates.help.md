# heel_pain_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The heel pain assessment survey is a collection of questions designed to gather information about a patient's heel pain experience. It helps healthcare professionals assess the severity and characteristics of the pain, as well as the patient's medical history and treatment history. This survey is used to support the diagnosis and treatment of heel pain conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Provide the patient's age in the "Patient Age" field.
3. Enter the patient's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email Address" field.
5. Enter the patient's medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field.
6. Select the date of the visit in the "Visit Date" field.
7. Describe the location of the pain in the "Pain Location" field.
8. Indicate the duration of the pain in the "Pain Duration" field.
9. Select the frequency of the pain in the "Pain Frequency" field.
10. Select the intensity of the pain in the "Pain Intensity" field.
11. Select one or more symptoms related to the pain from the list provided in the "Pain Characteristics" field.
12. Select one or more symptoms from the list provided in the "Symptoms" field.
13. Select one or more past injuries from the list provided in the "Past Injuries" field.
14. Select one or more medical conditions from the list provided in the "Medical Conditions" field.
15. Describe any previous treatments received by the patient in the "Previous Treatment" field.
16. Describe the current treatment the patient is receiving in the "Current Treatment" field.
17. Indicate whether the patient is using any medication for their condition in the "Medication Use" field.
18. Describe the names of any medications the patient is taking in the "Medication Names" field.
19. Describe the doses of the medications the patient is taking in the "Medication Doses" field.
20. Indicate if the patient has any allergy in the "Allergy" field.
21. Describe any allergies the patient has in the "Allergy Names" field.
22. Enter the name of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
23. Describe the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
24. Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
25. Enter the email address of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Email" field.
26. The patient must sign this form to confirm their identity.
