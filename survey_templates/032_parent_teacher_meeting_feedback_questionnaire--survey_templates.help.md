# Parent Teacher Meeting Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from parents or guardians on the effectiveness of teacher-student communication during a parent-teacher meeting. This feedback will help teachers to identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to their teaching methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the school ID to associate the feedback with the corresponding school.
2. Enter the date of the parent-teacher meeting.
3. Provide your name as a teacher.
4. Rate the effectiveness of communication during the meeting on a scale of 1-5.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting on a scale of 1-5.
6. Rate your ability to plan future activities or meetings on a scale of 1-5.
7. Add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Id (1)**: Enter the school ID to associate the feedback with the corresponding school.
* **Meeting Date (2)**: Select the date of the parent-teacher meeting.
* **Teacher Name (3)**: Enter your name as a teacher.
* **Feedback Score (4)**: Rate the effectiveness of communication during the meeting on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1: Poor communication
	+ 2: Fair communication
	+ 3: Good communication
	+ 4: Very good communication
	+ 5: Excellent communication
* **Overall Satisfaction (5)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
* **Communication Effectiveness (6)**: Rate your ability to communicate with parents effectively during the meeting on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1: Poor communication
	+ 2: Fair communication
	+ 3: Good communication
	+ 4: Very good communication
	+ 5: Excellent communication
* **Future Planning (7)**: Rate your ability to plan future activities or meetings on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1: Poor planning
	+ 2: Fair planning
	+ 3: Good planning
	+ 4: Very good planning
	+ 5: Excellent planning
* **Feedback Comments (8)**: Add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* Use this form to provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your ratings.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please include them in the feedback comments section.
* This form is for collecting feedback on specific parent-teacher meetings, not for general feedback or evaluations.
