# Cell Expansion Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on a cell expansion training session. It is intended to help training administrators and program managers evaluate the effectiveness of the training and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required questions, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the text fields to provide more information.
4. Make sure to save your progress frequently, as the form may time out if you are away from your computer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Your name as it appears on your employee identification card or other official document.*
* **Email Address**: Your work email address, if you have one.*
* **Department**: The department or section you belong to, if applicable.*
* **Survey Date**: The date you attended the training session.*
* **Training Session Date**: The date the training session took place.*
* **Trainer Name**: The name of the instructor who led the training session.*
* **Training Duration**: The length of the training session, in hours or days (optional).*
* **Relevance of Training Content**: How well did the training content relate to your work? (Select from Highly Relevant, Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Marginally Relevant, or Not Relevant)*
* **Comprehensiveness of Content**: How comprehensive was the training content? (Select from Excellent, Good, Adequate, Limited, or Insufficient)*
* **Clarity of Training Materials**: How clear were the training materials? (Select from Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear, or Very Unclear)*
* **Practical Application of Concepts**: How easily can you apply what you learned? (Select from Easily Applicable, Applicable, Somewhat Applicable, Difficult to Apply, or Not Applicable)*
* **Instructor Knowledge and Expertise**: How would you rate the instructor's knowledge and expertise? (Select from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Poor)*
* **Instructor Communication Skills**: How well did the instructor communicate the concepts? (Select from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Poor)*
* **Instructor Engagement with Participants**: How engaging was the instructor with participants? (Select from Very Engaging, Engaging, Neutral, Not Very Engaging, or Disengaging)*
* **Overall Training Satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with the training session? (Select from Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied)*
* **Value of Training for Time Invested**: How valuable do you think the training was for the time invested? (Select from Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, or Wasted Time)*
* **Would you Recommend this Training to Colleagues?**: Would you recommend this training to your colleagues? (Select from Definitely, Probably, Maybe, Probably Not, or Definitely Not)*
* **Greatest Strengths of the Training**: What did you like best about the training? (Text field)*
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: What would you suggest to improve the training? (Text field)*

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact the survey administrator.
* This form is for feedback purposes only and is intended to improve the training program.
* Your feedback is valuable and appreciated.
