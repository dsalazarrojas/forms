# Museum Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us understand your visit experience and gather your feedback. Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and thank you for your contribution to the museum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply scroll through each page, fill in the requested information, and submit at the end. You can save your progress at any time and return to it later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Information** (Visitor Information, text, optional):
	+ Fill in your name, address, and any other details you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Visited Times** (Visited Times, number, optional):
	+ If you've visited the museum before, enter the number of times you've been here. This will help us understand your visit frequency.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, optional):
	+ Leave your contact details so we can follow up with you about your visit or provide updates about our museum.
* **Questions About The Museum** (Questions About The Museum, select multiple, optional):
	+ Choose one or more of the options that best describe your experience or thoughts about our museum.
	+ - What is the main reason you come to our museum? 
	+ - Are you a repeat visitor?
	+ - Do you plan on visiting other museums in the area?

## Tips
- Take your time to answer the questions carefully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our museum staff.
- We appreciate your feedback and look forward to seeing you again!
