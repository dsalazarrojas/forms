# Workforce Agility Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Agility Benchmark Survey is designed to assess an employee's adaptability level and identify areas of improvement. It helps measure adaptability through self-assessment and feedback, allowing employees to gauge their own adaptability and develop strategies for growth.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the option that best describes your current state or experience.
2. Take your time to answer each question honestly, and try to be as specific as possible when filling in text fields.
3. For number and select_one fields, choose one of the provided options.
4. For text fields, provide a brief description of your thoughts or experiences.
5. Review your answers before submitting the survey to ensure you have provided accurate and thoughtful responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is this survey for** (`survey_header`, `note`, not required): This page introduces the purpose and objectives of the survey. Read it carefully to understand what you will be assessing.
* **How would you rate your adaptability level** (`adaptability_scale`, `number`, not required): Rate your adaptability on a scale of 1-10, where 1 means 'low' and 10 means 'high'.
* **How important is adaptability to you** (`adaptability_score`, `number`, not required): Assess the importance of adaptability in your role, with 1-10 as the scale.
* **What is the frequency of adapting to new things** (`adaptability_frequency`, `select_one`, not required): Choose from the options: 'Agile and flexible', 'Sometimes', 'Rarely', or 'Never'.
* **How often do you encounter agility challenges** (`challenges_scale`, `number`, not required): Rate the frequency of encountering challenges on a scale of 1-10.
* **How important is overcoming challenges** (`challenges_score`, `number`, not required): Assess the importance of overcoming challenges in your role, with 1-10 as the scale.
* **Please describe an agility challenge you've faced** (`challenges_description`, `text`, not required): Describe a specific challenge you have faced, providing details and context.
* **How aware are you of current agility challenges** (`challenges_awareness`, `select_one`, not required): Choose from the options: 'High', 'Medium', or 'Low'.
* **How often do you provide feedback** (`employee_feedback`, `select_one`, not required): Select from the options: 'Regularly', 'Occasionally', or 'Rarely'.
* **How often do leaders provide feedback** (`leader_feedback`, `select_one`, not required): Choose from the options: 'Regularly', 'Occasionally', or 'Rarely'.
* **How often do you feel like you're part of the team** (`departmentalization`, `select_one`, not required): Rate your sense of belonging to the team, with options: 'Often', 'Sometimes', or 'Rarely'.
* **How much collaboration do you experience within your department** (`teamwork`, `select_one`, not required): Rate collaboration level with options: 'High', 'Medium', or 'Low'.
* **How much support do you receive from leadership** (`leadership_support`, `select_one`, not required): Assess leadership support, with options: 'High', 'Medium', or 'Low'.
* **How important is adaptability to your role** (`adaptability_importance`, `select_one`, not required): Rate the importance of adaptability, with options: 'High', 'Medium', or 'Low'.
* **Have you ever received training on adaptability** (`adaptability_training`, `select_one`, not required): Choose from the options: 'True' or 'False'.
* **What is the impact of lack of adaptability** (`adaptability_implications`, `select_one`, not required): Select the impact of lacking adaptability, with options: 'High', 'Medium', or 'Low'.
* **What strategy do you think is most effective for adaptability** (`adaptability_strategies`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the most effective strategy, with options: 'Communication', 'Training', or 'Leadership'.
* **What is the adaptability barometer score** (`adaptability_barometer`, `number`, not required): Rate your current adaptability barometer score on a scale of 1-10.
* **What is the adaptability barometer score score** (`adaptability_barometer_score`, `number`, not required): Rate the significance of the adaptability barometer score, on the same 1-10 scale.
* **How important is adaptability barometer score** (`adaptability_barometer_importance`, `select_one`, not required): Rate the importance of the adaptability barometer score, with options: 'High', 'Medium', or 'Low'.
* **How often is adaptability barometer score updated** (`adaptability_barometer_frequency`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the update frequency, with options: 'Often', 'Sometimes', or 'Rarely'.
* **How aware are you of the adaptability barometer score** (`adaptability_barometer_awareness`, `select_one`, not required): Rate your awareness of the barometer, with options: 'High', 'Medium', or 'Low'.
* **What is the impact of high or low adaptability barometer score** (`adaptability_barometer_implications`, `text`, not required): Describe the implications of a high or low adaptability barometer score.
* **What strategy do you think is most effective for adaptability barometer score** (`adaptability_barometer_strategies`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the most effective strategy, with options: 'Communication', 'Training', or 'Leadership'.
* **What is the final step in the adaptability assessment** (`survey_conclusion`, `text`, not required): Summarize your thoughts and reflections after completing the survey.
