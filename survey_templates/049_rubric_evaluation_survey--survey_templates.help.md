# rubric_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rubric evaluation survey is a form used to assess a person's performance based on a set of predetermined criteria. It is designed to gather information about their strengths and weaknesses and provide a fair and comprehensive evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of the rubric and the criteria you are evaluating.
2. Answer each field accurately and honestly, based on the person's performance.
3. For "rubric_title" field, enter the title of the rubric being evaluated.
4. For "rubric_criterion_1", "rubric_criterion_2", "rubric_criterion_3", "rubric_criterion_4", and "rubric_criterion_5" fields, select the corresponding criteria that the person has met or not met.
5. For "comments" field, provide any additional comments or observations you have about the person's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **rubric_title** (`rubric_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the rubric being evaluated. This is a required field that should be filled out accurately.
* **rubric_criterion_1** (`rubric_criterion_1`, select_one, required): Select whether the person has met the first criterion.
* **rubric_criterion_2** (`rubric_criterion_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select all criteria that the person has met.
* **rubric_criterion_3** (`rubric_criterion_3`, select_one, required): Select whether the person has met the third criterion.
* **rubric_criterion_4** (`rubric_criterion_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select all criteria that the person has met.
* **rubric_criterion_5** (`rubric_criterion_5`, select_one, required): Select whether the person has met the fifth criterion.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the person's performance.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Review the rubric and criteria carefully before completing the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the criteria, please consult with the rubric owner or relevant parties.
* Make sure to complete the form for each evaluation separately to avoid duplication.
