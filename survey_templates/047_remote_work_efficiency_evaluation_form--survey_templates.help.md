# Remote Work Efficiency Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Work Efficiency Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about your work experience as a remote worker. It will help us understand the challenges you face, the tools you use, and your overall efficiency level in remote work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of work you do by choosing the appropriate option from the "Remote Work Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the number of work hours you typically spend on remote work (if required).
3. Select your current efficiency level (if required).
4. Type the challenges you face in remote work. This is a free-form text field, so describe your experiences in as much detail as you need.
5. List the tools you use for remote work. This could include software applications, hardware devices, or any other tools that help you stay productive.
6. Describe your primary methods of communication while working remotely.
7. Describe your primary methods of collaboration while working remotely.
8. Describe your typical work environment while working remotely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Remote Work Type** (`work_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of work you do, such as Full-time, Part-time, Freelance, or Other.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required: false): Enter the number of hours you typically spend on remote work.
* **Efficiency Level** (`efficiency_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose your current efficiency level, High, Medium, or Low.
* **Challenges Faced in Remote Work** (`challenges`, text, required: false): Describe the difficulties you experience while working remotely.
* **Productivity Tools** (`productivity_tools`, text, required: false): List the tools you use for remote work, such as software or hardware.
* **Communication Methods** (`communication_methods`, text, required: false): Describe the primary methods of communication you use while working remotely (e.g., email, phone, video calls, etc.).
* **Collaboration Methods** (`collaboration_methods`, text, required: false): Describe the primary methods of collaboration you use while working remotely (e.g., team chats, project management tools, etc.).
* **Work Environment** (`work_enviroment`, text, required: false): Describe your typical work environment while working remotely (e.g., home office, coffee shop, etc.).

## Tips
* You can fill in only the necessary information, as some fields are optional.
* If you're unsure about a field, you can always reach out to us for clarification.
* Be as detailed as possible in your responses to help us get the most accurate picture of your remote work experience.
