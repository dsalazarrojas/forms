# neighborhood_watch_program_feedback_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from community members about their experiences with the neighborhood watch program. This feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes to better serve the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the neighborhood watch program in the "program_title" field.
2. Enter your name in the "name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone number" field.
5. Provide your feedback about the program in the "feedback" field.
6. Select all applicable categories for your feedback in the "category" field.
7. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "comment" field.
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-5 in the "rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### program_title (id: 1)
* **Program Title**: Enter the name of the neighborhood watch program being evaluated.

### name (id: 2)
* **Name**: Enter your name for the purpose of contact and reference.

### email (id: 3)
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.

### phone_number (id: 4)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for further discussion or follow-up.

### feedback (id: 5)
* **Feedback**: Provide your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions about the neighborhood watch program.

### category (id: 6)
* **Category**: Select all applicable categories for your feedback, such as:
	+ Neighborhood
	+ Crime and Safety
	+ Other

### comment (id: 7)
* **Comment**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the program.

### rating (id: 8)
* **Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so we can better understand your concerns and suggestions.
* If you have already submitted feedback, please do not repeat your comments.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for further assistance.
