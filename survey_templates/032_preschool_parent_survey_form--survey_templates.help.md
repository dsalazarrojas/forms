# Preschool Parent Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Parent Survey Form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from parents about the preschool environment, teachers, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the answer that best describes your response for each question.
2. Enter any comments or suggestions you may have.
3. Make sure to save your answers and review them before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with our school** (`parent_satisfaction`):
  + Choose one of the following options:
    - 1: Very dissatisfied
    - 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
    - 3: Neutral
    - 4: Somewhat satisfied
    - 5: Very satisfied
* **How would you rate the communication between teachers** (`communication`):
  + Choose all that apply:
    + Very good
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor
* **How supportive are your child's teachers** (`teacher_support`):
  + Enter a numerical value to rate how supportive you think your child's teachers are.
* **What do you like most about our school** (`school_environment`):
  + Enter a text description of what you like most about the school.
* **What do you dislike most about our school** (`school_environment_2`):
  + Enter a text description of what you dislike most about the school.
* **How supportive are your child's teachers** (`teacher_support_2`):
  + Choose one of the following options:
    - Very supportive
    - Somewhat supportive
    - Not very supportive
    - Not at all supportive
* **How available are your child's teachers** (`teacher_availability`):
  + Choose one of the following options:
    - Very available
    - Somewhat available
    - Not very available
    - Not at all available
* **How many students are in your child's class** (`class_size`):
  + Enter a numerical value for the number of students in your child's class.
* **What type of teaching style is most used** (`teaching_style`):
  + Choose one of the following options:
    - Traditional
    - Progressive
    - Montessori
    - Other
* **Do you have any suggestions or feedback?** (`feedback`):
  + Enter any thoughts or comments you may have about the school or your experience.
