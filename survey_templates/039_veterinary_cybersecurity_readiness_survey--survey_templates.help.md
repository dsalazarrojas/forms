# Veterinary Cybersecurity Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Cybersecurity Readiness Survey is a self-assessment tool designed to help veterinary clinics evaluate their current cybersecurity posture and identify areas for improvement. This guide will walk you through the form fields and their intended use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the practice location from the options provided.
* Enter the number of clinics (if applicable).
* Identify your current cybersecurity concerns (select all that apply).
* Assess your current cybersecurity readiness (on a scale of 1-10).
* Provide details about your current cybersecurity plan (if any).
* Classify your sensitive data (public, private, or confidential).
* Describe your data classification policy in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Veterinary Cybersecurity Readiness Survey** (Text Field, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your survey (if any).
* **Practice Location** (Select One, Select One, Optional):
	+ Select the practice location where this survey is being completed.
* **Number of Clinics** (Number, Number, Optional): Enter the number of clinics associated with this practice location.
* **Cybersecurity Concerns** (Select Multiple, Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Select all that apply:
		- Compliance
		- Data Breach
		- Cyber Insurance
		- IT/IS Security
		- Other
		- Regulatory Compliance
* **Cybersecurity Readiness** (Number, Number, Optional): Assess your current cybersecurity readiness on a scale of 1-10.
* **Cybersecurity Plan** (Text Field, Text, Optional): Describe your current cybersecurity plan, if any.
* **Data Classification** (Select One, Select One, Optional):
	+ Classify your sensitive data as public, private, or confidential.
* **Data Classification Details** (Text Field, Text, Optional): Provide details about your data classification policy.
