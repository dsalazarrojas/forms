# Online Teaching Tools Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Online Teaching Tools Survey, a form designed to gather feedback on online teaching tools and their ease of use. This survey aims to understand your experiences and perceptions of various online teaching tools, such as Google Classroom, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Discord.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions carefully to understand what we're asking.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *) as accurately as possible.
4. For the "what_online_teaching_tools_do_you_usually_use" question, select all online teaching tools you commonly use.
5. For the "how_do_you_rate_the_ease_of_use_of_the_online_teaching_tools" question, choose your rating from the provided options (Easy, Medium, Hard).
6. For the "what_were_the_main_challenges_you_faced_when_using_the_online_teaching_tools" question, type your answer in the text field.
7. For the "would_you_recommend_the_online_teaching_tools_to_other_teachers" question, choose your recommendation from the provided options (Yes or No).
8. Finally, fill in your email and phone number (if applicable) in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Online Teaching Tools Survey Form** (`online_teaching_tools_survey_form`): This is the header for the survey form.
* **Online Teaching Tools Survey Title** (`online_teaching_tools_survey_title`): Provide a title for your survey response.
* **What online teaching tools do you usually use?** (`what_online_teaching_tools_do_you_usually_use`): Select all online teaching tools you commonly use from the list:
	+ Google Classroom
	+ Zoom
	+ Microsoft Teams
	+ Discord
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How do you rate the ease of use of the online teaching tools?** (`how_do_you_rate_the_ease_of_use_of_the_online_teaching_tools`): Choose your rating from:
	+ Easy
	+ Medium
	+ Hard
* **What were the main challenges you faced when using the online teaching tools?** (`what_were_the_main_challenges_you_faced_when_using_the_online_teaching_tools`): Type your answer in the text field.
* **Would you recommend the online teaching tools to other teachers?** (`would_you_recommend_the_online_teaching_tools_to_other_teachers`): Choose your recommendation from:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the online teaching tools.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Submit** (`submit`): Once you've completed the survey, click submit to submit your response.
