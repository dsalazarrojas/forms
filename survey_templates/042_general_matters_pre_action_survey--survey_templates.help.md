# General Matters Pre Action Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the general matters pre-action survey, a critical step in ensuring project readiness and success.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant category for this survey from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief description of the project or action being assessed.
3. Choose the tool assigned to the project (e.g., Chatjimmy).
4. Indicate whether early feedback is expected or required.
5. Identify whether concerns should be captured during the survey.
6. Select whether guiding support is expected or required.
7. Determine the project's readiness status (Yes or No).
8. Provide any additional feedback or comments (optional).
9. Identify who is involved in the project (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Matters Pre-Action Survey** (`general_matters_pre_action_survey_title`): Enter a brief title or description of the project or action being assessed.
* **Category** (`general_matters_pre_action_survey_category`): Select the category for this survey from the options "Survey Templates" or "Other".
* **Assigned Tool** (`general_matters_pre_action_survey_assigned_tool`): Choose the tool assigned to the project from the available options (e.g., Chatjimmy or Other).
* **Early Feedback** (`general_matters_pre_action_survey_early_feedback`): Indicate whether early feedback is expected or required (Yes or No).
* **Capture Concerns** (`general_matters_pre_action_survey_capture_concerns`): Identify whether concerns should be captured during the survey (Yes or No).
* **Guiding Support** (`general_matters_pre_action_survey_guiding_support`): Select whether guiding support is expected or required (Yes or No).
* **Readiness** (`general_matters_pre_action_survey_readiness`): Determine the project's readiness status (Yes or No).
* **Additional Feedback** (`general_matters_pre_action_survey_additional_feedback`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the project or action (optional).
* **Who Involved** (`general_matters_pre_action_survey_who_involved`): Identify who is involved in the project (Yes or No).
