# Personal Development Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Development Assessment Form is designed to help professionals assess their current progress and identify areas for growth and improvement. This form is used to gather information about the individual's job title, years of service, salary range, areas of growth, and coaching focus, as well as plan next steps for development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your accurate and truthful information.
2. Answer each field carefully, making sure to follow the instructions and guidance provided.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they accurately reflect your current situation and goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name is the first name you were given at birth.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name is the last name you were given at birth.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your work email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Your work phone number.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Your current job title.
* **Job company** (`job_company`, select_one, required): Select the company you currently work for.
* **Years of service** (`years_of_service`, number, required): The number of years you've been working for your current company.
* **Salary range** (`salary_range`, select_multiple, required): Select your current salary range.
* **Areas of growth** (`areas_of_growth`, select_multiple, required): Select the areas where you'd like to see growth and improvement.
* **Coaching focus** (`coaching_focus`, text, optional): The area where you'd like to focus your coaching efforts.
* **Areas for improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required): Describe the areas where you'd like to see improvement.
* **Next steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Describe the actions you plan to take to achieve growth and improvement.
* **Feedback comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Any comments or feedback you'd like to provide.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Any suggestions or recommendations you have for your organization or team.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Any further comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **Assessment date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): The date of the assessment.
* **Coaching status** (`coaching_status`, select_one, required): Select your current coaching status.
* **Coaching frequency** (`coaching_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of your coaching sessions.
* **Coaching location** (`coaching_location`, select_one, required): Select the location of your coaching sessions.
* **Coaching method** (`coaching_method`, select_one, required): Select the method of your coaching sessions.
* **Coaching outcome** (`coaching_outcome`, select_one, required): Select the outcome of your coaching sessions.
* **Assessment by** (`assessment_by`, select_one, required): Select who performed the assessment.
* **Assessment type** (`assessment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of assessment.
* **Follow-up contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, optional): Your preferred method of contact for follow-up discussions.
