# Parent Teacher Organization Guidelines Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Parent Teacher Organization Guidelines Feedback" form is a survey to gather feedback from teachers and parents to help us improve the parent-teacher organization guidelines. Your input will help us identify areas that need improvement, ensuring that the guidelines are clear, useful, and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the current parent-teacher organization guidelines to ensure you are providing specific and focused feedback.
2. Answer each question honestly, providing your opinion on the guidelines.
3. You can skip any optional fields if you don't have an opinion or don't want to provide the information.
4. Fill out the form and submit it to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Teacher Organization Guidelines Feedback (<name>ptotitle)**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback regarding the parent-teacher organization guidelines.
* **Category (<name>pto_category)**: Choose how you perceive the parent-teacher organization guidelines. You can select from the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Effectiveness (<name>pto_effectiveness)**: Choose how effective you find the parent-teacher organization guidelines to be. You can select all the options that apply:
	+ Clear
	+ Confusing
	+ Somewhat Confusing
* **Clarity (<name>pto_clarity)**: Choose how clear you find the parent-teacher organization guidelines to be. You can select one option:
	+ 0 - No Clarity
	+ 1 - Some Clarity
	+ 2 - A lot of Clarity
* **Usefulness (<name>pto_usefulness)**: Choose how useful you find the parent-teacher organization guidelines to be. You can select one option:
	+ 0 - Not Useful
	+ 1 - Somewhat Useful
	+ 2 - Very Useful
* **Recommendation (<name>pto_recommend)**: Choose how much you recommend the parent-teacher organization guidelines to others. You can select one option:
	+ 0 - Not Recommended
	+ 1 - Somewhat Recommended
	+ 2 - Very Recommended
* **Comments (<name>pto_comments)**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions regarding the parent-teacher organization guidelines, please enter them here.
* **Contact Information (<name>pto_email, pto_phone)**: Please provide your email or phone number so we can get in touch with you to follow up on your feedback.
