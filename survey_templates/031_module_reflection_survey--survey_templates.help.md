# Module Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness and impact of a module. It's meant to help us understand what worked well and what didn't in the module, and to identify areas for improvement for future module development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and provide your genuine feedback.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by *).
4. You can type as much or as little as you'd like in the text fields.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. What was the overall effectiveness of this module?** (`title`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate the overall effectiveness of the module on a scale of 1-10.
* **2. How many hours were used for this module?** (`session_length`, `number`, required): Please enter the total number of hours you spent on the module.
* **3. What are the learning goals of this module?** (`learning_goals`, `text`, required): Describe the learning goals you had in mind when taking this module. This could include anything from specific skills or knowledge you wanted to acquire to general areas of improvement.
* **4. What did you find most valuable about the content?** (`reflection_on_content`, `text`, required): Think about the content of the module and what you found most valuable or helpful. This could be a specific concept, idea, or piece of information that stood out to you.
* **5. What did you find most valuable about the structure?** (`reflection_on_structure`, `text`, required): Describe the module's structure and what you found most valuable or helpful. This could be the layout, organization, or overall flow of the module.
* **6. What did you find most valuable about the facilitators?** (`reflection_on_facilitators`, `text`, required): Think about the facilitators of the module and what you found most valuable or helpful about them. This could be their communication style, expertise, or overall approach.
* **7. What did you find most valuable about the resources?** (`reflection_on_resources`, `text`, required): Describe the resources provided with the module and what you found most valuable or helpful. This could be videos, readings, or any other materials.
* **8. What are some areas for improvement?** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, required): Think about the module and what you could do to improve it. Be specific and provide concrete suggestions.
* **9. Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to add them here. This is an opportunity to provide any further feedback or insights you may have.
