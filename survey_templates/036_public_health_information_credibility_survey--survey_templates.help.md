# public_health_information_credibility_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the credibility and trustworthiness of public health information sources.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with a "select_one" or "select_multiple" type, choose the relevant option that best describes the information source.
3. For questions with a "note" type, provide a brief explanation of the information source.
4. Ensure that you answer all questions, even if you are unsure about a particular source.
