# In Flight Meal Options Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The In Flight Meal Options Voting Form is designed to gather information about passengers' meal preferences and experiences on board a flight. This form is intended to help us improve our in-flight meal services. By filling out this form, you will be helping us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct answer for each field, following the instructions below.
2. Fill out all required fields. Required fields are marked with a red asterisk (*).
3. You can submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Flight Meal Preferences** (`flight_meal_preferences`): Select your preferred meal type (Chicken, Beef, Fish, or Salad) for the flight.
* **Flight Feedback** (`flight_meal_feedback`): If you have any feedback or comments about the meal service, please feel free to share.
* **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your boarding pass or ID.
* **Passenger E-mail** (`passenger_email`): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`): Enter the flight number, if you know it.
* **Meal Purchased** (`meal_purchased`): Indicate whether you purchased a meal during the flight (True or False).
* **Meal Type** (`meal_type`): Select the type of meal you purchased (Hot Food, Cold Food, or No Preference).
* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate your overall experience with the meal service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Flight Status** (`flight_status`): Indicate the status of your flight (On Time, Delayed, or Cancelled). If your flight was cancelled, please let us know how we can improve.
* **Flight Departure Time** (`flight_departure_time`): Enter the departure time of your flight.
* **Flight Arrival Time** (`flight_arrival_time`): Enter the arrival time of your flight.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Meal Preferences 2** (`meal_preferences_2`): Select your preferred meal time (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack, or Other).
* **Meal Preferences 3** (`meal_preferences_3`): Select your preferred meal preferences (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack, or Other).
* **Flight Meal Service** (`flight_meal_service`): Select the type of meal service you experienced (Self-Service, Onboard, or Not Applicable).
* **Meal Frequency** (`meal_frequency`): Select how often you would like to receive meals on board (Once, Multiple Times).
* **Meal Type 2** (`meal_type_2`): Select the type of meal you would like to have (Hot Food, Cold Food, or No Preference).
* **Food Type** (`food_type`): Select the type of food you prefer (Meat, Fruits, or No Preference).
* **Drink Type** (`drink_type`): Select the type of drink you would like to have (Coffee, Tea, Juice, or No Preference).
* **Food Type 2** (`food_type_2`): Select the type of food you prefer (Hot Food, Cold Food, or No Preference).
* **Flight Meal Preferences 3** (`flight_meal_preferences_3`): Select your preferred meal preferences (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack, or Other).
* **Flight Status 2** (`flight_status_2`): Indicate the status of your flight (On Time, Delayed, or Cancelled).
* **Flight Meal Preferences 4** (`flight_meal_preferences_4`): Select your preferred meal preferences (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack, or Other).
* **Passenger Phone** (`passenger_phone`): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Flight Meal Preferences 5** (`flight_meal_preferences_5`): Select your preferred meal preferences (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack, or Other).

**Tips**:
- Please answer all required fields. Required fields are marked with a red asterisk (*).
- You can submit the form multiple times, but please be honest and provide accurate information for each submission.
- We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our in-flight meal services.
