# optical_illusion_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on how users perceive a series of optical illusions. The goal is to understand the user's perception of the illusions and analyze the results for further research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction section, which provides context and explanation of the form.
2. Answer each question according to your perception. You may choose from the options provided.
3. For the multiple choice question, select all the options that apply to your perception.
4. Provide feedback in the text box if you have any comments or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This section provides background information on the optical illusions you will be presented with. Read it carefully before proceeding.
* **What** (`question1`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your answer to the question "What" by selecting one of the options provided. Your answer will help us understand your perception.
* **What** (`question2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your answer to the question "What" by selecting one of the options provided. This will help us understand your perception of the second question.
* **Which** (`question3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply to your perception of the question "Which". This will help us understand your perception of the multiple choice question.
* **Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any comments or concerns you have about the form or the questions. Your feedback is valuable to us.
