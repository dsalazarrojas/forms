# trade_show_floor_layout_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from trade show attendees on the trade show floor layout. It helps the organizers to understand what attendees liked or disliked about the layout and what suggestions they have for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Provide your feedback and suggestions in the text fields.
4. Select your product type from the options provided.
5. Add any additional comments or comments you have about your experience.
6. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trade Show Feedback** (`trade_show_layout_feedback`): This is a free-text field where you can provide your feedback on the trade show floor layout.
  * Required: Yes
  * Type: Text
  * Hint: Please provide your thoughts about the trade show floor layout.

* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`): This is a free-text field where you can provide any suggestions you have for improving the trade show floor layout.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Text
  * Hint: Please enter your suggestions here.

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): This field is where you can enter your name.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Text
  * Hint: Please enter your name.

* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): This field is where you can enter your email address.
  * Required: Yes
  * Type: Email
  * Hint: Please enter your email address.

* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): This field is where you can enter your phone number.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Text
  * Hint: Please enter your phone number.

* **Booth Number** (`booth_number`): This field is where you can enter the number of your booth.
  * Required: Yes
  * Type: Text
  * Hint: Please enter your booth number.

* **Product Type** (`product_type`): This is a select multiple field where you can select the type of product you are interested in.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Hint: Select your product type from the options provided.

* **Comments** (`comments`): This is a free-text field where you can add any additional comments or comments about your experience.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Text
  * Hint: Please enter your comments here.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible with your feedback and suggestions.
* If you have multiple suggestions, you can enter them in the suggestions field.
* If you have any additional comments, you can enter them in the comments field.
* If you need any further clarification, please contact the trade show organizers.
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure the organizers can contact you.
