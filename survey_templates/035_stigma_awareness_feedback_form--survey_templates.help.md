# Stigma Awareness Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback on stigma related to a specific program or initiative. It is meant to be completed by individuals who have participated in or have knowledge of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
3. Enter your Email address to help us contact you with any follow-up questions or concerns.
4. If applicable, provide your Phone number to allow us to reach out to you directly.
5. Select your Program Type from the options provided.
6. If you selected "Other" in the Program Type field, please provide a brief explanation in the "If other, please specify" field.
7. Choose your Stigma Perception from the options provided.
8. Select the Program Impact from the options provided.
9. Provide any Additional Info you think would be helpful in understanding your feedback.
10. Select the Date and Time you completed the form.
11. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documentation.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documentation.
* **Email** (3): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Phone** (4): This field is optional and only required if you have a phone number you would like to share. If not, you can skip this field.
* **Program Type** (5): Select the type of program you are providing feedback on, such as School or Nonprofit.
* **If other, please specify** (6): If you selected "Other" in the Program Type field, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Stigma Perception** (7): Choose how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement about stigma related to the program.
* **Program Impact** (8): Select how you perceive the impact of the program on stigma.
* **Additional Info** (9): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **Date** (11): Enter the date you completed the form in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time** (12): Enter the time you completed the form in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Submit** (10): Click to send your feedback to us.
* **Submit Button** (10): This button will send your completed form to us.
