# Pre Training Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Training Skills Assessment form is used to gather information about an individual's background, skills, and expectations from the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, Role, and Department.
2. Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to the following questions:
	* Skills: Do you have any of the skills listed below?
	* Training Needs: Do you need any training on the listed topics?
	* Session Language: Is the session to be conducted in English?
	* Trainer: Do you need a specific trainer for the session?
3. Enter the following information:
	* Date: Date of the training session
	* Time: Time of the training session
	* Location: Location of the training session
	* Session Length: Length of the training session in hours
	* Session Frequency: Frequency of the training session
	* Session Schedule: Schedule of the training session
	* Session Type: Type of the training session
	* Training Topic: Topic of the training session
	* Session Facility: Facility where the training session will be held
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the training session
