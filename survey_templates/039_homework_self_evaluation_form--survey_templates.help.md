# Homework Self Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information on students' homework performance, including effort, challenges, and learning outcomes. It helps teachers evaluate and provide feedback to students, identifying areas of strength and weakness. This process enables data-driven decision making to support student growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all fields that apply to your performance on the current homework assignment.
2. Be as specific as possible when describing your challenges and learning outcomes.
3. Use the effort scale score to evaluate your effort on the assignment.
4. Rate yourself and your teacher on the self-assessment and teacher evaluation questions.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback you think would be helpful to your teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Homework Effort** (`homework_effort`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value to rate your effort on the homework assignment, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Homework Challenges** (`homework_challenges`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the challenges you faced during the assignment.
* **Homework Learning Outcomes** (`learning_outcomes`, text, optional): Describe the learning outcomes you achieved from the assignment.
* **Assignment Completion Date** (`assignment_completion_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the assignment.
* **Assignment Due Date** (`assignment_due_date`, date, optional): Enter the original due date of the assignment.
* **Assignment Title** (`assignment_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the assignment.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from your teacher.
* **Self Evaluation** (`self_evaluation`, text, optional): Enter your self-assessment on the assignment.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the assignment.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the assignment.
* **Effort Scale Score** (`effort_scale_score`, number, optional): Enter your self-assessment of your effort on the assignment.
* **Self Assessment Rating** (`self_assessment_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall performance on the assignment.
* **Teacher Evaluation** (`teacher_evaluation`, select_one, optional): Rate your teacher's evaluation of your performance on the assignment.
* **Teacher Suggested Actions** (`teacher_suggested_actions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any actions your teacher suggested you take.
* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback you have for your teacher.
* **Teacher Feedback** (`teacher_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback you have for your teacher.
