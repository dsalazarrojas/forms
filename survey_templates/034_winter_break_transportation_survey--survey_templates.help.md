# Winter Break Transportation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Winter Break Transportation Survey" is a form designed to gather information about your travel plans during the winter break. This information will help the institution understand your travel preferences and needs, which will aid in providing better support and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by answering each question carefully.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your responses.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief introduction to your travel plans.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: To give a brief overview of your plans.
* **Which travel method will you choose for this winter break?**
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: Fly, Drive, Other
	+ Purpose: To identify the mode of transportation you plan to use.
* **Trip details**: Please provide more details about your trip.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: To gather more information about your trip.
* **Start Date**: Date of departure.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Purpose: To record the date you plan to start your trip.
* **End Date**: Date of return.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Purpose: To record the date you plan to return.
* **Number of travel party members**: How many people will be traveling with you?
	+ Type: Number
	+ Purpose: To determine the number of people accompanying you.
* **Accessibility needs**: If you have any special needs, please specify here.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: To identify any specific requirements you may have.
* **Total travel distance**: Approximate distance of your trip.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Purpose: To gauge the distance of your trip.
* **Total travel duration**: Approximate duration of your trip.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Purpose: To gauge the duration of your trip.
* **Frequency of trips during winter break**: How frequently do you plan to travel?
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Options: Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently
	+ Purpose: To determine how often you plan to travel.
* **Mode of transportation**: What mode of transportation do you plan to use?
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: Car, Train, Bus, Plane, Other
	+ Purpose: To identify the mode of transportation.
* **Estimated travel cost**: Approximate cost of your trip.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Purpose: To gauge the estimated cost of your trip.
* **Accommodations**: If applicable, please provide details about your accommodations.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: To gather more information about your accommodations.
* **Emergency contact person**: If applicable, please provide the contact person's name and number.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: To gather more information about your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact number**: If applicable, please provide the contact number.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: To gather more information about your emergency contact.
* **Medical conditions**: If applicable, please provide any medical conditions you have.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: To identify any medical conditions you may have.
* **Health Services**: If you have any specific health needs, please specify here. (Note field for administrative purposes)
	+ Type: Note
	+ Purpose: To gather more information about your health needs.
* **Campus Location**: Where will you be traveling to or staying on campus?
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: On Campus, Near Campus, Off Campus
	+ Purpose: To determine your location.
* **Parking information**: If you plan to park, please indicate here. (If not applicable, please select "Not Applicable")
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Options: True, False, Not Applicable
	+ Purpose: To determine if you will be parking.
* **Parking lot**: If applicable, provide more details about the parking lot.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: To gather more information about the parking lot.
* **Drop off location**: If applicable, provide more details about the drop-off location.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: To gather more information about the drop-off location.
* **Transportation service**: If you plan to use any transportation service, please indicate here.
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Options: Shuttle, Taxi, Ride-hailing
	+ Purpose: To identify any transportation services you plan to use.
* **Campus map**: If you have any specific campus map details, please provide here. (Note field for administrative purposes)
	+ Type: Note
	+ Purpose: To gather more information about campus map details.
* **Additional comments**: Any additional comments or information about your trip.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: To gather more information about your trip.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click submit to save your responses.
