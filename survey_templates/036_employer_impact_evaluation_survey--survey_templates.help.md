# employer-impact-evaluation-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect subjective evaluations from employers regarding their impact, likely for internal feedback and improvement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the "What do you think about the new initiative?" field (Feedback Question) to provide your thoughts and opinions on the new initiative.
* Choose one of the options in the "Select One" field, if applicable.
* Select multiple options in the "Select Multiple" field, if applicable.
* Enter your date of birth using the date picker field.
* Choose a time in the "Time" field, if applicable.
* Add any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if applicable.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can share your thoughts and opinions on the new initiative.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the two options provided, either "Yes" or "No", to express your opinion.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple options from the two available options, "Yes" or "No", to express your opinion.
* **Date of birth** (`date_picker`, date, required/optional): Use this field to enter your date of birth.
* **Time** (`time_picker`, time, required/optional): Choose a time in this field, if applicable.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the initiative.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in this field, if applicable.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in this field, if applicable.
