# Favorite Poster Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Favorite Poster Voting form is designed to collect user feedback on their favorite posters. This form is used to gather information about the user's favorite poster, including the poster's name, URL, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Favorite Poster Voting form, follow these steps:

1. **1. What is your favorite poster?**: Select your favorite poster from the options below. You can choose multiple posters.
	* Type: Select Multiple
	* Options: Poster 1, Poster 2, Poster 3
2. **2. Why do you like this poster?**: Enter your reason for liking this poster. This is a free-text field, so you can type anything you want.
	* Type: Note
3. **3. What is the name of your favorite poster?**: Enter the name of your favorite poster.
	* Type: Text
4. **4. What is the URL of your favorite poster?**: Enter the URL of your favorite poster.
	* Type: Text
5. **5. What is the name of the submitters?**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the poster.
	* Type: Text
6. **6. What is the email of the submitters?**: Enter the email of the person who submitted the poster.
	* Type: Email
7. **7. What is the phone number of the submitters?**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the poster.
	* Type: Text

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* This form is meant to be filled out for your favorite poster.
* Please note that the "Additional comments" fields are not necessary, but they are there if you have any additional thoughts or comments about the poster.
* If you choose to display the submitter's information publicly, it will be visible to everyone who visits our website.
* You can choose to display the submitter's information publicly by selecting "Yes" in the checkbox.
