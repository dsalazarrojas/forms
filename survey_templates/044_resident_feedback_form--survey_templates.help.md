# Resident Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resident Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from residents to help improve their living community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the purpose and importance of your feedback.
2. Select all the amenities that you have used or would like to use in our building.
3. Share your opinions and thoughts about our community in the text fields.
4. Provide any suggestions you have to improve our community.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1): This is a note field to provide a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Resident info** (Page 1): This is a text field where you can provide any information about yourself or your living situation. 
* **Building Amenities** (Page 1): This is a select multiple field where you can choose all the amenities that you have used or would like to use in our building. Options include:
	+ 24/7 Front Desk
	+ Fitness Center
	+ Pool
* **Resident Opinions** (Page 2): This is a text field where you can share your opinions and thoughts about our community.
* **Resident Suggestions** (Page 2): This is a text field where you can provide any suggestions you have to improve our community.
* **Resident Feedback** (Page 3): This is a text field where you can provide any feedback or comments you'd like to share about our community.
* **Resident Comments** (Page 3): This is a text field where you can add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback that can help us improve our community.
* If you have any suggestions, please make sure to provide specific details and examples.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
