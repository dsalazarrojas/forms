# Short Term Rental Guest Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from guests who have stayed in short-term rental properties managed by our company. The purpose of this survey is to understand their experiences, identify areas for improvement, and provide a platform for guests to share their thoughts on their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Check-in Date
	* Checkout Date
	* Name
	* Email
	* Will Recommend
2. Answer the questions based on your stay:
	* Stay Type
	* Property Name
	* Rating (if you have a comment)
	* Overall Experience
	* Communication
	* Cleanliness
	* Accuracy
	* Value for Money
	* Amenities
	* Location
	* Service Quality
	* Any Other Feedback
3. Add any comments or suggestions in the Comments and Property Address fields.
4. Enter the Property Manager's contact information (if applicable):
	* Property Manager Name
	* Property Manager Email
	* Property Manager Phone

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stay Type (Required)**: Please select the type of accommodation you stayed in (e.g., Airbnb, Vacation Rental, Serviced Apartment, etc.).
* **Check-in Date (Required)**: Enter the date you checked in to the property.
* **Checkout Date (Required)**: Enter the date you checked out of the property.
* **Name (Required)**: Enter your name to help us keep track of your feedback.
* **Email (Required)**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Will Recommend (Required)**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend our short-term rental properties to others. Please select Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neutral, Somewhat Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
* **Any Other Feedback (Optional)**: Share any additional feedback or comments about your stay.
* **Property Name (Optional)**: If you're providing feedback for a specific property, enter its name.
* **Rating (Optional)**: Enter a rating (1-5) to rate your experience.
* **Overall Experience (Optional)**: Describe your overall experience during your stay.
* **Communication (Optional)**: Rate the communication from the property manager or staff.
* **Cleanliness (Optional)**: Rate the cleanliness of the property.
* **Accuracy (Optional)**: Rate the accuracy of the property's description and listing.
* **Value for Money (Optional)**: Rate whether you felt you got value for money during your stay.
* **Amenities (Optional)**: Describe the amenities you found during your stay.
* **Location (Optional)**: Rate the location of the property.
* **Service Quality (Optional)**: Rate the quality of service you received during your stay.
* **Comments (Optional)**: Add any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Property Address (Optional)**: Enter the address of the property you stayed in.
* **Property Type (Optional)**: Describe the type of property you stayed in (e.g., apartment, house, etc.).
* **Property Manager Name (Optional)**: Enter the name of the property manager or staff member you interacted with.
* **Property Manager Email (Optional)**: Enter the email address of the property manager or staff member.
* **Property Manager Phone (Optional)**: Enter the phone number of the property manager or staff member.
