# KOL Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The KOL Review Form is used to collect feedback from Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) on specific aspects of a product or service. This form helps to gauge their performance, engagement, and recommendation of the product. It's a crucial tool for businesses to understand the strengths and weaknesses of their products and services from the perspective of their target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the KOL Review Form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and provide honest answers based on your experience with the product or service.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the relevant option that best describes your experience.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions, as indicated by the required label next to each question.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "KOL Recommendation" field to provide more detailed insights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### KOL Form Questions (Page 1)
* **KOL Review Form Questions** (`kol_form_questions`, note, required: false): This section is meant to be filled with any questions or comments about the product or service. You can provide any relevant feedback or insights you have about your experience with the product.

### KOL Performance (Page 2)
* **KOL Performance** (`kol_form_performance`, select_one, required: false): This question assesses your overall performance of the product or service. Please select "Yes" if you believe the product or service met or exceeded your expectations, and "No" otherwise.

### KOL Engagement (Page 3)
* **KOL Engagement** (`kol_form_engagement`, select_multiple, required: false): This section examines your engagement with the product or service. You can select all that apply to describe your level of engagement, such as "Yes" for being engaged, "No" for not being engaged, and "Other" for any other reasons.

### KOL Recommendation (Page 4)
* **KOL Recommendation** (`kol_form_recommendation`, select_one, required: false): This question gauges your recommendation of the product or service. Please select "Yes" if you would recommend the product or service to others, and "No" otherwise. You can also provide additional feedback or insights in the provided space.
