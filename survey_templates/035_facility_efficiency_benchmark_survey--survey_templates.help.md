# Facility Efficiency Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on your facility's energy and water usage, waste diversion rate, and greenhouse gas emissions. The data collected will help you assess your current performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all applicable options for your current facility performance metrics (page 2).
2. Select all applicable efficiency practices your facility uses (page 3).
3. Describe your facility's current sustainability goals (page 4).
4. Choose how you currently collect data (page 5).
5. Indicate whether you share your data with others (page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Introduction
* **Introduction** (`facility_efficiency_benchmark_survey_form`, text, optional): This page provides an introduction to the survey.

### Page 2: Facility Performance Metrics
* **What are your current facility performance metrics?** (`facility_performance_benchmark`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Water usage per square foot
	+ Energy consumption per square foot
	+ Waste diversion rate
	+ Greenhouse gas emissions per square foot
	+ Other (please specify)
	You can select all applicable options that apply to your facility.

### Page 3: Efficiency Practices
* **Which efficiency practices do you currently use?** (`benchmarking_practices`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Energy audits
	+ Commissioning
	+ Energy-efficient design
	+ Greenhouse gas inventory
	+ Other (please specify)
	You can select all applicable practices that apply to your facility.

### Page 4: Sustainability Goals
* **What are your facility's sustainability goals?** (`sustainability_goals`, text, optional): Describe your facility's current sustainability goals and objectives.

### Page 5: Data Collection
* **How do you currently collect data?** (`data_collection`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Manual records
	+ Spreadsheets
	+ Software applications
	+ Other (please specify)
	You can select all applicable methods that apply to your facility.

### Page 6: Data Sharing
* **Do you share your data with others?** (`data_sharing`, select_one, optional):
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
	Indicate whether you share your data with other departments, teams, or external partners.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- Select all applicable options for each field to ensure you are accurately representing your facility's performance and practices.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the form author for guidance.
