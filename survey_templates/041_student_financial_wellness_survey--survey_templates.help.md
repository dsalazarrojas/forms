# Student Financial Wellness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Student Financial Wellness Survey is designed to help students assess their financial well-being, identify areas for improvement, and provide valuable insights for support services. This form is intended to be completed by students to help them gain a better understanding of their financial habits, challenges, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each section carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the relevant options for each question or field.
3. Ensure you fill out all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, `false`): This is a brief overview of the survey and its purpose. Read through this section to ensure you understand the survey's intent and guidelines.
- **Financial Habits** (`financial_habits`, `text`, `false`): Describe your current financial habits, such as spending, saving, and budgeting practices.
- **Financial Challenges** (`financial_challenges`, `text`, `false`): Share any specific financial challenges you are currently facing, such as debt, savings, or financial difficulties.
- **Financial Support** (`financial_support`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Indicate whether you have access to any financial support, such as scholarships, loans, or part-time jobs. Select all that apply.
  - **Yes**: You have access to financial support.
  - **No**: You do not have access to financial support.
- **Financial Literacy** (`financial_literacy`, `text`, `false`): Share your level of understanding regarding basic financial concepts, such as budgeting, saving, and investing.
- **Financial Resilience** (`financial_resilience`, `text`, `false`): Describe your financial coping mechanisms during periods of financial stress or uncertainty.
- **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, `text`, `false`): Share your short-term and long-term financial goals, such as paying off debt or saving for education expenses.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `false`): Provide any additional information or comments about your financial situation.
- **Contact** (`contact`, `email`, `false`): You can provide an email address or other contact information to receive further support or guidance.
