# Facility Management Modernization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information and feedback from facility staff on their perceptions of facility modernization. It is designed to help us understand your experiences and thoughts on our current facility management practices and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question as honestly as possible.
2. For questions with multiple-choice options, select all that apply.
3. You can add any additional comments or feedback in the "Feedback" field.
4. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Types** (`facility_types`, Select Multiple, not required):
	+ This question asks about the types of facilities you are responsible for managing. Select all types that apply to your work.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, Text, not required):
	+ Please describe any challenges you face in your daily work related to facility management.
* **Modernization Initiatives** (`modernization_initiatives`, Select One, not required):
	+ This question asks about your perceptions of modernization initiatives in facility management. Select "Yes" if you think we have modernized our practices, "No" if you don't.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, not required):
	+ Share any additional comments or suggestions you have on how we can improve our facility management practices and services.
