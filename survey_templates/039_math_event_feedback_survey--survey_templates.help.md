# Math Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on math events, such as competitions, workshops, or training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What date did the event take place?** (`event_date`, `date`, Optional): This field is where you report the date the math event took place. Select a date from the calendar or enter it in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, `text`, Optional): This field is for the title of the math event. Enter a brief description of the event, such as "Math Competition" or "Workshop on Calculus".
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, Optional): This field is for the location of the event. Enter the full address or a brief description of the location, such as "University of [Name]" or "Online Platform".
* **Event Outcome** (`event_outcome`, `text`, Optional): This field is for a brief description of the event outcome. Describe the result or outcome of the event, such as "Winner" or "Successful completion".
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, `number`, Optional): This field is for a subjective feedback score on the event. Rate the event from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Feedback Text** (`feedback_text`, `text`, Optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback on the event. Enter any relevant details or suggestions.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, Optional): This field is for the contact email of the person who attended the event. Enter the email address of the person who would like to be contacted for further questions.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, Optional): This field is for the contact phone number of the person who attended the event. Enter the phone number of the person who would like to be contacted for further questions.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `select_one`, Optional): This field is for selecting the person assigned to the event. Choose the user who was in charge of the event.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, Optional): This field is for selecting the tool used for the event. Choose the tool that was used during the event.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
