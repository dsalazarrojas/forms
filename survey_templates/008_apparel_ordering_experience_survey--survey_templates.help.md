# Apparel Ordering Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their apparel ordering experience. It's intended to help us understand what we do well and what we can improve on to better serve our customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your details and contact information.
2.  Answer questions about your ordering experience, including how easy it was to find products, how smooth the checkout process was, and how good the website navigation and product descriptions were.
3.  Rate your experience with our delivery services, including timeliness, condition of the order, and packaging quality.
4.  Share any experience you had with our customer support team, and let us know how we can improve your shopping experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your first name as you want it to appear in our records.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **Required**): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Order Reference Number** (`order_reference`, `text`, Optional): If you have an order reference number, please enter it here.
* **Finding Products** (`finding_products`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate how easy it was to find the products you wanted:
	+ Very easy
	+ Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Difficult
	+ Very difficult
* **Website Navigation** (`website_navigation`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate how good our website navigation was:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Product Descriptions** (`product_descriptions`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate how clear and helpful our product descriptions were:
	+ Very helpful
	+ Helpful
	+ Somewhat helpful
	+ Not very helpful
	+ Not helpful at all
* **Checkout Process** (`checkout_process`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate how smooth our checkout process was:
	+ Very smooth
	+ Smooth
	+ Okay
	+ Complicated
	+ Very complicated
* **Delivery Timeliness** (`delivery_timeliness`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate how timely our delivery services were:
	+ Faster than expected
	+ On time
	+ Slightly delayed
	+ Significantly delayed
	+ Never received
* **Delivery Condition** (`delivery_condition`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the condition of your order upon arrival:
	+ Perfect condition
	+ Good condition
	+ Acceptable condition
	+ Poor condition
	+ Damaged
* **Packaging Quality** (`packaging_quality`, `select_one`, Optional): Rate the quality of our packaging:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Customer Support** (`support_section`, `note`, Optional): If you contacted our support team, please share your experience.
* **Support Response** (`support_response`, `select_one`, Optional): Rate your experience with our support team:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Did not contact support
* **Improve Shopping Experience** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, Optional): Share any suggestions on how we can improve your shopping experience.
* **Would Order Again** (`would_order_again`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate whether you would order from us again:
	+ Definitely yes
	+ Probably yes
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably not
	+ Definitely not

## Tips

* Please answer questions honestly and truthfully.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Improve Shopping Experience" field.
* If you have any feedback about your order or experience, please contact our support team at [support email or phone number].
* Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback! Your input helps us improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers.
