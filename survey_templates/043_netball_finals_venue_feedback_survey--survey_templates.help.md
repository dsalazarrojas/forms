# Netball Finals Venue Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees about their experience at the netball finals venue. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better services to our attendees in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Answer the questions about the venue's facilities, such as parking and toilets.
3. Rate the quality of the venue's facilities.
4. Check if you found the staff helpful during the event.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Venue Name** (text): Enter the name of the venue where you attended the netball finals.
  - **Event Date** (date): Enter the date of the event you attended.
  - **Event Time** (time): Enter the time of the event you attended.
  - **What was the quality of the venue's parking facilities?** (select one): This question helps us understand the quality of parking facilities, such as availability, cleanliness, and accessibility.
    - Parking was good
    - Parking was poor
    - Parking was fair
    - Parking was not available
  - **How would you rate the quality of the venue's toilet facilities?** (select one): This question helps us understand the cleanliness and accessibility of the toilets.
    - Excellent
    - Good
    - Fair
    - Poor
  - **Were there enough staff members available to assist you during the event?** (multiple select): Check if you found the staff helpful and available during the event.
    - True
    - False
  - **Contact Name** (text): Enter your name so we can follow up with you.
  - **Contact Email** (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
  - **Contact Phone** (text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
  - **Comments** (note): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience at the venue.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form only once you have attended the event and have an opinion about the venue's facilities.
- If you have already submitted feedback, please do not fill this form again.
- Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
- If you have any additional feedback, please use the comments field.
