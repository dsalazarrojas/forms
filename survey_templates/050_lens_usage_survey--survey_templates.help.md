# Lens Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to understand your experiences and preferences regarding the Lens tool. By completing this form, you will help us identify areas of improvement and potential features to add to the Lens.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully, ensuring you answer all questions honestly.
2. For questions with options, select all that apply.
3. For text questions, provide a brief but thorough answer.
4. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction (1)**: This page provides context about the Lens usage survey.
- **How often do you use the Lens in your work (2)**: Select the frequency with which you use the Lens.
  - Rarely: You rarely use the Lens.
  - Occasionally: You occasionally use the Lens.
  - Frequently: You frequently use the Lens.
  - Almost always: You almost always use the Lens.
- **How long have you been using the Lens? (3)**: Select the time period of your usage.
  - Less than a month: You have been using the Lens for less than a month.
  - 1-3 months: You have been using the Lens for 1-3 months.
  - 4-6 months: You have been using the Lens for 4-6 months.
  - More than 6 months: You have been using the Lens for more than 6 months.
- **How many lenses do you use in your daily work? (4)**: Answer with the number of Lenses you use daily.
- **Can you give an example of a recent project where the Lens was instrumental in your work? (5)**: Provide a brief description of a recent project where the Lens was useful.
- **Have you used the Lens to support your decision-making process? (6)**: Select 'True' if you use the Lens for decision-making, 'False' otherwise.
- **How likely are you to recommend the Lens to other users? (7)**: Select how likely you are to recommend the Lens.
  - Not likely: Not likely to recommend.
  - Neutral: Neutral on recommending.
  - Likely: Likely to recommend.
  - Very Likely: Very likely to recommend.
- **What do you use the Lens for? (8)**: Select the reason for your usage of the Lens.
  - Data analysis: For data analysis.
  - Project planning: For project planning.
  - Other: Specify other reasons.
- **What do you think is the most important feature of the Lens? (9)**: Select the most important feature of the Lens.
  - Easy to use: Easy to use.
  - High quality data: High quality data.
  - Customizable reports: Customizable reports.
  - None of the above: Other features.
- **Have you used the Lens to create a dashboard or report? (10)**: Select 'True' if you created a dashboard or report, 'False' otherwise.
