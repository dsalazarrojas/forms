# Peer Network Interest Exchange Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer Network Interest Exchange Form is designed to collect information about your preferences and interests in a network setting. This form helps us understand your availability, communication preferences, and other essential details to better connect you with the right people.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. The form is divided into several sections, and each field is explained in more detail below.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Community Name** (`community_name`, text, optional): This is where you can enter the name of the community you are interested in or already a part of.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Offering** (`offering`, text, optional): Describe what you can offer to the community in terms of skills, resources, or expertise.
* **Need** (`need`, text, optional): Specify what you are looking for in the community, such as guidance, support, or collaboration.
* **Interest** (`interest`, text, optional): Share your area of interest or specialization.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Indicate your role or position within the community or project.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a specific date if relevant to your availability or interest.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a specific time if necessary.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your interest or availability.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Choose who you would like to be assigned to or work with within the community.
* **Community type** (`community_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of community you are interested in or already a part of.
* **Member since** (`member_since`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate when you have been a member of the community, such as year, month, or quarter.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you are available or interested in meeting.
* **Frequency 2** (`frequency2`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional frequencies you are available or interested in.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more locations where you are available or interested in meeting.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate when you are available, such as morning, afternoon, evening, or 24/7.
* **Preferred contact** (`preferred_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred method of communication, including phone, email, or text message.
* **Preferred language** (`preferred_language`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred language for communication.
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select a preferred time for meetings or interaction.
* **Assigned to 2** (`assigned_to2`, select_one, optional): Choose another person you would like to be assigned to or work with.
* **Community status** (`community_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the community, such as public, private, or invite-only.
* **Community status 2** (`community_status2`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional community status options.
* **Frequency 3** (`frequency3`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional frequencies you are available or interested in.
