# High School Sports Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your current and future participation in sports, as well as your interests and barriers to participation. Your responses will help inform future planning and development of sports programs and resources at your school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or enter your answers for each question. If a field is required, it will have a red asterisk (*) next to it. Please answer all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Sports Involvement
* current_sports_involvement (`select_multiple`, *): Select all the sports you are currently involved in, such as:
	+ Yes: I am currently involved in the following sports
	+ No: I am not currently involved in any sports
### Interested in Further Education (`select_one`, *): Select if you are interested in further education related to sports, such as:
	+ Yes: I am interested in further education
	+ No: I am not interested in further education
### Barriers to Participation (`text`, *): If you are not currently involved in sports, please select the reasons why:
	+ (e.g. lack of time, lack of interest, etc.)
### Future Sports Involvement (`select_multiple`, *): Select all the sports you would like to be involved in in the future, such as:
	+ Yes: I would like to be involved in the following sports in the future
	+ No: I do not plan to be involved in any sports in the future
### Athletic Programs (`select_multiple`, *): Select all the athletic programs you are interested in, such as:
	+ Yes: I am interested in the following athletic programs
	+ No: I am not interested in any athletic programs
### School Support (`text`, *): If you are interested in sports, but do not know where to go for support, please provide more information:
	+ (e.g. lack of resources, coaches not available, etc.)
### Feedback (`note`): Is there anything else you would like to share with us about sports at your school?
	+ (e.g. suggestions, concerns, etc.)
### Your Name (`text`, *): Please provide your name:
	+ (e.g. John Doe)
### Email (`email`, *): Please provide your email address:
	+ (e.g. johndoe@example.com)
### Phone (`text`, *): Please provide your phone number:
	+ (e.g. 123-456-7890)
### School (`select_one`, *): Select your school:
	+ Yes: I attend a school that offers sports programs
	+ No: I do not attend a school that offers sports programs
### Grade Level (`select_one`, *): Select your current grade level:
	+ High: I am in high school
	+ Low: I am not in high school (e.g. middle school, elementary school)
### Year (`select_one`, *): Select the current school year:
	+ Yes: I am currently in the following school year
	+ No: I am not currently in the following school year
### School Year (`select_one`, *): Select the school year you are currently in:
	+ Yes: I am currently in the following school year
	+ No: I am not currently in the following school year
### Student ID (`text`, *): Please provide your student ID:
	+ (e.g. 123456)
### Survey Completed (`select_one`, *): Select whether you have completed this survey before:
	+ Yes: I have completed this survey before
	+ No: I have not completed this survey before

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are not sure about a particular question, please provide as much information as you can.
* If you have any suggestions or concerns about sports programs at your school, please share them in the "Feedback" field.
* Your responses will be kept confidential.
