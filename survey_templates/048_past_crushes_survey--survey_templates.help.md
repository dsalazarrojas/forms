# Past Crushes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about users' past and current crushes. The form consists of several questions that gather data on the characteristics of the user's first and current crushes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by selecting the corresponding answers for each question.
2. Answer each question truthfully, as the form is designed to collect sensitive information.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Crush** (`first_crush`): Enter the name of your first crush.
* **First Crush Age** (`first_crush_age`): Enter the age of your first crush. This field is required.
* **How Knew First Crush** (`how_knew_first_crush`): Select how you knew your first crush (Friend, Classmate, Social media, or Other).
* **First Crush Duration** (`first_crush_duration`): Select the duration of your first crush (A few weeks, 1-3 months, 4-6 months, 7-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or More than 5 years).
* **First Crush Status** (`first_crush_status`): Select whether you had a crush on someone in the past (Yes or No). This field is required.
* **First Crush Gender** (`first_crush_gender`): Select the gender of your first crush (Male or Female). This field is required.
* **First Crush Reason** (`first_crush_reason`): Enter the reason for your first crush (if applicable).
* **Current Crush Status** (`current_crush_status`): Select whether you are currently having a crush on someone (Yes or No). This field is required.
* **Crush Duration** (`crush_duration`): Select the duration of your current crush (A few weeks, 1-3 months, 4-6 months, 7-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or More than 5 years).
* **Crush Reason** (`crush_reason`): Enter the reason for your current crush (if applicable).
* **How Knew Current Crush** (`how_knew_current_crush`): Select how you knew your current crush (Friend, Classmate, Social media, or Other).
* **Current Crush Gender** (`current_crush_gender`): Select the gender of your current crush (Male or Female). This field is required.
* **Crush Level** (`crush_level`): Select the level of your current crush (Casual or Serious). This field is required.
* **Note** (`note`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your current crush. This field is optional.

## Tips

* Please answer each question truthfully, as the form is designed to collect sensitive information.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about a question, please select "Other" or "Unknown" as the answer.
