# Dental Graduates Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about dental graduates' interests and preferences to help us better understand their needs and tailor our support services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
* Answer each question honestly and accurately based on your personal information.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
* Double-check your responses for any typos or errors before submitting.
* Submit the form once you have finished filling it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (User Info)**: A text field where you can enter your name, username, or any other identifier you would like to be known by.
* **Education Info (Education Info)**: A text field where you can describe your educational background, including your degree, institution, and relevant courses or training programs.
* **Work Experience (Work Experience)**: A text field where you can describe your work history, including any relevant job titles, work hours, or job responsibilities.
* **Job Preferences (Job Preferences)**: A text field where you can state your ideal job preferences, such as job type (e.g., part-time, full-time), industry, or location.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Use clear and concise language when answering each question.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification or assistance.
* This form is for internal use only, so please do not share your responses with anyone outside our organization.
