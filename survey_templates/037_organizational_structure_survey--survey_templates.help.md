# organizational_structure_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "organizational_structure_survey" form is used to collect information about the organizational structure of a company. This includes understanding the reporting relationships, team members, and other internal structures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each page carefully and understand what is being asked.
2. Fill in the "prophets" field with the name of your department or team.
3. Select the appropriate option for "prophets-role" based on the role or title of the person in your department.
4. Choose the job titles for the people in your department in the "jobtitle" field.
5. Enter the number of people in your department in the "department_headcount" field.
6. Calculate and enter the percentage of headcount for your department in the "department_headcount_percentage" field.
7. Enter the name and size of your team in the "team_structure" field.
8. Fill in the size of your team in the "team_size" field.
9. Choose your reporting manager in the "reporting_manager" field.
10. Select the level of your reporting relationship in the "reporting_level" field.
11. Choose the reporting path for your team in the "reporting_path" field.
12. (Optional) Fill in any additional information about your organizational chart in the "org_chart" field.
13. (Optional) Choose the type of your organizational chart in the "orgcharttype" field.
14. (Optional) Choose the size of your organizational chart in the "org_chart_size" field.
15. (Optional) Choose the level of complexity of your organizational chart in the "orgchartlevel" field.
16. (Optional) Choose the scale of your organizational chart in the "orgcharts_scale" field.
17. (Optional) Choose the type of your organizational chart in the "orgchartyype" field.
18. (Optional) Choose the color of your organizational chart in the "orgchartcolor" field.
19. (Optional) Choose the type of image to use for your organizational chart in the "orgchartimage" field.
20. (Optional) Enter the URL of the image for your organizational chart in the "orgchartimageurl" field.
21. (Optional) Choose the width and height of the image for your organizational chart in the "orgchartimage_width" and "orgchartimage_height" fields.
22. (Optional) Choose the size of the image for your organizational chart in the "orgchartimagesize" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **prophets** (1): Enter the name of your department or team.
* **prophets-role** (2): Select the role or title of the person in your department.
* **jobtitle** (4): Choose the job titles for the people in your department.
* **department_headcount** (5): Enter the number of people in your department.
* **department_headcount_percentage** (6): Calculate and enter the percentage of headcount for your department.
* **department_headcount_range** (7): (Optional) Enter any additional information about your department headcount.
* **teamstructure** (8): Enter the name and size of your team.
* **team_size** (9): Enter the size of your team.
* **teamsizerange** (10): (Optional) Enter any additional information about your team size.
* **reporting_manager** (11): Choose your reporting manager.
* **reporting_level** (12): Select the level of your reporting relationship.
* **reportingpath** (13): Choose the reporting path for your team.
* **org_chart** (14): (Optional) Fill in any additional information about your organizational chart.
* **orgcharttype** (15): (Optional) Choose the type of your organizational chart.
* **orgcharts_size** (16): (Optional) Choose the size of your organizational chart.
* **orgchartlevel** (17): (Optional) Choose the level of complexity of your organizational chart.
* **orgcharts_scale** (18): (Optional) Choose the scale of your organizational chart.
* **orgchartyype** (19): (Optional) Choose the type of your organizational chart.
* **orgchartcolor** (20): (Optional) Choose the color of your organizational chart.
* **orgchartimage** (21): (Optional) Choose the type of image to use for your organizational chart.
* **orgchartimageurl** (22): (Optional) Enter the URL of the image for your organizational chart.
* **orgchartimage_width** (23): (Optional) Choose the width of the image for your organizational chart.
* **orgchartimage_height** (24): (Optional) Choose the height of the image for your organizational chart.
* **orgchartimagesize** (25): (Optional) Choose the size of the image for your organizational chart.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the correct format for entering percentages and numbers.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your company's internal documentation or seek assistance from your supervisor.
