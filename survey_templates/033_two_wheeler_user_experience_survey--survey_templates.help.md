The purpose of this form is to collect data from users about their experience with two-wheelers, including their frequency of usage, mode of transportation, and opinions on certain statements. The form is designed to be completed by individuals who have ridden a two-wheeler at least once in their lifetime. If you have not ridden a two-wheeler, please do not proceed with this form.

Before starting, please note that you will be asked questions that are multiple-choice, multiple-answer, and single-select questions. The form has two pages, and the questions are divided into fields for easier completion.

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in the following order:
	* Page 1: Answer the questions regarding your one-time experience with two-wheelers.
	* Page 2: Answer the questions regarding your long-term experience with two-wheelers.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one or more of the provided options.
3. For multiple-answer questions, select all that apply from the given options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **one_time_ridden** (`Number`, **1**): This question asks you to report a specific instance when you rode a two-wheeler. Please provide the exact number of times you have ridden a two-wheeler.
* **how_many_hours** (`Number`, **2**): This question asks about the total amount of time you have spent riding a two-wheeler. Please enter the total hours spent riding.
* **mode_of_transportation** (`Select One`, **3**): This question asks about your mode of transportation used when you last rode a two-wheeler. Please select one of the options: Walk, Bike, or Other.
* **how_much_do_you_agree_with_the_following_statement** (`Select Multiple`, **4**): This question is about your opinion on a specific statement. Select all that apply from the provided options: Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neither Agree nor Disagree, Strongly Disagree, or Disagree.
* **what_is_your_age** (`Number`, **5**): This question asks for your age, which will help us understand the demographics of our respondents.
* **how_long_have_you_been_rideing** (`Select Multiple`, **6**): This question asks about your long-term riding experience. Select all that apply from the provided options: Never, 1-3 months, 1-6 months, 6 months - 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-3 years, 3-5 years, 5 years or more.
* **how_much_do_you_agree_with_the_following_statement_2** (`Select One`, **7**): This question is similar to the previous one, asking about your opinion on another statement. Select one of the options: Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neither Agree nor Disagree, Strongly Disagree, or Disagree.
* **mode_of_transportation_2** (`Select Multiple`, **8**): This question asks about your mode of transportation used when you last rode a two-wheeler. Select all that apply from the provided options: Walk, Bike, or Other.

## Tips

* Please take your time to answer the questions carefully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for help.
* This form is designed to collect data for improving two-wheeler user experience, so your feedback is valuable to us!
