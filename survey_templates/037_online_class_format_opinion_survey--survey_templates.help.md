# Online Class Format Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback on online classes offered by schools.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields:
	*   School Name: Enter the name of the school you are providing feedback on.
	*   Instructor Name: Enter the name of the instructor who led the online class.
	*   Instructor Email: Enter the email address of the instructor (if available).
	*   School Email: Enter the email address of the school (if available).
2.  Choose your answers for the questions that apply to you:
	*   Training Team: Select "Yes" if you believe the online class had a well-organized training team. Otherwise, select "No".
	*   Virtual Learning Experience: Rate the quality of the virtual learning format from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
	*   School URL: Select the correct option for your school from the list.
3.  Provide any additional comments or recommendations if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **School Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the school you are providing feedback on.
*   **Instructor Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the instructor who led the online class.
*   **Instructor Title** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about the instructor's title or position.
*   **Instructor Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the instructor (if available).
*   **School Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the school (if available).
*   **Training Team** (select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you believe the online class had a well-organized training team.
*   **Virtual Learning Experience** (select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the virtual learning format from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
*   **Challenges Faced** (text, optional): Describe any challenges or difficulties you faced during the online class.
*   **Recommendations** (text, optional): Share any suggestions or recommendations for improvement.
*   **Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
*   **Rating** (number, optional): Rate your experience with the online class from 1 to 5.
*   **Submitted By** (text, optional): Enter your name, which will be displayed next to your submission.
*   **School URL** (select_one, optional): Select the correct option for your school from the list.
*   **Submit** (button): Once you have completed the form, click "Submit" to send your feedback.

**Tips**: Please be as detailed and accurate as possible in your answers to provide useful feedback for the school and instructor. Your submission will help improve online classes for future students.
