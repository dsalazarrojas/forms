# job_satisfaction_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the job satisfaction of employees, helping managers and HR representatives understand how employees perceive their work and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to the job satisfaction question from the options provided.
2. Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
3. Provide a phone number so we can contact you (optional).
4. Select your department from the options provided (optional).
5. Optionally, provide any additional comments you may have about your job satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **job_satisfaction_assessment** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the options that best describes your current job satisfaction.
* **overall_job_satisfaction** (`number`, required/optional): If you feel the need to express a numerical value for your overall job satisfaction.
* **job_satisfaction_score** (`number`, required/optional): If you feel the need to express a score out of 100 for your job satisfaction.
* **job_satisfaction_comments** (`note`, required/optional): Provide any comments about your job satisfaction.
* **job_satisfaction_email** (`email`, required/optional): Your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **job_satisfaction_phone** (`text`, required/optional): Your phone number so we can contact you.
* **job_satisfaction_department** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your department from the options provided.

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be filled out, while those marked as "optional" are not mandatory. Please answer honestly and accurately, and if you need help or clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
