# Student Food Survey Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Food Survey Template is designed to collect data on students' eating habits, including their preferences for fruits, vegetables, pizza, beverages, snacks, and morning eating habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to your eating habits. You will be presented with a series of questions, each asking about a specific food or beverage category. Select the options that accurately describe your eating habits, and proceed to the next question.
