# Nail Care Frequency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your nail care habits and preferences. It will take approximately [insert time] to complete. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to provide valuable information to our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the options that best fit your responses.
3. If you have any additional notes or comments, please use the "Additional Notes" field.
4. Review your responses before submitting the form.
5. Click the "Submit Form" button to send your responses to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (Page 1)** (`user_information`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form where you can provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Frequent Nail Care Activities (Page 2)** (`nail_care_habits`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the nail care activities you perform frequently. You can choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Get manicure
	+ File and shape nails
	+ Moisturize hands/nails
	+ Use hand sanitizer
	+ Use nail polish remover
* **Nail Preferences (Page 3)** (`nail_preferences`, select_one, optional): Please select your preferred nail color:
	+ Natural nail color
	+ Bright nail color
	+ Nail art
* **Nail Products Used (Page 4)** (`nail_products_used`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the nail products you use:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Preferred Nail Products (Page 5)** (`nail_products_preferred`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the nail products you prefer:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **How often do you perform the activities listed above? (Page 6)** (`nail_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Please select how often you perform the activities:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **How often do you perform the activities listed above? (Page 7)** (`nail_frequency_2`, select_multiple, optional): This field is very similar to the previous one, it seems to be a duplicate. Please select how often you perform the activities:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Additional Notes (Page 8)** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes, please use this field.
* **Email (Page 9)** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone (Page 10)** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **User Agreement (Page 11)** (`user_agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Please select whether you agree to the terms and conditions:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Date (Page 12)** (`date`, date, optional): Please enter the date.
* **Time (Page 13)** (`time`, time, optional): Please enter the time.
* **Submit Form (Page 14)** (`submit`, text, optional): Please review your responses and click this button to submit the form.
* **Confirm (Page 15)** (`confirm`, text, optional): This field is likely for confirmation. Please select 'Yes' if you have reviewed your responses.
* **Confirm Yes (Page 16)** (`confirm_yes`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you have reviewed your responses.
* **Confirm No (Page 17)** (`confirm_no`, select_one, optional): Please select 'No' if you have not reviewed your responses.

Please note that the last two pages seem to be duplicates. It is recommended to review and remove them from the form to improve user experience. 

This help guide is provided to assist you in understanding the form's purpose and functionality. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact our team.
