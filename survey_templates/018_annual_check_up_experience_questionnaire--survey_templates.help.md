# Annual Check Up Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from patients on their experience with our healthcare provider. Your responses will help us to identify areas of improvement and provide better care for you and other patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Full Name** (`patient_name`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format (e.g., 01-01-1990).
3. **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address if you want us to follow up with you regarding your feedback. This is optional, but it will help us to contact you if we need more information.
4. **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your best contact number.
5. **Visit Date** (`visit_date`): Enter the date of your visit.
6. **Provider Name** (`provider_name`): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
7. **Visit Type** (`visit_type`): Select the type of visit you had:
	* Annual Physical
	* Wellness Exam
	* Preventive Care
	* Follow-Up
	* Specialist Consultation
	* Other
8. **Appointment Scheduling Experience** (`appointment_scheduling`): Rate how easy it was to schedule an appointment:
	* Very Easy
	* Easy
	* Neutral
	* Difficult
	* Very Difficult
9. **Wait Time** (`wait_time`): Rate how long you waited to see the provider:
	* Less than 15 minutes
	* 15-30 minutes
	* 31-45 minutes
	* 46-60 minutes
	* Over 60 minutes
10. **Wait Time Satisfaction** (`wait_time_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with the wait time:
	* Very Satisfied
	* Satisfied
	* Neutral
	* Dissatisfied
	* Very Dissatisfied
11. **Staff Friendliness** (`staff_friendliness`): Rate the friendliness of staff:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Average
	* Below Average
	* Poor
12. **Staff Professionalism** (`staff_professionalism`): Rate the professionalism of staff:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Average
	* Below Average
	* Poor
13. **Provider Communication** (`provider_communication`): Rate how well the provider communicated:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Average
	* Below Average
	* Poor
14. **Provider Listening Skills** (`provider_listening`): Rate how well the provider listened to your concerns:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Average
	* Below Average
	* Poor
15. **Time Spent with Provider** (`time_with_provider`): Rate how much time you spent with the provider:
	* More Than Enough
	* Adequate
	* Rushed
	* Very Rushed
16. **Explanation Clarity** (`explanation_clarity`): Rate how clear the provider's explanations were:
	* Very Clear
	* Clear
	* Somewhat Clear
	* Unclear
	* Very Unclear
17. **Facility Cleanliness** (`facility_cleanliness`): Rate the cleanliness of the facility:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Average
	* Below Average
	* Poor
18. **Facility Comfort** (`facility_comfort`): Rate the comfort of the facility:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Average
	* Below Average
	* Poor
19. **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the visit:
	* Very Satisfied
	* Satisfied
	* Neutral
	* Dissatisfied
	* Very Dissatisfied
20. **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`): Rate how likely you are to recommend this provider:
	* Very Likely
	* Likely
	* Neutral
	* Unlikely
	* Very Unlikely
21. **Likelihood to Return** (`likelihood_to_return`): Rate how likely you are to return for future care:
	* Very Likely
	* Likely
	* Neutral
	* Unlikely
	* Very Unlikely
22. **Best Aspect of Visit** (`best_aspect`): Describe what went well during your visit.
23. **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`): Describe what could be improved during your visit.
24. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any other feedback or comments about your visit.

**Tips**

* Be honest and specific when answering each question.
* Provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field to provide more information.
