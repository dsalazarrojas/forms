# Functionalities Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their selection of functionalities, overall satisfaction, and any improvement ideas they may have. It consists of 8 fields that cover various aspects of the user's experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page, which provides a brief overview of the form.
2. On the "Select Features" page, choose the features that you have used or are interested in.
3. On the "Overall Satisfaction" page, select how satisfied you are with your experience.
4. If you have any improvement ideas, type them in the "Improvement Ideas" page.
5. If you want to add any additional comments, use the "Comments" page.
6. Enter your email address on the "Email" page.
7. Optionally, you can enter your phone number on the "Phone" page.
8. Finally, review the "Submission Confirmation" page to ensure you are ready to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction page that explains the purpose of the survey.
### Select Features
* **Select Features** (`feature_selection`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the features that you have used or are interested in. You can choose multiple features.
### Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose how satisfied you are with your experience. Options are Satisfied and Dissatisfied.
### Improvement Ideas
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, `note`, required: false): Type any improvement ideas you have in this text area.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments you would like to share.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Optionally, enter your phone number.
### Submission Confirmation
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, `note`, required: false): Review this page to confirm your submission.
