# Lead Generation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the lead generation practices within departments. It's a survey aimed at understanding how teams currently approach lead generation, identify areas where they can improve, and determine key contributors to this process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. For each question, provide your honest and thoughtful responses in the text boxes or select from the provided options.
2. If a question is not relevant to your department, please indicate so in the text box provided.
3. Answer each question to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What is your department's lead generation process?
* **What is your departments lead generation process?** (`lead_generation_survey_form_questions_1`, text, optional): This question is designed to understand the current state of your department's lead generation process. Please provide a brief description of how your team generates leads. For example, "We use social media advertising" or "We rely on referrals from existing customers."

### How do you rate your departments lead generation practices?
* **How do you rate your departments lead generation practices?** (`lead_generation_survey_form_questions_2`, number, optional): This question is a subjective rating system to gauge your department's lead generation effectiveness. Choose a number from 1-5, where 1 is "poor" and 5 is "excellent". If you choose a number, explain your reasoning in the text box.

### What are some specific areas where lead generation practices are weak?
* **What are some specific areas where lead generation practices are weak?** (`lead_generation_survey_form_questions_3`, text, optional): This question is designed to help identify areas where your department can improve its lead generation processes. If there are any specific steps or processes that you feel need adjustment or improvement, list them here.

### What role do you see as the biggest contributor to lead generation?
* **What role do you see as the biggest contributor to lead generation?** (`lead_generation_survey_form_questions_4`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided to indicate which role you see as the biggest contributor to lead generation in your department. If you feel that neither option is correct, choose "Other" and provide a brief explanation.

### What role do you see as the biggest contributor to lead generation?
* **What role do you see as the biggest contributor to lead generation?** (`lead_generation_survey_form_questions_5`, select_multiple, optional): This question is similar to the previous one, but allows for multiple selections. Indicate which roles you see as significant contributors to lead generation in your department. You can select from the provided options or choose "Other" and explain your choice.

### What are some ways to improve lead generation practices?
* **What are some ways to improve lead generation practices?** (`lead_generation_survey_form_questions_6`, text, optional): This question is designed to gather suggestions on how your department can enhance its lead generation processes. Provide any ideas or insights you have on how to improve lead generation in your department.

### What are some ways to improve lead generation practices?
* **What are some ways to improve lead generation practices?** (`lead_generation_survey_form_questions_7`, text, optional): This question is identical to the previous one, providing another opportunity to offer suggestions on improving lead generation practices in your department.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you're unsure about a question or feel it's not relevant, indicate so in the text boxes.
- If you need more information or clarification on any of the questions, please contact your supervisor or administrator.
