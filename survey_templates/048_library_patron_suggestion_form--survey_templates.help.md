# Library Patron Suggestion Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Library Patron Suggestion Form is a tool for library patrons to suggest new forms for the library's collection. By filling out this form, you can help us understand what types of forms would be beneficial and interesting for our patrons.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a title for your suggested form.
2. Describe the form you are suggesting in a few sentences.
3. Choose the category that best fits your form.
4. Select the relevant tags for your form.
5. Add any relevant keywords for your form.
6. Provide any additional comments about your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): This is the title of the form you are suggesting. Give it a descriptive and attention-grabbing title that accurately represents your form.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the form you are suggesting in a few sentences. This will help us understand the purpose and content of your form.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best fits your form. This will help us understand the subject area of your form.
* **Form Tags** (`form_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select the tags that are relevant to your form. This will help us understand the themes and topics of your form.
* **Form Keywords** (`form_keywords`, select_multiple, optional): Select the keywords that are relevant to your form. This will help us search and categorize your form.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your form. This is a good place to provide more context or explain why you think this form would be useful.
* **Form File** (`form_file`, text, optional): If you have any additional files or resources related to your form, you can attach them here.

## Tips
* Please make sure the form you are suggesting is not already available in the library's collection.
* Be specific and clear when describing your form and its purpose.
* If you have any additional resources or references, please attach them to your form using the "Form File" field.
