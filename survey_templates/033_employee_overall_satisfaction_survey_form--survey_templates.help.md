# Employee Overall Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Overall Satisfaction Survey Form is a tool to gauge employee satisfaction with their work environment and company culture. This form helps management understand the strengths and weaknesses of their workplace and identify areas for improvement. It's essential that employees complete this form truthfully and thoroughly to provide valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To start, please select the rating for Overall Happiness from 1 to 5, indicating how satisfied you are with your job overall.
*   Next, write down any thoughts or comments related to your understanding of the company's mission.
*   Then, choose all the reasons you would recommend this company to colleagues, friends, or family.
*   Finally, provide reasons why you would recommend the company, elaborating on your previous selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Overall Happiness (select_one)**: Please select a rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
*   **Mission Understanding (text)**: Share your thoughts or comments about your understanding of the company's mission. Try to be as specific as possible.
*   **Recommend Company (select_multiple)**: Choose all the reasons you would recommend this company to others.
*   **Why Would You Recommend (text)**: Briefly explain why you would recommend this company, referring back to your previous selection in "Recommend Company".
*   **Note**: Each section is optional but will greatly help management gain a comprehensive understanding of the employee experience.

## Tips

*   Be honest and thorough in your responses to provide valuable insights.
*   Please complete this form at your own pace, there is no deadline for submission.
*   If you're unsure about any field or have a question, please reach out to the form maintainer for assistance.
