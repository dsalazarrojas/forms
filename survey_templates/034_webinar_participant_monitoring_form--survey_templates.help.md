# Webinar Participant Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from webinar participants to track their demographics, feedback, and participation type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Provide any feedback you have about the webinar in the **Feedback** field.
6. Select your participant type from the options provided in the **Participant Type** field.
7. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional: false): Enter your first name to help us better understand your demographics.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional: false): Enter your last name to help us better understand your demographics.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional: false): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with any updates or follow-up information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional: false): Enter your phone number to allow us to follow up with you if needed.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required/optional: false): Provide any feedback you may have about the webinar. This will help us improve future webinars.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, required/optional: false): Select whether you are an **Attendee**, **Speaker**, or **Host**.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional: false): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please enter them here.
