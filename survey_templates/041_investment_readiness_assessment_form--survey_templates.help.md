# Investment Readiness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the readiness of an investment opportunity by gathering information from the investor.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete the form title field with a brief description of the investment opportunity.
2.  Select the category of the investment opportunity.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the investment opportunity.
4.  Enter the name of the company.
5.  Enter the business model.
6.  Indicate whether the team members are available to work on the project.
7.  Enter the amount of investment required.
8.  Select the location of the business.
9.  Choose the current funding status of the business.
10. Enter the industry of the business.
11. Enter the business website.
12. Enter the social media handles of the business.
13. Enter the business email address.
14. Enter the phone number of the business.
15. Enter the address of the business.
16. Enter the address line 2 of the business (optional).
17. Enter the city of the business location.
18. Indicate whether the business is a yes or no for being registered in the state.
19. Enter the postal code of the business location.
20. Enter the country of the business location.
21. Enter a brief description of the business.
22. Enter the name of the investor.
23. Select the status of the investment (Active or Inactive).
24. Enter a message to the investor (if any).
25. Enter the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the investment opportunity.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose the category of the investment opportunity.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required/true): Provide a detailed description of the investment opportunity.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required/true): Enter the name of the company.
* **Business Model** (`business_model`, `text`, required/true): Enter the business model.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Indicate whether the team members are available to work on the project.
* **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, `number`, required/true): Enter the amount of investment required.
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the location of the business.
* **Funding Status** (`funding_status`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose the current funding status of the business.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, required/true): Enter the industry of the business.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, required/true): Enter the business website.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, `text`, required/true): Enter the social media handles of the business.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, `email`, required/true): Enter the business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/true): Enter the phone number of the business.
* **Address 1** (`business_address_1`, `text`, required/true): Enter the address of the business.
* **Address 2** (`business_address_2`, `text`, optional/true): Enter the address line 2 of the business (if applicable).
* **City** (`business_city`, `text`, required/true): Enter the city of the business location.
* **State Registration** (`business_state`, `select_one`, required/true): Indicate whether the business is registered in the state.
* **Postal Code** (`business_postal_code`, `text`, required/true): Enter the postal code of the business location.
* **Country** (`business_country`, `text`, required/true): Enter the country of the business location.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, required/true): Enter a brief description of the business.
* **Investor Name** (`investor_name`, `text`, required/true): Enter the name of the investor.
* **Investment Status** (`investment_status`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select the status of the investment (Active or Inactive).
* **Investor Message** (`investor_message`, `text`, required/true): Enter a message to the investor (if any).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date of submission.
