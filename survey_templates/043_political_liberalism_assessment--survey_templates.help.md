# Political Liberalism Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Political Liberalism Assessment is a form designed to evaluate an individual's political views, behaviors, and beliefs on various aspects of liberalism. This assessment aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of one's political ideology, values, and participation in political activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page carefully, as they are designed to gather specific information about your political views and behaviors.
2. Review each field label for clear instructions on what is expected of you.
3. If you are unsure about any question, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.
