# Supervised Visit Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on visits supervised by a supervisor. The supervisor will use this form to record their observations, outcomes, and feedback on the session. This form is intended to be completed by the supervisor after each visit, and it will help to track progress and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the order they appear on the page.
2. Fill in the text fields with your observations, feedback, and other relevant information.
3. Select the correct answer for the supervisor outcome (question 4).
4. Enter the session date and time if applicable.
5. Enter the supervisor's name and contact information if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Outcome
• **Session Outcome** (`session_outcome`, text, optional): Describe the outcome of the session, including any challenges or successes.

### Observation
• **Observation** (`observation`, text, optional): Share any observations you made during the session.

### Feedback
• **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the session, including any areas for improvement.

### Supervisor Outcome
• **Supervisor Outcome** (`supervisor_outcome`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral

### Session Date
• **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the session.

### Session Time
• **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the session.

### Supervisor Name
• **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supervisor.

### Supervisor Phone
• **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the supervisor's phone number.

### Supervisor Email
• **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): Enter the supervisor's email address.
