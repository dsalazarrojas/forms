# Travel Package Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their travel package experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that you fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) on each page.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Improvement" field to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Package Details** (1, text, *): Describe your travel package in a few words. This will help us get an idea of what you experienced.
* **When did you travel?** (2, date, *): Enter the dates you traveled, starting from the earliest date.
* **Travel Agency** (3, select_one, *): Choose one of our partner travel agencies that you used for your trip.
* **Tour Operator** (4, select_multiple, *): Select all tour operators that were part of your travel package.
* **Travel Agent** (5, text, *): Enter the name of the travel agent who assisted you with your trip.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (6, select_one, *): Rate your overall satisfaction with the travel package, using the options provided.
* **Improvement** (7, text, ): If you have any suggestions or comments about your travel package, use this field to provide more information.
* **Contact Details** (8, note, ): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can use this field to provide more details about your experience.
