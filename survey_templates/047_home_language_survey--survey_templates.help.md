# Home Language Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Home Language Survey is a form designed to collect information about a student's language proficiency, language usage, and other relevant details. The survey aims to gather data on language patterns among students in schools.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read and understand the instructions above.
2. Fill out all the required fields.
3. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
4. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, `number`, **required**: false): This is an optional field that allows you to provide additional information about the student if needed.
* **Language Proficiency** (`language_proficiency`, `select_one`, **required**: true): Choose the language you are most proficient in from the options provided.
* **Additional Languages** (`additional_languages`, `select_multiple`, **required**: false): Select any additional languages you speak or understand beyond the ones listed.
* **Language Group** (`language_group`, `select_one`, **required**: true): Choose the group of languages you belong to from the options provided.
* **Language Spoken at Home** (`language_spoken_at_home`, `select_one`, **required**: true): Indicate the language most spoken at home.
* **Language Spoken Outside** (`language_spoken_outside`, `select_one`, **required**: true): Indicate the language most spoken outside the home.
* **Language First Spoken** (`language_first_spoken`, `select_one`, **required**: true): Indicate the language you first spoke.
* **Preferred Language** (`language_preferred`, `select_one`, **required**: false): Choose your preferred language from the options provided.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, **required**: false): You may provide any additional comments or notes about the student's language use.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**: false): You may provide a contact email if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**: false): You may provide a contact phone number if needed.
* **Date of Survey** (`date_of_survey`, `date`, **required**: false): Record the date the survey was completed.
* **Time of Survey** (`time_of_survey`, `time`, **required**: false): Record the time the survey was completed.
* **Time of Year** (`time_of_year`, `time`, **required**: false): Record the time of year the survey was completed.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, **required**: true): Provide the student's age.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, **required**: true): Choose the student's grade level from the options provided.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, **required**: true): Provide the name of the school the student is from.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, **required**: true): Enter a unique identifier for the student.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, **required**: true): Enter the name of the teacher who filled out this form.
* **Subject** (`subject`, `select_one`, **required**: false): Choose the subject the survey is related to from the options provided.
* **Grade** (`grade`, `number`, **required**: false): Provide the student's grade level (if any).
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, `number`, **required**: true): Enter a unique student number.
* **Class** (`student_class`, `select_one`, **required**: true): Choose the student's class from the options provided.
* **School Code** (`school_code`, `text`, **required**: true): Enter the school's code.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, **required**: true): Enter who submitted the form.
