# CCTV Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers who have undergone a post-installation service review for CCTV systems. The survey aims to measure customer satisfaction with the quality of the equipment, technician's professionalism, and overall experience with the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Read the instructions below to understand each field and its purpose.
*   Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your abilities.
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated as "required" in the field explanations).
*   Once you have completed the form, submit it to let us know your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name_cctv`, `text`, required): Please enter your company or organization's name.
* **Date of Installation** (`installation_date_cctv`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the CCTV system was installed.
* **Lead Technician Name** (`technician_name_cctv`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the lead technician who performed the installation.
* **Rate the quality of the CCTV equipment installed** (`equipment_rating_cctv`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of the CCTV equipment from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **How satisfied are you with the camera image clarity?** (`image_clarity_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the camera image clarity from Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Rate the technician's professionalism and punctuality** (`technician_professionalism_cctv`, `select_one`, required): Rate the technician's professionalism and punctuality from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **How easy is it to use the CCTV software/app?** (`ease_of_use_software`, `select_one`, required): Rate how easy or difficult it is to use the CCTV software from Very Difficult to Very Easy.
* **Were you provided with adequate training on how to use the system?** (`training_quality_cctv`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you were provided with excellent, basic, or no training on how to use the system.
* **Did the technicians leave the installation area clean?** (`area_cleanliness_post_install`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether the technicians left the installation area clean or not.
* **Rate your overall experience with our company** (`overall_experience_cctv`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall experience with our company from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **How likely are you to refer us to a friend or colleague?** (`referral_likelihood_cctv`, `select_one`, required): Rate how likely you are to refer us to a friend or colleague from Not Likely to Very Likely.
* **How can we improve our service?** (`improvement_suggestions_cctv`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our service, please enter them here.
* **May we use your feedback as a testimonial on our website?** (`contact_for_testimonial_cctv`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you would like us to use your feedback as a testimonial on our website.
* **I have completed the survey** (`survey_completion_check_cctv`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Done" to confirm you have completed the survey.
