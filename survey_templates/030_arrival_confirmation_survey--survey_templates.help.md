This help guide is for the "Arrival Confirmation Survey" form. The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and comments from guests about their arrival experience at a hotel or similar establishment. The survey is designed to gather information on various aspects of the guest's stay, including the ease of check-in process, the friendliness and helpfulness of staff, the cleanliness and comfort of the room, the quality of facilities and amenities, and overall satisfaction with the stay.

The survey is intended to be completed by guests after they have arrived at the hotel, typically during or shortly after check-in.

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
2. Please select the most accurate option for each question, using the scale provided.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please type them in the text fields provided.
4. Make sure to indicate whether or not you would like to be contacted with follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): This is the field where you will enter your name as a guest.
- **Reservation Number** (`reservation_number`, text, optional): This field is where you will enter your reservation number, if applicable.
- **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): This field is where you will select the date you arrived at the hotel.
- **Ease of Check-in Process** (`check_in_ease`, select_one, required): This is a scale to rate how easy or difficult it was to check-in.
- **Check-in Time** (`check_in_time`, time, optional): This field is where you will select the time you arrived at the hotel.
- **Staff Friendliness and Helpfulness** (`staff_friendliness`, select_one, required): This is a scale to rate how friendly and helpful the staff were.
- **Staff Knowledge and Professionalism** (`staff_knowledge`, select_one, required): This is a scale to rate how knowledgeable and professional the staff were.
- **Room Cleanliness** (`room_cleanliness`, select_one, required): This is a scale to rate the cleanliness of your room.
- **Room Comfort and Amenities** (`room_comfort`, select_one, required): This is a scale to rate the comfort and amenities of your room.
- **Room Matches Expectations** (`room_matches_expectations`, select_one, required): This is a scale to rate how well your room met your expectations.
- **Overall Facility Cleanliness** (`facility_cleanliness`, select_one, required): This is a scale to rate the overall cleanliness of the facilities.
- **Amenity Satisfaction** (`amenity_satisfaction`, select_one, required): This is a scale to rate your satisfaction with the facilities and amenities.
- **Overall First Impression** (`overall_first_impression`, select_one, required): This is a scale to rate your overall first impression of the hotel.
- **Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): This is a scale to indicate whether or not you would recommend the hotel.
- **Likelihood of Returning** (`likelihood_return`, select_one, required): This is a scale to rate how likely you are to return to the hotel.
- **What Did You Like Most** (`positive_feedback`, text, optional): This field is where you can type what you liked most about your stay.
- **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): This field is where you can type areas for improvement.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can type any additional comments.
- **May We Contact You** (`email_contact`, select_one, optional): This is a checkbox to indicate whether or not you would like to be contacted with follow-up questions.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please feel free to type them in the text fields provided.
* Make sure to select all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
