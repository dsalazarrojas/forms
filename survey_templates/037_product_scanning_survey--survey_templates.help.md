# Product Scanning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback about their experience with the product scanning feature in a retail store. The goal is to understand the user's perception of the scanning ease of use, information accuracy, and overall satisfaction with the product. This feedback will help us improve the product and enhance the shopping experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question as accurately as possible.
2. Use the dropdown menus and radio buttons to select your answers.
3. Type your answers in the text fields.
4. Submit your feedback when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <page name="Introduction">
* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the product scanning feature.

### <page name="Scanning Ease Of Use">
* **Scanning Ease Of Use** (number): How easy is it to scan items? Please rate from 1 (very difficult) to 5 (very easy).

### <page name="Information Accuracy">
* **Information Accuracy** (select_one): How accurate is the information about items? Please select from the following options:
	+ Very accurate
	+ Somewhat accurate
	+ Not very accurate

### <page name="Item Scanning Frequency">
* **How often do you scan items?** (select_multiple): How often do you scan items? Please select from the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely

### <page name="App Scanning Experience">
* **Scanning Experience** (number): How do you rate the scanning experience? Please rate from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).

### <page name="Customer Support">
* **Customer Support** (text): If you have any issues with customer support, please describe them here.

### <page name="Store Hours">
* **Store Hours** (time): If applicable, please provide the store hours.

### <page name="Customer Favourites">
* **Customer Favourites** (select_one): How do you rate your overall feelings about the product? Please select from the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad

### <page name="Overall Feelings">
* **Overall Feelings** (select_multiple): What are your overall feelings about the product? Please select from the following options:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral

### <page name="Suggestions">
* **Suggest Something** (text): If you have any suggestions for improvements, please describe them here.

### <page name="Customer Service">
* **Customer Service** (text): If you have any comments about customer service, please describe them here.

### <page name="Additional Comments">
* **Additional Comments** (note): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please describe them here.
