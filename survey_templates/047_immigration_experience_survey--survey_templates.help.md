# Immigration Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immigration Experience Survey is a tool used to collect information about your experiences with immigration-related services. This form will help us understand your challenges, satisfaction levels, and the support you have received.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each question and ensure you are answering questions that are relevant to your experiences.
2. Complete each section according to your best knowledge and provide honest answers.
3. If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to include them in the "comments" section.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for us to contact you back.
5. Fill out the form completely and click submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What were some of the biggest challenges you faced?** (`challenges`, text, required: false): This question is asking about any difficulties or obstacles you encountered during your immigration process. Please provide as much detail as possible about your experiences.
* **What kind of support have you received so far?** (`support`, text, required: false): Describe any type of support you have received, such as from authorities, organizations, or individuals.
* **Overall, are you satisfied with the services you received so far?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction level with the services you have received on a scale of 1 to 3, with 1 being "not satisfied" and 3 being "very satisfied".
* **Have services improved since you arrived?** (`services_improved`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the services that have improved since you arrived in your destination country.
* **What were some of the challenges that were solved?** (`challenges_solved`, select_multiple, required: false): List the challenges that were resolved during your immigration process.
* **Are you still facing any challenges?** (`still_facing_challenges`, select_one, required: false): Rate your current challenge level on a scale of 1 to 3, with 1 being "not facing any challenges" and 3 being "facing many challenges".
* **Are you still facing services?** (`still_facing_services`, select_one, required: false): Rate your current level of service usage on a scale of 1 to 3, with 1 being "not facing any services" and 3 being "facing many services".
* **What kind of help have you sought or received?** (`help_sought`, select_multiple, required: false): Describe the type of help you have sought or received during your immigration process.
* **What kind of services have you sought or received?** (`services_sought`, select_multiple, required: false): List the services you have sought or received during your immigration process.
* **Any additional comments?** (`comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your experiences.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you back.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Provide the time you have spent on your immigration process.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Provide the date you started your immigration process.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required: false): Enter the country you are from.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state you are from.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city you are from.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): Select your gender.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, select_one, required: false): Select your ethnicity.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required: false): Enter your occupation.
* **Education** (`education`, select_one, required: false): Select your level of education.
* **Income** (`income`, number, required: false): Enter your income.

Note: Please answer questions honestly and according to your experiences.
