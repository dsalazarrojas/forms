# Financial Service Experience Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Service Experience Report Form is designed to gather feedback on customers' experiences with financial service providers. This form helps identify areas for improvement and ensures that the financial service provider can provide the best possible experience for its customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and email (fields 1 and 2).
2. Choose the type of service you interacted with (field 4).
3. Rate your experience with the following aspects:
	* Service quality (field 5).
	* Staff attitude (field 6).
	* Communication skills (field 7).
	* Resolution time (field 8).
4. Choose how often you would like to give feedback (field 13).
5. If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in field 10.
6. If you'd like to rate your overall experience, you can do so in field 9.
7. If you'd like to provide more information about your experience, you can do so in fields 14, 15, 16, 17, and 18.
8. If you have any feedback about the staff member you interacted with, you can type their name in field 14.
9. Finally, provide any additional information you'd like to share about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number (optional).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of service you interacted with (e.g., Bank, Insurance, Financial Advisor).
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_multiple, required): Rate your experience with the service quality (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Not Satisfied).
* **Staff Attitude** (`staff_attitude`, select_multiple, required): Rate your experience with the staff attitude (Friendly, Polite, Not Polite).
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_multiple, required): Rate your experience with the communication skills (Good, Very Good, Excellent).
* **Resolution Time** (`resolution_time`, select_multiple, required): Rate your experience with the resolution time (Less than 1 minute, 1-2 minutes, 2-5 minutes).
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your overall experience (Excellent, Good, Fair).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Provide a numerical rating of your experience (1-5).
* **Resolution Date** (`resolution_date`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of resolution (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to give feedback (Once, Multiple Times).
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide feedback about the staff member, enter their name here.
* **Service Branch** (`service_branch`, text, optional): Enter the branch where you interacted with the service provider.
* **Customer Branch** (`customer_branch`, text, optional): Enter your branch or department (e.g., personal account, business account).
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **Postal Code** (`customer_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Resolution Method** (`resolution_method`, text, optional): Describe the resolution method used (e.g., phone, email, in-person).
* **Customer Occupation** (`customer_occupation`, text, optional): Enter your occupation or job title.
* **Age** (`customer_age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (`customer_gender`, select_one, required): Choose your gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, text, optional): Enter your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone, in-person).
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, text, optional): Enter the staff member's email address (if you'd like to contact them directly).
* **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`, text, optional): Enter the staff member's phone number (if you'd like to contact them directly).
