# Online Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and preferences from end-users regarding their experience with online learning courses. The responses will be used to optimize course content, format, and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback is valuable to us.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience with online learning courses.
3. For fields that allow multiple selections, choose all that apply.
4. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, provide them in the "Do you have any suggestions or feedback for our e-learning team?" field.
5. Enter your email and phone number if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates on our platform.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you use online courses?** (`course_experience`, `select_one`): This question asks about your frequency of using online courses. Please select the option that best represents your usage.
* **What difficulty level do you find the course difficulty?** (`difficulty_level`, `select_one`): This question inquires about your experience with the difficulty level of courses. Choose the option that best describes your perception.
* **What kind of content do you like in the course?** (`content_materials`, `select_multiple`): This question asks about your preferences regarding course content. Choose all that apply from the options provided.
* **How much time do you commit to your learning?** (`time_commitment`, `select_multiple`): This question is about the time you dedicate to learning. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Do you have any suggestions or feedback for our e-learning team?** (`feedback`, `note`): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any feedback or suggestions you may have.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): If you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates, please provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`): If you want to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as they will help us improve our platform.
* If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the "Do you have any suggestions or feedback for our e-learning team?" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact information].
