# Physiotherapy Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience with a physiotherapy service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal information and details about your experience with the physiotherapy service.
2. Answer each question according to your honest opinion.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the comments field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you if needed.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of your physiotherapy session.
* **Satisfaction Level**: Rate your level of satisfaction with the service you received. Choose from: Satisfied, Dissatisfied.
* **Service Quality**: Rate the quality of service you received. Choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Communication**: Rate how well you were communicated with by the physiotherapist. Choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service. Choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Physical Therapist**: Rate the skill and care of the physiotherapist. Choose from: Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Follow-up Care**: If you received any follow-up care or advice from the physiotherapist, select 'True'. Otherwise, select 'False'.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback about your experience, enter them here.

## Tips
* Be honest in your answers, as your feedback is valuable to us.
* If you're unsure about any question, just do your best to answer it according to your experience.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
