# neurodiversity_in_learning_environments_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

<thinking>

This form is designed to collect information from educators and support staff in learning environments about their experiences and challenges when supporting neurodivergent students. The goal is to gather data that can be used to improve support services and accommodations for neurodivergent students. Please note that the form is meant for people working in schools or universities and is focused on supporting students with neurodevelopmental disorders.

If you are a parent or guardian, you may find this form irrelevant. If you have already filled out this form in the past, you can skip it this time. 

</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of education you work in (primary, secondary, or higher).
2. Choose your role in the school or university (student, teacher, staff, or other).
3. Indicate how well you support neurodivergent students (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
4. Provide comments about supporting neurodivergent students.
5. Describe the accommodations and support you provide for neurodivergent students.
6. Add comments about accommodations and support for neurodivergent students.
7. Check if neurodivergent support services are available at your school or university.
8. Add comments about neurodivergent support services.
9. Share recommendations for improving support for neurodivergent students.
10. Add comments about recommendations for improvement.
11. Add any additional comments.
12. Enter your e-mail address (if you want to be contacted about your responses).
13. Enter your contact phone number (if you want to be contacted about your responses).
14. Enter any other contact information you'd like to provide.
15. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school or university you work in.
* **What category of education do you work in?** (`primary`, `secondary`, `higher`): Choose the category of education that best fits your role.
* **What is your role in the school or university?** (`student`, `teacher`, `staff`, `other`): Select your job title or position.
* **How well do you support neurodivergent students?** (`excellent`, `good`, `fair`, `poor`): Be honest about your experience in supporting neurodivergent students.
* **Comments about supporting neurodivergent students**: Share any comments or thoughts about how you support neurodivergent students.
* **What accommodations and support do you provide for neurodivergent students?**: Describe the accommodations and support you provide for neurodivergent students.
* **Comments about accommodations and support for neurodivergent students**: Add any additional comments about the accommodations and support you provide.
* **Are there any neurodivergent support services available at your school or university?** (`True`, `False`): Indicate if there are support services available for neurodivergent students.
* **Comments about neurodivergent support services**: Share any comments or thoughts about the support services available.
* **What recommendations do you have for improving support for neurodivergent students?**: Share any recommendations for improving support for neurodivergent students.
* **Comments about recommendations for improvement**: Add any additional comments about your recommendations.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your responses.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your responses.
* **Other Contact Information**: Enter any other contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
