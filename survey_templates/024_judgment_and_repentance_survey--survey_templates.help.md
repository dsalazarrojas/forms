# Judgment And Repentance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you explore your emotional response to your own behavior and actions. It will ask you questions about your feelings, thoughts, and behaviors related to remorse, repentance, and self-reflection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, being as honest and truthful as possible.
2. Select the correct response or answer for each question.
3. Enter any relevant notes or comments that you'd like to add to your responses.
4. Once you have completed all questions, click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro`, text, required): This is a text input field where you can briefly introduce yourself and provide some context for your responses.
* **How did you feel after committing the behavior?** (`reflection`, text, required): In this field, describe how you felt after the behavior in question. Be honest and detailed in your response.
* **What was the nature of your remorse after the behavior?** (`remorse_nature`, text, required): Describe the nature of your remorse, e.g., guilt, regret, or shame.
* **How did you respond to your remorse?** (`remorse_response`, select_one, required): Choose how you typically respond to feelings of remorse. You can select "Yes" if you tend to respond in a constructive way, or "No" if you tend to avoid or deny it.
* **How often do you experience feelings of remorse after your actions?** (`remorse_frequency`, text, required): Describe how often you experience remorse after your actions.
* **How strongly do you associate remorse with personal change?** (`remorse_strength`, number, required): Rate how strongly you associate remorse with personal growth and change.
* **What is your attitude towards self-reflection and introspection?** (`self_reflection_attitude`, text, required): Describe your attitude towards self-reflection and introspection.
* **What is the relationship between remorse and personal growth?** (`remorse_guilt`, text, required): Describe the relationship between remorse and personal growth.
* **How do you respond to feelings of remorse in others?** (`remorse_in_others`, select_one, required): Choose how you respond to feelings of remorse in others. You can select "Yes" if you empathize with them, or "No" if you tend to avoid or deny it.
* **How often do you experience feelings of guilt after your actions?** (`guilt_frequency`, text, required): Describe how often you experience feelings of guilt after your actions.
* **What is the value of remorse in personal and social relationships?** (`remorse_intensity`, text, required): Describe the value you place on remorse in your personal and social relationships.
* **How do you measure the intensity of remorse?** (`remorse_measurement`, number, required): Rate the intensity of remorse using numbers.
* **How frequently do you repent for past actions?** (`repentance_frequency`, text, required): Describe how frequently you repent for past actions.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Choose "Submit" to complete the form, or select "Other" to save and continue later. If you select "Submit", please enter the submission date and time in the next fields.
* **Submission Date** (`submission`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you completed the form.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to include with your responses.
