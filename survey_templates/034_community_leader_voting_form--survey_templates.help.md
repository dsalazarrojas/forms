# Community Leader Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from community members who are voting for their leaders, including their identification, eligibility, and voting preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your full legal name in the "Full Legal Name" field.
3. Enter your voter registration ID or membership number in the "Voter Registration ID or Membership Number" field.
4. Confirm your eligibility by selecting "I Confirm Eligibility" in the "Eligibility Confirmation" field.
5. Select one candidate for each leadership position (President, Treasurer, Secretary, and Youth Representative).
6. Select the most important issue for the new leadership from the list of options.
7. Sign your name electronically by typing it in the "Electronic Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* 	**Voter Registration ID or Membership Number**: If you have a membership number or ID, enter it here. This helps the community association verify your membership status.
* **Eligibility Confirmation**: Confirm that you are a registered member in good standing. You can select "I Confirm Eligibility" or choose "Other" if you have a valid reason for not being eligible.
* 	**Candidate for Community Board President**: Select one of the available candidates or choose "Write-in Candidate" and provide a name in the "Write-in Candidate for President" field.
* 	**Write-in Candidate for President**: If you selected "Write-in Candidate" above, enter the name of the person you would like to vote for.
* 	**Candidate for Community Treasurer**: Select one of the available candidates or choose "Write-in Candidate" and provide a name in the "Write-in Candidate for Treasurer" field.
* 	**Candidate for Community Secretary**: Select one of the available candidates or choose "Write-in Candidate" and provide a name in the "Write-in Candidate for Secretary" field.
* 	**Candidate for Youth Representative**: Select one of the available candidates or choose "Write-in Candidate" and provide a name in the "Write-in Candidate for Youth Representative" field.
* 	**Most Important Issue**: Select the most important issue for the new leadership from the list of options.
* 	**Electronic Signature**: Type your name to verify your vote.
* 	**Date of Election Submission**: Enter the date you are submitting this form, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your vote is counted.
* If you selected "Write-in Candidate" for a leadership position, provide a valid name in the corresponding field.
