# Continuing Education Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with the course, evaluating its content, instructor, and overall quality. The feedback will help us improve our educational offerings and better serve our learners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the introduction on the first page, which explains the purpose of the form and encourages users to share their feedback.
2. On the next page, provide the title of the course you are evaluating.
3. Enter the name of the instructor who delivered the course.
4. Proceed to the ratings section, where you will rate the course content, instructor effectiveness, and materials provided.
5. Choose your ratings using the provided scale (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree).
6. In the "Detailed Feedback" section, describe one key concept or skill you learned from the course.
7. Optionally, suggest an improvement for us to consider.
8. Rate your experience with the learning platform (LMS) used for the course.
9. Decide if you would recommend the course to a colleague.
10. Leave any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" section.
11. Finally, enter the date you are providing the feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Course Evaluation --**: Introduces the purpose of the form and encourages users to share their feedback.
* **Course Title**: Enter the title of the course you are evaluating, which will help us understand the specific course being assessed.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor who delivered the course.
* **-- Content and Delivery Ratings --**: Evaluate the course content, instructor effectiveness, and materials provided using the provided scale.
	+ **The course content was relevant to my professional goals**: Rate the relevance of the course content to your professional goals.
	+ **The instructor was knowledgeable and effective**: Rate the instructor's teaching skills and presentation.
	+ **The materials and resources provided were helpful**: Rate the quality of the materials and resources used in the course.
* **-- Detailed Feedback --**: Describe one key concept or skill you learned from the course.
* **What was the most valuable part of this course**: Describe a valuable aspect of the course.
* **What is one thing that could be improved**: Suggest an improvement for the course.
* **Rate your experience with the learning platform (LMS)**: Evaluate your experience with the platform used for the course.
* **Would you recommend this course to a colleague**: Decide if you would recommend the course to a colleague.
* **Any additional comments or feedback**: Leave any additional comments or feedback about the course.
* **Date of Feedback**: Enter the date you are providing the feedback.
* **Overall Rating of the Program (0-10)**: Rate the overall quality of the program.
