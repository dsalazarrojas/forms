# Cross Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on their cross-training experiences. The data collected will help trainers and program managers to understand the effectiveness and areas for improvement of the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.
2. Please fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. You can add any additional comments or feedback in the "Feedback" field.
4. Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send it to the form owner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, **optional**): Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the training program you attended.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, **optional**): Share any additional comments or feedback about the program.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when filling in the fields.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, use the "Feedback" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
