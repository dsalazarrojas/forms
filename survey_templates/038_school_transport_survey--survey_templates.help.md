# School Transport Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Transport Survey is designed to gather information about school transport, including questions about transportation options, frequencies, and incidents. The survey aims to understand student and parent experiences with school transportation, helping the school administration make informed decisions about transportation services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information.
2. Answer all questions truthfully, and don't leave any fields blank unless specified as optional.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
4. If you encounter any issues or have trouble completing the form, reach out to the school administration for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_transport_survey_1_label** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments or observations you have about the school transportation.
* **school_transport_survey_2_label** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of times you use the school transportation service in a week. This will help us understand the usage frequency.
* **school_transport_survey_3_label** (Number, Optional): Enter the time it takes for the school transportation service to arrive at your stop. This will help us understand the service duration.
* **school_transport_survey_4_label** (Select One, Optional): Have you ever had a positive experience with the school transportation service?
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **school_transport_survey_5_label** (Select Multiple, Optional): Which of the following services have you used?
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **school_transport_survey_6_label** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the school transportation.
* **school_transport_survey_7_label** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the latest incident you experienced with the school transportation service.
* **school_transport_survey_8_label** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the latest incident you experienced with the school transportation service.
* **school_transport_survey_9_label** (Text, Optional): Enter any details about the latest incident you experienced with the school transportation service.
* **school_transport_survey_10_label** (Text, Optional): Enter any other information about the school transportation service.
