# Zoom Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoom Event Feedback Survey is designed to gather information about your experience at a Zoom event. This survey aims to collect your thoughts, opinions, and ratings to help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand the questions being asked.
2. Choose the answer options that best describe your experience.
3. Enter any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide.
4. Fill in any required fields, such as your participant name and email (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience** (`Overall Experience`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one of the options that best describe your overall experience at the Zoom event: Very Good, Good, or Bad.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional):
	+ This field is currently not being used for this form; however, it is provided for future reference. You may leave it blank.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional):
	+ Enter any comments or feedback you'd like to provide about your experience. This is a free-text field where you can express your thoughts.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your participant name as it appears on your participant list or registration.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address if you wish to receive any follow-up communication or surveys about the event.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional):
	+ You may enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted directly. This is not required.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional):
	+ You don't need to fill this field unless specifically required or requested by the event organizers. Enter the date on which you attended the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional):
	+ Like the date field, this is not required unless specified otherwise. Enter the time you attended the event, if needed.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional):
	+ Similar to the date field, this is not required. Enter the date of the event, if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields, especially your participant name and email, if applicable.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact the event organizers for support.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and helps us improve future events.
