# Negligence Law Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help evaluate an organization's compliance with laws related to negligence. It is not intended for use in legal proceedings or as a substitute for professional legal advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read each question carefully and select the most appropriate answer based on your organization's policies and procedures.
*   Complete each question in its entirety, even if you are unsure of the answer. This will help ensure that you are providing accurate information and allowing for a comprehensive evaluation.
*   If you are unsure of any answer, you can select "Not Sure" as it will still allow for a thorough evaluation.
