# Coffee Shop Customer Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Coffee Shop Customer Experience Survey, a form designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience at the coffee shop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the page with the corresponding section header that best fits your experience. For example, if you want to provide feedback about the customer service, select the page with the "Service Experience" header.
2. Fill out the fields in each page according to your experience. For each field, select the option that best describes your experience.
3. When you are done filling out the form, submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Customer Information --** (`section_customer_info`): Please fill out this section with your basic information, if required.
* **Date of Your Visit** (`visit_date`): Please select the date of your visit to the coffee shop.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`): Select the time of your visit from the available options.
* **How often do you visit our coffee shop?** (`frequency`): Choose how often you visit the coffee shop.
* **-- Service Experience --** (`section_service`): Please review the service experience section.
	+ **Wait time at counter** (`wait_time`): Rate the wait time at the counter from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Too long" and 5 is "Very quick".
	+ **Staff friendliness and courtesy** (`staff_friendliness`): Select the level of staff friendliness and courtesy from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Unfriendly" and 5 is "Very friendly".
	+ **Staff knowledge about menu items** (`staff_knowledge`): Choose the level of staff knowledge about menu items from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Lacking" and 5 is "Expert".
	+ **Order was made correctly** (`order_accuracy`): Rate the accuracy of your order from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Wrong order" and 5 is "Perfect".
* **-- Product Quality --** (`section_product`): Please review the product quality section.
	+ **Quality of coffee** (`coffee_quality`): Select the quality of the coffee from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
	+ **Temperature of your drink** (`drink_temperature`): Choose the temperature of your drink from the available options.
	+ **Quality of food items** (`food_quality`): Rate the quality of food items, if you purchased any, from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
	+ **Value for money** (`value_for_money`): Choose the value for money from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor value" and 5 is "Excellent value".
* **-- Atmosphere and Ambiance --** (`section_atmosphere`): Please review the atmosphere and ambiance section.
	+ **Cleanliness of the shop** (`cleanliness`): Rate the cleanliness of the shop from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Dirty" and 5 is "Very clean".
	+ **Noise level** (`noise_level`): Select the noise level from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Too quiet" and 5 is "Too loud".
	+ **Seating comfort** (`seating_comfort`): Choose the seating comfort from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very uncomfortable" and 5 is "Very comfortable".
* **-- Recommendation and Loyalty --** (`section_likelihood`): Please review the recommendation and loyalty section.
	+ **Would you recommend this coffee shop?** (`recommend`): Select whether you would recommend this coffee shop from 1 to 5, where 1 is "No, definitely not" and 5 is "Yes, definitely".
	+ **Likelihood of returning** (`likelihood_return`): Rate your likelihood of returning from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very unlikely" and 5 is "Very likely".
* **-- Feedback --** (`section_feedback`): Please provide any further feedback or suggestions.
	+ **What did you like most about your visit?** (`what_liked_most`): Share what you liked most about your visit.
	+ **Areas for improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`): Suggest any areas for improvement.
	+ **Suggestions for new menu items** (`new_items`): Suggest new menu items you'd like to see.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
