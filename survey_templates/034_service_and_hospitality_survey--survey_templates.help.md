# Service And Hospitality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow service businesses to collect guest feedback using a drag-and-drop interface and no-code form builder. The form template helps organize form submission data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category for your survey template from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter a description of your survey template to provide context and details.
3. Review the description and make sure it accurately reflects your survey template.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Templates** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Choose the category for your survey template from the dropdown menu. You can select from two options: "Survey Templates" or "Business Surveys".
* **The Service and Hospitality Survey form template from Jotform helps service businesses collect guest feedback with a drag-and-drop interface, no-code form builder, and organized form submission data collection.** (`description`, text, required: false): This field is a text area where you can enter a description of your survey template. 
* **None** (`description_helper`, note, required: false): This field is for additional notes or comments, but is currently left blank.
