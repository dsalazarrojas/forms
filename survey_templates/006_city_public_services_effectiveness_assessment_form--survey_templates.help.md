# City Public Services Effectiveness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from residents on the effectiveness of various city public services. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name and neighborhood information.
2. Choose how long you've lived in the city.
3. Rate the effectiveness of each service (police, fire, public works, parks and recreation, waste management, transportation, library, and public health services) on a scale of 1-5.
4. If you have specific experiences or suggestions about each service, please provide detailed feedback.
5. If you could rate the response time for city services, choose from the options provided.
6. Evaluate the effectiveness of city communication about services.
7. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name.
* **Neighborhood** (`neighborhood`, text, required): Enter the name of your neighborhood or area of the city where you reside.
* **How long have you lived in this city** (`years_resident`, select_one, required): Choose how long you've lived in the city: Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, or Over 10 years.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for follow-up if needed.
* **Police Services - Effectiveness Rating** (`police_services`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of police services on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not effective and 5 is very effective.
* **Fire Services - Effectiveness Rating** (`fire_services`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of fire services on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not effective and 5 is very effective.
* **Public Works and Maintenance - Effectiveness Rating** (`public_works`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of public works and maintenance services on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not effective and 5 is very effective.
* **Parks and Recreation - Effectiveness Rating** (`parks_recreation`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of parks and recreation services on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not effective and 5 is very effective.
* **Waste Management and Recycling - Effectiveness Rating** (`waste_management`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of waste management and recycling services on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not effective and 5 is very effective.
* **Transportation Services - Effectiveness Rating** (`transportation`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of transportation services on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not effective and 5 is very effective.
* **Library Services - Effectiveness Rating** (`library_services`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of library services on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not effective and 5 is very effective.
* **Public Health Services - Effectiveness Rating** (`health_services`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of public health services on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not effective and 5 is very effective.
* **Which service are you most satisfied with and why** (`most_satisfied`, text, optional): Provide feedback on which service you're most satisfied with, including why.
* **Which service needs the most improvement and why** (`least_satisfied`, text, optional): Provide feedback on which service you think needs the most improvement, including why.
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, select_one, optional): Evaluate the response time for city services: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **City Communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Rate the effectiveness of city communication: Very effective, Effective, Somewhat effective, or Not effective.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have.
