# Auditee Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from auditees about their experience with an audit. The goal is to help the audit team improve their performance and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your name (optional) and email address (optional) if you want to provide them for follow-up purposes.
2. Answer all the questions honestly, choosing the corresponding ratings and select options.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you need.
4. Once you have filled out all the questions, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Your name will help us keep track of your responses and follow up with you if needed. If you prefer to remain anonymous, you can leave this blank.
* **Email Address**: Your email address will help us contact you for further questions or follow-ups. If you're concerned about sharing your email address, you can leave it blank.
* **Department**: Please enter the department that was audited.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Survey Date**: Please enter the date of this survey.
* **Audit Name**: Enter the name of the audit you experienced.
* **Audit Start Date**: Enter the date when the audit began.
* **Audit End Date**: Enter the date when the audit ended.
* **Audit Team Professionalism**: Rate the professionalism of the audit team (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Auditor Knowledge**: Rate the knowledge and expertise of the auditors (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication Quality**: Rate the quality of communication from the auditors (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Respect Shown**: Rate how respectfully you were treated (Very respectfully, Respectfully, Neutral, Disrespectfully, Very disrespectfully).
* **Audit Disruption**: Rate how disruptive the audit was to operations (Not disruptive at all, Minimally disruptive, Moderately disruptive, Very disruptive, Extremely disruptive).
* **Resource Requirements**: Rate how reasonable the resource requirements were (Very reasonable, Reasonable, Neutral, Unreasonable, Very unreasonable).
* **Timeline Reasonable**: Rate whether the audit timeline was reasonable (Very reasonable, Reasonable, Neutral, Unreasonable, Very unreasonable).
* **Findings Fairness**: Rate the fairness of the audit findings (Very fair, Fair, Neutral, Unfair, Very unfair).
* **Recommendations Usefulness**: Rate the usefulness of the recommendations (Very useful, Useful, Neutral, Not useful, Not useful at all).
* **Value Added**: Rate whether the audit added value to your department (Significant value, Moderate value, Some value, Little value, No value).
* **Would Cooperate Again**: Rate whether you would cooperate with this audit team again (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Neutral, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **Improvement Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions for improvement (open-ended text field).
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments you may have (open-ended text field).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the audit (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your answers.
* Provide as much detail as you need for open-ended questions.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to participate in this survey!
