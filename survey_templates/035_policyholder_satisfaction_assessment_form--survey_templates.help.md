# Policyholder Satisfaction Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and satisfaction data from policyholders to help us improve our services and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form to understand what each question is asking.
2. For select_one fields, choose one of the provided options that best fits your answer.
3. For select_multiple fields, choose multiple options that best fit your answer.
4. For select_one fields with numbers, use the corresponding option (e.g., "Excellent" for 1-4).
5. For the date field, select the date you have been a policyholder.
6. For the time field, select the time you have been using our services.
7. For the email field, enter your email address.
8. For the phone number field, enter your phone number.
9. For the feedback field, provide any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your overall satisfaction with our service quality?** (`service_quality`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the provided options to describe your overall experience with our service quality. Choose "Excellent" for a very positive experience.
* **How do you rate our communication during the policyholder journey?** (`communication`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple options that best fit your experience with our communication.
* **What is your overall policyholder experience?** (`policyholder_experience`, number, required: false): This field is optional. If you'd like to provide a numerical representation of your experience with our services.
* **What level of satisfaction do you currently have with our service?** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the provided options to describe your level of satisfaction with our service.
* **How much time do you spend on our services?** (`time_spent`, time, required: false): This field is optional. If you'd like to provide an estimate of the time you spend on our services.
* **Which communication channel do you prefer to use for contacting us?** (`communication_channel`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the provided options as your preferred communication channel.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you have a contact person assigned to your account. If you don't have a contact person, select "No".
* **Do you have any feedback for us?** (`feedback`, note, required: false): This field is optional. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide it here.
* **Your Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Your Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date you have been a policyholder.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select the time you have been using our services.
* **How long have you been our policyholder?** (`policyholder_since`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the provided options to describe how long you have been our policyholder.
* **What would be the reason for not being satisfied?** (`reason_not_satisfied`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple options that best fit the reason for not being satisfied with our services.
* **How likely are you to renew our policy?** (`policyholder_renewal`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the provided options to describe your likelihood of renewing our policy.
* **Would you recommend our policy?** (`policyholder_recommend`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the provided options to describe whether you would recommend our policy.
* **Are you satisfied with our service?** (`policyholder_satisfied`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the provided options to describe your level of satisfaction with our service.
* **How likely are you to refer our policy to others?** (`policyholder_referrals`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the provided options to describe your likelihood of referring our policy to others.
* **How satisfied are you with our policyholders?** (`policyholder_satisfaction_level`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the provided options to describe your level of satisfaction with our policyholders.
* **How likely are you to refer our policy to others?** (`policyholder_referrals_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the provided options to describe your likelihood of referring our policy to others.
* **What is your overall satisfaction score with our policyholders?** (`policyholder_satisfaction_score`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the provided options to describe your overall satisfaction score with our policyholders.
* **What is your level of satisfaction score with our policyholders?** (`policyholder_satisfaction_score_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the provided options to describe your level of satisfaction score with our policyholders.
* **How would you rate our policy service quality?** (`policyholder_service_quality`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the provided options to describe your experience with our policy service quality.
* **What is your level of policy service quality?** (`policyholder_service_quality_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the provided options to describe your level of policy service quality.
