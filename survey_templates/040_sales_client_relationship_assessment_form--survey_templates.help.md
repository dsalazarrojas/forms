# Sales Client Relationship Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the quality of your relationship with a client, helping you to evaluate and rate key aspects of your interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the client's file and ensure you have a good understanding of their relationship.
2. Answer each question honestly, selecting the best rating for your experience with this client.
3. Fill in any additional comments or notes that you believe are relevant to the relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Page 1): This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **What kind of relationship do you have with this client?** (Page 2, client_assessment_1):
	+ Select one or more of the following options:
		- Very Strong
		- Strong
		- Fair
		- Weak
* **How would you rate the quality of the communication with this client?** (Page 2, client_assessment_2):
	+ Choose one of the following options:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
* **How would you rate the speed of issue resolution with this client?** (Page 2, client_assessment_3):
	+ Select one or more of the following options:
		- Less than 1 day
		- 1-3 days
		- 4-7 days
		- 1-2 weeks
		- More than 2 weeks
* **How would you rate the level of effort required to resolve issues with this client?** (Page 2, client_assessment_4):
	+ Choose one or more of the following options:
		- Low
		- Moderate
		- High
* **How would you rate the level of trust you have with this client?** (Page 2, client_assessment_5):
	+ Select one or more of the following options:
		- High
		- Moderate
		- Low
* **Client Feedback** (Page 3, client_feedback):
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback about your relationship with this client.
* **Client Satisfaction** (Page 3, client_satisfaction):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the client on a scale of 1-5.
* **Relationship Notes** (Page 3, relationship_notes):
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about your relationship with this client.

## Tips
* Ensure you only complete this form for clients with whom you have a relationship.
* Avoid duplicating assessments for the same client.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to your manager or support team.
