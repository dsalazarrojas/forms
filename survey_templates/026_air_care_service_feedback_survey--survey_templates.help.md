# Air Care Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the air care service experience, including service details, technician performance, and areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Provide Service Details
Provide general information about the service visit, including the service details and date of service.

### Step 2: Rate Your Experience
Rate your experience with the provided services, including timeliness, professionalism, quality of work, cleanliness, and whether your issue was resolved.

### Step 3: Add Additional Feedback
Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, optional)
Enter your name to help us identify you in our records.

### 2. **Date of Service** (`Date of Service`, date, required)
Enter the date of the service visit.

### 3. **Technician Name** (`Technician Name`, text, optional)
If you know the name of the technician who performed the service, you can enter it here.

### 4. **Type of Service** (`Type of Service`, select_one, required)
Select the type of service you received, such as Routine Maintenance / Tune-up or Emergency Repair.

### 5. **Service Ratings** (`Service Ratings`, note, optional)
This section provides space for you to provide ratings for your experience.

### 6. **Timeliness** (`Timeliness`, select_one, required)
Rate how on time the technician arrived for the service visit.

### 7. **Professionalism** (`Professionalism`, select_one, required)
Rate how courteous and professional the technician was during the visit.

### 8. **Quality of Work** (`Quality of Work`, select_one, required)
Rate the quality of the work performed by the technician.

### 9. **Cleanliness** (`Cleanliness`, select_one, required)
Rate the cleanliness of the work area after the service visit.

### 10. **Was your issue resolved?** (`Was your issue resolved?`, select_one, required)
Indicate whether your issue was fully resolved or not.

### 11. **Likelihood to Recommend** (`Likelihood to Recommend`, number, required)
Rate how likely you are to recommend our services to a friend or family member.

### 12. **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional)
Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
