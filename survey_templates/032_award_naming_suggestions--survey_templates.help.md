# Award Naming Suggestions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect suggestions and ideas for a new name for an award. The award being renamed will be updated to reflect the new name, so it's essential to provide clear and concise suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing your full name and email address. This information will be used to contact you for further questions or discussion.
2.  Choose your affiliation type, which will help us understand your relationship with the organization.
3.  Provide information about the award being renamed, including the current category and name.
4.  Offer your suggestions for a new name for the award, including a rationale for each suggestion.
5.  Select the key attributes that the new name should reflect, such as innovation, leadership, or service.
6.  Choose the tone you prefer for the new name.
7.  Let us know if you are willing to join the naming committee to help make the final decision.
8.  Finally, confirm that your suggestions are original and certify that you are providing honest and unbiased ideas.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`contributor_name`, required): This is your full name that will be used to contact you.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): This is the email address we will use to contact you for follow-up questions or discussion.
*   **Affiliation** (`affiliation_type`, required): Choose your relationship with the organization, such as Staff/Employee, Organization Member, Partner/Sponsor, or Member of the Public.
*   **Current Award Category** (`award_category_context`, required): Provide the current category of the award.
*   **Current Award Name** (`current_name`, optional): Enter the current name of the award.
*   **Suggested New Award Name 1** (`suggestion_1_name`, required): Provide your primary suggestion for a new name.
*   **Rationale for Suggestion 1** (`suggestion_1_rationale`, required): Explain why your suggested name fits the award.
*   **Suggested New Award Name 2** (`suggestion_2_name`, optional): Provide an alternate suggestion for a new name.
*   **Rationale for Suggestion 2** (`suggestion_2_rationale`, optional): Explain why your alternate suggested name fits the award.
*   **Key Attributes** (`attribute_reflection`, required): Select all the key attributes that the new name should reflect.
*   **Preferred Name Tone** (`preferred_naming_tone`, required): Choose the tone you prefer for the new name, such as Formal and Authoritative, Modern and Catchy, Creative and Abstract, or Traditional and Prestigious.
*   **Join the Naming Committee** (`committee_interest`, required): Let us know if you are willing to join the committee to make the final decision.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_suggestions`, optional): Provide any other thoughts or ideas about the naming process.
*   **Certify Originality** (`submission_confirmation`, required): Confirm that your suggestions are original and honest.
