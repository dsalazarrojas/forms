# patient_generated_health_data_trust_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on an individual's trust in healthcare data, their concerns and preferences, and their frequency of sharing health data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best fit your answers to each question. Most questions have multiple options, so select all that apply. You can also type in answers for the questions that allow text responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your level of trust in healthcare** (`trust_in_health_data`, `text`, optional): This question asks about your general level of trust in healthcare data. You can describe your feelings and thoughts about this in the text response field.
* **How do you feel about sharing health data** (`health_data_sharing`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks how you feel about sharing your health data. Select the answer that best fits your feelings, such as "Strongly Agree", "Somewhat Agree", "Not Sure", "Disagree", or "Strongly Disagree".
* **What benefits do you see in sharing health data** (`data_sharing_benefits`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about the benefits you see in sharing health data. Select all that apply from the list, including "Improved treatment", "Increased access to care", "Other (please specify)".
* **What risks do you see in sharing health data** (`data_sharing_risks`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about the risks you see in sharing health data. Select all that apply from the list, including "Loss of control", "Identity theft", "Financial loss", "Other (please specify)".
* **What is your understanding of data security** (`health_data_security`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your understanding of data security. Select from the options "Very secure", "Somewhat secure", "Not secure", or "Not sure".
* **What do you think about data sharing policies** (`health_data_sharing_policies`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your opinion on data sharing policies. Select from the options "Good", "Fair", "Bad", "Very bad", or "Not sure".
* **How often do you share health data** (`data_sharing_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your frequency of sharing health data. Select the answer that best fits your habits, such as "Frequently", "Occasionally", "Rarely", or "Never".
* **What concerns do you have about sharing health data** (`data_sharing_concerns`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about your concerns about sharing health data. Select all that apply from the list, including "Confidentiality", "Security", "Privacy", "Other (please specify)".
* **Who should control health data** (`patient_control_over_data`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about who you think should control health data. Select from the options "Patients", "Doctors", "Hospitals", or "Other (please specify)".
* **How do you like to share health data** (`data_sharing_preferences`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your preferences for sharing health data. Select the answer that best fits your habits, such as "Directly", "Indirectly", or "Neither".
* **What channels do you prefer for health data sharing** (`health_data_sharing_channels`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about your preferred channels for sharing health data. Select all that apply from the list, including "Online", "In-person", "Phone", "Text", "Email", or "Other (please specify)".
* **How often do you share health data** (`health_data_sharing_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your frequency of sharing health data. Select the answer that best fits your habits, such as "Frequently", "Occasionally", "Rarely", or "Never".
* **Would you use patient-generated data for healthcare** (`patient_generated_data`, `text`, optional): This question asks about your willingness to use patient-generated data in healthcare. You can describe your feelings and thoughts about this in the text response field.
* **How often do you share patient-generated health data** (`patient_generated_data_frequencies`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about your frequency of sharing patient-generated health data. Select all that apply from the list, including "Frequently", "Occasionally", "Rarely", or "Never".
* **What is the purpose of patient-generated health data** (`patient_generated_data_purpose`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about the purpose of patient-generated health data. Select all that apply from the list, including "Health monitoring", "Health tracking", "Health improvement", or "Other (please specify)".

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions. Your input will help improve healthcare data sharing practices.
* Remember, all answers are optional, so feel free to skip or leave questions blank if you're not comfortable answering them.
* If you're unsure about a question, select the answer that best fits your feelings or habits.
* If you have any other concerns or preferences about sharing health data, you can mention them in the "Other (please specify)" field.
