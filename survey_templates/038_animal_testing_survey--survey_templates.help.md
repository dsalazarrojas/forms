# Animal Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from individuals about their knowledge, awareness, and opinions on animal testing practices. The survey aims to understand respondents' stances on animal testing, their awareness of alternatives to animal testing, and their involvement in animal advocacy. The results of this survey will help researchers and organizations better understand the public's perception of animal testing and its implications on animal welfare.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each question from the available choices.
2. Fill in the additional comments or thoughts in the text field if you want to provide more information.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Tips

* Please take your time to answer each question carefully.
* Remember that there are no right or wrong answers, this survey aims to understand your perspectives and opinions.
* If you are unsure about a question, select 'Other' or 'Unsure' as your response.
* You can add any additional comments or thoughts in the text field at the end of the form.
