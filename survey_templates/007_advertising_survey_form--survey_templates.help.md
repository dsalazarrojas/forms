# Advertising Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather insights from consumers about our advertising efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the form carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Select the options that best fit your experiences with our ads.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to use the text field at the end of the form.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked with *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Consumer Insights --**: This is an introduction to the form and a hint about what we are trying to learn from you.
* **Have you seen our ads recently?* (`ad_recall`, `select_one`, required: true): This question helps us understand if you've recently interacted with our ads. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Where did you see our ad?* (`ad_source`, `select_one`, required: false): This question helps us know where you saw our ad. Select the primary location where you saw our ad.
* **Was the message of the ad clear?* (`message_clarity`, `select_one`, required: true): This question helps us understand if our message was clear and concise. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **How relevant was the ad to you?* (`relevance`, `select_one`, required: true): This question helps us know how relevant our ad was to you. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Did the ad make you want to learn more?* (`engagement`, `select_one`, required: true): This question helps us understand if our ad piqued your interest. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **How did the ad change your perception of our brand?* (`brand_perception`, `select_one`, required: false): This question helps us know if our ad changed your opinion about our brand. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **What did you like or dislike about the ad?* (`feedback_text`, `text`, required: false): This text field is for any additional comments or feedback you might have about our ad.
* **-- About You --**: This is a divider to separate the questions about the ad from the demographic information.
* **Age Group* (`age_group`, `select_one`, required: false): This question helps us know your age group. Select the option that best describes your age.
* **Gender* (`gender`, `select_one`, required: false): This question helps us understand your gender identity. Choose the option that best describes you.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* If you're unsure about any question, choose the option that best fits your experience.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, use the text field to share them.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
