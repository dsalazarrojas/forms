# tech_startup_app_feature_interest_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `tech_startup_app_feature_interest_poll` form is a survey template designed to gather information from tech startups about their interest in various features. This form helps us understand the needs and preferences of tech startups, which enables us to provide better solutions for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Introduction** page, which provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
2. On the **Company Information** page, enter your company's details, including name, website, and size (small, medium, or large).
3. Next, select the type of company you represent (e.g., Tech Startups, E-commerce, Software Development, etc.).
4. Choose the stage of your company (e.g., Startup, Series A, Series B, etc.).
5. On the **Contact** page, enter your contact information, including email, phone number, job title, and name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Page 1)
* **Introduction**: This page provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.

### Company Information (Page 2)
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name.
* **Company Website**: Enter your company's website URL.
* **Company Size**: Select the size of your company (small, medium, or large).
* **Company Type**: Select the type of company you represent (e.g., Tech Startups, E-commerce, Software Development, etc.).
* **Company Stage**: Select the stage of your company (e.g., Startup, Series A, Series B, etc.).

### Contact (Page 3)
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling out the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* This form is designed to be completed by tech startups, so please only fill it out if you represent a company in this industry.
