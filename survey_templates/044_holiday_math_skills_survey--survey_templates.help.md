# Holiday Math Skills Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess holiday makers' math skills, specifically their confidence in their math abilities and their practice with different math topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions in the form according to your math skills and experience.
2.  Select the math topics you have practiced or are confident with.
3.  Respond to the question about your confidence in your math abilities.
4.  If you have any additional challenges or difficulties with math, provide more information in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Math Practice** (`holiday_math_skills_survey_math_practice`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the math topics you have practiced recently. Select all that apply.
    +   Multiplication
    +   Division
    +   Addition
    +   Subtraction
    +   Fractions
    +   Decimals
    +   Percentages
    +   Geometric sequences
    +   Algebra
    +   Geometry
    +   Calculus
*   **Confidence** (`holiday_math_skills_survey_confidence`, select_one, optional): Choose your level of confidence in your math abilities.
    +   Yes
    +   No
*   **Challenges** (`holiday_math_skills_survey_challenges`, text, optional): If you have any additional math challenges or difficulties, describe them here.
*   **Additional Challenges** (`holiday_math_skills_survey_additional_challenges`, text, optional): If you have any other math-related difficulties or challenges, provide more information here.
*   **Title** (`holiday_math_skills_survey_title`, text, optional): Enter a title to describe the survey.
*   **Description** (`holiday_math_skills_survey_description`, note, optional): Enter a description for the survey, if needed.
