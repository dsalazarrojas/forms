# Best Online Service Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to nominate their favorite online services for recognition and reward. The form collects information about the service, including its official name, website URL, category, and user experience. The form also asks nominators to rate the service's performance in various areas and provide a brief description of their experience with the service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your profile information, including your name and email address.
2. Enter the official name of the online service being nominated.
3. Provide the URL for the service's website or app.
4. Select the primary category of the service (e.g. E-commerce, Software as a Service, etc.).
5. Rate the service's performance in several areas, including User Experience, Customer Support, Security, and Value for Money.
6. Describe how the service has improved your daily life or business.
7. Highlight a single key strength of the service.
8. Summarize your nomination argument.
9. Optionally, add any additional comments or feedback.
10. Provide your recommendation to others.
10. Enter the date of your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`nominator_name_input`): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the nomination recognition.
* **Your Email Address** (`nominator_email_input`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Online Service Name** (`service_name_text`): Enter the official name of the digital service being nominated.
* **Website or App URL** (`service_website_url`): Enter the URL for the service's website or app.
* **Service Category** (`service_category_select`): Select the primary category of the service (e.g. E-commerce, Software as a Service, etc.).
* **User Experience and Interface Design** (`user_experience_rating`): Rate the service's user experience and interface design, from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Customer Support and Communication** (`customer_support_rating`): Rate the service's customer support and communication, from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Security and Privacy Features** (`security_privacy_rating`): Rate the service's security and privacy features, from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Value for Money and Free Tier Quality** (`value_for_money_rating`): Rate the service's value for money and free tier quality, from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Innovation and Feature Set** (`innovation_features_rating`): Rate the service's innovation and feature set, from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **How this Service Improved Your Daily Life or Business** (`service_impact_desc`): Describe how the service has improved your daily life or business.
* **Single Best Thing About this Service** (`single_best_feature_desc`): Highlight a single key strength of the service.
* **Why Should this Service Win the Award** (`win_justification_detailed`): Summarize your nomination argument.
* **Would You Recommend this Service to Others** (`recommendation_intent`): Select whether you would recommend the service to others.
* **Any Other Comments or Feedback** (`final_additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Date of Nomination** (`nomination_date_timestamp`): Enter the date of your nomination.
