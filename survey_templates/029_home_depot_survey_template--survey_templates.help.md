# Home Depot Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic contact information from customers, enabling us to respond to their inquiries efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out the form with your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **home_depot_survey_template** (`note`, required/optional: not applicable):
This page serves as an introduction to the form, and is not a field to be filled out. It is here to provide a brief description of the form's purpose.
- **customer_name** (`text`, required/optional: no):
Please enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification. This will help us better understand who the inquiry belongs to.
- **contact_email** (`email`, required/optional: no):
Enter the customer's email address where they can be reached for further communication.
- **contact_phone** (`text`, required/optional: no):
Enter the customer's phone number where they can be reached for further communication.
