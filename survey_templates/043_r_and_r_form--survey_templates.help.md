R And R Form - Help Guide
======================
## Purpose
The R And R Form is used to collect information for a nomination process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the second name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone number field.
5. Select a date for the nomination in the nomination date field.
6. Enter a name for the award in the award name field.
7. Enter a description of the nominee in the description field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **last name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **phone number** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **nomination date** (date, required: false): Select a date for the nomination.
* **award name** (text, required: false): Enter a name for the award.
* **description** (note, required: false): Enter a brief description of the nominee.
