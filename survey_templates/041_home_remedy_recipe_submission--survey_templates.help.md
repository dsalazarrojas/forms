# Home Remedy Recipe Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect home remedy recipes and tips from users. It is meant to be a platform for users to share their personal recipes and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by filling in your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Next, provide the name of the remedy you are submitting.
3. Enter the ingredients needed for the remedy.
4. Describe the steps or directions for making the remedy.
5. Decide whether to upload photos or videos to support your submission.
6. If uploading photos or videos, select "Yes" to the "Photos" field.
7. If uploading photos, enter the file path for the uploaded file in the "Upload Photos" field.
8. Finally, type a brief review of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name, which will be publicly visible on our platform.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name, which will be publicly visible on our platform.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your submission.
* **Remedy Name**: Enter a name for your home remedy recipe.
* **Remedy Ingredients**: List the ingredients needed to make your remedy.
* **Remedy Directions**: Describe the steps or process for making your remedy.
* **Photos/Video Upload**: Decide whether or not to upload photos or videos to support your submission.
* **Upload Photos**: If you selected to upload photos, enter the file path for the uploaded file.
* **Review**: Type a brief review or description of your submission.
