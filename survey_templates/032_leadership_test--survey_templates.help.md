# leadership_test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership_test form is a survey that aims to understand people's perceptions of leadership, its characteristics, and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Please read the introduction page, which explains the purpose of the form.
2. Answer the questions on the subsequent pages by typing your responses in the text boxes provided.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please review the introduction page for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent** (Select One, required): Please indicate your agreement or disagreement with the terms and conditions of the form.
* **What is leadership?** (Text, optional): Answer this question to share your understanding of what leadership means to you.
* **Leadership is** (Text, optional): Share your thoughts on what leadership is.
* **Description** (Text, optional): Provide a brief description of your understanding of leadership.
* **What is a good leader?** (Text, optional): Describe what you think makes a good leader.
* **What are the characteristics of a leader?** (Text, optional): List the qualities or characteristics that you think a leader should have.
* **What are the key qualities of a leader?** (Text, optional): Share your thoughts on the most important qualities of a leader.
* **What are the key responsibilities of a leader?** (Text, optional): Describe the primary responsibilities of a leader.
* **What are the key roles of a leader?** (Text, optional): Share your thoughts on the roles that a leader typically has.
* **What are the responsibilities of leaders?** (Text, optional): Describe the tasks or duties that a leader is expected to perform.
* **What are the roles of leaders?** (Text, optional): Share your thoughts on the roles that leaders typically have.
* **What are the key roles of a leader?** (Text, optional): Describe the main roles of a leader.
* **Page 2** (Text, optional): This is a blank page for you to add any additional thoughts or comments.
* **Intro Question 6** (Text, optional): Answer this question to share your thoughts on the roles of a leader.
* **Intro Question 7** (Text, optional): Answer this question to provide more information about leadership.
* **What are the key responsibilities of a leader?** (Text, optional): Describe the key tasks or duties of a leader.
* **What are the qualities of a good leader?** (Text, optional): Share your thoughts on the qualities that make a good leader.
* **What are the roles of leaders?** (Text, optional): Provide information about the roles that leaders typically have.
* **What are the responsibilities of leaders?** (Text, optional): Describe the tasks or duties that leaders are expected to perform.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Please provide clear and concise answers.
* Feel free to add any additional comments or thoughts on each page.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please review the introduction page for more information.
