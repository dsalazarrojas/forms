# Favorite Notification Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Favorite Notification Feedback" form is designed to collect feedback from users about their favorite notifications. This form helps us understand how often users receive notifications, through which channels, and with what level of importance and pain. Your feedback will help us improve the notification system to better serve your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of your favorite notification from the options provided.
2. Choose the type of notification you receive and prefer.
3. Select the frequency at which you receive notifications.
4. Rate the importance of notifications.
5. Rate the pain caused by notifications.
6. Choose the source and channel of notifications you receive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Favorite Notification**: Please select the category of your favorite notification from the options provided.
* **Category**: Choose the type of notification you receive and prefer.
* **Notification Type**: Select the type of notification you receive and prefer.
* **Notification Frequency**: Select the frequency at which you receive notifications.
* **Notification Importance**: Rate the importance of notifications (not at all, somewhat, very, not much).
* **Notification Pain**: Rate the pain caused by notifications (not at all, somewhat, very, not much).
* **Notification Source**: Choose the source of notifications you receive.
* **Notification Channel**: Choose the channel through which you receive notifications.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback about your notification experience.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information (email) so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
* **Notification Importance Level**: Rate the level of importance of notifications (not at all, somewhat, very, not much).
* **Notification Pain Level**: Rate the level of pain caused by notifications (not at all, somewhat, very, not much).
* **Notification Pain Importance**: Rate the importance of the pain caused by notifications (not at all, somewhat, very, not much).
* **Notification Pain Frequency**: Select the frequency at which you experience pain from notifications.
* **Notification Pain Source**: Choose the source of notifications that cause pain.
* **Notification Pain Channel**: Choose the channel through which you experience pain from notifications.

# Tips
* Please answer each question as accurately as possible to provide us with helpful feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* Your feedback is crucial to improving our notification system and we appreciate your time in completing this form.
