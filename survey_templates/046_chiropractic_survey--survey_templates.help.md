# Chiropractic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience at a chiropractic practice, including their overall satisfaction, wait time, and professionalism of the staff.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your name, if you wish to be identified, or leave it blank.
2. Enter the date of your visit to the chiropractic practice.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction level with the care you received.
4. Indicate how long you had to wait during your visit.
5. Evaluate the professionalism of the staff you interacted with.
6. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, Optional): Please enter your name, if you wish to be identified in the feedback. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of your visit to the chiropractic practice. This will help us track the timing of your experience.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the care you received. Please choose from the options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Wait Time Experience** (`wait_time_experience`, select_one, Optional): Indicate how long you had to wait during your visit. Please choose from the options:
	+ Under 10 minutes
	+ 10 to 30 minutes
	+ Over 30 minutes
* **Staff Professionalism** (`staff_professionalism`, select_one, Required): Evaluate the professionalism of the staff you interacted with. Please choose from the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, Optional): If you have any other comments, suggestions, or feedback about your experience, please enter it here.

## Tips

* Please take a moment to review each question carefully before answering.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Your feedback is appreciated and will help us improve our services.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
