# Skills-Based Hiring Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on hiring practices and trends from various industries, aiming to provide insights on skills-based hiring approaches, adoption trends, challenges, and HR and recruiting processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each page and field carefully, ensuring you provide accurate and relevant information for each question.
2.  Fill out each field according to the instructions provided for each page and question.
3.  Take note of any required fields, marked as such within the form, and ensure you complete them before moving on.
4.  If you're unsure about any field or question, use the provided hints as a guide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Job Description (Page 1)** (`Job Description`, `text`, required: false): Describe your company's current job description. This field is an open-ended text field where you can provide a brief overview of your company's job description, including key responsibilities and expectations.
*   **Hiring Practices (Page 1)** (`Hiring Practices`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the hiring practices your company currently uses or would like to adopt. Select as many options as applicable from the provided list.
*   **Challenges (Page 1)** (`Challenges`, `date`, required: false): Record any dates associated with past or current challenges your company has faced. This field is intended for date-specific information.
*   **Adoption Trends (Page 1)** (`Adoption Trends`, `text`, required: false): Provide any observations or notes about trends you've observed in adopting new hiring practices. This field is an open-ended text area for your comments.
*   **Industries (Page 2)** (`Industries`, `select_one`, required: false): Identify the industry or sector your company operates in, choosing one from the provided options.
*   **HR and Recruiting Insights (Page 2)** (`HR and Recruiting Insights`, `note`, required: false): Note any insights or observations you have about HR and recruiting processes within your organization or industry. This field is for free-form note-taking.
*   **Skills-First Hiring (Page 2)** (`Skills-First Hiring`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether your company adopts a skills-first hiring approach or not. Select from the provided options.
*   **Consistent Data Collection (Page 2)** (`Consistent Data Collection`, `text`, required: false): Describe how data is collected and maintained within your organization, especially if it's consistent and reliable. This field is for open-ended text input.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate and meaningful data is collected.
*   If you're unsure about any field or question, reach out to the form's administrators or creators for clarification.
*   This form is designed to be completed by HR and recruiting teams or individuals responsible for hiring practices and processes within their organizations.
