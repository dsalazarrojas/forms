# Automotive Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers about their experience at the automotive service center. It helps us understand what you liked or disliked about your visit and how we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Provide your personal details, including your name and email address.
2. Specify the date of your service visit.
3. Choose the type(s) of service performed.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service, as well as your satisfaction with specific aspects of the visit, such as the quality of work, timeliness, and communication.
5. Share any areas for improvement you think we could focus on.
6. Let us know if you would recommend our service to others and how likely you are to return.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (Customer Name): This field is required to identify the person filling out the form and is used for communication purposes.
  \* Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or driver's license.
- **Email Address** (Email Address): We use this to contact you about your feedback and any future service needs. Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
  \* Use the same email address associated with your account or service visit.
- **Phone Number** (Phone Number): This is an optional field, but we appreciate it if you provide a phone number so we can follow up with you if needed.
  \* Enter your phone number without any special characters or symbols.
- **Customer ID or Account Number** (Customer ID or Account Number): This field is optional, but it helps us identify your account and service history. If you're unsure, you can leave it blank.
  \* Enter your customer ID or account number if available.
- **Service Date** (Service Date): This is the date of your service visit. This is a required field to help us track your visit.
  \* Enter the date of your service visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Type of Service Performed** (Type of Service Performed): Choose the type(s) of service that was performed during your visit.
  \* Select all services that apply from the list.
- **Service Advisor Name** (Service Advisor Name): This field is optional, but we appreciate it if you can provide the name of your service advisor.
  \* Enter the name of the service advisor who assisted you.
- **Mechanic or Technician Assigned** (Mechanic or Technician Assigned): This field is optional, but it helps us identify the person who worked on your vehicle.
  \* Enter the name of the mechanic or technician assigned to your service.
- **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service provided.
  \* Choose from the scale: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
- **Quality of Work Performed** (Quality of Work Performed): Rate the quality of the work performed by our team.
  \* Choose from the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
- **Timeliness** (Timeliness): Rate how timely your service visit was.
  \* Choose from the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
- **Communication with Advisor** (Communication with Advisor): Rate how well your service advisor communicated with you.
  \* Choose from the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
- **Facility Cleanliness** (Facility Cleanliness): Rate the cleanliness of our facility.
  \* Choose from the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
- **Value for Money** (Value for Money): Rate how good of a deal you think you got with our service.
  \* Choose from the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
- **What Did We Do Well** (What Did We Do Well): Share with us what we did well during your service visit.
  \* Enter a brief description of what we did well.
- **Areas for Improvement** (Areas for Improvement): Share with us any areas where we could improve.
  \* Enter specific suggestions or ideas for improvement.
- **Would You Recommend Our Service** (Would You Recommend Our Service): Let us know if you would recommend our service to others.
  \* Choose from the scale: Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably Not, Definitely Not.
- **Likelihood of Returning** (Likelihood of Returning): Rate how likely you are to return to our service center.
  \* Choose from the scale: Very Likely, Likely, Uncertain, Unlikely, Very Unlikely.
- **May We Contact You About Your Feedback** (May We Contact You About Your Feedback): We use this to follow up on your feedback and get in touch with you about any future service needs.
  \* Choose from the scale: Yes, No.

Note: The form is designed to be completed in a few minutes and should take less than 5 minutes to finish. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
