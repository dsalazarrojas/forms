# Business Pitch Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for evaluating and scoring pitches presented at a business pitch event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Judge Name" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
2. Select the event name from the "Event Name" field.
3. Choose your top three picks from the "Company Name" fields.
4. Score each of your top picks using the score fields.
5. Indicate how important each of the evaluation criteria are to you using the score weight fields.
6. Provide your reason for choosing your top pick in the "Reason for Top Pick" field.
7. Optional: provide any areas of improvement for the pitchers and standout qualities that impressed you.
8. Check if you are interested in investing in any of the pitches.
9. List any companies you are interested in.
10. Check if you are willing to offer mentoring to any of the participants.
11. Provide any additional feedback you have about the pitches or event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Judge Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation and follow-up.
* **Event Name** (4, text, required): Select the name of the event you are participating in.
* **Company Name - First Choice** (7, text, required): Enter the name of your top pick.
* **Score for First Choice** (8, number, required): Score your top pick out of 100.
* **Company Name - Second Choice** (9, text, required): Enter the name of your runner-up.
* **Score for Second Choice** (10, number, required): Score your runner-up out of 100.
* **Company Name - Third Choice** (11, text, optional): Enter the name of your third choice.
* **Score for Third Choice** (12, number, optional): Score your third choice out of 100.
* **Innovation Score Weight** (13, select_one, required): Choose how important innovation is in your scoring.
* **Market Potential Score Weight** (14, select_one, required): Choose how important market potential is in your scoring.
* **Team Quality Score Weight** (15, select_one, required): Choose how important team quality is in your scoring.
* **Financial Projections Score Weight** (16, select_one, required): Choose how important financials are in your scoring.
* **Reason for Top Pick** (17, text, required): Write a brief description of why you chose your top pick.
* **Standout Qualities** (18, text, optional): Describe any standout qualities of your top pick.
* **Areas for Improvement** (19, text, optional): Suggest areas where the pitches could be improved.
* **Investment Interest** (20, select_one, required): Indicate if you are interested in investing in any of the pitches.
* **Companies of Interest** (21, text, optional): List any companies you are interested in.
* **Mentoring Offer** (22, select_one, optional): Offer mentoring to any of the participants.
* **Additional Feedback** (23, text, optional): Provide any other comments about the pitches or event.
