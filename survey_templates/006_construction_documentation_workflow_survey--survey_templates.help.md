# Construction Documentation Workflow Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from construction professionals about their current documentation workflow, with the goal of identifying areas for improvement and providing solutions to enhance efficiency and quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question truthfully, providing as much detail as possible. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Current Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required)
Your current job title in the construction industry.

### 2. **Primary Department** (`primary_department`, `select_one`, required)
Select the department that you primarily work in: Project Management, Site Supervision, Estimating/Quantity Surveying, Administration, Health and Safety, Quality Control, or Document Control.

### 3. **Average Weekly Time Spent on Documentation** (`doc_creation_time`, `number`, required)
Please indicate how many hours per week you spend on documentation-related tasks.

### 4. **Primary Document Storage Method** (`document_storage_method`, `select_one`, required)
Select how project files are currently stored: Physical Files/Folders, Local Network Drive, Cloud Storage (e.g., Dropbox), Industry Specific DMS (e.g., Procore, Aconex), or Hybrid (Mix of several methods).

### 5. **Ease of Document Retrieval** (`retrieval_ease_rating`, `number`, required)
Rate the ease of retrieving documents from 1 (Very difficult) to 10 (Instant and easy).

### 6. **Biggest Bottleneck in Current Documentation Process** (`biggest_bottleneck`, `text`, required)
Identify the biggest challenge you face in your current documentation process.

### 7. **How Often Do You Encounter Outdated Document Versions?** (`version_control_frequency`, `select_one`, required)
Select how often you encounter outdated documents: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.

### 8. **Have You Experienced Rework Due to Incorrect/Missing Documentation?** (`rework_due_to_docs`, `select_one`, required)
Select if you have ever experienced rework due to documentation issues: Yes - Frequently, Yes - Occasionally, or No - Never.

### 9. **Average Approval Cycle Time for a RFI or Submittal** (`approval_cycle_time`, `select_one`, required)
Select the average time it takes to get approval for a request for information or submittal: Less than 24 hours, 1-3 Days, 3-7 Days, or More than 1 week.

### 10. **Preferred Training Type for New Documentation Systems** (`preferred_training_type`, `select_multiple`, not required)
Select how you would prefer training to be delivered: In-person workshops, Online video tutorials, User manuals (PDF), or One-on-one coaching.

### 11. **Suggestions for Improving Workflow Efficiency** (`system_improvement_suggestions`, `text`, not required)
Provide any suggestions you have for improving the documentation workflow.

### 12. **May We Contact You for Further Details?** (`contact_for_follow_up`, `select_one`, required)
Select if you are available for further follow-up: True or False.

### 13. **Email Address for Follow-up** (`email_for_follow_up`, `email`, not required)
If you selected "True" for the previous question, provide your email address for follow-up contact.
