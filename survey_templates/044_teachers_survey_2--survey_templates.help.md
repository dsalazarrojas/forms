# Teachers Survey 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences, opinions, and perceptions regarding innovative practices in education.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please read each question carefully and respond with your true feelings and experiences.
- Make sure to answer all the questions, but feel free to skip any that do not apply to you.
- If a question does not have an option that accurately reflects your answer, select "No" or "Not Applicable" to indicate that.
- You can add additional comments to any question to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Teaching Innovation** (`teaching_innovation`, select_one): Please select whether you have implemented innovative teaching practices in your classes.
  - 'Yes' - You have implemented innovative teaching practices in your classes.
  - 'No' - You have not implemented innovative teaching practices in your classes.
- **Learning Innovation** (`learning_innovation`, select_multiple): Please select the aspects of learning in which you have implemented innovative practices. 
  - 'Yes' - You have implemented innovative learning practices in these areas.
  - 'No' - You have not implemented innovative learning practices in these areas.
- **Teaching Methodology** (`teaching_methodology`, select_one): Please select whether you use innovative teaching methodologies in your classes.
  - 'Yes' - You use innovative teaching methodologies in your classes.
  - 'No' - You do not use innovative teaching methodologies in your classes.
- **Student Engagement** (`student_engagement`, text): Please describe how you have promoted student engagement in your classes.
- **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, text): Please describe how you have received support for your innovation projects and teaching.
- **Innovation Project** (`innovation_project`, text): Please describe your innovation project, if you have one.
- **Evaluation Innovation** (`evaluation_innovation`, text): Please describe how you have evaluated innovation projects and practices.
- **Evaluation Project** (`evaluation_project`, select_multiple): Please select the innovative evaluation methods you have used. 
  - 'Yes' - You have used these innovative evaluation methods.
  - 'No' - You have not used these innovative evaluation methods.
- **Teacher Innovation** (`teacher_innovation`, select_one): Please select whether you have implemented innovative teaching practices as a teacher.
  - 'Yes' - You have implemented innovative teaching practices as a teacher.
  - 'No' - You have not implemented innovative teaching practices as a teacher.
- **Teacher Innovation Project** (`teacher_innovation_project`, select_multiple): Please select whether you have implemented innovative projects as a teacher. 
  - 'Yes' - You have implemented innovative projects as a teacher.
  - 'No' - You have not implemented innovative projects as a teacher.
- **Teacher Innovation Project Improvement** (`teacher_innovation_project_improvement`, select_multiple): Please select whether you have improved your innovative projects as a teacher. 
  - 'Yes' - You have improved your innovative projects as a teacher.
  - 'No' - You have not improved your innovative projects as a teacher.
- **Overall Innovation Improvement** (`overall_innovation_improvement`, text): Please describe how you have improved innovation in your institution.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note): Please add any comments you may have about innovative practices and projects in your institution.
- **Form Completed** (`form_completed`, time): Please indicate when you completed this form.
- **Survey Completed** (`survey_completed`, select_one): Please indicate whether you have completed the survey.
  - 'Yes' - You have completed the survey.
  - 'No' - You have not completed the survey.
- **Submission** (`submission`, text): Please add any other comments or information you wish to submit.
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, text): Please describe your overall satisfaction with innovative practices and projects.
- **Student Satisfaction** (`student_satisfaction`, text): Please describe student satisfaction with innovative practices and projects.
- **Innovation Project Improvement** (`innovation_project_improvement`, text): Please describe how you have improved innovation projects.
- **Teacher Innovation Project Improvement** (`teacher_innovation_project_improvement`, text): Please describe how you have improved your innovative projects as a teacher.
