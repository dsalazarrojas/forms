# Office Organization Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from employees on their office space, resources, and work environment. The goal is to identify areas for improvement and help create a more efficient and satisfying work space.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Please think about your recent experiences and observations in the office.** This will help you provide honest and constructive feedback.
2. **Answer each question carefully, considering your preferences and needs.** If you're unsure about a question, try to provide the best answer you can.
3. **Submit your feedback.** Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send your feedback to the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What suggestions do you have for improving office space? (`office_space_suggestions`, text, optional)
Please share any ideas you have for making the office more comfortable, productive, and enjoyable.

### What resources do you need to improve productivity? (`office_resouces_suggestions`, text, optional)
Share any tools, equipment, or materials that you feel are essential for your work.

### How do you feel about your work environment? (`employee_satisfaction`, select_one)
*   **Very satisfied:** You're extremely happy with your current work environment.
*   **Somewhat satisfied:** You're relatively happy, but there's room for improvement.
*   **Neutral:** You're neither satisfied nor dissatisfied.
*   **Somewhat dissatisfied:** You're relatively unhappy with your current work environment.
*   **Very dissatisfied:** You're extremely unhappy with your current work environment.

### How well do you feel supported by your manager? (`manager_support`, select_multiple)
*   **Fully supported:** Your manager is always available and helpful.
*   **Mostly supported:** Your manager is generally supportive, but there's room for improvement.
*   **Neutral:** You're not sure about your manager's level of support.
*   **Somewhat supported:** You feel somewhat supported, but there are some issues.
*   **Not supported at all:** You don't feel supported by your manager.

### What are your preferred communication channels? (`communication_channels`, select_multiple)
*   **Email:** You prefer exchanging emails with your team or manager.
*   **Phone:** You prefer phone calls for communication.
*   **Chat:** You prefer chatting with your team or manager.
*   **In-person:** You prefer face-to-face communication.

### What office equipment do you need to be more productive? (`office_equipment`, select_multiple)
*   **Monitor:** A new monitor would be helpful for work.
*   **Keyboard:** A new keyboard would improve your typing experience.
*   **Mouse:** A new mouse would enhance your cursor control.
*   **Headset:** A headset would reduce background noise and improve focus.
*   **Printer:** A new printer would save you time and effort.

### What is your ideal noise level in the office? (`noise_level`, select_one)
*   **Quiet:** You prefer a completely silent work environment.
*   **Moderate:** You're comfortable with moderate background noise.
*   **Loud:** You prefer a lively and noisy work environment.

### Do you feel your work-life balance is adequate? (`work_life_balance`, select_one)
*   **True:** Yes, you feel your work-life balance is adequate.
*   **False:** No, you don't feel your work-life balance is adequate.

### Is there anything else you'd like to add or suggest? (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional)
Please share any additional thoughts or ideas you have for improving the office or your work experience.
