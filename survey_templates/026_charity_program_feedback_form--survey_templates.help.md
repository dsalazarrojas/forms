# Charity Program Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants who have attended a charity program. The purpose of this form is to help us understand your experience and opinions about the program's content, delivery, and overall value. Your input will help us improve future programs and make them more relevant and engaging for you and other participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up).
2. Select the program you attended.
3. Rate the program's content and facilitation.
4. Share your thoughts on the program's pacing, venue, and overall satisfaction.
5. Provide any recommendations for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, Optional): This is your name, which will be used to identify you in our records if you provide it. You can leave it blank if you prefer to remain anonymous.
- **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, Optional): If you want to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address here. It's not required, so you can leave it blank if you prefer.
- **Which Program Did You Attend** (`program_attended`, text, Required): Choose the name of the program you attended.
- **Date of Program** (`program_date`, date, Required): Enter the date you attended the program.
- **First Time Attending This Program** (`first_time_attending`, select_one, Optional): If this is your first time attending the program, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
- **Relevance of Program Content** (`content_relevance`, select_one, Required): Rate how relevant the program's content was to you.
- **Quality of Content and Information** (`content_quality`, select_one, Required): Rate how valuable and informative the program's content was.
- **Ease of Understanding Content** (`comprehension_level`, select_one, Optional): Select how easy or difficult it was to understand the program's content.
- **Quality of Facilitators or Instructors** (`facilitator_quality`, select_one, Required): Rate how well the program's facilitators performed.
- **Participant Engagement and Interaction** (`engagement_level`, select_one, Optional): Select how engaging you found the program.
- **Program Pacing** (`pacing`, select_one, Optional): Rate how well-paced the program was.
- **Venue and Facilities** (`venue_quality`, select_one, Optional): Rate the quality of the program's venue and facilities.
- **Did You Learn Something New** (`learned_something_new`, select_one, Required): Select if you gained new knowledge or insights from the program.
- **Will You Apply What You Learned** (`apply_learnings`, select_one, Optional): Select if you plan to use the information or skills gained from the program.
- **Overall Satisfaction with Program** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
- **Recommendations for Improvement** (`recommendations`, text, Optional): Share any suggestions on how to improve the program.
- **Will You Attend Future Programs** (`attend_again`, select_one, Optional): Select if you plan to attend future programs.
- **Overall Satisfaction with Program** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, Required) is repeated for consistency, use `overall_satisfaction` only.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback.
* Select "Optional" fields only if you're willing to provide your name and email address.
* Rate your answers based on your genuine experience and opinions.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please provide them in the "Recommendations for Improvement" section.
