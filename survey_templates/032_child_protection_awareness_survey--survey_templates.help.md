# Child Protection Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your awareness and preparedness in identifying and reporting signs of child abuse, as well as your experience with training and institutional policies related to child protection.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that accurately reflect your current situation.
3. For questions that require a "text" type, provide a detailed answer in the provided space.
4. Make sure to select all applicable options for "select_multiple" type questions.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Are you aware of the signs of child abuse?** (`aware_of_signs`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select the option that best describes your level of awareness:
		- Yes - Fully Aware: You have a clear understanding of the signs of child abuse.
		- No - Not Aware: You are not familiar with the signs of child abuse.
		- Unsure - Need Information: You are unsure or lack information about the signs of child abuse.
* **Do you know who to contact if you suspect abuse?** (`know_reporting_contact`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select the option that best describes your level of knowledge:
		- Yes - I know the procedure: You are aware of the reporting process and who to contact.
		- No - I am unsure: You are not familiar with the reporting procedure.
* **Have you received child protection training?** (`received_training`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select the option that best describes your experience:
		- 'True': You have received official training on child protection.
		- 'False': You have not received such training.
* **How recently was your last training?** (`last_training_date`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select the most accurate option:
		- Within the last 1 year: Your last training was within the last 12 months.
		- 1 to 3 years ago: Your last training was between 12 and 36 months ago.
		- Over 3 years ago: Your last training was more than 3 years ago.
		- Never had training: You have never received child protection training.
* **Are child protection policies clearly visible?** (`policies_visible`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select the option that best describes your experience:
		- Yes - Clearly Posted: Child protection policies are easily accessible and visible.
		- No - Hard to Find: You have to actively search for child protection policies.
		- Not Applicable: Child protection policies are not applicable or relevant in your work environment.
* **Do you feel confident in the reporting procedure?** (`reporting_confidence`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select the option that best describes your level of confidence:
		- Strongly Agree: You feel very confident in the reporting procedure.
		- Agree: You feel fairly confident in the reporting procedure.
		- Neutral: You are neutral or have mixed feelings about the reporting procedure.
		- Disagree: You do not feel confident in the reporting procedure.
* **What additional resources would help improve awareness?** (`additional_resources`, `text`, required)
	+ Provide a detailed answer in this space about what additional resources you would find helpful to improve awareness on child protection.
* **Preferred learning formats** (`preferred_formats`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Select all the formats you prefer for learning about child protection:
		- Online Training Modules
		- In-person Workshops
		- Print Handouts and Guides
		- Educational Videos
* **Final comments or suggestions** (`final_comments`, `text`, required)
	+ Provide any final comments or suggestions about the survey or child protection awareness in general.
* **Date of Survey** (`survey_date`, `date`, required)
	+ This field is automatically populated with the current date.
