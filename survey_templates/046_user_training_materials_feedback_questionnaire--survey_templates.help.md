# user_training_materials_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "user_training_materials_feedback_questionnaire" form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with training materials. This guide will help you understand each field and complete the form efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
- Read each field explanation below for guidance on specific questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Participation (Session Participation)** (`1`, `text`, optional): This field is used to record the user's participation in the training session. Please enter a brief description of your participation in the session.
* **Session Content (Session Content)** (`2`, `text`, optional): In this field, please describe your impression of the session content, such as how it met your needs or if there were any notable aspects.
* **Materials Usefulness (Materials Usefulness)** (`3`, `number`, optional): On a scale of 1-5, rate the usefulness of the training materials provided. One (1) means they were not useful, while five (5) means they were very useful.
* **Instructor Feedback (Instructor Feedback)** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the instructor's teaching skills by choosing one of the options: "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Materials Suggestions (Materials Suggestions)** (`5`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving the training materials, please provide them here.
* **Overall Experience (Overall Experience)** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how your overall experience with the training materials was by selecting "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Training Goals (Training Goals)** (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all the training objectives that were met during the session: "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Training Length (Training Length)** (`8`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how long you felt the training session was by selecting "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)** (`9`, `text`, optional): If there's anything else you'd like to add or discuss, please include it here.
* **Email (Email)** (`10`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up contact.
