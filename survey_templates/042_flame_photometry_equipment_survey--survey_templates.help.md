# Flame Photometry Equipment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The goal of this survey is to gather information about flame photometry equipment from users, aiming to understand their satisfaction with the equipment, identify any recurring issues, and determine the priority of improvements needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each field, and:

* Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
* Enter your Email address in the "Email" field, following the format of `[email]@` followed by the domain name (e.g., `user@example.com`).
* Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
* Enter a brief description of the equipment you are referencing in the "Equipment Name" field.
* For the "Satisfaction Rating" field, rate your satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 10, with 1 being the least satisfied and 10 being the most satisfied.
* In the "Recurring Issues" field, describe any recurring problems you have encountered with the equipment.
* Select the priority of improvement you believe is needed from the options: High, Medium, or Low.
* If you have any time constraints for submission, enter them in the "Submission Time" field in the format of `HH:MM:SS` (e.g., 14:30:00 for 2:30 PM).
* Finally, indicate the date you would like to submit your response by entering it in the "Submission Date" field in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., 2022-07-25 for July 25, 2022).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Equipment Name** (`Equipment Name`, text, required: false): Describe the flame photometry equipment you are referring to.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`Satisfaction Rating`, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the equipment on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Recurring Issues** (`Recurring Issues`, text, required: false): Describe any recurring problems you have with the equipment.
* **Priority of Improvement** (`Priority of Improvement`, select_one, required: false): Select the level of priority you believe is needed for improvement.
* **Submission Time** (`Submission Time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you would like to submit your response.
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, date, required: false): Indicate the date you would like to submit your response.
