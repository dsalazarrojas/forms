# # Vent Noise Elimination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vent Noise Elimination Survey is designed to collect information on the status of vent noise elimination in facilities. This form helps facilities management or maintenance teams gather necessary data to assess and improve their vent noise elimination efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read the **Introduction** section to understand the purpose and context of this survey.
2.  Select the relevant **facility_info** that applies to your facility from the provided options.
3.  Indicate the **vent_type** that you're experiencing issues with (Steam Leak, Water Leak, or Other).
4.  Choose all relevant **vent_location** where you've encountered the issue (Floor 1, Floor 2, or Other).
5.  Enter the **report_date** when you last reported the vent noise issue.
6.  Report the current **repair_status** (Not Repaired, Partially Repaired, or Fully Repaired).
7.  Check if a **follow_up_required** is needed to resolve the issue (Yes or No).
8.  Enter any **comments** or observations you may have about the vent noise issue.
9.  Click **Submit** to submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction**: This is a non-interactive section that provides context and background information about the survey.
*   **facility_info**: Select the correct facility where you're experiencing the vent noise issue.
*   **vent_type**: Choose the type of vent noise issue you're experiencing (e.g., Steam Leak or Water Leak).
*   **vent_location**: Identify all locations where the vent noise issue has occurred.
*   **report_date**: Enter the date when you last reported the vent noise issue.
*   **repair_status**: Report the current status of repairs for the vent noise issue (Not Repaired, Partially Repaired, or Fully Repaired).
*   **follow_up_required**: If further action is required to resolve the issue, select 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **comments**: Enter any additional information, observations, or feedback about the vent noise issue.
*   **Submit**: Click to submit your completed survey response.
