# Job Training Success Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Training Success Evaluation Form is used to assess the effectiveness of job training sessions, gather feedback from participants, and capture valuable data for future improvements. This form helps track training session success, identify areas for improvement, and provide insights for future training initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the training session you are evaluating.
2. Select the applicable options for Participant Feedback that best describe your experience with the training session.
3. Enter the date you completed the training session.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback as the trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Session** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter the title of the training session being evaluated.
* **Participant Feedback** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Select the options that best describe your experience with the training session:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Date Completed** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter the date you completed the training session.
* **Trainer Comments** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback as the trainer.
