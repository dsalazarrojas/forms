# Dietary Habits And Frequency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dietary Habits And Frequency Survey" is a form designed to collect information about an individual's eating habits, physical activity level, and other related health-related data. This form is typically used in medical or health-related settings to gather accurate and comprehensive information about a person's lifestyle and habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the answer that best describes your eating habits and physical activity level.
3. Fill in the numerical fields with your accurate height, weight, body mass index (BMI), waist and hip circumferences, and other measurements.
4. If you have a medical history or family history, choose the correct option from the dropdown menu.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Dietary Habits And Frequency Survey** (`general_info`, `note`, `required`: false): This is the title of the form and a brief introduction to the survey questions.
  * This field is not editable and serves as a reminder of the form's title and purpose.
- **Food Frequency** (`food_frequency`, `select_multiple`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting how often you consume foods or beverages.
  * Rarely: You eat these foods rarely or very rarely.
  * Sometimes: You eat these foods sometimes, but not always.
  * Often: You eat these foods frequently, but not daily.
  * Always: You eat these foods daily.
- **Portion Control** (`portion_control`, `number`, `required`: false): Please enter the portion size you usually consume for each food type.
  * This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
- **Healthy Eating** (`healthy_eating`, `select_one`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting how often you eat healthy foods.
  * Always: You always eat healthy foods.
  * Rarely: You rarely eat healthy foods.
  * Sometimes: You sometimes eat healthy foods.
  * Never: You never eat healthy foods.
- **Healthy Drinking** (`healthy_drinking`, `select_multiple`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting how often you drink healthy beverages.
  * Always: You always drink healthy beverages.
  * Rarely: You rarely drink healthy beverages.
  * Sometimes: You sometimes drink healthy beverages.
  * Never: You never drink healthy beverages.
- **Meal Frequency** (`meal_frequency`, `number`, `required`: false): Please enter the number of meals you usually have in a day.
  * This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
- **Breakfast Frequency** (`breakfast_frequency`, `number`, `required`: false): Please enter the number of breakfasts you usually have in a week.
  * This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
- **Physical Activity Level** (`physical_activity_level`, `select_multiple`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting your physical activity level.
  * Always: You always engage in physical activity.
  * Rarely: You rarely engage in physical activity.
  * Sometimes: You sometimes engage in physical activity.
  * Never: You never engage in physical activity.
- **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, `number`, `required`: false): Please enter the number of hours you usually sleep per day.
  * This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
- **Smoking History** (`smoking_history`, `select_one`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting your smoking history.
  * Never: You never smoked.
  * Former: You have quit smoking.
  * Current: You currently smoke.
- **Alcohol History** (`alcohol_history`, `select_one`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting your alcohol history.
  * Never: You never consumed alcohol.
  * Former: You have quit drinking alcohol.
  * Current: You currently drink alcohol.
- **Medication History** (`medication_history`, `select_one`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting your medication history.
  * None: You do not take any medication.
  * Some: You take some medication.
  * Multiple: You take multiple medications.
- **Family History** (`family_history`, `select_multiple`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting any family history of health conditions.
  * None: Your family has no history of health conditions.
  * 'Yes': Your family has a history of health conditions.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `select_multiple`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting any medical history or conditions you have.
  * None: You do not have any medical conditions.
  * 'Yes': You have some medical conditions.
- **Physical Activity** (`physical_activity`, `select_multiple`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting whether you engage in physical activity.
  * None: You do not engage in physical activity.
  * 'Yes': You engage in physical activity.
- **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, `select_one`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting your sleep quality.
  * Very good: You usually have very good sleep quality.
  * Good: You usually have good sleep quality.
  * Fair: You usually have fair sleep quality.
  * Poor: You usually have poor sleep quality.
- **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, `number`, `required`: false): Please enter the number of times you exercise per week.
  * This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
- **Weight Control** (`weight_control`, `select_one`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting whether you control your weight.
  * 'Yes': You control your weight.
  * 'No': You do not control your weight.
- **Body Mass Index (BMI)** (`body_mass_index`, `number`, `required`: false): Please enter your body mass index (BMI).
  * This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
- **Waist Circumference** (`waist_circumference`, `number`, `required`: false): Please enter your waist circumference in centimeters or inches.
  * This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
- **Hip Circumference** (`hip_circumference`, `number`, `required`: false): Please enter your hip circumference in centimeters or inches.
  * This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
- **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, `required`: false): Please enter your weight in kilograms or pounds.
  * This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
- **Height** (`height`, `number`, `required`: false): Please enter your height in centimeters or inches.
  * This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
- **Age** (`age`, `number`, `required`: false): Please enter your age in years.
  * This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
- **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, `required`: false): Please answer this question by selecting your gender.
  * Male: You are male.
  * Female: You are female.
  * Other: You identify as other.
