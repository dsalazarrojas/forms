# learning_committee_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The learning committee feedback form is designed to collect feedback from users about the committee's performance, suggestions, and areas of improvement. This form is intended to help the committee better understand the user's thoughts and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide your genuine feedback.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select only one answer per question.
3. For open-ended questions, provide detailed and constructive feedback.
4. Ensure that you provide a valid email address if you choose to provide feedback via email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (text, optional): A brief introduction to your feedback, explaining why you are submitting this form.
- **What** (select_one, required): Select one option that best describes your feedback about the learning committee.
  - Option 1: The committee is doing a great job.
  - Option 2: The committee is not meeting my expectations.
  - Option 3: I need more information about the committee.
- **When** (select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to describe when you experienced the issue with the committee.
  - Option 4: During a meeting.
  - Option 5: After reviewing documentation.
  - Option 6: In a one-on-one discussion.
- **Where** (date, optional): If relevant, provide the date when you experienced the issue with the committee.
- **How** (select_one, required): Select one option that best describes how you felt about the committee.
  - Option 7: Confident and supported.
  - Option 8: Confused and unsupported.
  - Option 9: Uncomfortable and unsupported.
- **Why** (text, optional): Provide your reasons for choosing the above option.
- **comments** (text, optional): Additional comments or suggestions.
- **email** (email, optional): Provide your email address to allow the committee to follow up with you directly.
