# Advertising Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on your experiences and opinions about advertisements, including your demographics, advertising exposure, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your situation or preference.
3. If you're unsure about a question, choose the option that seems most accurate.
4. Take your time, and do not rush through the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Range**: Select the age group that you identify with, from "Under 18" to "65 or over".
* **Gender Identity**: Choose your gender identity from the list, including "Male", "Female", "Non-binary", "Other", or "Prefer not to say".
* **Household Income**: Select your approximate household income range, from "Under $25,000" to "$150,000+".
* **Ad Platforms Used**: Choose the platforms where you most frequently see advertisements, such as "Television", "Social Media", "Online Videos", etc.
* **Daily Ad Exposure**: Select how many advertisements you see per day, from "Less than 10" to "More than 100".
* **Ad Attention Level**: Choose how often you pay attention to advertisements, from "Always" to "Never".
* **Ad Relevance To You**: Select how relevant you find advertisements to your interests, from "Very relevant" to "Very irrelevant".
* **Ad Creativity Rating**: Rate the creativity of most advertisements, from "Very creative" to "Not creative at all".
* **Trust In Advertisements**: Choose how much you trust the information in advertisements, from "Completely trust" to "Do not trust".
* **Ad Influence On Purchase Decisions**: Select how much advertisements influence your purchase decisions, from "Greatly influence" to "Do not influence".
* **Ad Triggering Purchase Behavior**: Choose if advertisements have ever prompted you to buy something immediately, from "Yes, often" to "Never".
* **Brand Recall From Ads**: Select how well you can recall brands from advertisements, from "Excellent recall" to "Cannot recall".
* **Preferred Ad Format**: Choose your preferred format of advertisement, from "Video", "Static Image", "Text-based", etc.
* **Acceptable Ad Frequency**: Select how many advertisements you consider acceptable to see per hour, from "1-2" to "No ads preferred".
* **Suggestions For Improvement**: Share your thoughts on what advertisers could do to improve their effectiveness, in a text box.

Note: This guide is a general explanation to help you understand the form's purpose and how to complete it. Please answer all questions honestly, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or concerns.
