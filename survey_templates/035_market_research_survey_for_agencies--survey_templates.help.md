# Market Research Survey For Agencies - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about agencies for market research or survey purposes. This form is designed to collect data about agencies' marketing background, services offered, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Marketing Background" field with a brief description of your agency's background and services offered.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Company Type" based on your agency's type (e.g., digital marketing agency, advertising agency, etc.).
3. Fill in your "Job Title" and "Name" in the respective fields.
4. Provide your "Email", "Phone Number", "Address", and "Company Website" in the respective fields.
5. Describe your agency's "Job Function" and "Company Description" in the respective fields.
6. Specify your "Target Audience" and "Survey Goals" in the respective fields.
7. Provide an estimate of your "Expected Outcome" and "Budget" for the survey.
8. Specify the "Time Allowed" for completing the survey.
9. Fill in your "Contact Name", "Contact Phone", "Contact Email", and "Contact Address" in the respective fields.
10. Provide any "Additional Info" about your agency.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Marketing", "Advertising", and "Design" services offered by your agency.
12. Click the "Submit" button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marketing Background**: A brief description of your agency's background and services offered.
* **Company Type**: Select "Yes" if your agency is a digital marketing agency, advertising agency, etc. Select "No" otherwise.
* **Job Title**: Your job title within the agency.
* **Name**: Your name.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number.
* **Address**: Your agency's address.
* **Company Website**: Your agency's website.
* **Job Function**: A description of your agency's main function or service.
* **Company Description**: A description of your agency's services and offerings.
* **Target Audience**: Who does your agency target with its services?
* **Survey Goals**: What are your goals for this survey?
* **Expected Outcome**: What do you expect to achieve with this survey?
* **Budget**: Your estimated budget for the survey.
* **Time Allowed**: How much time do you expect to spend on the survey.
* **Contact Name**: Your name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email**: Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Address**: Your address for contact purposes.
* **Additional Info**: Any additional information about your agency.
* **Marketing**: Select "Yes" if your agency offers marketing services. Select "No" otherwise.
* **Advertising**: Select "Yes" if your agency offers advertising services. Select "No" otherwise.
* **Design**: Select "Yes" if your agency offers design services. Select "No" otherwise.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your survey.
