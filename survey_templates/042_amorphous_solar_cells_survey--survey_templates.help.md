# Amorphous Solar Cells Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form was created to collect feedback on amorphous solar cells, a type of solar cell technology. It is designed to gather information from professionals, students, consumers, and investors on their familiarity with the technology, its advantages and disadvantages, and their opinions on its future potential and industry growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting your category from the list provided in the "What Best Describes You" question.
2.  Answer each question in the relevant section, providing as much detail as you can.
3.  Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
4.  If applicable, provide additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions as truthfully and accurately as possible.
* Select all options that apply for multiple-choice questions.
* For fields that ask about "if applicable," only respond if the question is relevant to your experience or expertise.
* For open-ended fields like "Additional Comments" and "Suggestions for Industry," feel free to elaborate as much as you need.
* This form is designed to gather feedback on amorphous solar cells, a technology that may have varying levels of familiarity among respondents. Be honest about your level of understanding and knowledge.
