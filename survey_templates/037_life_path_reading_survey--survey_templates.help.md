# Life Path Reading Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your preferences for a life path reading, which is a type of consultation that provides insights into your life's path and potential. The form helps us understand your preferences for the type of reading, how to receive the reading, length, frequency, payment methods, and communication preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Choose your preferred type of reading (e.g., Astrology, Numerology, Tarot, or Reiki).
2. Select how you would like to receive the reading (e.g., Email, Phone, In-person, or Online).
3. Indicate the length of the reading you would like (e.g., 30, 60, 90, or 120 minutes).
4. Choose how often you would like to receive readings (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
5. Select your preferred method of payment (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
6. Confirm if you will be available for a free consultation (e.g., True, False, or Maybe).
7. Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up (e.g., Email, Phone, Text, or Social Media).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Preferences (Note)**: Please describe your general preferences for the life path reading.
- **Client Details (Text)**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
- **Reading Preferences (Select Multiple)**: Select all of the types of readings you are interested in (Astrology, Numerology, Tarot, or Reiki).
- **What type of reading do you prefer? (Select One)**: Choose the type of reading you are most interested in.
- **How would you like to receive the reading? (Select One)**: Select the method you prefer to receive the reading (Email, Phone, In-person, or Online).
- **How long would you like the reading to be? (Select One)**: Choose the length of the reading you are interested in (30, 60, 90, or 120 minutes).
- **How often would you like to receive readings? (Select One)**: Select how often you would like to receive readings (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
- **How would you like to pay? (Select One)**: Choose your preferred method of payment (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
- **Will you be available for a free consultation? (Select One)**: Confirm if you are available for a free consultation (True, False, or Maybe).
- **How would you like to be contacted for follow-up? (Select One)**: Select the method you prefer for follow-up communication (Email, Phone, Text, or Social Media).

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when answering these questions to ensure we can provide you with the best possible services.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to ask us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
