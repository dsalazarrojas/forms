# Lash Client Experience Review Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Client Experience Review Appointment Form is used to collect client feedback on their experience with a lash technician during an appointment. This feedback helps to identify areas for improvement and to evaluate client satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your appointment details, including the date and time of your appointment.
2. Rate the service you received from the lash technician using the Lash Technician Rating scale.
3. Provide any feedback or comments about your experience.
4. Enter your contact number and email address so that we can follow up with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Client Experience Review
* **Client Experience Review**: This is a text field where you can provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the lash technician. This is a required field, so please make sure to fill it out.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true

### 2. Appointment Date
* **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of your appointment with the lash technician.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: false

### 3. Appointment Time
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of your appointment with the lash technician.
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: false

### 4. Technician
* **Technician**: Enter the name of the lash technician who serviced you.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false

### 5. Lash Technician Rating
* **Lash Technician Rating**: Rate the service you received from the lash technician using the scale:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: false

### 6. Feedback
* **Feedback**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false

### 7. Comments
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false

### 8. Contact Number
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so that we can follow up with you if needed.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false

### 9. Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you if needed.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: false
