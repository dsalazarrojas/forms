# Youth Sports Parent Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents about their experience with the sports organization's communication. It will help us understand how well we are doing in terms of keeping parents informed and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can add any relevant context or comments about your experience. Feel free to type as much or as little as you need.
2. **Parent Name**: This is your name as a parent. Please type it as you would like it to be recorded.
3. **Parent Email**: This is your email address. If you have any issues with our communication, we'd like to keep you informed. Enter your actual email address.
4. **Communication Quality**: Choose how you rate our communication:
	* **Excellent**: We're doing a great job!
	* **Good**: It's okay, we can improve.
	* **Fair**: We're not doing so well.
	* **Poor**: We really need to work on this.
5. **Communication Purpose**: Choose what you're using this form for:
	* **Feedback**: Share your concerns or suggestions.
	* **Updates**: Let us know about any updates or news.
	* **General info**: Just want to know more about our organization.
6. **Areas for Improvement**: Select all the areas where you think we can improve:
	* **Scheduling**: How we manage schedules.
	* **Team info**: Information about teams, players, and events.
	* **Other**: Any other area you think we can improve.
7. **Additional Comments**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please add them here.

## Tips
* Be honest and open about your experience with our communication.
* Choose the options that best fit your situation.
* If you have any other feedback or questions, don't hesitate to reach out.

Please complete this form to help us improve our communication with you and your family.
