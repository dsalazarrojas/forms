# search_dish_and_view_co2_impact - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "search_dish_and_view_co2_impact" form is designed to collect detailed information about the Co2 impact of a specific dish or food product. This form is intended to help users calculate and view the Co2 impact of the dish, including Co2 footprint, Co2 emissions, Co2 saved, Co2 offset, and Co2 reduced. The form also collects other relevant details such as serving size, serving method, and Co2 impact source.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Dish Details" field with the specific dish or food product you want to calculate the Co2 impact for.
2. Enter the serving size of the dish in the "Serving Size" field.
3. Choose the serving method from the options provided in the "Serving Method" field.
4. If applicable, select the Co2 impact source from the options provided in the "Co2 Impact Source" field.
5. If you selected "Co2 Impact Source Other", provide additional details in the "Co2 Impact Source Other Details" field.
6. Enter the Co2 footprint value in the "Co2 Footprint Value" field. You can select the units of measurement from the options provided in the "Co2 Footprint Units" field.
7. Provide any additional Co2 footprint calculations or references in the "Co2 Footprint Calculations" and "Co2 Footprint Reference" fields.
8. If applicable, enter the Co2 impact value in the "Co2 Impact" field.
9. If applicable, select whether the Co2 impact was reduced, saved, or offset in the "Co2 Reductions" field.
10. If applicable, enter the Co2 offset value in the "Co2 Offset" field.
11. Finally, provide your name, email, phone number, and food business or individual information as relevant to your role in the Co2 impact calculation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dish Details**: Enter the name of the specific dish or food product you want to calculate the Co2 impact for.
* **Serving Size**: Enter the serving size of the dish in the specified unit of measurement (e.g., gram, pound, etc.).
* **Serving Method**: Choose how the dish is served, such as raw, cooked, or frozen.
* **Co2 Footprint**: Enter the Co2 footprint value of the dish.
* **Co2 Emissions**: Enter the Co2 emissions value of the dish.
* **Co2 Saved**: Enter the Co2 saved value of the dish.
* **Co2 Offset**: Enter the Co2 offset value of the dish.
* **Co2 Reduced**: Select whether the Co2 impact was reduced, saved, or offset.
* **Co2 Impact Source**: Select the source of the Co2 impact, such as default or provided by the food business.
* **Co2 Impact**: Enter the Co2 impact value of the dish.
* **Food Business**: Select whether you are completing this form as an individual or a food business.
* **Food Business Name**: If you selected food business, enter the name of the business.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Co2 Footprint Units**: Select the units of measurement for the Co2 footprint, such as metric or imperial.
* **Co2 Footprint Value**: Enter the Co2 footprint value of the dish.
* **Co2 Footprint Calculations**: Provide any additional Co2 footprint calculations or references.
* **Co2 Footprint Reference**: Enter any additional Co2 footprint references.
* **Co2 Footprint URL**: Enter any additional Co2 footprint URL.
* **Co2 Impact Source Other**: If you selected "Co2 Impact Source Other", provide additional details about the source.
* **Co2 Impact Source Other Details**: Enter any additional details about the Co2 impact source.
* **Co2 Footprint Source**: Enter the source of the Co2 footprint information.
* **Co2 Footprint Units**: Select the units of measurement for the Co2 footprint.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about the dish or food product.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct Co2 impact source and units of measurement.
* Double-check your calculations and entries for accuracy.
* If you are completing this form as a food business, ensure that you have entered the correct business name and information.
