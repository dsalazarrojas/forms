# Nutritional Counseling Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutritional Counseling Effectiveness Survey is designed to gather information on the effectiveness of nutritional counseling programs. This survey aims to assess the level of satisfaction with the nutritional counseling provided and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your survey in the "Nutritional Counseling Effectiveness Survey" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your survey in the "Description" field.
3. Select the category of your survey from the "Category" dropdown menu. You can choose "Survey Templates" or "Other".
4. Enter a category ID (if necessary) in the "Category ID" field.
5. Provide a detailed description of your survey in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nutritional Counseling Effectiveness Survey** (`form_title`, text, optional):
    Enter a title for your survey that accurately reflects its purpose and scope.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, optional):
    Enter a brief description of your survey, including its goals and objectives.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional):
    Select the category of your survey from the options provided. Choose "Survey Templates" if your survey is a template, or "Other" if it's a unique survey.
* **Category ID** (`form_category_id`, number, optional):
    Enter a unique identifier for your category, if required.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, optional):
    Enter a detailed description of your survey, including any specific questions or goals.
 
## Tips
This survey is a general guideline for assessing the effectiveness of nutritional counseling programs. If you have any specific questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to your supervisor or contact person.
