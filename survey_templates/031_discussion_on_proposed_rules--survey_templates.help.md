# Discussion On Proposed Rules - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information about proposed rules from users. The purpose of this form is to gather input from multiple categories, allowing administrators to understand user preferences and adjust proposed rules accordingly. It is essential to provide accurate and clear information for this form to function correctly.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Be sure to enter your First name, Last name, and Date correctly.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the correct format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
4. Select the appropriate Category and Subcategory from the provided options.
5. Enter any comments or feedback in the text field.
6. Click "Submit" to submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments or suggestions you have about the proposed rules.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for future reference or to receive updates on the proposed rules.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2022).
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 08:00 AM).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): This is a field for additional comments or notes. If you have any concerns or questions, please use this field.
* **Email list** (`email_list`, `email`, optional): Enter a list of email addresses separated by commas.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or multiple categories that apply to your feedback.
* **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, `select_one`, required): Select one subcategory that best describes your feedback.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the proposed rules.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click this button to submit your feedback.
* **First name** (`first_name_repeat`, `text`, optional): This field is currently not required and not recommended to be filled. It is a duplicate field of First name, and filling it in will cause a submission error.
