# Eco Friendly Business Investor Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from eco-friendly business investors on their investment habits, priorities, and communication preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select "Often", "Rarely", or "Never" to answer the question about how often you invest in businesses with a positive impact on the environment.
2. Choose the type of impact that matters most to you from the options listed.
3. Describe what drives your investment decision.
4. Enter the number of investments you make per year.
5. Enter your expected average investment size.
6. Select all that apply for your preferred investment amount range.
7. Choose the type of business model that you prefer.
8. Select the industry that you focus on.
9. Choose your preferred method of communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What makes an eco-friendly business** (`title`, `text`, `required: false`): This is a text field where you can describe what makes a business eco-friendly. Please provide a brief explanation.
* **How often do you invest in businesses with a positive impact on the environment** (`label`, `select_one`, `options: [Often, Rarely, Never]`, `required: false`): Choose how often you invest in businesses with a positive impact on the environment. This will help us understand your investment habits.
* **What type of impact matters most to you** (`label`, `select_one`, `options: [Environmental, Social, Financial, Growth]`, `required: false`): Select the type of impact that matters most to you. This could be environmental, social, financial, or growth.
* **What drives your investment decision** (`label`, `text`, `required: false`): Describe what drives your investment decision. This could be financial returns, personal values, or other factors.
* **How many investments do you make per year** (`label`, `number`, `options: []`, `required: false`): Enter the number of investments you make per year.
* **What is your expected average investment size** (`label`, `number`, `options: []`, `required: false`): Enter your expected average investment size.
* **What is your preferred investment amount range** (`label`, `select_multiple`, `options: [Less than $10,000, $10,000-$50,000, $50,000-$100,000, More than $100,000]`, `required: false`): Select all that apply for your preferred investment amount range.
* **What type of business model** (`label`, `select_one`, `options: [Scalable, Sustainable, Social, Hybrid]`, `required: false`): Choose the type of business model that you prefer.
* **What industry do you focus on** (`label`, `select_one`, `options: [Energy and Environment, Food and Agriculture, Healthcare, Finance]`, `required: false`): Select the industry that you focus on.
* **How do you prefer to communicate** (`label`, `select_one`, `options: [Email, Phone, In-person meetings, Video conferencing]`, `required: false`): Choose your preferred method of communication.
