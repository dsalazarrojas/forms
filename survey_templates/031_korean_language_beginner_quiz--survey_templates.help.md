# Korean Language Beginner Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Korean Language Beginner Quiz is a survey designed to assess your basic language skills and subject matter expertise in Korean. It is intended to help us better understand your language abilities and provide tailored guidance and resources to support your learning journey.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing your name, email address, phone number, and other requested information.
2. Select your language level from the options provided.
3. Choose the relevant quiz type and subject area of interest.
4. If you have achieved a different language proficiency level than listed, please describe it in the "Language Proficiency Other" field.
5. Indicate your subject of interest and any additional comments or notes.

* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Language Level** (`language_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current language level from the options provided.

* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are taking the quiz.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your quiz experience.
* **Language Proficiency** (`language_proficiency`, `select_one`, required): Select your current language proficiency level.
* **Language Proficiency Other** (`language_proficiency_other`, `text`, required): If you have achieved a different language proficiency level than listed, describe it in this field.
* **Subject** (`subject`, `select_one`, required): Choose the subject area of interest.
* **Subject Other** (`subject_other`, `text`, required): If not listed above, describe the subject area of interest.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing your language level and subject area of interest.

- If you have achieved a different language proficiency level than listed, please describe it in the "Language Proficiency Other" field.
- If you have any other subject area of interest not listed, please describe it in the "Subject Other" field.
