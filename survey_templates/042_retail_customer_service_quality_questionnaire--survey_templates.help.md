# Retail Customer Service Quality Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Customer Service Quality Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with a retail store's customer service. This form is used to gather information about the customer's visit, including the store location, date of visit, and interactions with sales representatives and store staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Choose the store location where you shopped by selecting the corresponding option from the list.
* If you wish to provide the date of your visit, enter it in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 01/01/2022).
* Provide the name of the sales representative who assisted you, if available.
* Rate the sales representative's quality by selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor from the list.
* Select all applicable options that describe the store environment, such as Cleanliness, Organization, and Product Availability.
* Rate the helpfulness and friendliness of the store staff by selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Location** (`store_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the store location where you shopped. This will help us to understand which store you are providing feedback on.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required: false): If you wish to provide the date of your visit, enter it in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`. This will help us to track your visit.
* **Sales Representative Name** (`sales_rep_name`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name of the sales representative who assisted you, if available.
* **Sales Representative Rating** (`sales_rep_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the sales representative's quality by selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor from the list.
* **Sales Representative Quality** (`sales_rep_quality`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the level of quality of the sales representative's service. This will help us to understand their performance.
* **Store Environment** (`store_environment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable options that describe the store environment, such as Cleanliness, Organization, and Product Availability.
* **Store Staff Helpfulness** (`store_staff_helpfulness`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the helpfulness of the store staff by selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor from the list.
* **Sales Staff Helpfulness** (`sales_staff_helpfulness`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the helpfulness of the sales staff by selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor from the list.
* **Store Staff Friendliness** (`store_staff_friendliness`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the friendliness of the store staff by selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor from the list.
* **Sales Staff Product Knowledge** (`sales_staff_product_knowledge`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the product knowledge of the sales staff by selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor from the list.
* **Store Staff Helpfulness with Issue** (`store_staff_helpfulness_with_issue`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the helpfulness of the store staff with an issue by selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor from the list.
* **Sales Staff Resolution** (`sales_staff_resolution`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the resolution of the sales staff by selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor from the list.

## Tips

* Please fill out as much or as little information as you are comfortable providing.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please describe them in the comments section (not shown here).
* Your feedback is highly valued and will help us to improve our customer service.
