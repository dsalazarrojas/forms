# Home Security Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Security Survey form is designed to collect information on home security features and ratings from clients. It's essential to fill out this form to provide detailed insights into your home security setup, allowing us to better understand your needs and provide personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Home Security Survey form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the first question: "What are you trying to accomplish with this survey?" This field is optional, and you can provide a brief description of your goals or objectives for the survey.
2.  Enter your contact information (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or updates) in the "Contact Information" field.
3.  In the "Client Information" field, provide any relevant details about your client or property.
4.  In the "Security Features" field, select all the security features that are currently installed on your property, such as "Motion-sensitive lighting" or "Security cameras."
5.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or updates).
6.  Provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
7.  Rate your overall security experience using the "Security Survey Rating" field.
8.  In the "Additional Comments" field, provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share about your home security setup.
9.  Provide a signature in the "Client Signature" field to confirm that you've filled out the form accurately.
10.  In the "Security Features 1" field, select all the security features currently installed on your property that you want to rate.
11.  Provide the date and time of the survey in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
12.  Rate your security experience for this particular property using the "Security Survey Rating 1" field.
13.  In the "Additional Notes" field, provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share about your home security setup.
14.  Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What are you trying to accomplish with this survey?** (Optional text field): This field is for providing a brief description of your goals or objectives for the survey.
    
*   **Contact Information** (Optional text field): This field is for entering any contact information so that we can get in touch with you for follow-up questions or updates.
    
*   **Client Information** (Optional text field): This field is for providing any relevant details about your client or property.
    
*   **Security Features** (Select multiple options field): This field allows you to select all the security features currently installed on your property, such as "Motion-sensitive lighting" or "Security cameras."
    
*   **Email** (Optional email field): This field is for entering your email address so that we can contact you for follow-up questions or updates.
    
*   **Contact Number** (Optional text field): This field is for providing your contact number so that we can contact you for follow-up questions or updates.
    
*   **Security Survey Rating** (Optional select one field): This field is for rating your overall security experience.
    
*   **Additional Comments** (Optional note field): This field is for providing any additional information or comments about your home security setup.
    
*   **Client Signature** (Optional text field): This field is for providing a signature to confirm that you've filled out the form accurately.
    
*   **Security Features 1** (Select multiple options field): This field is for rating the security features currently installed on your property.
    
*   **Date** (Optional date field): This field is for providing the date of the survey.
    
*   **Time** (Optional time field): This field is for providing the time of the survey.
    
*   **Security Survey Rating 1** (Optional select one field): This field is for rating your security experience for this particular property.
    
*   **Submit** (Optional text field): This field is for confirming your submission of the form.
    
*   **Additional Notes** (Optional note field): This field is for providing any additional information or comments about your home security setup.
