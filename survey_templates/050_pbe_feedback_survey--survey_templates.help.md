# PBE Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PBE Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from students on their experiences with Prophets. The form is used to assess how Prophets inform their classes, perform in class, encourage student attendance, create lesson plans, provide feedback, communicate effectively, and evaluate student outcomes. Your responses will help us improve the quality of Prophets' work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully, understanding what it's asking.
2. Select the responses that best fit your answers to each question.
3. Provide specific examples or details to support your feedback.
4. Leave a note if you have any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`id: 1`): This question is an introduction to the survey, providing an opportunity to understand the context and purpose of the form.
* **How Do Prophets Inform Their Classes** (`id: 2`): Please select all the ways that Prophets inform their classes, such as through lectures, discussions, or presentations.
* **How Do Prophets Perform In Class** (`id: 3`): Choose the level of performance that best describes Prophets' teaching skills, such as 1 (very poorly), 2 (fair), 3 (average), 4 (good), or 5 (excellent).
* **Do Prophets Encourage Students To Attend Class** (`id: 4`): Select the level of encouragement that best describes Prophets' attitude towards student attendance, such as 1 (very poorly), 2 (fair), 3 (average), 4 (good), or 5 (excellent).
* **How Effective Are The Lesson Plans** (`id: 5`): Choose the level of effectiveness that best describes the lesson plans, such as 1 (very poorly), 2 (fair), 3 (average), 4 (good), or 5 (excellent).
* **What Is The Best Way To Give Feedback** (`id: 6`): Select all the methods that you believe are the best way for Prophets to give feedback, such as in-person, email, or online.
* **Does Prophets Communicate Effectively** (`id: 7`): Choose the level of communication effectiveness that best describes Prophets, such as 1 (very poorly), 2 (fair), 3 (average), 4 (good), or 5 (excellent).
* **What Are The Student Outcomes** (`id: 8`): Please select all the student outcomes that you believe are the results of Prophets' teaching, such as better understanding, improved skills, or increased confidence.
* **What Is The Classroom Environment** (`id: 9`): Choose the level of comfort and engagement that best describes the classroom environment, such as 1 (very uncomfortable), 2 (fair), 3 (average), 4 (good), or 5 (excellent).
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 10`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions that you may have about Prophets or their teaching methods.
* **Conclusion** (`id: 11`): Leave a final summary of your feedback and any closing comments.
* **How Do We Evaluate Prophets** (`id: 12`): Choose the criteria that you believe should be used to evaluate Prophets' performance, such as attendance, participation, or assessment.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and thoughtfully.
* Provide specific examples or details to support your feedback.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help improve Prophets' work.
