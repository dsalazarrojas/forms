# # Reservations And Goals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reservations And Goals Survey form is designed to collect information from customers or potential customers about their reservations and goals. This information will be used to better understand their needs and preferences, and to tailor the experience accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the date of your reservation.
2. Enter your name and contact information.
3. Choose the service you require (Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner).
4. Indicate your priority level (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Select the time of your reservation.
6. Provide any additional message or information about your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Date**: Enter the date you would like to make your reservation.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your reservation.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address so we can send you confirmations and updates about your reservation.
* **Service Required**: Select the type of service you need (Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner).
* **Priority**: Indicate how urgent your reservation is (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Reservation Time**: Select the time you would like to make your reservation.
* **Message**: Enter any additional information or requests you have about your reservation.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure we can process your reservation correctly.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the "Message" field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* You can always add or remove fields as needed, depending on your preference.
* Please be as specific as possible when selecting your reservation date and time. 
* If your priority is High, you can expect a faster response time for your reservation request.
