# Men’s Skincare Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about men's skincare habits, preferences, and behaviors. Your responses will help us understand men's attitudes towards skincare and inform product development and marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the survey:

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer all questions truthfully.
3. Select the options that best describe your situation.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**
	+ This is the first page of the form, which provides a brief introduction to the survey and explains its purpose.
* **Age**
	+ Select your age from the options provided.
* **Gender**
	+ Select 'Yes' if you identify as male, 'No' if you do not.
* **Men's Current Skincare Routine**
	+ Select all the options that describe your current skincare routine.
* **Current Skincare Product Usage Frequency**
	+ Select how often you use skincare products.
* **Openness to New Products**
	+ Select all the options that describe your openness to trying new skincare products.
* **Men's Skincare Product Interest**
	+ Select all the options that describe your interest in different product features.
* **Men's Skincare Product Importance 10-100**
	+ Select all the options that describe the importance of different product features for a 10-100 scale.
* Note: These questions are repeated for different age ranges and product features. Please answer each question separately for each range and feature.

Please answer all questions truthfully and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
