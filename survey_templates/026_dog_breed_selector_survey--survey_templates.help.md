# dog_breed_selector_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you find the perfect dog breed that suits your lifestyle and preferences. Please answer the following questions to get started.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the sections below, and we'll do the rest to suggest a dog breed that's right for you!

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household**: Enter a brief description of your household, e.g., "I live with 2 people" or "I have a 1-bedroom apartment".
* **Lifestyle**: Tell us about your daily routine and activities, e.g., "I'm a busy professional" or "I love outdoor activities".
* **Preferences**: Select one or more breeds that appeal to you, e.g., "Golden Retriever" or "Other (please specify)".
* **Energy Level**: Choose how much energy you have to dedicate to exercising and playing with your dog, e.g., "High" for very active or "Low" for a more relaxed pace.
* **Size**: Select the size of the dog you're interested in, e.g., "Small" for a compact breed or "Extra Large" for a bigger companion.
* **Budget**: Enter your estimated budget for dog-related expenses, e.g., food, vet bills, toys, etc. (Note: This field is optional and will help us provide more tailored suggestions.)
* **Contact**: If you'd like to be contacted by a professional dog trainer or breeder, enter your email address. (Note: This field is optional and will only be used for follow-up conversations.)
* **Next Steps**: This is a space for you to note any additional information or questions you'd like to discuss with us.
