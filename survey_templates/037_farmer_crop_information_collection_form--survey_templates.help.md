### Farmer Crop Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data about farmers' crops to support various agricultural activities. It aims to gather essential information about crop types, planted and harvested dates, yields, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form in the following steps:
    *   Provide a brief description of the crop you want to collect information on.
    *   Choose the type of crop.
    *   Select the season for the crop (Summer, Fall, Spring, or Winter).
    *   Enter the date when the crop was planted.
    *   Enter the date when the crop was harvested.
    *   Describe the yield of the crop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Crop Details** (`crop_details`)

*   Provide a brief description of the crop you want to collect information on. This field helps us understand the specific details of the crop you're dealing with.

### **Crop Name** (`crop_name`)

*   Enter the name of the crop you're collecting data on. This field helps us to identify the type of crop.

### **Crop Type** (`crop_type`)

*   Select all the types of crops that apply to your data. For example, if you have both wheat and maize, select both.

### **Season** (`season`)

*   Choose the season for the crop (Summer, Fall, Spring, or Winter).

### **Planted Date** (`planted_date`)

*   Enter the date when the crop was planted.

### **Harvest Date** (`harvest_date`)

*   Enter the date when the crop was harvested.

### **Yield** (`yield`)

*   Describe the yield of the crop. This can include information about the quantity, quality, or any other relevant details.
