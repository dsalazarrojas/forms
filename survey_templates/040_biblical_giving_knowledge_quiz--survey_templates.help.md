# Biblical Giving Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge of biblical principles related to giving and stewardship. It will guide you through a series of questions that cover various topics, from the Hebrew word for giving to the significance of stewardship in Christianity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each section carefully, as indicated in the section headers.
2. Select the option that best answers each question.
3. Provide honest answers based on your understanding of the Bible and your personal experiences with giving.
4. Take your time and review each question before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification. This will help us keep track of your quiz results.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive your quiz results. We will not share your email address with anyone.
* **Church or Community Name** (`church_name`, text, required): If you are affiliated with a church or community, please enter its name.
* **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`, date, required): Please select the date you are taking this quiz.
* **Question 1 - What is the Hebrew word for giving or offering** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Choose the correct word:
	+ Tzedakah (a common Hebrew term for giving)
	+ Shalom (peace or harmony)
	+ Hesed (a type of giving or mercy)
	+ Chaburah (a Hebrew word for giving, but not as commonly used)
	+ I don't know (if you are unsure of the answer)
* **Question 2 - In the Old Testament, what was a tithe** (`question_2`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer:
	+ One tenth of income or produce
	+ One fifth of income or produce
	+ Voluntary offering
	+ Temple tax only
	+ I don't know (if you are unsure)
* ...
* **Question 12 - Which passage teaches that giving is better than receiving** (`question_12`, select_one, required): Choose the correct passage:
	+ Acts 20 35
	+ Romans 12 6
	+ 1 John 3 17
	+ Proverbs 21 14
	+ I don't know (if you are unsure)
* **How Do You Currently Practice Giving** (`giving_practices`, text, optional): Describe your current giving practices, if you'd like to share.
* **What Motivates Your Giving** (`motivation`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reasons that motivate your giving. 
	+ Faith and gratitude
	+ Compassion for others
	+ Church/ministry support
	+ Personal conviction
	+ Family example
	+ Community impact
* **How Would You Rate the Quiz Difficulty** (`quiz_difficulty`, select_one, required): Select how difficult you found the quiz:
	+ Too easy
	+ Easy
	+ Just right
	+ Difficult
	+ Too difficult
* **Interest in Learning More About Biblical Giving** (`learning_interest`, select_one, required): How interested are you in learning more about biblical giving?
	+ Very interested
	+ Somewhat interested
	+ Neutral
	+ Not interested
	+ Need more study
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any thoughts or questions about the quiz, please share them here.
