# staff_availability_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "staff_availability_survey" form is used to collect information about staff availability for work hours and preferred days of work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the project manager or team lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Survey Details** (`survey_details`, `text`, required)
* This field is a brief introduction to the form. Please describe your availability for work hours and preferred days of work.

### **Working Hours Start** (`working_hours_start`, `number`, optional)
* This field is used to select the start time of your work hours. Please select a time from 8:00 am to 15:00 pm.

### **Working Hours End** (`working_hours_end`, `number`, optional)
* This field is used to select the end time of your work hours. Please select a time from 8:00 am to 15:00 pm.

### **Preferred Days** (`preferred_days`, `select_multiple`, required)
* Select the days of the week you are available to work. Please choose one or more days from the list:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday

### **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `select_multiple`, required)
* Select the times of the day you are available to work. Please choose one or more times from the list:
	+ 8:00 am
	+ 9:00 am
	+ 10:00 am
	+ 11:00 am
	+ 12:00 pm
	+ 13:00 pm
	+ 14:00 pm
	+ 15:00 pm

### **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional)
* This field is an optional field for any additional comments or information you may want to provide. Please feel free to include any relevant details about your availability.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any conflicting information or concerns, contact the project manager or team lead.
* This form will help us create an optimal schedule for you and your team. Thank you for your cooperation!
