# NPS Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The NPS (Net Promoter Score) Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers on their satisfaction levels and likelihood to recommend our product or service. This data will help us identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to enhance customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the open-ended questions.
