# Internship Program Results Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Internship Program Results Report Form" is used to evaluate the performance and satisfaction of interns within a program. It helps assess the skills and knowledge gained, overall satisfaction, and areas for improvement for both the intern and the organisation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the internship application form to ensure you have access to the correct information.
2.  Select the status of the internship application (Completed, Deferred, or Rejected).
3.  If the internship was completed, provide the evaluation date.
4.  If you completed the internship, specify the evaluation method used (e.g., survey, feedback session, etc.).
5.  Select your overall performance during the internship (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory).
6.  List any skills you gained during the internship.
7.  Provide feedback to the organisation regarding your experience.
8.  Provide feedback to the intern, if applicable.
9.  Add any additional comments about your experience.
10. Record any evaluation notes or observations.
11. Identify any action items that arose from the internship.
12. Track the status of action items (Open, In Progress, Closed).
13. Confirm that you have signed the evaluation form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Internship Application** (`1`, `number`, optional): Enter the unique ID of the internship application, if applicable.
* **Application Status** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the internship application (Completed, Deferred, or Rejected).
* **Evaluation Date** (`3`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation if the internship was completed.
* **Evaluation Method** (`4`, `text`, optional): Specify the method used to evaluate your performance (e.g., survey, feedback session, etc.).
* **Intern Performance** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your overall performance during the internship (Excellent, Good, or Satisfactory).
* **Skills Gained** (`6`, `text`, optional): List any notable skills you gained during the internship.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`7`, `note`, optional): Record your overall satisfaction with the program. Use this field to elaborate on your experience and thoughts.
* **Comments** (`8`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback to the organisation about your experience.
* **Feedback to Intern** (`9`, `text`, optional): Offer feedback to the intern, if applicable.
* **Additional Comments** (`10`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Evaluation Notes** (`11`, `text`, optional): Record any notes or observations from the evaluation.
* **Action Items** (`12`, `text`, optional): Identify any action items that arose from the internship.
* **Action Items Status** (`13`, `select_multiple`, optional): Track the status of action items (Open, In Progress, Closed).
* **Evaluation Form Signed** (`14`, `text`, optional): Confirm that you have signed the evaluation form.

By following this guide, you should be able to effectively and efficiently complete the "Internship Program Results Report Form" for your evaluation.
