# Principles Of Management Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about management roles within an organization, to help create effective training programs tailored to management needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the organization from the options provided.
2. Indicate how often you lead training programs.
3. Enter the number of hours you have available each week for training.
4. Choose your current level of training experience.
5. Enter the number of team members you are directly responsible for.
6. Enter the average number of hours you work with your team per week.
7. Select the primary purpose of your role in the organization.
8. Enter the number of years you have been in your current role.
9. Select the types of training or development opportunities you would benefit from.
10. Select the primary industry or sector of your organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your role in the organization?** (`principle1`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ This field asks for your current position within the organization, such as Manager, Trainer, Team Lead, or HR Specialist.
* **How often do you lead training programs?** (`principle2`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field asks for the frequency of your involvement in leading training programs.
* **How much time do you have available each week for training?** (`principle3`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field asks for the number of hours available each week for training.
* **What is your current training experience?** (`principle4`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ This field asks for your current level of training experience, such as Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
* **How many team members are you directly responsible for?** (`principle5`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field asks for the number of team members you are directly responsible for.
* **On average, how many hours per week do you work with your team?** (`principle6`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field asks for the average number of hours per week you work with your team.
* **What is the primary purpose of your role in the organization?** (`principle7`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ This field asks for the main reason you are in your current position, such as Leadership, Training, Support, or Development.
* **How many years of experience do you have in your current role?** (`principle8`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field asks for the number of years you have been in your current position.
* **What type of training or development opportunities do you think you would benefit from?** (`principle9`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ This field asks for the types of training or development opportunities you would find beneficial, such as Soft skills training, Leadership development, or Technical skills training.
* **What is the primary industry or sector of your organization?** (`principle10`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ This field asks for the main industry or sector of your organization, such as Finance, Healthcare, or Education.
