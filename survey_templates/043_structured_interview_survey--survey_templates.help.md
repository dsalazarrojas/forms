# structured_interview_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "structured_interview_survey" form is designed to collect data from interviewers and candidates in a structured format. It helps to gather information about the candidate's performance, feedback, and progress throughout the interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, and ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
2. Be accurate and truthful when answering the questions and providing feedback.
3. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (text)**: Please provide a brief introduction to the interview process, including the date, time, and location of the interview.
* **Interviewer Details (text)**: Enter the name and contact information of the interviewer, including their name, email, and phone number. (Required)
* **Candidate Details (text)**: Enter the name and contact information of the candidate, including their name, email, and phone number. (Required)
* **Evaluation (select_one)**: Choose one of the following options for your evaluation of the candidate:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neither Agree or Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ Disagree
	+ Not Applicable
* **Comments (text)**: Enter any additional comments about the candidate's performance. (Optional)
* **Action Items (select_multiple)**: Choose the action items that apply to the candidate's performance:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Overall Rating (number)**: Enter a numerical rating of the candidate's performance, on a scale of 1-10. (Required)
* **Evaluation Scale 2 (select_one)**: Choose one of the following options for your evaluation of the candidate's performance:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neither Agree or Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ Disagree
	+ Not Applicable
* **Overall (number)**: Enter a numerical rating of the candidate's performance, on a scale of 1-10. (Required)
* **Additional Comments (text)**: Enter any additional comments about the candidate's performance. (Optional)
* **Follow-up questions (text)**: Enter any follow-up questions or areas for improvement. (Optional)
* **Follow Up Action Items (select_multiple)**: Choose the action items that apply to the candidate's performance:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Overall Rating 3 (number)**: Enter a numerical rating of the candidate's performance, on a scale of 1-10. (Required)
* **Feedback Comments (text)**: Enter any feedback comments about the candidate's performance. (Optional)
* **Follow Up Comments (text)**: Enter any follow-up comments about the candidate's performance. (Optional)
* **Follow Up Action Items 2 (select_multiple)**: Choose the action items that apply to the candidate's performance:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Overall Rating 4 (number)**: Enter a numerical rating of the candidate's performance, on a scale of 1-10. (Required)
* **Report ID (number)**: Enter a unique identifier for the report. (Required)
* **Report Date (date)**: Enter the date of the report. (Required)
* **Report Time (time)**: Enter the time of the report. (Required)
* **User ID (number)**: Enter the ID of the user who completed the form. (Required)
* **Created At (date)**: Enter the date and time the form was created. (Required)
* **Updated At (date)**: Enter the date and time the form was updated. (Required)
* **Submitted At (date)**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted. (Required)
* **Form ID (number)**: Enter the ID of the form. (Required)
* **Assigned To (text)**: Enter the name and contact information of the person assigned to review the report. (Optional)

Please note that all fields marked as "Required" are mandatory and must be filled out correctly to ensure the accuracy and reliability of the data collected.
