# Quality Of Life Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the quality of life (QoL) of patients. It asks about the patient's pain, anxiety, sleep patterns, medical history, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer for each question, using the options provided.
2. Fill in the text fields with your answers or comments.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) symbol.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Questions (1)
* **General Questions 1** (text): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your general health or well-being.

### Pain Scale (2)
* **Pain Scale** (select_one): Choose how much pain you have been experiencing lately:
	+ None
	+ A little
	+ Somewhat
	+ A lot
	+ Extremely

### Anxiety Scale (3)
* **Anxiety Scale** (select_one): Choose how much anxiety you have been experiencing lately:
	+ None
	+ A little
	+ Somewhat
	+ A lot
	+ Extremely

### Sleep Questions (4)
* **Sleep Questions** (select_multiple): Choose how many hours of sleep you get per night:
	+ 6-7 hours
	+ 7-8 hours
	+ 8-9 hours
	+ 9-10 hours
	+ More than 10 hours

### Medical Questions (5)
* **Medical Questions** (text): Enter any medical conditions, allergies, or other health concerns you have.

### Medication List (6)
* **Medication List** (text): List any medications you are currently taking.

### Medical History (7)
* **Medical History** (text): Enter any relevant medical history or procedures you have undergone.

### Allergies (8)
* **Allergies** (text): List any allergies you have.

### Medical Procedure Questions (9)
* **Medical Procedure Questions** (text): Enter any questions or concerns about medical procedures you have undergone.

### Medical Condition Questions (10)
* **Medical Condition Questions** (text): Enter any questions or concerns about your medical condition.

### Medical Condition (11)
* **Medical Condition** (text): Enter any relevant medical condition you have.

### Medical Procedure (12)
* **Medical Procedure** (text): Enter any medical procedures you have undergone.

### Patient Status (13)
* **Patient Status** (select_one): Choose your current status:
	+ Active
	+ Not active

### Medical Practice Name (14)
* **Medical Practice Name** (text): Enter the name of your medical practice or clinic.

### Medical Practice Location (15)
* **Medical Practice Location** (text): Enter the location of your medical practice or clinic.

### Patient Age (16)
* **Patient Age** (text): Enter your age.

### Patient Gender (17)
* **Patient Gender** (text): Enter your gender.

### Patient Contact (18)
* **Patient Contact** (text): Enter your contact information (phone number, email, etc.).

### Medical Staff Name (19)
* **Medical Staff Name** (text): Enter the name of the medical staff member who is assisting you.

### Medical Staff Title (20)
* **Medical Staff Title** (text): Enter the title of the medical staff member.

### Medical Staff Location (21)
* **Medical Staff Location** (text): Enter the location of the medical staff member.

### Medical Staff Contact (22)

### Medical Staff Email (23)
* **Medical Staff Email** (text): Enter the email of the medical staff member.

### Medical Staff Phone (24)

### General Questions 1 (26)
* **General Questions 1** (text): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your general health or well-being.

### General Questions 2 (27)
* **General Questions 2** (text): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your general health or well-being.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your answers are recorded accurately.
