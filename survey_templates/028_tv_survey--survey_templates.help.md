# TV Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TV Survey form is designed to collect feedback on TV-related topics from users. This form helps us understand user preferences and opinions on various aspects of TV, including ratings, categories, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the TV content you are providing feedback on.
2. Provide a brief description of the TV content, such as a show title or genre.
3. Rate the TV content on a scale of 1-10.
4. Choose the expertise level you feel is relevant to the TV content.
5. Select the channel where the TV content is available.
6. Rate the channel's availability and quality.
7. If you have any improvement suggestions, please provide them in the text field.
8. If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (tv_survey_category, text, optional): Select the category that best describes the TV content you are providing feedback on. This could be a genre, such as comedy or drama, or a specific type of TV show, such as a sports game or a news broadcast.
* **Description** (tv_survey_description, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the TV content, such as a show title or a brief summary.
* **Ratings** (tv_survey_ratings, number, optional): Rate the TV content on a scale of 1-10.
* **Expertise** (tv_survey_expertise, select_multiple, optional): Choose the level of expertise you feel is relevant to the TV content. This could be an area of study, a profession, or a hobby.
* **Channel Name** (tv_survey_channel_name, text, optional): Select the channel where the TV content is available. This could be a TV network, a streaming service, or a satellite provider.
* **Channel Ratings** (tv_survey_channel_ratings, number, optional): Rate the channel's availability and quality on a scale of 1-10.
* **Improvement** (tv_survey_improvement, text, optional): If you have any improvement suggestions, please provide them in the text field.
* **Online Storage** (tv_survey_online_storage, select_one, optional): Select the online storage option that best describes where you would like to store the TV content. This could be a cloud storage service, a personal computer, or a mobile device.
* **Additional Comments** (tv_survey_additional_comments, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them in the text field.
