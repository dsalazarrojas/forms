# dietary_preferences_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey template is designed to collect information about an individual's dietary preferences and habits.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer each question truthfully, as this information will help us better understand your needs and preferences.
* Select one option for each question. If you have multiple preferences or restrictions, select all that apply.
* Make sure to complete each section thoroughly, as this will help us understand your overall dietary profile.
