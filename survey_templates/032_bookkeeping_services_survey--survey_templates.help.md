# Bookkeeping Services Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your current bookkeeping practices, challenges, and preferences. Your responses will help us provide more effective bookkeeping services tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in your full name as it appears on your business cards or identification.
- Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your responses.
- If your business has a name, enter it. Otherwise, you can skip this field.
- Select the number of employees in your company.
- If you have an annual revenue, select the approximate range. If not applicable, select "None".
- Indicate how you currently handle bookkeeping tasks.
- If you use accounting software, select the name of the software you are using.
- Rate your satisfaction with your current bookkeeping system.
- Select all that apply regarding your current bookkeeping challenges.
- Select the services you would be interested in using.
- If you are looking to spend money on bookkeeping services, select a budget range. If not applicable, select "None".
- Indicate how often you would need support or services.
- Choose when you would like to make a decision about our services.
- If you have any suggestions for our services, feel free to share them.
- Select your preferred contact method for future communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (Text Field): Enter your full name as it appears on your business cards or identification.
* **Email Address** (Email Field): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your responses.
* **Company Name** (Text Field): If your business has a name, enter it. Otherwise, you can skip this field.
* **Company Size** (Select One): Select the number of employees in your company.
* **Annual Revenue** (Select One): If you have an annual revenue, select the approximate range. If not applicable, select "None".
* **Current Bookkeeping Method** (Select One): Indicate how you currently handle bookkeeping tasks.
* **Accounting Software Used** (Select One): If you use accounting software, select the name of the software you are using.
* **Satisfaction with Current System** (Select One): Rate your satisfaction with your current bookkeeping system.
* **Current Bookkeeping Challenges** (Select Multiple): Select all that apply regarding your current bookkeeping challenges.
* **Services You Would Use** (Select Multiple): Select the services you would be interested in using.
* **Monthly Budget for Services** (Select One): If you are looking to spend money on bookkeeping services, select a budget range. If not applicable, select "None".
* **Desired Service Frequency** (Select One): Indicate how often you would need support or services.
* **Timeline for Decision** (Select One): Choose when you would like to make a decision about our services.
* **Features or Services You Recommend** (Text Field): If you have any suggestions for our services, feel free to share them.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Select One): Select your preferred contact method for future communication.

## Tips
- Be as accurate and honest as possible when providing your information.
- Select all that apply for your current bookkeeping challenges, as this will help us understand your needs better.
- If you have any questions or need clarification about any field, feel free to ask.
- Your responses will be kept confidential and used to improve our bookkeeping services.
