# wellness_treatment_customer_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers who have received a wellness treatment at our facility to provide feedback on their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient name as we previously recorded it in our system.
2. Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up information.
3. Enter the name of the treatment you received.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the treatment (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
5. Enter any comments or feedback you have about your experience.
6. Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you with any follow-up questions.
7. Enter the date you visited our facility.
8. Enter the time of your visit.
9. Enter the duration of your visit.
10. Enter the name of the staff member who provided your treatment.
11. Enter the title of the staff member who provided your treatment.
12. Enter the email address of the staff member who provided your treatment.
13. Enter the name of the department where your treatment was provided.
14. Enter the email address of the department where your treatment was provided.
15. Enter the name of the facility where your treatment was provided.
16. Enter the address of the facility where your treatment was provided.
17. Enter any notes about your visit.
18. Enter any notes from the staff member who provided your treatment.
19. Enter a summary of the treatment outcome.
20. Enter your follow-up plan.
21. Enter the date of your next visit.
22. Enter the time of your next visit.
23. Enter any follow-up notes.
24. Finally, let us know if follow-up has been done or not.

* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as we previously recorded it in our system.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up information.

* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of your visit.
* **Visit Time**: Enter the time of your visit.
* **Visit Duration**: Enter the duration of your visit.
* **Staff Member Name**: Enter the name of the staff member who provided your treatment.
* **Staff Member Title**: Enter the title of the staff member who provided your treatment.
* **Staff Member Email**: Enter the email address of the staff member who provided your treatment.
* **Department Name**: Enter the name of the department where your treatment was provided.
* **Department Email**: Enter the email address of the department where your treatment was provided.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where your treatment was provided.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility where your treatment was provided.
* **Visit Notes**: Enter any notes about your visit.
* **Staff Notes**: Enter any notes from the staff member who provided your treatment.
* **Treatment Outcome**: Enter a summary of the treatment outcome.
* **Follow-up Plan**: Enter your follow-up plan.
* **Next Visit Date**: Enter the date of your next visit.
* **Next Visit Time**: Enter the time of your next visit.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Enter any follow-up notes.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the treatment (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
