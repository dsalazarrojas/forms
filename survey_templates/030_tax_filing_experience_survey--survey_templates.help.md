# Tax Filing Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on your recent tax filing experience. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve the tax filing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read the instructions and questions carefully. Answer each question as accurately as possible, and don't hesitate to ask for help if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Intro** (`intro_page`, `text`, required): Please read the instructions and questions carefully before starting.
*   **Satisfaction** (`question_1`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the level of satisfaction for the following experience.
    - Highly Satisfied: You were very satisfied with your experience.
    - Somewhat Satisfied: You were somewhat satisfied with your experience.
    - Neither Satisfied Nor Dissatisfied: You were neither satisfied nor dissatisfied with your experience.
    - Somewhat Dissatisfied: You were somewhat dissatisfied with your experience.
    - Very Dissatisfied: You were very dissatisfied with your experience.
