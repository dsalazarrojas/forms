# real_estate_advisor_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on the real estate advisor service, allowing the service provider to improve their offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your form submission (page 1). Provide a brief description of your experience with the real estate advisor service.
2. Rate your experience using the rating system (page 2). Select one of the following options:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
3. Add any comments about your rating (page 3). This is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback on your experience.
4. Rate your satisfaction level (page 4). Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
5. Add comments about your experience (page 5). This is an optional field for you to provide any additional feedback.
6. Enter your contact email (page 6). This will allow the service provider to contact you directly if they need more information.
7. Enter your contact phone number (page 7). This will allow the service provider to contact you directly if they need more information.
8. Add any additional comments (page 8). This is another opportunity to provide more feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your experience with the real estate advisor service.
* **Rating** (`advisor_rating`, number, required: false): Select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`, text, required: false): Add comments about your rating.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add comments about your experience.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so the service provider can contact you directly.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so the service provider can contact you directly.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional feedback.
