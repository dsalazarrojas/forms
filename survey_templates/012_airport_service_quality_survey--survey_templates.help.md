# Airport Service Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the quality of services provided by the airport. Your input will help airport management understand how they can improve the overall passenger experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Submit" to start the form.
2. Enter the date you visited the airport.
3. Choose the airport you visited from the list provided.
4. Select the purpose of your visit.
5. Rate the ease of check-in process from 1 (Very Difficult) to 5 (Very Easy).
6. Rate the security screening process from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
7. Rate the security screening wait time from 1 (Too Long) to 5 (Quick).
8. Rate the terminal cleanliness from 1 (Dirty) to 5 (Spotless).
9. Rate the wayfinding and signage quality from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
10. Rate the seating availability and comfort from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
11. Rate the quality and cleanliness of restrooms from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
12. Rate the options for food and beverages from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
13. Rate the pricing of food and beverages from 1 (Too Expensive) to 5 (Good Value).
14. Rate the availability of retail shopping options from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
15. Rate the availability and speed of WiFi from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
16. Rate the helpfulness and professionalism of staff from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
17. Rate the availability of accessibility services from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
18. Rate the information provided about parking facilities from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
19. Rate the availability of ground transportation options from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
20. Rate the overall airport experience from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
21. Rate how likely you are to recommend this airport from 1 (Very Unlikely) to 5 (Very Likely).
22. Add any suggestions for improvement (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date (1)**: Enter the date you visited the airport.
* **Airport Name (2)**: Select the airport you visited from the list provided.
* **Purpose of Visit (3)**: Choose the reason for your visit.
* **Ease of Check-in Process (4)**: Rate how easy or difficult it was to check-in for your flight.
* **Security Screening Process (5)**: Rate the quality of the security screening process.
* **Security Screening Wait Time (6)**: Rate how long or short you think the wait time was.
* **Terminal Cleanliness (7)**: Rate how clean or dirty you found the terminal to be.
* **Wayfinding and Signage Quality (8)**: Rate the quality of signs and directions provided in the terminal.
* **Seating Availability and Comfort (9)**: Rate the comfort and availability of seating options.
* **Restroom Quality and Cleanliness (10)**: Rate the quality and cleanliness of the restrooms.
* **Food and Beverage Options (11)**: Rate the variety and quality of food and beverage options.
* **Food and Beverage Pricing (12)**: Rate the price of food and beverages.
* **Retail Shopping Options (13)**: Rate the availability and variety of retail shopping options.
* **WiFi Availability and Speed (14)**: Rate the availability and speed of WiFi.
* **Staff Helpfulness and Professionalism (15)**: Rate the helpfulness and professionalism of staff.
* **Accessibility Services (16)**: Rate the availability and quality of accessibility services.
* **Parking Information and Ease (17)**: Rate the quality and availability of parking information.
* **Ground Transportation Options (18)**: Rate the availability and quality of ground transportation options.
* **Overall Airport Experience (19)**: Rate the overall quality of your experience at the airport.
* **Likely to Recommend (20)**: Rate how likely you are to recommend this airport.
* **Suggestions for Improvement (21)**: Add any suggestions or comments for improvement.

Remember, your feedback will help airport management improve the service quality and make your next visit more enjoyable!
