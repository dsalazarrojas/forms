# corporate_retreat_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from corporate retreat attendees to improve future event planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are filling it out correctly.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Provide clear and concise responses in the text fields.
4. Select the correct options for the questions that ask for selections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to our corporate retreat feedback survey** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field for any initial welcome message or introduction to the survey.
* **How satisfied were you with the event** (`satisfaction_level`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the event on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Session 1** (`session_one`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply to your experience with Session 1.
* **Session 2** (`session_two`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option that best describes your experience with Session 2.
* **Session 3** (`session_three`, `text`, required: false): Share your thoughts about Session 3 in the text field.
* **Date of the event** (`date_of_event`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event in the date format (e.g., 2024-03-16).
* **Time of the event** (`time_of_event`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event in the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Location of the event** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Describe the location of the event.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the event.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all the required questions.
* Use clear and concise language to answer the questions.
* If you are unsure about a question, contact the event organizers for clarification.
* The goal of this form is to provide constructive feedback to help improve future corporate retreats.
