# Fruit Juice Quality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the quality of a fruit juice. It has multiple sections, each with different fields related to various aspects of the fruit juice's quality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of fruit juice being assessed from the "Fruit" field.
2. Assess the quality of the juice based on your senses (taste, aroma, appearance, color) and rate it from 1-4 (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
3. Evaluate the texture and consistency of the juice by selecting from the provided options.
4. Choose the packaging type and material used for the juice.
5. Enter the price of the juice if available.
6. Provide information on the fruit variety and manufacturing location.
7. Check if the juice meets any certification requirements.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments about the juice.
