# Acoustics Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge on various acoustics-related topics. It consists of a series of questions that cover fundamental concepts in acoustics, including sound waves, frequency, speed of sound, decibel scale, acoustic materials, reverberation, Doppler effect, and self-assessed difficulty.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your student name in the first field.
2. Select the correct answer for each question from the provided options.
3. Rate the difficulty of the quiz at the end, if you feel it's necessary.
4. Enter the date and time you completed the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name to identify yourself as the quiz taker.
* **What is sound?** (`q1_sound_definition`, select_one, required): Choose the correct definition of a sound wave.
	+ A longitudinal wave
	+ A transverse wave
	+ A stationary particle
* **Frequency is measured in** (`q2_unit_of_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose the correct unit of measurement for frequency.
	+ Decibels
	+ Hertz
	+ Watts
	+ Amps
* **Sound travels fastest in** (`q3_speed_of_sound`, select_one, required): Choose the medium through which sound travels fastest.
	+ Air
	+ Water
	+ Steel
	+ Vacuum
* **A 10dB increase represents a 10x increase in** (`q4_decibel_scale`, select_one, required): Choose the correct interpretation of a 10dB increase.
	+ Speed
	+ Intensity
	+ Wavelength
* **Select materials that absorb sound** (`q5_acoustic_materials`, select_multiple, required): Choose all materials that can absorb sound.
	+ Foam
	+ Glass
	+ Carpet
	+ Metal
	+ Mineral Wool
* **What is reverberation?** (`q6_reverberation`, select_one, required): Choose the correct definition of reverberation.
	+ Echo
	+ Persistence of sound after source stops
	+ Sound refraction
* **The change in frequency due to motion is** (`q7_doppler_effect`, select_one, required): Choose the correct explanation of the Doppler effect.
	+ Reflection
	+ Absorption
	+ Doppler Effect
* **Rate the difficulty of this quiz** (`self_assessed_difficulty`, number, optional): If you feel the quiz was too easy or too hard, rate your difficulty here.
* **Date Taken** (`date_taken`, date, required): Enter the date and time you completed the quiz.
