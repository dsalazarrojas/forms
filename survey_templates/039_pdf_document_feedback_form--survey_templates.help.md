# pdf_document_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **pdf_document_feedback_form** is used to collect feedback and ratings from users about a PDF document. This feedback is essential for improving the document's quality, accuracy, and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely**: Provide your honest feedback and ratings for each field.
2. **Be specific with your feedback**: Clearly describe what you found helpful or unhelpful about the document.
3. **Check for errors**: Double-check your entries for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **PDF Document**: Please enter the title of the PDF document you are providing feedback on.
* **Rate This Document**: Select a rating (1-5) based on your overall satisfaction with the document:
	+ 1: Not helpful at all
	+ 2: Somewhat helpful
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Mostly helpful
	+ 5: Very helpful
* **What Did You Find Helpful?**: Enter your feedback about what you found helpful in the document. This could be a specific section, figure, or sentence that helped you.
* **Any Suggestions?**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them in the text area below.
* **EMail Address**: Enter a valid email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Comments**: This is a free text area where you can add any additional comments or feedback about the document.
