# Team Member Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Team Member Engagement Survey is designed to collect feedback from team members on various aspects of their work experience, including their satisfaction with their work environment, manager, and overall well-being. This data will help managers and leadership identify potential areas for improvement and provide targeted support to team members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and understand what is being asked.
2. Choose the response that best reflects your honest opinion.
3. Fill in all required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
4. If you need help with a particular question, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
