# Solar Program Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Program Survey form is designed to collect information about solar-powered projects, helping us to understand the effectiveness and efficiency of our solar program. Please fill out this form to provide your input and help us improve our program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible for each field.
- Make sure to review the fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure you haven't missed any important information.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any relevant background information about the project. Feel free to describe the project's purpose, goals, or any notable features.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the company or organization running the solar project.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the physical address of the company or organization running the solar project.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of the person responsible for the project.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the project.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the project.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, optional): Describe the specifics of the project, such as its goals, scope, or timeline.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the current status of the project from the dropdown options: Active or Inactive.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether the project is located in one or multiple locations, using the dropdown options: Yes or No.
* **Solar System Size** (`solar_system_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the size of the solar system, if relevant.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the project's start date, if applicable.

Note: If a field is marked as optional, feel free to skip it if it doesn't apply to your project.
