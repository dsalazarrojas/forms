# Canadian Financing Initiative Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Canadian Financing Initiative Survey is designed to collect information from applicants seeking financing under the Canadian government's financing initiatives. The form helps the government assess applicants' qualifications and project viability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section carefully, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a fair assessment.
3. Review and update your application before submitting to avoid duplication and ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the full legal name of your entity.
* **Type of Entity** (`entity_type`, select_one, required): Choose the legal structure of your organization, e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, or Non-Profit.
* **Primary Industry** (`primary_industry`, select_one, required): Select the sector that best describes your work, e.g., Agriculture, Manufacturing, Technology, or Finance.
* **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, required): Indicate your organization's total gross revenue from the last fiscal year, e.g., Less than $500k, $500k-$2M, or Over $10M.
* **Province of Operation** (`province_operation`, select_one, required): Enter the province where your organization is primarily based, e.g., Alberta, BC, or Ontario.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the individual representing your organization.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email for communication.
* **Purpose of Funding** (`funding_purpose`, select_multiple, required): Specify how you plan to use the funds, e.g., Expansion, Equipment, Working Capital, or R&D.
* **Estimated Funding Required** (`funding_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of funding you are seeking in CAD.
* **Current Financial Challenges** (`financial_challenges`, text, required): Describe any current barriers to growth.
* **Have you applied for other initiatives** (`previous_applications`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have applied for other government or private grants/loans.
* **Anticipated Job Creation** (`job_creation`, number, required): Estimate the number of new roles you anticipate creating within 12 months.
* **Community Impact Description** (`community_impact`, text, required): Describe how this funding will benefit your local community.
* **Likelihood of Project Success without Funding** (`project_success_likelihood`, select_one, required): Evaluate the likelihood of your project's success without funding, e.g., Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, or Very High.
* **Preferred Financing Type** (`preferred_financing`, select_one, required): Select your top choice for funding structure, e.g., Loan, Grant, Equity, or Hybrid.
* **Document Upload Consent** (`upload_consent`, select_one, required): Agree to provide full financial records upon request.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any further comments or details.
