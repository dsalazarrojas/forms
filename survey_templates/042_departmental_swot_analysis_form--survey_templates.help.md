# Departmental SWOT Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by department members for a SWOT analysis, which helps identify factors that can impact the department's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in every section completely and accurately, considering each field's purpose and description below.
- Choose "Yes" or "No" for each multiple-choice question.
- Select only one option for the SWOT score question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Internal Environment** (`internal_environment`): This section asks if the department is well-maintained, organized, and managed. Check if the internal environment is favorable to the department's success.
	+ If "Yes", the department is well-maintained and has a positive internal environment.
	+ If "No", the department needs improvement in its internal environment.
* **External Environment** (`external_environment`): This section assesses the department's external situation, such as market conditions, competition, or regulatory pressures. Consider the department's ability to adapt and respond to these external factors.
	+ If "Yes", the department is well-adapted to the external environment.
	+ If "No", the department struggles with external factors.
* **Organizational** (`organizational`): This section examines the department's structure, management, and resources. Consider if the department's organizational aspects support its success.
	+ If "Yes", the department has a solid organizational foundation.
	+ If "No", the department's organizational structure hinders its progress.
* **Internal Resources** (`internal_resources`): This section reviews the department's financial, physical, human, and technological resources. Assess if the department has sufficient resources for its tasks.
	+ If "Yes", the department has adequate resources.
	+ If "No", the department lacks sufficient resources.
* **Internal Capabilities** (`internal_capabilities`): This section focuses on the department's abilities and skills, such as its technical expertise or innovation capacity. Evaluate if the department has the necessary skills for its success.
	+ If "Yes", the department possesses the necessary capabilities.
	+ If "No", the department lacks the necessary skills.
* **Internal Strengths** (`internal_strengths`): This section identifies the department's strong points, such as its expertise or customer relationships. Consider the department's internal strengths that support its success.
	+ If "Yes", the department has notable internal strengths.
	+ If "No", the department lacks internal strengths.
* **Internal Weaknesses** (`internal_weaknesses`): This section highlights the department's challenges or limitations, such as inefficient processes or inadequate communication. Assess if the department has internal weaknesses that hinder its progress.
	+ If "Yes", the department has notable internal weaknesses.
	+ If "No", the department has fewer internal weaknesses.
* **External Threats** (`external_threats`): This section examines the external factors that may harm the department, such as economic downturns or regulatory changes. Consider if the department is vulnerable to external threats.
	+ If "Yes", the department faces notable external threats.
	+ If "No", the department is not vulnerable to external threats.
* **External Opportunities** (`external_opportunities`): This section identifies external factors that may benefit the department, such as market trends or partnerships. Assess if the department is aware of and can capitalize on external opportunities.
	+ If "Yes", the department has notable external opportunities.
	+ If "No", the department lacks awareness of external opportunities.
* **SWOT Score** (`swot_score`): This question asks you to select one option that best describes your department's SWOT situation, considering all the previous questions. Please choose the option that best fits your assessment.
	+ Select the option that aligns with your evaluation of the department's internal and external factors.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling in the form.
- Consult with your colleagues and superiors when needed.
- Review and revise your answers to ensure consistency and coherence.
- Use this SWOT analysis to inform your department's strategic planning and decision-making process.
