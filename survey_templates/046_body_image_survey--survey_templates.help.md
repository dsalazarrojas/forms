# Body Image Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on a person's body image, including satisfaction with different aspects of their body, media and social media influence, exercise motivation, body comparison frequency, general confidence level, and additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly, selecting one option that best reflects your feelings and experiences.
2. For questions with a "Select One" type, choose one of the provided options.
3. For questions with a "Text" type, enter your answer in the provided field.
4. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent ID**: This is an optional field to help us identify you, but it is not required. If you choose to provide it, please enter your anonymous identifier.
* **Age**: This question asks for your age.
* **Gender**: This question is asking you to select how you identify your gender.
* **Ethnicity**: This question is optional and allows you to select your ethnic background.
* **Height**: If you want to provide your height, please enter it in feet and inches.
* **Weight**: If you want to provide your weight, please enter it in pounds.
* **Overall Body Satisfaction**: This question asks for your overall satisfaction with your body.
* **Satisfaction When Clothed**: This question asks for your satisfaction with your body when you are clothed.
* **Satisfaction in Swimsuit**: This question asks for your satisfaction with your body when you are in a swimsuit.
* **Satisfaction in Formal Wear**: This question asks for your satisfaction with your body when you are in formal attire.
* **Satisfaction in Workout Clothes**: This question asks for your satisfaction with your body when you are in workout clothes.
* **Satisfaction with Face**: This question asks for your satisfaction with your face.
* **Satisfaction with Arms**: This question asks for your satisfaction with your arms.
* **Satisfaction with Abdomen**: This question asks for your satisfaction with your abdomen.
* **Satisfaction with Thighs**: This question asks for your satisfaction with your thighs.
* **Satisfaction with Legs**: This question asks for your satisfaction with your legs.
* **Media Influence on Body Image**: This question asks for the influence of media on your body image.
* **Social Media Influence**: This question asks for the influence of social media on your feelings about your body.
* **Exercise Motivation**: This question asks for your motivation for exercising.
* **Body Comparison Frequency**: This question asks for how often you compare your body to others.
* **General Confidence Level**: This question asks for your general confidence level.
* **Body Image Impact on Daily Life**: This question asks for the impact of your body image on your daily life.
* **Support System**: This question is optional and asks if you have support for your body image concerns.
* **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or thoughts you would like to share.
