# Veterinary Marketing Outreach Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from veterinary professionals and businesses about their marketing outreach efforts and experiences with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all relevant marketing channels used by your veterinary clinic or business.
2. Provide the awareness level of our marketing efforts among your target audience.
3. Indicate the effectiveness of our marketing channels.
4. Provide additional comments or feedback about your experience with our marketing outreach efforts.
5. If you are representing a veterinary clinic or business, provide the contact information of the person completing this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marketing Channels** (`survey_channel`, select_multiple): Select all marketing channels used by your veterinary clinic or business, such as social media, email, print advertising, etc.
* **Campaign Awareness** (`campaign_awareness`, number): Provide the awareness level of our marketing efforts among your target audience (e.g., 1-10).
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_one): Select the target audience of your veterinary clinic or business (e.g., Animal Health Brands, Veterinary Clinics, Pet Owners).
* **Participant Type** (`survey_participant`, select_one): Indicate the type of person completing this form (e.g., Veterinarian, Veterinary Technician, Clinic Staff).
* **Marketing Channel Frequencies** (`marketing_channels`, select_multiple): Indicate the frequency of use of marketing channels (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Effectiveness Level** (`effectiveness_level`, number): Provide the level of effectiveness of our marketing channels.
* **Additional Comments** (`survey_comments`, note): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our marketing outreach efforts.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email): Provide the contact email address of the person completing this form.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Provide the contact phone number of the person completing this form.
* **Veterinary Clinic Name** (`veterinary_clinic_name`, text): If you are representing a veterinary clinic, provide the name of the clinic.
* **Veterinary Clinic Address** (`veterinary_clinic_address`, text): If you are representing a veterinary clinic, provide the address of the clinic.
* **Veterinary Clinic City** (`veterinary_clinic_city`, text): If you are representing a veterinary clinic, provide the city of the clinic.
* **Veterinary Clinic Zip** (`veterinary_clinic_zip`, text): If you are representing a veterinary clinic, provide the zip code of the clinic.
* **Veterinarian** (`veterinarian`, text): If you are representing a veterinary clinic, provide the name of the veterinarian.
* **Veterinarian Phone** (`veterinarian_phone`, text): If you are representing a veterinary clinic, provide the phone number of the veterinarian.
* **Clinic Staff** (`clinic_staff`, text): If you are representing a veterinary clinic, provide the name of the clinic staff.
* **Clinic Staff Phone** (`clinic_staff_phone`, text): If you are representing a veterinary clinic, provide the phone number of the clinic staff.
* **Animal Health Brand** (`animal_health_brand`, select_one): Select the animal health brand associated with your veterinary clinic or business.
* **Survey Participation** (`survey_participation`, select_one): Indicate your level of participation in our marketing outreach efforts (e.g., Participating, Not Participating).
* **Marketing Channel Impact** (`marketing_channel_impact`, select_multiple): Indicate the impact of marketing channels on your business (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Channel Awareness** (`channel_awareness`, select_one): Select the level of awareness of our marketing efforts among your target audience (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Survey Participation** (`survey_participation`, select_one): Indicate your level of participation in our marketing outreach efforts (e.g., Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neither Agree nor Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our marketing outreach efforts.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are representing a veterinary clinic or business, please ensure that you are authorized to complete this form and that the information provided is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please contact us directly.
* This form is for survey purposes only and will help us improve our marketing outreach efforts.
