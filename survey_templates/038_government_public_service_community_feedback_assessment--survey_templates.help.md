# Government Public Service Community Feedback Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Government Public Service Community Feedback Assessment form is a tool for gathering feedback from the public on their experiences with government public services. This form is designed to collect data on various aspects of the services provided, including service areas, contact methods, and overall satisfaction. The information collected will be used to identify areas for improvement and to help provide better services to the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the survey questions in the space provided.
2. Select the service areas that you have interacted with during your recent experience with a government public service.
3. Choose how you typically contact the service you interacted with (e.g. phone, email, in-person).
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service you received.
5. If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the space provided.
6. Indicate whether you would like to submit your feedback to the government (or not).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey questions**: This section is where you will answer the questions about your recent experience with a government public service. Please provide as much detail as you can about your experience.
- **Service areas**: This section allows you to select the areas of government service that you have interacted with recently. Please select all that apply.
- **Service area**: In this section, you will select one service area from the options provided.
- **Contact method**: Here, you will select how you typically contact the service you interacted with.
- **Email**: Please provide your email address if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive information about future services.
- **Phone**: If you have a phone number, please provide it here.
- **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions for improvement, please provide them in this space.
- **Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service you received, using the scale provided.
- **Submit**: Finally, indicate whether you would like to submit your feedback to the government. If you are unsure, you can opt-out and your feedback will not be submitted.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions or options, you can contact the government for clarification.
- Your feedback is confidential and will be used to improve services for the community.
- If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the comments section.
