# Abstract Decline Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from abstract declinees to improve the submission process. Your response will help us understand your experience and suggestions for enhancements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly to provide valuable feedback.
2. Please answer all required questions, marked with \* (star).
3. You can skip the optional sections if you feel they are not applicable to your experience.
4. If you have additional comments or feedback, feel free to include them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, *): Enter your full name as you would like to be listed in the feedback response.
* **Email address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, *): Provide your email address to contact you for follow-up questions or to acknowledge your feedback.
* **Organization or Institutional Affiliation** (`affiliation`, `text`, *): If applicable, enter your organization or institutional affiliation.
* **Title of the Declined Abstract** (`abstract_title`, `text`, *): Enter the title of the abstract that was declined.
* **Name of the Conference, Journal, or Program** (`conference_or_program`, `text`, *): Enter the name of the conference, journal, or program where your abstract was submitted.
* **How Clear was the Decline Notification** (`decision_clarity`, `select_one`): Rate how clear the decline notification was.
	+ Very clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat unclear
	+ Very unclear
* **Did you Receive Constructive Feedback with the Decline Notice** (`feedback_received`, `select_one`): Choose whether you received feedback with the decline notification.
	+ Yes - detailed feedback
	+ Yes - brief feedback
	+ No feedback was provided
* **If Feedback was Provided, How Helpful was it for Improving your Work** (`feedback_helpfulness`, `select_one`): Rate the helpfulness of the feedback you received.
	+ Very helpful
	+ Somewhat helpful
	+ Not helpful
	+ Not applicable
* **How would you Rate the Timeliness of the Decision Notification** (`timeliness`, `select_one`): Rate the timeliness of the decline notification.
	+ Very timely
	+ Acceptable
	+ Somewhat delayed
	+ Significantly delayed
* **How Fair do you Feel the Review Process was** (`fairness_perception`, `select_one`): Rate the fairness of the review process.
	+ Very fair
	+ Somewhat fair
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat unfair
	+ Very unfair
* **Were the Review Criteria Clearly Communicated Before Submission** (`review_criteria_transparency`, `select_one`): Rate whether the review criteria were clearly communicated before submission.
	+ Yes - very clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ No - criteria were not clear
* **Would you Submit an Abstract to this Venue in the Future** (`would_submit_again`, `select_one`): Choose whether you would submit an abstract to this venue in the future.
	+ Definitely yes
	+ Probably yes
	+ Unsure
	+ Probably not
	+ Definitely not
* **What Improvements would you Suggest for the Review and Communication Process** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, *): Share your suggestions for improving the submission process.
* **How would you Rate your Overall Experience with the Submission Process** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`): Rate your overall experience with the submission process.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Any Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, `text`, *): Share any additional comments or feedback.
