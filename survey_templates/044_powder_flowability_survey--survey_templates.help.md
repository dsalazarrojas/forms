# Powder Flowability Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Powder Flowability Survey is a form designed to collect user feedback and input on the powder flowability of various powders. This data will be used to improve the design and production of powders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the correct options for each question based on your experience with the powder.
2. Ensure that you answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or assistance.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.
