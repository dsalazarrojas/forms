# euthanasia_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form aims to collect data for a study on euthanasia-related attitudes, behavior, and demographic information. The questions are designed to elicit respondents' opinions, experiences, and demographics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through each page carefully to ensure you understand each question.
2. Answer each question according to your genuine experiences and opinions.
3. Provide accurate demographic information, if applicable.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **demographic_info**: Please provide your demographic information, such as your age, gender, and ethnicity. This will help us understand your background and context.
* **attitudes**: Share your attitudes or opinions on the topic of euthanasia.
* **behavior**: Describe your behavior or actions regarding euthanasia.
* **euthanasia**: Please select all that apply regarding your experiences or opinions on euthanasia.
* **age**: Enter your age to help us understand your demographic range.
* **gender**: Select your gender from the provided options.
* **ethnicity**: Select your ethnicity from the provided options.
* **education**: Choose your level of education from the provided options.
* **income**: Enter your annual income to ensure we can analyze the data accurately.
* **employment**: Select your employment status from the provided options.
* **healthcare**: Describe your experiences or opinions on healthcare related to euthanasia.
* **medical_history**: Provide any relevant medical history that may be related to euthanasia.
* **contact_info**: Enter your contact information, including name, address, and phone number, to ensure we can follow up with you if needed.
* **emergency_contact**: Please provide an emergency contact person's name and phone number.
* **survey_date**: Enter the date you completed this survey.

Note that all questions marked as required must be completed to ensure accurate and valid data.
