# Marital Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marital Satisfaction Survey is a tool to help you understand your relationship satisfaction levels. Please answer the questions as honestly as possible, and do not hesitate to provide feedback or comments about your spouse.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your relationship with your spouse.
2. Be honest and provide the best possible answer to each question.
3. You can answer 'Yes' or 'No' for select fields.
4. Enter a specific time for field 5.
5. Enter a number for field 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Marital Satisfaction Survey 1
* **What do you think about your relationship satisfaction?** (`marital_satisfaction_survey_1`, `note`, optional): Describe your thoughts and feelings about your relationship.
* **What do you think about your marriage satisfaction?** (`marital_satisfaction_survey_2`, `note`, optional): Share your thoughts and feelings about your marriage.
* **What do you think about your communication with your spouse?** (`marital_satisfaction_survey_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer 'Yes' if you usually have effective communication with your spouse, or 'No' if you don't.
* **How often do you have a romantic date night with your spouse?** (`marital_satisfaction_survey_4`, `select_one`, optional): Answer 'Yes' if you regularly have date nights with your spouse, or 'No' if you don't.
* **What time do you usually go to bed?** (`marital_satisfaction_survey_5`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you usually go to bed, e.g., 10:30 PM.
* **How many times have you had an argument with your spouse in the last week?** (`marital_satisfaction_survey_6`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of arguments you've had with your spouse in the last week.
* **What do you think about your sex life with your spouse?** (`marital_satisfaction_survey_7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer 'Yes' if you are satisfied with your sex life, or 'No' if you're not.
* **How satisfied are you with your financial situation with your spouse?** (`marital_satisfaction_survey_8`, `note`, optional): Share your thoughts and feelings about your financial situation with your spouse.
* **How satisfied are you with your overall relationship?** (`marital_satisfaction_survey_9`, `select_one`, optional): Answer 'Yes' if you are satisfied with your overall relationship, or 'No' if you're not.

## Tips
* Answer the questions honestly and with as much detail as possible.
* Remember, this is a personal survey, and your answers are for your eyes only.
* If you have any feedback or comments, feel free to add them at the end of the form.
