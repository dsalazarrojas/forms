# AI Tutoring Research Participation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in participating in the AI tutoring research study. It gathers information on your profile, preferences, and availability to help researchers in better understanding how AI tutoring can be effectively used.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a smooth completion process, follow these steps:

* Carefully read each field's label and hint provided.
* Enter your details accurately and honestly.
* Choose the options that best fit your situation.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: Your complete legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: A valid email for the research study communications.
* **Age Group**: Select the age range that applies to you:
	+ Under 18
	+ 18-24
	+ 25-34
	+ 35-44
	+ 45-54
	+ 55-64
	+ 65+
* **Highest Education Level Completed**: Select the highest level of education you have achieved:
	+ High School
	+ Some College
	+ Bachelor's Degree
	+ Master's Degree
	+ Doctoral Degree
	+ Other
* **Subject Areas of Interest**: Select the subjects you would like AI tutoring in. You can choose multiple subjects:
	+ Mathematics
	+ Science
	+ Language Arts
	+ Social Studies
	+ Foreign Languages
	+ Computer Science
	+ Other
* **Current Grade Level**: If you're currently enrolled in school, provide your current grade level. (Optional)
* **Previous Experience with AI Tutoring**: Have you used AI-based learning tools before?
	+ None
	+ Limited
	+ Moderate
	+ Extensive
	+ Professional
* **Learning Goals**: What do you hope to achieve through AI tutoring?
	+ Enter your specific goals and objectives.
* **Preferred Learning Style**: How do you learn best?
	+ Visual
	+ Auditory
	+ Reading/Writing
	+ Kinesthetic
	+ Mixed
* **Available Study Time**: How many hours can you dedicate to tutoring per week? (Optional)
* **Technology Access**: What devices do you have access to for AI tutoring?
	+ Desktop Computer
	+ Laptop
	+ Tablet
	+ Smartphone
	+ Internet Connection
	+ Printer
* **Consent to Participate**: I agree to participate in the AI tutoring research study.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Consent to Data Collection**: I agree to allow collection of my learning data for research purposes.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Weekly Availability**: What times are you typically available for AI tutoring sessions? (Optional)

This guide will help ensure that the form is completed accurately and effectively, providing valuable information for the research study.
