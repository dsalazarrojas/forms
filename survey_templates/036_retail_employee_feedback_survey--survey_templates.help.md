# Retail Employee Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from retail employees on their work experience, helping management or HR teams understand their store's operational aspects and employee needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your store location from the list of options.
2. Choose your role within the retail store.
3. Enter your employee name if applicable.
4. Share your feedback on your experience.
5. Choose the store's operational hours of opening and closing.
6. Select the days and durations of your store's operational hours.
7. Evaluate your rating of the store's performance.
8. Provide the date of your feedback.
9. Choose whether you have been assigned to work at a specific store location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Location**: This field lets you select your current store location. You can choose one of the options listed.
* **Role**: Select your role within the store from the provided options.
* **Employee Name**: If you're an employee, enter your name here. It's an optional field.
* **Feedback**: This is where you can provide your feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Store Hours**: Select the time your store opens and closes.
* **Store Open Time**: Choose the time your store opens. This is related to store_hours field.
* **Store Close Time**: Choose the time your store closes. This is related to store_hours field.
* **Store Open Days**: Select the days your store is open.
* **Store Open Durations**: Choose the duration of your store's operational hours.
* **Store Close Days**: Select the days your store is closed.
* **Store Close Durations**: Choose the duration of your store's operational hours.
* **Employee Rating**: This is an optional rating for your employee's overall performance.
* **Feedback Date**: Enter the date you're providing this feedback.
* **Assigned To**: Select whether you have been assigned to a specific store location.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the relevant fields to provide a comprehensive feedback.
* Select only the options that accurately reflect your experience.
* Ensure you provide clear and concise feedback in the 'Feedback' field.
* Use the 'Employee Rating' field to give an honest evaluation of your performance.
