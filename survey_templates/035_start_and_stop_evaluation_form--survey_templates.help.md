# Start And Stop Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the start and stop dates of evaluation processes, allowing users to track and manage their progress.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the start date for the evaluation process.
2. Select the stop date for the evaluation process.
3. Choose the evaluation process to be evaluated from the list of available options.
4. Select the programs related to the evaluation process.
5. Decide whether to continue or halt the evaluation process.
6. Provide a reason for your decision, if necessary.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **start_date**: Enter the date when the evaluation process began.
* **stop_date**: Enter the date when the evaluation process ended.
* **evaluation_process**: Select the process being evaluated, such as "Process 1", "Process 2", or "Process 3".
* **evaluation_program**: Select the programs related to the evaluation process, such as "Program 1", "Program 2", or "Program 3". You can select multiple programs.
* **decision_to_continue**: Decide whether to continue or halt the evaluation process. Options are "Continue" or "Halt".
* **evaluation_reason**: Provide a reason for your decision to continue or halt the evaluation process.
* **evaluation_comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the evaluation process.
* **submit**: Click to submit the evaluation form and record your changes.
