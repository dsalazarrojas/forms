# Holiday Card Signature Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for holiday cards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
2. Type a personal message for the card recipient in the "Greeting Message" field.
3. Add your signature to the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (User Name, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it should appear on the holiday card.
* **Greeting Message** (Greeting Message, text, required/optional): Type a personal message for the card recipient. This is your chance to express your holiday wishes or send a special greeting.
* **Signature** (Signature, text, required/optional): Add your handwritten or digital signature to the holiday card. This can be a simple printed signature or an artistic flourish, depending on your preference.
