# Anime Character Interaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anime Character Interaction Survey is a research tool designed to collect information about how anime fans interact with anime characters. The survey aims to understand audience behavior and preferences, helping creators and fans better understand their audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Respondent Information section with your details.
2. Answer the questions in the Viewing Habits section about your anime watching habits.
3. In the Character Preferences section, select your favorite character archetypes and traits.
4. In the Character Attachment section, rate your emotional connection with characters and share your favorite anime character.
5. In the Community and Fandom section, describe your engagement with the anime community and preferred platforms.
6. Finally, provide any additional feedback or suggestions in the Additional Feedback section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`your_name`, Text, Required): Your name will be used to address you in our research results. Please enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): This will be used for follow-up communication, if needed. Enter a valid email address.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, Select One, Optional): Select your age range.
* **How much anime do you watch** (`anime_experience`, Select One, Required): Choose your viewing frequency.
* **Favorite Anime Genres** (`preferred_genres`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all genres that appeal to you.
* **Where do you watch anime** (`viewing_platform`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your preferred platforms.
* **Favorite Character Archetype** (`favorite_character_archetype`, Select One, Optional): Select the type of character that appeals to you.
* **Most Important Character Traits** (`character_traits`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the traits that make a character compelling to you.
* **How do you interact with characters** (`interaction_experiences`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your behaviors.
* **Emotional Connection** (`emotional_connection`, Select One, Optional): Rate your emotional investment with characters.
* **Favorite Anime Character** (`character_favorites`, Text, Optional): Share your favorite anime character.
* **Why is this your favorite character** (`why_favorite`, Text, Optional): Explain what makes this character special.
* **Community Engagement** (`community_engagement`, Select One, Optional): Describe your level of engagement with the anime community.
* **Preferred Community Platforms** (`community_preferences`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose where you interact with fans.
* **Fan Content Interests** (`fan_content_interest`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your favorite fan content types.
* **Suggestions for Future Anime Character Design** (`suggestions`, Text, Optional): Share your ideas for future character design.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Any other thoughts or feedback.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Select all applicable genres and platforms.
* Choose your favorite character archetypes and traits.
* Rate your emotional connection with characters realistically.
* Provide specific examples when sharing your favorite characters and experiences.
* Keep your responses clear and concise.
* Thank you for participating in this survey!
