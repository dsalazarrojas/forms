# Facility Modernization Progress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Modernization Progress Survey is used to track the progress of facility modernization projects. It helps the program team to identify which projects are completed, which ones are ongoing, and which ones are facing obstacles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the phase of the modernization project that you are most concerned with.
2. Choose whether the phase is completed (`Yes`) or not (`No`).
3. Select all phases that have been completed.
4. Describe any obstacles you are experiencing.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback about the project.
6. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
7. Enter your phone number for follow-up calls or notifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Modernization Progress Survey** (`facility_modernization_progress_survey`, `select_one`):
	+ This is a select-one field where you will choose the phase of the modernization project that you are most concerned with.
* **Site** (`site_location`, `select_one`):
	+ This field is used to select the site where the modernization project is located.
* **Select a site** (`site_location_options`, `select_multiple`):
	+ In this field, select all the sites that apply to your modernization project.
* **What phase of the modernization project are you most concerned with?** (`renovation_phase`, `select_one`):
	+ This field is used to select the phase of the modernization project that you are most concerned with.
* **What is the current progress of your phase?** (`current_progress`, `select_multiple`):
	+ In this field, select all phases that have been completed.
* **What obstacles are you experiencing?** (`obstacles`, `text`):
	+ Describe any obstacles you are facing with your modernization project.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`):
	+ Add any additional comments or feedback about the project.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`):
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`):
	+ Enter your phone number for follow-up calls or notifications.
