# Contest Scoring Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contest Scoring Survey is a critical tool for contest organizers to understand the quality of contest submissions and make informed decisions about award distribution. The data collected from this survey will help improve future contests and provide valuable insights into the strengths and weaknesses of submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and honestly as a judge.
- Make sure to select the appropriate category for each submission.
- Use the 1-10 scale for scoring questions (e.g., 1 = Poor, 10 = Exceptional).
- Provide constructive feedback in the "Constructive Feedback for Contestant" field.
- Select the final award recommendation for each submission.
- Enter the date of evaluation for each submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Judge Full Name** (`judge_full_name`, text, required): Enter your name and initials as a judge.
* **Judging Category** (`judging_category`, select_one, required): Select the specific category for the submission (e.g., Creative Arts, Technical Performance, Presentation Skills, Innovation and Impact).
* **Contestant Name or ID** (`contestant_id_name`, text, required): Enter the name or ID of the contestant.
* **Entry Title/Description** (`entry_title`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the submission.
* **Originality and Creativity** (`score_originality`, number, required): Rate the submission's originality and creativity using the 1-10 scale.
* **Technical Execution** (`score_technical_execution`, number, required): Rate the submission's technical execution using the 1-10 scale.
* **Presentation and Delivery** (`score_presentation`, number, required): Rate the submission's presentation and delivery using the 1-10 scale.
* **Relevance to Theme** (`score_relevance`, number, required): Rate the submission's relevance to the contest theme using the 1-10 scale.
* **Constructive Feedback for Contestant** (`judge_comments`, text, required): Provide specific examples of what the contestant did well or could improve.
* **Final Award Recommendation** (`final_recommendation`, select_one, required): Select the final award recommendation for the submission (e.g., Gold/First Place, Silver/Second Place, Bronze/Third Place, Honorable Mention, No Award).
* **Date of Evaluation** (`judging_date`, date, required): Enter the date of evaluation.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to provide accurate and reliable data.
