# Recycling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recycling Survey is a tool designed to gather information on your household's recycling habits, knowledge, and participation in recycling programs. Your responses will help us better understand your perspective and make informed decisions to improve recycling practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all required questions (identified by *) by providing your honest feedback.
2. Select one or multiple options for each question where applicable.
3. Be honest and provide accurate information about your household's recycling habits and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recycling Survey** (`recycling_survey_first_page`): This is the title of the survey, and it is not a question to answer.
* **How often do you recycle?** (`survey_recycling_habits`): Select how often you participate in recycling activities.
	+ High: You recycle frequently and regularly.
	+ Medium: You occasionally recycle.
	+ Low: You rarely recycle.
	+ None: You do not recycle at all.
* **How did you learn about recycling?** (`recycling_education`): Select where you learned about recycling.
	+ School: You learned about recycling in school.
	+ Internet: You learned about recycling online.
	+ Friend: You learned from a friend or family member.
	+ Family: You learned from your family.
* **Recycling Facts** (`recycling_facts`): This is a text field where you can provide any additional information or thoughts about recycling.
* **Household Waste Production** (`household_waste`): Enter the approximate amount of waste produced by your household per week.
* **How often do you recycle?** (`recycling_frequency`): Select how often you participate in recycling activities.
	+ Frequently: You recycle daily or almost daily.
	+ Occasionally: You recycle occasionally.
	+ Rarely: You rarely recycle.
* **Participation in Recycling Programs** (`recycling_participation`): Select whether you participate in recycling programs.
	+ Yes: You participate in recycling programs.
	+ No: You do not participate in recycling programs.
* **Barriers to Recycling** (`recycling_barriers`): Select one or more barriers to your participation in recycling.
	+ Convenience: It is not convenient for you.
	+ Cost: It is too expensive.
	+ Lack of Knowledge: You do not know where to recycle or how to do it.
* **Recycling Goals** (`recycling_goals`): Select one or more of your recycling goals.
	+ Increase Recycling: You want to recycle more.
	+ Reduce Waste: You want to reduce waste.
	+ Other: You have other recycling goals.
* **Any other comments?** (`survey_conclusion`): Enter any additional information or comments about your recycling habits and experiences.
* **Survey Completion Date** (`recycling_survey_completion`): Enter the date and time you completed this survey.
* **Time Spent on Recycling** (`time_spent_recycling`): Enter the approximate time spent on recycling activities per week.
* **How often do you recycle?** (`recycling_frequency_of_recycling`): Select how often you participate in recycling activities.
	+ Daily: You recycle daily or almost daily.
	+ Weekly: You recycle weekly.
	+ Monthly: You recycle monthly.
	+ Rarely: You rarely recycle.
* **Household Items Recycled** (`household_items_recycled`): Select one or more household items you recycle.
	+ Plastic Bottles: You recycle plastic bottles.
	+ Newspaper: You recycle newspaper.
	+ Cardboard: You recycle cardboard.
* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_completion_date`): Enter the date and time you completed this survey.
* **Survey Completion Time** (`survey_completion_time`): Enter the approximate time spent completing this survey.
* **Any other comments?** (`recycling_survey_completion_comments`): Enter any additional information or comments about your survey experience.
* **Recycling Awareness** (`survey_recycling_awareness`): Select your level of recycling awareness.
	+ High: You are highly aware of recycling.
	+ Medium: You are moderately aware of recycling.
	+ Low: You are not very aware of recycling.
