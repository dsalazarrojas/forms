# Organic Social Media Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback about your experiences with our organic social media platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (form_title): This is the title of the feedback you are providing. Please enter a brief but descriptive title that summarizes your feedback.
* **Name** (name): If you'd like to receive updates or have your feedback associated with your identity, enter your name here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Email** (email): If you'd like to receive responses or updates about your feedback, enter your email address here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Feedback** (feedback): Describe your overall experience with the social media platform. This can include what you liked or disliked about your experience.
* **Social Media Platform** (social_media_platform): Please select which social media platform you were interacting with (e.g. Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, etc.).
* **Post Type** (post_type): Describe the type of post you were engaging with (e.g. video, photo, story, post, etc.).
* **Post Link** (post_link) and **Post Link URL** (post_link_url): If you're providing feedback about a specific post, enter the title and URL of that post here. If not, you can leave these fields blank.
* **Post Comments** (post_comments): Describe the comments or interactions you had with the post.
* **Rating** (rating): Rate your experience from 1-5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Review** (review): Write a detailed review of your experience with the platform.
* **Recommended** (recommended): If you'd like us to consider this feedback for future development, check this box.
* **Why Recommended** (why_recommend): If you checked "Recommended", please explain why you think this feedback is valuable.
* **Not Recommended** (not_recommended): If you don't think this feedback is valuable, check this box.
* **Why Not Recommended** (why_not_recommended): If you checked "Not Recommended", explain why you think this feedback is not valuable.
* **Suggestion** (suggestion): If you have any suggestions for how we can improve, enter them here.
* **Category** (category): Select one or more categories that best describe the type of content or topic you're providing feedback about (e.g. Entertainment, Sports, Politics, etc.).
* **Category Other Description** (category_other_description): If you selected "Category" Other, describe in more detail what that category means to you.
* **Submit** (submit): Once you've filled out all the fields, click Submit to send your feedback to us.
