# Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about a service provider. It is intended to gather information about user's experience, preferences, and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read the "Introduction" section carefully and provide any general comments or feedback about your experience.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
3. For questions with multiple-choice options, select one or multiple options that best fit your experience.
4. For questions with text fields, provide as much information as possible.
5. If you have any questions or need help with this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
**Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide any general comments or feedback about your experience.

### 1. What did you think of our service?
**What did you think of our service?**: Answer this question truthfully, and choose one of the options: Very Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Bad.

### 2. How would you rate our service?
**How would you rate our service?**: Answer this question truthfully, and choose one of the options: Very Good, Fair, or Bad.

### 3. How likely are you to recommend our service?
**How likely are you to recommend our service?**: Answer this question truthfully, and choose one of the options: Very Likely, Not Very Likely, or Not at All Likely.

### 4. What did we do well?
**What did we do well?**: Provide a text response to this question, answering what the service provider did well in your experience.

### 5. What did we do poorly?
**What did we do poorly?**: Provide a text response to this question, answering what the service provider could improve on.

### 6. How can we improve?
**How can we improve?**: Provide a text response to this question, suggesting ways the service provider can improve your experience.

### 7. Thank you for your feedback
**Thank you for your feedback**: A note field where you can express your appreciation for your feedback.

### 8. Email
**Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to receive updates or follow-ups about your feedback.

### 9. Name
**Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.

### 10. Additional feedback
**Additional feedback**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

### 11. How long did you spend on our service?
**How long did you spend on our service?**: Answer this question truthfully, and provide a time in the format "HH:MM:SS".

### 12. How many times have you visited our service?
**How many times have you visited our service?**: Answer this question truthfully, and provide a number.

### 13. How much money did you spend on our service?
**How much money did you spend on our service?**: Answer this question truthfully, and provide a number.

### 14. When did you visit our service?
**When did you visit our service?**: Answer this question truthfully, and provide a date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".

### 15. At what time did you visit our service?
**At what time did you visit our service?**: Answer this question truthfully, and provide a time in the format "HH:MM:SS".

### 16. How many services have you purchased from us?
**How many services have you purchased from us?**: Answer this question truthfully, and provide a number.

### 17. How much time did you spend on our service?
**How much time did you spend on our service?**: Answer this question truthfully, and provide a time in the format "HH:MM:SS".

### 18. How much time did you spend per visit?
**How much time did you spend per visit?**: Answer this question truthfully, and provide a time in the format "HH:MM:SS".

### 19. What service did you visit?
**What service did you visit?**: Choose one of the options: Service A, Service B, or Service C.

### 20. What services did you visit?
**What services did you visit?**: Choose one or multiple options: Service A, Service B, Service C, or Service D.

### 21. Did you visit any other services?
**Did you visit any other services?**: Answer this question truthfully, and choose one of the options: Yes or No.

### 22. What did you do during your last visit?
**What did you do during your last visit?**: Choose one or multiple options: Service A, Service B, Service C, or Service D.

### 23. What did you do during your last visit?
**What did you do during your last visit?**: A note field where you can provide a text response to this question, answering what you did during your last visit.

### 24. Thank you for your feedback
**Thank you for your feedback**: A note field where you can express your appreciation for your feedback.
