Coronavirus Survey - Help Guide
==============================

## Purpose
The Coronavirus Survey is a form used to gather information from individuals regarding their experiences and responses to the COVID-19 pandemic. This form helps collect relevant data to aid in public health responses and understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first name** in the "first_name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **last name** in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your **email address** in the "email" field. This is not a required field.
4. Enter your **date of birth** in the "date_of_birth" field. This is a required field.
5. Enter your **phone number** in the "phone" field. This is not a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and information related to the survey.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate information for **first_name** and **last_name** fields.
* For **date_of_birth**, use the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., `1990-01-01`).
* If you have any issues while submitting the form, try refreshing the page and resubmitting.
