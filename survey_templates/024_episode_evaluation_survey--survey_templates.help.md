# episode_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The episode_evaluation_survey form is used to evaluate episodes, such as TV shows or movies, to gather feedback from users on their experience. This form is used for quality control and improvement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter episode_id**: Enter the title of the episode you are evaluating.
2. **Select rating**: Choose how you rate the episode (Good, Fair).
3. **Enter feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions about the episode.
4. **Enter comments**: Provide additional comments about the episode.
5. **Select category_id**: Choose a category for the episode (Yes, No).
6. **Enter rating_score**: Enter a numerical score for the episode.
7. **Enter category_name**: Enter a category name for the episode.
8. **Enter rating_comment**: Enter a comment about the rating.
9. **Enter category_name2**: Enter another category name for the episode.
10. **Enter episode_title**: Enter the title of the episode.
11. **Enter episode_title2**: Enter another title of the episode.
12. **Enter rating_score2**: Enter another numerical score for the episode.
13. **Enter category_name3**: Enter another category name for the episode.
14. **Enter rating_comment2**: Enter another comment about the rating.
15. **Enter category_name4**: Enter another category name for the episode.
16. **Enter rating_score3**: Enter another numerical score for the episode.
17. **Enter category_name5**: Enter another category name for the episode.
18. **Enter rating_comment3**: Enter another comment about the rating.
19. **Enter category_name6**: Enter another category name for the episode.
20. **Select episode_rating**: Choose a rating for the episode (Good, Fair).
21. **Enter rating_average**: Enter an average rating for the episode.
22. **Enter episode_title3**: Enter another title of the episode.
23. **Enter episode_title4**: Enter another title of the episode.
24. **Enter episode_title5**: Enter another title of the episode.
25. **Enter episode_title6**: Enter another title of the episode.
