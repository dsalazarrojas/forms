# Bar Measurement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bar Measurement Survey is a form designed to collect data about bars and restaurants, specifically their bar counter measurements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the measurement survey from the calendar.
2. Enter the name and address of the bar or restaurant.
3. Provide the name of the surveyor and their company.
4. Measure and enter the bar counter length, depth, and height.
5. Specify the material used to make the bar counter.
6. Assess the condition of the bar counter.
7. Enter any additional measurements, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Survey Date (1)
* `Survey Date`: Select the date of the measurement survey from the calendar.

### Establishment Name (2)
* `Establishment Name`: Enter the name of the bar or restaurant.

### Location Address (3)
* `Location Address`: Enter the full address of the establishment.

### Surveyor Name (4)
* `Surveyor Name`: Enter the name of the surveyor.

### Surveyor Company (5)
* `Surveyor Company`: Enter the name of the surveyor company or organization (optional).

### Bar Counter Length (6)
* `Bar Counter Length (feet)`: Measure and enter the length of the bar counter from end to end.

### Bar Counter Depth (7)
* `Bar Counter Depth (feet)`: Measure and enter the front-to-back depth of the bar counter.

### Bar Counter Height (8)
* `Bar Counter Height (inches)`: Measure and enter the standard or custom height of the bar counter.

### Bar Material (9)
* `Bar Material`: Select the material used to make the bar counter (e.g., wood, granite, marble, etc.).

### Bar Condition (10)
* `Bar Condition`: Assess the current condition of the bar counter (e.g., excellent, good, fair, poor, etc.).

### Backing Wall Height (11)
* `Backing Wall Height (feet)`: Measure and enter the height of the wall behind the bar counter (optional).

### Shelving Width (12)
* `Shelving Width (feet)`: Measure and enter the width of shelving units (optional).

### Shelving Depth (13)
* `Shelving Depth (inches)`: Measure and enter the depth of shelves (optional).

### Number of Shelves (14)
* `Number of Shelf Levels`: Enter the number of shelves (optional).

### Beer Tap Locations (15)
* `Beer Tap Locations`: Describe where beer taps are located (optional).

### Number of Draft Lines (16)
* `Number of Draft Beer Lines`: Enter the total number of draft taps (optional).

### Bar Sink Dimensions (17)
* `Bar Sink Dimensions`: Enter the length, width, and depth of the bar sink (optional).

### Ice Bin Capacity (18)
* `Ice Bin Capacity (pounds)`: Enter the capacity of the ice bin in pounds (optional).

### Floor Material (19)
* `Floor Material`: Select the material used to make the floor (e.g., tile, wood, etc.).

### Floor Condition (20)
* `Floor Condition`: Assess the current condition of the floor (e.g., excellent, good, fair, poor, etc.).

### Number of Electrical Outlets (21)
* `Number of Electrical Outlets`: Enter the count of available electrical outlets (optional).

### Plumbing Connections (22)
* `Plumbing Connections`: Describe the number and location of plumbing connections (optional).

### HVAC and Ventilation Notes (23)
* `HVAC and Ventilation Notes`: Enter any special ventilation needs or requirements (optional).

### Additional Measurements (24)
* `Additional Measurements`: Enter any other dimensions or measurements not mentioned above (optional).
