# Ctl U Leasing Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ctl U Leasing Survey is used to collect information from users about their experience with leasing techniques. This form is used to gather data on the user's leasing habits and behaviors, which will help in improving the leasing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the survey (page 1, field 1).
2. Indicate whether or not you have used leasing techniques (page 1, field 2).
3. Provide a short description of your experience with leasing (page 1, field 3).
4. Select all the leasing techniques you have used (page 1, field 4).
5. If you have used any leasing techniques, provide a short description of how you have improved them (page 1, field 5).
6. Enter any relevant output or results from your leasing experience (page 1, field 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey_title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): A brief title for the survey.
* **category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have used leasing techniques. Choose 'Yes' if you have used them, and 'No' if you haven't.
* **description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a short description of your experience with leasing.
* **leasing_techniques** (`leasing_techniques`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the leasing techniques you have used.
* **leasing_techniques_improvement** (`leasing_techniques_improvement`, text, optional): If you have used any leasing techniques, provide a short description of how you have improved them.
* **output** (`output`, text, optional): Enter any relevant output or results from your leasing experience.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the survey administrators.
* This form is for the sole purpose of improving the leasing process, and all information collected will be kept confidential.
