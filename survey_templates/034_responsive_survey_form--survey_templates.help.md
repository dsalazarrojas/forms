# Responsive Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about our website's usability, customer support, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the link to access the form.
2.  Read each question carefully and select the answer that best fits your experience.
3.  Answer as many or as few questions as you want, as some questions are optional.
4.  For select_one questions, choose only one answer from the options.
5.  For select_multiple questions, choose all that apply.
6.  For number questions, enter a number between 1 and 10.
7.  For text questions, type your answer in the text field.
