# Photography Equipment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on photography equipment usage and satisfaction levels to help us understand how our equipment performs in real-world use cases, identify areas for improvement, and inform future product development decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer the questions to the best of your ability. Make sure to select the options that apply to your experiences with our photography equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Photography Equipment Survey** (`number`): This is a required field to help us keep track of the number of surveys completed.
* **2. Camera Body Type** (`select_one`): Choose the type of camera body you have used. Options are DSLR, Mirrorless, and Medium Format.
* **3. Lenses Used** (`select_multiple`): Select all the lenses you have used with our equipment. Options are Yes and No.
* **4. Flash Used** (`select_one`): Choose if you have used flash with our equipment. Options are True and False.
* **5. Tripod Used** (`select_one`): Choose if you have used a tripod with our equipment. Options are True and False.
* **6. Satisfaction Rating** (`select_one`): Rate your overall satisfaction with our equipment. Options are 1-5.
* **7. Purchase Intent** (`select_one`): Choose if you are likely to purchase our photography equipment. Options are True and False.
* **8. Photography Equipment Purchase** (`number`): This is a required field to help us track the number of photography equipment purchases.
* **9. Photography Equipment Satisfaction** (`number`): This is a required field to help us track the satisfaction level of our equipment.
* **10. Photography Equipment Purchase Intent** (`number`): This is a required field to help us track the purchase intent of our equipment.
* **11. Photography Equipment Rating** (`select_multiple`): Select all the features of our equipment that you are satisfied with. Options are Good and Fair.
* **12. Photography Equipment Purchase Frequency** (`select_one`): Choose how often you purchase photography equipment. Options are Never, Occasionally, Sometimes, Often, and Always.
* **13. Photography Equipment Satisfaction Level** (`select_one`): Rate your satisfaction level with our equipment. Options are Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, and Very Dissatisfied.
* **14. Photography Equipment Purchase Probability** (`select_one`): Choose the likelihood of purchasing our equipment. Options are Not Considering, Not Likely, May Be Likely, Likely, and Very Likely.
* **15. Photography Equipment Satisfaction Level Impact** (`select_one`): Choose the impact of satisfaction level on your purchase decision. Options are Not Applicable, Somewhat Applicable, Very Applicable, and Extremely Applicable.
* **16. Photography Equipment Purchase Probability Impact** (`select_one`): Choose the impact of purchase probability on your purchase decision. Options are Not Important, Somewhat Important, Very Important, and Extremely Important.
* **17. Photography Equipment Satisfaction Level Reasons** (`text`): Please provide reasons for your satisfaction level rating.
* **18. Photography Equipment Purchase Probability Reasons** (`text`): Please provide reasons for your purchase probability rating.
* **19. Additional Comments** (`text`): Any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our equipment.
* **20. Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **21. Phone** (`text`): Enter your phone number.
* **22. Date** (`date`): Enter the date you started using our equipment.
* **23. Time** (`time`): Enter the time you started using our equipment.
* **24. Date of Birth** (`date`): Enter your date of birth.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Select all options that apply to your experiences with our equipment.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the Additional Comments field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [support email].
