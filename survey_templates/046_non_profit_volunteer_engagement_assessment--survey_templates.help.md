# Non Profit Volunteer Engagement Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Non Profit Volunteer Engagement Assessment is a simple form designed to help you evaluate the engagement of volunteers at your non-profit organization. This form will help you assess volunteer satisfaction, retention, and feedback to inform future programs and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the corresponding page and start filling out the questions. You will be asked to provide ratings and comments for each section, which will help us understand your volunteer's experiences and suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Satisfaction** (`volunteer_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the volunteer experience, on a scale of 1-5. Please choose a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Volunteer Retention** (`volunteer_retention`, `number`, required: false): This question asks you to rate how likely you are to continue volunteering with our organization in the future, on a scale of 1-5. Please choose a number between 1 and 5.
* **Feedback and suggestions** (`feedback_and_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): This is a free-text field where you can provide any feedback or suggestions you have for our volunteer program. Please use this space to let us know how we can improve.
* **Program updates** (`program_updates`, `text`, required: false): This is another free-text field where you can share any updates or news about our programs or events that you think would be helpful for our volunteers to know. Please use this space to keep our volunteers informed and engaged.

## Tips
* Please answer these questions as honestly as possible, and feel free to provide as much detail as you need to support your answers.
* If you have any technical issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
