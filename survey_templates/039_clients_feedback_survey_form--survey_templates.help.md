# Clients Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with our services. By filling out this form, you will be helping us to understand what we do well and what we can improve on, allowing us to better serve you and our other clients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out each section as prompted. Each section is labeled with a title and an explanation of what it's about. You can type in your answers to the questions, and select from the options provided for any multiple-choice questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Feedback Survey** (`intro_header`, `note`, required: false`): This is the introduction to the form. It's not required, but it helps us get an idea of what you think about your experience with our services.
* **-- Your Information --** (`respondent_info`, `note`, required: false`): This section is for any additional information you might want to share with us about yourself.
* **Your Name** (`your_name`, `text`, required: false`): This is your name, which is optional but can be useful for follow-up if needed.
* **Email Address** (`your_email`, `email`, required: true`): Please provide your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`your_phone`, `text`, required: false`): This is your phone number, which is optional.
* **When Did You Purchase or Use Our Service?** (`purchase_date`, `date`, required: false`): This is the date when you made your purchase or used our service.
* **-- Overall Satisfaction --** (`satisfaction_section`, `note`, required: false`): This section is for your overall satisfaction with our services.
* **How Satisfied Are You with Your Purchase or Service?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: true`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select how satisfied you are with our services.
* **Rate Your Overall Experience - 1 to 10** (`satisfaction_rating`, `number`, required: true`): This is a numerical scale where you can rate your overall experience with our services.
* **-- Expectations vs Reality --** (`expectations_section`, `note`, required: false`): This section is for your comparison of our services with your expectations.
* **Did Our Product or Service Meet Your Expectations?** (`met_expectations`, `select_one`, required: true`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select whether our services met your expectations.
* **-- Value and Price --** (`value_section`, `note`, required: false`): This section is for your thoughts on the value and price of our services.
* **Did You Receive Good Value for Your Money?** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, required: true`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select whether you received good value for your money.
* **Satisfaction with Pricing** (`pricing_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select your satisfaction with our pricing.
* **-- Quality and Performance --** (`quality_section`, `note`, required: false`): This section is for your thoughts on the quality and performance of our services.
* **Quality of Product or Service** (`product_quality`, `select_one`, required: true`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select the quality of our product or service.
* **Product Durability or Service Reliability** (`durability`, `select_one`, required: false`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select the durability or reliability of our product or service.
* **-- Customer Service --** (`customer_service_section`, `note`, required: false`): This section is for your thoughts on the customer service we provided.
* **Quality of Customer Service** (`service_quality`, `select_one`, required: true`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select the quality of our customer service.
* **Speed of Response to Your Inquiries** (`response_time`, `select_one`, required: true`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select the speed of our response to your inquiries.
* **Staff Was Helpful and Professional** (`staff_helpfulness`, `select_one`, required: true`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select the helpfulness and professionalism of our staff.
* **Issues or Concerns Were Resolved Satisfactorily** (`issue_resolution`, `select_one`, required: false`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select whether your issues or concerns were resolved satisfactorily.
* **-- Loyalty and Recommendation --** (`loyalty_section`, `note`, required: false`): This section is for your thoughts on our loyalty and recommendation.
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend Us? - 0 to 10** (`recommend_likelihood`, `number`, required: true`): This is a numerical scale where you can rate how likely you are to recommend us.
* **Would You Purchase from Us Again?** (`repeat_purchase`, `select_one`, required: true`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select whether you would purchase from us again.
* **-- Comments and Feedback --** (`feedback_section`, `note`, required: false`): This section is for any additional comments or feedback you might have.
* **What Did We Do Well?** (`what_we_did_well`, `text`, required: false`): This is a text field where you can write what we did well in your opinion.
* **What Could We Improve?** (`what_to_improve`, `text`, required: false`): This is a text field where you can write what we could improve in your opinion.
* **Specific Feedback or Comments** (`specific_feedback`, `text`, required: false`): This is a text field where you can write any additional feedback or comments you might have.

## Tips

* Please be as specific and honest as possible when answering the questions.
* You can skip any sections that don't apply to you.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Specific Feedback or Comments" field.
* Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form and help us improve our services!
