# Educational AI Adoption Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the readiness of educational institutions to adopt AI technology. It will help your institution's management understand your experiences and challenges with AI adoption.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form carefully and honestly, as it will help your institution make informed decisions about AI adoption.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary permission to complete this form if you represent multiple institutions.
3. If you need help or have any concerns, please contact your institution's IT department or the form administrator.
4. Take about 10-15 minutes to complete this form, and don't worry if you need to take breaks.
