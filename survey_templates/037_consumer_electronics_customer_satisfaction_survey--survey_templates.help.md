# Consumer Electronics Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from customers about their consumer electronics products. It is intended to measure customer satisfaction, product quality, and overall experience with the product.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, please provide your First Name and email address (optional) in the first and second fields. This information is used to identify you and will allow us to contact you in case of any questions or follow-up.
2.  In the following fields, select the type of product you purchased by choosing the corresponding category from the dropdown menu. This could be a Smartphone, Tablet, Laptop, and so on.
3.  Enter the brand name of the product you purchased in the next field.
4.  Enter the model number or name of the product you purchased.
5.  If you've purchased the product, select the date you bought it from the calendar or enter the date manually.
6.  Select the location where you purchased the product. This could be from the brand website, an online retailer, an electronics store, or a department store.
7.  Indicate the price range of the product you purchased.
8.  Rate your usage frequency of the product by selecting how often you use it.
9.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the product, from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
10. Rate the product's build quality, value for money, ease of use, and feature satisfaction by selecting the corresponding ratings.
11. If applicable, rate the battery performance of the product.
12. Rate the overall performance of the product.
13. Rate the design appeal of the product.
14. Rate the product's reliability.
15. Rate the customer service you received if you contacted them.
16. If you experienced any issues with the product, select the problems you encountered.
17. Indicate how likely you are to recommend the product to others.
18. Let us know if you would repurchase from the same brand.
19. In the last two fields, you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the product and your experience with the brand.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (customer_first_name): Your first name - optional
* **Email Address** (customer_email): For follow-up - optional
* **Product Category** (product_category): Select the type of product you purchased - required
* **Product Brand** (product_brand): Enter the brand name of the product - required
* **Product Model** (product_model): Model number or name - optional
* **Purchase Date** (purchase_date): When did you buy the product - required
* **Purchase Location** (purchase_location): Where did you buy it - required
* **Price Range** (price_range): How much did you spend - required
* **Usage Frequency** (usage_frequency): How often do you use the product - required
* **Overall Satisfaction** (overall_satisfaction): Rate your overall satisfaction - required
* **Product Quality Rating** (product_quality_rating): Rate the build quality - required
* **Value for Money** (value_for_money): Rate the value - required
* **Ease of Use** (ease_of_use): How easy is it to use - required
* **Feature Satisfaction** (feature_satisfaction): How satisfied with features - required
* **Battery Life Rating** (battery_life_rating): Rate battery performance if applicable - optional
* **Performance Rating** (performance_rating): Rate overall performance - required
* **Design Appeal** (design_appeal): Rate the design and appearance - required
* **Reliability Rating** (reliability_rating): Rate product reliability - required
* **Customer Service Rating** (customer_service_rating): Rate customer support if contacted - optional
* **Issues Encountered** (issues_encountered): Select any problems experienced - required
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (likelihood_recommend): How likely to recommend - required
* **Intention to Repurchase** (repurchase_intention): Will you buy from this brand again - required
* **Best Features** (best_features): What do you like most - optional
* **Areas for Improvement** (areas_for_improvement): What could be better - optional
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Any other feedback - optional

**Tips** 
- If you have difficulty with the form, do not hesitate to contact us.
- You can save your progress and come back later to complete the form.
- Please be as detailed as possible in your answers.
