# Nonprofit Donor Communication Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from nonprofit donors on their communication preferences. It will help the organization better understand how often and through which channels they should send updates to their donors, and will also help confirm donor identities and accurate contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on the main page regarding your preferred frequency and channels of updates.
2. Confirm if you are a donor and provide your email address if you wish to receive updates.
3. Provide your name and phone number if you have not already done so.
4. Confirm that the email address you provided is accurate.
5. If you have any additional feedback or comments, please share them with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page**: This is the initial question set asking you about your preferred frequency and channels of updates.
	+ *How often do you receive updates from us*: This question asks you to rate how often you typically receive updates from us.
	+ *What is your preferred channel for receiving updates*: Select one of the channels that you would like to receive updates from us, such as Email or Social Media.
* **Updates Question**: This question is similar to the main question but specific to the type of updates you receive.
* **Preferred Channel**: This question asks you to select one or multiple channels through which you would like to receive updates.
* **Preferred Frequency**: This question asks you to select one of the frequencies at which you would like to receive updates.
* **Communication Question**: This question asks you how you would like us to communicate with you, and you can select multiple options.
* **Additional Feedback**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them with us.
* **Email**: If you would like us to contact you via email, please enter your email address.
* **Phone**: If you would like us to contact you via phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Name**: If you haven't already provided your name, please enter your full name.
* **Confirm Donor**: This question confirms if you are a donor and would like to receive updates.
* **Confirm Email**: This question confirms if the email address you provided is accurate.
* **Additional Feedback**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them. 

Note: This form is designed to be completed quickly and concisely, so feel free to skip questions that are not relevant to you.
