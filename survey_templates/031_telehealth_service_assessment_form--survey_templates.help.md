# Telehealth Service Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about a patient's needs and medical history to assess the suitability of telehealth services. It will help healthcare professionals determine if a patient is a good candidate for telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question carefully and honestly, using the options provided.
- If you're unsure about any question, leave it blank or ask for clarification from a healthcare professional.
- Use this form to gather information about the patient, do not use it as a template or placeholder.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Intake Information (Page 1)
* **Intake Information** (`intake_info`, `number`, required: false): This section is used to record essential details about the patient's intake. Please enter a unique identifier for each patient.

### Telehealth Service Information (Page 2)
* **Telehealth Service Information** (`telehealth_service_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended for you to enter any relevant information about the telehealth services being offered to the patient.

### Patient Health Status (Page 3)
* **Patient Health Status** (`patient_health_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the current status of the patient's health. Options are: Active, Inactive.

### Patient Care Goals (Page 4)
* **Patient Care Goals** (`patient_care_goals`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the goals that apply to the patient. Options are: Yes, No.

### Patient Medical History (Page 5)
* **Patient Medical History** (`patient_medical_history`, `note`, required: false): This field is intended for you to enter any relevant medical history about the patient.

### Patient Support Systems (Page 6)
* **Patient Support Systems** (`patient_support_systems`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select if the patient has any support systems in place. Options are: Yes, No.

### Patient Family History (Page 7)
* **Patient Family History** (`patient_family_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the relevant family histories for the patient. Options are: Yes, No.

### Telehealth Service Plan (Page 8)
* **Telehealth Service Plan** (`telehealth_service_plan`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended for you to enter a detailed plan for the telehealth services being offered to the patient.

### Patient Goals and Objectives (Page 9)
* **Patient Goals and Objectives** (`patient_goals_and_objectives`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the current status of the patient's goals and objectives. Options are: Yes, No.

### Medication History (Page 10)
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the medications the patient is currently taking. Options are: Yes, No.

### Medical History (Page 11)
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended for you to enter any relevant medical history about the patient.

### Patient Medical History Review (Page 12)
* **Patient Medical History Review** (`patient_medical_history_review`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select if a medical history review is required. Options are: Yes, No.

## Tips
- Use this form to ensure that the patient's needs are well understood and addressed.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, seek clarification from a healthcare professional.
