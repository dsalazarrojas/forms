# Land Inspection And Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the condition and potential risks associated with land inspections. It helps identify and categorize risks, providing a comprehensive overview of the land's state.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the provided sections and selecting the relevant information for each field.
2. Ensure you complete all required fields.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Assessment** (`id: 2`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to identify the presence or absence of risks associated with the land. Select all relevant risks from the provided options, which are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Property Inspections** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the property inspections conducted. 
* **Risk Identification** (`id: 4`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Answer with either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the risk has been identified.
* **Risk Characterization** (`id: 5`, `type: note`, required: false): Enter a description of the risk characterization, if necessary. This field is not required to be filled out but is provided for additional information. 
* **Risk Assessment Form** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or observations about the risk assessment.
* **Property Status** (`id: 7`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Choose 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the property's current status.
* **Land Condition Checklist** (`id: 8`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each land condition item.
* **Inspection Report** (`id: 9`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the date and time of the inspection, or the duration of the inspection. This is an optional field. 
* **Risk Score** (`id: 10`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter a numerical value to indicate the level of risk associated with the land. This is an optional field. 
* **Risk Management** (`id: 11`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether risk management measures are in place.
* **Risk Assessment Form 2** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or observations about the risk assessment.
* **Property Inspections 2** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the property inspections conducted. 
* **Risk Identification 2** (`id: 14`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Answer with either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the risk has been identified.
* **Risk Characterization 2** (`id: 15`, `type: note`, required: false): Enter a description of the risk characterization, if necessary. This field is not required to be filled out but is provided for additional information. 
* **Risk Assessment Form 3** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or observations about the risk assessment.
* **Property Status 2** (`id: 17`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Choose 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the property's current status.
* **Land Condition Checklist 2** (`id: 18`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each land condition item.

## Tips

* If unsure about any field, consult the relevant expert or risk management professional.
* Enter relevant information for each section, as this will aid in providing an accurate risk assessment.
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid duplication of effort.
* Review and update this form regularly to stay up-to-date with the land's condition and risks.
