# tenant_community_poll_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent from tenants for a poll or event organized by the property management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Tenant ID in the "Tenant ID" field.
2. Provide your answer to the "Poll Question" by typing your response in the text field.
3. Select your answer choices for the "Poll Answer" field.
4. If applicable, enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field and select the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
5. Enter any relevant details about the event in the "Event Details" field.
6. Select your preferred contact method (Email or Phone) in the "Contact Method" field.
7. Enter the name of the person to contact in the "Contact Person" field.
8. Enter the contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
9. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
10. Provide any additional comments in the "Tenant Comments" field.
11. If desired, provide comments from the manager in the "Manager Comments" fields.
12. Finally, submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant ID** (`tenant_id`, `number`, required): Enter your unique Tenant ID number. This will help the property management track your responses and ensure they are associated with you.
* **Poll Question** (`poll_question`, `text`, required): Provide your answer to the poll question by typing it in this text field.
* **Poll Answer** (`poll_answer`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your answer choices for the poll question from the options provided.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): If applicable, enter the date of the event. This will help the property management schedule the event accordingly.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Select the time of the event from the options provided.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional details about the event that you would like to share with the property management.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to be contacted (Email or Phone).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person to contact for any further questions or concerns.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Tenant Comments** (`tenant_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the event or poll.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, required): If you are the property manager, provide comments or feedback about the event or poll.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, required): Click this button to submit your form and provide your consent.
* **Cancel** (`cancel_button`, `text`, required): Click this button to cancel your submission and start over.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by tenants or individuals associated with a property or community.
