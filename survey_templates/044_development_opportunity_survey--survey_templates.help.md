# Development Opportunity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The development opportunity survey is designed to gauge your interest, feasibility, and potential for new project initiatives. The survey will help us understand your project's prospects, identify potential risks, and determine next steps for implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Go through each page of the survey form.
2. For each page, fill in the required information for that page.
3. Review and adjust your responses as needed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief name for your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Describe your project in a few sentences.
* **Team Interest** (`team_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your interest level in this project. Select all that apply:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Project Lead Time** (`project_lead_time`, select_one, optional): Choose the estimated lead time for your project:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Project Cost** (`project_cost`, number, optional): Enter the estimated project cost.
* **Project Priority** (`project_priority`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your project's priority. Select all that apply:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of your project:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, optional): Enter the project location.
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your project.
* **Project Comments** (`project_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other comments or feedback about your project.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all required fields (though most are optional).
- Be as detailed as possible for project description, project location, and project notes.
- Select all applicable options for team interest, project lead time, project priority, and project status.
- Use the project cost field for estimated project expenses.
