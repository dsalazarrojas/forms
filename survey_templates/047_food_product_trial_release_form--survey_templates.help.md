# food_product_trial_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "food_product_trial_release_form" is used to collect participant consent and gather essential information for product trials. It is a crucial step in the food product trial process, ensuring that participants understand the purpose and scope of the trial, and that they provide necessary contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully to understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Answer all required questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Enter your participant name and email address accurately.
4. Select your allergy status (if applicable).
5. Provide your phone number for follow-up purposes.
6. Specify your follow-up preferences.
7. Enter any additional data collection information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Consent** (1, text, required): This is the initial step to obtain participant consent for the food product trial. Please type "I understand and agree" to indicate your consent.
* **Allergies** (2, select_one, required): If you have any allergies, please select "Food Allergy" from the dropdown menu. If you don't have any allergies, select "None".
* **Follow-up Preferences** (3, text, required): Specify how you would like to be contacted for follow-up purposes, such as phone, email, or mail.
* **Data Collection** (4, text, required): Enter any additional information about data collection, such as any sensitive medical information or special instructions.
* **Participant Name** (5, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed during the trial.
* **E-mail** (6, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for trial updates and information.
* **Participant Phone** (7, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up calls or any trial-related updates.
