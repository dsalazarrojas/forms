# Dietary Restriction Validation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Dietary Restriction Validation Form is designed to collect information about your dietary preferences, allergies, and medical conditions to ensure that you receive the best care and accommodations during your visit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please select "None" if you do not have any food dietary restrictions.
2. Select all applicable food allergies you have.
3. If you have any medical conditions, please enter a brief description.
4. Choose your contact method (phone, email, or text).
5. If you have an emergency contact, please select "Yes".
6. If you have any special instructions, please enter them in the text box.
7. Finally, please review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dietary Restrictions** (`1`, `text`, required): This field is where you can enter any dietary restrictions you may have, such as vegetarian or gluten-free.
* **Food Allergies** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all allergies you have (Peanut, Egg, Fish, Shrimp, Crustacean, Dairy, Sesame).
* **Food Dietary Restrictions** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Select from None, Gluten-free, Vegetarian, Vegan, or Kosher to indicate your dietary preferences.
* **Special Diets** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Select from No Special Diets, Low-Sodium, Low-Sugar, Diabetic if you have any special dietary needs.
* **Medical Conditions** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of any medical conditions you have.
* **Contact Method** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Text).
* **Other Contact Method** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter any other contact method not listed above.
* **Medical Conditions Description** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of any medical conditions you have.
* **Special Instructions** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter any special instructions for your visit.
* **Emergency Contact** (`10`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have an emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Make sure to select "None" if you do not have any food allergies or dietary restrictions.
* If you select "Yes" for emergency contact, please provide their name and contact information.
* If you have any special instructions, please enter them clearly and concisely.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
