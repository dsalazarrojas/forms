# Send Your Message To The Universe - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who want to share their thoughts and experiences with the universe. The form asks about your practice habits, message to the universe, and demographic information to help us better understand your perspective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your title in the first field, which will be displayed as a header for your message.
2. In the second field, enter a reflection on your practice habits and experiences.
3. Choose how long you have been practicing in the third field.
4. Select whether you have sent your message to the universe in the fourth field.
5. (Optional) Enter a date for when you plan to send your message in the fifth field.
6. (Optional) Choose a time of day for when you plan to send your message in the sixth field.
7. (Optional) Enter any additional notes or comments in the seventh field.
8. Enter your email address in the eighth field, which will be used to contact you.
9. (Optional) Enter any additional phone numbers or contact information in the ninth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **send_your_message_to_the_universe_1 (Title)** (`send_your_message_to_the_universe_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter a title or header for your message to the universe. This will be displayed alongside your message.
* **reflection (Reflection)** (`reflection`, `text`, required: true): Share your thoughts and experiences related to your practice habits.
* **how_long_have_you_been_practicing (How Long Have You Been Practicing?)** (`how_long_have_you_been_practicing`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose how long you have been practicing, whether it's less than 1 year, 1-5 years, 5-10 years, or more than 10 years.
* **message_to_the_universe_2 (Have You Sent Your Message To The Universe?)** (`message_to_the_universe_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you have sent your message to the universe or not. If you have, what were the outcomes or results?
* **send_your_message_to_the_universe_6 (Optional Date)** (`send_your_message_to_the_universe_6`, `date`, required: false): Enter a date for when you plan to send your message to the universe. This is an optional field.
* **time_of_day (Time of Day)** (`time_of_day`, `time`, required: false): Choose a time of day when you plan to send your message to the universe, if applicable.
* **send_your_message_to_the_universe_8 (Optional Notes)** (`send_your_message_to_the_universe_8`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments related to your message or practice habits.
* **email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **phone (Phone)** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional phone numbers or contact information. This is an optional field.
