# Configurable List Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create and configure a list for various purposes such as task management, inventory, contact lists, and more. It is designed to be flexible and adaptable to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form Title**: Enter a descriptive title for your list.
2. **Your Name**: Enter your full name.
3. **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
4. **Phone Number**: Optional - enter your contact phone number.
5. **Organization**: Optional - enter your company or institution name.
6. **Type of List**: Choose the type of list you are creating (e.g. Task List, Contact List, etc.).
7. **List Name**: Enter a title for your list.
8. **List Description**: Enter a brief description of your list.
9. **Purpose of List**: Describe why you are creating this list.
10. **Item 1 Name**: Enter the name of the first item on your list.
11. **Item 1 Category**: Choose a category for the first item.
12. **Item 1 Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the first item (optional).
13. **Item 1 Priority**: Choose the priority level of the first item (High, Medium, Low).
14. **Item 1 Notes**: Enter any additional information about the first item.
15. **Item 2 Name**: Repeat the process for the second item.
16. **Additional Items**: Enter more items as needed.
17. **Total Number of Items**: Enter the total number of items on your list.
18. **List Status**: Choose the current status of your list (Draft, In Progress, Complete, Under Review).
19. **Due Date**: Enter the due date for the list (if applicable).
20. **Assigned To**: Choose the person responsible for your list.
21. **Sharing Permissions**: Choose who can view your list (Private, Team Only, Organization, Public).
22. **Additional Comments**: Enter any other information about your list.
23. **Submission Confirmation**: Confirm that you are ready to submit your list.
