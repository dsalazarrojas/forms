# Youth Sports Coach Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on the coaching style and performance of a youth sports coach. It is designed to help coaches identify areas of strength and weakness and provide suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections:
	* Section 1: Introduction (Note)
	* Section 2: Coaching Style (Multiple Select)
	* Section 3: Communication, Knowledge, and Organization (Select One)
	* Section 4: Positive and Negative Aspects (Text)
	* Section 5: Suggestions (Text)
	* Section 6: Contact Information (Email and Name)
	* Section 7: Additional Comments (Text)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, Note, required: false)
	+ This is a brief introduction to the survey.
* **What style of coaching do you find most appealing?** (`coaching_style`, Multiple Select, required: true)
	+ Please select all the coaching styles that you find most appealing.
	+ Options: Positive reinforcement, Negative reinforcement, Cooperative, Competition, Other
* **How would you rate the coach's communication?** (`communication`, Select One, required: true)
	+ Rate the coach's communication as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the coach's knowledge of the sport?** (`knowledge`, Select One, required: true)
	+ Rate the coach's knowledge as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the coach's organization?** (`organization`, Select One, required: true)
	+ Rate the coach's organization as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What are the most positive aspects of the coach?** (`positive_aspects`, Text, required: true)
	+ Share the most positive aspects of the coach's performance.
* **What are the most negative aspects of the coach?** (`negative_aspects`, Text, required: true)
	+ Share the most negative aspects of the coach's performance.
* **What suggestions do you have for the coach?** (`suggestions`, Text, required: true)
	+ Provide any suggestions you have for the coach's improvement.
* **Your Email** (`email`, Email, required: true)
	+ Enter your email address for feedback.
* **Your Name** (`name`, Text, required: true)
	+ Enter your name.
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (`comments`, Text, required: false)
	+ Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Tips

* Please answer each question truthfully and based on your genuine experience.
* Ensure that you only submit this form if you have had direct experience with the coach.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx)
