# lgbtq_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LGBTQ quiz is designed to be a brief assessment of the user's understanding of LGBTQ issues. It consists of multiple questions that cover various aspects of the LGBTQ community. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the LGBTQ quiz, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct answers for each question based on your knowledge.
3. Fill in any additional information you feel is necessary to provide context.
4. Submit your answers when you have completed the form.
