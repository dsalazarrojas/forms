# Field Survey Data Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for the publication of field survey data. It's crucial to complete this form carefully to ensure accurate and compliant data handling.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill out the form according to your knowledge and understanding of your role and responsibilities.
3. Make sure to sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect any additional information about the participant, such as their name, affiliation, or contact details. Please provide any relevant details that you think are necessary.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required: false): Please select "Yes" if you agree to the publication of your data, or "No" if you do not consent. If you select "No", your data will not be published.
* **Data Usage** (`data_usage`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the ways you agree your data can be used, such as for research, publication, or collaboration. Be cautious when selecting multiple options to ensure you understand the implications of your choice.
* **Publication Purpose** (`publication_purpose`, text, required: false): Describe the purpose of publishing your data, such as for academic research, conference presentation, or collaboration. Be specific about the intended use and audience.
* **Contact** (`contact`, email, required: false): Provide your email address or any other contact information so that the research team can reach out to you for further clarification or questions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm your consent. This is a formal declaration of your agreement to the publication of your data.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you sign this form, which is the date of your consent.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time when you sign this form, which is the time of your consent.
