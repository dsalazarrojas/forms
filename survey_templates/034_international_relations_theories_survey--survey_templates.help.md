# international_relations_theories_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help users complete the international relations theories survey form accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the "Submitter Name", "Submitter Info", "Email", and "Phone" sections.
2. Answer the questions related to international relations theories, selecting the appropriate options for each question.
3. For questions with multiple options, choose the one that best reflects your opinion or experience.
4. Provide any additional comments or questions in the "Submission Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: false): A brief explanation of the purpose and context of this survey.
	+ This is a text field where you can type in your brief introduction.
* **Theoretical Frameworks** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the international relations theories you are familiar with or have experience with.
	+ Choose from the list of available options: Realism, Idealism, Constructivism, Neo-Constructivism, Neorealism, and Liberalism.
* **Question 1** (`3`, `number`, required: true): Rate your familiarity with the theory selected in the previous question.
	+ Rate your familiarity from 1 to 10, where 1 is not familiar and 10 is very familiar.

	+ Rate your experience from 1 to 10, where 1 is no experience and 10 is a lot of experience.

	+ Type in any additional comments or questions you have.
