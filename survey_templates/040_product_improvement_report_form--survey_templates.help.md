# Product Improvement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Improvement Report Form is a tool for users to report any improvements or issues with our product. This form is designed to collect suggestions and insights from users to help us improve the product's functionality, user experience, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand what types of information we are looking for.
2. Select "Product Suggestions" or "Product Issues" sections based on your input.
3. Provide your feedback in the selected section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Suggestions (product_suggestions)
• **Product Suggestions (product_suggestions)** (`text`, optional): Suggest improvements to our product. Share your ideas and suggestions on how we can make our product better.

### Product Issues (product_issues)
• **Product Issues (product_issues)** (`text`, optional): Report any issues with our product. Describe the problems you've encountered and how we can fix them.

### Report Issues (report_issues)
• **Report Issues (report_issues)** (`note`, optional): Report any issues with our product. (Note: This field is for providing more details about the issue if needed)

### Product Improvement Report Form (product_improvement_report_form)
• **Product Improvement Report Form (product_improvement_report_form)** (note, required): This is a note field, but it is actually the title of the form, it is required to be filled in order to identify the form and its purpose.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing your feedback.
* Use clear and concise language.
* If you have any attachments or screenshots to support your suggestions or issues, feel free to attach them to your form.
* Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in helping us improve our product.
