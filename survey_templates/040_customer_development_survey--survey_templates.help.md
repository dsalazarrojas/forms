# Customer Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and insights from customers to improve customer development efforts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely, and make sure all required fields are filled.
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about any question, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Please enter your company name.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Please enter your company website (if applicable).
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Please select your company size from the options:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
* **Employee Count** (`employee_count`, number, optional): Please enter the number of employees in your company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your company email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your company phone number (if applicable).
* **Areas of Feedback** (`feedback_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all areas where you would like to provide feedback:
	+ Sales
	+ Marketing
	+ Product
	+ Development
	+ Support
* **Feedback Description** (`feedback_description`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your feedback.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all areas where you would like to see improvements:
	+ Cost
	+ Quality
	+ Functionality
	+ User Experience
	+ Performance
* **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all expected outcomes:
	+ Revenue Growth
	+ Customer Growth
	+ Market Growth
	+ Product Growth
	+ Process Growth
* **Actual Outcome** (`actual_outcome`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all actual outcomes:
	+ Revenue Growth
	+ Customer Growth
	+ Market Growth
	+ Product Growth
	+ Process Growth

## Tips

* If you're not sure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* Provide detailed and accurate information to help us better serve you.
* If you've already filled out this form, please do not submit again to avoid duplication.
