# Autism Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from users on their experiences and perceptions about people with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). The goal of this form is to understand how individuals in the community view and interact with autistic individuals, as well as identify areas where support mechanisms and resources are needed to improve social integration and inclusion.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions as honestly as possible.
2. Select the most accurate option for each question.
3. Provide detailed answers for text-based questions.
4. Ensure you complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Familiarity with Autism** (`familiarity_with_autism`): This question assesses how familiar you are with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Please select the option that best describes your level of familiarity.
* **Personal Connection** (`personal_connection`): This question aims to understand your relationship with the autism community. Select the option that best describes your connection.
* **Community Inclusion Opinion** (`community_inclusion_opinion`): This question seeks to gauge your opinion on the community's inclusion of people with autism. Select the option that best reflects your view.
* **Social Barriers Observation** (`social_barriers_observation`): This is an open-ended question where you can describe the main barriers you observe for people with autism to be fully integrated into the community.
* **Comfort Level Working with Autistic Colleagues** (`comfort_level_working`): This question aims to understand how comfortable you would feel working alongside someone with autism. Select the option that best describes your level of comfort.
* **Support Mechanisms Needed** (`support_mechanisms_needed`): This question seeks to identify the areas that need more focus in the community. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Community Resources Adequacy** (`community_resources_adequacy`): This question gauges your opinion on the current resources in your area. Select the option that best describes your view.
* **Suggested Improvements** (`suggested_improvements`): This is an open-ended question where you can describe the specific changes that would make the biggest difference in improving social integration and inclusion.
* **Participate in Awareness Events** (`participate_in_awareness_event`): This question seeks to understand if you would be interested in participating in future awareness events.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): This is an open-ended question where you can share any other thoughts or experiences you have about people with autism.
