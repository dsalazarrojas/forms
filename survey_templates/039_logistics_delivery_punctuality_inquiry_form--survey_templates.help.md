# Logistics Delivery Punctuality Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to report incidents of late, early, or on-time deliveries. It helps us understand the circumstances surrounding the delivery and identify areas for improvement to better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date of the delivery in the "Delivery Date" field.
2.  Enter the time of the delivery in the "Delivery Time" field.
3.  Enter the name of the carrier or transportation service used for the delivery.
4.  Choose the delivery status: "On Time," "Late," or "Early."
5.  Select the reason for the late delivery from the options provided or type in a custom reason under "Other (please specify)."
6.  Select the reason for the early delivery from the options provided or type in a custom reason under "Other (please specify)."
7.  Add any additional comments or details related to the delivery.
8.  Enter the name of the customer service representative who you've contacted or would like to contact.
9.  Enter the email of the customer service representative who you've contacted or would like to contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, Date, not required): Enter the date of the scheduled delivery. This helps us track delivery times.
    *   **Example:** 2023-03-15 (YYYY-MM-DD format)
*   **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, Time, not required): Enter the scheduled delivery time. This helps us pinpoint the exact time of delivery.
    *   **Example:** 14:30 (HH:MM format)
*   **Carrier Name** (`carrier_name`, Text, not required): Enter the name of the delivery carrier or transportation service used for the delivery.
*   **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, Select, not required): Choose the status of your delivery from the options below:
    *   **On Time**: If your delivery arrived at the scheduled time.
    *   **Late**: If your delivery was delayed beyond the scheduled time.
    *   **Early**: If your delivery arrived before the scheduled time.
*   **Reason for Late Delivery** (`reason_late_delivery`, Select, not required): Select the reason for your late delivery or provide a custom reason if it's not listed.
    *   **Traffic**
    *   **Weather**
    *   **Mechanical Issues**
    *   **Other (please specify)**
*   **Reason for Early Delivery** (`reason_early_delivery`, Select, not required): Select the reason for your early delivery or provide a custom reason if it's not listed.
    *   **Prioritization**
    *   **Scheduling**
    *   **Other (please specify)**
*   **Additional Comments** (`comments`, Note, not required): Add any additional information or comments related to the delivery that may help us improve our services.
*   **Customer Service Name** (`customer_service_name`, Text, not required): Enter the name of the customer service representative who you've contacted or would like to contact.
*   **Customer Service Email** (`customer_service_email`, Email, not required): Enter the email of the customer service representative who you've contacted or would like to contact.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure we can effectively address any issues.
*   If you need to report an urgent issue, contact customer service through other channels.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
