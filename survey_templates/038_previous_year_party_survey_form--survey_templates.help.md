# Previous Year Party Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on a party that took place in the previous year. Please answer the questions accurately to provide valuable insights for future party planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe the party theme, attendees, venue, food, dress code, and entertainment.
2. If applicable, enter the number of people who attended the party.
3. If applicable, enter the cost of the party.
4. Enter any comments or feedback about the party.
5. Enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the party theme?** (`party_theme`, select_one): Choose one of the options that best describes the theme of the party.
* **Who was a prophet at the party?** (`party_prophets`, select_one): Choose one of the options that best describes a person who played a prophetic role at the party.
* **How many people attended the party?** (`party_attendees`, number): Enter the number of people who attended the party.
* **Where was the party held?** (`party_venue`, select_one): Choose one of the options that best describes the location of the party.
* **What type of food was served?** (`party_food`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply from the options that best describe the food served at the party.
* **What was the dress code for the party?** (`party_dress`, select_one): Choose one of the options that best describes the dress code for the party.
* **What type of entertainment was provided?** (`party_entertainment`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply from the options that best describe the entertainment provided at the party.
* **How much did the party cost?** (`party_cost`, number): Enter the total cost of the party.
* **Do you have any comments about the party?** (`party_comments`, text): Enter any feedback or comments about the party.
* **What is your name?** (`party_planner_name`, text): Enter your name.
* **Was there a prophet at the party?** (`party_prophets_2`, checkbox): Check if there was a prophet at the party.
* **Was there a second prophet at the party?** (`party_prophets2`, checkbox): Check if there was a second prophet at the party.

Please complete the form accurately and submit your answers.
