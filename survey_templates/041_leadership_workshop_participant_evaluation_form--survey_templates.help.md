# Leadership Workshop Participant Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from workshop participants on the leadership workshop, helping us to improve future workshops. Please provide your honest feedback to help us understand what worked well and what can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. For the "Feedback on Facilitators" field, select all that apply.
3. For the "Rating of Participant" and "Rating of Content" fields, use a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
4. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your badge or ID badge. This will help us identify who completed the form.
- **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you attended the workshop. This will help us match your feedback with the specific workshop session you attended.
- **Feedback on Facilitators** (`feedback_on_facilitators`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	* Yes
	* No
- **Feedback on Content** (`feedback_on_content`, text, required/optional): Please provide any feedback you have on the workshop content, such as what you liked or disliked.
- **Rating of Participant** (`rating_of_participant`, number, required/optional): Use a scale of 1-5 to rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop content. 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
- **Rating of Content** (`rating_of_content`, number, required/optional): Use a scale of 1-5 to rate the quality of the workshop content. 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
- **Rating of Facilitators** (`rating_of_facilitators`, number, required/optional): Use a scale of 1-5 to rate how well the facilitators did. 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

## Tips
- Make sure to be honest and provide constructive feedback.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please include them in the "Feedback on Content" field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the workshop organizers or facilitators.
