# F1 Season Predictions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The F1 Season Predictions Survey is a tool to gather users' expectations and forecasts for the upcoming F1 Season. This survey aims to collect your predictions on various topics related to the season, including top drivers, constructors, and other relevant subjects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the drivers you think will perform well in the season.
2. Choose your top picks for the top drivers and constructors.
3. Provide your forecasts for the season.
4. Make your final picks for the top driver and top constructor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Season Picks** (`season_picks`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the drivers you think will perform well in the season. You can choose multiple drivers by selecting each relevant option.
* **Top Drivers** (`top_drivers`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your top picks for the drivers you think will perform well in the season.
* **Season Forecasts** (`season_forecasts`, text, optional): Provide your general forecasts for the season. This is an open-ended field where you can type in your thoughts and predictions.
* **Top Engineering** (`top_engineering`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" if you think engineering will play a significant role in the season's top performers.
* **Top Engineering Team** (`top_engineer`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you think a particular team will excel in engineering this season.
* **Top Constructor** (`top_constructor`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" if you think a particular constructor will win the season.
* **Top Drivers Picks** (`top_drivers_picks`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the drivers you think will be among the top performers in the season.
* **Season Forecast Picks** (`season_forecast_picks`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options for your general season forecasts, such as weather or circuit conditions.
* **Top Driver** (`top_driver`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you think a particular driver will win the season.
* **Top Constructor Picks** (`top_constructor_picks`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the constructors you think will be among the top performers in the season.
