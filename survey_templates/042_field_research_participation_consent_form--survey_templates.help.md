# Field Research Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Research Participation Consent Form is designed to collect information from researchers and institutions participating in field research. This form ensures they have given their consent and agree to the terms of participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the relevant information.
2. Review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Participation Consent Form** (`research_participation_consent_form`, text, optional): This field is a general description of the form and its purpose. You can leave it blank.
* **Researcher Name** (`researcher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the researcher who is participating in the field research.
* **Research Participating Institution** (`research_participating_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of the institution participating in the field research.
* **Research Participating Department** (`research_participating_department`, text, optional): Enter the name of the department participating in the field research.
* **Research Participating Institutional Review Board Approval** (`research_participating_institutional_review_board_approval`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the institution's review board has approved the research project, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Research Objectives** (`research_objectives`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the research objectives.
* **Participant Informed Consent** (`participant_informed_consent`, text, optional): Enter a statement confirming that participants have been informed of their rights and responsibilities.
* **Participant Right to Withdrawal** (`participant_right_to_withdrawal`, text, optional): Enter a statement confirming that participants have the right to withdrawal at any time.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter contact information for the researcher or institution.
* **Research Participating Agreement** (`research_participating_agreement`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the institution agrees to participate in the research, or 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
- Be sure to review the form for accuracy before submission.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the form schema for more information.
- If you are the researcher, ensure that you have informed participants of their rights and responsibilities before submitting this form.
