# Geography App Idea Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geography App Idea Submission form is a tool for collecting and sharing ideas about geography-related apps. If you have a idea for a geography app, this form is the right place to submit it.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit your idea, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Describe your app idea in the "Concept Description" field, focusing on its key features and functionalities.
3. Select the type of user you are submitting the idea as: Student, Educator, or Product Team.
4. Provide information about the school department you are affiliated with, if applicable.
5. Specify the school you are associated with, if applicable.
6. Enter the submission date and review date for your idea.
7. Choose the submitter type (Student, Educator, or Product Team) and provide any additional information about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the submission.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for further communication and contact purposes.
* **Geography App** (`geography_app`, `text`, required): Describe your app idea, focusing on its core features and functionalities.
* **Concept Description** (`concept`, `text`, required): Expand on your app idea, elaborating on its key features, functionalities, and potential uses.
* **Concept Type** (`concept_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of user you are submitting the idea as: Student, Educator, or Product Team.
* **School Department** (`school_department`, `text`, required): If you are affiliated with a school department, please provide its name.
* **School** (`school`, `text`, required): If you are associated with a school, please provide its name.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting the idea.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of user you are submitting the idea as: Student, Educator, or Product Team.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about yourself, if applicable.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you would like to have your idea reviewed.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear in your answers to help us better understand your idea.
* Proofread your submission to ensure accuracy and correctness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
