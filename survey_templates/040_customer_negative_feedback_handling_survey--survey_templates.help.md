# Customer Negative Feedback Handling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Negative Feedback Handling Survey is used to document and track customer feedback received on products or services. This form helps to ensure that feedback is properly recorded, categorized, and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form only when actual customer feedback has been received**. This form is meant to track specific customer feedback, not general feedback or comments.
2. Read each field carefully and fill it out accordingly.
3. Make sure to answer all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
4. If you are unsure about a field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Feedback Received** (`customer_feedback_received`): *Note field for customer feedback*
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`): *Select the type of feedback received: Yes or No*
* **Description** (`description`): *Enter a description of the customer feedback*
* **Issue Area** (`issue_area`): *Select the issue area related to the feedback: Yes or No*
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`): *Enter a description of the issue related to the feedback*
* **Resolution Action Plan** (`resolution_action_plan`): *Enter a description of the action plan to resolve the issue*
* **Follow-up** (`followup`): *Select the follow-up status: None or Time*
