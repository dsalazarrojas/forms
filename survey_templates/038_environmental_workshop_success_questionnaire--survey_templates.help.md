# Environmental Workshop Success Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Environmental Workshop Success Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness and satisfaction of an environmental workshop or training session. It helps organizers to identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions in the "Session Information" section as thoroughly as possible, providing specific details about your experience and expectations.
2. For questions with a "Check all that apply" option, select all the options that apply to your experience.
3. Use the "Additional Comments" section to provide any further thoughts or suggestions you may have.
4. Make sure to provide your email address and phone number if you want to be contacted for follow-up information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you feel about the session** (1)
	+ This question helps us understand your overall impression of the session.
* **What impact did you gain from this session** (2)
	+ Please answer this question in a few sentences, describing what you learned or gained from the session.
* **Did you achieve the learning objectives** (3)
	+ Check all the objectives that you achieved or partially achieved.
* **How many people attended** (4)
	+ Provide the exact number of people who attended the session.
* **How would you rate the facilitator** (5)
	+ Rate the facilitator's performance on a scale of 5.
* **What materials were provided** (6)
	+ Check all the materials that were provided during the session.
* **Overall satisfaction** (7)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale of 5.
* **How satisfied were you with this session** (8)
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the session on a scale of 5.
* **What could be improved** (9)
	+ Provide any suggestions or comments you may have about the session.
* **Did the session meet your goals** (10)
	+ Check all the goals that the session met or partially met.
* **How well did we manage the time** (11)
	+ Rate the time management of the session on a scale of 5.
* **Would you like to receive follow-up information** (12)
	+ Check how often you would like to be contacted for follow-up information.
* **How long was the session** (13)
	+ Provide the exact duration of the session.
* **Was the location good for you** (14)
	+ Check all the locations that were good for you.
* **Additional notes** (15)
	+ Provide any further comments or suggestions you may have.
* **How to contact you** (16)
	+ Check all the methods you prefer to be contacted for follow-up information.
* **How to be contacted** (17)
	+ Check all the methods you prefer to be contacted.
* **How often would you like to be contacted** (18)
	+ Check all the frequencies you prefer to be contacted.
* **Email** (19)
	+ Provide your email address.
* **Phone** (20)
	+ Provide your phone number.
* **Session date** (21)
	+ Provide the date of the session.
* **Session start time** (22)
	+ Provide the start time of the session.
* **Session end time** (23)
	+ Provide the end time of the session.
* **Session duration** (24)
	+ Provide the duration of the session.
* **Follow-up** (25)
	+ Rate your preference for follow-up contact on a scale of 5.
