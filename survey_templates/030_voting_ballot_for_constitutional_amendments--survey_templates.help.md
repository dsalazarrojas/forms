# Voting Ballot For Constitutional Amendments - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect votes from users on constitutional amendments. It consists of multiple pages, each with a specific set of questions or fields related to the amendments. The form will guide you through the process of providing your input.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and understand the questions or fields presented.
2. Select the relevant options for each amendment, taking into account the provided choices.
3. Enter your date and time of vote if applicable.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments if necessary.
5. Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your vote.
6. Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about your vote.
7. Finally, submit your vote by selecting one of the provided options for each amendment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Voting Ballot For Constitutional Amendments** (`title`): This is the title of the form, which provides a clear understanding of the form's purpose.
* **Amendment 1** (`amendment_1`): Select all the options that apply to your vote on Amendment 1. Please note that this field is mandatory.
* **Date of Vote** (`date`): If applicable, enter the date on which you cast your vote. This field is optional.
* **Time of Vote** (`time`): If applicable, enter the time on which you cast your vote. This field is optional.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`): If you have any additional comments or notes related to your vote, please provide them here. This field is optional.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your vote. This field is optional.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about your vote. This field is optional.
* **Vote Result** (`vote_result`): Select your preferred option for each amendment. Please make sure to select only one option per amendment. This field is mandatory.
