# Course Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Survey Form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from course participants, instructors, or administrators to help improve the course content, structure, and teaching methods.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully before starting to complete it.
- Fill in the required fields with accurate and clear information.
- Use the provided options or text box to provide your feedback.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **page-1** (text, required/optional): This is the first page of the form. Enter your name, email, or any other identifying information as requested.
- **page-2** (select_one, required/optional): Select the option that best describes your experience with the course.
	+ 'Yes': You have had a positive experience with the course.
	+ 'No': You have not had a positive experience with the course.
- **page-3** (note, required/optional): This is a text box for any additional comments or feedback you may have about the course.
- **page-4** (number, required/optional): Enter any relevant number values as requested.
- **page-5** (number, required/optional): Enter any other relevant number values as requested.
- **page-6** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply for any additional feedback about the course.
	+ 'Yes': You agree with the statement.
	+ 'No': You do not agree with the statement.
- **page-7** (select_one, required/optional): Select the option that best describes your opinion about a particular aspect of the course.
- **page-8** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply for any additional feedback about the course.
- **page-9** (date, required/optional): Enter the date as requested.
- **page-10** (time, required/optional): Enter the time as requested.
- **page-11** (note, required/optional): This is a text box for any additional comments or feedback you may have about the course.
- **page-12** (text, required/optional): Enter your thoughts or opinions as requested.
- **page-13** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address as requested.
- **page-14**, **page-15** to **page-23** (text, required/optional): Enter your thoughts or opinions as requested.
- **page-24** to **page-25** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about the course.

Note: Please review each page carefully to ensure you have completed all required fields and provided accurate information. If you have any questions or need clarification, please do not hesitate to contact the course administrator.
