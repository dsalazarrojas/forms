# Customer Effort Score (CES) Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Effort Score (CES) Survey is designed to measure the customer's perception of the effort required to resolve an issue with your company. It's a crucial tool for support teams to gauge their performance and make necessary improvements to provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of interaction you had with the company today (Online Purchase, Technical Support Request, Billing Inquiry, Product Return/Exchange, or General Information).
2. Answer the main question about how easy it was to handle your issue (Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree).
3. Explain why you gave this rating (optional, but helps us understand your perspective).
4. Indicate your level of satisfaction with the time taken to resolve your issue (Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied).
5. Rate whether the representative you spoke with was helpful (Yes, very helpful, Yes, somewhat, No, not helpful, or Not Applicable).
6. Confirm whether you had to contact us more than once for this issue (True or False).
7. Provide a suggestion on how we can improve (optional).
8. Grant permission to contact you for further discussion about your feedback (True or False).
9. If you granted permission, enter your email address so we can contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of interaction did you have today?**: Select the type of interaction you had with our company (e.g., Online Purchase, Technical Support Request, etc.).
* **The company made it easy for me to handle my issue**: Answer to what extent you agree with this statement (Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree).
* **Why did you give this rating?**: Explain the reasons behind your rating, if you'd like to provide more context.
* **How satisfied were you with the time it took to resolve your issue?**: Rate your satisfaction with the time taken to resolve your issue (Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied).
* **Was the representative you spoke with helpful?**: Rate the helpfulness of the representative you interacted with (Yes, very helpful, Yes, somewhat, No, not helpful, or Not Applicable).
* **Did you have to contact us more than once for this issue?**: Confirm whether you needed to contact us multiple times for this issue (True or False).
* **What is one thing we could do to make this process easier for you?**: Provide a suggestion on how we can improve (optional).
* **May we contact you to discuss your feedback further?**: Grant permission to contact you for further discussion about your feedback (True or False).
* **Your Email Address**: If you granted permission, enter your email address so we can contact you. 
* **Date of Interaction**: Please enter the date of your interaction with our company.
* **-- Thank You --**: A note to express our appreciation for taking the time to provide feedback.
