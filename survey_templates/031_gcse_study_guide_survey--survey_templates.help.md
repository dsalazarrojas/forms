This guide will help you complete the GCSE Study Guide Survey, which is designed to gather information about your study habits and preferences. The survey is used to understand your needs and provide guidance on effective studying for the GCSE exam. Please note that this survey is optional and your responses will be kept confidential. If you have already completed this survey, please do not fill it out again, as your responses will be treated as an update to your previous answers.

# GCSE Study Guide Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your study habits and preferences to help you prepare effectively for the GCSE exam.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select only one option for the "Which one of these study materials is most useful?" question.
3. Select multiple options for the "Which of these study materials are helpful for you?" question.
4. Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
5. Enter your phone number in the correct format (e.g., `01234 567890`).
6. Enter your time in 24-hour format (e.g., `12:00`).
7. Select the time you spend on revision.
8. Select the frequency of your revision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your school name where you study for the GCSE exam.
- **What is your Grade** (`grade_level`, number, required: false): Please select your current grade level (e.g., 11, 12, etc.).
- **What subject are you interested in** (`subject_interest`, text, required: false): Please select the subject you are interested in (e.g., mathematics, science, etc.).
- **What study materials are you using** (`study_materials`, text, required: false): Please list any study materials you are currently using for your GCSE exam (e.g., textbook, online resources, etc.).
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Do you have any feedback for us?
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number in the correct format (e.g., `01234 567890`).
- **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date you completed this survey in the correct format (e.g., `2022-01-01`).
- **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Please enter the time you spent on revision in the correct 24-hour format (e.g., `12:00`).
- **Any other information** (`note`, text, required: false): If you have any other information that might be helpful for us, please enter it here.
- **Which one of these study materials is most useful?** (`select_one_survey_materials`, select_one, required: false): Select the study material that you find most helpful.
- **Which of these study materials are helpful for you?** (`select_multiple_survey_materials`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the study materials that you find helpful.
- **How much time do you spend on revision?** (`study_time`, number, required: false): Please enter the amount of time you spend on revision in hours.
- **How often do you revise?** (`revision_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select the frequency of your revision.
