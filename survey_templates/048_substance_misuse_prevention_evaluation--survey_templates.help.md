# substance misuse prevention evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate programs aimed at preventing substance misuse. It is used to gather information about the programs' goals, objectives, target population, services offered, and other relevant aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information about the program.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
4. If you are unsure about any question or need more information, contact the form administrator at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Here is an explanation of each field:

* **School Programs** (`school_programs`, `text`, required: false): Describe the school programs or initiatives that are part of the substance misuse prevention program.
* **Program Goals** (`program_goals`, `text`, required: false): Specify the main objectives of the program, such as reducing substance misuse among students or providing education about addiction prevention.
* **Program Objectives** (`program_objectives`, `text`, required: false): Outline the specific, measurable goals of the program, such as reducing the number of substance misuse incidents among students or improving student attitudes towards addiction prevention.
* **Target Population** (`target_population`, `text`, required: false): Identify the specific group of people or demographics that the program is aimed at, such as students of a particular grade level or age group.
* **Program Services** (`program_services`, `text`, required: false): Describe the services offered by the program, such as counseling, education, or support groups.
* **Program Partners** (`program_partners`, `text`, required: false): List the organizations or individuals that are collaborating with the program.
* **Program Evaluation** (`program_evaluation`, `text`, required: false): Describe how the program is evaluated, such as through surveys, data analysis, or regular meetings.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Gather feedback from participants to better understand their experiences and perceptions of the program.
* **Perceived Benefits** (`perceived_benefits`, `text`, required: false): Ask participants to describe any benefits they perceive from the program.
* **Participant Engagement** (`participant_engagement`, `text`, required: false): Measure the level of engagement from participants, such as attendance rates or time spent on program activities.
* **Program Funding** (`program_funding`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about the funding sources and amounts for the program.
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, `text`, required: false): Describe the methods used to collect data, such as surveys, interviews, or focus groups.
* **Substance Use** (`substance_use`, `text`, required: false): Discuss the substance misuse issues that the program aims to address, such as tobacco, alcohol, or drug use.
