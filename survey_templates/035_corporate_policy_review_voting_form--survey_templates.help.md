# corporate_policy_review_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate policy review voting form is a tool for employees to participate in the review and voting process of company policies. It allows them to express their opinions and provide feedback on the policies, which helps in making informed decisions for the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the policy title in the **Policy Title** field.
2. Provide a brief description of the policy in the **Policy Description** field.
3. Choose your vote by selecting one of the options in the **Vote** field: Agree, Disagree, or Abstain.
4. If you have any additional feedback or comments, enter them in the **Feedback** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
6. If you have any comments or additional information, enter them in the **Comments** field.
7. Select the policy status from the **Policy Status** field: Active, Inactive, or Draft.
8. Choose a date for the review in the **Review Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policy Title** (`policy_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the policy you are reviewing.
* **Policy Description** (`policy_description`, text, required): Briefly describe the policy.
* **Vote** (`vote`, select_one, optional): Choose how you feel about the policy: Agree, Disagree, or Abstain.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the policy.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the policy: Active, Inactive, or Draft.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the review.
