# Ancient Human Sites Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ancient Human Sites Survey form is designed to collect information about ancient human sites, such as archaeological sites, settlements, or other significant locations from the past. The form helps researchers, surveyors, or enthusiasts document and categorize these sites, their conditions, and conservation priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your Researcher / Surveyor Name and Site ID Code, which will help us identify and link your survey data to the correct location.
2. Enter the Date of Survey, making sure to select the correct date of your visit to the site.
3. Provide the Geographic Coordinates (Lat/Long) of the site to help us locate it on a map.
4. Describe any Nearby Landmarks or Access Points that can aid in site identification or navigation.
5. Select the Primary Site Type from the provided options (e.g., Cave, Settlement, Burial Mound, Ritual Site, or Industrial/Mining).
6. Assess the Current Preservation Status of the site, choosing from the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Critically Endangered).
7. Check all that apply for Visible Archaeological Features you observed during your survey (e.g., Stone Tools, Pottery Shards, Structural Foundations, Rock Art, or Human Remains).
8. Describe any Immediate Threats to the site, such as erosion, development, or looting.
9. Add any Recommendations for the site's conservation or future excavation.
10. Prioritize the site's priority for further excavation (High, Medium, or Low).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researcher / Surveyor Name** (`researcher_name`, text, **required**): Enter your name and title (if applicable) for reference and identification purposes.
* **Site ID Code** (`site_id_code`, text, **required**): A unique code assigned to the site, helping us track and categorize data.
* **Date of Survey** (`date_of_survey`, date, **required**): The date you visited or surveyed the site.
* **Geographic Coordinates (Lat/Long)** (`geographic_coordinates`, text, **required**): The latitude and longitude coordinates of the site, aiding in precise location identification.
* **Nearby Landmarks or Access Points** (`nearby_landmarks`, text, **optional**): Describes features or points of interest near the site that may assist in navigation or identification.
* **Primary Site Type** (`site_type`, select_one, **required**): Select one of the provided types to categorize the site.
* **Current Preservation Status** (`preservation_status`, select_one, **required**): Choose the current condition of the site (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critically Endangered).
* **Visible Archaeological Features** (`visible_features`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all features you observed during the survey (e.g., Stone Tools, Pottery Shards, etc.).
* **Immediate Threats to Site** (`immediate_threats`, text, **optional**): Describe any hazards or risks affecting the site.
* **Recommendations** (`section_recommendations`, note, **optional**): Provide suggestions for the site's conservation or future excavation.
* **Priority for Further Excavation** (`priority_for_excavation`, select_one, **required**): Categorize the site's priority for further investigation or excavation (High, Medium, or Low).
