# Teens Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from teenagers about their interests, preferences, and experiences. It will help researchers to understand their favorite activities, foods, movies, music, places, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the intro page to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Answer the questions on each page by selecting the options that best match your preferences.
3. Make sure to answer all the questions, but do not worry if you are unsure about some of them.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the questions.
