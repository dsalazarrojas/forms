# ISP Sampling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on various aspects of Internet Service Providers (ISPs) to help evaluate and improve their quality of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the form in the order of the pages.
2. For each page, fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. If a field is optional, feel free to provide additional information if you wish.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **ISP Name** (`isp_name`, text, optional)
Enter the name of your current Internet Service Provider (ISP).

### **Sampling Interest** (`sampling_interest`, select_multiple, optional)
Select one or more of the following options to indicate your sampling interest:
* High
* Medium
* Low

### **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options to indicate your satisfaction level:
* Very dissatisfied
* Somewhat dissatisfied
* Neutral
* Somewhat satisfied
* Very satisfied

### **Current ISP** (`current_isp`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options to indicate your current ISP:
* AT&T
* Comcast
* Spectrum
* Other
* No current ISP

### **Why Other ISP** (`why_other_isp`, text, optional)
If you selected "Other" for your current ISP, provide a brief explanation in this field.

### **Why Not Other ISP** (`why_not_other_isp`, text, optional)
If you did not select "Other" for your current ISP, provide a brief explanation in this field.

### **ISP Satisfaction** (`isp_satisfaction`, text, optional)
Enter your thoughts on your current ISP's satisfaction level.

### **Would You Try** (`would_trial`, select_multiple, optional)
Select one or more of the following options to indicate if you would try a new ISP:
* Yes
* No

### **Why Try or Not** (`why_trial`, text, optional)
If you selected "Yes" to try a new ISP, provide a brief explanation in this field.

### **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional)
Provide any additional comments about your experience with your current ISP.

### **Service Level** (`isp_service_level`, number, optional)
Enter a numerical value for your current ISP's service level.

### **Internet Speed** (`isp_speed`, number, optional)
Enter a numerical value for your current ISP's internet speed.

### **ISP Security** (`isp_security`, text, optional)
Enter your thoughts on your current ISP's security level.

### **ISP Support** (`isp_support`, text, optional)
Enter your thoughts on your current ISP's support level.
