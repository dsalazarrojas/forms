# Subscribe To Mental Health Updates - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the "Subscribe To Mental Health Updates" form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose a category that best describes your interest in mental health updates. You can select one or multiple options from the list provided.
2. Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates.
3. Provide your name and phone number if you would like to be contacted through these means.
4. Enter your organization or affiliation if applicable.
5. Choose how you heard about us, such as through social media, a referral, or online search.
6. Select how you would like to be contacted, such as through email, phone, or mail.
7. Add any additional comments or notes if you have any specific requests or questions.
8. Confirm your email address and phone number to ensure accuracy.
9. Confirm your name and organization to ensure accuracy.
10. Review and confirm your comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Id (1)**: A unique identifier for the form.
* **Category (2)**: Choose a category that best describes your interest in mental health updates.
* **Description (3)**: Enter a brief description to help us better understand your needs.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted through this means.
* **Name (6)**: Enter your name.
* **Organization (7)**: Enter your organization or affiliation if applicable.
* **How did you hear about us (8)**: Select how you heard about us, such as through social media, a referral, or online search.
* **How can we contact you (9)**: Select how you would like to be contacted, such as through email, phone, or mail.
* **Comments (10)**: Add any additional comments or notes if you have any specific requests or questions.
* **Confirm Email (11)**: Enter your email address to confirm accuracy.
* **Confirm Phone (12)**: Enter your phone number to confirm accuracy.
* **Confirm Name (13)**: Enter your name to confirm accuracy.
* **Confirm Organization (14)**: Enter your organization to confirm accuracy.
* **Confirm Comments (15)**: Confirm your comments or notes.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate to prevent missed communications.
* If you have any specific requests or questions, please add them in the comments section.
* Our team will use the information provided to tailor our mental health updates to your interests and preferences.
* We value your privacy and will not share your contact information with any third parties.
