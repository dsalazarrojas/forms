# Template Wix Client Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for gathering client satisfaction feedback from clients who have worked with Wix.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the introduction above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that we may contact you with.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how satisfied you are with our service. You can choose between "Satisfied" and "Dissatisfied".
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Provide any comments or feedback you may have about your experience with Wix.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required/optional): If you have any additional feedback, please provide it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we receive your feedback.
* Be as specific as possible when providing your comments and feedback.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, feel free to contact us.
