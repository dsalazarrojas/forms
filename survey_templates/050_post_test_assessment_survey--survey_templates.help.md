# post_test_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The post_test_assessment_survey form is designed to gather feedback from participants after a training session. It helps the organization to understand their impressions, suggestions, and ratings on various aspects of the session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. You can skip any question that you feel is not applicable or you are not comfortable answering.
3. Please provide your feedback in as much detail as possible.
4. You can add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Do you have any other comments or suggestions about this training session?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Please provide a brief introduction to your feedback, explaining your overall impression of the training session.
* **What is your overall impression of this training session?** (question1): Rate your overall impression of the training session.
* **What did you find most useful in this training session?** (question2): Describe the most useful part of the training session.
* **What did you find least useful in this training session?** (question3): Describe the least useful part of the training session.
* **What would you suggest we improve in future training sessions?** (question4): Provide suggestions for improvement.
* **What would you suggest we add to future training sessions?** (question5): Suggest new topics or activities to be included in future sessions.
* **Is there anything that you would like to add that is not on this list?** (question6): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **How likely are you to recommend this training session to a colleague?** (question7): Rate your likelihood of recommending the training session.
* **How long did you spend in this training session?** (question8): Report the actual time spent in the session.
* **How long did you expect to spend in this training session?** (question9): Report the expected time spent in the session.
* **Was the pace of this training session too fast or too slow?** (question10): Rate the pace of the session.
* **How would you rate the instructor's presentation style?** (question11): Rate the instructor's presentation style.
* **How would you rate the quality of the course materials?** (question12): Rate the quality of the course materials.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with this training session?** (question13): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session.
* **Were there any problems or issues with your experience in the training session?** (question14): Describe any problems or issues you experienced.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions about this training session?** (question15): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Is there anything that you liked most about this training session?** (question16): Describe the most liked part of the session.
* **Is there anything that you liked least about this training session?** (question17): Describe the least liked part of the session.
* **Did you find the training session easy to use?** (question18): Rate the ease of use of the session.
* **Was the training session organized logically?** (question19): Rate the organization of the session.
* **Were you able to access any resources provided during the training session?** (question20): Report whether you were able to access resources.
* **Was this training session relevant?** (question22): Rate the relevance of the session.
* **Was this training session relevant to your job?** (question23): Rate the relevance to your job.
* **Did you have any difficulty with the technology used in this training session?** (question24): Rate the difficulty with technology.
* **Was this the first training session on this topic you've ever been to?** (question25): Report whether this was your first session on this topic.
