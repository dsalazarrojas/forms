# Tenant Annual Living Condition Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about the living condition of a tenant at the end of each calendar year. It is intended to be filled out by the tenant or their representative, and its purpose is to provide a comprehensive overview of the property's condition at the time of the annual check-in.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Annual Living Condition Check In Date** (`tenant_annual_living_condition_check_in_date`, date, required): Enter the current date in this field.
*   **Maintenance Prioritization** (`maintenance_prioritization`, select_one, required): Select the priority of the maintenance required for the property from the options A, B, C, D, or E.
*   **Tenant Annual Check In Comments** (`tenant_annual_check_in_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments or observations about the condition of the property.
*   **Organized Data Collection** (`organized_data_collection`, select_multiple, required): Select the methods used for data collection for the property from the options A, B, C, D, or E.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you enter the correct date in the "Annual Living Condition Check In Date" field.
*   Choose the correct priority of maintenance required for the property from the options A, B, C, D, or E.
*   Enter any additional information that is relevant to the condition of the property in the "Tenant Annual Check In Comments" field.
*   Select the correct methods used for data collection for the property from the options A, B, C, D, or E.
