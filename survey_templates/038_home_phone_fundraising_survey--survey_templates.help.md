# home_phone_fundraising_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The home_phone_fundraising_survey form gathers information on how you use phone to fundraise, the challenges you face, and areas where you think could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions based on your experience with phone fundraising.
2.  For questions that are multiple-select (e.g., "How often do you use phone to fundraise?" and "What are the main concerns when using phone to fundraise?"), select all applicable options from the provided lists.
3.  For questions that ask for specific details (e.g., "What date did you start using phone to fundraise?" and "What time of day do you usually use phone to fundraise?"), provide as much detail as you can.
4.  For the "Other" options in select_multiple questions, provide specific examples or descriptions of the challenges you face.
5.  In the "Please provide more information" type fields (e.g., "What are other concerns when using phone to fundraise?" and "More details on other areas for improvement"), give as much context and detail as necessary to explain your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you use phone fundraising?** (`home_phone_use`, select_one)
    + This question is to establish whether you are a phone fundraiser.
* **How often do you use phone to fundraise?** (`home_phone_use_for`, select_multiple)
    + This question is to understand the frequency of your phone fundraising activities.
* **What are the main concerns when using phone to fundraise?** (`home_phone_use_concerns`, select_multiple)
    + This question is to identify pain points associated with phone fundraising.
* **What are other concerns when using phone to fundraise?** (`home_phone_use_other_concerns`, note)
    + This field is for any other concerns not listed in the previous question, and you can provide as much detail as you want.
* **What is your email?** (`home_phone_use_email`, email)
    + This is your email address for communication.
* **What is your phone number?** (`home_phone_use_phone_number`, text)
    + This is your phone number for contact.
* **What date did you start using phone to fundraise?** (`home_phone_use_date`, date)
    + This is the date when you first began using phone fundraising.
* **What time of day do you usually use phone to fundraise?** (`home_phone_use_time`, time)
    + This is the time of day when you typically engage in phone fundraising.
* **What area of outreach do you think needs improvement using phone fundraising?** (`home_phone_outreach_improve`, select_multiple)
    + This question is to identify areas where you believe improvement is needed in phone outreach.
* **Other areas for improvement** (`home_phone_outreach_improve_other`, note)
    + This field is for any other areas of improvement not listed in the previous question, and you can provide as much detail as you want.
* **More details on other areas for improvement** (`home_phone_outreach_improve_other_details`, text)
    + This field is for further explanation of your thoughts on other areas of improvement.
* **Home Phone Outreach Improve Other Details 2** (`home_phone_outreach_improve_other_details_2`, text)
    + This field is for additional details on other areas of improvement.
* **Home Phone Outreach Improve Other Details Text** (`home_phone_outreach_improve_other_details_text`, text)
    + This field is for any other details you want to provide about other areas of improvement.
