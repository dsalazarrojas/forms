# Lash Growth Product Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help us understand your experience with our lash growth product. Your feedback will help us identify areas where our product excels and areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as accurately as possible. Each section is designed to collect specific information about your experience with our product. You can skip any sections that don't apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, note, required: false): This is an introductory page that explains the purpose of the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **How frequently have you used the product?** (`result_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the frequencies at which you have used our lash growth product. For example, you may select "Daily" if you use the product every day.
* **How effective was the product in achieving results?** (`result_quality`, number, required: false): If you've used the product, please rate its effectiveness on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is "Not Effective" and 5 is "Very Effective".
* **How satisfied are you with the product?** (`satisfaction`, number, required: false): Please rate your satisfaction with the product on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Have you experienced any side effects while using the product?** (`side_effects`, select_one, required: false): If you've experienced any side effects, please select "True". If not, please select "False".
* **Which area of the product could use improvement?** (`improvement_area`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the areas of the product that you think need improvement. For example, you may select "Price" if you feel it's too expensive.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for the product?** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): If you have any thoughts or ideas on how we can improve the product, please share them in this section.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
