# wheel_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wheel_survey_form is a feedback and rating system designed to gather user opinions on various aspects of a product or service. It is a multi-page form that allows users to provide their ratings and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page carefully and provide your rating and feedback as needed.
2. For the overall rating page (id: 2), select one of the available ratings (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) that best represents your overall rating.
3. For the rating fields (id: 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10), select one or more of the available ratings (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) that best represent your opinions.
4. For the feedback field (id: 3), provide any additional feedback or comments you have.
5. Enter your date of completion in the date field (id: 10).
6. If you have any email address, please enter it in the email field (id: 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Rating (id: 2)** (`Overall rating`, `number`, required): Select your overall rating of the product or service, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Prophet (id: 3)** (`Prophet`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the product or service.
* **Feedback (id: 4)** (`Feedback`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts about the product or service.
* **Rating 5 (id: 5)** (`Rating 5`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more ratings that best represent your opinion about this aspect of the product or service (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Rating 7 (id: 6)** (`Rating 7`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more ratings that best represent your opinion about this aspect of the product or service (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Rating 8 (id: 7)** (`Rating 8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more ratings that best represent your opinion about this aspect of the product or service (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Rating 9 (id: 8)** (`Rating 9`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more ratings that best represent your opinion about this aspect of the product or service (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Rating 10 (id: 9)** (`Rating 10`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more ratings that best represent your opinion about this aspect of the product or service (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Date (id: 10)** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Email (id: 11)** (`Email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address, enter it here.

## Tips
- Please answer each field honestly and as accurately as possible.
- If a field is marked as required, it is necessary to fill it out.
- If you have any further comments or feedback, please use the feedback field.
