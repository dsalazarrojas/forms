# Brewery Seasonal Beer Preference Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect your preferences and opinions about seasonal beers and our brewery's offerings. Your input will help us create more appealing beers that cater to your tastes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name for recognition purposes, if you wish.
2. Provide your email address for us to contact you about your preferences.
3. Select the upcoming season you are responding to (Spring, Summer, Fall, or Winter).
4. Indicate how often you visit our brewery (Weekly or more, 2-3 times monthly, Monthly, Several times per year, Occasionally, or First time).
5. Choose your favorite beer style.
6. Select your preferred beer strength (Light - Under 4% ABV, Session - 4-5% ABV, Standard - 5-6.5% ABV, Strong - 6.5-8% ABV, Very Strong - 8%+ ABV).
7. Choose the seasonal flavors you prefer (select multiple options).
8. Indicate your interest in seasonal limited editions.
9. Show your willingness to try new beers (Very open - love trying new styles, Open to new styles, Prefer familiar favorites, Stick to what I know, Never try new beers).
10. Indicate your interest in experimental brews (Definitely yes, True, Maybe, Probably not, Definitely not).
11. Select your seasonal ABV preference (Spring - Lighter lower ABV, Summer - Light crisp beers, Fall - Medium bodied beers, Winter - Higher ABV warming beers).
12. Choose your preferred packaging format (Draft on tap, Cans, Bottles, Flight tastings, Growlers).
13. Indicate your price consideration (Budget-friendly, Moderate pricing, Premium pricing, Willing to pay for special releases, Price doesn't matter).
14. Provide feedback on previous seasonal offerings (optional).
15. Select the features you must have in seasonal beers (Great taste, Balanced flavor profile, Unique ingredients, Consistency, Interesting story, Limited availability, Award potential).
16. Indicate your interest in collaborative beers (Yes, very interested, Interested, Neutral, Less interested, Not interested).
17. Show your interest in attending seasonal release events (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Maybe, Probably not, Definitely not).
18. Add any additional suggestions or ideas you have for our seasonal beers.

* **Your Name** (1, text, optional): Enter your name for recognition purposes.

* **Which Season Are We Planning For** (4, select_one, required): Choose the upcoming season you are responding to.

* **Your Favorite Beer Style** (6, select_one, required): Choose your favorite beer style.
* **Preferred Beer Strength** (7, select_one, required): Select your preferred beer strength.
* **Seasonal Flavor Preferences** (8, select_multiple, required): Choose the seasonal flavors you prefer.
* **Would You Like Seasonal Limited Editions** (9, select_one, required): Indicate your interest in seasonal limited editions.
* **How Open Are You to New Beer Styles** (10, select_one, required): Show your willingness to try new beers.
* **Interest in Experimental Brews** (11, select_one, optional): Indicate your interest in experimental beers.
* **Seasonal ABV Preference** (12, select_multiple, optional): Select your seasonal ABV preference.
* **Preferred Packaging Format** (13, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred packaging format.
* **Price Point Consideration** (14, select_one, optional): Indicate your price consideration.
* **Feedback on Previous Seasonal Offerings** (15, text, optional): Provide feedback on our previous seasonal offerings.
* **Must-Have Features in Seasonal Beer** (16, select_multiple, optional): Select the features you must have in seasonal beers.
* **Interest in Collaborative Beers** (17, select_one, optional): Indicate your interest in collaborative beers.
* **Would You Attend Seasonal Release Events** (18, select_one, optional): Show your interest in attending seasonal release events.
* **Additional Suggestions or Ideas** (19, text, optional): Add any additional suggestions or ideas you have for our seasonal beers.
