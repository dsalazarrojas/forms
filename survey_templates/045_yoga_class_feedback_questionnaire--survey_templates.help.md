# Yoga Class Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from yoga class attendees about their recent class experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Please fill out the form with your honest feedback about your recent yoga class.
- Make sure to answer all required questions, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "additionalComments" and "suggestions" fields respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (*) {text}
* This field is for you to enter your name.
* This information is required to ensure your feedback is attributed to you.
* Please use your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Age (*) {number}
* This field is for you to enter your age.
* This information is required to better understand the demographics of our yoga class attendees.
* Please enter your age in numeric value (e.g., 25).

### email (*) {email}
* This field is for you to enter your email address.
* This information is required but will not be shared or displayed publicly.
* Make sure to enter a valid email address as this is how we will contact you for follow-up questions if needed.

### classAttended {text}
* This field is for you to describe the yoga class you attended (e.g., "Yin Yoga", "Hatha Yoga", etc.).
* This information is required to ensure we have a accurate record of your class experience.
* Please be as specific as possible when describing the class.

### teacher {text}
* This field is for you to describe your teacher (e.g., "John Doe", "Jane Doe", etc.).
* This information is required to ensure we have accurate records of who led your class.
* Please be as specific as possible when describing the teacher.

### classType {select_one}
* This field is for you to select the type of class you attended (e.g., "Yin", "Yang", "Restorative", or "Other").
* This information is required to help us better understand the classes we offer.
* Please select the type of class that best describes your experience.

### Report {note}
* This field is for you to provide a brief description of your experience.
* This information is required but will not be publicly displayed.
* Please keep your response concise and to the point.

### additionalComments {text}
* This field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* This information is optional but greatly appreciated.
* Please use this field to provide as much detail as you like.

### suggestions {text}
* This field is for you to provide any suggestions or ideas for future classes or improvements.
* This information is optional but will help us grow and improve our classes.
* Please be as specific as possible when suggesting ideas.

### feedback {text}
* This field is for you to provide any general feedback about your experience.
* This information is optional but will help us understand your thoughts and feelings about our classes.
* Please be as honest and constructive as possible when providing feedback.
