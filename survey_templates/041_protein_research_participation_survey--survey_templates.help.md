# Protein Research Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from researchers in the field of protein research. Please answer the questions to help us understand your background, interests, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. The survey is divided into several pages, and each page will ask you to provide information about different aspects of your background and interests.
3. You can save your progress and come back to the form later if needed.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Researchers Background (Text)**: This is the first page of the survey. Please enter a brief description of your background as a researcher in the field of protein research.
* **labName (Text)**: This is the name of your lab where you work or are associated with. Please enter the name of your lab, if applicable.
* **Collaborations Interest (Select One)**: Please select whether you are interested in collaborating with others in the field of protein research. This will help us understand your willingness to collaborate and share knowledge.
* **Education (Select Multiple)**: Please select all the relevant education levels you have achieved, such as MS/MS or Ph.D.
* **Research Areas (Text)**: This is the second page of the survey. Please enter a brief description of the specific research areas you are interested in or have experience with, related to protein research.
* **Research Focus (Select Multiple)**: Please select all the relevant research areas you are focused on, such as Cancer or Cardiovascular.
* **Contact Info (Text)**: This is the third page of the survey. Please enter your contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **Collaboration Interest 2 (Select One)**: Please select whether you are interested in collaborating with others in other projects or areas.
* **Research Experience (Text)**: This is the final page of the survey. Please enter a brief description of your experience with research related to protein, if applicable.
Please let me know if you need anything else.
