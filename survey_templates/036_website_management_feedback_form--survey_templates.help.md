# Website Management Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the website's performance and usability. It is intended for customers who want to share their thoughts on their experience with our website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the "Website Performance" page and provide your feedback on the website's performance.
2. Choose the "Usability" page and provide your feedback on the usability of the website.
3. If you have a feedback email, enter it in the "Feedback Email" field. This will help us contact you if we need more information.
4. If you have a contact phone number, enter it in the "Contact Phone Number" field. This will help us contact you if we need more information.
5. Select which website features are important to you. Choose as many or as few as you like from the list.
6. Choose how you would like to submit your feedback:
	* If you are ready to submit, select "Submit".
	* If you'd like to save your feedback for later, select "Save for Later".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Website Performance
* **Website Performance** (Required): Describe your experience with the website's performance.
### Usability
* **Usability** (Required): Describe your experience with the usability of the website.
### Feedback Email
* **Feedback Email** (Optional): Enter a valid email address, if you'd like us to contact you.
### Contact Phone Number
* **Contact Phone Number** (Optional): Enter a valid phone number, if you'd like us to contact you.
### Website Features
* **Website Features** (Required): Select which website features are important to you. Choose as many or as few as you like from the list.
### Submit Feedback
* **Submit Feedback**: Choose how you'd like to submit your feedback. Select "Submit" to send your feedback immediately, or "Save for Later" to save it for later submission.
