# next_stream_character_preference_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, titled "next_stream_character_preference_survey", is designed to collect information about the user's preferences regarding characters and streams. It gathers data on the user's chosen character, stream content, and character choices, among other details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the character that best represents you from the options provided.
2. Provide a brief description of the stream content you are interested in.
3. Choose multiple character options that appeal to you.
4. Select the time of day when you are typically available to stream.
5. Choose whether you are comfortable with certain character preferences.
6. Add a note to provide any additional information or comments.
