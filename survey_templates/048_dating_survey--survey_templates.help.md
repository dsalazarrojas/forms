# Dating Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences and behaviors related to dating.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Enter your first name in the "What is your first name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "What is your email" field.
4. Choose your favorite food from the "Favorite food" dropdown menu.
5. Answer the "What do you like" and "What do you dislike" questions in the text fields.
6. Choose your favorite music in the "Favorite music" field.
7. Enter your hobby in the "What is your hobby" field.
8. Choose your favorite social media platform from the "Favorite social media" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the cities you have dated in the "What cities have you dated in" field.
10. Choose your favorite animal from the "Favorite animals" field.
11. Answer the "What do you like to listen" and "What do you dislike to listen" questions in the text fields.
12. Enter your favorite songs, movies, books, games, and places in the text fields.
13. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.
