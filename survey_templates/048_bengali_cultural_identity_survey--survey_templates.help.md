# Bengali Cultural Identity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to provide you with a step-by-step explanation on how to complete the Bengali Cultural Identity Survey.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, ensure you have all the required information and a computer/device with a stable internet connection.
- Go through the form page by page, and fill in the requested information for each field.
- Make sure to check the "How To Complete This Form" page again if you are unsure about any field or have questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Survey Date
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): The date you are taking this survey. Please enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.

### Page 2: Respondent Name
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): Your name. This field is optional and only for follow-up purposes.

### Page 3: Age Group
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required): Select the age group that best describes you. Please choose from 18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, 56-65, or 65+.

### Page 4: Gender
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, optional): Select your gender. Please choose from Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer Not to Say.

### Page 5: Country of Current Residence
* **Country of Current Residence** (`country_of_residence`, `text`, required): The country where you currently reside. Please enter the full name of the country.

### Page 6: Place of Birth
* **Place of Birth** (`birth_location`, `text`, optional): Where you were born. This field is optional and may not be relevant to you.

### Page 7: Fluency in Bengali Language
* **Fluency in Bengali Language** (`bengali_language_fluency`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of fluency in Bengali language. Please choose from Native Speaker, Fluent, Conversational, Some Knowledge, or No Knowledge.

### Page 8: How Often Do You Speak Bengali
* **How Often Do You Speak Bengali** (`bengali_language_use`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you speak Bengali. Please choose from Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.

### Page 9: Participation in Bengali Cultural Traditions
* **Participation in Bengali Cultural Traditions** (`cultural_traditions`, `select_one`, required): Select how actively you participate in Bengali cultural traditions. Please choose from Very Active, Active, Somewhat Active, Minimal, or None.

### Page 10: Do You Celebrate Bengali Festivals
* **Do You Celebrate Bengali Festivals** (`festival_celebration`, `select_one`, required): Select if you celebrate Bengali festivals. Please choose from Yes, Always, Yes, Sometimes, Occasionally, Rarely, or False.

### Page 11: What Language Is Spoken in Your Family
* **What Language Is Spoken in Your Family** (`family_language`, `select_one`, required): Select the languages spoken in your family. Please choose from Bengali Only, Bengali and English, Bengali and Other, English Only, or Mixed Languages.

### Page 12: How Strong Is Your Bengali Cultural Identity
* **How Strong Is Your Bengali Cultural Identity** (`cultural_identity_strength`, `select_one`, required): Select how strong your Bengali cultural identity is. Please choose from Very Strong, Strong, Moderate, Weak, or Very Weak.

### Page 13: How Important Are Bengali Food Traditions
* **How Important Are Bengali Food Traditions** (`food_traditions`, `select_one`, required): Select the importance of Bengali food traditions for you. Please choose from Very Important, Important, Somewhat Important, Not Very Important, or Not Important.

### Page 14: Involvement in Bengali Religious Practices
* **Involvement in Bengali Religious Practices** (`religious_practices`, `select_one`, optional): Select your level of involvement in Bengali religious practices. Please choose from Very Involved, Involved, Somewhat Involved, Minimally Involved, or Not Involved.

### Page 15: Connection to Bengali Community
* **Connection to Bengali Community** (`community_connection`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of connection to the Bengali community. Please choose from Very Connected, Connected, Somewhat Connected, Minimally Connected, or Not Connected.

### Page 16: Level of Pride in Bengali Heritage
* **Level of Pride in Bengali Heritage** (`cultural_pride`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of pride in Bengali heritage. Please choose from Very Proud, Proud, Somewhat Proud, Neutral, or Not Proud.

### Page 17: Challenges in Maintaining Cultural Identity
* **Challenges in Maintaining Cultural Identity** (`challenges_maintaining`, `text`, optional): If you face any challenges in maintaining your cultural identity, please describe them here.

### Page 18: Hopes for Passing on Heritage
* **Hopes for Passing on Heritage** (`hopes_for_heritage`, `text`, optional): If you have any hopes for passing on the Bengali heritage to the next generation, please describe them.

### Page 19: Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them here.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any field, please feel free to ask and we will be happy to clarify.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey and help us better understand the Bengali cultural identity.
