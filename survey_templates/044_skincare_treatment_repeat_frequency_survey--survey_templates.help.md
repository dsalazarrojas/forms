# Skincare Treatment Repeat Frequency Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your skincare treatment habits and preferences. Your feedback will help us improve our services and better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply select the options that best match your skincare treatment experiences. You can choose from the available options for each question, and you may leave comments or suggestions if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you usually get treatments?** (`treatment_frequency_1`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks about your treatment frequency. Please choose the option that best describes how often you usually receive skin care treatments. Options include Less than once a week, Once a week, Every 2 weeks, Every 3 weeks, and Every 4 weeks.
* **How satisfied are you with your treatments?** (`treatment_satisfaction_1`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks about your satisfaction level with the treatments you have received. Please choose the option that best reflects your feelings, from Very Satisfied to Not Satisfied.
* **What is your preferred visit frequency?** (`visit_frequency_1`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks about your preferred visit frequency for skin care treatments. Please choose the option that best suits your needs, from Once a Month to Every 3 Months.
* **How long would you like to have between appointments?** (`appointment_1`, `number`, required/optional): Unfortunately, no specific options are provided for this question.
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions for our service?** (`comments_1`, `text`, required/optional): This question allows you to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about our service. Feel free to share your thoughts and ideas.

## Tips

* Please answer questions honestly and accurately based on your experiences.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your participation and feedback in helping us improve our services.
