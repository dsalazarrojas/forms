# high_heels_market_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The high heels market survey is a tool designed to gather customer feedback on high heels products. It's intended to help us understand customer satisfaction, preferences, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the high heel product you are providing feedback on in the Product Name field.
2. Enter your email address to help us contact you if we need more information.
3. Share your feedback about your experience with high heels in the Feedback field.
4. Rate your experience with high heels using the Rating field.
5. Choose the relevant option from the Select One field that best describes your experience.
6. Select all applicable options from the Select Multiple field that describe your experience.
7. Choose a date for the date field.
8. Select a time for the Time field.
