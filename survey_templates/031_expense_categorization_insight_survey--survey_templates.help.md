# Expense Categorization Insight Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the categorization and review process of expenses, enabling you to gain insights on usage, frequency, and status of reviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. For each field, provide the most accurate and honest answers based on your experience with expense categorization and review process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expense Categorization** (`expense_categorization_insight_survey`, text, required: false): This is the main section where you'll provide information on your current expense categorization process. Please describe the categories you use and how often you review them.
* **Review Process** (`review_process`, text, required: false): Here, you can elaborate on your review process, including any notable steps or procedures you follow.
* **Categories** (`categories`, select_one, required: false): Choose from the options to indicate if you currently use the category or not.
* **Categorized Expenses** (`categorized_expenses`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all categories that you have successfully categorized.
* **Review Cycle** (`review_cycle`, date, required: false): Please indicate the frequency of reviews, if any.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select the frequency of reviews you perform.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the review process.
* **Categorized Expenses 2** (`categorized_expenses_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Similar to the previous question, select all categories that you have successfully categorized.
* **Expense Type** (`expense_type`, select_one, required: false): Indicate if you are dealing with a specific type of expense.
* **Review Process 2** (`review_process_2`, text, required: false): Elaborate on your review process, including any notable steps or procedures you follow.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your answers to provide valuable insights.
- If you're not sure about a particular field, feel free to ask for clarification.
- If you have any comments or suggestions, use the "Review Process 2" field to provide them.
