# Research Study Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from participants on the effectiveness, relevance, and impact of a research study. It helps researchers to identify areas for improvement and enhance the participant experience.

## How to Complete This Form

1. **Please answer all questions carefully and thoroughly**. Each question is designed to gather specific information about your experience with the study.
2. **Fill out the form in order**, from 1 to 24, unless otherwise indicated.
3. **Use the provided options for answers**. Select the option that best matches your experience or feelings.
4. **Add any additional comments**. If there is something not covered in the form, use the "additional_comments" field to share your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required false): Enter your name as it appears on your study consent form or ID.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required false): Enter your email address used during the study.
* **Study Effectiveness** (`study_effectiveness`, select one, required false):
	+ **Very Effective**: The study was highly effective and met its objectives.
	+ **Somewhat Effective**: The study was somewhat effective, but had some minor issues.
	+ **Not Effective**: The study was not effective and did not meet its objectives.
* **Study Relevance** (`study_relevance`, select multiple, required false):
	+ **Very Relevant**: The study was very relevant to your interests and needs.
	+ **Somewhat Relevant**: The study was somewhat relevant, but not highly relevant.
	+ **Not Relevant**: The study was not relevant to your interests and needs.
* **Study Impact** (`study_impact`, select one, required false):
	+ **High**: The study had a significant impact on your life or work.
	+ **Medium**: The study had a moderate impact on your life or work.
	+ **Low**: The study had a low impact on your life or work.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, select multiple, required false):

	+ **Low**: You were not satisfied with the study.
* **Participant Recommendation** (`participant_recommendation`, select multiple, required false):

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* Be as specific as possible when answering questions.
* Use the provided options for answers.
* Add any additional comments that are not covered in the form.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the researchers at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
