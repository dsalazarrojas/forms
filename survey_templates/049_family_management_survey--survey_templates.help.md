# Family Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Management Survey is a form designed to collect data on family dynamics, management, and various aspects related to family members and relationships. This form will help us better understand how families interact and manage their relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) in the label.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Dynamics**: A text input asking for a brief description of how family dynamics are managed within the household. This will help us understand the overall atmosphere of the family. 
* **Family Members**: A multiple-choice question asking if family members are present. Please select "Yes" if members are present and "No" otherwise.
* **Family Management**: A text input asking for a brief description of family management within the household. This will help us understand how the family handles daily tasks and responsibilities.
* **Family Relationships**: A single-choice question asking about the presence of certain relationships. Please select "Yes" if relationships are present and "No" otherwise.
* **Family Counselor 1-20**: Text inputs for providing information about different types of family counselors. Please fill out each field with relevant details about the counselor's name, specialty, or contact information.
  - Example: "John Smith, Parenting Specialist" or "Jane Doe, Marriage Counselor"

Please note that this form is for general guidance purposes only. If you have specific questions or need further clarification, feel free to reach out to us.
