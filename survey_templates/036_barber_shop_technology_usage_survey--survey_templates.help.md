# Barber Shop Technology Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about the technology usage of barber shops. We aim to understand how shops use various tools and systems, their challenges and pain points, and their interest in adopting new technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, based on your shop's current situation.
2. For questions with options, select the one that best describes your shop's current state or preference.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to leave a note in the "Additional Comments" section.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How many chairs/barbers are in your shop?** (`shop_size`, select_one)
	+ This question asks about the size of your shop.
* **Shop Location Type** (`shop_location_type`, select_one)
	+ This question asks about the location of your shop.
* **What booking/scheduling software do you use?** (`booking_software`, select_one)
	+ This question asks about the software you use for booking and scheduling appointments.
* **What is your primary Point of Sale (POS) system?** (`pos_system`, select_one)
	+ This question asks about the system you use for processing transactions.
* **Digital Marketing Channels Used** (`marketing_tools`, select_multiple)
	+ This question asks about the digital channels you use to reach new clients.
* **What is your biggest daily business challenge?** (`primary_business_challenge`, select_one)
	+ This question asks about the biggest challenge your shop faces daily.
* **Overall satisfaction with your current technology** (`tech_satisfaction`, select_one)
	+ This question asks about your satisfaction with your current technology stack.
* **Level of interest in AI-driven tools** (`ai_interest`, select_one)
	+ This question asks about your interest in using AI-driven tools.
* **Importance of having a mobile app** (`mobile_app_interest`, select_one)
	+ This question asks about the importance of having a branded mobile app.
* **Monthly budget for business software/technology** (`tech_budget`, select_one)
	+ This question asks about your shop's budget for business software and technology.
* **Would you like to receive a copy of the survey results?** (`wish_to_receive_report`, select_one)
	+ This question asks about your preference for receiving the survey results.
* **Email Address for Results** (`contact_email`, email)
	+ This question asks for your email address to share the survey results.
* **Date of Completion** (`completion_date`, date)
	+ This question asks about the date you completed this survey.
* **What technology do you currently use?** (`current_tech_header`, note)
	+ This question is a note field for you to describe your current technology stack.
* **Primary Technology Challenges** (`challenges_header`, note)
	+ This question is a note field for you to describe your shop's biggest challenges and pain points.
* **Interest in New Technology** (`future_tech_header`, note)
	+ This question is a note field for you to describe your interest in adopting new technologies.
