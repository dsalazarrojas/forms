# Pet Service Innovation Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect your opinions on pet services and businesses. Your participation will help us create better services for pets.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions on each page of the form, providing as much detail as you can.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one or more options that apply to you.
3. For text questions, enter your responses in the text boxes provided.
4. If you have any additional comments or ideas, use the "Open Ended" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Businesses** (Text, optional): Enter the names of pet businesses you have interacted with or know of.
* **Current Services** (Select Multiple, optional): Choose the services you currently use or have used from the list.
* **Satisfaction** (Select One, optional): Indicate how satisfied you are with the current services.
* **New Services** (Text, optional): Enter any new services you think would be beneficial for pet owners.
* **New Services Ideas** (Text, optional): Describe the new services you think would be beneficial for pet owners.
* **Open Ended** (Text, optional): Share any additional comments or ideas you have.
* **Email** (Email, optional): Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions).
* **Phone Number** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions).
* **Time** (Time, optional): Enter the time you spent on this survey.
* **Date** (Date, optional): Enter the date you completed this survey.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" are not required to be completed, but your input will help us better understand your needs and preferences.
