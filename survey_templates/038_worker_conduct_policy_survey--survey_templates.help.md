# Worker Conduct Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the worker's awareness and understanding of the company's conduct policy. It's meant to be a brief and concise survey to gather feedback and insights on the policy's effectiveness and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into three sections, each representing a different aspect of the conduct policy.
2. Please answer all questions truthfully, and make sure to provide as much detail as possible.
3. For each question, you can enter your response in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Awareness** (text, required): This section is where you can demonstrate your understanding of the company's conduct policy. Please provide a brief description or example of what you think the policy means to you.
* **Communication** (text, required): In this section, please describe how you typically communicate with colleagues, supervisors, or customers regarding the conduct policy. Be specific about any challenges or successes you've had.
* **Training** (text, required): Here, you can discuss your experiences with the conduct policy training provided by the company. Share any positive or negative feedback, or suggestions for improvement.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions as truthfully as possible.
* Keep your responses concise and focused on the topic.
* If you need help or clarification on any question, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.

Note: The form is divided into three sections to keep the questions organized and focused. Answering the questions in each section should take around 5-10 minutes.
