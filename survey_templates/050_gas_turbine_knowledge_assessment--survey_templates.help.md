# Gas Turbine Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to assess the knowledge and familiarity of gas turbine concepts, including fundamentals, efficiency, design, maintenance, safety procedures, and troubleshooting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply read each field description carefully and answer the questions accordingly. The form is divided into two main sections. The first section is for selecting options and the second section is for text-based answers. Be sure to fill in the blanks with your honest assessment of your knowledge.
