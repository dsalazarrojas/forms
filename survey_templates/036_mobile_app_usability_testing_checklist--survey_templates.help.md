# Mobile App Usability Testing Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your subjective feedback on the usability of a mobile app. Please answer the questions based on your real or hypothetical user experience with the app.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, answer the questions as they relate to your experience with the mobile app. You can choose all that apply or provide a brief explanation for each question. If a question doesn't apply, please select "N/A" or "No" in the provided options. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This section is an overview of the app's main features and layout. Please describe your first impression of the app's home screen.
* **Home Screen**: Enter your observations about the app's home screen, including any notable features, buttons, or elements that caught your attention.
* **Navigation**: Choose the navigation system you used (e.g., Apple, Samsung, Google) to navigate the app.
* **Search Bar**: Describe your experience with the search bar, including how you used it and any issues you encountered.
* **Login**: Indicate which social media platforms you used to log in (e.g., Facebook, Google, Apple) or select "None."
* **Account Creation**: Share your observations about creating an account, including any required information, steps, or difficulties you faced.
* **Account Management**: Describe your experience with managing your account, including any notable features or issues.
* **Account Settings**: Enter any notable settings or options you found in the app related to account management.
* **Notifications**: Describe any notifications you received or observed during your interaction with the app.
* **Settings**: Enter any notable features or options you found in the app's settings menu.
* **Search Bar Results**: Describe the search results you observed, including any notable features or issues.
* **Search History**: Describe your experience with the search history, including any notable features or issues.
* **Search Bar Searching**: Enter any notable observations about searching within the search bar.
* **Search Bar Empty**: Describe your experience with an empty search bar, including any notable features or issues.
* **Search Bar Cancel**: Enter any notable observations about canceling a search within the search bar.
* **Search Bar Clear**: Describe your experience with clearing a search within the search bar.
* **Home Screen Header**: Describe your experience with the header on the home screen.
* **Home Screen Footer**: Enter any notable observations about the footer on the home screen.
* **Account Screen**: Describe your experience with the account screen, including any notable features or issues.
* **Account Screen Header**: Enter any notable observations about the header on the account screen.
* **Account Screen Footer**: Describe your experience with the footer on the account screen.
* **Account Screen Header Content**: Describe any notable content within the header on the account screen.
* **Account Screen Body**: Enter any notable observations about the main content on the account screen.
* **Account Screen Footer**: Describe your experience with the footer on the account screen.
* **Account Screen Header Content Bottom**: Enter any notable observations about the content at the bottom of the header on the account screen.
* **Account Screen Footer Bottom**: Describe your experience with the footer at the bottom of the account screen.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* Use examples or screenshots to support your answers if possible.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section.
* This form is designed to collect your subjective feedback; please answer honestly and objectively.
