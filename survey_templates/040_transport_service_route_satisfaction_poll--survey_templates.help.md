# Transport Service Route Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from transport service riders on their satisfaction with the routes they use. It is intended to help transport service providers improve their services and better meet the needs of their riders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the page titled "Rider Feedback". This page asks for general feedback on the transport service.
2. Select the page titled "Route Details". Choose one or more routes that you have recently used.
3. Select the page titled "Route Performance". Rate the performance of the routes you have selected. (Note: This field is currently not populated with options but is intended for future use.)
4. Select the page titled "Frequency of Use". Choose how frequently you use each route. (Note: This field is currently not populated with options but is intended for future use.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rider Feedback** (page_1, note, required: false): This is a space for riders to provide general feedback on their experience with the transport service. You can share your thoughts on how to improve the service.
* **Route Details** (page_2, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more routes that you have recently used. This will help us understand which routes you're familiar with and what you think of them.
* **Route Performance** (page_3, number, required: false): Currently, this field is not populated with options. However, it's intended for you to rate the performance of the routes you've selected.
* **Frequency of Use** (page_4, select_one, required: false): Choose how frequently you use each route. The options are:
	+ **Everyday**
	+ **Weekly**
	+ **Rarely**
	+ **Never**
	+ (Note: You can only select one option for each route, as you're asked to do so for each route you've selected in the "Route Details" page.)

## Tips
- Please answer questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Select all routes you have recently used, even if you're not satisfied with them.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Rider Feedback" space.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact the transport service provider.
