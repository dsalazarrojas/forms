# user_registration_and_timeline_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user registration and timeline information for demographic analysis and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select your programs of interest from the "Program" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your timeline details in the "Timeline" field.
5. If desired, provide additional information about your goals and progress.
6. Submit the form by entering the date and time you submitted the form in the "Submitted By", "Submitted On", and "Submitted At" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text): Enter your name as it appears on your registration form or identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, email): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Program** (`Program`, select_multiple): Select all the programs you are interested in (e.g., Coaching, Education, Program).
* **Timeline** (`Timeline`, date): Enter your timeline details, including start and end dates.
* **Goals** (`Goals`, text): Optional: provide additional information about your goals and objectives.
* **Progress** (`Progress`, text): Optional: share your progress or updates related to your goals.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text): Enter the name of the person submitting this form.
* **Submitted On** (`Submitted On`, time): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Submitted At** (`Submitted At`, date): Enter the date you submitted this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper tracking and analysis.
* Double-check your email address for correct formatting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
