# Beverage Machine Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with the beverage machine in your department. Please answer the questions honestly, and let us know what you think about the machine's performance, ease of use, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field (if you would like to be contacted for follow-up).
3. Select your department from the "Department" field.
4. Choose the office location where the machine is located in the "Office Location" field.
5. Indicate the type of machine you use in the "Machine Type" field.
6. Choose how often you use the machine in the "Usage Frequency" field.
7. Rate your overall satisfaction with the machine in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.
8. Rate the quality of beverages produced by the machine in the "Beverage Quality" field.
9. Rate the cleanliness of the machine in the "Machine Cleanliness" field.
10. Indicate how easy it is to operate the machine in the "Ease of Use" field.
11. Rate the machine's reliability in the "Machine Reliability" field.
12. Rate the variety of beverage options available in the "Beverage Variety Rating" field.
13. List any common issues you have encountered with the machine in the "Common Issues Encountered" field.
14. Rate how quickly maintenance issues are addressed in the "Maintenance Responsiveness" field (if applicable).
15. List your preferred beverages in the "Your Preferred Beverages" field (if applicable).
16. Provide suggestions for improvement in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.
17. List any additional beverages you would like to see added in the "Additional Beverages Desired" field (if applicable).
18. Provide any additional comments about the machine in the "Additional Comments" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: This is the field where you should enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address**: If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your email address here. This is not a required field.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list to help us understand which group you belong to.
* **Office Location**: Choose the office or floor where the machine is located to help us track usage and maintenance patterns.
* **Machine Type**: Select the type of machine you use most frequently (e.g., Coffee Machine, Espresso Machine, etc.).
* **Usage Frequency**: Choose how often you use the machine, which will help us understand your usage patterns.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the machine on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Beverage Quality**: Rate the quality of beverages produced by the machine on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Machine Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of the machine on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Ease of Use**: Indicate how easy or difficult it is to operate the machine on a scale from Very Easy to Very Difficult.
* **Machine Reliability**: Rate the machine's reliability on a scale from Very Reliable to Very Unreliable.
* **Beverage Variety Rating**: Rate the variety of beverages available from the machine on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Common Issues Encountered**: Select any common issues you experience with the machine, such as Machine Out of Order, Runs Out of Supplies, etc.
* **Maintenance Responsiveness**: Rate how quickly maintenance issues are addressed on a scale from Very Responsive to Very Slow (if applicable).
* **Your Preferred Beverages**: Select your preferred beverages from the list to help us understand your preferences (if applicable).
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions for improvement, such as changes to the machine's performance, features, or functionality.
* **Additional Beverages Desired**: Suggest any additional beverages you would like to see added to the machine (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the machine, such as suggestions for improvement or concerns about maintenance.
