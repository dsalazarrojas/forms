# Seasonal Inventory Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers on their experience with seasonal products.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section of the form carefully and answer the questions as they appear.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or skip it if you are unable to provide an answer.
3. Use the text fields to provide as much detail as possible, and select the appropriate options for the questions that require them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Survey**: This section is designed to gather general information about your experience with our seasonal products.
* **Seasonal Product Info**: This section provides details about our seasonal products, such as their features and characteristics.
* **Customer Satisfaction**: This question asks about your level of satisfaction with our seasonal products.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You are very dissatisfied with the product.
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied: You are somewhat dissatisfied with the product.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about the product.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with the product.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are very satisfied with the product.
* **Product Recommendations**: This question asks if you recommend our seasonal products.
	+ Recommended: Yes, you recommend the product.
	+ Not Recommended: No, you do not recommend the product.
	+ Somewhat Recommended: You somewhat recommend the product.
* **Seasonal Item List**: This field is for you to list any seasonal items that you have purchased or are interested in.
* **Customer Insight Comments**: This field is for you to provide any additional comments or insights about your experience with our seasonal products.
* **Seasonal Insights**: This field is for you to provide any seasonal insights or observations.
* **Customer Suggestions**: This field is for you to provide any suggestions for our seasonal products or services.
* **Inventory Adjustments**: This field is for you to provide any adjustments that you would make to our seasonal inventory.
* **Seasonal Item Adjustments**: This field is for you to provide any adjustments that you would make to our seasonal products.
* **Customer Insights 1-5**: These fields are for you to provide any additional insights or comments about your experience with our seasonal products.
* **Seasonal Product Info 1-9**: These fields are for you to provide any additional information about our seasonal products.
