# Volunteer Course Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from volunteers about their course preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the courses you are interested in taking from the "What are your preferred courses" section.
2. Choose how often you would like to take courses from the "How often would you like to take the courses" section.
3. Enter your email address in the "Contact info" section.
4. Select the location where you would like to take the courses from the "Where would you like to take the course" section.
5. Rate the course from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest, in the "How would you rate the course" section.
6. If you have any other comments or feedback, you can provide them in the "Do you have any other comments?" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What are your preferred courses
* **What are your preferred courses** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the courses you are interested in taking.
### 2. How often would you like to take courses?
* **How often would you like to take courses** (`select_one`, `required`): Choose how often you would like to take courses.
### 3. Contact info
* **Contact info** (`email`, `required`): Enter your email address.
### 4. Where would you like to take the course?
* **Where would you like to take the course** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the location where you would like to take the courses.
### 5. How would you rate the course?
* **How would you rate the course** (`select_one`, `required`): Rate the course from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
### 6. Do you have any other comments?
* **Do you have any other comments?** (`note`, `not required`): If you have any other feedback or comments, you can enter them here.
