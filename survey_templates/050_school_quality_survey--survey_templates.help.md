# School Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from schools about their quality, location, and other details. This data will be used to evaluate and improve school standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct school.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
3. Use the provided options for School Size and School Level.
4. Keep the School Comments field for any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Address**: Enter the physical address of the school.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of the school. This field is required.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of the school.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher filling out this form.
* **Teacher Title**: Enter the title or position of the teacher filling out this form.
* **School Quality**: Rate the school quality on a scale of 1-10.
* **School Location**: Enter the location of the school.
* **School Size**: Select the approximate number of students at the school. Options are:
	+ Option 1: Small (less than 100 students)
	+ Option 2: Medium (100-500 students)
	+ Option 3: Large (more than 500 students)
* **School Level**: Select all that apply:
	+ Option 1: Primary
	+ Option 2: Secondary
	+ Option 3: Tertiary
* **School Comments**: Leave any additional comments or feedback about the school.

## Tips
* Be accurate and truthful when filling out this form.
* Use the correct school's name and information.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or the relevant department for assistance.
