# Agent Multiple Choice Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Agent Multiple Choice Quiz, designed to test your knowledge of real estate concepts and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in the required fields: **Agent Name** and **Email Address**.
2. Select the best answer for each question from the provided options.
3. Once you've completed all questions, click submit to receive your results via email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your real estate license.
* **Email Address** (`agent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive your quiz results.
* **-- Quiz Questions --** (`quiz_header`, note, required): This heading separates the questions from the introduction.
* **What is the primary purpose of a Comparative Market Analysis - CMA?** (`q1_cma`, select_one, required): Choose the correct answer from:
	+ To determine the exact tax value
	+ To estimate a property's market value
	+ To conduct a structural inspection
	+ To verify legal ownership
* **Which document is legally required to disclose property defects?** (`q2_disclosure`, select_one, required): Choose the correct answer from:
	+ Listing Agreement
	+ Seller's Property Disclosure Statement
	+ Purchase and Sale Agreement
	+ Title Insurance Policy
* **What does the term Escrow refer to in a real estate transaction?** (`q3_escrow`, select_one, required): Choose the correct answer from:
	+ A type of building material
	+ A neutral third party holding funds
	+ The process of landscaping
	+ A legal eviction notice
* **In most regions, who typically pays the real estate commission?** (`q4_commission`, select_one, required): Choose the correct answer from:
	+ The Buyer
	+ The Seller
	+ The Lender
	+ The Local Government
* **What is the standard duration of an Exclusive Right to Sell listing agreement?** (`q5_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the correct answer from:
	+ 30 days
	+ 3 to 6 months
	+ 1 year
	+ Indefinite
* **Which of the following is NOT a protected class under the Fair Housing Act?** (`q6_fair_housing`, select_one, required): Choose the correct answer from:
	+ Race
	+ Religion
	+ Political Affiliation
	+ Familial Status
* **What is a Lien on a property?** (`q7_lien`, select_one, required): Choose the correct answer from:
	+ A decorative fence
	+ A legal claim or encumbrance for debt
	+ A type of property tax
	+ A survey boundary marker
* **Thank you for completing the quiz. Your results will be emailed to you** (`quiz_footer`, note, required): This final message will be displayed after you've completed the quiz.
