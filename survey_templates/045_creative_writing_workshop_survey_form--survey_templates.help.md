# creative_writing_workshop_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation from attendees of the creative writing workshop. Your responses will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved in future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted.
2. Provide as much detail as you can in the text fields.
3. Select one option for the select fields.
4. Submit your form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome Message** (`welcome_message`, `text`, not required): Please feel free to write a brief message introducing yourself.
* **What was your experience at the workshop?** (`workshop_experience`, `text`, not required): Share your thoughts about your time at the workshop. This will help us understand how we can improve the experience for future attendees.
* **What did you find most helpful about the workshop content?** (`workshop_content`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options provided:
	+ Workshop exercises
	+ Workshop materials
	+ Presentations
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Do you have any suggestions or recommendations for future workshops?** (`suggestions`, `text`, not required): Share any ideas you have for how we can improve the workshop.
* **How would you rate your overall experience at the workshop?** (`rating`, `number`, not required): Choose a number between 1 and 5 to rate your experience.
* **Date of Workshop** (`date_of_workshop`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you attended the workshop.
* **Time of Workshop** (`time_of_workshop`, `time`, not required): Enter the time you attended the workshop.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `email`, not required): If you'd like to receive follow-up messages, please enter your email address.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional thoughts or comments, please share them here.
* **Would you like to receive follow up messages about future workshops?** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options provided:
	+ True
	+ False
