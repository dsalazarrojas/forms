# Floor Film Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather end-users' opinions about films on the floor. The survey aims to understand how often films are screened, the type of films being shown, the preferences of the audience, and their level of satisfaction with the current film offerings. This information will help facility managers to better understand their audience's needs and preferences, and make informed decisions about film programming and content.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of the survey. Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the purpose and scope of the survey. Read it carefully and make sure you understand what the survey is about.
2. Select your answer for **Film Type**. Choose one of the two options provided: "Film" or "Digital".
3. Indicate how often you **Screen Films**:
	* Often
	* Occasionally
	* Rarely
4. Describe your **Usage Patterns** in the text box provided. This will help us understand how you typically use the film screening facilities.
5. Provide your **Satisfaction** level with the current film offerings on a scale from 1 to 10.
6. For **Recommendations**, choose one or more options from the list provided:
	* Provide
	* Improve
	* Improve and Provide
7. Enter your **Contact Information** in the email field. This will allow us to get back to you with any questions or concerns.
8. (Optional) If you are a facility manager or have any additional comments, please use the **Facility Manager** and **Facility Location/Name/Notes** text boxes. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, not required): This is a brief overview of the purpose and scope of the survey.
* **Film Type** (`Film Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the two options provided: "Film" or "Digital". This will help us understand the type of content you typically screen.
* **Frequency of Screening** (`Frequency of Screening`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate how often you screen films:
	+ Often
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
* **Usage Patterns** (`Usage Patterns`, `text`, required): Describe your typical usage patterns in the text box provided.
* **Preferences** (`Preferences`, `note`, not required): This field is for additional comments or preferences that you would like to share with us.
* **Satisfaction** (`Satisfaction`, `number`, required): Provide your level of satisfaction with the current film offerings on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Recommendations** (`Recommendations`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more options from the list provided:
	+ Provide
	+ Improve
	+ Improve and Provide
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `email`, required): Enter your contact information so we can get back to you with any questions or concerns.
* **Facility Manager** (`Facility Manager`, `text`, not required): If you are a facility manager, please use this field to provide your name and contact information.
* **Facility Location** (`Facility Location`, `text`, not required): Enter the location of your facility.
* **Facility Name** (`Facility Name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of your facility.
* **Facility Notes** (`Facility Notes`, `note`, not required): Use this field for any additional comments or notes about your facility.

## Tips

- Please take your time to answer these questions honestly and thoroughly. Your feedback is valuable to us!
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us through the contact information provided.
- We appreciate your participation and look forward to hearing about your experiences with our film screening facilities!
