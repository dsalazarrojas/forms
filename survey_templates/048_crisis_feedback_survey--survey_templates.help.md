# Crisis Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Crisis Feedback Survey is a tool designed to gather information about your experience with a crisis situation. This survey will help us understand what happened, how you felt, and what we can do to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Each field is described in more detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **organization_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the organization you interacted with during the crisis situation. If you're providing feedback about an external organization, please include its full name.
* **name** (Text, Required): Enter your name so we can address you directly in our response.
* **feedback** (Text, Optional): Describe your experience with the organization's response to the crisis situation. Be as specific as possible with your account of what happened.
* **response_effort** (Number, Optional): Rate how much effort the organization put into responding to the crisis situation, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not much" and 5 is "a lot".
* **rating** (Number, Optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the organization's response, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **suggestions** (Text, Optional): Suggest any improvements or changes that you believe the organization could make to better handle future crisis situations.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
