# Transport Service Release Industry Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from partners about their experience with our transport service. We will use this feedback to improve and refine our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select your partner from the options in the "partner" field.
2. Choose your partner type from the "partner type" field.
3. Provide any comments or feedback in the "feedback" field.
4. Enter the date of release of your transport service.
5. Select a rating for the service you received.
6. Enter any comments or suggestions you may have.
7. Enter the date and time of review.
8. Enter the date and time of release.
9. Enter the review status.
10. Enter any comments about your experience.
11. Enter the date and time of feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Partner** (`partner`, select_one, required): Select the partner you are providing feedback about.
  * Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3

  - **Partner Type** (`partner_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of partner you are providing feedback about.
    * Options: value1, value2, value3

  - **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about your experience with our transport service.

  - **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of release of your transport service.

  - **Release Time** (`release_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of release of your transport service.

  - **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Select a rating for the service you received.
    * Options: value1, value2, value3

  - **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Enter any comments or suggestions you may have.

  - **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

  - **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

  - **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, optional): Select a rating scale.
    * Options: value1, value2, value3

  - **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select your overall satisfaction with the service.
    * Options: value1, value2, value3

  - **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, select_one, optional): Select any suggestions you may have.
    * Options: value1, value2, value3

  - **Internal Review** (`internal_review`, time, optional): Enter the internal review date and time.

  - **External Review** (`external_review`, time, optional): Enter the external review date and time.

  - **Internal Review Scale** (`internal_review_scale`, select_one, optional): Select the internal review scale.
    * Options: value1, value2, value3

  - **External Review Scale** (`external_review_scale`, select_one, optional): Select the external review scale.
    * Options: value1, value2, value3

  - **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, optional): Select the review status.
    * Options: value1, value2, value3

  - **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the review date.

  - **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, optional): Enter the review time.

  - **Release Date Time** (`release_date_time`, date, optional): Enter the release date and time of your transport service.

  - **Release Time Time** (`release_time_time`, time, optional): Enter the release time of your transport service.
