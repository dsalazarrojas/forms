# youth_crime_rehabilitation_attitudes_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect community perspectives and opinions on rehabilitation programs for youth. It aims to gather information on various aspects of crime rehabilitation, including community involvement, crime prevention, and survey participation. This form is not required, but providing your input will help us better understand community attitudes towards youth crime rehabilitation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately, providing your honest opinions and experiences.
2.  Select the relevant options for each field based on your understanding and knowledge.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or seek help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Community Perspectives**: Please share your views and opinions on the importance of community involvement in rehabilitation programs for youth. You can type your thoughts and feelings about the topic.
*   **Rehabilitation Programs**: Share your insights on the effectiveness and usefulness of rehabilitation programs for youth.
*   **Community Involvement**: Explain how you think community involvement can benefit or hinder youth crime rehabilitation.
*   **Crime Prevention**: Discuss your strategies or methods for preventing crime among youth.
*   **Survey Participation**: Let us know about your experiences or thoughts on participating in surveys like this one.
*   **Youth Crime Rehabilitation Attitudes Survey Important**: Please rate the importance of this survey to you.
*   **Youth Crime Rehabilitation Attitudes Survey Recommended**: Share your views on whether this survey is recommended for others.
*   **Additional Comments**: If you have any other thoughts, suggestions, or ideas related to the topic, please include them here.
*   **Email**: Provide your email address for contact purposes only.
*   **Phone**: Please provide your contact phone number (optional).
*   **Date**: Select the current date.
*   **Time**: Choose the current time.
*   **Note**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes.
*   **Select One**: Choose one option from the list.
*   **Select Multiple**: Choose multiple options from the list.
*   **Select All**: Select all options from the list.
*   **Select All Except**: Select all options except the one you don't want.
*   **Select None**: Do not select any option.
*   **Select None Multiple**: Do not select any option.
*   **Required**: Select one option that is required for you.
*   **Required Multiple**: Select multiple options that are required for you.
*   **Optional**: Select one option that is optional for you.
*   **Optional Multiple**: Select multiple options that are optional for you.
*   **Mandatory**: Select one option that is mandatory for you.
*   **Yes/No**: Select 'Yes' if you agree or 'No' if you disagree.
