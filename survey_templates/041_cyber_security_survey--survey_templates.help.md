# cyber_security_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the cyber security survey form with ease, ensuring you provide accurate and comprehensive information about your company's cyber security posture.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields carefully, as each one is crucial in providing a detailed picture of your company's security posture.
2. Use the provided labels and options as a guide to ensure accurate completion.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### what_is_a_cyber_security_survey
* **what_is_a_cyber_security_survey** (`text`, required/optional): This field is a free-text input where you can briefly describe the purpose and scope of the cyber security survey.

### company_size
* **company_size** (`select_one`, required): Select the size of your company from the provided options: small, medium, large.

### how_many_employees
* **how_many_employees** (`number`, required): Enter the total number of employees in your company.

### what_type_of_employees
* **what_type_of_employees** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the provided options: sales, marketing, engineering, support.

### employee_level
* **employee_level** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the provided options: junior, senior, manager, director.

### security_concerns
* **security_concerns** (`text`, required): Enter any security concerns or concerns you have about your company's cyber security posture.

### current_security_mechanism
* **current_security_mechanism** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the provided options: password, two factor, smartcard, other.

### current_security_measures
* **current_security_measures** (`text`, required): Enter any current security measures your company is taking.

### future_security_plans
* **future_security_plans** (`text`, required): Describe any future security plans or measures your company plans to take.

### employee_security_concerns
* **employee_security_concerns** (`text`, required): Enter any concerns your employees have about cyber security.

### company_security_policy
* **company_security_policy** (`note`, required): This is a note field where you can enter any additional information or comments about your company's security policy.

### security_training_program
* **security_training_program** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the provided options: phishing, password, security basics, none.

### security_incident_response
* **security_incident_response** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the provided options: incident response, incident respond, none.

### company_security_measures
* **company_security_measures** (`text`, required): Enter any current or future security measures your company is taking.

### security_concerns
* **security_concerns** (`text`, required): Enter any additional security concerns or concerns you have.

### additional_comments
* **additional_comments** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your company's security posture.

### email
* **email** (`email`, required): Enter your company's email address.

### phone
* **phone** (`text`, required): Enter your company's phone number.

### company
* **company** (`text`, required): Enter your company's name.

### job_title
* **job_title** (`text`, required): Enter your job title.

### department
* **department** (`text`, required): Enter the department you belong to.

### country
* **country** (`text`, required): Enter your country or region.

### time_zone
* **time_zone** (`time`, required): Enter your time zone.

### date
* **date** (`date`, required): Enter the current date.

### submit
* **submit** (`select_multiple`, required): Select how you want to submit the form: submit, Other.

Please fill out this form carefully, and if you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
