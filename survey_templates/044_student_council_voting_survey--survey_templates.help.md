# Student Council Voting Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Council Voting Survey is a form designed to gather information from students regarding their voting preferences and opinions on specific candidates. The survey aims to understand students' views on various candidates and their reasons for supporting or not supporting them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the survey, please follow these steps:

1. **Select your grade level**: Choose your current grade level from the options provided.
2. **Identify your student status**: Indicate if you are currently active or inactive as a student.
3. **Choose your voting preferences**: Select all the candidates you would like to vote for.
4. **State your support**: Indicate if you support or do not support the selected candidate(s).
5. **Explain your reasoning**: Provide a brief explanation for why you support or do not support the candidate(s).
6. **State your opinion on the other candidate**: Indicate if you support or do not support the other candidate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your grade level?** (High, Low, optional)
This field asks for your current grade level to help the survey understand your demographic.
* **What is your student status?** (Active, Inactive, optional)
This field helps the survey to identify whether you are currently active or inactive as a student.
* **Which candidate would you like to vote for?** (Select multiple options, optional)
Choose all the candidates you would like to vote for. This field allows you to select multiple candidates.
* **Do you support this candidate?** (Yes, No, optional)
Indicate if you support or do not support the selected candidate.
* **Do you support this other candidate?** (Yes, No, optional)
Indicate if you support or do not support the other candidate.
* **Why do you support this candidate?** (Text, optional)
Provide a brief explanation for why you support the candidate.
* **What is the reason you do not support this candidate?** (Text, optional)
Provide a brief explanation for why you do not support the candidate.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can select multiple options for the "Which candidate would you like to vote for?" field.
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise explanation for your answers in the "Why do you support this candidate?" and "What is the reason you do not support this candidate?" fields.
