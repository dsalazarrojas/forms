# Deadlock Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Deadlock Understanding Survey" is designed to collect information about your understanding of the concept of deadlock. This form will guide you through a series of questions to help us understand your perspective on deadlock.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Please select the correct category that best describes you (Student or Professional). If none of the options apply, choose "Other".
2. In the description field, briefly describe your understanding or experience with deadlock.
3. Indicate your role in relation to deadlock (Student, Instructor, or Other).
4. Provide your level of experience with deadlock, choosing from the options provided.
5. Select the level of education you have related to deadlock.
6. Choose the type of training or experience you have had with deadlock.
7. If applicable, provide any additional knowledge or insights you have about deadlock.
8. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
