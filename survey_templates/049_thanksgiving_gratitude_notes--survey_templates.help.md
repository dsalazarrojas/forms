# Thanksgiving Gratitude Notes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Gratitude Notes form is a way to express your gratitude for something or someone. It's a simple form that lets you share your thoughts and feelings with others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "What's on your mind" field and write a brief message expressing your gratitude.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field so that we can address you properly.
3. If you have a message, enter it in the "Message" field.
4. If you have a email address, enter it in the "Email" field.
5. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
6. Select how you heard about the form from the "How did you hear about the gratitude notes form" field.
7. Select who you are grateful to from the "Who are you grateful to" field.
8. If you want to schedule the form submission, you can enter a date and time in the "Date" and "Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What's on your mind** (`thanksgiving_gratitude_notes`, note, required): This is the main text field where you can write your message of gratitude.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): This field is for your name, so we can address you properly.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): If you have a specific message, enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have an email address, enter it so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you.
* **How did you hear about the gratitude notes form** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select how you heard about the form. Choose from:
	+ Social media
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Email
	+ Other
* **Who are you grateful to** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose who you are grateful to from:
	+ Family
	+ Friends
	+ Neighbors
	+ Colleagues
	+ Strangers
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you want to schedule the form submission, enter a date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you want to schedule the form submission, enter a time.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your form.
