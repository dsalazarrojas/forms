# Book Weekend Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a book-related event, specifically to gauge their weekend participation and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
- Make sure to select all applicable answers.
- If unsure about a particular question, you can provide more detailed explanations in the optional fields.
- Use the date field to record the date you complete this survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for updates and future event information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for event coordination.
* **Which Weekend Did You Attend** (`weekend_attended`, select_one, required): Select the weekend you attended the event.
* **Which Days Did You Attend** (`days_attended`, select_multiple, required): Select all the days you attended the event.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience with the event.
* **Event Organization Rating** (`event_organization`, select_one, required): Rate the organization and execution of the event.
* **Venue Rating** (`venue_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the event venue.
* **Sessions You Attended** (`sessions_attended`, select_multiple, required): Select all the sessions you attended during the event.
* **Favorite Session** (`favorite_session`, text, optional): Specify the session you enjoyed most.
* **Least Favorite Session** (`least_favorite_session`, text, optional): Describe the session you thought could be improved.
* **Speaker Quality Rating** (`speaker_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the speakers and their presentations.
* **Vendor Selection Rating** (`vendor_selection`, select_one, optional): Rate the vendors and their offerings.
* **Was It Worth Your Time** (`value_for_time`, select_one, required): Determine if the event was worthwhile for you.
* **Was It Worth the Cost** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate the value of the event compared to the cost.
* **How Many Books Did You Purchase** (`books_purchased`, select_one, optional): Estimate the number of books you bought during the event.
* **Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Determine if you would recommend this event to others.
* **Will You Attend Next Year** (`attend_next_year`, select_one, required): State your plans to attend the event next year.
* **What Could Be Improved** (`improvements_suggested`, text, optional): Suggest areas for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other feedback or comments about the event.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Describe how you heard about the event.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Record the date you complete this survey.

Note: This guide assumes that the form will be filled out on the same day or shortly after the event. If this is not the case, please adjust the survey date accordingly.
