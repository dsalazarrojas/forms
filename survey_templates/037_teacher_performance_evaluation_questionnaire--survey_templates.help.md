# Teacher Performance Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Teacher Performance Evaluation Questionnaire" is a form designed to collect feedback from students, parents, and other stakeholders on a teacher's performance. This form helps to assess a teacher's strengths and weaknesses, providing valuable insights for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each section based on your experience with the teacher.
2. Provide specific examples or comments for fields like "Student Feedback" and "Teacher Comments" to make your evaluation more detailed and helpful.
3. Be honest and objective in your evaluation, considering multiple perspectives when answering.
4. Make sure to save your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, note, optional): This section is for students to express their thoughts, opinions, or concerns about the teacher's performance. Please provide a brief, concrete response that reflects your experience with the teacher.
* **Teaching Style** (`teaching_style`, select_one, optional): This question asks whether the teacher's teaching style is effective or not. Choose "Yes" if you believe the teacher's teaching style is effective in engaging and educating you, or "No" otherwise.
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, select_one, optional): This question is about the teacher's ability to manage the subject area they're teaching. Choose "Yes" if you feel the teacher is knowledgeable and effective in their subject area, or "No" otherwise.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, select_multiple, optional): This section allows you to comment on the teacher's ability to manage student attendance. Choose from the options:
	+ "Yes" - You have good attendance and engagement from students.
	+ "No" - You have low attendance and engagement from students.
* **Parent Feedback** (`parent_feedback`, select_multiple, optional): This question is for parents to provide feedback on the teacher's performance. If you're a parent, please choose from the options that best reflect your experience and concerns. If you're not a parent, just skip this section.
* **Teaching Materials** (`teaching_materials`, select_multiple, optional): This section allows you to comment on the availability and effectiveness of the teaching materials provided by the teacher. Choose from the options:
	+ "Yes" - The teaching materials are sufficient and helpful.
	+ "No" - The teaching materials are insufficient or not effective.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, note, optional): This is a space for you to provide more detailed comments or suggestions for the teacher's improvement.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date when you evaluated the teacher's performance.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Please enter the time of day when you completed the evaluation.

## Tips

* Be specific and honest in your evaluation, avoiding vague or generic comments.
* Focus on the teacher's performance rather than personal opinions or biases.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you've provided accurate and helpful feedback.
* Remember that this form is for constructive growth and development, not criticism or complaint.
