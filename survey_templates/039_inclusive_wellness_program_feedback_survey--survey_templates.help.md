# inclusive_wellness_program_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience in the wellness program. The information collected will help us understand the program's strengths and weaknesses and provide valuable insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name and Email address.
2. Answer the program-specific questions regarding your experience.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the program (1-5).
4. Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
5. Choose how you would like to receive future communications from us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address in the format "[name]@[email.com](http://email.com)" (e.g., "[john.doe]@[example.com](http://example.com).
* **Program Name** (Program Name, text, optional): If you are providing feedback on a specific program, please enter its name.
* **Feedback Date** (Feedback Date, date, required): The date you are providing feedback for.
* **Time of Day** (Time of Day, time, optional): The time of day when you took the program.
* **Select Program Area** (Select Program Area, select_one, required): Choose the area of the program you were participating in (Wellness, Mindfulness, Self-Care, Nutrition, Other).
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Any additional thoughts or comments you may have about the program.
* **Rating (1-5)** (Rating (1-5), number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Suggest Improvements** (Suggest Improvements, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for future improvements to the program.
* **Would You Like To Receive Follow-Up Materials or Communications** (Would You Like To Receive Follow-Up Materials or Communications, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to receive future communications from us (True, False, or Unsure).
* **How Would You Prefer to Receive Contact** (How Would You Prefer to Receive Contact, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to receive contact (Email, Phone, In Person).
