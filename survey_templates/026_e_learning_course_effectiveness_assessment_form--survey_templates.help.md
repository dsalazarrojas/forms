# E Learning Course Effectiveness Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the effectiveness of E learning courses. The primary goal is to gather feedback and opinions from course participants to evaluate the course's quality, content, and overall value. This form will help us understand what works well and what areas need improvement, enabling us to refine and enhance the course experience for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Answer each question carefully and honestly**. This form is a survey, and your feedback is valuable to us.
2.  **Fill in the required fields (if any)**. These are marked as "Required" in the form.
3.  **Submit your answers** when you have completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **E learning course**: This field is a text input for you to describe the E learning course you have taken. Please provide a brief summary of the course content and your overall experience.
* **Category**: This is a select multiple field where you can choose one or more categories that apply to your experience with the E learning course. You can select 'Yes' if the course content was helpful, or 'No' if not. You can also select 'Yes' for other applicable categories.
* **Course id**: This is a numerical field to input your course ID, if any. If you don't have a course ID, you can leave this blank.
* **Rating**: This is a select one field where you rate the E learning course based on your experience. You can select from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest rating.
* **Comments**: This is a text area for you to add any additional comments or feedback about the E learning course. You can write as much or as little as you like.
* **Email**: This field is for your contact email address, which we will use to get back to you if needed. Please ensure this is entered correctly for us to get in touch with you efficiently.

## Tips

*   **Be as concise as possible** while providing detailed and honest feedback. This will help us understand your experience better.
*   **Use specific examples** to illustrate your points when providing comments or feedback.
*   **Check your answers carefully** before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
