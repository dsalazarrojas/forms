# Shift Handoff Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your shift handoff preferences, including team size, handoff frequency, type, and communication channels. Your responses will help managers and team leads understand your individual preferences and improve shift handoff efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. For each question, select the option that best represents your preference.
2. Answer the questions in the order they appear.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your team lead or manager for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Size** (`team_size`, select_one): Indicate whether you prefer handoffs with small, medium, or large teams.
* **Handoff Frequency** (`handoff_frequency`, select_one): Tell us how often you prefer handoffs, such as daily, weekly, or rarely.
* **Handoff Type** (`handoff_type`, select_one): Choose the type of handoff that suits you best, such as in-person, video conference, or email.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_one): Select the communication channels you prefer for handoffs, such as email, phone, or messaging apps.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple): Check all the areas where you would like to see improvements during handoffs, such as communication, clarity, or documentation.
* **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, select_one): Indicate how often you communicate with your team during handoffs, such as daily, weekly, or rarely.
* **Communication Methods** (`communication_methods`, select_multiple): Select all the methods you prefer for communication during handoffs, such as email, phone, or video conference.
* **Improvement Priorities** (`improvement_priorities`, select_multiple): Check all the aspects of handoffs that you would like to see improved, such as communication, clarity, or timing.
* **Communication Channels Preferred** (`communication_channels_preferred`, select_multiple): Select all the communication channels you prefer to use for handoffs, such as email, phone, or messaging apps.
* **Handoff Time** (`handoff_time`, date): Enter the time of your preferred handoff.
* **Handoff Date** (`handoff_date`, date): Enter the date of your preferred handoff.
* **Shift Time** (`shift_time`, time): Enter the time of your shift.
* **Shift Date** (`shift_date`, date): Enter the date of your shift.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email): Enter your manager's email.

Tips:
* Be honest and accurate in your responses, as this will help us better understand your preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your team lead or manager for assistance.
