# Mobile App Customer Experience Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback about their mobile app experience. The information gathered will help us understand how well our app is performing and where we need to improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Customer Name.
2. Enter your Contact Number.
3. Provide your Email Address.
4. In the Feedback section, describe your experience with the app.
5. In the Suggestion section, provide any suggestions you may have for improvement.
6. Indicate if you have encountered any issues while using the app.
7. Rate your overall experience with the app.
8. Choose how often you use the app.
9. Select which features you use frequently.
10. Check how often you check notifications.
11. Enter the date and time you last checked notifications.
12. Check how often you log in to the app.
13. Enter the date and time you last logged in.
14. Select how often you log out.
15. Enter the date and time you last logged out.
16. Rate the performance of the app.
17. Select which features of the app perform well.
18. Choose how often you use app features.
19. Select which features you last used.
20. Check how often you use the app's features.
21. Select which app features perform well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to identify yourself in the survey.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Share your thoughts about your experience with the app.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, `text`, required): If you have any ideas for improvement, share them here.
* **Issue Encountered** (`issue_encountered`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all the issues you have encountered while using the app.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience with the app (1-5).
* **How Often Do You Use App** (`how_often_use_app`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you use the app (Yes/No).
* **Frequency of Use** (`frequency_of_use`, `select_multiple`, required): Select which features you use frequently.
* **Last Used App Features** (`last_used_app_features`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose which features you last used.
* **How Often Check Notifications** (`how_often_check_notificaitons`, `select_one`, required): Check how often you check notifications (Yes/No).
* **Last Checked Notification** (`last_checked_notificaiton`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time you last checked notifications.
* **Last Checked Notification Time** (`last_checked_notificaitons_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you last checked notifications.
* **Last Checked Notification Date** (`last_checked_notificaiton_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you last checked notifications.
* **Last Logged In** (`last_logged_in`, `select_one`, required): Check if you have logged in recently (Yes/No).
* **Last Logged In Date** (`last_logged_in_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time you last logged in.
* **How Often Log Out** (`how_often_log_out`, `select_one`, required): Check how often you log out (Yes/No).
* **Last Logged Out** (`last_logged_out`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time you last logged out.
* **App Rating** (`app_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the overall performance of the app (Good/Fair).
* **App Features** (`app_features`, `select_multiple`, required): Select which features of the app perform well.
* **How Often Use App in Features** (`how_often_use_app_in_features`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you use app features (Yes/No).
* **Last Used in Features** (`last_used_in_features`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose which features you last used.
* **App Performance** (`app_performance`, `select_multiple`, required): Select which features of the app perform well.
* **App Performance Features** (`app_performance_features`, `select_multiple`, required): Select which features of the app perform well.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the app.
