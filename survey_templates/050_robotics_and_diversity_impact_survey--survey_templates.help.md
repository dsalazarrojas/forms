# robotics_and_diversity_impact_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of the "robotics_and_diversity_impact_survey" form is to collect demographic information and gather opinions on the impact of robotics on diversity and inclusion in a specific context, likely in an academic or professional setting. This form is designed to help gather data on how robotics and diversity intersect, and how the implementation and use of robotics can affect different demographics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and select the correct answers for each question.
2. For each field, select the option that best describes your demographic information or opinion.
3. If you have a response that does not match any of the provided options, you can select "Other" in the corresponding field and provide a brief description in the adjacent field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Demographics** (`demographics`, text, optional)
This field is a free-text response where you can provide any additional demographic information not listed in the other fields.

### **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options to describe your gender:
* Male
* Female
* Other

### **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options to describe your ethnicity:
* White
* Black or African American
* Hispanic or Latino
* Asian
* Other

### **Age** (`age`, number, optional)
Enter your age.

### **Highest Education** (`highest_edu`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options to describe your highest level of education:
* Less than High School
* High School
* Some College
* Bachelor's Degree
* Master's Degree
* Doctoral Degree

### **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options to describe your work experience:
* 1-5 years
* 5-10 years
* 10-20 years
* More than 20 years

### **Diversity Focus Area** (`diversity_focus_area`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all that apply to describe the areas where diversity impact is relevant:
* Hiring and recruitment
* Training and development
* Inclusivity in robotics
* Other

If you select "Other", you can provide a brief description in the adjacent field.

### **Diversity Focus Area Other** (`diversity_focus_area_other`, text, optional)
If you selected "Other" in the previous field, provide a brief description of your other diversity focus area.

### **Robotics Participation** (`robotics_participation`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all that apply to describe how you participate in robotics:
* High School
* College/University
* Community
* Other
* None
