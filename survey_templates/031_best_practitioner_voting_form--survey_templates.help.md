# Best Practitioner Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Best Practitioner Voting Form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from individuals who have observed or worked with a practitioner in their field. The form is used to evaluate a practitioner's performance in various categories, providing a comprehensive assessment of their skills and attributes. The collected data will be used to determine the top practitioners in their field, which will be considered for recognition and awards.

This form is intended for those who have had direct experience with the practitioner being evaluated. It is not meant for self-nomination or self-assessment. Please only fill out this form if you have had a direct experience with the practitioner.

**Note:** If you are a practitioner being evaluated and would like to self-nominate, please use the [Practitioner Nomination Form] (link to be added) instead.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with your honest and unbiased opinion of the practitioner you are evaluating.
2.  Ensure you have filled out all required fields.
3.  If you have had an extensive relationship with the practitioner, you can describe the duration of your relationship in the "Relationship Duration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Full Name** (`voter_full_name`, text, required): Please provide your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Your Email Address** (`voter_email_addr`, email, required): A valid email address is required for voting verification purposes.
*   **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name_text`, text, required): Identify the name of the practitioner you are evaluating.
*   **Practical Specialty** (`professional_specialty_select`, select\_one, required): Choose the primary field of practice of the practitioner.
*   **Practitioner's Organization or Clinic** (`practitioner_org_name`, text, required): Provide the name of the organization or clinic where the practitioner primarily works.
*   **Level of Expertise** (`expertise_level_rating`, select\_one, required): Rate the practitioner's level of expertise, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Outstanding.
*   **Quality of Care or Service** (`care_service_quality_rating`, select\_one, required): Rate the quality of care or service provided by the practitioner.
*   **Communication and Interpersonal Skills** (`communication_skills_rating`, select\_one, required): Rate the practitioner's communication and interpersonal skills.
*   **Ethical Conduct and Professionalism** (`ethical_conduct_rating`, select\_one, required): Rate the practitioner's ethical conduct and professionalism.
*   **Reputation in the Professional Field** (`reputation_field_rating`, select\_one, required): Rate the practitioner's reputation in their field.
*   **Why are you voting for this practitioner?** (`voting_reason_desc`, text, required): Briefly explain why you are voting for the practitioner.
*   **Describe a specific success story or experience** (`success_story_desc`, text, required): Share a moment that showcases the practitioner's excellence.
*   **One word that defines this practitioner** (`defining_descriptor_word`, text, required): Provide a word that best describes the practitioner's skills or attributes.
*   **How long have you known or worked with them?** (`relationship_duration`, select\_one, required): Select the most accurate range of time you've known or worked with the practitioner.
*   **Any other feedback for the awards committee?** (`committee_additional_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for the awards committee.
*   **Date of Vote** (`voting_submission_date`, date, required): Record the date you are submitting your vote.

By following these steps and filling out the form accurately, you will be able to provide a well-rounded evaluation of the practitioner's performance. Your input will help in identifying and recognizing exceptional practitioners in their field.
