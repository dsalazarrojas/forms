# Effective Explanation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is used to collect feedback on how well a survey was explained to you. Your input will help us improve the explanation of our surveys.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form fields as completely as possible.
* For each field, provide a clear and concise answer that answers the question asked.
* Do not leave any fields blank, as they are all required for us to get a complete picture of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): Please take a moment to introduce yourself and provide any background information about your experience with the survey.
* **Survey Title** (`survey-title`, text, required: false): What title would you give to this survey?
* **Survey Question** (`survey-question`, text, required: false): What is the main question that the survey is asking?
* **Answer Title** (`answer-title`, text, required: false): What would you title your answer to the survey question?
* **Explanation Title** (`explanation-title`, text, required: false): What would you title the explanation for your answer?
* **Rating 1** (`rating-1`, number, required: false): How clear was the survey explanation to you on a scale of 1-5?
* **Rating 2** (`rating-2`, number, required: false): How relevant was the survey to you on a scale of 1-5?
* **Rating 3** (`rating-3`, number, required: false): How well was the survey explanation explained on a scale of 1-5?
* **Rating 4** (`rating-4`, number, required: false): How likely are you to recommend the survey explanation to others on a scale of 1-5?
* **Rating 5** (`rating-5`, number, required: false): How easy was it to take the survey on a scale of 1-5?
* **Explanation** (`explanation`, text, required: false): Provide a brief explanation of your experience with the survey explanation.
* **Survey Comments** (`survey-comments`, text, required: false): Do you have any general comments about the survey?
* **Additional Comments** (`additional-comments`, text, required: false): Is there anything else you'd like to add about your experience with the survey explanation?
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: false): Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback, or "Cancel" to leave without submitting.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address to receive a copy of your survey feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you are willing, please provide your phone number so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Date of Survey** (`date-of-survey`, date, required: false): When did you take the survey?
* **Time of Survey** (`time-of-survey`, time, required: false): What time did you take the survey?
* **Location of Survey** (`location-of-survey`, select_one, required: false): Where did you take the survey? Choose one of the options:
	+ Online
	+ Offline
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Provide the physical location of where you took the survey.
* **Additional Location** (`additional-location`, text, required: false): Is there any other location where you took the survey that isn't listed above?
* **Instructor** (`instructor`, text, required: false): Who was the instructor of the survey?
* **Course** (`course`, text, required: false): What course was the survey part of?
* **Survey Type** (`survey-type`, select_one, required: false): What type of survey was this? Choose one of the options:
	+ Multiple Choice
	+ Open-Ended
* **Survey Comments 2** (`survey-comments-2`, text, required: false): Do you have any more comments about the survey?
