# Market Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "Market Satisfaction Survey," is designed to collect customer feedback on their experience with the store's services and products. The goal is to gauge customer satisfaction and help us understand how we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant answers to each field, based on your honest opinion about our service.
2. Ensure you answer all required questions (marked as "required").
3. Review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about our service?** (`customer_satisfaction`, Select Multiple, Required)
  Answer how you would rate our service on a scale from Very Good to Very Bad.
* **How would you rate your experience at this store?** (`customer_experience`, Select One, Required)
  Choose how you would rate your overall experience at the store on a scale from Excellent to Bad.
* **How would you rate the service of our staff?** (`customer_service`, Select One, Not Required)
  Answer how you would rate the service of our staff on a scale from Excellent to Bad.
* **How often do you receive updates on promotions and new products?** (`communication`, Date, Not Required)
  If you are unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **How long have you used our products or services?** (`product_range`, Number, Required)
  Answer how long you have been using our products or services.
* **Have you told a friend or family member about our brand?** (`market_reach`, Select Multiple, Not Required)
  Answer if you have recommended us to someone you know.
* **How often do you visit our store?** (`store_location`, Select One, Not Required)
  Choose how often you visit the store.
* **Overall how satisfied are you with our store?** (`overall_satisfaction`, Select One, Required)
  Answer how satisfied you are with the store on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
