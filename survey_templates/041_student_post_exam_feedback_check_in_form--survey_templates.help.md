# student-post-exam-feedback-check-in-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and answers from students after an exam. It helps teachers assess students' understanding and identify areas where they may need additional support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the questions carefully and provide honest feedback on your understanding of the subject.
2. Choose relevant options for the `learning-gaps` and `follow-ups` sections.
3. Write your thoughts and reflections in the `feedback`, `additional-comments`, `student-answers`, `teacher-feedback`, `teacher-comments`, and `additional-notes` sections.
4. Fill out the `review-dates`, `review-times`, `next-meeting-date`, `next-meeting-time`, and `next-meeting-place` sections with the relevant information.
5. Make sure to provide detailed feedback and insights in the `teacher-notes` section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the exam and your thoughts on it.
* **Student Name**: A text field where you can enter your name.
* **Student Email**: A field for your email address, in case your teacher needs to contact you.
* **Feedback**: A text field for you to express your thoughts and reflections on the exam.
* **Learning Gaps**: A multiple-choice section for you to identify areas where you may need additional help.
* **Follow-ups**: A single-choice section for you to suggest a follow-up meeting or action.
* **Additional Comments**: A text field for any additional thoughts or comments you may have.
* **Student Answers**: A text field for you to provide detailed answers to questions asked by your teacher.
* **Teacher Feedback**: A text field for your teacher's feedback and comments.
* **Teacher Comments**: A text field for your teacher's additional comments.
* **Review Dates**: A date field for you to enter the dates you would like to review the exam material.
* **Review Times**: A time field for you to enter the times you would like to review the exam material.
* **Next Meeting Date**: A date field for you to enter the date of the next meeting with your teacher.
* **Next Meeting Time**: A time field for you to enter the time of the next meeting with your teacher.
* **Next Meeting Place**: A text field for you to enter the location of the next meeting with your teacher.
* **Teacher Notes**: A text field for your teacher's notes and reminders.
* **Additional Notes**: A text field for any additional notes or comments you may have.

**Tips**

* Please be honest and detailed in your feedback and answers.
* Use specific examples to illustrate your thoughts and reflections.
* Make sure to proofread your entries carefully before submitting.
