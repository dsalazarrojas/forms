# Future Narrative Journal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Future Narrative Journal" form is a tool for users to reflect on their future goals, experiences, and personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and update any existing entries from previous sessions.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Five Year Reflection** (`five_year_reflection`, text, required): This field is for reflecting on your experiences over the past five years. Please type out any notable events, milestones, or achievements.
* **Future Goals Reflections** (`future_goals_reflections`, text, required): In this field, write about your goals you hope to achieve in the near future. This is a good space to describe your aspirations and targets.
* **Current Goals Reflections** (`current_goals_reflections`, text, required): This field is for reflecting on your current goals and objectives. Please type out any progress you've made or challenges you're facing.
* **Past Experiences** (`past_experiences`, text, required): Describe any significant events or experiences that have shaped your thoughts and feelings about your future.
* **Future Reflections** (`future_reflections`, text, required): This field is for reflecting on your thoughts and feelings about your future. Please type out any insights or realizations you've had.
* **Future Narrative Journal** (`future_narrative_journal`, text, required): Summarize your reflections and thoughts from the previous fields in this field.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the current date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the current time in the format 12:34 PM.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, text, optional): If your time zone is different from the default zone, please enter it here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you want to be contacted, please enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): If you want to be contacted, please enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): If you want to be contacted, please enter your address.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Choose one option from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Choose any number of options from the dropdown menu.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, optional): If you want to enter a decimal value, please do so here.
* **Integer** (`integer`, number, optional): If you want to enter an integer value, please do so here.
* **Time Zone Select** (`time_zone_select`, select_one, required): Choose one time zone from the dropdown menu.
* **Date Time** (`date_time`, date, required): Enter the current date and time in the format mm/dd/yyyy 12:34 PM.
* **Future Date** (`future_date`, date, optional): If you want to schedule a future date, please enter it here.
* **Future Time** (`future_time`, time, optional): If you want to schedule a future time, please enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you want to be contacted, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you want to be contacted, please enter your phone number.
* **Text** (`text`, text, optional): Add any additional text or comments.
* **Text Area** (`text_area`, text, optional): Add any additional text or comments in this larger text field.

## Tips
* Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Review and update any existing entries before submitting this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
