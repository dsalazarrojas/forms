# Brake System Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey helps us understand your vehicle's brake system and preferences to provide tailored recommendations for upgrades and maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your vehicle's make, model, and year from the corresponding fields.
2. Choose the current brake type, pad condition, and rotor condition of your vehicle.
3. Evaluate the current stopping performance and brake noise or issues.
4. Indicate your interest in brake upgrades and select the types of upgrades you're interested in.
5. Specify your vehicle's primary use and brake performance demands.
6. Provide your email address for further recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Year** (Number, Required): Enter the year of your vehicle's production.
* **Vehicle Make** (Text, Required): Select your vehicle's manufacturer.
* **Vehicle Model** (Text, Required): Enter your vehicle's model name.
* **VIN Number** (Text, Optional): If available, provide your vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number.
* **Current Brake Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of brake system installed in your vehicle.
	+ Disc brakes front only
	+ Disc brakes all wheels
	+ Drum brakes all wheels
	+ Mixed disc and drum
	+ ABS equipped
	+ Electronic brakes
	+ Other
* **Brake Pad Condition** (Select One, Required): Evaluate the current state of your brake pads.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Worn
	+ Critical
* **Brake Rotor Condition** (Select One, Optional): Assess the status of your brake rotors.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Warped
	+ Needs replacement
* **Current Stopping Performance** (Select One, Required): Rate your vehicle's stopping performance.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Adequate
	+ Poor
	+ Unsafe
* **Brake Noise or Issues** (Text, Optional): Describe any brake noises or problems you experience.
* **Brake Performance Fade or Loss of Pressure** (Select One, Optional): Evaluate the performance degradation of your vehicle's brakes.
	+ Never
	+ Rarely
	+ Sometimes
	+ Often
	+ Always
* **Interested in Brake Upgrades** (Select One, Required): Indicate your interest in upgrading your brake system.
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Maybe
	+ Not interested
	+ Already upgraded
* **Types of Upgrades Interested** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the brake system upgrades you're interested in.
	+ Performance pads
	+ Slotted rotors
	+ Braided brake lines
	+ Larger rotors
	+ Upgrade calipers
	+ ABS installation
	+ Exotic materials
	+ Other
* **Primary Vehicle Use** (Select One, Required): Specify how you primarily use your vehicle.
	+ Daily commute
	+ Street performance
	+ Track use
	+ Off-road
	+ Towing
	+ Mixed use
	+ Other
* **Brake System Demands** (Select One, Optional): Assess how hard you use your brakes.
	+ Light use
	+ Moderate use
	+ Heavy use
	+ Extreme use
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Provide your email address for further recommendations.
