# Household Information Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential household information from clients or customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about the household.
2. Provide details about the household members, including contact information and income.
3. Indicate the type of household (e.g., single-family, multi-family, or apartment).
4. Add any additional comments or notes as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Information**: Enter a brief description of the household. 
* **Household Details**: Enter more in-depth information about the household, such as the type of housing or neighborhood. 
* **Contact Details**: Enter the contact information of the household member(s) who will be the main point of contact. 
* **Household Size**: Enter the number of people living in the household. 
* **Household Income**: Enter the total income of the household. 
* **Head of Household Income**: Enter the income of the head of the household. 
* **Household Type**: Select the type of household (e.g., single-family, multi-family, or apartment). 
* **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number for the household. 
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the household. 
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the household. 
* **Household Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the household. 
* **Contact Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the contact person. 

Remember to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the form serves its purpose effectively. If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
