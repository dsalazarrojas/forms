# Workload Balance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the balance of workloads across multiple teams, collecting quantitative data on team size to inform staffing or resource allocation decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each team's section carefully, ensuring you're answering for the correct team.
2. For each team, estimate the number of people assigned to that team.
3. Enter the estimated number of people in the corresponding field.
4. Repeat this process for each team, until you've completed all sections.
