# Dyadic Communication Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dyadic Communication Assessment Form is used to assess the level of communication between two individuals. This form is designed to collect information on the support level and communication level of two individuals, their feedback, and any comments or observations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the two individuals you are assessing, one for each of the "Person 1" and "Person 2" fields.
2. Choose the support level and communication level for each individual from the available options.
3. Enter any feedback or comments you have for each person in the "Feedback" and "Comments" fields.
4. Enter the date and time of submission.
5. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This is a placeholder field for the form's introduction, and you do not need to fill it out.

### Person 1
* **Person 1** (`person_1`, select_one, required: false): Select the name of the first person you are assessing from the available options.

### Person 2
* **Person 2** (`person_2`, select_one, required: false): Select the name of the second person you are assessing from the available options.

### Support Level
* **Support Level** (`support_level`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the level of support you perceive between the two individuals from the available options.

### Communication Level
* **Communication Level** (`communication_level`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the level of communication you perceive between the two individuals from the available options.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Enter any feedback or observations you have for each person.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or observations you have for each person.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you submitted the form.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time when you submitted the form.

### Submitted By
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct options for each field based on your assessment.
* Enter any relevant feedback or comments for each person.
* Double-check the date and time you entered before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you are submitting the form in the correct format (date and time).
