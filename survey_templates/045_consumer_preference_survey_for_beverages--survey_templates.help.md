# Consumer Preference Survey For Beverages - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on consumer preferences for different types of beverages. Your input will help us better understand your habits and preferences, which will be used to improve our offerings and create products that cater to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling in your **First Name** (optional), **Email Address** (optional), and select your **Age Group**. This information helps us understand your demographic background.
2. Select your preferred **Beverage Category** from the list provided. This will help us narrow down the survey questions related to your preferred drink type.
3. Answer the questions about your **Consumption Frequency**, which is how often you drink your preferred type of beverage.
4. Provide your **Favorite Brands** (optional) for the selected beverage category.
5. Indicate your preferred **Flavors** for the selected beverage category. You can select multiple flavors if applicable.
6. Choose your **Sweetness Preference**, which is how sweet you like your beverages.
7. Select your preferred **Temperature** for the selected beverage category.
8. Choose your preferred **Container Type** for the selected beverage category.
9. Select your preferred **Beverage Size** for the selected beverage category.
10. Indicate where you usually **Purchase** your preferred beverages (e.g., grocery store, convenience store, gas station, etc.).
11. Select your **Typical Price Range** for the selected beverage category.
12. Choose how **Important** health factors are to you when making purchasing decisions.
13. Select the **Important Ingredients** you look for in a beverage.
14. Choose any **Ingredients to Avoid** in a beverage.
15. Indicate how **Brand Loyal** you are to a particular brand.
16. Answer how **Open** you are to trying new beverages.
17. Select how **Important** eco-friendly packaging is to you.
18. Choose your typical **Occasion** for consuming beverages.
19. Provide any **Flavor Suggestions** you would like to see in the future.
20. Finally, provide any **Additional Comments** you have about your preferences or experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name (optional).
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Age Group** (3): Select your age range.
* **Beverage Category** (6): Select the category of beverages you are answering about (e.g., soft drinks, water, juice, etc.).
* **Consumption Frequency** (7): How often you drink your preferred type of beverage.
* **Favorite Brands** (8): List your preferred beverage brands (optional).
* **Flavors** (9): Select multiple flavors you prefer for your selected beverage category.
* **Sweetness Preference** (10): Indicate how sweet you like your beverages.
* **Temperature Preference** (11): Select your preferred temperature for your selected beverage category.
* **Container Preference** (12): Select your preferred container type for your selected beverage category.
* **Size Preference** (13): Select your preferred size for your selected beverage category.
* **Purchase Location** (14): Select all locations where you purchase your selected beverage category.
* **Typical Price Range** (15): Indicate your typical price range for the selected beverage category.
* **Health Consciousness** (16): Choose how important health factors are to you when making purchasing decisions.
* **Important Ingredients** (17): Select the ingredients you look for in a beverage.
* **Ingredients to Avoid** (18): Select the ingredients you would like to avoid in a beverage.
* **Brand Loyalty** (19): Indicate how brand loyal you are.
* **New Product Openness** (20): Answer how open you are to trying new beverages.
* **Sustainability Importance** (21): Choose how important eco-friendly packaging is to you.
* **Occasion** (22): Select your typical occasion for consuming beverages.
* **Flavor Suggestions** (23): Provide any new flavors you would like to see in the future.
* **Additional Comments** (24): Provide any additional feedback or comments you have.

Note: The fields marked as **required** are mandatory and must be filled in to complete the survey.
