# Home Health Care Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a patient's home health care needs and circumstances. This form is intended to help medical professionals assess and provide appropriate care to patients in a home health care setting. Please fill out this form accurately and honestly to help us better understand your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first_name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last_name field.
3. Provide your address in the address fields (1 and 2).
4. Enter your zip code in the zip_code field.
5. If you have any medical conditions that require attention, select them in the medical_condition field.
6. If you take any medications, select all that apply in the medication_1 field.
7. Describe your current symptoms in the symptoms field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **optional**): This field is where you should enter your given name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **optional**): This field is where you should enter your surname as it is spelled.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, **optional**): Enter the street address you would like us to use for communication.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, **optional**): Enter the apartment or suite number if you have one.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, **optional**): Enter your postal code as a numeric value (e.g., 12345).
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, **optional**): Select any medical conditions that apply to you from the list provided.
* **Medication 1** (`medication_1`, select_multiple, **optional**): If you take any medications, select all that apply from the list provided.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, **optional**): Describe any symptoms you are currently experiencing.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* If you need to add any additional information, use the symptoms field.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
