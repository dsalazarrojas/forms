# Device Framework Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Device Framework Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess your knowledge and experience with a specific device framework. This quiz will help us understand your level of understanding and expertise in the field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all the questions to the best of your knowledge.
2. Make sure to choose the correct options for each question.
3. Fill in any text fields with your thoughts and experiences.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your experience level with the device framework?** (`first_question`, `select_one`, optional): This question determines your experience level with the device framework. Choose from the options 'Yes' or 'False' depending on whether you have experience with the framework.
- **How many years do you have working with the device framework?** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been working with the device framework. This question is required.
- **What type of device do you primarily work with?** (`device_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the options Device A, Device B, or Device C. This question is required.
- **What is your role within the team?** (`role`, `text`, required): Describe your role in the team. This question is required.
- **Can you describe your experience with debugging the device framework?** (`experience_debugging`, `text`, required): Share any relevant details about your experience with debugging the device framework. This question is required.
- **What is the most common issue you face with the device framework?** (`common_issues`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the options Bug A, Bug B, or Bug C. This question is required.
- **What is your preferred way to learn about new frameworks?** (`learning_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all that apply from the options YouTube tutorials, Online forums, Books, or Instructors. This question is required.
- **Have you attended any workshops or conferences related to the device framework?** (`workshop_attendance`, `select_one`, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' depending on whether you have attended workshops or conferences related to the device framework. This question is required.
- **How often do you attend online meetups or webinars about the device framework?** (`meetups_attendance`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose from the options Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly. This question is required.
- **Can you describe a challenging project you worked on with the device framework?** (`challenging_project`, `text`, required): Share your story about a challenging project you worked on with the device framework. This question is required.
- **How do you handle conflicts with team members when working with the device framework?** (`team_conflicts`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all that apply from the options Confuciusly, Aggressively, or Cooperatively. This question is required.
- **What do you think is the most exciting feature of the device framework?** (`exciting_feature`, `text`, required): Share your thoughts about the most exciting feature of the device framework. This question is required.

Tips:
- Make sure to answer all questions thoroughly.
- Be as accurate as possible.
- Take your time to think before submitting your form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
