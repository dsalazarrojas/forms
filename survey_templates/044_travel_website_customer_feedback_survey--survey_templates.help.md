# Travel Website Customer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions about our travel website. Your feedback will help us improve your experience and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select one or multiple options for each field, depending on your experience.
3. Fill in any text fields as requested.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief welcome message to introduce the survey.
* **how_did_you_book_your_travel** (`travel_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the type of travel you booked (domestic, international, or other).
* **how_did_you_book_your_travel** (`travel_booking_method`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ways you booked your travel (direct, agency, online, or offline).
* **when_did_you_travel** (`travel_dates`, `date`, required: false): If you remember, please enter the dates of your travel.
* **where_did_you_travel_to** (`travel_destination`, `text`, required: false): Write the name of the city or location you traveled to.
* **how_did_you_feel_about_your_travel** (`travel_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Please rate your experience (very satisfactory, satisfactory, neutral, or dissatisfactory).
* **how_did_you_feel_about_your_website_experience** (`website_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your experience with our website (very satisfactory, satisfactory, neutral, or dissatisfactory).
* **what_feature_do_you_like_most_about_your_website** (`website_feature`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the features you liked most about our website (design, content, or navigation).
* **what_feature_do_you_dislike_most_about_your_website** (`website_feature_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the features you disliked most about our website (design, content, or navigation).
* **website_improvement_suggestions** (`website_feature_3`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions for improvement, write them down here.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you back.
* **how_did_you_feel_about_your_travel_website** (`website_experience_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your experience with our travel website (very satisfactory, satisfactory, neutral, or dissatisfactory).
* **what_do_you_like_most_about_your_website** (`website_feature`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the features you like most about our website (design, content, or navigation).
* **what_do_you_dislike_most_about_your_website** (`website_feature_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the features you dislike most about our website (design, content, or navigation).
