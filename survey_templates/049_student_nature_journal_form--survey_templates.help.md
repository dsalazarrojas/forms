# Student Nature Journal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student's experiences and observations of the natural environment. It is meant to be filled out after a trip or excursion, and can help educators assess student engagement and participation in nature-based activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start at the beginning of the form**: You can begin filling out the form from the first page.
2. **Read each question carefully**: Make sure you understand what each question is asking before answering.
3. **Answer each question to the best of your ability**: Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
4. **Submit your answers**: Once you've completed all the questions, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title: Student Nature Journal Form**: This is the name of the form, and is for identification purposes only.
* **Outdoor Observations**: On this page, describe any observations or notes you made during your trip. This can be as simple as "We saw a lot of greenery and flowers" or more detailed, like "We spotted several species of birds and saw a few baby ducks swimming in the nearby pond."
* **Animal Spotted**: Choose all the animals you saw during your trip. Select multiple options if you observed multiple animals.
	+ **bird**: You saw a bird(s) during your trip.
	+ **butterfly**: You saw a butterfly during your trip.
	+ **fish**: You saw a fish during your trip.
	+ **frog**: You saw a frog during your trip.
	+ **insect**: You saw an insect during your trip.
	+ **mammal**: You saw a mammal during your trip.
	+ **reptile**: You saw a reptile during your trip.
	+ **squirrel**: You saw a squirrel during your trip.
* **Date**: Record the date of your trip. This can be in any format (e.g. "March 12, 2023" or "2023-03-12").
* **Weather**: Describe the weather during your trip. For example, "sunny and warm" or "cloudy and cool".
* **Temperature**: If you have a thermometer, record the current temperature in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
* **Location**: Describe the location of your trip. This can be a specific address, park name, or general area.
* **Time Spent Outside**: Estimate how many hours you spent outside during your trip.
* **Time Spent Engaged**: Estimate how much time you spent engaged in nature-based activities during your trip.
* **Notes**: Write any additional comments or reflections you have about your trip.
* **Teacher Comments**: If your teacher asks you to provide additional comments, write them here.

## Tips
* Try to be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out the form.
* Use proper spelling and grammar when writing your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your teacher or educator for guidance.
