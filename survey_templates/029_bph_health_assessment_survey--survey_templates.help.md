# BPH Health Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BPH Health Assessment Survey is a comprehensive form designed to collect information from patients about their urinary health. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's overall health profile, enabling them to provide personalized care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read the instructions for each section.
- Ensure you answer questions based on your true understanding and experiences.
- Fill out every required field as accurately as possible.
- Do not hesitate to ask for help if you are unsure about any question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name** (required)
* The **Patient Name** field is the first step in identifying who you are and ensuring your responses are associated with your correct identity.
* Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **Date of Birth** (required)
* This field helps us track the span of your health history, including your age and life stage.
* Enter your date of birth as it is recorded on your identification documents.

### 3. **Contact Phone Number** (required)
* This number helps us contact you in case we need additional information or clarification.
* Enter your contact number where you can be reached during the day and evening.

### 4. **Email Address** (optional)
* If you have an email address where we can reach you, enter it here.
* Please note that we respect your privacy and will not share your email with anyone.

### 5. **Any Known Medical Conditions?** (optional)
* List any known medical conditions you have been diagnosed with or are being treated for.
* This helps us understand your medical background and potential impacts on your urinary health.

### 6. **Current Medications** (optional)
* List all medications you are currently taking, including over-the-counter medications.
* This helps us understand your current treatment and potential impacts on your condition.

### 7. **Urinary Frequency During Day** (required)
* This question asks for the number of times you urinate during the day.
* Be as accurate as possible.

### 8. **Urinary Frequency at Night** (required)
* This question asks for the number of times you urinate during the night.
* Be as accurate as possible.

### 9. **How Does Night Urination Impact Sleep?** (required)
* Choose one of the options that best describes how night urination affects your sleep.
* This helps us understand the impact of your symptoms on your quality of life.

### 10. **Urinary Symptoms Experienced** (optional)
* Select all symptoms you experience from the list provided.
* This helps us understand the scope and nature of your symptoms.

### 11. **Urgency Severity** (required)
* Choose how severe the feeling of urgency is.
* This helps us understand the impact of your symptoms on your quality of life.

### 12. **How Long Have You Had These Symptoms?** (optional)
* This question helps us understand the duration of your symptoms.
* Enter the time frame in weeks, months, or years.

### 13. **Have Symptoms Been Getting Worse?** (optional)
* Select how your symptoms have changed over time.
* This helps us understand the progression of your condition.

### 14. **Impact on Quality of Life** (required)
* Choose how much your symptoms impact your daily life.
* This helps us understand how your symptoms affect you.

### 15. **Previous Treatments Tried** (optional)
* List any treatments you have tried for your condition.
* This helps us understand your treatment history and potential effectiveness.

### 16. **Family History of BPH or Prostate Issues?** (optional)
* If there is a family history of BPH or prostate issues, select true or false.
* This helps us understand potential genetic contributions to your condition.

### 17. **Additional Concerns or Questions** (optional)
* If you have any additional concerns or questions, enter them here.
* This helps us understand your thoughts and worries about your condition.
