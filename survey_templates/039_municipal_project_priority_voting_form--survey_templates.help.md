# Municipal Project Priority Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for municipal projects to prioritize their needs and requests. You will be asked to submit a brief description of the primary issue you want to address, along with your project's name, description, and other relevant details such as contact information and project status. Your input will help us prioritize and allocate resources efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Read the instructions carefully**: Understand the form's purpose and the questions asked.
2. **Fill in the required fields**: The contact email and project name are required to complete the form.
3. **Provide a brief description**: Give a short and concise description of the project.
4. **Choose a project status**: Select the current status of your project.
5. **Submit your answers**: Once you have filled out all the fields, click the submit button to send your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the primary issue that you want to address** (municipal_project_priority_voting_form_page_1):
	+ This field is a short description of the primary issue you want to address. Try to be specific and clear.
* **Project Name** (project_name):
	+ Enter a brief title for your project.
* **Project Description** (project_description):
	+ Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email):
	+ Enter your email address where you can be reached for further questions.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone):
	+ Optional, but if you want to be reachable via phone, provide your number.
* **Contact Address** (contact_address):
	+ Optional, but if you want to be reachable via mail or have a project location, provide your address.
* **Project Priority** (project_priority):
	+ Select the level of priority for your project: Low, Medium, or High.
* **Project Status** (project_status):
	+ Choose the current status of your project: Planning, In Progress, or Completed.

## Tips
* Be as specific and concise as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate representation of your project.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
* Ensure you fill out all the required fields (contact email and project name) before submitting.
* You can submit multiple entries, but ensure they are all relevant and distinct projects.
