# Retail Staff Customer Interaction Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their interaction with retail staff. It will help us understand their experiences and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the staff member prothys (Good, Fair, or Poor).
2. Enter the name of the staff member you interacted with.
3. Choose the type of interaction you had with the staff member (In-person, Phone, Chat, Email, or Other).
4. Enter the time spent with the staff member.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the experience (on a scale of 1-5).
6. Enter any areas where you think we could improve.
7. Provide any feedback about your experience.
8. Provide any suggestions for improvement.
9. Add any comments from the staff member about the interaction (if applicable).
10. Add any comments from the customer about the interaction (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interaction With Staff**: This field is where you'll describe the experience you had with the staff member.
* **Staff Member Prophets**: Please rate the staff member's prothys, where:
	+ Good: The staff member was very helpful and provided great service.
	+ Fair: The staff member was somewhat helpful and provided decent service.
	+ Poor: The staff member was not helpful and provided poor service.
* **Staff Member Name**: Enter the name of the staff member you interacted with.
* **Store Name**: Enter the name of the store where you interacted with the staff member.
* **Interaction Type**: Choose the type of interaction you had with the staff member, such as:
	+ In-person: Face-to-face interaction in the store.
	+ Phone: Call with a staff member.
	+ Chat: Online chat with a staff member.
	+ Email: Email exchange with a staff member.
	+ Other: Other types of interactions not listed above.
* **Time Spent**: Enter the amount of time spent with the staff member.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Improvement Areas**: Enter any areas where you think we could improve.
* **Feedback**: Provide any feedback about your experience.
* **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Staff Comments**: Add any comments from the staff member about the interaction (if applicable).
* **Customer Comments**: Add any comments from the customer about the interaction (if applicable).
