# Phenotypic Plasticity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phenotypic Plasticity Survey is a scientific form designed to collect data on the changes in physical traits or characteristics. It's intended for researchers and professionals in the field of phenotypic plasticity.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete the form by providing accurate and clear answers to each question.
2.  Start by selecting "Trait Variation" to describe the variation in traits you've observed or researched.
3.  Choose the relevant lab equipment used in your work from the list provided.
4.  Select all relevant research goals from the list to ensure you're on track with your objectives.
5.  Enter a numerical value for "Trait Measure" to record specific data related to traits.
6.  Select your level of education or qualifications from the list for "Education Level".
7.  Describe your research experiences in the "Research Experiences" field, focusing on any notable events or studies.
8.  Choose the relevant field teams or research groups from the list for "Field Teams".
9.  Finally, complete the remaining fields with any additional information or notes you deem relevant to the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Trait Variation (1)**: A text field for describing the variation in traits. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of the variation you've observed or researched.
*   **Lab Equipment (2)**: A select_one field with three options. Choose the type of lab equipment used in your work.
*   **Research Goals (3)**: A select_multiple field with three options. Select all relevant research goals to ensure you're on track with your objectives.
*   **Trait Measure (4)**: A number field for recording specific data related to traits. Please provide a numerical value.
*   **Education Level (5)**: A select_one field with three options. Choose your level of education or qualifications.
*   **Research Experiences (6)**: A text field for describing research experiences. Please elaborate on any notable events or studies.
*   **Field Teams (7)**: A select_multiple field with three options. Select all relevant field teams or research groups.
*   **Another Field (8)**: A text field for free-form text or notes. Please provide any additional information you deem relevant.
*   **More Fields (9)**: A text field for free-form text or notes. Please provide any additional information you deem relevant.
*   **Yet Another Field (10)**: A text field for free-form text or notes. Please provide any additional information you deem relevant.
*   **Even More (11)**: A text field for free-form text or notes. Please provide any additional information you deem relevant.
*   **Last Field (12)**: A text field for free-form text or notes. Please provide any additional information you deem relevant.
