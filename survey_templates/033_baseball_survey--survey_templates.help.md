# Baseball Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your opinions and experiences with baseball. Your input will help us understand your thoughts on various aspects of the game, including your demographics, involvement, and preferences. Your participation is voluntary and anonymous.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best match your thoughts and opinions.
3. Provide your name and age group, location, and favorite MLB team (if applicable).
4. Share your favorite aspect of baseball and a favorite memory (if you have one).
5. Answer the final questions about suggested improvements and whether you would recommend baseball to others.
