# App Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the app. Your input will help us understand what you like and dislike about the app, and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the survey date to indicate when you first started using the app.
2. Fill in your name (optional).
3. Provide your email address (optional).
4. Choose the name of the app you are providing feedback on.
5. Enter the version of the app you are using.
6. Select the type of device you use to access the app (e.g., iPhone, Android phone, iPad, etc.).
7. Indicate how long you have been using the app (e.g., Less than 1 week, 1-4 weeks, etc.).
8. Choose how frequently you use the app (e.g., Daily, Several times a week, etc.).
9. Rate the app's overall performance, ease of use, and other aspects.
10. Indicate your likelihood to recommend the app to others.
11. Rate the app's design and visual quality.
12. Rate the app's performance and speed.
13. Rate the app's stability and reliability.
14. Indicate whether the app's features meet your needs.
15. If features are missing, describe them in the text box below.
16. Identify the biggest area needing improvement.
17. Mention the best aspect of the app (optional).
18. Mention the worst aspect of the app (optional).
19. Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey date**: Please select the date when you first started using the app.
* **Your name**: If you'd like to provide your name for feedback, you can do so here (optional).
* **Email address**: If you'd like to provide your email address for follow-up or additional information (optional).
* **App name**: Select the name of the app you are providing feedback on.
* **App version**: Enter the version of the app you are using.
* **Device type**: Choose the type of device you use to access the app (e.g., iPhone, Android phone, iPad, etc.).
* **How long have you been using this app?**: Indicate how long you have been using the app.
* **How frequently do you use the app?**: Choose how frequently you use the app.
* **Overall app rating**: Rate the app's overall performance, ease of use, and other aspects.
* **Likelihood to recommend**: Indicate your likelihood to recommend the app to others.
* **Ease of use**: Rate the app's ease of use.
* **Design and visual quality**: Rate the app's design and visual quality.
* **Performance and speed**: Rate the app's performance and speed.
* **Stability and reliability**: Rate the app's stability and reliability.
* **Features meet your needs**: Indicate whether the app's features meet your needs.
* **What features are missing or needed?** (if any): If you feel certain features are missing or needed, describe them in this text box.
* **Biggest area needing improvement**: If you have identified an area that needs improvement, describe it in this text box.
* **Best aspect of the app** (optional): Mention the best aspect of the app you'd like to highlight.
* **Worst aspect of the app** (optional): Mention the worst aspect of the app that you'd like to highlight.
* **Additional comments or suggestions**: Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the app.

Note: Fields marked as optional can be skipped if you choose not to provide that information.
