# LLM Hallucinations Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LLM Hallucinations Quiz is designed to collect information about your experiences with LLM hallucinations. Your input will help us better understand the nature of LLM hallucinations and improve the performance of AI models.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions in a clear and concise manner.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select only one option that best describes your experience.
3. You may provide additional comments or explanations in the text fields.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What Are LLM Hallucinations?** (`hallucinations_definition`, required): Please describe your understanding of LLM hallucinations. This will help us understand your perspective on the topic.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, required, select_one): Indicate the level of severity of your LLM hallucination experience. Choose one of the options:
	+ a: I have not experienced any LLM hallucinations.
	+ b: I have occasionally experienced LLM hallucinations, but they are minor.
	+ c: I frequently experience severe LLM hallucinations.
* **Severity** (`severity`, required, select_multiple): Select all levels of severity that apply to your LLM hallucination experience:
	+ slight
	+ moderate
	+ severe
* **Examples** (`examples`, optional, text): If you have experienced LLM hallucinations, provide a brief description or an example that illustrates your understanding.
* **Prevention** (`prevention`, optional, text): Share any information you have about how you prevented or managed LLM hallucinations.
* **Next Steps** (`next-steps`, optional, note): Write any further comments or suggestions about what you would like to see in future updates or developments.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, required, select_one): Provide any further comments or explanations that might be helpful for our understanding of LLM hallucinations. Choose one of the options:
	+ a
	+ b
