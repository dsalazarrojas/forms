# ranking_survey_rate_your_preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user ratings and rankings for a series of topics. It allows users to rate their preferences and rankings for each topic, providing valuable feedback for improvement and optimization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in all required fields as specified by the `required` field attribute in each field.
* For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options from the provided list.
* For select_multiple fields, select all relevant options that apply.
* For date and time fields, use the format specified in the field label.
* For note and email fields, enter any additional information you feel is relevant.
* For phone field, enter your contact phone number if you want to be contacted.
* For ranking field, select all relevant rankings that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Preferences** (`user_preferences`, `text`, required): Please enter a brief description of your preferences.
* **Topic 1 Label** (`topic_1_label`, `select_one`, required): Choose the label for Topic 1 from the provided list.
* **Topic 1 Rating** (`topic_1_rating`, `number`, not required): Enter a numerical rating for Topic 1, e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.
* **Topic 1 Name** (`topic_2_name`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the name for Topic 1 from the provided list.
* **Topic 2 Label** (`topic_2_label`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the label for Topic 2 from the provided list.
* **Topic 2 Rating** (`topic_2_rating`, `number`, not required): Enter a numerical rating for Topic 2, e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.
* **Topic 2 Name** (`topic_4_name`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the name for Topic 2 from the provided list.
* **Topic 3 Label** (`topic_3_label`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the label for Topic 3 from the provided list.
* **Topic 3 Rating** (`topic_3_rating`, `number`, not required): Enter a numerical rating for Topic 3, e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.
* **Topic 3 Name** (`topic_4_name`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the name for Topic 3 from the provided list.
* **Topic 4 Label** (`topic_4_label`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the label for Topic 4 from the provided list.
* **Topic 4 Rating** (`topic_4_rating`, `number`, not required): Enter a numerical rating for Topic 4, e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.
* **Topic 4 Name** (`topic_4_name`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the name for Topic 4 from the provided list.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date in the format specified in the field label.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time in the format specified in the field label.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments you want to make.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your contact phone number if you want to be contacted.
* **Ranking** (`ranking`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all relevant rankings that apply.
