# Coffee Shop Service Speed Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Coffee Shop Service Speed Survey, designed to gather data on the service speed of your coffee shop. This survey aims to assess the speed of service, including wait times, staff availability, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct shift that corresponds to your visit.
2. Enter the date of your visit in the "Date of Visit" field.
3. Choose the time of your visit from the "Time of Visit" field.
4. Answer the questions about wait times, including the time waiting to order and receive your order.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the service speed and overall satisfaction with your visit.
6. Provide any improvement suggestions you have for the coffee shop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Visit**: Choose the time of your visit from the provided options (6am-9am, 9am-12pm, 12pm-5pm, 5pm-8pm, 8pm+).
* **Which Shift**: Select the shift during which you visited the coffee shop (Morning, Mid-morning, Afternoon, Evening, Late).
* **Type of Transaction**: Choose the type of transaction you made during your visit (Drink only, Food and drink, Food only, Pastry and drink).
* **Time waiting to order**: Enter the approximate time you waited to place your order in minutes.
* **Was the wait time to order acceptable?**: Choose whether the wait time was acceptable or not.
* **Time waiting to receive order**: Enter the approximate time you waited to receive your order in minutes.
* **Was the wait time to receive order acceptable?**: Choose whether the wait time was acceptable or not.
* **Total service time**: Enter the total time from entering to receiving your order in minutes.
* **Service speed rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the service speed from 1-5, where 1 is very slow and 5 is very fast.
* **How did speed compare to expectations?**: Choose how your expectations of service speed compared to what you experienced.
* **What affected the service speed?**: Select all the factors that affected the service speed.
* **Number of staff members visible**: Enter the approximate number of staff members you saw during your visit.
* **Staff appeared busy**: Choose whether the staff appeared busy or not.
* **How could service speed be improved?**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving the service speed.
* **Technology improvements that would help**: Provide any technology improvements that you think would help improve the service speed.
* **Overall satisfaction with visit**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your visit from 1-5, where 1 is very unsatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.

**Tips:**

* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, be sure to include them.
* Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve the service at our coffee shop!
