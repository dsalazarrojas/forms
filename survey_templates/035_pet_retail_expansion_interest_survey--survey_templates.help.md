# Pet Retail Expansion Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the feasibility of expanding pet retail services and products to various regions and types of pets. It aims to understand where and what type of pet retail expansion would be most suitable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you're answering all relevant questions.
2. Select the correct options for each question. If an option is not applicable or not relevant, please select "No".
3. If you have any comments or feedback, please use the text field at the end of the form to provide more information.
