# Technology Advancement Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technology Advancement Check In form is used to collect information about team members' technology adoption, feedback, and other relevant metrics. This form helps the team to stay updated on progress, identify areas of improvement, and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the answer to the first question on the first page.
2. Provide your feedback and suggestions for technology advancement in the second question.
3. Rate the tool and indicate if you need any improvements in the third question.
4. Answer whether you need any training or support in the fourth question.
5. List any suggestions for improvements in the fifth question.
6. Add any comments or feedback to the team in the sixth question.
7. Provide the current date in the seventh question.
8. Choose your time slot for submission in the eighth question.
9. Select whether you are the assigned team lead in the ninth question.
10. Provide the email address of the team lead in the tenth question.
11. Add any comments for the team lead in the eleventh question.
12. Select whether you submitted the form in the twelfth question.
13. Provide the date you submitted the form in the thirteenth question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Technology Advancement Check In** (`title`, `text`, required): This is the title of the form.
* **Yes/No** (`first_page`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have made any progress in technology advancement. Select "No" if you have not.
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, `text`, required): Provide your feedback and suggestions for technology advancement.
* **Tool Rating** (`tool_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the tool you are using from 1 to 5.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if you need any training or support.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required): List any suggestions for improvements.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): Add any comments or feedback to the team.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Provide the current date.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Choose your time slot for submission.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are the assigned team lead.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `text`, required): Provide the email address of the team lead.
* **Comments to Team Lead** (`comments_to_team_lead`, `text`, required): Add any comments for the team lead.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, required): Provide the date you submitted the form.
