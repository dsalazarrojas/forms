# Hand Hygiene Education Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about hand hygiene education within your organization. It will help us understand the level of hand hygiene education provided and its impact on hand hygiene practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all the options that apply.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the text fields to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role within the organization?** (`question1`, `select_one`): This question is to help us understand who is responsible for hand hygiene practices within your organization. Please select the role that best describes your position.
* **What level of hand hygiene education has your organization provided to date?** (`question2`, `select_multiple`): This question is to gather information about the education provided by your organization. Select all the options that apply to your organization's hand hygiene education programs.
* **How confident are you that hand hygiene practices have improved as a result of the education programs?** (`question3`, `number`): This question is to assess the impact of the education programs on hand hygiene practices. Select a confidence level from 0-10, where 10 is "very confident".
* **What methods of hand hygiene have been implemented in your facility?** (`question4`, `select_multiple`): This question is to gather information about hand hygiene methods implemented in your facility. Select all the options that apply.
* **How frequently do hand hygiene audits occur?** (`question5`, `time`): This question is to understand the frequency of hand hygiene audits. Select the frequency that best describes your experience.
* **What tools or resources are available to reinforce hand hygiene?** (`question6`, `select_one`): This question is to gather information about tools or resources available to reinforce hand hygiene practices. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **What opportunities are available to reinforce hand hygiene practices?** (`question7`, `select_multiple`): This question is to gather information about opportunities to reinforce hand hygiene practices. Select all the options that apply.
* **Is your facility participating in any hand hygiene certification or accreditation programs?** (`question8`, `select_multiple`): This question is to gather information about certification or accreditation programs. Select all the options that apply.
* **Have you noticed any improvements in hand hygiene practices over the last 6 months?** (`question9`, `select_multiple`): This question is to assess the impact of education programs on hand hygiene practices. Select all the options that apply.
* **Have you noticed any changes in hand hygiene practices as a result of the education program?** (`question10`, `select_multiple`): This question is to gather information about changes in hand hygiene practices. Select all the options that apply.
* **Have you noticed any changes in employee compliance with hand hygiene practices over the last 6 months?** (`question11`, `select_multiple`): This question is to assess the impact of education programs on employee compliance. Select all the options that apply.
* **Have you noticed any reduction in hand hygiene-related complaints or concerns over the last 6 months?** (`question12`, `select_multiple`): This question is to gather information about reduction in complaints or concerns. Select all the options that apply.
* **Have you noticed any reduction in hand hygiene-related infections over the last 6 months?** (`question13`, `select_multiple`): This question is to gather information about reduction in infections. Select all the options that apply.
* **Have you noticed any other benefits as a result of the hand hygiene education program?** (`question14`, `select_multiple`): This question is to gather information about other benefits. Select all the options that apply.
* **What other benefits as a result of the hand hygiene education program?** (`question15`, `text`): This question is to gather any additional comments or concerns. Please provide a brief description of any other benefits.
* **Are there any additional comments or concerns related to hand hygiene practices?** (`question16`, `text`): This question is to gather any additional comments or concerns. Please provide a brief description.
* **Are there any other comments or concerns related to hand hygiene practices?** (`question17`, `text`): This question is to gather any other comments or concerns. Please provide a brief description.
