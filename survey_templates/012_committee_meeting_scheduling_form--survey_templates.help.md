# Committee Meeting Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help committee members schedule meetings at times that are convenient for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields. This includes:

* Committee Title: Enter the name of the committee you are a part of.
* Your Full Name: Enter your full name.
* Primary Purpose of the Meeting: Briefly describe the purpose of the meeting.
* Which days of the week work best for you: Select the days of the week when you are available.
* Preferred time slots: Select the time slots that you prefer for the meeting.
* Option 1 - Monday, March 1: Indicate your availability for the proposed meeting date.
* Option 2 - Wednesday, March 3: Indicate your availability for the proposed meeting date.
* Option 3 - Friday, March 5: Indicate your availability for the proposed meeting date.
* Preferred Meeting Format: Choose the meeting format that you prefer (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* How much time should we allocate for this meeting: Choose the estimated duration of the meeting.
* Additional Comments or Scheduling Constraints: If you have any additional comments or constraints, enter them here.
* Date Submitted: This will be automatically filled out with the current date.
