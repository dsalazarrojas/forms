# music_interest_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge an individual's interest in various music genres, including rock, jazz, pop, electronic, hip hop, classical, country, bluegrass, folk, R&B, and country rock, and their corresponding interest levels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, navigate through the pages by clicking on the "Next" and "Previous" buttons.
2. For each page, select the music genres that you are interested in by choosing "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
3. Once you have completed all the pages, click on the "Submit" button to complete the survey.
