# Voice of the Customer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and contact information from customers, helping us understand their thoughts and experiences with our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the "Contact Information" section, which asks for your phone number or email address.
2. In the "Product Information" section, select the products you would like to provide feedback on.
3. In the "Feedback" section, type in your thoughts and comments about your experiences with our products and services.
4. Choose your preferred method of contact (phone or email).
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with our products and services.
6. In the "Comments" section, provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
7. Fill out the "Email" fields (note: we're not sure why there are so many of these, but it seems to be a requirement).
