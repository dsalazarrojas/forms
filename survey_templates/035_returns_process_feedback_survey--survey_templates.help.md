# returns_process_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the returns process. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best describe your experience from the available choices. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. How did we do?
* **How did we do?** (`customer_experience`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your overall experience with the returns process. Select one of the options that best describes your experience: Yes or No.
### 2. Was the product good?
* **Was the product good?** (`product_quality`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is about the quality of the product you received. Choose all that apply: Yes or No.
### 3. How was the return process?
* **How was the return process?** (`return_process`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is about the effectiveness of the return process. Choose all that apply: Yes or No.
### 4. How was the communication?
* **How was the communication?** (`communication`, select_one, required: false): This question is about the communication you received from us during the returns process. Select one of the options that best describes your experience: Yes or No.
### 5. What can we improve?
* **What can we improve?** (`improvement`, note, required: false): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any suggestions or feedback about how we can improve our services.
### 6. Return Date
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required: false): This is the date of your return. Please select the date from the calendar picker or enter it manually.
### 7. Return Time
* **Return Time** (`return_time`, time, required: false): This is the time of your return. Please select the time from the time picker or enter it manually.
### 8. Reason for Return
* **Reason for Return** (`return_reason`, select_one, required: false): This question is about the reason for your return. Select one of the options that best describes your reason: Yes or No.
### 9. Was the product returned?
* **Was the product returned?** (`product_returned`, select_one, required: false): This question is about whether the product was actually returned. Select one of the options that best describes your experience: Yes or No.
### 10. What was the contact method?
* **What was the contact method?** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: false): This question is about the method of contact you used during the returns process. Select one of the options that best describes your experience: Yes or No.
### 11. Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional information about your contact with us.
### 12. Survey Comments
* **Survey Comments** (`survey_comments`, text, required: false): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.

# Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you have any further comments or concerns, please use the Survey Comments field.
- Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and feedback.
