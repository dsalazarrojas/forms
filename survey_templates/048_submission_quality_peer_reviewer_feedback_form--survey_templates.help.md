# Submission Quality Peer Reviewer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Submission Quality Peer Reviewer Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from peer reviewers on the quality of submissions. This form helps authors to improve their work by providing constructive comments and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Reviewer Name**, which is the name that will be displayed on the feedback form.
2. Choose the **Submission Status** of the submission being reviewed, which indicates its current state.
3. Select the **Submission Type**, which categorizes the type of submission, such as Conference, Journal, or Workshop.
4. Rate the submission's quality using the **Reviewer Rating** field, which allows you to assign a score out of 10.
5. Provide any additional **Comments** about the submission, if desired.
6. Suggest **Feedback Suggestions** to the author, if you have any.
7. Finally, rate the submission's overall quality using the **Overall Rating** field, which also allows you to assign a score out of 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reviewer Name**: This field is where you enter your name as the reviewer. It is required for the feedback form.
* **Reviewer Affiliation**: This field is optional and allows you to enter your affiliation or institution name.
* **Submission Status**: This field has several options to choose from:
	+ Pending
	+ Under Review
	+ In Progress
	+ Accepted
	+ Rejected
* **Submission Type**: This field allows you to select the type of submission, such as:
	+ Conference
	+ Journal
	+ Workshop
	+ Other
* **Reviewer Rating**: This field is optional and allows you to rate the submission's quality on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments**: This field is optional and allows you to add any additional comments about the submission.
* **Feedback Suggestions**: This field is optional and allows you to suggest any feedback to the author.
* **Overall Rating**: This field is optional and allows you to rate the submission's overall quality on a scale of 1-10.
* **Submission Date**: This field is optional and allows you to enter the date of the submission.
* **Reviewer Notes**: This field is optional and allows you to add any additional notes about the submission.
* **Submission Comments**: This field is optional and allows you to add any comments about the submission.
* **Submission Type**: This field is optional and allows you to select the type of submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) in the label.
* Be as concise as possible when filling in fields, especially the **Comments** and **Feedback Suggestions** fields.
* Use the **Submission Status** field to indicate the current state of the submission.
* The **Overall Rating** field should reflect your overall assessment of the submission's quality.
* You can add as many **Reviewer Notes** and **Submission Comments** as needed to provide detailed feedback.
* The **Submission Date** field is optional and only needs to be filled in if the submission date is not already known.
