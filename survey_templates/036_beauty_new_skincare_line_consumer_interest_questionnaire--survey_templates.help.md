# Beauty New Skincare Line Consumer Interest Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data from potential customers about their skincare habits, preferences, and interests to help a new skincare line better cater to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your age group from the provided options.
2. Choose your gender identity from the options provided.
3. Enter your location (city, state, or country).
4. Indicate your current skincare routine frequency.
5. Select your skin type.
6. Choose your primary skin concerns.
7. List any current skincare products you are using.
8. Indicate your interest in a new skincare line.
9. Select the features that are most important to you in a new skincare line.
10. Choose the product types that interest you.
11. Select an acceptable price range for products in a new line.
12. Describe what represents good value to you in a skincare product.
13. Explain what drives your skincare purchases.
14. Select your preferred key ingredients.
15. List any ingredients you would like to avoid.
16. Choose your preferred product texture.
17. Indicate the importance of ethical sourcing for you.
18. Select your preferred packaging preference.
19. Indicate your likelihood to purchase a new skincare line.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Group (18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, 56+)**: Choose your age group to help us understand your demographic.
* **Gender (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer Not to Say)**: Select your gender identity to help us understand your preferences.
* **Location (city, state, or country)**: Enter your location to help us understand your region and cultural context.
* **Daily Skincare Routine Frequency (Never, Occasionally, Sometimes, Regularly, Very Regularly)**: Indicate how often you perform a skincare routine.
* **Skin Type (Dry, Normal, Oily, Combination, Sensitive)**: Select your skin type to help us understand your skin concerns.
* **Primary Skin Concerns (Acne, Sensitivity, Dryness, Oiliness, Aging, Dark Spots, Redness, Other)**: Choose your primary skin concerns to help us understand your needs.
* **Current Skincare Products Used (Brands and product types)**: List any current skincare products you are using.
* **Interest in New Skincare Line (Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, Very High)**: Indicate your interest in a new skincare line to help us understand your enthusiasm.
* **Most Important Features in New Line (Natural Ingredients, Cruelty-free, Dermatologist-tested, Affordable, Luxury, Sustainable)**: Select the features that are most important to you in a new skincare line.
* **Product Types of Interest (Cleanser, Toner, Serum, Moisturizer, Sunscreen, Mask, Eye Cream, Spot Treatment)**: Choose the product types that interest you.
* **Acceptable Price Range per Product (Under 20 dollars, 20-50 dollars, 50-100 dollars, 100-200 dollars, 200+ dollars)**: Select an acceptable price range for a product in a new line.
* **What Represents Good Value in Skincare (e.g. effectiveness, price, sustainability, etc.)**: Describe what represents good value to you in a skincare product.
* **What Drives Your Skincare Purchases (e.g. results, brand, price, recommendations, etc.)**: Explain what drives your skincare purchases.
* **Preferred Key Ingredients (e.g. Retinol, vitamin C, hyaluronic acid, etc.)**: List any preferred key ingredients.
* **Ingredients to Avoid (e.g. parabens, sulfates, etc.)**: List any ingredients you would like to avoid.
* **Preferred Product Texture (Lightweight, Medium, Rich, Cream, Gel, Serum, Varies by Product)**: Select your preferred product texture.
* **Importance of Ethical Sourcing (Not Important, Somewhat Important, Very Important, Critical)**: Indicate the importance of ethical sourcing for you.
* **Packaging Preference (Eco-friendly, Minimal, Luxurious, Refillable, No Preference)**: Select your preferred packaging preference.
* **Likelihood to Purchase This New Line (Very Unlikely, Unlikely, Neutral, Likely, Very Likely)**: Indicate your likelihood to purchase a new skincare line.
