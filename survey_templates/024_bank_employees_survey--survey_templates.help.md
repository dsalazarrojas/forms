# Bank Employees Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from bank employees on their job satisfaction, management, and work environment. The goal is to identify areas for improvement and provide insights to help the bank's management and HR teams make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct department from the list provided.
2. Choose your years of service at the bank.
3. Rate your overall job satisfaction, role clarity, tool adequacy, professional growth opportunities, and collaboration within your team.
4. Indicate your level of agreement or disagreement with statements related to management feedback, executive trust, work environment, and work-life balance.
5. Provide any suggestions for improvements.
6. Rate the likelihood to recommend the bank as a great place to work.
7. Enter the current date.
