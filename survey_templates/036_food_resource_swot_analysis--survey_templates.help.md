# Food Resource SWOT Analysis - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Resource SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) Analysis form is designed to help you gather information about a food resource. This form will help you identify key attributes of the food resource, providing a comprehensive overview for decision-making or further analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering details about the food resource on page 1.
2. On page 2, select the strengths and weaknesses of the food resource that apply.
3. Review your answers and provide a clear and concise description of the food resource.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Food Resource Details
* **Food Resource Details** (`Food Resource Details`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any relevant information about the food resource. Please provide a brief description of the food resource, including its name, type, or any other relevant details.

### Strengths
* **Strengths** (`Strengths`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the strengths that apply to the food resource from the provided options. You can choose one or multiple options, and they are not required. The available options are: 
    + Yes
    + No

### Weaknesses
* **Weaknesses** (`Weaknesses`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the weaknesses that apply to the food resource from the provided options. You can choose one or multiple options, and they are not required. The available options are: 
    + Yes
    + No

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information to ensure a clear understanding of the food resource.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure they accurately reflect the food resource's attributes.
