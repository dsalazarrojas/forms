# graduate_career_monitoring_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The graduate_career_monitoring_survey form collects data about your current employment status, location, company details, and work experience. The information collected is used for tracking and monitoring graduate careers.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form by following each section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employment Status** (1): A select multiple field where you can choose one or multiple options about your current employment status. Choose as many that apply from the list.
  + Options: 
    - Full-time
    - Part-time
    - Internship
    - Freelance
* **Current Employment** (2): A select multiple field where you can choose one or multiple options about your current employment status. Choose as many that apply from the list.
  + Options: 
    - Working
    - Unemployed
    - Retired
    - Pursuing education
    - Other
    - Prefer not to answer
* **Current Location** (3): A text field where you can enter your current city or location.
* **Company Name** (4): A text field where you can enter your current company's name.
* **Industry** (5): A select one field where you can choose a specific industry related to your job. Choose one that best fits from the list.
  + Options: 
    - IT
    - Finance
    - Healthcare
    - Education
    - Other
    - Prefer not to answer
* **Job Title** (6): A text field where you can enter your current job title.
* **Job Description** (7): A text field where you can enter a brief description of your current job role.
* **Job Function** (8): A select one field where you can choose a specific job function related to your job. Choose one that best fits from the list.
  + Options: 
    - Management
    - Development
    - Engineering
    - Other
    - Prefer not to answer
* **Years in current role** (9): A number field where you can enter the number of years you have been in your current role.
* **Years of Experience** (10): A number field where you can enter the total number of years of experience you have.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide or contact your administrator.
- Remember that the information collected is for official purposes only.
