# Digital Banking Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your experience with our digital banking services. Your input is valuable to us and will help us improve our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall digital experience with our institution on a scale of 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
2. Indicate the ease of access to your account information, on a scale of 1 (Very Difficult) to 5 (Very Easy).
3. Rate the ease of making online transactions, on a scale of 1 (Very Difficult) to 5 (Very Easy).
4. Choose how satisfied you are with our mobile banking services, selecting one of the following options: Very Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
5. Rate the quality of customer support you received, on a scale of 1 (Very Poor) to 5 (Very Good).
6. Choose how secure you feel about the security of our system, selecting one of the following options: Strong, Medium, or Weak.
7. Rate your overall user experience of our digital banking system, on a scale of 1 (Very Poor) to 5 (Very Good).
8. (Optional) Provide more detailed feedback on any aspect of your experience that we're not covering here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Digital Experience** (Required: false, Type: number): Rate your overall digital experience with our institution on a scale of 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
* **Account Accessibility** (Required: false, Type: number): Indicate the ease of access to your account information, on a scale of 1 (Very Difficult) to 5 (Very Easy).
* **Online Transactions** (Required: false, Type: number): Rate the ease of making online transactions, on a scale of 1 (Very Difficult) to 5 (Very Easy).
* **Mobile Banking** (Required: false, Type: select_one): Choose how satisfied you are with our mobile banking services, selecting one of the following options: Very Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
* **Customer Support** (Required: false, Type: number): Rate the quality of customer support you received, on a scale of 1 (Very Poor) to 5 (Very Good).
* **Security** (Required: false, Type: select_multiple): Choose how secure you feel about the security of our system, selecting one of the following options: Strong, Medium, or Weak.
* **User Experience** (Required: false, Type: number): Rate your overall user experience of our digital banking system, on a scale of 1 (Very Poor) to 5 (Very Good).

## Tips
* Answer the questions based on your honest opinion of your experience with our digital banking services.
* If you have any additional feedback, please provide it in the space provided below.
* Thank you for taking the time to help us improve our services!
