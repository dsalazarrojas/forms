# Team Superlative Nominations Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information about team nominations for recognition and awards within a team. The purpose of this survey is to identify outstanding team members and their contributions, and to provide opportunities for team members to be recognized and appreciated for their work.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the team member being nominated.
2. Provide their first name.
3. Provide their last name.
4. Enter their email address.
5. Describe the reason for the nomination.
6. Enter their manager's name.
7. Select the team they belong to.
8. Select the team leader's name.
9. Choose the team member's position within the team.
10. Enter the date and time of the recognition event.
11. Select the team nominator group or team nominators.
12. Enter any additional comments or reasons for the recognition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Title**: Enter a brief title or description for the nomination.
	+ This will be used as a heading for the nomination.
* **Nominee First Name**: Enter the first name of the team member being nominated.
	+ This is the first name of the person being nominated for recognition.
* **Nominee Last Name**: Enter the last name of the team member being nominated.
	+ This is the last name of the person being nominated for recognition.
* **Nominee Email**: Enter the email address of the team member being nominated.
	+ This is the email address of the person being nominated for recognition.
* **Nominee Job Title**: Enter the job title of the team member being nominated.
	+ This is the job title of the person being nominated for recognition.
* **Reason for Nomination**: Describe the reason why the team member is being nominated.
	+ Provide a brief explanation of why the team member is being recognized.
* **Nominee Manager Name**: Enter the name of the team member's manager.
	+ This is the name of the person who manages the team member being nominated.
* **Team Member**: Select the team that the nominee belongs to.
	+ Choose the team that the nominee is a part of.
* **Team Leader**: Select the team leader's name.
	+ Choose the name of the team leader who oversees the team.
* **Team Member Position**: Select the team member's position within the team.
	+ Choose the position within the team that the nominee holds.
* **Recognition Date**: Enter the date of the recognition event.
	+ Choose the date of the recognition event.
* **Recognition Time**: Enter the time of the recognition event.
	+ This is optional and only required if the recognition event is happening at a specific time.
* **Recognition Duration**: Enter the duration of the recognition event.
	+ This is optional and only required if the recognition event is happening over a period of time.
* **Recognition Reason**: Enter the reason for the recognition.
	+ Provide a brief explanation of why the recognition is happening.
* **Team Nominator Group**: Select the group or team that is nominating the team member.
	+ Choose the group or team that is nominating the team member.
* **Team Nominator**: Select the team members or leaders who are nominating the team member.
	+ Choose the team members or leaders who are nominating the team member.
* **Nomination Date**: Enter the date of the nomination.
	+ Choose the date of the nomination.
* **Nomination Time**: Enter the time of the nomination.
	+ This is optional and only required if the nomination is happening at a specific time.
* **Team Nominator Position**: Enter the position of the team nominator.
	+ This is optional and only required if the team nominator has a specific position.
* **Reason for Nominator Position**: Enter the reason for the team nominator's position.
	+ This is optional and only required if the team nominator has a specific position.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or reasons for the nomination.
	+ Provide any additional information that would be helpful for the recognition event.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by team members or leaders who are nominating team members for recognition.
* If you are not a team member or leader, please do not complete this form.
