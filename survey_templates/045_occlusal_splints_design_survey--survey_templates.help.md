# occlusal_splints_design_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather input and feedback on the design of occlusal splints, which are orthodontic appliances used to protect the teeth and jaws during orthodontic treatment. Your responses will help orthodontic professionals understand your preferences and challenges with current splint designs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, considering your personal experiences and preferences with occlusal splint designs.
2. Select all that apply for each multiple-choice question.
3. Type your answers in the text fields.
4. Enter your contact information at the end of the form to ensure we can follow up with you if needed.
