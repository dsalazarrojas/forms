# Menu Item Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their preferences regarding menu items. It's an optional survey meant to help improve menu offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select all applicable options for the first question "Do you prefer our menu items?" on page 1.
* Select one option for the question "Which item do you like best?" on page 2.
* Rate your preference for the menu item on page 3.
* Select one option for the question "Do you like our menu?" on page 4.
* Select all applicable options for the question "What is your favorite type of food?" on page 5.
* Select one option for the question "How often do you dine with us?" on page 6.
* Rate your satisfaction with our menu on page 7.
* Enter your name on page 8.
* Select one option for the question "How do you like this form?" on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you prefer our menu items?** (`menu_item_preference_survey`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all options that apply to your preference.
* **Which item do you like best?** (`menu_item_preference_2`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one option for the item you like best.
* **Rate your preference for our menu item** (`menu_item_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your preference for the menu item on a scale that will be provided in the form.
* **Do you like our menu?** (`menu_item_4`, select_one, required/optional): Select one option to indicate your preference.
* **What is your favorite type of food?** (`menu_item_5`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all options that apply to your favorite type of food.
* **How often do you dine with us?** (`menu_item_6`, select_one, required/optional): Select one option that best describes your frequency of dining with us.
* **How satisfied are you with our menu?** (`menu_item_7`, number, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with our menu on a scale that will be provided in the form.
* **What is your name?** (`menu_item_8`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or other official documents.
* **How do you like this form?** (`menu_item_9`, select_one, required/optional): Select one option to indicate your opinion of the form.
