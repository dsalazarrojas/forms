# employer_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employer_survey" form is used to collect and store data on employers in our system. It serves as a basic data collection tool for employer details such as name, job type, and work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the fields below for guidance on each section.
2. Ensure that you have a legitimate business reason for accessing or creating an employer entity.
3. Enter accurate and up-to-date information to the best of your knowledge.
4. Double-check your information before submission.
5. If you encounter any issues, contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **<employer>** (employer_info, text, required: false): Enter the name of the employer, which is a text description that should clearly identify the organization or entity.
- **<job_type>** (job_info, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the job type or category associated with the employer.
- **<work_experience>** (work_experience, note, required: false): Enter a note describing the employer's work experience or background.
- **<Salary>** (salary_info, number, required: false): Enter the salary information for the employer. Please ensure the format is correct and up-to-date.
- **Benefits** (benefits, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable benefits offered by the employer, such as health, dental, or vision insurance, retirement plans, paid vacation, and paid sick leave.
  - Health Insurance
  - Dental Insurance
  - Vision Insurance
  - Retirement Plan
  - Paid Vacation
  - Paid Sick Leave
- **Salary Frequency** (salary_frequency, select_one, required: false): Choose how often the employer pays their employees, such as daily, weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.

## Tips
- Be mindful of the employer's information accuracy and up-to-date nature.
- Review your answers before submission to avoid errors.
- If you need further assistance, contact our support team.
