# Road Trip Vehicle Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Road Trip Vehicle Checklist Form is used to gather information on the condition of a vehicle before and after a road trip. It ensures that all essential systems are checked and recorded, and any issues or concerns are noted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the vehicle's current condition and make sure that all relevant information is recorded accurately.
2. Check the corresponding fields for each section and mark the condition of the vehicle (e.g., "Full", "Low", "Maintenance Required", or "Not Checked").
3. If a field is unsure or unknown, mark it as "Not Checked" or "Unknown".
4. Record any issues or concerns and make sure to note them in the "Safety Checklist" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: This section is for recording general information about the vehicle, such as its current condition and any issues or concerns.
* **Tire Pressure**: Check and record the tire pressure of each tire, using the corresponding field for each tire.
* **Fluids**: Check and record the condition of the vehicle's fluids, such as engine oil, engine fuel, coolant, brake fluid, and transmission fluid.
* **Safety Checklist**: Record any issues or concerns that were found during the inspection, such as worn-out parts or needed repairs.
* **Brakes**: Check and record the condition of the brakes, using the corresponding field for each type of brake fluid.
* **Lights**: Check and record the condition of the vehicle's lights, such as headlights, taillights, and turn signals.
* **Belts**: Check and record the condition of the vehicle's belts, using the corresponding field for each belt.
* **Battery**:
	+ **Battery Type**: Record the type of battery used in the vehicle.
	+ **Battery Voltage**: Record the current voltage of the battery, if known.
	+ **Battery Chemistry**: Record the type of battery chemistry used in the vehicle.
* **Battery Type (2)**: Record the type of battery used in the vehicle.
* **Tire Pressure (Tire 1)**: Check and record the tire pressure of the first tire.
* **Tire Pressure (Tire 2)**: Check and record the tire pressure of the second tire.
* **Tire Pressure (Tire 3)**: Check and record the tire pressure of the third tire.
* **Tire Pressure (Tire 4)**: Check and record the tire pressure of the fourth tire.
* **Engine Oil**: Check and record the condition of the engine oil, using the corresponding field for each type of oil.
* **Engine Fuel**: Check and record the condition of the engine fuel, using the corresponding field for each type of fuel.
* **Coolant**: Check and record the condition of the coolant, using the corresponding field for each type of coolant.
* **Brake Fluid**: Check and record the condition of the brake fluid, using the corresponding field for each type of brake fluid.
* **Transmission**: Check and record the condition of the transmission, using the corresponding field for each type of transmission fluid.
* **Power Steering**: Check and record the condition of the power steering, using the corresponding field for each type of power steering fluid.
* **Air Filter**: Check and record the condition of the air filter, using the corresponding field for each type of air filter.

Note: This guide is a comprehensive resource to help users understand the purpose and usage of the form. It is recommended to review the form carefully before starting the inspection. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the vehicle's manufacturer.
