# Alumni Choir Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Choir Participation Survey is designed to gather information about your background, interest, and preferences related to the school choir. This survey will help us understand your potential involvement in the choir's activities, including concerts and performances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by asterisk (*)).
2. Choose your answers carefully, as they will help us tailor our communication and performance opportunities to your needs and interests.
3. Don't hesitate to add any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as you would like it to be used for communication purposes. 
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as you would like it to be used for communication purposes. 
* **Class Year**: Enter the year you graduated from the school. 
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates and communications from the choir. 
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for urgent matters. (Optional)
* **Current City**: Enter the city where you currently reside. (Optional)
* **Current State**: Enter the state or province where you currently live. (Optional)
* **Choir Experience**: Enter a brief description of your background in choir, including any experience or training you have. 
* **Did You Participate in School Choir**: Select whether you were a member of the school choir as a student. 
* **Voice Type**: Select your vocal range from the options provided. (Optional)
* **Years of Singing Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been singing. (Optional)
* **Current Musical Involvement**: Describe any current musical activities you are involved in. (Optional)
* **Interest in Alumni Choir**: Select your level of interest in participating in the alumni choir. 
* **Desired Participation Frequency**: Select how often you would like to participate in the choir's activities. (Optional)
* **Available Meeting Times**: Select the times when you are available to attend rehearsals. (Optional)
* **Interest in Concerts**: Select whether you would attend concerts and performances. (Optional)
* **Preferred Performance Types**: Select the types of concerts you are interested in attending. (Optional)
* **Will to Perform**: Select whether you would be willing to perform in concerts and events. (Optional)
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Select how you would like to receive updates and communications from the choir. 
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or questions you have about the choir's activities.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) and are necessary to complete the survey. Optional fields are not marked with an asterisk and can be filled out at your discretion.
