# Reader's Choice Awards Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for the Reader's Choice Awards, which are voted by the community to recognize and celebrate outstanding achievements in various categories.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the available award categories and select the category that best matches the nominee's achievement.
2. Enter the name of the nominee in the Award Name field.
3. Provide a brief description of the nominee's achievement in the Nomination Reason field.
4. Enter the name of the award winner in the Award Winner field.
5. Enter the date of the nomination in the Nomination Date field.
6. Select the year of the award in the Year field.
7. Choose the nomination status (Active or Inactive) in the Nomination Status field.
8. Choose the nomination type (Individual or Team) in the Nomination Type field.
9. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Award Category** (Select One):
	+ Select the category that best matches the nominee's achievement.
* **Award Name**: Enter the name of the award or title given to the nominee.
* **Nomination Reason**: Provide a brief description of the nominee's achievement.
* **Award Winner**: Enter the name of the award winner.
* **Nomination Date**: Enter the date of the nomination in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Year**: Enter the year of the award.
* **Nomination Status** (Select One):
	+ Choose Active if the nomination is current or has not been resolved.
	+ Choose Inactive if the nomination has been resolved or closed.
* **Nomination Type** (Select One):
	+ Choose Individual if the nominee is an individual person.
	+ Choose Team if the nominee is a team or group.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and select the correct award category to ensure accurate submission.
* Enter the name of the award winner in the format "Last Name, First Name".
* Keep the Nomination Reason field brief and concise.
* If the nomination status is Inactive, provide a brief explanation for the reason.
* If the nomination type is Team, list all team members in the Award Winner field, separated by commas.
