# MPH Concentration Change Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by students to notify advisors and other relevant parties of any changes to their current or proposed concentration. Please only complete this form if you are a student and have been approved to change your concentration. If you have already submitted this form for a concentration change, please do not resubmit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the approval of the relevant advisors and parties to change your concentration.
2. Enter your Student ID number in the "Student ID" field.
3. Enter the reason for your concentration change in the "Reason for Request" field.
4. Provide a brief rationale for your concentration change in the "Rationale" field.
5. If applicable, enter any comments from your advisor in the "Advisor Comments" field.
6. Select your new concentration from the options provided in the "New Concentration" field.
7. Select your current concentration from the options provided in the "Current Concentration" field.
8. Enter your expected graduation date in the "Expected Graduation Date" field.
9. Enter your expected graduation time in the "Expected Graduation Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, Number, Optional): Enter your student ID number. This field is used to identify you in our system.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief reason for your concentration change request. This will help your advisor understand your motivations.
* **Rationale** (`rationale`, Text, Optional): Provide a clear and concise statement explaining your rationale for changing concentrations. This will help your advisor assess your request.
* **Advisor Comments** (`advisor_comments`, Text, Optional): If your advisor has any comments or suggestions, enter them here.
* **New Concentration** (`new_concentration`, Select One, Optional): Select your new proposed concentration from the provided options.
* **Current Concentration** (`current_concentration`, Select One, Optional): Select your current concentration from the provided options.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (`expected_graduation_date`, Date, Optional): Enter your expected graduation date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Expected Graduation Time** (`expected_graduation_time`, Time, Optional): Enter your expected graduation time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
