# Property Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Survey form is used to collect essential information about a property, which will be used for various purposes such as real estate or construction documentation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the property address and any other relevant details.
2. Choose the correct structure type from the options provided.
3. Enter the property boundaries and any relevant charges or fees associated with it.
4. Check if the property guarantee is provided.
5. List any additional property features.
6. Enter any notes or comments related to the property.
7. Fill in your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
8. Select the correct property type from the options provided.
9. Enter the surveyor's name who conducted the survey.
10. Enter the date of the survey.
11. Enter any further notes or comments about the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required: false): Enter the property's street address.
* **Structure Type** (`structure_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct type of structure (House, Commercial, Other, Land).
* **Property Boundaries** (`property_boundaries`, text, required: false): Describe the property's boundaries, including size and dimensions.
* **Property Charges** (`property_charges`, number, required: false): Enter any charges or fees associated with the property.
* **Property Guarantee** (`property_guarantee`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if the property guarantee is provided (True, False).
* **Property Features** (`property_features`, text, required: false): List any additional features of the property.
* **Property Notes** (`property_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any notes or comments about the property.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct type of property (Single Family Home, Multi Family Home, Townhouse, Condo, Land).
* **Address** (`property_address`, text, required: false): Enter the property address again, if necessary.
* **Surveyor Name** (`surveyor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the surveyor who conducted the survey.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the survey.
* **Survey Notes** (`survey_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any further notes or comments about the survey.

# Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely, especially if the field is marked as "required". If you are unsure about any field or its purpose, please reach out to your administrator for assistance.
