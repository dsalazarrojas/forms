# Trauma Recovery Reflection Journal - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals track and reflect on their daily trauma recovery journey. It allows users to report their current mood, coping progress, personal reflections, trauma score, date, time, contact information (email and phone number), and other relevant details. This information can be used for personal development, research, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your daily information and reflections.
2. Select your mood for the current day from the dropdown menu.
3. Indicate your current progress in coping with trauma using the number field.
4. Write a brief reflection about how you're feeling in the personal reflections text box.
5. Rate your current trauma score by selecting from the range options.
6. Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Enter the current time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
8. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
9. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trauma Recovery Reflection Journal Form** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the main section where you can write about your daily trauma recovery experiences and reflections.
* **Daily Mood** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your current mood from the dropdown menu. This can help you identify patterns and changes in your emotions.
* **Coping Progress** (`3`, `number`, required/optional): Indicate your current progress in coping with trauma. This can help you track your growth and development over time.
* **Personal Reflections** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Write a brief reflection about how you're feeling today. This can help you process your emotions and identify areas for improvement.
* **Trauma Score** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Rate your current trauma score by selecting from the range options. This can help you track your progress and identify areas where you need additional support.
* **Date** (`6`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This helps you keep track of your progress over time.
* **Time** (`7`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the current time in 24-hour format (HH:MM). This helps you keep track of your daily activities and routines.
* **Email** (`8`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere when filling out this form.
* Try to complete it at the same time every day for consistency.
* Use this form as a tool to support your personal recovery and growth.
* Keep your entries confidential and secure.
