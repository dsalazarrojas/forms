# Frequency Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Frequency Assessment Survey" is a form designed to assess the frequency of certain habits and activities in your daily life. This survey is used to gather information on your personal habits, routines, and well-being. The data collected will help identify areas for improvement and provide insights for personal development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction page, which provides context and instructions for the survey.
2. On the "Habits" page, select the habits that you regularly practice from the options provided. You can choose one or more habits that apply to you.
3. On the "Routines" page, select the routines that you regularly practice from the options provided. You can choose one or more routines that apply to you.
4. On the "Well-being" page, provide a brief description of your current well-being state.
5. Finally, on the "Frequency Assessment" page, rate the frequency of your habits and routines by providing a number between 0 and 100. A higher number indicates that you frequently practice the habit or routine.
