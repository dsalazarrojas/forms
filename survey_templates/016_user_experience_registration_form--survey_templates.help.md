# User Experience Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The User Experience Registration Form is used to collect information about your user experience. This form is designed to help us understand your background and experiences, which will help us tailor our services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as required).
2. Fill out any optional fields that you feel are relevant to your user experience.
3. Ensure that you enter accurate and truthful information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your name as it is recorded in official documents. This will help us associate your user experience with your identity.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is recorded in official documents.
- **Email**: Enter your official email address for correspondence. This will help us keep in touch with you about your user experience.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for communication purposes.
- **User Experience**: Enter a numeric value representing your user experience. The higher the number, the more extensive your experience.
- **Workshop Type**: Select the type of workshop you attended or would like to attend. Options are Event, Study, and Workshop.
- **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop you attended or would like to attend.
- **Workshop Time**: Enter the time of the workshop you attended or would like to attend.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when filling out this form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
- You can ask for help or clarification at any time.
