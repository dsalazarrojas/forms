# Food Waste Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Waste Survey is used to gather information about food waste in your household or organization. This survey aims to understand your food waste sources, types, and reduction measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as accurately as possible.
2. Review the options provided for each question and select the most relevant one.
3. If you have any additional information that is not listed, please provide it in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **food_waste_survey** (Page 1, Text): This page is where you can describe your food waste situation in your household or organization.
* **food_waste_survey** field is a text field where you can provide a brief description of your food waste situation. Please type in your answers in the space provided. 
* **waste_sources** (Page 2, Select One): Please select the primary source of food waste in your household or organization.
* **waste_sources** field is a select-one field where you can choose the primary source of food waste. The options are:
	+ Over production: This is when you produce more food than you can consume.
	+ Loss: This is when food is lost or misplaced in your household or organization.
	+ Theft: This is when food is taken without permission.
* **food_sources** (Page 3, Select Multiple): Please select all relevant food sources that contribute to food waste in your household or organization.
* **food_sources** field is a select-multiple field where you can choose all relevant food sources that contribute to food waste. The options are:
	+ Over Production: This is when you produce more food than you can consume.
	+ Food spoilage: This is when food goes bad due to poor storage or handling.
	+ Expiration date: This is when food is past its expiration date.
	+ Over preparation: This is when you over prepare food and it goes to waste.
	+ Food waste: This is when food is thrown away without being eaten.
	+ Other: This is for any other food source not listed above.
* **waste_reduction_measures** (Page 4, Text): Please describe any measures you are taking to reduce food waste.
* **waste_reduction_measures** field is a text field where you can describe any measures you are taking to reduce food waste. Please type in your answers in the space provided.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest in your answers to provide the most reliable data.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please provide an explanation in the space provided.
* Remember to review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
