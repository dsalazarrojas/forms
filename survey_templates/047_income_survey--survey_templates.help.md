# Income Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Income Survey is a form designed to collect income-related information from users. It's essential to provide accurate and helpful data for our analysis and research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Income Survey form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to understand what information we're asking for.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "Required") with accurate and honest information.
3. You can leave the optional fields blank if you don't have the necessary information or if you're unsure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **income_levels** (`income_levels`, `number`, Required/Optional): This field asks for your current income level. Please respond with a numerical value.
* **occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, Optional): Provide a brief description of your occupation or job title.
* **annual_income** (`annual_income`, `number`, Optional): Enter your annual income (if you're unsure or don't know, please leave this field blank).
* **employment_status** (`employment_status`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Self-employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Student
* **monthly_income** (`monthly_income`, `number`, Optional): Enter your monthly income.
* **hourly_wage** (`hourly_wage`, `number`, Optional): Enter your hourly wage (if applicable).
* **annual_bonus** (`annual_bonus`, `number`, Optional): Enter any annual bonus or commission you receive.
* **tax_filing_status** (`tax_filing_status`, `select_one`, Optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Single
	+ Married
	+ Head of Household
	+ Partnership
* **household_members** (`household_members`, `number`, Optional): Enter the number of household members.
* **annual_bonus_type** (`annual_bonus_type`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Bonus
	+ Commissions
	+ Stock Options
* **employment_type** (`employment_type`, `select_one`, Optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Self-employed
	+ Unemployed
* **job_title** (`job_title`, `text`, Optional): Enter your job title or occupation description.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure or don't know the answer to a question, it's okay to leave that field blank.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us for support.
