# Team Management Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for assessing team management performance. It collects information about team dynamics, communication, feedback, leadership, and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have a clear understanding of the questions and fields.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
4. If you are unsure about any question or field, please ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro`, `note`, required): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully and understand the context before proceeding.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `text`, optional): This field is used to gather information about the team size. Please enter the number of people in your team.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, `text`, optional): Describe your current work environment, including any relevant details about your workspace or working conditions.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, `select_one`, optional): Please select "Yes" if you currently use this communication channel, or "No" if you do not.
* **Collaboration Frequency** (`collaboration_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the ways in which you collaborate with your team, including "Yes" for each applicable option.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): This field is for providing feedback about your team or work experience. Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership`, `text`, optional): Describe your team leadership style or any notable leadership qualities.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your performance as per the provided scale.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the areas in which you would like to improve.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required): Suggest any improvements or changes you have for the team or work process.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, `text`, required): Provide any action items or tasks that you or your team has to accomplish.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `date`, required): Enter the date for follow up.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field for providing any additional comments or information.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your rating.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Manager** (`manager`, `text`, required): Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `text`, required): Enter your team lead's name.
