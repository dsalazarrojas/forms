# instructor_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and evaluations from students about their instructors. This form helps instructors to improve their teaching quality, professionalism, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Answer each question based on your personal experience with the instructor.
3. For each question, choose the correct rating or write your answer in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, not required): This is a short text field for any introduction or comments you'd like to make before answering the rest of the questions.
* **Overall rating** (`8`, `number`, required): Rate the instructor's overall performance based on your experience.
* **Recommendations** (`9`, `text`, required): Write any recommendations you have for the instructor's improvement.
* **Feedback** (`10`, `text`, required): Write any feedback you have about the instructor's teaching quality, professionalism, etc.
* **Overall experience** (`11`, `number`, not required): Rate your overall experience with the instructor (1-5 scale).
* **Submission** (`12`, `text`, required): Confirm your submission by re-entering your name and any other required information.
* **Overall rating** (Note: This is the same as the "Overall rating" field, but it's repeated for consistency)
* **Professionalism** (`4`, `number`, not required): Rate the instructor's level of professionalism (1-5 scale).
* **Attitude** (`5`, `number`, not required): Rate the instructor's attitude towards students (1-5 scale).
* **Effectiveness** (`6`, `number`, not required): Rate the instructor's effectiveness in teaching (1-5 scale).
* **Presentation** (`7`, `number`, not required): Rate the instructor's presentation skills (1-5 scale).
* **Quality of teaching** (`3`, `number`, not required): Rate the instructor's quality of teaching (1-5 scale).

## Tips
* Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Answer each question based on your personal experience.
* Use the provided text fields to elaborate on your answers if needed.
* Make sure to confirm your submission before submitting the form.
